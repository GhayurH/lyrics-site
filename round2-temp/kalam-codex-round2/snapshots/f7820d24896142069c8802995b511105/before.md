Zindan Ki Sada Dr Amir Rizvi Lyrics
Yeh Aaj Bhi Baghdad Ke Zindan Ki Sada Hai.
Ik Zakhmi e Zindaan Hai Jo Sajday Mein Jhuka Hai.
Hai Hathkari Hathoon Mein Tu Paon Mein Hai Beriyaan.
Yeh Waris-e-Sajjad Hai Qaidi-e-Bala Hai.
Lo Khak-e-Shifa Ban Gayi Zindan Ki Mati.
Kazim Ka Lahu Hathkari Beri Se Gira Hai.
Iss Haal Pe Bhi Aa Ke Tamachay Hain Lagatay.
Aye Fatima Zahra Tere Betay Pe Jafa Hai.
Kazim Kabhi Thak Kar Jonhi Sotay Hain Zameen Par.
Lagta Hai Ke Mati Pe Kafan Jaisay Para Hai.
Qaidi Bhi Hain Our Baab-e-Hawaij Bhi Hain Kazim.
Aisa Koi Qaidi Kabhi Dekha Na Suna Hai.
Aye Mosa e Kazim Teri Khudami Ho Hasil.
Ab Majid-o- Amir Ki Yehi Dua Hai.
