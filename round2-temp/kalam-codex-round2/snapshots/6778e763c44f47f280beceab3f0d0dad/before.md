1
Ali Ki Beti Muhammad Raza Mungla Lyrics
Dehar Mein Zainab-e-Kubra Hai Ali Ki Beti.
Sani-e-Fatima Zahra Hai Ali Ki Beti.
Itrat-e-Ahmad-e-Mukhtar Hai Mashallah.

2
Zeenat-e-Haider-e-Karrar Hai Mashallah.
Ho Baho Fatami Kirdar Hai Mashallah.
Aks-e-Hasnain-o-Alamdar Hai Mashallah.
Sab Fazail Ka Saheefa Hai Ali Ki Beti.

3
Karbala Wale! Muhammad Ke Haseen Dilbar Ko.
Dilbar-o-Jan-e-Payamber Ko Ali Akbar Ko.
Qasim-o-Aoun-o-Muhammad Ko Ali Asghar Ko.
Oss Ne Aagosh Mein Pala Hai Jari Lashkar Ko.

4
Mazhar-e-Wasf-e-Khadija Hai Ali Ki Beti.
Sath Shabbir Ka Har Lamha Nibhaya Zainab!
Ban Ke Abbas Zamane Ko Dikhaya Zainab!
Tum Ne Sajjad Ko Sholon Se Bachaya Zainab!

5
Khutba Karrar Ke Lehjay Mein Sunaya Zainab!
Lailaha Ki Maseeha Hai Ali Ki Beti.
Misal-e-Zahra Tu Zamane Mein Hai Sab Se Aala!
Sani-e-Fatima Koi Nahi Sani Tera!

6
Tu Sakhi Bint-e-Sakhi Teri Sakha Sab Se Juda!
Sare Aalam Ke Hain Sultan Tere Dar Ke Gada!
Tu Shifat Ka Waseela Hai Ali Ki Beti!.
Bint-e-Haider Hai Tu Hasnain Ki Khwahar Zainab!

7
Jurat-e-Hazrat-e-Abbas Ki Mazhar Zainab!
Koi Dunya Mein Nahi Hai Tera Humsar Zainab!
Ab Bhi Hai Nasal-e-Yazeedi Ko Tera Dar Zainab!
Dabdaba Tera Ali Sa Hai Ali Ki Beti!.

8
Bani e Matam-e-Shabbir Hai Bibi Zainab.
Our Raza Deen Ki Taqdeer Hai Bibi Zainab.
Waris-e-Chadar-e-Tatheer Hai Bibi Zainab.
Aye Iram! Mehwar-e-Tehreer Hai Bibi Zainab.

9
Apne Aousaf Mein Yakta Hai Ali Ki Beti.
