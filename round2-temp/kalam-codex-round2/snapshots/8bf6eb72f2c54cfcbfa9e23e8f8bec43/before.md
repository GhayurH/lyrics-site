1
Aye Zia e Muneer e Wila e Ali Ahmad Timsaal.
Aye Ziya-e-Muneer e Wilaye Ali.
Har Taraf Hai Jahan Mein Teri Roshni.
Har Sitara Dehar Mein Tera Noor Hai.
Tujh Se Roshan Hai Soraj Bhi Our Chand Bhi.
Ba Khuda Sari Khilqat Mein Zeeshan Hai.
Khud Wilaye Ali Jis Ki Pehchan Hai.
Noor Ki Iss Haseen Anjuman Mein Suno.
Koi Buzar Hai Our Koi Salman Hai.
Koi Mesum Bhi Hai Daar Par Moatbar.
Koi Miqdad Hai, Our Qanbar Koi.
Mehfil-e-Shah-e-Deen e Khuda Our Shab.
Dekhta Hai Falak Bhi Yeh Manzar Ajab.
Bazm-e-Ahl-e-Attaye Wilaye Ali.
Youn Munawwar Hai Kis Roshni Ke Sabab.
Yaan Andhera Nahi Noor Hi Noor Hai.
Raat Ho Kar Bhi Chayi Nahi Teergi.
Kya Haseen Woh Nazara Tha Maidan Ka.
Khoob Mimber Sajaya Tha Palaan Ka.
Mustafa Ne Ali Ko Uthaya Tha Youn.
Jaisay Chehra Dikhatay Hoon Yazdan Ka.
Phir Muhammad Pukarey Keh Sun Lein Sabhi.
Jis Ka Maula Hoon Main Oss Ka Maula Ali.
Bar Sar-e-Dast-e-Khair Ul Wara Dekh Kar.
Lam Ya Lid Ka Haseen Faisla Dekh Kar.
Khush They Salman-o-Qanbar, Abuzar Bohat.
Ja Nasheen e Rasool e Khuda Dekh Kar.
Sab Mawali Wilayat Se Sarshar They.
Jin Ki Pehchan Pehle Se Thi Haidery.
Baat Ab Tak Samajh Mein Na Aayi Mujhe.
Kya Zarorat Thi Keh Koi Bakhin Kahey.
Yeh Niya Koi Mansab Tu Tha Hi Nahi.
Iss Pe Faiz Tu Karrar Pehle Se They.
Haal Haris Ka Dekha Tha Iss Wastay.
Dar Ke Marey Mubarak Thi Deni Pari.
Jo Ali Ki Wilayat Ka Munkir Howa.
Kalma Go Ho Gaya Phir Bhi Kafir Howa.
Justjuye Khuda Tu Bohat Ki Magar.
Rah-e-Zulmat Ka Aakhir Musafir Howa.
Roz-o-Shab Ummar Bhar Mehway Sajdah Raha.
Our Sajday Mein Lanat Barasti Rahi.
Dam Ali Ke Addoon Ka Bhartay Nahi.
Hum Kissi Ke Darane Se Dartay Nahi.
Hum Hain Ahmad Dua-e-Dil-e-Syeda.
Aisay Waison Ki Phonkon Se Martay Nahi.
Hum Tu Kehte Rahein Gay Sada.
Ya Ali Ya Ali Ya Ali Ya Ali.
