1
Allah Muhammad Ali Fatima Hasan Hussain.
Sochein Tu Lajawab Hai Yeh Shan-e-Panjtan.
Khud Khaliq-e-Azal Hai Qadardan-e-Panjtan.
Rab Ne Banai Inn Ke Tasaduq Yeh Kainat.
Ahl-e-Jahan Pe Yeh Bhi Hai Ahsan-e-Panjtan.
Tazheek Kar Raha Hai Woh Quran-e-Pak Ki.
Jhutla Raha Hai Gar Koi Farman-e-Panjtan.
Khaliq Ki Is Khudai Mein Iblees Ke Siwa.
Hai Koan Jo Nahi Hai Sana Khuwan-e-Panjtan.
Eman Ki Hai Baat Faqat Naam Ki Nahi.
Har Koi Ban Na Paye Ga Salman-e-Panjtan.
Ahl-e-Wila Ke Wastay Mehdi Ki Shakal Mein.
Jari Hai Dekho Aaj Bhi Faizan-e-Panjtan.
Ho Kar Shaheed Jab Koi Jata Hai Khuld Mein.
Woh Bill Khasos Hota Hai Mehman-e-Panjtan.
Darwaz-e-Jina Pe Sunehri Haroof Se.
 Yasir Lika Howa Hai Khiyaban-e-Panjtan.
