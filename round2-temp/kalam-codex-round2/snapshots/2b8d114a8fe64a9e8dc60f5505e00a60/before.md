1
Baghdad Shehar Mein Bara Zulm Howa Hai Asghar Khan.
Ik Lasha Pul Pe Be Dafan Para Hai.
Zanjeerein Pehan Kar Be Dafan Hai Leta.
Koi Rone Wala Iss Ka Beti Hai Na Beta.
Ghurbat Mein Kaisi Dunya Se chala Hai.
Giya Chodah Saal Ki Woh Qaid Nibah Ke.
Laein Hain Janaza Bhi Mazdoor Utha Ke.
Umat Ne Qatal Pe Haye Jashn Kiya Hai.
Ho Sab Ki Mola Hajat Yeh Pori.
Roze Ki Ziyarat Ho Jaye Manzori.
 Sajjad Our Asghar Ko Mola Ki DUa Hai.
