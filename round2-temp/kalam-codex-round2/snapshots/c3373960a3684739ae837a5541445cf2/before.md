Andhera kha gaya zindan ka, bali sakina ko
Rehaey mil nahi payey hai mar, kay bhi sakina ko

Koi pani ka rastay main kabhi, jo naam layta tha
Chacha ki yaad thi aati bohat, piyasi sakina ko

Diya jab bhi gaya pani tho lay, kar hichkiyan boli
Nahin peena hai pehlay bhai say, pani sakina ko

Kaha zain-ul-iba say waqt-e-, akhir yeh sakina nay
Jalay kurtay main dafnaana meray, bhai sakina ko

Ussay jo neend aati thi tho bas, baba kay seenay par
Na janay neend kaisay khak par, ayi sakina ko

Yeh keh kay ro paray ahl-e-haram, hukm-e-rehai par
Darayay gi humaray baad tan, haey sakina ko

Yeh noha qabr-e-sarwar par kiya, zainub nay ro ro kar
Bohat sharminda hoon zindaa main cho, raey sakina ko

Hai baba karbala main aur, bachi sham main ashhar
Ajab doori hai jo qismat nay dikh, laey sakina ko
