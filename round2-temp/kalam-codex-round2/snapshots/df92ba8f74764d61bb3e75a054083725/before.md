1
Akbar Seene Di Barchi Nu Piya Dil De Haal Sonaway.
Rab Jane Pehlaan Baba Ya Qasad Sughra Da Hun Lash Meri Te Aaway.
Sughra Nu Koi Deway Ja Ke Meri Maut Di Khabar.
Ohno Aakho Jehra Sehra Mere Layi Banaya Si Oh Wich Baqiye Dafnaway.

2
Mere Qadma De Nishanaa Kool Jehri Bethi Rendi Aye.
Ohno Keh Do Iss Mati Nal Hun Wehray Wich Meri Choti Jayi Qabar Banaway.
Barchi Kad Laan Main Babay De Aaon Taun Pehlaan E.
Aye Fikraan Ne Hun Mainu Barchi Kadda Kadda Mera Baba Na Mar Jaway.

3
Meriyaan Sahwaan Ukhari Jawan Babay Nu Aakh Do.
Samjhaan Ga Sughra Aa Gayi Jay Khat Baba Mere Seene Nal Aan Ke Laway.
Akhiyaan De Wich Disdiyaan Rayiyaan Saqlain Hasrataan.
Jiwein Akbar Kainda Howay Sughra Main Tur Chaliyaan Hun Kaun Jawab Likhaway.
