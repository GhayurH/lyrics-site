1
Alma Waliya Bhain Teri Mangdi Aye Dua.
Putraan De Samne Mawaan De Siraan Taun Koi Lut Le Na Rida.
Har Lash Kolon Lang Gayi Kehri Lash Te Khalowan.
Putraan Nu Chad Ke Ghazi Shabbir Nu Main Rowaan.

2
Rokan Na Ron Taun Pyasa Tin Dina Da Mera Mar Gaya Bhira.
Akbar Nu Wekhna Si Mere Veer Di Ibadat.
Shabbir Kool Ghazi Sughra Di Oh Amanat.
Sir Akbar Da Jiwein Mainu Aakhda Aye Maa Sughra Nu Bola.

3
Abbas Na Yateemi Ainj Di Kisay Te Aaway.
Aye Aakh Ke Sakina Haye Chehray Nu Bachaway.
Mainu Koi Na Kehway Dukhday Zakhm Mere Kanna Taun Hath Hata.
Pyasa Si Tin Dina Da Tin Waar Qatal Hoya.

4
Pehlaan Teer Mariya Aye Fair Nezay Te Proya.
Koi Maa De Samne Lab Ke Odha Bachra Na Sir Karey Juda.
Pardah Toon Mera Ghazi Ashor Tak Banaya.
Meri Maa Di Toon Dua Aye Mainu Sain Toon Bulaya.

5
Sir Mera Wekh Ke Shami Aakhday Ne Abbas Mar Gaya.
Likhda Aye Aas Najfi Ban Janda Oh Abbas Aye.
Jis Bhain Nu Bhira Di Zahra De Ghar Choon Aas Aye.
Zainab De Veer Da Sadqa Mang Laway Oh Kar Denday Ne Atta.
