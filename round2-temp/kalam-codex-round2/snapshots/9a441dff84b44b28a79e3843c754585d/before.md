1
Ali Mola Aa Gaye Sarwar Raza Karbalai
Kabay Ne Muskura Ke Kaha Ya Ali Madad.
Dewar-e-Dar Bana Ke Kaha Ya Ali Madad.
Aaya Khuda Ka Shair Tu Har But Ne Ba Khuda.
Qadmo Mein Sar Jhuka Ke Kaha Ya Ali Madad.
Khushiyaan Mana Zamane Ali Maula Aa Gaye.
ALLAH Ka Ghar Basaney Ali Maula Aa Gaye.
Darwaza Band Hai Tu Na Kabay Udas Ho.
Dewar Dar Bananey Ali Maula Aa Gaye.
Sadiyon Se Tha Andheron Mein Kaba Ghira Howa.
Ossay Noor Se Sajane Ali Maula Aa Gaye.
Aa Kar Karo Ziyarat Kabay Mein Ba Khuda.
Jo Rah-e-Rab Dikhaney Ali Maula Aa Gaye.
 Jawad Bharne Jholi Hum Bhi Chalo Chalein.
Liye Ilm Ke Khazaney Ali Maula Aa Gaye.
