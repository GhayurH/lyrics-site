Chand Jab Mah-e-Shaban Ka Dekhna Syed Mohammad Shah.
Oss Pe Likhi Hoi Karbala Dekhna.

1
Chahtey Ho Jo Arsh-e-Olla Dekhna.
Ik Nazar Janib-e-Karbala Dekhna.
Jane Do Bolay Shabeer Abbas Se.
Subh-e-Ashor Hur Aaye Ga Dekhna.

2
Anbiya Bolay Aapas Mein Fizza Hain Khush.
Ab Miley Ga Niya Mojza Dekhna.
Kaisay Kaba Banaya Barahim Ne.
Sheh Ka Bantay Hoye Taziya Dekhna.

3
Dekh Leta Hai Chodah Ki Janib Khuda.
Chahta Hai Woh Jab Aaina Dekhna.
Gar Samjhna Hai Zainab Ka Ahsaan Hai Kya.
Apni Beti Ke Sar Pe Rida Dekhna.

4
Pharpharata Hai Zikar-e-Ali Par Bohat.
Kaun Walid Hai Iss Ka Zara Dekhna.
Matam-o-Sajdah Dono Hi Barhaq Hain Par.
Kis Ko Milti Hai Kitni Jaza Dekhna.

5
Bolay Abbas Dekha Hai Abis Ne Jo.
Oss Ko Mosa Ki Aankhon Ne Kya Dekhna.
Bas Asad Zikar Kar Unn Ki Aoulad Ka.
Mustafa Dein Gay Tujh Ko Jaza Dekhna.
