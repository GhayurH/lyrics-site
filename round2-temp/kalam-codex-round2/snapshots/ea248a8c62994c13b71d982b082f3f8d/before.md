1
Asghar Pyare Asghar Pyare Ahmad Timsaal.
Asghar Pyaray Asghar Pyare, Aa Ja Na Maa Ke Dularay.
Chanay Laga Hai Andhera, Ab Sham Hone Lagi Hai.
Tere Bina Teri Madar, Ab Jan Khoney Lagi Hai.
Tum Laut Aao Na Beta, Main Jaon Sadqay Tumhare.
Aye Mere Mazloom Betey, Aye Mere Kam Sin Sipahi.
Tanha Zameen-e-Tapaan Par Kaisay Tumhein Neend Aayi.
Lori Sunai Thi Kis Ne? Dikhla Ke Chand Sitarey.
Kaisay Bhula Doon Woh Manzar, Woh Tera Maidaan Mein Jana.
Hal Min Ki Sun Kar Sadaein, Jholay Se Khud Ko Girana.
Our Teer Khana Galay Par, Iss Alqama Ke Kinaray.
Mujh Ko Qasam Hai Tumhari, Ab Dhop Mein Hi Rahon Gi.
Seene Mein Hai Sans Jab Tak, Matam Tumhara Karon Gi.
Our Teri Mazlomiyat Ke Parhti Rahon Gi Siparay.
Asbab Sab Lut Chuka Hai, Sar Par Na Mere Rida Hai.
Our Meri Aankhon Ke Aagay, Gehwara Tera Jala Hai.
Our Teri Pyari Behan Ke Nochay Gaye Goshwaray.
Gardan Shikasta Hai Teri, Our Oss Pe Tashna Labi Hai.
Zalim Tumhein Dhondtey Hain, Hathoon Mein Unn Ke Churi Hai.
Ik Shoor Barpa Hai Ran Mein, Koi Tera Sar Utaray.
 Ahmad Likhe Aaj Noha, Our Phir Woh Parh Kar Sunaye.
Roein Falak Par Farishtay, Quran Aanso Bahaye.
Matam Karein Anbiya Sab Asghar Ki Ghurbat Ke Marey.
