1
Akbar Di Qabar Te Ro Ro Ke Si Abid Wain Aye Panda.
Koi Veer Jay Jonda Honda Te Ohno Sham Da Haal Sunanda.
Har Mushkil Taun Wadh Mushkil Si Zindan Ich Qabar Banani.
Main Beriyaan Naal Sakina Di Riya Kaliyaan Qabar Bananda.

2
Jay Izn Honda Sab Taun Pehlaan Main Khud Banwaan Katwanda.
Kadi Teer Nu Jhalda Gardan Te Kadi Barchi Jigar Te Khanda.
Jerhay Thak Janday Oh Tur Janday Fair Dojay Shami Aanday.
Tin Din Taein Pathar Wasan Layi Riya Majma Badal Ke Aanda.

3
Ik Te Shami Utton Peshi Wich Ik So Mehman Safeer Aye.
Riya Zalim Sadhi Phophiyaan De Ghairan Wich Naam Bulanda.
 Akbar Abid Aye Wain Karey Kadi Chadraan Naal Sajaya.
Kadi Maqtolaan De Sir Rakh Ke Riya Oh Darbar Sajanda.
