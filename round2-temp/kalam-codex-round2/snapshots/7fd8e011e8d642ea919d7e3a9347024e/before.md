1
Allah Manzoor Duawaan Kare Bimar Diyaan
Khairi Watna Te Che Aawan Joqaan Sarkar Diyaan.
Maut Putraan Di Te Zainab Nu Nayi Oh Mar Saki.
Paishiyaan Mar Gayiyaan Sham De Darbar Diyaan.

2
Teer Wal Wekh Ke Hasiya Te Wadhai Gardan.
Wekho Jurtaan Ais Chote Jaye Azadar Diyaan.
Ro Ro Mar Jaye Gi Sughra Aye Jay Sunriyaan Khabraan.
Veer De Seene Te Barchi De Hoye Waar Diyaan.

3
Lay Kay Hathaan Wich Pyala Bethi Jholay Kool Aye.
Tangaan Rakhiyaan Ne Sakina Ne Alamdar Diyaan.
Jadoon Sir Nangay Gayiyaan Bibiyaan Wich Kofay De.
Aj Aaiyaan Shahiyaan Haider-e-Karrar Diyaan.

4
Tu v Aaya Nayi Oh Babal Main V Mardi Jawaan.
Kon Nigraniyaan Kar Si Tere Ghar Baar Diyaan.
Mere Veeraan Nu Mila De Ro Ro Aakhay Sughra.
Jharo Bardar Mein Nana Tere Darbar Diyaan.
