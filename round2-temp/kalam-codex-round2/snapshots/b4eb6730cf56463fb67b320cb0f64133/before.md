1
Aynj Bhain Te Bhira Ne Wekhi Sitam Di Peshi.
Sajjad Te Sakina Te Cha Gayi Zaeefi.
Aakhay Sakina, Abid Naqay Taun Veer Dig Ke.
Dadi De Waang Hoya Pehlu Mera V Zakhmi.

2
Rukhsar Neliyaan Te Zardi Aye Maut Wali.
Suniya Sakina Zalim Mangda Aye Wich Kaneezi.
Bazar Da Aye Manzar Abid Nu Marda Aye.
Majmay Sharabiyaan De Phophiyaan Ne Naal Qaidi.

3
Choon 4 Salaan Di Masooma Haye Satwein 7 Kothari Wich.
Tareekh Wich Na Koi Qaidan Main Wekhi Choti.
Mastoraan Khak Chadar Bazar Baal Pathar.
Rab Jane Kiwein Takiya Nook-e-Sina Taun Ghazi.

4
Sajjad Sar Si Chukda Baqir Da Sah Si Rukda.
Piyo Putraan De Gal Sanjhi Rasi Main Aisi Wekhi.
Lashay Jawaan Te Jiwein Shah Koniyaan Te Aaye.
Pathraan Che Owein Sabir Bazar Sain Guzri.
