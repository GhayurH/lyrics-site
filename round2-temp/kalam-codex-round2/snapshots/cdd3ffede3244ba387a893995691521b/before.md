Beriyan Hata Do Syed Mudassir Mehdi.

1
Maiyyat Se Maula Kazim Ke Beriyaan Hata Do.
Ho Pas Gar Nabi Ka Iss Ko Kafan Urha Do.
Haroon Ka Hukam Yeh Laya Tha Ibn-e-Khalid.
Qaidi Ki Jo Giza Hai Iss Mein Zehar Mila Do.
Barsao Lathiyaan Sab Kehta Tha Ibn-e-Shahik.
Kar-e-Nabi Ka Logon Kazim Ko Sab Sila Do.

2
Qaidi Ka Lasha Kandhey Pe Laney Walay Bolay.
Pul Aa Gaya Hai Maiyyat Kandhey Se Ab Gira Do.
Kazim Ke Pas Zainab, Kulsoom Aa Gayi Hain.
Baghdad Walon Majma Logon Ka Ab Hata Do.
Barson Ke Baad Kazim Aakhir Raha Hoye Hain.
Yeh Khabar Sham Ja Kar Zindan Mein Suna Do.

3
Kehtin Thin Fatima Yeh Kazim Ki Khol Beri.
Mehmood Aaj Apni Mannat Khud Hi Barha Do.
Zindan Ki Ghuttan Se Kehte They Ro Ke Kazim.
Kehna Ghuttan Hawa Se Mera Raza Bola Do.
Ashkon Se Apne Hadi Hai Ba Wozu Mudassir.
Aa Jao Siskiyon Ab Farsh-e-Azza Bicha Do.
