Baar Kuhsaar e Imamat Ka Bata Sakte Hain.
Bas Muhammad Hain Jo Haider Ko Utha Saktay Hain.

1
Dosra Kaun Hai Laye Jo Jawab-e-Haider.
Sirf Haider Hain Jo Ghazi Ko Bola Saktay Hain.
Ahl-e-Islam Ko Mohlat Hai Ali Ke Jaisa.
Hashar Tak Shair-e-Khuda Dhondh Ke La Saktay Hain.

2
Wosat-e-Asma e Husna Teri Rehmat Ke Nisar.
Hum Khuda Ko Bhi Ali Keh Ke Bola Saktay Hain.
Ilm Ke Shehar Ki Dewaron Pe Tehreer Hai Yeh.
Jinhein Aana Ho Woh Darwazay Se Aa Saktay Hain.

3
Chand Soraj Dar-e-Zahra Ke Diye Hain Jaisay.
Jab Zia Chahein Ghata Our Barha Saktay Hai.
Karbala Se Hai Abad Tak Ki Baharon Ko Sabaat.
Phool Khushbo Issi Sehra Se Kama Saktay Hain.

4
Ho Na Ho Karb-o-Bala Hi Mein Sajay Ga Mehshar.
Itnay Bachday Issi Basti Mein Sama Saktay Hain.
Jo Azza Khano Mein Ghazi Ke Alam Hain Akbar.
Mujh Se Behtar Marey Halaat Bata Saktay Hain.
