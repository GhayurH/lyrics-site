1
Ali Ali Mukhtar Hussain Fatehpuri.
Bole Na Bole Chahe Koi Yaar Ali Ali.
Kehti Hai Ab Bhi Kabay Ki Dewar Ali Ali.
Kartay Hain Jibraeel Mujhe Roz So Salam.
Kehta Hoon Subha-o-Sham Main So Bar Ali Ali.
Mehrab Se Boland Hai Soli Ka Tazkira.
Waan Kar Rahe Hain Meesum-e-Tammar Ali Ali.
Khaybar Mein Ho Rahi Hai Lo Bidat Ki Ibtida.
Khud Keh Rahe Hazrat-e-Sarkar Ali Ali.
Salman Ban Na Pao Gay Sajdoon Hi Se Faqat.
Jab Tak Na Panjgana Ho Takrar Ali Ali.
Tareekh Parh Lo Maon Ki Godi Se Qabar Tak.
Rehta Hai Khawar Jis Ko Lagay Khar Ali Ali.
 Majid Bata Rahi Hai Yeh Tareekh-e-Inteqam.
Majboor Ko Banata Hai Mukhtar Ali Ali.
