1
Asghar Ki Pyas Dunya Ke Darya Rula Gayi.
Har Ba Zameer Shaks Ko Yeh Baat Kha Gayi.
Yeh Dard Kaun Samjhay Keh Baba Ke Hathoon Par.
Chotay Se Aik Betay Ko Hai Maut Aa Gayi.

2
Mat Pocho Kaisay Tan Se Juda Sar Kiya Gaya.
Takleef Yeh Sakina Ko Sab Kuch Bhula Gayi.
Baba Ne Apne Betay Ki Turbat Banai Hai.
Our Maa Hai Apne Betay Ki Maiyyat Utha Gayi.

3
Woh Aag Jo Ke Bhai Ke Jholay Ko Thi Lagi.
Woh Aag Tu Behan Ka Hai Daman Jala Gayi.
Asghar Ki Maa Ne Ro Ke Yeh Sajjad Se Kaha.
Khaimon Ki Aag Betay Ke Jholay Mein Aa Gayi.

4
Yeh Dukh Hai Sham Jate Hoye Aik Qaidi Maa.
Betay Ke Ujray Lashay Ko Lori Suna Gayi.
Inn Gaud Walay Beton Ka Dukh Hota Hai Juda.
Maa Dhop Mein Guzar Ke Sab Ko Bata Gayi.

5
Asghar Ka Zikar Betay Ko Main Ne Sunaya Tu.
Sham-e-Ghareebaan Wali Udasi Hai Cha Gayi.
Yeh Dard Tu Hai Kazim-o- Haider Ko Kha Gaya.
Maa Kis Tarha Se Lash Ko Lori Suna Gayi.
