De Raha Hai Yeh Gawahi Har Shaheed E Karbala
Ash Hado Anna Aliyun Hujjatullah Ya Ali

1
Iss Sada Se Gunjti Hai Saari Maqtal Ki Fiza

2
Jab Sahar Aashur Ki Ubhri Saji Karbobala
Naam Mehzar Me Bahattar Ka Tha Likhha Ja Chuka
Khoon Se Maqtal Me Yeh Tehreer Sabne Kar Diya

3
Saare Ansaar E Husaini Paikar E Azm O Wafa
Ishq E Haider Se Tha Dil Labrez Har Ek Fard Ka
Naam E Haider Leke Sab SarwarPe Hote The Fida

4
Bar Sar E Maqtal Jo Aaya Murtuza Ka Ladla
Teen Din Ki Pyaas Thi Jalta Hua Dasht E Bala
Per Jari Ki Tegh Jo Uthhi To Uthhi Yeh Sada

5
Zeen Se Aaye Zamee Pe Sarwar E Zeeshan Jab
Dilme Jiske Ho Ali Woh Maut Se Ghabraye Kab
Kehke Yeh Khanjar Tale Shabbir Ne Sajda Kiya

6
Baad E Sarwar Jab Haram Pe Chha Gayi Shaam E Alam
Azm E Sarwar Dil Me Tha Zainab Ka Tha Jazba Na Kam
Haath Me Ghazi Ka Parcham Aur Labo Pe Bus Raha

7
Jab Aseero Ne Safar Bazaar Ka Taye Kar Liya
Shaam Ke Darbaar Me Aayi Jo Binte Sayyeda
Lehja E Haider Me Zainab Ne Yehi Khutba Diya

8
Koi Majlis Ho Juloos E Gham Ho Ya Farsh E Wila
Inki Zeenat Ka Sabab To Hai Ali Ka Tazkeera
De Rahe Hain Yeh Sadayein Isliye Ahle Aza

9
Ishq E Haider Ishq E Sarwar Se Hain Yeh Saari Ata
Ab Kalam "SALMAN" Ruk Sakta Nahi Hai Yeh Mera
Asl Me Yehi Shahadat Hai Aqeede Ki Waga
