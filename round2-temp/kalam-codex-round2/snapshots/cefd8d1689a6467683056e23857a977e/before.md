Meray baba meray baba

1
Aik bachi ka hay Noha meray baba meray baba
Hui hay sham aur na aaye baba meray baba

2
Pyas pani neend bholi chott-ti hay sans meri
Ghut raha hay dam hamara

3
Qaid ho kar jab se aayi reesma may hay kalayi
Hay dareeda jism sara

4
Khak chadar kahak bistar qabr jaisa hay ye manzar
Shab andheri din andhera

5
Bhai qaidi maa hay qaidi qaid kunba qaid may bhi
Lo khabar ab to khudara

6
Dil hay zakhmi kaan zakhmi rooh zakhmi kaan zakhmi
Moat hi day ab sahara

7
Tum kahan ho may kahan hoon bin tumharay be amaan hoon
Jis ne chaha us ne mara

8
Na chacha hay na baradar hath rakhay kon sar par
Dooba qismat ka sitara

9
Rona chahon ro na paoon soona chahon soo na paoon
Ye muqaddar hay hamara

10
Ab to aao ab to aao qabr meri khud banao
Ho ga ihsaan ye tumhara

11
Aey Rehan noha guftar tu Sakina ka hay nokar
Likh Sakina ka wo noha
