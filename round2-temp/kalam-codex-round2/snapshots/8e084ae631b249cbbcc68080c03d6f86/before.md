1
Sham Ki Galiyaan Kuja Our Ali Ki Betiyaan Al-Hafeez-o-Al-Amaan.
Aankh Se Bemar Ki Hai Khon Ka Darya Rawaan Al-Hafeez-o-Al-Amaan.
Baal Chehray Par Pare Hain Our Hathon Mein Rasan.
Aa Gayi Darbar Mein Be-Parda Ghazi Ki Behan.

2
Ro Raha Hai Ba-Wafa Ka Sar Sar-e-Nook-e-Sinna.
Ghairat-e-Sajjad Ne Yeh Zabt Kaise Kar Liya.
Yeh Tu Bas Bemar Jane Janta Hai Ya Khuda.
Qafilay Ko Kis Tarha Le Kar Chala Woh Sarbaan.

3
Kal Talak Kofay Ki Shehzadi Thi Zahra Ki Pali.
Aik Chadar Ki Sawali Hai Wohi Bint-e-Ali.
Bain Karte Hain Ali Our Fatima Noha Kuna.
Aaiye Yasrab Se Baba Fatima Ne Di Sada.

4
Aapko Ummat Se Yeh Ajar-e-Risalat Hai Mila.
Aaj Hai Aal-e-Nabi Na Meharmo Ke Darmiyaan.
Ho Ke Khush Yeh Sara Manzar Dekhte They Ummati.
Ik Nabi Zadi Ke Peeche Ik Nabi Zadi Chupi.

5
Naam They Syedaniyo Ke Our Zalim Ki Zabaan.
Jane Kis Dil Se Nisa Ne Kar Liya Noha Tamam.
Ro Diye Ahl-e-Azza Bhi Our Wajhi-e- Khush Kalam.
Bad-e-Majlis Bhi Raha Sab Ke Laboon Par Yeh Biyaan.
