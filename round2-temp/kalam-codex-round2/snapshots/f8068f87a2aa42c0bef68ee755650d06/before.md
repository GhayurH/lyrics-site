Azmat e Bibi Fatima Haider Rizvi Lyrics
Quraan Ke Lafz Bibi Ki Azmat Bataein Gay.
Kya Aayaton Mein Unn Ki Hai Midhat Bataein Gay.
Parh Kar Tu Dekh Aya-e-Tatheer Ghoor Se.
Alfaz Khud Batool Ki Ismat Bataein Gay.
Bezaar Hona Zahra Ke Dushman Se Dunya Ko.
Hai Sunat-e-Rasool Yeh Sunat-e-Bataein Gay.
Zahra Ke Khoon Ki Kitni Hai Taseer Yeh Hussain.
Nook-e-Sina Pe Kar Ke Tilawat Bataein Gay.
Mehshar Mein Hur Se Pochna Bakhshish Ke Wastay.
Kitni Hai Fatima Ki Zarorat Bataein Gay.
Dekho Dar-e-Batool Pe Tu Aag Ke Nishaan.
Kis Ko Tha Bugz Kis Ko Thi Ulfat Bataein Gay.
Sar Ko Dar-e-Batool Se Salman Uthaein Tu.
Dunya Mein Kis Ko Kehte Hain Janat Bataein Gay.
Hum Ko Imam-e-Asar Ka Hai Intezar Asad.
Aa Kar Woh Fatima Ki Haqeeqat Bataein Gay.
