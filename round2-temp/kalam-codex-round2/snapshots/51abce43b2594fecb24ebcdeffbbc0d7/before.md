Chaar Baras Ki Aik Zaeefa Ayi Hai Write Up

1
Chaar Baras Ki Aik Zaeefa Ayi Hai Zindano Mein.
Zakhmo Ke Dur Pehne Howay Hain Masooma Ne Kano Mein.
Sar Ke Baal Safaid Howay Hain Our Kamar Mein Kham Aaya.
Dewaroon Ke Sahare Bibi Chalti Hai Zindano Mein.
Abid Lash Behan Ki Rakh Ke Zindaan Mein Yeh Bain Kare.
Tere Naam Ki Qabar Nahi Hai Koi Qabristano Mein.

2
Isteqbal Mein Phool Nahi Par Pholon Jaise Zakhm Mile.
Ahl-e-Sham Ne Pathar Bante The Kam Sin Mehmano Mein.
Aaj Tamacho Ka Pehrah Hai Duroon Ki Nigrani Hai.
Kal Tak The Abbas Jahan Par Bibi Ke Nigrano Mein.
 Akbar Bain Sakina Ke Hain Our Aahein Sajjad Ki Hain.
Sadiyon Se Jo Goonj Rahe Hain Sehraon Veerano Mein.
