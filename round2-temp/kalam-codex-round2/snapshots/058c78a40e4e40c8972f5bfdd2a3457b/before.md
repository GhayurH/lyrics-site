Allah Ho Akbar Ali Ali Sibtain Haider.

1
ALLAH Ho Akbar Ali Ali Sab Ke Labon Par Ali Ali.
Haider-o-Safdar Ali Ali Ishq Ka Mehwar Ali Ali.
Maula Mera Khaybar Mein Taig Uthaye Jab Aaya.
Maut Ko Apni Dekh Ke Marhab Kanpa Thar Thar Ali Ali.
Jam-e-Wila Peetey Jao Dil Se Yehi Kehte Jao.
Mast Qalandar Ali Ali Pehla Number Ali Ali.

2
Kab Yeh Kissi Se Dartay Hain Ishq Mein Tere Jeetay Hain.
Tera Salmaan Tera Abuzar Tera Qanbar Ali Ali.
Awwal-o-Aakhir Ali Ali Batin-o-Zahir Ali Ali.
Waris-e-Mimber Ali Ali Rab Ka Lashkar Ali Ali.
Rook Sakay Ga Kab Koi Hum Tu Jary Bolein Gay Yehi.
Nook-e-Sina Par Ali Ali Zair-e-Khanjar Ali Ali.
