Aya Na Hoga Iss Tarha Rang-o-Shabab Rait Par.
Gulshan-e-Fatima Ke The Sare Ghulab Rait Par.

1
Jaan-e-Batool Ke Siwa Koi Nahi Khila Saka.
Qatra-e-Aab Ke Begair Itne Ghulab Rait Par.
Tarse Hussain Aab Ko Main Jo Kahon Tu Be Adab.
Lams-e-Lab-e-Hussain Ko Tarsa Hai Aab Rait Par.

2
Ishq Mein Kya Lutaiye Ishq Mein Kya Bachahiye.
Aal-e-Nabi Ne Likh Diya Sara Nisab Rait Par.
Lazat Sozash-e-Bilal Shoq-e-Shahadat-e-Hussain.
Jiss Ne Liya Younhi Apna Khitab Rait Par.

3
Jitne Sawal Ishq Ne Aal-e-Rasool Se Kiye.
Aik Se Barh Kar Ik Diya Sab Ne Jawab Rait Par.
Aal-e-Nabi Ka Kam Tha Aal-e-Nabi Hi Kar Gaye.
Koi Na Likh Saka Adeeb Aisi Kitab Rait Par.
