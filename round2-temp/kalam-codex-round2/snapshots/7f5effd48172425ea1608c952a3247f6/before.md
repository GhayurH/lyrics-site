1
Kardi Aye Yaad Zainab Abbas Di Wafa Nu.
Lay Gaye Ne Lut Ke Shami Ghazi Meri Rida Nu.
Main Putraan Di Jori Wari Nayi Bachiya Mera Veer.
Aakhri Welay Nehar De Pasay Takda Si Shabir.
Tere Bad Mariya Aye Ummat Ne Be-Khata Nu.
Behan Atharaan Veeraan Di Main Qaidan Aj Ho Gayi.
Lut De Maal Choon Ummat Kolon Chadraan Mangdi Rayi.
Ronda Aye Khon Abid Meri Wekh Ke Saza Nu.
Veer Di Pak Raza Nal Wadah Aynj Abbas Nibhaya.
Lath Gayiyaan Banwaan Dul Gaya Pani Veer Nu Fair Bulaya.
Kiwein Zeen Toon Laya Aye Maa Zahra Di Dua Nu.
Na Akbar Na Qasim Ghazi Naal Mere Beemar.
Takni Aan Main Sham De Pasay Disda Aye Bazar.
Tur Payi Main Sir Te Lay Ke Ik Saya-e-Khuda Nu.
Teer Asghar Di Pyas Mukai Haye Masoom Sakina.
Roway Kar Ke Yaad Jay Piyo Nu Maray Shimar Kamina.
Sunrda Nayi Koi Najfi Zainab Di Iltija Nu.
