1
Ay Akhi Abbas Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi
Hussain Kehte They Ro Ro Ke Aye Akhi Abbas.
Tumhare Dam Se Thi Khaimon Mein Roshni Abbas.
Sadaein Aatin Thin Khaimon Se Roney Walon Ki.
Galay Pe Chal Gayi Shabbir Ke Churi Abbas.
Yateem Bachoon Ke Pehrey Pe Keh Rahi Thi Behan.
Tumhare Baad Main Abbas Ban Gayi Abbas.
Rubab Kehti Thi Lagta Hai Ab Sakina Ki.
Tamachay Khatay Hi Guzrey Gi Zindagi Abbas.
Pukarti Rahi Zainab Tumhare Sar Ki Tarha.
Hamarey Sar Ki Rida Bhi Nahi Bachi Abbas.
Gira Jo Zeen Se Bhai Tu Kehti Thin Kulsoom.
Kamai Lut Gayi Umm Ul-Banin Ki Abbas.
Tumhara Pursa Doon Ja Kar Janab-e-Zahra Ko.
Hai Aarzu Yehi Akbar Ki Aakhri Abbas.
