1
Assi Na Ronday Bazaraan Wich Na Sir Wich Mitiyaan Panday.
Jay Baad Rasool, Batool De Ghar Ag Kalma Go Na Landay.
Jehri Fatah-e-Pak Mubahila Aye Ohno Wich Darbar Bulaya Aye.
Na Has Has Ke Ashab Ohno Darbar De Wich Jhutlanday.

2
Oday Putraan Qatal Ho Jana Si Ais Ag De Baad Yaqeen Hoya.
Na Pak Batool Di Zindagi Wich Ohno Putar Di Lash Wakhanday.
Wada Sabir Piyo Hasnain Da Aye Jinnay Tarafdi Zahra Wekhi Aye.
Koi Tutiyan Pasliyaan Naal Daso Haye Sah Ne Kiwein Aanday.

3
Jehri Itrat Pak Rasool Di Aye Ohno Dhiyaan Samne Mariya Aye.
Jay Wekh Lainday Odhay Maa Baba Haye Dowein Oh Mar Janday.
Tatheer Way Pak Rida Odi Wich Putar Di Lash Nu Paya Aye.
Aye Dukh Painday Din Te Najfi Ohno Raat De Wich Badlanday.
