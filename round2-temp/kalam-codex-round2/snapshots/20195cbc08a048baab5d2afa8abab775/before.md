La Raib Sadat Hai Khidmat Ali Akbar Ki.
Hum Sab Ki Ibadat Hai Midhat Ali Akbar Ki.

1
Quraan Tu Na Latay They Jibreel e Ameen Lekin.
Woh Dekhney Aate They Sorat Ali Akbar Ki.
ALLAH Ke Baad Inn Ka Takbeer Mein Naam Aaya.
Ta Hashar Azaan Mein Hai Shirkat Ali Akbar Ki.

2
Tazeem Se Ik Bosa Peshani Pe Detey They.
Jab Dekhtey They Sarwar Sorat Ali Akbar Ki.
Islam Ka Bachpan Tha Our Inn Ki Jawani Thi.
Hai Dein Ke Tahafuz Mein Mehnat Ali Akbar Ki.

3
La Raib Ali Akbar Shabeer Ki Dolat Hain.
Our Deen-e-Payamber Hai Dolat Ali Akbar Ki.
Quran Ki Sorat Hai Kalma Hai Shahadat Ka.
Hai Deen-e-Shujat Mein Beasat Ali Akbar Ki.

4
Bhar Poor Jawani Mein Qayam Jo Rahe Haq Par.
ALLAH Ne Dekhi Hai Qudrat Ali Akbar Ki.
Juz Kar-e-Nabowat Ke Milti Hai Payamber Se.
Sorat Ali Akbar Ki Seerat Ali Akbar Ki.
