1
Bhainr Roway Te Kahway Maanr Wadha De Ghazi.
Mere Wichrey Hoye Putraan Nu Mila De Ghazi.
Zindagi Howay Fazal Tera Jawani Manray.
Main Pareshan Haan Main Janra Ya ALLAH Janrey.
Ja Ke Pardais Meri Jori Liya De Ghazi.
Aa Gayi Eid Te Mawaan Ne Sajay Bachray.
Waang Shehzaday Yaan Masnad Te Bithaye Bachray.
Meri Ujari Hoi Godi Nu Wasa De Ghazi.
Sariyaan Bhainraan Ne Godaan Che Uthaye Veran.
Meriyaan Dhiyaan De Pardeson Na Aaye Veran.
Rondiyaan Bhainraan Nu Chan Veer Mila De Ghazi.
Aynj Lagda Aye Koi Zulm Haneri Aye Charhi.
Mere Sartaj Nu Maa Fatima Rondi Aye Khari.
Bhainr De Khabaan Di Tabeer Dasa De Ghazi.
Zikar Muslim Di Ghareebi Da Jehra Karda Aye.
Farz Matam Da Jehra Shaks Ada Karda Aye.
Odhi Tauqeer Zamane Te Wadha De Ghazi.
