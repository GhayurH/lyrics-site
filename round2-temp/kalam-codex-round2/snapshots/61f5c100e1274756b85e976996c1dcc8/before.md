Baba Teri Zahra nu ik payshi maar mukka gaee ayy

Jayrray boohay Nu Muhammed chumkay salam kehnday sann ummat onu awn jala gaee ayy …

Samnay lokka day Haq mangdi raee, baba main rondi raee, teri tehreer day tukkray kar kay tauheen kar day rayy, ayy gall mainu boht rula gaee ayy

Aasray puttran day main turdi rahiee rahwan tay diggdi raee

Teray darbaar ton ghar taie main mehsoos kar gaiyan, ghari waqt Razaa di agaee ayy

Ayataan parhdi raee naalay dasdi raee, main waris Nabiyan di

Fair vee louk meray khutbay nu, sawal karday rayy, ummat mera virsa kha gaee ayy

Aarn kay dheeyan nay, jadon takkaya si, baba meri haalat nu

Wassday vayrray chh Zainab meri, behosh hogai si, aynu shaam waqaalay paa gaee ayy
