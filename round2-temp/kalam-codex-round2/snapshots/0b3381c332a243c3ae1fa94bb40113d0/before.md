1
Allah Pardesiyaan Nu Watna Te Le Aaway
Bharjaiyaan De Sang Zainab Kiwein Sham Nu Jaway.
Abid Ne Bazaraan Wich Oh Dard Uthanrey Ne.
Jadon Phophiyaan Te Lokaan Ne Pathar Barsanrey Ne.

2
Beemar Koi Shala Zanjeer Na Paway.
Zainab Te Ruqaiya V Aj Qaidnaa Baniyaan Ne.
Majma Aye Laeena Da Bin Chadraan Khariyaan Ne.
Ghazi Nu Kaho Aa Ke Aj Parday Banaway.

3
Jis Peer Ne Lokaan Diyaan Haye Qaidaan Churaiyaan Ne.
Aj Oss Diyaan Tin Dhiyaan Bazaraan Che Aayiyaan Ne.
Koi Na Rehya Jehra Aj Qaidaan Choraway.
Ik Mari Wichoray Di Reh Gayi Aye Madine Wich.

4
Jidhay Veer Ne Jhalna Aye Phal Barchi Da Seene Wich.
Ja Nanay De Rozay Te Wich Deway Jalaway.
Ashor Di Shab Farwa Qasim Nu Sajaondi Rayi.
Ro Ro Ke Sakina V Asghar Nu Khadondi Rayi.

5
Khaimay Che Dukhi Zainab Akbar Nu Sajaway.
Qayam Aye Zamane Te Sadaat Di Sardari.
Manzoor Karey Maula Akhtar Di Azadari.
Gham Aal-e-Muhammad Wich Sada Athro Bhaway.
