1
Aankhein Laho Bahaein Gi Jab Bhi Azan Ho Gi.
Zindaan Mein Dil Sakina Baradar Ki Yaad Mein.
Our Sughra Baam-o-Dar Pe Akbar Ki Yaad Mein.
Ik Shama Woh Jalaye Gi Jab Bhi Azan Ho Gi.

2
Bazar Mein Darbar Mein Zainab Ko Sakina Ko.
Akbar Tumhare Bad Sar-e-Sham Hai Laila Ko.
Chadar Ki Yaad Aaye Gi Jab Bhi Azan Ho Gi.
Iss Rah-e-Sham Mein Tu Koi Aasra Nahi.

3
Zainab Ko Oss Ka Gham Hai Keh Sar Par Rida Nahi.
Sar Kis Tarha Chupaye Gi Jab Bhi Azan Ho Gi.
Dunya Mein Koi Aisi Azaan Phir Na Hoi.
Akbar Azan Aakhri Maqtal Mein Tum Ne Di.

4
Dunya Na Bhol Paye Gi Jab Bhi Azan Ho Gi.
Ik Maa Jo Apne Lal Ko Roti Rahi Sada.
Tum Bhi Asad Osse Zarah Mehshar Mein Dekhna.
Noha Woh Yeh Sunaye Gi Jab Bhi Azan Ho Gi.
