Kehti thi Zainab, Ay mere sheyro
Kehti thi Zainab, Mere dilero
Aun-o-muhammad mere, aun-o-muhammad

1
Khwaab ye Zainab ka tha, chaand sitare mere, chaand sitare
Mamu pe hongaye fida, goad ke pale mere, goad ke pale
Khwaab ye sacha huwa, Sar mere uncha raha, hone ko sheh par fida
Laal mere, sheyr mere chaand mere

2
Jaffar-e-tayyar ke, khun ke akkas ho, khun ke akkas
Husn mei Akbar ho tum josh mei Abbas ho, josh mei Abbas
Jang ke maydaan mei maut ke armaan mei, saya-e-quran mei
Aise utho, aise laro, maut pe tum qud paro

3
Haan meri takeed par dhyan tumhara rahe, dhyaan tumhara
Jake lab-e-nehr bhi pyaas gawara rahe, pyaas gawara
Dud na bakshungi mei, mu bhi na dekhungi mei, sheyr ki beti hu mei
Wada karo, aao chalo, tum na raho

4
Maana ke pyaase ho tum, pyaas pe qabu rahe, pyaas pe qaabu
Sheh ke alamdar ki, saans mei khushbu rahe, saans mei khushbu
Bas ye tamanna meri, laaj rahe khun ki, Hai ye wasiyat meri
Khawo qasam, jaawo hasham, hoga na kam

5
Aun ye kehna laga, bhayi Muhammad suno, bhaayi Muhammad
Aeik taraf mei badoun, eik taraf tum barho, eik taraf tum
Jang ho fowj-e-sitam, jang kare aise ham, maamu hame de alam
Deke hamain, amma kahe, jeete rahain

6
Dono jari jang ko, surat-e-andhi barhe, surat-e-andhi
Nana Ali ki tarha, maut pe haavi rahe, maut pe haavi
Milke jo hamla kiya, meymana-o-mesra, eik me dam hogaya,
Teygh-e-Ali, aise chali, dhoom machi

7
Dekh rahi thi jo ma, khaime ke darpar khadi, khayme ke dar par
Saath wahi thay khade hazrat-e-abbas bhi, hazrat-e-abbas
Bhaayi behen saath thay, sahib-e-awlaad thay, waris-e-ajdaad thay,
Kehne lage, khub lade, sheyr mere

8
Larte kahan tak bhala, pyaas ke maare huwe, pyaas ke mare
Jalti huwi khaak par dono tarapne lagay, dono tarapne
Bhaayi se kehne lagi, fakhr se bint-e-Ali, surkhru Zainab huwi,
Sadkhe tere, bhaayi mere, laal huwe

9
Aun-o-Muhammad na the, wo dur-e-nayaab thay, wo dur-e-nayaab
"SARWAR-O-REHAN" wo wakif-e-adab thay, wakif-e-adab
Hukm jo maa ne diya, rukh na kiya nehr ka, pyaas ka sadma saha,
Maa ne kaha, shukhr-e-khuda, huway fida
