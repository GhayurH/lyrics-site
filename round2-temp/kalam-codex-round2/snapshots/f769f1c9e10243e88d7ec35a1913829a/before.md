1
Ayat Hai Fatima Shakeel Baltistani.
Quraan Ki Aayaton Mein Woh Aayat Hai Fatima.
Woh Sorah-e-Kausar Ki Abarat Hai Fatima.
Youn Shan-o-Manzalat Mein Bohat Hi Boland Hai.
Fakhar-e-Ali Hain Umm-e-Taharat Hai Fatima.
Tazeem Ko Uthtey Hain Nabi Ahmad-e-Mursal.
Dono Jahan Mein Fakhar-e-Risalat Hai Fatima.
Tasbeeh-e-Fatima Wohi Parhta Hai Subha-o-Sham.
Jis Ke Ragoon Mein Khon-e-Taharat Hai Fatima.
Hasnain Ki Woh Maa Hain Dukhtar Nabi Ki Hain.
Jigar-e-Rasool Noor-e-Wajahat Hai Fatima.
Aankhon Pe Jo Para Hai Woh Pardah Hata Zarra.
Karday Zahoor-e-Mehdi Yeh Hajat Hai Fatima.
Gar Woh Kahin Tu Samne Jhukta Hai Chand Bhi.
 Farhat Jahan Mein Shama-e-Hidayat Hai Fatima.
