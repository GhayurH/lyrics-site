Ayatein Mukhtar Hussain Fatehpuri

1
Tazeem Fatima Ki Samajhti Hain Aayatein.
Uthtey Hain Jab Rasool Tu Uthti Hain Aayatein.
Rakhtay Hain Ikhteyar Yeh Zahra Ke Ladlay.
Gesu Sanwar Dein Tu Sanwarti Hain Aayatein.
Woh Our Ghar Hain Jin Pe Keh Badil Barastay Hain.
Yeh Ghar Ali Ka Hai So Barasti Hain Aayatein.

2
Fizza Yeh Faiz-e-Rizq-e-Ali-o-Batool Hai.
Teri Zaban Se Jo Nikalti Hain Aayatein.
Thamay Hoye Hain Zainab-o-Kulsoom Maa Ka Hath.
Quran Ke Saharey Pe Chalti Hain Aayatein.
Sehan-e-Abu Turab Hai Bint-e-Rasool Hain.
Naqsh-e-Qadam Jo Bantay Hain Banti Hain Aayatein.

3
Zahra Ke Ghar Se Loh Pe Hoti Hain Muntaqil.
Phir Loh Se Zameen Pe Utarti Hain Aayatein.
Woh Teen Roz Rotiyaan Our Sorah Dehar Ka.
Zahra Tumharey Rizq Pe Palti Hain Aayatein.
Hum Aayatein Sunaein Tu Akbar Jawab Mein.
Tasbeeh e Bint-e-Mustafa Parhti Hain Aayatein.
