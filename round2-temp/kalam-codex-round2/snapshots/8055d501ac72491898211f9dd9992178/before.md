Beti se ye farmane lage shahe Madina rona na Sakina
Baba se tera ab nahi hoga kabhi milna rona na Sakina

1
1. Hai narghaye ada me Mohammed ka nawasa marjayega pyase
Karlena hame yaad jo pani kabhi peena rona na Sakina

2
2. Aaja tujhe seene pe sulaleon me ghadi bhar pyasi meri dukhtar
Ab khak ke bisthar pe padega tumhe sona rona na Sakina

3
3. Dil maa ka hai chalni abhi Asghar ke alam me behosh hai gham me
Tum Rakhna khayal osko tadapne nahi dena rona na Sakina

4
4. Sab maregaye ab na raha koyi hamara hai kaun sahara
Ab Ahmade Mukhtaar ka luttha hai safeena rona na Sakina

5
5. Pursa milega tumko tamancheon se pidar ka ojde howe ghar ka
Ek aur naya zulm padega tumhe sehna rona na Sakina

6
6. Bhandwa ke rasan hatheon me jana hai soye Shaam sadma hai harek gaam
Bibi hai muqader me tere khaid bhi hona rona na Sakina

7
7. Sajde me bosagahe Mohammed jo kategi mushkil ye padegi
Tum Dekho gi in ankeon se khaimeon ka bhi jalna rona na Sakina

8
8. Bechainiya bhad jayegi furkath me hamari baba ki dulari
Ghurbath se maa ki gaud me meon dhap ke sona rona na Sakina

9
9. Zainab shahe wala jo howe rukhsath sadmeon ki thi shiddath
Jane lage to dard se phatne laga seena rona na Sakina
