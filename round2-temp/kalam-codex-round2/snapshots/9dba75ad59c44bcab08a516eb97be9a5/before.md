1
Ali Ko Sataaya Logo Ny Shaan Rizwan Hussain.
Nabi Ke Baad Ali Ko Sataya Logon Ne.
Ali Ka Jan Ke Rutba Bhulaya Logon Ne.
Qadam Qadam Pe Miley Hain Ali Ko Gham Itnay.
Hain Janasheen-e-Nabi Ne Sahay Sitam Itnay.
Ali Ko Khon Ke Aanso Rulaya Logon Ne.
Ho Kya Ali Ke Ghamo Ka Kissi Ko Andaza.
Ghamo Mein Gham Yeh Ali Ka Hai Aaj Tak Taza.
Ali Ki Zojah Pe Jo Hath Uthaya Logon Ne.
Utha Janaza Ali Ka Tha Noha Zainab Ka.
Tumhare Bachoon Ko Pardais Mein Yateem Kya.
Yeh Kaisa Zulm Hai Baba Kamaya Logon Ne.
Aseer Ho Ke Jo Zainab Thi Kofay Mein Aayi.
Pukari Baba Yeh Dekho Rasan Hai Pehnai.
Barehna Sar Mujhe Baba Phiraya Logon Ne.
Ali Ke Khon Ko Apna Nabi Ne Khoon Tha Kaha.
Ali Ke Sath Nabi Ka Bhi Hai Yeh Khon Baha.
Ali Ka Sajday Mein Jo Khoon Bahaya Logon Ne.
Nabi Ne Jis Ko Thi Dastar Apni Pehnai.
Ossi Imam Ki Gardan Mein Daal Kar Rasi.
Nabi Ke Shehar Mein Haye Phiraya Logon Ne.
Woh Dar Jahan Pe Farishton Ka Aana Jana Tha.
Woh Dar Ali Ka Jahan Sab Ko Sar Jhokana Tha.
Sitam Yeh Hai Keh Wohi Dar Jalaya Logon Ne.
Zahoor-e-Hazrat-e-Mehdi Ho Kar Duaein Asad.
Lein Unn Se Badlah Woh Jin Jin Ko Tha Ali Se Hasad.
Hai Kaisay Kaisay Ali Ko Sataya Logon Ne.
