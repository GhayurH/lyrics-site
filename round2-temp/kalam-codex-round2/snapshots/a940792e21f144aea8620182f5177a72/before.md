1
Raza Kay Dar Pe Ek Sajda Bohat Hai Syed Mudassir Mehdi.
Ubharna Tu Agar Chahta Bohat Hai.
Raza Ke Dar Pe Ik Sajdah Bohat Hai.
Barha De Jo Mujhe Qaroon Se Bhi.
Tumhare Dar Ka Ik Sikka Bohat Hai.
Hai Dewana Dar-e-Maula Raza Ka.
Jabhi Behlool Yeh Dana Bohat Hai.
Gharon Mein Hazari Kyun Na Karein Hum.
Hamara Rizq Youn Barhta Bohat Hai.
Jo Mujh Ko Bakhswaye Rooz-e-Mehshar.
Teri Midhat Ka Yeh Parcha Bohat Hai.
Ghatata Hai Tu Azmat Kyun Raza Ki.
Tujhe Kiya Hashar Mein Pitna Bohat Hai.
Do Aalam Ko Amma Mein Apni Rakhay.
Tere Gunbad Ka Woh Saya Bohat Hai.
Milaney Ke Liye Soraj Se Aankhein.
Zameen-e-Toos Ka Zarrah Bohat Hai.
Mila Deta Hai Jo Karb-o-Bala Se.
Tumhare Shehar Ka Naqsha Bohat Hai.
 Mudassir Hai Karam Maula Raza Ka.
Tabhi Tu Naqsh Yeh Likhta Bohat Hai.
