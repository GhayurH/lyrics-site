Chelum hay gharibeon ka Zainab zindan se chutkar ayi hay

1
1. Kehthi hay bahen ke haal ko dekho tho bahi ek baar
Is dali hamsheer ka pochoge nahi kya hale zaar
Ta umer teri yaad me royegi Zainab zar zar
Kho baithi bahathur ko Zainab kya khoob naseeba layi hay

2
2. Ale nabi par baad tere bhaiya howi kya kay jafa
Unteon ki nangi peet par bithlaye the hamko ehle jafa
Kehthe the tamashayi dekho ye bara gale ek reesman
Ithna bhi kisi ne na soncha ke ye Zehra ki jayi hay

3
3. Khaime hamare jalgaye bhaandhi gayi hatheon me rasan
Chini gayi chadar meri na de saki bhay ko kafan
Bazareon me darbareon me ho ayi ye laachar bahen
Paighame Hussaini duniya ko hamsheer teri deayi hay

4
4. Sajad ki gardan me tauq aur paeon me zanjeer thi ah
Thame howe unteon ke mehar chaltha raha kateon ki rah
Durre lagatha the aada beenar ki halath thi tabah
Chale pade the paon me har gaam pe thokar khayi hay

5
5. Beti tumhari qaid ke zulmo sitham na seh saki
Shimr-e-laeen ke zulm se kuch bhi na zuban se keh saki
Apne chacha aur baap se nadaa juda na reh saki
Masoom dasthe shumer se galeon pe tamache khayi hay

6
6. Zinda ke darpe ja ja kar baba ko bulathi thi hardum
Kehthi thi phoopi kyon hogayi duniya sari ham se barham
Ta marg yateema karthi rahi mazloom Hussaina ka matham
Zainab Sakina jaan ko zinda me khokar ayi hay

7
7. Bazoo kataye sothe ho Abbas ay shere jawan
Ye bekaso beyaar bahen tere bina jaye kahan
Parde ko akar roklo jane ko hay ye karvaan
Behno ki rida ka rakhwala shabeer ka tho shaidayi hay

8
8. Ojdi kisi ki maang tho khali kisi ki goad howi
Kho baithi koyi bete ko koyi bhay ki yaad me hay khoyi
Ek shab ki biyahi dulhan ko kya kehke bhala samjhaye koyi
Qasim bane markhad pe khadi dulhan teri mujrayi hay

9
9. Hilthi thi zamee larza the falak Zainab ajab wo alam tha
Rothi thi khade syedaniya dafnane lage abid laasha
Chalayi wathan na jayegi bin bhay ke khahar dukhiya
Ay karbala godi me teri Zehra ki sari kamayi hay
