1
Akbar Tur Gaya Aye Sughra Rondi Reh Gayi Aye.
Lag Ke Naal Diwaraan De Khali Ghar Wich Beh Gayi Aye.
Naal Mehmal De Ewein Khak Oddi Aye Jiwein.
Dil De Pak Madine Di Sabz Haweli Deh Gayi Aye.

2
Wain Qasad Karda Akbar Riya Na Bibi.
Sehrey Ganay Shagna De Surkh Haneri Le Gayi.
Dard Da Aye Manzar Sadiyaan Taun Khatam Nayi Hoya.
Maa Mehmal Te Beh Gayi Te Dhi Mehmal Taun Leh Gayi Aye.

3
Kaun Sughra Nu Kehway Barchi Jigar Che Lag Gayi.
Khon De Naal Madine Wal Aan Di Aas V Beh Gayi Aye.
Aal-e-Zahra, Akbar Yasrab Wasi Na Karbal.
Do Pasay Jageraan Wich Sirf Veerani Reh Gayi Aye.
