ZAKIR SHAMS UL HASSAN - Marsiya

1
Baba hai museebat ka safar mera muqadar
Main shaam ke zindaan say naa aaon gi palat kar

2
Main dortay oontoo say kayi baar giri hun
Maara hai mujhy shimr nay har baar utha kar

3
Pehnaye the jo eid pay tu nay mujhy Baba
Khenchay hai meray kaano say wo shimr nay gohar

4
Kehtay hain laeen tars say Abbas jjh kahan hai
Hastay hain ridaaon ko wo naizo pay saja kar

5
Lagta hai kay zindaan mein meri aakhri shab hai
Rotay hain mujhy dekh ke Sajjad aur Baqir
