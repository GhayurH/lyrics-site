1
Ali Ka Tarana Sarwar Raza Karbalai.
Rahe Ga Labon Par Ali Ka Tarana.
Zamana Kahey Lakh Mujh Ko Dewana.
Jahan Mein Ali Bas Mera Rehnuma Hai.
Ali Mera Aaqa Ali Paishwa Hai.
Kissi Ko Na Jano Kissi Ki Na Mano.
Mere Dil Mein Bas Hai Ali Ka Thikana.
Yeh Bolay Muhammad Ke Sun Le Khudai.
Ali Iss Jahan Mein Hai Tu Mera Bhai.
Tha Mosa Ko Haroon Se Jo Bhi Nisbat.
Hai Tum Se Hamara Wohi Dostana.
Jo Khum Mein Sada Di Keh Man Kuntu Maula.
Muhammad Yeh Bolay Khuda Ka Hai Kehna.
Mere Baad Hoga Jahan Ka Tu Rehbar.
Mere Baad Hoga Tumhara Zamana.
Mita Koi Sakta Nahi Hai Barabar.
Yeh Shabeer-o-Zahra, Ali Ke Hain Nokar.
Azadar Har Din Naye Ban Rahe Hain.
Laga Hai Wilayat Ka Jo Karkhana.
Yeh Asif Kahey Jan Hai Mera Haider.
Main Kalma Parhon Ga Faqat Tera Sarwar.
Ali Ko Hi Mana Jahan Mein Sahara.
Yeh Batein Nahi Hain Mehaz Shairana.
