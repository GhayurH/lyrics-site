Karbala vich oun di maa deway ghazi no sada
Tere Bajo lay gai aye sir to Zahara di rida

1
Jal gay khamay paiyan Rasiyan ban k qadi sham chaliya
Bhaway okhay veer ghazi sham ty kofay dy raa

2
Main bazaar vich toran di Adi nai aa veer ghazi
Kiway torna majmeya vich ujri behni no dasa

3
Jay ijazat Jung di ghazi Tenu MIL di karbala vich
Na kadi main sham jandi marda na mera bhira

4
Shala na koi ujray veeran jiway ujri bhen teri
Tera qatal lay reya aye veer ghazi mera naa

5
Mehdi roky akhy ZAINAB uth mohari sham chaliye
Main dukhi da hun aye bachaya Kalla abid asra
