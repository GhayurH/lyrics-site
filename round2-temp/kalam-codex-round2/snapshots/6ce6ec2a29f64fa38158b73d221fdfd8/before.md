1
Ali Ka Hota Hai Mohsin Hashmi.
Rajab Ki Terah Hai Charcha Ali Ka Hota Hai.
Nozool Kabay Mein Maula Ali Ka Hota Hai.
Jo Dar Ho Band Tu Dewar Dar Banati Hai.
Khuda Ke Ghar Mein Jab Aana Ali Ka Hota Hai.
Nuseriyon Ki Tarha Kyun Kahon Ali Ko Khuda.
Khuda Hai Woh Jisay Sajdah Ali Ka Hota Hai.
Hatay Jo Arsh Ka Parda, Khulay Jo Kabay Ka Dar.
Hamesha Samne Chehra Ali Ka Hota Hai.
Woh Kat Bhi Jaye Tu Haq Bolne Se Rukti Nahi.
Woh Jis Zaban Pe Narah Ali Ka Hota Hai.
Kalam Karta Hai Jab Bhi Kissi Nabi Se Khuda.
Tu Baat Cheet Mein Lehja Ali Ka Hota Hai.
Aye Jibreel Bicha Par, Bacha Le Jald Zameen.
Khuda Ke Hathoon Se Hamla Ali Ka Hota Hai.
Naseeb Ho Ya Ho Kuch Bhi Woh Banney Lagta Hai.
Bananey Ka Jo Iradah Ali Ka Hota Hai.
Ali Ke Betay Jo Sajday Mein Sar Jhukatay Hain.
Sar Our Fakhar Se Oncha Ali Ka Hota Hai.
Khud Ibrahim Bhi Kehte Hain Oss Ko Shia Asad.
Khuda Ka Banda Jo Shia Ali Ka Hota Hai.
