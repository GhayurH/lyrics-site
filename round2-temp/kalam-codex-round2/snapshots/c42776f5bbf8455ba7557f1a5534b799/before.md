1
Baba Ko Rotay Rotay Zahra Guzar Gayi Hain.
Maula Ali Ke Ghar Ko Veeran Kar Gayi Hain.
Zahra Tumhare Gham Mein Konain Ro Rahe Hain.
Kulsoom Ro Rahi Hain Hasnain Ro Rahe Hain.
Zainab Pukarti Hain Amma Kidhar Gayi Hain.
Bichrey They Jab Se Baba Tab Se Hi Mar Chukin Thin.
Mohsin Ke Baad Bibi Zindah Kahan Rahi Thin.
Pehle Hi Kab Thi Zindah Jo Keh Dein Mar Gayi Hain.
Zindah Rahi Hain Bibi Sadmay Utha Utha Ke.
Din Raat Hain Guzarey Aanso Baha Baha Ke.
Pehlu Shikasta Zahra Khasta Jigar Gayi Hain.
Zalim Ne Zakhm Aisa Pehlu Pe Hai Lagaya.
Ik Waar Ne Hi Oss Ke Aisa Asar Dikhaya.
Mohsin Bhi Mar Gaye Hain Zahra Bhi Mar Gayi Hain.
Darwaza Jab Jala Tha Mehway Fogaan Hain Tab Se.
Kulsoom Our Zainab Noha Kuna Hain Tab Se.
Jis Din Se Kar Ke Zahra Soona Yeh Ghar Gayi Hain.
Daman Ali Ka Thamay Bachay Pukartay Hain.
Baba Yeh Kaisay Sadmay Amma Ne De Diye Hain.
Daag-e-Yateemi De Kar Be Haal Kar Gayi Hain.
 Saqlain Karbala Mein Chalti Hain Jab Hawaein.
Har Samt Gonjti Hain:ZILL-E-RAZA: Sadaein.
Zahra Ke Ghar Ki Kaliyaan Ran Mein Bikhar Gayi Hain.
