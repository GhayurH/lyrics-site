Sughra Aye Ro Ro Wain Kare Sheraz Khan

1
Sughra Aye Ro Ro Wain Karey Mainu Bhul Gaye Sukh Te Chaan Akbar.
Main Sham Da Safar Te Nayi Kita Par Fair V Rul Gayi Aan Akbar.
Mere Rowan Te Pehrey Ne Veera Mera Haal V Dadi Warga Aye.
Nayi Apne Ghar Wich Le Sakdi Ro Ro Ke Tera Naa Akbar.
Shereen Nu Phophi Aakhiya Si Janj Odhay Shehar Le Jaway Gi.
Mainu Apne Kool Bolawein Toon Jadon Sehrey Laway Maa Akbar.

2
Main Kali Chali Wehriyaan Wich Mere Naal Na Veer Na Baba Aye.
Das Shehroon Bahir Rowan Layi Kiwein Bait Al-Huzan Banaa Akbar.
Main Howaan Ya Na Howaan Par Teri Pak Nishani Howay Gi.
Tainu Mera Haal Sunawan Gay Qadma De Pak Nishaan Akbar.
 Akbar Bibi Aye Wain Karey Aye Kaisa Khuwab Main Takiya Aye.
Piya Baba Khat Nu Dafan Karey Main Sehra Payi Dafnaa Akbar.
