Ya Ali Teray Kya Kehnay Syed Sajjad Naqvi.

1
Dekh Ke Tujh Ko Sajday Mein Bola Yeh Rab-e-Akbar.
Sadiyon Se Tha Raaj Buttoon Ka Kaun Karata Khali.
Tu Ne Aa Kar Aye Maula Meri Tauqeer Bacha Li.
Terah Rajab Ko Bool Utha Kaba Bhi Wajad Mein Aa Kar.
Tu Aala Hai Jaisa Hai Aisa Hi Khalq Se Rishta.
Aayat Laney Wala Hai Tera Shagird Farishta.

2
Our Zamane Mein Buzar Salman Hain Tere Nokar.
Pak Nasab Ho Gar Milta Hai Ishq-e-Ali Ka Tohfa.
Jitni Bari Naimat Hai Yeh Oss Ka Ho Shukar Bhi Aisa.
Dunya Ko Batlatay Hain Meesum Yeh Daar Pe Ja Kar.
Tere Hi Dam Se Qayam Hai Tauheed Ka Charcha Maula.
Soch Ke Yeh Quran Kissi Momin Ne Jab Bhi Khola.

3
Sorah-e-Ikhlas Parhi Tu Aaya Khud Hi Lab Par.
Hukam-e-Khuda Se Jab Aaye Betay Ko Le Kar Makka.
Unn Ko Bhi Maloom Tha Yeh Keh Kya Hai Khuda Ki Mansha.
Bolay Ibrahim Bhi Yeh Bunyad-e-Kaba Rakh Kar.
Aaye Jab Mairaj Se Wapis Ghar Jibreel Ke Maula.
Pocha Yeh Ashab Ne Kya Dekha Batlaein Aaqa.

4
Dekh Ke Haider Ki Janib Yeh Bool Uthay Peygamber.
Apne Karam Ka Faiz Zamane Bhar Ko Dikhaya Tu Ne.
Main Tha Faqeer Tumharey Dar Ka Lal Banaya Tu Ne.
Aaj Bhi Sehwan Mein Kehta Hai Yeh Shahbaz Qalandar.
Dil Mein Hamarey Rooz-e-Azal Se Yeh Tehreer Hai Kunda.
Bas ALLAH-o-Nabi Janein Tum Ho Woh Gorakh Dhanda.

5
Tera Mansab Hai Maula Idrak-e-Bashar Se Bhar Kar.
Beghair Hub-e-Ali Mudua Nahi Milta.
Ibadaton Ka Bhi Hargiz Sila Nahi Milta.
Khuda Ke Bando Suno Ghoor Se Khuda Ki Qasam.
Jisay Ali Nahi Miltay Khuda Nahi Milta.
Basad Talash Na Kuch Wosat-e-Nazar Se Mila.

6
Nishan-e-Manzil-e-Maqsood Rehbar Se Mila.
Ali Miley Tu Miley Khana-e-Khuda Se Humein.
Khuda Ko Dhonda Tu Woh Bhi Ali Ke Ghar Se Mila.
Jis Ke Gun Gatay Hain Woh Hum Ko Mehman Banaein.
Hai Dil Mein Arman Najaf Sajjad Our Hamza Jaein.
Tham Ke Rozay Ki Jali Hum Bhi Bolein Yeh Jhuk Kar.
