Tanhai may zindan ki yeh kehti thi Sakina
Baba ki judayi Main, na mar jayai Sakina

1
1) Daman main lagi aag to gohar bhi utarai
Baad aap kai adaa nai, tamachai mujhai maarai
Is haal-e-aseeri main, main mar jaun kahin na

2
2) Is haal main ghurbat main kahin chain na paya
Zulmat kai andherai main, laeeno nai sataya
Ab dhundai vala hai, is bachi ka kafeena

3
3) Jab ghar sai chalai thai sabhi saath thai apnai
Kaisa yeh sitam ho gaiya, mun phaira hai sab nai
Kab lot kai ponchai gain, ham ab shehar madina

4
4) Bhaiya Ali Akber bhi suyai khuld sidharai
Ammu bhi nahi kis ko, Sakina yeh pukarai
Nanna Ali Asgher hai, Na baba ka wo seena

5
5) Zindaan hai yateemi hai jafa aur sitam hai
Baba ki Judayi bhi huwi, dil main yeh gham hai
Mar ker bhi reha ab to, main shayad hun kabhi na
