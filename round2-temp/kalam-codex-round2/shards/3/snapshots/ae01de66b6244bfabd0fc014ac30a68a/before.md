1
Zameen Ta Arsh-e-Mualla Ali Nazar Aaye.
Jahan Nigah Ne Dekha Ali Nazar Aaye.
Hoi Thi Barsar-e-Meraj Rab Se Baat Magar.
Rasool Ko Pas-e-Lehja Ali Nazar Aaye.

2
Haan Aik Waqt Mein Chalees Mukhtalif Ja Pe.
Mukhalifon Ne Bataya Ali Nazar Aaye.
Janab-e-Mula Hasan Se Sakhi Qalandar Tak.
Har Aik Beta Ali Ka Ali Nazar Aaye.

3
Hasad Ki Aag Mein Sheikhain Jal Gaye Khum Mein.
Nabi Ke Sath Jo Maula Ali Nazar Aaye.
Kisi Ko Raam Kissi Ko Khuda Kissi Ko Wali.
Keh Jis Mein Zarf Tha Jaisa Ali Nazar Aaye.

4
Tera Aqeeda-e-Tauheed Dagmagaye Agar.
Khuda Ko Karte Na Sajda Ali Nazar Aaye.
Yeh Khon Saqi-e-Kausar Ka Faiz Hai Yasir.
Ali Ka Sara Gharana Ali Nazar Aaye.
