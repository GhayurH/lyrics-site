1
Yeh Azadari Nahi Rab Se Wafadari Hai.
Zikar-e-Shabir Bhi Khaliq Ki Zimadari Hai.
Yazeed(L) Dushman-e-Deen Dushman-e-Khuda Thehra.
Hussain Rooh-e-Khuda Deen Ki Panah Thehra.

2
Oss Ka Khof Tumhari Safon Pe Tari Hai.
Hussain Our Namaz-e-Khuda Mein Farq Nahi.
Azza Ho Jari Tu Samjho Namaz Tark Nahi.
Hussain Ke Hi Sabab Yeh Namaz Jari Hai.

3
Humein Pata Hai Ossay Haq Pe Kyun Samjhtay Ho.
Shikam Yazeed(L) Ke Luqmo Se Barh Ke Bethay Ho.
Haram Kha Ke Harami Ki Tarafdari Hai.
Tumhari Sazishein Hain Yeh Azza Bhi Ruk Jaye.

4
Magar Yeh Matami Kaisay Kahin Pe Jhuk Jaye.
Nasal-e-Fasiq Bhi Azadari Se Tu Hari Hai.
Dhamak Yeh Karti Hai Larzaan Zameer-e-Batil Ko.
Yeh Deed Ahad Ki Har Aik Nasal-e-Qatil Ko.

5
Door-e-Hazir Ke Yazeedon Se Yeh Bezari Hai.
Hai Tera Ajar Salamat Tera Sharaf Jhuda.
Hazar Sajday Jo Bugz-e-Hussain Mein Hoon Adda.
Madh-e-Hussain Ka Ik Harf Inn Pe Bhari Hai.
