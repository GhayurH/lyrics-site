1
Sher E Murtaza Ghazi Sayyed Zaire Naqvi.
Ali Hain Shair-e-Khuda, Shair-e-Murtaza Ghazi.
Wafa Ke Mulk Ka Tanha Hai Kibriya Ghazi.
Woh Jis Ke Naqsh-e-Qadam Par Sajod Wajib Hain.

2
Ali Ka Naqsh-e-Makarrar Hai Ba Khuda Ghazi.
Nabi, Imam Nahi Tu Magar Yehi Sach Hai.
Teri Bulandi Ki Shahid Hai Syeda Ghazi.
Tera Nasab Hai Ali La Shareek Teri Ada.

3
Teri Tarha Koi Hoga Na Ho Saka Ghazi.
Dua-e-Fatima Sorat Ali Wali Jaisi.
Hasan Hussain Ki Seerat Ki Inteha Ghazi.
Qalandari Ke Qabiley Ka Mojza Bhi Hai Tu.

4
Wilayaton Ke Aqeeday Ki Inteha Ghazi.
Yeh Tera Kala Alam Har Khushi Ka Mehwar Hai.
Tu Mushkilon Ka Waseela Hai Ba Khuda Ghazi.
Jalal-e-Kibriya Kehte Hain Murtaza Ko Batool.

5
Jalal-e-Haider-e-Karrar Ban Gaya Ghazi.
