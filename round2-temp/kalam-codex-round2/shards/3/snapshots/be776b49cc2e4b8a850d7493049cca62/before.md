1
Ya Zahra Isteghasa Bibi Fatima Wajhi Hasan.
Dar Aap Ka Hai Baab-e-Atta Ya Zahra.
Phailaye Hain Hum Dast-e-Dua Ya Zahra.
Woh Jis Se Payamber Ko Bhi Milti Thi Shifa.
Oss Chadar-e-Tatheer Ka Kar Do Saya.
Mil Jaye Mareezo Ko Shifa Ya Zahra.
Rahib Ko Tere Dar Se Mili Hai Aoulad.
Jo Godiyaan Veeran Hain Howein Aabad.
Aye Madar-e-Shah-e-Dosra Ya Zahra.
Pardah Rahe Mehfooz Kaneezon Ka Teri.
Parday Ki Nigehdar Hoon Zainab Bi Bibi.
Qayam Rahe Har Sar Pe Rida Ya Zahra.
Ho Sar Pe Sada Teri Dua Ka Saya.
Har Matami Mehfooz Rahe Maula Ka.
Ta Hashar Rahe Sheh Ki Azza Ya Zahra.
Aa Jaein Bas Ab Gaib Se Maula Mehdi.
Har Totay Hoye Dil Ki Hai Faryad Yehi.
Mil Jaye Inhein Izn-e-Khuda Ya Zahra.
Rota Hai Tumhein Gaib Mein Mehdi Ab Bhi.
Woh Soch Ke Tooti Hoi Pasli Teri.
Deta Hai Yeh Ro Ro Ke Sada Ya Zahra.
Jalta Howa Darwaza Giraya Haye.
Ummat Ne Ajab Zulm Yeh Dhaya Haye.
Mohsin Bhi Tera Qatal Howa Ya Zahra.
Zalim Ne Tujhey Aisa Tamacha Mara.
Chehray Pe Waram Aa Gaya Tere Zahra.
ALLAH Re Yeh Sabar Tera Ya Zahra.
Sadqay Mein Tere Bakhsh De ALLAH Khata.
Taseer Zabno Ko Youn Ho Jaye Atta.
Maqbool Ho Hum Sab Ki Dua Ya Zahra.
Be Jurm Aseeron Ki Bhi Sun Lo Rodad.
Tum Gaib Se Unn Logon Ki Karo Do Imdad.
Ho Jaein Sabhi Jald Reha Ya Zahra.
Muflis Pe Bhi Ho Jaye Karam Shehzadi.
Reh Jaye Gareebon Ka Bharam Shehzadi.
Khairat Jo Mil Jaye Zara Ya Zahra.
Farsh-e-Gham Shabbir Bichanay Se Humein.
Rozon Ki Ziyarat Pe Bhi Jane Se Humein.
Roke Na Kabhi Koi Waba Ya Zahra.
Har Cheez Luta Dein Gay Gham-e-Sheh Ke Liye.
Gardan Bhi Kata Dein Gay Gham-e-Sheh Ke Liye.
Hasil Ho Faqat Teri Raza Ya Zahra.
Zahra Hain Duaon Ka Waseela Jo Dabeer.
Badlay Gi Bohat Jald Sabhi Ki Taqdeer.
Har Dil Pe Wajih Ho Ga Likha Ya Zahra.
