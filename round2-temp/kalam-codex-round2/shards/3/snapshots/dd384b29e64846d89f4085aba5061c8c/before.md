1
Sajjad Di Maut Bazar De Wich Ayho Manzar Dasdi Aye.
Ghazi Nezay Te Ronda Aye Jadon Zainab Parda Mangdi Aye.
Haye Wadh Bheer Gayi Sharabiyaan Di Jadon Naam Liya Aye Zainab Da.
Zara Hoor Quran Suna Baba Na Naam Laway Oh Zainab Da.
Haye Has Has Ke Piya Kehnda Aye Wekho Bhainr Baghi Di Aandi Aye.
Bazar De Wich Sakina Ne Jadon Sir Babay Da Takiya Aye.
Os Waqt Bazar De Wich Zalim Kuj Aynj Da Zulm Kamaya Aye.
Oh Naizay Nal Hatanda Aye Jadoon Sir Babay Da Mangdi Aye.
Chup Kar Ja Bhainr Sakina Tun Kite Wakhri Qaid Na Ho Jaway.
Main Chup Veera Oddon Kar Jaan Gi Bas Sir Babay Da Aa Jaway.
Ohh Aakhri Sa Payi Laindi Aye Jadon Sir Nu Seene Landi Aye.
Baqir Aye Piyo Nu Puchda Aye Kyun Qabar De Wich Piya Rona Ayein.
Ais Wastay Rona Waan Bachra Main Zakhmi Pehlu Takiya Aye.
Meri Dadi Hunr Taein Baqiye Wich Nayi Pasay Tu Hath Chandi Aye.
Haye Jiyondiyaan Ji Sajjad Hasan Bazar-e-Sham Nu Nayi Bhuliya.
Koi Puchda Haal Te Das Mula Bas Sham Da Nam Hi Lainda Riya.
Bazar Che Chadar Mangda Riya Bas Gal Aye Jigar Nu Khandi Aye.
