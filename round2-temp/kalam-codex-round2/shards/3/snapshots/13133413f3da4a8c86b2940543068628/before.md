Saj Giya Mimber-e-Palaan Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein.
Ho Ga Man Kuntu Ka Elan Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein.

1
Martaba Kya Hai Ali Ibn-e-Abi Talib Ka.
Jan Jaein Gaye Yeh Anjan Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein.
Arsh-e-Aoula Ke Sahabi Bhi Karein Shirkat.
Ejtima Hai Bara Zeeshan Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein.

2
Kuch Tu Khush Hoon Gaye Bohat Sun Ke Misali Khutba.
Our Kuch Hoon Gaye Pareshan Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein.
Deen Ki Takmeel Ho Unwan-e-Wahi Hai Ya Nabi.
Rab Ka Jari Karein Farman Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein.

3
Shar Pasandoon Se Tahafuz Ke Liye Pehre Par.
Hoon Gaye Meesum Se Nigheban Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein.
Ya Ali Aap Ki Azmat Ke Tasaduq Yasir
Hai Nabi Tere Sana Khuwan Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein.
