Ye Chaand Kaisa Nikla Hai Al-e-Nabi Pe.
Har Taraf Moat Ka Saya Hai Hai Al-e-Nabi pe.

1
Khud Tukre Kar Ke Karbal Quran Ke Paron Ko.
Daag Bagi Ka Lagaya Hai Al-e-Nabi pe.
Dikha Ke Chadar Naizon Pe Rulaya Zainab Ko.
Sharabi Majma Hansaya Hai Al-e-Nabi pe.

2
Haye Kar Ke Barish Pathron Ki Musalmano Ne.
Kyun Garam Pani Giraya Hai Al-e-Nabi pe.
Bachaye Kaise Quran Haye Jalte Kabay Se.
Sar Pe Chadar Ka Na Saya Hai Al-e-Nabi pe.

3
Kya Zulm Dahaye Allah Hi Jane Abid Pe.
Keh Khon Ruk Hi Na Paya Hai Al-e-Nabi pe.
Yaqeen Hai Ye Ghazi Teri Atta Se Hi.
 Zeeshan Nohay Likh Raha Hai Al-e-Nabi pe.
