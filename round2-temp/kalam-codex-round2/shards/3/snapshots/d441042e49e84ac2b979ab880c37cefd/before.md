Tauba Hai Syed Mudassir Mehdi.

1
Kalma Parh Kar Shirk Ka Shewah Toba Hai.
Man Kuntu Ke Baad Saqeefa Toba Hai.
Dast-e-Wali Par Paish-e-Payamber Baiyat Ki.
Phir Bhi Wilayat Ko Jhutlaya Toba Hai.
Fath-e-Khaybar Ki Dolat Se Ho Ke Ghani.
Fatah-e-Khaybar Se Hi Dhoka Toba Hai.

2
Koi Talwaron Ke Saye Mein Soya.
Koi Nabi Ke Saye Mein Roya Toba Hai.
Ahl-e-Baqiya Ke Tukron Par Paltay Ho.
Dhatay Ho Unn Ka Hi Roza Toba Hai.
Aisay Bhi Shetan They Kalma Gowon Mein.
Jin Ko Dekh Iblees Bhi Bola Toba Hai.

3
Tuf Hai Laeeno Bag-e-Fidak Ko Gasab Kiya.
Uff Siddiqa Ko Jhutlaya Toba Hai.
Rishta Le Kar Nari Noor Ke Ghar Aaya.
Toba Toba Toba Toba Toba Hai.
Ishq-e-Ali Ka Dam Bhartay Rehtey Turab.
Phir Bhi Dil Mein Khuwaish-e-Dunya Toba Hai.
