Sharaab e Wilaay e Hassan Pee Rahay Hain Sayyed Zaire Naqvi.
Nasha Kar Ke Bhi Hosh Mein Jee Rahe Hain.

1
Mawaddat Ka Haq Bhi Yehi Hai Farishtay.
Bara-e-Hasan Pairhan Si Rahe Hain.
Jo Qaumon Pe Hadi Utaray Gaye They.
Hidayat Mein Unn Ki Woh Hadi Rahe Hain.

2
Hussain Our Abbas Jaisay Dilawar.
Hasan Mujtaba Ke Sipahi Rahe Hain.
Ghulaman-e-Zahra Hain Hum Loag Logo.
Azzal Se Hi Barah Imami Rahe Hain.

3
Hasan Ki Ghulami Pe Nazaan Hain Kitne.
Kabhi Jo Nabi Ke Sahabi Rahe Hain.
Laqab Syeda Ka Hai Kausar Azzal Se.
Ali Rooz-e-Awwal Se Saqi Rahe Hain.

4
Kabhi Giriya Zaari Kabhi Jashan Jari.
Sada Matami Khuld Mein Hi Rahe Hain.
Ajab Nam-e-Haider Pe Fatway Hain Jari.
Issi Kam Par Molvi Jee Rahe Hain.

5
Imamo Ke Hotey Bhi Jari Thi Sazish.
Muqassar Rahe Hain Nuseri Rahe Hain.
Dar-e-Syeda Ki Ghulami Mein Zaire.
Malaik Kuja Anbiya Bhi Rahe Hain.
