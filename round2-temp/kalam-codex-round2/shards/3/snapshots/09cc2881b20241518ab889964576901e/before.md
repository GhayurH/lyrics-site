1
Zindan Che Ro Ro Ke Pochdi Sakina Veer Nu
Kadon Mukniyaan Aye Qaidaan Ummat Ne Kadon Laounra Tere Pairaan De Zanjeer Nu.
Kayi Din Taun Main Tarsi Aan Haye Babay De Seene Nu.
Fair Bhawein Main Mar Jawaan Ik Waar Mila Ja Aa Ke Ronday Hoye Be Sheer Nu.

2
Masooma Di Maiyyat Te Ro Aoun Di Maa Aakhay.
Tera Kafan Bana Dendi Kaday Shimar Jay Na Khonda Haye Chadar-e-Tatheer Nu.
Sajjad Kehway Ro Ro Aye Sham Diyo Loko.
Main Hashar Da Zamin Haan Jay Qabar Di Thaan Mil Jaye Dafnaraan Aye Humsheer Nu.

3
Mar Janra Aye Zainab Ne Jay Rukyo Na Toon Shimra.
Ghazi Di Bhateeji Nu Na Mar Tamachay Toon Fizza Kehya Be Peer Nu.
Sah Kho Le Sakina De Zindan Di Sekhaan.
Tainu Saikdi Mar Gayi Aye Aj Kaun Deway Ja Ke Paigham Aye Shabeer Nu.

4
Was Koi Na Chal Sakiya Karbal Che Aye Chad Aayiyaan.
Ijaz-e-Imamat Nal Phophiyaan Nu Wikha Abid Aj Nanay Di Tasveer Nu.
Kithay Zahra Diyaan Paliyaan Kithay Majma Laeena Da.
Oh Khutbay Si Zainab De Rang Laya Jinna Akhtar Islam Di Jageer Nu.
