1
Humko Iss Say Piyar Hai.
Ana Aik Baar Hay
Khodena Bekaar Hai
Zindagi Gulzar Hai.

2
Yeah Ishq Ka Darbar Hai
Kissi Ke Gham Ko Bantna Hi Piyaar Hai.
Zindagi Gulzaar Hai.
Yeah Ishq Ka Darbaar Hai

3
Yahan Ghamoon Ko Palna Bekaar Hai.
Yahan Titliyaan Muskurati Hain.
To Geetoon Mein Dhal Jati Hain.
Yahan Jugnoo Jab Jagmagatay Hain.

4
To Rang Ban Jatay Hain.
Yeah Mana Ke Ankhoon Mein Ansoo Hain.
Diloon Mein Basi Bay Basi.
Chalo Yeah Bhi Mana Ke.

5
Apno Say Behter Hain Ajnabi.
Yahan Rastay Dushwaar Hain.
Or Manzalain Us Paar Hain.
Magar Isay Jinay Ko Dil Tayyar Hai.
