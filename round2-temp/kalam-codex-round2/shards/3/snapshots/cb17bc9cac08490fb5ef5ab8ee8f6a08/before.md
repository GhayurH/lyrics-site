1
Tere Qatil V Kalma Go Baba Hasnain Ne Ro Farmaya.
Tere Bajoon Maa Aina Kho Liya Aye Saday Sir Taun Piyo Da Saya.
Ghazi Toon Rook Le Zainab Nu Kali Ghar Choon Bahir Na Aaway.
Odi Sun Le Na Aawaz Koi Mera Naa Le Ke Na Wain Paway.

2
Jadon Zarb Lagi Mere Babay Nu Ohno Karbal Yaad Si Aaya.
Odi Farz Namaz Qabool Nayi Jay Aal Uttay Na Darood Parhey.
Eman Oda Kiwein Sabit Aye Jehra Qatal Nabi Di Aal Karey.
Aina Ajar-e-Risalat Wich Nana Sada Baba Mar Mukaya.

3
Farmaya Sabz Qaba Ro Ke Jadon Maa Watna Te Aawaan Ga.
Iss Zarb Taun Le Ke Zehar Taein Tainu Sara Haal Sunawaan Ga.
Taun Rona Aye Jadon Baqaiya Wich Main Apna Kafan Wakhaya.
Ik Sajdah Aye Wich Kofay De Ik Sajdah Karbal Hona Aye.

4
Aj Rondi Aye Zainab Baba Maqtal Che Sakina Rona Aye.
Zahra Diyaan Dhiyaan Nu Baba Aina Wich Pardais Rawaya.
Main Ban Ke Zahra Veraan Da Sari Zindagi Sath Nibhawaan Gi.
Jadon Veer Mera Be Watan Hoya Oday Naal Main Karbal Jawaan Gi.

5
 Najfi Har Veer Ne Zainab Nu Haye Ro Ke Gal Nal Laya.
