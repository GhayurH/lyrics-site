Sirr Neyzayan Tay, Kuj Qaidee Nay, Oh Qafila Ya Rab Kidar Gaya

Sirr neyzayan tay, kuj qaidee nay, oh qafila ya Rab kidhar gaya

Oh rahwan aj tak rondeeyan nay, jithon chan Zahra da guzar gaya.

Ujaray ghar dee khaak rulaa kay, Zainab rondee veer kuhakay

Vas da ghar Maa Zahra da, at payr cha keevain ujar gaya

Qabar itch teer zakam vee lar kay zaalim agay neyazay phar kay

Kat kay laash neyzay tay charee, Asghar da phat jigar gaya

Kufay dee jaageer dee maalik, bin chaadar tatheer dee waris

Mai ujaree jis veer dee khaatir, veer vee maithon vitchar gaya

Na chadaran na he sir tay saaya, kaafla ik darbaar itch aaya

Paak shareef gharanay noo, maahol sharabee takan gaya

Mauraan utay halqat aam ay, Abid puch da ay ho Shaam ai

Kuj burkay kuj chadaran qaidee, mangda Shaam itch upar gaya
