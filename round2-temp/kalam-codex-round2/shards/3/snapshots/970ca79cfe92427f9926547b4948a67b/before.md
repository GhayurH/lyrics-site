1
Shah-e-Karbala Milay Rajab Ali Khan.
Ghazi Se Aisay Hans Ke Shah-e-Karbala Miley.
Jaisay Ali Se Arsh Pe Hoon Mustafa Miley.
Main Ne Kiya Jo Roza-e-Abbas Ka Tawaf.
Ruk Ruk Ke Karbala Mein Mujhe Anbiya Miley.
Chalti Hai Phir Jahan Mein Phareray Ko Tham Kar.
Abbas Ka Alam Jo Hawa Ko Khula Miley.
Abbas Ka Bhatija Hoon Batlata Hoon Ossay.
Asghar Ko Aaj Ran Mein Zara Hurmala Miley.
Lagta Hai Jaisay Miltay Hoon Abbas Our Hussain.
Aa Kar Alam Ke Sath Jonhi Taziya Miley.
Yousuf Ne Bhaiyon Se Kaha Tum Bhi Ho Magar.
Bhai Miley Kissi Ko Tu Abbas Sa Miley.
Khush Ho Ke Dono Hathoon Se Khairat Detey Hain.
Abbas Ko Sakina Ka Jab Wasta Miley.
Nasha Rajab Ka Utray Na Shaban Tak Asad.
Peetey Raho Jahan Se Bhi Jam-e-Wila Miley.
