1
Turbat Pe Fatima Ki Mehdi Ka Yeh Noha Hai.
Rehta Hoon Yahan Jab Se Roza Tera Toota Hai.
Tooti Hoi Turbat Hai Mati Ka Saibaan Hai.
Zalim Yeh Sochta Hai Roza Tera Veraan Hai.
Main Ne Tu Yahan Dadi Baba Tera Dekha Hai.
Nikli Thi Andherey Mein Aye Dadi Teri Maiyyat.
Shair-e-Khuda Pe Oss Dam Kaisi Thi Woh Musibat.
Turbat Ka Nishaan Khud Se Dada Ne Mitaya Hai.
Bad Az Nabi Ke Tujh Pe Haye They Kaisay Din Woh.
Bas Aa Gaya Ik Dam Se Zalim Woh Zamana Jo.
Paygamber-e-Akram Pe Roney Nahi Deta Hai.
Main Aakhri Hujjat Hoon Khulaq-e-Do Aalam Ki.
Sab Badlay Chukaon Ga Jab Rab Ki Raza Ho Gi.
Har Ik Meri Nazron Mein Dushman Tera Phirta Hai.
Dil Dil Mein Tarapta Hai Mazloom Mera Maula.
Din Raat Baqiya Mein Karta Hai Aa Ke Giriya.
Har Ashk Mera Maula Zahra Se Chupata Hai.
Yaad Aata Hai Darwaza Jalta Howa Mehdi Ko.
Rasi Mein Dikha Haider Khinchta Howa Mehdi Ko.
Talwar Ke Qabzey Par Hath Apna Woh Rakhta Hai.
 Afraz Fatima Ki Turbat Pe Zairon Ne.
Mehdi Ke Sunay Aksar Hain Bain Fizaon Mein.
Sarkar Ke Pursay Se Ghamgeen Madina Hai.
