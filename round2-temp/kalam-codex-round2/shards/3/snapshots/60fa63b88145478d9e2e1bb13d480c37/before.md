1
Shah e Aman De Pak Janaze Te Bhaina Wain Paye Asghar Khan.
Veerna Tere Babay Diyaan Jaiyaan Rul Jana Tere Bajoon Dais Paraye.
Aina Te Dasda Ja Dardaan Di Mari Zainab Nu.
Karbala Wich Ladlay Qasim Nu Sang Bhaina De Bhain Kiwein Parnaye.

2
Gash Kar Gayiyaan Jadon Suniya Si Zahra Jaiyaan Ne.
Veer De Mazloom Janazay Te Yasrab De Lokaan Ne Teer Chalaye.
Jodah Ne Jo Zulm Maula De Naal Kita Aye.
Rab Karey Haye Waang Na Jodah De Dunya Te Koi Ainj Na Zulm Kamaye.

3
Jehray Pak Nabi De Har Welay Naal Rainday San.
Wich Qabar Aj Pak Muhammad Nu Kalma Go Ki Ajar Walawan Aaye.
Aj Pak Bibi De Veran Nu Naal Akbar De.
Anbiya V Ais Pak Janazay Te Pursay Layi Mazloom Nu Rowan Aaye.
