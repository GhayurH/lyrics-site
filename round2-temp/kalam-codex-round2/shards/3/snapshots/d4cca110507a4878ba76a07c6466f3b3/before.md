Ya Elahi Khair Kar Syed Kazim Hussain Zaidi.

1
Dar Jala Kar Hans Rahi Hai Ummat-e-Khair-Al-Wara.
Our Aahein Bhar Rahi Hain Dard Se Khair-Al-Nisa.
Beh Raha Hai Aik Janib Hazrat-e-Mohsin Ka Khoon.
Our Sholay Uth Rahe Hain Our Fizza Ki Boka.
Ho Gaya Pehlu Shikasta Ya Elahi Khair Kar.
Khon Mein Dobi Hain Zahra Ya Elahi Khair Kar.

2
Aisa Lagta Hai Keh Mohsin Ki Shahadat Ho Gayi.
Zakhm Hai Pehlu Pe Gehra Ya Elahi Khair Kar.
Pasliyan Daein Se Baein Fatima Ki Ho Gayin.
Ro Ke Yeh Kehti Hai Fizza Ya Elahi Khair Kar.
Aik Zalim Ne Kalai Fatima Ki Toor Di.
Yeh Bhi Hai Zainab Ko Sadma Ya Elahi Khair Kar.

3
Waqt-e-Aakhir Yeh Dua Hai Maa Ki Beti Ke Liye.
Yeh Nahi Ho Sar Bureda Ya Elahi Khair Kar.
Fatima Zahra Pukarein Toor Kar Band-e-Kafan.
Hai Mera Shabbir Pyasa Ya Elahi Khair Kar.
Ho Chuka Hai Yeh Sitam Bhi Fatima Ne Jab Kaha.
Kha Liya Main Ne Tamacha Ya Elahi Khair Kar.

4
Aik Din Turbat Se Uth Kar Fatima Kehne Lagin.
Ab Safar Hai Karbala Ka Ya Elahi Khair Kar.
Qabar Mein Bhi Keh Rahi Hain Fatima Khair-Al-Nisa.
Le Ke Ik Kurta, Amama Ya Elahi Khair Kar.
Noha Gar Kazim, Ali Hain Chin Gaya Yasir Fidak.
Haq Pe Hai Zahra Ke Qabza Ya Elahi Khair Kar.
