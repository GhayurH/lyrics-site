Syed e Sajjad Nay Meesum Kashmiri.

1
Sham Mein Woh Zakhm Khaye Syed-e-Sajjad Ne.
Khon Ke Aanso Bahaye Syed-e-Sajjad Ne.
Jo Sar-e-Bazar Na Mehram Nigahon Se Chaley.
Qalb Par Woh Teer Khaye Syed-e-Sajjad Ne.
Jab Kabhi Pocha Kissi Ne Khon Roney Ka Sabab.
Sham Ke Nohay Sunaye Syed-e-Sajjad Ne.

2
Maqtal-e-Karb-o-Bala Ke Sab Ghamo Se Hain Garaan.
Jo Alam Tanha Uthaye Syed-e-Sajjad Ne.
Taziyane Our Durray, Beriyaan Tauq-e-Sitam.
Gham Sahey Kya Kya Na Haye Syed-e-Sajjad Ne.
Ban Gaya Unn Ka Musalla Shah Ka Farsh-e-Azza.
Nohay Sajdoon Mein Sunaye Syed-e-Sajjad Ne.

3
Khoon Ke Aanso Our Nikley Dur Sakina Ke Agar.
Apni Aankhon Se Lagaye Syed-e-Sajjad Ne.
Karbala Ki Yaad Mein Har Dam Gham-e-Sheh Ke Charag.
Khoon Ke Ashkon Se Jalaye Syed-e-Sajjad Ne.
Hashar Mein Kuhram Barpa Ho Gaya:MEESUM,: Iram.
Daag-e-Ghairat Jab Dikhaye Syed-e-Sajjad Ne.
