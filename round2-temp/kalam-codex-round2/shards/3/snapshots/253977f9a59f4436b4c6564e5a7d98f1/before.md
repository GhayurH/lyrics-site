Zahran Bint-e-Asad Ka Lal Hao Jo.
Jiss Ka Batin Noor-e-Paigamber Hai Logo.

1
Mazhar-e-Rab Wo Abu Talib Ka Beta Ali Mola.
Janta Tha Hai Kahan Pe Gghar Ali Ka.
Par Jalana Tha Osse Munkir Ka Seena.
Ghoom Kar Kehta Raha Galiyon Mein Tara Ali Mola.

2
Baat Tu Achi Hai Sunne Mein Tumhari.
Maan Bhi Leta Main Lekin Aye Naseri.
Kibriya Ke Robro Hai Sar Basajdah Ali Mola.
Dosto Jahgra Nahi Tafheem Kar Lein.

3
Deein-e-Paigamber Ki Youn Taqseem Kar Lein.
Sari Dunya Hai Tumhari Our Hamara Ali Mola.
Rait Ki Dewaar Tu Sunte Hain Logo.
Neil Mein Pani Ki Dewarein Bhi Dekho.

4
Wo Assa Thame Howay Parhta Hai Mosa Ali Mola.
Rokna Chahe Bhi Ghar Hum Ko Zamana.
Dhond Hi Lete Hain Hum Koi Bahana.
Har Mawali Ka Yehi Akbar Hai Narah Ali Mola.
