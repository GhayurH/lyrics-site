1
Sarkar Ali Mola Mir Hasan Mir Lyrics
Hain Sari Khudai Ke Mukhtar Ali Maula.
Haider Ko Kahan Chor Ke Tum Jao Gay Bolo.
Har Ja Nazar Aaein Gay Zarra Aankh Tu Kholo.

2
Iss Paar Ali Maula Oss Paar Ali Maula.
Khaybar Mein Ajab Waqt Hai Islam Ke Opar.
Islam Ke Lashkar Se Pareshaan Hain Payamber.
Kuffar Pe Ho Jaye Yalgar Ali Maula.

3
Jis Waqt Chalein Bint-e-Asad Kabay Ki Janib.
Our Qadar-e-Mutliq Se Hoin Aisay Mokhatib.
Hansne Lagi Kabay Ki Dewar Ali Maula.
Hasnain Ka Sadqa Mujhe Ijaz Atta Kar.

4
Abbas Ka Sadqa Mujhe Andaz Atta Kar.
De Mujh Ko Bhi Meesum Ki Guftar Ali Maula.
Maula Gham-e-Shabeer Ki Jageer Atta Kar.
Maula Mujhe Hur Jaisi Taqdeer Atta Kar.

5
Main Hoon Tere Betay Ka Ghamkhuwar Ali Maula.
Hai Aarzu Meri Mujhe Rozay Pe Bola Lein.
Ik Bar Nahi Maula So Bar Bola Lein.
Yeh Iltija Karta Hai Karrar Ali Maula.
