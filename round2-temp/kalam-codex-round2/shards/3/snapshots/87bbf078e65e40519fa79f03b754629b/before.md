1
Zawaron Ke Youn Naaz Uthate Hain Farishte.
Palkon Ko Sar-e-Rah Bichate Hain Farishte.
Yeh Dar Kabhi Khudam Se Khali Nahi Rehta.
Aate Hain Farishte Kabhi Jate Hain Farishte.

2
Taqdeer Ko Jab Tak Dar-e-Sarwar Nahi Milta.
Insan Tu Kya Thokarein Khate Hain Farishte.
Waise Inhein Hajat Tu Nahi Naan-o-Namak Ki.
Iss Dar Ka Tabaruk Ho Tu Khate Hain Farishte.

3
Jab Dekhta Hoon Farsh-e-Azza Deeda-e-Dil Se.
Insaan Se Zyda Nazar Atte Hain Farishte.
Yeh Hazrat-e-Abbas Ka Roza Tu Nahi Hai.
Sheh Par Jo Sametay Hoye Aate Hain Farishte.

4
Abbas-e-Dilawar Ka Alam Choom Raha Hoon.
Dekho Mera Munh Choomne Aate Hain Farishte.
Parhte Hain Bilal Aap Jo Mula Ka Qasida
Sunne Ke Liye Arsh Se Aate Hain Farishte.
