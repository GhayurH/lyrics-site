1
Ya Ali Mere Zakhmi Pehlu Ko Zainab Se Chupana Sonu Monu.
Jab Mujh Ko Kafan Apne Hathoon Se Pinhana.
Jis Ne Mera Haq Chena Jis Ne Mujhe Rulwaya.
Dar Mera Jalaya Hai Jis Ne Mujhe Tarpaya.
Maiyyat Pe Meri Hargiz Oss Ko Na Bolana.
Aawaz-e-Fogaan Gonjay Jo Khana e Ismat Mein.
Our Band-e-Kafan Totein Betay Ki Muhabbat Mein.
Nazdeek Marey Oss Dam Shabar Ko Na Lana.
Dekha Jo Tumhein Rotay Gham Unn Ka Siwa Hoga.
Aye Shair-e-Khuda Tum Se Kehti Hai Yehi Zahra.
Bas Samne Bachoon Ke Aanso Na Bahana.
Marqad Mein Akela Hai Bin Maa Ka Mera Bacha.
Ahsas Rahe Oss Ko Nazdeek Hi Hain Baba.
Turbat Marey Mohsin Ki Ghar Mein Hi Banana.
Phir Roh-e-Muhammad Ko Marqad Mein Na Tarpaein.
Pamal Ossay Karne Woh Loag Na Aa Jaein.
Turbat Ka Nishaan Mera Dunya Se Chupana.
