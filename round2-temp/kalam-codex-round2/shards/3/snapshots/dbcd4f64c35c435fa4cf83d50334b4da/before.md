1
Sultan Raza Mola Ali Raza Karbalai
Momin Hai Woh Hai Jis Ka Eman Raza Maula.
Konain Ke Ho Tum Bhi Sultan Raza Maula.
Tu Shah-e-Do Aalam Hai Har So Teri Shahi Hai.
Har Subha Zamane Mein Deti Yeh Gawahi Hai.
Kar Kar Ke Teri Nobat Ayelan Raza Maula.
Meesum Wahin Janat Se Mimber Bhi Utha Laye.
Sath Ahl-e-Falak Sunney Sab Teri Sana Aaye.
Khola Jo Tere Dar Par Quran Raza Maula.
Jo Qum Mein Karamat Hai Yeh Sari Tumhari Hai.
Our Ilm Elahi Ka Jo Faiz Yeh Jari Hai.
Yeh Hai Tere Qadmo Ka Faizan Raza Maula.
Be Jan Hoye Yakdam Zindoon Ki Yeh Halat Thi.
Aankhon Ke Isharay Se Hi Aap Ne Jab Dali.
Tasveer Ke Sheron Mein Thi Jan Raza Maula.
Yousuf Teri Turbat Se Liptay Nazar Aate Hain.
Kahin Khak-e-Shifa Esa Tere Dar Ki Uthatay Hain.
Aisi Tere Rozay Ki Hai Shan Raza Maula.
Ik Yeh Hi Nahi Keh Tum Bas Zamin-e-Aaho Ho.
Qudrat Hai Tumhari Keh Tumhein Jab Bhi Pukaro Tu.
Ho Jati Hai Har Mushkil Aasan Raza Maula.
La As Aa Lo Kum Parh Kar Jo Haq Tera Jane Ga.
Wal Asar Ka Narah Hai Gar Haq Tera Manay Ga.
Na Ho Ga Khasaray Mein Insan Raza Maula.
Jo Toos Ki Wadi Mein Yeh Toor Ka Jalwa Hai.
Phaila Howa Har Janib Jo Noor Tumhara Hai.
Hai Noor-e-Elahi Ki Pehchan Raza Maula.
Lehjay Se Tere Lehja ALLAH Ka Ayyaan Hai.
Quraan Ki Qasam Tu Bhi Tu Bolta Quraan Hai.
Aayat Hi Lagay Tera Farman Raza Maula.
Aalam Mein Nahi Hoga Oss Ka Koi Hum Pala.
Kehte Hain Tere Zair Oss Ko Hi Man-o-Salwa.
Khaya Jo Tere Dar Ka Pakwan Raza Maula.
Jis Ke Liye Koshish Mein Sare Lagay Rehtey Hain.
Pakeeza Ilaqa Wo, Janat Jisay Kehte Hain.
Woh Hai Tere Rozay Ka Dalan Raza Maula.
Sab Tu Ne Hi Bakhsha Hai Kya Our Bhala Mangay.
Aasi Hai Asad Phir Bhi Yeh Tujh Se Dua Mangay.
Banna Hai Tere Dar Ka Darban Raza Maula.
