1
Sahib Ul Asr Mukhtar Hussain Fatehpuri.
Sahib Ul-Asr Se Juda Na Howa.
Youn Khasara Kabhi Mera Na Howa.
Unn Ko Mehsos Tu Kiya Main Ne.
Sirf Aankhon Ko Tajurba Na Howa.
Shab-e-Aagaz-e-Ghaibat-e-Kubra.
Chand Bhi Aaj Pur Zia Na Howa.
Jo Hain Tere Hazoor Se Mehroom.
Gaib Se Oss Ko Faida Na Howa.
Oss Ki Midhat Biyan Kya Ho Gi.
Jis Ke Namo Ka Tarjuma Na Howa.
Mustaqil Hain Nigahein Kabay Par.
Mera Tabdeel Zaviya Na Howa.
Main Utha Doon Ga Pardah-e-Mairaj.
Kya Karey Ga Agar Khuda Na Howa?.
Zindagi Bhar Likha Takallum Ne.
Haq-e-Midhat Magar Ada Na Howa.
