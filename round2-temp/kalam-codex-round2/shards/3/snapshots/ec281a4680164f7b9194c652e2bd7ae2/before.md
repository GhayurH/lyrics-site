1
Waqt Ye Aya Muslim Zaidi Brothers
Tujh Pe Pardais Mein Kya Waqt Yeh Aaya Muslim.
Haye Sab Chor Gaye Reh Gaye Tanha Muslim.
Tujh Se Larta Koi Aa Kar Yeh Thi Jurat Kis Mein.

2
Ik Ghara Khod Ke Poshida Kiya Phir Oss Mein.
Tujh Ko Dhokay Se Laeeno Ne Giraya Muslim.
Pani Darbar Mein Jab Paya Tu Yaad Aaye Hussain.
Khon Pani Mein Jo Dekha Tu Kaha Haye Hussain.

3
Bond Pani Na Piya Mar Gaya Pyasa Muslim.
Ashk Ghurbat Bhi Bahati Thi Teri Ghurbat Par.
Fatima Zahra Ne Aagosh Mein Thama Aa Kar.
Jab Amarat Se Gira Tha Tera Lasha Muslim.

4
Woh Jo Ro Ro Ke Yeh Kehte Hain Ke Baba Aao.
Ab Yateemi Ki Khabar Le Ke Hawao Jao.
Ja Ke Bachoon Se Kaho Mar Gaye Baba Muslim.
Assalam Aye Mere Aaqa Yeh Sada Jab Aayi.

5
Bolay Abbas Se Shabeer Woh Dekho Bhai.
Kitna Mazloom Hai Kofay Mein Hamara Muslim.
Sar Dar-e-Kofa Pe Rasi Se Bandha Hai Tera.
Haye Kya Haal Yeh Ummat Ne Kiya Hai Tera.

6
Jism Bazar Mein Khaincha Gaya Tera Muslim.
Kaisi Ghurbat Hai Qayamat Hai Na Dil Phat Jaye.
Kaisay Zeeshan Likhe Tere Musayab Haye.
Gham Mein Rota Hai Tere Sara Zamana Muslim.
