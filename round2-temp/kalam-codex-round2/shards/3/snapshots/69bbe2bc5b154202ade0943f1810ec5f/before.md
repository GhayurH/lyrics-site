Sab Sy Bara Hussain Raza Hassan Sadiq.

1
Hai Bandagi Ke Dais Mein Sab Se Bara Hussain.
Karta Raha Khuda Ke Liye Kya Se Kya Hussain.
Zikar-e-Ali Karey Hai Woh Toor-e-Sannan Par.
Mushkil Kusha-e-Deen Ka Mushkil Kusha Hussain.
Ik Bar Apna Aap Tu Unn Ke Sapurd Kar.
Phir Dekh Tere Wastay Kartay Hain Kya Hussain.

2
Khaliq Se Ishq Dekh Muhammad Ke Lal Ka.
Naizay Ki Nook Par Bhi Hai Midhat Sara Hussain.
Labaik Loag Kehte Hain Parwardigar Ko.
Parwardigar Kehta Hai Labaik Ya Hussain.
 Shaukat Ke Bas Mein Kab Teri Tauseef Ka Hunar.
Na Cheez Kiya Karey Teri Hamd-o-Sana Hussain.
