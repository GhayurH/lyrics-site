Yeh Waqt Aa Gaya Baba Tere Madine Mein.
Batool Ho Gayi Tanha Tere Madine Mein.

1
Hain Teri Beti Ko Rone Pe Youn Dilasay Mile.
Safaidi Aa Gayi Balon Mein Waqt Se Pehle.
Batool Ab Hai Zaeefa Tere Madine Mein.
Hasan Ke Samne Yeh Bhi Sitam Howa Mujh Par.

2
Rahe Ga Oss Ka Nishaan Har Batool Ke Rukh Par.
Laga Jo Mujh Ko Tamacha Tere Madine Mein.
Mujhe Qasam Mere Ghazi Ke Pak Hathon Ki.
Main Daein Hath Se Tasbeeh Parh Nahi Sakti.

3
Kuch Aisa Ajar Hai Paya Tere Madine Mein.
Gira Hai Mujh Pe Musaib Ka Aasmaan Baba.
Mera Adab Meri Tazeem Thi Jahan Baba.
Nahi Hai Ab Woh Madina Tere Madine Mein.

4
Jo Dar Se Uthay WOh Sholay Na Bhool Paon Gi.
Hai Taziyano Se Taigon Se Thokaron Se Kabhi.
Mujhe Diya Gaya Pursa Tere Madine Mein.
Jiyon Main Kaise Ali Ki Zabaan Se Yeh Sun Kar.

5
Salam Dena Kuja Ab Salam Karne Par.
Jawab Bhi Nahi Milta Tere Madine Mein.
Hai Jis Ki Taig Ke Sadqay Yeh Deen-e-Haq Phaila.
Yeh Gham Bohat Hai Meri Maut Ke Liye Baba.

6
Ossi Ko Qaidi Banaya Tere Madine Mein.
Lahad Pe Baba Ki Kehti Thin Syeda Muhsin.
Jahan Mein Aane Se Pehle Chala Gaya Mohsin.
Youn Teri Beti Ko Mara Tere Madine Mein.
