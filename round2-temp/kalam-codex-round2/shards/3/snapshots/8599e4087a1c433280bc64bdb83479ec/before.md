1
Sughra Roz Tera Jhola Veer Jholaway Asghar Khan.
Hunr Aa Ja Chotiyaan Waliya Bhain Teri Haye Ro Ro Na Mar Jaway.
Wich Khuwabaan De Jadon Loriyan Dewaan Tainu.
Tin Nokaan Wala Ik Teer Daraway Mainu.

2
Oss Teer De Barey Soch Ke Rataan Nu Ujari Da Jee Ghabraway.
Rakh Ke Jholay De Wich Tere Main Cholay Kalay.
Phul Sukhna De Uttay Paye Te Deway Balay.
Tere Soan Di Thaan Nu Veerna Chum Chum Ke Athro Payi Bhain Bahaway.

3
Galaan Mithiyan Naal Babay De Tu Karda Ho Sain.
Sunr Ke Chithiyaan Nu Hawaan V Tu Bharda Ho Sain.
Dil Chaonda Aye Wichri Bhain Da Rahwaan Te Tainu Turna Aap Sikhaway.
Samne Akhiyaan De Jad Aaway Te Dar Jawaan Main.

4
Wekh Ke Manzar Oh Rowaan Te Kurlawan Main.
Jadon Baba Athro Roar Ke Karbal Wich Tere Te Mitiyaan Paway.
Naal Veraan De Kadi Painr Na Wichoray Loko.
Dukh Bhaina De Layi Honday Na Thoray Loko.

5
Sughra Da Sadqa Bhaina De Dunya Te Rab Nikhray Veer Milaway.
Bhar Bhar Mashkaan Samar Sare Le Aao Panri.
Chotiyaan Balaan Nu Ro Ro Ke Piyao Panri.
Asghar Da Koi Matami Pursay Choon Na Pyasa Ghar Nu Jaway.
