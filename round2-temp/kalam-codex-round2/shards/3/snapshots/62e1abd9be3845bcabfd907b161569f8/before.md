Yaaa Hussain, Yaaaaaaaa Hussain, Yaaa Hussain, Ya Hussain

1
Tumhe Hussain, zamana salaam, kehta hai
Tumharay sajday ko, qaabaa salaam, kehtaa hai
Arab ke gumbajay, khijra salaam, kehta hai
Lahoo mein doob kay, sehra salaam, kehta hai
Tumhari pyaas ko, dariyaaa salam, kehta hai - H
Tumhe Hussain, zamana salaam, kehta hai - H

2
Sehar ke doobtay, taaray salaam, kehtay hai
Ubalti nehar kay, dhaaray salaam, kehtay hai
Khalilay haq kay, isharay salaam, kehtay hai
Kaalamay paak kay, paray salaam, kehtay hai
Jaabinay dehar ka, sajdaa salaam, kehta hai - H
Tumhe Hussain, zamana salaam, kehta hai - H

3
Haqiqaton ki, tajalli ubhaar, ne walay
Lahoo say din-e,-khuda ko sawaar, ne walay
Dilo mein jalwaye, vehdat utaar, ne walay
Gala kata kay, khooda ko pukar, ne walay
W oh dekho aarsha ka, pardaa salaam, kehta hai - H
Tumhe Hussain, zamana salaam, kehta hai - H

4
Har ek ko kho diyaa, aur haq ko paa, liya tumnayyy
Jawan betay ka, laasha utha, liya tumnay
Wafa-o-sabra ka, jaada bana, liya tumnay
Zaeef qalba ka, zor aazma, liya tumnay
Lahoo bhara hua, naizaa salaam, kehta hai - H
Tumhe Hussain, zamana salaam, kehta hai - H

5
Ajaal ki god say, akbar salaam, kehtay haiiii
Andheri kabra say, asghar salaam, kehtay haiii
Nabi rasool-o,-payambar salaam, kehtay haiii
Chiragh-o-masjido,-mimbaar salaam, kehtay haiii
Madinay walay, madina salaam, kehta haiii - H
Tumhe Hussain, zamana salaam, kehta hai - H

6
Chalay thay jaada-e, tasleem-o-razaa, kay liyayyy
Watan ko chod diyaa, arzay kar, bala ke liyayyy
Zaban-e-khushqa pay, chaalay uthay, duaa ke liyayyy
Hazaar thokarayyy, khayee thi magar, khuda kay liyayyy
Tumharay nana kaa, rauzaa salaam, kehta haiii - H
Tumhe Hussain, zamana salaam, kehta hai - H

7
Chamakti teghon ke, saaye mein go, diyon ka maqiiii
Dhadak rahay hai, qalajay dahal, rahi hai zameennnn
Paron ko apnay, samhalay hai jib, raeelay ameennnn
Na janay teer, galay say nikal, gaya kay nahinnnn
Kisi ka nanhaa saa, jhoola salaam, kehta hai - H
Tumhe Hussain, zamana salaam, kehta hai - H

8
Tumharay jaanay pay, maula bahoot, kamee hongiii
Bata kay jaao kay, kis tarha zin, daagi hongiii
Na dil mein noor na, ashkon mein taaz, gee hongiii
Na ab chiraghay, jalengi na ro, shni hongiiii
Tumhara taaziya, khaana salaam, kehta haiiii - H
Tumhe Hussain, zamana salaam, kehta hai - H
