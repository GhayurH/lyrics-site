Yeh Ghar Zahra Ka Hai Rajab Ali Khan.

1
Jahan Kaba Bhi Sajday Mein Jhuka Hai Yeh Ghar Zahra Ka Hai.
Nizam-e-Rab Yahin Se Chal Raha Hai Yeh Ghar Zahra Ka Hai.
Makeen Iss Ghar Ke Jab Jab Boltay Hain Wohi Alfaz Ban Jate Hain Aayat.
Yahan Quran Lehja Seekhta Hai Yeh Ghar Zahra Ka Hai.
Namaz-e-Fajar Se Pehle Jo Aa Kar Salam Iss Ghar Ke Dar Pe Kar Rahe Hain.
Yeh Peygamber Ka Bhi Qibla Numa Hai Yeh Ghar Zahra Ka Hai.

2
Chala Jata Hai Arsh-e-Kibriya Tak Magar Jibreel Ko Dekha Gaya Hai.
Yahan Aa Kar Ijazat Mangta Hai Yeh Ghar Zahra Ka Hai.
Farishtay Roz Aa Kar Pochtay Hain Keh Hona Kya Hai Ab Dono Jahan Ka.
Yahan Ke Aaj Se Kal Ban Raha Hai Yeh Ghar Zahra Ka Hai.
Yahan Ke Jo Makeen Hain Unn Ka Shajrah Falak Walay Bakhobi Jantay Hain.
 Raza Yeh Khana e Aal-e-Khuda Hai Yeh Ghar Zahra Ka Hai.
