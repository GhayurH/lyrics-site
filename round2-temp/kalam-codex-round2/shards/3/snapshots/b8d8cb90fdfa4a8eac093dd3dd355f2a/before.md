1
Wadh Gayi Aye Rat Akhiyaan Di Tak Bheer Bazari.
Kadi Chadraan Kadi Phophiyaan De Nangay Sir Riya Takda Muhari.
Ghazi Da Nam Le Ke Abid Ne Wain Kitay Aa Wekh Chacha Ghazi.
Aina Shamiyaan Tere Wangon E Mehmal Toon Teri Sain Utari.

2
Sunda Riya Azanaa Parhda Riya Namazaan Ya Kiti Pursadari.
Kadi Kandiyaan Kadi Pathraan Te Abid Ne Kiti Sajda Guzari.
Sajjad Tauq Pa Ke Jad Bhain Nu Uthaya Maiyyat Ne Wain Paya.
Meri Lash Aye Mera Chan Veer Nayi Honi Tere Tauq Toon Bhari.

3
Akhiyaan Da Khon Tak Ke Razi Na Shami Hoye Pathar Ray Wasanday.
Odhay Jism Toon Odhay Zakhma Toon Jad Taein Hoya Khon Na Jari.
Nanay De Mondhiyaan Te Jine Wekhiya Si Yasrab Awein Sham Aaya Akbar.
Riya Wekhda Ohhi Naizay Te Aye Nagri Haye Sham Di Sari.
