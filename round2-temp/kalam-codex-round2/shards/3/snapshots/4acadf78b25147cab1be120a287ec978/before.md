Zikr-e-Abbas Karon Kyun Na Azadaron Mein
Baat Hoti Hai Wafaon Ki Wafadaron Mein
Ho Na Ho Yeh Mah-e-Shaban Ki Chothi Shab Hai
Aaj Soraj Ki Zia Dekhi Gayi Taron Mein
Aaj Bhi Jaise Hain Ghazi Ki Hifazat Mein Hussain
Taziye Le Ke Alam Chalte Hain Bazaron Mein
Mustaqil Kehti Chali Aati Hai Tareekh-e-Jehad
Koi Abbas Na Ab Ho Ga Alamdaron Mein
Aks Mah-e-Bani Hashim Ka Baha Kar Le Jaein
Itna Pani Hi Nahi Sham Ki Talwaron Mein
Maut Bhi Aa Ke Palat Jaye Yeh Dawa Hai Mera
Hazari Bant Ke Dekho Kabhi Bemaron Mein
Jis Tarha Khailte Hain Seene Pe Matam Ke Nishaan
Iss Tarha Phool Bhi Khilte Nahi Gulzaron Mein
