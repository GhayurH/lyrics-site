1
Veer Way Tera Sajda Wekhan Aa Gayi Aan.
Tere Gal Te Chaldiyan Aye Zarbaan Mainu Umm Al-Mosayab Bana Gayiyaan.
Tainu Teraan Zeen Churai Main Chadar Sir Te Payi.
Tere Hal Min Diyaan Aye Hawaan Mainu Khaima Veer Chura Gayiyaan.

2
Tera Maqtal Wich Khalonra Aye Wela Nayi Si Aounra.
Haye Banwaan Ghazi Veer Diyaan Mere Sare Manr Muka Gayiyaan.
Tera Okha Sajdah Denra Main Jag Nu Polan Nayi Denra.
Tainu Zindah Rakhan Layi Amari V Main Qabar Nu Chad Ke Aa Gayi Aan.

3
Tera Maqtal Aaounr Toli Bazar Ich Akhiyaan Kholi.
Tainu Khanjar De Heth Main Wekhan Da Main Kiwein Farz Nibha Gayiyaan.
Meri Sain Da Aye Ahsan Aye Nalay Jaun Da Aye Eman Aye.
Meri Sain De Sabar Diyaan Kariyaan Nanay Da Deen Bacha Gayiyaan.
