1
Tapakta Hai Khoon Murtaza Ki Jabeen Se Syed Mudassir Mehdi
Koi Laye Marham Khudara Kahin Se.
Zabaan Be Zabno Ne Kholi Na Jao.
Aye Maula Youn Hum Se Na Daman Churao.
Patakhti Hain Murgabiyaan Sar Zameen Se.
Padar Bistar-e-Marg Pe Ro Raha Hai.
Tasawar Mein Zainab Ka Sar Jo Khula Hai.
Lahu Saaf Kartin Hain Zainab Jabeen Se.
Lahu Aansoon Mein Mila Ponchtay Hain.
Ali Halq-e-Shabeer Ko Chomtay Hain.
Chalaye Ga Haan Shimar Khanjar Yahin Se.
Yeh Boli Zameen Kofa-o-Karbala Ki.
Pisar Ki Nahi Hai Nahi Murtaza Ki.
Bacha Deen-e-Islam Teri Nahi Se.
Tapaktay Hain Aanso Nigahein Jhuki Hain.
Behan Bhai Ko Hosla De Rahi Hain.
Utho Mere Abbas Bhai Zameen Se.
Yeh Beti Se Rukhsat Ki Kaisi Ghari Hai.
Rida Baap Ke Khon Se Tar Hoi Hai.
Dikhai Gayi Karbala Bhi Yahin Se.
Ali Par Aye Bibi Yeh Ahsan Karna.
Pisar Sare Sarwar Pe Qurban Karna.
Liya Ehd Maula Ne Umm Ul-Baneen Se.
Padar Se Judai Ke Aasaar Dekho.
Nazar Bhar Ke Ab Aakhri Bar Dekho.
Bicharne Ki Raat Aa Gayi Shah-e-Dein Se.
Bhiyanak Hai Tareekh Haider Duhai.
Zamane Mein Khushiyon Bhari Eid Aayi.
Hai Sadaat Mein Soag Uneeswin Se.
