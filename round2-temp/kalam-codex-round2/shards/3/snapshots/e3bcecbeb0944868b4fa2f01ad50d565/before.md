Roti Rahi Hai Khon Imamat Kahan Kahan
Sajjad Tujh Ko Le Gayi Ghurbat Kahan Kahan
Sadaat Ke Bandhay Hoye Bazu Bataein Gay
Parwardigar Sabar Ke Aanso Bataein Gay
Abbas Ki Pari Hai Zarorat Kahan Kahan
Rodad Bekasi Ki Ro Kar Sunaein Gay
Zainab Se Pocho Sham Ke Rastay Bataein Gay
Abid Ke Dil Pe Guzri Qayamat Kahan Kahan
Pathar Ki Shakal Mein Hoi Barsat Khon Ki
Rahoon Mein Sham-o-Kofa Ke Ummat Rasool Ki
Deti Rahi Hai Ajar-e-Risalat Kahan Kahan
Paigham-e-Kirdgar Sunanay Ke Wastay
Deen-e-Rasool-e-Pak Bachaney Ke Wastay
Sajjad Ne Chukayi Qeemat Kahan Kahan
Parday Ka Bachpaney Se Hi Jis Ko Khiyal Tha
Ghar Mein Na Dekha Jis Ne Kabhi Maa Ko Be Rida
Haye Hoi Hai Qatal Woh Ghairat Kahan Kahan
Pur Khar Raston Se TAKALLUM Barehna Pa
Paidal Safar Howa Mere Bekas Imam Ka
Gir Gir Pari Safar Mein Musafat Kahan Kahan
