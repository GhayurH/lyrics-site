1
Zaat e Rab Zaat e Muhammad Mehdi Rizvi.
Youn Hoi Hai Zaat-e-Rab Zaat-e-Muhammad Se Ayyaan.
Nam-e-Ahmad Mein Chupi Hai Hamd-e-Rab-e-Do Jahan.
Kyun Taqabul Kar Raha Hai Aal Ka Ashab Se.
Bantne Walay Kahan Our Mangney Walay Kahan.
Har Ghari Qayam Hai Mujh Par Bint-e-Sarwar Ka Karam.
Kar Rahi Hai Zikar-e-Ahmad Har Ghari Meri Zabaan.
Wird Maula Kartay Hain Sallay Ala Ya Mustafa.
Jab Kabhi Shabbir Dekhein Chehra e Akbar Jawaan.
Iss Ke Sadqay Se Lehad Mein Roshni Ho Jaye Gi.
Matam-e-Akbar Ka Seene Par Saja Hai Jo Nishaan.
Youn Biyaan Sarkar Ne Mairaj Ka Qissa Kiya.
Sab Farishton Ke Labon Par Zikar-e-Haider Tha Wahan.
Jis Ke Sadqay Mein Howa Hai Khalq Yeh Aalam Tamam.
Kis Tarha Se Shan Qambar Ho Bhala Oss Ki Biyaan.
