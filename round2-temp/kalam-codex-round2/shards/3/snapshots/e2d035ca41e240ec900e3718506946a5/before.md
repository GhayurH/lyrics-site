Taqwa Ki Barhi Shan Mere Mola Taqi Se.
Khali They Jo Daman Bharay Maula Taqi Se.

1
Maula Ke Tabasum Se Hoi Arsh Pe Jhil Mil.
Jhurmat Hain Sitaron Ke Juray Maula Taqi Se.
Iss Dar Pe Chaley Aate Hain Abdal-o-Qalandar.
Mangein Wali Har Aan Kharey Maula Taqi Se.

2
Nadaar Hoye Sahib-e-Iqdar Jahan Mein.
Gumnam Bhi Zeeshan Bane Maula Taqi Se.
Tauseef Ki Taufeeq Bhi Inn Hi Ki Atta Hai.
Our Dolat-e-Irfan Miley Maula Taqi Se.

3
Miltay Hain Woh Tareekh Mein Farman Ki Sorat.
Alfaz Ke Jo Phool Jharay Maula Taqi Se.
 Hasnain Jalaye Gi Unhein Nar-e-Jahanum.
Jo Jo Bhi Jalay Jal Ke Maray Maula Taqi Se.
