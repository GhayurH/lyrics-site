1
Watu Izzo Mantasha Mir Hasan Mir Lyrics
Dunya Ali Ki Shan Ghataye Gi Kis Tarha.
Jab Keh Raha Hai Khaliq-e-Akbar Quran Mein.
Watu Izzu Man Tasha Watu Zillu Man Tasha.

2
Dein Ka Alam Rasool Ne Haider Ko De Diya.
Majma Sahabiyon Ka Khara Dekhta Raha.
Oss Waqt Hans Ke Hazrat-e-Jibreel Ne Kaha.
Maula Bana Jo Fatah-e-Khaybar Ghadeer Mein.

3
Ahl-e-Hawas Ko Aa Gaye Chakar Ghadeer Mein.
Kehne Lagay Yeh Jhoom Ke Qanbar Ghadeer Mein.
Sardar-e-Anbiya Ki Iyadat Ke Wastay.
Jo Ahl-e-Bait Khas They Chadar Mein Aa Gaye.

4
Yeh Fiqray Ban Ke Aaya-e-Tatheer Ne Kahey.
Dozakh Mein Jate Jate Woh Janat Mein Aa Gaya.
Ab Oss Ke Sar Ka Taj Hai Romal-e-Syeda.
Hur Ka Naseeb Aaj Bhi Deta Hai Yeh Sada.

5
Pori Hoin Na Hasratein Ashab-e-Mohtaram.
Bekar Rat Jagay Hoye ALLAH Ki Qasam.
Dast-e-Khuda Mein Aa Ke Yeh Kehne Laga Alam.
Darbar Mein Jab Aaya Aseeron Ka Qafila.

6
Sara Garor Zulm Ka Mati Mein Mil Gaya.
Mimber Pe Aa Ke Syed-e-Sajjad Ne Kaha.
Bacha Samajh Raha Tha Jo Asghar Ko Hurmala.
Khud Oss Ka Teer Oss Ke Liye Maut Ban Gaya.

7
Asghar Ko Hansta Dekh Ke Kehne Lagi Qaza.
Pathar Choya Tu Woh Gul-e- Nayab Ho Gaya.
Dekha Jo Khar Tu Gul-e-Shadab Ho Gaya.
Gar Main Ne Jab Ali Kaha Mahtab Ho Gaya.
