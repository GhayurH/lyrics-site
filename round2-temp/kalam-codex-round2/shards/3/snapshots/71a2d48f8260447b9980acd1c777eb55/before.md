Sajjad Takay Arshaan Wal Sham Walay Kinj Jawaan Main.
Phophiyaan Layi Chadar Di Ya Rab Hun Ki Tarteeb Banwaan Main.

1
Sadhay Ghar Diyaan Nuhaan Dhiyaan De Gal Rasiyaan Naal Paroye Ne.
Utton Zalim Aakhday Phophiyaan Nu Bazar Da Safar Karawaan Main.
Rukhsar Sakina De Zakhmi Te Hath Mere Zanjeeraan Wich.
Marham Layi Aye Woh Mumkin Nayi Zakhmaan Te Mitiyaan Lawaan Main.

2
Arshaan Wal Jis Dam Taknaa Waan Sanghaan Te Chadraan Disdiyaan Ne.

Toon Khaliq Ayin Toon Janda Ayin Sajjad Te Sajday Karda Si.
