1
Watna Te Mur Aa Ja Akbar Khat Ujari Da Aaya Aye.
Yasrab Sunjha Kar Ke Veran Kehra Dais Wasaya Aye.
Main Nanay De Rozay Uttay Deway Balay Mantaan De.
Ghar De Bohay Te Aasaan Da Veer Charag Jalaya Aye.

2
Wapis Aanday Mehmal Di Aawazaan Nu Main Taras Gayi.
Roz Udeekaan Koi Aakhay Sughra Akbar Aaya Aye.
Main Khabaan Wich Chachay Ghazi De Hath Khak Te Wekhay Ne.
Jehray Hathaan De Nal Chachay Tainu Veer Khadaya Aye.

3
Khuwabaan De Wich Rab Jane Kis Shehar Da Safar Main Kar Aayi.
Ainj Lagda Aye Qaidi Kar Ke Lokaan Shehar Phiraya Aye.
 Akbar Sughra Veer Akbar De Naam Tilawat Kar Di Rayi.
Akbar Akbar Kehndiyaan Bibi Aakhri Sans Mukaya Aye.
