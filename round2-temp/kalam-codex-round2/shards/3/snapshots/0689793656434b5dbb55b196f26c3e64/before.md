Zikar e Hussain Zikar-e-Khuda Aik Baat.
Iss Se Mila Ya Oss Ne Diya Aik Baat.
 Kashif Yeh Maan Kar Dar-e-Shabir Par Jhuka.
Rab Ki Atta Ya Iss Ki Atta Aik Baat Hai.
