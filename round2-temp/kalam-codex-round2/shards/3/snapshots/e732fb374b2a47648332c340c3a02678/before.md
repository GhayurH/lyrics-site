1
Taboot E Mola Sajjad Shehbaz Haideri.
Sajjad Mohari Ka, Taboot Yeh Utha Hai.
Gham-e-Chadar-e-Zainab Mein, Bara Khon Yeh Roya Hai.
Jis Maqtal Mein Be Goro Kafan, Haye Lash Pari Thi Baba Ki.
Oss Maqtal Se Janay, Yeh Kaisay Guzra Hai.
Katta Hai Jigar Haye Abid Ka, Mila Zehar Madine Mein Unn Ko.
Turbat Mein Sakina Ki, Ik Mehshar Barpa Hai.
Abid Ke Badan Par Neelay Nishaan, Our Zakhm Gawahi Detey Hain.
Zanjeer Se Zalimo Ne, Mazloom Ko Bandha Hai.
Khon Itna Behta Hai Ayeriyon Tak, Keh Surkh Zameen Ho Jati Hai.
Sajjad Ko Shamiyon Ne, Haye Kitna Mara Hai.
Hanstay They Laeen Our Martay They, Jab Ro Kar Abid Kehte They.
Na Maro Issay Logo, Masoom Sakina Hai.
Durron Se Kabhi Naizon Se Kabhi, Pathron Se Kabhi, Kabhi Thokaron Se.
Shabeer Ka Har Badla, Abid Ne Chukaya, Hai.
Zakhmi Tha Badan Rasta Tha Kathan, Our Sara Safar Tha Kanton Par.
Na Jane Kya Kaisay, Sajjad Ne Sajdah Hai.
Woh Majlis Thi Sheh Ke Gham Mein, Jisay Sun Kar Bibiyaan Roti Thin.
Darbar Mein Abid Ne, Diya Ro Ke Jo Khutba Hai.
Baba Ke Chacha Ke Bhaiyon Ke, Gham Ne Sajjad Ko Mar Diya.
Sajjad Youn Chalte Hain, Jaisay Koi Lasha Hai.
Roya Hai Lahu Chalees Bars, Ab Turbat Mein Bhi Rota Hai.
Sajjad Ka Har Ik Zakhm, Turbat Mein Bhi Taza Hai.
Baba Ke Jo Seene Pe Soti Thi, Zindan Mein Qabar Bani Oss Ki.
Kaisay Lasha Sakina Ka, Abid Ne Uthaya Hai.
Waqt-e-Aakhir Woh Abid Ke, Chehray Pe Likha Baqir Ne Parha.
Har Zulm Jo Abid Ne, Bazar Mein Dekha Hai.
Seena Akbar Ka Hai Kata Gaya, Jis Barchi Se Shehbaz-o- Asad.
Ossi Barchi Ne Haye Kata, Abid Ka Bhi Seena Hai.
