1
Sakina Ka Yeh Noha Syed Muhammad Raza Abidi.
Sakina Ka Yeh Noha Hai Mere Baba Ko Mat Maro.
Yeh Zahra Ka Dulara Hai Marey Baba Ko Mat Maro.
Kahan Paon Gi Yeh Seena Yeh Ab Bazu Kahan Hoonge.
Yateemi Ki Fiza Hogi Tamachon Ke Nishaan Hoonge.
Mera Dil Gham Se Phatta Hai Marey Baba Ko Mat Maro.
Zameen Par Zeen Se Aaya Jo Ahmad Ka Jigar Parah.
Sakina Ne Jo Dekha Baap Ko Tu Youn Kiya Giriya.
Issay Zahra Ne Pala Hai Marey Baba Ko Mat Maro.
Chaley Jo Ran Ko Shah-e-Karbala Kuhram Tha Barpa.
Sakina Ne Kaha Rahwar Se Baba Ko Mat Le Ja.
Gareebon Ka Sahara Hai Marey Baba Ko Mat Maro.
Nahi Ab Koi Khaimay Mein Siwaye Abid-e-Muztar.
Tumhein Deti Hai Rab Ka Wasta Shabeer Ki Dukhtar.
Bohat Mazloom Pyasa Hai Marey Baba Ko Mat Maro.
Abhi Maqtal Se Kandhoon Par Utha Laye Hain Akbar Ko.
Sula Kar Garam Reti Mein Abhi Aaye Hain Asghar Ko.
Ghamo Ne Aisay Ghaira Hai Marey Baba Ko Mat Maro.
Gira Jo Zeen Se Uzma Sakoon-e-Qalb-o-Jaan Zahra.
Faqat Ik Hi Sada Thi Jis Ne Bekas Ko Wahan Ghaira.
Mera Baba Akela Hai Marey Baba Ko Mat Maro.
