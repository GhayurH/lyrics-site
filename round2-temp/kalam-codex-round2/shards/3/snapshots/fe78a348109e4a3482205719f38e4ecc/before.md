1
Tamana Phir Machal Jaye Agar Tum Milne Aa Jao Jagjeet Singh.
Yeh Mosam Bhi Badal Jaye Agar Tum Milne Aa Jao.
Mujhe Gham Hai Keh Main Ne Zindagi Mein Kuch Nahi Paya.
Yeh Gham Dill Se Nikal Jaye Agar Tum Milne Aa Jao.

2
Yeh Dunya Bhar ke Jaghre Ghar Ke Qisay Kaam Ki Batein.
Bala Har Aik Tal Jaye Agar Tum Milne Aa Jao.
Nahi Milte Ho Mujh Se Tum Tu Sab Humdard Hain Mere.
Zamana Mujh Se Jal Jaye Agar Tum Milne Aa Jao.
