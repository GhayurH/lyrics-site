Ya Fatima Zahra, Ya Zahra, Ya Zahra, Ya Zhara, Ya Zahra.
Madar Do Jahan Fatima Jaan Dilbad-o-Basteem.
Muhiban-e-Do Hasteem Muhiban Do Basteem.
Aye Umm-e-Abeeha Aye Umm-e-Faqiha.
Aye Umm-e-Abeeha Bajuz Bageer Basteem.
Umeed-e-Shifat Zee Tu Aye Fatima Zahra.
Khatoon-e-Jinna Waris-e-Tatheer Sarapa.
Ashraf-e-Nisa Aks-e-Nabi Aks-e-Khudaija.
Meezan-e-Wafa Roh-e-Dua Fatima Zahra.
Uftad Dar-e-Khana Tera Pehlu Shikasta.
Ummat Ne Tera Haq Na Diya Fatima Zahra.
Mohsin Ki Shahadat Ka Sabab Ban Gayi Dunya.
Wirsa Hai Tera Ashk-e-Azza Fatima Zahra.
Aye Ashk Fishaan Bad-e-Nabi Bint-e-Payamber.
Tha Soag Mein Baba Ke Azakhana Tera Ghar.
Rotay They Tere Sath Ali Sarwar-o-Shabar.
Aye Khasta Jigar Mehway Boka Fatima Zahra.
Dar Karb-o-Bala Tashna Labi Tere Pisar Ki.
Taraji Hoi Kis Tarha Zahra Tere Ghar Ki.
Be Goor-o-Kafan Lash Rahi Noor-e-Nazar Ki.
Aalam Bakhuda Soag Mein Tha Fatima Zahra.
Bachoon Pe Tere Foaj-e-Sitamgar Ki Yalgar.
Zanjeer Bakaf Tauq-e-Garaan Abid-e-Bemar.
Darbar-e-Alam Qaid-e-Sitam Dukhtar-e-Ghamkhuwar.
Aada Ke Sitam Kunba Tera Fatima Zahra.
Aye:SARWAR-o-REHAN: Sar-e-Majlis-e-Shabeer.
Jab Fatima Hoti Hain Dil Afgar-o-Galugeer.
Manzar Woh Kahan Tak Koi Kar Sakta Hai Tehreer.
Rota Hai Qalam Likh Ke Sada Fatima Zahra.
