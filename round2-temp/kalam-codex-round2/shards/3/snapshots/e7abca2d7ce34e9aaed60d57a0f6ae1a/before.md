Shoor Sa Barpa Hai Kyun Medan Mein.
Kya Nabi Ne Keh Diya Ayelan Mein.

1
Mustafa Ke Baad Phir Ik Mustafa.
Bas Yehi Ayelan Tha Farman Mein.
Ho Chuka Sab Kuch Biyaan Balig Ke Sath.
Ab Jagah Baqi Nahi Quran Mein.

2
Aa Gaya Hathoon Mein Jab Haider Ka Hath.
Mustafa Ki Jan Aayi Jan Mein.
Seekh Li Ho Gi Kabhi Nad-e-Ali.
Nooh Youn Utra Nahi Tofan Mein.

3
Fatima Hasnain-o-Haider Mustafa.
Hamd Likhta Hoon Khuda Ki Shan Mein.
Garchay Woh Qanbar Nahi Nokar Tu Hai.
Kab Raha Akbar Bhala Nuqsan Mein.
