1
Syed-e-Sajjad Ne Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.
Syed-e-Sajjad Ne Kaisay Guzari Zindagi.
Khon Roye Karbala Ke Baad Sari Zindagi.
Zard Chehra Kar Diya Tha Karbala Ki Yaad Ne.

2
Surkh Kharey Bhi Nahi Pehne Kabhi Sajjad Ne.
Bas Aseeri Gham Rahi Ik Gham Ki Mari Zindagi.
Sham-o-Kofa Mein Hoye Kitne Ghamo Ke Samne.
Be Rida Jab Thi Phophi Na Mehramo Ke Samne.

3
Lamha Lamha Ho Gayi Abid Pe Bhari Zindagi.
Yaad Mein Be Sheer Ki Kehte They Ro Kar Har Ghari.
Yeh Tu Socha Bhi Na Tha Hum Ne Tasawar Mein Kabhi.
Itni Kam Hogi Ali Asghar Tumhari Zindagi.

4
Ab Khushi Ke Naqsh Iss Dil Mein Utar Saktay Nahi.
Karbala Mein Jo Miley Hain Zakhm Bhar Saktay Nahi.
Umar Bhar Karti Rahe Gi Aah-o-Zaari Zindagi.
Sath Ik Din Mein Azeezon Aqarba Ka Chut Gaya.

5
Oss Ke Dil Se Pochiye Jis Ka Bhara Ghar Lut Gaya.
Kaisay Katti Hai Gham-e-Furqat Ki Mari Zindagi.
Qabar Tak Ab Sath Le Jaein Gay Yeh Ranj-o-Mahan.
Be Kafan Baba Ke Lashay Ko Na De Paye Kafan.

6
Yeh Qalaq Abid Ko Tarpaye Ga Sari Zindagi.
 Akhtar-o- Sajjad Jab Darbar Ki Yaad Aa Gayi.
Chehra-e-Bemar-e-Gham Par Murdani Si Cha Gayi.
Sham Keh Keh Kar Tarapti Thi Bechari Zindagi.
