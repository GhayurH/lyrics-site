1
Koi Kazim Da Jay Howay Te Oh Rowan Aaye.
Assi Suniya Aye Kadi Qaidi Si Dada Aida.
Beriyaan Tauq Da Waqif Si Naam Abid Si Jida.
Aa Ke Zanjeer Aidi Lash Taun Abid Laye.

2
Veer Di Lash Te Jee Khol Ke Naa Roi Jehri.
Akhiyaan Meet Laiyaan Ne Odi Tazeem Layi.
Assi Bazaraan Che Wekhay Ne Guzarday Saye.
Qaid Khane Taun Aithay Aounda Hoye Muk Gaye Aan.

3
Ik Janazay Nu Uthaya Aye Te Bas Jhuk Gaye Aan.
Kiwein Shabeer Ne Karbal Che Bahatar Chaye.
Melay Cholay Da Kafan Jiwein Sakina Paya.
Ohwein Kazim De Badan Te V Aye Maila Chola.

4
Ho Sakay Aj Te Kafan Le Ke Sakina Aaye.
Chodah So Saal Guzar Gaye Ne Sitam Nu Akbar.
Chodah Salaan Di Aseeri Nayi Hoi Khatam Magar.
Aj V Baghdad Da Qaidi Aye Oda Naa Haye.
