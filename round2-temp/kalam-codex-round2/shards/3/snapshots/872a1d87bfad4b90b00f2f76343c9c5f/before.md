Shab Safar Mein Kat Rahi Thi Dil Mera Kehta Raha.
Jab Najaf Pohancha Tu Har Ik Samt Se Aayi Sada.

1
Bas Yeh Do Nuskhe Mareezon Ke Liye Akseer Hain.
Ik Najaf Ki Hai Fizza Our Dosri Khak-e-Shifa.
Khud Kaha Mula Ali Ne Qabar Mein Aaon Ga Main.
Iss Liye Sab Momino Ko Achi Lagti Hai Qaza.

2
Taig Jab Mukhtar Ki Chamki Banam-e-Karbala.
Zulfiqar-e-Haideri Ne Barh Ke Yeh Narah Kiya.
Janatein Karti Hain Sajda Meri Manzil Ki Taraf.
Yeh Jina Tu Aik Rasta Hai Baray-e-Karbala.

3
Har Marz Mein Syed-e-Sajjad Hi Ka Naam Lo.
Ik Yehi Bemar Hai Jo Sab Ko Deta Hai Shifa.
Jis Ko Dunya Ke Shifa Khane Ne Sehat De Sakein.
Hazrat-e-Abbas Ke Parcham Ki Oss KO Do Hawa.

4
Jis Ke Qadmoon Mein Hai Zam Zam Hath Mein Hai Zindagi.
Woh Mera Mula Raza Woh Mera Mula Raza.
Kya Wajhi Our Kya Sorat-e- Ali Akbar.
