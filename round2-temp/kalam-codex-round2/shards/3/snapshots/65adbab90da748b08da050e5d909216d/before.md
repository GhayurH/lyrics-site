1
Zahra Da Askari Samrey Wich Dukh Apne Aap Wandaway.
Piya Ghar Di Chaar Dwari Wich Kalla Apni Qabar Banaway.
Hum Naam Hasan Da Sochda Aye Ais Naam De Lok Ne Wairi.
Meri Maiyyat Te Daday Wangoon Ummat Na Teer Chalaway.

2
Yasrab Jo Dafan De Jhagrey Paye Oh Samrey Haal Na Howay.
Aithay Maa Zahra Da Baqiya Nayi Jithay Bhain Bhira Dafnaway.
Ghazi Jaye Veer Muhammad Ne Wich Bald Shahadat Payi.
Pardais Ich Koi Naal Nayi Jairha Aa Ke Sath Nibhaye.

3
Ik Bhain Aye Naal Musafir Di Tur Paye Na Naal Janazay.
Mazloom Di Aykho Hasrat Aye Maiyyat V Bar Na Jaway.
 Akbar Mehdi Di Imamat De Aagaz Da Haal Ajeeb Aye.
Panj Saal Da Putar Janaza Aj Babay Da Aap Parhaway.
