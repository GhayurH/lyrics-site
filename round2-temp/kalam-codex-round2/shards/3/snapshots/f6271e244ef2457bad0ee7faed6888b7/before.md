1
Tujhe Dekhna Hai Wozu Mera Tera Ishq Meri Namaz Hai.
Tu Hi Bakhsh Deta Hai Shahiyaan Tu Bara Ghareeb Nawaz Hai.
Tu Ibadaton Ka Sajod Hai Tu Shahadaton Ka Qiyam Hai.
Tu Wilayaton Ka Raku Bhi Hai Tu Hi LaIlaha Ka Raaz Hai.

2
Tu Nabi Ki Pusht Pe Ho Agar Tu Namaz-e-Haq Bhi Ruki Rahe.
Tu Hussain Hai Tu Hussain Hai Tu Namaz Ki Bhi Namaz Hai.
Tu Har Aik Cheez Se Be Niyaz Sifat-e-Khuda Bhi Hai Be Niyaz.
Tujhey Rab Kahon Ossay Rab Kahon Main Nahi Kahon Ga Yeh Raaz Hai.

3
Tu Nabi Ke Kandhey Pe Ho Agar Tu Khuda Ki Marzi Dikhai De.
Tere Hath Mein Do Jahan Hain Keh Nabi Ki Zulf-e-Daraz Hai.
Tu Malak Ko Bakhsh De Baal-o-Par Tu Kissi Ko Betay Atta Kare.
Tere Bachpaney Ki Zidoon Pe Bhi Parwardigar Ko Naaz Hai.

4
Tere Matami Tere Noha Gar Do Jahan Mein Sab Se Azeem Hain.
Tera Zikar Karna Nagar Nagar Tere Matami Ki Namaz Hai.
Tera Banda Majid E Khush Nawa Tere Dar Pe Sajdah Guzar Hai.
Ho Qaboliyat Ki Sand Atta Tu Bara Hi Banda Nawaz Hai.
