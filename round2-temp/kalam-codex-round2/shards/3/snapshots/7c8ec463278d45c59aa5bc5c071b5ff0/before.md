Ya Hussain

1
Yazid bola, bari foj mere hath me hay
Hussain bole, Hussain bole
Hussain bole natijaa, khuda ke hath me hay
Yazid bola, jo bayyat karo khazaana hay
Hussain. Hussain bole ke nana ko mu dikhaana hay
Yazid bola me zalim hoon, zulm karta hoon
Hussain, ya Hussain
Hussain bole me sabir hoon, sabr karta hoon

2
Wo khayme jal rahe hay, ye sham gharibaan hay
Ye ban me bikhri lashe,  Ab sham ka zindan hay
Wo khayme jal rahe hay, ye sham gharibaan hay
Ik do peher me ujra. Ghar ek fatema ka
Ye jis jagaa pe khayme,  Ab us jagaa dhuwan hay
Wo khayme jal rahe hay, ye sham gharibaan hay
Akbar ne jab azan di. Ye bekasi kahaa thi
Wo subho ki azan thi,  Ye sham ki azan hay
Wo khayme jal rahe hay, ye sham gharibaan hay
Besar koi namaazi. Be dast ko ilazi
Seenay me ik jawaa ke,  tuti huwi sinaa hay
Wo khayme jal rahe hay, ye sham gharibaan hay
Pehra badal gaya hay. Zainab ye karbala hay
Zainab ke hath me aa,  Abbas ka nishan hay
Wo khayme jal rahe hay, ye sham gharibaan hay
Kya dhund thi hay sinaa. Awaaz di o sakina
Seenay pe sone wali,  Baba tera yahaan hay
Wo khayme jal rahe hay, ye sham gharibaan hay
Ye ban me bikhri lashe,  Ab sham ka zindan hay
Wo khayme jal rahe hay, ye sham gharibaan hay
