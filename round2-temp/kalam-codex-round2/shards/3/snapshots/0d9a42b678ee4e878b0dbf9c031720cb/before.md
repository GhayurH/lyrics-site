Zakhmi Khuda Ke Ghar Mein Khuda Ka Wali Howa.
Yasrab Mein Sogowar Hamara Nabi Howa.

1
Logo Suno Hamara Woh Pehla Imam Hai.
Ismat Ke Silsilay Ki Jo Doji Kari Howa.
Jari Rahe Namaz Bhi Sail Ko De Zakat.
Dunya Mein Phir Na Koi Bhi Aisa Sakhi Howa.

2
Baqi Quran Parh Keh Khuda Se Sawab Lein.
Misal e Quran Sirf Nabi Ka Akhi Howa.
ALLAH Ka Aik Naam Ali Al-Azeem Hai.
Dekho Ali Ke Pyar Mein ALLAH Ali Howa.

3
Hasnain Ki Qasam Hai Keh Sare Jahan Mein.
Abbas Ke Padar Sa Na Koi Jari Howa.
Jab Se Tere Qadam Se Mila Hai Issay Sharf.
Tera Najaf Bhi Khuld-e-Barin Ki Ghali Howa.

4
Sar Pe Kiya Laeen Ne Aisa Sitam Ka Waar.
Apne Lahu Mein Tar Wahan Shair-e-Jali Howa.
Jis Ne Ali Ke Gham Mein Kiya Matam-e-Ali.
 Hasnain Woh Jahan Ke Gham Se Bari Howa.
