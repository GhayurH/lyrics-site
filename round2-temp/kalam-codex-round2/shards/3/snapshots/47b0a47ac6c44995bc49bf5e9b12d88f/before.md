1
Tur Gaya Aye Sajjad Dunya Toon Tur Gaya Aye.
Jis De Gal Wich Chali Saal Taein Ik Tauq Da Zakhm Riya Aye.
Aye Khas Taruf Abid Da Ainu Sham Da Qaidi Kehnday.
Ayedhiyaan Hawaan Da Naa Peshi Aye Hanjwaan Da Naam Rida Aye.

2
Waqif Humdard Te Sathi Ne Kuj Beriyaan Te Zanjeeraan.
Ayedhiyaan Sanjhaan Galiyaan Moraan Nal Qaidaan Wich Khas Jagah Aye.
Aaya Aye Sham Taun Akhiyaan Wich Tasveer Bazar Di Le Ke.
Wasday Pathraan Aisay Rang Bharay Har Manzar Surkh Hoya Aye.

3
Quran Di Aayat Safraan Wich Parhdi Aye Sari Dunya.
Aidhi Kand Te Likhda Shimar Riya Safraan Di Hoor Dua Aye.
Quran De Pak Mufasar Ne Tableeg Di Khatir Akbar.
Aayataan Parhday Babay Naal Kita Tableeg Da Safar Juda Aye.
