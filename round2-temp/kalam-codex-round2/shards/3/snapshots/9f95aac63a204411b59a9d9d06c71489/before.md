Sughra Da Khat Jad Aya Akber Day Sah Muk Gaye.
Shabbir Ainj Parh Ke Sunaya Akbar De Sah Muk Gaye.Qasida Ja Ke Kehwein Jo Main Khat De Wich Likhiya Jeendi Mar Gayi Sughra.

1
Jadon Qasad Haal Wasaya Akbar De Sah Muk Gaye.Haye Wichran Na Kadi Shala Bhaina Taun Haye Bhira Dukhra Ainj Da Likhiya.
Khat Bhain Da Seene Laya Akbar De Sah Muk Gaye.
Kiwein Jeendi Aye Payi Baajh Mere Haye Baba Mojzay Naal Wikha.
Do Ungliyaan Wich Dikhlaya Akbar De Sah Muk Gaye.

2
Chum Ke Zakhmi Seena Maula Ne Aye Akbar Da Keh Ke Haye Sughra.
Jadon Hath Barchi Nu Paya Akbar De Sah Muk Gaye.
Aakhay Sajjad, Akbar Barchi Taun Aye Wadh Ke Zakhm Bhain De Hijar Da Gham.
Haye Sughra Keh Ke Bulaya Akbar De Sah Muk Gaye.
