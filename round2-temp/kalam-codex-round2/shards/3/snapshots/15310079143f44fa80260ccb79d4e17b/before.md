Ya Ali Bismillah Rizwan Ali Zaidi
Ya Ali Haq Maula Ishq Ne Dar Khola Ya Ali Bismillah.

1
Dekhtey Hi Jalwa Jhomta Hai Kaba Ya Ali Bismillah.
Salman Ki Gardoon Pe Nazar Tab Se Jami Hai.
Sarkar Ke Honton Pe Abhi Nad-e-Ali Hai.
Muntazir Kab Se Hai Marka Khaybar Ka Ya Ali Bismillah.

2
Jibreel Pukara Mere Ustad Ali Hain.
Aagaz Se Hi Mere Mujhe Yaad Ali Hain.
Rab Ne Jo Bhi Pocha Keh Diya Oss Lamha Ya Ali Bismillah.
Aawaz Ka Aana Tu Kabhi Hath Barhana.

3
Mairaj Ki Shab Bhi Yeh Tera Chupna Chupana.
Raat Hai Jalwaon Ki Iss Liye Yeh Parhna Ya Ali Bismillah.
Sarkar Ko Sab Chor Ke Jate Hain Ohad Mein.
Borhay Hoon Jawaan Hoon Bhalay Aounchay Hoon Woh Qad Mein.

4
Ab Nabi Ko Dharas Aap Se Hai Lillah Ya Ali Bismillah.
Turbat Ke Sawaloon Ko Zara Dair Bhula Ke.
Masroof-e-Ziyarat Hain Mere Sath Farishtay.
Hal-e-Dil Sunney Ko Aaiye Ab Ya Maula Ya Ali Bismillah.

5
Jata Nahi Koi Yahan Marhab Ke Muqabil.
Ab Noor Hi Aa Sakta Hai Oss Shab Ke Muqabil.
Bhagney Walon Par Kya Bharosa Karna Ya Ali Bismillah.
Sehwan Ki Yeh Darti Hai Ali Walon Ki Basti.

6
Hai Nam-e-Ali Charon Taraf Ishq Ki Masti.
Lal Ke Mangton Ka Hai Yehi Ik Narah Ya Ali Bismillah.
Quran Ka Aagaz Howa Ism-e-Ali Se.
Quran Hi Kya Kun Ki Sada Ism Ali Se.

7
Khilqat-e-Aalam Ki Ibtida Hai Goya Ya Ali Bismillah.
 Touqeer Ka Khama Hai Tu Rizwan Ki Aawaz.
Midhat Ki Fizaon Mein Hai Alfaz Ki Aawaz.
Suntay Hi Yeh Midhat Keh Uthein Khud Zahra Ya Ali Bismillah.
