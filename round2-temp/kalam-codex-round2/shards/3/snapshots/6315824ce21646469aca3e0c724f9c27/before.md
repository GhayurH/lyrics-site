Tere Zahoor Key Din Qambar Kiyani Lyrics

1
Yeh Mojza Bhi Dikhey Ga Tere Zahoor Ke Din.
Tujhe Yeh Kaba Jhukay Ga Tere Zahoor Ke Din.
Namaz Esa Ne Iss Tarha Na Parhi Ho Gi.
Jo Tere Pichey Parhey Ga Tere Zahoor Ke Din.
Khuda Ke Ghar Mein Yaqeenan Namaz-e-Asar Ke Baad.
Azza Ka Farsh Bichay Ga Tere Zahoor Ke Din.

2
Haram Ke Gird Qalandar Ke Sath Matam Ka.
Phir Aik Halqa Banay Ga Tere Zahoor Ke Din.
Gham-e-Hussain Mein Jee Bhar Ke Nooh Kar Na Saka.
Woh Ro Ke Noha Karey Ga Tere Zahoor Ke Din.
Parhein Gay Maula Ki Dawood Manqabat Oss Din.
Har Ik Nabi Jo Sunay Ga Tere Zahoor Ke Din.

3
Hai Aik Yeh Bhi Haqeeqat Imam-e-Asar Tera.
Tawaf Kaba Karey Ga Tere Zahoor Ke Din.
Har Aik Matami Momin Sabeel Aab-e-Hayat.
Ba Dastay e Khizar Piye Ga Tere Zahoor Ke Din.
Kiya Tha Hazrat-e-Yaqoob Ne Jo Yousuf Ko.
Woh Sajdah Tujhe Ko Karey Ga Tere Zahoor Ke Din.

4
Firaq Mein Nahi Aadam Ka Shah Ke Gham Mein.
Zameen Pe Ashk Girey Ga Tere Zahoor Ke Din.
Kiya Tha Matam-e-Sarwar, Khalil Ne Oss Dam.
Woh Karbala Mein Karey Ga Tere Zahoor Ke Din.
Teri Sada Pe Hai Qambar Ko Yeh Yaqeen Itna.
Keh Qabar Se Bhi Uthay Ga Tere Zahoor Ke Din.
