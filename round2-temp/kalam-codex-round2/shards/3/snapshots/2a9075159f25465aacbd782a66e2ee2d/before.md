1
Shabbir Janaza Shabar Da Ghar Wapis Moor Liaye.
Zainab Ne Kambdiyan Hathaan Nal Lashay Choon Teer Hataye.
Ro Ro Ke Bhain Nu Dasda Si Zakhma Nu Wekh Ke Ghazi.
Ik Kafan Hi Lal Nayi Hoya Galiyaan V Rang Badlaye.

2
Tar Khon Che Wekh Ke Maiyyat Nu Zainab Roway Gash Khaway.
Shabbir De Munh Choon Nikli Aye Har Teer De Naal Ik Haye.
Aye Shehar Way Aman Di Ja Zainab Taboot Way Aman De Shah Da.
Veeran Madina Ho Jana Abbas Na Taig Chalaye.

3
Ayelan Ne Karday Yasrab Di Galiyaan Wich Loag Fasadi.
Aye Hasan Way Jan Muhammad Di Rozay Te Ja Na Paye.
Farman-e-Muhammad Hai Sabir, Shabar Mere Badan Da Tukra.
Yani Ummat Ne Hasan Nayi Mursal Te Teer Chalaye.
