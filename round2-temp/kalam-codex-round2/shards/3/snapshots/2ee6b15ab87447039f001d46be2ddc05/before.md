Taig e Ali Ki Jhan Jhan Jhan Jhan.
Gaunj Rahi Hai Maidano Mein.

1
Char Se Kissi Ke Jism Ko Chera.
San Se Kissi Ke Khod Ko Kata.
Tar Se Kissi Ke Munh Par Mara.
Dhar Se Kissi Ke Dhar Ko Giraya.

2
Tar Tar Sar Sar Dhar Dhar San San.
Maut Bhi Oss Se Poch Rahi Thi.
Kitne Marey Kar De Ginti.
Taig-e-Ali Yeh Sun Kar Nikli.

3
Chaar Gira Kar Phir Yeh Boli.
Bawan Tarippan Chawwan Pachpan.
Taigein Chup Hain Khanjar Chup Hai.
Rahzan Chup Hain Rehbar Chup Hai.

4
Antar Ka Har Mantar Chup Hai.
Khof Se Sara Lashkar Chup Hai.
Dhak Dhak Bolay Dil Ki Dharkan.
Sar Sar Mein Bhi Iss Ke Sur Tha.

5
Sam Pe Patka Jis Ko Patka.
Har Pasli Se Sargam Gonja.
Sa Re Gama Padha Ni Sa.
Raag Hain Iss Ke Behar-o-Ayeman.

6
Iss Ka Badan Hai Jaisay Mar Mar.
Gongi Ho Ke Bolay Far Far.
Charon Janib Iss Ki Sar Sar.
Porab Pacham Dakhan Uttar.

7
Ban Mein Dekho Iss Ka Jauban.
Dushman Ka Sar Ghora Ghorey.
Aadha Marey Aadha Choray.
Munh Jo Dekhey Munh Ko Toray.

8
Khudsar Ka Sar Phar Se Phoray.
Jhoom Raha Hai Sun Ke Tausan.
Lashay Par Hai Doja Lasha.
Kaisa Dholak Kaisa Tasha.

9
Pal Mein Tola Pal Mein Masha.
Raqs-e-Bismil Ka Hai Tamasha.
Toba Toba Iss Ki Jhan Jhan.
Kaisa Hai Sajjad Yeh Manzar.

10
Shoor Hai Har So Haider Haider.
Simtay Hain Jibreel Ke Sheh Par.
Kanp Rahi Hai Maut Bhi Thar Thar.
Iss Se Bacha Ke Apni Gardan.
