1
Kya Bataon Main Zamane Ko Fazeelat Inn Ki.
Chand Qatre Hain Magar Khob Hai Izat Inn Ki.
Behar-e-Aalam Se Kahin Zyada Hai Wusat Inn Ki.
Majlis-e-Shah Mein Behna Hai Ibadat Inn Ki.

2
Chashm-e-Pur Dard Ki Tauqeer Barha Dete Hain.
Ronaq-e-Majlis-e-Shabir Barha Dete Hain.
Aaj Kehta Hai Zamana Jo Inhein Ashk-e-Azza.
Yeh Laqab Majlis-e-Sarwar Ke Sabab Inn Ko Mila.

3
Sari Dunya Mein Koi Misal Nahi Hai Inn Ka.
Na Kissi Our Ne Paya Kabhi Aisa Rutba.
Pursa Dari Ki Riwayat Ka Unwan Bane.
Han Azadari-e-Shabir Ki Pehchan Bane.

4
Hai Azadar Ki Aankhon Se Taluq Inn Ka.
Muntazir Rehte Hain Har Waqt Baray-e-Pursa.
Qudratan Paya Hai Inn Sab Ne Saleeqa Aisa.
Naam-e-Shabir Suna Aankh Se Ikhraj Kya.

5
Zaigham-e-Saqi-e-Kausar Keh Paristar Hain Yeh.
Inn Ko Nisbat Hai Wafaon Se Wafadar Hain Yeh.
Un-Ginat Wasf Hain Inn Ke Gham-e-Sarwar Ki Qasam.
In KO ALLAH Ne Bakhsha Hai Ajab Jah-o-Hasham.

6
Hazrat-e-Jafar-e-Sadiq Se Riwayat Hai Raqam.
Jo Gham-e-Sheh Min Bahaye-e-Ga Woh Jaye Ga Iram.
Apni Taqdees Ka Ejaz Dikha Sakte Hain.
Aag Ik Pal Mein Jahanum Ki Bujha Sakte Hain.

7
Yeh Woh Aanso Hain Jo Har Aik Ki Qismat Mein Nahi.
Sahib-e-Dolat-o-Qowat Ki Itat Mein Nahi.
Yeh Nageene Kisi Tajir Ki Tijarat Mein Nahi.
Juz Azadar Kisi Our Ki Baiyat Mei Nahi.

8
Aaj Bhi Hur Ki Tarha Barsar-e-Paikar Hain Yeh.
Majlis-e-Shah Se Mansoob Hain Mukhtar Hain Yeh.
Jab Bhi Hota Hai Sar-e-Majlis-e-Sarwar Giriya.
Ashk Aankhon Se Bahate Hain Sabhi Ahl-e-Aza.

9
Chun Ke Le Jati Hain Romal Mein Apne Zahra.
Our Yasir Yehi Kehtin Hain Basad Aaho Buka.
Yeh Woh Aanso Hain Nahi Hai Koi Jin Ka Humsar.
Zakhm-e-Shabir Pe Lagte Hain Yeh Marham Ban Kar.
