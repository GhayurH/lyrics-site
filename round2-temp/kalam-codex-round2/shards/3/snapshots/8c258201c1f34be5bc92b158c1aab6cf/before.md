1
Shifaat Ki Azaan Mula Naqi Hain.
Karm Ki Dastaan Mula Naqi Hain.
Yeh Hujat Hain Khuday-e-Lam Yazal Ki.
Khuda Ke Tarjumaan Mula Naqi Hain.

2
Jahan Par Rifatein Bhi Sar Jhukaein.
Wo Azmat Ka Nishaan Mula Naqi Hain.
Sherini Gholte Hain Rooh-o-Dill Mein.
Bare Sherein Zubaan Mula Naqi Hain.

3
Yeh Nikhal-e-Taiba Ki Paak Konpal.
Sakha Ka Gulistaan Mula Naqi Hain.
Humein Kyun Hashar Ki Garmi Ka Dar Ho.
Hamara Sayebaan Mula Naqi Hain.

4
Sadat Jante Hain Nokri Ko.
Hamare Hukmuraan Mula Naqi Hain.
Main Inn Ke Dar Pe Sahil Jhuk Giya Hoon.
Bohat Hi Meharbaan Mula Naqi Hain.
