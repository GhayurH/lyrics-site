Khudara Neend Se Jago Utho Utho Amma
Sanwaro Baloon Ko Amma Libas Badlo Ab
Kyun Jagti Nahi Ab Tum Hai Kaisi Neend Ajab
Howa Hai Kya Tumhein Aisa Karo Na Hum Se Biyaan
Youn Chup Raho Gi Agar Hum Se Kuch Na Bolo Gi
Kahin Na Jaein Gay Jab Tak Na Aankh Kholo Gi
Qasam Se Baithay Rahein Gay Tumhare Pas Yahan
Ajeeb Haal Hai Dekho Tumhare Bin Ghar Ka
Siwaye Khof Ke Kuch Bhi Nazar Nahi Aata
Bala Ka Dasht Bana Hai Tumhare Baad Makaan
Jo Tum Na Ho Gi Tu Amma Kisay Bulaein Gay
Agar Khafa Ho Tu Keh Do Tumhein Manaein Gay
Tumhari Jan Pe Amma Nisar Kar Dein Gay Jaan
Aye Juzway Zaat-e-Payamber Hamari Maa Utho
Tumhare Dam Se Tu Roshan Hai Yeh Jahan Utho
Bahar Tum Se Hai, Tum Bin Tu Har Taraf Hai Khizan
Hasan Ne Aah Bhari Our Lipat Ke Maa Se Kaha
Mujhe Hai Yaad Jo Zalim Ne Tum Ko Mara Tha
Hai Meri Nazron Mein Ab Bhi Tamachay Ka Woh Nishaan
Hussain Barha Lagtay They Maa Ke Seene Se
Our Apni Aankhon Ko Mal Mal Ke Shah Kehte They
Aye Meri Maa Yeh Bata Do Keh Chain Paon Kahan
Batool-e-Azra Ki Maiyyat Ajeeb Maiyyat Thi
Keh Jis Pe Rooh-e-Payamber Bhi Khon Roti Rahi
They Khud Bahisht Ke Sardar Iss Pe Noha Kuna
Lipat Ke Maa Ke Janazay Se Dono Shehzaday
Tarap Tarap Ke Jo AHMAD Woh Bain Kartay They
Tu Jab Taraptay They Bache Tu Sath Roti Thi Maa
