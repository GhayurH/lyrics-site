Sadawaan Denda Aye Ibn Zahra Hal Min Nasir Yansurna.
Koi Ghurbat De Wich Aa Ke Daway Sath Mera Hal Min Nasir Yansurna.

 Akbar, Shabbir Aye Aakhay Na Wekho Khaimiyaan Pasay.
