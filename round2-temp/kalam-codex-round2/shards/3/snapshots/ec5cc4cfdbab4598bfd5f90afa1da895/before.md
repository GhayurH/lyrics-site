Nezay Pe Sar Tumhara Rasan Mein Mera Gala.

1
Ruk Jane Pe Lagaye Durray Mujhe Laeen Ne.
Chalti Hoon Tu Rasan Se Ghutta Hai Dam Mera.
Hum Sar Se Paon Tak Hain Zakhmo Se Chor Dono.
Pathar Tumhein Laga Hai Durrah Mujhe Laga.
Har Ik Qadam Pe Hum Ko Marey Gaye Tamachay.
Zakhmi Hain Gaal Mere Zakhmi Hai Sar Tera.

2
Ungli Tumhari Kati Angushtari Ki Khatir.
Do Baliyon Ki Khatir Mara Mujhe Gaya.
Zalim Ne Jab Pukara Mujh Ko Kaneez Keh Kar.
Deti Rahi Main Baba Ro Kar Tumhein Sada.
Aisa Safar Na Dekhein Ab Koi Baap Beti.
Kanton Pe Main Chali Hoon Nezay Pe Tu Chala.

3
Nezay Se Iss Liye Bhi Girta Hai Sar Chacha Ka.
Mujh Ko Gira Gira Ke Hanstay Hain Ashqiya.
Khak-e-Shifa Bana Hai Baba Kafan Tumhara.
Mera Kafan Bana Hai Kurta Jala Howa.
 Azlan Yeh Musayab Sajjad Ne Sunay They.
Chalees Saal Oss Ki Aankhon Se Khoon Baha.
