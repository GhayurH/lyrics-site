1
Tere Shagna Te Wain Painday Ne Teri Mehndi Te Khak Uddi Aye
Koi Maa Dunya Te Laray De Hath Mehndiyaan Walay Labhdi Aye.
Maa Aakhri Wari Wekh Laway Aye Aas V Zalimaan Tori Aye.
Kita Lash Nu Ainj Pamal Aina Ik Gathri De Wich Mori Aye.
Teri Lash Nu Qasim Jholi Pa Kubra De Wal Maa Takdi Aye.
Maa Toriya Putar Nu Zeen Uttay Jadon Mur Ke Khaimay Aaya Aye.
Maa Qasim Di Ik Wain Kita Main Qasim Kal Parnaya Aye.
Odi Lash Da Har Tukra Chum Ke Rab Jane Maa Ki Puchdi Aye.
Jadon Dhal Gayi Sham-e-Ghareebaan Te Aina Zalimaan Chadraan Laiyaan Ne.
Haye Mehndiyaan Walay Hathaan Wich Aina Zalimaan Rasiyaan Paiyaan Ne.
Ainj Wekhi Nayi Haye Dunya Te Khushiyaan Di Zindagi Mukdi Aye.
Ainj Landi Aye Hath Lash Nu Maa Naa Jaag Jaway Haye Lal Mera.
Jis Maa Ne Khon Di Gathri Wich Haye Wekhiya Putar Da Aye Sehra.
Ainj Chukya Aye Maa Jholi Wich Jiwein Jiyonday Lal Nu Chukdi Aye.
Taqseem Hoya Way Ainj Qasim Zahra De Lal Ne Labhiya Aye.
Tera Sabar Hussain Taun Kubra De Lekhaan Nu Kiwein Chukya Aye.
Oday Sabar Da Sadqa Aye Najfi Rat Qasim Di Naa Sukdi Aye.
