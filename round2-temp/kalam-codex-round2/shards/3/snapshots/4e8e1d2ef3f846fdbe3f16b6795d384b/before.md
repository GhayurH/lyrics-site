Sada e Zainab Zakir Bilawal Ali Hussaini.

1
Zindaan Se Aa Rahi Hai Bas Yeh Sada-e-Zainab.
Shabbir Tera Chehlum Kaisay Manaye Zainab.
Roney Pe Mere Bhaiyya Pabandiyaan Lagin Hain.
Ghurbat Pe Teri Kaisay Aanso Bahaye Zainab.
Zindan De Wich Ro Ro Ke Zainab Aye Wain Paway.
Chehlum Hussain Tera Kiwein Manaway Zainab.

2
Akbar Abbas Qasim Be Sheer Ka Yeh Chehlum.
Qaidon Mein Hi Karey Gi Shabeer Haye Zainab.
Hasrat Hai Ayho Veran Turbat Teri Te Beh Ke.
Akbar Putar De Nohay Tainu Sunaway Zainab.
Rasi Ne Banaye Hain Jo Neel Bazuon Par.
Ghazi Ko Kis Tarha Se Aa Kar Dikhaye Zainab.

3
Sar Pe Jo Rehne Deti Ummat Hussain Mere.
Banti Kafan Tumhara Ban Mein Rida-e-Zainab.
Qaid-e-Jafa Se Mujh Ko Mil Jaye Gar Rehai.
Hathoon Se Aa Ke Teri Turbat Banaye Zainab.
Youn Ja Ba Ja Jo Haq Ki Tableeg Kar Rahe Hain.
Yeh Sab Hai Matami Pe Mohsin Attaye Zainab.
