1
Tak Chan Wal Ghazi Akbar Dewan Abid Nu Dilasey.
Assi Janrne Aan Toon Janrna Aein Sang Phophiyaan Bhaina Sar Nangay Haye Sham De Pasay.
Toon Chan Wal Jiwein Takiya Ik Manzar Aisa Baniya.
Teri Akhiyaan Wich Assan Wekhay Ne Bazaraan Wich Jairay Milne Ne Dardaan De Khulasay.

2
Mere Bazoo Qalam Ne Honrein Aynu Phal Barchi Da Lagna.
Toon Hosla Apna Hari Na Assan Baaj Badan Wich Sham Tainu Ki Dainre Sahare.
Qasim Ne Ro Farmaya Sun Veeran Chache Jaya.
Aye Karbal Saday Naam Di Aye Teri Okhi Manzal Sham Di Aye Rab Toor Charhaway.

3
Zaianb Da Wara Aya Maa Putar Nu Cha Gal Laya.
Toon Nasal Imamat Di Bachra Main Tenu Kaliyaan Nayi Chandna Aye Maa De Ne Waday.
Khaime De Olay Beh Ke Mera Mula Jaun Aye Soche.
Aye Pyar De Rishte Muk Janrein Aye Chehre Pal Wich Luk Janrein Muk Janrein Dilasay.
