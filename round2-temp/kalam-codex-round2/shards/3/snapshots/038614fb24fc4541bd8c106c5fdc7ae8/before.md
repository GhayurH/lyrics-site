1
Uss Ke Rastay Mukhtar Hussain Fatehpuri
Oss Ke Rastay Ke Sabhi Zarray Munawar Dekhey.
Jis Ke Chehray Ki Taraf Chand Bikhar Kar Dekhey.
Tum Ho Mehboob-e-Khuda Shah-e-Madina Tum Ho.
Kis Mein Himmat Hai Tumhein Aankh Utha Kar Dekhey.
Teri Darbani Pe Nazaan Hai Zamana Sara.
Tere Dar Ke Tu Gadagar Bhi Abuzar Dekhey.
Kyun Na Phir Sahib-e-Ijaz Kahey Tum Ko Jahan.
Kalma Parhtay Hoye Jab Hath Pe Kankar Dekhey.
Aadam-o-Nooh Hoon Esa Hoon Ya Mosa Se Nabi.
Hum Ne Dekhey Na Kahin Par Tere Dar Par Dekhey.
Main Ne Samjha Hai Faqat Maqsad-e-Mairaj Yehi.
Noor-e-Zahra Ko Nabi Arsh Pe Aa Kar Dekhey.
Behar-e-Midhat Se Takallum Ne Bhari Mashk-e-Sukhan.
Dekhna Ho Jisay Kozay Mein Samandar Dekhey.
