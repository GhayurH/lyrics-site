1
Zindan Che Musa Kazim Da Aj Loko Khatam Jahad Hoya.
Be-Kafan Janaza Kazim Ka Haye Khak De Naal Aabaad Hoaya.
Musa Kazim De Zakhma Wich Paiwast Hoiyaan Zanjeeraan Ne.
Haye Baba Raza Da Qaid Wichon Kariyaan Paye Aazaad Hoya.

2
Kadi Hasan Hussain Kadi Zainab Nu Kadi Zahra Nu Samay Fizza.
Kazim Di Ghurbat Wich Ro Ro Be-Haal Aye Kul Ajdad Hoya.
Aynj Gal Wich Tauq Zanjeeraan Te Pairaan Wich Aablay Bhari Ne.
Lagda Aye Qaidi Kazim Nayi Ik Wari Fair Sajjad Hoya.

3
Mujha Gaye Shajar Fizawaan Wich Har Pasay Udasi Chayi Aye.
Qatil De Zulm Toon Qatal Ewein Wasda Sara Baghdada Hoya.
Aye Jam Shahdat Da Pee Ke Dunya Toon Parda Kar Gaya Si.
Par Sanwaan Mukiyaan Raza Te Ja Zeeshan Zulm Aiday Bad Hoya.
