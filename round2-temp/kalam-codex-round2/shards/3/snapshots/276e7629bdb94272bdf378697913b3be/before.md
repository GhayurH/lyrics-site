1
Jab Main Tasbeeh-e-Janab-e-Fatima Parhne Laga.
Arsh Tak Parwaz Thi ALLAH -o-Akbar Jab Kaha.
Parh Raha Tha Noor Ik Subhana Rabi Al-Aala.
Our Mere Dil Mein Thi Yeh Alhamd-o-Lillah Ki Sada.

2
Hamd Hai Al-Hamd Ke Dil Mein Bara-e-Fatima.
Be Ke Nuqtay Se Hai Aagaz-e-Sana-e-Fatima.
Ghar Khuda Ka Hai Kiya Aabad Ali Ne Tu Khuda.
Chahta Hai Keh Ali Ka Ghar Basaye Fatima.

3
Maqsad-e-Mairaj Ik Yeh Bhi Tha Keh Khud Mustafa.
Arsh Tak Chal Kar Khuda Se Lainay Aaye Fatima.
Pas Hai Parday Ka Warna Apne Haq Ke Wastay.
Har Nabi Ka Mojza Kar Ke Dikhaye Fatima.

4
Kehti Hain Esa Se Mariyam Dekh Lo Beta Issay.
Mehdi Tak Le Jaye Ga Yeh Naqsh-e-Paye Fatima.
Poch Kar ALLAH Se Jibreel Ne Sabit Kiya.
Janta Koi Nahi Raz-e-Kisaye Fatima.

5
Woh Razi ALLAH Bhi Gar Ho Tu Uth Sakta Nahi.
Jis Kissi Ko Apni Nazron Se Giraye Fatima.
Fatima Ke Dushmano Par Karna Phir Lanat Asad.
Oss Pe Wajib Hai Jo Khata Hai Ghizaye Fatima.
