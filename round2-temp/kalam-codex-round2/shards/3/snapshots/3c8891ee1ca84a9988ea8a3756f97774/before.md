1
Ya Rab Kise Putar Te Aisa Na Waqt Aye Soz Sheraz Khan.
Mitiyaan De Naal Abid Phophiyaan De Sir Lokaye.
Be Ghar Musafiraan De Pairaan Che Chale Was Gaye.
Pairaan De Chaliyaaan Wich Kandiyaan Ne Ghar Wasaye.

2
Wasde Pathar Ki Janan Kera Kitna Qeemti Aye.
Phophiyaan Onno Bachawan Phophiyaan Nu Oh Bachaye.
Veeran De Sir Te Aa Ke Ruk Jandiyaan Ne Nazraan.
Zainab Rida Nu Waikhe Ya Sir De Vain Paye.

3
Chehre Dey Neel Le Ke Chupdi Phire Sakina.
Babe Da Sir Safar Wich Odhay Samne Jad Aye.
Iss Dukh Nu Sukh Samaj Ke Sajday Che Dig Paye Ne.
Zindaan Che Aa Gaye Ne Bazaar De Sataye.

4
Phophiyaan De Bazoan Tun Rasiyaan Utar Betha Aye.
Hun Sochda Aye Abid Aye Neel Kinj Hataye.
Disda Aye Bazoan Te Hamsheer Da Janaza.
Abid Dua Layi Akbar Hathaan Nu Jad Uthaye.
