Haye viraa, Zainab chore chali, teriyyan pak jagiran noon

Kol je hondi te roro kaddi, Main teri laash chon teeran noon

Vir vee mere atharan, kharri mohtaj rida ton,

kaddi pathar nain o vajjde vekhe, Parde dar assiran noon

Ajje baqir vee ey kamsin, main vee pabande rassan han,

Tera sajjad hai lachar viran, Kiven sambhega zanjeeran noon

Varisan vali be varis, turri pardesch jaake,

Allah jane kiven zainab turr paiyy, Be kafan chhor ke viraan noon

Pak sajjad di kand tonh, chola chaya jaddon zainab,

Rondhi raiyy aun di maa takk takk ke, Kand te surkh lakeeranh noon

Naal seyyedan de jo akhtar, keeti jaggne ey dassade,

lokan bazaaranch patthar marey, Do jahanan de amiran noon
