1
Shala Mehndi Lawan Behna Hasan Sadiq.
Na Kehnday Veer Shala Watna Taun Door Howan.
Sughra De Waang Bhainra Na Rahwaan Che Beh Ke Rowan.
Shala Mehndi Lawan Bhainra Hathi Jag Te Bhirawaan Koon.

2
Na Koi Sughra Wang Wekhay Veraan Diyaan Rahwaan Koon.
Ban Wich Na Rulan Sehrey Na Koi Tukray Tukray Howay.
Pa Mitiyaan Sir Che Koi Banray Di Maa Na Roway.
Putraan De Sadmay Jhalnre Hunday Okhay Ne Mawaan Koon.

3
Sughra Nu Tere Bajoon Deway Dilasay Kehra.
Aa Wekh Howay Maithon Babal Da Sunjha Wehra.
Kiwein Rokaan Main Das Veran Aina Dardaan Te Hawaan Koon.
Wal Aa Ja Tu Chan Veran Sughra Aye Ro Ro Kehndi.

4
Bethi Aan Wich Udeekaan Teri Ghool Ke Main Mehndi.
Le Dil Che Mar Na Jawaan Mehndiyaan Diyaan Chawaan Koon.
Kali Beemar Sughra Chad Tur Gaya Aye Akbar.
Wichrey Ne Jehri Ja Taun Qasim Te Veer Asghar.

5
Dukhray Suna Ke Rowaan Ona Veer Main Thawaan Koon.
Shah Najam Di Dua Aye Sadqa Aal-e-Abba Da.
Nikhrey Na Jag Te Sanga Kisay Bhainr Te Bhira Da.
Na Lagay Nazar Shala Kisay Bhainr De Nawaan Koon.
