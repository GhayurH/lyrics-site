Hussain Mat Jao Syed Zain Ali Zaidi.

1
Pukari Zainab-e-Kubra Hussain Mat Jao.
Youn Hum Ko Chor Ke Tanha Hussain Mat Jao.
Jo Aaj Ran Mein Gaya Laut Kar Nahi Aaya.
Kharey Hain Samne Aada Hussain Mat Jao.
Nahi Bacha Koi Mera Siwaye Abid Ke.
Ujar Gaya Mera Kunba Hussain Mat Jao.

2
Keh Kamsini Mein Yateemi Ka Khof Hai Tari.
Bola Rahi Hai Sakina Hussain Mat Jao.
Ruko Zara Tumhein Jee Bhar Ke Dekh Loon Bhai.
Nahi Milo Gay Dobarah Hussain Mat Jao.
Madine Ja Ke Ossay Kya Bataon Gi Bhai.
Banam-e-Fatima Sughra Hussain Mat Jao.

3
Galay Laga Ke Sheh-e-Dein Ko Boli Bint-e-Ali.
Tumhein Qasam Meri Bhaiyya Hussain Mat Jao.
Tarap Rahe Hain Haram Khak Ura Rahi Hai Hawa.
Main Mar Na Jaon Khudara Hussain Mat Jao.
Kaha Hussain Ne Yasir Nibhaon Ga Wadah.
Bas Ab Na Kehna Aye Behna Hussain Mat Jao.
