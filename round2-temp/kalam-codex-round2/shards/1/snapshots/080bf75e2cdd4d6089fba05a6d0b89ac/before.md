Ghazi de baad zainab
Sar di rida luta ke
Bari dair taeen royi

1
Jado khaimey jal gayee san seeney alam no laa ke

2
Likyah eh rayiya ne oh ghum hogai sakina
Piya marda tamache rokasar tei kamina
Zainab bihra de di dei cholech bah buja ke

3
Lasha hasan dei chan da jis waqt khamey aya
Kubra no seeney laa key roya batal jaya
Ghatri batool jayi farwah di jholi paa key

4
Zalim tei tur gaya si oh khayam nu jala ke
Zahra dei ladlie dey hatach rasia paa ke
Makhtuma do alam be valch moh loka ke

5
Naizay dey naal zalim oh chadar jado lahai
Meri sain apnay sar wich karbal di khaak pai
Abbas bawafa di gal naal mashk laa ke

6
Ujri rubab da main oh ajj haal ki sunawa
Jado ghodianch paa ke bachrey khidan maaawa
Hyy bibi ladli dey johlich choley paa ke

7
Zaman mein matami di mehsharch Zahra jai
Zenei khuda di jis nai toqeer hai vadai
Baghi nai namazi shabir no mana ke

8
Jadon ghash chon hosh kita wekhi ajab lachari
Mazloome karbal da pochaia jadon muhari
Ahwal karbala da sajjad no suna ke
