Hussain Jate Hain Kabay Se Karbala Ki Taraf.

1
Hussain Pohanchay Jo Maka Tu Hajj Ke They Ayyam.
Tamam Loag Hain Shadaan Keh Aa Gaye Hain Imam.
Woh Hajj Tu Khas Hai Oss Ka Nahi Hai Aam Maqam.
Keh Jis Ki Aap Imamat Karey Zamaan Ka Imam.
Hussain Kehte They Jate Hain Nainawa Ki Taraf.
Hamarey Aakhri Sajday Ki Ab Ada Ki Taraf.
Qareeb Aa Gaya Din Hajj Ka Sheh Ne Tab Dekha.
Libas-e-Hajiyon Mein Foaj-e-Sham Hai Ba Khuda.
Abba Mein Apni Chupaye They Khanjar-o-Naiza.
Tarap Ke Reh Gaye Sarwar Zabaan Se Kuch Na Kaha.
Hussain Taktay Hoye Marzi-e-Khuda Ki Taraf.
Haram Se Aman Ke Niklay Chaley Qaza Ki Taraf.
Haram Mein Aaye Sheh-e-Dein Kaha Bahasrat-o-Yaas.
Hai Karna Umrah Mein Tabdeel Hajj Ko Badlo Libas.
Nazar Ki Janib Humsheer Paya Khof-o-Hiraas.
Lagaya Seene Se Humsheer Ko Bandhai Aas.
Kissi Bhi Taig Na Khanjar Na Ashqiya Ki Taraf.
Kaha Keh Dehyan Hai Zainab Teri Rida Ki Taraf.
Biyan Kaisay Woh Manzar Karey Ga Koi Anees.
Hussain Kabay Ke Pas Aaye Our Kaha Yeh Anees.
Hussain Jata Hai Kabay Se Le Ke Sare Anees.
Abad Talak Rahe Hurmat Teri Aye Mere Anees.

2
Zameen-e-Aman Se Kunbay Ki Qatal Ga Ki Taraf.
