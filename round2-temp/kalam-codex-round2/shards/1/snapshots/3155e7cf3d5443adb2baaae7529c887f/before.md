1
Fazeelat Payi Hai Kya Murtaza Ne Khuda Jane Nabi Jane.
Kiye Hain Kaam Kya Dast-e-Khuda Ne.
Abu Talib Ka Ghar Hai Yaan Hai Kaba.
Najaf Kursi Samma Arsh-e-Mualla.
Ali-e-Murtaza Ke Sab Thikane.
Safina Nooh Ka Sahil Pe Laya.
Ossi Ne Neel Mein Rasta Banaya.
Ali Ne Dekhe Hain Kitne Zamane.
Hijab-e-Noor-e-Wahid Ban Gayi Hai.
Elaj-e-Zoaf-e-Payghamber Wohi Hai.
Kissa Ko Kar Diya Kya Fatima Ne.
Batana Tha Unhein Anjam Unn Ka.
Button Ke Samne Dewar-e-Kaba.
Mein Rasta Kyun Kiya Tha La-Fatah Ne.
Rajab Ki Terah Ko Ik Fakhar-e-Esa.
Haram Mein Kaise Parhta Ja Raha Tha.
Sahife Kya Naye Our Kya Purane.
Woh Angothi Woh Lehja Our Woh Parda.
Ali Tha Maizbaan Kya Mustafa Ka.
Shab-e-Asra Hoi Kis Ke Bahane.
Zamana Lakh Akbar Sharah Likhe.
Lisan ALLAH Ko Kis Tarha Samjhe.
Yeh Sab Nehje Balagah Ke Khazane.
