Hashiya Bandh Ke Zainab Ka Qasida Likha
Karbala Likha Najaf Likha Madina Likha.
Hashiya Bandh Ke Zainab Ka Qaseeda Likha.
Unn Ko Rutbay Mein Kabhi Kam Na Zyada Likha.
Main Ne Zainab Ko Sada Sani-e-Zahra Likha.
Kar Diya Lehja-e-Mairaj Ko Zindah Tu Ne.
Warna Reh Jata Yeh Tareekh Mein Likha Likha.
Tarjuma Aya-e-Tatheer Ka Zainab Ke Beghair.
Jis Mufasar Ne Bhi Likha Hai Adhora Likha.
Sadiyon Pehle Kabhi Khaliq Ne Likha Tha Pardah.
Tu Ne Darbar Mein Yeh Lafz Dobarah Likha.
Tere Khutbon Pe Hai Hairan Abhi Tak Dunya.
Kabhi Quran Kabhi Nehj-e-Balaga Likha.
Shayari Fun Ki Talabgar Nazar Aayi Mujhe.
Main Keh Fankar Nahi Tha So Aqeeda Likha.
Niyatan Sani-e-Zahra Inhein Samjha Akbar.
Our Be Khof Inhein Umm-e-Abeeha Likha.
