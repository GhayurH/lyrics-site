Hassan De Jigar Da Tukra Faheem Ali.

1
Aye Hasan De Jigar Da Tukra Hay Jehra Tukriyaan Wich Taqseem Theye.
Aahdin Hik Raat Da Banra Hay Jehra Tukriyaan Wich Taqseem Theye.
Quran Samajh Ke Shagna Da Karbal Di Khak Te Jhanr Jhanr Ke.
Shabeer Ne Okha Chunriya Hay Jehra Tukriyaan Wich Taqseem Theye.
Aye Pehla Ghoot Hai Dunya Da Jaikon Shadi Di Dastar Pawa.
Maa Inna Lillah Parhya Hay Jehra Tukriyaan Wich Taqseem Theye.

2
Rab Janrey Ya Qasim Janrey Aikon Terah Saal Yateemi Wich.
Kiwein Maa Ne Okha Pa Liya Hay Jehra Tukriyaan Wich Taqseem Theye.
Tareekh Taun Poch Jo Oon Welay Ainda Phas Gaye Pair Rakabaan Wich.
Jada Wich Maqtal De Jhanriya Hay Jehra Tukriyaan Wich Taqseem Theye.
Rab Janrey Jo Wich Khaimiyaan De Kya Guzri Ho Si Amari Te.
Jadaan Gathri De Wich Waliya Hay Jehra Tukriyaan Wich Taqseem Theye.

3
Aye Khon Di Mehndi La Turya Aindi Saij Banri Hay Khak Shifa.
Aikon Sehra Maut Ne Badhiya Hay Jehra Tukriyaan Wich Taqseem Theye.
Hairan Haan Main Sehrey Di Ja Kyun Bhainraan Mawaan Phophiyaan Ne.
Ayein Ghoot Da Parhya Noha Hay Jehra Tukriyaan Wich Taqseem Theye.
Jehra Bahlool Ne Likhiya Hay Aye Dard Faheem Sunriya Sakoon.
Qayam Di Akh Taun Wasda Hay Jehra Tukriyaan Wich Taqseem Theye.
