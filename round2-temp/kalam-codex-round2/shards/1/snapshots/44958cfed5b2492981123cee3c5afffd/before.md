1
Kar Ke Sanad De Tukray Kalma Gowan Haye Jhutlayi Fatima.
Rondi Darbar Wicho Fasiq Diyaan Jhirkaan Sun Ke Bahir Aayi Fatima.
Keeta Majboor Bara Lokaan Hasnain Da Piyo.
Chup Ke Kalma Go Toon Haye Raat De Hanrey Wich Aynj Dafnai Fatima.

2
Meri Aagosh Che Dam Toriyan Aye Naal Mere.
Lay Ke Pasay Da Zakhm Be-Sheer Nu Gal Nal La Ke Haye Kurlai Fatima.
Puchdi Zainab Aye Veeran Maan Di Zaeefi Da Sabab.
Aakhay Zainab Nu Hasan Saday Qatal Diyaan Tehreeran Haye Wekh Aayi Fatima.

3
Kalma Go Kursi Nasheen Hasday Ray Main Saan Khari.
Poch Salman Kolon Badbakht Musalmanaa Ne Kiwein Satai Fatima.
Tere Zahoor Diyaan Matami Karday Ne Dua.
Laway Badla Mehdi Tosif Jina Dunya Te Hai Rowai Fatima.
