1
Haye Aise Sham Aai Hai Joan Rizvi.
Darbar-e-Sham Ke Bahir Aa Kar Jo Qafila Ruka.
Zainab Pe Teen Din Hoi Oss Ja Sitam Ki Inteha.
Aayi Sar-e-Hussain Se Roney Ki Oss Ghari Sada.
Dekha Sar-e-Hussain Tu Zainab Ne Ro Ke Yeh Kaha.
Dekho Hussain Zainab Ko Manzil Kahan Yeh Layi Hai Haye Aisay Sham Aayi Hai.
Ghurbat Pe Meri Logon Ne Har Ik Ghali Sajai Hai Haye Aisay Sham Aayi Hai.
Atharah Bhai Jeetay Jee Chadar Jo Mere Sar Ki They.
Naizon Pe Unn Ke Sar Hain Our Zainab Ki Be Ridai Hai Haye Aisay Sham Aayi Hai.
Pathar Teri Jabeen Par Mara Gaya Tha Jis Tarha.
Har Ja Pe Zarb Pathar Ki Main Ne Bhi Waisay Khayi Hai Haye Aisay Sham Aayi Hai.
Zainab Ko Maro Jab Bhi Yeh Qatil Tumhare Kehte Hain.
Fizza Ne Apne Saye Mein Teri Behan Chupayi Hai Haye Aisay Sham Aayi Hai.
Charon Taraf Kharey Hoye Khoonkhar Loag Hain Jahan.
Qaidi Bana Ke Zainab Ko Foaj-e-Laeen Layi Hai Haye Aisay Sham Aayi Hai.
Badlay Ali Se Lainay Hain Jis Ne Bhi Aa Ke Le Le Woh.
Bolay Laeen Sham Mein Beti Ali Ki Aayi Hai Haye Aisay Sham Aayi Hai.
Pathar Hain Taziyane Hain Our Jhirkiyaan Hain Tanay Hain.
Bazar Hai Hajoom Hai Har Samt Be Hayai Hai Haye Aisay Sham Aayi Hai.
Gham Bibiyon Ke Dil Mein Hai Maqtolon Ka Asad Magar.
Chehron Pe Gham Hai Sar Nangay Aoulad-e-Mustafai Hai Haye Aisay Sham Aayi Hai.
