1
Hasnain Jadoon Babay Da Taboot Uthaya.
Main Ujar Gayi Baba Ro Ro Ke Haye Zainab Ne Wain Paya.
Tatheer Diyaan Paliyaan Assi Fatima Di Jaiyaan.
Saday Pardiyaan Te Rab Ne Khud Ayataan Banaiyaan.
Fair Aap Tusi Baba Kofe Da Haye Bazar Kyun Wakhaya.
Aaj Pehli Wari Menu Naa Lay Ke Tu Bula Lay.
Banwaan Kafan Choon Kad Ke Zainab Nu Seene La Lay.
Shabir Layi Jiwein Kafan Wichon Haye Banwaan Nu Maa Ne Chaya.
Kyun Lasha Tera Kanbda Kyun Akhiyaan Rondiyaan Ne.
Das Baba Taithoon Teri Dhiyaan Aye Pochdiyaan Ne.
Asaan Rondiyaan Jad Baba Chadar Nu Haye Chehray Tere Te Laya.
Nanay Da Tu Ali Sain Ghazi Ali Bhira Da.
Mere Door De Ali Nu Ik War Mil Ja Baba.
Akh Khol Ke Wekh Tainu Milan Layi Haye Sajad Mera Aaya.
 Saqlain Nayin Takhiyul Tareekh Ne Way Dasiya.
Bas Piyo Di Maut Te E Abbas Ronda Takiya.
Iss Din Tu Elawa Nayin Ronda Hoya Haye Abbas Nazar Aaya.
