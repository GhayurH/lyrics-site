1
Hum Ali Ke Dewane Har Adda Ghadeeri Hai.
Ibtida Ghadeeri Hai Inteha Ghadeeri Hai.
Anbiya Imamo Ka Silsila Ghadeeri Hai.
Parh Lo Aya-e-Balag Khud Khuda Ghadeeri Hai.

2
Aaj Bhagne Wale Rasta Na Paein Gaye.
Maimana Ghadeeri Hai Maisarah Ghadeeri Hai.
Naam Le Ke Haider Ka Hum Yeh Jaan Lete Hain.
Kaun Hai Saqeefai Kaun Sa Ghadeeri Hai.

3
Oss Ko Kya Daraein Gi Mushkilein Zamane Ki.
Karbalai Dil Jis Ka Hosla Ghadeeri Hai.
Khud Hi Oss Ke Qadmo Mein Barh Ke Aye Gi Janat.
Barh Ke Jis Ko Haider Ne Keh Diya Ghadeeri Hai.

4
Kufar Our Batil Ki Bas Saqifa Manzil Hai.
Haq Pasand Logon Ka Rasta Ghadeeri Hai.
Dekh Kar Mujhe Akhtar Di Sada Yeh Rizwaan Ne.
Khuld Mein Chale Ayo Rasta Ghadeeri Hai.
