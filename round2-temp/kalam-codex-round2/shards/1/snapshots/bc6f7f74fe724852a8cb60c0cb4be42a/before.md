Haye Ghairat De Khuda Jadoon Malka e Haya Nu Be Parda Wekhiya.
Har Moor Uttay Fair Abid Nu Haye Marda Wekhiya.
Sir Babay Da Jinnay Mangiya Nayi Maqtal Taun Le Ke Sham Taein.
Wich Sham De Chadraan Phophiyaan Layi Haye Mangda Wekhiya.
Koi Wekhay Na Tainu Be Pardah Wich Sham Diyaan Bazaraan De.
Naizay Te Veer Quran Tera Haye Parhda Wekhiya.
Baba Akbar Qasim Ghazi Jang Kar Gaye Ne Wich Karbal De.
Gal Tauq Zanjeeraan Pa Abid Asaan Larda Wekhiya.
Jadon Kool Sakina Aandi Aye Koi Wakhra Dard Sunandi Aye.
Oda Chola Wekh Ke Ronda Aye Jehra Sarda Wekhiya.
Wajib Nabiyaan Te Keeti Aye Tazeem Khuda Ne Zahra Di.
Wich Bheer De Odhiyaan Dhiyaan Layi Rah Labda Wekhiya.
Nayi Bheer Shareefan Layi Najfi Aj Sham Diyaan Bazaraan Di.
Haye Le Mastoraan Naal Abid Kiwein Turda Wekhiya.
