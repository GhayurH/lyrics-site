1
Dhi Haider Di Chad Putran Nu Veraan Nal Karbal Jana Aye.
Aiday Sir Da Taj Nayi Bachiya Na Putraan Mur Ke Aana Aye.
Ro Aakhdi Aye Ghazi Karni Aan Main Duawaan.
Aa Jan Lal Mere Main V Eid Aye Manawaan.
Din Eid Da Aye Ghazi Main Putraan Nu Gal Lana Aye.
Ik Maa Te Naal Dhiyaan Din Eid Wala Howay.
Babay Nu Ron Dhiyaan Putraan Nu Maa Payi Roway.
Babay Di Lash Rul Gayi Aye Aj Putraan Safar Mokana Aye.
Abbas Te Ruqaiya Bas Kitiyaan Wafawaan.
Putraan De Baaj Lokaan Jadon Lutniyan Ridawaan.
Nayi Aana Veer Ghazi Karbal Wich Bhain Bolana Aye.
Kadi Wekhiya Nayi Jag Te Koi Ainj Da Zulm Karda.
Aye Aakh Ke Bhira Nu Takiya Nayi Veer Marda.
Na Mere Wal Toon Wekhin Jadon Sir Zalim Mera Lana Aye.
 Najfi Aye Jandi Aye Pardaisiyaan Nayi Aana.
Muslim Diyaan Putraan Nu Kisay Aan Nayi Bachana.
Warey Main Deen Toon Bachray Mainu Ummat Sham Wakhana Aye.
