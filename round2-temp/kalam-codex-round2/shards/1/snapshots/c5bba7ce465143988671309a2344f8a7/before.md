1) Dil may Akbar ke hay sina baba
Jaan balab hay ye neem jan baba

1
2) Aap kay hath thar tharate hain
Dekh kar khinchiye sina baba

2
3) Dekhne mujh ko aaye hain dekho
Dadi jaan aur dada jaan baba

3
4) Aap itna khayal rakhiye ga
Run may aye na meri maa baba

4
5) Mera ye haal dekh kar warna
Jaan dedegi meri maa baba

5
6) Khinch rahi hay rage tashanuj hay
Kyon na ragreon me ayriyaan baba

6
7) Ab to jatay ham khuda hafiz
Dam hay kuch dam ka mehmaan baba
