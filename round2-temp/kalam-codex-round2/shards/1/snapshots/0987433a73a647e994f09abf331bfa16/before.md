1
Gash Se Utho Sakina Jaan Dekho Baba Ka Sar Aa Gaya Hai.
Khul Raha Hai Dar-e-Zindaan Dekho Baba Ka Sar Aa Gaya Hai.
Jaag Jao Aye Baba Ki Pyari.
Rab Ne Faryad Sun Li Tumhari.

2
Aankh Kholo Yeh Maa Qurbaan Dekho Baba Ka Sar Aa Gaya Hai.
Jis Ke Seene Pe Sotin Thin Kal Tak Haye.
Aaj Sar Oss Ka Seene Pe Rakh Lo.
Poray Kar Lo Sabhi Armaan Dekho Baba Ka Sar Aa Gaya Hai.

3
Farq-e-Sarwar Ko Hathon Mein Le Lo.
Jo Bhi Kehna Hai Jee Bhar Ke Keh Do.
Meri Beti Sar-e-Zindaan Dekho Baba Ka Sar Aa Gaya Hai.
Tum Agar Roi Aye Shahzadi.

4
Our Takleef Baba Ko Ho Gi.
Ab No Rona Rahe Yeh Dehaan Dekho Baba Ka Sar Aa Gaya Hai.
Ghash Se Uthi Jo Bali Sakina.
Le Ke Sar Ko Woh Bekas Hazina.

5
Maa Se Boli Aye Amaa Jaan Dekho Baba Ka Sar Aa Gaya Hai.
Le Ke Baba Ke Sar Ko Sakina.
Aisi Soyi Ke Uthi Kabhi Na.
Phir Na Yasir Ho Aylaan Dekho Baba Ka Sar Aa Gaya Hai.
