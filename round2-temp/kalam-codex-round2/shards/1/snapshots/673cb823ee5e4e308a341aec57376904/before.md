Sidrah Se Aagay Ka Ilaqa Dhond Liya.
Yani Keh Abbas Ka Roza Dhond Liya.

1
Main Tu Dar-e-Abbas Pe Sajdah Raiz Howa.
Kabay Ne Khud Mera Sajdah Dhond Liya.
Dunya Mein Phailaye Hath Woh Kyun Jis Ne.
Bin Hathoon Ke Deney Wala Dhond Liya.

2
Jab Bhi Uthaon Hathoon Mein Ghazi Ka Alam.
Youn Lagta Hai Arsh Ka Paya Dhond Liya.
Dekha Jab Abbas Ki Turbat Par Pani.
Kis Ka Tha Darya Par Qabza Dhond Liya.

3
Woh Jis Mein Sara Quran Hai Poshida.
Woh Nam-e-Abbas Mein Nuqta Dhond Liya.
Janat Ka Tu Darwaza Hai Baab-e-Hussain.
Ab Jis Ne Bhi Yeh Darwaza Dhond Liya.

4
Matami Ne Bain Al Harmain Mein Chalte Hoye.
Arsh Talak Jane Ka Rasta Dhond Liya.
Koi Na Tha Bakhshish Ka Waseela Phir Ik Din.
Fitrus Ne Shabbir Ka Jhola Dhond Liya.

5
Ki Main Ne Abbas Ke Dushman Par Lanat.
Lanat Ne Khud Apna Nishana Dhond Liya.
Manzil-e-Haq Tak Khud Woh Pohanch Jaye Ga Asad.
Jis Banday Ne Farsh Azza Ka Dhond Liya.
