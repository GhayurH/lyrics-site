Dasht-e-bala may akhiri Zainab ki shaam hay

1
Haar simt hay dhuwan hi dhuwan ishdaham hay

2
1) Main jarahi hoon Karbobala choor kaar Hussain
Qaidi behan ka akhiri tujko salam hay

3
2) Bosay liya behan nay gulu-e-Hussain kay
Amma ka yeh diya howa mujh ko payam hay

4
3) Zainab lipat kay bolli yeh lashay Hussain say
Teray kafan ka ho na saka ihtimam hay

5
4) Islam beqhatar hi rehaga jehan may
Jab thak Hussain zinda tera pakh naam hay

6
5) Zainab ka hay yeh bayn saray dasht-e-Karbala
Baygor-o-bekafan shah-e-Aali moqam hay

7
6) Abid deyar-e-Shaam ko rothay hain aaj tak
Sajjad ki zuban pay Zainab ka naam hay

8
7) Pyaasi Sakina qaid thi is qaid khanay may
Jis may yeh ilm hona saka din hay Shaam hay
