Gard-e-Gham Se Mujhe Maila Nahi Hone Deta.
Mera Mula Mujhe Tanha Nahi Hone Deta.

1
Kam Se Kam Hai Yeh Teri Shan Ke Tera Nokar.
Itan Qadir Hai Sawera Nahi Hone Deta.
Bas Yehi Soch Ke Main Lait Giya Zair-e-Lahad.
Zikar-e-Shabir Andhera Nahi Hone Deta.

2
Mujh Pe Rehti Hai Habib Ibn-e-Muzahir Ki Nazar.
Yeh Aqeedah Mujhe Burha Nahi Hone Deta.
Jhoom Kar Sab Mere Mula Ka Lagao Narah.
Yeh Amal Qabar Mein Goonga Nahi Hone Deta.

3
Barh Gayi Shan Meri Naam-e-Ali Laite Howay.
Zikar Yeh Kam Mera Rutba Nahi Hone Deta.
Karbala Mujh Ko Bula Karb-o-Bala Ke Khaliq.
Kya Hai Jo Tera Iradah Nahi Hone Deta.

4
Tujh Ko Kehte Howay Mabood Theher Jata Hoon.
Mujh Ko Mushrik Tera Sajdah Nahi Hone Deta.
Meharbaan Itna Hai Mujh Par Mera Mula Raqib.
Kabhi Khali Mera Kasa Nahi Hone Deta.
