Haye Darya Pe Howa Qatal Alamdar-e-Hussain.
Tham Kar Roye Kamar Apni Shehanshah-e-Ummam.

1
Dil Yeh Pehle Hi Bahatar Ka Uthaye Hoye Gham.
Noha Kartay They Yehi Kartay Hoye Sheh Matam.
Zeen Se Ghorey Ki Jab Ghazi Alamdar Girey.
Khak Par Tham Ke Dil Syed-e-Abrar Girey.

2
Keh Ke Yeh Ahl-e-Haram Sare Dil Afgar Girey.
Aankh Mein Hazrat-e-Abbas Alamdar Ke Teer.
Our Ik Aankh Mein Khoon Dekh Keh Roye Hain Shabeer.
Bhai Iss Haal Mein Dekha Hai Tujhe Yeh Taqdeer.

3
Haye Sakina Tere Gohar Ki Khuda Khair Karey.
Haye Zainab Teri Chadar Ki Khuda Khair Karey.
Haye Ab Abid-e-Muztar Ki Khuda Khair Karey.
Kehte They Hazrat-e-Shabeer Mere Dil Ki Aas.

4
Mujh Ko Ik Bar Zara Bhai Pukaro Abbas.
Kehte Hi Bhai Mere Ghazi Ki Hai Ruk Gayi Sans.
Kartay They Peet Ke Sar Noha Yehi Maula Hussain.
Meri Dharas Tha Yehi Murtaza Ka Noor-e-Ain.

5
Ho Gaya Qatal Mera Bhai Nahi Dil Ka Chain.
Matam Hazrat-e-Abbas Bapa Kartay Raho.
Aye Zahoor Our Saleem Ab Yeh Azza Kartay Raho.
Yaad-e-Abbas Mein Yeh Keh Ke Buka Kartay Raho.
