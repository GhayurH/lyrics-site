Haye Ridawaan Lay Gayiyaan Bas Rondiyaan Mawaan Reh Gayiyaan
Rait Zakhma Toon Hatawan Layi Hawawaan Reh Gayiyaan
Lutya Aye Zalima Ne Tere Veer Di Abba Nu
Takni Aan Nehar De Wal Kadi Naizay Te Rida Nu
Aoundiyaan Maqtal Choon Ghazi Khali Rahwaan Reh Gayiyaan
Har Qaidi Karbala Da Aye Wain Sun Ke Roya
Mere Samne Akbar Da Sir Saang Te Paroya
Maa Teri Mar Gayi Aye Akbar Baqi Sanwaan Reh Gayiyaan
Sir Te Rida Nayi Zainab Pehray Te Aa Gayi Aye
Wich Karbala De Ghazi Ban Ke Wakha Gayi Aye
Nayi Riya Dunya Te Ghazi Par Wafawaan Reh Gayiyaan
Raitaan Che Lal Rul Gaye Phul Sehriyaan De Dul Gaye
Ik Wela Aisa Aaya Gham Bachriyaan De Bhul Gaye
Takdiyaan Shabir De Sir Nu Nighawaan Reh Gayiyaan
Ik Dhi V Labdhi Payi Aye Maqtal Che Piyo Da Seena
Onnay Qaid De Wich Marna Odha Naam Aye Sakina
NAJFI Kyun Ajar-e-Risalat Wich Sazawaan Reh Gayiyaan
