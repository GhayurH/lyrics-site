1
Dhiyan Des Paraye Wekhiya Babal Aakhri Wari.
Hun Nayi Disna Loko Dhiyaan Nu Zindagi Sari.
Watna Te Maa Da Qatal Hoya Wich Kofay Baba Tera.
Hun Wela Aa Gaya Aye Karan Sham Di Main Teyari.

2
Odhay Zakhmi Babay Hukam Dita Zainab Nu Rook Le Ghazi.
Mere Jondiyaan Qayam Rawway Meri Dhi Di Pardah Dari.
Meri Maa Layi Baad Nabi Baba Tusaan Bait Al-Huzan Banaya.
Mainu Rondiyaan Wekhay Gi Wich Sham De Bheer Bazari.

3
Mere Sir Te Wekh Rida Baba Bara Roya Aakhri Welay.
Aj Main Zainab Baba Tere Kafan Taun Chadar Wari.
Wich Kafan De Piyo Aj Zainab Da Najfi Ik Wain Sunawaan.
Onay Wekhiya Aye Raj Ke Babal Nu Pehli Wari.
