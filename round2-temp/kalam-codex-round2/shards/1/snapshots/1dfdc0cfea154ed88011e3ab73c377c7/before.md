Kal Door Ali Da Si Wich Kofe Amerana.
Aj Kofay De Wich Zainab Aayi Aye Aseerana.

1
Babay Di Ameeri Si Beti Di Aseeri Aye.
Oh Door Si Shahana Aye Haal Faqeerana.
Darwazay Te Zindaan De Ik Qaidi Namazi Aye.
Din Raat Karey Sajday, Sajday Wich Shukrana.

2
Raftar Taun Abid Di Disda Aye Nawa Qaidi.
Guftar Karey Zahir Andaz Sharefana.
Hokay Bhar Bhar Ke Te Gash Kar Gayi Ro Ro Ke.
Sunrya Jad Zainab Ne Darbar Da Talbana.

3
Shabbir Di Ya Maula Humsheer Na Mar Jaway.
Ik Dam Hai Muhari Da Sab Dais Hai Begana.
Nezay Te Veer Da Sir Sang Bhainr Hai Be Chadar.
Shabbir Shama Rab Di Humsheer Hai Parwana.

4
Halat Dil-e-Madar Di Tu Jane Khudawanda.
Jis Ne Ali Akbar Nu Ban Wekhay Na Gana.
Wich Hashar Nisar Oday Reh Janday Kayi Parday.
Chadar Jay Koi Muslim Kar Denda Jay Nazrana.
