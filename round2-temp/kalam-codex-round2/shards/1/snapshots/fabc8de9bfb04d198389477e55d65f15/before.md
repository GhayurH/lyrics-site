Jashan Khum Ke Maidaan Mein Saj Gaya Wilayat Ka.
Khud Qasida Parhte Hain Mustafa Wilayat Ka.

1
Dast-e-Murtaza Lekar Kehtey They Nabi Logo.
Tum KO Manna Ho Ga Faisla Wilayat Ka.
Apne Our Paraye Ki Ho Gayi Parkh Pal Mein.
Zikar Jab Bhi Mehfil Mein Chir Gaya Wilayat Ka.

2
Daar Par Bhi Hans Hans Kar Manqabat Woh Parhte Hain.
Jin Ke Ho Dil-o-Jaan Se Wasta Wilayat Ka.
Apni Hajatein Lekar Tum Najaf Chaley Jao.
Har Pehar Hi Rehta Hai Dar Khula Wilayat Ka.

3
Aane Dey Waiz Zara Askar Ke Betey Ko.
Phir Zamana Lautay Ga Dekhna Wilayat Ka.
Jo Haram Khatay Hain Jo Haram Peetay Hain.
Woh Tu Chakh Nahi Patay Zaiqa Wilayat Ka.

4
Haq Ke Aashiyaney Tak Pohanchna Jo Chaho Tum.
Tumko Le Ke Jayega Rasta Wilayat Ka.
Jo Fidak Ke Choron Ke Hami Hain Kaho Unse.
Mil Raha Hai Haider Se Silsila Wilayat Ka.

5
Hum Wilayati Hain Yeh Daway Loag Karte Hain.
Mantay Nahi Hain Kyun Woh Kaha Wilayat Ka.
Jo Bura Bhala Har Dam Marjiyat Ko Kehtey Hain.
Woh Samajh Nahi Paye Qaida Wilayat Ka.

6
Bad-e-Mustafa Kaisa Waqt Aa Gaya Ghar Ghar.
Dars Deney Jati Tumhein Fatima Wilayat Ka.
Daad Tu Afraz Ko Mil Hi Jaye Gi Magar.
Munkiro Ko Chubhta Hai Tazkira Wilayat Ka.
