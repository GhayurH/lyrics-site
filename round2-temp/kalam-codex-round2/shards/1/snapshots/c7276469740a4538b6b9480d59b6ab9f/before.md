Dhiyaan watan te mangan dua
Moosa Kazim watan te aa

1
Satwan laal wilayat da

2
Dinni aaram na raat koon chain aey
Har dhi rowndi aey kar kar vain aey
Har wailay rownda aey veer Raza

3
Karryan walay hik qaidi da
Cha tur paye mazdoor janaza
Rakhya pul Bughdad te ja

4
Pul te mayyat be waris qaida
Koi nae rowan wala jainda
Kalli rowndi aey maa Zahra

5
Aey Abbas ko baoon armaan aey
Maula tera tang zindan aey
Kar sean keewain namaz ada

6
Dohrra ----
Kul dunya toon hik maslay da
Thi Parwaz jawab aaj mangda aey
Ath pahar musalal koi qaidi
Zindan wich nae kahrr sakda aey
Wich qaid khanay tay qaidi di
Ik chak wee aukha lagta aey
Jainday chowda saal rukoo wich guzray
Dasso qaidi kahrray was da aey
-
Hay Abbas koon baoon armaan aey
Maula tera tang zindaan aey
Kar sain kiwain namaz ada
