Hai Anbiya Ka Wazifa Ali Ali Karna
Har Imtehaan Mein Hamesha Ali Ali Karna

1
Munafiqon Ko Muhibon Ko Rishte Daroon Ko
Bula Ke Khum Mein Sawa Lakh Jaan Nisaron Ko
Liya Rasool Ne Wadah Ali Ali Karna

2
Aye Logo Mere Janaze Ke Sath Chalte Howay
Mere Badan Ko Lahad Ke Spurd Karte Howay
Hai Ilteja Meri Har Ja Ali Ali Karna

3
Kaha Yeh Nooh Ne Khud Se Pohanch Ke Sahil Par
Safina Rakhte Howay Bhi Doob Jata Magar
Mujhe Bacha Giya Mera Ali Ali Karna

4
Aye Jane Wale Haram Ke Yeh Mashwarah Hai Mera
Jo Chahta Hai Tera Haj Kare Qabool Khuda
Tu Ja Ke Khana-e-Kaba Ali Ali Karna

5
Zaban-e- Mir Pe Akbar Yehi Tu Narah Hai
Zamane Bhar Ko Yehi Mashwarah Humarah Hai
Koi Jo Roke Zyadah Ali Ali Karna
