1
Jibreel Ka Narah Hai Ali Un Wali Ullah Mir Hasan Mir.
Nabiyon Ne Pukara Hai Ali Un Wali Ullah.
Gar Tum Na Samajh Pao Tu Khud Nooh Se Pocho.
Tofaan Mein Kinara Hai Ali Un Wali Ullah.

2
Chero Gay Jo Mahool Mein Phailein Gay Ujalay.
Norani Tarana Hai Ali Un Wali Ullah.
Maidan-e-Ghadeer Aaya Tu Bolay Yeh Payamber.
Thehro Thehro Thehro Mujhe Kehna Hai Ali Un Wali Ullah.

3
Gar Iss Se Phiray Tum Tu Namazein Bhi Hain Batil.
Eman Ka Kaba Hai Ali Un Wali Ullah.
Hum Murdah Zameeron Se Mokhatib Hi Nahi Hain.
Woh Bolay Jo Zindah Hai Ali Un Wali Ullah.

4
Darwaza-e-Janat Ki Taraf Jana Na Waiz.
Darwazay Pe Likha Hai Ali Un Wali Ullah.
Jholay Mein Mujhe Maa Ne Meri Loriyaan De Kar.
Bachpan Se Sikhaya Hai Ali Un Wali Ullah.

5
Quran Ki Aayat Hoon Yaan Nehj-e-Balaga.
Inn Sab Ka Khulasa Hai Ali Un Wali Ullah.
 Hamid Kissi Mushkil Ko Samjhta Nahi Mushkil.
Jab Iss Ka Wazeefa Hai Ali Un Wali Ullah.
