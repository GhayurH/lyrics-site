Kaise jiye gi Sughra Akbar teray baghair
Mar jaye gi ye dukhya Akbar teray baghair

1
Shadi pe tum bulana Sughra ne jab kaha
Hamsheer se ye bolay hamshakl e Mustafa
Hamshakl e Mustafa
Pehnay ga kaise sehra Akbar teray baghair

2
Baba tumhe qasam hay tab khat ko kholna
Bhayya se meray baba bas itna bolna
Bas itna bolna
Roti hay teri behna Akbar teray baghair

3
Khaime ke dar pe sheh ki hamsheer ne kaha
Maqtal may ja ke Mazhar Shabbir ne kaha
Shabbir ne kaha
Veeran hay ye dunya Akbar teray baghair
