Fizza Menda Akhri Vela Hay Hasnain Day Maa Hon Tay Banana Ay.

Karbal Ton Ghin Kay Shaam Tayeen  Zainab Di Rida Hon Ta Bananay .

Matan Kayn Dor Ich Sar Zainab Tay Chaan Veeran Di Thi Khatam Wanjay
Pathran Day Masum Vich Eiday  Saar Tay Saya Hun Tayn Bananay.

Ashoor Di Luti Howi Zainab Tay Jadan Sham-e-Ghariban Lah Paway
Jeewain Putar Ko Chaway Sholayan Vich  Ondi Ain Gawah Hun Tayn Bananay.

Jadan Chok Tay Dhair Hajom Howay Mendi Bhen Banaween Rah Eida
Bazaar Ich Aasra Ujri Da  Vish Shaam Day Raah Hun Tayn Bananay.

Jadan Shaam Ich Zakham Milay Ainja Saan Ghutan Lagay Ainday Nasir
Maindi Tarfon Jholi Paain Tu  Einday Aman Di Jaa Hun Tayn Bananay.
