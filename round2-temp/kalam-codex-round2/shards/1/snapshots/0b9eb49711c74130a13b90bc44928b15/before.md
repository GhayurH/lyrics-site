Haye Haider e Karrar Mukhtar Hussain Fatehpuri

1
Zakhmi Howa Masjid Mein Payamber Ka Alamdar Haye Haider-e-Karrar.
Lo Chal Gayi Talwar Katney Lagi Dastar Haye Haider-e-Karrar.
Rangeen Hoi Mehrab Wilayat Ke Lahu Se.
Islam Ke Ghar Mein Chali Eman Pe Talwar Haye Haider-e-Karrar.
Beti Ne Kaha Baba Ki Ho Khair Aye Ya Rab.
Kyun Sar Se Giri Jati Hai Chadar Meri Har Bar Haye Haider-e-Karrar.

2
Amma Ke Janazay Ko Bhi Uthtey Hoye Dekha.
Ab Zainab-o-Kulsoom Hain Baba Ki Azadar Haye Haider-e-Karrar.
Yeh Chom Ke Kehte They Ali Bazu-e-Zainab.
Inn Bazuon Ko Rasiyon Se Bandhein Gay Agyar Haye Haider-e-Karrar.
Aaya Lab-e-Haider Pe Salam Aakhri Jis Dam.
Sar Peet Ke Kehne Lagay Maula Ke Sab Ansar Haye Haider-e-Karrar.

3
Ashor Ka Manzar Hai Nigahon Mein Ali Ki.
Abbas Ko Ro Ro Ke Banatay Hain Alamdar Haye Haider-e-Karrar.
Hasnain Ne Baba Ke Janazay Ko Uthaya.
Ik Arsh Ke Kandhey Pe Hai Ik Arsh Namu Daar Haye Haider-e-Karrar.
Masjid Mein Ali Taig Na Sajday Mein Jo Khatay.
Tauheed Na Youn Aati Nazar Majid-o- Mukhtar Haye Haider-e-Karrar.
