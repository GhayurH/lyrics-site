Jo Puchiye Keh Kya Hain Fatima Karam Ki Inteha Hain Fatima.
Hijab-e-Kibriyai Hai Kisi Ki Kab Rasai Hai Keh Pardah-e-Khuda Hain Fatima.

1
Woh Shamil-e-Dorod Hain Woh Dakhil-e-Dorod Hain.
Woh Manzil-e-Dorod Hain Woh Qabil-e-Dorod Hain.
WOh Hasil-e-Dorod Hain Woh Hamil-e-Dorod Hain.
Keh Markaz-e-Kisa Hain Fatima.

2
Ayan Hain Woh Nihan Hain Woh Zamaan Hain Woh Makaan Hain WOh.
Sada-e-Kun Fakaan Hain WOh Jahan Khuda Wahan Hain Woh.
Zameen Hai Aadmi Ki Maan Zamen Ki Bhi Maa Hain Woh.
Keh Fatir-Al-Sama Hain Fatima.

3
Jo Panjtan Simat Gaye Tu Aik Zaat Ban Gayi.
Woh Aik Zaat Phail Kar Yeh Kainat Ban Gayi.
Yeh Kainat Saj Gayi Har Aik Baat Ban Gayi.
Pata Chaal Keh Kya Hain Fatima.

4
Woh Qool Hain Maqala Hain Woh Madin-e-Risala Hain.
Ali Ki Hum Niwala Hain Ali Ki Hum Pyala Hain.
Hussain Ka Hawala Hain Hasan Ko Jis Ne Pala Hai.
Woh Pak Syeda Hain Fatima.

5
Risalatein Wilayatein Imamatein Hidayatein.
Sharafatein Najabatein Taharatein Shahadatein.
Karamatein Sadaqatein Ibadatein Riazatein.
Inhi Ka Tu Sira Hain Fatima.

6
Woh Jalwa-e-Gayab Hain Woh Noor Ki Kitab Hain.
Khuda Ka Woh Hijab Hain Khuda Ka Woh Niqab Hain.
Keh Juz Nabi-o-Juz Ali Kahan Kisi Ko Taab Hai.
Khuda Ka Aaina Hain Fatima.

7
Ossi Keh Hain Yeh Behar-o-Bar Oss Ke Hain Yeh Khushk-o-Tar.
Ossi Ke Hain Shajar Hajar Ossi Ke Hain Gul-o-Samar.
Karon Kuch Our Mukhtasir Khuda Tu Woh Nahi Magar.
Khuda Se Kab Juda Hain Fatima.

8
Meri Sada Sun Aye Khuda Dilon Mein Sab Ke Noor Rakkh.
Keh Sarwar-O- Navaid Ko Munafiqat Se Door Rakh.
Kasafaton Se Door Rakh Keh Hazir-o-Huzoor Rakh.
Hamara Wasta Hain Fatima.
