1
Dar-e-Abbas Pe Jo Sar Ko Jhukaya Main Ne.
Khud Ko Phir Farsh Nahi Arsh Pe Paya Main Ne.
Khud Wafaein Mere Ghar Ka Tawaf Karne Lagin.
Jab Se Ghazi Ka Alam Ghar Pe Sajaya Main Ne.

2
Mere Idrak Ne Sidrah Ki Bulandi Ko Chowa.
Tab Qalam Midhat-e-Ghazi Mein Chalaya Main Ne.
Jabke Aankhon Ne Meri Dekha Hai Roz Tera.
Na Kahe Koi Keh Dekha Nahi Kaba Main Ne.

3
Meri Mati Mein Kahin Khak-e-Shifa Shamil Hai.
Rooz-e-Aashoor Tabhi Khon Bahaya Main Ne.
Mujh Ko Khud Bint-e-Paigamber Ne Dua Di Raqib.
Matam-e-Shah Mein Jab Hath Uthaya Main Ne.
