1
Har Khar o Khas Pe Rang Khushi Se Kali Ka Hai.
Dunya Mein Aaj Jashan Shabeeh-e-Nabi Ka Hai.
Akbar Ki Chomtay Hain Jabeen Uth Ke Khud Hussain.
Kya Laskaron Mein Aisa Sipahi Kissi Ka Hai.

2
Akbar Ki Shakal Mein Hai Yeh Rajat Rasool Ki.
Atharah Saal Door Bhi Paygambari Ka Hai.
Hum Ja Girey Jo Maut Pe Nam-e-Hussain Par.
Yeh Faisla Qaza Ka Nahi Zindagi Ka Hai.

3
Youn Karwaan Khamosh Hai Rehbar Ke Hukam Par.
Jo Faisla Diye Ka Wohi Roshni Ka Hai.
Matam Ki Aa Rahi Hai Sada Chand Raat Se.
Haan Haan Yeh Ghar Hussain Ke Ik Matami Ka Hai.

4
Jis Ne Liya Nahi Hai Zabaan Se Ali Ka Naam.
Rooz-e-Azal Se Zaiqa Oss Phal Ka Pheka Hai.
Hum Shakal-e-Mustafa Ki Sana Kar-e-Al-Amaan.
 Akbar Yeh Kam Phool Pe Sheesha Gari Ka Hai.
