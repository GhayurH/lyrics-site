1
Jab Tak Ali Akbar Ki Maa Zinda Rahi Roti Rahi.
Kariyal Jawaan Ki Maut Thi Khud Zindagi Roti Rahi.
Akbar Tu Marne Ki Raza Le Kar Chaley Shabeer Se.
Maa Phir Bhi Jeene Ki Dua Deti Rahi Roti Rahi.

2
Maidaan Ko Jab Akbar Chaley Maa Ne Duaein Bhar Ke.
Jab Tak Nazar Aaya Pisar Dar Pe Khari Roti Rahi.
Lash-e-Pisar Par Jis Ghari Shabeer Ne Giriya Kiya.
Shimar Laeen Hansta Raha Roh-e-Nabi Roti Rahi.

3
Maula Ne Jab Khainchi Sina Roney Laga Khoon Aasmaan.
Seene Mein Barchi Ki Anni Tooti Hoi Roti Rahi.
Beti Ka Khat Parhtay Rahe Betay Ke Lashay Par Khud Hussain.
Our Dukhiya Maa Maiyyat Se Haye Lipati Hoi Roti Rahi.

4
Shadi Na Bhai Ki Hoi Hasrat Na Pori Ho Saki.
Akbar Ki Rahoon Ko Ik Behan Haye Dekhti Roti Rahi.
Madar Ki Qismat Mein Sohail, Akbar Ka Gham Tanha Na Tha.
Waris Bhi Rukhsat Ho Gaya Be Warsi Roti Rahi.
