1
Jab Dil Ne Ya Hussain Kaha Joan Rizvi.
Jab Dil Ne Ya Hussain Kaha Tu Sakoon Mila.
Karb-o-Bala Mein Sajdah Kiya Tu Sakoon Mila.
Koi Elaj Hi Na Tha Dunya Mein Dhop Ka.

2
Main Zaar Zaar Rota Howa Karbala Gaya.
Jali Pe Aik Bosa Diya Tu Sakoon Mila.
Dil Tha Keh Thartharata Tha Mehshar Ke Khof Se.
Bunyad Jaisay Hil Gayi Ho Apne Jhof Se.

3
Hur Ka Qaseeda Main Ne Parha Tu Sakoon Mila.
Kabay Ka Dil Tha Haji Nahi Matami Milein.
Woh Chahta Tha Loag Issay Karbala Kahin.
Kala Ghilaf Orh Liya Tu Sakoon Mila.

4
Ja Saat Betay De Diye Maula Ne Jab Kaha.
Har Ik Hijab Loh Se Oss Dam Hata Diya.
Rahib Ne Khud Jo Dekh Liya Tu Sakoon Mila.
Hum Muflison Ka Maal Tu Ishq-e-Hussain Hai.

5
Sab Kuch Iss Aik Rah Pe Lotaney Mein Chain Hai.
Hum Ne Bichaya Farsh-e-Azza Tu Sakoon Mila.
Matam Ke Youn Nishan Merey Kam Aa Gaye.
Turbat Se Mujh Ko Le Ke Nakerain Chal Paray.

6
Pohancha Janaza Karb-o-Bala Tu Sakoon Mila.
Woh Phir Kabhi Urrey Ga Yeh Umeed Hi Na Thi.
Phir Aik Roz Pa Gaya Chokhat Hussain Ki.
Fitrus Dobarah Urney Laga Tu Sakoon Mila.

7
Kaisa Karam Zyarat-e-Ashora Ne Kiya.
Shajrah Jo Zalimo Ka Tha Hum Ko Bata Diya.
Unn Par Tabarah Hum Ne Kiya Tu Sakoon Mila.
Jis Din Se Sheh Ne Soye Hoye Ko Jagaya Tha.

8
Muztar Tha Soch Soch Ke Maqsad Hayat Ka.
Phir Maut Se Habib Mila Tu Sakoon Mila.
Karb-o-Bala Se Jis Ghari Guzra Koi Nabi.
Dil Muztarib Howa Ossay Thokar Koi Lagi.

9
Jis Dam Badan Se Khon Baha Tu Sakoon Mila.
 Akbar Ghubar-e-Sham Mein Dobi Thi Zindagi.
Aankhon Ko Na Gawar Thi Har Din Ki Roshni.
Shaban Ka Zahoor Howa Tu Sakoon Mila.
