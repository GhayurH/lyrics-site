Zalim di tak kay jafa, deway sada, sada nahi koyi aasra

1
Haey o medha mazloom baba dhee tedhi ku nay roan denday

2
Laash tay aan han pathar hatendi koul mekhu nahi ronay denday

3
1) Jain bhira tay bahou manr hay kyun ni aya teda veer Ghazi
Chacha turiya wal na hi muriya dhi tedi day eho wainr anday

4
2) Ro na bachri enjh kaar kay vain ay akhay ro ro Ghazi di bhen ay
Veer kus aye Shabbir kus gay pa kay rasiyan paye Shaam wenday

5
3) Na hi Qasim na raye jo Akbar lutt gaye ay hun saar di chadaar
Lagiyaan bhayi kiwain bhujaeen bay haya pay bau zulam dhendhi

6
4) Rasan pa kay jada medi sain ay ro ro akhay baba Hussain ay
Jhinkay jhaliyan turr Shaam chaliyan kol teday nay rouhn denday

7
5) Khakan saar wich baba rowaiyan laash tedi tay aay satiyaan
Waikh maidi rukhsar zakhmi daard mekhu nay soun denday

8
6) Hy o zalim meday durr lahavay konr lutday meday durr bachaway
Chacha waliya na baba waliya dhi tedi koo jhinka na denday
