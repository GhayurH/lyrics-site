Elahi Khair Howay Sham De Bazaraan Wich Batool Zadiyaan Sir Nangay Aa Gayiyaan.
Jinhan De Sir Te Howay Chaan Atharaan Veraan Di Ki Waqt Aa Gaya Rasiyan Oh Pa Gayiyaan.

1
Ali Taun Lainra Aye Jis Jis Ne Badla Aa Jao Bazar-e-Kofa De Wich Jis Ghari Ayelan Hoya.
Oh Hoi Pathraan Di Barish Keh Fi Aman ALLAH Te Zakhmi Phophiyaan Nu Abid Taun Na Pachan Hoya.
Dhiyaan Ali Diyaan Zakhma Che Choor Ho Ho Ke Sab Apne Baap De Badlay Chuka Gayiyaan.
Fizza Di Kand De Pichay Munh Luka Ke Rondi Aye Hazaran Lokaan De Majmay Che Aoun Di Amari.

2
Turi Madine Taun Aye Naal Apne Veran De Te Gaud Ais Namari Di Karbala Ujari.
Kadi Na Utray Jehra Fatima Diyaan Jaiyaan Nabi De Deen Nu Oh Rang La Gayiyaan.
Agar Na Dendiyan Parday Ali Diyaan Dhiyaan Te Deen Sham Taun Pehlaan Hi Mar Gaya Honda.
Ewein Batool De Gulshan Nu Lutiya Lokaan Ne Quran Pak V Ewein Bikhar Gaya Honda.

3
Nabi De Deen Di Beri Kadon Di Dub Jandi Aye Beri Deen Di Kanday Te La Gayiyaan.
Main Naal Bhainra De Darbar Wich Khaloti Aan Karandi Veer Da Chehlum Jay Karbala Hondi.
Kafan Khareed Ke Veran Main Nahin Pawa Sakdi Main Kajdi Lash Mere Sir Te Jay Rida Hondi.
Nabi Di Akhiyaan Da Akhtar Piya Aye Raitaan Te Razawan Rab Diyaan Ki Din Wakha Gayiyaan.
