1
De Rahi Thi Bint e Haider Khutba Bazar Mein Asghar Khan
Ummat Ne Youn Bhulaya Rutba Bazar Mein.
Sang Bari Ho Rahi Thi Fizza Bhi Ro Rahi Thi.
Na Tha Lahoye Abid Rukta Bazar Mein.

2
Quran Ke They Qari Our Kalma Go Nabi Ke.
Chadar Hi De Sakay Na Maqna Bazar Mein.
Sab Bibiyaan Thin Kehtin Sajjad Khoon Na Ro Tum.
Umm-e-Rubab Par Tha Sakta Bazar Mein.

3
Majmay Mein Shamiyon Ke Dekhi Jo Aal-e-Muztar.
Islam Kar Raha Tha Giriya Bazar Mein.
 Akhtar Woh Moor Aaya Muhari Ko Gash Jo Aaya.
Badla Gaya Tha Rang Bhi Kurta Bazar Mein.
