Kafir Howa Dunya Ka Musalman Ali AS Ka Ammar Yasir Magsi.
Ahsan Ali Ka Hai Yeh Ahsan Ali Ka.

1
Juzdan Ki Manind Hai Gar Qalb-e-Muhammad.
Juzdan Hai Quran Ka, Quran Ali Ka.
ALLAH Ne Banaya Hai Mujhe Khak-e-Najaf Se.
Insan Hoon Main Our Hoon Insan Ali Ka.

2
Dawa Hai Basad Naaz Ana Abdo Muhammad.
Hai Naat Muhammad Ki Yeh Ayelan Ali Ka.
Milna Ho Tu Kar Leta Hoon Bismillah Tilawat.
 Ammar Pata Kitna Hai Aasan Ali Ka.
