kaisay galay pe tere khanjar mein chalta dekhoon, aakhir Hussain maan hoon…

Gardan pe rakhe teri, apna gala kataa doon, aakhir Hussain maan hoon…

Jab zeen se giro tum kahin chot lag na jae.

maqtal ki sar zameen se  pathar toh main hataadoon akhir Hussain….

Momin jo kar rahe hai din raat Hussain ka gham.

kaisay main matami ko  jannat mein bhool jaoo, akhir Hussain…

Chaaron taraf se zaalim barsa rahe thay pathar

nazak badan mein kese  zakhmon say choor dekhu, akhir Hussain…
