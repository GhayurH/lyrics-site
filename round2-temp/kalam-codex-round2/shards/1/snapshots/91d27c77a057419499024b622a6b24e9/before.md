1
Ghazi Nu Bulaye Koi Ghazi Nu Bulaye
Aashoor Nu Wain Hussain Da Aye Main Kala Manzal Okhi Aye Abbas Madad Nu Aye.
Asghar Layi Ban Wich Choti De Main Aap Hi Qabar Banayi Aye.
Mere Jigar De Tukre Di Maiyat Hathan Naal Qabar Che Layi Aye.

2
Asghar Masoom De Chehre Nu Oh Rait De Naal Chupaye.
Akbar De Seene Cho Barchi Main Hosla Par Ke Khich Layi Aye.
Oday Jigar De Tukriyan Nu Tak Ke Yak Lakht Zaieefi Aa Gayi Aye.
Aydi Lash Uth Ke Naal Mere Wich Khaimiyaan De Le Aye.

3
Ayho Sochda Si Shabir Jadon Chaliya Khanjar Oddi Gardan Te.
Mere Bajon Koan Sanbhale Ga Be Waris Wasi Khaimiyaan De.
Hun Koan Way Jehra Zainab Di Chadar Nu Aan Bachaye.
Kis Welay Qasid Aaya Aye Sughra Da Haal Sunawan Layi.

4
Mere Koal Te Kuj V Bachiya Nayi Iss Waqt Jawab Likhawan Layi.
Akbar Di Thaan Te Ays Khat Da Oh Aap Jawab Likhaye.
Kayi Waar Main Aakhiya Qasid Nu Main E Baba Waan Sughra Da.
Oh Aakhda Sughra Dasiya Si Pehre Te Ghazi Howay Ga.

5
Oh Nahi Manda Shabir Humein Onno Aap Yaqeen Dilaye.
 Saqlain Abbas Di Banwaan Da Bara Maan Si Syed Zadiyaan Nu.
Har Bibi Ayho Kendi Si Jadon Ghaira Pay Giya Khaimiyaan Nu.
Abbas Honda Te Muk Jande Ghurbat Di Shaam De Saye.
