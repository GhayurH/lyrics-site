Ik Qaidi Beriyaan Tauq De Wich Bazar Guzarda Jaway Par Sir Na Apna Chaway.
Har Moor Te Hole Hole Oh Na Ghazi Da Dohraway Par Sir Na Apna Chaway.

1
Ainj Babay Qabar Banai Si Ainj Asghar Nu Dafnaya.
Aye Dasda Dasda Phophiyaan Nu Piya Bhain Di Qabar Banaway Par Sir Na Apna Chaway.
Babay Da Adab Nibhawan Layi Jadoon Qafila Raat Nu Rukda.
Jis Sang Te Sir Aye Babay Da Othay Aap Peshani Laway Par Sir Na Apna Chaway.

2
Aye Tang Galiyaan Haye Sham Diyaan Utton Bheer Bazar Che Chokhi.
Menu Rasta De Diyo Phophiyaan Layi Rat Ro Ke Aye Farmaway Par Sir Na Apna Chaway.
Tak Pak Ridawaan Naiziyaan Te Jee Khol Ke Ronraan Chanda Aye.
Ajay Phophiyaan De Sir Nangay Ne Ayho Soch Ke Neer Bahaway Par Sir Na Apna Chaway.

3
Choon Salaan Di Masooma Nal Kithe Nazraan Na Mil Jawan.
Odhay Shimar Ton Waal Chorawan Layi Odhay Samne Pay Oh Aaway Par Sir Na Apna Chaway.
Aye Sham Di Mushkil Ton Wadh Ke Akbar Aye Watan Di Mushkil.
Ki Guzari Sham Che Lokaan Nu Piya Abid Aap Sunaway Par Sir Na Apna Chaway.
