1
Dhal Gayi Aye Sham Ghareban Te Lay Aaya Naal Hanera.
Ik Nezay Te Chadar Meri Ik Nezay Te Sir Tera.
Muk Raat Gayi Aye Nawin Di Ais Raat Ne V Muk Jana.
Mangni Aan Duawaan Na Wekhaan Iss Raat Da Veer Sawera.

2
Ainj Muk Diyaan Khushiyaan Nayi Takiyaan Ais Raat Ne Aan Mokayiyaan.
Aidhey Daman Wich Sir Qasim Da Raitaan Wich Chamkay Sehra.
Koi Saang De Neray Le Jao Tainu Wekh Sakina Rondi Aye.
Meri Akhiyaan Te Haye Neel Paye Doron Nayi Disda Chehra.

3
Meri Chadar Nu Lehranday Ne Main Tere Sir Nu Wekhdi Rayi.
Meri Kaun Rida Nu Hath Panda Jaye Veer Na Marda Mera.
Ais Raat Da Chan V Charhya Aye Humshakal-e-Rasool Da Sir Loko.
 Najfi Hun Sughra Mar Jana Nayi Pana Akbar Phera.
