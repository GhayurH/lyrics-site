1
Ghazi Diyaan Behna Wasdiyaan Pathraan Wich Tin Din Tain Turdiyaan Ayiaan.
Bazar De Loki Thak Gaye Nayi Thakiyaan Zahra Jaiyaan.
Inj Sham De Safar Wich Be Naam Raiyaan Qabraan.
Kis Ja Te Kera Digiya Nayi Mawaan Nu V Khabraan.
Bas Maa Zahra Aye Jandi Jine Lashaan Khud Dafnaiyaan.
Zainab Aye Naam Kis Da Zalim Ne Jad Aye Puchiya.
Zahra Di Nuwaan Apna Ewein Farz Nibhaya.
Main Zainab Haan Main Zainab Haan Raiyaan Kaindiyaan Aye Bharjaiyaan.
Bewa Behan De Dukh Nu Bas Veer Janda Aye.
Qasim Di Maut Tu Wadh Onno Gham Aye Kha Giya Aye.
Lokaan Abid De Samne Kubra Nu Rasiyaan Paiyaan.
Ghazi De Bazowaan Nu Shabir Jiwein Chumiya.
Waqt-e-Rehai Onno Jadon Lutda Maal Miliya.
Sajad Ne Owein Qaid Wich Chadran Akhiyaan Nal Laiyaan.
Qabar-e-Sakina utte Sajad Wain Kita.
Mere Sabar Te Mneu Aa Seene La Laye Baba.
Putraan Nu Dafan Te Kita Aye Tu Dhiyaan Nahi Dafnaiyaan.
Ghazi De Bad Bhaina Be Aasra Jo Hoiyaan.
Sir Nangay Majmiyaa Wich Tin Din Khaloti Raiyaan.
Jeri Maa Di Qabar Te Rowan Layi Adh Raat De Wailay Aaiyaan.
Akbar Di Maut Tu Wadh Abid Layi Dukh Si Akbar.
Maiyaat Sakina Di Jad Rakh Ke Qabar De Andar.
Zakhmi Gardan Te Hathaan Choon Hathaan Nal Rasiyaan Layiyaan.
