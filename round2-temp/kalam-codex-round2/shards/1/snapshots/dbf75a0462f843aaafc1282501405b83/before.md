1
Hai Yeh Noha Hussain o Hasan Ka Choor Kar Amma Zahra Na Jao.
Gham Se Phatta Hai Mera Kaleja Choar Kar Amma Zahra Na Jao.
Kyun Na Roein Ali Bhi Tarap Kar.
Kyun Na Roein Lahad Mein Payamber.
Sun Ke Shabir-o-Shabar Ka Noha.
Zulm Ki Inteha Hai Khudaya.
Jalta Dar Fatima pe Giraya.
Zakhmi Pehlu Howa Syeda Ka.
Ro Rahe Hain Sabhi Gham Ke Marey.
Syeda Fatima Ke Dularay.
Ghar Mein Har Samt Mehshar Th Barpa.
Dukhtar-e-Mustafa Ka Janaza.
Shab Ki Tareeki Mein Haye Utha.
Waye Zahra Ki Ghurbat Khudaya.
Aaj Bhi Kami-o- Laeeq ALLAH Gawah Hai.
Dhoop Mein Qabar-e-Khair-ul-Nisa Hai.
Dill Ko Tarpa Raha Hai Yeh Noha.
