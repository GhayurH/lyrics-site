Ali Ke Ghar Ka Ujala Hussain Ka Chehra
Janab-e-Zahra Ki Dunya Hussain Ka Chehra

1
Paron Ko Dekh Ke Fitrus Yeh Bola Man Misli
Meri Murad Hoi Pori Arsh Choney Ki
Jo Main Ne Cho Liya Maula Hussain Ka Chehra

2
Nawasa Pusht Se Nana Ki Jab Utar Aaya
Surror Kaisa Tha Na Jane Tool-e-Sajdah Ka
Nabi Ne Uthtey Hi Choma Hussain Ka Chehra

3
Tajali Mangney Barson Woh Toor Par Aaya
Magar Naseeb Keh Be Hosh Ho Gaya Mosa
Jee Bhar Ke Dekh Na Paya Hussain Ka Chehra

4
Sanad Hai Iss Ki Ziyarat Ke Rooz-e-Ashora
Kabhi Bana Hai Yeh Aadam Kabhi Bana Esa
Tha Aik Waqt Mein Kya Kya Hussain Ka Chehra

5
Batool Kehti Thin Baba Se Waqt Tu Aaye
Woh Haal Kar De Ga Baiyat Ka Apne Sajday Se
Laeen Ko Yaad Rahe Ga Hussain Ka Chehra

6
Haram Ki Chat Pe Khara Ba Wafa Yeh Farmaye
Yeh Kaba Chalta Howa Khud Udhar Chala Jaye
Jidhar Ko Ho Mere Maula Hussain Ka Chehra

7
Woh Khal-o-Khad Ko Banata Tha Chomta Tha Khud Hi
Khud Apne Noor Se Jis Waqt Apne Andar Hi
Khuda Tarash Raha Tha Hussain Ka Chehra

8
Kharey Hoye They Farishtay Paron Ko Phailaye
Kissi Tarha Humein Aab-e-Hayat Mil Jaye
Abhi Dholayegi Zahra Hussain Ka Chehra

9
Falak Se Bhaij Diya Tha Ossay Zameen Pe Magar
Janab-e-Fatima Zahra Ki Aankhon Se Akbar
Khuda Bhi Dekhta Rehta Hussain Ka Chehra
