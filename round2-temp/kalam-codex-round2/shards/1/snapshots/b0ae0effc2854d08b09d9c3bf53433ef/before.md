Jabeen Surk Hui Hai Syed Ali Ghazi Zaidi.
Sajday Mein Namazi Ki Jabeen Surkh Hoi Hai.

1
Yeh Taig Ali Par Nahi Ahmad Pe Chali Hai.
Kofa Ke Yateemo Ne Kiye Bain Yeh Ro Kar.
Ab Kis Se Kahin Ja Ke Humein Bhok Lagi Hai.
Aye Momino Mara Gaya Sardar Tumhara.

2
Jibreel Ki Janib Se Manadi Yeh Hoi Hai.
Sar Baba Ka Dekha Tu Hasan Ro Ke Pukarey.
Kofay Mein Bhi Tehreer-e-Nabi Chak Hoi Hai.
Galtaan Hai Lahu Mein Abu Talib Ka Dulara.

3
Hairat Se Khari Khalq-e-Khuda Dekh Rahi Hai.
Main Kaisay Bhulaon Merey Maula Shab-e-Zarbat.
Ghazi Se Yehi Taig-e-Ali Poch Rahi Hai.
Ik Saf Hai Malaik Ki Hussain Aap Ke Humrah.

4
Hathoon Se Sar-o-Seena Khari Peet Rahi Hai.
Taboot-e-Ali Par Yeh Alina Ki Dua Hai.
Sukh Paye Woh Maula Jo Tere Gham Mein Dukhi Hai.
