Ham ko hay apni jaan se pyara gham e Hussain
Qayam rahay jahan may khudaya gham e Hussain

1
Roz e alast poocha gaya kya pasand hay
Be ikhtiyar ham ne pukara gham e Hussain

2
Jab chaha Syeda ke qadam aayain ghar meray
Farsh e aza bichaya manaya gham e Hussain

3
Kam zarf ko jahan may mila jab bhi iqtidar
Pehlay bana siftam ka nishana gham e Hussain
