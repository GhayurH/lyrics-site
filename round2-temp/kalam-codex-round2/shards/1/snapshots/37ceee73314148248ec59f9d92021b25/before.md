1
Hussain Ker Dein Gy Shahzad Abbas.
Ba Hukam-e-Fatima Zahra Hussain Kar Dein Gay.
Har Aik Kam Khuda Ka Hussain Kar Dein Gay.
Kisay Khabar Thi Keh Zakhmi Jabeen Ke Sajday Se.
Zameen Ko Arsh-e-Mualla Hussain Kar Dein Gay.
Yeh Keh Ke Fitrus-e-Be Par Chala Soye Yasrab.
Main Janta Hoon Keh Acha Hussain Kar Dein Gay.
Jo Oss Ko Dekhein Tu Yousuf Bhi Rashk Karne Lagin.
Hasen Jaun Ko Itna Hussain Kar Dein Gay.
Yazid Sham Talak Sar Hi Sar Milein Gay Tujhey.
Jo Teri Foaj Pe Hamla Hussain Kar Dein Gay.
Main Bikne Aa Gaya Bazar-e-Ishq-e-Haider Mein.
Mujhe Khareed Ke Mehnga Hussain Kar Dein Gay.
Jo Kam Lakhon Rasolon Ko Mil Ke Karna Tha.
Woh Kam Dasht Mein Tanha Hussain Kar Dein Gay.
 Naeem Dekhna Khaimay Ke Gul Charagon Se.
Zamane Bhar Mein Ujala Hussain Kar Dein Gay.
