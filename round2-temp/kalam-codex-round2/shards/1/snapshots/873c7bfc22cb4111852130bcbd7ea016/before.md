Har Chehre Mein Aati Hai Nazar Yaar Ki Surat.
Ahbab Ki Surat Ho Keh Agyar Ki Surat.

1
Seene Mein Agar Sooz Salamat Ho Tu Khud Hi.
Ashar Mein Dhal Jati Hai Afkar Ki Surat.
Jiss Aankh Ne Dekha Tujhe Oss Aankh Ko Dekhon.
Hai Iss Ke Siwa Kya Tere Deedar Ki Surat.

2
Pehchan Liya Tujh Ko Teri Shesha Gari Se.
Aati Hai Nazar Fun Se Funkar Ki Surat.
Surat Meri Aankhon Mein Samaye Gi Na Koi.
Nazroon Mein Basi Rehti Hai Sarkar Ki Surat.

3
Ashkoon Ne Biyaan Kar Hi Diya Raaz-e-Tamana.
Hum Soch Rahe The Abhi Izhar Ki Surat.
Iss Khak Mein Posheeda Hain Har Rang Ke Khake.
Mitti Se Nikalte Hain Jo Gulzar Ki Surat.

4
Dill Hath Pe Rakha Hai Agar Koi Khareede.
Dekhon Tu Zara Main Bhi Kharidar Ki Surat.
 Wasif Ko Sar-e-Daar Pukara Hai Kissi Ne.
Inkar Ki Surat Hai Na Iqrar Ki Surat.
