1
Jag Ron Nayi Denda Haye Baba Tere Baad Aye Ki Din Aaye Ne.
Tere Kalma Go Agaan Le Ke Mere Ghar Nu Jalawan Aaye Ne.
Tere Dafan De Welay Karday Ray Haye Sazish Wich Saqeefay De.
Hun Aina Da Munh Rakhne Layi Aina Wakhrey Poor Wichaye Ne.

2
Turbat Te Pak Muhammad Di Payi Rondi Maa Hasnain Di Aye.
Baba Ki Puchna Aye Aina Zakhma Di Pehlu De Zakhm Sawaye Ne.
Tere Jiondiyaan Jee Munh Rakhday Si Haye Baad Ich Akhiyaan Phair Laiyaan.
Aj Kul-e-Eman Nu Kujh Loki Haye Qaidi Banawan Aaye Ne.

3
Sukh Chain Haram Kita Sada Zahra De Raat Diney Ronrey.
Ainu Shehroon Bahir Bhaij Diyo Lokaan Ne Hukam Sunaye Ne.
Zainab Di Maa Manzori Kar Hasnain De Sadqay Nohay Di.
Tere Haq De Marey Jawan Te Najfi De Athro Aaye Ne.
