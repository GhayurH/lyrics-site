1
Haal Akbar Ka Tujhe Kaisay Sunaon Sughra.
Na Raha Bhai Tera Kaisay Bataon Sughra.
Haye Pardais Mein Dekhi Hai Musibat Kaisi.
Oss Ke Seene Se Sina Main Ne Nikali Hai Abhi.
Dil Ka Jo Haal Hai Woh Kaisay Dikhaon Sughra.
Kash Yeh Pal Tere Baba Ko Nahi Hota Naseeb.
Larkharata Howa Aaya Hoon Main Akbar Ke Qareeb.
Kis Tarha Lash Main Kandhey Pe Uthaon Sughra.
Yeh Tu Acha Howa Tum Aayi Nahi Karb-o-Bala.
Haal Tum Dekh Nahi Patin Kabhi Akbar Ka.
Kaisay Akbar Se Main Ab Tujh Ko Milaon Sughra.
Ab Bhi Akbar Ke Kalejay Se Lahu Jari Hai.
Tere Bhai Pe Qayamat Ki Sitamgari Hai.
Kaisay Akbar Ka Kaleja Main Bhulaon Sughra.
Ab Na Atharah Baras Wala Kabhi Aaye Ga.
Palney Wali Se Kis Tarha Jiya Jaye Ga.
Kaisay Zainab Ko Main Yeh Baat Bataon Sughra.
Haye Kis Tarha Tere Baba Ko Ab Aaye Ga Chain.
Na Hai Abbas Na Qasim Hai Akela Hai Hussain.
Ab Madad Ke Liye Main Kis Ko Bulaon Sughra.
Haye Zeeshan-o- Ali Kaisa Woh Manzar Tha Ajab.
Shah Akbar Ke Janazay Ko Uthane Lagay Jab.
Bolay Kis Tarha Main Yeh Farz Nibhaon Sughra.
