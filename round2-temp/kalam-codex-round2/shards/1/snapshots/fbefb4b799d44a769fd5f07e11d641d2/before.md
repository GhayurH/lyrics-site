1
Hath Nayi Labde Qasim Dus Kiwein Tainu Mehndiyan Lawan.
Gathri Choon Kad Kad Ke Bas Tukray Jordi Jawaan.
Khab Si Dil Da Mere Aynj Tere Shagan Manandi.
Ghazi Nu Alam Phara Ke Baraat De Naal Turandi.
Hun Jinj Nahin Te Maiyyat Iss Shan De Naal Uthawaan.
Khak Che Mil Gaya Mere Armana Da Sarmaya.
Kujh Kujh Murya Aye Qasim Nahin Pora Wapis Aaya.
Mitiyaan Wich Rat Reh Gayi Sumbaan Wich Reh Gayiyaan Sahwaan.
Piyo Taweez Bandhaya Main Gana Manya Tera.
Phophiyaan Dastar Pawai Main Sehra Manya Tera.
Das Qasim Gathri Nu Kehrey Shagan De Naal Manawaan.
Maut Da Sun Ke Teri Onay Kalay Kharey Paye.
Mainu Aan Ke Ujari Kubra Bas Ayho Puchdi Jaway.
Main Kiwein Hathaan Taun Mehndi Da Rang Lahawan.
Mawaan Thandiyaan Chawaan Kenday Ne Dunya Walay.
Jiday Putar Nu Shagna Welay Koi Zalim Mar Mokaway.
Oss Ambari Layi Akbar Ki Tupaan Te Ki Chawaan.
