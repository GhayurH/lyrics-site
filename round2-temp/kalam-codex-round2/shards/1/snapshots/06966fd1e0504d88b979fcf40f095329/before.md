Habash Ki Malka Haye Fizza.

1
Kaisay Ujar Gayi Hai Izzat Maab Fizza.
Sadaat Ke Dukhon Ki Natiq Kitab Fizza.
Yasrab Mein Karbala Mein Kofa Mein Sham Mein Bhi.
Har Ja Batoliyat Ka Thehri Hijab Fizza.
Jab Dasht Mein Raha Na Shabeer Ka Koi Bhi.
Phir Murtajiz Ki Tu Ne Thami Rakab Fizza.

2
Darbar Mein Zaeefi Teri Lahu Lahu Hai.
Jazboon Pe Aa Gaya Hai Lekin Shabab Fizza.
Akbar Tumhari Shadi Jab Se Hoi Hai Ban Mein.
Tab Se Kiye Hoi Hai Khoon Se Khizab Fizza.
Akbar Watan Pe Aaye Sehra Mujhe Dikhaye.
Bichari Ke Dekhti Hai Turbat Mein Khuwab Fizza.

3
Mojon Mein Bas Gayi Hai Tashna Labi Tumhari.
Wallah Furat Ab Tak Hai Aab Aab Fizza.
Jab Se Tumhare Sar Ki Chadar Turab Thehri.
Oss Roz Se Ali Hai Bas Boo Turab Fizza.
 Bahlool Ko Batana Apna Kaneez Zadah.
Qayam Ke Wastay Se Rooz-e-Hisab Fizza.
