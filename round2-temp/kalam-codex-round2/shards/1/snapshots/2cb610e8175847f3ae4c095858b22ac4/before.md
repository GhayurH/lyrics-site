1
Eman Reza Jaan Saleem Mastan 2024
Eman Hai Eman Hai Eman Raza Jan.
Hai Koi Nahi Aap Sa Rehman Raza Jan.
Maa Baap Se Barh Kar Koi Dolat Nahi Hoti.
Maa Baap Mere Aap Pe Qurban Raza Jan.
Aap Apni Sakhawat Ke Mutabiq Humein De Dein.
Mangein Gay Tu Ho Jaye Ga Nuqsan Raza Jan.
Din Raat Malak Likhtay Rahe Ajar Qurani.
Din Raat Main Parhta Tha Raza Jan Raza Jan.
Chehray Se Agar Apne Naqab Aap Hata Dein.
Yousuf Bhi Kahin Gay Keh Main Qurban Raza Jan.
Jali Ko Teri Tham Ke Mangi Hai Dua Yeh.
Niklay Teri Chokhat Pe Meri Jan Raza Jan.
Main Kyun Kahon Sultan-e-Khurasan Raza Jan.
Dono Jahan Ke Aap Hain Sultan Raza Jan.
Masooma-e-Qum Ko Sabhi Hajaat Bata Dein.
Rad Hone Ka Koi Nahi Imkan Raza Jan.
Sadqa Unhein De Dein Gay Tu Kar Lein Gay Hakomat.
Kasa Liye Aaye Hain Suleman Raza Jan.
Kehta Hai Mujhe Kafir-o-Mushrik Yeh Zamana.
 Fazil Hai Tere Ishq Mein Mastan Raza Jan.
