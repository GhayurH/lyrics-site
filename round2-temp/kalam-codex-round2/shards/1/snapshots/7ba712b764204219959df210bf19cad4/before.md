1
LaIlaha Ki Do Jahan Mein Aabro Shabeer Hai.
Dar Haqeeqat Deen-e-Haq Ki Aarzu Shabeer Hai.
Anbiya Bhi Roz-o-Shab Kartay Hain Tera Tazkira.
Sakinan-e-Arsh Ki Bhi Guftgu Shabeer Hai.

2
Tere Dam Se Aaj Tak Zindah Hai Deen-e-Kibriya.
Sheh Rag-e-Islam Mein Tera Lahu Shabeer Hai.
Sadiyon Pehle Dekha Tha Ik Khuwab Ibrahim Ne.
Oss Haqeeqi Khuwab Ki Tabeer Tu Shabeer Hai.

3
Zair-e-Khanjar Dekh Kar Sajdah Yeh Hatif Ne Kaha.
Haan Meri Tauheed Ki Bas Justaju Shabeer Hai.
Deen-e-Haq Sajid Haqeeqat Bas Inhi Ka Faiz Hai.
Hai Namaz-e-Ishq-e-Haider Our Wozu Shabeer Hai.
