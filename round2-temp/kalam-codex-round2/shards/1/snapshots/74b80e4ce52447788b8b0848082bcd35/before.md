1
Dhondne Nikle The Jo Duniya Mein Haider Dosra.
Mil Nahi Paya Unhein Ab Tak Qalandar Dosra.
Hai Kahan Jibreel Jo Rizwan Laya Hai Libas.
Kya Ali Ne Rakh Liye Hai Ghar Mein Nokar Dosra.

2
Hum Tumhein Dikhlaein Gaye Yousuf Ali Ki Aal Mein.
Tum Na Dikhla Pao Gaye Nabiyon Mein Akbar Dosra.
Iss Ko Mara Hai Ali Ne Yeh Ali Ka Kaam Hai.
Dekh Lo Hai Pehle Hise Ke Baraber Dosra.

3
Aab Darwaze Se Jaein Kyun Khawas-e-Kibriya.
Ho Ga Kabe Mein Ali Ke Waste Dar Dosra.
Le Tu Aye Hain Saqeefa Wale Rehbar Dosra.
Laein Gaye Lekin Kahan Se Khum Ka Mimber Dosra.

4
Keh Rahi Hain Yeh Adaein Asghar-e-Be-Sheer Ki.
Chahiye In Muthiyon Ko Bab-e-Khaiber Dosra.
Dekh Kar Sajjad Ko Mir-o- Takallum Kehte Hain.
De Diya Shabir Ne Ghar Mein Sukhnwar Dosra.
