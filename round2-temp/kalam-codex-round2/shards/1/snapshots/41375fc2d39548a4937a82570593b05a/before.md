1
Kal Sehra Tainu Laya Aj Wain Terey Pawaan
Putraan De Baad Jag Te Kiwein Jondiyaan Ne Mawaan.
Tere Sehrey De Phul Qasim Lal Mera Kumlaye Nayi.
Banri Te Banray De Hathoon Loko Ganay Laye Nayi.

2
Ik Din Di Banri Nu Main Teri Lash Kinj Wakhawaan.
Lut Layi Aye Musalmana Bewah Di Kamai Aye.
Ainj Lash Koi Nayi Aayi Jiwein Lash Teri Aayi Aye.
Rondi Aye Aakh Farwa Qasim Main Sadqay Jawaan.

3
Rul Mehndi Gayi Qasim Sehra V Rul Gaya Aye.
Raitaan Uttay Hasan Da Aj Khon Dul Gaya Aye.
Tere Baad Mera Koi Nayi Kinu Lal Main Bulawaan.
Gathri Che Lash Aayi Kuhram Mach Gaya Aye.

4
Haye Lal Aj Hasan Da Dunya Te Na Riya Aye.
Jehray Reh Gaye Ne Banra Tere Shagan Main Manawaan.
Shagna De Welay Mawaan Bachray Sajandiyaan Ne.
Wich Karbala De Najfi Aj Wain Pandiyaan Ne.

5
Tere Sehrey De Phul Pa Ke Teri Lash Nu Sajawaan
Noha Khuwan Party Ch Faiyaz Party Sialkot.
