1
Haq Ka Irfan Jo Paya Tu Ali Yaad Aaya Mir Hasan Mir.
Dil Mein Quran Samaya Tu Ali Yaad Aaya.
Aadam-o-Nooh Payamber Hoon Ya Hazrat Esa.
Younus-o-Khizar Hoon Ilyas Keh Hazrat Mosa.
Waqt Inn Par Bhi Jo Aaya Tu Ali Yaad Aaya.
Ghar Tha ALLAH Ka Mojood Makeen Koi Na Tha.
Koi Aaye Ga Yahan Yeh Bhi Yaqeen Koi Na Tha.
Dar Jo Dewar Mein Aaya Tu Ali Yaad Aaya.
Sainkaron Sorma They Our Pehalwan Hazar.
Go They Khaybar Mein Sabhi Yaar Magar Mere Yaar.
Mard Ka Zikar Jab Aaya Tu Ali Yaad Aaya.
Jism Ke Aik Se Do Tukray Barabar Dekhey.
Khoon Ki Barsat Hoi Urtay Hoye Sar Dekhey.
Ran Mein Abbas Jab Aaya Tu Ali Yaad Aaya.
Naiza-o-Teer-o-Tabar Kya Koi Khanjar Na Mila.
Lakh Dhonda Kahin Kuffar Ka Lashkar Na Mila.
Saaf Maidaan Nazar Aaya Tu Ali Yaad Aaya.
Apna Kuch Hosh Na Tha Itni Pareshani Thi.
Gham-e-Dunya Ne Mujhe Ghair Rakha Tha Phir Bhi.
Hath Momin Se Milaya Tu Ali Yaad Aaya.
Youn Tu Main Ratta Raha Sare Sawal Our Jawab.
Par Nakerain Ne Turbat Mein Mujhe Behar-e-Hisab.
Aa Ke Jis Waqt Jagaya Tu Ali Yaad Aaya.
Zikar Nabiyon Ka Suna Khoob Yakkay Bar-e-Degar.
Main Nuseri Tu Nahi Khair Se Wallah Magar.
Zikar ALLAH Ka Aaya Tu Ali Yaad Aaya.
Jo Keh Aqwal-e-Payamber Ko Bhula Baithay They.
Hum Ali Ko Bhi Nahi Jantay Jo Kehte They.
Unn Ko Mitti Mein Dabaya Tu Ali Yaad Aaya.
Qalb-e-Batil Ko Kayi Tukron Mein Battay Dekha.
Takht Kya Cheez Hai Darbar Ulattay Dekha.
Khutba Zainab Ne Sunaya Tu Ali Yaad Aaya.
Shab-e-Mairaj Ajab Baat Hoi Yeh Gohar.
Sun Ke Aawaz-e-Khuda Chonk Uthay Peygamber.
Jab Nazar Kuch Nahi Aaya Tu Ali Yaad Aaya.
