1
Farwa Tere Qasim Nu Asghar Khan
Farwa Tere Qasim Nu Aj Mehndi Lawan Aayiyaan.
Haye Phophiyaan, Phophiyaan Rondiyaan Rondiyaan Aiday Shagan Manawan Aayiyaan.
Athro Dool Ke Zainab Ne Kujh Hawaan Apniyaan Paiyaan.
Sain Rubab De Hokiyan Ne Mehndi Te Moraan Laiyaan.
Fair Ral Ke, Ral Ke Sariyaan Bibiyaan Hathaan Te Sajawan Aayiyaan.
Kharey Charhya Qasim Te Mangi Maut Ne Aan Wadhayi.
Sir Nazrana De De Ke Sab Janjiyaan Wayil Karai.
Aj Horaan, Horaan Aidi Phori Nu Karbal Che Wachawan Aayiyaan.
Hanjwaan De Phul Chum Chum Ke Farwa Gathri Te Paway.
Nori Mukhra Banray Da Ambari Nu Nazar Na Aaway.
Jadon Bhaina, Bhaina Joor Ke Tukriyaan Nu Aidi Shakal Banawan Aayiyaan.
Asghar Oh Sarbala Aye Jidhay Teraan Ne Sah Toray.
Maut Nu Tak Ke Hasda Si Nayi Hath Masoom Ne Joray.
Aynj Bachray, Bachray Waar Ke Mawaan Ne Islam Banawan Aayiyaan.
Shadi Diyaan Rasma Nayi Ajay Qasim Poriyaan Hoiyaan.
Wekh Ke Zewar Shagna De Phophiyaan Ne Pit Pit Roiyaan.
Rab Janrey, Janrey Ujari Dulhan Nu Kiwein Rasiyaan Pawan Aayiyaan.
Doli De Wich Samar Na Bethi Ho Gayi Bewah Jehri.
Oss Mazloma Bewah Ne Sunr Layi Aye Arzi Teri.
Tere Nohay, Nohay Diyaan Satraan Ne Aye Momin Nu Rawawan Aayiyaan.
