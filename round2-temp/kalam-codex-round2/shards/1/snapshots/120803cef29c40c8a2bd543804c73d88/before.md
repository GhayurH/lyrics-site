1
Hazoor Aap Mere Mayi Baap An Data.
Hazoor Eid Ka Din Rooz Tu Nahi Aata.
Hazoor Aaj Tu Naz-r-Ali Niyaz-e-Rasool.
Hazoor Aap Ke Ghar Mein Ho Rehmaton Ka Nazool.

2
Hazoor aaj Mile Jaan-o-Maal Ki Khairat.
Hazoor Aap Ke Ahl-o-Ayaal Ki Khiarat.
Hazoor Ahmad-e-Mursal Ki Aal Ka Sadqa.
Hazoor Fatima Zahra Ke Lal Ka Sadqa.

3
Hazoor Aap Ke Baitay Ki Our Baho Ki Khair.
Hazoor Aap Ke Bache Jiein Phalein Pholein.
Hazoor Aap Ko Maula Sada Sukhi Rakhe.
Hazoor Aap Ki Jholi Khuda Bhari Rakhe.

4
Hazoor Naam-e-Khuda Kar-e-Khair Farmaein.
Hazoor Aap Ke Dil Ki Muradein Bar Aaein.
Hazoor Aaj Gadagar Ko Bheek Mill Jaye.
Hazoor Kab Se Khara Hoon Hath Phelaye.

5
Hazoor Aane Do Aane Ki Baat Hi Kya Hai.
Hazoor Aankhein Churane Ki Baat Hi Kya Hai.
Hazoor Meri Sadaon Pe Ghoor Tu Kijiye.
Faqeer Yeh Nahi Kehta Galay Laga Lijiye.
