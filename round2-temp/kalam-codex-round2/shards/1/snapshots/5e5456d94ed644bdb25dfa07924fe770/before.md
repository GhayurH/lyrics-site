1
Ghar Jalane Aye Hein Aqeel Abbas Makhraj.
Haye Zahra Ka Musalmaan Ghar Jalanay Aaye Hain.
Mustafa Ko Qabar Mein Bhi Yeh Rulanay Aaye Hain.
Iss Ko Pehchano Aye Logo Yeh Hai Bint-e-Mustafa.
Qabar-e-Zahra Ko Ali Kyun Kar Chupaney Aaye Hain.
Kal Ko Bakhin Keh Rahe They Haider-e-Karrar Ko.
Bandh Kar Rasi Mein Haider Ko Le Jane Aaye Hain.
Aap Se Dawa Wafadari Ka Jo Kartay Rahe.
Aaj Le Kar Woh Mere Ghar Taziyane Aaye Hain.
Haye Dar Jalta Howa Jab Syeda Par Gira.
Aag Se Hasnain Madar Ko Bachaney Aaye Hain.
Teri Zahra Apne Ghar Mein Ro Nahi Sakti Tujhey.
Haye Kaisay Ghurbaton Ke Yeh Zamane Aaye Hain.
Soch Kar Rota Hai Qambar They Woh Kaisay Kalma Go.
Kabay Ka Jo Ghar Tha Kaba Woh Giranay Aaye Hain.
