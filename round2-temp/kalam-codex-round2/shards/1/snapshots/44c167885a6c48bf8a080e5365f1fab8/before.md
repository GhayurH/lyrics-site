1
Fatima Fatima Mukhtar Hussain Fatehpuri.
Markaz-e-Hal Atta Fatima Fatima.
Aayat-e-Kibriya Fatima Fatima.
Bola Soraj Palat Kar Ali Ya Ali.
Chand Ne Di Sadaein Nabi Ya Nabi.
Tara Kehta Raha Fatima Fatima.
Waqt-e-Mushkil Parhi Sab Ne Nad-e-Ali.
Our Ali Par Pari Jab Bhi Mushkil Koi.
Bolay Mushkil Kusha Fatima Fatima.
Ho Gayin Jis Ghari Aayatein Aik Ja.
Oss Ki Nisbat Se Sab Ka Taruf Howa.
Roh-e-Ahl-e-Kisa Fatima Fatima.
Ho Gaya Shamil-e-Ahl-e-Bait Nabi.
Baat Salman Ko Jab Samajh Aa Gayi.
Hai Hijab-e-Khuda Fatima Fatima.
Naar Se Noor Ki Simt Hur Aa Gaya.
Aik Nuqtay Mein Mujrim Se Mehram Kiya.
Our Woh Nuqta Hai Kya Fatima Fatima.
Baat Quraan Ke Lehjay Mein Karne Lagi.
Oss Ke Haq Mein Bhi Aayat Utarney Lagi.
Wird Fizza Ka Tha Fatima Fatima.
Mutafiq Hain Yahan Sare Ahl-e-Nazar.
Kuch Na Hota Kahin Yeh Na Hotin Agar.
Kun Ka Matlab Hai Kya Fatima Fatima.
Ik Nazar Bhi Nazar Se Milai Nahi.
Maut Ghar Mein Takallum Ke Aayi Nahi.
Jab Se Dar Par Likha Fatima Fatima.
