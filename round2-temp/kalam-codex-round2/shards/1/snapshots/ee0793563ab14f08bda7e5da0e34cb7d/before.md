Hasnain jadon chaa-ya, taboot murtaza da

1
Roya'n batool zadian, babay noo'n tor kai
Zahra dai vaih ray ho gaya aghaz karbala da

2
Zahara ghazab hai kaira,ai badla lai rahay nai
Hai kalma go ali dai, kio'n vair pai gai nai
Kissay hor tai asee kio'n ilzaam ai lai yay
Kalma goa'n ujaria ghar baar fatima da

3
Puchya ali nai dhee noo'n, keevain rasia'n paa sakay gee
Too'n ghar to'n tur nai sakdi, keevain shaam ja sakay gee
Keevain mangay gee chadra'n, bhaina'n dai vastay
Khairaiya ho ra hai saang tai wada teri rida da

4
Nanay dai baad veeran, hai kinj da vaila aaya
Na ambri sadi jag tai, na baap da ai saaya
Nanay dai dais chalee'ain, Kufai nu chor kai
Hath phar kai kee pukaria, hai bhain nai bhiraa da

5
Is Wastai ai amomin, matam ali da karday
Aj pahlee vaar latkay, saiyadania'n dai parday
Koofai ich koofian nai shamsheer chai laa kai
Vaqt-e-namaz marya damaad mustafa da
