Hain Jahan Mein Naib e Shabir Zain ul Abideen
Goya Hum Sab Ke Hain Chothe Peer Zain-ul-Abideen.

1
Hujat-e-Haq Hain Zamane Mein Ye Dunya Ke Liye.
Panjtan Ke Noor Ki Tanveer Zain-ul-Abideen.
Deen Bachaya Karbala Mein Hazrat-e-Shabir Ne.
Phir Bane Islam Ki Taqdeer Zain-ul-Abideen.

2
Mustafa Ki Betiyaan Bazar-e-Sham Our Nange Sar.
Dekhte Na Kash Ye Tasheer Zain-ul-Abideen.
Haye Ayam-e-Aseeri Be Basi Zindan-e-Sham.
Kya Thi Our Kya Ho Gayi Taqdeer Zain-ul-Abideen.

3
Sath Maa Behno Ke Peshi Our Darbar-e-Yazeed.
Yeh Sitam Our Sahib-e-Tauqeer Zain-ul-Abideen.
Jalte Khaimo Hi Mein Mar Jaein Keh Bahir Aein Hum
Pochti Thin Zainab-e-Dilgeer Zain-ul-Abideen.

4
Marzi-e-Qudrat Se The Bemar Ashora Ke Din.
Warna Ban Jate Yeh Khud Shamsheer Zain-ul-Abideen.
Hai Kalam Un Ka Duaon Ka Saheefa Kamila.
Hain Kitab Allah Ki Tafseer Zain-ul-Abideen.

5
Waris Ilm-ul-Dani The Wadiyat Kar Gaye.
Hazra-e-Baqir Ko Ye Jageer Zain-ul-Abideen.
Ashqiya Ke Zehar Se Mumtaz Mula Chal Base.
Hain Shaheed Joor-e-Be Taqseer Zain-ul-Abideen.
