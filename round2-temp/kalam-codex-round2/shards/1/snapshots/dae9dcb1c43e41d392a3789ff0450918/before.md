Kahin Dushman Ke Piaday Kahin Aswar Giray.
Jis Taraf Dekh Liya Shair Ne Do Chaar Giray.

1
Dushman-e-Aal-e-Muhammad Se Na Rakho Umeed.
Oss Ke Saye Mein Na Betho Keh Jo Dewar Giray.
Larkharati Hoi Dunya Mein Sanbhalne Ke Liye.
Hum Dar-e-Haider-e-Karrar Pe So Baar Giray.

2
Karbala Rukh Se Zara Dair Hata Le Jo Naqab.
Yeh Abhi Misar Ka Charhta Howa Bazar Giray.
Iss Tarha Moat Pe Ja Parte Thay Ansar-e-Hussain.
Jaisay Bazar-e-Muhabbat Pe Kharidar Giray.

3
Isko Abbas Ke Honton Ne Bulandi Di Hai.
Ab Yeh Mumkin Hi Nahi Pyas Ka Maiyyar Giray.
Sar Dar-e-Sibt-e-Payamber Pe Jhuka Le Waiz.
Iss Se Pehle Ke Tere Sar Se Yeh Dastar Giray.

4
Oss Ne Khutbaat Ke Lashkar Ko Diya Youn Tarteeb.
Pehle Sarkar Giray Baad Mein Darbar Giray.
Zindagani Tu Bina Khak-e-Shifa Youn Hai Bilal.
Har Qadam Raah Mein Jaise Koi Beemar Giray.
