1
Haye Bemaar De Zakhma Te Payiyaan Rondiyaan Ne Zanjeeraan.
Rat Akhiyaan Di Likh Gayi Aye Aiday Kafan Te Kujh Tehreeraan.
Aidi Pak Imamat Haye Dardaan Di Ibtida Aye.
Naizay Te Wekh Roya Ainu Sabar Da Khuda Aye.
Qaidi Quran Le Turya Jadon Be Pardah Tafseeraan.
Abid Ne Sari Zindagi Aye Wain Na Bhulaye.
Meri Maa Hijab Rab Da Koi De Diyo Rida Aye.
Tusaan Qaid Kar Laiyaan Ne Shabeer Diyaan Humsheeraan.
Beemar Kool Aa Ke Aye Aakhiya Sakina.
Kehra Zulm Reh Gaya Aye Jehra Kita Nayi Laeena.
Bas Mainu Marday Ne Koi Dasda Nayi Taqseeraan.
Takbeer Parh Ke Zalim Aiday Piyo De Sir Choon Laya.
Darbar Wich Azana Abid Choon Chup Karaya.
Tere Sabar Bana Gaye Ne Azana Te Takbeeraan.
Pairaan Che Beriyaan Ne Gal Tauq V Aye Paya.
Zanjeeraan Ne Zakhma Choon Har Qadam Te Dukhaya.
 Najfi Beemar Ne Likhiyaan Aynj Deen Diyaan Taqdeeraan.
