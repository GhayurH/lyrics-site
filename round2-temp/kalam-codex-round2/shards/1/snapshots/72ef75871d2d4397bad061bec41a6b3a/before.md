1
Haye Jeway Jeway Jawaan Thiya Akbar.
Dard Da Hik Jahaan Thiya Akbar.
Koi Sughra Koon Aye Dasa Deway.
Tang Veran Di Hun Laha Deway.
Maut Da Mehmaan Thiya Akbar.
Wain Ro Ro Karendi Hayi Laila.
Wekh Sughra Laho Di Mehndi La.
Husan Da Aasmaan Thiya Akbar.
Bad Taiday Hussain Kya Jeway.
Tu Na Sanhwaan Di Hun Qaza Thiway.
Tu Jo Babay Di Jaan Thiya Akbar.
Qatlaan Koon Hussain Puchda Ha.
Koi Maikoon Oh Ja Dikhaway Cha.
Qatal Maidaan Dithaan Thiya Akbar.
Lash Chut Gayi Aye Par Aye Wada Hayi.
Ghar Ohh Ja Ke Taidi Phuphi Wesi.
Zikar Taida Dithaan Thiya Akbar.
Piyo Aye Aakhay Jawan Hai Lasha.
Dekh Bachra Medi Zaifi Da.
Kya Ajab Imtehaan Thiya Akbar.
Piyas Apni Te Bhul Giya Maula.
 Jaun Ro Ro Ke Ha Ayho Aahda.
Qatal Pani Binaa Thiya Akbar.
