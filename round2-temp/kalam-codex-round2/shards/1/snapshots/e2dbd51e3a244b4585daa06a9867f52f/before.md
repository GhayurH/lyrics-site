Haider Se Fatima Ka Janaza Nahi Utha
Khaybar Shikan Ke Dil Se Yeh Sadma Nahi Utha
Ik Cheekh Mar Kar Yehi Kehte They Murtaza
Aisa Laga Hai Hath Pe Durrah Laeen Ka
Pehlu Se Dast-e-Fatima Zahra Nahi Utha
Gonji Nabi Ke Shehar Mein Gham Ki Azaan Magar
Uthta Raha Batool Ke Ghar Se Dhowaan Magar
Afsoos Koi Ahl-e-Madina Nahi Utha
Baithay They Ummati Sabhi Darbar-e-Aam Mein
Afsoos Syeda Ke Magar Ayhteram Mein
Koi Nabi Ka Chahney Wala Nahi Utha
Haye Ali Zameen Pe Thara Ke Gir Paray
Pehlu Ka Zakhm Dekha Tu Gash Kha Ke Gir Paray
Phir Kitni Dair Tak Mera Maula Nahi Utha
Youn Qabar-e-Syeda Ki Chupatay They Murtaza
Chalees Qabrein Tanha Banatay They Murtaza
Ik Ik Lehad Se Baba Hasan Ka Nahi Utha
Haq-e-Namaz Ko Kabhi Pora Kiye Beghair
Zahra Ke Qatilon Pe Tabarah Kiye Beghair
Hargiz Bhi Murtaza Ka Musalla Nahi Utha
Ik Gham Dil-e-Ali Mein Rahe Ga Yeh Umar Bhar
Dekha Unhein Baqaiya Mein Logon Ne Raat Bhar
Deney Par Unn Ko Koi Bhi Pursa Nahi Utha
MUKHTAR Woh Ali Ka Jo Ik Koh-e-Zabt Tha
Daman Kabhi Bhi Chora Nahi Jis Ne Sabar Ka
Bar Iss Se Bhi Batool Ke Gham Ka Nahi Utha
