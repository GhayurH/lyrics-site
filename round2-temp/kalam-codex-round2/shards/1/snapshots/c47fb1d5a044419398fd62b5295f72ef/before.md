1
Dhiyan Ne Jana Mar Baba Na Khali Wehra Kar Baba.
Roway Zainab Tak Khon Che Tar Tera Sar Baba.
Man Kuntu Maula Manya Na Be Ayetabaran Ne.
Mar Dita Damad Muhammad Bad Kirdaraan Ne.
Kalma Gowaan Na Rab Da Wekhiya Ghar Baba.
Maa Ghazi Di Rondi Gal La Ke Veer Hussain.
Sanu Aan Dilasay Deway Luk Luk Kardi Wain.
Kiwein Karna Maa Umm Ul-Banin Sabar Baba.
Baaj Ridawaan Main Jana Kiwein Sham De Rahwaan Te.
Sangaan Te Sir Hone Babal Naal Bhirawaan De.
Sir Mera V Wich Khon De Hona Aye Tar Baba.
Aoun, Muhammad Main Akbar, Asghar Taun Waar Jana.
Nasal Koha Ke Main Apni Aynj Deen Nu Rang Lana.
Okhay Painday Teri Dhi Karne Ne Sar Baba.
Toor Ke Babal Nu Marey Takraan Diwaraan Te.
Khak Apne Sir Pa Roway Zawwar Sadawaan De.
Hun Baaj Tere Rul Jana Aye Dar Dar Baba.
