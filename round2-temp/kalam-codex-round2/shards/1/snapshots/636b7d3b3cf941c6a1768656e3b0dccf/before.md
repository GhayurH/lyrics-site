Bilyaqeen Khulta Hai Youn Shajrah Dar-e-Abbas Par.
Kam Nasab Kartay Nahi Sajdah Dar-e-Abbas Par.

1
Shukar Ka Karta Hoon Main Sajdah Dar-e-Abbas Par.
Sajda-e-Mabood Hai Seekha Dar-e-Abbas Par.
Aik Din Janat Mujhe Lainay Ko Aayi Khuwab Mein.
So Gaya Janat Mein Main Jaga Dar-e-Abbas Par.

2
Jis Pe Chalte Jeetay Jee Tum Ko Khuda Mil Jaye Ga.
Rasta Jata Hai Woh Seedha Dar-e-Abbas Par.
Peshiyon Par Chand Haji Janib-e-Makka Gaye.
Hazari Ko Aa Gaya Kaba Dar-e-Abbas Par.

3
Jo Bhi Chaho, Jitna Chaho, Jab Bhi Chahe Mang Lo.
Wasta De Kar Sakina Ka Dar-e-Abbas Par.
Aankh Walay Khuwaish-e-Dunya Mein Andhay Ho Gaye.
Khush Hai Giriya Kar Ke Nabina Dar-e-Abbas Par.

4
Nokari Ka Our Kya Mangoon Main Malik Se Sila.
Sajdah Karta Hai Mera Beta Dar-e-Abbas Par.
Apne Bazu Kaat Kar Darwaish Ho Pursa Yahan.
Kam Se Kam Yeh Haq Hai Matam Ka Dar-e-Abbas Par.
