Rang-o-Boo Bag-e-Jinna Bhi Fatima Zahra Ka Hai.
Yeh Jahan Kya Woh Jahan Bhi Fatima Zahra Ka Hai.

1
Hai Bina-e-Laila Our Sabar Ka Sultan Jo.
Deen-e-Haq Ka Meharbaan Bhi Fatima Zahra Ka Hai.
Marziya Ki Marziyaan Hi Kibriya Ki Hai Riza.
Martaba Aala Yahan Bhi Fatima Zahra Ka Hai.

2
Izn Se Unn Ke Hi Gardish Mein Hain Yeh Shams-o-Qamar.
Our Zameen-o-Aasmaan Bhi Fatima Zahra Ka Hai.
Karbala Ki Jang Jeeta Ik Jari Che Mah Ka.
Woh Mujahid Be Zubaan Bhi Fatima Zahra Ka Hai.

3
Jis Muqam-e-Arsh Tak Jibreel Bhi Na Ja Sakay.
Tazkira Hota Wahan Bhi Fatima Zahra Ka Hai.
Hai Fidak Our Karbala Ka Muntaqim Hasnain Jo.
Mehdi-e-Aakhir Zamaan Bhi Fatima Zahra Ka Hai.
