Jay chandh na charda, Muharram da, zainab da veer na marda

Na lut dey loki ghar zahra da, na lath da sain da parda

Duhkiyari dardanmari, jidi shakal batool di sari, na rondi sughra beht medinah, ra tahkdi veer Akbar da

Na marday baal piyasay, sehrawan wich syeda day, zahkmi na honda pani badlay, gal chotay dey Asghar da

Aaj denda kon azana, na pardey logh namaza, naizay teh char ke tu Akbar da, Haye paak Quran na parda
