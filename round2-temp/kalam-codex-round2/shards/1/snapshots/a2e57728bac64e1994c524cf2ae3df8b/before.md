1
Kabhi Shaoor Se Socha Agar Na Hote Ali.
Zamane Kya Tera Hota Agar Na Hote Ali.
Khuda Tu Noor-e-Majasam Hai Oss Ke Hath Nahi.
Kabhi Wajood Mein Aati Yeh Kainat Nahi.
Yeh Raz Raz Hi Rehta Agar Na Hote Ali.
Hayat-o-Maut Ki Tanzeem Kis Tarha Hoti.
Jahan Mein Rizq Ki Taqseem Kis Tarha Hoti.
Nizam Koan Chalata Agar Na Hote Ali.
Khuda Khafi Hai Khuda Ko Na Manta Koi.
Ali Ke Sajdon Ne Mabood Ko Jala Bakhshi.
Keh WOh Khuda Bhi Na Hota Agar Na Hote Ali.
Nabi Ke Hukam Ke Aagay Woh Giya Bebas.
Qasam Ali Ki Woh Soraj Gharoob Se Wapis.
Kabhi Na Laut Ke Atta Agar Na Hote Ali.
Khuda Ne Chaha Jahan Walay Aytraf Karein.
Ali Ki Jaye Wiladat Ka Sab Tawaf Karein.
Na Hota Khana-e-Kaba Agar Na Hote Ali.
Bohat Hain Foaj Mein Farrar Jante They Nabi.
Jabhi Tu Aa Gaya Hukam-e-Nabi Pe Haq Ka Wali.
Ke Koan Jang Mein Jata Agar Na Hote Ali.
Ukhar Ke Dar-e-Kahaibar Ko Ik Ishare Se.
Lagaya Kashti-e-Islam Ko Kinare Se.
Yeh Kam Koi Na Karta Agar Na Hote Ali.
Suno Suno Keh Yeh Farman Hai Qalandar Ka.
Kisi Ko Janta Kab Hoon Ali Wali Ke Siwa.
Main Lal Lal Na Hota Agar Na Hote Ali.
Dilon Mein Bugz Liye Sath Sath They Kafir.
Ke Zulasheer Se Hijrat Ki Raat Tak Yasir.
Nabi Ko Koan Bachata Agar Na Hote Ali.
