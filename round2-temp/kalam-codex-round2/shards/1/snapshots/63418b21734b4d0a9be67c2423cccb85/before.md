Haye Ainj Shama Dhaliyan Hasan Sadiq
Daswein Diyaan Nana Wich Karbal Aynj Shama Dhaliyan.

1
Bhain Atharah Veraan Di Saan Reh Gayi Kali Aan.
Dendi Rayi Main Heth Khanjar De Veran Pak Namazi Nu.
Waliya Naa Oh Nehar Taun Ja Baon Sad Mari Main Ghazi Nu.
Kus Gaya Sajday Wich Veran Haye Samne Khali Aan.

2
Ray Na Asghar, Akbar, Qasim Na Parday Bharjaiyaan De.
Ghazi Bajoon Sar Gaye Nana Khaimay Zahra Jaiyaan De.
Naam Abbas Da Cha Ke Rowan Tatheer Che Paliyaan.
Chad Ke Lashaan Bin Kafno Pa Kariyaan Sham Che Aa Gayi Aan.

3
Raj Ke Ron Dita Na Mainu Rowan Taun Sikdi Rayi Aan.
Veer De Qatil Shimar Diyaan Main Jhirkaan Jhaliyan.
Nana Teri Ummat Taun Main Har Moor Te Chadar Mangdi Rayi.
Kha Ke Pathar Main Shamiyaan Taun Jayin Rahwaan Taun Haye Langdi Rayi.

4
Na Bhulnrey Oh Rah Maikon Na Bhul San Galiyaan.
Samdi Si Jo Seene Te Oh Reh Gayi Wich Zindana De.
Ja Ditti Na Ummat Ne Wich Apnray Qabarastana De.
Rah Wich Odi Qabar Bana Ke Watan Te Wali Aan.
