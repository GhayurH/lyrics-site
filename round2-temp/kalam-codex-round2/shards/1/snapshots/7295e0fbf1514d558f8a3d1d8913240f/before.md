1
Haye Zahra Ko Rulaya Syed Abbas Raza.
Haye Zahra Ko Rulaya Hai Musalmano Ne.
Raat Din Oss Ko Sataya Hai Musalmano Ne.
Ya Rasool Aap Ki Beti Pe Howa Zulm-o-Sitam.
Oss Ko Darbar Bulaya Hai Musalmano Ne.
Takht Par Baith Gaye Jhotay Khalifa Ban Kar.
Tera Farman Bhulaya Hai Musalmano Ne.
Jis Ki Tazeem Ko Uthtey They Nabi Oss Par Hi.
Jalta Darwaza Giraya Hai Musalmano Ne.
Bandh Kar Haider-e-Safdar Ke Galay Mein Rasi.
Oss Ko Galiyon Mein Phiraya Hai Musalmano Ne.
Kar Diya Zalimo Ne Aap Ke Mohsin Ko Shaheed.
Wadah Iss Tarha Nibhaya Hai Musalmano Ne.
Aap Ki Beti Jo Haider Ko Bachaney Aayi.
Taziyano Se Hataya Hai Musalmano Ne.
Kabhi Darwaza Jalaya Hai Madine Mein Aimaad.
Kabhi Khaimon Ko Jalaya Hai Musalmano Ne.
