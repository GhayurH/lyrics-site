1
Ghulamo Ki Nazrein Tumhein Dhondti Hain Bata Do Thikana Imam-e-Zamana
Hamarey Liye Hai Beghair Aap Ke Yeh Jahan Qaid Khana Imam-e-Zamana

2
Zara Al-Ajal Ki Sada Ko Asar Do, Humein Apne Aane Ki Kuch Tu Khabar Do
Kahan Tak Humein Our Furqat Ke Gham Mein, Hai Aanso Bahana Imam-e-Zamana

3
Yeh Dil Chahta Hai Keh Ab Aap Aaein, Jo Halat Hai Sab Robaro Hum Sunaein
Karein Behtay Pani Pe Likh Likh Ke Kab Tak, Areeza Rawana Imam-e-Zamana

4
Bohat Zulm Har Simt Barhney Laga Hai, Faqat Be Gunahon Ka Khoon Beh Raha Hai
Zarori Bohat Ho Gaya Hai Tumhara, Zamane Mein Aana Imam-e-Zamana

5
Sada Tum Ko Deen-e-Nabi De Raha Hai, Yahan Waqt Ka Phir Yazid Aa Gaya Hai
Chaley Aao Ghaibat Ka Pardah Utha Kar, Hussain-e-Zamana Imam-e-Zamana

6
Suna Hai Keh Koi Jazera Hai Aisa, Jahan Baith Kar Tum Chalatay Ho Dunya
Keh Pohancha Do Ab Oss Jazerey Ki Had Mein, Hamara Safena Imam-e-Zamana

7
Hasad, Bugz-o-Kina, Riya Kariyan Hain, Hamarey Amal Ke Sabab Doriyaan Hain
Jo Hum Saaf Dil Hoon Tu Ho Jaye Mumkin, Tumhein Dekh Pana Imam-e-Zamana

8
Khataein Hamari Jo Hain Bakhshwa Do, Humein Jaisa Tum Chahtey Ho Bana Do
Gunah Gaar Ko Bhi Karam Bantta Hai, Tumhara Gharana Imam-e-Zamana

9
Yeh Dil Sari Dunya Se Toota Howa Hai, Tumhare Siwa Kaun Ab Aasra Hai
Sunaein Na Tum Ko Tu Kis Ko Sunaein, Yeh Gham Ka Tarana Imam-e-Zamana

10
Tumhare Bana Muddatein Hain Guzari, Tum Aao Na Aao Yeh Marzi Tumhari
Hilal Our Mesum Ka Hai Kam Tum Ko, Musalsal Bolana Imam-e-Zamana
