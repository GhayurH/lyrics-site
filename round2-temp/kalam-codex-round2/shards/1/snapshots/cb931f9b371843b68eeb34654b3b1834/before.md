Zamane Mein Jo Charcha Fiqa-o-Deen-e-Mustafa Ka Hai.
Jo Sach Pocho Imam-e-Jaffar-e-Sadiq Ka Tohfa Hai.

1
Khuda-o-Mustafa Mula Ali Ke Naam Ka Kalma.
Haqeeqat Mein Imam-e-Jaffar-e-Sadiq Ka Tohfa Hai.
Jo Hai Aadam Ta Qayam Syed-e-Lolak Ka Shajra.
Wohi Shajra Imam-e-Jaffar-e-Sadiq Ka Shajra Hai.

2
Chalan Jo Anbiya-o-Mursaleen-o-Auliya Ka Hai.
Zamane Ne Imam-e-Jaffar-e-Sadiq Se Seekha Hai.
Jo Pehne Phir Rahe Hain Khuwaja-o-Ghoas-o-Qutab Data.
Yeh Utran Bhi Imam-e-Jaffar-e-Sadiq Ka Khirqa Hai.

3
Silay Mein Bait-e-Midhat Ke Milay Ga Bait Janat Mein.
Yeh Shayar Se Imam-e-Jaffar-e-Sadiq Ka Wada Hai.
Kahan Hai Taqat-e-Midhat Wajhi-o-:SIBT-E-JAFAR: Mein.
Yeh Khidmat Mein Imam-e-Jaffar-e-Sadiq Ki Hadiya Hai.
