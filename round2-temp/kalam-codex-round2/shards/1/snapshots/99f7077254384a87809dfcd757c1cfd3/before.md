1
Marhaba Sale Allah Aye Shah-e-Ibrar Hussain.
Rab-e-Akbar Bhi Hai Khud Tera Parastar Hussain.
Paish Karta Hoon Khulasa-e-Taruf Tera.
Walda Umm-e-Abeeha Hain Padar Shair-e-Khuda.
Qibla-e-Khana-e-Rab Hai Tera Ghar Bar Hussain.
Aalam-e-Ghaib Pe Chalta Hai Ishara Tera.
Zindagi Maut Pe Yaksaan Hai Ajarah Tera.
Az Zameen Taba Falak Hai Tera Darbar Hussain.
Tujh Ko Ansar Mile Aise Nahi Jin Ki Nazeer.
Paye Hain Hazrat-e-Muslim Ki Tarha Tu Ne Safeer.
Tu Khuda Ka Hai Khudai Teri Sarkar Hussain.
Dushman-e-Deen-e-Payamber Ka Jigar Chaak Howa.
Tha Jo Bait Ka Talabgar Tah-e-Khak Howa.
Jab Chali Karb-o-Bala Mein Teri Talwar Hussain.
Dekh Kar Jazba-e-Aysar Shah-e-Karb-o-Bala.
Khaliq-e-Aalam-e-Imkaan Ko Yeh Kehna Hi Para.
Main Madadgar-e-Jahan Mera Madadgar Hussain.
Dekhiye Dasht-e-Sitam Sabar Se Seraab Kiya.
Khud Alalat Mein Raha Deen Ko Shifa Yaab Kiya.
Hai Shifa Bakhsh Tera Abid-e-Bemar Hussain.
Aap Ke Roza-e-Aqdas Se Jo Ho Aate Hain.
Loag Phir Unn Ki Ziyarat Ke Liye Jate Hain.
Yeh Sharf Pata Hai Bas Aap Ka Zawar Hussain.
Lakh La-Char Ho Be Bas Ho Qawi Ban Jaye.
Jaun Ban Jaye Koi Hur-e-Jari Ban Jaye.
Jis Pe Ho Jaye Nazar Aap Ki Sarkar Hussain.
Hum Ko Khaliq Ne Kiya Khalq Issi Ki Khatir.
Barsar-e-Warq Har Ik Lafz Pukara Yasir.
Mera Malik Mera Mula Mera Sardar Hussain.
