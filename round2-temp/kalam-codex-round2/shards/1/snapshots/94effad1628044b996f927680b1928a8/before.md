1
Hum Ayse Wafa Keish Yahan Chand Rahe Hain
Aazaad Mansh Ho Ke Bhi Paband Rahe Hain.
Kehne Ko Tu Ache The Magar Bohat Doost Hamare.
Afsoos Keh Humdard Magar Chand Rahe Hain.

2
Dekhe Koi Hum Ahl-e-Junoon Ka Ye Karishma.
Hum Jaison Ke Marhoon-e-Khurd Mand Rahe Hain.
Ye Zarf Hamara Hai Wagarna Kahan Sab Loag.
Halaat-e-Dagar Goon Mein Bhi Khoor Sand Rahe Hain.

3
Youn Aalm-e-Tanhai Mein Katte Hain Shab-o-Rooz.
Jiss Tarha Ke Mudat Se Nazar Band Rahe Hain.
Iss Door Mein Be Loas Muhabat Khana “MUMTAZ”.
Sab Apne Hi Matlab Ke Garaz Mand Rahe Hain.
