1
Hussain Ki Marzi Mir Hasan Mir
Khuda Ki Marzi Hai Maula Hussain Ki Marzi.
Atta Karey Jisay Jitna Hussain Ki Marzi.
Bas Aik Fatima Zahra Hain Jo Samajhti Hain.
Hussain Se Bhi Zyada Hussain Ki Marzi.
Nabi Yeh Kehte They Rahib Naseeb Mein Tere.
Kami Karey Ya Izafa Hussain Ki Marzi.
Hai Aaj Teesri Shabaan Hussain Aate Hain.
Chaley Gi Aaj Se Har Ja Hussain Ki Marzi.
Qaza Se Hath Chura Kar Woh Ho Gaya Zindah.
Habib Jan Gaya Tha Hussain Ki Marzi.
Taveel Sajdah Payamber Ne Iss Liye Bhi Kiya.
Samajh Lein Ta Keh Sahaba Hussain Ki Marzi.
Ali Ne Pocha Keh Haq Mehar Kya Likha Jaye.
Tu Pukarein Sani-e-Zahra Hussain Ki Marzi.
Sakhi Hussain Ka Matam Khuda Ki Marzi Hai.
Hai Kirdgar Ka Sajdah Hussain Ki Marzi.
Yeh Karbala Hai Koi Hajj Nahi Keh Sab Aaein.
Yahan Pe Chalti Hai Maula Hussain Ki Marzi.
Rakhay Youn Dirham-o-Dinar Hur Ne Thokar Par.
Chalaye Gi Mera Sikka Hussain Ki Marzi.
Namaz Parhta Hoon Akbar Riza-e-Sheh Ke Liye.
Bachaye Gi Mera Sajdah Hussain Ki Marzi.
