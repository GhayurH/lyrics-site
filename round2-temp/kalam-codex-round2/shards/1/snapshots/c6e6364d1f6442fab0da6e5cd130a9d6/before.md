Kandhon pay sharifon nay haey Shah-e-Khurasan ka taboot uthaya hay

1
Dhokay say musalman nay Kazim kay dularay ko haye zehar pelaya hay

2
1) Is zehar-e-saqifa ka youn hotay asar dekha
Kazim ka kabhi tukray Shabbar ka jigar dekh
Mazloom Raza par bhi mamoon nay Mashad main yeh zulam kamaya hay.

3
2) Bhai ki muhabbat main nikli thi Madina say
Dushwar safar kar kay aaye thi paharon pay
Pardes main bhai say phir bhi na mili bibi is ghum nay rulaya hay

4
3) Mashad ko chalain Zahra haye zakhmi leya pehlo
Hay khakh-e-shifa saar main aankhon say rawan ansu
Ummat nay Muhammad ki Khaton-e-Qayamat ko phir Baqia churaya hay

5
4) Sang Zahra-o-Shabbar kay Yasrab say chalay Sarwar
Karbal say Hussain aaya chor aaya Najaf Haider
Haq dar hay lannat ka pardes main dil jis nay Panjatan ka dukhaya hay

6
5) Hain khakh basar hoorain Iran ki rahon main
Is ghum ki jhalak dekhi Nabiyon ki nighaon main
Rotay hain farishtay bhi zalim nay sitam kaisa Sadaat pay dhaya hay

7
6) Yeh Baab-e-Hawaij hain markaz hain shifaon ka
Jawad Raza Moula zamin hay rizaon ka
Bakhsha wohi jaeya ga jis nay meray Moula ka haye soogh manaya hay
