1
Do Ghari Bas Aankh Bhar Ke Dekh Loon Akbar Tujhe.
Aa Mere Betay Zara Ro Loon Galay Lag Kar Tujhe.
Iss Zaeefi Mein Aye Akbar Tu Hi Taqat Hai Meri.
Tu Salamat Hai Tu Binayi Salamat Hai Meri.
Yeh Bata De Kya Taras Aata Nahi Mujh Par Tujhe.
Main Naqabon Mein Chupa Doon Yeh Teri Tabindagi.
Tere Seene Par Main Dam Kar Doon Zara Nad-e-Ali.
Dasht Mein Ja Kar Nazar Lag Jaye Na Dilbar Tujhe.
Muntazir Hai Woh Keh Tujh Ko Dekh Kar, Kar Le Gi Eid.
Haye Meri Be Basi Our Haye Sughra Ki Umeed.
Kash Bas Ik Bar Le Ja Paon Beta Ghar Tujhe.
Kiya Karon Aye Lal Tujh Ko Rook Tu Sakta Nahi.
Haan Magar Tu Jaye Ga Tu Jee Main Paon Ga Nahi.
Baap Ab Tere Hawalay Jo Lagay Bahatar Tujhe.
Jang Kartay Kartay Thak Ja Tu Bola Lena Mujhe.
Joor Kar Himmat Bas Ik Aawaz De Dena Mujhe.
Tera Baba Dhondne Niklay Ga Jab Dar Dar Tujhe.
Girtay Uthtey Main Pohanch Jaon Ga Murjhana Nahi.
Dair Ho Jaye Agar Mujh Ko Tu Ghabrana Nahi.
Mujh Se Pehle Lainay Aa Jaein Gay Peygamber Tujhe.
Main Uthaon Ga Tera Lasha Akelay Bhar Ke Dam.
Sath De Dena Keh Pohanchin Jald Hi Khaimon Mein Hum.
Bibiyaan Roney Na Aa Jaein Barehna Sar Tujhe.
Main Tu Wadah Kar Ke Aaya Tha Nibhaon Ga Aye Lal.
Tujh Ko Jata Dekh Kar Aata Hai Mujh Ko Yeh Khiyal.
Kaisay Jane De Rahi Hai Zainab-e-Muztar Tujhe.
Aah Bhar Ke Shah Ne Haider Kaha Akbar Suno.
Reh Na Paye Ga Chala Aaye Ga Tere Pas Woh.
Dekhta Hai Apne Jholay Se Mera Asghar Tujhe.
