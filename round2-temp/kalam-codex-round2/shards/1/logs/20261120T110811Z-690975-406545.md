Reconstructed the lyric in Saraiki script and preserved the Roman wording in `romanLyrics`. Both editions have a four-verse opening and four numbered stanzas, with 20 verse lines each.

I updated the language from Urdu to Saraiki based on forms such as *menday*, *ku*, and *wanjna*. I read the ambiguous *don awan* as **دھوݨ آواں** and *Nasar* as **نصر**.
