Hik warri sir maiku babay da Sajaad bhira milwa eh,
Maida wadah hai wal na rosan.

1
Maiku piyo mazloom day hik wari
Rukhsar zara chumwa
Meda Wada Aey Wal Na Rosan, Hik war sir meko

2
1) Oh maiku khaak te neendar nehi ohndi jadaan samdi haan kan dukh wainday
Maiku Shimaar tamachay maray han
Mere eh rukhsar gawa eh,
Meda Wada Aey Wal Na Rosan, Hik wari sir meko

3
2) Chaon salan di Masooma de nehi sir day waal safaid honday
Main wang zaifa hogaiyan ya
Gaya dukh babay da kha eh,
Meda Wada Aey Wal Na Rosan, Hik war sir meko

4
3) Main piyo di matamdar jaidan zindan-e-Shaam ich mar waisan
Maiku dafan karan Sajaad bhira
Eho mela chola paa eh,
Meda Wada Aey Wal Na Rosan, Hik wari sir meko

5
4) Kya haal dasan Baqir jeewi makun Shaam di garmi kha gayi eh
Maku ghin wanjh is zindan vichon
Nehi lagdi veer hawa eh,
Meda Wada Aey Wal Na Rosan, Hik wari sir meko

6
5) Tu Sar babay Da Wanj Girrh Aah Shallah Rat Akhiyan Di Band Thirri
Jey Peyu Da Sar Meku Na Milya
Wal Muk Waisar Meday Saaye,
Meda Wada Aey Wal Na Rosan, Hik wari sir meko

7
5) Maidi maiyat amanat dafan karein ghin watan wanjhay tun naal apne
Maku babay de kol sama chorin
Jaidan Thivi qaid reha hai,
Meda Wada Aey Wal Na Rosan, Hik war sir meko

8
6) Main peyo mazloom musafir di peshawani chumsa kayi wari
Nal-e-akhsa bachri rul gayian
Saar piyo da seena la eh,
Meda Wada Aey Wal Na Rosan, Hik wari sir meko
