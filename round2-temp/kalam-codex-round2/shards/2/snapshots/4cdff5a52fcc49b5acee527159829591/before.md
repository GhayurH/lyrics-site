Yeh Karam Daimi Hussain Se Hai.

1
Zikar-e-ShabirKarta Rehta Hoon.
Rooh Mein Tazgi Hussain Se Hai.
Main Tu Bandah Hoon Choriye Mujh Ko.
Khud Khuda Ki Khudi Hussain Se Hai.
Mujh Ko Shabir Ke Laho Ke Qasam.
Aaj Naam-e-Ali Hussain Se Hai.

2
Keh Raha Hai Har Ik Shaheed Yehi.
Ab Meri Zindagi Hussain Se Hai.
Chal Raha Hai JO Yeh Nizam-e-Khuda.
Sab Yeh Mula Sakhi Hussain Se Hai.
Mera Apna Koi Kamal Nahi.
Mujh Ko Izat Mili Hussain Se Hai.

3
Hum Ko Matam Se Rokne Wale.
Teri Kya Dushmani Hussain Se Hai.
Sar Jhuka Le Wo Misal-e-Hur Ban Ke.
Jis Ko Sharmindagi Hussain Se Hai.
Ja Raha Hoon Main Karbala Raqib.
Bas Yeh Khush Qismati Hussain Se Hai.
