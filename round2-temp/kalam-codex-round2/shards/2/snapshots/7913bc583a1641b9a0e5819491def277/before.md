Khon Roye Ga Na Pocho Abid e Bemar Se.
Kis Tarha Guzri Thi Zainab Sham Ke Bazar Se.

1
Bach Gaya Nana Ka Deen Our Sajda e Shabbir Bhi.
Ho Ke Zainab Aa Gayi Hai Sham Ke Darbar Se.
Milta Izn-e-Jang Tu Qaidi Na Sham Mein Jate.
Shehpar e Ghazi Na Kam They Jafar-e-Tayyar Se.

2
Gham Uthane Ki Bhi Ab Iss Mein Nahi Baqi Sakat.
Behan Ka Lasha Uthay Ga Kis Tarha Beemar Se.
Jab Bhi Rukta Qafila Pochtay They Shad Ro Kar.
Taziyane Ki Zabaan Mein Qafila Salar Se.
