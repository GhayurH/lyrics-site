Sab Ridaon Se Mila Martaba Mujh Ko Juda.
Noor Walay Hain Jahan Aisay Manzar Mein Rahi.

1
Jis Mein Shabeer Rahe Main Ossi Ghar Mein Rahi.
Sar Pe Zahra Ke Rahi Yeh Taruf Hai Mera.
Kaisay Samjhay Ga Bhala Meri Azmat Ko Koi.
Kiya Ghataye Ga Bhala Meri Rifat Ko Koi.

2
Jab Keh Samjha Na Koi Mera Paiwand Hai Kya.
Meri Taqdeer Bana Yeh Nabowat Ka Chaman.
Haan Muhammad Ko Howa Jis Ghari Zoaf-e-Badan.
Jo Shifa Bantta Hai Main Bani Oss Ki Shifa.

3
Jis Ke Dam Se Hai Jahan Oss Ne Orha Hai Mujhe.
Jo Hai Hasnain Ki Maa Oss Ne Orha Hai Mujhe.
Barha Arsh Hai Bhi Tazkira Mera Howa.
Fatima Zahra Ali Our Hasnain-o-Nabi.

4
Bait-e-Imran Mein Youn Bazm-e-Tatheer Saji.
Mere Saye Mein Rahe Jin Ka Saya Na Bana.
Bach Nahi Sakta Kabhi Jo Hai Zahra Ka Addo.
Oss Pe Lanat Hai Meri Jo Hai Zahra Ka Addo.

5
Woh Kahin Ka Na Raha Jis Se Zahra Hain Khafa.
Unn Se Mansoob Hoi Jo Hain Janat Ke Qaseem.
Keh Hai Hawa Se Bari Malika Meri Waseem.
Iss Liye Dehar Mein Hai Martaba Mera Bara.
