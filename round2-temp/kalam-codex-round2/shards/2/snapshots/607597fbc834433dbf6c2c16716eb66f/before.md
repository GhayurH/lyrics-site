Mehndiyaan Kiwein Layiye Mehndiyaan Asghar Khan
Qasim Di Lash Te Aa Ke Sir Wich Mitiyaan Pa Ke Zahra Diyaan Jaiyaan Rondiyaan.

1
Chaar Choferay Khilray Ne Ban Wich Banray De Tukray.
Maiyyat Wangoon Har Tukray Te Apne Sir Nu Rakh Ke Phophiyaan Manwaan Ne Rondiyaan.
Izn Jehad Da Honda Jay Mastoraan Nu Chan Qasim.
Tera Sath Nibhandiyan Phophiyaan Tere Hissay Diyaan Zarbaan Assi Apne Siraan Te Sehndiyan.

2
Naal Hussain Jay Le Janday Oh Tukray Abba Wich Chukday.
Sehra De Wich Har Pasay Assi Chadraan Putar Wacha Ke Khud Chundiyan Teriyaan Mehndiyaan.
Naal Barat De Teri Jay Assi Sham Shehar Nu Janday.
Na Kalay Kaprey Panday Na Hath Rasiyaan Bandhwanday Na Aynj Aountaan Te Behndiyan.

3
Lal Dasa Kinj Kubra De Hathaan Taun Rang Mitawaan.
Bewah Banri De Hath Taun Shagna Diyaan Mehndiyaan Qasim Ik Raat De Wich Nayi Lehndiyan.
Wain Karan Akbar Aye Phophiyaan Farwa Gathri Na Wekhay.
Shagna Wich Lal Di Jay Te Sehrey Wich Maiyyat Uthay Manwaan Zindah Nayi Rehndiyan.
