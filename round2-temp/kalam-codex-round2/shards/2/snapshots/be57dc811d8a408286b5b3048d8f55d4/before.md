1
Qaseeda Fatima Ka Shahid Baltistani.
Yeh Quraan Hai Qaseeda Fatima Ka.
Khuda Zakir Hai Pehla Fatima Ka.
Simat Kar Sorah-e-Kausar Bana Hai.
Taruf Mukhtasir Sa Fatima Ka.
Yahan Par But Nahi Hai, But Shikan Hai.
Hai Ghar Kabay Ka Kaba Fatima Ka.
Khuda Ko Aayatein Dena Parin Hain.
Bohat Mehnga Hai Faqa Fatima Ka.
Wilayat Ko Bacha Kar Le Gayi Hai.
Ali Par Bhi Hai Qarza Fatima Ka.
Azakhana Bana Rakha Hai Main Ne.
Mere Ghar Mein Hai Hissa Fatima Ka.
Faqat Yeh Matami Dastay Nahi Hain.
Inhein Samjho Qabeela Fatima Ka.
Jabhi Jibreel Bhi Zair-e-Kisa Hai.
Yeh Nokar Hai Chaheta Fatima Ka.
Agar Salman Hoti Sari Dunya.
Azaan Mein Naam Hota Fatima Ka.
Yahan Mat Aazmao Apni Qismat.
Ali Se Tey Hai Rishta Fatima Ka.
Kahey Ga Daar Par Bhi Yeh Takallum.
Fidak Hai Fatima Ka, Fatima Ka.
