Mehrooma Maa Da Bachra Aye Zainab Ne Wain Aye Paya
Mere Veer De Pak Janaze Nu Haye Teeraan Naal Walaya
Kar Chaak Girebaan Qasim Da Maa Sir Wich Khak Aye Payi
De Khabar Toon Ja Ke Phophiyaan Nu Aj Fajar Aye Maut Di Aayi
Kehwin Hasan Ameer De Bachrey Nu Aj Ummat Yateem Banaya
Aj Putar Rasool Da Watna Te Ummat Kolon Rah Mangda Aye
Nayi Mangda Warasat Nanay Di Ik Qabar Di Ja Mangda Aye
Na Maro Teer Janazay Te Shabir Ne Ro Farmaya
Assa Rulna Aye Pardais De Wich Mera Veer Watan Te Ruliya
Mur Aayi Aye Maiyyat Oddi Har Band Kafan Da Khuliya
Mere Veer De Kafan Da Ummat Ne Rang Teeraan Nal Badlaya
Jadoon Ummat Pak Janazay Nu Haye Teraan Naal Sajaya
Pa Sir Dastar Imamat Di Pehla Okha Wela Aaya
Khichay Teer Hussain Janazay Chon Zainab Nu Kool Bulaya
Aaye Aakhri Welay Veer Milan Sab Bhaina Milan Nu Aaiyan
Riya Dar Te Ronda Ghazi Aye Andar Ne Zahra Jaiyaan
NAJFI Kiya Ro Ke Sabz Qaba Mera Ghazi Veer Nayi Aaya
