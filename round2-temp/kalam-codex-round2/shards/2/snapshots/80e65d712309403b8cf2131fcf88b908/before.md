1
Na Bhoolay Gi Kabhi Fizza Khurram Murtaza.
Teri Ghurbat Ko Ya Zahra Na Bholay Gi Kabhi Fizza.
Tera Dunya Se Youn Jana Na Bholay Gi Kabhi Fizza.
Tumhare Zakhmi Pehlu Se Woh Jitna Khon Tha Nikla.
Dar-o-Dewar Par Cheentay Paray Our Farsh Par Bikhra.
Woh Tere Khoon Ka Har Qatra Na Bholay Gi Kabhi Fizza.
Yeh Gham Qatal-e-Nabi Se Kam Nahi Tarpaye Ga Mujh Ko.
Uthaya Hath Tujh Par Our Laeen Ne Jis Tarha Tujh Ko.
Hasan Ke Samne Mara Na Bholay Gi Kabhi Fizza.
Giraya Thokaron Se Tujh Pe Zalim Ne Tha Darwaza.
Shikasta Pehlu Ka Tere Hai Bibi Mujh Ko Andaza.
Tere Mohsin Ka Mar Jana Na Bholay Gi Kabhi Fizza.
Hoon Jis Ki Pasliyaan Tooti Woh Kaisay Sans Le Paye.
Kabhi Karwat Jo Tum Badlo Tu Bibi Sans Ruk Jaye.
Tera Ghut Ghut Ke Woh Jeena Na Bholay Gi Kabhi Fizza.
Yeh Zainab Dekh Leti Tu Tarap Kar Gham Se Mar Jati.
Chupati Kyun Thin Tum Chehra Samajh Sakti Hoon Main Bibi.
Tere Rukh Par Nishaan Neela Na Bholay Gi Kabhi Fizza.
Asar Aisa Ali Par Ho Gaya Hai Tere Sadmon Ka.
Ali Ke Chehray Par Noha Likha Hai Tere Zakhmo Ka.
Hain Rotay Jis Tarha Maula Na Bholay Gi Kabhi Fizza.
Hasan Jab Baad Tere Maa Mujhe Keh Kar Bulaye Ga.
Bahan Keh Kar Mujhe Tera Bolana Yaad Aaye Ga.
Yeh Rutba Nokrani Ka Na Bholay Gi Kabhi Fizza.
 Asad Taboot-e-Zahra Se Lipat Kar Kehti Thi Fizza.
Teri Mazlomiyat Pe Qabar Tak Royon Gi Main Zahra.
Jo Tere Lab Pe Tha Noha Na Bholay Gi Kabhi Fizza.
