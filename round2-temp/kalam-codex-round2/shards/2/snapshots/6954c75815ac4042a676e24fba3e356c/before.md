1
Nana Kadon Aourein Ne Sughra De Bhira.
Main Deway Baldi Thak Gayi Aan Mainu Akbar Veer Mila.
Kardi Matam Odhay Hujray De Dar Behni Aan.
Tak Nana Qadam Nishaan Veraan Mere Mukday Jawanr Sah.
Hoya Okha Jeenra Baaj Akbar De Dunya Te.
Main Lar Lar Naal Aina Sahwaan De Mangni Aan Ro Ro Dua.
Ro Ro Rataan Nu Main Qabar-e-Nabi Te Arz Karan.
Meri Rondiyaan Akhiyaan Nu Nana Akbar Di Shakal Wakha.
Bethi Aas Teri Te Lainr Mainu Toon Aawein Ga.
Mere Maran Taun Pehlaan Chan Akbar Dukhiya Nu Gal Chal La.
Ujray Wehriyaan Nu Haye Aa Ke Mehdi Aanr Wasaway.
Aaway Akbar Mur Watna Te Deway Bhainr De Dard Muka.
Howay Pursa Dari Dar Te Tere Lal Saein.
Mang Sadqa Malka-e-Hijar Rida Wasay Lalan Da Wehra.
Poetess::Syeda Rida Zainab Jhelum:
