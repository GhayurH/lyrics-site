1
Masjid Mein Zakhmi Kabay Ka Sarwar Raza Karbalai.
Masjid Mein Zakhmi Kabay Ka Kaba Hai Aye Khuda.
Tar Khon Mein Wajhullah Ka Chehra Hai Aye Khuda.
Dewar-o-Dar Bhi Rotay Hain Kehte Hain Haye Ali.
Ghamgeen Aaj Jaisay Keh Sajdah Hai Aye Khuda.
Ayelan Howa Kis Ko Lagi Zarb Be Khata.
Jin-o-Bashar Yeh Bolay Keh Maula Hai Aye Khuda.
Fuztu Berab Al-Kaba Ki Aayi Sada Tabhi.
Quran Ke Yeh Qari Ka Lehja Hai Aye Khuda.
Jis Waqt Ghar Mein Laye Hasan Zakhmi Ko Tabhi.
Zainab Pukari Zakhmi Yeh Baba Hai Aye Khuda.
Madar Nahi Thi Aaj Tu Baba Juda Hoye.
Zainab Ka Aaj Kaun Sahara Hai Aye Khuda.
 Asif Ho Ya Keh Koi Ho Sarwar Jahan Mein.
Har Koi Sogowar Hi Dekha Hai Aye Khuda.
