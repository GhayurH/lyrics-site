Qaid mein wo janay wali mar gayi
Shaam roti anay wali mar gayi

1
Bolay Abid koi to de de kafan
Shimr se ghabranay wali mar gayi

2
Koi to Sajjad se kehde zara
Wo tamachay khanay wali mar gayi

3
Chaa gayi zindan par khamoshiyan
Hayee wo chilanay wali mar gayi

4
Safr mein wo Zainab o Kulsoom ko
Ablay dikhlane wali mar gayi

5
Mujhko is zindaan se lagta hai darr
Haye ye farmanay wali mar gayi

6
Shimr se dar ke phuppi ki oth mein
Haye wo chup janay wali mar gayi

7
Matmi ke sath ro tauqeer tu
Wo sazaein khanay wali mar gayi
