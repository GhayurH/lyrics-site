1
Qaid Ho Ke Jo Karb-o-Bala Se Chala Ab Wohi Qafila Sham Mein Aa Gaya.
Zulm Sehta Hai Our Khon Rota Hai Woh Naib-e-Kibriya Waris-e-Mustafa.
Sham Ke Raste Tang Hain Pehle Hi Haye Dono Taraf Hain Tamashai Bhi.
Our Tamashaiyon Ki Woh Tana Zani Jane Kaise Nabi Zadiyaan Hain Khari Kis Tarha Hai Khari.
Sar Pe Chadar Nahi Hath Bhi Bandhe Kaise Parda Karein Warisan-e-Haya.
Haye Kis Kis Tarha Se Satate Hain Yeh Dekh Rahon Mein Kante Bichate Hain Yeh.
Garam Pani Chaton Se Girate Hain Yeh Khon Abid Ko Mere Rulate Hain Yeh Haye Rulate Hain Yeh.
Zeenat-e-Murtaza Keh Rahi Hai Khuda Kon Hain Hum Zara Shamiyon Ko Bata.
Jab Howa Shor Logo Satao Inhein Bagiyon Ke Hain Bache Rulao Inhein.
Ghair Lo Our Naize Chubhai Inhein In Ke Waris Nahi Hain Batao Inhein Haye Batao Inhein.
Koi Lakari Liye Koi Pathar Liye Marne Aa Gaya Haye Zain-ul-Ebba.
Ik Jagah Beech Bazar Aisa Howa Sar Pe Abid Ke Jalta Howa Kuch Gira.
Jis Se Mula Ka Mere Ammama Jala Dekha Zainab Ne Kaise Yeh Manzar Bhala Haye Manzar Bhala.
Aag Abid Ke Phir Sar Talak Aa Gayi Hath The Jo Bandhe Woh Hata Na Saka.
Dair Tak Haye Bazar Sajte Rahe Qaidi Bahir Shehar Ke Hi Rote Rahe.
Kab Talak Jane Asif Haram The Khare Hukm Tha Koi Aaram Kar Na Sake Haye Na Kar Sake.
Phir Manadi Hoi Saj Gayi Har Gali Le Ke Aa Jao Ab Kunba-e-Murtaza.
