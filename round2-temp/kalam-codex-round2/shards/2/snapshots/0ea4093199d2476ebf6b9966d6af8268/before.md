Nana wich pardes day lutaya mera ghar lokan nay
Mainu bazaaran cha karwaey safar lokan nay

1
Mainu pehchano main Zahra di pali Zainab haan
Bhain Hasnain di haan binte Ali Zainab haan
Meray khutbay da liya kuch na asaar lokan nai

2
May Muhammad di nawasi haan dasain di rayaan
Waasta tera main mehmaan aan mai deyndee rayaan
Fair vee maar ditay meray putar lokan nai

3
Jeri gaardan nu tu chum da sain vay nana sayeen
Kee oda hal hoya bhulna ni mehshar tayeen
Os gaardan tay chala chor khanjar lokan nai

4
Waal nazay tay rida Shimar lahahi aa kay
Mainu bazaran day vich turaiya rassian paa kay
Sath maray meray Akbar Asghar lokan nai

5
Jinu kayee saal may burkay cha palaindee rayaan
Aap jaagan onu seenay tay suvaindee rayaan
Cheer chadaya oda Karbal cha jigar lokan nai

6
Rayiyaan pathraan diyaan barsataan che khutbay parhdi
Veer nu ro na saki dunya taun Nana dardi
Aisa dita aye Risalat da ajar lokaan ne

7
Keewain bazaara dee halate suno de Akhtar
Sani-e-Zahra pe waastade rahay jis da pathar
Alay Ahmed ki beti kis kadar lokhan nai
