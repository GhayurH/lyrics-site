Phophiyan Di Peshi Abid Nu Haye Khon Rawa Gayi Aye Asghar Khan
Ainu Maut Sakina Di Logo Beemar Banra Gayi Aye.

1
Ainay Apni Akhiyaan Nal Wekhi, Nayi Lathdi Pak Rida.
Mar Janda Haal Jaye Tak Lainda Oss Welay Zainab Da.
Bas Khabar Ridawaan Utran Di Aidhey Jigar Nu Kha Gayi Aye.
Mar Gayi Aye Sakina Shimar Kolon Nayi Loor Bachawan Di.

2
Par Yaad Raway Gi Abid Nu Wich Qaid Sawawan Di.
Kuj Dard Muka Gayi Aye Aidhey Kuj Dard Wadha Gayi Aye.
Pathraan Kharaan Te Koriyaan Taun Ainu Zakhm Nayi Aaye.
Aye Zakhm Sajjad Di Akhiyaan Nu Ghali Moraan Laye.

3
Chali Wariyaan De Jagay Nu Aj Maut Sawa Gayi Aye.
Aye Manzar Maithon Bhari Si Likhda Main Dard Kiwein.
Aye Wainr Mere Layi Ho Sakday Lagda Aye Saqlain Jiwein.
Aye Noha Sham Wali Bibi Khud Aa Ke Likha Gayi Aye.
