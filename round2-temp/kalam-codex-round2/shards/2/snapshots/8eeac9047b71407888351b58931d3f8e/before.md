1
Nayi Lash La Waris Aye Loko Ainu Lain Layi Zahra Ayi Aye.
Ummat Ne Kariyaan Beriyaan Te Jehri Zakhma Naal Sajai Aye.
Zahra Nu Pursa Deen Layi Aj Sham Di Malka Aana Aye.
Oss Welay Arsh V Hil Jana Jadon Zainab Wain Aye Pana Aye.
Mere Sir Wich Khak Way Karbal Di Main Sir Taun Chadar Layi Aye.
Zahra Nal Ik Zindan De Wich Haye Sham Di Qaidan Rondi Aye.
Aye Wain Karey Main Jananiyaan Wich Qaid De Maut Ki Hondi Aye.
Tere Jism Taun Wadh Zanjeeraan Ne Taun Kiwein Qaid Nibhai Aye.
Karbal Te Sham Da Har Qaidi Zindan De Bahir Aaya Aye.
Rat Ro Ke Abid Aakhda Aye Aj Gal Wich Tauq Main Paya Aye.
Ik Chadar Wich Kyun Lash Teri Aina Naal Zanjeeraan Payi Aye.
Jadon Le Dastar Yateemi Di Haye Pak Raza Layi Aaya Si.
Mitiyaan Te Sutiyaan Dhiyaan Nu Maa Ro Ro Aap Jagaya Si.
Dhiyaan Nu Gal Nal La Maula Nayi Mukni Hoan Judai Aye.
Oss Dhi NuMilda Ja Maula Tere Baad Jinnay Rul Jana Aye.
Jinnay Qum De Janglaan Wich Najfi Ik Maqtal Hor Banana Aye.
Rab Piyo De Gham Wich Ron Layi Ik Zahra Hor Banai Aye.
Noha Khuwan Party Ch Faiyaz Party Sialkot.
25 Rajab Shahadat Imam Mosa Kazim AS Noha
Matami Sangat
Anjuman-e-Sipah-e-Ahl-e-Bait Sialkot
