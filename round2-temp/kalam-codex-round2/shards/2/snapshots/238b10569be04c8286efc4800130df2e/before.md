Mera Aakhri Wela Aa Gaya Aye Sajjad Tu Cheti Aa Vera
Kujh Dair De Bajoon Ruk Janrey Masooma Bhainr De Sah Veera Aa Veera.

1
Zindan Di Garam Zameen Uttay Mainu Aj Taein Nahin Neend Aayi.
Tere Seene Sonra Chani Aan Aa Gal Humsheer Nu La Veera Aa Veera.
Mainu Qasam Hai Veer Ali Akbar Di Hunr Nahin Rondi Main Babay Nu.
Banr Ke Zamin Main Ujari Da Meri Wakhri Qaid Chura Veera Aa Veera.

2
Jehra Asghar De Jholay Wangoon Mera Sar Gaya Sham-e-Ghareebaan Wich.
Mainu Osay Kurtay Da Abid Dewin Toon Kafan Pawa Veera Aa Veera.
Ik Hoor Wasiyat Abid Nu Keeti Sain Sakina Ro Ro Ke.
Meri Lehad Ich Mere Nal Dafan Karein Ali Asghar Da Kurta Veera Aa Veera.

3
Hath Chuk Ke Qaidan Bhainr Layi Rat Rondiyaan Akhiyaan Naal Veran.
Teri Bhainr Sakina Mar Jaway Hunr Kar Sajjad Dua Veera Aa Veera.
 Qaisar Meri Sainr Di Maiyyat Choon Aye Dard Bhari Aawaz Aayi.
Meri Wakhri Qaid Te Nibh Gayi Aye Na Wakhri Qabar Banra Veera Aa Veera.
