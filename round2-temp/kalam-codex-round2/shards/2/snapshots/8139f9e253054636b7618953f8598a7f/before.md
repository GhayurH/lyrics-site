1
Haseen Tum Sa Rasolon Mein Nahi Dekha Ali Akbar.
Ziyarat Teri Karta Hai Tera Baba Ali Akbar.
Yeh Kis Ne Keh Diya Sarkar Ka Saya Nahi Koi.
Muhammad Mustafa Ka Aap Hain Saya Ali Akbar.

2
Nahi Ho Ga Tumhara Bhi Koi Saya Ali Akbar.
Kabhi Saye Ka Saya Ho Nahi Sakta Ali Akbar.
Diye Rahib Ko Jis Ne Saat Betay Apni Marzi Se.
Namaz-e-Shab Mein Oss Ne Aap Ko Manga Ali Akbar.

3
Khuda Ke Ladlay Ke Ladlay Ho Tum Jabhi Rab Ne.
Azaan Mein Bhi Tumhara Naam Hai Rakha Ali Akbar.
Farishtay Anbiya Sab Gyarween Shabaan Ko Aate Hain.
Dar-e-Shabeer Par Lainay Tera Sadqa Ali Akbar.

4
Na Kyun Kar Yaad Tujh Ko Dekh Kar ALLAH Ki Aaye.
Tera Dada Hai Jab ALLAH Ka Chehra Ali Akbar.
Woh Kagaz Wazan Mein Yousuf Ke Hum Pala Nazar Aaya.
Likha Tha Jis Pe Khali Naam Hi Tera Ali Akbar.

5
Ijazat Dein Muhammad Tu Ossay Bhi Aamna Keh Doon.
Woh Jis Bibi Ne Tum Ko Gaud Mein Pala Ali Akbar.
 Takallum Kaisay Pehchanein Gaye Oss Ko Loag Mehshar Mein.
Rasool ALLAH Ke Jab Sath Aaye Ga Ali Akbar.
