Kehti hai sakina

(Kehti hai sakina )..(4)

(Alatash ki sada e deti hai)

(Kehti hai sakina)..(2)

(1) Tab tum gaye dariya par,

Wapas nahi aye

pyasa hai mera asghar(a.s)

pyase hai wo pyaree

intezar jo karti ho aao na chacha jaan

(Kehti hai sakina )..(4)

(2) kehti thi phuphi meri,

ayenge chacha jaan

deti thi dilasa mujhe,

meri phuphi amma

sabr karo aye jaan ayenge chacha jaan

(Kehti hai sakina )..(4)

(3) baba nazar ane lage,

aap ka alam bhi

tab bhi meri ek aas,

ammu nahi tooti

afsous hai ammu tum bhi tho na aye na

(Kehti hai sakina )..(4)

(4) ammu tumhare baad,

Kya kya na wo guzre

Shamiyou ne akaar,

Gauhar mere chinee

Kitna muje wo dard pohoncha tha chacha jaan

(Kehti hai sakina )..(4)

(5) ammu hame bahooth

phirwaya gaya tha,

yaad ata hai manzar

tadpayagaya tha

mai tumko bolati rahi tum bhi tho na aye na

(Kehti hai sakina )..(4)

(6) Syed Raza hussain,

Zehra ke azadar

Rizvi jo tumne likha,

Shahzadi ka ye bain

bas khatm kar nisaar ye ghum ye dastaan

(kehti hai sakina)..(4)

alatash ki sadae (deti hai sakina)..(2)
