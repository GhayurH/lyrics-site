Nana Teri Zainab Nu Ais Sham De Lokaan Ne Baon Dard Wakhaye Ne.
Mere Veeraan Diyaan Lashaan Be-Goro Kafan Raiyaan Aina Jashan Manaye Ne.

1
Pursay De Layi Nana Koi Naiziyaan Sang Aaya Koi Sholiyaan Sang Aaya.
Zahra Diyaan Dhiyaan De Kise Burqay Lahaye Te Kise Khaimay Jalaye Ne.
Karbal Ton Turi Nana Main Baaj Ridawaan De Onna Okhiyaan Rawaan Te.
Taidi Dhiyaan Te Nohaan Nu Kar Qaid Musalmaan Bazar Sajaye Ne.

2
Saday Chehre V Zakhmi Hin Hath Pair V Zakhmi Hin Saday Zakhmaa V Zakhmi Hin.
Pardais Che Dhiyaan Ne Piyo Fateh-e-Khaibar De Ainj Badlay Chukaye Ne.
Main Aahdi Raiyaan Wal Wal Main Dhi Haan Muhammad Di Naalay Bachri Aan Zahra Di.
Tableeg De Badlay Wich Ainj Ajar Dita Lokaan Onna Pathar Wasaye Ne.

3
Darbar Sharabi Da No Ghantay Di Hik Peshi Teri Dhiyaan Te Nohaan Di.
Gustakh Fizawaan Ne Athro Mere Abid De Marjan Banaye Ne.
Kamsin Mere Lashkar De Hin Dorday Ontaan Te Mitiyaan Te Rahe Jhanrday.
Majboor Jay San Mawaan Oh Sikdiyaan Reh Gayiyaan Kise Baal Na Chaye Ne.
