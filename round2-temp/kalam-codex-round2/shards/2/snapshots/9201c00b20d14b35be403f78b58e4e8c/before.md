1
Nana Sughra Nu Raindiyaan Ne Tangaan Akbar Veer Diyaan Asghar Khan
Meri Ujari Jhook Wasa De Sanwaan Muk Chaliyaan Ne Humsheer Diyaan.
Main Qasad Nu Kehya Veer Akbar Nu Kehwein Mere Lafzaan Nu Veran Dair Tak Parhda Rawin.
Meri Akhiyaan Samjhein Veran Akbar Aye Satraan Nayi Tehreer Diyaan.

2
Main Suniya Aye Sitam Saday Khaimay Sarhay Jashan Wakhrey Ne Kitay Sangh Te Sir Charhay.
Ummat Ne Mubarakaan Ditiyaan Aa Ke Aynj Akbar Nu Jageer Diyaan.
Neziyaan Kita Aye Ki Naal Teraan Pochaan Khuwab Jo Wekhiya Aye Ki Taberaan Pochaan.
Assi Ibrahim Nu Ditiyaan Nana Sab Khabran Khud Tabeer Diyaan.

3
Main Te Karni Aan Dua Wain Bach Jandi Aye Dadi De Wehray Andar Khak Jayi Uddi Aye.
Main Mangni Aan Jis Dam Nana Khairaan Apne Asghar Chan Veer Diyaan.
Ghar Taun Akbar Nu Giyon Chodah Sadiyaan Hoiyaan Khushk Sughra Diyaan Nayi Ajay Akhiyaan Hoiyaan.
Na Safar Hussain Da Mukya Akbar Na E Hawaan Humsheer Diyaan.
