1
Mil Gayi Tujh Ko Khudai Ya Ali Kehnay Kay Bad Mir Hasan Mir.
Haan Yehi Hota Hai Bhai Ya Ali Kehnay Kay Bad.
Jaam-e-Kausar Le Liya Firdos Mein Ghar Le Liya.
Iss Qadar Dolat Kamai Ya Ali Kehnay Kay Bad.

2
Hajiyon Ko Ghaziyon Ko Khum Mein Sehba-e-Wila.
Khud Muhammad Ne Pilai Ya Ali Kehnay Kay Bad.
Main Ne Pocha Kamyabi Ki Sanad Milti Hai Kab.
Arsh Se Aawaz Aayi Ya Ali Kehnay Kay Bad.

3
Main Yahan Zinda Raha Tu Kya Hoi Hairat Ki Baat.
Kab Kissi Ko Maut Aayi Ya Ali Kehnay Kay Bad.
Aye Musalmaan Aa Galay Mil Tu Bhi Momin Ho Gaya.
Kaisa Jaghra Kya Larai Ya Ali Kehnay Kay Bad.

4
Maut Se Ik Rooz Panja Aazmai Ho Gayi.
Toor Di Main Ne Kalai Ya Ali Kehnay Kay Bad.
Qalb-e-Abuzar Ki Tarha Hur Ke Muqadar Ki Tarha.
Roshni Gohar Ne Pai Ya Ali Kehnay Kay Bad.
