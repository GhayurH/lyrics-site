Mere Malik Mere Mukhtar Tu Sab Janta Hai.

1
Beshak Yehi Sach Hai Keh Gunahgar Hain Ya Rab.
Lekin Tere Sarwar Ke Azadar Hain Ya Rab.
Tu Baksh De Aulad-e-Payamber Ke Tasaduq.
Bande Tere Bakhshish Ke Talabgar Hain Ya Rab.

2
Koan Hai Kitna Gunhagar Tu Sab Janta Hai.
Aib Dunya Ki Nigahon Se Nihan Hain Lekin.
Tu Hai Hum Sab Se Khabardar Tu Sab Janta Hai.
Khuwaish-e-Nafs Ki Takmeel Mein Dunya Wale.
Bhool Jate Hain Yeh Har Bar Tu Sab Janta Hai.
Qatal Karte Hain Azadaron Ko Chup Jate Hain.
Be-Khabar Hain Yeh Jafa Kar Tu Sab Janta Hai.
Phaink Kar Dushman-e-Haider Pe Falak Se Pathar.
Kar Diya Tu Ne Yeh Izhar Tu Sab Janta Hai.
Wasta Deta Hai Aulad-e-Nabi Ka Yasir
Kar Madad Meri Madadgar Tu Sab Janta Hai.
