Khatam Kab Hai Syeda Par Faisla Darbar Ka.
Karbala Barpa Kare Ga Saneha Darbar Ka.

1
Bad-e-Sarwar Gar Karon Tafseer Main Darbar Ki.
Qatal-e-Zahra Naam Rakh Doon Dosra Darbar Ka.
Dekh Kar Maa Ki Zaeefi Sochti Thin Betiyaan.
Kis Qadar Dushwar Hai Yeh Rasta Darbar Ka.

2
Haye Zahra Keh Ke Tanhai Mein Rote The Sada.
Haal Jab Bhi Yaad Karte Murtaza Darbar Ka.
Sazish-e-Ahl-e-Saqeefa Ka Yeh Hasil Dekhiye.
Karbala Se Sham Tak Hai Silsila Darbar Ka.

3
Main Tu Bint-e-Mustafa Hoon Our Woh Bint-e-Ali.
Kis Tarha Zainab Kare Gi Samna Darbar Ka.
 Jaun Bint-e-Mustafa Ab Bhi Hain Roti Qabar Mein.
Dard Hi Itna Bara Tha Ba-Khuda Darbar Ka.
