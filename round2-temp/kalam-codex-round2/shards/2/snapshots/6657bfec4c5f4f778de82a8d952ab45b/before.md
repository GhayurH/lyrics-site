Kehtay thay ye Abbas Sakina se ke dilbar
De do hamay mashkeeza abhi latay hain bhar kar

1
Bas itna karam haal pe Ammo ke ye kar do
Baba se riza ran ki dila do hamain ja kar

2
Ye suntay ke mashkeeza Sakina ne uthaya
Pohonchi liye Ammo ko huzoor e Shah e Safdar

3
Seenay se lipat jao chacha ke karo rukhsat
Tab jayain sooye nehr teri mashk ko le kar

4
Ho jaye agar dair to ghabrana na bibi
Pani abhi layain gay jo aa jaye mayassar

5
Tum tarrpo gi ya ro gi ya noha karo gi
Ham bhi wahan din raat guzarain gay tarrap kar

6
Parrhtay huay ye Noha alam laye sheh e deen
Matam karo maray gaye Abbas e dilawar

7
Woh soz day nohay ko Anees aap ke Zainab
Sun le to kaleja phatay ronay lagay pathar
