1
Munfarid Mojza-e-Noor-e-Khuda Hai Asghar.
Cheh Mahino Mein Rasoolon Se Bara Hai Asghar.
Tu Ne To Paon Zameen Par Nahi Rakhe Lekin.
Tere Pairon Pe Yeh Islam Khara Hai Asghar.

2
Yeh Batane Ko Keh Bait Nahi Karte Hum Loag.
Muthiyaan Beech Ke Maidaan Ko Chala Hai Asghar.
Aa Ke Dewar-e-Haram Choom Le Seena Mera.
Dil Ke Gehware Mein Mehman Howa Hai Asghar.

3
Chodah Sadiyon Se Hakomat Nahi Badli Isski.
Aaj Tak Mulk-e-Tabasum Ka Khuda Hai Asghar.
Sanam Hoi Iss Tarha Dada Ki Shujat Iss Mein.
Main Ne Likha Tha Ali Our Parha Hai Asghar.

4
Iss Ko Kehte Hain Haqeeqat Mein Shahadat Ka Asar.
Gardano Par Mere Bachoon Ne Likha Hai Asghar.
Titliyaan Shakh-e-Gulistaan Se Jo Lipti Hain Bilal.
Aisa Lagta Hai Ke Pholon Pe Likha Hai Asghar.
