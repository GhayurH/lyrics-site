Nazara Kya Hoga Mir Sajjad Mir Lyrics
Zahra Ke Ghar Mein Socho Tu Shabaan Ka Nazara Kya Hoga.
Utray Hain Jahan Itnay Soraj Oss Ghar Ka Ujala Kya Hoga.
Zainab Ki Aamad Ka Manzar, ALLAH ALLAH Haider Haider.
Jo Shair-e-Khuda Ke Lab Par Tha Beti Ka Qaseeda Kya Hoga.
Aate Hi Musallay Par Sheh Ne Madar Ke Sath Ibadat Ki.
Jab Pehla Sajdah Aisa Hai, Phir Aakhri Sajdah Kya Hoga.
Abbas Ko Seene Se Har Dam Shabbir Laga Kar Rakhein Gay.
Tum Kis Ka Jhulao Gay Jhola Jibreel Tumhara Kya Hoga.
Siffeen Mein Malik Kehte They, Malik Abbas Qayamat Hai.
Yeh Terah Baras Ka Hamla Tha Batees Baras Ka Kya Hoga.
Rajat Ke Door Mein Aaein Gay Debal, Hasaan-o- Anees-o- Dabeer.
Yeh Sare Mil Kar Likhein Gay Qasim Ka Jo Sehra Kya Hoga.
Zulfein Kaisi, Kaisi Aankhein Kaisa Chehra Ho Moazin Ka.
ALLAH Ne Zainab Se Pocha Akbar Ka Sarapa Kya Hoga.
Do Ungli Par Baab-e-Khaibar, Haider Ne Rakha Our Hans Ke Kaha.
Itna Tu Halka Hai Yeh Dar Ab Iss Se Halka Kya Hoga.
Nazdeek Nahi Aate Munkir Dartay Hain Hath Lagane Se.
Jis Shair Ka Parcham Aisa Ho, Oss Shair Ka Panja Kya Hoga.
 Sajjad Tere Dar Se Maula, Uthay Ga Nahi Misal-e-Salmaan.
Dewana Keh Kar Marein Gay Our Iss Se Zyada Kya Hoga.
