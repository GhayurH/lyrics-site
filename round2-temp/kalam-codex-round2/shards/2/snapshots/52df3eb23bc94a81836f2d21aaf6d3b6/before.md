Main Hussain Hun Ali Shanawar Lyrics

1
Gonji Bawaqt-e-Asar Sada Main Hussain Hoon.
Kato Na Mera Khushk Galla Main Hussain Hoon.
Sokhi Hoi Hain Shimar Mere Halq Ki Ragein.
Aahista Tu Churi Ko Chala Main Hussain Hoon.
Amma Kissi Ne Pani Ki Ik Bond Bhi Na Di.
Main Bar Bar Kehta Raha Main Hussain Hoon.

2

Aayi Furat Par Jo Sada Main Hussain Hoon.
Zanu Pe Rakh Ke Sar Ko Kaha Ik Zaeef Ne.
Akbar Kahan Hai Dard Bata Main Hussain Hoon.

3
