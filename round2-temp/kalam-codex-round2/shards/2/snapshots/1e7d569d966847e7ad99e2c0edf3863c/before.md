1
Paighambar-e-Aalam Ki Tasveer Ali Akbar.
Shabir Ki Aankhon Ki Tanveer Ali Akbar.
Walnajam Si Aankhein Hain Walail Se Gaiso Hain.
Zainab Ki Duaon Ki Taseer Ali Akbar.

2
Husan-e-Rukh-e-Yousuf Tu Bas Khuwab-e-Zulekha Hai.
Khuwab-e-Rukh-e-Yousuf Ki Tabeer Ali Akbar.
Haider Ka Muhammad Ka Hasnain Ka Zahra Ka.
Jo Noor Hai Unn Sab Ki Zanjeer Ali Akbar.

3
Lab Phool Dahan Khushbu Abro Bhi Hilali Hain.
Yani Yad-e-Qudrat Ki Tameer Ali Akbar.
Qamat Hai Muhammad Ki Qowat Shah-e-Mardaan Ki.
Quran-e-Imamat Ki Tehreer Ali Akbar.

4
Islam Hi Mar Jata Gar Jan Ko Dene Mein.
Ik Lamhe Ko Kar Dete Takheer Ali Akbar.
Lagta Tha Muhammad Hain Mehrab-e-Ibadat Mein.
Jab Kehte The Masjid Mein Takbeer Ali Akbar.

5
Khon-e-Abu Talib Se Nisbat Ki Gawahi Hai.
Mati Ko Kiya Tum Ne Akseer Ali Akbar.
Zainab Ke Waseele Se Rehan Dua Mango.
Lamhoon Mein Badal Dein Gaye Taqdeer Ali Akbar.
