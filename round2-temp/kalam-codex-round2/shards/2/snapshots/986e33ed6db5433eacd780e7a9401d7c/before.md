1
Kis Bulandi Par Khudaya Martaba Zahra Ka Hai.
Parda-e-Meraj Mein Bhi Tazkira Zahra Ka Hai.
Be-Niyazaon Mein Kahin Aapas Mein Hota Hai Hisab.
Jo Khuda Ka Hai Samajh Lein Fatima Zahra Ka Hai.

2
Shabar-o-Shabir Jaise Aliman-e-Ghaiab Bhi.
Dars Letay Hain Jahan Woh Madrisa Zahra Ka Hai.
Tohfa-e-Shadi Diya Dono Ko Yeh ALLAH Ne.
Arsh Haider Ka Hai Shehar-e-Karbala Zahra Ka Hai.

3
Iss Makaan Par Sorah-e-Kausar Ki Takhti Hai Lagi.
Ay Khuda-e-Lam Yazal Kaba Bhi Kya Zahra Ka Hai.
Arsh Ka Malik Takallum Aa Raha Hai Bar Bar.
Ossko Bhi Maloom Hai Farsh-e-Azza Zahra Ka Hai.
