Panjtan Ke Astane Ki Alag Hi Baat Hai Write Up
Sar Yahan Aa Kar Jhukane Ki Alag Hi Baat Hai.
Youn Tu Janat Ki Ghiza Khud Bhi Tabaruk Hai Magar.

Daar Se Midhat Sunane Ki Alag Hi Baat Hai.
