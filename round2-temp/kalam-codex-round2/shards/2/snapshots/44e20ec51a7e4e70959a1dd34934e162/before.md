Mera Naam Hai Shabbir Mir Hasan Mir.
Main Sibt-e-Payamber Hoon Mera Naam Hai Shabeer.

1
Kun Keh Ke Badal Sakta Hoon Main Waqt Ki Tehreer Mera Naam Hai Shabeer.
Khud Rab Ne Banaya Hai Mujhe Katib-e-Taqdeer Mera Naam Hai Shabeer.
Kehta Raha Rahib Se Yeh Masoom Iradah.
Aoulad Tujhey Deta Main Aadam Se Zyada.

2
Rahib Mujhe Rokay Hai Tere Zarf Ki Zanjeer.
Bint-e-Sheh-e-Lolaak Ki Jaan Kaun Hai Main Hoon.
Sardar-e-Jawanan-e-Jinna Kaun Hai Main Hoon.
Hai Bag-e-Jinna Mere Azadaron Ki Jageer.

3
Har Aik Kari Jis Ki Hai Tauheed Ka Nagma.
Woh Jis Se Bajay Dehar Mein Tauheed Ka Danka.
Abid Mera Pehne Ga Wohi Aahni Zanjeer.
Khaif Rahe Sakhti Se Teri Sare Payamber.

4
Har Chand Qadam Nabiyon Ke Jam Paye Na Tujh Par.
Kar Doon Ga Magar Karb-o-Bala Main Tujhey Taskheer.
Khaliq Ne Mere Nana Pe Quran Utara.
Baba Ke Liye Arsh Se Utra Hai Sitara.

5
Utari Hai Meri Maa Ke Liye Aaya-e-Tatheer.
Mehsos Yeh Hota Hai Keh Farmatay Hain Shabeer.
Ustad Kahin Gay Tere Ustad Ko Sab Mir.
Tum Dono Ki Karwaon Ga Main Izzat-o-Tauqeer.
