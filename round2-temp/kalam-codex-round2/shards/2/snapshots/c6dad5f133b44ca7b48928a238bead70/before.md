Kiya Hoti Hai Gurbat Sonu Monu 2023.
Kya Hoti Hai Ghurbat Yeh Bataya Gaya Baba.
Jab Aap Ke Lashay Se Uthaya Gaya Baba.
Jab Aap Ke Lashay Se Uthaya Tha Laeen Ne.
Tab Pehla Tamacha Bhi Lagaya Gaya Baba.
Maqtal Mein Jahan Aap Ki Gardan Ka Lahu Tha.
Oss Ja Pe Lahu Mujh Ko Rulaya Gaya Baba.
Mehshar Tha Qayamat Thi Jo Lashay Pe Chacha Ke.
Baloon Se Pakar Kar Mujhe Laya Gaya Baba.
Pehle Tu Tamachon Se Rulaya Gaya Mujh Ko.
Roi Jo Mujhe Our Rulaya Gaya Baba.
Dadi Ki Tarha Zakhmi Howa Hai Mera Pehlu.
Neza Mere Pehlu Mein Chubhaya Gaya Baba.
Beti Ne Kaha:NOOR ALI: Baap Ke Sar Se.
Kiya Haal Gareebon Ka Bataya Gaya Baba.
