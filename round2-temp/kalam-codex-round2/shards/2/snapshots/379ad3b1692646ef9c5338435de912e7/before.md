1
Aayaton Par Khumar Tari Hai.
Kar Ke Zainab Teri Sana Hum Ne.
Phir Tahajud Mein Shab Guzari Hai.
Jis Musallay Pe Aaye They Hasnain.
Rab Ne Zainab Wahin Utari Hai.
Sun Ke Khutba Ali Bhi Kehte Hain.
Meri Har Baat Ab Tumhari Hai.
Phir Gaya Hai Yazid Ka Chehra.
Tum Ne Baiyat Youn Munh Pe Mari Hai.
Khud Ko Bolay Kaneez-e-Zainab Woh.
Jis Ko Parday Ki Pasdari Hai.
Kaisi Yeh Hamd-e-Kirdgari Hai.
Kat Gaya Sar Namaz Jari Hai.
Mit Gaye Fatway Our Fatwa Farosh.
Sheh Ka Matam Abhi Bhi Jari Hai.
Kyun Banata Hai Munh Tabarey Par.
Kya Teri Unn Se Rishtay Dari Hai.
Jab Kiya Main Ne Matam-e-Shabeer.
Maa Ne Meri Nazar Utari Hai.
Tere Akbar Ka Hai Karam Bi Bibi.
Yeh Jo Ab Tak Azan Jari Hai.
Aye Takallum Woh Subh-e-Qismat Hai.
Sham Mein Sham Jo Guzari Hai.
