1
Qaid Khana Hai Aseri Hai Fatemah Ladak.
Qaid Khana Hai Aseeri Hai Sitam Hai Dar Hai.
Ab Sakina Ke Liye Yeh Hi Andhera Ghar Hai.
Yaad Asghar Ko Ajab Gham Ke Hawalay Se Kiya.
Maa Yeh Kehti Thi Yehi Teer Ossay Ran Mein Laga.
Dagh Rasi Ka Jahan Halq-e-Sakina Par Hai.
Apne Bachoon Ko Tu Seeno Se Lagaye Hoye Hai.
Par Sakina Ke Liye Sang Uthaye Hoye Hai.
Ahl-e-Aoulad Hai Har Shami Magar Pathar Hai.
Baap Ki Gaud Mein Sar Rakh Ke Jo Soti Thi Kabhi.
Qaid Khane Mein Koi Dekhey Ghareebi Oss Ki.
Aaj Aagosh-e-Sakina Mein Padar Ka Sar Hai.
Hind Ki Beti Yeh Boli Keh Khulay Sar Kyun Ho.
Ro Ke Boli Yeh Sakina Keh Khabar Hai Tum Ko.
Nook-e-Naiza Pe Woh Choti Si Meri Chadar Hai.
Ro Ke Phir Bibi Taruf Youn Karati Apna.
Main Sakina Hoon Woh Naizay Pe Hai Asghar Mera.
Woh Jo Ik Chand Sa Sar Hai Woh Ali Akbar Hai.
Haye Masoomo Ki Masoom Si Bachi Akbar.
Sham Ke Bachoon Se Kehti Thi Sakina Aksar.
Hum Tu Mehmaan Hain Madine Mein Hamara Ghar Hai.
