Na veer qadi jawan, bhenaa karan duawan

1
Main vekhan teriyan rahwan
Kado anaa ay Akbar mur watanaa te ajaa veerna

2
Tera ghar ton jana Akbar keeta mainu beemar ay
Meriyan mudatan diyan aiss marzan nu mukaa jaa veerna

3
Mainu yaqeen Akbar auna ay tu zaroor ay
Behn vichri da ayho maan vadhawan ajaa veerna

4
Behna de veer rowan gal laa ke khud manaunday
Dukhi Sughra nu veeran aap manawan ajaa veerna

5
Tera intezaar kar ke din raat roundi aan main
Kis din auna ay ek vaari eh dasa jaa veerna

6
Raatan haneriyan vich uth uth ke tenu vekhan
Rona ruk jaye mera behn nu seenay laa jaa veerna

7
Mohsin mehboob tera har velay zikr karday
Izzatan ehna diyan mehshar taayian vadha jaa veerna
