1
Kis Ko Teri Azmat Ka Ho Ahsas Sakina.
Jhuk Jata Hai Aagay Tere Abbas, Sakina.
Yeh Dad Qasiday Pe Nahi Kuch Mera Kamal.
Khud Lafz Bana Deti Hai Almas Sakina.

2
Kehte Hain Malak Loh Pe Kya Khoob Samaa Hai.
Shabeer Qalamkar Hain Qirtas Sakina.
Iss Wastay Jhukney Nahi Deti Hai Alam Tu.
Ghazi Ki Wafa Ka Hai Tujhe Pas Sakina.

3
Dunya Ke Masail Se Bara Tang Hoon Bibi.
Aana Hai Mujhe Jald Tere Pas Sakina.
Asghar Ke Tofail Bibi Azadar Ka Tere.
Qarza Ho Ada Door Ho Aflas Sakina.

4
Chadar Se Gurezaan Ho Azadar Jo Teri.
Kya Oss Ko Tere Parday Ka Ahsas Sakina.
Aysar Ke Quran Mein Taseer Jo Dekha.
Al-Hamd Khadija Hai Tu Wal Naas Sakina.
