1
Masooma e Qum Ky Leay Syed Qamar Abbas Naqvi.
Manqabat Jab Bhi Parhon Masooma-e-Qum Ke Liye.
Lafz Main Quraan Se Loon Masooma-e-Qum Ke Liye.
So Qadam Barh Aaye Gi Meri Taraf Khuld-e-Barin.
Ik Qadam Gar Main Barhoon Masooma-e-Qum Ke Liye.
Baap Our Bhai Imam Ism-e-Mubarak Fatima.
Iss Se Aagay Kya Kahon Masooma-e-Qum Ke Liye.
Sar Bulandi Paye Ga Dono Jahan Mein Sar Wohi.
Jo Bhi Sar Hai Sar Nagoon Masooma-e-Qum Ke Liye.
Hota Hai Masroof Midhat Dil Ki Har Dharkan Ke Sath.
Meri Shiryano Ka Khoon Masooma-e-Qum Ke Liye.
Kya Biyaan Koi Karey Tauseef Quraan Ki Tarha.
Min-o-An Our Joon Ki Toon Masooma-e-Qum Ke Liye.
Jis Ko Masoomeen Ki Imdad Par Shak Ho Sohail.
Woh Kahey Ga Kya Likhon Masooma-e-Qum Ke Liye.
