Nazool e Kausar
Aik Hasrat Mein Keh Zahra Bhi Zameen Par Aaye
Iss Liye Arsh Pe So Bar Payamber Aaye
Teen Din Rotiyaan Lainay Ke Bahane Bibi
Tere Khadam Tere Nokar Tere Chakar Aaye
Kaisa Dar Hai Keh Kabhi Aate Hain Bachoon Ke Libas
Koi Aayat Kabhi Tatheer Ki Le Kar Aaye
Kaisay Khud Chalti Hai Karti Hai Tilawat Kaisay
Sheikh Ko Chaki Ke Chakar Se Bhi Chakar Aaye
Kis Bulandi Pe Hai Ya Rab Yeh Kisaye Zahra
Jis Ke Saye Mein Ali Sar Ko Jhuka Kar Aaye
Mustafa Tanha Nahi Aaye Hain Parhne Ko Darood
Teri Chokhat Pe Sawa Lakh Payamber Aaye
Noor-e-Zahra Se Hoi Khalq Ali Ki Talwar
Le Ke Jibreel Jisay Apne Paron Par Aaye
Panjtan Jate Hain Najran Se Karne Ko Jehad
Koi Sacha Hai Madine Mein Tu Bahir Aaye
Inna Ataina Mein Shamil Hai Yeh Bibi AKBAR
Kaun Hai Ab Jo Khadija Ke Barabar Aaye
