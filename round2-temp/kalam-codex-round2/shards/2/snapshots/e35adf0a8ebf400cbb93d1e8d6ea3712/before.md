Mardam-e-Chashm Mere Dedah-e-Purnam Mein Rahe.
Khak Ache Hoon Wo Bemar Jo Shabnam Mein Rahe.

1
Khalq Masroof-e-Azza Shah Ke Matam Mein Rahe.
Hum Rahein Gham Mein Sada Mujrai Gham Hum Mein Rahe.
Dil Mein Dard Ashk-e-Azza Dedah-e-Purnam Mein Rahe.
Gham Muharam Mein Rahe Mujrai Hum Gham Mein Rahe.

2
Sar Khule Fatima Jab Majlis-e-Matam Mein Rahe.
Chahe Aankh Pe Romaal Muharam Mein Rahe.
Mujrai Hum Gham Sultan-e-Do Alam Mein Rahe.
Sorat-e-Shama Azza Halqa-e-Matam Mein Rahe.

3
Dast-e-Mazgaan Mein Sadaye Mir Sar Shak-e-Matam.
Sabha Esa Ki Tarha Panja-e-Mariyam Mein Rahe.
Gham-e-Sarwar Ki Ibadat Ka Mahina Hai Yeh.
Shamil-e-Aab-e-Wazoo Ashak Muharam Mein Rahe.

4
Dor-e-Aalim Mein Faqat Khana Nashini Dekhi.
Hum Nagine Ki Tarha Halqa-e-Khatam Mein Rahe.
Mutkalim Hain Rahi Fikr-e-Dalil-e-Aala.
Hujat Allah Ke Hum Muntazir Aalam Mein Rahe.

5
Matam-e-Aal-e-Muhammad Ki Safoo Par Hai Qaiyam.
Sahab-e-Amar Ke Mehkoom Hi Aalam Mein Rahe.
Aankh Mein Reh Ke Rahe Ashak Jalaye Murdam.
Yeh BHi Esa Ki Tarha Daman-e-Mariyam Mein Rahe.

6
Sozan-e-Hazrat-e-Esa Ki Tarha Se Khama.
Wasf-e-Zainab Ke Sabab Risht-e-Mariyam Mein Rahe.
Fikar-e-Mani Bhi Rahi Fikr-e-Sukhan Mein Nazim.
Hum Jiss Aalam Mein Rahe Our Hi Aalam Mein Rahe.
