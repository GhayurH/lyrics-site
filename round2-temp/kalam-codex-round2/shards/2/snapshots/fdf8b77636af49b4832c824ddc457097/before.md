Waqar-e-Deen-e-Khuda hay Hussain ka matam
Rassol-e-Haq say wafa Hay Hussain ka matam

1
1) Qaza Namaz Ki Roza Zakat Hajj Ki Hai
Magar Kabhi na Kaza Hussain ka matam

2
2) Tera naseeb Tu Zahra kai mujramon main huwa
Mujhai to Rab ki ata hai Hussain ka matam

3
3) Qasam Ali ki who mehshar main tajwar hoga
Kai jin kai ghar main bapa hai Hussain ka matam

4
4) Ba roz-e-hashar wo Abid kai paas baithai ga
Zehar nay kiya hay Hussain ka matam
