Kyun Tur Gaya Ayein Veera Har Pasay Ne Haneray
Mein Kandaan Naal Turni Aan Hath Veer Nahin Labday Mere
Khuda Jane Kiwein Abid Churaiyaan Meriyaan Banwaan
Mainu Lawan Layi Abid Ne Laiyaan San Okhiyaan Sanwaan
Mein Nayi Phardi Zanjeeraan Nu Nayi Zakhm Dukhandi Tere
Mainu Babay Ne Rab Kolon Duawaan Naal Mangiya Si
Te Ik Babay Da Sir Kolon Jadon Sajjad Langiya Si
Oh Yadaan Maut Ban Mainu Hun Veer Ne Paye Ghairay
Mere Dardaan De Hun Veera Gawah Zindan Rainay Ne
Jeray Ban Gaye Ne Baqi Sah Mein Wich Zindan Lainay Ne
Kare Aazad Na Koi Bazaraan De Wich Na Phairay
Kadi Qasim Nazar Aaway Kadi Aaway Ali Akbar
Kadi Aoun -o- Muhammad Te Kadi Aaway Ali Asghar
Mein V Karbal Che Mar Jandi Jithay Veraan Ne Laye Dheray
