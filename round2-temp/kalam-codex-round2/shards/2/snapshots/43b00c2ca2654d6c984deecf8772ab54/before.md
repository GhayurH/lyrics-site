Khuda Ka Bin Gaya Hota Sarwar Raza Karbalai.

1
Agar Banday Tu Sach Much Mein Khuda Ka Ban Gaya Hota.
Tu Razi Phir Zamane Mein Tujhi Se Mustafa Hota.
Buttoon Ko Kash Tu Toray Khuda Se Nata Gar Joray.
Tujhe Bhi Khuld Mein Banday Khuda Ne Ghar Diya Hota.
Lagata Dil Na Dunya Mein Kahey Ga Qabar Mein Murdah.
Kabhi Roza Rakha Hota Kabhi Sajdah Kiya Hota.

2
Shifat Karne Ummat Ki Jo Aaein Gay Tu Bolo Gay.
Mera Bhi Rehnuma Jag Mein Muhammad Mustafa Hota.
Hasan Ka Dekh Kar Chehra Tu Bola Hazrat-e-Yousuf.
Hasan Ki Tarha Se Main Bhi Zamane Mein Haseen Hota.
Main Dekhon Roza-e-Sarwar Yehi Khuwaish Hai Asif Ki.
Sharf Dunya Mein Gar Maula Mujhe Bhi Mil Gaya Hota.
