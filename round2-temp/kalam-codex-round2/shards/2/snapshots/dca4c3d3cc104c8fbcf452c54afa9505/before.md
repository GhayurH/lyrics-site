1
Mery Laal Chaiti Ajaa Kazmi Brothers.
Hunr Shama Pe Gayiyaan Mere Lal Cheti Aa Ja.
Asghar Main Teri Maa Takdi Aan Tere Rah.
Tera Jhola Main Jholawan Tainu Loriyaan Sunrawaan.
Tere Baad Nayi Aye Rayi Mainu Jeenr Di Aye Chah.
Nayi Chaan Te Bhainra Asghar Tere Baad Teri Maa Ne.
Deni Aan Main Sada Asghar Tu Wal Ke Aa.
Kiwein Pyas Main Bhojawan Panri Main Kinj Pilawaan.
Sir Te Nayi Rida Rehya Nayi Koi Aasra.
Jis Naizay Naal Teri Tehqeer Keeti Zalim.
Osay Naizay Naal Meri Layi Zalimaan Rida.
Hurmal Da Teer Tere Halqoom Te Ja Lagiya.
Main Wekhdi Rayiyaan Jiwein Tu Qatal Hoya.
Sehra De Wich Sakina Labdi Aye Tainu Asghar.
Mangdi Aye Ayeh Dua Mainu Veer Fair Mila.
Mangda Aye Amar Dua Koi Maa Tu Juda Na Howay.
Hoya Jiwein Juda, Bachra Rubab Da Asghar Rubab Da.
