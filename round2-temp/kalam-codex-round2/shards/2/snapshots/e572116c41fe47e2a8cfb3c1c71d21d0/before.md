Pur dard shahadat hai ye mohsin ki shahadat
Zehra pe musibat hai, ye mohsin ki shahadat!!

1
Darwaza jala dala hai hai, ghar bhi giraya haii;
Wa-allah qayamat hai, ye mohsin ki shahahadat!!

2
Jaltey huye darwaze ki hai ek musibat;
Us par se qayamat hai, ye mohsin ki shahadat!!

3
Eezaaein hayn dein jisne bhi khatoon e jenaan ko;
Us shakhs pe lanat hai, ye mohsin ki shahadat!!

4
Marqad me payambar ko bhi takleaf hai pahunchee;
Pur dard qayamat hai, ye mohsin ki shahadat!!

5
Hai zulm ki had aaj FIDA aal e nabi par;
Aafat hai, sad aafat hai, ye mohsin shahadat!!
