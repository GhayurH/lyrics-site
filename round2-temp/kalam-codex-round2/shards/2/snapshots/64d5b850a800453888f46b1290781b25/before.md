Mitti Madinay Ki Mukhtar Hussain Fatehpuri.

1
Jo Aankhon Mein Lagai Jaye Gi Mitti Madine Ki.
Tu Jalway Arsh Ke Dikhlaye Gi Mitti Madine Ki.
Safa, Marwa, Hira Ki Chotiyaan Bolein Shab-e-Hijrat.
Hamarey Aagay Ab Itraye Gi Mitti Madine Ki.
Yahan Kya Kam Hathoon Ka Yahan Kya Zikar Daman Ka.
Keh Palkon Se Uthai Jaye Gi Mitti Madine Ki.

2
Woh Insaan Jo Na Hasil Kar Sakay Irfaan Muhammad Ka.
Ossay Hargiz Nahi Apnaye Gi Mitti Madine Ki.
Juda Hoti Nahi Ibn-e-Ali Ke Paye Athar Se.
Zameen-e-Karbala Tak Jaye Gi Mitti Madine Ki.
Bhala Mujh Ko Zameen-e-Hind Kab Tak Rook Sakti Hai.
 Sarosh Ik Din Madine Jaye Gi Mitti Madine Ki.
