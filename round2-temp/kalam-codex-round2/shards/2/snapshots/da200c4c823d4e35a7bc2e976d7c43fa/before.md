Nad-e-Ali Ka Wird Karo Yeh Gham Wam Dar War Kya.
Nad-e-Ali Ke Samne Koi Mantar Wantar Kya.

1
Sher-e-Khuda Ne Khud Apni Talwar Ko Roka Tha.
Warna Aye Jibreel Tumhare Shehpar Wehpar Kya.
Jis Ne Apne Bachpan Mein Azdar Ko Cheera Ho.
Oss Ko Bhari Jawani Mein Yeh Khaibar Waibar Kya.

2
Dono Jahan Bhi Iss Ke Muqabil Aaye Tu Harein Gaye.
Haris Waris Marhab Warhab Antar Wantar Kya.
Jis Ke Dil Pe Hub-e-Ali Ki Mohar Lagi Hogi.
Oss Ke Liye Yeh Barzakh Warzakh Mehshar Wehshar Kya.

3
Lashkar-e-Batil Kyun Apni Tadaad Pe Nazaan Hai.
Izn Mile Abbas KO Tu Phir Lashkar Washkar Kya.
Ishq-e-Ali Ki Rah Mein Tu Jibreel Bhi Qasir Hain.
Ishq-e-Ali Ki Rah Mein Phir Yeh Nayyar Wayyar Kya.
