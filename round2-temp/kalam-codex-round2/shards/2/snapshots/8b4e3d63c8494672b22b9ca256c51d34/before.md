1
Ooncha Rahay Apna Alam – Khasosi Kalam – Sachay Bahi.
Jab Tak Hai Door-e-Aasmaan.
Sun Le Yeh Har Peer-o-Jawaan.
Aawaz Par Shabir Ki Barhta Rahe Yeh Karwaan.

2
Rukne Na Paein Yeh Qadam.
Har Qalab Par Chate Chalo.
Dunya Ko Batlate Chalo.
Ik Ik Mohaz-e-Zulm Ki Tasveer Dikhlate Chalo.

3
Har Dil Mein Bhar Do Sheh Ka Gham.
Kab Tak Na Mane Ga Koi.
Woh Waqt Aaye Ga Kabhi.
Har Qalb Par Cha Jaye Ga Apna Hussain Ibn-e-Ali.

4
Iss Dar Pe Har Sar Ho Ga Kham.
Har Darood Our Har Salam Madar-e-Hussain Tere Naam.
Zoja-e-Ali Sar Buland Fatima Azeem Arjumand.
Jis Ke Aastan-e-Pak Ke.

5
Qudsiyaan-e-Arsh Hain Ghulam.
Haan Woh Fatima Woh Fatima.
Aarzo-e-Qalb-e-Mustafa.
Jis Ke Ladle Hasan Hussain.

6
Jis Ki Betiyaan Falak Muqam.
Yeh Matam Sada Rahe Our Iss Se Siwa Rahe.
Yeh Farsh-e-Aza-e-Sibt-e-Nabi Ta Mehshar Bicha Rahe.
Kitni Hi Uthe Zulmat Ki Ghata.

7
Ho Kitni Hi Tareek Fiza.
Bujhne Nahi Paye Sham-e-Azza.
Kitne Hi Uthein Tofaan Magar Roshan Yeh Diya Rahe.
Ik Nasal Hatay Ik Nasal Barhe.

8
Ik Shama Bhuje Ik Shama Jalay.
Shabir Ka Matam Hota Rahe.
Naslan Bad-e-Nasal Younhi Jari Silsila Rahe.
Khasosi Kalam::Ali Muhammad Rizvi Sachay Bhai:
