Koi Bacha Le Mere Bhai Ka Gala Aa Ke Syed Ali Naqvi.
Kaha Behan Ne Yeh Satar Qadam Se Ro Ro Kar.
Ghareeb Kitna Muhammad Ka Yeh Nawasa Hai.
Keh Teen Roz Se Mera Hussain Pyasa Hai.
Chala Raha Hai Laeen Khushk Halq Par Khanjar.
Sitam Yeh Aal-e-Muhammad Pe Bar Bar Na Kar.
Aye Shimar Rook Le Khanjar Ab Our Waar Na Kar.
Sirhaney Bethi Hain Maa Hath Rakh Ke Gardan Par.
Kaha Yeh Shimar Se Zainab Ne Sun Sada Meri.
Ghareeb Bhai Ke Seene Se Tu Utar Ja Abhi.
Sakina Khaimon Se Aa Kar Na Dekhey Yeh Manzar.
Akela Pyasa Hai Mazloom Mera Bhai Hai.
Abhi Abhi Ali Akbar Ki Lash Uthai Hai.
Gira Hai Khak Pe Ghorey Se Yeh Bohat Thak Kar.
Khuda Kissi Bhi Behan Par Yeh Waqt Na Aaye.
Nazar Ke Samne Bhai Tarap Ke Mar Jaye.
Behan Ke Samne Katay Na Koi Bhai Ka Sar.
Tarapta Dekha Jo Zainab Ko Shimar Hansne Laga.
Ghareeb-e-Zahra Ki Gardan Pe Waar Karta Raha.
Pukarti Rahein Zainab Nahi Ruka Khanjar.
Qayamat Aa Gayi Zeeshan Our Ali Oss Dam.
Sar-e-Hussain Gira Kat Ke Khak Par Jis Dum.
Yeh Keh Kar Gir Gayin Zainab Zameen Pe Ro Ro Kar.
