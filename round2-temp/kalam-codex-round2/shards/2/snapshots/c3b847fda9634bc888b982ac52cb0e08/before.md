1
Mehman Qalandar Kay.
Aashiqon Ka Yahan Samandar Hai.
Sab Ke Dil Mein Wila-e-Haider Hai.
Aao Sehwan Chalein Ali Walo.
Aaj Tu Maizbaan Qalandar Hai.
Chalo Chalo Sehwan Bano Mehman Qalandar Ke.
Ghaus Qutub Abdaal Sabhi Darbar Qalandar Ke.
Mela Aaya Hai Lal Ka Mela Aaya Hai.
Sab Ko Bulaya Hai Lal Ne Sab Ko Bulaya Hai.
Bhool Nahi Sakti Dunay Ahsan Qalandar Ke.
Nobat Ki Aawaz Safar Mein Sath Rahe Har Dam.
Dil Ki Dharkan Bhi Dhamal Ke Sath Kahe Dam Dam.
Dil Yeh Kahe Main Ho Jaon Qurban Qalandar Ke.
Jab Sehwan Ki Dharti Aaye Qarz Yehi Hai Tum Par.
Ishq Ke Sajday Mein Gir Jana Nad-e-Ali Parh Parh Kar.
Youn Hi Sada Aabad Rahe Dalaan Qalandar Ke.
Mulk Ke Konay Kone Se Zawar Nikalte Hain.
Kyun Na Nikalein Hukam Yahan Lajpal Ke Chaltay Hain.
Sadqay Mein Aabad Hai Pakistan Qalandar Ke.
Kaisa Haseen Norani Manzar Aankh Jidhar Bhi Jaye.
Sehan Mein Mula Ghazi Ka Oncha Parcham Lehraye.
Manat Jis Ki Bhar Aaye WOh Aa Kar Diye Jalaye.
Lal Ghulabi Sabz Sunehri Chadar Koi Charhaye.
Nobat Koi Bajaye Koi Mehndi Yahan Sajaye.
Koi Sabeel Pilaye Koi Nazar-o-Niyaz Dilaye.
Dunya Mein Mashoor Hain Dastar Khuwan Qalandar Ke.
Lal Ke Khadim Hain Sakhi Lajpal Ke Khadim Hain.
Lal Ke Khadim Hain Ali Ke Lal Ke Khadim Hain.
Nokar Hain Yeh Mazhar Our Farhan Qalandar Ke.
