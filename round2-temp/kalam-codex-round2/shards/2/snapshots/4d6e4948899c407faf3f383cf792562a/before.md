Kiwain Aagai Zaeefi Syed Ali Ghazi Zaidi.
Kiwein Aa Gayi Zaeefi Zainab Khari Hairan Aye.

1
Bazaraan Taun Si Pehle Abid Mera Jawan Aye.
Karo Har Taraf Charagaan Bazaraan Nu Sajao.
Bagi Di Bhaina Aayiyaan Pathraan Da Meen Wasao.
Aa Gayi Aye Dhi Ali Di Hoya Sham Wich Ayelan Aye.

2
Galiyaan De Wich Hai Rulda Bazaraan Choon Guzarda.
Maiyyat Sakina Cha Ke Abid Riya Aye Puchda.
Kithay Dafan Kitay Janday Wich Sham De Mehman Aye.
Sajjad Li Aye Mushkil.

3
Asghar Da Kurta Veran Meri Lehad Wich Rakha De.
Ahsan Kar Qabar Da Abid Nishaan Mita De.
Tere Baad Aa Na Jaway Meri Qabar Te Shimar.
Tur Gayi Aye Chaar Sala Babay Di Pursa Daar Aye.

4
Riya Marda Tamachay Jinno Shimar Bar Bar Aye.
Mar Ke Tamachiyaan De Chehray Te Hai Nishan Aye.
Ho Qaid Sham Gayi Aye Pathar V Jhaldi Rayi Aye.
Zainab Da Ban Ke Pardah Darbar Wich Khari Aye.

5
Syedaan Te Sab Taun Wakhra Fizza Tera Ahsan Aye.
Tanveer Aqeel Qaidaan Wich Hondi Jad V Sham Aye.
Rukday Nayi Ayehday Athro Babay Da Laindi Naam Aye.
Zindan De Haneray Le Gaye Dukhi Di Jan Aye.
