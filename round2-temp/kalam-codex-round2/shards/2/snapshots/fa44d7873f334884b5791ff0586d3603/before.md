Mera Aka Hussain Sarwar Raza Karbalai.

1
Mera Aaqa Hussain Mera Hai.
Hai Bharosa Hussain Mera Hai.
Kaisay Dobay Gi Nooh Ki Kashti.
Woh Pukara Hussain Mera Hai.
Ho Gaya Sath Kibriya Oss Ke.
Jo Bhi Bola Hussain Mera Hai.

2
Aisa Sabir Tu Do Jahan Mein Nahi.
Dhond Le Ja Hussain Mera Hai.
Tum Rakho Sare Hurmala-o-Yazid.
Aik Tanha Hussain Mera Hai.
Poch Lein Jab Lehad Mein Aa Ke Malak.
Tum Yeh Kehna Hussain Mera Hai.

3
Ho Gaya Khuld Mein Wohi Dakhil.
Jo Pukara Hussain Mera Hai.
Bheek Mangoon Ga Na Kissi Dar Se.
Toor Kasa Hussain Mera Hai.
Bolay Asif Se Khuld Mein Sarwar.
Kar Bharosa Hussain Mera Hai.
