Likhon Main Kaise Fazilat Teri Raza Mola.
Nahi Main Koi Nabi Na Koi Khuda Maula.

1
Sab Aa Ke Sehan-e-Shifa Mein, Humein Talab Kar Lein.
Duaein Mang Rahi Hain Dua Raza Maula.
Meri Hayat Ka Aaho, Teri Panah Dhondney.
Issay Bhi Apne Charan Mein Chupa Raza Maula.

2
Mujhe Batana Hai Rutba Tumhare Qayam Ka.
Mujhe Bhi Debal e Mehdi Bana Raza Maula.
Woh Jis Ne Kar Li Ho Darbani Tere Rozay Pe.
Woh Poray Mulk Ka Hota Hai Badshah Maula.

3
Quran Khatam Kiya Oss Ne, Na Parhi Rehman.
Woh Jis Ne Tera Qaseeda Nahi Parha Maula.
Keh Jaisay Chand Chamakta Ho Aaftab Ke Sath.
Hai Qum Ke Sath Youn Mashhad Tera, Raza Maula.

4
Mere Shehar Mein Mujhe Aam, Samajhtay Hain Yeh Loag.
Unhein Bataein Sana Khuwaan Ka Martaba Maula.
Mere Liye Tu Yeh Mairaj-e-Zakiri Hogi.
Bas Apne Rozay Pe Taseer Ko Parha Maula.
