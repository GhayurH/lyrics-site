Mere Mola Reza Massam Hassan Karbalai.

1
Ibn-e-Khair-Un-Nisa Fakhar-e-Mushkil Kusha Mere Maula Raza.
Dar Tera Do Jahan Mein Hai Baab-e-Khuda Mere Maula Raza.
Aaj Har So Hai Terah Rajab Sa Samaa Hain Sajay Do Jahan.
Arsh Se Aaye Hain Sani-e-Murtaza Mere Maula Raza.
Tum Ho Tasveer Dada Ali Ki Imam, Tum Pe Lakhon Salam.
Tera Chehra Bhi Chehra Hai ALLAH Ka Mere Maula Raza.

2
Khud Qaza Kar Rahi Hai Hifazat Meri Parh Ke Nad-e-Ali.
Jab Se Bandha Hai Bazu Pe Sikka Tera Mere Maula Raza.
Shair Ko Zindah Qaleen Se Kar Diya Sab Ko Batla Diya.
Malik-e-Kun Ho Tum, Tum Ho Dast-e-Khuda Mere Maula Raza.
Tum Nabi-o-Ali Ke Ho Jan-o-Jigar, Fatima Ke Pisar.
Naaz Tere Uthata Hai Khud Kibriya Mere Maula Raza.

3
Chand Soraj Ko Deta Hai Woh Roshni Hai Qasam Noor Ki.
Tere Rozay Pe Jalta Hai Jo Har Diya Mere Maula Raza.
Tere Sehan-e-Shifa Mein Aye Ibn-e-Ali, Chashm-e-Dil Ne Meri.
Aate Jate Hoye Dekhey Hain Anbiya Mere Maula Raza.
Lab Pe Faizan-o- Meesum Ke Hai Yeh Dua Izn Kar Do Atta.
Jeetay Jee Dekh Lein Hum Bhi Roza Tera Mere Maula Raza.
