1
Labaik Ya Hussain Iss Door Ke Har Shimar Ko Mati Mein Mila Do.
Dushman Ke Iradon Ko Tah-e-Arz Daba Do.
Iss Door Ke har Shimar Ko Mati Mein Mila Do.
Utho Ke Sada Deta Hai Ab Waqt-e-Shahadat.

2
Jago Ke Bulati Hai Tumhein Ouj-e-Saadat.
Hai Milat-e-Momin Ko Hifazat Ki Zarorat.
Ab Sar Pe Kafan Bandh Ke Karni Hai Hifazat.
Labaik Ke Nare Se Zamane Ko Hila Do.

3
Eman Ki Tuqeer Hai Labaik Ka Narah.
Tehreek Ki Tadbeer Hai Labaik Ka Narah.
Haqdar Ki Jageer Hai Labaik Ka Narah.
Taseer Hai Shamsheer Hai Labaik Ka Narah.

4
Ab Narah-e-Labaik Ka Ejah Dikha Do.
Yeh Matam-e-Sarwar Na Ruka Hai Na Ruke Ga.
Yeh Parcham-e-Abbas Jhuka Hai Na Jhuke Ga.
Mula Ka Wafadar Bika Hai Na Bike Ga.

5
Sarwar Ka Azadar Mita Hai Na Mite Ga.
Paigham Humara Yeh Zamane Ko Suna Do.
Nazuk Hai Bohat Ghaibat-e-Kubra Ka Zamana.
Dunya Ne Banaya Hai Tashiyon Ko Nishana.

6
Har Gaam Pe Teyar Hai Ik Taza Fasana.
Hujjat Ki Qasam Hai Kissi Dhoke Mein Na Aana.
Tum Aik Ho Yeh Dushman-e-Milat Ko Bata Do.
 Yasir Ke Tawasut Se Wajhi Sab Ko Batao.

7
Terheek-e-Tahafuz Ke Liye Hath Uthao.
Labaik Ke Naron Ko Zabano Pe Sajao.
Ab Waqt Ha Awaz Se Awaz Milao.
Zanjeer-e-Sitam Toor Do Hur Ban Ke Sada Do
