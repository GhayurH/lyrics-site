Madad Karain Ya Fatima Zehra Syed Raza Abbas Zaidi.

1
Ya Fatima Zahra, Ya Fatima Zahra Faryad Hai, Faryad Hai Ya Umm-e-Abeeha.
Madad Karein Madad Karein Ali Ke Haq Ka Wasta Hai Bibi Fatima.
Kisay Sunaein Hal-e-Dil Hamara Kaun Hai Yahan.
Bas Aap Ka Hai Aasra Kareem-e-Karbala Ki Maa.
Chura Lo Gham Se Ab Humein.
Tumhara Naam Fatima Hai Bibi Fatima.

2
Dar-e-Hussain Dekh Loon Bohat Udas Hai Nazar.
Najaf Se Karbala Humein Bola Lein Arbaeen Par.
Labon Pe Ya Hussain Hai.
Hamarey Dil Mein Karbala Hai Bibi Fatima.
Bichaon Ga Main Kab Talak Saf-e-Azza Yahan Wahan.
Mujhe Woh Ghar Atta Karein Bapa Hoon Majlisein Jahan.

3
Hoon Ghar Mein Aap Ke Qadam.
Yeh Har Ghareeb Chahta Hai Bibi Fatima.
Aye Syeda Hai Aap Ko Hasan Hussain Ki Qasam.
Jo Khali Godiyaan Hain Ab Yeh Unn Pe Kijiye Karam.
Atta Ho Waris-e-Azza.
Labon Pe Bas Yehi Dua Hai Bibi Fatima.

4
Hai Jab Se Qaid Mein Pisar Adhora Lag Raha Hai Ghar.
Iss Intezar Mein Hai Maa Kab Aaye Ga Mera Pisar.
Rehai Ki Khabar Miley.
Aseer Jo Bhi Be Khata Hai Bibi Fatima.
Mareez-e-La Elaj Ko Dikhayiye Yeh Mojza.
Ba Haq e Abid-e-Hazeen Atta Karein Ossay Shifa.

5
Aye Raziya Aye Marziya.
Tumhare Hath Mein Shifa Hai Bibi Fatima.
Zahoor Ho Imam Ka Banaein Aap Ka Haram.
Hai Matami Ki Aarzu Bichay Wahan Bhi Farsh-e-Gham.
Shikasta Qabar Dekh Kar.
Hamara Dil Tarap Raha Hai Bibi Fatima.

6
Totay Hoye Dilon Ka Bas Ik Aasra Hain Aap.
Jis Ko Ali Pukarein Wohi Fatima Hain Aap.
Mushkil Kusha Ke Wastay Mushkil Kusha Hain Aap.
 Zeeshan Our Raza Ke Labon Ki Sada Hain Aap.
Dil Hai Udas Hum Hain Pareshan Fatima.
Mushkil Hamari Kijiye Aasan Fatima.
