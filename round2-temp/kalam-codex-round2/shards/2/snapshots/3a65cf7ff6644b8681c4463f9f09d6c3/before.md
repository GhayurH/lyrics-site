1
Logo Ro Aakhay Muhari Mere Wango Ban Ke Qaidi Koi Sham Che Aaway Na.
Phophiyaan Di Rida Akbar Jay Veer De Qatil Too Koi Mangda Jaway Na.
Howay Kise Di Na Koi Bhain Masooma Kadi Qaidi Veer De Naal.
Apne Hathi Zindaan Che Khood Ke Bhain Layi Koi Qabar Banaway Na.

2
Wich Sehrawaan Tur Tur Ke Na Howay Kadi Qaidi Koi Bemar.
Fair Behan Koi Bemar Bhira Diyaan Kariyaan Te Aynj Pani Paway Na.
Zainab Jhaliyaan Jiwein Jhirkaan Loko Abbas Di Maut De Baad.
Rab Dunya Te Zainab De Wangon Bhain Kise Da Maan Mokaway Na.

3
Deway Kise Nu Fair Saza Na Aynj Di Koi Zalim Dunya Te.
Haye Khoon Bhare Sir Tan Too Juda Hoye Veeraan De Naize Te Wakhaway Na.
Mangiyaan Duawaan Abid Ne Ro Ro Ke Ayho Maran Taein Saqlain.
Khaliq Mere Dunya Te Fair Koi Zalim Bazaar Sajaway Na.
