1
Mera Maula Hasan Woh Peshwa Hai Qambar Kiyani.
Mera Maula Hasan Woh Paishwa Hai.
Qaseeda Khuwan Jis Ka Khud Khuda Hai.
Hai Dastar Khuwan Aisa Mujtaba Ka.
Jahan Hai Rizq Sare Anbiya Ka.
Farishtay Jis Ke Dar Se Rotiyaan Lein.
Faqat Maula Hasan Ki Walidah Hai.
Muhammad Ne Muhammad Ko Uthaya.
Liya Bosa Hai Seene Se Lagaya.
Yeh Manzar Dekh Kar Aisa Laga Keh.
Koi Bichra Howa Jaisay Mila Hai.
Meri Taqdeer Jis Ke Hath Mein Hai.
Khuda Ki Baat Oss Ki Baat Mein Hai.
Looh-e-Mehfooz Par Tehreer Hai Jo.
Hasan Ke Hath Ka Likha Howa Hai.
Hasan Ka Martaba Sab Se Juda Hai.
Zameen-e-Aman Ka Yeh Hi Khuda Hai.
Qalam Se Dushman-e-Dein Ko Hai Mara.
Tadabur Mein Hasan Hi Mustafa Hai.
Jo Ik Gasib Ko Dekha Bolay Shabar.
Utar Mere Yeh Baba Ka Hai Mimber.
Badal Dala Hai Deen-e-Mustafa Ko.
Mere Alfaz Hi Teri Saza Hai.
Ameeron Ki Tarha Iss Mein Na Aao.
Adab Se Zikar Mein Sar Ko Jhukao.
Yehi Woh Zikar Hai Keh Jis Mein Qambar.
Tumhare Sath Shamil Kibriya Hai.
