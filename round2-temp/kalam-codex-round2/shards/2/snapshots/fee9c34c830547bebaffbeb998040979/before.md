Namaz e Wila Mir Hasan Mir Lyrics
Aye Zamin-e-Ghareebaan.

1
Aye Hami-e-Yateema.
Aye Raza Raza Jan.
Ada Youn Namaz-e-Wila Ho Rahi Hai.
Imam-e-Raza Ki Sana Ho Rahi Hai.

2
Karam Mujh Pe Maula Kiye Ja Rahe Hain.
Yeh Dunya Bhala Kyun Khafa Ho Rahi Hai.
Mubarak Ho Tujh Ko Aye Mashhad Ke Zair.
Abhi Khuld Tujh Ko Atta Ho Rahi Hai.

3
Yeh Sehan-e-Shifa Hai Woh Sarhad Jahan Par.
Ajal Zindagi Se Juda Ho Rahi Hai.
Tera Sikka Bandha Hai Bazu Pe Jab Se.
Mere Haq Mein Qum Mein Dua Ho Rahi Hai.

4
Bataya Hai Tasveer Ne Shair Ban Kar.
Youn Takhleeq-e-Khalq-e-Khuda Ho Rahi Hai.
Kya Zeeqad Ki Gyarween Aa Gayi Hai.
Haseen Our Qum Ki Fiza Ho Rahi Hai.

5
Khuda Ka Bhi Mujrim Ali Ka Bhi Mujrim.
Nuseri Ko Dohri Saza Ho Rahi Hai.
Sab Aahista Bolo Keh Sehan-e-Shifa Mein.
Kissi Dard-e-Dil Ki Dawa Ho Rahi Hai.

6
Fida Jaisay Hota Hai Jugnu Diye Par.
Youn Mashhad Pe Janat Fida Ho Rahi Hai.
Mozaif e Haram Mein Riza-e-Raza Se.
Atta Hal Atta Ki Atta Ho Rahi Hai.

7
Khuda Oss Se Kaisay Ho Razi Keh Jis Se.
Muhammad Ki Beti Khafa Ho Rahi Hai.
 Hasan Har Zamane Mein Shayar Ki Izzat.
Ba Faiz-e-Imam-e-Raza Ho Rahi Hai.
