1
Koi Banra Dunya Te Na Khon Di Mehndi Laway.
Na Kubra Wangoon Banri Rasiyaan Da Zewar Paway.
Karbal Di Tapdi Rait Uttay Qasim Nu Sehra Laya.
Tin Din De Pyasay Janjiyaan Nu Maa Qasim Di Farmaya.
Jiwein Shagan Manaye Main Ujari Koi Maa Na Shagan Manaway.
Yousuf Taun Sohne Qasim Te Ummat Nu Taras Na Aaya.
Oday Tukray Kar Ke Zalimaan Ne Haye Maa Di Jholi Paya.
Kiwein Tukray Joor Ke Tere Maa Tainu Kafan Pawaway.
Qasim Di Lash Nu Chunda Aye Piya Labh Ke Zahra Jaya.
Ummat Ne Sabar De Malik Nu Haye Kaisa Waqt Wakhaya.
Koi Tapdi Rait Choon Babal Aynj Dhi Da Lekh Na Chaway.
Gathri Wich Lash Aye Banray Di Maa Joor Ke Shakal Banai.
Kiwein Maa Ujari Ne Qasim Di Kubra Nu Lash Wakhai.
Ya Rab Kisay Bewah Maa Te Haye Aynj Da Waqt Na Aaway.
Haye Maut Tu Pehlaan Syedaan Ne Najfi Ik Kaaj Rachaya.
Aynj Aaya Har Koi Shadi Te Jiwein Dewan Pursa Aaya.
Nayi Sir Wich Khak Oh Panda Jehra Dean Mubarak Aaway.
