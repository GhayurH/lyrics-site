1
Meer E Karwan Abbas Hai Raza Hassan Sadiq.
Wafa Ke Karwaan Ka Meer-e-Karwaan Abbas Hai.
Hussain Ki Hai Aas Yeh Hasan Ki Jaan Abbas Hai.
Abbas Hain Jahan Jahan Wahan Wahan Hussain Hai.
Hussain Hain Jahan Jahan Wahan Wahan Abbas Hai.
Alam Hai Iss Ka Woh Alam Hai Jis Ka Saya Har Jagah.
Boland Hai Jo Be Baha Woh Aasmaan Abbas Hai.
Issi Ke Dam Qadam Se Hain Yeh Zindagi Ki Ronaqein.
Har Aik Sans Mein Sada Rawaan Dawaan Abbas Hai.
Issi Ka Main Mureed Hoon Issi Pe Main Bhi Hoon Fida.
Nishan-e-Haq Kahin Jisay Woh Haq Nishaan Abbas Hai.
Kahin Yeh Misal-e-Phool Hai Kahin Yeh Misal-e-Bo-e-Gul.
Kahin Nihaan Abbas Hai Kahin Ayyaan Abbas Hai.
Aye Razz Bolta Hai Yeh Abu Turab Ki Zabaan.
Jo Hai Jalal-e-Haidery Woh Be Gumaan Abbas Hai.
