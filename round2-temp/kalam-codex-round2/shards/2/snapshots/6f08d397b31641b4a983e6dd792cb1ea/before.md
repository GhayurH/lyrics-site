Nazar Atay Hain Syed Ali Naqvi Lyrics

1
Kaisay Kaisay Tere Darban Nazar Aate Hain.
Koi Meesum Koi Salman Nazar Aate Hain.
Yeh Gharana Abu Talib Ka Hai Inn Ke Ghar Mein.
Chalte Phirtay Hoye Quran Nazar Aate Hain.
Dil Ki Gehrayi Se Jab Nad-e-Ali Parhta Hoon.
Kam Mushkil Mujhe Aasan Nazar Aate Hain.

2
Dushmani Haider-e-Karrar Se Rakhne Walay.
Mujh Ko Anjam Se Anjan Nazar Aate Hain.
Jin Ka Eman Hai Eman-e-Abu Talib Par.
Bas Wohi Hum Ko Musalman Nazar Aate Hain.
Iss Tarha Peet Pe Khil Jate Hain Matam Ke Nishaan.
Phool Jaisay Sar-e-Guldan Nazar Aate Hain.

3
Yeh Tu Bas Ghar Mein Hi Tableeg Kiya Kartay Hain.
Sheikh Kab Bar Sar-e-Maidan Nazar Aate Hain.
Aap Muslim Hain Khilafat Mein Bhi Darjah Chotha.
Woh Hain Hindu Unhein Bhagwan Nazar Aate Hain.
Pairway Syed-e-Sajjad Hain Dekho Yasir.
Hum Ghareebi Mein Bhi Sultan Nazar Aate Hain.
