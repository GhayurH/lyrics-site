Quran Mustafa Hain To Yaseen Fatima Sibtain Haider.
Rab Ke Liye Hain Laiq-e-Tahseen Fatima.

1
Jo Aap Ka Nahi Hai Woh Tauheed Ka Nahi.
Rab Ne Bana Liya Hai Yeh Ain Fatima.
Khaliq Ne Unn Ko Markaz-e-Lanat Bana Diya.
Karne Jo Aaye They Teri Tauheen Fatima.

2
Maula Ali Hoye Keh Hussain-o-Hasan Hoye.
Lazim Hain Deen Ke Liye Yeh Teen Fatima.
Karta Hai Wird Sorah-e-Kausar Ka Subha-o-Sham.
 Mehdi Hai Teri Madah Ka Shauqeen Fatima.
