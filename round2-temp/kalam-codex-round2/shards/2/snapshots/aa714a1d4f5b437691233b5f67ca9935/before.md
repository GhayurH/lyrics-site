Rabba Manzoor Karein Meda Che Maah Da Mehman
Che Maa Da Mehman Kita Deen Taun Jo Qurban Aakhay Aye Natiq Quran

1
Jidda Jhola Reh Gaya Khali Jiday Amari Lad Ladaye Na
Jiday Cholay Paijay Sughra Ne Jehray Hinr Masoom Ne Paye Na
Jiday Sughra Nu Arman Kita Deen Taun Jo Qurban Aakhay Aye Natiq Quran

2
Jehra Nanay Naal Main Kita Si Oh Wadah Toor Nibhawaan Ga
Main Waar Ke Sab Kujh Deen Utton Nanay Da Deen Bachawaan Ga
Asghar Aakhri Daan Kita Deen Taun Jo Qurban Aakhay Aye Natiq Quran

3
Kha Teer Zulm Da Wich Gal De Jehra Dunya Taun Munh Moor Gaya
Jehra Maa V Keh Nayi Sagda Hay Pyasa E Sawaan Toor Gaya
Kul Da Kul Saman Kita Deen Taun Jo Qurban Aakhay Aye Natiq Quran

4
Wich Khaimay Jis Masoom Diyaan Amari Ne Takniyaan Rahwaan Ne
Jiday Lad Ladawan Di Khatir Paiyaan Jis Diyaan Sikdiyaan Banwaan Ne
Oss Amari Di Jan Kita Deen Taun Jo Qurban Aakhay Aye Natiq Quran

5
Khursheed, Rubab Da Wich Maqtal Deen-e-Islam Bacha Gaya Aye
Imran Da Khon Ali Asghar Kar Ke Aynj Sabar Wakha Gaya Aye
Hoye Anbiya Hairan Kita Deen Taun Jo Qurban Aakhay Aye Natiq Quran
