Mola Hasan Mohammed Abbas Karim Lyrics
Fatima Ki Jan Murtaza Ki Shan Maula Hasan.

1
Aap Ki Wila Aap Ki Sana.
Naam Aap Ka Le Ke Har Jagah.
Kar Rahe Hain Hum Aman-o-Aman Maula Hasan.
Haq Pisar Ka Hai Ghar Pe Baap Ke.

2
Keh Raha Hoon Yeh Baat Iss Liye.
Kaba Bhi Hai Aap Ka Makan Maula Hasan.
Kam Aap Ka Zulm Ki Qaza.
Kam Aap Ka Deen Ki Baqa.

3
Naam Aap Ka Deen Ka Nishan Maula Hasan.
Yaad Hai Humein Arz-e-Karbala.
Arzaq e Laeen Dhair Ho Gaya.
Iss Tarha Lara Aap Ka Jawan Maula Hasan.

4
Aap Se Laray Kya Koi Addo.
Aap Sa Nahi Koi Jangju.
Teer Hai Qalam Hath Hai Kaman Maula Hasan.
Aap Sa Sakhi Kab Koi Mila.

5
Bin Kahey Ossay Sab Howa Atta.
Jis Pe Ho Gaye Aap Meharban Maula Hasan.
Raat Bhi Mujhe Lagti Hai Sahar.
Kyun Bhala Mujhe Ho Kissi Ka Dar.

6
Mere Rehnuma Mere Pasban Maula Hasan.
Yeh Karim-o- Zeeshan Ne Kaha.
Kyun Na Hum Karein Zikar Aap Ka.
Aap Ke Liye Hai Mili Zaban Maula Hasan.
