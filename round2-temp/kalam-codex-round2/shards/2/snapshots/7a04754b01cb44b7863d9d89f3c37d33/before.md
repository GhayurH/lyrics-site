Khuda Ki Marzi Hai Mula Hussain Ki Marzi.
Atta Kare Jise Jitna Hussain Ki Marzi.

1
Farishte Oss Ke Liye Aa Ke Ban Gaye Darzi.
Uthaye Naaz Bhi Oss Ke Malaik Our Nabi.
Bana Le Jis Ko Bhi Naqa Hussain Ki Marzi.
Woh Dekho Aa Gaya Rahib Hussain Ke Dar Par.

2
Naseeb Mein Nahi Likha Tha Jis Ke Rab Ne Pisar.
Osse Bhi De Diya Beta Hussain Ki Marzi.
Yazeedi Jitne Hain Khud Munh Pe Apne Thokein Gaye.
Jo Matami Hain Wahan Sar Utha Ke Ghomein Gaye.

3
Chale Gi Hashar Mein Aaqa Hussain Ki Marzi.
Array Our Munkir-e-Sarwar Woh Waqt Aaye Ga.
Qasam Khuda Ki Tu Jis Dam Lahad Mein Jaye Ga.
Pata Chale Ga Ke Kya Hai Hussain Ki Marzi.

4
Malak Yeh Bole Keh Janat Mein Bhejein Kis Kis Ko.
Khuda Yeh Bola Woh Le Jaein Sath Jis Jis Ko.
Yeh Ab Hai Fatima Ki Ya Hussain Ki Marzi.
Malak Ne Maut Ke Bas Yeh Tareeqa Apnaya.

5
Woh Jab Bhi Matami Ki Rooh Qabaz Karne Gaya.
Tu Pehle Pochne Aaya Hussain Ki Marzi.
Namaz-e-Rab-e-Aula Ruk Gayi Hai Sajday Mein.
Hussain Pusht-e-Nabi Par Nabi Hain Sajday Mein.

6
Taveel Kitna Ho Sajda Hussain Ki Marzi.
Dar-e-Hussain Se Mere Liye Chali Izzat.
Issi Liye Mujhe Saqlain Mil Gayi Izat.
Rahi Nazar Mein Hamesha Hussain Ki Marzi.
