Pa Ke Siraan Che Mitiyaan Ro Kenr Zahra Jaiyaan.
Tere Qatilaan Ne Ghazi Naizay Te Chadraan Chayiyaan.

1
Das Cha Fazal Da Baba Kiwein Nu Nu Meel Tur Sunr.
Mastoraan Jeriyaan Na Kaday Ghar Choon Bahir Aayiyaan.
Sehra Che Rahey Tarapday Dowein Hath Katay Bhira De.
Jadon Zalimaan Ne Baniyaan Zainab Diyaan Kalaiyaan.

2
Veraan De Lashay Tak Ke Kulsoom Ro Ro Aakhay.
Sehra Che Lutiyaan Gayiyaan Meri Maa Diyaan Kamaiyaan.
Darya De Pasay Tak Tak Rondi Rayi Sakina.
Ghazi De Qatilaan Ne Khushiyaan Jadon Manaiyaan.

3
Sughra Taun Le Sakina Ik Dukh Aye Sab Da Sanjha.
Bhainraan Nu Mar Gayiyaan Veraan Diyaan Judaiyaan.
Ro Ro Bolandi Reh Gayi Ghazi Tainu Sakina.
Chotay Jaye Gal Na Ohday Jadon Shimar Rasiyaan Paiyaan.

4
Pathar Te Wasday Koray Paiyaan Jhaldiyaan Ne Sham Ich.
Ghazi De Sir Nu Tak Tak Ro Ro Ke Jag Sataiyaan.
Veraan De Lashiyaan Te Ghorey Na Koi Doraway.
Sunriyaan Asad Kisay Nayi Bhainraan Diyaan Dohaiyaan.
