1
Khush Amdeed Ya Ali Syed Raza Abbas Zaidi.
Khuda Ke Ghar Jab Aa Gaye Ali Maula Ali.
Tu Bola Kaba Dekh Ke Ali Maula Ali.
Main Kar Raha Tha Kab Se Intezar Aap Ka.
Khush Aamdeed Ya Ali.
Tera Rajab Hai Jashan Manao Maula Ali Ke Dewano.
Kaun Hai Momin Kaun Munafiq Narah Laga Kar Pehchano.
Lagao Narah Haidery Ali Maula Ali.
Halaliyon Ki Hai Khushi Ali Maula Ali.
Jala Do Munkiron Ko Likh Ke Tum Jagah Jagah.
Khush Aamdeed Ya Ali.
Kabay Mein Jab Aaye Muhammad Dekha Ali Ke Ghar Mein Ali.
Subhan ALLAH Masha ALLAH Kehte Hoye Pas Aaye Nabi.
Youn Aaye Unn Ke Hath Par Ali Maula Ali.
Ho Hamd Jaisay Naat Par Ali Maula Ali.
Ali Ne Aankhein Kholein Tu Nabi Ne Yeh Kaha.
Khush Aamdeed Ya Ali.
Khaybar Mein Jab Na Koi Bhi Marhab Se Lar Saka.
Jibreel Ne Phir Aa Ke Muhammad Se Yeh Kaha.
ALLAH Keh Raha Hai Ossay Dijiye Sada.
Iss Din Ke Wastay Jisay Haider Kaha Gaya.
Phir Youn Howa Ke Nad-e-Ali Lab Pe Aa Gayi.
Soye Madina Dekh Ke Kehne Lagay Nabi.
Aa Ja Ali, Aa Ja Ali, Aa Ja Ali, Aa Ja Ali.
Marhab Ko Pachar Ali.
Khaybar Ko Ukhar Ali.
Main Ne Kab Se Nahi Suni.
Teri Shair Si Dahaar Ali.
Koi Mard Nahi Marhab Se Kaun Laray Ga.
Mushkil Mein Deen Hai Mera Ali Aa Ja Ali.
Ab Yeh Alam Sirf Hai Tera Ali Aa Ja Ali.
Sara Lashkar Dekh Raha Tha Soye Madina Khaybar Se.
Lekin Ik Salman Tha Unn Mein Waqif Tha Jo Haider Se.
Woh Keh Raha Tha Shan Se Ali Maula Ali.
Woh Aaye Aasman Se Ali Maula Ali.
Falak Se Aap Aaein Gay Mujhe Yaqeen Tha.
Khush Aamdeed Ya Ali.
Istaqbal Ki Saf Mein Kharey They Arsh-o-Zameen Walay Mil Kar.
Pohanchi Jab Baraat Ali Ki Zahra Ke Darwazay Par.
Jabeen Pe Sehra Bandh Ke Ali Maula Ali.
Nabi Se Aa Ke Jab Miley Ali Maula Ali.
Galay Laga Ke Muskura Ke Bolay Mustafa.
Khush Aamdeed Ya Ali.
Ali Wali Ki Muhabbat Khuda Ki Rehmat Hai.
Muhib e Maula Pe Rehmat Zaror Barsay Gi.
Woh Jis Ke Dil Mein Adawat Hai Mere Maula Ki.
Bas Aisay Shaks Pe Lanat Zaror Barsay Gi.
Khushiyaan Ali Walon Ko Mubarak Unn Ko Mubarak Lanat Ho.
Haider Ki Tu Baat Alag Hai Jalnay Walon Se Keh Do.
Boland Hi Boland Hai Ali Maula Ali.
Mujhe Bohat Pasand Hai Ali Maula Ali.
Ali Ka Dil Pe Raaj Hai Tu Kyun Na Doon Sada.
Ishq-e-Ali Ki Mohar Lagi Hai Dil Par Aye Zeeshan-o- Raza.
Qabar Mein Hum Ko Kaisi Wehshat Kaisa Dar Our Kaisi Saza.
Main Janta Hoon Aaein Gay Ali Maula Ali.
Naqab Jab Hataein Gay Ali Maula Ali.
Ali Ko Dekhtey Hi Bool Uthay Ga Dil Mera.
