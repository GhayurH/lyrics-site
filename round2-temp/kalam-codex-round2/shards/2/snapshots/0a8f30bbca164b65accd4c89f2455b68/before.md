1
Kehti Rahi Sakina Le Ayo Pani Chacha.
Mar Jaein Hum Kahin Na Le Ayo Pani Chacha.
Piyasa Hai Mera Asghar Iss Waste Hoon Kehti.
Mango Gi Phir Kabhi Na Le Ayo Pani Chacha.

2
Shair-e-Khuda Ke Baitey Arman Hai Tujh Ko.
Talwar Mil Saki Na Le Ayo Pani Chacha.
Ye Sheer Khowar Bache Gash Kha Ke Gir Rahe Hain.
Mushkil Hai Inn Ka Jeena Le Ayo Pani Chacha.

3
Phailaye Hath Boli Uryan Sar Wo Bachi.
Chdar Tu Mil Saki Na Le Ayo Pani Chacha.
 Afraz Bad-e-Karbal Ayi Jo Qaid Khane.
Kehti Rahi Khazina Le Ayo Pani Chacha.
