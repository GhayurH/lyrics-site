Midhat Ali Naqi Ki Muqadar Ki Baat Hai.
Qatrey Ke Lab Par Aaj Samandar Ki Baat Hai.

1
Maula Naqi Ka Zikar Kiya Jab Tu Youn Laga.
Yeh Hai Nabi Ka Zikar Yeh Haider Ki Baat Hai.
Jis Ghar Mein Anbiya Paye Tazeem Aate They.
Oss Ghar Ke Aik Fard Ossi Dar Ki Baat Hai.

2
Taqwa Taqi Ka Sabar Hussain-e-Shaheed Ka.
Inn Ko Agar Mila Hai Tu Yeh Ghar Ki Baat Hai.
Ashab Inn Ke Buzar-o-Salmaan Misal They.
Yeh Ik Attaye Khaliq-e-Akbar Ki Baat Hai.

3
Jo Bhi Amal Tha Marzi-e-Parwardigar Tha.
Inn Ke Amal Mein Sari Payamber Ki Baat Hai.
Yeh Bhi Tamam Umar Rahe Zulm Ka Shikar.
Youn Inn Ke Tazkiray Mein Sitamgar Ki Baat Hai.

4
Seerat Inhi Ki Sari Hasan Askari Mein Thi.
Manzar Ki Baat Mein Pas Manzar Ki Baat Hai.
 Rehan Pyas Bujh Gayi Inn Ki Sana Ke Baad.
Yeh Bhi Tu Ibn-e-Saqi-e-Kausar Ki Baat Hai.
