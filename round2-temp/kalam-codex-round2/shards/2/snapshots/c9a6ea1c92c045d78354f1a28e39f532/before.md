Muk Aasray Gaye Parday Daraan De.
Ghazi Tere Badoon Bhaina Nu Takney Paye Gaye Moor Bazaraan De.

1
Kayi Dasht Veer Ghazi Saday Safar Che Guzrey.
Rahwaan Na Mar Gaye Ne Kayi Baal Qafilay De.
Kuj Dig Ke Mar Gaye Aountaan Taun Kuj Nu Mar Gaye Aount Sawaran De.
Aynj Veer Khatam Hoi Be Pardagi Di Halat.

2
Zalim Da Qaid Khana Baniya Rida Di Naimat.
Chan Veer Shukar De Sah Aaye Saye Wekhay Ne Chaar Diwaraan De.
Peshi Taun Pehle Pehle Phul Shamiyaan Wasaye.
Abid De Chaar Pasay Assi Apne Sir Wadhaye.

3
Veran Assi Ewein Rokay Ne Qaidi Hathaan Nal Pathar Hazaran De.
 Saqlain Deen Di Aye Tableeg Lazmi Si.
Rasiyaan Che Mojzay San Par Bad Dua Na Keeti.
Mukhtar Te Malik Dunya De Phirday Reh Gaye Waang Lacharan De.
