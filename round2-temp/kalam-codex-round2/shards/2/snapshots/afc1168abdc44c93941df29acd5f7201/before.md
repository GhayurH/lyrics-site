1
Rahwaar Ke Qadmo Mein Roti Sakina Hai.
Baba Na Mera Le Ja Kehti Sakina Hai.
Baba Ke Bin MUjhko Na Kabhi Chain Aaye Ga.
Seene Ko Bichona Jo Na Baba Banaye Ga.

2
Yeh Gham Judai Ka Mujhe Baba Sataye Ga.
Yeh Gham BHi Mere Dil Mein Baba Utar Gaya Hai.
Ammo Ko Bulati Hoon Jane Kidhar Gaya Hai.
Woh Chain Sa Zamana Mera Guzar Gaya Hai.

3
Baba Tumhare Bad Jo Zulm Sitam Kare Ga.
Na Meri Kamsini Pe Thora Reham Kare Ga.
Paband Salasal Yeh Ahl-e-Haram Kare Ga.
Ghurbat Ki Mere Baba Jab Sham Aaye Gi.

4
Ummat Tere Nana Ki Tab Khaimay Jalaye Gi.
Baba Humein Yeh Ummat Our Kitna Rulaye Gi.
Baba Tumhare Bad Musibat Yeh Aaye Gi.
Ummat Dikha Ke Pani Zameen Par Giraye Gi.

5
Pyasay Rahein Gaye Hum Na Hum Ko Pilaye Gi.
Noha Kuna Farman Howa Arz-o-Samaa Hai.
Rota Hamari Be-Basi Pe Ab Yeh Jahaan Hai.
Haye Bali Sakina Ke Lab Par Yeh Fogaan Hai.
