1
Nana Teri Ummat Ne Lut Layiyaan Ne Ridawaa Ch Faiyaz Party.
Na Hondi Aynj Main Be Pardah Ghazi Diyaan Hundiyan Banwaan.
Main Sham Bazaraan Wich Ro Ro Ke Kaindi Rayiyaan.
Nayi Veer Mera Bagi Khutbay Sunandi Rayiyaan.

2
Dhi Main Batool Di Zainab Darbar Che Kiwein Janwaan.
Majma Sharabiyaan Da Fizza De Ohlay Rayiyaan.
Be Warsaan De Wangoon Veran Main Rondi Rayiyaan.
Lutiya Aye Aynj Musalmana Wasdi Saan Naal Bhirawaan.

3
Naizay Uttay Tilawat Quran Di Aye Keeti.
Aisi Namaz Maula Teraan De Heth Neeti.
Shabeer Ne Kharediyan Ne ALLAH Diyaan San Jo Razawan.
Zanjeeraan Ne Jhukaya Ghairat Ne Lao Rawaya.

4
Rab Janrey, Janrey Abid Kiwein Oh Sham Aaya.
Gash Khaway Wekh Ke Mainu Ainu Kiwein Hosh Karawaan.
Be Kafan Lash Teri Haye Karbala Che Reh Gayi.
Tainu Chad Ke Sham Tur Gayi Sadma Aye Kiwein Seh Gayi.

5
Jaye Honda Was Mere Veran Tera Chehlum Aap Karawaan.
Zainab Di Be Basi Da Tainu Maula Wasta Aye.
Adna Jiya Amanat Karda Aye Iltija Aye.
 Tahir Te Rooz-e-Mehshar Nu Almaan Diyaan Howan Chawaan.
