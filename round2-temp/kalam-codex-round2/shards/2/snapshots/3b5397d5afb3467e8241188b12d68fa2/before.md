Kisay Kamsin Putar De Sir Taun Uth Jan Na Piyo De Saye.
Karo Band Akhiyaan Na Baba Ro Ke Mehdi Farmaye.

1
1- Chup Taziya Taan Aye Banda Dita Hukam Si Ayeh Maloon Aye.
Iss Ghar Choon Rowan Di Na Aawaz Kisay Di Aaye.
2- Babay Di Lash Te Aayi Mehdi Nu Yaad Sakina.
Na Ron Dita Rayi Khandi Zalim De Tamachay Haye.

2
3- Kehya Askari Maula Narjis Ainu Seene La Ke Ro Le.
Kuj Dair Nu Aye Ho Jana Tere Mehdi Lal Juda Aye.
4- Mera Lal Hawalay Rab De Mil Zehar Gayi Aye Mainu.
Hai Wasiyat Mera Janaza Mera Mehdi Aap Parhaye.

3
5- Tere Deen Di Khatir Chadiya Nana Asaan Shehar Madina.
Wich Samarra Be Watna Te Teri Ummat Zulm Kamaye.
6- Sajjad Laga Ke Seene Mehdi Nu Maula Aakhan.
Bad Nazraan Kolon Tainu Mera Khaliq Pak Bachaye.
