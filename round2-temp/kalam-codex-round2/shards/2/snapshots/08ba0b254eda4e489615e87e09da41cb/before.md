1
Na Maro Musalmano Ye Zahra Ka Chain Hai.
Kehti Rahi Yeh Zainab Mera Hussain Hai.
Ab Dasht-e-Karbala Mein Koi Nahi Raha.
Ghar Lut Gaya Rasool Ka Behno Ka Bain Hai.

2
Kyun Bar Bar Khanjar Chalata Hai Aye Laeen.
Zahra Dukhi Ka Sun Lo Yeh Noor-e-Ain Hai.
Jal Gaye Khiyaam Our Ridaein Bhi Chin Gayin.
Ghazi Nahi Raha Yeh Sakina Ka Bain Hai.

3
Roye Sada Hussain Pe Dua Hai Batool Ki.
Matam Karein Gaye Shah Ka Yeh Nasb-e-Ain Hai.
Na Haq Yeh Khon Tu Ne Bahaya Hai Kis Liye.
Yeh Murtaza Nabi Ke Dil Ka Tu Chain Hai.
