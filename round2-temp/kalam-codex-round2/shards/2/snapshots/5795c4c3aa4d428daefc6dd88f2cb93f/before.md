Khaki Hain Moajze Ka Koi Tajurba Karein Write Up

1
Khaki Hain Moajze Ka Koi Tajurba Karein – Imam Hasan Manqabat.
Madh-e-Hassan Ke Naam Pe Kar-e-Khuda Karein.
Shabar Ka Ishq Faqar Khilafat Ka Taj Fisq.
Iss Fasle Ki Roo Se Har Ik Faisla Karien.
Wajib Hai Ahl-e-Sham Pe Sulh-e-Hassan Ke Bad.
Taj-e-Shehi Ko Bheek Ka Kasa Kaha Karein.

2
Shabar Duaein Karte The Qasim Ka Ho Zahoor.
Zainab Yeh Chahti Hain Hassan Karbala Karein.
Karb-o-Bala Pukar Uthi Mehmal Ko Dekh Kar.
Zainab Qadam Rakhein Mujhe Khak-e-Shifa Karein.
Rizq Halal Khana Bhi Tab Tak Haram Hai.
Jab Tak Na Syeda Ka Qaseeda Parha Karein.

3
Saf Bandh Kar Khare Raho Kehte The Anbia.
Jab Fatima Hussain Ka Sadqa Diya Karein.
Hum Karbala Nazadoon Ki Hai Karbala Talab.
Janat Ke Bagh Lakh Haseen Hoon Howa Karein.
 Akbar Jo Chahte Hain Namazein Qabool Hoon.
Zahra Ke Dushmano Pe Tabara Kiya Karein.
