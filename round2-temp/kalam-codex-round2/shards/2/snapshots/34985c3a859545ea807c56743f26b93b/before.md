1
Nanna Mujahid Aagaya Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.
Ran Mein Jab Nana Mujahid Aa Gaya.
Youn Laga Hangam-e-Mehshar Cha Gaya.
Iss Ko Kehte Hain Nigah-e-Haidery.
Hurmala Jaisa Shaqi Ghabra Gaya.
Kehti Thi Bano Marey Nanhay Jarri.
Bah Gaya Tujh Ko Bhi Maqtal Bah Gaya.
Hansliyon Wala Bhi Khaybar Geer Tha.
Aik Pal Mein Qasar-e-Batil Dha Gaya.
Mil Na Payi Phir Kabhi Tujh Se Behan.
Gham Tera Sughra Ko Haye Kha Gaya.
Kat Nahi Sakti Zaban-e-Haq Kabhi.
Muskura Kar Be Zabaan Batla Gaya.
Be Zabaan Ki Haq Zabani Ko Salam.
Jo Kaleem Aaya Yahan Sharma Gaya.
