Mukiyan Na Udeekan Akbar Day Vichoray Sughara Nu Mukaya Ay

Roniya Main Rataan Nu Uth Kay Es Dard Nay Menu Nana Beemaar Banaya Ay

Aj Raat Da O Wela Naye Bhulda Ek Khwaab Daraya Menu

Hay Aakhri Sawaan Tay Akbar Wich Seenay Day Barchi Babay, Hath Barchi Nu Paya Ay

Naal Hanjoan Day Tehreer Likhi Main Ek Waar Tu Mil Ja Aakay

Teri Deed Day Bajon Haye Akbar Nayi Jeen Day Aadi Sughara Aye Yaad Karaya Ay

Karni Aan Subho Shaam Tasawur Jholay Di Main Dori Phad Kay

Wich Jholay Day Asghar Nu Paakay Naal Loriyan Day Kay Sughara Jhooley Nu Jhulaya Ay

Diwaraan Day Main Lay Kay Saharey Aa Bohiy Utay Bheni Aan

Eho Soch Kay Rawan Nu Takdi Kadon Avay Ga Shagnaan Wala, Jinoo Ghaar Main Bulaya Ay

Dil Dubda Ay Din Dasween Da Chariya Babay Di Khuda Khair Karay

Ay Haal Yatimaan Day Wangoon Bemaar Day Sir Ton Uthiya, Jewain Babay Da Saya Ay

Sabir Na Kadi Jyoondiyan Bhenan Enj Baaj Bhirawaan Jag Tay

Jeewain Veer Day Bajon Ay Wela Ena Ujray Gharaan Wich Kalyian Sughra Nay Bitaya Ay
