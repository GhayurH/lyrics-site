Wakey putar jawaan koo wal wal key jado marey kul mehman gaey

1
1) Tera wada ha aaj ratein da thanko akbar putar mala desan
Ajj veer tey gurbat ban gaey ai menday mare kul mehman gaey

2
2) Ajj sahil ban ke aya ha daa menat saal ataran de
Mendey Akbar bajo nein nibdi mendey mare kul mehman gaey

3
3) Dey puuue da safar akher kumar kul mursel bun wich denda rey
Cheki burchee puthar dey jigar vicho thei katil we herain ray
