Parho La Illaha Illallah

Koh Muhammed Pak Rasoolallah.

Ohnu Rulkay Mariyan Shaamiyan Nay  Jida Nana Dai Parda Sun Kalma.

Oh Saara Mulk Naamazi Si, Paar Rab Na Una Tai Razi Si
Bay Deen Utha Sab Qazi Si  Dita Aal-e-Rasool Tai Fatwa La.

Lai Veeran Meriyan Saravai, Tenu Ro Ro Vaajay Mara Iai
Abid Hath Puriyan Muhara Way  Menu Wekh Kai Ronda Shaam Da Rai.

Beta Ali Da Nabhi Da Nawasa Si, Odhai Hath Vich Deen Da Qasa Si
Tin Tin Da Yaaro Pyasa Si  Taati Rait Tay Roleya Deen Da Shah.

Veeran Ve Apna Nay Koiee, Beti Ali Di Karbal Vich Royi
Pardes Dai Vich Majboor Hoyi  Payan Hath Karian Pur Bismillah.

Jadoo Shaami Banga Milgaya, Karay Zainab Nana Rul Gayan
Sabna Diyan Sanga Hil Gaya Aan  Jadoo Aya Naam Muhammed Da.

Sab Kesay Kahani Phol Vekho, Tusi Saab Na Kitab Nu Khol Vekho
Sab Mulka Nu Tusi Tol Vekho  Nay Honay Aisa Bhain Bhira.

Javaid Aai Zahra Jaiyan Nai, Gaudi Paak Rasool Khadiyan Nai
Baan Qaidi Shaam Vich Aya Nai  Hai Lutlaya Ummat Nai Parda.
