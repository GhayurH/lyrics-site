1
Mustafa Ki Jo Yeh Nokari Hai Naat.
Hasil Hai Sharaf Muhammad Ke Ghulamo Ko Anokha.
Karte Hain Ghulam Inn Ke Faqeeri Mein Bhi Shahi.
Shehanshahi Se Bhi Qeemati Hai.

2
Takht Se Bhi Bari Taj Se Bhi Haseen Zakari Hai.
Mustafa Ki Jo Yeh Nokari Hai.
Shehansha Hain Yeh Sab Anbiya Ke.
Sab Nabi Inn Ke Dar Ke Gadagar.

3
Woh Hoon Salman, Bozar(AS Ya Qambar.
Sab Ke Sab Inn Ke Dar Pe Hain Nokar.
Sab Farishton Ka Sardar Hai Jo.
Oss Ko Bhi Nokari Yeh Mili Hai.

4
Apne Nokar Ka Mere Nabi Ne.
Martaba Iss Tarha Hai Barhaya.
Dunya Bhar Mein Jahan Bhi Giya Main.
Mujh Ko Dunya Ne Sar Pe Bithaya.

5
Jhuk Ke Milti Hai Mujh Se Yeh Dunya.
Nokari Ki Yeh Ujrat Mili Hai.
Qabar Mein Jab Farishton Ne Dekha.
Main Hoon Masroof Zikar-e-Nabi Mein.

6
Bhool Baithe Sawalaat Sare.
Jhoom Uthay Woh Ishq-e-Nabi Mein.
Sunne Aaye Hain Khud Panjtan Bhi.
Qabar Mein Khoob Mehfil Saji Hai.

7
Madah Khuwan-e-Muhmmad Jo Pohancha.
Ahl-e-Janat Ne Aankhein Bicha Deen.
Phool Barsaye Sab Anbiya Ne.
Shama-e-Tauheed Har Soo Jala Di.

8
Hans Ke Kehte The Yeh Hoor-o-Gilma.
Tujh Ko ALLAH Ne Izat Yeh Di Hai.
Jo Hai Gustakh Mere Nabi Ka.
Oss Se Nafrat Hai Farhan Mujh Ko.

9
Woh Jahanum Ka Indhan Bane Ga.
Yeh Batata Hai Quran Mujh Ko.
Dushman-e-Mustafa Mera Dushman.
Unn Ke Aashiq Se Bas Dosti Hai.
