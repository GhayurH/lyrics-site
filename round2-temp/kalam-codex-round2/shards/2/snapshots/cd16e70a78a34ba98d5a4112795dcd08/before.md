1
Kiya Hai Shan e Syeda Shahid Ali Shahid.
Kya Bataon Aap Ko Main Kya Hai Shan-e-Syeda.
Ban Ke Aate Hain Farishtay Sailan-e-Syeda.
Arsh Walon Ke Liye Hai Azmat-e-Noor-e-Khuda.

2
Farsh Walon Ke Liye Hai Markaz-e-Bazm-e-Kisa.
Rooh-o-Tasbeeh-o-Dua Hai Malka-e-Jod-o-Sakha.
Dehar Ki Ik Aik Aayat Dastan-e-Syeda.
Aayat-e-Ishq-e-Khuda Hai Aastan-e-Syeda.

3
Lab Pe Hai Teri Sana Aye Malka-e-Arz-o-Sama.
Hai Hijab-e-Rab-e-Akbar Teri Pakeeza Rida.
Arsh Ke Sare Farishtay Hain Tere Dar Ke Gada.
Saf Ba Saf Mehway Safar Hai Karwan-e-Syeda.

4
Hashar Tak Sar Par Rahe Ga Sayeban-e-Syeda.
Teri Ismat Ka Qaseeda Munkiron Par Hai Garaan.
Tera Khutba Har Zamane Ke Liye Haq Ki Azaan.
Chahiye Har Sahib-e-Eman Ko Teri Amma.

5
Jab Sar-e-Mehshar Sunai De Gi Shan-e-Syeda.
Khil Uthein Gay Masnadoon Par Zakiran-e-Syeda.
Khoon Rulati Hai Humein Zahra Ke Pehlu Ki Chubhan.
Aaj Bhi Taza Dilon Mein Hai Nabi Zadi Ka Gham.

6
Larkharaye Gasibon Kursi Nasheeno Ke Qadam.
Lehja-e-Karrar Hai Zoor-e-Bayan-e-Syeda.
Aaj Bhi Seena Sipar Hain Ashiqan-e-Syeda.
Chashma-e-Aab-e-Shifa Hai Humsar-e-Shair-e-Khuda.

7
Jis Ki Ik Nazar-e-Karam Hai Jalwah-e-Lutaf-e-Khuda.
Jan-o-Dil Tujh Pe Fida Aye Noor-e-Ain-e-Mustafa.
Aaj Shahid Ki Zabaan Par Hai Bayan-e-Syeda.
Dad Paye Ga Khuda Se Madah Khuwan-e-Syeda.
