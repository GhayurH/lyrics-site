1
Kevain Syedan Eid Manai Raza Hassan Sadiq.
Aj Baad Amari De Uth Gaya Saday Sir Taun Piyo Da Saya.
Babay De Janazay Te Dhiyaan Aye Ro Ro Hara Paya.
Rab Janrey Wich Pardesan Kiwein Syedaan Eid Manai.

2
Kandaan Nal Lag Lag Ronday Aynj Ummat Aal Rawai.
Aynj Ajar-e-Risalat Miliya Ummat Taun Pak Nabi Nu.
Wich Sajday Qatal Hai Kita Kalma Gowaan Ne Ali Nu.
Payi Ummat Eid Manandi Phori Sadaat Wachayi.

3
Asaan Jad Da Khuwab Aye Takiya Aye Sakoon Baba Neend Nayi Aaondi.
Asaan Wekhiya Ronda Nana Hay Maa Zahra Harey Pandi.
Takiya Jibreel V Ronda Payi Rondi Kul Khudai.
Paya Ronda Sara Kunba Si Haider De Naa Nu Cha Ke.

4
Jadon Waqt-e-Naza Dohaan Nu Babay Ne Kool Bola Ke.
Makhdoma Zainab Di Baan Shabeer De Hath Pharayi.
Hoya Qatal Ameer Al Momin Luti Syedaan Di Jageer Gayi.
Kofay Di Masjid Wich Oss Dam Mil Khuwab Di Aye Tabeer Gayi.

5
Haider De Sir Te Muljim Ne Jadon Aa Talwar Chalai.
Janat De Malik Ronday Paye Tak Ke Taqdeer V Roi.
Fil Qurba Wali Aayat Di Khursheed Hai Aynj Tafseer Hoi.
Ummat Haider De Yateema Nu Na Deen Dilasay Aayi.
