1
Logo Main Behan Hussain Di Haan Asghar Khan.
Bazariyon Mein Zainab Ahmad Di Nawasi Zahra Aye Meri Maa.
Kayi Aayataan Qurani Saday Pardiyaan Te Aayiyaan.
Sadi Chadraan Nu Rab Ne Tatheer Kadi Itrat Likhiya Aye Wich Quraan.

2
Main Khaliq-e-Haya Waan Abbas Veer Mera.
Ghazi Jaye Honda Jiyonda Main Wekhdi Kiwein Tusi Lainday Mera Naa.
Darbar Baqi Howay Naa Sham Baqi Howay.
Jaye Hoan V Muhammad Te Aoun De Sir Nu Ik Wari Keh Dewaan.

3
Shabeer Te Mere Layi Chadar Taun Qeemati Aye.
Lakh Maro Taziyane Shabeer De Sir Taun Nazraan Nayi Hatniyaan.
Haye Chali Saal Jehra Akhiyaan Choon Khon Roya.
 Saqlain Kha Gayiyaan San Abid De Jigar Nu Zainab Diyaan Peshiyaan.
