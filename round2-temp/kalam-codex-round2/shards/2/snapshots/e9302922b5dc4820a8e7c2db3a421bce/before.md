1
Qatil Ali Ke Dushman Khuda Ke Ali Abbas Khaku.
Qatil Sabhi Ali Ke Dushman Banay Khuda Ke.
Masjid Mein Murtaza Ka Sajday Mein Khoon Baha Ke.
Yeh Tu Ghadeer-e-Khum Ki Thi Dushmani Nabi Se.
Sufyaniyoon Ne Jis Ka Badlah Liya Ali Se.
Dastar-e-Mustafa Par Talwar Ko Chala Ke.
Jonhi Ali Ke Sar Pe Qatil Ne Zarb Mari.
Dastar Apne Sar Se Jibreel Ne Utari.
Qatal-e-Ali Pe Roya Nohay Suna Suna Ke.
Bad-e-Nabi Ali Ko Ummat Rahi Satati.
Ghayor-e-Do Jahan Ko Haye Rahi Rulati.
Zahra Pe Dar Gira Ke Haider Ka Ghar Jala Ke.
Ro Kar Tasali Detein Kulsoom Our Zainab.
Abbas They Taraptay Yeh Dekh Kar Ali Jab.
Rotay They Apne Seene Hasnain Ko Laga Ke.
Fizza Khiyal Rakhna Ro Kar Ali Yeh Bolay.
Main Janta Hoon Kal Yeh Marein Gay Kofay Walay.
Haider Ki Betiyon Ko Pathar Utha Utha Ke.
Beti Se Bolay Haider Mujhe Zakhm Jo Laga Hai.
Zainab Tere Ghamo Ki Kofay Mein Ibtida Hai.
Abhi Marhalay Hain Baqi Kitne Hi Karbala Ke.
Baba Ke Baad Zainab Aaye Ga Ik Zamana.
Tumhein Sath Karbala Mein Shabeer Ke Hai Jana.
Karne Hain Kam Tum Ne Sab Ibn-e-Fatima Ke.
Hasrat Se Murtaza Ne Betiyon Pe Jab Nazar Ki.
Sar Choma Betiyon Ka Our Ro Ke Yeh Dua Di.
Jeetay Rahein Mohafiz Tatheer Ki Rida Ke.
Qatil Ko Jaam Apne Haider Ne Jab Pilaya.
Oss Waqt Aik Manzar Ghazi Ko Yaad Aaya.
Ruktay Nahi They Aanso Abbas-e-Bawafa Ke.
Gash Kha Ke Gir Rahi Thin Sab Bibiyaan Asad Tab.
Le Jao Sath Mujh Ko Bolein Hasan Se Zainab.
Taboot Jab Ali Ka Betay Chaley Utha Ke.
