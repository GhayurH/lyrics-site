Ghar Bar Luta Kar Jangal Mein Jab Laut Ke Aayi Ghar Zainab.
Kabhi Apne Muhammad Ko Roi Kabhi Aoun Pe Noha Gar Zainab.

1
Bachoon Ke Hujray Mein Ja Kar Woh Khud Se Batein Karti Hain.
Phir Shukar Khuda Ka Karti Thi Ro Ro Ke Musallay Par Zainab.
Our Jab Yeh Sadaein Aati Thin Shabeer Pe Hum Qurban Hoye.
Kis Ko Yeh Kahey Tum Par Wari Tum Par Sadqay Madar Zainab.

2
Abhi Banhoon Mein Banhein Dalay Do Lal Yahan Se Aaein Gay.
Yeh Soch Ke Takti Rehti Thi Woh Apne Ghar Ka Dar Zainab.
Sar Kham Kar Ke Shehzadon Ke Mathay Ko Choma Karti Thi.
Leti Thi Balaein Bachoon Ki Phir Khud Se Lipta Kar Zainab.

3
Shab Ko Sunaye Rotay Hain Bachoon Ke Bistar Khali Hain.
Shehzadi Kis Par Dalay Gi Kyun Layi Hai Chadar Zainab.
Do Shehzaday Jin Se Lag Kar Hans Hans Ke Batein Karti Thi.
Takrati Hai Ab Roz-o-Shab Unn Dewaron Se Sar Zainab.

4
Jab Hosh Zara Bhi Aata Hai Abdillah Ko Batlati Hai.
Sheron Ki Larai Ke Qissay Our Maqtal Ke Manzar Zainab.
Main Aoun-o-Muhammad Ke Gham Mein Jab Ashk Bahaon:YASIR SHAH:.
Mujh Ko Hai Yaqeen Aati Hai Wahan Romal Liye Muztar Zainab.
