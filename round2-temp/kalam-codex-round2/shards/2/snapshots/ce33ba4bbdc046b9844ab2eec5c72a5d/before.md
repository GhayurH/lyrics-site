Mera Zakhmi Veer Hussain
Bhira Di Lash Nu Dhup Taun Bachan Aayi Aan
Main Apne Walaan Di Chawaan Banaon Aayi Aan
Mera Zakhmi Veer Hussain Aye, Rul Gayi Humsheer Hussain Aye
Main Qaidan Haan Tere Lashay Taun Kadan Kiwein Teer Hussain Aye
Kisay Bhain Da Veer Jay Qatal Howay Utton Loag Dilasay Denday Ne
Kidda Zulm Hai Veran Qatal Hoya Hoi Bhain Aseer Hussain Aye
Hik Veer Da Sar Hai Nezay Te Hik Bhain Di Chadar Nezay Te
Aj Bhain Bhira Di Ghurbat Te Roway Taqdeer Hussain Aye
Teri Lash De Chun Ke Nu Tukray Mera Hosla Wekh Main Jeendi Haan
Haye Badan Tera Sum Ghoriyaan De Gaye Jigar Nu Cheer Hussain Aye
Maikon Das Veran Tere Qatil Kyun Hans Hans Ke Sham Da Naa Chainda Aye
Main Sunrya Aye Iss Bazar De Wich Baon Hondi Aye Bheer Hussain Aye
Sar Chum Gin Sar Uryan Haan Main Teri Lehzay Di Mehman Haan Main
Jhal Jhal Ke Jhirkaan Chor Di Aan Teri Main Jageer Hussain Aye
IMRAN Aye Ujari Wain Kita Tera Khon Kadi Nayi Chup Sakda
Tera Qatal Sada Mere Bazoaan Te Reh Si Tehreer Hussain Aye
