1
Mola Raza Isteghasa Mir Sajjad Mir
Tum Gareebon Ka Bharam Ho Tum Hamari Aas Ho.
Door Reh Kar Door Kab Ho Tum Tu Dil Ke Pas Ho.
Hajat Rawa Mushkil Kusha Maula Raza Ya Maula Raza.

2
Jab Satata Hai Koi Tum Yaad Aate Ho Bohat.
Jab Rulata Hai Koi Tum Yaad Aate Ho Bohat.
Koi Nahi Tere Siwa Maula Raza Ya Maula Raza.
Be Khata Bhai Se Apne Jo Bhi Behnein Hain Juda.

3
Fatima Sughra Ki Sorat Tak Rahi Hain Rasta.
Ho Jaein Woh Jaldi Reha Maula Raza Ya Maula Raza.
Tere Qadmo Ke Nishaan Hain Tujh Se Hi Pur Noor Hai.
Tujh Se Hi Aabad Hai Yeh, Yeh Bhi Nishapur Hai.

4
Dil Hai Mera Khaima Tera Maula Raza Ya Maula Raza.
Ghar Bitha Kar Tu Khilata Hai Humein Kis Shan Se.
Rotiyaan Sab Ki Lagin Hain Tere Dastar Khuwan Se.
Raziq Hai Tu Konain Ka Maula Raza Ya Maula Raza.

5
Aap Ne Darbar Mein Jab Muskura Ke Kun Kaha.
Rooh Oss Mein Aa Gayi Jo Shair Tha Qaleen Ka.
Zindah Howa Kehne Laga Maula Raza Ya Maula Raza.
Chom Kar Roza Kaha Tha Ab Nahi Ho Gi Khata.

6
Aa Raha Hoon Laut Kar Daman Khata Se Hai Bhira.
Aaye Gi Phir Tum Se Haya Maula Raza Ya Maula Raza.
Sehan-e-Hur-e-Amli Sehan-e-Shifa Sehan-e-Ghadeer.
Aik Janat Mein Yeh Kitni Janatein Hain Be Nazeer.

7
Noor-e-Khuda Roza Tera Maula Raza Ya Maula Raza.
Kehte Hain Sajjad Esa Zaiqa Hai Aik Hi.
Karbala Ki Khak Bhi Sehan-e-Shifa Ki Khak Bhi.
Yeh Bhi Dawa Woh Bhi Dawa Maula Raza Ya Maula Raza.
