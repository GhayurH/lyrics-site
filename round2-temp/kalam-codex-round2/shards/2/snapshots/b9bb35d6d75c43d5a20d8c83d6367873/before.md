Loriyon sai sonay walay so rahay ho tum kahan
Do sada maqtal say Asghar dhundti phirti hay maa

1
1) Hathon pai mehndi lagati sar pai sehra bandh ti
Reh gayai armaan adhurai tum na ho payai Jawan

2
2) Ro pari sham-e-Ghariban dekh ker sooai Khaiyam
Uth raha hai ma kai dil sai ja kai khaimon sai dhuan

3
3) Siskian bharti hai maadari gaud veeran dekh kar
Khali jhulai sai lipat kai rorahi hai loriyan

4
4) Sharm sai derya ki lehrain pani pani ho gayain
Hay jab sookh-e-labon per Pheri asgher nai Zabaan

5
5) Maut ko aya paseena halq-e-asgher dekh ker
Kampta tha teer Hurmal haath mai larzi kamaan

6
6) Qabar main Jawad o Sadiq sath lai ker jayain gai
Dil main tera dard Asgher ankh sai ansoo rawan
