## Shared

- This is a transcription/review of ONE already-deduplicated original kalam, never English translation. For a non-English work only source-language verse and phonetic Roman transliteration belong in the file. Never add translated verses, explanations, source URLs, scraper credits, or processing logs as published content.
- The verified original-language reading is authoritative. Do not invent a missing source, infer variant wording from Roman alone, guess an OCR correction, add historical/sectarian details, or change source text to make two verse counts match. Leave evidence-dependent problems unresolved in your final response.
- Work only in the selected Markdown. Preserve filenames, aliases, URLs, IDs, recitation links, poet and takhallus within the poem, title meaning, meaningful variants, stanzaPattern, poet/reciter metadata, and existing verified wording. No unrelated poems, Basta, identity locks, trackers, imports, commits, pushes or broad normalizers.
- The Markdown BODY is the source-language poetry; Roman belongs only in YAML \`romanLyrics: |-\` with indented lines. Never use \`language: Roman Urdu\` or \`lang: ur-Latn\`. Original languages: Urdu \`language: Urdu, lang: ur, direction: rtl\`; Shahmukhi Punjabi \`Panjabi, pa-Arab, rtl\`; Saraiki \`Saraiki, skr-Arab, rtl\`; Balti \`Balti, bft-Arab, rtl\`; Arabic \`Arabic, ar, rtl\`; Persian \`Persian, fa, rtl\`. Genuine verified Latin originals may use pa/skr/bft and ltr; do not falsely reclassify. Preserve English originals as English/en/ltr. Do not infer language from a title or isolated words.
- Keep source \`title\` original-language and \`alternateTitle\` Roman where available, with meaningful poem-specific wording, not necessarily first verse. Roman title in Title Case; Roman verse text in sentence/proper-name case. Keep complete poet names. Preserve truthful tags and update only an actually resolved missing-edition tag, using \`tags: ["noUrdu"]\`-style YAML arrays, never redundant conflicting tags.
- Each actual verse is ONE physical line; retain original order, meaningful commas and accents, no ornamental period at verse end. Preamble is distinct from stanza 1. Standalone \`Saani\` identifies a continuation of opening, \`Antra\` identifies a stanza; remove these literal labels, not their poems. Number source stanzas using Urdu numerals \`۱ ۲ ۳\` (U+06F1, not \`١\`), Roman \`1 2 3\`; numbers stand alone, with no empty line after each. Separate stanza blocks with blank lines. Preserve real refrain meanings, avoid spurious repeated title refrains, and do not remove meaningful repetition.
- Preserve justified stanza grouping, including 4+2 six-verse and reversed second-line two-verse forms; those are layout semantics, not a reason to split physical verses. Preserve verified \`[[subtitle]]\`/\`[[refrain]]\` roles if present; never invent markers, stanzaLineExceptions or source-page fragment roles.
- When source and Roman both exist they must correspond verse by verse and stanza by stanza, except an existing *verified* stanzaLineExceptions. Do not shorten a Roman or source verse to force counts. Preserve real quotations and punctuation. Metadata edits require evidence.
- Preserve verified honorifics in original-language source; omit honorifics from Roman pronunciation aids unless the apparent honorific is genuinely part of the poem, rather than merely metadata. Keep in-verse takhallus as poetry, never treat tokens like \`:REHAN:\` as formatting or credits.
- Remove reciter \`x2\`/\`(x2)\` markers: at a verse end, remove marker and preceding space; between phrases of the same verse, replace with comma plus space. Never delete actual words.
- Only Urdu \`بی‌بی\` may contain U+200C ZWNJ; plural \`بیبیاں\` and \`بیبیوں\` must not. Do not introduce other ZWNJs. Never alter the source on a merely cosmetic Roman task.

## Roman

- Roman is a PHONETIC transliteration of the original, not independent wording or an English rendering. Use Latin ASCII throughout except \`ṅ\`/\`Ṅ\` specifically for Urdu ں (nasal); plain \`n\` for ن. Never use macrons, scholarly transliteration marks, curly apostrophes as ayn, combining accents, accented Latin letters or other diacritics.
- Long vowels are \`aa\`, \`u\`, \`i\`, not \`oo\`, \`ee\`. Urdu ے normally ends in \`e\` as in \`bole, wale, mere\`; distinct Punjabi pronunciation requires evidence. Distinguish \`jawaaṅ\` for جواں from \`jawaan\` for جوان.
- Urdu ڑ becomes \`r\`; preserve \`q\` for ق versus \`k\` for ک, \`z\` for ز/ذ/ض/ظ, \`s\` for س/ث/ص; where pronounced use \`bh, ph, th, dh, kh, gh, jh, chh\`. Pronounced ع and ء may use \`a\` when context requires, but never blindly insert extra vowels.
- Distinguish \`main\` (I) and \`mein\` (in), \`kya\` (what) and \`kiya\` (did). Write \`Kiun\` or \`Kiuṅ\` (not \`kyun\`), \`Husain\` (not \`Hussain/Hussein\`), \`Hasan\`, \`Bibi\`. Both \`Zehra\` and \`Zahra\` are legitimate poem-dependent spellings.
- Vocative اے is \`Ae\` (or \`ae\` mid-sentence), never \`E, Ay, Aye, Ey\` when vocative; do not confuse an izafat suffix \`-e-\` with a vocative. Retain meaningful izafat as in \`Gham-e-Husain\` but write separate conjunction و as \`o\`, as in \`Shab o Roz\`; no unnecessary hyphens. Write \`bewafa\` (not \`be-wafa\`) and \`baar baar\` (not \`baar-baar\`).
- Normalize without homogenizing contextual readings. Only verified source/pronunciation resolves ambiguous consonants, proper nouns, honorifics or dialect-specific vowels. Never add poetry that was absent from original; Roman stanza numbers must be Western 1,2,3.
- Examples: \`اے حسین\` → \`Ae Husain\`; \`شب و روز\` → \`Shab o Roz\`; \`غمِ حسین\` → \`Gham-e-Husain\`; \`جواں\` → \`jawaaṅ\`, but \`جوان\` → \`jawaan\`; \`بے وفا\` → \`bewafa\`; \`بار بار\` → \`baar baar\`; \`کیوں\` → \`Kiun\` or \`Kiuṅ\`.

## Source

- Work from the Roman text as a guide to pronunciation, NOT as evidence of the exact original script. Search ONLY for relevant existing locally available primary evidence or verified duplicate originals when necessary; do not perform broad corpus audits. If the exact reading cannot be verified, do not invent the source text: leave the file unchanged or make only independently verified corrections and explicitly report the missing evidence.
- When the Roman-only poem currently lives in the Markdown body, after verifying original script move that existing Roman faithfully into `romanLyrics: |-` and place the authentic source in the body. Do not treat Roman-only material as a separate English source, and never erase the only available Roman text. Retain the existing Roman edition even when source is not recoverable. If evidence proves an original Arabic-script Urdu, Panjabi, Saraiki, Balti, Arabic or Persian reading, restore it in the Markdown body with verified spelling and stanza boundaries, update the language code and missing-source tag, and preserve Roman order. A genuinely Latin-script original stays Latin, not an invented Shahmukhi/Urdu source.
- If the source cannot be established, stop that task for human verification; never disguise Roman transliteration as authentic original-language writing.

## Audit

- Examine EACH Roman verse against its exact source verse, not just line totals. Verify language-aware vowels, nasal consonants, phonetics, stanza alignment, digits, boundary labels, honorific handling and omission of English translations. Fix only supported discrepancies, leave uncertainty visible. An unchanged transliteration still needs human editorial sign-off.
- Avoid whole-repo discovery, unrelated duplicate passes, extra searches, and repeated test/build workflows. Work in one poem and give a short factual summary of actual edits and unresolved issues.
