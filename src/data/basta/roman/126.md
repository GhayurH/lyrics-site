Salam us par ke jo lakht-e-dil-e-Shabbir-e-Khuda bhi hai
Hazrat Kamal Ganjavi

Salam us par jo lakht-e-dil-e-Sher-e-Khuda bhi hai
Nawasa Mustafa ka, Fatima ka ladla bhi hai
Salam us par ke takmil-e-nubuwwat ka jo hasil hai
Jo khud rahi nahin, jada nahin hai ain manzil hai
Salam us par ke jis ka hashr tak paigham zinda hai
Lahu dene se jis ke ghairat-e-Islam zinda hai
Salam us par ke jis ka faiz-e-gauhar-bar baqi hai
Wafa zinda, muhabbat surkh-ru, isar baqi hai
Khuda-fahmi ki kaifiyat dilon par jis ne tari ki
Salam us par ke jo insaniyat ki qadr-e-aali hai
Salam us par ke jo insaniyat ki qadr-e-ala hai
Mashiyat ne imamat ke jise sanche mein dhala hai
(Bashukriya Salim Raza, Kalim Raza aur Professor Simin Kazmi)

Zikr hum karte rahenge hashr tak Shabbir ka
Shair o sozkhwan Wali Jalalvi

Zikr hum karte rahenge hashr tak Shabbir ka
Mutabar hai ye tariqa qalb ki tathir ka
Dard se na-ashna kya jane kya hai Karbala
Dard wala hi karega tazkira Shabbir ka
Hausla bhi kanp utha dekh kar azm-e-Husain
Khun-e-chullu mein liya jab Asghar-e-be-shir ka
Apne khutbon se hila kar rakh diya qasr-e-Yazid
[Ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]

Hausla Allahu Akbar Zainab-e-dilgir ka
Haq ada Karb-o-Bala mein kar diya Abbas ne
Quwwat-e-Haider ka aur Umm-ul-Banin ke sher ka
Yaad aate hain hamein bimar ke tauq-o-rasan
Is liye karte hain hum matam bapa zanjir ka
[Ek sher juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Hurr ka sar zanu-e-Shah par tha rumal-e-Batul
Aadmi dekha nahin aisa dhi taqdir ka
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed Wali Haider)

Fatima khari darwaze par Sham-o-sahar
Rasta takti thin pairon Akbar-e-dilgir ka

Kabhi fart-e-adab mein ashk-afshani nahin jati
Hain lab khamosh lekin marsiyakhwani nahin jati
Haram ki be-ridai ne liya hai intiqam aisa
Yazidiyat ki pardon mein bhi uryani nahin jati
Na bharte rang is mein gar lahu se Karbala wale
To aaj Islam ki surat bhi pehchani nahin jati
Hukumat Ahl-e-Duniya ke faqat hakim ke dam tak hai
Dilon par jo hukumat ho wo sultani nahin jati
Koi Haq ka mujahid sar bhat aata hai maidan mein
Khule sar Sham ke balwe mein Syedani nahin jati
Hazaron bandishen hoti hain matam pe shahidon ke
Shahab is par bhi apni marsiyakhwani nahin jati
