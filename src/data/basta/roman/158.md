Hazrat ko hua mah-e-Muharram jo safar mein

1
Hazrat ko hua mah-e Muharram jo safar mein
Ek dagh para aur bhi Sughra ke jigar mein
Nani se kaha marti hun duridi-e pidar mein
Ashur ki bhi Eid na ho lungi mere ghar mein

Kya janati thi aise bichhar jayen hue Baba
Wo din bhi kabhi ho ga ke phir aayen hue Baba

2
Kyun Nani Rajab tha ke sidhare the safar ko
Pure chhe mahine hue duridi-e pidar ko
Hajj kar ke phire ahl-e-matam-e watan khair o ghar ko
Phir qibla-ru o Kaaba gaye aakhir kidhar ko

Zahra ka Qamar suye Madina nahin aaya
Is chand ki ruyat ka mahina nahin aaya

3
Paida hue Asghar to payam-e-ajal aaya
Afsos ke jhule mein bahen ne na jhulaya
Bhaiya ko na ji bhar ke gale se bhi lagaya
Bichhre to kabhi khwab mein bhi munh na dikhaya

Dakhil-e Shah din ab bhi na ghar mein hue Nani
Asghar chhe mahine ke safar mein hue Nani
