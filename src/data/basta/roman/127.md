Shahon ka tazkira hai na lashkar ki baat hai
Syed Jawad Hasan

Shahon ka tazkira hai na lashkar ki baat hai
Jo Karbala mein lut gaya is ghar ki baat hai
Zikr-e gham-e Husain hamein kyun na ho aziz
Khushundi-e Khuda o Payambar ki baat hai
Majlis ek ehtijaj hai har zulm ke khilaf
Paigham-e adl, matam-e Sarwar ki baat hai
Hogi na Karbala ki kabhi khatm dastan
Kehne ko yun to sirf-e behtar ki baat hai
kis kis ko Roin Ahl-e Haram kis ki baat hai
Hai Umr bhar ka dagh bhare ghar ki baat hai
Ye imtihan-e sabr bahut sakht hai Husain
Mahshar mein be-radai-e khwahar ki baat hai
Qasid-e ko kya jawab din is-e baat ka Husain
Asghar ke khat mein shadi-e Akbar ki baat hai
[Ek sher juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Soz-e gham-e Husain mila jis ko mil-e gaya
(Bashukriya shair o marsiya khwan Syed Javed Hasan)

Anjum ye apne apne muqaddar ki baat hai
Syed Jawad Hasan

Kaho na hajat-rawai-e zikr-e Shabbir kya hai
Husain hi ne to sabit kya Khuda kya hai
Gham-e Husain dilon ka naqfaq dhota hai
Bas ab na puchho ke rone ka Faida kya hai
Razaye Haq ki har ek rah mein naqsh-e Husain
Mein Karbala se na jaun to rasta kya hai
Agar Husain ki sirat pe ho saka na amal
To phir ye Majlis o matam ka Faida kya hai
Palat na aate jo darya se tishna lab-e Abbas
To kaun janta is-e dahr mein wafa kya hai
Baqaye din ki zamanat hai Fatima ka pisar
Nahin Husain to Islam mein dharara kya hai
Radaye Ahl-e Haram chhin kar na khush hun lain
Wo jaan dene ko tayyar hain rida kya hai
Ye Karbala ke shahidon ne hal kya warna
Ke khabar thi fana-e kya hai aur baqa kya hai
(Bashukriya Maulana Doctor Syed Baqir Husain Zaidi)

Salami Karbala mein kya qayamat ki ghari hogi
Purnam Allah Abadi

Salami Karbala mein kya qiyamat ki ghari hogi
Phiri Shabbir ki gardan pe jis dam chal rahi hogi
Kaleja tham kar pair-e falak bhi reh gaya hoga
Kaleje par Ali Akbar ke barchhi jab lagi hogi
Mujhe jane do pani bhar ke ye Abbas kehte the
Kai din ki pyasi hai Sakina ro rahi hogi
Husain Ibn-e Ali ne ki hai jo tahigon ke saye mein
[Ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Nabi se besh-tar mahshar mein ummat-e bakhshwane ko
Husain Ibn-e Ali aayen hue duniya dekhti hogi
