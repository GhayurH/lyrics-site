Samjhaya zabt kar ke Janab-e Amir ne

1
Ay amtiyat roo ke ye fasla-e aza hai
Is-e mah-e safar mein safar-e khair aluri hai
Sadat mein faryad hai shewan hai buka hai
Ashur-e Muharram se fazon hashr bapa hai

Ek aur qayamat bhi isi roz hui hai
[Misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]

2
Is chand ne do dagh diye Aal-e Aal-e-Aba ko
Shabbir ko kabhi roo kabhi khair aluri ko
Sadat ko rahat hai na Ashab-e-Safa ko
Zahra ko na taskin na aram-e Khuda ko

rukh-e zard hai Mahbub-e Ilahi ko ye Tab hai
Khurshid ki hiddat hai to Bijli ki tarap hai

3
Wo Naza ke asar wo Kaunain ka Hadi
Is dhyan mein betab Shah ki Shahzadi
[Matn juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Ya hum Mursal aap pe taslim Khuda ho
Bachchon ka abhi biyah na dekha na shadi

4
Mahbub pyar kiya jo sitamgar-o-kabir ne
Samjhaya zabt kar ke Janab-e-Amir ne
Tadbir-e-ghusl-e-Shah-e-Umam ki, wazir ne
Nagah kisi ne dar-e daulat pe Nida di

Go hal-e-gham se ghair tha zauj-e-Batul ka
Kafnaya ke khud janaza uthaya Rasul ka

Ya khatm-e rasl aap pe taslim-e Khuda ho

Iqbal Kazmi
Gham tere ke alam ko Abbas ne uthaya
Abbas ke alam ko duniya utha rahi hai

Ijaz Aslam
Sadaq dil se gaya jo bhi dar-e Shabbir par
Hamara ke liye aun jo Hazrat ki Raza ho
