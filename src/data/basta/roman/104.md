Soz

Jab ran mein Zakhmi ho gaye Sultan-e Bahr o bar
Shafqat se hath Ghore ki gardan mein dal kar
Kehne lage Husain ke aata nahin nazar
Le chal mujhe to Akbar-e mah-e ro ki lash par

Ay apsa-e bawafa mere dil ko qarar de
Is-e ka sila tujhe Shah-e dildal sawar de

Ghora qadam qadam suye maqtal hua rawan
Pahunche na Shah ke zakhmon ko ta sadmas takan
Ek najwanon ki lash pe thehra wo be-kasi zaban
Bu songh kar Husain pukare yahi hai thas Han

Baitha jo Zuljanah to aap utre zini se
Do hath kanpte hue uthe zamin se
Ek naujawan ki lash pe thehra wo be-zaban

Karbala se jab Hussaini qafila luta hua
Bal-e Matami rota Madine ko chala
Dekh kar gor-e Sham-e-Ghariban Ahl-e-Bait-e Mustafa
Fatiha parh kar lage rone ba-sad aah o buka

Bolin Zainab az zamin-e Karbala bashim-e shor o shain
Saunp kar tujh ko chali hun apna main bhai Husain

Ay zamin tujh mein Ali Asghar mera pinhan hua
Ay zamin tujh mein Ali Akbar mera pinhan hua
Biyah ki shab Qasim-e muztar mera pinhan hua
Ne bhi pinhan hua thas Jafar mera pinhan hua

Itne gham thas mein le chali hun qatl ke bazar se
Par hua daman tera kya kya dar-e shahwar se
Bia ki shab Qasim Muzaffar mera pinhan hua

Shabbir ko Khaliq ne Sakina jo ata ki
Zainab ki si surat thi sab is mah-e mah-liqa ki
Jis dam diya godi mein use La ke chacha ki
Abbas ne hathon ko utha kar ye dua ki

Rakhe tujhe ay ladli Allah salamat
Duniya mein rahe sar pe tere Shah Salamat
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Zia-ul-Hasan o Zakir Husain baradaran)

Mirza Dabir

Jab Yazid apne gunahon se pasheman hua
Hans ke pehle sifat-e zakhm wo giryan hua
Subh kazib ki tarah chak gareban hua
Kul ki Shahzadiyon ke pechhne ka saman hua

Ek taraf samne darbar mein hadad aaye
Ek taraf Beriyan pehne hue Sajjad aaye
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed Ali Zaidi)
