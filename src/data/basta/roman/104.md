Soz

Jab ran mein zakhmi ho gaye Sultan-e-bahr-o-bar
Shafqat se hath ghore ki gardan mein dal kar
Kehne lage Husain ke aata nahin nazar
Le chal mujhe tu Akbar-e-mah-ru ki lash par

Ay asp-e-bawafa mere dil ko qarar de
Is ka sila tujhe Shah-e-Duldul-sawar de

Ghora qadam qadam su-e-maqtal hua rawan
Pahunche na Shah ke zakhmon ko ta sadma-e-takan
Ek naujawan ki lash pe thehra wo be-zaban
Bu sungh kar Husain pukare yahi hai, han

Baitha jo Zuljanah to aap utre zin se
Do hath kanpte hue uthe zamin se
(Bashukriya shair o sozkhwan Muzaffar Naqvi, Amrohvi)

---***---

Karbala se jab Husaini qafila luta hua
Ba-libas-e-matami rota Madine ko chala
Dekh kar gor-e-ghariban Ahl-e-Bait-e-Mustafa
Fatiha parh kar lage rone ba-sad-aah-o-buka

Bolin Zainab az zamin-e-Karbala ba-shor-o-shain
Saunp kar tujh ko chali hun apna main bhai Husain

Ay zamin tujh mein Ali Asghar mera pinhan hua
Ay zamin tujh mein Ali Akbar mera pinhan hua
Biyah ki shab Qasim-e-muztar mera pinhan hua
Aun bhi pinhan hua, Jafar mera pinhan hua

Itne gham, main le chali hun qatl ke bazar se
Pur hua daman tera kya kya dur-e-shahwar se
(Iltimas Surah Fatiha baraye sozkhwan Mushtaq Ali Khan)

---***---

Shabbir ko Khaliq ne Sakina jo ata ki
Zainab ki si surat thi sab us mah-liqa ki
Jis dam diya godi mein use la ke chacha ki
Abbas ne hathon ko utha kar ye dua ki

Rakhe tujhe ay ladli Allah salamat
Duniya mein rahe sar pe tere Shah salamat
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Zia-ul-Hasan o Zakir Husain baradaran)

Mirza Dabir

---***---

Jab Yazid apne gunahon se pashiman hua
Hans ke pahle sifat-e-zakhm wo giryan hua
Subh-e-kazib ki tarah chak-gareban hua
Kal ki shahzadiyon ke puchhne ka saman hua

Ek taraf samne darbar mein haddad aaye
Ek taraf beriyan pehne hue Sajjad aaye
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed Ali Zaidi)
