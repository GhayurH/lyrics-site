Salam

Salami jaan guzra hai ranj-o-gham khasan-e-Dawar ka
Nasim Amrohvi

Salami jaan guzra hai ranj-o-gham khasan-e-Dawar ka
Qalaq Sibtain ka, Zahra ka, Haider ka, Payambar ka
Sada shahra rahega jud-o-khulq-o-zor-e-Haider ka
Qatar-o-sher-o-angushtar ka, dar ka, ruh ke par ka

Ali ki tigh ke dam se hua har marka faisal
Uhud ka, Badr ka, Siffin ka, Khandaq ka, Khaibar ka
Ye panchon sure ay dil, Panjtan ki shan mein aaye
Qamar ka, Shams ka, Rahman ka, Maryam ka, Kausar ka

Fida-e-Shah ho kar Hurr ne, kis kis ka sharaf paya
Uwais-o-Zaid ka Ammar ka Salman ka Buzar ka
Nishan mit kar wafadari mein kaisa naam nikla hai
Zuhair-o-Muslim-o-Wahab-o-Habib-o-Hurr Safdar ka

Ghulam-e-Panjtan ko dar nahin in panch chizon ka
Ajal ka, jaan-kani ka, qabr ka, barzakh ka, mahshar ka
Mila hai rone walon ko sawab ek aah ka kya kya
Salat-o-saum ka khums-o-zakat-o-Hajj-e-Akbar ka

Siwaye tishnagi Shabbir ko ek ek sadma tha
Bhatijon bhanjon ka, bhai ka, Akbar ka, Asghar ka
[Chand misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Sakina le gayi ye panch dagh is bagh-e-alam se

Tamanchon ka, rasan ka, bap ka, ammu ka, gauhar ka
Tarap ke kehti thi Bano, karun kis kis ka main matam
Jawan ka, tifl ka, damad ka, dukhtar ka, shohar ka

Tah-e-khanjar Imam-e-Pak ko kis kis ka dhyan aaya
Bahen ka, beti ka, bimar ka, ummat ka, mahzar ka
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed Asghar Abbas Zaidi Farrukh Bahari, aur sozkhwan Aziz Zaidi o baradaran)

Hai salam us pe jo kehti thi sada, haye Husain
Raja Kanwar / Lucknow

Hai salam us pe jo kehti thi sada, haye Husain
Zalimon ne tujhe pani na diya, haye Husain
Ali Akbar tere samne barchhi khai
Qatl Asghar tere hathon pe hua, haye Husain

Dukh pe dukh sah ke jise Fatima ne pala tha
Us pe be-dinon ne ye zulm kiya, haye Husain
Na raha koi janaze ka uthane wala
Tan-e-zakhmi tera tiron pe raha, haye Husain

Ghore dauraye la'inon ne tere lashe par
Jaise tu Sibt-e-Payambar hi na tha, haye Husain
Jal gaye khaime chhini chadren saman luta
Baad tere hui hum par ye jafa, haye Husain

Jis jagah khaima-e-Zainab tha wahan se ab tak
Rat ko aati hai kanon mein sada, haye Husain
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Muhammad Wali, Syed Sajid Husain Zaidi)
