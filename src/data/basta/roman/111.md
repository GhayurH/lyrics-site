Salam

Salami jaan guzra hai ranj-o-gham khasan-e-Dawar ka
Nasim Amrohvi

Salami jaan guzra hai ranj o gham khasan-e-Khuda-e Dawar ka
Qaqqq Sibtain ka thas Zahra ka thas Haider ka thas Payambar ka
Sada shohrat rahe ga jadu o khalq o zor-e Haider ka
Qatar o sher o anghtar ka thas dar ka thas ruh ke par ka

Ali ki tegh ke dam se hua har markar fisal
Ahad ka thas badar ka thas safin ka thas Khundaq ka thas Khaibar ka
Ye panchon sure Ay dil thas Panjtan ki shan mein aaye
Qamar ka thas Shams ka thas Rahman ka thas Maryam-o-Hajra ka thas Kausar ka

Fadaye Shah ho kar Hurr ne thas kis kis ka Sharaf paya
Awis o Zaid ka amar ka salman ka Zahra ka
Nishan Mit kar wafadari mein kaisa naam nikla hai
Zehir o Muslim o waubhab o Habib o Hurr Safdar ka

Ghulam-e Panjtan ko dar nahin un panch chaizon ka
Ajal ka thas jaan ka ka thas qabr ka thas Barzakh ka thas mahshar ka
Mila hai rone walon ko isal-e-sawab ek aah ka kya kya
Salwat o Saum-o-salat ka Khums o zakat o Hajj-e Akbar ka

Siwaye tishnagi Shabbir ko ek Ek sadma tha
Jaan bhanjon ka thas bhai ka thas Akbar ka thas Asghar ka
Mila ke rone walon ko sawab ek aah ka kya kya
Sakina le gayi ye panch dagh is-e bagh-e alam se

Tamanchon ka thas rasan ka thas bap ka thas amun ka thas gauhar ka
Tarap ke kehti thi Banu thas karun kis kis ka mein matam
Jawan ka thas Tifl ka thas damdam ka thas dukhtar ka thas shauhar ka

Taha-e khanjar Imam-e Pak ko kis kis ka dhyan aaya
Bahen ka thas beti ka thas bimar ka thas ummat ka thas mahshar ka
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed Asghar Abbas Zaidi Farrukh Bahari, aur sozkhwan Aziz Zaidi o baradaran)

Hai salam us pe jo kehti thi Sayyida haye Husain
Raja Kanwar / Lucknow

Hai salam is pe jo kehti thi sada thas haye Husain
Zalimon ne tujhe pani na diya thas haye Husain
Ali Akbar tere samne Barchhi Khai
Qatl Asghar tere hathon pe hua thas haye Husain

Dukh pe dukh seh ke jise Fatima ne pala tha
Is pe be-kasi dinon ne ye zulm kya thas haye Husain
Na raha koi janaze ka uthane wala
Tan-e Zakhmi tera tiron pe raha thas haye Husain

Ghore dauraye lainon ne tere lashe par
Jaise to Sibt-e Payambar hi na tha thas haye Husain
Jal gaye khaime chhini Chadaren saman lita
Baad tere hui hum par ye jafa thas haye Husain

Jis jagah khamiy Zainab tha wahan se ab tak
Raat ko aati hai kanon mein sada thas haye Husain
Khajal gaye jaise chitthi chadrein saman luta
