Jab lut ke Karbala se asir-e-sitam chale

1
Jab lut ke Karbala se asir-e-sitam chale
Sajjad sar barahna ba-dard-o-alam chale
Rote saron ko pitte paband-e-gham chale
Zainab ne lash-e-Shah se kaha bhai hum chale

Marne se aap ke main ye iza uthati hun
Darbar mein Yazid ke sar nange jati hun

2
Hai hai mere musafir-e-Karb-o-Bala Husain
Hai hai mere gharib mere mah-liqa Husain
Hai hai tujhe na pani ka qatra mila Husain
Hai hai tamam tan tera tukre hua Husain

Pyase gale pe khanjar-e-bedad chal gaya
Hai hai tarap tarap ke tera dam nikal gaya

3
Le Nainawa Ali ki bidaat tujhe mili
Ay Karbala Khuda ki amanat tujhe mili
Ay khak meri man ki riyazat tujhe mili
Le ay zamin shama-e-imamat tujhe mili

Daman tera bhara meri kheti ujar gayi
Sarhad mein teri bhai se Zainab bichhar gayi

4
Ye keh ke sar ko pit ke roi wo dil-jali
Aa kar Najaf se hal mera dekho Ya Ali
Gardan rasan mein aap ki beti ke hai bandhi
Kehte ye Mariya se wo ba-chashm-e-tar chali

Hai hai main Karbala-e-Mualla mein lut gayi
Pardes mein, main aa ke baradar se chhut gayi

5
Mehman bula ke hum se wafa ki lainon ne
Kya kya na hum pe jaur-o-jafa ki lainon ne
Khinchi bhi zara na sharm-o-haya ki lainon ne
Gardan jafa se shahr ki Khuda ki lainon ne

Khaime jala ke Ahl-e-Sitam shad ho gaye
Karbala mein Aal ke barbad ho gaye
