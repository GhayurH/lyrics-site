Jo Karbala mein hui Shah par jafa suniye

1
Jo Karbala mein hui Shah par jafa suniye
Yaqat-e Asr ， Shah-e din ka majra suniye
Bayan-e himmat o sabr-e Shah-e hada suniye
Wo aati hai mulk almato ki sada suniye

Mila jo hukm ke sar se nikal ， jaan-e Husain
Kaha ke sakht hai Ya Rab ， ye imtihan-e Husain

2
Laga hai zakhm-e tabar bahah raha hai sar se lahu
Bhare hain khun mein jaan-e Rasul-e ke gesu
Gharib ja ke rakhun dil par kis tarah qabu
Ke is-e lahu mein hai Fatima ke dudh ki bu

Nida aai ke ankhen to dal ， ankhon mein
Kaha bahen ka hai is-e dam khayal ， ankhon mein

3
Nida ye aai ke gardan se khinch jaan un-e ki
Kaha main kya karun gardan pe chal rahi hai chhuri
Phir yai pakar ke ye chillati hai koi Ali ki
Na zabh kar mere bachche ko mein dua dun lungi

[Ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Jahan rawan tere khanjar ki aab hai zalim

4
Baba ujar raha tha mera ghar, main pas thi
Rota tha mujh pe mera muqaddar, main pas thi
Jab bap se chhuta Ali Akbar, main pas thi
Hansta tha tir kha ke jab Asghar, main pas thi

Dekha Huzur, mujh pe jo alam guzar gaya
Baba main chup rahi, mera Abbas mar gaya

5
Baba asir hai mera kunba, main kya karun
Bachchon pe barhti jati hai iza, main kya karun
Ye bosa gah-e risalat aab hai bilak
Baba main kya karun, mere Baba, main kya karun

Kijiye dua ke sabr mujhe Kirdigar de
Malik ye karb ruh ki manzil guzar de
