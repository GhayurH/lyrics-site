Jo Karbala mein hui Shah par jafa suniye

1
Jo Karbala mein hui Shah par jafa suniye
Ba-waqt-e-asr, Shah-e-din ka majra suniye
Bayan-e-himmat-o-sabr-e-Shah-e-Huda suniye
Wo aati hai Malak-ul-Maut ki sada suniye

Mila jo hukm ke sar se nikal, jaan-e-Husain
Kaha ke sakht hai Ya Rab, ye imtihan-e-Husain

2
Laga hai zakhm-e-tabar, beh raha hai sar se lahu
Bhare hain khun mein jaan-e-Rasul ke gesu
Gharib ja ke rakhun dil par kis tarah qabu
Ke is lahu mein hai Fatima ke dudh ki bu

Nida ye aai ke ankhen to dal, ankhon mein
Kaha bahen ka hai is dam khayal, ankhon mein

3
Nida ye aai ke gardan se khinch jaan in ki
Kaha main kya karun gardan pe chal rahi hai chhuri
Phir ye pakar ke ye chillati hai koi Ali ki
Na zabh kar mere bachche ko main dua dungi

Jahan rawan tere khanjar ki aab hai zalim
Ye bosa gah-e-risalat maab hai zalim
