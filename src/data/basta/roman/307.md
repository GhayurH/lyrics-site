[Hashr barpa hai Madine mein qayamat hai qarib — jari]

3
Madar-e-Jafar-e-Sadiq thin bikhre hue bal
Bain karti thin, tarap kar Haram-e-nek-khisal
Bibiyan deti thin har su dam-e-ranj-o-malal
Rote the wo jinhein Ashura ka malum tha hal

Pisar jo dete wo maqtal mein pyase sote the
Tifl tak siliyon ke dar se nahin rote the

4
Dafn kar ke jo Baqi se phire Ahl-e-Aza
Marqad-e-Hazrat Baqir se utha shor-e-buka
Ro ke ye keh ke gaye mil ke Shabbir-o-bala
Mere Asghar ke bhatije teri gardan pe fida

Asar-e-zulm gale se ye ayan hai ab tak
Rasan-e-zulm se chhilne ka nishan hai ab tak

Qaul-e-Sadiq hai sunen dil se ghulaman-e-Husain

1
Qaul-e-Sadiq hai sunen dil se ghulaman-e-Husain
Jo bhi gham mein mere Dada ke kare shewan-o-shain
Ya rulane ko parhe hal-e-Imam-e-Kaunain
Ya ho surat se ye izhar ke dil hai be-chain

Ajr-e-irfan-e-imamat ba-yaqin bakhsenge
Mere Nana use Firdaus-e-Barin bakhsenge

2
Is riwayat ka muhibbon mein hua jab charcha
Ja baja hone lagi Majlis-e-Shabbir bapa
Apni kafshon ko jahan chhorte the Ahl-e-Aza
Baith jate the wahan chupke se aa kar Maula

Barha dekhe gaye bazm mein ghamkhwaron ki
Saf karte hue nalain, azadaron ki

3
In majalis ke jo Mansur ko pahunche akhbar
Jazba-e-qatl-e-Shah-e-Pak hua, phir bedar
Talab agar aap the ek hujre mein aur chand Ansar
Aag lagwa di wahan charon taraf se ek bar

Par gaya ghul, phir Sahib-e-Meraj chala
Haye ghar-e-Fatima teri bar aaj chala
