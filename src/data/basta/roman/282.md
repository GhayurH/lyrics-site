5
Zahra ne kaha haif hai Ay banda ghafar
Kaisa to bashar hai ke murawwat nahin zinhar
Hazrat ka ye alam hai tujhe ijlat-e didar
Tab yun wo pukara ke makan Hal gaya ek bar

Bola gaya dukhtar-e Mahbub-e Khuda se
Khud khatm-e Rasul Chaunk pare is ki sada se

6
Bole meri dildar, Khuda Hafiz-o-Nasir
Ay bap ki ghamkhwar Khuda Hafiz o Nasir
Ay dukhtar-e-nachar, Khuda Hafiz-o-Nasir
Rukhsat hai ye bimardar Khuda Hafiz o Nasir

Kya Sabir-o-Shakir hai Khuda tujh ko jaza de
Ay bap ki shaida use aane ki Raza de

7
Phir Shah ne kai bar kaha shukr Khudaya
Aakhir mulk almato ko nazdik bulaya
Qudsi ne jo Guldasta-e Firdaus sanghaya
Phir dukhtar-e nashad ne dam tan mein na paya

Sar pit ke chillayen ke faryad-e Khuda ki
Mein lut gayi lo mere Baba ne qaza ki

Ay anbiyo roo ke ye fasl-e-aza hai
(Bashukriya salam-o-nauha-o-sozkhwan Razi Rizvi)

Tanha yaad mein Nabi ki jo Zahra ka ghair hal

1
Tha yaad mein Nabi ki jo Zahra ka ghair-e hal
Ankhon mein uthte baithte ansu the dil nidhal
Haider sharik-e gham the aur iftaf-e khurd sal
Un ke siwa kisi ko na tha un ka kuchh khayal

Roti thin sar patak ke mazar-e Rasulwal par
Tutti thi ek qayamat-e Kubra Batul par

2
Nauha parha jo yaad mein Baba ki subh-o-sham
Laye Ali ke pas shikayat ye khas-o-aam
Roti hain raat din jo Batul-e-falak-maqam
Din-e bhar ke kam raat ki nindein huin haram

Kitne hi is se barh ke bhi maghmum hote hain
Marte hain sab ke bap, kahin yun bhi rote hain

3
Sun kar Madine walon ka ye dil-e shakn Payam
The Sabir o halim magar ro diye Imamam-e
Ja kar Haram sira mein sunaye jo ye kalam
Ek aah bhar ke reh gayin Bint-e-Zahra-e Shah-e anmam-e

Itna kaha Huzur kuchh un ke kabhi na the
Mere hi bap the wo, kisi ke Nabi na the
