Salam

Bhai ki tarah se tum bhi to Ahl-e-iman ka sahara ho Zainab
Gham-e-Ahl-e-Bait

Karbala ki manzilat sabit hai har Unwan se
Waidi se tawarit se anjil se Quran se
Habi-e Ahl-e-Bait hai Haq ko bahr-e surat pasand
be-khudi ki shan se ho Ya khudi ki shan se

madh-e Haider, matam-e Shabbir, ehsas-e Wala
Jine wale tujh ko marna hai bare saman se
Man ne phir ye bhi na socha dekh kar Akbar ki jang
Hum ne atharah baras pala hai kis arman se

Dast-e-sitam-e matam dar khud hi khol le ga bab-e Khuld
Hum Azadaron ka maruza nahin Rizwan se
Karbala walon ne duniya ko diye kya kya sabaq
Qaid khana ki faza tak, jang ke maidan se

Hurr janin maqtal mein laya tha ani par jaan di
Zist kis Unwan se thi maut kis Unwan se
(Bashukriya soz o salam o nauhakhwan Syed Abrar Husain o Zulfiqar Mukhtar)
