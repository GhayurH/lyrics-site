Bhai ki tarah se tum bhi to iman ka sahara ho Zainab
Gham-e-Ahl-e-Bait

Karbala ki manzilat sabit hai har unwan se
Ved se Taurat se Injil se Quran se
Hubb-e-Ahl-e-Bait hai Haq ko bahr-e-surat pasand
Be-khudi ki shan se ho ya khudi ki shan se

Madh-e-Haider, matam-e-Shabbir, ehsas-e-wila
Jine wale tujh ko marna hai bare saman se
Man ne phir ye bhi na socha dekh kar Akbar ki jang
Hum ne atharah baras pala hai kis arman se

Dast-e-matamdar khud hi khol lega Bab-e-Khuld
Hum azadaron ka maruza nahin Rizwan se
Karbala walon ne duniya ko diye kya kya sabaq
Qaid-khana ki faza tak, jang ke maidan se

Hurr jinhen maqtal mein laya tha unhi par jaan di
Zist kis unwan se thi maut kis unwan se
(Bashukriya soz o salam o nauhakhwan Syed Abrar Husain o Zulfiqar Mukhtar)

Phenkan tha Zulfiqar ka sadqa utar ke

Khaibar ke dar ne khul ke ishara ye kar diya
Manzar yahi hain quwwat-e-Parwardigar ke
Rubah-e-jang Aun-o-Muhammad pe kehte the
Ye sher jane phut gaye kis kachhar ke

Laila ke dil ko dekh rahe hain Shah-e-Zaman
Akbar ko ran mein bheja hai gesu sanwar ke
Asghar jigar ko tham ke roti hai fauj-e-Sham
Tum tir kha ke aaye ho ya tir mar ke

Akbar tumhara bagh-e-jawani ujar gaya
Laila ne char din bhi na dekhe bahar ke
