[Tegh ka war jo Abbas ke shane pe laga — jari]

4
Garte hi khak par Ghazi ne kaha aao ne
Amdalim lakht-e dil-e aqda kusha aao koi
Amdalim Ay pisar-e sher-e Khuda aao koi
Ho karam mujh pe Ay Shah-e hada aao koi

Khaliq-e kul ka ye ehsan hua hai Aqa
Ye ghulam aap pe qurban hua hai Aqa

5
Husain ke Abbas ki awaz chale Shah-e-Huda
rah mein dast-e-sitam burida jo mile un ko liya
Kabhi sine se lagaya, kabhi un ko chuma
aaye Abbas ke nazdik Imam-e dusra

Khak aur khun mein ghaltan nazar aaye Abbas
Phir bhi mutlaq na pareshan nazar aaye Abbas

6
Shah darya mein baithe gaye jalti hui reti par
Apne zanu pe rakha pyar se Abbas ka sar
Ankh se khinch ke ek tir ko bole Sarwar
Bhai ko chhor ke tum bandhte ho rukhsat safar

Aaj pardes mein taqdir hamein lut gayi
Zor-e-bazu ka gaya aur kamar tut gayi

jab zanwaye Husain pe Abbas mar gaye

1
jab zanwaye Husain pe Abbas mar gaye
Sadme ghazab ke Sibt-e Nabi par guzar gaye
Khanjar-e alam ke dil se jigar tak utar gaye
Chillate the ke sher hamare kidhar gaye

wa hasrata ke bikis o be yar ho gaye
Sar kaise petin hath to be-kar ho gaye

2
Akbar ne ro ke arz ye ki ay Shah-e-Zaman
Rone se ab malin hue na Hazrat ke bhai jaan
Le chale ghar mein lash-e Alamdar-e najwanon
Aisa na ho nikal parin khaime se hi bayan

Darya pe nange sar kahin Bint-e-Zahra-e Ali na aaye
Bachchon ke sath le ke Sakina chali na aaye

3
Chadar orh ke Wan se Imam ammam chale
Farmaya lo Karim nigahban hum chale
Akbar to aage le ke wo par khun alam chale
Sar nange Syed-e aali bahm chale

Jangal mein shor-e-nala-o-faryad-o-aah tha
Hazrat ke pichhe apsa-e Alamdar-e Shah tha
