4
Girte hi khak par Ghazi ne kaha aao kani
Al-madad lakht-e-dil-e-Uqda-kusha aao kani
Al-madad ay pisar-e-Sher-e-Khuda aao kani
Ho karam mujh pe ay Shah-e-Huda aao kani

Khaliq-e-kul ka ye ehsan hua hai Aqa
Ye ghulam aap pe qurban hua hai Aqa

5
Sun ke Abbas ki awaz chale Shah-e-Huda
Rah mein dast-e-burida jo mile un ko liya
Kabhi sine se lagaya kabhi un ko chuma
Aaye Abbas ke nazdik Imam-e-do-sara

Khak aur khun mein ghaltan nazar aaye Abbas
Phir bhi mutlaq na pareshan nazar aaye Abbas

6
Shah-e-din baith gaye jalti hui reti par
Apne zanu pe rakha pyar se Abbas ka sar
Ankh se khinch ke ek tir ko bole Sarwar
Bhai ko chhor ke tum bandhte ho rakht-e-safar

Aaj pardes mein taqdir hamein lut gayi
Zor-e-bazu ka ghata aur kamar tut gayi

Jab zanu-e-Husain pe Abbas mar gaye

1
Jab zanu-e-Husain pe Abbas mar gaye
Sadme ghazab ke Sibt-e-Nabi par guzar gaye
Khanjar-e-alam ke dil se jigar tak utar gaye
Chillate the ke sher hamare kidhar gaye

Wa hasrata ke be-kas-o-be-yar ho gaye
Sar kaise piten hath to be-kar ho gaye

2
Akbar ne ro ke arz ye ki ay Shah-e-Zaman
Rone se ab milenge na Hazrat ke bhai jaan
Le chale ghar mein lash-e-Alamdar-e-naujawan
Aisa na ho nikal paren khaime se hi bayan

Darya pe nange sar kahin Bint-e-Ali na aaye
Bachchon ko sath le ke Sakina chali na aaye

3
Chadar orh ke wan se Imam-e-Umam chale
Farmaya lo Karim nigahban hum chale
Akbar to aage le ke wo pur-khun alam chale
Sar nange Syed-e-aali baham chale

Jangal mein shor-e-nala-o-faryad-o-aah tha
Hazrat ke pichhe asp-e-Alamdar-e-Shah tha
