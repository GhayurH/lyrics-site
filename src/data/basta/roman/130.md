[Safha 129 se jari]

Utho lahad se bahut raat ho gayi Asghar
Wo dekho khaime se man ne tumhein pukara hai
Sitam ke tir se chhedne wale
Ye tir to ne Sakina ke dil-e pe mara hai

Kis ki mayyat thi ke is rah guzar se guzre
Shah Naqvi

Kis ki himmat thi ke is-e rah-e guzar se guzre
Muskurate hue Shabbir jidhar-e se guzre
Maut qadmon ke nishan dhund rahi hai ab tak
Karbala wale Khuda jane kidhar se guzre
Bachchh gaya tha kahin Shabbir ka qissa shayad
Abhi rote hue kuchh log idhar-e se guzre
Dal dete rukh-e Asghar pe Aal-e-Aba Ay Shabbir
Zakhm-e gardan na kahin man ki nazar se guzre
Wohi kar sakta hai andana karb-e Akbar
Pyas mein Nakwan-e sinan jis ke jigar se guzre
Thi ye Sughra ki sada khat mera leti jaye
Karbala jate hue jo bhi idhar-e se guzre

Karbala o Sham o Kufa ja rahe hain aaj bhi
Roz-e Ashura jinhein Shabbir ne awaz di
(shair Ahl-e- bait Syed Sardar Husain Zaidi Marhum)

Chhorna nahin hum ko Karbala ka gham tanha
Shah Naqvi

Chhorte nahin hum ko Karbala ka gham tanha
warna chal nahin sakte hum to do qadam tanha
Shah ke azadaro, tum ko khauf-e mahshar kya
zamin-e shafaat hai ek ashk-e gham tanha
Phir koi gham-e duniya is ko qayamat nahin sakta
Bakhsh de jise Malik, Karbala ka gham tanha
Lut chuke Haram-e Shah ke, raat aai thi tar
Ab to chhor din unhein bani-e sitam tanha
Wo chale Haram-e Shah ke, nange sar suye Kufa
Aage aage unton ke ek asir-e gham tanha
Bandh de koi is-e mein Ek mashk-e chhoti si
Sona sona lagta hai panjj alam tanha
(Bashukriya salam khwan Ashraf Abbas o Imtiaz Raza Zaidi)

khak jo Marhab-o-Antar o Antar ko chata dete hain
Maulana Professor Mirza Akhtar Naqvi

Khak jo Marhab-o-Antar o Antar ko chancha dete hain
Sher ka jaam bhi qatil ko pila dete hain
hum sna karte hain, jin ki, hain Karim Ibn-e Karim
Haq pe aate hi ghar apna lita dete hain
Izzat o shohrat o Iqbal o daulat
Kya kahun apne ghulamon ko wo kya dete hain
Jis ko hota nahin kuchh hota-e Tathir ka pas
Hum use Majlis-e Sarwar se utha dete hain
