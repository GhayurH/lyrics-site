Jab dilbar-e-Zahra ki shahadat ka din aaya

1
Jab dilbar-e-Zahra ki shahadat ka din aaya
Pardes mein mehman ko lainon ne sataya
Ansar-o-bhai se bheje se phiraya
Mazlum ne sabr-e-Asadullah dikhaya

Saqqe ko tarai mein sisakte hue dekha
Matam mein Sakina ko bilakte hue dekha

2
Akbar bhi chale ran ko dil-e-Shah-e-Huda bhi
Shabbir bhi dekha, wo mah-e-liqa bhi
Maidan mein nare kiye aur dua bhi
Har zakhm pe ki bakhshish-e-ummat ki dua bhi

Nagah utha shor ye aflak ke upar
Lo gir gayi taswir-e-Nabi khak ke upar

3
Sine pe sinan kha ke gira Bano ka pyara
Khaime ki taraf dekh ke Baba ko pukara
Ya Shah-e-Huda aakhri mujra ho hamara
Ab azim-e-Firdaus hai mushtaq-e-nazara

Go neze ka phal rishta-e-jan kat chuka hai
Lekin paye didar dam ankhon mein ruka hai

4
Ay Syed-e-Abrar, Khuda Hafiz-o-Nasir
Ghabra ke kabhi man ki taraf murte the Sarwar
Tharra ke sambhalte the kabhi khate the thokar
Karte the kabhi rah mein daman se ankh tar

Rukh zard tha aur gesuon mein khak bhari thi
Ghamnak latakta tha qaba dhalki hui thi

5
Maqtal mein har ek lash pe jhuk jate the Sarwar
Dhundon ke kehte the ye mera nahin dilbar
Nagah mile khak pe ghash mein Ali Akbar
Dil tham ke baithe ye shahane ko pukar kar

Dilgir Rasul-us-Saqalain aaya hai beta
Tazim ko utho ke Husain aaya hai beta
