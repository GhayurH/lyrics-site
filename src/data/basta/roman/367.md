La Fata Ali / Pir-e-Tariqat Hazrat Badi Shah Warsi

[Ibtidai misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh] La Fata Ali
Rutba Allah kyun na ho Sher-e-Khuda Ali
Mominon ko mubarak Ali gaye (jab Khuda ko pukara Ali gaye)
Maut tere muhibbon ko aati nahin
Aa bhi jaat to phir bach ke jati nahin
Maut ka kya khatar ' maut se kya hai dar
Is se bachna bhi kya gayi gayi
Ghusl mayyat na kehna mere ghusl ko
Ajal mublub ko mat kafan naam do
Mein chala hun Ali se mulaqat ko

Jis ki thi arzu wo ghari gayi

Jis ka Maula tha mein is ka Maula Ali
Jab Nabi ne kaha din kamil hua
Marhaba Mustafa Rab bhi razi hua
Jabarabi aur qalandar par kya mashnar

Dar tera chhor kar koi jata hi nahin

Jo ghulami ka teri maza pa ghair
Ras jis ko teri naukri gayi
Maslak-e ishq mein mangna aib hai
Mein bhi khamosh hun wo bhi khamosh hain
Un ko sab hai khabar is liye chup hun mein
Kya hua ankh mein Gar nammi gayi
Khums dena mawaddat ki ek shart hai
Aur na dena bhi misl-e inkar-e-Fadak hai gunah
To wasi-e o Wali o Ali hi raha
Jante hi na the nafrat o dushmani
Ye tabarra bhi Wallah kya chiz hai
Hum ko aghyar se dushmani gayi
Mujh mein kab ahliyat thi teri madh ki
Mere Maula mein qurban tere ishq ke
Sibt Jafar ko bhi shairi gayi
