La Fata Ali / Pir-e-Tariqat Hazrat Badi Shah Warsi

Maqsud-e-kul aap hi hain Shah-e-La Fata Ali
Dast-e-Allah kyun na ho Sher-e-Khuda Ali
Bandon ko un ke fikr-o-masaib ka khauf kya
Hallal-e-mushkilat hain Mushkil-kusha Ali
Banda hun main unhi ka unhi ka ghulam hun
Maula mere Imam mere Peshwa Ali
Jis tarah ek zat Muhammad hai be-misal
Paida hua na hoga koi dusra Ali
Yahi to panch hain maqsud-e-kainat
Khair-un-Nisa, Husain-o-Hasan, Mustafa, Ali

Rubai / Pir-e-Tariqat Hazrat Badi Shah Warsi

Dar maikada-e-ishq Sikandar hastam

Dar dair-e-kharabat qalandar hastam

Dam-ba-dam Ali Ali / Maulana Kausar Niazi

Ajz hai meri bartari, fakhr mera naukri
Faqr meri qalandari, sar pe hai dast-e-Haideri
Pir mera Ali Ali, dam-ba-dam Ali Ali
Allah-o-Mustafa tere, donon jahan gada tere
Kaun-o-makan sada tere, qalb-o-jigar fida tere
Bagh-e-jinan teri gali, dam-ba-dam Ali Ali
Azmat-e-husn-e-Kibriya, ulfat-e-din-e-Mustafa
Dawat-e-fikr-e-ambiya, baiat-e-dast-e-auliya
Teri hi zat se chali, dam-ba-dam Ali Ali
Quwwat-e-bazu-e-Nabi, azmat-e-nau-e-adami
Satwat-e-ilm-e-aali, hashmat-e-hilm-e-Hashimi
Aap ka wasf Ya Ali, dam-ba-dam Ali Ali
Kausar khatm-e-gam ka, aap ke is ghulam ka
Dil jo hua hai gham-zada, aap ko yaad kar liya
Sar se har ek bala tali, dam-ba-dam Ali Ali
