La Fata Ali / Pir-e-Tariqat Hazrat Badi Shah Warsi

---***---

[Ibtidai misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh] La Fata Ali
Rutba Allah kyun na ho Sher-e-Khuda Ali
Mominon ko mubarak Ali aa gaye (Jab Khuda ko pukara Ali aa gaye)
Maut tere muhibbon ko aati nahin
Aa bhi jaye to phir bach ke jati nahin
Maut ka kya khatar ' maut se kya hai dar
Is se bachna bhi kya aa gayi aa gayi
Ghusl-e-mayyat na kehna mere ghusl ko
Ajal malbus ko mat kafan naam do
Main chala hun Ali se mulaqat ko

Jis ki thi arzu wo ghari aa gayi

Jis ka Maula tha main us ka Maula Ali
Jab Nabi ne kaha din kamil hua
Marhaba Mustafa Rab bhi razi hua
Jibrail aur Qanbar par kya munhasir

Dar tera chhor kar koi jata hi nahin

Jo ghulami ka teri maza pa gaya
Ras jis ko teri naukri aa gayi
Maslak-e-ishq mein mangna aib hai
Main bhi khamosh hun wo bhi khamosh hain
Un ko sab hai khabar is liye chup hun main
Kya hua ankh mein gar nami aa gayi
Khums dena mawaddat ki ek shart hai
Aur na dena bhi misl-e-Fadak hai gunah
Tu Wasi-o-Wali-o-Ali hi raha
Jante hi na the nafrat-o-dushmani
Ye tabarra bhi Wallah kya chiz hai
Hum ko aghyar se dushmani aa gayi
Mujh mein kab ahliyat thi teri madh ki
Mere Maula main qurban tere ishq ke
Sibt Jafar ko bhi shairi aa gayi
