[Ghar se jab bahr-e-safar Syed-e-Alam nikle — jari]

6
Shah pukare ke Khuda Hafiz o Nasir Amman
Aap se hota hai rukhsat ye musafir Amman
Muddat-e Zist koi dam mein hai aakhir Amman
Ab nahin aas ke aana ho yahan phir Amman

Samjhe the hum lahad mein Ek hi ja hun hue
Kya qayamat hai ke qabren bhi juda hun hue

7
Ye bayan kar ke chale wan se Shah-e-kaun-o-makan
be-kasi chehre pe chhai thi to ansu the rawan
Log pit ke is dam hue yun girya kanna
Pichhe tabut ke jis tarah se ho shor-e fughan

Ghul tha Ay Syed-e-zi-jah, Khuda ko saunpa
Ay Madine ke shhanhasha-e, Khuda ko saunpa

Jati hai bahar aaj Madine ke chaman se

1
Jati hai bahar aaj Madine ke chaman se
Hai bughz-e lainon ko shhanhasha-e Shah-e-Zaman se
Tang aaye hain Shabbir ab aada ke chalan se
Karte hain bibiyan ki taraf koch-e watan se

Farmate hain ye manzil-e aakhir ka safar hai
Mafdadin musafir hun kahan, kis ko khabar hai

2
Ab mein reh-e Mabud mein ghar chhor raha hun
Aram gaye-e khair-e bashar chhor raha hun
Sughra ko bhi badi-e tar chhor raha hun
Har chand hai bimar magar chhor raha hun

Ahbab ki furqat ka qalaq sath hai mere
Lekin ye masarrat hai ke Haq sath hai mere

3
Farmaya ye rukh kar ke suye qabr-e Payambar
Nana mujhe rone se phira te hain sitamgar
Reh reh ke yahi sochta hun badal-e muztar
Aisa na ho kat jaye Madine mein mera sar

Khun mera Madine mein jo bahah jaye ga Nana
Is-e shahr ki tauqir mein farq-e aaye ga Nana
