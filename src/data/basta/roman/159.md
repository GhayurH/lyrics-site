Jis ghari nahr pe khaime Shah-e-wala ke hue

1
Jis ghari nehr pe khaime Shah-e wala ke hue
Aur sitamgar mazham-e lab-e darya ke hue
Shah-e barham ye jafakaron se Farmaya ke hue
Dushman-e-jan mere ghar se mujhe bulwaye hue

Tum poyo pani thas Muhammad ka nawasa mar jaye
Tum hi Munsif ho thas jo mehman ho wo Pyasa mar jaye

2
Mein na aata tha thas Madine se bulaya tum ne
Rauza-e Ahmad-e Mursal ko chhuraya tum ne
Koh o sahra bhi ye darya bhi dikhaya tum ne
Haif hai tarik-e-duniya ko sataya tum ne

Rukh idhar-e ko jo kya mein ne to munh morte ho
Ek dagha pashaish ke duaiya pe mujhe chhorte ho
