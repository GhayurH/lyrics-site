Jis dam Hasan ka zahr se tukre jigar hua

1
Jis dam Hasan ka zahr se tukre jigar hua
Sipara-e dil wo aahon se zeri o zabr hua
Sam ka jo ruh-e Pak pe zahir asar hua
Hal is Imam-e Pak ka ek dam digar hua

Rahat mein farq-e aur shakl mein khalal para
Kat kar kaleja tasht mein andar nikal para

2
Farmate the Hasan ke bulao Husain ko
Tha dam ba dam ye hukm ke lao Husain ko
Bhai ka hal-e zar sanau Husain ko
Tukre mere jigar ke dikhao Husain ko

Keh do ke jald aaye rehlat ka waqt hai
Sun jaye kuchh ke wasiyat ka waqt hai

3
Aaye Husain itne mein bashim-e ashk bar
Chillate the ke aap ki ghurbat ke mein nisar
Shabbir ko chum ke kehte the bar bar
Mein tum pe sadqe Ay mere Nana ki yadgar

Mujh se zyada zulm o sitam tum pe hun hue
Hum qabr mein tumhari musibat pe roin hue

4
Barpa tha ahl-e-matam-e bait-e Payambar mein shor o shain
Bahnein pahurhain khati thin kar ke bain
Sine pe hath mar ke chillate the Husain
Ab uth gaya zamane se hum be-kasi kasun ka chain

Bazu hamara tut gaya wa musibata
Bap aaj hum se chhut gaya wa musibata

5
Bhaiya tumhari god mein nikle ga mera dam
Tum doge hum ko ghusl-o-kafan jab marenge hum
Ho ga tumhare pas na koi bajuz alam
Qatil sirhane hue ga Ya khanjar-e sitam

Shabbir to baad-e marg bhi rahat se suye ga
Lash-e tumhara Ghoron se pamal hue ga

Bashukriya sozkhwan Jawad Husain o Muhammad Ali o Haider Ali Zaidi

Qaisar Barahvi
Ehsas-e-gham-o-dard mein dhal jata hai
Insaf ka waraq ruh par charh jata hai
Jab kehta hai ghurbat ki kahani koi
Muslim ke yatimon ka khayal aata hai
