Salam

Zard chehra hai zaif-o-zar hun
Mir Anees

Zard chehra hai nahif-o-zar hun
Matam-e-Sajjad mein bimar hun
Kehti thi Zainab duhai Ya Ali
Sar barahna main sar-e-bazar hun

Kehte the Abid uthe kyun kar qadam
Ay sitamgaro, nahif-o-zar hun
Dam-ba-dam khincho na mere hath ko
Paon barh sakte nahin nachar hun

Main piyada tum ho ghoron par sawar
Kis tarah daurun bahut bimar hun
[Aakhri do misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Faizan Mahmud o Anees Zaidi)

Baghaur sun le zamana, Husain aise the
Shahir Lakhnavi

Baghaur sun le zamana, Husain aise the
Baqa, fana ko banaya, Husain aise the
Phuri ke niche wo Khaliq se pyar ki baten
Ajal ko ho gaya sakta, Husain aise the

Mukhalafat pe zamana tha us taraf lekin
Wahi kiya jo kaha tha, Husain aise the
Sipah-e-Sham shujaat ka loha man gayi

---***---

Dabe na lakhon se tanha, Husain aise the

Kamar ko bandh ke piri mein jalti reti se
[Aakhri misra ghair wazeh]

Uthaya bete ka lasha, Husain aise the
Jawan ki lash uthai, banai qabr-e-saghir
Kahin bhi azm na badla, Husain aise the
Hazar shukr ke sajde shahid karta hai
Bataya qatre ko darya, Husain aise the
(Bashukriya Musawwir o sozkhwan Mukhtar Haider Zaidi)

---***---

Majlison se kuchh malak shishon mein bhar kar le gaye
Haiyat Lakhnavi

Majlison se kuchh malak shishon mein bhar kar le gaye
Le gaye ansu Khuda jane ke gauhar le gaye
Hum isi dar ke gada hain hum isi dar ke faqir
Bhik jis dar se farishte aa ke aksar le gaye

Der se pahunche dar-e-Shah-e-Najaf par hum faqir
Ek uhda tha ghulami wo bhi Qanbar le gaye
Jo na le jana tha wo bhi sab sitamgar le gaye
Shah ka malbus-e-kuhan Zainab ki chadar le gaye

Dopehar mein ek tan par zakhm khaye sainkron
Ek dil par dagh, Shah-e-din bahattar le gaye
Bugz kya tha ashqiya ko Fatima ke lal se
Kuchh nahin to jholiyon mein bhar ke patthar le gaye

Sang-dil the kya unhein dard-e-yatimi ka qalaq
Kan se bali Sakina ke jo gauhar le gaye
Safha-e-qirtas par chalte larazta hai qalam
Abid-e-bimar ko ta Sham kyun kar le gaye
