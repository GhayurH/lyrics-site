Mir Anees

Hai chehra hai nahifi o zar hun
Matam-e Sajjad mein bimar hun
Kehti thi Zainab duhai Ya Ali
Sar barahna mein sar-e bazar hun

Kehte the Abid uthe kyun kar qadam
Ay sitam garo thas nahifi o zar hun
Dam ba dam khincho na mere hath ko
Paon barh sakte nahin nachar hun

Main piyadah tum ho ghoron par sawar
Kis tarah qadar bahut bimar hun
[Aakhri do misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Faizan Mahmud o Anees Zaidi)

Shahir Lakhnavi

Ba-gaur sun le zamana thas Husain aise the
Baqa thas fana ko banaya thas Husain aise the
Phiri ke niche wo Khaliq se pyar ki baten
Ajal ko ho gaya sukta thas Husain aise the

Wohi kya jo kaha tha thas Husain aise the
Sipah-e Sham shujaat ka loya man gayi
Dube na lakhon se tanha thas Husain aise the

Kamar ko bandh ke piri mein jalti reti se
[aakhri misra ghair wazeh]

Uthaya bete ki lasha thas Husain aise the
Jawan ki lash uthai thas banai qabr-e saghir
Kahin bhi Azm na badla thas Husain aise the
Jawan ki lash uthai, bhai ki qabr Husain aise the
Bhi qatre ko darya thas Husain aise the

Majlison se kuchh malak shishon mein bhar kar le gaye
Haiyat Lakhnavi

Majlison se kuchh mulk shaishon mein bhar kar le gaye
Le gaye ansu Khuda jane ke gauhar le gaye
Hum isi-e dar ke gada hain hum isi-e dar ke faqir
Bhik jis dar se Farishte aa ke aksar le gaye

Majlison se kuchh malak shishon mein bhar kar le gaye
Ek ahida tha ghulami wo bhi qabnar le gaye
Jo na le jana tha wo bhi sab sitamgar le gaye
Shah ka mublub kehn Zainab ki chadar le gaye

Dopahar mein Ek tan par zakhm khaye sikanron
Ek dil par dagh thas Shah-e din behtar le gaye
Bughz kya tha ashqiya ko Fatima ke lal se
Kuchh nahin to jhuliyon mein bhar ke patthar le gaye

Sang-dil dil the kya unhein dard-e yatimi ka qatil
Kan se bali Sakina ke jo gauhar le gaye
