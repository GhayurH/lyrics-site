Aaj maqtal mein ajab be-sar-o-saman hain Haram

1
Aaj maqtal mein ajab be-sar-o-saman hain Haram
Dil hain majruh khule sar hain pareshan hain Haram
Qatl-e-Shabbir se betab hain giryan hain Haram
Warison mein ab koi to hairan hain Haram

Zikr-e-mazlumi-e-Shah-e-Madani karte hain
Kabhi aahen to kabhi sina-zani karte hain

2
Ro ke farmati hain ye khwahar-e-Sultan-e-Anam
Utho Sajjad ke ab ho jata hai Sham
Jhutpata waqt hai kuchh der mein hone ko Sham
Ab na Qasim hain, na Abbas, na Akbar, na Imam

Dil pur-dard ye ek gham ki ghata chhai hai
Rat hone ko hai aur Sham-e-tanhai hai

3
Tarap ke kan mein pahunchi jo Sajjad ke Zainab ki sada
Khol kar ankh ye arz ba-sad aah-o-buka
Kya kahun aap se qabu mein nahin dil mera
Tap zyada bhi hai ghaflat bhi hai kuchh aaj siwa

Kaun mara gaya aur kaun juda hota hai
Mujh ko malum nahin hai ke ye kya hota hai
