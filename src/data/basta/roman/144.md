4
Phir su-e-Baqi rukh-e-mehr-e-nur phiraya
Ashk ankhon se bahne lage dil gham se bhar aaya
Mujra ke liye sar ba-adab apna jhukaya
Ro ro ke ye Khatun-e-Qiyamat ko sunaya

Amman tera ghar hota hai barbad khabar le
Sar pitti hai Zainab-e-nashad khabar le

5

Zainab jamal-e-ru-e-mashiyyat ka naam hai
Zainab jalal-e-sahib-e-ghairat ka naam hai
Zainab maal-e-kar-e-sadaqat ka naam hai

6

7
