[Jati hai bahar aaj Madine ke chaman se — jari]

4
Phir suye Baqi rukh-e-purnur pheraya
Ashk ankhon se bahe, lab ne jhukaya
Hujre ke liye sar adab apna jhukaya
Ro ro ke ye Khatun-e-Qiyamat ko sunaya

Amman tera ghar hota hai barbad khabar le
Sar pe pari hai Zainab-e-nashad khabar le

5
Aqa se tu ne tha bare naz se pala
Aada ne use aaj musibat mein hai dala
Hota hai juda mujh se tera munis-e-wala
Ab kaun karega tere madfan pe ujala
Jangal mein jo phir jaun khabar lene na aana
Khanjar ke tale god mein sar lene ko aana

6
Ghamkon ke phir awaz ye di ruh-e-Husain ko
Bhulai na bhula dena muhabbat ke chalan ko
Jangal se chala jaun jo main shahr, lekin ko
Aada se bacha lena zara aa ke bahen ko

Amman ne bahut chume hain Shabbir ke bazu
Bandhe na koi Zainab-e-dilgir ke bazu

7
Bas kar ye sukhan kanp gayi Zainab-e-muztar
Farmaya ye kya keh rahe hain Sibt-e-Payambar
Jurat hai kisi ki jo talwar kare mujh par
Abbas sa jari hai Zainab ka baradar

Shah bole bahen agar dua na honge
Is waqt tere sher tere pas na honge

8
Abbas guzar jayenge hath apne kata kar
Katwaye Ali Akbar pe honge zamin par
Chhir jayega barchhi ki ani se dil-e-Akbar
Ek tir se ho jayenge be-jan Ali Asghar

Khun-e-Aun o Muhammad ka bhi bah jayega ran mein
Main ek bachta rahunga bahen na guman mein
