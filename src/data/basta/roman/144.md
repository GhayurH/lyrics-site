[Jati hai bahar aaj Madine ke chaman se — jari]

4
Phir suye Baqi rukh-e mehr-e nur-e phiraya
Ashk ankhon se ne lage dil gham se bhar aaya
Hujre ke liye sar ba adab apna jhukaya
Ro ro ke ye Khatun-e qayamat ko sunaya

Amman tera-e ghar hota hai barbad khabar le
Sar pit-ti hai Zainab-e nashad khabar le

5
Zainab kamal-e se-e qudrat ka naam hai
Zainab khush-jamal-e roye mashiyyat ka naam hai
Zainab jalal-e Sahib-e ghairat ka naam hai
Zainab mal-e kar-e sadaqat ka naam hai

Zainab hai naam-e Azm-e risalat aab ka
Zainab hai naam-e dedi Turab ka

6
Zainab Aql-o-shuur-e din-e panahin ka naam hai
Zainab shaa-e nur-e Ilahi ka naam hai
Zainab Yazidiyat ki tabahi ka naam hai
Zainab Husainiyat ki baqa ka naam hai

Zainab hai naam azmat-e Aal-e Rasul ka
Zainab hai jazu thas din-e ke asl usul ka

7
Zainab dil-e Habib-e Ilahi ka chain hai
Zainab nazir-e Fateh-e badar o Shah-e-Hunain hai
Zainab Janab-e Fatima ki nur-e ein hai
Zainab sharik-e kar-e Imam-e Husain hai

Zainab Husainiyat ki mukammal katab hai
Zainab Yazidiyat ka madlil jawab hai

8
Zainab hai gulistan-e siyadat ki apne
Zainab hai Mustafa ki Din-o-shariat ki pasban
Zainab hai karwan-e hidayat ki pasban
Zainab hai har Nabi ki kar-e-nabuwwat ki pasban

Zainab ki walida hai risalat ke sath sath
Zainab rahe lungi zinda imamat ke sath sath
