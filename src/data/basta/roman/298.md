Zahr-e-dagha se ghair jo hal Hasan hua — jari

7
Nagah ahl-e-matam-e bait mein matam bapa hua
Zahra ka lal Rahi-e mulk-e baqa hua
Ghul par gaya Husain Hasan se juda hua
Zainab tarap ke kehti thin logon ye kya hua

Mayyat ke gird muztar-o-dilgir rote the
Qasim ko le ke god mein Shabbir rote the

8
Jis dam Hasan ko ro chuke namus-e Mustafa
Ghusl o kafan se faragh-e Shah-e hada
Le kar janaza khalf-e sher-e kabraya
Qabr-e Nabi ki samt chale khwish o Aqraba

Aurat ek aai lashkar-e afat nishan liye
Lashkri tha hath mein tir o kaman liye

9
Ay momino! asar ko batao paye Khuda
Ghar se nikal ke koi janaza bhi hai bhara
Lekin Hasan ke sath Madine mein jo hua
Rawi ne is tarah se tarikh mein Likha

Paiwasta is qadar the badan mein Hasan ke tir
Bhai bahen nikal rahe the badan ke tir

Zahr-e-dagha se ghair jo hal Hasan hua
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Masud Husain Rizvi o Javed Balgrami)

Kafan pehna ke jo Shabbar ko le chale Shabbir

1
Kafan pehna ke jo Shabbar ko le chale Shabbir
Rawan the sath Bani Hashim aur amir-o-faqir
Har Ek nauha kanna sab ka hal-e dil-e Tabibar-e
Hazaron ahl-e-matam-e wal-wast nala nala-kash hazin dilgir

Sar apna pitta har Ek nauha Gar hamrah
Jigar ke tukre liye tasht mein pisar hamrah

2
Irada tha ke qabr-e Nabi ke pas mazar
Hazar haif na razi hue magar ashrar
Lagaye tir Hasan ke janaze par ek bar
Kai khadang-e dar aaye kafan mein aakhir-e kar

Bula ke dasht mein ek roz aayen hue nawak
Wahan Husain ki mayyat uthain hue nawak

3
Kisi tarah na baz aaye jab jafa Banda-parwar
Kya Baqi mein mafdadin lash Shabbir ka
Hai ab ye arz Musalmanon se yaksar
Hai arzu ke shafaat karen Payambar

Tarah tarah ka sitam Aal par kya tum ne
Yahi ajr-e risalat jo kuchh diya tum ne
