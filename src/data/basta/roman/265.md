[Sham se jab Ahl-e-Bait gham-zada giryan chale — jari]

4
Hazrat Sajjad se Haram ke ye kalam
Pitte rote hue unton se utre tamam
Aaye jo lashon ke pas, haye wo nashad-kam
Sar ko liye pitne, warison ke liye naam

Chadaren sar se utar ansu bahane lage
Hathon se us ban ki khak sar par urane lage

5
Hazrat-e-Sajjad ne dekh kar ye majra
Bibiyon ko sath le, khub sa matam kiya
Sar jo shahidon ke the, bachche bhi un ko rakh
Sine-o-sar pit kar, ro ro ke nauha parha

Zahra ke masum ka aaj chaliswan hai
Syed-e-Mazlum ka aaj chaliswan hai

6
Wo jo Ibn-e-Hasan Shah ka aali-nasab
Biyah ke din haye haye, qatl hua tishna-lab
Shadi ka jora jo tha mil gaya mitti mein sab
Us ka bhi chehlum hai aaj haye sitam hai ghazab

Akbar-o-Asghar ka bhi aaj chaliswan hai
Shah ke lashkar ka bhi aaj chaliswan hai

Sham se jab Ahl-e-Bait gham-zada giryan chale

Pahunche asir-e-Sham se chhut kar jo Karbala

1
Pahunche asir-e-Sham se chhut kar jo Karbala
Taskhir ho chuka har ek sakht marhala
Utra jo bar sar se to yaad aaye aqraba
Ek ek marne wala nigahon mein phir gaya

Dil mein ghute hue jo arman ubal pare
Ashkon ke sath dil ke bhi tukre nikal pare

2
Koi tarap ke boli ke Akbar kahan ho tum
Chillai koi Qasim-e-be-par kahan ho tum
Ek man sadaen deti hai Asghar kahan ho tum
Zainab pukarin Abid-e-muztar kahan ho tum

Beta na dil pe bas hai na qabu hai sabr par
Lillah le chalo mujhe bhai ki qabr par

3
Boli lipat ke qabr se bhaiya main aa gayi
Bhaiya suna, main Sham talak be-rida gayi
Bhaiya Sakina hum se nigahen phira gayi
Nikli na qaid-khane se maut us ko kha gayi

Bachchi to ro chuki thi bahut thak ke so gayi
Lekin wo man ki god jo viran ho gayi
