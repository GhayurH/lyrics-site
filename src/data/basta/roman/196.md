Jab aa ke baith gaye sab wo Zahid-o-Abrar
Pukare Shah-e hada Gaur se sunen dindar
Mein is-e diyar se jaur-e falak se hun nachar
Phire hain Aal-e Habib-e Khuda se zulm nisar-e

Ye samtargim na par se munh ko morenge hue
Kisi tarah ye sitamgar na mujh ko chhorenge

5
Main un ke shar se aman paun, ghair mumkin hai
Watan mein aana pas, ghair mumkin hai
Yahan se paon bhi [lafz ghair wazeh], ghair mumkin hai
Main kal ko qatl se bach jaun, ghair mumkin hai

Khuda se sabr-o-raza ka hai khwastgar Husain
Bas ek raat ka mehman hai be-diyar Husain

6
Laga main kis ki ranj-o-alam ko rehne do
Faqat musafir-e-rah-e-adam ko rehne do
Tum rasida-o-paye-kar ko rehne do
Na tum raho, na Ali ke Haram ko rehne do

Hamare baad ye nachar dar-badar na phirein
Rasulzadiyan Kufe mein nange sar na phirein

Kitab mein hai shab-e-Ashur ka ye hal likha

1
Kitab mein hai shab-e-Ashur ka ye hal likha
Haram sira mein musalle pe the Imam-e hada
Kabhi tha shugh-e-tilawat tilawat kabhi ye Haq se dua
Ilahi sabr ki taqat ho mere dil ko ata

Ilahi Ahmad-e Mursal se Surkh ro rakhna
Is-e imtihanàn mein pyase ki aabru rakhna

2
Inhi duaon mein Guzri jo raat ek pahar
To aaye khamiy Ansar-e Pak par Sarwar
Shagaf-e dar se ye dekha ke jama hain saf dar
Habib kehte hain ye Bech mein khare ho kar

Ali ke dosto thas kul roz-e rastaris hai
Nabi ki Aal pe ye waqt jaan-nisari hai

3
Ye hal dekh ke shukr-e-Khuda bajalaye
Bahaduron ki tamanna pe tir-e-gham khaye
Wahan se khamiy hamshir ki taraf aaye
Bahen ka pyar jo dekha to gham se tharraye

Khud apne pyaron ko rukhsat kafan pehnaya hai
Abhi se rozan gulon ko kafan pinaya hai
