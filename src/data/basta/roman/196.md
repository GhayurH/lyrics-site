Jab aa ke baith gaye sab wo Zahid-o-Abrar
Pukare Shah-e-Huda ghaur se sunen dindar
Main is diyar se jaur-e-falak se hun nachar
Phire hain Aal-e-Habib-e-Khuda se zulm-shiar

Ye sangdil na jafaon se munh ko morenge
Kisi tarah ye sitamgar na mujh ko chhorenge

5
Main un ke shar se aman paun ghair mumkin hai
Watan mein aa ke pahunchaun ghair mumkin hai
Yahan se paun bhi [ghair wazeh] ghair mumkin hai
Main kal ko qatl se bach jaun ghair mumkin hai

Khuda se sabr-o-raza ka hai khwastgar Husain
Bas ek rat ka mehman hai be-diyar Husain

Kutub mein hai shab-e-Ashur ka ye hal likha

1
Kutub mein hai shab-e-Ashur ka ye hal likha
Haram-sara mein musalle pe the Imam-e-Huda
Kabhi tha shughl-e-tilawat kabhi ye Haq se dua
Ilahi sabr ki taqat ho mere dil ko ata

Ilahi Ahmad-e-Mursal se surkh-ru rakhna
Is imtihan mein pyase ki aabru rakhna

2
Inhi duaon mein guzri jo rat ek pahar
To aaye khaima-e-Ansar-e-Pak par Sarwar
Shigaf-e-dar se ye dekha ke jama hain saf-dar
Habib kehte hain ye bich mein khare ho kar

Ali ke dosto, kal roz-e-rastgari hai
Nabi ki Aal pe ye waqt-e-jan-nisari hai

3
Ye hal dekh ke shukr-e-Khuda baja laye
Bahaduron ki tamanna pe tir-e-gham khaye
Wahan se khaima-e-hamshir ki taraf aaye
Bahen ka pyar jo dekha to gham se tharraye

Khud apne pyaron ko rukhsat kafan pehnaya hai
Abhi se donon gulon ko kafan pehnaya hai
