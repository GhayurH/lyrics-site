Jabke paband-e-salasil hue bole Sajjad

1
Jabke paband-e-salasil hue bole Sajjad
Sabr wo de mujhe Ay Khaliq-e kal-kul Rab-e albada
Na katon paon jo kanton pe chillayen jallad
Taziyane maren na karun mein faryad

Ummat-e jad se imamat ka sila pana hai
Nange sar ko fafe mein man bahnon ko le jana hai

2
Keh ke ye tauq ko gardan mein sambhala ek bar
Aage aage chala pakre hue unton ki mahar
Pichhe Syedaniyan sar khole hue zar o qatar
Aage sawar Syed-e Mazlum ke neze pe sawar

Nakwan-e naziza se ye aati thi sadaye maghmum
haye be pardigi-e Zainab o am-e Kulsum

3
Ghair pahuncha ye qafila nazdaz-e darya
dekha ek sher hai be dast-e-sitam tarai mein para
Ro ke chillaye ke ammu teri ghurbat pe fida
Haye shane bhi kate aur na pani paya

Tazkira Ahl-e- wafa tera karen hue amun
Bawafa tum ko zamane mein kahin hue mein

4
Nagahan lash Sarwar ne sada di is dam
Sakht manzil hai tujhe sabr de Rab-e akram
Dhyan bibiyon-e ka rahe qaid mein beta har dam
Mere bimar tere sath hi chalte hain hum

Shimr-e zalim se Sakina ko bachana beta
Meri nadan ko sine se lagana beta

Jabke paband-e-salasil hue bole Sajjad
(Amir Rizvi Cholsi)
