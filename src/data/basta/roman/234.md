Jabke paband-e-salasil hue bole Sajjad

1
Jabke paband-e-salasil hue bole Sajjad
Sabr wo de mujhe ay Khaliq-e-kul, Rabb-ul-Ibad
Na rukin paon jo kanton pe chalayen jallad
Taziyane maren na karun main faryad

Ummat-e-jad se imamat ka sila pana hai
Nange sar Kufe mein man bahnon ko le jana hai

2
Keh ke ye tauq ko gardan mein sambhala ek bar
Aage aage chala pakre hue unton ki mahar
Pichhe Syedaniyan sar khole hue zar-o-qatar
Aage sar, Syed-e-Mazlum ke neze pe sawar

Nok-e-neza se ye aati thi sada-e-maghmum
Haye be-pardagi-e-Zainab-o-Umm-e-Kulsum

3
Algharz pahuncha ye qafila nazd-e-darya
Dekha ek sher hai be-dast tarai mein para
Ro ke chillaye ke ammu, teri ghurbat pe fida
Haye shane bhi kate aur na pani paya

Tazkira Ahl-e-Wafa tera karenge ammu
Bawafa tum ko zamane mein kahenge ammu

4
Nagahan lasha-e-Sarwar ne sada di us dam
Sakht manzil hai, tujhe sabr de Rabb-e-Akram
Dhyan bewaon ka rahe qaid mein beta har dam
Mere bimar tere sath hi chalte hain hum

Shimr-e-zalim se Sakina ko bachana beta
Meri nadan ko sine se lagana beta

Jabke paband-e-salasil hue bole Sajjad
(Amir Rizvi Cholsi)
