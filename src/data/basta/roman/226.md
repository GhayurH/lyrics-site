4
Wo be-kasi wo ghurbat-e-Aal-e-Aba ki rat
Aafat ka bun, ghazab ka andhera, bala ki rat
Pehli wo furqat-e-Shah-e-gulgun-qaba ki rat
Rone ki, pitne ki, alam ki, aza ki rat

Bachchon ki jaan-e-zar par sadme bare rahe
Jhulsi qanat mein shab bhar pare rahe

5
Muhlat jo zalimon ke sitam se mili zara
Bachchon ko dhundne ko chali Bint-e-Murtaza
Jab Dukhtar-e-Husain ka paya na kuchh pata
Darya ki samt mur ke pukari basad buka

Khwahar pe aur taza musibat guzar gayi
Abbas kuchh khabar hai, Sakina kidhar gayi

6
Ye keh ke qatl-gah mein sar ko buka gayi
Su-e-shahid Dukhtar-e-Khair-un-Nisa gayi
Kanon mein ek yatim ki awaz aa gayi
Dekha wo hal, kanp gayi, tharthara gayi

Ek bachchi ek lash pe uryan hui hai
Munh se na bolne ka gala kis ke ro rahi hai

7
Zainab qarib ja ke pukari ba-hal-e-zar
Hai hai, tu kis gharib ki bachchi hai, main nisar
Tutla ke wo yateem pukari ye bar bar
in ki hi nur-e-ain hun in ki gul'izaar

Ro kar ye keh rahi hun khaime ki kainat se
Baba mere bachao ko bula do Furat se

8
Sunta ye tha ke daur ke lipti ye sogwar
Ro kar kaha ke ay meri bachchi tere nisar
Sab ghar ko intizar hai, sab ghar ko intizar
Madar bhi be-qarar hai, khwahar bhi be-qarar

Mujh ko ye aas kab thi ke ab tum ko paungi
Lo waris-e-ghar chalo, main thik kar sulaungi

9
Hai hai, ye waqt aur ye andhi, ye intishar
Jangal ka bhi na khauf kiya, meri gul-izar
Be-sar pare hain dasht mein lashe ba-hal-e-zar
Kyun kar shanakht kar liya Baba ko, main nisar

Har uzw pamal, badan pash pash hai
Haan ho na ho ye Qasim-e-muztar ki lash hai
