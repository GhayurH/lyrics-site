[Jis dam suni Imam-e-Umam ne sada-e-Hurr — jari]

4
Zanu pe sar hai aap ke Ya Shah-e-bahr-o-bar
Mahbub-e-Kibriya hain idhar Sher-e-Haq idhar
Hain kaun ye muazzama, pahlu mein nauhagar
Shah ne kaha ke roti hain Amman barahna-sar

Tu hashr tak Imam-e-do-alam ke sath hai
Matam-e-Husain ke matam ke sath hai

5
Ye sun ke ghair ho gaya us dam jari ka hal
Zanu-e-Shah-e-din pe kiya Hurr ne intiqal
Khaime ke dar pe lash ko laya Ali ka lal
Sab bibiyon ne khol diye apne sar ke bal

Zainab yun roi Shah ke fidai ke waste
Jaise bahen tarapti hai bhai ke waste

[Makhaz: Jis dam suni Imam-e-Umam ne sada-e-Hurr]

Baqi jo rafiq-e-Shah-e-din reh gaye do char

1
Baqi jo rafiq-e-Shah-e-din reh gaye do char
Hasrat se unhein dekhte the Syed-e-Abrar
Ki barh ke Habib Ibn-e-Mazahir ne ye guftar
Ay pir-e-ghulam ab hai ijazat ka talabgar

Bande ko bhi marne ki raza dijiye Aqa
Firdaus ke raste pe laga dijiye Aqa

2
Shabbir ne sine se lagaya kai bari
Rukhsat jo kiya ankhon se ansu hue jari
Ghabra ke dar-e-khaima se Zainab ye pukari
Logo kaho maidan mein chali kis ki sawari

Ab kaun bachayega Shah-e-jinn-o-bashar ko
Sab chhore chale jate hain Zahra ke pisar ko

3
Ye sunte hi kohram hua Ahl-e-Haram mein
Pahuncha wo jari sher sa maidan-e-sitam mein
Kamil tha ze-bas ishq-e-Shahanshah-e-Umam mein
Be-tab tha Maula ki judai ke alam mein

Sina tha jari ka tabar-o-tir ki janib
Phir phir ke nazar karta tha Shabbir ki janib
