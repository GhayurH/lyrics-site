[Sar apna pit ke Fizza se Hind ne puchha — jari]

4
Ye zikr tha ke kanizon ka izdeham aaya
Hua ye Ghul sar-e Sultan-e tishna kam aaya
pukari Bint-e Ali-e maut ka Payam aaya
Bahen ka naam batane sar-e Imamam-e aaya

Kanizon ne sar-e Sultan-e marshaqin rakha
Huzur-e Hind ke La kar sar-e Husain rakha

5
Sar-e Husain jo aaya mahal mein misl-e mah-e
pukari pit ke, Hind Bint-e abd Allah
Lo aao Qaidiyo dekho ye kis ka sar hai aah
Sada ye Ijaz se be-hal tabah

[Ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Ye sar hai bhai ka Zainab mein hun batao bahen

6
Sada ye sunte hi uthi Batul ki jai
Qarib Hind ke aa ke ye baat farmai
Husain mar gaye Zainab hai qaid mein aai
Chhupaun kya ke batate hain khud mujhe bhai

Le li meri-e Fatima ke nur-e ein ka Pursa
Mein tujh ko deti hun bhai Husain ka Pursa

Qaid-khane se jo nazdik tha hakim ka mahal

1
Qaid-khane se jo nazdik tha hakim ka mahal
Sun ke awaz-e buka Hind ko aati thi na kul
Sharm ankhon mein diya aur na laga kajal
Kya hansi aaye bhala ho jab dil ka kanwal

Kabhi raton ko na aram se wo soti thi
Khwab se Chaunk ke uth baithti thi roti thi

2
Hakim-e Sham se ijazat le kar
Qaid-khane mein gayi Hind ba-hal-e-muztar
Hal gaya dil jo pari Qaidiyon par is ki nazar
Dekha sab bibiyon ko khak basar barahna sar

Ye to halat hai magar shukr Khuda karte hain
Na shikayat hai kisi ki na gila karte hain

3
Ek taraf khak pe leta hai koi azari
Maraz-e Tab ki wo shiddat ke ghash hai tarai
Paon sote hue jin se lahu hai jari
Hai ye zahir ke bas ab koch ki hai tayyari
