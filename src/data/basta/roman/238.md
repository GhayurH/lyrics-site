Diyar-e-Kufa mein jab Aal-e-Mustafa aai

1
Diyar-e-Kufa mein jab Aal-e-Mustafa aai
To ek khalq tamashe ko ja baja aai
Rasul-e Pak ki Itrat jo be-kasi rida aai
Sadaye girya Zahra o Murtaza aai

Naya sitam tha ke rone ko ji rasta tha
Nabi ki Aal par patthar ka menh barasta tha

2
Haram ne pesh-e darbar ki khabar jo suni
Bula ke Shimr-e lain ko pukarin Bint-e-Zahra-e Ali
Suna hai Sat so kursi nashin wahan hain shaqi
Hamein rida to arha de ke hum hain Aal-e Nabi

Pukara hans ke samtargim ye ho nahin sakta
Tumhein nasib ho chadar, ye ho nahin sakta

3
Ye sun ke ahl-e-matam-e Haram dil par dagh kha ke chale
Isi tarah se wo nachar sar jhuka ke chale
Hujm-e amim mein dukh seh ke gham utha ke chale
Lain ki bazm mein munh chhupa ke chale

Dar-e Amir par kya kya na shor o shar dekha
Likha hai Wan sar-e Muslim lahu mein tar dekha

4
Wo farq-e dekh ke Boli ye Zainab-e nachar
Mere gharib baradar mein tere sar ke nisar
Magar Husain ke sar se huin ankhen char
Qarib aa ke the Syed-e abrarar

Nida ye aai ke Ya Shah Assalam Alaik
Ali o Fatima ke mah-e Assalam Alaik

5
Pass-e fana bhi un-e afat mein ghira Maula
Ke bam se be-kasi tan-e be-kasi sar harah Maula
Bandha tha paon mein rassi ka ek harah Maula
Tamam shahr mein lashe khincha phir shahr Maula

Khabar dagha ki Imam-e ghori tak na gayi
Huzur ki meri arzi Huzur tak na gayi

6
Main likh chuka tha ke Shabbir ki samt jayen Huzur
Khuda ke waste tashrif yan na layen Huzur
Sipah-e-zulm se dhoka kahin na khayen Huzur
Dagha mein taq hain, koi idhar na aayen Huzur

Likha tha ye bhi ke bachche buka mein par gaye hain
Jo sath aaye the donon yahan bichhar gaye hain
