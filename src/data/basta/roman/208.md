Jab Mariya ke dasht mein warid hua wo lashkar — jari

7
Ay bhai karun kya mujhe khaime ka nigahban
Zainab ko to jana hai abhi ba-sar-e-uryan
Ahl-e-Haram-e-Shah ko hai dekhna zindan
Chale hain abhi khaima-e-shahana wo dauran

Qaid hongi sab Itrat-e-Sultan-e-Madina
Zalim ke tamanche bhi khayegi Sakina

Jab Mariya ke dasht mein warid hua wo lashkar

Jab Karbala mein Shah ka lashkar hua shahid

1
Jab Karbala mein Shah ka lashkar hua shahid
Akbar hua shahid aur Asghar hua shahid
Abbas aur Qasim-e-muztar hua shahid
Tanha Husain reh gaye sab ghar hua shahid

Zafar se bat jinn ne, ye ba-shor-o-shain ki
Ab tak khabar nahin tujhe Aqa Husain ki

2
Phire Sakina lab-e-zaban pani to piye
Akbar to khaye zakhm-e-sinan pani to piye
Asghar hua qatl, tishna-dahan pani to piye
Wan Aqa tera pyasa hai yan pani to piye

Kis munh se pesh-e-Fatima mahshar mein aayega
Us din bata ke kaun tujhe bakhshwayega

3
Zafar ne puchha tujh ko ye kyun kar hui khabar
Bola wo jinn Muhammad adab hath bandh kar
Ek din hua tha mera Najaf ki taraf guzar
Pahuncha jun hi main qabr-e-Janab-e-Amir par

Dekha zarih-e-Shah-e-Najaf thartharati hai
Hai hai Husain-e-pyare ki awaz aati hai
