Jab Karbala mein Shah ka lashkar hua shahid

1
Jab Karbala mein Shah-e ka lashkar hua Shahid
Akbar hua Shahid aur Asghar hua Shahid
Abbas aur Qasim muztar hua Shahid
Tanha Husain reh gaye sab ghar hua Shahid

Zafar se baat jin ne ， ye ba-shor-o-shain o shain ki
Ab tak khabar nahin tujhe Aqa Husain ki

2
Phire Sakina lab-e zaban pani to paye
Akbar to khaye zakhm-e sinan pani to paye
Asghar hua qatl ， tishna duhan pani to paye
Wan Aqa tera Pyasa hai Yan pani to paye

Kis munh se pesh-e Fatima-e mahshar mein aaye ga
Is din bata ke kaun tujhe bakhsahwiyo ga

3
Zafar ne puchha tujh ko ye kar hui khabar
Bola wo jin Muhammad adab hath bandh kar
Ek din hua tha mera Najaf ki taraf guzar
Pahuncha jo hi mein qabr-e Janab-e par

Dekha zarih-e Shah-e Najaf tharthara hai
Hai hai Husain-e ki awaz aati hai
