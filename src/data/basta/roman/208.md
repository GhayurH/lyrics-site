Jab Karbala mein Shah ka lashkar hua shahid

1
Jab Karbala mein Shah ka lashkar hua shahid
Akbar hua shahid aur Asghar hua shahid
Abbas aur Qasim-e-muztar hua shahid
Tanha Husain reh gaye sab ghar hua shahid

Zafar se baat jinn ne ye ba-shor-o-shain ki
Ab tak khabar nahin tujhe Aqa Husain ki

2
Phir se Sakina lab pe zaban pani to hai
Akbar to khaye zakhm-e-sinan pani to hai
Asghar hua qatl, nishan pani to hai
Wan Aqa tera pyasa hai yan pani to hai

Kis munh se pesh-e-Fatima hashr mein aayega
Us din bata ke kaun tujhe bakhshwayega

3
Zafar ne puchha mujh ko ye kyun kar hui khabar
Bola wo jinn ye chand adab hath bandh kar
Ek din hua tha mera Najaf ki taraf guzar
Pahuncha jo main qabr-e-Janab-e-Amir par

Dekha zarih-e-Shah-e-Najaf tharthara rahi hai
Haye Husain pyare ki awaz aa rahi hai
