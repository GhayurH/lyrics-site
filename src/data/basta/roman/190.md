Momino be-kas-o-be-yar hai Mazlum Husain

1
Momino be-kas-o-be-yar hai Mazlum Husain
Sakht afat mein giraftar hai Mazlum Husain
Dil-e-shikasta jigar-afgar hai Mazlum Husain
Kia Sara Seema O Lachar Hai Mazloom Hussain

Tir-e-kari hain lage zakhmon pe shamshiron ke
Nezon ke zakhmon mein paiwast hain phal tiron ke

2
Kya rahimi hai ke ghussa nahin aata hai zara
Kya karimi hai ke sar karte hain ummat pe fida
Kya tahammul hai ke har zakhm pe hai shukr-e-Khuda
Kya shujaat hai ke lakhon mein khare hain tanha

Tir bhi neze bhi sine pe liye jate hain
Par dya Nana ki ummat ko diye jate hain

3
Yun Tu Yan Ik Se Ik Zulum Hua Sheh Py Siwa
Teen Sadmu Mein Magar Zabt Ka Yara Na Raha
Ik Jawan Is Ka Bahtija Tha Hasan Ka Beta
Shab Ko Wo Dulha Bana Subha Shahedun Ko Mila

Us Ky Marne Ki Kahbar Bibiyun Ny Jab Pai Thi
Ma Dulhan Ko Liye Nange Sar Nikal Ai Thi

4
Dosra Gum Hai Ye Ik Bhai Tha Is La Safdar
Jab Se Wo Mar Gaya Seedhi Nahi Hoti Hai Kamar
Ab Bhi Rota Hai Use Haye Baradar Keh Kar
Be Kafan Lash Tu Uski Hai Pari Darya Par

Abhi Wo Lash Jab Dard Se Tharae Thi
Bachi Ik Haye Chacha Keh Ky Jo Chilai Thi

5
Teesra Hadsa Mein Kia Kahun Phatta Hai Jigar
Is La Ik Beta Tha Athara Baras Ka Akbar
Barcheyun Se Abhi Mara Gaya Wo Rashk E Qamar
Haef Hai Dafan Hui Rat Ko Jis Ki Madar

Ghum E Akbar Mein Usy Kia Kahun Kyunkar Dekha
Chism E Aflak Ny Zainab Ko Khilaye Sar Dekha

[Makhaz: Momino be-kas-o-be-yar hai Mazlum Husain / Mir Anees]
