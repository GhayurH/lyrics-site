Momino be-kas-o-be-yar hai Mazlum Husain

1
Momino be-kas-o-be-yar hai Mazlum Husain
Sakht afat mein giraftar hai Mazlum Husain
Dil-e shikasta jigar afgafir hai Mazlum Husain
Kya sar-e-asr-e-lachar hai Mazlum Husain

Tir kari hain lage zakhmon pe shamshiron ke
Nezon ke zakhmon mein paiwast hain phal tiron ke

2
Kya wahi hai ke gussa nahin aata hai zara
Kya karega ke sabr karte hain ummat pe fida
Kya mahal hai ke har zakhm pe hai shukr-e-Khuda
Kya shujaat hai ke lakhon mein khare hain tanha

Tir bhi tir se bhi sine pe liye jate hain
Par dua Nana ki ummat ko diye jate hain

3
Yun to yaan ek se ek zulm hua hai barpa
Kya karmi hai ke sar karte hain ummat pe fida
Kya tahmalm hai ke har zakhm pe hai shukr-e Khuda
Shab ko wo dulha bana, subh shahidon mein mila

Tir bhi neze bhi sine pe liye jate hain
Pidar-e Nana ki ummat ko diye jate hain

4
Tin sadmon mein magar zabt ka yara na raha
Ek jawan is ka bhatija tha Hasan ka beta
Jab se wo mar gaya use nahin hoti hai kamar
Ab bhi rota hai use haye baradar keh kar

Be-kasi kafan lash wo is ki hai pari darya par
Abhi wo lash-e ajab dard se tharrai thi

5
Larki ek haye chacha keh ke jo chillai thi
Atharah hadisa mein kya kahun phtata hai jigar
Is ka ek beta atharah baras ka Akbar
Barchhiyon se abhi mara gaya wo rashk-e Qamar

Haif hai dafn hui raat ko jis ki Madar
Gham-e Akbar mein use kya kahun kyun kar dekha
