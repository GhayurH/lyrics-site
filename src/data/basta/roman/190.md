Momino be-kas-o-be-yar hai Mazlum Husain

1
Momino be-kas-o-be-yar hai Mazlum Husain
Sakht afat mein giraftar hai Mazlum Husain
Dil-e-shikasta jigar-afgar hai Mazlum Husain
[Ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]

Tir-e-kari hain lage zakhmon pe shamshiron ke
Nezon ke zakhmon mein paiwast hain phal tiron ke

2
Dusra gham hai ye, ek bhai tha us ka Safdar
Jab se wo mar gaya sidhi nahin hoti hai kamar
Ab bhi rota hai use haye baradar keh kar
Be-kafan lash wo us ki hai pari darya par

Abhi wo lash ajab dard se tharrai thi
Larki ek haye chacha keh ke jo chillai thi

3
Kya rahimi hai ke ghussa nahin aata hai zara
Kya karimi hai ke sar karte hain ummat pe fida
Kya tahammul hai ke har zakhm pe hai shukr-e-Khuda
Kya shujaat hai ke lakhon mein khare hain tanha

Tir bhi neze bhi sine pe liye jate hain
Pidar-e-Nana ki ummat ko diye jate hain

4
Tin sadmon mein magar zabt ka yara na raha
Ek jawan us ka bhatija tha Hasan ka beta
Jab se wo mar gaya sidhi nahin hoti hai kamar
Ab bhi rota hai use haye baradar keh kar

Be-kafan lash wo us ki hai pari darya par
Abhi wo lash-e-ajab dard se tharrai thi

5
Larki ek haye chacha keh ke jo chillai thi
Tisra hadisa main kya kahun phatta hai jigar
Us ka ek beta atharah baras ka Akbar
Barchhiyon se abhi mara gaya wo rashk-e-qamar

Haif hai dafn hui rat ko jis ki madar
Gham-e-Akbar mein use kya kahun kyun kar dekha

[Makhaz: Momino be-kas-o-be-yar hai Mazlum Husain / Mir Anees]
