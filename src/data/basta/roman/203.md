Jab ran mein Sibt-e-Ahmad Mukhtar ghir gaya

1
Jab ran mein Sibt-e Ahmad-e Mukhtar ghar gaya
Syed thas gharib o be-kasi kis o be-kasi yar ghar gaya
Ahl-e-matam-e Haram ka qafila salar ghar gaya
Lashkar tamam ho gaya Sardar ghar gaya

Ghul tha amman do na Shah-e marshaqin ko
Nokon se barchhiyon ki gira Husain ko

2
Jis waqt tha ye hashr ye shor o shar tha
Aa pahuncha ek musafir-e ghurbat gham-zada idhar-e
Nikla tha ghar se shauq-e-Najaf mein wo khush-sair
Chhore hue watan use guzra tha sal bhar

Be-kasi khamnam ko ishq thas Khuda ke Wali ka tha
Mushtaq wo ziyarat-e-qabr-e-Ali ka tha

3
Pahuncha jo Karbala mein to dekha ye us ne hal
Tanha khara hai ek musafir lahu mein lal
Fajin sitam ki gird hain amada-e-qital-e
Chalte hain tir ， pani ka karta hai jab sawal

Az bas ke ahl-e-matam-e dard tha betab ho gaya
Pani ke mangne par jigar aab ho gaya

4
Kehne laga ke wo zezi qadar o Hind naam
Allah kis qadar hai pur-ashob ye maqam
Darya Khuda ne khalq ke bahr-e faiz-e amim
Marta hai be-ajal ye sitam kash ye tishna kam

Un-e se bashar dare jinhein khauf-e Khuda nahin
Jaldi nikal chalo ye thehrne ki ja nahin

5
Do char gam chal ke ye socha wo namwar
Mazlum ki dua hai har ek tarah ka asar
Wallah bargazi-e Haq hai ye khush nek-sair
Kar lijiye Iltimas dua hath bandh kar

Tahigon mein is ke pas chalo jo Khuda kare
Asan hun mushkilein jo ye be-kasi kis dua kare

6
Aaya jo kan pata hua wo Shah-e-din ke pas
Ki arz Assalam Alaik ay falak-asas
Maula jawab dijiye ye bole pur-dard-o-yas
Aata hua kidhar se tera ay Khuda-shinas

Arz us ne ki ghulam-e-Shah-e-Zulfiqar hun
Be-kas hun, be-nawa hun, gharib-ud-diyar hun
