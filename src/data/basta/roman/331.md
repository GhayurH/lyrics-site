Hilti hai zamin, rota hai falak, andhiyari chhane wali hai

Hilti hai zamin, rota hai falak, andhiyari chhane wali hai
Sab qatl hue, utho Karbala qayamat aane wali hai
Niche se sada di Banu ne Asghar ko chhupa lijiye Maula
Pani ke iwaz fauj-e-aada ab tir chalane wali hai
[Pehla misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Madar ke kaleje par bat teri Asghar tarpane wali hai
Bachchon ke liye Abbas jari ab pani lene aaye hain
[Mashk aur mauj wala misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Ghore se gire jab Shah-e-wala aai Zainab se ye sada
Ghar lut ke Nana ki ummat ab aag lagane wali hai

(Bashukriya shair-o-sozkhwan Qasim Husain Zaidi, Maulvi Meerthi)

Bint-e-Zahra ye kehti thi ro ro mere bhai ka lasha bata do

Bint-e-Zahra ye kehti thi ro ro mere bhai ka lasha bata do
Hum musibat-zadon ki dua lo mere bhai ka lasha bata do
Bap man mar gaye ghar se chhuti aa ke ghurbat mein afat ye tuti
Chhup gaye hum se Sultan-e-khush-khu mere bhai ka lasha bata do
Aag khaimon mein tum ne lagai mere Nana ki masjid jalai
Ab zyada na hum ko satao mere bhai ka lasha bata do
Kaisi afat mein hum sab pare hain kis musibat mein chhote bare hain
Mere Nana ki ummat ho tum to mere bhai ka lasha bata do

(Bashukriya Syed Tufail Abbas Rizvi, Steel Town Karachi)

Zakir Husain Zaidi / America (az Maula-e-Kainat)

Ali jaisa dusra par guzra nahin dekha
Kai sadiyon ne ki apni umr par naz
Zamana mor kar dekha, kabhi manzar nahin dekha

(Bashukriya baradar Kashif, shair-o-nauha khwan Baqir Husain Zaidi)
