[Pehla misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Piron ke astane kitne saje hue hain
Par roshni hai tujh se ay Jannat-ul-Baqia

Dil ki muraden ab bhi pate hain hum yahin se
Milta hai jo yahan se, milta nahin kahin se
Dharas bari hai tujh se ay Jannat-ul-Baqia

Tujh par jo hakimon ne pehre bitha diye hain
Apne tain wo teri azmat ghata rahe hain
Khaif bhi hai tujh se ay Jannat-ul-Baqia

[Ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Aulad-e-Ibrahim aur Aal-e-Saud ko bhi
Nafrat wohi hai tujh se ay Jannat-ul-Baqia

Aal-e-Saud hon ya hon Khariji-o-Najdi
Dhaya jinhon ne tujh ko donon jahan mein un ki
Zillat hui hai tujh se ay Jannat-ul-Baqia

Mah-e-aza hai hum ko Shawwal ka mahina
Tamir jab tu hogi wo din khushi ka hoga
Gham aur khushi hai tujh se ay Jannat-ul-Baqia

Jate hain jaise Mashhad, hum Karbala bhi jayen
Makka Madina Aqsa azad hum karayen
Banka yahi hai tujh se ay Jannat-ul-Baqia

Ajdad ka watan aur madfan hai, Sibt Jafar
Tujh par ho [lafz ghair wazeh] bhi rahmat, ay sarzamin-e-Sarwar
Ye din bhi hai tujh se ay Jannat-ul-Baqia

Qit'a / Syed Mahdi Zahir Razavi Kalimi

Ho zikr-e-Shah to faza dardnak kar pehle
Nawa-e-gham ke gareban ko chak kar pehle
Rahe zara bhi na araish-e-tarab baqi
Suron ko soz ke darya mein pak kar pehle

(Bashukriya sozkhwan Professor Suhail Jafari o Ayub Husain Bilgrami)
