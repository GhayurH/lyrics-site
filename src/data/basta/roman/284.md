Baba ko rote rote jo Zahra guzar gayi

1
Baba ko rote rote jo Zahra guzar gayi
Ghul par gaya ke Bint-e-Nabi kooch kar gayi
Faaqon ke ranj seh ke Huzur-e-pidar gayi
Mahbub-e-Kibriya ki azadar mar gayi

Atharwin baras ne ye aafat dikhai hai
Aal-e-Nabi ko charkh ne luta duhai hai

2
Sibtain ghar mein aaye jo betaab-o-beqaraar
Asma se poochne lage Amma ka haal-e-zaar
Wo boli neend agayi hai shukr-e-Kirdigar
Khana to jald khaa lo ke bhooke ho, main nisaar

Bole ke chayn dega zamaana to khayenge
Amma hamein khilayengi khaana to khayenge

3
Ye sun ke be-qarar hui wo jigar figaar
Chaadar zameen pe phenk ke chillai baar baar
Bachche hain inn ko sabr de ay mere Kirdigar
Ab wo khilane wali kahan tum pa main nisaar

Pyaro tumhari paalne wali guzar gayi
Khaaoge kis ke hath se Amma to mar gayi

4
Sher-e-Khuda thay muztar-o-maghmum ik taraf
Sar pit'ti thin Zainab-o-Kulsoom ik taraf
Pakre thay dil ko Syed-e-Masmoom ik taraf
Bismil thay khaak par Sheh-e-Mazloom ik taraf

Haider qareeb aaye to ik khat nazar para
Tarpe kuchh is tarah ke amaama utar para

5
Likha tha ye ke aakhri zehmat qubul ho
Ya Shah, tum Wasi-e-Janab-e-Rasul ho
Sadqa Huzur ka, mera maqsad husul ho
Munh se na keh saki ke hazin-o-malul ho

Meri wasiyatein na faramosh kijiyo
Awwal ye hai ke aap mujhe ghusl dijiyo

6
Doyem ye hai ke shab mein janaza uthaiyo
Murde ka saya bhi na kisi ko dikhaiyo
Yaan tak ke qabr bhi na kisi ko bataiyo
Sau sau jagah nishaan lahad ka banaiyo

Soyem ye hai ke paas yatimon ka kijiyo
Shafqat se boliyo kabhi ghurki na dijiyo
