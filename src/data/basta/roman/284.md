Baba ko rote rote jo Zahra guzar gayin

1
Baba ko rote rote jo Zahra guzar gayi
Ghul par gaya ke Bint-e-Nabi koch kar gayi
Faqon ke ranj seh ke Huzur-e-pidar gayi
Mahbub-e-Kibriya ki azadar mar gayi

Atharwin baras mein ye afat dikhai hai
Aal-e-Nabi ko charkh ne luta duhai hai

2
Sibtain ghar mein aaye jo betab-o-be-qarar
Asma se puchhne lage Amman ka hal-e-zar
Wo boli nind aai hai shukr-e-Kirdigar
Khana to jald kha lo ke bhuke ho, main nisar

Bole ke chain dega zamana to khayenge
Amman hamein khilayengi khana to khayenge

3
Ye sun ke be-qarar hui wo jigar-figar
Chadar zamin pe phenk ke chillai bar bar
Bachche hain un ko sabr de ay mere Kirdigar
Ab wo khilane wali marah guzar gayi

Pyaron tumhari palne wali guzar gayi
Khaoge kis ke hath se Amman to mar gayi

4
Sher-e-Khuda the muztar-o-maghmum ek taraf
Sar pitti thin Zainab-o-Kulsum ek taraf
Pakre the dil ko Syed-e-Masum ek taraf
[Ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]

Haider qarib aaye to ek khat nazar para
Tarpe kuchh is tarah ke amama utar para

5
Likha tha ye ke aakhri mujra qubul ho
Ya Shah, tum Wasi-e-Janab-e-Rasul ho
Sadqa-e-Huzur ka, mera maqsad husul ho
Munh se na keh saki ke hazin-o-malul ho

Meri wasiyatein na faramosh kijiyo
Awwal ye hai ke aap mujhe ghusl dijiyo

6
Duwam ye hai ke shab mein janaza uthaiye
Murde ka saya bhi na kisi ko dikhaiye
Yan tak ke qabr bhi na kisi ko bataiye
Sau sau jagah nishan-e-lahad ka banaiye

Suwam ye hai ke pas yatimon ka kijiyo
Shafqat se boliye na kabhi ghurki na dijiyo
