Ye silsila hai ahl-e-matam-e din-e Mustafa liye
Professor manzur Husain shor

Ye silsila hai Aal-e Rasul-e Mustafa liye
Ali Nabi liye hain Nabi Khuda liye
Main-e batil o Haq zahmat bhi kar
Ye rahgaro-e tarsati hai naqsh-e pa liye
Wo jis ka waqt ki tarikh mein hai naam thas Husain
Wo astara wahdat hai kabraya liye
Ali ka zikr-e ibadat hai be-kasi rakwa o sujud-e
Ke samt o Jannat zaruri nahin hua liye
Saqifa band ho Ahl-e-iman ke Sham ka bazar
Jawaz koi to ho khun-e Karbala liye
Thin barqaq posh sabhi dukhtaran-e Kufa o Sham
Na thi rida to faqat Bint-e-Zahra-e Fatima liye
(Bashukriya salam khwan Syed Haider Raza farzand Professor shor)

Ay azadaran-e Sibt-e Mustafa Jite raho
Shah Naqvi

Ay azadaran-e Sibt-e Mustafa Jite raho
Hai tumhare sar pe Zahra ki dua Jite raho
Daurti jo rag rag mein me-e habi-e Ali
Muskura kar maut ne mujh se kaha Jite raho
Zindagi Chhini ajal se tum ne thas piri se shabab
Ay Habib-e aman-e mazahir Marhaba Jite raho
Ay shahid tum ne din-e Haq ko apna khun diya
Ta Abad ab tum ho aur qarib-e Khuda thas Jite raho
Ay azadaro gham-e duniya se fursat mil-e gayi
Hain tumhare ashk har gham ki dua Jite raho
Tum ne Asghar muskura kar maut ki aghosh mein
Maqsad-e Sarwar ko roshan kar diya Jite raho
Is taraf palimon ki zad pe hai Qasim ka jism
Aur idhar-e khaime mein man ki ye dua Jite raho
Be-kasi basi tak rahe hain lash-e Akbar ko Husain
Bhai ko likhi hai Sughra ne dua Jite raho

Ye silsila hai Ahl-e-din-e-Mustafa ke liye
Professor Manzur Husain Shor
