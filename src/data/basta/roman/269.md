Jab afaton ke giraftar qaid se chhute

1
Jab afaton ke giraftar qaid se chhute
Imamam-e din ke azadar qaid se chhute
Asir o muztar o nachar qaid se chhute
Janab-e Abid-e bimar qaid se chhute

Buka ka izn-e jo paya to dil-e ko chain mila
Husain to na mile par sar-e Husain mila

2
Qayamat aai ke Shah-e hada ka sar aaya
Pahurhain khayen pisar ne bahen ne ghash khaya
Salam kar ke mariz-e alam ye chalaya
Bahut dinon mein asiron ko yaad farmaya

Faraqaq-e Shah mein qaidi khari uthate the
Ghiza ke badle faqat taziyane khate the

3
mariz-e Mariz-e-hijr ne kya kya na shor o shar dekha
Huzur-e sar par khun ko neze par dekha
Hujm-e amim-e Haram ke idhar-e idhar-e dekha
Asiron ko sar-e bazar nange sar dekha

Kharabe mein ye musibat guzar gayi Baba
Tumhari ladli ghut ghut ke mar gayi Baba

4
Haram jo qabr-e-Sakina pe nauhagar aaye
Hujum-e-yas mein zindan se chhut kar aaye
Sar-e Husain ko le kar wo nange sar aaye
Nida ye di ke Sakina utho pidar aaye

Imamam-e Pak ke sar ki balayen lo Bibi Bibi
Husain lene ko khud aa gaye hain chalo Bibi Bibi

5
[Ye band juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Lahad pe Gar ke ye chillai Banu nalan
Ye be-nasib bhi milne ko aai hai meri jaan

Chacha jo tumhein kehna suye gah mein ho Amman
(Nasim Amrohvi)

Tajammul Lakhnavi
Ke amun jaan se milne ko jati hun aman
Safar ka qasdas hai Asghar ke gham mein marti hun

Samar Hoshangabadi
Tumhari qabr-e Khuda ke supurd karti hun
Koi nemat na likh duniya gham-e-Shabbir se pahle
