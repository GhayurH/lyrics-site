Likha hai chhut ke Yasrib mein jab Haram aaye

1
Likha hai chhut ke Yasrib mein jab Haram aaye
Saron ko pitte basda gham o alam aaye
Bapa tha Ghul Haram-e Syed-e ammam aaye
Bayan karte the Sajjad ro ke hum aaye

Tamam kunbe maqtal mein kho ke aaye hain
Bajaye tohfa behtar ke dagh laye hain

2
Madine walo, hamein Karbala ne lut liya
Yatim mein hua Baba hue Shahid-e jafa
Hue asir-e asir-e-sitam Ahl-e-Bait wawali
Magar Madine mein phir aa ke kya suna kya

Jo thamta to jafa ashkar hoti thi
Ke pusht durron se meri figar hoti thi

3
Gaye Rasulwal ke rauze pe jis ghari Sajjad
Lipat ke qabr-e-mubarak se tab ye ki faryad
Khabar hai aap ko bhi hum pe kya hui bedad
Ke dopahar mein hua sara ghar ghar barbad

Tumhari Aal par ranj o alam kaisari hue
Husain qatl hue aur Haram asir hue

4
Chale makan ko rote Imamam-e jin o bashar
Tamam qafila tha sath sath nauhagar
Wafurwa-e gham hua jo khana Sarwar
Udasi chhai hai deorhi par aa ke sar

Ye ghair hal hua gham se jaan khone lage
Imamam-e dekh ke sunsan ghar ko rone lage

5
Gharz utar chuke naqon se jabke Ahl-e-Haram
Bichhai bibiyon ne ghar mein Tab saf-e matam
Kaha ye Fatima Sughra ne kyun na ho mujhe gham
Mili na aa ke Sakina Sham mein Ay sitam

Pukari Banu wo ji se guzar gayi Sughra
Sakina Sham ke zindan mein mar gayi Sughra

6
Khusus Hazrat-e Zainab ki thi ajab halat
Kabhi na pitne rone se hoti thi fursat
Ghiza bhi Azim-e-tark-e-watan hui taq ho gayi taqat
Kisi se milti na thin aap gham ki thi shiddat

Yahi ward-e zaban tum kidhar gaye bhai
Main aah jiti rahi aur mar gaye bhai
