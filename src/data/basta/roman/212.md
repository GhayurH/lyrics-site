Jab khatima ba-khair hua fauj-e-Shah ka

1
Jab khatima ba-khair hua fauj-e-Shah ka
Kausar pe qafila gaya pyasi sipah ka
Ghar lut gaya Janab-e risalat Panah ka
Khak ur rahi thi hal ye tha bargah ka

Wo bhai ， wo Rafiq ， na wo nur-e ein the
Narghe mein dushmanon ke akele Husain the

2
Par khun jabin ， phatte hue kare ， badan par khak
Chadar siyah ， ek gareban ， Hazar chak
Dil bhi jigar bhi sina par khun bhi dardnak
bikis ke hal par roye Imam Pak

Farmaya khun mein Akbar ro nahaye hain
Hum un ki lash chhor ke rukhsat ko aaye hain

3
Abbas ab kahan hain, kahan Akbar Husain
Aada ko barh ke rokne wala koi nahin
Khaime tak na jaye kahin Fauj-e fauj-e-Ahl-e-Kin
Rukhsat karo Husain Ay Zainab-e-hazin-e

La do Rasul-e-Pak ka rukhsat-e kehn hamein
Pehna do apne hath se kafan hamein

4
Wo Boli meri jaan nikal le to jaye
Khanjar-e ka halq pe chal le to jaye
Muztar hai ji bahen ka sambhal le to jaye
Achchha zara Sakina le to jaye

Balon pe khak ura ke munh Ansuon se Balon
Man jaye bhai mein tujhe ji bhar ke lun

5
Dekha ye keh ke Bani Sakina ko yas se
Lipti wo daur kar Shah-e-gardun-asas se
Taqat na thi kalam ki har chand pyas se
Boli wo tishna-kam Shah-e-Haq-shinas se

Kya is bala ke ban se natija safar ka hai
Sadqe gayi batao irada kidhar ka hai

6
Farmaya Shah ne han ye safar na-guzir hai
Aao gale lago ke ye waqt-e-akhir hai
Ab arzu-e-qurb-e-Khuda-e-Qadir hai
Tanha hain hum, sipah-e-mukhalif kasir hai

Tay ho ye marhala jo inayat Khuda kare
jis ka na koi dost ho, Bibi Bibi wo kya kare

Jab khatima ba-khair hua fauj-e-Shah ka
