Soz

Har dur ke naqib hain Quran o ahl-e-matam-e bait
Insan ka nasib hain Quran o ahl-e-matam-e bait
Allah ke qarib hain Quran o ahl-e-matam-e bait
Mahbub-e ke Habib hain Quran o ahl-e-matam-e bait

Haq ahl-e-matam-e bait ka hai Khuda ki kitab par
Lakhon durud Aal-e risalat aab par
(Bashukriya sozkhwan o shair Naqviya, fidwi Sikandar Zaidi / Bolan)

Banda Hazar sal ibadat agar kare
Aur zar Ba-qadr-e-zarf-e-samaat-e koh-e ahad rah-e Haq mein de
Hajj bhi padishad pa jo Hazar is ne hun kiye
Aur be-kasi gunah qatl ho wo zulm o jaur se

Habi-e Ali ki me nahin Gar dil ke jam mein
Jannat ki bu na pahunche lungi hargiz mashma mein
Hajj bhi piyada pa jo hazar us ne hon kiye

Maryam se bhi Batul ko rutba siwa mila
Baba use Rasul-e sa khair aluri mila
Beta har ek Shahid-e reh-e kabraya mila
Shauhar mila to khalq ka aqda kusha mila

Har ek apne martabe mein intikhab hai
Zahra hai be-kasi Misal Ali lajawab hai
(Bashukriya salam o nauhakhwan Iftikhar Ali Bahari Khan)

Chashm-e Nabi ke khwab ko kehte hain Sayyida
Ismat ke aftab ko kehte hain Sayyida
Quran ke jawab ko kehte hain Sayyida
Munh bolti kitab ko kehte hain Sayyida

Ye zat yun katab ki Tathir ban gayi
Lafzon mein dhal ke aiya Tathir ban gayi
