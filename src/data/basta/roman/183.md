Hamari lash yunhi dasht mein pari hogi

1
Mashk bhar kar su-e-khaima jo Alamdar chale
Rokne ke liye rasta, sitam-atwar chale
Ye ba-surat jo urate hue zinhar chale
Tir barsate hue lakh sitamgar chale

Ab laren ya alam-o-mashk sambhalen Abbas
Bag len ghore ki ya tir nikalen Abbas

2
Haye laakhon wo shaqi aur wo tanha Jarrar
Ay dil-e-afgar ke saqqe teri jurat ke nisar
Is tarah mashk ko danton mein dabaya ek bar
Jis tarah Sher-e-Ghazanfar ke munh mein ho shikar

War parte hain to ye sar ko jhuka lete hain
Mashk ko sina-e-zakhmi se chhupa lete hain

3
Dil mein kehte hain ke Ya Haider-e-Safdar aao
Bachchi mar jayegi mashkize ko tiron se bachao
Kabhi farmate hain ay Shamiyo ye qahr na dhao
Sar mera kat lo par mashk ko nawak na lagao

Qatl ke baad bhi Abbas ko iza dena
Par Sakina ki amanat use pahuncha dena

4
The isi tarah su-e-khaima Shabbir rawan
Yak-ba-yak chashm-e-mubarak mein gira ek paikan
Tir kha kar abhi sambhla bhi na tha Sher-e-Ziyan
Nagahan mashk chhidi, sar pe laga gurz-e-giran

Khun behta raha jab tak to na rahwar gire
Beh gaya aab to reti pe Alamdar gire

5
Munh se be-sakhta nikli ye gham-angez sada
Assalam ay jigar-o-jaan-e-Batul-e-Azra
Assalam ay pisar-e-Badshah-e-Uqda-kusha
Assalam ay umr-e-bagh-e-Rasul-e-do-sara

Chhor ke Syed-e-wala ke qadam jate hain
Ab Sakina se khabardar ke hum jate hain

6
Ye sada sunte hi Maula ne jigar ko thama
Zauf-e-piri ne shah-e-jinn-o-bashar ko thaama
Ghash jo aaya Ali Akbar ne pidar ko thama
Daurane uthe ke musafir ki kamar ko thama

Roye farmaya abhi tum bhi mujhe chhor chale
The isi tarah su-e-khaima Shabbir rawan
