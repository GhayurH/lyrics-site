Hamari lash yun hi dasht mein pari ho lungi

1
Mashk bhar kar suye khaima jo Alamdar chale
Rokne liye rasta thas sitam sitam-izhar chale
Ye ba sarata jo urate hue zahnar chale
Tir barsate hue lakh sitamgar chale

Ab laren Ya alam o mashk sambhalen Abbas
Bag len Ghore ki Ya tir nikalen Abbas

2
Baye lakhon wo shaqi aur wo tanha Jarrar
Us dil-e-angaar ke sath bari jurat ke nisar
Us tarah mashk ko danton mein dabaya ek bar
Jis tarah sher-e-ghazabnak ke munh mein ho shikar

War parte hain to ye sar ko jhuka lete hain
Mashk ko sina-e-zakhmi se chhupa lete hain

3
Haye wo shaqi aur wo tanha Jarrar
Ay dil-e afgafir ke se-e teri Jurat ke nisar
Is-e tarah mashk ko danton mein dibaya ek bar
Jis tarah sher-e ghazazar ke munh mein ho shakar

War parte hain to ye sar ko jhuka lete hain
Par Sakina ki amanat use pahuncha dena

4
The isi tarah suye khaima dilawar rawan
Yak-ba-yak chashm-e-mubarak mein gira ek paikan
Tir kha kar abhi sambhla bhi na tha Sher-e-Zaman
Nagahan mashk chhidi, sar pe laga gurz-e-giran

Khun behta raha jab tak to na zinhār gire
Beh gaya aab to bar-khak Alamdar gire

5
Dil mein kehte hain ke Ya Haider-e Safdar aao
Bachhi mar jaye lungi mashkize ko tiron se Bachao
Kabhi farmate hain Ay shamiyo ye qahr na dhao
Sar mera kat lo par mashk ko nawak na lagao

Qatl ke baad bhi Abbas ko iza dena
Par Sakina ki amanat use pahuncha dena

6
Ye sada sunte hi daure gaye ran mein Shah-e-din
Na falak sujhta tha aur na ankhon se zamin
Biyaban mein khun mein ghaltan nazar aaye mah-jabin
Khak par gir ke ba-sad dard pukare Shabbir

Haye ay bhai, qurban tumhare Shabbir
The isi-e tarah suye khaima Shabbir rawan
