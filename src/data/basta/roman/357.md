Jab kabhi ghairat-e-insan ka sawal aata hai

Jab kabhi ghairat-e-insan ka sawal aata hai
Bint-e-Zahra tere parde ka khayal aata hai
Ay khilafat tumhein Haq chhinne ka afsos nahin
Par gunahon ki sadaqat ka khayal aata hai
Is tarah tanha khare the Shabbir ran mein
Jis tarah chand ko taron mein zawal aata hai
Maut kis soch mein hai, lasha-e-Akbar pe khari
Kya Payambar ki jawani ka khayal aata hai
Shuhada ke pichhe ke piri hai zaban honton par
Aaj Shabbir ko Haider ka jalal aata hai
Kaun qaidi hai ke laye hain Kufa wale
Kuchh Muhammad ki nawasi ka khayal aata hai
 
Iltimas Surah Fatiha baraye isal-e-sawab
[Asma juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]

Banu ka ye arman tha abad makan hoga

Banu ka ye arman tha abad makan hoga
Ek roz wo hoga jab Asghar bhi jawan hoga
Lash aai jo Asghar ki man ro ke ye kehti thi
Beta tujhe maut aai ab kaun jawan hoga
Logo mere bachche ko maqtal se utha lao
Wan khak ka bistar hai aram kahan hoga
Aghosh mein rehte the kyun gor pasand aai
Wan khak ka bistar hai aram kahan hoga
Agah nahin thi main taqdir ke likhe se
Tifli mein mera bachcha ankhon se nihan hoga
Khud aan ke bachche ko do ghont pila jao
Main mangta hun pani ye tum ko kahan hoga
Be-shir ki turbat pe ye bain the madar ke
Sughra jo tumhein puchhegi kya mera bayan hoga

(Bashukriya salam-o-nauha khwan Syed Iqbal Husain Bali)
