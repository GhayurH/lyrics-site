[Kitab mein hai shab-e-Ashur ka ye hal likha — jari]

4
Ye keh rahe hain ke nusrat mein jad o ke karna
Mein wari pyari-e gahmig-e samdad karna
Kari uthaye Akhi ki bula ko rad karna
Bahaduro, mere man jaye ki madad karna

Wo kya ghulam jise ulfat-e Imam nahin
Jo ran mein kam na aaye to man se kam nahin

5
jo kam aao hue tum to duaen mein dun lungi
Jo phir ke aaye to surat bhi na dekhun gi
Tamam umr kabhi tum se main na bolun gi
Qasam se kehti hun, hargiz na doodh bakhshun gi

mein Shah hun lungi jo barbad kar ke aao hue
jo man ko chahne ho tum to mar ke aao hue

6
Bahen ki chah pe ro kar barhe Shah-e-zijahan
ye dar se padishad Shabbir ka hal dekha aah
Ke donon pahluon mein hain Hasan ke nur-e-nigah
Idhar hain Qasim, udhar hain Abdullah

ye keh rahe hain ke [ghair wazeh] ghar gaye hain afat mein
Main wari, jaan larana chacha ki nusrat mein

7
mein sadqe tum pe, bara Haq-e Shah-e wala hai
Hasan ke baad unhi ne to ghar sambhala hai
Kabhi na mujh ko, na tum ko alam mein dala hai
Pidar ki tarah bari shafqaton se pala hai

Chacha ke paon pe sadaqon ka khun bah jaye
Ilahi kal Zainab zinda ki baat reh jaye

[makhm: Kutub mein hai shab-e Ashur ka ye hal Likha / Nasim Amrohvi]
