[Kutub mein hai shab-e-Ashur ka ye hal likha — jari]

4
Ye keh rahe hain ke nusrat mein jad-o-kad karna
Main wari pairawi-e-Zaigham-e-Samad karna
Kari uthaye akhi ki bala ko rad karna
Bahaduro, mere man-jaye ki madad karna

Wo kya ghulam jise ulfat-e-Imam nahin
Jo ran mein kam na aaye to man se kam nahin

5
Jo kam aaoge tum to duaen main dungi
Jo phir ke aaye to surat bhi na dekhungi
Tamam umr kabhi tum se main na bolungi
Qasam se kehti hun hargiz na dudh bakhshungi

Main shad hungi jo barbad kar ke aaoge
Jo man ko chahne ho tum to mar ke aaoge

6
Bahen ki chah pe ro kar barhe Shah-e-zi-jah
Ye dar se piyadah Shabbir ka hal dekha aah
Ke donon pahluon mein hain Hasan ke nur-e-nigah
Idhar hain Qasim-e-Muzaffar, udhar hain Abdullah

Ye keh rahe hain ke [ghair wazeh] ghar gaye hain afat mein
Main wari jaan larana chacha ki nusrat mein

7
Main sadqe tum pe, bara Haq-e-Shah-e-wala hai
Hasan ke baad unhi ne to ghar sambhala hai
Kabhi na mujh ko na tum ko alam mein dala hai
Pidar ki tarah bari shafqaton se pala hai

Chacha ke paon pe donon ka khun beh jaye
Ilahi kal Zainab zinda ki bat reh jaye

[Makhaz: Kutub mein hai shab-e-Ashur ka ye hal likha / Nasim Amrohvi]
