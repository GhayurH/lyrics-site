Shair-e-Pakistan Sahba Akhtar
[Taarufi/ishtihari matn safhe par maujud hai; chand alfaz ghair wazeh]

Sham se jab Ahl-e-Bait gham-zada giryan chale

1
Sham se jab Ahl-e-Bait bassar-e uryan chale
Yani suye Karbala chak-e gareban chale
Le ke shahidon ke sar be-sar o saman chale
Karte hue bibiyan badal-e nalan chale

Aaj hai hai Hazrat-e Shabbir ka
Tishna dahan kashta-e khanjar o taqdir ka

2
Rah mein un se koi puchhta Gar aan kar
Kis ke ho namus tum kis ke ho lakht-e jigar
kis ne lita tumhein, kis ne kya be pidar
kehte the Ahl-e Haram is se yun bashim-e tar

Tum se bayan karen apne hum ahwal hum
Dafn karne chale Fatima ke lal ko hum

3
wo jo Haram-e Muhtaram, Shah-e ba-sad buka
Manzilein tay karte haye, pahunche dar-e-Karbala
Dur se wo qatl gah un ko dikhai diya
Ro ke ye kehne lage Hazrat-e zini albada

Unton se utaro tum ab karte hue shor o shain
rote chalo padishad, Yan se ba lash-e Husain
