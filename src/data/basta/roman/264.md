[Taarufi/ishtihari matn safhe par maujud hai; chand alfaz ghair wazeh]

Sham se jab Ahl-e-Bait gham-zada giryan chale

1
Sham se jab Ahl-e-Bait gham-zada giryan chale
Yani suye Karbala chak-gareban chale
Le ke shahidon ke sar, be-sar-o-saman chale
Karte hue ye bayan bal-afshan chale

Aaj hai chehlum Wallah Hazrat Shabbir ka
Tishnagan-e-kushta-e-khanjar-o-taqdir ka

2
Rah mein un se koi puchhta gar aan kar
Kis ke ho namon tum, kis ke ho gharib-jigar
Kis ne hai luta tumhein, kis ne kiya be-pidar
Kehte the Ahl-e-Haram un se yun ba-chashm-e-tar

Tum se bayan kya karen apne hum ahwal ko
Dafn hain karne chale Fatima ke lal ko

3
Wo jo Haram-e-muhtaram Shah ke ba-sad buka
Manzilein tay karte haye, pahunche dar-e-Karbala
Dur se wo qatl-gah un ko dikhai diya
Ro ke ye kehne lage Hazrat Zain-ul-Aba

Unton se utro ke ab karte hue shor-o-shain
Rote chalo piyada-pa [juzwi taur par ghair wazeh] Husain
