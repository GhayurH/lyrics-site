Phiri jo mominon ran se sawari Akbar ki

1
Phiri jo mominon se sawari Akbar ki
pukari walida, hum surat-e Payambar ki
Isi khayal mein deorhi se mein na thi sar ki
Ke phir bhi dekh lun taswir apne dilbar ki

Na dungwani ab kabhi rukhsat Nabi ke sani ko
Laga rakhungi kaleje se apne jani ko

2
Kahan hai lal mera lao pas mujh man ke
Laga lun sine se poton ko mili Zahra ke
Ye keh rahi thi ke Sibt-e-Rasul ne aa ke
Lapeti lash bichhaune pe sar ko navura ke

Pukari Zainab-e dil khasta-tan pit kar sar ko
Sipah-e Sham ne jane na diya Akbar ko

3
Ye shakl chand si sab khun mein bhar gayi hai hai
Wo shan aur wo shaukat kidhar gayi hai hai
Jigar se is-e ke sinan yun guzar gayi hai hai
Phuphi ke badle na mar gayi hai hai

Ye marne wala meri ankh ka ujala tha
Chhatti ke roz use ke mein ne pala tha

4
Ye keh ke pit ke sar ko pachhar jo Khai
Pukari Banu ke Akbar tumhari lash aai
Jawab do mujhe qurban hue ye dai
Mein dekhun Barchhi kaleje pe kis jagah Khai

Gaye the achchhe aur aaye lehlan hue
Isi larai ki khatir the tum jawan hue

5
Mein is-e irade ke himmat ke ho gayi wari
Na samjhi thi ke Khuld barabin ki hai tayyari
Ye keh ke lash se bete ki wo falak-mari
Lagi zamin pe tarapne ba nala o zari

Tarapte dekh ke un ko Imam rone lage
Shahid bete ka le ke naam rone lage

[Makhaz: Phiri jo mominon ran se sawari Akbar ki]

Har nemat-e-Khuda rah-e-har nemat koi hasrat
Naghmat-e-naat ban kar kaun-o-makan mein gunje

Iltimas Surah Fatiha baraye
