Qaid-khane mein talatum hai ke Hind aati hai

1
Qaid-khane mein talatum hai ke Hind aati hai
Ye zikr tha ke aa gaya khuni talkh-kam
Ruh-e-Ghalib mein wo zindan mein ghabrati hai
Laya sinan pe farq-e-lala-fam

Zindan-e-Sham nur se roshan hua tamam
[Ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]

2
Us sar ne di nida mere bimar assalam
Ghabra ke Ahl-e-Bait to tazim ko uthe
Qaidiyo utho, dua de ke karo istiqbal
Sajjad kanpte hue taslim ko uthe

Tum khule sar nahin, Huzur ab tumhein chadar dengi
Rahm-dil hain, abhi zindan se riha kar dengi

3
Thin kanizen zanan-e-hakim ki jalau mein jo rawan
Us sar se ro ke kehne lagi Hind-e-bawafa
Ay sar tu bolta hai to ye bhi mujhe bata
Munh pe sili ke nishan, pusht pe durron ke nishan

Saq-e-pa [lafz ghair wazeh] se zanjir mein tharrai hai
Kis ka Khuda hai, Aal hai kya naam hai tera
