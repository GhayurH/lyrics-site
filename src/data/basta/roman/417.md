Chhor de / Sibt Jafar


Hai Nabi se muhabbat ka dawa, tujhe un ki aulad se dushmani chhor de
Gar shafaat Payambar se darkar hai, un ke aghyar se dosti chhor de
Din-e-Haq ki zarurat jo bachchon ki thi, ghar se bahar the kuchh apne bachchon ke liye
Aise bachchon ki takzib karta ho jo chahe, us ko dushmani chhor de
Tu na Haq ka sarapa hai na kuchh aur, phir kyun kya
Jo kaha kar use phir bhulaya bhi kar, magar munafiq nahin, dushmani chhor de
Hai jo tujh pe giran naam Mushkil-kusha, tu jo Makka Hadis kis ki hua
Kyun tu dushman-e-Muhammad se bahar hai, tu hum se kam nisbat-e-Jafari chhor de
Dostdar-e-Nabi-o-Ali se dur gurez kare
Kyun wo kehta hai jo khud bhi karta nahin, buzdili chhor, yar-parasti chhor de
Tere nazdik kirdar kuchh bhi nahin, tu faqat marg-e-guftar se waghla
Khud amal karke auron ko talqin kar, wird-e-lafzi-o-zakiri chhor de
Hamd-o-Naat-o-sanaye-murshid ke siwa shairi kuchh nahin ji ka khayal hai
Hai ye tere liye bhi muzarr ay insan, ab buri man le shairi chhor de
Apne gareban mein bhi jhank le, apni islah kar, sidha ho
Sirf baton na kar Sibt Jafar, na mani ho to muballighi chhor de

Sharan haram / Dilawar Nigar
("Asri Ulum-o-Funun-e-Islami" par Dilawar Nigar Sahib ka manzum tabsira)

Sibt Jafar muhtaram hain jin ki ye tasnif hai
Is mein jo mazmun hai wo qabil-e-tarif hai
Fan-e-Quran aur sana bhi in ke mauzuat hain
Jitne mazmun hain wo sare wajah-e-malumat hain
Tajriba ilmi masail par mera mansab nahin
Kuchh nigahon mein magar ye bhi mera matlab nahin
Sozkhwani aur musiqa mein hai kya rabita
Ye sawal un se karen aalim hain jo ba-zabita
Meri apni raye ye hai sozkhwani hai sawab
Kyunke gane aur bajane se hai is ka ijtinab
Sozkhwani dar haqiqat nauha-e-purdard hai
Ye Husain ibn-e-Ali ke gham mein aah-o-sard hai
Sozkhwani ko ghina kehna sarasar jhut hai
Aur ghina ko matam kehna magar jhut hai
Main koi aalim nahin aur na ye mera maqam
Main samajhta hun ke musiqa nahin sharan haram
