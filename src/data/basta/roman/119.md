Khayal-e-Karbala hai aur main hun
Hasan Akbar Mirza

Khayal-e-Karbala hai aur main hun
Iram ka rasta hai aur main hun

Dar-e-Shabbir hai aur meri qismat
Maqam-e-iltija hai aur main hun

Husain Ibn-e-Ali mujh ko karam hain
Ghulami ka sila hai aur main hun

Bhala ab bhi na ho is dar se nisbat
Gham-e-Aal-e-Aba hai aur main hun

Adawat ki saza pate hain dushman
Muhabbat ki jaza hai aur main hun

Mera kasa hai aur Bab-e-Ali hai
Faqirana sada hai aur main hun

Udhar bimar-e-paband-e-salasil
Idhar aah-o-buka hai aur main hun
(Bashukriya shair Advocate Mahbub Raza Rizvi o Maulana Shafiq Rizvi)

Jo maratib hain Muhammad ke Khuda ke samne

Jo maratib hain Muhammad ke Khuda ke samne
Sab wo rutbe hain Ali ke Mustafa ke samne

Kaisi iza naz' ki, hum marte marte khush rahe
Apna dam nikla kiya Mushkil-kusha ke samne

Aate hi Haider ke yun chup ho gaye kyun ay malak
Ab nahin kuchh bolte Sher-e-Khuda ke samne

Apna banda dekh, ham donon mein kehta hai kise
Ae Naseeri aa chalein, tere Khuda ke saamne

Bhej de dozakh mein ya jannat mein, jo kuch hashr ho
Banda Haider hun, keh dunga Khuda ke saamne

Shimr se kehti thin Zainab chheen mat chadar meri
Kya kahega hashr mein, tu Mustafa ke saamne

Mal ke chehre par lahu Asghar ka kehte the Hussain
Main isi surat se jaunga Khuda ke saamne

Ay markaz-e-pas zindabad ay mimbar ke parcham
Karmin Bahari / Dar Lakhnavi

Ay markaz-e-iman zindabad ay sabr ke parcham zindabad
Har ashk tapak kar kehta hai Shabbir ka matam zindabad

Insan ka bhala kya zikr koi, karte hain farishte bhi sajde
Ay fakhr-e-malaik zindabad ay nazish-e-Adam zindabad

Har qaum mein matam hota hai, Shabbir ka gham yaad aata hai
Dil khinchte hain sab ke teri taraf ay mah-e-Muharram zindabad

Hindu ho ke Muslim ya Momin, jalwon se nazar hatti hi nahin
Ay shama-e-haqiqat zindabad ay nur-e-do-alam zindabad

Shabbir, ho Bharat ya Kashmir, Iran ki zamin ya Pakistan
Har mulk pe qabza tera hai ay Fateh-e-Azam zindabad

Kalma to hai Nana ka lekin, bajta hai nawase ka danka
Ae maqsad-e-Khaliq zindabad, ae raaz-e-do aalam zindabad

Bhai ki tarah se tum bhi to, iman ka sahara ho Zainab
Ismat ki rida ke saye mein, ae dusri Maryam zindabad

Shabbir teri ulfat ke liye, Islam ki koi shart nahin
Jhukhti hai jabin Noor aksar, ae qibla-e-aalam zindabad
