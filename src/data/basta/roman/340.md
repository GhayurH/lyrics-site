Mere Baba... mere Baba......

Bhaiya jab mara gaya aap kahan the Baba
Tir Asghar ke laga aap kahan the Baba
Qatl Abbas hua aap kahan the Baba
Mere Baba mere Baba

Sab ye kehte hain ke qismat aur pani
Band karta hai kahan kaun kisi par pani
Ye sitam kam na hua aap kahan the Baba
Mere Baba mere Baba

Haye bachchon ke liye jang jo Abbas ne ki
Aisi bahaduri shaqi na saki
Ek ek bazu kata aap kahan the Baba
Mere Baba mere Baba

Aaya Asghar sar-e-maidan to hua aur sitam
Sukhe honton pe zaban apne phirai jis dam
Tir jab us ke laga aap kahan the Baba
Mere Baba mere Baba

Mera walid Husain jab nahin mila Baba
Lashe pe pamal kiya aap kahan the Baba
Mere Baba mere Baba

Baad-e-neza hamara koi waris na raha
Ashqiya aa gaye khaimon mein hua hashr bapa
Jab chhini meri rida aap kahan the Baba
Mere Baba mere Baba

Sun ke faryad bap ne meri beti mat ro
Aazmaish ye hai hum sab ki abhi sabr karo
Dil magar kehta raha aap kahan the Baba
Mere Baba mere Baba

Main to har gam pe tha sath tumhare beti
Chup tha bas marzi-e-mabud ke mare beti
Tum ne ye kaise kaha aap kahan the Baba
Mere Baba mere Baba
