Rubaiyat

Rubai

Dus din ye wo hain ke nauhagar hai Zahra
Thame hue hathon se jigar hai Zahra
Kya pith ho sar pe khak urao logo
Kal Sham se khole sar hai Zahra
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed Muhtasham Naqvi, Amir Naqvi)

Rubai

---***---

Man kehti thi kya malal jhele honge
Bunis nahin sath kis ke khele honge
Ye raat andheri aur darawna jangal
Asghar mere qabr mein akele honge

Rubai

---***---

Hairat mein hun kyun jahan mein aaya pani
Darya mein hai kis liye samaya pani
Ab lakh baras abr barse to kya
Shabbir ne marte dam na paya pani

Rubai

---***---

Phir chand Muharram ka nazar aaya hai
Phir josh pe abr-e-chashm-e-tar aaya hai
Kya khub nasib hain azadaron ke
Farzand-e-Rasul un ke ghar aaya hai
(Bashukriya shair Khurshid Abbas, Farwat Masud, Naujawanan)

Raghib Muradabadi

---***---

Ghairon se bhi kya faiz koi pata hai?
Apna hi to apnon pe taras khata hai
Dar-pesh ho jab bhi koi mushkil Raghib
Be-sakhta lab pe Ya Ali aata hai

Rubai

---***---

Kya pyas thi jis se sara lashkar tarpa
Kya zakhm-e-sinan tha jis se Akbar tarpa
Machhli bhi na tarpe kabhi yun khushki mein
Jis tarah se tir kha ke Asghar tarpa

Mir Anees

---***---

Abid ko dawa aur na ghiza dete hain
Sota hai to zanjir hila dete hain
Sadat ko qaid is mahine mein kiya
Qaidi ko Muharram mein chhura dete hain
(Bashukriya shair o sozkhwan Sardar Abbas Muhtasham o Mukhtar Husain)

Rubai

---***---

Rishta gham-e-Sarwar se laga rakha hai
Bajuz Panjtan Pak ke, kya rakha hai
Hum mar gaye hote gham-e-Sarwar ki qasam
Is marsiyakhwani ne jila rakha hai
