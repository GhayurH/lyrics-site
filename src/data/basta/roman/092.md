Rubaiyat

Mir Anees

Mazlum na Shah-e Bahr o bar, sa hoga
Menh tiron ka yun kisi pe bura hoga!
Pyase rahe Karbala mein jis tarah Husain
Yun qabr bhi pani ko na tarsa hoga
(Kutub: Nau Atash labist jinhon ne khud raw pani ko haram qarar de rakha hai)

Rubai

Haider ki fazilat ka bayan hai ab tak
Tarikh-e zamana nigran hai ab tak
jis dar se gayin Bint-e Asad Kaaba mein
Diwar mein is dar ka nishan hai ab tak

Rubai

Hasil jise Aqa ki huzuri ho jaye
Duniya ke gham o ranj se duridi ho jaye
Ay Sall-e-Ala-e Ali Majlis-e par nur-e Husain
Nari bhi yahan aaye to nuri ho jaye

Mir Anees

Bulbul ko gul pasand gulon ko hua pasand
Hum bu Turabiyon ko hai khak-e shifa pasand
Ye apni apni waz hai ay sakinān-e-dost
Tum ko Iram pasand hamein Karbala pasand

Rubai

Hukm-e hakim hai kisi ka na rahe sar baqi
Aaj duniya mein na Haider ka rahe ghar baqi
Wo reh-e Haq pe mate par na mita dar un ka
Lafz ”Haider“ ye batata hai ke hai dar baqi

Mir Anees

Mar jo farzand to kya chara hai
Han marg, ilaj-e dil-e sad para hai
Asghar ko lita qabr mein kehte the Husain
Aram karo ab yahi gahwara hai
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed Ansar Husain Zaidi)

Mir Anees

Maidan mein koi jane wala na raha
Aur koi gala katane wala na raha
Jo mara gaya is ko utha laye Husain
Shabbir ka koi lane wala na raha

Mirza Adib

Jab naam-e-Ali munh se nikal jata hai
Har koi musibat se to mil jata hai
Kya naam hai is naam ke sadqe ho Mirza
Karta hua insan sambhal jata hai
