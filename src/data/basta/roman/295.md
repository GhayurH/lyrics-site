Sharbat-e-talkh se Shabbir ne wo kulfat pai — jari

4
Kehte kehte ye sukhan, rukh pe zardi chhai
Nigah-e-yas ne bahnon pe qayamat dhai
Phaila ke hath bhai se bhai lipat gaya
Abhi milne bhi na payin ke hichki aai

Tasht ki samt jo munh uth ke jhukaya afsos
Ghatte ho ho ke kaleje se nikal aaya afsos

5
Lakht-e-dil munh se ugalne lage paiham jo Hasan
Peet kar rone lagi ye bhi behen wo bhi behen
Sar jhukaye hue rote the Shah-e-tishna-dahan
Bhar gaya chhote bare kitne hi tukron se lagan

Dil-e-sad paash ke kitne ginta koyi kiunkar tukre
Aai awaz-e-jigar ke hain bahattar tukre

6
Ghar se Masum ka tabut jo bahar nikla
Jama Ahl-e-Aza bhi gale sar nikla
Qurban jaun dekhiye ye Mujtaba ka hal
Ummid ab nahin hai bachche Fatima ka lal

Kuchh kiya shor ke soyenge na sone denge
Qabr-e-Ahmad ke qarib dafn na hone denge

7
Le chale ghar ko jo wapas wo janaza nashad
Faryad hai tabah hui Aal-e Mustafa
Ya Nabi ummat-e-marhum ki dekho bedad
Kya bura lal Hasan tha, na tumhari aulad

Ek aan Sadme se nahin bhai sambhalte hain
Reh reh ke ab kaleje ke tukre nikalte hain

8
Le ke tabut ko kunba jo lipat kar aaya
Pit kar Zainab-o-Kulsum ne ghash khaya
Kis ko gardun ne bajuz in ye din dikhlaya
Arsh tharane laga, ruh-e-Amman chillaya

Aao ay Amman, ek gham taza dekho
Ghar mein aaya hai jo wapas wo janaza dekho

Sharbat-e-talkh se Shabbir ne wo kulfat pai
