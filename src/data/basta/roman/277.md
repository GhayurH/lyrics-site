Han dosto koi na hua ab shor-o-shain mein

1
Han dosto kami na ho ab shor o shain mein
Zainab bhi hain azaye Shah-e marshaqin mein
Phat jayen dil wo dard hai khaya hai bain mein
Lo Fatima bhi aa gayin bazm-e Husain mein

Chehre pe khak bal pareshan kiye hue
Godi mein ek nanha sa lasha liye hue

2
Goya ye keh rahi hain Batul-e falak maqam
Ay be-watan ke taziya-daro, mera salam
Matlab ye hai ke aaj Majalis huin tamam
rukhsat talab hain Ahl-e aza, Madar-e Imamam-e

Dil par hujm-e gham hai aur ranj o malal ka
Pursa Batul-e Pak ko do un ke lal ka

3
Ro lo ke ab ye tazkira-e gham bhi khatm hai
Fasla-e azaye Sarwar-e alam bhi khatm hai
Matam karo ke aaj ye matam bhi khatm hai
Majlis bhi marsiya bhi Muharram bhi khatm hai

Haider bhi sab ke sath mein ansu bahate hain
Han ab Husain taziya-khane se jate hain

4
Ay rone walo ankhon se daryaye khun bahao
Matam karo Husain ka aur sar pe khak urao
Farsh-e aza lipti ke rakho alam uthao
Das din ka mehman chala taziya uthao

Ashkon ko nazr de ke Imamam-e anmam ko
Rukhsat karo Husain alaihi Assalam ko

5
Ro kar kaho ke Ay Shah-e abrarar alwida
Ay karwan-e dard ke salar alwida
Ay ummat-e-Rasul ke ghamkhwar alwida
Karik jawan pisar ke azadar alwida

kya jane agle sal jiyen hue, marenge hue hum
Par qabr mein bhi taziya dari karen hue hum

6
Ay be diyar o be sar o saman alwida
Ay ummat-e Rasulalwida-e ke mehman alwida
Haider ki ruh Fatima ki jaan alwida
Ay Mustafa ki god ke Quran alwida

Ashre ke khatm hote hi hum se bichhar gaye
Maula tumhare taziya mein ujar gaye

Han dosto koi na hua ab shor-o-shain mein
(Nasim Amrohvi)
