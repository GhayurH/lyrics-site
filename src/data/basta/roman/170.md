[Zainab jo nange paon khari thi qarib-e-dar — jari]

4
Chillaye-e godiyon ke tale hath de ke Shah
Munh se milao munh ke mera hal hai tabah
Ankhon mein dam tha bole kyun kar wo rashk-e mah-e
Rote the nazbaz dekh ke Abbas Arsh jah-e-Muhammad

Duniya se koch karte the bachche pale hue
Hathon mein the Husain ke mein dhale hue

5
Kuchh kuchh abhi tha dam ke unhein le chale Imam
Ek lash ko to aap liye the Shah-e Imam
Aur ek lash thas Hazrat-e Abbas-e Hind naam
Khaima tha samne ke bizaat hui tamam

Jite na pahunche haye ghazab khaima gah mein
Donon saghir mar gaye ansaye rah mein

6
Chillaye ro ke Hazrat-e Abbas-e namwar
Chhuta tamam ho gaya Shah-e Bahr o bar
Hazrat pukare hath pe kaleja pe mar kar
Hai hai bura bhi mar gaya Viran hua ye ghar

Man muntazir hai chak gareban kiye hue
Kis munh se jaun khaime mein lashe liye hue

[Makhaz: Zainab jo nange paon khari thi qarib-e-dar]

Rukhsat hua Husain se jab naujawan pisar

1
Rukhsat hua Husain se jab naujawan pisar
Kuchh dur sath sath chale Shah-e Bahr o bar
Tarik tha zamana kuchh aata na tha nazar
Patti bandhi thi shafqat-e fatrai ki ankh par

Har Rukn ki thi fikr Shah-e marshaqin ko
Ruh-e Khalil dekh rahi thi Husain ko

2
Larta raha jawan pisar dekhte rahe
Tanha pe sikanron ki nazar dekhte rahe
Baran-e-tegh o tir o tabar dekhte rahe
Dil tharthara raha tha magar dekhte rahe

Akbar pe war parte the jab fauj-e-Sham ke
Reh jate the Husain kaleje ko tham ke

3
Hasrat se ran mein jang pisar dekhte the Shah
Zainab khari thin khaime ke dar par ba ashk o aah
Dil par the hath chehr Shabbir par nigah
Parhti thin roye Shah-e mein ahwal-e razm gah

Jab dekhti thin bhai ko khushkhsh Muskurati thin
Parti thi jab jabin pe shakn thas kanp jati thin
