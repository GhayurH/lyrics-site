[Jab hua Yusuf-e-Zahra ka safar Yasrib se — jari]

4
Bole peshani pe munh rakh ke Imam do jahan
Kya khafa ho gayin Baba se tum Ay rahat-e jaan
Ja ke Kufe mein agar maut ne di hum ko aman
Wada karte hain ke hum tum ko bula len hue wahan

Der mein pahunche sawari to na ghabrana tum
Sath nani ke meri jaan chali aana tum

5
Baten karte the ye Sughra se Shah kaun o makan
Aaye jo itne mein hamshakh-e Rasul-e do jahan
Dekh kar bhai ko Boli wo ba-sad aah o fughan
Aao Bhaiya Ali Akbar mein tumhare qurban

Apna kuchh bas nahin Baba to hamein chhor chale
Tum bhi kya khwahar-e nashad se munh mor chale

6
Hum to mehman koi din ke hain na jao bhai
Ab yaqin hai ke hamein aa ke na pao bhai
Lo mujhe zazfal-e samn bu ki sogwar bhai
Lun balayen mein zara pas to aao bhai

Alam-e Mariz-e-hijr mein duniya se safar karte hain
Koi hasrat to na reh jaye ke hum marte hain

7
Khak par Gar ke tarapne lagi wo khasta-tan jigar
Roye bimar ko sine se laga kar Sarwar
Dil jo bhar aaya Sakina ka pukari ro kar
Apna kuchh bas nahin Allah nigahban khwahar

Chhut ke hum tum se ba-sad ranj o alam jate hain
Lo bahen aa ke gale mil lo ke hum jate hain

[Bashukriya sozkhwan Professor Syed Ali Zakir o Tanzim Naqvi]
