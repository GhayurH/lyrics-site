[Jab zakhm kha ke Bint-e-Ali ke pisar gire — jari]

4
Shane hila hila ke pukare Shah-e hada
Mamun nisar hun mein aao to ek zara
Kuchh hal-e dil sunate hue jao mein fida
Ye sun ke donon sheron ne ki ankhen nim wa

Dekha rukh-e Imam to ghabra ke reh gaye
Yaad aa gayi wo baat ke tharra ke reh gaye

5
Maula ne pyar se kaha hai thas ye kya kya
Larzan hain jism kis ka tumhein dar hai mein fida
Shah ke qadam pakar ke pukare wo mah-e mah-liqa
Dharaka hamein ye hai kahin Amman na hun khafa

Zahmat hui Imam-e falak bargah ko
Baskin na wo ke hum ne bulaya hai Shah ko

[Iltimas Surah Fatiha]

Zainab jo nange paon khari thi qarib-e-dar

1
Zainab jo nange paon khari thi qarib-e dar
Abbas ja ke dete the ye dam ba dam khabar
Roin na ab Huzur thas muhim ho chuki hai sar
Lakhon se lar rahe hain aap ke pisar

Zor aaj phir dikha diya khair-e kashais ka
Ab to unhein hai hath mein maidan-e larai ka

2
Ye zikr tha ke Hazrat ko di sada
Abbas aao ghar gaye Zainab ke dil dilruba
Daure sada ye sunte hi Abbas bawafa
Ne rah mein Ali Akbar ne ye kaha

Zinda rakha falak ne thas ye ansu bahane ko
Chaliye Huzur jate hain lashe uthane ko

3
Pyari bahen ke beton ka dekha jo Shah ne hal
Tan chak chak hai thas gul-e sad barg ki Misal
Uljhe hain Bech khaye hue gesuon ke bal
Idhar hain hont chand se chehre lahu se lal

Mathe hain shaqq saron se Amama bhi dur hain
Sab chhoti chhoti palsayan tahigon se chur hain
