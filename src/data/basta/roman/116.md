Salam

Jab koi tir-e-sitam sine pe khate hain Husain
Pir-e-Tariqat Sufi Jamil Shah Warsi

Jab koi tir-e-sitam sine pe khate hain Husain
Dekh kar pair-e falak ko muskurate hain Husain
Mustaqqat karne ko murushi khazna alam ka
Hazrat-e Abid ko sine se lagate hain Husain

Sabr ki talqin farmate hain Ahl-e-Bait ko
Nashish-e Hazrat Ali Asghar jo late hain Husain
Hai wohi rahmat mizaji apne Nana ki tarah
Zulm karte hain lain aur bhul jate hain Husain

Qabr se utha to aai ye Nida roz-e nashur
Chal tujhe wo sar-e mahshar bulate hain Husain

Taziyat ki jaye is mauqa pe ya dein tahniyat
Hazrat Qasim ko ab dulha banate hain Husain
Ho karam ya Shah-e-Mardan andar ya Fatir
Hazrat Abbas ke lashe pe jate hain Husain

Pardes Zainab jao bachcho Shah ki nusrat karo
Lautne Akbar ko kandhe par uthate hain Husain
Ya ilahi rahm kar na kuchh na phans jayen bibiyan
Aakhri rukhsat ko ab khaime mein aate hain Husain

Tere bachchon ka khila duniya Zainab khatm alsar
Maulana Zafar Ali Khan zehra

Tere bachchon ka khula duniya Zainab Khatam-ur-Rusul
aur sawari Shah-e khatm-e Mursalan, Fatima
[jari safha 117]

Hai yahi waqt un ka daman tham le
Allama Rashid Turabi

Hai yahi waqt un-e ka daman tham le
Girne wale ab Ali ka naam le
Marhubon ke sar pe hai tegh-e Ali
apna badla, Subh le ya Sham le

Hai Abuzar se wala to apne sar
Haq-parasti ka bhi ek ilzam le
be-murawwat admi mazaruz hai
Jis tarah jo mani-e Islam le

Dafn-e Asghar ho gaye Shah ne kaha
Mere bachche ab yahan aram le
Asr-e-Ashur aai awaz-e-Rasul
Fatima bazu se Zainab tham le

Qaid mein mein koi bachchi hai dafn
ek amanat aur mulk-e Sham le
Ay Turabi muft hai aab-e hayat
maut ke hathon se koi jam le
