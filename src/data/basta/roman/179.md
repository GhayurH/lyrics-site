Jab lash-e-pisar ko uthaya Husain ne

1
Jab lash pisar ko uthaya Husain ne
Sine se lakht-e dil ko lagaya Husain ne
Ankhon se bahr-e-ashk bahaya Husain ne
Ro ro ke ye bahen ko sunaya Husain ne

Tir-e alam jigar pe zafifi mein khaye hain
Mayyat jawan bete ki hum le ke aaye hain

2
ye sun ke Ahl-e bait mein mahshar hua ayan
Daurin hawas hawas-bakhta khaime se Ali bayan
Masnad pe lash-e Shah ne bathai ba-sad fughan
Mayyat pe Gar ke Banu ye karne lagin bayan

Kis ne mita diya mere naam o nishan ko
Kis ki nazar lagi mere karik jawan ko

3
Awaz ek samt se aai hal-e zar
Jald Aaiye Imam-e sher-e Kirdigar
sunte hi is-e sada ke hue Shah be qarar
Khaime ke dar pe aaye jo Maula-e-Ali Namdar

Dekha ke ek shakhs khara hai hawas hai
Naqa pe wo sawar hai Lekin udas hai

4
Shah ne kaha tu kaun hai ay gham ke mubtala
Ye sun ke Itra naqa se wo mard-e bawafa
Ki arz mein hun Khadam-e aulad-e Murtaza
Shah-e Shah-e-talab ne bheja hai agah hai Khuda

Deta hai jaan mushkil-e-Payambar ke waste
Kuchh tahfe is ne Bheje hain Akbar ke waste

5
Ro kar kaha Husain ne Akbar to mar gaya
Tahfe ka lene wala jahan se guzar gaya
Hum se bichhar ke aaj wo Dada ke ghar gaya
Piri mein wo jawan hamein barbad kar gaya

Sunta hai to jo khaime mein Ghul o shain ka
Matam ye ho raha hai isi nur-e ein ka

6
Daulat hamari lut gayi atharah sal ki
Mayyat pari hai khaime mein Banu ke lal ki
khun mein bhari hai shakl mere mah-e jamal ki
Shadi bhi na pai is khush khasal ki

Shah-e Shah-e-talab se ja ke to izhar kijiyo
Un-e tahfum par to Fatiha Akbar ka dijiyo
