10
Us ne kaha yahi mere Baba hain, main fida
Main dasht mein pukari phirti thi ja baja
Baba kidhar hain, koi bata do mujhe Khuda
Is lash ne pukara ke beti idhar ko aa

Is ko miri fughan se aziat bari hui
Main peetne lagi to ye laash uth khari hui

Namud-e-Sham-e-Ghariban hujum-e-afat hai

1
Namud-e-Sham-e-Ghariban hujum-e-afat hai
Qadam qadam par naya gham nayi musibat hai
Imam hain na Alamdar behr-e-nusrat hai
Faqat jale hue khaime hain aur Itrat hai

Haram hain fikr-o-taraddud mein sar jhukaye hue
Jo sarparast the sote the sar kataye hue

2
Wo dasht-e-zulm, wo tanhai, wo andheri rat
Wo fikr-o-yas, wo pyaron ke dagh, wo Sadat
Nabi ki ruh hifazat ko, ya Khuda ki zat
Bahen se Zainab-e-dilgir ne kahi ye bat

Haram mein aap rahen yakson ki nusrat ko
Main gird-e-khaima phirun rat bhar hifazat ko

3
Ye bat keh ke chalin Bint-e-Haider-e-Karrar
Zaban par nala-o-faryad hath mein talwar
Haram-sara se jo nikli wo muztar-o-nachar
To dekha aata hai khaime ki samt ek sawar

Pukarin Bint-e-Ali aur samt ja bhai
Ye bargah-e-Nabi hai idhar na aa bhai
