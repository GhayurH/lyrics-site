[Safha 133 se jari]

Gardish-e-dauran ki zanjirein abhi kat jayengi
Ya Ali keh kar to dekho, josh-e-iman ke sath
Hum ghalaman-e Ali ko khauf-e mahshar kis liye
Ya Ali keh kar guzar jayenge asani ke sath
Bhul sakta hi nahin Islam ehsan-e Husain
Mit nahin sakta gham-e Shabbir asani ke sath
Marhaba sabr-e Husain Ibn-e Ali sad Marhaba
Dil ke tukre kar diye qurbani ke sath
Har amal Shabbir ka Tathir-e Quran ban gaya
Har amal marbato tha ayat-e qarani ke sath
Madh-e Ahl-e-Bait ko to karta hai Qamar
Alam o bahr-e-fan ke atarat-e tang damana ke sath

Chali hai rah-o-rasm-e sadaqat Husain ke ghar se
Syed Aqil Abbas Jafari

Chali hai rah-o-rasm-e sadaqat Husain ke ghar se
Jahan ne pai ye daulat Husain ke ghar se
Qiyam-e Haq liye jaan tak lita dena
Pari hai ye bhi riwayat Husain ke ghar se
Jo tujh ko naz hai Islam par to Ay wazi
Tujhe mili hai ye nemat Husain ke ghar se
Janab-e Hurr hi nahin hum ne kitne logon ki
Badlit dekhi hai qismat Husain ke ghar se
Yazid kya tere Ajdad ne Bataya na tha
Abis hai khahshih-e baiat Husain ke ghar se
Mein Hurr ki tarah ghira-e hun yazidiyon mein Aqil
Yahin bhi Rakhta hun nisbat Husain ke ghar se

Karbala walon ke jaise imtihan hote nahin
Akhtar Husain puri

Karbala walon ke jaise imtihan hote nahin
is qadar zulm o sitam Ay asman hote nahin
yaad aata hai hamein karb o bula ka saniha
be-sabab hum raat din waqt-e fughan hote nahin
jaan de di Shah ne Lekin baiat-e fasiq na ki
Din-e ke Shabbir jaise pasban hote nahin
Karbala Jannat nishan Shabbir ke dam se hui
Karbala Jannat nishan warna gulistan hote nahin
Arsh-e de-e ki Khataen tahfaf Jannat bhi de
Hazrat-e Shabbir jaise ban hote nahin
Bhar liya Mashkiza munh ko phir kar Abbas ne
pyas mein aise wafa ke pasban hote nahin
Shimr se kehta koi Abid ko mat bair pehna
Basta zanjir aise natawan hote nahin
Yun zafifi mein jawan ho kar lare hain Ahl-e-Bait
Aise to ahd-e jawani mein jawan hote nahin
