[Safha 133 se jari]

Gardish-e-dauran ki zanjiren abhi kat jayengi
Ya Ali keh kar to dekho, josh-e-imani ke sath
Hum ghulaman-e-Ali ko khauf-e-mahshar kis liye
Ya Ali keh kar guzar jayenge asani ke sath
Bhul sakta hi nahin Islam ehsan-e-Husain
Mit nahin sakta gham-e-Shabbir asani ke sath
Marhaba sabr-e-Husain Ibn-e-Ali sad marhaba
Dil ke tukre kar diye qurbani ke sath
Har amal Shabbir ka tafsir-e-Quran ban gaya
Har amal marbut tha Ayat-e-Qurani ke sath
Madh-e-Ahl-e-Bait ko to karta hai Qamar
Ilm-o-fan ke aitiraf-e-tang-damani ke sath

Chali hai rasm-e-sadaqat Husain ke ghar se
Syed Aqil Abbas Jafari

Chali hai rasm-e-sadaqat Husain ke ghar se
Jahan ne payi ye daulat Husain ke ghar se
Qiyam-e-Haq ke liye jaan tak luta dena
Pari hai ye bhi riwayat Husain ke ghar se
Jo tujh ko naz hai Islam par to ay waiz
Tujhe mili hai ye nemat Husain ke ghar se
Janab-e-Hurr hi nahin hum ne kitne logon ki
Badalti dekhi hai qismat Husain ke ghar se
Yazid kya tere ajdad ne bataya na tha
Abas hai khwahish-e-baiat Husain ke ghar se
Main Hurr ki tarah ghira hun Yazidiyon mein Aqil
Yahin bhi rakhta hun nisbat Husain ke ghar se

Karbala walon ke jaise imtihan hote nahin
Akhtar Husainpuri

Karbala walon ke jaise imtihan hote nahin
Is qadar zulm-o-sitam ay asman hote nahin
Yaad aata hai hamein karb-o-bala ka saniha
Be-sabab hum rat din waqt-e-fughan hote nahin
Jaan de di Shah ne lekin baiat-e-fasiq na ki
Din ke Shabbir jaise pasban hote nahin
Karbala Jannat-nishan Shabbir ke dam se hui
Karbala Jannat-nishan warna gulistan hote nahin
Arsh-e-din ki khatayen tuhfa-e-Jannat bhi de
Hazrat-e-Shabbir jaise mezban hote nahin
Bhar liya mashkiza munh ko pher kar Abbas ne
Pyas mein aise wafa ke pasban hote nahin
Shimr se kehta koi Abid ko mat beri pehna
Basta-e-zanjir aise natawan hote nahin
Yun zaifi mein jawan ho kar lare hain Ahl-e-Bait
Aise to ahd-e-jawani mein jawan hote nahin
