[Ya Ali aaiye zindan mein Haram rote hain — jari]

4
Ya Ali aap ki poti ka ajab hai alam
Sahm jati hai ke aa na Shimr-e azlam
Rasan-e zulm ki tangi se ghatta hai dam
Nanhe hathon se kare bap ka kyun kar matam

Aah jo karti hai Tab Arsh barabin hilta hai
Ya Ali aap ki poti ka gala chhilta hai

Al-Abbas Fatiha
Baraye sozkhwan Imami Sahib

Sar apna pit ke Fizza se Hind ne puchha

1
Sar apna pit ke Fizza se Hind ne puchha
Are bata to sahi kya Husain qatl hua
Jhuji ki khwab mein Zahra ko nange sar dekha
Wohi Husain wohi dukhtar-e Zahra

Ghazab hua Shah-e wala se chhut gayi Zainab
Husain qatl hua aah lut gayi Zainab

2
Pukari Fizza zaban band kar to Ay khush Go
Majal hai kisi ki jo Lote Zainab ko
Zara to Gaur se Ay Bibi Bibi khub tu Socho
Husain qatl hua jis ka bhai ho

Wo Bibi Bibi qaid hua balwe mein jaye babarat hai
Bahen Husain ki ho be-kasi rida qayamat hai

3
Pukari Hind ke achchha na hal batlao
Mein hath jorti hun thori der thehar jao
Kaha kanizon se hakim talak zara jao
Wahan jo tasht mein ek sar hai is ko le aao

Kaho Yazid se wapas main jald kar dungi
Qasam Husain ki is sar ko mein na rakh lun lungi
