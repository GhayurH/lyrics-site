Soz

Jab naujawan ki lash na pai Husain ne
Thokar har ek gam pe khai Husain ne
Gar kar zamin pe khak arain Husain ne
Dil tham kar ye baat sunai Husain ne

Ankhon se sujhta nahin mujh dil malul ko
Ay zalimo thas dikha do Shabih-e-Nabi-e Rasul ko
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Nadim Jamal Zaidi o Hasan Mujtaba)

Shah-e-Mazlum se Abbas ne jis dam alam paya
Sararir-e qadar mein wo ho gaya Jafar ka hum paya
Falak bhi apne pesh-e manzilat Ghazi ne kham paya
Musafir ne nishan-e manzil-e mulk-e adam paya

Kaha bagh-e Iram ki bu abhi se mujh ko aati hai
Isi-e saye tale Khuld-e barabin ko rah jati hai
Falak bhi apne pesh manzil-shanasi ne gham paya

Nikle khaime se jo hathyaar lagaye Abbas
Charh ke rahwar pe maidan mein aaye Abbas
Ghul hua mashk o alam sath hain laye Abbas
Han magar bund bhi pani ki na paye Abbas

Gham hai pyason ka saje jang ke hathyar bhi hain
Larren bhi aaye hain pani ke tabalgar bhi hain
(Bashukriya sozkhwan wasi-e Haider o Wali Haider Zaidi)

Aah hai pyason ka bich jang ke be-qarari hai
Aur tawatar khabar aai ke bahut pas aaye
Pau-e gham Shah-e shadhan se ba-sad yas aaye
Boli taqdir ke ye jang unhein-e ras aaye

Barh ke Ghore ka shujaat ne qadam chum liya
Fath ne gushush daman-e alam chum liya
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Bahadur Husain Rizvi o Syed Ali)

Jab mashk bhar kar nehr se Abbas Ghazi ghar chale
Ek jam Kausar bhar liya aur Khuld se Haider chale
Hamrah Payambar chale thas Hamza chale thas Jafar chale
Maidan ka rasta rokne gaye ke lashkar chale

Aai ghata si Fauj-e kin kale alam khole hue
Abbas jhatapte sher se tegh do dam Tule hue
Hamrah-e-sher chale, Hamza chale, Jafar chale

Shabbar ka mah-e ru tha jalalat mein lajawab
Abru the Zulfiqar to chehra tha aftab
Bheegi huwein masein khabr-e-Aamad-e-ashbab
Tera baras ke sin mein the jaise Abu Turab

Behr-e-jihad surat-e-Haider tane hue
Jannat ke ishtiyaq mein dulha bane hue
