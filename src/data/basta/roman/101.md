Jab naujawan ki lash na pai Husain ne
Thokar har ek gam pe khai Husain ne
Gir kar zamin pe khak urai Husain ne
Dil tham kar ye bat sunai Husain ne

Ankhon se sujhta nahin mujh dil-malul ko
Ay zalimo, dikha do shabih-e-Rasul ko
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Nadim Jamal Zaidi o Hasan Mujtaba)

---***---

Shah-e-Mazlum se Abbas ne jis dam alam paya
Sarir-e-qadr mein wo ho gaya Jafar ka hum-paya
Falak bhi apne pesh-e-manzilat Ghazi ne kham paya
Musafir ne nishan-e-manzil-e-mulk-e-adam paya

Ha bagh-e-Iram ki bu abhi se mujh ko aati hai
Isi saye tale Khuld-e-barin ko rah jati hai
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Zakir Husain o Mahmud Tajali)

---***---

Nikle khaime se jo hathyar lagaye Abbas
Charh ke rahwar pe maidan mein aaye Abbas
Ghul hua mashk-o-alam sath hain laye Abbas
Han magar bund bhi pani ki na paye Abbas

Gham hai pyason ka saje jang ke hathyar bhi hain
Larne bhi aaye hain pani ke talabgar bhi hain
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Wasi Haider o Wali Haider Zaidi)

---***---

Shor hai Sham ke lashkar mein ke Abbas aaye
Aur tawatur khabar aai ke bahut pas aaye
Pau-e-gham Shah-e-Shahidan se ba-sad-yas aaye
Boli taqdir ke ye jang inhein ras aaye

Barh ke ghore ka shujaat ne qadam chum liya
Fath ne gosha-e-daman-e-alam chum liya
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Bahadur Husain Rizvi o Syed Ali)

---***---

Jab mashk bhar kar nahr se Abbas Ghazi ghar chale
Ek jam-e-Kausar bhar liya aur Khuld se Haider chale
Hamrah Payambar chale, Hamza chale, Jafar chale
Maidan ka rasta rokne kuffar ke lashkar chale

Aai ghata si fauj ki kale alam khole hue
Abbas jhapte sher se tigh-e-do-dam tole hue
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Hakim Syed Haider Abbas o Shams-ul-Hasan)

---***---

Shabbar ka mah-e-ru tha jalalat mein lajawab
Abru the Zulfiqar to chehra tha aftab
Bhigi hui masen khabar-e-amad-e-ashbab
Terah baras ke sin mein the jaise Abu Turab

Bahr-e-jihad surat-e-Haider tane hue
Jannat ke ishtiyaq mein dulha bane hue
