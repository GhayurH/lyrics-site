Para hai ghash mein Husain gharib ka jani

1
Para hai ghash mein Husain gharib ka jani
Munh hai khushi mein zulm o jaur ke bani
Dam namaz jo chunka ye Haider-e sani
Wuzu ke waste uth kar talab kiya pani

Jo pani aaya to munh se Ansuon se dhone lage
Phir hi nigahon mein Baba ki pyas rone lage

2
Tarap ke dil ne kaha haye Syed-e khush khu
Yahi to hai jo mila tin din jo bahr-e wuzu
Isi ki chah mein Kausar ko chale paye Amru
Isi ke waste amun ke kat gaye bazu

Yahi to wo hai jo be-kasi sher ne na paya tha
Isi ko mang ke gardan pe tir khaya tha

3
Gharaz ke aaye musalle par aur parhi wo namaz
Ke jis ke baad thi had-e wasal-e naz o naziyaz
Har Ek zikr-e tawil aur har ek sujud-e daraz
Pukarti thi ye ruh-e Husain-e sajda nawaz

Mujhe bhi naz hai jis par yahi wo itaat hai
Ye aakhri mere Sajjad ki ibadat hai

4
Hui tamam jo aakhir-e namaz-e jaan-e Rasulwal
Dua o Hamd ke lab chumta tha Hasan-e qubul
Diya jo shukr ke sajde ko Haq ki yaad mein tul
To Ek Asr ka yaad aaya sajda-e muqubul

Uthe jo rote hue koch kar gaye Sajjad
Kaha Husain Husain aur mar gaye Sajjad

5
Uthai daur ke Baqir ne lash-e ro ro kar
Tamam bibiyan matam mein pitne lagin sar
Mahla-e Bani Hashim mein Ghul macha ghar ghar
Utha jahan se azadar-e Sibt-e Payambar

Phata wo Aal se jo aasra tha ghurbat ka
Bujha chiragh-e Shah-e Karbala ki turbat ka

6
Nabi o Haider o Zahra ki ruh hai nashad
Husain ka hai ye nauha ke haye Ay Sajjad
Batul kehti hain ro ro ke Ya Khuda faryad
Sahe lungi dahr mein kya kya sitam meri aulad

Jafa se qatl jigar o qahr se mara
Mere mariz ko zalim ne zahr se mara

Para hai ghash mein Husain gharib ka jani
Nasim Amrohvi
