Mir Anees

Guzar gaye the kai din ke ghar mein aab na tha
Magar Husain se Sabir ko iztirab na tha
Husain aur talab-e-aab, ay Maaz Allah
Tamam karna thi hujjat, sawal-e-aab na tha
(Iqtibas intikhab az sozkhwan Ustad Ali Khan)

Qita

Sar-e-neza wo farq-e-Shah-e-Huda, balaghal ula bi kamalihi
Gaya Sham hamrah-e-ashqiya, kashafad duja bi jamalihi
Kabhi ki kisi ko na bad-dua, hasunat jamiu khisalihi
To ye Ahl-e-Nifaq ne kaha, sallu alaihi wa aalihi

Qita

Reh gayi dasht mein tanha to watan yaad aaya
Pani jab paya har ek tishna dahan yaad aaya
Le ke har chiz Madine se chali thi Zainab
Lash par bhai ki pahunchi to kafan yaad aaya

Maulana Professor Mirza Muhammad Ashfaq (Shorish Lakhnavi)

Jahan mein pesh-e-beti bap ki taswir karti hai
Bhare bazar mein Kufe ke jab taqrir karti hai
Use bhi chhin le barh kar koi zalim to mein janun
Rukh-e-Zainab ka parda chadar-e Tathir karti hai
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Azhar Husain, Muhammad Ali, Qadri Baloch)

Zawwar Amrohvi

Hazrat-e-Abbas Shah-e-La Fata ke sher hain
Khandaq-o-Khaibar ke wo ye Karbala ke sher hain
Kyun na hun har jang mein ye misl-e-Haider fathyab
Wo Khuda ke sher ye sher-e Khuda ke sher hain

Mir Hasan

Jis ne Shabbir se wafa ki hai
Us ki marzi bhi ab Khuda ki hai
Itni qimat na thi Furat teri
Jitni Abbas ne ada ki hai

Qita

Ali Akbar jo marne ja raha hai
Shabab aa kar bahut pachhta raha hai
Balayen le rahi hain uth ke Zainab
Magar dil hai ke baitha ja raha hai

Hazrat Amir Ali

Shimr fitrat soch jitne tir barsane lagi
Har aza khana pe itni hi bahar aane lagi
Hum ghulaman-e-Ali ko maut ka kya dar ke jab
Ya Ali hum ne kaha aur maut tharrane lagi
