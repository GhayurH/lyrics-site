Rubaiyat

Rubai

Jis ki ein Haram-e Haq mein wiladat ho jaye
Kyun na wo qubal arbab-e iradat ho jaye
Is ki khud apni ibadat ki ada kya hogi
Jis ke chehre pe nazar karna ibadat ho jaye

Hakim Arif Akbarabadi Marhum

Dil thas dil se milaya hai Talib ne
Kya rang jamaya hai Talib ne
Is aqd ke bare mein bhi kuchh lab kholo
Jo aqd parhaya hai Talib ne

Ghulam Aal-e-Aba Shair Naqvi

Maddah-e Panjtan ka dam bharte hain
Duniya ki musibaton se kab darte hain
Hai Chadar-e-Tathir ka saya hum par
Shabbir ka marsiya parha karte hain
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Khurshid Haider Naqvi)

Ghulam Aal-e-Aba Shair Naqvi

Iman ki taswir nazar aati hai
Quran ki tafsir nazar aati hai
Allah! tere ghar ki faza Ay Zahra
Tathir hi Tathir nazar aati hai

Mir Anees

Ay ahl-e-matam-e aza thas aza ke din aa pahunche
Gham ki Raten thas buka ke din aa pahunche
Faryad ke Fatima ki basti ujri
Abadi-e Karbala ke din aa pahunche

Rubai

Duniya se chala le ke jo naam-e-Haider
Jannat ko chala mera salam-e-Haider
Roka jo raqibon ne to Rizwan ne kaha
Aane do ise ye hai ghulam-e-Haider
