Dushman Muawiya hua Shah-e-Hasan ka jab

1
Dushman-e mawaiya hua Shah-e Hasan ka jab
Sakit wo chand roz raha phir ghazab
Marwan tha Madine ka hakim, adu-e-Rab
Bhijwaya is ko zahr likhe waqiat sab

Takid thi ke bughz hai Shabbir ki zat se
Khilwao zahr dukhtar-e se ke hath se

2
Is-e qahr ka wo zahr tha afat ka tha asar
Dam bhar mein tukre tukre hua Shah ka jigar
Ghul par gaya Hasan ka zamane se hai safar
Haider ka sabz posh tarapta hai farsh par

Zahra ki betiyon pe musibat phir aa gayi
Ghar lut raha hai azim-e Firdaus-e bhai hai

3
Chillate the Husain ke hai hua ye kya
Zainab pukarti thi are kis ne dua kya
Nagah tasht-e Sibt-e Nabi ne talab kya
The ho gayi shuru hui siwa

Afsos ye silkon Shah-e Hind khu ke sath
Girne lage kaleje ke tukre lahu ke sath
