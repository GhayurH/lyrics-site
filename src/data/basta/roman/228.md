4
Ruka na phir bhi jo shahsur-e Hind anjam
Ghazab mein gayin Tab khwahar-e Imamhi-e anmam
[Do misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]

Lagam pakri jo is sogwar ne barh kar
Gale mein dal din banhein sawar ne barh kar

5
Tarap ke surat-e basmal wo jaan khone laga
jigar larazne laga be qarar hone laga
Gham-e Hussainhi mein ashkon se munh dhone laga
Jhuka ke dosh par sar zar zar rone laga

Kaha na ro ke Khuda ka Wali hun Ay beti
Mein gham gham-zada tera Baba Ali hun Ay beti

6
Pukarin haye lita ghar na li khabar Baba
Bichhar gaya mera Akbar khabar na li Baba
Guzar gaya Ali Asghar khabar na li Baba
Kata Hussainhi ka sar khabar na li Baba

Haram ka lut gaya zewar madad na ki tum ne
Chhini ridaen jala ghar madad na ki tum ne

Shami jo shama-e-din-e-ilahi bujha chuke

1
Shami jo shama-e-din-e-ilahi bujha chuke
Nari khiyam-e Aal-e Nabi jala chuke
Malun sinan par sar-e Shuhada ke charha chuke
Be-din Nabi ke kunbe ko qaidi bana chuke

Tha bughz aisa Aal-e risalat-e Panah se
Be-warison ko le chale maqtal ki rah se

2
jab darmiyan ganj-e shahidan hua guzar
Aaye nazar azizon ke lashe lahu mein tar
Sadme se be-kason ke larazne lage jigar
Unton se Ahl-e-Bait gire kanp kanp kar

Dar pesh-e tha Faraqaq-e jo un dil khak-nashinon se
Lipata liya shahidon ke lashon ko sinon se

3
Lipti thi apne bete ke lashe se koi man
Koi bahen thi bhai ke lashe pe nim jaan
Darya ki samt thi koi zar-o-hazin rawan
Koi pukarti thi mera bap hai kahan

Ek sogwar khak urati thi dasht mein
Kuchh dhundti hui chali jati thi dasht mein
