Rubai

Rubai

Haider ki ata pe Hal ata Shahid hai
Aur tegh zanin pe Fata Shahid hai
Kaabe mein wiladat ke Payambar hain gawah
Masjid ki shahadat pe Khuda shahid hai
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Zahid Husain o Muzaffar Husain Zaidi)

Mir Anees

Ay badshah-e kaun o makan kaun-e
Ay aqda kashayaye do jahan akkinkir-e
Ab tang hai dushmanon ke hathon se Anees
Ya Hazrat-e Sahib alman akkinkir-e
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Nasir Abbas Naqvi o Javed Ali Zaidi)
