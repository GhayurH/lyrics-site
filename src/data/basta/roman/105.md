Soz

Sirhane lash-e-Hurr ke ye tana Shimr ne ki
Saza muhabbat-e-Ibn-e-Ali ki khub mili
Tabah ghar bhi kiya, muft tu ne jaan bhi di
Sada ye lash ne di dur ho tu, yan se shaqi

Ali Imam-e-man ast o manam ghulam-e-Ali
Hazar jaan-e-girami fida-e-naam-e-Ali
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Sabir Husain Zaidi / Karbalai Najaf Ali Karaji)

Nikle Haram ke unt jo maqtal ki rah se
Boli Sakina milte chalen lash-e-Shah se
Khushbu lahu ki aane lagi qatl-gah se
Rukhsat zarur chahiye Zahra ke mah se

ji bhar ke aaj khana-e zindan mein Roin hue
Ab kahe ko Husain ke sine pe soenge
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed Aal-e-Rasul)

Mohsin Naqvi Shahid

Ek ek karke bichharte the jab Ansar-e-Husain
Aasra koi zaifi ka, koi ruh ka chain
Ye jawan lash, wo kamsin, to udhar rahat-e-ain
Hichkiyan wo kisi bachchi ki, kisi man ke wo bain

Zindagi dard se bas dida-e-tar jaisi thi
Asr-e-Ashur qiyamat ki sahar jaisi thi

So gaye jab sabhi ashab, sar-e-dasht-e-bala
Akbar-o-Qasim-o-Abbas hue Shah pe fida
Kho gaye Aun-o-Muhammad, Ali Asghar bhi chala
Aaye maqtal mein Husain Ibn-e-Ali bahr-e-dua
Shukr karte, paye sajda kabhi jhuk jate the
Su-e-khaima kabhi barhte, kabhi ruk jate the

Ye kaun si mastura hai kya mang rahi hai
Islam ki khidmat ka sila mang rahi hai
Har sahib-e-ghairat se rida mang rahi hai
Pardes mein marne ki dua mang rahi hai

Afsurda hain aflak siyah-posh zamin hai
Jibrail zara dekh, ye Zainab to nahin hai
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Mahmud Akhtar Zaidi / Haidari)

Ali ki betiyan zindan mein jab asir huin
Imam-zadiyan hakim ki dastagir huin
Musibaten Haram-e-Shah par kasir huin
Gham-e-Husain mein Syedaniyan faqir huin

Kai dinon se mayassar na ab-o-dana tha
Andheri raat thi tuta sa qaid-khana tha
(Bashukriya shair o sozkhwan Ustad Syed Ali Shafiq Akbarabadi)
