Soz

Mohsin Naqvi Shahid

Ek ek karke bikharte the jab Ansar-e Husain
Aasra koi zaifi ka thas koi ruh ka chain
Ye jawan lash thas wo kamsin thas to idhar rahat-e ein
Hichkiyan wo kisi bachchi ki thas kisi man ke wo bain

Zindagi dard se bas dida-e-tar jaisi thi
Asr-e-Ashur qiyamat ki sahar jaisi thi

Sirhane-e lash Hurr ke ye tana Shimr ne ki
Saza muhabbat-e Ibn-e Ali ki khub mili
Tabah ghar bhi kya thas maffat to ne jaan bhi di
Sada ye lash ne di dur ho to thas Yan se shaqi

Ali Imam-e Man ast o matam ghulam-e Ali
Hazar jaan-e garmami fadaye naam-e Ali
Ghar bhi kya, saf tu ne jan bhi di

Nikle haram ke unt jo maqtal ki rah se
Boli Sakina mile chale lash-e-Shah se
Khushbu lahu ki aane lagi qatl-gah se
Rukhsat zarur chahiye Zahra ke mah se

Ji bhar ke aaj khana-e-zindan mein roenge
Ab ka hai wo Husain ke sine pe soenge
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed Aal-e-Rasul)

Ye kaun si mastura hai kya mang rahi hai
Islam ki khidmat ka sila mang rahi hai
Har Sahib-e ghairat se rida mang rahi hai
Pardes mein marne ki dua mang rahi hai

Afsardat hain afkal si posh zamin hai
Jabrib zara dekh thas ye Zainab to nahin hai
Har sahib-e-ghairat se rida mang rahi hai

Ali ki betiyan zindan mein jab asir huin
Imam Rasulzadiyan hakim ki dastigat huin
Masibatin Haram-e Shah par kaisari huin
Gham-e-Husain mein Syedaniyan faqir huin

Kai dinon se mayassar na ab o dana tha
Andheri raat thi tuta sa qaid khana tha
(Bashukriya shair o sozkhwan Ustad Syed Ali Shafiq Akbarabadi)
