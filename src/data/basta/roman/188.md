[Shabbir jabke ran se chale suye khaima-gah — jari]

4
Ye raat ke andhere, ye sahra, abhi bhi jao
Jangal mein hum gharib hain tanha, abhi bhi jao
Bhaiya bilak rahi hai Sakina, abhi bhi jao
Kya aaj raat doge na pahra, abhi bhi jao

Kehte the har bala se mujhe tum bachao ge
Kya qaid ho ke jaungi, us waqt aaoge

Likha hai jab koi hami na Shah-e-din ka raha

1
Likha hai jab koi hami na Shah-e-din ka raha
Aur aap zulm ki faujon mein rah gaye tanha
Hujum-e-yas ne charon taraf se gher liya
To nagahan dar-e-khaima se aai ran mein sada

Khabar lo jald Shabbir, Karbala-e-Zahra hai
Tumhare bachche ko jhule mein nind aai hai

2
Ye sun ke khaime ki janib gaye Imam-e-Huda
Qarib jhule ke pahunche to ro ke farmaya
Maaf kijiye, pidar ko ay beta
Ke ek pani ka qatra tumhein pila na saka

Khuda gawah bahut tum se sharm-sar hun main
Yaqin karo Ali Asghar, ke be-qarar hun main

3
Ye keh ke roye bahut aur pisar ko pyar kiya
Utha ke jhule se Hazrat ne apna mah-liqa
Palak pare the jo chehre pe ashk-bar, hata
Wo muskura ke bachche ne munh ko khol diya

Tari jo ashkon ki pani to muskurane laga
Zaban-e-khushk ko honton pe wo phirane laga
