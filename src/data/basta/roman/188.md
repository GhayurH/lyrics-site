[Makhaz: Shabbir jabke ran se chale su-e-khaima-gah]

Hazrat Imam Shafii
Kafa fi fazli maulana Aliyyin
Wuq'u ash-shakki fihi annahu Allah
Wa mata ash-Shafii wa laysa yadri
Aliyyun rabbuhu am rabbuhu Allah

Likha hai jab koi hami na Shah-e-din ka raha

1
Likha hai jab koi hami na Shah-e-din ka raha
Aur aap zulm ki faujon mein reh gaye tanha
Hujum-e-yas ne charon taraf se gher liya
To nagahan dar-e-khaima se aai ran mein sada

Khabar lo jald Shah-e-Karbala duhai hai
Tumhare bachche ko jhule mein nind aai hai

2
Ye sun ke khaime ki janib gaye Imam-e-Huda
Qarib-e-jhule ke pahunche to ro ke farmaya
Maaf kijiyo be-kas pidar ko ay beta
Ke ek pani ka qatra tumhein pila na saka

Khuda gawah tum se sharmsar hun main
Yaqin karo Ali Asghar ke be-qarar hun main

3
Ye keh ke roye bahut aur pisar ko pyar kiya
Uthaya jhule se Hazrat ne apna mah-liqa
Tapak pare jo chehre par ashk-e-Shah-e-Huda
Wo samjha pani hai munh ko khol diya

Tari jo ashkon ki pai to muskurane laga
Zaban-e-khushk ko honton pe wo phirane laga
