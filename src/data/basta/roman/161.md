[Safha 160 se jari]

4
Shah rone lage Zainab ko jo dekha be-tab
Shahr-e Banu ne kya Shah se ro kar ye khitab
Haq mein laundi ke hai kya hukm, Shah-e Arsh Janab
Aap farmaen to pakro Ghore ki rikab

Yun gawara hai mujhe faqa pe faqa Sahib
Aap ke dam se mein rakhti hun ilaqa Sahib

5
Ro ke farmane laga Haider-e-Karrar ka lal
Ran mein jab hue lungi khiti mere jad ki pamal
Banu tujh ko hai abis apni tabahi ka khayal
Jo meri bahnon ka ahwal wohi tera hal

Baiwa hona hai tujhe aur mujhe be-sar hona
Bint-e Zahra ki itaat se na bahar hona

[Makhaz: Jis ghari nahr pe khaime Shah-e-wala ke hue]

jis dam suni Imam ammam ne sadaye Hurr

1
Jis dam suni Imam-e ammam ne sadaye Hurr
Sine pe hath mar ke chillaye haye Hurr
Ro kar kaha rafiqon se dekhi wafiye Hurr
Farsh-e aza Haram ne Pachhai baraye Hurr

Khanchin jo Shah ne aah, dil-e be-qarar se
Niklein tarap ke Fatima Zahra ke mazar se

2
Daure idhar-e se ran ki taraf Syed-e ammam
Aaye Najaf se Haider-e Safdar Chashm-e nam
Awaz di Nabi ne ke Hurr ko liye hain hum
Jis waqt pahunche Shah to wo tarat tha dam

Sar katne ki fikr mein jo the wo hat gaye
Mehman se apne daur ke Hazrat lipat gaye

3
Zanu pe rakh liya sar-e Hurr aur ye kaha
Bhai Husain aaya hai aa Hosh mein zara
Ankhen qadam pe mal ke ye bola wo bawafa
Maula Hazar jaan se mein aap par fida

Jin ke liye zamin pe malaik sar jhukate hain
Wo log Khuld se mere lene ko aate hain
