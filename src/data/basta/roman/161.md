[Safha 160 se jari]

4
Shah rone lage Zainab ko jo dekha be-tab
Shahr-e-Bano ne kiya Shah se ro kar ye khitab
Haq mein laundi ke hai kya hukm, Shah-e-Arsh Janab
Aap farmayen to pakrun ghore ki rikab

Yun gawara hai mujhe faqa pe faqa Sahib
Aap ke dam se main rakhti hun ilaqa Sahib

5
Ro ke farmane laga Haider-e-Karrar ka lal
Ran mein jab huegi kheti mere jadd ki pamal
Bano tujh ko hai abas apni tabahi ka khayal
Jo meri bahnon ka ahwal wohi tera hal

Biwa hona hai tujhe aur mujhe be-sar hona
Bint-e-Zahra ki itaat se na bahar hona

Jis dam suni Imam-e-Umam ne sada-e-Hurr

1
Jis dam suni Imam-e-Umam ne sada-e-Hurr
Sine pe hath mar ke chillaye haye Hurr
Ro kar kaha rafiqon se dekhi wafa-e-Hurr
Farsh-e-aza Haram ne bichhai baraye Hurr

Khinchi jo Shah ne aah, dil-e-be-qarar se
Niklin tarap ke Fatima Zahra ke mazar se

2
Daure idhar se ran ki taraf Syed-e-Umam
Aaye Najaf se Haider-e-Safdar chashm-e-nam
Awaz di Nabi ne ke Hurr ko liye hain hum
Jis waqt pahunche Shah to wo torta tha dam

Sar katne ki fikr mein jo the wo hat gaye
Mehman se apne daur ke Hazrat lipat gaye

3
Zanu pe rakh liya sar-e-Hurr aur ye kaha
Bhai Husain aaya hai aa hosh mein zara
Ankhen qadam pe mal ke ye bola wo bawafa
Maula hazar jaan se main aap par fida

Jin ke liye zamin pe malaik sar jhukate hain
Wo log Khuld se mere lene ko aate hain
