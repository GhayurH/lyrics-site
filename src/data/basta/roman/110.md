Salam

Husain Ibn-e-Ali ka gham dil-e-muztar mein rehta hai
Rubai / Maulana Muhammad Mustafa Jauhar

Husain Ibn-e-Ali ka gham dil-e-muztar mein rehta hai
Bahr-e-unwan jaise ghar ka malik ghar mein rehta hai
Gham-e-Shabbir aur hubb-e-Ali hai zindagi apni
Ye jazba dil mein rehta hai, ye sauda sar mein rehta hai

Naye ga puchhne wale taharat dida-e-tar ki
Ye ansu Sayyida ke gosha-e-chadar mein rehta hai
Kisi surat Ali ke dar pe rehne ki jagah milti
Ye khurshid ziya-gustar isi chakkar mein rehta hai

Nikalta hi nahin bugz-e-Ali dushman ke sine se
Ye Marhab dusra hai jo isi Khaibar mein rehta hai
Farari se na ye puchho ke aakhir bhagta kyun hai!
Ye puchho bhagna tay hai to kyun lashkar mein rehta hai

Kabhi pahlu mein dhunda aur roti hain Rubab uth kar
Ye alam rat bhar yaad-e-Ali Asghar mein rehta hai
Bayan-e-marifat mein sidq se hatta nahin Jauhar
Dimagh us ka khayal-e-rifat-e-mimbar mein rehta hai

Ayah-e-Tathir ke maqsad ka mehwar Fatima
Maulana Muhammad Mustafa Jauhar

Ayah-e-Tathir ke maqsad ka mehwar Fatima
Yani sharh-e-azmat-e-Nur-e-Payambar Fatima
In se phaila Din-e-Haq in se barhi nasl-e-Rasul
Ek Kausar hain Ali aur ek Kausar Fatima

Kashti-e-Din-e-Khuda ki nakhuda sabit huin
Shabbar-o-Shabbir jaise din ka langar Fatima
Naam hai Tasbih-e-Zahra, kam Tasbih-e-Khuda
Zikr tera zikr-e-Haq Allahu Akbar Fatima

Teri khidmat mein hua fiqh ko Quran bar-zaban
Ay Lisan Allah ay iman-parwar Fatima
Ayah-e-Tathir likhne ke liye taqdir ne
Teri sirat ko banaya is ka mistar Fatima

Tu kare apne janaze ke liye shab ko pasand
Aur teri dukhtar ho balwe mein khule sar Fatima
Zahr Shabbar ko diya, tukre jigar ke ho gaye
Phir gaya Shabbir ki gardan pe khanjar Fatima
(Bashukriya shair o salamkhwan Syed Haider Zaidi Saeed)

Professor Mirza Qamar Abbas Wafa Kanpuri

Faqat shahid ka haq hai hayat baad-e-mamat
