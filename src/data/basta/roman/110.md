Salam

Husain ibn-e-Ali ka gham dil-e-muztar mein rehta hai
Rubai / Maulana Muhammad Mustafa Jauhar

Husain Ibn-e Ali ka gham dil-e muztar mein rehta hai
Bahr-e Unwan jaise ghar ka Malik ghar mein rehta hai
Gham-e Shabbir aur habi-e Ali hai Zindagi apni
Ye jazba dil mein rehta hai thas ye sauda sar mein rehta hai

Naye ga puchhne wale taharat dedi tar ki
Ye ansu Sayyida ke gushush chadar mein rehta hai
Kisi surat Ali ke dar pe rehne ki jagah milti
Ye Khurshid Ziya gustar isi-e chukkr mein rehta hai

Nikalta hi nahin bughz-e Ali dushman ke sine se
Ye Marhab-o-Antar dusra hai jo isi-e Khaibar mein rehta hai
Farari se na ye puchho ke aakhir bhagata kyun hai!
Ye puchho bhagana Tay hai to kyun lashkar mein rehta hai

Kabhi pahlu mein dhundat aur roti hain Rubab uth kar
Ye alam raat bhar yaad-e Ali Asghar mein rehta hai
Bayan-e marifat mein Sadaq se hitta nahin jauhar
Daghmad is ka khayal-e rifat-e-do-alam-e mimbar mein rehta hai

Safina ka puchhne wale taharat dida-e-tar ki
Ye ansu Sayyida ke gosha-e-chadar mein rehta hai

Hain-e Tathir ke maqsad ka muhur Fatima
Yani rehta-e azmat-e nur-e Payambar Fatima
Un-e se phaila din-e Haq un-e se barhi nasl-e Rasul
Ek Kausar hain Ali aur Ek Kausar Fatima

Kashti-e din-e Khuda ki Nakhuda sabit huin
Shabbar o Shabbir jaise din ka langar Fatima
Naam hai surat-e-tasbih Zahra thas kam surat-e-tasbih Khuda
Zikr tera zikr-e Haq Allah Akbar Fatima

Teri khidmat mein hua fiqh-o-shariat ko Quran bar zaban
Ay lasan Allah Ay iman Banda-parwar Fatima
Hai-e Tathir likhne liye taqdir ne
Teri sirat ko banaya is ka tera Fatima

Teri khidmat mein hua nafa ko Quran har zaban
Aur teri dukhtar ho balwe mein khule sar Fatima
Zahr Shabbar ko diya thas tukre jigar ke ho gaye
Phir gaya Shabbir ki gardan pe khanjar Fatima
Tu kare apne janaze ke liye shab ko pasand

Professor Mirza Qamar Abbas Wafa Kanpuri

Faqat Shahid ka Haq hai hayat baad-e bad-e-mamat
Magar wo log jo Sibt-e-Nabi ke zakir hain
