6
Nida ye aai ke sine se qabz kar le jaan
Kaha tiron se chhulni hai Ay mere Rahman
Abhi to mar ke Barchhi na hai ek shaitan
Aur ab to hai to-e zanwaye Shimr ye Quran

Ye karb hai ke rukh-e Pak hai hai Ya Rab
Tere Husain ke sine mein dard hai Ya Rab

7
Nida ye aai ke mazlumiyat ke rutba-shinas
Kamar se khinch le Sabir ki jaan be-waswas
Kaha falak ne tarap kar ba-dard-o-hasrat-o-yas
Kamar to tut gayi jab se mar gaye Abbas

Nida ye aai ke un ka kamal hai chehra
Kaha ke khun se Asghar ke lal hai chehra

8
Ye guftagu thi ke murjha gaya Rasul ka phul
Falak se aa gaye Ruh-ul-Amin, hazin-o-malul
Kaha malak ne ke munh dhanp lo baraye Rasul
Pisar ki lash pe kholengi apne bal Batul

Nida ye sun ke isi samt chal parin Zainab
[Ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]

9

Idhar tarapti hui Bint-e-Murtaza aai

Akhi ke halq-e-burida se ye sada aai

Nabi ki Aal tumhare supurd hai Zainab
