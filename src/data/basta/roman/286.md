[Jab khalq se waqt-e-safar Fatima aaya — jari]

4
Phir roin bahut mil ke gale beton se Zahra
Farmaya tumhein dulha bane aah na dekha
Fizza se kaha qabr-e-Nabi par unhein le ja
Royen na mere samne ye un ko behla

Ay Fizza kabhi ranj unhein hone na dena
Pyaron ko meri lash pe bhi rone na dena

5
Ye keh ke kiya band dar-e-hujra-e-athar
Sab khurd-o-kalan rone lage aa ke bahar
Awaz suni Kalima-e-Tayyib ki mukarrar
Phir kuchh na sada aai kaha sab ne ye ro kar

Lo uth gayi duniya se nishani Nabi ki
Rehlat hui aaj Rasul-e-Madani ki

Rawi bayan karta hai Zahra ka majra

1
Rawi bayan karta hai Zahra ka majra
Jab rafta rafta Sayyida ka ariza barha
Phir sahib-e-farash hui wa musibata
Taqat rahi na jism-e-mubarak mein mutlaqa

Aisa maraz barha ke ajal sar par aa gayi
Ek roz murdani rukh-e-Zahra pe chha gayi

2
Zainab sirhane baith ke karne lagi buka
Hai hai main kya karun meri Amman ko kya hua
Jhuk jhuk ke iztirab se deti thi ye sada
Li mujh se naz mein bhi na khidmat ye kya kiya

Kya jane ruh jism se kyun nikal kar gayi
Amman tumhare chehre ki rangat badal gayi

3
Uthiye sakhon ko pas bula kar bithaiye
Kya nosh kijiyega, main laun bataiye
Bhai gaye hain dahr se un ko bulaiye
Rone ko qabr-e-Ahmad-e-Mursal pe jaiye

Aisi bhi nind hoti hai bedar huiye
Ghar hai udas baithe hue hoshiyar huiye
