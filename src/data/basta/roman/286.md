4
Phir roin bahut mil ke gale beton se Zahra
Farmaya tumhein dulha bane aah na dekha
Fizza se kaha qabr-e Nabi par unhein le ja
Roin na mere samne ye un ko behla

Ay Fizza kabhi ranj unhein hone na dena
Pyaron ko meri lash pe bhi rone na dena

5
ye keh ke kya band-e dar-e hijjar-e Athar
Sab khurd o kalan rone lage aa ke bahar

Phir kuchh na sada aai kaha sab ne ye ro kar

Rawi bayan karta hai Zahra ka majra

1
Rawi bayan karta hai Zahra ka majra
Jab rafta rafta Sayyida ka ariza barha
Phir Sahib-e farash hua wa musibata
Taqat rahi na jism-e-mubarak mein mutlaqa

Aisa maraz barha ajal sar par aa gayi
Ek roz mardandi rukh-e Zahra par chha gayi

2
Zainab sirhane baith ke karne lagi buka
Hai hai mein kya karun meri Amman ko kya hua
Jhuk jhuk ke iztirab se deti thi ye sada
Li mujh se Naza mein bhi na khidmat ye kya kya

Kya jane ruh-e jism se kyun nikal kar gayi
Amman tumhare chehre ki rangat badal gayi

3
Uthiye sine ko pas bula kar bithaiye
Kya nosh kijiye gaght mein laun Bataiye
Bhai gaye hain dahr se un ko bulaiye
Rone ko qabr-e-Ahmad-e-Mursal pe jaiye

Aisi bhi nind hoti hai bedar hue
Ghar hai udas baithe hue hoshiyar hue
