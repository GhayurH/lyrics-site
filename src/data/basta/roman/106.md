Soz

Shimr-e pe ne, ye Hazrat-e Zainab se kaha
Uzr-haye karta hun ke ab bakhsh do, tum meri khata
kaha Zainab ne ke, Malun Ao bani-e jafa
To ne kata mere aage mere bhai ka gala

Baad-e marne jafaye to agar yaad yad-e-gham
az zamin dast-e-sitam baron aram o faryad yad-e-gham
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed Irshad Husain, Arshad Husain o baradaran)

Abid se ye watan mein kisi ne kya kalam
Guzre qalaq zyada kahan tum pe Ya Imam
Maula ne tin bar kaha, Sham Sham Sham
Sar nange mera kunba tha aur gird khas o amim

Bibiyon ke naam le ke adu ho hote the
Balwa ka sar bhi rota tha aur hum bhi rote the
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Muhammad Abbas Husain)

Sajjad ko bulaya dobara jo shaqi ne
Ye sunte hi Bibiyon ke dharakne lage sine
Farmaya bhatije se ye Tab Bint-e Ali ne
Mein kyun jo ranj uthaye mere ji ne

Kya jaye ab kya sitam ijad kare ga
Balwa ke hamein kaun si bedad kare ga

Wadi gayi pukar ki baton pe na jana
Dekho mein kahe deti hun kuchh de to na mana
Gar tegh rakhe halq pe gardan na hilna
Baba ki tarah mar ke sar apna kitana

saman jo kuchh lut ka tum paye ho beta
pehle sar-e Shah-e Shuhada laye ho beta
(Bashukriya Shair o sozkhwan Syed Ishtiaq Husain Hamdani)

Sakina qaid ho kar Sham ke zindan mein jab aai
Wo bachchi is andhere ghar ki tariki se ghabrai
Muqaddar ne ajab afat ki Pehli raat dikhlai
Zamin to farsh thi, saya fafng tha charkh-e minai

Phuphi ke sath roti thi na man ke pas soti thi
Barahna sar, khari zindan ke darwaze pe roti thi

Udhar se jo guzarta tha use kehti thi sunta ja
Main be-kas qaid mein hun ek ye paigham hai mera
Agar Baba milen tujh ko, to ye kahiyo qasam kha kar
Sakina par musibat hai, khabar lo ay Shah-e-wala
Agar tujh se kahen khaime mein sota chhor aaya hun
To kahiyo tu, dar-e-zindan pe rota chhor aaya hun
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Ustad Qadr Ali Khan Marhum)
