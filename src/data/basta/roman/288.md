[Duniya se jab guzar gayin Zahra jigar-figar — jari]

4
Zahra ko ghusl de chukin Zainab-e-hazin
Aa kar kafan pehnane lage Badshah-e-Din
Aaye Hasan Husain bhi rote hue qarin
Man ke gale lipat gaye donon wo naznin

Ijaz se Batul ne dil ko sambhal kar
Le lin balayen hath se kafan se nikal kar

5
Zainab ki bholi baton pe muztar the Murtaza
Amman ne bachchon ko gale se lagaya
Bachche ghar se the, lash ki balin pe baithe
Aur sar rakha qadmon pe man ke Husain ka

Sab ro rahe the dekh ke mayyat Batul ki
Duniya se uth rahi thi nishani Rasul ki

Duniya se aaj rehlat Bint-e-Rasul hai

1

---***---

Duniya se aaj rehlat-e-Bint-e-Rasul hai
Dast-e-ajal mein Ismat-e-Kubra ka phul hai
Qabr-e-Nabi larazti hai Yasrib malul hai
Husain-o-Murtaza se wada-e-Batul hai

Peti se lag ke Zainab-o-Kulsum roti hain
Is kamsini mein bachchiyan be-man ki hoti hain

2
Zainab ka hal ye hai ke ansu nahin rawan
Kulsum ko bhi deti hain paiham tasalliyan
Farma rahi hain chhoti bahen se ke meri jaan
Rehti nahin jahan mein hamesha kisi ki man

Be-bas thin hum ko chhor ke giryan chali gayin
Allah ne bula liya Amman chali gayin

3
Zainab ki bholi baton pe muztar the Murtaza
Asma ne bachchon ko gale se laga liya
Chupke khare the lash pe Mujtaba
Aur sar rakha tha qadmon pe man ke Husain ka

Sab ro rahe the dekh ke mayyat-e-Batul ki
Duniya se uth rahi thi nishani Rasul ki

4
Mayyat ke pas baith ke bole ye Murtaza
Bachcho sambhalo dil ko ke ye sabr hai jawe
Ab ehtimam karna hai mayyat ke ghusl ka
Asma ke sath sahn mein jao pidar fida

Tamil ho wasiyat-e-Bint-e-Rasul ki
Anjam den hum aakhri khidmat-e-Batul ki

5
Allah kitne zulm hue hain Batul par
Tamr raha hai karb se tarikh ka jigar
Rote taraste guzri hai Bibi ki umr bhar
Ashr har ek shab gayi qayamat har ek sahar

Shahzadi kaise zakhmon ki tere dawa karun
Ansu baha raha hun, ghulam aur kya karun

Duniya se aaj rehlat Bint-e-Rasul hai
