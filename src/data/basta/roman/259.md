Zindan mein jabke Dukhtar-e-Shabbir mar gayi

1
Zindan mein jabke Dukhtar-e-Shabbir mar gayi
Duniya se Fatima safar-e-khuld kar gayi
Sine ke dil pe dagh-e-judai ka dhar gayi
Tarap ke aain qarib Rubab, Bint-e-Ali

Kaha ke bachchi ko de dijiye mujhe Bhabhi
Khuda ki marzi-e-insan ka dakhl kya Mimi

2
Bazu hila ke Banu ne nala kiya
Ye roz roz ki izaon se bhi chhut gayi
Baten abhi to karti thin ansu baha baha
Sakit hai [misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]

Na din ko tarpegi ab aur na shab ko royegi
Kis waqt sans ruk gayi, kab dam nikal gaya

3
Lo hath jorti hai ye man ay Sakina jaan
Mujh ko bula lo tum ho jahan ay Sakina jaan
Dhundun nikal ke tum ko kahan ay Sakina jaan
Aur to qabr hai ye makan ay Sakina jaan

Main chahti hun maut se badtar hayat ko
Jinan mein bap ke sine se lag ke soyegi
