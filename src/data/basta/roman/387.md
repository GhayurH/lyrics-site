Hali gham sunayenge jab Imam aayenge — jari

Shak hai jin ko Allah ke adl aur adalat par
Mansab-e-Ala kar-e-nabuwwat par Sayyida ki ismat par
Murtaza ki Ahmad se muttasil niyabat par
Jin ko Shak hai barah par barahwin ki ghaibat par
Sab hi mar khayenge, jab Imam aayenge

Naam par Sahaba ke kam kafiron jaise
Naam par Sahaba ke kam fasiqon jaise
Naam par Sahaba ke kam munkiron jaise
Naam par Sahaba ke kam mushrikon jaise
Sab hi munh charain hue-jab Imam aayenge hue

Ye hamare makr-o-riya, shairana ayyari!
Kya hamare saum-o-salat aur ye azadari!
Kitna hai khulus in mein, kis qadar riyakari!
Kya Imam-e-khatir hum ne ki hai tayyari?!
kaise munh dikhain hue jab Imam aayen hue

Dawa muhabbat jo Subh o Sham karte hain
Un ke dushmanon se bhi rah o rah-o-rasm rakhte hain
Khums bhi nahin dete ghibatein bhi karte hain
[Ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]

Mominon se bhi dil mein bughz o keniha rakhte hain
kis tarah nabahin hue,jab Imam aayen hue
Jhut bolne ko hum mashghala samajhte hain
Baat baat par be-ja maslahat baratte hain
Aur munafiqat ko bhi maslahat hi kehte hain
Lahw-o-lahab o Lahw-o-laib ka saman hum gharon mein rakhte hain
kis tarah chhupayenge hue,jab Imam aayen hue

Nemat Din-o-shariat ko bojh hi samajhte hain
Lahu o Lahw-o-laib hi ko hum Zindagi samajhte hain
Sahiban-e-zar mita ko bara admi samajhte hain
Tang dast-e-sitam Momin ko bas yahni samajhte hain
Kya maqam payenge hue-jab Imam aayenge hue

Ye hamare makr-o-riya, shatirana ayyari
Kya hamare saum-o-salat aur ye azadari
Kitna hai khulus un mein kis qadar be-riya kar
Kya Imam ki khatir hum ne ki hai tayyari
