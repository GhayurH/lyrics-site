Ghar se jab bahr-e-safar Syed-e-Alam nikle

1
Ghar se jab bahr-e-safar Syed-e-Alam nikle
Sar jhukaye hue badiya-e-purnam nikle
Khwaish-o-farzand paye bandh ke baham nikle
Ro ke farmaya ke is shahr se ab hum nikle

Rat se girya-e-Zahra ki sada aati hai
Dekhen qismat hamein kis dasht mein le jati hai

2
Rukh kiya Shah ne su-e-qabr-e-Shahanshah-e-Anam
Bahr-e-taslim mujhe muttasil-e-Bab-e-Salam
Izn pa kar jo gaye qabr ke nazdik-e-Imam
Arz ki aaya hai aaj aakhri rukhsat ko ghulam

Ye makan hum se ab ay Shah-e-Zaman chhutta hai
Aaj Hazrat ke nawase se watan chhutta hai

3
Chain se sab hain gharon mein mujhe milta nahin chain
Sakht afat mein hai ab aap ka ye nur-e-ain
Tukre dil hota hai jab ro ke Haram karte hain bain
Nanhe bachchon ko bhala le ke kidhar jaye Husain

Shahr mein chain na jangal kya aman milti hai
Dekhe qabr-e-musafir ko kahan milti hai

4
Ye bayan kar ke jo tawiz se lipte Sarwar
Yun hi lahrai qabr aur thahrai zarih-e-anwar
Aai turbat se ye awaz-e-Habib-e-Dawar
Teri ghurbat mein sadqe mere mazlum pisar

Koi samjha na meri god ka pala tujh ko
Haye aada ne Madine se nikala tujh ko

5
Gayi din se teri madar nahin qabr mein chain
Aai thi shab ko mere pas ye karti hui bain
Ghar mera lutta hai faryad Rasul-e-Saqalain
Subh ko apna watan chhor ke jata hai Husain

Kehne aai hun ke munh qabr se morungi main
Apne bachche ko akela to na chhorungi main
