Likha hai jab shab-e-Ashur guzri ek pahar

1
Likha hai jab shab-e-Ashur guzri ek pahar
Husain aaye suye khaima Ali Akbar
Shagaf-e dar se ye dekha ke wo mah-e anwar
Farugh-e Hasan se dulha bana hai bistar par

Habib pas se man bar bar dekhti hai
Utha ke shama thas rukh-e gul-izar dekhti hai

2
Ye dekhte hi kaleja pe tir-e-gham khaye
Khuda ye sa'at-e-Sham ko bhi na dikhaye
Wahan se khaima-e-Abbas ki taraf aaye
Jari ki chah pe khud alam se tharraye

Kabhi Husain ke sadme se aah bharte hain
Kabhi husam ko saiqiq se Saf karte hain

3
Jari ke samne Kulsum jaan khoti hain
Kisi khayal mein ashkon se munh dhoti hain
Rida ko Ansuon se dam ba dam bhagti hain
Qarib baithi hui zar zar roti hain

Wo ranj-o-gham hai ke halat tabah karti hain
Falak ko dekh ke har bar aah karti hain

4
Wo keh rahe hain ke khwahar na yun buka ke
Huzur-e apni taraf se mujhe fida kijiye
Nasib ka na falak se koi gila-e kijiye
Pisar samajh ke mujhe Haq ada kijiye

Matiya-e Sibt-e Rasul-e anmam hai Abbas
Har ek aziz ka un ke ghulam hai Abbas

5
Bahen ki shan pe ro kar nishan abr-e-bahar
Husain walon se chale suye khaima-e-bimar
Qarib ja ke pukare ke ay mere dildar
Rasul-e-Pak ki masnad ke malik-o-mukhtar

Utho ke meri shahadat qarib aa pahunche
Gala katane ki sa'at qarib aa pahunche

6
Wo ro ke bole ke hai ye kya Baba
Hamari zist mein Hazrat ye na ho Khuda Baba
Mariz kyun na ho qurban, main fida Baba
Qarib-e-marg hun, marne se khauf kya Baba

Husain bole kahin wo bhi din ye chadhta hai
