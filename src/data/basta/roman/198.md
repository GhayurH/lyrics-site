Likha hai jab shab-e-Ashur guzri ek pahar

1
Likha hai jab shab-e-Ashur guzri ek pahar
Husain aaye su-e-khaima-e-Ali Akbar
Shigaf-e-dar se ye dekha ke wo mah-e-anwar
Furugh-e-husn se dulha bana hai bistar par

Ajib yas se man bar bar dekhti hai
Utha ke shama, rukh-e-gul-izar dekhti hai

2
Ye dekhte hi kaleje pe tir-e-gham khaye
Khuda ye saniha dushman ko bhi na dikhlaye
Wahan se khaima-e-Abbas ki taraf aaye
Jari ki chah pe fart-e-alam se chillaye

Kabhi Husain ke sadme se aah bharte hain
Kabhi husam ko saiqal se saf karte hain

3
Jari ke samne Kulsum jaan khoti hain
Kisi khayal mein ashkon se munh dhoti hain
Rida ko ansuon se dam-ba-dam bhigoti hain
Qarib baithi hui zar zar roti hain

Wo ranj-o-gham hai ke halat tabah karti hain
Falak ko dekh ke har bar aah karti hain

4
Wo keh rahe hain ke khwahar na yun buka kije
Huzur apni taraf se mujhe fida kije
Nasib ka na falak se koi gila kije
Pisar samajh ke mujhe sheh ka haq ada kije

Mutiy-e-Sibt-e-Rasul-e-Anam hai Abbas
Har ek aziz ka un ke ghulam hai Abbas

5
Behen ki shan pe ro kar nishan-e-abr-e-bahar
Husain wan se chale su-e-khaima-e-bimar
Qarib ja ke pukare ke ay mere dildar
Rasul-e-Pak ki masnad ke malik-o-mukhtar

Utho ke meri shahadat qarib aapohnchi
Gala katane ki saa'at qarib aapohnchi

6
Wo ro ke bole kamar se hai ye kya Baba
Hamari zist mein Hazrat ye jafa Baba
Mariz kyun na ho qurban, main fida Baba
Qarib-e-marg hun, marne se khauf kya Baba

Husain bole kahin wo bhi ran pe charhta hai
Jo gul isharon se paanchon namaazein parhta hai
