Muslim ke lal jabke Shahid-e-jafa hue

1
Muslim ke lal jabke Shahid-e-jafa hue
Dasht-e-fana se rahi-e-mulk-e-baqa hue
Ghurbat mein wo yatim jo nazr-e-qaza hue
Zainab bhi roin, Shah bhi mahw-e-buka hue

Do tir-e-gham dilon pe yakayak jo chal gaye
Bahr-e-raza Ali ke nawase machal gaye

2
Betab ho ke aaye wo ghazi jo man ke pas
Zainab zaban se bolin ba-dard-o-yas
Kyun bad-hawas aaye ho chehre hain kyun udas
Kya dekh kar larai ko tari hua hiras

Ran se phir aaye kaun tumhare gharane mein
Nani ki dhak baithi hai sare zamane mein

3
Kya kya guzar gaye Shah-e-din par gham-o-mihan
Bichhre musafat mein rafiqan-e-be-watan
Nazr-e-khizan hua mere man-jaye ka chaman
Muslim ke lal mare gaye, lut gayi bahen

Kis kis shahid ke liye ansu bahaoge
Ab kis ki lash dekh ke marne ko jaoge

4
Ye zikr tha ke Shah khud aaye bahen ke pas
Baithin wo sar jhuka ke Shah-e-be-watan ke pas
Bachche bhi roye baith ke Shah-e-Zaman ke pas
Nazdik tha ke aayen kaleje dahan ke pas

Ashkon se yun dileron ne daman bhigoye the
Sibtain jis tarah gham-e-Zahra mein roye the

5
Kehte the Shah-e-tishna-dahan, kya kare Husain
Ay Bint-e-Shah-e-Qila-shikan, kya kare Husain
Lutta hai hasraton ka chaman, kya kare Husain
Lillah kuchh batao bahen, kya kare Husain

Puchho to kyun ye rote hain kyun haye haye hai
Roye unhein Husain, tumhari ye raye hai

6
Mujh se phir ye sari Khudai, main kya karun
Kyun kar lutaun teri kamai, main kya karun
Bolin nasib mein hai judai, main kya karun
Amman se qaul hari hun bhai, main kya karun

Lillah rad na kije hadiya faqir ka
Deti hun wasta main Janab-e-Amir ka
