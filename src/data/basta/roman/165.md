Muslim ke lal jabke Shahid-e-jafa hue

1
Muslim ke lal jabke Shahid-e-jafa hue
Dasht-e-fana se rahi-e-mulk-e-baqa hue
Ghurbat mein wo yatim jo nazr-e-qaza hue
Zainab bhi roin thas Shah bhi mahv-e-aza-e buka hue

Do tir-e-gham dilon pe yakayak jo chal gaye
Bahr-e Raza Ali ke nawase machal gaye

2
Betab ho ke aaye wo ghazi jo man ke pas
Zainab zaban se Bolin ba dard o yas
Kyun bad-hawas aaye ho chehre hain kyun udas
Kya dekh kar larai ko tari hua hiras

Ran se phir aaye kaun tumhare gharane mein
Nani ki dhak baithi hai sare zamane mein

3
Kya kya guzar gaye Shah din par gham o andoh-o-mihan
Bichhre musafirat mein rafiqan-e-be-watan-e be-kasi watan
Nazr-e khizan hua mere man jaye ka chaman
Muslim ke lal mare gaye thas lut gayi bahen

Kis kis Shahid ke liye ansu bahaon hue
Ab kis ki lash dekh ke marne ko jaoge

4
Ye zikr tha ke Shah khud aaye bahen ke pas
Bahnein wo sar jhuka ke Shah-e be-kasi watan ke pas
Bachche bhi roye Baith ke Shah-e Shah-e-Zaman ke pas
Nazdik tha ke aayen kaleje dahan ke pas

Ashkon se yun dilarin ne daman bhugiyo the
Sibtain jis tarah gham-e Zahra mein roye the

Ankhon se yun dileran ne daman bhigoye the
Kehte the Shah tishna dahan thas kya kare Husain
Ay Bint-e-Zahra-e Shah-e Qali shakn thas kya kare Husain
Lutta hai Hasraton ka chaman thas kya kare Husain
Lillah kuchh batao bahen thas kya kare Husain

Puchho to kyun ye rote hain kyun haye haye hai
Roye unhein Husain thas tumhari ye Ro hai

6
Mujh se phir ye sari Khudai thas mein kya karun
Kar laraun teri kamai thas mein kya karun
Bolin nasib mein hai judai thas mein kya karun
Amman se Qaul-e hari hun bhai thas mein kya karun

Lillah rad na ka hadiha faqir ka
Deti hun wasta mein Janab-e Amir ka
