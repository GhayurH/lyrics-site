Muntazim Kaabe ka aa pahuncha safai ke liye
Qamar Jalalvi

Muntazim Kaabe ka aa pahuncha safai ke liye
Ae buton ab aur ghar dhoondo Khudai ke liye

Cheerte hain kalla-e-azhdar ko jhoole mein Ali
Aaj pehli mashq hai Khaibar kushai ke liye

Do kiya Marhab ko jab Haider ne to boli Zulfiqar
Haath aisa chahiye tegh azmai ke liye

Kehte the Abbas main saqqa hun fauj-e-Shah ka
Khoon ke darya baha dunga tarai ke liye

Ae uroos-e-tegh-e-Qasim ran mein ghoonghat tu utha
Sar liye lakhon khare hain runumai ke liye

Chaunk uthti thi Sakina naam sun kar Shah ka
Jab kabhi roti thi Zainab apne bhai ke liye

Ibn-e-Muljim ne lagai jab Ali ke sar pe tegh
Arsh se utre malaik bhi duhai ke liye

Kar diya kyun Ibn-e-Muljim, aise Maula ko shaheed
Tha jo ik mushkil kusha sari Khudai ke liye

Nazar pari jo Muhammad ki Aal ki surat
Maulana Riyaz-ud-Din Sarwari

Nazar pari jo Muhammad ki Aal ki surat
To goya dekh li Badr-e-kamal ki surat

Hasan Husain ki surat mein dekh le koi
Rasul-e-Pak ke husn-o-jamal ki surat

Farishte aate the har roz asmanon se
Zamin pe dekhne Zahra ke lal ki surat

Jalal us ka hai manind-e-mehr-e-alam-tab
Jamal us ka hai Badr-e-kamal ki surat

Wujud Sarwar-e-Alam ka aks hain Hasnain
Nazar-nawaz hai ye itisal ki surat

Mile Husain jise, Mustafa mile us ko
Wisal-e-Haq hai Nabi ke wisal ki surat

Husain ko to har ek gam par uruj mila
Magar Yazid ne dekhi zawal ki surat

Riyaz ishq-e-Husaini hai mera sarmaya
Yahi Husain hai mere maal ki surat

Silsila naam-e-Khuda, jazba-e-imani ka
Professor Maulana Zafar Jauhari

Silsila naam-e-Khuda, jazba-e-imani ka
Sang-e-bunyad hai Shabbir ki qurbani ka

Din ke waste Allah re, isar-e-Husain
Kabhi shikwa na kiya gham ki farawani ka

Ya Ali jis ki zaban par ho bhala us ke liye
Khauf kya zulm ka, dahshat ka, pareshani ka

Karbala, ho ke raha wo teri azmat pe nisar
Jaiza jis ne liya fitrat-e-insani ka

Kalima-go the sabhi Dasht-e-Bala mein lekin
Ek qatra bhi na pyason ko mila pani ka
