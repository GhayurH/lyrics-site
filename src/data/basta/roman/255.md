4
Gird-e-Abid ke phiri phir wo ba-hal-e-taghayyur
Rakh diya paon pe sar apna bana kar zanjir
Bole wo kaun, ye chillai kaniz-e-[lafz ghair wazeh]
Assalam ay rasan-o-tauq-o-salasil ke asir

Hai wasiyyat ka mahal marne pe tayyar hai tu
Kuchh kafan ke liye rakhta hai ke nadar hai tu

5
Gham na kha, gor-o-kafan mein tujhe dungi Wallah
Nange sar tere janaze ke chalungi hamrah
Marne wale tera kya naam hai, kab se hai tabah
Bole Maula, abhi [baqiya misra ghair wazeh]

Naam be-kas bhi hai, qaidi bhi hai, nadar bhi hai
Hal ye hai ke asiri bhi hai, azar bhi hai

6
Hind ne puchha maraz kya hai, kaha be-pidari
Ro ke boli ke dawa kya hai, kaha nauhagari
Phir jo daryaft kiya, kehne lage dar-badari
Boli leta hai khabar kaun, kaha be-khabari

Aah karne ka sabab puchha to farmane lage
Taziyanon ke nishan pusht pe dikhlane lage

7
Boli wo kaun se isyan pe mili ye tazir
Ro ke farmaya ke kuchh bhi nahin be-taqsir
Us ne [baqiya misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Bole [baqiya misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]

Kuchh kafan ke liye hamrah nahin laya hun
Bap ko chhor ke be-gor-o-kafan aaya hun

8
[Ibtidai lafz ghair wazeh] Zainab pe nazar kar ke kaha Ya Dawar
Fatima qaid se zindan mein aain kyun kar
Dekha Bano ko to kehne lagi ho kar shashdar
Koi shahzadi hai Iran ki ye nange sar

Qudrat-e-Khaliq-e-Qayyum nazar aati hai
Koi Zainab koi Kulsum nazar aati hai

Qaid-khane mein talatum hai ke Hind aati hai
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Jafar Raza o Ahmad Ali Jafari)
