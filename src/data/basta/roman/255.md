4
Gird-e-Abid ke phiri phir wo ba-hal-e-taghayyur
[Ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Bole wo kaun, ye chillai [lafz ghair wazeh]
Kis bap ka tu lal hai, kis man ka ladla

Kya Dukhtar-e-Rasul ka lakht-e-jigar hai tu
Qurban jaun mere Aqa ka sar hai tu

5
Gham na kha, gor-o-kafan main tujhe dungi Wallah
Nange sar tere janaze ke chalungi hamrah
Chillaya kanp ke ye sar-e-Sarwar-e-Zaman
Pyasa mera qatl mera watan

Naam be-kas bhi hai, qaidi bhi hai, nadar bhi hai
Hal ye hai ke asiri bhi hai, azar bhi hai

6
Nana mera Rasul hai, Baba Abul Hasan
Ro ke boli ke dawa kya hai, kaha nauhagari
Masum mera bhai hai, qaidi meri bahen
Boli leta hai khabar kaun, kaha be-khabari

Aah karne ka sabab puchha to farmane lage
Taziyanon ke nishan pusht pe dikhlane lage

7
Boli wo kaun se isyan pe mili ye tazir
Ahmad ko rone wali ka lakht-e-jigar hun main
Ali-o-Husain be-kas-o-be-par ka sar hun main
Bole [misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]

Ghash ho gayi ye sunte hi Hind neku-sair
Bap ko chhor ke be-gor-o-kafan aaya hun

8
Zainab sar-e-akhi ko pukari ba-chashm-e-tar
Dukhtar-e-Fatima zindan mein aai kyun kar
Naam apna kyun bata diya ay Shah-e-bahr-o-bar
Is ka nahin khayal ke Zainab hai nange sar

Qudrat-e-Khaliq-e-Qayyum nazar aati hai
Pehchan ke jo wo mujhe chadar uthayegi

Bashukriya sozkhwan Jafar Raza o Ahmad Ali Jafari
