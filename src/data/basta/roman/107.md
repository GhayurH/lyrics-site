Salam

Mujrai-e-jagah ayal mah-e-aza hota hai
Mir Anees

Bahr-e-jagah ayan mah-e-aza hota hai
Charkh pe matam-e-Shah-e-Shuhada hota hai
Rone walon ka bhi kya rutba hai Subhan Allah
Jin ke ashkon ka kharidar Khuda hota hai

Dekh kar Shah ko dam-e-naz' jo roye Abbas
Pyar se Shah gale kehne ye kya hota hai
Kyun khajil hote hain pani na mila to na mila
Wo kiya tum ne jo kuchh haq-e-wafa hota hai

Pher din ankhen jo Asghar ne pukari Bano
Dauro ay ji bibiyo dekho to ye kya hota hai
Shah ro dete the kehti thi Sakina jis dam
Pyas se sina mein dam ab to khafa hota hai

Kehti thi khalq-e-Khuda dekh ke Abid ko asir
Kahin bimar bhi rassi mein bandha hota hai
Tap-e-chehlum ye sada aati thi maidan mein Anees
Dekhen kab qaid se Sajjad riha hota hai
(Bashukriya Allama Nawab Haider Abidi)

Zikr-e-Shah kar ke muhibbin ko rulaya main ne
Mir Anees

Zikr-e-Shah kar ke muhibbon ko rulaya main ne
Apna ghar mujrai-e-Jannat mein banaya main ne
Shah ne farmaya karunga na gila ummat ka
Pani do roz na paya, to na paya main ne

Shah kehte the ke pyasa mera mehman hua qatl
Hurr ke marne ka ajab ranj uthaya main ne
Aai awaz-e-Ali ke na kurho ay beta
Ab-e-Kausar tere mehman ko pilaya main ne

Shah ne farmaya mere bhai ke bazu kate
Is pe bhi hath na ummat pe uthaya main ne
Mara Akbar ko to kehta tha yahi Ibn-e-Suair
Aaj taswir-e-Muhammad ko mitaya main ne

Shah ne qasid se kaha beti ko kya khat likhta
Aa ke yan ek ghari chain na paya main ne
Kahiyo Sughra se ke mushkil hai watan mein aana
Ab to mili isi jangal ko basaya main ne
