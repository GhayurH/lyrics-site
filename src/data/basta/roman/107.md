Salam

Majraji-e jagah ail mah-e aza hota hai
Mir Anees

Bahr-e jagah ayan mah-e aza hota hai
Charkh pe matam-e Shah-e Shuhada hota hai
Rone walon ka bhi kya rutba hai sabhan Allah
Janke ashkon ka kharidari Khuda hota hai

Dekh kar Shah ko dam-e Naza jo roye Abbas
Pyar se Shah gale kehne ye kya hota hai
Kyun khalj hote hain pani na mila to na mila
Wo kya tum ne jo kuchh Haq-e wafa hota hai

Phariden ankhen jo Asghar ne pukari Banu
Daudo Ay ji biyo dekho to ye kya hota hai
Shah ro dete the kehti thi Sakina jis dam
Pyas se Sine mein dam ab to khafa hota hai

Kehti thi khalq Khuda dekh ke Abid ko asir
Kahin bimar bhi rassi mein Bandha hota hai
Tab-e chehlum ye sada aati thi maidan mein Anees
Dekhen kab qaid se Sajjad riha hota hai
(Bashukriya Allama Nawab Haider Abidi)

Zikr-e Shah kar ke mubhin ko rulaya mein ne
Mir Anees

Zikr-e Shah kar ke muhibbon ko rulaya mein ne
Apna ghar majraji-e Jannat mein banaya mein ne
Shah ne Farmaya karun ga na gila ummat ka
Pani do roz na paya thas to na paya mein ne

Shah kehte the ke Pyasa mera mehman hua qatl
Hurr ke marne ka ajab ranj uthaya mein ne
Aai awaz-e Ali ke na kar Ay beta
Aab-e Kausar tere mehman ko pilaya mein ne

Shah kehte the ke pyasa mera jahan qatl
Is-e pe bhi hath na ummat pe uthaya mein ne
Mara Akbar ko to kehta tha yahi Ibn-e sairi
Aab-e-Kausar tere mehman ko pilaya main ne

Shah ne qasid-e se kaha beti ko kya khat likhta
Aa ke Yan Ek ghari chain na paya mein ne
Kahiyo Sughra se ke mushkil hai watan mein aana
Ab to mili isi-e jangal ko basaya mein ne
