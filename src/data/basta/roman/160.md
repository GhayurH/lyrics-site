[Jis ghari nahr pe khaime Shah-e-wala ke hue — jari]

4
Aab-dida ho lainon se ye bole Shabbir
Aap ke ab to dikhaye hamein jo kuchh taqdir
Khayenge ab to tigh-o-tabar-o-khanjar-o-tir
Par Khuda ka hai shahid ke main hun be-taqsir

Nahr se garmi ke mausam mein uthate ho mujhe
Kya milega tumhein nahaq jo satate ho mujhe

Haftum mah-e-Muharram ki hui jabke sahar

1
Haftum-e-mah-e-Muharram ki hui jabke sahar
Aur namaz-e-sahari se hue farigh Sarwar
Di khabardar ne jamiat-e-aada ki khabar
Shah-e-be-kas ne kaha, Khuda par hai nazar

Aane do aate hain jo tigh-o-sipar bandhe hue
Baithe hain marne pe yan hum kamar bandhe hue

2
Shab-e-hashtum se nuhum tak rahe sunte yahi hal
Har taraf se chale aate hain udhar Ahl-e-Zalal
Yan tarapte the pare Shah ke pyase atfal
Ahl-e-Ismat ko tha gham be-kasi-e-Shah ka kamal

Kehte the sab hain adu dost na aaya koi
Sibt-e-Ahmad ki madad karne na paya koi

3
Subh-e-qatl-e-Shah-e-Mazlum numayan hui jab
Uth ke Sajjad se, Shah ne poshak talab
Munh pe rumal liye roti thi baithi Zainab
Dauri kehti hui bhaiya na kahin ki rahi ab

Gar qadam aap ne khaime se nikala bhai
Phir raha kaun mera puchhne wala bhai
