Salam

Kaun qatil tha salami ke jahan aur bhi hai
Karam thas sabr o Raza ki Malik-e-saltanat-e-Kufa ka naam hai Shabbir
Karbala dekh ke hum samjhe ke Han aur bhi hai
Sadqe is dil ke jo ho habi-e Ali se abad
Is se behtar koi duniya mein makan aur bhi hai

Naam-e Shabbir pe be-kasi sakhta girayan hona
Baad-e kalme ke ye Ahl-e-iman ka nishan aur bhi hai
Barchhiyan mar ke Akbar ko pukare aada
Shah se puchho koi farzand-e jawan aur bhi hai

La ke shamhashe ko hathon pe pukare Maula
Nazr-e Haq ke liye ye ghunch duhan aur bhi hai
Bal khole hue lashe pe jo aayen Zahra
Khul gaya Hurr pe dam-e Naza ke man aur bhi hai

Hatkhari Beriyan pehna ke adu kehte the
Dekh bimar thas abhi tauq-e shikar aur bhi hai
Nile rukhsaron pe kanon se lahu hai jari
Gardan-e bali Sakina pe nishan aur bhi hai

Mirza Adib

Majraji paida hua tha sam-e Hasan ke waste
Aur bana khanjar Shah-e tishna dahan ke waste
Ay falak Zainab ko to ne kyun phiraya dar ba dar
Aur bana khanjar-e-Shimr tishna-dahan ke waste

Bole Abid Akbar o Asghar to hun Shah par nisar
Aur hum jite rahen ranj o mihan ke waste
Ay falak Insaf se tujh teri dur hai
Gardan-e Sajjad ho tauq o rasan ke waste

Shimr se kehti thi Zainab chhin mat meri rida
Ye rida rehne de bhai ke kafan ke waste
