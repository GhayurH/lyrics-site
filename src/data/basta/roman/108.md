Salam

Kaun qatil tha salami ke jahan aur bhi hai
Karam, sabr-o-raza ki saltanat ka naam hai Shabbir
Karbala dekh ke hum samjhe ke han aur bhi hai
Sadqe us dil ke jo ho hubb-e-Ali se abad
Us se behtar koi duniya mein makan aur bhi hai

Naam-e-Shabbir pe be-sakhta giryan hona
Baad-e-kalme ke ye iman ka nishan aur bhi hai
Barchhiyan mar ke Akbar ko pukare ada
Shah se puchho koi farzand-e-jawan aur bhi hai

La ke shashmahe ko hathon pe pukare Maula
Nazr-e-Haq ke liye ye ghuncha-e-dahan aur bhi hai
Bal khole hue lashe pe jo aayen Zahra
Khul gaya Hurr pe dam-e-naz' ke man aur bhi hai

Hathkari beriyan pehna ke adu kehte the
Dekh bimar, abhi tauq-e-giran aur bhi hai
Nile rukhsaron pe kanon se lahu hai jari
Gardan-e-bali Sakina pe nishan aur bhi hai

Manqabat Jafar Husain

Ruhi fidak naam tu Yasub-o-Haider ast
Zikrat ibadati ba-hadis-e-Payambar ast
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed Musa Rizvi o Munawwar Mehdi)

Wafa ki aakhri sarhad ko hum Abbas kehte hain
Mirza Adib

Mujrai paida hua tha sam-e-Hasan ke waste
Aur bana khanjar Shah-e-tishna-dahan ke waste
Ay falak Zainab ko tu ne kyun phiraya dar-ba-dar
Matam-e-Shabbir kya kam tha bahen ke waste

Bole Abid Akbar-o-Asghar to hon Shah par nisar
Aur hum jite rahen ranj-o-mihan ke waste
Ay falak insaf se tajwiz teri dur hai
Gardan-e-Sajjad ho tauq-o-rasan ke waste

Shimr se kehti thi Zainab chhin mat meri rida
Ye rida rehne de bhai ke kafan ke waste

Wafa ki jaan mita Ahl-e-dil ki nazron mein
Wo tishna-lab, lab-e-darya jo pyas rakhta hai
(Baqir Zaidi / London, Canada / America)
