[Baqi jo rafiq-e-Shah-e-din reh gaye do char — jari]

4
Piri mein jo dikhlai Jawanon ki shujaat
Tharrane lage uzw-e badan ghut gayi taqat
Dam charh gaya Garmi se hui pyas ki shiddat
Dil se kaha ab alam-e dar-e-fani se hai rukhsat

Ne lashkar-e aada ko na shamshir ko dekha
Kis yas se mur kar rukh-e Shabbir ko dekha

5
Sine chhida tiron se hua farq-e do para
Rag rag jo gayi phir na raha zabt ka yara
Shairaz ajaziye badan khul gaya sara
Garte hue Ghore se ye Hazrat ko pukara

Yan aap ka aana meri bakhshish ki sanad hai
Ay sher-e Ilahi ke pasrat waqt-e madad hai

6
Nagah saf-e chin pe aaye Shah-e Shah-e-zishan
Dekha ke wo Mazlum koi dam hai mehman
Lashe se lipat kar ye pukare ba-sad baad-e-afghan
Ay dost mere teri muhabbat ke mein qurban

Dikhla do mujhe zakhm kahan khaye hain bhai
Sine se lipat jao ke hum aaye hain bhai

[Makhaz: Baqi jo rafiq-e-Shah-e-din reh gaye do char]

Haftum se ta dahum jo hua qafile ka hal

1
Haftum se ta dahum jo hua qafile ka hal
Tahrir kar sake ye qalam ko nahin majal
Rad ho gaya jo itaat-e batil ka tha sawal
Bimar-e ashqiya mein ghira tha Nabi ka lal

Lekin sukun-e qalb mein koi khalal na tha
Lab khushk the jabin pe bal na tha

2
Zainab ke ladlon ne bhi jauhar dikha diye
Do nachin ne Lashon ke pushte laga diye
Jo sarma bane the wo ran se bhaga diye
Duniya ko jan-nisari ke raste bata diye

Kya jante paon sili-e mazalim ki Mauj mein
Aakhir Shahid ho gaye dushman ki Fauj-e mein

3
Zini albada farsh-e alalat pe be-kasi qarar
Koi ilaj aur na dawaon ka ikhtiyar
Sukhe Labun pe bas yahi aata tha bar bar
Ay Kash is-e Khums-o-jihad mein ho jate hum nisar

Do gam uth ke chalne ke qabil nahin hain hum
Dur sitam badalne ke qabil nahin hain hum
