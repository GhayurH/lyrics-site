Haftum se ta dahum jo hua qafile ka hal

1
Haftum se ta dahum jo hua qafile ka hal
Tahrir kar sake ye qalam ko nahin majal
Rad ho gaya jo taat-e-batil ka tha sawal
Anboh-e-ashqiya mein ghira tha Nabi ka lal

Lekin sukun-e-qalb mein koi khalal na tha
Lab khushk the jabin pe bal na tha

2
Zainab ke ladlon ne bhi jauhar dikha diye
Do nichon ne lashon ke pushte laga diye
Jo surma bane the wo ran se bhaga diye
Duniya ko jan-nisari ke raste bata diye

Kya jamte paon sail-e-mazalim ki mauj mein
Aakhir shahid ho gaye dushman ki fauj mein

3
Zain-ul-Abid farsh-e-alalat pe be-qarar
Koi ilaj aur na dawaon ka ikhtiyar
Sukhe labon pe bas yahi aata tha bar bar
Ay kash is jihad mein ho jate hum nisar

Do gam uth ke chalne ke qabil nahin hain hum
Dur sitam badalne ke qabil nahin hain hum
