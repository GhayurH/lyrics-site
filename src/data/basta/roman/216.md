Luta gaya jo Aal-e-Muhammad ka karwan

1
Luta gaya jo Aal-e-Muhammad ka karwan
Afsardat o malul Haram mein thin Ali bayan
Uthta tha dil se Zainab-e-maghmum-e ke dhuan
Tanha zamin par the Imam falak nishan

Sanche mein sabr o zabt o Raza ke dhale hue
Chehre pe khak-e turbat-e ashram hue

2
Yun ho rahi thi ruh-e Ruh-e-Payambar guftagu
Nana Khuda ne aaj kya mujhe Surkh ro
Islam ko mita na sake din ke adu
Batil ke dil mein reh gayi napaka arzu

Kya amharm hai chehra-e Haq dekh lijiye
Gul rang hai zamin ka waraq dekh lijiye

3
Is ka nahin malal ke khud tishna-lab hain hum
Sahil pe hai gira hua Abbaska alam
Tarikh-e rogar pe hai khun se raqam
Saqaye Saqqa-e-Ahl-e-Bait bazu hue Qalam

Reti par ek nishan-e wafa ibastab reh gaya
Pani ke sath khun Alamdar bahah gaya

4
Akbar zakhm-e Sine se ibla jo lahu
Is ne barhai aap ke waris ki aabru
Aisi jawan maut ke rone lage adu
Ajal ki ho lungi ， farishton ko arzu

Rishta Khuda se aur rag-e gardan kati hui
Kya saj rahi thi khak se zulfein kati hui

5
Kis shan se shahid hua Qasim jawan
Naushah jaise koi chala ho suye jinan
Sehre ke phul husn-e-amal ke fasana-khwan
Ek ek zakhm, zauq-e-shahadat ki dastan

Islam sarbuland hua, is inhaq mein
Apna shabab aap milaya hai khak mein

6
Dekha hai sab ne Aun-o-Muhammad ka shauq-e-jang
Kaisi shahid hone ki donon mein thi umang
Donon ki ek shan thi, donon ka ek dhang
Donon jama gaye dil-e-batil pe apna rang

Donon se maut aa ke ham-aghosh ho gayi
[Aakhri misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
