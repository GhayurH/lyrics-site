Qitaat

Qita

Kuchh to Haider, kuchh Ali, kuchh Murtaza kehne lage
Kuchh shab-e-hijrat, Muhammad Mustafa kehne lage
Darte darte hum ne to Allah ki qudrat kaha
Tauba tauba ye Nusairi to Khuda kehne lage

###

Kaleem-e-Aal-e-Aba Shahid Naqvi

Hamari kya hai ke hum sab to hain ghulam-e-Ali
Adu-e-naam-e-Ali bhi hain, zer-e-dam-e-Ali
Jo keh rahe hain ke hum naam-e-Ali na le koi
Wo khud ye kehte hue le rahe hain naam-e-Ali

###

Hazrat-e-Nafees Fatehpuri

Sote hi kab the Saqi-e-Kausar tamam raat
Karte the zikr-e-Khaliq-e-Akbar tamam raat
Bedar bakht-e-shab-e-hijrat gawah hai
Bas ek bar soye hain Haider tamam raat
(Bashukriya soz-o-salam khwan Doctor Sajjad o Shuja Rizvi)

###

Ustad Qamar Jalalvi Marhum

Mere liye taklif wo farmate hain
Aa aa ke mujhe khwab mein samjhate hain
Tum marsiya parhte ko jahan jate ho
Hum marsiya sunne ko wahan aate hain

###

Ustad Qamar Jalalvi

Musalmanon pe farz-e-Hajj ayan hai
Tawaf-e-Kaaba ek sirr-e-nihan hai
Haram ke gird phir kar dhoondte hain
Buton ka torne wala kahan hai

###

Agha Izzat-uz-Zaman Izzat Lakhnavi

Sab ke Mushkil-kusha hain mere Ali
Baat aisi dhaki chhupi kya hai
Shak agar hai pukar kar dekho
Hath kangan ko aarsi kya kya hai
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Akhtar Husain o Baqir Kazmi)
