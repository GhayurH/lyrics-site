Salam

Ahl-e-Bait-e-Pak se gustakhiyan be-bakiyan!
Jamil Lakhnavi

Ye Zainab deta tha kehna Husain hi ke liye
Karam ki bhik na mangon ga Zindagi liye
Husain khana Zahra mein aap kya aaye
Chiragh mil gaya Kaaba ko roshni liye

Khuda ne dobte suraj ko kar diya wapas
Nizam-ud-Din-e dahr badlan para Ali liye
Husain karte hain sairab Hurr ke lashkar ko
Kaleja chahiye dushman ki Dosti liye

Gham-e Husain mein ashkon ke bahah gaye darya
Zamana itna na Roya kabhi kisi ke liye
Jawan bete ki mayyat utha rahe hain Husain
Khuda ye waqt na laye kabhi kisi ke liye

Mein sath le ke Tajammul lahad mein jaun ga
Gham-e-Husain nahin sirf jite ji ke liye
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Jawad Husain Zaidi o Turab Jafari)

Doctor Professor Farhat Manzar Jafari

ek dur-e Islami duniya ka Husain mein aisa aata hai
Sibt Akbar Abadi

Ek-e dur-e salami duniya ka sadiyon mein aisa aata hai
Jab koi Payambar uthta hai Ya koi shahadat pata hai
Jab nauha Kaabe walon ka ro ro kar koi sunata hai
Sine mein ruh larzati hai jine se ji ghabarata hai

Ay nehr-e Furat Ay nehr-e Furat Afsos ke apni qismat par
Kausar ka saqi aaj tere sahil se Pyasa jata hai
Ay qahr-e Khuda is-e duniya par girti nahin kyun Bijli teri
Ghar se dur ek Pura kunba jangal mein luta jata hai

Insan tere patthar dil mein kyun dard nahin insanon ka
Dukh deta hai mazmulon ko Masumon ko tarastata hai
Sajjad asir jaur hue Afsos kisi ne ye na kaha
Ye paon sutun-e Kaaba hain zanjir kise pehnata hai

Samabim nazar aati hai mujhe har chiz udas aur azurda
fitrat-e ghamgin ho jati hai jab mah-e Muharram aata hai
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Doctor Alhaj Saeed-ul-Hasan o Shahab Haider)
