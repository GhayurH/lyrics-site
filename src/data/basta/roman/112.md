Allama Abdul Karim Warak

Gardan jhuki zamin pe jis dam Imam ki
Turbat hali Nabi alaihi Assalam ki
Dauraye Ghore thas tut pari Fauj-e Sham ki
Pyasa para hai saqi-e Kausar halq-e-burida sar

Yan ahl-e-matam-e bait rote rahe khun ki nadaniyan
Wan Naubaten machati rahi Fauj-e thas Sham ki
Dauraye ghore laut pari fauj-e-Sham ki
Adat thi is Lain ko sharb-e mudam ki

Khiti jali Batul ki thas Sadat kat gaye
Hurmat thi jin ki zat se bait al-ahran ki
Kunba lita Rasul alaihi Assalam ka
Ujri bahar rauza-e dar Assalam ki

Is sar zamin pe darsgah-e-aam ko thori si ja mile
Turbat hai jis zamin pe khair Khair-ul-Anam ki
(Iltimas Surah Fatiha baraye isal-e-sawab Syed Habib Razi ibn Syed Qaisar Jafari marhum)
