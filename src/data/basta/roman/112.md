Salam

Gardan jhuki zamin pe jis dam Imam ki
Allama Abdul Karim Warak

Gardan jhuki zamin pe jis dam Imam ki
Turbat hili Nabi alaihissalam ki
Dauraye ghore, tut pari fauj-e-Sham ki
Pyasa para hai Saqi-e-Kausar burida-sar

Yan Ahl-e-Bait rote rahe khun ki nadiyan
Wan naubaten machati rahi fauj, Sham ki
Haq par na ki Husain ne baiat Yazid ki
Aadat thi us la'in ko shurb-e-mudam ki

Kheti jali Batul ki, Sadat kat gaye
Hurmat thi jin ki zat se Bait-ul-Haram ki
Kunba luta Rasul alaihissalam ka
Ujri bahar Rauza-e-Dar-us-Salam ki

Us sar-zamin pe Dars ko thori si ja mile
Turbat hai jis zamin pe Khair-ul-Anam ki
(Iltimas Surah Fatiha baraye isal-e-sawab Syed Habib Razi ibn Syed Qaisar Jafari marhum)

Waris Syed-ul-Anam Husain
Yaad-e-Zain Shah Taji

Waris-e-Syed-ul-Anam Husain
Tum pe Allah ka salam Husain
Sab jawanan-e-khuld ke Sarwar
Hain Imam-e-Hasan, Imam Husain

Kausar-o-Salsabil ke mukhtar
Tishna-lab aur tishna-kam Husain
Jis ko paigham-e-aakhri kahiye
Hain wo Allah ka payam, Husain

Rakib-e-dosh-e-Sahib-e-Meraj
Allah Allah tera maqam Husain
Qudrat-e-intiqam thi phir bhi
Na liya tu ne intiqam Husain

Ruh ki tazgi hai teri yaad
Dil ki taskin tera naam Husain
Tera banda Zahin Taji hai
Tajwar main tere ghulam, Husain
