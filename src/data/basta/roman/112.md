Gahman hui Nabi alaihissalam ki
Dauraye ghore laut pari fauj-e-Sham ki

Yaan Ahl-e-Bait rote rahe khun ki nadiyan
Waan nok-e-neza bijli rahi fauj-e-Sham ki

Aadat thi us lain ko rishwat mudam ki
Kehti Ali betiyan, Sadat ki gayin

Kaaba laga Rasul alaihissalam ka
