Rawangi az Madina / Shahid-e-Abrar ne tark-e-watan kar diya

Aaj Madine mein hain gham ki farawaniyan
Aaj Madine mein hain dard ki tughyaniyan
Aaj Madine mein hain ashkon ki arzaniyan
Aaj Madine mein hain zalzala-samaniyan

Shahid-e-Abrar ne tark-e-watan kar diya
Qaum ke sardar ne tark-e-watan kar diya

Des se pardes mein zulm uthane chale
Din ki bigri hui baat banane chale
Quwwat-e-Islam ki shan barhane chale
Khun se be-shir ke rang jamane chale

Shahid-e-Abrar ne tark-e-watan kar diya
Qaum ke sardar ne tark-e-watan kar diya

Khalq ke ghamkhwar se shahr chhuta ghar chhuta
Sarwar-e-Kaunain ka rauza aur chhuta
Qabr-e-baradar chhuti, maskan-e-madar chhuta
Haye wo pyara gham jis se ke ghar bhar chhuta

Shahid-e-Abrar ne tark-e-watan kar diya
Qaum ke sardar ne tark-e-watan kar diya

Aaj Madina tere aine bikhar gaye
Zinat-e-sajda gaye, raunaq-e-mimbar gaye
Sahib-e-mimbar gaye, Waris-e-Haider gaye
Jane zara tu ne kyun jane wo kyunkar gaye

Shahid-e-Abrar ne tark-e-watan kar diya
Qaum ke sardar ne tark-e-watan kar diya

Ahl-e-Madina se keh do ke kuchh bole
Kaise ye ghaflat hui dida-e-dil khole
Nale kiye aah ki, ashkon se munh dhole
Jaan-e-Payambar gaya, sath na sab ho liye

Shahid-e-Abrar ne tark-e-watan kar diya
Qaum ke sardar ne tark-e-watan kar diya

Tujh ko khabar bhi hai kuchh ab na idhar aayenge
Tere musafir kabhi phir ke na ghar aayenge
Abid-e-Mazlum faqat le ke khabar aayenge
Dagh badal aayenge, khak-basar aayenge

Shahid-e-Abrar ne tark-e-watan kar diya
Qaum ke sardar ne tark-e-watan kar diya
