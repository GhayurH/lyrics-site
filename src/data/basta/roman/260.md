Zindan mein jabke Dukhtar-e-Shabbir mar gayi

Sar jo Shabbir ka zindan mein laye khuddam

1
Sar jo Shabbir ka zindan mein laye khuddam
Bhai ke sar ko kiya Zainab-e-muztar ne salam
Tha giraftar-e-salasil jo zamane ka Imam
Utha tazim ko le kar Shah-e-Mazlum ka naam

Tasht halqe mein liye kasrat-e-gham rone lage
Sar-e-Shabbir ko dekha to Haram rone lage

2
Sab ne matam kiya mil kar, hui Majlis barpa
Chum kar sar ko aqidat se ye Zainab ne kaha
Dukh uthaye hain bahut tum se bichhar kar bhaiya
Apni aghosh mein sar Baba Sakina ne liya

Dur rehte the bhi ruke hi kahan the ansu
Sar-e-Shabbir ki ankhon se rawan the ansu

3
Hal kehne ka ye lamha jo Sakina ko mila
Sar-e-Shabbir ko sine se laga kar ye kaha
Chhor kar hum ko gaye aap kahan ay Baba
Aap ke baad sitam tute hain hum par kya kya

Mere rukhsar pe jo nil ayan hain Baba
Ye mere munh pe tamanchon ke nishan hain Baba
