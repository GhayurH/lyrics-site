[Rukhsat hua Husain se jab naujawan pisar — jari]

4
Hathon se dil ko tham ke ran ki taraf chalin
Jate the larkharate udhar khud bhi Shah-e-din
Dam torta tha ran mein jahan Akbar-e-hazin
Zainab wahan Husain se pahle pahunch gayin

Aaye nazar jo zakhm tan-e-pash-pash par
Teora ke gir parin Ali Akbar ki lash par

5
Khaime mein lash aai to mahshar hua bapa
Laila bas itna keh sakin Aqa ye kya hua
Abid the ghash mein chaunk pare hosh mein aa gaya
Dekhi jo lash ho gaye phir ghash mein mubtala

Mur kar nazar mariz pe dali Husain ne
Kuchh soch kar nigah chura li Husain ne

6
Shayad diyar-e-Sham ka manzar nazar mein tha
Zakhmi gale ke tauq ka langar nazar mein tha
Mazlum qaidiyon ka muqaddar nazar mein tha
Be-asra bahen ka khula sar nazar mein tha

Bazu pe rassiyon ki girah garti jati thi
Koron ki sain sain ki awaz aati thi

Akbar ki lash jabke na pai Husain ne

1
Akbar ki lash jabke na pai Husain ne
Thokar har ek gam pe khai Husain ne
Gir gir zamin pe khak urai Husain ne
Dil tham kar ye bat sunai Husain ne

Ankhon se sujhta nahin mujhe dil makal ko
Ay zalimo, dikha do Shabbir-e-Rasul ko

2
Kyun kar dikhaun tum ko jo dil ka hai mere hal
Mehman ke ranj-o-gham ka tumhein chahiye khayal
Bazu pakar ke le chalo jis ja hai mera lal
Ghurbat pe meri rahm karo bahr-e-Zuljalal

Muztar hun shakl us ki dikha do Husain ko
Farzand-e-naujawan se mila do Husain ko

3
Ay fauj-e-zulm bato ke dilbar ko kya kiya
Be-kas ko tishna-kam ke be-par ko kya kiya
Ay zalimo, Shabbir-e-Payambar ko kya kiya
Milta nahin pata, Ali Akbar ko kya kiya

Rasta to bata do ke bhatka hun rah mein
Kis ne gira diya mere Yusuf ko chah mein
