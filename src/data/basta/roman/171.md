4
Hathon se dil ko tham ke ran ki taraf chalin
Jate the rakhrate idhar khud bhi Shah-e din
Dam tarat tha ran mein jahan Akbar-e hazin
Zainab wahan Husain se pahle pahunch gayin

Aaye nazar jo zakhm tan-e pash pash par
Teora ke Gar parin Ali Akbar ki lash par

5
Khaime mein lash aai to mahshar hua bapa
Laila bas itna keh saken Aqa ye kya hua
Abid the ghash mein Chaunk pare Hosh mein aa gaya
Dekhi jo lash ho gaye phir ghash mein mubtala

Mur kar nazar mariz pe dali Husain ne
Kuchh soch kar nigah chalari Husain ne

6
Shayad diyar-e-Sham ka manzar nazar mein tha
Zakhmi gale ke tauq ka langar nazar mein tha
Mazlum qaidiyon ka muqaddar nazar mein tha
Be-kasi aasra bahen ka khila sar nazar mein tha

Bazu pe rassiyon ki girah girti jati thi
Koron ki sain sain ki awaz aati thi

Akbar ki lash jabke na pai Husain ne

1
Akbar ki lash jabke na pai Husain ne
Thokar har ek gam pe khai Husain ne
Gar Gar zamin pe khak arain Husain ne
Dil tham kar ye baat sunai Husain ne

Ankhon se sojhta nahin mujhe dil-e maklak ko
Ay zalimo thas dikhado Shabbir-e Rasul ko

2
Kar dikhaun tum ko jo dil-e ka hai mere hal
Mehman ke ranj o gham ka tumhein chahiye khayal
Bazu pakar ke le chalo jis ja hai mera lal
Ghurbat pe meri Rahm karo bahr-e zillajal

Muztar hun shakl is ki dikha Husain ko
Farzand-e najwanon se mila do Husain ko

3
Ay Fauj-e zulm bato ke dilbar ko kya kya
Kis ko tishna kam ke be-kasi par ko kya kya
Ay zalimo thas Shabbir-e Payambar ko kya kya
Milta nahin pata thas Ali Akbar ko kya kya

Rasta to bata do ke bhatakta hun rah mein
Kis ne gira diya mere Yusuf-e ko chah mein
