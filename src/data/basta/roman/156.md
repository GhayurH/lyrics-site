Shah ne jab chand-e-Muharram ka safar mein dekha

1
Shah ne jab chand-e-Muharram ka safar mein dekha
Mahshar-e-qatl raqam ek satr mein dekha
Bal jabin falak-e-zulm-o-sair mein dekha
Maut ko lut machate hue dekha

Surkhi-e-khun shafaq-e-qalb-e-barmane lagi
Sham-e-Ashur ki taswir nazar aane lagi

2
Asp ko rok ke parhne lage ruyat ki dua
Zindagi bhar se zawali thi ye Hazrat ki dua
Wusat-e-rizq ka mafhum na sehat ki dua
Haq se ki arz main karta hun shahadat ki dua

Sabr wo bakhsh ke ta roz-e-jaza dhum rahe
Hashr tak naam mera Syed-e-Mazlum rahe

3
Kuchh mere waste aisa ho mubarak ye mah
Sadqe Islam pe ho jaun mile izzat-o-jah
Fidya-e-rah-e-raza de laqab ay Bar-e-Ilah
Khun mein tan gharq ho khushki mein safina ho tabah

Qaid hon Ahl-e-Haram ghar ki safai ho jaye
Sab sitam hon magar ummat ki bhalai ho jaye
