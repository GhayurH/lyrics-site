Shah ne jab chand-e-Muharram ka safar mein dekha

1
Shah ne jab chand Muharram ka safar mein dekha
Mahshar qatl raqam Ek satr mein dekha
Bal jabin falak-e zulm o nek-sair mein dekha
Maut ko lut Maut hue dekha

Sarkhir-e khun Shafaq-e qalb-e barmane lagi
Sham-e Ashur ki taswir nazar aane lagi

2
Apsa ko rok ke parhne lage riwayat ki dua
Zindagi bhar se zalwai thi ye Hazrat ki dua
Wusat-e rizq ka mafhum na sahta ki dua
Haq se ki arz mein karta hun shahadat ki dua

Sabr wo bakhsh ke ta roz-e jaza dhum rahe
Hashr tak naam mera Syed-e Mazlum rahe

3
