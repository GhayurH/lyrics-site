4
Qatl ho jab mera lashkar na jalal aaye mujhe
Sab azizon ke kahin sar na jalal aaye mujhe
Khun mein dube Ali Akbar na jalal aaye mujhe
Tarpe hathon pe jo Asghar na jalal aaye mujhe

Phul mera mera shukr baja laun mein
Aap nanhi si lahad khod ke duaon mein

5
Nikle arman jo zaban pyas se bahar nikle
Samne ankhon ke jaan-e-Ali Akbar nikle
Par gila munh se Ay Khaliq-e Akbar nikle
Tham lun dil jo bahen ghar se tarap kar nikle

Jo satain na daraon na saza dun un ko
Dagh par dagh wo din aur mein dua dun un ko

6
Shah ne Farmaya ke mahshar mein ye iqrar bhi hai
Darj-e fard-e Shuhada Qasim nachar bhi hai
Fauj-e ka zikr bhi hai naam-e Alamdar bhi hai
Marne walon mein Ali Akbar Jarrar bhi hai

Imtihan ka koi gushush na falak chhore ga
Mera Asghar bhi meri god mein dam tore ga

Ay Ahl-e-Aza phir alam o gham ke din aaye
1
Phir taziya o shewan o matam ke din aaye
Phir ke Shah-e do alam ke din aaye
Phir bekasi-e-Shah-e-do-alam ke din aaye

Phir jin o bashar mahv-e-aza hue aah o buka mein
Phir Fatima betab hain pyaron ki aza mein

2
Is gham se ghar abad hue Ahl-e-Aza ke
Har samt ye jalwe hain Shah ke har dusra ke
Qurban Itaye par-e Shah-e Khuda ke
Rauze se chale hain ghar par ghar ke

Ashkon se na kyun tar ho gareban-e hamara
Zahra ka jigar band hai mehman-e hamara

Ankhon se na kyun gham ho giryan hamara
Ho ga na yun kisi dilgir ka matam
Akbar ka kabhi gham thas kabhi be-kasi sher ka matam
Qasim ka alam thas bazwaye Shabbir ka matam
Gaye-e qaq ka matam thas kabhi zanjir ka matam

Masruf-e-aza-e aza qalb bhi hai ruh bhi tan bhi
Rote hain use ronha saki jis ko bahen bhi
