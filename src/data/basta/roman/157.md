Ay Ahl-e-Aza phir alam-o-gham ke din aaye

1
Ay Ahl-e-Aza phir alam-o-gham ke din aaye
Phir taziya-o-shewan-o-matam ke din aaye
Phir be-kasi-e-Shah-e-do-alam ke din aaye
Phir aai qiyamat ke Muharram ke din aaye

Phir jinn-o-bashar mahw hue aah-o-buka mein
Phir Fatima be-tab hain pyaron ki aza mein

2
Is gham se ghar abad hue Ahl-e-Aza ke
Har samt ye jalwe hain Shah ke har dusra ke
Qurban ata-e-par-e-Sher-e-Khuda ke
Rauze se chale hain ghar par ghuraba ke

Ashkon se na kyun tar ho gareban-e-hamara
Zahra ka jigar-band hai mehman hamara

3
Hoga na yun kisi dilgir ka matam
Akbar ka kabhi gham, kabhi be-shir ka matam
Qasim ka alam, bazu-e-Shabbir ka matam
Gah-e-qa ka matam, kabhi zanjir ka matam

Masruf-e-aza qalb bhi hai ruh bhi tan bhi
Rote hain use ro na saki jis ko bahen bhi
