Kya Sibt-e-Mustafa ki shahadat ki raat thi

1
Kya Sibt-e-Mustafa ki shahadat ki raat thi
Afat ki raat thi wo musibat ki raat thi
Alam ke Badshah ki rehlat ki raat thi
Zahra o Murtaza pe qayamat ki raat thi

Guzri qaqqq mein Fatima ke nur-e ein ko
Bahr-e Hasan mein nind na aai Husain ko

2
Takiye lagaye the Haram ne idhar-e idhar
Bazu ko koi thamta tha aur koi sar
Reh reh ke katta hai koi tegh se jigar
[Ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]

Sine se munh se tukre kaleje ke aate hain
