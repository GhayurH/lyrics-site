Kya Sibt-e-Mustafa ki shahadat ki raat thi

1
Kya Sibt-e-Mustafa ki shahadat ki raat thi
Afat ki raat thi, wo musibat ki raat thi
Alam ke Badshah ki rehlat ki raat thi
Zahra Murtaza pe qayamat ki raat thi

Guzri falak mein Fatima ke nur-e-ain ko
Phir huzn mein nind na aai Husain ko

2
