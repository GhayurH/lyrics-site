Kya Sibt-e-Mustafa ki shahadat ki rat thi

1
Kya Sibt-e-Mustafa ki shahadat ki rat thi
Afat ki rat thi wo musibat ki rat thi
Alam ke Badshah ki rehlat ki rat thi
Zahra-o-Murtaza par qayamat ki rat thi

Guzri qalaq mein Fatima ke nur-e-ain ko
Bahr-e-Hasan mein nind na aai Husain ko

2
Takiye lagaye the Haram ne idhar udhar
Bazu ko koi thamta tha aur koi sar
Reh reh ke katta hai koi tegh se jigar
[Ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]

Sine se munh se tukre kaleje ke aate hain
Nana bula rahe hain so duniya se jate hain

3
Fizza ne ja ke di Shah-e-zi-jah ko khabar
Dar-e-fana se aap ke bhai ka hai safar
Daure Husain chak-gareban, barahna sar
Dekha tarap rahe hain Shahanshah-e-bahr-o-bar

Rakh kar ke hath, sar pe sab khalq hat gayi
Phaila ke hath lash se Zainab lipat gayi
