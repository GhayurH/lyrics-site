[Jab chale Yasrib se Sibt-e-Mustafa suye Iraq — jari]

7
Sun ke ye baten hui har Ek ki halat tabah
Thi sadaye wa Husaina nala o faryad o aah
Is taraf hilta tha gandab is-e taraf rote the Shah
Dam ba dam Marqad pe thi ek yas o hasrat ki nigah

Haye jab rukhsat hue qabr-e Rasul-e Allah se
Dur tak dekha kiye mur mur ke hasrat rah se

8
Rukhsat-e khair al-sabhri se dil hua Shah ka figar
Aaye man ki qabr par rukhsat ko rote zar zar
Qabr-e Madar par jhuka milne ko jab wo be-kasi qarar
Fatima Zahra ki turbat kanp uthi Ek bar

Sare Ansar o Quraysh is waqt ji khone lage
Mard o zan Jitne khare the sab ke sab rone lage

Jab hua Yusuf-e-Zahra ka safar Yasrib se

1
Jab hua Yusuf-e Zahra ka safar Yathrib se
Chand Haider ka chala waqt-e sahar Yathrib se
Sath rahi hue sab khwish o pisar Yasrib se
Nikle maghmum Shah-e jin o bashar Yathrib se

Lahad-e khatm-e rasl thas turbat-e Zahra chhoti
Ghar buzurgon ka chhuta thas Fatima Sughra chhoti

2
Sughra kehti thi na gham kijiye bimari ka
Paon par girti hun mein mujh ko na chhoro Lillah
Hath mein jorti hun ay mere achchhe Baba
Rahm kijiye meri halat pe ye nidhal ho fida

Achchhi mein ho lun to hun Yan se rawana Baba
Ya mein duniya se guzar jaun to jana Baba

3
Keh ke ye Shah se munh pher ke wo rone lagi
Baat se bas na chala ashkon se munh dhone lagi
Yas jane se hui thas jaan hazin khone lagi
Haram-e Shah mein faryad o buka hone lagi

Un ko aram na tha be-kasi kis o tanha liye
Munh ko aate the jigar beti-e Sughra liye
