[Jab chale Yasrib se Sibt-e-Mustafa su-e-Iraq — jari]

7
Sun ke ye baten hui har ek ki halat tabah
Thi sada-e-Wa Husaina nala-o-faryad-o-aah
Us taraf hilta tha gumbad is taraf rote the Shah
Dam-ba-dam marqad pe thi ek yas-o-hasrat ki nigah

Haye jab rukhsat hue qabr-e-Rasulullah se
Dur tak dekha kiye mur mur ke hasrat rah se

8
Rukhsat-e-Khair-ul-Bashar se dil hua Shah ka figar
Aaye man ki qabr par rukhsat ko rote zar zar
Qabr-e-madar par jhuka milne ko jab wo be-qarar
Fatima Zahra ki turbat kanp uthi ek bar

Sare Ansar-o-Quraish us waqt ji khone lage
Mard-o-zan jitne khare the sab ke sab rone lage

Jab hua Yusuf-e-Zahra ka safar Yasrib se

1
Jab hua Yusuf-e-Zahra ka safar Yasrib se
Chand Haider ka chala waqt-e-sahar Yasrib se
Sath rahi hue sab khwaish-o-pisar Yasrib se
Nikle maghmum Shah-e-jinn-o-bashar Yasrib se

Lahad-e-Khatm-e-Rusul, turbat-e-Zahra chhuti
Ghar buzurgon ka chhuta, Fatima Sughra chhuti

2
Sughra kehti thi na gham kijiye bimari ka
Paon par girti hun main mujh ko na chhoro lillah
Hath mein jorti hun ay mere achchhe Baba
Rahm kijiye meri halat pe ye laundi ho fida

Achchhi main ho lun to hun yan se rawana Baba
Ya main duniya se guzar jaun to jana Baba

3
Keh ke ye Shah se munh pher ke wo rone lagi
Bat se bas na chala ashkon se munh dhone lagi
Yas jane se hui, jaan-e-hazin khone lagi
Haram-e-Shah mein faryad-o-buka hone lagi

Un ko aram na tha be-kas-o-tanha ke liye
Munh ko aate the jigar beti-e-Sughra ke liye
