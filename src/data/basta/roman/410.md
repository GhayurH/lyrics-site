Lena hi para jhuti Hadison ka sahara
Zahra se jo lena hua dushwar inkar-e-Fadak ka
Qurba ko Payambar ke wo karte nahin taslim
Ghairon ko batate hain jo Haq dar inkar-e-Fadak ka
Shayad unhein ye raz bhi malum nahin hai
Quran ki tauhin hai inkar-e-Fadak ka
un zalimon ke naam amal mein shamil
Narazi-e-Zahra bhi hai aur bar-e-Fadak ka
Jo likh ke Payambar ne diya ek wasiqah
chhura diya Zahra ko jo Haq dar inkar-e-Fadak ka
Zalim ne wo tahrir Nabi phar ke phenki
Aur Fatima se kar diya inkar-e-Fadak ka
Haqdar ko karta hai wirasat se jo mahrum
ta hashr rahe ga wo gunahgar inkar-e-Fadak ka
Hai Munkir-e-Zahra ki saza jin ko bhi malum
wo zikr bhi karte nahin sarkar inkar-e-Fadak ka

Har qadam tha wird-e-zaban dam hama-dam, dam Maula
Sibt Jafar

Kab thi rah mein teri fikr-e-besh-o-kam Maula
Har qadam tha wird-e-zaban dam hama-dam, dam Maula
Kab hamare bas mein thi baryabi is dar ki
Aap ne bulaya to aa gaye hain hum Maula
Jite ji na bhulega hum ko tera dastarkhwan
Nematein do aalam ki thin jahan baham Maula
Tere mehman ban kar chum kar tera sang-e-dar
Ho gaye hain duniya mein hum bhi muhtaram Maula
Aap jis se razi hon wo khushi ata kijiye
Aur bajuz gham-e-Sarwar ho na koi gham Maula
Ab koi nahin puchhta shahryar-o-takht-o-taj
Jab se hai nigahon mein aap ka hasham Maula
Har qadam teri janib qurbat-e-ilallah hai
Bichh rahe hain rahon mein Kausar-o-Iram Maula
Lag raha hai yun mujh ko jaise mil gai Meraj
Jab se chum aaya hun aap ke qadam Maula
