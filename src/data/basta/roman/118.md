Salam

Bais-e-rashk na kyun ho sar-e-kham ka muqaddar nikle
Doctor Professor Qamar Naqvi

Bais-e rashk na kyun Hurr ka muqaddar nikle
jo gada ho ke bhi qismat ka Sikandar nikle
Raat kya khatm hui Hurr ki to qismat chumki
Subh zadi-e ansan ka ye manzar nikle

Jis ne dekha, kya Masum pe Quran ka guman
Jabke Shabbir liye hathon pe Asghar nikle
Muskura kar sar-e maidan Ali Asghar ne kaha
Hum bhi, Bhaiya Ali Akbar ke barabar nikle

Pehle qurban to kiye Aun-o-Muhammad lekin
dekhiye Hazrat-e Zainab, Ali Akbar nikle
Sibt-e sarkar-e do alam ke ho Khadam Taqvi
Tum bazil-e Shah-e laulak sukhanwar nikle
(Bashukriya Shair o sozkhwan Syed Abid Husain Naqvi / Malan, al-Wardi)

Jo shab ko din-e bana lal o gauhar aise hote hain
Hakim Arif Akbar marhum

Josh kyun bada lal o gauhar aise hote hain
Hussaini asman ke mah-e o Akhtar aise hote hain
Jo chahin Karbala ko dam mein Gulzar-e Iram kardin
Gulistan-e Muhammad ke gul-e tar aise hote hain

jo dekha jang mein Aun o Muhammad ko ajal Boli
Sar-e maqtal kahin bachchon ke tewar aise hote hain
Ali Akbar ko ran mein dekh kar aada pukar uthe
Khuda ki shan hamshakh-e Payambar aise hote hain

Talab karta hai kya sail ata hota hai kya Mohsin
Kaho hote se dekhe banda Banda-parwar aise hote hain
(Bashukriya martabakhwan Syed Zamir Haider Naqvi, Haiderpur Khas)

Salam ba-huzur Sarkar Syed-ush-Shuhada Imam Husain
Syed Javed Raza (Islamabad)

Asr-e-Ashur hai aur chehra-e-taban Husain
Zer-e-khanjar hai ada-e-sajda chahan Husain
har muqaddar mein khan ulfat o irfan-e Husain
Karbala ho chuki, tazh hai gulistan-e Husain

Chahe insan ho, fitrat saf na bhi ho
Dard se rone ka saman hoga darban Husain

Umar bhar samne mein baithti jo nahin, baad Imam
bazwaye Shah se mujhe Sham-e Sham-e-Ghariban Husain
Waqt-e-rehlat na bhi hath ko khali rakhna
Jao duniya se to hath mein daman Husain

Takhti the wo behtar hi zamana mein Raza
Koi taqtul kahan aap ki mehman Husain
