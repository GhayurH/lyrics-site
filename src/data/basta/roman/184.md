[Mashk bhar kar suye khaima jo Alamdar chale — jari]

7
Us dil-e-angaar Chashm-e mubarak mein gara ek nisar
Tir kha kar na sambhala bhi na tha sher-e sud-o-ziyan
Nagahan mashk-e sher-e-ghazabnak sar pe laga ho-e shikar
Ab bhatije ki taslim, Imam-e-do-jahan

Nahr par aane ki taklif jo farmai hai
Aap ke sath Sakina to nahin aai hai

8
Bole Shabbir, wo khaime pe kari roti hai
Aap ki yaad mein betab hai, jaan khoti hai
Dam-ba-dam pyas ki iza jo hawa hoti hai
Nahr ko takti hai aur ankhon se nahr roti hai

Khun behta raha jab tak na zahnar gire
Ab meri lash ko khaime mein na le jaiye ga

Jab hue bazu-e-Abbas qalam darya par

Bahah gaya aab to reti par Alamdar gire

2
Jab hue bazwaye Abbas Qalam darya par
Gar ke thanda hua Hazrat ka alam darya par
Gharaq-e khun ho gaya wo Bahr-e karam darya par
Ghul tha Zakhmi hua saqaye-e Haram darya par

Mashk ko danton mein pakre hue yun lata hai
Ya Ali kehte hain aur mashk pe jhuk jate hain

3
Ghash ki Amad hai jigar soz the se hai kabab
Hain jo be-kasi dast-e-sitam thehtir nahin paon mein rakba
Pyase bachchon ke liye sine mein dil hai be-kasi tab
Gham yahi hai ke kahin mashk na zaiya na ho aab

Aap ke bachche ka saqqa hun, madad lazim hai
Aap ki beti ka saqqa hun, madad lazim hai

4
Kar ke munh suye Najaf kehte the badid tar
Ya Ali lijiye mujhe be-kasi kis o muztar ki khabar
Ay Shah-e aqda kusha bashid jin o bashar
Chahta hun ke is-e mashk ko pahunche na khauf-e-zarar

Aap ke bete ka shaida hun madad lazim hai
Aap ki poti ka Saqqa-e hun madad lazim hai
