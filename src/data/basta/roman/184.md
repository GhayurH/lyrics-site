7
Aye mire quwwat-e-bazu mire baba ke nishaan
abhi zinda ho ke jannat mein gaye bhai jaan
sun ke ye hosh jo aaya to kaha mein qurbaan
abd-e-naacheez ki taslim, imam-e-do jahan

Nehr par aane ki taklif jo farmai hai
Aap ke sath Sakina to nahin aai hai

8
Bole Shabbir, wo dehwri pe khari roti hai
Aap ki yaad mein betab hai, jaan khoti hai
Dam-ba-dam pyas ki iza jo hawa hoti hai
Nahr ko takti hai aur ashkon se munh dhoti hai

Khun behta raha jab tak na zinhar gire
Ab meri lash ko khaime mein na le jaiyega

Jab hue bazu-e-Abbas qalam darya par

1
Jab hue bazu-e-Abbas qalam darya par
Gir ke thanda hua Hazrat ka alam darya par
Gharq-e-khun ho gaya wo bahr-e-karam darya par
Ghul tha zakhmi hua Saqqa-e-Haram darya par

Mashk ko danton mein pakre hue yun lata hai
Dahan-e-sher mein jis tarah shikar aata hai

2
Ghash ki amad hai jigar-soz atash se hai kabab
Hain jo be-dast thaharti nahin paon mein rikab
Pyase bachchon ke liye sine mein dil hai betab
Gham yahi hai ke kahin mashk na zaya na ho aab

Tir-e-paiham jo kamanon se chale aate hain
Ya Ali kehte hain aur mashk pe jhuk jate hain

3
Kar ke munh su-e-Najaf kehte the ba-dida-e-tar
Ya Ali lijiye mujhe be-kas-o-muztar ki khabar
Ay Shah-e-Uqda-kusha Badshah-e-jinn-o-bashar
Chahta hun ke is mashk ko pahunche na zarar

Aap ke bete ka shaida hun madad lazim hai
Aap ki poti ka saqqa hun madad lazim hai
