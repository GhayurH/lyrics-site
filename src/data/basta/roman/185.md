Tegh ka war jo Abbas ke shane pe laga

1
Pe se kehte the Abbas ye bashim-e par aab
Yan se le chal mujhe Ay apsa-e wafadar-e tashab
Pyase hun hue jo sairab to hath ga isal-e-sawab
Kahin aisa na sari meri mehnat ho kharab

Haye kya aane kya bachchon ki halat ho lungi
Mar gayi pyasi Sakina to qayamat ho lungi

[makhm: jab hue bazwaye Abbas Qalam darya par]

Mirza Asad Allah Khan Ghalib
Ye ujjati ajab hai ke Ek dushman-e din
Ali se aage guzre aur [misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]

2
Dafta dusre bazu pe bhi ek war laga
Aur wo hath bhi shane se qalam ho ke gira

Keh rahe the ye fars se Khudara jaldi

3
Nagahan mashk pe aa kar laga ek tir-e-jafa
Kat ke bazwaye Alamdar zamin par aaya
Dusre hath mein mashkize ko aakhir thama
Aur is sadme se Abbas ka dil tut gaya

Tuti umid jo pyason ki, baad-e-yas gire
Abdida hue is-e Sadme se Abbas bahut

Mirza Asadullah Khan Ghalib
Dafatan Dusre bazu par bhi ek war laga
Aur wo hath bhi shane se Qalam ho ke gira
Mashk ko danton se Abbas-e jari ne thama
Fasalis khamiy ithar na kam hota tha

Keh rahe the ye fars se ke Khudaya jaldi
Ali se aa ke lare aur khata nahin us ki

Hakim Momin Khan Momin
Nagahan mashk par aa ke laga ek tir-e jafa
Aakhirash bahah gaya pani wo zamin par sara
Behte pani ko dilawar ne ba hasrat dekha
Aur is-e Sadme se Abbas ka dil tut gaya

Tutti ummid jo pyason ki ba-sad yas gire
Zarb ek sar pe lagi Ghore se Abbas gire
