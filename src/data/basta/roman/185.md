Tegh ka war jo Abbas ke shane pe laga

1
tegh ka war jo Abbas ke shane pe laga
Kat ke bazwaye Alamdar zamin par aaya
Dusre hath mein mashkize ko aakhir thama
aur Ghore ki bhi rftar ko kuchh tiz kya

yaad aane lagi bachchon ki unhein pyas bahut

2
Dafta dusre bazu pe bhi ek war laga
Aur wo hath bhi shane se Qalam ho ke gira
Mashk ko danton se Abbas-e jari ne thama
Fasalis-e khamiy-e Athar na kam hota tha

Keh rahe the ye fars se ke Khudaya jaldi
mujh ko to khidmat-e Shabbir mein pahuncha jaldi

3
