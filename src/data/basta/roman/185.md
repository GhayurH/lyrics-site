Tigh ka war jo Abbas ke shane pe laga

1
Tigh ka war jo Abbas ke shane pe laga
Kat ke bazu-e-Alamdar zamin par aaya
Dusre hath mein mashkize ko aakhir thama
Aur ghore ki bhi raftar ko kuchh tez kiya

Yaad aane lagi bachchon ki unhein pyas bahut
Aab-dida hue is sadme se Abbas bahut

2
Dafatan dusre bazu par bhi ek war laga
Aur wo hath bhi shane se qalam ho ke gira
Mashk ko danton se Abbas-e-jari ne thama
Fasla-e-khaima-e-athar na kam hota tha

Keh rahe the ye faras se ke Khudaya jaldi
Mujh ko tu khidmat-e-Shabbir mein pahuncha jaldi

3
Nagahan mashk par aa ke laga ek tir-e-jafa
Aakhirash beh gaya pani wo zamin par sara
Behte pani ko dilawar ne ba-hasrat dekha
Aur is sadme se Abbas ka dil tut gaya

Tutti umid jo pyason ki ba-sad-yas gire
Zarb ek sar pe lagi ghore se Abbas gire
