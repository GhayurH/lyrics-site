Soz

Khaime darya pe kate jab ke Shah-e wala ne
Gher Shabbir ko Tab Fauj-e sitam aara ne
Lab-e darya pe utrane na diya aada ne
Fauj-e-ada se ye Shabbir lage farmane

Ye bhi do char din ab hum pe guzar jayenge
Gar raza Haq ki hai to pyase hi mar jayenge
(Bashukriya nauha o sozkhwan Zafar Husain Shahid o baradaran / Lahore)

Tasbih-e-Fatima jo ada ki Imam ne
Jaswas ne khabar ye kahi aake samne
Ki nek-sair ghata ghata ki is-e dam ghulam ne
Ab-e-rawan bhi band kiya fauj-e-Sham ne

Fauj-e Khuda ko nehr se duridi nasib hai
Shah bole kya mazima Kausar qarib hai

Hua jo Shah ke lashkar mein qaht-e-pani pani ka
Jab aai Subh-e qatl-e Imam-e falak Waqar
Kabhi khayal tha Akbar ki naujawani ka
Kabhi malal tha Asghar ki be-zabani ka

Kabhi bahen ke liye be-qarar hote the
Kabhi Sakina ka munh dekh dekh rote the
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Mohsin Husain Amrohvi o Kirdar Husain Zaidi)

Jab na aada se kisi tarah safai thehri
Subh-e Ashur-e Muharram ko larai thehri
Zainab utha ke hath ye kehti thin bar bar
Shah ne farmaya bahen tum se judai thehri

Aaj pyaron se mulaqat-e mal-e-ghanimat jano
Ay bahen aaj ki ye raat mal-e-ghanimat jano

Jab raat ibadat mein basar ki Shah-e-Din ne
Sajde mein muhim ishq ki sar ki Shah-e-Din ne
Dekha jo safedi ko sahar ki Shah-e-Din ne
Mur kar rukh-e-Akbar pe nazar ki Shah-e-Din ne
Farmaya sahar qatl ki zahir hui beta
Ab uth ke azan do ke shab-e-akhir hui beta

Jab rat-e-shahadat mein barqi Shabbir ne
Pardisiyon pe Rahm kar Ay mere Kirdigar
Kar jo lungi bhai se chhut kar mein sogwar
Mur kar rukh-e-Akbar nazar ki shahdin ne

Sara jahan siyah hai Chashm-e par aab mein
Sar nange mein ne dekha hai Amman ko khwab mein
(Bashukriya shair o sozkhwan Syed Shahid Hasan Zaidi)
