Rubaiyat

Rubai

Karte hain Ahl-e-Dard hi chehlum Husain ka
Hai qudsiyon mein aaj tarannum Husain ka
Kehte hain mujh se log ke kyun Maulvi Zahur
Karte nahin ho zikr yahan tum Husain ka

Rubai

Ek samt shahidon mein gharib-ul-watani hai
Ek samt lainon mein shaqawat ki thani hai
Sab jante hain Zahur rutba un ka
Sadat hain aur fauj-e-Husain-o-Hasani hai

Rubai

Dil se na ho dard kam, Husain ka
Barhta rahe roz-e-gham, Husain ka
Shadab rahen ye Ahl-e-Majlis sare
Parhte rahen Zahur bhi chehlum Husain ka

Rubai

Abid na kabhi ranj-o-mihan se nikla
Nikla bhi to bas shukr-dahan se nikla
Bimar ne pehni jo nai kali poshak
Sab ne jana ke hai ye chand gahan se nikla

Rubai

Manqush hai dil par mere naam Haider
Allah ke ghar mein hai qiyam Haider
Baqir mujhe kyun arsa-e-mahshar ka ho khauf
Main roz-e-azal se hun ghulam-e-Haider

Rubai

Hurr ko Shabbir ne kya ran ki ijazat de di
Nar ko nur kiya aur shahadat de di
Kya sakhawat hai Husain Ibn-e-Ali ki wallah
Jam-e-Kausar ka diya rehne ko Jannat de di

Rubai

jab Hurr ka Be-gunah Shah-e ammam ne bakhsha
Qatre ko sharaf bahr-e-karam ne bakhsha
Gardun se nida aai ke ay Sibt-e-Nabi
Bakhsha jise tu ne use hum ne bakhsha

Rubai

Sibt-e-Khatm-ul-Anbiya ko kyun na royen Ahl-e-Dad
Jin ke marne ka rahega ghul ila Yaum-it-Tanad
Aise shahid ki shahadat ka to Haq bhi hai gawah
Aaya hai Quran mein bhi Kaf, Ha, Ya, Ain, Sad
