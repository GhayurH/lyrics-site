Wahi to fath-o-zafar ke nishan uthate hain
Haji Haq Rizvi

Wahi to fath-o-zafar ke nishan uthate hain
Jihad-e-Haq mein jo azar-e-jaan uthate hain

Nishan-e-rah jo roshan hain chhup nahin sakte
Ghubar agarche bahut karwan uthate hain

Yahi shanakht hai un ki ke mard-e-Haq-shewa
Azab-e-jaan pay-e-izhar-e-jaan uthate hain

Makan mein gunjta hai nara-e-Husain Husain
Ab is sada ko sar-e-la-makan uthate hain

Hain Karbala ki zamin hi se asman roshan
Isi ki khak se ye kahkashan uthate hain

Imam-o-muqtadi aise ke sajda-rezi mein
Zamin pe rakh ke jabin asman uthate hain

Kare hain kos asiron pe Shah ke, Tabish
Kabhi qadam to kabhi beriyan uthate hain
(Bashukriya shair Abbas Jafari / Japani, Champion Focus)

Haq ki manzil Karbala hai Haq ka jada Karbala
Haji Haq Rizvi

Haq ki manzil Karbala hai Haq ka jada Karbala
Yun to hai ek khitta-e-sahra nihada Karbala

Ek tasalsul hai Haq-o-batil ka Shabbir-o-Yazid
Hai Hunain-o-Badr-o-Khaibar ka iada Karbala

Sochiye to ek mashhad dekhiye to ek dasht
Kitni rangin Karbala hai kitni sada Karbala

Sare jazbe maslak-e-bas ek hi wahdat mein hain
Apni manzil, rasta, maqsad, irada Karbala

Aaj bhi insaniyat ki haq-numai ke liye
Gam gam Ibn-e-Ali hain, jada jada Karbala

Marke is ke Haq-o-batil ke sare marke
Kitni la-mahdud hai kitni kushada Karbala

Zindagi mein kash Tabish wo bhi din aaye ke hum
Sar ke bal jayen Madina pa-piyada Karbala
(Bashukriya shair o martabakhwan Syed Javed Hasan)
