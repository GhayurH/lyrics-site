Salam

Wahi to fath-o-zafar ke nishan uthate hain
Haji Haq Rizvi

Wohi to fath o Zafar ke nishan uthate hain
Khums-o-jihad-e Haq mein jo azar jaan uthate hain
Nishan-e rah jo roshan hain phap nahin sakte
Ghubar Agarche bahut karwan uthate hain

Yahi shanakht hai ke ke mard-e Haq Wahi
Azab-e jaan paye izhar-e jaan uthate hain
Makan mein gunjta hai narah Husain Husain
Ab is-e sada ko sar-e lamkan uthate hain

hain Karbala ki zamin hi se asman roshan
Isi-e ki khak se ye khashakan uthate hain
Imam o maqtami aise ke sajda razizi mein
Zamin pe rakh ke jabin asman uthate hain

Ke hain ke asiron pe Shah ke, tabash
Kabhi qadam to kabhi Beriyan uthate hain
(Bashukriya shair Abbas Jafari / Japani, Champion Focus)

Alhaj Syed Syed Amir Zaidi (Lahore)

Be-shak azim martaba Shah-e-Anam hai
Tarif kya karun ke wo ali maqam hai
Kaise nasib hogi ghulami wahan Saeed
Abbas ka azim jahan par ghulam hai
(Makhuz az: Roshan Shabein ke guldasta Surah Amj tak)

Haq ki manzil Karbala hai Haq ka jada Karbala
Haji Haq Rizvi

Haq ki manzil Karbala hai Haq ka jada Karbala
Yun to hai ek khatt sahra nahanda Karbala
Ek tasalsul hai Haq o batil ka Shabbir o Yazid
hai Hunain o Badr o Khaibar ka iada Karbala

Sauche to Ek Mashhad dekhiye to Ek dasht
kitni rangin Karbala hai kitni sada Karbala
Sare jazbe-e maslak-e bas Ek hi wahdat mein hain
apni manzil, rasta, maqsad, irada Karbala

aaj bhi insaniyat ki Haq numai ke liye
Gam gam Ibn-e Ali hain, jada jada Karbala
Marke us-e Haq o batil ke sare marke
Kitni lamdho hai kitni kashada Karbala

Zindagi mein Kash tabash wo bhi din aaye ke hum
sar ke bal jayen Madina pa padishad Karbala
(Bashukriya Shair o martaba khwan Syed Javed Hasan)
