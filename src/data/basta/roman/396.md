Nahin chahiye mujh ko duniya ki daulat
Hai kafi tumhari raza mere Maula
Na main tum ko bhulun, na tum mujh ko bhulo
Yahi tum se hai iltija mere Maula
Jinhein sab samajhte hain Shah-o-tawangar
Hain sab tere dar ke gada mere Maula
Na hajat-rawai agar aap ne ki
Hai kaun apna hajat-rawa mere Maula
Kahin log us ko Khuda, wo na mane
Koi aisa banda dikha mere Maula
"Alak al-hamd" tu ne rah-e-Haq dikhai
Sada rah-e-Haq par chala mere Maula
Jo aayen kahin "Ahmad" [lafz ghair wazeh]
Aina sidha rasta dikha mere Maula
Ye mana ke Ghaffar-o-Sattar hai tu
Gunahon se phir bhi bacha mere Maula

Jo aulad-e-Zahra pe rote hain aksar
Na duniya ke gham mein rula mere Maula
Munafiq na fasiq, na mushrik na kafir
Bana kar Husaini utha mere Maula
Bacha hum ko duniya ke ranj-o-bala se
Muhabbat-e-Shah-e-Karbala mere Maula
Jo ashrar hain un ko barbad kar de
Bas ab ho chuki iltija mere Maula
Tujhe wasta teri rajaiyat ka
Hamare hon qarze ada mere Maula
Jahan mein jo fitne bapa kar rahe hain
Na de un ko muhlat zara mere Maula
Apne Syed-us-Sajidin aur [lafz ghair wazeh]
De bimariyon se shifa mere Maula
