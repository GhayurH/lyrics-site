[Iltimas Surah Fatiha]

Pahunchi pisar ki lash pe jab Shah-e-bahr-o-bar

1
Pahunche pisar ki lash pe jab Shah-e Bahr o bar
Akbar pukare achchha, Khuda Hafiz Ay pidar
Hichki jo aai tham liya hath se jigar
Angari le ke rakh diya Shah ke qadam pe sar

Abad ghar lita Shah-e wala ke samne
Bete ka dam nikal gaya Baba ke samne

2
Zainab ne di sada abraba hai kis taraf
Ay asman wo arsh ka tara hai kis taraf
Ay abar-e Sham, chand hamara hai kis taraf
Ay arzaz-e Karbala wo sitara hai kis taraf

Hai hai sinan se jaan gayi mehman ki
Mayyat hai kis taraf mere Akbar jawan ki

3
Ay khush jamal gesuon wale, khan hai to
Hai meri gharibi ke pale, kahan hai to
wari khan lage tujhe bhale, khan hai to
kyun kar phuphi ko jigar sambhale, khan hai to

Utharon baras hai ke maut aa gayi tujhe
Ay nur-e-ain kis ki nazar kha gayi tujhe
