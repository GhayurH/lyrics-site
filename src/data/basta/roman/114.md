Salam

Husain yun hue ay mujrai watan se juda
Mir Anees

Husain yun hue ay mujrai watan se juda
Ke jaise bulbul-e-nashad ho chaman se juda
Sipah-e-Sham se tuka jo Hurr to bole malak
Wo aftab-e-darakhshan hua gahan se juda

Watan mein phir ke safar se na jite ji aaye
Ajab ghari thi ke Akbar hue bahen se juda
Nikala gardan-e-Asghar se tir jab Shah ne
Gale se bahne laga khun juda dahan se juda

Sakina mar gayi qaid-e-sitam mein ghut ghut kar
Magar na chand si gardan hui rasan se juda

Hifz-e-namus-e-ilahi, kar-e-shamshir-e-Husain
Professor Nasrin Naqvi

Hifz-e-namus-e-ilahi, kar-e-shamshir-e-Husain
Satwat-e-Din-e-Muhammad, husn-e-tadbir-e-Husain
Is se barh kar aur kya hogi fazilat ki dalil
Khud Rasul-e-Hashimi karte hain tauqir-e-Husain

Ghurur-e-sarkash taqaton ka sar na uthne payega
Hashr tak hai pay-e-batil aur zanjir-e-Husain
Sarwar-e-Kaunain ke itne chete kyun hue
Bas yahi dushman ki nazron mein hai taqsir-e-Husain

Kis zaban se ho bayan ye izz-o-shan-e-Ahl-e-Bait
Maulana Hasan Raza Khan Bahari

Kis zaban se ho bayan ye izz-o-shan-e-Ahl-e-Bait
Madh-go-e-Mustafa hai madh-khwan-e-Ahl-e-Bait
Mustafa izzat barhane ke liye tazim dein
Hai buland iqbal tera dudman-e-Ahl-e-Bait

In ke ghar mein be-ijazat Jibrail aate nahin
Qadr wale jante hain qadr-o-shan-e-Ahl-e-Bait
Ay shabab-e-fasl-e-gul ye chal gayi kaisi hawa
Kat raha hai lahlahata bustan-e-Ahl-e-Bait

Kis shaqi ki hai hukumat haye kya andher hai
Lut raha hai din-dahare karwan-e-Ahl-e-Bait
Fatima ke ladle ka aakhri didar hai
Hashr ka hangama barpa hai miyan-e-Ahl-e-Bait

Bagh-e-Jannat chhor kar aaye hain Mahbub-e-Khuda
Ay zahe qismat tumhari kushtagan-e-Ahl-e-Bait
Ghar lutana jaan dena koi tujh se sikh jaye
Jaan-e-ma, tum par fida ay khandan-e-Ahl-e-Bait
(Bashukriya Maulana Qari Hasan Mahmud)
