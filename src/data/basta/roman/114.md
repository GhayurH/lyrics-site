Salam

Husain yun hue ay mujarmi watan se juda
Mir Anees

Husain yun hue Ay mujarmi-e watan se juda
Ke jaise Bulbul-e nashad ho chaman se juda
Sipah-e Sham se tu jo Hurr to bole mulk
Wo aftab aftab-e-darakhshan hua ghan se juda

Watan mein phir ke safar se na Jite ji aaye
Ajab ghari thi ke Akbar hue bahen se juda
Nikala gardan-e Asghar se tir jab Shah ne
Gale se bahne laga khun juda dahan se juda

Sakina mar gayi qaid-e-sitam mein ghut ghut kar
Magar na chand si gardan hui rasan se juda

Hifz-e-namus-e-ilahi, kar-e-shamshir-e-Husain
Professor Nasrin Naqvi

Hafzif-e namus-e Ilahi thas kar-e shamshir-e Husain
Satwat-e-Maula-e din-e Muhammad thas Hasan-e tadbir-e Husain
Is-e se barh kar aur kya hogi fazilat ki dilli
Khud Rasul-e nasl-e-Hashimi karte hain tauqir-e Husain

Tar-e-ghurur-e sarkash taqaton ka sar na uthe paye ga
Hashr tak hai paye batil aur zanjir-e Husain
Sarwar-e Kaunain ke itne chete kyun hue
Bas yahi dushman ki nazron mein hai taqsir-e Husain

Kis zaban se ho bayan ye aushana-e ahl-e-matam-e bait
Maulana Hasan Raza Khan bahari

Kis zaban se ho bayan ye azo shan-e Ahl-e-Bait
Madh guye Mustafa hai madh Sozkhwan-e-Aal-e Ahl-e-Bait
Mard-o-zan ke apne pishte kyun hue
Hai buland Iqbal tera dushman-e Ahl-e-Bait

Un-e ke ghar mein be-kasi ijazat Jibrail aate nahin
Qadar wale jante hain qadar o shan-e Ahl-e-Bait
Ay shabab-e fasla-e gul ye chal gayi Kaisi hua
Kat raha hai Ahl-e-Bait bustan-e Ahl-e-Bait

Kis shaqi ki hai hukumat haye kya andher hai
Lut raha hai din Qadr karwan-e Ahl-e-Bait
Ay shabab-e-fasl-e-gul pe chal gayi kaisi hawa
Hashr ka hanghama barpa hai main-e Ahl-e-Bait

Kis ne hai hukumat haye kya andher hai
Ay hai qismat tumhari kisthatan-e Ahl-e-Bait
Ghar lutana jaan dena koi tujh se sikhi jaye
Jaan-e miyan thas tum par fida Ay khandan-e Ahl-e-Bait
Bagh-e-jannat chhor kar aaye hain Mahbub-e-Khuda
