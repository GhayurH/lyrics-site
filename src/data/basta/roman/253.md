Bhaiya hum ek rassi mein barah asir hain

1
Pahunchi jo qaid-khane mein Hind neku-sair
Dekha ke ek mariz para hai qarib-e-dar
Rota hai wan se koi koi pitta hai sar
Hathon se dil pakar ke pukari wo nauhagar

Dam-ba-dam ban gayi magar afat ghati nahin
Tab mein bhi is gharib ki beri kati nahin

2
Ansu baha ke shana hilaya jo chand bar
Ek bar ghash se chaunk ke bola wo nahif-o-zar
Be-kas ko kyun jagati hain Amman pisar nisar
Kya jaan-ba-lab hua koi masum dil-figar

Paida hue hain ashk bahane ke waste
Uthte hain hum janaza uthane ke waste

3
Wo boli sab ki khair hai ay zar-o-natawan
Main hun kaniz aap ki madar nahin yahan
Mushtaq-e-did aai hun zindan ke darmiyan
Itna mujhe bataiye ay Yusuf-e-Zaman

Ism-e-sharif kya hai, kaha sogwar hai
Puchha pidar ka naam, kaha be-diyar hai

4
Puchha kahan lute ho, kaha Haq ki rah mein
Puchha ye kab, kaha Muharram ke mah mein
Puchha ye kyun, kaha muhabbat ki chah mein
Puchha pidar kahan hain, kaha qatl-gah mein

Puchha jo ghar to ro ke kaha qaid-khana hai
Puchha ghiza mein kya hai, kaha taziyana hai

5
Ye zikr tha ke aa gaya khuni talkh-kam
Laya sinan-e-zulm pe ek farq-e-lala-fam
Zindan-e-Sham nur se roshan hua tamam
Is sar ne di nida: mere bimar, assalam

Ghabra ke Ahl-e-Bait to tazim ko uthe
Sajjad kanpte hue taslim ko uthe

6
Is sar se ro ke kehne lagi Hind bawafa
Ay sar, tu bolta hai to ye bhi mujhe bata
Kis ba-Khuda ki Aal hai, kya naam hai tera
Kis bap ka tu lal hai, kis man ka lal

Kya Dukhtar-e-Rasul ka lakht-e-jigar hai tu
Qurban jaun, kya mere Aqa ka sar hai tu
