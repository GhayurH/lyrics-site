Ay rozadaro aao buka ke ye roz hain

1
Ay rozadaro aah-o-buka ke ye roz hain
Sadat par nazul-e bula ke ye roz hain
Sar-e taj-e aswaya ki aza ke ye roz hain
Tum se wada-e-Sher-e-Khuda ke ye roz hain

Zakhmi hua Imamam-e tumhara namaz mein
Zalim ne rozadar ko mara namaz mein

2
Mah-e mubarak-e ramzan aur Khuda ka ghar
Tarpa wo nur-e ka wo shab-e qadar ki sahar
Pesh-e Khuda wo sajde mein sher-e Khuda ka sar
Aur haye tegh-e zulm wo farq-e par

Fawware khun ke zakhm-e jabin se baha kiye
Sajde mein aap Rabb-e-Ala alali kaha kiye

3
Daure ye keh ke janib-e Masjid wo Hind zat
Dubi hui lahu mein mili kashti-e Ruh-e-nijat
Mathe pe khun bap ka mil kar kami ye baat
Hai Ay uthaya qibla-ru o Kaaba pe kis ne hath

Rulaya Nana jaan ko Dar-us-Salam mein
Ban bap ka kya hamein mah-e samiya mein
