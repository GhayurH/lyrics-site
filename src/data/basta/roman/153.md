[Watan se chhut ke koi khasta-jan shahid na ho — jari]

4
Makan se nikli jo Taua to Boli ghabra kar
Ke aap kaun hain baithe hain kis liye dar par
Dubi zaban se kaha ek gharib hun, khwahar
Pila de aap mujhe bahr-e-Saqi-e-Kausar

Ye sun ke nazr-e Imam-e anmam le aai
Mahal mein ja ke wo pani ka jaam le aai

5
Wo aab pi ke bhi uthe na jab to is ne kaha
Ke pani pi chuke ab ghar ko jao bahr-e-Khuda
Amir karne ko Muslim ke phirte hain aada
ail, fikr mein hun hue ke reh gaye kis ja

Ghazab hai phir-e jo gharibon ke dil ko kul na pare
Talash mein koi Bibi Bibi kahin nikal na pare

6
Ye sun ke aah bhari aur kaha ba-dida-e-tar tar
Na baithte tere dar par kahin jo hota ghar
khan ail, jinhein fikr ho lungi Ay khwahar
Yahan bahen hai na bhai na zauja o dukhtar

Na jane wo bhi Na gham mein kis tarah se hain
Husain hum se juda, hum juda Husain se hain

7
Ye naam sun ke pukari wo Ashiq-e Zahra
Husain aap ke kya hain kaha mere Maula
Wo Boli naam o nasbib kuchh Bataiye Lillah
kaha ke mard-e gharib altin mein hi kya

watan wohi hai jahan Ahl-e bait rehte hain
Mujhe Husain ka adna ghulam kehte hain

8
Ye sun ke is ne tarap kar kaha bashadat-e gham
Ye khaksar bhi hai ek kaniz-e-Shah-e-Umam
Chhupao mujh se na Lillah, hal-e dard o alam
Do naam, tumhein jaan-e Fatima ki qasam

Kaha Haram se jo chhuta wo be-nasib hun main
Kaili-e Sibt-e Nabi, Muslim-e gharib hun mein

Bashukriya sozkhwan Ahliyat Sultana Rizvi

Hazrat Shadan Dehlavi

Baithi hui diwar uthani hogi
Gir pari hui dastar uthani hogi
Haq bhik nahin jo apni se mil jaye
Is ke liye talwar uthani hogi

Bashukriya sozkhwan Chaudhry Sajid Husain Zaidi o Naib
