Soz

Kya pesh-e Khuda Sahib-e tauqir hai Zahra
Am-e alshinin o Madar-e Shabbir hai Zahra
Khatun-e jinan Malik-e Tathir hai Zahra
Sar ta ba qadam nur-e ki taswir hai Zahra

Shauhar ko jo puchho to shhanhasha-e Yathrib hai
Beti hai Nabi ki ye nasab hai wo nasbib hai
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Aziz Haider Jalalvi o Nadim Jalalvi)

Ay roza dar o aah o buka ke ye roz hain
Sadat par nazul-e bula ke ye roz hain
Sar-e-taj-e-ausiya-e aswaya ki aza ke ye roz hain
Tum se wida-e-Sher-e-Khuda ke ye roz hain

Zakhmi hua Imam tumhara namaz mein
Zalim ne roza dar ko mara namaz mein

Sharik-e sabr-e Shah-e marshaqin hain Zainab
ke ain, Fatima ki nur-e ain hain Zainab
Dil-e Muhammad o Haider ka chain hain Zainab
Khuda ki rah mein bilkul Husain hain Zainab

Husain mard-e reh-e inqilab-e-Iran hain goya
Ye auraton mein amal ki kitab hain goya
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed Khadim Abbas Jalalvi)

Jurat mein Ali sabr mein Shabbir hain Zainab
Shamshir-e Tafsir-e-fasahat dam-e taqrir hain Zainab
Bint-e-Zahra-e Shah-e washlish ki Tanwir hain Zainab
Ghar Lutne pe bhi Sahib-e tauqir hain Zainab

Shabbir ke maqsad ki nigahban yahi hain
Ufsan Islam ka Unwan yahi hain

Jab watan se koch ki Muslim ke tayyari hui
khana-e Aal-e Aal-e-Aba mein girya o zari hui
Donon beton ki jo rukhsat man se yakbari hui
Harf-e-zan shauhar se apne yun wo dukhiyari hui
ek to dagh-e Faraqaq-e apna diye jate ho tum
Dusre mere kalejon ko liye jate ho tum

Kisi ne Kufe ke raste mein di ye Shah ko khabar
Ke qatl ho gaye Muslim aur un ke donon pisar
Na jayen aap bhi is samt ko to hai behtar
Tab is se kehne lage ro ke Shah-e jin o bashar

Ajal gham-rasida mamnam-e tum rum ba karb o bula
Guzasht nabat-e Muslim rasid nabat-e mere
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed Ijaz Husain Shahzad)
