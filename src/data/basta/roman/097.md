Soz

Kya pesh-e-Khuda sahib-e-tauqir hai Zahra
Umm-ul-Hasanain o madar-e-Shabbir hai Zahra
Khatun-e-jinan malik-e-Tathir hai Zahra
Sar ta ba qadam nur ki taswir hai Zahra

Shohar ko jo puchho to shahanshah-e-Arab hai
Beti hai Nabi ki ye hasab hai wo nasab hai
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Aziz Haider Jalalvi o Nadim Jalalvi)

---***---

Ay rozadar o aah o buka ke ye roz hain
Sadat par nuzul-e-bala ke ye roz hain
Sartaj-e-Ausiya ki aza ke ye roz hain
Tum se wida-e-Sher-e-Khuda ke ye roz hain

Zakhmi hua Imam tumhara namaz mein
Zalim ne rozadar ko mara namaz mein

---***---

Sharik-e-sabr-e-Shah-e-Mashriqain hain Zainab
Ke ain, Fatima ki nur-e-ain hain Zainab
Dil-e-Muhammad-o-Haider ka chain hain Zainab
Khuda ki rah mein bilkul Husain hain Zainab

Husain mard-e-rah-e-inqilab hain goya
Ye auraton mein amal ki kitab hain goya
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed Khadim Abbas Jalalvi)

---***---

Jurat mein Ali sabr mein Shabbir hain Zainab
Shamshir-e-fasahat dam-e-taqrir hain Zainab
Bint-e-Shah-e-Wash-Shams ki tanwir hain Zainab
Ghar lutne pe bhi sahib-e-tauqir hain Zainab

Shabbir ke maqsad ki nigahban yahi hain
Afsana-e-Islam ka unwan yahi hain

---***---

Jab watan se koch ki Muslim ke tayyari hui
Khana-e-Aal-e-Aba mein girya-o-zari hui
Donon beton ki jo rukhsat man se yakbari hui
Harf-e-zan shohar se apne yun wo dukhiyari hui
Ek to dagh-e-firaq apna diye jate ho tum
Dusre mere kalejon ko liye jate ho tum

---***---

Kisi ne Kufe ke raste mein di ye Shah ko khabar
Ke qatl ho gaye Muslim aur un ke donon pisar
Na jayen aap bhi us samt ko to hai behtar
Tab us se kehne lage ro ke Shah-e-jinn-o-bashar

Ajal rasida manam mi rawam ba Karb-o-bala
Guzasht naubat-e-Muslim rasid naubat-e-ma
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed Ijaz Husain Shahzad)
