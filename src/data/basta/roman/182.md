[Unwan juzwi taur par ghair wazeh: qarib lash-e Qasim jo pahunche...]

1
Qarib-e lash Qasim jo pahunche Sarwar-e din
To aise hal mein aaya nazar wo jism-e Husain
Ke thori der to hi saka na dil ko yaqin
Kahin tha kalchal hua jism, dast-e-sitam o pa the kahin

Ek aah bhar ke Shah-e-din zamin pe baith gaye
Jahan khare the larz kar wohin pe Baith gaye

2
Haram mein pahunche to dekha ke hashr hai barpa
Balak rahi hai Sakina, khamosh hai Kubra
[do misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]

mein ye to janati thi sar katiyan hue Qasim
ye kya khabar thi ke yun wapas aayen hue Qasim

3
Labun pe sabr ki bandash thi dil mein hashr bapa
Uthaye barh ke zamin se kate hue aza
Na jane kaise tan-e-muntashir ko jama kiya
Aal-e-Aba pe rakh ke uthaya janaziz Qasim ka

Larazte hathon pe jaise pahar uthaye hue
Chale Husain suye khaima sar jhukaye hue

4
Haram mein Pahunchun ga jab le ke lash Qasim ki
Pisar ko dekh ke yun pamal kya hoga
hai ghair hal mera, man ka hal kya hoga
Haram mein pahunche to dekha ke hashr hai barpa

Balak rahi hai Sakina khamosh hai Kubra
Tarap ke man ne suye lash pisar dekha

5
Jigar ko tham ke Bolin ye kya hua Bhaiya
Mein ye janati thi sar katiyan hue Qasim
Ye kya khabar thi ke yun wapas aayen hue Qasim
Kaha Imam ne Bhabhi na kijiye is-e ka malal

Hua shahidon mein ke aap ka ye lal
Wohi hamari bhi ho lungi jo un ki halat hai

6
Khuda ka shukr karo lash Qasim aa to gayi
hamari lash to laye ga bhi na ran se koi
Phir is pe ho ga ye tarafa sitam bhi Ay Bhabhi
Ke nacho le lungi labas-e badan bhi Fauj-e shaqi

[ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Jhulasti hogi hawa, dhoop bhi kari hogi
