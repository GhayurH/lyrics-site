Karbala ki sher-dil khatun Zainab assalam
Maulana Murtaza Azamgarhi

Karbala ki sher-dil khatun Zainab assalam
Assalam ay Sani-e-Zahra ay khush-o-nek naam
Dar-ba-dar ki thokaren aur wo masaib al-aman
Aur lekin tum Karbala se Kufa aur Kufa se Sham
Sham ke bazar mein pahuncha jo lut kar qafila
Kya qiyamat thi tamashe dekhte the khas-o-aam
Chhin gayi sar se rida bazu bandhe aur ankh nam
Sabr-o-zabt-e-Zainab dekh kar hai kya maqam
Ay sharik-e-kar-e-Haq ay hami-e-din-e-Nabi
Ay Muhammad ki nawasi tujh pe hon lakhon salam
Kis tarah tere masaib ka bayan Izzati se ho
Jab jigar phatta ho in azkar se ay nek naam
(Bashukriya salamkhwan Syed Baqir Husain Azmi)

Har samt jahan mein hai shohra-e-Ali Akbar ka
Shair o sozkhwan Kashif Zaidi

Har samt jahan mein hai shohra-e-Ali Akbar ka
Rutba hai khalaeq mein aala Ali Akbar ka
Shabbir jhulate the Abbas ke jhule ko
Abbas jhulate hain jhula Ali Akbar ka
Hamshakl-e-Payambar ke neza na laga zalim
Hai phul se bhi khush-tar sina Ali Akbar ka
Sarwar ne sinan khinchi jab sina-e-Akbar se
To sath kaleja bhi aaya Ali Akbar ka
Faryad thi Sarwar ki imdad karo Baba
Uthta nahin bete se lasha Ali Akbar ka
Puchha gaya jab Shah se kyun kham hai kamar Maula
Farmaya uthaya tha lasha Ali Akbar ka
Kaunain ki shahzadi deti hai dua Kashif
Zainab ko jo deta hai pursa Ali Akbar ka
(Iltimas Surat-e-Fatiha baraye sozkhwan Syed Shehzad Husain)

Ahl-e-Kufa ne dagha Muslim se be-taqsir ki

Ahl-e-Kufa ne dagha Muslim se be-taqsir ki
Khud likha: baiat karenge Hazrat Shabbir ki
Kis qadar zulm tha dushman-e-Payambar ke khilaf
Ek [lafz ghair wazeh] par charhai lashkar-e-be-pir ki
Zakhm khate jate the aur karte jate the dua
Khair ho ay Malik-e-kaun-o-makan Shabbir ki
Baad-e-qatl-e-Hazrat Muslim har ek be-din ne
Chhini thi phir tak amez wo tadbir ki
Dar pe latkaya sar-e-Muslim tamashe ke liye
Lash khinchi har gali kuche mein be-taqsir ki
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Arif Husain o Athar Jafari)
