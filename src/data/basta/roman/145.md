Rone bhi na payegi meri lash pe Zainab

1
Jab chale Yasrib se Sibt-e-Mustafa su-e-Iraq
Thi dar-o-diwar se paida sada-e-al-firaq
Jadd ke rauze pe gaye rukhsat ko ba-sad ishtiyaq
Arz ki Nana bulate hain mujhe Ahl-e-Nifaq

Ho gaya daryaft ye khat ke qarine se mujhe
Karte hain zalim juda is dam Madine se mujhe

2
Dekhna ye mausam-e-garma ye bachche nazanin
Dhup ki shiddat ye registan hai aur jalti zamin
Ab watan mein apne zinda wapas aane ke nahin
Aap ka rauza kahin hoga mera marqad kahin

Tafriqa ummat ne dala sar bhara hum chale
Fatima Sughra rahi marqad ke saye ke tale

3
Apni apni ja chhupe baithe hain sare wahsh-o-tair
Main chala hun is tapish mein dekhiye ummat ka bair
Ye safar aur sath kunba, log dushman, mulk ghair
Mujh pe jo guzre so guzre bachchon ki khair

Dekhiye Haq mein mere kya marzi-e-Ghaffar hai
Fatima Sughra juda ghar mein yahan bimar hai

4
Hai safar garmi ka aur bachche mere hamrah hain
Gul se nazuk-tar kahin ye rashk-e-mehr-o-mah hain
Manzilon darya nahin aur na koson chah hain
Dar-pay-e-tazlil-o-iza dushman-e-badkhwah hain

Hum hain aur ghurbat hai aur Khaliq ki zat-e-pak hai
Wadi-e-wahshat hai aur sahra-e-afatnak hai

5
Marqad-e-purnur se itne mein aai ye sada
Kya karun Shabbir dil sine mein tukre ho gaya
Gosha-e-marqad mein bismil sa tarapta hun para
Kya karun kuchh bas nahin hai jo raza-e-Kibriya

Is safar mein tujh ko chhorunga na ay dilkhwah main
Pitta rota chalunga Karbala hamrah main

6
Ay mere lakht-e-jigar aisi jagah hai Karbala
Ranj uthate aaye hain jis ja pe khasan-e-Khuda
Hazrat-e-Adam ne thokar khai khun pa se baha
Aa gaya tha wan talatum mein safina Nuh ka

Maurid-e-afat wo ja ay mere dilkhwah hai
Aur khususan wo jagah teri to wada-gah hai
