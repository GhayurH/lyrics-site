Rone bhi na paye lungi meri lash pe Zainab

1
Jab chale Yathrib se Sibt-e Mustafa suye Iraq
Thi dar o diwar se paida sadaye Al-firaq
Jad ke rauze pe gaye rukhsat ko basda Ishtiaq
Arz ki Nana bulate hain mujhe ahl-e-matam-e naqfaq

Ho gaya daryaft ye khat ke qarine se mujhe
Karte hain zalim juda is-e dam Madine se mujhe

2
Dekhna ye mausam-e garma ye bachche naznanin
Dhoop ki shiddat ye registan hai aur jalti zamin
Ab watan mein apne zinda wapas aane ke nahin
Aap ka rauza kahin ho ga mera Marqad kahin

Tafarraqa ummat ne dala sar bhara hum chale
Fatima Sughra rahi Marqad ke saye ke tale

3
Apni apni ja Chhupe baithe hain sare wahshish o tair
Mein chala hun is-e tashat mein dekhiye ummat ka ber
Ye safar aur sath kabnab-a-bab-tab-mab-sab-mat log dushman mulk ghair
Mujh pe jo guzre so guzre bachchon ki khair

Dekhiye Haq mein mere kya marzi-e-Ghaffar hai
Fatima Sughra juda ghar mein yahan bimar hai

4
Hai safar garmi ka aur bachche mere hamrah hain
Gul se nazuk tar kahin ye rashk-e mehr o mah-e hain
Manzilon darya nahin aur na kunwen chah hain
Dar paye talli o iza dushman-e badkhwah hain

Hum hain aur ghurbat hai aur Khaliq ki zat-e Pak hai
Wadi-e wahshat hai aur sahraye afat naka hai

5
Marqad-e par nur-e se itne mein aai ye sada
Kya karun Shabbir dil sine mein tukre ho gaya
Gushush Marqad mein basmal sa tarapta hun para
Kya karun kuchh bas nahin hai jo raza-e-Kibriya

Is-e safar mein tujh ko chhorun ga na Ay dil khahwa mein
Pitta rota chalungi ga Karbala hamrah mein

6
Ay mere lakht-e jigar aisi jagah hai Karbala
Ranj uthate aaye hain jis ja pe khasan-e-Khuda-e Khuda
Hazrat-e Adam ne thokar Khai khun pa se baha
Aa gaya tha Wan talatum mein safina wan ka

Mardar-e afat wo ja Ay mere dil khahwa hai
Aur khsusa wo jagah teri to wada gah hai
