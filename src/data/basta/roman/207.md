Jab Mariya ke dasht mein warid hua Zafar

1
Jab Mariya ke dasht mein warid hua Zafar
Maidan mein akele the khare Sibt-e-Payambar
Sar ta ba qadam khun mein aluda the Sarwar
Aur reg-e-biyaban thi bhari zakhmon ke andar

Is pyas mein munh ansuon se dhote the Maula
Ek chhoti si turbat pe khare rote the Maula

2
The lash-e-pisar par abhi girya-kunan Shah-e-Abrar
Jo Zafar jinn samne se aa gaya yakbar
Shah ne kaha ay shakhs khabardar, khabardar
Be-sar pare yan mere sab yawar-o-ansar

Ye phul hain sab bagh-e-Shah-e-jinn-o-bashar ke
Lashe nahin tukre hain ye Zahra ke jigar ke

3
Ye un ke hain lashe jo the mazlum ke yawar
Ye un ke hain lashe jo pyase hue be-sar
Tukre hue tighon se yahan Qasim-e-muztar
Ay shakhs yahan dafn hue hain Ali Asghar

Zainab ke yahan god ke pale ka lasha hai
Is ja pe mere gesuon wale ka lasha hai

4
Shah ne kaha tu kaun hai, kya hai tera naam
Yan kis liye aaya hai, matlab hai tera kya
Har chand ke be-kas hun, main zakhmi hun sarapa
Lekin mujhe majbur tu hargiz na samajhna

Sab mar gaye is par nahin nachar abhi hun
Be-kas hun magar kul ka madadgar abhi hun

5
Ki arz ye Zafar ne ke ay Syed-e-wala
Tera to madadgar hai wo Khaliq-e-Yakta
Tu kul ka madadgar hai parwa se tujhe kya
Par kijiye manzur meri arz ay Aqa

Lute na koi Aal-e-Rasul-e-Arabi ko
Khaime ki nigahbani ka do izn bhi mujh ko

6
Ye bat jo Shabbir ko Zafar ne sunai
Rone lage wo ba-dida-e-giryan-o-bukai
Yaad aa gayi namus ki us waqt tabahi
Kehne laga Zafar se wo Khaliq ka fidai

Muhtaj-e-Haram honge ek ek rida ke
Sadat ki izzat hai bas ab aage Khuda ke
