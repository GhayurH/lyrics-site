Kufe mein dakhila hai Safir-e-Imam ka

1
Kufe mein dakhal hai safarif-e Imam ka
Tazim se khamida hai sar khas o amim ka
Ghar ghar mein ghulghula hai durud o salam ka
Jo shakhs hai, mujassama hai ehtiram ka

Baiat ka qasd hai jo Imam-e-Arab ke sath
Muslim ke hath chumte hain sab adab ke sath

2
Tanha ki jang dushmanon ke dil hila gayi
Pandrah so surmaon, Jawanon ko kha gayi
Ibn-e Ibn-e-Ziyad ko jo madad ki sada gayi
Chehre pe rusiyah ke zardi si chha gayi

Bola chalao tegh na Hashim ke sher par
Makr-o-riya o dagha ke war karo is-e dilir par

3
Amada makr-o-riya par hue Makkar o nabakar
Kothon se phenkne lage patthar sitam-shiar
Karte the dur dur se tegh o tabar ke war
Angare marte the kahin chhup kar Ahl-e-Nar
