Kufe ko chala Qasid-e-Sughra jo watan se

1
Kufe ko chala Qasid-e-Sughra jo watan se
Tai rah-e-masafat bare ranj-o-mihan se
Nagah guzar jab hua us zulm ke ban se
Jab sun mein judai hui Shah ke sar-o-tan se

Wan dekha ke kuchh lashe pare khun mein tar hain
Zakhmi hain badan aur tanon par nahin sar hain

2
Dil mein ye laga sone wo Qasid-e-ghamkhwar
Kufe hi ki janib to gaye the Shah-e-Abrar
Aqa ki mere khair ho achchhe nahin asar
Haider ke gharane ke nishan sab hain namudar

Kuchh un ko qarabat thi Husain aur Hasan se
Sadat ki khushbu chali aati hai badan se

3
Rota hua wo lasha-e-Sarwar pe jo pahuncha
Faryad thi ke mere Aqa, mere Aqa
Kis tarah lainon ne tumhein gher ke mara
Is tarah ke hain zakhm ke dekha nahin jata

Kaise kalma-go the Rasul-e-Arabi ke
Kis zulm se mara hai nawase ko Nabi ke
