5

6

Sakina Sham ke zindan mein thi alam se nidhal

1
Sakina Sham ke zindan mein thi alam se nidhal
Kabhi chacha ka tasawwur kabhi pidar ka khayal
Kabhi Rubab se baten kabhi phuphi se maqaal
Kabhi khamosh kabhi Abid-e hazin se sawal

Chacha kab aayenge darya se laut kar bhaiya
Yahan se nehr hai kis qadar safar Bhaiya

2
Mein Karbala mein rahi muntazir nahin aaye
Lain le gaye mere gauhar nahin aaye
Jala diye gaye hum sab ke ghar nahin aaye
Sadaen deti rahi hum sab magar nahin aaye

Hawaye sard-e tharati mein jab mili ho lungi
Thake hue the bahut nind aa gayi ho lungi

3
Tarapti rehti thi masum yun hi Sham-o-sahar
Tasalliyan use dete the Abid-e muztar
Rubab roti thi bholi bholi baton par
Nigah-e-yas se takti thin Zainab be-par

Payam-e marg, Bani dil ki be kali aakhir
Chacha ke pas bhatiji chali gayi aakhir
