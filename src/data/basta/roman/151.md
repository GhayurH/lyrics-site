Muztar the shab-e-hashtum Zi-l-Hijja ko Shabbir

1
Muztar the shab-e-hashtum Zil-Hijja ko Shabbir
Tha qasd-e-musammam ke suye Kufa hun rahgir
Karte the kabhi yas se ro ro ke ye taqrir
Ab Yan se kahan dekhiye le jati hai taqdir

Phir kar jo watan jayen to jana na milega
Ab hum ko bajuz qabr thikana na milega

2
The Sibt-e Nabi koch ki tadbir mein is raat
Ki aa ke jo Ibn-e Ibn-e-Hanafiya ne mulaqat
Bhai se baghal-gir hue Shah-e-khush-auqat
Ki arz Muhammad ne ke Ay Qibla-e-Hajat hajata

Kufe ki taraf jane mein andashi jaan hai
Make mein makin hun ke buzurgon ka makan hai

3
Hazrat ne kaha hota hun nachar rawana
Bhata hai kise apna watan chhor ke jana
Mein bekas o Mazlum hun dushman hai zamana
Ab to hai Madine mein na make mein thikana

Hasir hai koi, dar paye azar hai koi
Hamid hai na koi, na madadgar hai koi

4
Munh pit liya sun ke Muhammad ne ye taqrir
Ro ro ke kaha gar hai yahi khwahish-e-taqdir
Namus ko chhoro yahin Ay Hazrat-e Shabbir
Hurmat se Haram rahen wo Sahib-e Tathir

Pardes mein kya jane kya jaur o jafa ho
Dar hai kahin Zainab na giraftar-e-bala ho

5
Zainab ki asiri ka sukhan sunte hi Us bar
Tharra gaya dil, rone lage Syed-e abrarar
Sunti thi ye taqrir jo sab Zainab-e nachar
Mahmal se Muhammad ko pukari ba dil zar

Ki tum ne sifarish meri kya aah baradar
Bhai se chhurate ho mujh ko ho baradar

6
Ye keh ke jo roi Asadullah ki pyari
Ibn-e Ibn-e-Hanafiya ke bhi ansu hue jari
Hazrat ne kaha bhai se ba girya o zari
Bahnein meri qaidi hon ye hai marzi-e-Bari

Kat kar sar-e Shabbir to neze pe alam ho
Pichhe khule sar qafil-e Ahl-e Haram ho
