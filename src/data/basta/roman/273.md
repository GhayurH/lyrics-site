Malik-e-saltanat-e-Kufa jo Mukhtar hue

1
Malik-e Malik-e-saltanat-e-Kufa-e Kufa jo Mukhtar hue
Intiqam-e-shuhada lene ko tayyar hue
Jitne qatil the Shah din ke giraftar hue
[Ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]

Is ne chan chan ke har ek bani-e shar ko mara
Khuli o Shimr o sinan o Umr ko mara

2
Ek din Kufe ke bazar mein ye shor hua
Ho gayi Syed-e Sajjad ki muqubul dua
Harmala qaid hua shukr khudandad-e Ali
Laye Mukhtar ke aage use ahl-e-matam-e wafa

Puchha kyun Arsh-e-Mualla ko hilaya zalim
Tir-e-Masum ko kyun tu ne lagaya zalim

3
Jo sitam to ne kiye un ka mujhe hal suna
Hathon ko Jor ke ye is ne sitam jor ne kaha
Tir chhupaye the tarkash mein watan se jo chala
Tin tiron ne to ki ran mein nishane se khata

Hun makr-o-riya Aal-e Payambar ko rulaya mein ne
Tin tiron ko nishane pe lagaya main ne

4
Ek to mashk ko jab le ke Alamdar chala
Surat-e sherid suye Syed-e abrarar chala
Mashk ko danton mein pakre hue Jarrar chala
Kat gaye shane jo talwar ka ek war chala

Meri bedad se bachchon ne na paya pani
Tir-e ek mar ke sab ne samaya pani

5
Dusre tir ka ab hal main karta hun bayan
Laye Asghar ko jo maidan mein Shah-e-do-jahan
Is-e qadar pyas ki shiddat thi ke ainthi thi zaban
Is ko hathon pe utha kar ye kya Shah ne bayan

Tum ko khauf-e ghazab-e Khaliq-e qahar nahin
Mein khata war hun bachcha to khata nahin

6
Guzar yaqin aaye ga ke ho tum tishna duhan
Rahm shayad unhein aa jaye ye hai mujh ko guman
Jis ghari ro ke ye bachche se kya Shah ne bayan
Sab ne dekha ke nikali Ali Asghar ne zaban

Jitne sar dar the munh se sab dhone lage
Fauj mein jitne sipahi the wo sab rone lage
