Sayyida Nisa-il-Alamin Hazrat Fatima Zahra Salamullah Alaiha

Khushi ki baat hai shadi ye paigham-e-masarrat hai
Magar phir bhi muhib-e-jaan-e-Nabi ke dil ki halat hai
Wohi beti ke jis ko bap ne pala hai man ban kar
Isi beti ki Payambar ke ghar se aaj rukhsat hai
Tumhein bhi hai lab par, ankhen par ansu bhi hain un ke
Judai ka alam hai aur shadi ki masarrat hai
Wo jis ko basat matni kaha karte hain Payambar
Isi lutf-e-Nabi ki aaj un ke ghar se rukhsat hai
Baba Fatima Zahra ka ghar Khaliq-e-Alam ne
Khushi hai hasb-e-fitrat aur gham hasb-e-zarurat hai
Mila hai Fatima ko Haider-e-Karrar sa shauhar
Ali par Khaliq-e-Kaun-o-Makan ki khas rahmat hai
