Jab aakhri salam ko khaime mein aaye Shah

1
Jab aakhri salam ko khaime mein aaye Shah
Rukhsat kya har ek ko bahalat-e tabah
Kubra par aur Sakina par hasrat se ki nigah
Farmaya sabr de tumhein khalaq-e mehr o mah-e

De kar dilasa aaye jo Sajjad ke qarib
Dekha para hai ghash mein wo Banu ka mah-e jabin

2
Bazu hila ke bole Shahanshah-e-namdar
Ghaflat ka waqt ye nahin utho pidar nisar
Zainab ki aur Banu ki chadar se ho hoshiyar
Ghabra ke uthe Syed-e Sajjad Ek bar

Dekha zamana hota hai khali Husain se
Bain mein hashr barpa hai Zainab ke bain se

3
Puchha pidar se aaye hain tanha Shah-e Shah-e-Umam
Na saya-e alam hai na Abbaszezi-e hasham
Kis hadise mein ho gayi Maula ki pusht kham
Chehre pe khun kaisa hai aur Chashm-e kyun hai nam

Qasim kahan hain Akbar-e kya hue
Shah ne kaha ke sab reh-e Haq mein fida hue

(dargha Amir Muhammad Rizvi ... [hawala juzwi taur par ghair wazeh])

4
Ummat ne Nana jaan-e jaan barbad kar diya
Tanha pidar hai halq aur khanjar-e jafa
Kuchh der baad khaime jalayenge ashqiya
Chhinenge hue sar se Zainab o Kulsum ki rida

Ab tum Imamhi-e Asr ho himmat se kam lo
Tufan ki zad mein Aal-e Nabi ki kashti hai tham lo

5
Maula ke is bayan pe mahshar bapa hua
Ahl-e-Haram mein rone ka ek ghulghula utha
Dauri Sakina aan ke daman pakar liya
Boli na ran ko jao Payambar ka wasta

Qurban jaun kis ke main sine pe soungi
Baba jo tum na hoge to raton ko roungi

6
Munh chum kar Husain ne godi mein le liya
Daman se ashk ponchh ke bole Shah-e-Huda
Bhai ka saya sar pe tumhare rakhe Khuda
Man bap kis ke zinda rahe hain yahan sada

[Ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Nani ko lene aayenge hum qaid-e-Sham mein

Jab aakhri salam ko khaime mein aaye Shah
