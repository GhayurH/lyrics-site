Soz

Jab thori raat qatl ki maidan mein reh gayi
Aur Al-firaq aa ke Saba ran mein keh gayi
Hamshir-e Shah-e din ki falak par Nigah-e-yas gayi
mehndi lahu ki dedi-e Purnam se bahah gayi

Jahan jaun ghari judai ki nazdik hoti thi
Munh dhanp dhanp Zainab-e nashad roti thi
(Bashukriya soz o salamkhwan Mazhar Husain Naqvi)

Ashur ki jo rat thi mahshar sifat thi
Be-kas musafiron pe qiyamat ki rat thi
Fauj-e shaqi lagaye hue apni ghat thi
Masdud tin roz se rah-e Furat thi

Kehti thi ye Sakina, taras hum pe khaiye
Marte hain hum, chacha hamein pani palaye

Jab Banu ne ran ki Ali Akbar ko Raza di
Is Ghazi ko marne ki nihayat hui shadi
Khaime se nikal bap ko jine ki dua di
aur paon pe gardan, Shah-e be kis ke jhuka di

Shah bole ke hum samjhe, jo hai dhyan tumhara
Pyare mere-e Allah nigahban tumhara
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Kirdar Husain Naqvi o Abbas Sahib)

Barchhi ki ani jab lagi Akbar ke jigar mein
Aur margaya dam Tor ke aghosh-e pidar mein
Shah ne kaha so taur tujhe le chalun ghar mein
Bazu mein na taqat hai na qaut hai jigar mein

Le jana teri lash ka dushwar hai beta
Sar apna bhi tan par ye mujhe bar hai beta
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed nzir Husain Naqvi o Haider Ali Zaidi)

Husain jab ke chale baad-e-dopehar ran ko
Na tha koi ke jo thame rikab-e-tausan ko
Husain chupke khare the jhukaye gardan ko
Sakina jhar rahi thin qaba ke daman ko

Na aasra tha koi Shah-e-Karbalai ko
Faqat bahen ne kiya tha sawar bhai ko
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed Ghulam Abbas Naqvi, Muhammad Taqi Amrohvi)

Asr ka waqt hai munis hain na yawar baqi
Na to Qasim hain na Abbas na Akbar baqi
Had hai ye ab nahin chhe mah ka Asghar baqi
Haif hai reh gaye tanha mere Sarwar baqi

sajde mein aur sitam ye tah-e-aflaak hua
Momino khatm-e-gham-e-Panjtan-e-Pak hua
(Bashukriya Farrukh Ali Syed Hasan Raza Sanganvi, Zakir Ali Wasti Rizvi)
