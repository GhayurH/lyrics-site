Insan ke liye maut hai gham-e-be-watani ka

1
Ansan liye maut hai gham be-kasi watnati ka
Jan-kah hai andoh o alam be-kasi be-watani ka
Sadma nahin kuchh maut se kam be-watani ka
Afat hai qiyamat hai sitam be-watani ka

Kanton ke alam Syed-e Sajjad se puchho
Aziye safar Muslim-e nashad se puchho

2
Ki sakht dagha kafiyon ne ghar mein bula kar
Sab phir gaye jin logon ke dawe the wafa ke
Lakhon hain adu janen kidhar jaan bacha ke
Afat mein giraftar hue Kufe mein aa ke

Yawar nahin hamdam nahin gham khawar nahin hai
Narghe mein hain aur koi madadgar nahin hai

3
Munh kar ke suye charkh Khudaya shukr Khudaya
Rahat hai ye bande ne jo kuchh zulm uthaya
Gham ye hai ke hai dur Yadullah ka jaya
Shabbir ke हाथों se kafan hum ne na paya

Duniya se suye Khuld koi dam mein safar hai
Yan hum pe jo kuchh ban gayi un ko khabar hai

4
Ye kehte the Muslim ke lainon ne qaza ra
Ek sang-dil-e sitam is lab-e majruh pe mara
Rish aur gareban mein lahu bhar gaya sara
Jab hal ye pahuncha to kahan jang ka yara

Aada se kaha dil mein zara Rahm ko ja do
Ghash aata hai pani mujhe thora sa pila do

5
Kab sunte the kis ka sukhan zulm ke bani
The qatl ki tadbir mein wo dushman-e jani
Le aai zaifa wohin ek jam mein pani
Qismat ne magar bajhne na di tishna duhani

Sukhi hui thi gul si zaban khushk gulu tha
Is pani ko munh se jo lagaya to lahu tha

6
Phanka use jab khak pe ba-dida-e-giryan girayan
Ek jam zaifa ne diya phir unhein is An
Pine bhi na paya tha koi ghont wo zishan
Pani mein juda ho ke gire gauhar-e gauhar-e-dandan

Farmaya ke sabit hua pyase hi marenge hue
Ab saqi-e Kausar hamein sairab karen hue
