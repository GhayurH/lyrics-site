Pahli manzil jo Madine se hui shuru tum ko

1
Pahli manzil jo Madine se hui Sarwar ko
Yaad sab karne lage Fatima Muztar ko
Dard-e-furqat ne jo be-tab kiya ghar bhar ko
Kaha Kubra ne ye bulwa ke Ali Akbar ko

Shah se puchh ke phir shahr mein jao bhaiya
Dil tarapta hai bahen ko meri lao bhaiya

2
Pas us ke nahin wan koi bhi nani ke siwa
Kaun uthayega use kaun pilayega dawa
Ghash se fursat use pahle hi na hoti thi zara
Ankh bhi ab to nahin kholti hogi Sughra

Nani ek dam ko juda us se na hoti hongi
Baithi gardan ko jhukaye hue roti hongi

3
Sun ke ye Shah se ja kar Ali Akbar ne kaha
Khaime mein furqat-e-Sughra se hai ek hashr bapa
Go, kisi Bibi ko fursat nahin rone se zara
Hal-e-Kubra mutaghayyar hai magar sab se siwa

Hukm gar paun, to bimar ko dekh aaun main
Maslahat ho to use sath yahan laun main

4
Ro ke Akbar se ye kehne lagi Bint-e-Haider
Kehiyo Sughra se ye tum meri taraf se Akbar
Phuphi qurban ho mat khoiyo jaan ro ro kar
Han dua kijiyo Allah se ye Sham-o-sahar

Kuchh na pardesiyon pe rah mein afat aaye
Safar-e-Kufa se Shabbir salamat aaye

5
Sun chuke sab ke jo Akbar ye payam-e-jankah
Charh ke Shabdiz-e-falak-sair pe li shahr ki rah
Bani Hashim ke mahalle mein jo pahuncha wo mah
Ja ke Sughra ko kisi ne ye khabar di nagah

Lo mubarak ho ke hamshakl-e-Payambar aaya
Ab na roo tumhein lene Ali Akbar aaya

6
Ponchh kar ankhon se ansu lagi kehne Sughra
Main na kehti thi ke bulwayenge mujh ko Baba
Aage deorhi ke jo ghore se utarte dekha
Hath phaila ke kaha aao main sadqe bhaiya

Apne Baba ki muhabbat mein qurban gayi
Tum mujhe lene ko aaye ho main pehchan gayi
