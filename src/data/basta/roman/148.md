Pahli manzil jo Madine se hui shuru tum ko

1
Pehli manzil jo Madine se hui Sarwar ko
Yaad sab karne lage Fatima-e muztar ko
Dard-e furqat ne jo be-kasi tab kya ghar bhar ko
Kaha Kubra ne ye Ali ke Ali Akbar ko

Shah se puchh ke phir shahr mein jao Bhaiya
Dil tarapta hai bahen ko meri lao bhaiya

2
Pas is ke nahin Wan koi bhi Nani ke siwa
Kaun uthaye ga use kaun pilayega ga dawa
Ghash se fursat use pehle hi na hoti thi zara
Ankh bhi ab to nahin khulti ho lungi Sughra

Nani ek dam ko juda is se na hoti hun lungi
Baithi gardan ko jhukaye hue roti hun lungi

3
Sun ke ye Shah se ja kar Ali Akbar ne kaha
Khaime mein furqat-e Sughra se hai ek hashr bapa
Goya kisi Bibi Bibi ko fursat nahin rone se zara
Hal-e Kubra mutaghayyar hai magar sab se siwa

Hukm Gar paon thas to bimar ko dekh aaun mein
Maslahat ho to use sath yahan laun mein

4
Ro ke Akbar se ye kehne lagi Bint-e-Zahra-e Haider
Kahiyo Sughra se ye tum meri taraf se Akbar
Phuphi qurban ho mat khoiyo jaan ro ro kar
Han dua kijiyo Allah se ye Sham o sahar

Kuchh na pardesiyon pe rah mein afat aaye
Safar-e Kufa se Shabbir Salamat aaye

5
Sun chuke sab ke jo Akbar ye Payam-e janka
Charh ke shab-e falak nek-sair pe li shahr ki rah
Bani Hashim ke mah mein jo pahuncha wo mah-e
Ja Sughra ko kisi ne ye khabar di Nagah

Lo mubarak ho ke mushkil-e Payambar aaya
Ab na roo tumhein lene Ali Akbar aaya

6
Ponchh kar ankhon se ansu lagi kehne Sughra
Mein na kehti thi ke bulain hue mujh ko Baba
Aage deorhi ke jo Ghore se utarte dekha
Hath phaila ke kaha aao mein sadqe Bhaiya

Apne Baba ki muhabbat mein qurban gayi
Tum mujhe lene ko aaye ho main pehchan gayi
