Ghore se jabke Qasim-e-gulgun qaba gira

1
Ghore se jabke Qasim gulgun qaba gira
Ghul par gaya ke nibabir mushkil kusha gira
Safdar jari bahadur o sher-e dagha gira
Khun mein nahan ke lakht-e dil-e Mujtaba gira

Garte hi Fauj-e zulm ka mamjam bahm hua
Zakhmi par aah-e narah Fauj-e sitam hua

2
Mara kisi ne pusht pe neza ko tan ke
Khinchi sinan-e-zulm kaleje mein aan ke
Koi tabar laga gaya mazlum jaan ke
Tegh kisi ka chal gaya sar par jawan ke

Pahlu kabhi, donon hath bhi be-kas figar the
Dhar ka ek jism tha, zarb-e-hazar the

3
Hazrat ko di sada ke chacha jaan aaiye
Khatki sinan-e zulm kaleje mein aan ke
Duniya mein koi dam ka hun mehman aaiye
Teghat kisi ka chal gaya sar par jawan ke

Pahlu bhi donon hath bhi yaksar figar the
Dulha ka Ek jism tha khanjar Hazar the

4
Dam torne laga jo ye keh kar wo dil-figar
Tifl-e-nazar ba-janib-e-aada mein ek bar
Daure udhar se tegh ba-kaf Shah-e-namdar
Ghoron se rondne lage lashe ko wan sawar

Sab girte ghore sine pe, pur-nur ho gaya
Tapon se kamsin ka badan chur ho gaya
