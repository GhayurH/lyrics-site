Ghore se jabke Qasim-e-gulgun qaba gira

1
Ghore se jabke Qasim gulgun qaba gira
Ghul par gaya ke nibabir mushkil kusha gira
Safdar jari bahadur o sher-e dagha gira
Khun mein nahan ke lakht-e dil-e Mujtaba gira

Garte hi Fauj-e zulm ka mamjam bahm hua
Zakhmi par aah-e narah Fauj-e sitam hua

2
Dam torne laga jo ye keh kar wo dil-figar
Tabl-e-zafar baja saf-e-aada mein ek bar
Daure udhar se tegh ba-kaf Shah-e-namdar
Ghoron se rondne lage lashe ko wan sawar

sab ghere Ghore sine pe nur ho gaya
Tapon se markabon ki badan chur ho gaya

3
