Ghore se jabke Qasim gulgun qaba gira

1
Ghore se jabke Qasim gulgun qaba gira
Ghul par gaya ke nabira-e-Mushkil-kusha gira
Safdar jari bahadur-o-Sher-e-dagha gira
Khun mein naha ke lakht-e-dil-e-Mujtaba gira

Girte hi fauj-e-zulm ka majma baham hua
Zakhmi par aah-e-nara-e-fauj-e-sitam hua

2
Dam torne laga jo ye keh kar wo dil-figar
Tabl-e-zafar baja saf-e-aada mein ek bar
Daure idhar se tigh-ba-kaf Shah-e-namdar
Ghoron se rondne lage lashe ko wo sawar

Sab ghere ghore sine pe nur ho gaya
Tapon se markabon ki badan chur ho gaya

3
Mara kisi ne pusht pe neze ko tan ke
Khatki sinan-e-zulm kaleje mein aan ke
[Ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Tigha kisi ka chal gaya sar par jawan ke

Pahlu bhi donon hath bhi yaksar figar the
Dulha ka ek jism tha harbe hazar the

4
Pahunche Husain lash pe jis dam ba-chashm-e-nam
Aata hua tha ankhon mein Ibn-e-Hasan ka dam
Sar apna pit kar ye pukare Shah-e-Umam
Qasim utho ke aaye hain milne ko tum se hum

Mozun na aa gayi Fatima ke nur-e-ain se
Baten to kuchh karo dam-e-aakhir Husain se

5
Hazrat ko di sada ke chacha jaan aaiye
Khadim hua Huzur pe qurban aaiye
Duniya mein koi dam ka hun mehman aaiye
Sar katne ka hota hai saman aaiye

Jallad pahunche tigh-e-do-paikar liye hue
Qatil khare hain hath mein khanjar liye hue

6
Dam torne laga jo ye keh kar wo dil-figar
Daure idhar se tigh-ba-kaf Shah-e-namdar
[Ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Ghoron se rondne lage lashe ko aan sawar

Sab tukre tukre sina-e-purnur ho gaya
Tapon se markabon ki badan chur ho gaya
