Lash-e-Akbar ki jo maqtal se utha laye Husain

1
Lash-e-Akbar ki jo maqtal se utha laye Husain
Naujawanon ko saf-e awwal se utha laye Husain
Chand ko Sham ke badal se utha laye Husain
Jaan bablab sher ko jangal se utha laye Husain

Di sada lash pe aan ke le ja Banu
Charh gaya Barchhi se Akbar ka kaleja Banu

2
Dekh le aakhri didar pisar marta hai
Samne ankhon ke ye nur-e-nazar marta hai
Ab koi dam mein tarap rashk-e Qamar marta hai
Munh se bahar hai zaban tishna jigar marta hai

Dam hai sine mein rakhmon se khun jari hai
Haye Banu! tera ghar Lutne ki tayyari hai

3
Pahunchi khaime mein jo Hazrat ki ye par dard sada
Muztarib ho gayi namus-e Rasul do sira
Kaha chala ke Sakina ne ke hai hai Bhaiya
Fizza Dauri suye dar phenk ke sar se rida

Pit-ti Zainab muztar nikal aayen bahar
Banu ghabra ke khule sar nikal aayen bahar

4
Shah ke kandhe pe dekha Ali Akbar ko nidhal
Hath phaila ke ye chillai ke hai mere lal
Kya ghazab ho gaya Ay badshah-e Hind khasal
Ras aaya na mere bachche ko atharwan sal

Tukre tahigon se badan ho gaya sara hai
Kaun tha jis ne mere sher ko mara hai

Ghore teghon se badan ho gaya sara hai hai
Ro ke Banu se ye farmane lage Sarwar-e din
Sar na pito abhi zinda hai mera mah-e jabin
Sans aati hai pe har dam hai dam-e bazu
Koch un ka bhi hai aur hum bhi hain marne ke qarin

Sab chhatte ab na raha koi hamara Banu
Is jawan bete ke gham ne hamein mara Banu

[Makhaz: Lash-e-Akbar ki jo maqtal se utha laye Husain]

Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed Zaigham Hadi o khanawada-e-Hadi Nasiri

Khatm Rasula Pak hi mein munhak rahein
Bibi Khadija aap ki azmat ko salam
Jo mihan din ki pyas par shukr-e-Khuda kare
Maula Ali ki boli Sakina hai is ka naam

[Makhuz az do manqabat, Syed Zulfiqar Husain Naqvi]
