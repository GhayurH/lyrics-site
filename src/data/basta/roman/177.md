Lash-e-Akbar ki jo maqtal se utha laye Husain

1
Lash-e-Akbar ki jo maqtal se utha laye Husain
Naujawan ko saf-e-awwal se utha laye Husain
Chand ko Sham ke badal se utha laye Husain
Jan-ba-lab sher ko jangal se utha laye Husain

Di sada lash pe aan ke le ja Bano
Chhid gaya barchhi se Akbar ka kaleja Bano

2
Dekh le aakhri didar pisar marta hai
Samne ankhon ke ye nur-e-nazar marta hai
Ab koi dam mein tarap rashk-e-qamar marta hai
Munh se bahar hai zaban tishna-jigar marta hai

Dam hai sine mein ruka zakhmon se khun jari hai
Haye Bano! tera ghar lutne ki tayyari hai

3
Pahunchi khaime mein jo Hazrat ki ye pur-dard sada
Muztarib ho gayi namus-e-Rasul-e-do-sara
Kaha chilla ke Sakina ne ke hai hai bhaiya
Fizza dauri su-e-dar phenk ke sar se rida

Pitti Zainab-e-muztar nikal aain bahar
Bano ghabra ke khule sar nikal aain bahar

4
Shah ke kandhe pe dekha Ali Akbar ko nidhal
Hath phaila ke ye chillai ke hai mere lal
Kya ghazab ho gaya ay Badshah-e-nek-khisal
Ras aaya na mere bachche ko atharwan sal

Tukre tighon se badan ho gaya sara hai
Kaun tha jis ne mere sher ko mara hai

5
Ro ke Bano se ye farmane lage Sarwar-e-din
Sar na pito abhi zinda hai mera mah-jabin
Sans aati hai pe har dam hai dam-e-bazpin
Koch un ka bhi hai aur hum bhi hain marne ke qarin

Sab chhute ab na raha koi hamara Bano
Is jawan bete ke gham ne hamein mara Bano

[Makhaz: Lash-e-Akbar ki jo maqtal se utha laye Husain]

Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed Zaigham Hadi o khanawada-e-Hadi Nasiri

Khatm Rasula Pak hi mein munhak rahen
Bibi Khadija aap ki azmat ko salam
Jo mihan din ki pyas par shukr-e-Khuda kare
Maula Ali ki boli Sakina hai is ka naam

[Makhuz az do manqabat, Syed Zulfiqar Husain Naqvi]
