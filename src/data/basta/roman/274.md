[Malik-e-saltanat-e-Kufa jo Mukhtar hue — jari]

7
Jab Ibn-e Ibn-e-Saud ne lashkar mein talatum dekha
Aaya ghabraya hua pas mere aur ye kaha
Jald kar kam tamam is-e ka na kar der-e zara
Mein ne ek tir seh pahlu idhar-e ko phanka

Mere ek tir ne donon ko barabar tora
Bazwaye Bazu-e-Shah guluye Ali Asghar tora

8
Jab gulu-e-Ali Asghar pe para mera tir
Gar para Shah-e ke hathon se ye tarpa be-kasi sher
Dekh kar charkh ko yas se roye Shah-e sher
Aur o kar kaha Hazrat ne ke Ay Rab-e Qadir

Naujawanon ka to fidya hua Akbar mera
Shiaon ke bachchon pe qurban hai Asghar mera

Malik-e-saltanat-e-Kufa jo Mukhtar hue

Husain, Sibt-e-Rasul-e-Zaman, salam alaik

1
Husain Sibt-e Rasulalaik-e zaznazmat salam Alaik
Husain dilbar-e Khaibar shaknkh salam Alaik
Mere Imamam-e gharib-e altin salam Alaik
Shahid o be-kasi lahad o be-kasi kafnfif salam Alaik

Ada ye aakhri khidmat karo azadaro
Nabi ke lal ko rukhsat karo azadaro

2
Husain aaj tumhein kainat roti hai
Tumhari tishna labib par Furat roti hai
Sahar ka chak gareban hai raat roti hai
Tumhari maut pe ab tak hayat roti hai

Tumhare gham mein zamana udas hai Maula
Har Ek taziya khana udas hai Maula

3
Husain tum ho zamane mein Fateh-e-Azam
Tumhi ne din-e-Payambar kiya hai mustahkam
Tumhari zat wajah-e khilqat-e admad
Na hote tum to na hota koi Khuda ki qasam

Salam tum par tumhare wafa sharon par
Salam khun mein dube hue sitaron par
