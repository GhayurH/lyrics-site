Ghali hai tishna labib ki katab pani mein
Rehan Azmi

Ghali hai tishna labib ki katab pani mein
Wafa dikha gayi Khaibar ka bab pani mein
jamal-e Hazrat-e Abbas, tegh o mashk o alam
Chumt rahe hain kai aftab pani mein
sawar-e Pyasa, rahwar-e Pyasa raha
Agarche dub chuki thi rakba pani mein
Pila pila ke shahidon ke naam par pani
nikali khub ye rah-e sawab pani mein
[Ek sher juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Gham-e Husain ke taza gulab pani mein
Agar Husain na hote to mahal-e gaya hota
Tamam-e kar-e risalat aab pani mein
Takankte lagta hai dil-e khun ho ke ankhon se
Najanane dekhti kya hain Rubab pani mein
Ajab shahadat-e Shabbir ka hai pass-e manzar
Sawal-e dasht mein dekha jawab pani mein
Lab-e Sakina se jo pyas ban ke abhra tha
Simat ke aa gaya wo iztirab pani mein
Laga ke band-e aab-e rawan Shah din par
Adu ki ho gayi mitti kharab pani mein
Bajuz shujaat-e Abbas namwar Rehan
Likha hai kis ne wafa ka nasbab pani mein

Majraji kehte hain Shabbir ka shaida mujh ko
Rehan Azmi

Majraji kehte hain Shabbir ka shaida mujh ko
Hashr ki dhoop ka phir hai kya khatra mujh ko
kehti thi Aun o Muhammad se ye Bint-e Zahra
Pei liya pani to dikhlan na chehra mujh ko
Bole Abbas ke sharminda hun bachchon se bahut
Janib-e khaima na le jaiye Aqa mujh ko
Lash-e Akbar pe ye farmate the ro kar Shabbir
Zakhm dikhlate ho dikhlan tha sehra mujh ko
Banu kehti thin meri sans raki jati hai
Mar dale ga ye thehra hua jhula mujh ko
Qaid mein mein Sakina ka bayan tha Baba
Aaiye dasht hai zindan ka andhera mujh ko
Aa ke roye pe Muhammad ki pukarin Zainab
Dar badar aap ki ummat ne phiraya mujh ko
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Waqar Husain o Naqvi Baradaran)

Iltimas Surah Fatiha baraye isal-e sawab
[Asma juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
