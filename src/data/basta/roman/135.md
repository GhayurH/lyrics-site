Ghuli hai tishna-labi ki kitab pani mein
Rehan Azmi

Ghuli hai tishna-labi ki kitab pani mein
Wafa dikha gayi Khaibar ka bab pani mein
Jamal-e-Hazrat-e-Abbas, tigh-o-mashk-o-alam
Chamak rahe hain kai aftab pani mein
Sawar pyasa, rahwar pyasa raha
Agarche dub chuki thi rikab pani mein
Pila pila ke shahidon ke naam par pani
Nikali khub ye rah-e-sawab pani mein
[Ek sher juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Gham-e-Husain ke taza gulab pani mein
Agar Husain na hote to mahal gaya hota
Tamam-e-kar-e-Risalat-Maab pani mein
Tapakne lagta hai dil khun ho ke ankhon se
Najane dekhti kya hain Rubab pani mein
Ajab shahadat-e-Shabbir ka hai pas-e-manzar
Sawal-e-dasht mein dekha jawab pani mein
Lab-e-Sakina se jo pyas ban ke ubhra tha
Simat ke aa gaya wo iztirab pani mein
Laga ke band-e-ab-e-rawan Shah-e-din par
Adu ki ho gayi mitti kharab pani mein
Bajuz shujaat-e-Abbas namwar Rehan
Likha hai kis ne wafa ka nisab pani mein

Mujrai kehte hain Shabbir ka shaida mujh ko
Rehan Azmi

Mujrai kehte hain Shabbir ka shaida mujh ko
Hashr ki dhup ka phir hai kya khatra mujh ko
Kehti thi Aun-o-Muhammad se ye Bint-e-Zahra
Pi liya pani to dikhlana na chehra mujh ko
Bole Abbas ke sharminda hun bachchon se bahut
Janib-e-khaima na le jaiye Aqa mujh ko
Lash-e-Akbar pe ye farmate the ro kar Shabbir
Zakhm dikhlate ho dikhlana tha sehra mujh ko
Bano kehti thin buri sans ruki jati hai
Mar dalega ye thehra hua jhula mujh ko
Qaid-khane mein Sakina ka bayan tha Baba
Aaiye dasht hai zindan ka andhera mujh ko
Aa ke rauze pe Muhammad ki pukarin Zainab
Dar-ba-dar aap ki ummat ne phiraya mujh ko
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Waqar Husain o Naqvi baradaran)

Iltimas Surah Fatiha baraye isal-e-sawab
[Asma juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
