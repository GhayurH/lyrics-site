Pahuncha jo ran mein Sher-e-zi-jah ka pisar

1
Pahuncha jo ran mein Shabbar zi jah-e-Muhammad ka pisar
Hujjat tamam kar ke dua ba kar o kar-o-far
Arzu ko di wo jhuke Mamun ke sar
Man ke Boli shukr khudandad-e Bahr o bar

Kunbe mein baat reh gayi wo kam ho gaya
Ran mein bap ka bara naam ho gaya

2
Baten ye ho reh thin Haram mein ke Ek bar
Qasim pe mil ke tut pari fauj-e-nabakar
Simte idhar-e idhar-e se hazaron zabon nisar-e
Charon taraf se parne lage lab pe war

Harbe liye the qurb mein jo bad-shuur the
Patthar wo marate the jo mamjam se dur the

3
Garte hi khak par jo hua ghash wo mah-e mah-liqa
Fauj-e adu mein fath ke bajon ka Ghul hua
Man ne sada suni to ye dil tham kar kaha
Meri kamai Hind lagi shukr-e kabraya

Bachche pe mere mehr hui zillajal ki
Lo naiyo barat charhi mere lal ki

4
Pahunche jo lash-e Ibn-e Hasan par bahal-e zar
Dekha ke sar ko katne wale hain bad nisar-e
Ghusse se kanpne lage Abbas-e Namdar
Kar ke Barhe sifat-e sher-e Kirdigar

Bhage adu jo dar ke to naqsha badal gaya
Bajj chal mein is yatim ka lasha kuchal gaya

5
Rote hue qarib jo aaye Shah-e hada
dekhe tamam uzw-e burida juda juda
hathon se dil pakar ke kaha wa mhmda
Ummat ka ye suluk to dekho paye Khuda

Ibn-e-Hasan ki jaan pe sadme guzar gaye
Lo Nana jaan Qasim-e-nashad mar gaye

Iltimas Surah Fatiha baraye isal-e-sawab
