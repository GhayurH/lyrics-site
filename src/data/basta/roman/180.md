Pahuncha jo ran mein Shabbar zi-jah ka pisar

1
Pahuncha jo ran mein Shabbar zi-jah ka pisar
Hujjat tamam kar ke dua ba-kar-o-far
Aarzu ko di wo jhuke namiyon ke sar
Man ke boli shukr Khudawand-e-bahr-o-bar

Kunbe mein bat reh gayi wo kam ho gaya
Ran mein bap ka bara naam ho gaya

2
Baten ye ho rahi thin Haram mein ke ek bar
Qasim pe mil ke tut pari fauj-e-nabakar
Simte idhar udhar se hazaron zabun-shiar
Charon taraf se parne lage lab pe war

Harbe liye the qurb mein jo bad-shuur the
Patthar wo marte the jo majma se dur the

3
Girte hi khak par jo hua ghash wo mah-liqa
Fauj-e-adu mein fath ke bajon ka ghul hua
Man ne sada suni to ye dil tham kar kaha
Meri kamai nek lagi shukr-e-Kibriya

Bachche pe mere mehr hui Zuljalal ki
Lo niyo barat charhi mere lal ki

4
Pahunche jo lash-e-Ibn-e-Hasan par ba-hal-e-zar
Dekha ke sar ko katne wale hain bad-shiar
Ghusse se kanpne lage Abbas-e-namdar
Lalkar ke barhe sifat-e-Sher-e-Kirdigar

Bhage adu jo dar ke to naqsha badal gaya
Bajal chal mein us yatim ka lasha kuchal gaya

5
Rote hue qarib jo aaye Shah-e-Huda
Dekhe tamam uzw-e-burida juda juda
Hathon se dil pakar ke kaha Wa Muhammada
Ummat ka ye suluk to dekho paye Khuda

Ibn-e-Hasan ki jaan pe sadme guzar gaye
Lo Nana jaan Qasim-e-nashad mar gaye

[Makhaz: Pahuncha jo ran mein Sher-e-zi-jah ka pisar / Nasim Amrohvi]

Iltimas Surah Fatiha baraye isal-e-sawab
