Gharat-e-khaima-e-Sarwar ko jab aaye aada

1
Gharat-e-khaima-e-Sarwar ko jab aaye aada
Nangi talwaren liye be-adab aaye aada
Shor bibiyon mein hua, be-ghazab aaye aada
Koi waris na raha sar par tab aaye aada

Aas ab kis ki hai kaun aah bachaye hum ko
In jafakaron se Allah bachaye hum ko

2
Mili bibiyan thin faryad ke hum lutte hain
Ay Khuda tere Payambar ke Haram lutte hain
Aaj be-jurm ye paband-e-alam lutte hain
Yun musafir kabhi pardes mein kam lutte hain

Aag parde ki qanaton mein laga di hai hai
Masnad-e-Ahmad-e-Mukhtar jala di hai hai

3
Thin bapa Aal-e-Nabi mein ye fughan-o-zari
Itne mein rakh huin jal ke qanatein sari
Nikle maidan mein ghabra ke Haram-e-Akbari
Barchhiyan tane hue gird the un ke nari

Dar se sine mein kisi Bibi ki sans urti thi
Larkhara kar koi bachcha liye gir parti thi

4
Pai gharat se jo aada ne faraghat Akbar
Umar-e-Saad se ek Shami ne ye ki guftar
Lut chuki Badshah-e-kaun-o-makan ki sarkar
Koi shai ke lashkaron ne ab nahin chhori zinhar

Haram-e-Shah mein ab aur to kya baqi hai
Sar par bibiyon ke bas ek rida baqi hai

5
Shimr bola ke ridaon ko bhi lo jald utar
Nahin hakim ke gunahgaron ko parda darkar
Sar barahna rahen namus-e-Rasul-e-Mukhtar
Be-kasaon ke jo hon unt karo un pe sawar

Qatl waris huwe dehshat hamein kia hai in ki
Jitna ro ro ke ye chillayen saza hai in ki

6
Sar-e-Zainab se rida lene lage jab azlam
Thartharai thi zamin, palta tha Arsh-e-Azam
Ro ke wo kehti thi ay zalim zara ki qasam
Maryam-o-Hajra se martaba mera nahin kam

Ghazab-e-Haq se kahan bach ke nikal paoge
Aah ek aisi karungi ke jal jaoge
