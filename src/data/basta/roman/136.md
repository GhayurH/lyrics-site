Kahan wo fikr-e duniya koi daman ger rakhte hain
Syed Aqil Abbas Jafari

Kahan wo fikr-e duniya daman ger rakhte hain
ke apne hath mein jo daman-e Shabbir rakhte hain
Shab-e Ashur ye uthte chiraghon se hua roshan
Andhere mein bhi kuchh chehre Ajib tauqir rakhte hain
Nazar aati nahin Lekin rag-e pe mein utren hai
Ajab talwar hai jo Asghar-e be-shir rakhte hain
Unhein maqna o chadar se kya bhala koi
Jo sar pe sabaina-e chadar-e Tathir rakhte hain
Zamin Hazrat Musa mein rakha hai qadam lekin
Na mansab-e-Ala se gharaz na khahshih-e jagari rakhte hain
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Aftab Zahra / Mona Begum Syed Ali Husain)

Husain karb o bula ko bana ke suye hain
Zafar Abbas Zafar

Husain karb o bula ko basa ke suye hain
Madina chhor ke jangal mein aa ke suye hain
Isi ke waste mangi thi muhlat-e Us shab
Husain Hurr ka muqaddar jaga ke suye hain
Jo sath le ke gaye dagh-e matam-e Shabbir
Chiragh apni lahad mein jala ke suye hain
Sakina pas na jhula na god-e Madar ki
Kahan ye Asghar-e nadan ja ke suye hain

Zaban-e khushk se Manga tha pyas mein pani
Mila hai tir magar muskura ke suye hain
Utha na zajuj Hurr un-e yatim bachchon ko
Tamanche Shimr ke Masum kha ke suye hain
Lahad bhi tar na hui haye be-kasi Husain
Par ki lash pe ansu baha ke suye hain
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed azadar Husain Kazmi)

ye matam ki sada zinda rahe lungi
Suhail Shah

Ye matam ki sada zinda rakhe lungi
hamein karb o bula zinda rakhe lungi
Alam ke saye mein ja ke tujh ko
[ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Ali ke ishq mein mar jaye jo bhi
Use is ki qaza zinda rakhe lungi
aza Shabbir ki marne na de lungi
Diyaye Sayyida zinda rakhe lungi
Main duniya se chala jaunga lekin
Mujhe Shah ki aza zinda rakhe lungi
hamesha qaid khane ki faza ko
Sakina ki buka zinda rakhe lungi
Sahil apne kafan mein sath le ja
tujhe khak-e shifa zinda rakhe lungi
(Bashukriya salam khwan Anees Haider Naqvi)
