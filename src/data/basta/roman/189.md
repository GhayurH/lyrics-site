[Likha hai jab koi hami na Shah-e-din ka raha — jari]

4
Kaha Husain ne pani tumhein pila layen
Chalo ge Nana ki ummat ke pas le jayen
Sitamgaron ko ye halat tumhari dikhlayen
Saghir jaan ke shayad adu taras khayen

Dahan ko khol ke sukhi zaban dikha dena
Ke tin roz se pyasa hun ye jata dena

5
Gharz wida kiya aur Shah-e-Imam chale
Sitamgaron ki taraf Shah-e-tishna-kam chale
Pisar ko hathon pe rakhe hue Imam chale
Qadam qadam pe udhar maut ke payam chale

Tamam pyason mein pyara jo tha ye pisar
Husain dhal se saya kiye the Asghar par

6
Pare se fauj ke naagaah Hurmalaa nikla
kamaan dosh se, chille se teer le ke chala
Gulue lakht-e-dil-e-Shah-e-Karbala taaka
Kaman mein teer ko jora shaqi ne aur ye kaha

Husain! ab wo pilata hun aab-e-sard in ko
Ke ta-ba-hashr lagegi na pyas kamsin ko

7
Ye keh ke tir ko jora, idhar ye hal hua
Ke halq chhid gaya, masum khun mein lal hua
Dahan se khun ugalne laga, nidhal hua
Ek aah man ki, aur intiqal hua

Pidar ne pyas se tasalli jaan ko dekha
Kabhi zamin ko, kabhi asman ko dekha
