4
Ki apne Nabi-zade ki kya izzat-o-tauqir
Sar ta ba qadam khun mein bhari chand si taswir
Kasrat se hain zakhm-e-tabar-o-khanjar-o-shamshir
Nezon pe to neze hain pare tiron pe hain tir

Ye gul-sa badan ghoron se pamal kiya hai
Hai hai mere Aqa tera kya hal kiya hai

5
Sughra ka ajab hal hai furqat mein tumhari
Ye zof ki shiddat hai ke ghash rehta hai tari
Jab ankh khuli ro ke ye Nani ko pukari
Kya aai safar se mere Baba ki sawari

Dam tan se nikal jayega is ranj-o-mihan mein
Kya janiye kab aayenge Shabbir watan mein

6
Phir yas se sar rakh diya lashe ke qadam par
Nauha tha ke hai hai Asadullah ke dilbar
Sughra jo tumhein puchhe to main kya hun ja kar
Us ko ye sunana hai ke ab aate hain Sarwar

Ye keh dun ke sar-e-tan se utara gaya Sughra
Pardes mein Baba tera mara gaya Sughra

[Makhaz: Kufe ko chala Qasid-e-Sughra jo watan se]

Imtihan-gah mein pahunche jo Shah-e-jinn-o-bashar

1
Imtihan-gah mein pahunche jo Shah-e-jinn-o-bashar
Zulm kya kya na se Karb-o-Bala mein aa kar
Aa gaya aakhri manzil pe shahadat ka safar
Sab ka khun beh gaya, ab reh gaye tanha Sarwar

Bap ke waste beti ka ariza laya
Ek qasid sar-e-maqtal, khat-e-Sughra laya

2
Khat mein likha tha ke bas ab jald hi bulwayen mujhe
Yaad mein bali Sakina ki na tarpayen mujhe
Furqat-e-Asghar-e-nadan mein na rulayen mujhe
Baba jaan, bhai se keh den ke wo le jayen mujhe

Bap ka dil hi samajhta hai jo halat hogi
Us ghari kaifiyat-e-zabt, qiyamat hogi

3
Khat parha, sabr kiya, dil se lagaya khat ko
Qabr-e-Asghar pe rakha, baithe, uthaya khat ko
Lash-e-Akbar pe gaye, parh ke sunaya khat ko
Ek pal bhi na nigahon se hataya khat ko

Khat se Sughra ki jo taswir ubhar aati thi
Zabt karne pe bhi ek aah nikal jati thi
