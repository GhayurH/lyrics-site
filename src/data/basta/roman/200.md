[Kufe ko chala Qasid-e-Sughra jo watan se — jari]

4
Ki apne Nabi Nabi-zade ki kya izzat o tauqir
Sar ta ba qadam khun mein bhari chand si taswir
Kasrat se hain zakhm-e-tir-o-khanjar-o-shamshir
Nezon pe to neze hain pare tiron pe hain tir

Ye gul-e sa badan Ghoron se pamal kya hai
Hai hai mere Aqa tera kya hal kya hai

5
Sughra ka ajab hal hai furqat mein tumhari
Ye zof ki shiddat hai ke ghash rehta hai tari
jab ankh khuli ro ke ye Nani ko pukari
Kya aai safar se mere Baba ki sawari

dam tan se nikal jaye ga is ranj o mihan mein
Kya janti, ab aayenge Shabbir watan mein

6
Phir pas se sar rakh diya lashe ke qadam par
Nauha tha ke hai hai Asadullah ke dilbar
Sughra jo tumhein puchhe to main kya kahun ja kar
is ko ye sunana hai ke ab aate hain Sarwar

Ye keh dun ke sar tan se utara gaya Sughra
Pardes mein Baba tera mara gaya Sughra

Imtihan-gah mein pahunche jo Shah-e-jinn-o-bashar

1
Imtihan gah mein pahunche jo Shah-e jin-e o bashar
zulm kya kya na se karb o bula mein aa kar
Aa gaya aakhri manzil pe shahadat ka safar
Sab ka khun bahah gaya, ab reh gaye tanha Sarwar

Bap ke waste beti ka ariza laya
Ek qasid-e sar-e maqtal, khat-e Sughra laya

2
Khat mein Likha tha ke bas ab jald hi bulwaein mujhe
Yaad mein bali Sakina ki na tarpayein mujhe
Furqat-e Asghar-e nadan mein na rulwayein mujhe
Baba jaan, bhai se keh din ke wo le jayen mujhe

Bap ka dil hi samajhta hai jo halat ho lungi
is ghari kafiyat-e zabt, qayamat ho lungi

3
khat parha, sabr kya, dil se lagaya khat ko
qabr-e Asghar pe rakha, baithe, uthaya khat ko
Lash-e Akbar pe gaye, parh ke sunaya khat ko
Ek pal bhi na nigahon se hataya khat ko

Khat se Sughra ki jo taswir ubhar aati thi
Zabt karne pe bhi ek aah nikal jati thi
