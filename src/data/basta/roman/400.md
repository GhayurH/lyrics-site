Naam Nani Fatima aur Fatima ki jaan-nashin
am-e Umm-ul-Banin

Naam Nani Fatima aur Fatima ki jaan-nashin
Sher-dil, aali-nasab bachchon ki man Umm-ul-Banin
Zauja-e-Sher-e-Khuda, Abbas se Ghazi ki man
Jo dua ki thi Ali ne us ke maqsad ki amin
Zainab-o-Kulsum-o-Shabbir-o-Hasan ki sarparast
zamza dar-e kar-e Sardar-e nsa-e alalmin
Un ke husn-e-tarbiyat ka jawedani shahkar
Bawafa Abbas, khidmat-guzar-e-Shah-e-Din
Saut ki aulad par kar de jo beton ko nisar
Ek bajuz Umm-ul-Banin duniya mein man aisi nahin
Waqiat-e-Karbala sunti rahin tafsil se
Kya kuchh tha ke hal-e-Abbas ka puchha nahin
Han magar naam-e-Husain aaya to ye ro kar kaha
Kya mera Abbas dam-e-Karbala mein tha nahin
Jab kabhi likhta hun Zair madh mein Abbas ki
Yaad aati hain mujhe be-sakhta Umm-ul-Banin

all-e all-e ye aizaz, ye rutba Fizza

all-e all-e ye aizaz, ye rutba Fizza
Haq ne bakhshi hai tujhe qurbat-e-Zahra Fizza
Tu nahin aina-e-Tathir mein shamil lekin
Tujh pe hai Malik-e-Tathir ka saya Fizza
Zainab-o-Shabbar-o-Shabbir ko pala tu ne
Teri aghosh hai Masumon ka jhula Fizza
Tujh ko man kehti thi aulad-e-Batul-e-Azra
teri Meraj hai ye Mansab-e-Ala aala Fizza
Haider-o-Fatima-o-Shabbar-o-Shabbir ke sath
Sall-e-Ala-e Ali teri bhi tausif mein aaya Fizza
Guftagu karti thi Quran ke hawale de kar
Aisi ek talib-e-kutub-e-Zahra Fizza
Har jagah misl-e-sipar tu rahi Zainab ke liye
Zarre khata bhi kya tu ne gawara Fizza
Madh-e-Fizza bhi hai Zahra ki khushi ay Zair
Dil ye kehta hai likhun tera qasida Fizza
