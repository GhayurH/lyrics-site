Chashm-e afkal ne Zainab ko khule sar dekha

1
Reti par Barchhi khaye para tha jo nur-e ein
Baithe the dil ko pakre hue Shah-e-mashriqain
Nagah ek kaniz pukari ba-shor-o-shain
dam tarat hai Pyasa be sher ya Husain

Lillah jald khaime mein tashrif laiye
Banu ka lal ruth gaya hai manaiye

2
Man boli aisi fal nahin sab pe late hain
Wari na yun kaho, mujhe waswase aate hain
Aqa tumhare pyas bujhane ko jate hain
Bachchon pe to jahan mein sabhi rahm khate hain

Kyun kar kahun ke un pe shaqi hath uthayenge
Masum jaan kar bhi na pani pilayenge

3
Ghul par gaya ke Asghar be sher alwida
Ay nur-e ein Hazrat-e Shabbir alwida
Man al-firaq kehti thi, hamshir alwida
Qismat pukari Ay hadhad-e tir alwida

Aal-e Nabi ko Mariz-e-hijr ke Sadme Barhe hue
Bachche bhi shor-e sun ke qarib-e khare hue

4
Asghar ne ro ke dasht mein aaye Shah-e hada
bachche ka munh dikha ke ye faujon ko di Nida
be zaban-e rahm ke qabil nahin hai kya
do din se is kali ko bhi pani nahin mila

bachcha nidhal hai, use thora sa aab do
surat-e sawal hai, use thora sa aab do

5
Abbas ke pisar ne pukara duhai hai
Marne chala Husain ka pyara duhai hai
Jata hai Shah ka raj-dulara, duhai hai
Aqa bichhar raha hai hamara duhai hai

Kehta hai dil ke sath chalo tir khaiyo
Baba ye keh gaye the ke jhula jhulaiyo

6
Zalim ne Hurmala se ishara mein kuchh kaha
Hansta hua wo shum kamin-gah ko chala
Bachche ko le ke pichhe hate Shah-e-Karbala
Nagah zamin kanp gayi, asman hila

Man Boli aisi fal nahin lab pe late hain
Wari na yun kaho mujhe waswas aate hain
