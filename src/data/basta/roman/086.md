Qita

Ali Janab bhi, bazu-e-Anjanab bhi hai
Khuda ka sher bhi hai aur Bu Turab bhi hai
Safon ko jorne wala Ali ba-waqt-e-namaz
Agar ho jang to phir saf-shikan khitab bhi hai

###

Kaleem-e Aal Aal-e-Aba Shahid Naqvi

Hasan ki sulh mein yun Karbala jalwa-gar hai
Hudaibiya mein jaise Fath-e-Makka muskurati hai
Ali ki khamoshi, Zahra ki hasrat, sulh-e-Shabbar ki
Ye wo ajza hain jin se Karbala tartib pati hai

###

Professor Sardar Naqvi

Aqidat paikar-e-alfaz mein jab jagmagati hai
Mujhe lafzon se apne nikhat-e-Firdaus aati hai
Janab-e-Fatima ke lal hain Jannat ke shahzade
Yahi wo baat hai Jannat ko jo Jannat banati hai
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Munis Rizvi, Shaukat Wanish o Nafis)

Qita

Lab pe hon naam un ka to parhte hain sab durud
Zehnon mein mustajab imamat Hasan ki hai
Jis ne un se chhini hukumat baad-e-qarib
Lekin dilon pe ab bhi hukumat Hasan ki hai

Qita

Husain Shah bhi hai aur badshah bhi hai
Husain din bhi hai aur din panah bhi hai
Na ki Yazid ki baiat, kata diya sar ko
Isi bina pe to bunyad-e-La Ilaha bhi hai

Qita

Jaise the zulm-o-sitam ke muqable mein Husain
Ali ki misl the Haq ke muamale mein Husain
Buland the sar-e-mahfil Nabi ke hathon par
Ghadir-e-Khum mein Ali aur Mubahale mein Husain
(Bashukriya Khatib Hazrat Allama Ali Karrar Naqvi)

###

Kaleem-e Aal Aal-e-Aba Shahid Naqvi

Apne dukhon mein aaya jo Shabbir ka khayal
Shikwa tarap ke shukr mein tabdil ho gaya
Har gham mein Karbala ne sahara diya hamein
Har gham gham-e-Husain mein tahlil ho gaya
