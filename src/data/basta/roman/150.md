Is darja be-qarar the Sultan-e-bahr-o-bar

1
Is darja be-qarar the Sultan-e-bahr-o-bar
Nikle the jab mein Hajj mein paye safar
Mur mur ke dekhte the, suye Kaaba thi nazar
Tha ranj qalb-e Shah pe chhatne ka is-e qadar

Kehte the wada hai ka bara Ishtiaq hai
Lekin Faraqaq-e Hajj bhi mere dil ko shaq hai

2
Majbur ho ke jata hun Ay Rab-e Zulminan
warna mein Chhorta na mazar-e Shah-e Shah-e-Zaman
Man ki lahad na Chhorta, na Marqad-e Hasan
Mar kar yahin pe dafn jo hota ye hai watan

Hota nasib qarib, Rasul-e Shah-e-Zaman ka bhi
Madar ki bhi lahad ka, mazar-e Hasan ka bhi

3
Razi magar hun main teri marzi pe ay Khuda
Jo jo parin hue mujh pe masaib uthaunga ga
Teri hi zat par mujhe takiya raha sada
Ab tujh se chahta hun ye quwwat bhi kar ata

Kat jaye ye bhi marhala sabr o Raza ke sath
Meri zaban pe shukr rahe har bula ke sath

4
Iza ho rah mein kahin Ya sakhti-e safar
Har waqt mein boshikhi pe rahe bas meri nazar
Mutlaq harsa ho na mujhe lakh hun khatar
Ho jaye ye bhi teri hi nusrat se jang sar

Hasrat ye hai ke sajde mein gardan agar kate
Ummat ki maghfirat ki dua mein ye sar kate

5
Gardan saghir ki hadhad-e tir ho to kya
Nazr-e ajal jo Akbar-e dilgir ho to kya
Abbas sa jari ho-e shamshir ho to kya
Neze ki Nakwan-e par sar-e Shabbir ho to kya

Pyase Shahid nehr pe mehman tamam hun
Ummat ki maghfirat ke ye saman tamam hun

6
Afsos ki jagah hai jo aisa ho badshah
Rehne na paye ghar mein wo ummat ka khair khahwa
Har ja hon qatl ke liye amada rusiyah
Paye Amman na khana Mabud mein bhi aah

Aise sitam hon jis pe wo majbur kya kare
Kyun kar na Hajj ko chhor ke karb-o-bala chale
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Akbar Husain Zaidi o Dilbar Zaidi)
