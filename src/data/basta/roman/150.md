Is darja be-qarar the Sultan-e-bahr-o-bar

1
Is darja be-qarar the Sultan-e-bahr-o-bar
Nikle the jab zamana-e-Hajj mein paye safar
Mur mur ke dekhte the, su-e-Kaaba thi nazar
Tha ranj qalb-e-Shah pe chhutne ka is qadar

Kehte the wada-gah ka bara ishtiyaq hai
Lekin firaq-e-Hajj bhi mere dil ko shaq hai

2
Majbur ho ke jata hun ay Rabb-e-Zulminan
Warna main chhorta na mazar-e-Shah-e-Zaman
Man ki lahad na chhorta, na marqad-e-Hasan
Mar kar yahin pe dafn jo hota ye hai watan

Hota nasib qurb, Rasul-e-Zaman ka bhi
Madar ki bhi lahad ka, mazar-e-Hasan ka bhi

3
Razi magar hun main teri marzi pe ay Khuda
Jo jo parenge mujh pe masaib uthaunga
Teri hi zat par mujhe takiya raha sada
Ab tujh se chahta hun ye quwwat bhi kar ata

Kat jaye ye bhi marhala sabr-o-raza ke sath
Meri zaban pe shukr rahe har bala ke sath

4
Iza ho rah mein kahin ya sakhti-e-safar
Har waqt main ba-khushi pe rahe bas meri nazar
Mutlaq hiras ho na mujhe lakh hon khatar
Ho jaye ye bhi teri hi nusrat se jang sar

Hasrat ye hai ke sajde mein gardan agar kate
Ummat ki maghfirat ki dua mein ye sar kate

5
Gardan Saghir ki hadaf-e-tir ho to kya
Nazr-e-ajal jo Akbar-e-dilgir ho to kya
Abbas sa jari tah-e-shamshir ho to kya
Neze ki nok par sar-e-Shabbir ho to kya

Pyase shahid nahr pe mehman tamam hon
Ummat ki maghfirat ke ye saman tamam hon

6
Afsos ki jagah hai jo aisa ho badshah
Rehne na paye ghar mein wo ummat ka khairkhwah
Har ja hon qatl ke liye amada rusiyah
Paye aman na khana-e-Mabud mein bhi aah

Aise sitam hon jis pe wo majbur kya kare
Kyun kar na Hajj ko chhor ke karb-o-bala chale
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Akbar Husain Zaidi o Dilbar Zaidi)
