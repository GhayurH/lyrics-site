Bare ghukhmarwa hain wo gham na kar hashmat na tum
Karam se Panjtan ke hashr mein bakhshish teri hogi

Majraji jis ne mere Maula ko pehchana nahin
Aisa lagta hai use Firdaus mein jana nahin
Dekho fartir gaya gahwar Shabbir tak
Kaun kehta hai farishton ne bhi pehchana nahin
Ban gaye sail gudai ke Sharaf ko dekh kar
Warna Ahl-e-Bait ko rotiyan khana nahin
Qabzib farzand-e Zahra mein hai zazfal-e Mustafa
Baat hai likhi hui ab is ko ujlanha nahin
De diya hai Fauj-e Hurr ko sara pani Shah ne
Zalimo ab Shah ke bachchon ko tarsana nahin
Kehte the ne o Muhammad se ye man ke hausle
Jang karna har taraiti ki taraf jana nahin
Karbala mein sabr-e Sarwar dekhne aao Khalil
Aap ko lash par maidan se lana nahin
Yun tarap kar kehti thi qabr-e Sakina se Rubab
Sab watan jate hain beti kya tumhein jana nahin

Meri ankhon se hain ashk-e gham-e Sarwar nikalte hain
Yawar Azmi

Meri ankhon se yun ashk-e Sarwar nikalte hain
Sadaf ke dahan tere jis tarah gauhar nikalte hain
Baraye bakhshish-e ummat-e siyal khane ko maqtal mein
Muhammad Mustafa ban kar Ali Akbar nikalte hain
Kaleja kanpta hai hamla ka dil dharakta hai
Kaman-e zulm ran mein torne Asghar nikalte hain
Liye mashk o alam jate hain suye darya
Ye Ghul hai ab Fateh-e Khaibar nikalte hain
Kaha Laila ne Rab khair karna mere Akbar ki
Sitamgar apne hathon mein liye khanjar nikalte hain
Shah din-e khutde baithe hain ran mein turbat-e Asghar
Zamin se apne sar ko phorte patthar nikalte hain
Chale jab jang ko ne o Muhammad Bolin ye Zainab
Dua ko ran mein mere Hamza o Jafar nikalte hain
Muhabbat-e Karbala walon ki yawar rang laye lungi
Jo un ki chah mein dube lab-e Kausar nikalte hain

Mujrimi jis ne mere Maula ko pehchana nahin
Maulana Syed Zeeshan Haider Jawadi
