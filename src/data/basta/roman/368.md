Ali aa gaye / Syed Sibt Jafar

Jab Khuda ko pukara Ali aa gaye, jab Ali aa gaye zindagi aa gayi
Zindagi bandagi roshni aa gayi, roshni aa gayi agahi aa gayi
Maut un ke muhibbon ko aati nahin, aa bhi jaye to phir bach ke jati nahin
Maut ka kya khatar, maut se kya hazar, is se bachna bhi kya! Aa gayi aa gayi
Ghusl-e-mayyat na kehna mere ghusl ko, ujle malbus ko mat kafan nam do
Main chala hun Ali se mulaqat ko, jis ki thi arzu wo ghari aa gayi
Jibrail aur Qanbar pe kya munhasir, dar tera chhor kar koi jata nahin
Jo ghulami ka teri maza pa gaya, ras jis ko teri naukri aa gayi
"Jis ka Maula tha main us ka Maula Ali" jab Nabi ne kaha din kamil hua
Marhaba Mustafa, Rab bhi razi hua, wo jo aana thi ayat wohi aa gayi
Teri masnad pe ghairon ko bithla diya, Naib-e-Mustafa, mazhar-e-kibriya
Tu Wasi-o-Wali-o-Ali hi raha, manzilat mein teri kya kami aa gayi
Maslak-e-ishq mein mangna aib hai, main bhi khamosh hun wo bhi khamosh hain
Un ko sab hai khabar is liye chup hun main, kya hua ankh mein gar nami aa gayi
Khums dena mawaddat ki ek shart hai, aur na dena bhi misl-e-Fadak hai gunah
Haq-e-Sadat-o-Zahra ke maqruz se, jab taqaza hua to hansi aa gayi
Aag le kar koi tere dar par gaya, resman le ke koi tere ghar gaya
Mere Maula tere karb-e-tanhai ka jab khayal aaya jigar chir gayi
Ishq jab tak na ho sher hota nahin, mujh mein kab ahliyat thi teri madh ki
Mere Maula main qurban tere ishq ke, Sibt Jafar ko bhi shairi aa gayi

Subh-e-Ashur Akbar se Shah ne kaha, ay Shabih-e-Nabi mere nur-e-nazar
Jo basar hui aakhri raat thi, ab azan-e-do-sahar aakhri aa gayi
Apne honton pe sukhi zaban pher kar, meri jaan tum bhi itmam-e-hujjat karo
Keh rahe the jab Asghar se Shah-e-din, ek mazalim mein fauj tishna aa gayi
Pyas se main to mar jata kuchh der mein, Hurmala tu ne na-haq ye bedad ki
Hurmala ki himaqat pe ye soch kar, nanhe Asghar ko ran mein hansi aa gayi
Mard koi na baqi bacha asr tak, sirf Sajjad the, wo bhi behosh the
Aur tha kaun nusrat ko Shabbir ki, be-kasi aa gayi, be-kasi aa gayi
Karbala aur Kufe ki bhi wahshaten kam na thin yun to Ahl-e-Haram ke liye
Ashk roke ruke phir na Sajjad ke yaad jab bhi kabhi Sham ki aa gayi
Karbala aur Kufe to ho aaye the log dushman, hain kyun ajnabi shahr ke
Khaye patthar to Zainab lagin puchhne itna fitna jagah kaun si aa gayi
