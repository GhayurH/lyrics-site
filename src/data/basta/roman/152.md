Watan se chhut ke koi khasta-jan shahid na ho

1
Watan se chhut ke koi khasta-tan jaan Shahid na ho
koi musafir-e be khanamaan shahid na ho
Kisi ke ghar pe koi mehman shahid na ho
Shahid ho to wo tishna-dahan shahid na ho

Safar mein maut ka aana bari musibat hai
Jo lash dafn na ho aur bhi qiyamat hai

2
Hua ye zulm zamane mein do gharibon par
Wo ek bekas o muztar hai Fatima ka pisar
Aur Ek Muslim-e be-par gharib o khasta-tan jigar
Koi na un ka muin tha na aap ka yawar

qalaq ye tha ke Shah-e marshaqin bhi bichhre
Paraye des mein do nur-e ein bhi bichhre

3
Munim-e khana hani the Muslim-e par gham
Unhein bhi qatl kya zalimon ne haye sitam
Ye inqilab-e-Iran ye rang-e do rang-e-dui-e alam
Aman ki fikr mein dar dar phire Safir-e-Haram

Barhi jo pyas bahut tharthara ke Baith gaye
Qarib-e shab dar-e tauha pe aa ke Baith gaye
