7
Ye sab to mere sath hain afat ke safar mein
Par Fatima Sughra ko main chhor aaya hun ghar mein
Hai us ki judai se ajab dard jigar mein
Dar hai kahin mar jaye na wo hijr-e-pidar mein

Roye to mere sar ki qasam dijiyo bhai
Bimar ko sine se laga lijiyo bhai

8
Sughra ka suna naam to Bano ye pukari
Keh dijiyo madar tumhein bhuli nahin pyari
Bahnon ko bhi hai ath pahar yaad tumhari
Asghar meri godi mein kiya karta hai zari

Raton ko tere gham mein nahin soti hai Kubra
Jab zikr tera hota hai tab roti hai Kubra

Watan se chhut ke koi khasta-jan shahid na ho

1
Watan se chhut ke koi khasta-jan shahid na ho
Koi musafir-e-be-khanaman shahid na ho
Kisi ke ghar pe koi mehman shahid na ho
Shahid ho to wo tishna-dahan shahid na ho

Safar mein maut ka aana bari musibat hai
Jo lash dafn na ho aur bhi qiyamat hai

2
Hua ye zulm zamane mein do gharibon par
Wo ek be-kas-o-muztar hai Fatima ka pisar
Aur ek Muslim-e-be-par gharib-o-khasta-jigar
Koi na un ka muin tha na aap ka yawar

Qalaq ye tha ke Shah-e-Mashriqain bhi bichhre
Paraye des mein do nur-e-ain bhi bichhre

3
Muqim-e-khana-e-Hani the Muslim-e-pur-gham
Unhein bhi qatl kiya zalimon ne haye sitam
Ye inqilab ye rang-e-do-rangi-e-alam
Aman ki fikr mein dar dar phire Safir-e-Haram

Barhi jo pyas bahut tharthara ke baith gaye
Qarib-e-shab dar-e-Taua pe aa ke baith gaye
