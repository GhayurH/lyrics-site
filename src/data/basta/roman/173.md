[Pahunchi pisar ki lash pe jab Shah-e-bahr-o-bar — jari]

4
Khaime mein Yan tarapti thi wo Sokhta jigar
Sab bibiyan bhi mahv-e-aza-e buka thin barahna sar
Niklein jo be-kasi hawas idhar-e se wo nauha Gar
Aaye idhar se lash liye Shah-e Bahr o bar

Dekha lahu rawan jo tan-e-pash pash se
Syedaniyan lipat gayin Akbar ki lash se

[makhm: pahunchi pisar ki lash pe jab Shah Bahr o bar]

[Makhaz: Pahunchi pisar ki lash pe jab Shah-e-bahr-o-bar]

Chhutna jo Shah se piri mein naujawan farzand
Chhuta jo Shah se piri mein najwanon farzand
Husain o khush qad o khush ro khush bayan farzand
Saidi o Abid o zezi jah-e-Muhammad o rutba dan farzand
Pidar ke tan ki tawan sare ghar ki jaan farzand

Bahar jis ki na dekhi khizan wo bagh hua
Qayamat aa gayi ghar Shah ka be-kasi chiragh hua

2
Badan se jaan chali jism thar tharane laga
Pasina aa gaya ji Shah ka sansane laga
Pisar jahan se chala tan se zor jane laga
Chale jo ran ko to har gam par ghash aane laga

Kabhi uthe to kabhi kar ke aah Baith gaye
Jigar mein dard ye utha ke Shah Baith gaye

Kabhi uthe to bhi gir ke aah baith gaye
Utha jo dard pukare ke haye haye pisar
Hazar haif jiyen hum thas jahan se jaye pisar
Ye kya ke hum talak aati nahin sadaye pisar
Pidar ki Zist kahan jab nazar na aaye pisar

Pisar ke dagh ne khanjar gale pe Phera hai
Chiragh jis mein na hue wo ghar andhera hai
