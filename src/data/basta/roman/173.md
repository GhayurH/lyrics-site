[Pahunche pisar ki lash pe jab Shah-e-bahr-o-bar — jari]

4
Khaime mein yan tarapti thi wo sokhta-jigar
Sab bibiyan bhi mahw-e-buka thin barahna-sar
Niklin jo be-hawas idhar se wo nauhagar
Aaye udhar se lash liye Shah-e-bahr-o-bar

Dekha lahu rawan jo tan-e-pash-pash se
Syedaniyan lipat gayin Akbar ki lash se

[Makhaz: Pahunche pisar ki lash pe jab Shah-e-bahr-o-bar]

Chhutna jo Shah se piri mein naujawan farzand

1

---***---

Chhuta jo Shah se piri mein naujawan farzand
Husain-o-khush-qad-o-khush-ru khush-bayan farzand
Saeed-o-Abid-o-zi-jah-o-rutba-dan farzand
Pidar ke tan ki tawan sare ghar ki jaan farzand

Bahar jis ki na dekhi khizan wo bagh hua
Qiyamat aa gayi ghar Shah ka be-chiragh hua

2
Badan se jaan chali jism thartharane laga
Pina aa gaya ji Shah ka sansanane laga
Pisar jahan se chala tan se zor jane laga
Chale jo ran ko to har gam par ghash aane laga

Kabhi uthe to kabhi kar ke aah baith gaye
Jigar mein dard ye utha ke Shah baith gaye

3
Thama jo dard pukare ke haye haye pisar
Hazar haif jiyen hum, jahan se jaye pisar
Ye kya ke hum talak aati nahin sada-e-pisar
Pidar ki zist kahan jab nazar na aaye pisar

Pisar ke dagh ne khanjar gale pe phera hai
Chiragh jis mein na hue wo ghar andhera hai
