Jab Haram qila-e-Shirin ke barabar aaye

1
Jab Haram qila-e-Shirin ke barabar aaye
Ghul hua ke Shah-e-Maula ma-lashkar aaye
Kaha Shirin ne ke asbab-e-dilbar aaye
Mere Maula, mere Sultan, mere Sarwar aaye

Nur-e-Haq, shan-e-Khuda, qudrat-e-Bari dekho
Jao logo, mere Aqa ki sawari dekho

2
Jin se roshan hai Madina, wo qamar aate hain
Jin ka maidan-e-Najaf mein wo ghar aate hain
Jis ke ghar Arsh pe hain, wo mere ghar aate hain
Ye khabar us ko na thi nezon pe sar aate hain

Keh rahi thi ke chiragh-e-Haram aata hai
Ay Musalmano, mubarak ho Husain aata hai

3
Tha khayal us ko jo chaugird to yawar honge
Bich mein lashkar-e-Islam ke Sarwar honge
Ghoron par naqa-e-Zainab ke barabar honge
Parda-e-mahmal sambhale Ali Akbar honge

Wan na mahmal thi na hashmat thi, na zebai thi
Sar-e-Shabbir ke hamrah bahen aai thi

4
Nagahan rah mein hua shor-e-matam
Sun ke munh faq hua dil hil gaye tharraye qadam
Dekhne ko jo gaye the hasham-e-Shah-e-Umam
Wo zan-o-mard phire khak urate baham

Sakht be-chain hui talib-e-aram-e-Husain
Dil par angusht-e-shahadat pe likha naam-e-Husain

5
Ek aurat ne ye bahar se nida di nagah
Haye Shirin tere arman mile khak mein aah
Ghar ka ghar ho gaya Khatun-e-Qiyamat ka tabah
Waris-e-Aal-e-Nabi mar gaya, Inna lillah

Hum ziyarat ko gaye the ke ye mahshar dekha
Haif sad haif ke Zainab ko khule sar dekha

6
Be-tahasha wo kehti hui dauri bahar
Khak munh mein tere kis munh se ye deti hai khabar
Kaun Zainab jise aai hai tu nange sar
Wo pukari ke Husain Ibn-e-Ali ki khwahar

Ek faqat main hi nahin dekh ke sab aaye hain
Risman bandh ke shanon mein adu laye hain
