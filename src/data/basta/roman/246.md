4
Bola hakim ke nihayat hai tujhe bap ki chah
Takht ke niche ye kya tasht mein hai, kar to nigah
Pas ja kar jo dekhne lagi ba-nala-o-aah
Khun mein duba use nazar aaya sar-e-Shah

Tasht par gir ke pukari ye hal aap ka hai
Lo phuphi jaan yahi sar to mere bap ka hai

5
God mein le ke sar-e-Ibn-e-Ali chillai
In khuli ankhon ke qurban tumhari jai
Aise bhule ke na beti kabhi tumhein yaad aai
Aati muddat mein ye kya shakl mujhe dikhlai

Kya khushi hai, lab-e-laal to kholo Baba
Kya ghazab ho gaya, kuchh munh se to bolo Baba

6
Tir is chand se mathe pe lagaya kis ne
Halq par khanjar-e-bedad phiraya kis ne
Khun is gardan-e-nazuk ka bahaya kis ne
Chhole se rasan mein mujhe tum se chhuraya kis ne

Amman sar pitti hain, kya unhein samjhaun main
Aao kurte mein chupa kar tumhein le jaaun main

Ghul hai darbar mein namus-e-Payambar aaye

1
Ghul hai darbar mein namus-e-Payambar aaye
Faqa-kash tishna-dahan be-kas-o-be-dar aaye
Ahl-e-Bait-e-Nabawi khole hue sar aaye
Samne hakim-e-be-din ke wo muztar aaye

Kat kar Hazrat-e-Shabbir ka sar laye hain
Abhi darbar mein namus-e-Husain aaye hain

2
Hind ghabra ke pukari ke are kaun Husain
Bola koi ke wohi Fatima ka nur-e-ain
Ali-o-Zainab hain wo sar pitte hain jo ba-bain
Aaj kya qabr mein honge Muhammad be-chain

Khana-e-Syed-e-Laulak mein koi na raha
Haye ab bichhan-e-Batul-e-Pak mein koi na raha

3
Hind ye sun ke khari ho ke lagi pitne sar
Bal bikhra diye aur phenki zamin par chadar
Aur ye chillati hui nikli mahal se bahar
Jald batlao ke shahzadiyan soti hain kidhar

Nange sar jaungi hai mera Aqa na raha
Kis ka parda ke Nabi-zadi ka parda na raha
