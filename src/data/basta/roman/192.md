Gahware se Husain jo Asghar ko le chale

1
Gahware se Husain jo Asghar ko le chale
Hathon pe rakh ke fidwa-e-Dawar ko le chale
Bahon mein Sham ke mah-e-anwar ko le chale
[Ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]

Farigh abhi nahin hui Akbar ke dagh se
Kuchh roshni hai ghar mein mere is chiragh se

2
Bazu ke is bayan se ghabraye Shah-e-din
Aakhir qarib fauj ke aaye Shah-e-din
Bachche ko rakh ke hathon pe chillaye Shah-e-din
Yaro samajh lo jo samjhaye Shah-e-din

Mujh ko to khayal chahiye kis ki jaan ka
Talwar kya qasur hai is be-zaban ka
