[Reti par barchhi khaye para tha jo nur-e-ain — jari]

7
Bazu chhida Husain ka, be-shir ka gala
Ek aah bhar ke reh gaye Mazlum-e-Karbala
Aqa tumhare pyas bujhane ko jate hain
Bachchon pe to jahan mein sab hi rahm khate hain

Kyun kar kahun ke un pe sitam hath uthayenge
Masum jaan kar bhi na pani pilayenge

Gahware se Husain jo Asghar ko le chale

1
Gahware se Husain jo Asghar ko le chale
Hathon pe rakh ke fidya-e-Dawar ko le chale
Badal mein Sham ke mah-e-anwar ko le chale
Chillai man kahan mere dilbar ko le chale

Farigh abhi nahin hui Akbar ke dagh se
Kuchh roshni hai ghar mein mere is chiragh se

2
Bano ke is bayan se ghabraye Shah-e-din
Aakhir qarib-e-fauj ke le aaye Shah-e-din
Bachche ko hath par pe chillaye Shah-e-din
Yaro samajh lo jo tumhein samjhaye Shah-e-din

Kuchh to khayal chahiye nanhi si jaan ka
Batao kya qasur hai is be-zaban ka

3
Ye sun ke Hurmala ne uthaya kaman ko
Tanka khata-shiar ne nanhi si jaan ko
Mara khadang-e-Shah ke abru-kaman ko
Hai hai mitaya Bano ke nam-o-nishan ko

Chillai maut haye na tujh ko aman di
Bachche ne saham ke hathon pe jaan di
