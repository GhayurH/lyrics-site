Tanha jo ran mein reh gaya Zahra ka gul-badan

1
Tanha jo ran mein reh gaya Zahra ka gul-badan
Ek huk dil mein uthi jo yaad aa gaya watan
sine mein gayi sar ko jhukaye sher-e Shah-e-Zaman
Zainab se bole lao hamara wo pairahan

Saughat Amman jaan ki pehnna chahiye hamein
Hum marne ja rahe hain kafan chahiye hamein

2
Allah! kitne zulm hue Batul par
Pehna kafan samajh ke use Shah ne ek bar
Zainab se bole chadar-o-bazu pe kisi pyar
Tharra raha hai karb se tarikh ka jigar

Ab hum hain aur khanjar-e-qatil ki dhar hai
Zainab mere Payam ki to zamza dar hai

3
Bhar is taraf to bhai bahen mein ye guftagu
Karta tha ghusl-e-bandi idhar lashkar-e-adu
Rukhsat talab idhar the Shah-e-sokhta-gulu
Rote tarapte Guzri hai Bibi Bibi ki Umr

Sine pe sone wali na roye hamare baad
Pahlu mein apni man ke wo soye hamare baad

4
Is guftagu ko bali Sakina ne jab suna
Mahshar har Ek shab thi qayamat har ek sahar
nanhe se hath Jor ke Boli wo mah-e mah-liqa
Shahzadi kaise zakhmon ki tere dawa karun

Mil jaye ka falak mein abhi itna rone ki
Ansu baha rahe hain ghulam aur kya karun

Zakhmi hue jo Haider-e-Safdar namaz mein

1
Zakhmi hue jo Haider-e Safdar namaz mein
Shamshir-e zulm chal gayi sar par namaz mein
Gulgun hui jabin-e-munawwar namaz mein
Sar ta qadam lahu se hue tar namaz mein

Sadma hua ye sun ke saghir o kabir ko
Zakhmi kya namaz mein gul ke Amir ko

2
Lo momino Imamam-e ki rehlat qarib hai
Hangam-e-hashr waqt-e-qayamat qarib hai
Aqa ki hum ghulamon se rukhsat qarib hai
Bagh-e jahan ba Jannat-e ulfat qarib hai

Sadme se jin-e o ansi-e o mulk jaan khuniyon hue
Aqa hamare mar ye keh ke roin hue

Asir Faizabadi
Asir-e-biwi fikr-e-Mustafa hai yahi
Chiragh-e-ilm jalate raho Ali ki tarah

Majlis-e-gham mein khari rahi hain Zahra
Kaun aata hai mere lal ko rone ke liye
