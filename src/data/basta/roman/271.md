Yasrib se Karbala ke musafir qarib hain

1
Yasrib se Karbala ke musafir qarib hain
Lut kar phire watan ko ajab gham nasib hain
Is-e hal se Habib-e Khuda ke Habib hain
Be-kasi kis hain sogwar Imamam-e gharib hain

Hazrat ke baad chain ki shaklein bigar gayin
Shahzadiyan ghazab ki tabahi mein par gayin

2
Sab shahr mein ajab talatum hai ja baja
Sughra ko khud-e gham se nahin Hosh-e dast-e-sitam o pa
Leti hui hai sahn mein munh par liye rida
Sine mein dil dharakne laga jab chali hawa

Kehti hai khalq se isi-e hasrat mein jayen hue
Kyun dil kabhi hamare musafir bhi aayen hue

3
Uthi ye keh ke Ashiq-e Sultan-e Namdar
Lekin qadam qadam pe giri wo nahifi o zar
Be-kasi ikhtiyar hans ke ye chillai Ek bar
Lo Nani mein to jati hun tum ghar se ho hoshiyar

[Ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Lo tum pe fida mere Baba Husain aaye

4
Sughra idhar-e se pit-ti pahunchi ba Chashm-e tar
Umm-ul-Banin bhi hathon se thame hue jigar
Puchha kidhar hain khwahar-e Sultan-e Bahr o bar
Fizza ne barh ke Hazrat-e Zainab ko di khabar

Zauja-e Rasullai-e Pak ki tashrif lai hain
Am-e Umm-ul-Banin bhi aai hain Sughra bhi aai hain

5
Matam ki saf ke pas jo pahunchi wo nek-naam
Rone lagin pukar ke Syedaniyan tamam
Kulsum barh ke paye tazim chand gam
Uthin ek aah khinch ke Zainab paye salam

Girne lagin to bazwaye Fizza pakar liya
Sughra ko dekhte hi kaleja pakar liya

6
Sughra ke munh ko dekh ke rote the sab ke sab
Bimar le ke naam-e pidar pit-ti thi jab
Kehti thi mil-e ke hath ghazab hua ghazab
Beti bahut udas hai Baba bulao ab

Riqqat hua na zabt kanizon se hat gayin
Sughra use uth ke Hazrat-e Zainab se lipat gayin

(Bashukriya sozkhwan Huzur Baradaran o Zuhur Baradaran)
