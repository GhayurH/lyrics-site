Ran mein Zainab ki jo aghosh ke pale aaye

1
Ran mein Zainab ki jo aghosh ke pale aaye
Donon kis shan se hathyar sambhale aaye
Ghore chakate hue bhale nikale aaye
Qatl karne ko sawaron ke risale aaye

Ja pare sher mukhtik hui talwaron mein
Ghir gaye donon jari lakh sitamgaron mein

2
Kahan do Tifl-e saghir aur kahan lakh adu
Lare is-e darja ke shal ho gaye donon bazu
Barchhiyan marin lainon ne jo pa kar qabu
Dagmagane lage rahwaron pe wo tishna-gulu

Misl-e gul dub gaye khun mein jame un ke
Munh pe sehra hue kat kat ke Amama un ke

3
Zof se har ne jhuk jhuk ke jo sambhale kai bar
Ghul machane lage maidan mein tab zulm-shiar
Lo mubarak ho ke mare gaye ye sina-figar
Garte hain Ghoron se Hazrat ki bahen ke dildar

Tukre tukre hain badan zakhm bahut khaye hain
Paon donon rikabon se nikal aaye hain

4
Di ye Hazrat ne sada tham ke hathon se jigar
Char ankhen meri tum se nahin hoti khwahar
Ahl-e kin mein ye Ghul karte hain khush ho ho kar
Tukre se hue Zainab ke bikis ke pisar

Nahin malum wo narghe mein kahan hain khwahar
Bhanje ankhon se mamon ki nihan hain khwahar

5
Abhi Zainab se ye kehte the Shah-e-karb-o-bala
Aur khari hathon se sar pit-ti thi wo Dukhiya
Itne mein bhanjon ki aai ye Hazrat ko sada
Lo ghulamon ki khabar Ay khalf-e sher-e Khuda

Tan se hum donon ke sar katne ki tayyari hai
Ay madadgar-e jahan, waqt-e maddaridi hai

6
Ye sada sunte hi daure gaye ran mein Sher-e-din
Na falak sar jhukta tha aur na ankhon se zamin
Biyaban se tegh jo baithi aage se lain
Khun mein ghaltan nazar aaye unhein wo mah-jabin

Khak par Gar ke ba-sad dard pukare Shabbir
Haye Ay bhanjujon qurban tumhare Shabbir
