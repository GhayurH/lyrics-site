[Jab zanu-e-Husain pe Abbas mar gaye — jari]

4
Samjhe ye sab ke bazu-e-Abbas kat gaye
Syedaniyon ke tan se lahu aur ghat gaye
Bachchon ke nanhe nanhe jigar gham se phat gaye
Rang ur gaye alam se kaleje ulat gaye

Har dil pe barq-e-ranj-o-yas gir pari
Khaime ke dar par zauja-e-Abbas gir pari

5
Zainab se ro ke kehne lage Sarwar-e-Zaman
Le kar nishan ko jaye kahan ab ye be-watan
Ab to na fauj hai na Alamdar-e-saf-shikan
Ghar lut gaya, alam ko barhao ab ay bahen

Lo ye nishani-e-Sher-e-Zuljalal sambhalo
Patka alam ka khol lo panja utar lo

6
Chhota ye Sheh se kehta tha ansu baha baha
Baba hamare ghar mein kab aayenge ay chacha
Aaya alam, phir un ke na aane ki wajah kya
Chhote se ro ke sab baron ne bhai ne kaha

Aqa ki mang aaj bhi sadme guzar gaye
Beta tumhein khabar nahin, Baba to mar gaye

Shabbir jabke ran se chale su-e-khaima-gah

1
Shabbir jabke ran se chale su-e-khaima-gah
Mashk-o-alam ko jhukaye hue ba-ashk-o-aah
Dil tham kar kate hue bazu pe nigah
Sar ta qadam Husain hain ek mustaqil karah

Le kar tabarrukat-e-Alamdar-e-Karbala
Su-e-khiyam jata hai Salar-e-Karbala

2
Girte sambhalte aa hi gaya khaima-e-Haram
Wo ruk gaye Husain ke barhte hue qadam
Zainab khari hai khaime ke dar par ba-chashm-e-nam
Shabbir keh rahe hain bahen lut aaye hum

Zainab hamara bhai hum se ho gaya
Sher-e-Khuda ka sher tharai mein so gaya

3
Sar pit ke ye kehti thi Zainab-e-jigar-figar
Abbas, mere sher kahan ho bahen nisar
Tum ho hifazat-e-Haram-e-Shah ke zimmedar
Bhaiya main kitni der se karti hun intizar

Bachche ye keh rahe hain unhein jam-e-ab dun
Bhaiya tum hi batao unhein kya jawab dun
