Soz

Kam aaye rufaqa Shah ke jab maidan mein
Aur akela raha wo mah-e-Arab maidan mein
Aa ke majraj kya Abbas ne Tab maidan mein
Ro ke ki arz ke hum jate hain ab maidan mein

Shah ne Farmaya ke tanha to na chhore jao
Bhai Abbas kamar meri na tore jao

Ay azizo kya bayan ho majaraje Ahl-e-Bait
Jab raha waris na koi sar pe haye Ahl-e-Bait
Aur laga Lutne wahan ismat Aur Ahl-e-Bait
Kya kahun is waqt ki mein haye haye Ahl-e-Bait

Pit kar ro ro ke kehte the yahi Ashur o shain
Hum ko mat luto laino thas hum hain namus-e Husain
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Sibt Nabi Naqvi o Rizwanat Naqvi)

Karwan-e salar ban jab karwan-e Karbala
Khak sar pe dalti is dasht-e Viran se chala
Rah mein un be-kason se jab koi tha puchhta
Kis ke tum namus ho aur kis ke ho Sahib aza

Kale kare wo dikha kar kehte the bashim-e ashk o aah
Is-e Hussaini qafile ka margaya hai badshah

Qarib-e Kufa jo randon ka qafila aaya
Khule saron ke tamashe ko sab jahan aaya
Imam har do sira misl-e misl-e-sarban aaya
Zaban-e hal se karta yahi bayan aaya

Na hai taam mein wo zaiqa na pani mein
Maza mila hai jo randon ki sarbani mein
(Bashukriya soz o salamkhwan Rizwan Ali Kazmi / Islamabad)

Taslim Amrohvi

Reti pe Barchhi khaye para tha jo nur-e ein
Baithe the dil ko pakre hue Shah-e-mashriqain
Nagah ek kaniz pukari ba-shor o shain
Dam tarat hai pyas se be-kasi sher Husain

Lillah-e jald khaime mein tashrif laiye
Banu ka lal ruth gaya hai manaiye

Abbas ne farmaya ke ghabrao na jani
Mili mili ke pilane ke liye late hain pani
Rukne ke nahin lakh hun Gar zulm ke bani
Kya dil se bhula denge teri tishna-dahani

Be-kasi mashk bhare nehr se aayen to qasam lo
Darya se hum aage kahin jayen to qasam lo
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed Hashmat Ali Zaidi)
