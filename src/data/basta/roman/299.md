Kafan pehna ke jo Shabbar ko le chale Shabbir
Nasim Lakhnavi

Han dosto ye waqt hai andoh-o-mihan ka

1
Han dosto ye waqt hai andoh-o-mihan ka
Matam hai jigar band-e Shah-e Qali shakn ka
Zahra ne kya chak-e gareban kafan ka
Sar khol do ab uthta hai tabut Hasan ka

Haider bhi buka karte hain Zahra o Nabi bhi
Abbas bhi rote hain Husain Ibn-e Ali bhi

2
Hamshir ne sar pit ke ye shor machaya
Mein kya karun logon mujhe qismat ne sataya
Be-kasi Be-rahmon ne Bhaiya ko mere zahr pilaya
Kis tarah bachche ga Asad-e Allah ka jaya

Bas ho gaya malum ke saman hain safar ke
Dekho to lagn bhar gaya tukron se jigar ke

3
Shabbir tarapne lage dastar patak kar
Nazdik tha dam tan se nikal jaye pharka kar
Bahnein jo baradar ke liye roin balak kar
Nale the ke sinon pe giri Barq kar kar

Zainab ka ye nauha tha ke ye kya hua logo
Mit jaya mera bikis o tanha hua logo
