Kafan pehna ke jo Shabbar ko le chale Shabbir
Nasim Lakhnavi

Han dosto ye waqt hai andoh-o-mihan ka

1
Han dosto ye waqt hai andoh-o-mihan ka
Matam hai magar band Shah-e-Qila-shikan ka
Zahra ne kiya chak gareban, Hasan ka
Sar khol do ab uthta hai tabut-e-Hasan ka

Haider bhi buka karte hain, Zahra-o-Nabi bhi
Abbas bhi rote hain, Husain ibn-e-Ali bhi

2
Shabbir ne sar pit ke ye shor machaya
Main kya karun logo, mujhe qismat ne sataya
Be-rahmon ne bhaiya ko mere zahr pilaya
Kis tarah bachega Asadullah ka jaya

Bas ho gaya malum ke saman hain safar ke
Dekho to kafan bhar gaya tukron se jigar ke

3
Shabbir tarapne lage dastar phenk kar
Nazdik tha zamin se nikal jaye bhar kar
Bahnein jo baradar ke liye roin palat kar
Nale the ke sinon pe giri barq tarap kar

Zainab ka ye nauha tha ke ye kya hua logo
Man-jaya mera nek-o-tanha hua logo
