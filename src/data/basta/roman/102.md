Josh Malihabadi

Ashna bahr-e-sadaqat ka Husain Ibn-e-Ali
Madrasa-e-dars-e-shahadat ka Husain Ibn-e-Ali
Mojiza-e-fikri najabat ka Husain Ibn-e-Ali
Hausla teri nabuwwat ka Husain Ibn-e-Ali

Jis ne bujhne di na shama-e-adamiyat, wo Husain
Sans jis ke dam se leti hai mashiyat, wo Husain
(Bashukriya qari o sozkhwan Raza Maulai o Muhammad Ali Maulai)

---***---

Jab chaman khak mein Akbar ki jawani ka mila
Pani pani kaha aur qatra na pani ka mila
Dagh Syedaniyon ko Ahmad-e-Sani ka mila
Shah ko kuchh na pata Bano ke jani ka mila

Ro ro farmate the taqat hai na binai hai
Beta mara gaya aur alam-e-tanhai hai
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Zafar Husain Zaidi o Sibt Muhammad Rizvi)

---***---

Ya Rab saghir sin mein koi be-pidar na ho
Baba kisi ka qatl huzur-e-nazar na ho
Pardes mein yatim koi dar-badar na ho
Balwe mein koi parda-nashin nange sar na ho

Qaidi hui pidar se chhuti bhai mar gaye
Ye sare dukh Sakina ke upar guzar gaye
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Iqlim Akhtar Zaidi o Iwaz Ali)

---***---

Abbas ko Husain jo darya pe ro chuke
Bhai se apne hath lab-e-nahr dho chuke
Yan tak ke qatl, Akbar-o-Asghar bhi ho chuke
Godi mein jo pake the wo mitti mein so chuke

Girte the Shah har ek tan-e-pash-pash par
Qasim ki lash par kabhi Akbar ki lash par
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Hasan Kazmi o baradaran)

---***---

Rula rahi hai dilon ko luti hui Sarkar
Na paidalon ke pare hain na markabon ki qatar
Ujar gaya wo chaman aur khizan hui wo bahar
Na koi hajib-o-darban na koi khidmatgar

Maqam-e-hu, ka hai jis ja nigah murti hai
Huzur ke dar-e-daulat pe khak urti hai
(Anjumani Bab-ul-Ilm / prashad peshtar)

---***---

Be-chain thi Sughra jo firaq-e-pidari se
Uth uth ke tahalti thi nasim-e-sahari se
Ay bad-e-saba marti hun dard-e-jigari se
Mil jayen to kahiyo mere baba safari se

Nargis ki tarah chashm su-e-dar nigran hai
Jald aao ke hasti ka chaman sarf-e-khizan hai
