Soz

Josh Malihabadi

Ashna Bahr-e sadaqat ka Husain Ibn-e Ali
Musdaras darsgah-e-aam-e shahadat ka Husain Ibn-e Ali
Mojiza fukri najabat ka Husain Ibn-e Ali
Hausla teri kar-e-nabuwwat ka Husain Ibn-e Ali

Jis ne bajhne di na shama-e Waqar-e-Adamiyat thas wo Husain
Sans jis ke dam se leti hai mashiyyat thas wo Husain
(Bashukriya Qari o sozkhwan Raza Maulai o Muhammad Ali Maulai)

Jab chaman khak mein Akbar ki jawani ka mila
Sans jis ke dam se leti hai bashariyat, wo Husain
Dagh Syedaniyon ko Ahmad-e sani ka mila
Shah ko kuchh na pata Banu ke jani ka mila

Ro ro farmate the taqat hai na binai hai
Beta mara gaya aur alam-e tanhai hai
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Zafar Husain Zaidi o Sibt Muhammad Rizvi)

Rab saghir sun-e mein koi be-kasi pidar-e na ho
Baba kisi ka qatl Huzur-e nazar na ho
Pardes mein yatim koi dar badar na ho
Balwe mein koi parda nashin nange sar na ho

Qaidi hui pidar-e se chhuti bhai mar gaye
Ye sare dukh Sakina ke upar guzar gaye
Pardes mein yatim koi dar badar na ho

Abbas ko Husain jo darya pe ro chuke
Bhai se apne hath lab-e nehr dho chuke
Yan tak ke qatl thas Akbar o Asghar bhi ho chuke
Godi mein jo pake the wo mitti mein so chuke

Garte the Shah har Ek tan-e pash pash par
Qasim ki lash par kabhi Akbar ki lash par
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Hasan Kazmi o Baradaran)

Rula rahi hai dilon-e ko luti hui sarkar
Na pidalon ke par hain na marukbon ki qatar
Ujar gaya wo chaman aur khizan hui wo bahar
Na koi hajib o darban na koi khidmat gar

Maqam ho thas ka hai jis ja nigah martai hai
Huzur ke dar-e daulat pe khak urti hai
(Anjumani Bab-ul-Ilm / prashad peshtar)

Be-kasi chain thi Sughra jo Faraqaq-e be-pidari se
Uthe uth ke tahlti thi Nasim-e saharis se
Ay bad-e-saba-e Saba marti hun dard-e dard-e-jigari se
Mil-e jayen to kahiyo mere Baba safari se

Nargis ki tarah Chashm-e suye dar digarman hai
Jald aao ke hasti ka chaman sirf-e khizan hai
