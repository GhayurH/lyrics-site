4
God phaila ke kabhi kehti hai dilbar ja
ruh-e be chain hai ja Ali Asghar ja
Dil tarapta hai mera god ke andar ja
Fatima ke liye ja, paye Haider ja

Aa mein sadqe gayi wari gayi qurban gayi

5
Khalq sab soti hai raton ko gharon mein apne
Hum agar lete hon, karwat to gham ko hum se
Nind aati mujhe, pahlu mein agar tum hote
tum to Ay lakht-e jigar gor-e mein Marqad ke gaye

Yaad is palne wali ki bhulai tum ne

Imam-e-Pak ko jab ashqiya ne gher liya

1
Imam-e Pak ko jab ashqiya ne gher liya
Musafiron ko sipah-e-jafa ne gher liya
Nabi ke lal ko fauj-e-dagha ne gher liya
Ali ke chand ko kali ghata ne gher liya

Sitam shuru hue sulh ka jawab mila
Ghazab hai saton tarikh se na aab mila

2
wo qahti-e aab, wo Sadat par Hujm-e alam
Nabi ki Aal mein wo shor-e Al-atash-e paiham
Wo fikr o yas wo andoh wo Imam ammam
Wo Shah ko Shauq-e ibadat wo zulm-e Fauj-e sitam

Mila na chain unhein tak Imam Sabir ko
Bas ek raat ki muhlat mili musafir ko

3
Aziz-o-yawar-o-nasir jo chand the hamrah
sabhon ko jama kya, Shah ne ba gham-e janka
[ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Janab-e-Akbar-o-Abbas-o-Qasim zi-jah

Suye Imamhi jo sab yar o yaar-o-aqraba aaye
[ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
