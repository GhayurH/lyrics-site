Karbala mein Shah-e-wala ke Haram lutte hain

1
Karbala mein Shah-e wala ke Haram lutte hain
Faqa kash tishna dahan kashta-e gham lutte hain
Dasht-e ghurbat mein giraftar-e sitam lutte hain
Shor barpa hai ye bibiyon-e mein ke hum lutte hain

Qatl-e-waris hue, saman-e-giraftari hai
Ya Ali aao ke waqt-e maddaridi hai

2
Hai ye faryad ke Ay baradar daudo
Koi chillati hai Abbashi-e dilawar daudo
Koi kehti hai tarap kar mere dilbar daudo
Man luti jati hai daudo Ali Akbar daudo

Dekho khun khawar adu Barchhiyan dikhlate hain
Tegh khincho ke lain ghar mein dhanse aate hain

3
Nanhe bachchon ka ye alam hai ke tharrate hain
God mein maon ki Dahshat se Chhupe jate hain
Nangi talwaren jo zalim unhein dikhlate hain
Bas to chalta nahin ashkon mein bhar late hain

Na to kar sakte hain faryad na ro sakte hain
Chupke Sahme hue ek ek ka munh takte hain

4
Lut chukin bibiyon ki jis waqt ridaen bhi tamam
Ghul hua phunko do ab bargah-e-Shah-e-Anam
Fauj-e aada se bhara hai Shah-e Mazlum ka ghar
Atish-e-zulm se jalne lage Hazrat ke khiyam

Shah ke namus-sara ek khule sar nikle
Hansliyan leta hai Asghar ki koi bani-e shar

5
Fauj-e-aada se bhara hai dasht-e-mazlum ka ghar
[Ek misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Khinchta hai koi kanon se Sakina ke gauhar
Lutta hai koi be rahm Haram ka zewar

Bande leta hai Ruqayya ke sitamgar koi
Chhinta hai sar-e Kulsum se chadar koi

Ashur Kazmi
Hairat ye hai ke mere qabile ke log bhi
Zulmat ko keh rahe hain "Ziya", Ya Ali madad
