Main nahin manta / Gauhar Jarchavi

Chand sikkon mein jo Shah ka gham bech de us azadar ko main nahin manta
Din ka duniya ke badle jo sauda kare us azadar ko main nahin manta

Koh-e-gham se jo apna rukh pher le, Ahl-e-Daulat ke dar par jo sajde kare
Mal-o-zar ko jo apna Khuda man le us azadar ko main nahin manta

Ghar gharibon ke majlis mein jaye nahin, zikr-e-Maula wahan par sunaye nahin
Jo zara kar nazar sau bahane kare us azadar ko main nahin manta

Ek gharib-ul-watan se milega bhi kya, Ahl-e-Daulat ko khush kar ke lun faida
Jo bulane pe majlis ke ye soch kare us azadar ko main nahin manta

Jis ki khushiyon ka mausam hai Mah-e-Aza, hanste honton se karta hai aah-o-buka
Ahl-e-girya se jo rakhe [lafz ghair wazeh] us azadar ko main nahin manta

Chashm-e-gham ko dikhave jo naz-o-ada, jeb bhar kar kahe haye Karb-o-bala
Jo dikhave ke ansu bahata rahe us azadar ko main nahin manta

Nauha-khwani ko jo git ka rang de, zikr-e-Maula ki qimat jo le kar ke le
Jo masaib parhe muskurate hue us azadar ko main nahin manta

Dosti mein ho jis ki shiar-e-adu, [baqi misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Jis ki rozi chalate hon ye raste us azadar ko main nahin manta

Karbala ke shahidon ka pani kare, [baqi misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Khud gham-e-Karbala jis se kehne lage us azadar ko main nahin manta

Kuchh samajhte nahin wo Zakir ka maqam, [baqi misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Jo ke ye soch kar sina-kobi kare us azadar ko main nahin manta

Aise tajir hain gar un ko mauqa mile, bech den ye to mashk-o-alam taziye
Aise saudagaron se jo sauda kare us azadar ko main nahin manta

Hosh kar hosh kar pasdar-e-aza, [baqi misra juzwi taur par ghair wazeh]
Jo ye sare sitam dekh kar chup rahe us azadar ko main nahin manta

Thi azadari kya aur kya ho gai, matam ki majlis bhi ek mashghala ho gai
Is tabahi ka sadma nahin hai jise us azadar ko main nahin manta
