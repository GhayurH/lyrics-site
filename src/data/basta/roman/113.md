Dil mein jis Muslim ke hubb-e-Saqi-e-Kausar nahin
Nasim Amrohvi

Dil mein jis Muslim ke hubb-e-Saqi-e-Kausar nahin
Phul hai khushbu nahin aina hai jauhar nahin
Man lun, kyun kar Nabi ko aur insanon ki misl
Jism hai saya nahin shakl-e-bashar hai zill nahin

Chhor kar Itrat ko le li agar Quran to kya
Bahr hai kashti nahin girdab hai langar nahin
Tab-e-nazara ho kya atwar se dar jaye na kyun
Ilm hai kamil nahin Musa hai wo Haider nahin

Be-kas-o-tanha khare hain dasht mein Sibt-e-Rasul
Chand hai tare nahin Sardar hai lashkar nahin
Dukhtaran-e-Fatima kyun kar chhupayen apne sar
Sharm hai miqna nahin bazar hai chadar nahin

Bazm mein maddah-e-Haider gar nahin ay Nasim
Tur hai jalwa nahin din hai Shah-e-Khawar nahin
(Bashukriya qari Hadis-e-Kisa o salam o nauhakhwan Zulimran)

Is se barh kar aur kya hoga hijab-e-Fatima
Nasim Amrohvi

Is se barh kar aur kya hoga hijab-e-Fatima
Ayah-e-Tathir hai adna niqab-e-Fatima
Zamin-e-nasl-e-Payambar hain yahi Bahrain-e-Pak
Malik-e-Kausar Ali, Kausar khitab-e-Fatima

Qudsiyon se Arsh par sun sun ke Zahra ki ata
Hal Ata bhi dhundta aaya hai bab-e-Fatima
Sabr, khamoshi, tahammul, zabt aur shukr-e-Khuda
In khasail mein hain bas Zainab jawab-e-Fatima

Taziyana bhi laraz jata hai jab karta hai yaad
Hazrat-e-Zainab ki piri aur shabab-e-Fatima
Khauf kya bhari hai gar isyan ka palla ay Nasim
Mere palle par hain mahshar mein Janab-e-Fatima
(Bashukriya shair o sozkhwan Sibt Hasan Zaidi Soz Nauha)

Kya musibat ho bayan be-sar-o-samanon ki
Ustad Sadiq Ali Khan Sadiq

Kya musibat ho bayan be-sar-o-samanon ki
Hum-nashin jin se rahi khak-e-bayabanon ki
Dekhi jatin nahin Abbas ne Zainab se kaha
Haye kumhlai hui suraten nadanon ki

Puchhti rehti thin din rat Sakina sab se
Chaukiyan baithi hain kyun dar pe nigahbanon ki
Sadiq afsos to ye hai ke Nabi ka beta
Zabh ho jaye hukumat mein Musalmanon ki
(Bashukriya nauhakhwan Zulfiqar Jafari o Ansar Jafari baradaran)
