Salam

Dil mein jis Muslim ke hubb-e-Saqi-e-Kausar nahin
Nasim Amrohvi

Dil mein jis Muslim ke habi-e saqi-e Kausar nahin
Phul hai khushubon nahin aina hai jauhar nahin
Man lo thas kyun kar Nabi ko aur insanon ki misl-e
Jism hai saya nahin shakl-e bashar hai zill-e nahin

Chhor kar Itrat ko le li agar Quran to kya
Bahr hai kashti nahin gardadar hai langar nahin
Tab-e nazzara ho kya sitam-izhar se dar jaye na kyun
Alam hai kamil nahin Musa hai wo Haider nahin

Chhor kar Itrat ko mangi mein agar Quran to kya
Chand hai Tare nahin Sardar hai lashkar nahin
Dukhtaran-e Fatima kyun kar chhupayenge apne sar
Sharm hai maqna nahin bazar hai chadar nahin

Bazm mein Maddah-e Haider Gar nahin Ay Nasim
Taur hai jalwa nahin din hai Shah-e shab-e-khawar nahin
(Bashukriya qari Hadis-e-Kisa o salam o nauhakhwan Zulimran)

Is se barh kar aur kya hoga hijab-e-Fatima
Nasim Amrohvi

Is-e se barh kar aur kya hoga hijab-e Fatima
Aiya Tathir hai adna niqab-e Fatima
Zamin-e nasl-e Payambar hain yahi bahrin-e Pak
Malik-e Kausar Ali thas Kausar khitab-e Fatima

Qadsisi se Arsh par sun sun ke Zahra ki ata
Hai ati bhi dhundta aaya hai bab-e Fatima
Sabr thas khamishi thas Fatima thas zabt aur shukr-e Khuda
Un-e khasaki mein hain bas Zainab jawab-e Fatima

Taziyana bhi larz jata hai jab karta hai yaad
Hazrat-e Zainab ki piri aur shabab-e Fatima
Khauf kya bhari hai Gar isyan ka pila Ay Nasim
Mere pale par hain mahshar mein Janab-e Fatima
(Bashukriya shair o sozkhwan Sibt Hasan Zaidi Soz Nauha)

Kya musibat ho bayan be-kasi saro samanon ki
Ustad Sadiq Ali Khan Sadiq

Kya musibat ho bayan be-kasi saro be-sar-o-samanon ki
Ustad Sadiq Ali Khan Sadiq
Dekhi jatin nahin Abbas ne Zainab se kaha
Haye kamhali hui saruten nadanon ki

Puchhti rehti thin din raat Sakina sab se
Hichkiyan baithi hain kyun dar pe nagahnabon ki
Sadiq Afsos to ye hai ke Nabi ka beta
Zabh ho jaye hukumat mein Musalmanon ki
(Bashukriya nauhakhwan Zulfiqar Jafari o Ansar Jafari baradaran)
