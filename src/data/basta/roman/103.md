Soz

Taslim Amrohvi

dulha bana jo bana Sarwar ka be zaban
Zainab balayen lene lagin ho ke nim jaan
Munh chum kar jo sab ne dua di ba-sad fughan
Asghar ka hath mathe pe rakh kar pukari man

Lo Bibi ab, ab apne kalejon ko tham lo
Rukhsat talab hai Asghar-e nadan salam lo
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Zafar Abbas o Intizam Husain)

Rehan Azmi

Asghar ki lash jab ke uthai Husain ne
Dekhi ba-gaur apni kamai Husain ne
Phir lash asman ko dikhai Husain ne
Taqrir ye zamin ko sunai Husain ne

Le Ay zamin meri amanat sambhal le
Mein punpatat hun ab tujhe Banu ka lal, le
(Bashukriya Shair o nauha khwan Muzaffar Haider Zaidi / pakrali)

Jab Zuhr tak Husain behtar ko ro chuke
Ek dopehar mein chhote se lashkar ko ro chuke
Qasim ko ro chuke Ali Akbar ko ro chuke
Turbat bana ke nanhi si Asghar ko ro chuke

Syedaniyon ne ro ke kaha shor o shain se
Lo ab zamana hota hai khali Husain se
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed Hasan Abbas Zaidi / achchhi)

Tha hukm ye Yazid ka pani bashar ne
Ghore piyaniye, sawar piyaniye aur shatar piyaniye
Jo tishna lab jahan ke hain wo be-khatar piyaniye
Yan tak ke sab charnd o pani aan kar piyen

Kafir bhi Gar piyen to na to Munh kijiyo
Par Fatima ke lal ko pani na dijiyo
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Wahid Husain Zaidi)

Kehti thi Banu Asghar-e jani kab tum ghar mein aao hue
Darya par se pei kar pani kab tum ghar mein aao hue
Ay mere jani Yusuf-e sani kab tum ghar mein aao hue
Apni dikhane shakl namani kab tum ghar mein aao hue

Sog mein tere beta mein ne pahnaihi kafnifi kali hai
Bhure balon wale ja, jhula tera khali hai
(Bashukriya Maulana Akhtar al-Husain Zain al-Din al-Husaini Muhammadi)

Jab Zulfiqar-e matam-e Asghar mein ro chuki
Nanhi si qabr khod ke khunbar ho chuki
Bachche ke sath Banu ki qismat ko so chuki
Karta lahu bhara hua ashkon se dho chuki

Pani na tha jo Shah chhirakte mazar par
Ansu tapak pare lab-e sher khawar par
(Bashukriya shair o sozkhwan Sardar Husain Zaidi / Canada, London)
