Soz

Taslim Amrohvi

Dulha bana jo Bano-e-Sarwar ka be-zaban
Zainab balayen lene lagin ho ke nim-jan
Munh chum kar jo sab ne dua di ba-sad-fughan
Asghar ka hath mathe pe rakh kar pukari man

Lo bibiyo, ab apne kalejon ko tham lo
Rukhsat talab hai Asghar-e-nadan salam lo
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Zafar Abbas o Intizam Husain)

Rehan Azmi

Asghar ki lash jab ke uthai Husain ne
Dekhi ba-ghaur apni kamai Husain ne
Phir lash asman ko dikhai Husain ne
Taqrir ye zamin ko sunai Husain ne

Le ay zamin meri amanat sambhal le
Main saunpta hun ab tujhe Bano ka lal, le
(Bashukriya shair o nauhakhwan Muzaffar Haider Zaidi / Pakrali)

Jab zuhr tak Husain bahattar ko ro chuke
Ek dopehar mein chhote se lashkar ko ro chuke
Qasim ko ro chuke Ali Akbar ko ro chuke
Turbat bana ke nanhi si Asghar ko ro chuke

Syedaniyon ne ro ke kaha shor-o-shain se
Lo ab zamana hota hai khali Husain se
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Syed Hasan Abbas Zaidi / Achhi)

Tha hukm ye Yazid ka pani bashar piyen
Ghore piyen, sawar piyen aur shutar piyen
Jo tishna-lab jahan ke hain wo be-khatar piyen
Yan tak ke sab charand-o-parand aan kar piyen

Kafir bhi gar piyen to na tu mana kijiyo
Par Fatima ke lal ko pani na dijiyo
(Bashukriya sozkhwan Wahid Husain Zaidi)

Kehti thi Bano Asghar-e-jani kab tum ghar mein aaoge
Darya par se pi kar pani kab tum ghar mein aaoge
Ay mere jani Yusuf-e-sani kab tum ghar mein aaoge
Apni dikhane shakl namani kab tum ghar mein aaoge

Sog mein tere beta main ne pehni kafni kali hai
Bhure balon wale aaja, jhula tera khali hai
(Bashukriya Maulana Akhtar-ul-Husain Zain-ud-Din-ul-Husaini Muhammadi)

Jab Zulfiqar-e-matam-e-Asghar mein ro chuki
Nanhi si qabr khod ke khunbar ho chuki
Bachche ke sath Bano ki qismat ko so chuki
Girta lahu bhara hua ashkon se dho chuki

Pani na tha jo Shah pichhrote mazar par
Ansu tapak pare lab-e-shir-khwar par
(Bashukriya shair o sozkhwan Sardar Husain Zaidi / Canada, London)
