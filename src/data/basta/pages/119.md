  Ay markaz-e-iman zindabad ay sabr ke parcham zindabad
  Krishan Bahari Lakhnavi

  Ay markaz-e-iman zindabad ay sabr ke parcham zindabad
  Har ashk tapak kar kehta hai Shabbir ka matam zindabad

  Insan ka bhala kya zikr koi, karte hain farishte bhi sajde
  Ay fakhr-e-malaik zindabad ay nazish-e-Adam zindabad

  Har qaum mein matam hota hai, Shabbir ka gham yaad aata hai
  Dil khinchte hain sab ke teri taraf ay mah-e-Muharram zindabad

  Hindu ho ke Muslim ya Momin, jalwon se nazar hatti hi nahin
  Ay shama-e-haqiqat zindabad ay nur-e-do-alam zindabad

  Shabbir, ho Bharat ya Kashmir, Iran ki zamin ya Pakistan
  Har mulk pe qabza tera hai ay Fateh-e-Azam zindabad

  Kalma to hai Nana ka lekin, bajta hai nawase ka danka
  Ae maqsad-e-Khaliq zindabad, ae raaz-e-do aalam zindabad

  Bhai ki tarah se tum bhi to, iman ka sahara ho Zainab
  Ismat ki rida ke saye mein, ae dusri Maryam zindabad

  Shabbir teri ulfat ke liye, Islam ki koi shart nahin
  Jhukhti hai jabin Noor aksar, ae qibla-e-aalam zindabad

  Ay markaz-e-iman zindabad ay sabr ke parcham zindabad
