  Sham se jab Ahl-e-Bait gham-zada giryan chale

  1
  Sham se jab Ahl-e-Bait, ba-sar-e-uryan chale
  Yani suye Karbala, chak-e-gareban chale
  Le ke shahidon ke sar, besar o saman chale
  Karte hue bibiyan ba-dil-e-nalan chale

  Aaj chaliswan hai Hazrat-e-Shabbir ka
  Tishna-dahan kushta-e-khanjar o taqdir ka

  2
  Rah meiṅ un se koi, puchhta gar aan kar
  Kis ke ho namus tum, kis ke ho lakht-e-jigar
  Kis ne luta tumhein, kis ne kiya bepidar
  Kehte the Ahl-e-Haram us se yun ba-chashm-e-tar

  Tum se bayaṅ kareṅ apne hum ahwal hum
  Dafn karne chale Fatima ke lal ko hum

  3
  Wo jo Haram-e-muhtaram, Shah ba-sad buka
  Manzilein tay karte haye, pahunche dar-e-Karbala
  Dur se wo qatl-gah un ko dikhai diya
  Ro ke ye kehne lage, Hazrat-e-Zain-ul-Abid

  Untoṅ se utro tum ab, karte hue shor o shain
  Rote chalo piyadah, yan se ba-lash-e-Husain
