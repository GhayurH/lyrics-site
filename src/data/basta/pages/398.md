  [[refrain]] Aaiye apne Maula se bateṅ kareṅ

  Al-madad, al-madad, al-aman, al-aman, al-ajal, al-ajal ya Imam-e-Zaman
  Tut parne ko haiṅ hum par ab bijliyaṅ, ay Imam-e-Zaman, al-aman al-aman
  Hum ne mana ke hum sab gunahgar haiṅ, aap ke naam-lewa haiṅ, hub-dar haiṅ
  Un ke rahm o karam par na chhoreṅ hameiṅ, dushman-e-din o millat jo ashrar haiṅ
  Uth rahe haiṅ jo fitne hamare khilaf, un ke madd-e-muqabil ianat kareṅ
  Marjaiyat, azadari nemat haiṅ, aap hi Maula in ki hifazat kareṅ
  Jin ko pas-e-shariat nahiṅ hai zara, un ka kya tazkira un ka shikwa bhi kya
  Zahiran Maula jo log dindar haiṅ, aap par hai ayan un ka sab majra
  Mangni, mehndi, shadi, walima huzur, apni khushiyoṅ meiṅ shamil hai fisq o fujur
  Had ye hai maut aur gham ke mauqoṅ pe bhi, din-ba-din ho rahe haiṅ shariat se dur
