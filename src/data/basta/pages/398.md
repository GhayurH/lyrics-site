  [[refrain]] Aaiye apne Maula se baten karen

  Al-madad, al-madad, al-aman, al-aman, al-ajal, al-ajal ya Imam-e-Zaman
  Tut parne ko hain hum par ab bijliyan, ay Imam-e-Zaman, al-aman al-aman
  Hum ne mana ke hum sab gunahgar hain, aap ke naam-lewa hain, hub-dar hain
  Un ke rahm-o-karam par na chhoren hamein, dushman-e-din-o-millat jo ashrar hain
  Uth rahe hain jo fitne hamare khilaf, un ke madd-e-muqabil ianat karen
  Marjaiyat, azadari nemat hain, aap hi Maula in ki hifazat karen
  Jin ko pas-e-shariat nahin hai zara, un ka kya tazkira un ka shikwa bhi kya
  Zahiran Maula jo log dindar hain, aap par hai ayan un ka sab majra
  Mangni, mehndi, shadi, walima huzur, apni khushiyon mein shamil hai fisq-o-fujur
  Had ye hai maut aur gham ke mauqon pe bhi, din-ba-din ho rahe hain shariat se dur
  Ahkamat-e-shariat hain tanqid ki zad mein, Maula madad aap farmaiye
  Ab nahin koi ummid-e-islah ki, Zulfiqar-e-Ali ke liye aa jaiye
