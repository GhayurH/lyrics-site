1
Sar-e-Naiza Jo Nikale They Wo Qaidi Ghar Nahi Aye.
Parein Hain Muntazir Lashein Ke Wapis Sar Nahi Aye.
Sitam Ki Aakhri Tasveer Pori Kis Tarha Ho Gi.
Agar Sajjad Ki Zanjeer Mein Baqir Nahi Aye.

2
Gale Shabir Ne Youn Kaat Di Thi Dhar Khanjar Ki.
Gale Katne Ko Hazir They Magar Khanjar Nahi Aye.
Tamasha Har Nagar Mein Dekhne Aal-e-Muhammad Ka.
Musalmaan Tu Bohat Aye Magar Kafir Nahi Aye.

3
Safar Mein Maon Bachoon Ko Pursa Dene Ki Khatir.
Koi Basti Na Aysi Thi Jahan Pathar Nahi Aye.
Zuban-e-Sabar Mein Akbar Sakina Kis Ko Kehte Hain.
Reha Ho Kar Bhi Jo Zindan Se Bahir Nahi Aye.
