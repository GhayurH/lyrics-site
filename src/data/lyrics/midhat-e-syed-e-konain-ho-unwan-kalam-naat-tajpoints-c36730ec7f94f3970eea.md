Midhat e Syed e Konain Ho Unwan Kalam Naat.
Munfarid Jazb Mawadat Se Ho Phir Shan Kalam.

1
Ilm Ke Shehar Se Nisbat Rahe Qayam Daim.
Mujh Pe Ho Jaye Issi Rabt Se Ahsan Kalam.
Jab Karon Mashaf Natiq Ki Warq Gardani.
Rooh Ka Hadiya Salwat Bachay Jan Kalam.

2
Khul Rahein Zooq-e-Adab Par Sabhi Asrar Pina.
Ilm Ke Dar Se Sand Ban Rahe Dewan Kalam.
Lafz Iss Shan Se Tazeen Hunar Ho Jaein.
Ahl-e-Fun Sun Ke Hoon Garwidah e Irfan Kalam.

3
Matla Noor-e-Azal Ki Ho Jhalak Lafzoon Mein.
Sab Mazameen Aqeedat Banein Faizan Kalam.
Fikar-e-Majhool Kahin Se Na Koi Dar Aaye.
Zooq Ho Soch Ki Dehleez Pe Darban Kalam.

4
Mere Izhar Ko Ya Rab Woh Tawanayi De.
Dil Mein Baqi Na Rahe Koi Bhi Arman Kalam.
Hama Tan Gosh, Sar Bazm-e-Sukhan, Qudsi Hoon.
Nudrat Nutq Se Mumtaz Rahe Shan Kalam.
