1
Bhaiya Ghareeb Zainab Tera Kafan Na Layi.
Lash-e-Hussain Par Hai Zainab Ki Yeh Dohayi.
Misal-e-Batool Haq Se Mehroom Hoon Main Ab Tak.
Shabir Tere Gham Mein Magmoom Hoon Main Ab Tak.
Tarpa Rahi Hai Mujh Ko Abbas Ki Judai.
Afsos Keh Sakina Nazoon Se Jo Pali Hai.
Jo Tum Ko Yad Kar Ke Zindaan Mein Chal Basi Hai.
Oss Ko Mili Na Bhaiyya Zindan Se Rehayi.
Hum Par Sitam Aye Bhaiyya Dhate Rahe Sitamgar.
Syedaniyo Ke Sar Mein Aati Thi Khak Ur Kar.
Bazar Ka Woh Manzar Zainab Na Bhol Payi.
Balon Se Munh Chupa Kar Bazar Mein Gayi Thi.
Nargay Mein Ashqiya Ke Be-Parda Main Khari Thi.
Guzari Hai Kya Behan Par Kaise Bataon Bhai.
Sajjad Ke Yeh Dil Par Dushwar Marhala Tha.
Zanjeer Ko Sanbhale Bemar Ro Raha Tha.
Aal-e-Nabi Khulay Sar Darbar Mein Jo Aayi.
Sab Se Bari Musibat Kya Hai Kisi Ne Pocha.
Bemar Sar Jhuka Kar Rotay Howay Yeh Bola.
Behno Ka Sham Jana Zainab Ki Be-Ridai.
Iss Aik Sanhe Par Aksar Muhib Ne Socha.
Barbad Ho Gaya Jab Aal-e-Nabi Ka Kunba.
Bad-e-Hussain Kyun Kar Baqi Rahi Khudai.
