Maqtal e Hazrat Imam Hussain Bas Ab Amma Chali Jao
Apne Nazdeek Jo Zahra Ko Tarapta Dekha
Joor Kar Hath Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Ne Yeh Kaha
Aap Ke Wastay Waday Ko Nibhaon Amma
Dein Ijazat Ke Main Sar Apna Kataon Amma
Kaha Shabeer Ne Ro Kar Chaley Ga Halq Par Khanjar Bas Ab Amma Chali Jao
Na Dekha Jaye Ga Tum Se Hamari Maut Ka Manzar Bas Ab Amma Chali Jao
Meri Gardan Pe Aye Amma Churi Zalim Chalaye Ga
Najis Zanu Woh Apna Jis Ghari Seene Pe Rakhay Ga
Ghutay Ga Dam Bohat Mera Chaley Gi Sans Ruk Ruk Kar
Bas Ab Amma Chali Jao
Har Ik Mushkil Mein Aye Amma Madad Ki Aap Ne Meri
Hamarey Sath Darya Se Uthai Lash Ghazi Ki
Tumhara Shukriya Tum Ne Uthaya Lasha-e-Akbar
Bas Ab Amma Chali Jao
Madina Yaad Aa Jaye Na Karbal Mein Tujhey Amma
Meri Darhi Pakar Kar Shimar Marey Ga Mujhe Amma
Tumhari Tarha Mere Bhi Parin Gay Neel Chehray Par
Bas Ab Amma Chali Jao
Yahin Se Alwida Keh Do Hamarey Pichey Mat Aana
Tumhara Pehlu Zakhmi Hai Nahi Tum Se Chala Jana
Humein Takleef Hoti Hai Jo Tum Chalti Ho Jhuk Jhuk Kar
Bas Ab Amma Chali Jao
Tarap Jaye Gi Abbas-e-Jarri Ki Lash Darya Par
Shaheedon Ke Dilon Par Phir Se Chal Jaye Ga Ik Khanjar
Nabi Ki Ladli Tum Ko Na Lag Jaye Kahin Pathar
Bas Ab Amma Chali Jao
Jo Tum Jhuk Jhuk Ke Chalti Ho Tumhein Pehchan Jaye Gi
Tumharey Bain Sun Sun Ke Tu Oss Ki Jan Jaye Gi
Kahin Ghabra Ke Zainab Aa Na Jaye Ran Mein Be Chadar
Bas Ab Amma Chali Jao
Na Jane Sabar Tha SAJJAD Kitna Shah Zadi Mein
Kata Shabeer Ka Sar Fatima Zahra Ki Jholi Mein
Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Kehte Rahe Chalta Raha Khanjar
Bas Ab Amma Chali Jao
