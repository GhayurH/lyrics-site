1
Barchi Ali Akbar Ke Kalajay Mein Lagi Hai Hasan Sadiq.
Dam Tortay Farzand Ko Maa Dekh Rahi Hai.
Aye Maut Thehar Ja Mere Akbar Ko Na Le Ja.
Rasta Ali Akbar Ka Behan Dekh Rahi Hai.

2
Zainab Ne Kaha Haye Mere Nazoon Ke Palay.
Barchi Tere Seene Pe Nahi Mere Lagi Hai.
Laila Ne Kaha Jis Ka Jawaan Lal Ho Aisa.
Oss Maa Ki Rida Foaj-e-Shaqi Cheen Rahi Hai.

3
Maa Kehti Thi Bin Biyah Ke Jab Aaye Tu Beta.
Dunya Se Sawari Teri Janat Se Chali Hai.
 Rehan Ka Noha Hai Ke Yeh Bazm-e-Azza Mein.
Akbar Teri Aawaz-e-Azaan Gaunj Rahi Hai.
