1
Barchi Ko Nikala Baap Ne Syed Ali Naqvi
Hath Rakha Our Kabhi Rakh Kar Hataya Baap Ne.
Kis Tarha Seene Se Barchi Ko Nikala Baap Ne.
Hath Barchi Par Rakha Munh Dosri Janib Kiya.
Band Aankhein Kein Kabhi Hathoon Ko Chehray Par Rakha.
Ho Nahi Paya Sabhi Kuch Aazmaya Baap Ne.
Ashk Aankhon Mein Theharte Hi Nahi Yeh Soch Kar.
Ayeriyaan Ragrein Jawaan Ne Haye Jis Dum Khak Par.
Sochta Hoon Kis Tarha Khud Ko Sanbhala Baap Ne.
Aasmaan Roney Laga Haye Zameen Thara Gayi.
Tha Ajab Manzar Ali Akbar Pukarey Ya Ali.
Khud Ko Jab Nazdeek Betay Ke Giraya Baap Ne.
Yaad Wadah Aa Gaya Sughra Se Jo Kar Aaye They.
Dekh Kar Akbar Madine Ki Taraf Roney Lagay.
Haal Sughra Ka Jo Akbar Ko Sunaya Baap Ne.
Haye Barchi Khainch Li Sheh Ne Kaha Jab Ya Ali.
Lash Khaimay Mein Jo Laye Tu Qayamat Ho Gayi.
Haal Jab Zainab Ne Pocha Na Bataya Baap Ne.
Soch Kar Shabbir Ki Ghurbat Labon Par Jan Hai.
Haye Zeeshan-o- Ali Yeh Kam Kab Aasan Hai.
Borhay Kandhey Par Jawaan Lasha Uthaya Baap Ne.
