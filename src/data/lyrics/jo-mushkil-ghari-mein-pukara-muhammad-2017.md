Jo Mushkil Ghari Mein Pukara Muhammad – Naat
Madad Ko Chale Aye Barah Muhammad

1
Nahi Aal Ke Bin Muhammad Kissi Ka
Koi Lakh Bole Hamara Muhammad
Aye Asma-e-Hasna Ijazat Hai Keh Doon
Ali(JJL) Ne Zameen Par Utara Muhammad

2
Zubano Ke Kham Taig-e-Haider Ne Nikle
Gunwaroon Ko Kab The Gawara Muhammad
Kissi Ne Ali Ki Haqeeqat Jo Pochi
Muhammad Ne Hans Ke Pukara Muhammad

3
Muhammad Muhammad Muhammad Muhammad
Yeh Kunba Hai Sare Ka Sara Muhammad
Yeh Akbar Ki Aamad Pe Shabir Bole
Bulaye Gaye Hain Dobara Muhammad
