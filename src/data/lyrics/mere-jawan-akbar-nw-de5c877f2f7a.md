Nowha kare maa laashe akbar par

tum qatl huve beta jeeti rahi maadar

ay mere jawaan akbar

veeraan hua beta tere baad mera ghar

kya tujhko teri maa ne is din ke liye paala

is maa ko zaeefi may kyun chod gaye beta

maa roti hai laashe pe tere aaj khule sar

ay mere……..

kehti thi phupi zainab jab byah rachaungi

akbar teri dulhan ki poshaak banaungi

afsos kafan bhi na tujhe aaya mayassar

ay mere……..

bemaar behan sughra takti hai tera rasta

pardes may tum aakar kyun bhool gaye waada

itna bhi nahi socha ke marjayegi qaahar

ay mere……..

humshakle nabi tumko kehta tha jahan sara

zainab tera laashe par karti hai yehi nowha

nana ki ziyarat ko taras jaaungi dilbar

ay mere……..

har dil ne kiya nowha har aankh hui purnam

kohraam hai qaimo may jannat may bapa matam

aur khaak udaate hai yehi kehke payambar

ay mere……..

phupiyaan bhi tadapti hai hamsheer bhi madar bhi

dada bhi tere roye shabbir bhi shabbar bhi

sadaat ke qaimo may qayamat ka hai manzar

ay mere……..

jab koi jawaan laash nazar aayegi beta

maa yaad tujhe karke tadap jaayegi beta

ek roz teri yaad may marjayegi madar

ay mere……..
