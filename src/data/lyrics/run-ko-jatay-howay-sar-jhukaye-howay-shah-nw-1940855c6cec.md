Run Ko Jatay Howay Sar Jhukaye Howay Shah Ne Ro Kar Kaha Alwida Alwidaa

Aya Waqat-e-Safar Ay Bahan Aj Ham Ho Rahay Hain Judaa Alwida Alwida

Shah Barh Ker Bahan Ke Galay Lag Gaye Or Dukhya Bahan Say Ye Kahnay Lagay

Meri Mazloom Pyasai Musafir Bahan Teraa Hafiz Khuda Alwida Alwida

Jis Say Darti Thi Tum Woh Ghari A Gaye Daikho Charon Taraf Teergi Cha Gaye

Dasht E Pur Hol Main Shaam Honay Ko Hay Lo Musafaar Chalaa Alwida Alwidaa

Lay Ke Bali Sakeena Ko Aghosh Main Nanhay Nanhay Say Hathon Ke Bosay Liye

Shah Ne Hasrat Say Baiti Ko Taktay Howay Ah Bhar Ker Kaha Alwida Alwida

Bola Beemar Baytay Say Aaqa Mera Ab Na Ayeyga Aye, Beta Baba Tera, Beta Baba Tera

Maut Ki Aahatain Taiz Honay Lagee Aarahee Hai Sada Alwida Alwida

Aya Khaimay Say Bahar Jo Mola Mera Bhai Beta Bhateeja Koi Bhi Na Tha, Hi Koi Bhi Na Tha

Aik Hasrat Say Chaaro Taraf Dekh Kar Sheh Nay Khud He Kaha Alwida Alwida

Jaab Tarap Kar Kaha Meray Shero Uthoo Aao Zainab Kay Bhai Koi Rukhsat Karo Aao Rukhsat Karo

Aik Aik Laash-e-bay Sir Say Aayee Sada Ayey Shah-e-karbala Alwida Alwida

Aisa Matam Karo Aisa Matam Karo Pak Bibi Kahay Marhaba Marhaba, Marhaba Marhaba

Khoon Ugaltay Howay Aik Aik Zakham Say Aaj Aayey Sada Alwida Alwida
