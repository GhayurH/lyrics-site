Daal do koi zakhmon pe chadar

Haay! Akbar ki maa aa rahi hai

Seena Akbar ka rakho chhupakar

Haay! Akbar ki maa aa rahi hai

Biwiyon milke halqa banaa lo

Maa jawa'n laal ki hai sambhalo

Marr na jaye woh laashe pe aakr

Haay! Akbar ki maa aa rahi hai

Dekh legi agar Umm-e-layla

Apne bacche ka zakhmi kaleja

Saans bhi lena payegi maader

Haay! Akbar ki maa aa rahi hai

Mannatein mankr jisko pala

Uske seene me zalim ka bhala

Kaise dekhegi mader yeh manzar

Haay! Akbar ki maa aa rahi hai

Jisko dulha banane ke din the

Jiski shaadi rachane ke din the

Usko dekhegi maa khoon me tarr

Koi arman dil ka na nikla

Loot gayi Umm-e-layla ki duniya

Bayen karne ko laash-e-jawa'n par

Haay! Akbar ki maa aa rahi hai..

Rakh ke khaime me Akbar ka laasha

Sar jhukha kr bahut roye maula

Jab kahaa biwi Fizzan rokar

Haay! Akbar ki maa aa rahi hai

Bayen vahis yeh khaime me goonja

Dekh le apne Yusuf ka laasha

Khaaq uDati hui piT-ti sar

Haaye! Akbar ki maa aa rahi hai
