Dil-e-Mehdi Mein Jis Gham Ki Har Ik Lamha Harrarat Hai.
Haan Oss Ka Ik Pehlu Turbat-e-Zahra Ki Ghurbat Hai.

1
Ujalay Roza-e-Sarwar Ke Ro Ro Kar Yeh Kehte Hain.
Andhera Qabar-e-Zhara Par Ho Yeh Kaisi Qismat Hai.
Jaffa Ke Taziyano Ne Yeh Oss Ka Hath Tora Hai.
Keh Jis Ke Hath Mein Roz-e-Qayamat Ki Shifat Hai.

2
Dar-e-Zahra Jalane Walay Yeh Be-Khoof Kehte They.
Muhammad Ka Yahan Rukna Yeh Ik Jhoti Riwayat Hai.
Muqam-e-Fatima Kya Hai Fidak Yeh Jan Kar Cheeno.
Zamane Ki Jo Rehmat Hai Yeh Oss Rehmat Pe Rehmat Hai.

3
Nasara Ne Rida Di Tum Magar Darbar-e-Nabvi Mein.
Musalman Ho Ke Jhutlate Rahe Zahra Ki Hairat Hai.
Muhammad Ke Wafadaro Zara Lo Naam Qatil Ka.
Chupana Qatil-e-Zhara Yeh ALLAH Se Bagawat Hai.

4
Banana Our Mitana Kehta Hai Chalees Qabroon Ka.
Siwa Haider Khabar Kis Ko Kahan Zahra Ki Turbat Hai.
Koi Wirsa Nahi Tha Our Na Zahra Mangne Aaein.
Yeh Kahin Maon Ke Bachoon Mein Hi Kehne Ki Jurrat Hai.

5
Koi Mehdi Ko Pursa De Koi Ashk Laho Roye.
Azadaro Shikasta Pehlu Zahra Ki Shahadat Hai.
Bhare Darbar Mein Oss Ko Rula Dala Hai Ghasib Ne.
Keh Jis Ki Jaun Masoomeen Par Wajib Itaat Hai.
