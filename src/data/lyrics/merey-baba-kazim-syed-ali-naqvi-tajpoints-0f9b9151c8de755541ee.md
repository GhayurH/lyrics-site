Merey Baba Kazim Syed Ali Naqvi Lyrics
Lab Pe Noha Hai Raza Ka Mere Baba Kazim.

1
Jakra Zanjeer Mein Lasha Mere Baba Kazim.
Dushmani Mein Hai Wilayat Ki Diya Zehar-e-Daga.
Aap Tu Baab-e-Hawaij They Bhala Kya Thi Khata.
Soch Ke Dil Hai Tarapta Mere Baba Kazim.

2
Shab Ibadat Mein Tu Din Guzrey Aziyat Mein Tere.
Jaisay Hoon Sadiyaan Har Aik Pal Hai Musibat Mein Tere.
Qaid Khana Tujhe Roya, Mere Baba Kazim.
Haye Mazdoor Uthatay Hain Janaza Oss Ka.

3
Deein Ki Rag Rag Mein Lahu Door Raha Hai Jis Ka.
Waqt Aaya Hai Yeh Kaisa, Mere Baba Kazim.
Dil Tarap Jata Hai Pursay Ko Koi Aata Hai.
Dil Ko Manzar Hai Madine Ka Jo Rulwata Hai.

4
Mujhe Ko Yaad Aati Hain Zahra Mere Baba Kazim.
Haye Afsoos Keh Kya Koi Bhi Insan Na Tha.
Poray Baghdad Mein Kya Koi Musalman Na Tha.
Jo Tera Lasha Uthata Mere Baba Kazim.

5
Phir Se Yaad Aaya Mujhe Karb-o-Bala Ka Manzar.
Jaisay Be Goor-o-Kafan Ran Mein Thi Lash-e-Sarwar.
Be Kafan Lasha Hai Tera, Mere Baba Kazim.
Farsh Jab Gham Ka Bicha, Pursay Ko Aaein Zahra.

6
Aik Masooma Ko Qum Se Bhi Hain Laein Zahra.
Sunney Ko Aaein Hain Noha Mere Baba Kazim.
Baal Bikhraye Hoye Aap Ke Lashay Ke Qareen.
Peetti Jati Hain Hathoon Se Kabhi Apni Jabeen.

7
Marsiya Parhti Hain Zahra Mere Baba Kazim.
Dekh Mashhad Mein Ali Gham Ke Hain Badil Chaye.
Lab Pe Hai Maula Raza Ke Yehi Noha Haye.
Aye Shakeel Aaj Hain Tanha, Mere Baba Kazim.
