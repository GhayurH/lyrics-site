1
Raza e Imam e Raza Chahta Hun Syed Mohammad Shah.
Riza-e-Imam-e-Raza Chahta Hoon.
Main Arsh-e-Olla Dekhna Chahta Hoon.
Raza Keh Rahe Hain Musalay Pe Aa Kar.
Muhammad Hoon Main Fatima Chahta Hoon.
Assa Ki Zarorat Nahi Bolay Mosa.
Main Nalain-e-Maula Raza Chahta Hoon.
Mere Dil Ki Batein Samjhta Hai Khaliq.
Raza Jantay Hain Main Kya Chahta Hoon.
Kaha Rab Ne Chunta Hoon Lehja Raza Ka.
Main Jab Bhi Kahin Bolna Chahta Hoon.
Koi Keh Raha Tha Yeh Sehan-e-Shifa Mein.
Main Esa Hoon Maula Shifa Chahta Hoon.
Main Mashhad Ki Mati Se Paida Howa Hoon.
Issi Mein Main Apni Jagah Chahta Hoon.
Mera Ghar Ho Jannat Mein Hur Ke Barabar.
Main Matam Ka Bas Yeh Sila Chahta Hoon.
Mere Sath Aao Sawa Lakh Nabiyon.
Main Ik Matami Qafila Chahta Hoon.
Go Debal Nahi Murtajiz Hoon Magar Main.
Imam-e-Raza Ki Qaba Chahta Hoon.
