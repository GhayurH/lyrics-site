1
Shabir Ne Aakhiya Zainab Nu Tainu Chadar Jan Toon Pyari Aye Ghazi Diyaan Khairaan Mang Zainab.
Toon Sham De Safar Te Jan Welay Ik Gal Da Khas Khiyal Karin.
Na Wekhin Sir Wal Ghazi De Na Gal Koi Lash De Nal Karin.
Aidi Lash De Kolon Sir Nangay Chup Kar Ke Jawin Lang Zainab Ghazi Diyaan Khairaan Mang Zainab.

2
Mainu Aakhda Si Mainu Das Mula Tainu Kiwein Veer Hussain Kahwaan.
Kinj Ho Sakda Aye Sain Meri Zainab Nu Apni Bhain Kahwaan.
Main Ummal Baneen Kaneez Da Chan Oh Pak Batool Da Ang Zainab Ghazi Diyaan Khairaan Mang Zainab.
Mainu Kehnda Si Aye Jan Welay Mera Peer Hussain Aye Wada De.

3
Meri Lash Nu Nayi Lay Jawein Ga Bin Pani Kol Sakina De.
Aye Aakhri Gal Abbas Kiti Gaya Phir Chehray Da Rang Zainab Ghazi Diyaan Khairaan Mang Zainab.
Jehra Kofa Wekh Ke Aayi Sain Rut Badal Gayi Onna Galiyaan Di.
Oh Shahi Si Tere Babay Di Utton Naal Hussain Da Ghazi Si.

4
Hun Kaliyaan Kiwein Guzray Gi Oh Moor Bazar Ne Tang Zainab Ghazi Diyaan Khairaan Mang Zainab.
 Akbar Si Wasiyyat Ghazi Di Jadon Pohancho Wich Zindan Tusi.
Pawein Zakhmi Ho Gayi Teraan Nal Iss Mashk De Wich Way Jan Meri.
Iss Mashk Nu V Dafna Choray Wich Qabar Sakina Sang Zainab Ghazi Diyaan Khairaan Mang Zainab.
