1
Ik Waris E Haider Hai Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.
Ik Waris-e-Haider Hai Jo Ghaibat Se Aaye Ga.
Kya Sheh Hai Azadari Dunya Ko Bataye Ga.
Kaba Se Alam Le Kar Iss Shan Se Nikley Ga.

2
Quran Imamat Ka Juzdan Se Nikley Ga.
Tauheed Ke Jalway Mein Islam Nahaye Ga.
Shabeer Ki Ik Majlis Kaba Mein Bapa Hogi.
ALLAH Ke Ghar Mein Bhi Yeh Rasam Ada Hogi.

3
Khud Farsh-e-Azza Apne Hathoon Se Bichaye Ga.
Aa Janey Do Parday Se Waris Ko Shaheedon Ke.
Urr Jaein Gay Dunya Mein Khud Hosh Yazeedon Ke.
Jab Hath Mein Haider Ki Talwar Uthaye Ga.

4
Abbas Ke Parcham Mein Pholon Ki Lari Hogi.
Ik Mashk Phareray Ke Seene Pe Pari Hogi.
Yeh Qarz Shahadat Ka Pyason Ki Chukaye Ga.
Iss Shair Ki Aamad Ka Andaz Ajab Hoga.

5
Ghazi Ki Nigahon Mein Haider Ka Ghazab Hoga.
Bholay Se Koi But Phir Kaba Mein Na Aaye Ga.
Har Dil Mein Ziyarat Ke Arman Barey Hoonge.
Esa Bhi Jamat Ki Khuwaish Mein Kharey Hoonge.

6
Jab Apna Musalla Yeh Pani Pe Bichaye Ga.
Zalim Ke Liye Akhtar Dunya Mein Tabahi Hain.
Hum Ahl-e-Azza Iss Ke Lashkar Ke Sipahi Hain.
Munkir Hai Jo Iss Ka Woh Janat Mein Na Jaye Ga.
