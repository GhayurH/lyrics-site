Hatho me Ali Asgar ko liye Shabbir Sadaa ye dete the

1
Kiyaa mujse larengi ye Foje jafaa. Nau Lakh is Lashkar ke liye

2
Mere Asgar Kafi hay

3
Khud iski sana Quran ki terha Quran shujaat kerta hay,  Hussain Ya Hussain
Ye Sher-e-Khuda ka pota hay Talwar bina larne ke liye

4
Mera Asgar kafi hay

5
Kamsin hay magar laachaar nahi Abbas ke jaise tewar hay,  Hussain Ya Hussain,  Bas ek ishare se apni tufaan bapa ker ne ke liye
Mera Asgar kafi hay

6
Rote to baras jaye badal bole to kayamat aajaye,  Hussain Ya Hussain,  Ay marhab-e-dashte karbobala khaybar ko tere jane ke liye
Mera Asgar kafi hay

7
Umat pe jafa mansoor nahi Mei sabir hu majboor nahi,  Hussain Ya Hussain,  ye Hukam-e-Mashiyat hay warna Abbas tere Badle ke liye
Mera Asgar kafi hay

8
Allah har Himat Baano ki khud fooj-e-sitam se kahti thi,  Hussain Ya Hussain,  Shabbir Pe Teer na Barsawo har teer sitam khane ke liye
Mera Asgar kafi hay

9
Jab ayi Sakina Maqtal me, Ek Laash se Ati thi ye sadaa,  Hussain Ya Hussain,  Madaar ki Godh me so jawo pehlu me mere so ne ke liye
Mera Asgar kafi hay

10
Sugra ko khabar ye ho na saki, Dunyaa se gaya piyasaa bhayi,  Hussain Ya Hussain,  usne to likha hay khat me yahi Ay baba mere sine ke liye
Mera Asgar kafi hay

11
"Rehan" nahi fikr-e-mehshar Jab bab-e-hawaj hay asghar,  Hussain Ya Hussain,  firdos me ibn-e-Zehra ke kadmo me jaga pane ke liye
Mera Asgar kafi hay
