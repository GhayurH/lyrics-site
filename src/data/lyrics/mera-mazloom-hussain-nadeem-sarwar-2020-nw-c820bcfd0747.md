Haye Mazloom Hussain; Mera Mazloom Hussain

Asr-e-Ashoor Mera Laal bada pyasa hai

1)

Yeh Muhammad ka nawasa hai Ali ka Beta

Kya khata hai Mere Betey ki batao tu zara

zalimoon kyon isey be-jurm-o-khata ghera hai

Haye Mazloom Hussain; Mera Mazloom Hussain

Haye Mazloom Hussain

Asr-e-Ashoor Mera Laal bada pyasa hai

2)

Haye tanhaayee ka aalam key koi paas nahin

Haye Qasim nahin Akbar; nahin Abbas nahin

Ya Ali aao; Mera Laal; bada tanha hai

Haye Mazloom Hussain; Mera Mazloom Hussain

Haye Mazloom Hussain

Asr-e-Ashoor Mera Laal bada pyasa hai

3)

Aik din mein bharey lashkar ka bhi gham dekha hai

Is ney Abbas ka Akbar ka; bhi gham dekha hai

Dafn kar key Ali Asghar ko; abhi aaya hai

Haye Mazloom Hussain; Mera Mazloom Hussain

Haye Mazloom Hussain

Asr-e-Ashoor Mera Laal bada pyasa hai

4)

Haye chalni hai jigar phir bhi nahin roya hai

Kitney din sey Tu Mere Laal; nahin soya hai

Aa Meri goud mein soja Tu; bohat jaga hai

Haye Mazloom Hussain; Mera Mazloom Hussain

Haye Mazloom Hussain

Asr-e-Ashoor Mere Laal bada pyasa hai

5)

Aa-ee-ye behr-e-Khuda hashr; bapa hai Baba

shimr khanjar liye Baali pey; khada hai Baba

Aap key Pyare Nawasey ka; gala kat-ta hai

Haye Mazloom Hussain; Mera Mazloom Hussain

Haye Mazloom Hussain

Asr-e-Ashoor Mera Laal bada pyasa hai
