Faqeerana Aaye Sada Kar Chale.
Mian Khush Raho Hum Dua Kar Chale.

1
Jo Tujh Bin Na Jeene Ko Kehte The Hum.
So Oss Ahad Ko Aab Wafa Kar Chale.
Shifa Apni Taqdeer Hi Mein Na Thi.
Keh Maqdoor Tak Dawa Kar Chale.

2
Woh Kya cheez Hai Aah Jis Ke Liye.
Har Ik Cheez Se Dill Utha Kar Chale.
Koi Na Umeedana Karte Nigah.
SO Tum Hum Se Munh Bhi chupa Kar Chale.

3
Bohat Aarzo Thi Ghali Ki Teri.
So Yaan Se Laho Mein Niha Kar Chale.
Dikhai Diye Youn Keh Be Khud Kiya.
Humein Aap Se Bhi Juda Kar Chale.

4
Jabeen Sajdah Karte Hi Karte Gayi.
Haq-e-Bandagi Hum Adda Kar Chale.
Parastish Ki Yaan Tak Keh Aye But Tujhe.
Nazar Mein Sabhoon Ki Khuda Kar Chale.

5
Jahre Phool Jis Rang Gulban Se Youn.
Chaman Mein Jahan Ke Hum Aa Kar Chale.
Na Dekha Gham-e-Dostaan Shukar Hai.
Humein Daag Apna Dikha Kar Chale.

6
Gayi Umar Dar Band-e-Fikar-e-Ghazal.
So Is Fun Ko Aisa Bara Kar Chale.
Kahin Kya Jo Poche Koi HUm Se Mir.
Jahan Mein TUm Aaye The Kya Kar Chale.
