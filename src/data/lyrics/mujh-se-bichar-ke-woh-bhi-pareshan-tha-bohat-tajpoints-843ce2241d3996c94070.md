1
Mujh Se Bichar Ke Woh Bhi Pareshan Tha Bohat.
Jis Ki Nazar Mein Kam Yeh Aasan Tha Bohat.
Socha Tu Be-Khaloos Thin Sab Oss Ki Qurbatein.
Jis Ke Begair Ghar Mera Veeran Tha Bohat.

2
Be-Khuwab Surkh Aankhon Ne Sab Kuch Bata Diya.
Kal Raat Dil Mein Dard Ka Tofaan Tha Bohat.
Yeh Kya Kiya Keh Pyar Ka Izhar Kar Diya.
Main Apni Iss Shikast Pe Hairan Tha Bohat.

3
Wehshat Mein Kyun Kissi Ke Girebaan Ko Dekhta.
Mere Liye Tu Apna Gireban Tha Bohat.
Ab Tu Koi Tamana Hi Baqi Nahi Rahi.
Yeh Shehar-e-Aarzo Kabhi Gunjaan Tha Bohat.
