Hal chal hai fouj e sham main Abbas atay hain

Teeron pay teer naizoo pay naizay chalatay hain

asgher ko maa chupati hai Jukh Jukh Kay godh main

Khaimoo ko toor toor ker jab teer atay hain

ghoray doraye sham say lashker jo atay hain

Kam umer bachay khaimoo main ghabraye jatay hain

Kya waqat per Gaya hai Mohammed ki aal per

Chader nahi hai baloon say mou ko chupatay hain

Dil pani pani Hota hai bachoon Ka piyas say

Sookhi zuban hontoo pay asgher phiratay hain
