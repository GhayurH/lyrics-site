1
Dilon Mein Pyar Bona Chahta Hoon.
Zamane Bhar Ka Hona Chahta Hoo.
Mujhe Ik Shaks Ka Hai Dard Aisa.
Main Sari Umar Rona Chahta Hoo.

2
Akathe Kar Ke Dunya Ke Musaib.
Samandar Mein Dabona Chahta Hoon.
Nahi Bardasht Hota Dukh Jahan Ka.
Main Khuda Pathar Ka Hona Chahta Hoo.

3
Mere Upar Zameen Ko Orh Dijay.
Sakoon Ki Neend Sona Chahta Hoo.
 Azai Bant Kar Sab Apni Khushiyaan.
Main Khali Hath Hona Chahta Hoo.
