Khoon ronda wain ponda bimaar ridawaan de

1
Har Moor Te Putar Hussain Da Mangda Burqay Manwaan De
Patharaan Diyaan Barshaan Har Moor Te Hondiyaan
Zahra Diyaan Jaiyaan Rondiyaan
Kofe Wich Badal De Vekhe Ne Zainab Matlab Chawaan De
Rakhiyaan Ne Izzataan Esai Bastiyaan
Oh Shehar Si Kalma Gowaan De
Jinein Okhe Shehar Guzarey Ne Zainab Sham De Rawaan De

2
Sada chacha jis tarha si baazu babay da
Seh baszu veeran tu mera
Menu Akabar kaun Abbas de vangu
Veer salaam vande

3
O Yasrab Shehar Si Veraan Da Maan Si
Aye Sham Aye Jonda Nayi Koi
Kis Hal Ich Safar Te Ayi Aye Zainab Naal Bhirawaan De
Ghazi Di Lash Te Aj Osse Sain De Rasiyaan Wich Bazu Qaid Ne
Jeri Zainab Sadqay Landi Rayi Ghazi Diyaan Banwaan De
Akbar Sab Bibiyaan San Duray Khandiyaan Ik Dojay Nu San Kaindiyaan
Saday Jondiyaan Putar Sajjad Nu Na Aye Shimar Sazawaan De
