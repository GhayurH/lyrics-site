Goonji ba waqt-e-asr sada may Hussain hoon
Kaato na mera khusk gala,  may Hussain hoon
Goonji ba waqt-e-asr sada may Hussain hoon,

1
Amma kissi nai paani ki ik bhund bhi na di
Mei baar baar kehta raha,  may Hussain hoon

2
Sookhi huwi hai sang mere halq teer se
Aahista tu churri ko chala,  may Hussain hoon

3
Shireen ke ghar pe pohncha jo ranon ka qaafila
Naizai sai aik sar na kaha,  may Hussain hoon

4
Darbaar main laeen ke kohraam mach gaiya
Mukhtaar se jo sar nai kaha, may Hussain hoon

5
Zanon pe rakh ke sar ko kaha ik zaeef ne
Akber kahan hai jald bata,  may Hussain hoon

6
Aey shaamiyo kafan na diya, mujh ko gham nahi
Day do meri behan ko rida,  may Hussain hoon

7
Amma kissi nai paani ka ik bhund bhi na di
May baar baar kehta raha,  may Hussain hoon

8
Mehsus ho raha hai ke, maqtal se aaj bhi
Aawaz aa rahi hai Raza,  may Hussain hoon
