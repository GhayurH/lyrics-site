Waqt e Zahoor Mir Hasan Mir Lyrics
Zahoor Ka Waqt Aa Gaya Hai Imam Se Lau Lagaye Rakhna.

1
Zahoor Ka Waqt Aa Gaya Hai Haram Pe Nazrein Jamaye Rakhna.
Zahoor Ka Waqt Aa Gaya Hai Charag-e-Ulfat Jalaye Rakhna.
Hai Inteqam-e-Hussain Baqi Woh Aane Wala Hisab Le Ga.
Aqeeda Apna Darust Rakhna Jo Unn Ki Nusrat Ki Hai Tamana.

2
Zahoor Ka Waqt Aa Gaya Hai Amal Ko Bhi Aazmaye Rakhna.
Na Neend Aaye Na Ongh Aaye Rakho Har Ik Par Nazar Jamaye.
Dil-o-Nazar Se Keh Samarra Se Na Jane Kis Rastay Se Aaye.
Zahoor Ka Waqt Aa Gaya Hai Har Aik Rasta Sajay Rakhna.

3
Na Hub-e-Dunya Se Kuch Ilaqa Na Ghair Se Rah-o-Rasam Rakhna.
Tumhari Dolat Hai Ilm-o-Taqwa Karey Ga Dajjal Oss Pe Hamla.
Zahoor Ka Waqt Aa Gaya Hai Tum Apni Ponji Bachaye Rakhna.
Yahin Kahin Aas Pas Hoga Woh Aane Wala Tha Aa Chuka Hai.

4
Hawari Esa Ke Sath Hoon Gay Mawali Maula Ke Sath Hoon Gay.
Zahoor Ka Waqt Aa Gaya Hai Mowaliyon Se Banaye Rakhna.
Siwaye Chand Aik Zahoor Ki Sab Alamatein Pori Ho Chuki Hain.
Baseerat-o-Marifat Tumhari Adoye Haq Ko Khatak Rahi Hain.

5
Zahoor Ka Waqt Aa Gaya Hai Dil-o-Nazar Ko Bachaye Rakhna.
Naye Naye Nagma Haye Midhat Jo Roz Keh Keh Ke La Rahe Ho.
Paye Ziyarat Jo:SIBT e JAFAR: Imam Ke Dar Pe Aa Paray Ho.
Zahoor Ka Waqt Aa Gaya Hai Tum Apna Bistar Lagaye Rakhna.
