Sir Nangay Phuphiyaan Mavaan Day Vekhe Abid Nay .

Haye Shaam Walay, Khooni Manzaar Kiwain Vekhe Abid Nay.

Sir Sangaan Tay Char Kay Hay Laasha Nay Gaiyaan
Bemaar Hi Janay Kiwain Dafaan Ey Hoiyaan
Haye Shaam Taye Piyu Day Qatal Kiwain Vekhe Abid Nay.

Gal Touq Tay Behri Bemaar Day Kaariyaan
Takh Shaam Nu Akhiyaan Taiyo Louh Diyaan Jahriyaan
Wich Bazaaran Day Haste Shaami Kiwain Vekhe Abid Nay.

Teri Qaid Ey Vakhri Na Rowi Sakina
Tu Khakh Tey Souna Nahi Ey Babay Da Seena
Haye Sabaar Teray Bhain Meri Kiwain Vekhe Abid Nay.

Tur Duniya Toh Challeya Jera Khoon Si Ronda .
Ajj Vereya Akhiri Koi Apna Tey Honda .
Haal Baqir Day Hai Yateema Wallay Vekhe Abid Nay.

Koi Qaidi Momin Mumtaz Na Howay .
Be Warissaan Wangou Pardes Na Roway .
Haye Ghairaat Day Zakhme Saray Kiwain Vekhe Abid Nay .
