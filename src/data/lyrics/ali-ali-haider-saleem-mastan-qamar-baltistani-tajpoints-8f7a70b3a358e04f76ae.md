1
Ali Ali Haider Saleem Mastan Qamar Baltistani.
Malik-e-Janat Saqi e Kausar Haider Haider Ali Ali Haider.
Sahib-e-Masjid Sahib-e-Mimber Haider Haider Ali Ali Haider.
Maidaan Mein Jab Aaye Haider Antar Wantar Bholay Mantar.
Seene Mein Sab Ki Sansein Atki Haider Ke Jab Badlay Tewar.
Kanpa Marhab Kanpa Antar Kanpay Sare Thar Thar Thar Thar.
Haider Ki Madar Ko Dekha Sehan-e-Haram Ne Rasta Banaya.
Aaein Bibi Aaein Andar Aapke Betay Ka Hai Kaba.
Mera Sharf Hai Shah-e-Najaf Hai Mera Kaba Mera Gohar.
Nemat-e-Uzma Fatima Zahra Jis Pe Ali Kartay They Sajdah.
Zikar-e-Zahra Jab Bhi Aata Khil Uthta Maula Ka Chehra.
Iss Liye Hanstay Jab Bhi Parhtay Inna Ataina Kal Kausar.
Palano Ke Mimber Par Jab Ahmad Ne Paigham Sunaya.
Jis Ne Mujh Ko Maula Mana Oss Oss Ke Hain Haider Maula.
Apne Bolay Haider Haider Ghairon Ko Aate They Chakar.
Haris Pe Jab Aaya Pathar Khof Mein Aaye Haji Aksar.
Sheikh Ka Tola Kanpa Thar Thar Kehte They Aapas Mein Mil Kar.
Bakhin Bakhin Bolo Foran Aa Na Jaye Hum Par Pathar.
Sheikh Ke Dil Mein Hasrat Thi Yeh Deen Ka Parcham Oss Ko Miley Ga.
Bolay Salmaan Khuwab Tha Tera Aa Gaya Parcham Lainay Wala.
Sheikh Yeh Bola Kaun Hai Aaya Bolay Salmaan Foran Barh Kar.
Hijrat Ki Shab Bistar Bola Kaun Hai Mujh Par Aa Kar Soya.
Khushbo Iss Ki Ahmad Jaisi Ahmad Se Hai Milta Julta.
Bolay Farishtay Sare Mil Kar Soye Hoye Hain Nafs-e-Payamber.
Mere Kafan Mein Khak-e-Shifa Thi Aankhon Mein Aanso Ki Lari Thi.
Seene Par Matam Ke Nishaan They Lab Par Mere Nad-e-Ali Thi.
Bolay Farishtay Fazil Tum Tu Le Aaye Ho Qeemati Johar.
