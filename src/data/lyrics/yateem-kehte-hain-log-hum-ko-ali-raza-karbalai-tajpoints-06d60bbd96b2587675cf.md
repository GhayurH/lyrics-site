1
Yateem Kehte Hain Log Hum Ko Ali Raza Karbalai.
Kiya Yeh Zainab Ne Ro Ke Noha Yateem Kehte Hain Loag Hum Ko.
Kahan Sidharay Ho Aao Baba Yateem Kehte Hain Loag Hum Ko.
Nikal Ke Turbat Se Aao Amma Humein Galay Se Lagao Amma.
Utha Hai Sar Se Padar Ka Saya Yateem Kehte Hain Loag Hum Ko.
Ali Ko Zahra Ke Baad Mara Ali Ke Bachay Hain Be Sahara.
Ghar Aisa Ujra Hai Mustafa Ka Yateem Kehte Hain Loag Hum Ko.
Galay Se Ik Dosrey Ke Lag Kar Ali-o-Zahra Ko Roein Dono.
Kaha Jo Zainab Ne Amma Fizza Yateem Kehte Hain Loag Hum Ko.
Janaza Maula Ka Utha Jis Dam Kiya Yeh Zainab Ne Ro Ke Matam.
Kafan Mein Baba Hai Tera Chehra Yateem Kehte Hain Loag Hum Ko.
Kuch Apne Bachoon Se Bolo Baba Kafan Se Banhein Tu Kholo Baba.
Galay Se Hum Ko Laga Lo Baba Yateem Kehte Hain Loag Hum Ko.
Janaza Jab Baba Ka Utha Tha Woh Waqt Zainab Ko Yaad Aaya.
Yeh Boli Kofay Mein Jab Sakina Yateem Kehte Hain Loag Hum Ko.
Ali Ki Maiyyat Pe Ro Ke Boli Asad Yeh Aoulad Fatima Ki.
Keh Chain Sab Laut Kar Hamara Yateem Kehte Hain Loag Hum Ko.
