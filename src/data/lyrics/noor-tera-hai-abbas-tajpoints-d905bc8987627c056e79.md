1
Noor Tera Hai Abbas Syed Mudassir Mehdi Syed Askari Hasan Zaidi.
Sare Aalam Mein Woh Ik Noor Tera Hai Abbas.
Sheh Ki Taat Mein Jo Sajday Mein Raha Hai Abbas.
Aik ALLAH Ki Ik Teri Ada Hai Abbas.
Yeh Jo Hathoon Ke Bina Jood-o-Atta Hai Abbas.
Pochiye Madar-e-Hasnain Se Kya Hai Abbas.
Ibn-e-Zahra Hai Min Al-Ahl-e-Kisa Hai Abbas.
Mustafa Ko Jo Muyassar Hai Atta Ki Sorat.
Tu Ossi Fatima Zahra Ki Dua Hai Abbas.
Sare Aalam Ka Jo Raziq Hai Yadullah Wohi.
Aarzu Mein Teri Mashgool-e-Dua Hai Abbas.
Jis Ke Hasnain Se Betay Hain Woh Mangay Hai Tujhe.
Chashm-e-Zahra Mein Khuda Jane Tu Kya Hai Abbas.
Mehar-o-Aysar Ka Mabood Hai Masjood-e-Wafa.
Sheh Ki Taat Mein Khudai Se Bara Hai Abbas.
Karbala Walay Muhammad Ki Hifazat Ke Liye.
Tu Ali Ban Ke Zamane Se Lara Hai Abbas.
Tera Hona Tu Hai Ghairat Ki Zamanat Ghazi.
Haq Hai Kehna Keh Tu Zainab Ki Rida Hai Abbas.
Chah Kar Bhi Na Mita Paye Gi Dunya Mujh Ko.
Tere Qadmo Mein Jo Nokar Ki Baqa Hai Abbas.
Sheh Ne Di Beti Sakina Ki Kaneezi Ke Liye.
Matami Beta Mujhe Tujh Se Mila Hai Abbas.
Dair Tak Zair-e-Alam Rehta Hoon Mehway Sajdah.
Yeh Meri Maa Ki Sharafat Ka Pata Hai Abbas.
Chomta Hoon Main Alam Matam-e-Zanjeer Ke Baad.
Matami Hoon Main Marey Khoon Mein Wafa Hai Abbas.
Yeh Iram Our Mudassir Yeh Teri Madah-o-Sana.
Tera Sadqa Hai Yeh Teri Hi Atta Hai Abbas.
