1
Madine Mein Aseeron Ka Hai Lut Kar Qafila Aaya.
Hoi Had Hai Yeh Ghurbat Ki Keh Sughra Ne Na Pehchana.
Khari Thi Rah Mein Sughra Nigahein Soye Rasta Thin.
Duaein Lab Pe Jari Thin Adaein Gham Ki Gehri Thin.
Kaha Abid Ne Ro Kar Yeh Tera Bhai Hoon Main Sughra.
Galay Se Jab Lagi Behna Tu Dono Phoot Kar Roye.
Gham-e-Shabbir Ki Batein Lagay Karne Sabhi Roye.
Labon Par Dard Tha Itna Falak Ne Bhi Kiya Giriya.
Kaha Sajjad Ne Behna Bohat Hum Ko Sataya Hai.
Kabhi Darbar Tha Dekha Kabhi Kocha Phiraya Hai.
Badan Par Zulm Ka Dekho Nishaan Ab Bhi Mere Gehra.
Sakina Par Hoi Barish Tamachon Ki Mere Hotey.
Our Oss Ke Cheentay Gohar Woh Roti Thi Mere Hotey.
Baha Tha Khon Kano Se Main Ne Chup Chaap Dekha Tha.
Kaha Sughra Ne Phir Ro Kar Kahan Baba Hamarey Hain.
Kahan Akbar, Kahan Asghar Kahan Qasim Dularay Hain.
Liya Jab Naam Ghazi Ka Tu Dil Par Gham Niya Toota.
Kaha Sajjad Ne Behna Na Pocho Haal Maqtal Ka.
Howa Qurban Hai Dein Par Dadi Zahra Ka Har Pyara.
Phophi Amma Ka Ummat Ne Sitam Se Pardah Tha Loota.
Baqiya Mein Sanagar Sab Gaye Zahra Ke Marqad Par.
Parha Noha Wahan Sagar, Maula Sajjad Ne Ro Kar.
Udasi Chayi Qabron Par Howa Mahool Matam Ka.
