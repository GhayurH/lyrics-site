1
Zehra Hasan Hussain Ki Parwardigar Hai.
Kya Manzalat Hai Martaba Kya Shandar Hai.
Zahra Hasan Hussain Ki Parwardigar Hai.
Ho Ke Boland Dekhti Hai Masjid-e-Nabi.
Aye Bi Bibi Tera Kitna Muqadas Mazar Hai.
Uthein Na Kyun Rasool Bhi Tazeem Ke Liye.
Zahra Ke Gird Noor-e-Khuda Ka Hisar Hai.
Quran Aap Ghoor Se Parh Kar Tu Dekhiye.
Zahra Ki Manqabat Hai Bari Shandar Hai.
Chaki Chala Ke Yeh Hi Chalati Hain Kainat.
Inn Ki Kaneez Gardish-e-Lail-o-Nihar Hai.
Masnad Pe Baithay Kanp Rahe Hain Fidak Ke Choor.
Khutba Hai Yeh Batool Ka Ya Zulfiqar Hai.
Jis Ne Sataya Fatima Bint-e-Rasool Ko.
Lanat Oss Aadmi Pe Kaho Be Shumar Hai.
Bint-e-Nabi Ki Shan Main Kaisay Biyaan Karon.
Sajdah Bhi Inn Ke Samne Sajdah Guzar Hai.
Chalti Hai Sirf Aal-e-Muhammad Ke Hukam Se.
Woh Kashti Jis Pe Nooh Ki Kashti Sawar Hai.
Haan Haan Aray Munafiqon Yeh Hi Tu Hai Nifaq.
Beti Se Bugz Tum Ko Muhammad Se Pyar Hai.
Kehte Hain Jis Ko:GOHAR-E-Nayab Asal Mein.
Zarrah Najaf Ki Khak Ka Woh Khaksaar Hai.
