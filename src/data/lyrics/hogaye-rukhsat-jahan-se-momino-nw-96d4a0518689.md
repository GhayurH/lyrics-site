Hogaye rukhsat jahan se momino Mola Naqi ع
Aaj phir sadaat ke ghar hai saff-e-matam bichi

Kat gayi ro ke jawani Sammara ki qaid mein
Yaad kar kar ke aseeri Sayed-e-Sajjad ع ki

Sar mein hai khaak-e-yateemi kartay hain matam Hassan ع
Zehar se Baba ki zindaan mein shahadat hogayi

Gulshane Aal-e-Nabi par hai udaasi chah gayi
Agaya zad mein fiza ki hai Gul-e-Baagh-e-Taqi ع

Pee liya jamay shahadat ay meray dasway Imam
Baksh di Islam ko yun aap ne hai zindagi

Ho Naqi ع ke qatilon par har ghari Allah ki lanat
Ban gaye Mola ع ke qatil hashr tak hai lanti

Yun uthi Jawad mayyit Shah ki pardes mein
Khud qaza taboot par hai askh barsaati rahi
