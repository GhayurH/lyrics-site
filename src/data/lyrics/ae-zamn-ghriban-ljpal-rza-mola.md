1
Haey Raza Mola Raza
Aey zaaminay ghareeban, Lajpal Raza mola
Uthkay kafan cho waikho, haey,  sada haal Raza mola

2
1) Edhi chaiti kanu paiyan mola Raza judaiyan
Lutiyan nay badshaiyan hogayiyan nay tabaiyan
Baghdad day qaidi da, haye,  haye laal Raza mola

3
2) Syed nun zehar milya dil qaidiyan da haliya
Babay iyan jo chaliya maarkay zindan chon waliya
Abid tay Musa Kazim, haye,  bay haal Raza mola

4
3) Chaiyan mout diyan chawan bhena wakhdiyan rawan
Aj ron layi bulawan Karbal gaiyan jo maawan
Bhena da vichora eh, haye,  teray naal Raza mola

5
4) Syed di qurbani hay khoon di rawani
Karbal waali di rani kha mout gayi jawani
Duniya tun turr paiya eh, haye,  bay misaal Raza mola

6
5) Eh dardan day khazanay Adil nohay bananay
Harfan yun ho na janay mola tu yun likhanay
Meray vain nun paray ga, haye,  haar saal Raza mola
