1
Yasrab Mein Ho Rahi Hai Tauheen Fatima Ki.
Ghurbat Pe Hans Rahe Hain Be Deen Fatima Ki.
Zakhmo Se Choor Hai Woh Itni Sitam Zadah Hai.
Logo Nabi Ki Beti Pehle Hi Ghamzada Hai.
Kyun Aah Le Rahe Ho Ghamgeen Fatima Ki.
Har Beti Ki Nazar Mein Woh Cheez Qeemati Hai.
Tohfay Mein Oss Ke Baba Se Jo Ossay Mili Hai.
Aye Kalma Go Wirasat Mat Cheen Fatima Ki.
Maiyyat Meri Pe Maula Saya Paray Na Unn Ka.
Meri Qabar Ka Nishaan Bhi Kuch Iss Tarha Mitana.
Turbat Na Dekh Paein Woh Teen Fatima Ki.
Aankhon Mein Ashk Lab Pe Tajeel Ki Dua Thi.
Mehdi Ka Naam Le Kar Rotay Rahe Ali Bhi.
Jis Dam Parhi Ali Ne Talqeen Fatima Ki.
Ahl-e-Azza Na Kam Hoon Matam Ki Yeh Sadaein.
Rukney Na Paein Aanso Tham Jaein Na Yeh Aahein.
Hai Hashar Tak Inhi Mein Taskeen Fatima Ki.
Sachi Hai Iss Qadar Yeh Hota Hai Jo Bhi Keh De.
Keh De Tu Anbiya Sab Aaein Gawahi Deney.
Taqdeer Ko Badal De Aameen Fatima Ki.
 Muhsin Inhi Sawalon Mein Amar-e-Haq Nihaan Hai.
Kis Ne Hai Dar Jalaya Kyun Qabar Be Nishaan Hai.
Kyun Raat Mein Hoi Hai Tadfeen Fatima Ki.
