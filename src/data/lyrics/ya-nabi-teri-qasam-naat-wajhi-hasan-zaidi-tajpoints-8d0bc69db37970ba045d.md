1
Ya Nabi Teri Qasam Naat Wajhi Hasan Zaidi.
Aap Ke Aane Se Iss Tarha Muqadar Chamkay.
Chan Aflak Pe Jis Tarha Ubhar Kar Chamkay.
Bas Nabi Ke Rukh-e-Anwar Ka Khiyal Aata Hai.
Chandni Raat Mein Jab Mah-e-Munawar Chamkay.
Aap Ke Paon Se Mas Ho Ke Rasool-e-Akram.
Ba-Khuda Sang Bhi Soraj Ke Barabar Chamkay.
Noor Se Tere Jo Takhleeq Howay Barah Qamar.
Ya Nabi Teri Qasam Teri Nehaj Par Chamkay.
Hum Chamak Sakte Nahi Aise Sitaron Ne Kaha.
Jaise Aflak-e-Shahadat Pe Bahatar Chamkay.
Jin Cheragon Mein Thi Ishq-e-Asadullah Ki Lau.
Mojza Dekhiye Tofan Ki Zad Par Chamkay.
Ishq-e-Haider Ke Ujalon Ka Asar Tha Warna.
Koan Mesum Ki Tarha Daar Pe Aa Kar Chamkay.
Ghair Mumkin Hai Jahan Hona Ujala Yasir.
Naqsh-e-Matam Meri Oss Qabar Ke Andar Chamkay.
