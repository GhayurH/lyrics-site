Pehlu Bhi Shikasta Hay Turbat Bhi Shikasta Hay
Kya Haal Yeh Ummat Nay, Zahra Ka Banaaya Hay.

1
Khatoon Koyi Ghum Say, Yun Churr Nayhi Dekhi
Aisi Koyi Duniya May, Mastoor Nayhi Dekhi
Athaara Baras May Hi, Lagti Jo Zaeefa Hay.

2
Darbaar May Zaalim Nay, Ki Aisi Pazeerayi
Kis Tarha Say Tu Bibi, Phir Laut Kay Ghaar Aayi
Baalon Ki Safaydi Nay, Saab Haal Sunaaya Hay.

3
Tu Roti Rayhi Ghaar May, Haider Say Bhi Chup Chup Kay
Ismat Ki Tarha Dukh Bhi, Parday May Rahay Teray
Kuch Dard Teray Bibi, Bus Jaanti Fizza Hay.

4
Do Aisay Janaazay Hay, Taarikh May Jo Ut Thay
Bus Ghaar Kay Hi Logo Nay, Dono Ko Diye Kaanday
Ek Fatima Zahra Hay, Ek Baali Sakina Hay.

5
Marham Teray Zakhmon Ka, Bibi Na Mila Ab Tak
Aulaad-e-umayya Ki, Baaqi Hay Jafaa Ab Tak
Ronay Peh Bhi Pehra Tah, Turbat Peh Bhi Pehra Hay.

6
Mismaar Tera Roza, Ummat Nay Kiya Jab Say
Baba Tera Rehta Hay, Turbat Peh Teri Tab Say
Kaab Ghumbad-e-Khizra May, Baba Tera Rehta Hay.

7
Mehdi Say Koyi Puchay, Kya Ous Peh Guzarti Hay
Awaaz Baqiayya Say, Jab Ronay Ki Aati Hay
Aankhon Say Lahu Rokar, Woh Bhi Yehi Kehta Hay.

8
Tasveer Haqikat Ki, Khwaabon Ko Banadijiye
Ai Bibi Taqallum Ko, Taahbir Dikhadijiye
Matam Teri Turbat Peh, Hotay Howay Dekha Hay.
