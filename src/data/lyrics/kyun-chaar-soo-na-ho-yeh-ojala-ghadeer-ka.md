Kyun Chaar Soo Na Ho Yeh Ojala Ghadeer Ka
Din Hai Dino Mein Sab Se Yeh Aala Ghadeer Ka

1
Jashan-e-Ghadeer Kaisay Manaein Gay Sheikh Jee
Kartay Faqat Halali Hain Charcha Ghadeer Ka
Hum Ne Lagaye Jaam Wilaye Ali Ke Aaj
Our Kuch Gharon Mein Aaj Hai Pursa Ghadeer Ka

2
Lakhon Kitabein Parh Le Magar Meri Nazar Mein
Be Aqal Hai Jo Na Ho Dewana Ghadeer Ka
Teeron Ki Tarha Seeno Mein Ayelan Ja Laga
Kya Khoob Tha Na Waisay Nishana Ghadeer Ka

3
Woh Keh Rahe Hain Unn Ke Barey Aaj Mar Gaye
Yani Keh Kha Gaya Unhein Sadma Ghadeer Ka
Aye Kash:ASAD AZMI: Maula Ali Kahin
Sun Rakha Hai Yeh Main Ne Qaseeda Ghadeer Ka
