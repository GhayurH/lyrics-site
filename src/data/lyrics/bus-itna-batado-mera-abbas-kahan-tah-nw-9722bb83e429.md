Bus Itna Batado Mera Abbas Kahan Tah

Jab Kafila Pohuncha Tah Madinay Kay Zara Paas

Maqtal May Howa Kya Yeh Suna Usnay Basad Yaas

Kat-ta Tha Jigar Dil May Nidamath Ka Tah Ehsaas

Haar Ek Say Yehi Poochti Ti Madar-e-abbas

Jab Gardan-e-Shabbir Peh  Khanjar Woh Rawan Tah

Bus Itna Batado Mera Abbas Kahan Tah.

Zahra Kay Pisar Janay Nabi Syed-e-wali

Akbar Kay Kalejay Say Sina Kaisay Nikali

Jab Khaak Peh Dum Tour-ta  Laila Ka Jawan Tah.

Maidan May Baysheer Ko Jab Laye Tay Shabbir

Naanay Ali Asghar Kay Jo Gardan Peh Laga Teer

Jab Baap Kay Hathon Pay  Pisar Tashna-dahan Tah.

Aqa Mera Maidan May Kyun Rehgaya Tanha

Haa Doodh Na Bakhshungi May Ye Kya Kiya Beta

Zahra Ka Pisar Khaak Peh  Jab Girya Kuna Tah.

Jab Bibiyan Jaltay Howay Qaimon Kay Ti Undar

Jab Zainab-e-dilgeer Ki Cheeni Gayi Chadar

Jab Gardan-e-beemar May  Woh Tauqe Giran Tah.

Abid Ko Liye Phirti Ti Jab Zainab-e-muztar

Jab Aag Lagayi Gayi Cheena Gaya Bistar

Phaila Howa Jab Shaam-e  Ghariban Ka Dhuan Tah.

Jab Shaam-e-ghariban May Bhi Pyaase Rahay Bachay

Jab Dukhtare Shabbir Kay Lagtay Tay Tamachay

Jab Uska Jigar Karbay  Yateemi Seh Tabah Tah.

Zainab Nay Kaha Usko Wafa Ka Tha Bahot Khawas

Jab Zibha Howay Bhai Tho Zindan Na Thay Abbas

Bin Haton Kay Tha Ghairatay Ilyaas Ka Laasha

Saahil Peh Tadapta Raha Abbas Ka Laasha.

Ye Sun Ke Kiya Shukr Magar Phir Bhi Hai Ehsas

Ab Tak Yeh Qamar Poochti Hai Madar-e-abbas

Jab Gardan-e-Shabbir Peh Khanjar Woh Rawan Tah.
