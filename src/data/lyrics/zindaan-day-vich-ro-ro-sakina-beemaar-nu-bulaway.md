Zindaan day vich ro ro Sakina beemaar nu bulaway
Kaleyaan qaid di kothi vich darni aan main veeraa kithay shimr naa aa javay

1
Sajjad jadon qaidaan mukk jaan Karbal jaaveen
Asghar di qabar tay jaa kay tu hayee pani uss tay paaveen
Nalay kavi behn nu tera enjj paani mangna din raat sataway

2
Roun nayi denda mainu zalim shimar kamina
Kehnda chup kar jaa qaidaniya tenu aap karawan Sakina
Maar tamachay moo tay zindaan day vich mainu kayi vaar daraway

3
Lash Sakina di loko Sajjad jadon chaayi
Tak khoon kanna chon jaari Baqir eh denda duhaayi
Ess haal ich kiwen loko mera Baba ujri nu kiwen dafnaway

4
Qaidi behn diyan tu sun lay veer sadaawan
Phuppi Zainab nu vi aakh deveen gayian muk hun meriyan sahwaan
Enna qaidaan nay Abid mainu maar mukaaya Masooma eh kurlaway
