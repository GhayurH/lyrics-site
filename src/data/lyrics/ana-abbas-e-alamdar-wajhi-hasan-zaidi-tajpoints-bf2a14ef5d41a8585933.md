1
Izn Le Kar Chala Nehar Ko Bawafa.
Ghaiz Mein Aa Ke Yeh Dushmano Se Kaha.
Abbas Abbas Abbas Aa Rahe Hain.
Sun Le Aye Foaj-e-Sitamdar Ana Abbas-e-Alamdar.

2
Chosti Si Mashk Mein Darya Ko Sama Sakta Hoon.
Taig Ki Gharmi Se Lashkar Ko Jala Sakta Hoon.
Ghaiz Mein Hai Meri Talwar Ana Abbas-e-Alamdar.
Mujh Ko Baba Ne Sikhai Hai Mere Taig Zani.

3
Main Bhi Hoon Misal-e-Ali Taig Chalane Mein Dhani.
Baap Ki Tarha Hoon Karrar Ana Abbas-e-Alamdar.
Hukam Hai Mujh Ko Faqat Pyason Ki Saqqai Ka.
Aik Ishara Jo Mile Mujh Ko Mere Bhai Ka.

4
Kar Doon Nu Lakh Ko Filnar Ana Abbas e Alamdar.
Meri Talwar Ki Gharm Se Pighal Jao Gaye.
Sar Pata Kar Abhi Maidaan Se Nikal Jao Gaye.
Gar Karon Main Abhi Yalgar Ana Abbas e Alamdar.

5
Itni Raftar Se Dushman Ko Qaza Doon Ga Main.
Malak Almaut Ki Bhi Sans Charha Doon Ga Main.
Main Hoon Jarraron Ka Jarrar Ana Abbas e Alamdar.
Haan Main Iss Mashk Mein Le Jaon Ga Bhar Kar Darya.

6
Aa Ke Ab Rasta Mera Jo Koi Roke Ga.
Ho Ga Woh Maut Se Do Char Ana Abbas e Alamdar.
Mera Andaz-e-Waga Sara Arab Jane Hain.
Dekh Loon Ghaiz Se Jis Ko Woh Ghazab Jane Hai.

7
Jeena Kar Deta Hoon Dushwar Ana Abbas e Alamdar.
Aye:WAJHI ZILL-E-RAZA: Ne Yeh Rajaz Khoob Likha.
Sun Ke Qirtas Ke Mathe Pe Paseena Aaya.
Sakhi Abbas ki Takrar Ana Abbas e Alamdar.
