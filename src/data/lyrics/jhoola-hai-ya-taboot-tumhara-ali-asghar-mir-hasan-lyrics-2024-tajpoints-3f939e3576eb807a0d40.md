Jhoola Hai Ya Taboot Tumhara Ali Asghar Mir Hasan Mir.
Madar Ke Labon Par Hai Yeh Noha Ali Asghar.

1
Pehlu Mein Mere Aaj Se Soye Gi Sakina.
Tum Baap Ki Aagosh Mein Sona Ali Asghar.
Sar Sare Shaheedon Ke Uthay Nezon Pe Lekin.
Utha Tera Nezay Pe Janaza Ali Asghar.

2
Oss Waqt Sakina Bhi Khari Dekh Rahi Thi.
Aray Nezon Ne Jo Dhonda Tera Lasha Ali Asghar.
Iss Tarha Se Rukhsar Tere Khon Mein Tar They.
Maa Dekh Na Payi Tera Chehra Ali Asghar.

3
Mohsin Ka Inhein Dijiyo Pursa Mere Beta.
Jab Gaud Mein Lein Gi Tujhe Zahra Ali Asghar.
Layi Hoon Teri Qabar Se Maula Ko Utha Kar.
Uthtey Hi Nahi They Shah e Wala Ali Asghar.

4
Akbar Na Sula Paein Gay Ke Tum Zid Nahi Karna.
Seene Ka Abhi Zakhm Hai Taza Ali Asghar.
Maa Boli Takallum Mujhe Marey Gaye Pathar.
Ummat Ne Diya Youn Tera Pursa Ali Asghar.
