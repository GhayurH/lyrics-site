Kazim Ki Zanjeerain Qaiser Abbas Lyrics
Taboot Se Kazim Ki Zanjeerein Latakti Hain.
Jo Jism Ke Hison Ko Kha Kha Ke Guzarti Hain.
Tabdeel Kiye Kitne Zindan Hain Qaidi Ke.
Aaram Nahi Pal Bhi Diya Zehar Hai Zalim Ne.
Zindan Mein Kazim Ki Ab Sansein Ulajhti Hain.
Baghdad Ke Pul Par Hain Be Goor-o-Kafan Kazim.
Ghurbat Pe Karein Jin Ki Yeh Arz-o-Samaa Matam.
Burqay Mein Koi Bibi Lashay Pe Sisakti Hain.
Aa Kar Do Raza Kandah Tadfeen Ko Aa Jao.
Aye Wali-e-Mashhad Tum Baghdad Chaley Aao.
Hai Dhop Bohat Maula Zanjeerein Bhi Tapti Hain.
Masroof-e-Ibadat Our Masroof-e-Azza Reh Kar.
Tableeg-e-Khuda Ki Hai Zindaan Ke Sitam Seh Kar.
Kazim Ki Sabhi Wasfein Sajjad Se Milti Hain.
Aao Aye Azadaro Dein Fatima Ko Pursa.
Mara Hai Laeeno Ne Be Jurm-o-Khata Maula.
Yeh Halqa-e-Matam Se Aawazein Ubharti Hain.
Shab-o-Roz Karein Giriya Halaat Pe Kazim Ke.
 Qaiser Bhi Parhey Noha Halaat Pe Kazim Ke.
Likh Likh Ke Ghulam Aankhein Haye Gham Se Barasti Hain.
