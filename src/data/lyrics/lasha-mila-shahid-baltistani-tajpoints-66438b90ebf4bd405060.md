Sheh Ko Youn Abbas Ka Bikhra Howa Lasha Mila.
Do Buredah Bazoon Ke Sath Mashkeza Mila.

1
Bibiyon Mein Wa Hijaba Ka Ajab Kuhram Tha.
Rastay Mein Bawafa Ka Jab Kata Shana Mila.
Hath Maltay Reh Gaye Shabeer-o-Zainab Dasht Mein.
Aik Gathri Mein Simat Kar Maa Ko Jab Dulha Mila.

2
Jis Ka Jee Chaha Sakina Ko Tamachay Marta.
Youn Musalmano Se Oss Ko Baap Ka Pursa Mila.
Mothiyaan Kholi Gayin Bano Ki Jab Marne Ke Baad.
Raakh Jholay Ki Mili Our Khoon Bhara Kurta Mila.

3
Boli Zainab Qaid Khane Ki Ghutan Apni Jagah.
Shukar Hai Syedaniyo Ko Kam Se Kam Pardah Mila.
Be Basi Se Gir Gaye Shabeer Jalti Rait Par.
Lasha-e-Akbar Pe Jis Dam Qasid-e-Sughra Mila.

4
Uth Gaye Tazeem Mein Umm Ul-Baneen Ke Lal Ki.
Naam Le Kar Koi Abid Se Jo Ghazi Ka Mila.
Hum Ne Bas Darwaish Mangi Marzi-e-Maula Hussain.
Warna Dar Se Bawafa Ke Jis Ne Jo Manga Mila.
