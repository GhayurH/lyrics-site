1
Galay Mila Na Kabhi Chand Bakht Aysa Tha.
Hara Bhara Badan Apna Darakht Aysa Tha.
Sitare Siskiyaan Bharte The oss Roti Thi.
Fasan-e-Jigar-e-Lakht Aysa Tha.

2
Zara Na Moom Howa Pyare Ki Hararat Se.
Chatakh Ke Toot Giya Dil Ka Sakht Aysa Tha.
Yeh Our Baat Keh Wo Lab The Phool Se Nazuk.
Koi Na Seh Sakay Lehja Karkht Aysa Tha.

3
Kahan Ki Sair Na Ki Tawasan-e-Takhiyul Par.
Humein Tu Yeh Bhi Sulemaan Ke Takht Aysa Tha.
Idhar Se Guzra Tha Mulk-e-Sukhan Ka Shehzadah.
Koi Na Jaan Saka Sazo Rakht Aysa Tha.
