1
Muslim Hussain Ka Wajhi Hasan Zaidi.
Mara Gaya Safeer-e-Hussain-e-Ghareeb Hai.
Ab Sibt e Mustafa Ki Shahadat Qareeb Hai.
Kofay Mein Qatal Ho Gaya Muslim Hussain Ka.

2
Gardan Kati Tu Pyasa Tha Muslim Hussain Ka.
Koi Mere Imam Ko Ja Kar Karey Khabar.
Kofay Ka Turk Kijiye Sibt e Nabi Safar.
Har Aik Se Yeh Kehta Tha Muslim Hussain Ka.

3
Pathar Barastay Dekh Ke Kehte They Al-Amaan.
Maula Ke Sath Aaeingay Ahl-e-Haram Yahan.
Yeh Baat Keh Ke Ro Para Muslim Hussain Ka.
Jalad Ne Qalam Jo Kiya Sar Ghareeb Ka.

4
Phailaye Dono Hathoon Ko Bethi Thin Syeda.
Dar-Al-Amara Se Gira Muslim Hussain Ka.
Pardais Mein Woh Totay Musayab Ghareeb Par.
Sab Martay They Ahl-e-Sitam Ghair Ghair Kar.

5
Zakhmo Se Choor Choor Tha Muslim Hussain Ka.
Zakhmi Dahan Ka Khon Gira Jam-e-Aab Mein.
Pani Piya Na Samne Pa Kar Janab Ne.
Pyasa Hi Qatal Ho Gaya Muslim Hussain Ka.

6
Pardais Mein Bichar Gaye Mazloom Ke Pisar.
Maloom Tha Keh Khon Ke Pyasay Hain Ahl-e-Shar.
Bachoon Ko Dhondta Raha Muslim Hussain Ka.
Hay Hay Qareeb-e-Eid Ruqaiya Ka Ghar Luta.

7
Rotay Hain Door Kofa Se Abbas-e-Bawafa.
Ab Dushmano Mein Ghir Gaya Muslim Hussain Ka.
Muslim Ki Betiyon Ko Laga Kar Galay Ke Sath.
Kehte They Shah-e-Deen Yeh Rakh Rakh Ke Sar Pe Hath.

8
Koi Kahey Na Mar Gaya Muslim Hussain Ka.
Har Ik Simat Ke Zulm Hasan Jis Mein Aa Gaya.
Yeh Qatal Hai Kitab-e-Shahadat Ki Fateha.
Pori Hai Aik Karbala Muslim Hussain Ka.
