1
Shimr Khanjar Na Chala Mir Hasan Mir.
Shimar Ne Gardan-e-Mazloom Pe Khanjar Jo Rakha.
Kuch Qadam Door Se Deti Rahi Zainab Yeh Sada.
Aakhri Bar Behan Chom Le Bhai Ka Galla Shimar Khanjar Na Chala.
Joor Kar Hath Yeh Kehti Rahi Bint-e-Zahra Shimar Khanjar Na Chala.
Teer Jee Bhar Ke Issay Mar Chuka Hai Lashkar.
Barchiyaan Pehlu Mein Tooti Hain Sitam Our Na Kar.
Khud Hi Aa Jaye Gi Kuch Dair Mein Bhai Ko Qaza.
Aakhri Mujh Se Mulaqaat Ossay Karni Hai.
Aisa Lagta Hai Koi Baat Ossay Karni Hai.
Itni Hasrat Se Mujhe Dekh Raha Hai Bhaiyya.
Minattein Karti Hoon Zalim Teri Aisa Tu Na Kar.
Asar Ke Waqt Mere Ghar Mein Andhera Tu Na Kar.
Sham Se Pehle Bhojata Hai Charag-e-Zahra.
Yeh Dil-o-Jan-e-Rasool-e-Arbi Hai Zalim.
Sath Mazloom Ke Yeh Be Adabi Hai Zalim.
Misal-e-Quran Hai Yeh Zanu Tu Seene Se Hata.
Sun Ke Hal Min Ki Sada Aayi Hai Ran Mein Bachi.
Chal Na Jaye Kahin Dono Pe Laeen Teri Churi.
Dekh Bhai Ke Gallay Par Hai Sakina Ka Galla.
Kaat Kar Sham Hi Le Jane Hain Sar Tujh Ko Agar.
Phair Le Aoun-o-Muhammad Ke Galon Par Khanjar.
Mere Bhaiyya Ke Na Kar Jism Se Tu Sar Ko Juda.
Dekh Sakta Nahi Tu Jo Nazar Aata Hai Mujhe.
Wasta Deti Hoon Tatheer Ki Aayat Ka Tujhe.
Hath Amma Ne Hai Shabeer Ki Gardan Pe Rakha.
Kaat Kar Sajday Mein Akbar Sheh-e-Wala Ka Galla.
Shimar Hansta Howa Shabeer Ke Seena Se Utha.
Gonjti Reh Gayi Maqtal Mein Yeh Zainab Ki Sada.
