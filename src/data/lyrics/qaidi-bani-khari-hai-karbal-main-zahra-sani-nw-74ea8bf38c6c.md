Qaidi Bani Khari Hai Karbal Main Zahra Sani.

Bhoolai Gi Kaisai Zainab Haye, Adaz Nabhi Jo Ki Hai Ummat Nai Merhbani.

Paband-e-Rasaan Zainab Chadar Bhi Nahi Ser Pai
Tuti Hai Qiyamat Yeh Zahra Kai Bahrai Ghar Main
Bazu Bandhai Huwai Hain Haye, Haider Ki Baitiyon Kai, Hai Shaam Ko Ravaani.

Karbal Na Gayi Bibi, Bethi Hun Mediney Main
Hai Barchi Lagi Kaisai, Terai Bahi Kai Seenai Main
Maar Jayai Gi Sughra To Haye, Zainab Nai Jab Sunayi, Asghar Ki Yeh Kahani.

Zahra Bhi Dua Deti, Mola Sai Ajar Milta
Is Kaam Kai Sadqai Main Firdos Main Ghaar Milta
Karbal Main Musalmano Haye, Do Boond Pila Dehtay, Asghar Ko Agaar Paani.

Qaidi Beemar Syed, Ankhon Sai Lahu Jari
Zainab Ki Aseeri Pai, Roya Hai Raat Sari
Bhoola Na Maart-e-Dam Tak Haye, Kaar Kaar Kai Yaad Roya, Akbar Ki Nojawani.
