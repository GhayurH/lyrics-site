Tujhe Jhoola Jhulaon, Main Tujhe Jhoola Jhulaon

Main Khwab Main Asghar Tujhe
Lori Bhi Sunaon

Laut Aao Safar Say Kay Bari Dair Hui Hay
Baytab Hoon Kab Say Tujhay
Seenay Say Lagaoun

Koi Mujhay Batlata Nahi Teri Nishani
Barsaat Main Teeron Ki
Kahan Dhoondnay Jaoun

Socha Tha Teri Salgira Ghar Main Karoungi
Takdeer Yeh Kehti Hai
Tera Sog Manaoun

Baba Say Tere Tera Janaza Na Uthay Ga
Ruk Ja Zara Abbas Ko
Dariya Say Bulaon
