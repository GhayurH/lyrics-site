Jhanrr Gaye Abbas Ghazi Laeeq Raza.

1
Jhanr Gaye Abbas Ghazi Rul Gayi Aal-e-Abba.
Ghin Gaye Naizay Laha Ke Bint-e-Haider Di Rida.
Dora.
Ayho Hai Dastoor Jo Ghorey Taun Aswar Jadaan V Lahanday.
Taan Taik Banra Ke Bazuaan Koon Oh Zeen Taun Khak Te Aahnday.
Jadaan Bazu Qalam Karaye Ghazi Kul Manr Muka Syedaan De.

2
Jhanr Munh De Bhar Abbas Piya Rahe Bhainr Bhira Kurlanday.
Rab Gawah Abbas Munh De Bhar Jhanriya Khak Te.
Zeen Taun Maqtal De Wich Jaan Leh Piya Bazu Kata.
Khak Di Chadar Pawa Shah Aakhiya Humsheer Koon.
Hunr Taiday Parday Di Zamin Banr Gayi Khak-e-Shifa.
Kar Badan Abbas Da Tukray Shaqi Aahday Rahe.

3
Hayi Bahon Shabeer Taikun Manr Ayein Abbas Da.
Chum Ke Bazu Veer De Humsheer Jhanr Payi Khak Te.
Dendi Reh Gayi Sainr Ujari Wa Hijaba Di Sada.
Dhi Koon Ro Shabeer Aakhay Mashk Khali Aa Gayi.
Mashki Chacha Taida Darya Te Pyasa Sam Gaya.
Tere Dehna Di Pyas Hai Jath Baal Pyasay Mar Gaye.

4
Hayi Jinna Khaimiyaan Uttay Pehra Sakhi Abbas Da.
Baad Ghazi De Riya Shabeer Da Koi Nahi.
Tahoon Shimar Khanjar Chalenda Aye Hath Ainday Zulfaan Ich Pa.
Dora.
Wich Karbal Walay Maqtal De Dhi Milanr Jo Piyo Koon Gayi Hai.
Kath Jhanriya Aye Aasra Dukhiyaan Da Fojaan Taun Puchdi Rayi Hai.

5
Ditha Khanjar Jo Chalda Babay Te Gal Gal Te Rakhdi Payi Hai.
Maliya Ayho Jiya Khums Tamachiyaan Da Masooma Zakhmi Thayi Hai.
Dhi Milanr Babay Koon Gayi Hai Kha Tamachay Jhanr Payi Hai.
Rahi Satendi Chacha Ghazi Aa Maida Baba Bacha.
Thi Gayi Hai Laeeq Qayam Di Nigah Bahlool Te.
Kar Ditta Abbas Ghazi Da Taikun Noha Atta.
