1
Koi Ja Ke Mere Veer Raza Nu Aye Paigham Pochaway.
Beemar Aye Bhain Teri Tere Hijar De Wich Chan Veran Kitay Wichri Mar Na Jaway.
Aa Mera Veer Raza Bhain Payi Mangdi Dua Mere Muk Jan Na Sah.
Tainu Jendiyaan Wekh Lawaan Ayho Meri Hasrat Mainu Gal Veran Aan Laway.

2
Dukh Nayi Bhulna Aye Bhira Gham Ayeh Jhalna Main Bhira Taun Kadon Walna Bhira.
Dhi Mosa Kazim Di Munh Mashhad De Wal Kar Ke Ro Ro Ke Wain Paway.
Ruli Sehrawan De Wich Okhiyaan Rahwaan De Wich Aakhri Sahwaan De Wich.
Arman Jay Mar Jawaan Khud Lehad Na Veran Mera Meri Maiyyat Aa Lahaway.

3
Tainu Aayi Main Millan Choriya Veer Watan Hona Qum Wich Main Dafan.
Tera Mashhad Wasda Rahway Kehway Parh Ke Innalillah Tere Rutbay Rab Wadhaway.
Pursa Ayeh Maula Raza Hai Masoma Qum Da Mangda Sajjad Dua.
Tusaan Bhain Bhirawaan De Jannat Wargay Ayeh Rozay Har Ik Nu Rab Wakhway.
