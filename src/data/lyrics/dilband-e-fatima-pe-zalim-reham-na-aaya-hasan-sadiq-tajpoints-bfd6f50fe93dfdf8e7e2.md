1
Dilband e Fatima Pe Zalim Reham Na Aaya Hasan Sadiq.
Mara Gaya Tashna Dahan Lasha Para Tha Be Kafan.
Dil Band-e-Fatima Pe Zalim Reham Na Aaya.
Tashna Labi Mein Tu Ne Mehmaan Ka Khoon Bahaya.

2
Be Goor-o-Be Kafan Tha Lasha Shah-e-Zaman Ka.
Sar Nook-e-Naiza Par Hai Darbar-e-Sham Aaya.
Abbas Tum Kahan Ho Lo Khaimay Jal Rahe Hain.
Be Pardah Bibiyon Pe Hai Aasmaan Ka Saya.

3
Asghar Jo Be Zabaan Tha Lekin Tha Khon-e-Haider.
Hurmal Ne Teer Khaincha Masoom Muskuraya.
Bazar-e-Sham Mein Jab Aayi Ali Ki Beti.
Tofan Pathron Ka Charon Taraf Se Aaya.

4
Quran Parhne Walay Woh Kaisay Musalmaan They.
Tasveer-e-Fatima Ko Darbar Mein Bulaya.
 Gulzar Karbala Mein Aaye Quran Walay.
Afsoos Be Kasoon Ko Pani Mila Na Saya
