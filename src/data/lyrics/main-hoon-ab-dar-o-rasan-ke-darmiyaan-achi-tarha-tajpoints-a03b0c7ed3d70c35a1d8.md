Main Hoon Ab Dar-o-Rasan Ke Darmiyaan Achi Tarha.
Seekh Li Hai Meesum Ki Zabaan Achi Tarha.

1
Tab Kahin Ja Kar Kumail-o-Buzar-o-Salmaan Bane.
Ishq-e-Haider Le Chuka Jab Imtehaan Achi Tarha.
Ho Chuki Tameer Kabe Ki Tu Khaliq Ne Kaha.
Dekh Lijiye Ya Ali Apna Makaan Achi Tarha.

2
Warna Bad-e-Mustafa Kar Do Gaye Kuch Rad-o-Badal.
Aaj Hi Salman Se Sun Lo Azaan Achi Tarha.
Meri Turbat Mein Farishtoon Ne Yeh Aapas Mein Kaha.
Dekh Lo Seene Pe Matam Ke Nishaan Achi Tarha.

3
Aye Farishto Fatima Ke Ghar Ki Yeh Kherat Hai.
Bandh Lo Daman Mein Tum Yeh Rotiyaan Achi Tarha.
Mere Bare Mein Lahad Ko Tha Yeh Hukam-e-Abuturab.
Rooz-e-Mehshar Tak Isse Rakhna Yahan Achi Tarha.

4
Teer Kha Kar Muskurane Se Yeh Sabit Ho Giya.
Maqsad-e-Sheh Janta Tha Be-Zabaan Achi Tarha.
Khud Ba Khud Qismat Ka Oss Ki Faisla Ho Ga Sarosh
Hur Agar Sun Le Ga Akbar Ki Azaan Achi Tarha.
