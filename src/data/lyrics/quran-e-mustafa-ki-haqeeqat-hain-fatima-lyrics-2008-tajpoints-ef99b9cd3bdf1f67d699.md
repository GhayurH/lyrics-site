1
Quran e Mustafa Ki Haqeeqat Hain Fatima.
Mairaj Mein Jo Utari Woh Aayat Hain Fatima.
Hasnain Maa Ke Qadmoo Mein Baithe Hain Iss Liye.
Janat Ke Shahzadoon Ki Janat Hain Fatima.

2
Hain Aalmeen Ke Liye Rehmat Rasool-e-Pak.
Our Khud Rasool Ke Liye Rehmat Hain Fatima.
Zoaf-e-Rasool Ki Hai Dawa Chadar-e-Batool.
Goya Rasool-e-Pak Ki Taqat Hain Fatima.

3
Haider Ka Naam Kyun Na Janab-e-Ameer Ho.
Haider Ki Sab Se Qeemati Dolat Hain Fatima.
Zahra Ko Aata Dekh Ke Uthte Hain Mustafa.
ALLAH Ke Nabi Ki Ibadat Hain Fatima.

4
Haider Ki Ja Nimaz Hai Aanchal Batool Ka.
Sajday Hain Murtaza Ke Zamanat Hain Fatima.
 Saqlain Sare Shia Dua-e-Batool Hain.
Khaliq Khuda Hai Bais-e-Khilqat Hain Fatima.
