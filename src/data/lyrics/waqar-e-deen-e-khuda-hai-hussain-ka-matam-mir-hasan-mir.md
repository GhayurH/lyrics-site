Waqar-e-Deen-e-Khuda Hai Hussain Ka Matam Mir Hasan Mir
Payamberon Ne Kiya Hai Hussain Ka Matam

1
Zamana Lakh Mukhalif Ho Ruk Nahi Sakta
Keh Fatima Ki Dua Hai Hussain Ka Matam
Yazid Iss Se Bari Our Kya Hai Teri Shikast
Keh Tere Ghar Mein Bapa Hai Hussain Ka Matam

2
Khuda Rasool Farishtay Imam Sab Hain Wahan
Jahan Kahin Bhi Bapa Hai Hussain Ka Matam
Khabar Nahi Hai Yeh Fatway Farosh Mufti Ko
Ali Ke Ghar Se Chala Hai Hussain Ka Matam

3
Kissi Bhi Ghair Ke Dar Par Woh Kaisay Phailay Ga
Jo Hath Karta Raha Hai Hussain Ka Matam
Aye Bint-e-Fatima Zahra Teri Atta Ke Tofail
Humein Naseeb Howa Hai Hussain Ka Matam

4
Baroz-e-Hashar Khabar Lein Gay Oss Ki Peygamber
Jisay Khabar Nahi Kya Hai Hussain Ka Matam
Wilaye Aal-e-Nabi Ki Tarha Se:MIR HASAN:
Mere Lahu Mein Mila Hai Hussain Ka Matam
