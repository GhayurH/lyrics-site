1
Pardes Che Dafan Hoye Haye Logo Bhain Bhira.
Mar Bhain Gayi Aye Qum Wich Te Mashhad Veer Raza.
1- Chad Watan Madina Toon Mashhad Nu Wasaya Aye.
Tere Baad Na Sukh Da Sah Ujari Nu Aaya Aye.

2
Nahin Veran Seh Sakdi Main Roog Judai Da.
2- Mazloom Imam Raza Mashhad Wich Tanha Aye.
Mil Zehar Gayi Haye Ayeh Ghareeb Ul Guraba Aye.
Aayi Lash Te Rowan Layi Chad Baqiya Maa Zahra.

3
3- Pardais Che Main Kala Bari Door Madina Aye.
Meri Jan Da Ayeh Dushman Mamoon Kamina Aye.
Aiday Zehar Muka Chadney Ne Main Pardesi De Sah.
4- Safraan Wich Rul Jana Mere Babay Di Jayi.

4
Asaan Jendiyaan Nahin Milna Kehya Maula Bhain Meri.
Chad Veer Gaya Aye Dunya Milsi Qum Wich Itela.
5- Gayiyaan Wichar Bhirawaan Taun Tin Bhaina Dunya Te.
Ik Zainab Ik Sughra Ik Dhi Kazim Di Aye.

5
Gayiyaan Rondiyaan Dunya Taun Hoye Ainj De Veer Juda.
6- Kitay Qatal Musalmana Ne Zahra De Jaye Ne.
Sajad Iss Ummat Ne Barey Zulm Kamaye Ne.
Baghdad Kadi Mashhad Wich Kadi Yasrab Karb-o-Bala.
