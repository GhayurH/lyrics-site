Jo Bhi Masroof-e-Salam-e-Shuhada Rehta Hay Nadeem Sarwar.
Go Woh Rehta Nahi Par Naam Sada Rehta Hai.

1
Aye Falak Bad-e-Fana Katay Gaye Dast-e-Hussain.
Ik Na Ik Zulm Tere Ghar Mein Niya Rehta Hai.
Shimar Kehta Tha Yehi Maa Hai Ali Akbar Ki.
Jis Ka Ik Hath Kalejay Pe Dhara Rehta Hai.

2
Hai Yeh Sharmindagi Pani Ke Na Pohchane Ki.
Naizay Pe Bhi Sar-e-Abbas Jhuka Rehta Hain.
Sham Hoti Hai Tu Ontoon Se Utartay Hain Haram.
Par Sar-e-Shah Tu Naizay Pe Charha Rehta Hai.

3
Ro Ke Yeh Hind Ki Beti Ne Sakina Se Kaha.
Sar Tera Kis Liye Har Waqt Khula Rehta Hai.
Woh Lagi Kehne Ke Yateemo Ki Nishani Hai Yehi.
Kurta Be Warsi Bachoon Ka Phata Rehta Hai.

4
Baap Mara Gaya Bhai Moye Zindaan Mein Phansi.
Iss Musibat Mein Bhala Hosh Baja Rehta Hai.
Tauf-e-Kaba Ka Tujhey Shoq Hai Uz Bas Keh Dabeer.
Muztarib Dil Sifat-e-Qibla Numa Rehta Hai.
