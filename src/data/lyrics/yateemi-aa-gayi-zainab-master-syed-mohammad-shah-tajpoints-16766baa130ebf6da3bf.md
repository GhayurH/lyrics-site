1
Yateemi Aa Gayi Zainab Master Syed Mohammad Shah.
Pukarein Fatima Zahra Yateemi Aa Gayi Zainab.
Na Raas Aaya Tujhe Kofa Yateemi Aa Gayi Zainab.
Abhi Qaidi Nahi Hai Tu Hai Iss Kofe Ki Shahzadi.
Nahi Hai Aaj Kofe Mein Tere Rone Pe Pabandi.
Yahan Jee Bhar Ke Ro Lena Yateemi Aa Gayi Zainab.
Nahi Reh Payi Turbat Mein Madina Choor Aayi Hoon.
Bhula Ke Zakhm Apne Main Baqaiya Choor Aayi Hoon.
Ali Ka Zakhm Hai Gehra Yateemi Aa Gayi Zainab.
Bahatar Zakhm Hain Tu Ne Jo Karbal Mein Uthane Hain.
Magar Kofe Mein Ummat Ne Bohat Badlay Chukane Hain.
Yeh Pehla Zakhm Hai In Ka Yateemi Aa Gai Zainab.
Qayamat Thi Qayamat Jab Ali Masjid Se Ghar Laye.
Pidar Ko Khon Mein Tar Jis Ghari Le Kar Pisar Aaye.
Pukarein Dekh Kar Fiza Yateemi Aa Gai Zainab.
Manaein Gaye Sabhi Khushiyaan Magar Tu Gham Manaye Gi.
Tere Ghar Soog Le Kar Hi Hameesha EID Aaye Gi.
Yehi Tohfa Hai Ummat Ka Yateemi Aa Gai Zainab.
Tere Dar Par Yeh Dastak Hai Kissi Sham-e-Ghareeban Ki.
Ridaein Cheenne Ki Mashq Karte Hain Musalmaan Abhi.
Ali Ka Cheen Kar Saya Yateemi Aa Gai Zainab.
Pidar Ko Dekh Kar Zakhmi Bohat Dilgeer Thi Zainab.
Nazar Baba Ke Chehre Par Pari Tu Ro Pari Zainab.
Laho Se Yeh Likha Dekha Yateemi Aa Gai Zainab.
Bapa Kuhram Muhsin Oss Ghari Dewar-o-Dar Roye.
Ali Ki Betiyaan Roein Haram Roye Pisar Roye.
Ali Ne Jab Ye Farmaya Yateemi Aa Gai Zainab.
