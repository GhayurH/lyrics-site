1
Jandi Wari Babay Ne Aye Sughra Nu Farmaya.
Kehra Teer Sahey Ga Gardan Te Jay Asghar Naal Na Aaya.
Toon Jandi Ayein Meri Manzil Aye Asghar Di Qabar Banani.
Jis Hasrat De Nal Babay Ne Kal Mohsin Nu Dafnaya.

2
Seene Te Sawawan Ga Ohno Jay Asghar Mainu Morin.
Main Aj Tak Sirf Sakina Nu La Seene Naal Sulaya.
Nanay De Deen Nu Dena Aye Main Che Mah Da Nazrana.
Mere Naal Rubab Ne Aj Taein Koi Hoor Sawal Nayi Paya.

3
Toon Maut Di Akh Wich Akh Pa Ke Hasna Aye Mere Bachray.
Har Roz Rubab Ne Asghar Nu Aye Jang Da Sabaq Sikhaya.
 Akbar Rab Jane Babay Ne Ki Keeti Aye Sargoshi.
Asghar, Sughra Di Jholi Choon Babay Di Jholi Aaya.
