Kehti Thi Zainab Haye Hussaina Nadeem Sarwar.
Ho Gayi Barbad Yeh Behna Karb-o-Bala Mein.

1
Karbala Kuch Na Diya Karb-o-Aziyat Ke Siwa.
Mere Daman Mein Bacha Kya Hai Musibat Ke Siwa.
Karbala Tujh Se Mila Kya Hai Qayamat Ke Siwa.
Subh-e-Ashor Azan-e-Ali Akbar Ki Qasam.

2
Mere Bhai Pe Chaley Teer Hazaron Paihum.
Chup Rahi Karb-o-Bala Hotey Rahe Hum Pe Sitam.
Mera Atharah Baras Wala Jiyala Akbar.
Kha Gayi Oss Ko Bhi Aye Karb-o-Bala Teri Nazar.

3
Main Ne Dekha Hai Ossi Lal Ka Barchi Mein Jigar.
Bhai Atharah They Do Betay They Koi Na Raha.
Ab Tu Tanhai Meri Deti Hai Mujh Ko Pursa.
Kya Bhara Ghar Tha Mera Kar Diya Mujh Ko Tanha.

4
Mere Abbas Pe Kya Guzri Bataon Kaisay.
Mashk Ik Bharne Main Do Bazu Qalam Hokay Girey.
Oss Ke Marne Se Hai Mehroom Behan Chadar Se.
Be Khata Qatal Howa Bhai Mera Jangal Mein.

5
Sainkaron Zakhm Kalejay Pe Lagay Ik Pal Mein.
Aag Pani Mein Laga Di Hai Mere Aanchal Mein.
Mere ALLAH Mujhe Sham Talak Jana Hai.
Koi Bhi Apna Nahi Shehar Bhi Begana Hai.

6
Teri Khushnodi Mein Rassi Mein Mera Shana Hai.
Ta Qayamat Rahe Rehan Aza-e- Sarwar.
Khon-e-Dil Behta Rahe Aankh Se Aanso Ban Kar.
Yeh Sada Kano Se Takrati Hai Ab Bhi Aksar.
