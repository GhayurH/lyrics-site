1
Haye jab Madinay se Chala qafla karbobala
Tha ye Sughra ke laboon par nawha
Ley chalo baba, mujhe ley chalo baba
Apne hamraah suwe karbo bala
Ley chalo baba, mujhe ley chalo baba

2
1. Apki yaad me ro ro ke mei marjawungi
Baba Akbar ki kasam chain nahi pawungi
Meri sanson ka bharosa na raha
Mujhe ley chalo baba

3
2. Kaflay walo ko pani bhi pilawungi may
Apni aghosh me asghar ko sulawungi may
Baba ye apse wadaa hei mera
Mujhe ley chalo baba

4
3. Mere kano me jo awaaz azaan ayengi
Mere Akbar ki mujhe yaad bohot ayengi
Bhayi ki yaad na ban jaye qazaa
Mujhe ley chalo baba

5
4. Kaise Akbar ke bina eid guzarungi may
Kabhi tumko kabhi amu ko pukaryngi may
Jab koi paas na honga baba
Mujhe ley chalo baba

6
5. Dhoop aur garm hawa hongi safar me dekho
Apne aachal me chupalungi ali asghar ko
Yu na Asghar ko karo mujse judaa
Mujhe ley chalo baba

7
6. Khwab Akbar ki urusi ke bohot dekhe thay
Khaak hojayenge ay baba sab armaan mere
Dil yahi soch ke rota hai mera
Mujhe ley chalo baba

8
7. Chacha abbas aur sakina phuphi Zainab se
Aakhri baar mila do muje baba sab se
Phir ye bimar nahi dengi sadaa
Mujhe ley chalo baba

9
8. Bhayya Abid ko dawaa roz pilawungi may
Unki raho me nigaaho ko bichawungi may
Tum kanizo ki tarha behr khudaa
Mujhe ley chalo baba

10
9. Acha mehmil me mujhe tumna bithaana baba
Tum muje raah me peydal hi chalaana baba
Na karungi me shikayat na gilaa
Mujhe ley chalo baba

11
10. May ne manaa ke nahi kaam mera manzar may
Baba itna to batado ke akele ghar may
Zindaa rehpayengi Sughra tanha
Apne hamrah suwe karbobala ley chalo baba, mujhe ley chalo baba
