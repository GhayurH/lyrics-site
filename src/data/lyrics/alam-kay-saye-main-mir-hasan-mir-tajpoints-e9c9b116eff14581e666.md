Alam Kay Saye Main Mir Hasan Mir Lyrics
Aye Alam Walay.

1
Oncha Rahe Apna Alam Jab Tak Bhi Hai Seeno Mein Dam.
Sajta Rahe Barhta Rahe Oncha Rahe Alam Abbas Ka Alam.
Alam Ke Saye Mein Abbas Ke Hubdar Baithay Hain.
Kahin Salman-o-Abuzar Our Kahin Mukhtar Baithay Hain.

2
Zabaan Mein Aaj Meri Ik Anokhi Si Rawani Hai.
Bohat Nazdeek Shayad Meesum-e-Tammar Baithay Hain.
Padar Se Mothiyaan Abbas Ki Jholay Mein Kehti Hain.
Abhi Se Karbala Jane Ko Hum Teyar Baithay Hain.

3
Mah-e-Shaban Hai Maula Pila Jam-e-Wila Hum Ko.
Mere Saqi Teri Chokhat Pe Sab Maykhar Baithay Hain.
Alam Bardari e Abbas Ki Taqreeb Jari Hai.
Abu Talib Ke Ghar Sare Alam Bardar Baithay Hain.

4
Uthay Matam Ki Khatir Our Majlis Ke Liye Baithay.
Na Hum Bekar Uthay They Na Hum Bekar Baithay Hain.
Gayin Hain Sham-o-Kofa Dekhney Abbas Ki Behnein.
Kahan Chup Kar Hamarey Shair Se Farrar Baithay Hain.

5
Tamasha Kar Rahi Hai Maut Maidaan Mein Kharey Ho Kar.
Badan Par Sham Walon Ke Youn Tere Waar Baithay Hain.
Fazail Par Ali Maula Ke Ruk Kar Mustafa Bolay.
Kabhi Phir Pochna Salman Abhi Agyar Baithay Hain.

6
Adab Se Naam Par Abbas Ke Ta Zindagi Abid.
Hazaron Bar Uthay Hain Hazaron Bar Baithay Hain.
Ali Asghar Ka Sadqa Hai Dilon Mein Ahl-e-Batil Ke.
 Takallum Teer Ki Sorat Tere Ashar Baithay Hain.
