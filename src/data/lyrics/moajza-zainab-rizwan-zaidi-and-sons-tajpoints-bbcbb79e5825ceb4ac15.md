1
Moajza Zainab Rizwan Zaidi and Sons.
Faqat Dikhaya Hai Tu Ne Yeh Mojza Zainab.
Kahin Ali Hai Kahin Par Hai Fatima Zainab.
Biyan Kaisay Ho Shehzadi Teri Shan Tujhe.
Khuda Ke Chehray Ne Khud Fakhar Se Kaha Zainab.
Paye Madad Tere Atharah Bhai Aa Pohanchay.
Jo Mushkilon Mein Kabhi Main Ne Keh Diya Zainab.
Tu Oss Mehal Ki Makeen Hai Qasam Taharat Ki.
Jahan Pe Rehti Hai Pehrey Pe Innama Zainab.
Jo Munkiran e Shah e La Fata Hain Dunya Mein.
Unhein Karey Gi Zamane Se La Pata Zainab.
Likha Hai Khak Pe Bait, Phir Oss Ko Pairon Se.
Masal Ke Khak Mein Tu Ne Mila Diya Zainab.
Andherey Sham Ke Jis Ne Mita Deiye Yaksar.
Khuda Ke Dein Ko Woh Tu Ne Diya Diya Zainab.
Biyan Shan Teri Koi Kar Nahi Sakta.
Tera Ghulam Shujat Ka Hai Khuda Zainab.
Jahan Mein Matami Sar Youn Utha Ke Chalta Hai.
Tumhare Dar Pe Jo Sar Ko Jhuka Diya Zainab.
Baqa-e-Sajdah Shabeer Ki Jo Baat Chali.
Jabeen Ne Chom Liye Tere Naqsh-e-Pa Zainab.
Sand Yeh Kafi Hai Bibi Teri Sakhawat Par.
Qasiday Parhti Hai Khud Tere Hal Atta Zainab.
Salam Karti Hai Tujh Ko Namaz Yeh Keh Kar.
Azza Ko Tu Ne Ibadat Bana Diya Zainab.
Yeh Ayhteram Yeh Haibat Ajeeb Hai Bibi.
Zaban Kanp Gayi Jab Kabhi Kaha Zainab.
Imam Hotey Hoye Iss Se Mashwara Le Kar.
Hussain Ne Yeh Bataya Hai Alima Zainab.
Yazid Kanp Gaya Our Jhoom Utha Khuda.
Tere Khitab Ka Lehja Kamal Tha Zainab.
Duaein Deta Hai Ghazi Bhi Tere Ashhar Ko.
Kiye Hain Shair Woh Tu Ne Issay Atta Zainab.
