Woh Waqt Yaqeenan Ayega Mir Hasan Mir.
Kabhi Aye Haqeeqat-e-Muntazir Nazar Aa Libas-e-Majaz Mein.

1
Keh Hazaron Sajday Tarap Rahe Hain Meri Jabeen-e-Niyaz Mein.
(Allama Dr Muhammad Iqbal)
Jab Hujat-e-Door Ke Hathoon Mein Ghazi Ka Alam Lehraye Ga.
Jis Din Sajjad Ke Ashkon Ki Tadad Batai Jaye Gi.

2
Our Unn Ashkon Ke Badlay Mein Talwar Chalai Jaye Gi.
Oss Sar Se Jism Juda Ho Ga Talwar Se Jo Takraye Ga.
Har Ik Mazloom Ki Aas Hai Yeh Hujjat Ka Shams Talu Ho Ga.
Phir Sham Jigar Se Utray Gi Sajjad Ka Khatam Raku Ho Ga.

3
Phir Noor-e-Aman Youn Phailay Ga Har Zulm Ko Rondha Jaye Ga.
Dadi Ka Haq Woh Mangay Ga Apni Talwar Ke Sath Kabhi.
Jo Hath Ridaein Lotatay Hain Kat Jaein Gay Woh Hath Sabhi.
Tatheer Ke Ghar Ki Sorat Phir Koi Ghar Na Loota Jaye Ga.

4
Aabad Baqiya Tha Jis Ja Woh Roza Kaisay Toota Hai.
Kis Kis Ne Yahan Kaisay Kaisay Sadaat Ke Ghar Ko Loota Hai.
Talwar Batati Jaye Gi Woh Khon Bahata Jaye Ga.
Bagi Ki Beti Jis Ne Bhi Choti Bibi Ko Bola Tha.

5
Namoos-e-Nabi Ko Rahoon Mein Durron Se Jis Ne Mara Tha.
Unn Sab Ko Bana Kar Qaidi Woh Bazar-e-Sham Mein Laye Ga.
Phir Ahl-e-Bait Madine Mein Aabad Karein Gay Ghar Apna.
Our Lein Gay Bani Hashim Mil Kar Kuch Parday Daroon Ka Badlah.

6
Abbas Ki Chup Bhi Totay Gi Be Sheer Bhi Taig Uthaye Ga.
 Gohar Mulkon Ki Sarhad Se Insaan Taqseem Nahi Ho Ga.
Haan Naam Nehad Mazahib Mein Quraan Taqseem Nahi Ho Ga.
Woh Adil Sari Dunya Ko Ik Adal Ka Shehar Banaye Ga.
