Mazloom Naqi Di Phori Te Sir Pitdi Zahra Aayi Aye.
Iss Gham Wich Rowanr Layi Sare Masoom V Naal Leyai Aye.

1
Wich Qaid De Rakh Ke V Zalim Din Raat Naqi Nu Marday Ray.
Daday Kazim Wangoon Maula Kayi Saal Di Qaid Nibhai Aye.
Aye Zulm Naqi Nu Qabar De Wich Rat Abid Waang Rawaway Ga.
Haye Qabar Hussain Pyasay Di Aiday Hondiyaan Zalimaan Dhayi Aye.

2
Chalday Si Naqi De Samne Jad Darbar Ich Jaam Sharab Walay.
Haye Zainab Keh Ke Sochda Si Kinj Seh Gayi Zahra Jayi Aye.
Haye Pak Naqi Nu Ghorey Nal Ainj Hakim-e-Waqt Bhajanda Si.
Shabbir Di Dhi Jinj Ghoriyaan Nal Haye Shimar Laeen Bhajayi Aye.

3
Haye Ghurbat Chaya Ghar Wich E Babay Da Janaza Askari Ne.
Te Maiyyat Piyo Di Putar Ne Khud Wich Ghar De E Dafnai Aye.
Tatheer Mizaj Imam Kithay Darbar Da Najis Mahool Kithay.
Aoulad-e-Nabi Har Door Ich Kyun Lokaan Darbar Bulayi Aye.
