1
Hasan Askari Ka Main Adna Gada Hoon.
Main Nam-e-Hasan Askari Pe Fida Hoon.
Ali Ke Hasan Ka Suna Kar Qaseeda.
Ali Ya Ali Ki Lagata Sada Hoon.

2
Jinhein Bugz Hai Tere Baba Se Qayam.
Woh Dono Jahan Mein Hi Jaltay Rahein Gay.
Tumharey Mohibbon Ki Kya Baat Maula.
Har Ik Matami Ka Main Nokar Sada Hoon.

3
Tumhari Shujat Se Aye Mere Maula.
Bohat Khof Khata Tha Dushman Tumhara.
Ossay Yeh Khabar Thi Hai Farzand-e-Haider.
Yeh Madde Moqabil Main Jis Ke Khara Hoon.

4
Imamat Pe Jis Ki Yeh Dunya Hai Qayam.
Hai Beta Tumhara Woh Hadi-e-Bar Haq.
Zahoor-e-Imam-e-Zamana Ho Ya Rab.
Subha Sham Main Yeh Dua Kar Raha Hoon.

5
Gadaye Hasan Main Hoon Mehdi Ki Raiyyat.
Mujhe Ghair Se Koi Matlab Nahi Hai.
Tu Inn Nisbaton Ki Badolat Jahan Mein.
Main Sab Se Alag Hoon Main Sab Se Juda Hoon.

6
Karam Mujh Pe Hai Isqadar Askari Ka.
Keh Jab Jab Bhi Main Ne Qalam Hai Uthaya.
Mujhe Rizq Milta Gaya Phir Sukhan Ka.
Qaseeda Main Hasnain Likhta Gaya Hoon.
