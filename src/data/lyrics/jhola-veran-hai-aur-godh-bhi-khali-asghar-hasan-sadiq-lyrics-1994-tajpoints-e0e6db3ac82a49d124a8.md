Jhola Veran Hai Aur Godh Bhi Khali Asghar Hasan Sadiq.
Maa Ko Batla De Kahan Basti Basa Li Asghar.

1
Sar Pe Chadar Jo Na Thi Maa Ne Kafan Ki Sorat.
Khak Maqtal Ki Teri Lash Pe Dali Asghar.
Mar Kar Teer Tabasum Ka Sitamgaron Ko.
Aabro Deen-e-Muhammad Ki Bacha Li Asghar.

2
Bhool Jaye Tera Gham Bali Sakina Kaisay.
Teri Sorat Hai Kahan Bholney Wali Asghar.
Baab-e-Khaybar Jo Uthatay Tu Na Hoti Diqqat.
Lash Sarwar Ne Teri Kaisay Utha Li Asghar.

3
Maut Ghabra Gayi Mathay Pe Paseena Aaya.
Maut Ki Aankh Mein Youn Aankh Hai Dali Asghar.
Kaisay Ho Jaye Kissi Dar Ka Bhikari Rehan.
Hai Azal Se Yeh Tere Dar Ka Sawali Asghar.
