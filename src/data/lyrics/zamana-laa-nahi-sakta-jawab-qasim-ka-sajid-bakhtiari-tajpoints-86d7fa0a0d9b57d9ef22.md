1
Zamana Laa Nahi Sakta Jawab Qasim Ka Sajid Bakhtiari.
Moheet-e-Husan-e-Hasan Hai Shabab Qasim Ka.
Tajali Dekh Ke Sajday Mein Gir Parin Yousuf.
Hata Dein Farwa Jo Rukh Se Naqab Qasim Ka.

2
Kiya Hussain Ke ALLAH Ne Ba Shakal-e-Hasan.
Hai Karbala Ke Liye Intehab Qasim Ka.
Chali Hasan Ke Qalam Jaisi Taig Qasim Ki.
Har Aik Waar Raha Kamyab Qasim Ka.

3
Gharoob Iss Liye Hota Hai Karbala Ki Taraf.
Hai Jata Chomney Dar Aaftab Qasim Ka.
Jinna Mein Satwein Shabaan Ko Jashan-e-Qasim Mein.
Hai Anbiya Ke Labon Par Nisab Qasim Ka.

4
Samajh Ke Naat Parha Hai Qabool Kar Lijay.
Qaseeda Main Ne Risalat Maab Qasim Ka.
 Asad Yeh Bikhrey Hoye Gulbadan Se Raaz Khula.
Hai Do Jahan Pe Karam Be Hisab Qasim Ka.
