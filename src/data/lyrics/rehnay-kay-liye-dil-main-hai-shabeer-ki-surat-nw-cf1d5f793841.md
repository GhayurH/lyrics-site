Rehnay Kay Liye Dil Main Hai Shabeer Ki Surat

Aaeena Badal Tha Nahin, Tasveer Ki Surat

Hasnain-o-Nabi Hai Dar-o-Zehra Hain Kisa Main
Tafseer Kay Paykar Main Hain, Tatheer Ki Surat

Qurbani-e-Shabeer Khaleel Aap Bhi Dekhayn
Yun Khwab Ko Mil Jati Hai, Tabeer Ki Surat

Kiya Jhaptay Hain Dushman Pay Habib-ibn-e-mazahir
Jo Mislay Kama Thay Vo Gaye Teer Ki Surat

Ab Aur Na Duniya Main Khaleel Aur Na Shabeer
Har Khwab Dikhata Nahi Tabeer Ki Surat

Hal Min Ki Sadaon Pay Machal Thay Thay Jo Asghar
Maa Dekh Kay Rehjati Thi Baysheer Ki Surat

Jab Aaya Hay Sajjad Kay Qadmon Ka Tasawur
Ashkon Main Uthar Ayee Hai Zanjeer Ki Surat

Batil Ki Fizaoon Main Wasi Haq Kay Fidaee
Hur Ban Kay Badal Daytay Hain Taqdeer Ki Surat
