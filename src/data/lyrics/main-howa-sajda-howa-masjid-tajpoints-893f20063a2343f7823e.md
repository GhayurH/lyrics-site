1
Har Koi Hai Aap Ke Dedar Ko Tarsa Howa.
Dekhta Rehta Hoon Hathoon Ki Lakeerein Raat Din.
Aap Ka Dedar Ho Shayad Kahin Likha Howa.
Aaiye Our Kun Ba Izni Keh Ke Zindah Kijiye.
Muntazir Hoon Zindagi Ki Qabar Mein Soya Howa.
Woh Mujasam Muntazir Our Main Adhora Intezar.
Al-Ajal Kehte Hoye Main Youn Bhi Sharminda Howa.
Sun Raha Hoon Aaein Gay Abbas Bhi Sath Aap Ke.
Kab Se Hai Mera Tasawar Bas Yahin Thehra Howa.
Toor-e-Kaba Par Tera Dedar Karne Ke Liye.
Sar Ke Bal Aaon Ga Maula Main Agar Zindah Howa.
Az Muhammad Ta Muhammad Dastakhat Sab Ne Kiye.
Sare Ghar Ke Faislay Se Faisla Hur Ka Howa.
Hal Atta Ki Aayatein Parh Kar Kiya Hai Khud Ko Sair.
Jab Kabhi Akbar Ali Walon Ke Ghar Faqa Howa
