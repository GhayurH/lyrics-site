1
Haye Maar Dita Lokaan Baba Mera Bhira Write Up
Sain Ghazi Di Wain Paway Karbal Wich Baaj Rida.
Aye Waqat Kisay Te Na Aaway Jera Aj Karbal Wich Aaya.
Ik Sang Te Meri Chadar Si Aik Sang Te Amari Jaya.
Main Odday Sir Nu Takdi Rayi Oh Chadar Takda Riya.
Main Zeen Te Aap Sawar Kita Wagaan Kulsoom Ne Phariyaan.
Jithay Veer Khare San Kal Taein Othay Teriyaan Nowaan Khariyaa.
Aakhri Welay Khaimiyaan Choon Aynj Turya Veer Mera.
Kiwein Noor Gawa Ke Akhiyaan Da Akbar Akbar Karda Si.
Kiwein Lash Te Pohanchiya Akbar Di Kiwein Digda Si Uthda Si.
Thori Pehlaan Jay Tu Aanda Aye Manzar Khud Takda.
Pawein Sar Ke Khaimay Rakh Hoye Na Fair V Pehra Chadiya.
Main Jandi Saan Toon Aawein Ga Par Main Nayi Tainu Sadiya.
Hosla Rakh Ke Kitay Ne Ghazi De Farz Adda.
Jeray Teeraan Naiziyaan Toon Bach Gaye Oh Ghair Lay San Bawaan.
Bare Okhay Rakh Te Khaimiyaan Di Oh Baal Sawaye Mawaan.
Baal Oh Sare Jag Paye Ne Hurn Aa Ke Aap Sawa.
Nu Lakh Teeraan Talwaaran Ne Odday Badan Te Zarbaan Layiyaan.
Bhar Bhar Ke Jholiyaan Shamiyaan Ne Zakhmaan Te Raitaan Paiyaan.
Lash Te Ghore Bhajday Re Riya Takda Poor Mera.
 Akbar Aye Aakhiya Haider Ne Zainab De Athro Chum Ke.
Naa Haal Suna Mainu Zakhmaa De Main Jarnda Haan Gham Tere.
Naal Zahra De Takda Riya Main Sara Sabar Tera.
