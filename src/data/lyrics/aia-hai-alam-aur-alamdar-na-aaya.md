Aya hay alaam aur alamdaar na aaya
Dariya seh dil-e-Haider-e-Kaarar na aaya

1
1) Ghairat ka taqaza hay alamdaar ki hasti
Bachchi seh sharamsaar wafadaar nah aaya.

2
2) Zalim nay meray gaalon peh maray hain tamachay
Logon mera Abbas chacha jaan na aaya.

3
3) Paani ke liye behja alamdaar ko shah neh
Afsoos musalmaano ko aitbaar na aaya

4
4) Baazu jo katay daaton seh mashkeeza dabaya
Ghoray seh ghira zeen peh aswaar na aaya
