Roodad-e-Mohabbat Kya Kahiye Kuch Yaad Rahi Kuch Bhool Gaye.
Do Din Ki Mussarat Kya Kahiye Kuch Yaad Rahi Kuch Bhool Gaye.

1
Jab Jaam Diya Tha Saqi Ne Jab Door Chala Tha Mehfil Mein.
Wo Hosh Ki Saat Kya Kahiye Kuch Yaad Rahi Kuch Bhool Gaye.
Ab Waqt Ke Nazuk Honton Par Majrooh Tarnum Raqsaan Hai.
Bedaar Mashiyat Kya Kahiye Kuch Yaad Rahi Kuch Bhool Gaye.

2
Ahsaan Ke Maikhane Mein Kahan Aab Fikar-o-Nazar Ki Qandeelein.
Aalaam Ki Shiddat Kya Kahiye Kuch Yaad Rahi Kuch Bhool Gaye.
Kuch Haal Ke Andhe Sathi The Kuch Mazi Ke Ayaar Sajan.
Ahbab Ki Chahat Kya Kahiye Kuch Yaad Rahi Kuch Bhool Gaye.

3
Kantoon Se Bhara Hai Daman-e-Dil Shabnam Se Sulagti Hain Palkein.
Pholon Ki Sakhawat Kya Kahiye Kuch Yaad Rahi Kuch Bhool Gaye.
Aab Apni Haqiqat Bhi Saghar Be Rabt Kahani Lagti Hai.
Dunya Ki Haqiqat Kya Kahiye Kuch Yaad Rahi Kuch Bhool Gaye.
