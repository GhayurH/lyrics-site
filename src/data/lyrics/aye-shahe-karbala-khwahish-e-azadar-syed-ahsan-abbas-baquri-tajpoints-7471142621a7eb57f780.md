1
Aye Shahe Karbala Khwahish e Azadar Syed Ahsan Abbas Baquri.
Aye Shah-e-Karbala Zahra Ka Wasta.
Har Matami Ki Hai Maula Yehi Dua.
Marne Se Pehle Phir Ik Bar Karbala Maula Humein Bola.

2
Dil Ro Rahe Hain Maula Ziyarat Ke Wastay.
Be Chain Hain Yeh Aankhein Ibadat Ke Wastay.
Aankhon Ko Karbala Ka Rasta Dikha Maula Humein Bola.
Pyasa Gala Jahan Par Asghar Ka Kat Gaya.

3
Barchi Se Seena Jis Ja Akbar Ka Phat Gaya.
Dein Aa Ke Oss Jagah Hum Pursa Khon Ka Maula Humein Bola.
Seeno Mein Ruk Na Jaye Maula Hamara Dam.
Matam Tumhari Ghurbat Pe Karne Aaein Hum.

4
Lagtay Nahi Hain Dil Maula Tere Bina Maula Humein Bola.
Bikhrey Paray They Tukray Tan-e-Pash Pash Ke.
Nohay Wahan Parhein Gay Qasim Ki Lash Ke.
Tukron Pe Jis Jagah Roti Thin Fatima Maula Humein Bola.

5
Hasrat Hai Rotay Rotay Mar Jaein Hum Wahan.
Haye Aap Ke Galay Par Khanjar Chala Jahan.
Our Be Kafan Jahan Lasha Tha Aap Ka Maula Humein Bola.
Ghazi Ke Khoon Se Nohay Wafa Ke Raqam Hoye.

6
Jahan Mashk Ko Bachatay Bazu Qalam Hoye.
Jahan Teer Mashk Mein Our Aankh Mein Laga Maula Humein Bola.
Jis Ja Pe Goshwaray Masooma Ke Chiney.
Rukhsaron Par Tamachay Sakina Ko They Lagay.

7
Rukhsar Apne Aa Kar Peetein Gay Oss Jagah Maula Humein Bola.
Ashor Hai Woh Ahsan Ya Arbaeen Hai.
Har Aik Matami Ko Asad Yeh Yaqeen Hai.
Abbas Aik Din Sab Ko Bulaye Ga Maula Humein Bola.
