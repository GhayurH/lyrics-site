1
Mera Mehdi Jab Ayega Shahid Baltistani
Kaha Zahra Ne Turbat Mein Mujhe Tab Chain Aaye Ga.
Baqaiya Ko Woh Zainab Ka Azakhana Banaye Ga.
Na Din Ko Roney Detey They Na Shab Ko Roney Detey They.

2
Kiya Mehdood Bait Al-Huzan Mein Ummat Ne Roney Pe.
Woh Sehray Hizn Aate Hi Madine Ko Banaye Ga.
Yahan Umm Ul-Baneen Ke Sath Soti Hain Meri Madar.
Hai Jalti Dhop Mein Madfan Mera Baqir Mera Jafar.

3
Woh Phir Mismar Qabron Ke Liye Saya Banaye Ga.
Tarap Jata Hai Mehdi Jab Ossay Aawaz Aati Hai.
Woh Bachi Aaj Bhi Abbas Ko Ro Kar Bulati Hai.
Sakina Kab Se Pyasi Hai Ossay Pani Pilaye Ga.

4
Rida-e-Zainab-o-Kulsoom Ke Gham Mein Woh Roya Hai.
Woh Thak Kar Sham Se Aa Kar Mere Pehlu Mein Soya Hai.
Mere Sajjad Ke Ashkon Ka Woh Badla Chukaye Ga.
Lipat Kar Ro Rahi Hai Aaj Bhi Woh Mere Qadmo Se.

5
Hasan Ki Qabar Be Saya Hoi Qasim Howa Tukray.
Jo Guzri Umm-e-Farwa Par Zamane Ko Bataye Ga.
Adhori Reh Gayi Dunya Mein Jo Tasveer Haider Ki.
Na Oss Ne Maa Kaha Mujh Ko Na Khud Main Ne Ziyarat Ki.

6
Yaqeen Hai Meri Mamta Ko Woh Mohsin Ban Ke Aaye Ga.
Dukhi Hoon Apni Turbat Mein Yeh Kaisa Door-e-Hazir Hai.
Azadari Ka Munkir Hai Koi Sajday Ka Munkir.
 Takallum Taig Haider Ki Woh Dono Par Chalaye Ga.
