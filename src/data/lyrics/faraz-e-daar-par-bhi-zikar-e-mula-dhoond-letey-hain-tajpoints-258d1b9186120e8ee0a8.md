Faraz-e-Daar Par Bhi Zikar-e-Mula Dhoond Letey Hain.
Jinhein Janat Bulati Hai Woh Rasta Dhoond Letey Hain.

1
Fazayal Par Ali Ke Lakh Parde Daal De Dunya.
Jo Nukta Sanj Hote Hain Woh Nukta Dhoond Letey Hain.
Faza Jaisi Bhi Ho Lekin Ali Ke Madah Tair.
Agar Behlool Hote Hain Tu Dana Dhoond Letey Hain.

2
Hamare Hath Hain Manoos Itne Daman-e-Haq Se.
Andhere Mein Bhi Hum Nehj-e-Balaga Dhoond Letey Hain.
Hamari Chasham-e-Bina Par Zamana Mehway-e-Hairat Hai.
Bukhari Mein Bhi Hum Man Kunto Mula Dhoond Letey Hain.

3
Yazeedi Patharon Mein Itefaqan Hur Bhi Shamil Tha.
Magar Jo Johari Hote Hain Hera Dhoond Letey Hain.
Khuda Ka Shukar Hai Jarrar Hum Mula Ke Sadqay Mein.
Kahin Bhi Hoon Magar Apna Qabeela Dhoond Letey Hain.
