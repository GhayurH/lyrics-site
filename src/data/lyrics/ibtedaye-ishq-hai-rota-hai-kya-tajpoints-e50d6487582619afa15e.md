Ibtedaye Ishq Hai Rota Hai Kya.
Agay Agay Dekhiye Hota Hai Kya.

1
Qafilay Mein Subha Ke Ik Shoor Hai.
Yani Ghafil Hum Chale Sota Hai Kya.
Sabz Hoti Hi Nahi Yeh Sar Zameen.
Tukham-e-Khowaish Dil Mein Tu Boota Hai Kya.

2
Yeh Nishan-e-Ishq Hain, Jate Nahi.
Daag Chati Ke Abs Dhota Hai Kya.
Gairat-e-Yousaf Hai Yeh Waqt-e-Azeez.
“MIR” Iss Ko Raigaaan Khota Hai Kya.
