1
Sitam Ki Guzri Hai Ik Aisi Raat Zahra Par Write Up
Utha Hai Samne Haider Ke Hath Zahra Par.
Laeen Ne Jaltay Hoye Dar Pe Mari Thi Thokar.
Gira Woh Aan Ke Sholon Ke Sath Zahra Par.

2
Faqat Hansay Hi Nahi Syeda Pe Darbari.
Palattay Reh Gaye Zahra Ki Baat Zahra Par.
Chura Rahi Thi Woh Na Mehramo Se Haider Ko.
Guzarti Reh Gayi Mehshar Ki Raat Zahra Par.

3
Laeen Jo Kehta Tha Zahra Ko Dawa Jhota Hai.
Salami Karti Thi Khaliq Ki Zaat Zahra Par.
Tha Taziyana Tamacha Tha Taig Ka Qabza.
Barastay They Sabhi Akbar Jo Sath Zahra Par.
