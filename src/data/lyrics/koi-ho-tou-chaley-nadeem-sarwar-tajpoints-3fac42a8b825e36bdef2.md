Koi Ho Tou Chaley Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics
Istegasa Hai Sar-e-Daar Koi Ho Tu Chaley.
Aik Pyasay Pe Hai Yalgar Koi Ho Tu Chaley.
Qafila So Gaya Maqtal Mein Sar-e-Sham Sabhi.
Reh Gaya Qafila Salar Koi Ho Tu Chaley.
Aik Beta Hai Jo Gehwara Se Utra Na Kabhi.
Dosra Beta Hai Beemar Koi Ho Tu Chaley.
Khud Ko Jholay Se Gira Detey Hain Jane Walay.
Kaun Aisa Hai Wafadar Koi Ho Tu Chaley.
Reh Gaya Naizon Ke Jangal Mein Musafir Tanha.
Yeh Shahadat Ke Hain Aasaar Koi Ho Tu Chaley.
Lamha Lamha Yeh Sada Deti Hai Ashor Ki Sham.
Beriyaan Hoti Hain Teyar Koi Ho Tu Chaley.
ALLAH ALLAH Yeh Lahu Pehne Hoye Tanhai.
Lut Gayi Kis Ki Yeh Sarkar Koi Ho Tu Chaley.
Yeh Hain Namoos-e-Nabi Khaimay Se Kaisay Niklein.
Kya Karey Sahib-e-Kirdar Koi Ho Tu Chaley.
Jane Yeh Kaun Pas Khaima Hai Giriyaan Giriyaan.
Hai Koi Iss Ka Azadar Koi Ho Tu Chaley.
Iss Tarha Se Dar-e-Zindaan Pe Sakina Roi.
Sath Roye Dar-o-Dewar Koi Ho Tu Chaley.
Kis Ke Lehjay Mein Ali Bool Rahe Hain Logo.
Kaun Bola Sar-e-Darbar Koi Ho Tu Chaley.
Beriyaan Jis Ki Musibat Pe Lahu Roti Hain.
Kaun Aaya Sar-e-Bazar Koi Ho Tu Chaley.
Jo Ho Oss Foaj Mukhalif Ka Sipahi Na Chaley.
Haan Magar Hur Sa Tarafdar Koi Ho Tu Chaley.
Yeh Sada Aaj Bhi Maqtal Se Hilal Aati Hai.
Kaun Haq Ka Hai Talabgar Koi Ho Tu Chaley.
