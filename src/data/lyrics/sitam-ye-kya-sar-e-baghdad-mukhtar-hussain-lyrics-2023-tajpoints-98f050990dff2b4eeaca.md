1
Sitam Ye Kya Sar e Baghdad Mukhtar Hussain Fatehpuri.
Sitam Yeh Kya Sar-e-Baghdad Dhaya Jata Hai.
Janaza Beriyon Mein Bhi Uthaya Jata Hai.
Aseer-e-Sham Ka Pota Hai Qaidi-e-Baghdad.
Ohad Ka Badla Abhi Tak Chukaya Jata Hai.
Musafiron Ke Liye Kya Nahi Hai Qabrastan.
Janaza Kya Kabhi Pul Pe Parhaya Jata Hai.
Hain Naizay Seene Ki Janib Tu Kuch Hain Pusht Ki Simt.
Raku Chodah Baras Se Nibhaya Jata Hai.
Utha Ke Laye Hain Mazdoor Lash Zindaan Se.
Ghareebi Kehte Hain Kis Ko Bataya Jata Hai.
Na Betiyon Hi Ka Baghdad Aana Hai Mumkin.
Na Baap Hi Se Madine Ko Jaya Jata Hai.
Janab-e-Zahra Ke Masoom Bachoon Ko Akbar.
Bata Ke Deen Ke Bagi Sataya Jata Hai.
