Sajjad De Athru Bazaar’ch Aakay Rung Badlaa Gaye

Jadon Shor Machaaya, Hy Shaamiyaan Ne Sung Barsaao

Aj Shaam De Qaidi Aa Gaye

Rondi Si Sakina Naa Lay Lay Ke,

Asghar Da Shaam Savairay,

Yaadon Diyan Pyaasan,Hy Veer Diyan, Rondi Mar Gayi

Enu Dukh Baysheer De Khaa Gaye

Ummat Jo Pooaaye Ali Abid Nay

Zanjeer Bee Maar Samajh Ke

Siro Nangiyan Si Phupiyaan, Hy Ronr Payiaan, Dukh Safraan De

Sajjad Di Kamar Jhuka Gaye

Sughra Te Sakina Jag Duniya Te

Kinj Di Kismat Hai Paayi

Ek Bhain Nu Maari, Hy Shaam Moii, Ek Nu Kalleya

Watna Te Chor Bhira Gaye

Zahra Diyan Jaayian Shaam Wichan Ne

Ro Ro Ke Mangan Ridawan

Rahi Khutbay Jo Pardi, Hy Aun Di Maa, Wich Bazaaran

Beemar Nu Khoon Ruwagaye

Akhtar Rab Jaanay Zahra De Nal

Si Wain Ummat Nu Kaira

Fir Chaadran Lutiyan, Hy Zaalimaa Ne, Wich Karbal De

Fir Khaymeya Nu Ag Laa Gaye
