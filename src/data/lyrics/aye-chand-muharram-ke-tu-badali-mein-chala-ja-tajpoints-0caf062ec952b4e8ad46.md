1
Aye Chand Muharram Ke Tu Badali Mein Chala Ja.
Tujhe Dekh Ke Mar Jaye Na Bemar Hai Sughra.
Ghar Zahra Ka Lutne Ki Khabar Tu Ne Sunai.
Tujhe Dekh Ke Roti Hai Muharram Mein Khudai.

2
Chodah So Baras Beete Sab Karte Hain Shika.
Milne Ke Liye Bhai Ko Be-Chain Bari Hai.
Kab Se Ali Akbar Ki Yeh Rahoon Mein Khari Hai.
Bichari Hai Yeh Muddat Se Isse Tu Na Nazar Aa.

3
Veran Gharon Mein Na Isse Neend Hai Aati.
Akbar Ki Judai Hai Isse Khon Rulati.
Qadmo Ke Nishan Dhanp Ke Bethi Hai Sar-e-Raah.
Gin Gin Ke Jo Sughra Ne Yeh Din Hain Guzare.

4
Zinda Hai Tu Akbar Ke Wadon Ke Sahare.
Din Raat Tarapti Hai Isse Our Na Tarpa.
Bhaiya Ki Judai Mein Pareshan Hai Rehti.
Har Roz Yeh Nana Ko Ro Ro Ke Hai Kehti.

5
Akbar Ne Mila Nana Main Mar Jaon Gi Tanha.
Qasad Ko Diya Khat Mein Yeh Paigham Likha Ke.
Ik Bar Tu Mil Ja Mujhe Seene Se Laga Le.
Pathrati Hoi Nazrein Kab Dekhein Gi Chehra.

6
Wadah Jo Kiya Behan Ko Seene Se Laga Ke.
Main Shadi Karon Ga Tere Paas Hi Aa Ke.
Main Saat Muharram Ko Lotoon Ga Na Ghabra.
Rone Nahi Dete Mujhe Raton Ko Musalmaan.

7
Bemari Se Be-Haal Hoon Kuch Rooz Ki Mehmaan.
Har Simt Se Hai Mujh Ko Ab Maut Ne Ghaira.
Behno Ka Tu Bhaiyon Se Rishta Hi Ajab Hai.
Tum Bhol Gaye Mujh Ko Yeh Kaisa Ghazab Hai.

8
Iss Aas Pe Zinda Hoon Dekhon Tera Sehra.
Sughra Ke Nasebon Mein Rona Hi Likha Hai.
 Sardar Masooma Ko Mili Kaisi Saza Hai.
Khat Aaya Na Akbar Ka Roti Rahi Sughra.
