Kul Kafa Sahib e Laula Hain Rasool-e-Arbi.
Bad-e-Haq Sab Se Hi Aoula Hain Rasool-e-Arbi.
 Yousufi Our Ho Kya Shan-e-Muhammad Mein Biyaan.
Mere Maula Ke Bhi Maula Hain Rasool-e-Arbi.
