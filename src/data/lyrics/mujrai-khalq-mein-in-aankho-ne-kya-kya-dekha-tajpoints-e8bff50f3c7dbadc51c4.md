Mujrai Khalq Mein In Aankho Ne Kya Kya Dekha.
Par Kahin Sibt e Payamber Sa Na Aaqa Dekha.

1
Main Ne Dekha Alam e Shah Ko Alodah e Khoon.
Main Ne Abbas Ko Be Ja Lab-e-Darya Dekha.
Main Ne Dekha Ali Asghar Ka Gala Khon Mein Tar.
Main Ne Zakhmi Ali Akbar Ka Kaleja Dekha.

2
Aik Gathri Sheh-e-Dein Pusht Pe Laye Oss Mein.
Main Ne Tukray Hoye Qasim Ka Sarapa Dekha.
Be Rida Khalq Ne Oss Bibi Ke Sar Ko Dekha.
Jis Ki Madar Ka Kissi Ne Na Janaza Dekha.

3
Kehti Thin Zainab-e-Muztar Keh Khuda Khair Karey.
Raat Ko Khuwab Mein Uryaan Sar-e-Zahra Dekha.
Ja Ke Zainab Ne Madine Mein Kaha Sughra Se.
Kahon Kis Munh Se Keh Pardais Mein Kya Kya Dekha.

4
Gir Paray Sibt e Nabi Tham Ke Hathoon Se Jigar.
Ali Akbar Ko Jo Reti Pe Tarapta Dekha.
Haye Kyun Ho Na Gayin Koor Keh Inn Aankhon Ne.
Qaid Khane Mein Sakina Ka Janaza Dekha.

5
Zulm Hai Mujrai Sajjad Ne Kya Kya Dekha.
Ghar Luta Qaid Hoye Baap Ka Lasha Dekha.
Phir Gaya Aankhon Mein Sheh Ka Rukh-e-Pur Khoon Monis.
Jab Kahin Maqtal-e-Shabeer Ka Naqsha Dekha.
