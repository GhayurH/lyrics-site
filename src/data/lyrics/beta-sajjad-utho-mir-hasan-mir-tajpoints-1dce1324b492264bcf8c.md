Beta Sajjad Utho Mir Hasan Mir Lyrics
Sham-e-Ghareebaan Mein Tha Zainab Ka Noha Beta Sajjad Utho, Beta Sajjad Utho.

1
Jal Gaye Khaimay Mera Koi Bhi Na Raha Beta Sajjad Utho, Beta Sajjad Utho.
Qasim Hain Na Ab Ali Akbar Qatal Hoye Abbas-e-Dilawar.
Chal Gaya Khanjar Sheh Ke Galay Par Koi Nahi Ab Monis-o-Yawar.
Tashna Labon Ke Khon Mein Dobi Karb-o-Bala Ki Pyasi Zameen Hai.

2
Mazlomo Bewon Ka Sahara Koi Nahi Ab Koi Nahi Hai.
Nanhay Mujahid Gaysoon Walay Shair-e-Jarri Ki Maa Ko Sanbhalo.
Hai Yeh Mere Asghar Ki Nishani Jholay Ko Jalnay Se Bacha Lo.
Mar Raha Hai Shimar Tamachay Neel Paray Hain Rukhsaron Par.

3
Sun Sun Ke Beti Ki Sadaein Kanp Uthta Hai Lasha-e-Sarwar.
Roko Sitamgaron Ko Roko Utho Mere Beta Uth Jao.
Shair Se Betay Ko Dekha Hai Khoon Mein Nahaye Barchi Khaye.
Dekho Zara Kya Zulm Howa Hai Dekh Hoi Hai Kaisi Tabahi.

4
Uth Ke Tasali Do Kubra Ko Bewah Hoi Ik Raat Ki Biyahi.
Madar Kitni Tarpi Roi Haye Kissi Ne Reham Na Khaya.
Teer Laga Asghar Ke Galay Par Mara Gaya Be Sheer Bhi Pyasa.
Shair Se Betay Ko Dekha Hai Khoon Mein Nahaye Barchi Khaye.

5
Dil Pe Jawaan Betay Ka Gham Hai Maa Akbar Ki Mar Na Jaye.
Ahl-e-Sitam Ne Oss Abid Ko Itna Sataya Itna Sataya.
Zulm Ossi Par Yeh Hoye Gohar Wohi Abid Baith Na Paya.
Sham-e-Ghareebaan Mein Zainab Ne Jis Se Kaha Beta Sajad Utho, Beta Sajjad Utho.
