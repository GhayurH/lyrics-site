1
Iss Gham Ne Azadaro Bohat Mujh Ko Rulaya.
Iss Saal Bhi Zawwaron Mein Jab Naam Na Aaya.
Dil Toot Gaya Karbala Na Jane Ke Gham Se.
Ro Ro Ke Kaha Main Ne Lipat Kar Yeh Alam Se.
Ik Bar Mujhe Bhi Tu Sada Do.
Abbas Mujhe Karbala Dikha Do.
Shabbir Ka Zawwar Bana Do.
Tum Jantay Ho Koi Nahi Mera Sahara.
Abbas Katay Hathoon Se Tum Kar Do Ishara.
Jab Yaad Hussain Aate Hain Rota Hai Mera Dil.
Mar Jaon Ga Maula Se Mila Do Na Khudara.
Kitna Be Chain Hai Dill, Karbala Hai Manzil.
Jald Aasan Karo, Maula Meri Mushkil.
Kab Aaon Ga Bas Itna Bata Do.
Milta Hai Koi Jab Safar-e-Ishq Se Aa Kar.
Rota Hoon Bohat Dair Galay Oss Ko Laga Kar.
Main Bhi Tu Azadar Hoon Kya Dil Nahi Mera.
Zawwaron Ki Khidmat Main Karon Paon Daba Kar.
Wahan Paidal Main Chalon, Sare Mokib Dekhon.
Wa Hussaina Keh Kar, Khak Chehray Pe Maloon.
Ik Bar Mashi Mujh Ko Kara Do.
Paon They Kahin Jism Kahin Hath Kahin They.
Roza Hai Jahan Khon Mein Tar Aap Wahin They.
Teer Aankh Mein Tha Chehra Bhi Zakhmo Se Bhara Tha.
Jab Zeen Se Girey Aap Ke Bazu Hi Nahi They.
Pursa Dena Hai Mujhe, Aap Ko Ro Ro Ke.
Jahan Dono Bazo, Khak Par Kat Ke Girey.
Dono Kaf-e-Abbas Dikha Do.
Seene Se Lipat Jata Hai Jaisay Koi Bacha.
Aisay Hai Mujhe Shah Ke Rozay Se Lipattna.
Mas Kar Ke Sabhi Apna Kafan Latay Hain Lekin.
Main Unn Ke Liye Le Ke Kafan Jaon Ga Maula.
Dil Tarapta Hai Mera, Aaj Bhi Aye Maula.
Be Kafan Ghurbat Mein, Hai Ghareeb-e-Zahra.
Mujh Ko Mere Maula Se Mila Do.
Jab Pehli Nazar Aap Ke Gunbad Pe Paray Gi.
Gir Jaon Ga Sajday Mein Dua Mangoon Ga Ghazi.
Har Saal Yahan Aata Rahon Aap Se Milney.
Sun Lena Alamdar Tum Aawaz Yeh Dil Ki.
Tum Hi Tu Suntay Ho, Haan Karam Kartay Ho.
Hum Azadaron Ke, Dil Mein Tum Rehtey Ho.
Tum Mojza Ab Koi Dikha Do.
Zawwaron Se Aati Hai Mujhe Aap Ki Khushbo.
Aankhon Se Nikal Aate Hain Maula Mere Aanso.
Hasrat Se Unhein Dekhta Rehta Hoon Main Ro Kar.
Oss Waqt Nahi Rehta Hai Dil Par Mere Qabo.
Dil Yeh Kehta Hai Mera, Aye Shehanshah-e-Wafa.
Karbala Mein Mera, Kab Bulawa Hoga.
Ab Jald Koi Rah Bana Do.
Abbas Jahan Par Hoi Akbar Ki Shahadat.
Kya Mere Muqadar Mein Nahi Hai Woh Ziyarat.
Akbar Ke Kalejay Pe Jahan Tooti Thi Barchi.
Tooti Thi Wahin Dilbar e Zahra Pe Qayamat.
Dekh Loon Main Ja Kar, Woh Maqam-e-Akbar.
Shah Pohanchay They Jahan, Khak Par Gir Gir Kar.
Akbar Se Muhabbat Ka Silla Do.
Dil Kitna Pareshan Hai Abbas Se Bolo.
Woh Baab-e-Hawaij Hain Tum Umeed Na Choro.
Aate Hain Woh Khud Shah Ke Zawwaron Ko Lainay.
Ik Bar Unhein Bali Sakina Ki Qasam Do.
Jald Woh Aaein Gaye, Shah Bulwaein Gay.
Our Tumhein Karb-o-Bala, Sath Le Jaein Gay.
Aye Meesum-o- Zeeshan Sada Do.
