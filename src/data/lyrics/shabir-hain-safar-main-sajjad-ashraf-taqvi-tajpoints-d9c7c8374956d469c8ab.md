Shabir Hain Safar Main Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.

1
Shabbir Hain Safar Mein Our Sath Zahra Sani.
Dohrayi Hasbona Ki Ummat Ne Phir Kahani.
Kabay Ke Malkon Ke Aehram Khol Dalay.
Aayat-e-Innama Ki Kuch Bhi Qadar Na Jani.
Nokheiz Khon-e-Akbar Hai Deen Ki Ragoon Mein.
Qayam Hai Jis Ka Sadqa Islam Ki Jawani.

2
Mukhtar Unn Ke Bachay Hain Aab Aab Kartay.
Fart-e-Haya Se Darya Hota Hai Pani Pani.
Quran Tazkira Hai Aal-e-Nabi Ke Ghar Ka.
Khon-e-Lab-e-Shaheedan Tafseer Hai Qurani.
Hai Iltija Raza Ki Paish-e-Safeer-e-Akbar.
Aankhon Se Khon Ban Kar Tapkay Teri Kahani.
