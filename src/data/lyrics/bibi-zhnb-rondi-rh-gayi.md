Bibi zainab rondhi rehgayi,
Ae kon hai eda naa kya hai, ae savaal yazid karendha je)

1
Uth uth ke mimbhar ton
Ho cha ungli-ishare denda je

2
Rab jaarne kitne deen guzre,
Bibi zainab paarni peeta nai
Hik beh koo(n) darbari
Bhar jaam sharaab paleyndha je

3
Ay shahi nay terey babey di,
Hurn kaidarn barnke aayi hai)
Kitthe veer abbas hai vi)
Enj beghayrat jhirdakendha je

4
Thalle sirr shabbir musafir da,
Uthe takhat te zalim beytha haan
Zulfaa(n) vich hatth paake)
Gustakkhi naal halendha je

5
Hairaarn hai kyun na nikalgaya,
Sajaad da dam darbaar andar
Kay vaari zainab koo(n)
Baghi di bhern saddenda je

6
Mere hatth paband vich rasiya(n) de,
Purdard nazaara lagda haan
Badmashaa koo(n) mangwaa
Har sern di vaal dikhendha je

7
Ik do ghantte di gaal nayi
Naushaad khardi rayi kayi ghhantte
Koi na de baharn di jaa
Ee ho val val hukam surnenda je
