Bhai me Zainab hoon
Pehchano mujko ay mere bhai

1
Zainab hoon me bhayi

2
Ye Karbala hai jagir teri
Ham aye teri jagir may
Marjaye zindan me hi sakina
Yehi likha tha taqdeer me

3
Zindan se chut kar me agayi hoon
Ranjo alam ki chadar liye
Bazar kufa me hai jo guzri
Ankho me sare manzar liye

4
Bhaya tumhari gairat ki khatir
Balonse parda karti rahi
Chadar nahi thi sar par jo meri
Ek ek kadam par marti rahi

5
Bazaar koofa se wo guzarna
Ahle haram ke salaar ka
Bhaya sajaya tha ashqiya ne
Aik aikk kona bazaar ka

6
Bhaya sakina ko ek shaqi ne
Manga kanizi me jis gharri
Me binte Zehra me binte Haider
Bhaya laraz kar me rehgayi

7
Kaisa andhera zindan tha wo
Jisme Sakina jaan aa gayi
Baba chacha se mujko milado
Maasuma ro ro kehti rahi

8
Kurta jo uska khun me bhara tha
Usko banaya meine kafan
Jab dafn karte the usko Abid
Tharra raha tha unka badan
