Wajan Maar Ke Babay Nu Rondi Aye Haye Masoom Sakina.
Kadi Aa Ke Wekh Kiwein Qaidaan Che Main Wasaya Chota Jiya Madina.

1
Yasrab Wasan Kitay Kiney Ghar Paye Wasane.
Mera Hashmi Muhalla Phophiyaan De Qaid Khane.
Mangni Aan Main Duawaan Zainab De Dar Da Boha Sarey Kaday Koi Na.
Aini Wari Veer Abid Mera Sir V Nayi Si Chumiya.

2
Jini Waar Wich Safar De Zalim Ne Walon Pharya.
Aynj Di Yateemi Aayi Kisay Reham Di Nazar Nal Takiya Mainu Kadi Na.
Meri Aqedataan Da Kaba Aye Mera Baba.
Jehri Kaba Rukh Dewar Aye Othay Naa Main Tera Likhiya.

3
Jadon Yaad Teri Aaway Rukhsar Apne Lawaan Tera Samajh Ke Main Seena.
 Akbar Oh Wain Paway Kadon Maut Aaona Aye.
Baba Main Apne Layi Ik Baqiya Banawana Aye.
Dadi Di Thaan Te Meri Turbat Banray Te Howay Pora Mera Madina
