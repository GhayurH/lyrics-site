Ya Fatema Zehra
Ya zehra

1
Maa dardo jahan fatema jaan dil bado basti
Muhibaan du hasti

2
Aey umme abhi haa
Aey umme faki haa
Aey umme abhi ha bajaza bager ye dasti
Muhibaan do hasti

3
1. Ummide shifa azze tu ay Fatema Zehra,
Khaatoon e jinaa warise tathir sarafaa,
Ashrafe nisaa akse nabi abde hudayda,

4
Mizane wafaa roohe dua Fatema Zehra.
Ya Fatema Zehra, ya Fatema Zehra
Maa dardo jahan...

5
2. Uftaade dare khaana tora pehlu shakista,
Ummat ne tera haq na diya Fatema Zehra,
Mohsin ki shahadat ka sabab ban gayi duniya,

6
Virsa hai tera ashke azaa Fatema Zehra.
Ya Fatema Zehra, ya Fatema Zehra
Maa dardo jahan...

7
3. Ay ashke fisha bada nabi binte payambar,
Tha sog me baba ke azaa khana tera ghar,
Rote the tere saath ali sawaro shabbar,

8
Aey khasta jigar mehre bukaa Fatema Zehra.
Ya Fatema Zehra, ya Fatema Zehra
Maa dardo jahan...

9
4. Dar karbobala tishna labi tere pisar ki,
Taa raj huwi kis tarha zehra tere ghar ki,
Begoro kafan laash rahi noore nazar ki,

10
Aalam ba khuda sog me tha Fatema Zehra.
Ya Fatema Zehra, ya Fatema Zehra
Maa dardo jahan...

11
5. Bacho pe tere fauj e sitamgaar ke yalghaar,
Zanjeer bakaf tauqe gara aabide beemar,
Darbare alam qaide sitam dukhtare ghamkwar,

12
Aada ke sitam kunba tera Fatema Zehra.

13
Ya Fatema Zehra, ya Fatema Zehra
Ma dardo jahan fatema jan dil bado basti.
Muhibaan do hasti
