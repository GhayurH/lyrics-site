Yeh Raaz Kya Hai Duniya Kay Bananay Valay

Kay Sahara Hai Tera  Deen Bachanay Valay

Tha Wo Darbar Magar Log Vohi Thay Nana
Tum Pay Hiziyan Ka Jo  Ilzaam Laganay Valay

Dar Kay Zalim Kay Tamachon Say Sakina Nay Kaha
Ub Tho Aajao Hummay  Paani Pilanay Valay

Kufa Aur Shaam Ka Dastoor Bhi Nana Dekha
Qatl Kartai Hain Wooh  Mehmaan Bulanay Valay

Kyun Larazti Hai Zameen Aur Udaasi Kyun Hai
Kon Hain Shaam Kay  Bazaar Main Aanay Valay
