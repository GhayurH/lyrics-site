1
Dunya Tu Fatima Ke Qadam Ka Ghubar Hai.
Yeh Marziya Hai Marzi-e-Parwardigar Hai.
Noor-e-Khuda Hai, Deen-e-Khuda Ka Hisar Hai.
Ismat Ka Yeh Gohar Hai Hayya Ka Waqar Hai.

2
Jis Par Rasool Pak Ka Sab Kuch Nisar Hai.
Yeh Fatima Hai Lakht-e-Rasool-e-Kibar Hai.
Yeh Sirray Kun Fakon Ka Adna Hai Ishara.
Ajsad-e-Khak-o-Noor Ki Tafheem Ka Jadah.

3
Insan-o-Aadmi Ke Tafarq Ka Nazara.
Rab Ki Taraf Se Nasal-e-Muhammad Ka Adah.
Hain Panjtan Chaman Tu Yeh Oss Ki Bahar Hai.
Mere Nabi Ko Tana Jo Detey They Hai Abtar.

4
Lo Khak Pari Aaj Sabhi Fitna Garoon Par.
Sardar-e-Anbiya Ki Hai Aagosh Mein Dukhtar.
Yani Quraan Ko Mil Gayi Ab Sorat Kausar.
Arz-e-Khuda Pe Aayat-e-Parwardigar Hai.

5
Roshan Assi Ke Noor Se Khursheed-o-Mahtab.
Jod-o-Karam Ka Khalq-e-Khuda Par Hai Ik Sahab.
Dastoor Hai Iffat Ka Tu Tatheer Ka Nisab.
Qadmo Mein Khuld Sar Pe Hai Taj-e-Abu Turab.

6
Ilm-e-Nabi Ka Baab Ali, Yeh Jidar Hai.
Hamil Hai Innama Ki Tu Tafseer-e-Hal Atta.
Markaz Mubahila Ka Hai Our Mehwar-e-Kisa.
Hai Tazkira Kalam-e-Mubarak Mein Ja Baja.

7
Jane Khuda, Nabi-o-Ali Unn Ka Martaba.
Fikar-e-Bashar Ki Had Nahi, Be Ikhteyar Hai.
Anwar Ki Barsat Hai Jashan-e-Saeed Mein.
Irfan Ki Sogat Hai Jashan-e-Saeed Mein.

8
Esiyaan Se Bhi Nijat Hai Jashan-e-Saeed Mein.
Bas Syeda Ki Baat Hai Jashan-e-Saeed Mein.
Yeh Zikar-e-Ahl-e-Bait Hi Apna Shaar Hai.
Lab Par Najam Ke Ahl-e-Wila Oss Ki Hai Sana.

9
Jis Ko Rasool Pak Kahin Umme Abeeha.
Jo Haider-e-Karrar Ki Humraz-o-Hum Nawa.
Jis Ka Wajood Mehwar-e-Takhleeq-e-Kibriya.
Rab Ki Taraf Se Kul Pe Jisay Ikhteyar Hai.
