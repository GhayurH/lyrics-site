Dewa Balya Na Phul Paye Aye Qabar Kidi Aye Rahwaan Wich.
Ik Masooma Si Mar Gayi Aye Qaidi Veran Diyaan Banwaan Wich.

1
Abid Nal Lipat Ke Rondi Si Kiwein Wakhri Qaid Nibhawaan Gi.
Chad Ke Kaliyaan Mainu Jawein Na Chan Veran Main Mar Jawaan Gi.
Sun Ke Galaan Zindaan Roway Jo Hoiyaan Bhain Bhirawaan Wich.
Mere Dardaan Da Ahsas Karein Kiwein Babay Nu Main Bhul Jawaan.

2
Tak Zalim Mojza Ujari Da Shabbir Di Dhi Main Dikhlawaan.
Mera Piyo Nezay Te Ro Piya Aye Ayeh Asar Hai Meriyaan Hawaan Wich.
Kyun Aayi Maut Haneray Sakina Nu Zainab Kiya Sajjad Dasa.
Abid Ne Kiya Ki Haal Dasaan Jadon Main Masooma Kool Gaya.

3
Babay Da Naa Si Oss Welay Ujari De Aakhri Sanwaan Wich.
Lakh Zulm Karey Aye Shimar e Laeen Main Rona Aye Main Rowaan Gi.
Babay Di Qabar Te Jawaan Gi Kadon Veer Aazad Main Howaan Gi.
Abid Taun Puchdi Tak Urday Panchi Aazad Hawawaan Wich.

4
Kiwein Lash Utaraan Lehad De Wich Ro Ke Sajjad Aye Farmaway.
Koi Aa Ke Bhain Nu Dafan Karo Qasim Ya Akbar Aa Jaway.
Rat Ronda Si Lasha Chuk Ke Abid Piya Phophiyaan Mawaan Wich.
Shabbir Di Jayi De Gham Wich Jehra Do Athro V Bahaway Ga.

5
Dukh Yaad Karey Ga Ujari De Jehra Taboot Uthaway Ga.
Ohno Sain Sakina Mehshar Tak Rakhay Gi Yaad Duawaan Wich.
Wakhrey Zindan Che Qaid Si Par Aawaz Te Aaondi Rendi Si.
Chup Kar Gayi Aye Kujh Dair Nu Oh Aa Ja Veran Jehri Kaindi Si.

6
Aynj Lagda Ujari Mar Gayi Aye Rondi Be Jurm Sazawaan Wich.
 Sajjad Oh Jhirkaan Jhaldi Rayi Bagi Di Dhi Sab Kenday San.
Jadon Yasrab De Wich Wasday San Sab Hashmi Naaz Uthainday San.
Ik Shahzadi Si Par Rul Gayi Oh Karbal De Sehrawan Wich.
