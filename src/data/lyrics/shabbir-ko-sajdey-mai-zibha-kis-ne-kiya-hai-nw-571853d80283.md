Shabbir Ko Sajde Mein Zibha Kisne Kiya Hai

Mazloom Ke Matam Se Humein Rokne Waalon Ghar Fatima Zahra Ka Taba Kisne Kiya Hai

Khaimein Bhi Jalaye Toh Ridaen Bhi Utaari

Syadaaniyan Sar Nange Kharhi Roti Thi Saari

Hindu They Yahoodi They Isaayee Ya Koi Aur

Itna Toh Bata Doh Yeh Gunna Kisne Kiya Hai

Besheer Ke Hal Qoon Pe Jab Teer Laga Tha

Aur Pyaas Se Masoom Ka Haye Khushk Gala Tha

Asghar Ne Zubhan Hoton Pe Jab Pheri Thi Logon

Tab Khoon Se Tar Khushk Gala Kisne Kiya Hai

Zanjeeron Mein Jakrha Hua Sajjad Muhaari

Aur Shimr Ke Zulmon Se Darri Bibiyan Saari

Jis Bibi Ka Suraj Bhi Haya Karta Tha Chup Kar

Uss Bibi Ke Sar Ko Be Rida Kisne Kiya Hai

Barchi Ne Joh Cheera Ali Akbar Ke Jigar Ko

Haye Kaise Uthaya Mere Maula Ne Pisar Ko

Laila Ka Pisar Lagta Tha Tasveer Nabi Ki

Sarkar-e-Risalat Ka Haya Kisne Kiya Hai

Abbas Wafadaar Ke Parcham Ki Nishaani

Yaad Aaye Issey Dekh Ke Karbal Ki Kahaani

Joh Haath They Zahra Ki Duaon Ka Nateeja

Un Haathon Ko Shaano Se Juda Kisne Kiya Hai

Akhtar Ko Yeh Ek Masla Sabhi Mil Na Bataen

Kiss Jurm Mein Looti Gayi Karbal Mein Ridaen

Darbar Mein Khutba Joh Parha Binte Ali Ne

Azaanon Se Phir Shor Bapa Kisne Kiya Hai
