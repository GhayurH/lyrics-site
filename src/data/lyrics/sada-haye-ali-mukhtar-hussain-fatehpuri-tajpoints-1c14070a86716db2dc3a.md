1
Sada Haye Ali Mukhtar Hussain Fatehpuri.
Aati Hai Masjid-e-Kofa Se Sada Haye Ali.
Qatal Sajday Mein Howa Shair-e-Khuda Haye Ali.
Aaj Bhi Masjid-e-Kofa Hai Gawahi Iss Ki.
Aa Ke Yeh Jibreel Ne Noha Kiya Haye Ali.
Zakhm Jo Maa Ki Shahadat Ka Laga Tha Dil Par.
Dil Mein Phir Hasnain Ke Taza Howa Haye Ali.
Tar Hoye Mimber-o-Mehrab Ali Ke Khoon Se.
Khon Haider Ka Musalay Pe Baha Haye Ali.
Choti Si Khwahar-e-Abbas Khadija BiBi.
Oss Pe Chayi Hai Yateemi Ki Bala Haye Ali.
Kis Tarha Zainab-o-Kulsoom Ki Halat Ho Biyaan.
Bhaiyon Se Yeh Galay Lag Ke Kaha Haye Ali.
Kiya Hasnain Ne Jis Waqt Janaza Teyar.
Tham Kar Ghazi Kafan Kehne Laga Haye Ali.
Uthi Jab Majid-o- Mukhtar Ali Ki Maiyyat.
Taig-e-Haider Se Bhi Aati Thi Sada Haye Ali.
