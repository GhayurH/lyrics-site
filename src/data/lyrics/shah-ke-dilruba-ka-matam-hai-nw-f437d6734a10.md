shah ke dilruba ka matam hai

akse khairul-wara ka matam hai

bandishe aab door ghar sehra

gul may karbobala ka matam hai

shah ke dilruba….

kheenchi galiyon may laash kaat ke sar

muslimay bekhata ka matam hai

shah ke dilruba….

halqe asghar may teer se pehlu

banu ke mehleka ka matam hai

shah ke dilruba….
