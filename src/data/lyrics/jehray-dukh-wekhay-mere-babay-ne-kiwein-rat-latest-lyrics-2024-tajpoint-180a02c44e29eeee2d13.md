Jehray Dukh Wekhay Mere Babay Ne Kiwein Rat Akhiyaan Di Ruk Jaway.
Jiday Samne Zainab De Sir Taun Koi Chadar Aan Ke Laway
.Sara Safar Bazaraan Da Baba Mere Naal Raku Wich Turya Aye.
Sah Chalda Si Ik Da Loko Aynj Tauq Dowaan Da Jurya Aye.
Jay Mur Ke Wekhay Phophiyaan Wal Fair Putar Nu Kaun Bachaway.
Oh Wela Mere Babay Te Bara Okha Loko Langiya Aye.
Wich Aakhri Khuwaish Bhain Odi Jadon Sir Babay Da Mangiya Aye.
Oss Bhain Di Kariyaan Pa Koi Zindan Che Qabar Banaway.
Kayi Wari Mere Babay Nu Bazar De Wich Gash Aaya Aye.
Har Wari Veer De Qatilaan Nu Maa Zainab Ro Farmaya Aye.
Koi Kalma Go Aa Ke Mere Abid Nu Hosh Karaway.
Sehra Wich Tapdiyaan Raitaan Te Aina Kayi Kayi Meel Turaya Aye.
Oss Welay Aan Sakina Ne Beemar Da Dard Wandaya Aye.
Jehra Peen Layi Pani Milda Si Aidi Kariyaan Te Oh Paway.
Jis Wehray Taun Guzran Welay Soraj V Ijazat Lainda Aye.
Zahra Di Pali Aye Zainab Najfi Hath Joor Aye Kainda Aye.
Jinno Turn Da Wal V Nayi Aanda Koi Neziyaan Naal Turaway.
