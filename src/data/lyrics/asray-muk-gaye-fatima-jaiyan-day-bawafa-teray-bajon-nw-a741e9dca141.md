Asray Muk Gaye Fatima Jaiyan Day Bawafa Teray Bajon

Tu Latheyaye Zeen Ton Lut

Khaimayan Ch Pay Gayi

Asi Hoiyan Be Rida

Tu Bawaan Kata Kay

Karbal Di Khaak Tay

Jadh Sam Gaya Ghazi

Fer Inna Ma Diyan Sab

Ayetan Nu Aakay

Gaya Shimr Rasiyan Paa

Teray Alam Layi Ghazi

Naizay Tay Char Gayan

Sab Chadran Ghirn Kay

Tu Alma Waleya Uth

Vaikh Karbala Vich

Har Bhen Di Wafa

Teri Laash Tay Sanu

Rowan Ton Shimr Nay

Enj Rokeya Ghazi

Saday Sar Vich Zakhm Tay

Mu Khoon Naal Pijh Gaye

Diti Rohn Di Saza
