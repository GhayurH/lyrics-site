1
Batool Kay Shayan Syed Mudassir Mehdi.
Harf-e-Sana Batool Ke Shayan Chahiye.
So Kam Se Kam Khuda Ka Qalamdan Chahiye.
Mariyam Pukarein Kuch Bhi Nahi Chahiye Mujhe.
Kuch Chahiye Tu Aap Ka Ahsan Chahiye.
Inn Ki Inayaton Ko Samajhne Ke Wastay.
Quran Ko Bhi Sorah Rehman Chahiye.
Phir Youn Howa Keh Bhaija Gaya Noor Aap Ka.
ALLAH Ne Kaha Tha Keh Pehchan Chahiye.
Ghar Kar Gayi Hai Tujh Mein Mawaddat Batool Ki.
Ab Our Kya Tujhe Dil-e-Nadan Chahiye.
Mairaj Ke Liye Woh Tere Dar Pe Aa Girey.
Sabit Howa Sitaron Ko Bhi Shan Chahiye.
Darwaza e Batool Ke Irfan Ke Liye.
Buzar Bhi Keh Rahe Hain Keh Salman Chahiye.
Umm Ul-Banin Bolein Suno Mere Ladlo!.
Qurban Ik Hussain Pe Har Jan Chahiye.
Bibi Ke Dushmano Pe Tabarah Kiya Karey.
Barzakh Ka Marhala Jisay Aasan Chahiye.
Kausar Hai Kya Yeh Hazrat-e-Abbas Se Samajh.
Gar Syeda Ki Zaat Ka Irfan Chahiye.
Yeh Madah Hai Keh Umm-e-Abeeha Kaha Gaya.
Yani Rasool Jaisa Sana Khuwan Chahiye.
Kehne Lagi Ali Ki Wilayat Aye Momino.
Pehle Mujhe Batool Pe Eman Chahiye.
Bibi Ki Aik Nazar-e-Karam Chahiye Mujhe.
Our Manqabat Sunanay Ke Doran Chahiye.
 Haider Yeh Madh-e-Fatima Zahra Ka Hai Usool.
Quran Ki Sana Hai Tu Quran Chahiye.
