Us Waqt Jab Ke Zainab Darbar Mein Gayi Thi
Khari Namaziyon Se Mehfil Saji Hui Thi
Zalim Ye Puchhta Tha Ghazi Tera Kahan Hai
Baalon Se Munh Chhupaye Zainab Ye Keh Rahi Thi

Ay Mere Baba Jan Ho Rahi Hai Azaan Sar Chhupaun Kahan
Majma-e-Aam Mein Bewatan Beaman Aa Gaya Karwan

۱
Main Bhi Hun Marziya Binte Khairun Nisa
Mere Lehje Mein Hai Baba Teri Ada
Naam Zainab Mera Tune Jo Rakh Diya Dede Apni Zuban

۲
Baba Jan Main Rida Maangti Reh Gayi
Baam-o-Dar Saj Gaye Jeete Ji Mar Gayi
Halqa-e-Shaam Mein Naam Sun Kar Mera Mar Gaya Natawan

۳
Haath Kaanon Pe Hai Neel Gaalon Pe Hai
Aur Nishan Jo Sakina Ke Shaanon Pe Hai
Har Nishan Se Yahi Aa Rahi Hai Sada Baba Jan Baba Jan

۴
Shaam-e-Aashur Se Shaam-o-Kufa Talak
Maine Maanga Nahi Hai Khiraj-e-Fadak
Meri Chadar Se Hai Meri Maa Ka Sukoon Sun Le Zalim Jahan

۵
Baba Jan Ye Tera Aakhri Tha Bayan
Teri Chadar Bachayega Ghazi Jawan
Mar Gaya Bawafa Chhin Gayi Hai Rida Kehti Hain Bibiyan

۶
Ruk Na Paya Mere Bawafa Ka Wo Sar
Nok-e-Naiza Se Aane Laga Khaak Par
Behan Ko Darbadar Dekh Kar Nange Sar Seh Na Paya Jawan

۷
Kal Thi Ha Aaj Main Bhi Hun Darbar Mein
Misl-e-Zahra Khari Hun Main Aghyar Mein
Us Taraf Hai Shaqi Is Taraf Reh Gayi Bepidar Betiyan

۸
Aisa Khutba Diya Maine Darbar Mein
Likh Raha Hai Jo Yawar Tere Pyar Mein
Lehja-e-Haidari Har Azaan Ke Liye Hai Sada-e-Azaan
