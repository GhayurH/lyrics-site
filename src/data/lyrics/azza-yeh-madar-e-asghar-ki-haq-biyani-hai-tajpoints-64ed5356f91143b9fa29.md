1
Azza Yeh Kha Gayi Sajjad Ki Jawani Hai.
Jo Noha Parhtay Hain Dukhiya Ka Matami Sheh Ke.
Tu Barhti Mehdi Ke Ashkon Ki Phir Rawani Hai.
Hussain Dhop Mein Pyasay Zibha Hoye Iss Ne.
Na Chaon Dekhi Piya Phir Na Sard Pani Hai.
Yeh Maein Kehti Hain Ro Kar Hussain Walon Ki.
Issi Ke Gham Mein Hi Aaye Jo Maut Aani Hai.
 Nisar Kaisay Sunein Gay Woh Matami Inn Ki.
Rodad Syed-e-Sajjad Ne Sonani Hai.
