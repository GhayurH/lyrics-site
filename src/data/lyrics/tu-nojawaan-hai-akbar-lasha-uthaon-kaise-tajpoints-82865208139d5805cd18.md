1
Tu Nojawaan Hai Akbar Lasha Uthaon Kaise.
Zainab Ko Tere Marne Ka Main Bataon Kaisay.
Chalte Hain Kohniyo Par Shabir Sochtay Hain.
Ghazi Ko Yaad Kar Ke Dil Mein Hi Ro Rahe Hain.
Betay Ki Lash Par Main Iss Tarha Jaon Kaisay.
Aayi Hai Zahra Ran Mein Rotay Hain Sath Haider.
Seene Pe Hath Rakhay Ke Yeh Sochtay Hain Akbar.
Seene Ki Barchi ALLAH Sab Ko Dikhaon Kaisay.
Maqtal Mein Aa Ke Qasad Akbar Ko Dhondta Hai.
Ghurbat Ko Dekh Meri Bas Rota Ja Raha Hai.
Akbar Ki Lash Hai Yeh Oss Ko Bataon Kaisay.
Roti Thin Umm-e-Laila Zainab Ko Dekhti Thin.
Lashay Ko Dekhti Thin Our Bas Yeh Sochti Thin.
Betay Ki Lash Ko Main Beta Bulaon Kaisay.
Hansta Tha Baba Zalim Jab Naiza Torta Tha.
Sabir Hai Tera Baba Mujh Se Yeh Keh Raha Tha.
Tukray Jigar Ke Tum Ko Ab Main Dikhaon Kaisay.
Tooti Kamar Sanbhalay Shabeer Chal Para Hai.
Nana Ki Shakal Wala Beta Jo Mar Gaya Hai.
Kariyal Jawan Lasha Tanha Uthaon Kaisay.
Shabeer Misal-e-Zahra Pehlu Pe Hath Rakh Ke.
Ro Kar Hunain Apni Beti Se Keh Rahe They.
Manzar Ab Iss Se Agla Tujh Ko Dikhaon Kaisay.
