Shah e Najaf De Sir Te Khanjar Shaqi Chalaya.
Jibreel Wain Karda Dar-e-Fatima Te Aaya.

1
Hill Gayi Zameen-e-Kofa Matam Hai Do Jahan Wich.
Babay Da Zakhmi Sir Jad Zainab Ne Jholi Paya.
Kholein Na Waal Zainab Babay Di Zindagi Wich.
Qayam Raway Hamesha Veraan Da Sir Te Saya.

2
Qatil Nu Wekh Pyasa Sharbat Pilaya Syed.
Ho Qaid Ibn-e-Muljim Jadon Samne Hai Aaya.
Mazloom Baon Hussain Aye Toon Aye Khush Naseeb Maula.
Miliya Kafan Hai Tainu Putraan Janaza Chaya.

3
Babay Da Zard Chehra Jadon Wekhiya Aye Dhiyaan.
Aj Main Yateem Ho Gayi Zainab Ne Wain Aye Paya.
Kaba Jaye Wiladat Masjid De Wich Shahadat.
Haider Siwa Kisay V Aizaz Nayi Aye Paya.

4
Hasnain Cha Janaza Tur Paye Najaf De Pasay.
Zainab Nu Yaad Oss Dam Maa Da Janaza Aaya.
 Tanveer Karbala Di Hoi Kofay Ibtida Aye.
Masjid De Wich Ali Da Na Haq Lahu Bahaya.
