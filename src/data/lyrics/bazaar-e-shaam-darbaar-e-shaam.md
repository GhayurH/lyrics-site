Bazaar e sham darbaar e sham
Hae hae zainab hai hai sham

1
Bazaar saja hai aur Zainab be chadar

2
1. Rahoon main loog sharabi aur bint e ali faryaadi
Aik chadar mang rahi hai ummat say syed zadi
Aur loog tamashai aaye hain ban kar

3
2. Her soo na paak nigaheen aur aal e nabi ki aahain
Sang bari karnai aae sab loog saja ker raheen
Is ghum say harum hai seena zan nauha gar

4
3. Yeh kaesi qaiyamat aaee taqdeer kahan per laai
Her zakhm saha hai dil per her chooT jigar par khaaei
Yeh shooq ki galiyaan kehti hain roo roo kar

5
4. Abbas ki khuaaher zainab hai bint e haider zainab
Baloon sae chuppa ker chehra phirti hai dar dar zainab
Hai kaesa qaiyamat ka logoon yeh manzar

6
5. Jis ghar say parda dari ki rasm hui thi jaari
Be parda daikhnae unko aae hai dunya sari
Zainb kae kalaejae par chaltae hain khanjar

7
6. Yeh zulm bhi kaesa dhaiya bazaar e sham sajaya
Hathoon sae ishara kar kae bibi ka naam bataya
Girta tha wahan bhi imam mera ghash kha ker

8
7. Haan boojh thae dil pae bhari thae eshK lahoo kae jaari
Soochae tatheer ki waris kaesay ho parda dari
Tae karna hai ab bin chadar aur safar

9
8. Zainab ka dard uthana do zakhm jigar par khana
Bhai kae halq pae khanjar aur shaam say ho kar jaana
Haye bhool na pae gi zainab yeh manzar

10
9. Safdar o haashim daikho qirtas o qalam rota hai
Yeh ajrae risalat daikho ummat say jo paya hai
Zehra ki baeti rooti hai nangae sar
