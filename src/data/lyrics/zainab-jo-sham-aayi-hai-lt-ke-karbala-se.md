Zainab jo Shaam aayi haey lut kay Karbala se

1
Tasveer Fatima ki,  mohtaaj thi rida se

2
1) Main hu Ali ke beti, hai mera naam Zainab
Majme mein fasiqon ke, ti inkalaam Zainab
Qaidi jo kaar liya hai,  bolo toh kuch haya se

3
2) Jaab khafile pey Shaami, pathar gira rahe teh
Katkar jigar ke tukday, aankhoN mein ah rahe teh
Dekha gaya na manzar,  yeh gaardishe Khuda se

4
3) Noke seena pay natiq, Quraan ro raha hay
Kariyan beemar ki aur, zindaan ro raha hai
Baychain thi Sakina,  hy khauf ki wajah se

5
4) Zahra ki betiyon pay, pathar girane walon
Tafseer-e-intama ko, qaidi banane walon
Socho bacho gai kaise,  Zahra ki badua se

6
5) Hay kaun ab dilaye, chaadar meri Shimar se
Neizey se kaise li hai, zalim ney mere saar se
Itna toh kehde koi,  Abbas-e-baWafa se

7
6) Ghaar baar Fatima ka, Shaami kala rahe teh
Ye dekh kaar farishtein, aasooN baha rahe teh
Kya qhoob hai nibhaayi,  ummat ne Mustafa se

8
7) Mushkil bada tha Akhtar, woh imtihaane Zainab
Zakhmon sey choor ta saab, hy kaarwane Zainab
Roti khadi hai Bibiyan,  sehmi howi jafa se
