Shimr Kehta Tha Mat Suno Mukhtar
Shimar Kehta Tha Mat Suno Mukhtar Main Ne Kaisay Hussain Ko Mara
Jab Howa Main Sawar Seene Par Lab Hilanay Laga Tera Maula
Pyas Mein Bad Duaein Deney Lagay Mujh Ko Shabeer Aisa Lagta Tha
Main Ne Honton Pe Kan Rakh Ke Suna Woh Tu Ghazi Ko Yaad Karta Tha
Kaisi Ghurbat Thi Ibn-e-Zahra Par Dekhti Thi Sakina Yeh Manzar
Mere Hathoon Mein Sheh Ki Darhi Thi Main Rulata Raha Ossay Hans Kar
Lash-e-Abbas Bhi Tarapney Lagi Jab Tamacha Hussain Ko Mara
Oss Ki Aankhon Mein Jane Kya Tha Ameer Koi Nazrein Nahi Milata Tha
Main Bhi Aankhein Na Oss Ki Dekh Saka Iss Liye Chehra Oss Ka Phair Diya
Yeh Wajah Thi Jo Pusht-e-Gardan Se Tere Maula Ko Zibha Main Ne Kiya
Woh Jo Ik Nehar Hai Wahan Jari Tera Maula Wahan Tha Zeen Se Gira
Umar-e-Sad Ne Kaha Mujh Se Qatal Iss Ko Nashaib Mein Karna
Main Yahan Qatal Ga Tak Mukhtar Oss Ko Zulfoon Se Khainch Kar Laya
Haan Woh Lamha Ajeeb Tha Mukhtar Mere Hathoon Mein Taiz Thi Talwar
Kund Ho Jata Tha Mera Khanjar Main Ne Youn Bhi Kiye They Terah Waar
Mujh Se Katta Na Tha Gala Oss Ka Oss Ki Maa Ne Tha Hath Rakha Howa
Aik Tela Hai Karbala Mein Jahan Ro Rahi Thi Behan Khari Oss Ki
Minattein Kar Rahi Thi Har Ik Ki Keh Rahi Thi Nahi Chalao Churi
Oss Ne Satar Qadam Se Kattay Hoye Apne Bhai Ka Khud Gala Dekha
Oss Ne Dekha Jab Aa Gayi Zainab Mujh Se Shabeer Ro Ke Kehne Laga
Shimar Jane De Oss Ko Khaimay Mein Apna Khanjar Chupa Le Oss Se Zara
Shimar Zanu Hata Le Seene Se Wasta Hai Tujhe Muhammad Ka
MIR SAJJAD Sun Ke Oss Ke Sitam Sare Darbari Bain Karne Lagay
Bola Shimar e Laeen Aye Mukhtar Kuch Dikhaon Agar Ijazat De
Jis Pe Khoon Tha Ghareeb-e-Zahra Ka Oss Ne Khanjar Woh Sab Ko Dikhlaya
