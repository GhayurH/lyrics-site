Ghar Fatima Zehra Ka Ajab Shaan Ka Ghar Hai

Ye Wahi Ki Manzil Hai Yeh Quran Ka Ghar Hai

Islam Ka Mahoul Main Eeman Ka Ghar Hai

Momin Ki Munafiq Ki Yeh Pehchaan Ka Ghar Hai

Kuch Laug Maray Jatay Hain Is Ranj-o-Mehan Main

Is Ghar Ka Jo Darwaza Hai Masjid Ke Sehan Main

Allaha Nay Is Ghar Ko Nabooat Say Nawaza

Duniya Main Isay Deen-o-Shareeat Say Nawaza

Ismat Say Imamat Say Wilayat Say Nawaza

Har Fard Ko Quran Ki Aayat Say Nawaza

Saanchay Main Masheeat Kay Ye Afrad Dhalay Hain

Sab Chadar-e-Tatheer Kay Saaye Main Palay Hain

Ye Azam Kay Faulad Isi Ghar Mein Milaingay

Ye Noor Kay Ajsaad Isi Ghar Mein Milaingay

Jibreel Kay Ustaad Isi Ghar Mein Milaingay

Is Satha Kay Afraad Isi Ghar Mein Milaingay

Makhlookh Ki Bas Aakhri Sarhad Nazar Aaya

Jis Fard Ko Dekha Wo Muhammad Nazar Aaya

Ashab Mukarram Hain Magar Apni Jaghan Par

Ghair Apni Jaghan Lakht-e-Jigar Apni Jaghan Par

Khaar Apni Jaghan Guncha-e-Tar Apni Jaghan Par

Sang Apni Jaghan Laal-o-Gohar Apni Jaghan Par

Pehlou Main Jo Baitha Ho Usay Dil Nahi Kehtay

Rehro Ko Kabhi Rehbar-e-Manzil Nahi Kehtay

Allaha Re Izzo Sharafe Bint-e-Risalat

Qandeel-e-Haram Sham-e-Shabistan-e-Imammat

Rehmat Jo Muhammad Hain To Ye Para-e-Rehmat

Wo Kulay Nabooat Hain To Ye Juzvay Nabooat

Asaan Na Kaho Marifat-e-Khatmay Rasul Ko

Jo Juz Ko Na Samjha Ho Wo Kya Samjhayga Kul Ko
