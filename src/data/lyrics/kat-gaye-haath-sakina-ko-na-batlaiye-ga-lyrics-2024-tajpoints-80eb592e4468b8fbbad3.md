Kat Gaye Haath Sakina Ko Na Batlaiye Ga Muhammad Raza Mungla.

1
Abbas Haye Abbas, Abbas Haye Abbas.
Kat Gaye Hath Sakina Ko Na Batlaiye Ga.
Ab Meri Lash Ko Khaimay Mein Na Le Jaiye Ga.
Samna Kaisay Sakina Ka Karon Ga Maula.
Na Nibha Paya Jo Bachoon Se Kiya Tha Wadah.
Aap Maqtal Se Mera Lasha Na Uthwaiye Ga.

2
Aap Se Aik Hai Aye Mere Aaqa.
Itna Pamal Badan Hai Keh Bikhar Jaye Ga.
Bas Yahin Darya Kinaray Mujhe Dafnaiye Ga.
Shah Zadi Se Yeh Kahiye Ga Nibhai Hai Wafa.
Sath Abbas Ke Ashal Ne Bhi Pani Na Piya.
Jab Alam Le Ke Yeh Khaimon Ki Taraf Jaiye Ga.

3
Aye Mere Aaqa Muhabbat Hai Bohat Hum Se Ossay.
Woh Agar Bolay Keh Ammo Se Mila Laein Mujhe.
Bas Sakina Ko Yahan Par Na Kabhi Laiye Ga.
Yeh Ghulam Aap Ko Gar Dekh Le Mil Jaye Sakon.
Teer Paiwast Howa Tha Tu Jama Aankh Mein Khon.
Khoon Hata Kar Zara Chehra Mujhe Dikhlaiye Ga.

4
Aap Tu Jantay Hain Aaqa Meri Majbori.
Hath Hotey Tu Qadam Bosi Ko Uthta Ghazi.
Main Na Uth Paya Yeh Amma Ko Na Batlaiye Ga.
Oss Ghari Dasht Mein Manzar Tha Qayamat Ka Aimaad.
Shah Se Hazrat-e-Abbas Ne Jo Ki Faryad.
Yeh Musibat Meri Abid Ko Na Batlaiye Ga.
