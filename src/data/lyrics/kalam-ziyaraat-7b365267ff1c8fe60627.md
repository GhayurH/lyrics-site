Jang nahi karni Mukhtar bhira main vich darbar day aye khari an

1
Rat ronda bachra bach jaway,  es aas tay kariyan paye khari an

2
1) Talwar di aur si Karbal vich hun jang day zawiye badal gaye
Main torr kay nal Sakina nu tableegh da zima chay khari an

3
2) Toray jang Karbal di muk gaye ay nahi silsala rukiya lashan da
Rahwan day vich Masooma day main kaye lashay dafnay khari an

4
3) Ahgay Karbal day maidan day vich bay kafan janazay choor aaye an
Daday Imran di nasal vichon hun ik Sajjad bachay khari an

5
4) Tu sir nahi chukda jandi han nahi vaikhna chahnda baj rida
Main khud Sajjad di kand olay sirr apna veer lukay khari an

6
5) Gohar Mukhtar ko sain akhay bay kafan bhira di bhen han main
Chadar di ja sirr pathar tay main sham-e-gharibaan paye khari an
