1
Deen e Islam Ko Parwan Charhaya Sheh Ne.
Nook-e-Neza Se Bhi Quran Sunaya Sheh Ne.
Teri Aagosh Mein Janat Ki Baharein De Kar.
Karbala Tujh Ko Bhi Gulzar Banaya Sheh Ne.

2
Dasht-e-Ghurbat Mein Ho Aa Jaiye Bachpan Ke Habib.
Likh Ke Khat Ibn-e-Mazahir Ko Bulaya Sheh Ne.
Dil Mein Parwano Ke Roshan Hoye Nusrat Ke Charag.
Jab Charag Shab-e-Ashor Bhojaya Sheh Ne.

3
Rakh Ke Abbas Ke Patkay Mein Alam Ko Dein Ke.
Foaj Ka Apni Alamdar Banaya Sheh Ke.
Phir Muyassar Ossay Aaye Ga Na Seena Mera.
Loriyaan De Ke Sakina Ko Sulaya Sheh Ne.

4
Jab Na Maqtal Mein Nazar Aaya Madadgar Koi.
Lash-e-Akbar Ko Akela Hi Uthaya Sheh Ne.
Daltay Nanhi Si Turbat Pe Kahan Se Pani.
Qabar-e-Be Sheer Pe Ashkon Ko Bahaya Sheh Ne.

5
Maa Ke Romal Mein Rakhwa Ke Maal Giriya.
Mere Ashkon Ko Gohar Bar Banaya Sheh Ne.
Main Kahan Our Kahan Noha e Shabbir Shamim.
Main Ne Woh Likh Diya Jo Mujh Se Likhaya Sheh Ne.
