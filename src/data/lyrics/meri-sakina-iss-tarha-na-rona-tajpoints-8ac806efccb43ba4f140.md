Meri Sakina Iss Tarha Na Rona Lyrics

1
Aakhari Salam
Tum Jao Kahan ALLAH Jane Maqtal Mein Rahein Kis Haal Mein Hum.
Ab Khuwab Mein Baba Aaein Gaye Chahe Sham Ka Woh Zindaan Howay.
Hum Jate Hain Milne Baba Se Ab Laut Ke Hum Ko Aana Nahi.
Kuch Dair Lipat Ja Seene Se Phir Baap ka Seena Milna Nahi.
So Jana Jahan Phophiyaan Keh Dein Chahe Sham Ka Woh Zindaan Howay.

2
Jane De Mujhe Maidaan Ki Taraf Dena Yeh Tujhe Qurbani Hai.
Rahwar Ke Mere Paon Na Par Meri Bachi Tu Syedani Hai.
Jeena Hai Tujhe Zahra Ki Tarha Chahe Sham Ka Woh Zindaan Howay.
Aa Poch Le Aansu Aankhon Se Tu Dekh Le Chehra Baba Ka.
Kal Ho Sakta Hai Aa Na Sake Pehchan Mein Chehra Baba Ka.
Hum Sath Tera Na Chorein Gaye Chahe Sham Ka Woh Zindaan Howay.

3
Abbas Ka Dil Akbar Ki Behan Jis Waqt Bandhe Gardan Mein Rasan.
Tum Baba Baba Mat Kehna Tarpe Ga Mera Be Goor Badan.
Asghar Ki Tarha Se Chup Rehna Chahe Sham Ka Woh Zindaan Howay.
Dekha Hai Rehan-o- Sarwar Ne Khud Sham Mein Ja Kar Yeh Manzar.
Selab Hai Pursa Daroon Ga Masroof-e-Buka Ta Had-e-Nazar.
Hai Sara Ilaqa Bibi Ka Chahe Sham Ka Woh Zindaan Howay.
