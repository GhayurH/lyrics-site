1
Jad ghazi ja k sam gya loko nahar kinaree
Chadran di zamanat mor lai sab parda daran ton haee tuty maanr ne sareee
1.mere allah zahra ty hai kinj da waila a gya
Aj behnr atharan veeran di maqatal wich bewarsan wang khaloti haee dy koi na saharee
2.asghar dy jholy non ro ro k masooma wakhdi
Kadi dar khamy dy ja ky nahar dy passee haee chache non pukaree
3.asghar dy laassy non haiee mazloom hathan ty cha kahara
Pya sochda hai kiven tapdiyan raitan dy wich haiee asghar non utaree
4.yaad aoundi karbal di akhtar aien wal waikh k
Jadoon alam da pak baneera nal hawa dy lainda haie hularee.
