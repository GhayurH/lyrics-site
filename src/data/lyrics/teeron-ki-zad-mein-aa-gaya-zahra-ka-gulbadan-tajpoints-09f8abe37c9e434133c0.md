Teeron Ki Zad Mein Aa Gaya Zahra Ka Gulbadan.
Sarmaya-e-Rasool Hai Shehzada-e-Aman.

1
Poshaak Jis Ki Khuld Se Laye They Jibraeel.
Ab Oss Ke Hai Badan Pe Zakhmo Ka Pairhan.
Barsay Hain Teer Itnay Lash-e-Hasan Pe Youn.
Rangeen Hai Lahu Mein Masmoom Ka Kafan.

2
Koi Mustafa Se Iss Ko Kaisay Juda Karey.
Mumkin Nahi Juz Oss Ke Takmeel-e-Panjtan.
Ik Tu Hi Tu Hasan Ka Nokar Nahi Ameer.
Har Mard-e-Bawafa Ka Sardar Hai Hasan.
