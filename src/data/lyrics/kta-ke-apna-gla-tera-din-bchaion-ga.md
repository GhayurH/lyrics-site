Kata kai apna gala tera dheen bachaun ga

1
May apnay khoon say rahay wafa sajaun ga

2
1) Howay na hongay abhi puray maa kay bhi armaan
Abhi to Kubra ka utra na hoga range hina
May khaima gah say Qasim ki lash laun ga

3
2) Hazar zulm karay nana chahay ahlay sitam
Teray Hussain ko hay teri azmatoon ki qasam
Kata to doonga magar sar na may jhukaun ga

4
3) Banain gay lashay merai samnay hi meray pisar
Wafa ka naz bhi soye ga jo woh soo-e-nehar
May phir bhi teray liyay ran main muskuraun ga

5
4) Khuda gawah hay teray deen ki baqa kay liye
Bhulana mujh ko peray nana jo Khuda kay liyai
To khoon teri shabih ka may bhool jaoon ga

6
5) Ta hashr bhool na paye ga zindagi meri
Jafa kay teeron main bhi misal bandagi meri
Main sath sath zamanay ko yaad aayun ga
