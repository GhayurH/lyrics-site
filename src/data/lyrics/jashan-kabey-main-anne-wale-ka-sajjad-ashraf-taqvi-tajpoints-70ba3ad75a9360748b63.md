Jashan Kabey Main Anne Wale Ka Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.

1
Jab Bhi Tera Rajab Ki Eid Aaye Ghar Sajatay Hain Muskuratay Hain.
Jashan Kabay Mein Aane Walay Ka Jo Halali Hain Woh Manatay Hain.
Jashan Hai Yeh Ghadeer Walay Ka Aao Iss Jashan Mein Jahan Walo.
Jin Pe Kausar Bhi Naaz Karta Hai Jaam Aisay Pilaye Jate Hain.
Khud Hawaein Diye Jalati Hain Chand Khud Chandni Bichata Hai.
Woh Hai Jashan-e-Nabi-o-Aal-e-Nabi Arsh Walay Bhi Jis Mein Aate Hain.

2
Dekh Kar Iss Wila Ki Mehfil Ko Keh Rahe Hain Farishtay Aapas Mein.
Khoob Fankar Hain Ali Walay Farsh Pe Janatein Sajatay Hain.
Ghazi Abbas Ka Alam Le Kar Hum Jo Barhtey Hain Ya Ali Keh Kar.
Manzalein Sath Chalne Lagti Hain Dar Ke Tofan Bhag Jate Hain.
Mera Dawa Hai Dekhna Ja Kar Sun Ke Zikar-e-Ali Zamane Mein.
Jin Ki Naslon Mein Tilmilahat Hai Woh Taraptay Hain Tilmilatay Hain.

3
Bar Sar-e-Bazm Har Munafiq Ka Dil Bhi Jalta Hai Khoon Bhi Jalta Hai.
Phir Bhi Koi Dhowaan Nahi Uthta Aag Hum Iss Tarha Lagatay Hain.
Kaun Gasib Hai Kaun Bagi Hai Kaun Talwarein Phaink Kar Bhaga.
Kis Ne Dhoka Diya Payamber Ko Hum Se Pocho Na Hum Batatay Hain.
Hum Mawali Ali Ke Hain Gohar Hum Hain Sajjad Ashiq-e-Haider.
Ya Ali Keh Ke Apne Hathoon Se Apni Taqdeer Khud Banatay Hain.
