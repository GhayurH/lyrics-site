1
Acha Yeh Mohabbat Ka Asar Dekh Raha Hoon.
Tum Hi Tum Nazar Aate Ho Jidhar Dekh Raha Hoon.
Kabay Ki Zarorat Hai Na Butkhane Ki Khowahish.
Jiss Simat Dekhate Ho Udhar Dekh Raha Hoon.
Yeh Hai Kashish-e-Husn Keh Hai Hasrat-e-Deedar.
Taqat Nahi Baqi Hai Magar Dekh Raha Hoon.
Kam Kam Sa Howa Jata Hai Taron Ka Jhalakna.
Aane Ko Hai Pigham-e-Sahar Dekh Raha Hoo.
Singer::Ahmad Hussain and Muhammad Hussain:
