Abbas E Ammu Janum Ali Jee Lyrics

1
Jan-e-Wafa-e-Aalam Abbas-e-Ammo Janum.
Darya Par Tum Jao Pani Le Kar Aao.
Pyasay Hain Hum Sab Pyasa Hai Asghar.
Woh Darya Behta Hai Sab Ka Galla Sokha Hai.
Tum Bhi Tu Pyasay Ho Baba Bhi Pyasa Hai.
Bhari Ho Lakhon Par Abbas-e-Ammo Janum.

2
Lo Yeh Alam Mashkeza Mere Pyaray Chacha.
Tum Ko Mili Saqayi Qurbaan Tum Pe Sakina.
Aye Sani-e-Haider Abbas-e-Ammo Janum.
Sokhay Hain Sab Kozay Samjhein Bachay Kaisay.
Sahil Par Pehra Hai Badil Bhi Na Barsay.
Kab Jaein Gay Hum Ghar Abbas-e-Ammo Janum.

3
Kya Foaj-e-Aada Hai Kya Iss Ka Pehra Hai.
Tum Ho Sani-e-Haider Pyasay Ho Tu Kya Hai.
Kya Rokay Ga Lashkar Abbas-e-Ammo Janum.
Jane Lagay Jab Ghazi Gonji Aah-o-Zaari.
Lab Pe Duaein Le Kar Pichey Chali Shehzadi.
Ahl-e-Haram Noha Gar Abbas-e-Ammo Janum.

4
Ammo Laut Ke Aana Hum Ko Na Rulwana.
Rakhay Khuda Hum Sab Ke Sar Pe Alam Ka Saya.
Aye Monis-o-Yawar Abbas-e-Ammo Janum.
Bolay Yeh Ro Kar Ghazi Wadah Karo Yeh Bibi.
Jab Yeh Alam Chup Jaye Aaye Alam Jab Khali.
Na Rona Yeh Keh Kar Abbas-e-Ammo Janum.
