1
Karbala Ki Rait Par Shabir Ne Sajda Kiya.
Sar Kata Ke Dasht Mein Hai Youn Bachaya La Illaha.
Qasim-o-Akbar Ko Sheh Ne Soye Maqtal Bhaij Kar.
Kar Diye Lakht-e-Jigar ISlam Par Sare Fida.

2
Aa Gaya Nook-e-Sina Par Deen Ki Khatir Hussain.
Dam Ba-Khud Tha Sara Alam Oss Ne Jab Quraan Parha.
Khaimo Mein Rone Lagin Sar Peet Kar Shahzadiyaan.
Hil Gaya Arsh-e-Bareen Jab Shah Pe Khanjar Chala.

3
Pyasa Tha Seh Rooz Ka Sawar-e-Doosh-e-Mustafa.
Qatal Tashna Ho Gaya Bint-e-Nabi Ka Ladla.
Karbala Ka Tha Musafir Ban Mein Be-Goor-o-Kafan.
Khak Ka Jis Ko Kafan Pehna Gayi Ro Kar Hawa.

4
Bhahi Ke Lashay Se Guzari Ho Ke Paband-e-Rasan.
Rehgayi Matam Ki Hasrat Boli Zainab Alwida.
Sar Kata Ke De Di Jis Ne Zindagi Islam Ko.
Aaj Hai Afraz Oss Ka Har Taraf Matam Bapa.
