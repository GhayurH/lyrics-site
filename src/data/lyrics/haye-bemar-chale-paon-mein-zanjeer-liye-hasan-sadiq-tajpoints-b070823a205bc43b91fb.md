Haye Bemar Chale Paon Mein Zanjeer Liye Hasan Sadiq.
Nangay Sar Haider-e-Karrar Ki Jageer Liye.

1
Na Liya Dharti Ne Khoon Our Na Falak Ne Chaha.
Mal Liya Chehray Pe Khoon Hathoon Mein Be Sheer Liye.
Raat Ko Khuwab Mein Akbar Ko Taraptay Dekha.
Aa Gaya Qasid-e-Sughra Wohi Tabeer Liye.

2
Dekh Kar Toota Alam Chadrein Sar Se Sarkin.
Soye Darya Hai Nazar Aankhon Mein Hai Neer Liye.
Khuwab Mein Dulhay Ko Karbal Mein Sajaya Najfi.
Ja Rahi Qabar Mein Dulhan Ko Bhi Taqdeer Liye.
