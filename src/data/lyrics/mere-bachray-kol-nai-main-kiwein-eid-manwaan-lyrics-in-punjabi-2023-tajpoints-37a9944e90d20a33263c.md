1
Mere Bachray Kol Nai Main Kiwein Eid Manwaan.
Karey Wain Ruqaiya Sir Pit Ke Suniyaan Na Jawan Hawaan.
Hath Joor Ruqaiya Aakhay Tainu Wasta Fazal Da Ghazi Mere Lal Mila Ik Wari.
Ik Putar Da Dukh Bhul Sakda Nayi Main Dowein Kinj Bhul Jawaan.

2
Be Chain Haan Main Oss Din Taun Mere Ibrahim, Muhammad Jis Din De Kofay Tur Gaye.
Koi Khair Khabar V Aayi Na Main Kis Nu Dard Sunawaan.
Haye Door Akhiyaan Taun Tur Gaye Hun Teek Na Mur Ke Aaye Main Sabar Karan Te Kiwein.
Gham Putraan Da Na Wekh Sakan Mar Jawan Jondiyaan Mawaan.

3
Muslim Diyaan Dhiyaan Ro Ro Shabeer Nu Deen Sadawaan Muk Jan Wichoray Mama.
Babal Veraan Nu Wekhan Layi Haye Gayiyaan Taras Nigahwaan.
Tainu Wasta Sain Ruqaiya Tere Wichar Gaye Putraan Da Aqdas Diyaan Arzaan Sun Le.
Har Momin De Dukh Door Howan Kar Laein Manzoor Duawaan.
