Hussain Kia hai Hussain kia hai

Tu phir bhi hum say, yeh poochta hai ….. Hussain Kia hai Hussain kia hai

) Zameer-e-Insaan jagaya jis nay .. Falak zameen ko banaya jis nay

Sina pay quraan sunaya jis nay .. nishaan-e-baatil mitaaya jis nay

khuda kay deen ko… bachaya jis nay … Hussain Woh hain.. Hussain Woh hain

) Khuda kay lashkar ka ameer-e-lashkar .. woh jis pay naaza hai rab-e-Akber

Hain jis kay ghar main farishtay naukar.. faqeer jiskay hain sab qalandar

Hain jis ki tashna ….labi samandar…. Hussain woh hai… Hussain woh hai

) Woh jisnay Karbobala banayee.. Woh jis nay bazm-e-wafa sajayee

Ali ka beta Hasan ka bhai… karay Jo nabiyoo ki rehnumaee

Shah ast Hussain… Baadshah ast Hussain.. Deen aas-e-Hussain… Deen pana ast Hussain

Khuda nay day dee …. jissay khudaee…. Hussain woh hai.. Hussain woh hai

) Hussain sardar-e-Alameen hai…Hussain dunya main misl-e-deen hai

risalato ka yehi ameer hai… bohot haseen hai bahut jamee hai

woh jiskay jaisa…. koi naheen hain ….. Hussain woh hai .. Hussain woh hai

) Zamana karwat zaroor layga… har aik moazzan azaan yeh dayga

zara tahar ja tu khud kahayga ….juloos jiska na ruk sakayga

Shah ast Hussain… Baadshah ast Hussain.. Deen aas-e-Hussain… Deen pana ast Hussain

woh jiska mataam… sada rahay ga …. Hussain woh hai.. Hussain woh hai

) Jo maqtalo main hayat baatay… bata do Gohar yeh woh sakhee hain

naheen hai kismat main teray baytay ….rasool-e-haq bhi yeh keh kay jeetay

jo saath baytay ….. ussi ko dayday … Hussain woh hai … Hussain woh hai
