Kaha Sakina ne Zainab se ye batao phuppi
Hamare khaimay logon ne kyun jalaye hain

1
Bataaon gi mere chacha ko zara anay do
Ae shimr(la) tu ne tamachay mujhe lagaye hain

2
Khata thi kya meray baba ki kya tha jurm kya
Ye teer baba pe ummat ne kyun chalaye hain

3
Thay teer itnay badan mein Sakina mil na saki
Hussain seenay pe beti sula na paye hain

4
Chiraag deene Muhammad ka bhuja dete ye log
Diye wo khoon se Shabbir ne jalaye hain
