Zikr Tera Jo Mustafa Sarwar Raza Karbalai.

1
Unn Ki Dehleez Pe Hai Sar Mera.
Zikar Hota Hai Arsh Par Mera.
Kar Raha Hoon Rasool Ki Main Sana.
Shajrah Kitna Hai Moatbar Mera.
Zikar Tera Jo Mustafa Na Karey.
Ho Zabaan Munh Mein Woh Khuda Na Karey.

2
Ghair Mumkin Hai Ho Nahi Sakta.
Sawal Koi Karey Our Atta Na Karey.
Hath Mein Oss Ke Boltay Kankar.
Mojza Yeh Tu Dosra Na Karey.
Iltija Hai Yeh Shehar Se Tere.
Rab-e-Akbar Mujhe Juda Na Karey.

3
Main Tu Beemar Hoon Madine Ka.
Mujh Ko Acha Koi Dawa Na Karey.
Hashar Mein Oss Pe Na Saya Hoga.
Aal-e-Ahmad Se Jo Wafa Na Karey.
Youn Sukhan:ASIF-E-Hazeen Pe Kabhi.
Band Sarwar Yeh Silsila Na Karey.
