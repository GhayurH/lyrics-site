1
Sitam Mujh Pe Hoye Baba Zia Abbas.
Sitam Mujh Par Hoye Baba Yeh Noha Fatima Ka Hai.
Tamam Ummat Ne Mil Ke Ghar Jalaya Fatima Ka Hai.
Tumhare Dil Ke Tukray Ko Tamachon Se Aziyat Di.
Bhula Baithay Hain Sab Zalim Jo Rutba Fatima Ka Hai.
Lagatay Roney Par Pehrey Ghazab Ka Zulm Dhatay Hain.
Gham-e-Ahsas Se Lab Par Yeh Jumla Fatima Ka Hai.
Zamana Ho Gaya Dushman Nahi Apna Koi Baqi.
Musalmaan Ne Kiya Pehlu Shikasta Fatima Ka Hai.
Kaleja Phat Gaya Mera Mujhe Jhota Bhi Keh Dala.
Bharay Darbar Mein Phir Haq Bhi Chena Fatima Ka Hai.
Miley Hain Zakhm Woh Mujh Ko Jigar Zakhmi Hai Dil Zakhmi.
Howa Hai Qatal Mohsin Bhi Yeh Nalah Fatima Ka Hai.
Kaleja Tukray Karta Hai Zia, Saaib Ka Yeh Noha.
Shikasta Pasliyaan Hain Dil Bhi Toota Fatima Ka Hai.
