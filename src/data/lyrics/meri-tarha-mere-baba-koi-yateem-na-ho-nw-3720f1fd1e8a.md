meri tarah mere baba ,koi yateem na ho hui hai jai se sakina, koi yateem na ho

yateem ho ke yatimi ka dard samjha hai ye gham saha nahi jata ,koi yatim na ho

suna tha log yatimon se pyar karte hain main bad nasib hun baba,koi yatim na ho

koi to sar pa muhabbat se hath rakh deta koi to deta dilaasa, koi yateem na ho

mujhe yatimi ka ehsaas yeh dilaata hai mera fata hua kurta,koi yateem na ho

zameen pe neend bhi aye to kis trha aye chhuta hai baap ka seena, koi yateem na ho

laeen ne kano se khechi hen baliyan meri lahoo luhaan hai chehra, koi yateem na ho

juda na ho kabhi irfan baap, beti se uthe na baap ka saya, koi yateem na ho
