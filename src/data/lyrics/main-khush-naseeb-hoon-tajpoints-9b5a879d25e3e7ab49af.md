Main Shaheed-e-Rah-e-Khuda Hoon.

1
Karam Hussain Ka Mujh Par Mazeed Ho Jaye.
Maron Jo Farsh-e-Azza Par Tu Eid Ho Jaye.
Ajeeb Jazba Hai Har Maa Duaein Karti Hai.
Khuda Karey Mera Bacha Shaheed Ho Jaye.
Aayi Hai Ik Shaheed Ki Yeh Khuld Se Sada.
Wajh-e-Hayat Ban Gayi Mere Liye Qaza.

2
Labaik Ki Uthti Thin Har Ik Samt Sadaein.
Khud Maut Mujhe Deti Thi Jeene Ki Duaein.
Tazeem Ko Phir Jhuk Gayin Khud Meri Nigahein.
Phir Murtaza Ne Mujh Ko Galay Se Laga Liya.
Yeh Dars Mujhe Apni Shahadat Se Mila Hai.
Shabeer Pe Marne Mein Hi Jeene Ka Maza Hai.

3
Ab Taba Qayamat Mera Ghar Karb-o-Bala Hai.
Qatra Tha Apni Zaat Mein Darya Se Ja Mila.
So Jan Se Qurban Mili Kaisi Sadat.
Mojood They Baleen Pe Meri Shah-e-Wilayat.
Oss Dam Thin Nigahein Meri Masroof-e-Ziyarat.
Kab Mera Dam Nikal Gaya Mujh Ko Nahi Pata.

4
Masoom Ke Anwar Ka Youn Ban Gaya Zair.
Jaisay Kissi Manzil Pe Pohanch Jaye Musafir.
Ghazi Se Mutahir Ne Kiya Hai Mujhe Tahir.
Sab Dhul Gaye Gunah Lahu Jab Mera Baha.
Turbat Se Andheron Ka Guzar Ho Nahi Sakta.
Mojood Hain Masoom Hai Mahool Kisa Ka.

5
Matam Kabhi Hota Hai Kabhi Hota Hai Giriya.
Mera Mazar Sheh Ka Azakhana Ban Gaya.
Main Kaisay Bhula Sakta Hoon Ashor-e-Muharram.
Zakhmo Se Badan Choor Tha Our Qarb Ka Aalam.
Yaad Aa Gayi Fitrus Ki Kahani Mujhe Oss Dam.
Dali Nazar Hussain Ne Har Zakhm Bhar Gaya.

6
Aye Dosto Ho Lakh Mukhalif Yeh Zamana.
Sar Apna Kabhi Zulm Ke Aagay Na Jhokana.
Nu Lakh Bhi Aa Jaein Tu Tum Khof Na Khana.
Meri Tarha Jo Tum Ko Bhi Deni Hai Yeh Sada.
Ab Mere Aaizza Ke Liye Hai Yeh Meri Baat.
Jeeta Tu Kahan Hoti Bhala Meri Yeh Oqaat.

7
Hai Khuld Mein Ghar Hur-e-Dilawar Ka Mere Sath.
Zarray Se Mujh Ko Shah Ne Soraj Bana Diya.
Paigham Takallum Yeh Zamane Ko Suna Do.
Rutba Jo Shaheedon Ka Hai Dunya Ko Bata Do.
Lau Shoq-e-Shahadat Ki Kuch Iss Tarha Barha Do.
Jaan De Ke Har Hussain Ka Ashiq Yeh De Sada.
