Kyun Na Serab Kare Sibt e Nabi Ka Lashkar Hasnain Akbar
Hur Se Takmeel Pe Pohanchay Ga Jari Ka Lashkar.

1
Nehar Par Aane Tu Do Tashna Labi Ka Lashkar.
Ghaat Ko Chor Ke Bhagay Ga Nadi Ka Lashkar.
Sabar Our Shukar Se Serab They Seene Unn Ke.
Nehar Pe Toota Nahi Tashna Labi Ka Lashkar.

2
Faisla Waqt Karey Kya Hai Yahan Fatha-o-Shikast.
Yeh Jigardar Ka Lashkar Woh Anni Ka Lashkar.
Koor Chashmi Ke Samandar Se Nikal Aate Hain Hur.
Hashar Beni Pe Ho Jab Deedah Wari Ka Lashkar.

3
Halqa-e-Sabar Mein Zanjeer Bakaf They Sabir.
Darbadar Hota Raha Darbadari Ka Lashkar.
Jis Jagah Maut Ka Dam Toot Gaya Tha Akbar.
Oss Se Aagay Tha Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Ka Lashkar.
