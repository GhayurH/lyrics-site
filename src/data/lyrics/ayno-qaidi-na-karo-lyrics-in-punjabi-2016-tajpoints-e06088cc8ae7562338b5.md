1
Ayno Qaidi Na Karo Koi Dardi Nahi Ayda Jera Parde Aoun Bachaye.
Reh Reh Ke Nehar Furat Walon Aawaz Abbas Di Aaway.
Main E Saman Aidi Chadar Da Zeen Taun Lathiya Jeray Welay Da.
Fair Jurtaan Aye Shimar Kitiyaan Ne Naam Zainab Da Sar-e-Aam Liya.
Hun Lut Ke Chadar Zainab Di Piya Naizay Te Lehraway.
Hajra Bach Ke Nayi Roi Aye Ainay Yaqoob Da Kita Aye Sabar.
Jiwein Yousuf De Layi Roya Nabi Le Ke Kurta Te Guzari Zindagi.
Aynj Kurta Le Ke Asghar Da Maa Akhiyaan De Nal Laway.
Kool Shabeer De Sowan Tur Gayi Wekh Zid Kar Ke Sakina Veera.
Assi Mastoraan Utton Be Pardah Kala Sajjad V Beemar Bara.
Koi Bachiya Nayi Jera Maqtal Choon Ohno Ja Ke Moor Leaway.
Manzalaan Sham Diyaan Sokhiyaan San Jay Te Abbas Aiday Naal Honda.
Moor Bazaraan De Khutbay Sare Yaan Te Shabeer Ya Ghazi Denda.
Hun Tak Tak Veran Neziyaan Te Payi Khutbay Aap Sunaway.
Yaad Abbas Di Jurat Kar Ke Ibn-e-Haider Taun Madad Mangda Aye.
Jithay Ghazi Da Alam Aaway Nazar Har Azadar Fakhar Karda Aye.
 Akbar Tazeem Wich Ghazi Di Sir Matami Da Jhuk Jaway.
