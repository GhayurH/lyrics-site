1
Janaze Par Hasan Ke Jo Jafa Ke Teer Aaein Gaye.
Na Tehrein Gay Galay Asghar Talak Woh Teer Jaein Gay.
Jinhoon Ne Tukray Maula Ka Jigar Shah Ko Dikhaya Hai.
Sina Se Chak Akbar Ka Yeh Seena Bhi Dikhaein Gay.

2
Jo Qasim Par Yateemi Ka Bhiyanak Waqt Laye Hain.
Yeh Hi Keh Kar Sakina Ko Yateema Bhi Bulaein Gay.
Jinhoon Ne Dafan Hone Par Bhi Pehrey Ko Laga Rakha.
Zulm Ki Had Hai Yeh Pani Pe Pehrey Bhi Lagaein Gay.

3
Yeh Ik Maiyyat Hai Jo Ghar Ko Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Laye.
Kayi Lashay Yeh Karbal Mein Akelay Hi Uthaein Gay.
Dua Hai Joun Aa Jaein Mere Maula Tu Ho Manzar.
Musayab Mujtaba Ka Hum Baqiya Mein Sunaein Gay.
