Ronaq e Dida e Hairan Abu Talib Hai Iftikhar Haider.
Roshni Ta Had e Imkan Abu Talib Hai.

1
Do Nigehban Hain Ummat Ke Nabi Our Imam.
Our Dono Ka Nigehban Abu Talib Hai.
Dil Ka Dalan Keh Hai Shor-e-Wila Se Pur Shoor.
Dil Ke Dalan Mein Mehman Abu Talib Hai.

2
Raaz Dunya Pe Payamber Ki Kafalat Se Khula.
Mansha-o-Nusrat-e-Yazdan Abu Talib Hai.
Maan Sakta Hai Kissi Ko Tu Nabi Ka Mohsin?.
Maan Sakta Hai Tu Phir Maan Abu Talib Hai.

3
Oss Ke Betay Ke Bhi Betay Hain Hussain Our Hasan.
Sahib-e-Lu Lu-o-Marjan Abu Talib Hai.
Aap Jaisay Karein Eman Pe Oss Ke Batein.
Mere Jaison Ka Tu Eman Abu Talib Hai.
