Zahra Ke Dar Ki Nukari

1
Karte Hain Sab Wali Nabi Zahra Ke Dar Ki Nukari

2
Shahi Se Barh Keh Qeemati Zahra Ke Dar Ki Nukari

3
Dunya Ki Kya Bisaat Hai Iss Ki Nazar Mein Khak Hai
Pakeeza Jis Ka Khon Hai Jis Ka Nasab Bhi Pak Hai
Karta Rahe Ga Bas Wohi Zahra Key Dar Ki Nukari

4
Muthi Mein Kainat Hai Shahi Mein Shan Yeh Kahan
Beti Kaha Rasool Ne Hasnain Keh Rahe Hain Maa
Fiza Ko Youn Azeez Thi Zahra Key Dar Ki Nukari

5
Zahra Ke Ahtram Ne Yeh Martaba Atta Kiya
Dozakh Mein Ja Raha Tha Woh Shabir Ne Bacha Liya
Hur ko Naseeb Ho Gayi Zahra Key Dar Ki Nukari

6
Arman Hai Batool Ka ALLAH Ko Pasand Hai
Khushiyon Ki Kainat Tu Ashk-e-Azza Mein Band Hai
Youn Kar Rahe Hain Matami Zahra Key Dar Ki Nukari

7
Dar-e-Fana Ko Choor Keh Momin Jo Koi Jaye Ga
Saya Abu Turab Ka Turbat Mein Phail Jaye Ga
Apna Asar Dikhaye Gi Zahra Key Dar Ki Nukari

8
Shamil The Ahl-e-Bait Mein Kitne Azeem Ho Gaye

9
Salman Ki Hayat Mein Do Hi Tu Aise Kam The

10
Har Dam Ali Ali Ali Zahra Ke Dar Ki Nukari

11
Alim Ko Har Khateeb Ko Zakir Ko Madah Khuwab Ko
Shayar Ko Soz Khuwan Ko Har Matami Jawan Ko
Bunyad-e-Zarf Par Mili Zahra Key Dar Ki Nukari
