1
Aj De Ga Kon Pehray Haye Shama Pay Gayiyan Ne.
Abbas Veer Aa Ja, Abbas Veer Aa Ja Chadraan V Le Gayiyaan Ne.
Shimar e Laeen Nu Tak Ke Masooma Dar Ke Rondi.
Tera Naam Le Ke Ghazi Ro Ro Tainu Bolaondi.
Munh Te Chaperan Chacha Tere Baad Pe Gayiyaan Ne.
Haye Raat Way Haneri Darday Nayi Baal Sonday.
Darya De Pasay Tak Ke Tainu Yaad Kar Ke Ronday.
Aa Wekh Parday Daraan Ro Ro Ke Kendiyaan Ne.
Teri Aas Te Main Ghazi Chadiya Watan Madina.
Meri Maa De Ghar Nu Lutiya Aa Wekh Le Laeena.
Banwaan Na Wekh Ghazi Rasiyaan V Pe Gayiyaan Ne.
Ghazi Jina De Parday Toon Aap Si Banaye.
Soraj Jina Nu Tak Ke Badlaan Che Munh Lokaye.
Aa Wekh Parday Daraan Aountaan Te Beh Gayiyaan Ne.
Wich Sham De Bazaraan Roi Aye Kul Khudai.
Be Ghairatan Ne Hasna Ronra Aye Zahra Jayi.
 Sardar Parday Daraan Har Zulm Seh Gayiyaan Ne.
