1
Bhaiya Main Aa Gayi Hoon Nana Ka Dein Bacha Ke.
Jaon Gi Ab Madina Chehlum Tera Mana Ke.
Maloom Hai Yeh Tum Ko Zainab Ujar Chuki Hai.
Lekin Teri Shahadat Ghar Ghar Pohanch Gayi Hai.
Shabash Do Behan Ko Bhaiyya Galay Laga Ke.
Bhaiyya Tumhari Zainab Ab Woh Nahi Rahi Hai.
Pehchan Lo Gay Mujh Ko Halat Badal Gayi Hai.
Chalti Hai Yeh Zaeefa Bhai Kamar Jhuka Ke.
Sajjad Our Baqir Dein Gay Gawahi Meri.
Khaye Hain Taziyane Bint-e-Ali Ne Phir Bhi.
Le Aayi Shamiyon Se Waris Tere Bana Ke.
Kaisay Bataon Bhaiyya Bazar Mein Jo Guzri.
Kehte They Sab Ke Maro Yeh Hai Ali Ki Beti.
Baba Ke Bhi Main Badlay Sab Aayi Hoon Chuka Ke.
Ummat Ne Tujh Pe Bhai Roney Kahan Diya Hai.
Jee Bhar Ke Main Ne Tera Matam Kahan Kiya Hai.
Roye Gi Aaj Zainab Farsh-e-Azza Bicha Ke.
Ghar Ja Ke Kiya Karon Gi Pehlu Mein Hi Sula Lo.
Akbar Se Keh Ke Mujh Ko Turbat Ki Ja Dila Do.
Malik Ho Tum Tu Bhai Jageer-e-Karbala Ke.
Yeh Keh Ke Aye Takallum Uthi Ali Ki Beti.
Abbas Ki Lehad Par Pohancha De Mujh Ko Koi.
Dikhla Doon Bawafa Ko Rasi Ke Neel Ja Ke.
