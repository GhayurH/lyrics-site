1
Shahzada Akbara Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics
Shehzada-e-Akbara Rasha Pas-e-Lona Waqt Nu Bahar De.
Khat Likhti Hai Sughra Bhaiyya Watan Aa Ja Waqt Nu Bahar De.
Khat Likhti Hoon Ashkon Se Bheega Hai Mera Daman.
Aa Ja Keh Bahar Aayi Soona Hai Mera Aangan.
Aye Bhai Khudara Rasha Pas-e-Lona Waqt Nu Bahar De.
Phir Gonjay Azaan Teri Nana Ke Madine Mein.
Phir Bhar De Khuda Bhaiyya Thandak Merey Seene Mein.
Pora Karo Wadah Rasha Pas-e-Lona Waqt Nu Bahar De.
Dekhon Main Tera Sehra Dulhan Teri Ghar Aaye.
Dedar Tera Bhaiyya Bas Naig Mein Mil Jaye.
Aanchal Mera Phaila Rasha Pas-e-Lona Waqt Nu Bahar De.
Beemar Behan Roye Kya Tum Ko Gawara Hai.
Bhaiyya Teri Furqat Ne Bin Maut Ke Mara Hai.
Kehti Hai Yeh Behna Rasha Pas-e-Lona Waqt Nu Bahar De.
Gul Khilney Ki Rut Aayi Pardesi Marey Bhai.
Tum Door Watan Se Ho Main Hoon Meri Tanhai.
Tarpao Na Bhaiyya Rasha Pas-e-Lona Waqt Nu Bahar De.
 Rehan Bhi Sarwar Bhi Kehte Hain Yehi Sughra.
Bethi Hi Rahi Dar Par Akbar Ki Behan Sughra.
Lab Par Tha Yeh Noha Rasha Pas-e-Lona Waqt Nu Bahar De.
