1
Jalal-e-Rab-e-Aula Hai Hussain Ka Ghazi.
Shabeeh-e-Aks-e-Khuda Hai Hussain Ka Ghazi.
Begair Hathon Ke Dunya Mein Hath Walon Ko.
Naseeb Bant Raha Hai Hussain Ka Ghazi.

2
Ho Kyun Na Iss Ke Waseelay Se Har Dua Pori.
Keh Fatima Ki Dua Hai Hussain Ka Ghazi.
Wajood-e-Nuqta-e-Ba Hain Agar Ali-e-Wali.
Tu Fakhar-e-Nuqta-e-Ba Hai Hussain Ka Ghazi.

3
Bata Raha Hai Humein Itmenan Zaianb Ka.
Gharor-e-Aal-e-Abba Hai Hussain Ka Ghazi.
Farat Khoof-e-Shujat Se Sokh Na Jaye.
Nehar Ki Samt Chala Hai Hussain Ka Ghazi.

4
Zamana Lal Qalandar Ko Na Samajh Paya.
Tu Kaise Samjhe Ga Kya Hai Hussain Ka Ghazi.
Bhara Howa Hai Mera Kasa-e-Talab Yasir.
Keh Mujh Ko Paal Raha Hai Hussain Ka Ghazi.
