Nana Tere Wasday Shehar Wichon Shabir Musafir Chaliya
Tere Kalmago Nayi Rehnr Denday Tere Jigar Day Tukray Nu
Sang Balaan Te Mastoraan Day Tere Deen Da Nasir Chaliya
Sughra Di Bani Majbori Aidi Shaqal Batool Di Pori
Mataan Loki Samjhan Zahra Aye Wich Sham Bazaraan Day
Aisay Layi Chad Ke Rondi Nu Aida Veer Ali Akbar Chaliya
Jithay Likhna Si Bismillah Othay Likh Ke Inna Lillah
Wich Karb-o-Bala Di Nagri De Ghar Bar Lutawanr Layi
Haye Lay Fehrist Musafiraan Di Dardaan Da Saudagar Chaliya
Jadoon Jandray Gharaan Nu Laye Aye Wainr Beemar Ne Paye
Koi Rab De Wastay Amri Nu Mera Paigham Pohanchway
Main Kiwein Veer Khadawaan Gi Mera Veer Ali Asghar Chaliya
Bach Janra Aye Deen Khuda Da Ghar Ujar Janra Zahra Da
Ghazi Nu Jang Na Karan Layi Shabir Paband Keeta
Taiyon Asghar Besheer Jaye Lay Naal Bahadur Chaliya
Meri Sughra Kaliyaan Reh Gayi Mainu Rondiyaan Chadni Payi Gayi
Tere Deway Mere Bad Nana Beemar Jalaway Gi
Hun Hoon Gaye Mail Naseebaan Nal Tere Dar Da Majawar Chaliya
Jadon Khaimiyaan Nu Ag Bha Gayi Maa Aoun Di Bahir Aa Gayi
Deway Mur Mur Haklaan Ghazi Nu Beemar Koo Aan Bacha
Kiwein Putar Sajjad Bachawaan Main Sajjad Mera Mar Chaliya
Jidi Khatir Pak Rasool Aye Sajda Cha Kita Tool Aye
Subhana Rabbi Al Ala Kiya Satar Dafa Ahmad Ne
AKHTAR Shabir Aj Satraan Di Tha Dainr Bahatar Chaliyaa
