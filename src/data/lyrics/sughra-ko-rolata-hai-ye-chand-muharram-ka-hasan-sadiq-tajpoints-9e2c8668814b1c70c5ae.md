Sughra Ko Rulata Hai Ye Chand Muharram Ka
Jab Samne Aata Hai Yeh Chand Muharram Ka

1
Hum Sorat-e-Zahra Se Bhai Ke Bicharne Ki
Khatoon-e-Qayamat Ke Ghar Bar Ujarney Ki
Rodad Sunata Hai Yeh Chand Muharram Ka

2
Kata Hai Gala Logo Seh Roz Ke Pyasay Ka
Pamal Howa Lasha Ahmad Ke Nawasay Ka
Har Ik Ko Batata Hai Yeh Chand Muharram Ka

3
Tasveer Dikhata Hai Ahmad Ki Jawani Ki
Har Shaks Ko Dunya Mein Karbal Ki Kahani Ki
Tafseer Batata Hai Yeh Chand Muharram Ka

4
Karbal Ki Ziyarat Ko Jo Aankh Tarasti Thi
Shabeer Tere Gham Mein Jo Aankh Barasti Thi
Oss Aankh Ko Bhata Hai Yeh Chand Muharram Ka

5
Imran Ko Shabar Ko Khaton e Qayamat Ko
Haider Ki Qasam Mazhar Sarkar-e-Risalat Ko
Turbat Mein Rulata Hai Yeh Chand Muharram Ka
