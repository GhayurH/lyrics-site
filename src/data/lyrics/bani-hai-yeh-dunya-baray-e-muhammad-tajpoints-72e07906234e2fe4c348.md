Bani Hai Yeh Dunya Baray-e-Muhammad.

1
Batata Hai Khud Yeh Khuda-e-Muhammad.

2
Shab-e-Qadar Quran-e-Samat Ki Sorat.
Mukamal Hoi Hia Sana-e-Muhammad.
Sar-e-Arsh Mairaj Ki Inteha Pe.
Na Pohancha Koi Bhi Siway-e-Muhammad.
Bare Chain Se Bistar-e-Mustafa Pe.
Ali So Rahe Hain Bajay-e-Muhammad.
Yeh Man Kunto Mula Se Sabit Howa Hai.
Wilay-e-Ali Hai Wilay-e-Muhammad.
Jabhi Tu Hai Horon Ke Chehray Darakhshan.
Lagati Hain Woh Khak-e-Paye-e-Muhammad.
Hai Pusht-e-Nabi Pe Baqay-e-Nabowat.
Wahi Aayi Sar Na Uthay-e-Muhammad.
Jo Barah Cherag-e-Imamat Hain Roshan.
Hai Unn Sab Mein Yasir Zia-e-Muhammad.
