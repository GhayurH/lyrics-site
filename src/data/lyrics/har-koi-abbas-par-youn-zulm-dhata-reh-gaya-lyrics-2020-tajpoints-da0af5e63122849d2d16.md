Har Koi Abbas Par Youn Zulm Dhata Reh Gaya.
Aamad-e-Shabeer Tak Lasha Bhi Aadha Reh Gaya.

1
Aik Ik Lasha Utha Maidan Se Dono Taraf.
Bas Wahan Abbas Our Maula Ka Lasha Reh Gaya.
Rakh Ke Khaimay Mein Alam Sheh Ne Tanabein Kaat Dein.
Youn Tasawar Mein Kafan Bhai Ko Deta Reh Gaya.

2
Do Katay Bazu Utha Kar Uth Na Paya Khak Se.
Jis Jagah Betha Wahin Shabeer Betha Reh Gaya.
Maa Yeh Kehti Thin Pukaro Mat Mujhe Beton Ki Maa.
So Gaya Abbas Tu Phir Kaun Zindah Reh Gaya.

3
Kaisay Naizay Par Woh Nazron Mein Sakina Ki Rahe.
Iss Liye Shayad Sar-e-Abbas Girta Reh Gaya.
Jis Ne Chalna Seekha Akbar Zainab-o-Kulsoom Se.
Oss Ka Sar Bhi Chadron Se Door Chalta Reh Gaya.
