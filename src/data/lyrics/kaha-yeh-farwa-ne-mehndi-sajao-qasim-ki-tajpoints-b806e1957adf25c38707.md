Kaha Yeh Farwa Ne Mehndi Sajao Qasim Ki.
Saja Ke Dasht Se Baraat Lao Qasim Ki.

1
Kaha Hussain Se Zainab Ne Joor Do Tukray.
Phir Oss Ke Bad Hi Behnein Bulao Qasim Ki.
Kaha Yeh Zainab-e-Muztar Ne Meri Kubra Se.
Main Chahti Hoon Keh Gathri Chupao Qasim Ki.

2
Hasan Ke Lal Ke Sab Bibiyaan Karo Matam.
Saf-e-Azza Pe Hi Rasmein Nibhao Qasim Ki.
Tumhare Ghar Mein Bhi Aaein Gi Fatima Zahra.
Khalos Se Saf-e-Matam Bichao Qasim Ki.

3
Rasan Hoon Hathon Mein Sar Par Ho Khak Maqtal Ki.
Is Ayhtmam Se Dulhan Ko Lao Qasim Ki.
Bikhar Na Jaein Kahin Ran Mein Phool Sehray Ke.
Zara Sanbhal Ke Maiyyat Uthao Qasim Ki.

4
Mujhe Hai Khof Keh Kubra Kahin Na Mar Jaye.
Na Aaj Terhriwein Mannat Barhao Qasim Ki.
Janab-e-Zahra Ne Mujh Se Kaha Ke Aye Zaire.
Tum Ahl-e-Hashar Ko Ghurbat Sunao Qasim Ki.
