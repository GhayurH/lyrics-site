1
Sawal Ummat Se Kar Raha Hai Hasan Ka Teeron Bhara Janaza.
Maqam Itrat Ka Pochta Hai Hasan Ka Teeron Bhara Janaza.
Chala Janazay Ke Peeche Peeche Wafa Ke Khaliq Ka Zinda Lasha.
Hussain Wapis Jo Le Chala Hai Hasan Ka Teeron Bhara Janaza.

2
Ali Ki Dukhtar Yeh Sochti Hai Ghareeb-e-Karbal Ka Kya Bane Ga.
Watan Pe Jab Samne Para Hai Hasan Ka Teeron Bhara Janaza.
Fidak Ke Ghasib Howay Hain Qabaz Nabi Ki Turbat Pe Kis Tarha Se.
Sawal Ban Ke Ruka Howa Hai Hasan Ka Teeron Bhara Janaza.

3
Hussain Maula Tum Hi Batao Kamaan Ki Zad Pe Hasan Ka Tan Hai.
Ya Tere Be Sheer Ka Gala Hai Hasan Ka Teeron Bhara Janaza.
Baraste Teeron Mein Jane Kyun Kar Ajeeb Hasrat Bhari Nigah Se.
Nabi Ke Roze Ko Dekhta Hai Hasan Ka Teeron Bhara Janaza.

4
Ab Hoon Gaye Manzar Yahin Tu Aksar Nabi Ki Itrat Ki Maiyyaton Pe.
Baraste Teeron Ki Ibtida Hai Hasan Ka Teeron Bhara Janaza.
Hijab-e-Ghaibat Mein Ro Rahe Hain Laho Jo Behlool Maula Qayam.
Youn Un Ki Aankhon Mein Bas Gaya Hai Hasan Ka Teeron Bhara Janaza.
