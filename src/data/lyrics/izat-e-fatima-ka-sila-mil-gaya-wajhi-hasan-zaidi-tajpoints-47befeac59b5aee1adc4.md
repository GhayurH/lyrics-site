1
Izat e Fatima Ka Sila Mil Gaya Wajhi Hasan Zaidi.
Hur Ko Ik Raat Mein Kibriya Mil Gaya.
Hal Atta Ki Atta Jis Pe Nazil Hoi.
Oss Ko Sarwar Ka Farsh-e-Azza Mil Gaya.

2
Baab-e-Zahra Pe Aaye Tu Bolay Malak.
La Makaan Ke Makaan Ka Pata Mil Gaya.
Muskuraye Hain Sajjad Muddat Ke Baad.
Yani Mukhtar Ko Hurmala Mil Gaya.

3
Thi Sada Baab-e-Zahra Pe Salman Ki.
Jibraeel Aao Arsh-e-Olla Mil Gaya.
Sari Dunya Se Woh Ho Gayi Be Niyaz.
Bait-e-Zahra Mein Fizza Ko Kya Mil Gaya.

4
Hai Yaqeen Oss Ko Mil Jaein Gay Khud Imam.
Murtajiz Ka Jisay Naqsh-e-Pa Mil Gaya.
Hain Hijab-e-Khuda Ki Kaneez Iss Liye.
Fizza Ko Lehja ALLAH Sa Mil Gaya.

5
Kya Mili Mustafa Ko Kisaye Batool.
Haan Shifa Khana e LaIlaha Mil Gaya.
Qabar Mein Maula Aaye Tu Dil Ne Kaha.
Aye Aimaad Ab Tujhey Bhi Khuda Mil Gaya.
