1
Rasool-e-Khuda Ne Jisay Maa Kaha Hai.
Janab-e-Khadija Si Maa Jis Ne Payi.
Jisay Rab-e-Akbar Ne Di Parsai.
Jo Sarkar Ke Pak Dil Ki Dua Hai.

2
Woh Jis Ka Padar Ashraf Al-Anbiya Ho.
Shareek-e-Safar Jis Ka Shair-e-Khuda Ho.
Woh Jis Ka Shajar Shajrah-e-Tayyaba Hai.
Jawanan-e-Janat Ke Sardar Betay.

3
Rasool-e-Khuda Ke Madadgar Betay.
Falak Par Bhi Mamnon Jis Ka Khuda Hai.
Kaneezi Mein Jis Ki Habash Ki Ho Malka.
Kahin Jis Ko Hasnain Bhi Amma Fizza.

4
Jahan Mein Faqat Aik Hi Malika Hai.
Koi Syeda Ke Barabar Kahan Ho.
Woh Jis Ke Tasaruf Mein Kaun -o-Makaan Ho.
Jo Mushkil Kusha Ki Bhi Mushkil Kusha Hai.

5
Woh Jis Ki Taharat Pe Tatheer Aayi.
Madine Mein Ismat Ki Basti Basai.
Jo Kulsoom-o-Zainab Ke Sar Ki Rida Hai.
Hussain-o-Hasan Our Zainab Jo Palay.

6
Ho Jis Baiyat Mein Ismaton Ke Ujalay.
Ke Jis Ke Gharanay Mein Mushkil Kusha Hai.
Jo Darbar-e-Gasib Ko Dhaney Gayi Thi.
Jo Choron Ki Shakalein Dikhaney Gayi Thi.

7
Nabi Ki Wirasat Ki Jo Warisa Hai.
 Zafar Tum Adab Se Parho Yeh Qaseeda.
Farishton Ne Aa Kar Kiya Jin Ko Sajdah.
Azza Our Azadar Jis Ki Dua Hai.
