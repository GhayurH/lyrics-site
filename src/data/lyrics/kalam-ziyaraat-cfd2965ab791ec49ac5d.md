Rukta nahi ankhon se haey khoon muhari ka
Ghairat bana sabab hay Abid ki beemari ka

1
Karti rahi tu mintan, nahi watan pohchaya
Majboor is tarha may hoya, tujhe kafa bhi na diya
Lasha utha ke roya wo bhain pyari ka

2
Rukda nae akhiyan chon haey khoon muhari da
Ghairat hay sabab baniya Abid di beemari da

3
1) Kardi rahi tu mintan nahi watan tay pohnchaya
Majbor ainjh may hoya tainu kafan wi na paya
Chuk laasha bohat roya o behan pyari da

4
2) Abid di wekh majma hoeyan akhiyan nay sawan
Ghazi di behan aa gaeye aay shoor wich bazaran
Keta aa kay wain shaami Zainab di sawari da

5
3) Mar gayi teri Sakina aa wain yaad tu Shabbir aay
Abid di hai majbori haye touq tay zanjeer aay
Kewain laasha uthaeya ga haey darda di mari da

6
4) Sir nangay dhee Ali di bazaran day wich turna
Shabbir day qatil toun Zainab da rida mangna
Sajjad tu puch manzar Zainab di lachari da

7
5) Ahlay haram nu zalim darbar wich bula kay
O Ayaatan Qurani naizay utay charha kay
Hilya na dil kisay wi hafiz kisay qari da

8
6) Zainab aay dheen kaar din a Shimar mar koray
Beemar hay o laghar bari dair da bey hosh aay
Muk jaway na saan zalim Sajaad muhari da

9
7) Aay shamiyo main Zainab haan main Batool jayaan
Sir nangay haath wich rasiyan ho qaid shaam ayi
Waikho na aiyngh tamasha haye meri lachari da

10
8) Aj tak nahi aay samghay Tanveer musalman aay
Shabbir da ghum karna aay dheen tay imaan aay
Jannat aay sila adna aidi matam dari da

11
Mera waqt hay akheeri, mayno babay keeta yaad aey
Meray kol rasan veeran palan di tu Sajjad aey
Muk safar gaya Abid, aj safran di mari da

12
Ghayyur mar na jaway wich Shaam de mazloom aey
Aey laal ooday qaidi Zainab aey tay Kulsoom aey
Sajjad nahi aadi aes bheerr bazari da

13
Rat akhyan di hay wadh gaye ho zard gaya chehra
Darbar hajay door hay kar hosla chan mera
Sir nezay te rownda aey Quran de qari da

14
Hun tak nae hay samjhay Tanweer Muslman
Shabbir da gham karna aey deen te eeman aey
Jannat hay sala adna aidi matam dari da
