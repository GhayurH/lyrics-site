Jis Ne Khuda Ke Liye Ghar Bar Lutaya.
Jis Ne Sina Par Humein Quran Sunaya.

1
Jis Ne Humein Mar Ke Bhi Jeena Hai Sikhaya.
Aisay Sardar Ko Shah e Abrar Ko Maula Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Kehte Hain.
Woh Mera Rahnuma Mera Aaqa Bhi Wohi.
Jis Ka Nana Hai Nabi Baap Waliyon Ka Wali.

2
Fatima Jis Ki Hain Maa Bhai Jaisa Na Koi.
Aisay Sultan Ko Oss Nigehban Ko.
Maula Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Kehte Hain.
Jagah Jagah Hai Gonjta Maula Hi Maula Ya Hussain.

3
Sare Sardar Jisay Apna Sardar Kahin.
Sare Masoom Jisay Apna Dedar Kahin.
Our Hum Jaisay Jisay Rab Ka Shahkar Kahin.
Aisay Sardar Ko Shah e Abrar Ko Maula Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Kehte Hain.

4
Syedi Mustafvi Hashmi-o-Jan-e-Nabi.
Kushta e Taig-e-Jafa Haye Hussain Ibn-e-Ali.
Hussain, Hussain Mazloom-e-Karbala Hussain.
Jab Azza Khana Saja Our Unhein Yaad Kiya.

5
Aa Gaye Ghar Mein Mere Dil Mera Shad Kiya.
Aik Be Ghar Ne Mere Ghar Ko Aabad Kiya.
Aisay Mehman Ko Shah e Zeeshan Ko Maula Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Kehte Hain.
Marzi-e-Rab Thi Yehi Chor Dein Apna Watan.

6
Woh Ghareeb Ul Watani Our Ujarta Woh Chaman.
Woh Shehanshah-e-Zaman Our Pamal Badan.
Aisay Mazloom Ko Aisay Masoom Ko Maula Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Kehte Hain.
Yeh Keh Diya Maula Ne Woh Kar Ke Dikhaya Hai.

7
Qurban Hoye Lekin Waday Ko Nibhaya Hai.
Aye Sheh Ke Azadaro Aa Jao Karo Matam.
Ik Bibi Ke Roney Ki Aati Hai Sada Har Dam.
Ghar Luta Diya, Maula.

8
Sar Kata Diya, Maula.
Khoon Baha Diya, Maula.
Dein Bacha Liya, Maula.
Be Kafan Raha, Maula.

9
Lasha Na Utha, Maula.
Pyasa Bhi Raha, Maula.
Ghar Ujar Gaya, Maula.
Dein Bana Diya, Maula.
