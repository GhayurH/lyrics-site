Dil Hi Jab Dard Ho Tu Kya Kijiye.

1
Hum Ko Faryad Karni Aati Hai.
Aap Sunte Nahi Tu Kya Kijiye.
Inn Buton Ko Khuda Se Kya Matlab.
Tuba Tuba Khuda Khuda Kijiye.
Ranj Uthane Se Bhi Khushi Ho Gi.
Pehle Dil Dard-e-Aashna Kijiye.

2
Arz-e-Shokhi Nashat-e-Aalam Hai.
Husan Ko Our Khud Numa Kijiye.
Dushmani Ho Chuki Beh Qadar Wafa.
Aab Haq-e-Dosti Aada Kijiye.
Moat Aati Nahi Kahin Ghalib.
Kab Tak Afsoos Zeest Ka kijiye.
