1
Elm Ne Mujh Se Kaha Ishq Hai Dewana Pan.
Ishq Ne mujh Se Kaha Elm Hai Takhmeen-o-Zan.
Bandah Takhmeen-o-Zan Kirm-e-Kitabi Na Ban.
Ishq Sarapa Hazoor Elm Sarapa Hijab.

2
Ishq Ki Garmi Se Hai Marka Kainat.
Elm Muqaam-e-Safaat Ishq Tamshaye Zaat.
Ishq Sakoon-o-Sabaat Ishq Hayat-o-Mamaat.
Elm Hai Paida Sawal Ishq Hai Pinhaan Jawab.

3
Ishq Ke Hain Mojzaat Saltanat-o-Fakar-o-Deen.
Ishq Ke Adna Ghulam Sahab-e-Taj-o-Nageen.
Ishq Makaan-o-Makeen Ishq Zamaan-o-Zameen.
Ishq Sarapa Yaqeen Our Yaqeen Fateh-e-Baab.

4
Shara-e-Mohabbat Mein Hain Ishrat-e-Manzal Haraam.
Shorash-e-Tofaan Halal Lazat-e-Sahil Haraam.
Ishq Peh Bijli Halal Ishq Peh Hasil Haraam.
Elm Hai Ibnul Kitaab Ishq Hai Umul Kitaab.
