1
Aagaz Ho Raha Hai Karbal Ki Kahani Ka.
Logo Yeh Janaza Hai Islam Ke Bani Ka.
Bibi Ne Kaha Baba Karbal Mein Chaley Aana.
Manzar Main Dikhaon Gi Akbar Ki Jawani Ka.

2
Kat Jaein Gay Bazu Bhi Abbas Bawafa Ke.
Teeron Se Hoga Chalni Mashkeza Woh Pani Ka.
Bikhrey Ga Karbala Mein Qasim Ke Sar Ka Sehra.
Khushiyaan Samait Le Ga Aalam Woh Veerani Ka.

3
Rotay They Farishtay Bhi Jab Arz-o-Sama Larza.
Taboot Uth Raha Hai Imran Ke Jani Ka.
Ta Hashar Mere Maula Mushtaq Rahon Tera.
Mil Jaye Sharf Mujh Ko Bas Teri Ghulami Ka
