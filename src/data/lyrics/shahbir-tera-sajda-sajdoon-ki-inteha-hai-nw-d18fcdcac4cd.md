Shahbir Tera sajda, sajdoon ki inteha hai

Khoon e Hussain se hi, buniyaad la ilah hai

Kayi bar teer ouskay, hathon say ghirthay dekha

Khata hai khauf dekho, Asghar say hurmala hai

Teri laash ko main Qasim, dhoondoo kahan kahan par

Tanha Hussain ran main, dethe yehi sada hay

Dastaar is tarhaan say akber ko shah nay bandhi

Sab bibiyon ne khaimay main, inna lillah parha hay

Abbas tere sar ne, dekha hay kaisay manzar

Ek nayzay sar buland hay, Ek nayzay pe rida hay

Khud apne laal bhejay, aagosh may azal ki

Kitna azeem karbal, maaon ka hausla hai

Bhai ka pyaar dekha, jag se anokha shahid

Hontoon pe naam zainab, khanjar talay gala hai
