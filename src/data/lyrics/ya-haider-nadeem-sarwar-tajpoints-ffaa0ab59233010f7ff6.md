Mera Ishq Bhi Tu Wajdan Bhi Tu Meri Jan Bhi Tu Eman Bhi Tu.

1
Mera Qibla Kaba Masjid Mimber Mazhab Our Quran Bhi Tu.
Mera Syed Peer Ameer Bhi Tu Mera Hadi Wali Imam Bhi Tu.
Mera Khuwab Bhi Tu Tabeer Bhi Tu Aagaz Bhi Tu Anjam Bhi Tu.
Meri Sham-o-Sahar Lehjay Ka Asar Mera Haal Khiyal Kamal Bhi Tu.
Mera Qaul-o-Fail Sawab Jaza Taqleed Baqa Ahwal Bhi Tu.
Kyun Khak Uraein Dar Dar Ki Bas Aik Kitab-e-Ishq Parhein.

2
Haan Chor De Yeh Fani Dunya Bas Aik Jahan Aabad Karein.
Woh Dekh Najaf Ka Rasta Hai Aa Oss Rastay Par Sath Chalein.
Har Aik Qadam Ho Ali Ali Hathoon Mein Alam Ho Ali Ali.
Naron Ka Bharam Ho Ali Ali Yeh Ishq Na Kam Ho Ali Ali.
Woh Dekh Shehanshah Our Gada Kehte Hain Najaf Mein Sab Mil Kar.
Sarkar-e-Najaf Ke Rastay Mein Kya Ashiq-e-Haider Miltay Hain.

3
Kahin Rabia Basri Our Buzar Salman Qasiday Parhtay Hain.
Kahin Qanbar Malik-e-Ashtar Hain Kahin Daar Pe Meesum Charhte Hain.
Maula-e-Qanbar Ali Ali Aaqa-e-Abuzar Ali Ali.
Salman Ka Rehbar Ali Ali Kahin Malik-e-Ashtar Ali Ali.
Sakhi Lal Qalandar Kehta Hai Wallah Ali Ka Main Nokar.
Maidan-e-Khum Mein Ahmad Ne Haider Ko Pukara Hai Maula.

4
Hathoon Pe Utha Ke Maula Ne Maula Ko Banaya Hai Maula.
Shah-e-Mardaan Shair-e-Yazdaan La Raib Hamara Hai Maula.
Man Kunto Maula Ali Ali Nabiyon Ne Pukara Ali Ali.
Waliyon Ka Sahara Ali Ali Sab Harey Na Hara Ali Ali.
Hai Barah 12 Imamo Mein Pehla Har Jang Mein Pehla Hai Number.
La Raib Ali Ke Jaisa Hi Jeedar Ali Ka Kunba Hai.

5
Jab Jab Bhi Sada Quran Ne Di Yeh Jan Nichawar Karta Hai.
Islam Ki Khatir Jeeta Hai Islam Ki Khatir Marta Hai.
Sajjad Ka Khutba Ali Ali Zainab Ka Lehja Ali Ali.
Abbas Ka Narah Ali Ali Hasnain Ka Saya Ali Ali.
Awwal Ho Ke Osat Ya Aakhir Iss Ghar Mein Hain Sab Ke Sab Haider.
Ali Janam, Ali Janam, Ali Jan Ast-o-Jananam.

6
Ali Dar Dast-o-Dar Danam, Ali Deen Ast-o-Emanam.
Ali Khuld Ast-o-Rizwanam, Ali Aayat-e-Quranam.
Ali Aayat-e-Subhanam, Ali Wird-e-Zaban-e-Man.
Ali Taveez-e-Jan-e-Man, Ali Raaz-o-Niyaz-e-Man.
Ali Soz-o-Godaz-e-Man, Ali Pusht-o-Panah-e-Man.
Ali Ki Badshahi Hai, Ali Ism-e-Elahi Hai.

7
Har Ik Meezan Mein Tola.
Zabaan Ke Sath Dil Bola Ali Maula.
Aye Mere Maula Ali Maula.
Mujhe Apna Bana Le, Ik Bar Sada De.
Mujhe Najaf Dikha Dey, Haidery Bana De.
Ya Maula.
