Bich Gaya Farsh-e-Azza Chayi Muharram Ki Fiza.
Har Taraf Gaunj Rahi Hai Tere Matam Ki Sada.
Yeh Tere Gham Ka Hi Sadqa Hai Hussain Ibn-e-Ali.
Aa Gayin Fatima Zahra Jo Azakhana Saja.

Sakoon-e-Dil Tera Hi Gham Qadam Qadam Tera Karam.
Jahan Jahan Gayi Nazar Hain Tere Ghazi Ke Alam.
Har Azadar Tera Ya Hussain.
De Raha Hai Yeh Sada Ya Hussain.

Yeh Azadari Alamdari Yeh Pursa Dari.
Har Yazeedi Pe Hai Bas Khof Issi Ka Tari.
Rokney Walay Yeh Ayelan Hamara Sun Lein.
Hashar Tak Matam-e-Shabeer Rahe Ga Jari.

Zulm Hoye Sitam Sahey Qadam Hamarey Na Rukay.
Haye Hussaina Ki Sada Detey Rahe Chalte Rahe.
Kaun Rokay Ga Bhala Ya Hussain.
Hum Hain Zahra Ki Dua Ya Hussain.

Meri Sansoon Ka Nahi Koi Bharosa Maula.
Dil Hai Be Chain Sada Sun Le Ghareeb-e-Zahra.
Tumko Akbar Ki Qasam Ab Ho Karam Yeh Mujh Par.
Jeetay Jee Dekh Loon Ik Bar Tumhara Roza.

Hai Karbala Sakoon-e-Man Hai Karbala Janoon-e-Man.
Baha Jahan Tera Lahu Bahey Wahan Pe Khon-e-Man.
Hai Yeh Arman Mera Ya Hussain.
Jaon Main Karb-o-Bala Ya Hussain.

Misal-e-Hur Mera Muqadar Bhi Jaga De Maula.
Mere Mathay Pe Bhi Romal Saja De Maula.
Aye Mere Maula-o-Aaqa Main Tera Khadam Hoon.
Jaisa Tu Chahe Mujhe Waisa Bana De Maula.

Sun Le Mere Dil Ki Sada Maula Mujhe Apna Bana.
Naam Tera Lab Pe Rahe Mujh Ko Nahi Khoof-e-Qaza.
Aye Zamane Ki Baqa Ya Hussain.
Zindagi Tum Pe Fida Ya Hussain.

Teri Khilqat Pe Do Aalam Ka Khuda Naaz Karey.
Oss Se Naraz Hai Rab Jo Tujhe Naraz Karey.
Tu Jisay Chahe Atta Kar De Pisar Aye Maula.
Tere Jholay Se Jo Mas Ho Tu Woh Parwaz Karey.

Fitrus-o-Rahib Ne Kaha Jo Bhi Mila Tujh Se Mila.
Tu Hai Sakhi Ibn-e-Sakhi Teri Atta Sab Se Juda.
Aye Dil-e-Shair-e-Khuda Ya Hussain.
Aye Muhammad Ki Dua Ya Hussain.

Aye Azadaro Suno Farz Nibhana Hai Tumhein.
Apne Bachoon Ko Azadar Banana Hai Tumhein.
De Ke Abbas-e-Dilawar Ka Alam Hathoon Mein.
Ik Wafadar Alamdar Banana Hai Tumhein.

Chahe Chaley Jaisi Hawa Bujh Na Sakay Ab Yeh Diya.
Bichta Rahe Farsh-e-Azza Jari Rahe Yeh Silsila.
Bas Rahe Lab Pe Sada Ya Hussain.
Zikar Hai Sab Se Bara Ya Hussain.

Haye Ashor Ka Din Kaisi Qayamat Laya.
Barchi Akbar Ko Lagi Chid Gaya Seena Oss Ka.
Qasim-o-Aoun-o-Muhammad Ali Asghar Na Rahe.
Jis Se Dharas Thi Woh Abbas-e-Dilawar Na Raha.

Jab Koi Baqi Na Raha Maula Tu Hi Ran Ko Chala.
Pyasa Hazaron Se Lara Gaib Se Aayi Yeh Sada.
Laut Aa Rab-e-Wafa Ya Hussain.
Tujh Se Razi Hai Khuda Ya Hussain.

Jab Suni Tu Ne Sada Shukar-e-Khuda Karne Laga.
Teer Barsaye Gaye Mara Kissi Ne Naiza.
Fatima Zahra Ne Kis Tarha Sanbhala Tujh Ko.
Haye Maqtal Mein Tu Jab Khak Pe Ghorey Se Gira.

Shimar Laeen Taig-e-Jafa Thamay Hoye Aagay Barha.
Seene Pe Zanu Ko Rakha Our Gala Kaat Diya.
Thi Yeh Zahra Ki Sada Ya Hussain.
Kat Gaya Tera Gala Ya Hussain.

Har Azadar Ka Tujh Se Hi Bharam Hai Maula.
Tere Matam Ke Sabab Seeno Mein Dam Hai Maula.
Tere Ashiq Tere NoKar Hain Tere Zakir Hain.
 Jaun -o- Zeeshan Pe Tera Hi Karam Hai Maula.

Ashk-e-Azza Behtay Rahein Gham Mein Tere Jeetay Rahein.
Haye Hussaina Ki Sada Detey Rahein Rotay Rahein.
Kya Muqadar Hai Mera Ya Hussain.
Lab Pe Noha Hai Tera Ya Hussain.
