Kaise Batool Sholon Se Kis Tarha Ab Bachaye.
Woh Jal Rahi Hai Aa Ke Yeh Koi Aag Tu Bhujaye.

1
Uth Jate They Payamber Tazeem Jis Ki Karne.
Woh Gir Pari Hai Aa Ke Oss Ko Zara Uthaye.
Quran Ka Maskan Hai Nabiyon Ki Sajdah Ga Hai.
Bolo Yeh Kalma Go Se Oss Ghar Ko Na Jalaye.

2
Bachoon Ke Samne Tu Kuch Bhi Na Boli Zahra.
Jo Dard Ho Raha Hai Woh Kis Ko Bataye.
Khaybar Shikan Na Roye Pehle Hi Ghamzada Hai.
Pehlu Shikasta Kaisay Haider Ko Woh Dikhaye.

3
Jo Be Kafan Rahe Ga Karb-o-Bala Mein Beta.
Apne Kafan Mein Oss Ko Seene Se Na Lagaye.
Tu Dard Likh Raha Hai Bibi Ke Aye Salamat.
Phir Tujh Ko Khud Payamber Na Noha Kyun Likhaye.
