Zindaan Mein Sakina Ko Yaad Aaya Woh Seena
Jis Seene Pe Soti Thi Masoom Sakina.

1
Kar Taras Zara Zalim Baba Ko Na Roun Gi
Main Goad Mein Madar Ki Aaram Se Soun Gi
Be-Reham Laeeno Ne Faryad Suni Na

2
Hai Maut Ki Khamoshi Tareeki Yeh Zindaan Mein
Kya Jurm Kiya Logo Masoom Si Mehmaan Ne
Koi Our TU Masooma Youn Qaid Hoi Na

3
Sajad Jo Zindaan Se Ho Qaid Khatam Teri
Ja Kehna Yeh Sughra Se Tum Ko Hai Qasam Meri
Main Dafan Hoon Zindaan Mein Aabad Madina

4
Har Waqt Chamakta Tha Karbal Mein Madine Mein
Taweez Ki Manind Tha Shabir Ke Seene Mein
Gum Ho Gaya Woh Kaise Zindaan Mein Nagina

5
Phat Jaye Na Ghairat Se Sajad Jigar Tera
Mere Ghar Ki Yeh Ronaq Hai Kehta Hai Pidar Tera
Yeh Soch Ke Abid Ko Kuch Hosh Rahi Na

6
Rukhsar Main Nana Ko Mehshar Mein Dikhaon Gi
Rodad Main Zindaan Ki Ro Ro Ke Sunaon Gi
Pabandi Hai Rone Pe Kehta Hai Kamina

7
Aye Dasht-e-Ajal Ruk Ja Bemar Ko Aane De
Baba Ke Liye Paigham Sunane De
Shayad Meri Turbat Pe Woh Aaye Kabhi Na

8
Haye Maut Bhi Hairaan Thi Masoom Ki Maiyyat Pe
Afsos Rahi Karti Ahamad Ki Raiyyat Pe
Tha Maut Ke Chehre Pe Ghairat Ka Paseena

9
Sardar Sakina Ko Zindaan Mein Dafan Kar Ke
Sajad Yeh Kehte The Na Kafan Mila Kar Ke
Ta Hashar Rolaye Gi Teri Maut Sakina
