Zindaan Main Sakina Ko Yaad Aya Woh Seena

Zindaan main Sakina ko yaad aya woh seena.

Jis seena peh soothi thi, hy hy  Masoom Sakina.

Kaar taarz zara zalim, baba ko nah rounghi

May godh main madaar ki, araam say sounghi

Bay reham layeeno nay, hy hy

Fariyaad sunni nah.

Hain mout ki khamoshi, tareek yeh zidaan main

Kya juram kiya logon, masoom si mehmaan nay

Koyi aur toh Masooma, hy hy

Youn kaid howi nah.

Haar waqt chamaak ta tah, Karbal main Medina main

Tahveez khi maaneed tah, Shabbir kay seenay main

Ghoom hogaya woh kaisay, hy hy

Zindaan main naginah.

Phaat ja-ay nah ghairaat say, Sajaad jigaar tera

Maray ghaar ki yeh ronaak hain, kehta hain pidaar tera

Yeh souch kay Abid ko, hy hy

Kuch hosh rahi na

Ruksaar may nana ko, mehshaar may dekhaoongi

Rudad main zindaan ki, roro kay sunaugi

Pahbaandhi hain ronay pay, hy hy

Kehta hain kamina.

Aye dasht ajaal rukha, beemar ko anay day

Baba kay liye koyi, pehghaam sunanay deh

Shahyad meri turbaat pay, hy hy

Woh aye kabhi nah.

Sardar Sakina ko, zindaan main dafan kaar kay

Sajaad yeh keh thay teh, nah kafaan millah maar kay

Tah hashaar rulayegah, hy hy

Teri mout Sakina
