1
Teri Zahra Madine Mein Syed Shahryar Mehdi.
Bohat Roi Hai Aye Baba Teri Zahra Madine Mein.
Mujhe Ahl-e-Madina Ne Bohat Mara Madine Mein.
Meri Tazeem Ki Khatir Jahan Par Aap Ruktay They.
Jalanay Aa Gayi Ummat Woh Darwaza Madine Mein.
Main Beton Ko Gawahi Ke Liye Le Kar Gayi Baba.
Mere Hasnain Ko Zalim Ne Jhutlaya Madine Mein.
Batao Rehmat-e-Alim Qayamat Our Kya Hogi.
Zameen Par Munh Ke Bal Zahra Giri Baba Madine Mein.
Miley Badlay Mein Zahra Ko Teri Tehreer Ke Tukray.
Teri Beti Ne Haq Apna Hai Jab Manga Madine Mein.
Namazein Jab Bhi Parhti Hoon Tu Dukhta Hai Mera Pehlu.
Sitamgar Ne Kiya Baba Sitam Aisa Madine Mein.
Zara Dekho Main Chalti Hoon Zaeefon Ki Tarha Baba.
Sahara De Ke Roti Hai Mujhe Fizza Madine Mein.
 Waseem Aisi Ghareebi Thi Gulsitan-e-Risalat Mein.
Dilasa Bhi Koi Deney Nahi Aaya Madine Mein.
