Al-Ajal Kehte Hoye Ahl-e-Azza Aao Sab Sham Chalein.
Pora Karne Ke Liye Ahd-e-Wafa Aao Sab Sham Chalein.

1
Jazba-e-Hur Se Ho Sarshar Tu Darna Kaisa.
Inki Ulfat Mein Jo Maut Aaye Tu Marna Kaisa.
Bad-e-Rehlat Bhi Agar Hai Jeena Aao Sab Sham Chalein.
Apne Azhan Ko Aamadah-e-Paikar Karein.

2
Dushman-e-Sani-e-Zahra Pe Chalo Waar Karein.
Farz Jo Farz Hai Woh Karne Ada Aao Sab Sham Chalein.
Foaj-e-Buzdil Hai Mazaraat Se Ar Sakti Hai.
Nasal-e-Farrar Hai Qabron Se Hi Lar Sakti Hai.

3
Gardanein Unn Ki Urane Ba-Khuda Aao Sab Sham Chalein.
Hum Azadar Hain Wajib Hai Wahan Par Jaein.
Waqt Hai Maqsad-e-Takhleeq Amal Mein Laein.
Hum Se Khush Hoon Gaye Sakina Ke Chacha Aao Sab Sham Chalein.

4
Khandan-e-Bani Hashim Se Adawat Ki Hai.
Phir Musalmano Ne Tauheen-e-Risalat Ki Hai.
Ahtejajan Wahan Dene Dharna Aao Sab Sham Chalein.
Hai Yaqeen Mujh Ko Ke Mehdi Wahan Aa Jaein Gaye.

5
Aa Ke Khud Farsh-e-Azza Anbiya Bichwaein Gaye.
Ho Gi Jab Majlis-e-Shabir Bapa Aao Sab Sham Chalein.
Turbat-e-Zainab-e-Kubra Pe Sitam Jari Hai.
Marqad-e-Bali Sakina Pe Sitam Jari Hai.

6
Fatima Zahra Ko Dene Pursa Aao Sab Sham Chalein.
Hazrat-e-Hujjat-e-Aakhir Ko Ho Yasir Ka Salam.
Sham Mein Barhte Mazalim Pe Hain Magmoom Imam.
Khatam Ghaibat Ho Chalo Karne Dua Aao Sab Sham Chalein.
