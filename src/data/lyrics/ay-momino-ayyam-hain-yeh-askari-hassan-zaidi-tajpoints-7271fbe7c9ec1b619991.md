1
Ay Momino Ayyam Hain Yeh Askari Hassan Zaidi
Aye Momino Ayyam Hain Yeh Aah-o-Boka Ke.
Peshani Se Hai Khon Rawaan Shair-e-Khuda Ke.
Yeh Dehyan Rahe Dekhey Mera Zakhm Na Zainab.
Youn Rakhna Mere Beton Mere Sar Ko Chupa Ke.
Bachoon Mein Bapa Kaisi Qayamat Yeh Hoi Hai.
Kofay Ke Yateemo Se Koi Pochay Tu Ja Ke.
Rotay Hoye Aaye Hain Nabi Apne Wasi Ko.
Zahra Bhi Chali Aayi Hain Mohsin Ko Utha Ke.
Ashor Ki Shab Aa Gayi Kya Yaad Ali Ko.
Seene Se Lagatay Hain Jo Zainab Ko Bola Ke.
Abbas Ko Jab Dekha Tu Ro Ro Ke Ali Ne.
Bosay Diye Bazu Pe Alamdar-e-Wafa Ke.
Al Sham Keh Ke Maula Bohat Giriya Kuna They.
Zainab Kabhi Kulsoom Ko Seene Se Laga Ke.
Kulsoom Pukari Mujhe Baba Se Mila Do.
Bhaiyya Zara Chehray Se Kafan Phir Se Hata Ke.
Kuhram Tha Kofay Mein Jo Jarrah Yeh Bola.
Ab Bachney Ke Aasaar Nahi Uqdah Kusha Ke.
Kofay Ki Fizaon Mein Hain Ustad Ke Gham Mein.
Jibreel e Ameen Noha Kuna Khak Ura Ke.
Hoga Na Biyaan Kaisay Bhala Shabbar-o-Shabbir.
Zainab Ko Le Ke Aaye Janazay Se Utha Ke.
Lo Ahmad-e-Mukhtar Ki Dolat Tumhein Sonpi.
Naib Ho Hasan Aaj Se Tum Shair-e-Khuda Ke.
Abbas Ne Baba Ki Nazar Dekh Ke Asim.
Bas Rook Liya Hath Ko Talwar Pe La Ke.
