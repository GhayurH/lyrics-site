Kesi yeh shaam aye aulaad e Sayeeda per

Sajjad ro rahay hain, Zainab say moo Chupa ker

aik samt bay kafan hai, bhai Ka aus Kay Lasha

Abbas Ki behen ka, baqi raha na perda

Ab soochti hai Zainab, mangay kafan ya chadar

Bazu kahin paray hain, Lasha kahin para hai

Zainab Ki baybasi per, ghazi tarap raha hai

Rooti hai bekasi Bhi, farshay Aza bicha ker

jo ghar main do qadam Bhi, paidal nahi chali hai

Aik din main woh hi Zainab, Abbas bun gayi hai

Pehray pay agaye hai, Tanha alam autha ker

Sajjad say lippat ker, kehti rahi Sakina

Ehsaas ho raha hai ,main hogaye yateema

Zalim Dara raha hai, naiza dekha dekha ker

lashon Kay darmiyaan woh, baba Ko dhoondti hai

Moo apna pait ker yeh, feryaad ker rahi hai

Qadmo mai hi sula do, baba mujhay bula ker

jab Agaye Najaf say, beti ko milnay baba

Zano pay rakh Kay sar yeh, kehnay lagi woh dukhiya

Zainab aujar Gaye hai, kerbobala basa ker

Khaimon Kay sath jhola, bistar Bhi jal Gaya hai

Namosay Mustafa Ko, baghi kaha Gaya hai

Ley jayeen gay subhaa Ko, qaidi humain bana ker

guzray Ki kesi Yawar, maqtal say shahzadi

Abid nay raat momin, yeh soch ker guzar

Zainab Ka naam lay ga, zalim Jo muskura Ker
