1
Zameen e Dasht Pe Bikhra Hussain Ka Lasha.
Koi Uthane Na Aaya Hussain Ka Lasha.
Batool Kehtin Tu Tukron Ko Tukray Dafnate.
Chupata Ghazi Ka Lasha Hussain Ka Lasha.

2
Rahe Ga Aaj Se Be Sheer Sheh Ke Seene Par.
Banay Ga Betay Ka Jhola Hussain Ka Lasha.
Jahan Jahan Se Uthaya Tha Sheh Ne Qasim Ko.
Wahan Wahan Nazar Aaya Hussain Ka Lasha.

3
Ikatha Kar Na Saki Tukray Nanhay Hathoon Se.
Utha Na Payi Sakina Hussain Ka Lasha.
Tamam Raat Rahein Ran Mein Murtajiz, Zahra.
Howa Na Dasht Mein Tanha Hussain Ka Lasha.
