Zindan Mein Roti Rahi Hai Bali Sakina.
Ro Ro Ke Woh Karti Rahi Hai Yaad Madina.

1
Dekho Aye Mere Baba Matti Pe So Gayi Hoon.
Aata Hai Mujhe Yaad Bohat Aap Ka Seena.
Paband Rasan Ho Ke Main Qaidi Bani Baba.
Bin Aap Ke Baba Mera Dushwar Hai Jeena.

2
Shabir Ki Beti Hai Ab Haal-e-Yateemi Mein.
Choti Si Umar Mein Hai Musayab Ka Khazina.
Ro Ro Ke Yeh Kehte They Sajjad Sakina Se.
Seene Pe To So Pao Gi Baba Ke Kabhi Na.

3
Sar Jo Mila Baba Ka Yeh Choom Ke Kehti Thi.
Karb-o-Bala Ta Sham Mila Chain Kahin Na.
 Farman-O- Waheed Inn Ke Rozay Ki Zyarat Ho.
Marqad Pe Jo Hasnain Bulaye Gi Sakina.
