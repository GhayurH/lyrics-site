Kon Kehta Hai Mujhe Shan-e-Sikandar De De.
Mere Mabood Mujhe Faqar-e-Abu Zar De De.

1
Malik-e-Looh-o-Qalam Behar-e-Jihad-e-Qalmi.
Mujh Ko Alfaz-o-Mafaheem Ka Lashkar De De.
Ishq Jo Tu Ne Awais-e-Qarni Ko Bakhsha.
Ho Jo Mumkin Tu Mujhe Oss Se Fazoon Tar De De.

2
Tajoor Bhi Mere Qadmo Mein Saadat Dhondein.
Zeenat-e-Sar Ko Jo Nalain-e-Paiyambar De De.
Jin Ko Sarkar Ne Bakhsha Sharf-e-Goyai.
Lal-o-Yaqoot Ne De Mujh Ko Woh Kankar De De.

3
Tere Mehboob Ne Jo Pait Pe Bandha Aksar.
Wusat-e-Rizq Na De Mujh Ko Woh Pathar De De.
Zahri Tan Ke Liye Our Na Kafan Ko Kapra.
Jo Mere Aib Chupa Le Wohi Chadar De De.

4
:SIBT-E-JAFAR: Ki Tu Bakhshihs Ko Yehi Kafi Hai.
Keh Ghulam-e-Dar-e-Aal-e-Paiyambar De De.
