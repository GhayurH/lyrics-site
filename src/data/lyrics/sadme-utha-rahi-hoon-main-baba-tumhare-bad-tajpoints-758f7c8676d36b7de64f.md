1
Sadme Utha Rahi Hoon Main Baba Tumhare Bad.
Darbar Ja Rahi Hoon Main Baba Tumhare Bad.
Baba Yeh Fatima Pe Mosibat Ka Waqt Hai.
Yeh Marhala Kathin Hai Qayamat Ka Waqt Hai.
Khutba Suna Rahi Hoon Main Baba Tumhare Bad.
Koi Mujhe Bataye Ke Kya Tha Mera Qasoor.
Sadma Hai Yeh Keh Shehar Ki Aabadiyoon Se Door.
Aanso Baha Rahi Hoon Main Baba Tumhare Bad.
Baba Tumhare Baad Yeh Mujh Par Sitam Howa.
Mohsin Mera Shaheed Howa Wa-Mosibata.
Yeh Gham Utha Rahi Hoon Main Baba Tumhare Bad.
Baba Ghareeb Beti Ki Ghurbat Tu Dekh Lo.
Umat Ne Haq Nahi Diya Bint-e-Rasool Ko.
Yeh Ajar Pa Rahi Hoon Main Baba Tumhare Bad.
Umat Ki Har Naqab Ko Rukh Se Hata Ke Aaj.
Darbar Mein Laeen Ke Khutba Suna Ke Aaj.
Haq Ko Bacha Rahi Hoon Main Baba Tumhare Bad.
