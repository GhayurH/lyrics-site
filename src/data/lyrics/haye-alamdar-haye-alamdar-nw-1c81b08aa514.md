Haye Alamdar haye Alamdar

Karte hai matam Abbas ka

Hogya marna sitam Abbas ka

Haye Alamdar haye Alamdar

Kehte the qamar tham ke yeh Seyyed e abrar

Haye Alamdar haye Alamdar

Yeh haal hai lashe ka sambhala nahi jata

Teer ankh se bhai ki nikala nahi jata

Imdad ko ajaiye Ya Haidar e Karar

Zainab ne teri aas pe chora tha madina

Mai kese bataon use saqqa e Sakina

Mara gaya Zainab teri chadar ka nighedar

Tum qatal hui hum pe bhi yeh chal jaigi shamsheer

Agyar mai reh jaigi tanha meri hamsheer

Zainab ki rida bachne ke baqi nahi aasar

Jis tarha ghar laye the masjid se Ali ko

Us tarha se le jainge khemay mai jari ko

Akber mere bhai ka janaza karo tayar

Bazoo bhi qalam hogaye aye bazuye Shabbir

Tukray hai badam abkhon mai pe wast hua teer

Kis ne ye meri maa ki duaon pe kiya waar

Aati hai sadaa loot lo namoos e Nabi ko

Ab marna asaan hai Hussain ibn e Ali ko

Marne pe tere jashan manate hai sitamgar

Akber hamain mushkil se bohat layein hain bhai

Maidan mai girte hui hum ayein hain bhai

Ankhein to zara kholo mere munis o ghamkhuwar

Ik baat tumhain karb ki batlata hun bhaiya

Jab bhi koi masooma ke marega tamacha

Ro ro ke pukare ge Sakina tumhain har baar

Tha mir taqalum yahi Shabbir ka noha

Jate hui pura karo armaan hamara

Aye bhai hamain bhai pukaro zara ik baar
