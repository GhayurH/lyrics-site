Shabbir Tera Sajda Sajdon Ki Inteha Hai.

Khoon-e-Hussain Say Hee  Buniyad Laillah Hai.

Kaye Baar Teer Uskay Hatho Say Ghirta Dekha
Khata Hai Khauf Dekho  Asghar Say Hurmala Hai.

Ghurbat Ki Sada Ran Main Daitay Hay Shah Baikas
Akbar Raha Na Asghar  Aur Na Hee Bawafa Hai.

Khud Apne Laal Pehjay Agoosh Main Ajaal Ki
Kitna Azeem Karbal  Maa’ou Ka Honsla Hai.

Abbas Tera Sar Nay Dekha Hai Kaisay Manzaar
Ik Naizay Saar Buland Hai  Ik Naizay Pay Rida Hai.

Dastar-e-Istarha Say Akbar Ko Shah Nay Bandhi
Sab Bibiyon Nay Khaimay  Inna Illah Para Hai.

Teri Laash Ko Main Qasim Dhondo Kaha Kaha Paar
Tanha Hussain Ran Main  Daitay Rahi Sada Hai.

Bhayi Ka Pyaar Dekha Jag Say Anokha Shahid
Honto Pay Naam Zainab  Khanjar Talay Gala Hai.
