Lasha Qasim Ka Nazar Aaya, Hassan Yaad Aaye

Us ghari roye bohat, Sheh ko Hassan yaad aaye

Mehendi armaano ki haa,to mey laga kar maa ney

Sehra maathay pey haye, sajaaya toh Hassan yaad aaye

Haye Qasim na teray, tukre badan ke hotay

Rokay seenay say haye, lagaa ya toh Hassan yaad aaye

Tukre Qasim ke chunne, aur aba main daalay

Baandh kar laasha haye, uthaya toh Hassan yaad aaye

Faizi Shabbir jo Qa,sim ka janaza laaye

Zakhmi teeron kay haye, janazay me Hassan yaad aaye
