Din Dhala Tu Rolane Ko Shaam Aa Gayi.
Zulm Zainab Pe Dhane Ko Shaam Aa Gayi.

1
Sab Ko Bichre Howay Yaad Aane Lage.
Phir Dukhe Dil Dukhane Ko Shaam Aa Gayi.
Sooni Goad Ujri Maang Our Atash Ki Sada.
Sab Ka Sabar Aazmane Ko Shaam Aa Gayi.

2
Koan Roke Na Akbar Na Abbas Hain.
Sheh Ke Khaimay Jalane Ko Shaam Aa Gayi.
Aa Rahe Hain Sakina Ki Janib Addo.
Kiya Tamache Lagane Ko Shaam Aa Gayi.

3
Koi Aya Na Lashoo Pe Rone Ko Jab.
Khud Hi Aanso Bahane Ko Shaam Aa Gayi.
Samne Hai Nazeer Aik Jalta Makaan.
Kya Bataon Zamane Ko Shaam Aa Gayi.
