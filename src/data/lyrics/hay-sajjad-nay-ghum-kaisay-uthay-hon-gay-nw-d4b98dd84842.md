Hay Sajjad Nay Ghum Kaisay Uthay Hon Gay

Loog Bazar May  Zainab Ko Jo Lay Hon Gay

Yaad Abbas Ki Zainab Ko Satta Ti Ho Gi
Loog Khaiyam Ko  Jab Luutnay Aay Hon Gay

Chor Ker Kufa Ko Hay Shaher-e-Nabi May Haider
Paak Sajjad Kay  Taboot Pay Aay Hon Gay

Bheer Bhi Aam Thi Aur Sir Pay Ridain Bhi Na Thin
Kaisay Namoos Nay  Phir Chehray Chupay Hon Gay

Jab Nabi Zadiyan Darbar May Sir Nangay Gain
Khoon Beemar Ki  Ankhon Nay Bahay Hon Gay
