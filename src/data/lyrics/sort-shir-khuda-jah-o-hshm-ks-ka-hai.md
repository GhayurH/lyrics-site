Surat-e-shair-e-Khuda jaho hasham kis ka hay
Lab-e-darya zara dekho yeh alam kis ka hay

1
1) Al-qama mit nahi sakta tairai seenai sai kabhi
Tujko maloom nahi nakshai qadam kis ka hay

2
2) Likh raha hai jo qalam hokai wafaon ki kitab
Karbala dekh to yeh hath qalam kis kai hay

3
3) Jab khulai bab-e-hawaij kai muanee muj per
Tab mai samjha hun kai mujh paar yeh karam kis ka hay

4
4) Koi bhi jhooti qasam jis ki nahi kha sakta
Merai Ghazi kai ilawa yeh barham kis ka hay

5
5) Nehr per lasha-e-Abbas tarapta kyun hai
Naam honton pai Sakina labai dam kis ka hay

6
6) Woh farishtain hon kai insaan sabhi shaamil hain
Aaisa dunya main batao zara gham kis ka hay
