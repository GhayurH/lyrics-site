1
Ya AbalFazl Mir Hasan Mir Lyrics
Karb Shabeer Ke Chehray Se Mitanay Walay.
Ranj Se Aal-e-Muhammad Ko Churanay Walay.
Tham Le Hath Pareshan Azadaron Ka.

2
Aye Sakina Ke Liye Bazu Katanay Walay.
Toot Jaye Na Meri Aas Aye Umeed-e-Haram.
Marey Zakhmo Ka Faqat Aap Ho Ghazi Marham.
Arbaeen Par Humein Bulwa Lein Ziyarat Ke Liye.

3
Aap Ko Pehlu Shikasta Kissi Bibi Ki Qasam.
Hum Ko Dunya Ka Tarafdar Na Hone Dena.
Sirf Baton Ka Azadar Na Hone Dena.
Sadqa Shehzadi Ruqaiya Ke Bandhay Hathoon Ka.

4
Hum Ko Mushkil Mein Giraftar Na Hone Dena.
Likh Do Taqdeer Mein Phir Karbala Jana Ghazi.
Apna Darbar Humein Jald Dikhana Ghazi.
Wasta Deta Hoon Zakhmi Ali Akbar Ka Tumhein.

5
Karbala Sare Jawano Ko Bolana Ghazi.
Oss Ghari Nazron Mein Ashor Ki Rodad Aayi.
Aanso Aankhon Mein Bharay Honton Pe Faryad Aayi.
Samne Pani Tha Our Pe Bhi Nahi Sakta Tha.

6
Mujh Ko Rozay Mein Teri Pyas Bohat Yaad Aayi.
Tum Ho Shabeer Ki Beti Ka Sahara Ghazi.
Bin Tumharey Na Howa Oss Ka Guzara Ghazi.
Shimar Ne Jab Bhi Sakina Ko Tamachay Marey.

7
Oss Ne Baba Ko Nahi Tum Ko Pukara Ghazi.
Oss Ghari Khaima-e-Sadaat Mein Kuhram Howa.
Khoon Bhara Jis Ghari Ran Se Tera Parcham Aaya.
Pyasi Mar Jaon Gi Our Pani Nahi Mangoon Gi.

8
Toor Kar Kozay Yeh Masoom Sakina Ne Kaha.
Tu Ne Acha Howa Bhai Ki Na Ghurbat Dekhi.
Tanay Deta Tha Koi Shah Pe Hansta Tha Koi.
Apne Bhai Ki Madad Ke Liye Ran Mein Aa Kar.

9
Lash Zainab Ne Uthai Hai Ali Akbar Ki.
Shah Ne Ro Kar Kaha Abal Fazl.
Utho Mere Bawafa Abal Fazl.
Tanha Hai Bhai Tera Abal Fazl.

10
Aakhri Lamhon Mein Shabeer Bohat Pyasay They.
Bari Hasrat Se Soye Nehar Nazar Kartay They.
Shimar Hans Hans Ke Lagata Raha Terah Zarbain.
Our Har Waar Pe Shabeer Sada Detey They.
