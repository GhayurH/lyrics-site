Ronay walon sher madinay main aik aisa bhi waqt aaya hai

Kalma goyon nay kalma wallon ko/ bharay darbar main rulaya hai

Hai muqadma rasool zadi ka/ bharay darbaar main wo ayee hai

Jin pay Allah’ bhi durood parhay/ aisay farzand sath layi hai

Ghasibanay fidak nay phir kesay/ Un gawahon ka dil dukhaya hai

Masnid-e-Mustafa pay ghasib hai/ samnay Mustafa ki beti hai

Mera haq daydo aye musalmano/ ro kay khairul nisa yeh kehti hai

Meray baba kay baad kyun tum nay/ Fatima pay yeh zulm dhaya hai

Woo labay zarf aye nabi zadi/ teray haq ko main janta hi nahi

Jo likhi thi tumharay baba nay/ main wo tehreer manta hi nahi

Chaak kerdi sanad Muhammad ki/ kheh khaha zor say lagaya hai

Qabray Ahmed’ pay jakay kehnay lagi/ Fatima ko naheef ker dala

Dekho nana tumhari umat nay/ kitna mujh ko zaheef ker dala

Mera mohsin shaheed ker dala/ meray pehloo pay dar giraya hai

Fatima sayeeda ko baba ki/ gar wirasat’ pay ikhtiyaar nahi

Phir who aurat’ nabi kay hujray ki/ aye musalmano vhir sadaar nahi

Jis nay mola Hasan ki mayyat pay/ teer hathon say khud challaya hai

Gham-e-Aalay aba main rehta hai/ zikray shabbir aam kerta hai

Matmi ko tauqeer nohon main/ is liye phir salam kerta hai

Is nay bibi batool kay gham main/ dil ko baytul huzun banaya hai
