I am Zainab- standing-before-you-with all success
You”ve killed most of us, how much-more can you oppress?

1
(1) In the name of Allah, I begin, most merciful
Surely evil is your end, and of every devil
Us standing-here will-never give-you any success
The-Prophet-of Allah”s family, have you aggressed.

2
(2) No-justice in robbing-our-veils and covering your own
Dis-Honor-Ing-our-faces, making in the markets-us roam
While you”ve proven your filth, your-an-cestor”s venom
By-killing our children, using your blunt weapons.

3
(3) Look-at-you striking Hussein”s lips with your dirty stick
The lips so holy, so blessed the Prophet used to kissed
Oh Allah, I pray please avenge this oppression
Jibraa-eel has been witness to our depression.

4
(4) How-amazing is it that us virtuous people
Have become targets of your treacherous evil
We grieve for the corpses-lying-in-the-sun-unshrouded
Our hope is in Allah, we”ll always be devoted

5
(5) Till-the-Day of Judgement, please listen daughters and sisters
From Zainab”s-sermon, wearing Hijab you can learn
Can we stand-with-our-Zainab on the Day of Judgement?
All she cried for was her veil and Hussain”s Kafan.
