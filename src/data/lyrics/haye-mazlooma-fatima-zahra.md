1
Jaise Sadme Aap Bibi Seh Gayin Baba Ke Bin.
Gar Yeh Par Jate Dino Par Misal-e-Shab Ho Jate Din.
Haye Masooma Fatima Zahra.

2
Hai Laqab Sidiqa Tera Jante The Yeh Sabhi.
Zulm Ke Darbar Mein Tu Phir Bhi Jhutlai Gayi.
Haq Nahi Paya Fatima Zahra.

3
Tere Dar Par Khud Risalat Jhuk Ke Karti Thi Salam.
Phir Bhi Ummat Ne Samjha Tera Mansab Our Maqam.
Dar Jala Dala Fatima Zahra.

4
Mit Nahi Paya Thi Bibi Baap Ka Gham Qalab Se.
Gham Niya Ik Mil Giya Hai Qatal-e-Muhsin Ka Tujhe.
Qalab Rota Tha Fatima Zahra.

5
Waqt-e-Niza Apne Wali Se Yehi Faryad Ki.
Aap Ki Khidmat Jo Pori Kar Na Payi Ya Ali.
Youn Hai Sharminda Fatima Zahra.

6
Bad Mere Pyar Se Bachoon Ko Rakhna Ya Ali.
Na Kabhi Mehsoos Hone Dena Madar Ki Kami.
Ab Chali Dukhiya Fatima Zahra.

7
Shab Ki Tareeki Mein Maiyat Ko Meri Le Jana Tum
Zalimo Ko Meri Maiyat Par Na Aane Dena Tum.
Ho Gi Mehway-e-Buka Fatima Zahra.

8
Baad Mere Karbala Mein Sheh Ki Nusrat Ke Liye.
Ya Ali Farzand Ik Abbas Hona Chahiye.
Mangti Hai Dua Fatima Zahra.

9
Zakhmi Pehlu Ko Sanbhale Zabt-e-Gham Kis Tarha Ho.
Jane Kyun Hasrat Se Dekha Karti Hain Shabir Ko.
Sochti Hain Kya Fatima Zahra.

10
Seena-e-Shabar Ko Choma Our Gala Shabir Ka.
Choom Ke Zainab Ke Bazu Ro Parein Be-Sakhta.
Kya Khiyal Aaya Fatima Zahra.

11
Kehti Hain Fizza Se Bibi Aye Kaneez-e-Ba-Wafa.
Tere Zime Zainab-o-Kulsoom Ko Main Ne Kiya.
Roti Hain Fizza Fatima Zahra.

12
Khana-e-Haider Mein Barpa Ik Qayamat Ho Gayi.
Waqt Woh Aaya Keh Bibi Ki Shahdat Ho Gayi.
Arsh Tharaya Fatima Zahra.

13
Zainab-o-Kulsoom-o-Fizza Shabar-o-Haider Yahan.
Door Kone Mein Khare Shabir Rote Hain Wahan.
Majrah Hai Kya Fatima Zahra.

14
Bole Haider Waqt-e-Rukhsat Maan Ko Kya Rulwaein Gaye.
Bole Sheh Amma Bulaein Gi Humein Tu Aaein Gaye.
Ro Parein Jo Suna Fatima Zahra.

15
Sun Ke Aawaz-e-Sheh-e-Dein Khul Gaye Band-e-Kafan.
Bolein Bibi Hath Utha Ke Aa Mere Gull Pairehan.
De Rahi Hai Sada Fatima Zahra.

16
Aik Na Ik Din Bane Ga Marqad-e-Weeran Bhi.
Dekhne Aaein Gaye Bibi Mazhar-o- Farhan Bhi.
Aap Ke Roza Fatima Zahra.
