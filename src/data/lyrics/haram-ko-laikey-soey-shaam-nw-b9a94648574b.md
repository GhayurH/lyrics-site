Haram Ko Laikey Soey Shaam Jab Bari Zainab

Kabhi Hussain Bani Aur Kabhi Ali Zainab

Woh Bay Khabar Thay Kay Tareekh Ho Rahi Hai Raqam

Joh Keh Rahay Thay Issay Tere Bay Basi Zainab

Khuda Ka Deen Hai Zinda Hussain Kay Dam Say

Hussainiat Ko Mili Tujh Say Zindagi Zianab

Yazidion Nay Bohat Koshishain Tu Kee Laikin

Kissi Say Kam Na Hoi Tere Bartari Zainab

Ussay Girana Tha Khutbo Say Shaam Ka Khaibar

Ali Kay Lehjay Mai Kyonkar Na Boolti Zainab

Ali Ko Joh Shab E Hijrat Khuda Nay Baichi Thi

Ali Say Tu Nay Woh Marzi Khareed Lee Zainab

Hoay Na Khatam Pas E Karbala Bhi Zulm O Sitam

Gaye Hai Shaam Talak Tere Tishnagi Zainab

Thay Ek Rasan May Joh Bara Galay Rasan Basta

Rasan Kholi Tu Sakina Nahi Rahi Zainab
