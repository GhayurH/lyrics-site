Youn Muhammad Se Rabta Rakha Hasnain Akbar.
Naam Beti Ka Fatima Rakha.

1
Syeda Aap Ke Bharosa Par.
Kibriya Ne Mubahila Rakha.
Dast-e-Haider Pe Dast-e-Zahra Hai.
Mojzay Par Hai Mojza Rakha.

2
Aik Chakki Ne Rizq-o-Aalam Mein.
Aik Chakar Ka Fasila Rakha.
Jis Ne Syed Ka Ayhteram Kiya.
Mohtaram Ism-e-Syeda Rakha.

3
Ahteram Janab-e-Zahra Ne.
Hur Pe Toba Ka Dar Khula Rakha.
Meri Khasta Dili Tere Mamnon.
Dar-e-Zahra Pe Mujh Ko La Rakha.

4
Munh Pe Salman Mil Raha Hai Ossay.
Jane Oss Khak Mein Hai Kya Rakha.
Ishq-e-Haider Ka Hum Ne Seene Tak.
Sirf Aane Ka Rasta Rakha.

5
Ik Mazar-e-Batool Hai Warna.
Tujh Mein Dunya Hai Our Kya Rakha.
Main Hoon Mimber Pe Yani Zahra Ne.
Jis Jagah Ka Hoon Oss Jagah Rakha.

6
Ya Elahi Hain Kaun Zair-e-Kisa.
Kis Ne Lehja Sawaliya Rakha.
Inna Ataina Walay Ne Akbar.
Huwal Abtar Pe Faisla Rakha.
