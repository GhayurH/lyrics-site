1
Akbar Aa Teri Bhain De Muk Gaye Sa.
Veer Sughra Teri Khat Hanjwaan De Nal Likhdi Tere Na.
Jis Din De Tur Gaye Oh Mainu Koi V Nahin Bulanda.
Kali Aynj Di Hoi Jiwein Kali Dadi Ewein Kali Sughra.

2
Aini Beemar Nahin Saan Jinna Keh Ke Chad Gaye Oh.
Roog Hun Lagiya Aye Veer Pardesi Da Hun Nahin Main Jeena.
Kubra, Sakina Dowein Tur Gayiyaan Naal Tere.
Mere Walon Aakhein Jadon Mehndi Lawan Nazraan Dewan La.

3
Jadon Aawein Ga Tu Akbar Ho Sakda Main Na Howaan.
Sehra Ban Ke Aawein Mere Armana De Phul Daein Qabar Te Pa.
Mera Khat Jadon V Miliya Akbar Jawab Dewin.
:AAL E ABDULLAH: Aye Rahwaan Takdi Hoi Chad Gayi Aye Dunya.
