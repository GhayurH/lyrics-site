Aye Noor E Khuda Mehwar E Anwaar Muhammad Muhammad Raza Mungla
Roshan Hai Tere Dam Se Yeh Sansar Muhammad.

1
Aamad Pe Teri Ilm Ka Har So Hai Ujala.
Our Qasar-e-Jahalat Howa Mismar Muhammad.
Bas Yeh Hi Dua Hai Keh Meri Qabar Ke Andar.
Ho Jaye Mujhe Aap Ka Dedar Muhammad.

2
Eman Ki Pehchan Mere Maula Muhammad.
ALLAH Ka Ahsan Mere Maula Muhammad.
Main Aap Ke Qurban Mere Maula Muhammad.
Hasnain Ki Hain Jan Mere Maula Muhammad.

3
Aye Kash Kabhi Hum Bhi Madine Ki Ghali Mein.
Hoon Aap Ke Mehman Mere Maula Muhammad.
Hum Ko Bhi Madine Ki Ziyarat Ho Muyassar.
Saman-e-Safar Rakha Hai Teyar Muhammad.

4
Ab Ungli Nahi Katay Gi Mar Jaye Gi Foran.
Gar Kar Le Zolekha Tera Dedar Muhammad.
Aye Zahra Ke Baba Hasnain Ke Nana.
Ya Shah-e-Madina Bigri Ko Banana.

5
Abdullah Ki Janib Se Tujhe Gaud Mein Le Kar.
Imran Ne Chomay Tere Rukhsar Muhammad.
Muradein Dil Ki Paein Gay Madine Hum Bhi Jaein Gay.
Humein Maloom Hai Aaqa Humein Ik Din Bulaein Gay.

6
Hal-e-Dil Hum Sunaein Gay Unn Ko Tham Kar Unkey Rozay Ki Jali.
Mere Aaqa Madine Ke Wali Dar Pe Aaon Main Ban Ke Sawali.
Shabbir Sirf Teri Muhabbat Mein Aaye Hain.
Akbar Ka Roop Le Ke Phir Ik Bar Muhammad.

7
Masnad Se Apni Uthtey Hain Tazeem Ki Khatir.
Batla Rahe Hain Zahra Ka Maiyar Muhammad.
Yeh Sar Tere Darbar Ki Chokhat Pe Jo Khum Hai.
Hasnain Ke Nana Yeh Tera Lutf-o-Karam Hai.

8
Qismat Mein Teri Aal Ki Ulfat Jo Raqam Hai.
Hasnain Ke Nana Yeh Tera Lutf-o-Karam Hai.
Yeh Teri Ghulami Jo Faqeeron Ka Bharam Hai.
Hasnain Ke Nana Yeh Tera Lutf-o-Karam Hai.

9
Deti Hain Duaein Jo Humein Aap Ki Dukhtar.
Mushkil Mein Chaley Aate Hain Jo Saqi-e-Kausar.
Jo Rizq Ki Tangi Se Bacha Letey Hain Shabar.
Kehte Hain Humein Apna Azadar Jo Sarwar.

10
Sach Kehte Hain Hum Khaliq-e-Aalam Ki Qasam Hai.
Hasnain Ke Nana Yeh Tera Lutf-o-Karam Hai.
 Haider Pe Younhi Jari Rahe Fazal Tumhara.
Aisay Hi Ossay Rakhna Wafadar Muhammad.
