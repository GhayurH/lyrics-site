Haey Asghar Haye Asghar
Huwi Shaam Na Aaya Asghar, May loori Sunaon Kisko

1
Nawha Tha Ye Maa Ke Lab-Par,, may Loori Sunaon Kisko

2
1. Haathoon pe gaye thay laykar, Shabbir Tujhe ay jaani,
Ye kehke tasalli di thi, pilwayunga isko paani,
Laut aaye hain khaali Sarwar,, may loori Sunaon Kisko
2. Kya baat huwi hai beta, kyun rooth gaye maadar se,
Jhule ko udasi dekar, haathon pe gaya tha ghar se,
Itna to bata de dil-bar,, may loori Sunaon Kisko
3. Ashoor ka din dhalte hi, mazloom-mo ki shaam aayi,
Din laashe ginte guzra, har shaam qayamat laayi,
Maa kehti rehi ye lut-kar,, may loori Sunaon Kisko
4. Ye ahed kiya hai beta, saaye may-na may jaungi
Kya mujko qabar thi beta, may itne gham paaungi,
Kehti thi yehi maa ak’sar,, may loori Sunaon Kisko
5. Paani ki jagha sahil say, ek teer gale par khaakar,
Mera be-sheer mujaahid, mera masoom-sa Ashgar,
Soota hai lehad ke andar,, may loori Sunaon Kisko
6. Tujh jaisi kali ko beta, Is tarah masal-dhala hai,
Tu phool tha mere ghar ka, Tera-rang badal dala hai,
Hai zakm teri gardan par,, may loori Sunaon Kisko
7. Zakmoon ke safar may beta, tu-bhi to nahin soya tha
Ye maa ko qabar hai Asghar, Tu shay ke-liya roya tha,
Tu soo-gaya aakhir thak-kar,, may loori Sunaon Kisko
8. In-saann nahin hai koyi, haywaan hai is jungle mayn,
Jinki thi pehre daari, O sogaye is maqtal mayn,
Ye dekh-ke khooni manzar,, may loori Sunaon Kisko
9. Har waqt rulaati rehti, jisko iski tanhaayi
Nawha tha “ZAFAR” is maa ka, jo lutt-ke Madine aayi,
Ay mere bahadur Asghar,, may loori Sunaon Kisko
