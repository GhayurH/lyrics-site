1
Ya Kareema.
Aye Masooma-e-Qum Aye Humsheer Raza.
Raaz Ki Baat Yeh Ik Mujh Ko Samajh Aayi Hai.
Asal Mein Aap Ki Mashhad Mein Shehanshahi Hai.
Qum Se Milti Hai Jabhi Sab Ko Mashhad Ki Riza.
Aap Masooma-e-Qum Our Woh Janab-e-Zainab.
Dono Behnein Hoin Maroof Wilayat Ke Sabab.
Woh Hain Humsheer Hussain Aap Humsheer Raza.
Fiqh-o-Irfan-o-Jafr Par Hai Hakomat Teri.
Ilm Ke Shehar Ke Shehari Hain Raiyyat Teri.
Ilm Ka Shehar Tera Oss Ka Darwaza Tera.
Apni Manind Tumhein Wahid-o-Yakta Rakha.
Tum Ko ALLAH Ne Iss Wastay Tanha Rakha.
Dono Aalam Mein Na Tha Koi Bhi Kufu Tera.
Sin Bhi Atharah Baras Misal-e-Janab-e-Zahra.
Teri Turbat Ka Sharf Turbat-e-Zahra Jaisa.
Fatmi Naam Tera Zainabi Kam Tera.
Wasta Syed-e-Mazloom Ka Tum Ko Bibi.
Wasta Asghar-e-Masoom Ka Tum Ko Bibi.
La Walad Hai Jo Unhein Kar Do Aoulad Atta.
Aap Ke Shehar Se Tasdeeq-e-Ghulami Le Kar.
 Mir Jab Palta Hai Chokhat Pe Salami De Kar.
Oss Ke Qadmo Ki Taraf Aasmaan Jhukney Laga
