1
Veeran Main Bemar Nahi Write Up
Akbar Nu Aakhein Qasad Mera Dard Wichora Veran Main Beemar Nayi.
Veerna Mainu Lakh Aytbar Tera Par Sanwaan Te Aytbar Nayi.
Kenday Ne Lok Ainu Jadon Hashmi Muhalla.
Chacha Hanfia V Ronday Saday Piyo Da Chum Musalla.
Aakhda Aye Na Aasaan La Sughra Hun Wasna Aye Ghar Bar Nayi.
Mehmal Taun Tu Lahaya Keeti Aye Main Itaat.
Aayat Samajh Ke Aye Gal Kardi Rayi Tilawat.
Jan Le Teri Sughra Taun Honda Tere Hukma Da Inkar Nayi.
Tera Hukam Veer Akbar Haye Main Te Bhul Nayi Payi.
Tu Aakhiya Si Leh Po Taiyon Karbala Nayi Aayi.
Fatima Hai Veran Naam Mera Main Mojzay Tu La Char Nayi.
Ho Gayi Aye Mainu Adat Kaliyaan Main Reh Lawaan Gi.
Aap Aa Sakay Te Aawein Aaway Na Chacha Ghazi.
Phophiyaan Aye Soch Ke Nayi Sona Aj Ghazi Pehrey Daar Nayi.
Sajjad Nu Aye Aakhein Kali Aye Teri Sughra.
Sadraan Umeedaan Lawaan Mere Naal Gana Sehra.
Qafila Veran Teyar Mera Par Qafilay Da Salar Nayi.
Kenday Ne Jeray Akbar Qismat Di Gal Aye Sari.
Ona Nu Das Kaneez Aye Taqdeer Ais Dar Di.
Karbala Ummat Ne Waar Kita Taqdeer Da Kita Waar Nayi.
