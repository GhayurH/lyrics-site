Sada Akbar Ki Ati Thi Mere Senay Main Barchi Hai Joan Rizvi.

1
Sada Akbar Ki Aati Thi Main Uth Kar Aa Nahi Sakta Mere Seene Mein Barchi Hai.
Meri Janib Chaley Aaein Meri Aawaz Par Baba Mere Seene Mein Barchi Hai.
Meri Janib Jo Barhtey Hain Bari Mushkil Se Chalte Hain Para Be Jan Takta Hoon Keh Girtay Our Uthtey Hain.
Saharey Deney Khud Aata Magar Mumkin Nahi Baba Mere Seene Mein Barchi Hai.
Woh Sab Saman Ko Bandhay Meri Rah Dekhti Ho Gi Watan Par Kash Pohanchaye Mera Paigham Yeh Koi.
Madine Kis Tarha Pohancho Nibhaon Kis Tarha Wadah Mere Seene Mein Barchi Hai.

2
Shikasta Pehlu Par Apne Shikasta Hath Ko Rakhay Sirhaney Mere Aap Aayi Hain Dadi Chal Ke Yasrab Se.
Uthoon Tazeem Mein Kaisay Ya Dadi Fatima Zahra Mere Seene Mein Barchi Hai.
Abba Se Apni Baba Jaan Bana Dein Zakhm Pe Pardah Mujhe Khaimon Mein Le Jane Se Pehle Dhanp Dein Seena.
Agar Zakhm-e-Jigar Dekha Tu Mar Jaye Gi Maa Laila Mere Seene Mein Barchi Hai.
Gira Hoon Zeen Se Jaisay Para Hoon Khak Par Waisay Main Karwat Le Nahi Sakta Lagay Hain Teer Kuch Itnay.
Nahi Ab Itni Bhi Taqat Badal Loon Pehlu Bhi Apna Mere Seene Mein Barchi Hai.

3
Ghadeer-e-Khum Ke Ayelaan Ka Har Ik Badla Utara Hai Muhammad Jan Kar Mujh Ko Musalmano Ne Mara Hai.
Badan Par Waar Hain Lakhon Hai Zakhmi Taigon Se Chehra Mere Seene Mein Barchi Hai.
Qaza Ke Waqt Par Mujh Ko Aziyat Kaisi Aati Hai Keh Jitni Dhop Barhti Hai Kuch Utni Pyas Barhti Hai.
Hai Yeh Tapta Howa Sehra Main Dhondon Kis Tarha Saya Mere Seene Mein Barchi Hai.
Kaha Akbar Yeh Akbar Ne Hain Aa Pohanchin Phophi Zainab Yehi Le Jaein Gi Baba Humein Khaimon Ke Andar Ab.
Keh Gham Se Aap Ki Aankhon Mein Tu Chaya Hai Andhera Mere Seene Mein Barchi Hai.
