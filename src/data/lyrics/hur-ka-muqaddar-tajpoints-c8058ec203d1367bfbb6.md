1
Aik Qatray Ko Gathari Bhar Mein Samandar Dekh Kar.
Rashk Nabiyon Ko Bhi Hai Hur Ka Muqaddar Dekh Kar.
Kyun Na Milta Hur Ko Daman-e-Hussain Ibn-e-Ali.
Paon Phelaye Thay Oss Ne Bhi Tu Chadar Dekh Kar.

2
Raat Bhar Soya Nahi Be-Chain Tha Bedar Tha.
So Gaya Khak-e-Shifa Ka Naram Bistar Dekh Kar.
Mil Gaya Romal-e-Zahra Zano-e-Shabir Bhi.
Bheek Sheh Ne Hur Ko Di Zarf-e-Gadagar Dekh Kar.

3
Subh-e-Ashora Jab Aaya Hur Jhukaye Apna Sar.
Sab Khataein Bakhsh Dein Sheh Ne Jhuka Sar Dekh Kar.
Napne Wale Qad-e- Rehan Bazm-e-Shair Mein.
Ho Gaye Hain Dam Bakhud Balay-e-Mimbar Dekh Kar.
