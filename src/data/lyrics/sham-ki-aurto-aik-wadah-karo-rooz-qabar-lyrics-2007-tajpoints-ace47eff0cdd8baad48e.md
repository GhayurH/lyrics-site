Sham Ki Aurto Aik Wadah Karo Rooz Qabar-e-Sakina Pe Aao Gi Tum.

1
Jab Talak Laut Kar Main Na Ayon Aa Ke Shama Jalao Gi Tum.
Sorat-e-Shah-e-Dein Be Kafan Bhi Mere Jaisi Aseer-e-Mehan Bhi.
Khali Kurte Mein Madfoon-e-Ghurbat Misal-e-Asghar Rahi Be Kafan Bhi.
Aik Yasrab Ki Thi Shahzadi Oss Ka Saqa Tha Abbas-e-Ghazi.
Mashq Le Kar Giya Soye Darya AUr Qabze Mein Kar Li Tarai.
Loriyon Mein Yeh Sachi Kahani Roz Iss Ko Sunao Gi Tum

2
Hum Ne Zindaan Se Payi Rehai Aab Behan Pass Ho Gi Na Bahi.
Baat Itni Si Bas Yaad Rakhna Meri Bachi Hai GHam Ki Stayi.
Tum Ne Bazaar Mein Tu Sataya Aab Na Iss Ko Satao Gi Tum.
Jab Kabhi Qaid Khane Mein Anna Be Rida Bibiyo Ko Na Lana.
Tum Ko Asghar Bhi De Ga Duein Iss Ki Tanhai Youn Mitana.
Chote Chote Se Bachon Ko La Kar Pass Iss Ke Sulao Gi Tum.

3
Qabar-e-Bekas Pe Aanso Bahane Haal Qaid-e-Sitam Ka Sunane.
Qafila Karbala Ko Chala Hai Iss Ke Baba Ka Chehlum Manane.
Itna Kehdo Hamari Taraf Se Iss Ka Chehlum Manao Gi Tum.
Shamiyo Ki Baho Betiyon Se Keh Rahi Thi Woh Akbar Yeh Ro Ke.
Yeh Amanat Hai Pardaisiyon Ki Kar Rahi Hoon Tumhare Hawale.
Mujh Se Ik Bar Bas Itna Kehdo Apna Wadah Nibhao Gi Tum.
