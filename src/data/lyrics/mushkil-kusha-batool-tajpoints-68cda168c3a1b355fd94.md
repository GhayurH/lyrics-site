Mushkil Kusha Ke Wastay Mushkil Kusha Batool.
Tasbeeh-e-Buturab Hai Mushkil Mein Ya Batool.

1
Mairaj Mein Khuda Se Miley Hain Kahan Rasool.
Parday Ke Oss Taraf They Faqat Murtaza Batool.
Ghar Se Hamesha Hoti Hai Beti Ki Rukhsati.
Tera Nikah Arsh-e-Khuda Par Howa Batool.

2
Firdos Se Mangaye Libas-e-Hasan Hussain.
Ghazi Ka Tum Ne Hath Se Kurta Siya Batool.
Chodah So Saal Pehle Jo Bhejein Thin Rotiyaan.
Ghaibat Mein Chal Rahi Hai Woh Ab Giza Batool.

3
Apne Ko Kirdgar Ne Dekha Garor Se.
Sar Apna Tum Ne Sajday Mein Jab Jab Rakha Batool.
Ab Our Kya Aye Sheh Ke Azadar Chahiye.
Deti Hain Tujh Ko Hath Utha Kar Dua Batool.

4
Chal Kar Mubahlay Mein Gayin Jeet Bhi Liya.
Lekin Kahin Miley Na Tere Naqsh-e-Pa Batool.
Shehzadi Aik:MIR TAKALLUM: Ki Kiya Basaat.
Dono Jahan Ko Hai Tera Aasra Batool.
