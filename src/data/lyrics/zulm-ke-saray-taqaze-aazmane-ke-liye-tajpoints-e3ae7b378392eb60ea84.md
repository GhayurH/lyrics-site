Zulm Ke Saray Taqaze Aazmane Ke Liye.
Bas Mili Ummat Ko Zahra Hi Satane Ke Liye.

1
Mangne Wirsa Gayi Thi Par Wahan Darbar Mein.
Tazyane Uth Gaye Haq Ko Chupane Ke Liye.
Hath Pehlu Our Rida Shahid Hain Bad-e-Mustafa.
Reh Gayin Umm-e-Abeeha Zakham Khane Ke Liye.

2
Dar Jalane Aaye The Ya Pochiye Salman Se.
Sach Tu Yeh Hai Aaye The Woh Arsh Dhane Ke Liye.
Gham Tu Yeh Hai Aap Ki Baba Saf-e-Matam Pe Bhi.
Aap Ka Aya Na Koi Gham Mananne Ke Liye.

3
Kyun Kiya Be-Aas Zahra Aayi Thi Darbar Mein.
Rasiyon Se Hath Zainab Ke Bachane Ke Liye.
Arsh Pe Matam Tha Our Jibreel The Noha Kunaan.
 Jaun Jab Zahra Uthi Darbar Jane Ke Liye.
