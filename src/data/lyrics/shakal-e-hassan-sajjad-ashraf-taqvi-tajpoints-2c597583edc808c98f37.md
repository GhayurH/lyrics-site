1
Shakal e Hassan Mein Rahmat e Parwardagar Hai Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.
Yeh Gulshan-e-Batool Ki Pehli Bahar Hai.
Yeh Baat Khud Namaz Se Pocho Bataye Gi.
Kitna Azeem Pusht-e-Nabi Ka Sawar Hai.

2
Dushman Ko Tu Ne Aisay Saleqay Se Maat Di.
Maula Tere Qalam Pe Fida Zulfiqar Hai.
Kya Dhondtay Ho Farq Hasan Our Hussain Mein.
Woh Sulha Ka Yeh Sabar Ka Parwardigar Hai.

3
Bazu Mein Jab Se Bandha Hai Taweez Baap Ne.
Qasim Ko Karbala Ka Bara Intezar Hai.
Neendein Orri Hoi Hain Hakomat Parast Ki.
Tere Qalam Ka Waar Kalejay Ke Paar Hai.

4
Waris Ali Ke Nafas Ka Tu Hai Zameen Par.
Qabzey Mein Tere Marzi-e-Parwardigar Hai.
 Akhtar Malak Bhi Aate Hain Mehfil Ke Farsh Par.
Inn Sab Namaziyon Ko Musallay Se Pyar Hai
