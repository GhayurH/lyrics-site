Zauf hay had say sive, jaay ga kis tarhan say shaam

Mera bemar Imam

-Dasht’ pur hol’ say iss’ dil’ pay, leyay zakm’ tamam

-Ashk’ gham’ dey da ay gham, naak’ say barisa tha hay

Dard’ ut’ tha hay kalay, jay may tharap’ ja tha hay

Jab tamachon pay behn’ lay thi, hay Abbas’ ka naam’

-Marika shaam’ ka har, haal’ may sar’ kar na hay

Meray bemar’ kop eh, dal hey safar’ kar na hay

Almadad’ sher’ khuda ra, may, katay hain tamam

-Koo may dubi huwi eh,saas’ key tasiveray hain

Took’ garden may hain aur, paon may zanjeran hain

Youn tho jakray nahi ja tha hain, habash kay bhi ghulam

-Haay ab’ marzi ay shabir’ ka aqas’ nahi

Jis ko manga tha ali nay wohi abbas’ nahi

Dard’ dil kis’ ko suna ay, pisaray sha hay anam

-Pushtay laghar say rawan, ho gayay koon kay dahray

Kis kadar shimray sitam ghar nay duray maray

Chur zakmon say musafar ka, huwa jism thamam

-Baykasi thu hey bata, kehsay yay manzar deakay

Kehsay bazaar’ may pupiyon ko kulay sar deakay

Gehratay man’ti ayi hoon jisay upna imam

-Kitni hasrat’ say Sakina pay nazar kar tha tha

Haay is kehd may ekhsay basar kar tha tha

Chen din may tha jahan aur na shab ko aaram
