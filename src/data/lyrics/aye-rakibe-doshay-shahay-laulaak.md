Ae raakib-e-dosh-e-Shah-e-laulaak
Ba izzo vikhaar wa Husaina

1
Seen pa tumhaare ya Shah-e-paak
Qaatil ho sawaar wa Husaina

2
Sar aap ka aur sinaan-e-Khuli
Farq aap ka aur makaan-e-Khuli
Aaluda-e-khaak wo rukh-e-paak...baaloṅ mein ghubaar wa Husaina

3
Khwaahar teri anjuman mein jaaye..
Beti teri aur tamaache khaaye...
Beta tera khayenche ya Shah-e-paak...uṅtoṅ ki mehnat wa Husaina
