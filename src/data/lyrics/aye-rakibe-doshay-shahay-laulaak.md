Aye rakibe doshay shahay laulaak
Ba izzo vikhaar wa Hussaina.

1
Seen pa tumhhare ya shahay paak
Qaatil ho sawaar wa Hussaina.

2
Sar aapka aur sinaane khooli
Farq aapka aur makaane khooli
Aaluda e khaak wo rukhe PAAK...baalon me ghubaar wa Hussaina

3
Khwaher teri anjuman me jaye..
Beti teri aur tamache khaye...
Beta tera khayenche ya shahay PAAK...uonton ki mehnat wa Hussaina
