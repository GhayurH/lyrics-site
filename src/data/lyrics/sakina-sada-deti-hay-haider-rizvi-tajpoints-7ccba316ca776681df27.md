Sakina Sada Deti Hay Haider Rizvi.

1
Baba Baba Ki Sakina Jo Sada Deti Hai.
Qaid Khane Ko Azakhana Bana Deti Hai.
Seena-e-Shah Pe Soney Ki Jo Aadi Thi Bohat.
Maa Ossay Khak Ke Bistar Pe Sula Deti Hai.
Sang Dil Kitni Hai Yeh Foaj Laeen Zadoon Ki.
Pani Pyason Ko Dikhati Hai Baha Deti Hai.

2
Husan-e-Yousuf Hai Tere Husan Pe Nazaan Akbar.
Khud Qaza Bhi Tujhey Jeene Ki Dua Deti Hai.
Shair Dil Maon Pe Dunya Ki Har Ik Maa Ka Salam.
Kaun Maa Betay Ko Marne Ki Raza Deti Hai.
Dekh Kar Soye Madina Yeh Pukari Zainab.
Nana Ummat Teri Be Jurm Saza Deti Hai.

3
Rah Mein Girta Hai Bacha Jo Zameen Par Koi.
Khud Ko Naqay Se Nabi Zadi Gira Deti Hai.
Ik Dukhi Maa Hai Jo Majlis Mein Sar-e-Farsh-e-Azza.
Sab Azadaron Ko Ro Ro Ke Dua Deti Hai.
Yaad Darbar Ki Aa Jati Hai Jis Waqt Sohail.
Qalb-e-Sajjad Ko Ik Zakhm Niya Deti Hai.
