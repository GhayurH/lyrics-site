1
Chal Dar e Shabir Par
Chal Dar-e-Shabeer Par Rutba Siwa Ho Jaye Ga.
Khana-e- Kaba Tera Bhi Hajj Ada Ho Jaye Ga.
Faislay Mein Dair Ki Tu Fasila Ho Jaye Ga.
Hur Nahi Bach Paye Ga Tu Hurmala Ho Jaye Ga.
Anbiya Se Keh Rahe Hain Mustafa Ghabrao Mat.
Aa Gaye Shabeer Ab Sab Ka Bhala Ho Jaye Ga.
Kiya Zarori Hai Keh Tujh Ko Mas Karey Zahra Ka Lal.
Kam Jholay Se Hi Jab Fitrus Tera Ho Jaye Ga.
Khol Kar Basta Adab Se Baith Kar Zanu Zara.
:SIBT-E-: Jafar Se Abhi Bhi Rabta Ho Jaye Ga.
Sheikh Ko La Kar Betha Do Mehfil-e- Shabeer Mein.
Dekhna Woh Jaltay Jaltay Koila Ho Jaye Ga.
Be Zabaan Apne Tabasum Se Karey Ga Woh Khitab.
Bolna Dushwar Tera Hurmala Ho Jaye Ga.
Karbala Ko Dekh Kar Sajjad Kehte Hain Hussain.
Naam Hi Iss Khak Ka Khak-e- Shifa Ho Jaye Ga.
