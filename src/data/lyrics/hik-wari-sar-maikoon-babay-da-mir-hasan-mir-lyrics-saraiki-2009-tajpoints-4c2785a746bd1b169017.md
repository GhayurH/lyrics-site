Hik Wari Sar Maikoon Babay Da Mir Hasan Mir.

1
Hik Wari Sar Maikoon Babay Da Sajjad Bhira Milwa Aye Maida Wada Aye Wal Na Rosaan.
Maikon Piyo Mazloom De Hik Wari Rukhsar Zara Chumwa Aye.
Maikon Khak Te Neendar Nayi Aoundi Jadaan Samndi Haan Kan Dukh Wainda Aye.
Maikoon Shimar Tamache Mare Hin Maiday Rukhsar Gawah Aye.
Choon Salaan Di Masooma De Nayi Sar De Waal Safaid Honday.
Main Wang Zaeefa Ho Gayi Aan Giya Dukh Babay Da Kha Aye.

2
Main Piyo Di Matam Daar Jadaan Zindan-e-Sham Ich Mar Waisaan.
Maikoon Dafan Kare Sajjad. Bhira Ayho Maila Chola Pa Aye.
Kya Haal Dasaan Baqir. Jiwi Maikoon Sham Di Garmi Kha Gayi Aye.
Maikoon Ghin Wanj Iss Zindan Wichon Nayi Lagdi Veer Hawa Aye.
Tu Sir Babay Da Wanj Ghin Shala Rat Akhiyaan Di Band Thiwi.
Jaye Piyo Da Sar Maikoon Na Miliya Wal Muk Waisan Maiday Sa Aye.
