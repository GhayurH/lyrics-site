Khoon me duba jalti zami per lasha e Sarwar parra hua hai
Doobta suraj charr ke bari per jaam lahu ka bana hua hai

1
Farq e shahedeen katt gaya rann me
Kaisa hua andher ye ban me
Mehre imamat aaya grehan me hashr kahan ab ruka hua hai

2
Zainab e muztar ka tha ye nauha
Kaisa ye din kismat ne dikhaya
Aaha jo kal tak bhara hua tha aaj wahi ghar luta hua hai

3
Bali Sakina roe na kyu kar
Khaye tamache chin gae gauhar
Suje hue rukhsaro pa uske khun abhi tak jama hua hai

4
Koi nahi ye shimr se kehta
Bali sakina ko na dein eza
Uth gaya sar se baap ka saya iska bahot dil dukha hua hai

5
Rann me gaya tha jo shashmaha
Teer gale per usne bhi khaya
Ik dukhiyari maa ka kaleja nawak ke gham se dukha hua hai

6
Reham kisi ko usper na aaya
Khaich ke bistar le gaye aada
Haye mera bimaar bhatija gashme abhi tak pada hua hai

7
Uth gaye sab zehra ke dulare
Tashna jigar kausar ko sidhare
Koi nahi ab sar pe hamare humse zamana phira hua hai

8
Kaaqi hai imaan ki izzat
Qayam hai arkaan e shariyat
Anwar khun e sheh ki badaulat din e payamber bacha hua hai....
