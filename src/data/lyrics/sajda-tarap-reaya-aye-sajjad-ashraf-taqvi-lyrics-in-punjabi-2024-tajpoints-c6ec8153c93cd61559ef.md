1
Sajda Tarap Reaya Aye Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.
Hurmat Da Aye Maheena Ramzan Ro Rehya Aye.
Sajdah Tarap Rehya Aye Quran Ro Rehya Aye.
Shabbir Taun Bari Aye Maula Ali Di Ghurbat.
Aisi Talu Hoi Aye Syedaan Te Subh-e-Zarbat.
Zainab Nu Sham Wala Zindan Ro Rehya Aye.
Sir Wich Rawai Mati Arsh-e-Barin Di Wasoon.
Nuqsan Kaisa Hoya Ibleesiyat De Hathoon.
Rehmaniyat Qatal Thayi Rehman Ro Rehya Aye.
Zalim Ne Zarb Mari Wajhullah Walay Sir Te.
Guzri Ajab Qayamat Zahra De Pak Ghar Te.
Kul-e-Eman Zakhmi Eman Ro Rehya Aye.
Maslak Di Gal Nahin Aye Gal Aye Naveed Haq Di.
Haider Da Qatal Dunya Sajjad Bhul Nahin Sakdi.
Insaniyat Qatal Thayi Insan Ro Rehya Aye.
