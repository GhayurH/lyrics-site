Avas^ Ahrahi Hay , Aik Sina ay Sina say

Akbar kay khulay gaysu, deakay na gayay Sha say

Mehnay dayka Shah ko ahtay, Har sheh laraz^ rahi tey

Karbal key pak^ dharti, Sal^waat^ par rahi tey

Pehgham^ kohi Akbar, Hum kya kahain Sughra say

-Darya kay shoor^ main tha, Akbar jawan ka mataam

Shabeer^ kar rahain hain, lakhtya jigar ka mathaam

Mataam key sada logoon, Ha ay ahti hay har ja say

-Baytay jawan ka lasha, maktal may dakhay tanha

Kham khagayi kamar^ tho, Jhuktay huway shah nay

kaha

Aik bar^ uto Akbar, Kuin roothey ho baba say
