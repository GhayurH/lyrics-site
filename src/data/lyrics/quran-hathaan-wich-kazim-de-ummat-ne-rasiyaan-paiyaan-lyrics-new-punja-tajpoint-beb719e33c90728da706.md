1
Quran Hathaan Wich Kazim De Ummat Ne Rasiyaan Paiyaan.
Fair Chodah Saal Iss Qaidi Nu Haye Dhiyaan Millan Nayi Aayiyaan.
Aynj Turya Rozay Taun Maula Jiwein Janda Mur Ke Aana Nayi.
Tere Putar Nu Teri Ummat Taun Nana Kisay Aan Bachana Nayi.
Haye Paye Gayiyaan Mazloom Diyaan Fair Dhiyaan Naal Jodaiyaan.
Kujh Aakhda Nayi Bas Ronda Aye Zindan De Dar V Hilday Ne.
Jadon Sham Nu Sham Da Wain Karey Udday Pakhro V Digday Ne.
Kiwein Qaidaan Mere Piyo Abid Phophiyaan De Naal Nibhayiyan.
Jino Zehar Miley Oh Tarafda Aye Kiwein Kazim Nu Maut Aayi.
Zindan Na Lagiyaan Barchiyaan Taun Kazim Di Lash Si Layi.
Kujh Lash Te Kujh Zanjeeraan Ne Hoiyaan Te Zehar Mokayiyaan.
Kar Ke Aoulad Zibha Meri Nayi Wair Ali Nal Mukya.
Mere Putar Nu Chodah Saal Aina Wich Haal Raku De Rakhiya.
Ainu Ron Layi Qaidnaa Aayiyaan Ne Jinna Sir Wich Mitiyaan Paiyaan.
Ik Dhi Howay Dukh Nayi Bhulda Haye Dhiyaan Hoan Atharaan.
Phophiyaan Te Mawaan Ruliyan Hoan Wich Sham Diyaan Bazaraan.
 Najfi Bas Roiyaan Naal Odhay Zindan Diyaan Tanhaiyaan.
