1
Wa waila sad wa waila
Kaisa ye din teera ka charrha - wa waila
Kar gayi Zehra aaj qaza - wa waila
Baap ke gham may haey ghazab haey
Din raat jo karti theen noha - wa waila

2
Saal abhi atharwan tha - wa waila
Marnay ka sin hargiz ye na tha - wa waila
Kha gayi haey haey kaisi qaza haey
Bibi ka na kuch armaan nikla - wa waila
Wa waila sad wa waila wa waila

3
Kyun na kalejain gham se phatain - wa waila
Ho gaye bin maa ke bachay - wa waila
Kehti hay Fizza haey ghaza haey
Hay kyun na hya khoon in ankhon se baha
Wa waila sad wa waila wa waila

4
Ghash may hain Shabbir e Muztar - wa waila
Peet-tay hain sar ko Shabbar - wa waila
Zainab o Kulsoom khak basar haey
Mehdi hay ik mehshar barpa
Wa waila sad wa waila wa waila
