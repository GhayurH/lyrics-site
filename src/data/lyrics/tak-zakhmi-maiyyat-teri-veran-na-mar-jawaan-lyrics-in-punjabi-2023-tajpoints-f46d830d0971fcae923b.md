1
Tak Zakhmi Maiyyat Teri Veran Na Mar Jawaan.
Lashay Te Teer Tak Ke Rayi Zainab Bhardi Hawaan.
Le Jao Janaza Wapis Ik Zalim Zulm Sunaya.
Asaan Dafan Karan Nayi Dena Aithay Pak Rasool Da Jaya.
Fair Hoiyaan Si Hasan Te Teraan Diyaan Haye Chawaan.
Jadon Ghar Choon Toriya Veran Asaan Kafan Safaid Pawaya.
Hoya Lal Kafan Kyun Tera Kis Zalim Zulm Kamaya.
Aaya Mur Ke Ghar Janaza Kita Matam Bhain Bhirawaan.
Kitna Mazloom Imam Aye Pehlaan Ummat Zehar Pilaya.
Fair Maiyyat Te Teer Chalaye Be Dafan Janaza Aaya.
Rab Jane Kyun Hasan Nu Ditiyan Be Jurm Sazawaan.
Kiwein Rondiyaan Wekhaan Maula Zahra Te Ali Diyaan Jaiyaan.
Kiwein Tarapda Wekhaan Qasim Arzaan Abbas Sunaiyaan.
Aakhay Ghazi De Ijazat Karan Maula Paish Wafawaan.
Ik Uthya Fair Janaza Zahra Te Ali De Baad Aye.
Arshaan De Wasday Sare Aaye Pursa Deen Sajjad Aye.
Nabiyaan Ne Kita Matam Hoiyaan Pur Dard Fizawaan.
