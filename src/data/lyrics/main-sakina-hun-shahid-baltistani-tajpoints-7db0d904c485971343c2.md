1
Main Sakina Hoon Mera Dard Hai Itna Baba.
Mujh Ko Zalim Ne Kaneezi Mein Hai Manga Baba.
Main Ne Dadi Ki Tarha Itnay Musayab Dekhey.
Chaar Barson Mein Hi Lagti Hoon Zaeefa Baba.

2
Jo Teri Lash Pe Doratay They Ghorey Ran Mein.
Woh Tamachon Se Mujhe Detey Hain Pursa Baba.
Kash Mar Jaon Main Asghar Ko Hi Rotay Rotay.
Main Kahan Karti Hoon Pani Ka Taqaza Baba.

3
Haye Pori Shiddat Se Mujhe Mara Tamacha Jis Ne.
Oss Ke Hathoon Se Mera Chehra Hai Chota Baba.
Ungliyon Ke Tu Kahin Par Hain Angothi Ke Nishaan.
Kaisay Pehchano Gay Tum Beti Ka Chehra Baba.

4
Iss Qadar Sham Ke Zindan Mein Tareeki Hai.
Hath Apna Bhi Nahi Mujh Ko Hai Dikhta Baba.
Bhaiyya Sajjad Se Nazrein Na Milaein Main Ne.
Main Ne Bazar Mein Jab Khaya Tamacha Baba.

5
Sham Walon Se Na Pani Na Kafan Mangoon Gi.
Main Na Ahsan Yahan Loon Gi Kissi Ka Baba.
Rakh Ke Munh Baap Ke Sar Par Woh Pukari Darwaish.
Ab Na Jagay Gi Kabhi Teri Sakina Baba.
