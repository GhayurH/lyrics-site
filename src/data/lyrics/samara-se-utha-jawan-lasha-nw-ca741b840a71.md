Samara say utha jawan lasha … al ajal ya imam adrikni

Tum ko pursa tumharay baba ka al ajal ya imam adrikni

Thar tharati hai samara ki zamen

Zulm kartay hain askari pay layein

Muntazir hai namaz ko lasha …. Al ajal ya imam adrikni

Tum namaz-e-hasan parhaty ho.

Hai abid ki bay basi dekho

Baap ki lash jo utha na saka…. Al ajal ya imam adrikni

Aaj gyara imam rotay hain

Khud rasool-e-anam rotay hain

Mehway girya hai Fatima Zahra… al ajal ya imam adrikni
