Alam Hamara Hai Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics
Yeh Jo Panja Hai Ik Bulandi Par.

1
Hai Yeh Shan-e-Alam Sar-e-Manzar.
Sar Bulandi Ka Ik Ishara Hai.
Yeh Jo Mashkeza-e-Sakina Hai.
Pyasay Bachoon Ka Yeh Safena Hai.

2
Ashiqo Saya-e-Dua Mein Chalo.
Haan Chalo Bazm-e-Bawafa Mein Chalo.
Aaj Bhi Yeh Alam Hamara Hai.
Har Azadar Ka Sahara Hai.

3
Aye Azadaro Khush Naseeb Hain Hum.
Hum Ko Shabeer Ne Pukara Hai.
Meri Tareekh Hai Azadari.
Meri Majlis Bhi Ik Idarah Hai.

4
Hai Gawahi Yehi Jaloos-e-Azza.
Kaun Jeeta Hai Kaun Hara Hai.
Iss Ka Maqtal Mein Faisla Hoga.
Hur Hamara Hai Ya Tumhara Hai.

5
Muktalif Hain Sabhi Shaheed Magar.
Aik Hi Sab Ka Istekhara Hai.
Bad-e-Takbeer Our Risalat Ke.
Meri Pehchan Ali Ka Narah Hai.

6
Aye Azadar Yeh Azakhaney.
Dekh Janat Ka Yeh Nazara Hai.
Hum Hain Sarwar Hilal Behar-e-Azza.
Ta Qayamat Yehi Sahara Hai.
