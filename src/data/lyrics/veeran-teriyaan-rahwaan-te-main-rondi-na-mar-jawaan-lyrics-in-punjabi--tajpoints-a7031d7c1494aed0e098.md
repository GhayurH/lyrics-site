1
Veeran Teriyaan Rahwaan Te Main Rondi Na Mar Jawaan.
Moor Moharan Veer Akbar Sun Bhain Ujari Diyaan Hawaan.
Taun Lajpal Da Bachra Ayein Bhaina De Dard Pehchanrey.
Mangdi Aye Beemar Dua Shala Veer Jawaniyaan Manrey.
Door Rawway Har Dard Kolon Akbar Tera Parchawaan.
Lagda Aye Din Thoray Ne Hun Mera Waqt Qareeb Aye.
Teri Judai Kar Gayi Aye Mainu Be Had Veer Ghareeb Aye.
Sochni Aan Ki Howay Ga Jay Mar Gayi Baaj Bhirawaan.
Khuwab Khiyalaan Chan Veran Haye Roog Jigar Nu Laya.
Wadah Kar Ke Aawan Da Mera Chacha Mur Nayi Aaya.
Sari Dunya Te Akbar Jidaiyaan Mashoor Wafawaan.
Maan Jinna Te Zainab Nu Jaidhi Mangdi Aye Khair Imamat.
Jayin Taein Dunya Baqi Aye Oh Bazu Rehn Salamat.
Bazu Mere Chachay De Sare Kunbay Diyaan Banwaan.
Khuwab De Wich Meri Dadi Ik Ban Veeran Wakhway.
Bazaraan De Moor Kadi Ik Tang Zindan Wakhway.
Aakhay Sughra Wekh Zara Zahra Diyaan Maqtal Gahwaan.
Wekhniyaan Wich Khuwabaan De Asman Ne Rang Badlaya.
Karda Barish Teraan Di Ik Chan Hai Dard Sataya.
Hal Min Nasir Yansurna Haye Gaunj Rayiyaan Ne Sadawaan.
Qatal Hoya Chan Laila Da Tauqeer Jadon Wich Karbal.
Disiya Na Laila Nu Na Rayi Zainab Turn De Qabil.
Rab Janrey Kinj Pohanchiyaan Ne Haye Lash Te Dowein Mawaan.
