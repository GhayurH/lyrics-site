Beemaar kya kare

1
Ummat jo ahle bait nabi par jafaa kare

2
Beemaar kya kareee

3
Rukhsat k waqt aake yeh bole shahe huda,
Aabid main jaa raha hoon nahi koe bhi raha,
Haatho ko apne jod kar sajjad ne kaha
Gar hukm ho toh aaapkaaaa
Beta vigha kare

4
Tukde hai naaseran-e-shahe karbala ke tann,
Kamsin ke naazni ke kae akraba ke tann,
Bikhre pade hue hai sabhi mahlaka ke tann,
Sab mar chuke ho jiske woh
Kaise jiya kare

5
Dhaate hai zulm abid-a-muztar pe ashkiya,
Kya jaane kitna khoo tere zakhmo se beh gaya,
Tauqe gira ke bojh se tann tut ta hua,
Kehde koe hawaa se,
Ke dheere chua kare

6
Purkhar raasto pe aziyat bhara safar,
Sajjad sabro zabt ke tasveer hai magar,
Saidaniyo ki sar ki taraf dekh ke agar.
Aankho se aasuo ka
Jo tufaan baha kare

7
Dil band shahe karbobala rokle kadam,
Chhaalo ne phhoot kar yeh kaha rok le kadam,
Ek pal ko saas le le zara rok le kadam,
Lekin hai zidd sitam ke
Musafir chala kare

8
Deen par pada jo waqt to, yeh sab khamosh hai,
Islam ki buka ke liyea sab khamosh hai,
Har zulm seh rahe hai magar lab khamosh hai,
Jal jaaye kainat agar
Bad dua kare

9
Dekha hai jisne kohe sitam tootte hue,
Ghar lut gaya ho jis ki nigaho ke saamne,
Woh jis ke saath ahle haram berida phire,
Phir kyu na ta-hayaat woh
Aaho buka kare

10
Saason ke dorr todd ke tum toh chale gaye,
Duniya se muh ko mod ke tum toh chale gaye,
Zindan me ham ko chodke tum toh chale gaye,
Bolo sakina abide
Bimaar kya kare

11
Bimaar pe nahif pe durro ke hai jafa
Ya rab na toot jaaye ye saaso ka silsila,
Zainab ne haat apne utha kar yeh ki dua,
Ab mere ladle ki
Hifazat khuda kare
