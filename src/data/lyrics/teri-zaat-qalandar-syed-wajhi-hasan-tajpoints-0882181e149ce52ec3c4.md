Teri Zaat Qalandar Syed Wajhi Hasan.

1
Manoos e Faya Kun Hai Teri Zaat Qalandar.
Rad Hoti Nahi Teri Koi Baat Qalandar.
Woh Zarf Parakhte Hain Faqeeron Ke Wagarna.
Pal Bhar Mein Badal Saktay Hain Halaat Qalandar.
Main Aap Ka Talib Hoon Tu Maidan-e-Amal Mein.
Ho Sakti Nahi Mujh Ko Kabhi Maat Qalandar.

2
Khud Rab-e-Ola Bhaijta Hai Aal-e-Nabi Par.
Tu Aap Bhi Hain Shamil-e-Salwat Qalandar.
Dhamal Haqeeqat Mein Hai Dam Aal Ka Bharna.
Samjha Na Zamana Tere Jazbat Qalandar.
Hain Aap Ke Baba Tu Barahim e Jawabi.
Kyun Dein Na Sawaloon Ke Jawabaat Qalandar.

3
Kuch Our Nahi Shart Hai Bas Zarf Barha Lo.
Aankhon Se Hata Dein Gay Hijabaat Qalandar.
Khud Aap Kuja Aap Ki Dehleez Ke Nokar.
Dikhlatay Hain Dunya Ko Karamat Qalandar.
 Yasir Main Karon Kyun Nahi Man Misli Ka Dawa.
Hai Saya Figan Sar Pe Tera Hath Qalandar.
