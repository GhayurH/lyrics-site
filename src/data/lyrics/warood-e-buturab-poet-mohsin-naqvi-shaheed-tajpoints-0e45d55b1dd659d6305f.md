1
Dhark Rahi Hai Zindagi Dilon Mein Izterab Hai.
Khala Bhi Hai Saja Saja Fiza Bhi Lajawab Hai.
Zubaan Zubaan Pe Mojzan Taran-e-Shabab Hai.
Harm Ki Sar Zameen Par Ajeeb Inqalab Hai.

2
Khuda Ke Ghar Mein Dosto Warood-e-Buturab Hai.
Bikhar Rahe Hain Dairey Jamal-e-Zuljilal Ke.
Jale Tu Jal Ke Bhuj Gaye Addo Nabi Ki Aal Ke.
Malang Keh Rahe Phir Dhamal Dal Dal Ke.

3
Mati Mati Hain Zulmatein Talu-e-Aaftab Se.
Khuda Ke Ghar Mein Dosto Warood-e-Buturab Hai.
Ruki Hoi Hain Nabz-e-Behar-o-Bar Ki Sab Rawaniyaan.
Zubaan Zubaan Pe Innama Ki Dilnasheen Kahaniyaan.

4
Har Aik So Rawaan Dawaan Khuda Ki Meharbaniyaan.
Har Aik Rukh Hai Aaina Ghazab Ki Aab-o-Taab Hai.
Khuda Ke Ghar Mein Dosto Warood-e-Buturab Hai.
Yahan Wahan Idhar Udhar Jali Jali Ki Dhoom Hai.

5
Chaman Chaman Kali Kali Wali Wali Ki Dhoom Hai.
Nagar Nagar Gali Gali Ali Ali Ki Dhoom Hai.
Yehi Saday-e-Dilnasheen Hamara Intehab Hai.
Khuda Ke Ghar Mein Dosto Warood-e-Buturab Hai.
