1
Na Ghazi Hay Na Mahmal Hay Hasan Sadiq.
Kiwein Mukna Aye Painda Zainab Da Na Ghazi Hay Na Mehmal Hay.
Ho Gaye Ne Bhain Taun Veer Juda Na Ghazi Hay Na Mehmal Hay.
Aynj Musalmana Tazeem Keeti Quran Diyaan Sab Satraan Di.
Har Qadam Te Sain De Qafilay Te Rayi Lahndi Barish Pathraan Di.
Aakhay Seh Ke Zainab Zulm Jafa Na Ghazi Hay Na Mehmal Hay.
Main Oon Zahra Di Dhi Zainab Jehri Pak Rasool Di Jayi Aye.
Maida Baba Haider Shair-e-Khuda Jaikon Jandi Kul Khudai Aye.
Kita Nayi Zalim Shimar Haya Na Ghazi Hay Na Mehmal Hay.
Konain Di Waris Hay Jehri Bin Chadar Sham Che Aayi Aye.
Bazar Che Bheer Hay Lokaan Di Khali Qaidi Zahra Jayi Aye.
Nayi Dendi Ummat Turn Di Rah Na Ghazi Hay Na Mehmal Hay.
Wich Tauq Zulm De Abid Hay Dukhaan Di Manzil Bhari Aye.
Ik Dard Aikon Mainday Parday Da Doja Kita Zoor Bemari Aye.
Haye Sham Che Ronda Khon Piya Na Ghazi Hay Na Mehmal Hay.
Ayho Wal Wal Ke Payi Puchdi Aye Tak Naizay De Wal Sain Maindi.
Haye Wich Darbar Sharabi De Sang Bharjaiyaan De Bhain Taindi.
Kiwein Jaway Veer Shabeer Dasa Na Ghazi Hay Na Mehmal Hay.
 Mumtaz Aye Ronda Har Welay Likh Haal Haye Parday Daraan De.
Kinj Pak Batool Di Dhi Loko Kitay Paidal Safar Bazaraan De.
Darbar Gayi Haye Baaj Rida Na Ghazi Hay Na Mehmal Hay.
