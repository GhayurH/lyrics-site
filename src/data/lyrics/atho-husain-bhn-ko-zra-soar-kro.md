1
Yeh may hun may, meri tanhai, ye tamaashayi
Lo ayi gyaara muharram hai. Kya khabar layi.
Chali ye janibe zindan tumhari maa jayi.
Sawaar ham ne kiya tha tumhe mere bhaayi.
Ab awo ya mujhe kehdo na intizaare karo
Utho Hussain bahan ko zara sawar karo

2
1. Sawar hum ne kiya tha, tumhe dame rukhsat,
Lagaam thaamti maine, bahaa labe rikat,
Kahan gayi wo muhabat, kahaa gayi chahath,
Amama apna mere sarpe, saaya baar karo
Utho Hussain bahan ko zara sawar karo

3
2. Watan se door hu kuch aur, door jaati hoon,
Utho utho mere bhiaya, tumhe jagaati hoon,
Koyi bhi aata nahi mai, jise bulaati hoon,
Qaraar do mere mujko na, beqaraar karo
Utho Hussain bahan ko zara sawar karo

4
3. Safar Madine se kaisa tha, ye safar kya hay,
Na mehmilay he na haudaj, na koi parda hay,
Aey sar mein khwab ye karbobala ka sehra hay,
Na jee sakungi tere baad, etebar karo
Utho Hussain bahan ko zara sawar karo

5
4. Dare furat pe aey sonay, waalay jaag zaraa,
Alam ke saaye me rukhsat, bahan ko kar bhiaya,
Suna hai sakth kathin hay, ye Shaam ka rasta,
Sehal ye raasta abbas-e, naamdaar karo
Utho Hussain bahan ko zara sawar karo

6
5. Mein awoongi mere bhiaya, zaroor awoongi,
Kafan urhawungi marqad, tera banawoongi,
Jahaa bhi jawoongi farshe, azaa bichaawoongi,
Mein qaid kaat ke lout awoo, intizzar karo
Utho Hussain bahan ko zara sawar karo

7
Ab awo ya mujhe kehdo na intizaare karo
Na jee sakungi tere baad, etebar karo
Utho Hussain bahan ko zara sawar karo
