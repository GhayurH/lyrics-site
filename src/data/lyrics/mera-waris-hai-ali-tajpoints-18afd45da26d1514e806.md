Mera Waris Hai Ali Write Up
Khum Ke Maidan Mein Ayelan Yeh Kartay They Nabi.

1
Aadam-o-Nooh Payamber Hoon Ya Hazrat Mosa.
Younus-o-Khizar Hoon Ilyas Ke Hazrat Esa.
Ghir Ke Mushkil Mein Yehi Kehta Tha Har Aik Nabi.
Qabar Mein Mujh Se Nakerain Ne Jab Yeh Pocha.

2
Kaun Waris Hai Tera Kaun Hai Maula Tera.
Mujh Se Pehle Yeh Meri Qabar Ki Mati Boli.
Maut Kya Maut Se Dar Jane Ka Qissa Kya Hai.
Sham Walon Ne Ali Walon Ko Samjha Kya Hai.

3
Ghir Ke Taigon Mein Bhi Kehta Tha Hajar Ibn-e-Addi.
Ghair-e-Farrar Ka Karrar Ka Haq Hai Mujh Par.
Meri Janib Na Karey Koi Sahabi Bhi Nazar.
Bola Khaybar Mein Alam Hath Lagaye Na Koi.

4
Khana-e-Haq Mein Nahi Jaein Gay Dar Se Maula.
Unn Pe Khul Jaye Ga Aabai Woh Rasta Unn Ka.
Jonhi Kabay Ke Qareeb Aa Ke Kahin Gay Mehdi.
Maut Ke Mathay Pe Aaye Ga Paseena Ran Mein.

5
Hurmala Aaj Se Tu Rota Phiray Ga Ban Mein.
Sab Se Kehti Rahi Maqtal Mein Yeh Asghar Ki Hansi.
Main Ne Aada Ke Saron Par Hi Kiya Apna Safar.
Meri Tapoon Se Hi Mar Jaye Ga Sara Lashkar.

6
Yeh Rajaz Parhtay Hoye Ran Mein Mojalli Nikli.
Mujh Ko Maula Mere Alfaz Se Pehchanein Gay.
 Mir Ko Lehja-o-Aawaz Se Pehchanein Gay.
Rooz-e-Mehshar Jo Sada Gonjay Gi Akbar Apni.
