1
Shabbir Tu Ne Aise Bhi Dard o Alam Sahe Hasan Sadiq.
Jin Ka Dehar Mein Samna Mursal Na Kar Sakay.
Maqtool Tujh Sa Dunya Mein Guzra Nahi Koi.
Khud Qatal Kar Ke Jis Ko Hain Qatil Bhi Ro Paray.

2
Zainab Pe Bekasi Ki Qayamat Guzar Gayi.
Abbas Tere Qatil Khaimon Mein Jab Ghusay.
Kitni Ghareeb Ho Gayi Ali Ki Baho Rubab.
Sab Khatam Jis Ke Rishtay Hain Ik Pal Mein Ho Gaye.

3
Ziyarat Mein Mere Barhwein Hadi Ka Hai Salam.
Unn Bibiyon Pe Jin Ke They Baloon Mein Munh Chupay.
Unn Ki Ameer Dunya Mein Koi Nahi Misal.
Rah-e-Khuda Mein Jin Ke Hain Naizon Pe Sar Charhey.
