Anna Majnoonal Ali Faqeer-e-Mula 1-6

1
Meri Masti Mein Qalandar Ki Riza Shamil Hai
Meri Hasti Mein Qalandar Ki Wila Shamil Hai
Meri Masti Mein Qalandari Ki Dua Shamil Hai
Meri Hasti Mein Qalandari Ki Atta Shamil Hai
Banda-e-Sahib-e-Parwaz Ki Taqleed Mein Hoon
Main Qalandar Sakhi Shahbaz Ki Taqleed Mein Hoon

2
Main Faqeer-e-Dar-e-Haider Hoon Zamane Walo
Ashiq-e-Saqi-e-Kausar Hoon Zamane Walo
Haan Dar-e-Ilm Ka Nokar Hoon Zamane Walo
Khadim-e-Shafa-e-Mehshar Hoon Zamane Walo
Feham-o-Idrak Se Bala Hai Faqeeri Meri
Kahin Zyda Hai Ameeron Se Ameri Meri

3
Mera Mazhab Hai Ali Meri Ibadat Hai Ali
Wird Jo Meri Zabaan Par Hai Aayat Hai Ali
Mere Lehjay Mein Sharafat Ki Sadaqat Hai Ali
Iss Gunahgar Ki Bakhshish Ki Zamanat Hai Ali
Meri Pehchan Hai Tarweej-e-Wilayat Karna
Mera Eman Hai Haider Se Muhabbat Karna

4
Jab Lagata Hoon Sar-e-Raah Ali Haq Ki Sada
Khud Ba-Khud Hota Hai Mujh Par Yeh Ayaan Kaun Hai Kya
Pal Mein Chal Jata Hai Insan Ke Shajray Ka Pata
Kaun Kis Nasal Ka Hai Kya Hai Aqeeda Kis Ka
Koi Khush Hota Hai Aksar Koi Jal Jata Hai
Dono Sorat Mein Magar Mujh Ko Maza Aata Hai

5
Ishq Mein Doob Ke Barh Jata Hai Jab Soz-o-Gudaaz
Our Kuch Wajd Dila Deta Hai Sankh Ka Saz
Tab Main Parhta Hoon Aqeedat Se Wilayat Ki Namaz
Apne Mula Se Biyaan Karta Hoon Sab Raaz-o-Niyaz
Pardah Aankhon Se Hijabaat ka Hat Jata Hai
Farsh Pe Bethe Hoye Arsh Nazar Aata Hai

6
Yeh Mera Gasht Yeh Aawaz-o-Sada Aam Nahi
Bheek Ke Wastay Ghoomo Yeh Mera Kam Nahi
Rind-e-May Kana-e-Khum Hoon Koi Badnaam Nahi
Jam-e-Midhat Na Piyon Aisi Koi Sham Nahi
Kocha Kocha Mera Phirna Tu Bahana Thehra
Mera Maqsad Tu Munafiq Ko Jalana Thehra
