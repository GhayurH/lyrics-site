Sajjad Kya Biyan Ho Woh Arbaeen Ka Khuwab.
Mati Ke Log Our Woh Sehan-e-Abuturab.
Nara Jo Gonjta Tha Janab-e-Ka.
Sara Haram Hi Lagta Tha Maidaan Ghadeer Ka.
Sab Ki Thi Chah Muthi Mein Kul Kainat Ho.
Dast-e-Khuda Ke Roze Ki Jali Pa Hath Ho.

WOh Zoaf Tha Keh Sara Badan Hi Nidhal Tha.
Sarkar Ki Zaree Pe Jana Mohal Tha.
Pehlu Mein Mere Sahib-e-Eman Tha Koi.
Darhi Mein Oss Ki Gard Thi Salman Tha Koi.
Bola Woh Muskurate Howay Band-e-Ahad.
Maula Ali Se Barh Ke Kaho Ya Ali Madad.

Jaise Hi Ya Ali Kaha Rasta Sa Ban Gaya.
Qadmo Mein Unn Ke Joor Ke Hathon Ko Phir Kaha.
Ya AbuTurab Chahiye Arsh-e-Olla Ka Izn.
Main Lene Aaya Hon Safar-e-Karbala Ka Izn.
Le Kar Abuturab Se Izn-e-Safar Barha.
Chokhat Pe Apne Maula Ki Sajda Adda Kiya.

Nikla Haram Se Jab Tu Main Dunya Se Aagay Tha.
Pehla Qadam Hi Sarhad-e-Sidra Se Aagay Tha.
Seedhe They Misal-e-Teer Jo Misal-e-Kaman They.
Jannat Ki Tarha Burhe Wahan Par Jawan They.
Manzar Woh They Keh Aqal Bhi Hairan Thi Meri.
Woh Karbala Ki Rah Thi Ya Ishq Ki Gali.

Zair Ki Jotiyaan Koi Seeta Howa Mila.
Nokar Ki Tarha Tha Koi Pani Liye Khara.
Bola Yeh Koi Kijiye Ghar Mein Mere Qayam.
Aaram Ka Hai Aap Ke Mojood Intezam.
Kehta Tha Chaliye Aap Ko Khana Khilaon Ga.
Chal Chal Ke Thak Gaye Hain Main Paon Dabaon Ga.

Ghazi Ki Saltanat Ki Ajab Zaib-o-Zain Thi.
Jis Samt Bhi Nazar Ki Niyaz-e-Hussain Thi.
Zair Hi Keh Ke Mujh Ko Bulaya Gaya Wahan.
Zair Hi Keh Ke Mujh Ko Khilaya Gaya Wahan.
Main Ne Bhi Jis Se Baat Ki Zair Kaha Ossay.
Jis Se Bhi Koi Cheez Li Zair Kaha Ossay.

Youn To Carroron Logon Mein Har Khas-o-Aam Tha.
Lekin Har Aik Shaks Ka Zair Hi Naam Tha.
Ahl-e-Zabaan Nahi They Magar Jan Jate They.
Maa Ki Tarha Isharon Ko Pehchan Jate They.
Kharcha Kiye Beghair Kayi Dolatein Milin.
Do Jannaton Ke Beech Kayi Jannatein Milin.
Itna Chala Keh Par Gaye Pairon Mein Aablay.
Kuch Dair Ruk Gaya Zara Aaram Ke Liye.
Sara Badan Thakan Se Bohat Choor Tha Mera.
Dil Ne Kaha Keh Ab Nahi Paidal Ka Hosla.
Itne Mein Ik Bazurg Pe Main Ne Nazar Jo Ki.
Raftar Un Ki Dekh Ke Himmat Meri Barhi.
Guzray WOh Muskuratay Howay Mere Pass Se.
Main Ne Kiya Jo Ghoor To Masnoyi Paon They.
Phir Tu Qareeb Tar Mujhe Apna Hadaf Laga.
Aik Aik Chala Paon Ga Dur-e-Najaf Laga.
Yeh Karbala Hai Aayi Jo Kano Mein Ik Sada.
Be Sakhta Zaban Pe Ik Shair Aa Gaya.
Karb-o-Bala Naseeb Se Jana Naseeb Ho.
Phir Laut Kar Wahan Se Na Aana Naseeb Ho.
Hum Pul Pe Chal Rahe They Keh Yaksar Dikhai Di.
Abbas Our Hussain Ke Rozon Ki Roshni.
Kis Se Unhein Milaon Ke Bas La-Jawab They.
Do Rozay Kab They Raat Ke Do Aaftab They.
Labaik Ya Hussain Ke Naray They Chaar So.
Kaise Biyaan Ho Dil Ne Jo Ki Mujh Se Guftgu.
Kehne Laga Keh Rukh Ko Zara Soye Sham Kar.
Hathon Ko Joor Bint-e-Ali Ko Salam Kar.
Kehta Tha Dil Jo Karb-o-Bala La-Zawal Hai.
Yeh Sham Wali Bibi Tumhara Kamal Hai.
Bas Soch Hi Raha Tha Karon Shukar-e-Kibriya.
Aansu Ne MUjh Se Pehle Wahan Sajda Kar Liya.
 Sajjad Kyun Kahon Ossay Rasta Zameen Ka.
Aflak Ka Safar Tha Safar Arbaeen Ka.
