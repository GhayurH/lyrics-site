Yun baaghiyon ne loota baaghe shah e zaman ko
Veeran kar diya hai phoolay phalay chaman ko

1
Phoolon ka zikr kaisa kaliyon ko bhi na chorra
Teer e sitam ne mara Asghar se gul badan ko

2
Maa jayi le rahi hay Shabbir ki balayain
Hasrat se dekhte hain Shah ghamzada behen ko

3
Balwe main sar barehna itrat hay Mustafa ki
Sibt e Nabi ka laasha mohtaj hay kafan ko

4
Gardan ke neel jis dam dikhlati thi Sakina
Kasta that aur barrh kar Shimr e laeen rasan ko

5
Bichrre huaey jahan ke mahshar main phir milenge
Samjha rahe hain Qasim ik raat ki dulhan ko
