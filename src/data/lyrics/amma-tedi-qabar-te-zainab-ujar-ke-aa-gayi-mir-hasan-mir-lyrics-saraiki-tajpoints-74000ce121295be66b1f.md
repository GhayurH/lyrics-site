1
Amma Tedi Qabar Te Zainab Ujar Ke Aa Gayi Mir Hasan Mir.
Ik Vi Na Bachiya Jeray Ghin Naal Main Bhira Gayi.
Tarvinja Mehmalaan Da Maide Naal Qafila Hayi.
Wal Taray Watan Te Baqi Karbal Te Shaam Kha Gayi.

2
Seene Te Soan Wali Mitiyaan Te Sondi Rayi Aye.
Nayi Mila Piyo Da Seena Okko Shaam Raas Aa Gayi.
Majmay Sharabiyaan De Darbar Fasiqaan De.
Tableeg Keete Zainab Okhe Safar Nibha Gayi.

3
Iss Dard Har Qadam Te Abid Nu Rat Rowaya.
Kul Anbiya Di Ghairat Wich Shaam Be Rida Gayi.
Kis Kis Ko Roaon Amma Kis Kis Ke Dukh Sunaon.
Veeran Ho Giya Ghar Bete Bache Na Bahi.

4
Bahi Ne Youn Chuni Thi Qasim Ki Lash Amma.
Hum Ne Sahabiyon Mein Jaise Sanad Uthai.
Kuch Zakhm Hain Ghazi Ki Maa Se Main Ne.
Zakhmi Kalaiyon Se Chadar Nahi Hatai.

5
Kehta Tha Zair-e-Khanjar Mazloom Pani Pani.
Lekin Ye Teri Zainab Pani Pila Na Payi.
 Maqsood Hosla Hayi Bint-e-Ali Da Jeri.
Itne Sitam Utha Ke Watna Te Jeendi Aa Gayi.
