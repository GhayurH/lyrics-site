1
Sab That Para Reh Jaye Ga Jab Aane Wala Aaye Ga.
Yeh Dharti Sona Ugalay Gi Yeh Charkh Gohar Barsaye Ga.
Kabay Ki Chat Par Ghazi Ka Ik Roz Alam Lehraye Ga.
Woh Aa Raha Hai Keh Jis Ke Aagay Kissi Ka Kehna Nahi Chaley Ga.
Beghair Farman-e-Ibn-e-Zahra Nizam-e-Dunya Nahi Chaley Ga.
Kahin Guzarish Nahi Chaley Gi Kissi Ki Sazish Nahi Chaley Gi.
Karey Ga Aa Kar Woh Adal Qayam Koi Sifarish Nahi Chaley Gi.
Chalaye Ga Woh Ali Ka Sikka Kissi Ka Sikka Nahi Chaley Ga.
Alag Na Ho Ga Koi Kissi Se Namaz Rozay Bhi Aik Hoon Gaye.
Imam Aaein Gaye Laut Kar Jab Har Ik Moazin Baghor Sun Le.
Azan Ho Gi Ghadeer Wali Yeh Kam Zyada Nahi Chaley Ga.
Ana Ke Jazboon Mein Behney Walo Hawa-e-Mazhab Mein Rehne Walo.
Koi Karey Ga Halal Tum Ko Haram Matam Ko Kehne Walo.
Dokanein Fatwon Ki Band Ho Gin Kissi Ka Fatwa Nahi Chaley Ga.
Likha Howa Hai Hadees Mein Bhi Zameen Najasat Se Door Ho Gi.
Chaley Gi Shamsheer Murtaza Ki Zaban-o-Millat Ki Qaid Kaisi.
Ali Ka Munkir Ho Chahe Jo Bhi Zameen Pe Zindah Nahi Chaley Ga.
Marein Gaye Sab Maslehat Ke Aadi Kahan Pe Jaein Gaye Yeh Fasadi.
Chupaein Gaye Kis Tarha Se Chehray Haram Se Ho Gi Yeh Jab Manadi.
Meri Hakomat Mein Iss Zameen Par Adoye Zahra Nahi Chaley Ga.
Fasadi Sare Bharein Gaye Aahein Samaa Yeh Dekhein Gi Sab Nigahein.
Nizam Tabdeel Ho Ga Sara Yeh Masjidein Hoon Ya Bargahein.
Woh Khud Chalaein Gaye Har Idarah Yeh Tera Mera Nahi Chaley Ga.
Yeh Farsh-e-Matam Hai Yaad Rakhna Kabhi Siyasat Yahan Na Karna.
Imanat-e-Syeda Hain Aanso Yahan Khiyanat Se Khud Hi Bachna.
Kahin Na Mehshar Ke Roz Zahra Tumhara Pursa Nahi Chaley Ga.
Khuda-e-Wahid Ka Faisla Hai Keh Noor Kamil Zaror Ho Ga.
Hazar Fitney Uthaye Dunya Wali Ka Lazim Zahoor Ho Ga.
Beghair Imam-e-Zamana Akbar Kabhi Zamana Nahi Chaley Ga.
