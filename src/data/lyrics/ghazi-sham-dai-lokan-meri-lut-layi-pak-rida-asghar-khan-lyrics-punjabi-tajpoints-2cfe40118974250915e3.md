1
Ghazi Sham Dai Lokan Meri Lut Layi Pak Rida Asghar Khan.
Darya Wal Tak Ke Bhain Zainab Teri Be Rida Dendi Veer Sada.
Baaj Kafno Na Koi Veer Howay Bhain Rasiyaan Na Wich Aseer Howay.
Koi Qaidi Poor Na Lashon Nu Aynj Chad Ke Jandiyaan Ro Aakhay Alwida.

2
Manr Jehra Si Teri Banwaan Te Naal Mukya Oh Teri Sanwaan De.
Hath Rasiyaan Ne Hun Sir Che Mitiyaan Pa Ke Veerna Roni Aan Be Khata.
Tere Bajoon Turn Di Aadi Nayi Aina Rahwaan Nu Main Te Jandi Nayi.
Ik Wari Aa Ke Bhain Nu Darya Taun Veerna Das Ja Sham De Rah.

3
Teri Rahwaan Sakina Wekhdi Aye Le Ke Payala Oh Veer Asghar Layi.
Ro Kehndi Si Tak Digday Alam Nu Tere Hoan Na Bachiya Veer Mera.
Be Rida Ho Ke Jehri Sham Gayi Jehri Raj Ke Bhira Nu Ro Na Saki.
Oss Bibi De Bachiyaan Da Sadqa Maula Kar Atta Sabir Nu Bachra.
