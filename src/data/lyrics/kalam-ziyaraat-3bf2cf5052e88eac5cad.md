Goonji Bawakt E Asr Sada, Main Hussain hoon

1
Kato Na Mera Khush Gala,
Mai Hussain Hun.

2
Sukhi Hui Hai Shimr (La) Mere Halq Ki Rage,
Aahista Tu Churi Ko Chala,
Mai Hussain Hun.

3
Amma Kisi Ne Pani Ek Boond Bhi Na Di,
Mai Bar Bar Kehta Raha
Mai Hussain Hun.

4
Kata Hua Gala Mera Dekha Na Jaienga,
Nana Main Hun Nawasa Tera,
Mai Hussain Hun.

5
Be Bazuo Ki Laash Tadap Kar Ulat Gai,
Aaie Furat Par Jo Sada,
Mai Hussain Hun.

6
Zano Pe Rakh Ke Sarko Kaha Ek Zaeef Ne,
Akbar Kahan Hai Jald Bata,
Mai Hussain Hun.

7
Aai Shamio Kafan Na Dia Mujh Ko Gam Nahi,
Dedo Meri Bahan Ko Rida,….. (2)
Mai Hussain Hun.

8
Darbar Me Laieen Ke Kohram Mach Gaya
Mukhtar Se Jo Sar Ne Kaha
Mai Hussain Hun

9
Mehsoos Ab Bhi Hota Hai Yeh Zaaiereen Ko,
Rauze Se Aa Rahi Hai Sada,
Mai Hussain Hun.
Goonji Bawakt Asr Sada, Main Hussain hoon
Aaisa Laga Raza Ki Shanawar Se Fatima,
Kehti Hai Bar Bar Suna,
Mai Hussain Hun.
Goonji Bawakt Asr Sada, Main Hussain
