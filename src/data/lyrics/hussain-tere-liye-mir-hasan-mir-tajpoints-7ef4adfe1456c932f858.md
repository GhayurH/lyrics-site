Hussain Tere Liye.
Hussain Wale Hain Zindah Hussain Tere Liye.

1
Inhein Batool Ne Behja Hussain Tere Liye.
Duaein Raton Ko Karte The Kis Liye Haider.
Yeh Raaz Karb-o-Bala Mein Ayan Howa Sab Par.
Ali Ne Manga Tha Beta Hussain Tere Liye.

2
Faqat Zuban Se Ya Laitani Nahi Kehte.
Saboot Dete Hain Halqe Yeh Pursa Daron Ke.
Laho Badan Ka Hai Sara Hussain Tere Liye.
Tere Begair Nahi Lagta Dil Paiamber Ka.

3
Bata Raha Hai Yeh Hum Ko Zahor Akbar ka.
Rasool Aye Dobara Hussain Tere Liye.
Na Rashk Aye Mujhe Apne Lal Par Kyun Kar.
Abhi To Roza Bhi Wajib Nahi Howa Oss Par.

4
Magar Wo Rakhta Hai Faqah Hussain Tere Liye.
Jaga Ke Maut Se Maula Habib Bole.
Qaza Na Chorti Tujh Ko Jo Aaj Mere Liye.
Qaza Ko Mar na Deta Hussain Tere Liye.

5
Uthein Gay Taziye Wadah Hai Sir Rahein Na Rahein.
Gham-e-Hussain Rahe Ga Pisar Rahein Na Rahein.
Jaloos Chalta Rahe Ga Hussain Tere Liye.
Har Ik Shaheed Ke Lab Par Yehi Tarana Hai.

6
Qaza Ko Tujh Se Mulaqat Ka Bahana Hai.
Main Baar Baar Maroon Ga Hussain Tere Liye.
Bashar Wo Sacha Nahi Dawa-e-Muhabbat Mein.
Wo Sir Kataye Ga Kiya Khaak Teri Nusrat Ke Liye.

7
Jo Sir Jhuka Nahi Sakta Hussain Tere Liye.
Hayat Matam-e-Sarwar Mein Waqf Ki Tu Ne.
Kiya Hamesha Takallum Ali Ali Tu Ne.
Tu Dekh Karte Hain Kya Kya Hussain Tere Liye.
