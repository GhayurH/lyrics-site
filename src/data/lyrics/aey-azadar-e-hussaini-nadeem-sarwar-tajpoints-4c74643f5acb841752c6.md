1
Aey Azadar e Hussaini Nadeem Sarwar.
Zindah Rahe, Zindah Rahe, Zindah Rahe.
Aye Azadar-e-Hussaini Yeh Chalan Zindah Rahe.
Haan Azakhaney Sajane Ki Lagan Zindah Rahe.

2
Jab Azakhana Sajay Tu Dil Banay Farsh-e-Azza.
Dharkano Mein Matam-e-Shah-e-Zaman Zindah Rahe.
Noha Khuwaan Matam Ke Saye Mein Safar Kartay Rahein.
Har Bayaz-e-Gham Khulay Har Anjuman Zindah Rahe.

3
Rooz-e-Ashora Jaloos-e-Gham Ko Kya Rahat Se Kam.
Yaad Mein Pyasay Aseeron Ki Thakan Zindah Rahe.
Aye Azadaro Chalo Mashk-o-Alam Ke Saye Mein.
Yeh Tajamul Yeh Hasham Yeh Baak Pan Zindah Rahe.

4
Jis Jagah Thi Maut Aasaan Zindagi Dushwar Thi.
Haan Wahan Zahra Ke Sare Gul Badan Zindah Rahe.
Sab Azakhaney Hamarey Sab Hi Apni Majlisein.
Aye Dua-e-Syeda Yeh Apna Pan Zindah Rahe.

5
Chotay Chotay Se Alam Bachoon Ke Hathoon Mein Rahein.
Dil Mein Taboot-e-Sakina Ki Chubhan Zindah Rahe.
Kar Rahe Hain Yeh Dua Zair-e-Alam Sarwar Hilal.
Aye Khuda Yeh Gham Ba Faiz-e-Panjtan Zindah Rahe.
