Roti aankhoon se ye sab dekh rahe hai Zehraس

Kalma go bethen hein

Khamoosh khari hai Zehraس

Mujhpe kia guzri qayamat ki ghari bhe dekhen

Mere baloon me mere baba safeedi dekhen

Jhuk gae meri kamar or Zaefi dekhen

Kaise phechanogay baba k yehe hai Zehraس

Ek he peeshi ne ye haal kia hai mera

Kaise guzrega aseeri me safar Zainabس ka

Cheenli jaegi jab sham e ghareeban me rida

Thame chadar ko yehe soch rahe hai Zehraس

Dar jala hogya pehlu bhe shikasta bibiس

Apko mara hai qunfuz ne tamacha Bibiس

Bangya apka maqtal ye madina bibiس

Ap par tooti qayamat ki ghari hai Zehraس

Koi mastoor na mushkil me khudaya ae

Fatimaس dil k sabhe dard kaise batlae

Maa kabhi bete kay hote na tamacha khae

Ghum tere seenay me nezay ki ani hai Zehraس

Hai giraftar Aliع chalti hai Zehraس peechay

Hath pehlu pe rakhe or Larkharate hue

Bain krte hue girti hai rida ko thame

Har qadum sath yon Haiderع kay chali hai Zehraس

Ye tamache ki wirasat to Sakinaس legi

Keel banjaegi Akberع k jigar me barchi

Khum ye sajjadع ko zainabس ko milegi peeshi

Apni aulaad me ghum bant chuki hai Zehraس

Is tarhan Bibiس tera sajda ada hota hai

Zakhm ka khoon musallay pe baha jata hai

Hath har waqt he phelu pe rakha rehta hai

Kaise tasveer masaeb ki bani hai Zehraس

Sabar qasim yon sar e ghaib kia hai usne

Teegh pr soch k hathoon ko rakha hai usne

Kyu nhe badla abhi tera lia hai usne

Tere Mehdiع ko aziyat ye bari hai Zehraس
