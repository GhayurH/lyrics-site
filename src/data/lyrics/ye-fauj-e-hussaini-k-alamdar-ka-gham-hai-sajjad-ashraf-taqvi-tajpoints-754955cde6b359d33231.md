1
Ye Fauj-E-Hussaini K Alamdar Ka Gham Hai Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.
Jitna Bhi Karein Matam Lagta Hai Keh Kam Hai.
Ghazi Ki Shahadat Par Kuhram Na Kuch Pocho.
Jo Dil Hai Tarapta Hai Jo Aankh Hai Purnam Hai.

2
Aaqa Ki Ziyarat Ko Tarapta Hai Dam-e-Aakhir.
Seene Mein Dilawar Ke Baqi Jo Koi Dam Hai.
Sheh Aaye Hain Darya Se Kis Haal Mein Aye Zainab.
Be Aab Hai Mashkeza Tar Khon Mein Parcham Hai.

3
Sarwar Ka Dam-e-Rukhsat Abbas Yeh Noha Hai.
Nasir Hai Koi Mera Na Ab Koi Humdam Hai.
Zainab Tere Parday Ka Ab Kaun Mohafiz Hai.
Kartay Hain Haram Giriya Abbas Ka Matam Hai.

4
Aaye Na Chacha Jaan Bhi Baba Bhi Nahi Aaye.
Nalah Koi Zindaan Mein Yeh Aaj Bhi Paihum Hai.
Iss Gham Mein Kaleem Aa Kar Dunya Kabhi Dekhey Tu.
Har Gham Ka Madawa Hai Har Zakhm Ka Marham Hai.
