1
Rul Gaya Aye Be Dafan Janaza Shabar Da Haye Sallay Ala.
Khol Ke Sar De Waal Kahway Zainab Dukhiya Haye Sallay Ala.
Kis Zalim Ne Aye Samjhaya Jodah Hasan Nu Zehar Pilaya.
Howray Kis Mozi Ne Ditti Aye Salah Haye Sallay Ala.

2
Hun Deway Ga Kaun Sahara Chad Chaliya Aye Veer Pyara.
Tur Piya Aye Aj Toor Ke Sanjha Sabz Qaba Haye Sallay Ala.
Na Akbar De Shagan Manaye Na Qasim Nu Sehrey Laye.
Hasrataan Le Ke Dil Wich Ho Gaya Veer Juda Haye Sallay Ala.

3
Hasan Abid Nu Kool Bulaya Na Ro Mere Veer Da Jaya.
Taun Phophiyaan Nal Takney Abid Sham De Rah Haye Sallay Ala.
Mangda Aye Ijaz Duawaan Howan Pawein Aakhri Sawaan.
Aal-e-Nabi Da Zikar Rawaan Har Dam Karda Haye Sallay Ala.
