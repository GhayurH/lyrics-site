Suno Arsh Walo Suno Farsh Walo Main Sehra Ali Ka Sunane Chala Hoon
Jo Sun Kar Ye Sehra Hain Jalte Hamesha Unhein Our Bhi Jalane Chala Hoon
Parho Tere Dada Ki Shadi Ka Manzar Aye Mere Imam-e-Zamana(AS) Madad Kar
Tasawar Mein Har Aik Momin Mohib Ko Ali(AS) Ka Barati Banane Chala Hoon
Khuda Kehta Ho Ga Agar Hota Kaba Mere Ghar Se Hi Jati Barat Iss Ki
Ye Mera Wali Hai So Is Ke Liye Main Madine Ko Kaba Banane Chala Hoon
Khuda Ke Wali Ki Hai Shadi Ka Moqa Siwaye Cheragon Ke Kuch Na Jale Ga
Kaha Ye Farishte Ne Hukm-e-Khuda Se Main Naar-e-Jahanum Bhujane Chala Hoon
Atta Kar Ke Haider(AS) Ko Zahra(SA) Si Zooja Pukara Falak Par Ye Mabood Mera
Ali(AS) Ne Banai Hai Qismat Jahan Ki Ali(AS) Ki Main Qismat Banane Chala Hoon
Khuda Keh Raha Tha Muhammad(SAWW) Falak Par Kayi Baar Aye The Zahra(SA) Ki Khatir
So Mairaj Ta Ab Niya Main Thikana Dar-e-Murtaza(AS) Ko Banane Chala Hoon
Ghari Rukhsati Ki TAKALLAUM Jab Ayi Hijab-e-Ilahi Se Awaz Ayi
Ali(AS) Meri Sorat Hai Zahra(SA) Ayat Main Ayat Ko Sorat Se Milane Chala Hoon
