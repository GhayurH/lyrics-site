1
Risalat Ka Logo Sila Mangti Hai.
Nabi Ki Nawasi Rida Mangti Hai.
Yeh Ismat Ki Malka Yeh Bint-e-Ali Hai.
Jo Pathron Ki Barish Mein Kab Se Khari Hai.
Faqat Do Qadam Rasta Mangti Hai.
Zibah Ho Gaye iss Ke Be-Misal Bhai.
Rasan Mein Kahan Se Kahan Tak Hai Aayi.
Yeh TUm Se Ba-Naam-e-Khuda Mangti Hai.
Karo Na Charagon Se Roshan Yeh Rahein.
Khudara Jhuka Lo Yeh Apni Nigahein.
Bhala Kya Yeh Iss Ke Siwa Mangti Hai.
Nabi Zadiyoon Ko Satate Ho Kyun Tum.
Inhein Naam Le Kar Bulate Ho Kyun Tum.
Yeh Marne Ki Apne Dua Mangti Hai.
The Farhan-o- Qurban Bibi Ke Jumlay.
Mujhe Mera Bhai Khule Sar Ne Dekhe.
Yeh Keh Ke Laeen Se Rida Mangti Hai.
