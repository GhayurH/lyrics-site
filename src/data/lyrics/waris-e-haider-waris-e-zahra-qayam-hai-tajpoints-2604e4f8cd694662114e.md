Waris-e-Haider Waris-e-Zahra Qayam Hai.

1
Al-Ajal Ya Imam Al-Ajal Ya Imam.

2
Qayam Se Yeh Sara Zamana Qayam Hai.
Zinda Hain Youn Sare Payamber Sare Imam
Aakhri Mansab Aakhri Ohda Qayam Hai.
Adam Ke Aytbar Ke Waris Hussain Hain.
Imran Ke Shear Ke Warish Hussain Hain.
Ahmad Ke Wirsa Dar Ke Waris Hussain Hain.
Haider Ki Zulfiqar Ke Warish Hussain Hain.
Woh Muntazir Jo Muntazir-e-Inteqam Hai.
Woh Waris-e-Hussain Alh Aslam Hai.
Dehshat Ke Iss Door Mein Zinda Hain Hum Loag.
Hum Ko Zinda Rakhne Wala Qayam Hai.

3
Kabe Ka Dar Pusht Pe Ho Ga Waqt-e-Zahoor.
Oss Ki Khatir Khan-e-Kaba Qayam Hai.

4
Shabir Dil Ka Hosla Mehdi Nazar Ke Chain.
Apni Jagah Pe Dono Hain Deen-e-Khuda Ke Dein.
Woh Noor-e-Fatima Hain Yeh Narjis Ke Noor-e-Ain.
Woh Waris-e-Rasool Hain Yeh Waris-e-Hussain.
Nikle Gi Taig Ibn-e-Sheh La-Fatah Ke Bad.
Ik Our Jang Honi Hai Ab Karbala Ke Bad.
Lena Hai ALLAH Ne Badal-e-Khoon-e-Hussain.
Qayam Hai Khaliq Ka Irada Qayam Hai.

5
Aik Muhammad Ki Khatir Yeh Dunya Bani.
Aik Muhammad Se Yeh Dunya Qayam Hai.

6
Garche Hussainio Pe Bohat Hai Zameen Tang.
Hain Muntazir Chalein Gaye Imam-e-Zamaan Ke Sang.
Roze Pe Sheh Ke Kehta Hai Parcham Ka Surkh Rang.
Ab Tak Hoi Na Khatam Qamar Karbala Ki Jang.
Ya Rab Woh Inteqam Ka Qisa Sunai De.
Karb-o-Bala Ka Dosra Hisa Dikhai De.
