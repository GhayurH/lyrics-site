Koi Zara Yeh Ja Kar Abbas Ko Bata Day.
Jane De Hum Ko Zalim Haris(L) Se Bole Bache.Phirte Hain Mare Mare Do Din Se Bhoke Piyase.Sar Katne Se Pehle Pani Humein Pila Day.
Lagte The Jab Tamachay Maa Ko Pukarte The.Hansta Tha Jab Sitamgar Kehte The Ro Ke Bache.Zalim Hamari Koi Taqseer Tu Bata Day.
Dil Par Asar Hai Itna Abbas(AS) Ki Wafa Ka.Khuwaish Bare Ki Hai Woh Qurban Pehle Ho Ga.Chota Yeh Chahta Hai Woh Pehle Sar Kata Day.
Jis Ko Ameer-e-Muslim Sheh Ne Laqab Diya Tha.Ulad Par Ossi Ki Aaya Hai Waqt Kaisa.Jo Chahe Ghurkiyaan De Jo Chahe Woh Saza Day.
Muslim(AS) Ki Thi Wasiyat Do Mere Ladlay Hain.Aafat Pari Hai Aisi Mujh Se Bichar Gaye Hain.Mar Jaon Jab Main Koi Maa Se Unhein Mila Day.
Dhondo Yahin Kahin Hain Un Se Bhi Badla Loon Ga.Hakim Yeh Keh Raha Tha Inam Oss Ko Doon Ga.Ghazi(AS) Ke Bhanjo Ko Jo Qaid Kar Ke La Day.
Kofe Ke Dar Pe Jis Dam Muslim(AS) Ki Lash Dekhi.:AKBAR: Kaha Yeh Maa Ne Ro Ro Ke Mere Wali.Batla De Bas Kahan Hain Aaqa Kaneez Zaday.
