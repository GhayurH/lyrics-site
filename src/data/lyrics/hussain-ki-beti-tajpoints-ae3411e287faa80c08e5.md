Taharton Ka Khulasa Hussain Ki Beti.
Hussain Ka Hain Asasa Hussain Ki Beti.

1
Rukh-e-Rubab Ka Qibla Hussain Maula Hain.
Dil-e-Rubab Ka Kaba Hussain Ki Beti.
Rasool Pak Ka Hain Naaz Fatima Zahra.
Hain Naz-e-Fatima Zahra Hussain Ki Beti.

2
Alam Ke Samne Pohancha Murad Pori Hoi.
Abhi Tu Sirf Kaha Tha Hussain Ki Beti.
Furat Khushk Hoi Par Ada Na Kar Payi.
Tumhari Pyas Ka Qarza Hussain Ki Beti.

3
Haqeeqatan Tu Jali Hai Rasool Ki Kamli.
Jala Nahi Tera Kurta Hussain Ki Beti.
Sabeelein Nasab Hain Har Samt Jo Zamane Mein.
Hai Teri Mashk Ka Sadqa Hussain Ki Beti.

4
Jo Dar Pe Aaye Ga Mere Murad Paye Ga.
Keh Main Hoon Bali Sakina Hussain Ki Beti.
Main Pursa Daar Hoon:YASIR RAZA: Sar-e-Mehshar.
Mujhe Banaye Gi Maula Hussain Ki Beti.
