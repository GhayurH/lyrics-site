Jab gayi Aal-e-Payamber Kerbala se Shaam tak
Sath tha zalim ka lashkar Kerbala se Shaam tak

1
1) Aik rasan main bazu-e-Zainab Sakina ka gala
Larkharai her qadam per Kerbala se Shaam tak

2
2) Ummat-e-Aasi se roker shah kai ahlai haram
Mangtai ja tai thai chadar Kerbala se Shaam tak

3
3) Bai rida ahlai haram ko her qadam aati rahi
Yad-e-Abbas-e-Dilawar Kerbala se Shaam tak

4
4) Chera balon se chupai ashk ankhon se rawan
Ja rahi hai bint-e-Haider Kerbala se Shaam tak

5
5) Allah Allah yeh wafa yeh jaan sari dekhiyai
Sath thai sheh kai bahatar Kerbala se Shaam tak

6
6) Ja rahin thi um-e-Laila zair-e-lab kehti huwi
Hai Akber Hai Akber Kerbala se Shaam tak

7
7) Chumta palkon se nakshai pai bint-e-Murtaza
Khab main ja ta jo mehshar Kerbala se Shaam tak
