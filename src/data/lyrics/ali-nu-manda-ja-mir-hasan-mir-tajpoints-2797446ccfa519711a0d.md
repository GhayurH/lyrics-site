Ali Nu Manda Ja Mir Hasan Mir
Tere Ishq Mein Ghomoon Nagar Nagar.
Tere Geet Main Gaon Ghali Ghali.
Main Safeer Teri Wila Ka Hoon.
Mera Kam Hai Dam Dam Ali Ali.
Murshid Man Ke Pak Ali Nu.
Haider Haider Karda Ja.
Bas Ali Nu Manda Ja.
Bakht Ko Tere Bhag Lagin Gay Door Ke Janat Aa Jaye Gi.
Ik Din Sari Dunya Tere Pichey Pichey Khud Aaye Gi.
Sansoon Ki Tasbeeh Bana Kar Naam Ali Ka Japta Ja.
Masti Mein Hain Basti Walay Tu Bhi Mere Yaar Ajab.
Tujh Ko Kya Maloom Nahi Hai Eid Ka Din Hai Terah Rajab.
Maa Ki Taharat Ka Ayelan Tu Jashan Mana Kar Karta Ja.
Abhi Tu Bint-e-Asad Thin Tawaf-e-Kaba Mein.
Keh Pathron Pe Khushi Se Ajeeb Lali Thi.
Khuda Ka Shukar Keh Ik Samt Ruk Gayin Warna.
Har Aik Simt Se Dewar Phatney Wali Thi.
Bint-e-Asad Khud Dein Gi Duaein Haider Haider Karta Ja.
Haider Haider.
Haq Ali Da Pehla Number.
Haq Ali Da Pehla Number.
Salman Da Maula Ali Ali.
Miqdad Da Aaqa Ali Ali.
Jag Palan Wala Ali Ali.
Arshaan Da Rakha Ali Ali.
Kalmay Da Kalma Ali Ali.
Kabay Da Kaba Ali Ali.
Hasnain Da Baba Ali Ali.
Sada Murshid Maula Ali Ali.
Sanu Palan Wala, Wekhan Wala, Dewan Wala Ali Ali.
Loki Apne Peraan De Lakh Naam Wadhawan Ki Honda Aye.
Rab Ne Jis Nu Izataan Ditiyaan Bas Osay Da Sir Ocha Aye.
Sab Taun Ocha Naam Ali Da Aykho Tasbeeh Parhda Ja.
Paish Tu Kar Tu Apni Niyat Zair-o-Zabar Woh Samjhaein Gay.
Quraan Mein Jo Raaz Chupay Hain Tujh Pe Zahir Ho Jaein Gay.
Hub-e-Ali Ko Dil Mein Basa Kar Aayat Aayat Parhta Ja.
LaIlaha Illallah, LaIlaha Illallah.
Ali Ki Taig Se Tareekh Pochiye Sab Ki.
Parha Tha Kis Ne Kahan LaIlaha Illallah.
Aye Na Howay Hashar De Haray Tainu Munh Di Khanari Paye Jaye.
Rozay Hajj Namazaan Tere Khaliq Tere Munh Te Marey.
Jaye Mehfooz Ibadataan Rakhniyaan Zikar Ali Da Karda Ja.
Tu Bhi Takallum Shamil Ho Ja Jata Hai Apno Ka Lashkar.
Rasta Tu Dushwar Hai Lekin Le Jaein Gay Woh Manzil Par.
Aagay Hain Salman-o-Abuzar Pichey Pichey Chalta Ja.
Dil Mein Basa Ke Pyar Ali De Bachiyaan Da.
Banr Ja Tu Hubdar Ali De Bachiyaan Da.
Mang Le Rahib Jinnay Putar Mangney Ne.
Lagiya Aye Darbar Ali De Bachiyaan Da.
Tere Dar Ke Siwa Mere Maula Mera Koi Thikana Nahi Hai.
Ya Ali Murtaza Mere Wali Tere Dar Se Na Jaon Ga Khali.
Bhar Do Jholi Jee Maula.
