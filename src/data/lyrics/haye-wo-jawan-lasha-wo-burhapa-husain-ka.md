Haey Ali Akbar Ali Akbar
Haey wo jawaan lasha wo burapaa hussain ka, Haey Ali Akbar

1
1) Ibne nameer tumse ye zainab ka hey sawaal,
Neze ko torte huwe aya na ye khayaal
(Ham shakl ye jawaan hai)shah-e- mashrakain ka

2
2) Wo jiske intizar me sudhara he be karaar,
Shayad wo khat me iska qayamat ko intizaar
(lakte jigar he fathe)beta hussain ka

3
3) Jab se chale khayam se ham shakl-e-mustafa,
Matam kuna hai arsh to larza me karbala
(He ham bapaa khayaam me)ek gul hai ben ka

4
4) Zainab tumhaare purke me kal tak tha jo pala,
Neze ka phal jo tutta to hatif ne di sadaa
(be jurm qatl leila ka)he noor-e-ayn ka

5
5. Jis roz se jawaan ki jawaani hai uth gayi,
Moje farat ki bhi rawaani hai root gayi
(be iktiyaar meer hai)Momin kai nain ka

6
6. Kaise uthaye lash ko mazloom-e-karbala,
Mu phair kar Hussain ne Allah se kaha
(Le imtihaan shok se)sabr Hussain ka
