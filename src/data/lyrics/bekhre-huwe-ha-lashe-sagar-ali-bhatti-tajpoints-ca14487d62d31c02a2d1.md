1
Bikhrey Hoye Hain Lashay Aoulad-e-Mustafa Ke.
Sare Lahu Lahu Hain Manzar Yeh Karbala Ke.
Zakhmo Se Choor Sare Be Sar Paray Hain Lashay.
Murjha Gaye Hain Ran Mein Yeh Phool Fatima Ke.

2
Akbar Ne Barchi Khayi, Asghar Ne Teer Khaya.
Kat Kar Girey Hain Bazu Abbas-e-Bawafa Ke.
Zainab Ke Chand Dono Marey Gaye Hain Ban Mein.
Tukray Hain Ran Mein Phailay Farwa Ke Meh Laqa Ke.

3
Nezon Ne Phool Zakhmi Sarwar Ke Kar Diye Hain.
Teeron Ne Kiya Zakhmi Aa Kar Foaj-e-Jafa Ke.
Jaltay Hain Sare Khaimay Yeh Sham-e-Ghareebaan Hai.
Zewar Bhi Lut Rahe Hain Aoulad-e-La Fata Ke.

4
Aal-e-Rasool Par Hai Maqtal Mein Kaisa Lamha.
Quran Parhne Walay Dushman Hoye Rida Ke.
 Fayaz Jab Yeh Noha Sagar Ne Parha Hai Tu.
Roney Lagay Hain Halqay Sare Ahl-e-Azza Ke.
