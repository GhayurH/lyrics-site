1
Aye Jaun Bhai Ho Tum Meray Mir Hasan Mir.
Abbas Ki Qasam Tumhein Lag Jao Na Galay.
Baba Bhi Aaye Hain Mere Nana Bhi Aaye Hain.
Chehray Ka Noor Dekhney Mosa Bhi Aaye Hain.
Yousuf Kharey Hoye Hain Ziyarat Ke Wastay.
Tujh Ko Duaein Aaj Se De Gi Dua-e-Noor.
Khushbo Tere Paseeney Ki Phailay Gi Door Door.
Mil Jaye Ga Lahu Tera Khon-e-Hussain Se.
Ahl-e-Haram Ko Tum Se Hai Dharas Mere Dalair.
Pehra Do Phir Khiyam Ka Utho Aye Mere Shair.
Kyun Ja Rahe Ho Tum Mujhe Ghurbat Mein Chor Ke.
Karna Dua Hussain Pe Aisa Na Waqt Aaye.
Koi Sada De Hum Ko Our Hum Se Chala Na Jaye.
Lasha Tumhara Dasht Se Hum Khud Uthaein Gay.
Aahista Kehna Saqi-e-Kausar Se Mera Haal.
Rakhti Thi Meri Pyas Ka Amma Bohat Khiyal.
Ghurbat Na Ho Biyaan Meri Amma Ke Samne.
Aaye Hain Zakhm Tum Ko Meri Jaan Kahan Kahan.
Inn Zalimo Ne Mari Hain Seene Pe Barchiyaan.
Shabeer Kis Tarha Se Tumharey Galay Lagay.
Tu Ne Gala Kataya Hai Pehle Hussain Se.
Dilwaein Gi Jaza Woh Sheh-e-Mashreqain Se.
Chadar Utha Ke Deti Hain Amma Dua Tujhey.
Seene Laga Ke Kehne Laga Ibn-e-Fatima.
Khushbo Se Teri Mehkay Gi Ta Hashar Karbala.
Boo Hai Tere Paseeney Mein Kis Ne Kaha Tujhey.
Jaldi Thi Dekho Jaun Ko Sajjad Kis Qadar.
Dhaal Our Zirrah Ko Phaink Ke Seena Kiya Sipar.
Jis Dam Suna Hussain Alh Aslam Se
