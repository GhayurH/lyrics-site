Lorian wala bhira, jal gayai jhoola tenda
Shaam waindi paiyan Asghar veer wey hunr alwida alwida

1
Devan lut da haal kiya raas aayi nahi kerbala

2
1. phul jiya tenda badan, hai jo mohtaj e kafan
Waqt honda bakht honda, kar ke waindi mein dafan
Piyoo wangu tu benava, maa wangu mein be rida

3
2. teer jhul ke sum gaiyan, de ke wakhra ghum gaiyan
Veer malkul mot koon bhi, toon kara matam gaiyan
Waikh rondi rahi kaza, akhri tendi ada

4
3. lori sunr hamsheer ton, ujri maa dilgeer ton
Raj handawain veer shala, veer di jageer toon
Haan khari daindi dua, hath mein rasiyan walay cha

5
4. dekh hath azad nai, jholi bhi abaad nahi
Bhainr te maa kol bachiya, kujh bhi tenday baad nahi
Ujree koon ai taan dasaa, kiwain jeesan tain siwa

6
5. kiya dasaan besheer we, apni mein taqdeer we
Bohoon maikun babay da qatil, jhirkaan daindai veer wey
Hai teri maqtal gawah, kujh bhi nai sada riya
