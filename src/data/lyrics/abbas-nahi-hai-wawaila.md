(Abbas nahi hai wawaila)
(Wawaila haye wawaila)

1
1. (Shabbir hai maktal mei tanha)

2
2. Ghazi ke lahu me duba huwa jab ran se alam wapas aya

3
Zainab ne ye rokar farmaya Abbas nahi hai wawaila

4
3. Hatho se kamar ko tham huwe sheh sar ko jukaye rote hai

5
Ek bhayi huwa bhayi se judaa Abbas nahi hai wawaila

6
4. Akabr ke janaze par akar ro ro ke mere maula ne kaha

7
Me khud hi uthaawunga lasha Abbas nahi hai wawaila

8
5. Jab shimr-e-laeen tarpaaya roro ke sakina ne ye kaha

9
Tum aa ke bachalo ey baba Abbas nahi hai wawaila

10
6. Jab chaha kiya bachi pe sitam jab chaha tamacha mar liya

11
Karti hai Sakina aaho buka Abbas nahi hai wawaila

12
7. Zainab ye duaey karti thi baba ko sadaaey de de kar

13
Chin jaye na mere sar se ridaa Abbas nahi hai wawaila

14
8. Manga jo yatimo ne pani kya karti bhala zehra sani

15
Ye kehke kaleja thaam liya Abbas nahi hai wawaila

16
9. Dekho jo ali ki beti ne maydan me jalte khayme ko

17
Zainab ne bari hasrat se kaha Abbas nahi hai wawaila
