1
Karbala Se Jo Kabi Ho Ke Hawa Aati Hai Mir Hasan Mir.
Kissi Masoom Ki Hichki Ki Sada Aati Hai.
Maa Samajhti Hai Dulhan Laon Gi Aaya Hai Shabab.
Sheh Samajhtay Hain Keh Akbar Ki Qaza Aati Hai.

2
Haan Dharkta Na Ho Marqad Mein Kahin Qalb-e-Rubab.
Ali Asghar Ali Asghar Ki Sada Aati Hai.
Kaun Sota Hai Kinaray Tere Aye Nehar-e-Furat.
Teri Mati Se Abhi Bo-e-Wafa Aati Hai.

3
Koi Beemar Na Parhta Ho Tahajud Ki Namaz.
Raat Ko Beriyon Ki Kyun Yeh Sada Aati Hai.
Jab Kabhi Zyada Main Beemar Jo Hota Hoon Shaheed.
Khud Bakhud Mere Liye Khak-e-Shifa Aati Hai.
