Rab janay kinj haq lain laey Zehra ujri darbar gaye

1
Enjh lagda si jeewain pak Rasool di kujh vi lagdi naye
Rondi darbar choon bahaar gaye

2
1) Lagda-e-hizyaan di halat vich teray piyo nay sanad likhi hosi
Haey mimbar tay baithay malooon di Zehra ro ro kay
Sundi logon guftar gaye

3
2) Zehra tay sani-e-Zehra nay ki ajab muqadar paye nay
Aik haq mangiya darbar ich ja babay day sahabiyan ton
Aik ban qaidan bazaar gaye

4
3) Khatoon-e-jina jadon ro ro kay haq mangiyan bad kirdaran ton
Kyun kalma gowan babay di tehreer nu na maniya
Eh gal ujri nu maar gaye

5
4) Aaj vi haat rakh kay pasay tay khatoon qayamat rondi aey
Jeday ron tay pehray baad Mohammed ummatiyan laye nay
Khabray kinjh zulam sahar gaye

6
5) Akhtar jo pakh Mohammed di maiyat vi choor kay tur gaye sun
Majboor hokay oona badbakhtan day samnay haq badlay
Haye Zahra pehli waar gaye
