1
Qaidi Hun Main Baba Jaan Ali Shanawar – Ali Jee.
Ab Shah Zadi Kahan.
Na Apna Woh Ghar Hai Na Apna Madina.
Woh Banhein Chacha Ki Tumhara Woh Seena.
Bolo Khudara Tum Ho Kahan Main Kahan.
Jis Din Se Tum Se Juda Ho Gayi Hoon.
Dekho Tu Baba Main Kya Ho Gayi Hoon.
Ujra Makaan Hai Rehti Hoon Ab Main Jahan.
Kehti Na Thi Dekho Na Jao Baba.
Taras Khao Mujh Par Taras Khao Baba.
Tum Kya Gaye Ho Mujh Par Gira Aasmaan.
Jaisay Hi Naizay Pe Tum Aaye Baba.
Main Ne Tamachay Bohat Khaye Baba.
Rukhsar Par Hain Abhi Tak Sitam Ke Nishaan.
Manga Kaneezi Mein Baba Kissi Ne.
Phainka Meri Simt Sadqa Kissi Ne.
Paidal Chali Hoon Kanton Ke Main Darmiyaan.
Milney Ko Hum Se Chaley Aao Baba.
Ik Bar Chehra Dikha Jao Baba.
Aye Pyaray Baba Phir Hum Kahan Tum Kahan.
Chalo Sham Chalte Hain:REHAN-o-SARWAR:.
Wahan Dekhna Kya Hai Chehlum Ka Manzar.
Bibi Sakina Kartin Hain Ab Tak Fogaan.
