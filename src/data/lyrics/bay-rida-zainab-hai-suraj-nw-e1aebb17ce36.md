Berida Zainab Hai Suraj Aaj Kyu Chupta Nahi.

Hai Charagha Shaam Main  Koyi Diya Bhujta Nahi.

Kis Tarha Guzri Hain Un Peh Shaam Kay Bazaar Main

Khoon Hai Sajjad Ki  Aankhon Say Kyu Rukta Nahi.

Aik Hee Zanjeer Main Paband Hain Dono Imam

Isliye Garrdan Jhuki  Sajjad Chal Sakta Nahi.

Ban Kay Fateh Shaam Zainab Aaj Bhi Hai Shaam Main

Mitt Gaya Naam-e-yazeed  Koyi Nishaan Milta Nahi.

Jab Karam Mumtaz Ho Aur Ho Ata-e-fatima

Khud Likhatay Hain Woh Noha  Phir Karam Rukta Nahi.
