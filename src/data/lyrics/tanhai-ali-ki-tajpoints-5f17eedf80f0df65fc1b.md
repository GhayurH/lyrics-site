1
Tanhai Ali Ki.
Haye Zahra Teri Tanhai Hai Tanhai Ali Ki.
Haye Gham Mein Tere Jati Rahi Benayi Ali Ki.
Darbar Mein Zalim Ke Gayi Bint-e-Payamber.

2
Kya Waqt Tha Kuch Kar Na Saka Fateh-e-Khaiber.
Haye Hanste Rahe Ghurbat Pe Tamashai Ali Ki.
Paigham-e-Wilayat Liye Ghar Ghar Gayi Zahra.
Shohar Ke Liye Dhaal Thi Jab Tak Rahi Zinda.

3
Haye Dunya Ko Batati Rahi Sachai Ali Ki.
Kehte The Ali Zahra Se Kya Waqt Hai Bibi.
Ab Hum Ko Salam Aa Ke Bhi Karta Nahi Koi.
Haye Hai Baad-e-Nabi Kaisi Pazeerai Ali Ki.

4
Dar Jalta Howa Fatima Zahra Pe Giraya.
Mohsin Ki Shahadat Ka Sabbab Ban Gayi Dunya.
Haye Tasveer Mukamil Nahi Ho Payi Ali Ki.
Bahir Kabhi Hujray Mein Kabhi Qabar-e-Nabi Par.

5
Har Raat Ki Tareeki Mein Ulaad Se Chup Kar.
Haye Nikli Hai Tujhe Dhoondne Benai Ali Ki.
Zahra Ki Judai Ko Lagaye Howay Dil Se.
Pachees Baras Maula Ne Chup Chaap Guzare.

6
Haye Ik Hujre Mein Kul Dunya Simat Ayi Ali Ki.
Oss Waqt Takallum Wo Zamane Ka Wali Tha.
Sajjad Jo Zainab Ko Liye Shaam Se Guzra.
Haye Islam Ko Khamoshi Bacha Layi Ali Ki.
