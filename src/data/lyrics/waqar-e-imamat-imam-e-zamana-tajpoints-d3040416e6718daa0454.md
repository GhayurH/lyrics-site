Waqar e Imamat Imam e Zamana Lyrics
Nishan-e-Nabowat Imam-e-Zamana.

1
Ho Chashm-e-Inayat Imam-e-Zamana.
Badal Do Yeh Qismat Imam-e-Zamana.
Areeza Mera Mauj Le Kar Chali Hai.
Ba Andaz-e-Midhat Imam-e-Zamana.

2
Basi Hai Marey Dil Mein Rooz-e-Azal Se.
Tumhari Muhabbat Imam-e-Zamana.
Qayamat Se Pehle Jo Mumkin Nahi Hai.
Tumhari Ziyarat Imam-e-Zamana.

3
Tu Phir Aaj Ki Shab Bapa Ho Jahan Mein.
Achanak Qayamat Imam-e-Zamana.
Madina Ho Kaba Ho Hum Hoon Keh Esa.
Hain Sab Ki Zarorat Imam-e-Zamana.

4
Humein Jeetay Jee Mar Dalay Gi Ab Tu.
Tumhari Yeh Furqat Imam-e-Zamana.
Bajuz Sharbat e Deed Tashna Labon Ko.
Nahi Koi Hajat Imam-e-Zamana.

5
Karey Gi Zamane Mein Insaf Qayam.
Tumhari Adalat Imam-e-Zamana.
Likhe Kaisay Rehan Midhat Tumhari.
Yeh Hogi Jasarat Imam-e-Zamana.
