Ibadaton Ka Hai Kalma Hussain Kafi Hai.
Shariyaton Ka Sahara Hussain Kafi Hai.

1
Shifaton Ke Liye Syeda Ne Farmaya.
Hussain Kafi Hai Mera Hussain Kafi Hai.
Kissi Waba Se Na Darna Kabhi Azadaro.
Har Ik Marz Ke Liye Ya Hussain Kafi Hai.

2
Jinna Ke Dar Pe Kharey Anbiya Yeh Kehte Hain.
Humein Tu Bas Bin-e-Zahra Hussain Kafi Hai.
Zara Yeh Neend Se Insaniyat Tu Uth Jaye.
Pukarey Ga Yeh Zamana Hussain Kafi Hai.

3
Nasab Kharab Kabhi Bool Hi Nahi Sakta.
Halali Sirf Kahey Ga Hussain Kafi Hai.
Ali Ke Ghair Pareshaan They Youn Sar-e-Mehshar.
Dar-e-Jinna Pe Likha Tha Hussain Kafi Hai.

4
Hasan Batool Ali Mustafa Tu Door Ki Baat.
Jinah Ke Wastay Hussain Kafi Hai.
Jab Anbiya They Kharey Sar Jhukaye Rooz-e-Alast.
Tu Rab Yeh Bola Ke Tanha Hussain Kafi Hai.

5
Sawal-e-Qabar Se Pehle Nishan-e-Matam Ho.
Farishta Dekh Ke Bola Hussain Kafi Hai.
Kiya Jo Matam-e-Zanjeer Tu Shifa Ke Liye.
Har Aik Zakhm Yeh Bola Hussain Kafi Hai.

6
Jahan Mein Dekh Musalman Bas Yeh Kehta Hai.
Mere Nabi Ka Nawasa Hussain Kafi Hai.
 Wajih Our Yeh Mirza Tu Hain Ghulam-e-Hussain.
Tu Inn Ke Qalb Mein Tanha Hussain Kafi Hai.
