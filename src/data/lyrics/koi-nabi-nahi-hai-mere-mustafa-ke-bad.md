1
Koi Nabi Nahi Hai Mere Mustafa Ke Bad
Shair-e-Khuda Nahi Hai Koi Murtaza Ke Baad
Na Hai Kissi Ke Betay Hussain-o-Hasan Se Our
Na Fatima Si Maa Hai Koi Fatima Ke Baad

2
Itnay Sitam Hoye Hain Muhammad Ki Aal Par
Hoti Rahi Hai Ibtida Har Inteha Ke Baad
Jaisi Parhi Hussain Ne Teeron Ki Chaon Mein
Aisi Namaz Phir Na Hoi Karbala Ke Baad

3
Qatil Ko Bhi Atta Kiya Sharbat Hazoor Ne
Itna Karam Hai Logo Itni Jafa Ke Baad
Abbas Tere Naam Ko Main Apne Ishq Se
Pehle Wafa Ke Likhon Ya Likhon Wafa Ke Baad

4
Shabeer Keh Ke Bhai Bulatay Rahe Jisay
Hur Our Kya Sawal Karey Iss Atta Ke Baad
Darbar-e-Sham Mein Kaha Zainab Ne Sun Yazid
Dartay Nahi Kissi Se Bhi Hum Tu Khuda Ke Baad
