Dulhan sog-e manaye (Sehra duba jaaye)

1
(Maa roti hai kasim ki aur (Dulhan-e sog-e manaye)

2
1. Khun-e ka dariya sar se guzra,  Sehra duba jaaye
Dulhan sog-e manaye. Maa roti hai kasim ki aur (Dulhan-e sog-e manaye)

3
2. Kangna mendhi masnar-e sehra (Gham ki ek-e nishani)
Kajol anchal ghehne madar (aur purshad-e shahani)
(Dub-e gaye sab gham ke andar)Dulha ran ko chaye
Dulhan sog-e manaye. Maa roti hai kasim ki aur (Dulhan-e sog-e manaye)

4
3. Rehna saki ek shab bi suhagan (Kasim teri dulhan)
Masnat-e matam ban gaya kasim (Tere ghar ka anghan)
(Pursa dene shadi ke ghar)Aaj-e barati aye
Dulhan sog-e manaye. Maa roti hai kasim ki aur (Dulhan-e sog-e manaye)

5
4. Dulha koi hatyar-e sajaa kar (Ghar se kahi jataa hai)
Jis sar pe sehra bandha he (Neze par ata hai)
(Ran se nahi tum kasim aye)Lashe ke tukre aye
Dulhan sog-e manaye. Maa roti hai kasim ki aur (Dulhan-e sog-e manaye)

6
5. Majburi shabbir-e ki dekho (Tukre lashe ke sunkar)
Ik ganchri me dulha sehra (Dil me dard-e ka khanjar)
(Maktal se yu ghar ate hai)Chot-e jigar pe khaye
Dulhan sog-e manaye. Maa roti hai kasim ki aur (Dulhan-e sog-e manaye)

7
6. Mehendi ke din matam gar me (Khak-e bala hai sar me)
Aysa dulha aysi dulhan (Kon-e he mehro gar me)
(Dulhan-e rasta dekh-e rahi hai)Dulha mara jaye
Dulhan sog-e manaye. Maa roti hai kasim ki aur (Dulhan-e sog-e manaye)

8
7. Behno ko kya nek-e milaa hai (Ansu aa hai lasha)
Milgaya kasim ka wo phupi ka (Kyu na kare wawaila)
(Ek ek tukre noor-e nazar ke)Maa na kahi marjaye
Dulhan sog-e manaye. Maa roti hai kasim ki aur (Dulhan-e sog-e manaye)
