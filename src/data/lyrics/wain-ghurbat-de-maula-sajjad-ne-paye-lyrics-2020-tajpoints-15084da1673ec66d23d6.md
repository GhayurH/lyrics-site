Wain Ghurbat De Maula Sajjad Ne Paye Write Up

1
Phophiyaan Te Mawaan De Burqay Jadon Lokaan Aan Lahaye.
Khon De Athro Sajjad Di Akhiyaan Choon Hoye Pehli Dafa Jari.
Jadon Zainab De Sir Di Chadar Nu Shimar Ne Hath Si Laye.
Janwar Apne Serab V Karday Ray Chawaan Wich Ban De Ray.
Dhup Wich Baithay Qaidi Bachay Ray Takday Door Taun Saye.
Zalimaan Kita Shabeer De Sar Kitay Ayelan Aye Wakhra Si.

2
Darbar Taein Aye Sar Shami Hathaan Choon Honda Jaye.
Dig Payi Zainab Turday Hoye Naqay Taun Tak Bazu Ghazi De.
Bajiyaan Hoiyaan Banwaan Nal Kiwein Hun Khak Taun Bazu Chaye.
Maut Te Akbar Bazar Guzarday Hi Sajjad Nu Aa Gayi Si.
Par Aidiyaan Sanwaan Ne Mukan Wich Chali Saal Ne Laye.
