Hy veeran gharan vich, rovay beemar kharee

Aisee vitchree na milee fair Ali Akbar nu kadee

Veer vitchray na kisay bai,n da Akbar waangoon

Maut ban janda ai ai roag dukhee baina layee.

Aja padesiyan veera mai, teray raah veykhaan

Jora shagna da banaya mai veeran’ teray layee.

Qasid Aaja-ven tay Akbar nu, kaveen ro rokay

Saavan gindee hoyee humsheer nu mil ja cheytee.

Main haan beemar veeran keevain, teray kowl aavan

Baad Karbal dee Medinay town ay dushwaar kharee.

Jyundee maar jaway gee Sughra nay, jadown ay sunaya

Kha gayee Akbar day kalayjay nu zulam dee barchee.

Khair hovay teri Akbar mai, dua’van kardee

Tun tay dasveen nu veeran’ layn menu aana see.

Naal Asghar nu vee lay avay, jay almaan vaala

Fair mai jaan naa deyvaan kadee saree zindagi.

Allah janay keevain nibh gayee, aidee kalayan Akhtar

Jaag kay raatan guzaray sain khaaban de daree.
