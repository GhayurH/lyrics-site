Ro Ro Ghazi Nu Bulaway Zainab.
Shimar Mangda Asaan Toon Chadraan v Kiwein Hun Parday Bachaway Zainab.

1
Ghazi Aa Gayi Main Tere Maan Uttay Samiyaa Toon Nehar Dey Kinarey Veran.
Ho Ke Paband Veran Shamiyaan Di Nangay Sir Sham Noon Jaway Zainab.
Munh Luka Ke Fizza Di Kand Olay Veer Di Qatal Gah Che Rondi Aan.
Charon Janib Hai Bheer Qatilaan Di Kithon Bhaina Nu Langaway Zainab.

2
Aounda Takiya Jo Khali Alam Tera Naal Phatiyaan Hoya Si Mashkiza.
Zulm Ki Ki Asaan Te Hoye Ne Kino Hun Ja Ke Dasaway Zainab.
Shamiyaan Daaj Lutiya Kubra Da Ban Qaidan Teri Khaloti Aye.
Mehndiyaan Walay Hath Numanri De Khari Veeran Nu Dikhaway Zainab.

3
Jay Na Karda Paband Ghazi Nu Izn Jang Da Hussain Jay Denda.
Ho Nayi Sakda Si Fair Qaid Ho Ke Munh Nu Walaan Che Lukaway Zainab.
Bad Qatal-e-Abbas Toon Akhtar Darbadar Ho Gaiyaan Fatima Jaiyaan.
Sang Barsandiyaan Badmashaan Di Bheer Rahwaan Choon Hataway Zainab.
