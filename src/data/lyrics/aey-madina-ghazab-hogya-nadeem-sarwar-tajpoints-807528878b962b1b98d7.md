1
Aey Madina Ghazab Hogya Nadeem Sarwar.
Qafila Jo Watan Laut Aaya.
Aa Ke Baab-e-Madina Pe Thehra.
Gird Ujray Hoye Qafilay Ke.
Pursa Daroon Ne Halqa Banaya.
Shor-e-Matam Utha Shor-e-Giriya Utha.
Aye Madina Ghazab Ho Gaya.
BiBi Kulsoom Ka Tha Yeh Noha.
Shehar-e-Nana Ke La Taqabaleena.
Shehar Nana Ke Hum Aaye Ujray Hoye.
Khair Muqaddam Ke Qabil Kahan Hum Rahe.
Shehar Nana Ke La Taqabaleena.
Haye Nana Bhara Ghar Ka Ghar Lut Gaya.
Chadrein Lut Gayin Maal-o-Zar Lut Gaya.
Tha Muharram Ka Kaisa Maheena.
Chomtay They Jisay Aap Nana Sada.
Terah Zarboon Se Kata Gaya Woh Gala.
Shimar Ke Dil Mein Tha Kitna Keena.
Bhai Atharah They Koi Bhi Na Raha.
Chup Maheeney Ka Asghar Bhi Mara Gaya.
Lut Gaya Sab Hamara Khazeena.
Asar-e-Ashor Naizon Pe Sar Aa Gaye.
Aayi Sham-e-Ghareebaan Tu Khaimay Jalay.
Sar Pe Chadar Kissi Ke Bachi Na.
Be Rida Hum Ko Dar Dar Phiraya Gaya.
Zulm Hai Kocha Kocha Sajaya Gaya.
Barishien Pathron Ki Ruki Na.
Haye Amma Galay Se Laga Lo Humein.
Apne Marqad Mein Lillah Chupa Lo Humein.
Iss Qadar Pehle Paidal Chali Na.
Ho Gaya Hashar:REHAN-o-SARWAR: Bapa.
Ro Ke Shehzadiyoon Ne Yeh Jis Dam Kaha.
Sath Aayi Na Bali Sakina.
