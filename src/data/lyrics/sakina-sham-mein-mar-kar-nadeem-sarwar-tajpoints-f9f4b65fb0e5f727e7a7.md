1
Sakina Sham Mein Mar Kar Nadeem Sarwar.
Sakina Sham Mein Mar Kar Yehi Samjha Gayi Logo.
Judai Baap Ke Seene Ki Mujh Ko Kha Gayi Logo.
Qafas Ghurbat Udasi Shimar Ka Dar Pyas Ki Shiddat.
Sitam Itnay Hoye Masoom Ko Maut Aa Gayi Logo.
Woh Maa Jo Dhop Mein Aksar Ali Asghar Ko Roti Thi.
Sakina Ki Qaza Oss Par Qayamat Dhaya Gayi Logo.
Bandhay Hathoon Pe Ik Beemar Laya Behan Ki Maiyyat.
Yeh Aoulad-e-Nabi Par Kaisi Ghurbat Cha Gayi Logo.
Behan Ki Lash Hathoon Pe Liye Sajjad Kehte They.
Gulsitan-e-Imamat Ki Kali Murjha Gayi Logo.
Padar Ki Khoon Bhari Gardan Pe Bachi Ne Jo Lab Rakhay.
Yeh Manzar Dekh Kar Tu Khud Qaza Ghabra Gayi Logo.
Chacha, Baba, Baradar Sab Ke Sab Tu Karbala Soye.
Sakina Ko Najane Sham Kaisay Baha Gayi Logo.
Likho Shaukat Parho Sarwar Batao Sari Dunya Ko.
Ali Ki Ladli Poti Shahadat Pa Gayi Logo.
