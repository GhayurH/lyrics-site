1
Tang De Ke Tur Gaya Veer Mera Karbala
Khali Ghar Ne Kool Mere Ya Wichora Reh Gaya.
Naal Ohday Jeewanr Diyaan Qasmaan Sariyaan Tut Gayiyaan.
Sangtaan Sohnrey Veer Diyaan Sughra Kolon Chut Gayiyaan.

2
Turday Mehmal Taun Mainu Haye Kaliyaan Lehanra Pe Gaya.
Phar Ke Akbar De Palu Nu Mintaan Kardi Rayi Saan Main.
Palu Akbar Ne Choraya Samajh Gayi Saan Reh Gayi Aan Main.
Le Ke Athro Taang Walay Wekhdi Rayi Qafila.

3
Mehmalaan Nu Turday Welay Kayi Kayi Wari Takdi Rayi.
Janr Walay Tur Gaye Main Ochiyaan Ro Ro Dakdi Rayi.
Jehray Kathiyaan Sunr Subha Nu Sham De Ho Gaye Juda.
Osiyaan Paondi Rayi Main Wekh Ke Sajjad Nu.

4
Ohday Chehray Te Likhi Hoi Dard Di Faryad Nu.
Naal Hanjwaan De Kisay Da Pe Na Jaway Wasta.
Main Qaza Nu Aakhdi Rayi Aye Wichora Sehanr De.
Ik Wari Akbar Bhira Nu Watna Te Aa Lainr De.

5
Fair Bhawein Toor Jawein Sanwaan Da Aye Silsila.
Tur Gaye Akbar Nu Le Ohday Babay De Amar.
Sughra Ohday Faislay Nu Man Taan Gayi Si Par Samar.
Wadah Satwein Wala Ohno Mananaa Okha Pe Gaya.
