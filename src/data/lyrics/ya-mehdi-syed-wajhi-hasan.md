Ya Mehdi Syed Wajhi Hasan Lyrics
Nazim-e-Kainat Aye Maula Sahib-e-Iqtidar Ya Mehdi.
Hum Tu Kya Hain Keh Khud Khuda Ko Bhi Hai Tera Intezar Ya Mehdi.
Hai Jalal-e-Khuda Jalal Tera, Hai Jamal-e-Nabi Jamal Tera.
Haibat e Murtaza Bhi Hai Tujh Mein Shah-e-Duldul Sawar Ya Mehdi.
Wasta Syeda Ka Kar De Karam Tu Yadullah Hai Khuda Ki Qasam.
Teri Qowat Khuda Ki Qowat Hai Sahib-e-Zulfiqar Ya Mehdi.
Tu Hi Qibla Hai Tu Hi Kaba Hai Ba Khuda Tu Haqeeqi Juma Hai.
Sab Ibadaat Ki Sadaein Hain Hai Hamara Qarrar Ya Mehdi.
Karbala Se Sadaein Aati Hain Haal Kya Ho Gaya Madine Ka.
Ab Bhi Be Saya Qabar-e-Zahra Hai Kab Banay Ga Mazar Ya Mehdi.
Tu Dikha Hum Ko Chehra-e-Khaliq Kar De Parchar Phir Fazail Ka.
Deta Hai Mimber-e-Saloni Sada Kar Muqassar Pe Waar Ya Mehdi.
Hum Ko Jaldi Nahi Hai Aye Maula Tu Nahi Aa Raha Hai Marzi Teri.
Haan Magar Teri Rah Takti Hain Aankhein Hain Ashkbar Ya Mehdi.
Muntazir Hai Tera Gharana Bhi Zulm Ka Inteqam Lainay Aa.
Hai Zamana Bohat Kharab Magar Hai Tera Aytbar Ya Mehdi.
Gham Hai Kis Baat Ka Tujhe Yeh Bata Hai Madad Gaar Jab Tera Maula.
Hal Teri Sari Mushkilein Hoon Gi Tu Bhi Dil Se Pukar Ya Mehdi.
Tu Hai Hakim Tu Hai Imam-e-Zamaan Tere Qabzey Mein Hai Yeh Sara Jahan.
Arsh Ko Farsh Karday Farsh Ko Arsh Hai Tujhe Ikhteyar Ya Mehdi.
Hoon Barahim, Esa Our Mosa Ya Hoon Woh Jibreel-o-Mikaeel.
Matami Sab:AIMAAD NAQVI: Bhi Kartay Hain Tujh Se Pyar Ya Mehdi.
