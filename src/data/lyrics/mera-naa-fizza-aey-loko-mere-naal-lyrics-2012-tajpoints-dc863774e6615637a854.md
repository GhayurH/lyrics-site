1
Mera Naa Fizza Aey Loko Mere Naal Hussain Da Jaya.
Assi Wasday Shehar Madine De Sanu Waqat Ne Shaam Wakhaya.
Tatheer Di Chaar Diwari Di Kha Qasma Kendi Paiyaan.
Main Pak Batool De Veray Wich Zainab Nu Turandi Raiyaan.
Jinna Toriya Aye Tusaan Galiyaan Wich Unna Main Nayi Turn Sikhaya.
Ya Chalay Janday Hathaan De Ya Jandi Aye Khud Zahra.
Shabir Te Zarbaan Ne Chaliyaan Ho Qatal Giya Ghar Sara.
Ajay Chalay Vi Na Khatam Hoye Tusaan Putar Hi Maar Mukaya.
Chadraan Da Dukh Bimar Kita Digda Aye Kadi Uthda Aye.
So Saa Lainda Har Moor Utay Fair Kidray Ja Ke Turda Aye.
Tusaan Qadam Qadam Te Abid Nu Haye Itna Khon Rowaya.
Aye Choon Salaan Di Masooma Tanu Ki Khabaraan Aye Ki Aye.
Jinu Aan Tamache Marde oh Shabir Di Qimti Dhi Aye.
Diti Rahib Nu Ulaad Jinay Unay Mantaan Naal Aynu Paya.
 Akbar Hath Kar Ke Ghazi De Sir Wal Fizza Farmaway.
Menu Khalq Wafawaan Naal Kita Zahra Abbas De Sadqay.
Main Sirf Kaneezi Mangi Sii Unay Ghar Di Maa Sadwaya.
