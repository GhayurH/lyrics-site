1
Badan Sy Nok e Naiza Tak Kazmi Brothers
Sar Jo Chey Mah Ke Be Sheer Ka Nezay Pe Charha.
Nook-e-Neza Se Yeh Asghar Ki Sada Aane Lagi.
Badan Se Nook-e-Neza Tak Mera Sar Aa Gaya Amma.

2
Tera Asghar Bhi Akbar Ke Barabar Aa Gaya Amma.
Galay Par Teer Khaye Chain Se Soya Howa Tha Main.
Achanak Qabar Mein Neza Utar Kar Aa Gaya Amma.
Hai Kuch Teer-o-Sina Par Our Kuch Baba Ke Chehray Par.

3
Badan Mein Khon Jitna Tha Woh Bahir Aa Gaya Amma.
Bataein Aap Ne Yeh Raakh Jo Pehlu Mein Bandhi Hai.
Mera Jhola Bhi Kya Sholon Ki Zad Par Aa Gaya Amma.
Sar-e-Bazar Oss Lamhey Bhi Tum Ko Sabar Karna Hai.

4
Mere Sar Ki Taraf Koi Jo Pathar Aa Gaya Amma.
Bhala Tum Meri Masooma Behan Ko Kya Batati Ho.
Sakina Pochti Ho Gi Keh Asghar Aa Gaya Amma.
Mera Khoon Mein Bhara Kurta Liye Yasrab Chali Jana.

5
Samajh Lena Tumhare Sath Main Ghar Aa Gaya Amma.
Baroz-e-Hashar Ahsan Bas Yehi Sunney Ki Hasrat Hai.
Ali Asghar Kahin Mera Sukhan War Aa Gaya Amma.
Poete::Syed Ahsan Zaidi:
