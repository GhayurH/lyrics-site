Abbas Bulata Hai Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics
Dargah-e-Alamdar, Dargah-e-Alamdar.
Ya Baab-e-Hawaij Meri Tauqeer Barha De.
Abbas Katay Hathoon Se Taqdeer Bana De.
Zahra Se Wafadari Dunya Ko Sikhata Hai.
Parcham Ka Pharera Yeh Lehra Ke Batata Hai.
Dargah-e-Abualfazl Al-Abbas Ke Saye Mein.
Tafreeq Nahi Koi Apne Mein Paraye Mein.
Jo Bhi Yahan Aata Hai Khali Nahi Jata Hai.
Be Aas Mareezo Ko Dunya Ke Gareebon Ko.
Maskeno Yateemo Ko Roti Hoi Aankhon Ko.
Har Totay Hoye Dil Ki Yeh Aas Bandhata Hai.
Haider Ke Malangon Ki Iss Dar Pe Qatarein Hain.
Horoon Ki Farishton Ki Iss Dar Pe Qatarein Hain.
Jis Jis Ka Bulawa Hai Dora Chala Aata Hai.
Woh Maula Qadam Ho Ya Shahbaz Ki Nagri Ho.
Matam Jahan Hota Ho Majlis Jahan Hoti Ho.
Deney Ke Liye Pursa Zahra Ko Jo Aata Hai.
Mashhad Ho Najaf Ho Ya Maka Ho Baqiya Ho.
Woh Karb-o-Bala Ho Ya Shahzadi Ka Roza Ho.
Dunya Ka Koi Zair Jab Bhi Yahan Aata Hai.
Be Chain Hai Ghabra Ke Khaimay Mein Tehalta Hai.
Kya Jurm Kiya Aisa Kyun Hathoon Ko Malta Hai.
Aye Hur Teri Qismat Ka Tara Yeh Batata Hai.
Girtay Hoye Parcham Ko Dekha Sheh-e-Wala Ne.
Zainab Se Kaha Ro Kar Abbas Ke Maula Ne.
Hum Jate Hain Darya Par Dil Dobta Jata Hai.
Tum So Gaye Darya Par Dekho Tu Zara Uth Kar.
Shabeer Ko Ghairay Hain Nu Lakh Ka Yeh Lashkar.
Jab Teer Koi Dil Par Aaqa Tera Khata Hai.
 Rehan Ki Sarwar Ki Maula Se Wafaein Hain.
Abbas Ke Rozay Pe Noha Liye Aaye Hain.
Shahzadi Sakina Ka Noha Jo Sunata Hai.
