1
Ro Rahi Hai Aaj Tak Shabbir Khak e Karbala Hasan Sadiq.
Go Mualla Main Hoi Afsoos Tu Zibha Howa.
Jab Chupaya Main Ne Daman Mein Tere Be Sheer Ko.
Zalimo Ne Naizon Se Seena Mera Chalni Kiya.

2
Thartharai Khoob Peeti Our Roi Khoob Thi.
Hasan Ki Bewah Ke Armano Ka Sehra Jab Luta.
Robaro Mere Hoye They Anbiya Par Zulm Bhi.
Lasha-e-Akbar Tarapta Mujh Se Na Dekha Gaya.

3
Bachoon Ko Yeh Aas Thi Keh Chacha Pani Laye Ga.
Pani Ka Na Pohanchna Ghazi Ke Dil Ko Kha Gaya.
Go Main Pardah Ban Gayi Thi Waris-e-Tatheer Ka.
Khak Sar Zainab Mein Ya Rab Waqt Kaisa Aa Gaya.

4
Lasha-e-Aoun-o-Muhammad Par Koi Waris Na Tha.
Sabar-e-Zainab Dekh Kar Munh Ko Kaleja Aa Gaya.
Munkir-e-Sajda-e-Aadam Ho Ke Shaitaan Ho Gaya.
Sajda-e-Shabeer Najfi Azmat-e-Yazdaan Howa.
