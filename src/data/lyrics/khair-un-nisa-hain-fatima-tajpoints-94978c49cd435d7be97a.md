Khair-un-Nisa Hain Fatima Lyrics
Malka-e-Kaun-o-Makaan Khair-Al-Nisa Hai Fatima.
Phir Bhi Tum Yeh Pochtay Phirtay Ho Kya Hai Fatima.
Kyun Kahin Na Keh Muhammad Par Hoi Nazil Hain Aap.
Inna Anzalna Khuda Ne Jab Kaha Hai Fatima.
Tere Naqsh-e-Pa Talak Bhi Rijs Aa Sakta Nahi.
Sajdah Ga Mariyam Ki Tera Naqsh-e-Pa Hai Fatima.
Zindah Hotey Hi Sunaran Ka Pisar Kehne Laga.
Mar Ke Dekho Kis Qadar Mojaz Numa Hai Fatima.
Oss Ke Sajday Syed-e-Sajjad Kartay Hain Wasool.
Naam Sajdoon Mein Tera Jis Ne Liya Hai Fatima.
Anbiya Tak Ki Nazar Oss Ki Jabeen Par Hai Ruki.
Ik Tera Romal Kya Hur Ko Bandha Hai Fatima.
Ghar Bachaye Jo Tera Hum Jantay Hain Hur Hai Woh.
Ghar Jalaye Jo Tera Woh Hurmala Hai Fatima.
Lab Pe Jo Ibn-e- Asad Ke Har Ghari Hai Ya Hussain.
Yeh Tere Bachoon Se Ulfat Ka Sila Hai Fatima.
