1
Gaud Mein Apni Mujhe Phir Se Sula Lo Amma.
Mujh Ko Bachpan Ki Wohi Lori Suna Do Amma.
Dunya Tareek Hoi Jati Hai Ab Waqt Nahi.
Aakhri Chehray Ka Dedar Kara Do Amma.

2
Chatakh Gayi Hai Zabaan Pyas Ki Shiddat Se Meri.
Chand Bondein Mujhe Pani Ki Pila Do Amma.
Rait Ke Chubhne Se Zakhmo Mein Aziyat Hai Bohat.
Apne Romal Se Yeh Gard Hata Do Amma.

3
Ab Tu Hilney Ki Sakkat Bhi Nahi Dam Rukta Hai.
Ab Tu Pathar Nahi Marein Yeh Bata Do Amma.
Main Kaisay Dekhon Ossay Aankhon Mein Hai Khon Bhara.
Khud Hi Ahwal Sakina Ka Suna Do Amma.

4
Ran Mein Na Janein Kahan Rait Pe Bikhrey Hoon Gay.
Bazu Abbas Ke Maqtal Se Utha Lo Amma.
Na Dekh Paein Gi Behnein Yeh Khoon Fishaan Manzar.
Dar-e-Khaima Se Unhein Ja Ke Hata Do Amma.

5
Baad Marne Ke Mere Khaimay Jalein Gay Laeen.
Aag Ke Sholon Se Abid Ko Bacha Lo Amma.
Kab Se Mushtaq Hain Nana Bhi Mere Baba Bhi.
Pas Ab Aata Hoon Main, Unn Ko Bata Do Amma.

6
Sahara Do Keh Bas Ab Aakhri Sajdah Kar Loon.
Shimar Aata Hai Kalejay Ko Sanbhalo Amma.
Tarap Ke Rakh Deiye Zahra Ne Jo Khanjar Ke Talay.
Bolay Shabbir Keh Ab Hath Hata Lo Amma.

7
Na Dekh Paogi Amma Yeh Qayamat Ka Samaa.
Main Zibha Hota Hoon Nazron Ko Jhuka Lo Amma.
Galla Jo Bhai Ka Kattay Hoye Dekha Aye Najam.
 Razi Sada Yehi Gonji Keh Bacha Lo Amma.
