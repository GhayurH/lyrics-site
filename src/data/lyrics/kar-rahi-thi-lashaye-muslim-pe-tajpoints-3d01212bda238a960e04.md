Ya Ali Kofay Mein Phir Aayi Hai Rone Syeda.
Aap Par Jab Ibn-e-Muljim Ne Kiya Sajday Mein Waar.

1
Hath Pehlu Par Rakhe Yaan Ayi Thi Main Pehli Baar.
Aaj Phir Oss Haal Mein Zahra Ko Hai Aana Para.
Kofay Walon Ne Isse Mil Kar Hai Mara Ya Ali.
Lasha-e-Muslim Hai Aisay Parah Parah Ya Ali.

2
Jism Hai Sara Mere Bachey Ka Zakhmo Se Bhara.
Kis Tarha Main Ne Sanbhala Kis Tarha Thama Issay.
Kya Bataon Main Khari Thi Haath Phelaye Hoye.
Dar-Al-Amrah Se Jab Lasha-e-Muslim Gira.

3
Kuch Hi Din Mein Phir Yahan Aaon Gi Main Roti Hoi.
Iss Ke Bachon Par Chale Gi Haris Ki Churi.
Main Sanbhalon Gi Unhein Aakar Ruqiyya Ki Jagah.
Dushmani Aal-e-Nabi Se Iss Tarha Dikhlaein Gaye.

4
Youn Muhammad Our Ibrahim Mare Jaein Gaye.
Naam Ka Bhi Paas Rakhein Gaye Na Kuch Ahl-e-Jafa.
Kar Rahe Hain Zulm Ki Tafseer Yeh Chehron Ke Neil.
Kat Rahi Hai Aaj Jo Dar Dar Ye Olad-e-Aqeel.

5
Hai Yaqeenan Yeh Mere Bachoon Se Ulfat Ki Saza.
Bain They Akbar Zaban-e-Syeda Par Bas Yehi.
Sath Zainab Ke Yahan Phir Mujh Ko Aana Hai Abhi.
Shaam Tak Le Jaye Ga Kofay Se Gham Ka Silsila.
