Rokar Sakina Shaam ke zindaan mein maargee.

Bano ke dilpe goya qiyamaat guzar gayee.

Aal-e-Nabhi pe tooti qayamat thi Shaam meinAbbas ki chahiti Sakina guzargayee.

Shimaar-e-layee ne cheene theh gohar kuch is tarahNahnni khamees khoo mein Sakina ki bhar gayee.

Zainab tadap ke reh gayee poocha Sakina neSar se phupi tumhare rida kyoo utar gayee.

Be sakhta pukara sar-e-shahe KarbalaZainab karo talaash Sakina kidhar gayee.

Zindaa mein jab Sakina ne paya sar-e-HussainMooh par pidar ke moo ko rakha aur margayee.

Ab tou Yazid soyega ratao ko chaen seRoti thi rozo shab jo vo bachchi guzargayee.
