Main Sir Urya Nay Haan Abbas

Main Sir urya nay haan Abbas keeway, Shaam jaawaa gi

Rida day baaj wala da, keeway parda banawa gi

Teray laashay tay royee naee, tay tur paee shaam day paasay

Hoya waada main rowan laee, teri turbat tay aawaangi

Meray babay koo hasrat raee, mera lehja O naee sunayaya

Main wich darbar dus veeran, keeway khutba sunawagi

Meri gardan tau huth meray, jo naee khulday dasa veeran

Shimar day zulm tau kivay, Sakina noo bachawaangi

Ajay taaee shimr day qabzay chay hai kuj karnaee sakdee

jay wapas mil gaee chadar, teri turbat tay paawaangi

zara na fikar kar veeran jay safra wich maikoo milaya

main apnay hissay da paani wee bala koo pilaawaangi

Wafa day Shehnshah ko dhee AAli dee june aadi raee

Tu shaam aawan day Abid koo, main Ghazi bun wikhaawaangi
