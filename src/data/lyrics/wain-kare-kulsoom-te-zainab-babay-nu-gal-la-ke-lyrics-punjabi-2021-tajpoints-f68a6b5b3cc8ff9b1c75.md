Wain Kare Kulsoom Te Zainab Babay Nu Gal La Ke.
Amari Wangoon Babal Saiyaan Chaliya Ayein Ronrey Pa Ke.

1
Jo Masjid Wich Zulm Si Hoya Wekh Ke Arsh Te Rab V Roya.
Kul-e-Eman De Sir Te Zalim Mari Zarb Si Aa Ke.
Ban Malka Tere Sang Aayi Keeti Aye Tazeem Khudai.
Aynj Lagda Aye Main Doji Wari Aanra Aye Rasiyaan Pa Ke.

2
Teri Shakal Da Veer Abbas Aye Umm Ul-Banin Di Aakhri Aas Aye.
Piyo Putraan Nu Ban De Sehrey Shagna Naal Saja Ke.
Charh Payi Khoni Surkh Haneri Aayi Baqiya Choon Amari Meri.
Haye Hasnain Nu Wekh Ke RoWay Sir Wich Mitiyaan Pa Ke.

3
Teri Maut Da Jo Saman Aye Ayho Karbal Da Unwan Aye.
Main Veraan Diyaan Lashaan Chad Ke Janra Aye Sham De Pasay.
Na Amari Na Kool Way Nana Wairi Lok Te Dais Begana.
Ronday Veer Be Warsaan Wangoon Gal Wich Banwaan Pa Ke.

4
Aakhri Welay Kar Ik Wadah Baba Sadqa Maa Zahra Da.
Wekhin Sham-e-Ghareebaan Mere Raat Nu Pehrey Aa Ke.
Tak Zahra Da Ghar Veeran Aye Ronda Nabiyaan Da Sultan Aye.
Aaya Naal Batool De Kofay Ronda Khak Ura Ke.

5
Ghar Sadaat De Phohri Paye Gayi Ummat Khushiyaan De Wich Paye Gayi.
Hasan Hussain Ne Ronday Tur Paye Piyo Di Maiyyat Cha Ke.
Wich Masjid De Khon Da Dulna Nayi Sardar Kaday V Bhulna.
Sajday Taun Pehlaan Qatil Nu Turya Aye Syed Jaga Ke.
