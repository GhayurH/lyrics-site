1
Hussain Razi Rahe Ameer Hasan Aamir.
Haider Ka Mojza Hussain.
Zahra Ka Mah Laqa Hussain.
Mafhoom e Karbala Hussain.
Hai Haq Ka Rehnuma Hussain.
Tafseer-e-Innama Hussain.
Zikar Mein Fikar Mein Rehta Hai Hussain.
Zehan Mein Qalb Mein Cha Gaya Hussain.
Hai Youn Khuda Se Hussain Razi.
Har Ik Raza Se Hussain Razi.
Hamesha Bichta Rahe Ghar Mein Mere Farsh-e-Azza.
Hussain Razi Rahe Bas Hussain Razi Rahe.
Zamana Rothay Ya Phir Chotay Koi Bhi Rishta.
Hussain Razi Rahe Bas Hussain Razi Rahe.
Yeh Majlisein Yeh Azakhaney Our Yeh Farsh-e-Azza.
Sabeelein Our Tabaruk Ko Bantne Ka Maza.
Hussain Ke Hi Liye Sab Hai Our Yeh Mansha.
Gaye Woh Ran Ki Taraf Jab Hussain Ne Chaha.
Sipahi Haq Ke Samajhtay They Falsafa Rab Ka.
Wohi Woh Kartay Rahe Jo Hussain Kehta Raha.
Yeh Apne Bachoon Ko Maon Ne Tarbiyaat Di Hai.
Gham-e-Hussain Manao Yeh Pursa Dari Hai.
Geo Hussain Ke Gham Mein Yeh Baap Ne Bhi Kaha.
Subhan Rabi Al Ala Rasool Parhtay Rahe.
Keh Unn Ki Pusht Pe Jab Tak Hussain Baithay Rahe.
Bina Hussain Ke Kamil Nahi Koi Sajdah.
Hussain Dil Hai Hussain Jaan Hai.
Dilon Ki Dharkan Mein Bhi Rawaan Hai.
Tapish Mein Gham Ki Yeh Saibaan Hai.
Hussain Tauheed Ki Zabaan Hai.
Khuda Ka Kalma Jahan Jahan Hai.
Hussain Mera Wahan Wahan Hai.
Parho Zyarat-e-Maula Khuda Ko Razi Karo.
Suno Hussain Suno Gar Parho Hussain Parho.
Azza Ke Farsh Pe Baitho Khuda Se Mango Dua.
Kahin Pe Bhai Ke Bazu Kahin Behan Ke Pisar.
Khuda Ki Rah Mein Di Hai Jawani e Akbar.
Jis Imtehan Mein Lati Rahi Khuda Ki Raza.
Yehi Nijat Ka Zariya Banay Ga Mehshar Mein.
Sar Apna Fakhar Se Oncha Rahe Ga Mehshar Mein.
Ab Iss Khiyal Se Barh Kar Bhi Our Kya Hoga.
Hussain Tu Jo Nahi Hai Tu Ghar Nahi Kuch Bhi.
Katay Jo Ishq Mein Tere Tu Sar Nahi Kuch Bhi.
Habib Ibn-e-Mazahir Ki Dosti Ne Kaha.
Kiya Hussain Ne Aamir Tujhe Safeer-e-Azza.
Atta Se Unn Ki Main Shayar-e-Ahl-e-Bait Howa.
Khuda Se Paon Shanawar Yeh Khidmaton Ka Sila.
