1
Allah Ke Shahkar Ka Hai Youm-e-WIladat
Abbas Alamdar Ka Hai Youm-e-Wiladat
Sarkar Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Jis Ke Hoon Mamnoon
Oss Shah-e-Wafadar Ka Hai Youm-e-Wiladat

2
Hai Wosat-e-Sad Fikar-o-Nazar Midhat-e-Abbas
Hai Zamzam-e-Lal-o-Gohar Midhat-e-Abbas
Sajday Mein Hain Qirtas-o-Qalam Bhi Pay Tazeem
Hai Mokhzin Fun Ilm-o-Hunar Midhat-e-Abbas

3
Hai Tazkara-e-Ghazai Fasahat Ka Karishma
Hai Tarz Sukhan Husan-e-Blagat Ka Karishma
Likhta Hoon Jo Is Shan Se Ashar Fazilat
Hai Maula Alamdar Se Nisbat Ka Karishma

4
Jo Ahl-e-Wafa Dunya Main Hai Oss Ka Hai Yeh Peer
Hai Iss Ki Aqeedat Bhi Zamane Mein Huma Geer
Kehti Hai Ossay Baab-e-Hawaij Sabhi Dunya
Pal Bhar Mein Badal Deta Hai Sail Ki Yeh Taqdeer

5
Abbas Alam Dari-e-Sarwar Ka Nishaan Hai
Iss Zaat Pe Qurban Shujat Ka Jahan Hai
Jo Qabal Shahadat Hi Ke Kehla-e-Behishti
Tareekh-e-Jahan Mein Yehi Bas Aik Jawaan Hai

6
Hai Oss Ka Alam Showkat-o-Satoot Ki Nishani
Tableeg-e-Nabowat Ki Risalat Ki Nishani
Hai Iss Ke Phareray Ki Jhalak Azmat-e-Islaam
Panjay Ka Hai Har Aks Karamat Ki Nishani

7
Uthta Hai Basad Jah-o-Hasham Parcham-e-Abbas
Hai Haq-o-Sadaqat Ka Alam Parcham-e-Abbas
Saye Mein Issi Ke Hain Amaan Dunya-o-Deen Ko
Ta Hashar Yeh Lehraein Ge Hum Parcham-e-Abbas
