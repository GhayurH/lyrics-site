Hussain Hussain Imam Hussain (or Hussain Hussain Hussain Hussain)

Shaheed-e-Karbala Hussain,
Aseer-e-Ashqiya Hussain
Bina-Ay La Illah Hussain,
Imam-e-Dau Jahan Hussain,
Ghareeb-o-Benava Hussain,
Zahra Ka Ladla Hussain,
Ali Ka Ladla Hussain

Aj Chehlum Tenda Guzargaya
Aj Rhondiye Ma Zahra Hussain

Haye Rang Li Jawani Akbar Di
Jedi Paal Vich Hui Ye Fana Hussain

Tera Sar Kateya Mera Ghar Luteya
Dovay Ujray Behn Bhira Hussain

Kitha Bachri Mushkil Kusha Da Hay
Aa Behn Di Qaid Chura Hussain

Ali Abid Zevar Paye Nay
Aa Shaam Di Rah Dikhla Hussain

Batil Diyan Jhirkan Pehgaye Ay
Masooma Khaak Tay Behgaye Ay
Aa Dhi Kun Gal Nal Laa Hussain
