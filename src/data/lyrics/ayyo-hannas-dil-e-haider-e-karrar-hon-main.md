Ayyo Hannas Dil-e-Haider-e-Karrar Huṅ Main

1
Ayyo Hannas Dil-e-Haider-e-Karrar Hoon Main
Sab Mere Samne Majboor Hain Mukhtar Hoon Main
Sorma Saf Shikan-o-Ghazi-o-Jarrar Hoon Main
Alam-e-Zar Se Kissi Foaj Ka Salar Hoon Main
Asadullah Ka Akas Mujhe Kehte Hain
Shair Hoon Iss Liye Abbas Mujhe Kehte Hain

2
Aarzu Hai Tu Koi Harf-e-Dua Hai Lekin
Jo Bhi Moti Hai Woh Sach Hai Khara Hai Lekin
Koi Mosa Koi Haron e Wafa Hai Lekin
Kanz-e-Mabood Nageno Se Bhara Hai Lekin
Kul-e-Eman-e-Imam-e-Azali Ne Manga
Main Akela Hoon Jissay Rab Se Ali Ne Manga

3
Al-Garz Behar-e-Manajat Uthay Dast-e-Khuda
Saj Gayi Boltay Quran Ke Honton Pe Dua
Rukh-e-Tareekh-e-Okhowat Pe Charha Rang-e-Wafa
Bait-e-Molood-e-Haram Dekh Ke Qudrat Ne Kaha
Ab Gharor-e-Shab-e-Tareek Zara Maand Bhi Ho
Barah Soraj Hain Jab Iss Ghar Mein Tu Ik Chand Bhi Ho

4
Rah Mushkil Ho Tu Aasan Bana Deta Hoon
Khar Zaron Ko Gulistan Bana Deta Hoon
Harf-e-Ruswa Ho Tu Zeeshan Bana Deta Hoon
Main Bhikari Ko Bhi Sultan Bana Deta Hoon
Apne Aaqa Ke Gulistan Ka Gul-e-Taza Hoon
Woh Agar Shehar-e-Sakhawat Hain Main Darwaza Hoon

5
Kya Kaha Shah-e-Abuzar Se Miley Ga Ke Nahi
Aab Kozay Ko Samandar Se Miley Ga Ke Nahi
Noor Kuch Mehar-e-Munawar Se Miley Ga Ke Nahi
Sochtay Ho Keh Mere Ghar Se Miley Ga Ke Nahi
Shab Ke Phanday Mein Gala Daal Diya Jhool Gaye
Kya Mera Baab-e-Hawaij Ka Laqab Bhool Gaye

6
Koi Zar Koi Gohar Koi Rida Mangta Hai
Yeh Zamana Mujhe De De Ke Sada Mangta Hai
Youn Tu Har Lehja-e-Beemar Shifa Mangta Hai
Aa Ke Kafir Koi Jab Mujh Se Dua Mangta Hai
Paish-e-Rab Kalma-e-Tauheed Parha Deta Hoon
Main Duaon Ko Musalman Bana Deta Hoon

7
Hai Shajar Aik Muradoon Ke Samar Barah Hain
Hai Sadaf Aik Taharat Ke Gohar Barah Hain
Aasmaan Aik Imamat Ke Qamar Barah Hain
Aik Hai Ahl-e-Nazar Noor-e-Nazar Barah Hain
Barah Anwar-e-Imamat Ka Jo Ik Ghalib Hai
Main Bhi Pota Hoon Ossi Ka Jo Abu Talib Hai

8
Chotay Bhai Ko Larai Ki Ijazat De Do
Taig-e-Bismil Ko Kalai Ki Ijazat De Do
Foaj-e-Batil Ki Safai Ki Ijazat De Do
Aaj Ke Din Tu Larai Ki Ijazat De Do
Hasrat-e-Taig Pe Kha Kha Ke Taras Katay Hain
Issi Din Ke Liye Batees Baras Katay Hain

9
Sahib-e-Mashk-o-Alam Khaimay Se Be Taig Chala
Phir Bhi Jibreel Ne Shehpar Ke Liye Wird Parha
Dar Se Khamosh Howa Our Bhi Kuch Dil Dharka
Jab Mili Shair Ki Aahat Tu Pukara Darya
Rah Takta Tha Main Jis Ki Wohi Salik Aaya
Gasibo Ghaat Se Bhago Mera Malik Aaya

10
Chup Hoi Nehar Tu Abbas Ki Gonji Aawaz
Banda e Khas Ossi Ka Hoon Jo Hai Banda Nawaz
Apne Hi Khoon Se Wozu Kar Ke Basad Ijz-o-Niyaz
Mujh Ko Parhna Hai Sakina Ki Muhabbat Ki Namaz
Meri Janib Se Shahadat Ko Zabaan De Di Hai
Ali Akbar Ne Saweray Hi Azaan De Di Hai

11
Aasmaan Tak Jo Gayi Shair-e-Nayastan Ki Pukar
Malak Al-Maut Ne Ghabra Ke Kaha Jee Sarkar
Lehja-e-Ghaiz Mein Bola Yeh Hussaini Jarrar
Sun Rahe Ho Jo Tarai Pe Hai Shor-e-Ashrar
Dushmani Mein Yeh Kayi Roz Se Bal Khatay Hain
Jao Darya Pe Khabar Kar Do Keh Hum Aate Hain

12
Pehrey Daroon Ki Sada Thi Keh Tarai Choro
Apne Bas Mein Nahi Hargiz Yeh Larai Choro
Ab Yeh Faryad Yeh Giriya Yeh Duhai Choro
Waqt Hai Bhag Nikalne Ka Kalai Choro
Ghaiz Mein Aa Ke Jo Talwar Chala Deta Hai
Yeh Parindon Ki Tarha Sar Ko Ura Deta Hai

13
Shair Bola Keh Halakat Ke Nishaan Tak Bhago
Tum Khiyalon Ke Musafir Ho Gumaan Tak Bhago
Bhag Saktay Ho Jahan Tak Bhi Wahan Tak Bhago
Sham Se Aaye Ho Hinda Ke Makaan Tak Bhago
Maut Ki Rah-o-Rawish Khainch Ke Le Aayi Hai
Tum Ko Nezay Ki Kashish Khainch Ke Le Aayi Hai

14
Gham-e-Sehyaan Ke Dilasay Ho Kahan Jate Ho
Jane Kitno Ki Khata Se Ho Kahan Jate Ho
Tum Tu Hinda Ke Nawasay Ho Kahan Jate Ho
Badar Ke Roz Se Pyasay Ho Kahan Jate Ho
Kasa e Lafz-e-Halakat Se Mani Pe Lo
Khoon Ke Pyaso Meri Talwar Se Pani Pe Lo

15
Shimar Buzdil Se Pahey Joor Manga Our Manga
Dair Kis Baat Ki Fill Foor Manga Our Manga
Apne Anjam Pe Kar Ghoor Manga Our Manga
Mujh Se Larna Hai Tu Foaj Our Manga Our Manga
Ashal-e-Zeerak-o-Hoshiyar Kuchal Dalay Ga
Itna Lashkar Mera Rahwar Kuchal Dalay Ga

16
Hai Mere Waar Ki Raftar Hawa Ko Maloom
Khadma Taig-e-Ali Hai Mere Hamlay Makhdoom
Sabhi Jurat Ki Safon Mein Hai Azzal Se Meri Dhoom
Jism Ajab Hone Lagin Gay Abhi Jaan Se Mehroom
Har Shujat Ke Dhani Ibn-e-Wali Aaye Hain
Tum Yeh Kehte Hoye Bhago Gay Ali Aaye Hain

17
Apne Dil Mein Hai Jawaan Fatah-e-Khaybar Ki Umang
Sang Ko Moom Karein Hum Tu Kabhi Moom Ko Sang
Zindagi Lamhon Mein Be Deno Pe Ho Jaye Gi Tang
Bhool Jao Gay Abhi Rustam-o-Sohrab Ki Jang
Hum Jo Talwar Ke Qabzey Mein Kalai Dein Gay
Unn Ke Ajdad Bhi Qabron Se Duhai Dein Gay
