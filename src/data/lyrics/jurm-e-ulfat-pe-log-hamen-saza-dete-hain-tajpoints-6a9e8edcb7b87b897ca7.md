Jurm-e-Ulfat Pe Log Hamen Saza Dete Hain.
Kaise Nadaan Hain Sholon Ko Hawa Dete Hain.

1
Hum Se Dewane Kahin Tark-e-Wafa Karte Hain.
Jaan Jaye Keh Rahe Baat Nibha Dete Hain.
Aap Dolat Ke Tarazo Mein Dilon Ko Tolein.
Hum Mohabbat Se Mohabbat Ka Sila Dete Hain.

2
Takht Kya Cheez Hain Our Laal-o-Jawahir Kya Hain.
Ishq Wale Tu Khudai Bhi Luta Dete Hain.
Hum Ne Dil De Bhi Diya Ahad-e-Wafa Le Bhi Liya.
Aap Ab Shoq Se De Lein Jo Saza Dete Hain.
