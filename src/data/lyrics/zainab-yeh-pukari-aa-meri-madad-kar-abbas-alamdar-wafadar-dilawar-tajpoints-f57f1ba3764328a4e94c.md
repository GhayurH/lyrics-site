Zainab Yeh Pukari Aa Meri Madad Kar Abbas Alamdar Wafadar Dilawar.
Hai Sham Mein Tanha Behna Teri Muztar Abbas Alamdar Wafadar Dilawar.

1
Aye Bhaiyya Sunu Mujh Pe Qayamat Guzaar Gayi.
Na Sochi Thi Kabhi Jo Woh Saat Guzar Gayi.
Imdad Ko Aao Aye Mere Baradar.
Kaise Bataon Tum Ko Sakina Pe Jo Guzari.

2
Keh Mangtay They Ossko Kaneezi Mein Yeh Shaqi.
Be-Haal Hai Bachi Haan Sun Ke Yeh Khabar.
Be-Parda Mujhe Dekh Ke Ghazi Mere Ghamkhuwar.
Naizay Se Gir RahaHai Tera Sar Jo Bar Bar.

3
Chadar Tu Dila Dey Aye Sani-e-Haider.
Baba Ke Samney Bhi Kabhi Main Nahi Gayi.
Yeh Teri Behan Aaj Hai Darbar Mein Khari.
Athara Azanein Suni Main Ne Yahan Par.

4
Main Ne HI Tu Ghazi Tujhe Chalna Tha Sikhaya.
Haan Meri Hi Maa Ne Tera Kurta Tha Banaya.
Kuch Yaad Karo Tu Bachpan Ke Woh Manzar.
Mushkil Kusha Hain Baba Mere Sab Ko Hai Khabar.

5
Zainab Ne Pukara Hai Tujhe Har Ghari Magar.
Aye Shair-e-Ali Aa Ban Kar Meri Chadar.
Jab Ghairay Howay They Humein Aye Bhai Sitamgaar.
Tauq-o-Rasan Mein Jakray Gaye Abid-e-Beemar.

6
Durron Ki Aziyyat Sehtey Rahe Muztar.
 Afraz Riha Ho Ke Jab Aayi Woh Karbala.
Abbas Ki Turbat Pe Yeh Karti Rahi Noha.
Uth Dekh Yeh Behna Aayi Hai Khulay Sar.
