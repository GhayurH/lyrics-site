1
Yeh Hum Ne Dekha Tha Khowab Pyare Nadi Kinare.
Zameen Pe Utre The Do Sitare Nadi Kinare.
Na Jane Guzre Hain Kitne Sawan Iss Aarzo Mein.
Kabhi Tu Koi Humein Pukare Nadi Kinare.

2
Wohi Shajar Hain Wohi Hain Saye Magar Paraye.
Hain Apni Basti Ke Rang Sare Nadi Kinare.
Utar Ke Mehtab Ban Giya Aina Kissi Ka.
Kissi Ne Baal Apne Youn Sanware Nadi Kinare.

3
Kabhi Idhar Se Guzar Ke Dekho Tu Yaad Aein.
Wo Qoal Apne Wachan Tumhare Nadi Kinare.
Kati Hai Ik Umar Humnasheen Ke Begair Apni.
Koi Tu Apni Tarha Guzare Nadi Kinare.

4
Duaein Deti Hain Bansuri Ki Sadaein Shab Ko.
Kabhi Na Sokhein Yeh sabz Chare Nadi Kinare.
Tumhein Na Dekha Tu Raigaan Raigaan Lage Hain.
Sharab Shabnam Shafaq Sharare Nadi Kinare.

5
Tumhein Na Paya Tu Mauj Dar Mauj Bat Gay Hain.
Yeh Shart Hum Iss Tarha Se Hare Nadi Kinare.
Yeh Ghar Ki Tanhaiyaan Tu Mohsin Sada Rahein Gi.
Chalo Sahar Ki Hawa Pukare Nadi Kinare.
