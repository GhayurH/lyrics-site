Amma eyo bazaaray.
Jis kitah eh Ali Abid nu,  Dadah beemare

1
1. Tu waal waal chadaraan maangdi ray,
Aithay burkay lok luttayndeh hun
Naye ma di val lukah sakdah
Abid lah challray

2
2. Mei jai jai jah to langdah hah,
Saab ppochday nah Zainab kairee ai
Teray paardey keetay nay disdi
Koyi dewaare

3
3. Koyi chahbaq maar turanday ey,
Koyi pathara naal maranda ey
Hoon Abid shear abur karay
Kenday saharey

4
4. Meri aakhiyaan saab khuch bhool vayseen,
Tera Shaam vitch toornah lah bhool si
Mera jigaar teh gharaat kitah han
Dardaan deh war ay

5
5. Tu malikah shaarm haydi ay,
Aithay khul mahole sharabi ey
Teoo vaikaan kaankaar jaamti gay
Badd kaar saray

6
6. Ay Shaam hay saara chaan bachra,
Mei dhi Haider khaaraal diyaan
Noushadd bazaar abur kitay
Zainab tayray
