Biyan Karta Hoon Darbar-e-Sham Ka Manzar.
Jahan They Saat So Kursi Nasheen Fitna Gar.
Yazid Jhoom Raha Tha Sharab Pe Pe Kar.
Our Aisi Bazm Mein Afsoos Aal-e-Peygamber.
Tha Unn Ke Samne Ik Bad Kalam Mimber Par.
Biyan Karta Tha Jhotay Yazid Ke Johar.
Jab Oss Ne Khatam Ki Taqreer Bad Kalami Par.
Khateeb-e-Jehal Se Bolay Yeh Abid-e-Muztar.
Keh Tu Ne Khush Kiya Makhlooq Ko Zaror Magar.
Khareeda Qehar-e-Khuda Tu Ne Mukhtasir Zar Par.
Aray Jahan Ho Zikar-e-Bad Atwar Woh Mimber Kab Hai.
Lakriyon Ka Hai Woh Anbar Woh Mimber Kab Hai.
Kaha Yazid Se Kuch Main Kahon Ijazat De.
Yazid Chup Tha Tu Darbari Sare Kehne Lagay.
Yazid De De Ijazat Issay Bhi Kehne De.
Yeh Kya Kahey Ga Larazta Hai Jo Naqahat Se.
Sanbhalay Tauq-o-Rasan Shan-e-Kirdgar Aayi.
Imam Aaye Ke Mimber Pe Zulfiqar Aayi.

Ya Hussain, Ya Hussain.
Ana Ibn-e-Maka-o-Mina, Ana Ibn-e-Zam Zam-o-Safa, Ana Ibn-e-Ali-o-Fatima.

1
Jis Ka Mimber Tha Wohi Barsar-e-Mimber Aaya.
Woh Akela Tha Magar Sorat-e-Lashkar Aaya.
Lab Kusha Sorat-e-Quran Woh Haider Aaya.
Jab Woh Khamosh Zabaan Barsar-e-Darbar Chali.
Karbala Mein Na Chali Thi Jo Woh Talwar Chali.

2
Lehja-e-Noor Mein Maula Ne Kaha Bismillah.
Laiq-e-Hamd-o-Sana Hai Tu Wohi Aik Khuda.
Jis Ne Hum Aal-e-Muhammad Ko Hai Takhleeq Kiya.
Jis Ko Maloom Hai Maloom Hai Mera Shajrah.
Jo Nahi Janta Woh Sun Le Taruf Mera.

3
Hum Ko ALLAH Ne Che Khobiyaan Bakhshein Ba Khuda.
Ilm Our Hilm Diya Our Dil-e-Momin Mein Rakha.
Hai Samaat-o-Fasahat-o-Shujat Wirsa.
Kam Na Thi Khobiyaan Che Jo Keh Yeh Izzat Ke Liye.
Sifatein Sath Milein Our Fazeelat Ke Liye.

4
Hum Mein Awwal Jo They Woh Ahmad-e-Mukhtar Hoye.
Koi Hamza Tu Koi Jafar-e-Tayyar Hoye.
Hum Hi Haider Hoye Abbas-e-Alamdar Hoye.
Hum Ko Mairaj-e-Risalat Bhi Imamat Bhi Mili.
Hum Ko Mehrab-e-Ibadat Mein Shahadat Bhi Mili.

5
Oss Ka Farzand Hoon Seh Roz Raha Jo Pyasa.
Darmiyaan Zeen-o-Zameen Jis Ka Raha Tha Lasha.
Jis Ke Sajday Ke Sabab Khak Bani Khak-e-Shifa.
Khak Our Khon Mein Doba Tha Amama Jis Ka.
Som-e-Aspaan Talay Ronda Gaya Lasha Jis Ka.

6
Ahl-e-Insaf Ab Insaf Se Itna Keh Dein.
Jis Ki Chokhat Pe Salami Ko Farishtay Utrein.
Nook-e-Naiza Se Laeen Unn Ki Ridaein Lotein.
Tum Ne Khaimay Nahi ALLAH Ka Ghar Khak Kiya.
Kalma Parh Parh Ke Muhammad Ka Jigar Chak Kiya.

7
Jis Ke Parday Ke Liye Aayat-e-Tatheer Aaye.
Sang Barsatay Hoye Unn Pe Yahan Tak Laye.
Haif Hai Roh-e-Muhammad Se Na Tum Sharmaye.
Hum Ne Paband-e-Rasan Ho Ke Tujhe Mara Hai.
Ibn-e-Marjana Tu Jeeta Tu Nahi Hara Hai.

8
Baat Jab Khul Gayi Sadaat-e-Muhammad Hain Yahan.
Ro Diye Ahl-e-Jafa Ubhra Bagawat Ka Samaa.
Our Ossi Shoor Mein Hakim Ne Diya Hukam-e-Azaan.
Saat Afrad Jo Yak Musht Azaan Deney Lagay.
Haq Mein Sajjad Ke Darbari Biyaan Deney Lagay.

9
Jab Moazzin Ne Kiya Kalma-e-Takbeer Ada.
Bolay Sajjad Bazurgi Hai Ossi Ko Zaiba.
Jaisay Hi Naam Muhammad Ka Moazzin Ne Liya.
Mur Ke Hakim Se Kaha Tere Iraday Bad Hain.
Yeh Muhammad Hain Tumhare Keh Hamarey Jad Hain.

10
Aib Kyun Apne Chupata Hai Azanein De Kar.
They Agar Tere Muhammad Tu Sitam Kyun Hum Par.
Daal Iss Tauq Pe Zanjeer Pe Ik Bar Nazar.
Zakhm Jo Tu Ne Diye Sab Dil-e-Beemar Mein Hain.
Betiyaan Hain Yeh Muhammad Ki Jo Darbar Mein Hain.

11
Tu Tu Kehta Hai Keh Hai Dhong Naooz Billah.
Na Payamber Na Wahi Aayi Na Quraan Utra.
Phir Azanein Bhi Tu Darbar Mein Hai Dilwata.
Le Munafiq Tere Chehray Se Utarti Hai Naqab.
Jis Ka Munkir Hai Woh Quran Tera Le Ga Hisab.

12
Iss Shaqawat Pe Bhi Hum Harey Nahi Jeet Gaye.
Naam Azano Mein Tumhare Nahi Hum Jeet Gaye.
Kya Yeh Qudrat Ke Isharey Nahi Hum Jeet Gaye.
Yak Bayak Fath-e-Mubeen Sab Ko Nazar Aane Lagi.
Marhaba Syed-e-Sajjad Sada Chanay Lagi.

13
Bolay Phir Abid-e-Beemar Yeh Izzat Dekhi.
Dekhi Yeh Takht-e-Sitamgar Yeh Izzat Dekhi.
Phir Gaya Tujh Se Yeh Darbar Yeh Izzat Dekhi.
Jis Ka Hami Ho Khuda Kaun Ossay Zillat De.
Izatein Oss Ki Hain ALLAH Jisay Izzat De.

14
Utray Mimber Se Tu Baba Ke Katay Sar Ne Kaha.
Marhaba Aye Mere Sajjad Ghareeb-e-Zahra.
Mojza Sarwar-o-Rehan Falak Ne Dekha.
Keh Khutba-e-Zainab-o-Sajjad Ne Woh Kam Kiya.
Hashar Tak Zikar-e-Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Aam Kiya.
