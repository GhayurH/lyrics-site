1
Al Madad Mustafa Naat Mohsin Naqvi.
Jal Rahe Hain Badan Dard Ki Dhop Mein.
Zindagi Dhal Gayi Zakhm Ke Roop Mein.
Dil Mein Kuhram Hai, Teergi Aam Hai.
Ik Nigah-e-Karam Aye Habib-e-Khuda.
Har Nafas Khoon Ugalney Laga Hai Bashar.
Ab Tu Mitnay Laga Farq-e-Sham-o-Sahar.
Aankh Majboor Hai, Rahguzar Door Hai.
Be Khabar Hai Nazar, Be Asar Hai Dua.
Jaur e Fasl-e-Khizaan Hai Chaman Ta Chaman.
Zair-e-Dast-e-Ajal, Zindagi Ki Kiran.
Az Karan Ta Karaan, Bas Dhuwaan Hi Dhuwaan.
Uz Ufaq Ta Ufaq Ranj-o-Gham Ki Ghata.
Loag Youn Mehw Hain Fikar-e-Dastar Mein.
Jaisay Khami Na Koi Kirdar Mein.
Aasmaan Zard Hai, Gard Hi Gard Hai.
Aadmiyat Hai Masroof-e-Aah-o-Boka.
Aman e Insaniyat Phir Se Mafqood Hai.
Fikar Ka Aaina Zang Aalod Hai.
Jism Se Rooh Tak, Same-o-Zar Ki Dhanak.
Chak Dar Chak Hai Ahl-e-Dil Ki Qaba.
Phir Se Oham Dil Ko Hain Ghairay Hoye.
Shehar Walon Ke Jangal Baseray Hoye.
Tere Dar Youzah Gar, Dar Badar, Dar Badar.
Kaun Zindah Karey Rasam-e-Jood-o-Atta?
Kafiron Ka Sitam Phir Tere Deen Par?
Zulm Ke Saye Arz-e-Falasteen Par.
Sar Zameen-e-Ajam, Waqf e Ranj-o-Alam.
Khon Se Gulbadan Khita e Nainwa?.
Khuwab-e-Manzil Mein Kyun Qafilay So Gaye?
Tere Miqdad-o-Meesum Kahan Kho Gaye.
Kya Hoye Woh Jari, Faqar Ke Johari.
Muzmahil Hain Rutein, Matami Hai Fiza.
Phir Gudaz e Abuzar Atta Kar Humein.
Misal-e-Salman Sholah Nawa Kar Humein.
Dard Ki Raat Mein, Gham Ki Barsat Mein.
Hum Faqeeron Ko Bhi Muskurana Sikha.
Tu Hai Sultan-e-Jageer-e-Shams-o-Qamar.
Tu Hai Shehzada e Wosat-e-Behar-o-Bar.
Aye Hakeem e Arab, Tu Hai Quraan Ba Lab.
Maqsad-e-Amar-e-Kun, Waris-e-Hal Atta.
