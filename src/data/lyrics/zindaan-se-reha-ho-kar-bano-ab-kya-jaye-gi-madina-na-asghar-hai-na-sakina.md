Zindaan Se Reha Ho Kar Bano Ab Kya Jaye Gi Madina Na Asghar Hai Na Sakina
Bibi Ke Liye Kitna Mushkil Iss Hal Mein Ho Ga Jeena Na Asghar Hai Na Sakina
Khali Jhola Shehzaday Ka Our Khon Mein Tar Ik Bali
Jab Dekhe Gi Ghar Mein Aa Kar Zindan Se Aane Wali
Tukray Tukray Ho Jaye Ga Be-Khanjar Oss Ka Seena
Ik Ki Turbat Tareeki Mein Our Dhop Mein Ik Ka Lasha
Woh Bhi Pyasi Dunya Se Gayi Our Mar Gaya Woh Pyasa
Kis Ko Bali Pehnaye Ab Our Kis Ko Jhulaye Jhola
Jab Khailtay Bache Dekhe Gi Kya Guzre Gi Bao Par
Yaad Aaein Gaye Jab Apne Bache Chal Jaye Ga Dil Par Khanjar
Jab Sath Nahi Dein Gi Sansein Kaise Woh Rahe Gi Zinda
Ab Iss Se Bara Kya Ho Ga Sitam Aye Chasham-e-Falak Tu Batla
Aulad Se Ronaq Hoti Hai Uthta Hai Jo Koi Lasha
Lekin Bano Ki Maiyyat Par Ab Kon Hai Rone Wala
Jo Baat Kahi Thi Bachon Se Oss Ne Ta Umar Nibhai
Na Pani Piya Thanda Oss Ne Na Khud Saye Mein Aayi
Bas Dhop Mein Bethi Sham-o-Sahar Kehti Hi Rahi Yeh Hazeena
Lori Ki Sada Noha Ban Kar Zindan Se Ghar Tak Aayi
Maa Se Sughra Yeh Pochti Hai Yeh Kaisi Hai Tanhai
Ab Maa Ne Agar Sach Bool Diya Tu Mar Jaye Gi Sughra
Nohay Mein Kahan Tak :MIR HASAN, REHAN: Woh Manzar Likhe
Aawaz Teri Nohay Mein Dhali Aati Hai Sada Matam Se
Yeh Keh Kar Aye Pursadaro Zahra Ko Pursa Dena
