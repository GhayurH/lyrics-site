Hai Wafaon Ka Hawala Tere Bazo Abbas.
Aasra Dast-e-Khuda Ka Tere Bazo Abbas.

1
Qasim-o-Asghar-e-Be-Sheer Sakina Akbar.
Aise Masoomo Ka Jhola Tere Bazo Abbas.
Dekh Kar Aoun-o-Muhammad Ko Wafa Kehti Hai.
Pisar-e-Sani-e-Zahra Tere Bazo Abbas.

2
Zulfiqar-e-Asadullah Banane Ke Liye.
Kibriya Dekh Raha Tha Tere Bazo Abbas.
Hamza-o-Jafar-o-Haider Abu Talib Hasnain.
Sab Ki Qowat Ka Khulasa Tere Bazo Abbas.

3
Dosri Taig-e-Ali AB Nahi Utray Gi Kabhi.
Khalq Hoon Gaye Na Dobara Tere Bazo Abbas.
Parda-e-Ghaib Se Aa Jaye Takallum Qayam.
Abhi Kar Dein Jo Ishara Tere Bazo Abbas.
