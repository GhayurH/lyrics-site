1
Muslim Ke Lal Khon Mein Dobay Lab-e-Furat.
Be Sar Badan Hain Khak Pe Haye Lab-e-Furat.
Larzah Hai Aasmaan Pe Tarapney Lagi Zameen.
Kehte Hain Ashkbar Yeh Sab Arsh Ke Makeen.
Ya Rab Kabhi Na Youn Koi Tarpay Lab-e-Furat.
Bachoon Se Jab Laeen Ne Suna Tha Madad Karo.
Mushkil Bari Hai Aao Na Baba Madad Karo.
Marey Shaqi Ne Rukh Pe Tamachay Lab-e-Furat.
Hathoon Mein Rasi Bandh Ke Khaincha Gaya Unhein.
Mara Gaya Tu Jan Se Mara Gaya Unhein.
Jo Kar Rahe They Shukar Ke Sajday Lab-e-Furat.
Yeh Bhi Sitam Howa Hai Tarai Ke Samne.
Mara Gaya Hai Bhai Ko Bhai Ke Samne.
Bhai Ka Dil Na Youn Kabhi Larzay Lab-e-Furat.
Zalim Ne Phir Se Taig Sitam Ki Chalai Hai.
Ab Iss Ne Chotay Bhai Ki Gardan Aurai Hai.
Ab Tak Hain Jis Ke Khon Ke Cheentay Lab-e-Furat.
Kat Kar Girey Zameen Pe Bachoon Ke Tan Se Sar.
Aati Sada Saron Se Keh Jaan Waar Di Magar.
Hum Be Khata Hain Tere Kinaray Lab-e-Furat.
 Saaib, Zia Parho Yeh Qayamat Ka Marsiya.
Muslim Ke Do Pisar Ki Ghareebi Ke Haal Ka.
Liptay Hain Jin Ke Aaj Bhi Lashay Lab-e-Furat.
