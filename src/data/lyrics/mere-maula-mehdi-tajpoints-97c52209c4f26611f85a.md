1
Jan Mushkil Mein Ghiri Hai Mere Maula Sab Ki.
Ab Tu Aa Jao Khudara Mere Maula Mehdi.
Intezar Aap Ka Maula Mere Jahan Ko Hai.
Mushkilein Jeene Mein Har Sahib-e-Eman Ko Hai.

2
Ab Mosafay Se Bhi Khatrah Yahan Pe Jan Ko Hai.
Aap Ki Maula Zarorat Yahan Insan Ko Hai.
Sath Abbas Ke Talwar-e-Ali Bhi Lao.
Pardah-e-Gaib Se Bahir Mere Maula Aao.

3
Maut Kya Cheez Hai Aye Maula Aani Jani Hai.
Ho Zahoor Aap Ka Jaldi Yeh Dua Mangi Hai.
Khana-e-Kaba Bhi Ab Hone Laga Khali Hai.
Aap Ke Aane Ki Maula Yehi Nishani Hai.

4
Kaun Hai Bad-e-Bilal Kis Ne Yahan Aana Hai.
Aap Ka Saya Hi Kabay Ki Chat Pe Chana Hai.
Hai Dua Yeh Keh Woh Din Jald Hi Aaye Maula.
Turbat-e-Zahra Pe Saya Tera Chaye Maula.

5
Farsh-e-Matam Tera Nokar Waan Bichaye Maula.
Turbat-e-sZahra Pe Ashkon Ko Bahaye Maula.
Turbat-e-Fatima Pe Matam-e-Zanjeer Bhi Ho.
Our Mere Samne Maula Teri Tasveer Bhi Ho.

6
Aap Aaein Gaye Tu Dunya Mein Sakoon Jab Ho Ga.
Khatama Dushman-e-Shabeer Ka Bhi Tab Ho Ga.
Aap Ke Aane Se Phir Pak Yeh Arab Ho Ga.
Aisa Hajj Ho Ga Keh Masror Phir Se Rab Ho Ga.

7
Aap Aaein Gaye Tu Dunya Yeh Pak Tar Ho Gi.
Aap Ke Aane Ki Na Jane Kab Khabar Ho Gi.
Roz Likhta Hoon:ASAD AZMI: Khat Maula Ko.
Our Yehi Kehta Hoon Maula Se Apne Main Ro Ro.

8
Maula Jaldi Se Meri Aarzu Yeh Pori Ho.
Meri Inn Aankhon Ko Tum Apne Noor Se Bhar Do.
Qabal Marne Se Ziyarat Ho Aap Ki Maula.
Ho Mere Samne Marne Se Qabal Kuch Aisa.
