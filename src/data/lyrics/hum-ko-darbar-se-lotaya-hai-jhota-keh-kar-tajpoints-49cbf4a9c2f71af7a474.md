Hum Ko Darbar Se Lotaya Hai Jhota Keh Kar.

1
Jis Ghari Laut Ke Darbar Se Aaein Zahra.
Sar Ke Balon Mein Safaidi Tu Kamar Mein Kham Tha.
Dekha Zainab Ne Tu Bolin Keh Zaeefa Ruk Ja.
Kahan Aati Ho Musalla Hai Yeh Maa Zahra Ka.
Apna Munh Peet Ke Zahra Yeh Pukarein Zainab.
Mujh Ko Pehchan Keh Maa Hoon Main Tumhari Zainab.
Meri Bachi Na Bula Mujh Ko Zaeefa Keh Kar.

2
Tujh Ko Aati Hai Kamar Meri Jo Yeh Kham Zainab.
Tu Sune Gi Tu Kere Gi Mera Matam Zainab.
Taziyana Mujhe Mara Hai Nikal Ja Keh Kar.
Jab Gawahi Ko Hoye Paish Mere Lakhkt-e-Jigar.
Jhirkiyaan Dene Lagay In Ko Sharabi Mil Kar.
Bhaiyon Ko Tere Tuhkraya Hai Bacha Keh Kar.
Tu Jo Kehti Hai Keh Darbar Se Kya Layi Hoon.
Purze Baba Ki Sannad Ke Main Utha Layi Hoon.
Phar Dali Hai Yeh Tehreer Fasana Keh Kar.
Qabar-e-Sarwar Pe Yeh Faryad Kiya Karti Thi.
Baba Kehti Hai Mujhe Aap Ki Ummat Jhoti.
Tum Bolatay The Mujhe Umm-e-Abeeha Keh Kar.
Bandh Kar Le Gaye Rasi Mein Ali Ko Ghar Se.
Roein Jab Fatima Zahra Tu Tamachay Barsay.
Bain Karti Rahi Haye Mere Baba Keh Kar.
Mujh Pe Jalta Howa Darwaza Giraya Baba.
Mera Mohsin Mere Pehlu Mein Hi Dam Toor Gaya.
Noha Karti Rahi Barbad Madina Keh Kar.
Ho Baqaiya Jo Asad Our Mazahir Jana.
Noha Oss Bibi Ki Turbat Pe Yehi Dohrana.
Woh Jo Roti Rahi Tu Umar Yeh Jumla Keh Kar.
