1
Haye Ya Hussain Sarwar Raza Karbalai.
Hashar Tak Jaye Gi Haye Ya Hussain.
Mit Nahi Sakti Aza-e-Ya Hussain.
Kya Wilayat Hai Meri Bolay Ali.

2
Karbala Se Sham Tak Sun Lein Sabhi.
Nook-e-Naiza Par Bataye Ya Hussain.
Oss Ki Chokhat Par Jhukay They Jitnay Sar.
Paein Gay Apni Ibadat Ka Samar.

3
Hone Do Bas Aashnaye Ya Hussain.
Jab Khuda Bola Keh Banday Mang Le.
Keh Diya Main Ne Gham-e-Shabeer De.
Kya Bhala Leta Bajaye Ya Hussain.

4
Hath Hain Seene Pe Dharney Ke Liye.
Paon Hain Majlis Mein Jane Ke Liye.
Zindagi Hai Yeh Bara-e-Ya Hussain.
Youn Rasan Basta Galay Mein Hai Yaqeen.

5
Ghut Na Jaye Dam Sakina Na Kahin.
Koi Bachi Ko Churaye Ya Hussain.
Hotey Hain Khaimay Sabhi Jal Kar Dhowaan.
Koi Dikhta Hi Nahi Apna Yahan.

6
Kaun Khaimon Ko Bhujaye Ya Hussain.
Kab Se Yeh Asif Tarapta Hai Tera.
Keh Raha Hai Har Ibadat Mein Raza.
Apna Roza Ab Dikhaye Ya Hussain.
