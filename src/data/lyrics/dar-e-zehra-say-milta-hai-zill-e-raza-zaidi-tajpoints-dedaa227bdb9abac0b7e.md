Dar e Zehra Say Milta Hai Zill e Raza Zaidi.

1
Bataein Kya Humein Kya Kya Dar-e-Zahra Se Milta Hai.
Kahin Se Jo Nahi Milta Dar-e-Zahra Se Milta Hai.
Kaha Hur Ne Adab Se Naam Jo Leta Hai Bibi Ka.
Ossay Romal Ka Tohfa Dar-e-Zahra Se Milta Hai.
Taluq Koi Na Koi Hai Dono Mein Bohat Gehra.
Khuda Se Jo Bhi Hai Manga Dar-e-Zahra Se Milta Hai.

2
Hai Masjid Ke Barabar Mein Azakhaney Ka Yeh Matlab.
Khuda Ke Ghar Ka Darwaza Dar-e-Zahra Se Milta Hai.
Agar Ho Ishq-e-Zahra Dil Mein Tu Mariyam Ko Khaliq Se.
Beghair-e-Baap Bhi Beta Dar-e-Zahra Se Milta Hai.
Hai Ghar Wala Kahan Par Yeh Tu Ghar Walay Bataein Gay.
Zamane Ko Pata Rab Ka Dar-e-Zahra Se Milta Hai.

3
Jo Apni Reesh Se Salman Ja Kar Chuntay Rehtey Hain.
Khazana Koi Tu Aisa Dar-e-Zahra Se Milta Hai.
Main Hadiya Zakiri Ka Tey Karon Tu Kyun Karon Keh Jab.
Bina Mangay Mujhe Itna Dar-e-Zahra Se Milta Hai.
Duaein Sirf Pori Hi Nahi Hoti Wahan Saqlain.
Saleeqa Bhi Duaon Ka Dar-e-Zahra Se Milta Hai.
