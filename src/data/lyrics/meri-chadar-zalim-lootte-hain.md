Hay Shamme Ghareeban, 6

1
Aye Shame Ghareeban Tu he Bata, Kyoun Ale Nabi per Zulm hua,
Khatoon-e-Jina, Zehra ka Chaman, Pardesh main kyoun Barbad hua.

2
Meri Chadar Zalim lootte hain Mere Bhai Hussain Kahan Hoo Tum. 4

3
Sar Khulete hain, Dil tootte hain, Meri Bhai Hussain Kahan ho tum.
Meri Chadar Zalim lootte hain Mere Bhai Hussain Kahan Hoo Tum 2

4
Wo kulla Zainab yahan pe khadi, Muqtal ki taraf nazreen hain jamein
Bechain behain ku hasrath hai, Surath nazar aye Bhai ki.
Lashoon pe Ghode doudte hain, Meri Bhai Hussain Kahan ho tum
Meri Chadar Zalim lootte hain Mere Bhai Hussain Kahan Hoo Tum 2

5
Mera Akbar Sher Jawan gaya, Khasim tha Hasan ki shaan gaya
Asgar jo nanha Mujahid tha, Hoonton pe liye Muskan gaya
Bache Pani ku taraste hain, Meri Bhai Hussain Kahan ho tum
Meri Chadar Zalim lootte hain Mere Bhai Hussain Kahan Hoo Tum 2

6
Tareeki may javoun main kahan, Khaime hain jale Utha ta hai Dhuaan
Ye khud ku musalma kehte hain, Inmain to nahi koi Insaan
Bachoon ku tamache legte hain, Meri Bhai Hussain Kahan ho tum
Meri Chadar Zalim lootte hain Mere Bhai Hussain Kahan Hoo Tum 2

7
Hum log hain aal payamber ki, main beti moula Hyder ki
Meri Maa ka saya kaneezon per, main hoon mohtaaj ek chader ki
Ye beghayarath mujhe deekhte hain, Mere Bhai Hussain Kahan hoo tum.
Meri Chadar Zalim lootte hain Mere Bhai Hussain Kahan Hoo Tum 2

8
Maa Zehra aap ku aana hai, darkar Malak aye Nana hai
Bemukhna O chadar hum sub ku, Bazaar-e-Sham main jana hai
Ayse bhi musafir hote hain, Mere Bhai Hussain Kahan hoo tum.
Meri Chadar Zalim lootte hain Mere Bhai Hussain Kahan Hoo Tum 2

9
Zalim ku dikha doon ayeena, Darbar main goonje meri Sada,
Awaaz meri sunn kar sub ku, yaad ayega Baba ka lehja,
Nazroon ke teer baraste hain, Mere Bhai Hussain Kahan hoo tum.
Meri Chadar Zalim lootte hain Mere Bhai Hussain Kahan Hoo Tum 2

10
Zindaan se rehayi pavongi, wapas jo Madeene javongi
Nana ku amma Zehra ku, Jo Guzri hai wo batavongi
Jo sunte hain wo Tadapte hain, Meri Bhai Hussain Kahan ho tum.
Meri Chadar Zalim lootte hain Mere Bhai Hussain Kahan Hoo Tum 2

11
Lafani ka maal yahi Ghum hain, ab girya kunah kul aalam hai
Wo bibi dua deeti hain, karta jo Hussain ka matam hai
Hai koun jo matam rokhte hain, Meri bhai Hussain Kahan ho tum.
Meri Chadar Zalim lootte hain Mere Bhai Hussain Kahan Hoo Tum 2
