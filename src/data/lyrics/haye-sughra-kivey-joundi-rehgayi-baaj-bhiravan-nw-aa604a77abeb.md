Haye Sughra Kivey Joundi Rehgayi Baaj Bhiravan Dey

Kivey Pehray Deyndi Rayi Ey  Haye Ujriyan Thavan Tey

Divey Baldi Thakgayi Akbar Nayi Aya

Khavrey Kyun Babul Ney Pardes Vasaya

Kadon Hown Gey Meyl Ey Nana  Haye Behn Bhiravan Dey

Khaab Doranay Ounday Menu Neend Nayi Aoundi

Sehrava Vich Daadi Ditee Neer Vakhuandi

Mey Khaab Ch Vekhiya Phupiyan  Haye Baaj Ridavan Dey

Ro Ro Pani Mangday Haye Baal Ayane

Ghazi Deray Laye Kyun Nehar Kinarey

Duniya Ton Pyasay Turgaye  Malik Daryawan Dey

Khoon Kivey Naa Rovay Sajjad Muhari

Pairaan De Vich Chalay Gal Tauq Vi Bahri

Sir Tak Tak Rovay Zakhmi  Phupiyan Tey Mavan Ney

Akthar Hurmal Nu Koyi Ja ke

Samjaye

Ki Milna Ey Zalim Tenu Zulm Kama Key

Asghar Jey Baal Nayi Hondey  Haye Laik Sazavan Dey
