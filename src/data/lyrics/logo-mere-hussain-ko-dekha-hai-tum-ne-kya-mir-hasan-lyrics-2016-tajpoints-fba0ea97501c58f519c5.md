1
Logo Mere Hussain Ko Dekha Hai Tum Ne Kya Mir Hasan Mir.
Aa Kar Yehi Jaloos Mein Kehta Hai Zuljanah.
Aati Hai Oss Ke Khon Ki Khusbo Jo Bar Bar.
Mehsoos Ho Raha Hai Yehin Hai Mera Sawar.
Aaya Hai Kya Jaloos Mein Mazloom-e-Karbala.
Syedaniyaa Tarapti Thin Mehshar Ki Thi Ghari.
Haye Hai Yaad Mujh Ko Aakhri Rukhsat Hussain Ki.
Zainab Ne Mujh Ko Sonpa Tha Beta Batool Ka.
Dekha Jo Main Ne Pyase Ki Aankhon Mein Intezar.
Qadmon Ko Choom Choom Ke Kehta Tha Bar Bar.
Sughra Ke Paas Le Chaloo Gar Hukam Ho Teyar.
Pyase Ka Mere Zeen Pe Sanbhalna Mohaal Tha.
Haye Barchi Jigar Mein Teer Badan Mein Yeh Haal Tha.
Utra Tha Ik Nashaib Mein Zahra Ka Ladla.
Mudat Se Hai Talash Ghareeb-ud-Deyar Ki.
Haye Pehchan Aik Yeh Bhi Hai Mere Sawar Ki.
Chehre Pe Khon-e-Asghar Be-Sheer Hai Mala.
Zakhmo Se Be-Watan Ke Baha Khoon Iss Qadar.
Haye Meri Jabeen Pe Baag Pe Zeen Par Rakaab Par.
Mazloom-e-Karbala Ka Laho Hai Laga Howa.
Gardan Hai Oss Ki Youn Bhi Takallum Jhuki Hoi.
Apna Sawar Dhond Raha Hai Yeh Aaj Bhi.
Bichra Hai Youn Hussain Se Ab Tak Nahi Mila
