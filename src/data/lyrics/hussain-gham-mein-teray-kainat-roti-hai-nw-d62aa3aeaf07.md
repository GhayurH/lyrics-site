Hussain Gham Mein Teray Kainat Roti Hai

Nikal Kay Rota Hai Din Chup Kay Raat Roti Hai

Pilanay Pani Chalay Jab Hussain Asghar Ko

Rubab Khaimay Ki Thamay Kanaat Roti Hai

Tu Is Tarha Say Luta Dosh-e-Mustafa Kay Palay

Teri Ghareebi Pe Allah Ki Zaat Roti Hai

Hussain Laye Thay Qalb-e-Hassan Kay Laashe Ko

Dulhan Ko Dekh Ke Sari Baraat Roti Hai

Mila Na Pani Tujhay Fatima Kay Lakht-e-jigar

To Teri Pyaas Pay Nehr-e-Furat Roti Hai

Utha Ke Lasha-e-Akbar Yahan Se Laykay Chalay

Tumhari Maut Pay Akbar Hayat Roti Hai
