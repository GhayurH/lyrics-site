Pas-e-Nazar Jo Haya Ke Cherag Jalte Hain.
Woh Dars-e-Karb-o-Bala Ke Cherag Jalte Hain.

1
Yeh Tere Aakhri Sajday Ka Moajza Hai Hussain.
Khuda Ke Ghar Mein Khuda Ke Cherag Jalte Hain.
Yazeediyon Se Ijazat Talab Nahi Karte.
Hussainiyo Ko Bata Ke Cherag Jalte Hain.

2
Jahan Mein Majlis-e-Shabir Ke Waseelay Se.
Shaoor-o-Fikar-e-Rasa Ke Cherag Jalte Hain.
Meri Lahad Mein Andhera Ho Kaise Mumkin Hai?
Meri Zubaan Pe Sana Ke Cherag Jalte Hain.

3
Bhekari Ban Ke Main Jab Lafz Un Se Mangta Hoon.
Mere Qalam Mein Atta Ke Cherag Jalte Hain.
Hussainiyat Ne Sikhai Hai Bandagi Mujh Ko.
Mere Labon Pe Dua Ke Cherag Jalte Hain.

4
Unhein Tu Subha Ka Soraj Salam Karta Hai.
Woh Jin Ke Ghar Mein Azza Ke Cherag Jalte Hain.
Yeh Saltanat Mere Ghazi Ki Hai Yahan Sahil.
Kinar-e-Aab Wafa Ke Cherag Jalte Hain.
