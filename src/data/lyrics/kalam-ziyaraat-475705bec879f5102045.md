Haye Sakina haaye Sakina

1
1) Maara gaya dariya pe jo shaidaye Sakina
Sheh rag se sada aati rahi haye Sakina
Haye Sakina haye Sakina
2) Sheh se kaha Abbas ne dar hai mujhe aaqa
Dariya pe kahen rooti na ajaye Sakina
Haye Sakina haye Sakina
3) Lashe se sada athi thi ham bhi na piyenge
Jab tak tumhe paani nahi mil jaye Sakina
Haye Sakina haye Sakina
4) Kis tarha se dikhlaeon tumhe shakl dobara
Jab paani hi mashkize se beh jaye Sakina
Haye Sakina haye Sakina
5) Majboor heon bazoo bhi nahi tum hi batao
Kis tarha se ammu tumhe behlaye Sakina
Haye Sakina haye Sakina
6) Aa jaiye yon zaujaye Abbas pukareen
Wali kahin ghurbath me na mar jaye Sakina
Haye Sakina haye Sakina
7) Zainab ne kaha zere alam peet ke sar ko
Matam karo mara gaya saqqaye Sakina
Haye Sakina haye Sakina
