1
Sakina Per Musibat Ki Huwi Hai Inteha Ghazi Syed Mehdi Rizvi.
Sakina Par Musibat Ki Hoi Hai Inteha Ghazi.
Kafan Kurtay Ka De Kar Oss Ko Dafnaya Gaya Ghazi.
Woh Roti Thi Tarapti Thi Koi Sunta Na Tha Oss Ki.
Ossay Zalim Ne Zindaan Mein Akelay Tha Rakha Ghazi.
Rasan Ik Thi Magar Haye Galay Bandhay Gaye Barah.
Rasan Choti Thi Khinchta Tha Sakina Ka Gala Ghazi.
Woh Pani Daal Deti Thi Akhi Abid Ki Beri Par.
Tumhare Baad Oss Ne Bhi Nahi Pani Piya Ghazi.
Hai Ab Tak Sham Ke Zindan Mein Shabbir Ki Beti.
Na Ho Payi Sakina Qaid-e-Zindaan Se Raha Ghazi.
Sadaein Tum Ko Deti Thi Chacha Ghazi Kahan Ho Tum.
Yateemi Ka Issay Zalim Ne Jab Tana Diya Ghazi.
Sar-e-Zindan Aye Qambar Qayamat Ka Tha Woh Manzar.
Sakina Ne Jo Pocha Kya Nahi Aaye Chacha Ghazi.
