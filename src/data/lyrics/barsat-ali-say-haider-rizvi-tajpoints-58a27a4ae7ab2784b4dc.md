Barsat Ali Say Haider Rizvi Lyrics

1
Sab Par Hai Rehmaton Ki Yeh Barsat Ali Se.
Patay Hain Rizq Arz-o-Samawaat Ali Se.
Mairaj Mein Hoi Hai Mulaqaat Ali Se.
Aaye Hain Khud Mila Ke Nabi Hath Ali Se.
Phaila Ke Hath Aap Kahin Ya Ali Madad.
Poshida Kab Hain Bandon Ke Halaat Ali Se.

2
Hijrat Ki Raat Ho Woh Ya Mairaj Ki Ho Raat.
Ojhal Howa Na Kuch Bhi Kissi Raat Ali Se.
Dunya Mein Bantta Hai Woh Behlool Ki Tarha.
Milti Hai Jis Kissi Ko Bhi Khairat Ali Se.
Momin Ko Iss Liye Bhi Nahi Khof Maut Ka.
Marne Ke Baad Hogi Mulaqaat Ali Se.

3
Kaba Ali Ke Aane Se Qibla Hai Ban Gaya.
Zahir Hoye Hain Kitne Kamalat Ali Se.
Sajdoon Samait Sare Woh Dozakh Mein Jaein Gay.
Rakhtay Hain Dil Mein Bugz Jo Hazraat Ali Se.
Maidan Koi Bhi Tha Wahan Haider-o- Asad.
Khayi Barey Baron Ne Bari Maat Ali Se.
