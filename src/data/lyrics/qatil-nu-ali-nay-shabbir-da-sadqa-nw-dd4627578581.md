Qatil nu Ali nay Shabbir da sadqa paani si pilaya

Sadqa-e-Zainab si, rasiyan nal bajiyan, bawan nu bulaya

Zainab ay soch kay, haye hairaan si bari

Main tay aye na bazaa-ran vich aana aye nahe kaddi

Fair kyon babay nay, Kufay shehar da menu, Bazaar vakhaya

Valaan nu khol kay, haye dhiyaan nay rondiyan

Babay di laash tay baikey aye vain pondiyan

Aagaye ay yateemi, uth gaya saday sir ton, Aj babay da saaya

Duniya ton jandiyaan, haye sub nu bula laya

Ghazi day hath chay Zainab da aye keh kay hath daya

Bhena day parday da, aj ton Ghazi tenu, Zamin main banaya

Manzar ajeeb si, haye Saqlain Kufay da

Uthiya janaza Yasrab dey Shah da si is tarah

Shaam waliyan royaan, Karbala dan shaheedan, Taboot uthaya
