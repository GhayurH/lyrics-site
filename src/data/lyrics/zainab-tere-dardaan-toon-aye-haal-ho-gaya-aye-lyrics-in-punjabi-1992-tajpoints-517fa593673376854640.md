1
Zainab Tere Dardaan Toon Aye Haal Ho Gaya Aye.
Sajjad De Kafan Da Rang Lal Ho Gaya Aye.
Aakhay Sajjd Mula Pawein Aakhir Ne Sanwaan.
Bhuliyaan Ajay V Mainu Nayi Sham Diyaan Ranwaan.

2
Zainab Tera Kyun Parda Pamal Ho Gaya Aye.
Tarpe Yateem Baqir Chumay Piyo Da Haye Janaza.
Heran Kul Khudai Wagda Aye Khon Taza.
Abid Zulm Ankokha Tere Naal Ho Gaya Aye.

3
Sadme Ne Lao Roanday Bazar Jan Walay.
Dukhre Jigar Nu Kha Gaye Darbar Jaan Walay.
Akhiyaan Choon Dil Da Zahir Aye Obaal Ho Gaya Aye.
Rasiyaan Hoiyaan Ne Rasiyaan Masoom Gardana Te.

4
Kuj Reham Khao Logo Mastor Qaidiyaan Te.
Pyasa Be-Hosh Sada Har Baal Ho Gaya Aye.
Onthaan Di Rasiyaan Nu Chad Ke Jaye Bainda Qaidi.
Barsaat Koriyaan Di Be-Taab Sainda Qaidi.

5
Karbal Toon Sham Taein Be-Haal Ho Gaya Aye.
Kita Kalam Abid Jad Aa Gaya Sharabi.
Darbar Nu Hila Gayi Guftar Boturabi.
 Sahib Pata Oh Welay Lajpal Ho Gaya Aye.
