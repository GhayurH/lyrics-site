Sar Se Baba Ka Saya Utha Haye Zainab Yateem Ho Gayin.
Bich Gaya Ghar Mein Farsh-e-Azza Haye Zainab Yateem Ho Gayin.

1
Hain Musallay Shareek-e-Azza Masjidein Kar Rahi Hain Buka.
Ho Gaye Qatal Shair-e-Khuda Haye Zainab Yateem Ho Gayin.
Ik Qayamat Hoi Turbat-e-Syeda Thartharane Lagi.
Jab Madine Mein Gonji Sada Haye Zainab Yateem Ho Gayin.

2
Zehar Aalodah Shamsheer Se Kul-e-Eman Ko Mar Ke.
Ibn-e-Muljim Sitam Dha Diya Haye Zainab Yateem Ho Gayin.
Zarb Sajday Mein Mari Gayi Kul-e-Eman Ko Jis Ghari.
Arsh Walon Ne Noha Kiya Haye Zainab Yateem Ho Gayin.

3
Bache Kozay Liye Dodh Ke Dar Pe Haider Ke Mojood Hain.
Koi In Ko Bataye Zara Haye Zainab Yateem Ho Gayin.
Aa Gayin Muztar-o-Dil Hazeen Mehway-e-Faryad Ummal Baneen.
Barh Ke Fizza Ne Pursa Diya Haye Zainab Yateem Ho Gayin.

4
Ghar Se Jis Dam Janaza Chala Aik Kuhram Barpa Howa.
Hath Sar Pe Hasan Ne Rakha Haye Zainab Yateem Ho Gayin.
Shehar Kofa Mein:YASIR RAZA: Gonjti Hai Musalsal Sada.
Aaj Rukhsat Howay Murtaza Haye Zainab Yateem Ho Gayin.
