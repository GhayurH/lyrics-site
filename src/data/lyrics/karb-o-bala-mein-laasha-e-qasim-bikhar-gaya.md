Karbobala main lasha ay Qasim bickar gaya

-Par’ deen ay haq ko zinda ay, Jawayd’ kar gaya

-Showk’ jihad’ nusratay sarvar kay josh may

Qasim ka aur chehra ay, anwar nikhar gaya

-Baba ka jehsay zehr’ say, tukray huwa jigar

Maqtal main mehsay hey tunay, Qasim bikhar gaya

-Wa Qasima kar shor’ say qehmay may hashr tha

Farwa thalash kar thi thi, Kasim kidar gaya

-Ay binte Mustafa tayray kunbay key kehr’ ho

Tasveeray Mujtaba tha jo, Kasim wo mar gaya

-Kasim ka noha sha hay shahidan nay jab parha

Har kalbay dard’ mand’ taak’, us ka asar gaya

-Madar kay bain thay yayhi kasim key lash par

Ub jey kay kya karoon, kay mera lal mar gaya

-Zahra jinna say ahi hain, sunnay thayra kalam

Ash’har bas ub tho tayra, mukadar savar gaya
