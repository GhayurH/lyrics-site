Ya Fatima Karam Hai Apka Joan Rizvi
Aouj Par Aap Ki Jab Shan Nazar Aati Hai.

1
Khud Khudai Mujhe Hairan Nazar Aati Hai.
Aapke Naam Se Mansoob Hoi Hai Jab Se.
Mujh Ko Tasbeeh Bhi Quran Nazar Aati Hai.
Hijab-e-Khaliq Zahra.

2
Ali Ki Raziq Zahra.
Kaun Samjhay Ga Aye Bibi Jo Aap Ka Hai Rutba.
Kehte Hain Janat Jisay Woh Aap Ka Aangan Hai.
Aap Ke Hi Noor Se Yeh Dunya Roshan Hai.

3
Jo Addo Hai Aap Ka ALLAH Ka Dushman Hai.
Fidak Kya Cheez Hai Zahra, Hai Sab Kuch Aap Ka Sadqa.
Aap Na Hotin Tu Kuch Bhi Takhleeq Nahi Hota.
Aap Hi Khair-Al-Nisa Our Tafseer-e-Kausar.

4
Malka-e-Kaun-o-Makaan Hain Khatoon-e-Mehshar.
Zeenat-e-Arsh-e-Ulla Hai Bibi Aap Ka Ghar.
Farishtay Arsh Se Aakar, Jahan Bantay Hain Nokar.
Farsh Par Bibi Bas Aisa Hai Aap Ka Darwaza.

5
Khuld Ke Jo Shehzaday Hain Aap Unn Ki Janat.
Rehmat-e-Dono Jahan Ke Ghar Ki Hain Rehmat.
Kab Kissi Ne Payi Hai Aye Bibi Yeh Azmat.
Hain Oss Ke Wastay Emaan, Jo Khud Hai Kul-e-Emaan.

6
Jo Dua Sab Ki Sun Le Woh Ghazi Hai Teri Dua.
Aap Jab Maidan Mein Pohanchi Aye Sadiqa.
Dekh Kar Najraniyon Mein Shoor Yehi Utha.
Noor Ka Yeh Qafila Hai Unn Se Nahi Larna.

7
Agar Yeh Hukam Suna Dein, Paharon Ko Palta Dein.
Dono Aalam Mein Inn Jaisa Koi Bhi Nahi Suna.
Mustafa Ke Ashiqon Na Ishq Karo Farzi.
Fatima Ki Marzi Mein Hai Ahmad Ki Marzi.

8
Mutahid Rehna Hai Tu Sun Lo Meri Arzi.
Hadees-e-Bizatto Mini, Na Bholein Shia Suni.
Sath Aa Jao Sab Mil Ke Detey Hain Bas Yeh Sada.
Aap Ka Haq Cheen Kar Jis Ne Khaya Bibi.

9
Jis Ne Bhi Sachaiyon Ko Jhutlaya Bibi.
Ghar Jalanay Aap Ka Jo Bhi Aaya Bibi.
Nahi Hai Koi Bhi Dar, Karon Ga Lanat Unn Par.
Koi Jalta Hai Jal Jaye Bas Razi Hoon Zahra.

10
Aap Ke Jo Dushmano Se Rakhay Humdardi.
Chahe Woh Jo Bhi Ho Oss Pe Lanat Hum Sab Ki.
Ghair Ho Ya Apna Ho Parwah Nahi Koi.
Nahi Ho Saktay Mubarah, Hamesha Hoga Tabarah.

11
Aap Haq Par Hain Jo Manay Hai Sirf Wohi Apna.
Aap Ke Hi Naam Se Hal Hoti Hai Mushkil.
Dar Badar Bhatkay Howon Ko Milti Hai Manzil.
Aap Se Hi Rakhta Hai Umeed Hamara Dil.

12
Humein Chadar Mein Apni, Hamesha Rakhna Bibi.
Har Hussaini Ke Honton Pe Hai Aik Yehi Narah.
Janti Hain Aap Hi Jo Haal Hamara Hai.
Aap Ki Chokhat Ka Hi Hum Sab Ko Sahara Hai.

13
Aap Ko Bas Aap Ko Mushkil Mein Pukara Hai.
Keh Jab Tak Dam Mein Dam Ho, Humein Na Koi Gham Ho.
Bas Gham-e-Sarwar Ho Dil Mein Our Samne Karb-o-Bala.
 Joan Our Zeeshan Bas Kartay Hain Aik Dua.

14
Dekhna Hai Aap Ka Tameer Humein Roza.
Barhwan Jo Aap Ka Ghaibat Mein Hai Beta.
Ijazat Dijiye Bibi, Chaley Aaein Ab Mehdi.
Jald Hat Jaye Chehray Se Ab Ghaibat Ka Pardah.
