Lash-e-Abbas Pe Sheh Jo Aaye Haye Haye Mir Hasan Mir.
Dekha Bhai Ko Khoon Mein Nahaye.

1
Liptay Bhai Ke Lashay Se Sarwar Our Bolay.
Kaisay Sadmay Falak Ne Dikhaye.
Choor Zakhmo Se Sara Badan Hai Haye Kaisay.
Bhai Ka Bhai Lasha Uthaye.

2
Dasht-e-Ghurbat Mein Bhai Ko Bhai Mere Bhai.
Chor Kar Iss Tarha Se Na Jaye.
Ro Ke Boli Sakina Ke Haye Haye Mere Ammo.
Aap Aisay Gaye Phir Na Aaye.

3
Aayi Sham-e-Ghareebaan Tu Zainab Ro Ke Boli.
Kaun Hai Ab Jo Aa Kar Bachaye.
Boli Zainab Madad Kar Madad Kar Shair Mere.
Meri Chadar Kahin Chin Na Jaye.

4
Dekhein Sheron Se Bhai Ka Lasha Aye Khudaya.
Waqt Behno Pe Aisa Na Aaye.
Mangay Ro Kar Sheh-e-Dein Ki Dukhtar Kozay La Kar.
Shimar Pani Zameen Par Bahaye.

5
Kar Rahe Hain Jafaein Sitamgar Aye Dilawar.
Sheh Ki Beti Kahin Mar Na Jaye.
Hain Sakina Ke Rukhsar Neelay Haye Itnay.
Zalimo Ne Tamachay Lagaye.
