Gir kar kabhi uthe kabhi rakha zameen pe sar

ubhla lahoo kabhi to sambhala kabhi jigar

hasrat se ki qayam ki jaanib kabhi nazar

karwat kabhi tadap ke idhar li kabhi udhar

uth baithe jab to zakhmo se barchi ke phal giray

teer aur tan may gad gaye jab moo ke bal giray

jangal se aayi fatema zehra ki ye sada

ummat ne mujhko loot liya wa mohamada

is waqt kaun haqqe rifaqat kare ada

zainab se boli fatema zehra basad buka

unees sau hai zakhm tan e chaak chaak par

zainab nikal hussain tadapta hai khaak par

kis chaah se paala isay aanchal may chupa kar

mai jaagti rehti isay raaaton ko sula kar

chakki bhi jo peesi hai to godhi may bitha kar

kis haal may dekha hai isay dasht may aakar

unees sau hai…

zainab nikal…

hai kaun jo mazloom ko teero se bachaye

seh roz ke pyase ko koi pani pilaye

zakhmo may bhari khaak koi aake chudaye

har laash uthayi hai isay kaun uthaye

unees sau hai…

zainab nikal…

abbas ke marne se kamar toot chuki hai

akbar ke bichad jaane se beenayi gayi hai

in aankhon may baysheer ki turbat bhi bani hai

hai aakhri lamhaat ajal sar pe khadi hai

unees sau hai…

zainab nikal…

kuch daye may khanjar mere bache pe chalega

afsos dam e zibha bhi pani na milega

khanjar ki kayi zarbo se sar iska katega

ghayoor hai beta hargiz na sahega

unees sau hai…

zainab nikal…

baakhi hai abhi jaan tera bhai hai zinda

ghode kahin dauda na de be-dard hai aada

haye haye koi bekas ko nahi poochne wala

bhai se mohabbat hai to maqtal may chali aa

unees sau hai…

zainab nikal…

ye bain mai sunti hoo tere baba ali se

ho jayenge pamaal abhi laash sabhi ke

kuch marhale baakhi hai ghareeb ul watani ke

ja haal zara dekh le is pyase aqi ke

unees sau hai…

zainab nikal…

aajiz hai yahan aa kar har ek sukhanwar

farhan anees e shahe waala ho ke mazhar

likh paaya nahi aaj talak koi wo manzar

jab kehti thi zainab se mere bibi tadap kar

unees sau hai…

zainab nikal…
