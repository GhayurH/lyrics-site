1
Garchay Likhi Hoi Thi Shahadat Imam Ki.
Lekin Mere Hussain Ne Hujat Tamam Ki.
Zainab Ki Be Ridayi Ne Sar Mera Dhak Diya.
Aagaz-e-Subha Noo Hoi Wo Shaam, Shaam Ki.
Ik Khowab-e-Khaas Chashm-e-Muhammad Mein Tha Chupa.
Tabeer Noor-e-Ain Muhammad Ne Aam Ki.
Bachoon Ki Piyaas Malik-e-Kausar Pe Shaaq Thi.
Saqi Ko Warna May Ki zarorat Na Jaam Ki.
Hur Sa Naseeb Badshehon Ko Nahi Naseeb.
Aaqa Se Mil Rahi Thi Gawahi Ghulam Ki.
Darya Pe Tishna Lab Hain Ye Sehra Mein Shaad-e-Kaam.
Dunya Ajab Hai In Ke Safar Our Qiyaam Ki.
Day Kar Raza Jo Chehra-e-Shabir Zard Hai.
Thi Iltijaye Jang Yeh Kiss Lalah Faam Ki.
Poetess::Parveen Shakir:
