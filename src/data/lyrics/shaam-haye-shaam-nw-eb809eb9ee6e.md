Shaam hi Shaam

-Teri ummat kay zavar pehn kar, deko nana challi houn mai Shaam

-Mai deti kafan tuj ko bahya, agar sar pay rida meray hothi

Ghar hothay kulay hath meray, theray zaqmo ko hathoon say dothi

Pabunday rasan sar barehna, deko bahya challi hoon mai shaam

-Theray bacho ko lay kar gayee mai, Shabar sari rothi rahee hoon

Kabee Ghazi ka lehja badal kar, mai pehray bhi daythee rahee hoo

In beva bahoon beteeyoon say, kuin karthay nahee ho kalam

-Theray abid pay tharee ghashee thi aur Kulsom sehme kare thi

Kabee dakoon mai zaqmi Sakina, jis kay daman may aag lagee thee

Kabee ro hoon may upnee rida ko, kabee dakoon mai jalthay khiyam

-Kabee nazray uta kar na dayka, jis Zainub ko tu nay birader

Pasay garden say hath` banday hain, aur sar par nahee maray chadar

Kesay Kufay main jaoon gi Ghazi, aur majmay may ahyan ga naam
