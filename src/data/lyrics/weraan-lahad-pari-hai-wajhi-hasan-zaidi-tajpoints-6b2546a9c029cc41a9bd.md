1
Be-Saibaan Hai Turbat Haq Se Nabi Ki Beti Mehroom Aaj Bhi Hai Weraan Lahad Pari Hai.
Haye Aye Kalma Go Bata De Manga Tha Jo Nabi Ne Woh Ajar Kya Yehi Hai Weraan Lahad Pari Hai.
Sholon Mein Ghir Gaye Hain Konain Ke Ujalay.
Kaba Jala Rahe Hain Khud Kalma Parhne Wale.

2
Zahra Ka Dar Jala Kar Kabay Ko Sar Jhukana Jurm Aisi Bandgi Hai Weran Lahad Pari Hai.
Rooh-Al-Ameen Bhi Jis Dehleez Par Khare The.
Khud Mustafa Bhi Jiss Ki Tazeem Kar Rahe The.
Bad-e-Nabi WOh Zahra Ghasib Sahabiyon Mein Ghantoon Khari Rahi Hai Weran Lahad Pari Hai.

3
Baba Ke Bad Iss Pe Din Kis Tarha Ke Aaye.
Mumkin Nahi Hai Zahra Pehlu Se Hath Uthaye.
Dono Jahan Ki Malka Ummat Se Zakhm Le Kar Marqad Mein Ro Rahi Hai Weraan Lahad Pari Hai.
Hum Kaise Bhool Jaein Bibi Tujhe Rolana.

4
Pehlu Pe Dar Girana Darbar Mein Bulana.
Sab Gham Tu Ik Taraf Hain Rone Ke Wastay Bas Yeh Baat Hi Bari Hai Weran Lahad Pari Hai.
Aulad Ke Ghamoon Se Fursat Isse Kahan Hai.
Afsoos Fatima Bhi Kitni Ghareeb Maa Hai.

5
Sadiyon Se Aaj Tak Jo Pursa Wosool Karne Khud Chal Ke Aa Rahi Hai Weran Lahad Pari Hai.
Ghaibat Ko Khatam Kar Do Aye Hadi-e-Zamana.
Ab Jald Laut Aaein Zahra Ke Ghar Ki Khushiyaan.
 Muhsin Yeh Maa Dobara Phir Muskuraye Zahra Sadiyaan Jo Ro Chuki Hai Weraan Lahad Pari Hai.
