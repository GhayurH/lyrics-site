1
Akbar Ne Di Azan Namudar Din Howa.
Aashor Ki Seher Se Azadar Din Howa.
Jitna Dhala Kuch Utna Hi Dushwar Din Howa.
Tishna Labi Ke Samne Dewar Din Howa.
Har Maa Ki Gaud Khali Thi Beta Bichar Giya.
Ik Din Mein Fatima Ka Bhara Ghar Ujar Giya.

2
Apne Tu Khair Apne The Mehmaan Na Bach Sake.
Teer-o-Sina-Khanjar-o-Talwar Sab Chale.
Ik Mukhtasir Se Fauj Pe Saye The Maut Ke.
Aaghaz Se Asar Wohi Anjam Tak Rahe.
Honti Thi Ronuma So Qaza Har Tarha Hoi.
Ik Baat Dopehar Mein Bahatar Tarha Hoi.

3
Akbar Ke Sar Ta Pa Muhammad Tha Ho Bahu.
Shabir Ki Hayat Wo Laila Ki Aarzo.
Naize Jo Ran Mein Karte The Sab Oss Ki Justuju.
Maa Dil Pe Hath Rakh Ke Yeh Sunti Thi Guftugu.
Kehte The Loag Seene Mein Barchi Uthar Do.
Akbar Pe Waar Kar Ke Muhammad Ko Maar Do.

4
Jis Waqt Ran Mein Shair Ke Sane Utar Gaye.
Zainab Thi Kehti Haye Sitam Kya Guzar Gaye.
Jin Pe Rida Thi Baal Wo Mati Se Bhar Gaye.
Abbas Ki Qaza Hoi Hum Loag Mar Gaye.
Aisa Dalair Bahi Bhala Kab Naseeb Ho.
Ab Mujh Se Barh Ke Kaun Jahan Mein Ghareeb Ho.

5
Qasim Ke Kya Shagun Howay Haye Lab-e-Furat
Har Rasm Khud Nibhati Rahi Maut Sath Sath.
Tapon Ki Zad Mein Aa Gayi Farwa Ki Kainat.
Choti Si Ik Rida Mein samai Thi Pori Zaat.
Mehndi Thi Ya Lahu Tha Tan-e-Pash Pash Par.
Gul Ka Guman Hota Tha Qasim Ki Lash Par.

6
Gulshan Ke Gul Tu Gul The Kali Bhi Na Bach Saki.
Andheer Mach Giya Tha Andheri Thi Zindagi.
Karki Kumaan Tu Khaimo Mein Madar Samaj Gayi.
Apne Hi Khoon Se Bhuj Gayi Asghar Ki Tishnagi.
Dunya Mein Kuch Kahe Bina Khamosh Ho Giya.
Aankhein Khuli Hi Reh Gayin Be Sheer So Giya.

7
Haye WO Sham Sham-e-Gharebaan Kahein Jise.
Furqat Ka Ik Payam Tha Sughra Ke Waste.
Sughra Tu Door Khat Se Bhi Akbar Na Mil Sake.
Dekhi Hai Maut Ne Yeh Azeeyat Qareeb Se.
Akbar Ki Lash Se Khat-e-Sughra Tha Itni Door.
Qabar-e-Nabi Se Tubat-e-Zahra Hai Jitni Door.

8
Akbar Janab-e-Sani-e-Zahra Ki Thi Yeh Baat.
Khanjar Se War Karta Tha Sheh Pe Wo Bad Sifat.
Bandhe Howay Hisar Thi Mein Tu Nazar Ke Sath.
Rakhe Howya The Halq Pe Amma Ne Apne Hath.
Khanjar Ke Raste Mein Jo Sheh Ayi Kat Gayi.
Shabir Ka Gala Meri Benai Kat Gayi.
