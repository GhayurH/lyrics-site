Aey meray Qasim

1
Lash e Qasim pe kaha Maa ne ye ro kar

2
Ho gaye pamal tum lut gayi madar

3
Chand sa chehra tera wo bharay bazo teray
Jism tera nazneen wo siyah geso teray
Yaad aayain gay mujhe ab to reh reh kar

4
Ba adab Qasim chalay mangnay ran ki raza
Shah bolay kis tarha kar doon may tum ko juda
Tujh may dikhti hay mujhe soorat e Shabbar

5
Mil gaya khat baap ka mil gaya Qasim ko chain
Bhai ki tehreer par khoob roye thay Hussain
Haq pe kar dena fida ran may apna sar

6
Jang may kar to diye Sheh ne Qasim ko wida
Dil karra kar kephir fee amanillah kaha
Dil pe qaboo na raha kia karay Sarwar

7
Jang kamsin ki kaheen aisi to dekhi nahi
Shaam ki deewar tak phat gayi foaj e shaqi
Hamla e Qasim may tha hamla e Haider

8
Itnay may aayi sada aap par aaqa salam
Al madad Mola meray aey zamanay ke Imam
Chal diye behr e madad Shah ye keh kar

9
Is qadar pamaal thay maa ne pehchana nahi
Jab talak Shabbir thay itna batlaya nahi
Dekho ye hay aap ka Qasim e muztar

10
Umm e Farwa ne kiye laash e Qasim ke tawaf
Ro ke kehti hay tujhe doodh karti hoon moaaf
Ho gayeen maa surkharoo ab sar e mehshar

11
Pal may sooni ho gayi mang sandal se bhari
Lut gayi khushyan meri reh gayi noha gari
Toar kar chooriyan dulhan kehti hay ro kar

12
Dhoondye milti nahi aur Kubra si dulhan
Beriyan payal bani aur kangna hay rasan
Jis ka dulha be kafa kud hay be chadar
