1
Ishq Mein Jaan Se Guzar Jaein.
Ab Yehi Jee Mein Hai, Keh Mar Jaein.
Yeh Humein Hain, Keh Qasar-e-Yaar Se Roaz.
Be Khatar Aa Ke Be Khabar Jaein.

2
Jamah Zaibi Na Pochiye Unn Ki!
Jo Bigarne Mein Bhi Sanwar Jaein.
Unn Ko Mad-e-Nazar Howa Parda.
Ahl-e-Shoq Aab Kaho Kidhar Jaein.

3
Shab Wohi Shahb Hai, Din Wohi Din Hain.
Jo Teri Yaad Mein Guzar Jaein.
Girya Shaam Se Tu Kuch Na Howa.
Unn Tak Aab Nalah-e-Sahar Jaein.

4
Dosh Tak Bhi Balaye Jaan Hain Woh Baal.
Jane Kya Ho Jo, Ta Kamar Jaein.
Shair Darasal Hain Wohi Hasrat
Sunte Hi Dill Mein Jo Uthar Jaein.
