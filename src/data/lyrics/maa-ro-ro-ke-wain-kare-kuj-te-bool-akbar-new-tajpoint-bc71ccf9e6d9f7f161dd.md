Maa Ro Ro Ke Wain Kare Kuj Te Bool Akbar New Noha.
Main Sham De Safar Te Jana Aye Hun Waqt Nayi Mere Kool Akbar.

1
1- Atharaan 18 Saal Da Chan Mera Haye Ain Rasool Jawani Da.
Mitiyaan Wich Shamiyaan Rool Dita Mera Dunya Taun Anmool Akbar.
2- Zainab Seena Payi Chumdi Aye Gash Kha Kulsoom Payi Digdi Aye.
Tera Piyo Mitiyaan Taun Nayi Uthda Tak Khaimiyaan Da Mahool Akbar.

2
3- Main Sir De Chityan Walaan Nu Tere Khon De Naal Khizab Kita.
Tak Ghurbat Maa Di Ik Wari Band Hondiyaan Akhiyaan Khol Akbar.
4- Kino Sehra Putar Sajawaan Main Kiwein Jinj Nu Toor Charhawaan Main.
Tere Shagna Da Arman Koi Nayi Bachiya Mere Kool Akbar.

3
5- Jini Zarbaan Tainu Lagiyaan Ne Odhay Khat Diyaan Oniyaan Satraan Ne.
Hath Jigar Taun Cha Ke Ik Wari Sughra Di Udeekaan Tool Akbar.
6- Akbar Aye Bibi Wain Karey Sunda Te Howein Ga Wain Mere.
Zainab Taun Puch Ke Aayi Aan Tainu Rowan Tere Kool Akbar.
