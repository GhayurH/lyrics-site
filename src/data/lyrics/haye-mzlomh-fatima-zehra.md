Haey Mazlooma Fatima Zehra

1
Kaise sadmay tu ne bibi haey uthaey raat din
Gar ye parr jate dino pe msil e shab ho jaatay din
Haey Masooma Fatima Zehra
Teri sachayi pe karta naaz hay Rabb e jaleel
Ummati darbar may tu haey jhutlai gayi
Haq nahi paaya Fatima Zehra
Teray dar par khud risalat jhuk ke karti thi salam
Par nahi Ummat ne samjha teray is dar ka maqam
Ghar jalaa daala, Fatima Zehra
Maaf kar do wali o Maula meray pyaray Ali
Aap ki khadmat jo poori kar na paoon ya Ali
Karti hoon naala, Fatima Zehra
Baad meray pyar se bachon ko rakhna ya Ali
Na kabhi mehsos honay dije ga meri kami
Ro diye Maula, Fatima Zehra
Shab ki tareeki may mayyat ko meri le jana tum
Shah Ali un zalimo ko hargiz na aanay dena tum
Teergi rakhna, Fatima Zehra
Baad meray Karbala may Sheh ki nusrat ke liye
Ya Ali farzand ik Abbas hona chahye
Karti hain ye dua, Fatima Zehra
Kehti hain Fizza ke bibi aey kaneez e ba wafa
Teray zimmay Zainab o Kulsoom ko may ne kia
Roti hain Fizza, Fatima Zehra
Zakhmi pehlu ko sambhalay roti hain dilgeer ko
Janay kyun hasrat se takti rehti hain Shabbir ko
Sochti hain kia, Fatima Zehra
Seena e Shabbar ko choomain aur kabhi Sheh ka gala
Choom kar Zainab ke baazo karti hain aah o buka
Yaad aaya kia, Fatima Zehra
Zakhmi tan se zakhm e man se ghair halat ho gayi
Waqt wo aaya ke bibi ki shahadat ho gayi
Arsh tharraya, Fatima Zehra
Gham abhi bhoola nahi tha meray baba ka mujhe
Ik naya gham mil gaya hay meray Mohsin ka mujhe

2
Zainab o Kulsoom o Fizza, Shabbar o Haider yahan
Door koonay may kharray Shabbir rotay hain wahan
Maajra hay kia, Fatima Zehra
Kehtay hain Haider kia madar ke qareen na aayain gay
Bolay Sheh Amma bulayain gi hamain jo aayain gay
Kia hay Rab ki raza, Fatima Zehra
