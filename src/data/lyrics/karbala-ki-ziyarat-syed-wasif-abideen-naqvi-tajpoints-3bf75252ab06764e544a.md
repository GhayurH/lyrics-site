1
Karbala Ki Ziyarat Syed Wasif Abideen Naqvi.
Teri Taraf Qadam Jo Barhatay Hain Karbala.
Haq Hai Khuda Ki Simt Woh Aate Hain Karbala.
Teri Haqeeqatein Jinhein Paney Ka Zooq Hai.
Woh Jin Dilon Mein Teri Ziyarat Ka Shoq Hai.
Shabeer Unn Dilon Ko Basatay Hain Karbala.
Barh Kar Janab-e-Hur Unhein Seene Lagaein Gay.
Mehshar Mein Sar Utha Ke Wohi Loag Aaein Gay.
Sar Teri Khak Par Jo Jhukatay Hain Karbala.
Unn Ko Duaein Deney Woh Khud Chal Ke Aati Hain.
Romal Unn Ki Aankhon Se Zahra Lagati Hain.
Aanso Jo Sheh Ke Gham Mein Bahatay Hain Karbala.
Abbas Ki Woh Jeet Ka Ayelaan Karatay Hain.
Zindah Hai Karbala Woh Jahan Ko Batatay Hain.
Abbas Ka Alam Jo Uthatay Hain Karbala.
Jin Ke Khameer Mein Hai Teri Aarzu Basi.
Mar Jaein Woh Kahin Bhi Samatay Hain Tujh Mein Hi.
Tere Karam Ki Bheek Jo Patay Hain Karbala.
Dunya Mein Ajar Paein Gay Pyasay Imam Se.
Serab Hoon Gay Hashar Mein Kausar Ke Jaam Se.
Pani Jo Zairon Ko Pilatay Hain Karbala.
Ashor-o-Arbaeen Pe Hai Zawwaron Ki Qatar.
Aaye Jo Aik Bar Woh Aata Hai Bar Bar.
Sar Bhi Paray Katana Katatay Hain Karbala.
Yeh Janta Asad Hai Keh, Hain Matami Ki Shan.
Matam Ke Zakhm Kab Hain Yeh Bakhshish Ke Hain Nishan.
Seene Pe Matami Jo Bachatay Hain Karbala.
