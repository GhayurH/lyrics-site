Chal paray Shabbir Karbal ho gaya veeran Medina

Rooti Akbar tujh ko Sughra  ray gayi tanha Medina

Saar bhi du gah ghaar bhi du gah bheno ki chadaar bhi duga

Deen bachanay kay liye noujawan Akbar bhi du gah

Abh na zinda lout kaar may  aaow gah Nana Medina

Bhenay lay kaar haram lay kaar Karbala ko ja raha hu

Aik ghum hay meray dil may jis ko lay kaar ja raha hu

Nana ummat nay nahi di  qabar ki bhi ja Medina

Ro raha hay aik sehra day raha hay woh sadaye

Khoon khudara day kay apna mola mujh jo ah basaaye

Ban jaow gah may muqaddas  jis tarha Makka Medina.

Subhe Ashura jo ghoonjhi bun may Akbar ki azaan

Bhenay rooyi phuphiyan royi rooti hay Akbar ki maa

Sun kay royi bhenay bichri  bhayi ki azaan Medina.

Jab chala tah kafila yeh oonto ki thi sou (100) qataray

Aj Shahid bibiyo ki koyi sunta na pukaray

Teen mehmil ek muhaari  kafila louta Medina.
