Bazar Sajaye Jawan Gaye Te Ummat Pathar Wasaye Gi.
Ro Zainab Aakhay Veer Tera Ainj Chehlum Bhain Manaye Gi.

1
Mangiyaan Ayeh Duawaan Bibi Ne Bach Jaway Putar Beemar Mera.
Howay Khair Elahi Be Chadar Maa Wich Darbar De Jaye Gi.
Tere Naal Haan Main Aakhay Zainab Toon Kala Nahin Na Ro Abid.
Kar Dafan Sakina Da Lasha Maa Zainab Dard Wandaye Gi.

2
Aoulad Haan Pak Muhammad Di Sanu Bagi Na Aakhay Koi.
Nezay Te Parh Quran Bhira Te Khutba Bhain Sunaye Gi.
Main Kali Reh Gayi Sham De Wich Toon Watan Te Ja Sajjad Bhira.
Aakhein Sughra Nu Meri Tarfoon Wapis Na Sakina Aaye Gi.

3
Ainj Sada Istaqbal Hoya Sir Zakhmi Ne Sab Qaidiyaan De.
Jhal Ummat De Pathar Veer Mera Zainab Teri Nasal Bachaye Gi.
 Sajjad Ayeh Abid Ro Aakhay Mainu Roog Laga Aye Ghairat Da.
Darbar Di Peshi Phophiyaan Di Mainu Qabar Che Khon Rawaye Gi.
