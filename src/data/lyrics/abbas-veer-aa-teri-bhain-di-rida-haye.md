Abbas veer Aa Teri Bhain Di Rida Haye Shimr Lahaway
Akhay Ro Ro fizza sadd Ghazi Nu, Saday Khaymay sar Gaye, Saanu aan bachaway..
Khokay Meri Rida Reyha Hasda Haye Mere Veer Da Qatil
Koi dardi Howay Jera naizay toun Tere Babay di dhee da aan parda Bachaway..
Sham toun Pehla Main, Sham Walay Haye Dukh Sehnay Ghazi,
Koi Qaidan Kehkay Menu Sang Maray, Koi Door o hi Teri peya mashaq wikhaway..
Lay kay Qasim Di Janj Qaidi Haye Tur Chaliyan Nay Bhaina
Kubran Dey Hathaan Wich Ferwa hun teri laash tay ro ro pae rasiyan lukaway
