1
Ya Ali Tu Waliyan Da Sultan Wajhi Hasan Zaidi & Son.
Sab Taun Ochiyaan Sab Taun Wadhya Shan.
Ya Ali Tu Waliyaan Da Sultan.
Terah Rajab Ko Kaba Bola.
Marey Qibla Marey Maula.
Mere Maula Aao Rakho Qadam.
Mere Aaqa Aao Rakho Qadam.
Qadmo Se Tere Barh Jaye Gi Meri Shan.
Matami Sare Tere Sawali.
Dar Pe Kharey Hain Ban Ke Bhikari.
Nokar Hain Tere Hoor-o-Malak.
Qadmo Mein Para Hai Tere Falak.
Darzi Hai Tere Hasnain Ka Woh Rizwan.
Hain Tere Shia Nooh-o-Aadam.
Tu Ne Banaya Sara Aalam.
Har Nabi Ke Lab Par Teri Sana.
Har Nabi Pe Wajib Teri Wila.
Hai Teri Wila Se Nabiyon Ki Pehchan.
Maula Ali Hai Jo Tera Munkir.
Dushman-e-Khaliq Hai Woh Muqassar.
Shaitaan Ka Pisar Munkir Hai Tera.
Milti Hai Ossay Lanat Ki Saza.
Bas Tera Mohib Dunya Mein Hai Insan.
Apna Aqeeda Sirf Yehi Hai.
Aik Khuda Hai Aik Ali Hai.
Yeh Kam Khuda Ka Karta Hai.
Murday Ko Zindah Karta Hai.
Bola Yeh Nuseri Haider Hai Yazdan.
Khaybar Ko Ukhara Haider Ne.
Marhab Ko Pachara Haider Ne.
Jab Ali Wali Ki Taig Chali.
Khush Ho Ke Bolay Pyaray Nabi.
Aye Mere Ali Main Wari Main Qurban.
Mere Ali Ki Zaat Ajab Hai.
Rab Ka Karam Hai Rab Ka Ghazab Hai.
Rakhta Hai Zamane Par Qudrat.
Qatil Ko Pilata Hai Sharbat.
Rehmat Pe Ali Ki Dunya Hai Hairan.
Shafa-e-Mehshar Ali Ali Saqi-e-Kausar Ali Ali.
Nafs-e-Payamber Ali Ali Zahra Ka Shohar Ali Ali.
Maula-e-Qanbar Ya Haider.
Aaqa-e-Abuzar Ya Haider.
Dekh Ke Tujh Ko Kehta Hai Salman.
Raas Hai Hum Ko Teri Faqeeri.
Rooz-e-Azzal Se Hum Hain Ghadeeri.
Mairaj Nazar Ko Hone Lagi.
Palaan Pe Jonhi Aaye Nabi.
Tha Toor Se Oncha Khum Ka Woh Palaan.
Mere Ali Ka Pehla Hai Number.
Kehta Hai Yehi Lal Qalandar.
Har Dam Tha Lab Par Nam-e-Ali.
Parwaz Thi Onchi Saein Ki.
Tha Lal Qalandar Ka Dam Dam Ayelan.
