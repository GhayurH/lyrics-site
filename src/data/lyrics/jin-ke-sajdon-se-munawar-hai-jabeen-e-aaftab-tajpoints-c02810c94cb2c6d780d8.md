Mere Harfon Ki Ibadat Un Khuda Walon Ke Naam.
Meri Sheh Rag Ka Laho, Nazr-e-Shaheedan-e-Wafa.
Mere Jazboon Ki Aqeedat Karbala Walon Ke Naam.
