1
Ik Ghareeb-ul-Watan Qaid Mein Mar Gaya.
Pul Peh Bagdad Ke Hai Janazah Rakha.
Khon Aankhon Se Apni Bahate Howay.
Hath Kari Beriyaan Tooq Pehne Howay.
Qaid Sajjad Ki Yaad Karta Howa.
Jiss Ke Athara Betey Jawaan Saal They.
Oss Ki Maiyat Ko Mazdoor Le Kar Chalay.
Bain Karti Chali Sath Mein Syedah.
Ajnabhi Shehar Hai Door Ahl-e-Watan
Tere Baba Ko Koan De Ghusal-o-Kafan.
Ayo Jaldi Watan Se Imam-e-Raza.
Sabar Karna Hai Sugrah Ke Jaisa Tujhe.
Rasta Dekhna Baap Ka Choor Dey.
Ja Ke Masoma-e-Qum Se Keh Dey Hawa.
Qaid Mein chal Basa Shah-e-Koon-o-Makaan.
Aab Tu Mazloom Ki Khool Do Beriyaan.
Tum ne Chaha Tha Jo Lo Wohi Ho Giya.
Lash Par Bhi Nahi Rone Wala Koi.
Waris-e-Deen Ki Haye Be Warisi.
Dhondti Hai Qaza Dafan Karne Ki Ja.
Bain Karte Takallum Sabhi Aa Gay.
Karbala Se Madine Se Our Sham Sey.
Chodah Barsoon Ka Qaidi Reha Ho Giya.
