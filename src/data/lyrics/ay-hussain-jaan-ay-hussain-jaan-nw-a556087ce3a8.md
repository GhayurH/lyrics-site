ay hussain jaan ay hussain jaan
ay ali ke laal ay shahe zaman
fatha karliye tune do jahan
muskura utha jabke bezuban
ay hussain jaan….

tu panahe deen tu hi shahe deen
warise nabi kaabae yaqeen
nazishe harram haq ke paasban
ay hussain jaan….

karbala jihad karbala qayam
tune hashr tak dediya payam
roohe karbala raaze kunfaka
ay hussain jaan….

deke karliya zulm se firaat
tujhse rehgayi ambiya ki laaj
ibne fathima faqre mursala
ay hussain jaan….

hurr ka tazkira hai waraq waraq
sunke aagaya jo sadaae haq
subhe karbala maaniye azaab
ay hussain jaan….

zindagi se door hogaye harram
jab hua shaheed aamile alam
jiske dosh par haq ka tha nishan
ay hussain jaan….

jabse hat gaye saare mursaleen
behgaye nabi khoon may jabeen
kehke ya ali khaynchi jo sina
ay hussain jaan….

jis mareez par tha sitam tamaam
dashte zulm se taaziya risham
wo aseere gham meere karwaan
ay hussain jaan….

madare hussain deti hai dua
goonjti rahe bas yehi sada
farshe khaak se ta ba aasma
ay hussain jaan….

zindagi tamam zikre karbala
nusrate imam girya-o-buka
lab pe suboh sham ho yahi fughan
ay hussain jaan….

do sada fareed matame hussain
koi gham na ho juz ghame hussain
hashr tak rahe bas yehi sama
ay hussain jaan….
