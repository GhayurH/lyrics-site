1
Kofa o Sham Ke Be Dard Sitamgaron Se.
Kis Qadar Gham Miley Sajjad Ko Agyaron Se.
Gar Koi Qafilay Walon Mein Se Gir Jata Hai.
Koray Sajjad Pe Zalim Wahin Barsata Hai.
Iss Tarha Paish Koi Aata Hai Beemaron Say?.
Khon Behta Howa Aankhon Se Yeh Batlata Hai.
Dekh Lo Ahl-e-Madina Tumhein Dikhlata Hai.
Kaisay Sajjad Hai Guzra Bharay Bazaron Se.
Oss Ka Gham Tauq Se Barh Kar Unhein Tarpata Tha.
Be Watan Ko Jo Safar Mein Woh Nazar Aata Tha.
Khon Behta Howa Hamsheer Ke Rukhsaron Se.
Jab Bhi Sajjad Ko Abbas Ki Yaad Aati Thi.
Be Ridai Unhein Ghar Walon Ki Tarpati Thi.
Lag Ke Rotay They Woh Zindaan Ki Dewaron Se.
Qatal-e-Shabeer Pe Khushiyaan Bhi Manatay Dekha.
Our Asad Oss Pe Tamaskhur Bhi Uratay Dekha.
Dard Sajjad Ko Kya Kya Miley Bazaron Se.
