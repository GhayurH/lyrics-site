Jo Nahi Janta Fatima Kon Hai Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi
Woh Kahan Janta Hai Khuda Kaun Hai.

1
Pochta Reh Gaya Jibreel-e-Ameen.
Ya Elahi Yeh Zair-e-Kisa Kaun Hai.
Rotiyaan Roz Le Jata Hai Syeda.
Teri Dehleez Ka Yeh Gada Kaun Hai.

2
Ik Muhammad Hain Mairaj Par Aye Khuda.
Paar Parday Ke Yeh Dosra Kaun Hai.
Qaba Qausain-o-Adna Ki Manzil Bata.
Muttasil Kaun Hai Our Juda Kaun Hai.

3
Main Ghulam-e-Ali Khadam-e-Syeda.
Shaikh-e-Magror Ab Tu Bata Kaun Hai.
Fatima So Rahi Hain Woh Gardish Mein Hai.
Aaj Chaki Bhala Peesta Kaun Hai.

4
Fatima Ki Dua Se Hoye Khalq Jo.
Aik Ahl-e-Azza Ke Siwa Kaun Hai.
Zikar-e-Aal-e-Muhammad Se Izzat Hai Sab.
Warna Akbar Humein Pochta Kaun Hai.
