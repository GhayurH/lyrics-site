1
Shah e Konain Da Janaza Jadoon Galiyaan Che Aaya Haye Maiyat Ne Wain Paye.
Kofe De Loko Shehar Saja Lo Kal Meriyaan Dhiyaan Layi Koi Na Bazar Sajaye.
Mere Vari Baar Aa Jawan Utha Ke Pathar.
Lain Badlay Meri Maiyat Te Wasa Ke Pathar.

2
Koi Shehar Da Wasi Qaidi Kar Ke Mere Poor De Balaan Te Na Aan Ke Pathar Wasaye.
Maan Bhaina Da Raway Jag Te Salamat Shala.
Jiwein Dunya Te Mera Ghazi Aye Almaan Wala.
Bhaina Di Chadar Ban Ke Jiwein Ghazi Da Qatal Aaj Mere Bazu Aan Lahaye.

3
Mera Lasha Bab-e-Kofa Te Lay Jaye Koi.
Othon Darbar Taein Menu Phiraye Koi.
Zahra Diyaan Jaiyaan Nu Galiyaan Wich Bin Chadar Baaj Ridawaan Na Jhirkaan Mar Phiraye.
Teer Gardan Che Meri Aa Ke Paro Lo Saray.

4
Kal KOi Mereyaan Putraan Nu Na Naizay Maray.
Noo Lakh Fojaan De Rahwar Bula Ke Wich Sehra De Meri Maiyat Pamal Karaye.
Shehar Wich Jiniyaan V Rasiyaan Ne Manga Lay Hakim.
Meri Dhiyaan Di Jagah Menu Pawa Lay Hakim.

5
Bawaan Gardan De Naal Paro Ke Meri Maiyat Ne Hakim Piya Neelay Dag Banaye.
Wainr Karda Riya Akbar Aye Najaf Da Wali.
Choon Salaan Di Sakina Nu Na Maray Koi.
Mere Kan Cheeray Mar Tamachay Meri Bachari De Badlay Mainu Wich Qaidaan Dafnaye.
