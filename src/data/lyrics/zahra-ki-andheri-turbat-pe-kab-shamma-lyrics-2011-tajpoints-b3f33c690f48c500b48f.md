1
Zahra Ki Andheri Turbat Pe Kab Shamma Jalanay Aao Gaye Mir Hasan Mir
Aye Muntaqim Kab Aao Gaye Aye Muntazir Kab Aao Gaye.
Zahra Ki Andheri Turbat Pe Kab Shama Jalanay Aao Gay.
Tooti Hoi Qabron Ko Maula Kis Roz Bananey Aao Gay.

2
Be Sheer Ki Nanhi Maiyyat Ko Turbat Se Nikala Tha Jis Ne.
Kab Oss Ke Lashay Ko Maula Naizay Pe Uthane Aao Gay.
Hai Chaar Baras Ki Ik Zahra Zindan Mein Sadiyon Se Qaidi.
Kab Bait Al-Huzan Se Tum Oss Ko Aazad Karanay Aao Gay.

3
Dhaya Gaya Roza Dadi Ka Dekha Hai Ujarta Samarra.
Jo Dagh Miley Hain Ghaibat Mein Kab Unn Ko Dikhaney Aao Gay.
Woh Sham Our Kofa Ke Rastay Woh Naqoon Se Girtay Bachay.
Hai Kaun Kahan Par Dafan Howa Kab Naam Bataney Aao Gay.

4
Masooma-e-Qum Our Maula Raza Hum Shakal-e-Payamber Our Sughra.
Yeh Bhai Behan Hain Bichrey Hoye Kab Unn Ko Milaney Aao Gay.
Beton Ke Janazon Par Akbar, Akbar Ke Liye Jo Roti Hai.
Kab Ujari Hoi Unn Maon Ko Insaf Dilaney Aao Gay.
