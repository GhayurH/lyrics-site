1
Haye Baba Aap Ke Bad Na Soyi Zahra Irfan Haider.
Musalmano Hamarey Pyaray Nabi Ki Ladli Beti Janab-e-Fatima Tuz Zahra Salam ALLAH Alaiha Ki Shahadat Ke Din Aa Gaye Hain.
Libas-e-Soag Pehan Lo Kyunke Iss Bibi Ki Shahadat Ka Din Ashora Se Kam Nahi Hai.
Ik Roz Madine Walon Ne Haider Se Kaha Zahra Se Kahin.
Woh Raat Mein Mehlan Ro Lein Ya Baba Ke Liye Din Mein Ro Lein.
Yeh Baat Ali Ne Batlayi Jab Fatima Zahra Ko Aa Kar.
Bolein Zahra Main Ro Loon Gi Ab Ghar Se Door Kahin Ja Kar.
Yeh Sun Ke Madine Se Bahir Ik Bait Al-Huzan Banaya Gaya.
Jahan Baith Ke Zahra Roti Thin Our Ro Ro Ke Yeh Kehti Thin.
Haye Baba Aap Ke Baad Na Soyi Zahra.
Bas Roti Rahi Haye Wawela.
Jis Ghari Jalta Howa Mujh Pe Woh Darwaza Gira.
Pasliyaan Toot Gayin Pehlu Shikasta Bhi Howa.
Tumhari Yeh Beti Sada De Rahi Thi.
Haye Mara Gaya Mohsin Mera.
Baba Tum Jab Se Gaye Ho Gayi Tanha Zahra.
Baap Ki Yaad Mein Jab Ghar Mein Bicha Farsh-e-Azza.
Issi Gham Mein Roi Yateemi Mein Koi.
Deney Nahi Aaya Hosla.
Kash Woh Waqt Kabhi Mujh Pe Na Aata Baba.
Main Ne Haider Ka Gala Rassi Mein Dekha Baba.
Gayi Jab Bachaney Lagay Taziyane.
Our Hath Mera Zakhmi Howa.
Baba Ik Maa Ke Liye Waqt Bohat Mushkil Tha.
Aik Zalim Ne Mere Rukh Pe Tamacha Mara.
Chupati Thi Chehra Hasan Dekhta Tha.
Jab Surkh Howa Chehra.
Mere Aane Pe Jahan Aap Bhi Uth Jate They.
Wohi Darbar Tha Our Samne Sab Baithay Rahe.
Akeli Khari Thi Fidak Mangti Thi.
Afsoos Mujhe Haq Na Mila.
Sirf Atharah Baras Mein Yeh Mera Aalam Hai.
Dekho Baloon Mein Safaidi Hai Kamar Bhi Khum Hai.
Zaeefa Hai Ab Tu Mera Haal Dekho.
Main Tham Ke Chalti Hoon Rasta.
Ro Ke Zeeshan Yehi Kehta Hai:IRFAN-E-AZZA:.
Cheen Kar Saya Laeen Ne Woh Shajar Kaat Diya.
Jahan Baithti Thi Bas Ab Dhop Mein Bhi.
Honton Pe Yehi Tha Marsiya.
