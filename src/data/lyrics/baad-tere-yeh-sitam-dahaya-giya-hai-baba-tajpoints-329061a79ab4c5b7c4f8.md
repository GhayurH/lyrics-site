1
Baad Tere Yeh Sitam Dahaya Giya Hai Baba.
Mujh Ko Darbar Mein Bulwaya Giya Hai Baba.
Kushk-o-Tar Jo Hai Sar-e-Dehar Mehar Hai Mera.
Phir Bhala Bag-e-Fidak Ka Mujhe Gham Kya Ho Ga.
Gham Tu Yeh Hai Mujhe Jhutlaya Giya Hai Baba.
Mein Ne Dawa Jo Kiya Meri Sanad Rad Kar Di.
Woh Sanad Jiss Pe Thi Tehreer Wasiyat Teri.
Oss Ko Do Tukray Bhi Karwaya Giya Hai Baba.
Aap Ke Bad Musayab Hi Musayab Totay.
Hakim-e-Waqt Ke Ahkam Pe Dekho Mere.
Ghar Ka Darwaza Bhi Jalwaya Giya Hai Baba.
Aag Le Ke Jo Mere Ghar Ki Taraf Aaye Addo.
Kar Diya Mera Laeeno Ne Shakista Pehlu.
Mujh Pe Darwaza Bhi Girwaya Giya Hai Baba.
Main Teri Yaad Mein Din Raat Buka Kar Thi.
Teri Ummat Mere Rone Pe Gila Karti Thi.
Ghar Se Be-Ghar Mujhe Karwaya Giya Hai Baba.
Kalma Goyon Ne Kiye Baad Tere Zulm Kaseer.
Qatal Muhsin Ko Kiya Our Tere Haider Ko Aseer.
Mujh Ko Har Gaam Pe Tarpaya Giya Hai Baba.
Ro Ke Yasir Yehi Kehti Thi Meri Shehzadi.
Sab Ko Maloom Th Main Koan Hoon Lekin Phir Bhi.
Aap Ki Beti Ka Haq Khaya Giya Hai Baba.
