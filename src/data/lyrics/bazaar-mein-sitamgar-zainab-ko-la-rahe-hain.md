Bazar main sitamgar Zainab ko la rahain hain Abbas ko bulao
Aalai Nabi ko Zaalim dar dar phira rahain hain Abbas ko bulao

1
1) Saidaniyan to sheh kai Matam main ro rahin hain
Aur bai haya sitamgar khushiyan mana rahain hain Abbas ko bulao

2
2) Aik ashk per hain jin kai dono jahan sadqai
Has has kai sham walai un ko rula rahain hain Abbas ko bulao

3
3) Bai khof hon kai zalim ghazib ghulam sadai
Sheh ki yatim bachi pai zulm dah rahain hain Abbas ko bulao

4
4) Beemar saraiban sai ghabrai ka boli Zainab
Ham bai rida khulai sar darbar ja rahain hain Abbas ko bulao

5
5) Aik ma tarap tarap kai feryad ker rahi hai
Sajjad kai sitamgar durai laga rahain hain Abbas ko bulao

6
6) Aik ashk par hai jin kay dunoon Jahan Sadqay
Hans hans kay Sham walay un ko rula rahay hain Abbas ko bulao

7
7) Phir waqt aa gaya hai dunyia mein aaj Gohar
Ahle-sitam jahan mein phir sar utha rahay hain Abbas ko bulao
