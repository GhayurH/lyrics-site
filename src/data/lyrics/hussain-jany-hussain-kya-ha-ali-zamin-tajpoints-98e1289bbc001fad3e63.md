Husain Jaane Husain Kya Hai

1
Likha Main Ne Muhammad Magar Parha Hai Hussain.
Wila Ka Markaz Bana Howa Hai.

2
Hussain Haq Ka Amen Bhi Hai.
Hussain Deen-e-Mobeen Bhi Hai.
Hussain Hi Deen Ki Panah Hai.

3
Hussain Tashahud Mein Murtaza Hai.
Hussain Sajday Mein Mustafa Hai.
Hussain Tasbeeh-e-Fatima Hai.

4
Hussain Awwal Hussain Aakhir.
Hussain Batin Hussain Zahir.
Hussain Tasveer-e-Kibriya Hai.

5
Hussain Hi Ki Hai Kul Khudai.
Hussain Hi Ki Hai Badshahi.
Hussain Takmeel-e-LaIlaha Hai.

6
Kissi Ko Hur Yeh Bana Raha Hai.
Kissi Ko Betay Dila Raha Hai.
Hussain Fitrus Ki Bhi Shifa Hai.

7
Hai Innama Jis Ki Maa Ki Chadar.
Qadeer Bhi Hai Hussain Qadir.
Woh Jis Ka Baba Bhi Murtaza Hai.

8
Hussain Likhti Hi Ja Rahi Hai.
Alam Pe Sar Ko Jhuka Rahi Hai.
Batool Par Har Ghari Atta Hai.
