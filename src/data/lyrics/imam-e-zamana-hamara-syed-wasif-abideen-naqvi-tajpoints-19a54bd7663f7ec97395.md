1
Imam e Zamana Hamara Syed Wasif Abideen Naqvi.
Muqadar Hai Youn Mojzana Hamara.
Hai Qayam Imam-e-Zamana Hamara.
Woh Qayam Zamana Yeh Jin Se Hai Qayam.
Hain Woh Bhaijtay Aab-o-Dana Hamara.
Hai Qayam Ka Yeh Gaib Se Faiz Jari.
Ho Kyun Na Mizaj Almana Hamara.
Woh Khud Bhi Kahin Keh Yeh Ashiq Hain Mere.
Ho Qayam Se Youn Ashiqana Hamara.
Jo Inkar Ke Teer Hujjat Ko Marey.
Woh Har Hurmala Hai Nishana Hamara.
Jhagarna Hai Aapas Mein Millat Ne Tu Phir.
Hai Kya Al-Ajal Ka Tarana Hamara.
Jo Gasib Fidak Ka Hai, Kehte Hain Qayam.
Wohi Tu Hai Dushman Porana Hamara.
Hain Ghayab Imam-e-Zamana Par Unn Se.
Taruf Nahi Gayebana Hamara.
Youn Dunya Mein Hi Dekh Li Hum Ne Janat.
Hai Karb-o-Bala Aana Jana Hamara.
Areeza Asad Kar Raha Hai Yeh Sabit.
Taluq Hai Unn Se Porana Hamara.
