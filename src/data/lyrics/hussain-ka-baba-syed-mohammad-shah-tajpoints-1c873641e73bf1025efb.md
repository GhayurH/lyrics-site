Hussain Ka Baba Syed Mohammad Shah.

1
Shaheed-e-Masjid-e-Kofa Hussain Ka Baba.
Qateel-e-Ghurbat-e-Zahra Hussain Ka Baba.
Issay Tu Mar Diya Tha Madine Walon Ne.
Howa Hai Qatal Dobarah Hussain Ka Baba.
Ridaein Mangti Zainab Jahan Se Guzrey Gi.
Har Oss Ghali Mein Hai Roya Hussain Ka Baba.

2
Woh Gham Sunata Tha Qabar-e-Batool Ko Apna.
Tha Socho Kitna Akela Hussain Ka Baba.
Khushi Qabool Kahan Thi Ossay Batool Ke Baad.
Baroz Eid Bhi Roya Hussain Ka Baba.
Kabhi Falak Kabhi Ghazi Ko Dekha Zainab Ne.
Lahu Mein Doba Jo Aaya Hussain Ka Baba.

3
Issi Ke Samne Zahra Tamachay Khati Rahi.
Ghareeb Ho Gaya Itna Hussain Ka Baba.
Pukara Aakhri Lamhon Mein Shukriya Fizza.
Hai Qarz Daar Tumhara Hussain Ka Baba.
Rukh-e-Batool Pe Muhsin They Ungliyon Ke Nishaan.
Yeh Gham Bhula Nahi Sakta Hussain Ka Baba.
