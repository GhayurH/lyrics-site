1
Baba Ka Bad Zehra Syeda Amber Naqvi.
Baba Ke Baad Zahra Kya Kya Sitam Sahey Gi.
Maula-e-Khum Ki Khatir Yeh Jan Apni De Gi.
Kya Thi Khabar Aye Baba Jhutla Ke Har Gawahi.
Haq Mera Mujh Se Teri Ummat Hi Cheen Le Gi.
Hans Kar Laeen Jis Dam Tehreer Phaar De Ga.
Ho Jaye Gi Zaeefa Tukray Yeh Jab Chunay Gi.
Ik Hath Gaal Par Hai Ik Mein Sand Ke Tukray.
Lekin Ali Se Hal-e-Darbar Na Kahey Gi.
Ik Shoor Tha Gira Do Darwaza Hum Bhi Dekhein.
Yeh Jaltay Dar Ko Thamay Kab Tak Khari Rahe Gi.
Tooti Hain Pasliyaan Tu Mushkil Hai Sans Lena.
Na Bachoon Na Ali Se Bas Fizza Se Kahey Gi.
Qabar-e-Nabi Pe Bachay Bolay Bohat Hai Zakhmi.
Nana Hamari Amma Ab Bach Nahi Sakay Gi.
Aaein Baqiya Mehdi Tu Naqsh Izn Pa Kar.
 Amber Yeh Noha Parh Kar Matam Wahan Karey Gi.
