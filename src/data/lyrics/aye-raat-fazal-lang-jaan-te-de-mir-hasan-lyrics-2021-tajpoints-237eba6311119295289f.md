Aye Raat Fazal Lang Jaan Te De Mir Hasan Mir Write Up
Aye Raat Fazal Lang Jan Te De Din Daswein Da Charh Jan Te De.
Fair Wekhin Baba Ki Karda Aye Ik Wari Wela Aan Te De.
Jadon Veer Hasan Di Maiyyat Te Lokaan Ne Teer Chalaye San Main Wain Odhon V Paye San.
Dewaan Badal Main Naqsha Yasrab Da Mainu Maula Taig Chalan Te De.
Aye Ghat Te Nayi Shehzadiyaan Sab Mainu Chacha Ghazi Kehndiyaan Ne Mera Naam Adab Nal Lendiyaan Ne.
Mainu Naal Bhatijiyaan De Maula Rishtay Da Maan Nibhan Te De.
Kal Charhda Soraj Wekhay Ga Ki Rang Meri Shamsheer Da Aye Te Ki Matlab Takbeer Da Aye.
Jang Fajar Taun Pehlaan Muk Jaway Ik Waar Akbar Aazan Te De.
Ghar Ujriya Bhain Ruqaiya Da Mera Muslim Kofiyaan Mar Dita Main Rondiyaan Dhiyaan Takda Riya.
Kar Fazal Duawaan Aj Maula Mainu Bhain Da Qarz Lahan Te De.
Jay Sham De Ik Ik Wehray Choon Aawaz Na Aaway Rowan Di Te Mera Naa V Ghazi Nayi.
Bas Maa Zahra De Naa Maula Mainu Apna Aap Azman Te De.
Main Wekhiya Sain Rubab Meri Payi Gal Asghar Da Chumdi Aye Barey Maan De Naal Aye Kehndi Aye.
Rakh Hosla Pani Aaway Ga Ghazi Nu Nehar Te Jan Te De.
Aye Karbal Aye Koi Yasrab Nayi Dar Pak Batool Da Jal Jaway Te Baba Taig V Na Chaway.
Main Sham Te Kofa Sarh Dewaan Koi Aag Da Aj Farman Te De.
Main Aaqa Zadi Zainab Di Akh Wich Athro Nayi Tak Sakda Ohno Maan Aye Meriyaan Banwaan Da.
Mera Sir Lathda Aye Ho Lath Jaway Bas Sain Da Veer Bachan Te De.
 Akbar Kehway Ghazi Aj De Layi Mainu Maa Zahra Ne Mangiya Si Maa Umm Ul-Banin Ne Dasiya Si.
Bhawein Naiza V Na De Mainu Bas Maula Maqtal Jan Te De.
