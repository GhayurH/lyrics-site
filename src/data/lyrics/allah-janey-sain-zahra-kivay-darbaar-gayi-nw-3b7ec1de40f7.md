Allah Janey Sain Zahra Kivay Darbaar Gayi

Haq Mangdhi Rahi Ro Ro Kay Sitam Gaara Ko, Isko Darbaar Di Peyshi Eno Tha Maar Gayi.

Khoob Badla Enu Mileya Piyo Dey Ehsaana Da

Paak Zahra Ki Guwaya Si, Musalman Ada

Haye Ummat Edha Vasda Hoya Ghar Saar Gayi

Isko Darbaar Di Peyshi Eno Tha Maar Gayi.

Jidey Darvasey De Jibreel Amey Jukda Si

Jidi Thazeem Layee Khatmey, Rasool Uthda Si

Rondi Mira Deh Ay Veeran Di Ay Hakdaar Gayi

Isko Darbaar Di Peyshi Eno Tha Maar Gayi.

Rokaya Rontho Kohnain Di Shahzadi Nu

Koun Si Ay Jiney Patlaya, Nabhi Zaadi Nu

Lekay Pehlu Chu Zakham Babay Di Ghum Khar Gayi

Isko Darbaar Di Peyshi Eno Tha Maar Gayi.

Kaya Zahra Ney Wasiat Nah Bulavi Zainab

Sath Shabbir Da Maarkay Vi Naba Vi Zainab

Esay Laye Awn Di Maa Shaam Deh Bazaar Gayi

Isko Darbaar Di Peyshi Eno Tha Maar Gayi.

Paak Zahra Terey Pursey Dey Azadar Aye

Rondey Pitdey Teri Zainab Dey, Wafadar Aye

Shaam Nu Ban Key Jo Veeran Di Azadar Gayi

Isko Darbaar Di Peyshi Eno Tha Maar Gayi.
