Veera Main Hun Watna Te Nayi Jana.
Main Apne Babay Nu Aj Ik Ik Zakhm Wakhana.
Be Kafan Mera Baba Raitaan Che Rul Gaya Aye.
Sokiya Nayi Khon Odha Kalma Go Bhul Gaya Aye.
Main Qaid Khane Nu Aj Maqtal Veer Banana.
Wich Karbala De Veera Asghar Layi Sahwaan Mangiyaan.
Chachay Di Lash Kolon Chup Kar Ke Veer Langiyaan.
Oh Karbala Wasda Aye Main Veera Sham Wasana.
Karni Aye Piyo Di Abid Main Aakhri Ziyarat.
Mainu Wekhna Aye Babay Aa Jaway Na Qayamat.
Main Kuj V Nayi Kaina Mere Chehray Haal Sunana.
Mere Veraan Te Babay Da Lagiya Aye Khon Ainu.
Zindan Wich Toon Veera Karna Aye Dafan Mainu.
Toon Ais Cholay Da Mainu Abid Kafan Pawana.
Deen-e-Khuda Te Najfi Ahsan Kar Gayi Aye.
Aakhay Na Koi Sakina Zindaan Che Mar Gayi Aye.
Sakina So Gayi Aye Aj Babay Aap Jagana.
