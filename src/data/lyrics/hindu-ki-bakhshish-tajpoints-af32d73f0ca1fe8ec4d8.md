1
Hindu Ki Bakhshish
Mehshar Mein Di Farishton Ne Dawar Ko Yeh Khabar.
Hindu Hai Aik Ahmad-e-Mursal Ka Madah Gar.
Hai Bot Parast Agarche Woh Lekin Hai Naat Go.
Ahmad Ki Naat Likhta Hai Dunya Mein Beshtar.
Hai Naam Dilo Raam Takhalus Hai Kausri.
Le Jaein Oss Ko Khuld Mein Ya Janib-e-Saqar.
Sunte Hi Yeh Malaikah Se Ik Anokhi Baat.
Farmaya Zuljalal Ne Janat Hai Oss Ka Ghar.
ALLAH Akbar Ahmad-e-Mursal Ka Yeh Lehaz.
Ki Haq Ne Lutf Ki Sag-e-Dunya Peh Bhi Nazar.
