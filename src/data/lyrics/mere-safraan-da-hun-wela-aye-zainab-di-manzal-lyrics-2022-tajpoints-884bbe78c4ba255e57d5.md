1
Mere Safraan Da Hun Wela Aye Zainab Di Manzal Door Aye.
Lang Aayiyaan Teri Lash Kolon Zainab Dadhi Majboor Aye.
Maqtolaan De Ainj Warsaan Nu Koi Qaid Nayi Karda Veera.
Turya Aye Naal Abid De Aj Kariyaan De Wich Noor Aye.
Koi Marda Aye Koi Jhirakda Aye Koi Ronr Na Deway Veera.
Honday Ne Qaidna De Layi Kuj Dunya Te Dastoor Aye.
Tere Bajoon Ghazi Musalmana Ainj Karbal Veer Choraya.
Dena Aye Pursa Abid Nu Rahwaan Wich Har Mastoor Aye.
Mere Naal Beemar Ik Qaidi Aye Jinay Gal Wich Tauq Aye Paya.
Gham Chadraan Da Kar Gaye Ne Har Zakhm Jida Na Soor Aye.
Main Hath Wekhay Maa Zahra De Rayi Putar De Gal Nu Landi.
Khanjar De Heth Rab Nu Kehya Teri Har Marzi Manzoor Aye.
Ik Ujray Qafilay Ne Najfi Fair Ainj Da Sabar Wakhaya.
Kainat Choon Mana Gaye Ne Quran Da Aye Manshoor Aye
Noha Khuwan Party Ch Faiyaz Party Sialkot.
Matami Sangat
Anjuman-e-Sipah-e-Ahl-e-Bait Sialkot
