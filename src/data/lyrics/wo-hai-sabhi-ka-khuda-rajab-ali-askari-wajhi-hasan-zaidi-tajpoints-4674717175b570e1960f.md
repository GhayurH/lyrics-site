1
Wo Hai Sabhi Ka Khuda Rajab Ali Askari Wajhi Hasan Zaidi.
Zamane Bhar Ka Khuda Hai, Woh Hai Sabhi Ka Khuda.
Magar Zamane Se Barh Kar Woh Hai Ali Ka Khuda.
Khizaan Raseda Nigahon Ki Bhi Sunay Ga Zaror.
Chaman Ka, Phool Ka, Khushbo Ka, Tazgi Ka Khuda.
Hussain Pak Ki Marzi, Khuda Ki Marzi Hai.
Mujhe Tu Lagta Hai, Woh Hai Hussain Hi Ka Khuda.
Charag-e-Bazm-e-Hussaini Kabhi Nahi Bhujta.
Sanbhalay Rakhta Hai Iss Lau Ko Roshni Ka Khuda.
Main Oss Khuda Ki Bhala Shan Kis Tarha Likhon.
Keh Jis Khuda Ka Wali Bhi Howa Kissi Ka Khuda.
Nabi Ko Rehmat-e-Aalam Banaya Hai Oss Ne.
Na Kyun Ho Rehmat-e-Kamil Mere Nabi Ka Khuda.
Ho Jis Ko Shoq-e-Shahadat Woh Aa Ke Le Jaye.
Hayat Bantta Rehta Hai Zindagi Ka Khuda.
Issi Ke Qaul Ki Tasdeeq Kar Raha Hoon Main.
Main Shayari Ka Mussadiq Woh Shayari Ka Khuda.
