Faras Ki Zeen Se Mir Hasan Mir Lyrics Write Up
Fars Ki Zeen Se BeShak Utar Gaye Hain Hussain.
Zameen Dhond Rahi Hai Kidhar Gaye Hain Hussain.
Karey Ga Shimar Ka Khanjar Tu Sirf Sar Ko Juda.
Wagarna Lasha-e-Akbar Pe Mar Gaye Hain Hussain.
Sar-e-Bureda Katay Hont Khon Chehray Par.
Ajeeb Haal Mein Shereen Ke Ghar Gaye Hain Hussain.
Main Milne Sham Ke Zindaan Mein Aaon Ga Tujh Se.
Yeh Baat Aakhri Beti Se Kar Gaye Hain Hussain.
Kahin Hain Qasim-o-Abbas Our Kahin Akbar.
Kahan Kahan Tere Tukray Bikhar Gaye Hain Hussain.
Qaza Ke Baad Bhi Bhai Behan Ke Sath Raha.
Jidhar Jidhar Gayi Zainab Udhar Gaye Hain Hussain.
Kissi Ne Mara Hai Pathar Behan Ke Kya Akbar.
Jo Kartay Kartay Tilawat Thehar Gaye Hain Hussain.
