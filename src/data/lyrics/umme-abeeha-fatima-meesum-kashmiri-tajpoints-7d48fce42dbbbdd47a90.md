1
Umme Abeeha Fatima Meesum Kashmiri
Qibla-e-Tatheer Bhi Hain Kaba-e-Ismat Batool.
Rehmat Ul-Alameen Ke Wastay Rehmat Batool.
Baap Ne Umm-e-Abeeha Keh Ke Yeh Samjha Diya.
Aap Ke Qadmo Mein Baba Jaan Ki Hai Jannat Batool.
Do Sharaf Ik Waqt Mein Hain Aap Ke Ya Fatima.
Bazat-e-Mehboob-e-Haq Umm-e-Abeeha Fatima.
Aap Se Hota Hai Sadir Noor Doran-e-Namaz.
Iss Sharaf Se Aap Kehlati Hain Zahra Fatima.
Marzi-e-Qudrat Ko Haq Ne Dhaal Kar Azkar Mein.
Naam Oss Ka Rakh Diya Tasbeeh-e-Zahra Fatima.
Ik Taraf Zoaf-e-Risalat Ki Shifa Our Ik Taraf.
Aap Ki Chadar Hai Haider Ka Musalla Fatima.
Hai Abuha Baluha Se Our Banuha Se Murad.
Aap Se Pehchana Jaye Haq Ka Kunba Fatima.
Khud Pe Itraye Na Kyun Mabodiyat ALLAH Ki.
Phir Musalay Par Hoi Hain Mehway Sajdah Fatima.
Jis Adeel-e-Haq Ka Hai Adal-e-Elahi Muntazir.
Gayarwaan Farzand Hai Woh Aap Hi Ka Fatima.
Iss Atta Par Shukar Se Sarshar Meesum Our Iram.
Aap Ke Dar Par Hoye Hain Sar Basajdah Fatima
