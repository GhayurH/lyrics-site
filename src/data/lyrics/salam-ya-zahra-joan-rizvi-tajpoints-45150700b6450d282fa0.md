Salam Ya Zahra Joan Rizvi
Ya Syeda Ya Fatima Ya Raziya Salam Ya Zahra.

1
Kab Mili Hai Yeh Kissi Ko Bhi Fazeelat Bibi.
Hujjaton Ke Liye Bas Aap Hain Hujjat Bibi.
Aap Hain Haider-e-Karrar Ki Taqat Bibi.
Raz-e-Kibriya Markaz-e-Kisa.

2
Madar-e-Hussain Bint-e-Mustafa.
Aap Ki Rida Mein Mustafa Ki Hai Shifa.
Keh Rahi Hai Yeh Zamane Se Hadees-e-Qudsi.
Kuch Nahi Hota Agar Aap Na Hotin Bibi.

3
Khud Khuda Kehta Hai Na Hotey Muhammad Na Ali.
Aap Ke Liye Khalq Hum Hoye.
Aap Ke Sabab Door Gham Hoye.
Bas Gham-e-Hussain Ki Khatir Humein Chuna.

4
Aap Woh Beti Hain Jo Baap Ki Maa Kehlaye.
Aap Ki Baat Khuda Se Bhi Na Tali Jaye.
Aap Ke Kehne Pe Abbas Zameen Par Aaye.
Mehwar-e-Wafa Aap Ki Dua.

5
Panja e Alam Parcham-e-Azza.
Har Alam Se Aa Rahi Hai Bas Yehi Sada.
Jan Le Sara Jahan Kaun Hain Kya Hain Zahra.
Oss Se Naraz Hai Rab Jis Se Khafa Hain Zahra.

6
Marziyaan Chalti Hain Jis Ki Woh Riza Hain Zahra.
Inn Se Dushmani Rab Se Dushmani.
Bugz Jo Rakhay Hai Woh Lanati.
Surkhuru Wohi Hai Jis Ne Dil Se Keh Diya.

7
Sakht Ahsan Faramosh Ho Ghaddar Ho Tum.
Ummati Ho Ke Muhammad Ke Gunahgar Ho Tum.
Haq Nahi Tum Ko Musalman Pukara Jaye.
Sirf ALLAH Ki Lanat Ke Hi Haqdar Ho Tum.

8
Jis Ne Bibi Ko Rulaya Hai Ho Oss Par Lanat Bolo Beshumar.
Jis Ne Haider Ko Sataya Hai Ho Oss Par Lanat Bolo Beshumar.
Jis Ne Darwaza Giraya Hai Ho Oss Par Lanat Beshumar.
Jis Ne Tehreer Ko Phara Hai Ho Oss Par Lanat Beshumar.

9
Aag Oss Dar Pe Jo Laya Hai Ho Oss Par Lanat Beshumar.
Jis Ne Shehzadi Ko Mara Hai Ho Oss Par Lanat Beshumar.
Jis Ne Jhutlaya Wilayat Ko Ho Oss Par Lanat Beshumar.
Jis Ne Tarpaya Nabowat Ko Ho Oss Par Lanat Beshumar.

10
Aal-e-Ahmad Ka Jo Qatil Hai Ho Oss Par Lanat Beshumar.
Jo Sitamgaron Mein Shamil Hai Ho Oss Par Lanat Beshumar.
Jab Woh Darwaza Gira Bibi Ne Kya Kya Dekha.
Pasliyaan Toot Gayin Pehlu Shikasta Dekha.

11
Apne Mohsin Ki Rawaan Sansoon Ko Rukta Dekha.
Hath Jal Gaye Khon Bhi Baha.
Zulm Aap Ne Kya Nahi Saha.
Haan Magar Ali Se Ja Ke Kuch Nahi Kaha.

12
Aisay Ummat Ne Diya Baap Ka Pursa Bibi.
Jis Ne Jis Tarha Se Bhi Chaha Rulaya Bibi.
Aik Zalim Ne Tamacha Bhi Lagaya Bibi.
Ro Rahe Hain Hum Haye Woh Sitam.

13
Aap Ko Miley Sirf Gham Hi Gham.
Hai Hussainiyon Ke Lab Pe Bas Yehi Sada.
Karbala Walon Pe Jitnay Bhi Sitam Dhaye Gaye.
Syeda Aap Pe Tanha Woh Madine Mein Hoye.

14
 Joan-o- Zeeshan Bhi Rotay Hain Yeh Ghurbat Sun Ke.
Haye Syeda Haye Fatima.
Wa Musibata Wa Musibata.
Aap Ko Salam Kar Rahi Hai Karbala.
