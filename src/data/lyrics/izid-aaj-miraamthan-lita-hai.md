Sar-e-Hussain Sakina ki goud may aaja
Yazid aaj mera imtehaan layta hay

1
Khari hai haathon ko pehlo,  ghum zada dukhiya

2
1) Woh haskay kehta hay dekhoon to kaisi ulfat thi
Suna hai Shah ko tumse bhari mohabbat thi
Tumhare haath mei ab meri,  laaj hay baba

3
2) Hamesha baat Sakina ki aap nay maani
Humari baat kabhi aaj tak nahi talli
Suboot chahiya zalim,  ko is mohabbat ka

4
3) Kafan say haath nikalay thay meri daadi nay
Woh ghaar ki baat thi ulfat kay bhi taqazay thay
Magar yahan peh Sakina,  hay aur phir ye jafaa

5
4) Zamin-e-Karbobala par woh karwatain lay gi
Yeh sunkay laash-e-alaamdar kaamp uthe gi
Khuda karay na sunay woh,  baat yeh mera saqqa

6
5) Dua kartay hain ahlay haram yeh roro kay
Khuda Hussain ki beti ko kamyaab karay
Nazar jhukaye hay beemar,  � e -Karbala mera

7
6) Mujhe bhi apnay masaib sunaiye baba
Jalay howay meray daman main aayiye baba
Sitam hai aaj tamashayi,  bangayi duniya

8
7) Mila jo ajre risalat bataungi tumko
Galay pay neel rassan kay dikhaungi tumko
Musibaton say guzarta,  hay bachpana mera

9
8) Mohabbaton ka takallum yeh moajza dekha
Saar-e-Hussain Sakina ki goud main ayaa
Na aaye baali Sakina,  kay phir labon pay sada
