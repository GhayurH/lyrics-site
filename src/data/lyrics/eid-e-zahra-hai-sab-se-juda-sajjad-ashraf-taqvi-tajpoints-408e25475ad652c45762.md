Lab Pe Momin Ke Hai Yeh Sada.
Eid-e-Zahra Hai Sab Se Juda.

1
Iss Ke Jaisa Nahi Dosra.

2
Momino Ki Hai Yeh Zindagi.
Iss Se Barh Kar Nahi Hai Khushi.
Dil Ki Qowat Hai Our Hosla.
Hoga Dunya Mein Apna Hi Raaj.
Taj Poshi Hai Maula Ki Aaj.
Ho Mubarak Tumhein Syeda.
Sheh Ke Qatil Ko Ghamkhuwar Ne.
Mara Chun Chun Ke Mukhtar Ne.
Koi Zalim Na Unn Se Bacha.
Har Taraf Mehfilein Hain Saji.
Khuld Se Aa Gaye Hain Nabi.
Kehte Hain Marhaba Marhaba.
Aa Rahe Hain Khud Hoor-o-Malak.
Le Ke Khushiyon Ki Rukh Par Chamak.
Jashan Hai Aaj Ghar Ghar Bapa.
Youn Tu Eidein Hain Kuch Our Bhi.
Iss Ki Sab Se Bari Hai Khushi.
Hai Yeh Makhsoos Rab Ki Atta.
Lanatein Umar Sad Par.
Aaj Bhaijay Na Kyun Har Bashar.
Mar Gaya Dushman-e-Fatima.
Muskuraye Hain Zain Al-Abba.
Sari Eidoon Se Hai Youn Siwa.
Oss Ka Rutba Bohat Hai Bara.
 Aarzoo Sun Ke Tere Sukhan.
Deney Aaye Dua Panjtan.
Jab Bhi Sajjad Ne Yeh Parha.
