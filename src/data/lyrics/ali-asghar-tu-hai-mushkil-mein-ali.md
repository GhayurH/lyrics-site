Ali Asghar Tu Hai Mushkil Mein Ali
Karbala Mein Jisay Dushman Ne Kaha Shair-e-Jali

1
Tere Dada Ne Tu Khaibar Ko Ukhara Asghar
Tu Ne Islam Ko Karbal Mein Bachaya Asghar
Tujh Ko Shabir Ne Maidaan Mein Bulaya Asghar
Jaisay Khaibar Mein Nabi Ne Bhi Pukara Tha Ali

2
Hum Tu Abbas Ke Parcham Se Dua Karte Hain
Apni Janein Ali Asghar Pe Fida Karte Hain
Dono Hajaat Ke Dar Hum Ko Atta Karte Hain
Dono Haider Ki Shabahat Hain Khuda Ke Hain Wali

3
Sans Le Paye Na Dushman Jo Ishara Kar Doon
Hath Uthaon Bhi Na Matti Ko Sitara Kar Doon
Tum Agar Chaho Jahan sara Tumhara Kar Doon
Baat Yeh Man Lo Hum Chotay Bare Sab Hain Ali

4
Chacha Abbas Ke Jaisa Na Jawaan Hoon Baba
Bhai Akbar Ki Tarha Na Hi Bara Hoon Baba
Aaj Maidan Mein Main Aise Laron Ga Baba
Ke Sabhi Dushman-e-Deen Bhi Yeh Pukarein Gaye Ali
