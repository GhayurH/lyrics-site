Tar Khoon Mein Sajda Ga Hai Namazi Laho Laho.
Kha Ke Zarb Jabeen Pe Ali Aaj Surkh Ru Hai.

1
Dastar-e-Mustafa Hai Khon-e-Ali Se Rangeen.
Tauheed Ke Nigehbaan Kiya Khon Se Wozu Hai.
Deen-e-Khuda Ki Khatir Khon-e-Ali Baha Hai.
Bach Jaye LaIlaha Bas Haider Ki Aarzu Hai.

2
Baqiya Larz Utha Hai Muljam Ke Iss Zulm Par.
Khair-Al-Nisa Ka Phir Se Zakhmi Howa Pehlu Hai.
Jibreel Noha Gar Hain Rotay Hain Baam-o-Dar Bhi.
Matam Arsh Farsh Pe Howa Jari Chaar So Hai.

3
Fuztu Be Rab-e-Kaba Mein Arooj-e-Bandagi Hai.
Hain Mutmain Ali Tu Pareshan Par Addo Hai.
Dobi Hoi Lahu Mein Namaz-e-Fajar Hai Azhar.
Iss Zulm Se Jo Nikla Gaya Asar Tak Lahu Hai.
