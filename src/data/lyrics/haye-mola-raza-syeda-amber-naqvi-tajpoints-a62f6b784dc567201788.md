1
Haye Mola Raza Syeda Amber Naqvi
Bekas Imam Mera Dunya Se Chal Basa Hai.
Zehar-e-Jafa Raza Ko Mamoon Ne Diya Hai.
Aal-e-Nabi Pe Kaisa Toota Sitam Khudaya.

2
Marqad Mein Fatima Ko Ummat Ne Hai Rulaya.
Masooma Bibi Qum Ke Lab Par Yeh Marsiya Hai.
Kis Ko Zehar Diya Hai Mamoon Tujh Pe Lanat.
Kis Par Sitam Kiya Hai Mamoon Tujh Pe Lanat.

3
Aayi Sada Yeh Haq Se Ummat Ka Paishwa Hai.
Kaisay Na Sar Ko Peetein Kyun Kar Karein Na Matam.
Maula Raza Ke Gham Mein Hai Soag Mein Yeh Aalam.
Ahl-e-Azza Mein Gham Se Mehshar Sa Ik Bapa Hai.

4
Farsh-e-Azza Pe Bethi Mehway Boka Hai Zahra.
Maula Imam-e-Mehdi Ghaibat Mein Roye Mera.
Misal-e-Saton e Kaba Ummat Ne Dha Diya Hai.
Arsh-e-Barin Pe Matam Farsh-e-Zameen Pe Matam.

5
Sar Peeto Bibiyon Sab Ghurbat Ka Hai Yeh Aalam.
Zehar-e-Jafa Se Tukray Tukray Jigar Howa Hai.
Sultan-e-Shah Mashhad Arab-o-Ajam Ke Maula.
Farzand-e-Mosa Kazim Be Warison Ke Aaqa.

6
Matam Karo Raza Ka Taboot Uth Raha Hai.
Ahl-e-Azza Ke Aanso Zahra Ke Dil Ka Marham.
 Amber Yeh Jan Apni Iss Gham Pe Waar Dein Hum.
Likhta Rahon Main Nohay Kami Ki Yeh Dua Hai.
