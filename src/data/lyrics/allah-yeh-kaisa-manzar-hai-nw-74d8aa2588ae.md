Allah Yeh Kaisa Manzar Hai

Allah yeh Kaisa Manzar Hai, Allah yeh Kaisa Manzar Hai

Beparda shamiyon me auladey payamber hai

Nida dehraha hai yeh haskar manadi

Ae logon gharon se nikal aao jaldi

Sazaen do inko yeh baghi hain baghi

Bajte hain shadiyane  Har haath main pathar hain…

Kahan khof khaata hai zalim kisi se

Bulata hai ek ek ko wo naam leke

Har ek bibi chupti hai Zainab ke peeche

Khud ko chupaaye Zainab  Fizza ki pusht par hai

Batata hai shimr aake khud shamiyon ko

Ke kaata hai mene isi se saron ko

Dikhata hai lehra ke phir bibiyon ko

Haaton me us shaqi ke  Ek khoon bhara khanjar hai

Huwa hai asar itna beenayion par

Ajab kushma kush me hai Asghar ki madar

Har ek neza bardaar ke paas jakar

Ro-Ro ke puchti hai  Kya yeh mera Asghar hai

Aseeron pe kaisi musibat bani hai

Har ek bibi bazaar me dhoond ti hai

Sakina kahin bheerh me kho gayi hai

Meh-shar se pehle barpa  Bazaar me meh-shar hai

Qayamat me kaisi qayamat hai barpa

Takallum hai godi me Asghar ka laasha

Adalat me khaliq ki aati hai Zehra

Nazre juki hain sabki  Nabiyon ke labon par hai
