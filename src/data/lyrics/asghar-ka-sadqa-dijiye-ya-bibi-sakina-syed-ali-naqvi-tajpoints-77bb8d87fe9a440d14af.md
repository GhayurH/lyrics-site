1
Asghar Ka Sadqa Dijiye Ya Bibi Sakina Syed Ali Naqvi.
Jo Godiyaan Khali Hain Unhein Kijiye Aoulad Atta Bibi Sakina.
Ro Ro Ke Musalay Pe Dua Karti Hain Maein.
Ho Mojza Betay Mere Ghar Laut Ke Aaein.
Jo Qaid Hain Be Jurm-o-Khata Jald Woh Ho Jaein Reha Bibi Sakina.
Taboot Se Sajjad Ke Woh Liptay Hoye Hain.
Beemar Jo Ro Ro Ke Shifa Mang Rahe Hain.
Sajjad Ke Sadqay Mein Mukamal Inhein Mil Jaye Shifa Bibi Sakina.
Har Bhai Salamat Rahe Behno Ko Bharam Hai.
Shehzadi Tumhein Zainab-o-Ghazi Ki Qasam Hai.
Har Bhai Yeh Kehta Hai Salamat Rahe Behno Ki Rida Bibi Sakina.
Abbas-e-Alamdar Ki Hain Aap Bhateeji.
Woh Aap Ka Kehna Nahi Talein Gay Kabhi Bhi.
Keh Dijiye Ammo Se Bola Lein Woh Humein Karb-o-Bala Bibi Sakina.
Aye Bibi Samajh Sakti Hain Bas Aap Mera Gham.
Chat Par Mujhe Lehrana Hai Abbas Ka Parcham.
Majlis Ke Liye Chahiye Ghar Pori Ho Yeh Meri Dua Bibi Sakina.
Woh Bazu Rahein Baqi Uthatay Hain Jo Parcham.
Woh Hath Salamat Rahein Kartay Hain Jo Matam.
Mah-e-Muharram Mein Younhi Bichta Rahe Farsh-e-Azza Bibi Sakina.
 Zeeshan-o- Ali Par Bhi Karam Kijiye Bibi.
Ho Aap Ke Baba Ke Siwa Gham Na Koi Bhi.
Hum Parhtay Rahein Noha Younhi Rotay Rahein Ahl-e-Azza Bibi Sakina
