Esyaan nahi ye matam kartay rahay hain saray
Is farz main shaamil hain Allah kai Nabi piyarai

1
1) Rota furaat bhi hai bachon ki tishnagi per
Pani sai peetai hain Abbas ko kinarai

2
2) Main dekhun kis terha sai kertai nahi ho matam
Kariyal jawaan ka lasha ho saamnai tumharai

3
3) Bai ghoro qafan ho kunmbai terai ki laashain
Khaiamai jala kai terai koi chadarain utarai

4
4) Paband rassiyon main Zahra ki betiyan thi
Ham kyun na sar ko peetain Aabid ko korai maarai

5
5) Ehsaan hussain ka hai zibh-e-azeem ban ker
Qurbaan ker kai apnai hain bacha diyai tumharai

6
6) Najafi Hussaini matam momin ki ibadat hai
Kerti hai hur-o-hilma kertai hain chaand terai
