1
Karbala Khurrasan Mai Allay Hassan Rizvi.
Dikhai Deti Hai Phir Karbala Khurasaan Mein.
Shaheed Ho Gaye Maula Raza Khurasaan Mein.
Udhar Masooma Idhar Hai Raza Ne Dam Tora.
Batool Giriya Karein Qum Mein Ya Khurasaan Mein.
Shafaq Mein Surkhi Hai Jaisay Thi Rooz-e-Ashora.
Hussain Phir Se Kya Mara Gaya Khurasaan Mein.
Jigar Rasool Ka Tukray Howa Madine Mein.
Zehar Jo Maula Raza Ko Mila Khurasaan Mein.
Yeh Bain Kartin Hain Ro Ro Ke Aliya Zainab.
Hasan Ke Qatal Ko Dohra Diya Khurasaan Mein.
Ali Ke Lal Raza Ko Shaheed Kar Dala.
Ada Ali Se Youn Badlah Howa Khurasaan Mein.
Jigar Ko Thamay Kharey Hain Tamam Jin-o-Malak.
Raza Ko Rotay Hain Sab Anbiya Khurasaan Mein.
Qabool Kar Le Khudaya Dua Yeh Mohsin Ki.
Bichaon Jake Main Farsh-e-Azza Khurasaan Mein.
