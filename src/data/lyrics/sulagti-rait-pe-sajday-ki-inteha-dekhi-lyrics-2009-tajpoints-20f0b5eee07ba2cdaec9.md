1
Sulagti Rait Pe Sajday Ki Inteha Dekhi.
Rasan Mein Waris-e-Tatheer Be-Rida Dekhi.
Yeh Sajda Woh Hai Ke Jis Ne Bachaya Deen-e-Khuda.
Agar Yeh Sajda Na Hota Tu Kuch Bhi Na Hota.

2
Issi Hi Sajday Mein Bunyad-e-La-Ilah Dekhi.
Kataya Sajday Mein Sar Deen Ki Baqa Ke Liye.
Na Taj-o-Tauq Ko Dekha Na Maal-o-Dolat Ko.
Faqat Hussain Ne ALLAH Ki Riza Dekhi.

3
Ajeeb Waqt Tha Ke Patharon Ki Barish Mein.
Sina Ki Nook Pe Dekha Quran-e-Natiq Ko.
Laboon Pe Sorah-e-Tatheer Ki Sada Dekhi.
Khuda Ka Shukar Hai Sughra Gayi Na Tu Karbal.

4
Nahi Tu Aaj Bhi Logon Ne Yeh Samajhna Tha.
Bazar-e-Sham Mein Qaidi Hai Fatima Dekhi.
Sajjad Khon Na Rota Tu Our Kya Karta.
Ghayoor Qaidi Ne In Khoon Aalood Aankhon Se.

5
Khari Hazaron Mein Tafseer-e-Innama Dekhi.
Sakina Door Ke Lipati Thi Apne Baba Se.
Ali Ki Betiyaan Behosh Ho Gayin Akhtar.
Chidi Jo Teeron Se Shabir Ki Abba Dekhi.
