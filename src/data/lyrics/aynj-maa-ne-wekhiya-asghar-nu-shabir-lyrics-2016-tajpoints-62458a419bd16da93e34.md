1
Aynj Maa Ne Wekhiya Asghar Nu Shabir Dhi Jholi Pa Ke.
Jiwein Maiyyat Wekhi Jandi Aye Chehray Taun Kafan Hata Ke.
Wasday Soraj Di Garmi Taun Asghar Di Lash Bachani Si.
Karbal Diyaan Tapdiyaan Raitaan Wich Ainay Putar Di Qabar Banani Si.

2
Shah Thandiyaan Thawaan Labda Riya Rukhsar Zameen Te La Ke.
Kithay Asghar Mera Reh Gaya Aye Nayi Aan Rubab Ne Pochiya Si.
Ashor De Din Da Maula Te Aye Sab Taun Okha Wela Si.
Maa Loriyaan Dendi Jandi Si Asghar Da Kurta Cha Ke.

3
Shabeer Bhira Di Ghurbat De Ahsas Nu Dil Wich Rakhiya Aye.
Darya Taun Murdiyaan Asghar Nu Onay Naal Abba De Kajiya Aye.
Ghazi De Kolon Langiya Aye Asghar Di Lash Chupa Ke.
Ohno Qabar Banawan Welay V Karbal Wich Baqiya Yaad Rayi.

4
Kadi Baba Baba Wain Karey Kadi Zahra Zahra Kainda Si.
Hathaan Nal Qabar-e-Asghar Da Shabeer Nishan Mita Ke.
Shabeer Da Rab Hi Janda Aye, Aye Sabar Di Manzil Si Keri.
Munh Ro Ro Chumda Janda Te Shabeer Aye Kainda Janda Si.

5
Seene Te Sawawan Ga Tainu Kuj Dair Che Asghar Aa Ke.
Shayar Ne Jeray Kagazaan Te Nohay Ali Asghar De Likhday.
 Saqlain Adab Nal Chumiya Kar Quran De Wangoon Aye Warqay.
Aye Dhup Wich Bethi Maa De Sir Te Chaan Karday Ne Ja Ke.
