Lash Se Lipati Hoi Kehti Thi Maa Mara Gaya.
Iss Zaeefi Mein Mera Akbar Jawaan Mara Gaya.

1
Aik Betey Se Juri Thi Maa Ki Sari Kainaat.
Aaj Laila Ke Liye Sara Jahaan Mara Gaya.
Ghamzada Bhai Zameen Ko Dekhta Hi Reh Gaya.
Jab Yeh Qatil Ne Kaha Akbar Yahan Mara Gaya.

2
Haye Ghurbat Teen Din Ki Pyas Ka Mara Howa.
Nehar Our Khaimo Ke Bilkul Darmiyaan Mara Gaya.
Ab Duaon Se Koi Laila Ka Nata Hi Nahi.
Jis Ko Jeene Ki Dua Deti Thi Maa Mara Gaya.

3
Ik Nazar Qasad Ko Dekha Our Akbar So Gaye.
Aamad-e-Mehmaan Hoi OUr Mezbaan Mara Gaya.
Ahl-e-Gham Seeno Pe Akbar Hath Apne Maar Kar.
Keh Rahe Hain Zulm Ka Naiza Yahan Mara Gaya.
