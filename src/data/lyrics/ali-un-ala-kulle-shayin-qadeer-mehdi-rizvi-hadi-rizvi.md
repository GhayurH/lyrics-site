1
Ali Un Ala Kulle Shayin Qadeer Mehdi Rizvi Hadi Rizvi
De Rahi Hai Yeh Qudrat Sada Aliun Ala Kulle Shayin Qadeer
Yehi Kehna Hai ALLAH Ka Aliun Ala Kulle Shayin Qadeer
Raat Ko Din Karey Kun Hai Jo Bhi Kahey
Mati Gonday Faqat Our Aadam Banay
Oss Ki Marzi Hai Har Faisla

2
Tha Woh Soraj Tu Kya Tere Dar Ka Gada
Ik Ishara Kiya Our Palta Diya
Phir Woh Soraj Bhi Kehne Laga

3
Ali Azeem, Ali Alam, Ali Janam, Ali Ali
Ali Aala, Ali Bala, Ali Aaqa, Ali Ali
Ali Rehbar, Ali Bartar, Ali Behtar, Ali Ali
Ali Batin, Ali Zahir, Ali Athar, Ali Tahir, Ali Qayam, Ali Qadir
Aayi Terah 13 Rajab Khush Halali They Sab
Zard Sa Ho Gaya Chehra Munkir Ka Tab
Jab Halali Ke Lab Se Suna

4
Pohanchay Arsh-e-Olla Syed Al Osiya
Dil Bohat Khush Howa Jab Nazar Aa Gaya
Sidrat Ul Muntaha Par Likha

5
Woh Nuseri Jo Tha Unn Ko Bola Khuda
Mar Kar Khud Ossay Zindah Bhi Kar Diya
Woh Utha Yeh Hi Kehta Howa
