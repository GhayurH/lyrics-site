Hussain Ka Gham Khwar Jaye Ga Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.

1
Khak-e-Shifa Ke Sath Azadar Jaye Ga.
Tanha Kahan Hussain Ka Ghamkhuwar Jaye Ga.
Aye Ishq-e-Ahl-e-Bait Ki Kashti Ke Munharif.
Kaisay Beghair Nao Ke Oss Paar Jaye Ga.
Behlool Tu Bahisht Bana Our Main Taziya.
Dekhein Gay Kis Taraf Ko Kharedar Jaye Ga.

2
Har Teer Hurmala Tera Bekar Jaye Ga.
Pota Ali Ka Kaisay Bhala Haar Jaye Ga.
Khud Hurmala Ki Taak Mein Bethi Hai Maut Bhi.
Kab Jane Chut Ke Qaid Se Mukhtar Jaye Ga.
Khat Khainch Kar Zameen Pa Abbas Hat Gaye.
Hai Kis Mein Dam Jo Maut Ke Oss Paar Jaye Ga.

3
Paidal Chaley Gi Sari Reaya Yazid Ki.
Naizay Pe Karbala Ka Zameen Daar Jaye Ga.
Laila Jawaan Ki Lash Pe Yeh Keh Ke Gir Pari.
Akbar Tumhara Hijar Humein Mar Jaye Ga.
Guzrey Gi Kya Sakina Ki Nanhi Si Jan Par.
Khaimay Mein Jab Hussain Ka Rahwar Jaye Ga.

4
Khati Rahi Tamachay Behan Mere Samne.
Yeh Dagh Le Ke Qabar Mein Beemar Jaye Ga.
Darya Se Aa Ja Hath Batanay Hussain Ka.
Akbar Ka Lasha Kaisay Alamdar Jaye Ga.
Socha Tha Kis Ne Akhtar-o- Sajjad Yeh Kabhi.
Kunba Nabi Ka Ho Ke Giraftar Jaye Ga.
