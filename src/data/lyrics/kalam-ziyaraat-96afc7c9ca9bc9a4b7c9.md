Tur payi ay shaam Zainab Sajjad day saharay
Shabbir naal koi nai naa Ghazi pehray daar ay

1
Tu vekh veer kitna maahol yeh kharaab ay
Kulsoom Farwa Laila meray naal ay Rubab ay
Majma sharabiyaan da tu aap day jawaab ay
Puchda yazeed(la) keri dhee Haider-e-Karaar ay

2
Sir tay nayi eh sayaa Abbas day Alam da
Pathran di wasdi baarish jis raah ton qaidi langda
Baba day qatilaan ton reya shaam taayiin mangda
Zahra di Paak chadar behni day goshwaray

3
Ro akhay Farwa vekhay tukray jigar Hassan day
Teeran day mee wasaye jhagray paye dafan day
Qismat vakhaye Qasim tukray teray badan day
Main teraan saal bachra gin gin kay din guzaaray

4
Akhay Sakina Bibi gal baahwan paa bhira day
Nib veh si qaid vakhri na fikar kar Sajjad ay
Roundi main marr na jaawan rakhi behn nu tu yaad ay
Har shaam kar saan veeran main tera intezaar ay

5
Tanveer shaam aayi Malka sharam hayaa di
Beghairataan nay keeti hai shehar vich munaadi
Akhay shimr(la) tu paidal turnaa Rasoolzaadi
Yasrab nayi eh Zainab eh shaam da bazaar ay
