Mere Bemar Mola Syed Kazim Hussain Zaidi.
Mere Beemar Maula Tere Jism Se Khon Rista Raha.

1
Zakhm Phattay Rahe Tera Zakhmi Gala Khoon Ugalta Raha.
Jab Sakina Ke Kano Se Gohar Chiney Aap Ke Samne.
Our Tamachon Se Rukhsar Zakhmi Hoye Aap Ke Samne.
Aankhein Pathra Gayin Ashk Khoon Ban Gaye Khon Behta Raha.

2
Be Rida Dekh Kar Bint-e-Zahra Ka Sar, Sar Wahin Jhuk Gaya.
Phir Sada Di Yeh Abbas Ko Aye Chacha Sab Yeh Kaisay Howa.
Aik Lasha Nehar Par Yeh Sun Kar Sada Bas Tarapta Raha.
Sar Khulay Behno Ke Sar Khuli Bibiyaan Dil Pareshan Tha.

3
Majma e Aam Tha Be Rida Aayatein Zakhmi Quran Tha.
Koi Parhtay Hoye Innama, Hal Atta Khon Rota Raha.
Jab Phophi Ke Khulay Sar Pe Aankhein Rukein Khon Se Bhar Gayin.
Pocha Sajjad Ne Ke Rida Kya Hoi Bibiyaan Ro Parin.

4
Kapkapane Laga Dekh Kar Nangay Sar Jaisay Mar Hi Gaya.
Kaisay Bazar Se Main Guzar Paon Ga Main Tu Mar Jaon Ga.
Bibiyaan Sath Hain Kaisay Seh Paon Ga Main Tu Mar Jaon Ga.
Bola Beemar Bas Qabal Bazar Se Mujh Ko Aaye Qaza.

5
Tauq Gardan Mein Tha Paon Mein Beriyaan Hath Mein Hathkari.
Oss Pe Durray Sitamgar Ke Beemar Par Our Noha Gari.
Parh Ke Nad-e-Ali Zulm Sehta Raha Sabar Karta Raha.
Garam Kitni Zameen-e-Sitamgar Thi Pocho Beemar Se.

6
Khoon Ugaltay Rahe Aablon Se Bharay Paon Sajjad Ke.
Shukar Karta Raha Khon Rota Raha Our Chalta Raha.
Sughra Tujh Se Main Akbar Mila Na Saka Oss Ko La Na Saka.
Main Ne Khat Parh Liya Par Tere Bhai Ko Khat Suna Na Saka.

7
Keh Ke Sajjad, Sughra Se Gash Ho Gaya Our Wahin Gir Gaya.
Shor-e-Agyar Mein Our Bazar Mein Bibiyaan Aa Gayin.
Shar Ke Darbar Mein Bazm-e-May Khar Mein Bibiyaan Aa Gayin.
Bas Aye Mirza-o- Kazim Youn Beemar Ka Ashk Ruk Na Saka.
