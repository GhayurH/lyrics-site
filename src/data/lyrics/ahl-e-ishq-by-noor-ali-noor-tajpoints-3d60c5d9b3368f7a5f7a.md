Hamari Maout Bhi Banti Hai Bais-e-Majlis.
Hum Apni Maout Bhi Nazr-e-Imam Karte Hain.
Hum Ahl-e-Ishq Ka Rishta Hai Oss Qabele Se.
Jahan Kate Howay Sir Bhi Kalam Karte Hain.
