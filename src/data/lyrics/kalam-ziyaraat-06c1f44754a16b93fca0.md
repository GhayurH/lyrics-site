Bazar disday rahay sham nairay aandi rahi
Qadam qadam Tay ridawan di aas jandi rahi...

1
Sajad tenu bachawan ch chadraan lagiyan
Meri Sakina day kanna di waaliyan lagiyan
Beemar laal nu aay akh Kay jagandi rahi...

2
Na kidray sham e ghareeban day maray marr jawan
Aay soch rondi si zainab na bal darr jawan
Safeen ghazi di safraan day wich sunandi rahi...

3
Aseer banhwaan nay waala nay chehra kajeya aay
Ajeeb haal ch waqif bhira da mileya aay
Oh Apnay Aap nu mukhtar toun chupaandi rahi...

4
Faraz hussain tay abbas day nibhaye nay
Ditay nay pehray onay khutbay vi sunaye nay
Veeraan nu rondi tay Abid nu vi bachandi rahi...

5
Oh Shaam dy rahwan ty jis shehr wich jandy san
Ayy Kalma gowan di basti ay jaan laindy san
Jadon ve pathran di Barish Siraan ty Landi rahi...

6
Madiny walyan Zahra sa da maanr na rakhya
Ayy Shaam waly kiwain Maanr rakhdy Zainab da
Ayy oho Paishi se Akbar Jehri Dhee Nibhandi rahi..,
