1
Apna Samaj Ke Zehar Pila Dijiye Mujhe.
Uthe Na Ta Keh Aap Ki Janib Nazar Koi.
Jitni Bhi Tohamtein Hain Laga Dijiye Mujhe.
Kyun Aap Ki Khushi Ko Mera Gham Kare Udas.
Ik Talkh Hadsa Hoon Bhula Dijiye Mujhe.
Sidq-o-Safa Ne Mujh Ko Kiya Hai Bohat Kharab.
Makar-o-Riya Zaror Sikha Dijiye Mujhe.
Main Aap Ke Qareeb Hi Hota Hoon Har Ghari.
Moqa Kabhi Pare Tu Sada Dijiye Mujhe.
Har Cheez Dasteyaab Hai Bazar Mein Adam.
Jhoti Khushi Khareed Ke La Dijiye Mujhe.
