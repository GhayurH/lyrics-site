1
Shukriya Ya Fatima Shahid Baltistani
Matami Mujh Ko Banaya Aap Ne Mazloom Ka Shukriya Ya Fatima.
Meri Mati Mein Bhi Rakh Di Aap Ne Khak-e-Shifa Shukriya Ya Fatima.
Jab Bichaya Farsh-e-Matam Hazrat-e-Shabeer Ka.
Pursa Lainay Qabar Se Aaein Mere Ghar Fatima.
Yeh Mera Kacha Makaan Bhi Rashk-e-Janat Ho Gaya Shukriya Ya Fatima.
Dil Bhatakta Tha Mera Dunya Ke Gham Mein Dar Badar.
Aap Ne Kar Ke Meri Kotahiyon Ko Darguzar.
Bakhshwane Ke Liye Karb-o-Bala Bulwa Liya Shukriya Ya Fatima.
Zulm Ka Aiwan Khutbay Se Giraya Aap Ne.
Haq Miley Ya Na Miley Sar Na Jhukaya Aap Ne.
Nusrat-e-Mazloom Ka Hum Ko Dikhaya Rasta Shukriya Ya Fatima.
Jab Bandhi Gardan Mein Rasi Shah-e-Khaybar Geer Ke.
Apne Wali Ko Bachaya Zalimo Se Aap Ne.
Hashar Tak Haider Ke Lab Par Hai Tabhi Yeh Marsiya Shukriya Ya Fatima.
Ahteram-e-Fatima Ka Yeh Mila Oss Ko Sila.
Oss Ka Qad Bhi Anbiya Se Aik Lamhey Mein Barha.
Rukh Baqiya Ki Taraf Kar Ke Yeh Di Hur Ne Sada Shukriya Ya Fatima.
Ja Ke Balway Mein Bachaya Ismat-e-Shabeer Ko.
Tarbiyaat Youn Aap Ne Di Zainab-e-Dilgeer Ko.
Meri Behno Ko Bhi Parday Ka Saleeqa Aa Gaya Shukriya Ya Fatima.
Jab Suni Maa Ne Madine Mein Yeh Zainab Se Khabar.
Ho Gaye Shabeer Pe Qurban Oss Ke Sab Pisar.
Keh Ke Yeh Umm Ul-Baneen Ne Shukar Ka Sajdah Kiya Shukriya Ya Fatima.
Dekhtey Hain Aik Hasrat Se Teri Tooti Lehad.
Our Bhala Darwaish Kya Hogi Kareemi Ki Sand.
Tu Ne Har Ik Matami Ka Dil Baqiya Kar Diya Shukriya Ya Fatima.
