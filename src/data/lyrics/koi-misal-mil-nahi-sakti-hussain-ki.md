Koi Misal Mil Nahi Sakti Hussain Ki

1
Alfaz Tangdast Maani Faqeer Hain
Nutaq-o-Biyaan Ke Harf Matalab Haqeer Hain
Mehdood Zehan Apni Hadoon Mein Aseer Hain
Hum Past Kya Likhein Ke Woh Kitne Ameer Hain
Bas Ik Yehi Misal Hai Zahra Ke Chain Ki
Koi Misal Mil Nahi Sakti Hussain Ki

2
Aagaz-e-Kainat Se Pehlay Thi Iss Ki Zaat
Takhleeq-e-Mojzaat Se Pehle Thi Iss Ki Zaat
Kun Ke Takalfaat Se Pehle Thi Iss Ki Zaat
Yani Har Aik Zaat Se Pehle Thi Iss Ki Zaat
Khaliq Se Pehle Kaise Sanwarti Yeh Kainat
Mula Issay Banate Tu Banti Yeh Kainat

3
Har Shay Abhi Adam Mein Thi Mojod Kuch Na Tha
Tareek-o-Taray Shab Na Sawera Tha Ba-Khuda
Sajda Guzar Noor Faqat Tha Hussain Ka
Jis Jis Jagah Woh Alam-e-Lahoot Mein Jhuka
Khaliq Ne Apne Noor Se oss Ko Saja Liya
Utna Elaqa Arsh-e-Moalla Bana Liya

4
La Shay Ki Mumlikat Mein Sakhi Ne Qadam Rakha
Lahoot Mein Jahan Bane Mula Ke Naqsh-e-Pa
Utha Wahin Wahin Khameer-e-Malaika
Rab-Al-Malaikaat Paron Par Likha Howa
Dunya Samjh Mein Aaya Yeh Rutba Hussain Ka
Jibreel Kyun Jhulatay They Jhola Hussain Ka

5
Apni Sipah Ka Sahib-e-Ayjad Khud Hussain
Seeno Mein Sans Ban Ke Hai Aabad Khud Hussain
Hai Lashkar-e-Hussain Ki Bunyad Khud Hussain
Abbas Khud Hussain Hain Sajjad Khud Hussain
Ban Kar Yaqeen Seeno Ke Andar Hussain Hain
Ik Do Nahi Yeh Poray Bahatar Hussain Hain

6
Tauheed Iss Ke Sajda-e-Aakhir Ka Hai Qayam
Har Waar Iss Ka Adal-e-Imamat Ka Ahtemam
Khon-e-Nabi Shareek-e-Nabowat Hai Gaam Gaam
Mehshar Khuda Ke Zimay Hai Mula Ka Inteqam
Panchon Usool Ka Bhi Yeh Wahid Usool Hai
Shabir Ke Beghair Yeh Sab Kuch Fazool Hai

7
Kehti Thi Karbala Mera Mehman Aa Gaya
Farmarawa Mera, Mera Sultan Aa Gaya
Aayat Le Ke Bolta Quran Aa Gaya
Ab Kaisi Be-Basi Mera Rehman Aa Gaya
Shirkat Bata Rahi Hai Yeh Betay Ameer Ki
Khul Kar Hussain Baat Karein Gaye Ghadeer Ki

8
Akbar Jo Sheh Ne Aakhri Sajda Adda Kiya
Razi Hoon Main Hussain Se Khaliq Ne Di Sada
Aye Ayhad-e-La-Shareek Tu Sajday Se Sar Utha
Utra Hai Teri Karb-o-Bala Mein Tera Khuda
Teri Lahad Mein Aaj Se Khud Ko Basaon Ga
Alam Mein Dosra Main Mualla Banaon Ga
