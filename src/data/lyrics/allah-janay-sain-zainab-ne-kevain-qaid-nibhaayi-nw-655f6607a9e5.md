Allah Janay Sain Zainab Ne, Kevain Qaid Nibhaayi

Kevain Safr Kittay, Baaj Bhiraawan, Hy Dard Sataayi

Abid Nu, Pathran De Wich, Kadi Safraan Ch Sakina, Nu Kadi Bhaina Nu

Qaidi Hathaan De Nal, Saambh Di Reh Gayi, Zahra Di Kamaayi

Apne Hi, Ghar De Wich Jis Dum, Aakay Abdulla Ne Pucheya, Ke Tu Bibi Kon Ay

Bas Ronday Hoya, Khaak Utha Ke, Zainab Ne Uraayi

Zaalim De, Labaan Utay Aaya, Jadon Naa Fatimah Zahra, Da Bharay Majme Wich

Maa Di Azmat Layi, Khutbay Parhdi, Dhi Zahra Di Aayi

Amri De, Naal Mil Ke, Jevain Rondi Ay Koi Dhi, Aiway Zainab Royi

Jadon Maa Di Rida, Waqt Rihaai, Gal Sain Ne Laayi

Jadh Wi Suraj, Sawairay Charda Si, Aap Jukh Jaandi Si Wich Haal-e Rako De’ Zainab

Enjh Baalan Uthay, Rayi Chaawan Kardi, Hy Zahra Di Jaayi

Aj Wi Saqlain, Janaab E Mehdi, Ona Raawan Ton Hataande, Oday Pairaan De Nishan

Jinna Rawaan Uthay, Shaamiyan Zainab, Kalli Qaidi Turaayi
