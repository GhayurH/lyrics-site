1
Jab Ap Ayeinge Mukhtar Hussain Fatehpuri
Galay Milo Sab Darood Parh Kar Hasad Ki Dewar Toor Walo
Dilon Mein Bugz-o-Hasad Raha Tu Hussain Ka Gham Kahan Rahe Ga
Labon Pe Ghaibat Dilon Mein Nafrat Faraibi Aankhein Makan Gasabi
Bola Rahe Ho Jissay Batao Woh Aa Gaya Tu Kahan Rahe Ga?
Utha Ke Gaib Ka Pardah Jab Aap Aaein Gay
Dikhai De Ga Sawera Jab Aap Aaein Gay
Sikhaya Kis Ne Tha Godi Mein Bolna Unn Ko
Yeh Khud Bataein Gay Esa Jab Aap Aaein Gay
Sakoon Se Betha Ho Pehlu Mein Shair Ke Aaho
Yeh Dekhney Ko Miley Ga Jab Aap Aaein Gay
Abhi Tu Nikla Hai Soraj Magar Andhera Hai
Naseeb Hoga Ujala Jab Aap Aaein Gay
Nikalay Jaein Gay Ahl-e-Nifaq Qabron Se
Nabi Ko Chain Miley Ga Jab Aap Aaein Gay
Khajorein Chak e Shikam Se Nikali Jaein Gi
Hisab Hoga Fidak Ka Jab Aap Aaein Gay
Kabhi Ali Ki Zubaan Se Jo Loag Suntay They
Sunai De Ga Woh Khutba Jab Aap Aaein Gay
Abhi Tu Dhop Mein Fatwon Ki Hain Abu Talib
Shajar Bhi Orhay Ga Saya Jab Aap Aaein Gay
