Hay wiraan ghurran wich, roway bimar kharri
Aaisi wichri na milli feer Ali Akbar nu Kadi

1
Weer Wichray Na Kesay Beh, Ne Da Akbar Wagoon
Maut Ban Janda Hur Khaab Dhukhy Behna Da

2
Meaa Bimar Wiran Keenway, Teray Kool Anwa
Baat Karbal Di Madeenai Tu A Dushwar Buri

3
Qasda Janwee Tay Akbar Nu, Kaween Ro Ro Kai
Sanwa Gin Di Haoi Humsheer Nu Mil Ja Chayti

4
Allah Janay Keenway Nib Gaee, Odi Kulyaan Akhtar
Jaag Kay Raatan Guzaray Sain Khowaban Di Duri

5
Aja Purdesia Wira Mae, Teray Rah Weekaan
Jora Shugna Da Bunaya Mai Weran Teray Lai
