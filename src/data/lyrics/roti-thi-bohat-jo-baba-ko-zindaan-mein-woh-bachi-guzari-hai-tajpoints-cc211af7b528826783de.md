1
Roti Thi Bohat Jo Baba Ko Zindaan Mein Woh Bachi Guzari Hai.
Iss Char Baras Ki Umar Mein Woh Rab Jane Kitna Tarpi Hai.
Godi Mein Sakina Ko Le Kar Mula Farmate The Logo.
Woh Ghar Hi Nahi Hai Mere Liye Jis Ghar Mein Meri Bachi Na Ho.

2
Dunya Kya Jane Yeh Bachi Mazloom Ko Kitni Pyari Hai.
Woh Waqt Ajeeb Tha Mula Par Koi Na Raha Jab Apno Mein.
Tab Aakhri Rukhsat Ki Khatir Shabir Jo Aaye Khaimo Mein.
Baba Se Lipat Kar Dair Talak Oss Waqt Yeh Bachi Royi Hai.

3
Woh Sham-e-Ghareeban Thi Zalim Jab Khaimo Ke Andar Aaye.
Fursat Hi Kahan Thi Yeh Kehte Aye Bachi Utaro Dur Apne.
Youn Khainchte Hain Dur Kano Se Haye Baba Chilai Hai.
Pehchan Sake Na Apne Bhi Kuch Hal Hai Aisa Bachi Ka.

4
Shabir Ki Oss Shehzadi Ke Kano Se Laho Bhi Hai Rista.
Taseer Tamacho Ki Dekho Rukhsar Ki Rangat Neeli Hai.
Sab Chotay Bare Ik Rasi Mein Hai Oss Ke Galay Mein Bhi Rasi.
Maqtal Se Kofay Ki Janib Le Jaye Jate Hain Qaidi.

5
Sin Kam Jo Hai Masooma Ka Uthti Hai Kabhi Gir Jati Hai.
Zindan Mein Aksar Dekha Ke Asif Woh Na Sone Payi Hai.
Uth Bethi Khuwab Se Chonk Ke Woh Jab Neend Kabhi Ossay Aayi Hai.
Baba Baba Keh Kar Roti Our Sab Ko Sath Rolati Hai.
