Aao Tareekh Sunaon Main Shab-e-Hijrat Ki.
Kis Tarha Saye Mein Talwaron Ke Soye The Ali.

1
Taig Ki Chaon Mein Sona Koi Aasan Nahi.
Kul-e-Emaan Hai Ali Aam Musalman Nahi.
Dosra Hota Tu Bedar Nahi Hota Kabhi.
Chand Se Yeh Abu Talib Ka Qamar Kehta Tha.

2
Aaj Ki Raat Ibadat Ka Hai Andaz Niya.
Aaj ALLAH Se Sote Howay Batein Hoon Gi.
Bas Yehi Soch Ke Hamla Nahi Kar Pate The.
Sare Kufar Isharon Mein Yehi Kehte The.

3
Hain Nabi Soye Howay Jaga Howa Ho Ga Ali.
Han Yehi Siddique Hai Our Yehi Ameen Hain Logo.
Kitna Haider Pe Bharosa Hai Nabi Ko Dekho.
Fatima Jaisi Amanat Bhi Ali Ko De Di.

4
Haj Adda Aa Ke Farishton Ne Kiya Hai Apna.
So Giya Bistar-e-Ahmad Pe Ameer-e-Kaba.
Aaj Kabay Ki Tarha Ourh Ke Kali Kamli.
Ban Ke Soein Gaye Ali Aaj Nabi Hairat Kya.

5
Waqiya Yaad Karo Shaib-e-Abi Talib Ka.
Inn Ke Bistar Pe Tu Sone Ke Ali Hain Aadi.
Ayatein Karti Hain Yeh Aaj Bhi Ayelan Ali.
Shab-e-Bedari Teri Neend Pe Qurban Ali.

6
Tere Sone Se Hoi Deen-e-Khuda Ki Chandi.
:MIR SAJJAD: Woh Jab Subha Madine Ko Chala.
Rasta Rokta Aa Kar Jo Mere Mula Ka.
Sare Makay Mein Na Tha Inta Bahadur Koi.
