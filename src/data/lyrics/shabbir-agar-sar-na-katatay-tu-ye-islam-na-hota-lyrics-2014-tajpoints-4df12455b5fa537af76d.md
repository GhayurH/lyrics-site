1
Shabbir Agar Sar Na Katatay Tu Ye Islam Na Hota.
Matam Se Na Munh Phairo Aray Oh Namaziyo Shabbir Jo Sajdah Na Bachatay Tu Yeh Islam Na Hota.
Sajday Mein Paray Aisay Keh Phir Sar Na Uthaya.
Nezay Pe Charh Ke Shah Ne Quran Sunaya.
Akbar Ne Di Aazan Jo Subh-e-Ashor Ko Shakal-e-Nabi Aazaan Na Sunatay Tu Yeh Islam Na Hota.
Zainab Ne Khud Ujar Ke Dein Ko Bacha Liya.
Chadar Luti Tu Khak Ka Burqa Bana Liya.
Zakhmo Se Choor Le Ke Aseeron Ka Qafila Sajjad Agar Sham Na Jate Tu Yeh Islam Na Hota.
Sajjad Yeh Kehte They Tum Mujh Ko Saza Do.
Yeh Zahra Ki Hai Ladli Zainab Ko Rida Do.
Bazaron Mein Darbaron Mein Namoos-e-Muhammad Sadmay Agar Na Jaan Pe Uthatay Tu Yeh Islam Na Hota.
Matam Gunah Hai Mulla Fatway Laga Gaya.
Our Matami Hussain Ke Pursoon Mein Aa Gaya.
Momin Tu Janati Hai Yeh Zahra Ka Faisla Sadaat Agar Khud Ko Bachatay Tu Yeh Islam Na Ho.
 Akhtar Ka Likha Noha Parhta Hai Zamana.
Alfaz Issay Deta Hai Panjtan Ka Gharana.
Haider Ki Betiyon Ke Malak Pursa Daar Hain Nori Saf-e-Matam Na Bichatay Tu Yeh Islam Na Hota
