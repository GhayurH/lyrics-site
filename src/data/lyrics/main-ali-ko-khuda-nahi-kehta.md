Hai Raaz-e-Hoo Isse Seene Mein Tham Kar Rakho
Agar Ho Mast-e-Alastu Iss Ko Barmala Na Kaho
Bas Iss Liye Main Ali Koo Khuda Nahi Kehta
Keh Khud Ali Ne Kaha Mujhe Khuda Na Kaho
