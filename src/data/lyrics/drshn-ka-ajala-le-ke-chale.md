Darshan ka ujala ley keh chaley, ankhon main andhera chor gaye
Akbar ko kahan ki jaldi thi, baba ko akeyla chor gaye

1
Asghar ko thi ran ki dhun mein kahan, maata ke dharaktey dil ki khabar
Nannhey se sipahi khaemey mein, tehra hua jhoola chor gaye

2
Abbas ne dariya chheen ke bhi, bachchon hi ki khatir mashk bhari
Pyase they magar pyase hi phirey, dariya ko chalakta chor gaye

3
Qaedi to buhut se dekhey hain, poochey koi un ke jee se magar
Jo goad ke paaley bachon ko, maidaan mein sota chor gaye

4
Sar kaat ke Koofey walo ney, ye aur naya aphraad kiya
Maidaan ki jalti raeti per, Shabbir ka lasha chor gaye
