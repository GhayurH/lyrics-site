1
Madinay Walay Naat Khurram Murtaza.
Tere Darbar Mein Kuch Laon Bhala Kyun Lab Par.
Ya Nabi Tujh Ko Mere Dil Ki Hai Jab Sari Khabar.
Khoob Darbar Tumhara Hai Madine Walay.
Jahan Bin Mangay Hi Milta Hai Madine Walay.
Rizq Din Raat Jo Makhlooq-e-Khuda Khati Hai Na.
Tere Bachoon Ka Woh Sadqa Hai Madine Walay.
Tera Qudrat Ne Banaya Nahi Saya Phir Bhi.
Do Jahan Par Tera Saya Hai Madine Walay.
Anbiya Oss Ke Hi Qatron Se Sabhi Khalq Hoye.
Tere Rukh Par Jo Paseena Hai Madine Walay.
Abu Talib Hoon Khadija Hoon Ya Hoon Umm-e-Salma.
Naat Mein Inn Ka Bhi Hissa Hai Madine Walay.
Arsh Par Tujh Ko Bola Kar Jo Diya Khaliq Ne.
Fatima Zahra Woh Tohfa Hai Madine Walay.
Anbiya Dekh Ke Kehte Hain Ali Ke Sar Par.
Aap Ka Jachta Amama Hai Madine Walay.
Koi Ashab Mein Salman-o-Abuzar Sa Nahi.
Jo Isharey Tere Samjha Hai Madine Walay.
Hichkiyaan Aa Rahi Hain Yeh Jo Musalsal Mujh Ko.
Kya Madine Ka Bulawa Hai Madine Walay?.
Likh Asad Dunya Ki Thokar Se Woh Gir Saktay Nahi.
Jin Ka Hath Aap Ne Thama Hai Madine Walay.
