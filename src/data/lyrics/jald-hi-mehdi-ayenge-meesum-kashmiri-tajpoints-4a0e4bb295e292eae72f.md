Jald Hi Mehdi Ayenge Meesum Kashmiri.
Bach Na Sakein Gay Dushman-e-Zahra Jald Hi Mehdi Aaeingay.

1
Adal-e-Ali Dekhey Ga Zamana Jald Hi Mehdi Aaeingay.
Hogi Namaz-e-Esa e Mariyam Yousuf-e-Zahra Ke Pichey.
Karne Imamat Bar Sar-e-Darya Jald Hi Mehdi Aaeingay.
Zikar Ali Ka Khul Ke Karein Gay Mimber-e-Kaba Par Qayam.

2
Jashan Ghadeeri Tarz Ka Hoga Jald Hi Mehdi Aaeingay.
Kufar Ke Rukh Par Hoga Paseena Zulm Bhi Thar Thar Kanpay Ga.
Raaj Faqat Mazloom Ka Hoga Jald Hi Mehdi Aaeingay.
Qabar Se Sab Aada Ko Utha Kar Lein Gay Woh Badlah Sarwar Ka.

3
Zindah Jalein Gay Dushman-e-Zahra Jald Hi Mehdi Aaeingay.
Ishq-e-Ali Mein Qatal Hoye Hain Jab Bhi Hussaini Sajday Mein.
Unn Ka Lahu Har Bar Pukara Jald Hi Mehdi Aaeingay.
Mil Ke Karein Gay Ahl-e-Azza Tameer Baqi e Aqdas Ki.

4
Hoga Wahan Phir Matam-e-Maula Jald Hi Mehdi Aaeingay.
Rajat Ho Tu Mashk Ke Badlay Taig Sakina Dein Gi Unhein.
Hoga Jarri Abbas Ka Hamla Jald Hi Mehdi Aaeingay.
Shah-e- Iram Ke Chahney Walay Jis Mein Rahein Gay Khush Meesum.

5
Jald Hi Aaye Ga Woh Zamana Jald Hi Mehdi Aaeingay.
