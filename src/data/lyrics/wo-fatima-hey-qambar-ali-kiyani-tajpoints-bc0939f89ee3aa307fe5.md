1
Wo Fatima Hey Qambar Ali Kiyani Lyrics
Azzal Se Jis Ki Rida Ke Saye Mein Kibriya Hai Woh Fatima Hai.
Zaman Se Pehle Jo Noor Haq Se Juda Howa Hai Woh Fatima Hai.
Salamati Ki Woh Raat Jis Mein Zamane Taqdeer Pa Rahe Hain.

2
Malaika Our Rooh Jis Ke Zameen Pe Farman La Rahe Hain.
Woh Lailatul Qadar Jis Ka Dawa Ma Adraka Hai Woh Fatima Hai.
Tu Sirf Hafiz Hai Aayaton Ka Woh Aayaton Ki Mohafiza Hai.
Woh Rotiyaan Bhejein Tu Khuda Bhi Falak Se Aayat Bhaijta Hai.

3
Tu Jis Ke Sadqay Mein Sorah-e-Dehar Parh Raha Hai Woh Fatima Hai.
Sada Yeh Mariyam Ki Aa Rahi Thi Dua Karo Woh Na Bad Dua De.
Mubahila Jo Bapa Howa Tu Na Zindah Esayi Bach Sakein Gay.
Main Lakh Esa Ki Maa Hoon Lekin Tumhein Pata Hai Woh Fatima Hai.

4
Kaha Khuda Ne Keh Aye Muhammad Tere Liye Yeh Jahan Basaya.
Ossay Banaya Ali Ki Khatir Ali Ko Oss Ke Liye Banaya.
Khuda Muhammad Ko Jis Ke Barey Mein Keh Raha Hai Woh Fatima Hai.
Hasan Pe Aman-o-Amma Ki Dolat Hussain Par Kahf Ki Woh Aayat.

5
Zaban-e-Zainab Pe Hukam Uskut Jalal Kulsoom Par Qanat.
Jo Bazu-e-Ba Wafa Pe Likhi Hoi Dua Hai Woh Fatima Hai.
Khuda Malaik Se Keh Raha Tha Woh Oss Ke Baba Woh Oss Ka Shohar.
Suno Suno Aasman Walo Woh Oss Ke Betay Woh Oss Ki Chadar.

6
Har Aik Masoom Jis Ke Hone Se Innama Hai Woh Fatima Hai.
Hussain Ke Gham Mein Roney Wali Khuda Se Zahra Ne Qaum Mangi.
So Teen Shaban Ki Qasam Hai, Hai Kaun Khaliq Jo Pochay Koi.
Main Barmala Yeh Kahon Ga Akbar Woh Fatima Hai Woh Fatima Hai.
