Zikar Jab Zainab-e-Muztar Ka Bayaan Hota Hai.
Khon Sajjad Ki Aankhon Se Rawaan Hota Hai.

1
Dast-e-Shabeer Se Be Sheer Ne Sabit Yeh Kiya.
Hashmi Khon Tu Bachpan Se Jawaan Hota Hai.
Nook-e-Naiza Se Lehad Mein Se Nikalein Lasha.
Zulm Aisa Yeh Zamane Mein Kahan Hota Hai.

2
Bhai Beton Ke Hoon Be Goor-o-Kafan Lashay Paray.
Aisay Aalam Mein Qayamat Ka Samaa Hota Hai.
Fatima Zahra Wahan Khud Hi Pohanch Jati Hain.
Zikar-e-Hasnain Shareefain Jahan Hota Hai.

3
Aao Sajjad Ki Aankhon Se Lahu Tu Dekho.
Manzar-e-Karb-o-Bala Taza Ayyaan Hota Hai.
Tu Ameer Aik Nahi Shah Ke Gham Mein Shamil.
Mehway Gham Aaj Tu Har Kaun-o-Makaan Hota Hai.
