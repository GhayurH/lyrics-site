Haq Mor Mera Pehchan Menu Mera Rakhiya Naam Khuda Zahra.
Tu Mangiyaan Gawahiyaan Mere Taun Le Aayi Pak Gawah Zahra.

1
Kun Aakh Dewaan Jay Main Ro Ke Rahway Dunya Te Kuj Na Baqi.
Rab Morda Nayi Sun Ghasaba Toon Karey Hath Cha Ke Jay Dua Zahra.
Jageer Mere Naa Khuld Di Aye Loh Laak Di Waris Haye Baba.
Ditti Musalmana Haye Baaj Tere Darbar De Wich Jhutla Zahra.

2
Hasnain Sunaya Haal Jadon Nanay Nu Maa Di Peshi Da.
Parh Nad-e-Ali Yaseen Teri Aayi Khutbay Wekh Suna Zahra.
Qunfuz Ne Mere Hath Toray Chariyaan De Naal Ne Haye Baba.
Mera Ghar Jaliya Burqa Jaliya Aynj Seh Gayi Zulm Jafa Zahra.

3
Main Jan Gayiyaan Hun Baaj Mere Zainab Di Chadar Nayi Bachni.
Aayi Wich Darbar De Tak Logo Putraan Di Surkh Abba Zahra.
Parh Ism Khuda Tosif Jadon Khutbay Da Ain Aagaz Hoya.
Oss Welay Matami Babay Di Ban Gayi Sab Layi Aye Qaza Zahra.
