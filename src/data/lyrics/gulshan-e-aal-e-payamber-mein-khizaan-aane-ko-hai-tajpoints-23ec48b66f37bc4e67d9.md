Haidar-o-Safdar Ja Saya Sar Se Uth Jane Ko Hai.

1
Aaj Zainab Ko Nazar Aane Laga Bazar-e-Sham.
Fatima Kya Phir Kissi Darbar Mein Jane Ko Hai.
Zainab-o-Kulsoom Rakhna Hosle Apne Buland.
Aaj Phir Koi Qayamat Ki Khabar Aane Ko Hai.
Jab Giray Ghore Se Ghazi Tu Sakin Ne Kaha.
Dur Mere Our Chadar-e-Tatheer Chin Jane Ko Hai.

2
Nanay Nanay Bazuon Mein Daal Kar Choti Si Mashk.
Shair-e-Haq Abbas Ki Kuch Shan Dikhane Ko Hai.
Aaj Ki Shab Roza-e-Khatam-e-Rasul Hil Jaye Ga.
Ibn-e-Muljim Aaj Kuch Aisa Ghazab Dhane Ko Hai.
Zainab-o-Kulsom Ko Seene Se Kar Lo Ab Juda.
Baap Ke Chehre Ki Rangat Ab Badal Jane Ko Hai.

3
Chah Gayi Hai Kyun Udasi Shabar-o-Shabir Par.
Saaf Zahir Hai Yateemi Sar Pe Chah Jane Ko Hai.
Karbala Walon Ki Shayad Tashnagi Ka Hai Khiyal.
Saqi-e-Kausar Zara Pehle Chale Jane Ko Hai.
Ta Qayamat Khoon Roye Gi Zameen-e-Karbala.
Zulm Ki Kali Ghata Woh Sar Pe Chah Jane Ko Hai.
