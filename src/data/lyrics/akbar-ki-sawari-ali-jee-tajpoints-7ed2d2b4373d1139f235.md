Wawela Haye Wawela.

1
Na Ja Akbar Na Ja.
Khaimon Se Sadaein Aati Hain.
Akbar Ki Sawari Jati Hai.
Khaimon Mein Bapa Hai Wawela.
Hum Shakal-e-Payamber Jata Hai Dil Baap Ka Doba Jata Hai.
Sar Peet Rahi Hai Maa Dukhiya Akbar Ki Sawari Jati Hai.

2
Atharah Baras Nazoon Se Jisay Parwan Charhaya Zainab Ne.
Gulshan Hai Ujra Zainab Ka Akbar Ki Sawari Jati Hai.
Kehti Thi Sakina Ro Ro Kar Laut Aao Bhai Ali Akbar.
Qadmo Se Lipati Masoma Akbar Ki Sawari Jati Hai.
Akbar Se Sakina Jab Lipati Shabeer Ko Sughra Yaad Aayi.
Ossay Kaisay Bataye Yeh Baba Akbar Ki Sawari Jati Hai.

3
Kuch Dair Ruko Beta Akbar Main Tum Ko Bana Tu Loon Dulha.
Afsoos Na Raas Aaya Sehra Akbar Ki Sawari Jati Hai.
Teri Yaad Bohat Tarpaye Gi Maa Jeetay Jee Mar Jaye Gi.
Madar Ke Labon Par Hai Noha Akbar Ki Sawari Jati Hai.
Sheh Kehte They Jao Akbar Mujhe Dekhtey Rehna Mur Mur Kar.
Deta Hai Sada Borha Baba Akbar Ki Sawari Jati Hai.
