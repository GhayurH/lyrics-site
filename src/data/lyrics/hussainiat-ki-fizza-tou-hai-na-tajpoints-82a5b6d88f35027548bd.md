1
Hussainiat Ki Fizza Tou Hai Na.
Sada Sada Yeh Sada Tou Hai Na.
Zameen Hai Aasman Se Behter.
Keh Oss Pe Farsh-e-Azza Tou Hai Na.

2
Hussain Ke Dar Se Khak Le Lo.
Keh Khak Mein Bhi Shifa Tou Hai Na.
Dua-e-Zahra Baray-e-Momin.
Karm Ki Yeh Inteha Tou Hai Na.

3
Hussain Ka Bhi Parho Na Kalma.
Hussain Bhi Mustafa Tou Hai Na.
Yeh Dil Musalsal Dharak Raha Hai.
Sakhi Ka Matam Bapa Tou Hai Na.

4
Ali Ki Ulfat Baghair-e-Sajda.
Adda Bhi Ho Par Qaza Tou Hai Na.
Janab-e- Shaukat Ka Fun Kahan Hai.
Ali Wali Ki Atta Tou Hai Na.
