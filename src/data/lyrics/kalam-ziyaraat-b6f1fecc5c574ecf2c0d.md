Ali walay hain ham ham ko mitanay kon aaye ga
Mawaddat ke charaghon ko bujhanay kon aaye ga

1
Ali ko tu ne mana hi nahi mushkil kusha waaez
Bata ab nazaa may teray sarhanay kon aaye ga

2
Payambar is liye bhi mutmayin thay jang e Khaibar may
Bhanwar may deen ki kashti bachnay bachanay kon aaye ga

3
Himayat ghasibon ki karnay walo kuch ye socha hay
Tumhay naar e Jahannum se bachanay kon aaye ga

4
Ghulaman e Abu Talib hain ham dekhain gay mehshar may
Wahan par kufr ke fatway laganay kon aaye ga

5
Abu Talib ke beton ki tarha Islam ki khaatir
Khud apnay khoon se maqtal sajanay kon aaye ga

6
Kaha ho ga Ali ke zakhm se girtay huay khoon ne
Rida e Zainab e Muztar bachnay kon aaye ga

7
Ali ne chahnay walon se shayed ye bhi poocha ho
Meray Shabbir ka lasha uthanay kon aaye ga

8
Tamachay Shimr ke kha kar Sakina roey gi jab bhi
Sakina ko sitamgar se bachanay kon aaye ga

9
Zafar meri tasalli ke liye maloom hay mujh ko
Meri turbat may bakhshish ke bahanay kon aaye ga
