Zainab tayray dardan toon yeh haal ho gaya aye
Sajjad Dai Kafan Da,  Rang Laal Ho Gaya Aye

1
1) Akhai Sajjad Mola pavain akhari ni savaan
Bhuliyan ajai vi mainu nahin Shaam di a ravan
Zainab tera kyun perda,  Pamaal Ho Gaya Aye

2
2) Darday yateem pa ker chumain pioo da hai janaza
Hairaan kul khudai wagda ai khoon taza
Abid zulam anokha,  taray naal ho gaya hai

3
3) Sadmain nai lahu rovandai bazar jarn valai
Tukrai/Dukhrai?? Jigar nu kha gayai derbar jarn valai
Akhiyon chon dil da zahir,  aio ho haal ho gaiya aye

4
4) Kassiyan hoyan nai rassiyan massom gardana tai
Kuch reham khao logo majboor qaidiyan tai
Piyasa baihosh sada,  her baal ho gaiya aye

5
5) Untha di rassiyan nu chad kai jai benda qaidi
Barsat koraiyan di baitab sehnda qaidi
Kerbal chon shaam tayeen,  Bai Haal ho gaiya aye

6
6) Kita salaam abid, Ghabraa gaia sharabi
Darbar nu hila gai, guftar buturabi
????oon vailay,  lajpaal ho gaya ai

7
7) Pathran nai keeta zakhmi Zainab dai paak sar nu
Bijihiyan hoyan nai zulfaan Zainab dikhavai her nu
Aabid tarap tarap kai,  Bai Haal Ho gaiya aye
