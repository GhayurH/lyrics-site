Karbala say sham tak Sajjad Khon Rota raha
Dekhy lashy bekafan ZAINAB ko dekha be rida

1
Zakham hi zakham thy pao me chalay kasay bimar
Kar diya bora u say Jis nay wo hai sham bazar

2
Raho me Ro Ro k wo ZAINAB ki chadar mangta
Lash Akbar ki jawan dekhi waha Khon me ter

3
Nok e niza pay chara dekha hai asghar ka bi sir
Dekha sir Abbas ka ghoray ki garden me para

4
Aisay abid pay karo zulm ye uth na pay
Naam lety raho ZAINAB ka esay gush aye
Mar na Jay e raho ne ZAINAB tanha asra

5
Nok e neza pay rida es ko dikhaty jao
Sham tak Khon mohari ko rolaty jao

6
Har qadam pay ye kahy hay sham ZAINAB berida
Ghr jo ZAINAB ko bazaro me na laty mehdi

7
Khon e shabir ko Sajad na bhola tha kabhi
ZAINAB o Sajad k khotbo say zindan deen howa
