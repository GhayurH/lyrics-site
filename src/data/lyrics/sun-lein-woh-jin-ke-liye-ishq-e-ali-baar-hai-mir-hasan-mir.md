1
Sun Lein Woh Jin Ke Liye Ishq-e-Ali Baar Hai Mir Hasan Mir
Aaj Bhi Parday Mein Ik Haider-e-Karrar Hai
Oss Ko Hai Sab Ki Khabar Rakhta Hai Sab Par Nazar
Kaun Hai Ghaddar Our Kaun Wafadar Hai

2
Qayal-e-Shaitaan Tu Hai Qayal-e-Ghaibat Nahi
Haye Musalman Bhi Kitna Samajhdar Hai
Lagta Hai Taig-e-Ali Guzri Hai Yaan Se Abhi
Ran Mein Jo Charon Taraf Lashon Ka Anbar Hai

3
Lakh Dawaein Karein Theek Na Ga Kabhi
Aal-e-Muhammad Ke Jo Bugz Mein Beemar Hai
Munkir-e-Ashk-e-Azza Soch Le Itna Zarra
Gaib Mein Betha Hai Jo Woh Bhi Azadar Hai
