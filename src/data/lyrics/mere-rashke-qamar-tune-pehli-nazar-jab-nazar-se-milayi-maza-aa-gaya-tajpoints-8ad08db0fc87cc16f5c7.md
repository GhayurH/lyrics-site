Mere Rashke Qamar Tune Pehli Nazar Jab Nazar Se Milayi Maza Aa Gaya.
Barq Si Gir Gayi Kam Hi Kar Gayi Aag Aisi Lagai Maza Aa Gaya.

1
Jaam Mein Ghool Kar Hussan Ki Mastiyaan Chandni Muskurai Maza Aa Gaya.
Chand Ke Saye Mein Aye Mere Saqiya Tu Ne Aisi Pilayi Maza Aa Gaya.
Nasha Sheshe Mein Angrai Lene Laga Bazm-e-Rindaan Mein Sagar Khanakne Lage.
May Kaday Pe Barsane Lagin Mastiyaan Jab Ghata Ghir Ke Chayi Maza Aa Gaya.

2
Be-Hijabana Woh Samne Aa Gaye Our Jawani Jawani Se Takra Gayi.
Aankh Unn Ki Lari Youn Meri Aankh Se Dekh Kar Yeh Larai Maza Aa Gaya.
Shaikh Sahib Ka Eman Bik Hi Giya Dekh Ke Husan-e-Saqi Pighal Hi Gaya.
Aaj Se Pehle Yeh Kitne Magroor The Lut Gayi Parsai Maza Aa Gaya.

3
Aankh Mein Thi Haya Har Mulaqat Par Surkh Aariz Howay Wasal Ki Baat Par.
Oss Ne Sharma Ke Mere Sawalaat Pe Aise Gardan Jhukai Maza Aa Gaya.
Aye Fanaa Shukar Hai Aaj Bad-e-Fanaa Oss Ne Rakh Li Mere Pyar Ki Aabro.
Apne Hathon Se Oss Ne Meri Qabar Par Chadar-e-Gul Charhai Maza Aa Gaya.
