1
Zahra Ki Betiyon Ki Ridaein Utar Gayin.
Sajad Ko Zaeef Sadaein Yeh Kar Gayin.
Lashoon Ko Rasiyon Mein Jakar Kar Liye Phiro.
Syedaniyaan Tu Ghar Se Nikalte Hi Mar Gayin.

2
Jatein Thin Jo Kabhi Kabhi Zahra Ki Qabar par.
Jane Khuda Hujoom Se Kaise Guzar Gayin.
Zulm-o-Sitam Ki Taiz Hawaein Thin Jab Chalein.
Sab Gulshan-e-Batool Ki Kaliyaan Bikhar Gayin.

3
Gudoon Se Rah-e-Shaam Mein Gir Gir Ke Mar Gay.
Bachoon Ke Lashey Dekh Ke Maein Bhi Mar Gayin.
Ramlah Yeh Pochti Thi Sakina Se Qaid Mein.
Zakhmi Hain Kaan Baliyaan Bibi Kidhar Gayin.

4
Bazar Leke Jao Na Be Pardah Zalimo.
Mar Jaein Gi Hussain Ki Behnein Agar Gayin.
 Sarwar Tu Kiya Likhe Ga Nabi Zadiyon Ke Gham.
Samjho Ke Shaam Jane Se Pehle Hi Mar Gayin.
