Maar ditta lokan, Baba mera bhira.

1
1. Sain Ghazi di vain paway, Karbal vich baajh rida.

2
2. Eh wakat kissay te na aaway, jhaira aj karbal ich aaya.
Ik saang te meri chadar si, ik saang te amdri jaya.
Main odhay sir nun takdi rahi, oh chadar takda raya.

3
3. Main zeen tay aap sawar kitta, wagan Kalsoum ne phariyan.
Jithay veer kharay san kal tha hi, othay teriyan nuhaan khariyan.
Akhri wailay khaimiyan chon, injh turya veer mera.

4
4. Keewein noor gawaya ankhiyan da, Akbar Akbar karda si.
Keewein lash te pohonchya Akbar di, keewein digda si uthda si.
Thori phelan jay ton aanda eh manzar khud takda.

5
5. Nu lakh teeran talwaran ne, odhay badan te zarban laiyan.
Bhar bhar ke jholiyan shamiyan nay, zakhman tay raitan paiyan.
Lash tay ghoray bhajday rahay, raya takda poor mera.

6
6. Jhairay teeran naiziyan ton bach gaye, oh ghair laye san bhawan.
Barhay okhay rakh te khamiyan di, oh baal suwaiye mawan.
Baal o saray jaag gaye payi ne, hun aakay aap suwa.

7
7. Akbar eh aakhya haider ne, Zainab day athru chum kay.
Na haal suna mainnu zakhman day, main janda haan ghum tere.
Naal Zahra day takda raya, main sara sabar tera.
