1
Aakhri Raat Hai Sakina Ki Lyrics Write Up
Shama Zindan Mein Manga De Koi Aakhri Raat Hai Sakina Ki.
Ja Rahi Hai Hussain Ki Beti Aakhri Raat Hai Sakina Ki.
Yeh Jo Gardan Pe Hai Rasan Ka Nishaan Sath Turbat Mein Iss Ki Jaye Ga.

2
Koi Umm-e-Rubab Se Keh De Ab Yeh Chehra Nazar Na Aaye Ga.
Aaj Jee Bhar Ke Dekh Lo Bibi Aakhri Raat Hai Sakina Ki.
Pas Baba Ke Jane Wali Hai Ab Tamachay Na Koi Marey Ga.
Qabar Mein Tu Ghamo Ki Mari Ke Koi Gohar Nahi Utaray Ga.

3
Ja Ke Turbat Mein Chain Paye Gi Aakhri Raat Hai Sakina Ki.
Hai Janab-e-Ameer Ki Poti Inteha Iss Pe Hai Ghareebi Ki.
Alam-e-Naza Ho Gaya Tari Sans Rukhney Lagi Hai Bani Ki.
Reesma Ab Tu Khol Do Iss Ki Aakhri Raat Hai Sakina Ki.

4
Roney Wali Ki Chalti Sansoon Ko Aaj Iss Tarha Roka Jata Hai.
Band Ho Jaye Shoor Giriya Ka Sar-e-Shabeer Laya Jata Hai.
Hai Mulaqaat Baap Beti Ki Aakhri Raat Hai Sakina Ki.
Shoor Yeh Beriyon Ka Kehta Hai Koi Uthta Hai Koi Girta Hai.

5
Hai Khabar Yeh Ghareeb Bhai Ko Dam Behan Ka Nikalne Wala Hai.
Abhi Le Gi Yeh Aakhri Hichki Aakhri Raat Hai Sakina Ki.
Ro Ke Sajjad Khud Se Kehte They Kaisay Maiyyat Uthaon Ga Iss Ki.
Qaid Zanjeer Mein Hain Hath Mere Qabar Kaisay Banaon Ga Iss Ki.

6
Be Kafan Jaye Gi Behan Meri Aakhri Raat Hai Sakina Ki.
Kaisa Manzar Tha Qaid Mein Akbar Chaar Janib Tha Shoor Giriya Ka.
La Ke Sajjad Ne Sakina Ki Gaud Mein Sar Jo Rakha Baba Ka.
Sar-e-Shabeer Se Sada Aayi Aakhri Raat Hai Sakina Ki.
