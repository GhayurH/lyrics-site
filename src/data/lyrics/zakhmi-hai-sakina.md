Baba, mere Baba

Chalna chaahun to chala bhi nahi jaata
Paaon zakhmi ye uthta hi nahi mera
Sona chaahun to soya bhi nahi jaata
Pusht zakhmi hai zakhmon se hai khun behta
Saans leti hun to dukhti hain pasliyaan
Dadi Zahra ki tarah tuti hain pasliyaan

Rukhsaar mere zakhmi, kaanon se lahu jaari
Bunde bhi chhine mere, kurta bhi jala Baba
Haaye itna mujhe maara Baba
Haaye zakhmi hai Sakina

1
Dhundti phirti thi main aap ka laasha Baba
Ek zaalim ne mujhe rok ke mujh se puchha
Dhundti kis ko hai, Baba ka tere naam hai kya
Mere Baba hain Husain ibn-e-Ali, main ne kaha
Phir ghaiz mein woh aaya
Chehre pe mujhe maara
Reti pe gira daala
Main kehti rahi Baba

2
Raah mein chalte hue qaafila thehraaya gaya
Paani le kar jo wahaan Shimr-e-lain aaya tha
Apne lashkar ko wahaan paani se sairaab kiya
Paani dikhla ke mujhe saara wahin phenk diya
Phir hans ke woh kehta tha
Pyaasa tujhe rakhunga
Baalon ko mere khencha
Aur khun risa Baba

3
Pusht zakhmi hui Sajjad ki to main ne kaha
Haaye maaro na use bhai hai bimaar mera
Sun ke aawaaz meri Shimr meri samt barha
Bhai ko chhor diya phir woh sitam mujh pe kiya
Maara mujhe tamaancha
Chehre se lahu nikla
Woh haath sitamgar ka
Bhaari tha bara Baba

4
Khaa ke zaalim ke tamaanche mujhe ghash aata tha
Phir tamaanchon se hi woh hosh mein le aata tha
Hans ke kehta tha bula Baba ko Ammu ko bula
Jitna tu royegi utna hi tujhe maarunga
Phir dil mera ghabraaya
Aur jism bhi tharraaya
Ye haal mera dekha
Na reham kiya Baba

5
Mera nanha sa gala rassi se bandhwaya gaya
Mujh ko panjon pe kabhi kaanton pe chalwaya gaya
Tez chalte hue naaqon se bhi girwaya gaya
Sarbarahna mujhe bazaar mein phirwaya gaya
Thi itni thakan Baba
Paaon bhi na uthta tha
Us waqt jo ghash aaya
Aise jagaaya Baba

6
Jab main zindaan se rihaai ki khabar paaungi
Dadi Zahra ki main turbat pe chali jaaungi
Apni tuti hui pasli unhein dikhlaaungi
Chaar saala main zaeefa hun ye batlaaungi
Hum dono hain mazluma
Ek jaisa hai gham apna
Zaalim ne jo rulwaya
Dono ne kaha Baba

7
Kaisa woh Mazhar o Farhan tha gham ka manzar
Qaid khaane mein Sakina ko mila Baba ka sar
Roi kuch der woh munh Baba ke munh par rakh kar
Bas yehi kehte hue kar gayi duniya se safar
Ye aakhri jumla tha
Ab sabr nahi hota
Sar rone laga Shah ka
Beti ne kaha Baba
