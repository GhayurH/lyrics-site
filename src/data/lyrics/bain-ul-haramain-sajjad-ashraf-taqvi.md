Bain Ul Haramain Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi

1
Bas Issi Soch Mein Betha Hoon Main Bain Al-Harmain
Iss Se Aagay Bhi Kahin Hai Koi Had-e-Qausain
Ik Taraf Sajday Mein Sar Aik Taraf Sajday Mein Dil
Ik Taraf Hazrat-e-Abbas Hain Ik Samt Hussain
Itna Pakeeza Musalla Hai Diyar-e-Shabeer
Sar Ba Sajdah Nazar Aate Hain Jahan Par Konain

2
Jo Hai Quran, Wohi Aal-e-Muhammad Ka Biyan
Main Tu Bas Itna Hi Samjha Hoon Hadees-e-Saqlain
Arsh Par Pohanchay Nabi, Farsh Pe Utra Hai Khuda
Aik Mairaj-e-Nabi, Aik Hai Mairaj-e-Hussain
Aik Hi Dar Se Shifa Leti Hai Makhlooq-e-Khuda
Aik Hi Dar Hai Jo Karta Hai Ilaj-e-Darain

3
Husan Alfaz Barha, Zikar Haseen Hone Laga
Jab Ada Mere Labon Se Howa Nam-e-Hasnain
Jane Kyun Loag Yeh Samjhay Keh Khuda Likha Hai
Ya Ali Main Ne Likha Daal Ke Jab Bhi Wawain
Daman-e-Kaun-o-Makaan:NOOR ALI: Bhar Jaye
Jhar Dein Hazrat-e-Abbas Jo Apni Nalain
