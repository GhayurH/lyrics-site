1
Haye Veeran Gharan Wich Roway Bemar Khari
Aisi Wichri Na Mili Fair Ali Akbar Nu Kadi.
Veer Wichrey Na Kisay Bhain Da Akbar Wangoon.
Maut Banr Janda Aye Roog Dukhi Bhainraan Layi.

2
Aa Ja Pardesiya Veera Main Tere Rah Wekhaan.
Jora Shagna Da Banraya Main Veran Tere Layi.
Qasida Jawein Te Akbar Nu Kehwein Ro Ro Ke.
Sahwaan Gindi Hoi Humsheer Nu Mil Ja Cheti.

3
Naal Asghar Nu V Le Aaway Jay Almaan Wala.
Fair Main Janr Na Dewaan Kadi Sari Zindagi.
Jondi Mar Jaway Gi Sughra Ne Jadon Aye Sunrya.
Kha Gayi Akbar De Kalejay Nu Zulm Di Barchi.

4
Main Aan Beemar Veran Kiwein Tere Kool Aawaan.
Waat Karbal Di Madine Taun Aye Dushwar Bari.
ALLAH Janre Kiwein Nibh Gayi Aidi Kaliyaan Akhtar.
Jaag Ke Rataan Guzarey Sainr Khabaan Di Dari.
