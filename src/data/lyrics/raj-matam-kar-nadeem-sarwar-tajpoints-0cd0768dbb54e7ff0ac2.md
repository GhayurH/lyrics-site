1
Raj Matam Kar Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics
Yeh Sham-e-Ghareebaan Hai Bin Chadar Zainab Roti Hai.
Khaimon Ka Talaya Karti Hai.
Dekha Ke Sawar Ik Aata Hai.

2
Samjhi Koi Lootney Aata Hai.
Zainab Ki Aawaz Aayi Ke Ruk Ja Sawar Tu.
Dukhay Dil Pe Karta Hai Waar Kyun.
Woh Aane Wala Yeh Bool Utha Pehchan Main Baba Hoon Tera.

3
Pehchan Ke Baba Ko Zainab Gayi Bibiyon Ki Janib.
Aray Maida Baba Aaya Hai Bibiyo Koi Chadar Sir Te Daal De.
Main Baba Ali Aan Haan Zainab Mere Seene Lag Ghabra Nahin.
Kar Matam Kar Ghabra Nahin Haan Raj Matam Kar Ghabra Nahin.

4
Meri Zainab Meri Jan Hai Tu Mere Kunbay Ka Arman Hai Tu.
Youn Ghut Ghut Ke Na Ro Zainab.
Tu Sabir Syed Zadi Hai Konain Ki Tu Shehzadi Hai.
Tu Tarsi Lagti Hai Matam Ki.

5
Yeh Karbal Dais Paraya Hai Be Goor Tera Maa Jaya Hai.
Yeh Sham-e-Ghareebaan Hai Zainab.
Atharah Baradar Wali Tu Ik Chadar Ki Hai Sawali Tu.
Kya Haal Howa Tera Zainab.

6
Akbar Ki Maut Ne Mar Diya Seena Hai Tera Zakhmo Se Bhara.
Teri Chadar Lut Gayi Karbal Mein.
Tu Khak Pe Ujari Bethi Hai Teri Amma Zahra Roti Hai.
 Rehan Sada-e- Sarwar Thi.
