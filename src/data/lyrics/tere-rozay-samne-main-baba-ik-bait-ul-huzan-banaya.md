Tere Rozay Samne Main Baba Ik Bait ul Huzan Banaya
Aj Tere Lal Da Koi Tainu Haye Pursa Deen Nayi Aaya
Jinhay Haq Mera Si Gasab Kita Teri Qabar Di Ja V Khoyi
Odhay Zulma Te Main Lal Hasan Fair Sari Zindagi Roi
Har Welay Rondi Teri Zahra Nu Ik Hor Zakhm Aina Laya
Ummat De Zehar Ne Kitay Ne Jehray Jigar De Tukray Tere
Oh Waqt Qayamat Da Honra Jadon Maqtal De Wich Mere
Yaad Aawan Gay Aye Jadon Baba Shabeer Ne Qasim Chaya
Khichay Teer Ne Zainab Lash Wichon Haye Kafan Shabeer Pawaya
Jehray Teer Chalaye Musalmana Ona Kafan Da Rang Badlaya
Rehnra Shabeer Be Kafan Baba Ainu Apna Kafan Pawaya
Mere Putar De Pak Janazay Wich Ik Aisa Teer V Aaya
Teri Kalma Go Ik Maa Baba Mere Lal Te Teer Chalaya
Maa Kiwein Aakhay Ga Koi Ohno Jinhay Hasan Te Teer Chalaya
Iss Zehar Da Naam Saqeefa Aye Aidhey Asar Nu Karbal Kenday
Aiday Dard Sajjad De Athro Ne Sadiyaan Tak Zakhm Aye Rainday
Samne Roza-e-Nabi, NAJFI Baqiya Te Nayi Koi Saya
