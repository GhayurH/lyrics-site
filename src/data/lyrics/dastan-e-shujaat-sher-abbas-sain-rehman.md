1
Dastan E Shujaat Sher Abbas Sain Rehman
Dastan-e-Shujat Ka Darain Mein Aik Be Misal Kirdar Abbas Hai
Hain Ali Ke Atharah Hi Betay Ali Par Sabhi Mein Alamdar Abbas Hai
Sahib-e-Takht-e-Aqlain Ghairat Hai Yeh Our Charag-e-Hareem e Sharafat Hai Yeh
Dur-e-Shehwar e Behar-e-Sakhawat Hai Yeh Haq Tu Yeh Hai Keh Haq Ki Haqeeqat Hai Yeh
Woh Duaein Jo Bibi Ne Sajdoon Mein Ki Unn Duaon Ka Maiyar Abbas Hai
Inn Ke Lehjay Mein Hashim Ki Hashmat Bhi Hai Inn Ke Tewar Mein Hamza Ki Haibat Bhi Hai
Har Ada Inn Ki Imran Ka Aks Hai Inn Ki Banhoon Mein Jafar Ki Taqat Bhi Hai
Pyar Hai Mustafa Ka Hussain-o-Hasan Our Hasnain Ka Pyar Abbas Hai
Mashk Mein Kyun Na Darya Utha Laye Woh Fatima Ne Jissay Apna Beta Kaha
Baab-e-Khaybar Tha Kul Hath Pe Aap Ke Ya Nigahon Mein Lashkar Tha Nu Lakh Ka
Ya Ali Hai Agar Zulfiqar Aap Ki Fatima Ki Tu Talwar Abbas Hai
Nakhl e Azmat Ka Shereen Samar Hain Yehi Ismaton Ke Sadaf Ke Gohar Hain Yehi
Noor Ki Sidrat Ul-Muntaha Hain Yehi Hashmi Aasmaan Ke Qamar Hain Yehi
Jin Ke Saye Mein Akbar Bhi Paltay Rahe Husan Ka Aisa Minar Abbas Hai
Hal Atta Innama Ke Jo Misdaq Hain Woh Bhi Ghazi Ke Aane Ke Mushtaq Hain
Arsh Se Farsh Tak Inn Ki Jageer Hai Sari Makhlooq Inn Ki Gadageer Hai
Jo Ali Ki Shujat Ka Waris Bhi Hai Aisa Aala Zamindar Abbas Hai
Qabar Kya Hai Andhera Sa Ghar Hai Jahan Marifat Ke Siwa Koi Sapna Nahi
Woh Bohat Hi Andheri Jagah Hai Jahan Koi Yawar Nahi Koi Apna Nahi
Tum Se Milney Lehad Mein Ali Aaein Gay Bolo Tanhai Mein Our Kya Chahiye
