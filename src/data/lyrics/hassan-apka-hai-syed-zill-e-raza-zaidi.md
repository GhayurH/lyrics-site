1
Hassan Apka Hai Syed Zill e Raza Zaidi.
Yeh Jo Kuch Bhi Ma Bain Arz-o-Sama Hai.
Khuda Ki Qasam Yeh Khuda Keh Raha Hai.
Yeh Chand Our Yeh Soraj Yeh Gehray Samandar.
Yeh Hain Kashtiyan Jo Samandar Ke Andar.
Yeh Jo Bhi Jahan Mein Jahan Chal Raha Hai.
Hain Wal Lail Zulfein Tu Walfajar Matha.
Hain Walnoor Aankhein Tu Washams Chehra.
Jo Quraan Mein Rab Ne Qaseeda Parha Hai.
Jo Mashriq-o-Magrib Bana Kar Dikha De.
Jo Ik Mard Ko Pal Mein Aurat Bana De.
Khuda Ke Siwa Kis Ka Yeh Mojza Hai.
They Hairaan Sahabi Yeh Kya Majrah Hai.
Yeh Kaisa Fareza Ada Ho Raha Hai.
Nabi Jo Yeh Masjid Mein Naqa Bana Hai.
Woh Lehjay Ki Narmi Fasahat Balagat.
Woh Barh Barh Ke Hamlay Woh Hathoon Ki Taqat.
Yeh Jo Roop Qasim Pe Aaya Howa Hai.
Yeh Hoor-o-Malaik Yeh Bagaat Meway.
Yeh Qasar-e-Zamurd Yeh Nehrein Yeh Chashmay.
Yeh Janat Mein Jo Kuch Sajaya Gaya Hai.
Nabi Ne Kaha Hain Hasan Aap Aisay.
Jo Jholay Mein Bhi Looh-e-Mehfooz Parh Le.
Qalam Woh Qalam Jis Ka Malik Khuda Hai.
Mujhe Khof Kya Gar Bura Hai Zamana.
Dua Aap Ki Bhi Hai Shana Ba Shana.
Karam Yeh Jo:ZILL-E-RAZA: Par Howa Hai.
