Akbar Kay Janazay Pay Mir Hasan Mir.
Akbar Haye Haye Akbar, Akbar Haye Haye Akbar.

1
Haye Jis Waqt Gira Zeen Se Hum Shakal-e-Payamber.
Shabeer Ko Zainab Ne Sada Di Yeh Tarap Kar.
Akbar Ke Janazay Pe Mujhe Le Chalo Bhaiyya.
Hum Mil Ke Utha Lein Gay Jawaan Lal Ka Lasha.

2
Atharah Baras Walay Ka Sar Gaud Mein Le Kar.
Main Nad-e-Ali Parhti Rahon Gi Aye Baradar.
Tum Seena-e-Akbar Se Nikalo Gay Jo Naiza.
Jo Zakhm Lagay Hain Woh Dikhaye Ga Na Tum Ko.

3
Shayad Woh Takaluf Mein Bataye Ga Na Tum Ko.
Main Maa Hoon Magar Mujh Se Chupa Hi Nahi Sakta.
Main Janti Hoon Kaisay Jawaan Hotey Hain Betay.
Our Dekhney Par Jaein Agar Unn Ke Janazay.

4
Ab Main Bhi Samajh Sakti Hoon Aoulad Ka Sadma.
Aane Nahi Dongi Main Kissi Ko Bhi Wahan Par.
Jee Barh Ke Galay Milna Jawaan Lal Se Ja Kar.
Main Maut Se Keh Dongi Zara Dair Thehar Ja.

5
Baba Mere Baba Ki Sada De Tu Raha Hai.
Aawaz Se Lagta Hai Bohat Door Gira Hai.
Lo Hath Mera Thamo Bohat Door Hai Jana.
Oss Karb Ki Halat Se Nikalay Ga Tumhein Kaun.

6
Aray Woh Ayeriyaan Ragray Ga Sanbhalay Ga Tumhein Kaun.
Chadar Se Bana Loon Gi Main Kuch Dair Ko Pardah.
Tooti Hai Kamar Aap Ki Aye Mere Baradar.
Gir Gir Ke Uthein Gay Tu Hansein Gay Yeh Sitamgar.

7
Main Aap Ko Banney Tu Nahi Doon Gi Tamasha.
Roti Hai Koi Bibi Musalsal Pas-e-Pardah.
Jab Se Hai Suna:MIR TAKALLUM: Tera Noha.
Bhai Se Galay Mil Ke Behan Boli Dobarah.
