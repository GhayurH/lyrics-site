Luti Paak Rasool Di Aal, Tapdiyan Raitan Te

Hoya Chan Zahra Paamaal, Tapdiyan Raitan Te

Lokon Bibi Zahra Saani, Paak Batool Di Paak Nishaani

Khari Ban Wich Khol Ke Waal, Tapdiyan Raitan Te

Daahda Ummat Zulam Kamaya, Pyaase Gal Te Teer Chalaya

Haye Maareya Asghar Baal, Tapdiyan Raitan Te

Farwa Akhay Zahra Jaya, Dhup Te Onay Dera Laaya

Aa Vekh Rubab Da Haal, Tapdiyan Raitan Te

Bhaina Khariyan Rassiyan Paa Ke, Kyun Nai Mureya Nehr Te Jakay

Aaa Ummal Baneen Da Laal, Tapdiyan Raitan Te

Kalla Alam Hai Khaymay Aaya, Chaha Ghazi Kyun Nai Aaya

Ayho Pyasay Karan Sawaal, Tapdiyan Raitan Te

Chan Qasim Di Rul Gayi Mehndi, Tapdi Rait Te Dul Gayi Mehndi

Maa’n Vekhdi Hasrat Naal, Tapdiyan Raitan Te

Jhunreya Akbar Barchi Khaa Ke, Paak Hussain Musafir Akhay

Hy Ujreya Hussan Jamaal, Tapdiyan Raitain Te

Ay Tauqir Ki Darad Sunraway, Ki Abid Da Haal Vikhaway

Jadon Tureya Beriyan Naal, Tapdiyan Raitan Te.
