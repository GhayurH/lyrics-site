Araam Kahan x4

Kishte Aalam, Mein Zainab Ko Bhala

Qatl Shabbir Bhi Abbas Bhi Akbar Bhi Huey

Kat Gaye Aun-o-Mohammad Ke Bhi Maqtal Mein Galay

Laasha-e-Ibn Hassan Hogaya Pamaal Jafa

Koi Maara Gaya Talwaar Se Naizey Se Koi

Laash Maqtal Mein Kisi Dhoola Ki Pamaal Hui

Hogaye Baazu-e-Abbas Alamdar Juda

Sar Shaheedon Ke Sarey Nauke Sina Qaid Haram

Aik Doh Ka Nahi Seene Mein Bahattar Ka Hai Gham

Berhiyan Pehne Huey Laaghiron Beemaar Chala

Ro Ro Kehti Hai Sakina Mere Ammou Aao

Mujhko Maare Na Layeen Aake Inhey Samjao

Kyun Nahi Sunte Ho Deti Hoon Tumhe Kabse Sada

Bachey Joh Khaak Pe Girtey Hain Kuchal Jaate Hain

Gham Se Dil Maon Ke Seene Se Nikal Jaate Hain

Koi Sunta Nahi Bewaon Ki Faryad-o-Buka

Chin Gayi Sar Se Rida Jal Gaya Asbaab Tamaam

Baade Shabbir Na-Mudaar Hui Kaisi Yeh Shaam

Khoon Kaano Se Baha Bachi Ka Daaman Bhi Jala

Qaafila Jaata Hai Ab Shaam Ke Bazaaron Mein

Chalte Hain Ahle Haram Zulm Ke Angaaron Mein

Har Qadam Hoti Hai Beemaar Par Aik Taaza Jafa

Aankh Roti Hai Qalam Likhta Hai Noha Rehan

Hai Karam Mujhpe Shah-e-Karbobala Ka Rehan

Noha Jab Tak Na Likhoon Roz Shahe Deen Ka Naya
