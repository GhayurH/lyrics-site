1
Sibt e Payambar Ayen Ge Mehdi Rizvi.
Dekhna Mehshar Mein Jab Sibt e Payamber Aaein Gay.
Sath Mein Abbas Unn Ka Saya Ban Kar Aaein Gay.
Aamad-e-Zainab Pe Hoga Yeh Khushi Ka Ahtamam.
Chadrein Le Kar Muhammad, Zahra Ke Ghar Aaein Gay.
Momino Par Jab Kabhi Mushkil Ghari Jo Aaye Gi.
Aye Sakina Ke Chacha Woh Tere Dar Par Aaein Gay.
Jab Lagaya Main Ne Apne Ghar Pe Ghazi Ka Alam.
Ab Yahan Sare Farishtay Sar Jhuka Kar Aaein Gay.
Hoga Jab Bhi Izn Mujh Ko Hazrat-e-Shabbir Ka.
Karbala Se Mujh Ko Lainay Khud Bahatar Aaein Gay.
Chaar Ko Haider Ke Ghar Mein Phir Se Haider Ka Zahoor.
Gayarah Shabaan Ko Muhammad Ban Ke Akbar Aaein Gay.
Ab Yeh Dekhein Gay Malak Sare Teri Parwaz Ko.
Teesri Shaban Jab Fitrus Tere Par Aaein Gay.
Hashar Mein Bolein Gay Maula Jab Yeh Esa Ho Kahan.
Tab Wahan Labaik Kehte Kehte Qambar Aaein Gay.
