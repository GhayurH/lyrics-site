1
Terah Rajab Ki Sham Ali Ali Karein Gaye
Mula Tere Ghulam Ali Ali Karein Gaye
Nobat Kahin Dhamal Ki Uthi Hai Jo Sada
Oss Par Malang Sare Qalandar-o-Auliya

2
Pee Kar Wila Ka Jam Ali Ali Karein Gaye
Sunat Hai Momino Ko Muhammad Ki Mohtaram
Har Gaam Woh Rakhein Gaye Youn Tauheed Ka Bharam
Le Kar Khuda Ka Naam Ali Ali Karein Gaye

3
Madh-e-Abu Turab Ki Rasi Ko Tham Ke
Pehle Tu Muskuraein Gaye Munkir Ke Samne
Phir Nazar-e-Inteqam Ali Ali Karein Gaye
Yasrab-o-Samrah Ho Ya Woh Sham-o-Karbala

4
Dushwar Jitna Ahl-e-Wila Ka Ho Rasta
Har Gaam Har Muqam Ali Ali Karein Gaye
Zikar-e-Ali Se Aqal Ki Hairani Door Ho
Milta Hai Aagehi Ka Tabaruk Shaoor Ko

5
Youn Anbiya Tamam Ali Ali Karein Gaye
Taskeen-e-Qalab-e-Fatima Zahra Se Hai Dua
Kar Dijiye Atta Humein Woh Din Ghadeer Ka
Jab Aakhri Imam Ali Ali Karein Gaye
