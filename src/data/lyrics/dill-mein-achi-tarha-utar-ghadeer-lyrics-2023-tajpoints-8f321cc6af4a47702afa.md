Dill Mein Achi Tarha Utar Ghadeer Hasnain Akbar.
Ja Namazon Pe Bhi Pukar Ghadeer.

1
Chaar Mosam Bhi Aaye Our Gaye.
Hai Sada Se Sada Bahar Ghadeer.
Zulashera Yaqeen e Zaat-e-Khuda.
Our Muhammad Ka Aytbar Ghadeer.

2
Hain Woh Mohrein Alla Qaloobe Ke Naam.
Jin Dilon Ko Hai Na Gawar Ghadeer.
Dil Pukarey Ga Utni Bar Ali.
Tum Pukaro Gay Jitni Bar Ghadeer.

3
Goya Pohanchaya Hi Nahi Islam.
Hai Oss Aayat Ki Yadgar Ghadeer.
Taig-e-Haider Ki Misal Dekhta Hai.
Sari Naslon Ke Aar Paar Ghadeer.

4
Gar Woh Hotey Rasool Ke Naib.
Kam Se Kam Hota Chaar Bar Ghadeer.
Ashado Ana Ya Rasool ALLAH.
Tujh Ko Rakhay Ga Barqarar Ghadeer.

5
Kaun Bakhin Kahey Ga Dekhein Gay.
Jab Manaye Gi Zulfiqar Ghadeer.
Jis Ko Akbar Khushi Naseeb Nahi.
Hum Se Le Le Woh Mistar Ghadeer.
