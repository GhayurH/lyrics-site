1
Mara Hai Fatima Ko Hasnain Abbas Lyrics
Mara Hai Fatima Ko Sab Ne Rula Rula Kar.
Zahra Ka Dar Jala Kar Zahra Pe Dar Gira Kar.
Turbat Se Fatima Ki Aati Yehi Sada Hai.

2
Pehlu Se Hath Mera Ab Tak Nahi Hata Hai.
Mehdi Mujhe Sanbhalo Ghaibat Se Jald Aa Kar.
Darbar Se Zaeefa Ghar Ki Taraf Chali Hai.
ALLAH Hi Janta Hai Kitni Dafa Giri Hai.

3
Laye Hain Apne Ghar Tak Hasnain Khud Utha Kar.
Zalim Ka Taziyana Jis Hath Par Laga Hai.
Woh Hath Fatima Ka Uth Hi Nahi Raha Hai.
Parhti Hain Ab Namazein Ik Hath Hi Utha Kar.

4
Pehlu Mein Dard Itna Hai Keh Tarap Rahi Hai.
Chalna Bhi Ab Hai Mushkil Uth Uth Ke Gir Rahi Hai.
Chalti Hai Aik Shana Dewar Se Laga Kar.
Keh Keh Yateem Khud Ko Shabeer Jab Keh Roye.

5
Do Hath Fatima Ke Bandhay Kafan Se Nikley.
Seene Pe Roi Apne Shabeer Ko Sula Kar.
Haye Manzar Bara Kathin Tha Jis Dam Laga Tamacha.
Sar Bint-e-Mustafa Ka Dewar Se Laga Tha.

6
Munh Peettay They Haider Aankhon Se Khoon Baha Kar.
Hilney Laga Nabi Ka Oss Dam Mazar Aasi.
Zahra Lehad Pe Aa Kar Ro Ro Ke Jab Pukari.
Chehray Ke Neel Dekho Baba Rida Hata Kar.
