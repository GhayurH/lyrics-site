1
Fizza Tujhe Salam Zahra Se Kiya Wadah Mir Hasan Mir.
Zahra Kiya Wadah Nibhati Rahi Fizza.
Har Moor Pe Zainab Ko Bachati Rahi Fizza.
Jo Sani-e-Zahra Ko Bachane Mein Lage The.
Sajjad Se Wo Zakhm Chupati Rahi Fizza.
Jab Pocha Kahan Reh Gay Amma Mere Baba.
Sughra Se Bohat Dair Chupati Rahi Fizza.
Aya Jo Mulaqat Ko Abbas Ka Beta.
Bas Shano Ko Aankhon Se Lagati Rahi Fizza.
Shabir Ki Beti Ho Ya Beti Ho Nabi Ki.
Har Umar Ki Zahra Ko Bachati Rahi Fizza.
Darwaza-e-Zahra Kabhi Khaimo Se Haram Ke.
Uthte Howay Sholon Ko Bhujhati Rahi Fizza.
Bacho Se Lipat Kar Kabhi Zainab Se Lipat Kar.
Pathar Jo Baraste Rahe Khati Rahi Fizza.
Kha Kar Kabhi Pathar Kabhi Duray Aye Takallum.
Qarz Aal-e-Muhammad Ka Chukati Rahi Fizza.
