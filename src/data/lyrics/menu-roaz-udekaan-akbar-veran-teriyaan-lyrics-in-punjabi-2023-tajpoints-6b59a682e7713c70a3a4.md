1
Menu Roaz Udekaan Akbar Veran Teriyaan.
Rab Nawin Jiwein Aa Akbar Muk Jawan Tangaan Meriyaan.
Tere Naa De Charag Jalaye Sehrey Hanjwaan Naal Banaye.
Mere Wichrey Veran Nana Mur Ke Watna Te Ne Aaye.
Mera Aaway Akbar Ik Wari Nana Main Ghama Wich Ghairiyaan.
Fair Aj Khuwab Haqeeqat Lagda Fajar Di Nana Dar Gayiyaan.
Khak Uttay Haye Ronda Baba Wekh Ke Nana Mar Gayiyaan.
Tere Naal Nana Ronday Mursal Tak Nabiyaan Nazraan Phiriyaan.
Har Tukra Bibi Chumdi Aye Haye Inna Lillah Parhdi Aye.
Khushiyaan Welay Putar Nu Rondi Janj Kadi Na Chardi Aye.
Ja Ja Te Roliyaan Musalmana Tasveraan Nana Teriyaan.
Kand Piyo Te Main Wekhiya Nana Hath Khanjar Pharya Zalim Ne.
Tere Quran Nu Qatal Kita Te Tere Quran De Qariyaan Ne.
Main Wekhiyaan Nana Naal Dadi Paiyaan Rondiyaan Dhiyaan Teriyaan.
Veraan Waliyaan Behna Mango Sariyaan Ro Ro Ke Dua.
Howay Na Kisay Behan De Kolon Ainj Kadi Tauqeer Juda.
Sadqa Akbar Manzoor Howan Behna Mangan Duawaan Jehriyaan.
