Ali Ki Khatir Abbas Haider Lalji and Yusuf Okera
Banai Khaliq Ne Sari Dunya Ali Ki Khatir
Yeh Behas Ab Chor Do Hai Kya Kya Ali Ki Khatir
Woh Arsh Par Hai Rajab Ki Terah Ali Ki Khatir
Ali Hai Kabay Mein Aane Wala Ali(Jala Jalalahu) Ki Khatir
Wahan Talak Hai Zaror Chokhat Ali Ke Ghar Ki
Jahan Se Soraj Palat Ke Aaya Ali Ki Khatir
Kuch Iss Tarha Daar Par Tha Meesum Ka Chehra Roshan
Keh Jaisay Ho Aik Chand Nikla Ali Ki Khatir
Aray Muqassir Na Parh Tu Quran Dekh Iss Mein
Jagah Jagah Hai Likha Qaseeda Ali Ki Khatir
Nahi Keh Bas Jibraeel Quran Hi Tha Lata
Kabhi Kabhi Tha Faqat Woh Aata Ali Ki Khatir
Ali Ka Hai Ikhteyar Kitna Yeh Unn Se Pocho
Jo Mar Ke Zindah Hoye Dobarah Ali Ki Khatir
Woh Saib Jis Mein Tha Nisf Noor-e-Batool Woh Bhi
Hai Ban Ke Phir Zulfiqar Utra Ali Ki Khatir
Banaya Jab Oss Ne Fizza Ko Fatima Ki Khatir
Khuda Ne Salman Bhi Banaya Ali Ki Khatir
Ali Ka Shia Banay Ga Kab Tak “Khud Apni Khair”
“ASAD Bas Ab Ban Ali Ka Shia Ali Ki Khair”
