Hashar Mein Rang Laye Ga Khon e Shahedaan Dekhna Hasan Sadiq.
Shaheed-e-Karbala.

1
Hai Deen Ki Baqa.
Zabeeh e Nainawa.
Hussain Badshah.
Hashar Mein Rang Laye Ga Khon-e-Shaheedan Dekhna.

2
Aaein Gi Jab Fatima Hal-e-Pareshaan Dekhna.
Robaro Baba Ke Jab Khatoon-e-Janat Aaein Gi.
Khali Jholi Sarwar e Konain Ko Dikhlaein Gi.
Bilyaqeen Tharaye Ga Mehshar Ka Maidaan Dekhna.

3
Rakh Diya Barchi Ne Akbar Ka Kaleja Cheer Ke.
Rukh Badla Walay Hain Ummat Ne Teri Tasveer Ke.
Malik-e-Kausar Zara Ummat Ke Ahsaan Dekhna.
Asghar-e-Masoom Ke Lab Par Sawal-e-Aab Hai.

4
Bond Pani Ke Liye Woh Kis Qadar Betab Hai.
Nehar Se Pani Nahi Detey Musalmaan Dekhna.
Zainab-o-Kulsoom Se Kehta Tha Shimar e Bad Gohar.
Sham Mein Jao Gi Nangay Sar Phiro Gi Dar Badar.

5
Karbala Ke Baad Ab Tareekh-e-Zindaan Dekhna.
Gham Bhala Gulzar Kya Iss Dil Ko Rooz-e-Hashar Ka.
Jo Hai Aoulad-e-Payamber Ki Muhabbat Se Bhara.
Bakhshwa Dein Gay Ossay Yasrab Ke Sultaan Dekhna.
