1
Beta Abu Talib Ka Zakhmi Howa Masjid Mein.
Sad Aah Khudawanda Zakhmi Howa Masjid Mein.
Sajday Mein Jhukaya Sar Jab Haider-e-Safdar Ne.
Talwar Chala Di Tab Bin e Muljim-e-Abtar Ne.
Damad Payamber Ka Zakhmi Howa Masjid Mein.
Tar Khon-e-Yadullah Se Mehrab-o-Musalla Tha.
Fuztu Berab e Kaba Haider Ki Zabaan Par Tha.
ALLAH Ka Jab Pyara Zakhmi Howa Masjid Mein.
ALLAH Ki Masjid Mein Kuhram Howa Barpa.
Har Ashiq-e-Haider Ke Lab Par Yehi Noha Tha.
Hum Be Kason Ka Aaqa Zakhmi Howa Masjid Mein.
Mehboob-e-Khuda Marqad Mein Aanso Bahatay Hain.
Jibreel Falak Se Yeh Ab Noha Sunatay Hain.
Ustad Jo Hai Mera Zakhmi Howa Masjid Mein.
Hasnain Se Ro Ro Kar Zainab Ne Kaha Bhaiyya.
Masjid Mein Ghazab Kaisa Baba Pe Howa Bhaiyya.
Kehte Hain Mera Baba Zakhmi Howa Masjid Mein.
Masjid Mein Joon Hi Pohanche Shabbir Liye Shabar.
Tar Khoon Mein Padar Dekha Tu Bolay Galay Lag Kar.
Kis Tarha Se Youn Baba Zakhmi Howa Masjid Mein.
Aye Mohtasham e Pur Gham Kabay Ki Sada Har Pal.
Aati Hai Bana Jis Se Main Mohtaram-o-Afzal.
Malik Woh Mera Yakta Zakhmi Howa Masjid Mein.
