1
Ali Mola Ali Mola Ahsan Raza.
Fida-e-Mustafa Hoon Main Mera Maula Ali Maula.
Nisar-e-Murtaza Hoon Main Mera Maula Ali Maula.
Zameen Pe Aasmano Mein Yehi Tu Naam Chalta Hai.
Ali Azam Ali Shafi Ali Aala Ali Maula.
Kaha Man Kuntu Maula Jab Peygamber Ne Sahaba Se.
Zameen Boli Falak Bola Ali Maula Ali Maula.
Lehad Mein Jab Nakeeron Ne Kaha Maula Kisay Mana?.
Zabaan Ke Sath Dil Bola Ali Maula Ali Maula.
Ataon Par Ataein Mil Rahi Hain Shah-e-Tayyaba Se.
Pukarey Ja Raha Hai Har Ali Wala Ali Maula.
Batan-e-Aarzu Totay Marey Dil Ke Haram Se Jab.
Tu Phir Oss Jan Ke Kabay Mein Hai Gonja Ali Maula.
Gumaan Haq Ka Mazallah Phir Ho Jata Haider Par.
Shab-e-Hijrat Jo Na Kehti Keh Haan Soya Ali Maula.
 Aneer Aal-e-Abba Ko Jab Pukara Yeh Sada Aayi.
Na Ghabra Aye Marey Mangtay Keh Hai Tera Ali Maula.
