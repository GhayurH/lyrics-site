Uth Ja Mere Sajjad Irfan Haider Lyrics
Sham-e-Gharebaan, Jaltay Khaimay Uthta Dhuwaan Aoun-o-Muhammad Ki Woh Maa Aayi Sajjad Ke Khaimay Mein.
Kaha Shana Hila Kar Rotay Hoye Aabad Rahe Gi Karb-o-Bala, Chal Sham Karein Aabad.
Uth Ja Mere Sajjad, Mera Maa Jaya Nahi Aaya.
Sun Zainab Ki Faryad, Uth Ja Mere Sajjad.
Amma Ka Hath Galay Par Tha Kuch Door Se Dekha Tha Main Ne Haye.
Maqtal Mein Sokhi Gardan Par Jab Shimar Ne Teraan Waar Kiye.
Woh Bhai Mera Wawela Mara Gaya Pyasa Wawela.
Jis Bhai Par Qurban Hoi Pyasi Meri Aoulad, Uth Ja Mere Sajjad.
Khaimon Mein Hi Jal Jaye Na Khaimon Se Nikal Jaein Bahir Haye.
Kya Hukam Hai Tera Zainab Ko Ik Bar Bata De Tu Uth Kar.
Na Qasim Hai Na Asghar Hai Abbas Hai Our Na Akbar Hai.
Bas Tera Sahara Hai Hum Ko Bhaiyya Shabbir Ke Bad, Uth Ja Mere Sajjad.
Yeh Sham-e-Ghareebaan Hai Beta Teri Pehli Sham Imamat Ki Haye.
Teri Salari Mein Sham Talak Karna Hai Safar Ban Kar Qaidi.
Na Mehmal Hai Na Hodaj Hai Na Lashkar Ki Woh Saj Dhaj Hai.
Jab Hath Bandhin Gay Gardan Se Ghazi Aaye Ga Yaad, Uth Ja Mere Sajjad.
Be Maqna-o-Chadar Naqay Se Humein Jana Hai Sajjad Jahan Haye.
Aye Lal Suno Kaisay Hoga Sadaat Ka Istaqbal Wahan.
Bazar Mein Aane Se Pehle Rokein Gay Humein Darwazay Pe.
Galiyon Ko Sajaya Jaye Ga Naye Hoon Gay Sitam Ejad, Uth Ja Mere Sajjad.
Sajjad Teri Masoom Behan Zalim Ke Tamachay Khaye Gi Haye.
Mangay Ga Kaneezi Mein Hakim Darbar Mein Jab Woh Jaye Gi.
Mar Jaye Gi Durray Kha Kha Kar Zindan Mein Oss Ko Dafna Kar.
Tujhe Rona Hai Chalees Baras Na Hoon Gay Kabhi Hum Shad, Uth Ja Mere Sajjad.
Al Sham Al Sham Al Sham Suna Our Aankhein Kholein Abid Ne Haye.
Zainab Ne Kaha Aye Zeeshan-o- Irfan Sahara Detey Hoye.
Maqtal Mein Paray Unn Lashon Ka Chehlum Karne Unn Pyason Ka.
Aaein Gay Dobarah Karb-o-Bala Jab Hoon Gay Hum Aazad, Uth Ja Mere Sajjad.
