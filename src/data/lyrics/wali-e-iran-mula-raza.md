1
Wali e Iran Mula Raza
Kijiye Lillah Malik Mushkilein Aasan
Aye Ali Ke Ladlay Fatima Ki Jan
Aap Ke Darbar Se Khali Nahi Lauta Koi
Jo Kari Jis Ne Dua Fill Foor Woh Pori Hoi
Khalq Ki Hajat Rawai Hai Teri Pehchan
Aap Hai Misl-e-Ali-e-Murtaza Mushkil Kusha
Aap Ka Darbar Hai Sab Ke Liye Bab-e-Atta
Aap Ki Hai Badshahi Waris-e-Quran
Khoob Langar Bat Raha Hai Aap Ki Sarkar Se
Koi Bhoka Ja Nahi Sakta Tere Darbar Se
Iss Qadar Mula Wasee Hai Tera Dastar Khuwan
Jo Koi Lachar Ho Bemar Ho Mula Raza
Aaye Woh Sehan-e-Shifa Mein Our Piye Aab-e-Baqa
Hai Shifa Khana Tere Darbar Ka Dalaan
Ya Raza Be-Had Hoon Sharminda Main Bad Aamal Par
Malka-e-Qum Ke Tasaduq Ho Karam Be-Haal Par
Hon Moafi Ka Talib Mujh Pe Kar Ahsan
Iss Liye Main Sahib-e-Ilm-o-Hunar Hon Ya Raza
Nam Mein:YASIR RAZA: Ke Naam Aata Hai Tera
Main Tere Sadqay Tere Wari Tere Qurban
