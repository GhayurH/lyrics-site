1
Yaad Aate Hain Tumhein Dekh Ke Nana Akbar.
Dekh Loon Tum Ko Main Jee Bhar Ke Tu Jana Akbar.
Maa Ko Arman Hai Keh Dulha Bana Le Tum Ko.
Sehra Ik Bar Zara Sar Pe Sajana Akbar.

2
Gham Nahi Yeh Keh Chaley Jao Gay Maqtal Ki Taraf.
Gham Hai Keh Tum Ne Nahi Laut Ke Aana Akbar.
Aakhri Bar Main Nana Ki Ziyarat Kar Loon.
Sar Pe Dastar Pehan Kar Yeh Dikhana Akbar.

3
Jaisay Dar Fatima Zahra Ka Giraya Waisay.
Kalma Goyon Ne Tumhein Zeen Se Girana Akbar.
Waqt-e-Aakhir Kaha Akbar Ne Sisak Kar Baba.
Kehti Thi Sughra Mujhe Bhool Na Jana Akbar.

4
Ghutniyon Chalte Hoye Kehte They Ro Kar Shabeer.
Kitna Mushkil Hai Teri Lash Tak Aana Akbar.
Karbala Mein Karo Zeeshan-o- Asad Ko Bhi Talab.
Khon Hum Ne Hai Tere Gham Mein Bahana Akbar.
