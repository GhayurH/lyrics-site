Fizza Teri Wafa Ki Hadh Akhri Yehi Hai

1
Zainab Kay Sath Tu Bhi Bazaar Mein Khari Hai

2
Tu Nay To Syedon Ka Abaad Ghar Bhi Dekha
Aur Kis Tarha Hua Wo Barbaad Ghar Bhi Dekha
Kis Nay Kissay Sataya Tu Khoob Jaanti Hai

3
Jub Hassan Ka Janaza Wapis Hay Ghar Mein Aaya
Farwa Kay Saath Ro Kay Qasim Galay Lagaya
Lash-e-Hassan Say Tu Bhi Rahi Teer Khainchti Hai

4
Baandhi Hui Rasan Mein Zainab Jo Shaam Aayi
Teri Qamar Kay Peechay Chupti Thi Zahra Jayee
Har Mor Pay Dilasay Zainab Ko Day Rahi Hai

5
Yasrab Ki Taraf Daikhay Ro Ro Kay Zahra Jayee
Teri Betiyon Pay Nana Kaisi Qayamat Aayi
Fizza Kay Bhi Hain Waris Yahan Mera Na Koi Hai

6
Jub Roti Thi Sakina Har Baar Chup Karaya
Aur Gash Say Paak Abid Ko Tu Nay Bhi Jagaya
Zainab Kay Saath Jhirkein Fizza Bhi Kha Rahi Hai

7
Aal-e-Nabi Pay Gham Ki Bunyaad Hai Saqifa
Din Raat Ab Hasan To Apna Yehi Wazifa
Jisnay Inhay Rulaya Wo Shakhs Lanati Hai
