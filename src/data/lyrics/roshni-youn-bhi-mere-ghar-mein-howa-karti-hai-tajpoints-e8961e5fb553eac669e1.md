Roshni Youn Bhi Mere Ghar Mein Howa Karti Hai.
Khon Ke Bond Se Qandeel Jala Karti Hai.

1
Tu Ne Pocha Na Kabhi Shola-e-Namnaak Ka Haal.
Aatisheen Ashk Mein Kyun Aankh Raha Karti Hai.
Zulmat Shab Se Ubharti Hoi Cheekhein Tu Sunu.
Yeh Woh Awaz Hai Jo Hashar Bapa Karti Hai.

2
Jab Guzarta Hoon Muhabbat Ke Shabistano Se.
Oss Ki Khushbu Mujhe Pehchan Liya Karti Hai.
Choot Hai Dil Ki Magar Aankh Ko Rote Dekha.
Kaun Mujrim Hai Saza Kis Ko Mila Karti Hai.

3
Apne Darwaze Pe Takhgti Tu Koi Nasab Karo.
Ghar Ki Dewar Makeenu Se Gila Karti Hai.
Ache Waqton Pe Fareedi Na Kabhi Naaz Karo.
Aazmaish Kabhi Youn Bhi Howa Karti Hai.
