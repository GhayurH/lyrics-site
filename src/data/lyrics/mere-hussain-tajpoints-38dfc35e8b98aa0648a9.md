Lipat Ke Shah Se Kehtin Hain Fatima Ro Ke.
Mere Hussain Haan Mere Hussain.

1
Main Kitne Teer Nikalon Keh Tum Ko Chain Miley Bata Hussain Zyada Hai Dard Kis Ja Par.
Keh Maa Nikal Le Woh Teer Ya Ali Keh Kar Tarapna Dekha Nahi Jata Ab Tu Madar Se.
Woh Jis Ko Pyar Se Atharah Saal Pala Hai.
Ossi Ke Seene Pe Zalim Ne Naiza Mara Hai.

2
Jawaan Ke Seene Se Khainchi Hai Khud Sina Tu Ne.
Na Hath Pehlu Se Utha Na Tham Sakay Aanso.
Tumhein Tu Yaad Hai Zakhmi Howa Mera Pehlu.
Chupaya Na Pao Gaye Pehlu Ka Dard Ab Maa Se.

3
Tumhein Rakha Hai Sada Apni Chaon Mein Main Ne.
Bala Ki Dhop Hai Maqtal Mein Aye Mere Bachay.
Tumhari Lash Pe Saya Karon Gi Baloon Se.
Qaza Ne Tujh Se Mujhe Kamsini Mein Cheen Liya.

4
Zamana Ho Gaya Tujh Se Miley Hoye Beta.
Laga Loon Phir Tujhe Seene Tu Mujh Ko Chain Miley.
Naseeb Tak Na Howa Jalti Khak Ka Bistar.
Dam-e-Akheer Tumhara Badan Hai Teeron Par.

5
Tumhein Yeh Teer Tarapney Talak Nahi Detey.
Hussain Bolay Mubarak Yeh Maa Se Rotay Hoye.
Aye Maa Nikal Lo Bas Teer Mere Pehlu Se.
Jabeen Ho Sajday Mein Jis Waqt Mera Dam Nikley.
