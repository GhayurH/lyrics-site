1
Kon Qayal Tha Salami Keh Jina Our Bhi Hai Nadeem Sarwar.
Karbala Dekhi Tu Hum Samjhay Ke Haan Our Bhi Hai.
Sadqay Oss Dil Keh Jo Ho Hub-e-Ali Se Aabad.
Iss Se Bahatar Koi Dunya Mein Makaan Our Bhi Hai.

2
Nam-e-Shabeer Pe Be Sakhta Giriya Hona.
Baad Kalmay Keh Yeh Emaan Ka Nishaan Our Bhi Hai.
Barchiyaan Mar Ke Akbar Ko Pukarey Aada.
Sheh Se Pocho Koi Farzand Jawaan Our Bhi Hai.

3
Falak-e-Peer Tujhey Sheh Ki Zaeefi Ki Qasam.
Ali Akbar Sa Zamane Mein Jawaan Our Bhi Hai.
Maa Ne Qasim Se Kaha Subha Ko Tum Ho Gay Shaheed.
Iss Liye Biyah Ki Jaldi Meri Jaan Our Bhi Hai.

4
Baal Kholay Hoye Lashay Pe Jo Aaein Zahra.
Khul Gaya Hur Pe Dam-e-Naza Keh Maa Our Bhi Hai.
Apne Farzandoon Ke Marne Pe Bhi Giriya Na Kiya.
Dehar Mein Zainab-e-Mazloom Si Maa Our Bhi Hai.

5
Sheh Se Zainab Ne Kaha Tum Jo Ho Mushtaq-e-Qaza.
Meri Amma Ka Koi Fateha Khuwaan Our Bhi Hai.
Lashay Pamal Sar-e-Sham Jo Hotey Hain Nazeer.
Bag-e-Zahra Pe Sitam Bar-e-Khizaan Our Bhi Hai.
