1
Emaan Ki Qasam Mazhar-e-Eman Hai Zainab.
Quraan Ki Qasam Bolta Quran Hai Zainab.
Ewan-e-Yazeedi Ki Jo Bunyad Hila De.
Har Dor-e-Yazeedi Mein Wo Elaan Hai Zainab.

2
Tu Lakht-e-Dil-e-Fatima Beti Hai Ali Ki.
Nana Bhi Tera Khalq Ka Sultan Hai Zainab.
Parde Ka Tasawar Hai Tere Naam Se Qayam.
Tu Azmat-e-Mastoor Ka Irfan Hai Zainab.

3
Iss Paak Gharane Ki Hai Tu Saniy-e-Zahra.
Ismat Mein Nabi Zadi Ki Tu Jan Hai Zainab.
Momin Ho Koi Momina Sab Tere Fidai
Umat Tere Aysar Peh Qurban Hai Zainab.

4
Jiss Ne Bhi Aqidat Se Tujhe Yaad Kiya.
Tu Misl-e-Ali Oss Pe Meharban Hai Zainab.
Tu Deen Ki Nusrat Ke Liye Ban Gayi Haider.
Dunya Tere Iss Jazbe Peh Heran Hai Zainab.

5
Qurban Rah-e-Haq Mein Kiye Ayoun-o-Muhammad.
Yeh Bhi Tu Tera Deen Peh Ehsan Hai Zainab.
 Mumtaz Ko Sadqe Mein Mila Tere Sabhi Kuch.
Mill Jaye Yeh Rutbah Kahan Asaan Hai Zainab.
