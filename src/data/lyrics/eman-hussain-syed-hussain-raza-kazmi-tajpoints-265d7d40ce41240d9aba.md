1
Eman Hussain Syed Hussain Raza Kazmi.
Unn Ka Tauheed Pe Mazboot Hai Eman Hussain.
Hai Haqeeqat Mein Jinhein Aap Ki Pehchan Hussain.
Alam-e-Aqal Jo Paye Tera Irfan Hussain.
Hamd Likhein Teri Tauseef Mein Insan Hussain.
Teen Shaban Ki Shab Arsh Pe Parhta Hai Wohi.
Tujh Pe ALLAH Ne Likha Hai Jo Dewan Hussain.
Raaz Yeh Fitrus-o-Rahib Pe Inayat Se Khula.
Tu Jo Keh De Woh Nahi Talta Yazdan Hussain.
Hinduon Ko Yehi Kehte Hoye Main Ne Hai Suna.
Hind Mein Aata Tu Kehte Tujhey Bhagwan Hussain.
Qul Ho Wallah Ahad Ki Tera Sajdah Hai Daleel.
Our Kya Ho Tera Tauheed Pe Ahsan Hussain.
Sath Jibreel Ke Kar Ke Teri Turbat Ka Tawaf.
Madah Karti Hai Teri Sorah-e-Rehman Hussain.
Hoz-e-Kausar Pe Woh Ghazi Ko Sunatay Hoon Gay.
Manqabat Keh Ke Teri Buzar-o-Salman Hussain.
Qalb-e-Ayub Ne Ki Jis Ki Tilawat Har Dam.
Chashm-e-Yaqoob Mein Woh Sabar Ka Quran Hussain.
Har Azza Khane Mein Mimber Jo Tera Sajta Hai.
Oss Pe Hai Rashk Kuna Takht-e-Suleman Hussain.
Hai Asad Hazrat-e-Hur Se Yeh Aqeedat Ka Sila.
Dar Pe Le Aaye Bana Kar Tujhey Mehman Hussain.
