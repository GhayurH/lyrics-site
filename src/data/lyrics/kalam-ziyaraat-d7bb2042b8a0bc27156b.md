Zainab kay khulay saar pe Abid ki nazaar hain

1
Roti hain lahoo aakhay,  aur tukray jigaar hain

2
1) Haar simbt charaghan hai aur Shaam main jashan jaari
Bay dard dareechon say pathron ki hay tayari
Bazaar ki zulmat say,  Zainab ka guzaar hain

3
2) Hy Shaam ki galliyon main Abid nay di sadayain
Zehra ki betiyoon ko day di koyi ridayain
Is zulm ki nagri main,  koyi aisay bashar hain

4
3) Zainab kay guzarnay ka jab rasta banaye
Seenay peh karay matam Sajjad tarap jaaye
Beemar ko ghaash aaye,  hota na safaar hain

5
4) Sajjad chaloon kaisay chalna na mujhe aaye
Mahool sharabhi main bay parda mujhe laaye
Jaati hai jidhaar Zainab,  darbaar udhaar hain

6
5) Gir gir kay sambhalta hay youn koray na barsawo
Bayhosh hay sajday main Abid peh reham khawo
Shabbir bay kafan ka,  maghmun pisaar hain

7
6) Pucha kisi nay akaar jaab saarbarn say ja kay
Tukray how kaleja kin manzalon peh ja kay
Abid pukaray logo,  woh Shaam shehar hain
