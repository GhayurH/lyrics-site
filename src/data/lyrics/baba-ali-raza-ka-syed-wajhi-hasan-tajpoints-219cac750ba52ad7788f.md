1
Baba Ali Raza Ka Syed Wajhi Hasan.
Tar Khon Ho Chuka Hai Baba Ali Raza Ka.
Zindaan Mein Chal Basa Hai Baba Ali Raza Ka.
Zindaan Mein Aik Bachi Kazim Ko Yaad Aayi.
Jhankar Beriyon Ki Abid Ka Gham Sunati.
Youn Khon Ro Raha Hai Baba Ali Raza Ka.
Pehlu Pe Hath Rakhay Zahra Bhi Aa Gayi Hai.
Taboot Ke Qareen Se Aawaz Aa Rahi Hai.
Mazloom-o-Be Khata Hai Baba Ali Raza Ka.
Sokhay Hoye Labon Se Parhta Raha Hai Noha.
Shabeer Ki Tarha Se Yeh Bhi Raha Hai Pyasa.
Tashna Guzar Gaya Hai Baba Ali Raza Ka.
Dena Ossay Dilasa Khud Ko Woh Ab Sanbhalay.
Masooma Meri Beti Hai Ab Tere Hawalay.
Betay Se Keh Raha Hai Baba Ali Raza Ka.
Maula Hasan Ne Jis Dam Tukray Jigar Ke Ugalay.
Mojood Thi Behan Bhi Hathoon Mein Tasht Le Ke.
Tanha Tarap Raha Hai Baba Ali Raza Ka.
 Asim Wajih Dar Pe Jo Chaho Ja Ke Mango.
Apni Dua Mein Unn Ki Beti Ka Wasta Do.
Yeh Bhi Yad-e-Sakha Hai Baba Ali Raza Ka.
