Karbobala mein toota sitam ka jo aasmaan
Dil reza reza ho gaya ya Sahib az-Zamaan

1
Kis tarah dein woh Zainab o Laila ko hausle
Dil thaame apna khaak pe Shabbir gir pare
Lehje mein Mustafa ke di Akbar ne jab azaan

2
Roya hun kis qadar ke bandhi meri hichkiyaan
Ik maa pe kaisa waqt tha main kya karu bayaan
Sunta hun woh sunaati thi jhule ko loriyaan

3
Laasha kya dikh gaya unhein Haider ke sher ka
Nau laakh ghursawaaron ko ik khel mil gaya
Tori hain dor dor ke Ghazi ki pasliyaan

4
Ik din mein apne pyaaron ke laashon ko ro liya
Ab dekhta behan ko hai majme mein berida
Naize pe bhi na khatm hua Sheh ka imtehaan

5
Shaami khare hain naizon pe chaadar liye hue
Aur kuch khare hain haathon mein patthar liye hue
Kehte hain aane do zara Haider ki betiyaan

6
Maula gham-e-Husain mein khunrang khaak hai
Dil gham se phat raha hai jigar chaak chaak hai
Khaimon se uth raha hai Asad Azmi dhuan
