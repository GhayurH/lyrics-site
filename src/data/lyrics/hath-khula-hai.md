Lehrata Alam Dekh Ke Youn Lagta Hai Mujh Ko
Jaise Keh Koi Bab-e-Munajaat Khula Hai
Dunya Hai Tere Dar Ki Gada Iss Liye Ghazi
Dunya Ko Khabar Hai Keh Tera Haath Khula Hai
