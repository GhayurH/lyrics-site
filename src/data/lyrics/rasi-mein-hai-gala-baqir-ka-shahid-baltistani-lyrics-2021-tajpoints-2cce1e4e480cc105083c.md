1
Rasi Mein Hai Gala Baqir Ka Shahid Baltistani.
Chal Para Karb-o-Bala Se Jab Hussaini Qafila.
Ik Rasan Mein Pusht-e-Abid Our Tha Baqir Ka Gala.
Sans Khanjar Ki Tarha Mehsos Jab Hone Lagi.
Apne Baba Jan Se Ro Kar Yeh Baqir Ne Kaha.
Aye Mere Baba Aye Mere Baba.
Rassi Mein Gala Hai Baqir Ka Tu Holay Holay Chal Baba.
Mushkil Hai Safar Mar Jaon Ga Tu Holay Holay Chal Baba.
Parhni Hai Namaz-e-Mazlomi Baba Tu Imamat Karta Ja.
Khud Dhop Bhi Hum Pe Roti Hai Jalta Hai Musalla Reti Ka.
Main Shukar Ka Sajdah Kar Loon Ada Tu Holay Holay Chal Baba.
Kulsoom Sakina Zainab Hai Our Sath Ruqaiya BiBi Hai.
Iss Aik Rasan Mein Aye Baba Har Umar Ki Zahra Qaidi Hai.
Girti Hai Meri Amma Fizza Tu Holay Holay Chal Baba.
Ab Dhop Mein Itni Shiddat Hai Jaltay Hain Aseeri Ke Zewar.
Pani Tu Nahi Hai Aanso Hi Main Inn Pe Chirak Doon Ga Aa Kar.
Aaram Tujhe Mil Jaye Ga Tu Holay Holay Chal Baba.
Aankhon Se Rawaan Hai Khon Tere Jo Teri Phophi Hai Be Pardah.
Meri Bhi Phophi Hai Be Chadar Woh Naam Sakina Hai Jis Ka.
Khichta Hai Rasan Se Oss Ka Gala Tu Holay Holay Chal Baba.
Islam Bachaney Ki Khatir Nikli Hai Sharafat Be Chadar.
Paon Ke Chalay Rotay Hain Chalti Hai Imamat Kanton Par.
Yeh Pehla Safar Hai Zainab Ka Tu Holay Holay Chal Baba.
Dekha Nahi Jata Aye Baba Mujh Se Yeh Qayamat Ka Manzar.
Rukti Hai Kabhi Jalti Hai Kabhi Zainab Ke Badaltay Hain Tewar.
Yeh Sham Ko Jata Hai Rasta Tu Holay Holay Chal Baba.
Gir Gir Ke Jo Chalti Hai Zainab Mehsos Mujhe Bhi Hota Hai.
Sajjad Pukarey Aye Beta Mera Bhi Kaleja Phatta Hai.
Jab Sunta Hoon Tera Noha Tu Holay Holay Chal Baba.
Gar Taiz Chala Tu Aye Beta Bazar-e-Sitam Aa Jaye Ga.
Aahista Agar Beemar Chaley Durron Ki Aziyat Khaye Ga.
Our Tere Labon Par Hai Beta Tu Holay Holay Chal Baba.
Manzoor Aziyat Hai Mujh Ko Baqir Ne Takallum Di Yeh Sada.
Har Gaam Pe Teri Marzi Hai Tu Jaisay Chahe Chal Baba.
Main Ab Na Kahon Ga Aye Baba Tu Holay Holay Chal Baba.
