1
Nabi Ki Goad Ka Pala Khuda Shiaar Hussain.
Ibadatoon Ka Bharam Deen Ka Wiqar Hussain.
Hai Bab-e-Ilm Ka Anmool Shahkar Hussain.
Tahafzaat Shariyat Ka Zaimedar Hussain.
Tasarfaat Mile Hain Nabi Se Ghuti Mein.
Tamam Aalam-e-Imkaan Hain Iss Ki Muthi Mein.
Sune Yeh Sara Jahan Ibn-e-Abu Turab Hai Yeh.
Kitab-e-Azmat-e-Islam Ka Nisab Hai Yeh.
Gharoob Hota Nahi Aisa Aaftab Hai Yeh.
Yeh Khandan-e-Muhammad Ka Shahzada Hai.
Nabi Ko Palne Wala Issi Ka Dada Hai.
Yeh Moajza Hai Tu Bas Moajza Dikhata Hai.
Bala Ki Taiz Hawa Mein Diya Jalata Hai.
Zameen Ko Arsh Ki Dehleez Se Milata Hai.
Yeh Chahta Hai Tu Ik Shab Mein Hur Banata Hai.
Mila Hai Sari Khudai Pe Iqtidar Isse.
Khuda Ne De Diye Sab Apne Ikhtiyar Isse.
Karm Nawaz Nazar Katib-e-Naseeb Hussain.
Adab Salam Kare Jis Ko Woh Adeeb Hussain.
Sina Ki Nook Pe ALLAH ka Khateeb Hussain.
Yazeediyat Ke Har Ik Marz Ka Tabeeb Hussain.
Khuda Misal Hai Jab Chahe Kya Se Kya Kar De.
Hussain Maut Ko Bhi Zindagi Atta Kar De.
Gham-e-Hussain Ke Dushman Aye Munkir-e-Sarwar.
Aye Fatway Baaz Munafiq Yazeedani-e-Shar.
Ulat De Bugz Ke Parde Ko Rukh Se Tang Nazar.
Hamare Kale Libasoon Pe Aytraz Na Kar.
Siya Poshi Agar Kufr Ka Hawala Hai.
Khuda Ke Ghar Ka Bata Kyun Ghilaf Kala Hai.
Diyar-e-Ilm Misal-e-Najaf Hussain Ka Dar.
Ali Ke Lal Ka Zahra Ke Noor-e-Ain Ka Dar.
Hasan Ke Bhai Ka Mola-e-Sadqain Ka Dar.
Hai Be-Misal Jahan Shah-e-Mashreqain Ka Dar.
Dar-e-Hussain Se Yasir Jo Bheek Leta Hai.
Woh Ahl-e-Ilm Ko Khairat-e-Ilm Deta Hai.
