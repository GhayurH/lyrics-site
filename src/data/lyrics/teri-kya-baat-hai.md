1
Teri Kya Baat Hai
Sun Ke Zainab Ke Khutbaat Darbar Mein Bole Shah-e-Zaman Teri Kya Baat Hai
Laaj Rakh Li Hai Tu Ne Bare Bahi Ki Meri Choti Behan Teri Kya Baat Hai
Theek Se Koi Leta Na Naam-e-Khuda

2
Gar Na Hoti Bapa Dahar Mein Karbala
Our zinda Rakhi Tu Ne WO Karbala Bint-e-Khaiber Shikan
Ghair Ke Zikar Par Tilmilata Hai Tu
Zikar-e-Haider Pe Bas Muskurata Hai Tu

3
Dehan Rakhta Hai Youn Mere Jazbaat Ka Mere Mula-E-Mani
Aasmano Se Utari Hai Meri Tarha
Teri Khilqat Bhi Noori Hai Meri Tarha
Fatima Ne Kaha Zulfiqar-e-Ali Meri Choti Behan

4
Tujh Ko Aakar Salami Sitare Ne Di
Teri Jaisi Kissi Ki Na Shadi Hoi
Aqad Tera Khuda Ne Parha Arsh Par Murtaza Ki Dulhan
Youn Bhi Mehboob Rakhte Hain Tujh Ko Nabi

5
Tu Ne Pehli Gawahi Wilayat Ki Di
Tujh Ko Hasil Hai Heeron Pe Youn Bartari Aye Aqeeq-e-Yaman
Apne Shohar Ko Marne Se Roka Nahi
Kis Tarha Se Jiyon Gi Ye Socha Nahi

6
Teri Himat tere Hosle Ko Salam Aye Wahab Ki Dulhan
Aisa Be-Lafz Asghar Ne Khutba Diya
Jis Ki Haibat Se Maidaan Larzne Laga
:MIR SAJJAD: Khaliq Ki Ayi Sada Aye Khuda-e-Sukhan
