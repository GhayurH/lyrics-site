1
Kisnay Mara Hay Sakina Waheed Rizvi.
Sar Yeh Shabeer Ka Kehta Tha Hazeena Kis Ne Mara Hai Sakina.
Khoon Mein Tar Kurta Hai Kyun Tera Sakina Kis Ne Mara Hai Sakina.
Apne Baba Ko Batao Zara Rodad-e-Safar.
Neel Durron Ke Hain Kyun Beti Tere Shano Par.
Kis Ne Marey Hain Tamachay Tere Beti Munh Par.
Itni Be Rehmi Se Cheeney Hain Tere Kis Ne Gohar.
Laaj Kis Ne Tere Bachpan Ki Rakhi Na.
Chin Gayi Kaisay Mere Baad Tumhari Chadar.
Sar Meri Rahat-e-Dil Ka Hai Yeh Kyun Khon Mein Tar.
Zalimo Ne Kahan Shehzadi Ko Marey Pathar.
Dil Mein Jo Gham Hain Batao Tu Mujhe Jan-e-Padar.
Haye Kab Se Meri Bachi Tu Hansi Na.
Kyun Hai Badli Hoi Chehray Ki Tumhare Rangat.
Kaisay Poshaak Jali Kaisay Hoi Yeh Halat.
Haye Labon Par Tere Hai Pyas Ki Kitni Shiddat.
Paon Mein Chalay Hain Kyun Kaisi Uthai Zehmat.
Apne Ghar Mein Kabhi Tum Itna Chali Na.
Baba Logon Ne Tere Baad Hai Mujh Ko Mara.
Shah Zadi Ko Kaneezi Ke Liye Bhi Manga.
Aisay Sadaat Ki Hurmat Ko Bhi Pamal Kiya.
Dena Chaha Tha Mujhe Logon Ne Aa Kar Sadqa.
Mujhe Se Pocha Yeh Kissi Ne Bhi Kabhi Na.
Chalte Naqay Se Giratay They Kabhi Mujh Ko Laeen.
Zakhmi Hoti Rahi Baba Meri Gir Gir Ke Jabeen.
Aap Ki Yaad Mein Jab Jab Bhi Main Roti Thi Kahin.
Taziyana Liye Aa Jata Tha Phir Shimar Wahin.
Phir Nazar Meri Tere Sar Se Hatti Na.
Sar Mila Baba Ka Zindaan Mein Tu Itna Roi.
Rakh Ke Sar Baba Ke Sar Pe Na Asad Phir Boli.
Ro Ke Bas Itna Kaha Baba Na Ab Jana Kahin.
Rotay Rotay Hoye Masoma Jo Khamosh Hoi.
Sar Se Aati Thi Sada Bolo Sakina.
