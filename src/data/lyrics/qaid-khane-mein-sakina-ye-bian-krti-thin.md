1
Qaid khanay may Sakina ye bayan karti thi - Kya andhera hay phupee
Shamma mangwaeeye warna may nahi jeenay ki - Kya andhera hay phupee
1) Apnay seenay se lagalo may tumharay sadqay - goad me lay lo mujhe
Dil dharakta hay mera khauf se tharrata hay ji - kya andhera hay phupee
Qaid khane may Sakina ye bayan karti thi - Kya andhera hay phupee
2) Kyon layeen qaid me laye hain mujhe kya maloom - haye baba mazloom
Gaye maidaan ko jis din se khabar meri na li - kya andhera hay phupee
Qaid khane may Sakina ye bayan karti thi - Kya andhera hay phupee
3) Baba aa jate to phir Shimr ko hota maloom - kaun hay ye masoom
Kya samajh kar mujhay yon ghurkiyan deta hay shaqi - kya andhera hay phupee
Qaid khane may Sakina ye bayan karti thi - Kya andhera hay phupee
4) Aaj ki shab ko bhi kya baba nahi aayengay - mujhko tarpayain gay
Unke seenay pe agar soti to neend aa jati - kya andhera hay phupee
Qaid khane may Sakina ye bayan karti thi - Kya andhera hay phupee
5) Baad mere jo yahan howe pidar ka aana - unse ye farmana
Mar gaye ghut ke andheray may tumhari beti - kya andhera hay phupee
Qaid khane may Sakina ye bayan karti thi - Kya andhera hay phupee
6) Sabr karti hoon magar ho nahi sakta mujhe se - yaan se lay chaliye mujhe
Warna pachtao gi zinda na rahoon gi may kabhi - kya andhera hay phupee
Qaid khane may Sakina ye bayan karti thi - Kya andhera hay phupee
7) Pani lanay ko kahan rooth gaye mujh se chahca - aap hi sochain zara
Hotay aamo to may is tarha tamachay khati - kya andhera hay phupee
Qaid khane may Sakina ye bayan karti thi - Kya andhera hay phupee
8) Poochnay ki meri un say nahi himmat parti - aey meri achchi phupee
Mujh ko batlaye kiyun roti hain chup chup kay chahci - kya andhera hay phupee
Qaid khane may Sakina ye bayan karti thi - Kya andhera hay phupee
