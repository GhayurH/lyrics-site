Our hearts are bleeding on your destined pain.
How can we leave you ya Hussain?

1
(1) While you have put off all the empty lamps;
While you have pulled up all the torn curtains;
While this darkness screams a cry of silence;
The cries of Zainab”s sorrow, we can listen...

2
(2) The little girl has kept her hopes for water high with us;
With a broken cup to us from tent to tent she keeps on run;
The hiccups of the infant tear our hearts so deeply;
Then why do you ask us to go, how can we leave?

3
(3) We see wrinkles covering your face in a night”s span;
You”re asking us to leave; are you doubting with who we stand;
Please take this sword and kill us now for Zainab to believe;
But our lives are just for you. For you our lives from us can leave.

4
How can we leave you Ya Hussain? How can we leave you Ya Hussain?
(4) We know Oh Master, you don”t need us to be here;
Because your brother, the standard-bearer is here;
But how will we answer your grand-father on the Reckoning Day,
Should we leave you all alone on Ashura, we fear.

5
(5) Since the day, you”ve camped in this barren land;
We hear a lady scream and wail your name at nights;
We know Zahra has come to witness Karbala”s fight;
She cries this out with us, wailing on your plight…
