Hussain
Halo momino halo Karbala

1
Manzoor kai Zahra dua
Razi nabbi razi khuda

2
Manzoor kai Zahra dua
Razi nabbi razi khuda

3
Aey momino tayyar theyo manzoor the pehnji dua
Ibn-e-alia ja aashiqo gadh ji kayo shukr-e-khuda
Moula naseeban main likhi zawwari-e-shah-e-huda

4
Manzoor kai Zahra dua
Razi nabbi razi khuda

5
Khuld-e-bareen aa Karbala sardar aa moula hussain
In khoobsoorat baagh jo singhaar aa moula hussain
Karbal main zawwaran mathaa rehmat wasey the ja ba ja

6
Manzoor kai Zahra dua
Razi nabbi razi khuda

7
Moulaiyo in maa ayan touqeer aa abbas ji
Hi Karbala ji sar zameen jageer aa abbas ji
Aa hey awaan jo muntazir ghazi alamdar-e-wafa

8
Manzoor kai Zahra dua
Razi nabbi razi khuda

9
Shabbira ja zawwar thee arshia chan farshia chan
Mazlooma ja sabh matami khak-e-shifa khey ta chuman
Hoor o malak jin o bashar sabh noukari kan tha adaa

10
Manzoor kai Zahra dua
Razi nabbi razi khuda

11
Karbobala tey momino ehsaan aa shabbira jo
Ohna jaaey tey sajdo kayo moula jithey sajdo kayo
Manzil asaan ji Karbala
Hee aa asaan ji darsgaah

12
Manzoor kai Zahra dua
Razi nabbi razi khuda

13
Jawwad sarwar jaa okhey Zainab kithey rooi saggi
Mazloomaan jey laashey wata chup chaap the guzri waai
Ohna jaae tey kariyo halli
Shabbira jo matam bapa

14
Manzoor kai Zahra dua
Razi nabbi razi khuda
