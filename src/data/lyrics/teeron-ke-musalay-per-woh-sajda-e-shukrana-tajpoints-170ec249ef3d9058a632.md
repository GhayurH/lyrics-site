1
Shabir Ne Batlaya Islam Pe Mar Jana.
Kuch Iss Tarha Lash Ayi Ik Raat ke Biyahe Ki.
Afsoos Keh Madar Ne Bete Ko Na Pehchana.
Socho Tu Musalmano Yeh Baat Koi Kam Hai.
Ahmad Ki Nawasi Ka Darbar Mein Aa Jana.
Haider Ki Shujat Ka Andaz Nazar Aaya.
Woh Sham-e-Ghareeban Mein Zainab Ka Na Ghabrana.
Mula Ki Wasiyat Thi Abbas Dilawar Ko.
Talwar Nahi Khainchi Aaqa Ka Kaha Mana.
Ik Teer Ali Asghar Ki Gardan Mein Laga Aa kar.
Masoom Ka Hans Dena Our Maut Ka Ghabrana.
Barchi Ali AKbar Ke Seene Se Nikal Ayi.
Dekha Na Giya Sheh Se Youn Dil Ka Nikal Aana.
Darbar Mein Logon Se Fizza Ne Kaha Ro Kar.
Aati Hai Nabi Zadi Tazeem Ko Jhuk Jana.
Ta-Hashar Rulaye Ga Mula Ki Teyari Par.
Duldul Ki Rakabon Se Beti Ka Lipat Jana.
Maiyat Ali Asghar Ki Hathon Pe Utha Bole.
ALLAH Tere Aagay Hai Shabir Ka Nazrana.
Bhole Ga Zamane Ko Manzar Na Kabhi Nasir.
Masoom Ki Maiyat Ko Shabir Ka Dafnana.
 Nasir Ka Yeh Dawa Hai Banta Hai Hussain Aise.
Sanche Mein Imamat Ke Quran Ka Dhal Aana.
