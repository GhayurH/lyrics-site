1
Aik Abbas Ko No Lakh Ne Mil Kar Mara.
Teer Mara Kabhi Neza Kabhi Pathar Mara.
Sar-e-Abbas Ko Nezay Pe Uthane Ke Liye.
Rakh Ke Sar Khak Pe Nezay Ko Hai Oss Par Mara.

2
Jeetay Jee Kar Diye Abbas Ke Tukray Tukray.
Our Tukron Ko Bahatar Ke Barabar Mara.
Shimar Ne Jab Bhi Sakina Ko Tamacha Mara.
Sar-e-Abbas Ke Nazdeek Bola Kar Mara.

3
Lash-e-Abbas Pe Youn Jashan Manatay They Laeen.
Jaisay Ghazi Nahi Shabbir Ka Lashkar Mara.
Mashk Pe Teer Nahi Mara Hai Hurmal Tu Ne.
Dar Haqeeqat Hai Yeh Abbas Ke Dil Par Mara.

4
Fatima Zahra Ko Ghar Ja Ke Tamachay Marey.
Majma e Aam Mein Zainab Ko Bola Kar Mara.
Bolein Zainab Ke Yateemi Ka Howa Hai Ahsas.
Jab Se Ummat Ne Mera Shair Dilawar Mara.
