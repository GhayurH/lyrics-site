Chupa Teron Mein Kyun Aise Janaza Mujtaba Ka Hai.
Hai Iss Ka Jurm Bas Itna Yeh Beta Fatima Ka Hai.

1
Yeh Keh Ke Teer Maiyyat Pe Hain Barsaye Musalmaan Ne.
Issay Maro Kafan Mein Bhi Yeh Pyara Mustafa Ka Hai.
Jisay Sun Kar Muhammad Hanfiya Ko Bhi Hai Gash Aaya.
Hasan Ki Pak Maiyyat Pe Woh Noha Aliya Ka Hai.

2
Ali Qasim Na Ho Tanha Hamesha Pas Tum Rehna.
Ali Akbar Se Ro Kehta Musafir Karbala Ka Hai.
 Nisar Aal-e-Muhammad Ko Hai Khata Chadron Ka Gham.
Rulata Mujtaba Ko Bhi Lehad Mein Gham Rida Ka Hai.
