1
Gonji Bawaqat-e-Asar Sada Main Hussain Hoon.
Kato Na Mera Khushk Gala Main Hussain Hoon.
Amma Kissi Ne Pani Ki Ik Bond Bhi Na Di.
Main Bar Bar Kehta Raha Main Hussain Hoon.

2
Sokhi Hoi Hain Shimar(L) Mere Halk Ki Ragein.
Ahista Tu Churi Ko Chala Main Hussain Hoon.
Sheerein Ke Ghar Pe Pohancha Jab Randoon Ka Kafila.
Naize Se Aik Sar Ne Kaha Main Hussain Hoon.

3
Darbar Mein Laein Ke Kohram Mach Giya.
Mukhtar Se Jo Sar Ne Kaha Main Hussain Hoon.
Zano Pe Rakh Ke Sar Ko Kaha Ik Zaeef Ne.
Akbar Kahan Hai Dard Bata Main Hussain Hoon.

4
Aye Shamiyon Kafan Na Diya Mujh Ko Gham Nahi.
De Do Meri Behan Ko Rida Main Hussain Hoon.
Mehsoos Ho Raha Hai Ke Maqtal Se Aaj Bhi.
Awaz Aa Rahi Hai Raza Main Hussain Hoon.
