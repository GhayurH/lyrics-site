1
Hashar Mein Zahra Pukari Gham Sunanay Aayi Hoon.
Pasliyaan Tooti Thin Kaisay Yeh Bataney Aayi Hoon.
Kis Tarha Mujh Ko Musalmano Ne Tha Zakhmi Kiya.
Mar Ke Mujh Ko Tamacha Badla Haider Se Liya.
Neel Hai Taza Abhi Tak Yeh Dikhaney Aayi Hoon.
Jab Gayi Darbar Tehreer Muhammad Pas Thi.
Kar Chukay Tukray Musalmaan Tab Se Dil Mein Aas Thi.
Kya Sand Par Tha Likha Sab Kuch Bataney Aayi Hoon.
Mere Mohsin Ke Liye Veeran Dunya Ho Gayi.
Jan Apni Apne Baba Ki Wilayat Par Jo Di.
Haan Ossi Bachay Ko Oss Ka Haq Dilaney Aayi Hoon.
Hai Yehi Fitrat Ke Beta Maa Ke Pehlu Mein Rahe.
Kaisay Batlaon Ke Jab Sab Dar Ke Opar Se Gaye.
Jo Bhi Kuch Hasnain Ne Dekha Bataney Aayi Hoon.
Mera Maula Haq Pe Tha Par Haq Mila Na Kyun Ossay.
Hath Pehlu Par Rakhay Ghar Ghar Gayi Main Kis Liye.
Thi Wilayat Sab Pe Wajib Yeh Bataney Aayi Hoon.
Pochta Tha Jab Koi Haider Se Mushkil Kab Barhi.
Ro Ke Kehte They Mere Jab Samne Zojah Giri.
Gham Ossi Mushkil Kusha Ke Main Barhanay Aayi Hoon.
Layi Hoon Main Khoon Bhara Kurta Mere Shabeer Ka.
Our Jis Se Chid Gaya Nana Gala Be Sheer Ka.
Teen Phal Ka Teer Mehshar Mein Dikhaney Aayi Hoon.
Sham Ki Rahoon Mein Bhi Our Qaid Mein Na So Saki.
Shimar Ke Dar Se Abhi Tak Hai Woh Bachi Jagti.
Main Sakina Ko Sakoon Se Ab Solaney Aayi Hoon.
Meri Turbat Par Assa Le Ke Chali Aati Thi Woh.
Bhai Bhai Keh Ke Har Din Haye Gash Khati Thi Woh.
Haan Ossi Sughra Ko Akbar Se Milaney Aayi Hoon.
Apne Zakhmi Hath Se Jis Shair Ko Manga Sada.
Kat Gaye Shaney Bhi Oss Ke Aankh Mein Bhi Teer Tha.
Do Alam Hathoon Ke Oss Ke Main Sajane Aayi Hoon.
Hashar Ke Maidaan Mein Boli Aye Raza Bint-e-Nabi.
Chadrein Looti Hain Jin Hathoon Ne Ahl-e-Bait Ki.
Haan Unhi Hathoon Ko Dozakh Mein Jalanay Aayi Hoon.
