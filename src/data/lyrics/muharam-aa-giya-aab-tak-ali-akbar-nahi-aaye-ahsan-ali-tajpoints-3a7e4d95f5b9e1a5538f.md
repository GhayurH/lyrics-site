1
Muharam Aa Giya Aab Tak Ali Akbar Nahi Aaye Ahsan Ali.
Jo Dekha Chand Sughra Ne Kaha Dill Thaam Kar Haye.
Koi Tu Aye Dam Seene Mein Atka Hai Mere Bahi.
Khabar Aye Keh Tum Ayo Keh Neend Aye Keh Maut Aye.

2
Take Jati Hai Rasta Aik Tuk Dehleez Par Baithe.
Hawa Aye Keh Gard Aye Keh Abar Aye Keh Dhoop Aye.
Tabiyat Ghar Mein Ghabrai Tu Uth Kar Dar Pe Aa Bethi.
Kahan Jaye Woh Uth Kar Aab Agar Dill Oss Ka Ghabaraye.

3
Koi Aata Hai Darwaza Khula Rakhne Se Aye Sughra.
Nahi Aata Koi Dill Ko Tere Yeh Koan Samjhaye.
Hawa Se Khak Urte Sab Ke Hujray Dekh Kar Veeraan.
Kare Kya Gar Woh Deewaron Se Apna Sar Na Takraye.

4
Jo Pohncha Nama Bar Khat Le Ke Sughra Ka Tu Kab Pohancha.
Pare The Dill Ko Pakre Khak Par Akbar Sina Khaye.
 Naveed Oss Ke Labon Par Bas Yehi Noha Tha Aye Bahi.
Kiya Tha Wada Aane Ka Na Subha Aye Na Shaam Aye.
