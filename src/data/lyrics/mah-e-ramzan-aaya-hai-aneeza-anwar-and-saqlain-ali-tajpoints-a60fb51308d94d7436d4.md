1
Mah-e-Ramzan Aaya Hai Aneeza Anwar and Saqlain Ali.
Mah-e-Ramzan Aaya Hai Humein Khushiyaan Atta Karne.
Jhuka Hai Sar Yeh Sajday Mein Chalein Razi Khuda Karne.
Mubarak Ho Keh Phir Tum Ne Mah-e-Ramzan Paya Hai.

2
Issi Mah-e-Muqadas Mein Suno Quran Aaya Hai.
Khuda Ne Tohfa Bhaija Hai Karam Ki Inteha Karne.
Maheena Hai Yeh ALLAH Ka Khuda Ki Khas Rehmat Hai.
Musalmano Ki Iss Mah Se Bari Hi Khas Ragbat Hai.

3
Waseela Bach Ke Bakhshish Ka Yeh Aaya Hai Atta Karne.
Fizaein Gaunj Uthi Hain Duroodon Se Salamo Se.
Aagaz-e-Sahar Hoti Hai Khuda Ke Pak Namo Se.
Chaley Hain Nam-e-Khaliq Se Hi Din Ki Ibtida Karne.

4
Bara Hi Khas Rakhna Hai Khiyal Ab Rozay Daroon Ka.
Sabeelon Se-o-Langar Se Sabhi ALLAH Ke Pyaron Ka.
Bulao Ghar Mein Apne Tum Unhein Roza Kusha Karne.
Masajid Bhi Hoi Aabad Hain Zikar-o-Ibadat Se.

5
Jabeen Roshan Hain Dil Roshan Muhabbat Se Sakhawat Se.
Keh Ab Masroof Hilqat Hai Sabhi Jod-o-Sakha Karne.
Gunahon Pe Jo Nadam Hain Tu Rakh Unn Ka Bharam Maula.
Mujeeb-o-Dawa Tu Hi Hai Dikha Apna Karam Maula.

6
Jhukein Darbar-e-Ahad Mein Yeh Ashkon Se Dua Karne.
Yeh Rehmat Hai Yeh Barkat Hai Jahanum Se Hai Aazadi.
Hai Lailatul Qadar Iss Mein Hai Yeh Shaitaan Ki Hai Barbadi.
Likha Hai Kalam Azhar Ne Sabhi Ka Hi Bhala Karne.
