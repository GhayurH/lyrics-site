Zehra Sachi Hain Mir Sajjad Mir Lyrics

1
Sachay Ho Tu Sab Ko Batana Zahra Sachi Hain.
Jhota Hai Yeh Sara Zamana Zahra Sachi Hain.
Fakhar-e-Mariyam Hukam Jo Detein Gasib Ke Darbar Mein Bhi.
Aate Falak Se Kehne Esa, Zahra Sachi Hain.
Fizza Ne Pocha Main Ne Suna Hai Aap Falak Par They Masroof.
Bolay Ali Hans Kar Aye Fizza, Zahra Sachi Hain.

2
Aakhri Matamdar Kahey Ga Jate Hoye Yeh Janat Mein.
Pora Kiya Hai Apna Wadah Zahra Sachi Hain.
Kitne An Parh Jahil They Tum Samjhay Nahi Tehreer-e-Nabi.
Tum Ko Nabi Ne Likh Ke Diya Tha Zahra Sachi Hain.
Maa Se Dulhan Ki Maut Yeh Boli Malka-e-Kun Hain Yeh BiBi.
Beti Ho Jaye Gi Zindah Zahra Sachi Hain.

3
Eid Ke Kaprey Aa Jaein Gay Kab Our Kaisay Rab Jane.
Beton Ko Tu Itna Pata Tha Zahra Sachi Hain.
Zahra Ko Jhutlaya Jis Dam Chup They Madine Walay Magar.
Cheekh Raha Tha Roza Nabi Ka Zahra Sachi Hain.
Khuwab Mein Jane Kaun Sa Manzar Dekha Kamsin Shabar Ne.
Chonk Ke Kehta Tha Shehzada Zahra Sachi Hain.

4
Honton Par Tatheer Ki Aayat Qabar Ki Mati Hathoon Mein.
Kehte They Ro Ro Ke Maula, Zahra Sachi Hain.
Wadah Kiya Tha Betay Se Main Zibha Ke Waqt Aa Jaon Gi.
Dekho Nibhaya Apna Wadah Zahra Sachi Hain.
Door Ke Matha Chom Liya Beti Ne Ali Ki Aye Sajjad.
Ghazi Ne Pehli Bar Jo Bola Zahra Sachi Hain.
