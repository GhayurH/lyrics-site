Shabir Ne Ro Ro Ke Pukara Ali Akbar.
Youn Choor Ke Na Jao Khudara Ali Akbar.

1
Hum Jeete Rahein Our Tumhe Aaye Shahadat Kaisi Hai Qayamat.
Baba Ko Nahi Tere Gawara Ali Akbar.
Na Aoun-o-Muhammad Hain Na Qasim Hain Na Ghazi Kya Ayi Tabahi.
Ghar Lut Gaya Pardais Mein Sara Ali Akbar.

2
Umat Ke Liye Tum Ne Lutai Yeh Jawani Aye Yousaf-e-Sani.
Ahsan Hai Yeh Hum Pe Tumhara Ali Akbar.
Jab Teri Shahadat Ki Khabar Paye Gi Zainab Mar Jaye Gi Zainab.
Phir De Ga Osse Kon Sahara Ali Akbar.

3
Warid Howay Shabir Jo Lashe Pe Pisar Ke Dil Tham Ke Bole.
Be-Reham Tha Jis Ne Tujhe Mara Ali Akbar.
 Yasir Kaha Sarwar Ne Ke Dam Toor Rahe Ho Munh Moor Rahe Ho.
Hum Jan Gaye Tera Ishara Ali Akbar.
