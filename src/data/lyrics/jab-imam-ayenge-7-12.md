Jab Imam Ayenge 7-12

1
Dawa-e-Muhabbat Jo Subho-o-Sham Karte Hain
Unn Ke Dushmano Se BHi Rah-o-Rasam Rakhte Hain
Khums Bhi Nahi Dete Gheebatein Bhi Karte Hain
Momino Se Bhi Dil Mein Bugaz-o-Keena Rakhte Hain
Kis Tarha Nibhaein Gaye Jab Imam Ayenge
Jhoot Bolne Ko Hum Mashgala Samajte Hain

2
Baat Baat Par Beja Maslehat Baratte Hain
Our Munafiqat Ko Bhi Maslehat Hi Kehte Hain
Laho-o-Loab Ke Samaan Hum Gharon Mein Rakhte Hain
Kis Tarha Chupaein Gaye Jab Imam Ayenge
Naimat-e-Shariyat Ko Bojh Hi Samajhte Hain
Laho-o-Loab Hi Ko Hum Zindagi Samajhte Hain

3
Sahiban-e-Zar Ko Bara Aadmi Samajhte Hain
Tang Dast Momin Ko Bas Younhi Samajhte Hain
Kya Moqam Paein Gaye Jab Imam Ayenge
Apne Zati Dushman Ko Dozakhi Samajhte Hain
Apne Aap Ko Lekin Janati Samajhte Hain
Amar Our Nehi Ko Bas Wajbi Samajhte Hain

4
Dil Faraib Baton Ko Shayari Samajhte Hain
Baat Kya Banaein Gaye Jab Imam Ayenge
Alajal Jo Kehte Hain Aa Gaye Tu Kya Ho Ga
Kya Hai Apni Teyari Paish Hum Karein Gaye Kya
:SIBT-E-JAFAR: Apna Tu Kul Yehi Sarmaya
Hamd-o-Naat-Soz-o-Salam Our Manqabat Noha

5
Hum Yehi Sunaein Gaye Jab Imam Ayenge
Zehar Nu Imamo Ko Ashqiya Ne Dilwaya
Our Giraya Darwaza Syeda Pe Jalta Howa
Murtaza Se Mula Ko Bhi Shaheed Karwaya
Aal-e-Mustafa Ko Kya Mubtalaye Karb-o-Bala
Kya Saza Na Paein Gaye Jab Imam Ayenge
