Tayra mamnoon hay khuda zainub tu immat da ahsira zainub

Ahoy tareek di gawiyi hay tu islam di baka zainub

Veer nay lailaha bachaya hay

Vich sajd ay day sar kataya hay

Tu bachaya hussain da maksa kithi apni nasal kuha zainub

Matami tay na ahway ohki ghari

Rehna motaj ooway sehn kadi

an rakna eh rozay mehshar tain jari matam da silsila zainab
