Shabbir teray gham main har aankh bawazu hay
Her dil main sog tera kai Khuda-e-Dard tu hay

1
1) Akber ki lash dekho ban main tarap rahi hay
Kai rasool-e-Akharai ka yeh shabab hu bahu hay

2
2) Aain Kalim dekhain yeh kis nabi ka sar hai
Naizai pai jo Khuda sai masruf-e-guftagu hay

3
3) Makhduma do jahan ko jhutladiya jis nai
Derbar us laeen ka Zainab sai rubaru hay

4
4) Bach kai Zaheen rehna kuch log hain jahan main
Dil Main Yazeediyat hai aur lab pai Allahu Hay
