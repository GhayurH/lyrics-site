1
Momino Haider-e-Karrar Ka Matam Kar Lo.
Haan Muhammad Ke Alamdar Ka Matam Kar Lo.
Khak Urao Keh Ali Shair-e- Khuda Marey Gaye.
Bazu-e-Ahmad-e-Mukhtar Ka Matam Kar Lo.

2
Jis Ke Dar Se Na Gaya Khali Sawali Koi.
Oss Sakhi Sahib-e-Kirdar Ka Matam Kar Lo.
Jo Keh Panchon Mein Bhi Hain Barah Imamon Mein Bhi Hain.
Sare Nabiyon Ke Madadgar Ka Matam Kar Lo.

3
Har Wali Kehta Hai Yeh Kar Ke Ali Ka Matam.
Mere Aaqa Mere Sarkar Ka Matam Kar Lo.
Mil Ke Jibreel Ke Ustad Ka Pursa De Do.
Sahib-e-Duldul-o-Talwar Ka Matam Kar Lo.

4
Apne Qatil Ko Diya Jis Ne Keh Jam-e- Shereen.
Kausari Jaam Ke Mukhtar Ka Matam Kar Lo.
Kofa-o-Karb-o-Bala Sham Se Aati Hai Sada.
Ujray Sadaat Ke Gulzar Ka Matam Kar Lo.
