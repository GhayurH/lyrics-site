1
Yeh Gham Woh Gham Hai Keh Dono Jahan Rotay Hain.
Yateem Aaj Imam-e-Zaman Hotey Hain.
Haye Hasan Askari Maula.
Aye Bin-e-Zahra Askari Maula.
Shah-e-Samarra Askari Maula.
Haye Qayamat Ki Ghari Aa Gayi Kaisi.
Saye Se Mehroom Tere Ho Gaye Mehdi.
Ho Ke Juda Rota Raha Aap Ka Beta.
Aye Mere Baba Askari Maula.
Kaisa Aseeri Ka Sitam Tum Pe Howa Hai.
Tere Liye Ghar Tera Zindan Bana Hai.
Maula Tera Ghar Mein Howa Dafan Janaza.
Haye Ghurbata Askari Maula.
Samarra Har Saal Chaley Aate Hain Zawwar.
Aap Ka Taboot Uthatay Hain Azadar.
Farsh-e-Azza Bichta Raha Bichta Rahe Ga.
Lijiye Pursa Askari Maula.
Sare Azadaron Ke Lab Par Yeh Dua Hai.
Wasta Tujh Ko Tere Betay Ka Diya Hai.
Dil Mein Basa Roza Tera Mehdi Ke Baba.
Hum Ko Bolana Askari Maula.
Qaidi-o-Mazloom Mere Gayarwin Sarkar.
Deney Ko Pursa Tumhein Aaye Hain Azadar.
Pursa Qabool Ho Aye Mere Syed-o-Sardar.
Hum Loag Teri Nazar-e-Karam Ke Hain Talabgar.
Gham Yeh Tera Dil Mein Sada Zindah Rahe Ga.
Roti Hai Dunya Askari Maula.
Jab Bhi Tere Naam Ki Hoti Hai Yeh Majlis.
Sath Mein Zahra Ke Chali Aati Hai Narjis.
Dekh Zara Mehway Boka Hain Teri Zojah.
Karti Hain Noha Askari Maula.
Pardah-e-Ghaibat Mein Jo Beta Hai Tumhara.
Bhejein Ossay Jald Pareshan Hai Dunya.
Sun Lo Dua Behar-e-Khuda Waris-e-Kaba.
Hai Yeh Iltija Askari Maula.
 Meesum -o- Azlan Pe Bas Itna Karam Ho.
Naam Tere Chahney Walon Mein Raqam Ho.
Kar Do Atta Hum Ko Sila Nohay Ka Maula.
Khuwab Ho Pora Askari Maula.
