1
Sham De Rahwaan Te Haye Bemar Turda Jaway
Qaidna Naal Aye Mere Fatima Jaiyaan Ne Ro Ro Ke Dasda Jaway.
Fikar Masooma Di Abid Nu Standi Raindi.
Jeondi Aye Ya Mar Gayi Aye Sakhtiyaan Saindi.

2
Saaf Akhiyaan Da Laho Kar Ke Moor Ke Too Wali Gardan Nu Takda Jaway.
Beriyaan Koray Te Zanjeer Da Gham Koi Nayi.
Khon Akhiyaan Cho Aa Giya Aye Aydi Jadoon Shimar Laeein.
Chadar-e-Sani-e-Zahra Samne Abid De Lehra Ke Hasda Jaway.

3
Ayday Akbar Sir V Naizey Te Charhaya Aye.
Kyun Na Bemar Khon Ronde Oh Naiza Tak Ke.
Veer De Sir Nu Muhari Naal Akhiyaan De E Ro Ro Chumda Jaway.
Wadh Gayi Beerh Bazari Te Qaiyamat Ho Gayi.

4
Ayi Awaz Haye Abid Sakina Kho Gayi.
Jewein Akbar Di Lash Nu Riya Baba Labhda Aynj Behn Labhda Jaway.
Sir Te Shabir Da Naize Tu Talawat Karda Aye.
Qaidi Bachra Aytaan Te Hawaan Barhda Aye.

5
Nangay Sir Phophiyaan Ne Meri Soch Ke Ronda Hoya Ghazi Nu Takda Jaway.
Naal Phophiyaan Ne Bazaraan Wich Nange Pairi.
Kiwein Chun Le Rastiyaan Choo Oh Kanday Qaidi.
Khar Rawaan De Chunan Layi Pair Akbar Abid Kandiyaan Te Rakhda Jaway.
