Baba Sakeena vein kardi ray menoo eh gal dasa baba

1
Darhone qaid kahne cho
Kado hosa reha baba

2
Na poophiya kol heh mere na meri maa hee ohndhi eh judai sare kumbe dee teri dhee noo rowandi eh
Milan menoo nahi ohnda mera abid bhira baba

3
Darhone qaid kahne wich bara dada hanera heh
Pata nahi raat kinjh pehndi kadoo charda sawera eh
Kasam teri ethe ohndi nahi taza hawa baba

4
Tamache tere qatil neh teri bachri noo mare neh
Yaman tho jere aye san mere oh durr utare neh
Meri urdan deh wich rasiya gaya qatal pawa baba

5
Jadoo zakhma deh wich mere eh zalim reith pehndi eh
Sakeena qaid kahne dee zameen teh uth dee behndhi eh
Bara hee yaad ahnda eh menoo seena tera baba

6
Tere seene teh bistar teh mere baba meh sohndhi sa
Birah akbar kehnda da c jado bechain hondi saa
Meh aj mitiya teh sohndi ah koyee nahi asra baba

7
Eh duniya bohat zalim eh yateema noo sathan dee eh
Tamache maar keh wich gurdan teh rasiya pawandi eh
Dua eh roz mangdi ah menoo ah weh qaza baba

8
Muhammed mustafa nane dee hon toqeer deh sadke
Jeri Akbar tho wichri eh thoo us hamsheer tho sadke
Azadara noo karbal dee zawari teh bhula
