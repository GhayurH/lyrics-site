Muslim Ka Lasha Rait Par Syed Muhammad Shah.

1
Iss Tarha Zahra Ne Daman Ko Bichaya Rait Par.
Kat Gaya Muslim Ka Sar Lekin Na Aaya Rait Par.
Lash Kofa Mein Giri Thi Baad Mein Muslim Teri.
Pehle Sar Shabeer Ne Apna Giraya Rait Par.
Ro Para Abbas Ka Sar Dukhtar-e-Muslim Ne Jab.
Bar Sar-e-Bazar Baba Ko Bulaya Rait Par.

2
Aye Khuda Shohar Ke Gham Mein Youn Na Baithay Phir Koi.
Jaisay Qismat Ne Ruqaiya Ko Bithaya Rait Par.
Dekh Kar Muslim Ki Aankhein Khon Roti Reh Gayin.
Be Ridai Mein Khari Zainab Ka Saya Rait Par.
Rah-e-Kofa Janti Hai Jab Tamachon Se Giri.
Soag Muslim Ka Sakina Ne Manaya Rait Par.

3
Har Taraf Phaili Hoi Hai Khon-e-Muslim Ki Mehak.
Iss Tarha Zalim Ne Lashay Ko Phiraya Rait Par.
Ehteraman Gir Gaye Our Oss Jagah Bosay Diye.
Khon Abid Ne Jahan Muslim Ka Paya Rait Par.
Dekhney Aayi Hain Behnein Jaun Pehne Rasiyaan.
Kis Tarha Muslim Ne Maqtal Hai Basaya Rait Par.
