1
Batool Zadiyaan Babay Di Lash Te Aaiyaan.
Zainab Kulsoom Nu Qismat Ne Ghariyaan Aye Ki Wakhaiyaan.
ALLAH Di Zaat Nu Kita Aye Jis Ne Razi.
Sajday Che Kus Gaya Konain Da Namazi.
Dhiyaan Gal Lag Lag Ke Ron Paiyaan Haye Gham Diyaan Sataiyaan.
Wehra Batool Da Lutiya Aye Musalmanaa.
Dushman Kyun Ban Gaya Sadaat Da Zamana.
Karbal Toon Pehlaan Zainab Ne Mitiyaan Kyun Sir Che Payiyaan.
Dhiyaan Ali Diyaan Maiyyat Te Ron Kaliyaan.
Veraan Toon Puchdiyaan Nazaan Diyaan Paliyaan.
Sada Kon Sahara Dunya Te Raiyaan Dendiyaan Dohaiyaan.
Nori V Arsh Toon Ronday Farsh Te Aa Gaye.
Mula De Matami Ban Sare Anbiya Aa Gaye.
Jadoon Rondiyaan Dhiyaan Zahra Ne Gal La Ke Chup Karaiyaan.
Taboot Aan Ke Hasnain Jadoon Chaya.
 Akhtar Aye Sain Da Baqiye Choon Wain Aaya.
Kyun Sham Toon Pehlaan Ho Gayiyaan Sir Nangay Meriyaan Jaiyaan.
