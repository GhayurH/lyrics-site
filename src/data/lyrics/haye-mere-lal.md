Jab Qasim ke tukaro ko uthane lage Hussain
Laila ne kiya bain

1
Haey meray jawan Qasim
Haey meray laal, ab howey pamaal, Kaise karo bayan

2
Ab kuch hi deir me mein hojaon gi rukhsat, Karbobala se
Tum aa kar Maa aur Phophi jaan ko bachana

3
Haey meray laal, ab howey pamaal, kaise karo bayain

4
Ab kaun Sakina ko dilaen ga dilasein
Ammo bhi chalein jaaen ge, Asgher bhi jaen ga
Beta tumare liye hum sajaaen ge sehra
Ab tum bhi chalein, Kaun ha ga jo mujhe, dilasein dilaen ga

5
Haey meray laal, ab howey pamaal, kaise karo bayain

6
Ab me dua karti ho ke phuri howe qaza
Ab tub jaa rahe ho to Zainab se to milo
Roun gi puri raat, hay merein jawaan
