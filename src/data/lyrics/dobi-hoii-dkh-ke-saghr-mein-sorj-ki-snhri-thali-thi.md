Doobi hui dukh ke saaghar may Suraj ke sunehri thali thi
Is Chand ki das ko sanjh talak Shabbir se dunya khali thi

1
Baba hi ke dam ke sathi thay Akbar bhi gaye Asghar bhi gaye
Taknay ko rahi aik aik ka munh Chati pe jo sonay wali thi

2
Yun jag may na lagi aag kaheen Is dhab se na ujrra bagh kabheen
Sookhi hui patti patti thi Tooti hui daali daali thi

3
Haider ke gharanay walay sab Satwant bhi thay sawant bhi thay
Sehjay may uthi li bandon ne Maalik ne jo bipta dali thi

4
Sarwar pe Hasan ke vidhwa ne Do chand se tukrray war diye
Bachay to jiyalay thay hi magar Mata bhi barray dil wali thi

5
Kia lut ke gaye Karbal se haram Jab aayi hain sakhyan milnay ko
Jis mang ko dekha ujrri thi Jis goad ko dekha khali thi

6
Ye jee se guzarnay walay thay Ye baat pe marnay walay thay
Kab moat se darnay walay thay Sao bar ki dekhi bali thi

7
Najmi ye Hussaini chokhat hay Yaan aa ke muradain milti hain
Is dawar se bhiksha le ke chalay Aaye thay to jholi khali thi
