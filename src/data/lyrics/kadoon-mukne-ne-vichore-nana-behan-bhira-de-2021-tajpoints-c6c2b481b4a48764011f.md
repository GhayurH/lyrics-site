Kadoon Mukne Ne Vichore Nana Behan Bhira De Write Up
Tere Rooz Deway Balaan Mere Wichre Veer Mila De.

1
Bas Ayna Ken Main Ayi Aan Tere Roze Te Haye Main Nana.
Nishan Veeran De Pairaan De Mitday Janday Pay Ne Nana.
Jay Aye Nishan Muk Gay Sa Muk Janre Sughra De.
Main Khowab Takiya Aye Haye Nana Menu Koi Kehnda Piya Aye.

2
To Kis Nu Khat Payi Likhni Aye Koan Hoan Jeyonda Haye Riya Aye.
Sughra Qabar Bana Ke Tu Aapey Khat Dafna De.
Main Wekhiya Aye Koi Akbar Nu Haye Maran Aa Riya Aye.
Kuj Ayna Naizay Da Dar Nana Main Sughra Nu Dara Riya Aye.

3
Mang Di Rayi Dowaan Koi Khowab Tu Aan Jaga De.
Udeekaan Kha Gayiyaan Sughra Nu Haye Pul Gay Ne Nana Bistar.
Sakina Kis Tarha So Sakdi Si Haye Soch Zara Akbar.
Akhiyan De Naal Wekhay Jinay Marday Veer Shehzaday.
