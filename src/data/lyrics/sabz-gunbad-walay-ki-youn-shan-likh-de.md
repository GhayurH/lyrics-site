1
Sabz Gunbad Walay Ki Youn Shan Likh De
Jo Kahe Haider Kahe Imran Likh De
Sochna Kaisa Hai Kitna Rutba-e-Ahmad
Ishq Ki Mairaj Kar Dewaan Likh De

2
Sajda To Mairaj Momin Ki Hai Beshak
Ishq-e-Sarwar Sajday Ki Hai Jaan Likh De
Gham Manata Hi Nahi Our Phir Khushi Mein
Khush Nahi Hai Jo Ossay Shaitan Likh De

3
Naat Hai Gar Parhni Phir Quran Ko Parh
Naat Hai Ghar Likhni Phir Quran Likh De
Nikle Jab Jaan Aaqa Ke Dar Par Hi Ho Sar
Aye Malak Mera Yehi Arman Likh De

4
Bad-e-Sarwar Kon Hai Waris Hai Jhagra
Le Qalam Our Khum Ka Phir Ayelan Likh De
Fatima Ka Baba Sarwar Ka Taruf
Shan-e-Ahmad Mein Yehi Farman Likh De
