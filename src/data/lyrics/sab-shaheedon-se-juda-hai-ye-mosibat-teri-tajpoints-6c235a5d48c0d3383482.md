1
Sab Shaheedon Se Juda Hai Ye Mosibat Teri.
Ik Din Mein Hoi Dobar Shahadat Teri.
Hichkiyaan Leti Rahi Pyas Ki Shidat Se Hayat.
Samne Nazron Ke Behta Raha Dary-e-Furat.
Our Kumlati Rahi Phol Se Sorat Teri.
Zalimo Ne Tujhe Aisa Kiya Para Para.
Kabhi Naiza Kabhi Paikane Sitam Se Mara.
Kitni Pur Dard Hai Asghar Yeh Musibat Teri.
Bistar-e-Khak Pe Zindaan Mein Kahan Soti Hai.
Ab Tu Din Raat Teri Yaad Mein Bas Roti Hai.
Mar Dalay Gi Sakina Ko Yeh Furqat Teri.
Aasmaan Bar-e-Alam Dekh Ke Tharata Raha.
Sabar-e-Ayub Ko Maidan Mein Ghash Aata Raha.
Our Shabir Banate Rahe Turbat Teri.
Hashmi Khoon Ka Asar Ran Mein Dikhaya Tu Ne.
Pathron Ko Bhi Tabasum Se Rolaya Tu Ne.
Marhaba Marhaba Sad Marhaba Himat Teri.
Mamta Aag Kalejay Mein Laga Deti Hai.
Neend Aate Hi Teri Yaad Jaga Deti Hai.
Maa Ko Sone Nahi Deti Hai Muhabbat Teri.
Yeh Wajhi-e-Shah-e-Wala Yeh Bilal-e-Sarwar.
Do Ke Dono Teri Dehleez Ke Adna Nokar.
Inn Ko Darkar Hai Mehshar Mein Shifat Teri.
