1
Phal Barchi Da Kha Ke Akbar Babay Nu Faryad Kare.
Sughra Nu Aye Aakho Mainu Waday Taun Aazad Karey.
Zeen Taun Lathda Wekh Ke Akbar Aakhay Khaliq Deen Wafa Da.
Dewo Ijazat Jang Di Mainu Surkh Karan Main Rang Sehra Da.
Hath Mal Mal Ke Shah Diyaan Mintaan Akbar Da Ustad Karey.
Phal Barchi Da Okhriyan Sanwaan Khuliyan Akhiyaan Weliyan Rahwaan.
Tainu Qasad Veer Akbar Da Das Main Kiwein Haal Sunawaan.
Khat Sughra Da Seene La Ke Wain Ayho Sajjad Karey.
Maut Teri Da Sun Ke Maa Di Akhiyaan De Wich Chaye Haneray.
Dar Nayi Labda Hun Khaimay Da Phirdi Payi Aye Chaar Choferay.
Lash Teri Te Le Aawan Layi Kaun Aidi Imdad Karey.
Khol Ke Sir De Waal Safaid Aye Mehndi Khon Di Landi Rayi Aye.
Putar Da Seena Chum Ke Laila Ro Ke Ayho Kaindi Rayi Aye.
Koi Kisay Di Jholi Ya Rab Ewein Na Barbad Karey.
Ibrahim Taun Okhi Manzil Ismail Tu Wadh Sarmaya.
Akbar Apna Zakhm Chupaya Babay Apna Sabar Wikhaya.
Pak Hussain De Wangoon Akbar Jag Te Kaun Jehad Karey.
