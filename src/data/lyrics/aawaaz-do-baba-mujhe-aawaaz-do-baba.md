Aawaaz do Baba mujhe, aawaaz do Baba
Ikkisviṅ Ramzan ko ye Zainab ka tha nauha

1
Amma to gayi chhor ke ab aap na jaao
Kiuṅ ruth gaye beti se, itna to bataao
Zainab hai bulati tumhe, kuchh bolo Khudara

2
Aankheiṅ to zara khol ke, dekho meri haalat
Chehroṅ pe yatimoṅ ke barasti hui ghurbat
Khaliq ke siwa koi, nahiṅ aaj hamara

3
Kis tarah se wo Eid manaenge bataao
Mamloos bhala kis se mangaenge bataao
Jin bachchoṅ ke sar par na ho maa baap ka saaya

4
Tum kitne sakhi ho, ye zamane ko pata hai
Khud pyase rahe, logoṅ ko sairab kiya hai
Har mor pe duniya ne, tumhe phir bhi sataya

5
Socho to zara guzregi, kya kya mere dil par
Aankheiṅ meri dekheṅgi, bhala kaise wo manzar
Hasnain ke kandhoṅ pe, jab utthega janaza

6
Hasnain ko samjhaeṅ ke Ghazi ko manaeṅ
Mujhko kabhi Kulsoom ko sine se lagaeṅ
Maa Umm-e-Banin roti hai, sar pit ke apna

7
Roye mera Shabbir to behaal hai Shabbar
Kya bit gayi dekhiye Kulsoom ke dil par
Abbas ke rone se, tarapta hai kaleja

8
Hai nauha kunaṅ aaj bilakti hui aankheiṅ
Kehti hai ye Allah se rukti hui saanseṅ
Kiuṅ uth gaya duniya se yatimoṅ ka sahara

9
Imran o Saeed aao dua mangeṅ Khuda se
Bibi ke wasile se hameṅ Shaam bula le
Shehzadi ke roze par, parheṅ mil ke ye nauha
