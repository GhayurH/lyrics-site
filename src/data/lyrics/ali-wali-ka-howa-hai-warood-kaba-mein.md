1
Ali Wali Ka Howa Hai Warood Kaba Mein.
Falak Se Pohanchi Saday-e-Darood Kaba Mein.
Issi Liye Tu Howa Hai Zahoor Asadullah.
Rahe Na Baqi Butoon Ka Wajood Kaba Mein.
Yeh Aa Rahi Hai Sada Minbar Saloni Se.
Hain Shehar Tu Iss Ka Dar Hai Ali.
Ahata Iss Ki Fazilat Ka Ho Nahi Sakta.
Hamare Feham-o-Farasat Se Balatar Hai Ali.
Ali Hai Haider-o-Safdar Ali Shah-e-Mardaan.
Raqam Hain Sab Ke Dillon Par Fazilatein Oss Ki.
Ho Jung Khaibar-o-Khandaq Ahad Keh Badar-o-Hunnain.
Hain Deein Ki Fatah Mubeen Sab Shujatein Iss Ki.
Ali Woh Nasir-o-Mushkil Kushay-e-Alam Hai.
Khuda Ne Bakhshi Hain Jiss Ko Karamtein Sari.
Ali Ko Jiss Ne Madad Ke Liye Pukara Hai.
Ali Ne Taal Dein Oss Ki Mosibatein Sari.
Khuda Ke Noor Ka Hisa Nabi Nabi Ka ALi.
Nabi Ali Ke Sab Anwaar Aik Jaise Hain.
Agar Ho Shak Hijrat Ko Dekh Le Dunya.
Khuda Ke Dono Yeh Shehkar Aik Jaise Hain.
Nabi Ka Khas Ye Farman Hai Momineen Karam.
Sajao Zidar Ali kar Ke Mehfilein Apni.
Khuda Ki Ain Ibadat Hai Zikar-o-Yaad Ali.
Issi Amal Se Abarat Hain Majlisein Apni.
Ali Wali Hai Wo Dast-e-Khuda Zamane Mein.
Keh Jiss Ka Dast-e-Karam Mazhar-il-Ajaib Hai.
Khuda Our Oss Ki Khudai Bhi Jiss Pe Naaz Kare.
Yehi Wo Fakhar-e-Nabowat Nabi Ka Naib Hai.
13 Rajab Jashan-e-Molod-e-Kaba Ali Ibn-e-Abi Talib Mubarak.
