Mera Ghaar Jalaya Lutiyan Ridawan, Ro Akay Zainab Kis Pas Jawan.

Abid Bachawan, Ya Aag Bujhawan  Ro Akay Zainab Kis Pas Jawan.

Yusaf Tou Sonay Meray Veer Maarey, May Shaam Chaliyan Baykafan Saaray
Kabaran Banawan, Ya Rasiyan Pawan  Ro Akay Zainab Kis Pas Jawan.

Meh Deen Bachawan Vich Shaam Aayi, Sab Loug Veikan Meri Bay Ridayee
Khutbay Sunawan, Ya Mou Lukaawan  Ro Akay Zainab Kis Pas Jawan.

Khaimey’ch Qasim Di Laash Aayi, Chum Chum Kay Tukray Kavey Zahra Jayee
Hun Sehray Lavan Ya Saf Vichawan  Ro Akay Zainab Kis Pas Jawan.

Jis Veer Day Laiye Chadeya Madina, Jiway Qatal Hoya Bhulna Kadeena
Hun Kafan Pawan, Ya Shaam Jawan  Ro Akay Zainab Kis Pas Jawan.
