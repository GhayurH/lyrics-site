Rutba Azeem Paya Hai Ummul Baneen Ne.
Haider Ka Ghar Basaya Hai Ummul Baneen Ne.

1
Zahra Ke Ladle Jo Mile Munh Dikhai Mein.
Sajday Mein Sar Jhukaya Hai Ummul Baneen Ne.
Mula Ali Ke Ghar Ko Lagaye Hain Char Chand.
Dunya Ko Jagmagaya Hai Ummul Baneen Ne.

2
Yeh Bibi Fatima Ki Dua Ka Asar Hai Jo.
Ghazi Sa Shair Paya Hai Ummul Baneen Ne.
Aaqa Kahin Na Kyun Bhala Ghazi Hussain Ko.
Unn Ko Adab Sikhaya Hai Ummul Baneen Ne.

3
Ummul Baneen Ke Hathon Mein Ghazi Ka Hath Hai.
Asli Alam Uthaya Hai Ummul Baneen Ne.
Matam Kisi Bhi Hal Mein Sheh Ka Na Chorna.
 Saqlain Yeh Bataya Hai Ummul Baneen Ne.
