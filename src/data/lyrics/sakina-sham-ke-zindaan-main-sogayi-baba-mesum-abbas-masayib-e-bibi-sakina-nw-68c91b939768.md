Sakina Shaam ke zindaan me so gayi Baba

1)

Tamache Shimr se kha-ti thi, jab bhi ro-ti thi

Andheri raat me uth uth ke, bas ye keh-ti thi

Meri aseeri pe ro-ti he, bekasi Baba

Sakina Shaam ke zindaan me so gayi Baba

2)

Ay Baba aap ke seene ki, us ko aadat thi

Zameen pe sone ki koshish me, jaag-ti hi rahi

Teri yateema ko neendh aayi, na kabhi Baba

Sakina Shaam ke zindaan me so gayi Baba

3)

Phupi se keh-ti thi ro ro ke, ye batado phupi

Sitam ki qayd se ghar apne, ja sakenge kabhi

Usse rihai na mar kar bhi, mil saki Baba

Sakina Shaam ke zindaan me so gayi Baba

4)

Banayi aap ne maqtal me, qabr Asghar ki

Lehed banayi he zindaan me, me ne muztar ki

Karhi jo fatiha roti-thi, hathkari Baba

Sakina Shaam ke zindaan me so gayi Baba

5)

Kafan lahu bhara kurta hi, he Sakina ka

Diya ghusl bhi ashkoN se, ay mere Baba

Kafan bhi rota tha aur me bhi, qabr bhi Baba

Sakina Shaam ke zindaan me so gayi Baba

6)

Nahi zara sa ujala bhi, us ki turbat par

Andhere karne lage nauha, us ki turbat par

Kare ga kaun yahaN aa ke, roshini Baba

Sakina Shaam ke zindaan me so gayi Baba

7)

Bayan kayse kareN ye, Mesum o Mazhar

Jo bayn Baba se karte the, Abid e Muztar

Sakina Shaam ke zindaan me, so gayi Baba

Sakina Shaam ke zindaan me so gayi Baba
