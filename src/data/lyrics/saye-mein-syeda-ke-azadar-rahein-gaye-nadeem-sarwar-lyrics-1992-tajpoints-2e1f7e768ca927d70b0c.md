1
Saye Mein Syeda Ke Azadar Rahein Gaye Nadeem Sarwar.
Jab Hoga Sawa Naizay Pe Khursheed-e-Qayamat.
Saye Mein Syeda Ke Azadar Rahein Gay.
Barhta Hi Chala Jaye Ga Shabeer Ka Matam.
Aaye Jo Gham-e-Sheh Ke Alamdar Rahein Gay.
Hum Qabar Mein So Jaein Gay Jab Be Khabri Se.
Matam Ke Nishaan Rooh Mein Bedar Rahein Gay.
Har Cheez Ko Dunya Mein Fana Hona Hai Ik Din.
Qayam Gham-e-Shabeer Ke Aasaar Rahein Gay.
Janat Mein Agar Majlis-e-Shabeer Na Hogi.
Hum Ahl-e-Azza Khuld Se Bezaar Rahein Gay.
Ik Bar Zara Nam-e-Ali Le Ke Tu Dekho.
Mushkil Mein Madadgar Woh Har Bar Rahein Gay.
Be Jan Khudaon Se Kaha Bint-e-Asad Ne.
Kabay Mein Tu Ab Haider-e-Karrar Rahein Gay.
Zainab Ka Khulay Sar Bharay Darbar Mein Jana.
Sajjad Issi Dard Se Beemar Rahein Gay.
Woh Mashk Ka Chidna Woh Sakina Ka Bilakna.
Abbas Ke Seene Pe Sada Waar Rahein Gay.
 Rehan Mera Nama-e-Amal Hai Noha.
Parwana-e-Janat Mere Ashar Rahein Gay.
