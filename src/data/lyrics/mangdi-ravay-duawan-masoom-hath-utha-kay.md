Mangdi ravay duawan Masoom hath utha kay
Ek waari mera Baba mainu seenay laavay aa kay

1
Abid main dardaan maari khakaan tay souni rayi aan
Nai labda mainu Baba veeran main taras gayi aan
Kar pyaar Babay wangu mainu seenay tay lita kay

2
Rahwan tay door beh kay main kol ja nai sakdi
Sir samnay babay da par hath main laa nai sakdi
Koi pyaar karan devay sir ohda jholi paa kay

3
Tu khaak uttay rowein main naizay uttay tarpan
Meri ladli Sakina manzar oh kiwen bhul jaan
Kiwen shimar juda keeta hath vaalan ich si paa kay

4
Zainab meri Sakina mainu jaan ton azeez ay
Shabbir day galay da bohat paak eh taweez ay
Saffran di sakhtiyan ton rakhii ennu tu bachaa kay

5
Saffran chh naal meray tu Sakina ruldi raayi ay
Babay ton juda hoyi ajj veer ton hogayai ay
Sajjad rovay Abid ohdi qabar hayee bana kay
