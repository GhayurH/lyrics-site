1
Lo Mill Gayi Zindaan Se Sakina Ko Rehai
Sajjad Ne Yeh Kehte Howay Lash Uthai.
Baba Chale Ayo Ke Main Tanha Hoon Yahan Par.
Bas Itna Bata Jao Ke Dafnao Kahan Par.

2
Kya Qaid Mein Hi Dafan Kare Behan Ko Bahi.
Ghar Jane Ki Hasrat Tu Bar Awar Na Hoi Par.
Dam Toot Giya Ho Gayi Azad Tu Dukhtar.
Maa Beti Ka Munh Choom Ke Deti Thi Dohai.

3
Aab Shimar Tamache Na Isse Maar Sake Ga.
Aram Se Soye Gi Tah-e-Khak Yeh Dukhiya.
Lashe Se Yehi Kehti Rahi Fatima Jayi.
Bahi Ne Zameen Khood Ke Jab Qabar Sanwari.

4
Rote Hoye Maiyat Soye Turbat Jo Utari.
Zindan Ki Deewaron Se Awaz Ye Ayi.
Band Aankhein Kiye Qabar Mein Aram Kare Gi.
Maa Shana Hilati Rahe Phir Bhi Na Uthe Gi.

5
Youn Maut Ne Lori Hai Sakina Ko Sonai.
Abid Pe Takallum Woh Mosibat Ki Ghari Thi.
Jin Hatho Pe Khaili Thi Wo Masoom Si Bachi.
Un Hathon Ne Turbat Bhi Sakina Ki Banai.
