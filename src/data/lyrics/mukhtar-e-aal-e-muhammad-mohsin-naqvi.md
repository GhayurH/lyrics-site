Mukhtar e Aal e Muhammad Mohsin Naqvi

1
Khursheed-e-Shujat Ki Kiran Hai Mera Mukhtar
Be Khof Khiyalon Ka Badan Hai Mera Mukhtar
Israr-e-Aqeedat Ka Chaman Hai Mera Mukhtar
Dharti Pe Daleri Ka Gagan Hai Mera Mukhtar
Mukhtar Ki Haibat Hai Woh Arbab-e-Sitam Mein
Rasha Nazar Aata Hai Moarikh Ke Qalam Mein

2
Mukhtar Ko Hum Loag Barhatay Nahi Had Se
Lekin Humein Nafrat Hai Zamane Ke Hasad Se
Ab Tak Jo Sar Afraz Ho Sarwar Ki Madad Se
Mehshar Mein Miley Dad Jissay Haq Ke Asad Se
Aisa Koi Sawant, Jari, Hur Nahi Dekha
Mukhtar Sa Phir Koi Bahadur Nahi Dekha

3
Bijli Ko Kabhi Gir Ke Palattay Hoye Dekho
Ya Jang Mein Dharti Ko Ulattay Hoye Dekho
Tofaan Kabhi Qatron Mein Simattay Hoye Dekho
Chiriyon Pe Oqabaon Ko Jhapattay Hoye Dekho
Chomay Jo Kabhi Maut Koi Zard Sa Matha
Tum Sochna Mukhtar Ki Talwar Mein Kya Tha

4
Aye Qismat Islam Ke Manhos Sitaro
Tugyani Tashkaik Ke Behtay Hoye Dharo
Aye Maka Tah-e-Khak Saqeefa Ke Kinaro
Chehron Se Riya Pash Naqabein Tu Utaro
Pehle Kissi Maloon Ki Taeed Karo Tum
Phir Shoq Se Mukhtar Pe Tanqeed Karo Tum

5
Mukhtar Ka Chehra Hai Keh Subhon Ka Warq Hai
Matha Hai Keh Ik Safah e Anjeel Adaq Hai
Rukhsar Ki Rangat Hai Keh Ijaz-e-Shafaq Hai
Honton Pe Dhanak Hai Keh Yeh Debacha e Haq Hai
Hamlay Hain Keh Aasar e Payamber Ki Dua Ke
Mukhtar Ke Bazu Hain Keh Parcham Hain Qaza Ke

6
Aankhein Hain Keh Fanoos Rukh-e-Arsh-e-Barin Par
Palkein Hain Keh Jhomar Hain Sitaron Ki Jabeen Par
Zulfein Hain Keh Badil Se Rukh-e-Mohar-e-Mubeen Par
Haibat Hai Keh Ik Hashar Sa Kofay Ki Zameen Par
Yeh Phool Yeh Jugnu Yeh Falak Taab Sitarey
Mukhtar Ki Talwar Se Jhartay Hain Shararey
