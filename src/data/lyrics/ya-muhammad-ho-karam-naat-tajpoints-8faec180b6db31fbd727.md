1
Ya Muhammad Ho Karam Kuch Baat Likhon Aap Ki.
Hamd Rab Ki Our Sana Sadaat Likhon Aap Ki.
Aap Ne Our Murtaza Ne Mil Ke Jo Jangein Larein.
Poch Kar Haider Se Woh Gazwat Likhon Aap Ki.

2
Fatima Se Izn Le Kar Hadia-e-Tabreek Mein.
Moka-e-Mailad Par Ik Naat Likhon Aap Ki.
Phool Midhat Ke Sajaon Le Ke Faizan-e-Hasan.
Sidq-e-Dil Se Uswa-e-Hasnat Likhon Aap Ki.

3
Khud Kahin Shabbir Mujh Ko Apne Nana Ka Muhib.
Jab Hussaino Mini Se Main Baat Likhon Aap Ki.
Mujh Ko Phir Khushnodi-e-Sajjad Bhi Mil Jaye Gi.
Sab Se Ola Sab Se Aala Zaat Likhon Aap Ki.

4
Kuch Moattar Lafz Mujh Ko Dein Gay Jab Baqir Imam.
Phir Main Unn Ko Joor Kar Barkaat Likhon Aap Ki.
Jafar-e-Sadiq Ke Dar Se Haq Parasti Seekh Kar.
Phir Wafadari Se Main Haq Baat Likhon Aap Ki.

5
Mosa-e-Kazim Se Le Kar Bheek Main Rizq-e-Sukhan.
Aap Ka Sehra Parhon Baraat Likhon Aap Ki.
Baith Kar Ik Raat Main Maula Raza Ke Shehar Mein.
Waqt-e-Hijrat Jo Howa Woh Raat Likhon Aap Ki.

6
Khair Se Sab Kuch Mila Hai Sadqa-e-Maula Taqi.
Mujh Pe Rehmat Ki Hoi Barsat Likhon Aap Ki.
Hadi-e-Bar Haq Naqi Maula Ki Chokhat Chom Kar.
Do Jahan Ki Nematein Sogat Likhon Aap Ki.

7
Askari Ka Wasta Nazar-e-Karam Ho Ya Nabi.
Naat Ki Sorat Mein Kuch Adaat Likhon Aap Ki.
Ghaibat-e-Qayam Ka Ab Hasnain Ho Jald Ahtatam.
Samne Mehdi Ke Phir Se Naat Likhon Aap Ki.
