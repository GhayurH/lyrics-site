Shab Unwi Mah Ramzan Di Hayi Rona Pak Ameer Namaz De Wich.
Ditha Ronda Saqi-e-Kausar Koon Ro Payi Taqdeer Namaz De Wich.

1
Jadaan Jibraeel Ayelan Kita Thi Qatal Gaya Hai Pak Ali.
Hayi Umm Ul Musayab Musallay Te Jhanr Payi Tatheer Namaz De Wich.
Takbeerat Al Ahraam Parhi Aaye Hath Makhdoom De Gal Tonrein.
Dhiyaan Te Nohaan De Hath Wich Yaad Aaye Zanjeer Namaz De Wich.

2
ALLAH o Akbar Naal Syed Hath Khol Qiyam Shorow Kita.
Akbar Da Seena Yaad Aaya Parhi Jaan Takbeer Namaz De Wich.
Makhdoom Qiyam De Baad Jadaan Kita Pak Raku Taan Pag Dhal Gayi.
Bazar Ich Turda Nazar Aaya Sajjad Aseer Namaz De Wich.

3
Aya Sajde Wich Tah Aa Kaazi, Payshaani Khoon ich Tar Thi Gayi
Shabbir De Babay Wain Kitta, Kus Gaye Shabbir Namaz De Wich

4
Lagi Sir Te Zarb Qaza Jis Dam Gayi Deed Ali Di Karbal Do.
Aoulad De Sare Zakhma Di Ubhari Tasveer Namaz De Wich.
Sajday Wich Akhiyaan Band Kar Ke Gohar Shah Dhi Da Naa Chaya.
Raaz Umm Ul Musayab Howanr De Ghul Gayein Akheer Namaz De Wich.
