1
Kabhi Khud Pe Kabhi Halat Pe Rona Aaya.
Baat Nikli Tu Har Ik Baat Pe Rona Aaya.
Hum Tu Samjhe The Keh Hum Bhool Gay Hain Un Ko.
Kya Howa Aaj Yeh Kis Baat Pe Rona Aaya.

2
Kis Liye Jeete Hain Hum Kis Ke Liye Jeete Hain.
Barha Aise Sawalaat Pe Rona Aaya.
Kaun Rota Hai Kissi Our Ki Khatir Aye Dost.
Sab Ko Apni Hi Kissi Baat Pe Rona Aaya.
