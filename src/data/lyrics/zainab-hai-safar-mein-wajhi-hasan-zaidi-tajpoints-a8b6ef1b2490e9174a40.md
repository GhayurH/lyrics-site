Zainab Hai Safar Mein Wajhi Hasan Zaidi.

1
Haye Khak Hai Sar Mein Zainab Hai Safar Mein.
Kab Hogi Reha Qaid Se Kab Jaye Gi Ghar Mein.
Ik Waqt Tha Kofa Ki Shehzadi Thi Zainab.
Kis Haal Mein Aayi Hai Yeh Baba Ke Shehar Mein.
Bikhrey Hoye Baloon Se Munh Dhanp Lo Bibi.
Mahool Sharabi Hai Teri Rah Guzar Mein.

2
They Tan Pe Phattay Kaprey Our Kan Bhi Zakhmi.
Abid Ne Utara Jo Sakina Ko Qabar Mein.
Tareekh Teri Jang Ko Dohrati Rahe Gi.
Asghar Jo Lari Tu Ne Choti Si Umar Mein.
Jis Parday Ka Zamin Tha Abbas-e-Dilawar.
Hai Zakhm Ossi Parday Ka Abid Ke Jigar Mein.

3
Kanton Pe Jo Chalta Hai Ghairat Ka Khuda Hai.
Dunya Ne Nahi Jana Kyun Khum Hai Kamar Mein.
Kyun Aa Ke Madine Mein Bhi Khoon Rota Raha Hai.
Bazar Ka Har Zulm Hai Abid Ki Nazar Mein.
Shabbir Ke Matam Se Haye Rokney Walo.
Zainab Ki Yeh Sunat Hai Momin Ki Nazar Mein.
