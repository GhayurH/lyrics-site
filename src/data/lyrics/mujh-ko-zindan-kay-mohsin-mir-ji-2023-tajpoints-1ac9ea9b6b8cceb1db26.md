Mujh Ko Zindan Kay Mohsin Mir Ji 2023.
Mujh Ko Zindaan Ke Andheron Se Nikalo Baba.

1
Thak Gayi Hoon Mujhe Seene Pe Sula Lo Baba.
Sunti Hoon Shimar e Laeen Dhond Raha Hai Mujh Ko.
Iss Se Pehle Keh Woh Phir Marey Tamachay Mujh Ko.
Apni Banhoon Mein Sakina Ko Chupa Lo Baba.
Janti Hoon Keh Badan Aap Ka Zakhmi Hai Magar.
Zakhm Gehray Hain Bohat Dard Zyada Ho Agar.
Ya Ali Keh Ke Kalejay Se Laga Lo Baba.
Chin Gayi Sar Se Rida Aap Na Aaye Lekin.
Mera Daman Bhi Jala Aap Na Aaye Lekin.
Bhaiyya Abid Ko Tu Durron Se Bacha Lo Baba.
Kan Zakhmi Hain Merey Neel Hain Rukhsaron Par.
Zulm Seh Seh Ke Meri Khum Hoi Jati Hai Kamar.
Gash Na Aa Jaye Kahin Mujh Ko Sanbhalo Baba.
Reham Khata Nahi Koi Woh Yateema Hoon Main.
Umar Hai Chaar Baras Our Zaeefa Hoon Main.
Ik Nazar Apni Sakina Pe Bhi Dalo Baba.
Boltay Kyun Nahi Kya Aap Khafa Ho Mujh Se.
Gar Khafa Ho Tu Bata Do Main Manaon Kaisay.
Hal-e-Dil Apni Sakina Ko Suna Lo Baba.
Bas Kahin Se Meri Phophiyon Ki Ridaein La Do.
Ho Sakay Tu Marey Bhai Ki Dawaein La Do.
Ya Tu Iss Qaid Se Hum Sab Ko Chura Lo Baba.
Arsh Hilta Tha Khalil Iss Ke Musayab Sun Kar.
Jab Woh Kehti Thi Lahu Rota Tha Shabbir Ka Sar.
