Chuth Raha Hai Nana Ka Aasta Meri Zainab

bhai ka hua dushman ye jahan meri zainab

tang hai zameen mujh par har taraf say nargha hai

ho qareeb bhai par imtehan meri zainab

chuth raha hai….

dekhta rahon kab tak zulm ye zamanay ka

ab rahi hai sanso main ab azaan’ meri zainab

chuth raha hai….

wada apna tifli ka ab mujhey nibhana hai

hath main rasan hogi pasban meri zainab

chuth raha hai….

bhai ka safar tere lazmi hai aye khwahar

alqama pay theyreyga karvan meri zainab

chuth raha hai….

deen e haq ki nusrat ko karbala ke jungle may

khoon main nahayega karvan’ meri zainab

chuth raha hai….

bad mere ye layeenzulm tum pay dhayenge

baazuon may bandhenge rassiyan meri zainab

chuth raha hai….

qaid ho ke jaogi shaam aur kufay may

pehney hoga abid bhi beriyan meri zainab

chuth raha hai….

sheh safar ka pas manzar kah ke chup huey saghar

main hoon haq ki marzi par shadmaan’ meri zainab

chuth raha hai….
