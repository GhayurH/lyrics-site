Lasha e Shabbir Ko Dekha Syed Wajhi Hasan Zaidi.

1
Be Goro Kafan Lasha-e-Shabeer Ko Dekha.
Zainab Ne Ujarti Hoi Taqdeer Ko Dekha.
Tukray Nazar Aane Lagay Bhai Ke Badan Ke.
Ik Bhai Ne Jab Bhai Ki Tehreer Ko Dekha.
Akbar Ke Kalejay Mein Lagi Zulm Ki Barchi.
Tar Khon Mein Sarkar Ki Tasveer Ko Dekha.

2
Roye Kabhi Ghazi Ke Katay Hath Ko Sarwar.
Hasrat Se Kabhi Bazu-e-Humsheer Ko Dekha.
Zainab Ne Tarap Kar Kaha Haye Mera Bhai.
Jab Halq Pe Chalti Hoi Shamsheer Ko Dekha.
Yasrab Kabhi Kofa Tu Kabhi Karb-o-Bala Mein.
Luttay Hoye Sadaat Ki Jageer Ko Dekha.

3
Chadar Ko Bohat Aaya-e-Tatheer Bhi Roi.
Jab Baal Khulay Zainab-e-Dilgeer Ko Dekha.
Roi Yad-e-KAl-Khair Ki Aayat Bhi Tarap Kar.
Jab Qaid Mein Khud Markaz-e-Tauqeer Ko Dekha.
Khoon Roya Hilal Our Wajih Arsh-e-Barin Bhi.
Bazar Mein Jab Malka-e-Tatheer Ko Dekha.
