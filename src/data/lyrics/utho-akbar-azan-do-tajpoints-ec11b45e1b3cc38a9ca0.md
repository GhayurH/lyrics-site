Utho Akbar Azan Do Write Up
Lehjay Mein Mustafa Ke Keh Kar Khuda Ko Akbar Haq Ka Biyan Do.

1
Ho Aaj Ashhadu Se Tauheed Ki Manadi.
Hum Shakal-e-Mustafa Ab Ho Baat Mustafa Ki.
Pyasay Labhoon Se Aisi Do Aaj Tum Gawahi Gonjay Jahan Do.
Be Shak Ali Wali Ki Yeh Jang Hai Hamari.

2
Sardar-e-Mutaqeen Ka Rakhna Biyan Jari.
Baba Ki Hai Yeh Hasrat Parh Kar Ali Ki Midhat Maqtal Mein Jan Do.
Asal-e-Salat Kya Hai Oss Ko Batao Gay Tum.
Khair-Al-Amal Ki Janib Hur Ko Bulao Gay Tum.

3
Sun Kar Azaan Tumhari Hogi Sipah Pori Hum Ko Zaban Do.
Tooti Hoi Sina Bhi Seene Se Khainch Loon Ga.
Sabir Hoon Karbala Ka Sabit Qadam Rahon Ga.
Phir Kar Ke Aik Sajdah Maqtal Mein De Ga Baba Youn Imtehan Do.

4
Ik LaIlaha Pe Akbar Ik Syeda Ka Akbar.
Aisay Ibadatein Do Hoti Hain Sajdah Ga Par.
Hotey Hain Matami Ke Seene Par Our Jabeen Par Aisay Nishan Do.
