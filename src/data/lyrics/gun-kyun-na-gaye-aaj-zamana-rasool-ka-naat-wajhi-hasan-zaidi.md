1
Gun Kyun Na Gaye Aaj Zamana Rasool Ka – Naat Wajhi Hasan Zaidi
Dunya Ko Palta hai Gharana Rasool Ka
Ik La-Makaan Ki Wosat-e-Imkaan Bata Gaya
Mairaj-e-Shahjehaat Pe Jana Rasool Ka

2
Sab Ko Bata Raha Hai Wilayat Ki Manzilat
Kandhoon Pe Murta Ko Uthana Rasool Ka
Tareekh Ki Nigah Ne Mehbooz Kar Liya
Behr-e-Madad Ali Ko Bulana Rasool Ka

3
Woh Hai Gada-e-Fatima Jis Jis Ko Yad Hai
Darwaza-e-Batool Pe Aana Rasool Ka
Pakeezgi Lahu Ki Barhata Hai Ba-Khuda
Sadqa Kahin Bhi Jab Mile Khana Rasool Ka

4
Pehray Laga Rahe Hain Sar-e-Janat-ul-Baqi
Jo Nam Chahte Hain Mitana Rasool Ka
Hai Panjtan Ke Ishq Ki Sorat Mein Jalwah Gar
:YASIR RAZA: Ke Dil Mein Khazana Rasool Ka
