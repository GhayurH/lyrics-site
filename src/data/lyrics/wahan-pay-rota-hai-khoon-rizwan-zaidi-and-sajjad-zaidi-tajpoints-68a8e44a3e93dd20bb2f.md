1
Wahan Pay Rota Hai Khoon Rizwan Zaidi and Sajjad Zaidi.
Wahan Pe Rota Hai Khoon Ab Bhi Aasman Hussain.
Jahan Pe Loota Gaya Tera Karwan Hussain.
Lagi Thi Seena-e-Akbar Pe Jis Jagah Barchi.

2
Jahan Pe Doob Gayi Khon Mein Shabeeh-e-Nabi.
Jahan Pe Qatal Howa Tera Nojawan Hussain.
Jahan Pe Pyas Se Be Sheer Tera Rota Raha.
Jahan Pe Nanhay Mujahid Ke Teer Mara Gaya.

3
Jahan Pe Qata Howa Tha Tera Biyan Hussain.
Jahan Hoye They Tere Bhai Ke Qalam Bazu.
Jahan Kiya Tha Alamdar Ne Lahu Se Wozu.
Jahan Howa Tha Tera Shair Neem Jan Hussain.

4
Jahan Pe Tukron Mein Bikhra Howa Tha Ik Dulha.
Jahan Pe Sehra Para Kar Raha Tha Wawela.
Jahan Ujar Gaya Kubra Ka Kul Jahan Hussain.
Jahan Pe Khatam Howa Tha Hussain Tera Safar.

5
Jahan Pe Khushk Galay Par Tere Chala Khanjar.
Jahan Mitaya Gaya Tha Tera Nishan Hussain.
Jahan Pe Koi Na Tha Aasra Gareebon Ka.
Jahan Pe Chin Gayi Syedaniyon Ke Sar Se Rida.

6
Jahan Tha Noha Kuna Tera Khandan Hussain.
Jahan Pe:GOHAR-E-: Gham Tashna Lab They Sehmey Hoye.
Jahan Tamachay Lagay They Teri Sakina Ke.
Tere Yateem Jahan Par They Be Aman Hussain.
