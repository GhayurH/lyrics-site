1
Ishq-e-Haider Ke Samandar Mein Utar Jane Do.
Mujh Ko Iss Rah Mein Ab Had Se Guzar Jane Do.
Mujh Pe Wajib Hai Wilayat Ki Gawahi Dena.
Koi Iqrar Se Darta Hai Tu Dar Jane Do.

2
Main Tu Karta Hi Rahon Ga Yahan Parchar-e-Wila.
Mujh Ko Iss Ishq Mein Marna Hai Tu Mar Jane Do.
Mujh Ko Karni Hai Ada Ja Ke Wilayat Ki Namaz.
Tum Mujhe Meesum-e-Tammar Ke Ghar Jane Do.

3
Meri Nas Nas Se Bhi Aaye Gi Ali Haq Ki Sada.
Tum Zara Our Aqeeday Ko Nikhar Jane Do.
Khud Sajaein Gay Ali Walay Wahan Bazm-e-Wila.
Tum Ali Walon Ko Janat Mein Sanwar Jane Do.

4
Khud Hi Likhein Gay Jawabaat Nakerain Sabhi.
Kushboye Khak-e-Shifa Qabar Mein Bhar Jane Do.
Kar Raha Hoon Main Nawazish Yahan Zanjeer Zani.
Tum Lahu Mera Bhi Karbal Mein Bikhar Jane Do.
