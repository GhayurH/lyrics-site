Aye Baade Saba Jake Ye Ammu Ko Batana
Pyasi Hai Sakina
Yaad Aaye Sakina Toh Chacha Laut Ke Aana

۱
Is Chhotey Se Sin Me Woh Sitam Mainey Uthhaye
Allah Kisi Ko Na Yateem Aisa Banaye
Khud Apney Lahu Me Koi Bachchi Na Nahaye
Mai Tumko Bulati Rahi, Tum Bhi Toh Na Aye
Mai Bann Gayi Baad Asr Laiyno Ka Nishana

۲
Ghar Laut Ke Jatey Hai Har Ek Shaam Parindey
Kismat Me Likhey Hai Meri Zinda Ke Andherey
Kab Tak Mai Jiyungi Yuhi Ashqo Ke Saharey
Lagta Hai Ke Marr Jaoongi Ab Dur Watan Se
Hoga Na Mayasser Mujhe Ghar Laut Ke Jana

۳
Jiss Roz Se Asghar Ko Mila Baba Ka Seena
Uss Roz Se Ammu Nahi So Payi Sakina
Iss Haal Me Jaoongi Mai Kiss Tarah Madina
Aa Jaye Mujhe Maut Hai Kiss Kaam Ka Jeena
Jagi Hu Bahut Aakey Chacha Mujhko Sulana

۴
Paani Ki Talab Mujhko Khilati Rahi Thhoker
Kehta Tha Laiyn Doonga Mai Faujo Ko Pilaker
Jab Shimr-e-Laiyn Ker Chuka Serab Yeh Lashker
Dikhla Ke Mujhe Phek Diya Paani Zameen Per
Tab Samjhi Mai Kya Hota Hai Ummeed Dilana

۵
Ek Jaam Hua Shaam-e-Ghariban Me Mayasser
Woh Kuza Mai Rakh Ayi Thi Asghar Ki Lahad Per
Tha Mujhe Se Bhi Chhota Mera Bhayya Mera Dilbar
Sharminda Mai Ho Jati Chacha Pyas Bujha Ker
Ab Apney Hi Haton Se Meri Pyas Bhujana

۶
Ek Rassi Me Hum Sabke Gale Bandh Ke Aada
Kertey Huey Tash-Heer Chaley Janib-e-Kufa
Aur Us Pe Musibat Ye Thi Qad Mera Tha Chhota
Jab Uthhti Thi Phuphiya Toh Gala Khichta Tha Mera
Gardan Ka Meri Zakhm Chacha Dekhney Aana

۷
Thi Dil Ki Tamanna Wahi Maut Aaye Mujhe Bhi
Turbat Hai Tarayi Pe Jahan Merey Chahca Ki
Ek Chhoti Si Sahil Pe Baney Qabr Bhi Meri
Yeh Aakhri Khwahish Bhi Na Ho Payegi Poori
Mar Ker Bhi Rahega Mera Zinda Me Thhikana

۸
Anwar Yeh Sada Percham-e-Abbas Se Aayi
Mai Bhool Nahi Sakta Sakina Meri Bachchi
Kaatey Gaye Bazoo Merey Majboor Tha Bibi
Mashqeeza Alam Me Hai Bandha Dekh Le Ab Bhi
Percham Ka Merey Kaam Hai Duniya Ko Batana
