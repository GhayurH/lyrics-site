Mangdi Shimar Toun Zainab Rida Ay Enj Dhi Ali Di Hoi Ghareebe

Reya Kon Baqi, Kis Nu Bulaaye
Enj Dhi Ali Di Hoi Ghareebe

Maqtal Cho Aayi, Ede Vaal Khul Gaye, Darbaar Aya, Onu Veer Bhul Gaye
Kardi Maran Di, Zainab Dua Eh, Enj Dhi Ali Di Hoi Ghareebe

Jis Bhen De Veer, Hovan Atharaan,Rul Gaye Eh Kivein O Vich Bazaara
Bacheya Na Koi, Abid Siva Eh, Enj Dhi Ali Di Hoi Ghareebe

Pehli Vaar Zainab Jado Kufay Ayi, Veeraan Da Saya, Babay Di Shahee
Duji Vaar Kufay, Ayi Rasiyaan Pa Ke, Enj Dhi Ali Di Hoi Ghareebe

Pamaal Takea Babey Da Seena, Bhul Zakhm Apne Gayee Hai Sakina
Puchdi Najaf Da Zainab Toun Raah Eh, Enj Dhi Ali Di Hoi Ghareebe
