1
Loriyon Se Sonay Walay Hasan Sadiq.
Loriyon Se Soney Walay So Rahe Ho Tum Kahan.
Do Sada Maqtal Se Asghar Dhondti Phirti Hai Maa.
Hathoon Pe Mehndi Lagati Sar Pe Sehra Bandhti.
Reh Gaye Armaan Adhoray Tum Na Ho Paye Jawaan.
Ro Pari Sham-e-Ghareebaan Dekh Kar Soye Khiyam.
Uth Raha Hai Maa Ke Dil Se Ya Keh Khaimon Se Dhowaan.
Siskiyaan Bharti Hai Madar Gaud Veraan Dekh Kar.
Khali Jholay Se Lipat Ke Ro Rahi Hain Loriyaan.
Sharam Se Darya Ki Lehrein Pani Pani Ho Gayin.
Haye Jab Sokhay Labon Par Phairi Asghar Ne Zabaan.
Maut Ko Aaya Paseena Halqay Asghar Dekh Kar.
Kanpta Tha Teer-e-Hurmal Sath Mein Larzi Kamaan.
Qabar Mein Jawad-o- Sadiq Sath Le Kar Jaein Gay.
Dil Mein Tera Dard Asghar Aankh Se Aanso Rawaan.
