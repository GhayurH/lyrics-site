1
Pukaro gi Main baba ko bhala maray tamachay tu
Tu chahta hai main na rou mujhy baba mila dy tu
Rokay gi na meri ahy hai jab tak bi meri saansay
Main Chekho gi chilao gi bhala jo bi saza dy to

2
Agar chahta hai zindan me khamoshi ho to ye soon lay
Main ho jao gi chup lekin Ha sir baba ka LA dy to
Andhra hi Andhra hai rokay ga na mera matam
Bhala chahay ye zindan ko Qabar meri bana dy to

3
Ye zindan ki dewaro pay laho mera pukaray GA
Mere ronay pay aye zalim bhala pehray laga dy to
Mila sir to Masooma nay shimar say ye kaha Johar
Mera lasha uthay GA mera bhaia boula dy to
