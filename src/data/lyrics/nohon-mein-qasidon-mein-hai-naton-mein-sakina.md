Nohon Mein Qasidon Mein Hai Naton Mein Sakina
Shamil Hai An Al-Hamd Ki Baton Mein Sakina
Shabeer Ke Sajdoon Ne Khudawand Se Tujh Ko
Manga Hai Tahajud Bhari Raton Mein Sakina
Jibreel Ke Sheh Par Se Muqadas Tera Jhola
Zainab Ne Jholaya Tujhe Hathoon Mein Sakina
Woh Arsh Woh Mamora Woh Kaba Woh Saheefay
Tehreer Tera Naam Hai Saton Mein Sakina
Hum Loag Ghulaman-e-Sakina Hain Azal Se
Our Zaat Ka Yeh Fakhar Hai Zatoon Mein Sakina
Woh Mulk-e-Wafadari-e-Abbas Hai Jis Ke
Shehroon Mein Sakina Hai Dehaton Mein Sakina
Jaloot Ke Zindan Mein Parhta Raha Barson
Taboot-e-Sakina, Manajaton Mein Sakina
Pani Na Tujhe Milne Ki Padash Mein Ik Din
Rab Aag Laga De Ga Furaton Mein Sakina
AKBAR Ka Bharam Rakhiyo Keh Nohon Ke Elawah
Kuch Bhi Nahi Amal Ke Khaton Mein Sakina
