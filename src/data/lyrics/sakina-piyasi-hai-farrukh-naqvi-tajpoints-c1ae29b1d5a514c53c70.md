Sakina Haye Safar Mein Yeh Dukh Uthati Hai Sakina Pyasi Hai.
Keh Pani Foaj Dikha Kar Ossay Girati Hai Sakina Pyasi Hai.

1
Sitam Ke Teer Se Mashkeza Na Bacha Paye.
Na Pani La Sakay Tere Liye Chacha Haye.
Sina Pe Ghazi Ke Sar Se Sada Yeh Aati Hai Sakina Pyasi Hai.
Pukari Dekh Ke Naizay Pe Pyasay Baap Ka Sar.

2
Yeh Beti Aadi Hai Soney Ki Tere Seene Par.
Na Chain Aata Hai Baba Na Neend Aati Hai Sakina Pyasi Hai.
Zabaan Pe Iss Ki Jo Pani Ka Naam Aaye Ga.
Tu Khoon Jo Rota Hai Sun Kar Woh Seh Na Paye Ga.

3
Gala Hai Khushk Par Abid Se Woh Chupati Hai Sakina Pyasi Hai.
Kahin Se Urr Ke Jo Zakhmo Pe Rait Parti Hai.
Tu Do Katay Hoye Hathoon Ko Yaad Karti Hai.
Najane Kaisay Woh Zakhmo Se Rait Hatati Hai Sakina Pyasi Hai.

4
Bandha Hai Tang Rasan Mein Gala Youn Dukhta Hai.
Keh Sans Khushk Galay Mein Ghuttan Se Rukta Hai.
Ossay Khud Apni Yeh Halat Bohat Rulati Hai Sakina Pyasi Hai.
Dil-e-Rabab Mein Asghar Ka Gham Bhi Taza Hai.

5
Na Maa Bhula Saki Be Sheer Ka Janaza Hai.
Our Ab Rabab Ko Yeh Baat Khaye Jati Hai Sakina Pyasi Hai.
Tamachay Shimar Ne Pyasi Ke Munh Pe Marey Hain.
Jo Kan Zakhmi Hain Zalim Ne Dur Utaray Hain.

6
Sar-e-Hussain Hai Rota Woh Jab Batati Hai Sakina Pyasi Hai.
 Asad Bata Ke Yeh Beti Ko Apni Rota Hai.
Kabhi Jo Samne Beti Ke Pyasa Hota Hai.
Mujhe Tu Pani Meri Beti Tu Pilati Hai, Sakina Pyasi Hai.
