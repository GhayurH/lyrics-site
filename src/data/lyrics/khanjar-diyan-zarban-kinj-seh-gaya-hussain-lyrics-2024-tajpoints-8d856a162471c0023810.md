1
Khanjar Diyan Zarban Kinj Seh Gaya Hussain.
Hun Rab De Hawalay Veran Haye Zainab Kitay Wain.
Satar Qadam Taun Wekhdi Rayiyaan Khon Aalodah Ambari Jaya.
Na Kat Mere Veer Di Gardan Hath Joor Ke Roway Sain.

2
Quran Rasool Khudai Main De Ke Putar Bachai.
Aye Jane Dard Ki Ummat Ainu Putar Jay Deney Pain.
Kalay Kaprey Pa Ke Rondiyaan Mariyam, Hawa, Aasiya, Zahra.
Main Ronday Wekhay Nana, Aadam, Mursal, Saqlain.

3
Sir Di Chadar Lut Ke Ghazi Puchday Ne Aj Aan Bachaway.
Hun Veer Bola Oh Ghazi Mainu Sham De Loki Kain.
Hashar Taein Matam Raina Aye Sadqa Pak Ali Asghar Da.
 Tosif Dua Mehdi Di Shala Matami Wasday Rain.
