Tauheed Ka Jamal Hai Sajdah Hussain Ka.
Tableeg-e-La Zawal Hai Sajdah Hussain Ka.

1
ALLAH Ki Jo Zubanein Hain Unhon Ne Yeh Kaha.
Sajdoon Ka Zuljalal Hai Sajdah Hussain Ka.
Apne Lahu Se Likh Gaye Abid Yeh Har Jagah.
Khaliq Ka Hum Khiyal Hai Sajdah Hussain Ka.

2
Dekha Jo Karbala Mein Tu Khaliq Bhi Keh Utha.
Meri Tarha Kamal Hai Sajdah Hussain Ka.
Tauheed Ki Taraf Koi Uthay Ga Kaisay Hath.
Tauheed Ki Tu Dhaal Hai Sajdah Hussain Ka.

3
Misal-e-Hussain Iss Ki Bhi Koi Misl Nahi.
Ahad Hai Be Misal Hai Sajdah Hussain Ka.
Iss Ka Koi Jawab Hai Aaye Woh Samne.
Mehshar Talak Sawal Hai Sajdah Hussain Ka.

4
Ab Tak Hai Jis Ke Khof Se Batil Ka Sar Nagoon.
Wahdat Ka Woh Jalal Hai Sajdah Hussain Ka.
ALLAH Se Pocha Azhar Ne Kya Kya Hai Tere Pas.
Rab Ne Kaha Filhaal Hai Sajdah Hussain Ka.
