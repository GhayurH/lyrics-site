Aate hai za’areen e karbobala

haathon may leke nishan e wafa

matami hai aur dilon may wila

kaale libaas aur shahe aza

lab pe sajaye huwe ye sada

jannat hai roza tera jannat hai poori ye karbobala

ai walla ai walla ai walla hussaina

karbala jannat hai karbala

walla jannat hai karbala

as salam hussain ya hussaina

yabna rasool ya hussaina

as salam hussain ya hussaina

yabna zahra ya hussaina

aaye ziarat ko hum, khaate hai teri qasam

matam ye hoga na kam, aankhein rehegi ye nam

jannat hai roza tera…

shaukhe ziarat jo tha, maula mai paidal chala

khaab ye poora hua, dekha haram to kaha

jannat hai roza tera…

pani tere shehr ka, kausar se meetha laga

uski hawa hai dawa, khaak hai khaake shifa

jannat hai roza tera…

jaye jahan bhi nazar, aaye nazar sar hi sar

khud si bali ya bashar, hai ye sada humsafar

jannat hai roza tera…

ek taraf dar tera, ek taraf ba-wafa

do jannato ki hawa, ashk huwe lab kusha

jannat hai roza tera…

rauze ka husn hai kya, gumbat arshe khuda

fanoose shams o zuha, meenaar auje wafa

aaraishe bam o dar, seera ho jis se nazar

nasb hai dewaar par, jaise ke sham o qamar

jannat hai roza tera…

haye qateel e jafa, tu yahan pyasa raha

mujhko sare karbala, koi na pyasa mila

ay mere tashnadahan, tu to raha be-kafan

jaate hai log watan, le ke yahan se kafan

jannat hai roza tera…

haye shahe karbala, haye qateel e jafa

za’airon pe tere, ek wo waqt tha

haath pairon ko katwake bhi ba-khuda

tere rauze pe aate thay ahle aza

tujhpe fida kar diye, maa’on ne haste huwe

ghar may thay kitne diye, bas yehi kehte huwe

jannat hai roza tera…

farhan o mazhar kaho, dunya ko paigham do

rauzaye sheh pe chalo, aur yehi kehte raho

jannat hai roza tera…
