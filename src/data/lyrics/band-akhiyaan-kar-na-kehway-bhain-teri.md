Band Akhiyaan Kar Na Kehway Bhain Teri Aye Sham Ban Gayi Maqtal Hai Meri
Main Wendi Rayiyaan Har Zarb Teri Aye Sham Ban Gayi Maqtal Hai Meri
Nu Lakh Da Majma Mere Khulay Waal Aye Abid Taun Puch Le Turna Mohal Aye
Teri Sohni Nibh Gayi Hun Wari Meri Aye Sham Ban Gayi Maqtal Hai Meri
Paidal Tura Ke Oss Nu Le Aao Puchda Yazid Aye Mainu Dasao
Ais Qafilay Wich Zainab Hai Kehri Aye Sham Ban Gayi Maqtal Hai Meri
Chehlum Manawan Karbal Che Aayi Gal La Qabar Nu Kehway Zahra Jayi
Tak Kiwein Ujari Hai Bhain Teri Aye Sham Ban Gayi Maqtal Hai Meri
Fizza De Waris V Aaye Nana Zainab Da Waris Aaya Koi Na
Sir Nangay Rondi Rayi Dhi Hai Teri Aye Sham Ban Gayi Maqtal Hai Meri
Ainj HASAN Lutiya Zahra Da Ghar Aye Ghorey De Gal Wich Ghazi Da Sar Aye
Dasaan Musibat Main Kehri Kehri Aye Sham Ban Gayi Maqtal Hai Meri
