Band akhiyaan kar na, kehway bhain teri
Ae Shaam ban gayi maqtal hai meri
Main wendi rahi har zarb teri

۱
Nau lakh da majma, mere khulle waal ae
Abid ton puchh le, turna muhaal ae
Teri sohni nibhi gayi hun waari meri

۲
Paidal tura ke us nu le aao
Puchhda Yazid ae, mainu dasaao
Ais qaafile wich Zainab hai kehri

۳
Chehlum manaawan Karbal ch aayi
Gal laa qabr nu, kehway Zahra jaayi
Tak kiwein ujri hai bhain teri

۴
Fizza de waaris vi aaye Nana
Zainab da waaris aaya koi na
Sir nangay rondi rahi dhi hai teri

۵
Har jaa te veeraṅ patthar maiṅ khaandi
Gir jaanda Abid, us nu uthaandi
Oh bhir gayi ae veeraṅ bathteri

۶
Ainj Hasan lutiya Zahra da ghar ae
Ghore de gal wich Ghazi da sar ae
Dassaaṅ musibat maiṅ kehri kehri
