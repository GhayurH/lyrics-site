1
Sath Shabir Ke Sajday Mein Khuda Bolay Ga.
Kis Ne Socha Tha Banday Mein Khuda Bolay Ga.
Koi Mosa Se Kahe Aa Ke Mile Asghar Se.
Iss Ki Muskan Ke Jalway Mein Khuda Bolay Ga.

2
Aa Ke Quran Zara Nook-e-Sina Pe Sunna.
Ab Tu Shabir Ke Lehjay Mein Khuda Bolay Ga.
Tere Seene Se Muhammad Ki Sada Aaye Gi.
Ali AKbar Tere Naizay Mein Khuda Bolay Ga.

3
Aa Gayi Bint-e-Ali Khak Ki Chadar Aurhe.
Ab Issi Khak Ke Parday Mein Khuda Bolay Ga.
Mere Abbas Alam Tu Ne Uthaya Aise.
Tere Parcham Ke Phareray Mein Khuda Bolay Ga.

4
Lasha Qasim Ka Sada Deta Hai Yeh Maqtal Se.
Mere Har Aik Hi Tukray Mein Khuda Bolay Ga.
Bole Haider Mera Khutba Tu Mukamal Kar De.
Meri Dukhtar Tere Khutbay Mein Khuda Bolay Ga.

5
Loag Barsaein Gaye Jab Aap Pe Pathar Abid.
Marhaba Aap Ke Raste Mein Khuda Bolay Ga.
Hoon Gaye Matam Mein Qayam Se Khuda Tak Saray.
Aaj Bahlool Ke Nohay Mein Khuda Bolay Ga.
