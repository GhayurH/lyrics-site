Aey shame gharibaan
Ro ro ke fughan karti thi yeh saniye Zehra

1
1) Is dashte me maara gaya,  bhai mera pyaasa

2
2) Kehti thi khari madare Asghar ye tarap kar, maara gaya Asghar
Veeran hay haey meray,  shehzaday ka jhula

3
3) Laila nay kaha haey may arman barhati, usay dulha banati
Tha sher sa beta mera,  athara baras ka

4
4) Nauha ye Sakina ka tha dur chin gaye meray, lagtay hain tamache
Jaakar mere ammu ko,  bataa dain labe darya

5
5) Bas baba ke sine hi pe soti hay sakina, batla day khudaara
Is ban may kahaa dhundhu,  kaha hay mere baba

6
6) Khouf aata hay masuma ko shaq hota hay seena, darti hay Sakina
Kiyun barhta hay is dashte,  bayabaa may andhera

7
7) Jaakar mere nana ko khabar karde watan may, may lut gaye ban me
Maqtal may hay be goro kafan,  unka nawasa

8
8) Kal shaadi hui aur sitam aaj ye dekha, bewa hui Kubra
Ghar lut gaya maara gaya,  ek raat ka dulha

9
9) aaya na palat kar mera Abbase dilaawar, chin jaayegi chaadar
Ab koi gariboon kaa,  nahi poochnay waala

10
10) khud jis pay mujhe naaz tha kal sham thi aisi, aur ab ye gharibi
Athara mere bhaayi thay,  our ab hoon may tanha
