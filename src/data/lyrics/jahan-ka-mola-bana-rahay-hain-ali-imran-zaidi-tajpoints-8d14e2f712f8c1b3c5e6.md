1
Jahan Ka Mola Bana Rahay Hain Ali Imran Zaidi.
Nabi Ali Ko Ba Hukam-e-Khaliq Jahan Ka Maula Bana Rahe Hain.
Tamam Nabiyon Ki Our Apni Yahan Risalat Bacha Rahe Hain.
Boland Hathoon Mein Hath Kar Ke Jo Taptay Sehra Mein Hajiyon Ko.
Khuda-e-Barhaq Ka Faisla Hai Jo Muskura Kar Suna Rahe Hain.
Nabi Yeh Pehle Hi Keh Chukay Hain Khuda Jo Kehday Woh Bolta Hoon.
Nabi Ne Man Kuntu Jab Kaha Hai Tu Sheikh Kyun Tilmila Rahe Hain.
Wilayat-e-Shair-e-Kibriya Ka Howa Jo Ayelaan Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein.
Hai Kis Ka Eman Mujh Pe Kitna Wahan Nabi Aazma Rahe Hain.
Jisay Howa Shak Nabi Ke Opar Gira Tha Pathar Ossi Ke Opar.
Tu Sochein Hizyan Kehne Walay Pahar Khud Par Gira Rahe Hain.
Woh Kar Rahe Hain Ada-e-Sunnat Ada Youn Ujrat Bhi Ho Rahi Hai.
Ali Ki Midhat Jo Sun Rahe Hain Jo Madh-e-Haider Suna Rahe Hain.
Jinhoon Ne Mere Nabi Ko Mana Magar Nabi Ki Kabhi Na Mani.
Woh Hi Madine Ka Our Woh Hi Nizam-e-Kaba Chala Rahe Hain.
Ba Faiz e Majlis Ba Faiz e Mehfil Ba Faiz e Nazar-o-Niyaz Zaigham.
Nabi Ki Beti Ka Hai Yeh Sadqa Jo Kha Rahe Hain Khila Rahe Hain.
