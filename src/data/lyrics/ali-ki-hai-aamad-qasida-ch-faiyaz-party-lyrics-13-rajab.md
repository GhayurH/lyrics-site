1
Ali Ki Hai Aamad Qasida Ch Faiyaz Party
Ali Ki Hai Aamad Keh Terah 13 Rajab Hai Qasam Hai Khuda Ki Yeh Manzar Ajab Hai.
Dekh Kar Khuda Ne Arsh Se Nazara Aasman Sadqa Kiya.
Ali Aaye ALLAH Ali Aaye ALLAH Ali Aaye ALLAH Ali Aaye.

2
Ali Hai Wali Hai Yeh Shair-e-Jali Hai Yeh Kul-e-Emaan Hai Nabi Ka Wasi Hai.
Ghar Se Chali Ali Ki Maa Hai Na Imran Pocha Keh Jana Kahan Hai.
Yaqeen Tha Zameen Par Koi La Makaan Hai Mariyam Ko Roka Gaya Kaha Rab Ne Mera Yeh Ghar Hai.
Quran Ne Kiya Yeh Ayyaan Hai Haider Kabay Ki Jaan Hai.

3
Sajdoon Ki Sar Zameen Ban Gaya Hai Kaba Jhoom Kar Mustafa Kaha Ali Aaye.
Jo Aasmaan Ke Raston Ko Zameeno Ke Rastoon Se Behtar Bataye.
Jo Israr e Tauheed Ban Kar Ho Aaye Koi Baat Inn Ko Bataye Na Shan Ali Ki Chupaye.
Mushkil Mein Woh Ali Ko Pukarey Har Mushkil Hal Ho Jaye.

4
Ghar Hai Woh Ali Ka Ghar Hai Jo Khuda Ka Uthi Har Taraf Se Sada Ali Aaye.
Qasida Khuwan Party Ch Faiyaz Party Sialkot.
Matami Sangat
Anjuman-e-Sipah-e-Ahl-e-Bait Sialkot
