Ghazi tere bajon rul javay gi aal nabi di saari
Ro aakhay Zainab na kar veera maqtal val tyaari

1
1) Haaye tapdiyan kariyan chumkey puchdi Abid nu
Masoom Sakina ro ro ke
Kis gal tu dushman hogayi veera saadi duniya sari

2
2) Na ghut paani da mileya Asghar pyasay nu
Na Ghazi khaimay aa sakya
Jhooley nu tak tak rondi rayi Asghar di behn vechari

3
3) Lagda ae nana vi mere nal ruseya ae
Nahi dhukrey sunda ujrii de
Akbar mein tere aun diyan mantaan man man ke haari

4
4) Mantaan mang mang ke rab ton tenu mangeya si
Tu chup kar key tur chaleya aen
Je chaleya aen te behna nu gal laa ja jandi vaari

5
5) Lakh hon yazid(la) Hussain da matam rukna nai
Akhtar gal haq di kehnda ae
Ta hashar jahan te rehna hai mazloom da matam jaari
