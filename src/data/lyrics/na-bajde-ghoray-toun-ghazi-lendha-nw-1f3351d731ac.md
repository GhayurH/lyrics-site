Na Bajde Ghoray Toun Ghazi Lendha, Na Saar Bay Raina Main Shaam Jaandi.

Na Tauq Gaal Vich Sajjad Panda, Na Saar Bay Raina
Main Shaam Jaandi.

Jay Meray Ghazi Nu Tu Laaran Di,
Kaddi Ijazaat Hussain Denda
Na Kala Akbar Di Laash Chanda
Na Saar Bay Raina
Main Shaam Jaandi.

Jay Meray Ghazi Bhira Na Maarda,
Main Shaam Day Vich Kaddi Na Ruldi
Na Koyi Bhagi Di Bhain Aanda
Na Saar Bay Raina
Main Shaam Jaandi

Hussain Raye Meray Dil Ch’ Hasrat,
Main Teray Laashay Tay Vain Poundi
Na Jhirkaan Daykay Koyi Uthanda
Na Saar Bay Raina
Main Shaam Jaandi.

Dou Jag Da Mukhtaar Mera Abid
Kafan Sakina Da Maangreya Ay
Na Vakhri Oodhi Kabar Banada
Na Saar Bay Raina
Main Shaam Jaandi.
