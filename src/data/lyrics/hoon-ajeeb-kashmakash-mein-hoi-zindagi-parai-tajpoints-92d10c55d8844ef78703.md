1
Hon Ajeeb Kashmakash Mein Hoi Zindagi Parai.
Mere Paas Teri Yaadein Tere Pass BeWafai.
Mere Dil Ko Tu Ne Cheena Meri Pareet Jag Ne Loti.
Mera Dil Bhi Hai Paraya Meri Pareet Bhi Parai.
Tujhe Khuwab Mein Hi Dekhon Yeh Bharam Bhi Aaj Toota.
Tere Khuwab Kaise Dekhon Mujhe Neend Hi Na Ayi.
