1
Abid nay kaha aakay kareeb bintay Ali say
Ab suway watan chaliye phuphi-jaan may sadkay
Sunkar ye khabar zakh may jigar phaat gaye saaray
Markhad seh lipat kaar kiye Zainab nay yeh naalay

2
May jab bhi watan jaaungi bhaiya
Bin teray na ghaar jaaun gi bhaiya

3
1) Athara baras ka Ali Akbar Bhi yehi hai
Cheh maa ka nanha Ali Asghar bhi yehi hai
Hay khaakh basaar Farwa ka dilbar
Pamaaal badan Qasim-e-mustaar
Kis dil say may ghaar jaaungi bhaiya

4
2) Kya din tay kay ahbaad tah Zahra ka gulistaan
Yeh kiski nazar khagayi saab hogaya veeran
Baaki nayhi ab koyi nigaybaan
Saab bhayi meray hogaye bay jaan
Maar jaungi maar jaungi bhaiya

5
3) Abbas say aur tum say hay Zainab ko nidaamat
Zindaan may choor aayi hu dono ki amaanat
Kya toot gayi mujh peh qayamat
Kis darja hay Zainab ko khijaalat
Woh laab pay nayhi laaungi bhaiya

6
4) Souta hay mera dhoop may nanha Ali Asghar
Kehti hai yeh Banno kay hai waali bhi yehi par
Zindaan may ek maar gayi dukhtar
Baithegi na ab saaye may maadar
Kya karkay may sumjhaungi bhaiya

7
5) Tayyaar nayhi suway watan jaanay ko Laila
Woh kehti hai may choor du kis tarha say tanha
Athara baras ka mera beta
Ray jaayega is bun may akela
Kaisay usay sumjhaungi bhaiya

8
6) Ahmad ki khabar sunkay Anees aayengay saaray
Yeh neel numaaya hai jo baazu peh rasan kay
Ghar pooch liye mujh say kisi nay
Batlaungi unko may yeh kaisay
Bus sharam say maarjaaungi bhaiya
