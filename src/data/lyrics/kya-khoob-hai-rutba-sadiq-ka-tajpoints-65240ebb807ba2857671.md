1
Sadiq Ki Aal Our Khud Sadiq Kya Khoob Hai Rutba Sadiq Ka.
Quran Ke Lab Par Rehta Hai Har Waqt Qaseeda Sadiq Ka.
Sarwar Ki Natein Parh Kar Bhi Woh La Hasil Reh Jaye Ga.
Seene Mein Jis Ne Bugz Apne Rakha Poshida Sadiq Ka.

2
Har Aik Ko Yeh Taufeeq Nahi Masoom Ka Jashan Mananay Ki.
Masror Nazar Woh Aaye Ga Hai Jo Bhi Chonida Sadiq Ka.
Sakhiyon Ke Pak Gharanay Ki Khushiyon Mein Hum Khush Rehtey Hain.
Qismat Mein Hamari Likha Hai Youn Jashan Manana Sadiq Ka.

3
Islam Ki Asal Haqeeqat Hi Ahmad Ki Aal Se Ulfat Hai.
Hubdar-e-Nabi Kehlaye Woh Jo Hai Garvida Sadiq Ka.
Baqir Ke Lal Ke Naam Se Hi Yeh Deen-e-Haq Mansoob Howa.
Har Door Hai Maula Jafar Ka Har Aik Zamana Sadiq Ka.

4
Sadaat Hi Makkay Walay Hain Sadaat Madine Walay Hain.
Sarkar Ki Nisbat Se Dekho Hai Makka Madina Sadiq Ka.
Sarkar-e-Imam-e-Jafar Ki Shiyon Par Khas Ataein Hain.
Dunya Mein Humein Jo Kuch Bhi Mila Har Cheez Hai Sadqa Sadiq Ka.

5
Iss Dar Se Sabhi Ne Faiz Liya Jo Jo Bhi Liya Be Aib Liya.
Hai Ilm Ke Shehar Ke Dar Ka Ghar Be Misl Dareecha Sadiq Ka.
Aousaf Bohat Hain Jafar Ke Hasnain Mein Aik Batata Hoon.
Shiyat Ka Bani Kehlana Hai Wasf Hameda Sadiq Ka.
