Manao Khushiyan Rajab Ka Mahena Aaya Hai.
Bas Apne Sath Woh Khushiyaan Hi Khushiyaan Laya Hai.

1
Yakam Rajab Ko Wiladat Hoi Hai Baqir Ki.
Rasool-e-Pak Ka Hum Naam Jis Ne Paya Hai.
Rajab Ki Panch Ko Aaye Jahan Mein Maula Naqi.
Ali Hai Chotha Muhammad Taqi Ka Jaya Hai.

2
Rajab Ki Nu Ko Tawald Howa Ali Asghar.
Laqab Bhi Baab-e-Hawaij Khuda Se Paya Hai.
Rajab Ki Daswein Ko Paida Hoye Taqi Maula.
Jo Tesra Hai Muhammad Raza Ka Jaya Hai.

3
Rajab Ki Terah Ko Kabay Mein Aaye Maula Ali.
Jidar-e-Kaba Ne Hans Hans Ke Dar Banaya Hai.
Aye Mohtasham Do Muhammad, Hain Teen Ali Aaye.
Rajab Ne Sab Se Bara Yeh Maqam Paya Hai.
