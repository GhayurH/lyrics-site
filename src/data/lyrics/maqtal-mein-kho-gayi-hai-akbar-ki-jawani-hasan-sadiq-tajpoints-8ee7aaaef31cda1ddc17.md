Maqtal Mein Kho Gayi Hai Akbar Ki Jawani Hasan Sadiq.
Soyi Hai Sina Kha Ke Payamber Ki Nishani.

1
Sughra Ko Madine Mein Ajab Khuwab Yeh Aaya.
Luttay Hoye Dekhi Hai Baradar Ki Jawani.
Tukray Ali Akbar Ke Kalejay Ke Jo Dekhey.
Yaad Aa Gayi Phir Maut Ko Shabar Ki Jawani.

2
Atharah Baras Ka Tha Din Biyah Ke Aaye.
Tar Khoon Mein Magar Ho Gayi Akbar Ki Jawani.
 Rehan Sheh-e-Wala Ka Daman Howa Khali.
Bachpan Raha Asghar Ka Na Akbar Ki Jawani.
