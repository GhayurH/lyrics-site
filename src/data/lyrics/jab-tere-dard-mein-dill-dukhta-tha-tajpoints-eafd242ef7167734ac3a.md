Jab Teri Dhun Mein Jiya Karte The.
Hum Bhi Chup Chaap Phira Karte The.

1
Ankh Mein Piyas Howa Karti Thi.
DIll Mein Tofan Utha Karte The.
Loag Aate The Ghazal Sunne Ko.
Hum Teri Baat Kiya Karte The.

2
Sach Samajte The Tere Wadon Ko.
Raat Din Ghar Mein Raha Karte The.
Kissi Veerane Mein Tujh Se Mil Kar.
Dill Mein Kya Phool Khila Karte The.

3
Ghar Ki Dewar Sajane Ke Liye.
Hum Tera Naam Likha Karte The.
Woh Bhi Kya Din THe Bohla Kar Tujh Ko.
Hum Tujhe Yaad Kiya Karte The.

4
Jab Tere Dard Mein Dill Dukhta Tha.
Hum Tere Haq Mein Dua Karte The.
Bhujne Lagta Jo Chehra Tera.
Daag Seene Mein Jala Karte The.

5
APne Jazbon Ki Kamandon Se Tujhe.
Hum Bhi Taskheer Kiya Karte The.
Apne Aanso Bhi Sitaron Ki Tarha.
Tere Honton Pe Sajaya Karte The.

6
Chairta Tha Gham-e-Dunya Jab Bhi.
Hum Tere Gham Se Gila Karte The.
Kal Tujhe Dekh Ke Yaad Aya Hai.
Hum Sukhanwar Bhi Howa Karte The.
