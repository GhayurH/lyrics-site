1
Jo qaid ho ke chala karwan e aal e nabi
Nazar Sakina ki Sajjad e natwan pe parri
Ke tazyanay bhi jis ko laga rahay thi shaqi
Tarrap ke is tarha Shimr e laeen se kehni lagi
Khol de bairryan bhai beemar hay

2
Soch behr e Khuda ye hay aal e aba
Yun na durre laga bhai beemar hay
Khol de bairryan bhai beemar hay

3
Pehlay hi is ko gham kia diye is ko kam
Ab na kar ye sitam bhai beemar hay
Khol de bairryan bhai beemar hay

4
Kia milay ba tujhe is ko yun maar ke
Ye azziyat na de bhai beemar hay
Khol de bairryan bhai beemar hay

5
Dekh aey bad guman kar na ye sakhtiyan
De de is ko amaan bhai beemar hay
Khol de bairryan bhai beemar hay

6
Jism hay khoon se tar ab to kuch rehm kar
Ye hay raah e safar bhai beemar hay

7
Khoon rota raha Shabbar e be nawa
Sun ke us ki sada bhai beemar hay
Khol de bairryan bhai beemar hay
