1
Haq Zahra Ka Chupaya Kis Ne Kon They Woh.
Roza Makhdoma Ka Kis Zalim Ne Giraya Kis Ne Kaun They Woh.
Markaz-e-Tauheed Tha Dar Jo Woh Dar Kalma Go.
Aag Ke Sholon Se Raakh Tha Kis Ne Banaya Kis Ne Kaun They Woh.

2
Naam Sarwar Ne Liya Jis Ka Hai Parh Ke Darood.
Haye Oss Bibi Ko Tu Keh Ke Hai Bulaya Kis Ne Kaun They Woh.
Sath Woh Layi Thi Jo Oss Sand Ke Tukron Mein.
Fatima Bibi Ko Lasha Qasim Ka Dikhaya Kis Ne Kaun They Woh.

3
Woh Madine Ki Ghali Our Sitam Ki Inteha.
Samne Shabbar Ke Hath Zahra Pe Uthaya Kis Ne Kaun They Woh.
Thokaron Se Marna Taziyane Marna.
Zakhmi Lashay Jaisa Haal Bibi Ka Banaya Kis Ne Kaun They Woh.

4
Betiyaan Thin Dekhtin Woh Musibat Ki Ghari.
Bandh Kar Rasiyaan Ali Ko Madine Mein Phiraya Kis Ne Kaun They Woh.
Pochtay Hain Ro Ke Yeh Kalma Go Se Matami.
Dar Gira Ke Mohsin Khon-e-Mohsin Hai Bahaya Kis Ne Kaun They Woh.
