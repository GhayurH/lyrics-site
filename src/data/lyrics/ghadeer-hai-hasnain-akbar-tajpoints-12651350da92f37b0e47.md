Ghadeer Hai Hasnain Akbar

1
Kuch Youn Dil-o-Damag Pe Chayi Ghadeer Hai.
Kabay Mein Bhi Humein Nazar Aayi Ghadeer Hai.
Har Rooz-e-Eid Ashk Bahane Ke Bawojod.
Sajjad Ne Khushi Se Manai Ghadeer Hai.
Sajday Tu Oss Ke Zail Mein Mehfooz Ho Gaye.
Maula Ne Karbala Mein Bachai Ghadeer Hai.

2
Tasdeeq Kar Rahi Hain Yeh Baligh Ki Aayatein.
Deen-e-Rasool-e-Haq Ki Kamai Ghadeer Hai.
Lehja Badal Liya Hai Khud Apne Habib Se.
Jaisay Khuda Ki Sari Khudai Ghadeer Hai.
Apne Taluqat Ki Bunyad Hai Ali.
Hum Ko Woh Baha Gaya Jisay Bhayi Ghadeer Hai.

3
Ishq-e-Ali Mein Dosra Meesum Na Ho Saka.
Jis Ne Zabaan Kata Ke Sunai Ghadeer Hai.
Ghar-e-Hira Hai Kuntu Se Pehle Ka Tazkira.
Ab Iss Ke Baad Jo Bhi Hai Bhai Ghadeer Hai.
 Akbar Faqeer Loag Jahan Se Guzar Gaye.
Bholay Howon Ko Yaad Dilai Ghadeer Hai.
