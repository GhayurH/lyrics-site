1
Zainab Kay Samny Syed Wajhi Hasan
Bhai Behan Pe Kaisi Ghurbat Ki Yeh Ghari Hai.
Satar Qadam Se Zainab Manzar Yeh Dekhti Hai.
Pyasay Galay Pe Zalim Khanjar Chala Raha Hai.
Gin Gin Ke Waar Kar Ke Hans Hans Ke Dekhta Hai.
Maqtal Mein Aa Na Jaye Roti Hoi Sakina.
Khaimon Ko Dekhti Hain Mur Mur Ke Bint-e-Zahra.
Jab Shimar Shah-e-Dein Ke Seene Pe Baithta Hai.
Bethi Hoi Hai Maa Bhi Shabeer Ke Sarhaney.
Kehti Hai Aayi Hoon Main Beta Tujhe Bachaney.
Zakhmi Hain Hath Maa Ke Our Bhai Ka Gala Hai.
Ro Ro Ke Hath Apne Chehray Pe Marti Hai.
Abbas Tum Kahan Zainab Pukarti Hai.
Jab Shimar Shah-e-Dein Ke Baloon Ko Khainchta Hai.
Khanjar Ko Rook Le Aye Khanjar Chalaney Walay.
Yeh Woh Gala Hai Jis Ko Choma Hai Mustafa Ne.
Gardan Pe Hath Rakh Ke Zahra Ne Yeh Kaha Hai.
Dil Phat Raha Hai Kitne Hain Zakhm Iss Ke Tan Mein.
Sab Ne Rula Rula Ke Mara Hai Iss Ko Ran Mein.
Zahra Hi Janti Hain Bhai Ka Haal Kiya Hai.
Mazloom Be Watan Par Aisay Na Tu Jafa Kar.
Kyun Zulm Dha Raha Hai Aye Shimar Kuch Haya Kar.
Sokhi Hoi Ragoon Ko Khanjar Se Katta Hai.
Yeh Thak Chuka Hai Ran Mein Lashay Utha Utha Ke.
Kyun Oss Pe Hans Raha Hai Khanjar Dikha Dikha Ke.
Yeh Hai Ghareeb-e-Zahra Kyun Iss Ko Marta Hai.
Haan Woh Wajih Ghurbat Zeeshan Kaisay Likhe.
Waar Aakhri Kiya Jab Zalim Ne Muskura Ke.
Kat Ke Badan Se Haye Sar Khak Par Gira Hai.
