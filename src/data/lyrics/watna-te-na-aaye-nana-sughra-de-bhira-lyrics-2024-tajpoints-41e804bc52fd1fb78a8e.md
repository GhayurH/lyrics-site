1
Watna Te Na Aaye Nana Sughra De Bhira.
Tere Deway Baal Ke Nana Sughra Rondi Aye Mere Wichrey Veer Mila.
Akbar V Tu Ayein Main V Aan Zahra Haye Bas Farq Hai Aina.
Kal Piyo Te Dhi Saan Nana Haye Aj Dunya Te Ban Gaye Aan Bhain Bhira.

2
Akbar Ya Asghar Koi Veer Te Aaway Haye Mere Maan Wadhaway.
Ais Aas Te Nana Bethi Sunjiyan Rahwaan Te Mar Jaway Na Sughra.
Mere Sangti Ban Gaye Hokay Te Hawaan Haye Kinu Dard Sunawaan.
Jay Nahin Aana Si Akbar Bhain Nu Das Jandon Na Takdi Tere Rah.

3
Ik Khuwab Taun Dardi Akh Meetdi Nahin Main Haye Main Jagdi Rendi.
Main Takiya Khoni Sehra Wich Ik Banra Jidda Saang Te Hai Sehra.
Din Raat Duawaan Nadir Aye Karda Haye Nalay Hokay Bharda.
Akbar Te Sughra Waang Na Shala Dunya Te Koi Wichrey Bhain Bhira.
