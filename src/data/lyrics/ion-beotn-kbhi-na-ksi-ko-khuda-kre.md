Yun bewatan kabhi na kisiko khuda kare...

1
Gurbat mei bhai se na behen ko juda kare,

2
1. Zainab ko maut aaye isi pal khuda kare
Bhaiyya ko maar daale behen jee ke kya kare

3
2. Quraan se ahlebayt ko jo bhi juda kare
Aiso ka roz e hashr khuda hi bhala kare

4
3. Bhaiyya tumhare baad rida sar se chhinn gayi
Zainab tumhare dafn ka saaman kya kare

5
4. Kehti thi yeh Sakina tamaacho pe shimr ke
Allah yun kisiko na be aasra kare

6
5. Uss kaum ko zamana bhala kya mitaaega
Jiske liye rasool ki beti dua kare

7
6. Afsar kabhi juda na ho dil se gham e Hussain
Jab tak meri yeh zindagi mujhse wafa kare
