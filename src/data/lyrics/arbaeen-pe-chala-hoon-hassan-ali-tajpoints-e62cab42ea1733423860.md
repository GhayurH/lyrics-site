Arbaeen Pe Chala Hoon Hassan Ali Lyrics
Mere Maula Ne Mujh Ko Bulaya.
Bas Yehi Soch Kar Ro Raha Hoon.
Na Koi Khof Na Koi Dar Hai.
Kya Hasen Ishq Ka Yeh Safar Hai.
Sab Ke Lab Par Hai Haye Hussaina.
Karbala Ki Taraf Har Nazar Hai.
Aaj Paidal Najaf Se Main Chal Kar.
Karbala Ki Taraf Ja Raha Hoon.
Har Taraf Mojza Ho Raha Hai.
Jo Bhi Chaha Mujhe Mil Gaya Hai.
Zairon Ke Jo Dil Ki Hai Chahat.
Mere Maula Ko Sab Kuch Pata Hai.
Jald Aa Jaein Ghaibat Se Mehdi.
Har Qadam Bas Yehi Mangta Hoon.
Mashk Thamay Hain Ghazi Ke Nokar.
Sab Ko Pani Pilatay Hain Ro Kar.
Hai Yeh Pyasi Sakina Ka Sadqa.
Koi Pyasa Nahi Ab Yahan Par.
Haan Sabeelein Nazar Aa Rahi Hain.
Iss Safar Mein Jahan Dekhta Hoon.
Karbala Mein Qadam Jab Rakhon Ga.
Apne Maula Se Ro Kar Kahon Ga.
Shukriya Mujh Ko Zair Banaya.
Bas Yeh Keh Keh Ke Rota Rahon Ga.
Samne Hoga Ab Woh Nazara.
Jis Ki Khatir Main Ab Tak Jiya Hoon.
Teer Asghar Ne Khaya Jahan Par.
Aik Jhola Rakha Hai Wahan Par.
Chid Gaya Teer Se Halq Oss Ka.
Kya Sitam Ho Gaya Be Zabaan Par.
Yaad Aati Hai Maula Ki Ghurbat.
Haal Asghar Ka Jab Sochta Hoon.
Haye Laila Ka Akbar Woh Pyara.
Jis Ke Seene Mein Naiza Utara.
Shah Kehte They Lasha Utha Kar.
Kis Ne Mere Muhammad Ko Mara.
Kaisay Maula Ne Maa Ko Bataya.
Bas Yeh Mehsos Karta Raha Hoon.
Doon Ga Maula Ko Ghazi Ka Pursa.
Jaltay Khaimay Udasi Ka Pursa.
Our Satar Qadam Se Main Doon Ga.
Bibi Zainab Ko Bhai Ka Pursa Tha.
Kaisay Dekha Tha Bhai Pe Khanjar.
Sochtay Sochtay Ro Diya Hoon.
Aye Hasan Our Zeeshan Aao.
Khud Ko Maula Ki Janib Barhao.
Yeh Safar Har Baras Ho Muqadar.
Iss Dua Ke Liye Hath Uthao.
Har Baras Sab Se Kehta Rahon Main.
Apne Maula Se Milney Chala Hoon.
