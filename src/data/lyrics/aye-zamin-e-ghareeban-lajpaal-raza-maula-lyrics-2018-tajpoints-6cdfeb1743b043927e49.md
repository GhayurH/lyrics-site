1
Aye Zamin e Ghareeban Lajpaal Raza Maula.
Uth Ke Kafan Choon Wekho Sada Haal Raza Maula.
Aidi Cheti Kano Paiyaan Maula Raza Judaiyaan.
Lutiyaan Ne Badshahiyaan Ho Gayiyaan Ne Tabahiyaan.

2
Baghdad De Qaidi Da Sohna Lal Raza Maula.
Chaiyaan Maut Diyaan Chawaan Behna Wekhdiyaan Rahwaan.
Aj Roon Layi Bulawaan Karbal Gayiyaan Jo Mawaan.
Behna Da Wichora Aye Tere Naal Raza Maula.

3
Syed Di Qurbani Hai Khon Di Rawani.
Karbal Wali Kahani Kha Maut Gayi Jawani.
Dunya Toon Tur Piya Aye Be-Misal Raza Maula.
Syed Nu Zehar Miliya Dil Qaidiyaan Da Hiliya.

4
Pa Beriyaan Jo Chaliya Mar Ke Zindaan Choon Waliya.
Abid Te Musa Kazim Be-Haal Raza Maula.
Qatil Mamoon Tera Ditha Lash Na Sawera.
Adh Raat Da Hanera Zalim Sipah Da Ghera.

5
Ro Ro Ke Zahra De Khule Waal Raza Maula.
De Dardaan De Khazane Adil Nohay Banane.
Harfaan Nu Oh Na Jane Maula Tu E Oh Likhane.
Tere Wain Nu Parhe Ga Har Saal Raza Maula.
