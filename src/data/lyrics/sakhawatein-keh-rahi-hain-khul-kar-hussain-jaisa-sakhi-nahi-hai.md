Sakhawatein Keh Rahi Hain Khul Kar Hussain Jaisa Sakhi Nahi Hai.
Sakhi Bohat Hain Jahan Mein Par Hussain Jaisa Sakhi Nahi Hai.

1
Jo Kar-e-Shabir Ban Gaye Hain Fazeelaton Ke Liye Faeelat.
Woh Mojzat-e-Mukhairana Sunaon Ga Main Bashakal-e-Midhat.
Zaror Tum Bhi Kaho Gaye Sun Kar Hussain Jaisa Sakhi Nahi Hai.
Har Aik Iss Baat Se Hai Waqif Woh Chahe Munkir Ho Ya Momin.

2
Sakhi Mukhaiyar Nabi Ke Ghar Mein Hai Aik Se Barh Ke Aik Lekin.
Falak Ke Neeche Falak Ke Upar Hussain Jaisa Sakhi Nahi Hai.
Agar Yeh Chahein Tu Chand Taray Uar Ke In Ke Qadmo Ko Chomein.
Tamam Farshi Tamam Arshi Tawaf Karne Ko Gird Ghomein.

3
Hai Iss Ka Ahsan Sab Ke Upar Hussain Jaisa Sakhi Nahi Hai.
Hussain Mula Ne Bachpane Se Sakhawaton Ke Hunar Dikhaye.
Keh Bal-o-Par Jal Chuke The Mere Mujhe Dobara Par Dilaye.
Pukara Fitrus Uran Barh Kar Hussain Jaisa Sakhi Nahi Hai.

4
Galay Laga Kar Janab-e-Hur Ko Zameen Se Aasmaan Banaya.
Pisar Atta Kar Ke Nasal-e-Rahib Ka Nunqata Silsila Chalaya.
Hai Iss Ka Andaz Sab Se Behtar Hussain Jaisa Sakhi Nahi Hai.
Jo Mustahiq The Unhi Ki Khatir Tamam Ranj-o-Alam Bhula Kar.

5
Shab-e-Khamoshaan Ki Teergi Main Ashrafiyaan Pusht Par Utha Kar.
Kare Jo Imdad Ghar Pe Ja Kar Hussain Jaisa Sakhi Nahi Hai.
Payambaron Se Jo Ho Na Paya Woh Kam Kar Ke Dikha Raha Hai.
Keh Sar Kata Ke Aye Rab-e-Kaba Teri Khudai Bacha Raha Hai.

6
Gawahi Deta Hai Kund Khanjar Hussain Jaisa Sakhi Nahi Hai.
Humein Yaqeen Hai:WAJHI,YASIR: Zaban Sachi Yehi Kahe Gi.
Binaye Tauheed Dhondte Ho Iss Ke Sajday Talay Mile Gi.
Yahi Haqeeqat Hai Banda Parwar Hussain Jaisa Sakhi Nahi Hai.
