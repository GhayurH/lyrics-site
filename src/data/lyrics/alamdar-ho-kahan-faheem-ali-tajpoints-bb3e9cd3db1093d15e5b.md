1
Shabeer Kamar Tham Ke Detey Rahe Sada Alamdar Ho Kahan.
Imdad Karo Aa Ke Meri Mere Ba Wafa Alamdar Ho Kahan.
Har Lash Uthai Hai Zaeefi Mein Alamdar.
Maqtal Mein Akela Hoon Mere Monis-o-Ghamkhuwar.
Mushkil Hai Suno Tum Se Judai Ka Marhala Alamdar Ho Kahan.
Kuch Pal Mein Ghareebi Bhi Parhey Gi Mera Noha.
Jane Se Tere Mujh Pe Qayamat Hoi Barpa.
Sehra Mein Nabowat Ka Bikhar Jaye Ga Kunba Alamdar Ho Kahan.
Kehti Hai Nabi Zadi, Nabi Zadi Se Mil Kar.
Qasim Hai Kahan Hai Kahan Bhaiyya Ali Akbar.
Ab Kaun Banaye Ga Mere Baad Yeh Pardah Alamdar Ho Kahan.
Ahsas-e-Yateemi Se Tarap Jati Hai Bibi.
Roti Hoi Seene Mein Simat Jati Hai Bibi.
Pehlu Mein Para Rehta Hai Toota Howa Koza Alamdar Ho Kahan.
Kehti Hai Nabi Zadi Meri Baat Yeh Mano.
Bhaiyya Mere Parday Ke Mohafiz Ko Bulao.
Dil Mera Tarapta Hai Labon Par Hai Yeh Noha Alamdar Ho Kahan.
Ghazi Meri Ghurbat Yeh Sada Deti Hai Ro Kar.
Dekho Meri Aankhon Se Zarra Aa Sakay Yeh Manzar.
Maqtal Mein Muhammad Ka Nawasa Howa Tanha Alamdar Ho Kahan.
 Yawar Faheem Jan-e-Payamber Ka Yeh Noha.
Jati Hai Jan Jaye Har Haal Mein Parhna.
Nohon Ki Sadaon Mein Sada Tum Bhi Lagana Alamdar Ho Kahan.
