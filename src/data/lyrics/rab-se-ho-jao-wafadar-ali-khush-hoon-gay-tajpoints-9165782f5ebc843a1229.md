1
Rab Se Ho Jao Wafadar Ali Khush Hoon Gay.
Dil Se Ban Jao Azadar Ali Khush Hoon Gay.
Apni Ulfat Ka Youn Ahsas Dilane Aao.
Majlis-e-Shah Mein Tum Ashk Bahane Aao.
Ban Ke Ashkon Ke Kharedar Ali Khush Hoon Gay.
Jo Yeh Kehte Hain Keh Hai Ya Ali Kehna Bidat.
Aise Logon Pe Kiye Jao Hamesha Lanat.
Munkiron Se Raho Be-Zaar Ali Khush Hoon Gay.
Pocha Meesum Se Kissi Ne Keh Aqeedat Kya Hai.
Madh-e-Haider Ki Sar-e-Daar Zarorat Kya Hai.
Bool Uthe Meesum-e-Tamar Ali Khush Hoon Gay.
Ban Ke Mukhtar Jo Paigham-e-Fana Dete The.
Jab Kissi Shah Ke Qatil Ko Saza Dete The.
Keh Ke Karte The Har Ik Waar Ali Khush Hoon Gay.
Apna Kirdar Banao Zara Qanbar Ki Tarha.
Maal Ko Choro Karo Ishq Abuzar Ki Tarha.
Phaink Do Darham-o-Dinar Ali Khush Hoon Gay.
Naik Aamal Ki Tum Arsh Pe Shamein Bhejo.
Bas Unhein Majlis-o-Milad Ki Khabrein Bhejo
Parh Ke Ulfat Ke Yeh Akhbar Ali Khush Hoon Gay.
Paish-e-Haider Jo Sar-e-Hashar Main Jaon Ga Wajhi
Apne Mula Ko Yeh Sab Shair Sunaon Ga Wajhi
Sun Keh Saqlain Ke Ashar Ali Khush Hoon Gay.
