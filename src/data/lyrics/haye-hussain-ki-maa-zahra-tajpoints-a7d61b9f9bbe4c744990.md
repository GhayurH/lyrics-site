1
Kafan Se Phailaye Apne Bazu Hussain Ko Maa Bola Rahi Hai
Hain Band Aankhon Se Ashk Jari Pisar Ko Seene Laga Rahi Hai

2
Madine Walo Sakoon Mubarak Na Shor-e-Giriya Boland Ho Ga
Nabi Ko Din Raat Roney Wali Lo Aaj Dunya Se Ja Rahi Hai

3
Galay Se Beta Laga Rahi Hai Magar Hai Chehray Pe Karb Kaisa
Yeh Lag Raha Hai Hussain Se Maa Shikasta Pehlu Chupa Rahi Hai

4
Ali Ki Nazrein Hain Aasmaan Par Andhera Yasrab Mein Phail Jaye
Janaza Zahra Ka Ab Uthay Ga Siyah Shab Barhti Ja Rahi Hai

5
Ajeeb Rukhsat Hai Fatima Ki Na Jane Hai Sham Yaad Aayi
Jo Ro Ke Kulsoom Our Zainab Ke Aaj Gesu Bana Rahi Hai

6
Hain Soney Walon Ke Ashk Jari Solaney Wali Bhi Ro Rahi Hai
Keh Fatima Ke Yateem Bachoon Ko Aaj Fizza Sula Rahi Hai

7
Imam-e-Mehdi Ke Pas Akbar Ossay Hai Pohanchani Yeh Amanat
Jo Fizza Dar Ko Jalanay Wali Har Aik Lakri Utha Rahi Hai
