Ya Syeda Ya Fatima Joan Rizvi Lyrics

1
Madh-e-Zahra Koi Kya Biyaan Kar Sakay.
Kam Yeh Bas Khuda Ki Zabaan Kar Sakay.
Ya Ali Shukriya Iss Atta Ke Liye.
Di Zabaan Fatima Ki Sana Ke Liye.
Itni Si Ma Khalaqtu Ki Tafseer Hai.
Khalq Sab Kuch Howa Fatima Ke Liye.

2
Fatima Ki Kisa Hai Woh Mojaz Numa.
Mangein Jis Ko Nabi Bhi Shifa Ke Liye.
Rakh Di Hum Ne Dar-e-Fatima Par Jabeen.
Dil Mein Khuwaish Thi Arsh-e-Olla Ke Liye.
Tara Utra Tu Munh Utray Keh Kar Kayi.
Fatima Hain Faqat Murtaza Ke Liye.

3
Chor Do Gasibon Ki Tarafdari Ab.
Le Chalo Qabar Mein Kuch Zia Ke Liye.
Wahin Takhleeq Abbas Ki Ho Gayi.
Fatima Jab Uthi Thin Dua Ke Liye.
Unn Ka Romal Hai Mera Rukhsar Hai.
Kya Jaza Hai Yeh Ashk-e-Azza Ke Liye.

4
Dar Pe Zahra Ke Khud Apne Hathoon Shaqi.
Aag Laye They Apni Chita Ke Liye.
Mil Gaya Hur Ko Dehleez-e-Zahra Pe Woh.
Hum Tarastey Hain Jis Naqsh-e-Pa Ke Liye.
Aana Masjid Se Zahra Ke Ghar Tak Asad.
Yeh Bhi Mairaj Thi Mustafa Ke Liye.
