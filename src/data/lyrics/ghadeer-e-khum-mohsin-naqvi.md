Ghadeer e Khum Mohsin Naqvi Lyrics
Woh Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein Azaan Hoi Woh Saji Khiyal Ki Anjuman.
Woh Rukh-e-Shaoor Pe Zoo Pari Woh Barha Hayat Ka Bankpan.
Woh Kali Khatak Se Chatak Gayi Woh Bhatak Bhatak Ke Chali Pawan.
Lab Aarzu Pe Dhanak Pari Khila Khuwaishon Ka Hasen Chaman.
Dil-e-Kehkashan Mein Utar Gayi Woh Rida-e-Dasht Shikan Shikan.
Woh Sawariyon Ke Qadam Rukay, Jhukay Farsh Par Woh Thakay Badan.
Woh Falak Ko Dekh Ke Hans Para Rukh Regzar Ka Bholpan.
Woh Zameen Ke Dil Mein Hai Aarzu Keh Milaye Aankh Zara Gagan.
Soye Murtaza Barhey Mustafa Bamizaj e Rasam-o-Rah-e-Kafan.
Woh Nabi Wasi Se Mila Hai Youn Miley Jaisay Chand Se Ik Kiran.
Woh Ali Ka Hath Boland Hai Kahan Reh Gaye Yeh Zameen Zaman.
Sar-e-Takht-e-Reeg Hai Lab Kusha Woh Ameer-e-Mehfil-e-Panjtan.
Kaha Mustafa Ne Keh Zindagi Meri Baat Aaj Se Aam Kar.
Hai Yehi Ali Mera Jansheen Mere Jansheen Ko Salam Kar.
