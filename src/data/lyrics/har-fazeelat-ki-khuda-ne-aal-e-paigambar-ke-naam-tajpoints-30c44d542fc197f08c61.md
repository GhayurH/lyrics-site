1
Har Fazeelat Ki Khuda Ne Aal e Paigambar Ke Naam.
Jis Pe Utra Tha Sitara Arsh Se Oss Ghar Ke Naam.
Meri Aankhein Kar Rahi Hain Naam-e-Akbar Ka Tawaaf.
Main Areeza Likh Raha Hoon Khaliq-e-Akbar Ke Naam.

2
Yeh Tu Hai Teri Karamat Zulfiqar-e-Haidery.
Warna They Maloom Kis Ko Marhab-o-Antar Ke Naam.
Jab Bhi Quraan Kholta Hoon Aisa Lagta Hai Mujhe.
Sorah-e-Kausar Ho Jaise Saqi-e-Kausar Ke Naam.

3
Ishq-e-Aal-e-Mustafa Ka Dars Dene Ke Liye.
Hashar Tak Zinda Rahein Gaye Buzar-o-Qambar Ke Naam.
Shafa-e-Mehshar Habib-e-Kibriya Sun Lijiye.
Manqabat Ki Sab Azanein Hain Ali Akbar Ke Naam.

4
Qalab-e-Momin Our Dar-e-Janat Pe Hain Likhe Howay.
Mustafa Zahra Ali Shabir Our Shabar Ke Naam.
Aye Muhib Jis Dar Se Milti Hai Mawaddat Ki Zakaat.
Meri Sansein Mere Qirtas-o-Qalam Oss Dar Ke Naam.
