BABA MERA BABA – BABA MERE BABA
SUGHRA KA THA YE NAUHA – BABA AEY MERE BABA

1
HAA’N MUJHKO MADEENE ME AB CHAIN NA AYEGA
JAB AAP NA HONGE TO GHABRAEGA DIL MERA

2
BABA KOI SHIKWA BHI TUM SE NA KARUNGI MAI’N
BAITHA DO AGAR CHAHE BAITHI HAI JAHA’N FIZZA

3
JHOOLA BHI JHULAUNGI, LORI BHI SUNAUNGI
PAHLU ME SULAUNGI ASGHAR KO MERE BABA

4
LAAO ALI ASGHAR KO MAI’N PYAR ZARA KAR LOO’N
PHIR MUJHSE JUDA KAR KE TUM USKO LIYE JANA

5
KAMSIN HO SAFAR KAISE KAR PAOGE TUM BHAYYA
HAYE YE BHALA KAISA UNNISWA DIN AYA

6
BHAYYA ALI AKBAR SE KAH DO KI WATAN AAKAR
BAS MUJH PA TARAS KHA KAR TUM SATH LIYE JANA

7
RUDAD-E-SAFAR LIKH KAR BAS KHATM KARO NAUHA
SUGHRA KE LABO’N PAR THA BABA AEY MERE BABA

8
KAISE YE KAHU’N AKHIR HAI’N LAFZ SHUJA APNE
BIBI KI INAYAT HAI LIKKHA HAI JO YE NAUHA
