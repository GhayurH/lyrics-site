Kuch Na Kissi Se Boleinge.
Tanhai Mein Ro Lein Gay.

1
Hum Be Rah Rawoon Ka Kya.
Sath Kissi Ke Ho Leinge.
Khud Tu Howay Ruswa Lekin.
Tere Bhaid Na Kholein Gay.

2
Jewan Zehar Bhara Sagar.
Kab Tak Amrit Gholein Gay.
Hijar Ki Shab Sone Wale.
Hashar Ko Aankhein Kholein Gay.

3
Phir Koi Aandhi Uthey Gi.
Panchi Jab Par Tolein Gay.
Neend Tu Kya Aye Gi Faraz
Maut Ayi Tu Soo Lein Gay.
