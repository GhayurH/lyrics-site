Ridawaan Leh Gayiyaan Ghazi Te Bhaina Be Rida Ho Gayiyaan
Teri Lash Tu Alma Waliya Veerana Kiwein Juda Ho Gayiyaan
Tu Laran De Layi Paband Sein Jiwein Assi Ron De Layi Paband Veerana
Teri Maqtal Gaah De Haye Wichon Pa Ke Rasiyaan Qaidna Be-Khata Ho Gayiyaan
Jinna Bawaan De Uttay Sanu Maan Si Barey Oh Bawaan Teri Leh Gayiyaan Veerna
Tu Rait Te Ja Ke Sam Gaya Ein Pardey Daraan Be-Aasra Ho Gayiyaa
Teri Lash De Layi Banr Gayiyaan Ne Kafan Karbal Diyaan Jiwein Mityaan Aye Veerna
Zahra Diyaan Jaiyaan De Layi Odh Ke Mitiyaan Aye Rida Ho Gayiyaa
Assi Sham Di Taraf Paidal Na Jandiyaan Jay Deen De Faraz Honday Na Veerna
Tere Gham Wich Ghazi Wekh Lay Rawaan V Farsh-e-Azza Ho Gayiyaan
Akh Khol Ke Jadoon Abid Ne Wekhiya Na Khaimay V Raye Na Chadraan Veerna
Aynu Ghirat Aye Kha Gayiya Marzaan Aydhiyaan La-Dawa Ho Gayiyaan
Teri Maut Di Khabar Ayho Kiga Aye Asar Ghash Kha Ke Dig Payi Kamsin Aye Veerna
Aidhay Cholay Nu Haye Bha Lagi Aasaan Aidhiyaan Parah Parah Ho Gayiyaa
Aynj Karne Ne Guzar Bazaraan De Safar Sang Qatilaan De Haye Rastay Aye Veerna
Pathraan Di Barish Si Wasi Bhiana Teriyaan Zakhmi Zakhmi Ho Gaiyaan
Hath Rasiyaan Wich SAMAR Bhaina Aye Kehndiyaan Tur Payiyaan Sham Wal Das Ghazi Veerna
Teri Turbat Nu Aan Ke Gal Lan Gayiyaan Jaye Reh Ho Gayiyaan
