Hun Aja Veer Na Sonu Monu.
Hunr Aa Ja Veerna Teri Yaad Sataway Sughra Beemar Nu.

1
Nayi Bhulday Veran Pardais Che Ja Ke Bhainra De Pyar Nu.
Veera Main Dukhiya Nu Deway Kaun Dilasay.
Dushman Ne Loki Sab Aasay Pasay.
Payi Roz Udeekaan Tera Wadah Akbar Satwein De Waar Nu.

2
Main Wekhdi Reh Gayi Jadon Qafila Turya.
Sare Karbal Tur Gaye Oh Koi Lainr Na Murya.
Tere Bajoon Veran Rowaan Ujray Gharaan Wich Lag Ke Dewar Nu.
Teri Veer Judai Maithon Sahi Na Jaway.

3
Mangan Roz Duawaan Shala Akbar Aaway.
Ik Wari Aa Ke Veera Shakal Wakha Ja Dukhiya La Char Nu.
Kiwein Toriya Aa Ke Be Deen Sipahiyaan.
Nalay Chadraan Lutiyaan Hath Rasiyaan Paiyaan.

4
Sajjad Muhari Roway Rat De Athro Phar Ke Mohar Nu.
Teri Rah Wich Beh Ke Nit Osiyaan Pawaan.
Kehra Mera Dardi Jinno Haal Sunawaan.
Ki Qismat Meri Main Naeem, Akbar De Tarsan Dedar Nu.
