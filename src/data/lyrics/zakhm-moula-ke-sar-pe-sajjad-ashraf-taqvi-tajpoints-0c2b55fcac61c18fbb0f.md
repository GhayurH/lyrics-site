1
Zakhm Maula Ke Sar Pe Aaya Hai.
Ibn-e-Muljim Ne Zulm Dhaya Hai.
Bandagi Ki Boland Hain Aahein.
Banda Parwar Lahu Nahaya Hai.

2
Hichkiyaan Le Ke Ro Rahi Hai Namaz.
Sajdah Gahon Ne Gham Manaya Hai.
Roshni Pochti Hai Kabay Ki.
Shame Emaan Ko Kyun Bhojaya Hai.

3
Gham Se Shabbar Ka Phat Raha Hai Jigar.
Bar Ummat Magar Uthaya Hai.
Dil Phata Ja Raha Hai Sajday Ka.
Kis Ne Sarwar Ka Dil Dukhaya Hai.

4
Aabdeedah Hain Zainab-o-Kulsoom.
Gham Ka Badil Dilon Pe Chaya Hai.
Mere Maula Tera Jawab Nahi.
Sheer Qatil Ko Bhi Pilaya Hai.

5
Ro Rahe Hain Yateem Kofay Ke.
Kaun Ab Inn Ke Sar Ka Saya Hai.
Ghar Khuda Ka Larz Raha Hai Kaleem.
Ja Raha Hai Jo Ghar Ka Jaya Hai.
