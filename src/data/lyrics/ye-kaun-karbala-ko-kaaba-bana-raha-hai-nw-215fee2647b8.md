Ye Kaun Karbala Ko Kaaba Bana Raha Hai

Tauheed Ko Yeh Kiska Sajda Bacha Raha Hai

Aayat Hai Rasan Main Bikre Hue Hain Paaray
Naizay Pey Aaj Quriyan, Qur’an Sunaraha Hai

Beton Ko Daike Sadqa Aur Behnon Ki Ridaain
Rozay Azal Kiya Jo Waada Nibha Raha Hai

Jiskay Galay Kay Bosay Letay Thay Khud Muhammad
Zalim Usi Galay Pay Khanjar Chala Raha Hai

Maqtal Main Istarha Say Bikra Yateem Qasim
Chun Chun Kay Tukray Maula Laasha Bana Raha Hai

Aagosh Main Jo Maa Ki Sota Tha Pyaara Asghar
Aagosh Main Lahed Ki Baba Sula Raha Hai

Mujkho Tamachay Maray Kanon Say Durr Hain Cheenay
Ab Has Kay Mujhko Zalim Tera Sir Dikha Raha Hai

Jab Tak Rahe Gi Duniya Roshan Rahe Gi Shahid
Shabeer Jo Lahoo Say Sham`may Jala Raha Hai
