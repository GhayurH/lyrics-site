1
Khon Rota Hai Qalam Our Bataon Kaise.
Maut Achi Thi Yeh Abid Ne Kaha Khoon Ro Kar.
Sath Sadaat Ke Bazaron Mein Jaon Kaise.
Sar Barehna Hain Phophi Our Behan Sath Mere.
Chadar-e-Waris-e-Tatheer Urhaon Kaise.
Koi Pathar Liye Aata Hai Koi Teer-o-Tabar.
Hath Hain Mere Bandhe Haye Bachaon Kaise.
Apne Gham Bhool Gayin Zainab-o-Kulsoom Magar.
Sochti Thin Keh Sakina Ko Bachaon Kaise.
Sar-e-Mazloom Nahi Barhta Sakina Gum Hai.
Iss Biyabaan Mein Osse Dhond Ke Laon Kaise.
Sar Barehna Hai Pas-e-Pusht Bandhe Hath Mere.
Our Darbar-e-Yazeedi Hai Main Jaon Kaise.
Maa Issi Shehar Ki Shahzadi Howa Karti Thin.
Qaidi Ban Kar Main Ossi Shehar Main Jaon Kaise.
Jab Sakina Ko Kaneezi Mein Tha Manga Oss Dam.
Guzari Sajjad Pe Kya Joan Bataon Kaise.
