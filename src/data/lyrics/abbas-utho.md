zakhmo se choor choor thay mazloome karbala
khanjar sambhale haath may shimr e layeen badha
baithi thi apne laal ke nazdeek sayyeda
ek baar aayi kano may mazloom ki sada
abbas abbas abbas
rukh suwe alqama kiya karne lagi ye bain
abbas utho tumko sada deta hai hussain
sadmo se tishnagi se dilafgaar hai hussain
zakmo se choor choor hai lachaar hai hussain
kitne sitamgaro may giraftaar hai hussain
teri madad ka talabgaar hai hussain
abbas utho…
sab kho chuka hai ban may saharay mera hussain
noha kuna hai dard ke maaray mera hussain
karta hai zer e lab jo ishaare mera hussain
tujhko nahi to kisko pukaare mera hussain
abbas utho…
sheh ke galay pe shimr ne rakhi juhin churi
wo dekh qaimagaah se zainab nikal padi
aankhein meri hussain ki ashkon se hai bhari
chin jaye na behan ki rida fiqr hai badi
abbas utho…
tanha sitamgaro ne isay ghayr liya hai
jee jaisa jiska chaah sitam usne kiya hai
teero se patharon se isay zakhmi kiya hai
ab jaanibe hussain sitamgaar badha hai
abbas utho…
tere supurd karke gaye thay jisay ali
kya yaad wo wassiyate aakhir nahi rahi
maqtal hai ye hussain ka aur qatl ki ghadi
sunkar fughaan hussain ki kehne lagay ali
abbas utho…
khanjar sambhale haath may hai shimre bad sitaar
karti hai bayn maut buka karti hai hayaat
ek din may lutgayi mere bache ki kayenaat
dekhe suye qayaam kabhi jaanibe furaat
abbas utho…
is haal may bhi shukre khuda karta hai hussain
khamosh rehke zulm o sitam sehta hai hussain
teri wafa ka tujhko sila deta hai hussain
is bekasi may tumko sada deta hai hussain
abbas utho…
farhaan o mazhar abdi kaisa wo khwaab tha
ashoor ka wo roz tha hum aur karbala
pahunchay paye ziyarate abbas e bawafa
kano may aayi ek sisakti hui sada
abbas utho…
