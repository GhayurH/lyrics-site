Almaan Walay Mere Almaan Walay, Almaan Walay Mere Mashkaan Walay.
Shah-e-Wafa Hai Sunta Sada Hai Karta Atta Hai Oh Mera Peer Ghazi.

1
Mera Peer Ghazi Mera Peer Ghazi Maula Wafa Ka Oh Mera Peer Ghazi.
Dunya Mein Jis Ka Parcham Yeh Sohna Ghar Ghar Saja Hai Oh Mera Peer Ghazi.
Umm-Ul-Baneen Ki Aankhon Ka Tara Jab Maula Ghazi Dharti Pe Aaya.
Oss Maa Ka Rutba Kitna Hai Aala Haan Jis Ko Mila Hai Oh Mera Peer Ghazi.

2
Ghazi Ka Rutba Hum Kya Bataein Zahra Ne Jis Ko Dein Hain Duaein.
Haider Ne Jis Ke Hain Naaz Uthaye Haan Aisay Pala Hai Oh Mera Peer Ghazi.
Baba Bhi Maula Bhai Bhi Maula Dono Ka Pyara Ghazi Bhi Maula.
Har Dar Pe Jis Ki Hai Badshahi Haan Woh Badshah Hai Oh Mera Peer Ghazi.

3
Naizay Pe Aaya Jab Maula Ghazi Behno Ke Sar Par Chadar Nahi Thi.
Sare Safar Mein Rahein Band Aankhein Haan Rota Raha Hai Oh Mera Peer Ghazi.
Bholein Gay Hum Yeh Manzar Kabhi Na Roti Tarapti Dar Par Sakina.
Pyasi Sakina Tere Gham Mein Chacha Haan Pyasa Gaya Hai Our Mera Peer Ghazi.

4
 Jawad -o- Sarwar Ne Sach Kaha Hai Sab Kuch Wafa Hai Sab Kuch Wafa Hai.
Aoulad-e-Zahra Se Pyar Karna Haan Samjha Gaya Hai Oh Mera Peer Ghazi.
Molaiyon Ka Har Dam Hai Narah Panjtan Ka Pyara Jiye Mashkaan Wala.
Suchay Dilon Ka Sucha Sahara Jiye Ghazi Maula Jiye Almaan Wala.
