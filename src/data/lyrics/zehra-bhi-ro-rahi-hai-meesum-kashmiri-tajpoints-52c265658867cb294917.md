Zehra Bhi Ro Rahi Hai Meesum Kashmiri.

1
Zahra Bhi Ro Rahi Hai Zainab Bhi Ro Rahi Hai.
Maa Ki Musibaton Par Beti Tarap Uthi Hai.
Rukhsat Kiya Hai Maa Ko Zainab Ke Aansoon Ne.
Darbar Mein Jo Zahra Haq Mangney Gayi Hai.
Girtay Hoye Hai Dekha Madar Pe Jaltay Dar Ko.
Zainab Hai Kamsini Hai Ghurbat Hai Bekasi Hai.

2
Hai Pehlu-e-Shikasta Par Hath Fatima Ka.
Our Fatima Ki Dukhtar Dil Tham Kar Khari Hai.
Zahra Ke Zakhm-e-Pehlu Ko Dekh Kar Jo Ubhari.
Zainab Ne Murtaza Ki Woh Cheekh Bhi Suni Hai.
Yasrab Ta Sham-o-Kofa Zulm-o-Sitam Ki Zad Par.
Ya Dukhtar-e-Nabi Hai Ya Dukhtar-e-Ali Hai.

3
Tameer Jald Roza Ho Jaye Syeda Ka.
Ghaibat Se Aaein Maula Hasrat Meri Yehi Hai.
Hai Dard Naak Manzar Halqoom-e-Sheh Pe Khanjar.
Madar Bhi Dekhti Hai Khwahar Bhi Dekhti Hai.
 Izhar Bas Taraptay Dekha Hai Fatima Ko.
Zainab Ki Zindagi Bhi Kitni Dukhon Bhari Hai.
