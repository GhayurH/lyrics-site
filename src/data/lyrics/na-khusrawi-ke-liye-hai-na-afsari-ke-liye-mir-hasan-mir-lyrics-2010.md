Na Khusrawi Ke Liye Hai Na Afsari Ke Liye Mir Hasan Mir
Zaban Waqf Hai Bas Madh-e-Askari Ke Liye

1
Agar Na Milti Unhein Noor-e-Askari Ki Zakat
Tarastey Chand Sitarey Bhi Roshni Ke Liye
Giraya Aap Ka Roza Jinhoon Ne Aye Maula
Shikar Pehla Banein Gay Woh Aakhri Ke Liye

2
Imam Phir Se Bulaein Mujhe Bhi Samarra
Taras Rahi Hai Jabeen Kab Se Bandagi Ke Liye
Mere Imam Ka Humsar Woh Kaisay Latay Bhala
Mila Na Jab Koi Qanbar Ki Humsari Ke Liye

3
Yeh Bolay Daar Pe Meesum Main Kaisay Ruk Jaon
Zaban Di Hai Mujhe Jab Ali Ali Ke Liye
Ali Ko Dekh Ke Baleen Pe Main Pukaron Ga
Aye Meri Maut Thehar Ja Tu Do Ghari Ke Liye

4
Mujhe Hai Fakhar Keh Main Unn Ke Dar Ka Nokar Hoon
Farishtay Khud Jahan Aate Hain Nokari Ke Liye
Nabi Ki Aal Se Takrao Kya Zarori Hai
Tareqay Our Bhi Raij Hain Khudkushi Ke Liye

5
Alam Bara-e-Sahaba Tha Jang-e-Khaybar Mein
Alif Se Sab Ke Liye Ain Se Ali Ke Liye
Ab Iss Se Bhar Ke Sharf Aadmi Ka Kya Ho Ga?
Ruki Hoi Hai Qayamat Ik Aadmi Ke Liye
