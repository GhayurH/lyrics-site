1
Beh Raha Hai Khoon Ka Darya Hasan Sadiq.
Beh Raha Hai Khoon Ka Darya Lasha-e-Shabeer Se.
Pani Pani Ki Sada Aati Hai Zakhm-e-Teer Se.
Lasha-e-Akbar Para Hai Khon Mein Doba Howa.
Keh Do Wapis Laut Jaein Qasid-e-Humsheer Se.
Karbala Ke Dasht Mein Tha Jurm Pani Mangna.
Ho Gaye Khamosh Asghar Hurmala Ke Teer Se.
Jis Ke Bosay Le Ke Milta Tha Muhammad Ko Sakoon.
Woh Gulu Kata Gaya Hai Aaj Kund Shamsheer Se.
Sar Barehna Ho Gayi Hain Lut Gayi Hain Chadrein.
Jin Ko Dhanpa Tha Nabi Ne Chadar-e-Tatheer Se.
Sham Mein Jao Gi Nangay Sar Mere Marne Ke Baad.
Ho Ke Rukhsat Keh Rahe They Shah-e-Dein Humsheer Se.
Aaye Hain Ahl-e-Haram Gulzar Kyun Darbar Mein.
Abid-e-Beemar Hain Jakrey Hoye Zanjeer Se.
