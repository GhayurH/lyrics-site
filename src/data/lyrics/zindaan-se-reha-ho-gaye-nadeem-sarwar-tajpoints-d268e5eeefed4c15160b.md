1
Zindaan Se Reha Ho Gaye Nadeem Sarwar.
ALLAH Mere ALLAH Zindaan Se Raha Ho Gaye Sadaat.
Zindaan Se Raha Ho Gaye Sadaat.
ALLAH Yeh Kya Ho Gaye Sadaat.
Khud Karb-o-Bala Ho Gaye Sadaat.
Yeh Kaisi Rehai Hai Ke Dil Khush Nahi Hota.
Kya Reh Gaya Zindaan Mein Keh Har Qaidi Hai Rota.
Haan Apni Sakina Se Juda Hotey Hain Sadaat.
Aye Shamiyo Ab Chain Se Aaram Se Sona.
Be Chain Karey Ga Na Kissi Bachi Ka Rona.
Jo Shab Ko Boka Karti Thi Roye Gi Kabhi Na.
Zainab Ne Kaha Qaid Mein Hai Mera Khazana.
Aye Aurton Jab Sham Dhalay Iss Taraf Aana.
Turbat Pe Sakina Ki Bhi Aaye They Nana.
Sajjad Ne Zewar Jo Aseeri Ke Utaray.
Sokhay Hoye Sab Zakhm Harey Ho Gaye Sare.
Sar Dekh Ke Baba Ka Tarap Kar Woh Pukarey.
Zainab Ne Rida Pa Ke Kaha Shukar-e-Khuda Hai.
Abbas Mere Bhai Magar Sadma Bara Hai.
Sar Nangay Humein Sara Jahan Dekh Chuka Hai.
Kulsoom Jo Ik Lafz Abhi Tak Na Thi Boli.
Jis Waqt Rasan Bazu Se Sajjad Ne Kholi.
Kehne Lagi Kya Jaye Watan Khali Hai Jholi.
Yeh Qafila Jab Karb-o-Bala Laut Ke Aaya.
Dekha Keh Para Dhop Mein Hai Bhai Ka Lasha.
Zainab Ne Sar-e-Khak-e-Bala Khud Ko Giraya.
Kya Shan Thi Sadaat Ki Jab Ghar Se Chaley They.
Mehmal Thi Amari Bhi Thi Mashkezay Bharay They.
Abbas-e-Alamdar Alam Kholay Hoye They.
Galiyon Mein Madine Ki Ajab Shoor Bapa Hai.
Sughra Se Abhi Aa Ke Kissi Ne Yeh Kaha Hai.
Aa Sughra Tera Baba Watan Laut Raha Hai.
Rozay Pe Muhammad Ke Jo Syedaniyaan Pohanchin.
Aye:SARWAR-o-REHAN: Nigahein Bhi Jhuki Thin.
Dikhla Ke Nishaan Rasi Ke Zainab Yeh Pukari.
