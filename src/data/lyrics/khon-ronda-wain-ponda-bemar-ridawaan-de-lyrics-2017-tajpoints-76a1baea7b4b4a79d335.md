Khon Ronda Wain Ponda Bemar Ridawaan De Write Up

1
Khon Ronda Wain Ponda Bemar Ridawaan De.
Har Moor Te Putar Hussain Da Mangda Burqay ManwaanDe.
Patharaan Diyaan Barshaan Har Moor Te Hondiyaan Zahra Diyaan Jaiyaan Rondiyaan.
Kofe Wich Badal De Wekhe Ne Zainab Matlab Chawaan De.
Rakhiyaan Ne Izataan Esai Bastiyaan Oh Shehar Si Kalma Gowaan De.
Jinein Okhe Shehar Guzarey Ne Zainab Sham De Rawaan De.

2
O Yasrab Shehar Si Veraan Da Maan Si Aye Sham Aye Jonda Nayi Koi.
Kis Hal Ich Safar Te Ayi Aye Zainab Naal Bhirawaan De.
Ghazi Di Lash Te Aj Osse Sain De Rasiyaan Wich Bazu Qaid Ne.
Jeri Zainab Sadqay Landi Rayi Ghazi Diyaan Banwaan De.
 Akbar Sab Bibiyaan San Duray Khandiyaan Ik Dojay Nu San Kaindiyaan.
Saday Jondiyaan Putar Sajjad Nu Na Aye Shimar Sazawaan De.
