Islam Ki Tarveej Ka Unwan Khadija Rehan Azmi.
Har Sahib-e-Eman Ka Eman Khadija.

1
Arman e Khadija They Nabi Hum Ne Yeh Mana.
Paygamber-e-Aakhir Ka Thin Arman Khadija.
Khatoon-e-Jinna Jis Ki Hai Beti Yeh Wohi Hain.
Qurbaan Teri Azmat Pe Dil-o-Jan Khadija.

2
Islam Hai Ahsan Musalmano Pe Rab Ka.
Islam Pe Hai Aap Ka Ahsan Khadija.
Gar Deen Ki Tableeg Mein Zar Tera Na Hota.
Yeh Deen Na Charhta Kabhi Parwan Khadija.

3
Yeh Majlis-e-Shabbir Hai Janat Ki Basharat.
Jari Hai Tera Aaj Bhi Faizan Khadija.
Tauseef Teri Ho Nahi Sakti Hai Bashar Se.
Likh Walay Agar Lakh Bhi Dewan Khadija.

4
Dukhtar Hai Teri Chadar-e-Tatheer Ki Malik.
Shohar Hai Tera Bolta Quran Khadija.
Munkir Teri Azmat Ka Agar Koi Bashar Hai.
Bakhshish Ka Nahi Phir Koi Imkan Khadija.

5
Kehne Ko Tu Azwaj e Muhammad Hain Kayi Our.
Hai Sab Se Juda Aap Hi Ki Shan Khadija.
 Rehan Sana Madar-e-Zahra Ki Raqam Kar.
Yeh Jan Ke Bakhshish Ka Hain Saman Khadija.
