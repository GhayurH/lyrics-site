1
Zainab Se Pehle Zahra Darbar Mein Gayi.
Ummat Ne Kahan La Ke Khara Kar Diya Mujhe, Beti Rasool Ki Yeh Sochti Rahi.
Hasnain Shareefain Bhi Rotay Rahe Kharey.
Darbar Mein Maa Chunti Rahi Sand Ke Tukray Sab Loag Hans Deiye.
Zahra Ki Gasibon Ne Tazeem Khoob Ki.
Haye Bad-e-Nabi, Zahra Ko Roney Nahi Diya.
Jaltay Hoye Darwazay Se Pehlu Kiya Zakhmi Ummat Ne Kya Kiya.
Kaisi Batool Pe Haye Aayi Hai Be Basi.
Yaran e Muhammad Ne Hai Zahra Ko Dukh Diya.
Loota Hai Musalmano Ne Haq Aal-e-Abba Ka Yaad Aayi Karbala.
Apne Pidar Ko Logo Beti Na Ro Saki.
Jibreel Bhi Jis Bibi Ke Dar Ka Khiyat Hai.
Mazloma-o-Mehroma Ke Shohar Ke Hath Mein Sab Kainat Hai.
Oss Ne Sitam Bhi Jhailay Par Bad Dua Na Di.
Jo Bibi Mubahila Mein Gayi Lauti Shan Se.
Ghairon Ne Ossay Beti Kaha Sar Bhi Tha Dhanpa Jageer Bhi Di Thi.
Ummat Ne Woh Bhi Akhtar Jageer Loot Li.
