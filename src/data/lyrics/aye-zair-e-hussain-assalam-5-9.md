Aye Zair-e-Hussain Assalam 5-9 Lyrics

1
Hajj Ke Beghair Hajj Ka Fareza Ada Kiya
Mosam Ki Koi Fikar Na Khoof-e-Qaza Kiya
Chalon Se Apne Marham-e-Zakhm-e-Ana Kiya
Tu Ne Payada Pa Safar-e-Karbala Kiya
Qatrey Tere Paseeney Ke Kya Kam Aaye Hain
ALLAH Ne Unhi Se Farishtay Banaye Hain

2
Kis Tarha Se Biyan Ho Manzar Woh Ishq Ke
Paidal Jo Karbala Ko Nikaltey Hain Qafilay
Koi Najaf Se Our Koi Basra Ki Rah Se
Hathoon Mein Ya Hussain Ke Parcham Liye Hoye
Zair Jo Karbala Ki Taraf Dorey Jate Hain
Abbas Chal Ke Khud Unhein Lainay Ko Aate Hain

3
Hoti Hai Ik Qatar-e-Mokib Wahan Saji
Khidmat Mein Zaireen Ki Rehta Hai Har Koi
Pani Liye Koi Tu Koi Ban Ke Malshi
Unn Khadmo Ke Ishq Pe Kehta Hai Dil Yehi
Kartay Hain Saaf Aa Ke Paseena Jaben Se
Milne Hussain Aate Hain Jab Khadmain Se

4
Dulhan Ki Tarha Rah Sajatay Hain Ashiqain
Khaimay Muhabbaton Ke Lagatay Hain Ashiqain
Jo Saal Bhar Mein Rizq Kamatay Hain Ashiqain
Dolat Woh Zairon Pe Lotatay Hain Ashiqain
Kehte Hain Phir Bhi Qalb Hamarey Na Bhar Sakay
Hum Zair-e-Hussain Ki Khidmat Na Kar Sakay

5
Jo Rastay Mein Karb-o-Bala Ke Guzar Gaye
Kaisay Kahon Ke:MIR TAKALLUM: Woh Mar Gaye
Pardais Mein They Laut Ke Woh Apne Ghar Gaye
Aaye Ajal Bataon Woh Zair Kidhar Gaye
Dar-e-Fana Se Mulk-e-Baqa Le Gaye Hussain
Khud Aa Ke Unn Ko Karb-o-Bala Le Gaye Hussain
