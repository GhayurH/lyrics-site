Dhoondthi Phirthi Hai Mader Hai Mera Asghar Kahan

Tera Jhoola Jal Gaya Aey Mere Tishna Dahaan
Dhoondthi Phirthi Hai Mader Hai Mera Asghar Kahan

Ub Jioun Kis Kay Saharay Paas Mere Kya Raha

Dhoondthi Phirthi Hai Mader Hai Mera Asghar Kahan

Dhoondthi Phirthi Hai Mader Hai Mera Asghar Kahan

Dhoondthi Phirthi Hai Mader Hai Mera Asghar Kahan

Dhoondthi Phirthi Hai Mader Hai Mera Asghar Kahan
