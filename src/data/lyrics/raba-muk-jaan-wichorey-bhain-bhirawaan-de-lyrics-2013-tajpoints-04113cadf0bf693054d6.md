Raba Muk Jaan Wichorey Bhain Bhirawaan De Asghar Khan
Watna Wal Phera Paway Jino Toriya Si Sughra Ne Naal Duawaan De.

1
Raat Uttay Aye Veer Ro Ro Ke Din Langda Aye Wich Udeekaan De.
Rab Janrey Meel Kadon Honrey Tere Bajoon Bhainr Ujari Was Pay Gayi Rahwaan De.
Tere Naa De Suneray De De Ke Pakhwaan Nu Main Bhaijdi Rayi Aan.
Akbar Nu Aakho Mil Jaway Din Aakhri Aa Gaye Ne Hunr Meriyaan Sanwaan De.

2
Khuwab Takiya Aye Beriyaan Pa Ke Veer Sajjad Turda Janda Aye.
Rab Khair Karey Sehrawan Wich Quran De Waris Ne Muhtaj Ridawaan De.
Roz Sukhna De Deway Balaan Main Nana Tere Udas Rozay Te.
Mainu Aan Saheliyan Aakhan Sughra Kadon Muknrey Ne Dukh Dard Bhirawaan De.

3
Naal Ambari Te Naal Bhaina De Shala Akbar Jawaniyaan Marein.
Safraan Wich Hunr Naseeb Tainu Saye Chan Veran Way Babay Diyaan Chawaan De.
Pak Bibi Di Noor Akhiyaan Da Ro Ro Ke Haye Mukda Jaway Samar.
Veraan De Dard Wichoray Wich Beemar Te Hoye Ne Aynj Asar Jafawan De.
