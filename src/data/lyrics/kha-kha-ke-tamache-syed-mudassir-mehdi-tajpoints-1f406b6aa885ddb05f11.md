1
Kha Kha Ke Tamache Syed Mudassir Mehdi
Kha Kha Tamachay Jo Zameen Par Main Giri Thi.
Yeh Teri Sakina Pe Qayamat Ki Ghari Thi.
Zalim Ki Hatheli Merey Chehray Se Bari Thi.
Yeh Teri Sakina Pe Qayamat Ki Ghari Thi.
Yeh Haal Tumhein Kaisay Sunaon Mere Baba.
Mara Tha Sitamgar Ne Mujhe Aisa Tamacha.
Angothi Laeen Ki Merey Chehray Mein Gari Thi.
Yeh Teri Sakina Pe Qayamat Ki Ghari Thi.
Main Kehti Thi Bachi Hoon Taras Kha Aye Sitamgar.
Hans Hans Ke Laeen Mujh Ko Dikhata Raha Khanjar.
Kya Aisi Khata Mujh Se Mere Baba Hoi Thi.
Yeh Teri Sakina Pe Qayamat Ki Ghari Thi.
Baba Yeh Sakina Pe Teri Zulm-o-Sitam Tha.
Rukhsar Mere Lal They Chehray Pe Waram Tha.
Jari Tha Lahu Kano Se, Marne Ki Kami Thi.
Yeh Teri Sakina Pe Qayamat Ki Ghari Thi.
Chalti Thi Churi Dil Pe Batatay Hoye Tum Ko.
Ro Ro Ke Sada Deti Thi Ammo Chaley Aao.
Jis Waqt Sakina Teri Sholon Mein Ghiri Thi.
Yeh Teri Sakina Pe Qayamat Ki Ghari Thi.
Gardan Ki Taraf Meri Zara Dekho Tu Baba.
Tum Sochtay Hoon Gay Yeh Bhala Neel Hai Kaisa.
Rasi Meri Gardan Mein Sitamgar Ne Kasi Thi.
Yeh Teri Sakina Pe Qayamat Ki Ghari Thi.
Darbar Mein Oss Waqt Bapa Shoor-o-Fogaan Tha.
Sar Peet Ke Sab Roney Lagin Bibiyaan Baba.
Sar Tasht Mein Tha Aap Ka, Zalim Ki Chari Thi.
Yeh Teri Sakina Pe Qayamat Ki Ghari Thi.
Bazar Mein Darbar Mein Rotay Hoye Baba.
Pasli Ke Jo Main Dard Ka Karne Lagi Shikwa.
Dadi Ne Meri Aa Ke Tasalli Mujhe Di Thi.
Yeh Teri Sakina Pe Qayamat Ki Ghari Thi.
Aye Baba Suno Mera Musayab Bhara Noha.
Zalim Ne Kaneezi Mein Sakina Ko Jo Manga.
Sajjad Pe Yeh Sun Ke Ajab Tari Gashi Thi.
Yeh Teri Sakina Pe Qayamat Ki Ghari Thi.
Bachi Yeh Jary Baap Se Kehti Rahi Paihum.
Le Jaiye Zindan Se Ghutta Hai Mera Dam.
Honton Pe Yateema Ke Sada Zeen Yehi Thi.
Yeh Teri Sakina Pe Qayamat Ki Ghari Thi.
