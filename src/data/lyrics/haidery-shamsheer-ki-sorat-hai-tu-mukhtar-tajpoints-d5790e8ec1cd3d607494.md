1
Haidery Shamsheer Ki Sorat Hai Tu Mukhtar.
Har Door Mein Momin Ki Zarorat Hai Tu Mukhtar.
Zulm-o-Sitam Ka Door Yeh Phir Se Hai Aa Gaya.
Aa Jaye Ke Iss Sitam Ki Halakat Hai Tu Mukhtar.

2
Aal-e-Nabi Ka Badla Chukaya Tha Marhaba.
Mera Hai Ishq Meri Muhabbat Hai Tu Mukhtar.
Aal-e-Nabi Ke Ghar Mein Khushi Ka Samaa Hai Aaj.
Inn Ke Liye Le Aaya Yeh Saat Hai Tu Mukhtar.

3
Izzat Ke Sath Takht-o-Hakomat Mile Tumhein.
Karta Jo Panjtan Ki Hamayat Hai Tu Mukhtar.
 Farman Naam Aap Ka Leta Hai Shoq Se.
Iss Ke Zehan Our Qalab Ki Taqat Hai Tu Mukhtar.
