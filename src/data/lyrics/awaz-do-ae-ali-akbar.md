Jati Hai Meri Jaan, Maqtal Mein Hai Dhuwaan, Beta Ho Tum Kahan
Mara Hai Kis Tarha Se Tumhein Sochta Hoon Main
Pathar Hata Hata Ke Tumhein Dhondta Hoon Main
Charon Taraf Hai Khoon, Milta Nahi Sakoon, Lashon Ke Darmiyaan
Kuch Bhi Dikhai Deta Nahi Main Ghareeb Hoon
Khushbo Se Lag Raha Hai Tumhare Qareeb Hoon
Aye Mere Dil Ke Chain, Baba Ke Noor-e-Ain, Ab Lo Na Imtehaan
Main Ne Suna Hai Ghorey Se Tum Khud Girey Nahi
Aye Mere Lal Baloon Se Khaincha Gaya Tumhein
Tum Ho Yahin Kahin, Kyun Boltay Nahi, Khamosh Hai Zabaan
Kaisay Girey Ho Mujh Ko Batao Zeen Se
Lagta Hai Tum Ne Khayi Hai Barchi Laeen Se
Chehra Bhi Zard Hai, Seene Mein Dard Hai, Kuch Tu Karo Biyaan
Maqtal Mein Aate Aate Bhi Mara Gaya Mujhe
Doba Howa Hoon Khon Mein Pathar Ke Zakhm Se
Zakhmi Hai Yeh Jigar, Dekho Mere Pisar, Chehray Pe Hain Nishaan
Himmat Karo Zara Si Kalejay Ko Tham Kar
Hai Waqt-e-Asar Jago Mere Nojawaan Pisar
Laila Ki Aas Hai, Zainab Udas Hai, Do Aakhri Aazaan
Tum Khud Ali Ho Beta Zara Hosla Karo
Barhney Laga Hai Dard Tu Nad-e-Ali Parho
Aankhein Hain Meri Nam, Sughra Ki Hai Qasam, Ragro Na Ayeriyaan
Do Maon Ne Jawan Kiya Tum Ko Paal Kar
Khaimon Mein Ja Ke Kaisay Milaon Ga Main Nazar
Main Unn Se Kya Kahon, Behta Hai Tera Khoon, Kaisay Sahey Gi Maa
Azlan Ro Ke Kehta Raha Yeh Mera Imam
Beta Hamarey Haal Pe Hansti Hai Foaj-e-Sham
Dhundla Hai Sab Jahan, Hai Surkh Aasmaan, Chalti Hain Aandhiyaan
