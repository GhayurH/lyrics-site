Ghazy ko bachpane me maa ye sabak parhaye
Tere bazuo ke hote,  Zainab na ghum uthaye

1
1) Haider ki zawjiyat me jis din se hu mein aayi
Unke labo ke mene bas ye duwa hai paayi
Ya rab wo laal de jo Zainab ke kaam aaye.

2
2) Maana ke tu banega salaar-o-meer lashkar
Lekin hussain ka tu bankar rahenga nokar
Itna khayal rakhna sar maa ka jhuk na jaaye.

3
3) Aka hussain ko tum bhayi kabhi naa kehna
Jab tak hain saan�s baaqi bankar gulam rehna
Meri dua hai tumko sakaayi raas aaye.

4
4) Mein hu kaneez-e-Zahra Fizza se phir bhi kam hu
Ye soch soch kar me bas mubtilaa-e-gham hu
Tere sabab se beta zehra na ruth jaaye.

5
5) Ye ghar hai sayyeda ka is ghar ki shaan parda
Is ghar ki betiyo ko suraj na dekh paaya
Shahzadiyo sar tak teri nazar naa jaaye.

6
6) Ab karbala ka manzar raavi ne yun likha hai
Abbas foj-e-sheh ka salaar bangaya hai
Kiski majaal hai jo Sheh se nazar milaaye.

7
7) Ashur ko hai tooti pyaaso pe kya qayamat
Larne ke milna paayi jarrar ko ijaazat
Abbas ja rahe hai mashko alam uthaaye.

8
8) Ghazi ke raaste par nazre hai bibion ki
Aur hai dua musalsal zainab ke ye labon ki
Meri rida ka zamin ay kaash lawt aaye.

9
9) Rehan Azmi jab be dast ho ke Ghazi
Maqtal me girgaya tha reyti pe Karbala ki
Madar ki wo nasihat Ghazi ko yaad aaye.
