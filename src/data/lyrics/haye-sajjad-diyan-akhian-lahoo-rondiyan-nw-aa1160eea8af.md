Haye sajjad diyan akhian lahoo rondiyan raiyaan

Zahra diyan jaiyan tay jadon pathraan diyan haye barishan paiyaan

Abbas teray baaj teray piyoo dian jaiyaan

Baykafan bhirawaan diyan haye lashaan ton hin rondiyan gayiaan

Shabbir di gardan tay jo chaliaan teraan zarbaan

Onaan ton kitay bhari Ali Abid Nay haye sakhtiaan sahiyaan

Sajjad tay ik raat ch cha gayee ay zaeefi

Beemar raya takda jadon phupian di haye chadran layiaan

Zainab nay rasan bandh k o farz nibhaya

Akhtar jo faraz poora is deen layee karna si nabiaan
