Yay mayra Aghosh madar chornay ka wakt hay

-ma mujay lori na day/ ab jagnay ka wakt hay

-Ay musalmano zara/ bachay ko deako ghor say

Kya Ali/ Asghar key garden katnay ka wakt hay

-Behgaey jab mashk’ tho/ Abbas nay khud say kaha

Ab kahan/ kehmon may wapis luwt nay ka wakt hay

-Jaanthi hoon main thujhay/ sotha nahi kuch dayr

taak

Yay tayray/ soonay meray ab jagnay ka wakt hay

-Halkaay matham may aa/ pohnchi jo ma baysheer key

Manglo/ akbar kay yay /ab mangnay ka wakt hay
