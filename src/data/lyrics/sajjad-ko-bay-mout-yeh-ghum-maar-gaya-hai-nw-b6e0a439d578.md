Sajjad Ko Bay Mout Yeh Ghum Maar Gaya Hai

Bay Parda Haram Saath Hain  Aur Shaam Chala Hai

Naa Maar Sakina Ko Tamachay Ay Sittamgar
Ehsaas E Yateemi He Bari Sakht Saaza Hai

Ay Shimar E Laien Kis Pay Tu Barsata Hai Kooray
Abid Tou Bari Dair Say Bay Hoosh Parha Hai

Looh Agai Zindan Say Rayha Ho Kay Sakina
Sajjad Kay Seenay Say Jo Aik Laasha Laga Hai

Shikwa Nahi Zindan Say Koi Bint E Ali Ko
Kia Kum Hai Kay Deewaron Nay Parda Tou Kiya Hai

Sir Ghazi Ka Tharra Kay Girra Nook E Senna Say
Shaiyad Sir E Zainab Ko Khula Dekh Liya Hai

Kehtin Thi Yeh Sughra Huwa Kia Karbobala Main
Qasid Teray Daman Pay Lahoo Kis Ka Laga Hai

Maqtal Say Chali Aati Hai Zahra Ki Sadain
Ya Rab Yeh Kahan Ka Ka’afla Salar Chala Hai

Bhayta Hay Shab O Rooz Lahoo Ankhon Ab Tak
Sajjad Nay Tasheer Ka Wooh Zehar Piya Hai

Zanjeeron Ki Aawaz Ubharti Rahi Shabar
Is Qaidi Ko Kia Roog Hai Kioun Jaag Raha Hai

Darbare E Jafakar May Youn Aatin Hain Zainab
Chehray Pay Lahoo Aun O Mauhammed Ka Malla Hai
