Haye Sajjad Ne Ghum, Kaise Uthaaye Hongay

Log Bazar Main, Zainab Ko Jo Laaye Hongay

Yaad Abbas Ki Zainab, Ko Satati Hogi

Log Khayam Ko, Jab Lootnay Aaye Hongay

Chor Kar Kufa Ko Haye She,r-e Nabi Main Haider

Paak Sajjad Ke, Taboot Pe Aaye Hongay

Jab Nabi Zaadiyan Darba,ar Main Sar Nangay Gayi

Khoon Beemar Ki, Aankhon Ne Bahaye Hongay

Bheer Bhi Aam Thi Haaye Sar Pe, Ridaen Bhi Na Thi

Kaise Namoos Ne, Phir Chehre Chupaaye Hongay
