1
Imam-e-Zamana Ki Nazar.
Hassan Ke Lal Kuch To Hosla Do.
Khabar Aane Ki Hum Sab Ko Suna Do.
Utha Do Ab Tu Parda Apne Rukh Se.
Mere Mula Zara Darshan Kara Do.
Zamana Thak Giya Hai Rah Takte.
Isse Tum Aa Ke Thora Aasra Do.
Koi Sunta Nahi Faryad Aaqa.
Kidhar Jaein Humein Kuch Tu Bata Do.
Sakina Iss Liye Piyasi Khari Hai.
Keh Pehle Hurmala Ko Tum Saza Do.
Abhi Abid Ki Aankhein Khoon Mein Tar Hain.
Tum Iss Bemar Ko Ab Tu Dawa Do.
Abhi Syedanio Ke Sar Hain Zakhmi.
Tum Un Par Aa Ke Marham Hi Laga Do.
Sada Matam Ki Aati Hai Falak Se.
Hamari Bhi Sada Oss Mein Mila Do.
Humein Bhi Chadar-e-Zainab Ka Gham Hai.
Hamare Sar Pe Zahra Ki Rida Do.
Abhi Ghazi Ke Hain Maqroz Mula.
Alam Ghar Ghar Pe Ab Oss Ka Laga Do.
Bacha Lo Ghar Mera Kubra Ke Sadqay.
Meri Ujri Hoi Dunya Basa Do.
 Batool Ab Thak Gayi Joor-o-Alam Se.
Isse Zahra Ke Sadqay Mein Dua Do.
Poetess::Fakhira Batool Naqvi:
