1
Suno Ahl-e-Watan Hum Pe Inayat Hai Yeh Malik Ki.
Humein Chodah Ke Sadqay Mein Mili Chodah Ko Aazadi.
Muhammad Ali Ko Hum Logo Karein Aao Salam Mil Kar.
Diya Rehne Ko Ghar Oss Ne Yeh oss Ki Hai Meharbani.

2
Sabz Parcham Pe Aye Logo Yeh Jo Hai Chand Our Tara.
Karein Iss Pe Fida Janein Watan Ki Hai Yeh Nishani.
Qaid-e-Azam Ki Mehnat Ka Yehi Rang Hai Suno Logo.
Khushi Se Jee Rahe Hain Hum Yeh Oss Ne Ki Hai Aasani.

3
Watan Pe De Ke Qurbani Shaheedon Ka Lahu Bola.
Yeh Mati Dais Ki Logo Humein Hai Jan Se Pyari.
Watan Se Hai Muhabbat Yeh Keh Likha Hai Nazam Main Ne.
Mili Farman Lafzoon Ki Muhabbat Se Yeh Rawani.
