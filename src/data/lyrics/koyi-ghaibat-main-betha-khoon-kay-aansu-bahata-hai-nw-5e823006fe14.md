Koyi Ghaibat Main Betha Khoon Kay Aansu Bahata Hai

Ussay Zainab Ka Jab  Bazaar Jaana Yaad Aata Hai.

Koyi Ghumkhwar Ghaibat Main Mayyasar Hee Nahi Usko
Suttan Ghaayee Main Haar  Noha Woh Khud Ko Hee Sunata Hai.

Un Hee Galiyon Main Chal Chal Kaar Jahan Chalti Rayhi Zainab
Nishaan Zainab Kay Qadmo Kay Woh Haathon Say Milata Hai.

Baqiye Main Sada-e-ay Wah Hussaina Goonjh Uthti Hai
Woh Jab Bhi Qabr-e-zahra  Aakay Seenay Say Lagata Hai.

Nayhi Thamgay Hain Uski Aankhon Say Aansu Kisi Soorat
Ussay Baysheer Ka  Jab Muskurana Yaad Aata Hai.

Pidar Ki Godh Ki Khaatir Sakina Jab Bhi Roti Hai
Woh Loree Baap Kay  Lehje Main Beti Ko Sunata Hai.

Khayal Aata Hai Jab Bhi Parda-e-zainab Ka Mehdi Ko
Kabhi Talvaar Paar  Woh Haath Rakhta Hai Hatata Hai.

Woh Hujjat Hai Us Hee Ka Kaam Hai Is Dard Ko Sehna
Kisi Khakee Kay Dil Main  Kaun Yeh Ghum Kab Samaata Hai.
