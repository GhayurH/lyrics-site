1
Khanjar Talay Hussain Hai Teeron Ki Jhari Hai.
Aisi Namaz Kis Ne Zamane Mein Parhi Hai.
Woh Hai Hussain Khon Se Jis Ne Wozu Kiya.
Teeron Ki Ja Namaz Pe Al-Hamd Ki Sada.
Sajday Ko Dekh Maut Bhi Hairan Khari Hai.
Nook-e-Sina Pe Jis Ne Quran Sunaya.
Urr Urr Ke Garam Rait Ne Kafan Oss Ka Banaya.
Halq-e-Buredah Lash Jo Maqtal Mein Pari Hai.
Seene Mein Barchi Hath Mein Hai Lal Ka Jigar.
Shabeer Ka Jigar Hai Yeh Shabeer Ka Pisar.
Seene Mein Jis Ke Barchi Zalim Ne Gari Hai.
Apne Hi Garam Khon Mein Tarpay Ali Akbar.
Tha Teer Galay Mein Rahe Hanstay Ali Asghar.
Yeh Foaj-e-Hussaini Hi Karbal Mein Lari Hai.
Dein Ki Bina Hai Sajdah Haider Ke Pisar Ka.
Hai De Raha Gawahi Yeh Waqt Asar Ka.
Bikhra Hai Lasha Ban Mein Our Dhop Kari Hai.
