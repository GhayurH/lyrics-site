1
Baba Mujhe Mara Hai Khun Nikla Hai Bohat Mara Hai Sain Rehman.
Chehra Mera Khud Dekho Kitna Mara Hai Bohat Mara Hai.
Jis Ka Ghazi Sa Chacha Ho Woh Tamachay Khaye.
Nanhi Zulfoon Se Pakar Kar Bhi Samne Laye.
Hanstay Hoye Shimar Kahey Main Ne Mara Hai.
Apne Bachoon Ko Mujhe Dekh Kar Chupatay Hain.
Mere Chehray Ki Woh Halat Se Dar Youn Jate Hain.
Pholon Se Nazuk Chehra Youn Ujara Hai.
Hoti Be Hosh Hoon Baba Main Tamachon Se Agar.
Hosh Bhi Mujh Ko Tamachon Se Dilata Hai Shimar.
Aisay Sulata Hai Mujhe Youn Jagata Hai.
Janta Tha Yeh Shimar Hoon Main Ladli Teri.
Iss Liye Oss Ne Bigari Hai Yeh Sorat Meri.
Kyun Keh Mera Yeh Chehra Tujh Ko Pyara Hai.
Khol Kar Band e Lehad Chehra Behan Ka Dekha.
Apni Zanjeer Ko Chehray Par Mar Kar Yeh Kaha.
Likh Aye Salamat Sab Se Zyada Mara Hai.
