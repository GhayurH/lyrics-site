Ghazi De Samne Zainab Nu Haider Aye Ro Farmaway.
Nanay Da Deen Bachawana Aye Bhawein Chadar V Leh Jaway.

1
Iss Kofay Wich Kofay Walay Lakh Pathar V Aan Wasawan.
Sar Zakhmi Bhawein Ho Jawan Islam Te Zakhm Na Aaway.

2
Zainab Di Chadar Nu Chum Ke Kita Baab-e-Ilm Ne Wain Aye.
Koi Pardah Daar Shareefan Di Ya Rab Bazar Na Jaway.

3
Kar Khatam Wasiyat Babay Ne Kehya Naal Ne Veer Atharaan.
Veraan De Pehrey Wich Zainab Aj Tur Ke Aap Wakhaway.

4
Har Qadam Te Khutbay Parhne Ne Taiyon Mere Jan Taun Pehlaan.
Mainu Zainab Mere Lehjay Wich Parh Ke Quran Sunaway.

5
Kehya Sabir, Maula, Fizza Nu Dhi Sharmaan Wali Zainab.
Mere Samne Aaway Ik Wari Mainu Baba Aakh Bulaway.
