Chan Wekh Muharram Da Nana Jind Zainab Di Ghabrai Aye.
Tera Diwa Bujh Bujh Janda Aye Ais Khuwab Ne Jan Mukai Aye.

1
Jis Din Da Chan Charhya Nana Mera Veer Hussain Na Sonda Aye.
Ki Aakhiya Almaan Walay Choon Ghazi Piya Luk Luk Ronda Aye.
Aynj Lagda Aye Mere Ujran Di Rut Nana Neray Aayi Aye.
Aina Lokaan Meri Maa Nana Wich Bait Al-Huzan Rawai Si.

2
Haq Kar Ke Ghazab Meri Maa Da Fair Wich Darbar Bulayi Si.
Kal Maa Meri Wich Darbar Gayi Aj Meri Wari Aayi Aye.
Tere Bajoon Nana Musalmana Sare Farman Bhulaye Ne.
Tere Putar Hasan Di Maiyyat Te Aina Lokaan Teer Chalaye Ne.

3
Aj Ummat Tere Haye Darwazay Te Le Ajar-e-Risalat Aayi Aye.
Khaliq Mangda Wajid Aye Dua Sadqa Be Jurm Aseeraan Da.
Pori Kar Hajat Matami Di Sadqa Oday Putar Te Veraan Da.
Jehri Wasdi Gayi Haye Watna Taun Kar Khali Jholi Aayi Aye.
