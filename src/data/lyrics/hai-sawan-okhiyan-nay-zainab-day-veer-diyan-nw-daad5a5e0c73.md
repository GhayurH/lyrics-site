Hai Sahwaan Aokhian Nay Zainab Day Veer Diyan

Raitan Day Kad Diyan Paiyan Ragan Shabbir Diyan

Mai Qatal Sajday Ch Veeran Na Vekhdi Raiyan
Na Maro Sattar Qadam Ton Pukaardi Raiyan
Khanjar Di Zarban Nay Raiyan Kalayja Cheer Diyan

Hussain Akhay Bhira Nu Ay Apnay Gal Lakay
Abbas Taras Gayavan Tu Menu Veer Akay
Na Fir Hichkiyan Rukhiyan Ghazi Day Peer Diyan

Main Tere Sajday Ko Sadqay Rasool Ay Akay
Main Teray Sabar Ko Sadqay Batool Ay Akay
Na Fir Lajanay Rakhiyan Ali Ameer Diyan

Anokha Jag Ton Hai Larda Ay Jang Tu Takaya
Haye Kha Ke Teer Tu Peyou Da Buland Sar Rakheya
Galay Na Tordhiyan Jis Nai Anokha Teer Diyan

Hussain Bhain Day Khamay Ch Aakay Ronda Raya
Rakhi Khayal Tu Abid Da Behn Mai Chalaya
Hai Rahwaan Aokihan Meray Puttar Asseer Diyan

Hur Apnay Putran Nu Lakay Shabbir Ko Lay Aya
Reya Ay Mafia Mangda Roya Te Kur Laya
Mola Tu Badliyan Sochan Mere Zameer Dhiyan

Ghum E Hussain Nu Jagtay Sada Manawangay
Nishan Seenayte Matam Da Laykay Jawangay
Kamar Te Sir Te Nay Zarban Mola Zanjeer Dhiyan

Sakina Mash Nu Chakay Ay Ghazi Khola Ayi
Lay Aday Pani Tu Mumtaz Mashq Gal Payi
Haye Kushq Bhulyian Nay Chacha Meray Sagheer Dhiyan
