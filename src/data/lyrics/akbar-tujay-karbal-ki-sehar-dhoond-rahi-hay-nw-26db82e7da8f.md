Akbar Tujay Karbal Ki Sehar Dhoond Rahi Hay

Tujay Jai Say Muazan Ko  Fajr Dhoond Aai Ri Hay.

Aai Meray Jawan Laal Zara Dekh Kay Jaana
Barchi Tera Maqtal Main  Jigar Dhoond Rahee Hay.

Kabhie Nana Kay Rozay Pai Kabhie Babay Shahair Sai
Sughra Teray Aah Nay Ki  Khabar Dhoond Rahee Hay.

Na Dekha Shabab Aisa Na Hai Deykhee Jawanee
Khood Maut Bee Ab Tera  Hijar Dhoond Rahee Hay.

Hayraan Hai Sabee Lowg Teray Laashay Pay Qasim
Maa Khoon Kee Gataree Mai  Pisar Dhoond Rahee Hay.

Kaan Soya Garam Rait Pay Ai Chaand Say Asghar
Maa Teri Anderyron Mai  Qabar Dhoond Rahee Hay.

Mushkil Hoowa Qaidee Say To Bayhoshee Mai Chalna
Adil Har Zanjeer  Safr Dhoond Rahee Hay.
