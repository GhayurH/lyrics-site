Phir Chahe Sakina Ko Wo Jitna Bhi Sata Lay.

1
Tha Bain Sakina Ka Hai Rukhney Ko Mera Dil.
Jata Hai Rida Cheen Ke Be Sheer Ka Qatil.
Aasan Yeh Kar Dein Marey Bhaiyya Meri Mushkil.
Gohar Bhi Nahi Chahiye Na Chahiye Mehmil.
Kar De Jo Marey Sar Ki Rida Mere Hawalay.
Phir Chahe Sakina Ko Woh Jitna Bhi Sata Le.

2
Khanjar Se Gala Sheh Ka Mere Samne Kata.
Pathar Tan-e-Mazloom Pe Marey Gaye Bhaiyya.
Zakhmo Pe Magar Baba Ke Ab Rait Na Walay.
Gohar Mere Cheeney Yeh Sitam Main Ne Saha Hai.
Khoon Mera Sitamgar Ke Hathoon Pe Laga Hai.
Bas Mera Lahu Hathoon Se Woh Apne Hata Le.

3
Rahwaron Ko Teyar Jo Kartay Hain Musalmaan.
Bhaiyya Yeh Sitam Dekh Ke Mar Jaein Gi Amma.
Pamali Se Lasha Ali Asghar Ka Bacha Le.
Karta Hai Dukhi Shah Ki Humsheer Ko Jaisay.
Lehrata Hai Woh Chadar-e-Tatheer Ko Jaisay.
Naizay Pe Marey Baba Ka Sar Youn Na Uchalay.

4
Talwaron Se Chehray Pa Kayi Zakhm Lagay Hain.
Mazloom Ke Yeh Hont Tu Pehle Se Katay Hain.
Ab Baba Ke Honton Se Chari Apni Hata Le.
Hum Se Sahi Jati Nahi Tashoon Ki Aziyat.
Hanstay Hain Laeen Dekh Ke Hum Saron Ki Halat.
Rastay Se Hamarey Yeh Tamashai Hata Le.

5
Main Apne Liye Chaon Tu Nahi Mangti Bhaiyya.
Inn Sham Ki Rahoon Mein Faqat Waqt De Itna.
Daman Se Karon Saaf Tere Pairon Ke Chalay.
 Akbar Thin Tarap Kar Yeh Sakina Ki Sadaein.
Rakhta Hai Sar-e-Ghazi Pe Behno Ki Ridaein.
Yeh Chadrein Woh Sar Se Chacha Jaan Ke Utha Le.
