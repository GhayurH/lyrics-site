Islam Se Kamal Wafa Ki Hussain Ne.
Kya Umar Javedani Atta Ki Hussain Ne.

1
Jigra Kahan Hai Aisa Kissi Maa Ke Lal Ka.
Khanjar Tale Namaz Adda Ki Hussain Ne.
Aisa Koi Na Qariy-e-Quraan Na Hai Khateeb.
Nook-e-Sina Pe Hamd-o-Sana Ki Hussain Ne.

2
Jis Khak Se Gurezaan Rahe Anbiya Karam.
Woh Khak Bhi Tu Khak-e-Shifa Ki Hussain Ne.
Dast-e-Hussain Mein Thi Risalat Rasool Ki.
Touheed Bhi Bachayi Khuda Ki Hussain Ne.

3
Kaisi Shikast-e-Faash Hoi Hai Yazeed Ko.
Jeeti Hai Jung Karb-o-Bala Ki Hussain Ne.
Shashma Ka Sahi Ali Asghar Qabool Kar.
Khaliq Ki Bargah Mein Dua Ki Hussain Ne.

4
Asghar Ka Khoon Apne Hi Chehre Pe Mal Liya.
Rakh Li Hai Laaj Arz-o-Sama Ki Hussain Ne.
Mushkil Azai Ab Kahan Tafreeq-e-Khair-o-Shar.
Zehan-e-Bashar Mein Aisi Zia Ki Hussain Ne.
