Haya ala khayral amal

1
Ya hussain
Nokri Shabbir ki
Mere khun ki taharat kehti
Mujhe har shahi se achhi hay
Nokri Shabbir ki
Ye sab ko mayasar hoti nahi
Ye hur jayson ko milti hay
Nokri Shabbir ki
1) Ye mansab jisko miljaye, zehra ki duayen pata hay
Wo khaak zarra hokar bhi, khud khake shafa banjata hay
Ye nemat to hum jeson ki
Taqdir banaye rakhti hay
Nokri Shabbir ki
2) Ye farshe aza tabuto alam, ye nawhe majlis aur matam
Pani ke sabilen aur langar, masum ali asgher ki qasam,
Hum ehle aza ki mazhub me
Kehlati thi kehlati hay
Nokri Shabbir ki
3) ye log jo nawha parhte hay ye log jo matam karte hay
Ye maa behne jo roti hay zakir jo khitabat karte hay
Darwazaye zehra par inki
Ta roze qayamat paki hay
Nokri Shabbir ki
4) Shabbir ke jitne nokar hay sera bejame kausar kai,
Khud naam aza khano ke jo hay qismat ke abuzar qambar hay
Is izat ko khud binte ali
Quran ki zuban me kehti hay
Nokri Shabbir ki
5) Majlis me siyasat mat karna, maula se baqawat mat karna,
Is farshe aza par ake talak, is zikr ki kimat mat karna,
Bemaas jo matam karte hay
Bus unko shafa at deti hay
Nokri Shabbir ki
6) wo sajadaye akhir jalti zamin, jis sajde ka sani hi nahi,
Khanjar ke tale jis jam rakhi, Shabbir ne haye zakhme jabin,
Jibril ne akar di ye sada
Manzur khuda ne karli hay
Nokri Shabbir ki
7) ye log jo nawha parhte hay ye log jo matam karte hay
Ye maa behne jo roti hay zakir jo khitabat karte hay
Darwazaye zehra par inki
Ta roze qayamat paki hay
Nokri Shabbir ki
8) Shabbir ke jitne nokar hay sera bejame kausar kai,
Khud naam aza khano ke jo hay qismat ke abuzar qambar hay
Is izzat ko khud binte ali
Quran ki zuban me kehti hay
Nokri Shabbir ki
9) "Rehan" kalam bhi nokar hay kaghaz bhi ghulame sarwar hay
Darvesh se badhar lafz me aa, likhta jo azaa ka manzar hay
Kehti hay meri noha goyi
Taqdir me teri likhdi hay
Nokri Shabbir ki
Ye sab ko mayasar hoti nahi
Ye hur jayson ko milti hay
