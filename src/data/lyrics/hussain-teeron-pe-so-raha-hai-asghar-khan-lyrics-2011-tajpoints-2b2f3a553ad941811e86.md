Hussain Teeron Pe So Raha Hai Asghar Khan

1
Zameen Par Hai Na Zeen Par Hai Hussain Teeron Pe So Raha Hai.
Behan Ki Har Sans Par Nazar Hai Hussain Teeron Pe So Raha Hai.
Badan Kahin Pe Tarap Raha Hai Kahin Pe Bazu Tarap Rahe Hain.
Keh Jaisay Abbas Ko Khabar Hai Hussain Teeron Pe So Raha Hai.
Khuda Khabar Ab Padar Uthay Ga Ya Phir Sakina Hi Chal Basay Gi.
Keh Lash Ke Woh Qareeb Tar Hai Hussain Teeron Pe So Raha Hai.

2
Ajab Aziyat Hai Dono Janib Sakon Mil Ke Bhi Kab Muyassar.
Boland Nook-e-Sina Pe Sar Hai Hussain Teeron Pe So Raha Hai.
Udhar Jo Madar Yeh Ro Rahi Hai Rida-e-Zainab Na Loto Logo.
Idhar Yeh Chadar Bhi Noha Gar Hai Hussain Teeron Pe So Raha Hai.
Woh Rooz-e-Ashor Kaisay Akbar Kissi Bhi Saye Mein Chain Paye.
Hai Yaad Jis Ko Keh Do Pahar Hai Hussain Teeron Pe So Raha Hai.
