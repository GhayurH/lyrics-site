1
Sajjad Ne Ro Faryad Kiti Haye Ainj Da Waqt Na Aaye.
Koi Mere Wangoon Phophiyaan Nu Le Ke Bazar Na Jaye.
Ashor De Manzar Ne Loko Mainu Beemar Banaya Aye.
Haye Zalimaan Khaimay Saar Dittay Nezay Taun Rida Nu Laya Aye.
Koi Bain Bhira De Lashay Te Aaway Na Rasiyaan Paye.
Ghazi De Bazu Qalam Hoye Zainab Di Chadar V Leh Gayi.
Jadon Akbar Digiya Ghorey Taun Babay Di Nazar V Muk Gayi.
Dowein Manzar Saday Kunbay De Gaye Jigar Nu Loko Kha Aye.
Haye Manzar Kofay Shehar Da Si Kiwein Oh Sajjad Bhulaye.
Tazeem De Badlay Lokaan Ne Daday De Badlay Laye.
Meri Phophiyaan Mawaan Ro Ro Ke Rehyaan Mangdiyan Najaf Da Rah Aye.
Karbal Taun Le Ke Sham Taein Rehya Shimar Ohno Tarpanda.
Kadi Aan Tamachay Marda Si Kadi Aountaan Naal Doranda.
Kehndi Si Sakina Veer Abid Mere Muk Jane Ne Sah Aye.
Abid Di Pak Jawani Nu Zainab Di Peshi Mar Gayi.
Zindan Che Maut Sakina Di Aidi Rat Nu Hoor Wadha Gayi.
Sochaan Zeeshan Bazar Diyaan Ohdi Sanwaan Nu Gayiyaan Kha Aye.
