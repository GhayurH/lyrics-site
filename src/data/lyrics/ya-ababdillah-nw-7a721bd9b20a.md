as salaam ay shah-e-mazloom ghareebul ghuraba ya ababdillah

kaash hotay ya azadaar sar-e-karbobala ya ababdillah

ya ali keh ke sina kheynch to li sinay se ay payambar zaaday

khoon rulwaeyga lakhtey dil e umme laila ya ababdillah

sadma e bewagi fatima kubra nahi kam ban-e-qasim ki qasam

ek ye daag bhi aashure ke din tune saha ya ababdillah

hasrat o yaas se taktay thay haram suye furaat kar ke abbas ko yaad

sar e zainab se utari gayi jis wakht rida ya ababdillah

arsh hilta hai fiza may hai tamachon ki sada aasma kyun na gira

ek masoom ko milti hai yateemi ki saza ya ababdillah

dafn ke baad bhi qabron ko laeen khodtey hain aur sar kaat-tay hai

ferq e baysheer hai shahid sar-e-nauk e naiza ya ababdillah
