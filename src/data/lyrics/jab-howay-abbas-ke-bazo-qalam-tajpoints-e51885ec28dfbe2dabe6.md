1
Jab Howay Abbas Ke Bazo Qalam.
Ho Gaye Be-Aasra Ahl-e-Haram.
Zeen Se Aaya Zameen Par Jab Jarri.
Ho Gayi Gham Se Kamar Tab Sheh Ki Kham.

2
Tere Bazu They Rida Mere Liye.
Keh Ke Youn Aankhein Hoin Zainab Ki Nam.
Bachon Ke Hathon Se Kozay Gir Gaye.
Jab Giray Jarar Se Mashk-o-Alam.

3
Ho Gaya Kuhram Khaimo Mein Bapa.
Khon Mein Tar Aaya Jab Wapis Alam.
Bain Yeh Ro Kar Sakina Ne Kiya.
Chacha Ab Pani Nahi Mangin Gaye Hum.

4
Lasha-e-Abbas Se Ho Kar Juda.
Khaimo Ko Uthtay Na They Sheh Ke Qadam.
Hai Dua Afraz Par Rakhna Sada.
Aye Alamdar-e-Wafa Apna Karam.
