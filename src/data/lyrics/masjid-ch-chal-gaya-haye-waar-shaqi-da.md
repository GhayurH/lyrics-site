Masjid ch chal gaya
Haye Vaar shaki da

1
Sajde ch zakhmi hoya sir sher e jali da
(1) Sehne Ali ch logon,paye vain bibiyaan nai
Zainab Ruqqaya kulsoom behosh hogaya nai
Uthiyaan jadon janaza
Waliyaan de wali daan
(2) Hora nai vaal khole, malka nai kitta matam
Jibril Arsh utthai ditti saade jis dam
Umat nai mar chadiya
Damaad nabi da
(3) baabey nu zakhmi tak kai Abbas bhon bechene
Ik duje nu gal lakai rondai hussan hussaine
Hai akhiri deedar aj
Ahmad dai wasi da
(4) Baba kyun bolta nahi zainab payi bolawe
Ik var uth ke kehde Quran teno sunawe
Diyaan de naal baba
Injh tay nahi kari nah
(5)kesa amal fidaye haq dai imam kitta
Qatil nu pesh apne sharbat da jam kitta
Husne salooq vekho
Mere mola Ali da
Masjid ch chal gaya hai
