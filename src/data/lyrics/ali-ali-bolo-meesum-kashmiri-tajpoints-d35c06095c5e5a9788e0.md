Hai Hukam-e-Mursal-e-Dawar Ali Ali Bolo.
Laga Ke Narah-e-Haider Ali Ali Bolo.

1
Khuda Ke Noor Ka Mazhar Ali Ali Bolo.
Hai Janasheen-e-Payamber Ali Ali Bolo.
Nabi Ke Deen Ka Rehbar Ali Ali Bolo.
Hai Asal Waris-e-Mimber Ali Ali Bolo.

2
Fazeelaton Ka Samandar Ali Ali Bolo.
Rasool Zadi Ka Humsar Ali Ali Bolo.
Kaho Azaan Mein Ali Un Wali ALLAH Zaror.
Yazidiyat Ko Kuchal Kar Ali Ali Bolo.

3
Ali Ke Wasf Hain Itnay Shumar Kaun Karey.
Yeh Kam Tera Hai Parwardigar Kaun Karey.
Yeh Soch Kar Na Banaya Khuda Ne Our Ali.
Shigaf Kabay Mein Ab Bar Bar Kaun Karey.

4
Khushi Se Jhom Ke Kehta Hai Khana-e-Kaba.
Hai Aaj Aamad-e-Haider Ali Ali Bolo.
Nabi Ne Dars Diya Ya Ali Madad Keh Kar.
Madad Ko Aate Hain Haider Ali Ali Bolo.

5
Faraz-e-Daar Se Meesum Ki Aa Rahi Hai Sada.
Na Rakho Dil Mein Koi Dar Ali Ali Bolo.
Kiya Rasool Ne Kab Chup Chupa Ke Yeh Ayelan.
Kaha Hai Bar Sar-e-Mimber Ali Ali Bolo.

6
Jo Chahtey Ho Lehad Mein Na Hoon Sawal-o-Jawab.
Tu Zindagi Mein Barabar Ali Ali Bolo.
Sada Hai Hazrat-e-Shehbaz Ki Yeh Sehwan Se.
Hain Paishwa-e-Qalandar Ali Ali Bolo.

7
Lagao Seene Se Khush Ho Ke Momino Ko Sada.
Munafiqon Ko Jala Kar Ali Ali Bolo.
Jo Chahtey Ho Keh Manzar Ho Misal-e-Bag-e- Iram.
Tu Apne Ghar Ko Saja Kar Ali Ali Bolo.
