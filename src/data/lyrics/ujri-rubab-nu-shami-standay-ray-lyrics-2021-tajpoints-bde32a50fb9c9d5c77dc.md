1
Ujri Rubab Nu Shami Standay Ray.
Ainu Bar Bar Har Moor Uttay Tin Nokaan Teer Wakhanday Re.
Rondiyaan Kaindi Rayi Sham Bazar Andar Na Pathar Wasao Logo.
Asghar De Sir Walay Naizay Te Loki Pathar Wasanday Ray.

2
Wekhdi Rayi Ujari Rondiyaan Akhiyaan Nal Aidi Tin Salaan Di Dhi Nu.
Mawaan De Sang Ik Rasi Nal Ban Ke Kayi Meel Turanday Ray.
Wain Pandi Dasdi Rayi Lokaan Nu Rubab Main Pak Batool Di Nu Haan.
Ohno Fair V Bagi Qaidan Hi Bazar De Loag Bulanday Ray.

3
Bibi Ro Ro Ke Ayho Aakhay Bazariyaan Nu Mere Naal Hussain Di Jayi.
Oh Mangdi Rayi Guzran Da Rah Te Shami Bheer Wadhanday Ray.
Sham De Lokaan Layi Aye Tamasha Si Koi Gum Sakina Si Hoi.
Jis Pasay Labdi Jandi Si Oss Pasay Bheer Wadhanday Ray.

4
Ainj V Sain Uttay Sham Wich Zulm Hoye Bazar Guzar De Welay.
Odhay Samne Asghar De Sir Nal Oh Apne Baal Khadanday Ray.
Maa Si Saqlain Oh Maa Sham Darbar Taein Asghar Te Rayiyaan Nazraan.
Oss Pasay Amari Turdi Rayi Jis Pasay Sir Le Janday Ray
