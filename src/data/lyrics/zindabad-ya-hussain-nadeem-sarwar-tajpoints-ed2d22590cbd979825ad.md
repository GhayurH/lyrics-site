Zindabad Ya Hussain Nadeem Sarwar.
Gonjti Fizaein Hain Matami Sadaein Hain.
Jis Taraf Nazar Dalein Qafilay Hazaron Hain.
Qafilon Ke Seeno Mein Dard Ke Safeno Mein.

Inn Ki Karbala Manzil Hai Hatheliyon Par Dil.

Hussainiyat Ka Saibaan Muhabbaton Ka Aashiyaan.
Tu Nabi Ka Zaib-o-Zeen Hai Tu Ali Ka Noor-e-Ain Hai.
Fatima Ke Dil Ka Chain Hai LaIlaha Ya Hussain Dein Panah Ya Hussain.
Salam Shah-e-Karbala Tu Iradah-e-Khuda.
Bara Buland Hosla Nahi Hussain Dosra.

Hussain Izaton Ka Badshah Marhaba Ya Hussain Cha Gaya Ya Hussain.

Lahu Se Surkh Posh Mein Raha Josh Kya Ya Hussain Hosh Kya Ya Hussain.
