1
Paikaan Baras Rahay Hain Taboot Par Hasan Ke.
Ummat Mita Rahi Hai Aasar Panjtan Ke.
Baba Ke Dil Ke Tukray Tu Ne Ginay Bahatar 72.
Tukray Hazar Hoon Gaye Qasim Tere Badan Ke.

2
Zainab Pukari Bhai Dil Dobata Hai Mera.
Uglo Na Dil Ke Tukray Sadqay Gayi Dahan Ke.
Bait-ul-Nabi Se Haye Utha Hai Phir Janaza.
Yasrab Ki Bastiyon Mein Jagray Hain Phir Dafan Ke.

3
Do Baar Mil Gaya Hai Tum KO Kafan Aye Maula.
Muhtaj Hi Rahein Gaye Shabir Tu Kafan Ke.
Zahra Jahan Giri Thin Lasha Wahan Na Jaye.
Phir Zakhm Khul Na Jaein Zair-e-Kafan Hasan Ke.

4
Qabar-e-Hasan Pe Oss Dam Larza Tha Aik Tari.
Sadaat Ne Dikhaye Kuch Neel Jab Rasan Ke.
Tan Par To Hai Hasan Ke Go Chid Gaya Hai Sara.
Muhtaj Hi Rahein Gaye Shabir Tu Kafan Ke.

5
Shabar Ne Jeete Jee Na Qasim Ka Biyah Dekha.
Dekhe Na Kale Kapre Ik Raat Ki Dulhan Ke.
Yasrab Mein Karbala Mein Baghdad-o-Samra Mein.
Afsos Phool Bikhre Zahra Tere Chaman Ke.

6
Taboot Se Lipat Ke Qasim Pukare Baba.
Aansu Akhi Ke Dekho Nohay Sunu Behan Ke.
Aakhir Ki Hichkiyon Mein Shabar Ne Roke Chome.
Shabir Ka Gala Our Bazu Bari Behan Ke.

7
Zainab Hai Baal Khole Ummat Hai Teer Tolay.
Pocho Na Sham Ke Jab Yeh Haal Hain Watan.
Bole Hasan Keh Zainab Yeh Ghar Rahe Ga Jal Kar.
Bazu Tumhare Hoon Gaye Halqay Mein Ik Rasan Ke.

8
Saye Mein Murtaza Ke Bichre They Fatima Se.
Our Aaj Paas Maa Ke Aaye Hain Lash Ban Ke.
Maula Nisar Apni Shamsheer Kyun Uthatay.
Jabke Woh Janasheen They Payghambar-e-Aman Ke.
