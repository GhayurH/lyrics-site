1
Ay Muhammed Ki Ladli Zahra Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.
Aye Muhammad Ki Ladli Zahra.
Tera Khadam Hai Har Nabi Zahra.
Roz Hogi Rasool Ko Mairaj.
Ab Tu Dunya Mein Aa Gayi Zahra.
Dono Betay Miley Musalay Par.
Chal Ke Kaba Kahan Gayi Zahra.
Gasibon Our Munkiron Ke Beech.
Kar Rahi Hai Ali Ali Zahra.
Qasas Al-Anbiya Bhi Jhoom Uthay.
Jab Kahani Teri Suni Zahra.
Jab Ali Aik Hai Khuda Bhi Aik.
Kaisay Mumkin Hai Dosri Zahra.
Oss Ko Uth Kar Na Kyun Salam Karon.
Hur Ne Tazeem Teri Ki Zahra.
Hum Ne Dankay Ki Chot Par Rakhi.
Tere Dushman Se Dushmani Zahra.
Noor Satay Hai Bait-e-Haider Se.
Kya Musalay Pe Hai Khari Zahra.
Chal Ke Jana Para Shab-e-Mairaj.
Phir Muhammad Ko Hai Mili Zahra.
Hai Bara Aadmi Takallum Bhi.
Teri Karta Hai Nokari Zahra.
