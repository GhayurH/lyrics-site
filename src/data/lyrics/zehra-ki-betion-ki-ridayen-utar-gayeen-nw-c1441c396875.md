Zehra ki Betion Ki Ridayen Utar Gayeen

Sajjad Ko Zaeef Sadayen Yeh Kargaeen

Lashon Ko Rassion Main, Jakar Kar Liye Phiro

Saidanian To Ghar Se Nikalte Hi mar Gayeen

Jati Thi Jo Kabhi Kabhi, Zahra Ki Kabr P

Jane Khuda bazaar Se Kaise Guzar Gayeen

Zulm O Sitam Ki Taiz, Hawaien thin jab chalin

Sub Gulshan E Batool Ki Kalyaan Bikhar Gayeen

Godoun say Rahay Sham Main, Gir Gir Ke Mar Gaye

Bacchon Ki Lash Dekh Ke Maaien Bhi Mar Gayeeno

Ramla Yeh Poochti Thi, Sakina Se Qaid Main

Zakhmi Hain Kaan Balian Bibi Kidhar Gayeen

Bazaar Leke Jaao Na, Beparda Zalimon

Mar Jayen gi Hussain Ke Behnain Agar Gayeen
