1
Jo Chal Raha Hai Nizam-e-Dunya Karam Hai Tera Hussain Ki Maa.
Namaz, Roza, Azan, Sajda, Hai Tera Sadqa Hussain Ki Maa.
Khiyal Zahra, Kamal Zahra, Jamal Zahra, Jalal Zahra.
Wafa Ki Masjid, Haram Khuda Ka, Hayya Ka Kaba Hussain Ki Maa.

2
Tere Khilone Mangaon Beta Main Chand Soraj Bulaon Beta.
Yeh Keh Ke Ghar Mein Jhula Rahi Hai Hasan Ka Jhola Hussain Ki Maa.
Jo Tere Dushman Hain Sare Kafir, Kahan Pe Jaein Gaye Bach Ke Aakhir.
Ali Bhi Tera, Nabi Bhi Tera, Khuda Bhi Tera Hussain Ki Maa.

3
Adab Se Hathoon Ko Bandha Hoga Yeh Hur Ne Dil Mein Bhi Bola Hoga.
Hussain Ki Simt Ja Raha Hoon Sanbhal Lena Hussain Ki Maa.
Zameen Tumhari, Falak Tumhara, Namak Tumhara, Tumhare Darya.
Zamana Sadiyon Se Kha Raha Hai Tumhara Sadqa Hussain Ki Maa.

4
Iss Hi Mein Tauheed Our Risalat Iss Hi Mein Quran Our Wilayat.
Nisab e Aal-e-Rasool Hai Yeh Tumhara Khutba Hussain Ki Maa.
Woh Hujjaton Par Bhi Ain Hujjat Bas Iss Ke Qadmo Tallay Hai Jannat.
Kahan Pe Ummat Ki Sari Maein, Kahan Pe Zahra Hussain Ki Maa.
