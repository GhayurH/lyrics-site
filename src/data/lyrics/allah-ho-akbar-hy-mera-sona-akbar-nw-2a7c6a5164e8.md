Allah Ho Akbar, Hy Mera Sona Akbar
Dai Aakhri Putar Azaan  Howai Deen Toun Aj Qurbaan.

1
Par Putar Ashaadan Laillah Hai Tera Istakbaal Kaza
Ay Hurab Di Haii Manshaa, Chand Ghariyaan Da Mehmaan.

2
Sab Dayn Gawaii Arz-o-sama Mera Nana Paak Rasullallah
Aayii Rondi Maan Zahra, Teri Bachra Sun Azaan.

3
Kaho Heya Ala Kairal Amal, Asa Piyu Putar-a-nai Hona Katil
Naii Karni Ummat Haya, Mun Khaliq Da Farmaan.

4
Mera Baba Imamul Mutaqeen, Terai Lah Unal Honiay Surkh Zameen
Piya Qambda-e-karbobla, Tera Katil Da Sun Aylaan.

5
Aa Ibrahim Khalillullah, Hona Akbar Barchi Naal Zibah
Akiyaan Tu Patiya Laa, Nai Barchi Kichan-e-Asaan.

6
Honda Putar Akihyaan Da Noor Akbar, Ai Ha Jag Da Haii Das Toor Akbar
Nahi Jiiundai Piyu Bachra, Jadoun Marday Putar Jawaan.
