1
Kiwein Darbar Gayi Mir Hasan Mir.
Kiwein Darbar Gayi Be-Rida Bint-e-Ali Jeri Nazaan Di Pali.
Pa Ke Hath Kariyaan Jadaan Sham De Bazar Aayi.
Dadhi Majboor Te Be-Chain Si Zahra Jayi.

2
Aakhay Sajad Phophi Bheer Bazaar Bari Tu Aye Nazaan Di Pali.
Jainkoon Farmaya Nabi Pak Ne Dill Da Tukra.
Haye Afsoos Maidi Sainr Koon Haq Na Miliya.
Oh Jadaan Khali Wali Qabar Babe Di Hali Haye Nazaan Di Pali.

3
Chadraan Lutiyaan Te Khaime V Jalaye Lokaan.
Fatima Jaiyaan Koon Bazaar Wikhaye Lokaan.
Shehar Te Shehar Phiri Rul Gayi Aal-e-Nabi Jairi Nazaan Di Pali.
Veer Ghazi Da Jaddaan Dhal Giya Sir Taun Saya.

4
Sainr Zainab Te Kadi Sukh Da Na Waila Aaya.
Kadi Kofe Te Kadi Sham Darbar Gayi Haye Nazaan Di Pali.
Veer Sajjad Dukh Bhain Koon Chad Chad Na Jawein.
Bhain Ujari Nu Kafan Sham Da Tu Na Pawein.

5
Kar Ke Matam Aye Chali Wich Zindaan Aye Mari Jairi Nazaan Di Pali.
Cha Ke Hathaan Te Sakina Koon Aye Abid Soche.
Kon Sunrda Aye Meri Kis Nu Dasawaan Ja Ke.
Char Salaan Di Dukhi Bhainr Mar Gayi Aye Meri Jairi Nazaan Di Pali.
