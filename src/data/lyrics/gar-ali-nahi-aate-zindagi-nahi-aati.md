1
Gar Ali Nahi Aate Zindagi Nahi Aati.
Zindagi Nahi Atti Roshni Nahi Aati.
Roshni Nahi Atti Bandagi Nahi Aati.
Bandagi Nahi Aati Aagahi Nahi Aati.
Roh Ke Gulistaan Mein Tazgi Nahi Aati.

2
Nafs Baich Kar Apna Kis Sakon Se Soya.
Oss Ka Aik Shab Sona Kainat Ne Dekha.
Jab Talak Na Ho Takiya Marzi-e-Elahi Ka.
Our Na Gar Muyasar Ho Bistar-e-Rasool-e-Khuda.
Murtaza Ko Bistar Par Neend Nahi Atti.

3
Woh Rasool Ka Bistar Gar Na Tu Bhula Deta.
Qatal-e-Marhab-o-Antar Gar Na Tu Bhula Deta.
Fateh-e-Khandaq-o-Khaiber Gar Na Tu Bhula Deta.
Woh Ghadeer Ka Mimber Gar Na Tu Bhula Deta.
Shaikh Tere Hise Mein Teergi Nahi Aati.

4
Kalma Jis Ka Parhte Ho Bugz Unn Se Rakhte Ho.
Bheek Ke Bhi Talib Ho Dam Bhi Un Ka Bharte Ho.
Unn Ke Dushmano Se TUm Rah-o-Rasam Rakhte Ho.
Dawa-e-Muhabbat Hai Dushmani Bhi Karte Ho.
Kaise Umati Ho Tum Sharam Bhi Nahi Aati.

5
Go Sakon Milta Hai ALLAH Ho Ke Naroon Mein.
Garmiyon Ke Rozon Mein Be-Riya Namazoon Mein.
Soz-e-Ishq Milta Hai Matami Jawano Mein.
Betha Nahi Jab Tak Aadmi Malangoon Mein.
Dil Lagi Tu Aati Hai Aashiqi Nahi Aati.

6
Jo Bhi Wird-e-Nad-e-Ali Subh-o-Sham Karta Hai.
Jo Darood-e-Aal-e-Nabi Subh-o-Sham Parhta Hai.
Gham Hussain Ka Rakh Ke Dam Ali Ka Bharta Hai.
Unn Ke Dushmano Par Jo Be-Shumar Karta Hai.
Pass Aise Banday Ke Be-Kuli Nahi Aati.

7
Kuch Nahi Rakah Wallah Falsafa Ki Behsoon Mein.
Marifat Nahi Milti Maktabi Kitaboon Mein.
Kuch Asar Nahi Baqi Waizon Ki Dantoon Mein.
Ab Koi Nahi Aata Molvi Ki Batoon Mein.
Be-Wilay-e-Aal-e-Nabi Aagahi Nahi Aati.

8
Ishq Jis Se Karte Hain Oss Ki Baat Bhi Mane.
Uswa-e-Nabi-o-Ali Har Qadam Par Apnaein.
Jitne Kam Ache Hain Unn Ko Kal Pe Mat Talein.
Tang Dast Momin Ki Dastgeeri Farmaein.
Aaj Se Amal Kijiye Kal Kabhi Nahi Aati.

9
Bheek Bhi Nahi Milti Pait BHi Nahi Bharta.
Ilm Bhi Nahi Milti Inn Ke Dar Par Aaye Bina.
Tajurba Hai Yeh Mera YaniSibt-e-Jafar Ka.
Dil Pe Choot Khaye Bina Unn Se Lo Lagaye Bina.
Lakh Misray Keh Lijiye Shayari Nahi Aati.
