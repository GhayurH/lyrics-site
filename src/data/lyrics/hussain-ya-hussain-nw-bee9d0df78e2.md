Hussain ya hussain

Zahra ka ladla be jurm/o bay kahta, haay mara gaya

Gurbat key/ yay shab hay/ bay parda/ zainab hay

Karti hay / ro ro kay / Zainab yayhi fugan

Wo jo lahu lahu hay/ khaliq key abru hay/ ay shah hay kerbala

Mehshar talak khuda, mamnoon hay tayra

Tu haq’ ka /rehbar hay /nabioon say /bartar hay

Tayray hey/ sadqay may /baqi hay yay jahan

Zainab’ ko bas yay ghum hay/ is baat ka alam hay/ bar khak’ nenava

Zahra kay lal ka lasha para raha,

Zainab’ ko /yad ahey/ bhai key/ tanhaey /

Jab run say/ ja ta tha/ pyason ka kariwan

Sehmi huwi Sakina/ pyasi hay wo hazina /day thi rahi sada

Gawhar mayray chinay, daman bhi jal gaya

Suna yay/ sehra hay/ eh baba/ jangal main

Yay dukhiya/ maqtal main / dunday tumhay kahan

Baba ko ronay wali/ senay pay sonay wali/

Ab sari zindagi baba ko ro ay gey kehsay wo soay gey

Ghabra kar/ kehmon say/ nikhlay gey/ pyasi wo/

Dunday gey/ baba ko/ lashon kay darmian
