1
Ho Gaya Sham Ke Zindaan Mein Andhera Baba.
Ghut Na Jaye Kahin Dam Qaid Mein Mera Baba.
Mar Kar Munh Pe Tamachay Mere Dur Cheen Liye.
Mere Kano Ka Zakhm Ab Bhi Hai Gehra Baba.

2
Sona Chahti Hoon Magar Aankh Yeh Lagti Hi Nahi.
Mere Soney Pe Laga Khof Ka Pehra Baba.
Aisa Lagta Hai Hai Keh Yeh Raat Meri Aakhri Hai.
Aa Ke Ik Bar Dikha Dijiye Chehra Baba.

3
Aaye Ga Lasha Uthane Bhaiyya Sajjad Mera.
Jane Kab Ho Gi Subha Ho Ga Sawera Baba.
Saal Chodah So Guzar Gaye Zulm Barhta Hi Raha.
Aisa Lagta Hai Wahin Waqt Hai Thehra Baba.

4
Baliyaan Aap Ne Kano Mein Mere Dalein Thin Jab.
Yaad Aata Hai Mujhe Pal Woh Sunehra Baba.
Pohancha Abrar Jo Ik Bar Mere Rozay Par.
Dekh Kar Roney Laga Maut Ka Sehra Baba.
