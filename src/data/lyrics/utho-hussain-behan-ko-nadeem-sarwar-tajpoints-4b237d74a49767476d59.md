Utho Hussain Behan Ko Nadeem Sarwar.

1
Yeh Main Hoon Main Meri Tanhai Yeh Tamashai.
Lo Aayi Gayarah Muharram Hai Kya Khabar Layi.
Chali Yeh Janib-e-Zindaan Tumhari Maa Jayi.
Sawar Hum Ne Kiya Tha Tumhein Mere Bhai.
Ab Aao Ya Mujhe Keh Do Na Intezar Karo.
Utho Hussain Bahan Ko Zara Sawar Karo.

2
Sawar Hum Ne Kiya Tha Tumhein Dam-e-Rukhsat.
Lagam Thami Thi Main Ne Bahalat-e-Riqqat.
Kahan Gayi Woh Muhabbat Kahan Gayi Chahat.
Amama Apna Mere Sar Pe Saya Bar Karo.
Watan Se Door Hoon Kuch Our Door Jati Hoon.
Utho Utho Mere Bhaiyya Tumhein Jagati Hoon.

3
Koi Bhi Aata Nahi Main Jisay Bulati Hoon.
Qarar-e-Jaan Mere Mujh Ko Na Be Qarrar Karo.
Safar Madine Se Kaisa Tha Yeh Safar Kya Hai.
Na Mehmilein Hain Na Hodaj Na Koi Pardah Hai.
Hai Sar Mein Khak Yeh Karb-o-Bala Ka Sehra Hai.
Na Jee Sakoon Gi Tere Baad Aytbar Karo.

4
Aray Furat Pe Aye Soney Walay Jaag Zara.
Alam Ke Saye Mein Rukhsat Behan Ko Kar Bhaiyya.
Suna Hai Sakht Kathin Hai Yeh Sham Ka Rasta.
Sehal Yeh Rasta Abbas-e-Namdar Karo.
Main Aaon Gi Mere Bhaiyya Zaror Aaon Gi.
Kafan Urhaon Gi Marqad Tera Banaon Gi.

5
Jahan Bhi Jaon Gi Farsh-e-Azza Bichaon Gi.
Main Qaid Kaat Ke Laut Aaon Intezar Karo.
Ragoon Mein Khon Badan Mein Jo Sans Jari Hai.
Tumhari:SARWAR-o-REHAN: Zimma Dari Hai.
Jahan Jahan Mere Bhai Ki Pursa Dari Hai.
Tum Apne Nohon Se Dunya Ko Sogowar Karo.
