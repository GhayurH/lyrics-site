Hussain yaa Hussain (4)

1
(1)Naame khuda duniya me jab tak bhi rahega
Jaa mere maula tera naam rahega
Barwaa haider tera aaega jahaan me
Khoon ka badla woh tera keh k yeh lega
……yaa hussain(2) yaa Aba abdillah
Yaa hussain(3) sarallah……

2
(2)Deen e islam jo zindaa h tera sadqa hai
Baade Allah faqt maula tera charcha hai
Asre Ashur me jo tune adaa kar dala
Aisa ab tak na zamane me hua sajda hai
……yaa hussain(2) yaa Aba abdillah
Yaa hussain(3) sarallah……

3
(3)tune duniya ko bataya hai k mazloom h kya
Apne kirdaar se dikhlaya h masoom h kya
Zor e baatil ka bharam tod dia h tune
Aur batalaya hai islam ka mafhoom h kya
……yaa hussain(2) yaa Aba abdillah
Yaa hussain(3) sarallah……

4
(4)karbala hoti na abaad bayaan kya hota
Maqsade deen e Mohammad b ayaa kya hota
Chahata tha jo yazid aaj woh sab kuch hota
Hukme Allah b kho jaata nigaah kya hota
……yaa hussain(2) yaa Aba abdillah
Yaa hussain(3) sarallah……

5
(5)karbala jung thi be deen sitamgaaro se
Qul e imaan ki baatil k taraf daaro se
Jo bhi kehta h k yeh siyasati jung thi
Raabta uska h islaam k gaddaaro se
……yaa hussain(2) yaa Aba abdillah
Yaa hussain(3) sarallah……

6
(6)maqsade karbobala zinda rahega logo
Jo b mazloom h zaalim se ladega logo
Bas isi baat ka shabbir ne paigaam dia
Jo b momin h yehi baat kahega logo
……yaa hussain(2) yaa Aba abdillah
Yaa hussain(3) sarallah……

7
(7)Deen e islam jo zindaa h tera sadqa hai
Baade Allah faqt maula tera charcha hai
Asre Ashur me jo tune adaa kar dala
Aisa ab tak na zamane me hua sajda hai

8
(8)tune duniya ko bataya hai k mazloom h kya

Aur batalaya hai islam ka mafhoom h kya

9
(9)aao batlaye tmhe kaun hussain ibne Ali
Jiski mayyat sar e maidaan kafan ko tarsi
Jiske laashe ko b ghodo se h pamaal kia
Jiski majboor behan laash pe kehti hi rahi
……yaa hussain(2) yaa Aba abdillah
Yaa hussain(3) sarallah……

10
(10)sabr o isaar na bhulega zamana tera
Khoon e asgher nahi bhulega zamana tera
Laashe akbar ko utha kandhe pe lana tera
Sunke ghazi ki sada rait pe girna tera
……yaa hussain(2) yaa Aba abdillah
Yaa hussain(3) sarallah……

11
(11)asre ashur hua zakhomo se jab chur badan
Har taraf shor utha qatl hue shahe zaman
Khoon barsa h falak se yeh fizaa rone lagi
Kaanp uthi h zami kaanp utha saara ban
……yaa hussain(2) yaa Aba abdillah
Yaa hussain(3) sarallah……

12
(12)khaak pe sajda woh tera ins pe zehra
Bhook teri pyaas teri garam woh sehra
Bach gaya Allak ka deen yeh tera sadqa
Dono jahano me h faqt hai tera charcha
……yaa hussain(2) yaa Aba abdillah
Yaa hussain(3) sarallah……

13
(13)jisne naize pe quraan padh k dikhaya sabko
Hum hi hai Aale mohammad yeh bataya sabko
Tmne be deen musalmano sitam zard kiye
Sar be rehna sare bazaar phiraya sabko
……yaa hussain(2) yaa Aba abdillah
Yaa hussain(3) sarallah……

14
(14)meri awaaz ko taaqat de mere maula hussain
Sach likhu mujhe himmat de mere maula hussain
Saari duniya kahe naukar h Ali deep tera
Bas mujhe itni hi izzat de mere maula hussain
……yaa hussain(2) yaa Aba abdillah
Yaa hussain(3) sarallah……
