Main Aa Raha Hoon Sakina.

1
Hai Bas Khatam Hone Ko Har Imtehan.
Koi Ghaib Se De Raha Hai Sada.

2
Nigahoon Mein Meri Hai Ab Tak Wo Lamhe.
Rehai Harm Ko Mili Jiss Tarha Se.
Aseeron Ko Abid Gay Le Ke Jaise.
Tujhe Bhi Chura Kar Main Zindaan Se.
Bohat Jald Le Jaon Ga Karbala Main Aa Raha Hoon Sakina.
Azadari Hoti Thi Gurbat Pe Teri.
Tere Dill Ko Hoti Thi Kitni Tasali.
Magar Aab Na Matam Na Majlis Hai Koi.
Tere Pass Tanhaiyaan Reh Gayin.
Raha Aab Na Koi Tera Aasra Main Aa Raha Hoon Sakina.
Laho Ban Giya Kaise Aankhoon Ka Pani.
Hoi Maqtal Abid Ki Kaise Jawani.
Suno Ga Main Bazar Teri Zubani.
Karon Ga Main Khud Sath Giriya Tere.
Na Ro Shahzadi Tehar Ja Zara Main Aa Raha Hoon Sakina.
Tujhe Aab Na pardais Koi Kahe Ga.
Watan Le Ke Tujh Ko Yeh Mehdi Chale Ga.
Madina Watan Tha Madina Rahe Ga.
Tarapti Hai Milne Ko Bemar Se.
Mila Doon Ga Sughra Se Wadah Mera Main Aa Raha Hoon Sakina.
Nishaan Hai Bibi Jo Rukh Par Tumhare.
Hai Aise Hi Kuch Daag Dill Par Hamare.
Nazar Mein Hamari Ye Manzar Hai Saray.
Baqaiya Ta Samarah Karb-o-Bala.
Chukane Ko Badla Har Ik Zulm Ka Main Aa Raha Hoon Sakina.
Palat Kar Na Aya Jo Darya Se Ghazi.
Kissi Se Bhi Tu Ne Na Phir Manga Pani.
Meri choti Zahra Tu Kab Se Hai Piyasi.
Bhojane Ko Sadiyon Ki Tishna Labi.
Tere Pass Khud Ban Ke Saqa Tera Main Aa Raha Hoon Sakina.
 Takallum Wo Kehta Yeh Ho Ga Tarap Kar.
Main Yasrab Se Takta HOon Khud Taig-e-Haider.
Teri Alajal Ki Sadaon Ko Sun Kar.
Ossi Tarha Atta Hai Lab Par Mere.
Katay sar Ne Tha Jiss Tarha Se Kaha Main Aa Raha Hoon Sakina.
