1
Sorah-e-Kahaf Sunata Hai Yeh Sar Kis Ka Hai.
Khon Aankhon Se Bahata Hai Yeh Sar Kis Ka Hai.
Chalte Chalte Jo Sakina Kahin Gir Jati Hai.
Khud Ko Naizay Se Girata Hai Yeh Sar Kis Ka Hai.

2
Jis Taraf Jata Hai Bazar Mein Sar Akbar Ka.
Oss Taraf Dekhta Jata Hai Yeh Sar Kis Ka Hai.
Jab Bhi Dakhil Kissi Bazar Mein Hote Hain Haram.
Pehla Pathar Yehi Khata Hai Yeh Sar Kis Ka Hai.

3
Raat Hoti Hai Tu Masoom Si Ik Bachi Ko.
Lori Naizay Se Sunata Hai Yeh Sar Kis Ka Hai.
Parh Ke Quran Ki Aayaat Sar-e-Zainab Se.
Dehyan Logon Ka Hatata Hai Yeh Sar Kis Ka Hai.

4
Yeh Samma Kash Na Dekhe Koi Khowahir Iss Ki.
Shimar(L) Zulfon Se Uthata Hai Yeh Sar Kis Ka Hai.
:MIR SAJJAD: Nahi Kya Koi Waris Iss Ka.
Iss Ko Har Aik Satata Hai Yeh Sar Kis Ka Hai.
