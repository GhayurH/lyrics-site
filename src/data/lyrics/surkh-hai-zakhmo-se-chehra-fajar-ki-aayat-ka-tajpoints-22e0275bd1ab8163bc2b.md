1
Surkh Hai Zakhmo Se Chehra Fajar Ki Aayat Ka.
Sham Ke Sab Pathron Par Khon Hai Sadaat Ka.
Iss Qadar Shiddat Se Mara Tha Tamacha Shimar Ne.
Qabar Tak Chehray Pe Ik Neela Nishaan Tha Hath Ka.

2
Ho Chukin Pandrah Namazein Be Ridai Mein Ada.
Jane Din Niklay Ga Kis Din Qaidiyon Ki Raat Ka.
Haye Pardah Chom Kar Zindan Ki Dewar Ko.
Shukar Kartay They Ada Abid Khuda Ki Zaat Ka.

3
Manqabat Se Bugz Hai Yeh Sham Ke Darbar Ko.
Aik Ik Misra Hai Qaidi Mustafa Ki Naat Ka.
Fatima Ki Beti Ne Akbar Bandhay Hathoon Ke Sath.
Sham Walon Ko Dikhaya Aaina Oqaat Ka.
