Kya Raha Khaimo Mein Sheh Ke Ik Udasi Reh Gayi.
Sirf Rone Ko Muhammad Ki Nawasi Reh Gayi.

1
Saj Gayi Qasim Ke Tukron Se Udhar Karb-o-Bala.
Maa Idhar Baitay Ki Dulhan Ko Sajati Reh Gayi.
Cheenta Tha Shimar Chadar Our Zainab Bar Bar.
Apni Chadar Ke Mohafiz Ko Bulati Reh Gayi.

2
Khoon-e-Dill Abbas Ka Sab Beh Giya Ran Mein Magar.
Dill Mein Bas Ik Baat Zainab Ki Rida Ki Reh Gayi.
Aye Musalmano Tumhari Ghairatein Kya Ho Gayien.
Tum Tamashayi The Zainab Munh Chupati Reh Gayi.

3
Qaid Mein Bali Sakina Ko Mila Baba Ka Sar.
Munh Pe Munh Rakh Kar Jo Soyi Maa Jagati Reh Gayi.
Madar-e-Asghar Na Bethi Saye Mein Asghar Ke Bad.
Saye Mein Ayi Tu Zindah Lash Baqi Reh Gayi.

4
Har Tamache Par Sakina Munh Pe Rakh Kar Nane Hath.
Neel Rukhsaron Ke Ghazi Ko Dikhati Reh Gayi.
Aa Gay Rehan-o- Sarwar Karbala Se Laut Kar.
Aaj Tak Khushbo Badan Mein Karbala Ki Reh Gayi.
