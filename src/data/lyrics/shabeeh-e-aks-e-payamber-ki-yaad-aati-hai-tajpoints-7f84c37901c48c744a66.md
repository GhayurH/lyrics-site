1
Shabeeh-e-Aks-e-Payamber Ki Yaad Aati Hai.
Namaz-e-Fajar Mein Akbar Ki Yaad Aati Hai.
Jo Naz-e-Zainab-e-Muztar Tha Maa Ka Pyara Tha.
Phophi Ko Ab Ossi Dilbar Ki Yaad Aati Hai.

2
Sakina Sughra Ko Armaan Tha Jis Ki Shadi Ka.
Behan Ko Apne Baradar Ki Yaad Aati Hai.
Woh Jis Ko Nazoon Se Pala Tha Bibi Zainab Ne.
Ab Oss Hi Mah-e-Munawar Ki Yaad Aati Hai.

3
Woh Jis Ke Husan Ka Sadqa Thi Zaat Yousuf Ki.
Ossi Hussain Ke Akbar Ki Yaad Aati Hai.
Rakha Hijab Mein Saloon Tha Jis Ko Zainab Ne.
Aab Oss Hi Laila Ke Dilbar Ki Yaad Aati Hai.

4
:UMAIR KAZMI: Yaad Aati Nainawa Mujh Ko.
 Rubab Jab Kabhi Sarwar Ki Yaad Aati Hai.
