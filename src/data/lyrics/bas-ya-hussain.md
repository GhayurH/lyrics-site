  1

  Baali pe tere aayi hai khud maadar e hussain

  Is martabe ke baad to sab tujhko mil gaya

  Ay hurr hussain mil gaye rab tujhko mil gaya

  Kuch nahi mila usay tu nahi mila jise

  Tu jisko mil gaya rab us ko mil gaya

  Bas ya hussain


  2

  Ye bas hussain ki ataa ye bas hussain ka jigar

  Jo keh diya wo motabar jo de diya to saara ghar

  Karam hussain ka karam kisi pe hogaya agar

  Mile kisi ko baal o par kisi ko mil gaya pisar

  Hurr bana diya usay jo ek baar mil gaya

  Tu jisko mil gaya...


  3

  Adam ki poori nasl pe ehsaan kaun tha

  Jis par khuda ko naaz wo insan kaun tha

  Kunbe ke saath deen pe qurban kaun tha

  Nok e sina pe qaari e quran kaun tha

  Ay karbala bata tera mehman kaun tha


  4

  Bas hussain ka jise ye ikhtiyar mil gaya

  Ye ikhtiar kitna waseeh o buland hai

  Saara jahan hussain ki muthi mein band hai

  Badla hai karbala mein taqaza e inqelaab

  Naize pe ab jo sar hai wohi sar buland hai


  5

  Aiwaan e inbisaat mein jannat na kar talash

  Ye to gham e hussain ke aansu mein band hai

  Tu jisko mil gaya...

  Bas ya hussain...

  Daastan hussain ki samajh mein aagayi agar

  To hurr ki rehguzaar par karega ishq ka safar


  6

  Tadap tadap uthega tu kahega raat raat bhar

  Hussain bas hussain hai sina pe ya zameen par

  Shaah e karbala se jiske dil ka taar mil gaya

  Tu jisko mil gaya...

  Ek do pehar mein jisne bhara ghar luta diya


  7

  Nazaan khuda hai jispe wo sajda adaa kiya

  Apne lahoo se khaak ko khaak e shifa kiya

  Ashoor ek roz tha sadiyon pe chaa gaya

  Karbobala ko arshe mualla bana diya

  Salam ya hussain rab ka aitbaar mil gaya


  8

  Tu jisko mil gaya rab us ko mil gaya

  Jab ran mein zulfiqar e ali nagahan chali

  Bich bich gayi safon pe safein wo jahan chali

  Chamki to is taraf udhar aayi wahan chali

  Dene ko daad arsh se pyase ki maa chali

  Muddat ka taal meinl tha barson ka saath tha


  9

  Jaisi wo zulfiqar thi waisa hi haath tha

  Kha kha gayi safon ko jidhar aayi zulfiqar

  Geh chup gayi to gaah nazar aayi zulfiqar

  Sar par chamak ke taaba kamar aayi zulfiqar

  Zee kaat kar zameen pe utar aayi zulfiqar

  Phir to ye ghul hua ke duhaai hussain ki


  10

  Allah ka ghazab hai ladayi hussain ki

  Hussain ya hussain

  Aayi nida e ghaib ke shabbir marhaba

  Is haath ke liye thi ye shamsheer marhaba

  Ye aabroo ye jang ye tauqeer marhaba

  Bas bas hussain bas mere dilgeer marhaba


  11

  Waqt e namaz e asr hai takbeer marhaba

  Labbaik kehke taygh rakhi sheh ne niyam mein

  Palti sipaah aayi qayamat jahan mein

  Lo khanjaron mein tayghon mein bhaalon mein ghir gaye

  Tanha hussain barchiyon waalon mein ghir gaye


  12

  Rehaan o sarwar e hazeen suno ye aisa raaz hai

  Hussain keh rahe thay bas khuda pe mujhko naaz hai

  Na bhai na pisar raha na koi chaara saaz hai

  Azaan hai hussain hai khuda e be-niyaaz hai

  Na zeen na zameen par ye aakhri namaz hai


  13

  Jis ke dil mein hussain rehta hai

  Farsh majlis ka jo bichata hai

  Jo bhi nauha kahin sunaata hai

  Le ke naam e hussain rota hai

  Us ko zehra salaam karti hai'
