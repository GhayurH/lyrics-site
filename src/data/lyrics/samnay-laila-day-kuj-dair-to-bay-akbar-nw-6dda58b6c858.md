Samnay Laila Day Kuj Dair To Bay Akbar

Main Tarasgaiyan Kadhi Maa Aakay
Ik Vaar To Keh Akbar

Abbas Gawah Hay Mera, Nahi Naam Lawangi Tera
Menu Akbar Nay Maa Keha Hay
Mera Waada Hay Akbar

Har Saal Banoundi Raiyan, Sochan Ch Sajandi Raiyan
O Saal Atharan Day Cholay
Tu Vekh Lavin Akbar

Sughara Nu Dekay Vichora Gal Paalea Maut Da Jora
O Khairi Wapis Nahi Aaye
Jere Maqtal Gaye Akbar

Marjavin Maa Na Javin Na Pyu Nu Maar Mukavin
Tere Bajon Sadhay Khol Kohi
Nahio Rehni Sheh Akbar

Abbas Da Ay Ehsan Ay, Aj Akbar Mera Mehmaan Ay
Zainab Ton Laila Day Tain
Kita Fasila Tay Akbar

Rab Adal Jaanay Da Haal Ay Akbar Ik Wakhra Laal Ay
Maawan Nu Bachray Nahi Labnay
Haye Tere Jaye Akbar
