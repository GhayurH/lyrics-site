1
Rooh e shabbir woh manzar to bata.
Jab huyi shehre madina se judai hogi.
Yeh to zenab hi bata sakti hai.
Laut kar kese madiney may woh aayi hogi.

2
Kar ke Rozey ki taraf cherha yeh boley shabbir
Ham rahey ya na rahey nana huzur.
Hashr takk aap ki ummat ki bhalai hogi.Rooh e shabbir...

3
Lash Akber ki utha laye jo Khemo may Hussain.
Umm e Laila hi bata sakti hai.
Kis Gazab ki woh Qayamat thi jo tutti hogi. Rooh e shabbir...

4
Jaltey khemo may zhulasti huyi sakina masum.
Ae, Falak tu ne yeh kese dekha.
Turbat e zahra madiney may dahalti hogi. Rooh e shabbir...

5
Rakh ke lasho ko kataro may yeh boley shabbir.
Tu jo Razi hai to Ham Razi hai.
Sar e Taslim ke teri yehi marzi hogi. Rooh e shabbir....

6
Ho ke Taiyyar chaley shah jo Maqtal ki taraf.
Yeh sakina ko koi ja dekhe.
Kis Tarah Ghodo ki Tango se woh lipti hogi. Rooh e shabbir...

7
Mujh ko bhi hashr may rakhna Tu Ghulamo may Hussain.
Teri raho may parra hoga kahin.
Aas Ek Nazar e Inayat ki lagai hogi.. Rooh e shabbir...
