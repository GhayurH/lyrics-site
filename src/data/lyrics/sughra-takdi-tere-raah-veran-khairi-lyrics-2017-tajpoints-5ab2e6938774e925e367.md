Sughra Takdi Tere Raah Veran Khairi Write Up

1
Sughra Takdi Tere Raah Veran Khairi Watna Te Kadi Aa Veran.
Bhain Bemar Teri Nu Tenu Mehndi Lawan De Ne Bare Cha Veran.
Qafila Toor Ke Te Muk Gayi Aye Jiwein Zindagi Othe Hi Ruk Gayi Aye.
Menu Mehmal Toon Lathan Da Tera Manzar Jawan Da Nayi Bhulda Veran.
Veer Tehreer Ik Bahana Aye Mere Khat Nu Toon Seene Laona Aye.
Ayho Samjhe Gi Aye Sughra Menu Apne Seene Nal Toon Liya La Veran.

2
Koi Aythe Charag Nayi Balda Haal Chali Gharaan Da Aye Hoya.
Haye Dadi Di Qabar Te Jiwein Rendah Baqiye Wich Aye Hanera Veran.
Sorah Yousuf Tilawat Kar Ke Meri Halat Ajeeb Hondi Aye.
Awein Lagda Aye Watan Te Mera Yousuf Safraan Toon Nayi Murna Veran.
Haye Akbar Aye Kaisi Si Ghurbat Wekhi Sughra Naa Veer Di Turbat.
Naa Hi Sughra Di Qabar Toon Apne Seene Lawan Layi Kadi Aya Veran.
