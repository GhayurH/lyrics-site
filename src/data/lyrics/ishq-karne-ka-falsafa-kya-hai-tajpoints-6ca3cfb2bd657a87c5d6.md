1
Ishq Karne Ka Falsafa Kya Hai?
Pehle Samjho Tu Karbala Kya Hai?
Bolay Be Sheer Jane Do Maidaan.
Main Bataon Ga Hurmala Kya Hai.

2
Jism Mera Hai Khon Bhi Mera.
Tujh Ko Zanjeer Se Howa Kya Hai?
Matam-e-Shah Our Wilaye Ali.
Our Islam Mein Rakha Kya Hai?

3
Hur Bano Gay Tab Hi Tu Samjho Gay.
Khaima-e-Shah Ka Diya Kya Hai?
Dosri Kis Tarha Baney Zahra.
Dosra Our Murtaza Kya Hai?

4
Sajda-e-Mustafa Ne Batlaya.
Ibn-e-Zahra Ka Martaba Kya Hai?
Akbar-o-Mustafa Ali-o-Jarri.
Ab Samajh Aaya Aaina Kya Hai?

5
Jalta Dekha Jo Deep Khaima Ka.
Tab Haqeeqat Khuli Hawa Kya Hai?
Baat Karne Chaley Ho Haider Ki.
Tum Ko Salman Ka Pata Kya Hai?

6
Aik Bhai Mila Hai Dojay Se.
Our Mairaj Mein Howa Kya Hai?
Iss Ko Matam Ki Saf Mein Dekha Nahi.
Yeh Musallay Pe Mangta Kya Hai?

7
Khat Ke Khainchney Se Hi Addo Bhagay.
Ab Samajh Aaya Bawafa Kya Hai?
Bolay Salman Se Yehi Buzar.
Kuch Tu Batlao Murtaza Kya Hai?

8
Tanz Hai Teer Par Yeh Asghar Ka.
Our Hansi Ka Bhi Tarjuma Kya Hai.
Ibn-e-Zahra Ne La Kaha Tab Hi.
Hum Samajh Paye LaIlaha Kya Hai.

9
But Kada Ho Gaya Hai Qasar-e-Yazid.
Bint-e-Haider Ne Yeh Kaha Kya Hai?
Pochta Hai Imam-e-Waqt Raza.
Aye Phophi Jan Faisla Kya Hai?
