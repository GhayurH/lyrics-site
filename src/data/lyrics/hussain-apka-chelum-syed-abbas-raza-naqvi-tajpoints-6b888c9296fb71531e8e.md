1
Hussain Apka Chelum Syed Abbas Raza Naqvi.
Hussain Aap Ka Chehlum Mananay Aayi Hoon.
Sahey Hain Dard Jo Hum Ne Bataney Aayi Hoon.
Tumhare Baad Woh Bhaiyya Sitam Hoye Hum Par.

2
Chalaya Kanton Pe Zalim Ne Marey Hain Pathar.
Nishan Chalon Ke Tum Ko Dikhaney Aayi Hoon.
Rasan Mein Barah Galay They Na Sar Pe Thi Chadar.
Tamasha Kartay They Zalim Hamara Hans Hans Kar.

3
Tamam Rahoon Ke Qissay Sunanay Aayi Hoon.
Mila Hai Ajar-e-Risalat Yeh Kalma Go Se Humein.
Ridaein Loot Lein Sadqay Khajoor Ke Phainkay.
Dilon Mein Bugz Hai Kitna Bataney Aayi Hoon.

4
Kari Thi Dhop Yeh Beemar Gash Mein Aata Tha.
Sina Ki Nook Se Oss Ko Jagaya Jata Tha.
Bacha Ke Betay Ko Tere Sirhaney Aayi Hoon.
Qadam Qadam Pe Tamachay Hain Shimar Ne Marey.

5
Safaidi Baloon Mein Rukhsar Ho Gaye Neelay.
Sakina Mar Gayi Pyasi Bataney Aayi Hoon.
Sakina Kehti Thi Karb-o-Bala Agar Jana.
Mere Padar Ki Lehad Par Do Gul Charha Dena.

6
Rida Main Phool Ke Badlay Charhaney Aayi Hoon.
Likhe Ga Kaisay Yeh Asmee Bhi Dard Ka Manzar.
Yeh Bain Zainab-e-Kubra Ne Jab Kiye Ro Kar.
Main Teri Qabar Pe Khud Ko Giranay Aayi Hoon.
