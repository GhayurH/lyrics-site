1
Farwa Gathri Seene Nal La Lay Qasim Lay Aayan.
Uth Sir Wich Mityaan Pa Lay Shagan Mana Lay Qasim Lay Aayan.
Gathri Nu Khol Farwa Tukray Mila Badan Day.
Imdad Layi Zainab Nu Kool Bula Lay Qasim Lay Aayan.

2
Hathan day tukriyan Nu Aidhay Tukriyan Choon Lab Ke.
Fair Banray Nu Parna Lay Mehndiyan La Lay Qasim Lay Aayan.
Kubra Nayi Jandi Ke Ohh Bewah Ho Gayi Aye.
Sad Meri Dhi Odhay V Waal Khula Lay Qasim Lay Aayan.

3
Maiyyat Te Paan Layi Tu Sehray De Phul E Chun Lay.
Dastar Meri Nu Aidha Kafan Bana Lay Qasim Lay Aayan.
 Saqlain Wain Kita Shah Ne Ke Mar Na Jawan.
Qasim(AS0 Diyan Bhaina Kolon Lash Chupa Lay Qasim Lay Aayan.
