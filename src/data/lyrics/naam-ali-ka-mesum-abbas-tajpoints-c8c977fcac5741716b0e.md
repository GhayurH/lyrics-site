Bara Pyara Lagta Hai Naam Ali Ka.

1
Mera Tu Wazeefa Hai Naam Ali Ka.

2
Chand Par Bhi Likha Hai Naam Ali Ka.
Oss Ko Pukaro Jo Hai Rab Ka Bhi Pyara.
Totay Dilon Ka Jo Hai Wahid Sahara.
Utra Tha Jis Ke Dar Pe Arsh Se Tara.
Ghamo Se Kinara Hai Naam Ali Ka.
Nam-e-Ali Tu Sab Ki Bigri Bana De.
Kashti Bhanwar Mein Ho Tu Paar Laga De.
Girtay Howon Ko Thamay Jeena Sikha De.
Ali Pe Hi Jachta Hai Naam Ali Ka.
Kaisay Ghamo Se Main Ne Khud Ko Bachaya.
Kis Tarha Mushkilon Se Daman Choraya.
Nam-e-Ali Ka Togra Ghar Mein Lagaya.
Kaisa Pyara Togra Hai Naam Ali Ka.
Rehta Khiyalon Mein Hai Dil Ka Makeen Hai.
Kabhi Kabhi Lagta Hai Woh Tu Yahin Hai.
Mehsos Hota Hai Dikhta Nahi Hai.
Mere Sath Rehta Hai Naam Ali Ka.
Naam Ali Ka Dard Mitaye Sab Ki Mushkil Talay.
Rab Ne Bana Ke Sari Dunya Kar Di Ali Ke Hawalay.
Naam Ali Ka Kar Ke Wazeefa Khul Gaye Aqal Ke Talay.
Taat Ke Bistar Par Sota Hai Sare Jahan Ko Palay.
Momin Ka Tarana Ali Ali.
Hikmat Ka Khazana Ali Ali.
Har Aik Zamana Ali Ali.
Narah Mardana Ali Ali.
Namo Mein Pyara Ali Ali.
Sardar Hamara Ali Ali.
Nam-e-Ali Hai Mazhar, Meesum Ka Narah.
Salman Our Qanbar Ka Wird Yehi Tha.
Aaqa Se Nokar Ka Hai Rishta Porana.
Rab Se Milata Hai Naam Ali Ka.
