Sar-e-Hussain Kata Hai Jiss Ibteda Ke Liye.
Hai Be Rida Sar-e-Zainab Oss Inteha Ke Liye.

1
Bataya Hur Ne Yeh Parh Kar Hussain Ka Kalma.
Keh Ya Hussain Zarori Hai La Illah Ke Liye.
Charha Ke Bano Ne Nani Si Aastino Ko.
Sanwara Hai Ali Asghar Ko Bhi Waga Ke Liye.

2
Abhi Hai Khaimay Ke Dar Par Khari Hoi Zainab.
Abhi Na Halq Pe Khanjar Chala Khuda Ke Liye.
Sakina Teri Khamoshi Peh Hai Bapa Kohram.
Tars Raha Hai Yeh Zindaan Teri Sada Ke Liye.

3
Pisar Ke Seene Se Haye Wo Khainch Kar Barchi.
Uthana Shah Ka Hathoon Ko Wo Dua Ke Liye.
 Navaid Sani-e-Zahra Se Maang Un Ka Karam.
Safina Chahiye Iss Qulzam-e-Sana Ke Liye.
