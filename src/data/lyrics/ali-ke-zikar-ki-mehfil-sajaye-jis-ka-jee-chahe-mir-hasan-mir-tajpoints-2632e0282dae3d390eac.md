Ali Ke Zikar Ki Mehfil Sajaye Jis Ka Jee Chahe Mir Hasan Mir.
Kamaye Khuld Jitni Jee Mein Aaye Jis Ka Jee Chahe.

1
Karey Matam Ubharey Naqsh Matam Ke Sar-e-Seena.
Khud Apne Hath Se Soraj Banaye Jis Ka Jee Chahe.
Jo Hoga Mard Khaybar Mein Alam Paye Ga Kal Woh Hi.
Nabi Tu Keh Chukay Ab Tilmilaye Jis Ka Jee Chahe.

2
Alam Abbas Ka Aali Nasab Insaan Uthatay Hain.
Yeh Woh Parcham Nahi Aaye Uthaye Jis Ka Jee Chahe.
Ali Ke Naam Ka Narah Laga Kar Chehray Parh Parh Kar.
Sar-e-Mehfil Nasab Namay Sunaye Jis Ka Jee Chahe.

3
Kiya Hai Khalq Jis Ne Aag Ko Oss Ki Ijazat Hai.
Ali Ke Dushmano Ka Jee Jalaye Jis Ka Jee Chahe.
Lahu Pakeeza Hoga Jan Chut Jaye Gi Har Gham Se.
Azadari Ka Nuskha Aazmaye Jis Ka Jee Chahe.

4
Najaf Hai Yeh Nikal Bahir Yeh Woh Basti Nahi Waiz.
Chala Aaye Jahan Par Munh Uthaye Jis Ka Jee Chahe.
Wila Ke Noor Mein Gohar Ki Tarha Ghusal Farma Kar.
Sada Soraj Ki Tarha Jagmagaye Jis Ka Jee Chahe.
