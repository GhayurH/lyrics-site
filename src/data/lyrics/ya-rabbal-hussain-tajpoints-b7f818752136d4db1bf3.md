1
Ya Rab Tu Mujhe Zair-e-Shabir Bana De.
Aankhon Ko Meri Roza-e-Shabir Dikha De.
Aasaan Nahi Majawari-e-Roza-e-Shabir.
Pakeeza Laho Ho Tu Bana Karti Hai Taqdeer.

2
Zikar-e-Hussain Mera Lahu Paak Bana De.
Ya Rab Ho Pori Maut Se Pehle Meri Hasrat.
Main Choo Sakoon Shah Ki Turbat.
Yeh Sar Mera Shabir Ki Chokhat Pe Jhuka De.

3
Zikar-e-Hussain Hi Hai Malaik Ki Ibadat.
Woh Aa Ke Azadar Ki Karte Hain Hifazat.
Mujh kam Naseeb Ko Tu Yeh Aizaz Dila De.
Mehshar Mein Azadar Ka Dekho Gaye Yeh Mansab.

4
Daman-e-Alam Sar Pe Tu Kausar Mein Hoon Gaye Lab.
Yeh Woh Hai Jise Madar-e-Shabir Dua De.
Jab Shimar Sheh-e-Deen Ka Gala Kaat Raha Tha.
Chilati Thin Zainab Wa-Hussaina Mera Bhaiya.

5
Zalim Tu Mere Bhai Ko Ik Bar Dikha De.
Manzar Woh Bhula Sakti Nahi Shah Ki Dukhtar.
Dobar Sar-e-Naiza Uthaya Gaya Asghar.
Bas Kehti Thi Asghar Se Mujhe Koi Mila De.

6
Wadah Hai Yeh Zawar Sheh-e-Karb-o-Bala Ka.
Janat Hai Samar Majlis-o-Matam Mein Baka Ka.
Zair Tu Mere Naam Ke Aagay Bhi Laga De.
Jhuk Jaye Gar Hussain Ki Chokhat Pe Koi Sar.

7
Pa Jaye Hur Si Manzilat Fitrus Ki Tar Par.
Aye Sibt-e-Nabi Khon Ke Ejaz Dikha De.
Bas Mustafa Ne Kufar Se Alim Ko Nikala.
Our Panjtan Ne Parcham-e-Islam Sanbhala.

8
Ye Rab Mujhe Bhi Sahib-e-Aakhir Se Mila De.
Dekha Hai Hasan Mojza Iss Door Ke Andar.
Har Saal Barhta Rehta Hai Logon Ka Samandur.
Tum Aao Chalo Koi Yeh Hum Ko Bhi Nida De.
