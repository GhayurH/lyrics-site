Tera Kalma Gowan Baba Minu Bemar Banaya
Teri Zahra Nu Lokaan Darbar Bola Ke Haye Bohat Rawaya
Mere Bohay Utton Tur Ke Ewein Guzrey Loki
Kadi Pehlu Taun Apne Main Sajdiyaan Wich V Haye Hath Nahin Chaya
Tere Badoon Main Samajh Gayi Kinu Kehnday Peshi
Tere Shehar De Lokaan Ne Mainu Pak Madine Wich Sham Wakhaya
Jay Esayi Oh Mubahlay Walay Aithay Honday
Main Mar Jandi Baba Jadon Kalma Gowaan Dar Mere Te Giraya
Mainu Hakim Ayho Aakhay Tainu Masla Bhuliya
Iss Gal Ne Zahra Nu Main Deen Di Waris Nu Mar Mukaya
Piyo Di Turbat Uttay AKBAR Ayho Aakhay Bibi
Mainu Dhiyaan Te Putraan Aj Waang Yateema Haye Seene Lagaya
