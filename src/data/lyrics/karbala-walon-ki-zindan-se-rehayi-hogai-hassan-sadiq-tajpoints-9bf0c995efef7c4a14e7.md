1
Karbala Walon Ki Zindan Se Rehayi Hogai Hassan Sadiq.
Turbat-e-Bali Sakina Se Judai Ho Gayi.
Iss Qadar Marey Tamachay Haye Shimar-eeBadkar Ne.
Hashar Tak Woh Maut Baba Ki Rolai So Gayi.

2
Momino Munh Ko Chupa Lo Noch Dalo Sar Ke Baal.
Sar Barehna Dasht Mein Zahra Ki Jayi Ho Gayi.
Misal Pholon Ke Chunay They Lash Ke Tukray Hussain.
Iss Qadar Pamal Zahra Ki Kamai Ho Gayi.

3
Asghar-e-Masoom Ka Woh Muskurana Maut Par.
Hashar Tak Sharminda Darya Ki Tarai Ho Gayi.
Sar Kata Gulzar Jab Mushkil Kusha Ke Lal Ka.
Ta Qayamat Deen Ki Mushkil Kushai Ho Gayi.
