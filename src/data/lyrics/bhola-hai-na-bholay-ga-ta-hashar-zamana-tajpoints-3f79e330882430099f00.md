Bhoola hai na bhoolega ta hashr zamaana
Darbaar-e-Yazeedi meiṅ tera khutba sunaana

۱
Tere parde pe qurbaan tere bhai ke sadqe
Aata hai jise deen pe ghar baar lutaana

۲
Shabbir ne ye dars zamaane ko diya hai
Aage kisi zaalim ke kabhi sar na jhukaana

۳
Hurmal ye tera jurm kabhi chhup nahiṅ sakta
Paani ke ewaz teer se Asghar ko sulaana

۴
Shabbir ki azmat hai ye Shabbir ki jurrat
Maqtal meiṅ wo Akbar ke laashe ko uthaana

۵
Mushtaq kiye ja tu isi dar ki ghulaami
Bakhshish ka nahiṅ aisa koi aur thikaana
