1
Zahra Nu Ali Ne Musalmana Toun Chup Chup Ke Dafnaya.
Aapay Raat Nu Qabar Bana Ke Khud Aap Nishan Mitaya.
Aye Soch Ke Ali Ne Qabran Banaiyaan Chali Be-Hurmati Na Howay.
Taiyon Shehar De Lokaan Kolon Haye Dafan Da Waqt Chupaya.

2
Ik Hath De Naal Bibi Chaki Chalandi Rayi Aye Bachre Palendi Rayi Aye.
Jairay Din Da Boha Digya Nayi Hath Pasay Toan Chaya.
Fizza Aye Wain Paye Zahra Nu Kinj Bachaye Darwaza Jal Riya Aye.
Ossay Balday Bohay Utton Haye Zalim Turda Aaya.

3
Mula Nu Ayho Fikran Taboot Naal Kidray Zainab V Aa Na Jaway.
Baqiye Taein Mur Mur Baba Bas Ghar Wal Takda Aaya.
 Saqlain Di Dua Aye Har Matami Nu Mula Apna Tu Dar Wakha De.
Bibi Pak De Pursay De Wich Ro Ro Ke Wasta Paya
