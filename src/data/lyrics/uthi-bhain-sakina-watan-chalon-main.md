Shaam diyan qaidan chutyan
Sajjad qafila ghin ke Sakina di mazar te aaye
Aa ke dowain hath bhain de mazar ke rakhyan
Rat ro ke Syed aey piya aadha aey

1
Uthi bhain Sakina watan jaloon
May Shaam di qaid nibhai waindan
Makoon maaf karain pardes de wich
Teri kalli qabar banai waindan

2
Matan samjhain mailay cholay da
Maikon kafan pawaya Abid ne
Azad thiwan de baad wi may
Poshak purani paye waindan

3
Das veer maikoon kadan watan vaisoon
Har roz jo puchdi han maikoon
Teko dasan aayan tedi turbat tay
Kul qaidi watan walai waindan

4
Tera choota veer te Karbal wich
Thi teer de naal shaheed gaya
Sughra koon dikhawan de kitay
Asghar day cholay chai waindan

5
Mureed Sajjad ne rat ro ke
Mangi rukhsat sain Sakina toon
Hamsheer bahoon majboor haan may
Gal aakhri qabar koon laye waindan
