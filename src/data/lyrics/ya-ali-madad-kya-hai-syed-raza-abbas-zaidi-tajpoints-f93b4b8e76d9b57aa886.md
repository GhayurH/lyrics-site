1
Ya Ali Madad Kya Hai Syed Raza Abbas Zaidi.
Ya Ali Ya Murtaza Shair-e-Khuda.
Hai Mera Maula Mera Mushkil Kusha.
Jo Kahey Ya Ali Kya Hai.
Oss Ke Eman Ki Sand Kya Hai.
Khud Parhi Mustafa Ne Nad-e-Ali.
Phir Bhi Kehta Hai Mustanad Kya Hai.
Hai Khuda Ka Bhi Aik Naam Ali.
Aye Muqassar Ab Iss Ki Rad Kya Hai.
Hum Barha Detey Hain Agar Had Se.
Tum Bata Do Ali Ki Had Kya Hai.
Hai Attaye Hussain Mera Hunar.
Phir Bhi Mujh Se Tujhe Hasad Kya Hai.
Sath Jab Hai Charag-e-Hub-e-Ali.
Khoof-e-Tareeki-e-Lehad Kya Hai.
Apni Chadar Mein Paon Rakhta Hoon.
Janta Hoon Ke Mera Qad Kya Hai.
Dekho Hur Our Hurmala Ka Chalan.
Jan Lo Naik Kya Hai Bad Kya Hai.
Pocho Ansar-e-Sheh Se Pocho Sohail.
Maut Kiya Cheez Hai Shehad Kya Hai.
