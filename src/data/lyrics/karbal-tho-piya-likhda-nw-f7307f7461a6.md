Karbal Tho Piya Likhda, Hun Waqt Riya Koi Nai, Biya Kia Mei Likhaan.

Qasim Da Jo Nay Likhya Oh Ku Banara Banaa Baytha
Udeh Sehreh Saja Baytha Udeh Shagan Mana Baytha
Tedee Bhayna De Hathan Tay Hun Rang-e-Hina Ko Nai Biya Kia Mei Likhaan.

Lug Nazar Gayi Ujrii Tendeh Yusaf-e-Sani Koon
Umat Ne Hai Raj Lutya Akbar Di Jawani Koon
Tendiya Phupiya Te Mawaan Da Sajaad Siwa Koi Nai Biya Kia Mei Likhaan.

May Jawan Mara Baytha Meh Nadaan Koha Baytha
Habib Mazahir Jay Mehman Mara Baytha
Inj Khoon De Wich Tar Haan Sajde Di Jaga Koi Nai Biya Kia Mei Likhaan.
