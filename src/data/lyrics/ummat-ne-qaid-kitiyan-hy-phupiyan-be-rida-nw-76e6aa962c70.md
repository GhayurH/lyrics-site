Ummat Ne Qaid Kitiyan, Hy Phupiyan Be Rida, Taiyon Abid Rut Roya
Bardaash Kar Na Sakeya, Ghairat Da Ay Khuda

Kitthay Majma Layinaa Da, Kithay Zahra Diyan Palliyan
Muu Waalaan De Nal Kaj Ke, Bazaran De Wich Kharriyan
Mungdi Rayi Burke Ro Ro, Konain Di Malka

Zainab De Khutbay Sun Ke, Hasde Si Shaami Saaray
Tohfay Samajh Ke Patthar, Jadon Qaidiyan Nu Maaray
Baalan Nu Zakhmi Kitta, Be Jurm Be Khata

Sir Nangay Rulaya Ay, Kul Nabbiyan Di Ghairat Nu
Kyun Kalmagoan Kitta, Sharminda Sharafat Nu
Naizay Te Rahi Ay Rondi, Abbas Di Wafa

Enjh Shimr Kaminay Ne, Ik Zulm Kamaya Ay
Sung Ghoray Se Safraan’ch, Sakina Nu Dauraya Ay
Chaoon Saalan Di Ujri Da, Koi Kitta Na Haya

Sajjad Muhari Nu, Ro Kehndiyan Masrooran
Kitthay Jaiye Di Saansaa Kun, Zakhmaan De Naal Chooraan
Kalli Fizza Saareya Nu, Kevain Devey Hosla

Sajjad Ne Vekhe Hin, Akhtar Oh Zulm Saray
Kevain Shimar Layin Koray, Aydhi Paak Kund Te Maaray
Fir Vi Na Mu Chon Nikli, Ummat Layi Baddua
