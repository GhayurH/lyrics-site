1
Sajjad Muhari Akhiyaan Choon Khon Ronda Wain Aye Pa Ke.
Phophiyaan Nu Baaj Rida Takda Na Mar Janda Phal Barchi Da Kha Ke.
Veer Koi Na Riya Qaid Zindan Andar Haye Ghurbat Aidi.
Bas Kaliyaan Ro Ro Ke Dil De Haal Sunaway Kariyaan Nu Gal La Ke.

2
Tauq Zanjeeraan Nayi Ainu Beemar Kita Hai Zakhm Ghairat Da.
Ismat De Kabay Te Pathar Wasaye Lokaan Wich Bazar Liya Ke.
Baap Choon Ro Ro Ke Hai Sakina So Gayi Na Koi Wain Karey.
Rat Akhiyaan Choon Ronda Dasda Aye Phophiyaan Nu Bhain Da Lasha Cha Ke.

3
Wekh Ke Bachray Nu Saang Taun Kainda Riya Ayho Shabeer Da Sar.
Sajjad Teri Qismat Phophiyaan De Naal Tera Rulna Sham Che Aa Ke.
Aye Musalmana Ne Zakhm Aisay Laye Haan Jigar Te Dongay.
Rukya Na Khon Kadi Karbal Taun Le Ke Sabir Aida Qabar Che Ja Ke.
