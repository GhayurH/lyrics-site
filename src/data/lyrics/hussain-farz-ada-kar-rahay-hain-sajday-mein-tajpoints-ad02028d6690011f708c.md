Hussain Farz Ada Kar Rahay Hain Sajday Mein.
Yazidiyat Ko Fana Kar Rahe Hain Sajday Mein.

1
Hussain De Ke Bahatar Saron Ki Qurbani.
Khuda Ka Shukar Ada Kar Rahe Hain Sajday Mein.
Hayat Deen-e-Muhammad Ko Aye Musalmano.
Ba-Waqt-e-Asar Atta Kar Rahe Hain Sajday Mein.

2
Zamane Walo Yeh Sabar-e-Hussain Tu Dekho.
Keh Dushmano Ka Bhala Kar Rahe Hain Sajday Mein.
Laeen Ki Kund Churi Chal Rahi Thi Gardan Par.
Magar Hussain Dua Kar Rahe Hain Sajday Mein.

3
Yeh Ummati Hain Ke Parh Kar Rasool Ka Kalma.
Sar-e-Hussain Juda Kar Rahe Hain Sajday Mein.
 Urooj Khon Se Apne Hussain Ibn-e-Ali.
Zameen Ko Khak-e-Shifa Kar Rahe Hain Sajday Mein.
