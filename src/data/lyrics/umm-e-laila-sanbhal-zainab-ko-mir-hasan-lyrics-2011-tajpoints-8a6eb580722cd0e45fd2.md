1
Umm e Laila Sanbhal Zainab Ko Mir Hasan Mir.
La Raha Hoon Main Lasha-e-Akbar Umm-e-Laila Sanbhal Zainab Ko.
Bolay Hoon Gay Hussain Yeh Ro Kar Umm-e-Laila Sanbhal Zainab Ko.
Nojawaan Lash Hai Zaeefi Hai Meri Nazron Se Dekh Manzar Ko.
Kaisay Humsheer Ko Sanbhalon Ga Main Sanbhalay Hoye Hoon Akbar Ko.
Tera Ahsan Hoga Yeh Mujh Par Umm-e-Laila Sanbhal Zainab Ko.
Iss Ke Seene Se Le Ke Nam-e-Ali Main Ne Barchi Abhi Nikali Hai.
Imtehaan De Chuki Meri Ghurbat Ab Teri Bekasi Ki Bari Hai.
Tu Bhi Iss Waqt Ya Ali Keh Kar Umm-e-Laila Sanbhal Zainab Ko.
Do Nageeney They Iss Ki Jholi Mein Dono Akbar Pe Kar Deiye Sadqa.
Sabar Aisa Keh Apne Beton Par Main Ne Rotay Issay Nahi Dekha.
Hai Magar Ab Yeh Lasha-e-Akbar Umm-e-Laila Sanbhal Zainab Ko.
Rook Rakha Hai Apne Ashkon Ko Sab Se Chup Chup Ke Aahein Bharti Hai.
Main Samjhta Hoon Madar-e-Akbar Teri Mamta Pe Jo Guzarti Hai.
Rakh Ke Apne Kalejay Par Pathar Umm-e-Laila Sanbhal Zainab Ko.
Aisay Lash-e-Jawaan Uthaye Hoon Ayeriyaan Khat Banati Aati Hain.
Inn Nishano Ko Pichey Pichey Mere Amma Zahra Mitati Aati Hain.
Oss Ki Nazrein Na Dekhein Yeh Manzar Umm-e-Laila Sanbhal Zainab Ko.
Aye Takallum Woh Sham-o-Kofa Ka Tey Howa Hoga Iss Tarha Se Safar.
Amma Zainab Sanbhalna Maa Ko Kehte Hoon Gay Sina Se Yeh Akbar.
Our Hoga Hussain Ke Lab Par Umm-e-Laila Sanbhal Zainab Ko
