1
Dil Mera Sooz-e-Nehaan Se Be Mohaba Jal Giya.
Aatish-e-Khamoosh Ke Manind Goya Jal Giya.
Dil Mein Zooq-e-Wasal-o-Yaar Tak Baqi Nahi.
Aag Iss Ghar Mein Lagi Aysi Keh Jo Tha Jal Giya.

2
Main Adam Se Bhi Pare Hoon, Warna Gafil! Barha.
Meri Aah-e-Aatsheein Se Baal-e-Anqa Jal Giya.
Arz Kijiye Johar-e-Andesha Ki Garmi Kahan?
Kuch Khiyal Ayat Tha Wehshat Ka Keh Sehra Jal Giya.

3
Dil Nahi Tujh Ko Dikhta Warna Dagoon Ki Bahaar.
Iss Cheragaan Ka Karoon Kiya Karfarma Jal Giya.
Main Hoon Our Afsurdagi Ki Aarzo, Ghalib Keh Dil.
Dekh Ker Tarz-e-Tapaak-e-Ahl-e-Dunya Jal Giya.
