Suno Akbar Ki Jang Imran Abbas Zawar Ali Raza

1
Ya Rab Mujhe Nafees Sa Unwan Chahiye
Alfaz Mein Taqadus e Quran Chahiye
Nook-e-Qalam Mein Wosat-e-Imkan Chahiye
Idrak Mein Bhi Khaima-e-Imran Chahiye
Mozu Pe Mere Husan Ka Maiyar Dad De
Akbar Main Tab Parhon Ga Karrar Dad De

2
Socho Agar Naqab Ulat Doon Tu Kya Rahe
Phir Dasht-e-Karbala Mein Rahe Tu Khuda Rahe
Ya Phir Hamarey Baba Ki Khiyam Ga Rahe
Ya Phir Batool e Pak Ke Sar Ki Rida Rahe
Kaun-o-Makaan Jo Hiddat e Mehshar Mein Aaein Gay
Hum Teeno Tab Batool Ki Chadar Mein Aaein Gay

3
Akbar Yeh Bolay Marzi-e-Mehshar Khiram Chal
Dil Hi Nahi Keh Khaincho Main Teri Lajam Chal
Youn Chal Keh Murtajiz Tujhey Bhaijay Salam Chal
Mujh Se Hai Izraeel Ko Thora Sa Kam Chal
Tameel e Hukam Maula Basad Naaz Karni Hai
Chalna Hai Dorna Hai Ya Parwaz Karni Hai

4
Hum Ne Khuda Ko Jhuk Keh Bataya Namaz Hai
Hum Oss Ki Nazar Hain Woh Hamari Niyaz Hai
Hum Wo, Woh Hum Hain Kaisay Hai Yeh Bhi Raaz Hai
Jo Sar Hamarey Talwon Pe Hai Sarfaraz Hai
Dil Jis Ka Ho Azan Se Haider Nikal De
Koshish Karey Woh Pehle Keh Akbar Nikal De

5
Pohanchi Jo Wast e Ran Mein Woh Laila Ki Sajdah Ga
Dali Sipah-e-Sham Pe Ain ALLAH Ne Nigah
Woh Roab Tha Nazar Mein Keh ALLAH Ki Panah
Jismo Pe Kapkapi Thi Zabno Pe LaIlaha
Nazron Ka Jaal Itna Bara Phainkta Hai Yeh
Har Aankh Ko Laga Keh Mujhe Dekhta Hai Yeh

6
Akbar Yeh Keh Ke Aagay Barha Ya Ali Madad
Tha Taig Ka Wajood-e-Qaza Ya Ali Madad
Nabiyon Ka Wird Taiz Howa Ya Ali Madad
Had Hai Keh Shamiyon Ne Kaha Ya Ali Madad
Oss Sancha e Nigah Mein Dhal Jati Karbala
Rukh Se Paseena Girta Tu Jal Jati Karbala

7
Dil Hai Saron Ki Fasal Ko Katon Magar Nahi
Oncha Hamarey Talwon Se Koi Bhi Sar Nahi
Hum Sadqa Rook Dein Tu Yeh Shams-o-Qamar Nahi
Hum Mein Tu Kya Uqab Mein Fojon Ka Dar Nahi
Dil Hai Namona Dekho Tu Thoray Se Mar Doon
Talwar Toor Kar Tumhein Ghorey Se Mar Doon

8
Jis Ne Sar-e-Hussain Ki Qeemat Lagai Hai
Qeemat Mein Kya Lagaya Hai Wahdat Lagai Hai
Ya Kibriya Ki Sari Mashiyat Lagai Hai
Ya Phir Jahan-e-Kun Ki Hakomat Lagai Hai
Yeh Sab Hamarey Ghar Ke Gada Gar Keh Pas Hai
Warna Tu Inn Se Qeemati Mera Libas Hai

9
Uthi Nigah-e-Akbar-e-Jarrar Soye Foaj
Phir Izraeel Aane Laga Robaro e Foaj
Jaisay Keh Moot Songhne Nikli Ho Bo-e-Foaj
Lag Sa Gaya Tha Maut Keh Munh Ko Lahoye Foaj
Har Ik Addo Ka Jism Tha Khoon Mein Atta Howa
Har Seena Youn Tha Jaisay Girebaan Phata Howa

10
Dast-e-Asad Mein Taig Keh Dharney Ki Dair Thi
Maidan-e-Jang Mein Bhi Utarney Ki Dair Thi
Akbar Keh Ossay Amar Hi Karne Ki Dair Thi
Dopara Badan Ho Gaye Marne Ki Dair Thi
Shukar-e-Khuda Hai Dasht Mein Aa Kar Tu Bach Gaye
Jibreel Dekhta Tha Mere Par Tu Bach Gaye
