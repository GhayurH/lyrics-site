1
Ro Ro Ke Ali Kyun Letay Hain Zainab Ka Naam Musalay Par.
Kya Yaad Ali Ko Aaya Hai Bazar-e-Sham Musalay Par.
Khoon Jari Tha Zakhmi Sar Se Rote They Namazi Saf Mein Kharay.
Kis Tarha Hasan Ne Ro Ro Kar Ki Fajar Tamam Musalay Par.

2
Kya Maa Ki Tarha Lenay Tum Ko Masjid Mein Chali Aaye Zainab.
Baba Ko Ja Kar Haye Do Fizza Mera Paigham Musalay Par.
Zakhmi Pehlu Jab Yaad Aaya Zakhmi Peshani Bhool Gaya.
Zahra Ki Musibat Par Roya Mazloom Imam Musalay Par.

3
Yeh Pehli Fajar Hai Zainab Ki Sajday Mein Sakoon Milta Hi Nahi.
Dil Dobta Hai Aata Hi Nahi Ik Pal Aaram Musalay Par.
Dewar-e-Kaba Beth Gayi Al-Hamd Lahu Mein Doob Gayi.
Ummat Se Mila Hai Mula Ko Kaisa Inam Musalay Par.

4
Oss Ghairatdar Ki Ghurbat Par Roti Hai Ali Ki Ghurbat Bhi.
Chalees Bars Jo Kehta Raha Asham Asham Musalay Par.
Shabir Ki Janib Se Akbar Qatil Ko Pilaya Tha Sharbat.
Jab Yad Ali Ko Aaya Tha Ik Tashna Kaam Musalay Par.
