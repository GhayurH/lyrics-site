Parhti Hai Ye Kabe Ki Dewaar Qaseeda Tera.
Aik Aik Kar Ke Sare Rishte Chor Rahe The Daman.

1
Waqt-e-Qaza Tha Main Tanha Tha Rukne Lagi Thi Dharkan.
Aye Mula Agar Tum Nahi Aate Maut Se Main Mar Jata.
Iss Se Behtar Hamd-e-Khuda Kya Koi Bhala Likhe Ga.
Jis Ko Ali Sajda Karta Hai Khud Wo Khuda Kya Ho Ga.

2
Kafi Hai Touheed Samajhne Ko Tera Ik Sajda.
Kaba Kahan Tha But Khana Tha To Ne Rak Li Izat.
WO Tu Kahiye Bint-e-Asad Ne Kar Li Khud Zehmat.
Sehan-e-Abu Talib Mein Warna Kaba Chal Kar Aata.

3
Nad-e-Ali Hai Un Ka Wazifa Khaiber-o-Khandaq Lori.
Maut Se Un Ko Dar Nahi Lagta Maut Hai Un Se Darti.
Zikar Se Tere Shair Howa Hai Qaum Ka Bacha Bacha.
Ishq-e-Ali Se Zainbia. Aazaad Karaya Oss Ne.

4
Kya Hain Ali Ke Nokar Ye Dunya Ko Bataya Oss Ne.
Jald Kahe Gi Qabza Kar Ke Dunya Par Hizbullah.
Kaise Hain Ye Dewane Kyun In Ko Nahi Lagta Dar.
Maut Bhi Thi Sakte Main Keh Akhir Kya Hain Ali Ke Nokar.

5
Dekh Ke Apni Maut Ko Jab Yeh:Sibt-e-Jaffar: Bola.
Masjid Mein Jab Naam-e-Khuda Par Bheek Kissi Ne Mangi.
Hath Barha Kar Dast-e-Khuda Ne Apni Angothi De Di.
 Akbar Sail Se Pehle Ye Rab-e-Akbar Bola.
