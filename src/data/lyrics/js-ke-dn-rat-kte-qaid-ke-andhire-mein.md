Jis ke din raat katay qaid ke andhairay may
Pursa denay usay Zehra bhi to aayi ho gi

1
Haey pardes may Ruqqaya bhi bain karti ho gi
Aur Zainab bhi bulayee hogi

2
Jis nai zanjeer utaaray teray rasm-e-Sajaad nibhanay waalay
Koyi apna to teray paas na tha, aur mayyat teri mazdooron nay uthayee hogi.

3
Shaam sai aayi Sakina ujri apnay baba ko bhi na rona saki
Jis kai pairon main aablay hongay, aur woh roti ho gi shaamiyon ki woh satayee hogi.

4
Zehar angooron jab bhar kai diya aik zaailm nai meray mola ko
Musa Kazim kai paak seenay main, zehar harooni nay hy kya aag lagayee hogi.

5
Qaidee Imam kai janazay main apni nazrain jhuka kay pursa do
Aik aik aadaa taboot ko andar, aaj jo royai ga, us ki mehshar main rasayee hogi.
