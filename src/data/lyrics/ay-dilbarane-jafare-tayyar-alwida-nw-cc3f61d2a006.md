Ay Dilbarane Jafare Tayyar Alwida

binte ali ye bachon se kehti thi baar baar

ran ko sidharo auno mohamed ye maa nisar

ay dilbarane jafare….

jafar ka ifteqar badana jihad may

nana ki apni shan dikhana jihad may

ay dilbarane jafare….

shere khuda ki beti ke tum dono sher ho

maidan may jao fatha may ab kuch na dayr ho

ay dilbarane jafare….

har baat apne dil ke siwa ho to baat hai

wo ran pade ke hashr bapa ho to baat hai

ay dilbarane jafare….

ghayre rahe sipah magar moo na modna

hyder ki yaadgar ho maidan na chorna

ay dilbarane jafare….

shimro umar ke sar jo utaro to khush hoo mai

dushman ko jhoom jhoom ke maaro to khush hoo mai

ay dilbarane jafare….

ab to ye aarzoo hai ke tum ran may kaam aao

chehre ke badle khoon bhare chehre mujhe dikhao

ay dilbarane jafare….

bacho zaeef mamu ko zehmat na deejiyo

laashe uthaaye bhai to jumbish na kijiyo

ay dilbarane jafare….

pyase jo aaoge to sila degi tum ko maa

khud daud kar gale se laga legi maa

ay dilbarane jafare….
