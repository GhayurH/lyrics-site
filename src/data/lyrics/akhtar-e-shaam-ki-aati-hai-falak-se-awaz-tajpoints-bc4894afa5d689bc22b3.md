Sajdah Karti Hai Sahar Jis Ko Wo Hai Aaj Ki Raat.
Rah-e-Yaak Gaam Hai Himat Ke Liye Arsh-e-Bareen.
Keh Rahi Hai Ye Musalman Se Mairaj Ki Raat.
