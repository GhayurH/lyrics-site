1
Re Manzar Samne Nazraan De.
Sajjad De Dil Wich Dard Re.
Har Wailay Sham De Safraan De.
Tasheer Bazar Che Hondi Aye.
Payi Zainab Sham Che Aandi Aye.
Zainab Tu Pehlaan Aa Pohanche Ne.
Sham Che Qafile Khabran De.
Dukh Dard Kataan Layi Abid De.
Saah Our Wadhaan Layi Abid De.
Abid De Samne Mawaan Ne.
Kadi Naam Le Naa Putraan De.
Ayontaan Tu Digde Balaan Layi.
Jehri Ayontaan Aaap Banaiyaan San.
Sajjad Patay Piya Puchda Aye.
Ohna Chotiyaan Chotiyaan Qabraan De.
Aye Nayi Dasdi Kina Laho Wagiya.
Keri Bibi Da Ki Haal Hoya.
Tareekh Bas Ayho Dasdi Aye.
Munh Pij Gay Wasday Pathraan De.
Naize Jadoon Chadraan Landay San.
Doo Pasay Zakhm Pay Aande San.
Sir Zakhmi Ho Gay Bibiyaan De.
Dil Zakhmi Ho Gay Chadraan De.
 Akbar Har Zakhm Nu Sehn Layi.
Kandiyaan Te Turda Rehn Layi.
Hath Bajiyaan Nazaan Paliyaan Ne.
Hath Khool Dite San Sabraan De.
