1
Aye Sheh-e-Mazloom Aye Sibt-e-Payamber Alwida.
Aye Shaheed Karbala Zahra Ke Dilbar Alwida.
Hai Azza Khana Yeh Dil Matam Tera Dharkan Mein Hai.
Tera Gham Subho Masa Har Lehza Rooh-o-Tan Mein Hai.
Kushta-e-Joor-o-Jafa Aye Haq Ke Mazhar Alwida.
Aye Shehanshah-e-Wafa Dast-e-Buredah Aslam.
Taat-o-Aysar Ke Zindah Qaseeda Aslam.
Saqi-e-Tashna Dahan Shair-e-Dilawar Alwida.
Aye Jawan-e-Hashmi Aye Saf Shikan Tum Par Salam.
Rahat-e-Jaan Laila-o-Shah-e-Zaman Tum Par Salam.
Aye Qateel-e-Nainawa Aks-e-Payamber Alwida.
Jeetay Jee Tukray Howa Jis Ka Badan Oss Par Salam.
Aik Gathri Mein Bandha Tha Jis Ka Tan Oss Par Salam.
Aye Hasan Ke Gulbadan Farwa Ke Dilbar Alwida.
Lo Salam Aoun-o-Muhammad Haider-o-Jafar Ke Shair.
Kamsini Mein Kar Dikhaya Nakhwat-e-Batil Ko Zair.
Sani-e-Zahra Ke Aye Nanhay Sipa Gar Alwida.
Aye Qateel-e-Teer-e-Seh Shoba Ali Asghar Salam.
Aye Seham-e-Lab Kusha Aye Fatah-e-Lashkar Salam.
Aye Sheh-e-Mazloom Ke Masoom Askar Alwida.
Basta Shud Zanjeer-o-Tauq-e-Khar Ho Tum Par Salam.
Ibtalao Ranj-o-Gham Ke Rah Rao Tum Par Salam.
Sabir-o-Shakir Pyam-e-Haq Ke Mazhar Alwida.
Aye Hazeena Mubtalaye Be Ridai Aslam.
Fatah-e-Sham-o-Halab Zahra Ki Jayi Aslam.
Aye Binaye Farsh-e-Matam Sheh Ki Khwahar Alwida.
Ya Rabab-e-Khasta Tan Kulsoom-o-Laila Aslam.
Ya Ruqaiya-o-Sakina Umme Farwa Aslam.
Qafila-e-Be Kasaan Be Maqna-o-Chadar Alwida.
Aslam Ibn-e-Mazahir, Aousja, Dawood-o-Jaun.
Aslam Aye Wahab-e-Kalbi Jafar-o-Hamza-o-Aoun.
Kushta Gaan-e-Rah-e-Haq Aye Sheh Ke Lashkar Alwida.
Phir Azza Khane Sajein Gay Ja Ba Ja Hoga Yeh Gham.
Yeh Jaloos-o-Majlisein Jari Rahein Gi Aye Najam.
Deta Hai Pursa Asad Jan-e-Payamber Alwida.
