1
Kissi Mein Dam Nahi Itna Sar-e-Shehar-e-Sitam Nikle.
Zameeron Ki Sada Par Bhi Na Tum Nikle Na Hum Nikle.
Karam Ki Aik Yeh Sorat Bhi Ho Sakti Hai Maqtal Mein.
Teri Talwar Bhi Chalti Rahe Our Khoon Bhi Kam Nikle.

2
Na Main Waiz Na Main Zahid Mareez-e-Ishq Hoon Saqi.
Mujhe Kya Uzzar Peene Mein Agar Seene Se Gham Nikle.
Muhabbat Mein Yeh Dil Azariyaan Achi Nahi Hotin.
Jisse Dekho Teri Mehfil Se Woh Ba-Chashm-e-Nam Nikle.

3
Sitaro Se pare Koi Humein Awaz Deta Hai.
Kahin Aisa Na Ho Saein Wahan Se Bhi Sanam Nikle.
Meri Har Baat Par Hanste Howay Kehte Hain Wo Baqi.
Tera Arman Tu Shayad Kabhi Aglay Janam Nikle.
