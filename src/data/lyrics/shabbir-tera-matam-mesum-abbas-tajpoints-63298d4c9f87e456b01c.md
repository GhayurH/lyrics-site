1
Mazloom-e-Karbala.
Zabeeh-e-Karbala.
Qateel-e-Nainawa.
Syedaniyaan Karti Hain Shabeer Tera Matam.
Aye Waris-e-Quranam Aye Shah-e-Shaheedanam.
Be Sar Hain Sabhi Lashay Pamali Ki Zad Par Hain.
Jalti Hoi Reti Par Sadaat Khulay Sar Hain.
Our Noha Kuna Aalam Aye Shah-e-Shaheedanam.
Naizon Ka Ghana Jangal Our Raat Ka Sanata.
Pehrey Pe Nabi Zadi Karti Hai Yehi Noha.
La Char Hoye Ab Hum Aye Shah-e-Shaheedanam.
Beton Ko Nahi Roti Ghamkhuwar Teri Khwahar.
Ghazi Ko Bulati Hai Ik Hath Rakhay Dil Par.
Be Chain-o-Dil Afgaram Aye Shah-e-Shaheedanam.
Ab Lootney Aate Hain Sadaat Ko Yeh Zalim.
Kis Darjah Rolatay Hain Sadaat Ko Yeh Zalim.
Ab Jaein Kahan Purgham Aye Shah-e-Shaheedanam.
Haq Teri Muhabbat Ka Kulsoom Ada Karti.
Aoulad Agar Hoti Tu Tujh Pe Fida Karti.
Karti Hai Bapa Paihum Aye Shah-e-Shaheedanam.
Baba Hain Kahan Mere Hain Bain Sakina Ke.
Mar Jaon Gi Ro Ro Ke Bin Shah-e-Madina Ke.
Kya Iss Ko Bataein Hum Aye Shah-e-Shaheedanam.
Be Pardah Behan Teri Bazar Mein Jaye Gi.
Amma Ki Tarha Zainab Darbar Mein Jaye Gi.
Kya Iss Se Bara Hai Gham Aye Shah-e-Shaheedanam.
Humsheer Rasan Basta Jab Qaid Mein Jaye Gi.
Zindah Mein Tera Matam Ik Roz Karaye Gi.
Be Monis-o-Be Yaram Aye Shah-e-Shaheedanam.
Jee Bhar Ke Kiya Matam Abbas-e-Dilawar Ka.
Qasim Ali Asghar Ka Humshakal-e-Payamber Ka.
Ta Hashar Karein Gay Hum Aye Shah-e-Shaheedanam.
Yeh Mazhar-o- Meesum Ki Din Raat Azadari.
Manzoor Karein Zahra Yeh Nohon Ki Teyari.
Ik Pal Bhi Nahi Ho Kam Aye Shah-e-Shaheedanam.
