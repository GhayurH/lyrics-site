1
Sab Ko Dhoka Hai Aa Raha Hoon Main
Aa Raha Hoon Na Ja Raha Hoon Main.
Khokhala Hota Ja Raha Hai Wajood.
Khud Ko Andar Se Kha Raha Hoon Main.

2
Jis Se Milna Hai Jaisay Matlab Ka.
Waisa Chehra Bana Raha Hoon Main.
Mujh Se Miliye Tu Yeh Khiyal Rahe.
Aap Ke Kam Aa Raha Hoon Main.

3
Taiz Ro Sansein Sust Ro Dharkan.
Darmiyaan Larkhara Raha Hoon Main.
Bolta Ja Raha Hai Dil Mein Koi.
Dair Se Sunta Ja Raha Hoon Main.

4
Nafs-e-Sarkash Pe Kar Raha Hoon Kam.
Janwar Ko Sadha Raha Hoon Main.
Jhankta Phir Raha Hoon Logon Mein.
Jaisay Dunya Chala Raha Hoon Main.

5
Jab Makeen Ghar Ke Sare Andhay Hain.
Kis Ko Kis Se Chupa Raha Hoon Main.
Jane Kiya Khal-o-Khad Hoye Akbar.
Aainay Toray Ja Raha Hoon Main.
