Haye Yeh Bachi Kaun Hai, Yeh Tho Sakina Lagti Hai

Aati Hai Maqtal Ki Taraf
Dekha Hai Kyun Sue-e-Najaf
Kiya Iska Dada Hai Ali
Yeh Tho Sakina Lagti Hai

Aati Hai Jo Roti Huee
Sadmon Say Jaan Khoti Huee
Pardh Thi Huee Nad-e-Ali
Yeh Tho Sakina Lagti Hai

Kyun Ro Kay Jaan Dethi Hai Yeh
Kyun Siskiyan Lethi Hai Yeh
Kya Margaya Iska Kohi
Yeh Tho Sakina Lagti Hai

Kanon Say Kyun Khoon Hai Rawan
Uff Yeh Tamachon Kay Nishan
Yeh Zulm Aur Yeh Kamsini
Yeh Tho Sakina Lagti Hai

Kausar Yeh Maqtal Ki Faza
Tarpa Rahi Hai Dil Mera
Taari Hai Gham Ki Teeragi
Yeh Tho Sakina Lagti Hai
