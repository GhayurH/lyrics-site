Amma Achi Amma Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics

1
Dakhil Jo Madine Mein Hoye Ahl-e-Madina.
Jate Hoye Kuch Our They Ab Kya Hai Qareena.
Na Akbar-o-Abbas Na Humrah Sakina.
Our Gham Se Har Aik Bibi Ka Labraiz Tha Seena.
Jab Ghar Ke Qareen Aa Gayi Kunbay Ki Sawari.
Sughra Ne Jo Dekha Tu Yeh Ro Ro Ke Pukari.

2
Amma Achi Amma Mera Kunba Kahan Hai.
Amma Achi Amma Mera Baba Kahan Hai.
Sughra Kya Poch Liya Sughra Tu Ne Sab Kuch Tu Ayyaan Hai Chehray Se.
Le Dekh Safaid Yeh Baal Mere Mat Poch Kahan Hai Ab Kunba.
Sughra Jisay Jan Se Pyara Kehti Thi Din Jis Ke Liye Tu Ginti Thi.
Be Goor-o-Kafan Hai Baba Tera Mat Poch Kahan Hai Ab Kunba.

3
Sughra Jis Kunbay Ko Tu Pochti Hai Woh Karb-o-Bala Ki Khak Howa.
Sab Chand Chupay Karbal Mein Tere Mat Poch Kahan Hai Ab Kunba.
Sughra Woh Aik Zaeef Jawan Jo Hai Pehchan Issay Sajjad Hai Yeh.
Issay Sham Ki Galiyaan Mar Gayin Mat Poch Kahan Hai Ab Kunba.
Sughra Woh Asghar Jo Chey Mah Ka Tha Woh Bhi Na Raha Woh Bhi Na Raha.
Maa Dhop Mein Bethi Roti Hai Mat Poch Kahan Hai Ab Kunba.

4
Sughra Jab Sham-e-Ghareebaan Aayi Thi Zainab Pe Bari Tanhai Thi.
Ghar Jalta Tha Chinti Thi Rida Mat Poch Kahan Hai Ab Kunba.
Sughra Darti Thi Andherey Se Jo Bohat Woh Bali Sakina Teri Bahan.
Ab Tak Hai Woh Zindaan Mein Sughra Mat Poch Kahan Hai Ab Kunba.
ALLAH Sughra Se Utha Hoga Kyunkar Aye Sarwar Aye Rehan Yeh Gham.
Jis Waqt Phophi Ne Ro Ke Kaha Mat Poch Kahan Hai Ab Kunba.
