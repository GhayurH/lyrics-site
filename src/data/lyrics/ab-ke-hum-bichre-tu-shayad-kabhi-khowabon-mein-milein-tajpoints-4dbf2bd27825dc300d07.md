1
Ab Ke Hum Bichre Tu Shayad Kabhi Khowabon Mein Milein.
Jiss Tarha Sokhe Howay Phool Kitaboon Mein Milein.
Dhoond Ujray Howay Logon Mein Wafa Ke Moti.
Yeh Khazane Tujhe Mumkin Hai Kharabon Mein Milein.

2
Gham-e-Dunya Bhi Gham-e-Yaar Mein Shamil Kar Lo.
Nasha Barhta Hai Sharabein Jo Sharabon Mein Milein.
Tu Khuda Hai Na Mera Ishq Farishton Jaisa.
Dono Insaan Hain Tu Kyun Itne Hijabon Mein Milein.

3
Aaj Hum Daar Pe Khainche Gay Jinn Baton Par.
Kya Ajab Kal Wo Zamane Ko Nisabon Mein Milein.
Aab Na Wo Hain Na Wo Tu Hai Na Wo Mazi Hai Faraz.
Jaise Do Shaks Tamana Ke Sarabon Mein Milein.
