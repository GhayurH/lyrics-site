Kun Ki Hai Kainat Mashhad Mein.
Pori Hoti Hai Baat Mashhad Mein.

1
Mustanad Baat Hai Keh Jate Hain.
Sirf Aala Sifaat Mashhad Mein.
Sari Dunya Se Loag Aate Hain.
Seekhney Deniyaat Mashhad Mein.

2
Oss Ki Janat Mein Zindagi Guzri.
Jis Ki Guzri Hayat Mashhad Mein.
Apni Aankhon Ko Sar Nagoon Rakhna.
Karna Yeh Ayhtiyaat Mashhad Mein.

3
Kabhi Giriya Kabhi Wila Ka Khumar.
Hain Ajab Kaifiyaat Mashhad Mein.
De Raha Hai Sada Yeh Sehan-e-Shifa.
Maut Ko Hogi Maat Mashhad Mein.

4
Sach Hai Mojaz Numa Hain Maula Raza.
Kyun Na Hoon Mojzaat Mashhad Mein.
Hai Yehi Yasir -o- Rajab Ki Dua.
Maula Aaye Wafaat Mashhad Mein.
