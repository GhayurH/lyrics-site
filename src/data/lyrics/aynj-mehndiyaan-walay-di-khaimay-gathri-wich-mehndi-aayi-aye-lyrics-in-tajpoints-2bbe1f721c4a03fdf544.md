1
Aynj Mehndiyaan Walay Di Khaimay Gathri Wich Mehndi Aayi Aye
Banray Di Maa Nu Ro Syed Banray Di Lash Wakhai Aye.
Shabeer Nu Tak Tak Ke Zainab Payi Rondi Te Kurlandi Si.
Qasim Di Lash Te Ro Ro Ke Farwa Nu Seene Landi Si.
Ais Haal De Wich Janj Qasim Di Aayi Le Ke Zahra Jayi Aye.
Gathri Nu Khol Ke Takdi Aye Hath Ganiyaan Walay Labhdi Aye.
Gathri Nu Seene La La Ke Banray Nu Maa Aye Kehndi Aye.
Uth Wekh Sakina Bhainr Teri Tainu Mehndi Lawanr Aayi Aye.
Raj Shami Zulm Kamanday Rahey Aynj Badar De Badlay Landay Rahey.
Qasim Di Lash De Chunr Tukray Shah Ro Ro Ke Farmanday Rahey.
Haye Zalimaan Rait Ich Rool Diti Mere Veer Di Pak Kamai Aye.
Kubra Nu Gal Nal La La Ke Har Bibi Wainr Aye Paya Aye.
Banri De Shagan Manaye Na Banray Ne Sehra Laya Aye.
Teraan Talwaraan Qasim Nu Haye Khon Di Mehndi Layi Aye.
Aye Manzar Asad Sataway Ga Sajjad Nu Bohat Rawaway Ga.
Kiwein Watan Te Ja Ke Sughra Nu Sajjad Aye Haal Sunaway Ga.
Shabeer Ne Maqtal Choon Kyun Qasim Di Maiyyat Chayi Aye.
