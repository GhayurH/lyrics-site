Zahra darbar ch rondi rayee darbaari saaray hansday raye

Haq Len gayee hussnain di maan ashaabi saaray takday raye

Ayataan parh parh quraan Dian zahra nay sunayiaan lokaan nu

Darbar ch saaray ashaabi gal haq di kehn ton chakday raye

Haye baad nahi hasnain di maa haq Len gayee darbar day wich

Zahra darbaar ch rondi rayee darbari sen nu takday raye

Darbaar ch saaray ashaabi bethay san sirf dikhaway layee

Insaaf kissay vi na kita ik doojay da moo rakhday raye

Ahmed nay jo tehreer likhi ashabiaan nay jhutla chori

Mehfil wich aenj day log vi san sach bolan ton jehray bachday raye

Kissay shay di hor zaroorat nayee Quran hi saanoon kafi Aye

Mimbar day faasiq lokaan noon gal haq di kehn ton dakday raye
