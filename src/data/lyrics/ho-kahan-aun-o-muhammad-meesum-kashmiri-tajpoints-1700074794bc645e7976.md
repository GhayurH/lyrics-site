Ho Kahan Aun o Muhammad Meesum Kashmiri.

1
Hai Zulm Ka Tofaan Ho Kahan Aoun-o-Muhammad.
Sar Nangay Hoi Maa Ho Kahan Aoun-o-Muhammad.
Aa Kar Tu Zarra Dekho Keh Maqtal Mein Sheh-e-Dein.
Hain Khon Mein Galtaan Ho Kahan Aoun-o-Muhammad.
Zainab Ne Jisay Pala Woh Humshakal-e-Payamber.
Kuch Dam Ka Hai Mehmaan Ho Kahan Aoun-o-Muhammad.

2
Khaimay Mein Alam Khali Hi Abbas Ka Aaya.
Sab Hotey Hain Giriyaan Ho Kahan Aoun-o-Muhammad.
Noshah Yeh Qasim Bhi Tu Ab Deen-e-Khuda Par.
Hone Ko Hai Qurbaan Ho Kahan Aoun-o-Muhammad.
Sheh Kehte They Be Sheer Ne Halqoom Pe Khayi.
Ik Zulm Ki Paikaan Ho Kahan Aoun-o-Muhammad.

3
Din Dhal Gaya Rotay Hoye Ab Dasht-e-Bala Mein.
Hai Sham-e-Ghareebaan Ho Kahan Aoun-o-Muhammad.
Zainab Ne Kaha Haye Re Hum Phirtay Hain Dar Dar.
Koi Nahi Pursaan Ho Kahan Aoun-o-Muhammad.
Lut Kar Jo Ghar Aayi Hai Tu Izhar Tarap Kar.
Kehti Hai Yehi Maa Ho Kahan Aoun-o-Muhammad.
