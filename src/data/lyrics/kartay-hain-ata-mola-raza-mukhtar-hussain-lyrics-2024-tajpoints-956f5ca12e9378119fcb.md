Kartay Hain Ata Mola Raza Mukhtar Hussain Fatehpuri.

1
Mudda Dil Mein Ho Kartay Hain Atta Maula Raza.
Lab Pe Aane Hi Nahi Detey Sada Maula Raza.
Aarzu Thi Zindagi Mein Dekh Loon Jannat Ko Main.
Ghar Se Nikla Our Mashhad Aa Gaya Maula Raza.
Ho Rahi Hain Door Jism-o-Rooh Se Bemariyaan.
Samne Nazron Ke Hai Sehan-e-Shifa Maula Raza.

2
Janib-e-Mashhad Chala Hoon Iss Yaqeen Ke Sath Main.
Sath Hai Masooma-e-Qum Ki Dua Maula Raza.
Dekh Kar Hairaan Hain Jo Naqsh-e-Qadam Ke Mojzay.
Kaisay Samjhein Gay Tumhari Inteha Maula Raza.
Ja Ke Dekha Hi Nahi Oss Ne Haram Mein Aap Ke.
Jo Yeh Kehta Hai Nahi Milta Khuda Maula Raza.

3
Aik Bas Quran Kar Sakta Hai Midhat Aap Ki.
Yaan Jissay Khud Aap Kartay Hain Atta Maula Raza.
Hum Andheron Se Nikal Aaye Tu Kya Khud Aaftab.
Aap Ke Rozay Se Leta Hai Zia Maula Raza.
Ab Zamane Ki Hawaon Se Nahi Darta Siraj.
Mil Gaya Hai Jab Se Oss Ko Dar Tera Maula Raza.
