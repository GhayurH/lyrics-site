Ye kon qatl hogaya
Haye haye labe furaat

1
Ye koun qatl hogaya pyaasa lab-e-furaat
Kyun surkh hai lahu se, khudaayaa, labe furaat
Ye koun,  Ye koun qatl hogaya pyaasa lab-e-furaat

2
Ye gham naseeb koun hai bhaayi ko dhundta
Thaame huwe qamar ko,  jo aaya, labe furaat

3
Ye koun,  Ye koun qatl hogaya pyaasa lab-e-furaat
Halmin ki dasht mei ye sadaa de raha hai koun
Laasha tarap raha hai,  ye kiska labe furaat

4
Ye koun,  Ye koun qatl hogaya pyaasa lab-e-furaat
Tashna labon ka qaafela saalaar margaya
Ya rab macha hai shor,  ye kaisa lab-e-furaat

5
Ye koun,  Ye koun qatl hogaya pyaasa lab-e-furaat
Barchi ye kis jawaan ke sine mei gar gayi
Armaan kiske rehgaye,  tashna labe furaat

6
Ye koun,  Ye koun qatl hogaya pyaasa lab-e-furaat
Baazu buridaa mashk liye jaa raha hai koun
Daale huwe hai koun,  ye geraa labe furaat

7
Ye koun,  Ye koun qatl hogaya pyaasa lab-e-furaat
Tukre ye kiski laash ke chunta hai ik zaeef
Paamaal kiska hogaya,  laashaa labe furaat

8
Ye koun,  Ye koun qatl hogaya pyaasa lab-e-furaat
Hardam tere huzuur hai "aakil" ki ye dua
Do gaz zamin ataa ho,  khudaaya labe furaat

9
Ye koun,  Ye koun qatl hogaya pyaasa lab-e-furaat
