1
Zainab Ne Jisko Pala Nori Hijab Mein Hasan Sadiq.
Woh Qatal Ho Gaya Hai Ayehad-e-Shabab Mein.
Akbar Ki Lash Dekhi Tu Shabeer Ne Kaha.
Sughra Ko Kya Likhon Ga Khat Ke Jawab Mein.

2
Khaimay Se Niklay Akbar Tu Sab Ko Gumaan Howa.
Sarwar Hoon Jaisay Baithay Zeen e Oqab Mein.
Oss Ko Bhi Zalimo Ne Hai Naizay Pe Charhaya.
Jee Bhar Ke Jo Na Khaila Gaud-e-Rubab Mein.

3
Matam Ka Masla Hai Yeh Hum Se Na Pochiye.
Sab Kuch Likha Howa Hai Umm Ul-Kitab Mein.
Tapka Lahu Jo Ban Mein Shabeer Ka Ameer.
Ubhra Woh Aaj Ban Kar Shakal-e-Ghulab Mein.
