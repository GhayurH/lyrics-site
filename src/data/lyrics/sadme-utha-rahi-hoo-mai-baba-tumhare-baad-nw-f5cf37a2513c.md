Darbaar Jaa Rahi Hoo Mai Baba Tumhare Baad

Baba Ye Fatema Pe Musibat Ka Waqt Hai

Ye Marhala Kathin Hai Qayamat Ka Waqt Hai

Khutba Suna Rahi Hoo Mai

Khutba Suna Rahi Hoo Mai Baba Tumhare Baad

Sadme Utha Rahi

Koi Mujhe Bataye Ke Kya Tha Mera Kasoor

Sadma Ye Hai Ke Shehr Ki Aabadiyon Se Door

Aansu Baha Rahi Hoo Mai

Aansu Baha Rahi Hoo Mai Baba Tumhare Baad

Sadme Utha Rahi

Baba Tumhare Baad Ye Mujpar Sitam Hua

Mohsin Mera Shaheed Hua Wa Musibta

Ye Gham Utha Rahi Hoo Mai

Ye Gham Utha Rahi Hoo Mai Baba Tumhare Baad

Sadme Utha Rahi

Baba Ghareeb Beti Ki Ghurbat To Dekhlo

Sadme Utha Rahi

Darbar May Layeen Ke Khutba Suna Ke Aaj

Sadme Utha Rahi
