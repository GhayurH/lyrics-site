Darmian lashon ke maqtal may khari hay Zainab

1
Is andharay may kisay dhond rahi hay Zainab
1) Sab ki nazrain hain chamaktay huay khanjar ki taraf
Bhai ka khusk gala dekh rahi hay Zainab

2
2) Hath utha kar karoon kis tarha se lashon ko salam
Hath rassi may bandhay hay khari hay Zainab

3
3) Chor kar bhai ko maqtal se ho rukhsat kaisay
Lash uthi hay phir uth ke giri hay Zainab

4
4) Sheh pe chaltay huay khanjar ko bhala kia maloom
Kis tarha khak pe ghash kha ke giri hay Zainab

5
5) Teray sar par yeh na nilay nikaltay dam tak
Teray balon may jo yeh khak pari hay Zainab

6
6) Din ki dhop aah gaye aur raat ki oos aa bi gaye
Saj raha hay ab bhi darbar khari hay Zainab

7
7) Deen bachanay ke liye ghar se jo nikli thi Naved
Bach gaya deen magar kaisay luti hay Zainab
