1
Jalta Hua Bistar Mukhtar Hussain Fatehpuri.
Jalta Howa Bistar Koi Beemar Na Dekhey.
Be Jurm-o-Khata Tauq-e-Garaan Bar Na Dekhey.
Mar Jaye Na Sadmay Hi Se Beemar Elahi.
Be Pardah Haram Ko Sar-e-Bazar Na Dekhey.
Mango Yeh Duaein Shab-e-Ashor Sakina.
Yeh Raat Kabhi Subha Ke Aasaar Na Dekhey.
Thi Fikar Yeh Zainab Ko Rida Bhi Nahi Sar Par.
Iss Haal Mein Abbas-e-Alamdar Na Dekhey.
Sar Naizay Se Ghazi Ne Giraya Hai Zameen Par.
Zainab Ka Khula Sar Koi Badkar Na Dekhey.
Marey Hain Sitamgar Ne Sakina Ko Tamachay.
Zalim Ne Zara Phool Se Rukhsar Na Dekhey.
Milti Hain Kaleem Oss Ko Sakina Se Duaein.
Bachoon Ko Jo Be Yaar-o-Madadgar Na Dekhey.
