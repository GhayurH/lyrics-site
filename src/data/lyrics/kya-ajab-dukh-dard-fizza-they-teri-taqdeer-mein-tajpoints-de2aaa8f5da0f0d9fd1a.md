1
Kya Ajab Dukh Dard Fizza They Teri Taqdeer Mein.
Woh Tera Door-e-Zaeefi Our Tu Zanjeer Mein.
Zalimo Yeh Kya Kiya Ro Kar Yeh Fizza Ne Kaha.
Lash-e-Akbar Par Khare Ho Kar Yeh Fizza Ne Kaha.

2
Qatal Kunbay Ka Tere Woh Bhi Teri Jageer Mein.
Tu Broy-e-Kar Lai Azam-o-Istaqlal Ko.
Tu Ne Chaha Tha Dil-o-Jaan Se Nabi Ki Aal Ko.
Tu Rasan Basta Hoi Hai Sirf Iss Taqseer Mein.

3
Karbala Se Sham Ki Janib Chala Jab Qafila.
Na Koi Syedanio Ke Sar Pe Burqa Na Rida.
Tay Safar Hone Laga Phir Nara-e-Takbeer Mein.
Jo Ghulam-e-Panjtan Hain Woh Sabhi Tere Ghulam.

4
Kar Rahe Hain Dekh Matam Tere Gham Mein Khas-o-Aam.
Teri Kam Aayi Zaeefi Deen Ki Tameer Mein.
Aa Rahe Hain Haq Ki Janib Ahl-e-Dil Ahl-e-Nazar.
Yeh Mawaddat Ka Taqaza Hai Koi Samjhe Agar.

5
Ho Gaye Masroof Momin Matam-e-Shabir Mein.
Teri Rag Rag Mein Azai Hai Gham-e-Aal-e-Rasool.
Yeh Dua Hai Hashar Ke Din Tujh Pe Razi Ho Batool.
Hai Tera Dil Aabdedah Aaina-e-Tehreer Mein.
