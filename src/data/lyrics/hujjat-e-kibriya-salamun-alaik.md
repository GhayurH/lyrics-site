Hujjat e Kibriya Salamun Alaik Mir Hasan Mir
Nayab-e-Mustafa Salamun Alaik

1
Manzalein Teri Janib Hain Mehway Safar
Khalq Ke Rehnuma Salamun Alaik
Tere Hathoon Pe Baiyat Sakhawat Ne Ki
Shah-e-Jod-o-Sakha Salamun Alaik

2
Tera Jah-o-Hasham Dabdaba Dekh Kar
Keh Uthay Anbiya Salamun Alaik
Aisi Binayi De Jo Tujhey Dekh Le
Aks-e-Shair-e-Khuda Salamun Alaik

3
Chaar Janib Se Yeh Aa Rahi Hai Sada
Aao Behar-e-Khuda Salamun Alaik
Ab Tu Aa Jaiye Ab Tu Aa Jaiye
Waris-e-Karbala Salamun Alaik
