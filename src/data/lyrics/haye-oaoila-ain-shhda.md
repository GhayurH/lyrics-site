Haey wawaila, ayna shuhada
Ayna alabbas o ya zainab, ayna qasim ayna hussain, ayna shuhada

1
1) Qatl hoey jab sibtey payamber tan sey houwa jis waqt juda sar,
Tham kay daman-e-dukhtar-e-haider nauha kiya fizza nay yeh rokar,

2
2) Dekho hamari tanhai par tashey bajaney lageh he sitamgar,
Kis ko bulawu dasht main jaakar koi naheen ghamkhwar yahan par,

3
3) Foj-e-sitam main hay tayyari khaimon ki janib aate he naari,
Kuch to kaho maan tum pay ho wari looten gaya aada ridayna hamari,

4
4) Dasht-e-blaaa aur khauf ka aalam koi karey nauha koi matam,
Kaun bhala de zakhmoon ka marham kisko dikhaayna zakhm-e-jigar ham,

5
5) Roney lagee maqtal ki hawaaye aane lagee matam ki sadaaye,
Aag laeen jis waqt lagaaye us waqt ham sab kisko bulaaye,

6
6) Jalney lagey saadaat kay khayme uthneh lagey jab aag kay sholey,
Choonk uthey wo shiddat-e-tap say tham kay dil sajjad yeh boley,

7
7) Jaao koi akbar ko bulaawo koi mere qasim ko sadaa do,
Koi khabar ammu ko sunaao baba kahan hain kuch to bataawo,

8
8) Nauha yeh aasif karti thee fizza doob gayaa qismat ka sitara,
Maut hoi furqat main gawara shah kay bina ho kaisey guzara,

9
9) Charon taraf ek gham ka saman hay darya ki mojon main shor-e-fughan hay,
Khak basar shabbir ki maan hay us kay labon par gham ki azan hay,
