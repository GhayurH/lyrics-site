1
Hazaraan Loag Ne Ghazi Te Zainab Be Rida.
Har Moor Te Aandi Si Haye Sham Bazaraan Wich Fizza Di Aye Sada.
Banwaan Te Neel Paye Pairaan Wich Paye Gaye Chalay Sir Zakhmi Pathraan Naal Aye.
Kis Haal Turi Ghar Taun Te Sham Taein Aanday Ki Haal Ho Gaya.

2
Zainab De Naal Safar Kardi Rayi Ban Ke Chadar Saya Si Dhup De Andar.
Teri Bhain Ruqaiya Ne Abbas Nibhai Aye Teri Tarha Wafa.
Main Kiwein Haal Sunaa Guzrey Aan Kiwein Galiyaan Hath Qaidi Gal Wich Rasiyaan.
Digda Te Kadi Uthda Bazar Choon Abid Da Langiya Aye Qafila.

3
Karbal Taun Sham Taein Na Arzi Chadraan Rayiyaan Lath Gayiyaan Sir Taun Mitiyaan.
Aazad Yadullah Di Dhiyaan De Jay Hath Honday Sir Laindiyaan Chupa.
 Akbar Aye Sain Kihya Ghazi De Sir Wal Tak Ke Ki Aakhaan Kiwein Guzrey.
Ayelan Shimar Kita Sajjad Di Phophiyaan Nu Deway Na Koi Rah
