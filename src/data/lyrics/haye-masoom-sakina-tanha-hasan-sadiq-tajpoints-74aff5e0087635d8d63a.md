1
Haye Masoom Sakina Tanha Hasan Sadiq.
Haye Masoom Sakina Tanha Zindaan Mein Dam Toor Gayi.
Bhai Phophi Our Ghamzada Maa Ko Dekho Rota Chor Gayi.
Chaar Taraf Hain Zulm Ke Bani Qaid Mein Hai Ghanghor Andhera.
Oss Pe Sitam Ik Our Howa Hai Pyas Kaleja Toor Gayi.
Dil Mein Rehai Ka Armaan Tha Mar Ke Bhi Jo Pora Na Howa.
Jism Se Rooh Nikal Kar Lekin Qaid Ke Talay Toor Gayi.
Sabar Tu Hai Shabeer Ka Wirsa Yeh Beti Hai Sabir Ki.
Sabar Ko Jo Iss Chaar Baras Mein De Ke Niya Ik Moor Gayi.
Pani Pani Kartay Kartay Khon Ragoon Mein Sokh Gaya.
Jheel, Samandar, Darya Ko Sharminda Kar Ke Chor Gayi.
Khon Bhara Abbas Ka Parcham Sab Ki Aas Ko Toor Gaya.
Pyasi Bani Gham Mein Chacha Ke Koza Apna Toor Gayi.
Rishta Bhateeji Our Chacha Ka Karb-o-Bala Mein Jab Toota.
Apne Rishtay Ko Woh Bachi Mashk-o-Alam Se Joor Gayi.
Zikar-e-Sakina Zikar-e-Zainab Kartay Raho Rehan Sada.
Jitni Door Talak Qismat Se Sansoon Ki Hai Door Gayi.
