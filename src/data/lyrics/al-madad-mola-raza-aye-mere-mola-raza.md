Al Madad Mola Raza Aye Mere Mola Raza
Aye Ghareeb Ul-Gurba Aye Mere Maula Raza
Aap Hain Roh-e-Ali Aap Jan-e-Zahra
Aap Ke Dar Ke Liye Baat Mashoor Hai Yeh
Woh Hai Darbar-e-Raza Wahan Dastoor Hai Yeh
Jahan Bin Mangay Hoye Pori Hoti Hai Dua
Inn Ke Darbar Ki Phir Dekhney Shan Chalo
Dil Yeh Kehta Hai Chalo Haan Khurasan Chalo
Bas Khurasan Mein Hai Khuld Ki Aab-o-Hawa
Chahe Lagta Hai Lagay Kissi Mufti Ko Bura
Har Nafas Dil Se Mere Yehi Aati Hai Sada
Tum Ho Quran Mera Tum Hi Mera Kaba
Sehmey Sehmey Se Milein Jahan Humraz Tamam
Dono Aalam Mein Faqat Aisay Hain Do Hi Maqam
Aik Hai Khak-e-Shifa Aik Hai Sehan-e-Shifa
Hai TAKALLUM Ki Dua Aye Imam-e-Hashtum
Phir Se Dikhlaein Humein Janat-e-Mashhad-o-Qum
Phir Se Ho Jaye Humein Rizq-e-Dedar Atta
