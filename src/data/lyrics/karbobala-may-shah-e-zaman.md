Karbobala main shah e zaman ghar ko lutaany aye hain

1
Ummat e jad k bigray kaam banane aaye hain

2
Kehti thi Zainab a.s aey baba koi sahara ab na raha
Jaan k tanha ehl e sitam khemay jalaney aye hain

3
Dhoop hai jalta sehra hai baap ka dil khoon hota hai
Nanhey mujahid ummat ka kaam banany aye hain

4
Ro kay Sakina a.s ney kaha ye behr e madad aao dada
Zaalim mery baaba ko naizay laganey aye hain

5
Gir chukay Akbar a.s ghorey sy shah ka sathi koi nahi
Tooti kamar ko pakrey huye laash uthany aaye hain

6
Bano nay rokar kaha hashr hua abid a.s barpa
Jaltay hain khaimy dushman e deen qaidi bnany aye hain
