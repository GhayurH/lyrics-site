Karbal cho piya likhda
Hun waqt riya koi nai
Biya kia mei likhaan

Tediyan phuphyan te mawan da
Sajjad siwa koi nae
Biya kia may likhan

Kus gaye nae sajan, kalla piya marinda aey
Ya dhi da khat aaya aey
Qasid andaa aey koi Hussain aey may dhi da khat ghin aayan
Syed ro ke anda aey oo putraan moya Hussain may aan
Syed dhi da khat chaa ke uthan aaya aey jithan Akbar di laash aey
Anda aey Akbar uthi bhain da khat aa gaya aey
Syed Akbar de khoon di siyahi bana ke khat likhna shroo kita
Syed likhda aey tainda oon wailay wo khat milya aey Sughra
Oo jadan thi gaye rasm tamam aey
Paan sehray surkh qaleen utay way oo
Woo kita Akbar hun aaram aey
Asaan mulk Iraq pasand kita aey
Woo taqdeer de man ihkaam aey
Kal jang Akbar di ghin waisan wo
Kulsoom te Zainab Shaam aey

1
Qasim da jo nay likhya oh ku banara banaa baytha
Oonde sehre saja baytha oonde shagan mana baytha
Tedee bhayn de hathan tay
Hun rang-e-hina ko nai
Biya kia mei likhaan

2
Lag nazar gayi ujrii tendeh yusaf-e-sani koon
Umat ne hai raj lutya Akbar di jawani koon
Koi rasm wi shaadi di
May keeti ada koi nae
Biya kia may likhan

3
may jawan mara baytha meh nadaan kuha baytha
Habib Mazahir jay mehman mara baytha
Inj khoon de wich tar haan
Sajde di wi jaa koi nai
Biya kia mei likhaan

4
Tain je likhya aey Ghazi te
Hin dhair gila may koon
Hun day saan gilay ujrri
Giya chorr bhira may koon
Pursa aina Ghazi da
Jhalya Fazal di maa koi nae
Biya kia may likhan

5
Jairray veer di ulfat day
Innay tohfay diwa bhaijay
Jairray chotian walay de
Hin cholay siwa bhaijay
Aawan da ukoon moqa
Ummat ne dita koi nae
Biya kia may likhan

6
Hun mang dua ujrri
Jafar da sakhi aaway
Sehray taiday veeran koon
Watna te oo khud paaway
Bhee reet dua meri
Koi aur dua koi nae
Biya kia may likhan
