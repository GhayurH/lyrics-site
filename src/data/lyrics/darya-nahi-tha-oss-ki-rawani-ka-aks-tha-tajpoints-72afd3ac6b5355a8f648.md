1
Main Tishna Lab Tha Samne Pani Ka Aks Tha.
Dasht-e-Bala Mein Naqsh-e-Kaf-e-Pa Nahi The Woh.
Ujray Musafir Ki Kahani Ka Aks Tha.
Yeh Eijz Maslehat Ki Alamat Ka Naam Hai.
Woh Tamkanat Kahan Thi Jawani Ka Aks Tha.
Ankhon Ki Putliyon Mein Udasi Ki Shakal Thi.
Our Khamshi Mein Choat Porani Ka Aks Tha.
Mehka Howa Tha Raat Ka Daman Guzashta Raat.
Har Aik Samt Rat Ki Rani Ka Aks Tha.
Yeh Raaz Bhi Azai Khula Hum Pe Waqt-e-Marg.
Har Saans Tu Fana Ki Nishani Ka Aks Tha.
