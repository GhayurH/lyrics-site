Mere Aaqa Mere Maula

mere bedast jari, mere ghazi abbas

aabroo pyas ki tune labe darya rakhi

tere rehwaar ke tapo ki garaj sunte hi,

fauj sar pat baaghi

khud ba khud tham gaya darya ka bhi behta pani

mere aaqa mere maula…

aslahe may tera hatyaar hai bas ek naiza

koi talwaar nahi

phir bhi larzeeza tere roab se hai har shami

mere aaqa mere maula…

is tarha baandh ke naize se bhara mashkeeza

haat nam ho na saka

tujhsa mohtaaj falak ne bhi na dekha koi

mere aaqa mere maula…

al atash ki wo sadayein pe jigar kat-ta hai

aur dil rota hai

tazeeyane hai musalsal jo sama-ad pa teri

mere aaqa mere maula…

doo badoo aake lade tujhse bhala kiski majaal

chupke karne lage waar

katgaye haath kamigah se talwar chali

mere aaqa mere maula…

haath jaise hi kate mashq ko daaton may liya

rukh kiya qaimo ka

daf-atan mashq pe ek teer laga mashq chidhi

mere aaqa mere maula…

mashq se pani ka behna tha ke dil baith gaya

ab to kuch bhi na raha

aise may durz laga sar pa qayamat tooti

mere aaqa mere maula…

dhal gayi shaam bade raat ke saaye shahid

teergi phail gayi

toota naiza bhi ye bibi koi maqtal ko chali

mere aaqa mere maula…
