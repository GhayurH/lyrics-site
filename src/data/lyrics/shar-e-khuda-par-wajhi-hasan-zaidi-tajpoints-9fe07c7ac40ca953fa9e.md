Shar e Khuda Par Wajhi Hasan Zaidi

1
Kya Waqt Musibat Ka Hai Yeh Shair-e-Khuda Par.
Khoon Dekhtey Hain Apna Woh Zainab Ki Rida Par.
Kaisay Kahin Abbas Keh Zakhmi Hoye Baba.
Behno Ko Bohat Naaz Hai Amma Ki Dua Par.
Behno Ko Madine Liye Jate Hain Baradar.
Ab Unn Ko Bharosa Nahi Kofay Ki Hawa Par.

2
Ashor Ko Bhi Aaj Hi Ro Lo Meri Bibi.
Pabandi Nahi Aaj Teri Aah-o-Boka Par.
Jate Hoye Kar Lijiye Beti Ki Ziyarat.
Aankhein Tu Ali Kholiye Zainab Ki Sada Par.
Iss Tarha Bahaya Gaya Khon-e-Abu Talib.
Darya Mein Bahaya Tu Uraya Hai Hawa Par.

3
Parhne Nahi Aaye Jo Muhammad Ka Janaza.
Khushiyaan Woh Manatay Rahe Haider Ki Qaza Par.
Uneeswin Ramzan Ki Hoti Hain Azanein.
Aa Jao Musalmano Sabhi Farsh-e-Azza Par.
 Akbar Kahin Shohar Kahin Beton Ka Lahu Hai.
Utari Thin Kabhi Aayatein Zahra Ki Kisa Par.
