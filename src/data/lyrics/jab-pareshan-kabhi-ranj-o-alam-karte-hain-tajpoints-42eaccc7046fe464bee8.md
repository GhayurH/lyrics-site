1
Jab Pareshan Kabhi Ranj-o-Alam Karte Hain.
Hum Azzadar Nazar Soye Alam Karte Hain.
Apne Bacho Ko Balaon Se Bachane Ke Liye.
Naam-e-Abbas Ko Paishani Pe Dam Karte Hain.

2
Hukam-e-Quran Bhi Hai Zeenat-e-Eman Bhi Hai.
Hum Yehi Soch Ke Mazloom Ka Gham Karte Hain.
Apne Dukh Mein Kabhi Rona Bhi Gawara Na Kiya.
Zikar-e-Shabir Pe Bas Aankh Ko Nam Karte Hain.

3
Kar Ke Zanjeer Zani Khoon Baha Kar Apna.
Jaan Nisari Ka Haseen Baab Raqam Karte Hain.
Humein Fursat Hi Nai Zikar-e-Sheh-e-Wala Se.
Iss Liye Tazkira Aghyar Ka Kam Karte Hain.

4
Paak Zahra Ke Mukhalif Pe Tabara Kar Ke.
Hum Khata Kar Tu Saman-e-Iram Karte Hain.
Main Kahan Our Kahan Midhat-e-Aal-e-Ahmad.
Yeh Karemi Hai Woh Sahil Pe Karam Karte Hain.
