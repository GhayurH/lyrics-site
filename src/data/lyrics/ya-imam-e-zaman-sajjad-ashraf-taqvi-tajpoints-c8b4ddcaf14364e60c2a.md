1
Ya Imam E Zaman Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.
Aap Ka Muntazir Hai Yeh Sara Jahan.
Aap Aaein Gay Tu Ho Ga Roshan Jahan.
Mere Maula Khudara Chaley Aaiye.
Mujh Gunahgar Ko Aap Apnaiye.
Sun Lein Maula Mere Dard-e-Dil Ki Zabaan.
Aap Ki Foaj Ka Ik Sipahi Banoon.
Jan Qadmo Mein Main Aap Ke Waar Doon.
Robaro Aap Ke De Doon Apni Main Jaan.
Maula Har So Hain Dushman Ki Ayariyaan.
Seh Sakein Gay Na Hum Ab Yeh Makariyaan.
Misal-e-Meesum Yahan Kat Rahi Hai Zabaan.
Aaj Sar Par Kharey Hain Mere Khaibari.
Jag Mein Chayi Hoi Har Taraf Marhabi.
Aaein Talwar-e-Haider Ka Le Kar Nishaan.
Main Tu Takta Hi Rehta Hoon Soye Haram.
Jane Kab Hoga Inn Aansoon Pe Karam.
Beh Ke Kehte Hain Yeh Mere Ashk-e-Rawaan.
Roz Parhta Hoon Maula Dua-e-Ehd.
Meri Hoti Hai Maula Yeh Jadojehad.
Aap Ko Mere Maula Main Paon Kahan.
Arbaeen Pe Bhi Dhonda Bohat Aap Ko.
Rooz-e-Ashor Bhi Aap Ki Zaat Ko.
Aap Ka Noha Khuwaan Pa Saka Na Nishaan.
Hai Labon Par Raza Ke Our Sajjad Ke.
Chomtay Hain Qadam Apne Ajdad Ke.
Jin Ke Dam Se Mila Aap Ka Saibaan.
