Karo Matam Hussain Ka Azadaro Nadeem Sarwar.
Parho Kalma Nabi Ka Musalmano.

1
Deen Ne Maula Shabeer Ko Di Sada.
Amma Zahra Ke Marqad Se Rukhsat Howa.
Lab Pe Nam-e-Khuda Dil Mein Thi Karbala.
Din Bhi Garmi Ke They Sath Bachoon Ka Tha.

2
Phir Bhi Marne Chala Woh Musalmano.
Sheh Ke Humrah Thin Bibiyaan Betiyaan.
Nojawaan Sath They Sath Tha Be Zabaan.
Haye Kis Shan Se Woh Hoye They Rawaan.

3
Aik Beemar Leti Rahi Hichkiyaan.
Shor-e-Matam Utha Tha Musalmano.
Do Muharram Ko Karb-o-Bala Aa Gaye.
Alqama Ke Kinaray Jo Khaimay Lagay.

4
Ashqiya Ne Kaha Aa Ke Shabeer Se.
Apne Khaimay Yahan Se Utha Lijiye.
Waqt Kya Aa Gaya Tha Musalmano.
Khatam Karb-o-Bala Mein Larai Hoi.

5
Sari Foaj-e-Khuda Khak Par So Gayi.
Aayi Sham-e-Ghareebaan Raha Na Koi.
Itrat-e-Mustafa Soye Zindaan Chali.
Nangay Sar Bibiyaan Hain Musalmano.

6
Haye Bazar-e-Kofa Ka Woh Marhala.
Betiyaan Thin Muhammad Ki Jab Be Rida.
Gir Ke Naqoon Se Bachoon Ki Thi Yeh Sada.
Alwida Madar-e-Ghamzada Alwida.

7
Aasmaan Ro Raha Tha Musalmano.
Mere Sajjad Boli Yeh Bint-e-Ali.
Janti Hai Mujhe Sham Ki Har Ghali.
Kal Talak Shehzadi Thi Main Sham Ki.

8
Khak Urao Keh Mujh Ko Na Dekhey Koi.
Yeh Tha Zainab Ka Noha Musalmano.
Bolay Sajjad Hathoon Mein Zanjeer Hai.
Be Ridai Phophi Ab Tu Taqdeer Hai.

9
Sabar Aal-e-Muhammad Ki Jageer Hai.
Hum Hain Aal-e-Nabi Bas Yeh Tafseer Hai.
Keh Keh Yeh Ro Diya Tha Musalmano.
Yeh Jo:REHAN-o-SARWAR: Ki Aawaz Hai.

10
Taba Karb-o-Bala Isski Parwaz Hai.
Chodah Sadiyon Se Matam Ka Ijaz Hai.
Inn Ke Dar Ka Gada Lal Shahbaz Hai.
Hai Karam Syeda Ka Musalmano.
