MERE MONIS MERE GAHMKHAAR KAHAN HO ABBAS
KIS MUSIBAT ME HAI BHAIYA YE BEHAN DEKH TO LO
MERE GHAAZI MERE HAATHON ME RASAN DEKH TO LO
KOI YAWAR NA MADADGAAR KAHAN HO ABBAS
WHO NAQAHAT HAI CHALA BHI NAHI JATA MUJHSE

AL MADAD SHEH KE ALAMDAR KAHAN HO ABBAS
KYA KAHUN KIS SE KAHUN HAAL BHALA ABID KA
KAT GAYA TAUQE KE KAATON SE GALA ABID KA
SAAS LENA BHI HAI DUSHWAAR KAHON ABBAS
MUJHSE PUCHE KOI EHSAAS GHARIBUL WATANI
HAR NAFAS DIL ME MERE CHUBTI HAI BARCHI KI ANI
MAR GAYA AKBARE JARAAR KAHAN HO ABBAS
HABASHI AAGAYE FIZZA KI HIMAYAT KE LIYE
KYOON NAHI AATE BHALA TUM MERI NUSRAT KE LIYE
TAAZIYAANON KE HAI BOCHAAR KAHAN HO ABBAS

KHUSHK AANKHEN HAIN MAGAR KAAN LAHU ROTE HAIN
MARSIYA PARTE HAIN RUKSAAR KAHAN HO ABBAS

BERIYAAN BHI TUMHE RO RO KE SADA DETI HAI
QAID HAI ABIDE BEEMAR KAHAN HO ABBAS
KAISE HOTA MERI GHERAT KO GAWARA BHAIYA
NAAM LEKAR MUJHE ZAALIM NE PUKAARA BHAIYA
HAMPE HAASTE HAIN SAB AGIYAAR KAHAN HO ABBAS
KHUL KE ROTI NAHI MAZLOOM YATEEMA BHAIYA

KITNA KARTI THI TUMHE PYAAR KAHAN HO ABBAS
