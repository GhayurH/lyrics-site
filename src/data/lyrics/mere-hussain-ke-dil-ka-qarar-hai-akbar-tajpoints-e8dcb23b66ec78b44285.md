Mere Hussain Ke Dil Ka Qarar Hai Akbar.
Janab-e-Laila Ke Ghar Ki Bahar Hai Akbar.

1
Main Manta Hoon Keh Yousuf Nabi Haseen Thay Magar.
Jahan Mein Husan Ka Parwardigar Hai Akbar.
Saja Kar Sar Pe Amama Pisar Ke Bolay Hussain.
Dikhane Khaibari Manzar Tiyar Hai Akbar.

2
Uqaab Par Jonhi Baitha Na Koi Jan Saka.
Rasool Hai Ya Ke Oss Par Sawar Hai Akbar.
Na Oss Ko Chahiye Dolat Na Chahiye Janat.
Teri Dehleez Ka Jo Bhi Zawwar Hai Akbar.

3
Haan Koi Jaye Jina Mein Ya Koi Dozakh Mein.
Aqeeday Par Hi Tu Daromadar Hai Akbar.
Batata Dunya Ko Afraz Hai Ke Dar Pe Tere.
Jawani Aaj Bhi Sajda Guzar Hai Akbar.
