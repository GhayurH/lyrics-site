Haye Qayamat Ka Hai Samaa Chanay Lagi Sham-e-Ghareeban

Khemaa-e-Sadaat Se Utha Dhua

Dhal Gaya Din Khatm Larayi Hoyi

Maut Ki Khamoshi Hai Chahi Hoyi

Chaak Girebaan Khudae Hoyi

Khaak Basar Fatima Jayi Hoyi

Larzi Zameen Kaanp Utha Aasmaa

Noor-e-Nazar Raj Dulare Gaye

Zainab-e-Dilgeer Kay Pyare Gaye

Shah Kay Ansaar Bhi Mare Gaye

Aas Thi Jin Par Wo Sahare Gaye

Loot Liya Maut Ne Yeh Karwaan

Khoon-e-Haider Sher-e-Jari Ka Alam

Ho Gaya Shabbir Ka Sar Bhi Qalam

Khushian Manane Lagi Fauj-e-Sitam

Ghum Se Tabah Haal Hai Ahl-e-Haram

Lut Gayi Konain Ki Shahazadian

Aun-o-Mohammed Nahi Akbar Nahi

Qasim-o-Abbas Dilawar Nahi

Haye Koi Moonis-o-Yawar Nahi

Khaima Nahi Makna-o-Chadar Nahi

Dasht Main Hai Aal-e-Nabi Be Amaa

Madar-e-Akbar Ka Ajab Haal Hai

Deta Hai Jab Koi Tassali Usse

Kehti Hai Dil Thaam Kay Rootay Howe

Kho Gaye Is Ban Main Sahare Mere

Mar Gaya Haye Mera Karyal Jawaa

Haye Yeh Bechargi Yeh Bekasi

Deta Nahi Us Ko Dilaasa Koi

Darmiyan Laashon Kay Akeli Khari

Kehti Hai Bachi Koi Sehmi Hoyi

Dhoondnay Jaaon Tumhay Baba Kahan

Maa Hoon Har Ek Ranj Uthaoon Gi Main

Rotay Huwe Khud Chali Jaaoon Gi Main

Dhond Ke Baysheer Ko Laaoon Gi Main

Us Ke Bina Jee Nahi Paoon Gi Main

Reh Gaya Haye Mera Bacha Kahaan

Hai Ye Gohar Azmat-e-Bint-e-Ali

Sabko Sambhala Bhi Hifazat Bhi Ki

Gham Main Shah-e-Deen Kay Bhi Roti Rahi

Mamta Pay Aanch Bhi Aanay Na Di

Maa To Hay Bas Aun-o-Muhammad Ki Maa
