1
Ba-Yaad-e-Khoon-e-Shaheedaan Ba-Naam-e-Aazadi.
Karein Gaye Dil Se Sada Ayehtaram-e-Aazadi.
Sunao Sare Watan Ko Pyam-e-Aazadi.
Tamam Ahl-e-Watan Ko Salam-e-Aazadi.

2
Hum Kahin Rahein Hum Yehi Kahin Ho Shad Pakistan.
Pakistan Hamari Jan.
Sabz Parcham Par Tera Jo Chand Tara Hai.
Ba-Khuda Hum Ko Yeh Apni Jaan Se Pyara Hai.

3
ALLAH Kare Ta-Hashar Rahe Aabad Pakistan.
Khetiyaan, Sehra, Samandar, Wadiyaan Teri.
Sari Dunya Se Haseen Aabadiyaan Teri.
Sab Mil Ke Lagaein Narah Zindabad Pakistan.

4
Meri Jan Pakistan Aazad Rahe.
Yeh Dua Lab Par Hai Pakistan Ki Khatir.
Panjtan Iss Ke Sada Hoon Hami-o-Nasir.
Ho Koi Nagar Hai Sham-o-Sahar Bas Yad Pakistan.
