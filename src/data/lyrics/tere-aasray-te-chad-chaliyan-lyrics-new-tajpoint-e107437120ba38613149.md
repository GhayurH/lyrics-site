Tere Aasray Te Chad Chaliyan Sab Bhain Bhira Ghazi.
Kehya Pak Ali Mehfooz Rahway Zainab Di Rida Ghazi.

1
1- Talwar Chali Koi Dard Nahin Ayeh Dard Rawanda Aye.
Zainab Nu Turna Nahin Ondha Paye Sham De Rah Ghazi.
2- Rakhein Yaad Wasiyat Babay Di Umm Ul-Banin Jaya.
Tere Jendiyaan Zainab Na Roway Karein Ainj Di Wafa Ghazi.

2
3- Main Wekh Riyaan Jo Baad Mere Ummat Ne Zulm Dhana Aye.
Rona Meri Dhiyaan Yasrab Wich Kadi Karb-o-Bala Ghazi.
4- Ayeh Kofa Raas Nahin Aaya Mere Putraan Te Dhiyaan Nu.
Ban Ke Nigran Ridawaan Da Toon Watan Te Ja Ghazi.

3
5- Roye Maula Ali Chum Ke Bazu Toon Maan Zahra Jaiyaan Da.
Khaton e Janat Khaliq Taun Liya Mang Ke Dua Ghazi.
6- Sajjad Sabar Di Had Muk Gayi Rehya Ronda Almaan Wala.
Kehya Haider Mera Bachra Na Talwar Utha Ghazi.
