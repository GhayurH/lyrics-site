1
Beysheer Mere Aja Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi
Be Sheer Mere Aa Ja Tujhe Lori Sunaon Gi.
Apne Ali Asghar Ko Godi Mein Solaon Gi.
Milney Ke Liye Maa Se Kuch Dair Ko Aa Jao.
Mujh Dukhiya Pe Kya Guzri Sab Haal Sunaon Gi.
Gar Foaj-e-Sitam Aayi Our Cheeni Meri Chadar.
Iss Dasht-e-Biyaan Baan Mein Main Kis Ko Bulaon Gi.
Godi Mein Tujhe Le Kar Main Socha Yeh Karti Thi.
Iss Shair Ko Main Apne Abbas Banaon Gi.
Aye Lal Tasawar Mein De De Ke Tujhe Lori.
Maqtal Mein Tere Khali Jholay Ko Jholaon Gi.
Jate Hoye Khaimay Se Jo Tu Ne Kiye Beta.
Unn Tere Isharon Ko Main Bhool Na Paon Gi.
Aana Ho Agar Mushkil Aawaz Do Madar Ko.
Maqtal Mein Tumhein Lainay Main Khud Chali Aaon Gi.
Yeh Kehte Hoye Gohar Gash Kar Gayi Woh Madar.
Main Tere Bina Asghar Wapis Nahi Jaon Gi.
