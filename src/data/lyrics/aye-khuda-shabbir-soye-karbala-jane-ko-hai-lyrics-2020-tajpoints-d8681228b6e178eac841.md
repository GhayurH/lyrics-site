Aye Khuda Shabbir Soye Karbala Jane Ko Hai.
Sath Zahra Our Zahra Ki Kisa Jane Ko Hai.

1
Barh Ke Aye Zanjeer-e-Dar Daman Abba Ka Tham Le.
Fatima Ke Ghar Se Jan-e-Fatima Jane Ko Hai.
Ghar Se Tu Hajj Ka Iradah Kar Ke Nikley Hain Hussain.
Jane Kis Janib Madine Ki Howa Jane Ko Hai.

2
Bandh Liya Saman Koi Tu Ossay Bhi De Sada.
Fatima Sughra Tumhara Qafila Jane Ko Hai.
Phir Badal Jaye Ga Yasrab Mein Madina Aaj Se.
Chor Kar Akbar Madina Mustafa Jane Ko Hai.
