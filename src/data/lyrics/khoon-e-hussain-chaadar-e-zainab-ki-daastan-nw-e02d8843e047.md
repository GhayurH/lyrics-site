Khoon-e-Hussain Chaadar-e-Zainab Ki Daastan
Kaanp-e-Zameen Sun Kai Jise Roay Aasmaan

Ya Mustafa Ridaen Bhi Ummat Nai Cheenli
Ab Ja Kai Sir Chupaa Ai Teri Betiyaan Kahaan

Washat Say Qatl Gahhmai Chonkay Garaat Bhar
Asgher Ko Jungalo Mai Pukaaray Gi Maa Kahaan

Zainab Kai Baazvon Mai Rasan Kya Andhair Hain
Abbas Bawafa Ali Akbar Jawaan Kahaan

Baad-e-Hussain Soyi Sakina Na Chain Sai
Bhoolay Kai Baazvon Mai Bandhi Raismaan Kahaan
