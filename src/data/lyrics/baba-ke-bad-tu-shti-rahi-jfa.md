Hurr Party 2010

1
Baba ke baad tu sehti rahi jafa ay umme abiha ya zahra

2
Ummat nabi ke baap hi aale nabi se phir gayi
Haq ghasibon se maangne darbaar me khari rahi
Ghutla diya tujhe haq bhi nahi diya

3
Dewaar o dar ke darmiyan tori gayi hai pasliya
Mohsin shaheed hogaya tujh ko wo keh saka na maa
Itne sitam ke baad bhi shikwa nahi kiya

4
Uthta tha ehteraam me tere liye mera nabi
Tatheer ki sanad tujhe quran me khuda ne di
Lekin yahan tera samjha na martaba

5
Is dar pa aake mout bhi aaghe kabhi nahi barhi
Jibreel sa malak raha deta hai roz hazri
Haaye wo ghar tera kisne jala diya

6
Manzar tumhari mout ka bhoole ga kaise ye jahaan
Bibi teri gharibi pe rota rahega aasmaan
Taboot raat me bibi utha tera
