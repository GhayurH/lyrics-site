1
Kiya Baap Se Is Tarha Juda Hoty Hain Akbar Syed Raza Abbas Zaidi.
Maqtal Ki Zameen Our Qayamat Ka Woh Manzar.
They Gaud Mein Shabeer Ki Zakhmi Ali Akbar.
Betay Ki Taraf Dekh Ke Jab Ya Ali Keh Kar.
Ik Hath Rakha Seene Pe Ik Hath Sina Par.
Seene Se Sina Khainch Ke Kehne Lagay Sarwar.
Kya Baap Se Iss Tarha Juda Hotey Hain Akbar.
Meri Jan Akbar Tu Yaad Bohat Aaye Ga.
Haye Mere Lal Akbar Ik Bar Pukaro Baba.
Kya Tum Se Kahon Kis Tarha Pohancha Hoon Sirhaney.
Main Ghutniyon Chal Kar Tumhein Aaya Hoon Uthane.
Aankhein Na Karo Band Meri Jan Chalo Ghar.
Takleef Hai Seene Mein Yeh Ahsas Hai Hum Ko.
Mehsos Karo Tum Bhi Tu Iss Baap Ke Gham Ko.
Mar Jaon Ga Youn Ayeriyaan Ragro Na Zameen Par.
Iss Haal Mein Gar Samne Beta Tera Hota.
Zakhmo Ki Taraf Dekh Ke Kya Tu Nahi Rota.
Do Hosla Seene Se Laga Lo Mujhe Uth Kar.
Chehray Pe Naqab Iss Liye Rehta Tha Tumhare.
ALLAH Nazar Na Lagay Akbar Ko Hamarey.
Phir Kis Ki Nazar Kha Gayi Humshakal-e-Payamber.
Kyun Baap Ki Aagosh Mein Dam Toor Rahe Ho.
Ghurbat Mein Akela Humein Tum Chor Rahe Ho.
Kyun Hichkiyaan Letey Ho Marey Samne Dilbar.
Zalim Ne Sina Mar Ke Jab Zeen Se Giraya.
Hathoon Se Jigar Tham Ke Tu Khak Pe Aaya.
Chehray Pe Tere Khon Hai Zakhmi Hai Tera Sar.
Kuch Dair Mein Le Aaye Ga Qasad Khat-e-Sughra.
Hum Se Na Parha Jaye Ga Tum Bhi Na Tarapna.
Jis Waqt Pukaron Ga Main Khat Seene Pe Rakh Kar.
Akbar Ne Jab Aagosh Mein Li Aakhri Hichki.
 Zeeshan-o- Raza Khaimay Se Zainab Ne Sada Di.
Mar Jaein Gay Shabeer Teri Lash Utha Kar.
