1
Mera Rehbar Ali Dua e Subh Azadar e Batool.
Dua-e-Subha Ka Umeed-e-Sham Ka Matlab.
Rajab Ke Chand Se Pocho Imam Ka Matlab.
Hawa Udhar Se Chali Hai Idhar Se Taig-e-Ali.
Lughat Pe Aaj Khulay Ga Khiram Ka Matlab.
Ali Ke Dast-e-Khuda Mein Hai Adal Ki Meezan.
Ali Ki Taig Mein Hai Qatal-e-Aam Ka Matlab.
Pata Hai Sorah-e-Ikhlas Kis Ko Kehte Hain.
Pata Hai Kya Hai Sajod-o-Qiyam Ka Matlab.
Khulay Gi Zaat-e-Ali Kis Tarha Zamane Par.
Abhi Khula Nahi Dunya Pe Naam Ka Matlab.
Nahi Jo Hum Mein Koi Bhi Ghadeer Ka Rishta.
Tu Iss Ke Baad Dua Our Salam Ka Matlab?.
Zamana Karta Hai Quraan Ki Aayaton Se Mazzaq.
Nikal Letey Hain Sab Apne Kam Ka Matlab.
Nisab e Buzar-o-Salman Ko Parho Akbar.
Agar Samajh Mein Na Aaye Ghulam Ka Matlab.
