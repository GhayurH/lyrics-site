Midhat e Shah e Zaman Raza Hasani.

1
Zahra Ke Lal Jan-e-Rasool-e-Zaman Hussain.
Khaybar Shikan Ali Hain Tu Batil Shikan Hussain.
Dunya Mein Do Nawasay Nabi Ke Hain Munfarid.
Pehle Kissi Ka Naam Nahi Tha Hasan Hussain.
Yeh Hai Tumhare Khon Ke Chenton Ka Mojza.
Sarsabz Hai Jo Deen-e-Nabi Ka Chaman Hussain.

2
Yeh Sab Tumhare Ism-e-Girami Ka Faiz Hai.
Hota Na Aaj Lafz Kahin Panjtan Hussain.
Aye Kash Mujh Ko Oss Ka Tayammum Naseeb Ho.
Jo Khak Ban Gayi Thi Tumhara Kafan Hussain.
Yeh Tere Khandan Ki Midhat Ka Faiz Hai.
 Mehshar Hai Apni Zaat Mein Khud Anjuman Hussain.
