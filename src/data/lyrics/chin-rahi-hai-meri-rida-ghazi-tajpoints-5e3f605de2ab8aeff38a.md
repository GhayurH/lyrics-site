1
Rooz-e-Ashor Waqt-e-Asar Khuda.
Qatal Jab Shah-e-Karbala Ka Howa.
Aayi Sham-e-Ghareebaan Wawela.
Zulm Dhaney Barhey Jo Ahl-e-Jafa.

2
Ro Ke Zainab Ne Di Sada Ghazi.
Chin Rahi Hai Meri Rida Ghazi.
Mere Sar Par Nahi Koi Pardah.
Kaisay Darbar Jaon Gi Bhaiyya.

3
Hai Bara Sakht Marhala Ghazi.
Tu Sabeel Aisi Aaj Koi Bana.
Be Rida Mujh Ko Koi Dekhey Na.
Aa Rahi Hai Mujhe Haya Ghazi.

4
Rah Kanton Se Pur Hai Wawela.
Kis Tarha Tey Kare Ga Yeh Rasta.
Mera Beemar Karbala Ghazi.
Yaad Nana Ki Jab Bhi Aaye Gi.

5
Kis Ke Chehray Ko Ab Main Dekhon Gi.
Mera Akbar Tu Mar Gaya Ghazi.
Taptay Sehra Mein Rooz-e-Ashora.
Bosa Gah-e-Nabi Pe Wawela.

6
Khanjar-e-Shimar Bhi Chala Ghazi.
Qaid-e-Ghurbat Mein Shah-e-Dein Ka Pisar.
Aankhon Se Ro Raha Hai Khon-e-Jigar.
Hai Ghamo Ki Yeh Inteha Ghazi.

7
Bazuon Mein Mere Rasan Hai Bandhi.
Kis Tarha Chehra Main Chupaon Gi.
Pathron Ka Hai Silsila Ghazi.
Hain Tamachay Addo Ne Youn Marey.

8
Dono Rukhsar Ho Gaye Neelay.
Hai Sakina Pe Yeh Jafa Ghazi.
Charon Janib Hai Majma-e-Agyar.
Aane Wala Hai Sham Ka Bazar.

9
De Mujhe Aa Ke Hosla Ghazi.
Aye Wajih Our Hilal Gham Se Howa.
Aal-e-Ahmad Mein Aik Hashar Bapa.
Ro Ke Zainab Ne Jab Kaha Ghazi.
