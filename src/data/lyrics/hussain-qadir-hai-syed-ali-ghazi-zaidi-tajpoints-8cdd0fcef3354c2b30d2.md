1
Hussain Qadir Hai Syed Ali Ghazi Zaidi.
Hai Matami Ka Aqeeda Hussain Qadir Hai.
Khuda Ke Izn Se Maula Hussain Qadir Hai.
Qasam Khuda Ki Wirasat Mein Aayi Hai Qudrat.

2
Qadeer Baap Ka Beta Hussain Qadir Hai.
Jo Sheh Ke Faiz Se Fitrus Ko Mil Gaye Sheh Par.
Malak Woh Urr Ke Pukara Hussain Qadir Hai.
Mere Naseeban Wich Likhi Nayi Aoulad Aye.

3
Keeti Hussain Meri Jholi Aabad Aye.
Sat Putar De Aakhiya Rahib Tu Ja Hussain Qadir Hai.
Yeh Karbala Mein Kaha Hur Se Bint-e-Zahra Ne.
Tere Naseeb Pe Mera Hussain Qadir Hai.

4
Bata Raha Hai Yeh Sajday Ka Tool Mufti Ko.
Teri Namaz Pe Mera Hussain Qadir Hai.
Kaha Habib Se Haider Ke Lal Ne Hans Kar.
Main Hukam Deta Hoon Uth Ja Hussain Qadir Hai.

5
Nahi Hai Koi Zarorat Issay Ijazat Ki.
Jo Chahta Hai Karey Ga Hussain Qadir Hai.
Sina Ki Nook Pe Quraan Sunaya Jata Hai.
Pukari Dekh Ke Fizza Hussain Qadir Hai.

6
Janab-e-Jaun Bhi Yousuf Dikhein Gay Mehshar Mein.
Kahey Gi Khud Yeh Zulaikha Hussain Qadir Hai.
 Hussain-o- Ghazi Hoon Ya Phir:AIMAAD NAQVI: Ho.
Lagaya Sab Ne Yeh Narah Hussain Qadir Hai.
