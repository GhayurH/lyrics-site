1
Ghar Mein Allah Ke Ali Par Yun Syed Ahsan Abbas Baquri
Ghar Mein ALLAH Ke Ali Par Youn Zulm Ummat Ne Haye Dhaya Hai.
Tha Amama Ali Ka Khon Mein Tar Sar Ko Sajday Se Jab Uthaya Hai.
Peet Kar Jibraeel Ne Sar Ko Di Sadaein Yeh Ro Ke Sidrah Se.
Hai Ghazab Hajiyon Ne Masjid Mein Deen Ke Kabay Ko Giraya Hai.
Jis Ali Ko Nabi Ne Kabay Mein Apne Kandhoon Pe Khud Uthaya Hai.
Haye Afsoos Oss Ka Ummat Ne Khon Masjid Mein Hi Bahaya Hai.
Khon Mein Tar Amama Dekha Tu Betiyon Ki Ridaein Yaad Aaein.
Dil Pe Haider Ke Zakhm Kofay Mein Sham Walon Ne Youn Lagaya Hai.
Qatal-e-Zahra Kiya Madine Mein Taig Ali Par Chalai Kofay Mein.
Haye Sadaat Ko Saqeefay Ne Ja Baja Iss Qadar Sataya Hai.
Taig Sar Par Lagi Jo Maula Ko Khoon Bikharne Laga Musalay Par.
Youn Bikhar Kar Lahu Ne Haider Ko Manzar-e-Karbala Dikhaya Hai.
Haye Ummat Yeh Pyasa Marey Gi Oss Ke Bachoon Ko Dasht Mein Jis Ne.
Apne Qatil Ko Apne Hissay Ka Jam-e-Sharbat Bhi Khud Pilaya Hai.
Bugz Khum Se Tha Ya Saqeefay Ka Our Badar-o-Ohad Ke Badlay Mein.
Zarb Sar Par Ali Ne Khayi Hai Fatima Ne Tamacha Khaya Hai.
Ibtida Jo Hoi Hai Kofay Mein Inteha Iss Ki Karbala Mein Hai.
Ro Ke Zainab Ko Apne Seene Se Keh Ke Maula Ne Yeh Lagaya Hai.
Zakhmi Pehlu Ali Ko Zahra Ka Yaad Tab Ahsan-o- Asad Aaya.
De Ke Hasnain Ne Sahara Jab Zakhmi Baba Ko Khud Uthaya Hai.
