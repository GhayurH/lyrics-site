Jehray Safraan Che Milay Ch Faiyaz Party
Jehray Safraan Che Miley Tere Dard Main Wandawaan Sajjad Toon Dasa.

1
Karbala Jana Aye Rawaan De Wich Na Abid Akhiyaan Di Rat Wadha.
Main Deen-e-Khuda Da Waris Haan Maa Kiwein Dard Sunawaan.
Teri Qaid Muki Ik Saal Bajoon Tainu Kiwein Khon Rawawaan.
Sare Dukh Ik Pasay, Ik Pasay Jehra Takiya Main Tainu Be Rida.

2
Mainu Moray Ne Sir Musalmana Jiwein Lutda Maal Koi Moray.
Akbar De Sir Nu Ghusal Deway Zulfaan Choon Khon Nachorey.
De Ke Do Mawaan Ne Sughra Da Veer Akbar Liya Deen Nu Bacha.
Shabeer Nu Pursa Dena Aye Sajjad Kiwein Odi Bachri Da.

3
Ki Dasaan Ki Oday Naal Hoya Choon Salaan Di Wichri Da.
Kehra Aakhay Ohno Zindana Wich Sakina Le Aakhri Ne Sah.
Jadon Qafila Turya Karbal Taun La Warsaan Waang Rawaiyaan.
Karbal Taun Sham Diyaan Rawaan Aina Khon De Naal Sajaiyaan.

4
Digday Onthaan Taun Ray Mawaan De Lal Abid Aina Aounth Le Nasa.
Mainu Bheer Che Ro Ro Kehndi Si Mera Zanjeer Halaway.
Oh Mere Ohlay Chup Jaway Jadon Shimar Si Nazri Aaway.
Neelay Rukhsar Mere Kan Dukhday Veer Mere Deway Hoor Na Saza.

5
Meri Kand Te Koray Barasday Ray Pairaan Choon Khon Si Risda.
Turya Na Jaway Bheer Wichon Zalim Si Mainu Khichda.
Wekh Nayi Sakda Main Haye Mur Ke Phophiyaan Mawaan Bazar De Oh Rah.
Aina Zalimaan Mere Babay Da Haye Pehle Watan Choraya.

6
Pardais Na Tapdiyaan Raitaan Te Haye Sir Mazloom Da Laya.
Kyun Zibha Kita Si Pyasa Si Tin Dina Da Haye Sabar Da Khuda.
Tin Onthaan Te Ik Qafila Si Jehra Chehlum Karan Layi Aaya.
Veraan Diyaan Qabraan Nu Najfi Zainab Ne Gal Nal Laya.

7
Ro Ke Sajjad Kehya Maa Rokan Wala Koi Nayi Veraan De Wain Pa.
Noha Khuwan Party Ch Faiyaz Party Sialkot.
Matami Sangat
Anjuman-e-Sipah-e-Ahl-e-Bait Sialkot
