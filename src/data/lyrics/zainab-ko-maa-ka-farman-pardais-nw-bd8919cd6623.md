Zainab ko maa ka farman, pardais main rulaye

Piyasay ko kesay pani, piyasi behan pilaye

Baba to karbala main, us kay shaheed ho gaye

bazu kata kay chacha darya pay wo bhi sogaye

ab kon jo Sakina, ko shimr say bachaye

Dhonda bohat hay shah nay, sehra main Ibn e Shabbar

Qasim ko jab sameta bolay Hussain ro kar

ye laash lay kay khaimay, Shabbir kesay jaye

Yarab kisi ki baiti, na aisie ho satayeii

na samnay behn Kay, ho qatl koi bhai

piyasi qabar na koi, pardais main banaye

Ahmad ki betion Kay, balway main sar khulay hain

Sar ko jhukaye Abid khamosh chal rahay hain

Sajjad ki aseeri, kainaa’t ko rulaye

Pursay ki ais jahan main, her rasam hai batati

Marta hai jab bhi koi duniya Hasan hai jaati

Laikin Ghareeb Zahra, khud pursa lainay aye
