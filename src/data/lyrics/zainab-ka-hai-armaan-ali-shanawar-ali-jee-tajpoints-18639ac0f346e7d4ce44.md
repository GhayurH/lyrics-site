Zainab Ka Hai Armaan Ali Shanawar Ali Jee.

1
Zainab Ka Hai Arman Hussain Ka Matam.
Chodah So Baras Se Hai Dunya Mein Qayam.
Sajday Mein Sar Kataya Islam Ko Bachaya.
Shabeer Ne Jaan De Ke Waday Ko Nibhaya.
Ahmad Ka Nawasa Tha Zahra Ka Dulara Tha.
Nu Lakh Laeeno Ne Pyasa Jisay Mara Tha.

2
Iksath San-e-Hijjri Ko Daswein Ki Qayamat Ko.
Chodah So Baras Beetay Maula Ki Shahadat Ko.
Islam Ke Mohsin Ka Sar Naizay Pe Aaya Hai.
Sadaat Ke Khaimon Ko Aada Ne Jalaya Hai.
Har Saal Muharram Mein Ik Soag Ke Aalam Mein.
Masroof Zamana Hai Mazloom Ke Matam Mein.

3
Ashor Zamane Mein Paigham Yeh Lata Hai.
Shabeer Ki Ghurbat Par Jo Ashk Bahata Hai.
Zindaan Ke Andherey Mein Ghurbat Ke Saweray Mein.
Dafnaya Sakina Ko Abid Ne Jo Kurtay Mein.
:REHAN-o-SHANAWAR: Ne Sarwar Ne:ALI JEE: Ne.
Nohon Ki Ibadat Se Aabad Rakhay Seene.
