Hy Zainab veer di maut diyan ghariyaan ghunaindi reh gaye

Nap kay der khaimay da hy, takraan marayndi reh gaye.

Lab baysheer day hil gaaye bahan pani

Ropayee gurbat taun baun nimani

Tin dinaan taun pyasi hy

Paani mangayndi rehgaye.

Veer day walaan chay hath Shimar da

Saamnay bhaynee day haaye kusda

Hun tay Ghazi aaway haaye

Wal wal saddayndee rehgaye.

Na Hussain koun maarein wasta ay

Kalli bhaynee da ay asra ae

Veer day qatil koun hy

Mintaan karayndee rehgaye.

Ghauray bhajday rehgaaye dhayndi rehgaye

Ban kay baybas bibi rondi rehgaye

Cha kay dhee kamsin koun hy

Baba dayndi rehgaye.

Veer saaray tur gaaye Shaam thee gaye

Ek munadi pal wich aam thi gaye

Lut ghinno hun burqay haaye

Abid bachayndi reh gaye.
