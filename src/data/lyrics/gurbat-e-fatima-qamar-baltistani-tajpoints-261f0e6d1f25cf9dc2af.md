1
Gurbat e Fatima Qamar Baltistani Lyrics
Zameen Pe Arsh Giraya Gaya Madine Mein.
Jo Syeda Ko Rulaya Gaya Madine Mein.
Sadaein Baba Ko Detey Hoye Jo Roi Batool.

2
Jigar Ko Tham Ke Jannat Mein Ro Rahe They Rasool.
Khuda Ke Ghar Ko Jalaya Gaya Madine Mein.
Shaheed Ho Gaye Mohsin Ujar Gayi Zahra.
Kaha Batool Ne Ro Kar Khozaini Ya Fizza.

3
Ali Ka Khon Bahaya Gaya Madine Mein.
Nabi Ke Ghar Ko Musalman Hi Jalatay They.
Ali Ko Bandh Ke Rasiyon Mein Woh Phiratay They.
Youn Fatima Ko Sataya Gaya Madine Mein.

4
Boland Sholay They Har Simt Rotay They Bachay.
Nabi Ki Beti Thi, Zalim Tha, Taziyane They.
Youn Karbala Ko Dikhaya Gaya Madine Mein.
Nabi Ki Beti Ke Chehray Pe Neel Tha Gehra.

5
Chupati Reh Gayi Zainab Se Apna Woh Chehra.
Sitam Ka Farsh Bichaya Gaya Madine Mein.
Baqi Se Kabay Ko Jab Bhi Hawaein Aati Hain.
Bonaiyya Mehdi Ki Ab Bhi Sadaein Aati Hain.

6
Sitam Woh Zahra Pe Dhaya Gaya Madine Mein.
Aye Shams Jin Ki Sadaqat Bharam Thi Nabiyon Ka.
Unhein Pukar Ke Darbar-e-Aam Mein Jhota.
Yeh Kaisa Ehd Nibhaya Gaya Madine Mein?
