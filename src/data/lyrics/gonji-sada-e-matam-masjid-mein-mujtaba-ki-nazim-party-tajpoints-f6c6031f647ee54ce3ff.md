1
Gonji Sada e Matam Masjid Mein Mujtaba Ki Nazim Party.
Dastar Khoon Mein Tar Hai Haroon e Mustafa Ki.
Jibreel Ka Hai Noha Kya Zulm Dhaya Diya Hai.
Kofay Mein Deen e Haq Ka Kaba Gira Diya Hai.

2
Qatal e Ali Se Haye Ummat Ne Na Haya Ki.
Seene Laga Ke Maula Zainab Ko Ro Paray They.
Ro Ro Ke Phir Ali Ne Beti Ke Hath Chomay.
Bolay Kathin Hai Manzil Ab Beti Karbala Ki.

3
Zarbat Dikha Rahi Hai Manzar Ajab Ali Ko.
Manzar Mein Dekhtey Hain Kofay Ki Har Ghali Ko.
Our Fikar Kha Rahi Hai Zainab Teri Rida Ki.
Zahra Ka Ghar Ujarney Ke Aa Gaye Zamane.

4
Jaisay Tamacha Khaya Kal Haye Fatima Ne.
Khaye Gi Ab Tamachay Aoulad Fatima Ki.
Karne Ko Haye Matam ALLAH Ke Wali Ka.
Pursa Khuda Ko Deney Mazlomi e Ali Ka.

5
Aati Hai Matami Saf Masjid Mein Anbiya Ki.
Badlah Asad Ab Iss Ka Mehdi Hi Lein Gay Aa Kar.
Masjid Mein Jo Khuda Ka Chehra Tha Khon Mein Tar.
Tauheen Ki Gayi Thi Masjid Mein Yeh Khuda Ki.
