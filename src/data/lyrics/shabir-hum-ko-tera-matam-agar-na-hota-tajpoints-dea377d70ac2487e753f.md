Shabir Hum Ko Tera Matam Agar Na Hota.
Marte Bhi Na Agar Tu Jeena Hunar Na Hota.

1
Bekar Khoon Ki Gardish Be Sood Dharkane Thin.
Matam Ke Hath Ka Jo Dil Pe Asar Na Hota.
Khushiyon Mein Ghar Ke Pathar Ban Jata Dil Hamara.
Iss Mein Jo Shah-e-Dein Ka Gham Jalwa Gar Na Hota.

2
Matam Jo Karta Tu Bhi Tu Dil Mein Tere Waiz.
Hirs-e-Jina Na Hoti Dozakh Ka Dar Na Hota.
Aab-e-Hayat Dard-e-Shabir Gar Na Banta.
Tu Zehar-e-Dard-e-Dunya Youn Be Zar Na Hota.

3
Hoti Damishq Tak Phir Roze Ki Tere Wosat.
Gar Dafan Karbala Mein Hi Tera Sar Na Hota.
Tu Sar Jo Na Katata Tu Aaj Iss Jahan Mein.
Muslim Zaror Hote Islam Par Na Hota.

4
Sun-ni Na Hoti Rab Ne Gar Khud Azan-e-Akbar.
Jo Shab Thi Shab Hi Rehti Waqt-e-Seher Na Hota.
Ye Shair Gar Na Kehta Milti Baqa Na Mujh Ko.
Marta Zaror Zaidi Lekin Amar Na Hota.
