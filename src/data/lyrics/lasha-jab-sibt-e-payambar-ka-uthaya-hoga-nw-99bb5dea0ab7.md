Lasha Jab Sibt-e-Payambar Ka Uthaya Hoga.

Khuld Main Chain Muhammad Ko Na Aya Hoga

Saar Khulay Lehad Say Tab Fatima Nikli Hongi
Zehar Jaida Nay Jo Shabbar Ko Pelaya Hoga

Qabar Main Hassan Tu Us Waqt Hi Utray Hongay
Maa Nay Jab Daman-e-Tatheer Bichaya Hoga

Dil Kay Sab Hassan Nay Arman Nikalay Hongay
Sehra Qasim Ko Khayalon Main Lagaya Hoga

Dastan Hassan Nay Apni Jo Sunayi Hogi
Maa Nay Ro Ro Kay Kalayjay Say Lagaya Hoga

Jo Kay Madar Kay Janazay Pay Na Ghaar Say Nikli
Kaisay Beemar Unhain Shaam Main Laya Hoga
