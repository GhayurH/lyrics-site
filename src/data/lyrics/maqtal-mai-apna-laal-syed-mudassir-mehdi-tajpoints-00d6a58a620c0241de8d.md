Maqtal Mein Apna Lal Sulaye Gi Fatima.
Zakhmi Pisar Ko Lori Sunaye Gi Fatima.

1
Lashay Ke Pas Bethi Rahe Gi Woh Raat Tak.
Shabbir Ko Na Chor Ke Jaye Gi Fatima.
Pathar Hataye Lash Se Zainab Ne Iss Liye.
Betay Ko Dhondti Hoi Aaye Gi Fatima.

2
Seene Se Phir Lagaye Gi Betay Ki Lash Ko.
Nu Lakh Teer Pehle Hataye Gi Fatima.
Girtay Hoye Hussain Ko Bazu Se Tham Kar.
Akbar Ki Lash Tak Ossay Laye Gi Fatima.

3
Do Lakht Pasliyon Se Hata Le Gi Apna Hath.
Zakhmi Pisar Se Zakhm Chupaye Gi Fatima.
Har Ik Qadam Pe Dekhey Gi Tukray Hussain Ke.
Ghazi Ko Har Qadam Pe Bulaye Gi Fatima.

4
Lash-e-Padar Pe De Gi Sakina Ko Hosla.
Ik Fatima Ko Seene Lagaye Gi Fatima.
Ban Kar Gawah Aaye Gi Zainab Bhi Murtajiz.
Mehshar Mein Jab Hussain Ko Laye Gi Fatima.
