Gham e Hussain Ka Maqsad Piaray Khan

1
Gham-e-Hussain Ka Maqsad Badal Nahi Sakta
Khiyam Jal Gaye, Paigham Jal Nahi Sakta
Yeh Baat Bhool Gaye Dushman-e-Abu Talib
Rasool Kufar Ke Daman Mein Pal Nahi Sakta
Hussain Zindah Hain, Jab Tak Zamana Baqi Hai
Yazidiyat Ka Shajar Phool Phal Nahi Sakta

2
Woh Jis Ki Aankh Mein Aanso Gham-e-Hussain Ke Hain
Yaqeen Hai Atish-e-Dozakh Mein Jal Nahi Sakta
Na Sheh Ko Subh-e-Alam Se Dara Shab-e-Ashor
Hussain Keh Dein Tu Soraj Nikal Nahi Sakta
Nikal Tu Aaye Ga Nawak Galuye Asghar Se
Magar Rubab Ke Dil Se Nikal Nahi Sakta
