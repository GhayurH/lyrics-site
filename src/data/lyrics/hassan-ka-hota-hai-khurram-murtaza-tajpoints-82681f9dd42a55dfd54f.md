Hassan Ka Hota Hai Khurram Murtaza.

1
Woh Toor-e-Husan Pe Jalwa Hasan Ka Hota Hai.
Nazool Arsh Se Maula Hasan Ka Hota Hai.
Qatar Bandhay Farishtay Hain Pas Jholay Ke.
Har Ik Ne Jhola Johlana Hasan Ka Hota Hai.
Khuda Tarashney Lagta Hai Dehar Ka Sorah.
Kabhi Jo Mannati Roza Hasan Ka Hota Hai.

2
Saron Ko Jaisay Hai Kar Deti Zulfiqar Qalam.
Qalam Se Waisa Hi Hamla Hasan Ka Hota Hai.
Hai Baap Betay Ka Ilm-e-Khuda Pe Aik Sa Haq.
Ali Ka Jitna Hai Utna Hasan Ka Hota Hai.
Fiza Mein Hoti Hai Khushbo Ali Ke Lehjay Ki.
Shorow Jaisay Hi Khutba Hasan Ka Hota Hai.

3
Hasan Zameen Pe Sakhawat Hi Itni Kartay Hain Keh.
Khuda Ke Arsh Pe Charcha Hasan Ka Hota Hai.
Nawaz Deti Hain Dolat Khadija Bibi Ossay.
Woh Jis Ke Ghar Mein Bhi Sikka Hasan Ka Hota Hai.
Wahan Pe Loag Hoon Sarsabz Kis Tarha Na Asad.
Jahan Labon Pe Qaseeda Hasan Ka Hota Hai.
