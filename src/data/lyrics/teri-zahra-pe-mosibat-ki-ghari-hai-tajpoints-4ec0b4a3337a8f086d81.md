1
Teri Zahra Pe Mosibat Ki Ghari Hai.
Kaise Darwaza Hataon.
Kaise Mohsin Ko Bacahon.
Saans Seene Mein Ruki Hai.
Hath Mein Jiss Ke Mera Hath Diya Tha Tum Ne.
Keh Ke Man Kuntu Jisse Maula Kaha Tha Tum Ne.
Main Ne Haar Moor Pe Baba.
Sath Oss Ka Hai Nibhaya.
Haan Mera Jurm Yehi Hain.
Teri Umat Ne Bana Di Hai Yeh Halat Baba.
Koi Pasli Bhi Nahi Meri Salamat Baba.
Saans Leti Hoon Main Jiss Dam.
Rukne Lagta Hai Mera Dam.
Sirf Marne Ki Kami Hai.
Kabhi Darbar Mein Peshi Ko Bolate Hain Humein.
Kabhi Darwaze Pe Aa Aa Ke State Hain Humein.
Mere Yeh Phool Se Bache.
Pochte Hain Yehi Mujh Se.
Kya Khata Hum Se Hoi Hai.
Sharm Atti Hai Bhala Tum Ko Bataon Kiase.
Yeh Tamacho Ke Nishaan Tum Ko Dikhaon Kaise.
Tum Na Pehchan Sako Gay.
Dekh Kar Mujh Ko Kaho Gay.
Kya Meri Zahra Yehi Hai.
Yeh Azeeyat Yeh Khamoshi Nahi Dekhi Jati.
Mujh Se Tanhai Ali Ki Nahi Dekhi Jati.
Tera Pegham Sunanne.
Haq Wilayat Ka Batane.
Zahra Ghar Ghar Mein Gayi Hai.
Aap Ke Soag Mein Tareek na Dekha Giya Ghar.
Aag Darwaze Pe Umat Ne Laga Di Aa kar.
Baba Jaan Baad Tumhare.
Dekhiye Ghar Mein Hamare.
Roshni Kaise Hoi Hai.
Qabar-e-Zahra Se Takallum Yeh Sada Aati Hai.
Kitni Sadiyon Se Meri Qabar Pe Veerani Hai.
Phool Turbat Peh Charha De.
Ik Diya La Ke Jala De.
Kya Madine Mein Koi Hai.
