1
Sattar Qadam Sy Pyasi Behen Dekhti Rahi Kazmi Brothers.
Pyasay Galay Pe Bhai Ke Chalti Rahi Churi.
Sattar Qadam Se Pyasi Behan Dekhti Rahi.
Kehti Thi Kya Issi Liye Peesi Thi Chakiyaan?.

2
Chadar Se Saaf Karti Thi Zakhmo Ko Ro Ke Maa.
Zakhmi Sar-e-Hussain Tha Zahra Ki Gaud Thi.
Rokay Hoye They Pyar Mein Khanjar Ki Dhaar Ko.
Betay Se Kitna Pyar Tha Duldul Sawar Ko.

3
Rakhay Hoye They Sheh Ke Galay Par Gala Ali.
Khanjar Se Sheh Ki Zulfein Hatanay Laga Laeen.
Darhi Ki Simt Hath Barhanay Laga Laeen.
Aankhon Pe Hath Rakhay Hoye Tha Har Ik Nabi.

4
Maqtal Mein Chaar Saal Ki Zahra Ko Gaud Mein.
Ik Bar Phir Utha Le Sakina Ko Gaud Mein.
Afsoos Qatilon Se Ijazat Nahi Mili.
Chehra Watan Ki Simt Woh Karta Tha Bar Bar.

5
Aankhon Mein Tha Bhara Howa Sughra Ka Intezar.
Haye Ghareeb-e-Zahra Ki Aankhein Khuli Hoi.
Kehta Tha Woh Isharon Mein Zainab Se Jao Ghar.
Aankhon Ko Band Kar Lo Keh Kat Jaye Mera Sar.

6
Seene Pe Sheh Ke Zanu Tha Rakhay Hoye Shaqi.
Lasha Furat Par Kabhi Utha Kabhi Gira.
Karb-o-Bala Mein Hashar Takallum Bapa Howa.
Abbas Tum Kahan Ho Sada Jab Yeh Sheh Ne Di.
