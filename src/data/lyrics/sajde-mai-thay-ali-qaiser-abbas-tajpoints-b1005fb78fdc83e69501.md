1
Shamsheer-e-Zulm Chal Gayi Sajday Mein They Ali.
Kofay Mein Qatal Ho Gaye Hain Murtaza Ali.
Hasnain Our Abbas Ko Zainab Ne Yeh Kaha.
Madar Ki Judai Ka Abhi Zakhm Tha Hara.
Ab Ja Rahe Hain Chor Kar Baba Mera Ali.
Chadar Tumhari Betiyon Ke Sar Na Rahe Gi.
Har Moor Pe Haye Qatilon Ki Bheer Rahe Gi.
Jaein Na Humein Chor Kar Mushkil Kusha Ali.
Aanso Baha Ke Kofay Ke Yeh Loag Kehte They.
Mushkil Kusha Hamari Tu Imdad Kartay They.
Maskeno Our Gareebon Ka Tha Aasra Ali.
Zainab Ke Sar Ko Chom Ke Bolay Meri Dukhtar.
Haider Ki Tarha Bolna Tum Sham Mein Ja Kar.
Aa Jaye Yaad Dunya Ko Shair-e-Khuda Ali.
 Qaiser, Zawar Maula Ne Abbas Se Kaha.
Rab Ne Hussain Ke Liye Beta Tujhe Chuna.
Karta Wafa Pe Naaz Hai Baba Tera Ali.
