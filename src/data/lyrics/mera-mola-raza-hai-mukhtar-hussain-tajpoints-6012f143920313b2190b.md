1
Mera Mola Raza Hai Mukhtar Hussain Fatehpuri
Aye Mere Maula Raza.
Aye Ghareeb Ul-Gurba.
Aye Shehanshah-e-Khurasaan.

2
Woh Jis Se Raza Mand Mera Maula Raza Hai.
Khush Bakht Hai Razi Ba Khuda Oss Se Khuda Hai.
Janat Ke Makeeno Ko Bhi Aata Hai Yahan Lutf.
Iss Shan Se Mehki Hoi Mashhad Ki Fiza Hai.

3
Kya Ho Gaya Gar Kar Liya Hum Ne Yahan Sajdah.
Iss Dar Pe Tu Islam Bhi Sajday Mein Para Hai.
Jab Main Ne Pukara Unhein Hal Ho Gayi Mushkil.
Ik Bar Nahi Aisa Har Ik Bar Howa Hai.

4
Jo Mangna Hai Mang Lo Mil Jaye Ga Tum Ko.
Mashhad Ke Shehanshah Ka Darbar Saja Hai.
Tu Khuld Mein Ja Sakta Hai Batlaon Ga Lekin.
Pehle Yeh Bata Tu Kabhi Mashhad Bhi Gaya Hai.

5
Jo Detey Hain Seene Pe Tere Naam Ki Dastak.
Bas Unn Ke Liye Khuld Ka Darwaza Khula Hai.
Hasil Hai Zamane Ka Tujhe:GOHAR-E-: Nayab.
 Mukhtar Tujhe Khas Yeh Maula Ki Atta Hai.
