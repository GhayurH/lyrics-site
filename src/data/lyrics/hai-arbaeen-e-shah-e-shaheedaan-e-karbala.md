Hai Arbaeen-e-Shah-e-Shaheedaan-e-Karbala
Pohanchay Hain Turbaton Pe Aseeran e Karbala

1
Matam Se Kanpta Hai Bayaban e Karbala
Zainab Pukari Goor e Ghareeban e Karbala
Lashay Pe Kar Na Payi Thi Matam Hussain Ka
Aayi Hoon Karne Sham Se Chehlum Hussain Ka

2
Bhaiyya Salam Zainab-e-Dilgeer Ka Salam
Ujari Hoi Batool Ki Tasveer Ka Salam
Aayi Hai Chut Ke Qaid Se Humsheer Ka Salam
Shano Pe Hai Jo Zulm Ki Tehreer Ka Salam

3
Ik Saal Qaid Katay Howon Ka Salam Hai
Ab Tak Hamari Aankhon Mein Zindan-e-Sham Hai
Kis Tarha Saal Hum Ne Guzara Hai Kya Kahin
Reh Reh Ke Hum Ko Logon Ne Mara Hai Kya Kahin

4
Zakhmi Badan Tamam Hamara Hai Kya Kahin
Par Dil Pe Zakhm Ab Bhi Tumhara Hai Kya Kahin
Zindan Mein Yeh Gham Tha Koi Gham Na Kar Sakay
Hum Saal Bhar Shaheedon Ka Matam Na Kar Sakay

5
Darya Pe Jab Se So Gaya Abbas-e-Bawafa
Sab Maan Totay Hum Ne Uthai Har Ik Jafa
Yeh Dagh Dil Pe Our Qayamat Gira Gaya
Abbas Hai Kahan Pe Yeh Kehte They Ashqiya

6
Ghazi Ko Jab Sakina Bulati Thi Bar Bar
Rukhsar Par Tamachay Woh Khati Thi Bar Bar
Har Zulm Se Na Oss Ko Main Bhayya Bacha Saki
Pyasi Rahi Bhi, Pyas Na Oss Ki Bhuja Saki

7
Sonpi Thi Aap Ne Jo Amanat Na La Saki
Afsoos Na Sakina Reha Ho Ke Aa Saki
Kya Kya Bataon Kitne Ghamo Ki Satayi Hoon
Zindaan Mein Oss Ki Qabar Bana Kar Main Aayi Hoon

8
Seh Roz Karbala Mein Hoi Majlis-e-Azza
Sehra Mein Shoor-o-Shain Ki Matam Ki Thi Sada
Qabron Pe Apne Pyaron Ki Matam Jo Kar Liya
Abid Ne Aa Ke Zainab-e-Muztar Se Yeh Kaha

9
Uthiye Phophi Keh Ashk Wahan Bhi Bahaein Gay
Pyason Ko Rotay Rotay Madine Mein Jaein Gay
Kyunkar Ghareeb Jaye Gi Pardais Se Watan
Mati Mein Mil Gaye Sabhi Zahra Ke Gulbadan

10
Iss Daag-e-Karbalai Se Hai Zindagi Kathin
Aye Kash Keh Yahan Pe Hi Mar Jaye Yeh Behan
Ja Kar Watan Mein Haal Sunaon Gi Kis Tarha
Sughra Se Apne Zakhm Chupaon Gi Kis Tarha

11
Hai Arbaeen Aye Mere Shabeer Marhaba
Pohanchi Hai Qabar Par Teri Humsheer Marhaba
Hogi Yahan Pe Rozon Ki Tameer Marhaba
Uth Kar Kaho Ke Zainab-e-Dilgeer Marhaba

12
Mashoor Kar Ke Teri Shahadat Ko Aayi Hoon
Baba Ki Main Bacha Ke Wilayat Ko Aayi Hoon
Matam Karo Keh Fasl-e-Azza Ahtatam Hai
Sheh Ki Azza Nahi Hai Tu Jeena Haram Hai

13
Hum Hain Hasan Anees Gham-e-Na Tamam Hai
Sansein Rawaan Hain Arzoye Inteqam Hai
Kaisay Dilon Ke Chain Ko Hum Alwida Kahin
Kaisay Gham-e-Hussain Ko Hum Alwida Kahin
