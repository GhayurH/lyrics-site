Basheer Hai Pyasa Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.
Be Sheer Hai Pyasa Mera Be Sheer Hai Pyasa.

1
Pardais Mein Ik Ujari Hoi Maa Ka Tha Noha.
Takleef Ke Aasar Namodar Hain Rukh Par.
Pyasa Hai Kayi Roz Se Sadqay Ho Yeh Madar.
Do Bond Issay Pani Pila Laiye Maula.

2
Shabeer Se Phir Kehne Lagi Bano-e-Dilgeer.
Lillah Karein Shah-e-Huda Pani Ki Tadbeer.
Maula Kahin Mar Jaye Na Pyasa Mera Bacha.
Sarwar Ne Kaha Gham Na Karo Bano-e-Muztar.

3
Pehnao Issay Kaprey Naye Madar-e-Asghar.
Hum Le Ke Chaley Jaein Gay Iss Ko Soye Darya.
Teyar Kiya Asghar-e-Be Sheer Ko Maa Ne.
Barh Ke Liya Aagosh Mein Phir Shah-e-Zamaan Ne.

4
Our Soch Ke Maa Baap Ne Paihum Kiya Giriya.
Bano Ne Kaha Jata Hai Aada Ke Muqabil.
Aye Maula Yehi Soch Ke Phatta Hai Mera Dil.
Kamsin Hai Kahin Dar Nahi Jaye Mera Beta.

5
Al-Garz Kaha Shah Ne Maidan Mein Ja Kar.
Pani Ka Talabgar Hoon Aye Foaj-e-Sitamgar.
Darkar Hai Kamsin Ke Liye Aab Zarra Sa.
Pani Na Diya Teer-e-Sitam Halq Pe Mara.

6
Our Nana Mujahid Soye Firdos Sidhara.
Maidan Mein Ro Ro Ke Yeh Maula Ne Pukara.
Aye Katib-e-Taqdeer Duhai Hai Duhai.
Nanhi Si Lehad Hath Se Sarwar Ne Banai.

7
Dafna Ke Kaha Arz-e-Bala Le Tujhe Sonpa.
Kartay Hain Boka Syed-e-Sajjad Ke Baba.
 Yasir Dil-e-Bano Pe Kara Waqt Hai Aaya.
Sheh Aate Hain Hathoon Pe Liye Bachay Ka Lasha.
