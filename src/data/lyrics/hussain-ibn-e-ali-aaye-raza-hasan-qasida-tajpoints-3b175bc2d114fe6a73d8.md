1
Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Aaye Raza Hasan Qasida.
Jahan Mein Rehmatein Le Kar Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Aaye.
Nahi Jin Ka Koi Humsar Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Aaye.
Tha Fitrus Naam Jis Ke Par Khuda Ne They Jala Dalay.
Jisay They Dekh Kar Ibrat Pakarte Aasmaan Walay.
Ossi Be Par Ko Deney Par Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Aaye.
Khuda Jin Pe Hai Nazaan Aaj Woh Dunya Mein Aaye Hain.
Hoye Kaun-o-Makaan Mein Har Taraf Rehmat Ke Saye Hain.
Hoye Masror Hain Sarwar Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Aaye.
Ali-o-Fatima Ke Ghar Mein Aaya Hai Nageen Aisa.
Qasam Yazdaan Ki Dunya Mein Nahi Koi Haseen Aisa.
Sarapa Noor Ke Paikar Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Aaye.
Sana Khuwaan Hoon Main Panjtan Ka Hisab-e-Qabar Kya Lein Gay.
Farishtay Lehad Mein Aa Kar Yehi Mujh Ko Nida Dein Gay.
Utha Khursheed Apna Sar Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Aaye.
