1
Be Dard Musalmaan Tu Khushiyaan Mana Rahe Hain.
Arab-o-Ajam Ke Maula Dunya Se Ja Rahe Hain.
Kuch Tu Lehaz Kartay Damad-e-Mustafa Ka.
Sajday Mein Jo Ali Pe Haye Zulm Dha Rahe Hain.

2
Ahmad Ke Baad Zahra Ab Chal Paray Ali Bhi.
Aasaar Panjtan Ke Zalim Mita Rahe Hain.
Baloon Ko Kholay Zainab, Kulsoom Ro Rahi Hain.
Ghar Ghar Charag Kofi Shami Jala Rahe Hain.

3
Barsein Gay Teer Paihum Taboot Par Hasan Ke.
Maula Yeh Waqt-e-Aakhir Ro Ke Bata Rahe Hain.
Niklay Hain Aag Le Kar Kuch Loag Saqeefa Se.
Sadaat Ke Gharon Ko Ab Tak Jala Rahe Hain.

4
Tu Seekh Ghar Mein Chalna Woh Sham Ka Safar Hai.
Teri Qaid Ke Zamane Nazdeek Aa Rahe Hain.
Maula Ke Janazay Par Jibreel Ro Ke Bolay.
 Sardar Anbiya Ke Ghamkhuwar Ja Rahe Hain.
