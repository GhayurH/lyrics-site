1
Allah ho Akbar, haey maida sona Akbar
Day aakhri puttar azaan
Howay deen toun aj qurbaan

2
1) Par putar ashaadan Laillah hai tera istakbaal karan
Ayuhu rab di haii manshaa, chand ghariyaan da mehmaan

3
2) Sab dayn gawaii arz-o-sama mera nana paak Rasullallah
Aaye rondi maan Zahra, teri bachra sunan azaan

4
3) Kaho heya ala kairal amal, asa piyu putar-a-nai hona katil
Naii karni ummat haya, mun khaliq da farmaan

5
4) Mera baba Imamul mutaqeen, terai lah unal honiay surkh zameen
Piya qambda-e-Karbobla, tera katil da sun aylaan

6
5) Aa Ibrahim Khalillullah, hona Akbar barchi naal zibah
Akiyaan tu patiya laa, nai barchi kichan-e-asaan

7
6) Honda putar akihyaan da noor Akbar, ai ha jag da haii dastoor Akbar
Nahi jiundai piyu bachra, jadoun marday putar jawaan
