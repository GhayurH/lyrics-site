Aa gaya wapas janaza khuld ke sardar ka
Ban gaya matam kada ghar,  Ahmed e Mukhtar ka

1
Na chalao na chalao teer is ki laash par
Ye nawasa hay laeeno,  Syed e Abrar ka

2
Khainchti hay laash se teeron ko bint e Fatima
Ab nigahon may hay manzar,  Shaam ke bazaar ka

3
Ya Hasan lut jaye gi chadar teri hamsheer ki
Samna bhai karay ga khanjar e ghamkhar ka

4
Momino taaza hua phir se fadak ka masala
Aashkara ho gaya,  ab raaz us inkaar ka

5
Qol sab jhutla diye ummat ne Shah e deen kay
Bugz ghasab ke dilon may,  Haider e Karrar ka

6
Kahir ho Shehzad bint e Fatima ki khair ho
Pesh laazim aaye ga,  ab waqea darbar ka
