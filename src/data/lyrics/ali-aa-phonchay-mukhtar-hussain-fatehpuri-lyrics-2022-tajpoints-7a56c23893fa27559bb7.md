Ali Aa Phonchay Mukhtar Hussain Fatehpuri Write Up
Abhi Tu Bint-e-Asad Thin Tawaf-e-Kaba Mein.

1
Keh Pathron Pe Khushi Se Ajeeb Lali Thi.
Khuda Ka Shukar Keh Ik Simt Ruk Gayin Warna.
Har Aik Simt Se Dewar Phatney Wali Thi.
Bool Uthi Kabay Ki Dewar Ali Aa Pohanchay.

2
Mere Qibla Mere Sarkar Ali Aa Pohanchay.
Aaj Se Pehle Tu Main Kya Tha Faqat Aik Makaan.
Zoja-e-Hazrat-e-Imran Ka Hai Yeh Ahsaan.
Ab Mujhe Mil Gaye Ghar Bar Ali Aa Pohanchay.

3
Fatha Khaybar Na Howa Jab Tu Nabi They Hairaan.
Kiya Jibreel Ne Tab Aa Ke Nabi Se Yeh Biyaan.
Gham Na Khaein Mere Sarkar Ali Aa Pohanchay.
Ghar Mein ALLAH Ke Jab Aaye Ali Shair-e-Khuda.

4
Dant Kar Hatif-e-Ghaibi Ne Button Se Yeh Kaha.
Chor De Ghar Mera Agyar Ali Aa Pohanchay.
Kya Bhala Misar Ke Bazar Ka Ho Zikar Wahan.
Khud Muhammad Bhi Khuda Bhi Ho Kharedar Jahan.

5
Chal Gaya Makkay Ka Bazar Ali Aa Pohanchay.
Hakim-e-Sham Zara Poch Padar Se Apne.
Badar Ki Jang Mein Ajdad Tere Aaye Tu They.
Bhagay Sab Phaink Ke Hathyar Ali Aa Pohanchay.

6
Soormaon Ke Muqabil Jonhi Aate They Ali.
Thartharatay Hoye Aapas Mein Woh Kehte They Yehi.
Ab Yahan Rukna Hai Bekar Ali Aa Pohanchay.
Qabar Mein Matam-e-Sarwar Ka Sila Youn Paya.

7
Ban Gayi Qabar-e-Azadar Najaf Ka Saya.
Bool Uthay Sheh Ke Azadar Ali Aa Pohanchay.
Mere Naron Ka Jonhi Taba Najaf Shoor Gaya.
Kahan Mushkil Gayi Our Oss Ka Kahan Zoor Gaya.

8
Main Tha Mushkil Mein Giraftar Ali Aa Pohanchay.
Aa Gaye Majid-o- Mukhtar Jo Kabay Mein Ali.
Shoor Utha Toor Pe Woh Noor Howa Tha Jo Jali.
Kar Lo Oss Noor Ka Dedar Ali Aa Pohanchay.
