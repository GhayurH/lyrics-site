1
Roze Nabi Taun Aayi Aye Ahmad Di Aye Sada Sheraz Khan.
Aa Wekh Zahra Aa Ke Aj Mariya Aye Pehla Tehreer Da Gawah.
Mehnat Da Meri Lokaan Ki Ajar Moriya Aye Haye Maan Musalmana Aynj Mera Toriya Aye.
Teraan Te Wekhiya Aye Sari Hayat Jis Da Banda Saan Zuljinah.

2
Shabbir De Galay Te Khanjar V Chalana Aye Haye Tainu Bachri Zainab Janglaan Na Turana Aye.
Lekhaan Cha Teri Likhiya Yasrab Taun Sham Taein Qabraan Da Silsila.
Taboot-e-Mujtaba Te Barey Zulm Aye Kamaya Haye Zehar Dita Jodah Ik Teer Aye Chalaya.
Mere Hasan Di Maiyyat Teraan Naal Surkh Kar Ke Ditti Lokaan Aynj Wala.

3
Shabbir Roya Qasim Teraan Na Hath Nu Pa Ke Haye Ghazi Janazay Nu Le Jawein Kand Te Cha Ke.
Ro Intezar Kardi Haye Ladlay Hasan Da Baqiye Che Fatima.
Har Matami Ne Mehdi Ayho Gham De Wain Paye Haye Deen Pursa Maula Taboot Le Ke Aaye.
Gal Pa Ke Kalay Kharey Tosif Janazay Te Paye Wain Murtaza.
