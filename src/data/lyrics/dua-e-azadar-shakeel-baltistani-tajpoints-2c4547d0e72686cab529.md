Dua e Azadar Shakeel Baltistani Lyrics
Dil Mein Hai Teri Hi Ziyarat Ki Dua.
Karb-o-Bala Karb-o-Bala Karb-o-Bala.
Sab Se Pehle Aye Sakhi Shukar Karta Hoon Ada.
Rooz-e-Khilqat Se Hi Main Tera Hubdar Bana.
Thapkiyaan Maa Ne Jo Deein Woh Banein Seena Zani.
Loriyon Mein Bhi Suna Karbala Karb-o-Bala.
Dil Ki Dharkan Bhi Kahey Ya Hussain Haye Hussain.
Deed Se Pehle Kahin Dam Na Ruk Jaye Hussain.
Tujh Ko Jabir Ki Qasam Aye Shehanshah-e-Ummam.
Apne Rozay Pe Bola Mujh Ko Zawwar Bana.
Saal Bhar Dekhta Hoon Tere Rozay Ki Taraf.
Haye Ab Tak Na Mila Mujh Ko Lekin Yeh Sharf.
Laut Aata Hai Koi Jab Bhi Turbat Se Teri.
Oss Ki Khak-e-Kaf-e-Pa, Rota Hoon Takta Howa.
Izn Tera Jo Miley Joor Loon Rakht-e-Safar.
Saal Bhar Faqa Karon Tu Bhi Razi Ho Magar.
Jab Bhi Fehrist Banay Nam-e-Abbas Likhe.
Hoga Ahsan Tera Naam Likh Dena Mera.
Oss Sakina Ki Qasam Ladli Thi Jo Teri.
Jo Ziyarat Ka Tere Le Ke Arman Gayi.
Youn Pareshan Main Hoon, Jaisay Zindan Mein Hoon.
Qaid Se Kar De Reha, Apne Rozay Pe Bola.
Izn Tera Jo Miley Sar Ke Bal Aaon Ga Main.
Har Qadam Misal-e- Sahaab Ashk Barsaon Ga Main.
Teri Turbat Pe Sakhi Jaan Nikal Jaye Meri.
Meri Takhleeq Ko Youn Hogi Mairaj Atta.
