Syeda Ya Fatima Minhal Abbas.
Syeda Ya Fatima Ya Tahira.

1
Aap Ka Humsar Zamane Mein Kahan.
Aap Ke Dar Par Jhuki Hai Yeh Jabeen.
Aap Sa Darain Mein Koi Nahi.
Aap Ke Zair-e-Asar Sara Jahan.

2
Dukhtar-e-Khair-Al-Wara Bhi Aap Hain.
Zoja-e-Mushkil Kusha Bhi Aap Hain.
Shabar-o-Shabeer Ki Hain Aap Maa.
Dijiye Aoulad Be Aoulad Ko.

3
Kijiye Aabad Har Barbad Ko.
Ab Hata Dein Gham Ka Bibi Saibaan.
Dijiye Khairat Har Maqroz Ko.
Kijiye Aazad Har Mabhoos Ko.

4
Aap Ka Dar Chor Kar Jaein Kahan.
Wasta Hai Asghar-e-Masoom Ka.
Wasta Hai Zainab-o-Kulsoom Ka.
Door Kar Dein Har Balaye Na Gehan.

5
Kijiye Manzoor Meri Iltija.
Dekhna Chahon Diyar-e-Karbala.
Mujh Pe Bhi Ho Ik Nigah-e-Meharbaan.
Yeh Qalaq Hai Keh Aap Ko Tarpa Diya.

6
Oss Laeen Ne Aap Ko Jhutla Diya.
Aap Ko Rotay Hain Arz-o-Aasmaan.
Haye Woh Darbar Jana Aap Ka.
Haq Luta Ke Laut Aana Aap Ka.

7
Ashk Aankhon Se Hamari Hain Rawaan.
Aap Ke Hi Naam Se Tauqeer Hai.
Ba Asar Jo Bhi Meri Tehreer Hai.
Kijiye Apni Rida Ka Saibaan.
