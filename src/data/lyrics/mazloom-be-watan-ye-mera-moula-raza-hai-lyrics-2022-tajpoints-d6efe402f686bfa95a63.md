1
Mazloom Be Watan Ye Mera Moula Raza Hai Lal Hussain Haideri.
Zalim Ne Zehar Logo Mere Shah Ko Diya Hai.
Mamoon Abbasi Ki Inayat Hai Yeh Logo.
Haye Aal-e-Payamber Ki Itaat Hai Yeh Logo.
Syedon Se Zara Dekho Yeh Ummat Ki Wafa Hai.
Sab Aankhein Karo Band Duhai Hai Duhai.
Ik BiBi Azadaro Yahan Sham Se Aayi.
Lipti Hai Janazay Se Nahi Sar Pe Rida Hai.
Aaye Hain Janazay Pe Nabi Shah-e-Ummam Bhi.
Kulsoom, Ruqaiya Bhi Hain Masooma-e-Qum Bhi.
Woh Bibi Hai Karbal Mein Jali Jis Ki Abba Hai.
Youn Loota Musalmano Ne Zahra Ke Chaman Ko.
Haye Zehar Diya Jaisay Mere Maula Hasan Ko.
Dohrayi Gayi Haye Wohi Iss Pe Jafa Hai.
Masooma-e-Qum Deti Hain Ro Ro Ke Duhai.
Aye Kash Mila De Koi Mujh Se Mera Bhai.
Sughra Ki Tarha Haye Behan Jis Se Juda Hai.
Qurban Mere Maula Pe Ho Jan Hamari.
Maqroz Mere Maula Ki Yeh Dunya Hai Sari.
Yeh Martaba Ho Ke Bhi Ghareeb Ul-Gurba Hai.
Haye Rotay Farishtay Hain Bhi Zameen Our Zamaan Bhi.
Yeh Loh-o-Qalam Arsh-e-Barin Kaun-o-Makaan Bhi.
Khair-Un-Nisa Ke Lal Ke Matam Ki Sada Hai.
