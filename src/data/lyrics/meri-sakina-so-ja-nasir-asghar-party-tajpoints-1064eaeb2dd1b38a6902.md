1
Meri Sakina So Ja Nasir Asghar Party
Aa Ja Asghar Ki Jagah Meri Sakina So Ja.
Jagti Maa Ne Kaha Meri Sakina So Ja.
Jo Tujhey Khof Hai Shayad Main Ossay Kam Kar Doon.

2
Aa Ja Aankhon Pe Tere Nad-e-Ali Dam Kar Doon.
Door Kyun Bethi Hai Aa Meri Sakina So Ja.
Baap Ke Seene Pe Adat Hai Tujhey Soney Ki.
Meri Godi Tere Qabil Tu Nahi Hai Phir Bhi.

3
Hai Mere Dil Ki Sada Meri Sakina So Ja.
Chaar Barson Mein Na Zehmat Di Kabhi Tu Ne Mujhe.
Apni Khidmat Ka Mujhe Aaj Tu Moqa De De.
Hoga Ahsan Tera Meri Sakina So Ja.

4
Qaid Khane Mein Jo Aa Jaein Gay Tere Baba.
Khud Utha Doon Gi Tujhey Shana Hila Kar Tera.
Acha Kuch Dair Zara Meri Sakina So Ja.
Mujh Se Dekha Nahi Jata Yeh Tera Dard-e-Jigar.

5
Yeh Batatay Hoye Acha Tu Nahi Lagta Magar.
Ab Na Aaein Gay Chacha Meri Sakina So Ja.
Sabiron Ka Yeh Qareena Tu Nahi Hai Bibi.
Yeh Hai Pardais Madina Tu Nahi Hai Bibi.

6
Kar Ke Bas Shukar-e-Khuda Meri Sakina So Ja.
Oss Tarha Soyi Takallum Woh Yateema Bibi.
Maa Uthati Rahi Uthi Na Sakina Bibi.
Phir Na Bano Ne Kaha Meri Sakina So Ja.
