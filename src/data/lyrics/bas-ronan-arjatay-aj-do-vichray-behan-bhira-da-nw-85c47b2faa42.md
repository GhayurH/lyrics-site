Bas Ronan, Arjatay Aj Do Vichray Behan Bhira Da

Shala Veer Kisay Day Vichran Na

Jivain Vichraya Hai Sughara Da

Matam Ay Ki Khadi Jupni Nahi Ainj Wazi Ravani Rukhni Nahi

Kis Mu Naal Jannat Javarn Gaye

Jina Khoyaa Haq Sayeda Da

Do Teer Di Kar Manzoor Duah Mola Sadqa Matamdaran Da

Vekha Ujardya Dera Jakenay
