Chupke Chupke Raat Din Aanso Bahana Yaad Hai.
Hum Ko Ab Tak Aashqi Ka Wo Zamana Yaad Hai.

1
Ba Hazaraan Izterab-o-Sad Hazaraan Ishteyaq.
Tujh Se Wo Pehle Pehal Dil Ka Lagana Yaad Hai.
Baar Baar Uthna Ossi Janib Nigah-e-Shooq Ka.
Our Tera Garfe Se Wo Ankhein Larana Yaad Hai.

2
Tujh Se Kuch Milte Hi Wo Be Baak Ho Jana Mera.
Our Tera Danton Se Wo Ungli Dabana Yaad Hai.
Khainch Lena Woh Mera Parde Ka Kona Dafatan.
Our Dopate Se Tera Woh Munh Chupana Yaad Hai.

3
Jaan Kar Sota Tujhe Wo Qasd-e-Pabosi Mera.
Our Tera Tukhra Ke Sar, Wo Mera Muskurana Yaad Hai.
Tujh Ko Jab Tanha Kabhi Pana Tu Az Rah-e-Lehaz.
Haal-e-Dil Baton Hi Batoon Mein Jatana Yaad Hai.

4
Jab Siwa Mere TUmhara Koi Dewana Na Tha.
Sach Kaho Kuch Tum Ko Bahi Wo Karkhana Yaad Hai.
Ghair Ki Nazron Se Bach Kar Sab Ki Marzi Ke Khilaf.
Wo Tera Chori Chupe Ratoon Ko Aana Yaad Hai.

5
Aa Giya Gar Wasal Ki Shab Bhi Khain Zikr-e-Faraaq.
Wo Tera Ro Ro Ke Mujh Ko Bhi Rolana Yaad Hai.
Dopehar Ki Dhoop Mein Mere Bulane Ke Liye.
Wo Tera Kothe Pe Nange Paon Aana Yaad Hai.

6
Aaj Tak Nazroon Mein Hai Wo Sohbat-e-Raaz-o-Niyaz.
Apna Jana Yaad Hai, Tera Bulana Yaad Hai.
Methi Methi Chair Kar Batein Nirali Piyar Ki.
Zikr Dushman Ka Wo Baton Mein Urana Yaad Hai.

7
Dekhna Mujh Ko Jo Bargishta To So So Naaz Se.
Jab Mana Lena Tu Phir Khud Rooth Jana Yaad Hai.
Chori Chori Hum Se Tum Aa Kar Mile The Jiss Jagah.
Mudtein Guzrein, Par Aab Tak Wo Thikana Yaad Hai.

8
Shooq Mein Mehndi Ke Wo Be Dast-o-Paa Hona Tera.
Our Mera Wo Chairna, Gud Godana Yaad Hai.
Bawojood-e-Iday-e-Itqa Hasrat Mujhe.
Aaj Tak Ahad-e-Hawas Ka Wo Fasana Yaad Hai.
