Bandagi Ki Laaj Jo Rakh Le Mir Hasan Mir

1
Bandagi Ki Laaj Jo Rakh Le Woh Banda Our Hai.
Jo Tah-e-Khanjar Kiya Jaye Woh Sajdah Our Hai.
Khoon Ki Azmat Our Mathay Ka Paseena Our Hai.
Anbiya Se Kya Taqabul Mera Maula Our Hai.
Iss Liye Chun Kar Sitara Arsh Se Bhaija Gaya.
Kyunke Zohrah Ka Dar-e-Zahra Se Rishta Our Hai.

2
Garq Kar Ke Bidatton Ko Mere Aanso Har Baras.
Muftiyon Se Pochtay Hain Koi Fatwa Our Hai.
Shair Ne Yeh Alqama Ke Pehrey Daroon Se Kaha.
Iss Pe Qabza Ho Chuka Kya Koi Darya Our Hai.
Apni Palkein Bhi Bichao Farsh-e-Gham Ke Sath Sath.
Sab Ka Aana Our Hai Zahra Ka Aana Our Hai.

3
Alqama Abbas Ke Cholu Ka Andaza Na Kar.
Tujh Mein Pani Hi Nahi Cholu Tu Gehra Our Hai.
Iss Liye Aa Kar Woh Esa Ko Parhaein Gay Namaz.
Sheer-e-Mariyam Our Hai Our Sheer-e-Zahra Our Hai.
Shair Keh Detey Hain Hum Bhi Aye:BILAL-E-KAZMI:.
Madh-e-Maula Mein Magar Meesum Ka Lehja Our Hai.
