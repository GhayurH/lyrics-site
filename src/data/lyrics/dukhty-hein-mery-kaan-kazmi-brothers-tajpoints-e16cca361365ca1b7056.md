1
Dukhty Hein Mery Kaan Kazmi Brothers.
Sajjad Zindaan Ke Andheron Se Mujh Ko Tu Bacha Le.
Bhai Mujhe Aagosh-e-Imamat Mein Chupa Le.
Dukhtay Hain Mere Kan Sula Do Mujhe Bhaiyya Sula Do Mujhe Bhaiyya.
Tum Maa Ki Tarha Lori Suna Do Mujhe Bhaiyya.
Lagta Hai Andherey Se Dar Teri Sakina Ko.
Tum Sar Mere Baba Ka Dila Do Mujhe Bhaiyya.
Dekha Hai Inn Aankhon Ne Baba Ka Gala Kattay.
Bholon Gi Bhala Kaisay Bata Do Mujhe Bhaiyya.
Main Saat Muharram Se Pyasi Hoon Mere Bhaiyya.
Do Bond Ho Gar Pani Pila Do Mujhe Bhaiyya.
Bhaiyya Mere Kano Se Ab Tak Hai Lahu Jari.
Tum Aa Ke Zarra Marham Laga Do Mujhe Bhaiyya.
Karta Hai Sitam Zalim Soney Bhi Nahi Deta.
Aagosh-e-Imamat Mein Chupa Do Mujhe Bhaiyya.
Sajjad Se Sakina Ro Ke Yeh Fazal Boli.
Chehra Mere Asghar Ka Dikha Do Mujhe Bhaiyya.
