1
Sabeel e Imam Hussain Mir Hasan Mir
Nehrein Rawaan Hain Faiz-e-Shah e Mashreqain Ki.
Pyaso Piyo Sabeel Hai Nazar-e-Hussain Ki.
Nam-e-Maula Pe Hain Pani Jo Pilane Walay.
Haan Yehi Loag Hain Firdos Mein Jane Walay.
Sirf Shabeer Ka Dar Hai Jo Yeh Phailaye Hain Hath.
Warna Yeh Loag Kahan Mang Ke Khaney Walay.
Teri Rahoon Mein Bichaein Gay Farishtay Aankhein.
Farsh Mazloom Ki Majlis Ka Bichanay Walay.
Farwa Deti Hain Duaein Sada Aabad Rahein.
Ro Ke Mehndi Mere Qasim Ki Uthane Walay.
Hath Utha Kar Tujhey Detey Hain Dua Hur e Jari.
Paon Shabeer Ke Zair Ke Dabane Walay.
Do Katay Hath Tujhey Girney Nahi Dein Gay Kabhi.
Chotay Hazrat Ka Alam Ghar Pe Lagane Walay.
Hai Yaqeen Khuld Mein Woh Jaein Gay Sab Se Pehle.
Rasiyaan Thamay Jaloson Ko Chalaney Walay.
Ik Guzarish Hai Keh Abbas Keh Lashay Pe Na Ja.
Chadar-e-Zahra Ko Naizay Pe Uthane Walay.
Dar-e-Zindan Pe Bethi Hai Sakina Utha Ja.
Shah Zadi Tere Baba Nahi Aane Walay.
Shah Kehte They Keh Jao Ali Akbar Jao.
Hum Nahi Lasha-e-Abbas Se Jane Walay.
Aakhri Waqt Bhi Aayi Nahi Saye Mein Rubab.
Aisay Dekhey Na Kahin Wadah Nibhanay Walay.
Banney Kab Detey They Zindan Se Bahir Turbat.
Neel Chehray Pe Sakina Ke Bananey Walay.
Kya Ijazat Hai Takallum Bhi Tere Sath Chaley.
Qabar-e-Zahra Pe Diya Ja Ke Jalanay Walay.
