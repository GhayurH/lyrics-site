1
Bibi Zainab Ki Guftar Azadar e Batool.
Bibi Zainab Ki Jo Guftar Nazar Aati Hai.
Har Yazeedi Ke Liye Mar Nazar Aati Hai.
Tere Khutbon Ki Balagat Pe Karein Ghoor Tu Phir.
Jurat-e-Haider-e-Karrar Nazar Aati Hai.
Iss Liye Shoq Se Parhta Hoon Main Khutbat Tere.
Tu Ulattay Hoye Darbar Nazar Aati Hai.
Aasmano Ko Zameeno Pe Gira Sakti Hai.
Yeh Bazahir Jo Giraftar Nazar Aati Hai.
Hashar Se Pehle Abhi Hashar Dikha De Sab Ka.
Shamiyon Jo Tumhein La Char Nazar Aati Hai.
Woh Amarat Jo Banati Rahi Sufyan Ki Aal.
Tere Qadmo Mein Woh Mismar Nazar Aati Hai.
Sar Bhi Urta Howa Baiyat Ka Nazar Aata Hai.
Jab Tere Khutbay Ki Talwar Nazar Aati Hai.
Khon Se Tere Tarotaza Hai Ab Dein Ka Shajar.
Our Har Shakh Samar Bar Nazar Aati Hai.
Meri Naslon Pe Yeh Ahsan Hai Tera Bibi.
Jo Har Ik Pusht Azadar Nazar Aati Hai.
Shan Woh Khak-e-Dar-e-Bint-e-Ali Ki Hai Asad.
Sar Pe Rakh Loon Tu Yeh Dastar Nazar Aati Hai.
