maula hussain ibne ali

rasme wafa tujhse chali

apni khabar bhi na rahi

Ay mehware emaan e man

bismillahi noor e…

bismillahi noor e…

dono se hai roohe baqa

wo sajdagahe khudsiyan

ye karbala khaake shifa

wo ibteda ye inteha

dono shahadat ka sila

tay silsila tay silsila
