Haye Tha Chehra Zakhmi Shikasta Pehlu Ajab Qayamat Ki Woh Ghari Thi
Nabi Ki Beti Dam-e-Wasiyat Ali Se Jab Ro Ke Keh Rahi Thi
Aye Mere Maula Chor Ke Tanha Jati Hai Ab Dukhtar-e-Nabi
Gham Tumhara Syeda Ke Sath Jaye Ga

Haye Ali Bekasi Teri
