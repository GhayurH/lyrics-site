1
Khandaan e Risaalat Ka Noor E Nazar Hai Imam e Hassan Ali Raza Jafri.
Rehmat e Do Jahan Ka Lakht-e-Jigar, Hai Imam-e-Hasan.
Khandan-e-Risalat Ka Noor-e-Nazar, Hai Imam-e-Hasan.
Le Ke Paigham Mera Tu Jibreel Ja.
De Keh Dastak Wahan Zahra Ko Bata.
Tere Baba Sa Hai Tera Pehla Pisar, Hai Imam-e-Hasan.
Le Ke Aagosh Mein Kehte They Yeh Nabi.
Tu Hai Beta Mera Tu Hai Jan-e-Nabi.
Jis Se Aabad Hai Mere Dil Ka Nagar, Hai Imam-e-Hasan.
Teri Madar Ko Tujh Se Mili Woh Khushi.
Sajda e Shukar Se Sar Uthati Nahi.
Aisa Tohfa Khuda Tu Ne Bhaija Hai Ghar, Hai Imam-e-Hasan.
Jis Ko Taleem De Khud Rasool-e-Khuda.
Tarbiyaat Khud Karein Jis Ki Shair-e-Khuda.
Aisa Rutba Hai Paya Kissi Ne Agar, Hai Imam-e-Hasan.
Unn Ki Shahi Hai Kitni Wasee-o-Areez.
Jin Ki Taqleed Mein Ho Maula Hussain.
Jis Ke Mahkoom Hain Sare Jin-o-Bashar, Hai Imam-e-Hasan.
Chor Kar Takht Ko Yeh Jataya Unhein.
Hoon Ameer-e-Arab Yeh Bataya Unhein.
Jin Ki Tazeem Dil Se Karey Har Bashar, Hai Imam-e-Hasan.
Jo Sajata Hai Dastar Imam-e-Hasan.
Ghar Mein Aate Hain Unn Ke Imam-e-Hasan.
Jis Ke Sadqay Mein Milta Hai Rizq-o-Ajar, Hai Imam-e-Hasan.
Tere Dar Pe Ho Aana Naseeb Bar Bar.
Faiz Milta Rahe Bas Humein Bar Bar.
Kar Dein Mumtaz Jis Ki Faqat Ik Nazar, Hai Imam-e-Hasan.
