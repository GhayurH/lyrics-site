1
Mola Bolaiyga Mera Mola Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.
Maula Bulaye Ga Mera Maula Bulaye Ga.
Mujh Ko Diyar-e-Ishq Ka Zair Banaye Ga.
Barhta Rahon Ga Kehta Howa Main Ali Ali.
Reh Khulti Jaye Gi Jo Qadam Barhta Jaye Ga.
Tasbeeh-e-Ashk Hogi Azaan Suntay Hi Shorow.
Akbar Ka Naam Sun Ke Ajab Josh Aaye Ga.
Aaya Jo Kham Kamar Mein Thakan Se Kahin Marey.
Nam-e-Habib Loon Ga Tu Kham Munh Ki Khaye Ga.
Zikar-e-Charag Khaima-e-Sarwar Karon Ga Main.
Aa Kar Falak Se Chand Bhi Rasta Dikhaye Ga.
Noha Karein Gay Arz-o-Sama Mere Sath Sath.
Dil Dasht-e-Jaan Mein Farsh-e-Azza Youn Bichaye Ga.
Nikley Gi Phir Kaleem Ziyarat Ki Bhi Sabeel.
Nam-e-Hussain Le Ke Jo Aanso Bahaye Ga.
