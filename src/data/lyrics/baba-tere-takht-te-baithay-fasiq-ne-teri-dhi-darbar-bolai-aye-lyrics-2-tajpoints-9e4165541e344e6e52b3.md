1
Baba Tere Takht Te Baithay Fasiq Ne Teri Dhi Darbar Bolai Aye.
Tere Baad Quran De Qariyaan Ne Konain Di Waris Haye Baba Darbar De Wich Jhutlai Aye.
Khamosh Kara Ke Haye Mainu Tohmat Hazyan Di Laonday Ray.
Nayi Haq Mangdi Ilzam Na La Kurlandi Rayi Darbar De Wich Haye Pak Rasool Di Jayi Aye.

2
Main Khuld De Waris Le Aayi Haye Deen Gawahiyaan Fasiq Nu.
Tehreer Teri De Kar Tukray Teri Zahra Aynj Darbar Wichon Haye Khali Wekh Walayi Aye.
Tazeem Jehray Dar Di Baba Tere Naal Farishtay Karday Ray.
Ag La Ke Mere Bohey Te Jibreel Jithay Sajday Karda Aina Oh Dehleez Jalayi Aye.

3
Zainab Ro Pochiya Fizza Taun Mainu Amari Nazar Nayi Aaondi Aye.
Pehchan Zara Zainab Bichari Teri Maa Nanay Di Ummat Ne Aa Wekh Zaeef Banai Aye.
 Tosif Hashar Taein Matami Nu Aye Sadma Khon Rawaway Ga.
Sun Barhwan Lal Wilayat Da Tainu Kar Ke Yaad Teri Amari Wich Baqiye De Kurlayi Aye.
