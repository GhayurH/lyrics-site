Bhari jawani main Akbar ko mout ayi hay
Teri dhuaee khudaya teri dhuayi hay

1
1) Yeh baat toot kay barchee neh khud batayi hai
Yeh tujh ko dekh kay barchee bhi ladkhadayi hay

2
2) Tarap rahee hai jo Akbar ki laash maqtal main
Zaroor Sughra ko Akbar ki yaad ayi hay

3
3) Salaam us pay keh jis baap neh zaeefi main
Jawaan shear say betay ki laash uthayi hay

4
4) Na sir peh sehra hai koyi na haath main kangna
Yeh kaisi mehndi hai jo haaton pay lagyi hay

5
5) Gareeb maa ko bhala kaisay chain ajaye
Jawaan betay ki maiyat abhi to ayi hay

6
6) Teh bain lasha-e-Akbar pay yeh Sakina kay
Utho utho mere bhaiya Sakina ayi hay

7
7) Zameen pay behti howi kaar rahi hai aah-o-buka
Ajeeb karb kay alam main Zahra jayi hay

8
8) Pata hai kaun ta Akbar namaazion tum ko
Yehi woh shear hai jis neh azaan bachayi hay

9
9) Tarap kay khama-o-qirtos roye hai Gohar
Zuban pay jab kabhie Akbar ki baat ayi hay
