1
Asghar Tera Tabassum Woh Kam Kar Gaya Hai Hasan Sadiq.
Batil Ko Ta Qayamat Na Kaam Kar Gaya Hai.
Hal Min Ka Istegasa Dena Hussain Ka.
Zainab Ke Dil Mein Paida Kuhram Kar Gaya Hai.

2
Goya Woh So Gaya Hai Karbal Ki Khak Mein.
Maa Ko Woh Mubtalaye Aalaam Kar Gaya Hai.
Goya Keh Ab Zamane Mein Ghazi Nahi Mojood.
Lekin Wafa Ka Dunya Mein Woh Naam Kar Gaya Hai.

3
Abid Lahu Ke Aanso Roya Hai Go Hussain.
Dunya Mein Aam Tera Paigham Kar Gaya Hai.
Be Sheer Ki Tadfeen Se Lagta Hai Youn Ameer.
Jaisay Woh Maa Ki Gaud Mein Aaram Kar Gaya Hai.
