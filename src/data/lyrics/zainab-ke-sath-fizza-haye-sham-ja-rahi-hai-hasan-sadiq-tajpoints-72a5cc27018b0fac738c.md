Zainab Ke Sath Fizza Haye Sham Ja Rahi Hai Hasan Sadiq.
Har Gaam Par Zaeefa Sadmay Utha Rahi Hai.

1
Ho Sham Ho Ya Kofa Ghazi Ke Baad Fizza.
Zahra Ki Betiyon Ka Pardah Bana Rahi Hai.
Iss Din Ke Wastay Hi Mangi Thi Zindagani.
Youn Keh Ke Taziyane Mazloma Kha Rahi Hai.

2
Chora Na Zulm Seh Kar Zainab Ka Sath Iss Ne.
Zahra Se Jo Kiya Tha Wadah Nibha Rahi Hai.
Bazar Sham Ka Hai Zainab Ka Sar Khula Hai.
Sar Ko Jhukaye Fizza Aanso Baha Rahi Hai.

3
Kandhey Pe Kal Issi Ke Zahra Ka Tha Janaza.
Sang Aaj Qaid Ho Ke Zainab Ke Ja Rahi Hai.
 Jawad Ashk Khoon Ke Sajjad Kyun Na Roye.
Mulk-e-Wafa Ki Malka Dunya Se Ja Rahi Hai.
