1
Har Azadar Ko Zawar Banaya Jaye Master Ali Zamin.
Karbala Mein Mujhe Har Bar Bulaya Jaye.
Woh Jo Kehta Hai Ali Aane Se Batil Hai Namaz.
Oss Ka Shajrah Bhi Zamane Ko Dikhaya Jaye.
Mujh Ko Adat Hai Jaloson Mein Alam Chonay Ki.
Meri Turbat Pe Alam Pak Lagaya Jaye.
Chor Ke Kaisay Main Matam Ko Musallay Pe Jhukon.
Mujh Ko Mulla Ke Na Fatwon Se Draya Jaye.
Baab-e-Hikmat Se Khird Pa Ke Bhi Hasrat Hai Yehi.
Mujh Ko Behlool Sa Dewana Banaya Jaye.
Jis Ko Dunya Ke Tabeebon Se Nahi Milti Shifa.
Ja Ke Fitrus Ke Maseeha Ko Dikhaya Jaye.
Hashar Mein Hath Mein Ghazi Ka Alam Le Ke Chalon.
Mujh Ko Bas Matami Halqay Se Uthaya Jaye.
Meri Rag Rag Hai Ghadeeri Mujhe Fatway Na Suna.
Bas Wilayat Ka Sabaq Mujh Ko Sunaya Jaye.
Aik Hasrat Hai Mere Hathoon Se Mehshar Mein Batool.
Mujrim e Fatima Zahra Ko Jalaya Jaye.
Poetess::Fakhira Batool Naqvi:
