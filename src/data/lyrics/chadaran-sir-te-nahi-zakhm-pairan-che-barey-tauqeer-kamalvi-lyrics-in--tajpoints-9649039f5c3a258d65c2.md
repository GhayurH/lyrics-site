1
Chadaran Sir Te Nahi Zakhm Pairan Che Barey Tauqeer Kamalvi.
Chadraan Sir Te Nayi Zakhm Pairaan Che Barey Pathraan Kharaan De.
Kiwein Turyaan Hosan Bhainri Mazloom Diyaan Wich Bazaraan De.
Raat Nu Jinna Di Amari Da Janaza Uthya.

2
Aa Ke Jibreel Sada Jinna De Dar Te Rukya.
Ona Mastoraan Nu Painday Karne Paye Gaye Sang Ashraran De.
Kool Sajjad De Aa Wainr Aye Paway Zainab.
Baaj Chadar De Kiwein Sham Nu Jaway Zainab.

3
Veer Ghazi V Nayi Lagay Majmay Bachra Bad Kirdaraan De.
Naal Babay De Jehri Sainr Na Bolay Loko.
Baad Zahra De Rayi Fizza De Ohlay Loko.
ALLAH Janrey Kiwein Boldi Rayi Ho Si Naal May Kharaan De.

4
Ro Sakina Ne Kehya Loko Na Rat Ron Diyo.
Mere Sajjad Nu Do Ghariyaan Zara Soon Diyo.
Koi Jag Dunya Te Paish Aynj Nayi Aounda Naal Bemaran De.
Tang Bazar Andar Ro Paiyaan Phophiyaan Mawaan.

5
Sir Te Abid De Jadon Kitiyaan Pathraan Chawaan.
Rang Tabdeel Hoye Wekhiya Dunya Ne Nezay Aswaran De.
Hoya Ayelan Yateema Nu Sataya Jaway.
Mar Ke Koray Muhari Nu Turaya Jaway.

6
Wain Gonjan Loko Fatima Jaiyaan De Wich Bazaraan De.
Ayho Tauqeer Sada Ro Ke Duawaan Karda.
Mawaan Bhainra Da Raway Shala Salamat Parda.
Kaho Aameen Tusi Rehnr Aabad Sada Ghar Azadaran De.
