Khanjaar dhi dhaar thalay maaray ambhiyaan vaanga

Gussal dah kauf nahin  fikaar hain riddah vaanga

Khuday deena chenah, laal enjh ko ha ay hunr

Sabar Hussain dah ay  honsla imaam vanga

Sataar kadaam tay nahin, vendhi kusdah veeran ku

Nah bhain bahir avay  faislah bhira vanga

Sakinah vekh dee raee, raha chacha Ghazi dah

Au zeen choor gaya  ShenShah waafah vanga

Pathar hataan dhi kaday, labdhi Saynr veeran nu

Sajaad takh dah raya  majmah bayhaya vanga

Ay zikhaar majlis-e- Shabbir, tai zanjeer zaani

Ay pursa ajaar meri  Saynr dhi dua vanga
