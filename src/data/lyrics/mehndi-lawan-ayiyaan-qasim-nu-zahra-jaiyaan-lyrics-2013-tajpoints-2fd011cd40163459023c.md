1
Mehndi Lawan Ayiyaan Qasim Nu Zahra Jaiyaan Asghar Khan
Aj Phophiyaan Dard Sataiyaan Haye Sir Wich Mitiyaan Paiyaan Banra Parnawan Aayiyaan.
Ik Ik Tukray Nu Chum Chum Ke Aye Farwa Aakhay.
Uth Qasim Meriyaan Saina Masmoom Hasan Diyaan Bhaina Tere Shagan Manawan Aayiyaan.

2
Naal Chawaan De Bana Ke Joray Shagna Walay.
Qasim Diyaan Chachay Jaiyaan Ro Mangdiyan Laag Wadhayiyaan Sehrey Nu Sajawan Aayiyaan.
Mitiyaan Choon Naal Mazloom De Aj Ro Ro Ke.
Karbal Da Ujriya Lara Farwa Da Raaj Dulara Horaan Ne Chawan Aayiyaan.

3
Gal La Ke Sain Kubra Nu Wich Karbal De.
Raj Raj Ke Phophiyaan Roiyaan Veraan Te Putraan Roiyaan Jadon Rasiyaan Pawan Aayiyaan.
Aynj Di Shadi Samar Hoi Aye Shah Zaday Di.
Aidhey Janji Mar Gaye Sare Phophiyaan Ne Burqay Warey Aye Deen Bachawan Aayiyaan.
