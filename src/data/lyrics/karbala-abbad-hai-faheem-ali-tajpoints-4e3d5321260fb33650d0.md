1
Kaun Kehta Hai Ke Bas Yeh Ik Jagah Aabad Hai, Hai Jahan Tak Kibriya Karbala Aabad Hai.
Dekh Tu Ashor Ko Yeh Kis Tarha Aabad Hai, Hai Jahan Tak Kibriya Karbala Aabad Hai.
Kis Ki Yeh Taseer Hai Jo Matam-e-Zanjeer Hai Munh Chupata Phir Raha Har Dushman-e-Shabeer Hai.
Mojza Hai Karbala Yeh Mojza Aabad Hai, Hai Jahan Tak Kibriya Karbala Aabad Hai.
Yeh Azza Khane Hamarey Khaima-e-Shabeer Hain Aaj Bhi Mojood Inn Mein Malik-e-Taqdeer Hain.
Ko Ba Ko Sharaf-e-Hidayat Ba Khuda Aabad Hai, Hai Jahan Tak Kibriya Karbala Aabad Hai.
Yeh Sabeelein Yeh Niyazein Yeh Wasee Tar Ahtamam Nazar-e-ALLAH Our Yeh Hai Sath Mein Niyaz-e-Imam.
Ik Attaye Karbala Ka Silsila Aabad Hai, Hai Jahan Tak Kibriya Karbala Aabad Hai.
Rubai: Shaukat Raza Shaukat.
Jis Ne Bhi Jan-o-Dil Se Mawaddat Qabool Ki.
Oss Ne Bas Aik Ashk Mein Janat Wasool Ki.
Dedar Jis Ne Kar Liya Qabar-e-Hussain Ka.
Samjho Ke Oss Ne Ki Hai Ziyarat Rasool Ki.
Shehanshahoon Ke Shehanshah Hain Mere Maula Hussain Rahat-e-Qalb-e-Muhammad Syeda Ke Dil Ka Chain.
Jis Ke Dil Mein Hain Muhammad Yeh Sada Aabad Hai, Hai Jahan Tak Kibriya Karbala Aabad Hai.
Karbala Insaniyat Ki Ik Mukamal Dars Ga Hai Baqa Insaniyat Ki Ba Tufail-e-Karbala.
Har Jagah Insaniyat Ka Aasra Aabad Hai, Hai Jahan Tak Kibriya Karbala Aabad Hai.
Yeh Tu Aisa Zakhm-e-Dil Hai Jo Kabhi Bharta Nahi Woh Munafiq Hai Jo Maula Gham Tera Karta Nahi.
Farsh Se Arsh-e-Olla Tak Gham Tera Aabad Hai, Hai Jahan Tak Kibriya Karbala Aabad Hai.
Aaj Gar Koi Jahan Mein Hai:FAHEEM-E-Karbala Yeh Tera Lutf-o-Karam Hai Aye Kareem-e-Karbala.
Qalb Mein Qurban Ke Bhi Nainawa Aabad Hai, Hai Jahan Tak Kibriya Karbala Aabad Hai.
