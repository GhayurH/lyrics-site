Zain Ul Abideen Tera Bara Okha Painda Aye.
Chehra Wekh Ke Zainab Da Aye Wain Ali Da Aye.

1
Meri Maiyyat Cha Ke Takda Jawein Galiyaan Nu Moraan Nu.
Tere Sabar Di Pehli Hi Manzil Aye Kofa Aye.
Aisay Ghar De Samne Peshi Honi Kal Teri Phophiyaan Di.
Samne Aisay Bohey De Darbar Da Boha Aye.

2
Aj Iss Shehar Tu Daday Layi Toon Ronda Yasrab Nu Chaliyaan Ayein.
Kal Iss Shehar Ich Chadar Layi Rat Rondiyaan Aouna Aye.
Bait Al-Huzan Banaya Si Main Teri Dadi Layi Yasrab Wich.
Tere Rowan Layi Bachra Zindan Da Wehra Aye.

3
Tere Samne Teriyaan Phophiyaan Aayataan V Holi Parhdiyaan Ne.
Kal Te Sham Di Rahwaan Wich Har Shehar Che Khutba Aye.
 Akbar Pak Wasiyat Sun Ke Abid Daday Nu Ro Aakhay.
Lagda Aye Meri Akhiyaan Layi Hun Sirf Hanera Aye
