1
Abbas, Abbas, Abbas, Abbas.
Jo Bali Sakina Ka Hai Chacha Oss Ka Hai Pharera Sar Pe Khula.
Jo Baab-e-Nijat Kholta Hai Dil Mushkil Mein Yeh Bolta Hai.
Waris Hai Mera Ibn-e-Haider Rehmat Ban Ke Mere Sar Par.
Alam Ka Saya Hai, Alam Ka Saya Hai.
Maqbool Meri Karta Hai Dua Saqa-e-Haram Woh Rab-e-Wafa.
Jab Koi Mushkil Aati Hai Dil Mera Jab Ghabrata Hai.
Tab Mera Yaqeen Mujh Ko Le Kar Ghazi Ke Alam Par Aata Hai.
Mere Dil Se Yeh Aati Hai Sada Tal Jaye Gi Mere Sar Se Bala.
Tanhai Mein Dil Yeh Kehta Hai Munh Phair Le Chahe Sab Dunya.
Chahe Na Ho Mere Pas Koi Par Sath Tu Hai Maula Mera.
Dil Mera Toor Nahi Sakta Woh Mujh Ko Chor Nahi Sakta.
Jab Unn Ki Atta Hogi Mujh Par Jab Unn Ka Sadqa Paon Ga.
Main Apne Ghar Ki Chat Par Ik Ghazi Ka Alam Lagwaon Ga.
Har Momin Ke Dil Ki Hai Dua Har Matami Yeh Deta Hai Sada.
Jab Chehlum Karne Maula Ka Main Izn-e-Ziyarat Paon Ga.
Haider Ke Dar Se Karb-o-Bala Main Paidal Chal Ke Jaon Ga.
Saqa-e-Sakina Ka Hai Karam Yeh Soch Ke Hi Barhta Hai Qadam.
Abbas Ka Badlah Lainay Ko Haider Ke Pisar Ko Aana Hai.
ALLAH Ke Ghar Ke Bhi Opar Ghazi Ke Alam Ko Lagna Hai.
Kabay Pe Jo Parcham Dekhein Gay Khud Mehdi-e-Doraan Bolein Gay.
Ab Chahe Jan Ka Khatrah Ho Maula Ke Jaloos Mein Jana Hai.
Jhukney Nahi Paye Ahl-e-Azza Iss Alam Ko Oncha Rakhna Hai.
Haan Meri Hifazat Karta Hai Har Shia Ali Ka Kehta Hai.
Hum Hami Hain Mazlomo Ke Ab Samne Jo Bhi Aa Jaye.
Yeh Keh Ke Sare Hussaini Jawaan Iss Door Ke Zulm Se Takraye.
Kuch Jan Ki Ab Parwah Nahi Iss Baat Ka Hai Mere Dil Ko Yaqeen.
Woh Qafila Shah-e-Wala Ka Jab Shehar-e-Nabi Se Nikla Tha.
Sadaat Ke Sar Pe Jalwa Figan Saqa-e-Haram Ka Panja Tha.
Kehti Thi Yeh Zainab Bint-e-Ali Mere Dil Mein Nahi Hai Khof Koi.
Yeh Shayari Yeh Noha Khuwani Yeh Hum Pe Attaye Ghazi Hai.
Emaan Hai Nabeel Our Asghar Ka Yeh Sheh Ki Karam Nawazi Hai.
Iss Safar Mein Aagay Barhtey Rahein Hum Nohay Younhi Parhtay Rahein.
