Veeran Gharon Mein Qaid Sy Youn Wapsi Hui Kazmi Brothers Write Up
Sham Se Laut Ke Aaye Hain Madine Walay.

1
Veraan Gharon Mein Qaid Se Youn Wapisi Hoi.
Koi Diya Jala Na Kahin Roshni Hoi.
Yeh Haal Hai Keh Betiyaan Pehchanti Nahi.
Zainab Khud Apne Ghar Mein Bhi Ab Ajnabi Hoi.

2
Zainab Ke Bazuon Pe Youn Neelay Nishan Hain.
Ya Phir Rasan Hai Hathoon Mein Ab Tak Basi Hoi.
Akbar Shaheed Ho Gaye Lekin Woh Intezar.
Sughra Hai Aik Waday Se Ab Tak Bandhi Hoi.

3
Mehmal Tu Ja Ke Laut Bhi Aaye Hain Sham Se.
Ik Maa Ki Zindagi Hai Wahin Par Ruki Hoi.
Nanhi Si Baliyon Pe Lahu Se Likhi Hai Woh.
Jo Baat Thi Sakina Ke Rukh Par Likhi Hoi.

4
Naizay Se Jo Utari Thi Shimar-e-Laeen Ne.
Zainab Ke Khon Se Hai Woh Chadar Bhari Hoi.
 Akbar Saron Pe Karb-o-Bala Ki Jo Khak Thi.
Oss Par Hai Qaid Khane Ki Mati Jami Hoi.
