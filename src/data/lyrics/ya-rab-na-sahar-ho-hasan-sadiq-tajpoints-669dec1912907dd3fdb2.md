Ya Rab Na Sahar Ho Hasan Sadiq Lyrics
Pyason Ke Liye Maut Ka Paigham Sahar Hai.
Chal Jaye Ga Shabeer Ki Gardan Pe Bhi Khanjar.
Iss Raat Chamaktay Hain Falak Par Jo Sitarey.
Lut Jaye Ga Ik Roz Mein Zahra Ka Bhara Ghar.
Abbas Ke Shaney Bhi Qalam Hoon Gay Lab-e-Nehar.
Chin Jaye Gi Kal Zainab-o-Kulsoom Ki Chadar.
Jo Seene Pe Shabeer Ke Soti Hai Sakina.
Dhondney Gi Na Mil Jaye Gi Lut Jaein Gay Gohar.
Beemar Ka Bistar Bhi Addo Khainchein Gay Aa Kar.
Pehnaein Gay Beemar Ko Woh Aahni Langar.
Yeh Raat Agar Dhal Gayi Soraj Nikal Aaya.
Murjhaye Ga Pardais Mein Zahra Ka Gul-e-Tar.
 Rehan Dua Zainab-e-Muztar Ki Yehi Thi.
Aye Palney Walay Mere Kunbay Pe Karam Kar.
