1
Akhiyaan Fazal Diyaan Takdiyaan Reh Gayiyaan.
Ghazi Diyaan Bhaina Diyaan Chadraan Jadoon Sir Ton Lay Gayiyaan.
Lutday Ray Musalmaan Phophiyaan Day Goshwaray.
Ronda Riya Aye Tak Ke Jadoon Lokaan Khaimay Saray.

2
Kiwein Baldiyaan Banwaan Wichon Phophiyaan Abid Bacha Gayiyaan.
Karbal Che Vari Lokaan Lut Laiyaan Jad Ridawaan.
Chaliya Na Koi Charah Riya Bharda Hokay Hawaan.
Phophiyaan Diyaan Banwaan Wich Loko Jadoon Rasiyaan Pay Gayiyaan.

3
Kainda Riya Aye Maa Nu Ik War Day Ijazat.
Dunya Nu Aj Wakhwaan Ainj Piyon Di Main Shujat.
Zahra Diyaan Jaiyaan Aye Na Samjhan Assi Kaliyaan Reh Gayiyaan.
Babay Di Shakal Wala Dunya Toon Tur Gaya Aye.

4
Khali Alam Bhira Da Jadoon Khaimay Aa Gaya Aye.
Oss Waqt Yateemi Diyaan Ghariyaan Bhaina Te Cha Gayiyaan.
Sham-e-Ghareeban Guzari Lashay Kitay Pamal Aye.
Sir Naiziyaan Te Cha Ke Jiwein Shami Tur Pay Naal Aye.

5
Jurtaan Fazal Diyaan ALLAH Janre Kiwein Zulm Seh Gayiyaan.
Iss War V Qalam Ne Tafseer Kul Sunai.
Iss Gal Di Dendi Payi Aye Tareekh V Gawahi.
Syedaniyaan De Gham Diyaan Galaan Kuj Baqi Reh Gayiyaan.
