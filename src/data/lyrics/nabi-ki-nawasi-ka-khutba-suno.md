Nabi Ki Nawasi Ka Khutba Suno

1
Jurat Mere Dadhiyaal Ke Aangan Se Chali Hai
Ismat Mere Nanhiyaa Ke Gulshan Mein Pali Hai
Wallah Mere Ghar Ka Har Ik Fard Wali Hai
Jiss Baap Ki Zeenat Hoon Woh Rutbe Mein Ali Hai
Main Haider-e-Karrar Ki Akas Lagoon Gi
Bolon Gi Tu Ghazi Ko Bhi Abbas Lagoon Gi

2
Har Khuwab Ko Sharminda-e-Tabeer Karon Gi
Baba Hi Ke Andaz Mein Taqreer Karon Gi
Darbar Mein Bas Midhat-e-Shabir Karon Gi
Tareekh Ke Seene Pe Yeh Tehreer Karon Gi
Maidan Mein Dar Jati Hai Farrar Ke Beti
Darbar Mein Dat Jati Hai Karrar Ki Beti

3
Aaein Gaye Nazar Haider-e-Karrar Ke Taiwar
Lehjay Se Dikha Doon Gi Main Talwar Ke Taiwar
Yaad Aaein Gaye Aada Ko Alamdar Ke Taiwar
Dekhe Ga Khuda Khud Meri Guftar Ke Taiwar
Tabreeq Mile Mujh Ko Muhammad Ki Taraf Se
Khutba Jo Parhoon Sham Mein Daad Aye Najaf Se

4
Soyi Hoi Taqdeer Jaga Sakti Hoon Main Bhi
Zehno Ke Khialaat Bata Sakti Hoon Main Bhi
Har Rah Ki Dewar Gira Sakti Hoon Main Bhi
Phir Dar Pe Sitaron Ko Bula Sakti Hoon Main Bhi
Youn Dast-e-Yadullah Ka Ejaz Dikha Doon
Chahoon Tu Abhi Sham Ko Dunya Se Mita Doon

5
Haq Se Bhi Giya Haq Ko Chupane Ke Liye Tu
Karta Hai Jatan Takht Bachane Ke Liye Tu
Aamadah Ho Deen Ko Mitane Ke Liye Tu
Dar Dar Pe Jhuka Jis Ko Jhukane Ke Liye Tu
Leta Hai Khiraj Oss Ka Hunar Aaj Bhi Tujh Se
Khud Dekh Le Oncha Hai Woh Sar Aaj Bhi Tujh Se

6
Lamhaat Mein Yeh Moajza Kar Jata Hai Shabir
Marta Nahi Zehno Mein Utar Jata Hai Shabir
Simte Tu Ikai Mein Nikhar Jata Hai Shabir
Phailay Tu Bahatar Mein Bikhar Jata Hai Shabir
Toheed Ki Izat Ko Bachaya Hai Issi Ne
Islam Ko Islam Banaya Hai Issi Ne

7
Tareekh Malay Gi Tere Chehre Pe Siyahi
Thokein Gaye Teri Qabar Pe Ghairat Ke Sipahi
Dein Gi Teri Zilat Ki Sab Aqwam Gawahi
Ab Aa Hi Gayi Hoon Tu Karon Gi Yahan Shahi
Kar Doon Gi Qalam Mufti-e-Kazib Ke Qalam Ko
Ghar Ghar Pe Laga Doon Gi Main Ghazi Ke Alam Ko
