Zahra da paak baba, surn dhi diyan duhaiyan
Rovarn tai faasiqaan nai, pabandiyan nai laiyaan

1
1) Eh log tera pursa mainu nayee dairn aayai
Baithai rahay saqeefai, mimber tai dee dilayai
Tin din dai baad aakay, ehna phuriyan bichaaiyaan

2
2) Mainu zalamaan nai baba darbaar vich bulaya
Tukray sanad dai ker kai, eh faisala surnaaya
Saanu qabool nayee aai, Zahra diyan gavahiyaan

3
3) Ik vaar uth kai vaikho, ki Fatima da haal aai
Teri ladali dai ho gayai, yak dam safaid baal aai
Haq lairn layee gayeen saan, ho kay zaheef aaiyaan

4
4) Ag laa kai merai ghar nu ehna nai khaaraiya eh
Gaudi merich mera Mohsin vi maaraiya eh
Gardan ch Murtaza dai rassiyan vi ehna paiyaan

5
5) Main jaarn gayeen aan baba, rul jaarni teri aal aai
Vich Kerbala dai ho si mera puttar pamaal eh
Rassiyan hathaan ch paa kai tursan batool jaiyaan

6
6) Tauqeer Fatima da jo gham mana gaiya eh
Damaan-e-Mustafa dai saayaich aagaiya eh
Khaliq nai naimaitaan vi ohday layi banaaiyaan
