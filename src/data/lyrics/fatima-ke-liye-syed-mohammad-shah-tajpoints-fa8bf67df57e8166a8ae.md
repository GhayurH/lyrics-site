1
Fatima Ke Liye Syed Mohammad Shah.
Har Fazeelat Bani Fatima Ke Liye.
Keh Bana Hai Ali, Fatima Ke Liye.
Aayatein Jab Bhi Aayi Tu Baithay Rahe.
Uth Rahe Hain Nabi Fatima Ke Liye.
Darzi Ban Kar Malak Arsh Se Aa Gaya.
Zaat Badli Gayi Fatima Ke Liye.
Do Musafir Hoye Ik Nabi Ik Ali.
Jin Ki Mairaj Thi Fatima Ke Liye.
Naam Zainab Rakha Rab-e-Akbar Ne Our.
Fatima Bhaij Di Fatima Ke Liye.
Aik Fizza Hai Jo Bool Sakti Hai Bas.
Meri Khilqat Hoi Fatima Ke Liye.
Lab Pe ALLAH Ho Akbar Tha Our Khud Bakhud.
Chakki Chalne Lagi Fatima Ke Liye.
Kaba Qausain Tak Aa Gaye Mustafa Salman.
Baat Hone Lagi Fatima Ke Liye.
Hum Azadar Jannat Mein Jaein Gay Bas.
Kyun Ke Janat Bani Fatima Ke Liye.
Faz Kuruni Ki Tafseer Ban Kar Gaye.
Farsh Par Matami Fatima Ke Liye.
Kijiye Zindagani Mein Yeh Kijiye.
Dushmani Dosti Fatima Ke Liye.
Ghasiban-e-Fidak Pe Tabarah Karo.
Bas Yehi Hai Khushi Fatima Ke Liye.
Dar Pe Salman Hathoon Ko Joray Hoye.
Maut Thehri Rahi Fatima Ke Liye.
