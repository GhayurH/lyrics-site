1
Madina Mere Sarkar Dikha Den Haider Rizvi.
Mujh Ko Bhi Madina Mere Sarkar Dikha Dein.
Inn Aankhon Ko Apna Kabhi Dedar Kara Dein.
Chahein Jo Agar Aap Tu Kya Kya Nahi Hota.
Ya Shah-e-Ummam Mera Muqadar Bhi Jaga Dein.
Galiyon Mein Madine Ki Parhon Naat Main Aaqa.
Dein Izn-e-Safar Our Mujhe Taufeeq-e-Sana Dein.
Woh Dil Bhala Dunya Ki Taraf Kaisay Ho Mayal.
Jis Dil Mein Hoon Sarkar-e-Madina Ki Hi Yadein.
Dar Dar Pe Bhataktay Hoye Insano Se Keh Do.
Aa Kar Dar-e-Sarkar Pe Sab Sar Ko Jhuka Dein.
Kirdar Bananey Pe Jo Aaein Kabhi Sarkar.
Qanbar Koi Buzar Koi Salman Bana Dein.
Mangay Jo Koi Aaqa Se Hasnain Ka Sadqa.
Oss Par Dono Hathoon Se Khazaney Woh Luta Dein.
Ya Aaqa Yeh Haider-o- Asad Ki Hai Guzarish.
Hubdaron Ko Dunya Ke Har Ik Gham Se Chura Dein.
