Khuwab Mein Jalwa Numa Mukhtar Hussain Fatehpuri

1
Khuwab Mein Jalwa Numa Jalwah-e-Sarkar Howa
Main Jo Soya Tu Muqadar Mera Bedar Howa
Jis Ne Abbas Ke Rozay Ki Ziyarat Kar Li
Woh Ujalon Ka Sanadyafta Zawwar Howa
Ulfat-e-Hazrat-e-Abbas Ne Marne Na Diya
Maut Se Milna Milana Tu Kayi Bar Howa

2
Woh Muhibbon Ki Iyadat Ke Liye Aate Hain
Barha Main Bhi Issi Shoq Mein Beemar Howa
Jis Ko Ghazi Tere Parcham Ki Hawaein Na Milein
Oss Ko Jeena Hi Nahi Marna Bhi Dushwar Howa
Buturab Our Shariyat Se Bagawat Ki Thi
Sheikh Turbat Mein Pohanchtay Hi Giraftar Howa
