Rab Janay Kevain, Lungiyan Nay Sajjad Diyan Ghariyaan

Jadh Saath Waalay Dar Tay, Takda Si Phupiyaan Kharriyan, Kevain……

Khof e Khuda Na Kitta, Kalmay Day Haamiyon Nay

Pathraan Da Mi Wasaaya, Wich Shaam Shaamiyon Nay

Jadh Shaam Day Wich Aaiyan, Yaseen Diyan Larriyan,Kevain

Ghar Chon Na Baar Aaiyan, Bazaar Na Wekhay Nay

Ayna Dard Sataiyaan Nay, Darbaar Na Wekhay Nay

Bin Chaadran Jo Khariyan, Hin Pardadaar Barriyan, Kevain……

Sung Meray Nay Mastooran, Karaan Ki Mai Dasa Baba

Saday Parday Bana Baba, Quraan Sunaa Baba

Naizay Tay Sunr Ke Syed, Jadon Surataan Si Parhiyaan, Kevain….

Gal Touq Tay Zanjeeran, Quraan Na Chaanda Si

Sir Naizay Tay Syed Da, Reya Neer Wahanda Si

Wich Karbala Muhaari, Jadh Si Muharaan Parhiyaan, Kevain….
