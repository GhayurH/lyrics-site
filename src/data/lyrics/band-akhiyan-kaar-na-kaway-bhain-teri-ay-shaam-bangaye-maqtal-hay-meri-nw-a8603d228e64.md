Band Akhiyan Kaar Na Kaway Bhain Teri, Ay Shaam Bangaye Maqtal Hay Meri

May Vaindee Raiyaan Haar Zarb Teri  Ay Shaam Bangaye Maqtal Hay Meri

Noh Lakh Dha Majma, Meray Khullay Vaal Ay

Abid Toh Puchlay Turna Muhaal Ay

Teri Sohni Nibhgaye Hun Waari Meri

Ay Shaam Bangaye Maqtal Hay Meri.

Paidal Turha Kay, Usnu Lay Aawo

Puchda Yazid Ay Mainu Daswo

Es Kafilay Vich Zainab Hay Kairi

Ay Shaam Bangaye Maqtal Hay.

Fizza Day Waris, Vi Aaye Nana

Zainab Dah Waris Aaya Koyi Nah

Sirr Nangay Rondhi Raye Dhee Hay Teri

Ay Shaam Bangaye Maqtal Hay Meri.

Chelum Manawan, Karbal Ch Aayi

Ghaal La Kabar Nu Kaway Zahra Jaayi

Dus Kiwain Ujri Hay Behn Teri

Ay Shaam Bangaye Maqtal Hay Meri.

Haar Jah Tay Veeran, Pathaar May Khaandi

Girr Jaanda Abid Us Nu Uthandi

Oh Bheer Gaye Ay Veeran Batheri

Ay Shaam Bangaye Maqtal Hay Meri.

Enjh Hassan Lutiya, Zahra Da Ghaar Ay

Ghoray Day Ghal Vich Ghazi Da Saar Ay

Dasan Museebat May Kairi Kairi

Ay Shaam Bangaye Maqtal Hay Meri
