1
Khuda Ki Azmat Bata Raha Hai Ali Ka Sajda
Jahan Ko Shar Se Bacha Raha Hai Ali Ka Sajda
Koi Tu Hai Jo Wahi Nabi Par Utarta Hai
Koi Tu Hai Jo Mizaj-e-Kun Ko Ubharta Hai
Wohi Khuda Hai Jata Raha Hai Ali Ka Sajda
Jo Be-Aqeeda Dilon Ki Tauqeer Ban Gaya Hai
Jo Mushrikon Ke Zehan Mein Tameer Ho Gaya Hai
Har Aise But Ko Gira Raha Hai Ali Ka Sajda
Chalo Ke Khair-ul-Amal Ki Khatir Qadam Barhao
Agar Ho Ashiq Ali Wali Ke Tu Jald Aao
Keh Masjidon Mein Bula Raha Hai Ali Ka Sajda
Jo Apne Zehno Ki Khudsari Par Array Howay Hain
Ali Ki Seerat Ke Jo Muqabil Khare Howay Hain
Unhein Haqeeqat Dikha Raha Hai Ali Ka Sajda
Suno Ke Tareekh Bhi Sadein Laga Rahi Hai
Daleel-e-Fuztu Rab-il-Kaba Bata Rahi Hai
Nuseriyat Ko Mita Raha Hai Ali Ka Sajda
Halak Kar De Gi Tujh Ko Teri Yeh Be-Niyazi
Tere Aqeeday Se Mera Mula Nahi Hai Razi
Tu Jaan Kar Bhi Bhula Raha Hai Ali Ka Sajda
Bura Lage Tu Lage Aye:YASIR RAZA: Kisi Ko
Likha Wohi Hai Pasand Jo Hai Ali Wali Ko
Nazar Se Parde Utha Raha Hai Ali Ka Sajda
