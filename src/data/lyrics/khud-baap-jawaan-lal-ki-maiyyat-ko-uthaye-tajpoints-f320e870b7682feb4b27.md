Khud Baap Jawaan Lal Ki Maiyyat Ko Uthaye.
Ya Rab Kissi Dushman Pe Bhi Yeh Waqt Na Aaye.

1
Sheh Se Ali Akbar Ka Janaza Na Uthay Ga.
Darya Se Koi Ja Ke Alamdar Ko Laye.
Youn Taiz Qadam Janib-e-Khaima Chale Sarwar.
Ghabra Ke Khain Ran Mein Na Zainab Nikal Aaye.

2
Sehray Ki Jagah Dekhe Jawaan Betay Ki Maiyyat.
Taqdeer Kissi Maa KO Yeh Manzar Na Dikhaye.
Youn Sham Pohanchne Ki Takallum Ko Hai Jaldi.
Noha Ali Akbar Ka Woh Zainab Ko Sunaye.
