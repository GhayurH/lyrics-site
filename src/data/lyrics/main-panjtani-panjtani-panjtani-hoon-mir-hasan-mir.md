1
Main Panjtani Panjtani Panjtani Hoon Mir Hasan Mir.
Dawa Hai Mera Nar-e-Jahanum Se Bari Hoon.
Main Panjtani Panjtani Panjtani Hoon.
So Jaye Jo Qismat Tu Jaga Leta Hoon Khud Hi.
Har Bigra Howa Kam Bana Leta Hoon Khud Hi.
Main Banda-e-Sarkar-e-Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Hoon.
Dewana-e-Haider Hoon May Ishq Se Sarshar.
Abbas Ka Nokar Hoon Sheh-e-Dein Ka Azadar.
Phir Kyun Na Kahon Main Keh Muqadar Ka Dhani Hoon.
Muhtaj Nahi Hoon Main Zamane Mein Kissi Ka.
Sadqa Mujhe Milta Hai Bohat Aal-e-Nabi Ka.
Iss Dar Ka Bhikari Hoon Do Aalam Mein Ghani Hoon.
Yeh Baat Hawa Karti Hai Aandhi Ki Zabaan Mein.
Jo Panjtan-e-Pak Ke Dushman Hain Jahan Mein.
Main Naam-o-Nishaan Unn Ka Mitanay Ko Chali Hoon.
Kehta Hai Azadar Ka Har Hath Yeh Uth Kar.
Har Ik Munafiq Hai Mere Panjay Ki Zid Par.
Main Hath Hoon Momin Ka Har Ik Aisa Qavi Hoon.
Har Ahl-e-Arab Ahl-e-Ajam Ahl-e-Adab Ko.
Panj Waqt Namaz Aa Ke Batati Hai Yeh Sab Ko.
Main Aal-e-Muhammad Ke Gharanay Mein Pali Hoon.
Kehte Hain Jisay Khuld Woh Ghar Mere Liye Hai.
Ta Arsh-e-Barin Izn-e-Safar Mere Liye Hai.
Main Koi Farishta Hoon Malak Hoon Na Pari Hoon.
Roshan Meri Taqdeer Hai Bezaar Hai Ahsas.
Hasnain Ki Ulfat Ke Ujalay Hain Mere Pas.
Main:GOHAR-E-Nayab Hoon Farzand-e-Wali Hoon.
