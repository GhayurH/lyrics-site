Kafan Bandh Sar Pe Baraye Baqa Shahadat Saadat Ka Nara Laga.
Yeh Kar-e-Shujat Hai Kar Ibitda Shahadat Saadat Ka Nara Laga.

1
Keh TU Hai Paristar-e-Khaiber Shikan Alam Tere Sar Pe Hai Saya Figan.
Pareshaniyon Ka Tujhe Khof Kya Shahadat Saadat Ka Nara Laga.
Bara Motasham Hai Tera Martaba Hai Hasil Tujhe Syeda Ki Dua.
Zara Apne Molai Tewar Dikha Shahadat Saadat Ka Nara Laga.

2
Hai Tu Bu-Turabi Azadar Hai Nabi Ke Gharane Ka Hubdar Hai.
Ghulam-e-Shehansha-e-Karb-o-Bala Shahadat Saadat Ka Nara Laga.
Yazeedi Anasir Ka Hai Samna Hai Matloob Phir Hur Ka Josh-e-Wiga.
Wafadariyaan De Rahi Hain Sada Shahadat Saadat Ka Nara Laga.

3
Na Kar Fikar Dunya-e-Fani Hai Yeh Kabhi Na Kabhi Jan Jani Hai Yeh.
Tu Behter Yehi Hai Amar Ho Ke Ja Shahadat Saadat Ka Nara Laga.
Jasarat Dikhane Ke Lamhe Hain Yeh Shahadat Ke Pane Ke Lamhe Hain Yeh.
Yahi Waqt Hai Jag:YASIR RAZA: Shahadat Saadat Ka Nara Laga.
