1
Kabe Pe Likh Do Ye Misra Joan Rizvi.
Kabay Pe Likh Do Yeh Misra Innal Batila Kana Zahuqa.
Dekho Dekho Aa Gaye Maula Innal Batila Kana Zahuqa.
Woh Jo Kabay Ke Hain Waris Ab Woh Aane Walay Hain.
Woh Kabay Ki Chat Par Parcham Ab Lehrane Walay Hain.
Haq Hai Kya Our Batil Kya Hai Yeh Samjhane Walay Hain.
Khul Jaye Ga Sab Ka Aqeeda Innal Batila Kana Zahuqa.
Koi Nahi Rokay Ga Tum Ko Khul Ke Ho Ga Phir Pursa.
Aye Maula Ke Matamdaron Taiz Rakho Zanjeer-o-Qama.
Ghar Mein Khuda Ke Khon Ka Pursa Khud Karwaein Gay Maula.
Soch Ke Dena Mufti Fatwa Innal Batila Kana Zahuqa.
Kabay Mein Jitnay Bhi Sanam Hain Phir Se Giraye Jaein Gay.
Khud Se Bachay Hain Jitnay Hakim Sare Laye Jaein Gay.
Jitnay Bhi Darbar Bachay Hain Sare Dhaye Jaein Gay.
Kehta Hai Yeh Daar Al Amara Innal Batila Kana Zahuqa.
Khud Se Khuda Jo Ban Baithay Hain Hoga Inn Sab Ka Bhi Hisab.
Jo Kuch Bhi Kartay Aaye Hain Dena Paray Ga Oss Ka Jawab.
Sab Ko Bola Ke Samne Apne Maula Karein Gay Aisay Khitab.
Kehta Hai Tum Sab Ka Shajrah Innal Batila Kana Zahuqa.
Bag-e-Fidak Ke Gasib Se Bhi Aap Ne Lena Hai Badlah.
Taig-e-Jari Bhi Dekh Rahi Hai Aap Ke Aane Ka Rasta.
Aap Khuda Ka Wadah Hain Jo Maqtal Mein Sarwar Se Howa.
Pora Hoga Rab Ka Wadah Innal Batila Kana Zahuqa.
Aap Hi Hazir Aap Hi Nazir Kaisa Rutba Aap Ka Hai.
Shiyon Ke Sar Par Jo Dekhein Maula Saya Aap Ka Hai.
Taktay Hain Khud Hazrat-e-Esa Jis Ko Rasta Aap Ka Hai.
Aap Se Hat Kar Sab Hai Kharaba Innal Batila Kana Zahuqa.
Aap Khuda Ki Hujjat-e-Aakhir Aap Hi Hadi Aap Wali.
Aap Hasan Ke Bagh Mein Goya Narjis Ki Masoom Kali.
Aap Mein Aks-e-Peygamber Bhi Aap Mein Jah-o-Jalal-e-Ali.
Haider Jaisa Aap Ka Hamla Innal Batila Kana Zahuqa.
Aap Ke Jaisa Koi Nahi Hai Maula Sari Dunya Mein.
Aisay Kareem Our Aisay Sakhi Hain Aaqa Sari Dunya Mein.
Aap Ke Hi Aane Ka Bapa Hai Charcha Sari Dunya Mein.
Aap Ke Dam Se Hai Yeh Zamana Innal Batila Kana Zahuqa.
Esa Bhi Azlan Kharey Hain Sath Imam-e-Hujjat Ke.
Unn Ki Imamat Mein Kartay Hain Apne Khaliq Ko Sajday.
Sachay Aik Taraf Hain Aik Taraf Sare Jhotay.
Reh Jaye Ga Lashkar Sacha Innal Batila Kana Zahuqa.
