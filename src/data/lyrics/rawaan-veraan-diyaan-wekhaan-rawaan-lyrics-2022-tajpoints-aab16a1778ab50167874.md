1
Rawaan Veraan Diyaan Wekhaan Rawaan
Veraan De Aan Taun Pehlaan Wichri Na Mar Jawaan.
Mainu Yaad Asghar Di Aaway Khali Jhola Behan Jholaway.
Band Ho Jawan Tak Ke Akhiyaan Khuwabaan Wich Tainu Behan Khadaway.
Karni Aan Main Galaan Asghar Khali Hondiyaan Banwaan.
Tere Qadam Nishanaa Nu Main Hath Lawaan Taun Nazri Aawein.
Main Roni Aan Veer Oss Welay Na Taun Mainu Gal Nal Lawin.
Ainj Lagda Aye Pak Nabi De Pairaan Nu Hath Lawaan.
Aj Di Raat Payi Aakhay Mainu Sughra Tere Veer Nayi Aana.
Nayi Rehna Oh Sir Akbar Da Jis Sir Te Taun Sehra Lana.
Apniyaan Aasaan Nu Main Akbar Kiwein Kafan Pawawaan.
Ainj Tur Gaye Ne Sare Nana Jiwein Maiyyat Chad Janday Ne.
Kuj Din Chad Ke Ohvi Nana Qabraan Uttay Mur Aanday Ne.
Baba Veer Te Behna Nana Mainu Chad Gayiyaan Mawaan.
Mukdi Jaway Raat Nawin Di Naal Aiday Sughra Muk Jana.
Lagni Aye Barchi Akbar Nu Waqt Ne Sughra Layi Ruk Jana.
De Nayi Sakdi Jehri Loko Veraan Da Parchawaan.
Rab Jane Oh Manzar Loko Ujriyaan Ne Jadon Watan Te Aana.
Rat Ro Ke Najfi Abid Ne Sughra Nu Aye Kool Bolana.
Main Abid Tainu Akbar Ban Ke Pehlaan Gal Nal Lawaan.
Noha Khuwan Party Ch Faiyaz Party Sialkot.
Matami Sangat
Anjuman-e-Sipah-e-Ahl-e-Bait Sialkot
