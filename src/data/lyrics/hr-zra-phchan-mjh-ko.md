Hurr zara pehchaan mujhko bolta Quran hun main.

1
Marzeeyay Rab-e-Jalee hoon, dard ka sultan hoon main.

2
1) Tu mere bachpan ka wada manga ta tujhko Khudah se
Tera aana Karbala mein likha hai meri Raza se
Tu bahattar main hai shamilHurr teri pehchaan hun main.

3
2) Main Hussain ibn Ali hun malik-e-khuld-e-barri hoon
Muthi main dariya hai meray fitratan piyasa nehi hun
Hurr meray seenay seh lag jaaaj ka mehmaan hun main.

4
3) Deen hai meray karam pe ambiya ki laaj hun main
Ab khudah ko fiqar kaisa Karbala mein aaj hun main
Yeh zameen meri shaha datla ilah ki jaan hun main.

5
4) Jung hoti gar gawara bhejta Abbas ko main
Kuch na rehta Karbala mein khatam karta pyaas ko main
Haq o ki batil ki laraimain bhi aik maidaan hun main.

6
5) Sir mera naizay pe hogi saath chadar bhi hogi
Balon ka parda banaye awn ki madar bhi hogi
Mujh pe ehsaan hai zainabdeen pe ehsaan hun main.

7
6) Dekhna Hurr meray ghum may har jagah matam bhi hongay
Khoon jismo se bahein ga momino mein hum bhi hongay
Hun azadaron ka maulanana ka farman hun main.

8
7) Kufr kay rastay chala tha aabay kauser mil gaya hai
Raat mein Shahroz Hurr ko roz-e-mehshar mil gaya
Hurr ki kia taqdeer badliaaj bhi hairaan hun main.
