Qatl Hue Sher E Khuda Mesum Abbas.
Kartay They Jibreel Yeh Noha Qatal Hoye Shair-e-Khuda.

1
Roney Lagi Masjid-e-Kofa Qatal Hoye Shair-e-Khuda.
Khana-e-Zahra Mein Jab Pohanchi Yeh Gham Ki Khabar.
Taig-e-Jafa Chal Gayi Haider-e-Karrar Par.
Khon Mein Doba Hai Musalla Qatal Hoye Shair-e-Khuda.

2
Kofay Ki Galiyon Mein Thi Aik Qayamat Bapa.
Bewah Yateemo Mein Yeh Nalah-o-Shewan Utha.
Kaun Humein De Ga Sahara Qatal Hoye Shair-e-Khuda.
Baap Ko Iss Haal Mein Dekh Ke Roye Pisar.

3
Zakhmi Paray Hain Ali Sara Badan Khoon Mein Tar.
Keh Ke Yehi Phainka Amama Qatal Hoye Shair-e-Khuda.
Aik Chatai Pe Phir Unn Ko Litaya Gaya.
Khana-e-Zahra Mein Jab Maula Ko Laya Gaya.

4
Karti Thin Shehzadiyaan Noha Qatal Hoye Shair-e-Khuda.
Dekhi Jo Jarrah Ne Zakhmi Jabeen-e-Ali.
Shabbar-o-Shabeer Se Ro Ke Woh Bola Yehi.
Kaisay Kahon Aap Se Jumla Qatal Hoye Shair-e-Khuda.

5
Daag-e-Yateemi Mila Syed-e-Mazloom Ko.
Kaun Sanbhalay Ga Ab Zainab-o-Kulsoom Ko.
Sab Se Bara Hai Yehi Sadma Qatal Hoye Shair-e-Khuda.
Ro Ke Hi Unees Our Bees Ka Din Kat Gaya.

6
Aayi Jo Ikeeswin Kar Gaye Maula Qaza.
Gaunj Raha Tha Yehi Noha Qatal Hoye Shair-e-Khuda.
Roti Hain Shehzadiyaan Kunba Dil Afgar Hai.
Haider-e-Karrar Ka Aakhri Didaar Hai.

7
Ab Hai Tasali Na Dilasa Qatal Hoye Shair-e-Khuda.
 Mazhar-o- Meesum Younhi Nohay Ka Ho Haq Ada.
Lafz Parhein Marsiya Our Labon Par Boka.
Kehte Hoye Aik Hi Jumla Qatal Hoye Shair-e-Khuda.
