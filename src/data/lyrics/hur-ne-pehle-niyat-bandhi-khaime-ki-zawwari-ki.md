Hur Ne Pehle Niyat Bandhi Khaime KI Zawwari Ki
Phir Chehray Pe Khak Mali Our Sajday Ki Teyari Ki
Munkar Our Nakeer Se Pehle Qabar Ki Mati Pochay Gi
Tu Ne Kitne Zakhm Kamaye Kitni Matamdari Ki
Shab Bhar Jagney Walay Hur Ne Waqt-e-Sahar Ayelan Kiya
Farsh-e-Azza Par Sab Se Pehle Main Ne Shab Bedari Ki
Ik Lashkar Nu Lakh Ka Lashkar Our Natija Sadiyon Ka
Aik Wafadari Ki Saf Thi Baqi Sab Ghadari Ki
Matam Ki Taqleed Mein Apne Kunbay Ki Tosee Hoi
Hum Ne Soag Mananay Walay Dil Se Rishta Dari Ki
Ghaat Pe Qabza Karne Walay Adil Ne Ik Cholo Ki
Ik Masoom Gawahi Pa Kar Had Darya Par Jari Ki
Parda, Giriya, Badla, Qaziya, Mujrim, Fard-e-Jurm, Saza
Khutbon Ne Aasan Bachai Har Manzil Dushwari Ki
Ik Romal Sawari AKBAR Le Jaye Gi Sidrah Tak
Itni Qeemat Tu Banti Hai Ashkon Ki Khuddari Ki
