1
Mazloom Hussain Ki Humsheera Nadeem Sarwar.
Mazloom Hussain Ki Humsheera Islam Bachaney Aayi Hai.
Shabeer Ka Sari Dunya Ko Paigham Sunanay Aayi Hai.
Ashor Ke Din Jis Bibi Ka Aabad Chaman Barbad Howa.
Sab Bhai Bhatijay Marey Gaye Saman Jala Ghar Bar Luta.
Kuch Bhi Na Bacha Lekin Zainab Yeh Deen Bachaney Aayi Hai.
Har Khutba Iss Ka Majlis Hai Har Aanso Iss Ka Matam Hai.
Ashor Basa Hai Aankhon Mein Our Dil Mein Bahatar Ka Gham Hai.
Yeh Ahl-e-Azza Se Farsh-e-Azza Ghar Ghar Bichwanay Aayi Hai.
Hum Par Pathar Barsatay Ho Hanstay Ho Jashan Manatay Ho.
Syed Ka Khon Bahatay Ho Our Bagi Bagi Kehte Ho.
Shabeer Tu Pak Namazi Hai Yeh Tum Ko Bataney Aayi Hai.
Konain Ki Malka Ki Beti Woh Sharam-o-Haya Ki Shehzadi.
Haye Sham-e-Ghareebaan Mein Jis Ki Chadar Cheeni Thi Woh Bibi.
Dunya Ki Maon Behno Ko Pardah Sikhlanay Aayi Hai.
Jis Ne Bhai Ki Gardan Par Chalte Hoye Khanjar Dekha Tha.
Na Chupney Doon Gi Khon Tera Shabeer Se Jis Ka Wadah Tha.
Woh Zainab Sham Mein Bhai Ka Matam Karwaney Aayi Hai.
 Sarwar Jawad Reha Ho Kar Jab Aayi Karb-o-Bala Muztar.
Haye Peet Ke Sar Matam Kar Kar Chilayi Bhai Ki Turbat Par.
Uth Bhai Hussain Tera Chehlum Teri Behan Mananay Aayi Hai.
