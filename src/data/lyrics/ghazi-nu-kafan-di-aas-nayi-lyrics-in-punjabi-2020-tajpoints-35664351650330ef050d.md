1
Ghazi Nu Kafan Di Aas Nayi
Ghazi Nu Kafan Di Aas Nayi Na Pyas Bhojanra Chanda Aye.
Bas Maran Taun Pailaan Khaima Ga Wich Mashk Pochanra Chanda Aye.
Sir Veer Da Wekh Ke Naizay Te Aidi Katiyaan Banwaan Tarap Gayiyaan.
Lagda Aye Veer Da Sir Apnray Seene Nal Laonran Chanda Aye.
Ainu Dukh Nayi Badan Te Zarbaan Da Bas Akhiyaan Te Na Waar Hunr.
Aye Doron Disday Aaqa Taun Nazraan Nayi Hataran Chanda Aye.
Piya Akbar Wekhda Aye Khaimiyaan Choon Ainu Ghazi Waar Sikhaye Sanr.
Aj Zarbaan Sainr Da Sabaq V Aye Akbar Nu Sikharan Chanda Aye.
Zainab De Khaimay Wal Ghazi Rukh Kita Zeen Taun Lainr Welay.
Oh Apnran Kaba Takda Hoya Dunya Taun Janra Chanda Aye.
Assi Khadam Aan Oh Malik Ne Har Welay Dasda Rainda Si.
Abbas Fazal Nu Lagda Aye Abbas Banaran Chanda Aye.
 Saqlain Furat Da Panri Nayi Haq Mehar-e-Bibi Zahra Aye.
Aye Dig Na Jaway Rait Uttay Abbas Bacharan Chanda Aye.
