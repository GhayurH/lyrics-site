Mehwe Dua hai Zahra, Takbeer woh sunaaye, mera Mahdi jald aaye
Ek haath hai pehlu par, karti hain dua haye, ek haath woh uthaye

1
1. Aakar hisaab le woh, ummat se har sitam ka
Jo usne ham pe dhaaye
Mere hare k mujrim ko kabr se uthaaye, aur phir sazaa sunay

2
2. Baba ke baad mujhse, cheena mera Fadaq bhi
Haqq maangne gayi toh
Ilzaam jhooth darne, ke mujhpe kyon lagaye, joothe gawa banyein

3
3. Hasnain-e ke baba ki masnad pe aan bethain
Aur aankhein kyun azaa se
Hasnain-e ke baba ke kis mo laqab hatayein, kyun dardo gham bharaye

4
4. Mere hassan ko aakhir dafna ne na diya kyun
Pehlu mein nana jaan ke
Aur phir hassan ke, lashe par teer kyun chalayen, masmoom karke haye

5
5. Mehdi tera bulakar, ye hurmala se pooche
Woh kya khata karenga
Jo apni maa ko madar kehna na paaye, khud hurmala batayein

6
6.aasif dua ye maange, hum sheh ko ronay wale
Haathon ko ab uthaayein
Nusrat ko uski pohnche, jaise hi woh bulayein, apni sina uthaayein
