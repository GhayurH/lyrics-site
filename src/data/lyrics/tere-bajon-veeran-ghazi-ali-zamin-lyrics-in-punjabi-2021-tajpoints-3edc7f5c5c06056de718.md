1
Tere Bajon Veeran Ghazi Ali Zamin.
Tere Bajoon Veran Ghazi Aynj Shama Pe Gayiyaan.
Mera Sir Zakhmi Haye Khon Aalodah Wich Mitiyaan Reh Gayiyaan.
Wekh Sajjad Zara Kiwein Wasda Aye Bhira.
Odi Nagri Wich Asaan Aanr Taun Pehlaan Aountaan Taun Leh Gayiyaan.
Khaimay Saran Loki Jiwein Maran Loki.
Ahsas Hoya Pardais Che Veran Assi Kaliyaan Reh Gayiyaan.
Sir Taun Chadar Leh Gayi Haye Main Rondi Reh Gayi.
Tere Baaj Veran Hathaan Wich Mere Haye Rasiyaan Pe Gayiyaan.
Haye Main Takdi Reh Gayi Kiwein Jiyondi Reh Gayi.
Teri Lash Uttay Jadon Teriyaan Banwaan Mainu Labnriyaan Pe Gayiyaan.
Haye Darbaraan Wich Tang Bazaraan Wich.
Mera Naa Le Ke Oh Deinr Sadawaan Mainu Sunniyaan Pe Gayiyaan.
Lal Akbar Si Mera Kha Gayi Karb-o-Bala.
Jo Likhiyaan Sanr Sughra Ne Duawaan Oh Likhiyaan Reh Gayiyaan.
Haye Walail Diyaan Veer Jarnail Diyaan.
Nu Lakh Qatil De Hath Wich Takiyaan Oh Zulfaan Reh Gayiyaan.
Deway Balday Si Jithay Lok Hasday Si Jithay.
Tere Abid Di Akhiyaan Wich Veran Oh Galiyaan Reh Gayiyaan.
Haye Qasim Na Riya Ro Ke Zainab Ne Kehya.
Aynj Bikhar Gaya Phul Tera Farwa Bas Kaliyaan Reh Gayiyaan.
Aye Musafat Kar Gayi Haye Sakina Mar Gayi.
Teri Ladli De Pairaan Diyaan Zakhmi Haye Taliyaan Reh Gayiyaan.
Haye Zulmaa Da Safar Koi Saya Na Shajar.
Thak Haar Ke Ghazi Teri Qabar Di Chawein Main Veran Beh Gayiyaan.
Aayi Ghazi Di Sada Sainr Na Howein Khafa.
Shabeer Diyaan Hukmaa Wich Bajhiyaan Sab Jurtaan Reh Gayiyaan.
 Naeem Sadiq Te Raza Hai Muzamil Di Dua.
Ral Ke Wekhan Oh Sham Di Nagri Jo Sangtaan Reh Gayiyaan.
