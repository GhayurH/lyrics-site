1
Darbar Sharabi De Kinj Parde Daraan Aa Gayiyaan
Kayi Bastiyan Lang Ke Sar Nangay Zahra Jaiyaan Nanay Da Deen Bacha Gayiyaan.
Zainab Nu Gham Apni Chadar Da Buliya Oss Welay.
Jadon Bharjaiyaan V Akbar, Qasim Diyaan Mawaan Darbar Ich Baaj Rida Gayiyaan.

2
Ik Sham Ghareebi Wali Wich Karbal De Zainab Te.
Aye Chanan Walay Sab Din V Qaidaan Andar Ghurbat Di Sangaan Kha Gayiyaan.
Bainraan De Sabar Di Manzil Si Oh Kehri Rab Jane.
Veraan Diyaan Apne Be Kafan Dafan Lashaan Nu Jadon Chor Ke Wich Sehra Gayiyaan.

3
Balway Wich Wekh Ke Phophiyaan Na Mar Jaye Haye Abid.
Aisay Layi Akhiyaan Ghayoor Jigar De Lahu Chal Apne Te Pardah Pa Gayiyaan.
Ais Layi Saqlain Aye Sham Diyaan Qaidaan Te Khush Hoiyaan.
Bazar Guzar Ke Zindan Diyaan Diwaraan De Parday De Wich Aa Gayiyaan.
