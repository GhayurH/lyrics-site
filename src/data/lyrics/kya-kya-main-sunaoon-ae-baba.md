Hadier Se Tha Noha Beti Ka Kya Kya Main Sunaoon Aye Baba
Khaimay Bhi Jalay Samaan Bhi Luta Kya Kya Main Sunaon Aye Baba
Sab Yawar-o-Nasir Qatal Hoye Din Sara Kata Rotay Rotay
Maqtal Ka Tha Qissa Dard Bhara Kya Kya Main Sunaon Aye Baba
Ab Aaye Ho Tum Baba Mere Hain Sham-e-Ghareebaan Ke Saye
Beemar Hai Bas Marne Se Bacha Kya Kya Main Sunaon Aye Baba
Woh Aoun-o-Muhammad Lal Marey Maidaan Mein Gaye Our Khoob Laray
Phir Bhi Na Bacha Bhaiyya Mera Kya Kya Main Sunaon Aye Baba
Pamal Howa Tapoon Se Badan Jannat Ko Sidhara Ibn-e-Hasan
Mushkil Se Utha Oss Ka Lasha Kya Kya Main Sunaon Aye Baba
Tasveer-e-Payamber Bach Na Saki Seena Pe Lagi Oss Ke Barchi
Tab Haal Bura Tha Laila Ka Kya Kya Main Sunaon Aye Baba
Lashkar Se Addo Ke Takra Kar Abbas Ne Bhar Li Mashk Magar
Darya Pe Gaye Pani Na Piya Kya Kya Main Sunaon Aye Baba
Che Mah Ke Bachay Asghar Ki Nawak Se Hoi Gardan Zakhmi
Sarwar Ne Kaha Innalillah Kya Kya Main Sunaon Aye Baba
Ghorey Se Gira Jab Bhai Mera Oss Waqt Qayamat Thi Barpa
Har Simt Tha Shoor Wawela Kya Kya Main Sunaon Aye Baba
Rotay They SHAMIM Oss Waqt Ali Rodad Ko Sun Kar Beti Ki
Zalim Ne Rawa Har Zulm Rakha Kya Kya Main Sunaon Aye Baba
