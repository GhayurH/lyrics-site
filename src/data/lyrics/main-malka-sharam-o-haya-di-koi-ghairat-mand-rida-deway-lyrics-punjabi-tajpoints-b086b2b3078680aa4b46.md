1
Main Malka Sharam o Haya Di Koi Ghairat Mand Rida Deway.
Nayi Paidal Turn Di Aadi Main Majmay Nu Door Hata Deway.
Waris Tatheer Di Haan Main Mere Ghar Aaya Quran Aye.
Mera Nana Mursal-e-Azam Hai Nalay Dada Pak Imran Aye.
Main Waqt Di Zahra Haan Logo Mainu Turn Da Koi Rah Deway.
Meri Maa Di Arshaan Taun Chadar Khud Khaliq Ne Bhajwayi Si.
Meri Maa De Bajoon Oh Chadar Mere Hissay De Wich Aayi Si.
Koi Shimar Taun Mainu Oh Chadar Haye Ja Ke Moor Liya Deway.
Narah Takbeer Boland Kar Ke Lutay Khaimay Be Taqseran De.
Pamal Janazay Ho Gaye Ne Sir Saang Te Charh Gaye Veraan De.
Mere Putar Bhatijay Mar Dittay Koi Mainu Pursa Aa Deway.
Ik Putar Hai Qaidi Naal Mere Jidi Ghairat Kamar Jhuka Gayi Aye.
Darbar Di Peshi Abid De Akhiyaan Choon Khon Rawa Gayi Aye.
Mere Putar Beemar Muhari De Koi Tauq Zanjeer Laha Deway.
 Azhar Aye Sham De Lokaan Nu Bazar Che Aakhay Bint-e-Ali.
Mainu Ajar-e-Risalat Aa Diyo Haye Loko Main Haan Bint-e-Nabi.
Nayi Fidak Ridawaan Mangdi Main Tableeg Da Koi Sila Deway.
