Bibi hath kad kafan wicho akhe karbala da gareeb ah bachra

1
Akhri waari dukhi ambri koo
Akhe seene de naal laah bachra

2
Menkoo turbat deh wich rukheh wendi
Lash tedi koo na bhulesa meh
Jada mangsi thoo paani tarap uthi
Allah jane kabar ch maa bachra

3
Jeewe ambri kafan vicho chadke
Tenkoo seene deh naal lehndi eh
Eewe sadmaar mekoo gal lahvi
Jada akha meh karbala bachra

4
Eho armaan rehgaya dil wich
Raaj keh amri nahi ditha tenkoo
Baad babe deh meri zindagi koo
Ditha darbaar meh muka bachra

5
Raat ashoor jeewe karbal wich
Hoke bardah eh vein kar kar keh
Tere sajde dee jhaah koo ah karsa
Naal waala dee meh safa bachra

6
Ewew lagda tah vein aj tayi
Sain zahra koyi karendhi heh
Mere har il zakham da mol posi
Laal narjis da hun teh aah bachra
