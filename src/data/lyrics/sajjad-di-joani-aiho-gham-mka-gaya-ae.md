Yehi gham to kha gaya hay

۱
Sajjad ki jawani, yehi gham to kha gaya hay
Bikhri hain kyun bazaron may
Yun aayatain qurani

۲
Maa Aon ki pukaray, de de koi to chadar
Mushkil may koi kar de, Syedon pe mehrbani
Asghar ko yad karti, har ujri goad maa ki
Uske qatl pe cha gayi, kainat may hairani
Dikhla ke yun baha dain, oonton ko bhi pila dain
Syedon ke bachay pyasay, reh gaye na paya pani
Lipta ke apni banhain, bhar ke Sakina aahain
Mujhe maar bhayya de gi, is qaid ki veerani
Rasiyon may hath dekhay, Kubra ke mehndi walay
Ankhon se jari ho gayi, tabhi khoon ki rawani
Sajjad ke jigar ko, yeh baat cheer gayi hay
Tareekh likh hi day gi, darbar ki kahani
