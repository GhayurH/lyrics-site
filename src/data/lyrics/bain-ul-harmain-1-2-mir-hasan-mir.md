Khaimazan Hain Yeh Jinna Do Haram Ke Darmiyaan
Aashiqon Ke Karwaan Hain Do Haram Ke Darmiyaan

1
Aasmaan Wale Kahan Hain Do Haram Ke Darmiyaan
Do Jahan Ke Do Jahan Hain Do Haram Ke Darmiyaan
Jis Muhammad Se Yahan Chahein Mulaqatein Karein
Aiye Iss Arsh Par Allah Se Batein Karein

2
Hai Aik Taraf Abbas Tere
Our Aik Taraf Hain Mula Hussain
Aye Arz-e-Yaqeen Kabe Ki Zameen
Kehti Hai Tujhe Kaba Qosain

3
Yeh Zameen Chand Sitaron Ki Jabeen Lagti Hai
Kehkishaan Iss Ke Tasaduq Mein Haseen Lagti Hai
La-Makani Ki Hadoon Se Yeh Qareen Lagti Hai
Yeh Tu Qosain Se Aagay Ki Zameen Lagti Hai

4
Jo Arsh Pe Tha Pehle Nalain
Oss Ne Bhi Utari Hai Nalain
Han Bolne Lagti Hai Khamoshi-e-Jazbaat Yahan
Ishq Ki Jari Hai Sadiyon Se Ibadaat yahan

5
Sare Aashiq Humein Mil Jate Hain Ik Sath Yahan
Our Nabiyon Se Bhi Hoti Hai Mulaqat Yahan
Adam Bhi Yahin Esa Bhi Yahin
Milte Hain Yahin Par Zulqarnain
