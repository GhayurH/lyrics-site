Aye Sham e Gharebaan Hasan Sadiq Lyrics
Zainab Ki Sada Aayi Aye Sham-e-Ghareebaan.
Kya Bhai Ki Khata Thi Aye Sham-e-Ghareebaan.
Sab Khaimay Jal Chukay Hain Chadar Nahi Hai Sar Par.
Akbar Raha Na Ghazi Aye Sham-e-Ghareebaan.
Seene Pe Soney Wali Khati Hai Ab Tamachay.
Had Ho Gayi Jafa Ki Aye Sham-e-Ghareebaan.
Jaltay Hoye Gharon Mein Jhola Bhi Jal Gaya Hai.
Rota Hai Aasmaan Bhi Aye Sham-e-Ghareebaan.
Gash Mein Para Hai Abid Kaisay Jagaye Zainab.
Hai Har Taraf Udasi Aye Sham-e-Ghareebaan.
Be Sar Paray Hain Lashay Zainab Kisay Sada De.
Looti Gayin Ridaein Aye Sham-e-Ghareebaan.
 Rehan Ran Mein Tarpa Ik Bawafa Ka Lasha.
Zainab Ne Jab Sada Di Aye Sham-e-Ghareebaan.
Bazu Bandhay Hoye Hain Qaidi Bani Hoi Hai.
Kofay Ki Shah Zadi Aye Sham-e-Ghareebaan.
