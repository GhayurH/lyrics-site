Hai Dua Yaad Magar Harf-e-Dua Yaad Nahi
Mere Nagmaat Ko Andaz-e-Nawa Yaad Nahi

1
Main Ne Palkon Se Dar-e-Yaar Pe Dastak Di Hai
Main Wo Sail Hoon Jisse Koi Sada Yaad Nahi
Main Ne Jin Ke Liye Rahoon Mein Bichaya Tha Laho
Hum Se Kehte Hain Wohi Ehd-e-Wafa Yaad Nahi

2
Kaise Bhar Ayin Sar-e-Shaam Kissi Ki Ankhein
Kaise Tharai Cheragon Ki Ziya Yaad Nahi
Siraf Dhundlaye Sitaron Ki Chamak Dekhi Hai
Kab Howa, Koan Howa, Mujh Se Juda, Yaad Nahi

3
Zindagi Jabr-e-Musalsal Ki Tarha Kati Hai
Jane Kiss Jurm Ki Payi Hai Saza Yaad Nahi
Ayo Ik Sajdah Karein Aalm-e-Madhoshi Mein
Loag Kehte Hain Keh Sagar Ko Khuda Yaad Nahi
