1
Akbar Tere Rah Haye Takdi Aye Sughra
Na Mar Jawaan Baaj Bhirawaan De Watna Te Veran Aa.
Veer Mar Jawan Te Jondiyaan Bhaina Layi Kadiyan Sanwaan Ne.
Tere Badoon Maut V Na Aayi Na Chalday Ne Hun Sah.

2
Pak Nanay Di Main Qabar Te Main Aakh Aayi Veer Nayi Hun Aane.
Hun Kehra Aye Dard Wandaway Jo Dunya Te Sughra Da.
Aakhay Akbar Piyo Nu Mainu Qatil Mera Kainda Si Keh Main.
Tere Babay Nu Wekhna Seene Choon Kinj Kadda Aye Naiza.

3
Zakhm Te Hath Rakh Ke Piyo Nu Akbar Aakhay Aakhna Sughra Nu.
Jinj Mehmal Taun Bhain Taun Utari Sain Aynj Zeen Taun Main Lathiya.
Jiwein Akbar, Sughra Jag Taun Tur Gayi Rondi Wekhdi Rahwaan Nu.
Aynj Hi Akbar Aakhri Sanwaan Wich Takda Si Khat Da Rah.
