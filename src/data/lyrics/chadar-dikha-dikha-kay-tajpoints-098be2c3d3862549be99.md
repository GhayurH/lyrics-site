Chadar Na Kafan Hai Door Watan.

1
Be Pardah Behan Hathon Mein Rasan.
Abbas Ki Behan Ko Shami Rula Rahe Hain Chadar Dikha Dikha Ke.
Rasta Bana Rahi Hai Bint-e-Ali Ka Fizza Kantay Hata Hata Ke.
Pardais Mein Kissi Par Aisa Na Waqt Aaye.
Nar Mehramo Mein Koi Youn Be Rida Na Jaye.
Karte Hain Yeh Duaein Ahl-e-Haram Ke Bachey Jholi Utha Utha Ke.

2
Bhai Behan Ki Aankhien Nam Hoti Ja Rahi Hain.
Bint-e-Ali Ki Sansein Kam Hoti Ja Rahi Hain.
Khanjar Chala Rhaa Hai Zainab Ke Dil Pe Zalim Khanjar Dikha Dikha Ke.
Ik Bachi Keh Rahi Hai Aao Madad Ko Baba.
Dadi Ki Tarha Main Bhi Ab Ho Gayi Zaeefa.
Zakhmi Kiya Hai Pehlu Naqay Se Zalimo Ne Mujh Ko Gira Gira Ke.

3
Parday Mein Bhi Jo Bibi Chalti Na Thi Zyda.
Pardais Mein Ab Un Par Aaya Hai Waqt Kaisa.
Laye Hain Sham Walay Un Shah Zadiyon Ko Paidal Chala Chala Ke.
Woh Shamiyon Ke Tanay Woh Muskuratay Chehray.
Be Parda Sath Apne Bint-e-Ali Ko Le Ke.
Sajjad Kaise Bholein Oss Bheer Se Guzarna Nazrein Jhuka Jhuka Ke.

4
Jate Hain Jis Taraf Bhi Ahl-e-Haram Khule Sar.
Khati Hain Pehle Durra Khati Hain Pehle Pathar.
Apne Lahu Mein Tar Hain Zahra Ki Betiyon Ko Fizza Bacha Bacha Ke.
Zakhmo Ki Woh Takallum Tadad Jante Hain.
Mehdi Ko Bhi Khabar Hai Sajjad Jante Hain.
Maray Hain Kis Ne Kitne Haider Ki Ladli Ko Pathar Utha Utha Ke.
