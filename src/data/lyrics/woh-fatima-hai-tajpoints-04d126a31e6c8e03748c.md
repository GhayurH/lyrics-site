1
Woh Jiski Tauseef Hal Atta Hai Woh Fatima Hai.
Jo Fakhar-e-Mariyam Hai Tahira Hai Woh Fatima Hai.
Khuda Ne Awwal Jisay Banaya.
Woh Jis Ki Khatir Jahan Sajaya.
Wohi Jo Nabiyon Ki Bhi Dua Hai Woh Fatima Hai.
Woh Jis Ke Dar Ke Gada Malak Hain.
Woh Jis Ke Mamnoo Sada Malak Hain.
Wohi Jo Tafseer-e-Innama Hai Woh Fatima Hai.
Quran Jis Ki Sana Se Pur Hai.
Woh Jiski Chokhat Banati Hur Hai.
Jo Nuqta Hai Khat Hai Dairah Hai Woh Fatima Hai.
Fazeelatein Oss Ki Kya Biyaan Hoon.
Keh Jis Ke Sadqay Mein Do Jahan Hoon.
Nabi Ki Beti Hai Marziya Hai Woh Fatima Hai.
Wohi Jo Zarron Ko Shams Kar De.
Talab Se Pehle Hi Jholi Bhar De.
Woh Jis Ne Fikar-o-Hunar Diya Hai Woh Fatima Hai.
