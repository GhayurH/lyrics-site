Roway Na Door Watan Tu Shala Bemar Koi Hasan Sadiq.

1
Waang Sajjad Na Koi Beemar Wekhay Phophiyaan Wich Bazar.
Howay Na Door Watan Taun Shala Beemar Koi.
Nangay Sir Wekhay Na Phophiyaan Bharay Bazar Koi.
Dais Apne Che Na Pardesi Koi V Howay.
Dekh Bharjaiyaan Koon Sir Nangay Koi Na Roway.
Jaway Har Moor Te Rukdi Na Parday Daar Koi.

2
Wekhay Na Putar De Gal Tauq Zulm De Painday.
Naizay Taun Wekhay Na Bachri De Koi Dur Lehnday.
Shala Gash Khaway Sina Te Na Bar Bar Koi.
Sham Wich Poor Jiwein Le Ke Hai Beemar Gaya.
Wekh Ke Bheer Bazari Koon Jiwein Ronda Rehya.
Roway Na Khon De Athro Aynj Salar Koi.

3
Koi Mastoor Kadi Shala Na Paway Kariyaan.
Wekh Darbar De Wich Bhainra Te Phophiyaan Khariyaan.
Howay Sajjad De Wangoon Na Be Qarrar Koi.
Tera Khursheed Karey Maula Aye Ro Ro Ke Dua.
Gham Kisay Nu Na Miley Koi Tere Gham De Siwa.
Roway Gham Tere De Bajoon Na Azadar Koi.
