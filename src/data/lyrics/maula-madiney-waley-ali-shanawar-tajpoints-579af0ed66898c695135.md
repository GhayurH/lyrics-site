Maula Madiney Waley Ali Shanawar Lyrics
Jin Ka Nana Nabi Jin Ka Baba Ali.

1
Jin Ki Maa Fatima Jin Ka Bhai Hasan.
Woh Hain Maula Hamarey Hussain Maula Madine Walay.
Karbal Mein Aa Gaye Hain Karbal Basaney Walay Maula Madine Walay.
Khaimay Saja Rahe Hain Wadah Nibhanay Walay Maula Madine Walay.

2
Yeh Dar Woh Pak Dar Hai Jo Fatima Ka Ghar Hai.
Yeh Pyaray Mustafa Ka Maula Ali Ka Ghar Hai.
Maqtal Mein Aa Gaye Hain Quraan Sunanay Walay Maula Madine Walay.
Islam Ne Pukara Quran Ne Sada Di.

3
Yasrab Ka Shahzada Yasrab Ki Shahzadi.
Nikley Safar Ko Ghar Se Kalma Bachaney Walay Maula Madine Walay.
Ro Ro Ke Boli Sughra Dil Dobta Hai Mera.
Veraan Kiya Madina Jangal Mein Hai Basera.

4
Mujh Ko Rula Gaye Kyun Khushiyaan Lotaney Walay Maula Madine Walay.
Damad Ke Dularay Beti Ke Shah Zaday.
They Teen Din Ke Pyasay Sab Phool Mustafa Ke.
Qatrey Ko Hain Yehi Tu Darya Bananey Walay Maula Madine Walay.

5
Sab Lut Gayi Kamai Akbar Ne Barchi Khayi.
Asghar Ne Teer Khaya Deti Thi Maa Duhai.
Teeron Ke Saye Mein Hain Sajday Mein Jane Walay Maula Madine Walay.
Hal Min Ka Istegasa Jab De Chuka Woh Pyasa.

6
Aawaz-e-Irjai Ne Aa Kar Diya Dilasa.
Aa Mere Pas Aa Ja Lashay Uthane Walay Maula Madine Walay.
Ik Shoor Tha Falak Par Kehta Tha Rab-e-Akbar.
Jibreel Badilon Ka Jaldi Bichao Bistar.

7
Janat Mein Aa Rahe Hain Quraan Bachaney Walay Maula Madine Walay.
 Salman Our Shanawar Khush Bakht Hoon:ALI JEE:.
 Rehan Ki Tamana Sarwar Ki Jo Dua Thi.
Khidmat Qabool Kar Lein Qismat Bananey Walay Maula Madine Walay.
