1
Maiyyat Pe Zainab Ne Kaha Baba Abhi Mat Jaiye.
Aankhein Tu Apni Kholiye Khamosh Kyun Hain Boliye.
Kya Ho Gaye Hum Se Khafa Baba Abhi Mat Jaiye.
Maiyyat Uthi Gar Aap Ki Baba Yahin Mar Jaon Gi.
Mujh Mein Nahi Ab Hosla Baba Abhi Mat Jaiye.
EID Aa Rahi Hai Baba Jaan Kaise Rahein Gi Betiyaan.
Veeran Ghar Ho Jaye Ga Baba Abhi Mat Jaiye.
Kyun Mere Bazu Choom Ke Aise Tarap Kar Roye The.
Batlaein Kay Yaad Aa Gaya Baba Abhi Mat Jaiye.
Bazar Kaise Jaon Gi Darbar Kaise Jaon Gi.
Main Seekh Loon Chalna Zara Baba Abhi Mat Jaiye.
Jitne Bhi Hain Kofi Khare Pathar Na Marein Yeh Mujhe.
Keh Dijiye In Se Zara Baba Abhi Mat Jaiye.
Aata Nahi Hai Oss Ko Chain Bhola Nahi Maa Ko Hussain.
Mar Jaye Ga Bhai Mera Baba Abhi Mat Jaiye.
Ghabraein Gi Amma Meri Ghusal-o-Kafan Ke Bad Bhi.
Khoon Beh Raha Hai Aap Ka Baba Abhi Mat Jaiye.
Parda Chine Ga Jab Mera Aaein Gaye Milne Karbala.
Yeh Wada Kar Lijiye Zara Baba Abhi Mat Jaiye.
 Sajjad Kaisi Thi Ghari Roti Thi Khud Bint-e-Ali.
Jis Dam Yateemo Ne Kaha Baba Abhi Mat Jaiye.
