Barsaat may teeron ki ye kiska janaza hai

1
Batlao musalmano ye kiska nawasa hai
Aey laal karo matam aankhon ko bandh karke
Lipti hui mayyat se ye fatema zehra hai

2
Hasnain tere gham may jo aansoo bahayega
Jayega wo jannat may zehra ka ye wada hai

3
Aey qalm bata tere kyun aansoo nikal aaye
Kya tune yateemo may qasim ko bhi dekha hai
