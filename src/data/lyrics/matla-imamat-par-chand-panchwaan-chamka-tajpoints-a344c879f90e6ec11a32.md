Matla Imamat Par Chand Panchwaan Chamka.
Ho Gayi Zameen Roshan Our Aasmaan Chamka.

1
Baap Abid-o-Sajid Roh-e-Bandagi Dada.
Maa Ki Tarbiyaat Ka Bhi Naqsh Zofshaan Chamka.
Kuniyat Abu Jafar Our Laqab Mila Baqir.
Ilm Inn Ka Dunya Mein Ban Ke Kehkashan Chamka.

2
Ilm Ki Farawani Fikar Ki Jahan Bani.
Ilm Inn Ki Hikmat Se Ho Ke Be Khizaan Chamka.
Kamsini Ke Aalam Mein Karbala Mein Kofa Mein.
Qalb Inn Ka Har Lamhey Ho Ke Khonchkaan Chamka.

3
Baqir Aloom Inn Ko Iss Liye Bhi Kehte Hain.
Inn Ke Baad Dunya Mein Jehal Phir Kahan Chamka.
Sorat-e-Ali Yeh Bhi Mushkilon Mein Kam Aaye.
Sailon Ki Qismat Ka Door Rayegaan Chamka.

4
Sadqay Inn Ke Waday Par Our Wafa-e-Wadah Par.
Manqabat Yahan Likhi Khuld Mein Makaan Chamka.
Faiz Panjtan Ka Hai Aye Rehan Yeh Tujh Par.
Shayari Ne Di Izzat Ban Ke Madah Khuwaan Chamka
