Dhop Mein Bethi Hoi Maa Yeh Sada Deti Hai.
Maut Aulad Ki Be-Haal Bana Deti Hai.

1
Yeh Hawa Hai Jo Samajhti Hai Tamana Maa Ki.
Khali Jhola Ali Asghar Ka Jhula Deti Hai.
Shimar Aata Hai Tu Zindaan Mein Baho Haider Ki.
Khon Mein Doba Howa Kurta Chupa Deti Hai.

2
Teer Se Lipte Hoye Hanste Hain Ran Mein Asghar.
Our Maa Khaimo Mein Jeene Ki Dua Deti Hai.
Khak Maqtal Ki Sada Deti Thi Aao Asghar.
Aaj Madar Ki Jagah Khak Sula Deti Hai.

3
Yaad Maa Ki Jo Satati Hai Ali Asghar Ki.
Aao Ik Bharti Hai Our Khak Urra Deti Hai.
Umar Bhar Jagti Rehti Hain Woh Maein Akbar.
Jin Ke Bachoon Ko Qaza Ran Mein Sula Deti Hai.
