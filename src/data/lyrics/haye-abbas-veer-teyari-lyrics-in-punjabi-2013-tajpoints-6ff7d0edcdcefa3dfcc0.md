1
Haye Abbas Di Bhain Pukardi Aye Ho Wal Watna Te Tur Waisan Shabir De Pardadar Aye.
Oh Maikon Nayi Bhulda Veran Koon Hait Khanjar Piya Kusda Shimar Laeen Aye.
Khair Howay Veran Di Hoi Khaimay Andar Pukar Aye.
Pardah Koon Bachaisi Sada Veer Abbas Tu Dair Na Laween.
Naal Waris Zahra Jayi Hayi Zainab Parda Daar Aye.
Kar Dua Toan Zahra Jayi Haye Sada Abbas Di Aayi.
Shala Bach Bazu Jawan Teri Chadar Koon Utaray.
Darya Di Haye Taraf Rawana Cha Ke Mashak Te Alam Abbas Aye.
Chote Chote Balaan Koon Hunr De Si Kon Sahare.
Janrdi Haan Main Sab Kuj Ghazi Tu Khaliq Koon Razi Karday.
Main Janra Abid Janre Te Janre Sham Bazar Aye.
Qadma Te Hath Rakh Ke Ghazi Aakhiya Haye Sain Dukhi Koon.
Mere Bad Salar Hosi Tere Qafilay Da Bemar Aye.
Mangdi Aye Humsheer Duawaan Teri Shala Pori Utray.
Fikar Maikon Noshad Eho Kiwein Waisaan Wich Darbar Aye.
