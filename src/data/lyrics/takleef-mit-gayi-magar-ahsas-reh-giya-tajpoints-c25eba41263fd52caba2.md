1
Khush Hoon Ke Kuch Na Kuch Tu Mere Pass Reh Giya.
Pal Bhar Mein Oss Ki Shakal Na Ayi Agar Nazar.
Yak Dam Ulajh Ke Rishta-e-Anfaas Reh Giya.
Photo Mein Dill Ki Choot Na Tabdeel Ho Saki.
Naqlein Utar Utar Aqaas Reh Giya.
Woh Jhote Motiyon Ki Chamak Par Phisal Gayi.
Main Hath Mein Liye Howay Almas Reh Giya.
Ik Hum Safar Ko Kho Ke Yeh Halat Hoi Adam.
Jangal Mein Jiss Tarha Koi Be Aas Reh Giya.
