Ya Aba Abdillah Rohi Wa Jismi Lakal Fida Mir Hasan Mir.
Har Lasha Deta Jata Hai Maqtal Se Yeh Sada.

1
Hum Ne Hai Kiya Aap Ki Madar Se Yeh Wadah.
Majlis Na Rukay Gi Kabhi Matam Na Rukay Ga.
Kat Jaein Chahe Hath Yeh Kat Jaye Yeh Gala.
Dartay Hain Kahan Maut Se Hum Ahl-e-Shahadat.

2
Hai Jan Se Barh Kar Tere Rozay Ki Ziyarat.
Kehte Hoye Yeh Qafilay Jate Hain Karbala.
Zalim Na Bana Dein Kahin Ik Our Baqaiya.
Phir Maula Wohi Sham Hai Our Aap Ka Kunba.

3
Izn-e-Jehad Kijiye Mehdi Ko Ab Atta.
Hal Min Jo Shabeer Ne Maqtal Mein Sada Di.
Ashab Tarapney Lagay Tanhai Pe Sheh Ki.
Uth Uth Ke Deta Jata Tha Har Lasha Yeh Sada.

4
Sajjad Ne Baba Ki Jo Turbat Ko Banaya.
Shabeer Ke Seene Pe Jo Asghar Ko Sulaya.
Lashay Se Keh Ke Dosra Lasha Lipat Gaya.
Darbar Na Jaon Gi Main Kehti Thi Yeh Khwahar.

5
Rotay Hoye Shabeer Ko Dekha Jo Sina Par.
Labaik Keh Ke Chal Pari Darbar Syeda.
Jis Waqt Ke Zindan Mein Pyason Ke Sar Aaye.
Zainab Ne Wahan Aoun-o-Muhammad Ke Bajaye.

6
Bhai Ke Sar Ko Gaud Mein Letey Hoye Kaha.
Aankhon Mein Takallum Hai Woh Ashor Ka Manzar.
Ghaibat Ko Azza Khana-e-Shabeer Bana Kar.
Paihum Imam-e-Asar Ke Honton Pe Hai Sada.
