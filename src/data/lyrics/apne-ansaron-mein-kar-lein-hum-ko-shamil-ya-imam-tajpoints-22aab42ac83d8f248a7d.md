Apne Ansaron Mein Kar Lein Hum Ko Shamil Ya Imam.
Aap Ki Khak-e-Qadam Hai Apni Manzil Ya Imam.

1
Aap Kab Aaein Gaye Koi Tu Ishara Dijiye.
Ik Musayab Ka Samandur Hai Kinara Dijiye.
Dobne Wali Hai Yeh Kashti Sahra Dijiye.
Zindagi Tofan Hai Aap Sahil Ya Imam.

2
Parda-e-Ghaibat Ke Bare Mein Kahein Hum Our Kya.
Jo Shab-e-Mairaj Pardah Tha Sar-e-Arsh-e-Ula.
Our Jis Parde Se Dast-e-Kibriya Zahir Howa.
Hai Wohi Parda Yaqeenan Teri Mehmil Ya Imam.

3
Jab Nida Ho Gi Abu Talib Ka Pota Aa Giya.
Koi Parhta Ho Ga Unn Ko Dekh Kar Sal-e-Alla.
Aap Ko Haider Samaj Kar Koi Keh De Ga Khuda.
Koi Taha Bole Ga Koi Muzamil Ya Imam.

4
Aap Ke Humrah Sab Shia Azadari Karein.
Aap Khud Aa Kar Jalusoon Mein Alamdari Karein.
Our Yeh Mufti Na Bidat Ki Sanad Jari Karein.
Matami Ko Ho Teri Taeed Hasil Ya Imam.

5
Aap Ka Aana Hai Taroon Par Damak Ki Wapisi.
Chahne Walon Ke Chehroon Par Chamak Ki Wapisi.
Aap Ke Aane Hi Par Ho Gi Fidak Ki Wapisi.
Aap Kar Sakte Hain Har Ik Gham Ko Zayal Ya Imam.

6
Unn Ki Midhat Ka Sila Saqlain Hum Youn Paein Gaye.
Door-e-Mehdi Mein Jo Mailadoon Ke Moqay Aaein Gaye.
Jitne Madah-e-Ali Hain Sab Bulaye Jaein Gaye.
Aap Ki Mojodgi Mein Ho Gi Mehfil Ya Imam.
