1
Har Ranj Saha Sadma Bhi Kya Kya Na Uthaya Hasan Sadiq.
Bazar Bhi Halaat Ne Zainab Ko Dikhaya.
Pathar Tu Barastay They Magar Aag Bhi Barsi.
Jab Qafila Abid Ka Bazar Mein Aaya.

2
Har Zulm Pe Sajjad Ki Aankhon Tu Hain Barsein.
Par Sham Ke Bazar Ne Kuch Zyada Rulaya.
Kuch Ranj Tu Makhdoma Ko Wirsa Mein Miley They.
Par Waqt Ki Gardish Ne Ossay Bohat Sataya.

3
Zainab Ke Muqadar Pe Hai Taqdeer Bhi Hairaan.
Zindagi Mein Kissi Lamha Bhi Haye Chain Na Paya.
 Ameer Bacha Dein Ko Tu Shabeer Gaye They.
Shabeer Ko Har Haal Mein Zainab Ne Bachaya.
