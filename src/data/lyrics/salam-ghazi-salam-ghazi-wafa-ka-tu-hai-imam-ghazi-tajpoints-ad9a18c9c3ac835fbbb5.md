1
Salam Ghazi Salam Ghazi Wafa Ka Tu Hai Imam Ghazi.
Jagah Jagah Hai Alam Gawahi Dilon Pe Tei Hai Badshahi.
Laboon Pe Tera Hi Naam Ghazi Wafa Ka Tu Hai Imam Ghazi.
Tere Alam Ka Hai Hum Pe Saya Alam Pe Saya Hai Panjtan Ka.
Buland Tera Muqam Ghazi Wafa Ka Tu Hai Imam Ghazi.
Alam Ka Jab Se Howa Hai Saya Zameen-e-Karb-o-Bala Pe Mula.
Tera Hai Chalta Nizam Ghazi Wafa Ka Tu Hai Imam Ghazi.
Tu Bawafa Hai La-Fata Hai Be-Aasron Ka Tu Aasra Hai.
Jahan Mein Charcha Hai Aam Ghazi Wafa Ka Tu Hai Imam Ghazi.
Hussain Mula Se Pyar Karna Hussain Par Jaan Nisar Karna.
Yehi Hai Tera Pyam Ghazi Wafa Ka Tu Hai Imam Ghazi.
Jo Tere Bazo Qalam Na Hote Aseer Ahl-e-Haram Na Hote.
Na Jati Zainab Youn Sham Ghazi Wafa Ka Tu Hai Imam Ghazi.
Ghulam Sharminda Hai Yeh Mula Na Lasha Khaimo Mein Ke Le Jana.
Tha Lab Pe Tere Kalam Ghazi Wafa Ka Tu Hai Imam Ghazi.
 Jawad-O- Sarwar Jab Aaye Sarwar Bureedah Bazo Tha Ibn-e-Haider.
Tu Kaise Karta Salam Ghazi Wafa Ka Tu Hai Imam Ghazi.
