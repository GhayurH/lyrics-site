1
Dunya Mein Aaj Phaila Howa Inteshar Hai.
Halaat Keh Rahe Hain Had-e-Intezar Hai.
Yeh Hum Ne Kab Kaha Keh Humein Jang Se Pyar Hai.
Pardah Utha Ke Ghaib Se Aaein Bari Hai Aas.
Lein Adal Ki Fiza Mein Kabhi Chain Ki Tu Sans.
Iss Zindagi Ka Mula Kahan Aytbar Hai.
Kar Dein Agar Zahoor Qayamat Ki Ho Ghari.
Har Door Ke Yazeed Ko Lag Jaye Kap Kapi.
Pora Hisab Ho Jo Abhi Tak Udhar Hai.
Pani Pe Aa Ke Mula Musalla Bichaiye.
Isa Parhein Namaz Imamat Karaiye.
Sahil Se Moj Kehti Yehi Bar Bar Hai.
Jo Bani-e-Sitam Hai Na Koi Bhi Bach Sake.
Kis Mein Hai Dam Ajal Ka Jo Ab Samna Kare.
Na Charhne Ko Pahar Na Chupne Ko Gaar Hai.
Zahra Ka Lal Ghaib Se Aaye Tu Dekhna.
Qabron Se Woh Nikal Ke Kahin Gaye Yeh Dekhna.
Yeh Tu Wohi Hai Hath Wohi Zulfiqar Hai.
Likh Kar Areeza Yaad Bhi Karte Hain Subho Sham.
Majlis Ka Manqabat Ka Bhi Karte Hain Ayehtamam.
Zikar-e-Zakat-o-Khums Hi Zehno Pe Baar Hai.
Shorash Kahin Fasad Kahin Fitna Saziyaan.
Apno Ke Darmiyan Yeh Hoti Laraiyaan.
Tehzeeb Kya Yehi Hai Yehi Kya Shaar Hai.
Majlis Mein Koi Jaye Ga Masjid Mein Koi Our.
Matam Koi Kare Ga Tu Aamal Koi Our.
Aal-e-Nabi Ne Kya Yeh Kaha Aik Baar Hai.
Mula Kisi Tarha Se Humein Aik Kijiye.
Likha Areezay Mein Hai Yehi Dekh Lijiye.
Mula Dilon Ko Joor Do Sab Ki Pukar Hai.
 Rizvi Bas Aik Dard Ka Darya Tha Beh Gaya.
Ashkoon Ne Gir Ke Joan Areezay Pe Likh Diya.
Woh Keh Gaye Jo Haal-e-Dil-e-Be-Qarar Hai.
