Shaheed e karbala ka mazloom e karbala ka

farzand e mustufa ka

matam rahenga husain ka

jaari rahega ye hukm e khuda se

ghar ghar may bapa hai zainab ki ata se

rukne nahi denge matam ka silsila

waada hai hamara ye sher e khuda se

shaheed e karbala…

bhoolenge nahi ham ta hashr sheh ka gham

ye noha padhenge jab tak hai dam may dam

aankhon may rahega barsaat ka mausam

gham ho ya khushi ho bas hoga tera gham

shaheed e karbala…

matam hai qayadat matam hai riyazat

hum ahle aza ki matam hai zaroorat

noha hi azaan hai noha hi aqaamat

sajda bhi ibadat matam bhi ibadat

shaheed e karbala…

mansab ye bada hai humko jo mila hai

matam ke liye jo zehra ne chuna hai

taqleeq hamay jo khaaliq ne kiya hai

taqleeq ka maqsad matam hi rakha hai

shaheed e karbala…

matam hi wafa hai matam hi baqa hai

matam ka musalla ye farshe aza hai

tareekh jo kholo us may ye likha hai

ye matam o noha nabiyon ne kiya hai

shaheed e karbala…

tatheer ka matam hamsheer ka matam

qurane aza ki tafseer ka matam

sajjad ke gham may zanjeer ka matam

har haal may karna dilgeer ka matam

shaheed e karbala…

farhan se mazhar ye kehta hai ro kar

yeh matam o noha hai karz lahoo par

tehreer hamari awaaz tumhari

haa zinda rakhenge matam ko zameen par

shaheed e karbala…
