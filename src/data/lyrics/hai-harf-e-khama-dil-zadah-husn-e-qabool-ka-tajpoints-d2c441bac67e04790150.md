Hai Harf-e-Khama Dil Zadah Husn-e-Qabool Ka
Yani Khiyal Sar Mein Hai Naat-e-Rasool Ka

1
Reh Pairvi Mein Oss Ki Keh Gaam-e-Nakhist Mein
Zahir Asar Hai Maqsad-e-Dil Ke Wasool Ka
Wo Muqtad-e-Khalq-e-Jahan Aab Nahi Howa
Pehle Hi Tha Imam Nafoos-o-Aqool Ka

2
Surma Kya Hai Waza Pe Chashm-e-Ahl-e-Qudas
Ahmad Ki Rahguzar Ki Khak Our Dhool Ka
Hai Muthid Nabi-o-Ali-o-Wasi Ki Zaat
Yaan Harf Moatbar Nahi Har Boalfazool Ka

3
Dho Munh Hazar Pani Se Soo Baar Parh Darood
Tab Naam Le Tu Oss Chamanstaan Ke Phool Ka
Hasil Hai Mir Dosti Ahl-e-Bait Agar
Tu Gham Kya Nijaat Ke Apne Hasool Ka
