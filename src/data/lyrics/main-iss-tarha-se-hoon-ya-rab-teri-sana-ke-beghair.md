Main Iss Tarha Se Hoon Ya Rab Teri Sana Ke Beghair
Ke Jaise Jee Nahi Sakta Koi Hawa Ke Beghair

1
Na Mujh Se Naat Ka Ik Lafz Bhi Raqam Hota
Khuda-e-Aal-e-Muhammad Teri Atta Ke Beghair
Qasam Khuda Ki Adhori Kitab Lagti Thi
Biyaz-e-Shair Mujhe Naat-e-Mustafa Ke Beghair

2
Na Meri Hamd Mukamil Hai Mustafa Ke Beghair
Na Meri Naat Mukamil Hai Murtaza Ke Beghair
Wasila Aal-e-Nabi Ka Na Ho Tu Rehte Hain
Dua Asar Ke Beghair Our Asar Dua Ke Beghair

3
Faqeer-e-Shah-e-Najaf Hoon Koi Mazzaq Nahi
Hamesha Milta Hai Mujh Ko Meri Sada Ke Beghair
Sakoon Milta Nahi Mustafa Ko Our Kahin
Aye Shah Zadi-e-Alam Teri Rida Ke Beghair

4
Daleel Koi Nahi Thi Khuda Ke Hone Ki
Ali-o-Fatima Hasnain-o-Mustafa Ke Beghair
Saza Ke Baad Jaza Hai Agar Tu Aye Waiz
Tu Kaise Mil Gayi Hur Ko Jaza Saza Ke Beghair

5
Ali,Rasool, Khuda Asal Mein Hai Yeh Tarteeb
Tum Inteha Ko Na Pohancho Gay Ibtida Ke Beghair
Koi Bhi Ho Razi Allah Ho Nahi Sakta
Aye Bint-e-Rehmat-e-Alam Teri Riza Ke Beghair
