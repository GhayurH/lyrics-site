Ay Dilbaran-e-Jafar-e-Tayyar Alwida, Alwida, Alwida, Alwida

Binte Ali Ye Bachon Say Kehti Thi Baar Baar (x3)

Ran Ko Sidharo Aun-o-Muhammad Ye Maa Nisar

Jafar Ka Ifteqar Bharana Jihad May (x3)

Nana Ki Apni Shan Dikhana Jihad May.

Shair-e-Khuda Ki Beti Kay Tum Dono Shair Ho (x3)

Maidan May Jawo Khud Fatha Ab Kuch Na Dair Ho.

Haar Baat Apne Dil Kay Siwa Ho To Baat Hai (x3)

Woh Ran Paray Kay Hashr Bapa Ho To Baat Hai.

Ghayre Rahay Sipah Magar Mou Na Moor Na (x3)

Haider Ki Yaadgar Ho Maidan Na Chorna.

Shimro Umar Kay Saar Jo Utaro To Khush Hu May(x3)

Dushman Ko Jhoom Jhoom Kay Maaro To Khush Hu Mai.

Ab Toh Ye Arzoo Hai Kay Tum Ran May Kaam Aawo (x3)

Chehray Kay Badle Khoon Bhare Chehre Mujhe Dikhao.

Bacho Zaeef Mamu Ko Zehmat Na Deejiyo (x3)

Lashay Uthaaye Bhai To Jumbish Na Kijiyo.

Pyaase Jo Aaoge To Sila Degi Tum Ko Maa (x3)

Khud Daud Kar Gallay Say Laga Legi Maa.
