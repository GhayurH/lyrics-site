1
Koi Samaj Nahi Sakta Hussain Ka Rutba.
Hai Anbiya Se Bhi Aala Hussain Ka Rutba.
Tamam Nabiyon Ki Aqalein Bhi Gar Khuda De De.
Azal Se Taba Abad Maut Bhi Na Aaye Mujhe.
Main Phir Bhi Likh Na Sakon Ga Hussain Ka Rutba.
They Ikhteyar-e-Muhammad Mein Looh Our Qalam.
Pisar Naseeb Mein Rahib Ke Khud Kiye Na Raqam.
Samajh Le Ta Keh Zamana Hussain Ka Rutba.
Ali Ne Beti Se Ki Ho Gi Aa Ke Sargoshi.
Kaha Hai Bhai Ko Tere Yazeed Ne Baghi.
Bata De Sani-e-Zahra Hussain Ka Rutba.
Hai Lafziyaat Ki Yeh Kaisi Kaisi Majbori.
Tamam Aayatein Tehreer Ho Gayin Phir Bhi.
Abhi Bhi Baqi Hai Likhna Hussain Ka Rutba.
Hussain Chahein Tu Hota Hia Murdah Khud Zinda.
Bohat Se Log Nahi Mantay Yeh Baat Zara.
Habib Uth Ke Batana Hussain Ka Rutba.
Har Aik Soray Ka Main Tarjuma Batata Gaya.
Ossay Main Hamd Se Wannas Tak Sunata Gaya.
Kissi Ne Poch Liya Tha Hussain Ka Rutba.
Namazi Hanp Rahe The Ajeeb Halat Thi.
Taveel Sajday Se Sar Ko Utha Ke Bole Nabi.
Samajh Tu Aa Gaya Hoga Hussain Ka Rutba.
Kaha Yeh Rab Ne Yaqin Mein Badal Na Jaye Gumaan.
Khuda Na Lagne Lagay Yeh Tumhein Aye Ahl-e-Jahan.
Sanbhal Sanbhal Ke Samjhna Hussain Ka Rutba.
Kissi Ne Imla Likhaya Hai Tu Ne Likha Hai.
Tere Qalam Pe Takallum Atay-e-Zahra Hai.
Wagarna Kon Likhe Ga Hussain Ka Rutba.
