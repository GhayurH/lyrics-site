1
Kya Yehi Islam Tha Phaila Howa Bad e Nabi.
De Ke Sam Mara Gaya Apne Hi Ghar Sibt e Nabi.
Aye Khuda Dunya Mein Teri Zulm Dekhey Hain Bohat.
Maiyyaton Pe Teer Chalte Tu Nahi Dekhey Kabhi.

2
Lash-e-Baiyat Sar Kati Be Jaan Rahe Gi Ta Abad.
Iss Ki Gardan Hai Meri Tehreer Se Kati Hoi.
Teri Ummat Aye Nabi Kyun Itni Zalim Ho Gayi.
Ghar Se Nikli Aik Maiyyat Ghar Ko Wapis Aa Gayi.

3
Takht Ko Taraaj Pal Mein Kar Gayi Sulh-e-Hasan.
Thi Qalam Ke Zoor Par Yeh Bhi Fatha Islam Ki.
Tasht Mein Ajar-e-Risalat Ko Ugaltay Hain Imam.
Ghar Mein Ahl-e-Bait Ke Hai Phir Saf-e-Matam Bichi.

4
Har Tarha Ka Zulm Dha Kar Kaisay Hai Tu Mutmain.
Jis Ka Kalma Parh Rahe Ho Oss Ki Yeh Aoulad Thi.
Gar Nahi Sajid Basai Dil Mein Ik Qabar-e-Hasan.
Yeh Hai Teri Khud Numai Yeh Tera Matam Nahi.
