Dar Jal Chuka Hai Yousuf e Zahra Kab Aao Gay.

1
Zahra Sadaein Deti Hain Maula Kab Aao Gay.
Zakhmi Hain Fatima Tooti Hain Pasliyaan Ya Sahib Alzamaan.
De Kar Sadaein Aap Ko Leti Hain Hichkiyaan Ya Sahib Alzamaan.
Pehlu Shikasta Ho Gaya Jaldi Sanbhal Lo.
Bint-e-Nabi Ko Aag Se Aa Kar Nikal Lo.
Maula Tumhari Dadi Hain Sholon Ke Darmiyaan.

2
Dekha Tu Ho Ga Zulm Nigah-e-Imam Se.
Beton Ke Samne Kabhi Logon Ke Samne.
Khaye Nabi Ki Beti Ne Durray Kahan Kahan.
Afzal Jo Abu Turab Hain Kul Kainat Se.
Khaybar Ko Fatha Kis Ne Kiya Apne Hath Se.
Woh Shair-e-Kibriya Kahan Our Rasiyaan Kahan.

3
Madar Ka Chehra Takti Hain Hasrat Se Bar Bar.
Zainab Ke Sath Sath Hain Kulsoom Be Qarrar.
Chehray Pe Neel Dekh Ke Roti Hain Betiyaan.
Ik Zalzala Sa Tari Tha Qabar-e-Rasool Par.
Zalim Ne Aisay Hath Uthaya Batool Par.
Bikhrein Zameen Pe Toot Ke Zahra Ki Baliyaan.

4
Kis Din Zahoor Pao Gay Aye Ibn-e-Fatima.
Batla Rahi Hai Zahra Ki Yeh Zair-e-Dar Sada.
Yasrab Mein Tum Ne Dekhi Hai Zakhmo Se Choor Maa.
Aye Waris-e-Rasool Qayamat Phir Aa Gayi.
Ghaibat Mein Tum Ho Our Shahadat Phir Aa Gayi.
Aa Kar Suna Tu Do Humein Ghurbat Ki Dastaan.

5
Har Dushman-e-Batool Ko Tukron Mein Bant Do.
Tum Ko Qasam Hussain Ki Woh Hath Kaat Do.
Jis Hath Ka Hai Bibi Ke Rukhsar Par Nishaan.
Ayyam-e-Pursa Dari e Zahra Ka Wasta.
Kartay Hain Qum Mein:MIR TAKALLUM: Yeh Sab Dua.
Matam Karey Baqaiya Mein Har Matami Jawaan.
