1
Khuda Ka Banda Nuseriyon Ka Bana Khuda Hai Woh Murtaza Hai.
Jo Mushkilon Mein Madad Zamane Ki Kar Raha Hai Woh Murtaza Hai.
Nabi Ka Naib Issay Bana Kar Khuda Ne Bhaija Hai Dekho Haider
Buttoon Ko Aa Khuda Ghar Se Bhaga Raha Hai Woh Murtaza Hai.

2
Ho Badar Ya Keh Ho Jang-e-Khaybar Nabi Ne Manga Khuda Se Haider
Nabi Dushman Wastay Jo Qehar-e-Khuda Hai Woh Murtaza Hai.
Ghadeer Mein Jo Kaha Nabi Ne Ossay Na Hargiz Bholana Logo
Keh Baad Apne Woh Jis Ko Maula Bana Raha Hai Woh Murtaza Hai.

3
Bano Na Hargiz Ali Humsar Jis Ka Bana Hai Yeh Sag Qalandar
Zar Ko Jis Ne Pal Mein Abuzar Bana Diya Hai Woh Murtaza Hai.
Palat Soraj Howa Tha Wapis Isharay Bas Ik Kiya Ali Ne
Qaseeda Farman Jis Ka Tum Ko Suna Raha Hai Woh Murtaza Hai.
