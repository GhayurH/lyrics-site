Binte Zahra Ka Khula Sar Hai Khuda Khair Kare
Aur Har Haath Mein Pathar Hai Khuda Khair Kare

۱
Hur Ke Bete Pe Hi Shabbir Bahut Roe The
Ab To Laash-e-Ali Akbar Hai Khuda Khair Kare

۲
Maarte Hain Wahan Naizon Ko Zameen Par Zalim
Dafn Jis Ja Ali Asghar Hai Khuda Khair Kare

۳
Kuch Qadam Door Khari Dekh Rahi Hai Zainab
Shimr Ke Haath Mein Khanjar Hai Khuda Khair Kare

۴
Chaunk Kar Khwaab Se Kehti Thi Watan Mein Sughra
Kaisi Mushkil Mein Mera Ghar Hai Khuda Khair Kare

۵
Peechhe Deewar Hai Aur Jalta Hua Dar Aage
Beech Mein Binte Payambar Hai Khuda Khair Kare

۶
Berida Jaana Hai Zainab Ko Wahan Par Ke Jahan
Har Koi Dushman-e-Haider Hai Khuda Khair Kare

۷
Hai Chhari Haath Mein Zalim Ke Labon Par Hai Hansi
Tasht Mein Sheh Ka Rakha Sar Hai Khuda Khair Kare

۸
Mir Sajjad Ye Shirin Ne Kaha Rote Hue
Kis Ki Naize Pe Ye Chaadar Hai Khuda Khair Kare
