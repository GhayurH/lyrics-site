Sajday Wich Gaye Haider Ghulam Ali Party.

1
Sajday Wich Gaye Haider Hoya Surkh C Musalla.
Zakhmi Ali Nu Tak Ke Roya Arsh-e-Mualla.
Yateem Ho Gayi Aye Aoulad Aj Ali Di.
Hun Aal-e-Mustafa Aye Tere Hawalay ALLAH.
Hoiyaan Surkh Fizawaan Fizza Ne Wain Kita.
Aj Wasda Ujriya Aye Syedaan Da Ik Muhalla.

2
Teraan Te Hona Tera Sajdah Hussain Mere.
Katna Aye Taun Karbala Wich Ummat De Hathoon Kalla.
Chuk Ke Janaza Tur Paye Shabar Shabeer Ghazi.
Zainab Da Baba Deen Tu Qurban Ho Ke Chala.
Hona Nayin Dar Ona Nu Adeel Koi Hashar Da.
Jinna De Hath Che Hona Maula Ali Da Palla.
