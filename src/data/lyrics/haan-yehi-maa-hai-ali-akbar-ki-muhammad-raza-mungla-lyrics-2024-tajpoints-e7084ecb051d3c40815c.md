Haan Yehi Maa Hai Ali Akbar Ki Muhammad Raza Mungla.

1
Jis Ne Rakha Hai Hath Seene Par.

2
Ro Ke Kehti Hai Haye Ali Akbar.

3
Sun Ke Aawaz Azaan Our Tarap Jati Hai.
Yaad Hum Shakal-e-Payamber Ki Ossay Aati Hai.
Ro Rahi Hai Azan Sun Sun Kar.

4
Dard Betay Ke Bicharne Ka Nazar Aata Hai.
Dekhtey Hain Jo Rukh Pak Tu Yeh Lagta Hai.
Jaisay Barchi Se Kat Gaya Hai Jigar.

5
Balwa Aam Mein Youn Pardah Banaya Apna.
Khon Chehray Pe Lagaya Hai Ali Akbar Ka.
Orh Rakhi Hai Dard Ki Chadar.

6
Chalta Jata Hai Jahan Iss Ke Jawaan Lal Ka Sar.
Sath Nezay Ke Chali Jati Hai Iss Ki Bhi Nazar.
Loag Pehchan Letey Hain Aksar.

7
Karbala Mein Jo Maqam-e-Ali Akbar Pe Gaya.
Aik Bibi Ke Sisakney Ki Sadaon Ko Suna.
Mustaqil Aaj Bhi Hai Noha Gar.

8
Khon Mein Dobi Hoi Lash Jawaan Dekhi Hai.
Apne Farzand Ke Seene Mein Sina Dekhi Hai.
Samne Jis Ke Mar Gaya Hai Pisar.

9
Kitna Pyara Tha Inhein Apna Jawaan Lal Raza.
Haye Oss Dard Ka Ahsas Hai Kitna Gehra.
Zindagi Bhar Rahi Jo Khak Basar.
