1
Samajh Ke Zahra Sataya Gaya Sakina Ko.
Qadam Qadam Pe Rulaya Gaya Sakina Ko.
Talab Kiya Kissi Zalim Ne Jab Kaneezi Mein.
Chupa Liya Ossay Zainab Ne Apni Godi Mein.

2
Na Pocho Kaisay Bachaya Gaya Sakina Ko.
Woh Pusht-e-Naqa Pe Tanha Woh Taiz Raftari.
Sanbhalti Kaisay Safar Mein Hussain Ki Pyari.
Giri Nahi Hai Giraya Gaya Sakina Ko.

3
Sar-e-Hussain Ko Taktay They Abid-e-Muztar.
Tarapney Lagti Thi Ghazi Ki Lash Darya Par.
Tamacha Jab Bhi Lagaya Gaya Sakina Ko.
Woh Apna Hal-e-Yateemi Sunanay Aayi Thi.

4
Padar Ki Lash Se Pathar Hatanay Aayi Thi.
Laga Ke Durray Hataya Gaya Sakina Ko.
Rubab Bain Yeh Turbat Pe Aa Ke Karti Thi.
Ghareeb Ho Gayi Kitni Hussain Ki Beti.

5
Ghali Ghali Mein Phiraya Gaya Sakina Ko.
Hijab-e-Gaib Ka Malik Woh Dard Rakhta Hai.
Uthaye Mashk Takallum Talash Karta Hai.
Kahan Pe Pani Pilaya Gaya Sakina Ko.
