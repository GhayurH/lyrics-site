Teri laash ko may Asghar dafnaoon ab kahaan
Dhondhain gay tujh ko naizay charon taraf yahan

1
Jhoolay may ho raha hay kyun beqarar dekho
Karwat badal raha hay kyun baar baar dekho
Jab se suni hay is ne,  Akbar teri azaan

2
Tera mazaar beta hathon se may sajata
Afsoos meray Asghar majboor tera baba
Khud hi mita raha hay,  teri qabr ka nishaan

3
Manzar ye dekhtay hi arsh e bareen hila hay
Ghutno ke bal hay baitha chehray pe khoon mala hay
Sabir Hussain jaisa,  leta hay siskiyan

4
Nazuk galay pe wazni jo teer seh gaya hay
Rota nahi hay bas ye ghayyur sochta hay
Khaimo se cheekh sun ke,  kaheen aa na jaye maa

5
Foaj e sitam ne dekhi taseer e khoon e Haider
Dushman ko dekhtay hain Asghar yun sar utha kar
Lab pe hay muskurahat,  chehray pe itminaan

6
Saqlain jab bhi hoon gay zaahir Imam e Aakhir
Rutba milay ga aala sab hoon gay un ke nokar
Asghar ke matmi aur,  Asghar ke noha khawan
