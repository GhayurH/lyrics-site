Chalo Hussain Tumhein Karbala Bulati Hai.
Saday-e-Fatima Zahra Lahad Se Aati Hai.

1
Tawaf Karta Tha Jiss Ghar Ka Khana-e-Kaba.
Keh Hajion Ki Jamat WO Ghar Jalati Hai.
Qadam Hussain Uthate Hain Soye Karb-o-Bala.
Qadam Se Lipti Hoi Kainat Jati Hai.

2
Khabar Hai Sham-e-Ghareeban Tere Andhere Ko.
Nabi Ke Roze Pe Sughra Diye Jalati Hai.
Bata Aye Mah-e-Muharam Yeh Koan Bibi Hai.
Woh Baal Khol Ke Bas Chand Dekhe Jati Hai.

3
Yeh Koan Bibi Hai Our Kiss Ki Rah Takti Hai.
Na Maut Aati Hai Iss Ko Na Neend Aati Hai.
 Naveed Kiya Howa Labaik Kyun Nahi Kehte.
Sada Tu Dasht Se Hal Min Ki Aab Bhi Aati Hai.
