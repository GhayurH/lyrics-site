1
Bhain Sughra Tera Jhola Asghar Roz Jholaway Asghar Khan.
Kaliyaan Beemar Veran Tere Khali Rah Takdi Na Mar Jaway.
Sughra Di Iltija Si Khat Wich Keh Veer Akbar.
Jadon Galaan Pehli Wari Karey Mera Sohna Asghar.
Aakhein Rubab Maa Choon Asghar Choon Pehla Mera Naam Sikhaway.
Qasad Ne Karbala Wich Aa Ke Aye Wain Kita.
Akbar Rehya Na Bi Bibi Asghar Le Aaway Jehra.
Hunr Teriyaan Sogataan Bas Baba Tera Tere Putar Choon Paway.
Sughra Aye Wainr Paway Aye Khuwab Marday Ne.
Aina Das Ja Aa Ke Asghar Ki Hoya Aye Galay Te.
Main Wekhiya Aye Baba Ro Ro Ke Teri Gardan Chumda Jaway.
Quran Khuwab Wich Main Turbat Banaonda Takiya.
Kujh Barchiyaan Taun Apni Aayat Banaonda Takiya.
Mitiyaan De Wich Chupa Ke Apnray Hathaan Nal Aap Nishan Mitaway.
Babay Da Wasta Jay Sadqa Rubab Maa Da.
 Saqlain Di Dua Aye Howay Qabool Maula.
Jag Te Meri Abeeha Sain Sakina De Nokraan Wich Kehlaway.
