1
Zakhm Ik Hath Se Pehlu Ka Chupati Hai Batool.
Dosrey Hath Se Chaki Ko Chalati Hai Batool.
Koi Tu Aaye Mere Baba Ka Pursa Deney.
Ghar Mein Yeh Keh Ke Azza Khana Bichati Hai Batool.

2
Shab Ki Tareeki Mein Apne Hi Janazay Ke Liye.
Apne Hi Hathoon Se Taboot Banati Hai Batool.
Umar Bhar Kaisay Yeh Bholay Ga Hasan Ko Manzar.
Kaisay Haider Ko Sahaba Se Churati Hai Batool.

3
Aik Din Jaye Gi Darbar Meri Zainab Bhi.
Aahein Bhar Bhar Ke Yeh Fizza Ko Batati Hai Batool.
Kaisay Shabeer Ki Gonji Thi Sada Maiyyat Pe.
Khol Ke Band-e-Kafan Pas Bulati Hai Batool.

4
Aap Ke Baad Howa Baba Hai Kaisa Yeh Sitam.
Jaltay Darwazay Ko Pehlu Se Hatati Hai Batool.
Baat Yeh Likhtay Hoye Dil Mera Kanpa Hai Hunain.
Zakhm Pehlu Ka Ali Se Hi Chupati Hai Batool.
