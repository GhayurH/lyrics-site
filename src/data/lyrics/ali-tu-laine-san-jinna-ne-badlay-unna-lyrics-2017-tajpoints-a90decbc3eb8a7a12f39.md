1
Ali Tu Laine San Jinna Ne Badlay Unna Ne Zulmaan De Arman Pore Kitey.
Sir Sangaan Te Charey Musalmana Ne Odhi Aal Taun Badar Auhad De Sab Nuqsan Pore Kitey.
Loki Satanday Re Oh Khutbay Sunandi Rayi Aye.
Zainab Ne Pathraan Di Barish Andar Apne Shabir Bhira De Haye Farman Pore Kitey.

2
Shabir Sang Te Si Zainab Si Rasiyaan Wich.
Aynj Dowein Karday Re Tilawat Dowaan Ne Sham Bazar Wich Quran Pore Kitey.
Akbar Di Than Si Laila Qasim Di Ja Si Farwa.
Aynj Har Ja Zainab De Ais Safar Wich Abbas Di Ja Te Tuqiya Ne Si Maan Pore Kitey.

3
Kanday Wichaye Rah Wich Pathar Akathay Kitay.
Umat Ne Zahra Diyaan Dhiyaan Khatir Aynj Istaqbal De Sare Si Saman Pore Kitey.
 Akbar Rehai Welay Jadoon Sham Taun Turi Si.
Dastaraan Sir Gohar Te Sab Chadraan Zainab Ne Shamiyaan De Layi Sab Mehman Pore Kitey.
