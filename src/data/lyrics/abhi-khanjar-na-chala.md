Jab Giray Ghode Se Maqtal May Shahe Karbobala
Khanjare Zulm Liye Shimre Sitamgaar Badha
Di Tarap Kar Dar-e-Khaime Se Ye Zainab Ne Sada
Abhi Khanjar Na Chala

1
Toot Mat Behre Khuda Fatima Zehra Ka Chayn
Aur Kuch Dayr Teherja Aray Zaalim Ke Hussain
Bakshishe Ummate Jad Ki Abhi Kartey Hai Dua

2
Teen Din Hogaye Pyasa Hai Nabi Ka Jani
Zibha Karna Hai To Do Ghoont Pilade Pani
Kund Khanjar Hai Mere Bhai Ka Sookha Hai Gala

3
Bosagaahe Nabavi Hai Ye Gala Ay Zaalim
Sheh-ragay Deen Pe Khanjar Na Chala Ay Zaalim
Choomte Thay Isi Halqoom Ko Mahboob E Khuda

4
Chor Kar Khuld Zara Fatahey Khyber Aalay
Aur Kuch Dayr Teher Ja Ke Payambar Aalay
Aati Hi Hogi Wo Aa Lene De Amma Ko Zara

5
Kya Karoon Jaon Kahan Kis Ko Pukaroon Bhai
Kaise Dekhegi Tumhein Todtey Dam Maa Jaayi
Ya Ilahi Mujhe Shabbir Se Pehle Le Utha

6
Haye Kis Dil Say Karoon Tazkiraye Joro Jafa
Kat Gaya Saamne Zainab Ke Shahe Deen Ka Gala
Cheekhti Reh Gayi Zainab Sar E Maqtal Ba Khuda
