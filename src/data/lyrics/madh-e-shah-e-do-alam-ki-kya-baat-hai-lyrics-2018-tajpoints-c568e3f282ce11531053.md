1
Madh-e-Shah-e-Do Alam Ki Kya Baat Hai.
Hamd Ki Hamd Hai Naat Ki Naat Hai.
Aik Do Shab Musafat Ka Kya Tazkira.
Unn Ki Har Raat Mairaj Ki Raat Hai.

2
Sorah-e-Fatah Ke Baiti Aidihim.
Jis Pe Bait Karein Aap Ka Hath Hai.
Zaat Ki Ayhad-e-Fakhar-o-Mubahaat Mein.
Mustafa Ki Ghulami Meri Zaat Hai.

3
Boond Dar Boond Sab Aayatein Aayatein.
Sehan Mein Tees Paron Ki Barsaat Hai.
Kuch Tu Musa Ko Batlaein Jalwah Hai Kya.
Aap Ki Tu Khuda Se Mulaqaat Hai.

4
Unn Ko Ajwa Hai Khak-e-Baqaiya Humein.
Apni Apni Madina Ki Sogat Hai.
Hai Qasam Shehar Ki Aap Ke Shehar Ki.
Iss Ke Aagay Tu Janat Bhi Dehaat Hai.

5
Karbala, Mashhad-o-Kazmain-o-Najaf.
Woh Madina Hai Jo Shehar-e-Sadaat Hai.
Hai Anayaat-e-Aaqa Se Akbar Sukhan.
Warna Lafz-o-Lugat Ki Yeh Auqaat Hai.
