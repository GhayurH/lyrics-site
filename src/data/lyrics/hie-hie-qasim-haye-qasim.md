Haey haey Qasim haey Qasim

1
Qaidi ban ker paband-e-rasan, Lashoun say jo guzray ehl-e-haram
Her bibi apnay piyaroun say, Hoti thi vida ker k matam
Ek bibi Fatima Bint-e-Hassan, Pamal badan Qasim ki bahan
Kisi ek ja say rukhsat na hui,  Toukroun mein jo tha bhai ka badan
Kabhi is tukray per haey Qasim, Kabhi os tukray per haey Qasim
Teri Bhen garib nou nai bhulna
Teray qatal da manzar haey Qasim

2
Kabhi is tukray per haey Qasim, Kabhi os tukray per haey Qasim
1. Kabhi sehra daikh ke routi thi, Kabhi ghash kha k gir jati thi
Pamal hua tha bhai jahan
Os khoon pay gir ker haey Qasim
2. Kis shan say tum dulha thay banay, Kis shan say thi barat chali
Baithi rahi dulhan hujray mein
Aaye na palat ker haey Qasim
3. Farwasa nay kaha bayta Qasim, Keh dayna dadi Zehrasa say
Ghurbat ki kamai tudoon mein
Bhaiji hai saja ker haey Qasim
4. Uth Qasim daikh teri dulhan, Teri lash pay rounay aai hai
Aey kaash ye shadi raas aati
Buss jata tera ghar haey Qasim
5. Her sath moharram ko mehndi, Teri yaad mein Qasim uthti hai
Doolha doolha Qasim doolha
Hota hai laboun per haey Qasim
6. Jawwad, Rehan tay Sarwar nu, nae bhulnay vain os bibi day
Jenday sadqay aj vi jaari ay
Teda matam ghar ghar haey Qasim
