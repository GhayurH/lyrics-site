1
Zamin Hain Raza Mola Meesum Kashmiri.
Maula-e-Jahan Murshad-e-Man Shah-e-Khurasan Zamin Hain Raza Maula.
Sardar-e-Jinna, Rehbar-e-Dein, Dehar Ke Sultan Zamin Hain Raza Maula.
Bazu Pe Bandha Hai Jo Tere Naam Ka Sikka.

2
Karta Hai Hifazat Woh Ali Walon Ki Har Ja.
Har Door Mein Har Haal Mein Momin Ke Nigehban.
Markaz Hai Hidayat Ka Tere Ilm Se Mashhad.
Masooma-e-Qum Faiz Rasaan Qum Mein Hain Be Had.

3
Kehte Hain Paye Ilm Yehi Deni Dabistan.
Jab Ghar Se Nikalta Hai Kissi Kam Se Momin.
Maa Bandh Ke Bazu Pe Ossay Sikka-e-Zamin.
Sar Chom Ke Karti Hai Wida Apni Dua Se.

4
Kehti Hai Banaye Ga Yeh Har Gham Se Bala Se.
Sarwar Ke Azadaron Ka Eman Yehi Hai.
Gar Baat Hifazat Ki Ho, Saman Yehi Hai.
Aalaam-o-Musayab Mein Wohi Dast-e-Atta Hain.

5
Hum Sab Ke Nigehban Shehanshah Raza Hain.
Ik Parcham-e-Abbas-e-Dilawar Ki Ziyarat.
Ik Sikka-e-Bazu Ki Tere Naam Se Nisbat.
Yeh Dono Mohafiz Hain Yehi Kehta Hai Eman.

6
Mashhad Ki Ziyarat Ka Bhi Aizaz Juda Hai.
Main Mashhadi Kehlata Hoon Yeh Fakhar Siwa Hai.
Maula Ki Inayat Hai Unhi Ka Hai Yeh Ahsan.
Yalgar-e-Sitam Ho Keh Chalein Aandhiyaan Gham Ki.

7
Mushkil Ho Pareshani Ho Mosam Ho Koi Bhi.
Har Lamha Ali Walon Ko Rehta Hai Yeh Eqan.
Aye Wali-e-Mashhad Pisar-e-Mosi-e-Kazim.
Tu Misal-e-Padar Baab-e-Hawaij Hai Tu Hakim.

8
Khadam Tere Darbar Ke Kartay Hain Yeh Ayelan.
Har Saal Atta Ho Tere Rozay Ki Ziyarat.
Ban Jaye Teri Madah-o-Sana Wajh-e-Shifat.
Mehshar Mein Ho Meesum Ki Iram Ki Yehi Pehchan.
