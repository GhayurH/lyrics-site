Yaa Ali Sher-e-khuda
Al madad sher-e-khuda

1
Tere taboot se lipte......
Huay Ghaazi ki sada

2
1) Mujhko mil jaati raza hashr bapa kar deta
Ibne Muljim ko toh lamhon mein fanah kar deta
Hausla sabr ka ghazi ko
Hausla sabr ka ghazi ko ataa ho baba

3
2) May toh majboor tha baba ki wasiyat ke liye
Sheh ne roka mujhe karbal ki zarurat ke liye
Warna kar deta mai har ek
Warna kar deta mai har ek ka sar tan se juda

4
3) Maine dekha hai woh zainab ka nikalta aansoo
Umme kulsum ki palkon pe tarrapta aansoo
Kya karun mai bhi tha majboor
Kya karun mai bhi tha majboor paey izne khuda

5
4) Tere taboot pe sheh ka woh lipatna haaye
Umme Kulsum ka Zainab ka woh girya haaye
Roze daaro ka jigar phatne
Roze daaro ka jigar phatne laga sunke bukaa

6
5) Khoon ke tukdo ko dekha hai meri aankhon ne
Zakhme zarbat ko bhigoya haio mere ashkon ne
Sabr bhi bol uthaa
Sabr bhi bol uthaa, Sabr karun main kitna

7
6) Sab janaze pe tere roye yatimi ke sabab
Kaam mushkil tha mere waaste baba yeh sab
Maine yeh kehke uthaaya
Maine yeh kehke uthaaya tha tumhara laasha

8
7) Hai kalam gash mein jo ekkees(21) ka gham soch liya
Tere FEHMI ne ghaazi ka alam soch liya
Bebasi soch ke ghaazi
Bebasi soch ke ghaazi, mera dil khoon royaa
