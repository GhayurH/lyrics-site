1
Dilon se Dur Thori Hai Syed Mudassir Mehdi.
Nazar Se Door Hai Maula Dilon Se Door Thori Hai.
Keh Tanha Karna Aal ALLAH Ka Manshoor Thori Hai.
Yeh Jalwa Woh Sahey Ga Bas Keh Jo Bolay Ga Ya Zainab.
Yeh Jalwa Gah-e-Mehdi Hai Yeh Koi Toor Thori Hai.
Yeh Oss Ki Marzi Jab Chahe Ulat De Parda e Ghaibat.
Imam-e-Waqt Hai Woh Waqt Se Majboor Thori Hai.
Basar Din Raat Karta Rehta Hai Mehdi Ki Khidmat Mein.
Yeh Jo Jibreel Hai, Younhi Koi Mashoor Thori Hai.
Yeh Mehdi Ke Rukh-e-Anwar Ka Ik Sadqa Samajh Lijay.
Sitaron Our Soraj Mein Wagarna Noor Thori Hai.
Areeza Le Ke Ghazi Ke Alam Par Aa Gaya Hoon Main.
Mujhe Maloom Hai Mehdi Yahan Se Door Thori Hai.
Agar Shabbir Ka Hai Gham Agar Mehdi Se Hai Ulfat.
Chala Phir Pusht Par Zanjeer Tu Mazoor Thori Hai.
Yeh Jo Haider Utha Hai Maut Ke Bistar Se Taza Dam.
Sada-e-Qum Ba Izni Di Gayi Hai Soor Thori Hai.
