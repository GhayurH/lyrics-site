Ali Shaoor Ki Sarhad Mein Aa Saka Bhi Nahi.
Ye Inteha-e-Ali Hai Keh Inteha Bhi Nahi.

1
Ali Khuda-o-Muhammad Ke Bad Sab Se Buland.
Yeh Mere Zehan Ki Had Hai Magar Pata Bhi Nahi.
Tamam Mushkilein Hal Ho Gayin Hain Lamhoon Mein.
Abhi Main Soch Raha Tha Ali kaha Bhi Nahi.

2
Ali Se Hashar Mein ALLAH Khud Yeh Keh De Ga.
Koi Tera Bhi Nahi Hai Tu Phir Mera Bhi Nahi.
Tera Libas-e-Shariyat Wila Ke Dhagay Se.
Agar Sila Bhi Nahi Hai Tu Phir Sila Bhi Nahi.

3
Andheri Qabar Mein Yeh Sochna Keh Ajar-e-Rasool.
Wahan Diya Bhi Nahi Hai Yahan Diya Bhi Nahi.
Ali Wali Ke Addo Our Kya Saza Ho Tujhe.
Namaz Parh Bhi Raha Magar Adda Bhi Nahi.

4
Sada-e-Khum Pe Jo Labaik Keh Nahi Sakta.
Osse Sada Ke Liye Phir Koi Sada Bhi Nahi.
Ajeeb Marz Hai Bugz-e-Ali Wali Jis Mein.
Koi Dawa Bhi Nahi Hai Koi Shifa Bhi Nahi.

5
Bagair Ishq-e-Ali Tu Ne Iss Zamane Mein.
Taveel Umar Guzari Magar Jiya Bhi Nahi.
Jise Ali Ki Muhabbat Pe Maut Aayi Hai.
Maza Yeh Hai Ke Woh Mar Bhi Gaya Mara Bhi Nahi.

6
Pata Chale Ga Yeh Mehshar Mein Molvi Jee Ko.
Amal Hazar Kiye Our Kuch Kiya Bhi Nahi.
Ali Wali Ki Muhabbat Mili Nahi Jis Ko.
Woh Parsa Bhi Hai Agar Tu Paar Sa Bhi Nahi.

7
Yeh Aik Do Hain Keh Woh Aik Dekh Parde Mein.
Keh Faisla Bhi Nahi Our Fasla Bhi Nahi.
Yeh Mojza Hai Karamat Hai Be-Niyazi Hai.
Ali Khuda Bhi Lage Hai Magar Khuda Bhi Nahi.

8
Ali Ke Pyar Ki Aatish Mein Aadmi Ka Badan.
Agar Jala Bhi Nahi Hai Tu Phir Jila Bhi Nahi.
Crror Mangne Wale Crror Bar Bhi Lein.
Hussain WOh Hai Keh Jis Ne Kabhi Gina Bhi Nahi.

9
Kafan Mein Ghaza-e-Khak-e-Shifa Tere Munh Pe.
Agar Mala Bhi Nahi Hai TU Kuch Mila Bhi Nahi.
Tere Amal Ke Sahifon Mein Ibn-e-Zahra Pe.
Agar Buka Bhi Nahi Hai Tu Phir Baqa Bhi Nahi.

10
Tumhare Kan Ne Hukam-e-Rasool Man Kunto.
Suna Nahi Hai Tu Phir Laik-e-Sana Bhi Nahi.
Aseer-e-Ulfat-e-Aulad-e-Mustafa Shaukat.
Koi Raha Bhi Nahi Hai Tu Phir Reha Bhi Nahi.
