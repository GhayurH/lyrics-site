1
Qaid Mein Dala Naqi Ko Ajar Ummat De Rahi Hai.
Aal Se Kya Dushmani Hai Samarra Tak Jo Gayi Hai.
Zulm Youn Barhta Gaya Hai Ya Ali Se Dushmani Mein.
Karbala Se Sham Tak Jo Qaid Mein Zainab Chali Hai.

2
Syeda Kehti Rahein Darbar Mein Sachi Hoon Logo.
Ro Ke Guzri Gham Issi Mein Ya Naqi Ki Zindagi Hai.
Sun Bano Abbas Ne Youn Mil Ke Badlah Le Liya Hai.
Sar Hain Nezon Par Uthaye Maut Roti Dekhti Hai.

3
Syeda Ke Gham Mein Hain Tu De Rahe Pursa Hain Hum Yeh.
Mar Dala Hai Naqi Ko Aa Gayi Yeh Kya Ghari Hai.
Roney Aate Hain Naqi Ko Barhwein Aaqa Mere.
Matami Halqoon Mein Jo Jarrar Hoti Roshni Hai.
