“Noon” Ki Our Wal Qalam Ki Har Satar Ki Eid Hai
Phir Andherey Munh Chupatay Phir Rahe Hain Ja Baja
Dehar Mein Ik Bar Phir Waqt-e-Sahar Ki Eid Hai
Aaj Se Abtar Hai Peygamber Ka Har Dushman Suno
Aaj Peygamber Ke Haan Pehle Pisar Ki Eid Hai
Hai Nuzool-e-Natiq e Quran Qurano Ke Ghar
Aaj Yani Fatima Zahra Ke Ghar Ki Eid Hai
Saaton, Ghariyon Ki Khushiyaan Deedni Hain Deedni
Pandarhwin Ramzan Ki Tu Har Pahar Ki Eid Hai
Lalaway Wal Qadar Ki Pur Noor Aamad Ka Hai Din
Gulshan-e-Kausar Ke Har Barg-o-Shajar Ki Eid Hai
Aaj Youm-e-Marg e Aadaye Muhammad Hai Suno
Aaj Haider Ke Dil-o-Jan-e-Jigar Ki Eid Hai
Kul Khalaiq Mehway Midhat Hain Bara-e-Mujtaba
Har Zameen, Har Aasmaan, Har Behar-o-Bar Ki Eid Hai
Abar-e-Nesaan Khoob Ahmad Aap Par Barsa Hai Aaj
Aaj Mairaj-e-Sukhan Ke Iss Safar Ki Eid Hai
