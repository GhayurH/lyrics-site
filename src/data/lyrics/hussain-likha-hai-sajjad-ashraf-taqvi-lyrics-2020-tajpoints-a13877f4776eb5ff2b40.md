Hussain Likha Hai Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi Write Up

1
Jabeen-e-Deen-e-Khuda Par Hussain Likha Hai.
Ali-o-Zahra Ne Mil Kar Hussain Likha Hai.
Jahan Se Chaho Parho Dastan-e-Karb-o-Bala.
Har Ik Warq Pe Barabar Hussain Likha Hai.
Jalein Gay Our Na Thakein Gay Kabhi Bhi Aye Fitrus.
Keh Ab Tumhare Paron Par Hussain Likha Hai.

2
Sawal Aik Tha Baiyat Tere Fasaney Mein.
Jawab Mein Tu Bahatar Hussain Likha Hai.
Hussaino Mini Likhon Tu Min Al-Hussain Ke Baad.
Yeh Lagta Hai Keh Payamber Hussain Likha Hai.
Siyah Ghilaf-e-Haram Yeh Bata Raha Hai Humein.
Keh Iss Makan Ke Bahir Hussain Likha Hai.

3
Rafo Nahi Hai Yeh Zakhm-e-Azza Ki Abjad Se.
Siyahi Khoon Se Bana Kar Hussain Likha Hai.
Hamarey Hath Uthein Kis Tarha Na Matam Mein.
Hamarey Seeno Ke Andar Hussain Likha Hai.
Likha Hai Chand Ke Seene Pe Jaisay Nam-e-Ali.
Ali Ke Seene Pe Akbar Hussain Likha Hai.
