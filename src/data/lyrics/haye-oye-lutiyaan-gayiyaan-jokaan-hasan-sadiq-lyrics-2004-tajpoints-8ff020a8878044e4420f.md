Haye Oye Lutiyaan Gayiyaan Jokaan Hasan Sadiq.

1
Aaya Aye Alam Khali Alamdar Nayi Aaya.
Rondi Rayi Sakina Haye Oh Gal Alam Nu La Ke.
Haye Oh Lutiyaan Gayiyaan Jokaan Wich Pardais De Aa Ke.
Darya De Pasay Tak Ke Rayiyaan Rondiyaan Ne Kariyaan.
Pandi Rayi Aye Zainab Bharjaiyaan Nu Hath Kariyaan.
Umm Ul-Banin Da Chan Haye Oh Jadon Waliya Na Ja Ke.

2
Mazloom Di Sawari Jad Wal Ke Khaimay Aayi.
Kithay Baba Maida Pochiya Shabeer Di Oh Jayi.
Piya Roway Zuljinah V Haye Oh Sar Apna Jhuka Ke.
Hoi Tukray Tukray Maqtal Shabar Di Aye Nishani.
Sumbaan De Heth Rul Gayi Qasim Di Haye Jawani.
Kubra Da Baba Roway Haye Oh Phul Lash Te Pa Ke.

3
Pathraan De Heth Lashaan Paiyaan Waindiyaan Ne Mawaan.
Agaan Khaimiyaan Nu La Ke Lut Laiyaan Ne Ridawaan.
Kurlandi Rayi Aye Zainab Haye Oh Beemar Nu Cha Ke.
 Khursheed Wafa Oss Di Na Bhul Si Aye Zamana.
Odha Sadqa Rehniyaan Ne Ta Hashar Aye Azana.
Jis Deen Hay Bachaya Haye Dowein Bazu Kata Ke.
