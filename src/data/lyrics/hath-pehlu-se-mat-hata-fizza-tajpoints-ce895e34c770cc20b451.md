1
Ro Ke Kehti Thin Fatima Zahra Hath Pehlu Se Mat Hata Fizza.
Ho Sake Tu Hata De Darwaza Hath Pehlu Se Mat Hata Fizza.
Jalta Darwaza Youn Giraya Hai Pasliyaan Toor Di Hain Zalim Ne.
Sans Rukti Hai Dam Nikalta Hai Hath Kaise Uthaon Pehlu Se.

2
Dard Mujh Se Saha Nahi Jata Hath Pehlu Se Mat Hata Fizza.
Meekh Pehlu Mein Ho Gayi Paiwast Sans Letay Hoye Bhi Darti Hoon.
Pehle Pehlu Ko Main Dabati Hoon Phir Qadam Ko Zameen Pe Rakhti Hoon.
Maut Ke Bad Bhi Na Uthay Ga Hath Pehlu Se Mat Hata Fizza.

3
Kaise Dekhe Gi Sani-e-Zahra Parda Chadar Se To Banane De.
Abhi Kamsin Hain Zainab-o-Kulsom Zakhmi Pehlu Mujhe Chupane De.
Mar Hi Jaein Na Dekh Kar Dukhiya Hath Pehlu Se Mat Hata Fizza.
Janti Hoon Keh Iss Madine Mein Koi Imdad Ko Na Aaye Ga.

4
Dekhte Hain Mari Taraf Mula Mujh Ko Wadah Nibhana Hai Apna.
Jaldi Jaldi Mujhe Utha Fizza Hath Pehlu Se Mat Hata Fizza.
Dekh Kar Haal Apni Madar Ka Haye Nana Se Bain Karta Hai.
Rang Dekho Hasan Ke Chehray Ka Jaise Ghairat Se Dam Nikalta Hai.

5
Dekh Le Ga Woh Khon Hai Behta Hath Pehlu Se Mat Hata Fizza.
Youn Hi Karti Thin Sajda Khaliq Ko Chaki Akbar Younhi Chalati Thin.
Dekh Kar Syeda Ki Halat Ko Jab Bhi Fizza Madad Ko Aati Thin.
Ro Ke Parhti Thin Aik Hi Noha Hath Pehlu Se Mat Hata Fizza.
