1
Sakina Aayi Jo Maqtal Wich Aakhay Alwida Baba.
Main Ujari Naal Bole Haan Main Walran Nayi Wala Baba.
Ayho Dukh Hay Taida Lasha Dafan Koi Nayi Kafan Koi Nayi.
Kafan Taikoon Main Dewaan Haan Maidi Hondi Rida Baba.

2
Yateemi Taan Sakina Da Muqadar Haye Hashar Torein.
Yateema Waang Mityaan Tey Kiwein Samnraan Aein Dasa Baba.
Teraan Zarbaan Zakham Taiday Main Girndi Rahi Gawah Haan Main.
Maiday Kolon Taan Hath Zakhmi Na Wal Wal Toon Luka Baba.

3
Sada Jageer Akbar Di Shala Baba Hunda Manrein.
Mile Sham Ich Dua Kar Cha Qabar Di Koi Ja Baba.
Aakhay Shahan Masooma Saday Rowan Te Pabandiyaan.
Izn Honda Maiyyat Taida Wanja Haan Main Saha Baba.
