Kyun Chand Muharram Ka Itna Udas Hai.
Lagta Hai Yaad Iss Ko Asghar Ki Pyas Hai.

1
Issay Dekh Kar Shabbir Ne Behno Se Yeh Kaha.
Kuch Khoof-e-Ashqiya Nahi Jab Tak Abbas Hai.
Sar Peet Ke Sughra Ne Kaha Chand Dekh Kar.
Akbar Teri Shadi Ki Behna Ko Aas Hai.

2
Ik Jhola Hai Ik Sehra Kuch Yadein Siskiyaan Hain.
Jeene Ka Yeh Bahana Sughra Ke Pas Hai.
Iss Chand Mein Ujra Hai Ghar Aal-e-Muhammad Ka.
Har Zulm Ka Gawah Yeh Gham Ka Akas Hai.

3
Syeda Ki Yeh Hasrat Hai Har So Hain Azza Khane.
Hai Sogawari Ghar Ghar Kala Libas Hai.
Ro Ro Ke Yeh Kehti Thin Zawar Bibiyaan.
Yeh Chand Muharram Ka Aaya Na Raas Hai.
