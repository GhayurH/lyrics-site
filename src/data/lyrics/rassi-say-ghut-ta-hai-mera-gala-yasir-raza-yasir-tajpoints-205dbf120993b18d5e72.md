1
Rassi Say Ghut ta Hai Mera Gala Yasir Raza Yasir.
Rasi Se Ghutta Hai Mera Gala Baba.
Dam Ruk Raha Hai Mera Aa Kar Bacha Baba.
Jab Se Chini Baliyaan Kano Se Khoon Hai Rawaan.
Aa Kar Laga De Mujhe Khak-e-Shifa Baba.
Baba Tere Baad Main Itni Ghareeb Ho Gayi.
Logo Ne La Kar Mujhe Sadqa Diya Baba.
Bas Marta Hai Mere Munh Par Tamachay Shaqi.
Mujh Ko Batata Nahi Meri Khata Baba.
Dadi Ki Tarha Mujhe Mil Jaye Koi Jagah.
Jee Bhar Ke Ro Loon Tujhey Aye Be Khata Baba.
Sar Se Mere Cheen Ke Talwar Ki Nook Pe.
Lehra Raha Hai Laeen Meri Rida Baba.
Zulfein Meri Khainch Kar Kehne Laga Ik Laeen.
Ja Kar Bola Hai Kahan Tere Chacha Baba.
Asghar Ke Lashay Ko Jab Naizay Pe Laya Laeen.
Deti Thi Ammo Ko Main Ro Kar Sada Baba.
Teri Shahadat Mujhe Zindan Tak Le Gayi.
Main Sar Ta Pa Ho Gayi Ab Marsiya Baba.
Zindaan Mein:YASIR RAZA: Bibi Ne Noha Kiya.
Na Raas Aayi Humein Karb-o-Bala Baba.
