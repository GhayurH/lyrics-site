1) Thay bain Sakina kay Abbas nahi aayey
Derya pai aayai aaisay Abbas nahi aayey

2) Aisa to na hota tha jab tak thai Medinai main
Kya Kerbobala Aayai Abbas Nahi Aayai

3) Dil wahi thi mainai hi baba sai rida ran ki

Kyoon rooth gayai mujh sai Abbas Nahi Aayai

6) Panchi hon ya manus hon her Shaam palatain hain
Kia bat Khuda janai Abbas Nahi Aayai

7) Asbab luta sara khaimain bhi lutai sarai
Dur cheenai gayai merai Abbas Nahi Aayai

8) Beemar bahtiji per nani si bahtiji per
Kia kia na sitam tutai Abbas Nahi Aayai
