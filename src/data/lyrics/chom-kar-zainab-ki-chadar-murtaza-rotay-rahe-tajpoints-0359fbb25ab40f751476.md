1
Chom Kar Zainab Ki Chadar Murtaza Rotay Rahe.
Sham Ki Qaidan Ko Keh Ke Alwida Rotay Rahe.
Fatima Ke Baad Main Ne Naaz Se Pala Tujhey.
Keh Ke Haye Sham Haye Karbala Rotay Rahe.

2
Aaj Kyun Aaya Nahi Hai Rizq Hum Ko Bantne.
Kofay Ki Galiyon Mein Sab Be Aasra Rotay Rahe.
Aap Ke Naib Ko Mara Sajday Mein Aye Ya Nabi.
Aa Ke Maiyyat Par Ali Ki Anbiya Rotay Rahe.

3
Choma Hai Shabbar Ka Seena Our Gala Shabbir Ka.
Zehar Our Khanjar Ka Kar Ke Tazkira Rotay Rahe.
Kaisay Dekhon Ro Rahi Hain Baba Zahra Zadiyaan.
Dein Izn Talwar Thamay Bawafa Rotay Rahe.

4
Ho Gaya Kofay Mein Tera Ghar Veraan Baba Ki Jaan.
De Zahra Ko Tasaliyan Mustafa Rotay Rahe.
Mushkilein Hoon Gi Bari Bazar Our Darbar Mein.
De Ke Fizza Ko Ali Yeh Hosla Rotay Rahe.

5
Tum Ho Fatah-e-Karbal Ke Our Zainab Sham Ki.
Keh Ke Yeh Shabbir Se Haye La Fatah Rotay Rahe.
Kya Karein Sajjad-o- Asghar Woh Bhala Manzar Biyaan.
Keh Ke Maula Betiyon Ko Alwida Rotay Rahe.
