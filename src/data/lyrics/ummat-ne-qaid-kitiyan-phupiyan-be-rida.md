Ummat ne qaid kitiyan, haaye phupiyan be-rida
Taiyon Abid rut roya
Bardaasht kar na sakeya, ghairat da ae Khuda

1
Kitthay majma laeena da, kitthay Zahra diyan palliyan
Munh waalan de naal kaj ke, bazaaran de wich kharriyan
Mangdi rahi burqe ro ro, Konain di Malka

2
Zainab de khutbe sun ke, hasde si Shaami saare
Tohfe samajh ke patthar, jadon qaidiyan nu maare
Baalan nu zakhmi kitta, be-jurm be-khata

3
Sir nangay rulaya ae, kul nabiyan di ghairat nu
Kyun kalmagoan kitta, sharminda sharafat nu
Naize te rahi ae rondi, Abbas di wafa

4
Enjh Shimr kamine ne ik zulm kamaya ae
Sang ghore de safraan ch Sakina nu dauraya ae
Chauan saalan di ujri da koi kitta na haya

5
Sajjad mahari nu ro kehndiyan masrooran
Kitthay jaaiye di saansan kun, zakhmaan de naal chooran
Kalli Fizza saareyan nu kevain deve hosla

6
Sajjad ne vekhe hin Akhtar oh zulm saare
Kevain Shimr laeen korre, edhi paak kand te maare
Phir vi na munh chon nikli ummat layi baddua
