Meri Aankhon Ko Aankhon Ka Kinara Kon De Ga.
Samandur Ko Samandur Mein Sahara Kon De Ga.

1
Mere Chehre Ko Chehra Kab Inayat Kar Rahe Ho.
Tumhein Mere Siwa Chehra Tumhara Kon De Ga.
Muhabbat Neela Mausam Ban Ke Aa Jaye Gi Ik Din.
Ghulabi Titlion Ko Phir Sahara Kon De Ga.

2
Meri Aawaz Mein Aawaz Kis Ki Bolti Hai.
Mere Geeton Ko Geeton Ka Kinara Kon De Ga.
Badan Mein Aik Sehra Jal Raha Hai Bujh Raha Hai.
Mere Daryaon Ko Qaiser Ishara Kon De Ga.
