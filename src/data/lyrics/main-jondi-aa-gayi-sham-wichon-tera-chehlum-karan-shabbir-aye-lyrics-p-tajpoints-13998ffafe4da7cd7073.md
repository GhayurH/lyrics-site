1
Main Jondi Aa Gayi Sham Wichon Tera Chehlum Karan Shabbir Aye.
Jay Hoor Koi Sham Fatha Karni Hazir Teri Humsheer Aye.
Aye Akhiyan Sham De Hakim Ne Aaway Paidal Tur Ke Zainab.
Bazar Uboor Jiwein Kita Main Janni Aan Ya Jane Rab.
Mera Dil Ghabraya Oss Welay Jadon Sham Che Wadh Gayi Bheer Aye.
Karbal Taun Le Ke Sham Taein Bara Zulm Sajjad Te Hoya.
Sir Nangay Wekh Ke Phophiyaan De Sajjad Bari Rat Roya.
Tera Abid Putar Ghayoor Hoya Kul Dardaan Di Tasveer Aye.
Jadon Pochiya Hussain, Sakina Da Maa Aoun Di Khak Te Beh Gayi.
Sajjad Nu Hosla Dain Wali Oh Sham De Wich Haye Reh Gayi.
Nayi Kafan Pawaya Sakina Nu Na Laye Odhay Zanjeer Aye.
Munh Rakh Ke Veer Di Qabar Uttay Kul Sham Da Haal Sunaya.
Mera Veer Hussain Namazi Aye Kul Shamiyaan Nu Manwaya.
Hun Koi Nayi Bagi Aakhay Ga Main Ainj Keeti Taqreer Aye.
 Izhar Dua Aye Karda Aye Howay Arz Meri Manzoor Aye.
Roway Chaan Mashkoor Te Almaan Di Howay Har Dukh Qabar Da Door Aye.
Ainu Qarb Miley Odha Mehshar Wich Jehra Ronda Rat De Neer Aye.
