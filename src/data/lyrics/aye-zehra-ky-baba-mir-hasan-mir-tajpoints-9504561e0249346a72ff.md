Aye Zehra Ky Baba Mir Hasan Mir Lyrics

1
Aye Zahra Ke Baba Aye Shah-e-Madina Youn Beti Ko Teri Sataya Gaya Hai.
Shikasta Hai Pehlu Shikasta Hai Bazu Lahu Fatima Ka Bahaya Gaya Hai.
Madine Ki Kya Thi Main Halat Bataon Tumhein Syeda Ki Musibat Bataon.
Kabhi Aag Dar Par Lagai Hai Oss Ke Kabhi Hath Oss Par Uthaya Gaya Hai.
Sada Aa Rahi Thi Yeh Ghar Yeh Mita Do Jo Rehtey Hain Ghar Mein Unhein Bhi Jala Do.
Taharat Ka Kaba Jalanay Ki Khatir Jamat Ko Sari Bulaya Gaya Hai.

2
Koi Bhi Na Samjha Shah e La Fatah Ko Yeh Gham Kha Gaya Hai Teri Fatima Ko.
Madine Ki Galiyon Mein Qaidi Bana Kar Tere Jansheen Ko Phiraya Gaya Hai.
Na Uthtey They Bachay Na Ruktay They Aanso Sanbhalay Farishton Ne Dono Ke Bazu.
Bari Mushkilon Se Janazay Se Maa Ke Hussain-o-Hasan Ko Hataya Gaya Hai.
Ali Murtaza Ki Ajab Thi Ghareebi Teri Ladli Par Hansay Jab Namazi.
Sand Kardi Tukray Mazzaq Iss Tarha Se Teri Syeda Ka Uraya Gaya Hai.

3
Giri Khak Par Arsh Par Rehne Wali Shikasta Hoi Oss Ke Kano Ki Bali.
Lahu Chashm-e-Zahra Mein Bharne Laga Tha Tamacha Hi Aisa Lagaya Gaya Hai.
Ali Ko Jo Masjid Bulaya Gaya Tha Teri Ladli Ko Jalal Aa Gaya Tha.
Ali Ne Diya Wasta Apne Haq Ka Madine Ko Aisay Bachaya Gaya Hai.
Ali Ne Banai Thin Chalees Qabrein Pata Pochti Hain Takallum Ki Aahein.
Zameen-e-Madina Mein Jane Kahan Par Khazana Tumhara Chupaya Gaya Hai.
