1
Sajde mein Sar Hai, Halq Pe Khanjar Ki Dhar Hai
Banda Yeah Koun? Aysa Ibadat Guzar Hai
Khamosh Kaise Tu Mere Parwardigar Hai
Dar Par Bahen Hai, Pusht Pe Khatil Sawar Hai
Is Haal Main Bhi Lab Pe Yehi Bar Bar Hai
Sad Shukr, Sad Shukr, Sad Shukr Mere Ya Rab

2
Haan Ye Wahi Hussain Alahis Salaam Hai
Teri Taraf Se Jis Pe Darud-o-Salaam Hai
Sibt-e-Nabi Hain Ye Shahe Aali Maqaam Hai
Jis Kai Laboon Pe Eik Hi Kalma Mudam Hai
Sad Shukr, Sad Shukr, Sad Shukr Mere Ya Rab

3
Ye Wo Hai Jis Kai Haath Main Zulf-e-Rasul Thi
Tere Nabi Ku Iski Har Ek Zid Qabul Thi
Baba Ali Isi Ka Thaa Madar Batul Thi
Uski Zaban Pe Baat Yahi Misle Phool Thi
Sad Shukr, Sad Shukr, Sad Shukr Mere Ya Rab

4
Kehte Hain Dast-e-Fatema Zahra Kai Aable
Paala Hai Chakki Pees Kai Madar Nai Pyar Sai
Jannat Se Kapre Eid Pai Bheje Khuda Jise
Shukr-e-Khuda Main Baat Wo Kuyun Kar Na Yeah Kahe
Sad Shukr, Sad Shukr, Sad Shukr Mere Ya Rab

5
Aulad Walo Tumhi Karo Dil Sai Faisla
Wo Shakhs Jiska Beta Ho Ham Shakl-e-Mustafa
Dekhe Usi Pisar Ka Wo Sina Chida Huwa
Neza Nekaalte Huwe Sine Sai Di Sadaa
Sad Shukr, Sad Shukr, Sad Shukr Mere Ya Rab

6
Rukh Suwe Asmaan Kiya Khaliq Ku Di Sada
Asghar Shaheed Ho Gaya Akbar Guzar Gaya
Lashakar Kahan Ka Mera Biradar Nahi Raha
Par Bekas-o-Malool Ne Shikwa Nahi Kiya
Sad Shukr, Sad Shukr, Sad Shukr Mere Ya Rab

7
Naraaz Tu Ho Mujh Sai Mujhe Kab Qubul Hai
Tu Khush Ho Ay Kareem Mujeh Sab Qubul Hai
Qaid-e-Haram Asiri-e-Zainab Qubul Hai
Kya Tujh Ku Meri Baat Yeah Yaa Rab Qubul Hai
Sad Shukr, Sad Shukr, Sad Shukr Mere Ya Rab

8
Zakhmoon Sai Choor Choor Tha Jab Fatema Ka Laal
Lakht-e-Dil-e-Ali-o-Nabi Gham Se Tha Nidhaal
Chehre Pe Khoon Mal Kai Basad Hasrat-o-Malaal
Ki Arz Sheh Ne Shukar Hai Ay Rab-e-Zuljalaal
Sad Shukr, Sad Shukr, Sad Shukr Mere Ya Rab

9
Kaisi Kahoon Raihaan Jo Sheh Pe Guzar Gaie
Bete Shaheed Ho Gaie Bachi Bichar Gaie
Zainab Dayar-e-Sham Main Jab Nange Sar Gaie
Nouk-e-Sinaa Se Suwe Falaq Ye Khabar Gaie
Sad Shukr, Sad Shukr, Sad Shukr Mere Ya Rab
