Zabaan Ke Sath Dil Bola Ali Mola Nadeem Sarwar.
Zabaan Ke Sath Dil Bola Ali Maula.

1
Ali Ki Badshahi Hai Ali Ism-e-Elahi Hai.
Har Ik Meezan Mein Tola Zabaan Ke Sath Dil Bola.
Ali Maula, Ali Maula, Ali Maula, Ali Maula.
Lagay Jab Haidery Narah Jahan Mein Gaunj Hoti Hai.

2
Kabhi Nad-e-Ali Parh Kar Kaho Ya Fatah-e-Khaybar.
Tu Mushkil Hogi Mushkil Mein Ali Ka Naam Hi Sun Kar.
Nuseri Ne Khuda Mana Khuda Ne La Fata Jana.
Khuda Ko Woh Hi Samjhay Ga Ali Ko Jis Ne Pehchana.

3
Ali Soraj Ko Palta De Ali Darya Ko Thehra De.
Ali Chahe Tu Munkir Par Qaza Ki Aag Barsa De.
Ali Amil Ali Kamil Ali Kashti Ali Sahil.
Agar Insaf Se Pocho Ali Islam Ka Hai Dil.

4
Ali Hai Mazhar-e-Yazdaan Ali Hai Bolta Quraan.
Nabowat Jism Ki Sorat Ali Oss Jism Mein Hai Jaan.
Saloni Ki Sada Ban Kar Abhi Rehan Our Sarwar.
Koi Aawaz Aati Hai Jo Chaho Poch Lo Aa Kar.
