1
Haye Mara Gaya Abbas Ko Hussain Raza Kazmi.
Bain Tha Sheh Ka Hai Youn Mara Gaya Abbas Ko.
Bad-e-Qasim Tukray Tukray Kar Diya Abbas Ko.
Qatal Gar Abbas Ho Jaye Tu Kya Jee Paye Ga.

2
Jeetay Jee Shabeer Tu Sehra Mein Hi Mar Jaye Ga.
Mar Do Ab Kehte They Sab Ashqiya Abbas Ko.
Zeen Jab Abbas Ne Ghorey Ki Ran Mein Chor Di.
Bolay Sheh Meri Kamar Teri Qaza Ne Toor Di.

3
Kar Diya Mujh Se Laeeno Ne Juda Abbas Ko.
Lash Uthwa Do Mere Abbas Ki Aao Zara.
Akbar-o-Qasim Ko Sheh Ro Ro Ke Detey They Sada.
Khak Par Jab Zeen Se Dekha Gira Abbas Ko.

4
Do Sada Ghazi Ko Zainab Dekhey Woh Burqay Lutay.
Hai Kaha Abbas Hans Hans Kar Laeen Kehne Lagay.
Kehte They Sadaat Behno Ki Rida Abbas Ko.
Jab Lagin Talwarein Ran Mein Hathoon Par Shabeer Ko.

5
Samne Aa Kar Kissi Zalim Ne Mara Teer Tu.
Ro Ke Sheh Ne Yaad Ossay Lamhey Kiya Abbas Ko.
Kat Gaye Bazu Alam Na Khak Par Girney Diya.
Sar Kata Kar Apna, Sar Shabeer Ka Oncha Kiya.

6
Hashar Tak Roti Rahe Gi Ab Wafa Abbas Ko.
Khak Uratein Bain Kartin Darya Ki Janib Asad.
De Sadaein Ya Ali Ki Kehti Thin Woh Al-Madad.
Dekhti Thin Jab Tarapta Fatima Abbas Ko.
