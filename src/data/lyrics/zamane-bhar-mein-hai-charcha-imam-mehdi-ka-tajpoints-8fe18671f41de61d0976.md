Zamane Bhar Mein Hai Charcha Imam Mehdi Ka.
Hai Kainat Ka Pora Nizam Mehdi Ka.

1
Imam-e-Waqt Ki Onchi Hai Manzlat Sab Se.
Buland Martaba Aala Moqam Mehdi Ka.
Najoom Shams-o-Qamar Aasman Our Zameen.
Pukartay Hain Yeh Har Waqt Naam Mehdi Ka.

2
Tumhein Bachaye Ga Iss Door-e-Purfitan Mein Wohi.
Nijat Ke Liye Daman Lo Tham Mehdi Ka.
Khuda Ki Sari Ataein Inhi Ke Dam Se Hain.
Dua Ko Rab Se Milana Hai Kam Mehdi Ka.

3
Marey Imam Ki Ghaibat Pe Bhonkne Walo.
Tumhein Tu Kafi Hai Bas Ik Ghulam Mehdi Ka.
Jo Main Ne Madh-e-Imam-e-Zamaan Likhi Hasnain.
Haqeeqatan Hai Yeh Mujh Par Inam Mehdi Ka.
