1
Rukhsat e Ali Akbar Syed Mudassir Mehdi.
Maqtal Ki Ijazat Ke Liye Aaye Jo Akbar.
Sadaat Ke Khaimon Mein Tha Ik Hashar Ka Manzar.
Gham Dil Ka Ayyaan Tha Sheh-e-Mazloom Ke Rukh Par.
Maula Ne Kaha Lal Ko Seene Se Laga Kar.
Akbar Teri Maa Maut Teri Seh Na Sakay Gi.
Iss Peeri Mein Hum Se Teri Maiyyat Na Uthay Gi.
Dekha Rukh-e-Akbar Ko Kayi Bar Pidar Ne.
Phir Sheh Ne Kaha Choom Ke Seene Ko Pisar Ke.
Aye Lal Yehi Soch Ke Phatta Hai Kaleja.
Maqtal Mein Sina Tere Kalejay Mein Lagay Gi.
Sar Choom Ke Akbar Ka Yeh Zainab Ne Boka Ki.
Marne Ko Na Ja Aye Merey Bhaiyya Ki Kamai.
Armaan Hai Keh Ik Bar Tujhe Dulha Banaon.
Mat Ja Keh Kafan Tera Na Maa Dekh Sakay Gi.
Nana Ka Amama Sar-e-Akbar Pe Saja Kar.
Hum Shakal-e-Payamber Ko Wahin Dulha Bana Kar.
Sarwar Se Lipat Kar Yehi Kehti Rahein Zainab.
Bhaiyya Meri Akbar Ke Bina Kaisay Katay Gi.
Lipati Hoi Daman Se Yeh Kehti Thi Sakina.
Maqtal Mein Youn Marne Ke Liye Jao Na Bhaiyya.
Zindah Hai Tere Aane Ki Umeed Pe Ab Tak.
Mar Jaye Gi Sughra Tere Bin Kaisay Jaye Gi.
Uthti Thin Kabhi Girti Thin Sadmay Se Zameen Par.
Akbar Se Kahey Jati Thin Laila Yeh Tarap Kar.
Totay Gi Merey Lal Sina Tere Jigar Mein.
Ta Umar Magar Tees Merey Dil Se Uthay Gi.
Kya Jane Ga Zainab Ki Muhabbat Ki Koi Had.
Sadqay Mein Tere Jis Ne Diye Aoun-o-Muhammad.
Jis Ne Tere Gham Mein Na Liya Beton Ka Pursa.
Sar Peet Ke Akbar Tera Matam Woh Karey Gi.
Aye Jan-e-Pidar Ik Teri Ulfat Mein Jaye Hum.
Nana Ki Jo Yaad Aayi Tujhe Dekha Kiye Hum.
Akbar Se Kaha Shah Ne Mat Ja Keh Tere Baad.
Tasveer-e-Payamber Bhi Na Phir Ghar Mein Dikhey Gi.
Yeh Baat Iram Our Mudassir Na Bholana.
Sarwar Ke Haram Mein Gham-e-Akbar Na Sunana.
Warna Larz Uthein Gay Dar-o-Baam Haram Ke.
Our Turbat-e-Shabeer Bhi Khoon Roney Lagay Gi.
