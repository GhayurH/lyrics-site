Bas Wohi Lamha Meri Zeest Ka Pyara Ho Ga.
Jis Ghari Gunbad-e-Khazra Ka Nazara Ho Ga.

1
Khuwab Mein Aa Ke Mujhe Rehmat-e-Aalam Ne Kaha.
Naat LikhoKeh Karam Tum Pe Hamara Ho Ga.
Mujh Ko Mehshar Ki Kari Dhoop Mein Mere Aaqa.
Aap Ke Saya-e-Rehmat Ka Sahara Ho Ga.

2
Mere Aaqa Tu Mujhe Izn-e-Takallum De De.
Bin Teri Naat Kahe Kaise Guzara Ho Ga.
Mujh Ko Maloom Hai Jaon Ga Madina RAQIB.
Jab Bhi Hassnain Ke Nana Ka Ishara Ho Ga.
