Imraan Ka Yeh Sharf Hai Khuda Ki Nigah Mein
Apna Rasool De Diya Unn Ki Panah Mein
Woh Jis Rasool Ki Thi Do Aalam Pe Ik Nazar
Imran Unn Ko Rakhtay They Apni Nigah Mein
Dushman Khuda Ka Maan Ke Nabod Kar Diya
Aaya Jo Koi Unn Ke Bhatijay Ki Rah Mein
Kar-e-Khuda Mein Hath Bata Kar Nabi Ka Woh
Salar Ban Gaye Hain Khuda Ki Sipah Mein
Shaban Mein Youn Kufar Ka Chehra Utar Gaya
Imran Itnay Utray Hain Iss Aik Mah Mein
Imran Ke Hussain Ki La Dekh Ghoor Se
Aaein Gay Khud Nazar Woh Tujhey LaIlaha Mein
Kabay Se Poch Hazrat-e-Imran Ka Maqam
Shamil Hai Unn Ka Khon Har Ik Sajdah Ga Mein
Yaqoot Bach Ke Chamkay Ga Ta Hashar Unn Ka Naam
Aye Deen-e-Kirdgar Tumhari Kullah Mein
Imran Pehla Naam Hai Oss Darsgah Ka
Pal Kar Jawan Dein Howa Jis Darsgah Mein
Lag Jaye Unn Ki Aah Tu Mit Jaye Kainat
Yeh Kam Se Kam Asar Hai ASAD Unn Ki Aah Mein
