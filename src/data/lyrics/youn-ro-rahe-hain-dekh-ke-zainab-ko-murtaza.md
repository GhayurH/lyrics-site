Shayad Khayal Aaya Hai Bazar-e-Sham Ka
Zainab Ke Thay Yeh Bain Madine Chalo Hussain
Kofa Hamare Rehne Ke Qabil Nahi Raha
Sajjad Bain Karte Hain Dada Hain Khush Naseeb
Beton Ne Mil Ke Baap Ko Ghusal-o-Kafan Diya
Pehla Yeh Imtehan Hai Ghazi Ke Sabar Ka
Abbas Chup Hai Samne Qatil Hai Baap Ka
Zainab Na Ro Abhi Mujhe Aa Jaye Ga Sakoon
Romal La Ke Bandh Do Sar Par Batool Ka
Ik Bibi Chal Rahi Hai Janaze Ke Sath Sath
Lagta Hai Qabar Choor Ke Aayi Hain Fatima
Mula Kyun Khak Aap Ne Dali Hai Zakhm Par
Kya Yaad Aa Gayi Hai Musallay Pe Karbala
Darbar-e-Sham Jaon Ga Zainab Main Tere Sath
AKBAR Yeh Keh Ke Ho Gaye Khamosh Murtaza
