1
Chadar e Fatima Shadman Raza Lyrics
Ya Zahra, Ya Zahra.
Rizq Hai Junbish-e-Chadar-e-Fatima.
Rozi-e-Aalmeen Chadar-e-Syeda.
Aye Madar, Bekas Madar, Mazloom Madar.
Teri Khak-e-Pa Se Na Hum Sar Uthaein.
Wahin Par Ajal Ko Galay Se Lagaein.
Rahe Teri Chadar Ka Saya Saron Par.
Shifat Ke Saye Mein Hum Sar Chupaein.
Falak Par Dilon Ke Tujhi Se Ujala.
Jahano Ko Teri Dua Ne Sanbhala.
Hoon Kaisay Hi Tofaan Hoon Kaisi Qayamat.
Musibat Se Tu Ne Hamesha Nikala.
Yeh Palkein Itaat Ki Daim Bichaein.
Hamesha Issi Dar Pe Yeh Sar Jhokaein.
Ghulamo Ki Khuwaish Dua Our Tamana.
Gharanay Pe Zahra Ke Janein Lutaein.
Ghazab Hai Tere Ghar Ka Woh Dar Jala Tha.
Jo Baab-e-Shifa Tha Jo Baab-e-Atta Tha.
Payamber Bhi Oss Dar Pe Aate They Paihum.
Khuda Ki Riza Tha Jo Baab-e-Khuda Tha.
Addo Se Larein Bhi Ali Ko Bachaya.
Saha Taziyana Tamacha Bhi Khaya.
Wilayat Ki Khatir Hi Mohsin Ganwaya.
Imamat Ke Mansab Ko Phir Bhi Bachaya.
Ali Ke Galay Mein Jo Rassi Bandhi Thi.
Musibat Yeh Zahra Ke Dil Pe Bari Thi.
Na Tha Koi Yawar Ghazab Ki Ghari Thi.
Ali Ke Woh Daman Ko Pakray Khari Thi.
Yeh Mukhlis Pe Our Shadmaan Pe Karam Hain.
Gham-e-Syeda Mein Jo Aankhein Yeh Nam Hain.
Mawaddat Ki Rahoon Ke Yeh Humsafar Hain.
Wilayat Ke Rastay Pe Yeh Hum Qadam Hain.
