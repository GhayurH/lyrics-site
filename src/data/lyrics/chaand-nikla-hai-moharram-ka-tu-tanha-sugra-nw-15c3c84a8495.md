Chaand nikla hai moharram ka tu tanha sugra

dekh kar roti hai beemaar, sar-e-rah sugra

jub nikalta hai moharram main tau sab rotay hain

aik beemaar nazar aati, hai tanha sugra

shaam dhultee hai tau aajatay hain punchee ghar ko

daytee veeraan gharo say hai, yeh sada sugra

teray charnay say ujar jayeyga ghar zahra ka

aaj chup jaa tujhay kehti hai, ye dukhi ya sugra

dil main hai aik tamanna kay milay akber say

raat din karti hai rozay pay, yeh dua sughra

jis tarah bichree hoon main aur na bichray koi

karti nana say hai ro ro kay, yeh shikwa sugra
