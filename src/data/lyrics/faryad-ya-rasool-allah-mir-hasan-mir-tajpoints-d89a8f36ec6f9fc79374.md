Faryad Ya Rasool Allah Mir Hasan Mir
Hazoor.

1
Aap Ki Dukhtar Ka Dar Jalaya Gaya.
Hazoor Hazoor.
Aap Ki Dukhtar Pe Dar Giraya Gaya.

2
Aap Ki Turbat Se Chand Qadmo Par.

3
Aap Ki Masjid Se Chand Qadmo Par.
Hazoor Hazoor.
Aap Ne Aawaz Tu Suni Hogi.

4
Bain Tu Kano Mein Gonjtay Hoon Gay.

5
Shoor Wohi Shoor Qaid Karne Ka.

6
Lakriyaan Darwazay Par Giranay Ka.

7
Shoor Wohi Shoor Baab-e-Hitta Par.

8
Qehqahon Ka Thokaron Ka Dastak Ka.
Hazoor Hazoor.
Shoor Woh Darwazay Ke Ukharne Ka.

9
Bain Tah-e-Dar Hadees-e-Bazat Ka.

10
Bain Wohi Bain Al-Madad Fizza.

11
Shoor Wohi Shoor Taziyane Ka.
Hazoor Hazoor.
Neel Ke Chehray Pe Sabt Hone Ka.

12
Shoor Wohi Shoor Bain Wohi Bain.

13
Zulm Hoye Our Be Tahasha Hoye.

14
Ashk Bahey Our Be Tahasha Bahey.

15
Iss Pe Bhi Ismat Ki Pasdari Rahi.

16
Phir Bhi Ali Ko Nahi Pukara Gaya.
