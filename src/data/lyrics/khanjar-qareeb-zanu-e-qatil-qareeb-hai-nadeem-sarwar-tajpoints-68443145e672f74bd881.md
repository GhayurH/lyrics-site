1
Khanjar Qareeb Zanu-e-Qatil Qareeb Hai Nadeem Sarwar.
Jab Kat Gaya Zahra Ka Chaman Dasht-e-Bala Mein.
Shamil Howa Pyason Ka Lahu Khak-e-Shifa Mein.
Koi Na Raha Foaj-e-Imam-e-Do Sara Mein.
Our Ghir Gaya Haider Ka Pisar Ahl-e-Jafa Mein.
Ik Khushk Galla Our Kayi So Naiza-o-Khanjar.
Aray Nu Lakh Ka Rela-o-Akela Woh Dilawar.
Jab Mera Hussain Fatima Ka Chain.
Pusht-e-Rahwar Se Jab Khak Par Gira.
Khanjar Qareeb Zanu-e-Qatil Qareeb Hai.
Aaj Imtehan-e-Sabar-e-Hussain-e-Ghareeb Hai.
Jaan Se Qareeb Tar Mere Parwardigar Dekh.
Ashiq Ka Apni Raig-e-Tapan Par Qarrar Dekh.
Mera Yaqeen Dekh Mera Aytbar Dekh.
Main Kitna Mutmain Hoon Idhar Aik Bar Dekh.
Pyasa Hoon Teen Roz Ka Zakhmo Se Choor Hoon.
Ya Rab Gawah Rahiyo Keh Main Be Qasoor Hoon.
Che Mah Ho Gaye Keh Watan Se Bhi Door Hoon.
Ab Teri Bargah Mein Tere Hazoor Hoon.
Ik Qalb-e-Na Tawaan Pe Bahatar Ka Dagh Hai.
Atharah Saal Ke Ali Akbar Ka Dagh Hai.
Bazu Bureda Shair Ka Asghar Ka Dagh Hai.
Lagna Abhi Luti Hoi Chadar Ka Dagh Hai.
Yeh Keh Ke Jo Khamosh Hoye Sibt-e-Payamber.
Dekha Ke Sirhaney Hai Khari Madar-e-Muztar.
Shehzadi Ke Sar Par Hai Pari Khak Ki Chadar.
Kehti Hain Woh Sar Gaud Mein Shabeer Ka Le Kar.
Mazloom Ki Imdad Ko Aata Nahi Koi.
Pani Bhi Dam-e-Marg Pilata Nahi Koi.
Zakhmo Pe Lagi Khak Chorata Nahi Koi.
Haye Haye Mere Bachay Ko Bachata Nahi Koi.
Haider Se Kaha Zahra Ne Khof Aata Hai Sahib.
Maa Hoon Mera Dil Dard Se Tharata Hai Sahib.
Yeh Dekhiye Dunya Se Pisar Jata Hai Sahib.
Khanjar Ko Liye Shimar Chala Aata Hai Sahib.
Farmane Lagay Maa Se Basad Ranj Yeh Maula.
Kuch Dair Mein Ab Koch Hai Dunya Se Hamara.
Aye Amma Chali Jaiye Maqtal Se Khudara.
Kis Tarha Se Dekho Gi Tarapta Howa Lasha.
Ik Bar Jo Khaimon Ki Taraf Yaas Se Dekha.
Dekha Ke Dar-e-Khaimay Pe Roti Hai Sakina.
Ik Hook Uthi Qalb Mein Roney Lagay Maula.
Zainab Se Isharon Se Kaha Aye Meri Behna.
Aawaz-e-Gaib Aayi Palat Aa Hussain Aa.
Ab Imtehan Tera Mukamal Yahan Howa.
Khon-e-Pisar Se Tu Hai Wozu Ab Tu Kar Chuka.
Yeh Sun Ke Sheh Ne Sajday Mein Sar Rakh Ke Yeh Kaha.
Jab Pohanchi Behan Bhai Ke Lashay Pe Khulay Sar.
Dekha Keh Laeen Phair Chuka Halq Pe Khanjar.
Our Shimar Liye Jata Hai Sar Gesu Pakar Kar.
Aye:SARWAR-o-REHAN: Karo Yaad Woh Manzar.
