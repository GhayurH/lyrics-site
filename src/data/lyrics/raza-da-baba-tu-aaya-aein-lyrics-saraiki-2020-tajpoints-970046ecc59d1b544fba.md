Raza Da Baba Tu Aaya Aein Lyrics Saraiki
Raza Da Baba Taun Aaya Ayein Tur V Chaliyaan Ayein Dasa Na Maula Kadon Teek Hunr Judaiyaan Ne.

1
Athawin Eidaan Guzar Gayiyaan Intezaraan Wich Aa Wekh Rondiyaan Dhiyaan Diyaan Dohaiyaan Ne.
Ditha Hai Khab Tusaan De Main Aaounr Taun Pailaan Mokanr Nanay Koon Denday Mein Anbiya Wekhay.
Habib Hur Te Abuzar Bilal Qanbar V Tainday Mazar Te Ronday Mein Bilqaza Wekhay.
Sunrey Ne Baqiya Wichon Wainr Dadi Zahra De Siraan Na Paiyaan Ne Khakaan Batool Jaiyaan Ne.

2
Ghareeb Dhiyaan Ne Ro Ro Ke Piyo Taun Puchiya Hay Hathaan Koon Baba Taun Chadar Na Kyun Lukaya Hay.
Aina Hathaan Da Jay Saya Naseeb Nahin Sakoon Khuda De Baad Saday Sir Te Kainda Saya Hay.
Taun Chodah Saal Pichon Aaya Ayein Aj V Qaidi Ayein Dasa Na Baba Aye Zanjeeraan Kain Pawaiyaan Ne.
Mein Kya Dasawaan Taqaza Hai Ain Fitrat Da Aye Piyo Te Dhiyaan Da Rishta Ajeeb Honda Hay.

3
Putar Te Honday Ne Bunyad Baap Daday Di Dhiyaan Da Jag Te Anokha Naseeb Honda Hay.
Samajh Gayiyaan Aye Mulaqaat Aj Akheri Hay Siraan Te Jokhaan Yateemi Ne Aa Wasiyaan Ne.
Taun Baad Mudataan De Aaya Ayein Chor Chaliya Hay Dasa Ke Jawein Keray Dais Hunr Teyari Hay.
Taiday Taun Baad Sakhi Lal Maula Jafar Da Bari Be Chain Asaan Zindagi Guzari Hay.

4
Koi Te Haal Dasa Kyun Khamosh Hay Maula Keray Dina Te Ajaan Honiyaan Rehaiyaan Ne.
Miley Rehai Okon Sadqa Mosa Kazim Da Koi V Qaidi Azadar Jo Tera Howay.
Mila De Sadqa Maidi Sain Di Judai Da Wichar Gaya Jay Kisay Bhainr Da Bhira Howay.
Oh Bibiyaan Diyaan Ghazi Taun Jholiyaan Bhar De Jinna Jinna Ne Tere Dar Te Aasaan Laiyaan Ne.
