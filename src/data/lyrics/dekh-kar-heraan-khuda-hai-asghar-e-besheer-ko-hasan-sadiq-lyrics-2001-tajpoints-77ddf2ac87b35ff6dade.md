1
Dekh Kar Heraan Khuda Hai Asghar e Besheer Ko Hasan Sadiq.
Kar Diya Do Lakht Jis Ne Hurmala Ke Teer Ko.
Charh Ke Naizay Pe Bachaya Tu Ne Asghar Lailaha.
Deen-e-Ahmad Pe Howa Youn Teer Kha Kar Tu Fida.

2
Marhaba Kehna Para Khud Katib-e-Taqdeer Ko.
Qaid Ki Tanhaiyaan Our Iss Pe Zalim Ke Sitam.
Bhool Jaye Gi Laeen Ko Our Kano Ke Zakhm.
Qabar Bholay Gi Na Teri Haye Kabhi Humsheer Ko.

3
Sath Jab Baba Ke Asghar Ko Na Paye Gi Rubab.
Le Ke Khali Gaud Kaisay Sham Jaye Gi Rubab.
Bas Khiyal Aata Hai Maqtal Mein Yehi Shabeer Ko.
Jholta Naizay Pe Jhola Na Yeh Bholay Ga Jahan.

4
Ta Abad Roein Gay Oss Ko Yeh Zameen-o-Aasmaan.
Kar Gaya Na Kaam Zalim Ki Jo Har Tadbeer Ko.
Dekh Kar Asghar Ka Hansna Ro Rahe They Ashqiya.
Dekh Asghar Ki Shujat Jhomtay They Anbiya.

5
Sare Mil Kar Dekhtey They Deen Ki Tanveer Ko.
Aa Rahi Khursheed Hai Ab Bhi Sada-e-Karbala.
Na Chupay Ga Oss Ka Khoon Hai Jo Binaye Lailaha.
Lakh Dunya Yeh Chupaye Khon-e-Be Taqseer Ko.
