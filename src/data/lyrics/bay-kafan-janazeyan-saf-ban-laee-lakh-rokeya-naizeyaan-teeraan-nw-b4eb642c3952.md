Be kafan janazeyaan saf ban layi, lakh rokeya naizeyaan teeraan
Sajjad parhawan aaya ae, Babay diyaan panj takbiraan

۱
Pehli takbir janazay di, kujh hamd hoi kujh wain ae
Sajjad kiya Mabood tere iss abd da naam Hussain ae
Tu wahdahu te ho gaya aeṅ, aidhi luttiyaan kul jageeraan

۲
Duji takbir ch Abid ne kiya, lai Nanay da naam ae
Summaan naal kar pamaal gaye, rahwaar durood salaam ae
Maiṅ khaak chon chun chun joriyaan ne, rehmat diyaan ae tasveeraan

۳
Teeji takbir kiya Abid, dassaaṅ qudrat bhain bhira di
Shah onniyaan laashaan chukkiyaan ne, roi Zainab onni waari
Jinniyaan Tatheer di chadar te ne lahu diyaan paak lakeeraan

۴
Chauthi takbir gawaahi si, aithe aa ke Abid roya
Kiya Maalik ae kam Zainab te Kulsoom de zimme hoya
Hun ja ke gawaahiyaan den giyaan, wich Shaam aidhi hamsheeraan

۵
Panjwi takbir ton baad Akbar ae keh mayyat dafnaai
Kise zaalim ik anguthi layi, us hath di ungli layi
Jis hath naal Baba likhiyaan san duniya diyaan kul taqdeeraan
