Dushwar Kitni Qaid Thi Kazim Imam Ki Hasan Sadiq.
Dekhi Na Kabhi Subha Aayi Na Sham Ki.

1
Haye Kis Qadar Woh Kothari Tang-o-Tareek Thi.
Na Sajdah Hi Ki Ja Thi Na Thi Qiyam Ki.
Kuch Zehar De Ke Marey Our Kuch Qatal Kiye.
Tazeem Khoob Ki Hai Khair Al Anam Ki.

2
Zanjeer-o-Tauq Tu Rag-o-Pay Mein Paiwast They.
Mazdoor Le Ke Aaye Maiyyat Imam Ki.
 Najfi Adab Se Chom Le Beri Malang Ki.
Jo Aaj Tak Batati Hai Tareekh Sham Ki.
