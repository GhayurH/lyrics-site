Mureed Hoon Ali Ka Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.

1
Mujhe Dhondti Hai Janat Main Mureed Hoon Ali Ka.
Meri Aouj Par Hai Qismat Main Mureed Hoon Ali Ka.
Koi Yeh Zabaan Kata Kar Sar-e-Daar Keh Raha Tha.
Na Ghatay Gi Meri Himmat Main Mureed Hoon Ali Ka.
Main Tasawar-e-Najaf Mein Abhi Ayetakaf Mein Hoon.
Mujhe Karne Do Ibadat Main Mureed Hoon Ali Ka.

2
Bhala Kya Nazar Mein Laon Tere Takht-o-Taj Ko Main.
Marey Pas Hai Yeh Dolat Main Mureed Hoon Ali Ka.
Mere Qad Se Bhi Barey Hain Mere Naaz Uthane Walay.
Hai Qadam Qadam Pe Izzat Main Mureed Hoon Ali Ka.
Meri Qabar Mein Farishtay Marey Hont Chom Lein Gay.
Jo Sunein Gay Zair-e-Turbat Main Mureed Hoon Ali Ka.

3
Sar-e-Aam Karna Lanat Tere Qatilon Pe Zahra.
Hai Azal Se Meri Adat Main Mureed Hoon Ali Ka.
Jahan Sainkaron Farishtay Hain Magan Ali Ali Mein.
Hai Wahan Bhi Meri Shohrat Main Mureed Hoon Ali Ka.
Mujhe Our Chahiye Kya Bhala Zindagi Mein Akhtar.
Hai Khuda Ki Yeh Inayat Main Mureed Hoon Ali Ka.
