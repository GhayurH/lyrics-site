1
Zakhm Chupana Mir Hasan Mir Lyrics
Akbar Ne Sina Khayi Hai Unn Ko Na Batana.
Baba Phophi Amma Se Mera Zakhm Chupana.
Kis Tarha Se Dekhein Gi Woh Hum Dono Ki Ghurbat.

2
Woh Roein Tu Aa Jaye Gi Khaimon Mein Qayamat.
Main Zakhm Chupa Leta Hoon Tum Aanso Chupana.
Tasveer-e-Muhammad Hai Barey Nazoon Se Pali.
Mar Jaye Na Khaimay Mein Meri Palney Wali.

3
Hargiz Yeh Mera Hath Na Seene Se Hatana.
Mari Hai Sina Seene Pe Yeh Keh Ke Laeen Ne.
Pholon Ki Tarha Pala Hai Akbar Ko Phophi Ne.
Lashay Pe Abba Khaimon Se Pehle Hi Urhana.

4
Iss Bar-e-Shahadat Ko Utha Le Gi Imamat.
Bhai Se Chupana Nahi Ik Bhai Ki Ghurbat.
Sajjad Ko Tafseer Shahadat Ki Sunana.
Mamta Unhein Lashay Pe Mere Layegi Baba.

5
Be Sakhta Woh Mujh Se Lipat Jaein Gi Baba.
Mumkin Ho Tu Lashay Se Unhein Door Hatana.
Jo Khak Bhari Hai Mere Baloon Se Chura Do.
Chehray Pe Lahu Hai Mere Daman Se Hata Do.

6
Woh Zid Jo Zyada Karein Bas Chehra Dikhana.
Youn Naiza Sitamgar Ne Mara Mere Baba.
Pehlu Bhi Shikasta Hai Hamara Mere Baba.
Yaad Aaye Ga Dadi Ka Unhein Zakhm Porana.

7
Iss Haal Mein De Sabar Khuda Bint-e-Ali Ko.
Akbar Ka Salam Aakhri Keh Dena Phophi Ko.
Zindah Bara Mushkil Hai Mera Khaimay Mein Jana.
Har Baat Meri Aap Ki Behno Ke Liye Hai.

8
Bas Aik Wasiyat Meri Sughra Ke Liye Hai.
La Kar Khat-e-Sughra Mere Lashay Pe Sunana.
Dam Tortay Akbar Ko Na Pohanchay Koi Sadma.
Munh Phair Ke Youn Rotay Ho Maloom Hai Baba.

9
Par Samne Sadaat Ke Aanso Na Bahana.
Oss Aakhri Hichki Mein Takallum Howa Mehshar.
Akbar Ne Kaha Hath Jo Peshani Pe Rakh Kar.
Lo Hum Ko Ijazat Do Humein Door Hai Jana.
