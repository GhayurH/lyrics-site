Abbas Abbas Meri Madad Ko Aao Na Mesum Abbas.

1
Totay Dilon Ki Aas Aye Mere Maula Abbas.

2
Yeh Dunya Sitam Jab Dhati Hai Ya Koi Musibat Aati Hai.
Bas Tere Alam Ke Panjay Par Yeh Meri Nazar Ruk Jati Hai.
Abbas Abbas Har Gham Se Churao Na.
Main Doob Raha Hoon Aye Maula Jeene Ka Nahi Koi Rasta.
Jab Tum Ko Bulata Hai Koi Tum Oss Ko Bachatay Ho Maula.
Abbas Abbas Mujh Ko Bhi Bachao Na.
Sab Karb-o-Bala Ko Jate Hain Our Dil Ki Muradein Patay Hain.
Ik Main Hi Tarasta Hoon Maula Kyun Aap Mujhe Tarpatay Hain.
Abbas Abbas Zawwar Banao Na.
Yeh Khuwab Dikhaya Hai Tum Ne Rozay Pe Bulaya Hai Tum Ne.
Rozay Se Lipat Kar Sochoon Ga Seene Se Lagaya Hai Tum Ne.
Abbas Abbas Seene Se Lagao Na.
Jab Aath 8 Muharram Aati Hai Maa Saqqa Mujhe Banwati Hai.
Jis Ghar Mein Hazari Hoti Hai Wahan Teri Sawari Aati Hai.
Abbas Abbas Mere Ghar Bhi Tu Aao Na.
Girton Ko Sahara Detey Ho Dobay Tu Kinara Detey Ho.
Jab Toot Raha Ho Koi Dil Tum Hi Tu Dilasa Detey Ho.
Abbas Abbas Girta Hoon Uthao Na.
Zainab Ki Sada Yaad Aati Hai Abbas Sakina Pyasi Hai.
Dil Tukray Hoye Jata Hai Mera Woh Sokhi Zabaan Dikhlati Hai.
Abbas Abbas Ossay Pani Pilao Na.
Kartay Hain Boka Mesum, Mazhar Aata Hai Yaad Wohi Manzar.
Hal Min Ki Sadaein Detey Hoye Shabbir Yeh Kehte Hain Ro Kar.
