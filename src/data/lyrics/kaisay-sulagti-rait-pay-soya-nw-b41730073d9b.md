Kaisai sulgti rait pay soya mera Asghar.

Yeh soach kay roti rahi  baysheer ki maadar.

Sehra main goonjti thi Sakina ki sadaayain
Bhartee thi wo aahain
Lay aaye hoon paani main kahan hai tu biraadar.

Masooma lagi ronay Baba ko lipat kay
Hai Shimar kay dar sai
Lehra ta howa khanjar aya jo sitamghar.

Sughra say koyi kehday deeyai saaray bujhaa day
Akbar ko bula day
Aik zakam laga kaari Akbar kay jigar paar.

Hai waqt-e-wida shah nay apno ko pukara
Koyi day day sahara
Ghoray pay charhoon kaisay, Qasim hai na Akbar.

Thay chak giray ban, rotay thay farishtay
Matam ta arash pai
Jab Sibt-e-Payamber pai, chala Shimar ka khanjar.

Yaad Aa gayi Abbas ki, Haye Bint-e-Ali ko
Zahra ki kaali ko
Naizay say utari jab, bay deen nay chadar.
