Shahe Shaheedan Shahe Shaheedan

roohe shariyat saahibe quran

teri shahadat faqre rasoolan

shahe shaheedan….

gardan par shamsheer-e-sitamgar

khoon may dooba sur ka manzar

har pe tashakkar khushk labon par

taygh talay bhi sajdaye yazdan

shahe shaheedan….

koi barchi ka phal khakar

koi galay par teer talakar

koi soye haath katakar

dashte bala may maut hai rakhsa

shahe shaheedan….

phhol se rukhsaron pe tamachay

dekho zaalim aise maaray

bahci ka dum ruk ruk jaaye

usko rulaye sham ka zindan

shahe shaheedan….

suraj par andhiyare chaaye

qaima qaima larza sholay

sehmay sehmay pyase bachay

sekh rahe hai sham-e-ghariban

shahe shaheedan….

phail gaya jab sham ka saaya

maqsad ki takmeel ka bayda

teri hi khaahar ne uthaaya

ashoora ta hashr ka maidan

shahe shaheedan….

khoon ki cheeton se ta zinda

shahid maqtal ka har zarra

apni khilqat par sharminda

ik-sat hijri sar ba girayban

shahe shaheedan….
