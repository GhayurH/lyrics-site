Roothne Wale Ko Kis Dil Se Manane Jaein.
Naye Mil Jaein Agar Zakhm Porane Jaein.

1
Mina Na Barse Tu Rahe Piyas Se Dharti Be-Kul.
Barshein Hoon Tu Parindon Ke Thikane Jaein.
Woh Jahangir Na INsaf Waisa Logo.
Hum Kahan Adal Ki Zanjeer Hilane Jaein.

2
Neend Bedari Se Behter Hai Keh Aaram Se Hoon.
Aankh Khul Jaye Tu Sab Khuwab Sohane Jaein.
Hai Wo Andoh-e-Dil-o-Jaan Ki Nishani Phir Bhi.
Roz Oss Ki Qabar Pe Hum Phool Charhne Jaein.

3
Aise Pagal Bhi Nahi Chahne Wale Tere.
Haal Dunya Ko Gham-e-Dil Ka Sonane Jaein.
Shehar-e-Khobaan Mein Muhabbat Ki Agar Aye Baghi.
Barishein Hoon Tu Kabhi Hum Bhi Nahane Jaein.
