Churwa Do Humein Gham Se Yehi Waqt-e-Karam Hai
Ya Fatima Zahra Tumhein Bachoon Ki Qasam Hai
Aoulad Ki Naimat Se Hain Mehroom Jo Maein
Be Sheer Ke Jholay Pe Woh Karti Hain Duaein
Umeed-e Karam Aap Ka Hi Baab-e-Karam Hai
Har Ranj-o-Bala Door Rahe Hai Yehi Faryad
Shabeer Ke Matam Ke Liye Woh Rahe Aabad
Abbas-e-Alamdar Ka Jis Ghar Pe Alam Hai
Rizwan-o-Malaik Hain Palay Aap Ke Dar Se
Kyun Bandah-e-Momin Bhala Phir Rizq Ko Tarsay
Hatam Ka Nahi Yeh Tu Dar-e-Shah-e-Ummam Hai
Sajjad Ke Sadqay Ossay Aazad Kara Dein
Sughra Ke Tasaduq Ossay Apno Se Mila Dein
Momin Jo Koi Qaid Mein Be Jurm-o-Sitam Hai
Dena Hai Mujhe Aap Ko Shabeer Ka Pursa
Bibi Mujhe Jaldi Se Bola Lijay Madina
Sansein Yeh Batati Hain Keh Ab Waqt Bhi Kam Hai
Khushiyon Ki Talab Aap Se Ki Hai Na Karein Gaye
Hum Aap Ki Marzi Pe Raza Mand Rahein Gaye
Darkar Humein Aap Se Shabeer Ka Gham Hai
Hai Meri Dua Aap Se Aye Malka-e-Janat
Sar Apna Utha Kar Woh Chaley Taba Qayamat
Jis Jis Ki Jabeen Aap Ki Dehleez Pe Khum Hai
Aye Kafoye Ali Jald Unhein Kafo Atta Kar
Arsay Se Hain Bethi Hoi Kuch Betiyaan Ghar Par
Iss Baat Ka Maa Baap Ko Har Waqt Alam Hai
Mehdi Se Lehad Aap Ki Dekhi Nahi Jati
Ab Pardah-e-Ghaibat Se Unhein Bhaij Do Bibi
Yeh Meri Duaon Mein Dua Sab Se Aham Hai
Yeh Kehte Hain Beemar Keh Minber Se Dua Do
Tum Zakir-e-Sarwar Ho Shifa Hum Ko Dila Do
Bibi Tere Hathoon Mein TAKALLUM Ka Bharam Hai
