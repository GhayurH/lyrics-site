1
Yaar Kuch Bhi Ho Yaar Hota Hai.
Sath Oss Ke Jo Hai Raqeeb Tu Kya.
Phool Ke Sath Khaar Hota Hai.
Jab Woh Hote Hain Sehan-e-Gulshan Mein.
Mosam-e-Nu Bahar Hota Hai.
Kash Hote Hum Oss Ke Pholoon Mein.
Oss Galay Ka Jo Haar Hota Hai.
Dost Se Kyun Bhala Na Khate Faraib.
Dost Pe Aytbar Hota Hai.
Jab Wo Aate Nahi Shab-e-Wadah.
Mauth Ka Intezar Hota Hai.
Wasal Mein Bhi Khiyal-e-Hijar Se Dil.
Be-Sakoon Be-Qarar Hota Hai.
Hum Bare Khush Naseeb Hain Warna.
Aap Ko Kis Se Pyar Hota Hai.
Teer Woh Teer Neem Kash Tu Nahi.
Dil Ke Jo Aar Par Hota Hai.
Husan-e-Ikhlaq Aye Roos-e-Hayat.
Sab Se Acha Singhar Hota Hai.
Ishq Ki Kainat Ka Purnam.
Husan Parwardigar Hota Hai.
