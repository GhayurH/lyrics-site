1
Masooma-e-Qum Ki Azmat Kya Kare Koi Biyaan.
Iss Ki Chokhat Par Paye Sajda Hai Jhukta Aasmaan.
Fatima Hai Naam Our Seerat Mein Bhi Hain Fatima.
Sani Dunya Mein Koi Masooma-e-Qum Ka Kahan.

2
Aap Ka Roza Jo Dekha Pa Liya Be-Had Sakoon.
Koi Khuwaish Na Rahi bas Ab Nikal Jaye Yeh Jaan.
Aap Ki Midhat Jo Chairi Khush Hoye Kuch Our Kahin.
Jal Gaye Dil Itne Ke Uthne Laga Bibi Dhuwaan.

3
Shahzadi Hajtein Dil Mein Liye Aaya Hoon Main.
Mujh Ko Ghairay Hai Mosibat Dijiye Bibi Amaan.
Mosa-e-Kazim Ki Dukhtar Ka Karam Afraz Par.
Khadam-e-Haram-e-Raza Iss Ko Kahe Har Ik Zabaan.
