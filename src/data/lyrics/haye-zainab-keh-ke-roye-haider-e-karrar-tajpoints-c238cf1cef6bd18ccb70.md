Haye Zainab Keh Ke Roye Haider-e-Karrar.

1
Khon Pusht-e-Sar Se Jari Tha Magar Har Bar.

2
Rotay Rotay Gardan-e-Shabeer Ko Dekha.
Aah Bhar Kar Chadar-e-Tatheer Ko Dekha.
Ho Rahi Hai Karbala Bhi Sham Bhi Teyar.
Lo Yateemi Zainab-o-Kulsoom Pe Chayi.
Kuch Tabeebon Ki Tababat Kam Na Aayi.
Ibn-e-Muljim Ne Kiya Tha Sar Pe Aisa Waar.
Jab Pilaya Jam-e-Shereen Ibn-e-Muljim Ko.
Tashnagi Yaad Aa Gayi Karbal Ki Maula Ko.
Bazu-e-Abbas Pe Karne Lagay Jab Pyar.
Chom Kar Beti Ka Sar Phir Hath Ko Choma.
Karbala Se Sham Paidal Jaye Gi Dukhiya.
Aablay Paon Mein Hoon Gaye Aablon Mein Khar.
Zindagi Zahra Ko Rotay Kat Gayi Maula.
Sham Jane Our Beti Aap Ki Maula.
Aap Ke Iss Dard Pe Rotay Hain Matamdar.
Bolein Fizza Ghar Mein Jaldi Aaiye Maula.
Ro Rahi Hain Fatima Ki Betiyaan Maula.
Ghar Se Bahir Aa Na Jaein Haye Pardah Daar.
Rukhsat-e-Haider Ki Haider Kiya Ghari Ho Gi.
Maut Bhi Maula Ke Paon Chomti Ho Gi.
Hain Ali Khaliq Se Milne Ke Liye Teyar.
