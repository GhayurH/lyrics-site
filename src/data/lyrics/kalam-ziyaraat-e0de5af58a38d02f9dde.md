Nalay kartay thay Sheh e Karb o bala wa waila
Bhai darya pe mera mara gaya wa waila

1
Aaj bin bhai ka may ho gaya is sehra may
Qatl bhai mera aada ne kiya wa waila

2
Jis ki khaatir meray bhai ne kataye baazo
Mashk woh chid gayi aur paani bahaa wa waila

3
Dhara e ahle haram quwwate bazo Abbas
Koi pardes may mera na raha wa waila

4
Foaj e Shabbir ka thanda hua raiti pe alam
Ho gaya khatama e foaj e Khuda wa waila

5
Kis ka matam karon noha parrhon kis ko royon
Haey ghurbat may huay sab hi juda wa waila

6
Le ke halqay may alam laash ke badlay Zainab
Bibi kar kar karo faryad o buka wa waila

7
Kon halmin ki sada sun ke bachaye ga mujhe
Qatl honay lagay Akbar bhi chala wa waila

8
Dilbar e Fatima ki jaisi tamanna thi Anees
Waisay hi kartay hain ye ahle aza wa waila
