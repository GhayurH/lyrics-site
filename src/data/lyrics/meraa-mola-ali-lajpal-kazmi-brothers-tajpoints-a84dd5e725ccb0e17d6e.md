Meraa Mola Ali Lajpal Kazmi Brothers.

1
Ali Ali Haq, Ali Ali Haq Ali Mola, Ali Maula.
Jind Ali Meri Jan Ali Meri Shan Ali Pehchan Ali.
Nam-e-Ali Hai Naam Khuda Ka Sab Se Behtar Sab Se Aala.
Ewein Nayi Zamana Sara Ali Nu Pukarda.
Mera Maula Ali Lajpal, Mera Maula Ali Lajpal.
TAJpoINT.com.

2
Ali Kabay Mein Utra Kibriya Ke.
Ali Kabay Se Nikla But Gira Ke.
Ali Ne Maslay Hal Kar Deiye Hain.
Rasolon Ke Muhammad Ke Khuda Ke.
Meesum, Abuzar, Salmaan Parhtay Hain Kalma.
Ali Makhlooq Mein Aagaz-o-Awwal.

3
Ali Par Deen Khaliq Ka Mukamal.
Sakhi Ki Badshahi Hai Abhi Tak.
Karam Maula Ka Jari Hai Musalsal.
Zahra Ka Shohar Haider Do Jag Ka Data.
Hum Unn Ka Behar-e-Musibat Mein Naam Letey Hain.
Jo Dobtay Hoye Beray Ko Tham Letey Hain.

4
Khusha Naseeb Ke Aaqa Ki Ik Ziyarat Mein.
Sawab Hajj Ka Ali Ke Ghulam Letey Hain.
TAJpoINT.com.
Gawahi Dein Gay Khud Sare Payamber.
Badal Jaye Ga Tera Bhi Muqadar.
Bulandi Phir Teri Qismat Mein Hogi.

5
Adab Se Jhuk Marey Maula Ke Dar Par.
Sajdah Ada Kar Ke Kehta Hai Zohrah.
Nabiyon Ka Yeh Uqdah Kusha Aadam Ka Pehla Modua.
Nooh-e-Niji Ka Aasra Yaqoob Ke Dil Ki Dua.
Haan Aye Adoy-e-Murtaza Haider Ka Tu Humsar Dikha.
Jis Ko Khuda Banda Kahey Banda Kahey Jis Ko Khuda.

6
Haq Eliya, Haq Eliya, Haq Eliya, Haq Elia.
Ali Ka Aastana Aas Meri.
Ali Se Unsiyat Hai Khas Meri.
Ali Meri Duaein Sun Raha Hai.
Sifarish Hai Sakhi Abbas Meri.
Deta Hai Sab Ko Maula Bachoon Ka Sadqa.

7
Mushkil Kusha Maula Ali Hasanat Jameeo Khisalehi.
Shair-e-Khuda Kul Ke Wali Salu Alehe Wa Aalehi.
Kabhi Dewar Hilti Hai Kabhi Dar Kanp Jata Hai.
Ali Ka Naam Sun Kar Ab Bhi Khaybar Kanp Jata Hai.
TAJpoINT.com.
Karam Hum Par Hai Yeh Bint-e-Nabi Ka.

8
Ali Ka Kha Ke Gatay Hain Ali Ka.
Khudaya Yeh Tera Ahsan Hum Par.
Humein Muhtaj Na Rakha Kissi Ka.
Aik Hi Takallum Apna Waris Waseela.
Haq Di Nigawaan Wich Sukh Diyaan Sanwaan Wich.
Rakhay Rab Sariyaan Nu Ali Di Panawaan Wich.
