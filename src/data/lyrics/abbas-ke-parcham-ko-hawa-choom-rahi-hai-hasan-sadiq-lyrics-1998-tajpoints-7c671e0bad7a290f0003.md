Abbas Ke Parcham Ko Hawa Choom Rahi Hai Hasan Sadiq.
Lagta Hai Ke Zahra Ki Dua Chom Rahi Hai.

1
Aa Dekh Sakina Tere Abbas Ka Lasha.
Darya Ke Kinaray Pe Qaza Chom Rahi Hai.
Youn Bheegti Jati Hai Fiza Cho Ke Alam Ko.
Jaisay Ke Yeh Zainab Ki Rida Chom Rahi Hai.

2
Sar Apna Patakta Hai Kinaron Pe Yeh Pani.
Ja Arsh Ko Matam Ki Sada Chom Rahi Hai.
Behta Howa Pani Hai Keh Abbas Ke Aanso.
Mashkezay Ko Ummat Ki Jafa Chom Rahi Hai.

3
Sar Chom Ke Ghazi Ka Kaha Ibn-e-Ali Ne.
Abbas Qadam Tere Wafa Chom Rahi Hai.
 Jawad Yeh Noha Hai Shehanshah-e-Wafa Ka.
Har Lafz Ko Ashkon Ki Ghata Chom Rahi Hai.
