Keh Raha Hai Yehi Khuda-e-Rasool.
Sari Dunya Bani Bara-e-Rasool.

1
Main Bhi Sidra Ke Paar Jaon Ga.
Gar Main Ban Jaon Khaak-e-Pay-e-Rasool.
Apne Markaz Ko Choor De Soraj.
Apni Janib Agar Bolaye Rasool.

2
Oss Ke Dil Mein Samaye Kaise Khuda?
Jis Ke Dil Mein Nahi Samaye Rasool.
Tum Ne Oss Ko Bana Diya Ustad?
Chomta Hai Jo Naqsh–e-Paye Rasool.

3
Jitni Tauheed Ki Bulandi Hai.
Nazar Utne Buland Aye Rasool.
Naaz Un Ke Na Kyun Uthaye Khuda.
Jin Ko Kandhon Pe Khud Uthaye Rasool.

4
Sirf Haider Hi Mera Waris Hai.
Khum Se Atti Hai Ye Sada-e-Rasool.
Naat Kehne Laga Hoon Main Raqib.
Kuch Nahi Yeh Bajiz Atta-e-Rasool.
