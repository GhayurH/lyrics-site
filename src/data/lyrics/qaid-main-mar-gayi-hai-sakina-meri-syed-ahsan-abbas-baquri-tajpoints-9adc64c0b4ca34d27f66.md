1
Qaid Main Mar Gayi Hai Sakina Meri Syed Ahsan Abbas Baquri.
Bolay Sajjad Yeh, Qaid-e-Tanhai Mein, Haye Ab Chal Basi Hai Sakina Meri.
Pani Lainay Tha Bhaija Chacha Jan Ko.
Mashk Mein Lag Gaya Teer Aaye Na Woh.

2
Oss Ko Qatil Chacha Ka Satata Raha.
Shimar Rukh Par Tamachay Lagata Raha.
Naam Pani Ka Ab, Lab Pe Lati Nahi,
Itni Sehmi Hoi Hai Sakina Meri.

3
Hath Pehlu Pe Dadi Ki Tarha Rakhay.
Youn Musayab Safar Mein Hain Iss Ko Miley.
Thori Thori Safaidi Bhi Baloon Mein Hai.
Itnay Gham Sham Walon Ne Iss Ko Diye.

4
Chaar Barson Mein Hi, Borhi Zindan Tak,
Haye Ab Ho Chaki Hai Sakina Meri.
Jab Bhi Urtay Parinday Nazar Aate Hain.
Pochti Hai Yeh Bhaiyya Kahan Jate Hain.

5
Kab Watan Ja Ke Sughra Se Mil Paein Gay.
Qaid Se Chut Ke Kab Apne Ghar Jaein Gay.
Qaid Khane Mein Main, Rota Hoon Dair Tak,
Mujh Se Jab Pochti Hai Sakina Meri.

6
Tauq-o-Zanjeer Se Barh Ke Ik Dukh Hai Yeh.
Har Ghari Iss Liye Khon Rota Hoon Keh.
Sham-o-Kofa Ke Darbaron Bazaron Mein.
Bheer Mein Be Hayaon Mein Maykharon Mein.

7
Dar Badar Iss Qadar, Qaidi-o-Be Rida,.
Kyun Phirai Gayi Hai Sakina Meri.
Maa Se Bani Ko Youn Door Rakha Gaya.
Keh Ossay Ik Alag Qaid Khana Mila.

8
Siskiyaan Le Ke Woh Aahein Bharti Rahi.
Yaad Baba Ko Ro Ro Ke Karti Rahi.
Dair Se Qaid Mein, Kitni Khamoshi Hai,
Lagta Hai So Rahi Hai Sakina Meri.

9
Aa Ke Wapis Watan Mein Bataney Lagay.
Zakhm Seene Mein They Jo Dikhaney Lagay.
Pocha Sughra Ne Jonhi Sakina Ka Tu.
Ro Ke Sughra Ko Seene Lagane Lagay.

10
Bolay Sajjad Phir, Qabar Mein Ja Ke Bhi,
Rasiyon Se Bandhi Hai Sakina Meri.
Sar Jab Ahsan Mila Qaid Mein Baap Ka.
Baat Asad Ro Ke Bibi Yeh Kehne Lagi.

11
Baba Tum Se Bichar Kar Guzar Jaon Gi.
Baba Teri Judai Mein Mar Jaon Gi.
Qaid Khane Mein Phir, Tanha Sajjad Ko,
Chor Kar Mar Gayi Hai Sakina Meri.
