Haye Qayamat Ka Hai Samaa, Chane Lagi Sham-E-Garibaan

Haye Qayamat ka hai samaa

Khemaa-E-Sadaat Se Otha Dhowaa, Haye Qayamat ka hai samaa

Dhal Gaya Din Khatm Larayi Hoyi, Maut Ki Khamoshi Hai Chahi Hoyi

Chaak Girebaan Khudae Hoyi, Khak Basar Fatima Jayi Hoyi,

Larzi Zamee Kanp Otha Aasmaa, Haye qayamat ka hai sama

Noor-E-Nazar Raj Dulare Gaye, Zainab-E-Dilgeer Ke Pyare Gaye

Shah Ke Ansaar Bhi Mare Gaye, Aas Thee Jin Per Who Sahare Gaye,

Loot Liya Maut Ne Yeh Karwaan, Haye qayamat ka hai sama

Khun-E-Haider Sher-E-Jari Ka Alam, Ho Gaya Shabbir Ka Sir Bhi Qalam

Khooshiyaan Manane Lagi Fauj-E-Sitam, Gum Se Tabah Haal Hai Ahle Haram

Lot Gayi Kauneen Ki Shah Zaadiyaan, Haye qayamat ka hai samaa

Aun O Mohammed Nahi Akber Nahi, Sarwar O Abbas Dilawar Nahi

Haye Koi Moonis O Yawar Nahi, Khemai Nahi Makna O Chadar Nahi

Dasht Main Hai Aal-E-Nabi Be Amaa, Haye qayamat ka hai samaa

Madar-E-Akber Ka Ajab Haal Hai , Deta Hai Jab Koi Tassali Usse

Kahti Hai Dil Thaam Ke Roote Howe, Kho Gaye Is Ban Main Sahare Mere

Mar Gaya Haye Mera Karyal Jawaa, Haye qayamat ka hai samaa

Maa Hoon Har Ek Ranj Othaoon Gi Main, Rote Howe Khud Chali Jaaooon Gi Main

Dhond Ke Be-Sheer Ko Laaoon Gi Main, Us Ke Bina Jee Nahi Pa-Oon Gi Main

Rah Gaya Haye Mera Baccha Kahaan, Haye qayamt ka hai samaa

Haye Yeh Be-Chargi Yeh Be-Kasi, Deta Nahi Us Ko Dilaasa Koi

Laashoon Pe Akele Khari, Kahti Hai Bacchi Koi Sahmi Hoyi

Dhondne Jaaon Tumhe Baba Kahaan, Haye qayamat ka hai samaa
