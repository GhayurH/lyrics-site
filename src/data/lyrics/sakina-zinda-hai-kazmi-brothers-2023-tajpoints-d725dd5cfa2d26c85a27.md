1
Sakina Zinda Hai Kazmi Brothers 2023.
Mila Nahi Tera Seena Sakina Zindah Hai.
Tamachay Kha Ke Bhi Baba Sakina Zindah Hai.
Laeen Ne Dur Ko Utara Nahi Hai Khaincha Hai.
Ossay Bataiye Bhaiyya Sakina Zindah Hai.
Woh Dekh Aayi Hai Baba Ki Lash Maqtal Mein.
Na Jane Kaisay Khudaya Sakina Zindah Hai.
Katay Saron Ki Sada Thi Yeh Sham-e-Ghurbat Mein.
Na Mar Shimar Khudara Sakina Zindah Hai.
Woh Nook-e-Naiza Se Asghar Ka Sar Gira Detey.
Kabhi Jo Dekhna Hota Sakina Zindah Hai.
Ossay Batool Se Nisbat Hai Dard Sehney Mein.
Keh Neela Ho Gaya Chehra Sakina Zindah Hai.
Hain Oss Ke Charon Taraf Oss Ke Baap Ke Qatil.
Sitam Ke Bheer Mein Tanha Sakina Zindah Hai.
Woh Bar Bar Ossay Aount Se Girata Raha.
Laeen Dekh Raha Tha Sakina Zindah Hai.
Bazar-e-Sham Mein Gash Kha Keh Jab Gira Abid.
Yeh Sun Keh Hosh Mein Aaya Sakina Zindah Hai.
Hai Siskiyon Ki Sada Ab Bhi Sham Mein Ahsan.
Abhi Bhi Lagta Hai Aisa Sakina Zindah Hai.
