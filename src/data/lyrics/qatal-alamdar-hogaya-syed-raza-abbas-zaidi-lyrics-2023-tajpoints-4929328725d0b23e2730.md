Qatal Alamdar Hogaya Syed Raza Abbas Zaidi
Zainab Ki Nazar Fizza Par Fizza Ki Nazar Hussain Par Hussain Ki Nazar Alam Par.

1
Alam Gira Hussain Gir Gaye Fizza Girein Zainab Girein.
Gonji Maqtal Mein Yeh Hussain Ki Sada Haye Hum Ghareeb Ho Gaye.
Qatal Alamdar Ho Gaya Haye Hum Ghareeb Ho Gaye.
Shair Jaisa Bhai Tha Mera Haye Hum Ghareeb Ho Gaye.

2
Hum Ko Yeh Gham Hai Tum Ko Sahara Na De Sakay.
Aisay Tu Koi Bhi Na Gira Jaisay Tum Girey.
Ghorey Se Girtay Hoye Haye Alamdar.
Bazu Nahi They Tere Haye Alamdar.

3
Kis Ne Sanbhala Tujhe Haye Alamdar.
Chehra Tera Zakhmi Ho Gaya Haye Hum Ghareeb Ho Gaye.
Socha Nahi Tha Aisay Pukaro Gay Tum Mujhe.
Arman Tha Lehad Mein Utaro Gay Tum Mujhe.

4
Yaad Hai Bachpan Humein Haye Alamdar.
Nazoon Se Pala Tumhein Haye Alamdar.
Aaj Meri Gaud Mein Haye Alamdar.
Bhai Tera Dam Nikal Gaya Haye Hum Ghareeb Ho Gaye.

5
Doba Howa Lahu Mein Woh Dekhey Gi Jab Alam.
Pyasi Sakina Kaisay Sahey Gi Tumhara Gham.
Bethi Hai Iss Aas Pe Haye Alamdar.
Pani Chacha Laein Gay Haye Alamdar.

6
Kaisay Bataon Ossay Haye Alamdar.
Marey Gaye Nehar Pe Chacha Haye Hum Ghareeb Ho Gaye.
Abbas Teri Behnein Tere Gham Mein Roein Gi.
Soti Thin Tere Dam Se Jo Aankhein Na Soein Gi.

7
Behno Ko Yeh Maan Tha Haye Alamdar.
Zindah Hai Bhai Mera Haye Alamdar.
Dhondein Gi Chehra Tera Haye Alamdar.
Mar De Ga Unn Ko Gham Tera Haye Hum Ghareeb Ho Gaye.

8
Tum Jantay They Hum Mein Nahi Hai Yeh Hosla.
Lasha Bhi Apna Tum Ne Uthane Nahi Diya.
Itni Ijazat Tu De Haye Alamdar.
Bazu Utha Loon Tere Haye Alamdar.

9
Kaisay Sameto Tujhe Haye Alamdar.
Tu Kahan Kahan Bikhar Gaya Haye Hum Ghareeb Ho Gaye.
Uthtey Hoye Hussain Se Utha Nahi Gaya.
Aao Humein Sahara Do Akbar Se Yeh Kaha.

10
Chor Ke Lasha Uthay Haye Alamdar.
Khaimon Ki Janib Chaley Haye Alamdar.
Kehte They Sar Peet Ke Haye Alamdar.
Dekh Aye Zameen-e-Karbala Haye Hum Ghareeb Ho Gaye.

11
Khaimon Mein Aa Ke Shah Ne Zainab Se Jab Kaha.
Dharas Thi Jis Ke Dam Se Woh Abbas Mar Gaya.
Sun Ke Yeh Aah-o-Fogaan Haye Alamdar.
Kehne Lagin Bibiyaan Haye Alamdar.

12
Chor Gaye Tum Kahan Haye Alamdar.
Kaun Ab Bachaye Ga Rida Haye Hum Ghareeb Ho Gaye.
Ghazi Ki Lash Laye Hoon Jaisay Shah e Zaman.
Aisay Alam Ko Dekh Ke Lipati Thi Har Bahan.

13
Dekh Ke Apni Rida Haye Alamdar.
Behno Ne Ro Kar Kaha Haye Alamdar.
Hum Se Juda Ho Gaya Haye Alamdar.
Bain They Zeeshan Our Raza Haye Hum Ghareeb Ho Gaye.
