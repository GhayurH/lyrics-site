Nusrat Ko Hum Jaein Gaye
Jab Bhi Mula Aaein Gaye
Labaik Ya Mehdi Labaik Ya Mehdi
Batil Ko Taraj Karein Gaye
Sheh Ke Nokar Raaj Karein Gaye

1
Shah-e-Najaf Ke Chahne Walo
Ishq-e-Muhammad Ke Matwalo
Pakeeza Maon Ke Jaiyalo
Aazadi Ke Jaam Uchalo
Tofano Mein Ooab Raha Hai
Takht-e-Saudi Doob Raha Hai

2
Shair-e-Khuda Ke Shair Ke Dar Se
Taig Do Paikar Ke Mehshar Se
Shirk Ke Do Tair Be-Par Se
Qabar-e-Nabi Ke Idhar Udhar Se
Tagoti Purze Ukhrein Gaye
Garay Hoye Murday Ukhrein Gaye

3
Sath Mein Dast-e-Buredah Ho Ga
Ashoray Ka Badla Ho Ga
Taig-e-Ali Ka Jalwa Ho Ga
Bas Hamlay Pe Hamla Ho Ga
Haider Ka Akas Laray Ga
Youn Samjho Abbas Laray Ga

4
Ahl-e-Haq Ayjad Hansein Gaye
Gham Khurda Afrad Hansein Gaye
Jurat Ke Folad Hansein Gaye
Khud Mula Sajad Hansein Gaye
Ho Ga Zahra Ka Dil Shad
Ae Gul-e-Narjis Zindabad

5
Paye Ga Tarteeb Jo Lashkar
Deed Ke Qabil Ho Ga Manzar
Meesum Salmaan Aur Abuzar
Aaein Gaye Firdos Se Uth Kar
Dushman Mohlat Ko Tarsay Ga
Charon Janib Khoon Barsay Ga

6
Chahne Walo Mat Ghabrao
Ghazi Ke Parcham Ko Uthao
Nad-e-Ali Honton Pe Sajao
Dushman Ke Aagay Dat Jao
Maut Ke Ghat Utar Jatay Hain
Darne Walay Mar Jate Hain

7
Majlis-e-Sarwar Ho Gi Barpa
Matam Ho Ga Dasta Dasta
Bazu Hoon Gaye Musa-o-Esa
Aur Daood Parhein Gaye Noha
Nasab Jari Ka Parcham Ho Ga
Kabay Ke Andar Matam Ho Ga

8
Jalwah Jalwah Manzar Manzar
Noor Ka Saya Noor Ki Chadar
Sehan-e-Haram Mein Ho Ga Minbar
Aur Minbar Pe Waris-e-Haider
Sirf Saloni Ajra Ho Ga
Nehaj-e-Ali Mein Khutba Ho Ga

9
Parh Ke Qasida Kar Ke Midhat
Tum Bhi Wajhi-e-Zikar Imamat
Karte Rehna Haj-e-Aqeedat
Paye Mubarak Choom Choom Ke
Gard-e-Imamat Ghoom Ghoom Ke
