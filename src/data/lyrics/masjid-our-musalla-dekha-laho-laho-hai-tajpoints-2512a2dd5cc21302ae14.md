1
Masjid Our Musalla Dekha Laho Laho Hai.
Kaisi Fajar Hoi Hai Kofa Lahu Lahu Hai.
Aayi Hai Lo Qayamat Kehta Hai Jibraeel.
Sar Ko Hai Peetta Jo Logo Hai Mikaeel.

2
Jane Khuda Kyun Tera Chehra Lahu Lahu Hai.
Khaliq Yeh Tere Banday Dushman Hoye Tere.
Aaya Jo Tere Ghar Mein Oss Ko Hain Woh Ghairay.
Main Ne Khudaya Kaba Dekha Lahu Lahu Hai.

3
Logon Ne Haye Badla ALLAH Ke Deen Ko.
Mara Hai Sab Ne Mil Ke ALLAH Ke Zeen Ko.
Roti Hain Yeh Namazein Sajdah Lahu Lahu Hai.
Noha Ali Ka Parhne Aaye Hain Anbiya.

4
Halqa Banaye Matam Kartay Hain Anbiya.
Parday Mein Haye Mehdi Rota Lahu Lahu Hai.
Aao Khudara Logo Pursa Nabi Ko Do.
Matam Karo Ali Ka Pursa Jali Ko Do.

5
Sar Peetta Zamana Phirta Lahu Lahu Hai.
Jane Jarrar Kya Yeh Bint-e-Ali Ka Haal.
Ghazi Ko Ro Ke Dekhey Sar Pe Rida Woh Daal.
Hona Yeh Karbala Mein Pardah Lahu Lahu Hai.
