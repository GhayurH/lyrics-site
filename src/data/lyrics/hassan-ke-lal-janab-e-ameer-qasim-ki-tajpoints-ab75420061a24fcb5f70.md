1
Hassan Ke Lal Janab-e-Ameer Qasim Ki.
Jahan Mein Milti Nahi Hai Nazeer Qasim Ki.
Wo Jis Ko Daad-e-Shujat Hussain Ne Di Thi.
Ho Shan Kaise Biyaan Oss Naseer Qasim Ki.

2
Mera Aqeeda Hai Dono Jahan Mein Phaili Hoein.
Yeh Khusboein Hain Yaqeenan Safeer Qasim Ki.
Wafa Ka Bani Bhi Nazaan Ho Jis Ki Himat Par.
Buland Shan Hai Oss Ba-Zameer Qasim Ki.

3
Ajal Bhi Oss Ki Shahadat Pe Ro Pari Ran Mein.
Jo Lash Tukron Mein Dekhi Sagheer Qasim Ki.
Ye Roshni Jo Zehan Ko Mere Atta Hoi Hai.
Ye Sari Dain Hai Mah-e-Muneer Qasim Ki.

4
Khuda a Shukar Baja Laon Jo Naseeb Hoi.
Mere Qalam Ko Ye Midhat Ameer Qasim Ki.
Kissi Aroos Ki Mehndi Ko dekh Kar Sahil.
Mujhe Rolati Hai Yaad-e-Kaseer Qasim Ki.
