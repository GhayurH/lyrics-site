Aaja Meray Ladlay, Aaja Meray Ladlay Main, Lorriyan Sunavan

1
Tenu Labdiyan Asghar, Meri Khalli Bhaavan.

2
Gowdi Aaja Meray Asghar
Shaam Bachra Hogayi Ay.
Khalli Peenda Tera Taak Kay
Maa Ay Ro Ro Kay Rayhi Ay.
Tenu Gaal Naal Laakay,
Teray Laad Ladavaan.

3
Kayray Pasay Turr Gaya Ay,
Meray Bachray Ghaar Nu Aaja.
Tenu Lorriyaan May Sunavaan,
Menu Aakay Ay Dasaa Ja.
Putraan Bajoun Keevain,
Joundiyaan Nay Maavan.

4
Laash Teri Dhup Tay Raygayi,
Menu Asghar Ay Nayhi Bhulna.
Zakam Laykay Dil Day Andaar,
May Bazaaran Vich Hay Rulna.
Menu Bhul Gaiyaan Asghar,
Teray Bajoun Chavaan.

5
Kholda Mumtaaz Jud Vi,
Koyi Maqtal Di Kitaab Ay.
Hai Lahu Unvaan Oda,
Perna Jud Asghar Da Baab Ay.
Haar Sattar Tay Maa Day,
Hanju Hokay Havaan.

6
Muskura Kay Teer Khaveen,
Kaar Day Hurmal Nu Hairaan Ay.
Mera Bachra Karbala Wich,
Ambiya Da Tu Yon Maan Ay
Deen Di Aj Jaan Ay,
Teri Aakhri Savaan.
