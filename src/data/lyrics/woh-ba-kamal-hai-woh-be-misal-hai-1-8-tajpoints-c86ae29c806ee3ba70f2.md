Woh Ba-Kamal Hai Woh Be-Misal Hai 1 8.
Jo Phool Fatima Ka Woh La-Zawaal Hai.

1
Palay Hain Khon Se Abu Talib Ne Chand Phol.
Ik In Mein Hai Imam Tu Hai Dosra Rasool.
Ik Asal-e-Deen Dosra Hai Deen Ka Usool.
Jo Inka Hai Khiyal Khuda Ka Khiyal Hai.

2
Awwal Imam Ali Hai Imamat Ka Tajdar.
Imran Ke Chaman Mein Jo Laya Nayi Bahar.
Jis Ko Atta Ki Khaliq-e-Akbar Ne Zulfiqar.
Jiska Wajood Deen-e-Muhammad Ki Dhaal Hai.

3
Baad-e-Ali Hasan Hai Risalat Ka Aina.
Tora Hia Jis Ne Takht-o-Hakomat Ka Aina.
Hai Ho-Bahu Rasool Ki Seerat Ka Aina.
Kata Qalam Se Jis Ne Burai Ka Jaal Hai.

4
Paida Hoye Hussain Alaihis Salam Jab.
Jibreel Dar Pe Aaye Salami Ko Ba-Adab.
Bolay Piyam Deta Hai Dono Jahan Ka Rab.
Hum Ko Pasand Dil Se Yeh Zahra Ka Lal Hai.

5
Ik Phool Fatima Ka Ibadat Ka Zaib-o-Zain.
Zain-ul-Iba Woh Abid-o-Sajid Dil-e-Hussain.
Sajday Ko Jiske Pyar Kare Rab-e-Mashraqain.
Khutbon Mein Jiske Tamkanat-e-Zoljilal Hai.

6
Baqir Bhi Aik Phol Hai Sehan-e-Batool Ka.
Jiski Ragoon Mein Khoon Ali Our Rasool Ka.
Mushkil Kusha Jahan Mein Woh Har Dil Malol Ka.
Nasal-e-Abu Jehal Ka Issi Se Qattaal Hai.

7
Ik Sadiq-ul-Khiyal Hai Woh Sadiq Al-Wajood.
Jafar Hai Jafri Ke Shajar Ki Hai Woh Namood.
Maqbool Bargah-e-Khuda Jiske Hai Sajood.
Iski Faqar Ke Tabay Muhammad Ki Aal Hai.

8
Ik Lal Inka Mosi-e-Kazim Falak Asas.
Jiske Badan Pe Jad Ki Shariyat Ka Hai Libas.
Ilm-e-Nabi Ki Sari Fiza Iss Ke Aas Pas.
Jad Ki Tarha Se Deen-e-Nabi Ki Woh Dhaal Hai.
