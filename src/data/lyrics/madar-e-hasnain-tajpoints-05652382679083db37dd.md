1
Sar Ta Ba Qadam Noor Fishaan Fatima Zahra.
Aye Madar-e-Sardar-e-Jinna Fatima Zahra.
Kaun Aaye Teri Azmat Ke Barabar Zahra.
Tu Hai Hasnain Shareefain Ki Madar Zahra.

2
Hai Fazeelat Ki Yeh Mairaj Ke Khud Shah-e-Ummam.
Kehte Hain Umm-e-Abeeha Tujhey Aksar Zahra.
Unn Farishton Ke Bhikari Hain Sabhi Ahl-e-Jinna.
Rotiyaan Ghar Se Tere Aaye Jo Le Kar Zahra.

3
Jis Ke Saye Mein Muhammad Ko Sakoon Milta Hai.
Sari Dunya Mein Faqat Hai Teri Chadar Zahra.
Bhaij Kar Sorah-e-Kausar Yeh Kaha Khaliq Ne.
Tere Dushman Ko Khuda Rakhay Ga Abtar Zahra.

4
Farsh Majlis Ka Bichaon Yaan Karon Jashan Bapa.
Bilyaqeen Lati Hain Tashreef Mere Ghar Zahra.
Hum Asad Ahl-e-Wila Bas Yehi Kartay Hain Dua.
Kash Apna Humein Keh Dein Sar-e-Mehshar Zahra
