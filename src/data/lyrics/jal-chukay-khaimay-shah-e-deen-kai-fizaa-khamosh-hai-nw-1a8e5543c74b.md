Jal Chukay Khaimay Shah-e-Deen Kai Fizaa Khamosh Hai

Hoo Ka Aik Aalam Hai Dasht-e-Nainava Khamosh Hai

Mehv-e-Hairat Hoon Zamana Kis Qadar Hai Sang Dil
Sun Raha Hai Kerbala Ka Waqaya Khamosh Hai

Ghash Main Hai Beemar Lab Ko Thaab-e-Goyaee Kahan
Chin Rahi Hai Sar Say Zainab Kai Rida Khamosh Hai

Ya Ilahi Konsi Qooway Dil-e-Zainab Main Hai
Day Kay Bachon Ko Shahadath Ki Dua Khamosh Hai

Ro Tho Layn Khul Kar Zara Akbar Ko Maan Hat Jaeeyai
Shah-e-Deen Paas-e-Adad Say Maamtha Khamosh Hai

Her Qadam Per Hai Jo Bhai Ki Wasiyat Ka Khayal
Taziaanay Khaakay Bint-e-Murtaza Khamosh Hai

Khoon Main Doobi Huwee Hai Sibt-e-Paighambar Ki Laash
Kashti-e-Ummat Bacha Ker Na Khuda Khamosh Hai

Kay Karain Kis Dil Say Dayn Shabeer Marnay Ki Razaa
Sar Jhukaaey Samnay Bhai Khara Khamosh Hai

Arahi Hai Al-Atash Ki Khaima-e-Sheh Say Sada
Sar Kataaey Ghaat Per Saqqa Para Khamosh Hai

Main Nay Jub Poocha Bata Kya Hai Wafa Ki Inteha
Kehkay Ya Abbas Tareekh-e-Wafa Khamosh Hai
