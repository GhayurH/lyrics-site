Hussain ik nahi hogay qatal rasool e khuda

1
Hussain noh minee wah ana minil hussain neh gava

2
Mera nawasah jeh baiyat yazeed deh kar landa
Nah rainda kalima na koi khuda dah naa lainda
Beena e deen neh khuda mai hussain neh deen neh para

3
Hussain neh rabu noo heh piyara meh ay dasendah riya
Khuda dah hukamah heh sajedeh noo tul e dhenda dah riya
Mera Hussain nin na maro nabi dee ah yee sadah
