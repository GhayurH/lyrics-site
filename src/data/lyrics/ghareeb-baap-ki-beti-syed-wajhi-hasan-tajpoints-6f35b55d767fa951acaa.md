Ghareeb Baap Ki Beti Syed Wajhi Hasan.
Ghareeb Baap Ki Beti Ko Itna Mara Hai.

1
Addo Ne Jitna Sakina Ko Chaha Mara Hai.
Yahan Jalaye They Khaimay Wahan Jalaya Tha Ghar.
Nishaan Hain Dadi Ki Hi Tarha Mere Chehray Par.
Pakar Ke Zulfoon Se Mujh Ko Tamacha Mara Hai.

2
Na Khaya Meri Yateemi Pe Bhi Taras Koi.
Tumhari Yaad Mein Jab Bhi Sisak Ke Main Roi.
Dikha Ke Mujh Ko Kata Sar Tumhara Mara Hai.
Jo Mere Chehray Pe Yeh Zakhm Hai Angothi Ka.

3
Tamam Ummar Aye Baba Yeh Bhar Na Paye Ga.
Tamacha Shimar Ne Chehray Pe Aisa Mara Hai.
Ajab Thi Sham-e-Ghareebaan Mein Bekasi Meri.
Bananey Khud Ko Main Darya Ki Simt Jab Dori.

4
Main Munh Ke Bal Giri Youn Mujh Ko Naiza Mara.
Howa Tha Dadi Ka Sar Taig-e-Zulm Se Zakhmi.
Shigaf Ho Gaya Sar Mera Bhi Hai Khoon Jari.
Youn Pathron Se Sar-e-Shehar-e-Kofa Mara Hai.

5
Chacha Hasan Ki Tarha Khoon Ugalti Hoon Dekho.
Gira Ke Doortay Naqay Se Iss Tarha Mujh Ko.
Addo Ne Kar Ke Yeh Pehlu Shikasta Mara Hai.
Gallay Mein Tauq Ka Jo Zakhm Hai Dukhana Hai.

6
Yeh Hai Sakina Ka Bhai Issay Rolana Hai.
Addo Ne Keh Ke Yeh Abid Ko Durrah Mara Hai.
Hamarey Samne Serab Ho Gaya Lashkar.
Jo Hum Ne Shimar Se Pani Talab Kiya Barh Kar.

7
Gira Ke Khak Pe Pani Ka Koza Mara Hai.
 Wajih Karb-o-Bala Kya Likhe Ga:MIR HILAL:.
Sina Ki Nook Pe Roya Lahu Batool Ka Lal.
Laeen Ne Bachi Ko Jab Taziyana Mara Hai.
