1
Tere Qatilaan Nalon Wadh Veera Meray Qatil Wich Bazaraan Day.
Har Qadam Te Mainu Wekhday Ne Haye Majmay Veer Hazaran De.
Abbas De Jiondiyaan Bas Meri Quran Ziyarat Karda Si.
Mere Piyo Diyaan Akhiyaan Naal Meri Rehman Ziyarat Karda Si.

2
Aj Pehli Wari Veer Turi Haye Naal Main Pehrey Daraan De.
Howay Mard Shareef Te Nahin Langda Assi Langiyaan Oss Bazar Wichon.
Sajjad Ne Chadraan Phophiyaan Layi Haye Mangiyaan Oss Bazar Wichon.
Kalma Go Le Ke Aaye Ne Hath Ban Ke Parday Daraan De.

3
Maqtal Taun Le Ke Sham Taein Jino Labh Ke Zalimaan Mariya Aye.
Oday Cholay Di Ag Dasdi Aye Ohno Khaimiyaan De Nal Sariya Aye.
Na Wekhin Veer Toon Nezay Toon Oday Munh Te Hath Badkaraan De.
Lang Jawaan Veer Main Bheer Wichon Jay Turn Di Koi Ja Deway.

4
Rukna V Okha Aye Veera Na Jan Da Koi Rah Deway.
Ag Sutday Veer Baneriyan Toon Lag Jan Na Naal Dewaraan De.
Kiwein Hosh Karawaan Abid Nu Uth Lal Mera Asaan Thakna Nayi.
Mukya Na Aj Bazar Asaan Dowaan Ne Abid Bachna Nayi.

5
Tere Jigar Te Phat Ne Chadraan De Aye Zakhm Nayi Talwaraan De.
Hoya Taruf Wich Darbar Odha Bagi Di Bhain Te Naa Zainab.
Sajjad Kehya Ro Ke Najfi Nayi Qaidan Meri Maa Zainab.
Bethi Takht Te Aal Ghulamaan Di Gal Tauq Paye Sardaraan De.
