1
Shah-e-Khurasan, Waris-e-Quran, Ya Maula Raza Jan.
Yeh Sehan-e-Shifa Hai, Our Lab Pe Dua Hai, Darban Bano Main Bhi, Kar Dijiye Ahsan.
Jab Dar Pe Tere Aaye Bala Tal Gayi Maula.
Ik Hirz e Elahi Hai Tera Naam Bhi Maula.

2
Hum Loag Salamat Hain Tere Dam Se Hi Maula.
Shah-e-Mashhad Tera Hai Saron Par Saya.
Kis Baat Ka Dar Hum Ko Jab Tu Hai Nigehban.
Maqroz e Karam Hai Tere Dar Ka Yeh Zamana.

3
Hai Teri Sakhawat Ka Sakhawat Mein Bhi Charcha.
Iran Hi Kya Khati Hai Dunya Tera Sadqa.
Tere Darbar Talak Mangney Aaye Malak.
Khaliq Ne Tere Ghar Ko Bakhshi Hai Alag Shan.

4
Masooma-e-Qum Ki Qasam Ijaz Dikha Do.
Tum Khuwab Mein Hi Apna Dedar Kara Do.
Rukhsar Mere Qabar Ki Jali Se Laga Do.
Noor-e-Ain-e-Mosa Mere Aaqa Maula.

5
Faryad Meri Sun Lo Mein Tum Pe Hoon Qurban.
Jo Ishq Hai Tujh Se Ossi Inam Ka Sikka.
Hai Zamin-e-Aaho Yeh Barey Kam Ka Sikka.
Bazu Pe Jo Bandha Hai Tere Naam Ka Sikka.

6
Sorat-e-Nad-e-Ali Hai Asar Iss Ka Bhi.
Mushkil Ho Safar Jitna Kar Detey Ho Aasan.
Tum Toos Ke Madfon Shehanshah-e-Ajam Ho.
Tum Sare Zamane Pe Hi Mayal Ba Karam Ho.

7
Sarkar Tum Hi Khak Nasheeno Ka Bharam Ho.
Ya Mughees Al Shia Tum Ho Woh Darwaza.
Bakhshish Ka Banaya Hai Rab Ne Jisay Meezan.
Maula Raza, Maula Raza, Maula Raza.

8
Salam un Alaik Ghareeb Al-Watan Ghareeb Imam.
Salam Aap Pe Aye Dilbar-e-Rasool-e-Anam.
Salam Aap Pe Syed Rauf Shah-e-Zaman.
Salam Aap Pe Pursan e Hal-e-Khasta e Tan.

9
Salam Aap Pe Aye Sogwar e Karb-o-Bala.
Salam Aap Pe Aye Be Qarrar-o-Karb Zadah.
Salam un Alaik Anees Al Nafos Ya Maula.
Salam Aap Pe Shams Al Shamos Ya Maula.

10
Monis Ho Zaeefon Ke Moeen Ul Zoafa Ho.
Hami Ho Gareebon Ke Ghareeb Al Guraba Ho.
Tum Bhi Tu Azadar-e-Qateel-e-Alabra Ho.
Har Ghari Har Lamha Rahe Mehway Giriya.

11
Tum Ne Bhi Rah-e-Haq Mein Sab Kar Diya Qurban.
Mamoon e Laeen Ne Yeh Ajab Zulm Kiya Hai.
Angor Mein Maula Ko Mere Zehar Diya Hai.
Neela Hai Badan Pas Bohat Waqt-e-Qaza Hai.

12
Dard Ka Aalam Hai Har Taraf Matam Hai.
Be Chainiyan Barhti Hain Kuch Pal Ke Hain Mehman.
Bibi Koi Thamay Hoye Pehlu-e-Shikasta.
Ro Ro Ke Khurasan Mein Parhti Hai Yeh Noha.

13
Aoulad-e-Payamber Ka Hai Dushman Yeh Zamana.
Haal Shabar Sa Howa Khoon Ugalta Hai Raza.
Phir Aaj Howa Mera Pardais Mein Nuqsan.
Aye:MIR HILAL: Aaj Bhi Dawa Hai Yeh Mohkam.

14
Har Sahib-e-Irfan Ko Maloom Hai Meesum.
Aye Sakin-e-Mashhad Teri Mati Mein Hai Aalam.
Wajad Mein Rehta Hoon Jab Ali Kehta Hoon.
Phir Khatam Kiya Main Ne Dil Kehta Hai Quran.
