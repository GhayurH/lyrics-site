Shair-e-Khuda Damad-e-Payamber Haider Haider.
Shafa-e-Mehshar Saqi-e-Kausar Haider Haider.

1
Bolna Main Ne Jab Seekha Tha.
Maa Ne Meri Yeh Mujh Se Kaha Tha.
Bolo Beta Jan Laga Kar Haider Haider.
Meri Wasiyat Yaad Yeh Rakhna.

2
Qabar Ke Bahir Tum Sab Kehna.
Khud Main Kahon Ga Qabar Ke Andar Haider Haider.
Waqif They Aadab-e-Wila Se.
Kalma Adhora Kaisay Parhtay.

3
Dastay Nabi Par Bolay Kankar Haider Haider.
Shabar Our Shabeer Ho Razi.
Sehwan Wala Peer Ho Razi.
Bolo Sakhi Shahbaz Qalandar Haider Haider.

4
Dono Hain Yeh Roh-e-Wilayat.
Dono Hain Yeh Jan-e-Wilayat.
ALLAH Akbar ALLAH Akbar Haider Haider.
Zikar-e-Ali Par Pabandi Thi.

5
Lekin Zainab Aisay Boli.
Gaunj Utha Darbar Ke Andar Haider Haider.
Oss Ko Laga Rehta Hai Khatrah.
Bigra Howa Hai Jis Ka Shajrah.

6
Aa Nahi Sakta Oss Ki Zabaan Par Haider Haider.
Maut Se Darta Hai Na Dara Hai.
Ishq Takallum Bool Raha Hai.
Daar Ke Opar Zair-e-Khanjar Haider Haider.
