1
Logo Rasta Do Mere Sath Pardah Daar Hain.
Rasiyon Mein Jo Bandhi Hain Beemar Hain La Char Hain.
Yeh Pardah Daar Jo Hain Tatheer Mein Pali Hain.
Ahmad Ki Betiyaan Hain Yeh Izzat-e-Jali Hain.
Bagi Na Inn Ko Samjho Yeh Khuld Ki Sardar Hain.
Ummat Se Jo Lagay Hain Woh Zakhm Si Rahi Hain.
Jeene Ko Jee Nahi Hai Phir Bhi Yeh Jee Rahi Hain.
Jis Din Se Lut Gayi Hain Oss Din Se Yeh Bedar Hain.
Paband Hoon Rasan Mein Chalne Ka Rasta Do.
Main Khon Ro Raha Hoon Tum Hi Mujhe Bata Do.
Le Kar Kahan Se Guzron Har Taraf Tu May Khar Hain.
Inn Ki Taharton Ka Quran Bhi Gawah Hai.
Itnay Sitam Sahey Hain Mamnon LaIlaha Hai.
Rab Ki Raza Ki Qaidi Darain Ki Mukhtar Hain.
Bagi Samajh Ke Jis Pe Barsa Rahe Ho Pathar.
Hai Waqt Ki Woh Zahra Hai Bizato Payamber.
Rasiyon Mein Sath Qaidi Haye Dukhtar-e-Karrar Hain.
Yeh Tauq Our Zanjeerein Mere Sath Noha Gar Hain.
Kyun Aayatein Qurani Haye Aaj Nangay Sar Hain.
Aye Kalma Go Batao Kya Iss Ki Yeh Haq Daar Hain.
Bazar Se Youn Guzra Dukhiyon Ka Qafila Tha.
Kar Kar Ke Yaad Manzar Bazar-e-Sham Wala.
Mehdi Ke Sath Azhar Noha Kuna Azadar Hain.
