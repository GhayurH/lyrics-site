Ghar laut k ghar main pehle diya kis tarah jalaya ZAINAB nay,
Bas ahh bhari aur ghut ghut kay beyto ko pukara ZAINAB nay.

1
1. Phir koi hookh uthi dil se phr shaam e gariban yaad aayi
Hujray may AON o MOHAMMAD kay,, dekha jo andhera ZAINAB nay

2
2. Aik shaur utha ujray ghar may haey AKBAR haye ASGHAR
SUGHRA nay sambhala ZAINAB ko,, SUGHRA ko sambhala ZAINAB nay

3
3. Kis tarah se ZAINAB aur SUGHRA aik dosre ko pehchan nahi
SUGHRA kay baal safaid huay,, lia kala joora ZAINAB nay

4
4. Gash anay se pehle dono ne aik dojay ko bas thaam lia,
SUGRA ne jo dekha ZAINAB ko,, SUGHRA ko jo dekha ZAINAB nay
