1
Aabroo e La Ilaha Sajda e Shabbir Hai Hasan Sadiq.
Tere Dam Se Ya Hussaina Narah-e-Takbeer Hai.
Likh Diya Khoon Se Hussaina Parcham-e-Islam Par.
Sar Katana Rah-e-Haq Mein Deen Ki Tauqeer Hai.

2
Lasha-e-Akbar Utha Kar Bolay Shah-e-Karbala.
Khak-o-Khoon Mein Sarwar e Konain Ki Tasveer Hai.
Dekhiye Ayub Aa Kar Sabar Ki Manzil Zara.
Hathoon Pe Shah-e-Zaman Ke Lasha-e-Be Sheer Hai.

3
Kis Tarha Dafnaye Zainab Lash Ko Teri Hussain.
Hathoon Mein Rasi Bandhi Hai Sar Khulay Humsheer Hai.
Iss Tarha Irfan Hota Hai Fareez e Haq Ada.
Khon Mein Dobi Hoi Peshani-e-Shabbir Hai.
