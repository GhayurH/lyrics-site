1
Qasid mera khat lejaa
Jaa tera bhalaa hogaa
Baba mera Kufe me
Mehmaan bana hoga

2
Qasid mere baba ki pehchan badi ye hai
Abbas Alam pakde nazdik khada hoga

3
Qasid mere bhai ka hai naam Ali Akbar
Humshakle payambar hai sehra bhi bandhaa hoga

4
Mil jaaye kahi tanha jo tujko Ali Akbar
Itna hi fakad kehna kab vaada adaa hoga

5
Sab kuch wo too le lena jo dega mera bhai
Akbar ne mera tohfa idi ka rakha hoga

6
Chalte hue ghutniyo pe aajaye Ali Asgar
Godi mei utha lena ehsaan tera hoga
