1
Qayamat Eid Say Pehlay Shahid Baltistani.
Ali Ke Ghar Mein Barpa Hai Qayamat, Eid Se Pehle.
Hoi Zainab Ke Baba Ki Shahadat, Eid Se Pehle.
Ali Ke Ghar Se Le Kar Arsh Tak Bas Gham Ka Mosam Hai.
Sabab Se Iss Ke Ab Ramzan Bhi Mah-e-Muharram Hai.
Lagi Hai Jo Sar-e-Haider Pe Zarbat, Eid Se Pehle.
Janaza Uth Raha Hai Shah-e-Khaybar Geer Ka Logo.
Na Pocho Haal Kya Hai Zainab-e-Dilgeer Ka Logo.
Khushi Hasnain Ki Hoti Hai Gharat, Eid Se Pehle.
Kaha Ashab Ne Sar Peet Kar Hasrat Rahi Baqi.
Imamat Mein Ali Ki Phir Namaz-e-Eid Parhne Ki.
Qaza Ne Cheen Li Yeh Bhi Sadat, Eid Se Pehle.
Dar-e-Zahra Jala Kar Bhi Hasad Ki Aag Baqi Hai.
Humein Kya Thi Khabar Nana Sada Zainab Ki Aati Hai.
Dobarah Khum Ka Badlah Le Gi Ummat, Eid Se Pehle.
Ali Ke Wastay Tu Eid Masjid Mein Shahadat Thi.
Mulaqat e Khuda Ki Iss Qadar Haider Ko Thi Jaldi.
Jaga Kar Khud Diya Qatil Ko Sharbat, Eid Se Pehle.
Saf-e-Matam Bicha Do Sogawaro! Aaj Ghar Ghar Mein.
Baha Do Apni Aankhon Ke Yeh Darya, Yaad-e-Haider Mein.
Miley Gi Tum Ko Inn Ashkon Ki Qeemat, Eid Se Pehle.
Ali Ko Halat-e-Roza Mein Mara Kalma Goyon Ne.
Lehad Mein Bhi Muhammad Ko Sataya Kalma Goyon Ne.
Rulaya Syeda Ko Zair-e-Turbat, Eid Se Pehle.
Dua Mango Keh Rah-e-Hijar-e-Mehdi Jald Khul Jaye.
Keh Raz-e-Inteqam-e-Karbala Darwaish Khul Jaye.
Miley Zeeshan Ko Bhi Yeh Basharat, Eid Se Pehle.
