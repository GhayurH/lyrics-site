1
Muslim Jo Qatal Ho Gaye Kofe Ki Rah Mein Rehan Azmi.
Sansein Dhalein Hussain Ki Karb-o-Karah Mein.
Lamhon Mein Haye Kaisa Zamana Badal Gaya.
Jo Sath They Woh Mil Gaye Qatil Sipah Mein.

2
Dhokay Se Pehle Ghaira Safeer-e-Hussain Ko.
Le Aaye Khainchtay Hoye Phir Qatal Ga Mein.
Do Lal Sath Aaye They Woh Bhi Bichar Gaye.
Dono Bhataktay Phirtay They Hal-e-Tabah Mein.

3
Muslim Shaheed Ho Gaye Be Jurm-o-Be Khata.
Tha Bugz Ahl-e-Kofa Ke Qalb-e-Siyah Mein.
Sar Kaat Ke Badan Ko Zameen Par Gira Diya.
Muslim Pe Zulm Yeh Howa Maula Ki Chah Mein.

4
Pehle Namaz Sath Parhi Phir Mukar Gaye.
Shamil Hoi Rasool Ki Ummat Gunah Mein.
Misal-e-Hussain Misal-e-Alamdar e Karbala.
Muslim Shaheed Ho Gaye Quraan Ki Rah Mein.

5
Muslim Bhi Karbala Ke Shaheedon Mein Aik Hain.
Zahra-o-Mustafa-o-Ali Ki Nigah Mein.
 Rehan Asar-e-Nao Ke Yazeedon Se Kyun Daroon.
Rehta Hoon Main Tu Nad-e-Ali Ki Panah Mein.
