Jo Bandagi Ka Naaz Hai Haqeeqat-e-Namaz Hai Woh Banda-e-Khuda Hussain Hai.

1
Nazar Hai Khaimagah Par Zameen-o-Aasman Ki.
Sakoot Mein Hai Zindagi Ghari Hai Imtehan Ki.
Hai Sans Maut Ki Ruki Larz Rahi Hai Roshni.
Diya Bhuja Raha Hussain Hai.
Aye Jane Walay Ruk Zara Udas Kyun Hai Iss Qadar.
Qalam Hai Oss Ke Hath Mein Hussain Se Tu Baat Kar.

2
Yeh Looh Phir Banaye Ga Tujhe Pisar Dilaye Ga.
Khuda Ka Ladla Hussain Hai.
Sumo Se Zuljina Ke Yeh Arsh Kyun Lipat Gaya.
Falak Pe Mehar Ruk Gaya Furat Piche Hat Gaya.
Hai Zalzala Zameen Ko Abhi Tu Aasteen Ko.
Ulatne Ja Raha Hussain Hai.

3
Falak Pe Likhe Jate Thay Naseeb Kal Ki Baat Thi.
Kareem Ke Zahoor Se Rawish Yeh Ab Badal Gayi.
Yahin Pe Ho Ga Sab Raqam Utha Ke Looh Our Qalam.
Zameen Pe Aa Gaya Hussain Hai.
Ameer Ko Salam Kar Kissi Ne Jab Yeh Di Sada.
Pukara Muslim-e-Ali Main Qaid Hoon Tu Kya Howa.

4
Salam Kyun Karon Issay Pata Nahi Hai Kya Tujhe.
Ameer Bas Mera Hussain Hai.
Hussain Ne Yeh Tarbiyat Batool Se Hi Payi Thi.
Bacha Li Apne Khon Se Wilayat-e-Ali Wali.
Gali Gali Nagar Nagar Ali Ali Hai Ab Magar.
Ali Ke Lab Pe Ya Hussain Hai.

5
Ajab Safar Hai Ishq Ka sakoon Aablon Mein Hai.
Najaf Se Karbala Talak Yeh Shoor Qafiloon Mein Hai.
Chalo Hussainiyo Chalo Zara Sa Taiz Our Chalo.
Tumhein Bula Raha Hussain Hai.
Woh Apne Dost Ke Liye Qaza Ka Sar Utar De.
Majal Kya Hai Maut Ki Habib Ko Maar De.

6
Hayat Maut Laye Gi Palat Ke Ab Tu Aaye Gi.
Zaban De Chuka Hussain Hai.
Takallum-e-Hussainiyat Yeh Oss Ka Inteqam Hai.
Ali Ki Ladli Hai Ab Azza Ka Sab Nizam Hai.
Bata Rahi Hai Yeh Zameen Yazeediyat Kahin Nahi.
Jagah Jagah Pe Ya Hussain Hai.
