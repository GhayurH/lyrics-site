1
Hussain Shaheed-e-Karbala Nadeem Sarwar.
Aye Karbala, Aye Karbala, Aye Karbala.
Hussain Shaheed-e-Karbala, Hussain Shaheed-e-Karbala.
Zabeehe Be Khata Shudi Qateel-e-Dasht-e-Nainawa.
Zabeehe Dasht-e-Nainawa Qateel-e-Dasht-e-Nainawa.
Shad Ast Naz-e-Hal Atta Ilaj-e-Dard-e-La Dawa.
Jahan Hai Dard-e-Karbala Wahan Wahan Karbala.
Waqar-e-Chashm-e-Nam Toi Shaheed-e-Mohtaram Toi.
Jahan Hussain Rehnuma Wahan Wahan Karbala.
Shabab-e-Akbar-e-Jawaan Nishana Kird Shamiyaan.
Jahan Jawaan Ka Gham Bapa Wahan Wahan Karbala.
Ba Har Rukh-o-Behar Jahat Baqa Baqa Hussainiyat.
Jahan Khuda Hai Ba Khuda Wahan Wahan Karbala.
Shaheed-e-Karbala Hussain Binae La Ilaha Hussain.
Jahan Jahan Teri Sada Wahan Wahan Karbala.
Alam Jaloos-o-Taziye Yeh Majlisein Zyaratein.
Jahan Charag-e-Mustafa Wahan Wahan Karbala.
Saji Hoi Aabadiyan Rida Beghair Bibiyaan.
Jahan Dikhai De Rida Wahan Wahan Karbala.
Jigar Jigar Sina Sina Lahu Lahu Dhowaan Dhowaan.
Jahan Hussaini Silsila Wahan Wahan Karbala.
:REHAN-o-SARWAR-E-: Hazeen Gham-e-Hussain Mein Jabeen.
Jhuki Miley Gi Jis Jagah Wahan Wahan Karbala.
