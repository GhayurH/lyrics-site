Aa Gahi Ko Hussain Kehte Hain.

1
Jis Ka Aashiq Hai Khaliq-e-Akbar.
Oss Sakhi Ko Hussain Kehte Hain.
Charh Ke Naize Pe Jo Kalam Kare.
Bas Ossi Ko Hussain Kehte Hain.
Khushboein Keh Rahi Hain Pholon Se.
Tazgi Ko Hussain Kehte Hain.

2
Sheh Ke Gham Mein Jo Ro Ke Ho Mehsos.
Oss Khushi Ko Hussain Kehte Hain.
De Do Fatwa Moinuddin Pe Jo.
Dein Hi Ko Hussain Kehte Hain.
Maut Ne Kar Liya Hai Tay Raqib.
Zindagi Ko Hussain Kehte Hain.
