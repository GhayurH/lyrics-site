Qaidi Piyo De Naam Te Dhiyaan Ne Wain Aye Paye
Sadhay Babay Di Maiyyat Veer Koi Te Pul Baghdad Taun Chaye.

1
Tauq Gardan Wich Lash Kariyaan Wich Saday Te.
Ummat Ki Ki Zulm Kamaye Koi Nanay Nu Dasaye.
Kaisi Nagri Aye Sham Bhul Gayi Aye Pul Te Aye Maiyyat.
Aj Taein Sham De Wich Te Assi Zindan Wasaye.

2
Jiday Dunya Te Lal Atharaan Ohno Be Waris.
Aina Samajh Liya Te Haye Mazdoor Bulaye.
Kitay Yasrab Wich Wain Dhiyaan Ne Akbar Yasrab Te.
Karbal Sham Taun Rowan Aaye Tatheer De Saye.
