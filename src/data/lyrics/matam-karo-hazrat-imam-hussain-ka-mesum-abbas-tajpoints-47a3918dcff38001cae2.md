1
Matam Karo Hazrat Imam Hussain Ka Mesum Abbas.
Matam Karo Hussain Alh Aslam Ka.
Ik Din Mein Ghar Ujar Gaya Logo Imam Ka.
Sokha Howa Tha Syed-e-Mazloom Ka Galla Pani Nahi Mila.

2
Gardan Pe Sheh Ke Shimar Ne Khanjar Chala Diya Roti Thin Syeda.
Kya Haal Kar Diya Gaya Aali Maqam Ka.
Ik Din Mein Dil Pe Zakhm Bahatar Ke Khaye Hain Khoon Mein Nahaye Hain.
Hur Jaun Our Habib Ke Lashay Uthaye Hain Aanso Bahaye Hain.

3
Khaimon Talak Janaza Jo Laya Ghulam Ka.
Seene Se Apne Betay Ke Naiza Juda Kiya Haye Wah Mosibata.
Zahra Ka Lal Dasht Mein Be Noor Ho Gaya Haye Wah Mosibata.
Oss Be Watan Ka Haye Sheh-e-Tashna Kam Ka.

4
Zainab Ke Dil Pe Kaisi Qayamat Guzar Gayi Dunya Ujar Gayi.
Maqtal Mein Sheh Ki Lash Ki Be Hurmati Hoi Woh Dekhti Rahi.
Pamal Lasha Ho Gaya Shah-e-Anaam Ka.
Maula Ke Khandan Pe Barhti Gayi Jafa Cheeni Gayi Rida.

5
Chalta Tha Garam Rait Pe Kanton Pe Qafila Bazar Aa Gaya.
Naizay Pe Dekhta Raha Manzar Jo Sham Ka.
Pyason Ko Yaad Kar Ke Sabeelein Lagao Tum Pani Pilao Tum.
Kala Libas Pehno Alam Bhi Sajao Tum Youn Gham Manao Tum.

6
Farsh-e-Azza Bichao Shaheedon Ke Naam Ka.
Ik Pal Na Bhool Paein Gay Ghurbat Hussain Ki Jab Tak Hai Zindagi.
Youn Hi Karein Gay Meesum-o-:AZLAN AZMI: Maula Ki Nokari.
Zahra Se Ajar Hum Ko Miley Ga Kalam Ka.
