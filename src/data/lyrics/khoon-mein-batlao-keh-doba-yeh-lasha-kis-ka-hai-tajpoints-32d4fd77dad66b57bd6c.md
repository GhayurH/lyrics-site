Khoon Mein Batlao Keh Doba Yeh Lasha Kis Ka Hai.
Pul Pe Bagdad Ke Rakha Janaza Kis Ka Hai.

1
Dekh Kar Lasha-e-Kazim Pukaray Jin-o-Malak.
Lasha Mazdoron Ne Haye Uthaya Kis Ka Hai.
Jis Ne Katay Hain Bars Chodah Qaid Khane Mein.
Pisar Woh Socho Zara Ahl-e-Jafa Kis Ka Hai.

2
Khon-e-Imraan Ke Siwa Mere Khuda Batla Tu.
Tere Iss Deen Pe Ahsaan Yeh Sara Kis Ka Hai.
Hain Jis Ke Betay Utharan Para Hai La-Waris.
Qaid Mein Baba Yeh Ummat Ne Mara Kis Ka Hai.

3
Pul-e-Bagdad Pe Kehte The Log Aapas Mein.
Ghar Yeh Pardais Mein Barbad Howa Kis Ka Hai.
Imam-e-Waqt Hi Afraz Jan Jaein Gaye.
Khoon Se Likha Gaya Aakhir Yeh Noha Kis Ka Hai.
