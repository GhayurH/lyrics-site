Janab E Hazrat e Abbas Ko Bulaya Karo Syed Raza Abbas Zaidi
Maula Abbas, Maula Abbas.

1
Maula Hain Hamarey Dil Unhein Pukarey.
Bin Bazuon Ke Jo Taqderein Likhtay Hain.
Baab Ul Hawaij Fazal Ke Baba.
Bali Sakina Ke Hain Chacha.
Unko Bulao Sab Gham Sunao.
Hain Woh Sakhi Badshah.
Diya Jala Ke Sakina Ke Naam Ka Ghar Mein.
Janab-e-Hazrat-e-Abbas Ko Bulaya Karo.
Kahan Kahan Woh Hamari Madad Nahi Kartay.
Qasam Sakina Ki Abbas Rad Nahi Kartay.
Beghair Mangay Woh Sab Kuch Atta Karein Gay Tumhein.
Sakina Bibi Ka Farsh-e-Azza Bichaya Karo.
Bohat Qareeb Abbas Ke Yeh Shehzadi.
Jo Dekhni Ho Muhabbat Chacha Bhateeji Ki.
Tum Apne Ghar Mein Sajatay Hoye Azakhana.
Alam Pe Choti Si Ik Mashk Bhi Sajaya Karo.
Misal-e-Karb-o-Bala Hai Karam Nawazi Ki.
Yeh Kamsini Mein Bhi Mushkil Kusha Hai Ghazi Ki.
Chacha Se Keh Ke Ghamo Se Tumhein Chura Lein Gi.
Sakina Bibi Ko Sab Hal-e-Dil Sunaya Karo.
Aye Kash Dekh Lein Ghazi Hamarey Ghar Ki Taraf.
Atta Ho Aath Muharram Ko Hazari Ka Sharf.
Chacha Ke Sath Bhateeji Bhi Mere Ghar Aaye.
Dua Yeh Mang Ke Ghazi Ki Nazar Khaya Karo.
Tum Iss Ke Naam Ki Mannat Utha Ke Mango Dua.
Hussain Sadqay Mein Iss Ke Karein Gay Beti Atta.
Tumhare Ghar Mein Jab Aa Jaye Chand Si Beti.
Kaneez-e-Bali Sakina Ossay Banaya Karo.
Jisay Namazon Mein Manga Sakoon Sakoon Keh Kar.
Sakon Kaisay Miley Woh Nazar Na Aaye Agar.
Issay Bola Ke Yeh Aksar Hussain Kehte They.
Sakina Hum Se Kabhi Door Tum Na Jaya Karo.
Salam Oss Pe Jo Girti Sanbhalti Rehti Thi.
Woh Jis Ke Galon Ki Rangat Badalti Rehti Thi.
Tamachay Kha Ke Woh Zalim Se Ro Ke Kehti Rahi.
Main Ik Yateem Hoon Mujh Par Na Hath Uthaya Karo.
Yeh Zaireen Se Zeeshan Our Raza Keh Do.
Kissi Ka Sham Agar Karbala Se Jana Ho.
Lehad Pe Bibi Ki Ik Mashk Sath Le Ja Kar.
Yeh Pani Bhaija Hai Abbas Ne Bataya Karo.
