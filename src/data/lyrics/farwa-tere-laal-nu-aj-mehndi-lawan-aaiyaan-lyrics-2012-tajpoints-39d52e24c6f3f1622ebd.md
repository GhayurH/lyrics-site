1
Farwa Tere Laal Nu Aj Mehndi Lawan Aaiyaan.
Haye Phophiyaan Qasim De Aj Shagan Manawan Aaiyaan.
Tukriyaan Walay Laray Nu Das Farwa Kinj Parnaiye.
Kehray Kehray Tukray Nu Shagna Di Mendi Laiye.

2
Sehray De Pholaan Nu Aidi Lash Te Pawan Aaiyaan.
Pochiya Zainab Ne Ki Dewaan Qasim Naaz Main Tere.
Wasda Wehra Ujar Gaya Na Putar Bachay Ne Mere.
Chadraan Nu Tere Sir Di Assi Wail Karawan Aaiyaan.

3
Janj Charahn Toon Pehlaan Mar Gaye Qasim Janji Sare.
Rait Garam Te Mar Gaye Ne Mawaan De Raaj Dularay.
Assi Qasim Tere Janji Mitiyaan Choon Chawan Aaiyaan.
Sajri Mehndi Hathaan Te Kiwein Kubra Rasiyaan Paway.

4
Sham De Okhay Safraan Wich Teri Bewah Na Mar Jaway.
Uth Qasim Tainu Phophiyaan Dukh Dard Sunawan Aaiyaan.
Rakh Ke Gathri Maqtal Wich Shabir Ne Hath Jadoon Kholay.
Wekhiya Kubra De Babay Thaan Thaan Toon Qasim Bolay.

5
Aj Horaan Arshaan Toon Imdad Karawan Aaiyaan.
Nayi Dastoor Samar Jag Da Aynj Rasam Viyah Di Howay.
Laray Di Sehra Bandi Te Dulhan Aa Ke Roway.
Rab Janre Karbal Wich Kiwein Rasam Nibhawan Aaiyaan.
