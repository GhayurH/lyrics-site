1
Maa Bhe Na Ho Duniya Main Aur Baap Bhe Mar Jaye Syed Raza Abbas Zaidi.
Ahsas-e-Yateemi Hai Veraan Hai Ali Ka Ghar.
Bin Baap Ke Yeh Pehli Raat Aayi Hai Bachoon Par.
Roti Thin Kahin Zainab Shabeer Kahin Shabar.

2
Fizza Ne Yeh Dekha Tu Kehne Lagin Ro Ro Kar.
Maa Bhi Na Ho Dunya Mein Our Baap Bhi Mar Jaye.
ALLAH Kissi Par Bhi Yeh Waqt Nahi Aaye.
ALLAH ALLAH ALLAH ALLAH.

3
Yeh Raat Khuda Jane Kis Haal Mein Guzrey Gi.
Bachoon Ki Nigahon Mein Tasveer Hai Baba Ki.
Baba Se Juda Ho Kar Kis Tarha Jiya Jaye.
Maiyyat Jahan Rakhi Thi Zainab Wahin Bethi Hai.

4
Abbas Ke Chehray Ko Ab Dekh Ke Roti Hai.
Kehti Hai Koi Mere Baba Ko Bola Laye.
Hathoon Mein Ruqaiya Ke Baba Ka Musalla Hai.
Kulsoom Ke Hathoon Mein Baba Ka Amama Hai.

5
Baba Nahi Aaein Gay Ab Kaun Yeh Batlaye.
Maa Yaad Jo Aaye Tu Ho Aate They Turbat Par.
Rukhsat Kiya Baba Ko Hasnain Ne Yeh Keh Kar.
Milne Kahan Jaein Gay Jab Baap Ki Yaad Aaye.

6
Ab Mujh Ko Rulata Hai Shehzadi Ka Woh Kehna.
Fizza Mere Bachoon Se Tum Door Nahi Rehna.
Maa Baap Ke Bin Jeena Mushkil Hai Bohat Haye.
Iss Alam-e-Ghurbat Mein Maa Zindah Agar Hoti.

7
Bin Baap Ke Bachoon Ko Seene Se Laga Leti.
Aagaz-e-Yateemi Hai Dil Gham Se Na Phat Jaye.
Bholi Hai Kahan Zainab Woh Chomna Shano Ko.
Jab Sham Mein Dekhey Gi Rasi Ke Nishano Ko.

8
Yaad Aaye Gi Baba Ki Oss Waqt Bohat Haye.
 Zeeshan-o- Raza Haye Noha Tha Yeh Fizza Ka.
Ab Betiyon Ke Sar Pe Saya Nahi Baba Ka.
Mushkil Hai Yateemo Pe Yeh Raat Guzar Jaye.
