Khuda Mohafiz-e-Quraan Hai Our Bachaye Hussain
Khuda Ke Waday Khuda Ki Tarha Nibhaye Hussain

1
Wo Dono Phir Kabhi Tabdeel Ho Nahi Sakte
Jise Banaye Hussain Our Jise Mitaye Hussain
Ye Kainat Tu Kun Se Banayi Khaliq Ne
Banai Karb-o-Bala Kehke Sirf Haye Hussain

2
Kissi Bhi Door Mein Hote Wo Karbala Aate
Howay The Khalq Bahatar Faqat Baraye Hussain
Ajeeb Raat Wo Guzri Thi Hur Ki Fitrat Par
Keh Neend Oss Ko Sulaye Osse Jagaye Hussain

3
Abhi Abhi Fitrus Ko Main Ne Dekha Tha
Likha Howa Tha Paron Par Hai Ye Attaye Hussain
Main Lafziyaat Ka Adam Banana Chahta Hoon
Mujhe Bhi Chahiye Thori se Khak-e-Paye Hussain

4
Ali Ki Jitni Zarorat Hai Utni Ghazi Ki
Wo Murtaza-e-Muhammad Hain Ye Murtaz-e-Hussain
Khud Ahl-e-Ishq Samaj Lein Gaye Naam Kis Ka Hai
Main Sirf Sabar Hi Likh Doon Agar Bajaye Hussain
