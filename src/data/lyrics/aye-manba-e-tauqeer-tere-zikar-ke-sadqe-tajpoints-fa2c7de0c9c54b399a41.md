1
Aye Manba-e-Tauqeer Tere Zikar Ke Sadqe.
Lafzon Mein Hai Taseer Tere Zikar Ke Sadqe.
Hai Naam Tera Rooh Ki Taskeen Ka Bais.
Khush Haal Hain Dill Geer Tere Zikar Ke Sadqe.

2
Haq Baat Zamane Mein Ayan Karta Rahoon Ga.
Dam Dam Sakhi Shabir Tere Zikar Ke Sadqe.
Mera Tu Hai Eman Juri Hai Mere Maula.
Sanso Ki Yeh Zanjeer Tere Zikar Ke Sadqe.

3
Maula Tera Shayar Hoon Yeh Dunya Bhala Kya Hai.
Dill Kar Liye Taskheer Tere Zikar Ke Sadqe.
Mujh Ko Tu Hamesha Hi Mili Hai Mere Shaha.
Har Ranj Mein Tadbeer Tere Zikar Ke Sadqe.

4
Mamnoon Meri Nasal Bhi Hai Teri Atta Par.
Hai Aouj Pe Taqdeer Tere Zikar Ke Sadqe.
Gumnami Ki Rahon Mein Para Tha Kabhi Sahil.
Har Simt Hai Tasheer Tere Zikar Ke Sadqe.
