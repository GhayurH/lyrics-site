Haye Qaidaan Ch Ro Ro Ke Sakina Mar Gayi Aye
Sir Lay Jao Ayday Babay Da Chup Kar Gayi Aye.

1
Sanwaan Nu Rehai Mil Gayi Aye Par Ayno Nayi.
Kadoon Laash Aydi Zindan Wicho Bahar Gayi Aye.
Aylaan Hoya Aye Sham Wale Zindanaa Wich.
Bara Rondi Si Bachi Jeri Oh Mar Gayi Aye.

2
Puchda Riya Aye Oh Shamiyaan Nu Shabir Da Sir.
Kyun Rondi Nayi Kithe Aaj Meri Dukhtar Gayi Aye.
Meri Saanj Muk Gayi Aye Baba Baqir Aakhe.
Mere Hise Di Zainab Safraan Wich Mar Gayi Aye.

3
Be Kafan Behan Di Qabar Uttay Abid Aakhe.
Mil Shamiyaan Tu Wapis Teri Chadar Gayi Aye.
Saqlain Tamache Khandi Rayi Par Hari Nayi.
Aye Zulm Tu Nahi Babay De Piyar Tu Har Gayi Aye.
