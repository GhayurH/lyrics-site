Veer Akbar kevain jeevan aan gi bhen ujri nou das da ja

Maar chadyan, Teray ghamane veera, haye na pardees vasa

Veer Akbar..

Aye lazmi nayi Akbar, chan veeran, meriyan sariyan man’nae

Mennu tu, BABAY de man kay Akbar mehmal tou aap laha

Khali gharan day jhandray, kyun khola, kiss nay rehna way’lay

Naal apnay, meray ghaman diyan toun, kunjiyan we laindan ja

Ujray teray ton ghar tay, booaye tak, Sara bankay java

Meray layen, aye ho Safa tay Marwa, Hajj aye ho veer mera

Akbar samajh naye aondi, marzi seeki, peer Hussain sakhi di

Rab janay duniya Chay baqi reh gaye, jadon vichray behen bhira
