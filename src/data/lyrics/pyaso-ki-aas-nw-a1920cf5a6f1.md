aasman dekh raha tha

saqqa koi faraat pe aaya tha tashnalab

mashkeeza bhar ke jaanibe qaima chala wo jab

barse wo teer chidgayi mashke sakina jab

roya alam faraat wafa mashk aur sab

goonji maqtal may shahe deen ki sada

qatl darya pe alamdar huwa

aur pyaso ki aas toot gayi

aaya maqtal se alam khoon may bhara

aur pyaso ki aas….

din dhala sham-e-ghariban ke andhere chaye

bas sakina ne yehi roke kaha

aur pyaso ki aas….

qatl darya pe….

ab mere sar se rida jayegi zainab ne kaha

rodiye sunke shah-e-karbobala

aur pyaso ki aas….

qatl darya pe….

tere ammu to teri pyas pe qurban huve

sheh ne ye bali sakina se kaha

aur pyaso ki aas….

qatl darya pe….

haath may sukhe huve kooze liye baithe thay

marsiya pyaas ka maula se suna

aur pyaso ki aas….

qatl darya pe….

khoon-e-abbas jo parcham se tapakte dekha

dukhtar-e-shah ka dil doob gaya

aur pyaso ki aas….

qatl darya pe….

roke kulsoom se fizza ne kaha shehzadi

teri chadar ka muhafiz bhi gaya

aur pyaso ki aas….

qatl darya pe….

dono haathon se kamar thame huwe sheh ne kaha

bujh gaya meri umeedon ka diya

aur pyaso ki aas….

qatl darya pe…

al-atash kehke jo rote thay wo khamosh huve

chalyi mahol pe matam ki faza

aur pyaso ki aas….

qatl darya pe…

roye sadaat to darya ka kanara roya

zindagi royi kahin royi qaza

aur pyaso ki aas….

qatl darya pe…

ye sada mazhar o irfan bani hai nauha

qatl darya pe alamdar hua

aur pyaso ki aas….

qatl darya pe…
