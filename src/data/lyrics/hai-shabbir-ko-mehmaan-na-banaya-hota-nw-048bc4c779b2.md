Hai Shabbir ko mehmaan na banaya hota
Hai Zahra ka kalayja na dukhaayaa hota

1
Shah pay aata na burhapaa na qamar khum hoti
Ali Akbar ka jo laasha na uthaaya hota

2
Hurmala yeh tho bata kia tera nuqsaa hota
paani bay sheer ko tunay jo pilaaya hota

3
Aye musalmaa teri bukshish ki woh zaamin hoti
kaash tunay dil-e-Zahra na dukhaaya hota

4
Bint-e-Zahra teri chadar ko na lut ta koi
sir pay Abbas Alamdaar ka saaya hota
