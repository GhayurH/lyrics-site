Kesay darbaar meiṅ gayi hogi ae nabuwat ki aabroo Zehra
Hans rahe haiṅ tamaam kalma go karti ro kar jo guftagu Zehra

1
Jo masaaib Batul par aaye din pe parte to raat ho jaati
Karti jo baddua kabhi Zahra khatm ye kaainaat ho jaati
Baad baba ke na sukuṅ paaya kaise jiti rahi hai tu Zehra

2
Tere Shabbir ne Karbal meiṅ din bachaane ko sar kataaya hai
Teri sunnat ada ki Zainab ne khutba darbaar meiṅ sunaaya hai
Tu sahaabi ke saamne royi wo sharaabi ke rubaru Zehra

3
Kabhi Yasrab ki paak basti meiṅ kabhi Karbal ke hi bayaabaaṅ meiṅ
Kabhi Kufe ki tang galiyoṅ meiṅ aur kabhi Shaam waale zindaaṅ meiṅ
Din ki sarfaraazi ko har dam kaam aaya tera lahu Zehra

4
Ye to sab raaviyoṅ ne likha hai hashr meiṅ jab Batul aayegi
Khun bhara kurta paak bete ka baazu Abbas ke bhi laayegi
Maatami jo Husain ka hoga us ko kar degi surkhru Zehra

5
Ye wo Bibi hai jis ke bachchoṅ ko saare Nasraaniyoṅ ne maana tha
Hai darbaar meiṅ Musalmaaṅ ne in ki azmat ko na pehchaana tha
Chun ke tahrir ki subi tukre dekhti ro ke chaar su Zehra

6
Jo tera ghar jalaane aaya hai wo to Jannat meiṅ ja nahiṅ sakta
Jo tere ghar pe ro diya hai kabhi usko Dozakh jala nahiṅ sakta
Us ki bakhshish Hasan nahiṅ hogi jo bhi hoga tera adu Zehra
