“Bhai Ko Bhai Yaad Aya…”

Haye Hasan Haye Hussain

Taweez e hasan jab qasim ne shabbir ko dikhlaya

Bhai ko bhai yad aya

Khat bhai ka parh kar roye

Dil dard se tharraya

Bhai ko bhai yad aya

Taveez e Hasan jab Qasim ne Shabbir ko dikhlaya

Bhai ko bhai yad aya

. Ek pal ke liye nazro’n mein phira, bhai ki shahadat ka manzar

kya bain kiye the behno’n ne, tha tasht mein tukre tukre jigar

qasim ka kahi’n ye haal na ho, is baat ne tarpaya

. Tehreer mein Shabbar ne ye kaha, taskeen e mohabbat de dena

maqtal ki ijazat jab mange, qasim ko ijazat de dena

tab noha kia aur Qasim ko, seene se liptaya

. Khat parh ke ijazat di sheh ne, hathyar sajaye Qasim ne

talwar laga kr patke mein, jauhar wo dikhaye Qasim ne

sadqa jo utara Zainab ne, dil Shah ka bhar aya

. Ghore se gira jab ibne hasan, maula ko pukara wa waila

pahuche jo Shah e dee’n maqtal mein, pamal badan tha Qasim ka

bhai ke pisar ko maula ne, is haal mein jab paya

. Phir apni aba ke daman mein, Qasim ko sameta maula ne

kis tarha uthau’n ye laasha ghazi ko pukara maula ne

qasim ka sarapa tukro mein taqdeer ne dikhlaya

. Khaime mein jo hi laaye laasha, sar ahle haram ne peet lia

fizzah ne kaleja thaam lia, zainab ke labo’n par noha tha

farwa ki taraf dekha sheh ne, aankho mein lahoo aya

. Mesum wo qayamat ka manzar, tehreer mein laya hai Mazhar

gathri ko jo hi khoola Sheh ne, saadat huye phir khaak basar

ashko’n ko ali ka beta bhi, tab rok nahi paya.

bhai ko bhai yad aya..
