Chale Ao Aey Zawaaro Chale Ao
Meri awaaz pe labbaik pukaare jaao

1
Labbaik ya Hussain
Naam tum sab ke mere dil pe hain likhe aao
Apne maa baap ko is paak safar me laao
Apne bachon ko bhi ehraame azaa pehnaao
Mere zawaaro meri karbobala aa jaawo
Ehd karta hun azaadaaro shifat ke liye
Aao zawaaro chale aao ziyaarat ke liye
Chale aawo

2
Labbaik ya Hussain
Khush raho aey meri rudaa sunaane waalo
Apni khushion me mera sog manaane waalo
Sare alam me mera farsh bichane waalo
Garmi sardi me meri qabr pe aane waalo
Mujhko maalum hai tum logon ki haajat aawo
Tum pe ho jaayegi allah ki inaayat aawo
Chale aawo

3
Labbaik ya Hussain
Jaanibe Karbobala jab koyi barhta hai qadam
Har qadam par mere zawaar pe hota hai karam
Saaya karta huwa abbas ka chalta hai qadam
Muntazir rehti hai zehra mere baba ki qasam
Mere zawaaro mere dard ke maare aawo
Meri maa fatema zehra ke dulaaro aawo
Chale aawo

4
Labbaik ya Hussain
Tum jo kehte ho abad wallah ma ninsa hussain
Amma zehra bhi yahi karti hain ro ro kar bain
Meri mazlumi pe roye hain shahe badr o honain
Mere baba bhi hain ab tak mere gham me be chain
Tum nahi bhule to mein bhi nahi bhula tum ko
Yaad chalte huwe khanjar me bhi rakhta aawo
Chale aawo

5
Labbaik ya Hussain
Tum jo lehraate ho haathon me uthaaye parcham
Tum ko maalum hai ho jaata hai taaza mera gham
Yaad aata hai mujhe khun me duba wo alam
Jis ke jhuk jaane se tuta mere bacchon pe sitam
Mere abbas ki jaagir sajaane aawo
Hazri roza e Abbas pe khaane aawo
Chale aawo

6
Labbaik ya Hussain
Mere zawaaro suno ye zameen Karbobala
Mujhse pehle to yahaan koi bhi bas na saka
Ye zameen Karbobala karb ka majmua hai
Hazrate aadam o hawwa ka yahin girya hai
Sher ne Hazrate Isa ko yahin roka hai
Aur phir takhte sulaimaan yahin larza hai
Is zameen par Ibraahim ka rahwaar dhasaa
Paa e musaa me lagi chot lahu behne laga
Zabihallah ke haivaan rahe pyaase yahaan
Kashtiye nooh se takraaya yahin par tufaan
Is zameen par to pareshaan huwa har insaan
Mere baba bhi yahin karte rahe aah o fugaan
Meri hijrat thi madine se hidaayat ke liye
Ye zameen maine kharidi thi shahaadat ke liye
Chale aawo ay zawaaro chale aawo

Labbaik ya Hussain
Do moharram ko yahaan mera rahwaar ruka
Saath kunba tha mera mein akela bhi na tha
Aao zawaaro mere saath chalo
Thaam kar dil mera maqtal dekho
Ye maqaame Ali Akbar hai jahaan
Mere bacche pe jahaan royi azaan
Ye maqaame Ali Asghar hai jahaan
Aaj bhi bethi huwi roti hai maan
Ye jagaah wo hai mere zawaaro
Laashe Qasim huwi paamaal idhar
Do maqaamat jo aate hain nazar

Aur ye tilla e Zainabyahai

Meri turbat se lipat jaati hai
