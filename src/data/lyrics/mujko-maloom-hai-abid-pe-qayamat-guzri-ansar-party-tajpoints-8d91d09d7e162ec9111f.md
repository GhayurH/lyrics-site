1
Mujko Maloom Hai Abid Pe Qayamat Guzri Ansar Party.
Bhare Bazaar Se Jab Malka-e-Ghairat Guzri.
Maula Shabir Se Sajjad Talak Aate Howay.
Khon Mein Doob Ke Maqtal Se Imamat Guzri.

2
Har Bashar Roya Hai Ik Jaisa Bhare Aalam Mein.
Iss Ke Ashkon Si Dunya Mein Na Rangat Guzri.
Jab Gire Ghash Se Tu Bemar Gire Sajday Mein.
Bhari Sajdon Se Sajjad Ki Fitrat Guzri.

3
Tauq-o-Zanjeer Pehan Kar Tu Chala Haal-e-Ruku.
Sham Tak Kaise Haye Teri Musafat Guzri.
Qari Shabir Zamane Mein Bare Dekhe Hain.
Nook-e-Naiza Pe Bas Teri Tilawat Guzri.

4
Har Harf Sham Ke Seen Mein Laga Zainab Ka.
Koi Mastoor Na Youn Sahib-e-Khatabat Guzri.
Zarf-e-Qatil Ko Parkhne Ke Liye Asghar Tha.
Kisi Masoom Ki Aisi Na Shahadat Guzri.

5
Jis Ke Parde Ka Nigheban Tha Abbas-e-Jari.
Kaise Darbaron Se Bunyad-e-Sharafat Guzri.
Choomta Rehta Hai Adil Tu Unhin Rahon Ko.
Mula Abid Ki Jis Rah Se Ziyarat Guzri.

6
Kay Bazaar Main Zainab Kuch Istara Jaisa
Haaya Kay Kabaar Main Lipati Howi Mayaat Guzari
Dil Pata Khoon Jigaar Cheer Peh Aansu Hai Bana
Haye Sajaad Peh Kya Kya Na Museebat Guzari
