Mere baba ke azadar

1
Vaada hai sakina ka aey sheh ke azadaaron
Meray vaasta
Tum mera vaasta daikar, meray ammu ko bulaana

2
Meray Baba kay Azadaar pareshaan na ho

3
Tumhein har ghum say museebat say bacha laaeingay
Do mera wasata Abbas chaley aaeingay
Hai madadgaar Alamdaar pareshaan na ho

4
Meray Ammu ka Alam ghar mein sajaey rakhna
Haan sada Karbobala dill mein basaaey rakhna
Main duaa karti hoon so baar pareshaan na ho

5
Meray Ammu meray Baba ka hai saaya tum par
Ta qayammat rahay rehmat yeh khudaaya tum par
Ghumme Sarwar kay talabgaar pareshan na ho

6
Jo uthatay hain sada meray chacha ka parcham
Jo bhi karta hai jahaan meray padar ka maatam
Meray Ammu kay wafadaar pareshaan na ho

7
Meray bhaiyya Ali Akbar ka hai sadqa jo jawaan
Unko milti hai khuda aur Mohammad ki amaan
Ho agar barsaray paikar pareshan na ho

8
Jo nahin sahibe aulaad sunnay woh behnay
Vasta Asghar-e-Besheer ka daikar maangay
Bus duaa mangay dilafgaar pareshaan na ho

9
Aanch aaey na kabhi majlis o matam pay sunno
Tum yazeedo kay lieay aahani deewar bano
Hoga Allaha madadgaar pareshan na ho

10
Jo Azadar sarre Rahe Khuda Qatl huay
Chadar-e-Fatima Zahra kay hain un par saeey
Yeh shahedeen kay hain ansaar pareshan na ho

11
Adeeya, Saania, Mahum pay ata-e-Zehra
Kash Safdar yeh kahein hashr mein khud Masooma
Teray maqbool hai ashaar pareshan na h
