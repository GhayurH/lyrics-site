1
Aye Khoni Lariyaan Wala Sehra Qasim Ne Paya.
Be Shagan Qatal Hoya Maula Hasan Da Jaya.
Aynj Maqtalaan Che Logo Banray Kadi Nayi Aaye.
Jinj Shagan Zalimaan Ne Qasim De Ne Manaye.
Rah Takdiyaan Ne Bhaina Banra Ajay Nayi Aaya.
Koi Rasam Naa Hoi Ujari Hussain Jayi.
Jadon Lash Ban Ke Gathri Qasim Di Khaimay Aayi.
Kiwein Hussain, Qasim Nu Gathri Che Pa Ke Chaya.
Haye Bag-e-Zahra Choon Phul Toriya Qaza Ne.
Mazloma Da Aye Manzar Dasiya Aye Karbala Ne.
 Akhtar, Hussain Ujari Karbal Nu Ja Wasaya.
