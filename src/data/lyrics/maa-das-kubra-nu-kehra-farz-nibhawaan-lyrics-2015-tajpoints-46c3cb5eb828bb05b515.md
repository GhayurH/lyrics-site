1
Maa Das Kubra Nu Kehra Farz Nibhawaan.
Tere Naal Madine Jawaan Yaan Qabar-e-Qasim Te Matam Di Saf Bichawaan.
Jay Aakhay Maa Te Aj Taun Be Warsaan Di Qabraan Te Roz Deway Balaan.
Phul Lab Ke Sehrawan Choon Qasim De Sehrey De Qabraan Uttay Main Pawaan.

2
Kul Dunya Meri Howay Abbas De Rozay Taun Babay De Rozay Taein.
Ik Qabar Te Doji Qabar Te Main Misal-e-Safa Marwa Har Roz Aawaan Jawaan.
Howay Ga Pyasa Aj V Che Ma Da Veer Mera Sehra Di Rait Andar.
Hunr Nehar Te Pehra V Nayi Main Bhar Bhar Ke Mashkaan Turbat Odhi Te Pawaan.

3
Sughra Da Khat Sunnan Di Hasrat Nu Le Ke Akbar Dunya Taun Tur Gaya Si.
Jee Karda Aye Akbar Nu Odhi Qabar Uttay Ja Ke Har Roz Khat Sunawaan.
 Saqlain Aye Wain Kardi Qabraan Nu Chumdi Chumdi Tur Gayi Madine Kubra.
Sughra Wichri Nu Ja Ke Main Sham Te Karbal Da Sab Haal Maa Sunawaan.
