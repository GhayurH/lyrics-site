Ab toh Ghazi ko do sada Zainab,
Shimar khaimay may aah na jayay kahin.
Keh rahee thee Fiza yeh ro ro kar,
Ab to khaimay bee jhal chukay Bibi,
Zulm zaalim na aur daayay kahin.
1) Pakh Zahra ka ghaar jhaalnay ki, mussalman ki purrani ahdaat hai
Pakh Zahra ka ghaar jhaalnay ki,phir yeh ummat na ghaar jhaalye kahin.
2) Ab Sakina na jaana maqtal may, dhoor say Shimaar dekhta hoga
Ab Sakina na jaana maqtal may, Phir tamachay nah yeh lagaye kahin.
3) Momino may jo hay girobandee, mola Mazloom kay jalooso may
Momino may jo hay girobandee, phir na Abid ko khoon rulaye kahin.
4) Aik hasrat thee Paak Zainab ki, kay alamdaar usko jeetay jee
Aik hasrat thee Paak Zainab ki, Bhen kay kaar kabhi bhulaye kahin.
5) Sougaye nehar kay kinaray paar, phir na khaimay main asakay Ghazi
Sougaye nehar kay kinaray paar, yea wafa peh na harf aaye kahin.
6) Bharayga-e-Batool main Akhtar, kaar raha hai dua yea ro ro kaar
Bharayga-e-Batool main Akhtar, Karabala ki zameen dikhaye kahin.
