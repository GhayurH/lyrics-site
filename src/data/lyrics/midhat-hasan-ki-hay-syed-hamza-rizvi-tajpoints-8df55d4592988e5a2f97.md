1
Midhat Hasan Ki Hay Syed Hamza Rizvi.
Yeh Jo Meri Zaban Pe Midhat Hasan Ki Hai.
ALLAH Ka Karam Hai Yeh Rehmat Hasan Ki Hai.
Batlaya Karbala Mein Yeh Qasim Ki Taig Ne.
Deen-e-Khuda Ko Kitni Zarorat Hasan Ki Hai.
Sarsabz Kaisay Hoon Na Bhala Dunya Mein Woh Loag.
Jin Ki Zameen-e-Dil Mein Muhabbat Hasan Ki Hai.
Chahein Tu Raat Ko Karein Din, Din Ko Kar Dein Raat.
Har Amar-e-Kirdgar Pe Qudrat Hasan Ki Hai.
Mango Khuda Se Ya Keh Hasan Se Hai Aik Baat.
Jaisi Khuda Ki Wesi Sakhawat Hasan Ki Hai.
Paya Hai Fatima Ne Hasan Ko Namaz Mein.
Jo Fatima Ki Hai Wohi Ismat Hasan Ki Hai.
Lanat Khuda Ki Aisay Har Ik Shaks Par Asad.
Jis Ne Kabhi Na Mani Hasan Ki Hai.
