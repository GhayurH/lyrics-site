Aye Nana Teri Ummat Say Tera Deen Bacha Kar.

Teri Beh Ghaar Zainab Ahyi Hay, Bay Chadar Zainab Ayi Hay, Tera Deen Bacha Kar Laye Hay.

Pucha Jo Kisey Nay Zainab Say Bhai Hay Kahan Tho Zainab Nay

Aik Mutti Khak Utahey Hay, Aur Khak Hawa May Urai Hay.

Bay Jaan Hujray Chup Darwazay Sakti Parday Bay Jum Bishlab

Ya Ghaar May Hay Aik Sanata, Ya Zainab Key Tanhai Hay.

Maqtal May Lagi Thee Khey Matay Dheen Boli Day Tay Thay Dushmanay Dheen

Eh Nana Mehnay Kheematay Deen, Apni Chadar Say Chukayey Hay.

Quran Tha Jo Shabbir Ka Than Maqtal May Sumoon Say Ronda Gaya

Juzdanay Tha Jo Than Ka Kurta, Wo Koon May Dubalayi Hay.

Aus Chadar Say Hey Kabay Key Hurmat Ka Banay Ga Gilaf Naveed

Bhai Par Say Sadqa Kar Kay, Jo Chadar Zainab Layi Hay.
