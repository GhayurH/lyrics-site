1
Hussain Likha Hai Mukhtar Hussain Fatehpuri.
Koi Mitaye Ga Kyun Kar Hussain Likha Hai.
Khuda Ne Khud Mere Dil Par Hussain Likha Hai.
Yeh Mustafa Shab-e-Mairaj Bolay Aye Jibreel.
Woh Dekho Arsh-e-Barin Par Hussain Likha Hai.
Khuda Ne Pocha Kahan Par Utaron Main Quraan.
Nabi Yeh Bolay Jahan Par Hussain Likha Hai.
Malak Ne Pocha Aye Fitrus Yeh Noor Kaisa Hai.
Woh Bola Bar Sar-e-Shehpar Hussain Likha Hai.
Lehad Mein Bolay Farishtay Kahan Azab Karein.
Keh Iss Ke Jism Ke Opar Hussain Likha Hai.
Woh Bibi Iss Liye Romal Le Ke Aati Hain.
Hamarey Ashkon Ke Andar Hussain Likha Hai.
Furat Bhi Tu Aye Majid Hai Talib e Kausar.
Baro e Chashma e Kausar Hussain Likha Hai.
