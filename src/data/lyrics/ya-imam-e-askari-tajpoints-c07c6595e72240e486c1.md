1
Ya Imam-e-Askari.
Behjiye Bete Ko Maula Muntazir Hain Matami Ya Imam-e-Askari.
Dastan-e-Zulm Maula Ab Jahan Se Khatam Ho.
Jab Talak Mehdi Na Aye Rook Dijiye Waqt Ko.
Alajal Keh Kar Guzarti Ja Rahi Hai Har Sadi.
Zalimo Ne Zehar Maula Aap Ko Aisa Diya.
Dast-o-Pa Ke Nakhino Se Khoon Jari Ho Giya.
Aap Ki Ghurbat Ko Dekha Yad Shabar Aa Gayi.
Dafan Sheh Ne Jiss Tarha Se Apne Asghar Ko Kiya.
Aa Giya Nazron Mein Haye Manzar-e-Karb-o-Bala.
Apni Turbat Aap Ne Jo Ghar Mein Khud Teyar Ki.
Youn Tu Sara Samarra Tha Rone Walon Mein Magar.
Haye Bahi Keh Kar Roye Koi Teri Lash Par.
Ik Behan Zainab Ke Jaisi Kash Ke Hoti Teri.
Ghar Mein Dakhal Ho Gay The Zulm Dhane Ke Liye.
Pursa Dene Ke Bajaye Qatal Karne Ke Liye.
Dhondte The Charon Janib Tere Mehdi Ko Shaqi.
Parda-e-Ghaibat Mein Mehdi Bhi Tarap Kar Reh Giya.
Giyarah Sadiyon Baad Yeh Phir Se Sitam Dhaya Giya
Aap Ke Roze Ka Girna Hai Shahadat Dosri.
Madar-e-Shabir Ka Tameer Roza Dekh Le.
Ghar Mein Khaliq Ke “TAKKALUM” Sheh Ka Matam Dekh Le.
Kam Se Kam Itni Barha Do Matami Ki Zindagi.
