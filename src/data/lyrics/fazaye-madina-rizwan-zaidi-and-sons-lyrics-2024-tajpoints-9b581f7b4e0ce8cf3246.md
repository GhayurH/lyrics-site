Fazaye Madina Rizwan Zaidi and Sons
Fazaye Madina Rizwan Zaidi & Sons.

1
Hawa-e-Madina Fizaye Madina.
Batati Hai Har Pal Hi Khushbo Luta Kar.
Madine Ki Subhein Madine Ki Shamein.
Madine Ki Ratein ALLAH Ho Akbar.

2
Yeh Qariye Yahan Ke Yeh Rastay Yahan Ke.
Falak Se Yeh Utray Hain Aaye Jinna Se.
Yeh Shehar-e-Madina Hai Shehar-e-Nabi Hai.
Hai Kab Iss Se Koi Zameen Our Barh Kar.

3
Hai Ulfat Muhammad Ki Shamil Lahu.
Hai Mairaj Ki Raat Jab Guftgu Mein.
Sitaron Se Aagay Bhi Jati Hai Nazrein.
Nazar Mujh Ko Aata Hai Ouron Se Behtar.

4
Yehi Aarzu Hai Yehi Hai Tamana.
Kabhi Kash Main Bhi Tu Jaon Madina.
Kahon Main Diyar-e-Muhammad Mein Ja Kar.
Aye Aaqa Tumhara Hoon Main Bhi Tu Nokar.

5
Howa Khalq Jis Ke Liye Aasmaan Bhi.
Yeh Mashriq Yeh Magrib Samandar Yeh Geeti.
Bana Sara Aalam Hi Jis Ke Tasadduq.
Woh Zaat-e-Payamber Hai Zaat-e-Payamber.

6
Meri Izatein Hain Muhammad Ke Dam Se.
Bachatay Hain Mujh Ko Yeh Har Aik Gham Se.
Main Yeh Janta Hoon Main Yeh Manta Hoon.
Na Sarkar Hotey Tu Phirta Main Dar Dar.

7
Madine Ki Galiyon Mein Phirta Rahon Ga.
Main Naat-e-Nabi Hi Parhon Ga Suno Ga.
Bulaein Agar Mujh Ko Shehar-e-Madina.
Tu Phir Soye Janat Mein Dekhon Na Mur Kar.

8
Main Hoon Khak-o-Kankar Meri Kya Hai Hasti.
Issi Wastay Tu Har Ik Bazm Mein Hi.
Suna Main Ne Izhar Jab Nam-e-Aaqa.
Tu Paas e Adab Se Jhuka Hai Mera Sar.
