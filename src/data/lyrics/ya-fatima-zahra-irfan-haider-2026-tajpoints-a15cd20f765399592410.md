Ayelan Suno Aye Dunya Walo Ayyam-e-Aza-e-Zahra Hain.
Our Pyaray Nabi Ki Beti Ka Har Ghar Mein Matam Barpa Hai.

1
Jo Kehti Thin Yeh Ro Ro Kar Adfini FeeLail Ya Ali.
Aao Mila Kar Ik Kam Karo Zahra Ka Matam Aam Karo.
Ban Jao Matamdar Razi Hoon Gay Sarkar.
Haq Bachta Hai Iss Bibi Ka Ho Sham-o-Sahar Bas Aik Sada.

2
Fatima Baz Atto Mini Hai Yeh Farman-e-Nabi.
Ummat Ne Jan Kar Bhi Aap Ko Rulaya Bibi.
Jab Dar Giraya Bibi Marey Gaye Mohsin Bhi.
Na Bholein Gay Hum Bibi Zakhmi Pehlu Aap Ka.

3
Aap Darbar Se Jab Laut Ke Aaein Shehzadi.
Atharah Saloon Mein Lagti Thin Ik Zaeefa Jaisi.
Baloon Mein Thi Safaidi Our Kamar Bhi Khum Thi.
Yeh Haal Tha Keh Zainab Ne Bhi Nahi Pehchana.

4
Ghurbat Mein Sath Apne Wali Ka Nibhati Rahein.
Zakhmi Halat Mein Bhi Wilayat Ko Bachati Rahein.
Dar Khatkhatati Rahein Sab Ko Batati Rahein.
Jab Tak Zindah Rahein Ali Ko Na Tanha Chora.

5
Chup Chup Ke Aap Ne Hasnain Se Aanso Bahaye.
Apne Baba Ke Madine Mein Itnay Zakhm Khaye.
Ghusal Deney Ke Liye Haye Ali Jab Aaye.
Tooti Pasliyaan Dekhein Cheekh Ke Roye Maula.

6
De Chukay Aap Ko Jab Saat Ridaon Ka Kafan.
Rakh Kar Janaza Wahin Baith Gaye Khaybar Shikan.
Bolay Hata Kar Kafan Aao Hussain-o-Hasan.
Bachay Jab Maa Se Liptay Ghar Mein Yeh Kuhram Tha.

7
Aaye Hain Dekh Ke Zeeshan Our:IRFAN-E-AZZA.
Kitni Udasi Hai Turbat Pe Na Saya Na Diya.
Tameer Ho Baqaiya Khatam Ho Ab Andhera.
Roshan Ho Aap Ka Roza Bhi Misal-e-Karbala.

8
Na Bholein Gay.
Na Bholein Gay.
Na Bholein Gay.
Na Bholein Gay.

9
Darbar Kabhi Na Bholein Gaye,
Aye Bibi Aap Ka Hum Zakhmi Rukhsar Kabhi Na Bholein Gay.
Hum Kehte Rahein Gay Ro Ro Kar Aye Bibi Aap Ke Hathoon Par,
Talwar Ke Dastay Se Jo Howa Woh Waar Kabhi Na Bholein Gay.
