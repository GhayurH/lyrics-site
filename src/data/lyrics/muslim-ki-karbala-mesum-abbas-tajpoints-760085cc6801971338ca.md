Muslim Ki Karbala Mesum Abbas Lyrics

1
Haye Muslim-e-Ghareeb.
Logo Yeh Shehar-e-Kofa Muslim Ki Karbala Hai.
Ik Be Watan Yahan Bhi Fojon Mein Ghir Gaya Hai.
Ashor Se Bhi Pehle Ashor Ka Hai Manzar.
Khush Ho Rahe Hain Zalim Muslim Ka Khoon Baha Kar.
Talwar Hai Laeen Ki Mazloom Ka Gala Hai.

2
Barsay Hain Teer Iss Par Marey Gaye Hain Naizay.
Lakhon Se Phir Bhi Muslim Larta Raha Akelay.
Na Bhai Hai Na Betay Na Koi Aasra Hai.
Phainka Gaya Hai Jis Dam Inn Ko Bulandiyon Se.
Aawaz Tootney Ki Aayi Thi Pasliyon Se.
Mazloom Ke Labon Par Bas Haye Syeda Hai.

3
Hota Gaya Izafa Bekas Ki Bekasi Mein.
Khaincha Gaya Hai Lasha Kofay Ki Har Ghali Mein.
Shabeer Ki Tarha Se Sar Jism Se Juda Hai.
Kofay Mein Dar Badar They Muslim Ke Shah Zaday.
Apne Padar Ko Aakhir Kaisay Kafan Woh Detey.
Sajjad Par Jo Aaya Waisa Hi Marhala Hai.

4
Qasid Sheh-e-Huda Ka Pyasa Tha Be Watan Tha.
Chalees Din Hoye They Lasha Bhi Be Kafan Tha.
Jo Sheh Ke Sath Hoga Sab Kuch Wohi Howa Hai.
 Azlan Our Meesum Tha Syeda Ka Noha.
Aye Mere Lal Tujh Ko Mara Gaya Hai Pyasa.
Muslim Ki Lash Par Bhi Zahra Ki Yeh Boka Hai.
