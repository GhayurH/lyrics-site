Zakhmi Thi Kitni Maa Syed Ali Meesum Abidi.

1
Dafna Ke Fatima Ko Jo Ghar Aaye Murtaza.
Bachoon Ko Dekhtey Hi Kaha Haye Fatima.
Tarpay Suni Behan Se Jo Bhai Ki Guftgu.
Zainab Se Keh Raha Tha Yeh Mazloom-e-Karbala.
Zainab Se Ro Ke Kehta Tha Zahra Ka Ladla Zakhmi Thi Kitni Maa.
Rotay Hoye Hussain Ke Lab Par Yeh Bain Tha Zakhmi Thi Kitni Maa.

2
Bhai Behan Ki Bandh Gayin Yeh Keh Ke Hichkiyaan.
Bazu Pe Our Hath Pe Durray Ka Tha Nishaan.
Tooti Hoi Thin Pasliyaan Pehlu Shikasta Tha Zakhmi Thi Kitni Maa.
Mushkil Se Hath Rakh Ke Woh Sajdah Bhi Karti Thin.
Tasbeeh Aik Hath Se Woh Phir Na Parh Sakein.
Qunfuz Ne Taziyane Se Kaisa Sitam Kiya Zakhmi Thi Kitni Maa.

3
Kehte They Nana Jan Keh Ik Waqt Aaye Ga.
Zahra Ko Zakhm Jab Yeh Zamana Lagaye Ga.
Nana Ke Baad Jald Hi Woh Waqt Aa Gaya Zakhmi Thi Kitni Maa.
Haye Woh Aik Neel Tha Jo Maa Ke Chehray Par.
Zalim Ne Hath Uthaya Tha Yeh Mujh Ko Hai Khabar.
Afsoos Ke Hussain Magar Kuch Na Kar Saka Zakhmi Thi Kitni Maa.

4
Dar Thokaron Se Jalta Howa Unn Pe Dhaya Diya.
Our Dar Pe Paon Rakh Ke Shaqi Ghar Mein Aa Gaya.
Namoos-e-Mustafa Ko Youn Pamal Kar Diya Zakhmi Thi Kitni Maa.
Woh Zakhm Samne They Jo Hum Dekhtey Rahe.
Lekin Woh Zakhm Maa Ke Jigar Par They Jo Lagay.
Zainab Hussain Zakhm Wohi Dekhta Raha Zakhmi Thi Kitni Maa.

5
Hamla Dar-e-Batool Pe Itna Howa Shadeed.
Madar Zameen Pe Gir Gayin Mohsin Howa Shaheed.
Khoon Madar-e-Hussain Ka Bikhra Tha Har Jagah Zakhmi Thi Kitni Maa.
Bhai Behan Yeh Keh Ke Asad Roye Dair Tak.
Mara Hamari Maa Ko Bohat Chena Hai Fadak.
Hum Oss Ko Yeh Bataein Gay Mehdi Jab Aaye Ga Zakhmi Thi Kitni Maa.
