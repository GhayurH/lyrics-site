1
Inn Labon Par Sana Hussain Ki Hai.
Main Bhi Karta Hoon Matam-e-Zanjeer.
Mujh Ko Hasil Riza Hussain Ki Hai.
Phir Na Chal Iss Pe Aye Adoy-e-Hussain.
Yeh Zameen-e-Khuda Hussain Ki Hai.
Mere Asghar Ko Ghoor Se Dekho.
Oss Mein Sari Adda Hussain Ki Hai.
Sare Aalam Ki Hai Baqa Shabir.
Our Zainab Baqa Hussain Ki Hai.
Oss Ko Mehshar Ka Koi Khaoof Nahi.
Jis Ke Dil Mein Wila Hussain Ki Hai.
Mujh Se Kehne Lagi Khuda Ki Kitab.
Mujh Mein Sari Sana Hussain Ki Hai.
Mar Giya Hai Hussain Ka Dushman.
Dekh Majlis Bapa Hussain Ki Hai.
Karbala Ja Ke Dekh Lo Raqib.
Phaili Har Soo Zia Hussain Ki Hai.
