Yeh Na Thi Hamari Qismat Keh Wisal-e-Yaar Hota.
Agar Our Jeete Rehte Yehi Intezar Hota.

1
Tere Wadah Par Jiye Hum Tu Yeh Jaan Jhoot Jana.
Keh Khushi Se Mar Nah Jate Agar Aytbar Hota.
Teri Nazuki Se Jana Keh Bandah Tha Ayhad-e-Booda.
Kabhi Tu Na Toor Sakta Agar Astawar Hota.

2
Koi Mere Dil Se Poche Tere Teer-e-Neyam Kash Ko.
Yeh Khalish Kahan Se Hoti Jo Jigar Ke Paar Hota.
Yeh Kahan Ki Dosti Hai Keh Bane Hain Dost Naseh.
Koi Charah Saaz Hota Koi Gham Gusaar Hota.

3
Rag-e-Sang Se Tapakta Woh Laho Keh Phir Nah Thamta.
Jisse Gham Samaj Rahe Ho Yeh Agar Sharar Hota.
Gham Agarchey Jaan Gusal Hai Peh Kahan Bachein Keh Dil Hai.
Gham-e-Ishq Gar Nah Hota Gham-e-Roozgar Hota.

4
Kahon Kiss Se Main Keh Kya Hai Shab-e-Gham Buri Bala Hai.
Mujhe Kya Bura Tha Marna Agar Aik Baar Hota?
Howay Mar Ke Jo Hum Ruswa Howay Kyun Nah Garq-e-Darya.
Nah Kabhi Janazah Uthta Nah Kahin Mazaar Hota.

5
Osse Koan Dekh Sakta Keh Yagana Hai Woh Yakta.
Jo Doi Ki Boo Bhi Hoti Tu Kahin Do Char Hota.
Ye Masail-e-Tasawof Yeh Tera Biyan Ghalib.
Tujhe Hum Wali Samajte Jo Na Badah-e-Khawar Hota.
