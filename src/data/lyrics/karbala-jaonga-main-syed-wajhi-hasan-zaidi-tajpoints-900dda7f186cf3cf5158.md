1
Karbala Jaonga Main Syed Wajhi Hasan Zaidi.
Karbala Aarzu Karbala Justaju.
Dil Ki Thandak Bhi Hai Aankh Ka Hai Wozu.
Izn-e-Ghazi Jab Miley Ga Yeh Sharf Paon Ga Main.
Aik Din Aik Din Aik Din Aaye Ga Aisa Karbala Jaon Ga Main.
Chahe Mujh Ko Sath Roti Ke Namak Khana Paray.
Tey Har Ik Aalam Mein Hoon Gay Ishq Ke Yeh Rastay.
Khuwab Jo Dekha Hai Tu Tabeer Bhi Paon Ga Main.
Bain-e-Al-Harmain Ja Kar Royon Ga Main Zaar Zaar.
Ik Taraf Ghazi Ka Roza Ik Taraf Sheh Ka Mazar.
Apni Aankhon Mein Woh Manzar Bhar Ke Le Aaon Ga Main.
Ghar Se Le Kar Ja Raha Hoon Dil Mein Bas Iss Aas Ko.
Jis Ghari Dekhon Ga Ja Kar Roza-e-Abbas Ko.
Turbat-e-Abbas-e-Ghazi Se Lipat Jaon Ga Main.
Yaad Kar Ke Karbala Walon Ke Bachoon Ki Woh Pyas.
Main Lagata Tha Sabeelein Apne Ghar Ke Aas Pas.
Ab Sabeelein Alqama Par Ja Ke Lagwaon Ga Main.
Jab Maqam-e-Asghar-e-Be Sheer Aaye Ga Nazar.
Main Bohat Giriya Karon Ga Apne Dil Ko Tham Kar.
Nazar Asghar Ki Wahan Sharbat Pe Dilwaon Ga Main.
Jab Maqam-e-Akbar-e-Mehro Nazar Aa Jaye Ga.
Gham Ali Akbar Ke Seene Ka Mujhe Rulwaye Ga.
Matam-e-Akbar Karon Ga Ashk Barsaon Ga Main.
Ishq Mein Ghazi Ke Mere Dil Ne Khayi Hai Qasam.
Main Kaf-e-Abbas Par Ja Kar Uthaon Ga Alam.
Noha Parh Kar Royon Ga Our Sab Ko Rulwaon Ga Main.
Har Azadar-e-Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Ki Hai Dua.
Jo Raqam Nohay Mein Kardi Aap Ne:ZILL-E-RAZA:.
Jab Karam Zahra Karein Gi Tab Sakoon Paon Ga Main.
