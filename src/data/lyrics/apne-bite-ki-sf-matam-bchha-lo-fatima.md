Apne bhete ki safay matam bicha lo Fatima

1
Aa geya maah-e-aza aanso baha lo Fatima

2
1) Aag se Sajaad ko aakar nikalo Fatima
Are aakar nikalo Fatima
Jal na jaaye aag se muztar bacha lo Fatima

3
2) Cheen kar chadar saro se legeye aada-e-deen
Hai le geye aada-e-deen
Nange saar Zainab hai chadar tou udha lo Fatima

4
3) Zakmh inte hai kay ghode par sambhal saktey nahi
Hai sambhal saktey nahi
Gir na jaaye laal ko aakar sambhaalo Fatima

5
4) Are tashnalab Shabbir ki himmit pe keh kay marhaba
Hai himmat pe keh kar marhaba
Apne seene say zara padh kar laga lo Fatima

6
5) Bolsa lena hai agar peshaani-e-Shabbir ka
Are peshaani-e-Shabbir ka
Pehle Khaak-o-khoon tou chehre se hata lo Fatima

7
6) Sar khule ahle haram hai Shaam ke baazaar mein
Hai Shaam ke bazaar mein
Bibiyo paar aake chadar tou udha lo Fatima

8
7) Yaad kar kay ro rahi hai baap ki aaghosh ko
Hai baap ki aaghosh ko
Godh mein badh kaar Sakina ko utha lo Fatima

9
8) Seena-e-Akbar se barchi ka nikalna hai muhaal
Hai barchi nikal na hai muhaal
Kaampta hai haath shah ka tum nikaalo Fatima
