Chashm e Azza Shoar Nahi Lag Rahe They Woh – Hum
Jaisay Aap Ko Behtar Lagay Radeef Waisay Samajh Lein
Chashm-e-Azza Shaar Nahi Lag Rahe They Woh / Hum
Gham Yeh Hai Ghamgusar Nahi Lag Rahe They Woh / Hum
Kal Raat Roney Walon Ki Ik Bheer Thi Magar
Giriye Ke Pasdar Nahi Lag Rahe They Woh / Hum
Aaye Hoye They Soag Mananay Kuch Iss Tarha
Huliye Se Sogowar Nahi Lag Rahe They Woh / Hum
Sab Ke Saron Mein Khak Chamakti Thi Raat Din
Oss Par Bhi Khaksaar Nahi Lag Rahe They Woh / Hum
Honton Pe Haye Haye Ka Tha Shor-e-Mutmain
So Nafs-e-Be Qarrar Nahi Lag Rahe They Woh / Hum
Dil Mein Kissi Ki Qabar Basaney Ke Dawaydar
Ujra Howa Mazar Nahi Lag Rahe They Woh / Hum
AKBAR Kahan Se Barhta Bhala Al-Ajal Ka Noor
Jab Shama-e-Intezar Nahi Lag Rahe They Woh / Hum
