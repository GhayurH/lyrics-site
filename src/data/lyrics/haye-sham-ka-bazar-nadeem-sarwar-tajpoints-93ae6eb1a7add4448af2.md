Sajjad Se Roney Ka Sabab Poch Raha Hai
Jo Sham Mein Guzri Hai Woh Sab Poch Raha Hai

1
Woh Lakh Tamashai Be Pardah Phophi Behnein
Kaisay Na Lahu Roein Beemar Ki Phir Aankhein
Iss Gham Ki Aziyat Ne Ossay Mar Diya Hai

2
Kis Tarha Qarrar Aaye Beemar Ko Aye Logo
Sar Naizon Pe Pyaron Ke Aate Hain Nazar Jis Ko
Be Jurm Musafir Ki Yeh Kaisi Saza Hai

3
Aankhon Se Hai Khoon Jari Ghamkhuwar Hai Bemari
Zanjeer Hai Pairon Mein Our Tauq Bhi Hai Bhari
Jalti Hai Zameen Sari Our Garam Hawa Hai

4
Jageer Nahi Mangi Zanjeer Nahi Badli
Saye Mein Nahi Betha Poshaak Nahi Badli
Maa Behno Ki Khatir Woh Rida Mang Raha Hai

5
Zindaan Ke Musayab Bhi Har Waqt Rolatay Hain
Ashkon Ko Magar Apne Zainab Se Chupatay Hain
Mar Jaye Na Zainab Yeh Kahin Soch Raha Hai

6
Zindaan Se Reha Ho Ke Bhi Zindaan Mein Hain Sajjad
Ik Nanhi Si Turbat Jo Wahan Karti Hai Faryad
Mar Kar Bhi Rahon Qaid Mein Kya Meri Khata Hai

7
Yeh Zakhm Bhi Sajjad Ko Jeene Nahi Deta
Zakhmo Se Sakina Ke Jo Chipka Raha Kurta
Woh Khoon Bhara Kurta Hi Kafan Oss Ka Bana Hai

8
Bazar Mein Darbar Mein Zindan Mein Roye
Chalees Baras Maut Ke Arman Mein Roye
Rehan Mera Maula Tarapta Hi Raha Hai
