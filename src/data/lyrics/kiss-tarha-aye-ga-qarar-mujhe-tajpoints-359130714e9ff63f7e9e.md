1
Oss Ne Dekha Hai Bar Bar Mujhe.
Kho Giya Hoon Ghamon Ke Sehra Mein.
Tu Kahan Hai Zara Pukar Mujhe.
Ashk Aankhon Se Youn Nikal Aye.
Jane Kya Keh Gayi Bahar Mujhe.
Kya Bataon Sitarah-e-Sehri.
Hai Abhi Kissi Ka Intezar Mujhe.
