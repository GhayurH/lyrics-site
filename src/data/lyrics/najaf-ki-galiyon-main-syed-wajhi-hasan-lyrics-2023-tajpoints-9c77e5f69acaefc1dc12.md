Najaf Ki Galiyon Main Syed Wajhi Hasan
Naseeb Apna Badal Raha Hoon.

1
Najaf Ki Galiyon Mein Chal Raha Hoon.
Ali Ke Tukron Pe Pal Raha Hoon.
Najaf Ki Galiyon Mein Chal Raha Hoon.
Qadam Zameen Par Rawaan Hain Lekin.

2
Nigahein Gunbad Pe Ruk Gayin Hain.
Hasen Manzar Mein Dhal Raha Hoon.
Qasam Khuda Ki Chowa Tha Jin Se.
Ali Ke Rozay Ki Jaliyon Ko.

3
Woh Hath Chehray Pe Mal Raha Hoon.
Abhi Tu Nikla Hoon Main Haram Se.
Magar Yeh Dil Mujh Se Keh Raha Hai.
Chalo Main Phir Se Machal Raha Hoon.

4
Yeh Mere Mushkil Kusha Ka Dar Hai.
Sakon Dil Ka Bata Raha Hai.
Main Mushkilon Se Nikal Raha Hoon.
Yeh Thokarein Na Gira Sakein Gi.

5
Zabaan Pe Mere Ali Ali Hai.
Yahan Main Khud Hi Sanbhal Raha Hoon.
Ghadeer Wala Hoon Main Yeh Zid Hai.
Saqeefa Walon Pe Hogi Lanat.

6
Main Apni Zid Pe Atal Raha Hoon.
Safar Hai Zeeshan Pehla Lekin.
 Wajih Kyun Aisa Lag Raha Hai.
Najaf Mein Jaisay Main Kal Raha Hoon.
