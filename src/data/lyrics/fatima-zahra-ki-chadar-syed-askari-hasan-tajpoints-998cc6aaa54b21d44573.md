Fatima Zahra Ki Chadar Syed Askari Hasan.
Hai Azadaron Ke Sar Par Fatima Zahra Ki Chadar.

1
Barkatein Utari Hai Le Kar Fatima Zahra Ki Chadar.
Kitne Anwar Sametay Hoye Aayi Hai Nazar.
Jab Se Yeh Khalq Hoi Khuld Bana Iss Ka Ghar.
Iss Se Roshan Hoye Horoon Ke Bahishti Zewar.

2
Iss Ke Aousaf Bayani Mein Na Ko Tahi Ho.
Panjtan Pak Ne Jo Aouj Rasa Payi Ho.
Iss Ke Dedar Ko Khul Jata Hai Masoom Ka Dar.
Misal-e-Pakeeza Sift Yeh Bhi Hijabon Mein Rahi.

3
Kun Se Ho Kar Bhi Yadullah Ke Hathoon Mein Rahi.
Yeh Musalla Bhi Rahi Hai Shab-e-Rukhsat Aa Kar.
Ghair Muslim Bhi Issi Se Tu Musalman Hoye.
Iss Ki Azmat Pe Nazar Kartay Hi Hairan Hoye.

4
Jis Ko Orhay Hoye Rehti Hain Nabi Ki Dukhtar.
Fatima Zahra Ki Chaki Tere Saye Mein Chali.
Meharbaan Tujh Pe Har Ik Lamha Khudai Bhi Rahi.
Muskuratay Rahe Khud Dekh Ke Tujh Ko Haider.

5
Jan Lo Yeh Bhi Fazeelat Hai Issi Chadar Ki.
Chahney Walon Pe Yeh Saya Figan Bhi Hogi.
Hashar Ki Dhop Se Paye Ka Amma Har Nokar.
Bait-e-Ismat Se Yeh Darbar-e-Khilafat Mein Gayi.

6
Yeh Tu Ma Bain Imamat Ki Hifazat Mein Gayi.
Hai Gawahi Teri Kazib Pe Thi Khamosh Nazar.
Mariyam-o-Hajrah Sarah Ne Sharf Paya Hai.
Khud Ziyarat Ke Liye Kaun Nahi Aaya Hai.

7
Anbiya Bhi Tere Zair Hain Haya Ki Paikar.
Tu Payamber Ke Liye Zoaf Mein Bach Jaye Tabeeb.
Taraye Saye Mein Tu Hasnain Ali Aaye Habib.
Tere Hi Wastay Aati Rahi Aayat Mein Khabar.

8
Hur Assi Lehza Imamat Ki Nazar Jan Gaya.
Hosh Mein Aa Ke Haqeeqat Ko Woh Pehchan Gaya.
Bandh Ke Aaye Ga Kuch Dair Mein Issko Sar Par.
Guftgu Baap Ki Our Beti Ki Hai Iss Ne Suni.

9
Iss Ke Aane Pe Khari Shan Nabowat Bhi Hoi.
Our Salami Ko Kayi Bar Rukay Paygambar.
Khud Tu Mamoli Si Chadar Hai Bhala Our Hai Kya.
Aap Se Jur Ke Hi Tatheer Hoti Hai Yeh Rida.

10
Iss Ki Azmat Hai Falak Dhanpey Gi Yeh Kis Ka Sar.
Kash Mas Ho Yeh Hasan Tere Kafan Se Bhi Zara.
 Askari Aarzu Hai Teri Bhi Yeh Har Lehza.
Iss Ke Hi Wird Se Hum Patay Hain Mehka Howa Ghar.
