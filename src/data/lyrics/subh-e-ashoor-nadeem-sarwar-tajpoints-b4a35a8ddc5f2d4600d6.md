1
Subh-e-Ashoor Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics
Subh-e-Ashor Ali Akbar Teri Azaan, Teri Azaan, Teri Azaan.
Kaleja Tham Ke Khaimay Mein Roi Maa, Roi Maa, Teri Azaan.
Suni Farishton Ne Aahoon Ke Darmiyaan, Darmiyaan, Teri Azaan.

2
Salam Lehja-e-Rasool Ki Shabahat, Salam Marzi-e-Mabood Par Shahadat.
Shahadaton Ki Zameen Par Thi Aasmaan, Thi Aasmaan, Teri Azaan.
Woh Tum Jo Ashhadu An Lailaha Bolay, Darichay Qudsiyoon Ne Kholay.
Khuda Ke Zikar Se Saji Hoi Zabaan, Saji Zabaan, Teri Azaan.

3
Pas-e-Azan Woh Ja Namazein, Shahadaton Ki Tamana Khuda Ki Yadein.
Zameen Garam Pe Sajdoon Ke They Nishaan, They Nishaan, Teri Azaan.
Hussain Jis Ko Sun Ke Roye, Parinday Shakh Par Na Soye.
Sada-e-Gham Suni Gayi Kahan Kahan, Kahan Kahan, Teri Azaan.

4
Woh Khaima Ga Tak Jo Aayi, Teri Azan-e-Karbalai.
Jigar Ko Tham Ke Yeh Keh Rahi Thi Maa, Rahi Thi Maa, Teri Azaan.
Hawa Ke Doosh Par Watan Tak, Azaan Pohanch Gayi Behan Tak.
Behan Tarapney Lagi Le Ke Hichkiyaan, Hichkiyaan, Teri Azaan.

5
Sina Jigar Pe Tum Ne Khayi, Azaan Ki Aabro Bachai.
Rahe Na Tum Magar Bachi Rahi Azaan, Rahi Azaan, Teri Azaan.
Jigar Se Khoon Ubal Raha Tha, Sina Mein Dil Ulajh Chuka Tha.
Namaz Ko Tu De Gayi Magar Amma, Magar Amma, Teri Azaan.

6
Woh Karbala Mein Zikar Lailaha Karna, Binaye Lailaha Ko Gawah Karna.
Wohi Zameen Pe Our Wohi Sar-e-Sina, Sar-e-Sina, Teri Azaan.
Azaan Mein Lehja-e-Payamber, Ayyaan Tha Jab Rehan-o- Sarwar.
Kaleja Tham Ke Khaimay Mein Roi Maa, Roi Maa, Teri Azaan.
