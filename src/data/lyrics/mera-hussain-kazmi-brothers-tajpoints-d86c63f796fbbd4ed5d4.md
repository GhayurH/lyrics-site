Jo Naz-e-Zahra Hai Woh Hai Hussain.
Mera Hussain, Mera Hussain.

1
Dein Jis Se Zindah Hai Woh Hai Hussain.

2
Iss Ki Riza Kibriya Ki Riza.
Rahib Ko Ba Izn-e-Rab-e-Olla.
Jo Betay Deta Hai Woh Hai Hussain.
Kaba Yeh Sajdoon Se Bola Ruko.
Main Sab Ka Qibla Hoon Lekin Suno.
Jo Mera Qibla Hai Woh Hai Hussain.
Khaliq Ko Pyari Hai Iss Ki Khushi.
Mimber Bana Le Jo Pusht-e-Nabi.
Sajday Barhata Hai Woh Hai Hussain.
Par Le Ke Fitrus Yeh Keh Ke Ura.
Jo Hai Farishton Ka Mushkil Kusha.
Qismat Jo Likhta Hai Woh Hai Hussain.
Aisi Khatabat Kabhi Na Suni.
Nook-e-Sina Ki Bulandi Se Bhi.
Rab Jis Ko Sunta Hai Woh Hai Hussain.
Zahra Ka Beta Hai Sab Se Kareem.
Khaliq Ki Tarha Ghafoor Ur Raheem.
Hur Yeh Batata Hai Woh Hai Hussain.
Dawa Sukhan Ka Kiya Na Kabhi.
Apna Aqeeda Raza Hai Yehi.
Jo Shair Deta Hai Woh Hai Hussain.
