Karte Te Sarwar Buka, Hai Kamar Jhukgai
Mergaya Bhayi Mera Hai Kamar Jhukgai

1
Mergai Hum Jeteji, Chut Gai Hum Se Aki
Hogai Bazo Juda, Hai Kamar Jhukgai

2
Kuwaate Bazo Tha Wo, Kalb Ki Takat Thaw Wo
Hogai Hum Be Nawa, Hai Kamar Jhukgai

3
Le Chalo Akbar Waha, Bhai He Mera Jaha
Jald Chalo Mehlaka, Hai Kamar Jhukgai

4
Dar Me Kari Beqarar, Kurte He Wo Intezar
Usse Kahunga Me Kya, Hai Kamar Jhukgai

5
Pyass Se Sub Hai Nidhaal, Pani Ka Hoga Sawaal
Kehna Wo Sab Behgaya, Hai Kamar Jhukgai
