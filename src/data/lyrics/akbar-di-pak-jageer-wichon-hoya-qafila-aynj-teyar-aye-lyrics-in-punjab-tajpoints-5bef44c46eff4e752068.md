1
Akbar Di Pak Jageer Wichon Hoya Qafila Aynj Teyar Aye.
Be Parda Nabi Zadiyaan Zanjeeraan Che Salaar Aye.
Quran Diyaan Tafseeraan Nu Leh Turya Sham Muhari Aye.
Aidhi Kand Tey Bojh Way Ghairat Da Haye Gal Wich Tauq V Bhari Aye.

2
Aye Khair Howay Iss Qafilay Di Agay Sham Wala Bazar Aye.
Karbal Toon Leh Ke Sham Taein Abid Ne Sabar Wakhaya Aye.
Is Piyo Te Veer De Qatilaan Nal Baon Okha Safar Nibhaya Aye.
Ainu Dukh Nayi Zanjeeran Da Gaya Dukh Parday Da Maar Aye.

3
Iss Qafiley Da Ki Haal Dasaan Kinj Sham Che isteqbaal Hoya.
Leh Payi Si Barish Pathraan Di Har Qaidi Da Sir Lal Hoya.
Wadh Gayi Bheer Bazaraan Di Haye Turna V Dushwar Aye.
Har Zulm Nu Abid Bhul Waisi Bhul Waisi Qatal Bhirawaan De.

4
Kand Te Likhiyaan Tehreeraan Nu Dukh Sab Pathreeliyaan Rawaan De.
Riya Aakhri Sa Taein Rat Ronda Nayi Bhul Sakiya Darbar Aye.
Ik Kamsin Qaidan Qafile Wich Jiday Kanna Choon Rat Jari Aye.
Haye Baba Baba Kardi Aye Bara Rondi Dardaan Mari Aye.

5
Aye Tak Halat Odhi Ronday Ne Haye Naizay Te Ghamkhuwar Aye.
Ainu Ghirat Mar Mukaya Aye Kha Dard Gaye Rawaan De.
Bhajday Onthaan Toon Azhar Re Digday Bachray Mawaan De.
Rab Janre Kiwein Takda Si Masooma Nu Beemar Aye.
