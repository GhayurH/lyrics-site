Hai Mera Naam Ali Asghar Main Ali Ibn-e-Hussain Ibn-e-Ali.

1
Aqeedaton Ke Gohar Rolna Bhi Aata Hai.
Dar-e-Nijaat Mujhe Kholna Bhi Aata Hai.
Bata Diya Ali Asghar Ne Aa Ke Maqtal Mein.
Main Be Zabaan Hoon Magar Bolna Bhi Aata Hai.

2
Sin Mein Chota Hoon Sakhawat Hai Meri Sab Se Bari.
Ariyaan Mujh Ko Ragarne Ki Zarorat Hi Nahi.
Kuch Mere Samne Pani Ki Haqiqat Hi Nahi.
Main Jo Keh Doon Abhi Sehra Mein Ho Kauser Jari.
Maqsad-e-Hazrat-e-Shabir Adhora Rehta.
Gar Na Labaik Main Hal Min Ki Sada Par Kehta.
Aaj Allahumma Labbaik Nahi Kehta Koi.
Meri Jurat Wo Mera Naam-o-Nasab Jante Hain.
Bargah-e-Ali Asghar Ka Adab Jante Hain.
Teer Khud Ban Ke Kama Aate Hain Chokhat Pe Meri.
Waqt Mushkil Jo Na De Nad-e-Ali Parhne Ka.
Tum Ne Oss Waqt Hai Bas Ya Ali Asghar Kehna.
Behr-e-Imdad Main Aa Jaon Ga Khud Ban Ke Ali.
Tum Samajte Ho Ke Sin Mera Abhi Chota Hai.
Ali Asghar Ho Ke Akbar Wo Ali Hota Hai.
Tum Pe Medan Mein Khul Jaye Ga Ye Raaz Abhi.
Raaz Ye Meri Shahadat Se Howa Hai Zahir.
Roz-e-Aashoor Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Ki Khatir.
Main Allah Ki Janib Se Larai Hai Lari.
Daad Dete Hain Mere Waar Pe Mehbob-e-Khuda.
Aa Rahi Hai Mere Kano Mein Musalsal Yeh Sada.
Wah Kya Baat Hai Jeete Raho Aye Chote Ali.
De Do Paigham Takallaum Yeh Jahan Ko Ja Kar.
Koi Rahib Koi Fitrus Abhi Baqi Hai Agar.
Mere Gehware Ke Nazdeek Chala Aaye Abhi.
