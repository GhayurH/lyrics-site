1
Qabool Ho Yeh Dua Meri Aye Khuda-e-Hussain.
Atta Ho Teri Karon Main Bhi Kuch Sana-e-Hussain.
Humein Bhi Jana Muyassar Ho Jald Karb-o-Bala.
Barah-e-Rast Humein Tujh Se Ab Malaye Hussain.

2
Na Ho Gi Kam Mere Dil Se Yeh Hasrat-e-Dedar.
Hazar Bar Bhi Gar Karbala Bulaye Hussain.
Dua-e-Fatima Zahra Hai Qaum-e-Matamdar.
Naseeb Isska Na Phir Kyun Bhala Jagaye Hussain.

3
Yazid Kul-e-Najasat Yeh Kul-e-Emaan Hai.
Phir Osske Samne Sar Kis Tarha Jhukaye Hussain.
Khuda Ka Hukam Hai Sajday Se Sar Uthay Na Rasool.
Tumhari Pusht Se Jab Tak Utar Na Jaye Hussain.

4
Khaloos-e-Dil Se Jo Ashor Ko Karey Faqa.
Tu Saal Bhar Ossay Bhoka Na Phir Sulaye Hussain.
Aza-e-Sheh Mein Na Karna Kabhi Riyakari.
Kahin Yeh Ho Na Keh Jannat Se Bhi Bhagaye Hussain.

5
Sawab Iska Nahi Kam Quran Khuwani Se.
Mere Labhoon Pe Jo Jari Hai Yeh Sana-e-Hussain.
Sada Farishton Ki Aayi Yeh Qabar Mein Alam.
Utho Utho Keh Hain Milne Ko Tujh Se Aaye Hussain.
