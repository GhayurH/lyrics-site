HAAYE HAAYE
SEHRA TAPTA SEHRA
JALTE KHAIME BIKHRE LAASHE
DE RAHE HAIN YEH LAASHE DUHAAI
HAAYE
JAL GAYE KHAIME CHIN GAYE CHADAR
MAARE GAYE SAB BHAI
KHAIMO KA PEHRA DETI HAI
ZAINAB(s.a.) OR TANHAI
SHAM-E-GHAREEBA AAYE

1
AAG LIYE JAB AGAYA LASHKAR
CHEEN LI CHADAR PHOONK DYA GHAR
JALNE LAGA BEEMAR KA BISTAR
USPE GHASHI HAI CHAAYE
ABID(a.s.) KO SHOOLO SE UTHA KAR
PUSHT PE ZAINAB(s.a.) LAAYE

2
BACHO KO ZAINAB(s.a.) NAY JO DEKHA
UN MAIN NAHI THI BAALI SAKINA(s.a.)
GHABRA KAR GHAZI(a.s.) KO SADA DI
ROO KAY BEHAN CHILLAI
PAAS TUMHARE BAALI SAKINA(s.a.)
AAYI HAI KIA BHAI(a.s.)

3
UJRA GHAR HAI IS KAY HAWALE
EK ZAINAB(s.a.) KIS KIS KO SAMBHALE
SAHMAY BACHAY UJRI MAAEN
HAR BIBI GHABRAI
ZAINAB(s.a.) NAY SADAAT KO DEKHA
NAAD-E-ALI(a.s.) DUHRAI

4
KON BARHA YEH JANIB E KHAIMA
GHAIZ MAIN ZAINAB(s.a.) NAY LALKARA
AANAY WALAY LOUT JA WAPAS
DETI HOON MAIN DUHAI
AB KIA LOOTNE AYA HAI TU
LUT GAYEEE SAARI KAMAI

5
ULTI NAQAB OR BOLAY HAIDER(a.s.)
BABA ALI(a.s.) HOON AY MERI DUKHTAR
LAG JA GALAY JEE KHOOL KAY ROO LAY
TU HAI GHAMOO KI SATAAI
PHIR TO ITNA ROOI ZAINAB(s.a.)
KHUD KO ROK NA PAYEE

6
AISA THA PUR DARD WOH MANZAR
KAISE KAHAIN FARHAN OR MAZHAR
BOLI ZAINAB(s.a.) BAAP SAY ROO KAR
DEKHO ZARA TANHAI
MAIN AIK ZINDA LAASH HOON BABA(a.s.)
MUJH KO MOUT NA AAYEE
