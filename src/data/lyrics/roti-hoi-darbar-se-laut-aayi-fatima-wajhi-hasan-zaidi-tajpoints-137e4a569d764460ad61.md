Roti Hoi Darbar Se Laut Aayi Fatima Wajhi Hasan Zaidi.
Na Jane Kya Wahan Se Dekh Aayi Fatima.

1
Iss Baat Ka Nahi Gham Jageer Cheen Li.
Rona Tu Yeh Hai Tum Ne Jhutlai Fatima.
Mohsin Ko Bhool Jana Insaniyat Nahi.
Pehchano Muhammad Ki Hai Jayi Fatima.

2
Ro Ro Ke Keh Rahi Hai Yeh Qabar-e-Rasool Par.
Tere Baad Dosrey Ne Hai Satai Fatima.
Zainab Se Keh Rahi Hai Teri Rida Ki Khair.
Manzar Bazar-e-Sham Ka Dekh Aayi Fatima.

3
Khud Hi Bata Musalmaan Tere Sitam Ke Baad.
Dunya Mein Kitne Din Hai Jee Payi Fatima.
 Tauqeer Kya Likhon Main Kaisay Biyaan Karon.
Ummat Ne Kaisay Kaisay Hai Rolai Fatima.
