Qaidi ghin aao

1
Tusan eewain istaqbal karo, rang poshakaan dai laal karo
Vut khoob bazaar saja kai

2
1) Daio tohfay shahar dai wasdian koon, milo gal nalay la kai lokan noon
Har ghar tai mashal jalao, din eid dai wang manao
Bharkeelay kapray pa kai

3
2) Jadon dakhal theewan shaam dai vich, pahlay bazaar-e-awaam di wich
Jari mai noshi kar daivo, har shakhs dai hath pathar daivo
Har ghar ton pathar wasa kai

4
3) Bulwan jat sharabian koon, na chadran daiwo baghian koon
Na koi taras vakhao, ja ja tai uth rukwao
Bazaar da rash wadhwa kai

5
4) Bazar jadon kar paar????, Kuch dair taee'n darbar????
Bujh shamaan eewayn ruk ruk kai, rasian dee tar ich luk luk kai
Bazar azaan suna kai...

6
5) kuj baazi khaid toon shatranj dee, vunj paishee ho see ainj anj dee
Har ik da naa'n cha cha ghin toon, vut vaan valaida raj poojh toon
Jaldi darbar saja kai

7
6) Shah ee ailaan ai sun sun kai, rakh kamar tai hath shah jhuk jhuk kai
Sir sut tai hath ro kainda ai, vut najaf dai pasay vainda ai
Jadon har val daurda akhay...
