1
Wafa Ko Naaz Hai Jis Par Ossay Abbas Kehte Hain.
Lagay Jo Sani-e-Haider Ossay Abbas Kehte Hain.
Jo Pathar Par Alam Garey Ossay Kehte Hain Sab Haider.
Alam Garey Jo Pani Par Ossay Abbas Kehte Hain.

2
Ho Qabza Nehar Par Pani Bhi Chulo Mein Chalakta Ho.
Karey Lab Phir Bhi Jo Na Tar Ossay Abbas Kehte Hain.
Ulat De Aik Hamlay Mein Jo Geeti Sorat-e-Haider.
Misal-e-Fatah-e-Khaybar Ossay Abbas Kehte Hain.

3
Jarri, Safdar, Alamdar-e-Hussaini, Bawafa Ghazi.
Hoon Jis Mein Iss Qadar Johar Ossay Abbas Kehte Hain.
Ali Ka Khon Zahra Ki Dua Shabeer Ki Chahat.
Banay Inn Sab Se Jo Mil Kar Ossay Abbas Kehte Hain.

4
Jo Hukam-e-Shah Se Rokay Rahe Talwar Ko Apni.
Jo Tanha Bhi Lagay Lashkar Ossay Abbas Kehte Hain.
Wafa Kya Hai Wafadari Ka Matlab Khon Se Apne.
Likhe Jo Hath Katwa Kar Ossay Abbas Kehte Hain.

5
Kahey Jo Bhai Ko Aaqa Ghulami Par Jo Nazaan Ho.
Jisay Bhai Kahin Sarwar Ossay Abbas Kehte Hain.
Marey Ghazi Ki Yeh Pehchan Bhi Rehan Kaisi Hai.
Sajay Jis Ka Alam Ghar Ghar Ossay Abbas Kehte Hain
