Zulmat Nishaan Hai Dahar Tu Hai Roshni Ali.
Larzaan Hai Jis Se Maut Bhi Woh Zindagi Ali.

1
Aisi Nahi Hai Our Koi Qurb Ki Misal.
Khatam-e-Rusul Hai Chand Tu Hai Chandgi Ali.
Tu Wo Ameer Hai Keh Tere Sab Faqeer Hain.
Jaisa Hai Tu Nahi Koi Aisa Ghani Ali.

2
Mashgool-e-Zohad Our Bhi Barson Rahe Magar.
Dunya Mein Be-Misal Teri Bandagi Ali.
Hai Teri Zaad Hi Wilayat Ki Ibeda.
Sab Auliya Ghulam Hai Tere Wali Ali.

3
Paida Kiya Harm Mein Tujhe Zaat-e-Paak Ne.
Maqsood Thi Jahan Mein Teri Bartari Ali.
Iss Aik Baat Par Mujhe Kafir Kaha Giya.
Kyun Subha-o-Sham Hai Mere Lab Par Ali Ali.
