1
Dhal Gayi Aye Sham Akbar Kyun Bhain Nu Lain Nayi Aaya.
Ais Dhaldi Sham Ne Chan Akbar, Sughra Nu Mar Mukaya.
Toon Janna Ayein Tere Baaj Bhira Teri Bhain Beemar Ne Mar Jana.
Ais Khali Ghar Di Qabar De Wich Mainu Aap Toon Akbar Laya.

2
Main Wekhiya Khuwab Te Jan Gayi Kyun Wekh Ke Mainu Rondiyaan San.
Main Burqay Samajh Ke Chumdi Rayi Phophiyaan Ne Kafan Si Paya.
Beemar Haan Main Veran Mere Sahwaan Da Koi Aytbar Nayi.
Tere Aan Di Taang Ne Maut Kolon Mainu Aj Tak Veer Bachaya.

3
Aj Fair Main Mur Ke Aa Gayi Aan Mainu Wekh Ke Ghar Piya Ronda Aye.
Aina Rondiyaan Kandaan Nu Akbar Main Aj Da Haal Sunaya.
Yaqoob Nabi Naloon Dukh Bohta Najfi Aida Yousuf Mar Gaya Aye.
Ainj Roya Jonday Putar Nu Oh Akhiyaan Da Noor Ganwaya.
