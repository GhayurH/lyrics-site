1
Muhammad Hamarey Bari Shan Walay.
Woh Nabiyon Mein Rehmat Laqab Paney Walay.
Khuda Jis Ke Seene Pe Quraan Utaray.
Mudassir Kahey Our Muzamil Pukarey.
Woh Noor-e-Khuda Do Jahan Ke Ujalay.
Jab Aaye Zameen Par Muhammad Hamarey.
Falak Ne Zameen Par Bichaye Sitarey.
Uthay Sar Zameen-e-Arab Se Yeh Narey.
Nawasay Jo Roye Tu Roye Muhammad.
Woh Jab Tak Na Soye Na Soye Muhammad.
Do Aankhein Nabi Ki They Dono Nawasay.
Nawasa Mazar-e-Muhammad Pe Aaya.
Kaha Pyaray Nana Yeh Wadah Hai Mera.
Hai Islam Ab Karbala Ke Hawalay.
Sada Aaye Qabar-e-Muhammad Se Beta.
Duago Hain Nana Ali Our Zahra.
Chaley Ghazi Abbas Parcham Sanbhalay.
Woh Ashor Ka Din Muhammad Ka Kunba.
Jo Maqtal Mein Dein Ko Lahu De Raha Tha.
Yeh Keh Keh Ke Seeno Pe Khaye Hain Bhalay.
 Rehan-o-:ALI JEE: Azadar-e-Sarwar.
Ghulaman-e-Haider Ghulam-e-Payamber.
Sada Iss Sada Se Zamana Mila Le.
