Kon beemar ye zanjeeron may jakrra aaya
Towq o zanjeer ko Sajjad pe rona aaya

1
Shah ne khaich to li seenay se Akbar ke sina
Sath lipta hua barche ke kaleja aaya

2
Khoon may doobi hui Akbar ki jawani dekhi
Haey aaya bhi to kab qasid-e-Sughra aaya

3
Rah takti rahi bachay ki jigar tham ke maa
Khoon may dooba hua besheer ka kurta aaya
