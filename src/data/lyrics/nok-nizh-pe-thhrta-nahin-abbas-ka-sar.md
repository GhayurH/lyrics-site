Ek wafadar ki ghairat ka ye lagta hy asar
Noke neza pe teherta nahi ABBAS ka sar

1
Dekh ker ZAINAB e dilgeer ka phat-ta hy jigar

2
Aankh ZAINAB se milaye to milaaye kese
Apna mu bali SAKINA ko dikhaye kese
Dar hy KALSOOM ke chehrey na par jaye nazar

3
Ek nazar dekh le ABBAS mere bhai mujhe
Werna maarey ga ye ehsas mere bhai mujhe
Tu ne dekha bhi nahi mujhko main hon nangey sar

4
Mujhko maloom hy bhaiya k wafadar hy tu
KERBALA waley shaheedon ka alamdar hy tu
Aaj bhi tere bharosey pe main kerti hon safar

5
Jaanti hon ke sakina se tu sharminda hy
Aur tere khoon main dooba hua mashkiza hy
Teri aankhon main hy piyaason ki tarap ka manzar

6
Ye alag baat ke paband e rasan hon bhaiya
Fakhr hy mujhko ke main teri behen hon bhaiya
Aaj bhi foooj e yazidi ko hy is baat ka dar

7
Tere hotey hue dekha na kisi ne mujhko
Aur tere baad main maara hy sabhi ne mujhko
Haaye dikhlaon main koron ke nishaan ab kiunker

8
Jis ne aqa hi kaha bhai ko bhai na kaha
Uska sar kese sar e sheh ke barabar aata
Sach to ye hy ke ye hy maa ki nasihat ka asar

9
Haaye pardes main beghar nazar aaye ZAINAB
Aankh kholey to khuley sar nazar aaye ZAINAB
Band aankhon main chupaye hy bala ka manzar

10
Jiske shanoon pe tha zainab ko bharosa asif
Bekafan uska tarai pe hy lasha asif
Kese tehrir karun soch k phat-ta hy jigar

11
