Hussain tu Nay Jo Sajday main Sar Jukaya hay

Behen nay kehmay kay darsay, quran utaya hay

Makam aay arz shay ho lasay, sada yay ahtey hay

Hussaintu nay mera lahilla bacha ya hay

Sada azan kay gungi, jo sub hay ashura

Behen yay samjey mera bhai laut ahya hay

Wo jis kay bosay liya kar, tay thay habibay khuda

Usi jagan say shimar nay laho baha ya hay

Rida tho lut gayi laykin, Janabay Zainab nay

Baray wo sook say sajad ko bacha ya hay
