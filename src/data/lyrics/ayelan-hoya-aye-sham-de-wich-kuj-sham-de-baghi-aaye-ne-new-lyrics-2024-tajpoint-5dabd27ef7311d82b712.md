Ayelan Hoya Aye Sham De Wich Kuj Sham De Baghi Aaye Ne.
Ghurbat Abid Di Wekhan Layi Har Moor Te Shami Aaye Ne.

1
Hondi Aye Istaqbal Layi Har Pasiyon Barish Pathraan Di.
Yasrab De Malik Sham De Wich Aj Pehli Wari Aaye Ne.
Maqtolaan Karbal Wekhi Si Te Sham Da Maqtal Warsaan Ne.
Jiwein Sir Sanghaan Te Zakhmi San Ainj Waris Zakhmi Aaye Ne.

2
Zainab Di Aseeri Wekhan Layi Ja Ja De Lok Safeer Aaye.
So Mulkaan Choon Tasheer Layi Ban Ke Darbari Aaye Ne.
Shereen Nu Koi Aye Khabar Karey Jinj Le Ke Zainab Aayi Aye.
Kuj Neziyaan Te Kuj Rasiyaan Wich Akbar De Janji Aaye Ne.
