1
Manqabat Parhta Hai Teri Rab-e-Akbar Ya Ali.
Teri Thokar Mein Fana Hai Teri Thokar Mein Baqa.
Murda Kar Deti Hai Zinda Teri Thokar Ya Ali.
Koan Si Naimat Nahi Betay Ke Dastar Khuwan Par.
Khud Guzara Jau Ki Sokhi Rotiyon Par Ya Ali.
Janatein Apni Bana Kar Bechta Hai Shehar Mein.
Yeh hai Tere Aathwein Darje Ka Nokar Ya Ali.
Do Sagi Behno Ki Tarha Fatima Our Zulfiqar.
Batein Karti Hain Tere Bare Mein Aksar Ya Ali.
Mard Bhi Dewar Shaq Dekhe Tu Hat Jaye Magar.
Aagay Bharti Ja Rahi Hain Teri Madar Ya Ali.
Dekh Kar Abbas Ko Sifeen Mein Larta Howa.
Khud Ali Kehne Lage Allah o Akbar Ya Ali.
Sirf Adam Hi Nahi Har Aadmi Ka Tha Wojood.
Khak Thi Jo Aap Ki Muthi Ke Andar Ya Ali.
Naza Ke Aalam Mein Yeh Zarra Nawazi Shukriya.
Tum Takallum Ke Liye Khud Aaye Chal Kar Ya Ali.
