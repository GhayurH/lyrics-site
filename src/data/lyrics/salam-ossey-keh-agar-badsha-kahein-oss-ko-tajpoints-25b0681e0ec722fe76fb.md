Salam Ossey Keh Agar Badsha Kahein Oss Ko.
Tu Phir Kahein Keh Kuch oss Se Siwa Kahein Oss Ko.

1
Na Badsha Na Sultaan Ye Kiya Staeish Hai.
Kaho Ke Khamis-e-Aal-e-Abba Kahein Oss Ko.
Khuda Ki Rah Mein Shahi-o-Khusarwi Kaisi.
Kaho Keh Rerbar-e-Rah-e-Khuda Kahein Oss Ko.

2
Khuda Ka Bandah Khuda Wandgar Bandoon Ka.
Agar Kahein Na Khudawand Kiya Kahein Oss Ko.
Frog-e-Johar-e-Emaan Hussain Ibn-e-Ali.
Keh Shama Anjuman-e-Kibriya Kahein Oss Ko.

3
Maseeh Jiss Se Kar Akhaz Faiz-e-Jaan Bakhshi.
Sitam Hai Kishta Taig-e-Jafa Kahein Oss Ko.
Wo Jiss Ke Matamiyoon Peh Hai Salsabeel Sabeel.
Shaheed-e-Tashna Lab-e-Karbala Kahein Oss Ko.

4
Addo Ki Sama-e-Raza Mein Jagah Na Paye Wo Baat.
Keh Jin-e-Anns-o-Malak Sab Baja Kahein Oss Ko.
Bohat Hai Paya Gard-e-Rah-e-Hussain Buland.
Baqadar-e-Feham Hai Gar Kemiya Kahein Oss Ko.

5
Nazara Sooz Hai Yaan Tak Har Aik Zarah Khaak.
Keh Loag Johar-e-Taig-e-Qaza Kahein Oss Ko.
Hamare Dard Ki Ya Rab Kahein Dowa Na Milay.
Agar Na Dard Ki Apne Dowa Kahein Oss Ko.

6
Hamara Munh Hai Keh Dein Oss Ke Husan-e-Sabar Ki Daad.
Magar Nabi-o-Ali Marhaba Kahein Oss Ko.
Wo Reig-e-Taftah Wadi Pe Gaam Farsa Hai.
Keh Talban-e-Khuda Rehnuma Kahein Oss Ko.

7
Imam-e-Waqt Ki Yeh Qadar Hai Keh Ahl-e-Anaad.
Piyadah Le Chalein Our Na Saza Kahein Oss Ko.
Yeh Ijtehad Ajab Hai Keh Aik Dushman-e-Deein.
Ali Se Aa Ke Lare Our Khata Kahein Oss Ko.

8
Yazeed Ko Tu Na Tha Ijtehad Ka Paya.
Bura Na Maniye Gar Bura Kahein Oss Ko.
Ali Ke Bad Hassan Our Hassan Ke Bad Hussain.
Kare Jo Inn Se Burai Bhala Kahein Oss Ko.

9
Nabi Ka Ho Na Jisse Aytqad Kafir Hai.
Rakhe Imam Se Jo Bugz Kiya Kahein Oss Ko.
Bhara Hai Ghalib-e-Dil Khasta Ke Kalam Mein Dard.
Galat Nahi Hai Keh Khonein Nawa Kahein Oss Ko.
