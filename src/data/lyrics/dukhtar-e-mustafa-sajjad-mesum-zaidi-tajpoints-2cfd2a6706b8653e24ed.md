Dukhtar e Mustafa Sajjad Mesum Zaidi.

1
Dukhtar-e-Mustafa Ka Ummat Ne Dar Jalaya Hai Zulm Dhaya Hai.
Dar Gira Kar Nabi Ki Dukhtar Par Iss Ke Mohsin Ko Mar Dala Hai.
Bolein Zainab Se Fatima Zahra Mujh Ko Yeh Baat Khaye Jati Hai.
Apne Hi Haq Ko Mangney Ke Liye Mujh Ko Darbar Mein Bhi Jana Hai.
Pasliyaan Iss Tarha Se Tooti Thin Dard Aisa Raha Keh Dukhiya Ne.
Zakhmi Pehlu Se Hath Ko Apne Martay Dam Tak Nahi Hataya Hai.

2
Jab Ali Ke Galay Mein Rassi Thi Fatima Ki Boka Thi Aye Baba.
Aao Imdad Ke Liye Aao Zulm Ki Zad Pe Kul Ka Maula Hai.
Mujtaba Hoon Hussain Ya Zainab Sab Hi Liptay Hain Maa Ki Maiyyat Se.
Sar Jhukaye Ali Bhi Rotay Hain Ghar Mein Zahra Ke Hashar Barpa Hai.
Teri Mazlomiyat Pe Aye Zahra Ashk Barsaye Kul Ke Maula Ne.
Our Phir Raat Ke Andherey Mein Tere Taboot Ko Uthaya Hai.

3
Ro Ke Darbar Mein Yeh Kehti Hai Haan Khuda Ki Qasam Main Sachi Hoon.
Our Tukray Sand Ke Chunti Hai Waqt Kya Fatima Pe Aaya Hai.
Ja Rahi Ho Jo Chor Kar Dunya Yeh Tu Socho Aye Fatima Zahra.
Kis Ko Maa Keh Ke Yeh Bulaye Ga Kaun Dunya Mein Ab Hussain Ka Hai.
Paish Izhar Tum Bhi Kar Dena Mustafa Ko Batool Ka Pursa.
Jis Ke Har Aik Gham Ne Turbat Mein Mustafa Ko Bohat Rulaya Hai.
