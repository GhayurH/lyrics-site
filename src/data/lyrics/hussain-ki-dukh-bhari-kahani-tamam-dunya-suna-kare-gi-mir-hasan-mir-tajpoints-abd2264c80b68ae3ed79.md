1
Hussain Ki Dukh Bhari Kahani Tamam Dunya Suna Kare Gi Mir Hasan Mir.
Jo Ro Paray Ga Ossay Jahan Mein Ali Ki Beti Dua Karey Gi.
Hussain Ki Lash-e-Be Kafan Se Yeh Keh Ke Zainab Juda Hoi Hai.
Jo Tere Maqtal Mein Bach Gaya Hai Woh Kam Meri Rida Karey Gi.

2
Hussain Baqir Se Keh Rahe They Meri Sakina Ko Sath Rakhna.
Safar Ke Har Moor Par Yeh Bani Tumhein Dilasay Diya Karey Gi.
Ajeeb Maa Hai Keh Chup Maheeney Ka Lal Qurban Kar Rahi Hai.
Kabhi Jo Asghar Ki Yaad Aayi Rubab Zindaan Mein Kya Karey Gi.

3
Nabi Ke Rozay Pe Ik Zaeefa Janab-e-Zainab Se Keh Rahi Thi.
Keh Bad-e-Abbas Har Qadam Par Meri Ruqaiya Wafa Karey Gi.
Hussain Wadah Hai Yeh Behan Ka Rehai Zindaan Se Paye Gi Jab.
Behan Tumhari Yazid Ke Ghar Tumhari Majlis Bapa Karey Gi.
