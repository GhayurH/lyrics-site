1
Takhleeq e Fatima Hai Abbas e Baa Wafa Sibtain Haider.
Tanveer e Murtaza Hai Abbas-e-Bawafa.
Umm Ul-Banin Jis Ki Tilawat Karey Wohi.
Sorah-e-Hal Atta Hai Abbas-e-Bawafa.
Shabeer Ki Nigahon Ka Markaz Bana Raha.
Zainab Ka Aasra Hai Abbas-e-Bawafa.
Kis Ki Majal Iss Se Jo Takraye Dehar Mein.
Ghairat Ka Badshah Hai Abbas-e-Bawafa.
Hum Ne Alam Ko Tham Ke Bas Al-Madad Kaha.
Mushkil Kusha Bana Hai Abbas-e-Bawafa.
Youn Tu Abu Turab Hai Yakta Jahan Mein.
Sab Ko Ali Laga Hai Abbas-e-Bawafa.
Ik Hath Mein Hai Mashk Tu Ik Hath Mein Alam.
Be Taig Lar Raha Hai Abbas-e-Bawafa.
Larney Ki Hasraton Ko Jo Dil Mein Hi Le Gaya.
Tasveer-e-Karbala Hai Abbas-e-Bawafa.
Ismat Ke Jo Hijabon Mein Palta Raha Batool.
Tauheed Ki Baqa Hai Abbas-e-Bawafa.
