Zindan may aaj Tanha Haye Piyasi Sakina (slwt)

Baba Ko dhondti hai Haye Piyasi Sakina

Zindan may mar gaaein houn yahan dafan na karna
Miyaat meri lay jana meray Shehair Madina

Jo Char saal Paya Seenay Pay Chaein Maynay
Teri Ladli nay Paya Teray Baad Kahee Na

Rukhsaroo Kay Kanno Kay Nahi yaad Zakhum Baba
Buss Yaad Reh Gaya Hai Zakhmi Tera Seena

Par Des may Kahee Na Mar Jaoun Khuda Meray
Mujh ko na chor Jaaey Haye Mera Safina

Zay Ray Lehed Duaein Karti Hai Aaj bhi woh
Meri Qabar pay na Aaeey Kabhi Shimir Kamina

Sajjad Kyoun Salamat Na Aakhoun Say Khoun Bahaeey
Mutti May Chupa Aaya Baba Ka Nagina
