1
Tujhse Waada Hai Fatima Zahra Kazmi Brothers.
Tujh Se Wadah Hai Fatima Zahra Tere Aanso Nahi Bholaein Gay.
Teri Mazlomiyat Pe Roein Gay Tera Farsh-e-Azza Bichaein Gay.
Tujh Pe Kya Kya Sitam Na Dhaye Hain Taziyane Bhi Tu Ne Khaye Hain.

2
Zulm Ki Inteha Hai Kalma Go Tere Ghar Ko Jalanay Aaye Hain.
Tere Behtay Hoye Lahu Ki Qasam Hum Bhi Apna Lahu Bahaein Gay.
Haye Bad-e-Nabi Na Jane Kyun Itni Be Reham Ho Gayi Dunya.
Teri Dunya Ujaar Kar Bibi Chain Se Khud Tu So Gayi Dunya.

3
Tere Mohsin Ko Marne Walay Kya Muhammad Ko Munh Dikhaein Gay.
Haq Tu Yeh Hai Keh Dard-e-Dil Tera Ahl-e-Gham Bhi Samajh Nahi Paye.
Daal Kar Gardan-e-Ali Mein Rasan Teri Nazron Ke Samne Haye.
Jis Tarha Dil Tera Dukhaya Hai Hum Kabhi Bhi Na Bhool Paein Gay.

4
Apne Bachoon Ke Sath Tera Woh Bharay Darbar Mein Kharey Rehna.
Apna Haq Mangna Zamane Se Our Ummat Ka Zulm Bhi Sehna.
Kaun Mujrim Hai Syeda Tera Hum Yeh Naslon Ko Bhi Bataein Gay.
Pasliyaan Toor Dein Teri Bibi Samne Murtaza Ke Mara Hai.

5
Kaun Jane Ali Ne Kis Dil Se Tujh Ko Zair-e-Lehad Utara Hai.
Teri Turbat Pe Baith Kar Maula Tere Jane Ka Gham Manaein Gay.
Jab Yeh Dekha Keh Chal Basein Zahra Di Sada Yeh Hussain Ne Ro Kar.
Mere Shiyon Ko Bakhshwa Dena Mujh Se Wadah Karo Yeh Aye Madar.

6
Inn Ka Zamin Hoon Rooz-e-Mehshar Tak Apne Waday Ko Yeh Nibhaein Gay.
Shukar Hai Syeda Ka Yeh Akhtar Hum Pe Mehshar Mein Bhi Karam Hoga.
Paon Farsh-e-Azza Pe Hoon Gay Our Sar Pe Abbas Ka Alam Hoga.
Jab Talak Syeda Na Chahein Gi Apni Jannat Mein Hum Na Jaein Gay.
