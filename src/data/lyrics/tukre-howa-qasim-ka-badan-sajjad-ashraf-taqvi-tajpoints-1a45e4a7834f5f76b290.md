1
Tukre Howa Qasim Ka Badan Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.
Tukray Howa Qasim Ka Badan Sehrey Ki Lariyaan Bikhar Gayin.
Bewah Hoi Ik Shab Ki Dulhan Sehrey Ki Lariyaan Bikhar Gayin.
Aisi Sitam Ki Barsi Ghata Gul Sa Badan Tukron Mein Bata.
Kaisay Chunein Ab Shah-e-Zaman Sehrey Ki Lariyaan Bikhar Gayin.
Ran Mein Gira Ghorey Se Pisar Gham Se Phata Madar Ka Jigar.
Doob Gayi Hasrat Ki Kiran Sehrey Ki Lariyaan Bikhar Gayin.
Ibn-e-Hasan Our Jalti Zameen Jism Kahin Sehra Hai Kahin.
Mehka Howa Khushbo Se Hai Ran Sehrey Ki Lariyaan Bikhar Gayin.
Rang-e-Hina Yeh Kaisa Racha Maa Ka Sahara Tak Na Bacha.
Ujra Tamanaon Ka Chaman Sehrey Ki Lariyaan Bikhar Gayin.
Lash-e-Pisar Zanu Pe Dharey Khon-e-Jigar Aankhon Mein Bharay.
Roti Hai Keh Kar Khasta-e-Tan Sehrey Ki Lariyaan Bikhar Gayin.
Haye Zaeefi Our Yeh Sitam Kehte Hain Ro Kar Shah-e-Ummam.
Tukray Hoi Tasveer-e-Hasan Sehrey Ki Lariyaan Bikhar Gayin.
Dasht-e-Bala Mein Teri Dulhan Tu Hi Bata De Ibn-e-Hasan.
Kaisay Sahey Yeh Ranj-o-Mahan Sehrey Ki Lariyaan Bikhar Gayin.
Haye Yeh Kaisi Shadi Hoi Bewah Koi Shehzadi Hoi.
Aisa Laga Soraj Mein Gahan Sehrey Ki Lariyaan Bikhar Gayin.
Dasht Se Akhtar Aayi Khabar Kis Ki Lagi Dulha Ko Nazar.
Maa Ke Kalejay Mein Hai Chubhan Sehrey Ki Lariyaan Bikhar Gayi.
