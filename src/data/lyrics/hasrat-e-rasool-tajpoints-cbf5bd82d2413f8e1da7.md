Aye Madine Ke Rehne Walo Tum Youn Risalat Ka Na Sila Detey.
Kyun Rulaya Hai Meri Zahra Ko Mustafa Ko Na Youn Jafa Detey.

1
Dil Muhammad Ka Ab Bhi Rota Hai Qabar Veeran Aaj Tak Hai Kyun.
Meri Zahra Ki Qabar Par Logo Ik Diya Aan Kar Jala Detey.
Sada Aa Rahi Hai Yeh Qabar-e-Nabi Se Keh Meri Risalat Ka Kuch Tu Sila Do.
Mera Sabz Gunbad Madine Ke Logo Meri Fatima Ki Lehad Pe Laga Do.

2
Charagaan Kiya Tum Ne Meri Lehad Par Magar Dil Muhammad Ka Khoon Ro Raha Hai.
Andherey Mein Hai Qabar Zahra Ki Dekho Wahan Ja Ke Bas Ik Diya Hi Jala Do.
Meri Qabar Pe Tum Ne Saya Kiya Hai Magar Fatima Ki Lehad Dhop Mein Hai.
Meri Qabar Pe Jo Yeh Chadar Rakhi Hai Yeh Zahra Ki Ja Ke Lehad Pe Charha Do.

3
Jo Pursa-e-Shabeer Zahra Ko Deney Koi Aaye Kyun Kar Ossay Roktay Ho.
Laeeno Ka Batlao Qabza Yeh Kyun Hai Baqiya Se Apna Yeh Pehra Hata Do.
Meri Fatima Hi Tu Bazat Hai Mera Issay Main Ne Umm-e-Abeeha Kaha Hai.
Mere Baad Kyun Meri Maa Ko Sataya Ossay Kyun Rulaya Mujhe Yeh Bata Do.

4
Yahan Ik Taraf Hai Jo Roza Nabi Ka Baney Oss Taraf Ik Haram Fatima Ka.
Tu Ma Bain Inn Ke Bhi Ho Aik Rasta Madine Mein Bhi Karbala Si Bana Do.
Meri Aal Hain Mere Lakht-e-Jigar Hain Hasan Our Sajjad Baqir-o-Sadiq.
Yeh Pathar Hain Kyun Unn Ki Qabron Pe Dekho Koi Aa Ke Pholon Ki Chadar Bicha Do.

5
Najaf Muntazir Karbala Muntazir Hai Khuda Muntazir Mustafa Muntazir Hain.
Aye Mere Pisar Aakhri Ab Tum Aa Ke Hamarey Yeh Dushman Jahan Se Mita Do.
Attaye Muhammad Hoi Tujh Pe Qambar Dil-e-Mustafa Ki Yeh Hasrat Likhi Hai.
 Wajih Tum Bhi Jao Musayab Parho Yeh Baqiya Mein Ja Kar Yeh Noha Suna Do.
