1
Sher-e-Khuda Ke Sher Ka Abbas Naam Hai.
Our Mustafa Ke Sher Ka Abbas Naam Hai.
Shah-e-Huda Ke Sher Ka Abbas Naam Hai.
Haan Fatima Ke Sher Ka Abbas Naam Hai.

2
Dunya Mein Jitna Loag Hain Sab Inn Se Zair Hain.
Bint-e-Asad Ke Ghar Mein Tu Sare Hi Shair Hain.
Bait-e-Ali Mein Aaye Jo Ghazi Ba Zaib-o-Zain.
Khush Ho Ke Aaye Ghar Mein Shehanshah-e-Mashreqain.

3
Aagosh-e-Sheh Mein Aaye Tu Ghazi Ne Khole Nain.
Bhai Ke Hath Dekh Ke Kehne Lage Hussain.
Taqat Bhi Shair Ki Hai Shakanja Bhi Sher Ka.
Bazu Bhi Sher Ke Hain Tu Panja Bhi Sher Ka.

4
Abbas Youn The Noor Ke Ghairon Ke Darmiyaan.
Ik Chand Jaise Ubhray Saweron Ke Darmiyaan.
Jaise Koi Dalair Dalairon Ke Darmiyaan.
Abbas Tu Pale Bhi The Sheron Ke Darmiyaan.

5
Bahir WOh Ghar Se Aate The Kuch Iss Waqar Se.
Lagta Tha Sher Nikla Hai Apni Kachar Se.
Shairon Mein Ho Rahi Hai Tasheer Sher Ki.
Lehjay Mein Nazm Ke Hai Yeh Taqreer Sher Ki.

6
Awaz Hai Wajhi Ki Tehrer Sher Ki.
 Saqlain Ne Dikhai Hai Tasveer Sher Ki.
Ba Roab-o-Ba-Waqar-o-Jari-o-Dalair Hai.
 Saqlain Mere Shairon Ka Har Lafz Sher Hai.
