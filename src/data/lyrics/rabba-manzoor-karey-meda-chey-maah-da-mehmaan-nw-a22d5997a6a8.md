Rabba Manzoor Karey Meda Chey Maah Da Mehmaan

Chey Maah Da Mehmaan, Itta Deen To Jo Qurbaan, Akhiye Naatiq Quraan

Jada Jhoola Rehgaya Khaali Ye Jada Ummadi Laad Lagaaye Aa
Jada Choley Wenjey Sughra Nu Jede Hen Maasoom De Waaye Aa
Kidhe Sughra Nu Armaan, Itta Deen Ko Jo Qurbaan, Akhiye Naatiq Quraan

Jeda Naane Laal Me Ki Taasir Oo Waada Tod Nibhaa Waanda
My Waarke Sab Kuch Deen Utto Naane Da Deen Bachaa Waanda
Asghar Aakhri Jaan, Itta Deen To Jo Qurbaan, Akhiye Naatiq Quraan

Khaa Teer Zulam Da Wich Gal Tey Jeda Duniya Ku Muu Mod Gaya
Jeda Paani Key Nai Sagda Hey Pyasa Eesawa Tod Gaya
Kul Da Kul Saamaan, Itta Deen To Jo Qurbaan, Akhiye Naatiq Quraan

Wich Qaimey Diss Maasoom Diyaa Amdi Ne Takdiya Raawande
Jede Laad Ladaawan Di Qaatir Payya Dass Diyaa Tak Diyaa Haawande
Wass Amdii Di Jaan, Itta Deen To Jo Qurbaan, Akhiye Naatiq Quraan

Kursheed Rubaab Da Wich Maqtal Deene Islaam Bachaa Gayaye
Imraan Da Qoon Ali Asgar Karke Inj Sabr Wasaa Gayaye
Howey Ambiyaa Hairaan, Itta Deen To Jo Qurbaan, Akhiye Naatiq Quraan
