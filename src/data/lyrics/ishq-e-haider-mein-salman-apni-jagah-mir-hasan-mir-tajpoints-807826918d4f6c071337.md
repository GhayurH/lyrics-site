1
Ishq e Haider Mein Salman Apni Jagah Mir Hasan Mir.
Par Nuseri Ka Irfan Apni Jagah.
Baab-e-Janat Pe Rizwan Apni Jagah.
Bait-e-Zahra Ka Darban Apni Jagah.

2
Aayatein Youn Tu Quraan Ki Sab Hain Magar.
Hukam-e-Baligh Teri Shan Apni Jagah.
Janat Achi Jagah Hai Yeh Mana Magar.
Karbala Tera Arman Apni Jagah.

3
Naza Mein Chehra-e-Murtaza Dekh Kar.
Aa Gaye Sare Osan Apni Jagah.
Kya Namaz-o-Azza Ka Taqabul Wafa.
Jism Apni Jagah Jan Apni Jagah.
