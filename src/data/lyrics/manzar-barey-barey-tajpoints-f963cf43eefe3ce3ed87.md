1
Shaban Mein Hain Jashan Ke Manzar Barey Barey.
Utray Hain Aasmaan Se Zameen Par Barey Barey.
Pehli Ko Jashan-e-Aamad-e-Zainab Manatay Hain.
Tohfay Mein De Ke Beti Ko Chadar Barey Barey.

2
Itni Bulandiyaan Ossay Romal Se Milein.
Kartay Hain Rashk Hur Pe Payamber Barey Barey.
Taqseem Chotay Chotay Se Tukron Mein Ho Gaye.
Qasim Ko Jo Dikhatay They Tewar Barey Barey.

3
Baiyat Ka Sar Kyun Aoun-o-Muhammad Na Kattay.
Chotay They Par Uthaye They Khanjar Barey Barey.
Nabiyon Se Barh Ke Lagtay They Ashor Walay Din.
Rutbay Mein Karbala Ke Bahatar Barey Barey.

4
Nook-e-Sina Hai Daar Hai Ik Zair-e-Taig Hai.
Madh-e-Ali Ke Kitne Hain Minber Barey Barey.
Kehti Thi Fizza Itna Na Salman Fakhar Kar.
Dast-e-Khuda Hain Mere Bhi Sar Par Barey Barey.

5
Hans Hans Ke Sanp Sab Ko Bataye Ga Hashar Mein.
Roye They Kaisay Gar Ke Andar Barey Barey.
Yeh Sab Karam Hai Meesum-e-Tammar Ka Asad.
Jo Mantay Hain Tujh Ko Sukhnwar Barey Barey.
