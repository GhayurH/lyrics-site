1
Tum Roz Meri Qabr Pe Ana Abul Hasan Syed Raza Abbas Zaidi
Zahra Se Haal Pochne Jab Aaye Murtaza.
Ro Ro Ke Fatima Ne Wasiyat Mein Yeh Kaha.
Mere Hasan Hussain Ko Lana Abu Al-Hasan.

2
Tum Roz Meri Qabar Pe Aana Abu Al-Hasan.
Tashna Labi Mein Oss Ko Aata Nahi Hai Chain.
Raton Ko Mangta Hai Pani Mera Hussain.
Uth Uth Ke Oss Ko Pani Pilana Abu Al-Hasan.

3
Khaya Tha Jab Tamacha Tha Samne Hasan.
Rota Hai Mere Gham Mein Woh Mera Gulbadan.
Tum Oss Ko Pas Apne Bithana Abu Al-Hasan.
Aaye Ga Jo Mere Ghar Ban Kar Dua Meri.

4
Kurta Siyya Hai Oss Ka Ik Hath Se Ali.
Abbas Ko Yeh Baat Batana Abu Al-Hasan.
Jab Betiyon Ko Meri Yaad Aaye Ya Ali.
Parday Mein Shab Ke Haye Dekho Kabhi Kabhi.

5
Unn Ko Bhi Meri Qabar Pe Lana Abu Al-Hasan.
Phir Ro Ke Bolein Zahra Aye Waqt Ke Imam.
Tum Ko Main Hath Utha Ke Kaisay Karon Salam.
Uthta Nahi Hai Ab Mera Shana Abu Al-Hasan.

6
Jab Mujh Ko Ghusal Deney Aao Gay Ya Ali.
Kuch Aisay Zakhm Hain Jo Dekho Gay Oss Ghari.
Bachoon Se Mera Haal Chupana Abu Al-Hasan.
Maiyyat Se Meri Aa Ke Liptay Ga Jab Hussain.

7
Nazuk Bohat Hai Dekho Woh Mere Dil Ka Chain.
Aahista Oss Ko Maa Se Chorana Abu Al-Hasan.
Kab Chain Se Rahon Gi Bachoon Ki Fikar Mein.
Zehmat Main De Rahi Hoon Yeh Aakhri Tumhein.

8
Bachoon Ka Mujh Ko Haal Sunana Abu Al-Hasan.
 Zeeshan Our Raza Woh Kehti Thin Ya Ali.
Zahra Ka Sath Tum Ne Chora Nahi Kabhi.
Tanhai Mein Bhi Sath Nibhana Abu Al-Hasan.
