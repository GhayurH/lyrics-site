Shimr Ne Pehle Sakina Ko Dikhaya Khanjar Mir Hasan Mir
Baad Mein Gardan-e-Sarwar Pe Chalaya Khanjar.

1
Kabhi Haider Ne Gala Rakha Kabhi Zahra Ne.
Kitne Masoomo Pe Zalim Ne Chalaya Khanjar.
Baap Par Waar Tu Beti Pe Tamachay Barsay.
Jab Bhi Qatil Ka Sakina Ne Hataya Khanjar.

2
Ik Angothi Ke Liye Kaat Di Angusht e Hussain.
Lash Par Sheh Ki Tujhey Reham Na Aaya Khanjar.
Qatal Zainab Ko Kiya Cheen Ke Chadar Sar Se.
Iss Tarha Shimar Ne Naizay Ko Banaya Khanjar.

3
Ik Sharabi Ko Aziyat Ka Kahan Andaza.
Jis Tarha Chaha Sitamgar Ne Chalaya Khanjar.
Aaye Sajjad Jo Bazar-e-Madina Mein Kabhi.
Tu Barh Ke Qassab Ne Jaldi Se Chupaya Khanjar.

4
Phair Kar Munh Ko Ali Kehne Lagay Ya Zahra.
Itni Be Dardi Se Zalim Ne Phiraya Khanjar.
Wah Hussaina Ka Howa Shoor Takallum Barpa.
Shimar Mukhtar Ke Samne Jab Laya Khanjar.
