Do Sabir Hai Qayanat Andar Ek Abid Ek Shabbir Hai

Ek Behna Samnay Qatal Hoya Ek Behna Naal Aseer Aye.

Ek Lash Udha Kay Asghar Di Kur Nubiyaan Noo Haraan Gaya

Ek Chuk Kay Lash Sakina Di Hath Labda Qabar Da Rasta Rayya

Badi Mushkil Naal Hay Dafan Kitay Ik Puttar Tay Ek Humsheer Aye.

Mazloom Hussain Noo Karbal Vich Chup Ghazi Veer Di Kah Gaye Aye

Bazarahan Tay Darbarahan Vich Sajaad Noo Khon Rowa Gaye Aye

Oh Burqaaya Mungdi Shaamiyo Toon Jiday Ghar Uttree Tahtheer Aye.

Ek Nayzay Tay Ghum Karda Hay Zainab Di Parda Dari Da

Karbal Tu Lay Kay Shaam Taayee Nahi Rokya Khoon Muhari Da

Dowahan Payeoo Puttaraan Nay Wahkee Ha Duhkee Zainab Di Tushheer Aye.
