Akber tu jaandi vaar na aakhi sughra nu alwida

Dus veeran teray bajon, mein keewein jeewangi, sun bhen di sada

Jay veer tur gaya, menu kalleya chor kay, hai saanga tor kay, marjana, mein veeran, teriyan raawan tey, tak tak kay teray rah

Watna tey veerna, jay mur nai aawana, karbal wasawana, hath cha kay bhen ujri, nuu tu dayjaween, mar jaan di dua

Wada ay bhen da, baandi sadawagi, teray naal jawangi, menu veeran, karbal dey, paasay jaarn walay, mahmal toh na laha
