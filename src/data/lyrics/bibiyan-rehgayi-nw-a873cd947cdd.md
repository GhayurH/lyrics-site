Sogaya lashkar ay shame ghariban

bibiyan rehgayi

Jalte huve qaimo ki bujhti raakh par

bibiyan rehgayi

Dil se lagaye daagh bahattar nauhagar

bibiyan rehgayi

Sham e ghariban se kuch pehle

jhoola chaadar bistar tha

pehlu may hamshakle payambar

godh may maa ki asghar tha

godhiyan khaali maange ujri be-pisar

bibiyan rehgayi

jalte huve qaimo ki….

Ha ye abu talib ka kunba

betiyan saari hyder ki

azmate zehra azmate maryam

izzat thi payghambar ki

chaadarein maangti name payambar dar-badar

bibiyan rehgayi

jalte huve qaimo ki….

Dhalta suraj soch raha hai

waqte sahar kya manzar tha

mehwe ibadat zere alam

shabbir ka sara lashkar tha

sogaya lashkar jaagne wali raat bhar

bibiyan rehgayi

jalte huve qaimo ki….

Darya maujein maar raha hai

pyaas abhi tak baaqi hai

shayad ghazi laut ke aaye

aas abhi tak baaqi hai

dekh zara zainab ke baradar jaag kar

bibiyan rehgayi

jalte huve qaimo ki….

Koi nahin jo poochle aakar

kya huvi zainab teri chaadar

tere to thay athara baradar

kyun hai akeli pehre par

humsafar maqtool huve sab humsafar

bibiyan rehgayi

jalte huve qaimo ki….

Socho zara rehan o sarwar

sham e ghariban ka wo manzar

chaak girayban aaye jo hyder

dekh ke zainab ko pehre par

rodiye hyder qaime maqtal dekh kar

bibiyan rehgayi

jalte huve qaimo ki….
