1
Sham Chali Aan Ghazi Das Veera Kinj Jawaan Baaj Rida De.
Ghazi Nu Ro Ro Aakhay Aj Malka Sharam Haya Di Meri Chadar Veer Dawa De.
Main Aoun-o-Muhammad Akbar Putar Tu Warey Ne.
Odhay Jigar Che Aakhir Zalimaan Naizay Marey Ne.
Main Reh Gayi Sadqay Laondi Shabeer Di Nasal Bachaondi Kalla Bachiya Putar Sajjad Aye.
Kal Sham-e-Ghareebaan Lang Gayi Raat Guzar Gayi Aye.
Din Charh Gaya Veer Sakina Hunr V Jagdi Aye.
Beh Khak Te Rondi Jaway Bas Mashk Nu Takdi Jaway Ainu Aa Ke Aap Sawa De.
Ik Masla Te Hal Karday Dardaan Mari Da.
Mainu Das De Veran Matlab Bheer Bazari Da.
Jiwein Jang Da Han Akbar Nu Ghazi Tu Aap Sikhaya Mainu Turna Bheer Sikha De.
Main Chun Layi Alam Guzar Sham De Safraan Di.
Mainu Lagdi Aye Bhain Ruqaiya Veran Tere Jayi.
Ohno Aakhiya Putar Sajjad Aye Aj Tu Abbas De Baad Aye Kar Poray Farz Bhira De.
 Akbar Zainab Di Sunr Ke Aah-o-Zaari Nu.
Sir Naizay Te Shabeer Da Kahey Dukhyari Nu.
Chup Chaap Tu Sham Nu Tur Ja Abbas Nu Hunr Na Tarpa Na Zainab Hunr Tu Sada De.
