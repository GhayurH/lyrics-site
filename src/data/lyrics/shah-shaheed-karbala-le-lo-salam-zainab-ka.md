Shah-e-shaheed e Karbala
Lay lo salaaam Zainab ka

1
Aayi hoon phir se nainawa
Lay lo salam Zainab ka

2
Haey bhaiya bahatar ka chehlum karoon gi
Bhaiya bahatar ka chehlum karoon gi
Dil khol kar aaj matam karoon gi
Farash-e-aza lo bich gaya
Lay lo salam Zainab ka
Aayi hoon phir se nainawa
Lay lo salam Zainab ka

3
Haey bhaiya lahoo mera Sajjad roya
Bhaiya lahoo mera Sajjad roya
May soo saki aur na Sajaad sooya
Har har qadam thi Karbala
Lay lo salam Zainab ka
Aayi hoon phir se nainawa
Lay lo salam Zainab ka

4
Haey bhaiya watan ja ke may kia karoon gi
Bhaiya watan ja ke may kia karoon gi
Ujrray Madeenay may kaisy rahoon gi
Mar jaey gi yeh gham zadaaa
Lay lo salam Zainab ka
Aayi hoon phir se nainawa
Lay lo salam Zainab ka

5
Haey may bhi yaheen zairoon may rahon gi
May bhi yaheen zairoon may rahon gi
Aur zairon ki may khidmat karoon gi
Yeh waqat phir kab aaye ga
Lay lo salam Zainab ka
Aayi hoon phir se nainawa
Lay lo salam Zainab ka

6

Khoon may bhara kurta le ja rahi hoon
Laikin tumhari qasam kha rahi hoon
Haroon gi na may hosla
Lay lo salam Zainab ka
Aayi hoon phir se nainawa
Lay lo salam Zainab ka

7

Saray shaheed ab tumhary hawaly

Haey lay ja rhi hoon qafilaaa
Lay lo salam Zainab ka

Lay lo salam Zainab ka

8
Haey bhaiya Sakina ko na layi Zainab
Bhaiya sakina ko na layi Zainab

Haey roti rahi wo mehlaqa
Lay lo salam Zainab ka
