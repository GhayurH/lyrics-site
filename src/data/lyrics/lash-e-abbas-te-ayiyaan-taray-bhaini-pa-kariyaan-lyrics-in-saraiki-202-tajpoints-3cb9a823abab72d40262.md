1
Lash e Abbas Te Ayiyaan Taray Bhaini Pa Kariyaan.
Uthi Khaliq Pak Wafa Da Tak Bhaina Ne Ujar Gayiyaan.
Taiday Zeen Taun Lahanr Di Dair Hoi Sadi Shimar Ridawaan La Laiyaan.
Sakoon Neziyaan Naal Marenday Ray Tak Bhaina Ne Chup Khariyaan.

2
Koi Mard Howay Taan Mumkin Hay Bandhay Hathaan Nal Ho Aswar Wanjay.
Hin Bandhay Hoye Hath Zahra Jaiyaan Kiwein Mehmalaan Te Charhiyaan.
Keeti Ali Akbar Di Aynj Shadi Haye Sain Rubab Te Zainab Ne.
Gayiyaan Rait Garam Choon Labdiyaan Hin Haye Sehrey Diyaan Lariyaan.

3
Sar Ghazi Da Bazar De Wich Haye Saang Taun Jhanr Jhanr Lahanda Aye.
Kulsoom, Ruqaiya Sar Nangay Bazar De Wich Khariyaan.
Shala Qaidiyaan Bhainrin Na Aawan Gaye Veer Di Lash Te Banr Qaidi.
Ghazi Diyaan Bhainrin Adam Jiwein Aa Lash Uttay Jhanriyaan.
