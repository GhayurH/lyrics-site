1
Jandeyan Karbal Akbar Nu Deway Bhain Sada Asghar Khan.
Veerna Main Kad Taein Haye Takney Tere Rah.
Tere Aawan Diyaan Udeekaan Ne Mainu Beemar Aynj Banaya Aye.
Veerna Jay Naa Aayon Mere Muk Janrey Ne Sah.

2
Main Musafir Haan Bhain Karbal Da Meri Manzil Sina Diyaan Nokaan.
Rool Denray Barchi Ne Tere Sughra Sare Chah.
Wadah Zibh-e-Azeem Babal Ne Veer Lagda Aye Hun Nibhana Aye.
Jan Gayi Main Mar Jana Hun Hafiz Veer Khuda.

3
Shakal-e-Zahra Hussain Di Jayi Haye Shakal-e-Rasool Nu Aakhay.
Rondiyaan Main Mar Jawaan Naa Hijraan De Was Pa.
Hajj Mera Tawaf Jay Howay Sain Malka Hijar De Rozay Da.
Karda Aye Hath Joor Dua Haye Asghar Naal Raza
