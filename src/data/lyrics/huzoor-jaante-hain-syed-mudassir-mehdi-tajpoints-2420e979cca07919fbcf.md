Huzoor Jaante Hain Syed Mudassir Mehdi.

1
Janab-e-Fatima Zahra, Hazoor Jantay Hain.
Hai Tum Ko Kis Ne Sataya, Hazoor Jantay Hain.
Yeh Kis Ne Fatima Zahra Ka Shehar-e-Yasrab Mein.
Kiya Hai Pehlu Shakista, Hazoor Jantay Hain.
Hadees-e-Bizat Mini Ke Rukh Se Dekhein Agar.
Howa Hai Qatal Yeh Kis Ka, Hazoor Jantay Hain.

2
Jahan Pe Ruk Keh Muhammad Darood Parhtay They.
Woh Dar Hai Kis Ne Jalaya, Hazoor Jantay Hain.
Madine Walay Chupatay Rahe Haqeeqat Ko.
Hai Kaun Qatil-e-Zahra, Hazoor Jantay Hain.
Janab-e-Zahra Thin Darbar Mein Khari Lekin.
Jagah Se Koi Na Utha, Hazoor Jantay Hain.

3
Dar-e-Batool Ke Pichey Batool Ke Humrah.
Hai Aik Nana Janaza, Hazoor Jantay Hain.
Nabi Ke Baad Shahadat, Bharay Madine Mein.
Khuda Ka Shair Hai Tanha, Hazoor Jantay Hain.
Tamacha Mara Hai Qunfuz Ne, Syeda Ko Magar.
Yeh Hukam Kis Ne Diya Tha, Hazoor Jantay Hain.

4
Bhula Ke Qaul-e-Payamber, Ali Ki Gardan Ko.
Tha Kis Ne Rasi Se Jakra, Hazoor Jantay Hain.
Khud Apni Maa Pe Madine Mein Zulm Hotey Hoye.
Hasan Ne Kaisay Hai Dekha, Hazoor Jantay Hain.
Janab-e-Fatima Zakhmo Se Choor Hain Kitni.
Yeh Fizza Jane Ali Ya Hazoor Jantay Hain.

5
Nabi Ki Beti Ka Yasrab Mein Kis Liye Logo.
Utha Hai Shab Mein Janaza, Hazoor Jantay Hain.
Nabi Ki Beti Ko Durrah Lagane Walay Ka.
Barhaya Kis Ne Wazifa, Hazoor Jantay Hain.
Gham-e-Batool Mein Farsh-e-Azza Pe Zain-o- Jary.
Hai Kaun Pursay Ko Aaya, Hazoor Jantay Hain.
