Haider Hoon Main Haider
Kehte They Ali Kofay Mein Yeh Bar Sar-e-Minber Haider Hoon Main Haider.

1
Hairan Hai Dunya Kyun Mere Mojzay Sun Kar Haider Hoon Main Haider.
Soraj Mere Kehne Pe Bhala Kyun Na Palatta.
Tara Meri Dehleez Pe Kaisay Na Utarta.
Main Ne Hi Jalaye They Bhuja Deta Main Yaksar.

2
Main Gaib Hoon Dunya Ko Yeh Idrak Nahi Hai.
Haan Mujh Pe Kissi Naam Ka Italaq Nahi Hai.
Bas Mujh Ko Samajh Saktay Hain ALLAH-o-Payamber.
Hain Rab Ki Sadaqat Ke Hawalay Mere Bas Mein.

3
Quran Mein ALLAH Ne Khaein Meri Qasmein.
La Raib Laqab Hai Mera Quran Ke Andar.
Iss Baat Ki Qudrat Hai Faqat Mere Qalam Mein.
Tasveer Banata Hoon Main Madar Ke Shikam Mein.

4
Takhleeq Pe Khaliq Ne Kiya Mujh Ko Muqarar.
Gar Qatal Kiya Jaon Tu Main Qatal Na Hoon Ga.
So Bar Bhi Maut Aaye Gi Phir Bhi Na Maron Ga.
Be Bas Hai Mere Samne Yeh Maut Ka Chakar.

5
Pohanchay Sar-e-Mairaj Nabi Jis Ghari Logo.
Bas Hath Milaney Ka Wahan Izn Tha Mujh Ko.
Jee Karta Tha Keh Doon Main Yeh Parday Se Nikal Kar.
Maa Ne Kabhi Marhab Ki Yeh Marhab Se Kaha Tha.

6
Tu Lakh Bahadur Hai Magar Oss Se Na Larna.
Maidan Mein Aa Jaye Agar Koi Yeh Keh Kar.
Tum Kis Ko Daratay Ho Youn Naam Apne Bata Ke.
Woh Our They Maidan Se Jo Bhag Gaye They.

7
Main Aam Sahabi Nahi Aye Marhab-o-Antar.
Jo Loag Samajhtay Hain Main Wallah Nahi Hoon.
Bas Jan Lo Sab Kuch Hoon Main ALLAH Nahi Hoon.
Mabood Hai Akbar Woh Mera Khaliq-e-Akbar.
