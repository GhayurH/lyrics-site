1
Gham Se Mere Bachon Ko Bachana Meri Fizza.
Hai Tere Hawalay Yeh Gharana Meri Fizza.
Shabbar Ke Liye Do Do Kafan Rakhna Bana Kar.
Teeron Bhara Taboot-e-Hasan Aaye Ga Jab Ghar.

2
Taboot Ko Zainab Se Chupana Meri Fizza.
Ik Door Kara Tum Pe Abhi Aana Hai Bibi.
Bazar Mein Be Pardah Tumhein Jana Hai Bibi.
Yeh Baat Na Zainab Ko Batana Meri Fizza.

3
Zainab Meri Bazar Mein Jaye Gi Khulay Sar.
Har Moor Par Malono Se Yeh Khaye Gi Pathar.
Har Moor Pe Sath Oss Ka Nibhana Meri Fizza.
Rakh Lena Kayi Chadrein Saman-e-Safar Mein.

4
Rakh Lena Ridaein Sabhi Jo Bhi Milein Ghar Mein.
Ran Ke Liye Jab Hona Rawana Meri Fizza.
Dekho Abhi Masoom Bohat Hain Mere Bachay.
Gham Se Abhi Magmoom Bohat Hain Mere Bachay.

5
Tum Door Kahin Inn Se Na Jana Meri Fizza.
Ki:NOOR ALI:, Fatima Zahra Ne Wasiyat.
Bachoon Ko Mere Sabar Ki Tum Karna Hidayat.
Ro Kar Mujhe Mat Unn Ko Rolana Meri Fizza.
