Koofay Main Karbala Mir Hasan Mir

1
Kofay Mein Karbala Ke Hain Aasaar Ya Hussain.
Shair-e-Khuda Pe Chal Gayi Talwar Ya Hussain.
Khak Urr Rahi Hai Masjid-e-Kofa Mein Har Taraf.
Phir Lut Gayi Rasool Ki Sarkar Ya Hussain.
Zainab Ko Dekh Dekh Ke Rotay Hain Murtaza.
Yaad Aa Raha Hai Sham Ka Bazar Ya Hussain.

2
Behno Ke Ashk Ponchne Walay Tu Aap Hain.
Haye Magar Sakina Ke Rukhsar Ya Hussain.
Thehrey Ga Terah Zarboon Pe Ja Kar Yeh Silsila.
Sajday Mein Aaj Pehla Howa Waar Ya Hussain.
Kozon Mein Shair Aaya Tu Taktay They Bar Bar.
Tere Labon Ko Haider-e-Karrar Ya Hussain.

3
Yaad Aa Raha Hai Naza Mein Maula Ko Woh Sama.
Haye Zainab Hai Our Kocha-o-Bazar Ya Hussain.
Ab Maa Ke Sath Baap Ko Roein Gi Betiyaan.
Gham Our De Ga Eid Ka Tehwar Ya Hussain.
 Akbar Kiya Ali Ne Shahadat Se Ahtamam.
Kofay Se Chun Ke Apne Wafadar Ya Hussain.
