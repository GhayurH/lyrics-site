1
Ansu Huwe Rawaan Taha Narsinh Lyrics
Maula Hasan Ki Aankhon Se Aanso Hoye Rawaan.
Chehray Pe Maa Ke Dekha Tamachay Ka Jo Nishaan.
Zalim Ne Aag Dar Pe Lagai Thi Jis Ghari.

2
Bint-e-Rasool Thi Pas-e-Darwaza Hi Khari.
Pehlu Pe Jalta Dar Woh Giraya Hai Al-Amaan.
Shabar Ne Rakha Hathoon Ko Khwahar Ki Aankhon Par.
Tukray Na Dil Ho Dard Ke Manzar Ko Dekh Kar.

3
Mohsin Ko Mar Wala Yeh Keh Kar Giri Hai Maa.
Chehray Ka Pardah Khon Mein Doba Howa Tamam.
Pehlu Sanbhalay Karti Thin Fizza Se Yeh Kalam.
Meri Hain Tooti Haye Dekho Yeh Pasliyaan.

4
Zalim Se Fizza Ne Tha Bachaya Batool Ko.
Mara Hai Durrah Our Tamacha Batool Ko.
Dam Ruk Raha Tha Honton Se Bhi Khon Tha Rawaan.
Zakhmo Ka Khon Thamta Nahi Hai Batool Ka.

5
Chehra Bhi Zard Ho Gaya Bint-e-Rasool Ka.
Ik Dam Zaeefa Ho Gayin Mazloma Na Tawaan.
Zahra Pe Zulm Dekh Ke Roney Lagay Hussain.
Sar Ka Amama Phaink Ke Karne Lagay Yeh Bain.

6
Pala Hai Hum Ko Peeas Ke Iss Maa Ne Chakiyaan.
 Saaib Janaza Le Ke Chaley Iss Tarha Imam.
Betay They Sath Ghar Mein Rukein Betiyaan Tamam.
Youn Janat Ul-Baqee Ko Jata Tha Karwaan.
