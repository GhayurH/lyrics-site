1
Qalam Jo Lashkar-e-Qirtas Ka Salar Bana.
Hasan Ke Hath Ka Har Lafz Hi Talwar Bana.
Hasan Ke Samne Ik Jang Ka Maidan Bana.
Har Aik Lafz Wahan Aahni Kirdar Bana.

2
Banaye Zair-o-Zabar, Teer-o-Sina Maula Ne.
Hasan Ka Dast-e-Elahi Wahan Sardar Bana.
Hasan Ne Hath Se Takhleeq Kiya Hai Jin Ko.
Har Ik Warq Pe Sipahi Woh Zulfiqar Bana.

3
Hudaibiya Ki Tarha Phir Se Yeh Maidan Saja.
Yahan Wajood-e-Hasan Ahmad-e-Mukhtar Bana.
Qalam Se Natiq-e-Quran Ne Aayat Likhein.
Bara-e-Aman Yadullah Se Shahkar Bana.

4
Chala Yeh Lashkar-e-Alfaz Jo Dushman Ki Taraf.
Tu Oss Ka Safah e Qirtas Hi Rahwar Bana.
Ali Ke Lal Ne Ijaz Dikhaya Aisa.
Hasan Ka Likha Hi Maidan Mein Lalkar Bana.

5
Main Chahta Tha Likhon Ishq-e-Hasan Kagaz Par.
Miley Haroof Tu Ik Lafz Janisar Bana.
Attaye Fatima Zahra Hai Yeh Qambar Ka Qalam.
Hasan Ki Madah Mein Jo Zeenat-e-Ashar Bana.
