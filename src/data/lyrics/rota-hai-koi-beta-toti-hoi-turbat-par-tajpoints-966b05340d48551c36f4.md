1
Rota Hai Koi Beta Toti Hoi Turbat Par.
Kyunkar Na Laho Roye Madar Ki Woh Ghurbat Par.
Yeh Ajar Risalat Ka Ummat Ne Diya Kaisa.
Filqurba Ki Aayat Ka Matlab Hi Badal Dala.
Rone Na Koi Aaya Mohsin Ki Shahadat Par.
Dekh Aayi Hai Masjid Mein Jo Zulm-o-Sitam Zahra.
Balon Ki Safaidi Se Ho Jaye Ga Andaza.
Guzari Jo Qayamat Thi Khaton-e-Qayamat Par.
Zahra Ki Gawahi Ko Jis Tarha Hai Jhutlaya.
Tehreer Ke Purzon Ko Jis Tarha Uraya Tha.
Kab Unn Ko Yaqeen Ho Ga Nana Ki Risalat Par.
Chalees Banaein Thi Qabarein Jo Madine Mein.
Mula Yeh Samajhte The Nafrat Thi Jo Seenay Mein.
Woh Zulm Howa Zahir Shabar Ki Shahadat Par.
Aati Hai Baqaiya Se Mehdi Ki Sada Ab Bhi.
Har Gham Ki Nishani Woh Hai Pas Mere Maa Ki.
Jo Lakriyaan Laye The Zalim Dar-e-Rehmat Par.
Ya Fatima Zahra Woh Kab Bhola Tera Mehdi.
Darwaze Talay Mujh Ko Ro Ke Jo Sada Di Thi.
Hoon Giriya Kunaa Ab Bhi Iss Waqt-e-Musibat Par.
Dunya Ko Haqeeqat Yeh Maloom Nahi Qasim.
Bas Bagah-e-Fidak Par HI Naraz Tha Kab Hakim.
Zahra Ne Shahdat Tu Payi Hai Wilayat Par.
