1
Main Sar Uryan Haan Mir Hasan Mir.
Main Sar Uryan Haan Abbas Kiwein Sham Jawaan Gi.
Rida De Baaj Walaan Da Kiwein Pardah Banawaan Gi.
Taiday Lashay Te Roi Nayi Te Tur Payi Sham De Pasay.
Hoya Wadah Main Rowan Layi Teri Turbat Te Aawaan Gi.
Mainday Babay Koon Hasrat Rayi Mainda Lehja Oh Nayi Suniya.
Main Wich Darbar Das Veran Kiwein Khutba Sunawaan Gi.
Maindi Gardan Taun Hath Mainday Jo Nayi Khulday Dasa Veran.
Shimar De Zulm Taun Kiwein Sakina Koon Bachawaan Gi.
Ajay Ta Shimar De Qabzey Che Hai Kujh Keh Nayi Sangdi.
Jay Wapis Mil Gayi Chadar Teri Turbat Te Pawaan Gi.
Zarra Na Fikar Kar Veran Jay Safraan Wich Maikon Miliya.
Main Apne Hissay Da Pani V Balaan Koon Pilawaan Gi.
Wafa De Shehanshah Taun Dhi Ali Di Jaun Aahdi Rayi.
Tu Sham Aawan De Abid Koon Main Ghazi Ban Wikhawaan Gi.
