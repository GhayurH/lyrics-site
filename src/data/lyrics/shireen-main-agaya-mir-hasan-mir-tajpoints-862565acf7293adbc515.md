1
Shireen Main Agaya Mir Hasan Mir Lyrics
Lut Ke Jab Qafila Shereen Ke Makaan Tak Pohancha.
Nook-e-Naiza Se Yeh Aane Lagi Maula Ki Sada.
Wadah Howa Wafa Ik Sar Ne Di Sada Shereen Main Aa Gaya.

2
Ghar Mera Lut Gaya Zainab Hai Be Rida Shereen Main Aa Gaya.
Kuch Dair Ke Liye Hai Tere Ghar Mein Bas Qiyam.
Jaldi Se Kar Ghareeb Ki Majlis Ka Ahtamam.
Aaein Hain Fatima Farsh-e-Azza Bicha.

3
Marey Hain Sang Shehar Ke Logon Ne Iss Qadar.
Behno Ke Sath Sath Hai Zakhmi Mera Bhi Sar.
Pehchan Ab Zara Chehra Lahu Bhara.
Woh Keh Rahi Thi Bhai Mujhe Sharam Aaye Gi.

4
Shereen Ko Bint-e-Fatima Kya Munh Dikhaye Gi.
Zainab Hai Be Rida Ghar Ke Diye Bhuja.
Pairon Se Meri Behno Ke Kantay Nikal Le.
Woh Dekh Gir Rahi Hai Sakina Sanbhal Le.

5
Le Kar Tumhare Ghar Pyason Ka Qafila.
Rukhsat Ke Din Ridaein Tujhey Dein Thin Kis Liye.
Sar Nangay Mere Kunbay Ki Halat Se Jan Le.
Ab Waqt Aa Gaya La Chadrein Woh La.

6
Zakhmo Se Choor Chehray Ko Pehchanti Tu Ho.
Mehman Tum Hussain Ko Ab Manti Tu Ho.
Barh Kar Utaro Phir Naizay Se Sar Mera.
Baraat Le Ke Aaya Hoon Kariyal Jawan Ki.

7
Halat Magar Ajeeb Hai Iss Khandan Ki.
Dulha Ke Sath Hai Naiza Pe Sar Mera.
Tanay Tu Roz Sunti Thi Woh Bhool Jaein Gay.
Itnay Ameer Loag Tere Ghar Kyun Aaein Gay.

8
Ja Unn Se Keh De Ja Naizay Pe Hoon Tu Kya.
Shereen Rida Sanbhal Ke Dar Ki Taraf Barhein.
Haye Hussain Keh Ke Takallum Woh Ro Parin.
Zakhmi Labon Se Jab Aane Lagi Sada Shereen Main Aa Gaya.
