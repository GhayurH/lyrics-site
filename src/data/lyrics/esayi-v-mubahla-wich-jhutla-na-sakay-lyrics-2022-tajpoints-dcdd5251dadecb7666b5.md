1
Esayi V Mubahla Wich Jhutla Na Sakay Jiss Nu.
Tu Deen Da Masla Bhul Gayi Ayein Darbar Ich Ummat Kehndi Rayi Aayat Di Oss Waris Nu.
Aj Haal Ajeeb Aye Zahra Da Nayi Dar Yasrab Da Koi Khulda.
Nayi Putraan Nu Kuj Das Sakdi Dhiyaan Masoom Te Kamsin Ne Dukh Dard Sunaway Kis Nu.

2
Mariyam Di Ummat Man Gayi Si Jholay Che Gawahi Esa Di.
Par Musalmana Ne Zahra De Putraan Nu Sacha Manya Si Na Sacha Manya Iss Nu.
Si Sand Te Naam Muhammad Da Jehra Tukray Tukray Kar Chadiya.
Kitay Meem Digi Kitay Hey Dig Payi Kitay Meem Te Kitlay Daal Harf Aayi Sharam Nayi Be Hiss Nu.

3
Aye Kafan Ich Maa Da Wadah Si Main Karbal Millan Layi Aawaan Gi.
Tera Soag Manawan Har Ghar Wich Main Aap Sunnan Layi Jawaan Gi Shabeer Teri Majlis Nu.
 Akbar Hajay Mahdi Aana Si Par Hasrat Wekh Aye Zahra Di.
Balday Bohey De Heth Payi Ghurbat De Welay Dendi Aye Wajaan Ibn-e-Narjis Nu.
