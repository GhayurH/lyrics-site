1
Dar Gira Kaise Gira Kyun Syeda Roti Rahi?
Haye Gasib Hans Raha Tha Fatima Roti Rahi.
Yeh Watan Ka Haal Hai Bibi Ne Socha Sham Ka.
Chom Kar Zainab Ke Sar Ki Woh Rida Roti Rahi.
Jis Jagah Kal Tak Muhammad Baithtay They Oss Jagah.
Dekh Kar Baba Ke Qatil Kis Tarha Roti Rahi.
Dekh Kar Mati Pe Baba Ki Sand Woh Mar Gayi.
Tham Kar Woh Dil Ko Bas Hairat Zadah Roti Rahi.
Ro Ke Pocha Murtaza Ke Kyun Galay Mein Hai Rasan?
Aag Ke Sholon Mein Ghir Kar Woh Kisa Roti Rahi.
Gaud Khali Kar Gaya Ajar-e-Risalat Youn Diya.
Kis Tarha Socho Muhammad Ki Baqa Roti Rahi.
Zair-e-Khanjar Dekh Kar Shabbir Ko Woh Chup Rahi.
Dekh Kar Nezay Pe Beti Ki Rida Roti Rahi.
Yeh Faqat Mohsin Nahi Hai Aal-e-Ahmad Hai Batool.
Gaud Mein Kausar Liye Kyun Innama Roti Rahi?
Poetess::Fakhira Batool:
