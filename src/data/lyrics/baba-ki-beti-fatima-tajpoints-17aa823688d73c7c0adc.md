Aye Sakhi Baba Ki Beti Fatima.
Manqabat Parhta Hoon Teri Fatima.

1
Parhna Chahi Jab Bhi Teri Manqabat.
Sorah-e-Rehman Parh Li Fatima.
Khud Lab-e-Ahmad Se Kehta Hai Khuda.
Teri Marzi, Meri Marzi, Fatima.

2
Aasmaan Par Ja Ke Aayat Ban Gayi.
Aap Ke Hathoon Ki Roti Fatima.
Aap Ke Romal Se Shabeer Ne.
Ki Hai Hur Ki Taj Poshi Fatima.

3
Sajday Kar Kar Ke Khuda Ko Aap Ne.
Shan Ibadat Ki Barhai Fatima.
Sab Farishton Ka Hai Jo Sardar Woh.
Hai Tere Bachoon Ka Darzi Fatima.

4
Hal Atta Hoti Thi Tab Mehway Tawaf.
Peesti Thin Jab Bhi Chaki Fatima.
Raz-e-Magzoob-e-Alaihim Hai Asad.
Yani Hai Ghaiz-e-Elahi Fatima.
