1) Nana teri Zainab nai who dard uthain hain
Zalim ka zulam dekha Anso nikal aayai

1
2) Mai Shaam ki galiyon sai sar nangai guzar aayi
Nana teri ummat nai din yai bhi dikhayai hai n

2
3) Pathar jo barastai thai Zehra kai gharanai per
Is haal pai suraj nai khud ashk bahayai hai n

3
4) Yai khoon bhara kurta hai terai nawasai ka
Bhahi ki nishani ham terai liyai laayai hain

4
5) Gham kha gaya Zainab ko nana Ali Akber ka
Mat poocho watan mai ham kis terha sai aayai hain

5
6) Zindaan kai andhairai mai neend aai Sakina ko
Ham Qabar Sakina ki wahan chor kai aai hain

6
7) Soghat-e-safar na na pardais sai ham qaidi
Yai neel rasan kai hain jo shano pai layain hain
