1
Koi Ghaibat Mein Baitha Khon Ke Anso Bahata Hai.
Osse Zainab Ka Jab Bazaar Jana Yaad Aata Hai.
Koi Ghamkhuwar Ghaibat Mein Muyasar Hi Nahi Oss Ko.
So Tanhai Mein Har Noha WO Khud Ko Hi Sunata Hai.

2
Unhi Galiyon Mein Chal Chal Kar Jahan Chalti Rahi Zainab.
Nishaan Zainab Ke Qadmo Ke Wo Hathon Se Mitata Hai.
Baqiye Mein Sadaye Wah Hussaina Goonj Uthti Hai.
WO Jab Bhi Qabar-e-Zahra Aa Ke Seene Se Lagata Hai.

3
Nahi Thamte Hain Oss Ki Aankh Se Aanso Kissi Sorat.
Osse Be Sheer Ka Jab Muskurana Yaad Atta Hai.
Pidar Ki Gaud Ki Khatir Sakina Jab Bhi Roti Hai.
Woh Lori Baap Ke Lehje Mein Beti Ko Sunata Hai.

4
Khiyal Atta Hai Jab Bhi Parda-e-Zainab Ka Mehdi Ko.
Kabhi Talwar Par Wo Hath Rakhta Hai Hatata Hai.
Wo Hujat Hai Ossi Ka Kaam Hao Iss Dard Ko Sehna.
Kissi Khaki Ke Dil Mein John Ye Gham Kab Samata Hai.
