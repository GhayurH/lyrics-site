Karde Dil Ki Har Ik Umeed Shaheed Write Up
Vote Ke Naam Par Khareed Shaheed.

1
Khandani Siyasaton Ko Yahan.
Dosto Chahiyein Mazeed Shaheed.
Do Grohon Ki Jang Ka Matlab.
Wahan Hamid Yahan Hameed Shaheed.

2
Maein Roti Rahein Magar Rehbar.
Khailtey Reh Gaye Shaheed Shaheed.
Karzar-e-Wila Se Khud Kush Tak.
Ik Qadeem Our Ik Jadeed Shaheed.

3
Murshido Murshadi Salamat Baad.
Lakh Hotey Rahein Mureed Shaheed.
Hoor-o-Gilma Ke Khuwab Waiz Ke.
Wadah Janat Ka Our Waeid Shaheed.

4
Apne Barood-e-Kimiya Se Roz.
Chand Zindoon Se Kar Kasheed Shaheed.
Phool Mala Charhaye Jaein Gay.
Khush Bohat Hoon Gay Rooz-e-Eid Shaheed.

5
Pori Shiddat Ke Sath Nafrat Ki.
Istelahan Howa Shadeed Shaheed.
Baat Jab Bhi Chali Hakomat Ki.
Sab Pe Sabit Hoye Mufeed Shaheed.

6
Hum Likhein Gay Sada Hussain-e-Shaheed.
Shoq Se Sheikh Likh Yazid Shaheed.
Ho Jo Akbar Abu Turab Se Ishq.
Khak Se Hoon Gay Mustafeed Shaheed.
