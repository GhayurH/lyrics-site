1
Ya Imam e Raza Ilteja Hai Muhammad Raza Mungla.
Ya Imam-e-Raza Iltija Hai, Ya Imam-e-Raza Iltija Hai.
Taal Do Har Bala, Yeh Dua Hai Ya Imam-e-Raza Iltija Hai.
Hain Giraftar Hum Mushkilon Mein.
Dekh Lo Hum Ko Hain Uljhano Mein.
Bas Tumhi Se Humein Aasra Hai.

2
Hai Jo Bemari Dunya Mein Aayi.
Iss Ki Khatir Hai Tum Se Duhai.
Aap Ke Pas Maula Shifa Hai.

3
Be Kasoon Ki Kuch Imdad Kar Do.
Mushkilon Se Ab Aazad Kar Do.
Hath Mein Jab Riza-e-Khuda Hai.

4
Hum Hain Maula Tumhare Mawali.
Ban Ke Aaye Hain Dar Par Sawali.
Khali Daman Yeh Phaila Howa Hai.

5
Rozi Roti Se Jo Hain Pareshaan.
Mushkilein Kar Do Ab Unn Ki Aasaan.
Tum Se Umeed Sab Se Siwa Hai.

6
Kaun Hoga Phir Oss Ke Barabar.
Oss Ke Jaisa Hai Kis Ka Muqadar.
Jo Bhi Dar Ka Tumharey Gada Hai.

7
Aap Ke Dar Pe Aaya Hai Johar.
Our Raza Yeh Hi Kehta Hai Ro Kar.
Taal Do Sab Yeh Jitni Bala Hai.
