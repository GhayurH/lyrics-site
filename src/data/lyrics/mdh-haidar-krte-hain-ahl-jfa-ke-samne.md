Madh e Haider kartay hain ahle e jafa ke samnay
Ham charagh apnay jalatay hain hawa ke samnay

1
Ya Ali ham ne kaha aur mushkilain sab tal gayeen
Mushkilain thairain gi kiay mushkil kusha ke samnay

2
Koi dunya may nahi hay misl e Shah e La fata
Aazmana hay to dekhay koi aa ke samnay

3
Chorr kar nafs e Khuda ko hain talabgar e riza
Janay ke kis munh se aatay hain Khuda ke samnay

4
Sab ilaj de jism hay aur ye dawa e rooh hay
Sab dawain gard hain khak e shifa ke samnay

5
Wo hay sajdon ki jaghay aur ye hay sajdon ki ameen
Kaabay ko ham kaise layain Karbala ke samnay

6
Khoon e Asghar mal ke chahray par huay jaise Hussain
Surkharoo kia ho ga koyi yun Khuda ke samnay
