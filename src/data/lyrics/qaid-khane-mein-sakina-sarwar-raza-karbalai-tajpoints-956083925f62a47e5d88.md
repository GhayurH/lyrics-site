1
Qaid Khane Mein Sakina Sarwar Raza Karbalai.
Qaid Khane Mein Sakina Yeh Kehti Hai Baba Aao.
Baba Jaan Zindaan Ki Aziyat Se Ab Jaan Meri Churao.
Woh Andhera Hai Keh Dewar Se Takrati Hoon.
Girti Hoon Roti Hoon Baba Haye Chilati Hoon.
Wehshatein Deti Hain Pehra Aa Ke Dil Behlao.
Kya Meri Yaad Tumhein Aati Nahi Hai Baba.
Bhool Jata Hai Koi Beti Ko Iss Tarha Kya.
Kis Tarha Koi Door Rahe Baba Beti Ko Samjhao.
Bistar-e-Khuwab Hai Tanhai Hai Be Khuwabi Hai.
Meri Taqdeer Mein Likhi Hoi Betabi Hai.
Khak Par Kis Tarha Sotay Hain Aa Ke Yeh Batlao.
Ghut Ke Mar Jaon Gi Zindaan Mein Kissi Din Baba.
Jis Tarha Qaid Mein Rakha Gaya Mujh Ko Tanha.
Kya Bhala La Waris Hoon Baba Mujh Ko Yeh Batlao.
Ab Tu Zalim Mujhe Roney Bhi Nahi Deta Hai.
Bhai Sajjad Se Milney Bhi Nahi Deta Hai.
Guftgu Tum Khud Hi Karo Aa Kar Mera Dard Ghatao.
Yaad Aata Hai Mujhe Aap Ka Seena Baba.
Main Yehi Karti Hoon Zindaan Mein Noha Baba.
Seene Pe Aa Jaye Gi Dukhiya Banhein Tu Phelao.
Jab Sakina Ko Mila Zindaan Mein Sar Baba Ka.
Bolay Shabeer Tabhi Dekh Ke Oss Ka Chehra.
Aye Meri Pyari Dukhiya Beti Na Ro Ke Dard Barhao.
Haye Asif Se Yeh Sarwar Ne Kaha Woh Manzar.
Jab Mere Shah Ki Beti Woh Sakina Ghut Kar.
Mar Gayi Bolay Gi Na Dukhiya Zalim Se Churwao.
