Aa Gaye Asghar Bhi Run Mein Deen Bachanay Kay Liye

1
Ambia Ka Main Hu Nasir
Yeh Batanay Kay Liye

2
Hurmula Teri Kama Ko Taud Kay Rakh Dounga Main
Aaya Huun Main Naam Tera
Hi Mitanay Kay Liye

3
Jab Kama Hurmal Ki Dekhi Boli Khaimon Say Rubab
Ab Raho Taiyar Asghar
Teer Khanay Kay Liye

4
Hashr Tak Karti Rahay Gi Intezar Umme Rubab
Gaud Main Jhuulay Kay Aadhi
Ko Jhoolanay Kay Liye

5
Tha Kayamat Ka Sama Muntaz Pyason Ki Vo Shaam
Aagaye Zahra Kay Ghar Ko
Phir Jaalanay Kay Liye
