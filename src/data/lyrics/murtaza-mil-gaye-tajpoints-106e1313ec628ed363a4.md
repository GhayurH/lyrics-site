1
Murtaza Mil Gaye Our Kya Chahiye.
Kufar Hai Gar Kahon Dosra Chahiye.
Murtaza Ke Liye Fatima Chahiye.
Yani Al-Hamd Ko Hal Atta Chahiye.

2
Bolein Haider Ke Lehjay Mein Bint-e-Asad.
Hat Jidar-e-Haram Rasta Chahiye.
Murtaza Aasmano Pe Masroof Hain.
Bolay Salman Aa Tujh Ko Kya Chahiye.

3
Bolay Aadam Bana Loon Main Aadam Abhi.
Ya Ali Aap Ki Khak-e-Pa Chahiye.
Dekho Pardah Uthaya Tu Jalna Nahi.
Zikar-e-Mairaj Hai Hosla Chahiye.

4
Zoja-e-Murtaza Se Tawasal Karein.
Jis Ko ALLAH Se Mashwara Chahiye.
Iqtedar-e-Ali Ikhteyar-e-Ali.
Sochna Choriye Manna Chahiye.

5
Aik Hujray Mein Jis Ki Kati Zindagi.
Oss Se Kaisay Kahon Ghar Bara Chahiye.
Ajar Kya Doon Takallum Jo Bolay Ali.
Main Ne Jhuk Kar Kaha Bas Dua Chahiye.
