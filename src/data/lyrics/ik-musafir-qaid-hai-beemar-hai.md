Ik musafir qaid hay beemar hay
Karwan-e-sabr ka salar hay

1
1) Taigh Syed ke galay par chal gaye
Chadar e bint e payamber jal gaye
Aur phir sham e gharibaan dhal gaye
Bekasi hay Shaam ka bazaar hay

2
2) Dekh le dunay Yazidon ka nizam
Martay hain us ke durray ahl e Shaam
Jo mareez e be nawa e tashna kaam
Janashin e Syed e Abrar hay
3) Khoon rag e insaniyat may mar gaya
Raah e haq may tashnagi ka sar gaya
Jab payambar ka nawasa mar gaya
Chashm e haadi kis liye khoonkhar hay
