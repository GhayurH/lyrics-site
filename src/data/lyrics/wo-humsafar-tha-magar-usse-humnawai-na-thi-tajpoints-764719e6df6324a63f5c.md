Wo Humsafar Tha Magar Usse Humnawai Na Thi.
Keh Dhoop Chaon Ka Alam Raha Judai Na Thi.

1
Na Apna Ranj Na Apna Dukh Na Ouroon Ka Malal.
Shab-e-Firaq Kabhi Hum Ne Youn Ganwai Na Thi.
Muhabaton Ka Safar Kuch Iss Tarha Bhi Guzra Tha.
Shikasta Dil The Musafir Shikasta Payi Na Thi.

2
Adawatein Thin Tagaful Tha Ranjishein Thin Bohat.
Bicharne Wale Mein Sab Kuch Tha Be Wafai Na Thi.
Bicharte Waqt Unn Aankhon Mein Thi Hamari Ghazal.
Ghazal Bhi Woh Jo Kissi Ko Abhi Sunai Na Thi.

3
Kabhi Yeh Haal Keh Dono Mein Yak Dili Thi Bohat.
Kabhi Yeh Marhala Jaise Keh Aashnai Na Thi.
AJeeb Hoti Hai Rah-e-Sukhan Bhi Dekh Naseer.
Wahan Bhi Aa Gaye Aakhir Jahan Rasai Na Thi.
