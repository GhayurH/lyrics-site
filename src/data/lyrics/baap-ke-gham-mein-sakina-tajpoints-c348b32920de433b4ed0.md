1
Baap Ke Gham Mein Sakina Youn Dilase Paye Gi Soz Sheraz Khan.
Jab Bhi Baba Ko Sada De Gi Tamanche Khaye Gi.
Neli Par Jaein Na Kirne Neela Chehra Dekh Kar.
Roshni Zindan Mein Jate Howay Ghabraye Gi.

2
Apne Baloon Mein Basa Kar Apni Turbat Ke Liye.
Baap Ke Maqtal Ki Mati Qaid Mein Le Aye Gi.
Jhuriyan Chehre Pe Hoo Gi Baal Sab Hoo Gaye Safaid.
Sham Tak Bali Sakina Fatima Ban Jaye Gi.

3
Kaisi Ghurbat Hai Keh Kurta Laye Gi Be Sheer Ka.
Our Sakina Ki Jagah Maa Goshware Laye Gi.
Maut Hi Akbar Sula Paye Gi Oss Masoom Ko.
Baap Ki Maiyat Jo Apni Aankhon Mein Dafnaye Gi.
