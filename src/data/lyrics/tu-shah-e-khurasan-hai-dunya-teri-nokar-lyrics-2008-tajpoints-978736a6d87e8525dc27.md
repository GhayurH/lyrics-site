1
Tu Shah e Khurasan Hai Dunya Teri Nokar Aye Aathwein Haider
Sajda Teri Dehleez Pe Karta Hai Qalandar Aye Aathwein Haider.
Aankhoon Mein Basa Loon Tere Roze Ke Nazare.
Dewana Raza Ka Mujhe Har Dana Pukare.

2
Arman Hai Maut Aye Tu Mula Tere Dar Par.
Bigari Hoi Har Baat Faqeeron Ki Banai.
Hoti Hai Khurasan Se Khairat-e-Khudai.
Tu Faiz Ka Darya Hai Karam Ka Hai Samandar.

3
Iss Banda-e-Na Cheez Ko Anmool Bana De.
Mula Mujhe Khak-e-Dar-e-Behlool Bana De.
Qismat Pe Meri Naaz Kare Hur Ka Muqadar.
Hota Hai Bari Dhoom Se Shabir Ka Matam.

4
Roze Pe Tere Lagta Hai Har Waqt Muharam.
Aati Hai Sada Haye Hussaina Ki Barabar.
Mula Mujhe Iss Door-e-Munafiq Se Bacha Le.
Tujh Se Hoon Bohat Door Mujhe Paas Bula Le.

5
Chalta Hai Tere Hijar Ka Dill Par Mere Khanjar.
Ja Dekh Le Dunya Yeh Takallum Ka Hai Dawa.
Mashad Mein Koi Shaks Bhi Bhoka Nahi Sota.
Batta Hai Subha-o-Sham Tere Naam Ka Langar.
