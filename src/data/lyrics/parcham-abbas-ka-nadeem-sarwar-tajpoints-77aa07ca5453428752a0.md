Parcham Abbas Ka Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics

1
Haan Yehi Parcham Hussaini Foaj Ka Ban Kar Nishaan.
Jis Ghari Pohancha Tha Foaj-e-Ashqiya Ke Darmiyaan.
Har Taraf Se Aa Rahi Thi Bas Sada-e-Al-Amaan.
Narah-e-Abbas Tha Our Thi Farishton Ki Zabaan.
Iss Alam Ke Sath Hai Chota Sa Mashkeza Bandha.
Bazu-e-Abbas Par Jaisay Ho Taveez-e-Wafa.

2
Na Alam Iss Se Juda Hai Na Alam Se Yeh Juda.
Iss Alam Pe Saya Afgan Hai Dua-e-Syeda.
Tha Yehi Parcham Ali Ke Hath Mein Har Jang Mein.
Karbala Pohancha Dua-e-Syeda Ke Rang Mein.
Haan Issi Parcham Ko Gara Tha Ali Ne Sang Mein.
Dorta Hai Khon Iss Ko Dekh Kar Ang Ang Mein.

3
Haan Issi Parcham Se Aaya Karbala Mein Inqalab.
Le Ke Iss Ko Jab Gaya Darya Pe Ibn-e-Bu Turab.
Chom Li Mojon Ne Barh Kar Asp Ghazi Ki Rakab.
Iss Ke Panjay Ki Chamak Se Roshni Le Aaftab.
Jab Talak Oncha Raha Karbal Mein Ghazi Ka Alam.
Aa Sakay Na Khaima-e-Sarwar Mein Aada Ke Qadam.

4
Yeh Alam Thanda Howa Roney Lagay Shah-e-Ummam.
Ro Ke Kehte They Yehi Tha Mere Lashkar Ka Bharam.
Yeh Alam Dharas Tha Zainab Our Sakina Ke Liye.
Dhop Mein Ik Chaon Tha Yeh Shah-e-Wala Ke Liye.
Iss Ka Girna Maut Se Barh Kar Tha Aaqa Ke Liye.
Maut Ka Paigham Yeh Parcham Tha Aada Ke Liye.

5
Aaj Har Ghar Par Alam Hai Mere Ghazi Ka Saja.
Hazrat-e-Zainab Ki Pori Ho Gayi Aakhir Dua.
Marhaba Abbas-e-Ghazi Marhaba Sad Marhaba.
Aasmaan Tu Jhuk Gaya Tera Alam Oncha Raha.
Iss Alam Ki Shan Tu Oss Roz Dekhey Ga Jahan.
Aaye Ga Jis Roz Iss Parcham Ka Waris Barhwan.

6
Kufar Ki Basti Pe Totein Gi Hazaron Bijliyaan.
Momino Ke Sar Pe Ho Ga Iss Alam Ka Saibaan.
Yeh Alam Ab Tak Hussaini Foaj Ki Hai Yadgar.
Hashar Tak Qayam Rahe Ga Aytbar-o-Ikhteyar.
Dekhein Gay Rehan Iss Parcham Ki Sab Oss Din Bahar.
Iss Ko Le Kar Aaein Gay Jab Mehdi-e-Aali Waqar.
