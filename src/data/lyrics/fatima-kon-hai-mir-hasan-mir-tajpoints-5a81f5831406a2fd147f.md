Fatima Kon Hai Mir Hasan Mir Lyrics

1
Kaun Samjhay Bhala Fatima Kaun Hai.
Yaan Nabi-o-Ali-o-Khuda Kaun Hai.
Jo Na Sail Se Pochay Bata Kaun Hai.
Aisa Dunya Mein Hajat Rawa Kaun Hai.
Kaprey Rizwan Laya Hai Maloom Tha.
Dar Pe Fizza Ne Yeh Kab Kaha Kaun Hai.

2
De Na Paye Mukamal Jawab Anbiya.
Jab Yeh Pocha Gaya Syeda Kaun Hai.
Rab-e-Hasnain Aye Syeda Marziya.
Mera Arbab Tere Siwa Kaun Hai.
Uz Azal Ta Abad Itnay Kam Waqt Mein.
Kya Bataein Ali, Fatima Kaun Hai.

3
Hami-e-Qatilan-e-Janab-e-Batool.
Main Tu Insan Hoon Tu Bata Kaun Hai.
Baab-e-Zahra Pe Shab Mein Yeh Jaroob Kash.
Aye Abuzar Zara Dekhna Kaun Hai.
Roz Phirti Hai Bag-e-Fidak Ke Qareeb.
Yeh Bhatakti Hoi Aatma Kaun Hai.

4
Shab Mein Mairaj Ki Rab Ne Mehfil Rakhi.
Jis Ka Unwan Tha Syeda Kaun Hai.
Kar Ke Midhat Nabi-o-Ali Ne Kaha.
Ab Khuda Tu Bata Fatima Kaun Hai.
Kar Tabarah Takallum Tu Khul Jaye Ga.
Dushman-e-Syeda Kaun Tha Kaun Hai.
