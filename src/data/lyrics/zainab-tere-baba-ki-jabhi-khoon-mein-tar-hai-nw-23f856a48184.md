Zainab Tere Baba Ki Jabhi Khoon Mein Tar Hai

Haider Ke Alamdare  Husaini Pe Nazar Hai

Zainab Ke Khule Sar Pe Hai Haider Ki Nigahen

Yaad Aane Lagi Shaam Ke Bazaar Ki Raahen

Ekis Mahe Ramazan Ki  Hone Ko Saher Hai

Shabbir Ki Dharus Hai Yeh Zainab Ka Bharam Hai

Rutba Hai Yeh Ghazi Ka Yeh Saqaaey Harum Hai

Dil Band Hai Yeh Sarwar Ka  Haider Ka Jigger Hai

Socho Toh Musalmanon Ke Yeh Baat Barhi Hai

Haq Maangene Zehra Sar-e-Darbaar Kharhi Hai

Aye Ghairat-e-Islam Bata  Aaj Kidhar Hai

Woh Waqt Bhi Hoga Ke Koi Paas Na Hoga

Ghar Baar Lootey Ga Magar Abbas Na Hoga

Woh Jaaenge Bazaar  Ussey Yeh Bhi Khabar Hai

Iss Gham Ki Muhaafiz Hai Mohib Zainab-e-Dilgeer

Duniya Mein Joh Baaqi Hai Azadaariye Shabbir

Yeh Fatima Zehra Ki  Duaon Ka Asar Hai
