Tere Putar Ameer Da Lasha Mazloom Hussain Ne Chaya.
Tere Babay De Rozay Te Ummat Ne Pehra Laya.

1
Na Maa Na Baba Dunya Te Kinu Apna Dard Sunawaan.
Kulsoom Te Zainab Nu Nana Kiwein Veer Da Kafan Wakhawaan.
Main Tur Chaliya Tere Rozay Taun Nayi Veer Nu Main Dafnaya.
Meri Maa De Qatil Zalimaan Ne Tere Hujray Wich Dafnaye.

2
Koi Farq Ashab Te Putraan Da Ais Ummat Nu Samjhaye.
Aina Barish Kar Ke Teraan Di Tere Rozay Taun Partaya.
Muhtaj Nayi Assi Shahi De Nayi Ummat Wair Mokanday.
Dushman Di Lash Te V Nana Nayi Loki Teer Chalanday.

3
Nayi Zahra De Aj Putraan Te Haye Taras Kisay Nu Aaya.
Howay Nisbat Tere Naal Odi Nayi Wair Batool Da Mukna.
Pawein Chup Ke Teer Chalaway Oh Tareekh De Wich Nayi Chupna.
Kiwein Ho Sakdi Aye Maa Najfi Jinnay Hasan Te Teer Chalaya.
