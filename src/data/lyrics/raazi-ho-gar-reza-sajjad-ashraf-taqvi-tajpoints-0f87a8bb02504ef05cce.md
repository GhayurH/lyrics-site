Raazi Ho Gar Reza Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.

1
Razi Ho Gar Raza Tu Tera Bera Paar Hai.
Unn Ki Riza Mein Marzi-e-Parwardigar Hai.
Janat Nayi Nayi Si Lagay Gi Unhein Kahan.
Mashhad Ki Jin Ko Aab-o-Howa Sazgar Hai.
Hai Band Bazu Band Mein Apni Baqa Ka Raaz.
Zamin Raza Hain Our Humein Aytbar Hai.

2
Sehan-e-Shifa Ke Hotey Hoye Kya Waba Ka Khof.
Beemar Tera Tere Liye Be Qarrar Hai.
Sajdah Naseeb Ho Humein Baab-e-Murad Par.
Darkar Bas Yeh Izz-o-Sharf Bar Bar Hai.
Dekha Jo Kehkashan Ko Tu Dil Ne Kaha Keh Yeh.
 Sajid Raza Ke Rozay Ka Gird-o-Ghubar Hai.
