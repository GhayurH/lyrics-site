Zindaan Hai Lehad Meri Kurta Hai Kafan Mera Hasan Sadiq.
Karna Yahin Janaza Sajjad Dafan Mera.

1
Main Baap Ke Seene Pe Barey Chain Se Soti Thi.
Ab Khak Ke Bistar Pe Dukhta Hai Badan Mera.
Sajjad Se Kehti Thi Zindaan Se Raha Ho Kar.
Tum Jana Madine Ko Hai Sham Watan Mera.

2
Kehna Ke Chiney Gohar Khaye Hain Tamachay Bhi.
Aye Bhai Agar Pochay Akbar Ki Behan Mera.
Kokh Ujari Hai Madar Ki Nam Aankh Hai Khwahar Ki.
Asghar Ke Bina Bhaiyya Sona Hai Sehan Mera.

3
Baba Na Chacha Akbar Qasim Na Ali Asghar.
Ik Din Mein Ujara Hai Ummat Ne Chaman Mera.
 Jawad Yeh Kehta Hai Sadqay Mein Sakina Ke.
Sadaat Ke Gham Se Hai Wabasta Sukhan Mera.
