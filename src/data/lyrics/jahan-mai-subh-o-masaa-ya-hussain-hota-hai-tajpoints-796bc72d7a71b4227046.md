Jahan Mai Subh-o-Masaa Ya Hussain Hota Hai.
Khuda Ki Tarha Har Ik Ja Hussain Hota Hai.

1
Main Zuljinah Ka Leta Hoon Jis Ghari Bosa.
Meri Nigah Mein Mera Hussain Hota Hai.
Jabeen-e-Khuld-e-Barin Par Likha Hai Yeh Misra.
Ali Ho Baap Tu Beta Hussain Hota Hai.

2
Hazar Sajday Karey Koi Apni Koshish Se.
Hussain Bach Nahi Sakta Hussain Hota Hai.
Woh Jis Ki La Se Bachay LaIlaha Ilaha ALLAH.
Mere Aqeeday Mein Aisa Hussain Hota Hai.

3
Naha Ke Apne Paseeney Mein Matami Bolay.
Saron Pe Dhop Mein Saya Hussain Hota Hai.
Behan Ko Shukriya Kehne Waseem Sham Ki Simt.
Har Aik Sham Rawana Hussain Hota Hai.
