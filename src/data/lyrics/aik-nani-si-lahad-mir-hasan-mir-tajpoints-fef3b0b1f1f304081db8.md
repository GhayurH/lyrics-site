1
Aik Nani Si Lahad Mir Hasan Mir.
Aik Nani Si Lahad Ran Mein Banate Hain Hussain.
Khak Mein Dosre Mohsin Ko Chopate Hain Husain.
Dalte Jate Hain Asghar Ke Badan Par Mati.
Our Khaimo Ki Taraf Dekhte Jate Hain Husain.
Marte Dam Nane Se Hathon Ne Jise Thama Tha.
Ab Wo Daman Ali Asghar Se Chorate Hain Husain.
Lash-e-Hur Ne Kaha Romal Ye De Do Ja kar.
Zakhm Be Sheer Ka Hathon Se Chopate Hain Husain.
Dhondne Aaein Gaye Iss Lash Ko Naiza Bardar.
Chup Ke Bano Se Ye Zainab Ko Batate Hain Husain.
Kaha Sughra Ne Keh Mar Kar Tu Milon Gi Sheh Se.
Mujh Ko Maloom Hai Har Qabar Mein Aate Hain Husain.
Lash-e-Abbas Pa Kehti Thin Ye Zahra Ro Ke.
Utho Abbas Tumhein Kab Se Bolate Hain Husain.
Koi Zainab Ko Bola Laye Takallum Ja Kar.
Barchi Akbar Ke Kalejay Se Chorate Hain Husain.
