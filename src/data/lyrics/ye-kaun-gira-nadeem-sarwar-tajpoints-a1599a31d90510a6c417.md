Ye Kaun Gira Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics
Kaba Hai Siyah Posh Falak Mehway Boka Hai.

1
Yeh Gunbad-e-Aflak Se Kyun Shoor Utha Hai.
Kuhram Baqaiya Ki Fizaon Mein Bapa Hai.
Yeh Kaun Gira Haye Hussaina Reti Par.
Tha Waqt-e-Asar Arsh-e-Mualla Pe Shoor Tha.

2
Wah Hasrata Keh Zeen Se Zameen Par Koi Gira.
Kanpi Zameen Chashm-e-Malaik Ko Youn Laga.
Quran Rehal-e-Zeen Se Sar-e-Farsh Gir Para.
Dewar-e-Kaba Baith Gayi Arsh Gir Para.

3
Girtay Hi Iss Ke Kabay Ki Dewar Gir Gayi.
Sar Aasmaan Ka Jhuk Gaya Dastar Gir Gayi.
Insaniyat Ki Dehar Mein Sarkar Gir Gayi.
Zakhmo Se Noor Noor Hai Jhuk Kar Kaman Hai.

4
Hairaan Hai Maut Khof Nahi Itmenan Hai.
Yeh Ho Gaya Maloom Yeh Khasan-e-Khuda Hai.
Yeh Kaisa Bashar Hai Jo Zamane Se Juda Hai.
Konain Mein Kyun Iss Ke Liye Hashar Bapa Hai.

5
Kyun Iss Ke Liye Arsh-e-Mualla Pe Hai Matam.
Rotay Hain Nabi Sare Siyah Posh Hain Aadam.
Yeh Mard-e-Khuda Shah-e-Arab Aala Nasab Hai.
Al-Hamd Se Wal Naas Talak Iss Ka Hi Sab Hai.

6
Baba Ka Nasab Maa Ka Hasab Iss Ka Hasab Hai.
Teeron Ke Musallay Pe Jo Sajday Mein Gira Hai.
Islam Issi Sar Se Madad Mang Raha Hai.
Aye Shah-e-Mashreqain Hamara Salam Le.

7
Haider Ke Zaib-o-Zeen Hamara Salam Le.
Zahra Ke Dil Ka Chain Hamara Salam Hai.
Insaniyat Ke Deen Ka Eman Ka Salam.
Kar Lein Qabool:SARWAR-o-REHAN: Ka Salam.
