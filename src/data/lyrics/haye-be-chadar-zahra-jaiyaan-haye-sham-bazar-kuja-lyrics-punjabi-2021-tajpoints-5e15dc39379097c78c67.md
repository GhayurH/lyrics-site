Haye Be Chadar Zahra Jaiyaan Haye Sham Bazar Kuja.
Kar Hosh Beemar Putar Mera Darbar Aa Gaya.

1
Rat Akhiyaan Di Wadh Di Jaway Sajjad Aye Ro Ke Farmaway.
Kul Hashamiyaan Di Ghairat Aye Naa Le Ke Na Bola.
Ki Bhain Teri Da Jeena Aye Mainu Marda Shimar Kamina Aye.
Kali Zindan Che Mar Jaan Gi Mainu Veer Aa Bacha.

2
Bazar Sajay Jawan Gay Pathar V Wasaye Jawan Gay.
Kalma Gowaan Qaidi Keeti Aoulad-e-Mustafa.
Naizay Te Chota Sar Tak Ke Ik Dojay Nu Shami Puchday.
Maa Jeendi Aye Ya Mar Gayi Aye Ayeh Lal Way Jidda.

3
Jehra Dar Shabeer Te Aaway Ga Jehra Sada Soag Manaway Ga.
Aakhay Bi Bibi Bakhshiya Jaway Ga Jiwein Si Hur Bhira.
Nahin Likh Sakda Koi Dukh Tere Tusaan Wekhay Ne Sadmay Jehray.
 Sajjad Musayab Jo Likhda Teri Bibi Hai Atta.
