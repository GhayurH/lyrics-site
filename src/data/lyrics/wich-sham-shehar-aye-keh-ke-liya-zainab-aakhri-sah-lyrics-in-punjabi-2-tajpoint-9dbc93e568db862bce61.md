Wich Sham Shehar Aye Keh Ke Liya Zainab Aakhri Sah.
Kul Hashamiyaan Di Ghairat Main Te Baba Sham Kuja.

1
1- Kayi Sadmay Wekhay Zainab Ne Wich Sham Diyaan Bazaraan De.
Veraan De Sir Si Neziyaan Te Wekhi Nezay Te Rida.

2
2- Sab Janday Ne Pehchanday Nahin Kalma Go Pak Muhammad De.
Assi Haan Aoulad Muhammad Di Dittay Fair V Pathar Wasa.

3
3- Main Dhi Aan Hijab-e-Kibriya Di Na Rolo Wich Bazaraan De.
Nahin Zalimo Mangdi Rehaiyaan Main Dewo Chadar Nam-e-Khuda.

4
4- Mainu Mar Gaya Dukh Akbar Da Veran Tere Ali Asghar Da.
Main Aoun-o-Muhammad Waar Dittay Tere Akbar Da Sadqa.

5
5- Beshak Karbal Taun Sham Taein Dukh Wekhay Zainab Ne Veran.
Darbar Che Jana Par Veran Mainu Jendiyaan Mar Gaya.

6
6- Jiday Parday Da Naa Ghazi Si Sajjad Oh Rul Gayi Be Chadar.
Jehri Ghar Choon Bahir Na Aayi Kiwein Wekhay Sham De Rah.
