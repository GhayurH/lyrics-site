Haaye Sajjad haaye bimar
Sajjad lai ae mushkil ae Shaam da safar
Akbar bhira da qaatil Abid da humsafar

1
Hoya riha jadoṅ da chup chup Sajjad rehnda
Puchhe haal koi ro ke na Shaam da ae lainda
Jehre zulm vekhe Abid dunya nu ki khabar

2
Asghar da kurta veeraṅ meri lahad wich rakha de
Ehsaan kar qabr da Abid nishaan mita de
Tere baad aa na jaave meri qabr te Shimr

3
Tatheer di je waaris aj hoi berida ae
Is waaste maiṅ maangda haaṅ maran di dua ae
Sir uryaṅ vekh tera mera phat gaya jigar

4
Aakhe Rubab Abid mushkil hai mera jina
Asghar hai Karbala wich reh Shaam gayi Sakina
Hoi god meri khaali kis di lagi nazar

5
Ais Shaam de shahr wich Shabbir rul gayiyaṅ
Darbaar vekh veeraṅ teri maut bhul gayiyaṅ
Bimar mar na jaave Zainab nu ae fikar

6
Ronda ae khuṅ qaidi nahiṅ marz koi puraani
Ae Shaam kha gayi ae Sajjad di jawaani
Hoya zaif yakdam bazaaraṅ da asar

7
Jinn o bashar malaaik karde bari ibaadat
Lekin hai la-ilah di bunyaad ae shahaadat
Sajda Husain tera beshak azimtar

8
Tanveer aave Mehdi karo mil ke sab dua ae
Muk jaave raat rutaṅ di Abid nu chain aave
Aabaad phir hove ae Fatima da ghar
