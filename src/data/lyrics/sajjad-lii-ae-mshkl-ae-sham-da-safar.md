Haey Sajjad, haey beemar

1
Sajjad laey ay mushkil, aey Shaam da safar
Sajaad laey ay mushkil

2
Akbar bhira da qaatil,  Abid da humsafar
Sajaad laey ay mushkil, aey Shaam da safar
Sajaad laey ay mushkil

3
1) Hoya riha jadown da, chup chup Sajjad rehnda
Puche haal koyi ro kay, naa Shaam da ay laynda
Jeray zulm vekhay Abid,  duniya nu ki khabar
Sajaad laey aey mushkil, aey Shaam da safar
Sajaad laey aey mushkil

4
2) Asghar da kurta veeran, meri laid vich rakhade
Ehsan kaar qabaar day, Abid nishan mita day
Tere baad aa na javay,  meri qabar tay Shimar
Sajaad laey ay mushkil, aey Shaam da safar
Sajaad laey ay mushkil

5
3) Tatheer di jay waris, aj hooyi bay rida ay
Es wastay may mungda haan maran di dua ay
Sir uryan vekh tera,  mera phat gaya jigar
Sajaad laey ay mushkil, aey Shaam da safar
Sajaad laey ay mushkil

6
4) Aakhay Rubab Abid, mushkil hay mera jeena
Asghar haiyKarbala vich, reh Shaam gaye Sakina
Hoyee goad meri khali,  kis di lagee nazar
Sajaad laey ay mushkil, aey Shaam da safar
Sajaad laey ay mushkil

7
5) Ais Shaam day sheher vich, Shabbir rul gayee haan
Darbar vekh veeran, teri maut bhul gayee yaan
Beemar mar na javay,  Zainab nu aey fikar
Sajaad laey ay mushkil, aey Shaam da safar
Sajaad laey ay mushkil

8
6) Ronda aey khoon qaidi, nai marz koyi puraanee
Aey Shaam kha gayi aey, Sajjad di jawani
Hoya zaeef yakdam,  bazaaran da asar
Sajaad laey ay mushkil, aey Shaam da safar
Sajaad laey ay mushkil

9
7) Jinno bashar malayak, karday baree ibadat
Lekin ay la-ilah di, buniyaad di shahaadat
Sajda Hussain tera,  beyshaak azeem tar
Sajaad laey ay mushkil, aey Shaam da safar
Sajaad laey ay mushkil

10
8) Tanveer aaway Mehdi, karo mil ke sab dua ay
Muk jaavay rat rutaan di, Abid nu chain avay
Aabad faer howay,  Aery Fatima da ghar
Sajaad laey ay mushkil, aey Shaam da safar
Sajaad laey ay mushkil
