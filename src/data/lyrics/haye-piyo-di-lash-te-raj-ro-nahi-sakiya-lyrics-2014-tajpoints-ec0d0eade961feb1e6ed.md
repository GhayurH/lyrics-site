Haye Piyo Di Lash Te Raj Ro Nahi Sakiya Soz Sheraz Khan.

1
Haye Piyo Di Lash Te Raj Ro Nahi Sakiya Main Abid Sochda Rehna.
Mera Aye Dukh Wandawan Layi Koi Purse Layi Away Ga Main Rahwaan Wekhda Rehna.
Menu Phuphiyaan Di Banwaan Te Hajay Wi Neel Disday Ne.
Umat Rasiyaan Jo Baniyaan San Khaiyalaan Wich Phophiyaan Di Oh Rasiyaan kholda Rehna.
Bas ayni Piyas si Asghar Di Ik Chulu Che Bhuj Jandi.
Utha Ke Apne Hathaan Wich Main Pani Wekhda Rehna Te Atharo Dolda Rehna.

2
Zamane Nu Khabar Ki Aye Mera Nuqsan Ki Hoya.
Main Kyun Rataan Nu Uth Uth Ke Ali Akbar Ali Akbar Awazaan Marda Rehna.
Kise Wi Sheher Wich Ruk Ke Jaan Mera Qafila Turday.
Mere Maqtool Veeraan Cho Kise Da Sir Na Reh Jaway Main Naizay Wekhda Rehna.
Phuphi Zainab Di Chadar Aye, Aye Masooma Sakina Di.
Ridawaan Tak Sangaan Te Siraan De Naal Chadraan De Main Natay Jorda Rehna.
