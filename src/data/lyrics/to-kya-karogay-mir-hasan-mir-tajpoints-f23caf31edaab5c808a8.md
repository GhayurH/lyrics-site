1
To Kya Karogay Mir Hasan Mir Lyrics
Ali Ka Beta Hai Aane Wala.
Jab Aaye Ga Waris-e-Haqeeqi Koi Fasana Nahi Chaley Ga.
Ali Wali Ke Mukhalifon Phir Koi Bahana Nahi Chaley Ga.

2
Ali Ke Dushman, Ali Ka Beta Jo Laut Aaya Tu Kya Karo Gay.
Our Aa Ke Oss Ne Fidak Ka Tum Se Hisab Manga Tu Kya Karo Gay.
Ali Ki Aamad Ka Jo Nishaan Hai Abhi Bhi Tum Se Mita Nahi Hai.
Zahoor-e-Qayam Pe Phir Dobarah Hansa Jo Kaba Tu Kya Karo Gay.

3
Batao Fatwa Farosh Mufti Azza Ke Waris Ke Hukam Par Jab.
Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Ka Matam Haram Mein Ho Ga Tu Kya Karo Gay.
Tamam Nabiyon Ko Tum Jo Sahib Ali Se Afzal Bata Rahe Ho.
Namaz Ibn-e-Ali Ke Pichey Parhein Gay Esa Tu Kya Karo Gay.

4
Humein Jo Kehte Ho Shia Kafir Zamane Walo Yeh Soch Lena.
Baroz-e-Mehshar Khuda Ne Jannat Mein Hum Ko Bhaija Tu Kya Karo Gay.
Abhi Tu Kamtar Samajh Rahe Ho Magar Imam-e-Zamaan Ne Aa Kar.
Khud Apne Nazdeek Matami Ko Agar Bithaya Tu Kya Karo Gay.

5
Aray Tum Apni Kamzoor Mantiqon Se Yazid Ko Jo Bana Rahe Ho.
Bana Ke Qaidi Ghali Ghali Mein Ossay Phiraya Tu Kya Karo Gay.
Aray Jahan Chupo Gay Aye Munkiro Tum Makaan Kahey Ga Yahan Chupay Hain.
Batao Bad-e-Zahoor Tum Par Yeh Waqt Aaya Tu Kya Karo Gay.

6
Khuda Ali Ko Tu Keh Rahe Ho Magar Zara Dair Ko Tu Socho.
Kya Ali Ke Khuda Ko Aate Hi Oss Ne Sajdah Tu Kya Karo Gay.
Aray Takallum Se Jalnay Walo Zabaan Se Sholay Ugalney Walo.
Kahin Gay Maula Jab Oss Se Aa Kar Parho Qaseeda Tu Kya Karo Gay.
