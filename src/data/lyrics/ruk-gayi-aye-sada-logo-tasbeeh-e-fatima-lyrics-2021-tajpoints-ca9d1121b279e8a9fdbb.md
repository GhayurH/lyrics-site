Ruk Gayi Aye Sada Logo Tasbeeh-e-Fatima Di Write Up
Ainj Lagda Tur Gayi Aye Dunya Taun Haye Dhi Mustafa Di.

1
Kehnda Aye Putar Hussain Aidha Kafan Choon Banwaan Chah.
Bazu Te Tut Gaya Si Koi Kar De Imdad Syeda Di.
Aa Jandi Hukam Te Mariyam De Dar Choon Bujhawan Layi.
Jay Wasdi Hondi Loko Yasrab Wich Ummat Mubahila Di.

2
Zakhmi Aye Fatima Da Pehlu Neel Ne Chehray Te.
Aj Pehli Bach Gayi Aye Wich Yasrab Tasveer Karbala Di.
Zahra Di Qabar Bana Ke Te Wain Ali Kita.
Dunya Te Wadh Gayi Aye Aj Zahra Tanhai Murtaza Di.

3
Puchday Ray Teri Sadaqat Da Kaun Gawah Aithay.
Bhul Gaye Ne Loag Zahra Waris Way Kausar Te Innama Di.
Aa Gayi Aye Fidak Di Mehroma Dewo Ainu Pursa.
Mehshar De Roz Akbar Aani Aye Aawaz Kibriya Di.
