1
Kis Tarha Likhon Main Kya Rutba Abu Talib Ka Hai.
Mustafa Jis Ka Parhein Kalma Abu Talib Ka Hai.
Kis Tarha Tujh Ko Mile Gi Manzil-e-Haq Aye Akhi.
Jata Hai Jo Soye Haq Jadah Abu Talib Ka Hai.

2
Naaz Se Tere Nabi Bachpan Ke Iss Aalim Mein Bhi.
Ghomte The Jis Pe Woh Shana Abu Talib Ka Hai.
Hashar Tak Yeh Parcham-e-Nabvi Rahe Ga Sar Buland.
Khaliq-e-Akbar Ko Yeh Wadah Abu Talib Ka Hai.

3
Naam Hai Imran Tu Phir Dekh Lo Quran Mein.
Aal-e-Imraan Ka Bhi Tu Sorah Abu Talib Ka Hai.
Uthta Hai Har Door Mein Batil Ke Jo Rukhsar Par.
Matami Ke Roop Mein Panja Abu Talib Ka Hai.

4
Sun Lo Aye Na Pak Naslo Aap Ke Ajdad Par.
Ibtida Hi Se Raha Galba Abu Talib Ka Hai.
Paas Mat Jana Wahan Bistar Mein Jo Mojood Hai.
Hai Nahi Woh Khud Magar Naqsha Abu Talib Ka Hai.

5
Lo Kiye Deta Hoon Main Talkhees Unn Ke Shan Ki.
Sari Dunya Rab Ki Hai Qabza Abu Talib Ka Hai.
Ahleeyat Itni Kahan Thi Tujh Mein:ZAKIR YOUSUFI:.
Roop Mein Ashar Ke Tohfa Abu Talib Ka Hai.
