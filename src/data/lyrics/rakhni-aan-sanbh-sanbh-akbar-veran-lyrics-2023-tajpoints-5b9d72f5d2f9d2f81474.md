1
Rakhni Aan Sanbh Sanbh Akbar Veran Yadaan Teriyaan Sheraz Khan.
Pairaan De Nishanaa Nal Juriyan Ne Sanwaan Meriyaan.
Chan Charhda Jis Waqt Da Takiya Dil Mera Haye Dub Dub Jaway.
Khali Wehray Veer Haneray Ainj Lagda Jiwein Sah Na Aaway.
Sadaat Gharaan Wich Uddiyan Ne Har Pasay Mitiyaan.
Aanday Hoye Qafiliyan Diyaan Wehriyan Wich Aawaz Udeekaan.
Yousuf Taun Wadh Sohne Akbar Veer Diyaan Jinj Rahwan Wekhaan.
Yousuf Diyaan Bhaina V Ewein Nayi Rahwaan Wekhiyaan.
Safar Te Jawan Walay Sare Dardaan Wich V Sokhay Hoye.
Na Veerani Ghar Di Wekhi Na Kandhan Nal Lag Ke Roye.
Sughra Diyaan Manzalaan Ne Veran Safraan Tu Okhiyaan.
Waag Phari Na Ganay Paye Na Sehrey Diyaan Lariyaan Baniyaan.
Aisi Ghar Wich Kali Hoi Sariyaan Galaan Dil Wich Rayiyaan.
Tere Shagna Diyaan Jiniyaan V Main Galaan Sochiyaan.
Akbar Veer De Nawein Akbar Khat Sughra Ne Aisa Likhiya.
Rehndi Dunya Taein Har Ik Bhain Parhey Gi Aykho Noha.
Bhaina Diyaan Hoan Na Dunya Te Veraan Taun Doriyaan.
