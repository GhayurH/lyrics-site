1
Aye Sabz Qaba Mola Syed Raza Abbas Zaidi.
Aye Sabz Qaba Maula Aye Surkh Kafan Maula.
Barsat Hai Teeron Ki Kyun Tujh Pe Hasan Maula.
Yeh Kaisa Janaza Hai Jo Laut Ke Ghar Aaya.
Iss Teri Ghareebi Pe Roti Hai Behan Maula.
Haye Zehar-e-Hilahil Ne Kaisa Yeh Sitam Dhaya.
Tukray Hai Jigar Tera Zakhmi Hai Dahan Maula.
Abbas Yeh Ro Ro Ke Kehne Lagay Sarwar Se.
Dekhein Tu Zara Kitne Zakhmi Hain Hasan Maula.
Jab Tere Janazay Par Teeron Ki Hoi Barish.
Kis Tarha Sahi Tu Ne Teeron Ki Chubhan Maula.
Aoulad-e-Muhammad Par Jari Hai Sitam Haye.
Kyun Ahl-e-Madina Ka Badla Hai Chalan Maula.
Jis Tarha Jigar Tera Tukray Hai Issi Tarha.
Bat Jaye Ga Tukron Mein Qasim Ka Badan Maula.
Jin Hathoon Se Zainab Ne Teeron Ko Nikala Hai.
Hogi Inhi Hathoon Mein Ik Roz Rasan Maula.
Aye Maula Teri Tarha Zakhmi Hai Teri Turbat.
Hai Ghair Teri Khatir Apna Hi Watan Maula.
 Rehan-o- Raza Haye Giriya Na Karein Kyun Kar.
Phir Fatima Zahra Ka Ujra Hai Chaman Maula.
