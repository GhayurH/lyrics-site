1
Baba Beemar Nu Pawein Lay Ke Tu Na Ja Asghar Nu Choor Ja.
Ath Din Da Veer Hai Mera Melaan Da Safar Aye Tera Kinj Toran Main Bhira.
Jinay Maa Nu Raj Nayi Takiya Jera Maa Kehraan Nahi Sikhiya.
Fehrist De Wich Tu Baba Oss Veer Da Naam V Likhiya.

2
Te Mera Naa Mitwaya Mehmal Toon Aan Lahaya Das Ki Meri Khata.
Zara Tak Asghar Di Sorat Aidi Akbar Naal Shabahat.
Aye Parah De Ja Mainu Aidi Kardi Raan Gi Tilawat.
Mera Asghar Jad V Hasda Menu Akbar Warga Lagda Chad Ja Aye Aasra.

3
Jithe Qafila Tur Chaliya Aye Othay Sunrya AYe Sirf Qaza Aye.
Tusi Sare Maut De Rahi Kali Jeen Layi Sughra Aye.
Wich Khak Sula Nayi Lay Gi Mainu Wabar Di Ja Nahi De Gi Jageer-e-Karbala.
Jaye Maut Toon Bach Pawaan Gi Tere Samne Nayi Awaan Gi.

4
Jadoon Wapas Toon Aawein Ga Kand Kar Ke Khalo Jawaan Gi.
Toon Aakhda Reh Jawein Ga Rukh Phair Ke Wichari Sughra Babay Nu Munh Wakha.
Onno Shakal Nabi Di Manday Menu Shakal Batool Di Kehnday.
Lay Chaliyaan Aye Naal Akbar Nu Toon Maa Di Sorat Chad Ke.

5
Dadi De Wangoon Main V Ro Ro K Mar Jawaan Gi Main V Aan Fatima.
Hun Ki Soraj Te Saweray Sughra Layi Sirf Haneray.
Ja Lay Ja Sare Veeran Sab Chanan Aj Toon Tere.
 Akbar Aye Aakhri Gal C Jeri Piyo Nal Sughra Kiti Piyo Ronda Tur Gaya.
