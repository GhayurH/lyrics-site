Zindaan de darr te shamiyan nu kaway ro ro ke Sajjad ay
Main chaana mayyit Sakina da karo hath meray azaad ay

1
Baray din ton baad araam aya hun rondi nayi chup kargayi ay
Ay de chehray da rang badal gaya lagda dardan ton thak payi ay
Zalim marr behn gayi meri ay de hun ta rasan laha de

2
Ho ke pamaal Asghar baneya taweez Hussain de seenay da
Dhi kamsin shaam ich dafan hoyi seh seh ke zulam kameenay da
Rab janay vichar Rubab ton gayi kyun enjj choti aulaad ay

3
Mere samnay Akbar da qatil vich safar de veeran hasda ay
Mainu ronda vekh ke Abid vi seh sakda nai gash karda ay
Main marr vesaan ess zaalim nu aa Ghazi tu haya de

4
Ajj hukam dita jiwen shaam de vich akhay Ghazi karbal vich kehndi
Na rasan utay mere bazooa vich na sir ton vi chadar lehdni
Na enjj pardes de vich ruldi hondi veeran naal abaad ay

5
Bazaar de vich dakhil hondi jadon asar de velay Aun di maa
Kaway zalimo dhi han Zahra di pawen ajj turdi haan be parda
Kar desan wapis ek waari deyo chadran naam Khuda de

6
Ek turan da wal vi nayi anda dooja turan da raah vi nayi denday
Lo badr de badle Zainab ton ay pathar maar ke sab kehnday
Tak halat ujri Zainab di mar mar jeenda Sajjad ay

7
Meri qaidan behn ajj margayi ay tanveer aqeel Sajjad akhay
Hath kariyan walay bazooan te bazaran aa maayit chaa ke
Koi bashar Khuda pardesiyan da mainu qabristan vekha de
