1
Rozadaroon Mein Matam Bapa Hai Askari Hasan Zaidi
Deh Gaya Aaj Rukan-e-Hidayat Aa Gayi Syedon Par Qayamat.
Din Yeh Ashor Se Kam Nahi Hai, Hai Ali Ka Yeh Youm-e-Shahadat.
Arsh Se Yeh Sada Aa Rahi Hai Phir Se Zahra Ki Dunya Luti Hai.

2
Qatal Maula Mera Ho Gaya Hai Roza Daroon Mein Matam Bapa Hai.
Har Koi Khon-e-Dil Ro Raha Hai Roza Daroon Mein Matam Bapa Hai.
Ibn-e-Muljam Ne Zarbat Lagai De Raha Hai Musalla Duhai.
Khon Se Tar Namazi Howa Hai Roza Daroon Mein Matam Bapa Hai.

3
Dard Thamta Nahi Ik Bhi Lamha Khak Par Bas Taraptay Hain Maula.
Zakhm Syed Ko Gehra Laga Hai Roza Daroon Mein Matam Bapa Hai.
Khoon Se Rangeen Hai Sara Chehra Hai Namazi Pe Aalam Gashi Ka.
Iss Qadar Khon Sar Se Baha Hai Roza Daroon Mein Matam Bapa Hai.

4
Laye Baba Ko Masjid Se Kaisay Koi Hasnain Ke Dil Se Pochay.
Sadma Ummat Ne Kiya Kiya Diya Hai Roza Daroon Mein Matam Bapa Hai.
Betiyaan Meri Hain Mehway Noha Sun Na Le Koi Aawaz-e-Giriya.
Keh Ke Maula Mera Ro Raha Hai Roza Daroon Mein Matam Bapa Hai.

5
Dard Pehlu Ka Barhney Laga Hai Fatima Par Sitam Phir Howa Hai.
Sajday Mein Khon-e-Haider Baha Hai Roza Daroon Mein Matam Bapa Hai.
 Askari Kalay Kaprey Bhi Pehno Pursa Haider Ka Do Mustafa Ko.
Aye Hilal Aaj Dil Ghamzada Hai Roza Daroon Mein Matam Bapa Hai.
