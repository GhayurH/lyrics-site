1
Kamaal Ye Hai Naat Hussain Al Hussaini.
Beghair Jang-o-Jidal Fath-e-Mobeen Pana Kamal Yeh Hai.
Khud Apne Dushman Pe Abar-e-Rehmat Ki Tarha Chana Kamal Yeh Hai.
Khuda Ki Wahdaniyat Ke Aagay Jo Kufar Aaya Pahar Ban Kar.
Tu Kankaron Ki Zabaan Pe Kalma Khuda Ka Lana Kamal Yeh Hai.
Moashrah Woh Sitam Ka Markaz Tha Jis Mein Zalim Ka Bool Bala.
Ossi Mein Reh Kar Amen-o-Sadiq Laqab Ka Pana Kamal Yeh Hai.
Salam Tehzeeb Khulq-o-Ahsaan Yeh Sab Hain Husan-e-Salook Lekin.
Jo Zulm Dhaya Karey Iyadat Ko Oss Ki Jana Kamal Yeh Hai.
Dar-e-Nabi Pe Adab Se Kehta Hai Takht-e-Bilqees Laney Wala.
Hazoor! Chashm-e-Zadan Mein Qausain Ja Ke Aana Kamal Yeh Hai.
Har Ik Nabi Ne Khuda Ke Aagay Taveel Sajday Kiye Hain Lekin.
Jahan Zarori Hai Tool-e-Sajdah Wahan Barhana Kamal Yeh Hai.
Woh Noor-e-Paikar Jo Oss Ko Sidrah Ke Aagay Dekho Tu Kya Tajub.
Bashar Ki Sorat Turab Oss Ka Zameen Pe Aana Kamal Yeh Hai.
