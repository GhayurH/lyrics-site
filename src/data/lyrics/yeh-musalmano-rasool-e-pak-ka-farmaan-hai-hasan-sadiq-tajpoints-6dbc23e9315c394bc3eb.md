1
Yeh Musalmano Rasool e Pak Ka Farmaan Hai Hasan Sadiq.
Tum Mein Choray Ja Raha Hoon Aal Our Quran Hai.
Khoob Hai Ajar-e-Risalat Mustafa Ko Mil Raha.
Qatal Syed Ho Rahe Hain Jal Raha Quran Hai.

2
Jis Ke Lab Harkat Nahi Kartay Beghair Amar-e-Khuda.
Kis Tarha Yeh Keh Diya Tu Ne Ossay Hizyan Hai.
Dono Aalam Ke Liye Jo Bais e Rehmat Bana.
Kya Ossi Ki Aal Ke Hi Wastay Zindan Hai.

3
Deen-e-Ahmad Ki Baqa Ke Wastay Waiz Bata.
Juz Nabi Ki Aal Ke Kis Ka Howa Nuqsan Hai.
Al Hussaino Mini Se Yeh Saaf Zahir Ho Gaya.
Keh Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Mein Mustafa Ki Jan Hai.

4
Yeh Nishaan Matam Ke Dil Mein Gham Ali Ke Lal Ka.
Apni Bakhshish Ka Hamarey Pas Yeh Saman Hai.
Naam Ossi Hasti Ke Hai Yeh Aaj Ka Noha Ameer.
Jo Azal Se Anbiya Ka Momino Sultan Hai.
