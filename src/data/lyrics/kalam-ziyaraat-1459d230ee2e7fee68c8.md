بسم اللہ الرحمن الرحیم
Maula madinay walay

1
Jin ka nana nabi s.a.w
Jin ka baba Ali a.s
Jin ki maa Fatima a.s
Jin ka bhai Hassan a.s
Wo hain Maula hamaray Hussain a.s

2
Karbal main agaye hain Karbal basanay walay

3
Khaimay saja rahay hain Wada nibhanay walay

4
1. Yeh dar wo pak dar hai, Jo Fatima ka dar hai
Yeh payaray Mustafa ka, Maula Ali ka ghar hai
Maktal mai agaye hain, Quran sunanay walay
2. Islam nay pukara, Quran nay sada di
Yasrab ka shahazada, Yasrab ki shahazadi
Niklay safar ko ghar sai, kalma bachanay walay
3. Ro ro kay boli Sughra a.s, Dil dobta hai mera
Veeran kia Madina, Jungal may hai basaira
Mujh ko rula gaey kioun, Khushian lutanay walay
4. Damad kay dularay, Beti kay shahazaday
Thy teen din kay pyasay, Sab phool Mustafa kay
Katray ko hain yahi to, Darya bananay walay
5. Sab lut gaye kamaye, Akbar nay barchi khai
Asghar nay teer khaya, Daiti thi maa duhai
Teeroun kay saey may hain, Sajday may Jana walay
6. Halmin ka istaghasa, Jab dey chukka wo piyasa
Awaz-e-irjai nay, Aa kar diya dilasa
Aa meray pass aja, Lashay uthanay walay
7. Aik shoor ha falak par, Kehta tha Rab-e-Akbar
Jibreel badaloon ka, Jaldi bechao bister
Jannat may a rahay hain, Quran bachanay walay
8. Salman aur shanawar, Khush bakht hoon ali jee
Rehan ki tamna, Sarwar ki jo dua thy
Khidmat qabool kar lay, Qismet banany walay
