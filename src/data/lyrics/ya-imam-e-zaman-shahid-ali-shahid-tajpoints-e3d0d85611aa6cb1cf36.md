Ya Imam e Zaman Shahid Ali Shahid.

1
Tujh Se Faryad Hai Nasal-e-Bedar Ki.
Zulmaton Ke Andheron Se Bezaar Ki.
Jaisay Kofay Mein Meesum Se Khuddar Ki.
Apne Rehbar Se Qaum-e-Wafadar Ki.
Hum Hain Hazir Kahan Hain Woh Taig-o-Sina.
Mere Maula Main Tera Wafadar Hoon.

2
Tera Bazu Bhi Hoon Teri Talwar Hoon.
Qad Mein Chota Hoon Par Main Bhi Sardar Hoon.
Misal-e-Qasim Main Marne Ko Teyar Hoon.
Aazma Lijiye Mujh Ko Aye Meharbaan.
Main Ne Mana Keh Ghaflat Mein Insan Hai.
Par Hatheli Pe Mere Meri Jan Hai.

3
Tere Dam Se Baharein Hain Baran Hai.
Bin Tere Sari Dunya Bhi Zindan Hai.
Tujh Pe Qurban Hoon Mere Dono Jahan.
Meri Nazron Mein Hai Dasht-e-Karb-o-Bala.
Main Hoon Waris Bahatar Ke Kirdar Ka.
Main Bhi:ARIF HUSSAINI: Banoon Ga Tera.

4
Khoon Mein Doba Howa Hoga Lasha Mera.
Dijiye Mujh Ko Maqtal Mein Hukam-e-Azaan.
Zulm Kab Tak Yeh Dunya Sahey Jaye Gi.
Aap Ke Bin Yeh Bigri Na Ban Paye Gi.
Aap Aaein Gay Tu Roshni Aaye Gi.
Na Sakina Andheron Mein Ghabraye Gi.

5
Aap Hain Jis Yateema Pe Giriya Kuna.
Hum Hain Mamoor Batil Ke Inkar Par.
Khoon Hamara Hai Zalim Ki Talwar Par.
Soz-e- Shahid Mein Haider Ke Ashar Par.
Hai Kabhi Jo Masajid Mein Dewar Par.
Zair-e-Khanjar Kahey Gi Yeh Apni Zabaan.
