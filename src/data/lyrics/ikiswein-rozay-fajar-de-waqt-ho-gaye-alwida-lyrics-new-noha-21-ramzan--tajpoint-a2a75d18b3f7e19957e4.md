Ikiswein Rozay Fajar De Waqt Ho Gaye Alwida Lyrics.

1

Kalma Gowaan Sadhay Sir Taun Piyo Da Saya Kho Liya.
Shabar-o-Shabbir, Ghazi Nu Bola Ke Waro Waar.
