Mujko Maloom Hai Abid Peh Qayamat Guzari.

Bharay Bazaar Seh  Jab Malika-e-Ghairat Guzari.

Mola Shabbir Seh Sajaad Talak Atay Huway
Khoon May Doobh Kay  Maqtal Seh Imamat Guzari.

Qaari Shabbir Zamanay May Baray Dekhay Hain
Nok-e-Naiza Peh  Bus Teri Talawat Guzari.

Zarf-e-Qatil Ko Parakhnay Kay Liye Asghar Tah
Kisi Masoom Ki  Aisi Na Shahadat Guzari.

Haar Bashar Roya Hai Ek Jaisa Bharay Aalam May
Is Kay Ashko Si  Duniya May Nah Rangat Guzari.

Kay Bazaar Main Zainab Kuch Istara Jaisa
Haaya Kay Kabaar Main  Lipati Howi Mayaat Guzari.

Jiskay Parday Ka Nighaiban Tah Abbas-e-jaari
Kaisay Darbaaro Seh  Buniyad Sharafat Guzari.

Dil Pata Khoon Jigaar Cheer Peh Aansu Hai Bana
Haye Sajaad Peh  Kya Kya Na Museebat Guzari.

Jab Ghiray Ghaash Seh Toh Beemar Ghiray Sajday Main
Bhari Sajdou Seh  Sajaad Ki Fidaraat Guzari.

Choomta Rehta Hai Adil To Unhi Raahon Ko
Mola Abid Ki  Jis Raah Seh Ziarat Guzari.
