Ghurbat-e-Sajjad Saleem Mastan Wa Bradran

1
Kaisay Ho Biyaan Abid-e-Beemar Ki Ghurbat
Oss Chadar-e-Zainab Ke Azadar Ki Ghurbat
Yani Sar-e-Shabbir Ke Gham Khawar Ki Ghurbat
Jo Qaid Hai Oss Qafila Salar Ki Ghurbat
Ghar Lutnay Ke Gham Mein Jo Sakoon Se Nahi Soya
Chalees Baras Aankhon Se Jo Khon Hai Roya

2
Jaltay Hoye Khaimon Ka Dhowaan Dekha Hai Iss Ne
Luttay Hoye Ik Din Mein Jahan Dekha Hai Iss Ne
Sar Sab Ka Sar-e-Nook-e-Sina Dekha Hai Iss Ne
Bazar Mein Mehshar Ka Samaa Dekha Hai Iss Ne
Kab Zulm-o-Sitam Iss Qadar Ajdad Pe Guzrey
Jo Qaid Mein Ik Syed-e-Sajjad Pe Guzrey

3
Be Pardah Haram Jab Sar-e-Darbar Gaye They
Baithay They Wahan Loag Jo Badkar Barey They
Kab Zulm-o-Sitam Abid-e-Muztar Pe Rukay They
Behna Ko Kaneezi Mein Laeen Mang Rahe They
Humsheer Ka Naam Aaya Jo Badkar Ke Lab Par
Sajjad Lahu Rotay Rahe Khak Pe Gir Kar

4
Jab Sham Ke Zindan Mein Woh Qafila Pohancha
Tareeki Pe Karne Lagi Khud Raat Bhi Noha
Bhai Se Galay Lag Ke Pukari Yeh Sakina
Yaad Aata Hai Aye Bhaiyya Bohat Mujh Ko Madina
Baba Ko Bola Do Keh Woh Karwa Dein Gay Aazad
Baba Nahi Aaye Tu Main Mar Jaon Gi Sajjad

5
Rotay Hoye Jo Qaid Mein Woh Soyi Sakina
Kuch Dair Hi Guzri Uthi Kartay Hoye Giriya
Abid Se Kaha Lainay Mujhe Aate Hain Baba
Zindan Mein Sar Hazrat-e-Shabbir Ka Aaya
Zindan Bhi Gaunj Utha Ajab Aah-o-Fogaan Se
Rotay Hoye Baba Ko Sakina Gayi Jaan Se

6
Ik BiBi Ne Sughra Se Biyaan Ki Yeh Riwayat
Ghurbat Bhi Boka Karti Thi Aisi Thi Woh Ghurbat
Sab Bibiyaan Roti Thin Keh Aayi Thi Qayamat
Sajjad Pe Tha Sab Se Bara Waqt-e-Musibat
Pardais Se Yasrab Jo Nahi Laya Behan Ko
Zindan Ki Tareeki Mein Dafnaya Behan Ko
