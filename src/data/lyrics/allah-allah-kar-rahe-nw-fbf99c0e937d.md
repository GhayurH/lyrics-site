ajeeb shor hai ke shor may sukoon hai

ajab sukoon hai sukoon may junoon hai

ye wo junoon hai jo deen deen ka sukoon hai

ye kya ye kyun ye kaise hai ye sochna haram hai

kisi bashar ki baat kab khuda ka khud kalaam hai

fazkuruni az kurukum, vash kurooli wala takfurukun

quran may khud kehta hai khuda, wo mujhko kabhi jhutlaata nahi

aur mera shaakir rehta hai jo mera zaakir hota hai, mai uska zaakir banta hoo

allah allah kar rahe hai hussain, kar raha hai khuda hussain hussain

ya hussain, ya hussain

khauf ahle sitam pe hai kaari, zikre shabbir ab bhi hai jaari

jab khuda khud hai zaakir e sarwar, kaun rokega ye azadari

ishq ka tarnuma hussain hussain, kar raha hai khuda hussain hussain

ya hussain….

allah allah kar rahe..

kehdiya aaya e mawaddat ne, humse maanga hai kya risaalat ne

ab bhi akhlon pe hai paray parde, raaze quran na samjha ummat ne

ibne zehra hai ya hussain hussain, kar raha hai khuda hussain hussain

ya hussain….

allah allah kar rahe…

uski baatil namaz hojaye, shak jo zaate hussain may laaye

jo namaze khuda ko thukraye, tab wo matam kiye kaha jae

ay shahure waqa hussain hussain, kar raha hai khuda hussain hussain

ya hussain….

allah allah kar rahe….

kaise qaidi thay karbala walay, sheh ke maqsad ko saath leke chalay

kab wo ghaafil rahe hai sajdo se, qaid may bhi namaz padhte rahe

aur jaari raha hussain hussain, kar raha hai khuda hussain hussain

ya hussain….

allah allah kar rahe…

jab madine may aaye ahle haram, rozo shab tha hussain ka matam

ab bhi tareekh de rahi hai sada, ghar may sajjad ke khuda ki qasam

eid ke din hua hussain hussain, ya husain ya husain…..

sada ye ghaib se aati hai ibne zahra ki

ye kaisi bahes may uljhi hui hai qaum meri

namaz ka koi munkir hai majliso ka koi

takallum aur hai darvesh ki tamanna yehi

khudara jism ko tum jaan se juda na karo

namaz aur aza ka tum muqabla na karo
