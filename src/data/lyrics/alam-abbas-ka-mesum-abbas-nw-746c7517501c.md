. Hum Ne Alam Jab Ghar Mein Sajaya, Ghar Mein Rakhe ZEHRA Ne Qadam

Door Huye Sab Ranj O Masayeb, Phir Kaisi Mushkil Kaisa Ghum

Daste Karam Hai, SHEH Ka Bharam Hai

. Ye Jo Bandhi Hai Mashk E SAKINA, Isko Kaho Taweez E Wafa

Rishta Bhatiji Aur Chacha Ka, Aaj Talak Na Toot Saka

Ek Kahani, Iski Zabani

Sun Lo Agar Sunte Ho Sada

. Dekho ALAM Ka Surkh Pharehra, Chadar E ZAINAB Se Hai Bana

Jo Bhi ALAM Ke Saaye Mein Aya, Duniya Ke Sab Ghum Bhool Gaya

Ab Jaake Mana, Sara Zamana

Saya Fighan Ghar Ghar Pe Hua

. Aisa ALAM Duniya Mein Dikhao, Jiski Ata Ho Rab Ki Ata

Shaan E Azadari Ye Alam Hai, Dekho Azakhano Mein Zara

Mannatain Puri, Karta Hai Sabki

Zinda Hai Iske Dam Se Wafa
