Betay Ky Qatilon Sy Mir Hasan Mir

1
Akbar Mera Kahan Hai.
Betay Ke Qatilon Se Ik Baap Pochta Hai.
Koi Mujhe Bata De Lasha Kahan Para Hai.
Dil Par Meri Behan Ke Khanjar Chala Diya Hai.
Kis Ne Hamarey Ghar Ka Soraj Bhuja Diya Hai.
Din Mein Andhera Meri Aankhon Mein Ho Gaya Hai.

2
Pyasay Ko Be Watan Ko Itna Tu Mat Satao.
Chal Chal Ke Thak Gaya Hoon Logo Mujhe Batao.
Ab Our Kitna Baqi Maqtal Ka Fasila Hai.
Ghazi Ko Tum Ne Mara Main Chup Raha Na Bola.
Akbar Ko Mara Naiza Main Chup Raha Na Bola.
Tum Se Sawal Kar Loon Itna Tu Haq Mera Hai.

3
Maa Ko Beghair Dekhey Beta Guzar Na Jaye.
Pohancha Do Mujh Ko Jaldi Mazloom Mar Na Jaye.
Aawaz Aa Rahi Hai Woh Hichki Le Raha Hai.
Khaimay Se Jab Chala Tha Kehti Thi Meri Behna.
Pamal Ho Na Jaye Akbar Ki Lash Bhaiyya.
Kano Mein Mere Ab Tak Baqi Wohi Sada Hai.

4
Dil Betha Ja Raha Hai Ruktay Nahi Hain Aanso.
Jangal Se Aa Rahi Hai Oss Ke Lahu Ki Khushbo.
Kuch Sansein Chal Rahi Hain Ya Shair Chal Basa Hai.
Aoulad-e-Mustafa Se Kis Tarha Phir Gaye Ho.
Kariyal Jawaan Ka Marna Aasaan Samajh Rahe Ho.
Tum Loag Hans Rahe Ho Dil Mera Ro Raha Hai.

5
Ahl-e-Haram Ki Akbar Umeed Aakhri Hai.
Kehti Hai Yeh Zaeefi Mera Assa Yehi Hai.
Ab Do Qadam Bhi Chalna Dushwar Ho Gaya Hai.
Ghurbat Hai Our Takallum Ibn-e-Shah-e-Najaf Hai.
Koi Bola Iss Taraf Hai Koi Bola Oss Taraf Hai.
Aakhir Pukarey Maula Ghazi Tumhein Pata Hai.
