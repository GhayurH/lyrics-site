1
Phir Aik Janaza Hai Phir Shehar e Madina.
Haq Jeene Ka Baqir Se Ummat Ne Hai Chena.
Iss Shehar Mein Utha Tha Zahra Ka Janaza.
Our Hasan Pe Ummat Ne Kiya Teeron Ka Saya.
Khoon Rotay Hoye Dekha Dardoon Ka Hazeena.
Kar Kar Ke Yaad Roye Haye Maula-e-Baqir.
Zainab Ki Woh Aseeri Naizay Pe Woh Chadar.
Aayi Yaad Mar Gayi Jo Zindaan Mein Sakina.
Aoulad-e-Muhammad Ka Dushman Hai Yeh Zalim.
Baqir Ko Qatal Kar Do Kehta Hai Yeh Hakim.
Nana Tere Shehar Mein Mushkil Howa Jeena.
Tere Ghar Mein Kar Rahe Hain Sab Hajj Ki Teyari.
Our Ghar Pe Muhammad Ke Haye Zulm Hai Jari.
Ya Rab Yeh Bhool Baithay Hurmat Ka Maheena.
Jafar Ko Imamat Ka Mansab Jo Mila Hai.
Ilm-e-Nabi Ka Waris Phir Qatal Howa Hai.
Dukh Seh Ke Sikhaya Hai Jeene Ka Qareena.
Baqir Hai Gawah Sare Oss Zulm-o-Sitam Ka.
Jo Karbala Mein Sham Mein lnn Aankhon Ne Dekha.
Sar Naizay Pe Asghar Ka Akbar Ka Woh Seena.
Dukh Maula-e-Baqir Ke Asghar Ne Sunaye.
 Sajjad Bhi Zalim Ka Suno Naam Bataye.
Mere Maula Ka Qatil Hai Hashaam Kamina
