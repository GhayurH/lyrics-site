Karam Ke Our Ataon Ke Ik Samandar Ko.
Yahin Zameen Pe Khuld-e-Barin Ke Manzar Ko.

1
Tarasti Rehti Hain Aankhein Hussain Ke Dar Ko.
Aye Kash Dekhon Kabhi Tu Main Qabar-e-Sarwar Ko.
Hai Yehi Dil Ki Dua Jaon Main Karb-o-Bala.
Ho Muqadar Mein Likha Jaon Main Karb-o-Bala.

2
Ya Ali Sun Lo Sada Door Mushkil Kar Do.
Roza-e-Shah-e-Wafa Meri Manzil Kar Do.
Ho Karam Behar-e-Khuda Jaon Main Karb-o-Bala.
Band Sare Hi Kiye Iss Waba Ne Rastay.

3
Kyun Ziyarat Ke Liye Dil Na Mera Tarsay.
Phir Khulay Har Rasta Jaon Main Karb-o-Bala.
Aik Pal Hi Ko Sahi Sheh Ki Turbat Dekhon.
Aouj Par Main Bhi Zara Apni Qismat Dekhon.

4
Dekhon Janat Ki Fiza Jaon Main Karb-o-Bala.
Karbala Jaon Karon Matam-e-Shah-e-Ummam.
Ashk Aankhon Mein Rahein Youn Ho Shabeer Ka Gham.
Hai Tamana-e-Azza Jaon Main Karb-o-Bala.

5
Har Ghari Tashna Labhoon Ka Jo Ahsas Raha.
Iss Liye Wird-e-Zabaan Nam-e-Abbas Raha.
Bawafa Kehta Howa Jaon Main Karb-o-Bala.
Kya Karon Ab Tu Kahin Chain Milta Hi Nahi.

6
Karbala Aaye Nazar Tu Jhukaon Main Jabeen.
Mujh Ko Le Jaye Howa Jaon Main Karb-o-Bala.
Mere Maula Aaqa Ya Imam-e-Mehdi.
Ab Khabar Mil Jaye Aap Ke Aane Ki.

7
Gaib Ki Sun Loon Sada Jaon Main Karb-o-Bala.
Haan Mera Harf-e-Dua Husan-e-Guftar Howa.
Shah Se Ulfat Ka Phir Se Izhar Howa.
Jab Bhi Meesum Ne Kaha Jaon Main Karb-o-Bala.
