Pani Masooma Ne Manga Tu Tamachay Khaye
Roi Baba Ko Sakina Tu Tamachay Khaye

1
Jaltay Khaimo Se Nikalte Hoye Oss Bachi Ne
Rasta Pocha Najaf Ka Tu Tamachay Khaye
Cheenne Aaya Jo Bachi Ki Rida Shimar-e-Laeen
Iss Ne Chadar Ko Na Chora Tu Tamachay Khaye

2
Apne Chehray Ko Tamachon Se Bachane Ke Liye
Nanne Hathon Se Chupaya Tu Tamachay Khaye
Bhai Ka Nanna Sa Sar Girta Tha Jab Naizay Se
Iss Ne Godi Mein Uthaya Tu Tamachay Khaye

3
Kyun Mere Baba Pe Zalim Tu Chalata Hai Churi
Oss Ne Jab Shimar Se Pocha Tu Tamachay Khaye
Kofa-o-Sham Ke Bazar Mein Chalte Chalte
Thak Ke Bethi Jo Sakina Tu Tamachay Khaye

4
Gir Gayi Thi Woh Kahin Chalte Hoye Naqay Se
Shimar Jab Dhond Ke Laya Tu Tamachay Khaye
:MIR SAJJAD: Sakina Bhi Hai Zahra Ki Tarha
Ho Gayi Jab Yeh Yateema Tu Tamachay Khaye
