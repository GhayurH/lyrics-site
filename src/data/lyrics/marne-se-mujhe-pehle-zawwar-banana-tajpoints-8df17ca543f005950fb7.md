Marne Se Mujhe Pehle Zawwar Banana.

1
Aye Maula Mere Aaqa Pori Yeh Dua Karna.

2
Yeh Ashk Asasa Hai Kuch Pas Na Dolat Hai.
Kiya Meri Hi Qismat Mein Likhi Yehi Furqat Hai.
Tum Soyi Hoi Meri Qismat Ko Jagana.
Zainab Ke Waseelay Se Zahra Ke Waseelay Se.
Mangi Hai Dua Main Ne Parcham Se Lipat Kar Yeh.
Roya Hoon Bohat Maula Ab Our Na Rulwana.
Woh Din Bhi Kabhi Aaye Main Karbala Ko Jaon.
Dekhon Main Tera Roza Zawwar Main Kehlaon.
Khush Bakht Bachay Din Woh Ho Karbala Ko Jana.
Ashor Karon Maula Main Tere Hi Rozay Pe.
Mein Noha Parhon Tere Abbas Ka Ro Ro Ke.
Qismat Mein Meri Likh De Waan Ashk-e-Gham Bahana.
 Zaair Yeh Dua Teri Manzoor Hai Maula Ko.
Yeh Khuwab Mein Dekha Hai Kehte Hain Chaley Aao.
Yeh Noha Karbala Mein Aye Own Tum Sunana.
