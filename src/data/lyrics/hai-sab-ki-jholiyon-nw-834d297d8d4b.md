Shaam haye shaam

hai sab ki jholiyon may pathar ye kaunsi basti hai fizza

hota hai charagha kyun ghar ghar ye kaunsi basti hai fizza

shaam haye shaam

poocho to zara amma insay kyun dekhne humko aatay hai

har simt khushi ka aalam hai bazaar sajaye jaate hai

kyu eid ke jaisa hai manzar ye kaunsi basti hai fizza

shaam haye shaam

hai sab ki jholiyon…

sajjad ke aankhon se ab tak aansu hi baraste dekhe thay

ab dekh rahi hoo mai uski aankhon se musalsal khoon behte

poocho to kisi se ye jaakar ye kaunsi basti hai fizza

shaam haye shaam

baaghi aaye baaghi aaye ye kaisi manadi hoti hai

lagta hai zamane baad koi is shehr may shaadi hoti hai

kuch aisay baam o dar ye kaunsi basti hai fizza

shaam haye shaam

hum sab ke saro se behte hai kis waaste ye khoon ke dhaare

koi khaulta pani phayk raha hai koi phaynk raha hai angaray

sab mil kar maarte hai pathar ye kaunsi basti hai fizza

shaam haye shaam

waada jo kiya tha yasrab may aata hai yaad wohi waada

mehsoos mujhe ye hota hai takta hai koi mera rasta

kya shireen ka hai paas may ghar ye kaunsi basti hai fizza

shaam haye shaam

quran sunaye jaate thay shabbir sare noke naiza

rota tha takallum ibne ali jab poochti thi binte zehra

kyun girta hai abbas ka sar ye kaunsi basti hai fizza

shaam haye shaam
