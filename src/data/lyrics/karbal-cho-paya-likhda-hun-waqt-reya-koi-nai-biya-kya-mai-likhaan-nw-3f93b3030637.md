Karbal Cho Piya Likhda, Hun Waqt Reya Koi Nai, Biya Kya Mei Likhaan

Tendiya Phupiya Te Mawaan Da Sajjad Siwa Koi Nai Biya Kya Mei Likhaan

Qasim Da Jo Nay Likhya Oh Ku Banara Banaa Baytha

Udeh Sehreh Saja Baytha Udeh Shagan Mana Baytha

Tedee Bhayna De Hathan Tay Hun Rang-e-Hina Ko Nai Biya Kya Mei Likhaan

Lug Nazar Gayi Ujrii Tendeh Yusaf-e-Sani Koon

Umat Ne Hai Raj Lutya Akbar Di Jawani Koon

Koi Rasam Vi Shadi Di Mein Kiti Ata Koi Nai Biya Kya Mei Likhaan

May Jawan Mara Baytha Meh Nadaan Koha Baytha

Habib Mazahir Jay Mehman Mara Baytha

Inj Khoon De Wich Tar Haan Sajde Di Jaga Koi Nai Biya Kya Mei Likhaan

Tain Jo Likhiya Ey Ghazi Tay Hai Dhair Gila Maikoon

Hunrh Day Na Gilay Ujrhi Giya Chorh Bhira Maikoon

Pursa Ainja Ghazi Da Jhaliya Fazal Di Maan Koi Nai Biya Kya Main Likha

Jehrhay Weer Di Ulfat Tay Hinni Tohfay Diwa Bhaijay

Jehrhay Chotiyaan Waalay Day Hinni Jorhay Siwa Bhaijay

Pawanrh Da Okoon Moqa Ummat Nay Dita Koi Nai Biya Kya Main Likha

Hunrh Mang Duaa Ujrhi Jaffar Da Sakhi Aaway

Sehray Teday Weeran Koon Watna Tay Oo Khud Paaway

Baie Een Toon Duaa Meri Koi Aur Dua Koi Nain Biya Kya Main Likha
