1
Muslim Di Lash Nu Lokan Ne Haye Galiyan Wich Phiraya.
Haye Ral Ke Kofiyaan Ne Din Eid Da Manaya.
Qurban Inj Ne Kite Bachre V Sain De.
Mare Ne Jigar Goshe Ghazi Di Bhain De.
Haris Ne Sar Nu Wekho Gardan Taun Aye Lahaya.
Khushiyan Ne Aj Mananday Bachiyan Nu Mar Ke.
Haye Eid Ne Manonde Kaliyan Nu Mar Ke.
Haye Lokaan Ne Nabi Di Inj Aal Nu Mukaya.
Kainday Si Maro Aynu Bhai Hussain Da Aye.
Gardan Lahao Aidi Bachra Aye Sain Da Aye.
Badlay Di Aag Nu Lokan Kofay De Wich Bhojaya.
Akhiyan Si Wekhdi Aye Manzar Anjeeb Kofe.
Latki Aye Lash Wekho Muslim Ghareeb Kofe.
Qurban Kar Ke Khud Nu ALLAH Nu Hai Bachaya.
Bibi Aye Pak Daman Pursa Qabool Howay.
Har Matami Te Nori Sadqa Nazool Howay.
Manzoor Howay Arzi Jarrar Di Khudaya.
