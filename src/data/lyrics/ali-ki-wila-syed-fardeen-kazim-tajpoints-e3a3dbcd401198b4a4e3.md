Ali Ki Wila Syed Fardeen Kazim Lyrics
Ali Ki Wila Ka Nishaan Dekhtey Hain.
Jo Hajj Par Khuda Ka Makaan Dekhtey Hain.
Khushi Se Yeh Meesum Batata Hai Sab Ko.
Ali Aaj Meri Dukaan Dekhtey Hain.
Ali Ke Makaan Par Utarta Hai Tarah.
Sitarey Niya Aasmaan Dekhtey Hain.
Jala Tu Diya Hai Charag-e-Tabarah.
Uthay Ga Kahan Se Dhowaan Dekhtey Hain.
Sitaron Se Aagay Jahan Our Bhi Hain.
Wahan Bhi Ali Hain Jahan Dekhtey Hain.
Ali Ke Fazail Sahey Gi Na Dunya.
Kahin Chal Ke Koi Kunwaan Dekhtey Hain.
Safein Bandh Kar Aasmaan Walay Fizza.
Tere Hath Ki Rotiyaan Dekhtey Hain.
Uthaye Hoye Hain Hussain-o-Hasan Ko.
Nabi Apne Kaun-o-Makaan Dekhtey Hain.
Lehad Mein Farishtay Mujhe Chomtay Hain.
Jo Matam Ka Mere Nishaan Dekhtey Hain.
Abu Jahl Ko Rook Kar Ahl-e-Maka.
Tamachay Ka Munh Par Nishaan Dekhtey Hain.
Chalo Aaj Masjid Ki Chokhat Pe Akbar.
Ali Haq Ki De Kar Azaan Dekhtey Hain.
