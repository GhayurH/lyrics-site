Alwida Ya husain Ai husain Alwida
Alwida Ya husain Ai husain Alwida

1
Roke boli bahan ai husain alwida
Shaam o kufa ki raaho se lejaenge
Ye laeen har qadam par sitam dhaenge
Bandh rahi hai rasan

2
Jiska har phul hai yaadgar e iram
Aah karbobala me badaste sitam
Lutgaya woh chaman

3
Yaad aata rahega tera waqeya
Tujhko rota rahega zamana sada
Aie gharibul watan

4
Aaj tabut e sarwar se lipte raho
Aaj rote raho aur kehte raho
Haye shaahe zaman

5
Shaih ki ruksat hai viraan hai sara ghar
Kyu na hum bhi kahei peetkar apna sar
Ai shahe zulmenan

6
Matami farsh ab ghar se uthne lage
Taziye khane viraan hone lage
Aie shahe bekafan

7
Saal bhar apni aankhei bhigoenge hum
Dekhkar behte pani ko roenge hum
Haaye tashnadahan

8
Aie "Faseel" aarahi hai yeh kiski sada
Aie mere laal uthta hai farshe azaa
Haaye zakhmi badan
