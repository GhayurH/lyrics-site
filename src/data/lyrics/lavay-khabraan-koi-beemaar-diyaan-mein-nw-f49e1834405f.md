Lavay Khabraan Koi Beemaar Diyaan Mein Thak Pai’yaan

Ajay Paishiyaan Mukiyaan Nai, Darbaar Diyaan.

Na Ghar Chon Bahar Aayi, Na Safar Bazaari Keetay.

Meri Zindagi Day Din Lokon, Na Qaid Day Vich Si Beetay.

Meri Akhiyaan Vaaqif Nai, Bazaar Diyaan.

Jiday Khaimay Kol Na Parya, Kisay Ghair Nay Aan Qur’aan Ay.

O Faasiqaan Naal Kalaam Karay, Hovay Saamnay Puttar Javaan Ay.

Ay Ghairatan Aabid Nu, Haye Maar Gai’yaan.

Mayra Baab-ul-Ilm Hai Baaba, Har Haraf Mera Shamsheer Ay.

Ghaazi Di Jalaalat Mein Haan, Meri Jurtaan Vich Shabbeer Ay.

Sajjad Vaikhin Zarbaan, Guftaar Diyaan.

Sajjad Di Akhiyaan Chon Haye, Tayn Dulday Nay Yaaqoot Ay.

Ghum Dafan Ainaan Vich Mera, Har Athroo Ik Taaboot Ay.

Shaala Qaidaan Nibh Jaavan, Ghumkhaar Diyaan.

Kar Sabr Mera Hun Bachra, Teray Layi Ay Dard Tamaam Ay.

Teri Karbal Manzil Nai Si, Tera Aa Gaya Maqtal Shaam Ay.

Teri Jang Nu Zarooorataan Nai, Talvaar Diyaan.
