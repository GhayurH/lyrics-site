Haye Ali Haye Ali Haye Ali Haye

zainab ne jis ghadi suni purdard ye sada

aayi tadap ke dar ke qareeb ye deti hui nida

khanaye khaaliq may hui kaisi jafa haye

haye ali haye ali….

hasnain ko pukara wo aaye to ye kaha

masjid may jaake dekho to ye majara hai kya

kaisi ye awaaz hai jo arsh ko hilaaye

haye ali haye ali….

hasnain daude janibe masjid barehna pa

dekha to neem hogaya ay farte murtuza

bait ke pehlu may sukhan lab pe yehi laaye

haye ali haye ali….

halqe khai gileem pe zakhmi pidar hua

leke hue rawana suye bayte fatima

aaye dare zehra pe waqt ke sataye

haye ali haye ali….

ghar may nabi ke jab hue daakhil hassan hussain

ahle harram ne halqa kiya aur kiye ye bain

zainab-o-kulsoom ke lab par ye sukhan tha

haye ali haye ali….

waqt-e-madad hai ab faqat aap iski kijiye

maula aneese gham ki khabar jald lijiye

daure masaib may wo ghut ghut ke na marjaye

haye ali haye ali….
