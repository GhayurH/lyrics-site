1
Kiday Cholay Siway Gi Dowein Bachray Mar Gay Kino Cholay Poaway Gi.
Putran Di Maut Ki Aye Dukhiya Aye Jaan Gayi Aye.
Akbar Di Zindagi Layi Adh Adh Raat Nu Uth Ke Oday Sadqay Lahaway Gi.
Kofay Shehar De Dar Te Muslim De Sir Nu Tak Ke.
Rab Jane Zainab Layi Kyun Sain Ruqiya Haye Pardah Banaway Gi.
Qaidaan Che Soch Di Rayi Putran Nu Rowan De Layi.
Ghar Ja Ke Wehray Wich Ambari Sab Tu Pehlan Do Qabraan Banaway Gi.
Abbas Bawafa Di Hamsheer Bawafa Aye.
 Akbar Aye Safran Wich Apne Athro Dak Ke Asgha Nu Khadaway Gi.
