1
Mula Ab Aa Bhi Jaein Kab Se Bula Rahe Hain
Joor-o-Sitam Jahan Ke Barhte Hi Ja Rahe Hain
Dushman Jo Aap Ke The Hum Ko Sata Rahe Hain
Hote Hain Masjidon Mein Phir Aaye Din Dhamake
Mula Batein Kab Tak Apne Uthaein Lashe
Muhlat Na In Ko Dijay Jo Khoon Baha Rahe Hain
Kya Matami-o-Ghazi Kya Haji-o-Namazi
Ilm-o-Amal Se Aari Guftar Ke Namazi
Zahra-o-Murtaza Ka Sab Dill Dukha Rahe Hain
Yeh Nazar Our Majalis, Melad Our Mahafil
Sochein Kabhi Hum Inn Se Kya Kar Rahe Hain Hasil
Ikhlas Hi Nahi Jab Phir Kya Kama Rahe Hain
Unn Se Salam Kehna Bad-e-Saba Hamara
Aye Baqi Rehne Walo Hum Ko Na Bhool Jana
Tum Intezar Karna Ab Hum Tu Ja Rahe Hain
Aashor Ho Ya Chehlum Jab Bhi Howa Dhamaka
Ahl-e-Azza Ko Shehar-e-Qaid Mein Hum Ne Dekha
Kis Hosle Se Apne Lashe Utha Rahe Hain
Inam Bhi Mile Ga Shabash Bhi Mile Gi
Tum Karna:SIBT-E-JAFAR: Apna Mohasba Bhi
Tum Jin S Ho Mukhatib Woh Jald Aa Rahe Hain
