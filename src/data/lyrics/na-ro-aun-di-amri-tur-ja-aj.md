Na Ro Aun Di Ambree tur ja aj Shaam nu Baaj Rida Dai
Na Tak Enj Nehar Dai Paasai, Nayi Ohna veer Abbas ai taang muka dai

1
Aj Shaamiyan day ne pathraan de nazraanay
Tu Bharjayiyaan nal turna days beganay
Zainab tu perda daar Ay, kive vanj se wich bazaraan baaj rida de

2
Mun paak fiza dai holay sain luka lai
Lah Sir tun maa da burkha raseeyan paa lai
Rakhi hosla Zahra jayee, tenu turna payna’ay paidal naal sipha dai

3
Barbaad Gharan di kerdi pehrai daari
Akber da na murna ban’naya beemari
Payi kalaiya bain bain rovai, hai Aurn kai nayee koi sunda dukh Sughra dai

4
Putran nal rehnda maa tai piyo da naa ai
Koi Kendha nahi aj Zainab sadee maa ai
Vich Kerbobala dai lut layee, Kalma gohwaan nai Zainab dee aulaad ai

5
Aj sooraj nai vi chad chadaiya sharmona
Paiya Zainab nu sir nangaiya shaam’ch jana
Ghazi teri murshad zaadi, bazaran dai vich rul gayee teray baad Ay
