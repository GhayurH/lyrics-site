Haye wo bekasi

maqtal se aaya laashaye qasim

bhai hassan ki yaad satane lagi

zainab ne roke laashaye qasim pe ki buka

wa hassan sabz qaba be khata qasima

kal tera jigar tukdo may bata tha

aaj tera qasim tukdo may bat gaya

wa hassan…

dunya ne tujhko chain se rehne diya

tujhko zehr diya ye sitam khatm na hua

ye zulm chalte chalte qasim tak aa gaya

wa hassan…

ummat ne tere saath bada zulm ye kiya

tukde hua kaleja tera wa musibata

maqtal may tera qasim tukdo may batgaya

wa hassan…

tha chand fatema ka sitam ki ghataon may

ghar se chala janaza to teero ki chaon may

qasim ka laasha dhoop may pamaal hogaya

wa hassan…

tujhpar salaam ay mere mazloom-tar aqi

qasim ki tarbiyat may kamo koi na rakhi

akbar se pehle sheh pe qurban hogaya

wa hassan…

lekin tujhe kafan to mayassar tha bhai jaan

qasim to be-kafan hi gaya chor kar jahan

kyun-kar na mere lab pe har dam ho ye buka

wa hassan…

izne wigha hussain se jab mil nahi saka

taveez tera saath may laaya wo mehlaqa

dil thaam kar hussain ne be-saakhta kaha

wa hassan…

paiwast jitne teer thay taboot may tere

utne hi zakhm qasim e dilgeer ko lage

farwa ke lab pe aaya is gham se marsiya

wa hassan…

zainab ne roke mazhar o farhan ye kaha

bhai hassan ka daaghe judaai hara hua

ahle haram ne laashaye qasim pe ki buka

wa hassan…
