Chokhat-e-Shabir Par Sajda Mera Sajday Mein Hai.

1
Jis Jagah Par Aakhri Sajda Kiya Shabir Ne.
Zindagi Ab Tak Wahan Par Ba-Khuda Sajday Mein Hai.
Syeda Ki Pak Chokhat Par Tum Aa kar Dekh Lo.
Ismatein Jhuki Hain Har So Our Haya Sajday Mein Hai.
Kar Diya Shabir Ne Aisa Karam Oss Par Keh Bas.
Karbala Ki Khak Our Khud Karbala Sajday Mein Hai.

2
Karbala Mein Youn Nibhai Thi Wafa Abbas Ne.
Chodah Sadiyon Bad Bhi Ab Tak Wafa Sajday Mein Hai.
Bugz-e-Haider Ho Agar Dil Mein Tu Hai Sajda Haram
Ishq-e-Haider Ho Jo Dil Mein Tu Maza Sajday Mein Hai.
Yeh Deyar-e-Karbala Hai Yaan Adab Se Chal Zara.
Har Nabi Raqib Yahan Par Ja Ba-Ja Sajday Mein Hai.
