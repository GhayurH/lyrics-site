Rooh Mein Karbala Ki Aamad Hai.

1
Mujh Ko Mehsos Ho Rahe Hain Hussain.
Aaj Ki Raat Jagna Hai Mujhe.
Meri Aankhon Mein So Rahe Hain Hussain.
Hain Yehi Loag Tu Khuda Walay.
Mustafa Walay Murtaza Walay.
Mere Waris Hain Karbala Walay.

2
Tum Kabhi Ja Ke Karbala Dekho Kya Ataein Wahan Pe Hoti Hain.
Dil Ko Kitna Sakon Milta Hai Rad Balaein Wahan Pe Hoti Hain.
Kam Kartay Hain Anbiya Walay Mustafa Walay Murtaza Walay.
Har Bala Ko Shikast De Dena Our Wabaon Ko Bhi Hila Dena.
Gar Shifa Chahiye Musalmano Aa Ke Oss Dar Pe Sar Jhuka Dena.
Jin Ke Jholay Bhi Hain Shifa Walay Mustafa Walay Murtaza Walay.

3
Jab Kabhi Mushkilon Mein Aate Hain Bargah-e-Hussain Jate Hain.
Pak Ho Kar Alam Ko Chotay Hain Pak Ho Kar Alam Uthatay Hain.
Loag Jitnay Bhi Hain Wafa Walay Mustafa Walay Murtaza Walay.
Shah Ka Matami Ho Mushkil Mein Hum Ne Aisa Kabhi Nahi Dekha.
Door Oss Ki Balaein Hoti Hain Hai Yaqeen Oss Ko Fatima Zahra.
Hath Uthati Hain Jab Dua Walay Mustafa Walay Murtaza Walay.

4
Yeh Wasiyat Thi Shah-e-Wala Ki Mere Zair Ko Mehmaan Karna.
Aaj Bhi Khidmaton Mein Hain Masroof Ja Ke Khud Dekh Lo Khuda Hai Gawah.
Loag Jitnay Hain Nainawa Walay Mustafa Walay Murtaza Walay.
Jab Muharram Ke Roz Aate Hain Yaad Pyason Ki Hum Manatay Hain.
Chor Kar Karbala Ko Shah-e-Ummam Hind Ki Sar Zameen Pe Aate Hain.
Farsh Bich Jate Hain Azza Walay Mustafa Walay Murtaza Walay.

5
Qafila Ja Raha Hai Kofay Ko Yeh Manadi Sadaein Deta Hai.
Aagay Oss Qafilay Keh Ik Beemar Bas Issi Aik Gham Mein Rota Hai.
Be Rida Ho Gaye Rida Walay Mustafa Walay Murtaza Walay.
Aisa Pursa Howa Hai Majlis Mein Shor-e-Giriya Hai Our Hain Aahein.
Jo:WAJHI-E-HASAN: Ne Paish Kiye Meri Bibi Qabool Farmaein.
Shair Jitnay Bhi Hain Raza Walay Mustafa Walay Murtaza Walay.
