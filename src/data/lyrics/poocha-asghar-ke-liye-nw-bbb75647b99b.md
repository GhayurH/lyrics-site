poocha asghar ke liye shah ne kya maangte hai

roke bano ne kaha aabe bakha maangte hai

poocha asghar ke….

hiddate dasht se muztar hai faqat saaye ko

jaane zehra ka ye damane khaba maagte hai

poocha asghar ke….

apne cheh maah ke bache ke liye aada se

pani ek boondh shahe karbobala maangte hai

poocha asghar ke….

baskhde ummate jad tujhko mere khoon ki qasam

khaake payka ali asghar ye dua maangte hai

poocha asghar ke….

ahlebayte nabawi shaam ke bazaaro may

sar chupane ke liye ek rida maangte hai

poocha asghar ke…
