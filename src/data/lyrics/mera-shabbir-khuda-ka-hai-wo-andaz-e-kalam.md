Mera shabbir khuda ka hay who andaaz-e-kalam
Jahaan nakaam hain tashbeeh kay qareenay bhi tamaam

1
1) Husn iss naam may kitna hai may kia paaish karoon
Kaisi tarteeb may lafzoon ki ada paish karoon
Iski tauseef to hai iss kay musawwer ka hee kaam

2
2) aisa hai dard, yateemoon kay har aik dard liya
Seena zakhmoon say bhara, zakhm koi bhi na bhara
Sabr kay arsh-e-muazzam, terri azmat ko salam

3
3) jalwa-e-toor bani zakhmi jabeen-e-akbar
Chashm-e-pur aaab hai maanand-e-chasma-e-kauthar
Rukh pay payghaam-e-khun-e-asghar hai insaaanoon kay naam

4
4) deekho teeroon kay falak par hai zabeeh-e-akbar
Qaaba qausain key manzil pay ho jaisay sarwar[pbuh]
Jahaan haidar[as] nahi parday may hai zainab ka kalaam

5
5) meeray maalik may musaafir hoon lutta betha hoon
Haat khaali teeray dar par main ab aata hoon
Ab to ro ro kay sakina bhi gayey sooy-e-khayaam

6
6) ayey awaaz keh itni na inkisaari kar
May to chahta hoon meray paas yaan mukhtaari kar
Ye to kaafir hain bhaala jaanein keh kia terra maqaam

7
7) may hoon ahad tu munfirid hai payambar merra
May musawwer hoon tu logoon may tasawwur merra
May hooon ghafaar to bakhshish ka sabab terra hee naaam

8
8) barri qayyem hai yeh bhi sabr-o salaat terii
May misl maah to milal khauf may bardaasht terri
Terri har nafs-e-mutmayenna adda ko hai salaam

9
9) tu hai be-aas meeri aas ay merray shabbir
Ye merray haidari aasai bhi terray daman geer
Terray mazloom azadaroon par doozakh hai haraam
