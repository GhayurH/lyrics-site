1
Bhai Shabbir Ka Haider Ka Pisar Aata Hai Hasan Sadiq.
Jang Karne Bani Hashim Ka Qamar Aata Hai.
Aisa Tanha Tu Bahadur Koi Dekha Na Suna.
Baat Mein Apni Jo Lashkar Sa Nazar Aata Hai.

2
Yaad Aati Hai Sakina Ki Barey Karb Ke Sath.
Naam Abbas Ka Honton Pe Agar Aata Hai.
Khoon Bhara Aaya Alam Ro Ke Yeh Zainab Ne Kaha.
Bhai Tum Aaye Nahi Munh Ko Jigar Aata Hai.

3
Door Se Aata Alam Dekh Ke Bachi Boli.
Dekho Darya Se Chacha Laut Ke Ghar Aata Hai.
Sharam Se Rukta Nahi Naizay Pe Abbas Ka Sar.
Be Rida Kunba Jo Ghazi Ko Nazar Aata Hai.

4
Boli Zainab Keh Madad Aa Ke Karo Aye Bhai.
Aao Abbas Keh Zindan Ka Sar Aata Hai.
Noha Parhta Hai:HASAN SADIQ E: Pur Gham Rehan.
Hai Dua Ghazi Ki Oss Ko Yeh Hunar Aata Hai.
