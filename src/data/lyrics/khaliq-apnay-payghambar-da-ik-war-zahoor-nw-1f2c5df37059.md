Khaliq upnay payghambar da, Aik war’ zahoor wala kar Meinkou Akbar puthar ata kar

– Mehruma ma da puthar haan mein, mendi ay munzoor dua kar

– Menda nana turgaya duniya toon, uday roop’ da jald’ zahoor’ howay

Meinku day farizand’ rasool jaya, menda bachira ain’ darood howay

Sada haq’ koi dayvay na dayvay, tu upna kumas’ ada kar

– Ay maliq deak Husain han mein, va da har’ haal’ niba sak da

Mazlanday sohnay bachray da mein tehtul han’ak wala sak da

Piu puthar dey lash kewain chenda, karbal tuin aap niga kar

– Mendi behn dey hasirat hay khaliq’ ookon burkiya day vich palandi

Mein zimay wari chana haan, oodi lash koon aap sambhalan dey

Oodi amad naal jahan’ ootay mendi soorkuru karbobala kar

-Rat rovan walay shaam dai wich, menda Aabid putar beemar sahi,

Akbar bas saal athara da, menday wehray dah mehmaan sahi

Ohday karbal day mosam tai-en, Sajaad koo aan bhira kar

-Hovay mojza pak shabab honda, qainath wich pak quran wangoo

Oda jigar’ namaz jaya hoya, oda kad hovay azan wangoo

Oda toar day baray ai khaliq, mendi bhain day naal sila kar
