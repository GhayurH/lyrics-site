Farse ke Zeen say Beshak Utar gaye hain Hussain

1
Zameen Dondh rahi hay- Kidhar gaye hain Hussain

2
1) Karay Ga Shimir ka Khangar To Sir ko juda
Wagarna lasha e Akbar pay mar gaye hain Hussain

3
2) Sar e Bureda katay honth khon cheray per
Ajeeb hal may Sheern kay ghar gaye hain Hussain

4
3) May milnay sham kay zindan may - ayonga tujh say
Ye baat akhari beti say kar gaye hain Hussain

5
4) Khayeen hay Qasim o Abbas or Kahyee Akber
Khan khan teray Tukray Bekar gaye hain Hussain

6
5) Qaza kay baad bhi bahi - Behen kay saat raha
Jidhar Jidhar gaye Zainab Udar gaye hain Hussain

7
6) Kesi Nay mara hay pather behan kay kia Akber
Jo kartay katray tilawat Tehar gaye hain Hussain
