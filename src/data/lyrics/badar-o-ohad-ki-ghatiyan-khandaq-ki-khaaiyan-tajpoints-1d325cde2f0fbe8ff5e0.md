1
Badar-o-Ohad Ki Ghatiyan Khandaq Ki Khaaiyan.
Kaam Aa Rahi Hain Taig-e-Ali Ki Kamaiyaan.
Kar-e-Khudai Qabza-e-Qudrat Mein Hai Magar.
Sajday Ki Thokaron Mein Hain Jhuti Khudaiyaan.

2
Musa Assa Ko Choom Ke Kehta Tha Bar Bar.
Seekhi Kahan Se Tu Ne Yeh Mojiz Numaiyaan.
Maidaan Se Ja-Namaz Talak Aik Sa Mizaj.
Woh Halat-e-Raku Woh Hajat Rawaiyaan.

3
Roke Ga Kon War Teri Zulfiqar Ke.
Thamay Ga Kon Dast-e-Khuda Ki Kalaiyaan.
Mehway-e-Safar Thi Nasalon Mein Taig-e-Ali Ki Kaat.
Rah Waar Kar Raha Tha Safoon Ki Safaiyaan.

4
Manzar Banaya Hath Utha Kar Rasool Ne.
Phir Sonp Deen Ali Ko Sabhi Rahnumaiyaan.
 Akbar Koi Bhi Takht Pe Qabiz Rahe Magar.
Islam Pe Ali Ki Hain Farmarawaiyaan.
