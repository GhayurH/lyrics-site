Hasnain pay yateemi taqdeer lay ke aayi

1
Mola Ali ka ghaam main maghmoon khudayi

2
1) Talwaar-o-zehar dono baba kai sar pay baham
Baiton nai yeh virasat aik aik ker kay payee

3
2) Woh chal basa yateemon baivaon ka sahara
Karta tha jo hamesha dushman say bhalayee

4
3) Kaisa sitam huwa hay baad-e-Ali Khudaya
Ummat hai baitiyon ko bazaar-e-shaam layee

5
4) Hy kaamp utha tha Kufa Zainab ki aah buka sai
Jinno bashar malaaik day tay hain sab duhaee

6
5) Aghaaz ho gaiya hai Karbal ka ya khudaya
Sajday main jab shaki nay talwaar hay chalayee
