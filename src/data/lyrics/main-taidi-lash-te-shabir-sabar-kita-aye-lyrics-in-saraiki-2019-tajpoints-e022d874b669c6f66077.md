1
Main Taidi Lash Te Shabir Sabar Kita Aye.
Jadoon Ummat Ne Bhira Tehh Te Jabar Kita Aye.
Dhup Te Main Lash Taidi Wekh Nayi Saghdi Veera.
Mang Dua Hun Taidi Hamsheer Dey Muk Jawan Saah.

2
Zalimaa Ne Maida Veran Aye Ghar Kita Aye.
Haye Mitiyaan Ne Taidi Dhi Nu Neendar Nayi Aoundi.
Sar Taida Naizay Te Chup Chaap Hai Takdi Rehndi.
Haal Aye Taidi Sakina Da Shimar Kita Aye.

3
Le Ke Mashkiza Alam Nehar Te Ghazi Chaliya.
Jeh Tey Ha Manr Sakoon Ja Ke Na Wapis Waliya.
Haye Jaindi Aas Te Zainab Ne Safar Kita Aye.
Kya Dasaan Taikoon Jadaan Sham-e-Ghareebaan Aayi.

4
Naal Naizay De Maiday Sir Toon Rida Leh Gayi Si.
Haye Iss Haal Ne Zakhmi Aye Jigar Kita Aye.
Ki Main Aakhaan Gi Tadi Sughra Koon Yasrab Ja Ke.
Haal Barchi Da Na Sunr Ke Oh Dukhi Mar Jaway.

5
Taida Beemar Ne Bardasht Hijar Kita Aye.
Haye Afraz Ayho Keh Ke Tur Sain Maidi.
Wadah Ghar Ghar Che Hosi Veer Azadari Taidi.
Nanay De Deen Toon Qurban Tu Sar Kita Aye.
