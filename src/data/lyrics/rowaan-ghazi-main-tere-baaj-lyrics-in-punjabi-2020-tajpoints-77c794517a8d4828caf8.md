Rowaan Ghazi Main Tere Baaj Bhira Dewaan Pehrey Main Karbala Veera.
Tak Naizay Te Lehranday Ne Meri Pak Rida Veera.
Baal Pyasay Ronday Ne.
Pani Aaya Na Tu Ghazi Ronday Ne Na Sonday Ne.
Sa Okhay Ne Masooma De Pani Aan Piya Veera.
Wekh Tamachay Khandi Aye.
Baba Baba Kardi Ghazi Gash Te Gash Payi Khandi Aye.
Pe Neel Aiday Rukhsaraan Te Ainu Aan Bacha Veera.
Koray Mar Turanday Ne.
Roway Rat Akhiyaan Choon Ghazi Na Beemar Baithanday Ne.
Sir Na Chukda Sajjad Mera Gal Choon Tauq Laha Veera.
Veran Mar Mukaye Ne.
Bazu La Ke Tere Ghazi Mere Maan Mukaye Ne.
Tere Naa De Tanay Denday Ne Wekhaan Nehar De Rah Veera.
Aal Abdullah Rondi Aye.
Sari Aal Abba V Ghazi Tere Baaj Payi Rondi Aye.
Al-Hafiz Naal Hafeez Paran Tur Payi Sham De Rah Veera.
