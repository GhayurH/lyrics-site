Ay Shaam Ke Logon Mein Binte Ali Hoon
Mein Waris-e-Tatheer Hoon Namoos-e-Nabi Hoon

1
Bazaar Se Darbaar Mein Ley Aaey Ho Mujhko
Chahoon To Jala Sakti Hoon Khutbon Se Mein Tumko
Sabir Ki Bahen Hoon Jo Mein Khamosh Khari Hoon

2
Afsos Kaneezi Mein Usay Maang Rahay Ho
Tum Jiskay Ghulamon Ki Ghulami Mein Rahay Ho
Yeh Zulm Na Hoga Kabhi Mein Zinda Abhi Hoon

3
Zinda Tha To Dar Dar Ke Hamesha Hee Laray Ho
Ab Sar Hai To Honton Pe Chari Maar Rahay Ho
Tum Kitnay Bahadur Ho Mein Yeh Dekh Rahi Hoon

4
Tum Apne Payambar Ko Agar Dekhna Chaho
Naizay Pe Jo Yeh Sar Hai Isay Ghor Se Dekho
Ham Shakl-e-Payamabar Hai Yeh Mein Iski Phupi Hoon

5
Aur Iss Se Kathan Waqt Kya Hoga Meyrai Mabood
Fizza Ke Tarafdaar Hain Darbaar Mein Mojood
Mein Pusht Pe Fizza Ke Khari Dekh Rahi Hoon

6
Zehra Ko Bhi Darbaar Mein Tum Ne Tha Bulaya
Darbaar Mein Mein Bhi Hoon Magar Farq Hai Itna
Parday Mein Thi Voh Aur Mein Bey Parda Khari Hoon

7
Zanjeeron Mein Jakra Jo Yeh Beemar Khara Hai
Sajda Isay Kaabay Ki Faseelon Ne Kiya Hai
Mansab Mein Bara Voh Hai Mein Rishtay Mein Bari Hoon

8
Honay Lagi Darbaar Mein Mushtar Ka Azaanein
Rehan Idhar Naam-e-Nabi Goonja Fiza Mein
Bibi Ne Kaha Fakhr Se Mein Jeet Gaee Hoon
