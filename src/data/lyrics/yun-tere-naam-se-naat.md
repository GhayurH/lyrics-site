Yun Tere Naam Se Naat
Youn Tere Naam Se Iss Dil Ne Muhabbat Ki Hai
Naat Likhe Hoye Kagaz Ki Bhi Izzat Ki Hai
Raat Bhar Jaag Ke Ashar Likhe Hain Main Ne
Yani Kagaz Pe Tahajud Ki Ibadat Ki Hai
Khal-o-Khad Naam Ke Harfoon Se Tarashey Unn Ke
Ism-e-Ahmad Mein Muhammad Ki Ziyarat Ki Hai
Hum Ke Takheer-e-Zamana Ke Sabab Door Rahe
Hum Ne Hathoon Pe Nahi Zikar Pe Baiyat Ki Hai
Aayatein Chalti Rahein Aap Ke Pichey Pichey
Aap Ke Qadmo Se Quran Ne Hijrat Ki Hai
Gard Ne Midhat-e-Aflak Ki Hasrat Ki Hai
Apni Qamat Se Bari Baat Ki Niyat Ki Hai
Naat Goyi Jo Agar Ki Hai Kissi Ne AKBAR
Hasb-e-Taufeeq Nahi Hasb-e-Inayat Ki Hai
