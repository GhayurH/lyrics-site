Sughra Nu Maar Gaye Ne Akbar De Wichoray.
Mur Ke Tu Veer Aa Ja Haye Sohnri Shakal Wakha Ja Sah Reh Gaye Ne Thoray.

1
Tere Naa De Deway Balaan Taan Aye Sanwaan Chalan.
Deway Bhujhan Te Meri Sanwaan Ruk Ruk Jawan.
Chaldi Taiz Haneri Deway Nayi Veer Bujhandi Meri Sanwaan Choon Toray.
Muk Gaye Nishan Akbar Tere Pairaan Walay.

2
Was Gaye Ne Kamriya Di Kandaan Te V Jalay.
Rainda Sirf Hanera Ghar Di Veerani Veera Mainu Khanr Nu Doray.
Tere Taun Wadh Ke Kehra Khabran Rakhda Aye Nana.
Taiyon Main Aakhdi Aan Babay Mur Nayi Aanra.

3
Aaya Khuwab Daronra Main Wekhay Dorday Nana Ik Lash Te Ghorey.
 Akbar Zamana Hoya Akbar Yasrab Chadiya.
Sanwaan Te Muk Gayiyaan Ne Bhain Da Dukh Nayi Mukya.
Reh Gayi Bhain Banandi Haye Sehra Gana Mehndi Nalay Shagna De Joray.
