Zainab se pehle Zehra darbar may gayee

1
Ummat ne kahan lakay khara kardia mujhieh beti Rasool ki yeh sochti rahi

2
1) Hasnain sharifain bhi rotay rahay kharay,
Darbar mein maa chunti rahi sanad ke tukray sub Log hans diye,
Zahra ki ghasibon netazeem kia khoob ki.

3
2) Haye baad nabi Zahra ko ronay nehi dia
Jaltay huey darwazay se pehlo kia zakhmi
Ummat ne kia kiya kaisi Batool pe aayi hai bebasi

4
3) Yarana e Mohammad ne hai Zahra ko dukh dia
Loota hai musalmano ne haq aal e aba ka
Yaad aayi karbala apnay pisr ko logon beti na rosaki

5
4) Jibreel jis bibi ke dar ka khayat hai
Mazlooma o mehroma ke shohar ke haath mein sub kainaat hai
Usnay sitm bhi jhelay aur baddua na ki

6
5) Jo Bibi mubhaila mein gayi to thi shaan se
Ghairon ne ussay dekh kaha sir bhi to dhanpa,jageer bhi di thi
Ummat ne bhi woh Akhtar jageer loot li
