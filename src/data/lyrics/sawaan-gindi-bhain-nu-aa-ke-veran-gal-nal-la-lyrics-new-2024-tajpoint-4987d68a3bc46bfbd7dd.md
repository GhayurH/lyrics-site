1
Sawaan Gindi Bhain Nu Aa Ke Veran Gal Nal La
Jandiyaan Toon Veran Mur Naa Takiya Bhain Nu Main Takdi Reh Gayi Rah.
Mere Piyo Taqdeeraan Badliyan Ne Jidda Rahib Khas Gawah Baniya.
Saday Lafz Quran De Lafz Banay Ohda Shahid Aap Khuda Baniya.
Laindiyaan Sab Bhaina Mere Wal Kyun Wekh Ke Haye Apne Veer Chupa.
Chan Veran Roz Musalay Te Teri Yaad Che Athro Digday Ne.
Sare Rukan Namaz De V Veera Mainu Akbar, Akbar Puchday Ne.
Sukdiyan Nayi Akhiyaan Wich Sajday De Naa Tera Jadon Rab De Naal Liya.
Teri Roz Ziyarat Karni Aan Mainu Dain Dilasay Aanda Aye.
Tera Dukh Zahra Nal Sanjha Aye Mainu Aakh Rasool Aye Ronda Aye.
Takdi Rayi Main Veera Aanday Janday Qafilay Nayi Murya Veer Mera.
Ik Khuwab Main Akbar Wekhiya Aye Tainu Kiwein Veer Sunawaan Main.
Hath Babay Da Ik Barchi Aye Kiwein Manzar Veer Banawaan Main.
Aana Nayi Naa Aawein Jondiyaan Mere Veer Taun Ik Khat Mainu Khair Da Pa.
Oss Welay Karbal Wich Najfi Rang Asmana Da Badal Gaya.
Jadon Naa Sughra Da Le Maula Akbar Di Lash De Kool Gaya.
Kiwein Main Chukna Aye Sughra Tere Veer Nu Koi Deway Aan Chuka.
