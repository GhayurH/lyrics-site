Sajjad Nu Denday Ne Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.
Sajjad Nu Denday Ne Be Jurm Sazawaan.

1
Dukh Seh Ke V Syed Ne Mangiyaan Ne Duawaan.
Beemar Hai Muddat Toon Kiwein Turya Khuda Jane.
Nayi Tauq Turan Denday Tang Kita Hai Zakhma Ne.
Pairaan Choon Lahu Wagda Pathreliyaan Rahwaan.

2
Kar Taras Shimar Zalim Meri Bhain Sakina Te.
Aynj Lok Nayi Karday Kadi Zulm Yateema Te.
Kiwein Teriyaan Unglaan Choon Aiday Waal Chorawaan.
Nayi Khon Ajay Rukya Kana Choon Sakina Da.

3
Aakhay Ladli Babal Di Darbar Laeena Da.
Daso Nam-e-Khuda Aiday Kiwein Zakhm Lokawaan.
Chuk Lasha Sakina Da Har Moor Te Rukda Aye.
Sab Jandiyaan Rahiyaan Taun Beemar Aye Puchda Aye.

4
Kehri Jah Te Musalmano Aidi Qabar Banawaan.
Sajjad Di Ghurbat Te Bazar V Ronda Aye.
Tak Haal Yateema Da Sardar V Ronda Aye.
Gash Aanday Ne Syed Nu Ruk Jandiyaan Sawaan.
