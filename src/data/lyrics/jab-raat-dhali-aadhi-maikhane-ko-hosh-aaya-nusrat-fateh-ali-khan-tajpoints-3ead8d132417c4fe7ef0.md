Jab Raat Dhali Aadhi Maikhane Ko Hosh Aaya Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan.
Angrai Li Botal Ne Paimane Ko Hosh Aaya.

1
Utha Jo Naqab Unka Dewane Ko Hosh Aaya.
Jab Shama Hoi Roshan Parwane Ko Hosh Aaya.
Phir Dard Utha Dill Mein Phir Yaad Teri Ayi.
Phir Teri Mohabbat Ke Afsane Ko Hosh Aaya.

2
Inn Mast Nigahoon Ne Girte Ko Sanbhala Hai.
Saghar Ke Sahare Se Mastane Ko Hosh Aaya.
Woh Dekho Fana Dekho Jaam Aa Gay Gardish Mein.
Woh Mast Nazar Uthi Maikhane Ko Hosh Aaya.
