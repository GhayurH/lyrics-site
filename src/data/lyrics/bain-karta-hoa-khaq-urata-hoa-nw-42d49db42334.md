Bain kerta howa, Khak urata howa, tashna lab berida, lo chala khafla
Sun kay shulay fughan, aik kuhraam hai, barsaray Karbala, lo chala khafla

Rasme pardagari, jiss kay ghar say chali, Shahzadi wohi berida hogaye
Mar na jaye kahin, gham say bintay Ali, haye ab hoga kiya lo chala khafl

Apni surat dikha du mujhay meri jan, phir Khuda Janay Aana ho ya kay na ho
Aao Akbar utho, hum ko ruksat kero, maa nay roo kay kaha lo chala khafla

Sani e Fatima, binte Sheer e Khuda, kesay bazar main jaye gi beyrida
Kesay tey hoga woh, Sham ka raasta, Ro rahi hai fiza lo chala khafla

Piyar say jis ko pa, la tha Shabbir nay, Usko jakra gaya touq o Zanjeer say
Rah pur khaar par, Paa barahna safar, kesay hoga bhala lo chala khafla

Rakh khemay howay, sab asasa luta, besahara payambar ka kunba howa
Sham e ghurbat kay khay, di chalay Sham ko, lay chalay ashqiya lo chala khafla

Hain Nabi zadian, sar khulay bay rida, kiya ghazab hogaya kiya sitam hogaya
Bay kajawa sawa,ri aur aal e abba, shoor e matam utha lo chala khafla

Char janib say yeh, arahi hai sada, alwida alwida alwida alwida
Apnay piyaron kay la, shon say hokay juda, khon rota howa lo chala khafla
