1
Shabbir Se Sekhe Koi Islam Bachana Hasan Sadiq.
Aata Hai Jisay Naizay Pe Quran Sunana.
Shabeer Ne ALLAH Ki Har Baat Hai Mani.
ALLAH Ne Shabeer Ki Har Baat Ko Mana.

2
Tujh Ko Na Diya Roney Jo Kal Ahl-e-Sitam Ne.
Zainab Tere Bhai Ko Yeh Rota Hai Zamana.
Yaad Aata Hai Woh Manzar Shabeer Ka Maqtal Mein.
Teeron Bharay Seene Pe Sakina Ko Sulana.

3
Yeh Soch Ke Asghar Ne Liya Teer Galay Par.
Tha Mohsin-e-Islam Ko Teeron Se Bachana.
Asghar Ki Lehad Khod Ke Shabeer Yeh Bolay.
Aye Qabar Mere Bachay Ki Gardan Na Dikhana.

4
Tehzeeb Hai Islam Ki Chodah So Baras Se.
Gham Aal-e-Muhammad Ka Muharram Mein Manana.
Zahra Ki Sada Aati Hai Yeh Bazm-e-Azza Mein.
 Rehan Yeh Noha Sar-e-Mehshar Bhi Sunana.
