Bait-e-Ali Raza Mein Taqi Ka Zahoor Hai.
Khushbo Howa Mein Our Fizaon Mein Noor Hai.

1
Yeh Tifal-e-Nu Imam-e-Raza Ka Woh Tifal Hai.
Bachpan Mein Hi Kitab-e-Khuda Par Uboor Hai.
Aagosh Mein Sakina Ki Hai Aisa Mahtab.
Quran-e-Marifat Mein Jo Bain Al-Stoor Hai.

2
Yeh Bhi Hai Khandan-e-Risalat Ka Ik Charag.
Har Rijs Se Misal-e-Ali Yeh Bhi Door Hai.
Chothay Baras Mein Sar Pe Imamat Ka Taj Tha.
Sabit Howa Keh Noor Beharhaal Noor Hai.

3
Behlool Ho Gaya Koi Salman Ban Gaya.
Aal-e-Nabi Ki Zaat Ka Jis Ko Shaoor Hai.
Inn Ke Laqab Najeeb, Taqi, Murtaza Bhi They.
Jawad Woh Laqab Hai Jo Shan-e-Ghafoor Hai.

4
Narmi Gunahgaron Pe Rab Ki Hai Ik Sift.
Aousaf Mein Taqi Ke Bhi Shamil Saboor Hai.
 Rehan Madah Khuwaan Hoon Imam Taqi Ka Main.
Yeh Woh Maqam Hai Jahan Jaiz Garor Hai.
