1
Sakina Utho Meri Jaan Utho Rehai Mili Chalo Ghar Chalein.
Yeh Karti Thi Maa Lahad Par Buqa Meri Ladli Chalo Ghar Chalein.
Yahan Se Chalo Chalein Karbala Wahin Par Tumhein Milein Gaye Chacha.
Sitam Jo Howay Batana Unhein Dikhana Unhein Yeh Kurta Jala.

2
Chacha Jan Ko Dikhana Zara Rida Khoon Bhari Chalo Ghar Chalein.
Batana Unhein Sabhi Sakhtiyaan Mili Kis Qadar Tujhe Ghurkiyaan.
Hamari Rida Luti Kis Tarha Chini Kis Tarha Teri Baliyaan.
Batana Unhein Bhujji Kis Tarha Teri Tishnagi Chalo Ghar Chalein.

3
Padar Se Milo Tu Dill Joyi Ho Batana Unhein Bohat Royi Ho.
Qasam Lo Agar Tumhare Bina Sakina Kabhi Kahin Soyi Ho.
Lahad Baap Ki Mile Jab Tujhe Tu Kehna Yehi Chalo Ghar Chalein.
Lahad Jab Mile Tujhe Bhai Ki Tu Kehna Osse Meri Ladli.

4
Rehai Mili Humein Qaid Se Humein Ab Nahi Azeeyat Koi.
Mere Be-Zubaan Utho Ghar Chalein Shabih-e-Nabi Chalo Ghar Chalein.
Lab-e-Alqama Tu Karna Fogaan Teri Mauj Hai Abhi Tak Rawaan.
Meri Pyas Par Howay Jo Fida Chacha Ke Mere Hain Bazu Kahan.

5
Kahan Mashk Hai Kahan Hai Alam Khan Hai Jari Chalo Ghar Chalein.
Na Ab Gham Koi Na Uftad Hai Woh Zanjeer Se Ab Aazaad Hai.
Phophi Bhi Teri Tere Sath Hai Tere Sath Hi Woh Sajjad Hai.
Meri Gham Zada Shab-e-Gham Kati Sahar Ho Chuki Chalo Ghar Chalein.

6
Laboon Par Tere Yehi Baat Thi Watan Jaein Gaye Kabhi Hum Phophi.
Tujhe Shimar Ka Nahi Khoof Ab Sataein Gaye Na Tujhe Ab Shaqi.
Nikal Qaid Se Shorow Hum Karein Nayi Zindagi Chalo Ghar Chalein.
Ghareebon Ka Yeh Luta Qafila Wida Ho Ke Phir Watan Jaye Ga.

7
Tujhe Paye Jo Sughra Teri Tu Bemari Ko Mile Ki Dawa.
Galay Mill Ke Phir Bohat Roye Gi Woh Behna Teri Chalo Ghar Chalein.
Mazar-e-Nabi Larz Jaey Ga Behan Jab Teri Kare Gi Buka.
Kahan Hain Chacha Kahan Hain Padar Kahan Hai Bata Baradar Mera.

8
Sunane Ossay Yeh Rodad-e-Gham Yehi Be-Basi Chalo Ghar Chalein.
Giya Marqad-e-Sakina Pe Gar Kare Ga Wahan Yeh Mazhar Biyaa.
Atta Umar Kar isse Daimi Yeh Irfan Hai Tera Noha Khuwaan.
Wahan Ja Ke Phir Kahe Ga Nahi Mera Dill Kabhi Chalo Ghar Chalein.
