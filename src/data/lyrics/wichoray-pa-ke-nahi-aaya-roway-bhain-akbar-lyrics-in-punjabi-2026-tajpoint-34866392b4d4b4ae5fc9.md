1
Wichoray Pa Ke Nahi Aaya Roway Bhain Akbar.
Ais Khali Ghar Di Veerani Mere Sundi Aye Wain Akbar.
Ik Roz Sawal Aye Lokaan Da Mere Dil Nu Khon Rawanda Aye.
Jadon Puchda Aye Koi Mainu Nayi Aaya Lain Akbar.

2
Main Osay Welay Mar Jandi Tere Aan Di Aas Bachaya Aye.
Toon Naal Nayi Jana Sughra Jadon Aaya Kain Akbar.
Quran Jehray Ghar Te Aaya Oss Ghar De Chanan Ronday Ne.
Teri Fair Ziyarat Kiran Layi Sajday Wich Rain Akbar.

3
Meri Maa V Nayi Ais Dunya Te Jonday V Sare Tur Gaye Ne.
Yasrab Diyaan Hun Mastoraan V Mere Kool Na Bain Akbar.
 Najfi, Sughra Da Wasta Pa Aj Mang Le Odhay Veer Kolon.
Dunya Te Bhaina Veraan De Sadmay Na Sehn Akbar.
