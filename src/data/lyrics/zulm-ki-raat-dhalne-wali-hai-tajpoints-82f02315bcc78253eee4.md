1
Zulm Ki Raat Dhalne Wali Hai Waris-e-Abu Turab Aaye Ga.
Qatal Ho Jaein Gaye Adoy-e-Ali Zalimoon Par Azab Aaye Ga.
Al-Ajal Ki Sadaein Uthein Gi Qalab-e-Momin Pe Noor Barse Ga.
Fikar-e-Sufyan Munh Chupaye Gi Aadmi Roshni Ko Tarse Ga.
Ahl-e-Emaan Ki Rehbari Ke Liye Quaid-e-Inqelab Aaye Ga.
Panjtan Pak Jin Ke Rehbar Hain Inn Pe Hujat Imam Barah Hain.
Har Qadam Par Jo Hum Ko Parti Hai Woh Zarorat Imam Barah Hain.
Jald Hum Sab Ki Rahnumai Ko Barwaan Aaftab Aaye Ga.
Dushmanan-e-Kitab-o-Itrat Ke Har Iraday Ko Khak Kar De Ga.
Haq-e-Sadaat Cheenne Wale Har Addo Ko Halak Kar De Ga.
Aal-e-Sufyan Munh Chupaye Gi Pasban-e-Kitab Aaye Ga.
Sara Aalam Sakoon Paye Ga Ahl-e-Eman Muskuraein Gaye.
Adal Ho Ga Jahan Mein Qayam But Takabur Ke Toot Jaein Gaye.
Khill Uthey Ga Yaqeen Ka Chehrah Deen-e-Haq Par Shabab Aaye Ga.
Unn Ke Humrah Ausiyaa Hoon Gaye Unn Ke Humrah Anbiya Hoon Gaye.
Unn Ke Lashkar Ke Jo Sipahi Hain Woh Jari Hoon Gaye Ustifa Hoon Gaye.
Sach Tu Yeh Keh Jald Hi Akbar Le Ke Woh Aab-o-Taab Aaye Ga.
