Yasrab khabar na jae yasrab khabar na jae
Akber guzar gae hai yasrab khabar na jae

1
Sughra kahi ye sunka..ro ro ke mar na jae

2
Waqt e saher se abtak jo bhi gaya na palta
Baba mere chacha se kehdo udhar na jae

3
Zehra ke gulsitan ko beghar kiya hai jaise
Ya rabb kisi ka kunba aise bikhar na jae

4
Boli Sakina maa se mai ja rahi hu amma
Bhai mera akela jungle me darr na jae

5
Zindane shaam se ye zainabpukari rokar
Bhaiyya khabar lo akar bacchi ye marr na jae

6
Ik raat ki biyahi kis tarha dil sambhale
Rone ko gham ki maari mayyat pe gir na jae

7
Itne pade hai sadme bimaar o natawan per
Abid ki aaho zaari shamo saher na jae

8
Haye sakina kehkar abbas ke alam se
Nazre jamae rakho jab tak guzar na jae

9
Shabbir ki kahani Tauqeer ki zubani
Sunkar dilon se pehron gham ka asar na jae...
