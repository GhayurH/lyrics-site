Woh Karbala ki Shaam hai

1
Jis per hai zulm o jaur ki
Har intehaa tamaam

2
1. Woh Shaam jisko kalbe pareshaan kaha gaya
Woh shaam jisko shaame gariba kaha gaya
Woh shaam hai Husain ki
Bekas behen ke naam
2. Woh shaam jiske rukh se tapakti thi bebasi
Woh shaam jisme aale nabi be rida hui
Woh shaam jispe rote
Rahe umr bhar imam
3. Woh shaam jisme lut ta raha ghar Husain ka
Kunba tadap raha tha shahe mashrakain ka
Zulmo sitam ki aag me
Jalte rahe khayaam
4.Woh sham jo ki aale Mohammed ke waaste
Khole hui thi saare musibat ke raaste
Jisko dia naseeb ne
Rone ka sirf kaam
5. Woh shaam jisme kitne muqqadar bigadh gaye
Bhai,behen se maao se bache bichadh gaye
Deti thi zindagi bhi
Jaha maut ka payam
6. Woh shaam jisme aadmi haiwaan bangaya
Aale nabi ke bugz me shaitaan bangaya
Woh zulm bhi kie ke jo
The fitratan haraam
7. Woh shaam jispe ek qayamat guzar gayi
Lamho me kitni sadiyo ki aafat guzar gayi
Woh shaam jisme zulmo
Sitam hogaya tha aam
8. Woh shaam jisme lut gaya qunba rasul ka
Lamho me khaak hogaya gulshan batul ka
Jisme koi kisi ko bhi
Karta nahi salam
9. Woh shaam jisme thokare khaati thi bibiyaa
Gurbat pe apni roti thi Zehra ki betiyaa
Jiske Naseeb me hai
Faqat aasu o ke jaam
10.Purse me har yatim ko milti thi ghudkiaa
Baandhi gayi thi baazue Zainab me rassiyaa
Jisme hua na aale
Mohammed ka ehteraan
11. Khanjar gale pe sholo ki zad per makaan the
Mehmaan karbala ke ajab mezbaan the
Khaane ka intezaam na
Paani ka intezaam
12.Irfan khune dil se yeh nauha likha gaya
Us shaam ke naseeb me rona likha gaya
Din bhar jo dekhti rahi
Piyaaso ka katle aam
