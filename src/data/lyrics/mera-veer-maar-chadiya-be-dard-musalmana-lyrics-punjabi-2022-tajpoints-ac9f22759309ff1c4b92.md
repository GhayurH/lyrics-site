Mera Veer Maar Chadiya Be Dard Musalmana.
Bazaraan Wich Manaya Chehlum Main Ohda Nana.

1
Lakh Zulm Karo Logo Bhawein Koi Saza Dewo.
Nayi Mangdi Rehaiyaan Main Mere Sir Te Rida Dewo.
Main Dhi Aan Muhammad Di Kita Kisay Haya Na.
Ayeh Keh Ke Akhiyaan Choon Piya Qaidi Rat Roway.

2
Majboor Koi Shala Mere Warga Na Howay.
Haye Lasha Sakina Da Be Kafan Hai Dafnana.
Haye Wekh Ke Sar Nangay Piya Abid Gash Khaway.
Zainab Choon Fikraan Ne Sajjad Na Mar Jaway.

3
Ohdi Kha Gaya Jawani Darbar Wich Bolana.
Be Pardah Zainab Ne Bazar Choon Langna Aye.
Rab Jane Ayeh Manzar Kiwein Abid Takna Aye.
Bara Okha Ho Gaya Aye Sajjad Choon Bachana.

4
Assi Khon-e-Muhammad Haan Zainab Farmaya Aye.
Shabeer Ne Naizay Te Quran Sunaya Aye.
Rondi Si Sun Ke Zainab Rayiyaan Hondiyaan Azana.
Khanjar De Talay Kita Jo Sajdah Oh Yaad Aye.

5
Na Maro Veer Mera Rayi Kardi Faryad Aye.
Mar Jaway Gi Sakina Shabeer Jay Rehya Na.
Darbar Sharabi Da Main Zahra Di Jayi.
Aawaz Jidi Ghar Choon Bahir Na Kadi Aayi.

6
Ohno Deen Di Baqa Layi Khutba Piya Sunana.
 Sajjad Ali Abid Nu Iss Roog Mukaya Aye.
Meri Maa Da Naa Le Ke Zalim Bulaya Aye.
Bint-e-Ali Da Rona Zalim Da Muskurana.
