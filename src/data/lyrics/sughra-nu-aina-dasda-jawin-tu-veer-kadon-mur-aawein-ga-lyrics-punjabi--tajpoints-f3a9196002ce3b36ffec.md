1
Sughra Nu Aina Dasda Jawin Tu Veer Kadon Mur Aawein Ga.
Ais Wichri Bhain Nu Veer Akbar Kadon Aan Ke Seene Lawin Ga.
Tere Aasrey Te Main Chan Veran Veeran Gharaan Wich Rehna Aye.
Tu Wadah Kar Ke Ja Mainu Satwein Nu Lain Tu Aawein Ga.

2
Teri Shadi Da Arman Bara Tere Sehrey Da Arman Bara.
Ais Aas Te Zindah Rehna Main Kadon Veran Sehra Lawin Ga.
Tera Naam Azan Hai Sughra Di Teri Deed Namaz Aye Sughra Di.
Meri Pak Ibadat Howay Gi Jadon Veran Shakal Wakhawin Ga.

3
Jay Tere Bajoon Watna Te Veran Teri Sughra Mar Jaway.
Aye Yaad Rakhein Ais Ujari Nu Tu Aa Ke Kafan Pawawein Ga.
 Zeeshan Duawaan Karda Aye Har Matami Nu Maula Akbar.
Hai Wasta Bibi Sughra Da Apni Jageer Bolawein Ga.
