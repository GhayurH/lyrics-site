Wasiyat E Zehra Mesum Abbas Lyrics
Zahra Ki Ab Jahan Se Rukhsat Hai Ya Ali.

1
Tum Se Yehi Hamari Wasiyat Hai Ya Ali.
Ya Ali Meri Maiyyat Par Mera Haq Khaney Walay Na Aaein.
Darbar Mein Teri Zahra Ko Jhutlaney Walay Na Aaein.
Ya Ali Mere Baba Har Lamha Jis Dar Ko Choma Kartay They.

2
Oss Dar Ko Jalanay Walay Our Jalwaney Walay Na Aaein.
Ya Ali Meri Hasrat Thi Tum Ko Baba Ke Mimber Par Dekhon.
Haye Teri Jagah Oss Mimber Par Aa Janey Walay Na Aaein.
Ya Ali Sab Ahl-e-Saqeefa Ko Paigham Mera Yeh De Dena.

3
Baba Ke Pak Janazay Par Na Aane Walay Na Aaein.
Ya Ali Pehlu Bhi Shikasta Hai Baloon Mein Safaidi Chayi Hai.
Zahra Ko Teri Iss Halat Mein Pohanchaney Walay Na Aaein.
Ya Ali Mere Mohsin Par Giriya Haq Tha Mera Jo Cheen Liya.

4
Ya Ali Mujhe Martay Martay Bhi Tarpaney Walay Na Aaein.
Ya Ali Zainab Ki Aankhon Mein Mujhe Khof Dikhai Deta Hai.
Gardan Mein Teri Haye Tang Rasan Pehnane Walay Na Aaein.
Ya Ali Meri Maiyyat Ko Chup Kar Tareeki Mein Dafna Dena.

5
Roney Ke Liye Mujh Par Mujh Ko Rulwaney Walay Na Aaein.
 Salman Main Bibi Ka Noha Meesum Ke Liye Kaisay Likhon.
Jab Tak Janat Mein Le Ja Kar Likhwaney Walay Na Aaein.
Iltamas-e-Sorah Fateha Bara-e-Aysal-e-Sawab Shayar-e-Ahl-e-Bait Salman Abbas Azmi.
