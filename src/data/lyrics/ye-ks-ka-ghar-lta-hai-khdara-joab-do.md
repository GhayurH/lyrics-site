Yeh kis ka ghar luta hay khudara jawab do
Kis ka Hussain kis ki hay Zahra jawab do

1
1) Kyun khoon ro raha hai baita Hussain Ka
Kis Ka Hussain Kis ki Hai Zahra jawab do

2
2) Kubra ka taj loota musalmano nai mil ker
Zainab ka burqa kis nai utara jwab do

3
3) Arshai nabi ki aal kyun piyasi hi chal basi
Abad tha nehar ka kinara jawab do
