Utha koi janaaza phir Fatima a.s. kay ghar say

dunya tarap rahee hai faryaad kay asar say

yeh kisko ro rahee hai zainab tarap tarap kar

jannat main syedda nay chadar gira dee sir say

gardan main aaj shaayad araam aagaya hai

mola tarap rahay thay unees(19) ki seher say

Zainab kay baazoo ko Shabbir choomtay hain

kia jaaneyey Ali nay kia keh diya pisar say

koofay ka zaara zaara rota hai khoon kay aansoo

mehroom horahay hain Hasnain ab pidar say
