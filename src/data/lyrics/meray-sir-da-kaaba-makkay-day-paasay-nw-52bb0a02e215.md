Meray Sir da kaaba, makkay day paasay meray dil da Kaaba, Karbala hai

Makkay chay aab-e-zam zam pay peenday, tahn deen da payasa, Karbala hai

Haji dasaidan Baqiya udaas ay,

Lokan toun puchiya milya jawab ay

Haar roz apnay, bachray koon rowan, waindi ay Zahra, Karbala hai

Aan day Medina hai jaa aman di

Kyun zakhmi thee gayi mayyat Hassan di

Mola Hassan day, waris puttar da, haye lutiya sehra, Karbala hai

Abbas Ghazi day bazoo katay hin

Dariya kinaray pyasay kussay hin

Zawwar saaray, ro ro dasainday, Oh vagda dariya, Karbala hai

Mainku lowk aakhain daidan sala aain

Makkay koon jawain, haji sadawain

Hai gal bar haq, paar dil paya aakhay, Akbar da baba, Karbala hai

Iktiar sa koon ee gal ruvaya

Sarwar nay jehkoon moonday tay chaya

Ummat nay oonda, bay jurm keeta, pamaal laasha, Karbala hai
