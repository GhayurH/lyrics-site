Maa Ko Kafan Mein Dekha Tu Shabbir Ne Kaha.
Amma Jawab Do Meri Amma Jawab Do.

1
Bolo Khiyal Kaun Rakhay Ga Hussain Ka.

2
Ab Noor-e-Ain Keh Ke Bulaye Ga Mujh Ko Kaun.
Raton Ko Uth Ke Pani Pilaye Ga Mujh Ko Kaun.
Ab Kaun Mujh Ko Pyar Se Khana Khilaye Ga.
Baba Ko Kar Diya Hai Akela Smaj Ne.
Ab Apne Dil Ki Baat Bhala Kis Se Woh Kahey.
De Ga Jawab Kaun Ali Ko Salam Ka.
Kuch Mah Pehle Chor Gaye Hum Ko Nana Jaan.
Ab Tum Bhi Ja Rahi Mujhe Chor Kar Yahan.
Itna Bata Do Shehar Mein Apna Koi Raha?.
Dushman Tumhara Ab Bhi Woh Betha Hai Takht Par.
Main Kiya Karon Ga Oss Ghari Itni Tu Do Khabar.
Qatil Tumhara Samne Jab Mere Aaye Ga.
Haye Yeh Meri Kamsini Oss Par Yeh Be Basi.
Bhola Nahi Tha Amma Main Mohsin Ka Gham Abhi.
Noha Parhon Tumhara Keh Mohsin Ka Marsiya.
Yeh Aakhri Kalam Tha Shabbir Ka Aimaad.
Rotay Hoye Janazay Pe Sheh Ne Kaha Aimaad.
Hogi Na Tum Agar Tu Madina Hai Karbala.
