Kahan Ghareeb Ka Ghar Our Kahan Hussain Ki Maa.

1
Gham-e-Hussain Mein Kaisa Sharaf Yeh Paya Hai.
Keh Apne Hathon Se Main Ne Alam Sajaya Hai.
Suna Hai Aati Hain Maljlis Mein Fatima Zahra.
Yeh Baat Soch Ke Farsh-e-Azza Bichaya Hai.
Karam Yeh Majlis-e-Shabir Ne Kiya Warna.

2
Khabar Hai Bint-e-Rasool-e-Khuda Ke Aane Ki.
Badal Rahi Hain Hawaein Ghareeb Khane Ki.
Mera Naseeb Ke Mehmaan Hoin Meri Zahra.

3
Farishte Shah-o-Gadagar Sabhi Hain Paish-e-Nazar.
Hai Anbiya Ki Qatarein Lagin Mere Ghar Par.
Tumhare Aane Se Bibi Yeh Martaba Paya.

4
Yeh Noha Khuwani Yeh Soz-o-Salam Our Matam.
Hamare Ashk Hain Bibi Ke Zakhm Ka Marham.
Agar Na Hote Wasila Yeh Matam-o-Noha.

5
Ghareeb Khane Ko Janat Bana Diya Bibi.
Khushi Yeh Aisi Hai Jis Ne Phola Diya Bibi.
Har Aik Ashk Ne Aankhon Se Girte Girte Kaha.

6
Sar-e-Hussain Jo Shereen Ke Ghar Mein Aaya Tha.
Sar-e-Hussain Ke Hamrah Aaein Thin Zahra.
Jo Dekha Bibi Ko Shereen Ne Yeh Kiya Noha.
Kahan Ghareeb Ka Ghar Or Kahan Hussain Ki Maa.
Ghar Mein Mere Majlis Hoi Our Bich Giya Farsh-e-Azza.
Zakir Ne Jab Majlis Parhi Our Haal Rukhsat Ka Jo Parha.
Itna Roin Zahra Itna Roin Zahra.
Kahan Ghareeb Ka Ghar Or Kahan Hussain Ki Maa.
 Farha Our Mazhar Parha Noha Sheh-e-Mazloom Ka.
Tha Zikar Jab Aashoor Ka Shabir Par Khanjar Chala.
Itna Roin Zahra Itna Roin Zahra.
Kahan Ghareeb Ka Ghar Or Kahan Hussain Ki Maa.
