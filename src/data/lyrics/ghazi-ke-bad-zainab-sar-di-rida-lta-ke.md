Ghazi de baad zainab
Sar di rida luta ke

1
Bari dair tain roii
1) Jado khaimey jal gayee san seeney alam no laa ke bari dair tain roii

2
Barri dair taeen royi
2) Likyah eh rayiya ne oh ghum hogai sakina
Piya marda tamache rokasar tei kamina
Zainab bihra de di dei cholech bah buja ke bari dair tain roii

3
Barri dair taeen royi
3) Lasha hasan dei chan da jis waqt khamey aya
Kubra no seeney laa key roya batal jaya
Ghatri batool jayi farwah di jholi paa key bari dair tain roii

4
Barri dair taeen royi
4) Zalim tei tur gaya si oh khayam nu jala ke
Zahra dei ladlie dey hatach rasia paa ke
Makhtuma do alam be valch moh loka ke bari dair tain roii

5
Barri dair taeen royi
5) Naizay dey naal zalim oh chadar jado lahai
Meri sain apnay sar wich karbal di khaak pai
Abbas bawafa di gal naal mashk laa ke bari dair tain roiii

6
Barri dair taeen royi
6) Ujri rubab da main oh ajj haal ki sunawa
Jado ghodianch paa ke bachrey khidan maaawa
Hyy bibi ladli dey johlich choley paa ke bari dair tain roii

7
Barri dair taeen royi
7) Zaman mein matami di mehsharch Zahra jai
Zenei khuda di jis nai toqeer hai vadai
Baghi nai namazi shabir no mana ke bari dair tain roiii

8
Barri dair taeen royi
8) Jadon ghash chon hosh kita wekhi ajab lachari
Mazloome karbal da pochaia jadon muhari
Ahwal karbala da sajjad no suna ke bari dair tain roii

9
Barri dair taeen royi
