1
Hussain Ne Apna Dil ALLAH Ko Atta Kiya.
Our ALLAH Ne Hussain Ko Lakhon Dil Atta Kiye.
Ishq-e-Khuda Karbala.
Dars-e-Wafa Karbala.
Rehnuma Karbala.
Rah-e-Baqa Karbala.
Gham Ki Dawa.
Haq Ka Pata.
Dein Ki Sada.
Rab Ki Atta.
Seeno Mein Hai Karbala.
Zehno Mein Hai Karbala.
Dharkano Mein Karbala.
Sansoon Mein Hai Karbala.
Jo Kabhi Na Ja Saka Karbala.
Ho Ke Jo Bhi Aa Gaya Karbala.
Sun Sako Tu Do Dilon Ki Aik Hi Sada Hai.
Dil Hai Karbala Mein.
Dil Mein Karbala Hai.
Sotay Jagtay Nigah Mein Haram Sara Hai.
Zindagi Mein Aisa Koi Shaks Tu Dikha Nahi.
Jo Yeh Keh De Karbala Mein Manga Our Mila Nahi.
Dhondtay Ho Tum Kahan Hai Yahin Pe La Makaan.
Karbala Hussain Ki Hussain Ka Khuda Hai.
Iss Qadar Boland Yeh Zameen Be Sabab Nahi.
Tashna Lab Ki Sar Zameen Pe Koi Tashna Lab Nahi.
Rizq Ilm Aa Gahi Mil Rahi Hai Zindagi.
Jis Ke Hath Kat Gaye Yahan Woh Bantta Hai.
Imam Jafar-e-Sadiq Alh Salat-o-Wslam Farmatay Hain:.
Momino Karbala Jaya Karo.
Ta Ke Tum Janat Ke Rastoon Se Anjan Na Ho.
Zaireen Ka Jab Sharf Mila Hussain Ki Qasam.
Pehli Bar Karbala Mein Main Ne Jab Rakhay Qadam.
Kaifiyat Bataon Kya Hal-e-Dil Sunaon Kya.
Aisa Lag Raha Tha Bas Yehi Watan Mera Hai.
Har Nabi Ko Karbala Dikhai Hai Khuda Ne.
Karbala Ki Manzalat Batai Hai Khuda Ne.
Zakhm Ho Ya Ho Qaza Har Marz Ki Hai Dawa.
Sar Zameen-e-Karbala Ki Khak Bhi Shifa Hai.
Qarz Hai Yehi Lahu Pe Our Farz Bhi Yehi.
Majlis-e-Hussain Ko Hai Waqf Sari Zindagi.
Farsh-e-Gham Bichaein Hum Our Alam Sajaein Hum.
Bachpaney Mein Waldain Se Yehi Suna Hai.
Ya Zahra Ya Zahra.
Wadah Syeda Se Jo Kiya Tha Woh Nibhaein Gay.
Aane Wali Nasal Ko Bhi Karbala Sunaein Gay.
Ishq Ke Yeh Marhalay Tey Karein Gay Ishq Se.
Majlis-e-Hussain Se Yehi Sabaq Mila Hai.
Ghar Mein Jo Alam Saja Rahe Hain Hum.
Majlis-e-Azza Kara Rahe Hain Hum.
Har Baras Yeh Gham Mana Rahe Hain Hum.
Nohay Marsiye Suna Rahe Hain Hum.
Dawa Jab Kiya Hai Tu Saboot Bhi Diya Hai.
Zainab-e-Hazeen Ne Qaid-e-Sham Mein Yehi Kaha.
Mere Lal Mera Bhai Our Mera Bawafa.
Sab Ke Lashay Hain Wahan Main Aseer Hoon Yahan.
Iss Liye Tu Har Ghari Labon Pe Yeh Boka Hai.
 Meesum Our:MAZHAR ABIDI: Daleel Chahiye.
Jab Jahan Jisay Nijat Ki Sabeel Chahiye.
Oss Ko Mashwara Yeh Do Wird Raat Din Karo.
Bas Yehi Nijat Hai Agar Tu Keh Raha Hai.
Hussain Ya Hussain Ya Hussain Ya Hussain.
Kartay Rahein Ahl-e-Azza.
Lab Pe Rahe Zikar Tera.
Tujh Se Karein Younhi Wafa.
Tere Azadar Sada Hussain.
Hussain Tere Liye.
Aqeeda Bas Yeh Kahey Hussain Tere Liye.
Sada Yeh Dil Se Uthay Hussain Tere Liye.
Yeh Hath Khalq Hoye Hussain Tere Liye.
Yeh Dil Dharkta Rahe Hussain Tere Liye.
