1
Kaba Mein Ja Raha Tu Na Do Tana Kiya Kahin.
Bhola Hoon Haq-e-Sohbat-e-Ahl-e-Kunshit Ko?
Taat Mein Ta Rahe Na May-o-Angbeein Ki Laag.
Dozakh Mein Daal Do Koi Le Kar Behisht Ko.

2
Hoon Munharif Na Kyun Rah-o-Rasm-e-Sawab See.
Tehra Laga Hai Qat Qalam Sar-e-Noshist Ko.
 Ghalib Kuch Apni Saei Se Lahana Nahi Mujhe.
Khirin Jalay Agar Na Malah Khaye Kisht Ko.
