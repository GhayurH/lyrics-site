1
Kehnay Ko Hai Chay Mah Ka Bacha Ali Asghar.
Qad Mein Hai Magar Arsh Se Oncha Ali Asghar.
Tauheed Bhala Qarz Chukati Bhi Tu Kaisay.
Kar Detey Agar Dasht Mein Sajdah Ali Asghar.

2
Quran-e-Shujat Ka Hai Jo Sorah-e-Kausar.
Shabeer Pe Ban Kar Hai Woh Utra Ali Asghar.
Mil Jata Hai Jo Kuch Bhi Issay Tham Ke Mango.
Kabay Se Nahi Kam Tera Jhola Ali Asghar.

3
Esa Ne Kaha Mil Ke Yeh Fitrus Se Falak Par.
Hai Mujh Se Maseeha Ka Maseeha Ali Asghar.
Asghar Ki Hansi Aamad-e-Mehdi Ki Khabar Thi.
Aaein Gay Abhi Ran Mein Dobarah Ali Asghar.

4
Ab Tak Bin-e-Kahil Ki Hai Gardan Mein Woh Paiwast.
Kya Teer Tabasum Ka Chalaya Ali Asghar.
Mukhtar Se Leta Hai Asad Dad-o-Duaein.
Kar Ke Tere Qatil Pe Tabara Ali Asghar.
