Haider Ki Shahadat Sarwar Raza Karbalai.
Veeran Howa Kaba Haider Ki Shahadat Se.

1
Zahra Ka Luta Kunba Haider Ki Shahadat Se.
Mimber Se Sada Aayi Quran Bhi Rota Tha.
Mehrab-e-Ibadat Bhi Rotay Hoye Kehta Tha.
Hai Surkh Yeh Musalla Haider Ki Shahadat Se.

2
Pardais Mein Baba Hai Zainab Se Juda Hota.
Hai Oss Ki Yateemi Par Yeh Aasman Rota.
Matam Howa Hai Barpa Haider Ki Shahadat Se.
Fizza Ne Kaha Ro Kar Yeh Kaisi Musibat Hai.

3
ALLAH Mere Dil Par Yeh Sakht Aziyat Hai.
Ghar Syeda Ka Ujra Haider Ki Shahadat Se.
Hasnain Tere Sar Se Baba Ka Utha Saya.
Kehte They Nabi Ro Kar Hasnain Tum Pe Aaya.

4
Hai Waqt Ghareebi Ka Haider Ki Shahadat Se.
Noha Waheed-o- Sarwar Ro Ro Ke Yeh Parhtay Hain.
Jibreel Sar-e-Kaba Ayelan Yeh Kartay Hain.
Veraan Hai Jahan Sara Haider Ki Shahadat Se.
