1
Tur Wanj Musafir Multan Party 2013
Tur Wanj Musafir Be Watan Ayen Khak Te Khaimay Na La Main Haan Zameen-e-Karbala.
Ghin Aaein Taun Wasda Qafila Main Sirf Haan Ujran Di Ja Main Haan Zameen-e-Karbala.
Meesaq De Waday Aye Han Aithay Aaona Aye Zahra De Chan.
Main Aounday Sajday Naal Bas Banr Sagdi Haan Khak-e-Shifa Main Haan Zameen-e-Karbala.
Main Was Taan Sagdi Haan Magar Jay Khad Wanjay Nabvi Jigar.
Baon Kharch Theenda Hay Ithaan Kahin Baye Nagar Koon Wanj Dasa Main Haan Zameen-e-Karbala.
Yousuf Taun Sohnra Putar Hayi Aindi Ajaan Mas Lahndi Payi.
Sohnri Jawani Putar Di Ith Barchiyaan Wich Na Luta Main Haan Zameen-e-Karbala.
Jo Dhi Musafir Chayi Khara Ayein Bachri Koon Seene Layi Khara Ayein.
Aindi Yateemi Da Koi Taun Wainr Na Maikon Sunra Main Haan Zameen-e-Karbala.
Khaimay V Jal Wainday Ithaan Burqay V Dhal Wainday Ithaan.
Ghayoor Disda Peein Maikon Bhaini De Mehmal Na Balha Main Haan Zameen-e-Karbala.
Baon Aa Alambardar Hin Tur Gaye Alambardar Hin.
Jeenday Hathaan Wich Hai Alam Ayen Veer Diyaan Banhein Bacha Main Haan Zameen-e-Karbala.
 Gohar Alaya Be Watan Main Aap Haan Zahra Da Chan.
Taikun Wasanwar De Kitay Main Kharch Karna Aye Qafila Main Haan Zameen-e-Karbala.
