1
Ho Kar Aseer-e-Zulm Jonhi Dukhtar-e-Ali.
Kofay Ki Simt Gayarah Muharram Ko Jab Chali.
Dekha Ke Khak-o-Khoon Mein Hai Galtaan Para Howa.
Pamal Lasha-e-Dil-e-Zahra-o-Murtaza.
Rasi Mein Thi Kalaiyaan Our They Labhoon Pe Bain.
Mur Mur Ke Dekhti Thi Behan Lasha-e-Hussain.
Dekha Sakina Bethi Hai Baba Ki Lash Par.
Oss Ko Galay Laga Liya Zainab Ne Aan Kar.
Aye Beti Tujh Ko Rona Hai Ta Sham Kar Ke Bain.
Bhai Se Youn Juda Hoi Woh Gham Ki Mubtila.
Jis Tarha Rooh Jism Se Ho Jati Hai Juda.
Be Rooh Kar Rahi Thi Safar Khwahar-e-Hussain.
Bhaiyya Tumhari Lash Hai Sehra Mein Be Kafan.
Afsoos Tum Ko Dafan Na Kar Payi Yeh Behan.
Tum Uth Gaye Tu Uth Gaya Mere Bhi Dil Ka Chain.
Chora Tha Jab Madina Jo Halat Thi Shah Ki.
Lash-e-Hussain Par Wohi Halat Behan Ki Thi.
Mati Pe Jism Tha Sar-e-Naiza Sar-e-Hussain.
Qasim Ki Lash Hai Kahin Akbar Ki Lash Hai.
Farzand-e-Fatima Ka Jasd Pash Pash Hai.
Darya Kinaray Sota Hai Umm Ul-Baneen Ka Chain.
Aayi Hai Roh-e-Zahra Sar-e-Lasha-e-Imam.
Zainab Pukari Amma Main Jati Hoon Soye Sham.
Tere Sapurd Karti Hoon Amma Tera Hussain.
 Mukhtar Karbala Se Palat Kar Jo Hum Chaley.
 Majid Baha Ke Ashk Yehi Sochtay Rahe.
Chora Behan Ne Kaisay Bhala Lasha-e-Hussain.
