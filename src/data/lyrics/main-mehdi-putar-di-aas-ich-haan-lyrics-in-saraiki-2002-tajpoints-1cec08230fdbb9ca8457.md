1
Main Mehdi Putar Di Aas Ich Haan
Aanday Wainr Batool De Aj Taein Aaram Qabar Wich Aaya Nayi Main Mehdi Putar Di Aas Ich Han.
Main Putar Hussain Musafir Da Zakhmi Chola Dafnaya Nayi Main Mehdi Putar Di Aas Ich Han.
Mainda Kazim Bachra Hunr Taein Ohwein Qabar De Wich V Qaidi Aye.
Oh Chodah Saal De Qaidi Da Main Koi Zanjeer Lahaya Nayi Main Mehdi Putar Di Aas Ich Han.
Mainda Mehdi Bachra Puch Ghin Si Kiwein Wapis Maiyyat Walenday Hin.
Main Hun Taein Hasan De Kafan Utton Koi Surkh Nishan Mitaya Nayi Main Mehdi Putar Di Aas Ich Han.
Maindi Qabar Te Aa Ke Karbal Di Bas Sham-e-Ghareebaan Khar Gayi Aye.
Shabbir De Turn De Baad Kain Ath Aan Charag Jalaya Nayi Main Mehdi Putar Di Aas Ich Han.
Mainday Mohsin Taun Aagaz Thiya Maikon Roz Janazay Milday Hin.
Wadi Inna Lillah Parhdi Haan Main Pasay Taun Hath Chaya Nayi Main Mehdi Putar Di Aas Ich Han.
Sajjad Di Kand Te Shamiyaan De Har Zulm Di Satar Likhi Hoi Aye.
Main Kain Munsaf Koon Hunr Taein Sajjad Da Jigar Wakhaya Nayi Main Mehdi Putar Di Aas Ich Haan.
Mainday Fidak Koon Bait Ul Maal Samajh Mehshar Taein Kar Taqseem Gaye.
Maindi Pak Wirasat Wich Kain V Abbas Alam Lawaya Nayi Main Mehdi Putar Di Aas Ich Haan.
 Gohar Zahra Faryad Karey Bikhra Quran Hai Qabar Meri.
Mainday Apnray Babay Di Pag Da Kain Aan Ghilaf Pawaya Nayi Main Mehdi Putar Di Aas Ich Haan.
