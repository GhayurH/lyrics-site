1
Kahan Ho Mere Hussain Haider Rizvi.
Sada Yeh Deti Hai Behna Kahan Ho Mere Hussain.
Khari Hoon Dasht Mein Tanha Kahan Ho Mere Hussain.
Yahan Kissi Bhi Janazay Pe Sar Nahi Bhai.

2
Kahan Talash Karey Tujh Ko Fatima Jayi.
Bata Do Khud Mujhe Bhaiyya Kahan Ho Mere Hussain.
Pari Hain Lashon Pe Lashein Jahan Bhi Jaye Nazar.
Behan Ko Milti Nahi Hai Tumhari Koi Khabar.

3
Tum Hi Batao Khudara Kahan Ho Mere Hussain.
Tumhari Beti Ko Maqtal Mein Dhondti Hoon Main.
Tumhare Sath Hai Bachi Yeh Janti Hoon Main.
Kahan Hai Meri Sakina Kahan Ho Mere Hussain.

4
Tumhari Lash Se Pathar Behan Hataye Gi.
Jo Dagh Khoon Ke Lagay Hain Unhein Mitaye Gi.
Karon Gi Saaf Main Chehra Kahan Ho Mere Hussain.
Laeen Cheenaney Walay Saron Se Chadar Hain.

5
Koi Nahi Hai Na Abbas Hain Na Akbar Hain.
Laeen Jalatay Hain Khaima Kahan Ho Mere Hussain.
Ali Ki Beti Ko Haye Rasan Mein Bandha Gaya.
Gayi Hain Jab Sar-e-Bazar Zainab-e-Kubra.

6
Parha Hai Bas Yehi Noha Kahan Ho Mere Hussain.
Jala Hai Jis Ghari Azlan Khaima e Sadaat.
Suno Keh Dasht Mein Haider Ajab Hoi Hai Raat.
Pukari Zainab-e-Kubra Kahan Ho Mere Hussain.
