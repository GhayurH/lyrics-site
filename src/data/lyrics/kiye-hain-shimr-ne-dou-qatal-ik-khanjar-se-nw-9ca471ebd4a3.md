Kiye Hain Shimr Ne Dou Qatal Ik Khanjar Se,

Lahu Hussain Ka Poncha Behen Ki Chadar Se,

Ghareeb e Zahra Wahin Rotay Rotay Marjata

Behen Utha Ke Na Laati Jo Laash e Akbar Se,

Kisi Ne Baloun Se Pakra Koyi Utha Ke Chala,

Sar e Hussain Ko Maara Kisi Ne Pathar Se,

Ussay Uthaya Hai Baba Ki Laash Se Aise,

Ke Phir Sakina Nahi Soyi Shimr Ke Dar Se,

Lab e Hussain Pe Has Has Ke Maarta Tha Chari,

Yazeed Khail Raha Tha Hussain Ke Sar Se,

Bulaya Shaam Ke Zindaan Main Jab Sakina Ne,

Hussain Pyaase Palat Aaye Houz e Kausar Se,

Behen Ka Parda Taqallum Ussay Bana Na Tha,

Suna Raha Tha Woh Quran Sina Ke Mimbar Se,
