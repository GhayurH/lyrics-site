Maray gaye Shabbir fiza may ye sada hay
Zainab tera pardes may koyi na raha hay

1
Maa kehti thi may aa ke mana loon tumhe Asghar
Ik baar pukaro mera dil doob raha hay

2
Afsos ke ye qatil e Akbar ne na jana
Naiza to Muhammad ke kalejay pe laga hay

3
Hamsheer se milnay ko bohat tarrpa hay Akbar
Atki hui sanson may bhi Sughra ki sada hay

4
Fizza ne soey nehr ye Ghazi ko sada di
Abbas utho khatray may Zainab ki rida hay

5
Kal pehray pe Abbas thay aur aaj hay Zainab
Aey sham e gharibaan ye ajab tarz e jafa hay

6
Kon aa ke Sakina ko tamachon se bachaye
Bhai Ali Akbar hay na Abbas chacha hay

7
Islam bachanay ko Asar dasht e sitam may
Ashoor ke din khoon bahattar ka baha hay
