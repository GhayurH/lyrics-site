1
Matam e Sarwar Meesum Kashmiri.
Jahan Mein Matam-e-Sarwar Kabhi Na Kam Hoga.
Hamarey Sath Qayamat Talak Yeh Gham Hoga.
Woh Jis Ne Deen Ka Maqsad Hussain Se Samjha.
Jahan Mein Bas Woh Azadar Mohtaram Hoga.
Bhula Sakay Gi Na Nasal-e-Ali Ko Karb-o-Bala.
Yeh Waqiya Alvi Khon Se Raqam Hoga.
Jo Karwan-e-Hussaini Mein Ho Gaya Shamil.
Baqa Ki Rah Mein Aagay Wohi Qadam Hoga.
Ik Imtehaan Hai Yeh Inkar-e-Baiyat Fasiq.
Yeh Aisa Jurm-e-Wafa Hai Keh Sar Qalam Hoga.
Jo Paish Hongay Qayamat Ke Din Khuda Ke Hazoor.
Hamarey Doosh Pe Abbas Ka Alam Hoga.
Kamar Mein Khum Hai Magar Sar Nahi Hai Aap Ka Khum.
Habib Ibn-e-Mazahir Ka Yeh Hasham Hoga.
Yahan Pe Asghar-e-Masoom Jaan Bulb Hongay.
Muqablay Pe Wahan Lashkar-e-Sitam Hoga.
Hussainiyaat Ko Jalein Gay Asar-e-Hazir Mein.
Imam-e-Asar Ka Hum Par Agar Karam Hoga.
Hum Apne Dil Ke Warq Par Jo Likh Rahe Hain Hilal.
Hamara Harf Bhi Ashk-e-Azza Se Nam Hoga.
