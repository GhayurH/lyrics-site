Nikla tha janaza jo gar lootki aya hein

Kis kis tara se hal ko, omate ne sataya hein

Hi zare ne tokaray keye shabar ke jigger ke,

Hei teero ka baadal to taboot pe chaya hein

Mola hasan ke choom ke lab kethe the ahmed,

Hi in labo se jigre hasan tusht me aya hein

Hai fatheha khabar ko leyahai bana khadi,

Hai binte mohammad pe darvasa gira ya hein

Karbal me mera bhai the akabro asgar jube,

Korban karna kasum farva ko bathya hein
