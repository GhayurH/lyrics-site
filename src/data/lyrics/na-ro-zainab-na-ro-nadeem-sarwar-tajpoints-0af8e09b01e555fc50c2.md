Na Ro Zainab Na Ro Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics

1
Sham-e-Gharebaan, Zainab Tanha, Ghoor Andhera.
Maut Ka Saya, Badal Ban Ke Sar Par Chaya.
Aawazein Tapoon Ki Ubhrin.
Kaun Jalay Khaimon Ki Janib, Taiz Qadam Se Chalta Aaya.
Ponch Ke Aanso Zainab Dekhay, Dekhti Kya Hai Oss Ka Baba.
Baap Ke Seene Par Sar Rakh Ke.

2
Bilak Bilak Kar, Tarap Tarap Kar, Aisa Roi.
Na Ro Zainab Na Ro, Haan Mere Jigar Ke Tukray Na Ro.
Main Kaisay Na Royon Baba Main Ujar Gayi Baba.
Ho Mera Akbar, Ho Mera Asghar, Ho Mera Ghazi, Haan Tera Dilbar, Ho Main Ujar Gayi Baba.
Ho Ik Din Mein, Ho Ghar Ujra, Ho Rahe Bhai, Ho Na Beta, Ho Main Ujar Gayi Baba.
Ho Main Bekas, Ho Main Muztar, Ho Pardesi, Ho Main Be Ghar, Ho Main Ujar Gayi Baba.

3
Ho Gaya Ghazi, Ho Darya Par, Ho Girey Bazo, Ho Kat Kat Kar, Ho Main Ujar Gayi Baba.
Sarwar Pe, Ho Chala Khanjar, Main Ho Gayi, Ho Be Chadar, Ho Main Ujar Gayi Baba.
Ho Mara Hai, Ho Aada Ne, Ho Pyason Ke, Ho Lashkar Ko, Ho Main Ujar Gayi Baba.
Ho Ik Chadar, Mein Dekha, Ho Qasim Ka, Ho Jab Lasha, Ho Main Ujar Gayi Baba.
Ho Zindaan Mein, Ho Jana Hai, Ho Rasi Hai, Ho Shana Hai, Ho Main Ujar Gayi Baba.
Ho Rehan O, Ho Sarwar Ka, Ho Pursa Hai, Haan Yeh Noha, Ho Main Ujar Gayi Baba.
