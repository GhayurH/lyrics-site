Uth Nana, mere dard wanda, maiṅ vir kho ke aayi haaṅ
De pursa dukhiya Zainab nu, maiṅ daadi dard sataayi haaṅ

1
Aa dekh Nana, vich Karbal de, teri khoon vich dub dastaar gayi
Ae gal maiṅ dukhiya Zainab nu, haaye jiundi jaane mar gayi
Jinde jore arsh toṅ aande san, ohnu kafan maiṅ paa ke aayi haaṅ

2
Na puchh vich Shaam bazaaran de, jo Nana mera haal hoya
Taazim kiti inj lokan ne, mere vaalan da rang laal hoya
Vich Shaam bazaaran Shaamiyan toṅ, haaye patthar maiṅ khaa ke aayi haaṅ

3
Tere puttar Husain di dhi Nana, zindaan nu khoon ruwaa gayi aa
Sir katya vekh ke baabe da, oh tod masuma soṅ gayi aa
Zanjiraan sang maiṅ vich raahwaṅ, ohnu dafna ke aayi haaṅ

4
Na puchh meku tasvir teri, kiveṅ tukre tukre hoyi aa
Dukhi Zainab Akbar Qasim da, haaye Ghazi vir nu royi aa
Mazlum Husain di laash uttoṅ, maiṅ jhinkaaṅ khaa ke aayi haaṅ

5
Khurshid haya di malka aa, ro ro ke roze te aakhe
Maiṅ behnaṅ te bharjaaiyaṅ sang, hath rassiyaṅ te kariyaṅ paa
Mera parda bhaaveṅ ni bachya, tera din bacha ke aayi haaṅ
