1
Uth nana mere dard wanda main veer kuha k ai han
Dy pursa dukhya Zainab s.a nu main dadi dard satai han
1;aa dekh nana wich karbal dy teri khoon ich dub dastaar gai a gal main dukhya Zainab s.a nu haye jeondi jane mar gai jeendy joory arsh tu aandy san o ku kafan main paa k ai han
2;na puch wich shaam bazaran dy jo nana mera hal hoya tazeem kiti inj lokan ny mere walan da rang lal hoya wich sham bazaran shamian tu haye pathar main kha k ai han
3;tere puter Hussain di dhi nana zindan nu khoon ruwa gai a sir katya wekh k baby da o tod masuma soon gai a zanjeeran sang main wich raahwan o ku dafna k ai han
4; na puch meku tasveer teri kiwen tukry tukry hoi a dukhi zainab akbar qasim da haye ghazi veer nu roi a Mazloom hussain di lash uton main jhinkan kha k ai han
5;khursheed haya di malka a ro ro k rozy ty aakhy main behna ty bharjaiyan sang hath rasiyan ty kariyan pa mera parda baahhwen ni bachya tera deen bacha k ai han
