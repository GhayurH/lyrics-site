1
Pidar Ke Lehjay Mukhtar Hussain Fatehpuri.
Pidar Ke Lehjay Mein Guftar Karne Aaye Hain.
Boland Lafzoon Ka Maiyar Karne Aaye Hain.
Jo Kar Rahe Hain Siyasat Hussainiyat Ke Khilaf.
Hum Aisay Zehno Ko Beemar Karne Aaye Hain.
Jo Mere Bhai Ne Khainchi Thi Ran Mein Nezay Se.
Ossi Lakeer Ko Dewar Karne Aaye Hain.
Khuda Gawah Paye Inteqam-e-Khon-e-Hussain.
Hum Ik Aseer Ko Mukhtar Karne Aaye Hain.
Hai Jin Mein Betha Howa Chup Ke Sham Ka Marhab.
Hum Unn Faselon Ko Mismar Karne Aaye Hain.
Bacha Sako Tu Bacha Lo Yeh Sheh Ragein Apni.
Hum Aaj Khutbon Se Paykar Karne Aaye Hain.
Yazid Tujh Ko Yeh Shayad Abhi Nahi Maloom.
Keh Hum Tujhey Hi Giraftar Karne Aaye Hain.
Tha Aisa Lehja Ke Kofa Samajh Raha Tha Bilal.
Khitab Haider-e-Karrar Karne Aaye Hain.
