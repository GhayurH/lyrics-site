Luti kin fazaon me aaley payamber, na mai kehsakungi na tum sun sakogey
Huwa kaisey khaali ali ka bhara ghar, na mai kehsakungi na tum sun sakogey

1
Lagi dilpe barchi, jigar mu ko aya, sada aayi maqtal se baba kaha ho
Chaley kiska lasha uthaney ko sarwar, na mai kehsakungi na tum sun sakogey

2
Wo khoon khaar sehra wo wehshi hawaein, wo sunsaan jangal wo maa ki sadaein
Kahan sogaye jakey jooley se asghar, na mai kehsakungi na tum sun sakogey

3
Barha shimr khanjar liye kiski janib, pukara tha maqtal se kisne ali ko
Nazar aya sar kiska nok e sina par, na mai kehsakungi na tum sun sakogey

4
Kahu umm e laila ka mai haal kaisey, bayaan kya ho kulsoom ki bekasi ka
Utaari gayi kaisey zainab ki chadar, na mai kehsakungi na tum sun sakogey

5
Waqaar aah kis dilse ab mai bataun, jo guzri hai zainab ke qalb e hazeen par
Wo bazaar o darbar kufey ka manzar, na mai kehsakungi na tum sun sakogey
