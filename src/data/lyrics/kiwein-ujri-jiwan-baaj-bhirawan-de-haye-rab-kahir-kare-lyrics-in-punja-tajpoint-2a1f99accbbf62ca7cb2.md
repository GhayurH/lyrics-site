Kiwein Ujri Jiwan Baaj Bhirawan De.

1
Ro Ro Kaindi Rayi Wich Aye Duawaan De.
Haye Rab Khair Kare.
Moor Moharan Akbar Veran Desaan Nu, Chad Pardesan Nu.
Panbar Balday Ne Hanjwaan Hawaan De.
Khabaan De Wich Sarday Khaimay Wekhay Ne Lashay Wekhay Ne.
Waris Lutday Ray Pak Ridawaan De.

2
Nanay De Rozay Te Ja Ke Baith Jawaan Ro Ro Ke Aakhaan.
Rul Gayi Aal-e-Abba Wich Sehrawan De.
Phulaan Naloon Nazuk Mera Veer Asghar Maqtal Wich Akbar.
Chakar Julday Re Garam Hawawaan De.
Ayho Lafz Mere Dukh Dard Wandawan Gay Amar Banawan Gay.
Dukhray Likh Likh Ke Bhain Bhirawaan De.
