Shabbir Ki Jaan Ali Akbar Kazmi Brothers.

1
Khush Hai Zameen Khush Aasmaan Khush Hain Yeh Dono Jahan.
Aangan Mein Maula Hussain Ke Aaye Hain Aaj Ali Akbar.
Sajdoon Ki Chand Raat Azano Ki Eid Hai.
Dunya Ke Aaj Sare Jawano Ki Eid Hai.
Humshakal-e-Nabi Hum Nam-e-Ali Shabbir Ki Jan Ali Akbar.
Hathoon Pe Utha Ke Bolein Phophi Zainab Qurban Ali Akbar.

2
Raftar Haseen Guftar Haseen Sorat Bhi Haseen Seerat Bhi Haseen.
Sar Ta Ba Shabeeh-e-Ahmad Hai Akbar Sa Haseen Koi Bhi Nahi.
Hai Husan Ki Sari Dunya Ka Tanha Sultan Ali Akbar.
Washams Ke Jaisi Zulfein Hain Walnoor Tumhara Chehra Hai.
Baba Ke Liye Yaseen Ho Tum Kausar Hi Tumhara Shajrah Hai.
Wal Lail Ke Jaisi Aankhein Hain Tum Ho Quran Ali Akbar.

3
Aflak Se Kam Hargiz Yeh Nahi Jis Ghar Mein Tumhara Noor Hai Yeh.
Mosa Ke Liye Tha Toor Wohi Yousuf Ke Liye Tu Toor Hai Yeh.
Jalway Se Tumhare Hotey Hain Yousuf Hairan Ali Akbar.
Jo ALLAH Akbar Ki Hai Sada Yeh Bhi Tu Tumhara Sadqa Hai.
Tum Se Hi Azanein Zindah Hain Tum Se Hi Masjid Zindah Hai.
Hota Hai Din Mein Panch Dafa Yeh Hi Ayelan Ali Akbar.

4
Kuch Bhool Bhi Jate Hain Lekin Yeh Rasam Ada Hum Kartay Hain.
Betay Ko Bana Kar Hum Dulha Tera Hi Sehra Parhtay Hain.
Deti Hain Dua Zainab Hum Ko Hai Yeh Eman Ali Akbar.
 Azlan, Moazzam Our Aman Kartay Hain Dua Aabad Rahe.
Deta Hai Ali Bhi Yeh Hi Sada Hum Sab Ki Jawani Shad Rahe.
Yeh Zikar Tumhara Ban Jaye Apni Pehchan Ali Akbar.
