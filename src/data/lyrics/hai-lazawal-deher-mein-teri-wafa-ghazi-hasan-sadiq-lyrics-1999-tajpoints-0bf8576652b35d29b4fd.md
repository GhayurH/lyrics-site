1
Hai Lazawal Deher Mein Teri Wafa Ghazi Hasan Sadiq.
Fana Nahi Jisay Mar Ke Hai Woh Baqa Ghazi.
Faqat Moheet Nahi Karbala Pe Iss Ki Wafa.
Qadam Qadam Pe Howa Maula Par Fida Ghazi.

2
Lab-e-Furat Se Zahra Ki Aa Rahi Thi Sada.
Tu Meri Beti Ka Wahid Tha Aasra Ghazi.
Khiyam-e-Aal-e-Abba Mein Howa Hai Hashar Bapa.
Ali Ke Lal Se Jis Dam Howa Juda Ghazi.

3
Rahi Hai Dair Talak Bazu Chomti Zainab.
Umm Ul-Banin Ko Jis Dam Howa Atta Ghazi.
 Ameer Iss Ka Badal Koi Nahi Zamane Mein.
Jahan Mein Mulk-e-Wafa Ka Hai Kibriya Ghazi.
