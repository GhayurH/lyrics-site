Maimar-e-Arz-e-Karb-o-Bala Main Hussain Hoon.
Nokar Bani Hai Jis Ki Qaza Main Hussain Hoon.

1
Iss Pori Kainat Mein Shabeer Ke Siwa.
Guzrey Bohat Wali-o-Nabi Aali Martaba.
Lekin Koi Yeh Keh Na Saka Main Hussain Hoon.
Bhatka Howa Tha Rah-e-Mawadat Tak Aa Gaya.

2
Nar-e-Saqar Ko Chor Ke Janat Tak Aa Gaya.
Jab Hur Pe Raaz Khulney Laga Main Hussain Hoon.
Rahib Ke Saat Betay Tu Mamoli Baat Hai.
Mere Tu Ikhteyar Mein Kul Kainat Hai.

3
Jo Chahon Jis Ko Kar Doon Atta Main Hussain Hoon.
Mere Liye Nabi Ne Jo Sajdah Barha Diya.
Khaliq Se Main Ne Wadah Ossi Waqt Kar Liya.
Qarza Utar Doon Ga Tera Main Hussain Hoon.

4
Waqif Nahi Hai Woh Mere Jah-o-Jalal Se.
Baiyat Talab Hai Fatima Zahra Ke Lal Se.
Shayad Yazid Bhool Gaya Main Hussain Hoon.
Dedar Ka Tha Shoq Jo Khud Ko Mita Diya.

5
Maula Ne Hath Tham Ke Bachay Se Yeh Kaha.
Utha Ja Habib Uth Ke Bata Main Hussain Hoon.
Marhab Mizaj Jo Bhi Hai Sab Ko Pachar Doon.
Firon Jo Uthay Ossay Dozakh Mein Gar Doon.

6
Hai Meri Zulfiqar Assa Main Hussain Hoon.
Sar Jis Ka Kaat Kar Bhi Na Zalim Jhuka Sakay.
Khamosh Karna Chaha Tha Jis Ko Yazid Ne.
Nook-e-Sina Pe Bool Utha Main Hussain Hoon.

7
Chamkay Hain Mere Noor Se Zarray Zameen Ke.
Tum Kya Batao Gaye Mujhe Qanon Deen Ke.
Islam Mere Ghar Mein Pala Main Hussain Hoon.
Mumkin Nahi Yeh Kam Had-e-Mumkinaat Se.

8
Karb-o-Bala Ki Khak Pe Ghazi Ke Hath Se.
Main Ne Likhi Kitab-e-Wafa Main Hussain Hoon.
Ik Din Mein Aks-e-Fatah-e-Khaybar Bana Diya.
Lori Mein Kya Hussain Ne Oss Ko Suna Diya.

9
Asghar Bhi Ran Mein Kehne Laga Main Hussain Hoon.
Tu Matami Hai Mera Mujhe Bhi Hai Yeh Khabar.
Lekin Mere Amal Pe Takallum Amal Bhi Kar.
Phir Bakhsh Doon Ga Teri Khata Main Hussain Hoon.
