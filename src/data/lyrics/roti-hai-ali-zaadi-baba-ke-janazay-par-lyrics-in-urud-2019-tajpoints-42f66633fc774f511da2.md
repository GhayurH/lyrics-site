Roti Hai Ali Zaadi Baba Ke Janazay Par.
Band Aankhon Se Rotay Hain Har Bain Par Khud Haider.

1
Maa Choor Gayi Pehlay Ab Chor Chalay Tum Bhi.
Ab Tak Main Nahi Bholi Jalta Howa Baba Dar.
Yeh Kofay Ki Galiyon Mein Haider Ka Janaza Hai.
Yaan Mehmil-e-Zainab Hai Yeh Sham Ke Raste Par.

2
Hasnain Ke Rone Ki Chai Hai Sada Har So.
Sab Dekhte Hain Baba Ro Ro Ke Tera Bistar.
Har Rooz Musallay Par Dhondon Gi Tujhe Baba.
Dekhon Gi Bhala Kaise Ujra Howa Apna Ghar.

3
Aashoor Ke Din Baba Pehchan Sakay Mujh Ko.
Phailaya Hai Baloon Ko Yeh Soch Ke Chehray Par.
Kuhram Howa Barpa Oss Waqt Janaze Par.
Rukhsat Ki Ijazat Di Zainab Ne Jo Khud Aa Kar.

4
Ahsas Yateemi Ka Kehta Hai Mujhe Baba.
Zalim Se Bacha Paye Beti Yeh Teri Chadar.
 Zaire Yehi Lagta Tha Taboot Hai Shane Pe.
Mula Ki Shahadat Ka Likhta Tha Main Jab Manzar.
