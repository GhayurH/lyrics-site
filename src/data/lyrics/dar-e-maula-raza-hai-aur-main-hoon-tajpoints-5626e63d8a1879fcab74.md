1
Dar e Maula Raza Hai Aur Main Hoon.
Labhoon Par Marhaba Hai Our Main Hoon.
Mujhe Janat Ki Kyun Kar Aarzu Ho.
Khurasaan Ki Fiza Hai Our Main Hoon.

2
Farishton Ne Uthaye Naaz Mere.
Karam Ki Inteha Hai Our Main Hoon.
Para Rehne Do Sajday Mein Jabeen Ko.
Faqat Yeh Iltija Hai Our Main Hoon.

3
Abhi Waqt-e-Ajal Ruk Ja Yahan Par.
Yahan Baab-e-Shifa Hai Our Main Hoon.
Lehad Mein Darmiyaan Koi Nahi Hai.
Mera Mushkil Kusha Hai Our Main Hoon.

4
Sahara Kaun Hai Dunya Mein Mera.
Tumhara Aasra Hai Our Main Hoon.
Mujhe Arman Likhna Hai Qaseeda.
Riza-e-Kibriya Hai Our Main Hoon.
