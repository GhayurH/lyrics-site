Karbala Dill Mein Hai Mere Dil Azza Khane Mein Hai.
Hai Safar Ashkon Ka Our Manzil Azza Khane Mein Hai.

1
Matami Yani Dua-e-Hazrat-e-Zahra Hoon Main.
Our Meri Takhleeq Ka Hasil Azza Khane Mein Hai.
Nooh Kashti Le Ke Sheh Ka Noha Parhtay Aate Hain.
Har Taraf Tofaan Hai Our Sahil Azza Khane Mein.

2
Aye Musalmaan Dar Badar Bhatkay Ga Kab Tak? Aa Idhar.
Dhondta Hai Jis Ko Aye Ghafil Azza Khane Mein Hai.
Rashk Kar Apne Muqadar Par Azadar-e-Hussain.
Dekh Khud Ko Hai Tu Iss Qabil Azza Khane Mein Hai.

3
Har Alam Ke Panjay Mein Batla Rahi Hai Roshni.
Sorat-e-Ghazi Mah-e-Kamil Azza Khane Mein Hai.
Mushkilon Mein Ro Liya Mushkil Kusha Ke Lal Par.
Hal Hoi Aa Kar Meri Mushkil Azza Khane Mein Hai.

4
Roney Walay Ja Chukay Sab Sheh Ko Ro Ro Kar Magar.
Sham Se Aaya Howa Mehmil Azza Khane Mein Hai.
Rab e Kaba Ki Qasam Mehshar Mein Ho Ga Kamyab.
Jis Ka Ik Aanso Asad Shamil Azza Khane Mein Hai.
