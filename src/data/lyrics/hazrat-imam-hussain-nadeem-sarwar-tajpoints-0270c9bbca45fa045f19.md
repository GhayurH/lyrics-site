1
Hazrat Imam Hussain Nadeem Sarwar
Hazrat Imam Hussain Aleyhis Salam.
Daein Hasan Baein Jarri Pusht Panahi Ko Nabi.
Sar Pe Khudawand-e-Jali Seena Sipar Zahra Ali.

2
Mere Maula Ko Muyassar Hai Khazana Kaisa.
Inn Ka Rutba Hai Imamo Mein Shahana Kaisa.
Kaisay Kaisay Hain Naseebon Mein Ujalay Inn Ke.
Mere Aaqa Ko Mila Hai Yeh Gharana Kaisa.

3
Marhaba Marhaba Mustafa Ki Baat Mein.
Ba Khuda Ba Khuda Sari Kainat Mein.
Gaunj Raha Hai Bas Aik Naam.
Hazrat Imam Hussain Alh Aslam.

4
Lakhon Darood Aap Pe Lakhon Salam.
ALLAH Ne Kya Khoob Sanwara Hai.
Sarkar Ke Aangan Mein Utara Hai.
Har Qaum Ki Aankhon Ka Sitara Hai.

5
Zindah Hain Hussain Oss Ka Hai Kirdar Bhi Zindah.
Kirdar Jo Zindah Hai Tu Maiyar Bhi Zindah.
Maiyar Jo Zindah Hai Tu Afkar Bhi Zindah.
Afkar Jo Zindah Tu Azadar Bhi Zindah.

6
Ibtida Ibtida Mustafa Ke Doosh Par.
Inteha Inteha Naizay Par Buland Sar.
Qadam Qadam Nagar Nagar Ya Hussain.
Dilam Dilam Nazar Nazar Ya Hussain.

7
Sadaqaton Ki Rahguzar Ya Hussain.
Kaho Kaho Ba Chashm-e-Tar Ya Hussain.
Tareekh Mein Iss Jaisa Na Insan Miley Ga.
Aisa Na Koi Ashiq-e-Rehman Miley Ga.

8
Na Aisa Koi Shah-e-Shahedan Miley Ga.
Na Aisa Koi Sabar Ka Sultan Miley Ga.
LaIlaha LaIlaha LaIlaha Ka Pasbaan.
Dein Panah Dein Panah Mustafa Ka Tarjumaan.

9
Har Dil Mein Gham-e-Syed-e-Abrar Miley Ga.
Har Door Mein Mazloom Ka Ghamkhuwar Miley Ga.
Har Qaum Mein Shabeer Ka Zawwar Miley Ga.
Har Mulk Mein Maula Ka Azadar Miley Ga.

10
Har Jagah Har Jagah Momino Ke Darmiyaan.
Hai Sada Hai Sada Dharkano Ke Darmiyaan.
Hathoon Mein Alam Thamay Woh Aata Hai Azadar.
Haan Majlis-o-Matam Mein Hi Rehta Hai Azadar.

11
Maa Baap Hussaini Hoon Tu Banta Hai Azadar.
Maula Yeh Tumhara Hai Tumhara Hai Azadar.
Silsila Silsila Yeh Azza Ka Silsila.
Rabta Rabta Karbala Se Rabta.

12
Shabeer Ka Dil Dekhey Zarra Dekhey Zamana.
Wallah Kaha Tha Jo Wohi Kar Ke Dikhaya.
Khushnodi-e-Khaliq Mein Bharay Ghar Ko Lutaya.
Naizay Pe Bhi Aaya Tu Ossi Shan Se Aaya.

13
Badshah Badshah Hai Dilon Ka Badshah.
Cha Gaya Cha Gaya Do Jahan Pe Cha Gaya.
Iss Shan Se Maqtal Mein Qadam Rakhta Hai Shabeer.
Kab Jan Ki Parwah Kiya Karta Hai Shabeer.

14
Darta Hai Na Jhukta Hai Na Ghabrata Hai Shabeer.
Lakhon Bhi Mukhalif Hoon Tu Sach Kehta Hai Shabeer.
Hosla Hosla Shah-e-Dein Ka Hosla.
Sun Sada Sun Sada Do Jahan Ki Hai Sada.

15
Aao Karein Hum Sheh-e-Abrar Ka Matam.
Islam Ke Mohsin Ka Madadgar Ka Matam.
Asghar Ali Akbar Ka Our Ansar Ka Matam.
Zainab Ka Sakina Ka Alamdar Ka Matam.

16
Be Khata Be Khata Hai Ghareeb-e-Syeda.
Hai Bapa Hai Bapa Tera Gham Jagah Jagah.
Haye Sheh-e-Mashreqain Aye Dil-e-Zahra Ke Chain.
Ya Mere Maula Hussain, Hazrat Imam Hussain.

17
Aali Maqam Hussain Hussain, Tum Pe Salam Hussain Hussain.
Hazrat Imam Hussain Hussain, Hazrat Imam Hussain Hussain.
Haye Sheh-e-Karbala Aap Ka Ghar Lut Gaya Wadah Wafa Kar Diya.
Shah-e-Zaman, Zakhmi Badan, Tashna Dahan.
