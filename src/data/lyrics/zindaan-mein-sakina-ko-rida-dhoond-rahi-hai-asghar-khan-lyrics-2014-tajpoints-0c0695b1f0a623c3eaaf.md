1
Zindaan Mein Sakina Ko Rida Dhoond Rahi Hai Asghar Khan.
Pareshaan Qaid Mein Teri Rida Hai.
Sakina Tu Kahan Hai.
Zindaan Mein Sakina Ko Rida Dhond Rahi Hai.
Mehshar Ke Liye Apni Baqa Dhond Rahi Hai.
Ta Haal Aseeri Mein Koi Sar Hai Barehna.
Nanhi Se Rida Jis Ka Pata Dhond Rahi Hai.
Shano Se Katay Hathoon Mein Thamay Hoye Pani.
Go Ab Bhi Tujhey Roh-e-Wafa Dhond Rahi Hai.
Dar Dar Pe Bhatakti Hoi Sharminda Rehai.
Rasi Mein Bandha Kis Ka Gala Dhond Rahi Hai.
Hai Jis Ki Saza Mein Yeh Hashar Tak Ki Aseeri.
Tareekh Sakina Ki Khata Dhond Rahi Hai.
Pehne Hoye Hathoon Mein Rasan Aaj Bhi Nadir.
Masooma Rehai Ki Sada Dhond Rahi Hai.
