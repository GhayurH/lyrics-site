Qabar-e-Nabi Te Ro Ro Ke Fizza Wain Sonandi Aye.
Sun Nabiyaan De Sardar Nabi Teri Dhi Darbar Payi Jandi Aye.

1
Darbariyaan Rasiyaan Ban Ke Qaid Kita Aye Ali Nu.
Teri Zakhmi Dhi Haye Wadh Wadh Ke Payi Sir Da Taj Bachandi Aye.
Jis Dhi Nu Tusi Izataan Nal Maa Keh Ke Bulanday Si.
Aj Oh Zahra Wich Yasrab De Ummat Taun Durray Khandi Aye.

2
Jibreel V Chokhat Chum Ke Langda Si Jis Dar Choon.
Sultan Nabi Ummat Teri Oss Dar Te Aag Payi Landi Aye.
Tatheer Di Aayat Jis De Sach Diyaan Qasmaan Khaway.
Wich Masjid Oh Apne Sachay Howan Diyaan Qasmaan Khandi Aye.

3
Atharaan Baras Di Zahra Darbar Choon Ainj Aayi.
Ke Puchdiyaan Ne Sab Aurtaan Keh Aye Kaun Zaeefa Aandi Aye.
Kamb Gayi Qabar Muhammad Di Saqlain Aye Gal Sun Ke.
Bohay Hethaan Odhi Ladli Aye Utton Bheer Payi Langdi Jandi Aye.
