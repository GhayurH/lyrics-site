Fiza Mein Dard Hai Fogaan Hai
Ali Ki Aakhri Azaan Hai.

1
Yeh Kaisi Fajar Ki Ghari Hai.
Zameen Faryad Kar Rahi Hai.
Bohat Tareek Aasmaan Hai.

2
Nigahein Ro Rahi Hain Paihum.
Hawaein Kar Rahi Hain Matam.
Ali Ke Soag Mein Jahan Hai.

3
Haram Be Haal Ho Rahe Hain.
Sabhi Ghut Ghut Ke Ro Rahe Hain.
Ajab Takleef Ka Samaa Hai.

4
Yeh Khilqat Ki Amma Hai Logo.
Yeh Haider Ka Makaan Hai Logo.
Jahan Chayi Hoi Khizan Hai.

5
Kabhi Kofay Ne Na Suni Woh.
Magar Ik Bar Phir Hoi Woh.
Hoi Karbal Mein Jo Azaan Hai.

6
Ali Akbar Ne Jab Azaan Di.
Tu Arz-e-Karbala Pukari.
Yeh Meri Zeest Ka Nishaan Hai.

7
Dil-e-Shah-e-Zaman Ki Ghurbat.
Shabeeh e Mustafa Ki Rukhsat.
Kara Kitna Yeh Imtehaan Hai?.

8
Girey Maidan Mein Jo Akbar.
Tu Ghutno Chal Ke Aaye Sarwar.
Pukarey Lal Tu Kahan Hai?.

9
Pisar Ke Pas Jab Woh Pohanchay.
Tarap Kar Shah-e-Deein Pukarey.
Aye Dasht-e-Gham Yeh Meri Jaan Hai.

10
Kisay Ja Kar Padar Bataye.
Mere Kariyal Jawaan Ke Haye.
Kalejay Mein Lagi Sina Hai.

11
Jawaan Ki Lash Jab Uthai.
Tu Ghazi Ko Pukarey Bhai.
Kamar Tooti Hai Tu Kahan Hai?.

12
Sitam Ki Inteha Hai Ahmad.
Jala Hai Khaima e Muhammad.
Keh Jis Ka Har Taraf Dhowaan Hai.
