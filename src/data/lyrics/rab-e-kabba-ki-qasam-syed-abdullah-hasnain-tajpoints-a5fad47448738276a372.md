Rab E Kabba Ki Qasam Syed Abdullah Hasnain.

1
Uneeswin Ki Subha Ghar Se Ali Jane Lagay.
Lipat Ke Unn Se Parinday Ba Gham Chilaney Lagay.
Jo Waar Qatil-e-Be Nasal Ne Ali Pe Kiya.
Tu Maula Halat-e-Sajdah Mein Yeh Farmane Lagay.
Fuztu Berab Al-Kaba.
Rab-e-Kaba Ki Qasam Main Kamyab Howa.

2
Mera Rab Mujh Ko Hamesha Hi Boland Hi Rakhta.
Mera Dushman Hai Zamane Mein Past Hi Rehta.
Mere Rutbay Ko Bashar Koi Nahi Jan Saka.
Mere Aousaf Nabi Jane Ya Phir Jane Khuda.
Na Miley Jis Ko Khuda Oss Ko Mil Nahi Sakta.
Main Hoon Ik Raaz Khuda Ka Jo Khul Nahi Sakta.

3
Main Ne Har Door Mein Islam Ki Nusrat Ki Hai.
Main Ne Faqoon Mein Bhi Logon Pe Sakhawat Ki Hai.
Main Ne Dushman Pe Bhi Har Waqt Inayat Ki Hai.
Main Ne Makhlooq-e-Khuda Se Youn Muhabbat Ki Hai.
Jo Bhi Aata Hai Mere Dar Pe Atta Karta Hoon.
Main Zamane Se Hamesha Yeh Kaha Karta Hoon.

4
Aab-e-Kausar Main Jisay Chahon Pila Sakta Hoon.
Izn-e-Khaliq Se Main Murdoon Ko Jila Sakta Hoon.
Main Jo Chahon Tu Zamane Ko Hila Sakta Hoon.
Main Tu Magrib Ko Bhi Mashriq Se Mila Sakta Hoon.
Main Yadullah Bhi Lisan ALLAH Bhi Ain ALLAH Bhi.
Main Hoon Wajah ALLAH Bhi Our Main Hoon Wali ALLAH Bhi.

5
Main Hoon Ik Shair-e-Jali Haider-e-Karrar Bhi Hoon.
Apne Aaqa Ki Judai Mein Ashkbar Bhi Hoon.
Apni Zojah Ki Musibat Pe Azadar Bhi Hoon.
Main Hoon Mazloom Bhi Our Sahib-e-Talwar Bhi Hoon.
Qatal-e-Mohsin Bara Sabar Dikhaya Main Ne.
Jo Kiya Wadah Nabi Se Woh Nibhaya Main Ne.

6
Mere Bachoon Ko Mere Gham Mein Rulanay Walo.
Mujh Ko Pardais Mein Iss Tarha Satanay Walo.
Mere Sar Se Youn Mera Khon Bahane Walo.
Main Howa Kamyab Sun Lo Zamane Walo.
 Waheed-o- Abdullah They Mehway Ibadat Maula.
Our Yehi Kehte Rahe Waqt-e-Shahadat Maula.
