Laila Duain Kijiye Jeeta Rahay Akbar Syed Raza Abbas Zaidi

1
Marne Ke Liye Jab Chaley Maidan Mein Akbar.
Pichey Chaley Girtay Hoye Uthtey Hoye Sarwar.
Jab Door Howa Shah Ki Aankhon Se Woh Dilbar.
Laut Aaye Kaha Madar-e-Akbar Se Yeh Ro Kar.
Laila Duaein Kijiye Jeeta Rahe Akbar.
Betay Ke Haq Mein Maa Ki Dua Rad Nahi Hoti.

2
Rukhsat Ki Ghari Yaad Hai Tum Bhi Tu Wahin Thi.
Akbar Ke Kalejay Se Nazar Hatti Nahi Thi.
Laila Hamarey Lal Ka Nazuk Sa Hai Seena.
Seene Pe Na Beta Mera Khaye Koi Barchi.
Iss Baat Pe Shabeer Ka Dil Phatta Hai Gham Se.
Aisay Tu Kabhi Door Na Akbar Hoye Hum Se.

3
Akbar Ko Jeeta Dekh Kar Hum Jeetay Rahe Hain.
Akbar Ko Marta Dekh Kar Mar Jaein Gay Hum Bhi.
Main Bhool Nahi Paon Ga Ahsan Tumhara.
Iss Alam-e-Ghurbat Mein Do Tum Sath Hamara.
Akbar Mera Jawan Our Hum Par Hai Zaeefi.
Hum Se Tu Jawaan Lal Ki Maiyyat Na Uthay Gi.

4
Ab Jald Dua Ke Liye Hathoon Ko Utha Lo.
Aye Madar-e-Akbar Meri Zainab Ko Bacha Lo.
Atharah Saal Oss Ne Hi Akbar Ko Hai Pala.
Kariyal Jawaan Ki Lash Woh Na Dekh Sakay Gi.
Sehrey Ke Sajane Ka Jo Arman Hai Laila.
Arman Hi Reh Jaye Ga Akbar Jo Na Aaya.

5
Akbar Ko Apne Hath Se Hum Dulha Banaein.
Atharwin Mannat Bhi Tu Hum Ko Hai Barhani.
Aawaz Yehi Aati Hai Sughra Ki Watan Se.
Ik Bar Mila De Khuda Bhai Ko Behan Se.
Akbar Se Mil Na Payi Tu Mar Jaye Gi Sughra.
Maloom Hai Tum Ko Meri Beemar Hai Beti.

6
Yeh Kehte Hoye Laila Ne Hathoon Ko Uthaya.
Jis Tarha Se Yaqoob Ko Yousuf Se Milaya.
Akbar Ko Aik Bar Tu Sarwar Se Mila De.
Shabeer Ki Yeh Baat Main Ab Sun Na Sakoon Gi.
Laut Aaya Jo Maidan Se Shabeer Ka Dilbar.
Pyasa Hoon Bohat Baap Se Kehne Lagay Akbar.

7
Pani Ki Baat Aayi Tu Roney Lagay Maula.
Khaimay Ki Simt Dekh Kar Aawaz Yehi Di.
 Zeeshan-o- Raza Woh Jawaan Phir Ran Ko Sidhara.
Seene Pe Sina Khayi Tu Baba Ko Pukara.
Pohanchay Jawaan Ke Pas Jab Rotay Hoye Sarwar.
Barchi Yehi Kehte Hoye Seene Se Nikali.
