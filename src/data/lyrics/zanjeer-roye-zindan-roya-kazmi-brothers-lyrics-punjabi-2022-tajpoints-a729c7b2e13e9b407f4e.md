1
Zanjeer Roye Zindan Roya Kazmi Brothers.
Zanjeer Roye Zindan Roya Haye Ujar Gaya Baghdad Aye.
Khulay Waal Atharaan Dhiyaan De Hoya Aynj Kazim Aazad Aye.
Jadon Jigar De Tukray Jhanrday Hin Bara Yaad Karenda Aye Dhiyaan Nu.
Be Watan Musafir Qaidi Di Aj Konr Sunrey Faryad Aye.
Mil Zehar Gaya Aye Main Bachraan Nahi Hunr Dar Zindan Da Khol Dewo.
Hik Waar Main Jendiyaan Mil Aawaan Meri Baon Choti Aoulad Aye.
Sarwar De Satwein Waris Nu Aynj Miliya Ajar Risalat Da.
Chaya Maiyyat V Aa Mazdoraan Ne Rah Wekhay Putar Raza De.
Hik Putar Hai Jafar Sadiq Da Hik Dhi Mazloom Hussain Di Hai.
Jeray Maran De Baad V Qaidi Hin Nayi Kitay Ummat Aazad Aye.
Baghdad Rowaan Ya Karbal Wich Kofay Rowaan Ya Sham Rowaan.
Mosa Kazim Di Lash Uttay Aaye Wainr Hussain Di Maa De.
Natiq Quran De Warq Ummat Be Dardi Naal Wikher Dittay.
Kujh Wikhar Gaye Wich Mashhad De Kujh Wich Khurasan De Rah De.
Iss Chodah Saal Di Qaid Nu Jo Chodah So Saal Tu Ronda Hai.
 Imran Oh Waris Aanr Karey Sadaat De Ghar Aabad Aye.
