Zahra Darbar Che Rondi Rayi Darbari Sare Hasday Re
Haq Lain Gayi Hasnain Di Maa Ashab Nabi De Takday Re
Aayataan Parh Parh Quran Diyaan Zahra Ne Sunaiyaan Lokaan Choon
Darbar Che Sare Ashabi Gal Haq Di Kafan Taun Chakday Re
Haye Baad Nabi Hasnain Di Maa Haq Lain Gayi Darbar De Wich
Zahra Darbar Che Rondi Rayi Darbari Sain Choon Takday Re
Darbar Che Sare Ashabi Baithay San Sirf Dikhaway Layi
Insaf Kisay V Na Kita Ik Dojay Da Munh Rakhday Re
Ahmad Ne Jo Tehreer Likhi Ashabiyaan Ne Jhutla Chori
Mehfil Wich Aynj De Lok V San Sach Bolan Tu Jehray Bachday Re
Kisay Shey Di Hoor Zarorat Nayi Quran Hi Sanu Kafi Aye
Mimber De Fasiq Lokaan Choon Gal Haq Di Kafan Taun Dakday Re
Do Putraan De Naal Sain Gayi Darbar Choon Khali Mur Aayi
AKHTAR Haq Sain Da Khowan Walay Na Kakhday Re Na Lakhday Re
