1
Ik Ahsas Jagata Hai.
Sab Ko Rah Dikhata Hai.
Naik Amal Karne Ke Din Hain Khud Ko Naik Banao.
Jin Ko Dukhon Ne Ghair Liya Hai Unn Ke Dard Mitao.
Ik Paigham Sunata Hai Dil Ke Zakhm Mitata Hai.
Ramzan Ki Barkat Kya Kehna ALLAH Ki Rehmat Kya Kehna.
Sehri Ik Naimat Kya Kehna Din Raat Ibadat Kya Kehna.
Khushiyaan Eid Ki Lata Hai Bichrey Howon Ko Milata Hai.
Waqt-e-Sahar Ho Ya Aftari Dastar Khuwan Bicha Kar.
Farz Ibadat Karni Hai Bachoon Ko Sath Mila Kar.
Youn Aadab Sikhata Hai Ulfat Pyar Barhata Hai.
Bhatkay Hoye Ko Rah Dikhana Jhoot Se Nafrat Karna.
Bhokon Ko Roti Pohanchana Muflis Ka Dam Bharna.
Jo ALLAH Batata Hai Oss Rastay Pe Chalata Hai.
Subha Saweray Pehle Namazein Oss Ke Baad Tilawat.
Din Bhar Apna Rizq Kamana Our Rozay Ki Halat.
Jism Ko Pak Banata Hai Rab Ki Yaad Dilata Hai.
Maiyar-e-Ibadat Hai Ramzaan Zehno Ki Taharat Hai Ramzaan.
Sajdoon Ki Riyazat Hai Ramzaan Bakhshish Ki Zamanat Hai Ramzaan.
Dil Se Dil Ko Milata Hai Jeena Hum Ko Sikhata Hai.
 Mazhar-o- Meesum Aik Maheena Saal Mein Aisa Aaye.
Banday Se Mabood Ka Rishta Jo Mazbot Banaye.
Yaad Khuda Ki Lata Hai Dil Sajday Mein Jata Hai.
