Saamnay Laila De, Kuch Dair Toh Bayh Akbar

Mai Taras Gaiyan Kadhi Maa Akhay, Ik Waar Te Keh Akbar

Abbas Gawah Hai Mera, Nai Naam Lawangi Tera

Menu Akbar Nay Maa Akheya Ay, Mera Waada Hai Akbar

Marjavi Maa Na Jaavi, Na Pyu Nu Maar Mukaavi

Tere Bajon Saday Kol Koyi, Naiyo Rehni Shay Akbar

Abbas Da Ay Ehsaan Ay, Aj Akbar Mera Mehmaan Ay

Zainab Ton Laila De Tayin, Kitta Faasla Tay Akbar

Har Saal Banaadi Raiyaan, Sochaan’ch Sajaandi Raiyan

Tere Saal Athaara De Cholay, Aa Wekh Le Payh Akbar

Sughra Nu Day Ke Vichora, Gal Paa Leya Maut Da Jora

Oh Khairi Waapas Nai Aaye, Jairay Maqtal Gaye Akbar
