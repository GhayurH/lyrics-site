Yeh Janaza Hai Ali Ka Nadeem Sarwar.

1
Yeh Janaza Hai Ali Ka Shah-e-Khaibar Geer Ka.
Aaj Baba Mar Gaya Hai Shabar-o-Shabir Ka.
Fatima Zahra Ke Marqad Se Sada Aane Lagi.
Yeh Masayab Reh Gaya Tha Kya Meri Taqdeer Ka.
Ya Rasool ALLAH Yeh Jibreel Ne Ro Kar Kaha.
Aik Halqa Our Toota Noor Ki Zanjeer Ka.

2
Kyun Chalai Taig Haider Par Bin-e-Muljam Bata.
Qatal Quraan Kar Diya Kata Gala Tafsser Ka.
Gham Ali Ki Betiyon Ke Phir Se Taza Ho Gaye.
Gham Abhi Bholi Kahan Thin Madar-e-Dilgeer Ka.
Naza Mein Jane Ali Ko Kya Khiyal Aata Raha.
Munh Kabhi Zainab Ka Dekha Our Kabhi Shabir Ka.

3
Betiyon Ko Dekhte Hain Our Rote Hain Ali.
Haye Woh Zalim Tasawar Sham Ki Tashheer Ka.
Chotay Se Abbas Bhi Rote Hain Sar Ko Peet Kar.
Bachpana Abbas Ka Our Zakhm Dil Par Teer Ka.
Baap Ki Maiyat Se Zainab Ka Lipatna Bar Bar.
Dil Hila Deta Tha Rona Shah Ki Humsheer Ka.
