Kissi Pe Dosh Hi Kya Apna Bakht Aisa Tha.
Humein Tu Shehar-e-Nigaraan Bhi Dasht Aisa Tha.

1
Nazar Mili Tu Thi, Lekin Mizaj Kya Milte.
Wo Sangdil Tu Zubaan Ka Karakht Aisa Tha.
Tabah Kar Gaye Oss Ko Ossi Ke Andeshe.
Anna Parast Ko Khoof-e-Shikast Aisa Tha.

2
Woh Apni Rooh Se Bhi Tu Kabhi Bala Kehta.
Mera Tu Ahad Bhi Youm-e-Alst Aisa Tha.
Ossi Ke Jism Ki Dewar Gir Gayi Oss Par.
Osse Khabar Na Hoi Khud Mein Mast Aisa Tha.

3
Woh Mangta Tu Osse Jaan Bhi Nazar Kar Deta.
Farakh Dill Tha, Main Kab Tang Dast Aisa Tha.
Kamal Shaks Tha Mumtaz Tu Bhi Apni Jagah.
Zara Jhuka Na Usolon Ka Sakht Aisa Tha.
