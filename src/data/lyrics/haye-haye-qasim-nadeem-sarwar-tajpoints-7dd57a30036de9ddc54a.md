Haye Haye Qasim Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics
Qaidi Ban Kar Paband-e-Rasan Lashon Se Jo Guzrey Ahl-e-Haram.

1
Har BiBi Apne Pyaron Se Hoti Thi Wida Kar Ke Matam.
Ik BiBi Fatima Bint-e-Hasan Pamal Badan Qasim Ki Behan.
Kissi Ik Ja Se Rukhsat Na Hoi Tukron Mein Jo Tha Bhai Ka Badan.
Kabhi Iss Tukray Par Haye Qasim Kabhi Oss Tukray Par Haye Qasim.

2
Teri Behan Ghareeb Nu Nayi Bhulnra Tere Qatal Da Manzar Haye Qasim.
Kadi Iss Tukray Te Haye Qasim Kadi Oss Tukray Te Haye Qasim.
Kabhi Sehra Dekh Ke Roti Thi Kabhi Gash Kha Ke Gir Jati Thi.
Pamal Howa Tha Bhai Jahan Oss Khon Pe Gir Kar Haye Qasim.

3
Wich Rasiyaan De Paband Hoi Nayi Bhainr Ghareeb Da Was Koi.
Honday Hath Aazad Te Mar Jandi Tera Matam Kar Kar Haye Qasim.
Kis Shan Se Tum Dulha They Banay Kis Shan Se Thi Baraat Chali.
Bethi Rahi Dulhan Hujray Mein Aaye Na Palat Kar Haye Qasim.

4
Tainu Naal Duawaan Maa Ujari Maidan De Pasay Toriya Si.
Odhi Mehnat Kar Pamal Gaya Nu Likh Da Lashkar Haye Qasim.
Farwa Ne Kaha Beta Qasim Keh Dena Dadi Zahra Se.
Ghurbat Ki Kamai Tukron Mein Bheji Hai Saja Kar Haye Qasim.

5
Sanu Raas Na Aayi Karb-o-Bala Tu Baaj Kafan Main Baaj Rida.
Taiday Qatal De Baad Laeena Ne Maidi Lut Layi Chadar Haye Qasim.
Uth Qasim Dekh Teri Dulhan Teri Lash Pe Roney Aayi Hai.
Aye Kash Yeh Shadi Raas Aati Bas Jata Tera Ghar Haye Qasim.

6
Hoya Tukray Tukray Naal Zehar Mainday Babay Hasan Da Pak Jigar.
Hoi Tukray Tukray Wich Maqtal Taidi Lash Baradar Haye Qasim.
Har Saat Muharram Ko Mehndi Teri Yaad Mein Qasim Uthti Hai.
Dulha Dulha Qasim Dulha Hota Hai Labon Par Haye Qasim.

7
Nayi Ruk Sakdi Lachari Aye Sang Phophiyaan Naal Teyari Aye.
Uth Aakhri Wari Gal La Ke Ujari Nu Wida Kar Haye Qasim.
 Jawad Rehan Te Sarwar Nu Nayi Bhulnrey Wainr Oss BiBi De.
Jinday Sadqay Aj V Jari Aye Teda Ghar Ghar Matam Haye Qasim.
