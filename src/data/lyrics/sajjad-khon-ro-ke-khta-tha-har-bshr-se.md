Sajjad khoon ro kay kehta tha haar bashar say

1
Logo Rasool zadi,  mohtaj hay chadar say

2
1) Yaad aata tha safar may baba ka usko seena
Jab dekhti musalman na roti thi Sakina
Chahray ko chupa laiti,  zalim Shimar kay daar say

3
2) Aaye Kufay jab khulay sar Ghazi ki aqa zadi
Tatheer ki waris ko kisi nay bhi na rida di
Nikli ujar kay Zainab,  baba kaye shehar say

4
3) Sadat kay khaimon may kohram mach gaya tha
Chobees baras may boorha Sajjad ho gaya tha
Jis dum utari chadar,  bint-e-Ali kay sar say

5
4) Tanha Sakina qadaen ummat ki thi nibhati
Deevaron say takra kay baba ko thi bulati
Maar gayi hay juda ho kay,  Shabbir musafar say

6
5) Yeh zindagi to us ki kabhi na sakoon say hogi
Mehshar may bhi shafaat us ki Hassan na hogi
Ik baar gira day gi,  jisay Fatima nazar say
