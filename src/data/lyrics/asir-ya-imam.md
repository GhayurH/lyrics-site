Aseer ya imam aseer ya imam

1
Karbobala se kufa,kufe se taaba shaam bimar per jafa hai
Abid ke dil se pucho hai dard e na tamam har bibi berida hai

2
Hatho me hathkadi hai pairon me bediya hai
Shehzadiyan jina ki balwe me be ama hai
Nazre jhukae apni chalta raha imam har pal nayi jafa hai

3
Karbobala se nikle jis dum aseer hokar
Maqtal ki simt dekha aankho se khiin rokar
Be gor o bekafan thi laash e shahe anaam ye gham ki ibteda hai

4
Beemaar o natawan hai phir bhi nahi hai shikwa
Itne kiye hai sajde qaba hai sara sehra
Malik gawah rehna lab per hai tera naam tera hi tazkera hai

5
Ye kaisa imtehan hai faryaad haye haye
Ghairat se sar jhukaye kaise qadam badhaye
Nazro ke saamne hai logo ka iztemaam bazar aa gaya hai

6
Qaidi ki dastarazme hai qainaat saari
Bekas ghareeb bhi hai aur faiz bhi hai jaari
Sehra ki tashnagi ko apne lahu ka jaam dekar hi jaa raha hai

7
Ye kaunsi hai manzil kaisa maqaam aaya
Pote ka sabr dekho akar najaf se maula
Hotho se zalimo ke saidaniyo ke naam wo kaise sunn raha hai

8
Dil per Faseel usne khaye hai zakhm itne
Rehte hai yaad hardum manzar laju me dube
Abid ne haye harpal ashshham haye shaam nauha yahi padha hai....
