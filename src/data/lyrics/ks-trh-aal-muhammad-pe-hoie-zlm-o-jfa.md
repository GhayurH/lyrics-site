Kis tarha Aal-e-Mohammad pay huway zulm o jafa
Yeh bata Karbobala, yeh bata Karbobala

1
Pani hotay huway kyun Ibn-e-Ali pyaasa raha

2
1. Shab-e-Ashur ko dulha jo bana tha Qasim, hai dulha Qasim
Kis tarha dasht main woh ghoron say pamaal huwa
2. Jab Alamdar kay bazoo huway maqtal main juda, zeen par rukh na saka
Zeen say khaak pay kis tarha Alamdar gira
3. Ali Akbar jisay kehti thi Ali ki beti, woh tha hum Shakl-e-Nabi
Us kay laashay pay Hussain ibn Ali kaisay gaya
4. Laykay Beemar ka paigham jo Yasrab say chala, ashk barsata huwa
Hai kis waqt pay woh Qasid-e-Sughra poncha
5. Apnay Baba ko jo baba bhi bol na saka, ghutlion bhi na chala
Teer kaisay usi masoom ki gardan pay laga
6. Teer say jaldi vazan tak mu say khoon jari tha, lab pay tha Shukr-e-Khuda
Sajdah-e-Akhri Shabbir nay kis tarha kiya
7. Baap kay seenay pay bachi jo sada soti thi, Shaam-e-Ghurbat main wohi
Kis tarha teeron baray seenay pay soyee tanha
8. Qafila ghyara Muharram ko rawanon jo huwa, bay rida tha kunba
Sar jukaye huway kis tarha say Beemar chala
