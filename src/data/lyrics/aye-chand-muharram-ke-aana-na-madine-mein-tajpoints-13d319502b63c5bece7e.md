1
Aye Chand Muharram Ke Aana Na Madine Mein.
Phir Hashar Bapa Hoga Zahra Ke Dafiney Mein.
Yaad Aayegi Sughra Ko Akbar Kabhi Kubra Ki.
Shabir Ki Asghar Ki Our Bali Sakina Ki.
Ho Jayegi Sughra Ko Mushkil Bari Jeeney Mein.
Laila Ko Khiyalon Mein Akbar Nazar Aaein Gaye.
Dewar Mein Dar Dar Mein Dilbar Nazar Aaein Gaye.
Aaeingay Magar Aansu Aankhon Ke Naginey Mein.
Zainab Tujhe Dekhe Gi Our Roye Gi Bhai Ko.
Yaad Aayegi Maqtal Ki Bhai Ki Fidai Ko.
Maray Gaye Sarwar They Tere Hi Mahiney Mein.
Aye Chand Tera Chehra Sughra Na Kahin Dekhey.
Mar Jaye Gi Woh Dukhiya Ja Chup Ja Khin Ja Ke.
Mar Jaye Gi Ik Behna Yadon Ke Safiney Mein.
Sharminda Na Ho Jaye Madar Kahin Ghain Ki.
Aye Chand Nikalna Keh Ik Maa Hai Bohat Roti.
Yaad Aata Ossay Ghazi Roshan Tere Rehne Mein.
Pani Mein Tera Chehra Jis Dam Nazar Aata Hai.
Sajjad Ki Aankhon Mein Khon-e-Jigar Aata Hai.
Takleef Bohat Hoti Hai Pani Ko Bhi Peene Mein.
Rotey Hain Asad, Adil Hum Raton Ko Uth Uth Kar.
Hum Jab Bhi Tujhe Dekhein Yaad Aatay Humein Akbar.
Ik Dard Sa Uthta Hai Na Jane Kyun Seene Mein.
