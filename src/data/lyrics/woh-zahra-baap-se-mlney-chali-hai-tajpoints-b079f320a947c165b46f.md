1
Woh Zahra Baap Se Mlney Chali Hai.
Ali Ke Ghar Saf-e-Matam Bichi Hai.
Bohat Roti Thi Jo Baba Ko Apne.
Woh Khatoon-e-Qayamat Ja Rahi Hai.

2
Woh Bait Al-Huzan Mein Karti Hai Giriya.
Jo Beti Mustafa Ki Ladli Hai.
Jalanay Aa Gaya Jo Baab-e-Zahra.
Musalmano Yeh Kaisa Ummati Hai.

3
Howa Hai Neela Kyun Bibi Ka Pehlu.
Ali Ki Sans Seene Mein Ruki Hai.
Idhar Hasnain Sar Ko Peettay Hain.
Udhar Kulsoom-o-Zainab Par Gashi Hai.

4
Andherey Mein Utha Oss Ka Janaza.
Woh Bibi Jo Sarapa Roshni Hai.
Jisay Tazeem Detey They Muhammad.
Kafan Mein Aaj Woh Lipati Hoi Hai.

5
Zamane Ke Sahey Jis Ne Musayab.
Amaan Oss Ko Tah-e-Turbat Mili Hai.
Yeh Hai Taboot Oss Bibi Ka Akhtar.
Jo Aagosh-e-Risalat Mein Pali Hai
