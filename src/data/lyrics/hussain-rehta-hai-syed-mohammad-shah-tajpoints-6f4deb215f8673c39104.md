1
Hussain Rehta Hai Syed Mohammad Shah.
Dil-e-Batool Ke Andar Hussain Rehta Hai.
Jahan Khuda Hai Wahin Par Hussain Rehta Hai.
Khuda Ka Iss Se Bara Our Fakhar Kya Hoga.
Ke Oss Ke Sajday Mein Shab Bhar Hussain Rehta Hai.
Rasool Arsh Pe Jate Hain Jis Se Milney Ko.
Ossay Zameen Pe Bola Kar Hussain Rehta Hai.
Khuda Talak Ho Rasai Har Aik Banday Ki.
Issi Liye Tu Zameen Par Hussain Rehta Hai.
Meri Namaz Se Iss Ko Nikalne Walo.
Nabi Ke Sajdoon Mein Aksar Hussain Rehta Hai.
Woh Jis Ka Loh Pe Koi Pisar Likha Hi Nahi.
Ossay Bhi Saat Dila Kar Hussain Rehta Hai.
Bata Raha Hai Yeh Romal-e-Fatima Ka Sawal.
Hamarey Ashkon Ke Andar Hussain Rehta Hai.
Azan De Ke Jahan Ko Bata Gaye Akbar.
Maiyan-e-ALLAH o Akbar Hussain Rehta Hai.
Hain Masjidon Ke Barabar Mein Hi Azakhaney.
Khuda Ke Ghar Ke Barabar Hussain Rehta Hai.
Namaz-e-Shab Mein Sakoon Mangna Batata Hai.
Bina Sakina Ke Muztar Hussain Rehta Hai.
Har Aik Soch Ke Beemar Se Kaho Muhsin.
Hadood e Aqal Se Bahir Hussain Rehta Hai.
