1
Haye Kyun Masjid-e-Kofa Mein Bapa Hai Matam.
Roza Daroon Mein Namazi Ka Howa Hai Matam.
Khon Se Tar Hoi Mehrab Bhi Karti Hai Boka.
Taig Lagtay Hi Kaha Fuztu Berab Al-Kaba.
Aaj Hasnain Ne Baba Ka Kiya Hai Matam.
Aakhri Sehri Bhala Kaisay Bhulaye Kulsoom.
Sun Ke Baba Ki Azaan Aanso Bahaye Kulsoom.
Inn Yateemo Ki Tu Qismat Mein Likha Hai Matam.
Zakhmi Haider Ko Jo Le Jane Lagay Ghar Hasnain.
Apne Abbas Se Kehne Lagay Ro Kar Hasnain.
Hum Ko Maa Zahra Se Wirsay Mein Mila Hai Matam.
Ghamzada Zainab-o-Kulsoom Khari Thin Dar Par.
Betiyon Ke Liye Be Chain They Zakhmi Haider.
Be Ridai Ke Tasawar Ne Kiya Hai Matam.
Aaya Ikeeswaan Roza Tu Qayamat Laya.
Uth Gaya Baba Ka Hasnain Ke Sar Se Saya.
Mah-e-Ramzan Mein Ghurbat Ki Sada Hai Matam.
Eid Aaye Gi Tu Roye Gi Namaz Our Azaan.
Dekh Kar Roein Gay Sab Hujra-e-Haider Veraan.
Ghar Mein Zainab Ke Tu Har Waqt Bapa Hai Matam.
Gham-e-Haider Mein Hai Makhdoma-e-Konain Ka Gham.
Yeh Jo Rizwan-o- Zafar Uthtey Hain Taboot-o-Alam.
Gham-e-Haider Mein Sar-e-Farsh-e-Azza Hai Matam.
