1
Shehr e Raza Main Syed Wajhi Hasan.
Kyun Toor Ki Wadi Mein Issay Dhond Rahe Ho.
Tauheed Ka Akas Tu Mashhad Mein Miley Ga.
Tauheed Hai Youn Jalwa Numa Shehar-e-Raza Mein.
ALLAH Ka Milta Hai Pata Shehar-e-Raza Mein.
Main Aap Ko Ik Khuld Dikhata Hoon Zameen Par.
Chaliye Tu Marey Sath Zarra Shehar-e-Raza Mein.
Pocha Jo Nakerain Ne Le Jaein Kahan Par.
Be Sakhta Yeh Main Ne Kaha Shehar-e-Raza Mein.
Bas Sehan-e-Shifa Mein Hi Tu Mehdood Nahi Hai.
Milti Hai Mareezo Ko Shifa Shehar-e-Raza Mein.
Gar Maula Ke Rozay Pe Parha Jaye Janaza.
Phir Aaye Ga Marne Ka Maza Shehar-e-Raza Mein.
Ik Sehan-e-Ghadeeri Ke Tawassat Se Hi Har So.
Chalti Hai Wilayat Ki Howa Shehar-e-Raza Mein.
Masooma-e-Qum Ke Jo Waseelay Se Talab Ho.
Bar Aaye Gi Har Aik Dua Shehar-e-Raza Mein.
Mashhad Ki Azaan Sun Ke Bohat Lutf Mila Par.
Nobat Ki Hai Kuch Baat Juda Shehar-e-Raza Mein.
Ashor Ko Zanjeer Zani Karb-o-Bala Mein.
Our Umar Meri Beetay Khuda Shehar-e-Raza Mein.
Haan Meri Wiladat Ki Bhi Tareekh Yehi Hai.
Maula Se Mujhe Tohfa Mila Shehar-e-Raza Mein.
Haroon Kahan Tera Raha Naam-o-Nishaan Ab.
Shahi Hai Zarra Kis Ki Bata Shehar-e-Raza Mein.
Iss Shehar Mein Murday Bhi Jila Patay Hain Aksar.
Hai Saya Figan Aisay Baqa Shehar-e-Raza Mein.
Mehman Howa Jab Main Raza Ka Tu Mujhe Bhi.
Bakhshi Gayi Jannat Ki Giza Shehar-e-Raza Mein.
Dunya Na Mita Paye Gi Mashhad Ko Kissi Toor.
Rehtey Hain Marey Maula Raza Shehar-e-Raza Mein.
 Asim Ki Tamana Hai Keh Aye Shah-e-Khurasaan.
Chota Sa Faqat Ghar Ho Mera Shehar-e-Raza Mein.
