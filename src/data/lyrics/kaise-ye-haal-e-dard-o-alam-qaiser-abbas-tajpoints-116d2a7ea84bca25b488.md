1
Kaise Ye Haal e Dard o Alam Qaiser Abbas.
Alamaan, Alamaan, Alamaan, Alamaan.
Kaisay Yeh Hal-e-Dard-o-Alam Hum Karein Biyaan.
Ahl-e-Sitam Ne Toor Dein Zahra Ki Pasliyaan.

2
Mohsin Ko Qatal Kar Diya Dar Bhi Jala Diya.
Zahra Jhulas Ke Reh Gayin Sholoon Ke Darmiyaan.
Zainab Lipat Ke Roti Rahi Maa Ki Lash Se.
Yasrab Mein Gonjti Rahi Hasnain Ki Fogaan.

3
Mazloma Ka Janaza Uthaya Jo Raat Mein.
Zahra Ke Ghar Mein Cha Gayin Yakdam Udasiyaan.
Zalim Se Raat Bhar Youn Chupatay Rahe Ali.
Bint-e-Nabi Ki Qabar Ka Ab Tak Nahi Nishaan.

4
Ya Rab Yeh Zulm Kaisa Howa Hai Batool Par.
Ab Tak Sunai Deti Hain Yasrab Se Siskiyaan.
 Rizwan Likh Ke Halat-e-Darbar Ro Paray.
Zahra Wahan Khari Rahein Baithay They Sab Jahan.
