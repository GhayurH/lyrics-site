1
Kehte They Sar-e-Rah Jigar Tham Ke Sarwar.
Tarpay Ga Tere Gham Mein Shab-o-Roz Bhara Ghar.
Dil Dekh Ke Manzar Yeh Dehal Jaye Ga Mera.
Dam Jism Se Oss Waqt Nikal Jaye Ga Mera.
Dhondney Gi Ruqaiya Jo Tumhein Haye Tarap Kar.
Pardais Mein Hami Na Madadgar Tumhara.
Sunta Hoon Laeeno Ne Tumhein Ghair Ke Mara.
Phirta Hai Meri Aankhon Mein Reh Reh Ke Woh Manzar.
Muslim Tere Marne Ki Khabar Jab Se Hai Payi.
Hai Hashar Bapa Gulshan-e-Zahra Mein Aye Bhai.
Khaimon Mein Haram Rotay Hain Bewah Se Lipat Kar.
Sunta Hoon Ke Maloon Ne Chat Se Tujhe Phainka.
Galiyon Mein Rasan Basta Janaza Tera Khaincha.
Turbat Bhi Na Ho Payi Tujhe Haye Muyassar.
Nana Se Main Jab Apne Mulaqat Karon Ga.
Guzri Hai Jo Kofay Mein Woh Faryad Karon Ga.
Mara Hai Hazaron Ne Mere Bhai Ko Mil Kar.
Afsoos Muqadar Ne Mujhe Din Yeh Dikhaya.
Kandah Tere Lashay Ko Bhi Main De Nahi Paya.
Chalte Hain Mere Qalb Pe Yeh Soch Ke Khanjar.
Mehsos Fizaon Mein Nami Hone Lagi Hai.
 Mehdi Yeh Khudai Bhi Lahu Roney Lagi Hai.
Noha Nisar Yeh Shah e Mazloom Ka Sun Kar.
