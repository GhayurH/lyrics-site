Ghurbat main qatl ho gaya mazloom be watan, Main bhai bhai kehti thi laasha behen behen.

1
• Teeron pe tha rakha hua, laasha Hussain ka,
Dooba hua lahoo may tha kurta Hussain ka,
Aawaaz thi Hussain ki behna kafan kafan

2
• Ek dil pe daagh maine bahatar utha liye
Kuch daag maine qalb ke andar chupa liye
Chupte nahin chupane se daagh-e-rasan rasan

3
• Dekha hai baba main ne jo manzar bataoun kya
Naize pe tha rakha hua sar har shaheed ka
Ghodoun ke daudne se tha tukde badan badan

4
• Qasim ki laash laye the daaman may shah-e-deen
Sehre se be niyaaz thi nooshah ki jabeen
Ek shab ki reh na pai na jiski dulhan dulhan

5
• Barchi kaleja-e-ali akbar may jab lagi
Baba ko di sada kabhi aawaz mujh ko di
Kehta tha seena tham ke amma chubhan chubhan

6
• Ghazi ke baad aur qayamat guzar gayee
Pyaasoun ki aas tooth ke ban may bikhar gayee
Ek shoor al atash ka bapa tha dahan dahan

7
• Aayee na raas do ghadi pardes ki fiza
Kunba shaheed ho gaya mera ghar ujad gaya
Pardes des ghair ka apna watan watan
