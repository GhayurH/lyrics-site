Mere Bachoon Se Mere Zakhm Chupana Mola.

1
Jo Log Ali Un Wali Ullah Ke Hain Munkir.
Naraz Hoon Unn Se Na Mujhe Our Sataein.
Jin Logon Ne Darbar Mein Jhutlaya Hai Mujh Ko.
Hai Meri Wasiyyat Woh Janaze Mein Na Aain.

2
Raat Dhal Jaye Tu Maiyyat Ko Uthana Mola.
Aap Mere Liye Zehmat Yeh Gawara Karna.
Sochti Hoon Ke Ho Maiyyat Na Kahin Be-Parda.
Ho Sakay Tu Koi Taboot Banana Mola.
Sans Lene Mein Bhi Dushwari Hai Chal Sakti Nahi.
Pasliyaan Toti Hain Karwat Bhi Badal Sakti Nahi.
Youn Bani HoonMain Sazaon Ka Nishana Mola.
Fatima Aap Ki Tazeem Ko Uthti Lekin.
Zoaf Our Zakhmo Ke Bais Yeh Nahi Ab Mumkin.
Koi Khidmat Mein Kami Ho Tu Bohlana Mola.
Mujh Pe Ahsan Yeh Kar Dijiye Aye Mere Ali.
Yad Shabir Ko Aa Jaye Na Oss Ki Beti.
Mere Chehre Ke Nishano Ko Chupana Mola.
Ashk Kitne Hain Jo Aankhon Mein Na Laye Main Ne.
Aap Se Bhi Hain Bohat Dard Chupaye Main Ne.
Mere Pehlu Se Nahi Hath Hatana Mola.
Cheenne Walon Ne Kab Sirf Fidak Cheena Hai.
Mujh Se Rone Ka Kabhi Jeene Ka Haq Cheena Hai.
Khail Tha Unn Ke Liye Mujh Ko Rolana Mola.
Meri Zainab Abhi Kamsin Hai Zara Dehan Rahe.
Pyas Shabir Ko Lagti Hai Bohat Raat Gaye.
Mere Shabir Ko Pas Apne Solana Mola.
Waqt Ab Kam Hai Yeh Batlaya Hai Mere Dil Ne.
Aaye The Khuwab Mein Kal Shab Mujhe Baba Milne.
Ab Yahan Koi Nahi Mera Thikana Mola.
Bain Yeh Karte Hi Chup Ho Gayi Zahra Muhsin
Be-Khata Mar Diya Hai Mera Beta Mohsin.
Kab Bacha Hai Koi Jeene Ka Bahana Mola.
