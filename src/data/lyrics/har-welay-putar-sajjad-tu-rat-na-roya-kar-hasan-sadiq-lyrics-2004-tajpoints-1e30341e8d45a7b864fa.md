1
Har Welay Putar Sajjad Tu Rat Na Roya Kar Hasan Sadiq.
Zara Jhal Ke Ro Zainab Aakhay Chum Ke Beemar Da Sar.
Mere Akbar Qasim Tur Gaye Ne Na Ghazi Wal Ke Aaya Aye.
Ki Hoya Sakoon Zalim Ne Jay Wich Darbar Bulaya Aye.
Tere Khon De Athro Tak Tak Ke Phophi Zainab Ja Si Mar.
Rayi Puchdi Ro Beemar Taun Aye Kadon Veer Aye Rahwaan Mukniyaan Ne.
Hunr Lagda Aye Zindana Wich Aye Veran Sahwaan Mukniyaan Ne.
Haye Puchdi Ro Gal Pa Banwaan Kadon Janra Aye Wal Ke Ghar.
Aakhay Tak Ke Kand Diyaan Zakhma Nu Na Veer Kadi Hunr Rowaan Gi.
Jiday Baaj Nayi Ik Pal Reh Sakdi Odhay Seene Baaj Main Sowaan Gi.
Bas Aina Taun Main Dukhiya Te Ahsan Cha Veran Kar.
Jiwein Taidiyaan Akhiyaan Samdiyaan Ne Kapay Gal Choon Aynj Rat Samdi Nayi.
Maikon Yaad Oh Manzar Aounda Aye Phophi Ro Ro Farwa Langdi Nayi.
Haye Rat De Dulday Tak Athro Maikon Baon Lagda Aye Dar.
 Khursheed Di Akhiyaan Choon Athro Lakh Rokiyaan V Hun Nayi Rukday.
Oh Manzar Jad V Yaad Aaway Akhiyaan Choon Athro Nayi Sukday.
Ta Hashar Nayi Bhulnray Aal-e-Nabi Jiwein Dukhray Gayi Aye Jhar.
