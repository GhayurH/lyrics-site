1
Log Kehte Hain Kabay Mein Noor Aa Gaya
Loag Kehte Hain Kabay Mein Noor Aa Gaya Mera Dawa Hai Keh Noor Ghar Aa Gaya.
Yeh Makaan Bhi Tera La Makaan Bhi Tera Tujh Ko Aana Tha Apne Hi Ghar Aa Gaya.
Woh Hai Khair Un Nisa, Syeda, Tahira Jin Ki Chadar Ki Aayat Aaraish Banay.
Paya Jis Ne Laqab Ho Hijab-e-Khuda Our Fazeelat Mein Quraan Sataish Banay.
Mar Na Jaye Kahin Dushman-e-Mustafa Aaj Kabay Mein Zahra Ka War Aa Gaya.
Kun Kaha Kis Ne Mujh Ko Sunao Zara Kis Ne Mariyam Ko Esa Kiya Tha Atta.
Kaun Tha Toor Pe Jalwa e Noor Mein Kis Ne Hathoon Se Aadam Banaya Bata.
Rooz-e-Mairaj Thora Sa Pardah Hata Har Halali Ko Haider Nazar Aa Gaya.
Jis Haqeeqat Ka Ho Raaz Mushkil Kusha Oss Haqeeqat Ko Kya Naam Do Gay Bhala.
Pochtay Ho Yeh Kyun Oss Ne Kalma Parha Jo Risalat Ke Chehray Ko Parhta Raha.
Asman-e-Risalat Pe Imran Ke Aaj Sadqay Mein Najfi Qamar Aa Gaya.
Qasida Khuwan Party Ch Faiyaz Party Sialkot.
Matami Sangat
Anjuman-e-Sipah-e-Ahl-e-Bait Sialkot
