Labbaik ya Hussain

1
Khaliq hay lashareek to yakta Hussain hay
Naizay pe baad e qatl bhi, zinda Hussain hay

2
Hur ne kaha Hussain hay wahid sakhi tareen
Naqsh e qadam pe jis ke bani, raahe mustaqeem
Mabood ki wo aayat e Kubra Hussain hay

3
Suntay hi jis ke naam ko ansoo nikal parain
Chahay agar to chashma e zamzam ubal paray
Phir bhi kamal ye hay ke, pyasa Hussain hay
