1
Tumhe Dillagi Bhool Jaani Paregi Mohabbat Ki Raahon Mein Aa Kar To Dekho.
Tarapne Pe Mere Na Phir Tum Hanso Gaye Kabhi Dil Kissi Se Laga Kar To Dekho.
Wafaon Ki Hum Se Towaqo Nahi Hai Magar Aik Bar Aazma Kar Tu Dekho.
Zamane Ko Apna Bana Kar Tu Dekha Humein Bhi Tum Apna Bana Kar To Dekho.

2
Khuda Ke Liye Chor Do Ab Yeh Parda Keh Hain Aaj Hum Tum Nahi Ghair Koi.
Shab-e-Wasal Bhi Hai Hijab Iss Qadar Kyun Zara Rukh Se Aanchal Utha Kar To Dekho.
Jafaein Bohat Ki Bohat Zulm Dhaye Kabhi Ik Nigah-e-Karam Iss Taraf Bhi.
Hamesha Howay Dekh Kar Mujh Ko Barham Kissi Din Zara Muskura Kar To Dekho.

3
Jo Ulfat Mein Har Ik Sitam Hai Gawarah Yeh Sab Kuch Hai Pas-e-Wafa Tum Se Warna.
Satate Ho Din Raat Jis Tarha Mujh Ko Kissi Ghair Ko Youn Sata Kar To Dekho.
Agarche Kissi Baat Par Woh Khafa Hain Tu Acha Yehi Hai Tum Apni Si Kar Lo.
Woh Manne Na Manne Yeh Marzi Hai Un Ki Magar Unko Purnam Mana Kar To Dekho.
