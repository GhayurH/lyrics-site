1
Pukari Lash Pe Zahra Mere Hussain Hussain.
Kafan Bhi Tu Ne Na Paya Mere Hussain Hussain.
Main Aayi Hoon Tere Roney Ko Khuld Se Beta.
Mujhe De Uth Ke Dilasa Mere Hussain Hussain.

2
Issi Liye Tujhey Pala Tha Fatima Ne Kya.
Para Hai Dhop Mein Lasha Mere Hussain Hussain.
Lehad Ko Chor Ke Maqtal Mein Aayi Hain Zahra.
Phir Aaj Ujari Hoi Maa Ki Gaud Mein Aa Ja.

3
Sitam Ne Aaj Tere Ghar Ko Tujh Se Cheen Liya.
Bhari Jawani Mein Akbar Ko Tujh Se Cheen Liya.
Rula Rula Ke Tujhe Martay Hain Yeh Aada.
Main Maa Hoon Aye Marey Beta Mujhe Hai Yeh Ahsas.

4
Kamar Hai Tooti Teri Jab Se Chal Basa Abbas.
Sahara De Gi Tujhe Aaj Ban Mein Yeh Dukhiya.
Tu Hath Rakhta Hai Beta Kabhi Hatata Hai.
Aye Lal Fatima Zahra Se Kya Chupata Hai.

5
Main Janti Hoon Jo Hota Hai Dard Pehlu Ka.
Tumhare Sath Uthai Thi Lash Akbar Ki.
Kaho Tu Le Chalon Mazloom Mere Tum Ko Bhi.
Mujhe Hai Yaad Abhi Tak Khiyam Ka Rasta.

6
Woh Din Bhi They Barey Nazoon Se Tum Ko Pala Hai.
Na Dhop Mein Kabhi Bahir Tumhein Nikala Hai.
Our Aaj Sar Pe Tumhare Hai Dhop Ka Saya.
Miley Gi Maa Ki Jo Godi Sakon Pao Gaye.

7
Zamane Baad Marey Pas So Bhi Jao Gaye.
Tumhein Sunaon Gi Main Loriyaan Mera Wadah.
Sipah-e-Sham Se Tum Ko Chura Nahi Sakti.
Behan Hai Door Woh Maqtal Mein Aa Nahi Sakti.

8
Main Ran Mein Farz Nibhaon Gi Apni Zainab Ka.
Bapa Khiyam-e-Hussaini Mein Ho Gaya Mehshar.
Qalam Jo:MIR TAKALLUM: Howa Hussain Ka Sar.
Katay Galay Se Lipat Kar Yeh Maa Ka Tha Noha.
