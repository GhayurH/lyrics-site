Ali Mola Bane Dulha Mubarak Ho Mir Sajjad Mir
Sab Se Bari Khushi Hai Jahan Mein Rasool Ki.

1
Baraat Ja Rahi Hai Ali-o-Batool Ki.
Rasool-e-Pak Ka Saya Mubarak Ho Mubarak Ho.
Ali Maula Banay Dulha Mubarak Ho Mubarak Ho.
Hain Dulhan Fatima Zahra Mubarak Ho Mubarak Ho.

2
Koi Rizwaan Ko Bulwaye Kaho Janat Se Le Aaye.
Ali Ke Naap Ka Jora Mubarak Ho Mubarak Ho.
Kahan Hai Khuld Ka Mali Ghulabon Se Bharay Thali.
Banaye Janati Sehra Mubarak Ho Mubarak Ho.

3
Kaha Sakhiyon Ne Aye Bibi Thi Mehndi Nam-e-Haider Ki.
Haseen Kitna Hai Rang Aaya Mubarak Ho Mubarak Ho.
Hata Chehray Se Jab Sehra Tu Dekha Sab Ne Wajahullah.
Kaha Salman Ne Maula Mubarak Ho Mubarak Ho.

4
Nabi Ne Hans Ke Farmaya Ali Ka Chom Kar Matha.
Teri Qismat Mein Thi Zahra Mubarak Ho Mubarak Ho.
Koi Dawood Nu Aakho Khushi De Raag Hi Chero.
Rakhay Sehrey Da Sur Oncha Mubarak Ho Mubarak Ho.

5
Kaha Mariyam Ne Zahra Se Teri Qismat Pe Main Sadqay.
Barabar Ka Mila Rishta Mubarak Ho Mubarak Ho.
Zameen Sajjad Phir Chamki Liye Baraat Taroon Ki.
Dobarah Aa Gaya Zohra Mubarak Ho Mubarak Ho.
