Qirtas Pe Khama Ba Khuda Jaag Raha Hai.
Likhna Hai Jo Maula Ki Sana Jaag Raha Hai.

1
Shab Khon Ajal Mar Hi Sakti Nahi Mujh Par.
Mashhad Mein Mera Maula Raza Jaag Raha Hai.
Beemar Hi Ho Kar Main Khurasaan Chala Jaon.
Mere Liye Ik Dast-e-Shifa Jaag Raha Hai.

2
Darbar Mere Maula Ka Har Waqt Khula Hai.
Woh Kaba Jaan Qibla Numa Jaag Raha Hai.
Din Kya Hai Wahan Raat Bhi Bedar Miley Gi.
Soraj Ki Tarha Aik Diya Jaag Raha Hai.

3
Chokhat Pe Pohanch Jaon Zara Maula Raza Ki.
Tab Samjhon Mera Bakhat e Rasa Jaag Raha Hai.
Hain Maula Raza Kul Ke Maseeha Tu Mera Dil.
Kehta Hai Keh Imkan e Baqa Jaag Raha Hai.

4
Zamin Hai Tahafuz Ka Mere Maula Ka Sikka.
Bazu Pe Hifazat Ka Maza Jaag Raha Hai.
 Rehan Siyahi Se Sana Unn Ki Likhi Hai.
Woh Noor Hain Har Lafz Mera Jaag Raha Hai.
