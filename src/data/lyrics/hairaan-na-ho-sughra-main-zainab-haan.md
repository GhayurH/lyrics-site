Hairaan na ho Sughra main Zainab haan
Mainu maar ditta tere piyo de dardaan

1
Hoye vain bhare Sughra tera naa le ke
Jadon toriya maqtal nu tera veer jawaan

2
Aina ujre gharaan andar kadi wasda si
Jihnu lutya vich Karbal ral Musalmaan

3
Bemisaal shahaadat si tere Asghar di
Jihde qatl utte roya khud tir kamaan

4
Gal Aun o Muhammad di na puchh maithon
Shabbir da ban sadqa ho gaye qurbaan

5
Safraan vich rondi rahi raj Babal nu
Aayi maut Sakina nu jaa vich zindaan

6
Darbaar thayi peshi teri phuphiyaan di
Rahi saang te haaye parhda Shabbir Quraan

7
Kite safar raaku andar tere Abid ne
Bimaar di akhiyaan chon reha khun rawaan

8
Hun hasrat ae Bibi eho Adil di
Teri turbat vekhan layi kadi Shaam nu jaavaan
