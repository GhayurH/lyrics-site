1
Zikr-E-Karbala Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.
Zikar-e-Karbala Ja Namaz Par.
Aaye Ga Maza Ja Namaz Par.
Kaisi Aayatein Kaisi Bandagi.
Kiya Jaben Par Aati Roshni.
Khak-e-Karbala Jab Talak Na Thi.
Dil Nahi Laga Ja Namaz Par.
Kya Teri Nazar Kya Tera Shaoor.
Mat Talash Kar Bandagi Mein Noor.
Ahl-e-Bait Se Tu Agar Hai Door.
Kya Hai Faida Ja Namaz Par.
Majlis-e-Azza Thi Zameen Par.
Bheegi Bheegi Si Thi Har Ik Nazar.
Jibreel Ko Khoob Thi Khabar.
Kaun Kaun Tha Ja Namaz Par.
Tujh Se Bach Gayi Dein Ki Aabro.
Jan-e-Mustafa Gar Na Hota Tu.
Rakhta Apna Sar Kaun Qibla Ru.
Sochta Raha Ja Namaz Par.
Kyun Badal Gaya Aaj Yeh Usool.
Kaisay Kar Liya Aap Ne Qabool.
Kaun Aa Gaya Pusht Par Rasool.
Sajdah Ruk Gaya Ja Namaz Par.
Muskura Utha Dekho Aasman.
Karbala Hai Yeh Kaun Khush Biyan.
Kis Ne Di Hai Yeh Subha Ki Azan.
Hur Bhi Aa Gaya Ja Namaz Par.
Gham Rahe Sada Sheh Ka Zindah Baad.
Kaisay Chor Doon Karbala Ki Yaad.
Farsh-e-Gham Pe Jab Aa Gayi Murad.
Kyun Main Mangta Ja Namaz Par.
Irdgird Sab Ghomney Lagay.
Sajdah Shah Ka Chomney Lagay.
Dekh Karbala Jhomney Lagay.
Sare Anbiya Ja Namaz Par.
Kya Hasen Hai Tera Intehab.
 Akhtar Aa Gaya Fikar Par Shabab.
Shair Keh Diye Tu Ne La Jawab.
Ho Gayi Atta Ja Namaz Par.
