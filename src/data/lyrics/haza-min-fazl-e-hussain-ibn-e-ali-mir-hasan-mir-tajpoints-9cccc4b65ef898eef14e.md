1
Haza Min Fazl e Hussain Ibn e Ali Mir Hasan Mir.
Badshah-e-Jod-o-Sakha Waris-e-Noor-e-Mustafa.
Mohsin-e-Deen-e-Kibriya Hai Yeh Azano Ki Sada.
Haza Min Fazal-e-Hussain Ibn-e-Ali.
Aayat-e-Ishq Yeh Fitrus Ke Paron Par Hai Likhi.
Izzatullah Ne Kya Bakhshi Hai Izzat Dekho.
Fatima Zahra Ke Romal Ki Azmat Dekho.
Hur Jahan Jata Hai Uth Uth Ke Yeh Kehte Hain Nabi.
Sab Ki Bigri Hoi Taqdeer Banata Hai Hussain.
Jis Ko ALLAH Na Deta Ho Dilata Hai Hussain.
Dekh Kar Beton Ko Rahib Yeh Anjeel Parhi.
Jashan Aaqa Ke Nawasay Ka Mubarak Tujh Ko.
Sadqa De Apne Aqeeday Ka Mubarak Tujh Ko.
Teen Shaban Agar Hai Tere Honton Pe Hansi.
Ban Ke Aaya Hai Yeh Dunya Mein Ghulam-e-Shabbir.
Yeh Bataye Ga Zamane Ko Maqam-e-Shabbir.
Gaud Mein Le Ke Abal Fazl Ko Madar Boli.
Bolay Maula Keh Yeh Gustakhi Na Kar Bas Kar De.
Rooh Jaldi Se Mere Dost Ki Wapis Kar De.
Uth Ke Tasbeeh Yeh Phir Ibn-e-Mazahir Ne Parhi.
Kab Bhala Maut Ke Darya Mein Utarney De Ga.
Janwar Ko Bhi Tera Ishq Na Marne De Ga.
Murtajiz, Khizar Ki Manind Hai Zindah Ab Bhi.
Ya Ali Keh Ke Samaat Ko Barha Kar Dekho.
Kan Dewar Se Ik Bar Laga Kar Dekho.
Khana e Kaba Se Aawaz Yehi Aaye Gi.
Main Ne Esa Se Kaha Kaisay Shifa Detey Ho.
Aik Hi Lamhey Mein Murday Ko Jala Detey Ho.
Hans Ke Kehne Lagay Yeh Raaz Hai Sun Lo Tum Bhi.
Maslehat Hai Jo Abhi Ruk Gaye Mere Sarkar.
Keh Keh Yeh Parda e Ghaibat Mein Gayi Hai Talwar.
Shamiyon Chor Rahi Hai Jo Tumhein Taig-e-Ali.
Loh Ke Bas Mein Nahi Hai Na Qalam Mein Dam Hai.
Ba Khuda Saton Samandar Ki Siyahi Kam Hai.
Likhney Baithein Jo Har Ik Shey Pe Malak Our Nabi.
De Rahe Hain Yeh Sada Arsh Pe Jane Walay.
Sidra e Roza-e-Shabbir Pe Aane Walay.
Aaj Tu Teri Mulaqat Khuda Se Ho Gi.
Khud Se Bantay Nahi Yeh Loag Chunay Jate Hain.
Aaj Ghar Ghar Mein Tere Shair Sunay Jate Hain.
Yeh Jo Izzat Hai Zamane Mein Takallum Teri.
