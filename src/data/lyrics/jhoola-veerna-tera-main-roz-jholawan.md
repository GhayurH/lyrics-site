Jhoola Veerna Tera Main Roz Jholawan
Mainu Veran Shakal Wakha Ja Main Rondi Na Mar Jawaan
Mas Akhiyaan De Naal Kar Kar Ke Door Jholay Di Payi Halani Aan
Mere Wainr Nayi Tu Sunrda Main Athro Payi Barsawan
Veer Apnray Saheliyan Ne Hunr Teri Sughra Kolon Loka Le Ne
Mainu Das Tere Bajoon Asghar Kinu Chuk Ke Veer Khadawaan
Oss Din Taun Main Jagdi Payi Aan Jis Din Taun Main Khuwab Wich Takiya
Tere Aasay Pasay Takiya Main Maut Wala Parchawaan
Jach Bolan Di Aa Gayi Ho Si Galaan Mithiyan Taun Karda Howein Ga
Tenu Lori Dendiyan Ho San Safran Wich Phophiyaan Mawaan
Mangda SAMAR Aj Duawaan Aye Chotiyaan Walay Tere Pursay Choon
Sab Jholiyaan Bhar Bhar Jawan Be Aoulad Ne Jehriyaan Mawaan
