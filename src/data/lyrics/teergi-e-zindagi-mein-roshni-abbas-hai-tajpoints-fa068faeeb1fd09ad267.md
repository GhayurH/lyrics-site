1
Teergi e Zindagi Mein Roshni Abbas Hai.
Hai Ali Jis Ka Wali Oss Ka Wali Abbas Hai.
Karbal Mein Ba Khuda Misl e Muhammad Hain Hussain.
Our Muhammad Ki Jagah Hain Our Ali Abbas Hai.

2
Qowat e Shair Khuda Abbas Ke Hathon Mein Hai.
Foaj Ka Salar Hai Ya Foaj Hi Abbas?
Noor Hai, Quran Hai, Yeh Deen Hai, Islam Hai.
Murtaza Hai, Lafatah Hai, Haan Sabhi Abbas Hai.

3
Aashna e Manqabat Hai Iss Liye Meri Zabaan.
Bolta Hoon Jab Ali, Yeh Bolti Abbas Hai.
Asghar e Be Sheer Ne Samjha Diya Muskan Se.
Yeh Tabasum Dekh Lo Zinda Abhi Abbas Hai.

4
Haibat e Karar Ka Yeh Aina Hai Ho Baho.
Khud Khuda Ke Shair Ka Shair e Jari Abbas Hai.
Naam Hai Babul Hawaij Hajton Ka Dar Hai Yeh.
Kon Hai Aisa Sakhi Jaisa Sakhi Abbas Hai?

5
Kuch Nahi Manga Ali o Fatima Ne Haan Magar.
Aik Mushtarka Dua Mangi Wohi Abbas Hai.
Aaj Bhi Hai Karbala Abbas Ke Zair e Karam.
Aaj Bhi Karb o Bala Ki Zindagi Abbas Hai.

6
Kya Bahadur, Kya Sipahi, Foaj Ke Salar Bhi.
Bhag Kar Yeh Keh Rahe They Kya Yehi Abbas Hai.
Beh Gaya Jis Ka Laho Phir Bhi Alam Thamay Raha.
Alqama Iss Par Gawahi De Rahi Abbas Hai.

7
Naam Par Shabbir Ke Qurban Sab Kuch Kar Diya.
Afzal e Konain Teri Bandagi Abbas Hai.
Jurat e Ahmad Kahan Thi Keh Sakay Ik Shair.
Yeh Sukhan Yeh Shayari Tum Se Mili Abbas Hai.
