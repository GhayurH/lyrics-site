pyas bachon ki bhadak uthi thi pani dekh kar

sehm jaate thay magar eeza rasani dekh kar

pyas bachon ki….

kyon na rotay nowjawan bete ke laashe par hussain

zapt mumkin hi na tha khoon ki rawani dekh kar

pyas bachon ki….

kitni umeedein lagaye hongi maa behne phuphi

akbare gulgu-khaba ki nowjawani dekh kar

pyas bachon ki….

haale asghar kuch na poocha maa ne bas roti rahi

chehraye shabbir par khoon ki nishani dekh kar

pyas bachon ki….

khaak par pani bahakar shaad hotay thay layeen

ahlebayte paak ki tashna dahani dekh kar

pyas bachon ki….

kitna khasta haal tha abid ka daurane safar

khoon pisha kaate thay bar baarbani dekh kar

pyas bachon ki….

dekhle maa baap sehra iska ay rabbe jaleel

ye dua deti thi lila har jawani dekh kar

pyas bachon ki….

seenazan shabbir ki majlis may paakar ay anees

hai duago fathima ye nowhakhani dekh kar

pyas bachon ki…
