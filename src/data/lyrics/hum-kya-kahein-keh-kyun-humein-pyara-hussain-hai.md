Hum Kya Kahein Keh Kyun Humein Pyara Hussain Hai
Socho Tu Yeh Quran Hi Sara Hussain Hai

1
Naizay Pe Sar Boland Hai Ayelan Jeet Ka
Ab Kaun Keh Sakay Ga Keh Hara Hussain Hai
Hurmal Tu Sochta Hai Keh Asghar Masoom Hai
Kaisay Darey Ga Maut Se Sara Hussain Hai

2
Tareef Anbiya Se Pocho Jo Deen Ki
Keh Sab Ne Kaha Ke Deen Ko Sara Hussain Hai
Sare Shariyaton Ke Paband They Magar
Pusht-e-Nabi Se Kis Ne Utara Hussain Hai

3
Jis Ko Samajh Rahe Ho Quran Darasal
Kalb-e-Rasool Pe Woh Utara Hussain Hai
Insaan Ko Chor Dekhna Maidan-e-Hashar Mein
Ummat Ke Har Nabi Ka Sahara Hussain Hai

4
Rahib Se Pochiye Fitrus Se Pochiye
Kashti Bhanwar Mein Ho Phansi Tu Kinara Hussain Hai
Sare Bhanwar Hi Laut Gaye Ho Ke Namurad
Kyunke Khuda Ke Deen Ka Kinara Hussain Hai
