1
Aks e Haider e Karrar Mukhtar Fatehpuri.
Chota Sa Aks-e-Haider-e-Karrar Hath Par.
Shabbir Le Ke Aaye Hain Inkar Hath Par.
Asghar Ko Le Ke Gaud Mein Bolay Yeh Shah-e-Dein.
Lo Karbala Hai Ab Mere Teyar Hath Par.
Zanjeer Mein Jakar Ke Ossay Sham Laye Hain.
Baiyat Ki Lash Abid-e-Beemar Hath Par.
Janat Bana Ke Toor Doon Behlool Ki Tarha.
Qadmo Ki Unn Ke Khak Ho Ik Bar Hath Par.
Sun Lein Namazi Sare Jo Matam Se Hain Juda.
Sarson Nahi Jamatay Hain Zenhar Hath Par.
Hoon Nafs-e-Mutmain Ka Pisar Main Bhi Dekh Lo.
Roya Nahi Hoon Baba Ke Ik Bar Hath Par.
Hur Chal Diya Hai Janib-e-Abbas-o-Shah-e-Dein.
Deney Ko Jan Laya Hai Jarrar Hath.
Oss Ke Bandhay Ga Lazmi Romal-e-Syeda.
Shabbir Ne Uthai Hai Dastar Hath Par.
Ibn-e-Ali Ki Gaud Mein Ik Haider-e-Sagheer.
Baab Ul Hawaij Ka Hai Shahkar Hath Par.
Mukhtar Ki Tarha Se Howa Hoon Main Ab Faraz.
Jhola Utha Chuka Hoon Main Sarkar Hath Par.
