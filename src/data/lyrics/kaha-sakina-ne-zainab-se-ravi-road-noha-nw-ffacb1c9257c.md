Kaha Sakina(sa) ne Zainab(as) se ye batao phuppi
Hamare khaimay logon ne kyun jalaye hain

Bataaon gi mere chacha ko zara anay do
Ae shimr(la) tu ne tamachay mujhe lagaye hain

Khata thi kya meray baba ki kya tha jurm kya
Ye teer baba pe ummat ne kyun chalaye hain

Thay teer itnay badan mein Sakina(sa) mil na saki
Hussain(as) seenay pe beti sula na paye hain

Chiraag deene Muhammad(saw) ka bhuja dete ye log
Diye wo khoon se Shabbir(as) ne jalaye hain
