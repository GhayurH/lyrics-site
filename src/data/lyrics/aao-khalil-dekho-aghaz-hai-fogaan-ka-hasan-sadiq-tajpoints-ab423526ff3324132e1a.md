Aao Khalil Dekho Aghaz Hai Fogaan Ka Hasan Sadiq.
Kandhey Zaeef Ke Par Lasha Hai Nojawaan Ka.

1
Thamo Khalil Dil Ko Shayad Na Dekh Pao.
Humshakal-e-Mustafa Ke Seene Mein Phal Sina Ka.
Pardon Mein Jis Ko Pala Atharah Saal Zainab.
Do Lakht Hai Kaleja Maqtal Mein Oss Jawaan Ka.

2
Yaseen Ke Jigar Se Sheh Khainchtay Hain Barchi.
Dil Gham Se Phat Na Jaye Khaimay Ke Dar Pe Maa Ka.
Be Chain Ho Gayi Hai Subh-e-Ashor Asghar.
Andaz Ho Baho Hai Akbar Teri Azaan Ka.

3
Sughra Se Koi Keh Do Bibi Diye Bhuja Lo.
Na Aaye Ga Palat Kar Humshakal Nana Jaan Ka.
Akbar Ke Pak Khoon Se Rangeen Hai Khak-e-Karbal.
Rang Lal Ho Gaya Hai Jawad Aasmaan Ka.
