Chotey Hazrat Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics

1
Abbas Alamdar.
Kya Howa Kyun Udas Betha Hai.

Chal Wahan Rab Jahan Pe Milta Hai.

Woh Sakhi Dar Khula Hi Rehta Hai.

2

Zairo Aao Sakina Ki Dua Milti Hai.

3

4

Aik Mashkeza Bhi Panjay Se Bandha Rehta Hai.
Oss Pe Saqa-e-Sakina Bhi Likha Rehta Hai.
Yeh Alam Kya Hai Sakina Se Suno.

5

7
