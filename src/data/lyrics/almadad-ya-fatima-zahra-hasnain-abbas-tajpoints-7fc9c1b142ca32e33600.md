Almadad Ya Fatima Zahra Hasnain Abbas
Kijiye Hajat Rawai Wasta Abbas Ka Al-Madad Ya Fatima.

1
Aap Ke Dar Se Kabhi Bhi Koi Khali Na Gaya Al-Madad Ya Fatima.
Dunya Bharti Ja Rahi Hai Kitni Zulm-o-Joor Se.
Ho Gaya Mushkil Hamara Door Har Ik Door Se.
Dekhtey Din Raat Hain Mehdi Ka Hum Sab Rasta Al-Madad Ya Fatima.

2
Aap Ki Tasbeeh Ki Jis Ne Bhi Qudrat Jan Li.
Jis Kissi Ne Bhi Issay Parhne Ki Mannat Maan Li.
Mojza Khud Ho Gaya Tasbeeh Parh Kar Jab Kaha Al-Madad Ya Fatima.
Kis Ke Kiya Halaat Hain, Hai Aap Ko Sab Ki Khabar.

3
Har Ali Walay Ki Hai Bas Aap Ke Dar Par Nazar.
Hain Pareshaan Haal Jis Jis Ja Pe Bhi Ahl-e-Azza Al-Madad Ya Fatima.
Fani Dunya Mein Humein Kya Aish-o-Ishrat Ki Talab.
Chahiye Bas Yeh Azadaron Ko Rehne Ka Sabab.

4
Sorat-e-Chat Kijiye Bas Ik Azza Khana Atta Al-Madad Ya Fatima.
Iltija Yeh Aap Ki Khidmat Mein Zair Laye Hain.
Jo Dar-e-Shabeer Par Ab Tak Nahi Ja Paye Hain.
Iss Baras Ashor Pe Woh Sab Bhi Jaein Karbala Al-Madad Ya Fatima.

5
Aap Ki Khatir Hain Khaliq Ne Banaye Do Jahan.
Iss Liye Bhi Aap Ke Hain Mehar Mein Kaun-o-Makaan.
Yani Har Naimat Mein Shamil Ho Waseela Aap Ka Al-Madad Ya Fatima.
Sadqay Mein Mohsin Ke Unn Ki Godiyaan Bhar Dijiye.

6
Hain Jo Be Aoulad Unhein Aoulad Atta Kar Dijiye.
Aap Ki Marzi Khuda Ki Marzi Hai Ya Marziya Al-Madad Ya Fatima.
Aap Ke Ghar Chalti Hai Chakki Tu Sab Chalte Hain Ghar.
Rizq Dein Woh Kharch Ho Jo Majlis-e-Shabeer Par.

7
Parh Ke Sorah Hal Atta Ka Kar Rahe Hain Hum Dua Al-Madad Ya Fatima.
Mangtay Hain Haq Ki Hi Taufeeq Hasnain-o- Asad.
Hai Yehi Bakhshish Atta Ho Gar Ghulami Ki Sand.
Aap Par Eman Ho Jab Ho Hamara Khatama Al-Madad Ya Fatima.
