Gham Mein Besheer Ke Zakir Bilawal Ali Hussaini.

1
Gham Mein Be Sheer Ki Jo Ashk Bahata Hoga.
Yani Zahra Ki Dua Roz Woh Pata Hoga.
Unki Khushiyon Ki Zamanat Hai Gham-e-Karb-o-Bala.
Sheh Ke Shamshah-e-Pe Jo Rota Rulata Hoga.
Thi Sakina Issi Umeed Mein Asghar Mera.
Ho Ke Serab Abhi Nehar Se Aata Hoga.

2
Pyas Be Sheer Ki Maqtal Mein Bujhi Teeron Se.
Maa Ko Bas Gham Yehi Reh Reh Ke Satata Hoga.
Dekhti Hoon Gi Mere Bachay Ko Hasrat Se Rubab.
Jab Bhi Be Sheer Ka Jhola Woh Uthata Hoga.
Woh Bhi Majlis Mein Bohat Roye Ga Ik Roz Nafees.
Jo Bhi Asghar Ke Musayab Ko Sunata Hoga.
