Hasan Ki Zaat Syed Mudassir Mehdi.

1
Itni Fazeelaton Se Jori Hai Hasan Ki Zaat.
Misal-e-Ali Nahi Hai Ali Hai Hasan Ki Zaat.
Sab Ko Laga Tilawat-e-Quran Labon Pe Hai.
Mujh Ko Laga Keh Bool Rahi Hai Hasan Ki Zaat.
Sulh-e-Nabi, Shujat-e-Haider, Dil-e-Batool.
Yeh Sab Miley Tu Khalq Hoi Hai Hasan Ki Zaat.

2
Woh Karwaan Jo Aman Ki Janib Hai Gamzan.
Oss Karwaan Mein Sab Se Bari Hai Hasan Ki Zaat.
Woh Karbala Mein Dekhiye Qasim Ka Bankpan.
Lagta Hai Phir Jawan Hoi Hai Hasan Ki Zaat.
Har Aks Keh Raha Hai Hayat-e-Imam Ka.
Aaina-e-Sifat-e-Nabi Hai Hasan Ki Zaat.

3
Haan Innama Ki Sari Fazeelat Issi Mein Hai.
Aagosh-e-Innama Mein Pali Hai Hasan Ki Zaat.
Kya Had-e-Aytbar Tak Aata Ameer-e-Sham.
Dewar Ban Ke Ab Bhi Khari Hai Hasan Ki Zaat.
Kaisay Na Iss Atta Pe Mussadiq Ho Sajdah Raiz.
Mabood-e-Zuljalal Ne Di Hai Hasan Ki Zaat.
