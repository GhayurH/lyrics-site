Ya Zainab – English Qasida
Ya Zainab

1
Siddiqa-e-Sughra Ya Zainab
Fasihah, Balighah Ya Zainab

2
1) How blissful your birth was to Fatima Zahra,
How honored on-seeing-you was your father,
The heavens loved your fragrance, O” flower,
What honor! Allah named you. Zainab
Siddiqa-e-Sughra Ya Zainab
Fasihah, Balighah Ya Zainab

3
2) Your perseverance, courage and insight-
Like your brothers- In you-eloquence of your father lies
-In you chastity of your mother Zahra lies
Your-serenity reaching the highest of skies
Siddiqa-e-Sughra Ya Zainab
Fasihah, Balighah Ya Zainab

4
3) Are you the shining sun-or-are you the moon?
-With the love-I have for you, I”ll fall in a swoon
Your greatness, your success like heavens bloom
This slave in your love will keep singing this tune
Siddiqa-e-Sughra Ya Zainab
Fasihah, Balighah Ya Zainab

5
4) You reached the great zenith of eloquence
Without your hadith, the truth won”t be present
Karbal itself wonders on-the strength of your stand-
Against-oppression-regardless of-your circum-stance.
Siddiqa-e-Sughra Ya Zainab
Fasihah, Balighah Ya Zainab
