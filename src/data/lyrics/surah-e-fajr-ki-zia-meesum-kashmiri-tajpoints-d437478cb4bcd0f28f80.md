1
Surah-e-Fajr ki Zia Meesum Kashmiri.
Sorah-e-Fajar Ki Zia Hai Hussain.
Husan-e-Quran-e-Karbala Hai Hussain.
Panjtan Pak Ho Gaye Yakja.
Aaj Dharti Pe Aa Gaya Hai Hussain.
Fatima Murtaza Se Kehti Hain.
Ho Baho Husan-e-Mustafa Hai Hussain.
Par Milein Gay Ossay Kahan, Kaisay.
Khoob Fitrus Yeh Janta Hai Hussain.
Az Azal Tum Ho Mustafa Se Hussain.
Ta Abad Tum Se Mustafa Hai Hussain.
Bandagi Hai Meri Wilaye Ali.
Zindagi Meri Bas Azza Hai Hussain.
Kaat Dalay Jo Hans Ke Qatil Ko.
Tera Asghar Woh Sorma Hai Hussain.
Jo Nahi Aap Ka Khuda Ka Nahi.
Hai Khuda Ka Jo Aap Ka Hai Hussain.
Ik Tumhein Kho Ke Main Ne Kya Paya?.
Ik Tumhein Pa Ke Khoya Kya Hai Hussain.
Tere Dushman Pe Lanatein Karna.
Mere Emaan Ka Lazma Hai Hussain.
Bas Yehi Kehte Hain Iram Meesum.
Yeh Sharf Aap Ki Atta Hai Hussain.
