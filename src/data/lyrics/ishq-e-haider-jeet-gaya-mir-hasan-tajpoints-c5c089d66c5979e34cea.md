1
Ishq-e-Haider Jeet Gaya.
Be Khatar Toot Para Atish-e-Namrood Mein Ishq.
Zair-e-Khanjar Bhi Raha Sajda-e-Mabood Mein Ishq.
Ishq Chalta Hai Dehaktay Hoye Angaron Par.
Ishq Khud Maut Raqam Karta Hai Talwaron Par.
Ishq Ki Aag Mein Namrood Jhulas Jate Hain.
Khak Ho Jate Hain Jo Ishq Se Takratay Hain.
Haar Gaye Sab Teer-o-Khanjar Ishq-e-Haider Jeet Gaya.
Wah Re Kya Sardari Hai Shahi Hamesha Hari Hai.
Takht Shahi Ka Ho Gaya Takhta Zikar Ali Ka Jari Hai.
Ahl-e-Saqeefa Haar Gaye Tum Khum Ka Minber Jeet Gaya.
Kitne Dhamakay Tum Ne Kiye Phir Bhi Kahan Masoom Darey.
Hansti Hai Khud Maut Bhi Tum Par Bachoon Se Tum Haar Gaye.
Nad-e-Ali Jo Sun Ke Piya Woh Sheer-e-Madar Jeet Gaya.
Qismat Nasal-e-Umayyah Ki Jeet Gaya Phir Ibn-e-Addi.
Roza Jab Mismar Kiya Tu Lash Salamat Hi Nikli.
Aao Mubarak Bant Dein Oss Ko Phir Wohi Nokar Jeet Gaya.
Sath Ali Ke Jaye Ga Soye Janat Matwala.
Roein Gay Hum Par Hansne Walay Jab Yeh Kahey Ga Unn Se Khuda.
Shia Kafir Kehne Walo Dekh Lo Kafir Jeet Gaya.
Nazim-e-Nazam-e-Mehfil Hai Qurb-e-Meesum Hasil Hai.
Zikar Ali Ka Karta Tha Ab Zikar-e-Ali Mein Shamil Hai.
Haar Gaye Sab Dushman-e-Haider:SIBT-E-JAFAR: Jeet Gaya.
Ahl-e-Sitam Ke Hosh Urrey Oss Ne Ki Tableeg Aisay.
Haider Haider Haider Haider Ruk Nahi Paya Dunya Se.
Kehti Hai Yeh Sindh Ki Dharti Lal Qalandar Jeet Gaya.
Shukar Ada Kar Madar Ka Nokar Hai Tu Haider Ka.
Tere Aqeeday Ne Aye Takallum Kam Kiya Hai Khanjar Ka.
Jab Bhi Munafiq Aaye Muqabil Banda-e-Qanbar Jeet Gaya.
