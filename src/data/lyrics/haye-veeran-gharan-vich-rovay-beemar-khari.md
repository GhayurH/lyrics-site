Haye veeraan gharaan vich rovay bimaar khari
Aisi vichhri na mili phir Ali Akbar nu kadi

1
Veer vichhre na kisay bhain da Akbar vaangu
Maut ban jaanda ae eh rog dukhi bhainaan layi

2
Aa ja pardesiya veera main tere raah vekhaan
Jora shagnaan da banaaya main veeraan tere layi

3
Qasid aave te Akbar nu kahe ro ro ke
Saahvaan gindi hoyi hamsheer nu mil ja cheti

4
Main haan bimaar veeraan kivein tere kol aavaan
Vaat Karbal di Madine ton ae dushvaar bari

5
Jiyundi mar jaavegi Sughra ne jadon ae suneya
Khaa gayi Akbar de kaleje nu zulm di barchhi

6
Khair hove teri Akbar main duavaan kardi
Tu te dasvi nu veeraan lain mainu aana si

7
Naal Asghar nu vi le aave je alma vala
Phir main jaan na devaan kadi saari zindagi

8
Allah jaane kivein nibh gayi aidi kaliyaan Akhtar
Jaag ke raataan guzaare sain khvaabaan di dari
