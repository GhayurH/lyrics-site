Kaun Khatoon ye khaimon se sada deti hai
Kamp uthti hai zameen arsh hila deti hai

1
Kabhi taqdeer ataa karti hai Ghazi ko Alam
Kabhi Zainab ko Alamdar bana deti hai

2
Aik beemar ko kandhay se laga kar Zainab
Kabhi Akbar ko Ghazi ko sada deti hai

3
Ay Khuda teri Khudayi ka bharam reh jaye
Leh tere naam pe Zainab ye rida deti hai

4
Kitne pyaare hain tere Aun-o-Muhammad Zainab
Inko to maut bhi jeenay ki dua deti hai

5
Karbala ki yehi tareef suni hai Kausar
Khaak hai aur mareezon ko shifaa deti hai
