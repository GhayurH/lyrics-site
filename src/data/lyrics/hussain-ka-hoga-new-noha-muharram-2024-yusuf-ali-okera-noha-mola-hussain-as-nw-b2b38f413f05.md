Na Ab Hussain Na Lashkar Hussain Ka Hoga

Safar Bhi Zainab e Muztar Ka Be Ridaa Hoga

Sambhaalay Dil ko Yeh Shaam e Ghareeban Mein Zainab

Yeh Kehti Hii Rahi Bhaayi Hamaara Kya Hoga

Chalega Karwaan Mazloomo’n Bekason Ka Jab

Baghair Baazu Ke Ghazi Ko Dekhna Hoga

Baghair Parday Ke Karbal Se Kufa Shaam Talak

Kaleja Bibi Ka Chhalni Bhi Hogaya Hoga

Sadaayen Baaghi Ki Goonji Hai Aur Saath Kaha

Ali Ki Betiyon Ko Maaro Toh Bhala Hoga

Saron Pe Phenko Garam Paani Maaro Pathar Bhi

Lahoo Mein Dooba Woh Chehra Toh Jal Gaya Hoga

Chalaya Kaanton Bhari Raahon Mei Ussay Be Khataa

Wahin Woh Kaanton Pe Bimaar Toh Gira Hoga

Tarap Ke Kehti Thi “SAAIB” Wahaan Pe Binte Ali

Sina Pe Sar Na Uchaalo Tera Bhala Hoga
