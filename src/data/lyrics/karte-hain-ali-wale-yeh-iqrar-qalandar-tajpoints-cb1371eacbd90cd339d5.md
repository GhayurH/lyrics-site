1
Karte Hain Ali Wale Yeh Iqrar Qalandar.
Hai Misal-e-Najaf Aapka Darbar Qalandar.
Hasil Hain Tujhe Fatima Zahra Ki Duaein.
Nazaan Hain Tujh Pe Haider-e-Karrar Qalandar.

2
Na Rook Sakein Gaye Yeh Dhamake Humein Hargiz.
Kehte Hain Tere Rozay Ke Zawwar Qalandar.
Takfiriyon Ke Mil Gaye Matti Mein Iraday.
Hain Mehway Dhamal Aap Ke Hubdar Qalandar.

3
Abbas Alamdar Shah-e-Karbala Ke Hain.
Abbas-e-Jari Ke Hain Alamdar Qalandar.
Din Raat Tere Dar Pe Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Ki.
Karte Hain Azadari Azadar Qalandar.

4
Mashad Ka Karbala Ka Maza Pate Hain Woh Jo.
Karte Hain Tere Rozay Ka Deedar Qalandar.
Lein Gaye Qaboliyat Ki Sanad Tere Haram Se.
 Afraz Ke Likhe Howay Ashar Qalandar.
