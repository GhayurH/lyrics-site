1
Ishq-e-Sarkar Mein Dill Machalne Laga Jari Honton Pe Naat-e-Nabi Ho Gayi.
Dharkanein Muskura Kar Yeh Kehne Lagin Aaj Se Zindagi Zindagi Ho Gayi.
Main Ne Janat Tu Dekhi Nahi Ya Nabi Haan Magar Janta Hoon Faqat Main Yehi.
Aap Jiss Ja Se Guzre Hain Shah-e-Umam Rashk-e-Janat Woh Har Ik Ghali Ho Gayi.

2
Shoq-e-Deedar-e-Ahmad Mein Jo Hain Giray Meri Aankhon Se Aanso Woh Moti Bane.
Aap Ke Faiz Se Aanson Ki Jaza Meri Auqat Se Bhi Bari Ho Gayi.
Mujh Ko Maidan-e-Mehshar Ka Kyun Khof Ho Dil Wilaye Muhammad Se Sarshar Hai.
Hashar Mein Bakshwa De Gi Bas Yeh Wila Mere Aamal Mein Gar Kami Ho Gayi.

3
Gar Woh Dunya Mein Ya Qabar-o-Barzakh Mein Ho Har Jagah Lanti Hai Woh Barbad Hai.
Be-Rukhi Jis Ne Barti Payamber Se Ya Jis Sarkar Ko Be-Rukhi Ho Gayi.
Gonjta Hai Ba-Faiz-e-Rasool-e-Khuda Bazm-e-Rizwaan Mein Tera Taranum Raza.
Haan Yehi Faiz Jiss Dam Asad Par Howa Moatbar Oss Ki Yeh Shayari Ho Gayi.
