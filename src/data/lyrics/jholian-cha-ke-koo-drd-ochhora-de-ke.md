Jhowlian cha kay kawao dard vichora dai kai
Shala bhairan ton kadi horn ai veer juda

1
Chali darvazai tai jandrai khali vairai
Hai beemar kivain reh gayiai kali Sughra

2
Khali nai door door tak ravan
Ravan tai ho kai ban gayian Savaan
Nanai dai rozai tai kai vaari main puchayai
Kirai din aurn da wada ai Ali Akber da

3
Allah janai keevain din tai raat hondi hai
Inj lagda ai jeevain kainat rondi hai
Dadi Salma vi merai haal ton baichan hondi
Qabar wich ronda mainu vekh kai nana mera

4
Terai bajon ai keevain zindagi nibhaisan main
Asara koi nahi kerai paas javan main
Main na dunya tai hondi terai turnai to pailan
Kitho dhundha main tainu khaban dai wich aarn disa

5
Agayi Edi veer nayi aayai main tai kalli door hin saarai
Jayn hondi kaul tai Akber nu laoundi sehra
Naalai banwandi nawa chola Ali Asgher da
