Mukhtar Zindabad Mukhtar Zindabad.

1
Aye Qasar-e-Inteqam Ke Maimaar Zindabad.

2
Parcham Pe Yeh Hussain Ke Likha Hai Ba-Khuda.
Badla Khuda Ne Lena Hai Qatal-e-HussainA Ka.
Yani Hai Tu Khuda Ka Madadgar Zindabad.
Hasil Osse Bhi Baap Ke Jaisa Kamal Hai.
Taqat Tumhari Malik-e-Ashtar Ka Lal Hai.
Kya Muntahib Kiya Hai Alamdar Zindabad.
Chera Nahi Osse Jo Azadar Mil Giya.
Chora Nahi Jo Sheh Ka Gunehgar Mil Giya.
Aisa Hi Hona Chahiye Kirdar Zindabad.
Shimar-o-Umar, Tufail, Bin-e-Kahil-e-Shaqi.
Ibn-e-Numair, Ibn-e-Anas, Ibn-e-Wajabi.
Chun Chun Ke Tu Ne Mare Gunehgar Zindabad.
Ghar Ghar se Aa Rahi Hai Sada Ya Hussain Ki.
Ab Tak Sadaein Deti Hai Kofe Ki Har Gali.
Tu Zindabad Our Teri Sarkar Zindabad.
Munsaf Bhi Khud Hai Khud Hi Tu Apna Wakeel Hai.
Masoom Ki Khushi Tere Haq Mein Daleel Hai.
Razi Hain Tujh Se Abid-e-Bemar Zindabad.
Lamhe Nabi Ke Ghar Mein Kuch Aaye Hain Chain Ke.
Chun Kar Jo Tuj Ne Mare Hain Qatil Hussain Ke.
Mismar Kar Di Zulm Ki Dewar Zindabad.
Mukhtar Nama Haq Ki Takallaum Hai Dastaan.
Khoon Mein Niha Ke Kehti Hai Kofe Ki Surkhiyaan.
Chila Raha Hai Sham Ka Akhbar Zindabad.
