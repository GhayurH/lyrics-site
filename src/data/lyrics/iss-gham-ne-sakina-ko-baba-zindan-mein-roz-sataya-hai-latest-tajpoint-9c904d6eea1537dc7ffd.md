Iss Gham Ne Sakina Ko Baba Zindan Mein Roz Sataya Hai
Mere Baap Ki Turbat Dhop Mein Hai Zindan Mein Phir Bhi Saya Hai.

1
Main Hansne Walon Ke Dar Se Sajjad Ke Pichey Chupti Rahi.
Sar Nangay Tumhari Beti Ko Jab Pehla Shehar Phiraya Hai.
Main Sab Se Choti Thi Lekin Mujhe Sab Se Zyada Mara Gaya.
Main Apne Lahu Mein Doob Gayi Mera Itna Khon Bahaya Hai.

2
Cheeney Hain Gohar Kano Se Mere Marey Hain Tamachay Galon Par.
Kis Shey Ko Yateemi Kehte Hain Iss Tarha Mujhe Samjhaya Hai.
Main Pochti Thi Kya Aye Logo Chey Mah Ke Bagi Hotey Hain.
Jis Waqt Laeen Yeh Kehte They Bagi Ka Kunba Aaya Hai.

3
Main Jitnay Zoor Se Roti Thi Woh Utni Zoor Se Hansta Tha.
Baloon Se Mujhe Youn Pakray Hoye Darbar Talak Woh Laya Hai.
Gardan Mein Rasan Mati Ka Kafan Akbar Kano Se Khon Rawaan.
Zindaan Ke Andherey Goshay Mein Masooma Ko Dafnaya Hai.
