1
Jhukte Hain Jahaan Shah Bhi Tera Woh Aastaan
Abbas Tere Dar Sa Dunya Mein Dar Kahan
Tu Qamar-e-Bani Hashim Zahra Ki Tu Dua Hai
Saya Tere Alam Ka Do Jag Pe Ba-Khuda Hai
Tere Alam Ko Chomne Jhukta Hai Aasmaan
Shair-e-Khuda Hain Baba Our Shair-e-Karbala Tu
Tere Dar Pe Jo Bhi Aaye Mushkil Kare Kusha Tu
Tere Diye Jalata Hai Iss Liye Jahan
Tu Shehansha Wafa Ka Do Jag Pe Teri Shahi
Panjtan Hain Tujh Pe Nazaan Aisi Wafa Nibhai
Shahid Tere Alam Pe Panjtan Ka Yeh Nishaan
