1
Haay Sakina, haay haay Sakina
1) Zindaan hay lehad meri, kurta hay kafan mera
Karna yahi janaza,  Sajjad dafan mera

2
2) Mei baap ke sine pe, bhar cheyn se soti thi
Ab khaak ke bistar pe, dukhta hay badan mera

3
3) Sajjad se kehti thi, Zindaanse riha hokar
Tum jaana madine ko, hay Shaam watan mera

4
4) Kehna ke chhine gohar, khaaye hay tamaache bhi
Ay bhaayi agar pooche, Akbar ki bahan mera

5
5) Kok ujri hay madar ki, nam aankh hay khwaahar ki
Asghar ke bina bhayya, suna hay sehan mera

6
6) Baba na chacha Akbar, Qasim na Ali Asghar
Ik din mei ujaala hay, ummat ne chaman mera

7
7) "Jawwad" ye kehta hay, sadqe mei Sakina ke
Saadat ke gham se hay, wa basta sukhan mera

8
Karna yahi janaza,  Sajjad dafan mera
