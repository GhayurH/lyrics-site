1
Allah Hu Allah Muhammad Raza Mungla.
Tu Rab e Panjtan e Pak Hai Tu Yakta Hai.
Tu La Shareek Hai Koi Na Tere Jaisa Hai.
Tu Alamin Ka Khaliq Hai Sab Se Aala Hai.

2
Tere Hi Noor Se Konain Mein Ujala Hai.
Qabool Karta Hai Ashk-e-Nadamat-o-Toba.
Moaf Karta Hai Sab Asiyon Ko Tu Mola!
Tu Badshah-e-Do Aalam Tu Kibriya Yazdaan!

3
Tere Hi Qabza e Qudrat Mein Hain Zameen-o-Zamaan.
Har Ik Zabaan Pe Musalsal Hai Tera Zikar Rawaan.
Tu Meharbaan Bhi Hai Ghaffar Bhi Mere Rehmaan!
Tu Sab Se Barh Ke Jo Mushfiq Mizaj Rakhta Hai.

4
Pareshaan Haal Sadaon Ki Laaj Rakhta Hai.
Ba Haq e Fatima Aye Khaliq-e-Zaman-o-Jahan!
Har Ik Marz Se Atta Kar Tu Momino Ko Ammaan.
Nahi Hai Hum Mein Sakat Seh Sakein Mazeed Zyaan.

5
Tere Karam Se Salamat Rahein Bazurg-o-Jawaan.
Utha Ke Dast-e-Dua Ajazi Se Mangtay Hain.
Keh Tu Hi Sunta Hai Mola! Tujhi Se Mangtay Hain.
Elahi! Wasta Tujh Ko Rida-e-Zainab Ka.

6
Rahe Sharefon Ka Mehfooz Ta Abad Pardah.
Ba Haq e Bali Sakina Ho Unn Pe Khas Atta.
Unhein Naseeb Rahe Bibi Syeda Ki Raza.
Jinhein Hai Bakht Kushai Ki Chah, Kar Dena.

7
Khudaya! Jholiyaan Khali Hain Jo Bhi, Bhar Dena.
Jo Tang Dast Hain Banday Barha De Rizq Unn Ka.
Khula Rahe Tere Bandon Pe Tera Dast-e-Atta.
Hai Be Kasoon Ko Bhi Dunya Mein Aasra Tera.

8
Kar Unn Ki Mushkilein Aasaan Khuda-e-Jood-o-Sakha!.
Karam Ho Aal-e-Nabi Ka Hai Wasta Tujh Ko.
Basad Khalos Pukara Hai Aye Khuda Tujh Ko!.
Hai Iltija Dil-e-Momin Ki Rab e Arz-o-Sama!.

9
Ho Izn Hajj-o-Ziyaraat Ka Humein Bhi Atta.
Tawaf Hum Bhi Karein Barha Sar-e-Kaba.
Pukara Jaye Humein Zair-e-Sheh-e-Wala.
Dua Raza-o- Iram Ki Yeh Jald Pori Ho.

10
Imam-e-Asar Ka Ya Rab! Zahoor Jaldi Ho.
