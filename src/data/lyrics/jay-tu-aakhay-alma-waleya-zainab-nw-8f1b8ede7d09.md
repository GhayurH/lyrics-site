jay tu aakhay alma waleya zainba talwar uthaye
mangay shimar asaan ton chaadran nalay khaimaan nu ag laye

abbas day zeen to lehndeyan hi, zainab siron nangeyan ho gae ay tak nehar day pasay sain keya, maa aun di shaam nu jaye

jadon ghazi chareya ghoray tay, farwa nay waagan phariyan si maari kook fazal day babay nay, aynu yaad hassan di aye

maqtal wal jaadeyan ghazi nu, haye chaytay shakal muhammad di ajay waikhay nae aynay asghar tay, jevain hurmal teer chalaye

keeta umal baneen day bachray nu, paband hussain day hukmaan nay akhtar taiyon ghazi nehar utay, apnay baazu katwaye
