Aye Soch Ke Akbar De Qatil Wich Jigar De Barchi Mari.
Ya Mar Wesi Piyo Akbar Da Ya Ro Si Zindagi Sari.

1
Uth Akbar Akhiyaan Khol Zara Sughra Da Qasad Aaya Aye.
Likhiya Beemar Haan Mardi Haan Mil Ja Akbar Ik Wari.
Mainu Moaf Karein Piyo Akbar Da Baon Koshish Keeti Nayi Bachiya.
Main Aoun-o-Muhammad Kar Sadqay Akbar Di Nazar Utari.

2
Asaan Suniya Si Baon Sabir Hai Sardar Bahishtan Wala Hai.
Jadon Lash Jawan Di Chaway Gal Bhul Wesi Kul Sardari.
Kadi Digda Aye Kadi Uthda Aye Wich Maqtal De Chan Zahra Da.
Akbar De Qatil Hasday Ne Shabeer Di Wekh Lachari.

3
Jinno Burqay De Wich Paliya Si Ohdi Lash Nu Takna Pe Gaya Aye.
Munh Pitdi Sir Wich Pa Mitiyaan Roway Zainab Dardaan Mari.
Kardi Wadah Ali Akbar Nu Jehra Roway Ga Wich Dunya De.
 Qasim Oh Janat Jaway Ga Zainab Di Zimma Dari.
