1
Har Moaj Ko Seene Se Lagata Hai Aareeza.
Bigari Hoi Taqdeer Banata Hai Aareeza.
Majboor Hoon Main Taqat-e-Parwaz Nahi Hai.
Ja Kar Meri Faryad Sunata Hai Aareeza.

2
Jo Kuch Bhi Tamanein Mere Dill Mein Nihaan Hain.
Ik Aik Meri Baat Batata Hai Aareeza.
Youn Tu Hai Faqt Mehdi-e-Doraan Ki Ghulami.
Pehchan Meri Sab Se Karata Hai Aareeza.

3
Jo Kuch Bhi Mere Dil Mein Hai Mula KO Khabar Hai.
Phir Bhi Meri Har Baat Nibhata Hai Aareeza.
Paigham Rasani Koi Aasan Nahi Hai.
Takrata Howa Moajoon Se Jata Hai Aareeza.

4
Jo Shahoon Ke Darbar Mein Izzat Na Mili Thi.
Mula Ke Qadam Choom Ke Pata Hai Aareeza.
Tum Sirf Isse Kaghazi Purza Na Samajhna.
Sab Mushkilein Pani Mein Bahata Hai Aareeza.

5
Bal Khati Hoi Moajoon Mein So Jata Hai Ja Kar.
Soi Hoi Taqdeer Jagata Hai Aareeza.
Arzi Mein Jo Likha Hai Woh Manzoor Howa Hai.
 Athar Ko Yaqeen Ho Giya Jata Hai Aareeza.
