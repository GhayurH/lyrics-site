1
Sain Ruqaiya De Be Jurm Lal Maray.
Masoom Qatal Kitay Kofay Diyaan Lokaan Ne Haye Nehar Kinaray.
Hathaan Te Chuk Ke Lashaan Ronda Khizar Nabi Aye.
Kis Dais Choon Aaye Oon Kis Maa De Oh Shehzaday Quran De Parey.

2
Aa Gayi Aye Eid Hunr Te Babal Na Kool Veran.
Ya Rab Mila De Saday Muslim Di Jaiyaan Ron Haye Veer Pyaray.
Ghazi Di Bhain Dukhiya Putraan Nu Yaad Kar Ke.
Mainu Dasday Veer Ghazi Putraan De Bajoon Ujari Jiway Kiday Saharey.

3
Na Sir Da Taj Sir Te Na Kool Mere Bachray.
Gal Naal Dhiyaan La Ke Ro Ro Ke Sain Ujari Haye Waqt Guzarey.
Pursa Deiye Basharat Makhdoma Syeda Nu.
Muslim Diyaan Putraan Da Sab Noha Kain Walay Nalay Matami Sare.
