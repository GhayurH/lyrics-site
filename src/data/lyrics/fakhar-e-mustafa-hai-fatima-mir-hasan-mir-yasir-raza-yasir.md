Fakhar e Mustafa Hai Fatima Mir Hasan Mir Yasir Raza Yasir.
Fakhar-e-Mustafa Hai Fatima.
Zooj-e-Murtaza Hai Fatima.
Ho Jahan Pe Dein Ki Parwarish.
Bas Woh Ghar Tera Hai Fatima.
Aaein Gay Libas Khuld Se.
Tu Ne Keh Diya Hai Fatima.
Khana-e-Khuda Ka Aap Ke.
Ghar Se Rasta Hai Fatima.
Karti Hain Tawaf Ghairatein.
Kaba-e-Haya Hai Fatima.
Jo Hai Eliya-e-Do Jahan.
Oss Ki Ahliya Hai Fatima.
Muskura Ke Bolay Murtaza.
Meri Humnawa Hai Fatima.
Ab Bhi Mushkilon Mein Mojzan.
Tera Mojza Hai Fatima.
Kurta Shair Pe Jachay Na Kyun.
Aap Ne Siyya Hai Fatima.
Do Jahan Mein Lanati Hai Woh.
Jis Se Tu Khafa Hai Fatima.
:YASIR-E-: Hunar Ka Yeh Hunar.
Aap Ki Atta Hai Fatima.
