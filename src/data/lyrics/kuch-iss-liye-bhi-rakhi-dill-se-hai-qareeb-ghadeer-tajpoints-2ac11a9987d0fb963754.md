1
Kuch Iss Liye Bhi Rakhi Dill Se Hai Qareeb Ghadeer.
Mareez-e-Hijar Ki Khatir Hai Jo Tabeeb Ghadeer.
Aye Sheikh Tera Muqadar Hai Tu Saqifayi.
Hamara Bakht Keh Hum Ko Hoi Naseeb Ghadeer.

2
Yeh Jibraeel Ne Khum Par Kaha Muhammad Se.
Khuda Ne Bhaija Hai Paigham Aye Habib, Ghadeer.
Jo Korh Bakht Hain Aalam Mein Aaj Rotay Hain.
Mana Rahe Hain Jahan Bhar Ke Khush Naseeb Ghadeer.

3
Yeh Rooz-e-Khaliq-e-Akbar Hai Iss Liye Khum Mein.
Khitab Aaya-e-Balig Hai Our Khateeb Ghadeer.
Janab-e-Hazrat-e-Qayam Ke Sath Aye Haider.
Qasam Khuda Ki Manaein Gay Anqareeb Ghadeer.
