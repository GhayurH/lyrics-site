Abbas Ke Nokar Hain Pani To Pilayaingain
Badlay Mein Duaein Hum Shabeer Se Paein Gay
Shia Ho Ya Suni Ho Hindu Ho Ya Esayi
Jo Hur Ki Tarha Aaya Seene Se Lagaein Gay
Yeh Keh Ke Pilatay Hain Inn Hathoon Se Hum Pani
Iss Kam Na Aaye Tu Kis Kam Yeh Aaein Gay
Jaroob Kashi Kaisi Hum Palkein Bichaein Gay
Jis Rah Pe Sab Zair Chalte Nazar Aaein Gay
Pani Pe Lo Aao Pyason Ko Yaad Karo
Saqqay Ki Tarha Hum Yeh Aawaz Lagaein Gay
Hota Jo Loab-e-Nabi Be Shak Woh Mila Detey
Hum Sachay Hain Jhota Bhi Pani Na Pilaein Gay
Abbas Ziyarat Tu Karwaein Gay Har Sorat
Ya Hum Ko Bulaein Gay Ya Khuwab Mein Aaein Gay
Sans Aati Hai Sansoon Ki Shabeer Ke Matam Mein
Gar Chor Diya Matam Hum Jee Nahi Paein Gay
Yeh Fazal Sakina Se Bolay Jo Mila Pani
Baba Ki Qasam Pehle Hum Tum Ko Pilaein Gay
SAJJAD-o-TAKALLUM Our Yeh MIR HASAN Kya Hain
Khud Khizar Bhi Janat Mein Kehte Nazar Aaein Gay
