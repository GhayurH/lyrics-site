1
Ya Rab Mera Har Lafz Mir Hasan Mir
Ya Rab Mere Har Lafz Diya Aisa Bana De.
Jo Munkir-e-Zahra Ke Kalejon Ko Jala De.
Hum Fatima Zahra Ki Duaon Ka Asar Hain.
Himmat Nahi Dunya Mein Tashiyon Ko Mita De.
Zahra Se Taqabul Tu Bari Baat Hai Nadaan.
Tareekh Mein Tu Humsar-e-Fizza Hi Dikha De.
Woh Lanati Hai Jis Se Bhi Razi Nahi Zahra.
Lakh Oss Ko Zamana Razi ALLAH Bana De.
Hum Sab Hain Turabi Na Ulajh Hum Se Zamane.
Mati Mein Mila Dein Gay Tere Sare Iraday.
Kya Rakha Hai Islam Mein Ho Jaye Ga Maloom.
Ik Pal Ko Agar Fatima Zahra Ko Hata De.
Zahra Se Agar Itna Hasad Hai Tujhey Waiz.
Quran Se Tu Sorah-e-Kausar Ko Mita De.
Ho Jaye Gi Tangdasti Teri Khatam Yaqeenan.
Abbas-e-Alamdar Ki Bas Nazar Dila De.
Khud Fatima Zahra Bhi Duaein Tujhey Dein Gi.
Bas Shart Hai Ghazi Ka Alam Ghar Pe Laga De.
Jibreel Takallum Ne Jo Ashar Kahey Hain.
Le Ja Ke Unhein Arsh Ki Mehfil Mein Suna De.
