Ya Masooma e Qom Ek Nazar Ho Shahid Ali Shahid Baltistani.
Aalaam-o-Musayab Ka Andhera Hai Sahar Ho.

1
Ya Fatima Masooma-e-Qum Aik Nazar Ho.
Haan Aap Ka Dar Aal e Muhammad Ka Haram Hai.
Iss Dar Ki Miley Bheek Tu Kya Dolat-e-Jam Hai.
Aye Kash Issi Dar Pe Atta Aik Sahar Ho.

2
Khud Aap Ke Baba Ne Yeh Ayelan Kiya Tha.
Iss Lal Ki Azmat Pe Fida Oss Ka Ho Baba.
Irfan Jo Tera Ho Tu Janat Mein Bhi Ghar Ho.
Har Samt Se Aati Hain Sisakney Ki Sadaein.

3
Bhai Se Chutay Bhai Kahin Bachoon Se Maein.
Har Simt Tabahi Hai Kahin Rah-e-Mafar Ho.
Hum Ilm Ki Dolat Se Jo Mehroom Hoye Hain.
Iss Wastay Dunya Mein Bhi Mazloom Hoye Hain.

4
Iss Qaum Ke Hathoon Mein Sada Ilm Ka Dar Ho.
Jo Qaum Ke Rehbar Hain Woh Mujrim Se Miley Hain.
Har Moor Pe Zalim Ki Woh Taeed Karey Hain.
Iss Qaum Ke Rehbar Mein Baseerat Ka Hunar Ho.

5
Har Shab Ko Tum Azam Ka Paikar Bhi Bana Do.
Youn Deen Pe Mitne Ka Hunar Unn Ko Sikha Do.
Har Sans Pe Maula Ki Itaat Ki Mohar Ho.
Bibi Unhein Do Asghar-e-Be Sheer Ka Sadqa.

6
Aoulad Ki Naimat Se Tarapti Hain Jo Dukhiya.
Unn Maon Ki Aagosh Mein Jaldi Gul-e-Tar Ho.
Kyun Apne Gunahon Pe Pashemaan Na Hoon Har Dam.
Jab Karb-o-Bala Jane Se Mehroom Hoye Hum.

7
Yeh Saal Bhi Mehromi Mein Mera Na Basar Ho.
Bibi Tumhein Sultan-e-Khurasaan Ki Qasam Hai.
Ijaz Dikha Do Keh Yehi Waqt-e-Karam Hai.
 Jafar Ki Zabaan Mein Bhi Atta Kuch Tu Asar Ho.
