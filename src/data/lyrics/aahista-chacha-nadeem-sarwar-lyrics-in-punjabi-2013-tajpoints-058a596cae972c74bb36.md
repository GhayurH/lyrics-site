Aahista Chacha Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics
Jab Reha Ho Ke Sajjad Laute Watan.

1
Ro Paray Dekh Kar Haye Apna Chaman.
Doortay Rotay Pohanchay Hanfia Chacha.
Our Galay Se Bhatijay Ko Lipta Liya.
Cheekh Nikli Our Abid Ko Gash Aa Gaya.

2
Dono Hathoon Ko Seene Pe Rakh Kar Kaha.
Aahista Chacha, Sajjad Haye Sajjad.
Holay Gal Naal La Wekh Mainu Zara.
Main Haan Zakhmi Bara Aahista Chacha.

3
Aye Wekh Oh Haal Bhatijay Da.
Sajjad Naiyon Oh Sajjad Riya.
Maikon Kha Gaya Dard Ridawaan Da.
Khoon Akhiyaan Choon Nahin Rukda Chacha.

4
Ashor De Din Wich Karbal De.
Maiday Qatal Bhira Thi Gaye Sare.
Chachey De Bazu Qalam Hoye.
Babay Te Khanjar Chaliya Chacha.

5
Nahin Bhulne Safar Bazaraan De.
Maikon Kha Gaye Dukh Darbaraan De.
Oh Majmay Bad Kirdaraan De.
Rayi Sainr Lokandi Chehra Chacha.

6
Har Moor Te Pathar Wasday Ray.
Kofi Te Shami Hasday Ray.
Sanu Bagi Bagi Kenday Ray.
Ainj Sham De Pasay Turya Chacha.

7
Mainu Sham Shehar Ne Mar Dita.
Darbar Ne Choray Mar Muka.
Maikon Kha Gaya Dard Sakina Da.
Mainda Haal Aye Sham Ne Kita Chacha.

8
 Sarwar Jawad Riya Kainda.
Rat Ro Ke Baba Baqir Da.
Main Naal Bhirawaan Mar Janda.
Kyun Jiyonda Watan Te Waliya Chacha.
