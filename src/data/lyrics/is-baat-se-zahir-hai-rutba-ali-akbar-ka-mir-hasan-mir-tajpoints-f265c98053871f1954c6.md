1
Is Baat Se Zahir Hai Rutba Ali Akbar Ka Mir Hasan Mir.
Hota Hai Azano Mein Charcha Ali Akbar Ka.
Aye Hur Teri Qismat Ne Kyun Aaj Yeh Karwat Li.
Kya Subha Ko Dekha Tha Chehra Ali Akbar Ka.

2
Shabeer Jholatay They Abbas Ke Jholay Ko.
Abbas Jholatay Hain Jhola Ali Akbar Ka.
Pocha Gaya Yousuf Se Jab Husan Ke Barey Mein.
Hans Kar Kaha Yeh Tu Hai Sadqa Ali Akbar Ka.

3
Laila Ne Kaha Baithon Kis Tarha Main Saye Mein.
Phirta Hai Nigahon Mein Chehra Ali Akbar Ka.
Pocha Gaya Jab Sheh Se Kyun Kham Hai Kamar Maula.
Bolay Keh Uthaya Tha Lasha Ali Akbar Ka.
