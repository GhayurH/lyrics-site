Haye Sajjad
Sajjad Sajjad,

1
Tu rat rona je chad de vain
May vi dhup nu chad javangi

2
Tu khun rona jo chor de,
Me saaye me aa jaaungi,

3
1. Sajjad zara insaaf te kar,
Ay hukm na de israar na kar
Ayo soch ke he phat vainda jigar,
Meda ujar gaya he wasda ghar
Nith rowangi hun dhup vich me
Vich dhup te hi mar javange

4
Tu rat rona je chad de ve
May vi dhup nu chad javangi

5
2. Mai takiya ae bay goro kafan,
Dhup vich baba mazloom tera
Nae dhup choo uth sagdi bachra,
Dhup ban gaye aey maqsoom mera
Hin jad tak saan vich, saan mere
Vich dhup te sog manawange

6
Tu rat rona je chad de vain
May vi dhup nu chad javangi

7
3. Maraa gaya qaatil Asghar ka,
Asghar to nahi milne waala
Wo zakhmi gala jalta jula,
Nasoor hay meray seenay ka
Tu dil ko pakarna, chor de
Me saaye me aa jaaungi

8
Tu rat rona je chad de ve
May vi dhup nu chad javangi

9
4. Jo laashe Hussain te kita me,
O wada thor nibana he
Majboor na kar meku putar mera,
Meku saaya raas na aana he
Muk jaane oodon, saa meray
Jedo chaave putar me jaavange

10
Tu rat rona je chad de ve
May vi dhup nu chad javangi

11
5. Haiwan ko bhi zibha hote huwe,
Tu dekh nahi sakta beta
Qassab zabiha ko apne,
Tuje dekh ke dalte hai parda
Ghash kha kar girna, chor de
Me saaye me ajaaunge

12
Tu rat rona je chad de ve
May vi dhup nu chad javangi

13
6. Nai dhup jo uthi jekar me,
Tera bhi rona nai mukeya
Aj tey putar Sajjad tera,
Akhiyan da paani nay sukeya
Tere wagde athru, jay thal gaye
Me vee chaan te bejavange

14
Tu rat rona je chad de ve
May vi dhup nu chad javangi

15
7. Mere waali tum shahid rehna,
Mazboot abhi tak waada hai
Saye ki taraf jo leke challa,
Sajjad wo mera lasha he
Zainab mera shaana, chor de
Me saaye me aa jaaungi

16
Tu rat rona je chad de ve
May vi dhup nu chad javangi
