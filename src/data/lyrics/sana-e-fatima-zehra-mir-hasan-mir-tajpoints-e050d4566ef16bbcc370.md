1
Sana e Fatima Zehra Mir Hasan Mir
Likh Fatima Zahra Ki Sana Our Zyada.
Kar De Ga Karam Tujh Pe Khuda Our Zyada.
Kausar Ko Atta Kar Ke Sada Di Yeh Khuda Ne.
Banta Hai Khadija Ka Sila Our Zyada.
Maa Ne Kaha Karni Hai Mujhe Majlis-e-Zahra.
Beta Zara Mehnat Se Kama Our Zyada.
Aasar e Nadamat Jo Parhey Hur Ki Jabeen Par.
Shabbir Ne Romal Kasa Our Zyada.
Mehdi Se Mulaqaat Ho Youn Waqt Ki Chakki.
Aye Bibi Zara Taiz Chala Our Zyada.
Woh Dekh Dua Deti Hain Hasnain Ki Madar.
Matam Mein Zara Hath Utha Our Zyada.
Matam Ki Ibadat Ho Ya Sajday Ki Ibadat.
Dil Dono Namazon Mein Laga Our Zyada.
Rahib Ki Taraf Dekh Ke Shabbir Pukarey.
Yeh Saat Pisar Kafi Hain Ya Our Zyada.
Ik Noor Nazar Aaye Ga Humrah Ali Ke.
Mairaj Ka Pardah Jo Utha Our Zyada.
Jo Khud Ko Samajhti Hai Kaneez-e-Dar-e-Zahra.
Oss Sar Pe Zarori Hai Rida Our Zyada.
Mokib Se Koi Pyasa Nahi Jaye Takallum.
Zawwaron Ko Aawaz Laga Our Zyada.
