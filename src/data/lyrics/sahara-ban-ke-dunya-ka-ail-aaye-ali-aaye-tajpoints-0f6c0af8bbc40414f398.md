1
Sahara Ban Ke Dunya Ka Ail Aaye Ali Aaye.
Rajab Ki Terah Ko Mula Ail Aaye Ali Aaye.
Achanak Chup Giya Soraj Laga Pehra Youn Ismat Ka.
Ali Ki Maa Ki Aamad Par Jahan Khamosh Tha Sara.
Bani Dewar Darwaza Ail Aaye Ali Aaye.
Khuda Ka Ghar Bana Qibla Ali Tere Hi Aane Se.
Hai Khush Yeh Deen Muhammad Ka Ali Tere Hi Aane Se.
Karo Sab Shukar Ka Sajda Ail Aaye Ali Aaye.
Jari Aaye Sakhi Aaye Ail Aaye Ali Aaye.
Muhammad Ke Wasi Aaye Khuda Ke Hain Wali Aaye.
Chalane Aman Ka Sikka Ail Aaye Ali Aaye.
Liye Jibreel Guldasta Falak Se Dekho Aaya Hai.
Woh Aa Ke Farsh Par Dekho Sabhi Se Hans Ke Kehta Hai.
Lagao Haidery Narah Ail Aaye Ali Aaye.
Utha Ke Apni Mein Diya Sarwar Ne Bosa Jab.
Youn Halqa Muskurane The Lage Mushkil Kusha Ke Lab.
Bara Hai Yeh Haseen Lamha Ail Aaye Ali Aaye.
Sajao Mill Ke Dunya Ko Wiladat Aaj Hai Oss Ki.
Keh Jiss Ke Wastay Rab Ne Khudai Khalq Ki Sari.
Haan Ban Ke Shair ALLAH Ka Ail Aaye Ali Aaye.
Qaza Ke Bad Bhi Ja Kar Lahad Mein Zikar Ho Ga Yeh.
Lagate Jao Tum Waiz Jo Chaho Jitne Bhi Fatway.
Magar Yeh Ishq Bole Ga Ail Aaye Ali Aaye.
Kayi Beete Baras Kabe Ne Na Aisi Khushi Payi.
Ali Ke Aate Hi Kaba Button Se Ho Ga Khali.
Har Ik But Keh Ke Yeh Bhaga Ail Aaye Ali Aaye.
Nahi Afraz Dar Koi Humein Batal Ki Sazish Ka.
Adawat Rakhta Haider Se Hai Jo Oss Ko Kaho Itna.
Safaya Karne Batil Ka Ail Aaye Ali Aaye.
