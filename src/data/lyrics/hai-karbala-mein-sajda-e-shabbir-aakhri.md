Hai Karbala Mein Sajda-e-Shabir Aakhri
Shabir Ki Zabaan Pe Hai Takbeer Aakhri

1
Aal-e-Nabi Ke Aise Galay Bandhay Hoye Hain
Dunya Mein Jaise Reh Gayi Zanjeer Aakhri
Bint-e-Ali Youn Dalti Ho Khak Sheh Pe Kyun
Kya Soch Li Kafan Ki Yeh Tadbeer Aakhri

2
Dastar Apni Khak Pe Shabir Daal Kar
Ro Kar Hasan Ki Chunte Hain Tasveer Aakhri
Roya Khuda Hussain Ki Aankhon Se Dekh Kar
Chehray Pe Muskurahat-e-Be-Sheer Aakhri

3
Tarpe Ga Sughra Bhai Tera Garam Rait Par
Yeh Hi Hai Tere Khuwab Ki Tabeer Aakhri
Ghurbat Habib Jan Lo Iss Baat Se Meri
Shabir Likh Raha Hai Yeh Tehreer Aakhri

4
Teeron Se Hum Kalam They Oss Waqt Tak Kamaan
Jab Tak Hussain Par Na Chalay Teer Aakhri
Asghar Ki Qabar Khod Ke Kehne Lagay Yeh Sheh
Asghar Kiya Hai Ghar Tera Tameer Aakhri

5
Mohlat Zara Si Dair Ki Akbar Ko De Ajal
Deedar Kar Le Bhai Ka Hamsheer Aakhri
Karbal Ki Khak Mein Meri Mil Jaye Khak Bhi
 Zeeshan Ki Yeh Mula Ho Taqdeer Aakhri
