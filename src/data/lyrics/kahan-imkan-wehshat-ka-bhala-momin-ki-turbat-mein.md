Kahan Imkan Wehshat Ka Bhala Momin Ki Turbat Mein
Bicha Rehta Hai Jab Farsh-e-Azza Momin Ki Turbat Mein
Fishar-e-Qabar Kya Hai Baal Bhi Beeka Nahi Ho Ga
Gayi Hai Sath Jab Khak-e-Shifa Momin Ki Turbat Mein
Hoi Shehar-e-Khamoshan Mein Azaan Shabir Ke Gham Ki
Ali Ke Lal Ka Matam Howa Momin Ki Turbat Mein
Hussain Aaye Jo Le Kar Sath Apne Sab Shaheedon Ko
Simat Kar Aa Gayi Karb-o-Bala Momin Ki Turbat Mein
TAKALLUM Hai Wohi Mahool Jo Hota Hai Majlis Mein
Kahan Tabdeel Hai Aab-o-Hawa Momin Ki Turbat Mein
