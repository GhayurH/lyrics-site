Haaye zainab, haaye zainab
Kya dekhti hai Sughra, Zainab ke aasuon mein.

1
Manzar hai Karbala ka, Zainab ke aasuon mein.
1) Barchi ka zakhm taaza, ta zindagi rahega
Akbar ka hai kaleja, Zainab ke aasuon mein.
2) Sughra nazar uthaao, Qasim ka haal dekho
Bikhra hua hai laasha, Zainab ke aasuon mein.
3) Ay deeda-e-tassawur, tujhko dekhai dega
Chota sa ek jhula, Zainab ke aasuon mein.
4) Abbas ke burredan, HaathoN ki daastan hain
Behta hai gham ka dariya, Zainab ke aasuon mein.
5) Zindan-e-Shaam mein hain, choti si qabr lekin
Moujood hai Sakina, Zainab ke aasuon mein.
6) Ab Aun aur Mohammed, bhi yaad aa rahein hain
Hone laga izaafa, Zainab ke aasuon mein.
7) Zulm-o-sitam ke manzar, sab dekhle Madina
Hai Shaam aur Kufa, Zainab ke aasuon mein.
8) Shabbir pesh-e-dawar, jayengey saath lekar
Bheega hua amama, Zainab ke aasuon mein.
9) Be pardagi ka aalam, kaisay "Adeel" likhay
Khud dekhle zamana, Zainab ke aasuon mein.
