Mazloom ka matam hai, mazloom ka matam - M
(Mazloom ka matam hai, mazloom ka matam)

1
Logo ye tamashaa nahi
Is ronay pe hasna nahi

2
Kya milta hai matam mein, kis tarha bataaye
Mazloom ko rotay hai, mazloom jahaan meinnnnn
(Mazloom ko rotay hai, mazloom jahaan meinnnnn)
Patthar nahi sinay mein
(Patthar nahi sinay mein)
Patthar nahi sinay mein, dil hai to ye matam

3
Ghar fatema zehra ka, kis tarha say ujda
Jab hogi qayamat to, yun aayengi zehraaaaa
(Jab hogi qayamat to, yun aayengi zehraaaaa)
Ik haath mein do baazu
(Ik haath mein do baazu)
Ik haath mein do baazu, ik haat mein parcham

4
Jab pyaare mohammad se, main pyaar karunga
Phir uske nawaase ka, matam bhi karungaaaaa
(Phir uske nawaase ka, matam bhi karungaaaaa)
Jis ghar mein mohammad hai
(Jis ghar mein mohammad hai)
Jis ghar mein mohammad hai, us ghar ka ye matam

5
Logo nabizadi ka, bazaron mein aana
Aur baandh ke rassi mein, darbaro mein laanaaaaa
(Aur baandh ke rassi mein, darbaro mein laanaaaaa)
Is baat pe rotay hai
(Is baat pe rotay hai)
Is baat pe rotay hai, is darda ka matam

6
Zanjiron mein jakda hai, shabbir ka beta
Maa behne khule sar thi, khoon rota tha maulaaaaa
(Maa behne khule sar thi, khoon rota tha maula)
Sajjad se pucho to
(Sajjad se pucho to)
Sajjad se pucho to, kyun hai ye kamar kham

7
Khat fatema sughra ka, kis waqta mila hai
Jab bhayi sina khaye, reti pe padaa haiiiii
(Jab bhayi sina khaye, reti pe padaa haiiiii)
Khat kaun padhe tera
(Khat kaun padhe tera)
Khat kaun padhe tera, tanhai ka alam

8
Rehan ka sarwar ka, bakhshish ka waseela
Ye nauha ye maatam hai, ye farsha azaa kaaaaa
(Ye nauha ye maatam hai, ye farsha azaa kaaaaa)
Aur sar pe khula apnay
(Aur sar pe khula apnay)
Aur sar pe khula apnay, abbas ka parchamm

9
Logo ye tamashaa nahii, is ronay pe hasna nahi
