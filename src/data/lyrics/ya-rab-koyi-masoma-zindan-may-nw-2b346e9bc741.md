Ya Rab^ Koyi Masoma Zindan May Na Thanha Ho

Paband^ na howa an, ronay pay na pehra ho

-Thi jis ko neend^ ahti Shabir^ kay seenay par

Zindan daray zindan, jis^ kay liyay kaza ho

-Takrayay no wo kuin kar zindan key divaron say

Bhai say aur phupi say, jis^ ko juda kiya ho

-Zindano say ahti thi avaz^ sisakinay key

Jesay kay Sakina ko, har zindan ro ra ha ho

-Ha ay shaam^ key galio may rothi hay khaza jis ko

Jesay kay Sakina ka, koyi na janaza ho

-Bay kafn^ usay Abid^ than haey uta lay ay

Konain^ ka waris hay, jis^ bibi ka dada ho
