Tere baghair Asghar nai maa nu chain aanda
Lagda ae jeevain mainu Asghar paya bulaanda

1
1) Raah vekhdi Sakina puchdi ae baar baar ae
Kyun dair layi Aasghar payi kardi intezaare
Hogyai ae shaam aja dil maa da dubda janda

2
2) Tere baad hoya Asghar baba tera shaheed ae
Hoya hukm lut gaye khaimay saaray manao eiday
Jhoolay tere nu Asghar reya shimr(la) khud jalaanda

3
3) Che maa da baal socho kitna hai paani peenda
Je day veera na paani ho teer na chalainda
Hurmal(la) shaki nu Asghar reya pyaase lab wakhanda

4
4) Kadi goad vekhdiyan kadi jhula tera khaali
Bhuldi nai maa dukhi nu teri shakl bholi bhaali
Akh band karaan te chehra tera nazar hai andaa
