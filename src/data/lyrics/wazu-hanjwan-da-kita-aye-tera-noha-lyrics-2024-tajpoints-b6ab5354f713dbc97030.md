1
Wazu Hanjwan Da Kita Aye Tera Noha Paraan Mola.
Tere Lashay Te Likhiya Aye Musayab Da Quraan Maula.
Kariyaan Zanjeeraan Beriyan Lafz-e-Tatheer Banaya.
Te Zeraan Zabraan Ban Gayiyaan Oh Zakhma De Nishaan Maula.

2
Aye Millan Te Kariyaan Paiyaan Ne Maula Mur Ke Nayi Aana.
Hukam Howay Te Sutiyaan Ne Main Dhiyaan Nu Jagaan Maula.
Jehra Laan Layi Kariyaan Aaya Aye Ro Ro Ke Lash Te Aakhay.
Jism V Naal Tutda Aye Kiwein Kariyaan Main Laan Maula.

3
Karbal Taun Le Ke Aj Taein Har Lash Te Jehri Roway.
Tainu Rowan Layi Aayi Aye, Aye Zainab Di Aye Maa Maula.
La Waris Kar Ke Sut Gaye Zalim Ik Lash Si Najfi.
Jay Dhi Koi Wekh Le Manzar Oh Dukh Kiwein Sunaa Maula.

4
Imam Mosa Kazim AS Shahadat New Noha.
Noha Khuwan Party Ch Faiyaz Party Sialkot.
Matami Sangat
