Ibtidaa Husain, intehaa Husain, naakhuda Husain, hal ataa Husain

1
Shams o qamar taaba haiṅ tere sang-e-haram tujh pe waari
Koh o Safa tere sadqe Arsh ki zo tujh pe taari
Ruh ki azaan, Haq ka aasmaaṅ
Hai Khuda jahaaṅ, yeh bhi hai wahaaṅ

2
Aala mukarram zaahid hai jag se niraala aabid hai
Nur hai yeh bhi Khaaliq ka is ka Khuda bhi shaahid hai
Tera gham sada, hota hi raha
Mit na paaye ga, shor Karbala

3
Saare malak is ke nokar saare shahidoṅ se behtar
Rehte haiṅ qadmoṅ mein pare dur-e-Najaf ho laal o gohar
Baadbaaṅ Husain, saarbaaṅ Husain
Saaibaaṅ Husain, jaavedaaṅ Husain

4
Naaz-e-Khuda hai naaz-e-Nabi qamar-e-Bani Haashim ka wasi
Arshi maangte rehte haiṅ Ibn-e-Ali hai aisa sakhi
Baadshaah Husain, shehanshaah Husain
Diṅ panaah Husain, laa fatah Husain

5
Jalti zamiṅ par haaye para Shimar ka khanjar us pe chala
Khushk gala hai us ka bara waaye Husaina waawaila
Shikwa na kiya, sar kata diya
Ghar luta diya, diṅ bacha liya

6
Ibn-e-Mazaahir maare gaye Ghazi ke baazu na rahe
Qasim o Akbar qatal hoye kaun bacha hai jo aaye
Koi na raha tera aasra
Tanha reh gaya Ibn-e-Syeda

7
Dar se sadaaeiṅ deti hai Zainab meri roti hai
Baba ko na maar mere haaye Sakina kehti hai
Zulm hai bara, Shimar ne kiya
Dekho bekhata, kaata hai gala

8
Kehti hai Zainab ro kar meri bandhi haathoṅ mein rasan
Chhor ke jaauṅ kaise bhala bhai para be gur o kafan
Dasht mein para, laasha hai tera
Na kafan mila, ho gayi jafa

9
Asif o Sarwar karte haiṅ tujh se duaaeiṅ Ae Maula
Hum ko bula le roze pe sun le sadaaeiṅ Ae Maula
Sun dua Husain, kar ataa Husain
Ab bula Husain, Karbala Husain
