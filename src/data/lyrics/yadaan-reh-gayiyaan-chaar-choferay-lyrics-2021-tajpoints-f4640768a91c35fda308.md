1
Yadaan Reh Gayiyaan Chaar Choferay
Veraan Di Jagah Iss Wehray Wich Mantaan De Deviyaan Ne Kitay Baseray.
Ewein Lagiya Jiwein Lekhaan Te Qalam Phir Gaya.
Marey Samne Chachay Ghazi Ne Jis Dam Lakeer Phairi Naam Te Mere.

2
Aayat-e-Tatheer Jiwein Wekhdi Si Kisa.
Chan Veer Madine Wich Ewein Sughra Ne Roz Rastay Wekhay Batheray.
Fatima De Sare Soraj Karbala Tur Gaye.
Nayi Hun Charagaan Nal Mukney Maa Fatima De Ghar Choon Veer Haneray.

3
Tu Mera Khat Parh Na Sakiya Zindagi Muk Gayi.
Par Bhain Da Veran Wadah Aye Dunya Parhey Gi Nohay Mere Te Tere.
Qayam-e-Aal-e-Muhammad Nu Jiwein Syeda.
Aawazaan Ditiyaan San Akbar Sughra Ohwein Pukarey Sham Saweray
