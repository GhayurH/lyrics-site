1
Zindaan Ke Andheron Mein Sakina Ki Sada Hai.
Marti Hoon Keh Bhaiya Mera Dam Ghutne Laga Hai.
Kha Kha Ke Tamache Mere Rukhsar Hain Neele.
Umat Ne Mujhe Baba Ka Pursa yeh Diya Hai.

2
Hai Yad Mujhe Aaj Bhi Woh Sham-e-Ghareeban.
Zakhmi Hain Mere Kan Tu Kurta Bhi Jala Hai.
Chacha Se Kahin Aa Kar Zara Zulm Tu Dekhein.
Nani Si Bhatiji Pe Kya Zulm-o-Jafa Hai.

3
Baba Ki Tarha Mujh Ko Kafan Bhi Na Mile Ga.
Hargiz Na Samajhna Keh Behan Tujh Se Kafa Hai.
Masoom Sakina Ka Likha Noha Jo Main Ne.
Lafzon Ne Kiya Giriya Yeh Qamar Rota Raha Hai.
