1
Ridawan Qaid Hogayiyan Mir Hasan Mir
Ridawan Qaid Hogayiyan Maawan Qaid Hogayiaan Mir Hasan Mir.
Rasan De Wich Sakina Di Sadawaan Qaid Ho Gayiyaan.
Bhira Di Lash Te Zainab Aye Ro Ke Wainr Kardi Si.
Do Banwaan Kat Gayiyaan Ghazi Do Banwaan Qaid Ho Gayiyaan.
Sina Te Chaar Bachray San Ruqaiya Rasiyaan De Wich.
Ewein Umm Ul-Baneen Maa Di Wafawaan Qaid Ho Gayiyaan.
Aye Zainab Ro Ke Kaindi Si Jaga Ke Gash Taun Abid Nu.
Siraan Layi Khak Reh Gayi Aye Ridawaan Qaid Ho Gayiyaan.
Sina Te Babay Nu Tak Ke Sakina Wainr Kardi Si.
Kiwein Tainu Millan De Layi Main Aawaan Qaid Ho Gayiyaan.
Sar-e-Asghar Nu Tak Tak Ke Rubab Aye Wainr Kardi Aye.
Mere Hasday Hoye Chan Di Adawaan Qaid Ho Gayiyaan.
Ruqaiya Aakhiya Takiya Jadon Sar Almaan Walay Da.
Main Kinj Zalim Tu Sir Tera Churawaan Qaid Ho Gayiyaan.
Andherey Qaid Khane Wich Sakina Wainr Kardi Si.
Ewein Lagda Aye Soraj Di Shuawaan Qaid Ho Gayiyaan.
Dita Sajjad Ne Khutba Keh Shami Loag Na Aakhan.
Bani Hashim De Kunbay Di Anawaan Qaid Ho Gayiyaan.
Hathaan Wich Zahra Jaiyaan De Pawa Ke Rasiyaan Akbar.
Aye Has Ke Denday Re Shami Sadawaan Qaid Ho Gayiyaan.
