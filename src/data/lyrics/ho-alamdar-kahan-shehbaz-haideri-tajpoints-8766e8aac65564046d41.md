1
Ho Alamdar Kahan Shehbaz Haideri.
Ro Ke Zainab Ne Kaha Ho Alamdar Kahan.
Chin Gayi Sar Se Rida Ho Alamdar Kahan.
Barh Gaye Sham-e-Ghareebaan Ke Andherey Bhaiyya.
Sham Tak Hoon Gay Nahi Ab Tu Saweray Bhaiyya.
Ghar Hai Be Chain Bohat Jaldi Aa Jao Zara.
Barchiyaan Mere Jigar Par Youn Lagata Hai Laeen.
Naizay Par Sar Mujhe Akbar Ka Dikhata Hai Laeen.
Samne Aa Ke Mere Qatil Akbar Ka Hansa.
Rasiyaan Pehnein Gi Sab Ro Ke Main Yeh Sochti Hoon.
Haye Inn Bibiyon Se Ja Ke Bhala Kaisay Kahon.
Maan Tha Jis Pe Mujhe Ab Woh Bhai Na Raha.
Tum Jo Hotey Tu Youn Main Sham Na Jati Haye.
Badlay Youn Badar-o-Ohad Ke Na Chukati Haye.
Aa Ke Dekho Tu Mujhe Sham Tak Chalna Para.
Jab Sakina Ko Tamachon Ki Saza Milti Hai.
Dekh Kar Nehar Ki Janib Woh Tumhein Dhondti Hai.
Ro Ke Kehti Hai Yehi Nehar Se Aao Chacha.
Kyun Nazar Aata Nahi Lasha Tumhara Bhai.
Royon Kis Ja Pe Tujhe Logon Ne Mara Bhai.
Aik Bazu Hai Idhar Our Udhar Aik Para.
Shah-e-Bekas Chaley Maqtal Ko Jonhi Khaimon Se.
Zeen Par Bhai Ko Behno Ne Bithaya Ro Ke.
Hichkiyaan Le Ke Tabhi Di Yeh Zainab Ne Sada.
Aisay Pardais Mein Kya Koi Juda Hota Hai.
Keh Ke Bazaron Mein Abid Yeh Lahu Rota Hai.
Be Rida Ahl-e-Haram Our Main Qaidi Hoon Khara.
Shimar Logon Se Yehi Kehta Hai Maro Aa Kar.
Kofay Mein Kofay Ki Shehzadiyaan Khaein Pathar.
Majma-e-Aam Hai Keh Har Taraf Barhney Laga.
Zulm Abbas Ki Behno Pe Youn:SHEHBAZ-o-ASAD:.
Yeh Ali Our Muhammad Se Tha Sab Bugz-o-Hasad.
Zulm Sab Dekh Ke Yeh Kehti Thi Aik Rida.
