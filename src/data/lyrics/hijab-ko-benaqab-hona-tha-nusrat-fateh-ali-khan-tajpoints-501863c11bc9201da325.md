Hijab Ko Benaqab Hona Tha Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan.
Haan Mujh Hi Ko Kharab Hona Tha.

1
Khaili Bachpan Mein Aankh Macholi Bohat.
Aya Shabab Tu Hisab Hona Tha.
Sath Rehte The Raat Din Ki Tarha.
Tum Ho Mahtab Mujhe Aaftab Hona Tha.

2
Meri Aankhon Ka Kuch Qasoor Nahi.
Husan Ko Lajawab Hona Tha.
Tha Muqadar Mein Humara Milna.
Wagarna Hum Ne Kahan Kamyab Hona Tha.
