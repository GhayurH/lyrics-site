1
Sar e Abbas Atta Hai Mir Hasan Mir.
Kaha Zainab Ne Ye Ro Kar Sanbhalo Bibiyon Chadar Sar-e-Abbas Atta Hai.
Bula Laye Sakina Ko Koi Zindan Se Ja Kar Sar-e-Abbas Atta Hai.
Yeh Ghairat Ka Khuda Hai Iss Ki Ghairat Ka Taqaza Tha.

2
Khula Sar Dekh Ke Niazay Se Jo Yeh Sar Girata Tha.
Nahi Hai Pas Ghar Chadar Tu Rakh Lo Hath Hi Sar Par Sar-e-Abbas Atta Hai.
Bhara Ho Ga Laho Aankhon Mein Oss Ki Khud Hataon Gi.
Main Bachpan Ki Tarha Se Bhai Ka Chehra Dhulaon Gi.

3
Agar Mumkin Hai Tu De De Koi Pani Mujhe La Kar Sar-e-Abbas Atta Hai.
Mujhe Uthna Pare Ga Bibiyo Tazeem Ko Oss Ki.
Main Maa Umulbaneen Ki Samt Se Ghazi Ko Chomo Gi.
Sanbhale Sar Koi Aoun-o-Muhammad Ke Yahan Aa Kar Sar-e-Abbas Atta Hai.

4
Iss Ahsas Se Oss Ne Nazar Apni Jhukai Hai.
Osse Maloom Hai Yeh Kitna Ghairat Mand Bhai Hai.
Chupaye Kis Tarha Zainab Pare Hain Neel Shano Par Sar-e-Abbas Atta Hai.
Mila Pani Osse Pocha Tu Kya Batlaye Gi Zainab.

5
Bhala Osski Bhatigi Ko Kahan Se Laye Gi Zainab.
Nigahein Osski Dhondein Gi Sakina Ko Yahan Aa Kar Sar-e-Abbas Atta Hai.
The Sab Ki Jholiyon Mein Apne Apne Ladlon Ke Sar.
Ruqaiya Umm-e-Laila Umm-e-Farwa Madar-e-Asghar.

6
Uthi Kehte Howay Har Maa Sar Apne Lal Ka Rakh Kar Sar-e-Abbas Atta Hai.
 Takallum Shamiyon Ki Aurtein Bhi Roti Jati Hain.
Ali Ki Betiyaan Jaldi Se Zakhmon Ko Chupati Hain.
Khari Hai Hath Phailaye Hoye Khud Zainab-e-Muztar Sar-e-Abbas Atta Hai.
