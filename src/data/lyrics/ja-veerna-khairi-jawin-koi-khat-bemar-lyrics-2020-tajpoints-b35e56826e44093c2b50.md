1
Ja Veerna Khairi Jawin Koi Khat Bemar Nu Pawin.
Jadon Yaad Ujari Di Aaway Fair Mur Watna Nu Aawein.
Babay Di Marzi Akbar Mera Naa Lakh Waar Kataway.
Tu Apne Janjiyaan De Wich Mera Pehla Naam Likhawin.
Akhiyaan Tu Qafila Jad Tak Nayi Ojhal Honda Veera.
Ahsan Karein Bas Itna Mur Mur Ke Takda Jawein.
Aye Sanjhiyaan Mantaan Akbar Asaan Bhain Bhirawaan Maniyaan.
Apne Hissay De Deway Safraan Wich Veer Jalawin.
Main Akbar Banr Ke Ghar Wich Dewaan Gi Roz Azana.
Tu Safar Ich Sughra Banr Ke Asghar Nu Lori Sunawin.
Jaye Maut Zarori Ban Gayi Mainu Kool Bola Laein Apne.
Tu Karbal Wali Basti Na Kaliyaan Veer Wasawin.
 Akbar Akbar Nal Keeti Ik Aakhri Gal Aye Sughra.
Tere Bajoon Jaye Main Mar Gayi Munh Wekhan Lazmi Aawein.
