1
Mehv e Dua Hain Fatima Zehra Mukhtar Hussain Fatehpuri.
Mehway Dua Hain Fatima Zahra Utha Ke Hath.
Khaliq Atta Hussain Ko Hoon Ba Wafa Ke Hath.
Ik Aasmaan Hai Aik Zameen Darmiyaan Hai Rizq.
Chakki Chalaye Jate Hain Youn Syeda Ke Hath.
Abbas Ik Ki Aarzu Hai Ik Ki Parwarish.
Oss Fatima Ka Khuwab Hai Iss Fatima Ke Hath.
Ab Chahe Jitnay Mojzay Karti Phiray Yeh Khak.
Noor-e-Hussain Aa Gaya Hai Karbala Ke Hath.
Ghar Se Hussain Laye Hain Romal-e-Syeda.
Hur Aaye Ga Yazid Se Lazim Chura Ke Hath.
Mati Ko Ab Woh Chandi Banaye Tu Kya Ajab.
Fizza Ke Sar Pe Aa Gaye Hain Fatima Ke Hath.
Waqt-e-Zahoor Uthoon Ga Akbar Najaf Se Main.
Rakhein Gay Mere Sar Pe Ali Muskura Ke Hath.
