Allah manzoor jay kaar lenda dua Sughra di

Aan kay khair khabbar, puchday, bhira Sughra di

Rondiya chor kay turr gaye, tay na murr kay aye

Veer ajanday tay na, aondi kaza Sughra di

Nana thakk gaye main teray ro,zay tay manta manadi

Tu vi kyun sunda naiyo, aaho, bukka Sughra di.

Akhri velay jo bema,r nay harray paye

Kaash Akbar vi ay sun, lenda, sada Sughra di

Laikay chann veer nu ay faz,al da baba aaja

Chacha ujri howi murr, joakh’ vasa Sughra di.

Veer di shaadi da bema,r nu cha si Akhtar

Ikko barchi nay ditti, aas’ mukka Sughra di.
