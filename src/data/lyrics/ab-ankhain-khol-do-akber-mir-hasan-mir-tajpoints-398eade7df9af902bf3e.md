1
Ab Ankhain Khol Do Akber Mir Hasan Mir.
Jab Suni Shah Ne Aawaz Jawaan Betay Ki.
Lash Par Aa Ke Yeh Farmane Lagay Sibt e Nabi.
Ab Aankhein Khol Do Akbar Sirhaney Aa Gaya Baba.
Zaeefi Mein Jawaan Lasha Uthane Aa Gaya Baba.
Guzar Jaein Gay Hum Iss Imtehaan Se Ya Ali Keh Kar.
Nikal Jaye Gi Yeh Barchi Na Ghabrana Mere Dilbar.
Tumhara Hath Seene Se Hatanay Aa Gaya Baba.
Unhon Ne Kya Samajh Rakha Tha Akbar Be Sahara Hai.
Isharey Se Bata Do Kis Ne Kis Ne Tum Ko Mara Hai.
Ulat Kar Aasteen Mehshar Uthane Aa Gaya Baba.
Chali Aayi Thi Aadhay Rastay Tak Sani-e-Zahra.
Bari Mushkil Se Bhaija Hai Qasam De Kar Teri Beta.
Behan Kaisay Tarapti Hai Bataney Aa Gaya Baba.
Kaha Tha Kis Ne Seene Mein Tumharey Toor Kar Neza.
Yeh Manzar Dekh Kar Shabbir Zindah Reh Na Paye Ga.
Khabar Kar Do Ossay Barchi Churanay Aa Gaya Baba.
Laray Ho Dair Tak Thak Tu Gaye Ho Gay Mere Dilbar.
Mujhe Mat Rokna Hargiz Tumhein Meri Qasam Akbar.
Tumharey Chand Se Bazu Dabane Aa Gaya Baba.
Uthata Dhoom Se Maiyyat Agar Hota Madine Mein.
Kafan Main De Nahi Sakta Yehi Hai Dard Seene Mein.
Amama Tum Ko Nana Ka Urhaney Aa Gaya Baba.
Jo Rota Dekhti Mujh Ko Tarap Jati Meri Madar.
Main Khul Kar Ro Nahi Paya Muhammad Ke Janazay Par.
Jo Aanso Reh Gaye They Woh Bahane Aa Gaya Baba.
Nazar Kaisay Lagay Gi Ab Badan Sansoon Se Khali Hai.
Kalai Se Tumhari Kali Dori Khulney Wali Hai.
Phophi Ki Aakhri Mannat Barhanay Aa Gaya Baba.
Nazar Aa Jaye Ga Tum Ko Behan Hai Ab Kahan Beta.
Utho Dekho Hamari Ungliyon Ke Darmiyaan Beta.
Tumhein Sughra Ki Tanhai Dikhaney Aa Gaya Baba.
Parhi Nad-e-Ali Le Kar Chaley Sheh Lasha-e-Akbar.
 Takallum Aayi Pehlu Se Sada-e-Fatah-e-Khaybar.
Mere Beta Teri Himmat Barhanay Aa Gaya Baba.
