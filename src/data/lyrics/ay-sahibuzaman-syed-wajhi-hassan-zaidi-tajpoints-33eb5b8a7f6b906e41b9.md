Ay Sahibuzaman Syed Wajhi Hassan Zaidi.
Aye Malik-e-Jahan Aye Mukhtar Al-Madad.

1
Tu Hi Raheem Hai Tu Hi Ghaffar Al-Madad.
Kehte Hain Tujh Se Yeh Tere Hubdar Al-Madad.
Halqay Mein Keh Rahe Hain Azadar Al-Madad.
Aye Sahib Al-Zaman Meri Hai Yeh Aarzu.

2
Iyyaka Nastaeen Parhon Tere Robaro.
Nad-e-Ali Ko Parh Ke Areeza Bhi Likh Diya.
Mujh Par Bhi Ho Karam Ya Karam Ibn-e-Syeda.
Kuch Dair Mere Maula Tu Kar Mujh Se Guftgu.

3
Jo Kuch Bhi Hai Zameen Mein Our Aasman Mein.
Sab Kuch Hai Mere Maula Woh Teri Aman Mein.
ALLAH Ki Qasam Keh Hai Parwardigar Tu.
Hoti Hai Jo Azan Yeh Mehdi Ka Hai Karam.

4
Quran Parh Jawan Yeh Mehdi Ka Hai Karam.
Chalte Hain Do Jahan Yeh Mehdi Ka Hai Karam.
Sab Kuch Zameen, Zaman Yeh Mehdi Ka Hai Karam.
Nalain Mas Na Ho Agar Oss Ki Jaben Par.

5
Gir Jaye Aasman Bhi Aa Kar Zameen Par.
Farsh-e-Azza Pe Baithay Hain Hubdar Aap Ke.
Sab Muntazir Hain Aap Ke Dedar Ke Liye.
Ashk-e-Azza Se Kartay Hain Sab Aankhon Ka Wozu.

6
Sajday Jo Be Asar They Miley Ga Unhein Asar.
Jab Main Rakhon Ga Aap Ke Qadmo Pe Apna Sar.
Phir Oss Ghari Parhon Ga Main Iyyaka Nabudu.
Mosa Ki Yeh Dua Hai Zamane Ko Toor Kar.

7
Khuwaish Hai Do Jahan Ki Maula Zahoor Kar.
Dekhein Khuda Ke Noor Ko Hum Loag Chaar So.
Kabay Mein Aap Farsh-e-Azza Jab Bichaein Gay.
Phir Anbiya Bhi Matami Halqay Mein Aaein Gay.

8
Khul Jaye Gi Jahan Pe Qalandar Ki Ha-o-Ho.
Tujh Se Duaein Karta Hoon Tu Dastgeer Hai.
Main Janta Hoon Tu Hi Sami-o-Baseer Hai.
Rakhna Aimaad Naqvi Ko Har Lamha Surkhuru.
