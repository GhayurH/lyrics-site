Neend zindaan may Sakina ko jo aayi ho gi
Lori madar ne ye ro ro ke sunai ho gi

1
Ro ke soyee hay Sakina ko jagaey na koi
Yaad masooma ko baba ki dilaye na koi

2
1) Yeh thaki hari safar ki hay jagee ratoon ki
Sab parindon ko be keh do,  chehchahaye na koi
Ro ke soyee hay Sakina ko jagaey na koi

3
2) Naam yeh Shimr ka sunti hay to dar jati hay
Baat maqtal ki Sakina ko sunaye na koi
Ro ke soyee hay Sakina ko jagaey na koi

4
3) Aaj darogha-e-zindaan bhi dar na kholay
Aur Sajjad ke zanjeer hilaey na koi
Ro ke soyee hay Sakina ko jagaey na koi

5
4) Jaisay Abid ne uthaya hay behan ka lasha
Lash pardes may behnoon ki uthaey na koi
Ro ke soyee hay Sakina ko jagaey na koi

6
5) Qaidyon may sab se kamsin thay janab-e-Baqir
Wah ri ghurbat is tarha qaid nibhaey na koi
Ro ke soyee hay Sakina ko jagaey na koi

7
6) Jaisay Asghar ko hay chor aayi Sakina Akhtar
Bay kafan bhai ko yun chor ke aaey na koi
Ro ke soyee hay Sakina ko jagaey na koi
