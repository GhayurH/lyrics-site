1
Darbar Mein Khari Hoi Kehti Hai Fatima.
Baba Woh Ab Madina, Madina Nahi Raha.
Haq Mangti Hai Zahra Majma Hai Bagiyon Ka Baba Tere Watan Mein.
Pardesiyon Ki Tarha Haye Rehti Hai Ab Yeh Dukhiya Baba Tere Watan Mein.

2
Ab Loag Fatima Ko Pehchantay Nahi Hain.
Main Aap Ki Hoon Beti Yeh Mantay Nahi Hain.
Koi Nahi Hai Mera Haye Main Reh Gayi Hoon Tanha Baba Tere Watan Mein.
Gham Yeh Nahi Hai Mujh Ko Darwaza Jal Gaya Hai.

3
Iss Se Bara Sitam Tu Zahra Pe Yeh Howa Hai.
Dar Jal Raha Tha Mera Haye Koi Na Ghar Se Nikla Baba Tere Watan Mein.
Dar Mujh Pe Zalimo Ne Jalta Howa Giraya.
Main Gir Pari Zameen Par Gash Iss Tarha Se Aaya.

4
Mohsin Jahan Se Guzra Haye Pehlu Howa Shikasta Baba Tere Watan Mein.
Main Bhi Khari Hoi Thi Hasnain Bhi Kharey They.
Masjid Mein Meri Janib Sab Loag Dekhtey They.
Yeh Zakhm Na Bharay Ga Haye Main Ban Gayi Tamasha Baba Tere Watan Mein.

5
Koi Salam Aa Kar Karta Nahi Tha Pehle.
Ab Tu Jawab Dena Lazim Nahi Samajhtay.
Har Shaks Bhool Betha Haye Rutba Abu Al-Hasan Ka Baba Tere Watan Mein.
Ghut Ghut Ke Apne Ghar Mein Aanso Baha Rahi Hoon.

6
Har Zakhm Murtaza Se Apna Chupa Rahi Hoon.
Khaya Hai Taziyana Haye Zakhmi Hai Mera Chehra Baba Tere Watan Mein.
Fizza Ke Ab Saharey Main Uthti Baithti Hoon.
Kha Kar Tamacha Jab Se Main Khak Par Giri Hoon.

7
Yeh Zulm Mujh Pe Toota Haye Main Ho Gayi Zaeefa Baba Tere Watan Mein.
Baba Se Woh Takallum Shikwa Yeh Kar Rahi Hai.
Ummat Ko Fatima Se Kya Jane Dushmani Hai.
Ho Raat Ya Sawera Turbat Pe Hai Andhera Baba Tere Watan Mein.
