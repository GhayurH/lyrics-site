Ye Khushbu Aur Ye Saya Bolta Hai Mukhtar Hussain Fatehpuri.
Ali Guzrey Hain Rasta Bolta Hai.

1
Yeh Mimber Hai Saloni Ka Yahan Se.
Ghadeer-e-Khum Ka Maula Bolta Hai.
Ossay Tum Bheer Mein Bhi Dhond Lo Gay.
Ali Walay Ka Chehra Bolta Hai.

2
Mere Ashar Qanbar Sun Rahe Hain.
Samandar Chup Hai Qatra Bolta Hai.
Azakhano Mein Mimber Se Abhi Tak.
Ghadeer-e-Khum Ka Khutba Bolta Hai.

3
Yeh Arz-e-Khum Hai Soraj Ki Zabaan Mein.
Yahan Ka Zarra Zarra Bolta Hai.
Wilayat Ke Shajar Ka Zikar Sun Kar.
Hai Jis Ka Jaisa Shajrah Bolta Hai.

4
Meri Guftar Par Hairat Na Kijiye.
Ali Chahein Tu Murdah Bolta Hai.
Kabhi Suniye:BILAL-E-KAZMI: Ko.
Ali Ke Haq Mein Banda Bolta Hai.
