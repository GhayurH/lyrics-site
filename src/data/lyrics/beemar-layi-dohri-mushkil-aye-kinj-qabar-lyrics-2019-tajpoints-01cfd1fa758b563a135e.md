1
Beemar Layi Dohri Mushkil Aye Kinj Qabar Kare Tameer Aye.
Aidhi Banwaan Te Hathkariyaan Ne Hathkariyaan Te Hamsheer Aye.
Socho Te Sabar Di Had Muk Gayi Jithay Bhain Di Qabar Banai.
Ossay Zindan Che Kayi Din Taein Riya Qaidi Kiwein Veer Aye.

2
Wich Qabar De Pa Ke Maiyyat Nu Kiya Abid Bhain Sakina.
Karbal Da Malik Akbar Way Par Sham Teri Jageer Aye.
Zahra De Badlay Lainday Re Aynu Mar Ke SHami Zalim.
Aynu Baba Fatima Kehnda Si Aidhi Bas Ayho Taqseer Aye.

3
Asghar De Gal WIch Teer Laga Aidhay Gal Wich Rasiyaan Rajhiyaan.
Aynj Rasiyaan Kadiyaan Abid Ne Jiwein Babay Kadiya Teer Aye.
Kan Zakhmi Pairaan Wich Chalay Rukhsaraan Da Rang Neela.
 Saqlain Yateemi Iss Toon Wadh Nayi Honi Tafseer Aye.
