Bazaar Main Sitamgar, Zainab Ko La Rahay Hain, Abbas Ko Bulao

Aaley Nabi Ko Zalim, Dar Dar Phira Rahay Hain, Abbas Ko Bulao

Sehdanian Ko Sheh Key, Matam Main Ro Raheey Hain
Aour Bayhaya Sitamgar, Khusheeyan Mana Rahay Hain, Abbas Ko Bulao

Ik Ashq Per Hain Jin Key, Dono Jahan Sadqay,
Has Has Kay Sham Walay, Ounko Rula Rahay Hain, Abbas Ko Bulao

Be Khouf Ho Kay Zalim, Khasib Ghulam Zaday,
Sheh Kee Yateem Bachee, Bayzulm Dharahay Hain, Abbas Ko Bulao

Beemar Sariban Say, Ghabra Kay Bolee Zainab
Hum Bayrida Khulay Sar, Darbar Ja Rahay Hain, Abbas Ko Bulao

Ik Maa Tharap Tharap Kay, Faryad Kar Raheey Hay
Sajjad Kay Sitamgar, Duray Laga Rahay Hain, Abbas Ko Bulao

Phir Waqt Agaya Hay, Duniya Main Aaj Gohar
Ahley Sitam Jahan Main, Phir Sar Outha Rahay Hain, Abbas Ko Bulao
