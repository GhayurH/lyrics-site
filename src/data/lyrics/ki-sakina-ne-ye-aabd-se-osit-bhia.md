Ki Sakina nay yeh Abid se wasiyat bhaiya
Is zindan may banana meri turbat bhaiya

1
1) Chahti hoon may jahan soyoon woh mitti num ho
Tishnagi ki jo aziyat hay woh zara si kam ho
Hadiyo tak may hay pyas ki woh shiddat bhaiya
2) Tumko maloom hay dard ki shiddat meri
Dar e zindan pay parandoon ko jab dehkhti thi
Aur barh jati thi ghar janay ki chahat bhaiya
3) Qaid may baba ka sar aaya magar lash nahi
Lash pay ran may pohonchi thi to lash thi sar kaheen
Na hui poori mulaqat ki chahat bahiya
4) Tum agar paana meri baliyaan meri chadar
Meri Sughra ko woh day dena Madinay ja kar
Bhool paye gi na woh meri mohabbat bahiya
5) Ja kay batlana meray Fazl say rudaad meri
Choti dadi say bhi keh dena ye uftaad meri
Kis tarha tum nay uthayi meri mayyat bahiya
