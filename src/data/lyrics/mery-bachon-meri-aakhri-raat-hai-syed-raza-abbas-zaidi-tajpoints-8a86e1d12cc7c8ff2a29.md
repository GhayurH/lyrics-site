1
Mery Bachon Meri Aakhri Raat Hai Syed Raza Abbas Zaidi.
Woh Zakhmi Ali Our Woh Ikeeswin Ki Shab.
Bachoon Se Ali Bolay Qareeb Aao Mere Sab.
Sar Baap Ka Choma Tu Yeh Kehne Lagin Zainab.
Saya Mere Baba Ka Salamat Rahe Ya Rab.
Yeh Suntay Hi Munh Phair Ke Roney Lagay Haider.
Kyun Rotay Hain Jab Bachoon Ne Yeh Pocha Lipat Kar.
Ghar Mein Kuhram Tha Jab Ali Ne Kaha Mere Bachoon Meri Aakhri Raat Hai.
Kal Yahin Se Uthay Ga Janaza Mera Mere Bachoon Meri Aakhri Raat Hai.
Jab Kafan Orh Kar Ghar Se Jaon Ga Main.
Janta Hoon Bohat Yaad Aaon Ga Main.
Mujh Ko Dhondo Gay Hujray Mein Ja Kar Magar.
Chah Kar Bhi Tumhein Mil Na Paon Ga Main.
Dekh Lo Chehra Jee Bhar Ke Tum Baap Ka.
Mere Bachoon Meri Aakhri Raat Hai.
Haal Bazar Ka Aaya Jab Robaro.
Ro Ke Haider Ne Beton Se Ki Guftgu.
Mere Sar Se Jo Aaya Hai Beh Kar Abhi.
Saaf Kar Do Meri Aankh Se Woh Lahu.
Dekh Loon Main Zara Betiyon Ki Rida.
Mere Bachoon Meri Aakhri Raat Hai.
Dekho Abbas Jab Qaid Hogi Bahan.
Na Hi Tum Ho Gay Our Na Hussain-o-Hasan.
Mere Sajjad Ko Pas Lao Mere.
Oss Ne Chalna Hai Kanton Pe Seh Kar Chubhan.
Bosa Le Loon Main Sajjad Ke Paon Ka.
Mere Bachoon Meri Aakhri Raat Hai.
Mere Hotey Hoye Dar Giraya Gaya.
Fatima Ko Tamacha Lagaya Gaya.
Mar Chuka Tha Wahin Jab Mere Samne.
Hath Par Taziyana Lagaya Gaya.
Dafan Ho Jaye Ga Ab Badan Bhi Mera.
Mere Bachoon Meri Aakhri Raat Hai.
Meri Ik Zarb Par Dekho Rona Nahi.
Yaad Karna Woh Khanjar Woh Jalti Zameen.
Hoga Jab Maa Ki Godi Mein Betay Ka Sar.
Terah Zarbain Chalaye Ga Jis Par Laeen.
Aakhri Bar Main Chom Loon Woh Gala.
Mere Bachoon Meri Aakhri Raat Hai.
Jin Ko Khana Khilata Tha Ja Kar Ali.
Unn Ka Mere Elawah Nahi Hai Koi.
Tum Khiyal Unn Yateemo Ka Rakhna Hasan.
Baithna Sath Jab Yaad Aaye Meri.
Kal Se Doon Ga Unhein Kaisay Main Hosla.
Mere Bachoon Meri Aakhri Raat Hai.
Aye Raza Our Zeeshan Kya Waqt Tha.
Hath Zainab Ka Shabar Ko Sonpa Gaya.
Sar Jhuka Kar Bohat Roein Umm Ul-Baneen.
Aakhri Sans Mein Jab Ali Ne Kaha.
Sonp Doon Hath Ghazi Ko Shabbir Ka.
Mere Bachoon Meri Aakhri Raat Hai.
