Allah allah allah
Allahu kabla kullu shai, allahu bada kullu shai,

1
Allah allah allah,  Ya zainab-e-kubra
1) Aey bibi baniye azaa, peghaam karbala tera, jisne suna yahi kaha
Ya Zainab-e-Kubra
2) Tegh khutbo ki barsare darbaar, jaise chalti ho haideri talwaar,
Kat ke ghirte the zalimo ke jigar, jang-e-khaiber thi ya teri lalkaar
Ya Zainab-e-Kubra
3) Ay bibi baniye azaa, dine rasool bach gaya, quran ki thi ye sadaa
Ya Zainab-e-Kubra
4) Yaad ata hai shaam ka manzar, bibi sarpe tere na thi chadar,
Har taraf patharo ki baarish thi, teri poshaak thi lahu me tar
Ya Zainab-e-Kubra
5) Ay bibi baniye azaa, sadma tha ye bohot baraa, roya sare shahe hudaa
Ya Zainab-e-Kubra
6) Tune bacho ki di hai qurbani, haye re tera zokh imaani,
Pyaase bhayi ki yaad me tune, khun bahaya hai ankhe ka paani
Ya Zainab-e-Kubra
7) Ay bibi baniye azaa, ye zabta hosla tera, neze pe ser pukaarta
Ya Zainab-e-Kubra
8) Tere dar par ye istighasa hai, jo bhi farshe aza pe rota hai,
Us pe saya kare tera ghazi, wo jo mushkil kusha ka beta hai
Ya Zainab-e-Kubra
9) Ay bibi baniye azaa, hum momino ki ye dua, ashke azaa ka waastaa
Ya Zainab-e-Kubra
10) Kitne makrooz matami hai tere, karza tu ghaib se adaa karde,
Bibi tu to sakhi ki beti hai, tu to muflis ko baadshaah kar de
Ya Zainab-e-Kubra
11) Ay bibi baniye azaa, tu innama tu hal ataa, kehta hai sar jukaa huwa
Ya Zainab-e-Kubra
12) Qaid be jurma jo hai matamdaar, misle mazloom aabide bimaar,
Unko azaadiyo ko tohfa de, aaj tu misle haidare karrar
Ya Zainab-e-Kubra
13) Ay bibi baniye azaa, hum ko hai tera aasraa, ghairo se mangna hai kya
Ya Zainab-e-Kubra
14) Tere bhayi ne jaise rahib ko, saat bete ataa kiye phirto,
Tu bhi hamsheer us sakhi ki hai, dede awlaad chahiye jisko
Ya Zainab-e-Kubra
15) Ay bibi baniye azaa, awlaad aisi kar ataa, ho wo bhi matami tera
Ya Zainab-e-Kubra
16) Waasta us aseer bachi ka, rassiyo me bandha tha jiska gala,
Wo jo pyaasi thi mar gayi pyasi, koyi bacha na ab rahe pyaasa
Ya Zainab-e-Kubra
17) Ay bibi baniye azaa, har sabeel par likha, kehta hai khun bhayi ka
Ya Zainab-e-Kubra
18) Ay bibi baniye azaa, humse na haq adaa huwa, karde muafye khata
