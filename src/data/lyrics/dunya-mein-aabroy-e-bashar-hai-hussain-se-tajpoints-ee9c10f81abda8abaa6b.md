Dunya Mein Aabroy-e-Bashar Hai Hussain Se.
Wabasta Iss Liye Mera Ghar Hai Hussain Se.

1
Rang Shafaq Se Sham Ke Daman Pe Hai Likha.
Iss Kainat Mein Tu Sahar Hai Hussain Se.
Hum Loag Maut Ko Bhi Samajhte Nahi Hain Maut.
Mansoob Zindagi Ka Safar Hai Hussain Se.

2
Khaif Hussainiyat Se Rahe Gi Yazeediyat.
Islam Dushmano Ko Zar Hai Hussain Se.
Tarbiyat-e-Rasool Hai Iss Bat Ka Daleel.
Shadaab Aaj Dein Ka Shajar Hai Hussain Se.

3
Touheed Khairiyat Se Jahan Gamzan Rahe.
ROshan Har Aisi Rahguzar Hai Hussain Se.
Mehshar Ke Din Azai Yehi Kaam Aye Ga.
Dunya Mein Rab-o-Zab Agar Hai Hussain Se
