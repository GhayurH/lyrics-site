Bibi Zahra Jo Bhi Aaya Sarwar Raza Karbalai.

1
Bibi Zahra Jo Bhi Aaya Aap Ki Dehleez Par.
Oss Ne Jo Manga Woh Paya Aap Ki Dehleez Par.
Aasmano Ki Bulandi Mujh Se Neechay Reh Gayi.
Main Ne Jab Sar Ko Jhukaya Aap Ki Dehleez Par.
Arshiyon Ne Be Tahasha Naan Jo Ki Shakal Mein.
Le Ke Khana Khoob Khaya Aap Ki Dehleez Par.

2
Betiyon Ki Kis Qadar Tazeem Hai Sarkar Ne.
Roz Khud Aa Kar Bataya Aap Ki Dehleez Par.
Na Mila Jab Aasra Islam Ne Thak Haar Ke.
Deen Ka Khaima Lagaya Aap Ki Dehleez Par.
Khud Bakhud Uthnay Laga Yasir Nigah-e-Dehar Mein.
Iss Ne Jab Khud Ko Giraya Aap Ki Dehleez Par.
