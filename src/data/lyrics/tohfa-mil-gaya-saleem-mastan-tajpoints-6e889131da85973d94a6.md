1
Kya Hasen Shair-e-Khuda Ko Hai Yeh Tohfa Mil Gaya.
Sorat-e-Abbas Mein Mohsin Dobarah Mil Gaya.
Roza-e-Abbas Par Jis Dam Pari Oss Ki Nazar.
Khana-e-Kaba Pukara Mujh Ko Kaba Mil Gaya.

2
Dolat-e-Karoon Ko Mur Kar Na Dekhein Gay Kabhi.
Hum Faqeeron Ko Dar-e-Ghazi Ka Sadqa Mil Gaya.
Barh Rahi Hai Din Ba Din Ab Tazgi Islam Ki.
Deen Ko Abbas Ke Hathoon Ka Saya Mil Gaya.

3
Roza-e-Abbas Se Mas Kar Ke Laya Hoon Kafan.
Mujh Ko Jeene Ke Liye Anmool Nuskha Mil Gaya.
Tu Hai Hami Hurmala Ka Rehnuma Hur Hai Mera.
Tujh Ko Jhota Mil Gaya Our Mujh Ko Sacha Mil Gaya.

4
Aaj Se Meri Bhi Batein Hoon Gi Hur Ki Bazm Mein.
Mere Ashkon Ko Bhi Ab Romal-e-Zahra Mil Gaya.
Dar Badar Ab Phir Nahi Saktay Mere Misray Waseem.
Kyunke Meri Fikar Ko Ghazi Ka Khaima Mil Gaya.
