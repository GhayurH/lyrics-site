1
Pisar Ruqaiya Ke Be Haal Maray Gaye.
Nehar Ke Pas Hi Pyason Ke Sar Utaray Gaye.
Woh Jin Chehron Ko Maa Ne Tha Chom Kar Bhaija.
Tamachay Unn Ke Hi Chehron Pe Haye Marey Gaye.

2
Katay Saron Ko Uthaya Hai Haye Baloon Se.
Our Lashon Par Yeh Ghorey Bhi Guzarey Gaye.
Lahu Se Khak Se Neelon Se Haye Eid Ke Din.
Ladlay Muslim Ke Logo Youn Sanwarey Gaye.

3
Ruqaiya Beton Ke Kurtay Thi Chom Kar Roti.
Maut Se Mushkil Yeh Lamhey They Guzarey Gaye.
Howa Hai Joun Hamara Maqam Youn Oncha.
Matami Keh Kar Jo Logon Mein Pukarey Gaye.
