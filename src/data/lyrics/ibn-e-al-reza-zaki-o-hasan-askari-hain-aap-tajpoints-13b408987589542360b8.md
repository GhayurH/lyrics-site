Ibn e Al Reza Zaki o Hasan Askari Hain Aap.
Anwar-e-Mustafa Mein Dhali Roshni Hain Aap.

1
Mauj Aloom Behar-e-Sakha-o-Atta Bhi Hain.
Aks-e-Nabi Kahin, Kahin Bilkul Ali Hain Aap.
Woh Harf Jo Dua Mein Asar Ki Daleel Hai.
Irfan-o-Aa Gahi Ka Woh Harf-e-Jali Hain Aap.

2
Ik Harf Mein Chupi Hoi Pori Kitab Hain.
Lashkar Hain Apni Zaat Mein Woh Askari Hain Aap.
Beta Imam-e-Asar Hai Hujjat Khuda Ki Hai.
Waris Imam-e-Asar Ke Haq Ke Wali Hain Aap.

3
Islam Ko Aseer Na Hone Diya Kabhi.
Zindaan Mein Jad Kabhi Rahe Qaidi Kabhi Hain Aap.
Munkir Khuda Ke Deen Ke Abtar Hoye Tamam.
Bo Lahbiyat Mein Naqsh-e-Abu Talbi Hain Aap.

4
Insan Dosti Ka Sabaq Aap Se Mila.
Nazaan Hai Jis Pe Dosti Woh Dosti Hain Aap.
Maula Ne Apne Faiz Se Shayar Bana Diya.
 Rehan Unn Ke Dam Se:REHAN AZMI: Hain Aap.
