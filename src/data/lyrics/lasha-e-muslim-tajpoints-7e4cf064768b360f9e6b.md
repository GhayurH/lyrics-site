Lasha-e-Muslim Pe Haye Ro Ke Zahra Ne Kaha.
Ya Ali Mara Gaya Hai Mera Beta Be Khata.

1
Zalimo Ne Ghair Ke Mara Hai Iss Ko Iss Tarha.
Chand Mere Ghar Ka Haye Badliyon Mein Ghir Gaya.
Shehar-e-Kofa Syeda Ko Raas Na Aaya Kabhi.
Aaj Phir Roney Ki Khatir Hai Yahan Aana Para.

2
Haye Tanha Jan Ke Mara Hazaron Ne Issay.
Sar Ta Pa Iss Ka Badan Hai Sara Zakhmo Se Bhara.
Pehle Tu Dar-Al-Amara Se Giraya Haye Haye.
Our Phir Pamal Sab Ne Lasha-e-Muslim Kiya.

3
Lash Par Royon Main Iss Ki Ya Karon Inn Ko Talash.
Ho Gaye Ghurbat Mein Iss Ke Dono Bachay Bhi Juda.
Aye Safeer-e-Karbala Bas Yeh Hai Aizaz-e- Raza.
Aap Jo Maqbool Kijiye Yeh Likha Noha Mera.
