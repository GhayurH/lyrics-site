1
Muslim Ki Gaud Se Do Bachay Bichar Gaye Hain.
Kofay Ki Sar Zameen Par Tanha Bhatak Rahe Hain.
Muslim Pukarey Bacho Waqt-e-Hayat Kam Hai.
Kal Tak Jo Sath They Woh Kofi Palat Gaye Hain.

2
Pardesi, Ajnabi Our Dushman Hai Sari Nagri.
Yaad Aa Gayi Hai Maa Ki Bachay Sisak Rahe Hain.
Phirtay Hain Marey Marey Do Tashna Lab Musafir.
Bikhri Hoi Hain Zulfein Pairon Mein Aablay Hain.

3
Raat Aayi Khuwab Aisi Jaag Uthay Zakhmi Bhai.
Dekha Jo Bahein Kholay Baba Bola Rahe Hain.
Hum Hashmi Hain Haris Dartay Nahi Qaza Se.
Ishq-e-Hussain Mein Ab Katney Kayi Galay Hain.

4
Dakhil Hoye Jo Kofa Gash Kar Gayi Ruqaiya.
Aaya Salam-e-Muslim, Abid Tarap Gaye Hain.
Bachoon Se Milney Debal Ik Karwan Aaya.
Naizon Pe Chand Sar Hain Rasiyon Mein Kuch Galay Hain.
