Main Puttar Jawan Hussain Da Saan Menu Shaam Zaeef Banaya

Meri Mona Kammar Jhuka Chori  Tey Payshiyan Khoon Rwaya

Assi Loog Shareef Madinay Dey
Kadi Galiyan Mour Na Veekhay
Mastooran Tey Majbooran Nu
Main Aap Bazar Lay Aya

Main Ghairatan Wala Abid Han
Tusi Sabar Mera Azmao Na
Main Baywa Bhain Dey Haatan Vich
Khud Shaam Da Zeevar Paiya

Sajjad Di Khasta Haali Da
Ais Gal To Andaza Ker Lo
Jinay Qaidi Bhain Dey Laashay Nu
Aik Cholay Vich Dafnaya

Tafsseran Paak Quran Diyan
Ajj Baaj Gillaf Day Aiyan
Tussan Qaidi Parday Daaran Nu
Sir nangay Shaam Pharaiya

Sirr Saang Tay Aalma Valay Da
Ay Manzar Vekh Kay Dig Penda
Haath Rassiyan Zahra Jaiyaan Nay
Sirr Mittiyan Naal Lukaaya.

Tatheer Di Varis Majmeyaan Jay
Rayhi Boldi Naal Shaarbi Day
Baara Okha Zahra Jai Nu
Bazaar Abhoor Karaaya.

Taboot Te Pak Jageeraan Nu Akhiyaan Nal Bosay De Ke
Samar Ne Ujariyaan Dhiyaan Da Aj Sara Haal Sunaya
