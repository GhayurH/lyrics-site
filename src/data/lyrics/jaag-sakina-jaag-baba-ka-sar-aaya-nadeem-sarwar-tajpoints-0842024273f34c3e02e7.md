Jaag Sakina Jaag Baba Ka Sar Aaya.

1
Lo Ho Gayi Rehai Lo Qafila Chala.
Sar Jab Har Aik Shaheed Ka Sajjad Ko Mila.
Tab Marqad-e-Sakina Pe Sajjad Ne Kaha.

2
Baba Tumhara Aaya Giya Waqt Neend Ka.
Seene Pe Sone Wali Mati Pe So Gayi.
Baba Ko Rote Rote Jo Khamosh Ho Gayi.
Ro Ro Keh Maa Ne Qabar-e-Sakina Pe Yeh Kaha.

3
Bibi Karo Salam Adab Ka Muqam Hai.
Bibi Tere Sirhane Sheh-e-Tishna Kam Hai.
Aye Jan Jaldi Badlo yeh Kurta Phata Howa.

4
Baba Ko Dhondti Thin Sakina Utho Utho.
Raton Ko Jagti Thin Sakina Utho Utho.
Lo Aa Gaye Hain Baba Aye Jaan Jaag Ja.

5
Baba Ke Paas Sona Na Sona Zameen Par.
Rakho Labon Ko Lab Par Jabeen Ko Jaben Par.
Aada Ki Ghurkiyon Ka Zamana Guzar Giya.
Jaag Sakina Jaag Baba Ka Sar Aya.
Paigham Roz Deti Thin Ammo Ke Naam Tum.
Roti Thin Yaad Kar Ke Unhein Subh-o-Sham Tum.
Jago Keh Ja Rahe Hain Jahan Sote Hain Chacha.
Jaag Sakina Jaag Baba Ka Sar Aya.
Kis Tarha Soyi Bali Sakina Na Pochiye.
Ranj-o-Alam Se Phatta Hai Seena Na Pochiye.
Jagi Na Phir Kabhi Woh Bohat Maa Ne Di Sada.
Jaag Sakina Jaag Baba Ka Sar Aya.
Sadiyaan Guzar Gayi Hain Magar:SARWAR-O-REHAN:.
Zindan-e-Sham Roye Tu Rota Hai Aasman.
Ab Bhi Pukarte Hain Yehi Shah-e-Karbala.
Jaag Sakina Jaag Baba Ka Sar Aya.
