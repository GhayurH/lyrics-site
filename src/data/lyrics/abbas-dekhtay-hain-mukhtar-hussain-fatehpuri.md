Abbas Dekhtay Hain Mukhtar Hussain Fatehpuri.

1
Ghazi, Jarri, Ghazanfar.
Shair-e-Khuda Ke Dilbar.
Ain-e-Khuda Ke Mazhar.
Abbas Dekhtey Hain.
Ghaiz-o-Ghazab Mein Aa Kar Abbas Dekhtey Hain.
Ran Mein Bapa Hai Mehshar Abbas Dekhtey Hain.

2
Yeh Keh Ke Bhagtay They Darya Se Ghaat Walay.
Larna Tu Door Yaan Par Hain Zindagi Ke Lalay.
Hai Sans Lena Dobhar Abbas Dekhtey Hain.
Zainab Ke Ladlay Jab Maidaan Mein Larney Aaye.
Yeh Bool Kar Jarri Ne Hain Hoslay Barhaye.
Hamla Karo Barabar Abbas Dekhtey Hain.

3
Dushman Ka Jab Risala Khaimay Hatanay Aaya.
Khat Khainch Kar Jarri Ne Ghaiz-o-Ghazab Mein Bola.
Ab Aaye Koi Bhar Kar Abbas Dekhtey Hain.
Karb-o-Bala Ke Ban Ko Yaad Aa Gaya Hai Khaybar.
Haider Ke Ladlay Mein Haider Ke Hi Hain Tewar.
Sab Kanptay Hain Thar Thar Abbas Dekhtey Hain.

4
Hai Khof Se Kaleja Dushman Ka Pani Pani.
Hairat Se Ruk Gayi Hai Mojon Ki Bhi Rawani.
Saktay Mein Hai Samandar Abbas Dekhtey Hain.
Dushman Pukar Uthay Salar Ho Tu Aisa.
Lashkar Bana Diya Hai Ghazi Ne Apne Jaisa.
Lagta Hai Youn Bahatar Abbas Dekhtey Hain.

5
Kab Kis Ko Hai Bolana Chalti Hai Inn Ki Marzi.
Karni Ho Gar Ziyarat Bhejo Inhi Ko Arzi.
Karb-o-Bala Ka Daftar Abbas Dekhtey Hain.
Itni Pasand Aayi Saqayi e Sakina.
Haq Ne Inhein Nawaza Mehshar Mein Aisa Rutba.
Sab Intezam e Kausar Abbas Dekhtey Hain.
