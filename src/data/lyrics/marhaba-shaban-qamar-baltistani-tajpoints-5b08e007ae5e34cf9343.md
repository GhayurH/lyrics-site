Marhaba Shaban Qamar Baltistani Lyrics
Farsh-e-Mehfil Pe Hum Kar Rahe Hain Yeh Jo Zikar-e-Wila Baithay Baithay.
Ba Jamat Namaz-e-Mawaddat Ho Rahi Hai Ada Baithay Baithay.
Baal-o-Par Dein Gay Fitrus Ko Maula Apne Jholay Mein Hi Letey Letey.
Our Rahib Ko De Dein Gay Betay Shah-e-Karb-o-Bala Baithay Baithay.
Aye Tabeeb e Zamana Yeh Dekho La Dawa Jin Ko Tum Keh Rahe They.
Unn Mareezo Ko Sehan-e-Haram Mein Mil Rahi Hai Shifa Baithay Baithay.
Aa Gayi Thi Mere Ghar Mein Mushkil Aa Ke Foran Hi Wapis Gayi Thi.
Aik Chadar Bicha Kar Parhi Thi Bas Hadees-e-Kisa Baithay Baithay.
Meri Neki Badi Likhney Walay Mere Haq Mein Dua Likh Rahe They.
Mere Kandhoon Ke Dono Farishtay Jab Gaye Karbala Baithay Baithay.
Kyun Pareshaan Ho Ghabra Rahe Ho Aao Parcham Ke Saye Mein Baitho.
Jo Bhi Kehna Hai Ghazi Se Keh Do Pori Hogi Dua Baithay Baithay.
Sham Se Jab Bhi Zair Hain Aate Baith Jata Hoon Qadmo Mein Unn Ke.
Aarzu Hai Keh Aik Din Mujhe Phir Youn Hi Aaye Qaza Baithay Baithay.
Keh Do Akhtar Yeh Ulfat Nahi Hai Yeh Taqaza Wilayat Ka Kab Hai.
Hum Pe Wajib Hoin Jo Namazein Kar Rahe Hain Qaza Baithay Baithay.
