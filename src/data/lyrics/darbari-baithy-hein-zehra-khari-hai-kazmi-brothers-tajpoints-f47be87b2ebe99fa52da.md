Darbari Baithy Hein Zehra Khari Hai Kazmi Brothers.

1
Jo Samjhay Musalmaan Musibat Yehi Hai.
Keh Darbari Baithay Hain Zahra Khari Hai.
Sitam Hai Muhammad Ki Tehreer Parh Kar.
Sabhi Hans Rahe Hain Wohi Ro Rahi Hai.
Hijab-e-Khuda Hai Jo Bint-e-Nabi Hai.
Woh Darbar Mein Jane Kab Se Khari Hai.

2
Diya Aisay Ummat Ne Baba Ka Pursa.
Batool Apne Pehlu Ke Bal Gir Pari Hai.
Bata Mujh Ko Amma Mera Jurm Kya Tha?.
Yeh Mohsin Ki Ab Bhi Sada Aa Rahi Hai.
Karey Gi Na Zahra Ab Apna Koi Gham.
Ali Ke Galay Mein Rasan Dekh Li Hai.

3
Khiyal Aa Gaya Hai Be Rida Betiyon Ka.
Woh Ghazi Ka Kurta Jabhi Si Rahi Hai.
Hai Mushkil Bara Qabar-e-Zahra Banana.
Ali Ke Labon Pe Bhi Nad-e-Ali Hai.
Batool Ab Bhi Qabar-e-Shikasta Se Ahsan.
Imam-e-Zaman Ko Sada De Rahi Hai.
