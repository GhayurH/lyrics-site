1
Kis Ne Kab Kalma Parha Tha Yeh Kahani Phir Sahi.
Kaun Kab Maidaan Se Bhaga Yeh Kahani Phir Sahi.
Fatima Ke Dar Pe Taray Ko Utarta Dekh Kar.
Kis Ka Kaisay Utra Chehra Yeh Kahani Phir Sahi.

2
Bas Yeh Kafi Hai Ali Khaybar Ulat Kar Aa Gaye.
Kis Ne Kitna Maal Loota Yeh Kahani Phir Sahi.
Zulfiqar-e-Haidery Se Bach Gaya Tha Umar-e-Aas.
Haan Magar Kaisay Bacha Tha Yeh Kahani Phir Sahi.

3
Umar Bhar Naraz Jis Se Bint-e-Peygamber Rahi.
Oss Shaqi Ka Naam Kya Tha Yeh Kahani Phir Sahi.
Mat Karo Aapas Mein Jhagra Hai Yeh Paigham Habib.
Main Howa Tha Kaisay Zindah Yeh Kahani Phir Sahi.

4
Jis Assa Ke Aasrey Par Umar Bhar Mosa Rahe.
Oss Assa Par Kya Likha Tha Yeh Kahani Phir Sahi.
Pardah-e-Ghaibat Se Jab Bhi Aaye Ga Mera Imam.
Kis Ka Pehle Sar Urrey Ga Yeh Kahani Phir Sahi.

5
Iss Mein Ghairon Ke Nahi Apno Ke Bhi Naam Aaein Gaye.
Kaun Hai Dushman Ali Ka Yeh Kahani Phir Sahi.
Kaun Tha Mairaj Mein Sajjad Mehway Guftgu.
Rehne Do Parday Ka Pardah Yeh Kahani Phir Sahi.
