1
Ya Imam e Raza Ya Imam e Raza
Mujh Ko Bakhsha Sharf Apne Deedar Ka Ya Imam-e-Raza Ya Imam-e-Raza
Aap Ke Dar Pe Aya Ghulam Aap Ka Ya Imam-e-Raza Ya Imam-e-Raza
Yeh Jo Mashad Mein Hai Roza-e-Pur Zia Yeh Nagar Aap Ka Goya Arsh-e-Ullah
Iss Ke Har Baam-o-Dar Noor Ka Silsila Ya Imam-e-Raza Ya Imam-e-Raza
Aap Ke Jad Ali Aap Bhi Hain Ali Kun Fa Kaan Ke Tu Mukhtar Hai Aap Bhi
Aap Ko Bhi Nusehri Na Keh De Khuda Ya Imam-e-Raza Ya Imam-e-Raza
Zairon Par Karam Dekh Kar Aap Ka Dekh Kar Woh Shifa Bakhsh Baab-e-Shifa
Meri Aankhoon Se Ashkoon Ka Darya Baha Ya Imam-e-Raza Ya Imam-e-Raza
Jab Bhi Mushkil Pari Ya Bala Aa Gayi Ya Musayab Ke Tofan Aane Lage
Aa Giya Lab Pa Mere Be-Sakhta Ya Imam-e-Raza Ya Imam-e-Raza
Aap Chahein Tu Zinda Ko Murda Karein Zindagi Shir-e-Qaleen Ko Baksh Dein
Jo Kalam-e-Khuda Woh Kalam Aap Ka Ya Imam-e-Raza Ya Imam-e-Raza
Zikar Chirta Hai Jab Bhi Khurasan Ka Yaad Aati Hai Mashad Ki Jab Woh Fiza
Kehta Hai Dam Ba-Dam Qalb-e-:MAJID RAZA: Ya Imam-e-Raza Ya Imam-e-Raza
