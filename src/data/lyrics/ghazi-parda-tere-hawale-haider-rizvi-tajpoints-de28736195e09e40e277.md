1
Ghazi Parda Tere Hawale Haider Rizvi.
Zakhmi Ali Yeh Bolay Abbas Ko Bola Do.
Mera Waqt Aakhri Hai Zainab Ossay Sada Do.
Mere Baad Hai Tumhara Ik Aakhri Sahara.
Shabbar, Shabbir Mujh Ko Abbas Se Mila Do.
Meri Betiyon Ka Ghazi Pardah Tere Hawalay.
Mere Baad Teri Behnein Beta Tere Hawalay.
Tum Sath Sath Chalna Inn Se Juda Na Hona.
Meri Misal Inn Pe Ban Ke Saya Tere Hawalay.
Abbas Yeh Wasiyat Baba Ki Yaad Rakhna.
Mushkil Mein Sath Inn Ke Rehna Tere Hawalay.
Sani-e-Syeda Hai Mere Baad Yeh Na Roye.
Meri Jan Meri Beti Zahra Tere Hawalay.
Nazoon Se Syeda Ne Pala Hai Inn Ko Beta.
Yeh Syeda Ka Sara Kunba Tere Hawalay.
Kofa Ho Ya Madina Ho Sham Ya Keh Karbal.
Meri Betiyon Ka Beta Jana Tere Hawalay.
Aagosh Mein Qaza Ne Ab Le Liya Ali Ko.
Aye Maut Ke Farishtay Aaqa Tere Hawalay.
Darwazay Tak Tu Beti Sath Aayi Janazay Ke.
Ghazi Se Dar Pe Boli Baba Tere Hawalay.
Bint-e-Ali Hussaini Tujh Ko Duaein De Gi.
 Haider Yeh Sara Noha Parhna Tere Hawalay.
