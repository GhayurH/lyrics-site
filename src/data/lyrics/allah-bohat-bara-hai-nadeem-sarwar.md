Allah Bohat Bara Hai Nadeem Sarwar

1
Quran Mein Likha Hai ALLAH Bohat Bara Hai
Sab Ne Yehi Kaha Hai ALLAH Bohat Bara Hai
Har Had Se Mawra Hai ALLAH Bohat Bara Hai
Sab Ne Yehi Kaha Hai ALLAH Bohat Bara Hai
Yeh Mehro Mah-o-Anjum Daryaon Mein Talatum
Chalti Hoi Hawaein Aawaz Ka Taranum

2
Khush Rang Tairoon Ka Phaila Howa Tabasum
Tarteeb Waar Gunchay Aisay Keh Rashk-e-Anjum
Yeh Kun Ka Mojza Hai ALLAH Bohat Bara Hai
Mati Ke Paikaron Mein Tehreek Dalta Hai
Soye Falak Zameen Pe Taray Ochalta Hai
Pataal Se Jawahir Woh Hi Nikalta Hai

3
Parwardigar Woh Hai Dunya Ko Palta Hai
Har Had Se Mawra Hai ALLAH Bohat Bara Hai
Konain Mein Ayyaan Hai Ya Rab Jo Noor Tera
Hai Taab Kis Nazar Mein Dekhey Zahoor Tera
Tasbeeh Khawahaan Hum Kya Hai Koh-e-Toor Tera
Jis Ko Bhi Mil Gaya Hai Maula Shaoor Tera

4
Woh Mustafa Howa Hai ALLAH Bohat Bara Hai
Tu Ba Kamal Jaisa Waisay Tere Payamber
Sab Tere Madah Khuwaan Hain Sab Hi Tere Sana Gar
Kya Badshah-o-Qudsi Kya Muflis-o-Gadagar
Inn Sari Hastiyon Mein Woh Aamina Ka Dilbar
Woh Bhi Tu Lab Kusha Hai ALLAH Bohat Bara Hai

5
Qausain Ki Thi Manzil Hairan Anbiya They
Jibreel Ruk Gaye They Ik Marhalay Pe Aa Ke
Rastay Banaye Tu Ne Parday Hata Hata Ke
Mairaj Par Muhammad Pohanchay Teri Riza Se
Ik Shoor Gonjta Hai ALLAH Bohat Bara Hai
Jaisay Hi Guftgu Ki ALLAH Ne Nabi Se

6
Bolay Nabi Yeh Lehja Marboot Hai Ali Se
Hairaan Hoye Muhammad Iss Lehja-e-Khafi Se
Kya Guftgu Hai Meri ALLAH Ke Wali Se
Yeh Raaz Gaib Ka Hai ALLAH Bohat Bara Hai
ALLAH Ki Barai Youn Tu Sabhi Ne Ki Hai
Lekin Jo Karbala Ki Tareekh Bolti Hai

7
Nook-e-Sina Se Rab Ki Tauseef Ho Chuki Hai
Takbeer Zair-e-Khanjar Shabeer Ne Kahi Hai
Mazloom Ki Sada Hai ALLAH Bohat Bara Hai
Kis Ne Kiya Hai Sajdah Khanjar Talay Jahan Mein
Kantay Paray Hoye They Jab Pyas Se Zabaan Mein
Jab Aag Lag Rahi Thi Zahra Ke Gulistaan Mein

8
Chup Chup Ke Chand Taray Rotay They Aasmaan Mein
Maula Ne Tab Kaha Hai ALLAH Bohat Bara Hai
Kya Nafas-e-Mutmaina Sajjad Ne Tha Paya
Ik Ik Sitam Pe Jis Ne Sajday Mein Sar Jhukaya
Tauq-e-Garaan Pehan Kar Shikwa Na Lab Pe Laya
Kunbay Ko Apne Le Kar Ta Sham Tha Jo Aaya

9
Har Gaam Par Kaha Hai ALLAH Bohat Bara Hai
Seene Pe Barchi Kha Keh Akbar Ne Yeh Sada Di
Yeh Aakhri Hai Manzil Khushnodi-e-Khuda Ki
Khak-e-Shifa Bana Di Karb-o-Bala Ki Mati
Bolay:REHAN-o-SARWAR: Tareekh Karbala Bhi
Maula Ne Sach Kaha Hai ALLAH Bohat Bara Hai
