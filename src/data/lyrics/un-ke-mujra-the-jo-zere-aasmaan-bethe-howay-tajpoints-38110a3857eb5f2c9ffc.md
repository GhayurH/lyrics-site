Un Ke Mujra The Jo Zere Aasmaan Bethe Howay.
Bhoke Piyase Be Watan Be Khanmaan Bethe Howay.

1
Shoor Matam Sun Ke Ahl-e-Bait Ka Sab Ahl-e-Sham.
Shadiyaan Karte Tha Ghar Mein Shadmaan Bethe Howay.
Shah Oss par Bhi Utha Detey The Udda Ke Qadam.
Teer Tan Pe Dil Peh Daag Jaan Sataan Bethe Howay.

2
Wa Darega Dast Abid Mein Tu Ho Un Ki Muhar.
Our Ontoon Par Chalein Kuch Sarbaan Bethe HOway.
Karbala Se Shaam Tak Dam Dam Ki Jati Thi Khabar.
Ja Baja The Daak Par Sab Khat Rasaan Bethe Howay.

3
Umat-e-Aasi Ke Haq Mein Shah Ne Mangi Dua.
Janib Qibla Zameen Par Neem Jaan Bethe Howay.
Jab Madine Mein Shahadat Ki Khabar Ur Kar Gayi.
Kuch Khare Rote The Kuch Peer-o-Jawaan Bethe Howay.

4
Kofiyon Ne Khud Bula Kar Yeh Sitam Barpa Kiya.
Apne Ghar The Chain Se Shah Zamaan Bethe Howay.
Halq Par Khanjar Chala Sibt-e-Rasool Allah Ke.
Khai Hain Abid Ne Gham Ki Barchiyan Bethe Howay.

5
Bethe Bethe Pusht-e-Zeen Par Hi Pari Sheh Ne Namaz.
Zakham Kari The Bohat Ta Istakhwaan Bethe Howay.
Rah Tasleem-o-Raza Mein Ahle-e-Bain Mustafa.
Sabar Ka Karte The Baham Imtehaan Bethe Howay.

6
Hazrat Abid Ko Zindaan Mein Bhi Tha Itna Lehaz.
Hum Se Ghafil Ho Na Dar Par Pasbaan Bethe Howay.
Raat Ko Chup Chaap Hoti Thi Koi Dam Ko Agar.
Phir Hila Dete The Apni Beriyaan Bethe Howay.

7
Shah Ke Matam Mein Roye Hain Bohat Hoor-o-Malak.
Dekhna Janat Mein Bhi Hoon Gay Makaan Bethe Howay.
Haj Ziyaraat Kar Chuke Ab Karbala Ko Bhi Chalo.
 Daag Mudat Ho Gayi Tum Ko Yahan Bethe Howay.
