Main Kar Raha Hoon Sana Shukriya Hussain Tera.

1
Woh Tera Aakhri Sajda Bacha Giya Tauheed.
Yeh Keh Raha Hai Khuda Shukriya Hussain Tera.
Faqat Main Khak Thi,Khak-e-Shifa Banaya Mujhe.
Pukari Karb-o-Bala Shukriya Hussain Tera.
Namaz parhne Ko, Jate Howay Musalle Par.
Kaha Karo Yeh Sada Shukriya Hussain Tera.

2
Pohanch Ke Karbala, Mula Hussain Ke Dar Par.
Main Yeh Hi Keh Ke Jhuka Shukriya Hussain Tera.
Siway-e-Gham Ke Tere Mujh Ko Iss Zamane Mein.
Koi Bhi Gham Na Mila Shukriya Hussain Tera.
Jo Main Ne Khon Bahaya Tu Madar-e-Sheh Ki.
Hoi Naseeb Dua Shukriya Hussain Tera.

3
Hai Kainat Mein Insaniyaat Tere Dam Se.
Tu Sab Pe Farz Howa Shukriya Hussain Tera.
Baroz-e-Hashar Howa Oss Ka Shakiron Mein Shumar.
Jo Shaks Karta Raha Shukriya Hussain Tera.
Karm Yeh Dekh Lo Raqib Hussain Ka Mujh Par.
Main Shair Kehne Laga Shukriya Hussain Tera.
