1
Fatima Kay Baghair Wajhi Hasan Zaidi
Kuch Nahi LaIlaha Fatima Ke Beghair.
Deen Kya? Bizat e Mustafa Ke Beghair.
Na Mukamal Hai Farsh-e-Aza-e-Hussain.
Syeda Ki Hadees-e-Kisa Ke Beghair.
Dil Yeh Kehta Hai Janat Mein Koi Bashar.
Ja Nahi Sakta Hur Ki Raza Ke Beghair.
Khalq Hotey Nahi Kun Se Hum Matami.
Bint-e-Ahmad Tumhari Dua Ke Beghair.
Jo Nabi Arsh Par Jata Ho Marzi Se.
Ghar Mein Aaya Na Teri Raza Ke Beghair.
Kuch Nahi Ya Ali, Ya Hasan, Ya Hussain.
Fatima, Fatima, Fatima Ke Beghair.
Be Amal Tha Magar Tha Muhib e Batool.
Seedha Janat Mein Pohancha Saza Ke Beghair.
Mar Hi Jaein Namazein Na Ho Gar Darood.
Sans Aati Nahi Hai Hawa Ke Beghair.
Ibn-e-Mariyam Na Hota Maseeha Kabhi.
Syeda Tere Dar Al Shifa Ke Beghair.
Mana Salman Keh Kar Bulatay Hain Yeh.
Jao Agar Inn Ke Dar Par Ana Ke Beghair.
Bolay Jibreel Koi Nabi Kuch Nahi.
Syeda Fatima Ki Atta Ke Beghair.
Bibi Se Bugz Hai? Phir Tu Quran Parh.
Hal Atta Ke Beghair Innama Ke Beghair.
Pak Zahra Khuda Ki Tarha Hain Kareem.
Baat Sunti Hain Yeh Iltija Ke Beghair.
Bolay Mushkil Kusha Mushkilein Hal Na Hoon.
Syeda Fatima Tahira Ke Beghair.
Na Mukamil Hai Quraan Jo Pocha Gaya?.
Bola Kausar Keh Haan Syeda Ke Beghair.
Khud Shifa Mustafa Ko Bhi Milti Nahi.
Saya-e-Chadar-e-Fatima Ke Beghair.
Woh Hai Matam Ka Halqa Sun Aye Molvi.
Tu Wahan Ja Amama Qaba Ke Beghair.
Chal Wahan Hai Shifa Khana e Syeda.
Kaun Loota Wahan Se Shifa Ke Beghair?.
Rehta Be Saya Deen-e-Khuda Aye Aimaad.
Syeda Fatima Ki Rida Ke Beghair.
