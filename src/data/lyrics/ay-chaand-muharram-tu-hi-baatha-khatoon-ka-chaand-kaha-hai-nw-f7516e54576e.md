Ay Chaand Muharram Tu Hi Baatha, Khatoon Ka Chaand Kaha Hai

Abaad Hain Duniya Saari  Zahra Ka Chaman Veeran Hai.

Tu Par Jibareel Ki Zeenat Ka Ku Lasha Tera Pamal Hua

Bay Goro Kafan Ho Bhai Meray

Zainab Ko Yahee Armaan Hai.

Dekha Naa Suna Zaa Hir Aisa Zakhmo Sai Badaan Hai Choor Magar

Jaari Hai Zubaan Pai Zikera Khuda

Gardan Pe Khanjar Ravan Hai.

Kathi Hai Sakina Abid Ko Is Qaid Mai Mai Maar Jaowoongi

Chooro Na Akela Bhai

Thareekh Bohth Zindaan Hai.

Ai Kufiyon Mai Hun Bint-e-Ali Aur Waaris-e-Chadaar-e-Zahra Kee

Deyko Na Tamaasha Sharam Karo

Ab Zainab Saar Ureeyaan Hai.

Aulaad Ka Ghaam Bhaiyon Kay Varaam Kaat Ta Hai Kalayja Kadam Kadaam

Hai Kaisay Utayay Khoon Bharee

Akbar Kee Laash Jawaan Hai.

Tanveer Utta Kaar Asghar Ko Syed Nay Sawalay Aap Kiya

Tum Bhi Ho Musalman Reham Karo

Beysheer Kee Khushak Zubaan Hai.
