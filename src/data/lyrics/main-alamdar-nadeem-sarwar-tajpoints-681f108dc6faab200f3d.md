Alamdar, Alamdar.

1
Bawafa Ya Abbas.

2
Dekho Woh Tarai Mein Hai Ik Mard-e-Wafadar.
Rakhay Hai Alam Doosh Pe Pyason Ka Tarafdar.
Hai Gonji Issi Shair Ki Ghazi Ki Yeh Lalkar.
Gar Ijazat De Hussain Arsh Hila Sakta Hoon.
Iss Alam Se Nayi Tareekh Bana Sakta Hoon.
Haan Main Alamdar.
Main Ho Abbas-e-Ali Main Wafaon Ka Wali.
Baat Jab Meri Chali Gaunj Utha Ali Ali.
Haan Meri Wafa Ali Ada Ali Sada Ali.
Haan Meri Abba Ali Qaba Ali Nawa Ali.
Main Agar Chahon Tu Ik Hashar Utha Sakta Hoon.
Jalwa e Haider-e-Karrar Dikha Sakta Hoon.
Main Alamdar-e-Hussain Main Hoon Salar-e-Hussain.
Main Wafadar-e-Hussain Main Hoon Ghamkhuwar-e-Hussain.
Haan Mera Shah-e-Ummam Jan-o-Dillam Hussain Hussain.
Haan Mera Jah-o-Hasham Mera Bharam Hussain Hussain.
Shan-e-Shabbir Hai Kya Aaj Bata Sakta Hoon.
Tum Ko Aadab-e-Hussaini Main Sikha Sakta Hoon.
Yeh Muqadar Hai Mera Yeh Alam Mujh Ko Mila.
Main Alamdar-e-Wafa Haider-e-Karb-o-Bala.
Yeh Alam Khula Rahe Utha Rahe Saja Rahe.
Yeh Alam Jama Rahe Datta Rahe Sada Rahe.
Issi Darya Pe Alam Apna Saja Sakta Hoon.
Our Katay Hathoon Se Parcham Yeh Utha Sakta Hoon.
Main Tu Aaya Hi Nahi Jang Larney Ke Liye.
Mashk Bharni Hai Mujhe Pyasay Bachoon Ke Liye.
Hai Yeh Wadah Hai Sakina Se Nibhana Hai Mujhe.
Pani Lena Hai Kissi Se Bhi Na Larna Hai Mujhe.
Mujh Ko Larna Jo Para Kya Hoon Bata Sakta Hoon.
Ghaat Se Lakhon Ke Lashkar Ko Bhaga Sakta.
Aaya Darya Pe Jari Kanp Uthi Foaj-e-Shaqi.
Yeh Hai Abbas-e-Jari Ya Hai Waqai Ali.
Bhag Uthi Foaj-e-Jafa Ghazi Jidhar Jidhar Mura.
Jis Ka Munh Jidhar Utha Bhaga Gira Nahi Ruka.
Bola Abbas-e-Jari Taig Utha Sakta Hoon.
Karbala Mein Tumhein Siffeen Dikha Sakta Hoon.
Bhar Ke Mashkeza Chala Jab Alamdar-e-Wafa.
Aa Gayi Foaj-e-Jafa Ghir Gaya Maula Mera.
Haan Woh Mashkeza Chida Bazu Kata Pani Baha.
Ghazi Reti Pe Gira Shor-e-Qayamat Tha Bapa.
Aayi Ghazi Ki Sada Jan Luta Sakta Hoon.
Kaisay Pani Ke Bina Khaimon Mein Ja Sakta Hoon.
