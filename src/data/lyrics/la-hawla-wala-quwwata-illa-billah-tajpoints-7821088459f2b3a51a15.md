La Hawla Wala Quwwata illa Billah.
Main Our Karon Dushman-e-Haider Ki Sana.

1
Dolat Ke Liye Phir Gaye Haq Se Yaksar.
Tum Dushman-e-Shabir Ho Lanat Tum Par.
Hur Sham Ke Lashkar Se Yeh Keh Kar Nikla.
Haider Ki Wilayat Pe Jo Shak Karta Hai.

2
Eman Hai Mera Ke Woh Jab Marta Hai.
Parhti Hai Ajal Dekh Ke Oss Ka Chehra.
Aal-e-Abu Sufyan(L) Se Hamdardi Hai.
Aye Dunya Adawat Ki Bhi Had Hoti Hai.

3
Kafir Abu Talib Hain Tu Islam Hai Kya.
Tu Meri Kahi Baat Pe Shak Karta Hai.
ALLAH Ke Bande Ko Khuda Kehta Hai.
Yeh Keh Ke Nuseri Ko Ali Ne Mara.

4
Eman-o-Aqeedey Ki Dursti Kar Le.
Hai Shakal Buri Baat Tu Achi Kar Le.
Are Kehta Hai Muhammad Ko Tu Apne Jaisa.
Takrane Lagein Kofe Se Fojein Ja Ke.

5
Sheh Ne kiye Iss Tarha Se Satrah Hamle.
Har Hamle Pe Kehta Raha Ibn-e-Zahra.
Mat Ye Kaho Iblees Ke Jaise Hoon Gaye.
Socho Tu Zara Loag Wo Kaise Hoon Gaye.

6
Iblees Jinhein Dekh Ke Parhta Hoo Ga.
Bachna Aye Takallum Yeh Marz Hai Moozi.
Hain Samne Aankhon Ke Misalein Kitni.
Shetan Bana Deta Hai Shohrat Ka Nasha
