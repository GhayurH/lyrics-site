1
Ho Karam Hum Pe Phir Ik Bar Qalandar Saeen.
Sindh Walo Youn Tumhein Chain Se Sona Hai Naseeb.
Shehar-e-Sehwan Mein Hain Bedar Qalandar Saeen.
Tu Ne Lehra Diye Ghar Ghar Pe Alam Ghazi Ke.
Tujh Se Razi Hai Alamdar Qalandar Saeen.
Mera Dil Kehta Hai Shabir Bulate Hoon Gaye.
Lal Keh Kar Tumhein Har Bar Qalandar Saeen.
Gonjta Rehta Hai Har Waqt Tere Roze Par.
Nara-e-Haider-e-Karar Qalandar Saeen.
Hai Yaqeen Lashkar-e-Mehdi Mein Bhi Ho Gi Mojood.
Aap Ke Naam Ki Talwar Qalandar Saeen.
Inn Dhamakon Ki Dhamak Se Nahi Darne Wale.
Tere Darbar Ke Zawar Qalandar Saeen.
Woh Takallum Ki Tarha Dalay Ga Masti Mein Dhamal.
Jo Tujhe Dekh Le Ik Bar Qalandar Saeen.
