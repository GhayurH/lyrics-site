Jadon Akbar Nu Jandi Wari Sughra Ne Aakhiya Alwida.
Kadon Aawein Ga Veerna Ujari Nu Dasda Ja.Parh Ke Bibi Inna Lillah Veer Akbar Da Seena Chumiya.

1
Kiwein Nibhni Aye Bhain Di Main Janra Ya Khuda.Mintaan Maniyaan Deway Balay Sohna Akbar Sehrey Laway.
Haye Barchi Ne Rolne Chan Veran Mere Cha.
Naa Ro Sughra Chadday Rahwaan De Ijazat Karbal Jawaan.
Haye Nanay De Deen Nu Sughra Main Deney Sah.

2
Wadah Satwein Da Si Nana Mainu Lain Layi Akbar Aana.
Toon Naa Aayon Veerna Naa Ghazi Bawafa.
Tere Veer Nu Pursa Dewan Matami Sare Karbal Jawan.
Suno Bibi Aye Iltija Karey Mintaan Aye Raza.
