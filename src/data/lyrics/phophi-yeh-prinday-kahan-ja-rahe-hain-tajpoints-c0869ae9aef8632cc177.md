Phophi Yeh Prinday Kahan Ja Rahe Hain.

1
Sar-e-Sham Aik Din Sakina Ne Pocha.

2
Kahin Inke Bache Bhi Hain Qaid Mein Kya.

3
Yeh Zahir Hai In Se Ke Hain Shad Sare.
Khuda Ke Karam Se Hain Aazad Sare.
Hamare Muqaddar Mein Hai Qaid Khana.
Khain In Ke Bache Tu Pyase Nahi Hain.
Kahin In Ke Bache Tu Bhoke Nahi Hain.
Uthaye Hain Chonchon Mein Sab Aab-o-Dana.
Najaf Ka Koi In Mein Zair Bhi Hoga..
Madine Ka Koi Musafir Bhi Hoga.
Kahan Ke Hain Sakan Kahan Hai Thikana.
Biyan Karbala Ka Safar Ho Sake Tu.
Inhein Rook Lo Tum Agar Ho Sake Tu.
Sunaon Gi Main Inko Gham Ka Fasana.
Watan Ki Muhabbat Satati Hai Hum Ko.
Yeh Lagta Hai Sughra Bulati Hai Hum Ko.
Howa Hai Humein Ghar Gaye Aik Zamana.
Musibat Ka Alam Qafs Ka Andhera.
Howa Qatal Bhai Bhi Baba Bhi Mera.
Madine Ko Bhejon Gi Main Yeh Sandesa.
Watan Ki Thi Hasrat Sakina Pe Tari.
Howay Ashk Aankhon Se Zainab Ki Jari.
Bayan Sakina Pe Har Aik Chup Tha.
Sada Aa Rahi Thi Yeh Shahi Barabar.
Kabhi Aana Ho Jo Hamari Lahad Par.
Yahan Aa Ke Yeh Mera Noha Sunana.
