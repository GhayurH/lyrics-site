Kehti thi ye zainab e lachaar qatl alamdar hogaya hai

sar se meri jayegi dastaar qatl alamdar hogaya hai

Bain karo bibiyon haye alamdar e shah

zer e alam ye kaho haye alamdar e shah

bhai ke labon pe ye buka hai

zindagi ab hogayi dushwaar

Qatl alamdar…

kehti thi ye zainab…

hathon may kooze liye baithi hai ek ghamzada

koi batade usay kaisa sitam ho gaya

ab tu yehi bain kar haye alamdar e shah

aaya nahi laut kar haye alamdar e shah

mashq pe qurban hogaya hai

bali sakina ho khabar daar

Qatl alamdar…

kehti thi ye zainab…

bolay shahe karbala furqate abbas may

sab karo majlis bapa furqate abbas may

milke padho marsiya haye alamdar e shah

koi nahi aasra haye alamdar e shah

charon taraf fauje ashkiyan hai

koi nahi saaya e dewaar

Qatl alamdar…

kehti thi ye zainab…

aankhon se ye alqama jaari rahegi sada

tujhko mere ba-wafa yaad rakhehgi wafa

lab pe rahenge ye bain haye alamdar e shah

rota rahega hussain haye alamdar e shah

jeene se bezaar ho chuka hai

qatl kare mujhko sitamgaar

Qatl alamdar…

kehti thi ye zainab…

noha hai kulsoom ka haye shahe karbala

hogaya mujhko yakeen jayegi sar se rida

dil se uthi ye sada haye alamdar e shah

chor ke muhko gaya haye alamdar e shah

ab to har ek saans ek saza hai

kaise jiye ab ye dilafgaar

Qatl alamdar…

kehti thi ye zainab…

aur bhi to ab sitam dhayenge ye ashkiyan

dashte bala may mera bhai akela hua

mujhko yaqeen hogaya haye alamdar e shah

jayegi meri rida haye alamdar e shah

ahle haram ki yehi sada hai

hogaye azaad sitamgaar

Qatl alamdar…

kehti thi ye zainab…

Mazhar o farhan ye gham hai qayamat ka gham

zainab o shabbir ka haal karoo kya raqam

lab pe yehi thi sada haye alamdar e shah

daaghe judaai diya haye alamdar e shah

tu nahi to zindagi may kya hai

koi nahi dasht may ghamkhaar

Qatl alamdar…

kehti thi ye zainab…
