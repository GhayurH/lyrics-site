Baba Main Teri Zainab Hun Shahid Baltistani.
Ghar Se Jab Haider-e-Safdar Ka Janaza Utha.

1
Sun Ke Hasnain Bhi Roney Lagay Zainab Ki Sada.
Baba Mere Baba Main Teri Zainab Hoon.
Aakhri Bar Kalejay Se Lagao Baba, Baba Main Teri Zainab Hoon.
Toor Kar Band-e-Kafan Mujh Ko Bulao Baba, Baba Main Teri Zainab Hoon.

2
Kya Gawara Hai Tumhein Sar Se Hataon Chadar Bolo Kyun Chup Ho Padar.
Mujh Se Batein Karo Youn Munh Na Chupao Baba, Baba Main Teri Zainab Hoon.
Main Bhala Kaisay Judai Ka Uthaon Gi Yeh Gham Aap Ko Maa Ki Qasam.
Dosri Bar Yateemi Se Bachao Baba, Baba Main Teri Zainab Hoon.

3
Hukam Tha Aap Ka Zainab Se Chupa Kar Rakhna Kuch Na Iss Se Kehna.
Ab Tu Jate Hoye Woh Zakhm Dikhao Baba, Baba Main Teri Zainab Hoon.
Kaun Janat Ke Manga Kar Mujhe Ab De Ga Libas Eid Bhi Aa Gayi Pas.
Ab Main Zid Kis Se Karon Gi Yeh Batao Baba, Baba Main Teri Zainab Hoon.

4
Tum Ne Amma Ki Shahadat Pe Tasali Di Thi Abhi Zindah Hai Ali.
Ab Mujhe Kaun Sanbhalay Ga Batao Baba, Baba Main Teri Zainab Hoon.
Tanha Milne Ke Liye Tum Se Kabhi Aa Na Sakein Qabar Par Ja Na Sakein.
Iss Qadar Door Tu Basti Na Basao Baba, Baba Main Teri Zainab Hoon.

5
Aap Ke Baad Bhi Iss Tarha Wafadar Rahein Sang Bari Na Karein.
Kofiyon Se Zara Kehte Hoye Jao Baba, Baba Main Teri Zainab Hoon.
Baap Ko Iss Ne Takallum Na Di Aawaz Kabhi Sirf Ashor Ko Di.
Main Hoon Mushkil Mein Najaf Chor Ke Aao Baba, Baba Main Teri Zainab Hoon.
