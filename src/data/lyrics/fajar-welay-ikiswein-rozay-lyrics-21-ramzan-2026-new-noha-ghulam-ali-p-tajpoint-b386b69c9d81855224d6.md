1
Fajar Welay Ikiswein Rozay Aal Nabi Di Rul Gayi Aye.
Ainj Da Zalim Waar Chalaya Dastar Nabi Di Khul Gayi Aye.
Chuk Ke Piyo Nu Jis Welay Ghazi Apne Ghar Aaya.
Rondiyaan Bhaina Nu Gal La Ke Zainab Si Farmaya.

2
Rab Jane Kiwein Ummat Nanay Di Qatal Saday Te Tul Gayi Aye.
Hasan Di Amari Baqiya Chad Ke Rondi Kofay Aayi.
Ujar Gaya Mera Wasda Wehra Aakhay Dard Satai.
Wekh Ke Zakhmi Aj Haider Nu Zakhm Pasay Da Bhul Gayi Aye.

3
Ik Roza Daar Namazi Da Aj Surkh Musalla Hoya.
Wekh Ke Ghurbat Haider Di Aj Arsh Khuda Da Roya.
Jehra Wich Kabay De Aaya Si Ohdi Rat Masjid Wich Dhul Gayi Aye.
Dhi Nu Wekh Ke Aakhay Haider Chadar Da Rab Zamin.

4
Jag Taun Le Ke Tur Chaliya Aj Khon Che Tar Main Daman.
Tere Kitay Aj Taun Zainab Zulm Haneri Jhul Gayi Aye.
Zehar Hasan Nu Milna Aye Fair Kafan Che Lagne Teer Aye.
Karbal De Wich Qatal Hai Hona Pyasay Putar Shabbir Aye.

5
Takna Waan Piya Lash Teri Haye Wich Raitaan De Rul Gayi Aye.
:CHAN MIRAAN SARKAR: De Wehray Taboot Ali Da Aaya.
:MIRAAN BAQAR: De Putraan Ne Ro Ke Seene Laya.
Tasveer Oh Kofay Wali Azhar Ais Wehray Wich Khul Gayi Aye.
