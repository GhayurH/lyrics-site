Mola Mola Bol Musalaman Mola Mola Bol Mir Hasan Mir
Nam-e-Muhammad Jis Tarha Hai Rab Ko Nabi Ke Pyara

1
Nam-e-Ali Bhi Oss Tarha Hai Rab Ke Nabi Ko Pyara
Nuqta Nahi Dono Par Koi Dono Hain Anmool
Khaibar-o-Khandaq Badar-o-Ohad Yeh Sab Hain Ali Ke Tohfay
Kis Ne Sar-e-Maidan Diye Hain Bhag Bhag Kar Dhokay

2
Parh Tu Zara Tareekh Khool Ke kHul Jaye Gi Pool
Hum Hain Dar-e-Haider Ke Gadagar Jo Bhi Chaha Paya Hai
Khuddari Hai Apna Wateera Hath Nahi Phailaya Hai
Hum Nahi Jantay Kya Hota Hai Kasa Our Kashkool

3
Bugz Ki Raah Pe Chalnay Walay Qadam Qadam Pe Girtay Hain
Ilm Ke Darwazay Se Hat Kar Maray Maray Phirtay Hain
Aqal Ke Andhay Choor Yeh Dhanday Man Ki Aankhein Khool
Kufar Ki Iss Aandhi Se Musalmaan Kyun Hai Tu Dilgeer

4
Tere Sar Pe Saya Figan Hain Haider Our Shabir
Tere Muhafiz Qambar Salmaan Mesum Our Behlol
Mere Mula Ka Ik Aashiq Tha Behlol-e-Dana
Becha Zubaida Ko Jab Oss Ne Janat Ka Parwana

5
Bola Oss Ka Shohar Apna Ho Gaya Patiya Gool
Khuwab Mein Yeh Manzar Dekha Tu Our Bhi Kuch Ghabraya
Janat Mein Sone Ke Mehal Par Heeron Se Likha Tha
Qasar-e-Zubaida Hai Yeh Denay Walay Hain Behlol
