Alwida Maalike mashraqain alwida
Roke zainab yeh karti thi bain Alvida

1
1) Bhai kya kehke bewon ko samjhaaungi
Sar khule kaise darbaar mein jaaungi
Kaise bali sakina ko beh laaungi
Mere maajaaye tumko kahan paaungi

2
2) Bhai tum thokarein rann me khate rahe
Laashe maidan se khaime me laate rahe
Har tarha deene haq ko bachate rahe
Apni manzil ke nazdeeq aate rahe

3
3) Ab woh besheer ki bezubaani kahaan
Akbar-e-naujawaan ki jawaani kahaan
Ab woh abbas ki paasbaani kahan
Al atash ki sadaayein hain paani kahaan

4
4) Aao tumko gale se lagaye behan
Aao bhaiyya ke qurbaan jaaye behen.
Kaun baaki hai kisko bulaaye behen
Aao ghode pe khud hi bithaye behen

5
5) Aaj akbar ki mayyat pe main ro chuki
Haat afsos Abbas se dho chuki
Kisko kisko kahu sabko main kho chuki
Bhai ab zulm ki intehaa ho chuki

6
6) Rann se halmin ki ye naagahani sada
Choor zakhmo se hai ye badan shaah ka
Haye Aazam huwi zulm ki inteha
Kat gaya shahe bekus ka sukha gala
