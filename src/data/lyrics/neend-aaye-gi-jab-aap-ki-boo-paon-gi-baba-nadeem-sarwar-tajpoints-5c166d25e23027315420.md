Neend Aaye Gi Jab Aap Ki Boo Paon Gi Baba Nadeem Sarwar.
Jab Khaimay Mein Rukhsat Ko Sheh-e-Behar-o-Bar Aaye.

1
Chalai Sakina Mere Bekas Padar Aaye.
Sheh Kehte They Bibi Humein Ro Kar Na Rulao.
Phir Pyar Karein Hum Tumhein Munh Aagay Ko Lao.
Woh Kehti Thi Humrah Mujhe Le Ke Tu Jao.

2
Main Kiya Karon Maidan Mein Gar Ja Ke Na Aao.
Neend Aaye Gi Jab Aap Ki Boo Paon Gi Baba.
Main Raat Ko Maqtal Mein Chali Aaon Gi Baba.
Farmaya Nikalti Nahi Syedaniyaan Bahir.

3
Seene Pe Sulaein Gi Tumhein Raat Mein Madar.
Kyun Kehti Ho Maqtal Mein Chali Aaon Gi Baba.
Nanhay Se Kalejay Pe Mere Chalti Hai Talwar.
Khulta Nahi Kyun Aap Ne Bandhay Hain Yeh Hathyar.

4
Main Aap Ko Kho Kar Na Kabhi Paon Gi Baba.
Aati Hai Bala Sar Pe Tu Pehle Mujhe Waro.
Mar Jaon Gi Baba Na Sidharo Na Sidharo.
Jao Gay Jahan Main Bhi Wahin Jaon Gi Baba.

5
Shab Ho Gi Tu Phir Khak Pe Soein Gay Kahan Aap.
Seene Pe Tu Asghar Ko Sulaein Gay Wahan Aap.
Acha Main Wahin Khak Pe So Jaon Gi Baba.
Aane Do Agar Pyas Se Gash Aaye Ga Baba.

6
Tum Jeetay Raho Pani Bhi Mil Jaye Ga Baba.
Main Aap Ko Dekhon Gi Tu Jee Jaon Gi Baba.
Kuch Door Na They Hum Par Tumhein Neend Na Aayi.
Kya Howay Ga Jab Hogi Maheeno Ki Judai.

7
Bibi Na Kaho Main Nahi So Paon Gi Baba.
Woh Haal Anees Ab Nahi Ho Sakta Hai Marqoom.
Jis Tarha Chuti Baap Se Woh Dukhtar-e-Mazloom.
Noha Tha Yehi Jis Ka Na Jee Paon Gi Baba.
