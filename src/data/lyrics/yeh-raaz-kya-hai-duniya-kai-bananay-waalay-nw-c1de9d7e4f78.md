Yeh raaz kya hai duniya kai bananay waalay.

Bai sahara hai tera  dheen bachanay waalay.

Tah voh darbaar magar logh wohi thay nana

Tuj pai hiziyaan ka jo  ilzaam lagaanay waalay.

Kufa aur Shaam ka dastoor nirala dekha

Qatal kartay hain woh  mehmaan bulaanay waalay.

Kyun larazti hai zameen aur udaasi kyun hai

Koun hay Shaam kay  bazaar main aanay waalay.

Darkay zalim kay tamachon say Sakina nay kaha

Abh to aajao humay  paani pilaanay waalay.

pochi darbar mein to ro pari bibi zainab

yaad kya agai  amma ko sataney waaly.

shane haider bhi wohi lehja e haider bhi wohi

kaisey zainab ke ye  khutbey hilaney waaly.

kasthi rahil ki bhi par lagadey mola

kashi e ummate  islam bachaney waaly.
