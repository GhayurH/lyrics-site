1
Main Deen-e-Muhammad Ko Baqa Dene Chali Hoon.
Beemar Hai Woh Oss Ko Shifa Deney Chali Hoon.
Islam Tera Dhop Mein Batil Ki Hai Ya Rab.
Islam Pe Main Sar Ki Rida Deney Chali Hoon.

2
ALLAH Mera Hosla Tum Dekhna Ban Mein.
Abbas Sa Main Shah-e-Wafa Deney Chali Hoon.
Humshakal-e-Payamber Hai Mujhe Jan Se Pyara.
Main Tere Liye Oss Ka Gala Deney Chali Hoon.

3
Ab Rang Shahadat Ka Tere Dein Pe Charhey Ga.
Main Qasim-o-Kubra Ki Hina Deney Chali Hoon.
Tauheen Imamat Ki Hoi Dasht-e-Bala Mein.
Main Sham Mein Zalim Ko Saza Deney Chali Hoon.

4
Hum Aal-e-Muhammad Hain Nahi Zulm Se Dartay.
Sufiyan Ke Potay Ko Bata Deney Chali Hoon.
 Tauqeer Kaha Bibi Ne Matam Ki Sada Se.
Main Qasar-e-Yazeedi Ko Mita Deney Chali Hoon.
