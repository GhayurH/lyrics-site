Main Wekhi Aye Ruldi Mehndi Qasim Di Wich Sehrawaa
Banri De Ganay Kholaan Yaan Hathaan Diyaan Rasiyaan Lawaan
Ghazi Akbar Tu Pehlaan Ja
Taweez Hasan Da Chachay Nu Hath Peri La Ke Aap Wakha
Tainu Toraan Main Wich Maqtal De Na Wekhaan Teriyaan Rahwaan
Teri Mehndi Rang Badlaya Aye
Mere Lal Diyaan Taliyaan Uttay Jiwein Dubda Soraj Aaya Aye
Nayi Hath Oh Labday Lash Wichon Main Chum Akhiyaan Nal Lawaan
Aynj Moriya Qasim Musalmana
Ik Khak Te Khon Di Gathri Aye Jehri Mori Aye Nal Ahsana
Main Wekh Ke Khak Te Beh Gayi Aan Teri Lash Nu Jholi Pawaan
Ummat Aye Waqt Wikhaya Aye
Qasim Di Maa Ne Kubra Nu Haye Ro Ke Aye Samjhaya Aye
Jadon Putar Di Bewah Wain Kare Mar Jandiyaan Kubra Mawaan
Na Sehriyaan Wala Lal Maray
Jadon Dhal Gayi Sham Ghareebaan Te NAJFI Ik Maa Payi Wain Kare
Na Jana Howay Sham Nu Main Tere Naal Hi Main Mar Jawaan
