1
Fatima Zahra Ki Sana Karte Hain Syed Wajhi Hasan Zaidi.
Aayatein Kehti Hain Toseef-e-Khuda Kartay Hain.
Jab Ali Fatima Zahra Ki Sana Kartay Hain.
Misal-e-Zohra Chamak Uthtey Hain Muqadar Unn Ke.
Jo Dar-e-Fatima Zahra Pe Jhuka Kartay Hain.
Istekhara Teri Tasbeeh Se Kar Ke Bibi.
Marzi ALLAH Ki Hum Jan Liya Kartay Hain.
Hain Rasolon Ke Madadgar Magar Mushkil Mein.
Naam Zahra Ka Ali Wird Kiya Kartay Hain.
Martaba Kam Nahi Sidrah Se Dar-e-Zahra Ka.
Iss Jagah Aa Ke Payamber Bhi Ruka Kartay Hain.
Zaib Deta Hai Ossay Umm-e-Abeeha Ka Laqab.
Jis Ki Tazeem Rasool-e-Dosara Kartay Hain.
Lutf Aata Hai Samaat Ko Teri Midhat Ka.
Jab Bhi Hum Aaya-e-Tatheer Parha Kartay Hain.
Dar Haqeeqat Hain Wohi Loag Wafadar-e-Rasool.
Fatima Jo Tere Bachoon Se Wafa Kartay Hain.
Sare Aalam Mein Tera Naqsh-e-Qadam Hai Bibi.
Roshni Shams-o-Qamar Jis Se Liya Kartay Hain.
Woh Koi Bhi Hoon Jahanum Hai Thikana Unn Ka.
Jo Kissi Rukh Se Bhi Zahra Ko Khafa Kartay Hain.
Roshni Wird Kiya Karti Hai Unn Logon Ke Naam.
Dar-e-Zahra Pe Jo Aankhon Ko Mala Kartay Hain.
Khud Hi Aa Jati Hai Be Sakhta Lanat Lab Par.
Naam Jab Qatil-e-Zahra Ka Suna Kartay Hain.
Haq-e-Zahra Ke Liye Hum Tu Zabaan Kholein Gay.
Hum Nahi Unn Mein Jo Dushman Se Dara Kartay Hain.
Hum Tu Tofaan Mein Bhi Rakhtay Hain Tabarey Ka Charag.
Our Hi Hoon Gay Woh Jo Fikar-e-Hawa Kartay Hain.
Hum Tu Bas Kehte Hain Bar Dushman-e-Zahra Lanat.
Jane Kyun Sheikh Jee Ghar Baithay Jala Kartay Hain.
Inn Ki Takheel Hoi:MIR HILAL: Our Wajih.
Madah-e-Zahra Mein Jo Masroof Raha Kartay Hain.
