Umar Jaise Guzar Gayi Yakdam.

1
Jab Moafi Hayat Ke Samjha.
Howas-e-Maal-o-Zaar Gayi Yakdam.
Raat Darya Ka Band Tootne Se.
Kitni Makhlooq Mar Gayi Yakdam.
Gardish-e-Waqt Mere Daman Mein.
Ranj-o-Aalaam Dhar Gayi Yakdam.

2
Aas Deedar Ki Jo Toot Gayi.
Phir Hamari Nazar Gayi Yakdam.
Zindagi Ne Hazaar Zakhm Diye.
Moat Har Zakhm Bhar Gayi Yakdam.
Baat Pori Hi Karne Wala Tha.
Rooh Parwaaz Kar Gayi Yakdam.
