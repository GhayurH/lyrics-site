Mehboob Ki Mehfil Shahid Baltistani
Mehboob Ki Mehfil Ko Mehboob Sajatay Hain.

1
Aate Hain Wohi Jin Ko Sarkar Bulatay Hain.
Woh Qalb-e-Muhammad Par Utrein Thin Bohat Pehle.
Jibreel Jo La La Kar Aayat Sunatay Hain.
Jo Loag Haqeeqat Mein Ashiq Hain Muhammad Ke.

2
Woh Loag Yateemo Ko Seene Se Lagatay Hain.
Kar Dein Jo Karam Aaqa Insan Tu Insaan Hain.
Kankar Bhi Hatheli Par Quran Sunatay Hain.
So Kar Bhi Jagaye Ga Islam Ki Qismat Ko.

3
Sarkar Youn Haider Ko Bistar Pe Sulatay Hain.
Dafnatay They Zindah Hi Jis Door Mein Beti Ko.
Zahra Ko Mere Aaqa Maa Keh Ke Bulatay Hain.
Sardar-e-Jawanan-e-Jannat Hain Wohi Bachay.

4
Sarkar Mere Jin Ka Khud Jhola Jholatay Hain.
Ab Fikar Nahi Mujh Ko Darwaish Qayamat Ki.
Sarkar-e-Abu Talib Jab Naat Likhatay Hain.
