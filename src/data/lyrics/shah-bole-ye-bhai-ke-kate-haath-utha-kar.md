Sheh bolay yeh bhai keh katay haath utha kar
Ab yeh to kaho laash ko dhoondu main kahan paar

1
Abbas kahan ho meray Abbas kahan ho

2
1) In ghaam keh andhairo mein nazar kuch nahin aata
Chalta hu to payroo say chala bhi nahin jaata
Ab koun sambhalay mujhe Qasim hai na Akbar.

3
2) Is alaamay ghurbat mein hamay choor na jaana
Pardes mein tum bhai say muu mour na jaana
Hum tum say juda hokay na jee paeyngay pal bhar.

4
3) Paani keh leeay pehjata hum nay tumhay pyaaray
Darya pe yeh lagta hai keh tum bhi gey maaray
Ab door talak hai yahi tanhayi ka manzar.

5
4) Bachoo ka yeh kehna hai keh paani na peeangay
Syedniyan kehtin hain ka ab kaisay jeeangay
Tum jab say gaey ho to pareyshaan hai ghaar bhar.

6
5) Ab koi ghareebon ka nigehbaan kahan hai
Zinda main rahoo iss ka bhi imkaan kaha hai
Chin jaaegi ab Zainab-e-muztar ki bhi chadar.

7
6) Kehta hai nazeer aey meyray Abbas-e-alamdaar
Ho tum to hamaisha say hi bhai keh madadgaar
Avaaz to do dhoondray hain tumhein Sarwar.
