Meda Nuqsan Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics
Dil Nahi Lagta Mera Dil Nahi Lagta.

1
Lut Gaya Saman Mera Sab Howa Qurban Mera.
Ujray Hoye Ghar Mein Mera Dil Nahi Lagta.
Ronda Aye Jinda Zakhmi Jigar Honda Aye Loko.
Barbad Jinda Samne Ghar Honda Aye Loko.

2
Sajjad Aye Kehnda Aye Te Rat Ronda Aye Loko.
Karbal Che Bhara Ghar Mainda Veeran Hoya Aye.
Nuqsan Hoya Aye Mainda Nuqsan Hoya Aye.
Main Rat Kyun Na Rowaan Mera Nuqsan Hoya Aye.

3
Main Apne Musayab Da Kiwein Haal Sunawaan.
Main Zakhmi Jigar Khol Ke Kis Kis Nu Wakhawaan.
Dewaan Ga Sada Ro Ke Main Al Sham Sadawaan.
Har Dard Mainu Wekh Ke Hairan Hoya Aye.

4
Mar Jaway Koi Apna Te Sab Ronday Ne Loko.
Ghurba De Janazay V Dafan Honday Ne Loko.
Be Wali-o-Waris V Kafan Panday Ne Loko.
Be Goor-o-Kafan Sabar Da Sultan Hoya Aye.

5
Chacha Mainda Abbas Wafawaan Da Nagina.
Salar Si Lashkar Da Te Saqa-e-Sakina.
Darya Te Qalam Kitay Jinday Bazu Laeena.
Tar Khoon Che Ridawaan Da Oh Nigran Hoya Aye.

6
Ik Veer Si Akbar Mainda Humshakal-e-Payamber.
Doja Si Bhira Gul Taun V Nazuk Ali Asghar.
Teeja Si Mainday Chachay Da Chan Qasim-e-Muztar.
Wehra Mera Sunja Sar-e-Maidan Hoya Aye.

7
Loko Main Kadi Sham-e-Ghareebaan Na Bhulai.
Dastar-e-Imamat Main Jadon Sir Te Sajai.
Naizay Te Phophi Jan Di Chadar Nazar Aayi.
Oss Din Taun Mainday Ron Da Saman Hoya Aye.

8
Kyun Soag Sakina Da Na Rat Ro Ke Manawaan.
Mainu Kadi Babay Nu Rayi Dendi Sadawaan.
Muk:SARWAR-o-JAWAD: Gayiyaan Odhiyaan Sanwaan.
Masoma Da Matam Sar-e-Zindan Hoya Aye.
