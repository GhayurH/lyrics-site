Haye Akbar

kaise bhoole tujhe bhala umme laila akbar

koi dunya may hai kahan tere jaisa akbar

kaise bhoole tujhe…

mujhko hai ye khabar kya haal hai tera

tu laal hai mera

maa hoo mai mujhse na chupa

zakhm apna akbar

kaise bhoole tujhe…

girte thay baarha uthte thay baarha

dikhta hi kuch na tha

sheh ne haathon se dayr tak

ran may dhoonda akbar

kaise bhoole tujhe…

laila ki maamta zainab ki hasratay

sughra ki mannatay

qatl kitno ko kar gaya

ek naiza akbar

kaise bhoole tujhe…

kya din hai raat kya kya dard kya dawa

kuch yaad kab raha

yaad sughra ko reh gaya

tera chehra akbar

kaise bhoole tujhe…

maa baap ki tarha behno ko bhi teri

armaan reh gaya

tere khoon se hi bangaya

tera sehra akbar

kaise bhoole tujhe…

uth dekh bekasi mushkil hai ye ghadi

jaageer se teri

berida ja rahi hai maa shaam-o-koofa akbar

kaise bhoole tujhe…

kehti rahi ye maa uthkar salaam kar

aayi hai laash par

jisne athara saal hai

tujhko paala akbar

kaise bhoole tujhe…

laasha tadap gaya akbar ye marsiya

maa ke labon pe tha

tere jaane se lutgayi

maa ki dunya akbar

kaise bhoole tujhe…
