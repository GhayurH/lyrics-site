1
Taboot Pa Zahra Ke Mahfooz Sultanpuri.
Taboot Pa Amma Ke Rakhay Hoye Sar Dono.
Zahra Ke Janazay Par Rotay Hain Pisar Dono.
Jis Simt Nazar Dalo Oss Simt Udasi Hai.
Ik Ranj-o-Musayab Ki Dunya Mein Hawa Si Hai.
Hasnain Ke Chehron Par Ronaq Na Zara Si Hai.
Woh Dard-e-Judai Hai Thamay Hain Jigar Dono.
Iss Dard Ki Kashti Ko Tofaan Mein Kinara Do.
Hasnain Ko Behla Le Fizza Ko Ishara Do.
Aye Shair-e-Khuda Uth Kar Bachoon Ko Sahara Do.
Ghabra Ke Na Kar Jaein Dunya Se Safar Dono.
Ghamgeen Zara Dekho Ahwal Hai Dono Ka.
Dil Ranj-o-Musayab Se Pamal Hai Dono Ka.
Amma Ki Judai Mein Woh Haal Hai Dono Ka.
Liptay Hoye Baba Se Hain Noor-e-Nazar Dono.
Hasnain Janazay Par Jab Khak Basar Aaye.
Masoom Duaon Mein Kuch Itnay Asar Aaye.
Woh Bachday Kafan Totay Do Hath Nazar Aaye.
Mas Ho Gaye Amma Se Masoom Nazar Dono.
Taboot Ali Le Kar Jis Waqt Chaley Hoonge.
Hasnain Ajab Gham Ke Sanchay Mein Dhalay Hoonge.
Mamta Ne Bhi Ik Maa Ki Khud Hath Malay Hoonge.
Maa Keh Ke Barhey Hoonge Jab Lakht-e-Jigar Dono.
Dono Ke Liye Akhtar Kam Ranj Yeh Kab Hoga.
Yeh Soch Ke Rotay Hain Mahfooz Ghazab Hoga.
Pochay Gi Behan Maa Ko Our Haal Ajab Hoga.
Dafna Ke Janazay Ko Jab Aaeingay Ghar Dono.
