1
Alajal Ya Imam e Zamana Askari Hasan Zaidi.
Gaib Se Aaye Ga Ik Na Ik Din Yousuf-e-Fatima Dekh Lena.
Adal-o-Insaf Ka Iss Jahan Mein Hoga Roshan Diya Dekh Lena.
Arziyaan Samne Sab Rakhein Gay Robaro Unn Se Ik Din Milein Gay.
Khatam Ho Jaye Ga Doriyoon Ka Aik Din Silsila Dekh Lena.
Marifat Shah-e-Karb-o-Bala Ki Sari Dunya Ko Jis Roz Ho Gi.
Sath Mein Phir Musalay Ke Hoga Aik Farsh-e-Azza Dekh Lena.
Yeh Azadar Kis Ke Liye Hain Khud Jawab Iss Ka Tum Ko Miley Ga.
Yaad-e-Sarwar Mein Ashor Ke Din Khon Kitna Baha Dekh Lena.
Bad-e-Ashor Jo Hai Akela Jis Ko Beemar Samjha Hai Sab Ne.
Sham-o-Kofa Ko Abbas Ban Kar Woh Ulat Aaye Ga Dekh Lena.
Karwatein Le Rahi Hai Musalsal Jis Ke Dil Mein Ziyarat Ki Hasrat.
Hoga Sheh Ka Karam Oss Pe Ik Din Jaye Ga Karbala Dekh Lena.
Aye Wilaye Ali Ke Mukhalif Tere Shajrey Ki Kariyaan Hain Tooti.
Tere Rukh Se Barasti Hai Lanat Ja Ke Ghar Aaina Dekh Lena.
Matami Mit Nahi Saktay Hargiz Qatal Karti Rahe Lakh Dunya.
Hashar Tak Iss Jahan Mein Rahe Gi Fatima Ki Dua Dekh Lena.
Jis Ghari Zindagi Khatam Hogi Aankh Mehram Banay Gi Ali Ki.
Maut Pardah Hataye Gi Barh Kar Tum Bhi Wajh-e-Khuda Dekh Lena.
Aye Hilal Aik Soraj Lehad Mein Hashar Tak Jagmagata Rahe Ga.
Maut Ke Baad Ashk-e-Azza Ke Noor Ka Mojza Dekh Lena.
