Har nafas har ghari mere dil se yehi aa rahi hai sada
Ya Imam-e-Raza
Ya Sari ar-Reza

1
Dil Pe Maula Hai Mere Sirf Hakomat Teri.
Bajti Rehti Hai Mere Seene Mein Nobat Teri.
Baat Sabit Hoi Mere Seene Mein Bhi Ik Haram Hai Tera.
Ab Keh Mashhad Se Jo Palta Hoon Tu Mere Mohsin.
Sans Lena Mujhe Acha Nahi Lagta Lekin.
Phir Bhi Hai Yeh Sakoon Main Kahin Bhi Rahon Dil Wahin Hai Mera.

2
Teri Azmat Pe Bhala Kyun Na Dalalat Banta.
Shair Qaleen Ka Kis Tarha Na Zindah Hota.
Hai Haqeeqat Yehi Teri Marzi Mein Hi Hai Khuda Ki Riza.
Eid-e-Zilqad Ke Din Phir Se Hain Aaye Bibi.
Kijiye Hum Ko Atta Bhai Ke Sadqay Bibi.
Qum Se Mashhad Barhein Hum Yeh Kehte Hoye Aap Ka Shukriya.

3
Meri Iss Waqt Tere Dar Pe Nazar Hai Maula.
Our Tujhey Meri Musibat Ki Khabar Hai Maula.
Door Uftad Kar Meri Imdad Kar Tu Hai Mushkil Kusha.
Har Taraf Jang Hai Mushkil Hai Pareshani Hai.
Khon-e-Mazloom Ki Har Simt Hi Arzani Hai.
Lashkar-e-Zulm Pe Haq Ka Ghalba Rahe Ab Tu Behar-e-Khuda.

4
Manzil-e-Sabar-o-Wafa Se Jo Guzarey Gaye Hain.
Nam-e-Shabbir Pe Woh Loag Jo Marey Gaye Hain.
Arz Meri Suno Unn Shaheedon Ka Ho Our Rutba Siwa.
Dushman-e-Deen Pe Aate Hi Jo Dhaye Ga Ghazab.
Jis Ki Talwar Mein Abbas Ke Arman Hain Sab.
Ossi Dil Band Ko Apne Farzand Ko Izan Kar Dein Atta.

5
Rad Hoi Koi Dua Meri Mujhe Yaad Nahi.
Jis Ki Qismat Mein Aye Maula Koi Aoulad Nahi.
Misal-e-Rahib Ossay Jald Aoulad De Waris-e-Karbala.
Jeetay Jee Jan Se Apni Main Guzar Jaon Ga.
Jald Mashhad Na Bulao Gay Tu Mar Jaon Ga.
Aye Imam-e-Ghareeb Tum Ho Mere Tabeeb Jald Kar Do Dawa.

6
Iltija Aap Se Karta Hoon Imam-e-Hashtum.
Unn Ke Sadqay Mein Jinhein Kehte Hain Masooma-e-Qum.
Jo Pareshan Hai Jald Kar Dijiye Oss Ki Hajat Rawa.
Apne Iss Mir Ko Anmool Bana De Maula.
Mujh Ko Iss Door Ka Behlool Bana De Maula.
Main Azadar Hoon Kashf Bardar Hoon Tere Khudaam Ka.

7
Ghair mumkin ko yu hote hue dekha mumkin
Jaagti aankho se jannat me guzare kai din
Usko kya ho yaqee’n
Jisne dekha nahi
Kabhi rauza tera

8
Waqt kaisa yeh mere zehen me aya hai abhi
Jaise gehware ne fitrus ko shifa baati thi
Waise hi rozo shab
Baat ta hai shifa
Tera sehne shifa

9
Kaise samjhega zamana tere naukar ki hadei’n
Tere maaruf ne baandhi hai samandar ki hadei
Badhte toofaan ka
Rukh badalta gaya
Usne jab di sada

10
Punchne haal nakirain jab aaye mera
Maine dohraya jawaban jo qasida tera
Wujd me aake phir
Wo bhi kehne lage
Mai bhi kehne laga
