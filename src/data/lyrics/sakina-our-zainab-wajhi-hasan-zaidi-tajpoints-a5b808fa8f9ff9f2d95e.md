1
Sakina Our Zainab Wajhi Hasan Zaidi.
Sakina Our Zainab Dono Ka Hi Aik Sadma Hai.
Judai Asghar-o-Shabeer Ki Dono Ka Noha Hai.
Hoye Shabeer-o-Asghar Qatal Jab Tu Mar Gayin Dono.
Youn Ik Sadma Uthaye Sham Ki Janib Chalein Dono.
Utha Ik Sath Jaisay Dono Ka Ghar Se Janaza Hai.
Gallon Se Khon Behta Jab Nazar Bhaiyon Ka Aata Hai.
Jigar Par Hath Rakh Kar Dono Ka Rona Batata Hai.
Jigar Ik Teer Ne Our Ik Jigar Khanjar Ne Kata Hai.
Sakina Ko Tamachay, Taziyane Marey Zainab Ko.
Sar-e-Shabeer-o-Asghar Ne Kaha Aye Zalimo Thehro.
Na Maro Keh Sitam Yeh Dono Par Zahra Ke Jaisa Hai.
Kahin Par Qafila Jo Sham Ki Rahoon Mein Tha Rukta.
Tu Zainab Kehti Thi Abid Mera Shabeer Hai Tanha.
Sakina Kehti Jangal Mein Mera Asghar Akela Hai.
Hai Dekha Dasht Mein Ik Sath Zainab Our Sakina Ne.
Giraya Logon Ne Shabeer Ko Jo Mar Kar Naizay.
Zameen Mein Mar Kar Unn Naizon Se Asghar Ko Dhonda Hai.
Khinchay Rasi Sakina Ki Tu Oss Ka Sans Ruk Jaye.
Yeh Manzar Dekh Kar Zainab Wahin Ro Ro Ke Jhuk Jaye.
Bhateeji Our Phophi Dono Ko Youn Rasi Se Bandha Hai.
Hoye Jo Samne Shabeer-o-Asghar Qatal Behno Ke.
Woh Bhai Naizon Par Hain, Samne Unn Dono Bhaiyon Ke.
Inho Ne Pathron Se Sham Tak Ab Qatal Hona Hai.
Kissi Ne Samne Behno Ke Youn Bhaiyon Ko Na Mara.
Koi Sajjad Se Pochay Asad Behno Ka Yeh Sadma.
Keh Iss Sadmay Pe Ja Kar Qabar Tak Sajjad Roya Hai.
