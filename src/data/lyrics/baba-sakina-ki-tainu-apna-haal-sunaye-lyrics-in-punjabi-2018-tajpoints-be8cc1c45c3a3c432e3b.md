Baba Sakina Ki Tainu Apna Haal Sunaye.
Tere Jan Toan Bad Sakina Ne Haye Baba Har Wailay Tamache Khaye.

1
Aye Na Sochin Keh Kithe Dar Dar Ke Tainu Deni Aan Sadawaan.
Aye Mere Khon De Wich Wasiyaan Maa Zahra Diyaan Sawaan.
Koi Khof Nayi Aye Zalim Da Haye Teri Taang Mainu Tarpaye.
Mere Bakhtaan Che Reh Gaye Baba Qaid Khane De Haneray.

2
Muk Gaye Baba Meri Qismat De Ghazi De Naal Saweray.
Zindan Che Rakhiya Aye Baba Haye Mainu Sab Toan Kar Ke Juda Aye.
Jad V Yaad Aaye Gi Tainu Baba Teri Sakina.
Jawein Na Mainu Milan Layi Baba Nanay De Shehar Madina.

3
Zindan Che Milan Layi Aawein Haye Ayethey Main Baba Rehna Sada Aye.
Haye Zeeshan Sakina Aakhay Aynj Baba Halat Hoi.
Jhuk Meri Kamar Gayi Baba Main Akhiyaan Di Beenai Khoi.
Ik Pal V Soyi Nayi Baba Haye Tere Bajoon Neend Na Aye.
