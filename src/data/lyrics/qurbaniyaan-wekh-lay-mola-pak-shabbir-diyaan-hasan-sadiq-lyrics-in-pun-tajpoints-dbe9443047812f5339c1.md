1
Qurbaniyaan Wekh Lay Mola Pak Shabbir Diyaan Hasan Sadiq.
Pardais Che Rowan Bhaina Be Taqseer Diyaan.
Tati Rait Te Shah Diyaan Jokaan Panri Dakiya Zalim Lokaan.
Be Sheer De Gal Wich Nokaan Khobiyaan Teer Diyaan.

2
Khichay Barchi Imam-e-Zamana Piya Tarpay Roop Sohana.
Hoiyaan Aye Toqiraan Nana Teri Tasveer Diyaan.
Wal Aaway Almaan Wala Saday Parday Da Rakhwala.
Nit Khairaan Howan Shala Ghazi Veer Diyaan.

3
Zahra Diyaan Lutiyaan Chadraan Haye Musalmana Be Qadraan.
Laiyaan Pak Siraan Taun Chadraan Aynj Tatheer Diyaan.
Beemar Te Okhiyaan Ghariyaan Gal Tauq Moharan Phariyaan.
Na Khich Be Darda Kariyaan Aynj Zanjeer Diyaan.

4
Khulay Waal Te Baniyaan Banwaan Kofay Diyaan Rahwaan.
Aa Ghazi Wekh Sazawaan Bhain Aseer Diyaan.
Jidi Doosh Rasool Sawari Jidi Jantaan Wich Sardari.
Onay Zarbaan Sehyaan Bhari Kund Shamsheer Diyaan.
