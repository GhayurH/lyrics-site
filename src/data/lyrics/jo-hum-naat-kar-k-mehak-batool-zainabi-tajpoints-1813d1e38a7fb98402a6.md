1
Jo Hum Naat Kar K Mehak Batool Zainabi.
Jo Hum Naat Kar Ke Raqam Chomtay Hain.
Warq Chomtay Hain Qalam Chomtay Hain.
Yeh Natein Bhi Ilham Hoti Hain Hum Ko.
Samajh Kar Inhein Mohtaram Chomtay Hain.
Labon Se Lagatay Hain Khak-e-Madina.
Keh Jaisay Nabi Ke Qadam Chomtay Hain.
Bhala Kyun Na Chomein Dar-e-Mustafa Ko.
Issay Sakinan-e-Iram Chomtay Hain.
Kahan Hum Kahan Unn Ke Rozay Ki Chokhat.
Yeh Unn Ka Karam Hai Keh Hum Chomtay Hain.
Woh Nalain Jaan Jis Pe Jibreel De De.
Woh Nalain Arab-o-Ajam Chomtay Hain.
Liwaye Muhammad Ke Saye Mein Hoon Gay.
Jo Farsh-e-Azza Par Alam Chomtay Hain.
Jo Nana Ka Roza Nahi Chom Saktay.
Nawasay Ka Ja Kar Haram Chomtay Hain.
Jo Karb-o-Bala Se Madine Ko Jaein.
Aqeedat Se Hum Woh Qadam Chomtay Hain.
Jabeen Hum Jhukatay Hain Khak-e-Shifa Par.
Hai Tazeem Yeh, Kam Se Kam Chomtay Hain.
Bara Naam Mehshar Mein Unn Ka Asad Hai.
Jo Asma-e-Khair-Al-Ummam Chomtay Hain.
