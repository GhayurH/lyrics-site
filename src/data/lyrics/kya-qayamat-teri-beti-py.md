Kya Qayamat Teri Beti Py
Qabar-e-Ahmad Pe Fatima Zahra Ro Ke Baba Se Karti Hain Noha
Kya Qayamat Teri Beti Pe Pari Hai Baba
Mera Pehlu Howa Zakhmi Pasliyaan Tooti Hain Meri
Zindagi Ab Meri Dushwar Hoi Hai Baba
Mujh Pe Ilzam Lagata Tha Madina Sara
Meri Beti Ne Marey Barey Mein Kya Kya Na Suna
Khum Kamar Kyun Hoi Meri Kyun Hai Baloon Mein Safaidi
Mujh Se Zainab Yeh Meri Poch Rahi Hai Baba
Sirf Yeh Hi Nahi Baba Keh Main Darbar Gayi
Teri Beti Bharay Darbar Mein Ruswa Bhi Hoi
Takht Se Koi Na Utha Haye Betha Raha Majma
Magar Afsoos Teri Beti Khari Hai Baba
Tere Baad Aisay Zamane Ne Sataya Mujh Ko
Aik Lamhey Bhi Kahin Chain Na Aaya Mujh Ko
Pursa Youn Mujh Ko Diya Hai Haq Mera Cheen Liya Hai
Ab Samajh Aata Hai Kya Be Padari Hai Baba
Jalta Darwaza Gira Mujh Pe Baha Hai Mera Khoon
Hath Bhi Zakhmi Hoye Kaisay Main Tasbeeh Parhon
Ho Gayi Pehlu Shikasta Mera Mohsin Gaya Mara
Raat Din Ab Tu Faqat Noha Gari Hai Baba
Jis Ki Tazeem Ka Ayelan Kiya Tha Tu Ne
Rasiyaan Bandh Dein Zalim Ne Galay Mein Oss Ke
Loag Kartay Nahi Ahsas Oss Ko Bas Waday Ka Hai Pas
Yeh Samajhtay Hain Keh La Char Ali Hai Baba
Iss Tarha Mujh Pe Lagaya Gaya Pehra Baba
Kar Nahi Sakti Mein Gham Mein Tere Giriya Baba
Door Jake Yahan Ghar Se Roz Jati Hoon Main Roney
Apne Hi Shehar Mein Yeh Darbadari Hai Baba
Aa Gaye Aag Laeen Le Ke Jo Darwazay Par
Dar Jalaya Bhi Giraya Bhi Wohi Dar Mujh Par
Kitne Zalim Woh Laeen They Dar Ke Opar Se Woh Guzrey
Jantay They Keh Teri Beti Giri Hai Baba
