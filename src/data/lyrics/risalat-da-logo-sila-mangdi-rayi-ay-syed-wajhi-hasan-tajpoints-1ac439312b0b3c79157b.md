1
Risalat Da Logo Sila Mangdi Rayi Ay Syed Wajhi Hasan.
Bazaraan Che Zainab Rida Mangdi Rayi Aye.
Mainu Bad-e-Shabbir Maut Aaway Ya Rab.
Maindi Sain Ayho Dua Mangdi Rayi Aye.

2
Lahu Ro Ke Sajjad Kehte They Logo.
Rida Tum Se Yeh Ba Haya Mangti Hai.
Oh Mawaan Te Bhainraan Layi Shamiyaan Taun.
Ridawaan V Nam-e-Khuda Mangdi Rayi Aye.

3
Yeh Majma Hatao Nabi Ki Hai Beti.
Zara Si Guzarne Ki Ja Mangti Hai.
Mainu Zalimo Mera Asghar Milao.
Sakina V Apna Bhira Mangdi Rayi Aye.

4
Sada Qabar-e-Zainab Ki Hai Al-Ajal.
Yehi Bint-e-Zahra Dua Mangti Hai.
 Wajih Te Oh Razi Raway Shala Qambar.
Jehri Sainr Dhi Layi Baqa Mangdi Rayi Aye.
