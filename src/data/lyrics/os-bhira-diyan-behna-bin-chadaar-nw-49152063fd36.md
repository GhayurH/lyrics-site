Os Bhira Diyan Behna Bin Chadaar Haye Vich Shaam Bazaar Day Ayaan

Jis Veer Di Behna Day Pairan Di Uddi Khak Nay Parday Daari Lay Chadaran Payan.

Jeewein Valan Nu Chahray Tay Paya Sirr Tay Laye Ay Rait Sajad
Jiwain Nikliyan Khaimiyan Chon Sariyan Zahra Nay Parday Daariyan Khoob Sikhayan.

Teeran Talwaran Ton Jehray Bal Bach Gaye Ountan Ton Dig Kay Oh Maar Gaye
Lang Lang Kay Balan Ton Ountaan Nay Qaidan Mawan Day Samnay Qabran Banayan.

Moor Pahla Si Ajay Haar Pasay Hogayi Pathran Di Shaam Vich Baarish
Raiyan Samnay Abid Day Hondiyan Wadh Wadh Kay Pathar Kandiyan Zahra Jayian.

Ahkay Darbar Undar Takiya Si Behan Nay Honday Zulam Apnay Veeran Tay
Ohno Apni Museebitan Si Bhul Gayan Jadon Veer Day Lab Tay Zaliman Charahiyan Layan

Veer Masooma Da Kandiyan Tay Paira Nu Rakh Rakh Kay Turda Aaya Si
Kera Kadda Kandiyan Nu Veer Day Keetay Hath Qaidi Humsheer Day Aan Sipahiyan.

Kiwain Saqlain Likhay Manzar Oh Shaam Da Bazaar Vich Sakina Jadon
Vasday Hoay Pathran Nu Vaikh Kay Hasrat Nal Sir Tay Ghazi Day Nazran Jamaiyan.
