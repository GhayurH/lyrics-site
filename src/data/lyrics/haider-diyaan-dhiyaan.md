Haider Diyaan Dhiyaan
Shabeer Sadawan Layi, Sajjad Bachawan Layi Darbaraan Che Gayiyaan.

1
Abid Nu Aye Fikraan San Na Waal Nazar Aawan.
Zainab Nu Aye Sochaan San Aidhi Sanwaan Na Muk Jawan.
Bazaraan De Moraan Te Sajjad Ne Zainab Ne Band Rakhiyaan Ne Akhiyaan.

2
Chayi Raakh Jo Jholay Di Be Sheer Di Amri Ne.
Hathaan Te Laeena Ne Barsaye Barey Koray.
Maa Zakhmi Te Hondi Rayi Asghar Di Nishani Layi Band Rakhiyaan Ne Mothiyaan.

3
Aye Wain Si Laila Da Mehmal Taun Kiwein Utari.
Aye Sochiya Nayi Akbar Galiyaan Choon Kiwein Guzri.
Main Sham De Safraan Wich Naizay Te Tere Sir Nu Bas Wekhdi Rayiyaan.

4
Har Amari Di Hasrat Aye Jadon Lal Jawaan Howay.
Sang Odhiyaan Phophiyaan De Maa Laway Ohno Sehrey.
Par Farwa Ne Na Jane Kinj Gathri Che Baniyaan Ne Sehrey Diyaan Lariyaan.

5
Aakhay Ruqaiya Ro Ke Aoulad Karan Sadqay.
Main Zalimaan Nu Abid Maran Na Dendi Koray.
Umm Ul-Banin Di Dhi Aynj Ho Gayi Aye Qaidi Paiyaan Banwaan Che Rasiyaan.

6
Dardi Si Jadon Bibi Zalim De Tamachay Taun.
Oh Marda Si Ja Ke Masooma Di Phophiyaan Nu.
Phophiyaan Nu Banawan Layi Rukhsaraan Te Rakhiyaan Nayi Masooma Ne Taliyaan.

7
Tatheer De Wehray Taun Kadi Bar Na Jo Gayiyaan.
Oh Sham De Wich Akbar Paiyaan Sundiyaan San Jhirkaan.
Babay Di Wilayat Layi Har Zulm Nu Seh Gayiyaan Nazaan Diyaan Paliyaan.
