Pairan Wich Zakhm Sakina De Azadaran e Imam Hussain Lyrics In Punjabi

1
Pairaan Wich Zakhm Sakina De Pathraan Te Turdi Jandi Aye.
Ik Baap De Sir Nu Wekhan Taun Kayi Waar Tamachay Khandi Aye.
Sir Pathraan Naal Te Zakhmi Aye Rukhsar Te Neel V Aaye Ne.
Har Roz Yateemi Aiday Layi Dukh Wakhrey Naal Liyandi Aye.
Sehra Diyaan Tapdiyaan Raitaan Te Rahwaan De Khar Gawah Rehna.
Aye Choti Fatima Karbal Choon Kiwein Wal Sham De Aandi Aye.

2
Beemar Bhira Di Safraan Wich Rat Akhiyaan Di Wadh Jaway Naa.
Taiyon Aye Soch Keh Abid Taun Gardan De Neel Chupandi Aye.
Jehri Shabeer De Seene Te Sondi Si Aj Zindan Andar.
Band Akhiyaan Kar Ke Mitiyaan Te Bas Rondi Te Kurlandi Aye.
 Sabir Lagda Aye Qaid Andar Bas Aida Waqt Akheri Aye.
Babay De Sir Nu Ro Ro Ke Ghurbat De Wain Sunandi Aye.
