Karbal Ke Muhammed Ko Saja Ke Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.

1
Maula Mere Karbal Ke Muhammad Ko Saja Ke.
Rotay Rahe Phir Samne Akbar Ko Bitha Ke.
Tum Ne Kahin Dekha Hai Mera Chand Sa Beta.
Bhaija Tha Abhi Nana Ki Tasveer Bana Ke.
Le Aaj Tu Maa Ban Ke Issay Gaud Mein Le Le.
Le Aaya Hoon Laila Tere Dilbar Ko Utha Ke.

2
Akbar Ke Garam Seene Mein Barchi Utar Gayi.
Sab Deep Bujh Gaye Hain Sughra Ki Dua Ke.
Aye Ibrahim Dekhiye Sabar-e-Hussain Bhi.
Barchi Nikal Rahi Hai Madar Ko Dikha Ke.
Dekho Tu Naveed Hosla Iss Maa Ka Hai Kehta.
Roti Rahi Shabbir Ko Akbar Ko Bhula Ke.
