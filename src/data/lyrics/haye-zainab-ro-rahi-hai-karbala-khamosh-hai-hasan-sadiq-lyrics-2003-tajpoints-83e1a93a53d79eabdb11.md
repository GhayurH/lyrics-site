Haye Zainab Ro Rahi Hai Karbala Khamosh Hai Hasan Sadiq.
Jal Gaye Hain Sare Khaimay Alqama Khamosh Hai.

1
Chin Gayi Hai Sar Se Chadar Kya Karey Bint-e-Ali.
Oss Taraf Darya Kinaray Bawafa Khamosh Hai.
Aa Gayi Sham-e-Ghareebaan Ho Gaye Qaidi Haram.
Aablay Paon Ke Roye Rasta Khamosh Hai.

2
Ab Kahan Zainab Ko Chain Aaye Ga Akbar Ke Beghair.
Kha Ke Barchi Akbar-e-Gul Boo Ka Wafa Khamosh Hai.
Kaisay Guzrey Gi Rasan Basta Sar-e-Maqtal Behan.
Be Kafan Bhai Hai Ran Mein Be Rida Khamosh Hai.

3
Ro Chuki Zainab Bharay Ghar Ko Sar-e-Karb-o-Bala.
Karbala Se Sham Tak Sari Fiza Khamosh Hai.
Hai Qalam Ki Nook Mein Aanso Gham-e-Shabeer Ke.
Arsh Pe Rehan Shah-e-Anbiya Khamosh Hai.
