1
Sitaron Ki Chamak Mein Hai Zia-e-Jafar Sadiq.
Hoi Hai Khalq Yeh Dunya Bara-e-Jafar Sadiq.
Hain Bani Aap Maktab Ke Issi Ik Baat Ki Khatir.
Nahi Koi Mera Murshad Siwa-e-Jafar Sadiq.

2
Farishton Se Lahad Mein Fakhriya Keh Doon Ga Bismillah.
Main Apne Saath Laya Hoon Wila-e-Jafar Sadiq.
Haneefa, Shafai Ho Hazrat Malik Ya Ho Hanbal.
Ilm Ki Hai Inhi Sab Par Atta-e-Jafar Sadiq.

3
Sakhawat Mein Keh Sorat Mein Taharat Ho Keh Seerat Mein.
Haan Milti Hai Muhammad Se Adda-e-Jafar Sadiq.
Wasiyyat Yousufi Bakhshish Ki Khatri Bas Yeh Karta Hai.
Meri Turbat Pe Likh Dena Gada-e-Jafar Sadiq.
