Mazloom bay watan yeh mera Mola Raza hay

Haey jis ko zehar logo musalman ney diya hay

Sab ankhein karo band dhuhayee hay dhuhayee

Ik bibi azadaron yahan Shaam se aayi

Lipti hay janazay say nahi sar pay rida hay

Aaye hain janazay pay Nabi Shah-e-Umam bhi

Kulsoom Ruqaya bhi hain Masooma-e-Qum bhi

Wo bibi hay Karbal may jali jis ki aba hay

Rotay hain farishtay bhi zameen or zamaan bhi

Haey looh kalam arsh-e-barein kon-o-makan bhi

Khairul nisa ke la,al kay matam ki sada hay

Mamoon Abbassi ki enayat hay ye logo

Haey aalay payamber ki ataat hay ye logo

Sayyedon sey zara dekho ye umaat ki jafa hay
