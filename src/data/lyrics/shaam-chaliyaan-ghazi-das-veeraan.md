Shaam chaliyaan Ghazi das veeraan kinj jaavaan baaj rida de
Ghazi nu ro ro aakhe aj malka sharam haya di, meri chaadar veer dewa de

1
Main Aun o Muhammad Akbar puttar ton waare ne
Ohde jigar ch aakhir zaalimaan neze maare ne
Main reh gayi sadqe laundi, Shabbir di nasal bachaaundi
Kalla bachiya puttar Sajjad ae

2
Kal shaam-e-gharibaan lang gayi, raat guzar gayi ae
Din charh gaya veer Sakina hun vi jagdi ae
Beh khaak te rondi jaave, bas mashk nu takdi jaave
Ehnu aa ke aap sula de

3
Ik masla te hal karde dardaan maari da
Mainu das de veeraan matlab bhir bazaari da
Jivein larna jang Akbar nu Ghazi tu aap sikhaaya
Mainu turna bhir sikha de

4
Jinay lootiyan da jinay maani Bano Kubra da
Jinay saang te haalre Baba bichhri Sughra da
Mainu aa ke Shaam de rokha, bhainaan te gal vich rasiya
Tu Zainab aap pawa de

5
Main chun layi alam guzar Shaam de safraan di
Mainu lagdi ae bhain Ruqaiya veeraan tere jayi
Ohnu aakhiya puttar Sajjad ae aj tu Abbas de baad ae
Kar pure farz bhira de

6
Akbar Zainab di sun ke aah o zaari nu
Sar naize te Shabbir da kahe dukhyaari nu
Chup chaap tu Shaam nu tur ja, Abbas nu hun na tarpa
Na Zainab hun tu sada de
