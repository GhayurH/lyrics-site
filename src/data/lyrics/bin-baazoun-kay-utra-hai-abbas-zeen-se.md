Bin Baazoun Kay Utra Hai Abbas Zeen Se
Haye Kulsoom Ro Ke Kehti Thin Umm Ul-Banin Se
Bin Bazuon Ke Utra Hai Abbas Zeen Se
Kulsoom Ro Ke Kehti Hain Umm Ul-Banin Se
Bin Bazuon Ke Utra Hai Abbas Zeen Se
Umeed Pyasay Bachoon Ki Mujh Se Jori Rahe
Woh Chahta Tha Mashk-e-Sakina Bachi Rahe
Youn Teer Rokta Raha Apni Jaben Se
Baba Ka Zakhm Yaad Hai Gehra Tha Kis Qadar
Aisay Hi Tha Do Neem Mere Ba Wafa Ka Sar
Haider Ki Tarha Khon Tha Jari Jaben Se
Ik Aankh Mein Tha Teer Tu Ik Aankh Mein Lahu
Khaimay Ki Rah Dhond Raha Tha Woh Chaar So
Ik Gurz Ne Gira Diya Bekas Ko Zeen Se
Roh-e-Ali Ke Bain They Haye Mera Safeer
Sar Par Bhi Gurz Bazu Juda Aankh Mein Bhi Teer
Badlah Mera Liya Hai Mere Janeshen Se
Saqqa Haram Ka Dasht Mein Be Jan Ho Gaya
Ab Marna Hussain Ko Aasan Ho Gaya
Yeh Shoor Uth Raha Tha Sipah-e-Laeen Se
Ghazi Ki Maut Haal Yeh Zainab Ka Kar Gayi
Lagta Tha Jaisay Sani-e-Zahra Bhi Mar Gayi
Aisay Giri Woh Jaisay Gira Bhai Zeen Se
Amma Main Apne Betay Ka Matam Na Kar Saki
Had Yeh Hai Qaid Ho Ke Main Jab Sham Ko Chali
Bazu Utha Ke Chom Na Payi Zameen Se
AKBAR Behan Yeh Boli Ghazab Ki Jafa Hoi
Asghar Ke Jaisi Lash Mere Bhai Ki Bachi
Abid Ne Oss Ke Tukray Chunay Hain Zameen Se
