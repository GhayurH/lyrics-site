Mujhse logo Ali ka badla lo, wo hai Zainab Nabi ki beti hai
Kalma parh parh ke sang maaro mujeke ruqayya Ali ki beti hai

1
1.Kholo Zainab ke baazuon se rasan, rassiyo se mera ghala baandho
Kholo Zainab ke baazuon se rasan,
Jisko galiyon me tumne khincha tha, Dekho ye usi ki beti hai

2
2.Ghaazi Abbas ki behen hai ye, Sang barsao ya rida khayncho
Ghaazi Abbas ki behen hai ye,
Usko zinda zami me garna hai, Ke Khuda ke wali ki beti hai

3
3.Jo bhi pathar hain aaj haatho me, uspe barsao ke main haazir hu
Jo bhi pathar hain aaj haatho me,
Jiske pehlu pe dar giraya tha, ye yaqeenan ussi ki beti hai

4
4.Hai Hubul ka jo torney wala, or giraye hain jisne laat o manaat
Hai Hubul ka jo torney wala,
Ussey uzza ka badla lena hai, Ye yatima usi ki beti hai

5
5.Jisne azaad qaidiyon ko kiya, uski beti ko qaid karlo tum
Jisne azaad qaidiyon ko kiya,
Jiska ehsan kaaenaat pe hai, Haan yehi us sakhi ki beti hai

6
6.Taaziyano ki zadd pe jab aayi, phir kaha usne shamiyon se Naved
Taaziyano ki zadd pe jab aayi,
Jispe tumne sitam ki hadd tori, jaan lo ye usi ki beti hai
