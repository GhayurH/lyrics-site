Wafa thi inkay liye aur, ye wafa kay liye
Ali nay manga tha Ghazi ko Karbala kay liye

1
1) Hussain jhula jhulate tay, inko yeh kay kaar
Yeh kaam ayenge Zainab teri rida kay liye

2
2) Na jaane kis liyey Haider nay inkay bazu kay
Hazaar bosay gale say laga laga kay liye

3
3) Jari kay pairo ko chuma hai roz-e-ashura
Yeh khush naseeb rahi mojh-e-al qamaa kay liye

4
4) Yeh khizr-e-raah hai parchum bhi inka raah numaa
Samandaro kay sitaaro kay aur hawaa kay liye

5
5) Alaam kay saaye main aatay hi mill gayi sayhat
Taras rahay tay kahi din say jo shafaa kay liye

6
6) Sakina baba say iznay-e-wigaa dilaho hamay
Zara sa kaam ye kaardo faqat chacha kay liye

7
7) Laab-e-faraat jo baazu kalam howay inkay
Bohot baara tah yeh gham ruh-e-Syeda kay liye

8
8) Unhi kay naam peh Rehan harf miltay hai
Raza yeh naam wasila hay haar dua kay liye
