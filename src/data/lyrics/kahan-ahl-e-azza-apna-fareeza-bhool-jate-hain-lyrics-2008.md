Kahan Ahl-e-Azza Apna Fareeza Bhool Jate Hain
Gham-e-Shabir Mein Hum Dard-e-Dunya Bhool Jate Hain

1
Main Un Ko Hafiz-e-Quran Kaise Man Loon Waiz
Jo Quraan Yaad Kar Ke Ba Ka Nuqta Bhool Jate Hain
Inhein Waise Rasool ALLAH Ki Batein Tu Azbar Hain
Magar Maidan-e-Khum Mein Kya Howa Tha Bhool Jate Hain

2
Shab-e-Mairaj Ka Qisa Sunate Hain Aqeedat Se
Nabi Se Koan Tha Parde Mein Goya Bhool Jate Hain
Shujat Ki Yeh Auroon Ka Qasida Parhte Rehte Hain
Ali Asghar Ke Hansne Ka Saleeqa Bhool Jate Hain

3
Yeh Kaise Karwaan Hain Jo Madine Tak Tu Jate Hain
Magar Karb-o-Bala Jane Ka Rasta Bhool Jate Hain
Biyaan Karte Hain Aksar Deen Ki Tareekh Kalmago
Abu Talib Ka Khoon Maal-e-Khadija Bhool Jate Hain
