Zia-e-Khoon-e-Shaheedan Dilkashi Zainab Mir Hasan Mir.
Hussainiyat Ke Cheragon Ki Roshni Zainab.

1
Kiya Wo Rah Mein Bartao Kalma Goyon Ne.
Keh Jaise Thi Na Nawasi Rasool Ki Zainab.
Azay-e-Shah Mein Hi Zindagi Tamam Hoi.
Na Apne Aoun-o-Muhammad Ko Ro Saki Zainab.

2
Ali Ke Lal Ka Lasha Tarap Utha Oss Dam.
Tumhare Bazo Mein Jis Dam Rasan Bandhi Zainab.
Kissi Ne Mara Jo Pathar Tu Chup Rahi Lekin.
Kaha Jo Bagi Kissi Ne Tu Ro Pari Zainab.

3
Laho Mein Dobi Hoi Bariyaan Muhari Ki.
Safar Mein Tham Ke Dil Dekhti Rahi Zainab.
Hussain Choor The Zakhmo Se Phir Bhi Uth Baithe.
Kaha Jo Shimar Ne Khaime Se Aa Gayi Zainab.

4
Hijab Mein Bhi Jahan Se Guzarna Mushkil Tha.
Wahan Pe Kaise Khule Sar Khari Rahi Zainab.
Hussainiyat Ko Baqa Iss Liye Nisar Mili.
Yazeediyat Ke Moqabil Nahi Jhuki Zainab.
