1
Banta Nahi Wali Koi Aisa Kiye Beghair.
Dafnana Mujh Ko Jald Ali Hoon Gaye Muntazir.
Aye Logo Intezar Kissi Ka Kiye Beghair.
Nu Lakh Sar Jhuke Hain Tabasum Ke Samne.
Asghar Ne Jang Jeet Li Hamla Kiye Beghair.
Abbas Woh Jari Hai Jo Maidan-e-Jang Mein.
Larta Hai Bazoon Pe Bharosa Kiye Beghair.
Ghasib Fidak Ka Naak Ragarta Hi Reh Giya.
Janat Mein Hur Chala Giya Tuba Kiye Beghair.
Aashoor Wale Din Yeh Mujhe Maa Ka Hukam Tha.
Ghar Mein Na Aana Khon Ka Pursa Kiye Beghair.
ALLAH Keh Raha Hai Tawala Nahi Qabool.
Zahra Ke Dushmano Pe Tabara Kiye Beghair.
Qayam Na Zulfiqar Rakhein Gaye Niyam Mein.
Abbas Ke Kaleje Ko Thanda Kiye Beghair.
Taqleed Mein Hoon Main Bhi:ANEES-O-DABEER: Ki.
 Sajjad Shair Likhta Hoon Charba Kiye Beghair.
