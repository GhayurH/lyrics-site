1
Akbar Ka Lahu Mir Hasan Mir & Mir Qasim Mir.
Akbar Ka Lahu Sheh Ki Abba Tak Pohanch Gaya.
Dastar Se Woh Maa Ki Rida Tak Pohanch Gaya.
Aisay Giri Hai Khaimay Mein Akbar Ki Lash Par.
Zainab Ka Khon Khak-e-Shifa Tak Pohanch Gaya.
Laila Yeh Ro Ke Kehtin Thin Akbar Kahan Ho Tum.
Zalim Ka Hath Meri Rida Tak Pohanch Gaya.
Aawaz Ran Se Aayi Keh Baba Main Gir Para.
Girta Howa Hussain Sada Tak Pohanch Gaya.
Seene Mein Naiza Toor Ke Kehta Tha Yeh Laeen.
Dekho Hussain Apni Qaza Tak Pohanch Gaya.
Maula Zaeef Ho Gaye Kuch Dair Mein Magar.
Akbar Ka Lasha Aal-e-Abba Tak Pohanch Gaya.
Jis Hath Ne Giraya Tha Humshakal-e-Mustafa.
Naiza Liye Woh Hath Rida Tak Pohanch Gaya.
Barchi Gari Hoi Thi Tu Qasad Bhi Aa Gaya.
Sughra Ka Intezar Qaza Tak Pohanch Gaya.
