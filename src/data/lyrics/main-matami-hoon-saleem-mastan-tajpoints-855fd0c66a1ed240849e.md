Main Azadar Hoon Mera Khameer Khak-e-Shifa.
Main Azadar Hoon Nisbat Hai Meri Karb-o-Bala.

1
Main Azadar Hoon Main Fatima Zahra Ki Dua.
Main Azadar Hoon Azmat Mera Qad Hai Bara.
Zindagi Fatima Zahra Ne Atta Ki Hai Mujhe.
Sans Bas Matam-e-Sarwar Ke Liye Di Hai Mujhe.

2
Bint-e-Zahra Ka Hai Ahsan Azadar Hoon Main.
Ashk-e-Gham Hain Meri Pehchan Azadar Hoon Main.
Mujh Pe Nazaan Hain Suleman Azadar Hoon Main.
Kar Raha Hoon Main Jo Ayelan Azadar Hoon Main.

3
Sans Chalti Hai Meri Farsh-e-Azza Ki Khatir.
Main Tu Zindah Hoon Faqat Karb-o-Bala Ki Khatir.
Gaud Mein Maa Ne Sunai Hai Azza Ki Lori.
Di Hai Parcham Ke Talay Mujh Ko Wafa Ki Lori.

4
Kabhi Ghazi Kabhi Akbar Ki Sana Ki Lori.
Main Ne Jholay Mein Suni Karb-o-Bala Ki Lori.
Khud Ko Shabeer Ka Dewana Bana Rakha Tha.
Main Ne Jholay Ko Azza Khana Bana Rakha Tha.

5
Main Tu Bachpan Se Hi Janat Ki Fizaon Mein Raha.
Yani Abbas Ke Parcham Ki Hawaon Mein Raha.
Shahiyaan Bantne Walon Ke Gadaon Mein Raha.
Fatima Our Sakina Ki Duaon Mein Raha.

6
Martaba Sare Zamane Se Alag Paya Hai.
Main Ne Shabeer Ki Behno Ka Namak Khaya Hai.
Sar Uthatay Hoye Fatwon Ko Mita Deta Hoon.
Ya Ali Keh Ke Qadam Apna Barha Deta Hoon.

7
Qasar-e-Batil Ki Main Bunyad Hila Deta Hoon.
Hath Matam Ke Liye Main Jo Utha Deta Hoon.
Jo Hain Qatil Ke Tarafdar Woh Dar Jate Hain.
Jitnay Shabeer Ke Dushman Hain Woh Mar Jate Hain.

8
Kaun Samjhay Ga Zamane Mein Ishara Mera.
Ba Khuda Chodah Muhammad Hain Sahara Mera.
Roz Jata Hai Bulandi Pe Sitara Mera.
Hai Sabeelon Pe Tabaruk Pe Guzara Mera.

9
Karbalai Hoon Yeh Aizaz Diya Hai Rab Ne.
Mujh Ko Matam Ke Liye Khalq Kiya Hai Rab Ne.
Noha Zainab Ka Sunaein Gay Jo Mehdi Sarkar.
Matami Halqa Lagaein Gay Sab Hi Matamdar.

10
Hogi Masroof Sabeelon Mein Farishton Ki Qatar.
Har Taraf Hogi Faqat Haye Hussaina Ki Pukar.
Khana-e-Kaba Se Niklay Ga Jaloos-e-Ashor.
Qabar-e-Zahra Pe Bhi Jaye Ga Jaloos-e-Ashor.

11
Ya Ali Keh Ke Sikhaya Gaya Mujh Ko Chalna.
Tarbiyaat Mein Hi Sikhaya Gaya Matam Karna.
Matami Hoon So Mera Kam Hai Aagay Barhna.
Meri Fitrat Mein Nahi Khoof-e-Qaza Se Darna.

12
Bachpaney Se Hi Sada Zair-e-Alam Rehta Hoon.
Munkir-e-Haq Ke Ilaqay Mein Ali Kehta Hoon.
Mujh Se Pocho Main Batata Hoon Keh Hai Kya Matam.
Lashkar-e-Zulm Ke Chehray Pe Tamacha Matam.

13
Hai Tamana-e-Muhammad Ki Tamana Matam.
Jitna Rokay Ga Koi Utna Barhey Ga Matam.
Pardah-e-Gaib Se Mehdi Jo Waseem Aaein Gay.
Jo Bhi Matam Ke Mukhalif Hain Na Bach Paein Gay.
