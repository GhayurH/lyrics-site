Zulm Toon Pehlaan Ummat Nane Di Kujh Nayi Sochiya.
Sham Da Bazar Kuja Te Sani-e-Zahra Kuja.

1
Kar Ke Munh Darya De Wal Ro Ro Ke Zainab Aakhdi.
Ghazi Veran Baad Tere Lut Layi Ummat Rida.
Chachay Da Sar Wekh Ke Qaidi Sakina Aakhdi.
Shimar Ne Marey Tamachay Dur Le Kuna Choon La.

2
Rahwaan Ne Pathreliyaan Te Okhay Painday Sham De.
Jane Rab Aal-e-Nabi Ne Safar Kita Kis Tarha.
Nezay Te Quran Parhda Aye Piya Jis Da Aye Sar.
Dada Aida Abu Talib Te Nana Aida Mustafa.

3
Kaun Aye Zainab Pochiya Jad Darbar Wich Yazid Ne.
Rat Wadhi Akhiyaan Choon Abid Jiondiyaan E Mar Gaya.
Qasim, Akbar, Aoun-o-Muhammad, Fazal, Asghar Da Lahu.
De Ke Shah Zarkhaiz Keeti Ijaz Khoni Karbala.
