1
Kaha Karte Hain Hum Har Lehza Har Dam Ya Ali Mula.
Mita Deta Hai Har Gham Ism-e-Aazam Ya Ali Mula.
Kaha Man Kuntu Mula Jab Nabi Ne Khum Ke Maidaan Mein.
Kaha Ashab Ne Bhi Barh Ke Baham Ya Ali Mula.

2
Qutub Ho Ghus Ho Koi Wali Ho Ya Qalandar Ho.
Yehi Narah Hai Sab Ke Lab Pe Dam Dam Ya Ali Mula.
Koi Bhi Marka Ho Jang Ho Ya Koi Maidaan Ho.
Ruka Kufar Ka Dam Sun Ke Paiham Ya Ali Mula.

3
Alam Abbas Ka Jhukne Nahi Detey Jalosoon Mein.
Uthate Hain Alam Hum Keh Ke Har Dam Ya Ali Mula.
Hamara Deein Aqeedah Our Nazriya Fikar Our Mazhab.
Hamari Taig Naiza Our Parcham Ya Ali Mula.

4
Ali Ko Baab-e-Shehar Alam Keh Kar Mursal-e-Haq Ne.
Banaya Ilm-o-Hikmat Ko Majasam Ya Ali Mula.
Sharafat Ne Sharf-e-Shah-e-Najaf Ke Shehar Se Paya.
Karam Haider Ke Dam Se Hai Mukaram Ya Ali Mula.

5
Hai Looh-o-Kursi-o-Arsh-o-Jina Mein Our Sidrah Mein.
Zameen Mein Utar Dakhan Porab Pacham Ya Ali Mula.
Main Maal-o-Dolat-e-Dunya Ko Le Kar Kya Karon Majid.
Mera Paisa Mera Dinar-o-Dirham Ya Ali Mula.
