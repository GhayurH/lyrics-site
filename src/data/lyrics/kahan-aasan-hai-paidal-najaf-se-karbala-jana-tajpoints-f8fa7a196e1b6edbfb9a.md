Kahan Aasan Hai Paidal Najaf Se Karbala Jana.

1
Salam Aaye Zair-e-Mula Mubarak Josh-e-Zawari.

2
Ali Wala Najaf Ki Sar Zameen Par Jab Utarta Hai.
Wilay-e-Murtaza Se Our Bhi Emaan Nikharta Hai.
Keh Jab Josh-e-Mawaddat Apni Manzil Se Ubharta Hai.
Hussani Matami Yeh Kar-e-Mushkil Kar Guzarta Hai.
Nikal Parta Hai Zawari Ko Phir Molai Mastana.
Aqeeday Ke Bina Par Bhi Yeh Mushkil Kam Hota Hai.
Yeh Kar-e-Khair Hain Be-Mool Our Be-Daam Rota Hai.
Our Iss Iqdam Par ALLAH Ka Inam Hota Hai.
Falak Ke Sakino Mein Oss Bashar Ka Naam Hota Hai.
Isse Buzdil Nahi Kar Dein Amal Yeh Hai Dalairana.
Safar Mein Har Hussaini Zairon Ke Naaz Uthata Hai.
Koi Pani Pilata Hai Koi Khana Khilata Hai.
Bana Kar Apne Mehmaan Our Kuch Jazba Barhata Hai.
Bagarz-e-Khidmat-e-Zair Woh Qadmoon Ko Dabata Hai.
Yeh Izzat Yeh Sharf Pata Hai Bas Mola Ka Dewana.
Muhib-e-Shah-e-Wala Ke Siwa Koi Nahi Aisa.
Mosafat Tay Kare Jo Iss Tarha Se Fee Sabeelillah.
Safar Mein Sakhtiyaan Kar-e-Ibadat Soch Kar Sehna.
Keh Har Uthte Qadam Pe Shukar Khaliq Ka Ada Karna.
Nahi Humsar Koi Molaiyon Ka Sab Ne Yeh Mana.
Keh Jab Pocha Gaya Yeh Rah Mein Zawar Se Aakhir.
Humein Bhi Tu Bata Mehsoos Kya Karta Hai Tu Zair.
Kaha Oss Ne Chala Hoon Marqad-e-Shabir Ki Khatir.
Zameen Pe Paon Hain Lekin Falak Pe Soch Hai Yasir
Keh Milne Ja Raha Hai Dekh Le Shama Se Parwana.
