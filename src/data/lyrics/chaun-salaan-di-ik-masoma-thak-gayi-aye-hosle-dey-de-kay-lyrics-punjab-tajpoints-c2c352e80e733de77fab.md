1
Chaun Salaan Di Ik Masoma Thak Gayi Aye Hosle Dey De Kay.
Rat Akhiyaan Di Toon Rook Bhira Dukh Sare Bhain Nu De De.
Jehra Babay De Gal Choon Wagiya Oho Teri Akhiyaan Wich Aye.
Jehra Baqi Reh Gaya Lash Andar Oho Mere Kuna Wich Aye.
Aj Aakhri Raat Main So Jana Seene Te Jah Bas De De.
Jinnay Sir Babay Da Laya Aye Mainu Jhirkaan Mar Bolanda Aye.
Mere Munh Te Mar Chaperan V Mainu Veran Bohat Daranda Aye.
Na Shimar Aaway Meri Qabar Uttay Toon Wadah Bhain Nu De De.
Haye Wakhri Qaid Masooma Di Sajjad Nu Khon Rawandi Aye.
Kandaan Naal Takraan Mardi Rayi Babay Nu Bhain Bolandi Aye.
Mere Sare Dukh Muk Jawan Gaye Mainu Sir Babay Da De De.
Mere Sare Dard Wandandi Si Kariyaan Te Pani Pandi Si.
Aj Mar Gayi Aye Zindan Andar Maithon Sare Zakhm Chupandi Si.
Nayi Kafan Main Tainu Pa Sakda Toon Moafi Veer Nu De De.
Ki Raat Beemar Te Aayi Aye Zindan Che Qabar Banai Aye.
Babay De Seene Te Sondi Kiwein Mitiyaan Wich Dafnai Aye.
 Rizwan Oh Aj V Rondi Aye Ohno Mehdi Sabar Toon De De.
