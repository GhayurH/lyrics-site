Khat Fatima Sughra Ne Ye Baba Ko Likha Hai

Beemaar Hon Marti Hon Ye Sab Tumko Pata Hai

Jis roz se dekha hai moharram ka naya chand

ye kaise likun baba ke dil toot gaya hai

khat fathima sughra ne….

Wada to kiya baba nibhane nahi aaye

Bemaar se kis baat pe moo phayr liya hai

khat fathima sughra ne….

Baba mujhe dasti hai ye tanhai watan ki

ye ghar ki udaasi hai to phir khabr may kya hai

khat fathima sughra ne….

laut aate hai ghar shaam ke hotay hi parinde

kunba mera kyon ab bhi safar may hi raha hai

khat fathima sughra ne….

Tum jab se gaye ghar se ye ghar khabr hai meri

mai zindan hai aise ke koi laasha pada hai

khat fathima sughra ne….

Baba mere asghar ka to kuch haal sunao

us be-zaban ne mujhko kahin yaad kiya hai

khat fathima sughra ne….

Dekha Hai Hasan Sadiq Rehan Ke Nowho Mai

Ashkon Ka Samandar Hai Jo Palko Pe Ruka Hai

Khat fathima sughra ne….
