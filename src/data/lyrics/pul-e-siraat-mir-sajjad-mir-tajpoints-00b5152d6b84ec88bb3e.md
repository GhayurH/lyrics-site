Pul E Siraat Mir Sajjad Mir Lyrics
Jane Pul-e-Sirat Kab Aaya Kidhar Gaya.
Main Tu Ali Ka Hath Pakar Kar Guzar Gaya.
Main Aa Gaya Bahisht Munafiq Saqar Gaya.
Woh Apne Ghar Chala Gaya Main Apne Ghar Gaya.
Ghar De Ke Apna Bola Yeh Haider Se Kibriya.
Kuch Tu Tumharey Baba Ka Qarza Utar Gaya.
Main Bhi Utha Ke Dekhon Zara Taig-e-Murtaza.
Socha Hi Tha Yeh Sheikh Ne Kandah Utar Gaya.
Romal Fatima Ka Bara Kam Kar Gaya.
Sab Anbiya Kharey Rahe Our Hur Guzar Gaya.
Hamlay Ke Baad Ghaiz Mein Abbas Ne Kaha.
Khali Para Hai Dasht Yeh Kholi Kidhar Gaya.
Marhab Ali Ke Samne Aaya Tu Youn Howa.
Dharr Dhar Se Gir Ke Reh Gaya Our Sarr Se Sar Gaya.
Aaye Ali Tu Uth Gaya Tazeem Ke Liye.
Dunya Samajh Rahi Thi Ke Salman Mar Gaya.
Uth Uth Ke Dekhtey They Namazi Rasool Ko.
Utray Nahi Hussain Tu Chehra Utar Gaya.
Abbas Se Tawaqa Thi Jis Jang Ki Unhein.
 Sajjad Woh Tu Asghar-e-Be Sheer Kar Gaya.
