1
Pukarein Sani e Zahra Hussain Tanha Hai Write Up
Raha Na Bhai Na Beta Hussain Tanha Hai.
Gazab Hai Dekh Rahi Hoon Main Shimar Ka Khanjar.
Hai Janisar Shaheedo Yeh Dasht Ka Manzar.
Bachane Aayi Hain Zahra Hussain Tanha Hai.
Main Apne Bhai Ki Imdad Ke Liye Jaon.
Galay Pe Rakh Ke Gala Main Bhi Khalq Katwaon.
Na Rook Ab Mujhe Fizza Hussain Tanha Hai.
Pilaya Karti Thi Raton Ko Uth Ke Main Pani.
Qaza Ke Waqt Nahi Tishnagi Bujha Payi.
Keh Teen Rooz Ka Pyasa Hussain Tanha Hai.
Najaf Ko Dekh Ke Bharti Rahi Yehi Aahein.
Hazaron Marne Wale Hain Ya Ali Aaein.
Sitam Ki Taigon Mein Baba Hussain Tanha Hai.
Laeen Kehta Jee Bhar Ke Teer Barsao.
Keh Neem Jan Ko Ab Pathron Mein Dafnaon.
Hai Kon Rokne Wala Hussain Tanha Hai.
Ajeeb Alam-e-Ghurbat Hai Bint-e-Zahra Par.
Keh Khul Keh Keh Bhi Nahi Sakti Shah Ki Khuakhir.
Kahin Na Sun Le Sakina Hussain Tanha Hai.
Ali Ki Beti Ke Khutbon Ka Hai Asar Akbar.
Zamana Sar Ko Jhukata Hai Shah Ke Dar Par.
Yeh Koi Keh Nahi Sakta Hussain Tanha Hai.
