1
Waqt e Rukhsat Kuch Wasiyat Kar Rahe Hain Murtaza.
Ghamzada Hasnain Hain Our, Ro Rahi Hain, Syeda.
Kal Sar-e-Bazar Zainab, Jashan Hoga Hobaho.
Jo Manaya Tha Dar-e-Zahra, Ko Jala Kar, Be Haya.

2
Aye Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Tu, Sajda e Mabood Mein.
Zair-e-Khanjar Sar Kata Kar, Hai Bachana, Lailah.
Bawafa Waqt-e-Kathin Jab, Aaye Ga Tatheer Par.
Ghairat-e-Athar Par Jaan, Apni Kar Dena Fida.

3
Baap Ki Shahi Mein Zainab, Seekh Lo Chalna Sehan Mein.
Kal Sitamgaron Ke Humrah, Chalna Hoga, Be Rida.
Band Ho Jaein Gi Aankhein, Meri Jab Ahl-e-Haram.
Baad Mere Hai Tumhara, Hami-o-Nasir Khuda.

4
Khak Par Mimber Saloni, Ka Bana Kar Sham Mein.
Farz Khutbon Ka Tu Zainab, Ja Baja Karna Ada.
Phir Gaye Jo Qaul-e-Nabvi, Se Bharay Darbar Mein.
Kya Adab Kartay Woh Haider, Waris-e-Quran Ka.
