HAYE HASAN ASKARI
KAH RAHE HAIN ALI

1
JO ZAMANE KA HAI AB IMAM AKHIRI
WO PDHAYE NAMAZE JANAZA TERI
HUKME RAB HAI YE HI

2
GHAM SE KUL AMBIYA RO PADE ARSH PAR
ZALIMO’N NE TUJHE JAB DIYA HAI ZAHAR
HAI YE GHAM KI GHADI

3
EK MAHSHAR BAPA KARBALA ME HUA
ZULM JAB TUJH PE YE SAAMRA ME HUA
YE ZAMEE’N HIL GAYI

4
TERA MATAM MANAYEGA HAR HAAL ME
MEHDI-E-DEE’N KO BAS PANCHWE SAAL ME
HAI YATEEMI MILI

5
DEKH KAR LAASH TERI HASAN ASKARI
ROOH TADPI HAI MERI HASAN ASKARI
AAH DIL NE BHARI

6
AAJ RANJOOR HAIN TERE BABA NAQI
DARD SE CHOOR HAIN TERE BABA NAQI
KAHTE HAIN WO YE HI

7
QAIDKHANO ME GUZRI TERI ZINDAGI
UMR THI BAS TAQI SE HI ZYADA TERI
JAB SHAHADAT HUI

8
SABKE DIL KO HAI TERI SHAHADAT KA GHAM
AAJ GYARAH IMAMO KI AANKHE HAIN NAM
MAHWE GHAM HAI SABHI

9
YE DUA HAI SHUJA AUR YAQOOB KI
UMR BHAR HAM KARE YAA HASAN ASKARI
NAUKARI APKI
