Jeetay Raho Aun o Muhammed Mir Hasan Mir.
Aye Mere Pyaro Raaj Dularo.

1
Razi Hoi Maa Khuld Sidharo.
Lashon Pe Yeh Bachoon Ki Sada Deti Thi Madar.
Jeetay Raho Aoun-o-Muhammad.
Kya Jang Lari Pyas Mein Zainab Ke Dularo.

2
Ustad Ko Aadab Karo Aye Mere Pyaro.
Tareef Bohat Kartay Hain Abbas-e-Dilawar.
Maa Qarz Utaray Tu Bhala Kaisay Utaray.
Khul Kar Yeh Batatay Hain Humein Zakhm Tumhare.

3
Tum Ne Mere Bhai Ke Liye Khaye Hain Pathar.
Madar Na Kahey Gi Keh Koi Kam Karo Tum.
Jagay Hoye Raton Ke Ho Aaram Karo Tum.
Ab Tum Ko Jagaye Gi Na Mazloom Ki Khwahar.

4
Aye Dilbaran e Jafar-e-Tayyar.
Alwida, Alwida, Alwida, Alwida.
Shimar-o-Umar Ke Sar Jo Utaro Tu Khush Hoon Main.
Dushman Ko Jhoom Jhoom Ke Maro Tu Khush Hoon Main.

5
Bhai Ki Taraf Dekh Ke Dam Bhai Ne Tora.
Qabza Na Magar Hath Se Shamsheer Ka Chora.
Kya Khoob Hai Andaz Main Qurban Hoon Tum Par.
Ab Dil Mein Yeh Khuwaish Hai Jo Ghar Laut Ke Jaon.

6
Pani Main Sada Naam Pe Dono Ke Pilaon.
Aray Pyasay Jo Palat Aaye Ho Tum Nehar Pe Ja Kar.
Aye Dilbaran e Jafar-e-Tayyar.
Alwida, Alwida, Alwida, Alwida.

7
Bacho Zaeef Mamo Ko Zehmat Na Dijiyo.
Lashay Uthaye Bhai Tu Junbish Na Kijiyo.
Khoon Behta Raha Munh Se Shikayat Nahi Nikli.
Bazu Pe Chali Taig Jigar Par Lagi Barchi.

8
Ro Ro Ke Dua Detey Hain Khud Hamza-o-Jafar.
Mamta Ne Takallum Rakha Jazbat Pe Qabo.
Bhai Ke Liye Rakh Liye Bibi Ne Sab Aanso.
Sar Chom Ke Bachoon Ke Yeh Kehti Rahi Muztar.
