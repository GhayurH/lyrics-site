Yeh kaisa chand Muharram ka, dekha Sughra nay.

Kay jisay dekh-tay hi matam, kiya Sughra nay.

Mera kunba hay safar main, ya khuda khair ho unki

Yeh dua kartay hua rokay, kaha Sughra nay.

Mujh say milnay Ali Akbar, zuroor aawo gay ik din

Bhayi say jaatay howay waada, liya Sughra nay.

Mujhay din raat rulaye, yaad madar ki ay baba

Hitchkiyan lay tay howay khat main, likha Sughra nay.

Ek ajab khaab rulaye, jab say yeh chand hay nikla

Ek seenay main lagi barchi, dekha’ Sughra nay.

Sunkay Akbar ki azaan ko, yeh kahan Sughra nay Johar

Saans kyun baad teray Akbar, liya Sughra nay.
