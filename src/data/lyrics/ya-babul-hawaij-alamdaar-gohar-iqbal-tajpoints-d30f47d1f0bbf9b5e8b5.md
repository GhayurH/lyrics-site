1
Ya Babul Hawaij Alamdaar Gohar Iqbal.
Hijab-e-Sani-e-Zahra Ali Ka Aks-e-Haseen.
Aye Mere Bawafa Maula Abbas-e-Ali.
Ya Baab Ul Hawaij Alamdar Ya Baab Ul Hawaij Alamdar.
Qismat Ke Meri Malik-o-Mukhtar Ya Baab Ul Hawaij Alamdar.
Tera Hai Yeh Dast-e-Atta Darasal Dast-e-Khuda.
Bantta Hai Tu Bhi Nam-e-Khuda Sadqa Ali Ki Pak Aal Ka.
Tujh Se Jo Kabhi Mangay Woh Khali Na Gaya.
Zahra Ki Dua Abbas Zainab Ki Rida Abbas.
Noor-e-Jali Ka Noor-e-Nazar Shair-e-Jali Ka Hai Shair-e-Nar.
Hathoon Mein Iss Ke Bhi Hai Zoor-e-La Fata.
Shabeer Ki Aankhon Ke Chain Mere Tarsein Ziyarat Ko Nain.
Wasta Bhateeji Ka Hai Tujhe Azn Ik Sajday Ka De Mujhe.
Marne Se Pehle Hi Mujhe Karbala Bola.
Taqat Mein Jalal-e-Ali Haibat Mein Hai Ghaiz-e-Jali.
Lashkar-e-Sham Youn Dar Gaya Tu Ne Bas Dekha Our Mar Gaya.
Younhi Tu Nahi Dein Ka Tujh Ko Alam Mila.
Tasbeeh Parh Iss Naam Ki Tal Jaye Gi Bala Sham Ki.
Nam-e-Abbas Hai Aasra Mushkilon Mein Azadar Ka.
Issi Naam Ke Aagay Dushman Bhi Na Ruka.
Dekha Hai Asad Ne Yehi Tere Rozay Pe Khud Har Ghari.
Hotey Hain Yahan Kayi Mojzay Kaun Hai Woh Jo Yeh Na Kahey.
Oqaat Se Barh Kar Kiya Maula Ne Atta.
