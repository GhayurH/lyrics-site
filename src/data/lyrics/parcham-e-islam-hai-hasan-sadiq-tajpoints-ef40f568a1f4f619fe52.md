1
Parcham e Islam Hai Hasan Sadiq Lyrics
Maula Ghazi Ka Alam Hi Parcham-e-Islam Hai.
Apni Jad Amjad Se Paya Wirsa Mein Inam Hai.
Tu Shabeeh-e-Murtaza Our Waris-e-Jah-o-Hasham.
Urr Gaya Haibat Se Teri Kufar Ka Aaram Hai.
Jin Ke Nana Ke Liye Aalam Howa Takhleeq Hai.
Be Rida Woh Bibiyaan Hain Our Balwa Aam Hai.
Kis Qadar Teri Wafa Ke Charchay Hain Aqwam Mein.
Thi Wafa Be Naam Warna Tu Wafa Ka Naam Hai.
Matam-o-Majlis Azadari Shah Mazloom Ki.
Sari Dunya Ke Liye Haq Ka Yehi Paigham Hai.
Jab Lagay Pathar Barasney Tu Sakina Ne Kaha.
Yeh Sitamgaron Ki Nagri Phophi Amma Sham Hai.
Muntazir Apne Chacha Ki Hai Sakina Ladli.
Kab Se Asghar Ke Liye Woh Le Ke Bethi Jaam Hai.
Hai Ameer Apni Ibadat Tazkira Shabeer Ka.
Madh-e-Aal-e-Rasool Karna Yeh Hi Apna Kam Hai.
