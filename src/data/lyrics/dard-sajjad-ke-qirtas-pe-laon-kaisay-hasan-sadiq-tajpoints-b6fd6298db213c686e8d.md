Dard Sajjad Ke Qirtas Pe Laon Kaisay Hasan Sadiq.
Haye Rota Hai Qalam Lafz Banaon Kaisay.

1
Zakhm Jo Jism Pe Aaye Hain Dikha Sakta Hoon.
Dil Ne Jo Sham Mein Khaye Hain Dikhaon Kaisay.
Jin Ki Madar Ka Janaza Tha Utha Raat Ke Waqt.
Sar Barehna Sar-e-Bazar Bhulaon Kaisay.

2
Karbala Kofa Jo Sham Mein Takreem Hoi.
Roh-e-Ajdad Tarapti Hai Sunaon Kaisay.
Rasiyaan Paon Mein Chalay They Tamachon Ke Nishaan.
Hasrat-o-Yaas Ki Maiyyat Ko Uthaon Kaisay.

3
Pabejolaan Hoon Main Hai Tauq-e-Garaan Zaib-e-Galu.
Ontoon Se Girtay Hoye Bachay Uthaon Kaisay.
Abar Tha Pathron Ka Qehqahey Bhi Tanay They.
Tang Bazaron Se Bazari Hataon Kaisay.

4
Nu So Darbari They Namoos-e-Nabi Paish Hoi.
Hoon Ghayoor Khoon Na Aankhon Se Bahaon Kaisay.
Aye Zamane Ke Yazido Na Kaho Najfi Se.
Khak Ki Noor Si Oqaat Bataon Kaisay.
