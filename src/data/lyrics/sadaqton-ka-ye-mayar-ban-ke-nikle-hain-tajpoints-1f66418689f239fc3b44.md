1
Sadaqton Ka Ye Mayar Ban Ke Nikle Hain.
Haqiqaton Ke Alamdar Ban Ke Nikle Hain.
Nabhi Ke Sath Hain Hasnain Aur Ali-o-Batol.
Khuda Ke Noor Ka Shahkar Ban Ke Nikle Hain.

2
Jo Aik Roz Akhate Howay The Zair-e-Kisa.
Woh Aaj Haq Ke Madadgar Ban Ke Nikle Hain.
Anokhi Shan Se Pancho Mubahilah Ke Liye.
Khuda Ke Dein Ke Parastar Ban Ke Nikle Hain.

3
Koi Bhi Cheez Muqabil Nahi Thehar Sakti.
Kuch Aise Dhang Se Karar Ban Ke Nikle Hain.
Jo La Shareek Nahi Mante Hain Khaliq Ko.
Yeh Unn Ke Waste Talwar Ban Ke Nikle Hain.

4
Yeh Kainat Banai Gayi Hai Jin Ke Liye.
Aqeedaton Ke Woh Maimar Ban Ke Nikle Hain.
Peein Gay Jaam-e-Wila Mil Ke Aaj Hum Sahil.
Mawali Iss Liye Maikhowar Ban Ke Nikle Hain.
