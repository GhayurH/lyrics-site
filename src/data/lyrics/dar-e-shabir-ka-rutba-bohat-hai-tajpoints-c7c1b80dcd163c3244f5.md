1
Yeh Kis Ne Keh Diya Sajda Bohat Hai?
Tu Phir Kalma Bhi Aadah Hi Parhein Woh.
Jo Kehte Hain Humein ALLAH Bohat Hai.
Bhala Hote Na Kyun Be Hosh Mosa.
Ali Ka Noor Hi Chamka Bohat Hai.
Ali Ke Bugz Ki Hidat Hai Aisi.
Munafiq Ho Jahan Jalta Bohat Hai.
Nahi Doon Ga Main Zehmat Malikon Ko.
Meri Bakshish Ko Tu Fiza Bohat Hai.
Jonhi Behlool Ko Dekha Tu Socha.
Ilaqa Khuld Ka Sasta Bohat Hai.
Osse Jhuk Jhuk Ke Milti Hai Yeh Dunya.
Dar-e-Haider Pe Jo Jhukta Bohat Hai.
Ali Ke Dar Ka Main Nokar Hoon Raqib.
Meri Izat Ko Yeh Jumla Bohat Hai.
