Takni Aan Veer Rawaan Main Teriyaan Sham Saweray
Hun Khali Gharaan Che Deviyan Nal Nayi Mukday Veer Haneray
Khab Wich Khilray Hoye Lashay Wekhay Veraan De
Yadaan De Janazay Khilray Ne Jinj Mere Chaar Choferay
Veer Jageer Teri Karbala Di Khushiyaan Wich
Jay Honda Fidak Te Hissa V Kar Dendi Naam Main Tere
Wekh Taqseem Keeti Kaisi Veer Tangaan Ne
Aye Wehray Dewaraan Teriyaan Te Mantaan De Deway Mere
Hun Aaway Ga Veran Hoan Aaway Ga Veran
Bas Ayho Duawaan Mangdi Rayi Rahwaan Taun Nazraan Na Phairay
Wain AKBAR Aye Kita Babay Wekh Qasad Nu
Iss Welay Sajawaan Kinj Sughra Tere Veer De Sir Te Sehrey
