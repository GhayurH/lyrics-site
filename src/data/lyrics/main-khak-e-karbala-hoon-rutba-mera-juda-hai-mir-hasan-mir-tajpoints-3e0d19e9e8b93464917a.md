1
Main Khak-e-Karbala Hoon Rutba Mera Juda Hai Mir Hasan Mir.
Main Khak-e-Karbala Hoon Rutba Mera Juda Hai.
Bint-e-Ali Ne Mujh Ko Chadar Bana Liya Hai.
Karte Hain Rashk Mujh Par Kauser Ke Bhi Kinare.
Zainab Ke Sar Mein Main Ne Do Saal Hain Guzare.
Mera Har Aik Zarah Har Zulm Ka Gawah Hai.
Akbar Jawan Qasim Ghazi Se Chand Tare.
Zain-ul-Iba Ne Meri Aagosh Mein Utare.
Yani Laho Nabhi Ka Mujh Mein Mila Howa Hai.
Roti Hoon Yaad Kar Ke Wo Be Kasi Ka Manzar.
Maula Chupa Rahe The Mujhe Mein Jo Lash-e-Asghar.
Haan Oss Din se Aaj Tak Na Mujh Ko Sakoon Mila Hai.
Shuhda-e-Karbala Ke Zakhmo Ko Main Ne Choma.
Zahara Ki Betiyon Ke Qadmo Ko Main Ne Choma.
Iss Waste Hi Meri Taseer Mein Shifa Hai.
Akbar Ki Lash Par Jab Shabir Ja Rahe The.
Uthte The Baithte The Our Larkhara Rahe The.
Manzar Woh Aab Bhi Meri Aankhon Mein Phir Raha Hai.
Aye Zaereen Maula Chalna Yahan Sanbhal Kar.
Quran Fatima Ka Bikhara Howa Hai Mujh Par.
Na Jane Kis Jagah Par Iss Ka Laho Gira Hai.
Mera Naseeb Hai Aise Qudrat Ne Jagaya.
Khatoon Ke Pisar Ki Hai Maizbaan Banaya.
Mera Moalla Hona Shabir Ki Atta Hai.
Jis Ko Janab-e-Ahmad The Dosh Par Bithate.
Our Jibrail Jis Ko Rahe Loriyaan Sunate.
Jaye Namaz Oss Ka Seena Mera Bana Hai.
Akbar Ki Jis Ghar Se Jageer Ban Gayi Hoon.
Oss Waqt Se Harm Ki Tasveer Ban Gayi Hoon.
Sad Shukar Rab Ne Mujh Ko Aizaz Yeh Diya Hai.
 Tauqeer Tu Bhi Kar Le Aal-e-Abba Ka Matam.
Jo Sham Mein Gayi Thi Oss Be Rida Ka Matam.
Mamnoon Matmi Ki Makhdoma Fatima Hai.
