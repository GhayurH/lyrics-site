Woh Matmi Hai Syed Mudassir Mehdi

1
Sakoon-e-Qalb-e-Rasool Ka Jo Sakoon Bana Hai Woh Matami Hai
Qasam Hai Dil Ki Woh Jis Ki Dharkan Mein Karbala Hai Woh Matami Hai
Namazi Sare Qatar Mein Hain Abhi Hai Jari Hisab Unn Ka
Bina Hisab-o-Kitab Jannat Mein Jo Gaya Hai Woh Matami Hai
Khuda Hai Bani Tu Noha Khuwaan Nooh Our Dawood Soz Khuwaan Hai
Tu Mera Dil Yeh Pukara Yaqoob Our Kya Hai Woh Matami Hai

2
Rida-e-Bint-e-Nabi Ke Saye Mein Jo Pala Hai, Tu Woh Hai Abbas
Dua-e-Bint-e-Nabi Ke Saye Mein Jo Pala Hai Woh Matami Hai
Taqabul Iss Ka Nahi Karo Tum Jahan Ke Mashoor Muttaqi Se
Qasam Ibadat Ki Jo Ibadat Mein Be Riya Hai Woh Matami Hai
Woh Gaib Wala Bhi Pursa Dar-e-Rida-e-Zainab Hai Tu Yeh Keh Doon?
Koi Tu Hai Jo Nizam-e-Hasti Chala Raha Hai Woh Matami Hai

3
Issay Na Janat Ki Kuch Talab Hai Na Dar Hai Dozakh Ka Iss Ke Dil Mein
Hussain Ko Woh Beghair Lalach Ke Chahta Hai Woh Matami Hai
Yeh Rooz-e-Mehshar Ka Aik Manzar Hai Dil Ki Aankhon Se Main Ne Dekha
Jo Sab Se Pehle Janab-e-Hur Ke Galay Laga Hai Woh Matami Hai
Qasam Khuda Ki Azza Hussain Ibn-e-Fatima Ki Hai Kam Rab Ka
Zameen Par Jo Khuda Ka Yeh Kam Kar Raha Hai Woh Matami Hai
