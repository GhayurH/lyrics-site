1
Haal Kya Ho Gaya Sakina Ka Joan Rizvi.
Main Sakina Hoon Dekhiye Baba Haal Kya Ho Gaya Sakina Ka.
Jab Se Utha Hai Aap Ka Saya Haal Kya Ho Gaya Sakina Ka.
Mere Rukhsaron Ki Tarha Itnay Zakhmi Rukhsar Jo Tumhare Hain.
Meri Tarha Tumhein Bhi Zalimo Ne Haye Kitne Tamachay Marey Hain.
Haal Kya Ho Gaya Tera Baba Haal Kya Ho Gaya Sakina Ka.
Jab Hoye Zibha Aap Maqtal Mein Mar Gayi Thi Main Jeetay Jee Oss Din.
Aap Ke Baad Sham Tak Chal Kar Zindah Aa Jana Kab Yeh Tha Mumkin.
Chal Ke Aaya Hai Yeh Mera Lasha Haal Kya Ho Gaya Sakina Ka.
Kya Bataon Tumhari Beti Ko Sham Mein Shimar Kaisay Laya Hai.
Kabhi Ghoron Ke Sath Doraya Our Kabhi Naqay Se Giraya Hai.
Choor Zakhmo Se Hai Badan Sara Haal Kya Ho Gaya Sakina Ka.
Jaisay Baloon Se Sar Tera Pakra Waisay Baloon Se Mujh Ko Pakra Gaya.
Hath Rangeen Jo Tere Khon Se They Unhi Hathoon Se Mujh Ko Jakra Gaya.
Shimar Aisay Mujhe Satata Raha Haal Kya Ho Gaya Sakina Ka.
Tang Rasi Mein Tha Gala Mera Bolti Hoon Tu Bola Jata Nahi.
Pyas Se Khushk Thin Ragein Pehle Ab Ghuttan Hai Keh Sans Aata Nahi.
Hai Abhi Tak Mera Gala Dukhta Haal Kya Ho Gaya Sakina Ka.
Pusht Par Neel Taziyano Ke Kaisay Baba Tujhe Dikhaon Main.
Our Shano Pe Zakhm Naizon Ke Kitne Hain Baba Kya Bataon Main.
Zalimo Ne Mujhe Bohat Mara Haal Kya Ho Gaya Sakina Ka.
Zakhmi Rukhsaron Par Mere Jis Dam Phophi Amaan Ke Ashk Girtay Hain.
Gaud Se Unn Ki Uth Ke Dekhti Hoon Ro Ke Sajjad Sar Ko Peettay Hain.
Kehti Hain Jab Yeh Zainab-e-Kubra Haal Kya Ho Gaya Sakina Ka.
Chup Hoi Beti Haye Joun-o- Asad Baap Ke Sar Ko Dukh Sunatay Hoye.
Uth Ke Bolo Yeh Ro Ke Kehti Thi Zakhmi Beti Ko Maa Hilatay Hoye.
Bhai Lasha Utha Ke Kehta Tha Haal Kya Ho Gaya Sakina Ka.
