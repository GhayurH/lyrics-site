Aap Par Salam Zainab Mesum Abbas.
Bad-e-Sheh-e-Karbala Aap Ne Pora Kiya Kitna Bara Kam Zainab.

1
Maa Se Jo Kiya Tha Wadah Khoob Woh Nibhaya.
Sath Sath Bhai Ke Rahi Hain Ban Ke Saya.
Kam Jo Koi Na Kar Saka Woh Kar Dikhaya.
Karb-o-Bala Bhai Ne Fatha Ki Our Aap Ne.

2
Fatha Kiya Sham Zainab.
Qatal Ho Chuka Ali Ka Lal Jab Furat Par.
Bibiyon Ke Bain Cha Rahe They Kainat Par.
Sari Zimma Dariyaan Uthaein Apni Zaat Par.

3
Bibiyaan Ghabra Gayin Pehrey Pe Aap Aa Gayin.
Jab Jalay Khiyam Zainab.
Misal-e-Shah-e-Dein Nigahein Zulm Se Milaein Thin.
Fojein Jis Ghari Khiyam Lootney Ko Aaein Thin.

4
Abid-e-Hazeen Ko Aap Hi Bach Ke Laein Thin.
Dekh Ke Yeh Hosla Keh Rahe They Marhaba.
Khud Sheh-e-Anam Zainab.
Kaisay Kaisay Imtehan Bibi Aap Ne Diye.

5
Karbala Ke Dasht Se Aseer Ho Ke Jab Chaley.
Aap Hi Tu Aasra Thin Ahl-e-Bait Ke Liye.
Gham Mein Giraftar Thin Haye Phir Bhi Alamdar Thin.
Karbala Se Sham Zainab.

6
Kyun Na Ayhteram Aap Ka Ho Khas-o-Aam Mein.
Aap Hain Shareek-e-Kar Maqsad-e-Imam Mein.
Pehli Majlis-e-Hussain Woh Bhi Qaid-e-Sham Mein.
Koi Nahi Ho Saka Bani-e-Farsh-e-Azza.

7
Sirf Hai Aik Naam Zainab.
Zindagi Ko Waqf Kar Diya Khuda Ke Naam Par.
Dono Ladlay Fida Kiye Wafa Ke Naam Par.
Aik Aik Ashk Shah-e-Karbala Ke Naam Par.

8
Khutba Kuch Aisay Diya Haan Zulm Ko Ruswa Kiya.
Hai Yeh Sada Aam Zainab.
Bhaiyon Ko Har Ghari Rakha Dua Ke Saye Mein.
Aap Ka Yeh Sabar-o-Hosla Jafa Ke Saye Mein.

9
Hai Khuda Ka Deen Aap Ki Rida Ke Saye Mein.
Shukriya Kehte Rahein Aap Ko Sajdah Karein.
Aap Ke Ghulam Zainab.
Aap Ki Atta Jo Ho Tu Cho Lein Aasman Sab.

10
Sare Zakireen Noha Khuwan-o-Soz Khuwan Sab.
 Meesum Our Mazhar Abidi Karein Biyan Sab.
Kar Numayaan Sabhi Aap Ki Lachargi.
Aap Ka Maqam Zainab.
