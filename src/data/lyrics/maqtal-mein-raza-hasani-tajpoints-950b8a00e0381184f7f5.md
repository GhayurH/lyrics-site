1
Bahan Jo Karti Hoi Aayi Bain Maqtal Mein.
Tarap Gaye Tah-e-Khanjar Hussain Maqtal Mein.
Batool Dekh Rahi Hain Palat Palat Ke Magar.
Khamosh Hain Sheh-e-Badar-o-Hunain Maqtal Mein.

2
Juda Jo Iss Tarha Qatil Karey Ga Beti Ko.
Tu Baap Paye Ga Kis Tarha Chain Maqtal Mein.
Hussain Dekh Rahe Hain Khuda Ki Marzi Ko.
Agarchey Jata Raha Noor-e-Ain Maqtal Mein.

3
Zameen Ko Dekhtey They Our Aasmaan Ko Kabhi.
Utha Ke Lasha-e-Asghar Hussain Maqtal Mein.
Aray Furat Pe Aye Soney Walay Jaag Zarra.
Bola Rahe Hain Sheh-e-Mashreqain Maqtal Mein.

4
Woh Sajdah Ga Banaye Gi Bosa Gah-e-Rasool.
Bahan Parhey Gi Jonhi Maghribain Maqtal Mein.
 Raza Likhe Ga:HASAN KAZMI: Wohi Manzar.
Jo Dekhtey They Sheh-e-Kazmain Maqtal Mein.
