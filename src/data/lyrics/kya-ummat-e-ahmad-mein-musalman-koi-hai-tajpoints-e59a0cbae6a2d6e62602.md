Kya Ummat-e-Ahmad Mein Musalman Koi Hai.
Kyun Fatima Haq Apna Yeh Phir Mang Rahi Hai.

1
Kuch Pas Na Rakha Jo Sand Layi Thi Zahra.
Woh Dast-e-Nabi Ki Hi Bhala Likhi Hoi Hai.
Aanso Tere Rukhsar Pe Kiya Dekh Ke Bolay.
Zahra Tere Pichey Khara Majboor Ali Hai.

2
Detey Hoye Youn Ghusal Ali Cheekh Ke Roye.
Zakhmo Ke Nishaan Mujh Se Chupati Kyun Rahi Hai.
Gham Itnay Uthaye Hain Keh Baloon Mein Safaidi.
Atharah Baras Mein Hi Nazar Aane Lagi Hai.

3
Hasnain Ne Khushbo Jonhi Madar Ki Na Payi.
Rotay Howay Kehtey Hain Keh Amma Ki Kami Hai.
Tanha Nabi Zadi Pe Sitam Karb-o-Bala Sa.
Asghar Ki Shahadat Bhi Tu Mohsin Se Juri Hai

4
Jaisay Hi Ghaseeta Gaya Rasi Se Ali Ko.
Zahra Ossi Toti Hoi Pasli Mein Khari Hai.
Jis Hath Ko Choma Hai Hasan Khud Se Nabi Ne.
Ummat Ossi Bibi Pe Sitam Dhaney Lagi Hai.
