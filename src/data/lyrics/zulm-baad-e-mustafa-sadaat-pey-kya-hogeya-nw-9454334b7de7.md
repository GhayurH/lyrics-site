Zulm Baad e Mustafa Sadaat Pey Kya Hogeya

Zakhmi Masjid Mey Dukhi  Zainab Ka Baba Hogeya

Dhaurti Aati Thi Zainab Phir Rahi Thi Jaa Baja

Sani e Zahra Ke Dil Par  Hashr Barpa Hogeya

Momino Ansu Bahao Pursa Do Hasnain Ko

Aj Kufa Mey Qatal  Kabay Ka Kaba Hogeya

Ey Khuda Tujhko Mubarak Tera Ye Mah e Sayyam

Jismey Zainab Lutgayi  Aur Deen Zinda Hogeya

Chotey Bachay Kya Karey Maula Tere Janay Ke Baad

Fatima Zahra Ka Ghar  Duniya Mey Tanha Hogeya

Zindagi Akthar Guzarey Bankey Tera Nohakhawn

Pak Bibi Ye Mera  Armaan Pura Hogeya
