1
Laot Aao Darya Say Abbas Haider Rizvi.
Laut Aao Darya Se Abbas Aye Mere Abbas.
Bibiyon Ke Lab Pe Hai Yeh Har Ghari Noha Aye Mere Abbas.
Kehte They Shabeer Jab Se Tum Gaye Ho Chor Kar.

2
Aa Gayi Mujh Par Zaeefi Jhuk Gayi Meri Kamar.
Bhai Ka Dushwar Hai Ab Do Qadam Chalna Aye Mere Abbas.
Tere Jane Se Aye Bhaiyya Khush Hoye Hain Ahl-e-Shar.
Our Kamar Ko Tham Kar Rota Hai Zahra Ka Pisar.

3
Kar Gaye Ho Jab Se Ghazi Tum Humein Tanha Aye Mere Abbas.
Bas Zarra Si Dair Mein Woh Ba Khuda Lutney Ko Hai.
Tum Mohafiz Jis Ke They Ab Woh Rida Lutney Ko Hai.
Kaisay Chadar Ko Bachaye Zainab-e-Kubra Aye Mere Abbas.

4
Pani Laney Ke Liye Bhaija Tha Tum Ko Nehar Par.
Tum Na Aao Gay Palat Kar Kab Ossay Yeh Thi Khabar.
Roti Hai Bali Sakina Phaink Kar Koza Aye Mere Abbas.
Lashkar-e-Aada Ke Andar Hai Khushi Ka Ik Samaa.

5
Gir Nahi Jata Zameen Par Haye Kyunkar Aasmaan.
Mil Ke Sari Bibiyaan Jab Karti Hain Giriya Aye Mere Abbas.
Iss Qadar Tukray They Abbas-e-Jarri Ki Lash Ke.
Be Basi Pochay Zarra Koi Dil-e-Shabeer Se.

6
Chor Kar Aaye Hain Kaisay Bhai Ka Lasha Aye Mere Abbas.
Aye Hasan Lasha Na Laye Bhai Ka Shah-e-Ummam.
Rakh Diya Khaimon Mein La Kar Khon Mein Doba Alam.
Hai Yehi Lasha Jarri Ka Kehte They Maula Aye Mere Abbas.
