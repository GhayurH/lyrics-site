1
Ishq-e-Mula Ke Iwaz Ashiq Ko Janat Mil Gayi.
Sirf Janat Kya Hai Mehshar Ki Hakomat Mil Gayi.
Ghair Tha Sab Ke Liye Be-Naam Tha Guman Tha.
Zikar-e-Haider Ke Sabab Dunya Mein Shohrat Mil Gayi.

2
Rab Ki Nazron Mein Meri Bibi Ka Ho Ga Kya Muqam.
Jab Kaneezon Ko Bhi Fiza Jaisi Azmat Mil Gayi.
Aasmaan Walon Se Milne Ka Sharaf Mil Jaye Ga.
Gar Mujhe Bhi Karbala Aane Ki Dawat Mil Gayi.

3
Kon Hai Tukra Jigar Ka Fatima Ya Koi Our.
Ik Hadees-e-Mustafa Se Sab Haqeeqat Mil Gayi.
Manqabat,Nohay,Salam,Marsiye Par Bat Ki.
Mujh Ko Jab Bhi Aye Hasan Momin Ki Sohbat Mil Gayi.
