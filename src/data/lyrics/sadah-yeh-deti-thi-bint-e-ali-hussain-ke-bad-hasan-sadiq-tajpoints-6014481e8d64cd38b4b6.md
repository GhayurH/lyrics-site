Sadah Yeh Deti Thi Bint e Ali Hussain Ke Bad Hasan Sadiq.
Nahi Hai Maut Se Kam Zindagi Hussain Ke Baad.

1
Jehad Karti Gayi Karbala Se Sham Talak.
Ali Ki Ban Gayi Beti Ali Hussain Ke Baad.
Ali Ki Beti Ne Shamsheer-e-Istaqamat Se.
Badal Di Jang Ki Tareekh Bhi Hussain Ke Baad.

2
Galay Mein Tauq Rasan Basta Paon Mein Beri.
Yehi Hai Waris-e-Aal-e-Nabi Hussain Ke Baad.
Kaha Yeh Zoja-e-Hur Se Tarap Ke Zainab Ne.
Mitanay Aayi Hoon Tashna Labi Hussain Ke Baad.

3
Woh Jis Mein Jang Karein Sheer Khawar Bachay Bhi.
Phir Aisi Foaj Na Dekhi Gayi Hussain Ke Baad.
:REHAN AZMI: Jaisi Namaz Sheh Ne Parhi.
Ada Na Ho Saki Ab Tak Kabhi Hussain Ke Baad.
