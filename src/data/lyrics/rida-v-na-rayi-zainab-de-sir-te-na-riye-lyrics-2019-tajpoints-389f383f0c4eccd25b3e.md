1
Rida V Na Rayi Zainab De Sir Te Na Riye Sir Te Bhira.
Hath Rasiyaan Pa Ke Tur Payi Haye Loko Sham De Rah.
Sir Ne Ghazi De Leh Ke Naizay Toon Aye Dasaya Nayin Chadraan Rayiyaan.
Naizay Toon Leh Ke Dasiya Zainab Di Pak Rida Na Riye Sir Te Bhira.

2
Veer Naizay Toon Aayataan Parh Ke Jiwein Zainab Nu Aakhda Howay.
Tableeg Di Agli Manzil Hun Zainab Aap Nibah Na Riye Sir Te Bhira.
Choti Qaidan Koi Kahway Mainu Koi Mainu Kaneez Aakhda Aye.
Mainu Mar Ke Shimar Tamache Kehnda Ghazi Nu Bula Na Riye Sir Te Bhira.

3
Apne Hi Ghar De Samne Zainab Jadoon Kofay Che Aayi Peshi Layi.
Bhaina Nu Aakhay Ewein Rul Jandi Bhain Juda Na Riye Sir Te Bhira.
Maro Pathar Aye Baghi Aaye Ne Dhiyaan Haider Diyaan Ne Lo Badlay.
Dhiyaan Toon Lokaan Piyon Da Har Badla Aan Liya Na Riye Sir Te Bhira.

4
Khon Akhiyaan Chon Ronda Janda Aye Aye Nabi Zadiyaan Ne Kehnda Aye.
Aynj Lokaan Maar Ke Pathar Dita Ajar Risalat Da Na Riye Sir Te Bhira.
Sain Wapas Madine Aa Ke V Rooz Akbar Aye Wain Kardi Si.
Qaidaan Toon Wadh Ke Mainu Iss Gal Da Soag Riya Na Riye Sir Te Bhira.
