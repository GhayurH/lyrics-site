Na Pooch Mera Hussain Kya Hai?

1
Jahan-e-Azme Wafa Ka Paikar
Khrad ka Markaz, Junoon Ka Mahwar
Jamal-e-Zehra, Jamal-e-Haider
Zameer-e-Inssan, Naseer-e-Daawar
Zameen ka Dil, Asmaan Ka Yaavar
Dayar-e-Sabro Reza ka Dilbar
Kamal-e-Eessar ka Payambar

Arab Ka Sehra, Ajam Ka Zewar
HUSSAIN TASWEER-E-AMBIYA HAI

2

Hussain Maqsoom-e-Deen–o-Imaan
