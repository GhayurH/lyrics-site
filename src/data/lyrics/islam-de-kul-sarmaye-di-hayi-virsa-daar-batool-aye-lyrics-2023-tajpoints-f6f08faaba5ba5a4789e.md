1
Islam De Kul Sarmaye Di Hayi Virsa Daar Batool Aye.
Jenda Shehar Tu Bahir Rowanr Da Har Raat Rehya Mamool Aye.
Hasnain Gawah Ghin Ke Bibi Khari Kayi Ghantay Darbar De Wich.
Beh Mimbaraan Te Oh Aahday Ray Tera Kiran Sawal Fazool Aye.

2
Haq Mil Wainda Makhdoma Nu Kujh Sadmay Sainr De Ghat Wainday.
Afsoos Gawahi Putraan Di Na Keeti Ummat Qabool Aye.
 Kazim Tehreer Taun Bahir Hin Sadmay Kainat Di Malka De.
Jayin Sand De Purzay Chunr Chunr Ke Keeti Jayedad Wasool Aye.
