Ali Ke Naam Se Tan Man Mein Ik Surror Rahe.
Jissay Ali Se Adawat Hai Mujh Se Door Rahe.

1
Teri Nijat Na Ho Gi Agar Hai Bugz-e-Ali.
Ibadaton Ka Bhalay Tujh Ko Phir Gharor Rahe.
Woh Kamyab Hoye Pa Gaye Ali Ko Jo Loag.
Tabahi Unn Ka Muqaddar Jo Lashaoor Rahe.

2
Ali Ke Chahney Walon Ka Haq Hai Jannat Par.
Adoye Haider-e-Karrar Iss Se Door Rahe.
Baroz-e-Hashar Kahey Ga Khuda Yeh Munkir Se.
Hisab De Teri Niyyat Mein Jo Fatoor Rahe.

3
Adoye Haider-e-Safdar Hai Badboon Ka Shehar.
Phir Oss Ke Ghar Mein Solagta Bhalay Bakhoor Rahe.
Ali Se Bugz Na Rakhna Jo Chahiye Jannat.
Agar Hai Jana Jahanam Tu Phir Zaror Rahe.

4
Kahon Ga Qabar Mein Mujh Ko Bachaiye Lillah.
Jahan Mein Aap Ki Midhat Mein Hum Hazoor Rahe.
Jo Sochtay Nahi Haider Ko Maan Kar Hasnain.
Meri Dua Hai Keh Chehron Pe Unn Ke Noor Rahe.
