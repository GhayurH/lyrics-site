Utha Koi Janaza, Phir Sayeda Kay Ghar Say

Hasrath Baras Rahee Hai Haey, Aik Aik Baam-o-Dar Say

Yeh Kis Ko Ro Rahi Hai, Zainab Tharap Tharap Ker

Janath Mei Sayyada Nay Haey, Chader Giraadi Sar Say

Jis Nay Khuda Kay Ghar Ko, Bakshi Thi Aakay Zeenath

Wo Aaj Ho Raha Hai Haey, Rukhsath Khuda Kay Ghar Say

Hai Bund Aankh Shayad, Araam Aagaya Hay

Moula Tharap Rahay Thay Haey, Unnis Ki Seher Say

Zainab Kay Bazoo Un Ko, Shabeer Choomthay Hain

Kya Janeeyai Ali Nay Haey, Kya Kehdiya Pisar Say

Qathil Ko Apnay Thanda, Sharbath Pila Nay Wala

Bay Chayn Ho Rahay Hai Haey, Khud Zehr Kay Asar Say

Kufay Ka Zarra Zarra, Rotha Hai Khoon Kay Aansoo

Mehroom Ho Rahay Hain Haey, Hasnain Ub Pidar Say
