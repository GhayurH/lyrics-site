1
Aj Mola Ali Ki Aamad Hai Kashan Zaidi.
Jab Kabhi Mah-e-Rajab Sehan-e-Haram Se Guzra.
Muskuratay Hoye Kabay Ko Ali Yaad Aaya.
Mere Murshid Mere Peer Ali Sarkar Ki Aamad Hai.
Aaj Maula Ali Ki Aamad Hai.
Terah Rajab Ki Aisi Khushi Hai Hum Tu Manaein Gay.
Dunya Ko Maula Tere Qasiday Hum Tu Sunaein Gay.
Aye Mere Maula Haider Hum Loag Hain Tere Nokar.
Maa Baap Se Zyada Jis Se Kiya Oss Pyar Ki Aamad Hai.
Khana e Kaba Fakhar Karey Ab Apne Muqadar Par.
Tu Hi Hai Jaye Wiladat Oss Ki Jo Rab Ka Paikar.
Tu ALLAH Ka Ghar Hai Our Tera Makeen Haider Hai.
Haq Hai Keh Kahey Tu Bhi Tere Haqdar Ki Aamad Hai.
Haider Ki Wilayat Ka Tum Inkar Karo Par.
Kabay Mein Saji Bazm-e-Tarab Yaad Rahe Gi.
Ik Bar Tu Pohancho Kahin Dewar-e-Haram Tak.
Ta Hashar Tumhein Terah Rajab Yaad Rahe Gi.
Haq Hai Yehi Keh Aaj Sabhi Se Eid Milo Ja Kar.
Hain Roney Walay Jitnay Yahan Par Aaj Hansay Khul Kar.
Hum Maula Se Kehte Hain Hum Tere Liye Jeetay Hain.
Jo Basta Hai Har Dil Mein Oss Shehwar Ki Aamad Hai.
Hain Mad-e-Muqabil Haris-o-Marhab Kaun Barhey Aagay.
Maidan-e-Khaybar Soch Mein Hai Our Nazar Muhammad Pe.
Woh Nad-e-Ali Parhtay Hain Sab Se Salmaan Kehte Hain.
Woh Dekho Falak Se Maula Ke Jarrar Ki Aamad Hai.
Qabal Ali Our Bad-e-Ali Na Dekha Yeh Manzar.
Masjid Mein Sab Ashab Ke Aagay Jis Ne Kaha Aa Kar.
Jo Dil Mein Hai Woh Keh Do Jo Pochna Hai Woh Pocho.
Do Aalam Ke Sab Se Aala Maiyar Ki Aamad Hai.
Ishq-e-Ali Ko Dil Mein Basaya Kashan-o- Baqar.
Maula Se Tohfa Aisa Paya Rashk Karon Khud Par.
Yeh Likhna Our Yeh Parhna Kartay Hain Karam Woh Apna.
Youn Zehan Mein Shan-e-Maula Mein Ashar Ki Aamad Hai.
