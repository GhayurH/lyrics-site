1
Zinda Rahey Hussain Nadeem Sarwar.
Zindah Rahe Hussain Tera Gham Manaein Gay.
Aye Fatima Ke Lal Yeh Wadah Nibhaein Gay.
Ghar Mein Hamarey Aaein Gi Majlis Mein Fatima.
Farsh-e-Azza Ke Sath Yeh Dil Bhi Bichaein Gay.
Jholay Amariyaan Yeh Shabihein Yeh Taziye.
Tehzeeb-e-Gham Se Dard Ke Manzar Sajaein Gay.
Yeh Unn Ka Hai Jaloos Jo Pyasay Hoye Shaheed.
Hum Iss Ke Rastay Mein Sabeelein Lagaein Gay.
Kehte Hain Chotay Chotay Se Bachay Yeh Baap Se.
Ik Roz Baba Jaan Yeh Alam Hum Uthaein Gay.
Kuch Dair Zuljinah Mere Ghar Pe Rook Do.
Pyasay Ki Iss Sawari Ko Pani Pilaein Gay.
Bachpan Se Hai Dua Ke Banaein Gay Ghar Jahan.
Ashor Khana Ghar Mein Wahan Hum Sajaein Gay.
Ghar Mein Agar Ho Nazar-e-Sakina Tu Hai Yaqeen.
Oss Nazar Mein Sakina Ke Ammo Bhi Aaein Gay.
Kis Shan Ke Miley Hain Namazi Hussain Ko.
Teeron Ke Samne Yeh Musallay Bichaein Gay.
Bachoon Ko Apne Sajda-e-Shabeer Ki Qasam.
Matam Ke Sath Sath Namazein Sikhaein Gay.
Rah-e-Hussainiyat Ke Shaheedo Salam Lo.
Tum Ko Na Apne Dil Se Kabhi Hum Bholaein Gay.
Bad-e-Zahoor Hoga Muharram Ka Yeh Hasham.
Hum Karbala Imam Ke Humrah Jaein Gay.
Noha Bhi Hai Safeer-e-Azza Sarwar-o- Hilal.
Jis Dais Mein Bhi Jaein Gay Noha Sunaein Gay.
