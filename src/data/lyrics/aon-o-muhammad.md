Aun-o-muhammad, aun-o-muhammad mara jigar jaan mara awn-o-muhammad

1
1) Behnain hu sadke maa tere kurban
Mara jigar jaan mara aun-o-muhammad

2
2) Nehar kanare jaakar bhi, pani na piya pyaase hi rahe
Jaan fida ki mamu par, wada jo kiya madar se
Maa ka rakha maan ho gaye qurban
Mara jigar jaan mara aun-o-muhammad

3
3) Neem ye chote chote lekar izne vida mamu se liya
Kitne bare lashkar par jakar, jad ki tarha hamla jo kiya
Jang ka maidan kar diya sunsaan
Mara jigar jaan mara aun-o-muhammad

4
4) Shoq-e-shahadat dil me basaa ke jine ka armaan mita ke
Maa ki dua hatyaar banaa ke, aise gaye hatyaar sajaa ke
Saya-e-quran sar pe tha har aan
Mara jigar jaan mara aun-o-muhammad

5
5. Khun me nahaye dono bhaiyi shart-e-wafaa dono ne nibhaayi
Fakhr se zainab kehti thi pyaaro tum ne meri shaan barhayi
Rab ke ehsaan meri bari shaan
Mara jigar jaan mara aun-o-muhammad

6
6. Pani agar tum pikar aate dood ka hak mere na nibhaate
Meri duaye na pate zehra ko kya shakl dikhaate
Ban gaye pehchaan tum to meri jaan
Mara jigar jaan mara aun-o-muhammad

7
7. Lashe ye dono barso ki kehti thi ye madar kok jali
Haye meri aankho ka ujale legaya tum binaayi meri
Gham ka hai tufaan ma hai pareshaan
Mara jigar jaan mara aun-o-muhammad
