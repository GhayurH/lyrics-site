1
Muntazir Hain Aap Ke Ghulam Al Ajal Imam Mir Hasan Mir.
Ho Na Jaye Zindagi Ki Sham Al-Ajal Imam.
Har Taraf Fizaye Zulm Aam Ho Gayi.
Zindagi Bhi Maut Ka Pyam Ho Gayi.
Masjidein Sulag Uthein Tamam Al-Ajal Imam.
Sun Rahe Hain Iss Sadi Mein Aap Aaein Gay.
Taig Our Alam Bhi Sath Sath Laein Gay.
Karbala Ka Lein Gay Inteqam Al-Ajal Imam.
Khana-e-Khuda Bhi Muntazir Hai Aap Ka.
Rah Dekhtey Hain Qudas Our Karbala.
Samarra Se Kijiye Khiram Al-Ajal Imam.
Be Gharon Ko Nemat-e-Makan Ho Atta.
Har Mareez-e-La Dawa Ko Dijiye Shifa.
Hal Hoon Sab Ki Mushkilein Tamam Al-Ajal Imam.
Mimber-e-Hussain Se Khitab Kijiye.
Munkir-e-Azza Ka Ehtesab Kijiye.
Keh Rahe Hain Matami Tamam Al-Ajal Imam.
Woh Bhi Keh Rahe Hain Hum Mohib Hain Aap Ke.
Zikar-e-Shah Jin Ke Karobar Ban Gaye.
Majlison Ke Le Rahein Daam Al-Ajal Imam.
Kar Rahe Hain Mir Our Rehan Yeh Dua.
Khuld Ki Tarha Banay Mazar-e-Syeda.
Aap Hi Karein Yeh Ahtamam Al-Ajal Imam
