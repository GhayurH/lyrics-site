1
Syeda Tahira Tere Putar Hasan Di Maiyyat Te Zalima Teer Chalaye.
Jiwein Aaya Janaza Shabar Da Koi Maiyyat Na Ainj Ghar Aaye.
Bhaina Ne Maiyyat Tori Si Haye Veer Nu Kafan Pawa Ke.
Par Ummat Maiyyat Nu Mor Dita Haye Teraan Naal Saja Ke.
Ummat Ne Aynj Tazeem Kiti Gaya Kafan V Rang Badla Aye.
Quran De Qariyaan Kar Chori Zakhmi Aaya Tatheer Aye.
Nayi Wekhiya Haye Kisay Maiyyat Choon Payi Teer Kaday Humsheer Aye.
Baon Okha Shah Ne Dafan Kita Wich Baqiya Hasa Bhira Aye.
Aj Nanay De Haye Shehar De Wich Ki Zulm Haneri Chul Gayi.
Jadoon Veer Di Maiyyat Wekhi Te Babay Di Zarb Nu Bhul Gayi.
Be Waqt Yateemi Qasim Di Maidi Sain De Jigar Nu Khaye.
Dita Ajar-e-Risalat Syedan Nu Haye Aynj Da Zalim Lokaan.
Chumi Lash Hasan Di Kayi Wari Teraan Diyaan Tikhiyaan Nokaan.
Aj Apne Khon Che Surkh Hoya Wich Yasrab Sabz Qaba Aye.
Sajjad De Piyo Te Watan Uttay Be Waqt Ghareebi Cha Gayi.
Kita Dafan Irada Veer Da Te Teraan Di Barish Aa Gayi.
 Azhar Aynj Badar Te Ohad Wale Haye Zalma Qarz Chukaye.
