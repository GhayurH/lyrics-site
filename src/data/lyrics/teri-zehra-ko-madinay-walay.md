Teri Zahra Ko Teri Ummat Ne Madine Walay Bohat Tarpaya
Hai Zakhmi Zahra Teri Dukhtar Ka Laeen Se Aisa Tamacha Khaya
Sitam Aisa Keh Mera Ghar Jalaya Hai
Hai Totein Pasliyaan Pehlu Shikasta Hai
Raha Na Mohsin Qayamat Tooti Ali Ke Lab Par Hai Yehi Noha
Gayi Darbar Mein Jab Aap Ki Beti
Bataon Kis Tarha Ghurbat Bohat Roi
Tere Mimber Se Miley Hain Tanay Laeen Ne Mujh Ko Kaha Hai Jhota
Sitam Aisa Keh Jo Mujh Ko Rulaye Ga
Lehad Mein Ja Ke Bhi Na Chain Aaye Ga
Kissi Zalim Ne Teri Beti Ke Hai Rukh Pe Aisa Tamacha Mara
Gayi Haq Mangney Darbar Mein Jis Dam
Gawahi Na Meri Mana Koi Zalim
Kiye They Tukray Sand Ke Oss Ne Hasan Ke Aagay Tamacha Mara
Bataon Kya Mere Roney Pe Hai Pehra
Sitam Aisa Nahi Koi Jo Na Dekha
Ali Se Bolay Madine Walay, Madine Mein Ab Na Roye Zahra
Biyaan Kaisay Karey DANISH ALI Yeh Gham
Musayab Sun Ke Rota Hai Har Ik Aalam
Na Jane Kaisa Musalmano Ne Teri Zahra Se Liya Hai Badla
