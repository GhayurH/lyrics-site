Haye Mehrooma Fatima Zahra Haye Mazlooma Fatima Zahra.

1
Aap Ka Pehlu Shikasta Ho Giya Haye Batool.
Aap ka Dar Jal Diya Jis Dar Pe Rukhte The Rasool.
Waqt Hai Kaisa Fatima Zahra.
Keh Ke Man Kunto Jisse Mula Bana Gaye The Rasool.
Rasiyaan OssKe Galay Mein Dekhti Reh Gayin Batool.
Kya Kare Dukhiya Fatima Zahra.

2
Sharm Aati Hai Padar Se Aap Ki Beti Ko Jab.
Aap Na Hoon Gi Tu Zainab Kaise Reh Paye Gi Tab.
Jane Kya Ho Ga Fatima Zahra.
Aap Ka Shabir Sota Hi Nahi Bin Aap Ke.
Aap Ho Maa Oss Ki Batlaein Kahin Bin Aap Ke.
Kaise Soye Ga Fatima Zahra.

3
Piyasa Hoon Ratoon Ko Aksar Kehte Hain Uth Kar Hussain.
Bad Pani Shehzadi Aap Ke Woh Dill Ka Chain.
Kis Se Kangay Ga Fatima Zahra.
Waqt-e-Aakhir Yeh Wasiyat Fatima Zahra Ne Ki.
Rooz Turbat Par Meri Shabir Lana Ya Ali.
Ro Diye Mula Fatima Zahra.

4
Bole Haider Moaf Karna Ho Agar Mera Qasoor.
Rooz Marqad Par Tere Shabir Laon Ga Zaror.
Tujh Se Hai Wadah Fatima Zahra.
Jab Lage Turbat Mein Zahra Ko Sulane Murtaza.
Ro Ke Kehte The Bohat Roi Ho Yaan Aye Fatima.
Ab Nahi Rona Fatima Zahra.

5
Rab Ke Aagay Sajda Karne Beti Jab Woh Aakhri.
Oss Musalay Par Hi Bibi Ki Shahdat Ho Gayi.
Arsh Tharaya Fatima Zahra.
Dekhna Afraz Mehdi Ghaib Se Jab Aaein Gaye.
Sheh Ke Matamdaron Se Tameer Woh Karwaein Gaye.
Aap Ka Roza Fatima Zahra.
