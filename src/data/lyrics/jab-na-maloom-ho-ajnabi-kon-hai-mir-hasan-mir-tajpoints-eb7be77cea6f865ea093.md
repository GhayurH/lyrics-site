1
Jab Na Maloom Ho Ajnabi Kon Hai Mir Hasan Mir.
Makar Our Khud Numai Ke Iss Door Mein Jab Na Maloom Ho Ajnabi Kaun Hai.
Az Hadees-e-Nabi Le Ke Nam-e-Ali Janch Lo Kam Nasab Aadmi Kaun Hai.
Jo Khuda Ka Mohib Our Mehboob Hai Woh Jo Karrar Hai Ghair-e-Farrar Hai.

2
Shan Mein Jis Ke Hai Yeh Hadees-e-Nabi Woh Rajul Juz Ali-e-Wali Kaun Hai.
Shan-e-Haider Nomayan Ahadees Mein Inn Ke Aousaf Quran-o-Tareekh Mein.
Hal Atta Innama Kul Kafa La Fata Our Wali-o-Wasi-e-Nabi Kaun Hai.
Jis Ko Chahein Barhaein Yeh Ahl-e-Jahan Jis Ko Chahein Ghataein Yeh Ahl-e-Jahan.

3
Jitna Chahein Giranay Ki Koshish Karein Khud Bulandi Kahey Gi Ali Kaun Hai.
Ghair Ka Inn Ke Inn Se Taqabul Hi Kya Unn Ke Emaan Pe Shak Inn Pe Rab Ka Yaqeen.
Inn Pe Shak Bhi Howa Tu Khuda Ka Howa Jis Ko Ho Dawaye Humsari Kaun Hai.
Ik Nuseri Hi Par Kuch Nahi Munhisar Inn Ko Rab Keh Rahe Hain Barey Namwar.

4
Hum Tu Hain Rafzi Our Gali Magar Tum Batao Keh Yeh Shafai Kaun Hai.
Saye Ki Kya Zarorat Muhammad Ko Thi Misal-e-Saya Ali Har Jagah Sath They.
Zulashera Se Rehlat Talak Har Jagah Juz Ali Shaksiyat Dosri Kaun Hai.
Khandaq-o-Badar-o-Khaybar Hunain-o-Ohad Har Karay Waqt Mein Kam Aaye Ali.

5
Baad Matlab Nikalne Ke Yeh Ummati Pochtay Phir Rahe Hain Ali Kaun Hai.
Mere Maula Khuda Mustafa Murtaza Taba Qayam Yehi Silsila Noor Ka.
Banda-e-Murtaza Ko Khabar Hi Nahi Kharji Kaun Hai Nasbi Kaun Hai.
Hashar Mein Dekh Kar Meri Aao Bhagat Sab Ko Hairat Thi Yeh Aadmi Kaun Hai.
