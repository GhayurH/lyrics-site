Ali akber ki azaan

Akber Akber

Allahu Akber, Allahu Akber, Allahu Akber, Allahu Akber

Subh ashoora hui,maa ne ro ro ker suni, Ali Akber ki azaan

Akhri fajar hai yeh,is moazan ke liye,phir yeh awaaz Kahan

Dasht mai piyase moazan ne,Jo taqbeer kahi

Agayi apne musalle pe, Ali ki beti,Yaad phir ai use

Apne Baba ki azan,ro pari aun ki maa

Abhi kuch dair salamat hai,haram Ka parda

Sham ke waqt jab aye gi,azano ki sadaa

Jaan bachane ke liye,sar chupane ke liye,chadarein hongi Kahan

Ro ke baite ne kaha baap se,hasrat hai meri

Aap ki behan to sun lein gi,sadaa Akber ki

In hawaon se kahein rukh madine Ka karein,meri sughra hai wahan

Naam aya jo Muhammad ka to roye maula,Inna lillah parha aur yeh ghazi se kaha

Is ko marenge sinaa,yeh musalman bhala,reham khainge kahan

Aya jab naam e Ali uth ke yeh maula ne kaha,Mai fidaa aap pe Baba meri aulad fidaa

Teer Asghar ke lage,seena Akber ka chida,mujh pe khanjar ho rawan

Itna tarpe ga Hussain ibn e Ali ae beta,Teri maa ,teri phuphi,teri sakina sughra

Jee nahi payein ge sabhi,tujh ko barchi Jo lagi,tujh mai hum sub ki hai Jaan

Masjid e kufa nazar ane lagi karb o bala,do azano pe bohat royi hai bint e zehra

Aaj tak bhuli nahi ik baba ki azan,ik Akber ki azan

Kis qadar dard mai doobi thi sadaa Akber ki,gash se uth ker kaha Sajjad ne batlain phuphi

Sub kyun rote hain yahan,shor hai kaisa bapaa,kon deta hai azaan

Kesay manzar ho bhala mir taqallum wo raqam,bab e sa at pe huin 17 azane jis dam

Sun ke ik ik azaan,ro ke kehti thi wo maa mere Akber ho kahan
