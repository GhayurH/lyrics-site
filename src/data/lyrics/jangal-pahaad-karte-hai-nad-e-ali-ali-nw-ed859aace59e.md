Jangal Pahaad Karte Hai Nad-e-Ali Ali

mushkil ko meri hal karo mushkil kusha ali

hussain hussain hussain

ruksat ko ahlebait-e-nabi may gaye imam

qadmo pe lotne lagi saydaniya tamam

wo shor al-firaaq ka wo yaas ke kalaam

bache bhi sar patak- te thay le le ke sheh ka naam

rote thay yun to lipte hue sab hussain se

lekin kaleje phat-te thay zainab ke bain se

hussain hussain hussain

us waqt tha ajab sheh-e-deen par hoojume yaad

dhalna wo do pahar ka hua nim loo o pyaas

laashe pare hue thay azeezo ke aas paas

rokar falak ko dekhte thay shah-e-haq janas

ut-ta tha darde dil to qadam larkharate thay

farmake ya-ali-e-wali baith jaate thay

hussain hussain hussain

bhai ke sar ko chati se lipta ke ye kaha

batlao kya irada hai ay mere mehlekha

daste adab ko jodke bola wo bawafa

pyasi sakina marti hai ya shah-e-karbala

guzre hai teen din yuhin is pusht pa par

gar izn ho to pani ko jaun faraat par

hussain hussain hussain

ya cha chuke thay ghore pe abbas-e-haq shanaas

aaye sada bhatiji ki sunte hi dar ke paas

boli ye mashq deke sakina ba dard-o-yaas

qurban ammu jaan bujhado hamari pyaas

phat-ta hai dil atash se kaleja kabab hai

sakh-khayi kijiye ke ye kaare sawab hai

hussain hussain hussain

abbas ne kaha ke mera faqr hai ye kaam

bibi tumhare baap ka adna hai ye ghulam

di tumne aabru mujhe ay dukhtare imam

ab hogaya jahan may bahishti hamara naam

kausar mai samjhu dosh pe gar mashke aab ho

tum bhi due karo ke chacha kamiyab ho

hussain hussain hussain

chaati tak usne pani ko dekha jo ek baar

ghode ka dil hua sifat-e-mauje beqaraar

hasrat se moo phira ke nazar ki suye sawaar

bole ye baat jod ke abbase naamdar

tu pile ay faraz ke bahot tishna-kaam hai

hampar to be-hussain ye pani haram hai

hussain hussain hussain

bouchaar yek-bayak hui teero ki hai ghazab

tayghe chali aqab se shareero ki hai ghazab

ye shakl do jahan ke ameero ki hai ghazab

bujhne na paayi pyaas sagheero ki hai ghazab

naize thay aur chand se pehlu diler ke

teero ne chaan dala safeene ko sher ke

hussain hussain hussain

shane se yu ubal ke baha khoon ke al-amaan

thirra ke jhoomne lage abbas-e-nowjawan

machli ki tarha haath to reti pe tha tapan’

lekin juda na hoti thi qabze se ungliyan’

biddat hogayi thi jo is tafdari ke saath

talwar bhi tadap ti thi daste jari ke saath

hussain hussain hussain

raste may larkharate thay jab zoef se qadam

akbar sambhaal lete thay baba ko dam-ba dam

farmate thay tarap ke pisar se shahe-umam

jo haal ho baja hai ke bhai ka hai alam

baazu parak ke dilbare zehra ko le chalo

beta chacha ki laash pe baba ko le chalo

hussain hussain hussain

ghul pargaya ke sheh ke alamdar haaye haaye

ay noore chasme hydare karaar haaye haaye

ay ibne fathima ke madadgar haaye ha haaye haaye

ay fauje sheh ke jaafare tayyaar haaye haaye

chote se haath rakh ke dile dard naak par

de maara sar ko bali sakina ne khaak par

hussain hussain hussain
