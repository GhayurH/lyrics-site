1
Haider Ka Hamla Sarwar Raza Karbalai.
Kar De Ga Haider Ka Hamla Taein Taein Fish.
Marhab Ik Lamhey Mein Hoga Taein Taein Fish.
Dast-e-Ali Ne Waar Kiya Tu Mar Gaya Abad-e-Wod.
Ho Gaya Khud Hi Bhokne Wala Taein Taein Fish.
Ghasib-e-Zahra Soch Rahe They Janat Jaein Gay.
Janat Ka Rizwan Yeh Bola Taein Taein Fish.
Hub-e-Ali Gar Dil Mein Nahi Hai Soch Le Phir Dunya.
Roza Woza Sajdah Wajda Taein Taein Fish.
Zahra Se Shadi Ka Armaan Dil Mein Tha Jin Ke.
Unn Se Kehne Utra Tara Taein Taein Fish.
Jang Ke Maidaan Mein Kuch Aisay Shair Bhi Bhagay They.
Khud Tu Hoye Na Ho Gaya Ghora Taein Taein Fish.
Mere Nabi Ka Jo Bhi Ho Gustakh Zamane Mein.
Mar Ke Kehna Bomb Ka Gola Taein Taein Fish.
Dunya Ki Aisi Ki Taisi Malik Hain Maula.
 Asif Ka Hai Jag Se Kehna Taein Taein Fish.
