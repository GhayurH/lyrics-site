1
Jhola Veera Da Main Nana Roz Jholawan Asghar Khan.
Kadon Chotiyaan Walay Aawana Main Mangdi Roz Duawaan.
Sakhiyaan Ne Wichoray Paye Ne Maithon Apnray Bhira Lokaye Ne.
Tere Hanr Da Koi Baal Nayi Jinno Chuk Ke Veer Khadawaan.

2
Kool Hondi Te Seene La Laindi Tin Nokaan Kolon Bacha Laindi.
Tere Samne Ho Ke Rokdi Main Maut Wala Parchawaan.
Sughra De Dard Muk Janday Hanjo Akhiyaan De Veer Suk Janday.
Jay Jandi Wari Aidiyaan Man Lainda Veer Salawaan.

3
Watna Te Toon Veer Aa Jawein Mainu Sohni Shakal Wakha Jawein.
Teri Rah Payi Wekhaan Veerna Main Khol Ke Khali Bawaan.
:SHAH INAYAT: Aye Sughra Kehndi Rayi Cheti Asghar Jay Mur Ke Aa Jawein.
Tainu Babay De Nal Veerna Bolan Di Jach Sikhawan.
