Paani piyo to yaad karo, pyaas imame ki

1
Pyaaso sabile he ye
Shahido ke naame ki

2
1) Dono jaha ka pani hai, zehra ke lehre me
Ab kyu na ho hussaine ka, hak eik lehre me
Paani ki hai siyaat to hai, sheh ke gulaame ki

3
2) Paani hai sharme sare yatimo ke is kadar
Kese utha sakenga zamin, se wo apna sar
Kismat ne ki hussain alay, his salaam ki

4
3) Uske sabab hussain ka, bhayi hu wa shahid
Bundish laga ke pani ke, khush hogaya yazid
Darya me ek bhund ne thi, sheh ke naam ki

5
4) Sehra wo ho rahe hai shutar, asp or laeen
Pani magar sakina ke, lab tak gaya nahi
Lagti hai ab sabil yu ashko ke jaam ki

6
5) Ye surk or siya he sabi, laynge sarde jaa
Naam hussain se ye akidat hai eteram
Ab to shikast pani le sab, foj-e-shaam ki

7
6) Pyaaso sabile he ye
Shahido ke naame ki

8
7) Ashk sakina bazu, abbas ki kasam)
Ta hashre sheh ki pyaas pe, royenge ehle gham
He ye khalil dil ke hussaini ho sam ki

9
8) Asghar tarap ke margaya, ik bhunde ke liye
Darya kinare bazue, Abbas kat gaya
Naadan pyaase khub buja, hi hu saam ki

10
9) Kuz-e tamam-e bacho ke, ashko se bhargaye
Rokar asir kayd me, tashna jigar gaya)
Paani ke be wafaai ki sab, had tamam ki

11
10) Jalte rahe khayam kinaar-e-farat ke
Bujte rahe charaage musalsal hayaat ke
Mojo ko dekhti rahi beti imaam-e ki

12
11) Pyaaso sabile he ye
Shahido ke naame ki
