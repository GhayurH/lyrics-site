1
Behar-e-Khushnodi-e-Zahra Ho Sana-e-Abbas Mir Hasan Mir
Sarway Toba Se Bhi Aala Hai Liway-e-Abbas
Bas Alam Khalq Howa Hai Tu Baray-e-Abbas
Kon Sartaj Wafa Ka Hai Siway-e-Abbas

2
Jab Tak Iss Shair Ka Ik Naqsh-e-Qadam Baqi Hai
Youn Samajhiye Ke Wafaon Ka Bharam Baqi Hai
Nazd-e-Gehwara-e-Abbas Jo Shabir Gaye
Dekh Kar Chehra-e-Molood Sana Karne Lagay

3
Our Jabeen Dekh Ke Abbas Ki Phir Youn Bolay
Keh Do Yeh Ja Ke Ibadat Se Ke Woh Aah Bharay
Iteba Our Itaat Ka Nizam Aaya Hai
Mazhab-e-Zabt-o-Shujat Ka Imam Aaya Hai

4
Dekha Haider Ne Jo Shabir Ka Abbas Se Pyar
Muskura Kar Kaha Betay Se Padar Tum Pa Nisar
Chotay Bhai Ka Koi Naam Bhi Rakha Zeenihar
Ki Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Ne Yeh Ali Se Guftar

5
Ewaz-e-Tauheed Ka Akas Kahein Gaye Inn Ko
Baba Hum Aaj Se Abbas Kahein Gaye Inn Ko
Koi Abbas Ke Kirdar Ki Kya De Ga Misal
Inn Ki Aankhon Mein Hai Quran-e-Muhabbat Ka Jamal

6
Hai Khiyal Inka Wohi Jo Hai Imamat Ka Khiyal
In Ke Kirdar Ki Rifat Mein Nahi Koi Sawal
Aisi Rifat Ke Jahan Sarhad-e-Ismat Mil Jaye
Do Qadam Aagay Barhe Our Imamat Mill Jaye

7
Yeh Mawaddat Ke Gulistan Ki Be-Misal Bahar
Woh Chacha Hai Jo Bhatiji Ki Adaon Pa Nisar
Aas Bachon Ki Bhi Our Ahl-e-Haram Ka Hain Hisar
Aaj Bhi Foaj-e-Hussaini Ke Hain Parcham Bardar

8
Yeh Alam Choom Ke Janat Ki Hawa Pao Gaye
Iss Mein Tum Door Se Khushbo-e-Wafa Pao Gaye
Soch Kar Hath Lagana Yeh Hai Ghazi Ka Alam
Qalab Ko Paak Banana Yeh Hai Ghazi Ka Alam

9
Zehan Ka Rijs Hatana Yeh Hai Ghazi Ka Alam
Tab Kahin Issko Uthana Yeh Hai Ghazi Ka Alam
Warna Kufar Se Jab Jang Mein Takrao Gaye
Tum Bhi Maidaan Mein Alam Phaink Ke Bhag Aao Gaye
