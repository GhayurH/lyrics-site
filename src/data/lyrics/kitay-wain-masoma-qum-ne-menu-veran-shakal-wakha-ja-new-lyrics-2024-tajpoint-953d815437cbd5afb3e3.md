Kitay Wain Masoma Qum Ne Menu Veran Shakal Wakha Ja.
Fair Bhawein Main Mar Jawaan Mainu Veer Raza Gal La Ja.
Main Veran Syed Zadi Aithay Koi Nahin Mehram Mera.
Muk Chaliyaan Meriyaan Sahwaan Mainu Veran Aa Dafna Ja.

2
Koi Mere Veer Raza Nu Paigham Deway Mashhad Ja Ke.
Jehri Mar Gayi Wich Rahwaan De Oss Bhain Di Qabar Bana Ja.
Main Tere Hijar Che Veran Chad Watan Madina Aa Gayi Aan.
Meri Aa Gayi Aakhri Manzil Aa Veer Raza Qum Aa Ja.

3
Jiwein Humshakal Nabi Da Gaya Chad Rondi Mar Gayi Sughra.
