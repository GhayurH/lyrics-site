Aamad Aamad Harm-e-Shah Ki Darbar Mein Hai
Subah Se Jashan Ka Gul Shaam Ke Bazar Mein Hai

1
Suhbat-e-Aysh-o-Tarb Majlis-e-Gadar Mein Hai
Shoor-e-Faryad-o-Baka Itrat-e-Athar Mein Hai
Nobatein Bajti Hain Dushman Tu Khushi Hote Hain
Fatima Peetti Hain Sher-e-Khuda Rote Hain

2
Agay Agay Tu Hain Sajad Jhukaye Gardan
Paon Beri Mein Gala Tooq Mein Gardan Mein Rasan
Misal-e-Khursheed Falak Sharam Se Larzaan Hai Badan
Chaak Hai Gham Mein Girbiyan-e-Qaba Ta Daman

3
Beth Jate Hain Tu Jhunjla Ke Uthate Hain Laein
Boriyaan Nezeon Ki Shano Mein Chubhate Hain Laein
Pindliyaan Soji Hain Our Toq Se Chalta Hai Gala
Sakht Ayza Mein Hai Farzand-e-Sheh-e-Karb-o-Bala

4
Khar Talwon Mein Hain Maqtal Se Hai Paidal Jo Chala
Dhajiyan Paon Mein Bandhe Hai Wo Nazoon Ka Pala
Oss Ki Mazlomi Pe Be Tab Harm Hote Hain
Dedah Halqa Zanjeer Laho Rote Hain
