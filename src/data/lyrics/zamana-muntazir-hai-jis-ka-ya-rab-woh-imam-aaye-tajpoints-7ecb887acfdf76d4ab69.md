1
Zamana Muntazir Hai Jis Ka Ya Rab Woh Imam Aaye.
Woh Humnam-e-Muhammad Sorat-e-Khair Al-Anaam Aaye.
Imamat Ke Fazail Jab Bhi Mozu Kalam Aaye.
Mazameen-e-Mawadat Bilkhasos Mere Naam Aaye.

2
Nabi Ke Barhwein Farzand-o-Naib Ka Hai Naam Aaya.
Muhibbon Ki Taraf Se Ab Darood Aaye Salam Aaye.
Yeh Darbar-e-Imamat Hai Bari Iss Ki Fazeelat Hai.
Yehi Woh Dar Hai Jis Dar Par Farishtay Subha-o-Sham Aaye.

3
Hain Matwalay Mawali Aaj Molai Sabhi Raqsaan.
Keh Inn Ko Salsabeel-e-Khuld Se Kausar Ke Jaam Aaye.
Nahi Mumkin Yeh Joor-o-Zulm-o-Istabdad Mit Jaein.
Magar Oss Din Keh Jis Din Takht Par Apna Imam Aaye.

4
Phir Aaye Inqalab Adal Dein Ka Bool Bala Ho.
Hasool-e-Adal Ke Manshoor Mein Har Khas-o-Aam Aaye.
Zamana Phir Se Hai Zair-e-Aytab-e-Zulmat-e-Doraan.
Woh Mohar Noor Khatmi Martabat Khatam Imam Aaye.

5
Ho Koi Door-e-Doraan Sach Hai Har Firon Ra Mosa.
Koi Namrood Ya Phir Aaj Koi Be Lagam Aaye.
Abas Sabit Hoi Hain Koshishein Our Kawishein Sari.
Na Mehdi Aaein Jab Tak Kaisay Islami Nizam Aaye.

6
Zahoor-e-Qayam-e-Aal-e-Muhammad Hum Sabhi Dekhein.
Yeh Ghaibat Ke Uthein Parday Wali-e-Amar Aam Aaye.
Qaseeda Aap Ke Mumtaz Ne Kiya Khoob Likha Hai.
Sabhi Kehte Hain Garchey Our Bhi They Khush Kalam Aaye.
