1
Karbala Dekha De Mola Syed Raza Abbas Zaidi.
Aye Mere Maula Aye Mere Aaqa.
Marne Se Pehle Karbala Dikha De Karbala Dikha De.
Main Mar Na Jaon Tum Se Miley Bin.
Yeh Soch Kar Main Rota Hoon Har Din.
Mas Kar Ke Tere Rozay Se Maula.
Apna Kafan Sab Latay Hain Lekin.
Tera Kafan Main Laon Ga Mujh Ko.
Aye Be Kafan Koi Rasta Dikha De Karbala Dikha De.
Ghurbat Ka Aalam Sab Se Chupa Kar.
Karb-o-Bala Seene Mein Basa Kar.
Shoq-e-Ziyarat Mein Jee Raha Hoon.
Main Bhi Namak Se Rotiyaan Kha Kar.
Muflis Hoon Kaisay Rozay Pe Aaon.
Maula Tu Koi Mojza Dikha De Karbala Dikha De.
Zair Banoon Main Oss Ke Haram Ka.
Sab Par Hai Saya Jis Ke Alam Ka.
Tum Tu Kareem-e-Karb-o-Bala Ho.
Main Muntazir Hoon Tere Karam Ka.
Ab Bhi Jahan Hai Mashk-e-Sakina.
Mujh Ko Woh Roza-e-Bawafa Dikha De Karbala Dikha De.
Oss Bawafa Ka Chehra Jahan Par.
Zakhmi Howa Tha Ghorey Se Gir Kar.
Bazu Kahin They Paon Kahin They.
Kis Tarha Dekha Tum Ne Woh Manzar.
Ghazi Jahan Par Bikhra Howa Tha.
Haye Woh Nehar-e-Alqama Dikha De Karbala Dikha De.
Tum Ko Qasam Hai Oss Nojawaan Ki.
Jis Ke Jigar Mein Tooti Sina Thi.
Matam Karon Main Ja Kar Wahan Par.
Akbar Ke Baba Hasrat Hai Meri.
Khainchi Jahan Par Seene Se Barchi.
Aankhon Ko Meri Woh Jagah Dikha De Karbala Dikha De.
Ran Mein Jahan Tum Thak Kar Girey They.
Uthna Tha Mushkil Zakhmi They Itnay.
Phir Oss Maqam-e-Mehdi Se Chal Kar.
Tum Qatal Ga Tak Aaye They Kaisay.
Kohniyo Ke Bal Chalna Hai Mujh Ko.
Woh Rah-e-Sidratul Muntaha Dikha De Karbala Dikha De.
Yaad Aa Rahi Karb-o-Bala Ki.
Sun Lo Sada Zeeshan-o- Raza Ki.
Bulwa Lo Hum Ko Iss Arbaeen Par.
Hai Yeh Tamana Ahl-e-Azza Ki.
Paidal Najaf Se Aana Hai Chal Ke.
Janat Ka Mujh Ko Tu Rasta Dikha De Karbala Dikha De.
