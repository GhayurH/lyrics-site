Sakhi Abbas Alamdar

1
Jarri Abbas Alamdar
Tu Shair-e-Jali Ibn-e-Ali Ibn-e-Wali Hai
Kehti Hai Tujhey Karb-o-Bala Haider-e-Karrar
Tu Fatima Zahra Ki Duaon Ka Sila Hai

2
Khud Haider-e-Karrar Ne Manga Tu Mila Hai
Tu Bhi Hai Ali Teri Ragoon Mein Bhi Wila Hai
Tu Shah-e-Wafa Ishq-e-Manam Jan-e-Azadar
Aala Hai Zabardast Hai Sardaron Ka Sardar

3
Rutba Yeh Bhala Kis Ko Mila Dono Jahan Mein
Woh Tu Hai Keh Shabeer Bhi Hain Jis Ki Amma Mein
Shamil Hai Teri Marzi Bhi ALLAH Ki Haan Mein
Dharas Hai Ali Walon Ko Tujh Se Aye Wafadar

4
Bazu Bhi Nahi Phir Bhi Hai Tu Sab Ka Madadgar
Tu Jafar-e-Tayyar Ki Jurat Ka Libada
Tikta Hai Tere Samne Kab Koi Zyada
Tu Taig Chalaney Ka Agar Kar Le Iradah

5
Phir Maut Ke Chungal Se Nikalta Nahi Farrar
Buraq Ke Jaisi Hai Tere Ghorey Ki Raftar
Siffeen Mein Tu Aisay Lara Shair-e-Dilawar
Ik Bar Kiya Waar Tu Dekha Nahi Mur Kar

6
Dhar Chor Ke Maidan Mein Kat Kat Ke Girey Sar
Sab Kehne Lagay Aa Gaye Kya Haider-e-Karrar
Baba Ki Tarha Tu Bhi Chalata Raha Talwar
Yeh Bazu Salamat Hain Tu Bas Tere Karam Se

7
Dushman Tera Jalta Hai Tu Jalta Rahe Hum Se
Hum Door Nahi Hoon Gay Kabhi Tere Alam Se
Jo Bugz Rakhay Tujh Se Woh Lanat Ka Hai Haqdar
Iblees Hai Jo Teri Wafa Ka Karey Inkar

8
Woh Kabay Ki Chat Our Jalali Tera Khutba
Kabay Ne Liya Pehle Tere Qadmo Ka Bosa
Phir Kaba Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Se Yeh Pukara
Hai Ghaiz Mein Abbas Ko Rokein Mere Sarkar

9
Yeh Itna Jalali Hai Keh Gir Jaye Gi Dewar
Bas Gham Hai Yehi Aaj Dil-e-Ahl-e-Azza Mein
Afsoos Ijazat Na Mili Karb-o-Bala Mein
Be Dast Howa Mara Gaya Dasht-e-Jafa Mein

10
Yeh Kehte Hoye Jashan Manatay Hain Sitamgar
Haan Mara Gaya Lashkar-e-Shabeer Ka Salar
Woh Sham-e-Ghareebaan Woh Haram Our Udasi
Pani Na Mila Bali Sakina Rahi Pyasi

11
Be Pardah Hoi Haye Muhammad Ki Nawasi
Tu Qatal Howa Khaimay Jalay Lut Gaya Ghar Bar
Zainab Ki Sada Aati Rahi Khaimay Se Har Bar
 Zeeshan Hai Tu Jaun Tere Dar Ka Hai Nokar

12
Bas Dil Mein Yeh Arman Hai Aye Sani-e-Haider
Seene Se Laga Hum Ko Bhi Rozay Pe Bola Kar
Ho Jaye Karam Karb-o-Bala Dekh Lein Ik Bar
Kehte Hain Azakhana Saja Kar Yeh Azadar
