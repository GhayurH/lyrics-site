Momino Aao Suno Kya Sunana Chahta Hoon.
Ali Wali Ka Main Sehra Sunna Chahta Hoon.

1
Ali Hai Surah Tu Aayat Hai Fatima Zahra.
Quran Ali Hai Tilawat Hai Fatima Zahra.
Woh Ba Ka Nuqta Haqeeqat Hai Fatima Zahra.
Teri Qismat Bhi Kya Qismat Hai Fatima Zahra.

2
Ali-o-Zahra Ki Shadi Ka Ishara Aaya.
Salami Dene Tere Dar Pe Sitara Aaya.
Mila Jo Hukam Aye Khaliq-e-Akbar Hoga.
Kaha Malak Ne Muhammad Se Yeh Aakar Hoga.

3
Na In Ke Jaisa Kissi Ka Bhi Muqaddar Hoga.
Siwa Ali Ke Na Zahra Ka Humsafar Hoga.
Ho Jagmagata Har Ik Ko Na Mere Ghar Ka Ho.
Dulhan Nabi Ki Tu Dulha Mere Ghar Ka Ho.

4
Baat Yeh Pori Hoi Khaliq-e-Akbar Ki Qasam.
Keh Jagmagane Laga Sare Ka Sara Aalam.
Ali-o-Zahra Ki Shadi Ka Aa Gaya Mosam.
Dulhan Nabi Ki Tu Dulha Tha Khaliq-e-Zaigham.

5
Khushi Se Jhoom Rahe They Youn Farsh Wale Bhi.
Shareek Ho Gaye Shadi Mein Arsh Wale Bhi.
Keh Ho Rahi Thi Madine Mein Noor Ki Barsaat.
Chali Youn Haider Safdar Ki Momino Barat.

6
They Anbiya Bhi Shareek Our Kibriya Ki Zaat.
Yeh Keh Rahi Thi Farishto Se Sajao Kainat.
Hai Mere Ghar Ka Makeen Ghar Basane Jata Hai.
Khud Ali Apni Hi Qismat Banane Jata Hai.

7
Dar-e-Nabi Pe Jo Pohanchi Baraat Haider Ki.
Khari Thin Hawa-o-Mariyam Our Aasiya Bibi.
Nabi Ke Sehan Mein Horein Bhi Aa Gayi Sabhi.
Samaa Yeh Dekh Ke Kehne Laga Tha Rab-e-Jali.

8
Kuch Iss Tarha Bhi Falak Ko Sajaya Jaye Ga.
Yahan Pe Aqad Batool Parhaya Jaye Ga.
Rukhsati Fatima Zahra Ki Iss Tarha Hoi.
Choor Ke Ghar Ko Jo Susraal Fatima Thi Chali.

9
Inn Ke Jaisi Na Dilawar Kisi Ki Shadi Hoi.
In Ki Khatir Tu Sajaye Gaye Arsh Sabhi.
Khushi Mein In Ki Youn Sara Zamana Shamil Tha.
Anbiya Horein Kuja Arsh-e-Khuda Shamil Tha.
