Assalam Un Aleyk Ali Shanawar Ali Jee
Bar Zaban-o-Dillam Ya Hussain Ya Hussain.
Aslam o Alaik Ya Hussain Ya Hussain.
Aaye Maula Madine Se Karb-o-Bala.
Bhai Behno Ka Bachoon Ka Yeh Qafila.
Ghazi Abbas Ka Sar Pe Parcham Khula.
Aa Rahi Thi Sada Ya Hussain Ya Hussain.
Bhai Hai Mujtaba Baba Jaan Murtaza.
Our Madar Hain Jan-e-Nabi Fatima.
Nana Sallay Ala Ahmad-e-Mustafa.
Inn Ke Dil Ki Dua Ya Hussain Ya Hussain.
Aap Ke Ghar Ko Aada Satatay Rahe.
Band Pani Kiya Our Rolatay Rahe.
Aap Quran Parh Ke Sunatay Rahe.
Rahuma, Rehnuma Ya Hussain Ya Hussain.
Aa Rahi Hai Imam-e-Zamaan Ki Sada.
Aslam Aye Ghareeb Ul-Watan Marhaba.
Parh Ke Rotay Hain Hum Zyarat-e-Nahiya.
Aye Qateel-e-Jafa Ya Hussain Ya Hussain.
Haye Sham-e-Ghareebaan Na Bholein Gay Hum.
Haye Syedaniyaan Our Unn Pe Sitam.
Bibi Zainab Chalein Le Ke Dein Ka Alam.
Zainabi Azam Tha Ya Hussain Ya Hussain.
Pori:REHAN-o-SARWAR: Dua Ho Gayi.
Muntaqil Nasal Mein Ab Azza Ho Gayi.
Aye Shanawar:ALI JEE: Atta Ho Gayi.
Dolat-e-Karbala Ya Hussain Ya Hussain.
