Murtaza Kai Sai Mai Syeda Amber Naqvi
Mustafa Ke Saye Mein Our Kibriya Ke Saye Mein
Yeh Khudai Chal Rahi Hai Murtaza Ke Saye Mein
Muskura Ke Hazrat-e-Salman Kehte Hain Suno
Main Muqalid Hoon Ali Ka Tum Zara Yeh Dekh Lo
Jee Raha Hoon Main Musalsal Lailah Ke Saye Mein
Yeh Khafi Hai Yeh Jali Hai Yeh Wali Hai Yeh Wasi
Iss Mein Kuch Shak Hi Nahi Zill-e-Elahi Hai Ali
Jo Ali Ke Saye Mein Hai, Hai Khuda Ke Saye Mein
Nooh Bolay Fikar Kyun Kar Ho Mujhe Tofan Ki
Wird Hai Nad-e-Ali Ka Mere Lab Par Har Ghari
Khof Mushkil Ka Ho Kyun Mushkil Kusha Ke Saye Mein
Qul Ho ALLAH Ho Ahad Kehti Hai Iss Ko Hi Ahad
Yeh Hi ALLAH Hu Samad Ki Karta Aaya Hai Madad
Hain Meri Sansein Issi Hajat Rawa Saye Mein
