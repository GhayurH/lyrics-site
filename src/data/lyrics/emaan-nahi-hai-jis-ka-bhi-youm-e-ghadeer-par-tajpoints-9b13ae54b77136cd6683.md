1
Emaan Nahi Hai Jis Ka Bhi Youm e Ghadeer Par.
Lanat Khuda Bhi Karta Hai Oss Be Zameer Par.
Ayelan Ho Raha Hai Wilaye Ali Ka Aaj.
Mil Kar Darood Bhejiye Shiyon Ke Peer Par.

2
Ghairon Ko De Ke Bartari Bidat Na Kijiye.
Takmeel-e-Deen Ho Chuki Maula Ameer Par.
Mumkin Hai Aaj Bhi Wohi Munkir Pe Aa Parin.
Jo Sang Khum Mein Barsay They Haris Shareer Par.

3
Bidat Bata Rahe Hain Jo Nazar-o-Niyaz Ko.
Nazrein Jami Hain Unn Ki Bhi Halway Pe Kheer Par.
Jo Bhi Aseer Ulfat-e-Haider Ka Ho Gaya.
Qismat Bhi Naaz Karti Hai Aisay Aseer Par.

4
Maidan-e-Khum Dikhai Diya Dil Ki Aankh Se.
Do Bhaiyon Ko Dekha Hai Nazron Ke Teer Par.
 Hasnain Ne Jali Ke Ali Ka Likha Kalam.
Haider Ki Yeh Atta Hoi Adna Faqeer Par.
