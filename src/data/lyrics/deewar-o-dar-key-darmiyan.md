Keh Rahi Hain Fatima Dewar-o-Dar Ke Darmiyaan
Maskh Pehlu Ho Gaya Dewar-o-Dar Ke Darmiyaan
Aaiye Fizza Yahan Jaldi Meri Imdad Ko
Ghut Raha Hai Dam Mera Dewar-o-Dar Ke Darmiyaan
Ro Ke Kehtin Thin Mere Mushkil Kusha Aa Jaiye
Aap Ki Hai Syeda Dewar-o-Dar Ke Darmiyaan
Yaad Kar Ke Apne Baba Ko Sada Deti Rahein
Haal Tu Dekhein Mera Dewar-o-Dar Ke Darmiyaan
Rab Ne Jo Mairaj Par Tohfa Diya Tha Ya Nabi
Parah Parah Ho Gaya Dewar-o-Dar Ke Darmiyaan
Kalma Goyon Ne Janab-e-Fatima Ki Shakal Mein
Deen Zakhmi Kar Diya Dewar-o-Dar Ke Darmiyaan
Pasliyon Mein Garh Gayin Hain Garam Mekhein Ya Ali
Qatal Mohsin Ho Gaya Dewar-o-Dar Ke Darmiyaan
Aye Khuda Teri Kaneez-e-Khas Yani Fatima
Parh Rahi Hai Marsiya Dewar-o-Dar Ke Darmiyaan
Saaf Jab Aanso Kiye YASIR RAZA Aaya Nazar
Aik Maqtal BaN Gaya Dewar-o-Dar Ke Darmiyaan
