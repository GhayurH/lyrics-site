Aasma khoon rota raha

1
Haey Zainab ke do ladlay
Aa gaye khoon may doobay huay
Maa to tasbeeh parhti rahi
Aasman khoon rota raha
Aik bhai ka sookha gala
Kunda khanjar se kat-ta raha
Aur behan sar patakti rahi
Aasman khoon rota raha

Tashnagi khud tarrapti rahi

2
