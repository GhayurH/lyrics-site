1
Jo Zair-e-Kissa Parwaan Charhe Zainab Aisi Hasti Hai.
Jo Zeen-e-Aab-o-Jad Ban Ke Rahe Zainab Aisi Hasti Hai..
Wo Jis Ne Banai Apni Jagah Arbab-e-Kisa-e-Masoomo Mein.
Khud Fatima Jis Par Naaz Kare Zainab Aisi Hasti Hai.

2
Shabir Se Baqi Deen-e-Khuda Shabir Se Baqi Paigamber.
Shabir Rahe Zindah Jis Se Zainab Aisi Hasti Hai.
Dharas Hai Imam-e-Doraan Ko Zainab Sa Nighebaan Sath Mein Hai.
Abbas Kabhi Shabir Bane Zainab Aisi Hasti Hai.

3
Kunbe Ki Hifazat Bhi Karna Bazaar Mein Khutbe Bhi Dena.
Is Wasf Mein Hai Maa Se Aagay Zainab Aisi Hasti Hai.
Be Pardah Jisse Ummat Ne Kiya Our Apne Taein Ruswa Bhi Kiya.
Jo Umtiyon Ka Pardah Rakhe Zainab Aisi Hasti Hai.

4
Sarwar Ke Liye Dein Ki Khatir Jo War Diye Unn Beton Ka.
Gham Bhi Na Kare Pursa Bhi Na Le Zainab Aisi Hasti Hai.
Mukhtar Hai Wo Majboor Nahi Chahe Tu Badal De Pal Bhar Mein.
Halaat Jo:SIBT-E-JAFAR: Ke Zainab Aisi Hasti Hai.
