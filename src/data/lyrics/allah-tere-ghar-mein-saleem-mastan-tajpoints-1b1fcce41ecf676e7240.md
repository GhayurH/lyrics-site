1
Allah Tere Ghar Mein Saleem Mastan.
ALLAH Tere Ghar Mein Jo Banda Maken Hai.
Ain-e-Khuda Ali Hai Yeh Ain Al Yaqeen Hai.
Aati Nahi Niyam Se Bahir Beghair-e-Izn.
Haider Ki Zulfiqar Bhi Kitni Zaheen Hai.
Aksar Ali Se Kehti Thi Ahmad Ki Ladli.
Salman Ka Aqeeda Bohat Behtareen Hai.
Mera Nahi Hai Qaul, Muhammad Ki Hai Hadees.
Jo Bhi Ali Se Bugz Rakhay Woh Laeen Hai.
Beti Ka Aqad Kar Ke Muhammad Ne Yeh Kaha.
Haider, Khuda Ke Raaz Ka Wahid Amen Hai.
Khaybar Ke Roz Naqa e Saleh Ne Di Sada.
Duldul Sawar Aap Ka Duldul Hasen Hai.
Aye Sheikh Apne Rozay, Namazon Ki Fikar Kar.
Yeh Deen-e-Mustafa, Abu Talib Ka Deen Hai.
 Ahsaan Par Yeh Hazrat-e-Mohsin Ka Hai Karam.
Nalain-e-Murtaza Pe Jo Iss Ki Jaben Hai.
