Shah-e-Mardaan Shair-e-Yazdaan Qowat-e-Parwardigar Hai Ali.
Kaha Meesum Ne Daar Par Haan Mera Pyar Hai Ali.

1
Jan-e-Man Maula Ali Ishq-e-Man Rah-e-Najaf.
Kyun Na Dewana Ho Woh Dil Hain Jahan Shah-e-Najaf.
Main Ne Inn Aankhon Se Dekhi Hai Karam Gah-e-Najaf.
Sare Jahan Se Haseen Hai Najaf Ki Zameen.

2
Jahan Rehtey Hain Do Aalam Ke Badshah.
Yani Dono Jahan Mein Teri Sarkar Hai Ali.
Badshahon Ka Shehanshah Zabardast Ali.
Tu Bulandi Hai Tujhe Kaun Karey Past Ali.

3
Kar Diya Teri Muhabbat Ne Mujhe Mast Ali.
Dil Mein Dharkta Hai Tu Mera Maula Hai Tu.
Hum Malangon Ko Tujh Se Ishq Ho Gaya.
Jahan Batti Hain Shahiyaan Tera Darbar Hai Ali.

4
Lehja Tera Suntay Hi Badil Ne Garj Kar Kaha.
Naam Tera Aaya Chali Dhop Mein Thandi Hawa.
Dekh Tere Ishq Ne Dewana Mujhe Kar Diya.
Koi Nahi Sirf Ali Tu Hai Mera Badshah.

5
Sare Rindoon Ka Thikana Hai Tera Dar Haider.
Wajad Mein Jhomta Rehta Hai Qalandar, Haider.
Jab Bhi Lagta Hai Wahan Narah-e-Haider Haider.
Maula Jisay Tu Mila Woh Wali Ban Gaya.

6
Dekho Sehwan Se Aa Rahi Hai Yeh Sada.
Haiderium Qalandram Mera Sardar Hai Ali.
Mere Murshid Mere Maula Mere Maharaj Ali.
Sirf Dil Hi Nahi Zehno Pe Tera Raaj Ali.

7
Tujh Ko ALLAH Ne Pehnaya Hai Yeh Taj Ali.
Shah-e-Wilayat Hai Tu Meri Chahat Hai Tu.
Baad ALLAH-o-Nabi Sab Se Tu Bara.
Wah Kya Shandar Yeh Teri Dastar Hai Ali.

8
Teri Baton Mein Guzarti Hai Meri Sham Ali.
Hum Faqeeron Ka Sahara Hai Tera Naam Ali.
Dono Aalam Ki Madad Karna Tera Kam Ali.
Chalti Hai Marzi Teri Mere Maula Ali.

9
Tu Ne Jo Keh Diya Samjho Woh Ho Gaya.
Marzi-e-Kirdgar Ka Tu Kharedar Hai Ali.
Itni Taqat Thi Keh Jo Chahe Woh Kar Sakta Tha.
Woh Bina Taig Bhi Maidaan Mein Utar Sakta Tha.

10
Kaisay Talwar Chalani Hai Woh Sab Janta Tha.
Baad ALLAH Ke Woh Hukam-e-Nabi Manta Tha.
Qowat-e-Parwardigar Teri Ghurbat Pe Nisar.
Tu Ne Jo Sabar Kiya Koi Nahi Kar Sakta.

11
Tera Darwaza Jalaya Teri Zahra Ko Rulaya.
Teri Gardan Mein Rasan Thi.
Phir Bhi Tu Kuch Nahi Bola.
Mere Maula.

12
Tu Hi Tha Bad-e-Nabi Sab Ka Imam.
Phir Bhi Karta Nahi Tha Koi Salam.
Tu Madine Mein Kitna Tanha Tha.
Qabar-e-Zahra Pe Betha Rehta Tha.

13
Haye Ali Haye Haye Ali Teri Tanhai.
Kofay Se Jo Karb-o-Bala Tak Aayi.
Tere Har Waar Ka Youn Badla Liya.
Tere Bachoon Ka Gala Kaat Diya.

14
Tera Qasim Tera Ghazi Tera Akbar.
Mar Gaye Aoun-o-Muhammad, Ali Asghar.
Tera Ik Beta Teri Tarha Bohat Tanha Tha.
Tha Jo Maidan Mein Seh Roz Ka Bhoka Pyasa.

15
Dekh Kar Jis Ki Taraf Kehte They Lakhon Aada.
Ghair Kar Maro Issay Yeh Hai Ali Ka Beta.
Aaj Rotay Hain Issi Baat Pe Zeeshan-o- Raza.
Maula Har Door Mein Tera Hi Lahu Behta Raha.
