1
Maqtal ki taraf lasha-e-Shabbir ko dekha
Luttai huwai jab chadar-e-tatheer ko dekha

2
Zainab ko na khuch apni aseeri ka raha gham
Jab Abid-e-Beemar ki zanjeer ko dekha

3
Rasi main gala mun pai tamachon kai nishan thai
Sajjad nai jab lasha-e-hamsheer ko dekha

4
Hurmal nai kai baar kaman kainch kai roki
Dekha kabhi bachai ko kabhi teer ko dekha

5
Rehwar nai foujon main Alamdar ko dhundha
Tab teeron ki barsaat main Shabbir ko dekha
