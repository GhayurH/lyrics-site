1
Momino Do Mil Ke Kandah Hai Janaza Haider-e-Karrar Ka.
Chal Basa Zainab Ka Baba Hai Janaza Haider-e-Karrar Ka.
Kis Ne Yeh Mehrab Mein Rukan-e-Hidayat Dha Diya Yeh Sitam Kaisa Howa.
Noha Yeh Jibreel Ka Tha Hai Janaza Haider-e-Karrar Ka.

2
Rook Lo Bhaiyya Janaza Mere Baba Jan Ka Ro Ke Zainab Ne Kaha.
Dekh Loon Chehra Dobarah Hai Janaza Haider-e-Karrar Ka.
Koi Zainab Se Yeh Keh De Ro Le Jee Bhar Kar Abhi Sath Hain Bhai Sabhi.
Hai Salamat Tera Pardah Hai Janaza Haider-e-Karrar Ka.

3
Eid Hai Nazdeek Our Ghar Mein Qayamat Ho Gayi Hai Saf-e-Matam Bichi.
Keh Rahi Hain Ro Ke Zahra Hai Janaza Haider-e-Karrar Ka.
Kaun Kofay Ke Yateemo Ka Rakhay Ga Ab Khiyal Gham Se Hain Sare Nidhal.
Shoor Hai Wa Ghurbata Ka Hai Janaza Haider-e-Karrar Ka.

4
Baap Ke Qadmo Pe Sar Rakh Kar Haram Ki Aas Ne Yeh Kaha Abbas Ne.
Alwida Ghazi Ke Baba Hai Janaza Haider-e-Karrar Ka.
Teer Aaye Ga Janazay Par Na Pathar Aaye Ga Our Na Wapis Jaye Ga.
Shan Se Yeh Dafan Ho Ga Hai Janaza Haider-e-Karrar Ka.

5
Sham Ka Ahwal Jab Beti Se Kehte They Ali Gham Ke Marey Oss Ghari.
Zard Tha Maula Ka Chehra Hai Janaza Haider-e-Karrar Ka.
Hai Takallum Yeh Qayamat Rozay Daroon Ke Liye Pursa Daroon Ke Liye.
Aao Dein Mehdi Ko Pursa Hai Janaza Haider-e-Karrar Ka.
