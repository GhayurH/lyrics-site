Ai raat na dhalna kay ujad jaaye gi Zainab.

Suraj na nikal na kay ujad jaaye gi Zainab .

Ai raat tere dam se sala-mat hai abhi tak Dhal tay hi tere hashr mein gir jaaye gi Zainab.

Ai raat subho ho gi tou kohi na rahay ga Beemar ko phir lee kay kidhar jaaye gi Zainab.

Abid ka gala taukh hai aur paao mein bedi Bandwa kay rasan Shaam talak jaaye gi Zainab.

Shabbir ne ki thi jo nasehat dam e rukhsat Kyu kar na rasan bazu mein band waaye gi Zainab.

Khutb-o se tere kaamp geya shaam ka darbaar Kirdar tou hai dar hi ka apnaaye gi Zainab.

Sojaa Ali Asgar ke sulaa ti hai sakina,

Chacha se main kehti hoon agar paani hai peena,

tu na jhoolay main machalna ke ujad jaayegi zainab.

Ali Akbar ne azaan di toh Zainab ne kaha hoga,

Tu hai humshakle payammbar tera toh dukh bhi sada hoga,

tu na maidaan main nikalna ke ujad jaayegi zainab

Koi kal aaya toh hogi meri mayyat pe.

Ali akbar na rahega aur abbas ka bhai,

afsos na karna ke ujad jaayegi zainab
