Live Like Ali Die Like Hussain

1
ALLAH loves all who have love for Muhammad in their heart
He is the city of knowledge Ali its door and path
Hussain and Prophet Muhammad can never be a part
Because this is what Muhammad had said off from the start
Hussainiyo
Hussain-o-minni wa aana minal Hussain Hussain

2
For life and death they are the guidance and philosophy
The sacrifice of Hussain and the way of life of Ali
You only need to look at the final sujood of each
This is the art of life and death we should try to achieve
Hussainiyo
These were the words of Ali "Fuzztou bey Rabbil Kabaa"

3
Hussain-s the one whos the meaning of courage love and peace
He owns the earth and he owns everything within the seas
He is the path to the heavens this is a gurantee
To love Hussain is the best way to let your soul be free
Hussainyo
We follow him cause he giudes us to what were looking for

4
When growing up we have been taught this lesson everyday
Whithout a doubt you have been born to serve the Ahlulbayt
For loving them you may face torture agony and pain
But everytime you should never be scared to facee that hate
Hussainiyo
The Bravery of Ali is what you should always show.

5
By sacrificing his companions and his family
Hussain has showed us how to live our lives and how to be
To give whatever we have in ALLAHs name well see
Theres not a thing in this whole universe we cant achieve
Hussainyo
To lie our life in the way of ALLAH should be our goal.

6
Our aim in life is to do good and to discourage wrong
This is the best way to let Karbala live on and on
And even through we know that fourteen centuries have gone
The sacrifice of Hussain lives on in us and our soul
Hussainiyo
By standing for what he stood for answering his call

7
Hussains ultimate meaning of faith and devotion
He showed us how to conduct ourselves as creation
And then by saving humanity gave us salvation this is a lesson every age and generation
Hussainiyo
Because of him we have got harmony within our souls

8
To call ourselves the lovers of these personalities
We have to follow them and should reflect on what they did
To fast and keep up with our prayers is imperative
These are the acts that go together with azadari
Hussainiyo
Without one or the other faith cannot be whole

9
Ya Ali Ya Hussain
