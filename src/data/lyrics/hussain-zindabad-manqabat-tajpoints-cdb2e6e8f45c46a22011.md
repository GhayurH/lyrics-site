1
Hussain Zindabad Manqabat Lyrics
Bahisht Jis Ki Saltanat Hussain Woh Janab Hai.
Bara-e-Karbala Hussain Rab Ka Intekhab Hain.
Hussan La-Jawab Th Hussain La-Jawab Hain.
Hussain Kamyab Tha Hussain Kamyab Hai.
Hussain Zindabad Hussain Zindabad.
Yeh Kal BHi Zindabad Tha Yeh Ab Bhi Zindabad Hai.
Taran-e-Hussain Hai Haan Sath Do Hussainiyo.
Hussain Se Murad Lo Murad Lo Hussainiyo.
Hussain Jis Ko De De Oss Ki Nasal Ba-Murad Hai.
Quran Mein Hadees Mein Yazeed Ka Biyaan Nahi.
Yazeed Ki Tu Qabar Ka Kahin Bhi Koi Nishaan Nahi.
Haan Ja-Baja Hai Dahar Mein Hussain Ki Olad Hai.
Jahan Mein Sab Se Zyda Ashk Jis Ke Naam Par Baha.
Zamane La Gham-e-Hussain Ka Koi Jawab La.
Wohi Tu Kamyab Hai Yeh Narah Jis Ko Yaad Hai.
Nabi Ka Mulk Pak Hai Nadeem Our Rehan Hain.
Hamari Sarhadoon Pe Sab Hussain Ke Jawab Hain.
Karam Hai Mustafa Ka Apna Mulk Shad Baad Hai.
Hussain Jis Ko Aap Ka Dar-o-Deyar Mil Gaya.
Ossay Khud Apni Zindagi Ka Aytbar Mil Gaya.
Nafas Nafas Ossay Khuda Hazar Bar Mil Gaya.
Salam Ya Hussain Ka Jisay Jawab Mil Gaya.
