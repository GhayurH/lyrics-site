Pyarey Nabi Ka Pyara Nawasa Mir Hasan Mir
Darya Kinaray Do Din Ka Pyasa.

1
Yeh Karbala Ki Mehmaan Nawazi.
Yeh Kofiyon Ki Hangama Sazi.
Aisa Mujahid Aisa Namazi.
Karb-o-Bala Mein Yasrab Se Aa Ke.

2
Bhatkay Howon Ko Rasta Bata Ke.
Insaniyat Ka Paigham La Ke.
Ik Ik Wafa Ke Paikar Ko Le Kar.
Akbar Ko Le Kar Asghar Ko Le Kar.

3
Humrah Apne Ghar Bhar Ko Le Kar.
Woh Pal Atta Ke Ghar Ka Ujala.
Jo La Fatah Ki Godi Ka Pala.
Taqdees Wala Tatheer Wala.

4
Chehray Pe Kaisi Tabindagi Hai.
Mazlomiyat Ki Paindagi Hai.
Lashay Se Paida Kiya Zindagi Ne.
Ahl-e-Haram Ke Dil Hain Niralay.

5
Baithay Hoye Hain Kiya Gham Sanbhalay.
Ab Reh Gaye Hain Sab Roney Walay.
Basti Se Keh Do Sehra Se Keh Do.
Khushki Se Keh Do Darya Se Keh Do.

6
Mazloom Hai Woh Dunya Se Keh Do.
Aye Najam Dekho Yeh Haq Numai.
ALLAH Iss Ke Gham Ki Rasai.
Bardasht Na Hai Sari Khudai.
