1
Jab Mola Aayenge Jab Mola Aayenge
Dekhna Aik Rooz Manzar Iss Tarha Ka Aye Ga.
Manzaron Par Ik Manzar Muntaqil Ho Jaye Ga.
Haan Yehi Manzar Qayamat Ki Khabar Ban Jaye Ga.
Sari Dunya Mein Ujala Noor Ka Ho Jaye Ga.
Khana-e-Kaba Ki Chat Par Ik Alam Lehraye Ga.
Aye Azadaro Suno Gaye Jald Mula Ki Sada.
Qayam-e-Aal-e-Muhmmad Askari Ka Meh Luqa.
Aa Raha Hai Aa Raha Hai Marhaba Sad Marhaba.
Majlis-e-Shabir Mein Ab Shor-e-Giriya Dekhna.
Pursa Daro Farsh-e-Gham Par Apna Mula Dekhna.
Surkh Aandiyaan Uthein Gi Phir Samaj Mein Aye Ga.
Jis Ki Dunya Muntazir Thi Phir Woh Manzar Aye Ga.
Mosam-e-Insaaf Le Kar Mera Mula Aye Ga.
Kufar Hujron Mein Chupe Ga Darbadar Ho Jaye Ga.
Mukhtasir Lashkar-e-Imam-e-Asar Ka Cha Jaye Ga.
Aye Ga Jab Haidery Talwar Ka Woh Wirse Dar.
Dekhna Chalti Hai Kaise Taig-e-Shair-e-Kirdgar.
Phir Zameen-o-Aasmaan Tak Aik Hi Ho Gi Pukar.
Shah-e-Mardaan Shair-e-Yazdaan Qowat-e-Parwardigar.
La-Fatah Illah Ali La Saif Illah Zulfiqar.
Dekhna Iss Tarha Mula Majlisein Karwaein Gaye.
Majliso Mein Karbala Wale Bulaye Jaein Gaye.
Nara-e-Haider Lage Ga Panjtan Jab Aein Gaye.
Dekhna Unn Majlisoon Mein Har Shaheed Aa Jaye Ga.
Phir Tu Woh Matam Karein Gaye Aasmaan Hil Jaye Ga.
Chand Narjis Ka Falak Se Jab Zameen Par Aye Ga.
Aein Gaye Sare Farishte Auliya-o-Ambiya.
Aab-e-Kausar Ki Sabeelein Bhi Lagein Gi Jabaja.
Unn Ke Aate Hi Sitare Khud Zameen Par Aein Gaye.
Dekhna Shehar-e-Khamoshaan Mein Diye Jal Jaein Gaye.
Akbar-o-Abbas-o-Qasim Ke Liye Giriya Kuna.
Roein Gaye Matam Karein Gaye Jab Imam-e-Ins-o-Jaan.
Fatima Zahra Ki Aye Gi Yeh Awaz-e-Fugaan.
Aye Ghareeb-o-Be-Watan Aye Karbala Wale Hussain.
Tera Nana Kar Raha Hai Teri Mazlomi Pe Bain.
Farsh-e-Majlis Syed-e-Sajjad Khud Bichwaein Gaye.
Marsiya Parhte Howay Baba Ka Akbar Aein Gaye.
Hazrat-e-Abbas Bhi Apne Alam Ko Laein Gaye.
Noha Ik Rehan Se Mula Mere Likhwaein Gaye.
Phir Yeh Noha Sath Mein Sarwar Ke Sab Parhwaein Gaye.
