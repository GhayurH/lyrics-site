Sukoon Yani Hussain Mir Hasan Mir Lyrics

1
Sakon Yani Hussain.
Makeen Hoye Jo Mere Dil Mein Meharbani Hussain.
Jab Aayi Teesri Shaban Muskuraney Lagay.
Yeh Aanso Jantay Hain Teri Qadardani Hussain.
Tera Bhi Dost Ho Tere Addo Ka Dost Bhi Ho.
Tera Habib Nahi Hota Darmiyani Hussain.

2
Hayat, Hilm, Haqeeqat, Hadees, Haq, Hujjat.
Hain Tere Naam Ki “Hey” Mein Ajab Mani Hussain.
Bacha Lein Jan Meri Deen-e-Mustafa Ne Kaha.
Aye Shah Zadi Sakina Ke Baba Jani Hussain.
Hussain Kehte Hain Jhuk Jhuk Ke Shukriya Zainab.
Rasool Kehte Hain Jab Teri Meharbani Hussain.

3
Hain Muntazir Dar-e-Ghaibat Ko Khol De Malik.
Humein Bhi Teesri Shaban Hai Manani Hussain.
Yeh Dekh Dekh Ke Chakar Ajal Ko Aate Hain.
Kafan Mein Karta Hai Taqseem Zindagani Hussain.
Beghair-e-Laan Tumhare Har Aik Qatil Par.
Galay Se Mere Utarta Nahi Hai Pani Hussain.

4
Zara Si Dair Hai Shabaan Ki Chaar Charhne Do.
Zameen Ka Rang Hi Kar Dein Gay Aasmani Hussain.
Unhein Jaloson Mein Maa Baap Le Ke Aate Hain.
Tumhare Mannay Walay Hain Khandani Hussain.
Woh Chahe:MIR TAKALLUM: Ho Chahe:MIR HASAN:.
Tumharey Naam Hai Dono Ki Zindagani Hussain.
