Sughra Ko Meri Seenay Se Lipatiyo Zainab.

1
Kehna Keh Hussain Abn-e-Ali Pyasa Mara Hai.
Sharbat Pe Mera Fateha Dilwaiyo Zainab.
Mangay Jo Sakina Ko Kaneezi Mein Sitamgar.
Tum Minatein Kar Ke Osse Churwaiyo Zainab.
Chadar Ke Ewaz Balon Se Munh Apna Chupaye.
Darbar Mein Zalim Ke Chali Jaiyo Zainab.

2
Zalim Jo Sakina KO Kafan De Tu Na Lena.
Masooma Ko Kurtay Mein HI Dafanaiyo Zainab.
Saqa-e-Haram Tashna Dahan Qatal Howa Hai.
Yeh Qabar Pe Abbas Ki Likhwaiyo Zainab.
Masoom Jo Bache Hain Unhein Dodh Ke Koze.
Asghar Ke Mere Naam Pe Pilwaiyo Zainab.
