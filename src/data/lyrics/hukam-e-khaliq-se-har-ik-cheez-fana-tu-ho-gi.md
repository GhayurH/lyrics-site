Hukam e Khaliq Se Har Ik Cheez Fana Tu Ho Gi
Kuch Nahi Ho Ga Magar Karb-o-Bala Tu Ho Gi
Irjai Ki Koi Derina Sada Tu Ho Gi
Zulm Ki Rah Mein Dewar-e-Azza Tu Ho Gi
Main Bohat Dair Se Pohancha Tha Azza Khane Mein
Phir Bhi Shabeer Ki Madar Hai Jaza Tu Ho Gi
Main Habib Ibn-e-Mazahir Ke Muqadar Pe Nisar
Teesri Zindagi Mehdi Se Atta Tu Ho Gi
Paikar-e-Noor Ne Teeron Mein Kiya Tha Ayelaan
Sar Rahe Ya Na Rahe Hamd-e-Khuda Tu Ho Gi
Koi Shahid Na Ho Shabeer Ka Maqtal Mein Magar
Sani-e-Fatima Zahra Ki Rida Tu Ho Gi
Inn Dino Jari Hai Sehraye Muharram Ka Biyaan
Inn Dino Bad-e-Saba Aabla Pa Tu Ho Gi
Jaun Ke Qadmo Mein Darya Ke Kinaray AKBAR
Hum Ghulamo Ke Liye Thori Si Ja Tu Ho Gi
