1
Tasbeeh e Fatima Zehra Aqeel Abbas Makhraj.
Tasbeeh Lab Pe Dil Mein Wila Fatima Ki Hai.
Yeh Naat-e-Mustafa Bhi Sana Fatima Ki Hai.
ALLAH Ke Rasool Ne Sehat Ke Wastay.
Payi Hai Jis Rida Se Shifa Fatima Ki Hai.
Janat Ka Ghasiban-e-Fidak Sun Ke Sun Hoye.
Jis Dam Rasool-e-Haq Ne Kaha Fatima Ki Hai.
Masroof Hai Tilawat-e-Quraan Mein Fatima.
Quran Ke Labon Pe Sana Fatima Ki Hai.
Janat Mein Kaun Jaye Ga Dozakh Mein Kaun Kaun.
ALLAH Keh Raha Hai Riza Fatima Ki Hai.
Jis Se Shifaein Karti Hain Khud Bhi Shifa Talab.
Dono Jahan Mein Aisi Rida Fatima Ki Hai.
Oss Par Haram Nar-e-Jahanum Hoi Areeb.
Jis Qalb Mein Maken Wila Fatima Ki Hai.
