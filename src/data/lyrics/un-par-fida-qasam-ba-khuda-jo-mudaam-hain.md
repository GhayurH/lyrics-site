Un Par Fida Qasam Ba-Khuda Jo Mudaam Hain.
Un Ke Liye Bahisht Mein Kausar Ke Jaam Hain.

1
Barah Imam Wird-e-Zabaan Subh-o-Sham Hain.
Awal Ali-e-Pak Sheh-e-Khas-o-Aam Hain.
Our Dosray Hasan Sheh-e-Aali Maqam Hain.
Our Teesray Hussain Alh-e-Aslam Hain.

2
Chothey Imam Hain Mere Sajjad Dilfigar.
Our Panchwein Hain Baqir Zee Qadar Zee Wiqar.
Jafar Chatay Hain Sarwar Zee Jah Namdar.
Our Satwein Hain Musa-e-Kazim Yeh Dil Nisar.

3
Hain Aathwein Imam Raza Sarwar-e-Anam.
Aye Dil Nawein Imam Taqi Hain Buland Maqam.
Daswein Naqi Hain Girahwein Hain Askari Imam.
Our Barhwein Hain Mehdi-e-Deen Bhaij Tu Salam.

4
Din Raat Jo Bhi Wird Kare In Ke Naam Ko.
Yeh Naam Shah Karte Hain Pal Mein Ghulam Ko.
Pohanchao Subh-o-Sham Sada Iss Payam Ko.
Gar Peena Chahte Ho Jo Kausar Ke Jaam Ko.
