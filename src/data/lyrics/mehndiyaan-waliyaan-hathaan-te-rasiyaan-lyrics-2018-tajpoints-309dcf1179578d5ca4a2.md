1
Mehndiyaan Waliyaan Hathaan Te Rasiyaan Pay Gayiyaan.
Sir Sehriyaan Wala Naizay Te Aa Gaya Te Mawaan Khak Te Beh Gayiyaan.
Yousuf De Malkaan Da Teraan Baras Da Yousuf Taqseem Ewein Hoya.
Kuj Khak Che Miliya Summa Che Kuj Gaya Kuj Garam Hawawaan Lay Gayiyaan.

2
Farwa Aye Aakhdi Si Pathar Howan Gaye Sehra Te Khar Teri Mehndi.
Wich Sham De Qasim Amari Manaway Gi Jay Rasmaa Baqi Reh Gayiyaan.
Hawa Te Hajra Wi Arshaan Toon Aa Gaiyaan Si Shagna Di Le Ke Mehndi.
Par Sham-e-Ghareeban Shagna De Naam Te Mitiyaan Arshaan Te Lay Gayiyaan.

3
Qasim Di Lash Te Aye Farwa Ne Wain Kitay Kinj Maa Shagan Manaway.
Barat Ich Teri Kuj Bewaah Qaidnaa Te Kuj Lashaan Hi Reh Gayiyaan.
 Akbar Nabi Di Ummat Wakhri Khushi Manai Agg Khaimiyaan Nu Layi.
Oss Welay Taein Ohh Lut Di Rayi Daaj Nu Jad Taein Chadraan Na Leh Gayiyaan.
