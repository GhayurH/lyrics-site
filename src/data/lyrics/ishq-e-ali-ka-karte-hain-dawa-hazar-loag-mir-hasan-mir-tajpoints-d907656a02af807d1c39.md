Ishq-e-Ali Ka Karte Hain Dawa Hazar Loag Mir Hasan Mir.
Sachay Hain Apne Daway Mein Bas Waza Daar Loag.

1
Baitho Mawaliyan-e-Ali Mein Tu Ho Khabar.
Bholay Nahi Murawat-o-Aysar-o-Pyar Loag.
Gul Bhi Hain Khar Bhi Hain Riyaz-e-Rasool Mein.
Hairat Hai Gul Ko Chor Ke Chuntay Hain Khar Loag.

2
Hum Hain Mawaliyan-e-Nabi-o-Ali-o-Aal.
Khush Hum Se Mil Ke Hoon Gay Abuzar Shiaar Loag.
Ghazwaat Mein Hunain-o-Ohad Ke Paye Jehad.
Sarwar Ke Aas Pas They Ginti Ke Chaar Loag.

3
Batlaein Gay Farishtay Nuseri-o-Shafai.
Hotey Hain Iss Gharanay Mein Parwardigar Loag.
Raas Aayi Kis Ko Aal-e-Muhammad Se Dushmani.
Dozakh Mein Ja Ke Baith Rahe Beshumar Loag.

4
Maqbool-e-Bargah-e-Ali-o-Batool Hain.
Parhwa Rahe Hain Shair Jo Yeh Bar Bar Loag.
Hairat Saudiyon Ko Hai Bandish Ke Bawojod.
Rozon Mein Kaisay Aate Hain Parwana Waar Loag.

5
Banwa Tu Lein Woh Apne Baron Ke Mazar Bhi.
Laein Magar Kahan Se Aqeedat Guzar Loag.
Hasrat Se Dekhtey Hain Unhein Sakinan-e-Arsh.
Farsh-e-Azza Pe Baithay Hain Jo Khak Saar Loag.

6
Lainay Ko Inteqam-e-Shaheedan-e-Karbala.
Hain Muntazir Zahoor Ke Hum Sharmsar Loag.
Qurbani Kaisi Kaisi Azadar Detey Hain.
Detey Nahi Hain Apna Kissi Ko Bukhar Loag.

7
 Mukhtar Hoon Ya:MIR HASAN: Khush Rahein Sabhi.
Madah-e-Ahl-e-Bait Hain Bagh-o-Bahar Loag.
Hain:SIBT-E-JAFAR: Aap Jo Madah-e-Ahl-e-Bait.
Kartay Hain Aan Janab Pe Youn Aytbar Loag.
