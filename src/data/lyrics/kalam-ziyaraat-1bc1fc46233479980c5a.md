Tapti hui reti par, haey tukray badan hay
Pehchaano musalmano,  Yeh aulad-e-Hasan hay

1
1) Armaan tah shaadi kah tujhe dulha banay kah
Afsooz hain kay abh maa kay
Haato main kafan hain.

2
2) Bhaiyah teri mendhi main abh kisko lagawogi
Faryaad bukha karti
Ik ujri behan hain.

3
3) Doraye gaye gooray masoom keh lashay peh
Bikhra howa Karbal main
Qasim tera taan hain.

4
4) Beta hai kaha tu aab awaaz mujhe dehna
Tukro ko jaama karta
Hy Shah-e-Zamaan hain.

5
5) Qurbaani Hassan teri khaam agayi is dheen kay
Alay Nabhie kay sadkay
Yeh din jo roshaan hain.
