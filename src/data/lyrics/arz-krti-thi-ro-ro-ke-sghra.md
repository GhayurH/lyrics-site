Arz karti thi ro ro ke Sughra
Sath letay chalo mujh ko baba

1
.
Kon bekas ko taskeen day ga
Kon ho ga jo day ga dilasa
Sath letay chalay mujh ko baba
Tum se koi sawari na loon gi
Karbala tak may paidal chaloon gi
Ab judai nahi hay gawara

2
Ghar may kis tarha tanha rahoon gi
May to Asghar ko janay na doon gi
Ghar may rehnay bhi do jhoolay wala

3
Qafilay ko thahraye ga
Aayain gay kab ye farmayey ga
Bin tumharay rahoon gi na zinda

4
Likho gham ka Raza yun fasana
Ho gaya qafila jab rawana
Reh gayi peet-ti sar ko Sughra
