1
Pochy Zainab Sy Koi Faraz Ali Zaidi.
Pochay Zainab Se Koi Ghazi Ka Sadma Logo.
Dekha Zainab Ne Jawaan Bhai Ka Lasha Logo.
Shimar Kehta Tha Keh Abbas Nahi Aaye Ga.
Ab Koi Chadar-e-Zainab Na Bacha Paye Ga.
Baad Abbas Ke Zainab Hoi Tanha Logo.
Rona Zainab Ko Bohat Bachi Ka Tarpata Tha.
Zeen Se Girna Alamdar Ka Yaad Aata Tha.
Jab Chacha Jaan Ko Bulati Thi Sakina Logo.
Dekha Darya Ki Taraf Chin Gayi Jab Sar Se Rida.
Mar Gayin Marne Se Pehle Wahin Bint-e-Zahra.
Gash Pe Gash Khati Thin Yeh Dekh Ke Fizza Logo.
Dhond Kar Sham-e-Ghareebaan Mein Jo Laein Bachay.
Kis Tarha Bethi Thin Woh Pas Jalay Khaimon Ke.
Kaisay Ghazi Ki Tarha Se Diya Pehra Logo.
Sheh Ka Sar Dekh Ke Naizay Se Na Seh Pata Tha.
Haye Abbas Ka Sar Naizay Se Gir Jata Tha.
Koi Zainab Ko Tha Jab Marta Durra Logo.
Be Kafan Bhaiyon Ke Maqtal Mein Janazay Dekhey.
Tu Wahin Mar Gayi Thin Jeetay Jee Iss Sadmay Se.
Sham Yeh Jata Hai Zainab Ka Janaza Logo.
Lut Gaya Dasht Mein Jab Fatima Zahra Ka Chaman.
Bain Shabeer Ke Abbas Ke Karti Thi Bahan.
Dekh Kar Shimar-e-Shaqi Unn Ko Tha Hansta Logo.
Kaisay Maykhar Ke Darbar Mein Jana Likhe.
Kaisay Bibi Ka Asad Sar Ko Chupana Likhe.
Haye Abbas Ka Bas Lab Pe Hai Noha Logo
