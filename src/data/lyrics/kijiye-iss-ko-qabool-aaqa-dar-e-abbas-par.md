Kijiye Iss Ko Qabool Aaqa Dar-e-Abbas Par.
Kar Raha Hoon Main Jo Sajda Dar-e-Abbas Par.

1
Kyun Na Aa Jaye Bhala Kaba Dar-e-Abbas Par.
Jashan Hai Ghazi Ke Baba Ka Dar-e-Abbas Par.
Dekh Kar Mujh Ko Sar-e-Mimbar Yahan Bole Malak.
Aa Gaya Abbas Ka Banda Dar-e-Abbas Par.

2
Sab Tu Kabay Ki Taraf Jate Hain Hajj Ke Wastay.
Hajj Adda Kata Hai Khud Kaba Dar-e-Abbas Par.
Kaise Hoon Gaye Khuld Ke Raston Se Waqif Aap Sab.
Aana Jana Agar Nahi Ho Ga Dar-e-Abbas Par.

3
Jaise Rukte They Muhammad Dekh Kar Zahra Ka Dar.
Har Nabi Waise Hi Hai Rukta Dar-e-Abbas Par.
Khush Ghaadeer-e-Khum Mein They Salman-o-Abuzar Jis Tarha.
Aise Khush Hai Har Ali Wala Dar-e-Abbas Par.

4
Oss Ko Khud Abbas Dein Gaye Izn-e-Deedar-e-Najaf.
Aaj Jo Narah Lagaye Ga Dar-e-Abbas Par.
Jashan Shohar Ki Wilayat Ka Manane Ke Liye.
Aayi Hain Firdos Se Zahra Dar-e-Abbas Par.

5
Kaise Aa Jati Dar-e-Abbas Par Lene Mujhe.
Maut Ko Bhi Maut Ka Dar Tha Dar-e-Abbas Par.
Woh Dar-e-Abbas Tha Yaan Yeh Zameen-e-Toor Hai.
Soch Mein Hain Hazrat-e-Musa Dar-e-Abbas Par.

6
Mujh Se Ik Darweish Ne Yeh Khuwab Mein Aa Kar Kaha.
Zinda Rehna Hai Tu Ja Mar Ja Dar-e-Abbas Par.
Tere Fatwon Se Bhala Main Rukne Wala Hoon Kahan.
Dekh Mera Sar Hai Aye Mulla Dar-e-Abbas Par.

7
Fakhar Se Sar Arsh Se Takrane Lagta Hai Mera.
Jab Bhi Jhukta Hai Mera Bacha Dar-e-Abbas Par.
Rab-e-Kaba Ki Qasam Le Aao Tu Ho Jaye Ga.
Maut Ka Bemar Bhi Acha Dar-e-Abbas Par.
