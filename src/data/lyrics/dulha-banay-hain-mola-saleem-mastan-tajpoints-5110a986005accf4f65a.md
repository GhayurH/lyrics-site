1
Dulha Banay Hain Mola Saleem Mastan.
Al-Hamd Ki Khushi Hai Washams Jhomti Hai.
Har Simt Hai Ujala Har Simt Roshni Hai.
ALLAH Keh Raha Hai Dono Jahan Saja Do.

2
Ahmad Ke Ghar Ali Ki Baraat Ja Rahi Hai.
Khush Hai Khuda Ka Kaba Dulha Banay Hain Maula.
Dulhan Bani Hain Zahra Dulha Banay Hain Maula.
Jag Mein Siwaye Haider Koi Nahi Hai Aisa.

3
Jis Ko Chuna Khuda Ne Zooj-e-Janab-e-Zahra.
Utra Falak Se Tarah Dulha Banay Hain Maula.
Kaisay Bashar Karey Ga Tareef Murtaza Ki.
Poshaak Unn Ki Qudsi Nalain Unn Ki Arshi.

4
Our Noor Ka Amama Dulha Banay Hain Maula.
Munkir Hasad Mein Jal Kar Be Maut Mar Gaya Hai.
ALLAH Ki Taraf Se Oss Ki Yehi Saza Hai.
Bajta Hai Shadyana Dulha Banay Hain Maula.

5
Sab Auliya Kharey Hain Sab Anbiya Kharey Hain.
Dawood Murtaza Ka Sehra Suna Rahe Hain.
Parhtay Hain Sab Qaseeda Dulha Banay Hain Maula.
Hawa Our Aasia Hain Mariyam Hain Hajra Hain.

6
Tatheer Ki Rida Mein Janat Ki Malika Hain.
Manzar Hai Sab Khushi Ka Dulha Banay Hain Maula.
Washams Geet Gaye Walnoor Geet Gaye.
Khush Hain Ali-o-Zahra Kausar Bhi Muskuraye.

7
Quraan Khushi Se Jhoma Dulha Banay Hain Maula.
Ik Saf Mein Aa Gaye Hain Sab Ba Shaoor Insan.
Dekho Aimaad Naqi, Buzar Ho Ya Keh Salman.
Sab Ka Hai Yeh Tarana Dulha Banay Hain Maula.
