1
Tu Mujhey Rida De Day Main Tujhe Kafan De Doon.
Waqt Se Zakhm Khaye Hain Tujhe Bataon Kya.
Dil Pe Kiya Guzar Rahi Hai Main Tujhe Sunaon Kya.
Har Ghari Mere Laboon Pe Hai Yehi Buka.

2
Ghar Tu Mera Lut Chuka Ye Tu Hai Tujhe Khabar.
Janab-e-Damishq Aal-e-Mustafa Ka Hai Safar.
Mere Zakhmi Dil Ke Waste Hai Ye Dua.
Tu Bata Ke Tere Baad Jee Ke Kya Kare Behan.

3
Zabt-e-Gham Mohaal Ho Chuka Hai Mere Be Watan.
Aa Giya Diyar-e-Shaam Ka Wo Marhalah.
Aik Aik Sans Mein Chubhan Hai Teri Yaad Ki.
Rastoon Ki Tarha Se Kathan Hai Meri Zindagi.

4
Kuch Sakoon Meri Zindagi Ka Kar Atta.
Zindagi Ki Har Khushi Tu Tere Sath Hi Gayi Har Qadam.
Ik Zara Si Meri Arz Maan Le Tu Ho Khushi.
Toor De Sakoot Zulmatoon Ka Uth Zara.

5
Har Qadam Wafay-e-Ba Wafa Mujhe Rolaye Gi.
Be Ridai Ka Ye Gham Behan Na Jhail Paye Gi.
Iss Kathan Safar Ki Sehal Kar De Be Khata.
Sare Imtehan De Chuki Hai Meri Ghamzadah.

6
Keh Diya Hai Tu Ne KHud Mujhe Shareek-e-Karbala.
Shaam Ke Safar Mein Tu Shareek Ho Zara.
Tera Sar Sina Pe Mere Sath Sath Jaye Ga.
Kuch Qarar Teri Ghamzadah Ko Aise Aye Ga.

7
Yeh Bhi Ulfaton Ka Ho Ga Aik Silsilah.
Bint-e-Syedah Ki Ye Buka Thi:MAZHAR ABIDI:.
Shair-e-Kibriya Ki Beti Aaj Qaidi Ban Gayi.
Zabt Ki Hadoon Pe Aa Chuka Hai Dil Mera.
