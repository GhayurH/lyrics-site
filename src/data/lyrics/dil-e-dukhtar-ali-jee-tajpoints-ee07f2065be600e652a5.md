1
Haye Hussaina Waye Hussaina.
Aye Waye Sakina Aye Waye Sakina.
Yeh Rukhsat-e-Aakhir Hai Pukarey Sheh-e-Wala.
Aye Zainab-o-Kulsoom, Rubab Our Ruqaiya.
Tum Sab Pe Salam Aakhri ALLAH Ko Sonpa.
Lipati Hoi Qadmo Se Yeh Kehti Thi Sakina.
Aye Kul Ke Madadgar Madad Karne Ko Aao.
Ya Fatima Zahra Mere Baba Ko Bachao.
Baba Na Kaho Baba Tum Sath Raho Baba.
Kuch Hum Se Suno Baba Aagay Na Barho Baba.
Aaram Karo Baba Bara-e-Dil-e-Dukhtar Khaimay Mein Chalo Baba.
Main Jan Gayi Baba Pehchan Gayi Baba.
Qurban Gayi Baba Talwaron Mein Jao Gay.
Phir Tum Bhi Na Aao Gay Bara-e-Dil-e-Dukhtar Tum Hum Ko Na Tarpana.
Baba Yeh Mere Baba Lazim Hai Agar Jana.
Yeh Hum Se Karo Wadah Jab Shab Ka Ho Sanata.
Our Meri Sada Sunna Bara-e-Dil-e-Dukhtar Tum Laut Ke Aa Jana.
Sheh Bolay Sakina Jaan So Jan Se Main Qurbaan.
Iss Tarha Na Ho Giriyaan Ik Wadah Nibhana Hai.
Ik Qarz Porana Hai Bara-e-Dil-e-Dukhtar Bara-e-Dil-e-Baba Tum Aaj Dua Karna.
Pursa Hai Paye Maula Rehan-o-:ALI JEE: Ka.
Kartay They Haram Noha Sarwar Ne Kaha Beti.
Saat Hai Judai Ki Bara-e-Dil Dukhtar Bara-e-Dil-e-Baba Maqtal Na Chali Aana.
