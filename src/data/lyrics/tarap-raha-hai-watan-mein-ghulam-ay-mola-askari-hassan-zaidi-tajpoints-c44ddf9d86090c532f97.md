1
Tarap Raha Hai Watan Mein Ghulam Ay Mola Askari Hassan Zaidi.
Mujhe Bhi Le Chaley Iss Bar Koi Karb-o-Bala.
Main Sar Ko Peet Ke Royon Ga Aap Ke Dar Par.
Kabhi Sagheer Pe Qasim Pe Our Akbar Par.
Haram Mein Aa Ke Youn Dena Hai Aap Ko Pursa.
Main Daswein Shab Ko Lahu Apna Sab Baha Doon Ga.
Youn Pursa Maula Ko Har Ik Shaheed Ka Doon Ga.
Lipat Ke Jali Se Rona Hai Shah-e-Karb-o-Bala.
Ibadaton Ki Ho Mairaj Gar Haram Ho Tera.
Main Dekh Paon Ga Sajday Mein Ja Ke Apna Khuda.
Karon Ga Karb-o-Bala Ja Ke Jis Ghari Sajdah.
Zara Sa Turbat-e-Ghazi Se Main Le Kar Pani.
Baha Ke Qabar-e-Sakina Pe Main Kahon Ga Yeh Hi.
Chacha Ne Bhaija Hai Bibi Yeh Aap Ko Koza.
Main Ja Ke Tilla e Zainab Pe Ro Ke Sochon Zara.
Ke Kaisay Dekha Tha Bhai Ke Sar Ko Hotey Juda.
Bapa Ho Majlis-e-Bint-e-Ali e Shair-e-Khuda.
Jahan Pe Hazrat-e-Abbas Ke Katay Bazu.
Hasan Ke Lal Ka Jis Ja Bikhar Gaya Tha Lahu.
Bana Ke Khak Mujhe Oss Jagah Ura De Howa.
Najaf Mein Aap Ke Baba Se Le Ke Izn-e-Safar.
Qadam Barhaon Ga Dil Mein Khiyal Yeh Le Kar.
Safar Woh Sham Ka Bint-e-Ali Ne Kaisay Kiya.
Nazar Jo Aaye Ga Mujh Ko Hussain Ka Roza.
Labon Ko Si Ke Sunaon Ga Aansoon Se Sada.
Hain Mushkilon Se Meri Aap Aashna Maula.
Yehi Hai Har Ghari Asim Ki Askari Ki Dua.
Na Aankh Band Ho Jab Tak Na Dekhey Karb-o-Bala.
Khuda Mila De Ossi Khak Mein Khameer Mera.
