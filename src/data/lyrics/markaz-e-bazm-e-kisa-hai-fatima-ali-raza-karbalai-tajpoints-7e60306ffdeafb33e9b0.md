Markaz e Bazm e Kisa hai Fatima Ali Raza Karbalai.

1
Panjtan Mein Markaz-e-Bazm-e-Kisa Hai Fatima.
Khud Khuda Tera Hawala De Raha Hai Fatima.
Woh Nabi Jis Ke Liye Khud Uth Kharey Hoon Anbiya.
Woh Teri Tazeem Mein Khud Uth Raha Hai Fatima.
Chalte Chalte Aap Ne Chakki Zara Kya Rook Di.
Do Jahan Ka Waqt Jaisay Ruk Gaya Hai Fatima.

2
Aasmaan Par Barh Gayi Hai Roshni Jab Keh Abhi.
Aap Ne Paon Musallay Par Rakha Hai Fatima.
La Makaan Jis Mein Makeen Hai Woh Makaan Hai Aap Ka.
Yeh Makaan Hum Rutba e Arsh-e-Olla Hai Fatima.
Ab Tu Fizza Bhi Karein Gi Aayaton Mein Guftgu.
Aap Ke Ghar Ka Namak Jo Kha Liya Hai Fatima.

3
Hujjaton Par Bhi Hain Hujjat Aap Ka Yeh Martaba.
Koi Arif Aap Ka Hai Tu Khuda Hai Fatima.
Bibi Ka Haq Khaney Walay Jitnay Hain Sab Jhotay Hain.
Our Sachi Fatima Hai Fatima Hai Fatima.
Gar Qabool Uftad Zahay Izzo Sharf Bano e Khuld.
Jo Asad Ne Our Raza Ne Ki Sana Hai Fatima.
