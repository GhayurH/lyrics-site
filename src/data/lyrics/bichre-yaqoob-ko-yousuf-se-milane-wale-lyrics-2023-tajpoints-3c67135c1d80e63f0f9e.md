1
Bichre Yaqoob Ko Yousuf Se Milane Wale.
Muntazir Kab Se Hain Kazim Ke Gharanay Walay.
Qaid Khano Mein Hoye Jate Hain Be Jurm Shaheed.
Qaid Khano Se Aseeron Ko Churanay Walay.

2
Iss Ke Atharah Jawaan Saal Pisar Hain Lekin.
Chaar Mazdoor Hain Maiyyat Ko Uthane Walay.
Betiyaan Hain Keh Sada Deti Hain Baba, Baba.
Kaun Batlaye Keh Baba Nahi Aane Walay.

3
Chodah Masoomo Ki Nisbat Tu Nahi Thi Maula.
Aye Mere Chodah Baras Qaid Nibhanay Walay.
Maa Ko Akbar Yeh Koi Keh De Na Bhaijay Betay.
Zindah Bachtay Nahi Baghdad Ko Jane Walay.
