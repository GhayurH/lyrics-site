1
Ishq-e-Haider Ka Karte Ho Dawa Tu Amal Se Dikhana Pare Ga.
Apna Ghar Bhi Lutana Pare Ga Apna Sar Bhi Katana Pare Ga.
Jhoom Kar Tum Lagate Ho Naray Kaam Aasaan Nahi Mere Pyare.
Dunya Wale Zabaan Kaat Lein Gaye Barsar-e-Daar Jana Pare Ga.

2
Madh-e-Akas-e-Jali Na Parhon Main Ya Ali Ya Ali Na Karon Main.
Phir Main Kis Ki Karon Madah Khuwani In Ka Humsar Dikhana Pare Ga.
Khud Nigahon Se sarkein Gaye Parde Khaliq-e-Kun Ke Dekho Gaye Jalway.
Misal-e-Salman Haider Ke Dar Pe Apne Sar Ko Jhukana Pare Ga.

3
Baat Rab Ki Thi Lehja Ali Ka Our Matan Tha Elahi Wahi Ka.
Talib-e-Deed Hoon Aye Muhammad Arsh Pe Tum Ko Aana Pare Ga.
Yaad Behlol Ki Zindagi Kar Yaad Kar Door-e-Miqdad-o-Qambar.
Ishq-e-Haider Mein Fatway Lagein Gaye Tujh Ko Pathar Bhi Khana Pare Ga.

4
Chahte Ho Mile Sarfarazi Mustafa Tum Se Ho Jaein Razi.
Sar Najaf Mein Jhukana Pare Ga Karbala Tum Ko Jana Pare Ga.
Chahe:YASIR RAZA: Hoon Ya Adil Ishq Mein Hona Chahe Jo Kamil.
Rang Lag Jaye Ga Arefani Apne Khoon Mein Nahana Pare Ga.
