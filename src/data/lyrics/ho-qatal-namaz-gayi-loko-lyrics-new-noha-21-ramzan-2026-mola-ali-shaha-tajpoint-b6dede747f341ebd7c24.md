1
Ho Qatal Namaz Gayi Loko Ghar Rab Da Khono Khon Aye.
Talwar Chali Quran Uttay Bismillah Khono Khon Aye.
Jis Pasay Munh Kar Ke Loki Parhday Roz Namazaan.
Kabay De Wich Aaya Jehra Ohno Dain Sazawaan.
Jehra Chehra Rab Da Aye Loko Sajday Wich Khono Khon Aye.
Aj Kofay Wich Pehla Maqtal Zalimo Wekh Banaya.
Zarb Chala Ke Ibn-e-Muljim Pak Rasool Rawaya.
Tak Maut Aye Wain Payi Kardi Aye Asadullah Khono Khon Aye.
Bazu Chum Ke Haider Aakhay Ghazi Nu Gal La Ke.
Veer Shabbir Taun Waar Dewin Banwaan Karbal De Wich Ja Ke.
Tere Bazu Tak Ke Aye Aakhan Yadullah Khono Khon Aye.
Bhain Atharaan Veraan Di Jad Piyo Zakhmi Kool Aayi.
Wekh Ke Dhi De Sir Nu Babal Ro Ro Deway Duhai.
Dastar Meri De Waang Teri Honi Chadar Khono Khon Aye.
Aal Rasool Rawa Ke Lokaan Qaul Ghadeer Polaya.
Nasir Si Jehra Kul Nabiyaan Da Ohno Mar Mukaya.
 Rizwan Parhaya Ahmad Ne Oh Kalma Khono Khon Aye.
