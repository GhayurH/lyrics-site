Ab Bhi Aati Hain Sakin Ki Sadaein Logo Haye Zindano Se Ravi Road Lahore Party.
Mere Baba Ko Mila Do Main Duaein Doon Gi Siskiyaan Le Ke Jo Kehti Thi Musalmano Se.

1
Bolay Sajjad Sakina Yeh Muqaddar Tera, Bolay Sajjad Sakina Yeh Muqaddar Tera.
Be Kafan Tujh Ko Liye Phirta Hoon Bazaron Mein Our Lahu Aaj Bhi Jari Hai Tere Kano Se.
Ja Ke Maqtal Mein Pukari Yeh Sakina Ro Kar, Ja Ke Maqtal Mein Pukari Yeh Sakina Ro Kar.
Bhaiyya Qasim Mere Akbar Tum Kahan Ho Sare Mere Baba Ko Bacha Lo Aa Ke Shaitano Se.

2
Jab Chali Ghar Se Tere Sath Sabhi They Zainab, Jab Chali Ghar Se Tere Sath Sabhi They Zainab.
Aoun-o-Muhammad-o-Akbar Yaad Aate Hoon Gaye Laut Ke Aayi Jo Ho Gi Tu Bandi Khano Se.
Lab-e-Darya Se Alamdar Ki Aayi Yeh Sada, Lab-e-Darya Se Alamdar Ki Aayi Yeh Sada.
Aye Sakina Tujh Se Sharminda Hai Chacha Tera Pani Pohancha Na Saka Haye Katay Shano Se.

3
Aath Aazanein Bayak Waqt Fiza Mein Gonjien, Aath Aazanein Bayak Waqt Fiza Mein Gonjien.
Sani-e-Zahra Ke Khutbon Ko Dabane Ke Liye Shoor Barpa Kiya Darbar Mein Aazano Se.
Boli Masooma Phophi Reet Hai Yeh Shamiyon Ki, Boli Masooma Phophi Reet Hai Yeh Shamiyon Ki.
Kar Ke Paband-e-Rasan Our Gira Ke Pathar Iss Tarha Koi Tu Milta Nahi Mehmano Se.

4
Sath Ghoron Ke Masoomo Ko Doraya Jaye, Sath Ghoron Ke Masoomo Ko Doraya Jaye.
Kissi Qanon Ya Mazhab Mein Kabhi Aisa Salook Koi Insan Tu Karta Nahi Insano Se.
Qayam-e-Aal-e-Muhammad Yeh Hai Akhtar Ki Dua, Qayam-e-Aal-e-Muhammad Yeh Hai Akhtar Ki Dua.
Sadqa Hasnain Ka Aye Maula Sada-e-Matam Iss Tarha Hashar Talak Gonjay Azakhano Se.
