ye sham e ashoor ka hai manzar

hai tarb main itrat e payambar

jabeen pay tayer pay alqama ke

kisi ki maiyyat tarap rahi hai

ye koi mehve buka hai shayad

kisi nay ammu kaha hai shayad

barha hai shayad koi sitamgar

jabeen pay tayer pay alqama ke

kisi ki maiyyat tarap rahi hai

wo bholi bhali si ek bacchi

najaf ki janib jo tak rahi thi

kisi nay cheeney hain us ke gohar

jabeen pay tayer pay alqama ke

kisi ki maiyyat tarap rahi hai

atash say bacchay machal rahay hain

ye phool sholon main jal rahay hain

chaman main kumla gaey gul e tar

jabeen pay tayer pay alqama ke

kisi ki maiyyat tarap rahi hai

hujoom e gham say jo neem jan’ hai

wohi jo bemaar natavan hai

kisi nay kheyncha hai uska bistar

jabeen pay tayer pay alqama ke

kisi ki maiyyat tarap rahi hai

rahi jo kufay ki shahzadi

kisi nay shayad usay sada di

chini hai kausar ab uski chadar

jabeen pay tayer pay alqama ke

kisi ki maiyyat tarap rahi hai
