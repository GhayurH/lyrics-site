1
Wo Naqsh Inma Hain Imam Ali Raza.
Tasveer-e-Hal Atta Hain Imam Ali Raza.
Jab Tak Rahe Ga Maktab Tadrees-e-Haq Khula.
Taseer Kul Kufa Hain Imam Ali Raza.

2
Naib Yeh Aatwein Hain Masihaye Kainat.
Har Door Ki Dawa Hai Imam Ali Raza.
Takfeer Ki Zuban Pe Tale Laga Diye.
Iman Ki Sada Hain Imam Ali Raza.

3
Jo Bhi Khalos-e-Dil Se Itat Shoar Hain.
Un Sab Ke Peshwa Hain Imam Ali Raza.
Waali Wali-o-Shah-e-Khurasan-o-Shah-e-Toos.
Tazeen Auliya Hain Imam Ali Raza.

4
Hajat Raway-e-Khalq Khuda Zamin Najat.
Farzand-e-Mustafa Hain Imam Ali Raza.
Hazir Hoon Dar Pe Jo Bhi Jabeen Sai Ke Liye.
Woh Jante Hain Kya Hain Imam Ali Raza.

5
Hakam Ne Zehar De Ke Kiya Aap Ko Shaheed.
Mazloom Ashqiya Hain Imam Ali Raza.
 Mumtaz Jin Ke Dar Pe Ho Maqbool Har Dua.
Woh Hujat Khuda Hain Imam Ali Raza.
