1
Ridawaan De Diyo Rawaan De Diyo MSO Party
Kujh Dair Charag Bhuja Ke Sajjad Nu Sham De Loko Shama De Diyo.
Assi Izataan Sharmaan Walay Aynj Galiyaan Wich Sar Nangay Nayi Turn De Loko Aadi.
Tusi Chadraan Te Nayi Denday Zindan Diyaan Parday Layi Kandaan De Diyo.

2
Masoom Aye Bachray Saday Soraj Di Ag De Marey Din Dhup Wich Kadday Sare.
Sangaan Te Sadiyaan Chadraan Balaan De Siraan Te Kar Ke Chawaan De Diyo.
Quran Da Ik Ik Parah Tusaan Kita Parah Parah Tusi Mariya Kunba Sara.
Lao Sangaan Taun Siraan Nu Saday Ghar Diyaan Wapis Sanu Aayataan De Diyo.

3
Mainu Sar Diyo Babay Da Qasim De Phul Shagna De Sohne Akbar De Sehrey.
Asghar Da Sariya Jhola Chachay Ghazi Diyaan Katiyaan Banwaan De Diyo.
 Akbar Aakhay Abid Ro Ke Kujh Bachray Chotay Chotay Mar Gaye Aountaan Taun Dig Ke.
Aye Chan Dafnawan De Layi Qabraan Banwawan De Layi Thawaan De Diyo.
