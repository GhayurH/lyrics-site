1
Baon Okhi Ikki Ramzan Di Raat Aye Saqlain Ali
Dhiyaan De Sir Taun Hat Gaye Ne Hath Ain Kareemi Walay.
Rondi Aye Aj Bint-e-Ali Kar Wain Yateemi Walay.
Aoulad-e-Ali Te Baon Okhi Ikki Ramzan Di Raat Aye.
Iss Raat Di Sahar Yateem Hone Wich Kofay De Sadaat Aye.
Farman Rasool Da Qatal Kita Wich Masjid De Zalim Lokaan.
Quran Nu Wekh Ke Aj Zakhmi Rondi Har Aayat Aye.
Dhiyaan De Sar Te Hath Rakh Ke Piya Aakhay Hasan Da Baba.
Tusaan Fair Kofay Wich Aouna Aye Le Qasim Di Baraat Aye.
Rab Jane Kiwein Chup Kar Ke Haye Zulmaan Nu Seh Gaya Ghazi Aye.
Aidhi Zindagi Wich Bint-e-Ali Di Ujar Gayi Kainat Aye.
Islam Nu De Ke Khon Apna Fuztu Paye Aakhan Aj Maula.
Imran De Khon De Sadqay Bach Gayi Aazan Te Salat Aye.
Ditha Do Salaan De Abid Ne Tar Khon Na Wajhullah Aye.
Aye Noha Mere Layi Azhar Osay Abid Di Khairat Aye.
