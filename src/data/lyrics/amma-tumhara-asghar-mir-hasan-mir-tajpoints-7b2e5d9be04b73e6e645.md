1
Apne Hathoon Se Baba Ne Turbat Mein Sulaya Tha Mujh Ko.
Our Sham Ke Zalim Lashkar Ke Nezon Ne Sataya Tha Mujh Ko.
Turbat Mein Mujhe Rehne Na Diya Sar Mera Bhi Nezay Pe Utha.
Amma Tumhara Asghar Nezay Pe Aa Gaya Hai.
Do Bar Zibha Mujh Ko Ran Mein Kiya Gaya Hai.
Nook-e-Sina Pe Amma Dohra Hai Dard Mera.
Ik Nezay Par Barabar Mein Aap Ki Rida Hai.
Chalay Paray Hain Kaisay, Zakhmi Hoye Hain Kaisay.
Kuch Tu Batao Amma Hathoon Ko Kya Howa Hai.
Apna Khiyal Rakhna Meri Na Fikar Karna.
Dadi Ne Mujh Ko Apni Godi Mein Le Liya Hai.
Che Mah Ke Ali Ka Din Bhi Hai Che Muharram.
Maa Dekhti Hai Ab Bhi Jhola Kahan Saja Hai.
Nikla Tha Jab Watan Se Tha Gaud Mein Tumhari.
Humrah Ab Tumhare Sar Kat Ke Ja Raha Hai.
Ghazi Ka Hoon Bhatija Aankhein Main Kholon Kaisay.
Main Ne Suna Hai Amma Behno Ka Sar Khula Hai.
Nezay Se Main Sakina Ko Dekhta Raha Hoon.
Chehra Meri Behan Ka Zakhmo Se Bhar Gaya Hai.
Zindah Bhi Dhop Mein Thi Turbat Bhi Dhop Mein Hai.
Bano Ne Aye Takallum Wadah Nibha Diya Hai.
