Sakhawaton Ke Samandar Hain Imam Zamin
Ya Keh Doon Sani-e-Haider Hain Imam Zamin

1
Tumhare Dar Pe Jo Aaya Hai Salami Dene.
Sabhi Ke Jagay Muqaddar Hain Imam Zamin
Hai Arsh Walon Ka Mehwar Hussain Ka Roza.
Har Ik Imam Ke Mehwar Hain Imam Zamin

2
Jo Nishapur Ka Ferooz Hain Mashoor-e-Jahan.
Tere Qadam Ke Woh Pathar Hain Imam Zamin
Haan Sare Mulk Ke Rehbar Hain Khaminai Jari.
Our Aap Unn Ke Bhi Rehbar Hain Imam Zamin

3
Tera Wajood Mita Sakte Hain Mamoon-e-Laeen.
Keh Abn-e-Fatah-e-Khaibar Hain Imam Zamin
Yahan Bhi Yousufi Detey Hain Sab Ko Soni Sabeel.
Wahan Bhi Saqi-e-Kausar Hain Imam Zamin
