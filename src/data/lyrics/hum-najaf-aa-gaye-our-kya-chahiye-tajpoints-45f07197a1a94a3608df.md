Hum Najaf Aa Gaye Our Kya Chahiye.

1
Aye Khuda-e-Ali Bas Qaza Chahiye.
La Makaan Ke Makaan Ka Makeen Aa Gaya.
Aye Buttoon Ghar Niya Dhondna Chahiye.
Toor Par Tu Nahi Hum Ko Mosa Miley.
Ab Najaf Mein Unhein Dhondna Chahiye.
Aye Khuda Sirf Chalees Ghar Ko Nahi.

2
Sari Dunya Ko Hi Murtaza Chahiye.
Jab Se Kaba Bana Tab Se Zid Oss Ki Thi.
Murtaza Chahiye Murtaza Chahiye.
Teht Al-Lafz:MIR TAKALLUM MIR:.
Kya Bataon Ali Ki Shan Hai Kya?.
Rab Ke Jaisi Meri Zaban Hai Kya?.

3
Hum Dar-e-Murtaza Pe Baithay Hain.
Iss Se Bahatar Koi Aman Hai Kya?.
Hum Najaf Tak Ka Kar Chukay Hain Safar.
Iss Se Aagay Bhi Aasman Hai Kya?.
Aisay Kaisay Samajh Aaein Gay Murtaza.
Aye Muqassar Bara Hosla Chahiye.

4
Hum Se Kyun Chanda Masjid Ka Letey Ho Tum.
Bas Khuda Se Agar Mangna Chahiye.
Bolay Asghar Yeh Hanstay Hoye Baap Se.
Ab Khiloney Nahi Hurmala Chahiye.
Parh Ke Sajjad Tasbeeh-e-Zahra Kaho.
Ya Ali Aap Se Mashwara Chahiye.
