De Gawahi Ye Kainat Teri
Wahdah-o-La Shareek Zaat Teri.

1
Had-e-Adraak Se Fazoon Hai Tu.
Sab Galat Hai Kahin Jo Youn Hai Tu.
Jab Bhi Dekhein Manazr-e-Fitrat.
Un Se Zahir Hai Teri Qudrat.

2
Tere Qabze Mein Hai Hayat-o-Mamaat.
Hai Teri Marfat Ke Do Ye Nakaat.
Sab Qood-o-Qiyam Tere Naam.
Sajdha-o-Shukar Aam Tere Naam.

3
Asdaq-ul-Sadeqeen Hai Tu Khuda.
Ahsan-ul-Khalqeen Hai Tu Khuda.
Laik-e-Hamd Bhi Sana Bhi Tu.
Sahab-e-Jood-o-Sakha Bhi Tu.

4
Rab-ul-Arbaat Sab Ke Rozi Rasaan.
Hai Tera Naam Sab Ke Wird-e-Zubaan.
Ye Hawa Ye Fizaye Koon-o-Makaan.
Khushk-o-Tar Say Hai Teri Zaat Ayaan.

5
Shark Ho Garb Keh Junob-o-Shamaal.
Har Taraf Tera Husan Tera Jamaal.
Noor Har Cheez Se Ayaan Tera.
Noor Hi Noor Sab Jahan Tera.

6
Tere Agay Jhuke Hain Sar Sab Ke.
Tujh Se Aabaad Shaad Ghar Sab Ke.
Khalq Mein Sab Nishaan Tere Hain.
Ye Zameen Aasmaan Tere Hain.

7
Rab-e-Shehar-al-Haraam Hai Tu Hi.
Malk-e-Kul Nizaam Hai Tu Hi.
Sab Ke Sab Rukan Our Maqaam Tere.
Masjid-o-Mashe-al-Haram Tere.

8
Rahmatoon Ka Teri Hisaab Nahi.
Koan Si Shay Tera Nisaab Nahi.
Tere Dar Par Liye Jabeen-e-Niyaz.
Sar beh Sajdah Rahe Ga Ye Mumtaz.
