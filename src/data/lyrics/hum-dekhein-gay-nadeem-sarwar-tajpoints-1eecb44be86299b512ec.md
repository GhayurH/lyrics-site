Hum Dekhein Gay Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics
Hum Dekhein Gay Lazim Hai Keh Hum Bhi Dekhein Gay.

1
Alajal, Alajal, Alajal, Al-Ajal.
Yabna Zahra, Yabna Zahra.
Ya Syedi, Ya Murshadi, Ya Molai Ya Asar Alzamaan.
Sham-o-Sehar, Aye Rahbar, Aye Tajdar-e-Kaun-o-Makaan.

2
Noor-e-Khuda Aye Muntazir Hai Muntazir Yeh Sara Jahan.
Kehte Hain Sab Jin-o-Bashar Arz-o-Samaa-o-Dasht-o-Jabal.
Hain Mushkilein Lakhon Magar Hai Mushkilon Ka Tu Hi Hal.
Aane Lagi Dekho Sada Hayyia Ala Khair Al-Amal.

3
Woh Roz Keh Jis Ka Wadah Hai.
Jo Looh-e-Azal Pe Likha Hai.
Jo Tera Hai Jo Mera Hai.
Konain Mein Jis Ka Charcha Hai.

4
Jab Loh-o-Qalam Ke Chehray Se.
Parday Sarkaye Jaein Gay.
Kuch Raat Gayi Kuch Baqi Hai.
Pardon Mein Sahar Kuch Kehti Hai.

5
Dil Par Jo Yeh Dastak Hoti Hai.
Ghaibat Mein Koi Ik Hadi Hai.
Jo Hazir-o-Nazir-o-Qayam Hai.
Woh Aaein Gay Haan Aaein Gay.

6
Haan Dekh Badaltay Mosam Ko.
Kya Ho Gaya Nazam-e-Aalam Ko.
Chain Aata Nahi Hai Aakhir Kyun.
Be Chaini-e-Nasal-e-Aadam Ko.

7
Deney De Sada Oss Qayam Ko.
Har Uljhan Woh Suljhaein Gay.
Yeh Jafar-e-Sadiq Ne Hai Kaha.
Woh Roz Juma Hai Ashora.

8
Jab Unn Ki Sawari Aaye Gi.
Tazeem Karey Gi Karb-o-Bala.
Kabay Se Azaan Phir Gonjay Gi.
Woh Aik Alam Lehraein Gay.

9
Woh Masjid-e-Kofa Mein Aa Kar.
Majlis Ka Sajaein Gay Mimber.
Woh Pursa Lein Gay Dunya Se.
Shabeer Ka Khud Zakir Ban Kar.

10
Zainab Ke Musayab Kar Ke Biyaan.
Mimber Pe Hi Gash Kha Jaein Gay.
Aankhon Mein Imam-e-Zamana Ki.
Rehti Hai Sada Iksath Hijjri.

11
Kehte Hain Zyarat-e-Nahiya Mein.
Main Bhool Nahi Sakta Hoon Kabhi.
Mabain-e-Zameen-o-Zeen Jo Tha.
Woh Lasha Bhool Na Paein Gay.

12
Paigham-e-Imam Zamana Hai.
Gar Jald Mujhe Bulwana Hai.
Taqway Ke Musallay Par Ja Kar.
Paigham-e-Aman Phailana Hai.

13
Jab Ilm Ke Deep Jalao Gay.
Hum Ghaibat Se Aa Jaein Gay.
Tajeel Ho Ab Ibn-e-Zahra.
Hai Wird:REHAN-o-SARWAR: Ka.

14
Dunya Ko Zarorat Aap Ki Hai.
Deti Hai Sadaein Karb-o-Bala.
Woh Roz Keh Jis Ka Wadah Hai.
Woh Wadah Aap Nibhaein Gay.
