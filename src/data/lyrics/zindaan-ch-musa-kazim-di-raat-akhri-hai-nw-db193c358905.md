Zindaan Ch Musa Kazim Di Raat Akhri Hai

Bay Jurm Chodah Saal Di  Aj Qaid Muk Gayee Ay

Aida Howay Koi Waris
Jera Aa Janaza Chaway
Be Varisanday Wangun
Aidi Laash Pull Tey Pai Ay

Mango Duwain Jag Tey
Enjh Da Na Qaidi Howay
Jeidi Peerh Tey Jawani
Qaidan Chay Rul Gai Ay

Vasday Shaihir Janaza
Mazdooran Nay Hai Chaya
Gurbat Di Aaj Ay Maula
Ho Inteha Gai Ay

Maiyat Nu Chad Kay Tur Gay
Na Bayriyan Lahaiyan
Baqiyan Cho Akay Zahra
Maiyat Tey Rondi Rai Ay

Mumtaz Likh Kay Nauha
Maula E Moosa Kazim
Ronda Kalam Ay Mera
Tehreer Rondi Paiye
