Hussain Zulmaton Mein Gohar-e-Tabinda Hai.
Keh Jis Ke Hone Se Insaniyat Painda Hai.

1
Sawar-e-Dosh-e-Risalat Ke Sar Ne Naiza Par.
Quraan Suna Ke Dikhaya Shaheed Zindah Hai.
Na Pohancha Pani Na Lasha Bhi Jaye Khaimon Mein.
Kaneez Zadah Teri Beti Se Sharminda Hai.

2
Sitarey Maand Hain Matam Fizaein Karti Hain.
Gham-e-Hussain Mein Magmoom Har Parinda Hai.
Udas Roza Nabi Rota Hai Dil Sughra Ka.
Shaheed Teri Shabeeh Ho Gayi Keh Zindah Hai.

3
Jo Sar Barehna Nazar Aayi Ghazi Ko Zainab.
Yeh Sar Ne Gir Ke Bataya Shaheed Zindah Hai.
Na Taras Bachoon Pe Na Pas Kalma Walon Ka.
Sipah-e-Shimar Mein Najfi Har Ik Darinda Hai.
