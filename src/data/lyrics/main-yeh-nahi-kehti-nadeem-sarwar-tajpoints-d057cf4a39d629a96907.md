Main Yeh Nahi Kehti Nadeem Sarwar.
Ghar Se Jane Lagay Shabeer Tu Sughra Ne Kaha.

1
Le Chalo Mujh Ko Bhi Beemar Tumhein De Gi Dua.
Ho Jana Khafa Rah Mein Gar Roye Gi Sughra.
Yaan Neend Kab Aati Hai Jo Waan Soye Gi Sughra.
Baba Tanhai Saza Hai Mujhe Aisi Saza Na Do.

2
Main Yeh Nahi Kehti Keh Amari Mein Bitha Do.
Baba Mujhe Fizza Ki Sawari Mein Bitha Do.
Main Yeh Nahi Kehti Keh Amari Mein Bitha Do.
Sab Baithein Sawari Pe Main Paidal Hi Chalon Gi.

3
Wadah Hai Kissi Ko Koi Takleef Na Doon Gi.
Baba Baba Amma Se Main Hargiz Na Kahon Gi Keh Dawa Do.
Palkon Se Main Saya Ali Asghar Pe Karon Gi.
Amma Ki Sawari Se Bohat Door Rahon Gi.

4
Baba Baba Haan Keh Ke Meri Zeest Ke Kuch Roz Barha Do.
Main Pani Pilaon Gi Sawari Ko Tumhari.
Behlaon Gi Rastay Mein Sakina Ko Tumhari.
Baba Baba Jo Kam Kaneezon Ke Hain Sab Mujh Ko Bata Do.

5
Sab Nazrein Churatay Hain Koi Dam Nahi Bharta.
Sach Hai Koi Murday Se Muhabbat Nahi Karta.
Baba Baba Tauqeer Meri Yeh Hai Tu Marne Ki Dua Do.
Kya Guzrey Gi Jab Ghar Se Chaley Jaein Gay Akbar.

6
Kaisay Mujhe Har Baat Mein Yaad Aaein Gay Akbar.
Baba Baba Kab Aaein Gay Lainay Mujhe Bhaiyya Yeh Bata Do?.
Haan Apni Muhabbat Ka Main Izhar Tu Kar Loon.
Thehro Ali Asghar Ko Zara Pyar Tu Kar Loon.

7
Baba Baba Naqay Se Jhalak Nanhay Musafir Ki Dikha Do.
 Rehan Woh Beemar Yeh Thak Haar Ke Boli.
Aye:SARWAR-E-: Zeeshan Tamana Hai Meri.
Baba Baba Jate Ho Tu Turbat Meri Tum Khud Hi Bana Do.
