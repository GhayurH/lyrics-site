1
Zindagi Bad Tere Aise Bitayi Baba.
Zakhm Tha Pehlu Mein Our Teri Judai Baba.
Baad Tere Mujhe Darbar Mein Bulwaya Gaya.
Unn Gawahon Ki Gawahi Ko Bhi Jhutlaya Gaya.
Jin Gawahon Ki Gawah Sari Khudai Baba.
Bharay Darbar Mein Kuch Aisay Sawal Oss Ne Kiya.
Main Nabi Zadi Hoon Hargiz Na Khiyal Oss Ne Kiya.
Oss Ke Lehjay Ne Kamar Meri Jhukai Baba.
Khof ALLAH Ka Na Dar Tera Tha Oss Ke Dil Mein.
Cheen Kar Oss Ne Sand Baba Bhari Mehfil Mein.
Chak Kar Ke Teri Tehreer Aurai Baba.
Chain Se So Na Saki Jab Se Hai Darwaza Gira.
Pasliyaan Toot Gayin Sans Bhi Dushwar Howa.
Hath Pehlu Se Hata Apna Na Payi Baba.
Main Agar Baba Yeh Keh Doon Tu Kuch Ajeeb Nahi.
Koi Bhi Dunya Main Oss Jaisa Bad Naseeb Nahi.
Jis Ke Durray Ne Meri Tori Kalai Baba.
Chacha Salman Bhi Baba Hai Sahabi Tera.
Takht Pe Betha Yeh Kaisa Hai Sahabi Tera.
Mujh Ko Jhutla Ke Sharam Jis Ko Na Aayi Baba.
Meri Tauqeer Bhula Di Hai Musalmano Ne.
Meri Dehleez Jala Di Hai Musalmano Ne.
Kalma Goyon Ne Wafa Aisi Nibhai Baba.
