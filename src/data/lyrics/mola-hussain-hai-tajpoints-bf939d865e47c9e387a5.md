Tauheed Ki Baqa Maula Hussain Hai.
Kalmay Ka Kuliya Maula Hussain Hai.

1
Aqalon Pe Kya Tumhari Hain Pathar Paray Hoye.
Kehte Hain Masjidon Mein Moazin Kharey Hoye.
Logo Yazid Tha Maula Hussain Hai.
Saton Dobarah Zindah Kiye Hain Hussain Ne.

2
Rahib Ko Chodah Betay Diye Hain Hussain Ne.
Kitna Sakhi Mera Maula Hussain Hai.
Jibreel Ke Farishton Ko Iss Ki Nahi Khabar.
Nazil Barah-e-Rast Howa Yeh Batool Par.

3
Quran-e-Fatima Maula Hussain Hai.
Iss Ne Tu Woh Kiya Jo Kissi Ne Kiya Nahi.
Sare Khuda Ke Kam Hain Lekin Khuda Nahi.
Aqalon Se Mawra Maula Hussain Hai.

4
Aa Bhai Aa Ja Tera Muqadar Sanwar Gaya.
Abbas Ne Yeh Keh Ke Galay Se Laga Liya.
Hur Muntazir Tera Maula Hussain Hai.
Bazar Se Guzar Gayi Zainab Yeh Soch Kar.

5
Quran Parh Raha Hai Woh Naizay Ki Nook Par.
Zindah Abhi Mera Maula Hussain Hai.
Pani Pe Jis Ki Pyas Ka Hai Aaj Bhi Qiyam.
Bhar Bhar Ke De Raha Hai Jo Kausar Ke Sab Ko Jaam.

6
Pyasa Woh Saqiya Maula Hussain Hai.
Yeh Faisla Azal Se Hai Aqal-o-Shaoor Ka.
Haq Hai Ossi Ko:MIR TAKALLUM: Garor Ka.
ALLAH Ki Ana Maula Hussain Hai.
