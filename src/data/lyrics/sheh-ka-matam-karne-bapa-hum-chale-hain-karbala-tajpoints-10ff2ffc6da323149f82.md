1
Sheh Ka Matam Karne Bapa Hum Chale Hain Karbala.
Hai Karm Abbas Ka Hum Chale Hain Karbala.
Aarzo Thi Hum Sar-e-Aashoor Jaein Karbala.
Farsh Majlis Par Yehi Karte The Ro Ro Kar Dua.
Lo Bolawa Aa Giya Hum Chale Hain Karbala.
Kab Muyasar Tha Humein Aye Momino Zaad-e-Safar.
Par Sakhi Shabir Ki Hai Yeh Sakhawat Ka Asar.
Pursa Karne Ko Adda Hum Chale Hain Karbala.
Jis Jagah Rukhsar Zakhmi Ik Yateema Ke Howay.
Hum Tamachon Ka Wahan Pursa Kare Rote Howay.
Dil Mein Hai Mehshar Bapa Hum Chale Hain Karbala.
Zakhm Saqa-e-Harm Ka Hai Dil-e-Hasas Par.
Bazoon Ka Hum Karein Pursa Kaf-e-Abbas Par.
Yad Hai Oss Ki Wafa Hum Chale Hain Karbala.
Tala Zainab Pe Ho Yeh Soch Kar Matam Bapa.
Ik Behan Ke Samne Bhai Pe Khanjar Chal Giya.
Aankh Mein Ashk-e-Azza Hum Chale Hain Karbala.
Jab Humein Aaein Muqam-e-Akbar-o-Asghar Nazar.
Roein Tarpein Our Hum Giriya Karein Sar Peet Kar.
Karne Giriya-o-Baqa Hum Chale Hain Karbala.
:ADIL-O-SALMAN: Pyasa Sheh Ka Kunba Tha Jahan.
Oss Furat-e-Gham Pe Ho Matam Ki Nohon Ki Azaan.
Sun Li Mula Ne Sada Hum Chale Hain Karbala.
