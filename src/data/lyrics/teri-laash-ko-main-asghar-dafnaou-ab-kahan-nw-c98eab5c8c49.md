Teri Laash Ko Main Asghar Dafnaou Ab Kahan

Dhoonday Gay Tujh Ko Naizay  Chaaron Taraf Yahan.

Jhoolay Main Ho Raha Hay, Kyun Bay Karar Dekho
Kaarvat Badal Raha Hay Kyun Baar Baar Dekho
Jab Say Sunee Hay Isnay  Akbar Teri Azaan.

Manzar Yeh Dekhtay Hi, Arsh-e-Bareen Hilla Hay
Ghutnon Kay Bal Hay Betha Chehray Peh Khoon Maala Hay
Sabir Hussain Jaisa  Layta Hay Siskiyaan.

Tera Mazaar Beta, Haathon Say Main Saajata
Afsos Meray Asghar Majboor Tera Baba
Khud Hi Mita Raha Hai  Teri Qabr Ka Nishaan.

Nazuk Galay Peh Vazni, Jo Teer Seh Gaya Hai
Roota Nahi Hay Bus Ye Gayoor Sochta Hay
Khaimon Say Cheekh Sun Kay  Kahin Aana Jaye Maa.

Fojh-e-Sitam Nay Dekhi, Taseer-e-Khoon-e-Haider
Dushman Ko Dekhtay Hain Asghar You Saar Utha Kaar
Lab Pay Hay Muskurahat  Chehray Peh Itminaan.

Saqlain Jab Bhi Hongay, Zahir Imam-e-Aakhir
Rutba Milayga Aala Sub Hongay Unkay Nokar
Asghar Kay Matami Aur  Asghar Kay Noha Khan.
