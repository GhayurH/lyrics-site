1
Zainab Rondi Rayi Be Kafan Bhirawan Nu.
Haye Sajjad Riya Ronda Phophiyaan Di Ridawaan Nu.
Chadraan Utarde Ray Khaime V Sarday Ray.
Pathraan Di Barshaan Choon Sanu Guzarday Ray.

2
Haye Saday Layi Chunday Ray Pathreeliyaan Rawaan Nu.
Ik Raat Qaid Khane Chup Ho Gayi Sakina.
Raj Wain Kitay Phophiyaan Kuj Boli Nayi Sakina.
Haye Sir Babay Da Takda Si Oddi Rukdiyaan Sanwaan Nu.

3
Baba Jay Tere Sir Nu Wekhaan Te Koray Khawaan.
Phophiyaan Di Chadraan Nu Rowaan Te Koray Khawan.
Haye Sajjad Tera Baba Bachiya Aye Sazawaan Nu.
Ro Ro Ke Wain Pawan Kinj Apne Sar Uthawan.

4
Wekhan Te Chadraan Wal Nazraan Kadi Na Jawan.
Haye Sangaan Te Putraan De Sir Labhday Ne Mawaan Nu.
Mere Raku De Saye Koi Baal Jad V Aaye.
Zalim Shimir Ne Aa Ke Menu Tazyane Laye.

5
Haye Masoom Tarasde Ray Har Moor Te Chawaan Nu.
 Akbar Kise Putar Da Aynj Imtehan Na Howay.
Phophiyaan Da Haal Tak Ke Aye Soch Soch Roway.
Haye Sajjad Hataway Kinj Lokaan Di Nigawaan Nu.
