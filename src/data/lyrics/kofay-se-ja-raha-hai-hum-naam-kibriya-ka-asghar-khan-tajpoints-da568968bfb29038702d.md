1
Kofay Se Ja Raha Hai Hum Naam Kibriya Ka Asghar Khan.
Zainab Yeh Sochti Hai Kiya Ho Ga Ab Rida Ka.
Zainab Pukarti Hai Youn Chor Ke Na Jao.
Phat Jaye Ga Kaleja Abbas Bawafa Ka.

2
Shabeer Ko Bola Ke Gardan Ko Chomti Hai.
Aaya Khiyal Jis Pal Zainab Ko Karbala Ka.
Nuqta Hai Ba Ka Logo Yeh Deen Ki Baqa Hai.
Kyun Qatal Kar Diya Hai Gham Zaad Fatima Ka.

3
Abbas Pochtay Hain Baba Yeh Kiya Howa Hai.
Kyun Khon Beh Raha Hai Quran Ki Abba Ka.
Nana Ka Naam Le Kar Sar Peettay Hain Sare.
Kofay Se Kiya Janaza Utha Hai Mustafa Ka.

4
Hum Lut Gaye Hain Nana Shabeer Keh Rahe Hain.
Dunya Se Ja Raha Hai Chehra Tha Jo Khuda Ka.
Likhi Hai Jo Kahani Har Lafz Ro Raha Hai.
Likha Batool Tum Ne Noha Yeh Murtaza Ka.
