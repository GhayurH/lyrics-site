Sadiq e Aal e Muhammad ka janza hay utha
Sazishi/Lanti Mansoor ne hay zehr Mola ko diya

1
Rotay hain Moosa e Kazim sar ko apnay peet kar
Uth gaya Mola ke ghar se aaj saaya baap ka

2
Khoo hay dhokay se bahaya Mustafa ki aal ka
Kalma go tu ne Nabi ko hay diya kaisa sila

3
Chaak kar ke tum giraiban momino matam karo
Mola Baqir ke pidar ka khoon hay na haq baha

4
Ilm ki khushboo se jis ne dehr ko mehka diya
Ilm e Quran o shairiat sab inhi ki hay ata

5
Mola Jafar ka dain pursa mil ke saray matmi
Aaj phir Syeda ke ghar may hay bicha farsh e aza

6
Jafar e Sadiq ka sadma hay mila sadat ko
Khon hay Jawwad rota ghaib may Mehdi Huda
