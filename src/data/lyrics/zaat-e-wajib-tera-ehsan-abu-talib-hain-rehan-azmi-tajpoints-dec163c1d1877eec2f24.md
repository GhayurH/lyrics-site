1
Zaat e Wajib Tera Ehsan Abu Talib Hain Rehan Azmi.
Farsh Par Arsh Ke Insan Abu Talib Hain.
Kaisay Jhutlaye Ga ALLAH Ki Naimat Koi.
Mani-e-Sorah-e-Rehman Abu Talib Hain.

2
Hain Nabi Our Ali Natiq Quran Agar.
Ba Khuda Dono Ka Juzdan Abu Talib Hain.
Kul-e-Eman Muhammad Ki Zabaan Mein Hain Ali.
Kul-e-Eman Ka Eman Abu Talib Hain.

3
Jis Ki Godi Mein Nabowat Bhi Imamat Bhi Palay.
Yani Sultano Ke Sultan Abu Talib Hain.
Aal-e-Imran Ka Sorah Yeh Sada Deta Hai.
Hain Nabi Aal Tu Imran Abu Talib Hain.

4
Mutmain Ho Gaya Islam Baqa Se Apni.
Kyun Na Hoon Iss Ke Nigehban Abu Talib Hain.
Kul Eman Pisar Our Bhatija Hai Nabi.
Kitne Mazboot Musalman Abu Talib Hain.

5
Pocho Islam Se Kirdar Abu Talib Ka.
Woh Kahey Ga Meri Pehchan Abu Talib Hain.
Oss Ke Eman Pe Shak Jo Hai Daleel-e-Emaan.
Our Quran Ka Unwan Abu Talib Hain.

6
Aqad-e-Ahmad Ka Parhata Koi Kafir Kaisay.
Aqad-e-Khuwaan Qari-e-Quran Abu Talib Hain.
Har Ghari Zaat-e-Muhammad Ki Hifazat Ke Liye.
Rab Ki Bheji Hoi Burhan Abu Talib Hain.

7
Panjtan Gulshan-e-Emaan Ke Gul Taza Hain.
Our Unn Pholon Ka Guldan Abu Talib Hain.
Karbala Jao Tu Rehan Yeh Hoga Mehsos.
Khoon Fishaan Chak Gareban Abu Talib Hain
