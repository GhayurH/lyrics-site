1
Bare Baron Se Bara Hai Hussain AS Ka Asghar.
Hussainiyat Ki Baqa Hai Hussain AS Ka Asghar.
Gharor Karti Thi Jiss Hurmala Pe Foaj-e-Yazeed.
Ossi Pe Ran Mein Hansa Hai Hussain AS Ka Asghar.

2
Jo Mangna Hai Tu Jhole Ki Samt Aa Jao.
Ameer-e-Karb-o-Bala Hai Hussain AS Ka Asghar.
Kabhi shaoor Ki Aankhon Se Parh Kitab-e-Atash.
Warq Warq Pe Likha Hai Hussain AS Ka Asghar.

3
Qadam Utha Nahi Paya Magar Yazeed Tere.
Qadam Ukhar Giya Hai Hussain AS Ka Asghar.
 Bilal Aab Meri Qamat Ko Koan Nape Ga.
Meri Jabeen Pe Likha Hai Hussain AS Ka Asghar.
