Sakina Shaam Ke Zindaan Me So Gayi Baba Mesum Abbas.
Sakina Sham Ke Zindaan Mein So Gayi Baba.

1
Tamachay Shimar Se Khati Thi Jab Bhi Roti Thi.
Andheri Raat Mein Uth Uth Ke Bas Yeh Kehti Thi.
Meri Aseeri Pe Roti Hai Bekasi Baba.
Aye Baba Aap Ke Seene Ki Oss Ko Adat Thi.
Zameen Mein Soney Ki Koshish Mein Jagti Hi Rahi.
Teri Yateema Ko Neend Aayi Na Kabhi Baba.
Phophi Se Kehti Thi Ro Ro Ke Yeh Bata Do Phophi.
Sitam Ki Qaid Se Ghar Apne Ja Sakein Gay Kabhi.
Ossay Rehai Na Mar Kar Bhi Mil Saki Baba.
Banai Aap Ne Maqtal Mein Qabar Asghar Ki.
Lehad Banai Hai Zindaan Mein Main Ne Muztar Ki.
Parhi Jo Fateha Roti Thi Hathkari Baba.
Kafan Lahu Bhara Kurta Hi Hai Sakina Ka.
Diya Hai Ghusal Bhi Ashkon Se Aye Mere Baba.
Kafan Bhi Rota Tha Our Main Bhi Qabar Bhi Baba.
Nahi Zarra Sa Ujala Bhi Oss Ki Turbat Par.
Andherey Karne Lagay Noha Oss Ki Turbat Par.
Karey Ga Kaun Yahan Aa Ke Roshni Baba.
Biyan Kaisay Karein Gay Yeh Meesum-o- Mazhar.
Jo Bain Baba Se Kartay They Abid-e-Muztar.
