1
Ghar Main Aya Hai Qatil e Akbar Mir Hasan Mir
Shiddat-e-Sabar Se Mar Jaye Na Maa Akbar Ki.
Din Madine Mein Woh Taqdeer Ne Dikhlaya Hai.
Jis Ne Mari Thi Kalejay Mein Sina Akbar Ke.
Ban Ke Mehman Woh Sajjad Ke Ghar Aaya Hai.
Ghar Mein Aaya Hai Qatil-e-Akbar Umm-e-Laila Ko Ho Gayi Hai Khabar.
Sheh Taraptay Hain Qabar Ke Andar Maa Tarapti Hai Qabar Se Bahir.
Phir Wohi Kaifiyat Hai Laila Ki Haal Tha Jo Jawaan Ke Lashay Par.
Oss Ke Qadmo Ki Aahatein Sun Kar Maa Ne Thama Howa Hai Apna Jigar.
Chehra Karb-o-Bala Ki Janib Hai Pani Hathoon Mein Le Ke Roti Hai.
Phir Raha Hai Nigah Mein Maa Ki Ali Akbar Ki Maut Ka Manzar.
Umm-e-Laila Sanbhal Zainab Ko Kal Yeh Shabeer De Rahe They Sada.
Aaj Abid Phophi Se Kehte Hain Maa Ko Meri Sanbhaliye Ja Kar.
Qabar-e-Zahra Se Aa Rahi Hai Sada Iss Ne Mara Hai Mere Baba Ko.
Phir Madine Mein Ik Qayamat Hai Hil Raha Hai Mazar-e-Paygamber.
Ali Akbar Lipat Ke Qadmo Se Keh Rahe Hoon Gay Apne Baba Se.
Toot Jaye Na Zabt Ka Bandhan Hosla Maa Ko Dijiye Ja Kar.
Sar-e-Akbar Utha Ke Naizay Par Umm-e-Laila Ko Yeh Dikhata Tha.
Oss Ko Kaisay Woh Bhool Sakti Hai Jis Se Khaye They Sham Mein Pathar.
Yeh Kareemi Se Ruk Nahi Saktay Qatilon Ko Duaein Detey Hain.
Iss Se Pehle Ke Bakhsh Dein Abid Koi Mukhtar Se Kahey Ja Kar.
Tum Ne Sajjad Mujh Ko Pehchana Sun Ke Qatil Se Bolay Yeh Maula.
Tu Woh Ibn-e-Numayr Hi Hai Na Jis Ne Tukray Kiya Hamara Jigar.
Kal They Mehman Hum Tere Zalim Aaj Mehman Tu Hamara Hai.
Our Kuch Chahiye Bata Tujh Ko Abhi Keh Doon Phophi Se Main Ja Kar.
Dil Nahi Lagta Aaj Ghar Mein Mera Aye Takallum Yeh Maa Ka Noha Tha.
Tham Kar Koi Mere Bazu Ko Le Chaley Fatima Ki Turbat Par.
