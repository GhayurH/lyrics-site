Mere Tu Dil Mein Ali Ka Khiyal Rehta Hai.
Khiyal Sare Hi Din Sara Saal Rehta Hai.

1
Ali Ali Ki Sadaein Hain Dil Ki Dharkan Mein.
Mera Yeh Qalb Tu Mehway Dhamal Rehta Hai.
Rah-e-Hayat Mein Khul Kar Ali Ke Dushman Par.
Na Ho Tabarah Tu Dil Mein Malal Rehta Hai.

2
Ho Pas Jis Ke Janab-e-Ameer Ki Dolat.
Ghareeb Ho Ke Bhi Woh Maal-o-Maal Rehta Hai.
Faqat Ali Hai Ali Hai Ali Hai Sirf Ali.
Zabaan Pe Jis Ke Jawab-e-Sawal Rehta Hai.

3
Tu Bugz Rakhta Dil Mein Tu Jan Le Munkir.
Ali Ke Bugz Ka Sar Par Wabal Rehta Hai.
Guzarey Ishq-e-Ali Mein Jo Zindagi Apni.
Zamane Bhar Mein Woh Hi Ba Kamal Rehta Hai.

4
Jo Tu Samjhta Hai Abbas Hi Jalali Hai.
Ali Asghar Ke Pas Bhi Jalal Rehta Hai.
Ghulam Dil Se Hoon Izhar Main Bhi Haider Ka.
Mujhe Bhala Kahan Khoof-e-Zawwal Rehta Hai.
