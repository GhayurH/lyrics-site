1
Charag-e-Khana-e-Zahra Hussain Aaya Hai.
Dahar Mein Karne Ujala Hussain Aaya Hai.
Barai Aa Ke Ali Ne Thi Zeenat-e-Kaba.
Bachane Hurmat-e-Kaba Hussain Aaya Hai.

2
Khuda Yeh Bola Farishton Se Aaj Farsh Ko Bhi.
Bana Do Arsh Mualla Hussain Aaya Hai.
Zamane Bhar Ke Ghareebon Ko Yeh Khabar Kar Do.
Tumhara Ban Ke Sahara Hussain Aaya Hai.

3
Hazar Eidoon Se Barh Kar Yeh Din Hai Mere Liye.
Jahan Mein Mera Mula Hussain Aaya Hai.
Sada Yeh Deti Hai Karbal Haq Our Batil Ki.
Lakeer Khainchne Wala Hussain Aaya Hai.

4
Yazeed Tera Bhi Charcha Jahan Mein Ho Ga Yeh.
Karane Tujh Pe Tabara Hussain Aaya Hai.
 Jawad Keh Do Yeh Deen Se Keh Tum Na Fikar Karo.
Tumhare Gham Ka Mudawa Hussain Aaya Hai.
