1
Roti Hai Kyun Batool Meesam Zaidi Bhawana.
Roti Hai Kyun Batool Musalmaan Jawab De.
Ya Karbala Ki Sham-e-Ghareebaan Jawab De.
Kehte Hain Apne Baap Ki Waris Nahi Batool.
Aye Tajdar-e-Takht-e-Sulemaan Jawab De.
Loota Kafan Rasool Ka Ummat Ne Kis Tarha.
Sibt e Nabi Ka Lasha Hai Uryaan Jawab De.
Matam Ho Ghar Nabi Ke Tu Karta Hai Eid Kaun.
Aye Chodhwin Sadi Ke Musalmaan Jawab De.
Roti Rahi Nawasi Nabi Ki Kahan Kahan.
Pardah Karo Ke Sham Ka Zindaan Jawab De.
Kuch Loag Ghar Nabi Ka Jalanay Ko Aaein Hain.
Jaltay Hoye Khiyam-e-Shaheedan Jawab De.
Kofa Tere Bulanay Pe Aaye They Ahl-e-Bait.
Phir Kya Hoi Thi Hurmat-e-Mehmaan Jawab De.
Matam Ka Mangtay Ho Zaki Se Saboot Kya.
Thamo Jigar Ke Sahib e Doraan Jawab De.
