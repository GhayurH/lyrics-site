Lutt Gaye Alay Muhammad Kay Ghaaranay Walay.

Taarsay Paani Ko Bhi Kausar Kay Lutanay Walay

Hogayi Shaam Inhain Shaam Kay Bazaaraon Main

Aik Beemar Ko Laakhon Hain Sataanay Walay.

Paani Tujh Ko Na Milla Hay Meray Mazloom Hussain

Tashnagi Khanjar-e-Zalim Ki Bhujanay Walay.

Azm-e-Shabbir Ko Dekha Toh Pukar Uthay Khalil

Marhaba Lasha-e-Akbar Ko Uthanay Walay.

Ro Kay Kehti Thi Sakina Meray Aamu Hay Kahan

Laut Kaar Aaye Nahin Paani Pilaanay Walay.

Bolli Zainab Woh Giri Khaak Pay Tasveer-e-rasool

Chal Basay Yaad Bhi Nana Kay Dillanay Walay.

Hurr Ki Taqdeer Ko Aik Pal May Badal Daytay Hain

Aisay Hotay Hain Mohammed Kay Ghaaranay Walay.

Zahra Kehti Bakshwaa Lungi Unhain Mehshar Main

Jo Meray Laal Pai Aansoo Hain Bahanay Walay.
