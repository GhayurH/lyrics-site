1
Aaj Maidaan Mein Husain Ibn-e-Ali Ghaiz Mein Hain
Fatima Ghaiz Mein Hain Haq Ke Wali Ghaiz Mein Hain
Sath Shabir Ke Jitne Hain Sabhi Ghaiz Mein Hain
Aisa Lagta Hai Sawa Lakh Nabi Ghaiz Mein Hain
Apni Talwar Se Bazi Ye Palat De Ga Husain
Aaj Maidaan Ko Jahanum Mein Ulat De Ga Husain

2
Faisla Kun Mere Mula Ki Larai Ho Gi
Khon Mein Tar Abhi Darya Ke Tarai Ho Gi
Maut Ke Sar Pe Ghata Maut Ki Chayi Ho Gi
Aise Maidan Se Har Saf Ki Safai Ho Gi
Dein Gaye Kofe Mein Khabar Bhag Ke Jane Wale
Bhago Bhago Yahan Shabir Hai Aane Wale

3
Abhi Khul Jaye Ga Shabir Akele Kya Hain
Hashmi Shair Ke Aagay Ye Darinde Kya Hain
Samne Qamate Touheed Ke Bole Kya Hain
Sham Ke Sorma Apne Ko Samjhte Kya Hain
Ho Ga Iss Shan Se Hamla Asad-e-Talib Ka
Kalma Parh Parh Ke Marein Gaye Yeh Abu Talib Ka

4
Ho Gayi Jang Pe Aamadah Khuda Ki Qudrat
Ghaiz Ko De Ke Jagah Hat Gayi Piche Rehmat
Pyar Se Baat Ki Lekin Nahi Samjhi Umat
Cheh Mahine Se Bohat Shor Tha Bayat Bayat
Taig Kuch Dair Mein Jab Ibn-e-Ali Khole Ga
Koi Bayat Tu Kuja:Be: Bhi Nahi Bole Ga

5
Aastino Ko Ulate Hain Husain Ibn-e-Ali
Murtajeez Se Yahan Shamsheer Ali Ki Boli
Sath Dena Hai Tujhe Mera Ae Rahwar-e-Nabi
Aakhri Jang Hai Ye Qabl-e-Zahoor-e-Mehdi
Aaj Har Munkir-e-Mula Ko Jakarna Ho Ga
No Imamo Ki Taraf Se Mujhe Larna Ho Ga

6
Murtajeez Bola Mujhe Bhi Nahi Aata Hai Qarar
Sheh Ki Nusrat Ke Liye Main Hoon Azal Se Teyar
Dekh Chehre Pe Nomaya Hai Ghazab Ke Aasar
Jhurata Hoon Main Khak Apne Somo Se Har Bar
Shamiyo Ko Hai Batani Mujhe Had Bayat Ki
Paon Se Khod Raha Hoon Main Lahad Bayat Ki

7
Allah Allah Re Shabir Ki Aafat Ka Jalal
Taigein Kham Hoti Hain Halat Hoi Jati Hai Nidhal
Asar Se Pehle Howa Jata Hai Lashkar Ka Zawal
Shimar Kholi Umar-e-Saad Sabhi Be Haal
Khoof Se Aata Nahi Hai Bin-e-Kahil Ran Mein
Chupe Phirte Hain Alamdar Ke Qatil Ran Mein

8
Keh Ke Ya Zahra Jo Mula Ne Nikali Talwar
Dar Gaye Dekh Ke Kuch Loag Nirali Talwar
Kuch Ne Choron Ki Tarha Piche Chupa Li Talwar
Boli Ye Sham Ke Lashkar Se Jalali Talwar
Waar Se Apne Sabhi Fitne Karon Gi Tukre
Gin Na Paye Gi Qaza Itne Karon Gi Tukre

9
Afratafree Thi Wo Bhagdar Thi Wo Hulchul Tauba
Khon Se Aisi Bani Dasht Mein Daldal Tauba
Apne Halat Pe Khud Karta Tha Maqtal Tauba
Keh Raha Tha Malak-al-Maut Musalsal Tauba
Khud Ko Girte Howay Har Bar Sanbhala Oss Ne
Sar Bachate Howay Rohoon Ko Nikala Oss Ne

10
Dekh Kar Taig-e-Do Sar Nehar Se Sar Ke Zalim
Bhagte Phirte Hain Sab Lashkar-e-Shar Ke Zalim
Jaein Tu Jaein Kahan Shah Se Dar Ke Zalim
Ghat Ke Baqi Rahe Aur Na Ghar Ke Zalim
Ghar Mein Bhi Ghusne Na Aab Dein Gaye Inhein Ghar Wale
Kaf:Te::Ye: Se Badtar Howay Lashkar Walay

11
Dam Nikalta Tha Kissi Ka Koi Be Dam Dekha
Sakht Gharmi Mein Ajab Sardi Ka Aalam Dekha
Khon Jamte Howay Sharyano Mein Peham Dekha
Foaj Mein Jis Ko Uthaye Howay Parcham Dekha
Qatal-e-Abbas-e-Alamdar Ka Qazi Bankar
Sheh Ne Woh Hath Qalam Kar Diya Ghazi Bankar

12
Maut Bhi Raqs Mein Thi Kaisa Ajab Tha Manzar
Aik Anbar Tarai Pe Tha Lashon Ka Udhar
Ye Sama Dekhne Thehri Hoi Sab Ki Thi Nazar
Khoaf Se Nehar Mein Jo Doob Gaye The Ja Kar
Mojein Kehti Thi Unhein Maar Ke Thokar Niklo
Dushman-e-Hazrat-e-Shabir Ho Bahir Niklo

13
Dekh Kar Sheh Ka Ghazab Bole Farishte Peham
Hone Wali Hai Khudai Abhi Darham Barham
Bole Jibrail Na Ghabrao Farishto Iss Dam
Khaliq-e-Arz-o-Sama Hain Yehi Khaliq Ki Qasam
Kya Howa Aaj Khudai Jo Mita Dein Gaye Husain
Phir Se Dunya Nayi Kun Keh Ke Bana Dein Gay Husain

14
Nehar Bhi Kehti Thi Yeh Baat Yehi Sehra Bhi
Khof Ke Mare Howay Kehte Rahe Aada Bhi
Zinda Tu Zinda Sada Deta Tha Ye Murdah Bhi
Taig Bhi Haider-e-Karar Ki Hai Baita Bhi
Iss Se Bachne Ki Tamana Hai TU Lillah Parh Lo
Jitne Zinda Ho Ali-un-Wali Allah Parh Lo

15
Murtaza Ki Yeh Sada Atti Thi Shabash Husain
Maa Takallum Bhi Kahe Jati Thi Shabash Husain
Jad Ka Paigham Hawa Lati Thi Shabash Husain
Zaat-e-Wajib Bhi Yehi Farmati Thi Shabash Husain
Daad Dete Tere Ajdaad Bhi Aisa Larta
Main Badan Rakhta Tu Bilkul Tere Jaisa Larta
