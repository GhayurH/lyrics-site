Jun jun bazaar kareeb aaya

zainab jo ghazi yaad aaya

hathon se ya phir balon se chehre ko chupale shehzadi

ye sham shehr ki galiyan hai dil thaam le ay aaqazadi

fizza ne jab ye batlaya, zainab jo ghazi yaad aaya

jun jun bazaar….

mahol sharabi jab dekha baba ki wasiyat yaad aayi

koi to mujhe chadar laa de honton pe yehi faryad aayi

jab koi nahi chadar laaya, zainab jo ghazi yaad aaya

jun jun bazaar….

phir sham ke logon ne badla ajdad ka yun zainab se liya

chadar cheeni pathar maare aur garm kabhi pani phaynka

durra jo sakina ne khaya, zainab jo ghazi yaad aaya

jun jun bazaar….

abbas kahan hai ay zainab jab shimr hikharat se bola

ghash kahan lagay sab ahle haram kulsoom pukari wavaila

sajjad ko yasrab yaad aaya, zainab jo ghazi yaad aaya

jun jun bazaar….

khamosh rahi aulad e nabi karbal se yahan tak zulm saha

khooni ye manadi kehne laga ye kunba hai ek baaghi ka

mazloom jo baaghi kehlaya, zainab jo ghazi yaad aaya

jun jun bazaar….

har gaam naya ek zulm hua lachaar musafir zainab par

zainab ko rulaaya zaalim ne naaqon ke kareeb naiza laakar

shabbir ka sar jo dikhlaya, zainab jo ghazi yaad aaya

jun jun bazaar….

farhan qayamat ka gham hai mazhar ne likha hai kya nauha

shabbir bina abbas bina ye pehla safar tha zainab ka

jun jun bazaar….
