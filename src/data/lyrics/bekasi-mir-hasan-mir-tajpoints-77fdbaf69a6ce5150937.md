1
Bekasi Ka Sheh Ki Charcha Reh Gaya.
Mujrai Mehman Pyasa Reh Gaya.
Sab Hoye Serab Tujh Se Aye Furat.
Qafila Yasrab Ka Pyasa Reh Gaya.

2
Zohar Tak Sab Foaj Pohanchi Khuld Mein.
Sahib-e-Lashkar Akela Reh Gaya.
Iss Qadar Tha Khushk Hazrat Ka Galla.
Khanjar-e-Qatil Bhi Pyasa Reh Gaya.

3
Dagmaga Kar Jab Girey Ghorey Se Shah.
Kanp Kar Arsh-e-Mualla Reh Gaya.
Kehti Thi Maa Soye Asghar Qabar Mein.
Haye Khali Unn Ka Jhola Reh Gaya.

4
Zakhm Khatay Hi Jo Akbar Gir Paray.
Chid Ke Barchi Mein Kaleja Reh Gaya.
Jab Hoi Be Pardah Aoulad-e-Rasool.
Phir Jahan Mein Kis Ka Pardah Reh Gaya.
