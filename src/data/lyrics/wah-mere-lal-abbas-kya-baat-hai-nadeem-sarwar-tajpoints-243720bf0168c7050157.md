1
Wah Mere Lal Abbas Kya Baat Hai Nadeem Sarwar.
Ghazi Abbas Ko De Ke Dein Ka Alam.
Bolay Sibt-e-Nabi Mera Ik Kam Kar.
Mere Bazu Alamdar-e-Shah-e-Wafa.

2
Aye Ghani-o-Sakhi Mera Ik Kam Kar.
Aye Khuda-e-Wafa Syeda Ki Dua.
Juraton Ke Wali Mera Ik Kam Kar.
Aye Sab Ke Wali Aye Meri Zindagi.

3
Misal-e-Nad-e-Ali Mera Ik Kam Kar.
Chahta Hai Yeh Shabeer Bhai Mere.
Aaj Sab Tera Jah-o-Hasham Dekh Lein.
Iss Tarha Shamiyon Se Tu Kar Guftgu.

4
Lehja-e-Haidery Ka Bharam Dekh Lein.
Ab Yeh Pardah Utha Unn Ko Ja Keh Bata.
Azm-e-Shabeer Azm-e-Hussaini Hai Kya.
Hum Mohafiz Hain Quran-o-Islam Ke.

5
Kis Liye Aaye Hain Bar Sar-e-Karbala.
Bool Aisay Keh Khaliq Ko Kehna Paray.
Zaat-e-Haider Ke Akas Kiya Baat Hai.
Aaj Nehj-e-Balaga Ka Khaliq Kahey.

6
Wah Mere Lal Abbas Kiya Baat Hai.
Baat Aaqa Ki Ghazi Ne Jis Dam Suni.
Dafatan Shair Ne Aik Angrayi Li.
Sham Ki Fojon Mein Man Gayi Khalbali.

7
Loag Kehne Lagay Aa Gaye Kiya Ali.
Our Roh-e-Ali Muskuraney Lagi.
Bolay Abbas Yeh Lashkar-e-Sham Se.
Woh Jo Waqif Nahi Hai Mere Naam Se.

8
Ba Khabar Woh Nahi Apne Anjam Se.
Munsalik Main Bhi Hoon Khum Ke Paigham Se.
Youn Nawaza Mujhe Sheh Ne Inam Se.
Main Bataon Tumhein Kaun Shabeer Hai.

9
Aks-e-Islam Quraan Ki Tafseer Hai.
Khuld Bag-e-Iram Osski Jageer Hai.
Osske Qadmo Talay Hur Ki Taqdeer Hai.
Mere Barey Mein Oss Ki Yeh Taqreer Hai.

10
Yeh Ghulami-e-Sheh Ka Sharf Hai Mila.
Malka-e-Khuld Ne Mujh Ko Beta Kaha.
Naam Abbas Zainab Ne Mera Rakha.
Jhola Shabeer Ne Hai Jholaya Mera.

11
Phir Hasan Ne Galay Se Laga Kar Kaha.
Pyas Bachoon Ki Layi Yahan Tak Mujhe.
Mashk Ik Bhar Ke Le Jaon Ga Nehar Se.
Mere Aaqa Ne Roka Larai Se Hai.

12
Warna Rokay Mujhe Hosla Hai Kisay.
Main Hoon Betab Sun Lo Yehi Ik Sada.
Jab Mukamal Howa Zindagi Ka Safar.
Bola Shabeer Se Bawafa Nehar Par.

13
Apne Pairon Pe Rakh Lijiye Mera Sar.
Khush Hoon Umm Ul-Banin Jab Sunein Yeh Khabar.
Mere Betay Se Shabeer Ne Keh Diya.
Haye:REHAN-o-SARWAR: Ajab Waqt Tha.

14
Ho Ke Be Dast Jab Mera Ghazi Gira.
Bawafa Ka Alam Khoon Mein Tar Ho Gaya.
Roh-e-Zahra Pukari Sar-e-Karbala.
Tu Ne Saqayi Ka Haq Ada Kar Diya.
