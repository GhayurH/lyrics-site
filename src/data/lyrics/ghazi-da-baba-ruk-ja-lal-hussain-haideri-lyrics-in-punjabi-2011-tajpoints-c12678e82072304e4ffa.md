Ghazi Da Baba Ruk Ja Lal Hussain Haideri.
Ghazi Da Baba Ruk Ja Haye Ronday Tere Baal Aye.

1
Ik Wari Yateema Nu La Maula Gal De Naal Aye.
Tak Tere Janazay Nu Har Bibi Payi Roway.
Shala Na Piyo Da Saya Dhiyaan Taun Juda Howay.
Kulsoom Te Zainab Da Rab Janrey Kehra Haal Aye.

2
Dhiyaan De Sar Te Piyo V Honday Ne Thandiyaan Chawaan.
Puchdi Aye Kiwein Hundiyan Aye Sham Diyaan Rahwaan.
Jis Qaid Nibhani Aye Wich Sham Pora Saal Aye.
Aa Gayi Aye Janazay Te Baqiya Taun Maindi Sain Aye.

3
Tak Tera Kafan Maula Kardi Aye Payi Wain Aye.
Mohsin Di Dukhi Maa De Khul Gaye Ne Sar De Waal Aye.
Janat De Jawana De Sardar Ronday Paye Ne.
Haye Mohar-e-Nabowat De Aswar Jehray Ray Ne.

4
Ronday Ne Farishtay V Rul Gayi Nabi Di Aal Aye.
Main Maa Haan Ruqaiya Di Khaton Di Haan Mani.
Sartaj Ayho Karday Ujari Te Meharbani.
Das Kehrey Pasay Jawaan Bint-e-Asad Da Lal Aye.
