1
Maqtal e Imam Hussain Syed Askari Hassan Zaidi.
Shimar Ka Khanjar Rawaan Hai Khalq-e-Sarwar Par Abhi.
Karbala Ki Rait Sheh Ke Khoon Se Ho Gi Tar Abhi.
Shimar Baloon Se Hata Le Hath Gustakhi Na Kar.
Iss Taraf Hi Dekhti Hai Shah Ki Dukhtar Abhi.
Terah Zarbain Kund Khanjar Our Sokha Tha Gala.
Jism-e-Athar Se Juda Jaisay Howa Hai Sar Abhi.
Shimar Khanjar Our Sar Khaimon Mein Le Kar Aa Gaya.
Chal Raha Hai Bibiyon Ke Qalb Pe Khanjar Abhi.
Kund Khanjar Chal Chuka Ghorey Bhi Doraye Gaye.
Zalimo Ne Hath Mein Pakray Hain Kuch Pathar Abhi.
Asar-e-Ashora Hai Sham-e-Gham Hai Kofa Sham Hai.
Aal-e-Ahmad Ko Phiraya Jaye Ga Dar Dar Abhi.
Qabar Se Lipati Hoi Kehti Hai Maa Zindan Mein.
Uth Sakina Hum Reha Ho Kar Chalein Gay Ghar Abhi.
Sham Ke Bazar Mein Sajjad Khoon Roney Lagay.
Chashm-e-Maula Mein Nibha Hai Sham Ka Manzar Abhi.
Kat Gaye Abbas Ke Bazu Ridaein Lut Gayin.
Chal Raha Hai Halq-e-Abid Par Wohi Khanjar Abhi.
Hain Lahu Mein Hath Tar Chalay Hain Hathoon Par Kayi.
Bhai Ke Lashay Se Chun Kar Aayi Hai Pathar Abhi.
Gash Mein Hain Sajjad Zainab Our Sakina Aik Sath.
Ik Chari Mari Gayi Thi Sheh Ke Honton Par Abhi.
Maa Jali Jholay Ki Lakri Chomti Hai Bar Bar.
Surkh Kapron Mein Dikhey Hain Khuwab Mein Asghar Abhi.
Aaj Bhi Lagta Hai Haider Karbala Mein Fatima.
Chun Rahi Hain Lasha-e-Shabeer Se Pathar Abhi.
