1
Lashay Pe Jawaan Betay Ke Shabbir Pukaray, Uth Jao Aye Pyaray.
Zindah Tera Baba Tha Faqat Tere Saharay, Uth Jao Aye Pyaray.
Abbas Ke Jane Se Kamar Toot Gayi Thi.
Tum Sath Liye Jate Ho Binayi Hamari.

2
Agyar Sabhi Hanstay Hain Baba Pe Tumharay, Uth Jao Aye Pyaray.
Kehte Hain Sitamgar Keh Shabbir Hai Tanha.
Bhai Bhi Nahi Pas Nahi Hai Koi Beta.
Our Hoti Hain Ab Batein Mere Qatal Ke Baray, Uth Jao Aye Pyaray.

3
Sughra Ka Jo Khat Aaya Hai Yeh Baat Hai Likhi.
Main Sehra Liye Aaon Gi Wadah Raha Bhai.
Hasrat Hai Behan Ki Woh Teri Zulfein Sanwaray, Uth Jao Aye Pyaray.
Kuch Dair Mein Khaimon Pe Kiya Jaye Ga Hamla.

4
Phir Kaun Bachaye Ga Phophi Amma Ka Pardah.
Marey Gaye Abbas Bhi Darya Ke Kinaray, Uth Jao Aye Pyaray.
Laila Yeh Sada Deti Hain Hum Shakal-e-Payamber.
Jane Na Nazar Kis Ki Lagi Hai Tumhein Dilbar.

5
Khaimay Mein Chaley Aao Ke Maa Sadqay Utaray, Uth Jao Aye Pyaray.
Maqtal Mein Yeh Manzar Tha Qayamat Se Bhi Barh Kar.
Yeh Kehte Hoye Maula Ko Gash Aa Gaya Qambar.
Ik Bar Tu Lag Jao Galay Aa Ke Hamaray, Uth Jao Aye Pyaray.
