1
Okha Ho Gaya Akbar Layi Sughra Nu Samjhaona.
Toon Mantaan Mandiyaan Reh Jana Akbar Ne Mur Nayi Aaouna.
Aynj Band Howan Gay Aye Jandray Fair Khulney Nayi.
Aye Ghar Aye Wehray Howan Gay Par Wasi Koi Nayi Hona.

2
Kiwein Sughra Nu Kehway Bhejein Na Mehndi Karbal Wich.
Barchi Nal Mehndiyaan Laway Ga Tera Akbar Jag Taun Sohna.
Naal Babay De Bitha Veraan Nu Wekh Le Raj Ke.
Ik Wari Jay Aye Guzar Gaya Aye Wela Fair Nayi Aaouna.

3
Khak Te Qadma Di Daswein Nu Tu V So Jawein.
Karbal Wich Sham-e-Ghareebaan Nu Asaan Sariyaan Khak Te Sona.
Turday Mehmal Akbar Maiyyataan De Waang Lagday Ne.
Takdi Rayi Khuliyan Akhiyaan Nal Aye Sara Khab Darona.
