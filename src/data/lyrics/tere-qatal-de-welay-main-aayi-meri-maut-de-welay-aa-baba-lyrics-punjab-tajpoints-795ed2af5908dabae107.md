1
Tere Qatal De Welay Main Aayi Meri Maut De Welay Aa Baba.
Tainu Lagdiyaan Zarbaan Main, Zarbaan Main Wekhiyaan Ne Mere Wekh Tu Aakhri Sah Baba.
Karbal Tu Le Ke Sham Taein Mainu Ban Ke Safar Karaya.
Ik Pair Di Rasi Khol Aina Mainu Khich Ke Aount Taun Laya.
Dig Payi Saan Main Oss Waylay, Oss Welay Nayi Bulaya Mainu Aount Taun Aan Ke La Baba.
Mainu Phar Ke Walaan Taun Baba Haye Shimar Tamachay Marey.
Mainu Rondiyaan Wekh Ke Hasday Ray Baba Kalma Go Sare.
Kita Sabar Main Oss Waylay, Oss Welay Nayi Kehya Main Mere Shimar Taun Waal Chura Baba.
Tere Seene Wich Jadon Teer Lagay Fair Baba Neend Nayi Aayi.
Aouna Teraan Di Aj Tak Baba Teri Dhi Ne Peer Handayi.
Akh La Ke Main Nayi Wekhi, Nayi Wekhi Tere Bajoon Mainu Aakhri Waar Sawa Baba.
Tere Zakhmi Chehray Naal Mere Chehray De Zakhm Ne Ralday.
Kadi Piyo Dunya Te Putraan Di Nayi Rat Ainj Munh Te Malday.
Zindan Di Nayi Mukni, Nayi Mukni Qaid Meri Sahwaan Di Qaid Muka Baba.
Jehri Qaid Nibhai Main Baba Tainu Aakhri Waar Wakhawaan.
Tere Sir Nu Main Aj Gal La Ke Zindan De Wich Mar Jawaan.
Choon Salaan Di Dhi Najfi, Najfi Payi Aakhdi Aye Mainu Apne Kool Bola Baba.
