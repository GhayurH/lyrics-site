Main Mimber e Rasool Hoon Kya Dekhta Raha.
Main Fatima Pe Hoti Jafa Dekhta Raha.

1
Main Gasibon Ke Darmiyaan Mahool Dekh Kar.
Khatray Mein Fatima Ki Rida Dekhta Raha.
Gustakhiyon Se Boltay They Fatima Se Woh.
Sun Kar Ali Ko Rota Howa Dekhta Raha.

2
Main Samne Nabi Ke Jo Bantay They Bawafa.
Woh Loag Kaisay Bholay Wafa Dekhta Raha.
Bikhri Sand Tu Jaun Main Izhar-e-Dard Mein.
Chehray Pe Fatima Ki Qaza Dekhta Raha.
