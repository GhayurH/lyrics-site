1
Sangaan De Utay Behna Sang Ray Zainab De Bhira.
Aye Aakh Ke Lashaan Choor Ke Tur Pay Safraan Te Oh Sham Da Aye Rah Zainab Na Kali Ja.
Tere Safraan Che Jadon Aisi Manzil Ayi Dorde Naqey Uton Kidhre sakina Dig Payi.
Tenu Sang Toon Khabraan De Ga Veer Hussain Tera Wadah Aye Veer Da Roke Ga Kafila.

2
Teri Chadar Te Odhay Deen Da Kaba Aye Taiyon Sir Naizay Uton Ghazi Da Dig Painda Aye.
Kinj Away Sain Barabar Teri Chadar De Umm-ul-Baneen Da Bachra Aye Bawafa.
Ohh Madina Si Tu Jad Bar Ghar Tu Aandi Rahwaan Chad Do Loko Karda Manadi Ghazi.
Aye Sham Aye Aythe Sir Shabir Da Naizey Tu Aytaan Sunaye Ga Rasta Banaye Ga.

3
Teriyaan Banwaan Jiwein Shamiyaan Ne Bina Par Kina De Wicho Odhey Proiyan Rasiyaan.
Sir Naizey Te Na Rukiya Te Aynj Shamiyaan Ne Tere Shair Veer Da Sir Qaidi Kar Liya.
Pehre Takda Si Odhay Pehre Dewan Wala Riya Khutbe Sunda Khutbe Sunawan Wala.
Aynj Wekhi Akbar Behan Di Sham Bhirawan Ne Wekhi Aye Jis Tarha Zainab Ne Karbala.
