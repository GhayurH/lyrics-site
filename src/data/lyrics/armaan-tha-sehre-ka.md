Jis chaand se chehre pe armaan tha sehre ka
Hai saamne Laila ke ab us ka jawaan laasha

1
Maa ban ke jise paala Zainab ne hijaabon mein
Naushah bana dekha maa ne jise khwaabon mein
Woh laal jo ghar bhar ki aankhon ka ujaala tha

2
Dil Akbar-e-mehru ka sine mein nahi baaqi
Uljha hua barchhi mein dil bhi hai kaleja bhi
Jis sine mein dil maa ka behnon ka dharakta tha

3
Kaandha bhi koi dene aaya na janaaze ko
Bas baap tha bete ki mayyat ke uthaane ko
Ghurbat mein jise tanha Sarwar ne uthaaya tha

4
Sine ka lahu roka Zainab ne jo haathon se
Kehti thi koi ja ke Abbas ko batla de
Maara gaya barchhi se athaarah baras waala

5
Din byaah ke aaye to Akbar ko qaza aayi
Ab laut ke Sughra na aayega tera bhai
Ghar laut ke aane ka jis ne tha kiya waada

6
Ab tak hai Samar girya maqtal ki azaanon mein
Lagta hai kisi maa ki faryaad hai raahon mein
Ab tak yehi karti hai maqtal ki fiza noha
