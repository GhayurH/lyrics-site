1
Minal Hussain Ki Tafseer Hain Ali Akbar.
Nabi Ke Husan Ki Tasveer Hain Ali Akbar.
Khuda Ke Dein Ki Zeenat Hussain Ibn-e-Ali.
Muqam-e-Zeenat-e-Shabir Hain Ali Akbar.
Tawaf Azmatein Karti Hain Un Ka Sham-o-Sahar.
Wojod-e-Aya-e-Tatheer Hain Ali Akbar.
Qarar Syed-e-Sajjad Ki Nigahon Ke.
Sakoon-e-Zainab-e-Dilgeer Hain Ali Akbar.
Wo Jis Ki Taab Nahi La Sakte The Mosa Bhi.
Oss Aik Noor Ki Tanveer Hain Ali Akbar.
Ho Tujh Pe Nazaan Na Kyun Rifatein Zamane Ki.
Aye Karbala Teri Taqdeer Hain Ali Akbar.
Ho Iss Se Barh Ke Karam Our Mujh Pe Kya Sahil.
Khush Naseeb Mere Peer Hain Ali Akbar.
Manqabat::Shehzada-e-Ali Akbar:
