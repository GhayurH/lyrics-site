1
Woh Ghar Ke Hukam Jahan Fatima Ke Chaltay Hain.
Tamam Kam Wahin Se Khuda Ke Chaltay Hain.
Yeh Kaisi Rahguzar Hai Yeh Koan Sa Dar Hai.
Keh Mustafa Bhi Yahan Sar Jhuka Ke Chaltay Hain.

2
Jabeen Pe Fakhar Se Romal-e-Syeda Bandhe.
Amamay Walon Mein Hur Muskura Ke Chaltay Hain.
Keh Jaise Hath Mein Al-Maida Ka Sorah Ho.
Malak Youn Rotiyaan Teri Utha Ke Chaltay Hain.

3
Bana Na Le Koi Dunya Utha Ke Woh Mati.
Youn Naqsh-w-Pa Tere Haider Chupa Ke Chaltay Hain.
Aye Hur Na Ghabra Ye Karb-o-Bala Ki Basti Hai.
Yahan Pe Faislay Aal-e-Aba Ke Chaltay Hain.

4
Yeh Tere Faqoon Ka Sadqa Hai Aye Hussain Ki Maa.
Jo Ghar Sakoon Se Ahl-e-Azza Ke Chaltay Hain.
Kaha Ajal Se Takallum Ne Intezar Karo.
Sanay-e-Fatima Zahra Suna Ke Chaltay Hain.
