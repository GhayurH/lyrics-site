Zulm-o-Sitam Ki Intaha Hai
Yeh Karbala Hai, Yeh Karbala Hai

1
Kahi Phay Sur Hai Kahi Rida Hai

2
Ghazi Nahi Hain Akbar Nahi Hai,
Qasim Nahi Hain Asghar Nahi Hai
Aur Shah Ka Lasha Tanha Para Hai

3
Kerkay Dafan Masoom Asghar,
Shabbir Roye Nani Qabar Par
Asghar Ke Khun Se Chehra Bhara Hai

4
Dhono Jahan Mutthi Mei Jiskay,
Maqtal Mei Pyasa Shabbir Hain Woh
Hal Min Ki Tanha Deyta Sada Hai

5
Maqtal Mai Hain Joh Fakhray Yousuf,
Hum Shaqal Ahmad Hai Naam Akbar
Seenei Mei Uskay Tooti Sina Hai

6
Islam Ki Khatir Luta Hai,
Johar Mohammed Ka Hain Ghirana
Ab Sham Lut Nei Ghar Yeh Chala Hai
