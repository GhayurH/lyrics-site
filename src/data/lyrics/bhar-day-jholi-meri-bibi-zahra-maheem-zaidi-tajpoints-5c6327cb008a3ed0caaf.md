1
Bhar Day Jholi Meri Bibi Zahra Maheem Zaidi.
ALLAH Ke Rasool Ki Dukhtar Hain Fatima.
Hasnain-o-Zainabain Ki Madar Hain Fatima.
Mushkil Kusha Hain Haider-e-Safdar Jahan Ke.

2
Mushkil Kusha-e-Haider-e-Safdar Hain Fatima.
Bhar De Jholi Meri Bibi Zahra, Laut Kar Main Na Jaon Gi Khali.
Apne Bachoon Ka Sadqa Atta Kar, Dar Pe Aayi Hoon Ban Kar Sawali.
Fatima Hai Hijab-e-Khuda Hai, Tu Batool-e-Zaman Syeda Hai.

3
Umme Kulsoom-o-Zainab Ki Maa Hai, Raziya, Marziya, Tahira Hai.
Misal-e-Fizza Kaneezi Mein Le Le, Tu Hi Zamin Hai Meri Rida Ki.
Wasta Hai Tujhe Mustafa Ka, Noor-e-Hasnain Ka Murtaza Ka.
Be Kasoon Muflison Ghamzadon Par, Shahzadi Karam Ho Khudara.

4
Hum Tu Kya Anbia, Oliya Bhi, Tere Dar Se Na Lautay Hain Khali.
Qarz Daroon Ke Qarzey Ada Hoon, Jo Hain Qaid-e-Sitam Mein Reha Hoon.
Jin Ki Aagosh Veraan Hai Bibi Matami Betay Unn Ko Atta Hoon.
Hoon Shifayaab Beemar Momin, Tujh Se Hai Iltija Yeh Hamari.

5
Wasta Apni Shadi Ka Tujh Ko, Betiyon Ke Muqadar Jaga De.
Naik Hum Sar Atta Kar Ke Bibi Sab Ke Aabad Ghar Tu Kara De.
Hain Pareshaan Jo Maa Baap Unn Ki, Jald Kar De Tu Mushkilkushai.
Ishq-e-Aal-e-Abba Ki Jaza Dey, Dard-e-Dil Ki Humein Tu Dawa De.

6
Gaib Se Kar Ke Imdad Bibi Karbala Ki Ziyarat Kara De.
Tere Hasnain Ke Matami Hain, Tere Ghazi Ke Hum Hain Fidai.
Barh Rahe Hain Waba Se Masail, Kam Hoye Ja Rahe Hain Wasail.
Teri Chakki Hai Raziq Hamari, Hum Hain Bibi Tere Dar Ke Sail.

7
Bhar De Kashkool-e-Hajat Hamara, Tujh Se Faryad Hai Shahzadi.
Bas Iram Our Maheem Ki Hai, Iltija Ik Yehi Bibi Zahra.
Tere Aada Se Badlay Ki Khatir, Ho Zahoor Ab Imam-e-Zamaan Ka.
Tu Hai Razi Tu Razi Khuda Hai, Teri Marzi Hai Marzi Khuda Ki.
