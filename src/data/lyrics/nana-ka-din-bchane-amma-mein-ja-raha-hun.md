Nana ka deen bachane amma mai ja raha hu
Sukha gala katane amma mai ja raha hu

1
Zaalim watan hamara humse chuda raha hai
Deen e muhammedi ko apna bata raha hai
Qabe ki manzilat ko bejaan ghata raha hai
Baatil ka sar jhukane

2
Islam ke liye mai zulm o sitam sahunga
Ghar baar sab lutakar uff tak nahi karunga
Naize ki nol se bhi quraan mai padhunga
Har sabr aazmane

3
Zainab bhi sathme hai bacchon ko apne lekar
Mere bagair ik pal jeena bhi uska dubhar
Baade dahum phiregi bazar me khule sar
Kunba tera lutane

4
Lakhte jigar hai sughra mujhko aziz tar hai
Tasveer apki hai ye bhi mujhe khabar hai
Dekhunga kistarha se vo bhi barehna sar
Sughra ko khun rulake

5
Tere jigar ke tukde zeenat banenge rann ki
Pamal lash hogi qasim bin e hasan ki
Garden me teer hoga asghar se gulbadan ki
Laasho se rann sajane

6
Jis waqt laashe akber mujhse nahi uthegi
Imdad ko hamari dukhtar teri badhegi
Us waqt meri ghairat khudse kya kahegi
Laash e pisar uthane

7
Amma tumhara dilber zakhmo se chur hoga
Akber bhi door hoga ghazi bhi foor hoga
Phir bhi mei jabin per himmat ka noor hoga
Haan khoon me nahane

8
Amma hamare ghum me rota rahega aalam
Ashk e aza banenge zakhmo pe mere marham
Utthega ta qayamat mere akhi ka parcham
Shiyon ko bakshwane

9
Likhe Azeem kaise shahe zaman ki rukhsat
Zehra ne ladle ko kis tarha di ijazat
Sheh ka ye bain sunkar hilti thi maa ki turbat
Wapas kabhi na aane...amma mai ja raha hu
