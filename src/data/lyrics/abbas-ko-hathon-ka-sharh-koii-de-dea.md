1
(Ik khawaab main dekha kisi aalim nay yeh manzar
Kehta hai koyi karz mera zikhare hai tum paar
Mazloom-e-Abbas bayaan kyu nahin kartay
Tum loug kabhi meray masayib nahin paartay
Saar choor tah, bay wast ta, jab teer jabbi paar
Bin haato kay Abbas, giray kaisay zameen paar
Mushkil main hai Abbas
Museebat main hai Abbas)

2
Is dhubti khashti ko kinara koyi dayde
Abbas ko haatho ka sahara koyi dayde

3
1) Jab nehar say paani liye wapas howa Ghazi
Bhaghi howi woh fojhay adoor lout ka aayi
Baarish howi pyaasay peh wahan teer situm ki
Baazu bhi katay, mashk chedhi baig gaya paani

4
2) Sabir hai larayi kahan izmay milla hay
Dil toot gaya mashk peh jab teer laga hay
Ghazi ka laaho maskh Sakina say bahaa hay
Ghoray peh yeh lehrata yeh dekh raha hay

5
3) Bay ban ta daar kistara ghir tah zameen paar
Bay dosh ka saar kistara ghir tah hai zameen paar
Bay shark shajjar kistara ghir tah hai zameen paar
Bay datay bashar kistara ghir tah hai zameen paar

6
4) Ya Jafar-e-Tayaar madad kay liye aawo
Ya Syed-e-Abraar madad kay liye aawo
Ya Haider-e-Karar madad kay liye aawo
Aey sab kay madad ghaar madad kay liye aawo

7
5) Lehrata hai ghoray peh woh naam-e-shah-e-dilgeer
Sahi ki tara jism howa itnay lagay teer
Ghirta hai magar ghir nay main hojati hai tahkeer
Dekhay koyi mazloom-e-Abbas ki tasveer

8
6) Hay teer meri aankh main mushkil ki ghaari hay
Zakhmi hai badan mera ajaal paas khaari hay
Paani nahin pounchaya wasiyaat yeh kaari hay
Is waqt-e-Sakina say humay sharam baari hay

9
7) Jo saath nibhatay tay madad kay liye aaye
Jo naaz uthatay tay madad kay liye aaye
Jo mujhko sulatay tay madad kay liye aaye
Jhoola jo jhulatay tay madad kay liye aaye

10
8) Dushman meray akah kay yahan saray laheen hay
Kuch dair kay mehman hai hum khaakh nasheen hay
Jo haath giray khat kay tarap tay woh waheen hay
May kaisay dua maangu meray haath nahin hay

11
9) Saar say bahaa khoon tah dariya hai zameen taak
Aur bin haathon kay Abbas ko aanay hai zameen tak
Aur Zara ka Qamar kis tarah pouncha hai zameen tak
Hay pusht-e-faras fasila kitna hai zameen tak
