Kab Tak Tu Unchi Awaz Mein Bolega.
Teri Khatir Koan Dareecha Kholega.

1
Apne Aanso Apni Aankh Mein Rehne De.
Rait Pe Kab Tak Heeray Moti Rolay Ga.
Aao Shehar Ki Roshniyaan Hi Dekh Aaein.
Koan Hamari Khali Jaib Tatole Ga.

2
Lakh Mere Honton Par Chup Ki Mohrein Hoon.
Mere Andar Ka Fankar Tu Bole Ga.
Dekh Woh Apni Meeti Meethi Baton Se.
Apna Sara Zehar Tujhe Mein Gholay Ga.

3
Aye Sodagar Chahat Ki Jageeron Ke.
Kis Meezan Mein Tu Iss Jins Ko Tolay Ga.
 Mohsin Oss Ki Naram Tabiyat Kehti Hai.
Pal Do Pal Woh Mere Sath Bhi Holay Ga.
