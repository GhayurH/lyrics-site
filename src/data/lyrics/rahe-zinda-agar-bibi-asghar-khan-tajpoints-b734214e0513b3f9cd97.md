Rahe Zinda Agar Bibi Asghar Khan Lyrics
Haye Lut Gayi Teri Rida Tu Bach Gaya Hai LaIlaha.
Aye Dukhtar-e-Shair-e-Khuda Aaein Gay Hum Wadah Raha.
Rahe Zindah Agar Bibi Tere Rozay Pe Aaein Gay.
Dunya Ke Sitamgar Yeh Humein Rook Na Paein Gay.
Beti Hai Muhammad Ki Tumhein Jis Se Adawat Hai.
Kya Iss Se Bari Logo Tauheen-e-Risalat Hai.
Kehte Hain Musalmaan Yeh Tera Roza Giraein Gay.
Khud Pak Muhammad Ne Rakha Tha Naam Jis Ka.
Aye Khon-e-Abutalib, Zainab Tha Naam Iss Ka.
Kam Zarf Bhala Kaisay Teri Shan Ghataein Gay.
Aal-e-Nabi Ki Logo Yeh Jan Amanat Hai.
Unn Ki Duaon Se Hi Sansoon Ki Zamanat Hai.
Aal-e-Nabi Pe Apni Hum Jan Lutaein Gay.
Hurmat Teri Ka Dushman Jo Samne Aaye Ga.
Hai Hum Ko Yaqeen Zalim Woh Bach Ke Na Jaye Ga.
Mukhtar Ban Ke Bibi Tere Badlay Chokaein Gay.
Hum Aa Ke Tujhey Dein Gay Tere Bhai Ka Youn Pursa.
Aa Kar Karein Gay Matam Har Saal Ki Hum Tarha.
Dushman Hussainiyat Ke Kya Hum Ko Daraein Gay.
Pursa Qabool Kar Lo Asghar Ne Jo Diya Hai.
Noha Parhon Main Aa Kar Sajjad Ki Dua Hai.
Hai Aas Mujhe Bibi Kabhi Dar Pe Bulaein Gay.
