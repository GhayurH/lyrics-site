1
Behno Ka Gham Jo Khas Asasa Raza Ka Hai.
Ummat Ne De Diya Hai Zehar Bolay Mustafa.
Woh Mil Gaya Hai Oss Ko Jo Wirsa Raza Ka Hai.
Baghdad, Halla, Samrah, Qum Keh Rahe Hain Yeh.
Bikhra Howa Jahan Mein Kunba Raza Ka Hai.
Jee Bhar Ke Pani Peetey Nahi Karbala Ke Baad Hai.
Keh Khanwada Aaj Bhi Pyasa Raza Ka Hai.
Jaisay Nisar, Mohsin-o-Hasnain Ka Hai Gham.
Dil Mein Nabi Ke Aisay Hi Sadma Raza Ka Hai.
