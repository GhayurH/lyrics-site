1
Mere Veer Di Mayyat Te Teeran Diyan Chawaan.
Kiwein Kool Tere Nana Aidi Qabar Banawaan.
Dita Zehar Laeena Ne Hoya Tukray Jigar Shah Da.
Kar Wainr Kiya Farwa Ghar Ujriya Zahra Da.
Qasim Diyaan Phophiyaan Nu Kiwein Khabar Sunawaan.
Laya Taj Mere Sir Taun Be Reham Musalmana.
Ronda Aye Mera Qasim Aa Wekh Zara Nana.
Chad Roza Tera Nana Keray Dais Main Jawaan.
Chuk Pak Janazay Nu Shabeer Turay Ghar Taun.
Marey Teer Musalmana Haye Roza-e-Sarwar Taun.
Kiwein Veer Da Tan Athar Teraan Taun Banawaan.
Aj Nana Teri Ummat Haye Zulm Te Tul Gayi Aye.
Sazish Aye Saqeefay Di Aa Wekh Le Khul Gayi Aye.
Rakh Teer Kamana Te Mal Baithay Ne Rawaan.
Jinay Pehla Janazay Te Haye Teer Chalaya Aye.
Nisbat Ne Teri Nana Oda Shan Wadhaya Aye.
Aynj Zulm Kaday Nana Nayi Kardiyaan Manwaan.
Kulsoom Te Zainab De Nayi Wainr Sunay Janday.
Ro Kehn Janazay Te Aynj Ghar Nayi Kaday Aanday.
Mar Janr Shala Bhainraan Kar Veer Duawaan.
 Sardar Taun Zahra De Bachiyaan Da Sana Khuwaan Aye.
Jinna Deen-e-Muhammad Taun Sir Kitay Ne Qurbaan Aye.
Tareekh De Lafzaan Choon Main Kiwein Chupawan.
