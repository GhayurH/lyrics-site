Phans gaye paer rakaban vich Abbas bhira hun away haa
Maindi bhaini dar tu daindiyan hin aa zeen to app lahaway haa

1
1) Mianda akhri vaila hay veeran maikon nana Karbal ghinan aya
Mainda zor-e-kamar ghazi veeran maiko piyo di shakal dikhaway ha

2
2) Pain daindi rah tairi Ghazi mairi dhi masoom Sakina ay
Rakh bawan apniyan mashak utay aiko vanj ke te dhikhlaway ha

3
3) Taikon wasta oh mazloom da ay jihndi lash te ghoray bhajday rahay
Imam-e-zamana jhal pursay paya nazre karam di paway haa

4
4) Hin phuphyan bahna nal mairay paya mulk paraya dushman da
Akbar ve chor ke tur gaya ay hun tu tay wapis away haa

5
5) Shahbaz Qalandar paer jihday phans gaye howan rakaban vich
Oh bhajday ghoray to kiwain latha hosi ay ta dasaway haa
