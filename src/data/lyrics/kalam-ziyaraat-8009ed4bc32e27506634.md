  AEY FATIMA KAY LAAL TUJHE AAKHRI SALAM(2x)

  1
  Kaisi udasiyan hai ye gurbat ka waqt hai,
  Hum per ye aaj kaisi Musibat ka waqt hai,
  Bazme aza say aap kiRukhsat ka waqt hai,
  Ronaq hamari ghar Ki na lejayiye imam.

  2
  Hum shakle mustafa Ali akber ki wo azaan,
  Tadpati hai hame Ali asger ki hichkiya,
  Ab chharahi hai aaya Ye kaisi qamooshiya,
  Ayyam ye aza kay Howay jate hai tamam.

  3
  Ummol banin kay lal Alamdare karbala,
  Sine may jiskay rehgaya Armaan jihaad ka,
  Shaane bhi uske katgaye Mashkiza chhidgaya,
  Matam kare ge royege Hum zindagi tamam.

  4
  Abbhi lahu rolati hai Akber ki tashnagi,
  Abbas aur sakina ki Asgher ki tashnagi,
  Toone bhojadi pyaas Mai kanjer ki tashnagi,
  Ankho may le kay aaye Hai hum ansowo kay jaam.

  5
  Qaimo ki aag aur wo Her simth shoro shain,
  Wo allatash ki gunj Wo kamsini labo kay bain,
  Dorre tamache aur wo Her lab pay ya hussain,
  Ankho may abbhi baaqiHai wo karbala ki shaam.

  6
  Akber SALAM aey ali asgher tujhe SALAM,
  Ferwa kay chaand Qasime mozter tujhe SALAM,
  Abbas aey hussain Kay lashker tujhe SALAM,
  Aey bekas o gareeb Mere tashnalab imam.

  7
  Bes tery gham kay Sadqe may izzat hame mili,
  Moula tera karam hai Teri banda perwari,
  Tujh ko hamari fikrThi rukhsat kay waqt bhi,
  Hum bekaso tak aagayaMoula tera SALAM.
