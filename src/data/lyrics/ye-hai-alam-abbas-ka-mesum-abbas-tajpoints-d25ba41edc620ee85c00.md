Aye Logo Aao Tum Ko Bataon.

1
Rehmat-e-Bari Tum Ko Dikhaon.
Sare Jahan Ka Hai Ik Sahara.
Yeh Jo Alam Mere Ghar Pe Laga Hai.
Yeh Hai Alam Abbas Ka.
Gham Se Nikalay Mushkilein Talay.
Kaisa Waseela Rab Ne Diya.

2
Hum Ne Alam Jab Ghar Mein Sajaya Ghar Mein Rakhay Zahra Ne Qadam.
Door Hoye Sab Ranj-o-Musayab Phir Kaisi Mushkil Kaisa Gham.
Dast-e-Karam Hai Sheh Ka Bharam Hai Jis Ka Koi Sani Na Mila.
Yeh Jo Bandhi Hai Mashk-e-Sakina Iss Ko Kaho Taveez-e-Wafa.
Rishta Bhateeji Our Chacha Ka Aaj Talak Na Toot Saka.
Aik Kahani Iss Ki Zabani Sun Lo Agar Suntay Ho Sada.

3
Dekho Alam Ka Surkh Pharera Chadar-e-Zainab Se Hai Bana.
Jo Bhi Alam Ke Saye Mein Aaya Dunya Ke Sab Gham Bhool Gaya.
Ab Ja Ke Mana Sara Zamana Saya Figan Ghar Ghar Pe Howa.
Aisa Alam Dunya Mein Dikhao Jis Ki Atta Ho Rab Ki Atta.
Shan-e-Azadari Yeh Alam Hai Dekho Azakhano Mein Zarra.
Manattein Pori Karta Hai Sab Ki Zindah Hai Iss Ke Dam Se Wafa.

4
Hamza-o-Jafar Ka Yeh Alam Hai Hai Yeh Alam Haider Ka Alam.
Dein Gay Gawahi Shah-e-Zaman Bhi Hai Yeh Mere Lashkar Ka Alam.
Barhwan Haider Kabay Ki Chat Par Lehra Ke Iss Ko De Ga Sada.
Qatal Howa Jab Darya Pe Ghazi Toot Gayi Maula Ki Kamar.
Lasha Nahi Sheh Laye Jarri Ka Laye Alam Hi Khon Mein Tar.
Beti Se Bolay Shabeer Ro Ke Aaye Nahi Darya Se Chacha.

5
Karb-o-Bala Ke Dasht Ka Manzar Kaisay Likhon Rota Hai Qalam.
Bad-e-Jarri Aoulad-e-Payamber Noha Kuna Thi Zair-e-Alam.
Bali Sakina Karti Thi Noha Saye Mein Iss Ke Aaye Qaza.
Panja Alam Ka Meesum-o- Mazhar Dast-e-Karam Abbas Ka Hai.
Paal Raha Hai Sare Jahan Ko Yeh Bhi Hasham Abbas Ka Hai.
Jis Ne Jo Manga Oss Ne Woh Paya Phir Tu Zamane Ko Kehna Para.
