ye waqia hai moharam ki gyara ka bakhuda

baghair goro kafan thay shaheede karbobala

harram tamam thay chote bade rasan basta

na sar pa maqna-o-chadar na koi daad rasa

Adu nay aale nabi ko ye aake hukm diya

sawaar naaqo pe ho jao jaana hai koofa

wo naaqe aaye amari bajawe jun pa na thay

ba jabr aale mohamed sawar unpe huwe

kahoon mai kis tarha ke haath pusht par thay bandhe

sagheer bache thay maon ki chatiyon se lage

layeen bhagate thay naaqo ko is shaqawat se

na rak sake kise bache ko maa hifazat se

dhuan tha khafila bacha jo koi girta tha

wo dekti thi usay aur wo maa ko takta tha

jo bacha girta tha naaqe se dab ke marta tha

sadaye ya ali ka ek shor utta tha

udoo ye haadsa hone par bhi na rukte thay

bajaye rukne wo aur tayz chalte thay

sakina girgayi naaqe se ek shor utha

sare hussain maye naize ke zameen may gada

kaha ye shimr ne abid se jaake poocho zara

sar ab ye kyon nahi chalta hai kya hua jo ruka

ye pooch kar mujhe batlao door jaana hai

lagi jo dayr to phir tum ho taziyana hai

sambhale hatkadi aur bediya bade sajjad

khareebe sar hue shabbir ke to ki faryaad

nida ye sar ne ba-ejaz ye di ke sun rudaad

sakina girgayi naaqe se ban may zainulliba

meri sakina ko jab tak na koi laayega

sare hussain yahan se na aage jaayega

chali talaash ko jangal may zainabe muztar

sadaye deti thi kis ja ho ay meri dilbar

ye ittefak se ek samt pad gayi jo nazar

ye dekha baithi hai ek bibi yun jhukaye sar

siyaah posh khule sar hai aur roti hai

sakina godh may unki hai aur soti hai

kareeb jake ye zainab ne unse farmaya

tumhara shukriya ae bibi jo taras khaya

is ek neki se parwanae irum paaya

magar ae mohsina chehra na apna dikhlaya

mai roze hashr jo dekhun to tumko pehchanu

jo maango iske sile may wo tumko dilwadun

ye sunke sabr ka paymana bibi ka chalka

hate ke balon ko rukh se ba dard-o-yaas kaha

mai teri maa hoo tu pehcan mujko mahlakha

ay meri zainabe muztar salam le maa ka

mala hai chehre pe apne hussain ka ye lahoo

tu soong le tere bhai ki isme hai khusboo
