Baba Tere Gham Mein Yeh Bechain Sakina Hai Hasan Sadiq.
Zindaan Ke Andherey Mein Mushkil Mera Jeena Hai.

1
Din Raat Main Roti Hoon Our Khak Pe Soti Hoon.
Aagosh Hai Madar Ki Na Baap Ka Seena Hai.
Dafna Ke Sakina Ko Abid Ne Kaha Ro Kar.
Aye Khak Tujhe Sonpa Zahra Ka Nagina Hai.

2
Main Ro Bhi Nahi Sakti Zindaan Mein Tumhein Baba.
Paband Mujhe Karta Roney Se Kamina Hai.
Hain Kan Mere Zakhmi Our Neel Tamachon Ke.
Zalim Ke Tamachon Se Be Haal Sakina Hai.

3
Abid Ne Kaha Ro Kar Zainab Se Phophi Amma.
Har Zulm-o-Sitam Sehna Iss Ghar Ka Qareena Hai.
Kartay Hain Bapa Matam Dunya Mein Shah-e-Dein Ka.
Momin Ke Liye Irfaan Bakhshish Ka Khazeena Hai.
