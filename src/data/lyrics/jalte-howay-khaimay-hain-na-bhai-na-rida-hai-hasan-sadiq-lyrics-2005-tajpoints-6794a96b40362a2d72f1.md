1
Jalte Howay Khaimay Hain Na Bhai Na Rida Hai Hasan Sadiq.
Hai Dukhtar-e-Shair-e-Khuda Jo Karbala Mein Hai Be Rida.
Jaltay Hoye Khaimay Hain Na Bhai Na Rida Hai.
Ummat Se Mila Kaisa Yeh Zainab Ko Sila Hai.

2
Aal-e-Abba Ke Sar Hain Charhey Nook-e-Sina Par.
Lashay Paray Hain Har So Jo Sab Raig-e-Tapaan Par.
Pehnati Unhein Ban Mein Kafan Khak-e-Shifa Hai.
Abid Ko La Rahi Hai Woh Kandhoon Pe Utha Kar.

3
Our Shimar Ko Kehti Hai Zara Khoof-e-Khuda Kar.
Koi Na Raha Ik Yehi Beemar Bacha Hai.
Kartay Hain Sab Farishtay Shabeer Ka Matam.
Karti Hain Hoor-o-Gilma Be Sheer Ka Matam.

4
Matam Sheh-e-Be Kas Ka Sar-e-Arsh Bapa Hai.
Iss Baat Ki Aye Sham-e-Ghareebaan Hai Gawah Tu.
Taray Nahi Yeh Sani-e-Zahra Ke Hain Aanso.
 Khursheed Nahi Aaj Tu Khud Rota Khuda Hai.
