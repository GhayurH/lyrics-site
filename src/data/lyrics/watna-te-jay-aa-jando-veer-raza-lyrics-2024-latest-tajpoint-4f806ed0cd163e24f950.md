1
Watna Te Jay Aa Jando Veer Raza.
Na Takdi Rahwaan Teriyaan Haye Wich Pardesan Aa Ke Na Ruldi Wich Sehra.
ALLAH Jane Kiwein Turdi Qum Wich Aayi.
Na Mukdiyan Sanwaan Meriyaan Aynj Doriyaan Veran Pa Ke Na Hondi Veer Juda.

2
Sainr Sughra Wangoon Rahwaan Takdi Reh Gayi.
Na Mur Ke Aaya Veer Odha Na Murya Veran Mera Hun Muk Gaye Mere Sah.
Rab Jane Kitni Door Aye Dais Tera.
Kad Mukna Aina Safraan Ne Kadon Wekhaan Gi Main Tainu Nayi Mukda Veer Aye Rah.

3
Main Tere Layi Veer Aye Madina Chadiya.
Main Suniyaan Khabran Teriyaan Tainu Qaidi Veer Banaya Aye Hoya Zulm Bara.
Nori Mehshar Walay Naal Hairat Wekhan.
Jad Wekhay Si Jarrar Ona Haye Matamiyan De Seene Te Chamkay Noor-e-Khuda.
