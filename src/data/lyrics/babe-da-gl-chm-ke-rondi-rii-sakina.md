Babay da gal chumke rondi rahi sakina

1
1) Aa vekh chacha Ghazi jeri mere teh beetei ai
Mein kis no sona wa (jeri Shimar ne kite hai)
Meno mar ke tamache dur lai gaya kameena

2
2) Mere wango shala jag tei na yateem koi howe
Na kali bei ke rowe (na miteyah tei sowe)
Bare yaad awanda hai baba tera sena

3
3) Zindanich sakina hai baba baba kardi
Ghazi no yaad kare (ho ke tei hawan pardi)
Bhulna ne dukh meno mushtak e kadena

4
4) Teri yaad veer asghar din raat satandi ai
Teri pyas veer asghar (hai maar mukandi ai)
Bulgai e bhain teri hai veer pani pena
