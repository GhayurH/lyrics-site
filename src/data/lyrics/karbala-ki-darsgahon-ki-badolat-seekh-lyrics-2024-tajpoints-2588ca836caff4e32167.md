1
Karbala Ki Darsgahon Ki Badolat Seekh Li Hasnain Akbar
Hur Ne Sajdoon Ki Hussainiyat Se Niyat Seekh Li.
Hur Ne Apne Khaimay Se Shabbir Ke Khaimay Talak.
Aate Aate Ishq Ki Sari Shariyat Seekh Li.

2
Kis Ko Kehte Hain Amama Kya Hai Iss Sar Ka Waqar.
Hur Ne Ik Romal Bandhne Se Woh Izzat Seekh Li.
Ashhado Ana Ameer Ul Momineena Keh Utha.
Sun Ke Akbar Ki Azaan Hur Ne Ibadat Seekh Li.

3
Jan De Kar Bhi Bachaya Jata Hai Dil Ka Yaqeen.
Hur Se Dunya Ne Aqeeday Ki Hifazat Seekh Li.
Kitni Sadiyaan Ho Gayin Kitne Hi Logon Ne Yahan.
Hur Ka Qissa Suntay Suntay Hi Sharafat Seekh Li.

4
Aik Kunba Aik Khaima Chor Kar Nikla Tha Our.
Aik Hi Khaimay Talak Aane Mein Hijrat Seekh Li.
Ho Gaya Hur Ke Ghulamo Mein Main Shamil Ho Gaya.
Rooz-e-Ashora Ki Akbar Jab Ziyarat Seekh Li.
