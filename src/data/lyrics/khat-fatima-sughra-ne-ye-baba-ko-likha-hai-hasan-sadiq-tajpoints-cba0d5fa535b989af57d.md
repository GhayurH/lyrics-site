Khat Fatima Sughra Ne Ye Baba Ko Likha Hai
Beemar Hoon Marti Hoon Yeh Sab Tum Ko Pata Hai

1
Jis Roz Se Dekha Hai Muharram Ka Niya Chand
Yeh Kaisay Likhon Baba Keh Dil Doob Gaya Hai

2
Wadah Tu Kiya Wadah Nibhanay Nahi Aaye
Beemar Se Kis Baat Pe Munh Phair Liya Hai

3
Baba Mujhe Dasti Hai Yeh Tanhai Watan Ki
Yeh Ghar Ki Udasi Hai Tu Phir Qabar Mein Kya Hai

4
Laut Aate Hain Ghar Sham Ke Hotey Hi Parinday
Kunba Mera Kyun Ab Bhi Safar Mein Hi Raha Hai

5
Tum Jab Se Gaye Ghar Se Yeh Ghar Qabar Hai Meri
Main Zindah Hoon Aisay Keh Koi Lasha Para Hai

6
Baba Mere Asghar Ka Tu Kuch Haal Sunao
Oss Be Zabaan Ne Mujh Ko Kabhi Yaad Kiya Hai

7
Dekha HaiHasan Sadiq, Rehan Ke Nohon Mein
Ashkon Ka Samandar Hai Jo Palkon Pe Ruka Hai
