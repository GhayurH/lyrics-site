Dou Veer Menday Vich Maktal Day Pahlay Kahray Veer Don Awan

Shabbir Dasa Abbas Dasa.

Guzri Howi Shaam-e-Ghareeban Da  Main Kahra Haal Sunawan,

Shabbir Dasa Abbas Dasa.

Zara Dekh Tay Sahi Kahray Rang Day Vich Aaye Khari Haye Pak Batool Taindi

Kahray Qatil Ton Hun Mangiyan Hin

Main Saar Diyan Pak Ridawaan

Shabbir Dasa Abbas Dasa.

Naraz Theway Na Behan Utay Ik Chadar Haye Oha Lah Gaye Ay

Main Lutti Howi Shaam-e-ghareeban Di

Kahray Veer Ku Kafan Pawawan

Shabbir Dasa Abbas Dasa.

Dastoor Hay Ja Maqtoolan Ku Koyi Wal Wal Qatal Karainda Nahi

Ei Nagri Vich Kahray Adal Kon

Tendi Zakhmi Ungal Dikhawan

Shabbir Dasa Abbas Dasa.

Koyi Hukam Ta Behan Ko Day Nasar Kehray Rah Wanjna Abbas Bhira

Khara Puchda Ay Poor Yateeman Da

Menday Kolon Najaf Dyan Rahwan

Shabbir Dasa Abbas Dasa.
