1
Mera Akbar Kyun Nahi Aya Syed Raza Abbas Zaidi.
Haye Nana Aj Daswein Ho Gayi Mera Akbar Kyun Nahin Aaya.
Wehriyaan Andar Mainu Disda Chaar Choferay Bas Apne Veer Da Saya.
Sughra Khali Hujray Ujray Wehray Mera Kaliyaan Aithay Jee Ghabraway.
Pehriyan Uttay Na Chacha Ghazi Mera Te Na Amari Da Jaya.
Sunrdi Fajar Di Roz Azan Main Jis Dam Mainu Yaad Akbar Di Aaway Nana.
Bas Ayho Lagda Aawazaan Mar Ke Mainu Jiwein Akbar Veer Bulaya.
Sughra Yasrab De Wich Mar Jaway Gi Rahwaan De Wich Bethi Bethi.
Kaliyaan Akbar Jay Tu Haye Shagan Manaye Te Kaliyaan Sehra Laya.
Main Khab Ich Takiya Aye Nana Be Haal Ali Da Jaya.
Do Katiyaan Banwaan Nu Babay Ne Haye Khak Taun Aan Ke Chaya.
Kithay Lash De Tukray Qasim De Kithay Akbar Veer Di Maiyyat.
Main Khilriya Wekhiya Aye Nana Maa Zahra Da Sarmaya.
Wekhay Khabaan De Wich Raitaan Uttay Mere Veraan De Be Kafan Janazay.
Mitiyaan Andar Thaan Thaan Te Khilriya Hoya Maa Zahra Da Sarmaya.
Sochaan Raat Diney Main Sughra Ayho Sab Marey Gaye Jang Te Muk Gayi.
Tur Gayiyaan Mawaan Te Sham Nu Qaidi Ho Ke Kiney Lashaan Nu Dafnaya.
Dasiya Mainu Khabaan De Wich Nanay Honra Phophiyaan Qaidi Rasiyaan Andar.
Beriyaan Pa Ke Othay Veer Abid Ne Janra Jehra Shamiyaan Shehar Sajaya.
 Akbar Rondi Rondi So Gayi Sughra Veer Akbar Si Haye Khab Ich Aaya.
Aakhiya Sughra Mainu Maut Na Mohlat Ditti Taiyon Tainu Lain Na Aaya.
