1
Logo Janaza Hai Ye Daamad e Mustafa Ka Qaiser Abbas.
Aagaz Ho Raha Hai Kofay Se Karbala Ka.
Karne Timar Dari Aaye Yateem Bachay.
Kozon Mein Doodh Sare Laye Yateem Bachay.
Jis Dam Chala Hai Ghar Se Taboot Eliya Ka.
Ro Kar Pokarey Haider Abbas Ke Hawalay.
Beti Tumhari Chadar Abbas Ke Hawalay.
Ghazi Bachay Mohafiz Tatheer Ki Rida Ka.
Lipati Hoi Padar Se Roti Hai Bint-e-Haider.
Baba Bhi Chal Basay Hain Bichari Hain Hum Se Madar.
Jari Howa Hai Ghar Mein Yeh Silsila Azza Ka.
Maiyyat Ali Ki Le Kar Hasnain Ja Rahe Hain.
Matam Ki Saf Malaik Sare Bicha Rahe Hain.
Kartay Bapa Hain Matam Sab Shair-e-Kibriya Ka.
 Qaiser Parhey Ga Kaisay Likhe Ghulam Kaisay.
Sar Khon Mein Hai Doba Maula Ali Ka Aisay.
Jaisay Keh Khoon Fishaan Tha Manzar Woh Karbala Ka.
