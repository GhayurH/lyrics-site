Yeh Yaad Rakho Wo Ghurbat Mein Bhi Ghareeb Nahi.
Woh Ahl-e-Zar Bhi Agar Hai Tu Ahl-e-Zar Na Samaj.
Gham-e-Hussain Ki Dolat Jissay Naseeb Nahi.
