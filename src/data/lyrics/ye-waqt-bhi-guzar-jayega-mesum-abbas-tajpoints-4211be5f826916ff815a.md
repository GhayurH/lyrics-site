Ye Waqt Bhi Guzar Jayega Mesum Abbas.
Khud Ko Dilasay Yeh Deti Thi Sughra.

1
Yeh Khali Hujray Yeh Khamosh Ratein.
Kaisay Bhula Doon Sakina Ki Batein.
Samjha De Tu Apne Dil Ko Yeh Dukhiya.
Di Thi Tasali Yeh Akbar Ne Mujh Ko.

2
Khat Bhaij Kar Main Bola Loon Ga Tujh Ko.
Yeh Soch Ke Takti Rehti Hoon Rasta.
Jee Bhar Ke Asghar Se Khaili Kahan Main.
Oss Ka Homakna Bhi Bholi Kahan Main.

3
Khuwabon Mein Aa Kar Yeh Kehta Hai Bhaiyya.
Ro Ro Ke Teen Eidein Main Ne Guzarein.
Ashkon Ki Barsat Eidi Mein Paein.
Kehta Raha Mujh Se Gham Ka Yeh Darya.

4
Gin Gin Ke Din Zindagi Ke Guzaron.
Qasim Ko Ammo Ko Sab Ko Pukaron.
Yadoon Ne Har Dam Diya Yeh Dilasa.
Dadi Ke Marqad Pe Har Roz Ja Kar.

5
Mangoon Duaein Diyon Ko Jala Kar.
Samjhati Rehti Hain Bas Dadi Zahra.
 Meesum Se Mazhar Yeh Kehta Hai Ro Kar.
Vernay Ghar Mein Thi Sarwar Ki Dukhtar.

6
Noha Tha Ya Marsiya Tha Yeh Jumla.
Aoun-o-Muhammad Ke Veeran Hujray.
Rab Jane Phir Kab Yeh Aabad Hoon Gay.
Laut Aaye Ga Aik Din Tera Kunba.
