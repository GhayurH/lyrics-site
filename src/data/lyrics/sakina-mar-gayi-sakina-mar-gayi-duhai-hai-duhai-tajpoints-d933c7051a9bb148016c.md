Sakina Mar Gayi Sakina Mar Gayi Duhai Hai Duhai.
Jab Sar-e-Shabir Zindaan Mein Sakina Ko Mila.

1
Tab Safar Ka Haal Sara Apne Baba Ko Diya.
Bolte Hi Bolte Khamosh Bachi Ho Gayi.
Ro Ke Zainab Ne Kaha Yeh Syed-e-Sajjad Se.
Jao Hakim Ki Taraf Kehna Ossay Tum Yaad Se.

2
Ab Hameesha Ke LIye Aazad Bachi Ho Gayi.
Bap Ka Sar Kar Lo Maiyyat Se Juda Lakht-e-Jigar.
Munh Ko Aata Hai Kaleja Khud Mera Lakht-e-Jigar.
Aa Gayi Zindan Mein Kaisi Musibat Ki Ghari.

3
Haye Sakina Ki Sada Aane Lagi Zindan Se.
Jab Sar-e-Baba Churaya Abid-e-Bemar Ne.
Ho Gaya Kuhram Barpa Peettay They Sar Sabhi.
Jiske Rone Se Pareshaan They Sipahi Sham Ke.

4
Haan Sakoon Ki Neend Soeingay Woh Sab Hi Aaj Se.
Rone Wali Baap Ko Royegi Ab Na Phir Kabhi.
Dafan Karne Jab Chala Khuwakhir Ko Woh Bekas Imam.
Lash Bhi Zanjeeron Mein Jakri Thi Our Bebas Imam.

5
Kya Byaan Ho Oss Ghari Jo Ghurbat-e-Sadaat Thi.
Youn Nishaan Abid Mitatay Jatey They Khud Qabar Ke.
Darti Thi Afraz Bibi Iss Qadar Oss Shimar Se.
Hai Sakina Aaj Tak Marqad Mein Bhi Sehmi Hoi.
