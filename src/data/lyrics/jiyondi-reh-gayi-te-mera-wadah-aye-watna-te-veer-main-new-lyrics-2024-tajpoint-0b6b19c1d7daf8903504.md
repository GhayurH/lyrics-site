1
Jiyondi Reh Gayi Te Mera Wadah Aye Watna Te Veer Main Jawaan Gi.
Roza-e-Mustafa Te Ja Veera Haal Karbal Da Main Sunawaan Gi.
Wekhiya Main Sina Te Sir Tera Mainu Oh Wela Yaad Aaya Aye.
Bab Ke Nana Sawari Veer Teri Jinna Galiyaan De Wich Phiraya Aye.

2
Naam Le Le Ke Veer Main Tera Ona Galiyaan Che Wain Pawaan Gi.
Teri Zahra Si Qaid Khane Wich Baad Tere Oh Veer Mar Gayi Aye.
Sahwaan Si Thoriyan Te Dukh Botay Kuj Oh Mere Hawalay Kar Gayi Aye.
Aa Ke Wich Karbala De Main Veera Qabar-e-Asghar Te Pani Pawaan Gi.

3
Rab Ne Sajday De Wich Risalat Di Kand Te Shabbir Nu Bithaya Aye.
Wekhiya Main Hussain Di Kand Te Charh Ke Khanjar Shimar Chalaya Aye.
Teri Gardan Te Chaliyaan Zarbaan Main Maa Nu Baqiya De Wich Ginawaan Gi.
Do Qadam Uth Ke Hoor Tur Bachra Ro Ke Beemar Nu Main Kaindi Rayi.

4
Tere Sajjad Nu Turawan Layi Kand Te Main Taziyane Saindi Rayi.
Na Way Akbar Te Na Rehya Ghazi Kinu Hun Veer Main Bulawaan Gi.
Mainu Bhulda Nayi Ik Sawal Odha Ro Ke Jehra Sakina Rayi Puchdi.
Piyo De Nal Dhi Da Pyar Honda Aye Kyun Main Babay Nu Ro Nayi Sakdi.

5
Mariya Aye Mainu Musalmana Neel Maa Zahra Nu Wakhawaan Gi.
Aayi Ik Lash Si Bazaraan Wich Kafan Khak-e-Shifa Da Paya Aye.
Oss Qaidan Da Sham Wich Najfi Do Imamaa De Sir Te Saya Aye.
Ro Ke Shabbir Nu Oh Kaindi Rayi Tere Sajjad Nu Bachawaan Gi.
