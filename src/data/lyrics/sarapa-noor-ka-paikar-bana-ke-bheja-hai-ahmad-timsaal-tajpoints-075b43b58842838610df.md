Sarapa Noor Ka Paikar Bana Ke Bheja Hai Ahmad Timsaal.
Khud Apni Zaat Ka Mazhar Bana Ke Bhaija Hai.

1
Mujhe Khuda Ne Payamber Ki Aabro Ke Liye.
Jawab-e-Tana-e-Abtar Bana Ke Bhaija Hai.
Qasam Se Gaudi e Haider Mein Rab e Haider Ne.
Mujhe Bhi Dosra Haider Bana Ke Bhaija Hai.

2
Bara-e-Zeenat e Gulzar-e-Fatima Zahra.
Khuda Ne Mujh Ko Gul-e-Tar Bana Ke Bhaija Hai.
Ali Se Shair Ka Maiyar Samne Rakh Kar.
Phir Oss Pe Ain Barabar Bana Ke Bhaija Hai.

3
Laeen-e-Sham Ke Tan Se Jo Sar Utaray Ga.
Qalam Ko Main Ne Woh Khanjar Bana Ke Bhaija Hai.
Yeh Sulha Nama Nahi Karbala Hai Kagaz Par.
Yeh Tarz-e-Jang-e-Bahatar Bana Ke Bhaija Hai.

4
Khuda Ne Khainch Ke Sab Dushmanan-e-Zahra Ko.
Diyar-e-Naar Mein Bandar Bana Ke Bhaija Hai.
Wafa Ke Rab Ne Sana-e-Hasan Mein Khud Ahmad.
Har Aik Lafz Ko Gohar Bana Ke Bhaija Hai.
