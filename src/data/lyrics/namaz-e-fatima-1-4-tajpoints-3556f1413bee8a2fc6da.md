1
Namaz-e-Fatima 1-4
Dozakh Hai Kya Batana Hamara Hi Kam Hai.
Dozakh Batool-e-Pak Ki Ranjish Ka Naam Hai.
Ulfat Hai Jis Ko Aal-e-Muhammad Se Dosto.

2
Janat Ka Ahtemam Ossi Dil Ke Naam Hai.
Zahra Tere Karam Ki Khabar Pa Ke Aaye Hain.
Hum Gardish-e-Hayat Se Ghabra Ke Aaye Hain..
Tofan-e-Mushkilat Se Takra Ke Aaye Hain.

3
Daman Jo Tere Samne Phaila Ke Aaye Hain.
Lillah Khali Hath Na Lotaiye Humein.
Khairat Bas Hussain Ki Dilwaiye Humein.
Shohar Se Keh Ke Kam Hamare Karaiye.

4
Bigre Hoye Naseeb Hamare Banaiye.
Har Mushkil-e-Hayyat Se Hum Ko Bachaiye.
Olad Ka Na Gham Kabhi Hum Ko Dikhaiye.
Olad Jin Ki Hai Osse Saleh Qarar Dein.

5
Soni Hai Koi Gaud Tu Oss Ko Bahar Dein.
Dar Pe Tere Malak Bhi Jhukatay Hain Apna Sar.
Roti Koi Tha Le Gaya Our Koi Baal-o-Par.
Jur Jatay Tere Dar Se Woh Ho Ga Na Dar Badar.

6
Dunya-o-Aakhrat Mein Wohi Ho Ga Moatbar.
Bibi Bas Aik Bar Ishara Bhi Kijiye.
Hur Ki Tarha Naseeb Hamara Bhi Kijiye.
Dar Ka Tere Faqeer Bhi Sultan Ho Gaya.

7
Nazaan Malak Tha Jab Koi Darban Ho Gaya.
Kamil Muhib-e-Aal Ka Eman Ho Gaya.
Bibi Hamari Nasal Ko Woh Iftikhar Dein.
Qayam Ka Har Jawaan Ko Mujahid Qarar Dein.
