1
Sada Daita Hai Hussain Wajhi Hasan Zaidi.
Maidaan Mein Lartay Lartay Sada Deta Hai Hussain.
Amma Madad Ko Aao Bohat Pyasa Hai Hussain.
Aankhon Ke Samne Mera Akbar Guzar Gaya.

2
Gham Ki Khabar Ne Mera Jigar Khushk Kar Diya.
Pani Tu Dekhney Ko Bhi Ab Tarsa Hai Hussain.
Lashay Utha Utha Ke Bohat Thak Chuka Hoon Main.
Aanso Baha Baha Ke Bohat Thak Chuka Hoon Main.

3
Sokhay Galay Se Cheekh Nahi Sakta Hai Hussain.
Zainab Ne Chehra Dekh Ke Mujh Se Hai Yeh Kaha.
Bhai Tumharey Jism Mein Pani Nahi Bacha.
Rotay Bhi Ho Tu Aankh Se Khoon Rista Hai Hussain.

4
Amma Meri Zaban Mein Chalay Se Par Gaye.
Akbar Ne Mujh Se Rotay Hoye Jumlay Yeh Kahey.
Mujh Se Zyada Tera Gala Sokha Hai Hussain.
Amma Tumhein Tu Ilm Hai Lagti Hai Kitni Pyas.

5
Pani Nahi Miley Tu Atakti Hai Meri Sans.
Pyasa Hai Teen Din Se Magar Zindah Hai Hussain.
Soya Nahi Hoon Chain Se Ik Pal Bhi Raat Mein.
Dekha Hai Sokha Koza Sakina Ke Hath Mein.

6
Oss Ko Lita Ke Seene Pe Bas Roya Hai Hussain.
Lashay Utha Ke Hathoon Mein Ab Dard Ho Gaya.
Itna Lahu Baha Hai Badan Sard Ho Gaya.
Chalte Hoye Gira Hai Kabhi Betha Hai Hussain.

7
Ho Jao Paye Zakhmi Kahin Tum Na Mere Sath.
Amma Mere Galay Se Hata Lo Ab Apne Hath.
Khanjar Laeen Ka Rook Nahi Sakta Hai Hussain.
Chodah So Saal Beet Gaye Our Aaj Bhi.

8
Pani Agar Sabeel Se Peetey Hain Matami.
Unn Ko Faqat Khiyal Tera Aata Hai Hussain.
Kaisay Bataon Sham-e-Ghareebaan Mein Kya Howa.
Gham Barh Gaya Hai Mera Gala Khushk Ho Gaya.

9
Main Ne Abhi Kiya Nahi Bas Socha Hai Hussain.
Kaisay Biyan Ho Bhala:AZLAN AZMI:.
Maula Ki Mere Pyas Tu Barhti Chali Gayi.
Ab Do Qadam Bhi Chalta Hai Tu Girta Hai Hussain.
