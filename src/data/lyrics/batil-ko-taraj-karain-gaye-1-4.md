Batil Ko Taraj Karain Gaye 1-4.
Nusrat Ko Hum Jaein Gaye.

1
Jab Bhi Mula Aaein Gaye.
Labaik Ya Mehdi Labaik Ya Mehdi.
Batil Ko Taraj Karein Gaye.
Sheh Ke Nokar Raaj Karein Gaye.

2
Shah-e-Najaf Ke Chahne Walo.
Ishq-e-Muhammad Ke Matwalo.
Pakeeza Maon Ke Jaiyalo.
Aazadi Ke Jaam Uchalo.

3
Tofano Mein Ooab Raha Hai.
Takht-e-Saudi Doob Raha Hai.
Shair-e-Khuda Ke Shair Ke Dar Se.
Taig Do Paikar Ke Mehshar Se.

4
Shirk Ke Do Tair Be-Par Se.
Qabar-e-Nabi Ke Idhar Udhar Se.
Tagoti Purze Ukhrein Gaye.
Garay Hoye Murday Ukhrein Gaye.

5
Sath Mein Dast-e-Buredah Ho Ga.
Ashoray Ka Badla Ho Ga.
Taig-e-Ali Ka Jalwa Ho Ga.
Bas Hamlay Pe Hamla Ho Ga.

6
Haider Ka Akas Laray Ga.
Youn Samjho Abbas Laray Ga.
Ahl-e-Haq Ayjad Hansein Gaye.
Gham Khurda Afrad Hansein Gaye.

7
Jurat Ke Folad Hansein Gaye.
Khud Mula Sajad Hansein Gaye.
Ho Ga Zahra Ka Dil Shad.
Aye Gul-e-Narjis Zindabad.
