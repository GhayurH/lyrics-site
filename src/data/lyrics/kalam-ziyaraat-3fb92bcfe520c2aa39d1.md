Husain

1
Imam Jafar-E-Sadiq Irshad Farmate Hayn,
”Ke Jo Shaksh Imam Husain Ke Kabr Ki Taraf Paidal Chal Kar Aaya
Allah(Aj) Us Ke Liye Har Kadaam Ke Badle 1000 Nakiya Likh Deta Hay
Aur 1000 Gunah Maaf Kar Deta Hay
Aur 1000 Darjaat Baland Kar Deta Hay.”

2
Labbaik Ya Husain
Mai Ishq Ke Safar Pe Chala
Hare Ek Kadam Pe Karta Hua Shukr E Khuda
Mai Safar Pe Chala

3
Jab Maine Sar Zameene Najaf Par Rakhe Kadam
Dil Ne Mere Irada Kia Ya Shahe Ummam
Ye Tujh Se Mohabbat Hay Meri
Aau Jo Ziyarat Ko Teri Ibne Ali
Kar Le Qabool Mera Aqidat Bhara Salam
Paidal Najaf Se Karbobala Aaenga Gulam
Almadad Ya Shahe Karbala
Almadad Ya Shahe La Fatah

4
Mai Paidal Chala Karbala
Naaz Kismet Pe Karta Hua
Mai Paidal Chala Karbala

5
Mere Sine Me Bachpan Se Armaan Tha,
Aa Gaya Gar Waha Se Bulawa Mera,
Shahe Marda Ke Dar Se Suwe Karbala,
Apni Pairo Pe Chalta Hua Jaunga,
Shukre Maula Who Din Aa Gaya,

6
Mai Paidal Chala Karbala

7
Zebe Tankar Kar Ke Ehraam E Gum Chal Dia
Khud Pe Naade Ali Kar Ke Dum Chal Dia
Ek Chota Sa Lekar Alam Chal Dia
Tha Nigaho Me Sheh Ka Haram Chal Dia
Jab Ye Izn E Safar Mil Gaya

8
Mai Paidal Chala Karbala

9
Us Musafat Ka Ehwal Mujhse Suno,
Mai Najfa Se Chala Jis Ghadi Momino,
Yaad Karta Raha Maula Sajjad Ko,
Kya Kahun Hausla Jo Mila Dosto,
Har Kadam Nauha Padhta Hua,

10
Mai Paidal Chala Karbala

11
Nauha Karte Hue Sogwar Aa Gai,
Jis Taraf Aankh Utthi Hazar Aagaie,
Farsh Par Arsh Se Pursadar Aagaie,
Thak Ke Baitha To Khidmat Guzar Aagaie,
Jis Ka Jee Chaha Jaise Chala

12
Mai Paidal Chala Karbala

13
Chalte Chalte Jo Pairon Mein Chale Padhe,
Aa Gaie Yaad Sub Zakhm Sajjad Ke,
Yaad Aaie Aseeron Ke Jub Hausle,
Yun Laga Jaise Chale Padhe Hi Na The,
Un Aseeron Ko Rota Hua

14
Mai Paidal Chala Karbala

15
Imam Jafar E Sadiq Irshad Farmate Hain,
“Jis Ne Imam Husain Ki Ziyarat Kar Li
Goya Usne Rasoolallah(sawaw) Ki Ziyarat Kar Li.”

16
Sadiq E Aale Athar Se Bhi Suna,
Zairon Ke Liye Keh Gaye Mustafa(sawaw),
Jis Ne Dekha Haram Mere Shabbir Ka,
Us Ko Meri Ziyarat Hui Ba Khuda,
Is Aqidat Mein Behta Hua

17
Mai Paidal Chala Karbala

18
Dunia Walon Yehi Ishk Ka Hay Safar,
Kamzan Ambiya Bhi Hay Is Rah Par,
Is Safar Ki Fazilat Se Ho Ba Khabar,
Sakht Sardi Ho Ya Sakht Garmi Magar,
Sub Ke Lab Par Hay Bas Ek Sada

19
Mai Paidal Chala Karbala

20
Yadon Mein Hay Meri Abtak Woh Karwan

21
Manzil Ko The Rawan, Kay Tifl Kya Jawan,
Parda Nasheen Sub Maan Behne Betiyan,
Hathon Me Ya Husain Ke Parcham Liye Hue,
Mausam Ki Fikr Thi Na Thakan Ke Moamle,
Badhte Rahe Husain Ke Janib Yeh Kafle,
Har Char Gaam Par The Moakkib Bane Hue,
Unme Tha Zaieereen Ki Rahat Ka Ehtemam,
Jinnat Aur Farishte Bhi Karte The Ehtemam,
Logon Ke Ajdaham Me Dekha Yeh Mojiza
Na Bhook Se Nidhal Na Pyasa Koi Mila,
Sadqa Mere Husain Ka Bat Ta Tha Char Su
Aati Thi Rotiyon Se Mano Salva Ki Khushboo
Naqabil E Bayan Hay Jo Ehtemam The
Kuze Jo The Sabeel Pe Kausar Ke Jam The
Han Zair E Husain Ke Pairon Ki Khaak Se
Na Jaane Kitne Log Shifayab Hote The
Aksar Yeh Zaieereen Ke Kanon Ne Bhi Suna
Aati Hay Husain Ke Rauze Se Ek Sada
Yeh Log Mohtaram Hay,  Hamari Nigah Mein
Abbas Zaireen Ko Lelo Panah Mein

22
Labbaik Ya Husain
Saya Sar Pe Alamdar Ka

23
Mai Paidal Chala Karbala

24
Zairon Se Main Sun Ta Tha Aksar Yehi
Chahe Jis Raaste Se Bhi Jaaie Koi
Pehle Hongi Ziyarat Alamdar Ki
Bad Mein Kabre Shahe Zaman Aaiengi
Janne Ko Yeh Sub Majra

25
Mai Paidal Chala Karbala

26
Aaya Jub Samne Rauza Shabbir Ka
Gir Ke Be Sakhta Main Ne Sajda Kiya
Apne Maula Se Sajde Mein Ro Kar Kaha
Maangne Kutch Nahi Aaya Mai Ba Khuda
Shauk Teri Ziyarat Ka Tha

27
Mai Paidal Chala Karbala

28
Imam Jafar E Sadiq Ne Irshad Farmaya,
“Imam Husain Ki Ziyarat Tamam Aamal Se Afzal Hay,
Jo Shaks Imam Husain Ke Haq Ki Marefat Rakhte Hue
Unki Kabr Par Aaienga Woh Us Shaks Ki Tarha Hoga
Jisne Rasulallah(sawaw) Ke Sath 100 Haaj Kiye Ho,
Agar Tum Me Se Ek Admi Sari Zindagi Haaj Karta Rahe
Aur Usne Husain Ibne Ali Ki Ziyarat Na Ki Ho
To Who Allah(A.J.) Aur Uske Rasool(sawaw) Ke Haq Ka Tariq Hoga,
Kyunke Husain Ka Haq, Allah (A.J.) Ke Un Faraiz Mein Se Hay
Jo Ke Har Musalman Par Wajib Hay

29
Keh Di Yeh Baat Mazhar Ne Farhan Se
Haaj Pe Jaana Tha Pichle Baras To Mujhe
Main Ne Jaana Jo Kaabe Se Afzal Ushe
Karbala Ke Hi Phir Mai Ne Samaan Kiye
Phir Najaf Se Hi Rota Hua

30
Mai Paidal Chala Karbala
