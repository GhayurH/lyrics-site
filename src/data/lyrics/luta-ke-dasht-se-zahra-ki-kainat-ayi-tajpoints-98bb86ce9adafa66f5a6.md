Luta Ke Dasht Se Zahra Ki Kainat Ayi Mastar Mohammad Shah.
Ali Ki Beti Madine Mein Khali Hath Ayi.

1
Nazar Mein Sham-e-Ghareeban Ka Phir Giya Manzar.
Watan Mein Aa Ke Jo Zainab Pe Pehli Raat Ayi.
Na Nikle Sani-e-Zahra Ki Aankh Se Aanso.
Labon Pe Aoun-o-Muhammad Ki Jab Bhi Baat Ayi.

2
Utho Hassan Zara Kubra Ke Sar Pe Hath Rakho.
Ujar ke Qasim-e-Noshah Ki Hai Baraat Ayi.
Mareez Beti Ko Shabir Se Mila Na Saki.
Magar Wo Khak-e-Shifa Le Apne Sath Ayi.

3
Wo Jis Ke Lab Pe Tha Haye Sawal Chadar Ka.
Jo Banti Hoi Dunya Ko Hai Zakaat Ayi.
Meri Nigah Mein Khair-ul-Amal Hai Ye Matam.
Hamare Hath Takallum Ye Deniyaat Ayi.
