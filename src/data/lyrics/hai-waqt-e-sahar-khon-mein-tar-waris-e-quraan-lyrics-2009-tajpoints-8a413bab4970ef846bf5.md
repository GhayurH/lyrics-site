Hai Waqt-e-Sahar Khon Mein Tar Waris-e-Quraan.
Roti Hai Azaan Masjid-e-Kofa Bhi Hai Giryaan.

1
Lo Aaj Se Aghaz Howa karb-o-Bala Ka.
Makhdoma-e-Konain Ka Jari Howa Giriya.
Marqad Mein Muhammad Bhi Nazar Aye Pareshaan.
Kya Zehar Mein Dobi Hoi Shamsheer Chali Hai.

2
Lo Zakhmi Howa Sajday Mein Damad-e-Nabi Hai.
Dunya Se Chala Dekhiye Kabay Ka Nigehbaan.
Jibreel-e-Ameen Khaak Uratay Howay Aye.
Adam Bhi Yehi Noha Sunatay Howay Aye.

3
Kis Ko Dam-e-Mushkil Mein Pukare Ga Musalmaan.
Tu Ne Bin-e-Muljim Ye Sitam Kaisa Hai Dahaya.
Sajday Mein Namazi Ka Laho Kyun Hai Bahaya.
Abbas Ko Bachpan Mein Kiya Chak Giraybaan.

4
Kabay Ki Faseelon Pe Udasi Ki Rida Hai.
Our Qibla-e-Awal Mein Bhi Kuhraam Bapa Hai.
Sar Khuld Mein Horaan-e-Jinaa Ne Kiya Uryaan.
Husnain Ke Sar Par Hai Yateemi Ka Andehra.

5
Rukhsat Howa Soraj Ki Nigahon Se Sawera.
Zam Zam Mein Talatum Hai Tu Kausar Mein Hai Tofaan.
Aab Nad-e-Ali Parh Ke Nabi Kis Ko Bulaein.
Rukti Hi Nahi Sarwar-e-Konain Ki Aahein.

6
Kabay Ke Dar-o-Baam Bhi Matam Se Hain Larzaan.
 Rehan Hain Rizwaan-e-Jinaa Chaak Giraybaan.
Hai Bag-e-Iram Bag-e-Fidak Ki Tarha Giryaan.
Hasnain Ka Baba Hai Zara Dair Ka Mehmaan.
