Aa Rahi Hai Ye Sada Zainab e Dilgeer Ki Hasan Sadiq.
Lash Be Goor-o-Kafan Hai Dasht Mein Shabeer Ki.

1
Ab Nahi Abbas-e-Ghazi Qasim-o-Akbar Kahan.
Haye Ab Koi Uthaye Lash Be Taqseer Ki.
Kar Diya Quran Zakhmi Hurmala Ke Teer Ne.
Jantay Na They Musalmaan Azmatein Be Sheer Ki.

2
Aa Gayi Ummat Nabi Ki Aag Le Kar Hath Mein.
Laaj Rakh Lena Khudaya Chadar-e-Tatheer Ki.
Sar Diya Gulzar Jab Islam Par Shabeer Ne.
Khon Ke Qatron Ne Phir Khak-e-Shifa Akseer Ki.
