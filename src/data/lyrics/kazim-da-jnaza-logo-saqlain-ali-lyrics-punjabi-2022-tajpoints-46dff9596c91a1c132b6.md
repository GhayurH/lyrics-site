Kazim Da Jnaza Logo Saqlain Ali Lyrics Punjabi
Baghdad De Tang Zindan De Wich Ik Qaidi Aye Farmaye.
De Ajar-e-Risalat Aj Koi Mere Tauq Zanjeer Lahaye.
Jiwein Surkh Si Maqtal Karbal Da Ewein Surkh Aida Zindan Aye.
Nokaan Nal Lag Lag Hoya Aye Aiday Badan Da Rang Marjan Aye.
Har Qatra Khon Da Khak Uttay Aidhi Ghurbat Likhda Jaye.
Aye 14 Saal Da Qaidi Aye Nayi Mar Ke V Aazad Hoya.
Aiday Badan Di Surkhi Dasdi Aye Baon Zulm Maran Taun Baad Hoya.
De Jhotay Fatway Zalimaan Ne Aidi Maut Asbab Chupaye.
Dastoor Te Hai Jag Dunya Da Hin Zindagi Tak Tazeraan.
Nayi Wekhiya Haye Kisay Maiyyat Ne Ainj Paiyaan Hoan Zanjeeraan.
Jiwein Pak Raza De Babay Di Maiyyat Zanjeer Ne Paye.
Aj Fair Batool De Gulshan Te Ummat Ne Zarb Hai Layi.
Kar Qatal Hai Mosa Kazim Nu Baghdad Na Eid Manai.
Aidi Lash Nu Wekhan Aj Zalim Ik Shugal Samajh Ke Aaye.
Aye Apni Maut Hai Khud Marya Aidi Maiyyat Te Likhwaya Aye.
Baghdad De Chand Mazdoraan Ne Aida Aan Janaza Chaya Aye.
Be Warsaan Wangoon Chad Gaye Ne Koi Kazim Nu Dafnaye.
 Azhar Ik Sham Di Qaidan Hai Jehri Ro Ro Eho Kehndi Aye.
Mainday Wangoon Maiyyat Kazim Di Haye Wich Zanjeeraan Qaidi Aye.
Nayi Watan Te Waliya Fair Kazim Sah Wich Pardais Mokaye.
