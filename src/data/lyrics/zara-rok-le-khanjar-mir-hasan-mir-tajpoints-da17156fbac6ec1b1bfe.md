1
Zara Rok Le Khanjar Mir Hasan Mir Lyrics
Zainab Ko Dekha Sheh Ne Jo Nazdeek-e-Qatal Ga.
Oss Dam Ghareeb-e-Zahra Ne Qatil Se Yeh Kaha.
Zainab Nikal Aayi Hai Zara Rook Le Khanjar.

2
Maula Yeh Sada Detey They Aye Shimar Sitamgar.
Aakhir Tu Safar Hona Hai Iss Dar-e-Mahan Se.
Do Batein Tu Kar Lainay De Bhai Ko Bahan Se.
Aati Hai Meri Simt Meri Ladli Khwahar.

3
Tu Lakh Sitamgar Hai Magar Kam Yeh Kar Ja.
Aye Shimar Mere Seene Se Kuch Dair Utar Ja.
Mar Jaye Gi Iss Haal Mein Dekha Jo Baradar.
Bhai Ki Muhabbat Mein Woh Be Taab Hoi Hai.

4
Khaimay Se Bohat Taiz Woh Maqtal Ko Chali Hai.
Sar Se Meri Behna Ke Utar Jaye Na Chadar.
Dedar Karey Woh Mera Main Bhi Ossay Dekhon.
Zakhmi Hoon Magar Aakhri Tazeem Tu Kar Loon.

5
Aye Shimar Behan Hai Yeh Meri Maa Ke Barabar.
Zainab Chali Jaye Tu Sitam Chahe Jo Dhana.
Hat Jaye Bahan Meri Tu Khanjar Ko Chalana.
Kuch Dair Mere Shano Pe Rehne De Mera Sar.

6
Abbas Ki Khatir Mujhe Paigham Yeh De Gi.
Shayad Ali Akbar Ki Koi Baat Karey Gi.
Kuch Dair Tu Roney De Bahan Bhai Ko Mil Kar.
Mazloom Ki Tasveer Ko Aankhon Mein Basa Kar.

7
Shabbir Se Masoom Sakina Ko Mila Kar.
Wadah Hai Chali Jaye Gi Wapis Meri Khwahar.
Kehne Laga Maula Se Sitamgar Yeh Hans Kar.
Pehle Bhi Nikal Aayi Thi Yeh Khaimay Se Bahir.

8
Sheh Ne Kaha Jab Qatal Howa Tha Ali Akbar.
Sar Sheh Ka Juda Karne Mein Masroof Laeen Tha.
Kya Kehta Hai Mazloom Woh Sunta Hi Nahi Tha.
Kehta Raha Sar Tan Se Juda Ho Ke Bhi Akbar.
