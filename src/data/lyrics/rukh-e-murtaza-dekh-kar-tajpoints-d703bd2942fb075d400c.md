Rukh-e-Murtaza Dekh Kar.

1
Jee Rahe Hain Rukh-e-Murtaza Dekh Kar.
Rukh Badaltay Nahi Hum Howa Dekh Kar.
Aaye Mairaj Se Laut Kar Mustafa.
Ik Nishani Mein Dast-e-Khuda Dekh Kar.
Arsh Ki Kiya Fiza Hai Samajhtay Hain Woh.
Aaye Hain Jo Najaf Ki Fiza Dekh Kar.

2
Jane Woh Kaun Si Manzil-e-Noor Thi.
Ki Ibadat Ali Ne Khuda Dekh Kar.
Hai Ibadat Rukh-e-Murtaza Dekhna.
Muskuraye Ali Aaina Dekh Kar.
Kyun Na Sajday Karon Ya Ali Aap Se.
Ishq Kabay Ki Dewar Ka Dekh Kar.

3
Jan Leti Hai Dunya Keh Hum Kaun Hain.
Ja Namazon Pe Khak-e-Shifa Dekh Kar.
Zikar-e-Saley Aala Kar Raha Hai Khuda.
Aasmaan Se Dar-e-Fatima Dekh Kar.
Youn Ziyarat Ho Akbar Keh Dunya Kahey.
Aap Aaein Hain Kya Karbala Dekh Kar.
