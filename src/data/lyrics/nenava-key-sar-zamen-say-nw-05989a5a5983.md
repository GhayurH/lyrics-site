Nenava key sar zamen say

-Fatima Zahra ka kunba, Karbala may loot gaya

-Rat bhar bankey havay khak urati reh gayain

Chan bhee sharmo haya say, badaloon may chup gaya

-Sayadoon kay khoon key surk,i Falak par ahgayi

Reh guzari Karbala may, jab andhara chagaya

-Rozay ashura hai logo, aj zainub kaya karay

Karbal key rayt par hai, la shen paray hain jaba ja
