Sajjad Muhari Jonda Riya Kiwein Sham Che Aa Ke.
Tatheer Di Chadar Da Sadma Haye Jigar Te Kha Ke.

1
Jadon Shamiyaan Ne Pathar Zainab De Sir Te Marey.
Abbas Nu Takdi Rayi Zainab Nazraan Nu Utha Ke.
Phophiyaan Nu Naal Le Ke Sajjad Kiwein Guzrey.
Bazar Che Baithay Ne Shami Har Moor Saja Ke.

2
Kayi Waar Qatal Hoya Sajjad Bazaraan Wich.
Abid Nu Uthanday Si Gash Taun Naa Phophiyaan Da Cha Ke.
Akhiyaan Taun Wasda Jaway Abid Da Khon Sara.
Chanday Ne Nezay Te Chadar Abid Nu Wakha Ke.

3
Sajjad Da Jigar Na Haye Khon Hoor Roye.
Rondi Aye Aayi Abid Taun Zakhma Nu Chupa Ke.
 Zeeshan Mar E Janda Sajjad Bazaraan De.
Har Moor Jay Takda Akhiyaan Choon Aye Khon Hata Ke.
