1
Jab Uth Gaye Ali Ke Padar Is Jahan Se Saleem Mastan.
Lakhon Balaein Toot Parin Aasman Se.
Rakhtay They Jo Azeez Peygamber Ko Jan Se.
Jate Hain Soye Khuld Woh Fani Makan Se.

2
Sar Peetiye Nabi Ke Sana Khuwaan Guzar Gaye.
Logo Rasool-e-Haq Ke Chacha Jaan Guzar Gaye.
Sar Peettay Hain Nasir-o-Yawar Bhi Iss Ghari.
Hain Ghamzada Ali Ke Baradar Bhi Iss Ghari.

3
Rotay Hain Aah Saqi-e-Kausar Bhi Iss Ghari.
Be Chain Hain Khuda Ke Peygamber Bhi Iss Ghari.
Maka Ke Shahzadon Pe Aafat Guzar Gayi.
Sab Pursa Do Nabi Pe Qayamat Guzar Gayi.

4
Woh Abad-e-Khas Bari-e-Tala Nahi Raha.
Maka Ki Wadiyon Ka Ujala Nahi Raha.
Jis Ne Nabi-e-Haq Ko Sanbhala Nahi Raha.
Haye Haye Nabi Ka Palney Wala Nahi Raha.

5
Dada Ke Baad Jis Ne Nabi Ko Sanbhala Tha.
Woh Jis Ne Baap Ban Ke Muhammad Ko Pala Tha.
Jis Ne Kiye Rasool Pe Ahsan Mar Gaye.
Deen-e-Khuda Ke They Jo Nigehban Mar Gaye.

6
Sad Haif Mustafa Ke Chacha Jan Mar Gaye.
Hain Muztarib Rasool Keh Imran Mar Gaye.
Sar Par Urao Khak Keh Haider Malool Hain.
Roo Chacha Ke Gham Mein Payamber Malool Hain.

7
Seene Pe Jo Keh Apne Nabi Ko Sulata Tha.
Beton Se Barh Ke Naaz Jo Unn Ke Uthata Tha.
Har Dam Nabi Ko Garam Howa Se Bachata Tha.
Khud Bhoka Reh Ke Khana Nabi Ko Khilata Tha.

8
Jo Dhaal Ban Ke Rehta Tha Har Dam Rasool Ki.
Hai Chashm Oss Ke Wastay Purnam Rasool Ki.
Dekha Jo Aankh Khol Ke Baba Na Sar Pe They.
Tha Bachpana Keh Maa Bhi Gayin Iss Jahan Se.

9
Thi Aath Saal Umar Keh Dada Chaley Gaye.
Nazo Nam Se Pala Chacha Jan Ne Jisay.
Haye Haye Jahan Se Woh Chacha Jaan Chaley Gaye.
Aayi Nida-e-Gaib Keh Imraan Chaley Gaye.
