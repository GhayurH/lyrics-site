Aao Taboot Muhammad Ka Uthao Logo Shahid Baltistani
Aanso Zahra Ki Yateemi Pe Bahao Logo.

1
Aao Taboot Muhammad Ka Uthao Logo.
Sari Dunya Ko Wafadari Sikhao Logo.
Jis Aqeedat Se Bapa Karte Hi Mailad-e-Nabi.
Farsh-e-Gham Waise Muhammad Ka Bichao Logo.
Yeh Saf-e-Gham Hai Muhammad Ki Yahan Farq Nahi.
Haan Issi Saf Pe Zamane Ko Bulao Logo.
Ghar Mein Fatima Zahra Ke Andhera Chaya.
Aaj Ki Raat Charagon Ko Bhujao Logo.
Kha Ke Pathar Bhi Jo Ummat Ko Dua Dete Rahe.
Aise Mohsin Ka Na Ahsan Bhulao Logo.
Apne Gham Bhool Ke Deti Hai Khadija Yeh Sada.

2
Gham Mein Mazloom Ke Rona Hai Nabi Ki Sunnat.
Yeh Haqeeqat Na Zamane Se Chupao Logo.
Kyun Ho Khamosh Baqiya Ke Ujar Jane Pe.
Apne Sarkar Ko Aise Na Rulao Logo.
Apne Baba Ki Judai Mein Jo Chup Kar Roi.
Dill Ossi Fatima Zahra Ka Barhao Logo.
Baad Baba Ke Jise Haq Na Mila Dunya Se.
Oss Ke Haq Ke Liye Aawaz Uthao Logo.
Sabz Gunbad Se Takallum Yeh Sada Aati Hai.
Qabar-e-Zahra Pe Koi Saya Banao Logo.
