Chale Ga Tera Karm Ishara Tera Chale Ga.
Jahan Ko Khaliq Faqt Sahara Tera Chale Ga.

1
Raheen-e-Minat Har Aik Insaan Tera Khudaya.
Koi Jahaan Ho Magar Ajarah Tera Chale Ga.
Tere Hi Dast-e-Nagar Ye Duniya-o-Mafiha Hai.
Tera Jahan Hai Nazaam Sara Tera Chale Ga.

2
Bashar Mein Insaniyaat Teri Shart-e-Awoleen Hai.
Ibadtoon Se Kahan Guzara Tera Chale Ga.
Broz-e-Mahshar Har Ik Amal Ka Hisaab Ho Ga.
Bhara Howa Sirf Goshwara Tera Chale Ga.

3
Wo Muntahib Kardah Abad Makhsoos Chodah Tere.
Azal Se Tabah Abad Adarah Tera Chale Ga.
Bohat khata Kar Hai “Azai” Moaf Karna.
Mere Khudaya udhar Ishara Tera Chale Ga.
