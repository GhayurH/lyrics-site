1
Sog me dubi duniya thi saari
Aayi Ali ki ban me sawaari
Pehre pe Zainab dard ki maari

2
Baap ko dekha roke pukaari
Dekho to baba, haay, dekho to baba
Shaam-e-ghariba ka ye manzar

3
1. Kehti thi rokar zainab-e-muztar, Shaam-e-ghariba ka ye manzar,
Kehti thi rokar zainab-e-muztar, Shaam-e-ghariba ka ye manzar

4
2. Deti thi Zainab ban me duhaayi, Haaye mujhe kyu mawt na aayi
Khencha gaya beemaar ka bistar
Shaam-e-ghariba ka ye manzar
Kehti thi rokar zainab-e-muztar, Shaam-e-ghariba ka ye manzar

5
3. Teeron ne chayda baap ka sina, soye kaha par bali Sakina
Nawha kuna hai wo jalti zameen par
Shaam-e-ghariba ka ye manzar
Kehti thi rokar zainab-e-muztar, Shaam-e-ghariba ka ye manzar

6
4. Sukhe huwe kuzon ki sada hai, pyaase idhar hai udhar darya hai
Nawha yahi hai khushk labon par
Shaam-e-ghariba ka ye manzar
Kehti thi rokar zainab-e-muztar, Shaam-e-ghariba ka ye manzar

7
5. Dasht-e-bala mei bikhre hai laashe, tute huwe talwaar aur neza
Dekho zara ay fatah-e-khaybar
Shaam-e-ghariba ka ye manzar
Kehti thi rokar zainab-e-muztar, Shaam-e-ghariba ka ye manzar

8
6. Kaisi luti haay aal-e-payambar, legaye zalim cheen ke chadar
Chaand sitare roye ye kehkar
Shaam-e-ghariba ka ye manzar
Kehti thi rokar zainab-e-muztar, Shaam-e-ghariba ka ye manzar

9
7. Door huwe jab god ke paale, rut gaye is ghar se ujale
Rone lagi hai dekh ke madar
Shaam-e-ghariba ka ye manzar
Kehti thi rokar zainab-e-muztar, Shaam-e-ghariba ka ye manzar

10
8. Khaak mere baalon mei pari hai, khaak basar awlad-e-nabi hai
Sare kahani kehta hai rokar
Shaam-e-ghariba ka ye manzar
Kehti thi rokar zainab-e-muztar, Shaam-e-ghariba ka ye manzar

11
9. Baba inhe tawfeeq ye dena, hashr talak parhte rahe nawha
Yaad rahe "Irfan" aur "Mazhar"
Shaam-e-ghariba ka ye manzar
Kehti thi rokar zainab-e-muztar, Shaam-e-ghariba ka ye manzar
