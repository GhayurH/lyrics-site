Looriyon Say Sona Wala So gaya hay Teer’ say

-Hal’ koey ja kay pucha madaray baysheer say

-Jetini hay kerbala bayta yay bano nay kaha

Jhulay may layti huwi abbas key tasveer say

-Pair na sukhi zaban hotoon pay ehsa war tha

War jehsa kar nahi pa ya koey shamsheer say

-Teer kis dil say chalaya Asghar-e-masoom’ par

Ashika chud puch taytay hurmala bay peer say

-Deakh kar Asghar ka sar nayzay pay kehti tin Rubab

Deakhnay ko dil hey kehsa, yay mila taqdeer say

-Jab Sakina nay tha deakha teer ko halkoom may

Kehsi aa hay sard nikhli, thi dil-e-humsheer say

-Saray lashoon ko uta laay tay sheh ran say mugar

Lashay Asghar ut nahi pathi hay kuin Shabbir say

-Khas yay Ashhar pay fazal-e-Asghar-e-Masoom hay

Iss liyay alfaz miltay hain dar-e-baysheer say
