1
Khudi Ka Sir-e-Nihaan La ilaha ilallah.
Khudi Hai Taig-e-Fasaan La ilaha ilallah.
Yeh Door Apne Barhim Ki Talash Mein Hai.
Sanam Kadah Hai Jahan La ilaha ilallah.
Agarche But Hain Jamaat Ki Aasteeno Mein.
Mujhe Hai Hukam-e-Azaan La ilaha ilallah.
