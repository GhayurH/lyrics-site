Aj Veer Hasan De Lal Nu Phophiyaan Mehndiyan Laiyaan
Na Joray Paye Shagna De Haye Sir Wich Mitiyaan Paiyaan
Mera Veer Ameer Zamane Da Oday Lal De Kaaj Rachaye Ne
Aadam Taun Le Ke Esa Tak Sab Deen Mubarak Aaye Ne
Laiyaan Ne Hasan De Bachray Nu Arshaan Taun Mehndiyaan Aayiyaan
Main Janni Aan Teri Mehndi Ne Hun Qasim Rang Badlana Aye
Taun Gathri De Wich Chan Qasim Khaimay Wich Mur Ke Aaona Aye
Nayi Mangniyaan Bhaina Ne Qasim Tere Kolon Waag Pharaiyaan
Zahra Diyaan Jaiyaan Karbal Wich Farwa Nu Gal Nal Landiyaan Ne
Kadi Chumdiyaan Ne Sir Kubra Da Ro Ro Ke Shagan Manandiyaan Ne
Aj Wela Aye Loko Shagna Da Kal Toorna Aan Sipahiyaan
Ghorey De Summa Naal Asaan Phul Wekhney Qasim Sehrey De
Kainat De Malik Chunnay Ne Tukray Tan Athar Tere De
Lag Nazar Gayi Aye Khushiyaan Nu Paiyaan Rowan Zahra Jaiyaan
Roway NAJFI Mehndi Rul Gayi Aye Rul Gaye Qasim Tere Sehrey Ne
Jiwein Noor Di Tasbeeh Tutdi Aye Aynj Tukray Ho Gaye Tere Ne
Jholi Wich Chayi Lash Teri Sab Lashaan Kand Te Chayiyaan
