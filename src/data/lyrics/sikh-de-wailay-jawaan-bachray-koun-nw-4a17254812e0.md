Akbar, Mera Seheriyan Wala Akbar
Akbar, Mera Chotiyan Wala Akbar
Sikh De Wailay Jawaan Bachray Koun, Baghbonainday Hussain Tak Pondeh.

Inalillah Dee Sirf Ik Ayat, Paar Suendeh, Hussain Tuk Pondeh.

Dekh Tehtul Halak Peyamber Dah, Dhal Gey Hur Batool Di Chadar
Kambdeh Hath Naal Sir Batool An Dai, Buur Kheh Dendeh, Hussain Tak Pondeh.

Putreh Mehman Hove Lazai Da, Peyoo Dee Sahwaan Qyoon Deed Hut De Nai
Jug Toh Toorun Qyoon Pehleh Akbar Deh, Saag Rendeh, Hussain Tak Pondeh.

Hai Janaza Teyar Akbar Dah, Mustafa Alameen Dai Sudkay
Akhrie Waar Sonai Akbar Daa Moo Dikhen Deh Hussain Tak Pondeh.

Ai Deseven Jo Vuct Neye Melna, Peyoo Ko Ai Da Jigar Churan Ketai
Senai Barchi Lugun To Boh Pelai Hut Mulendai Hussain Tak Pondeh.

Enj Pug Putur Kown Juhan Otai, Koy Peyamber Nahey Punhda Sukeya
Mout Dey Pug Jo Hai To Hon Pug Da Lurvulendeh, Hussain Tah Pondeh.
