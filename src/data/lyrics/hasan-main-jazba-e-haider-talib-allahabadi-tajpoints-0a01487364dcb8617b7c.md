1
Hasan Main Jazba E Haider Talib Allahabadi.
Hasan Mein Jazba e Haider Dikhai Deta Hai.
Qalam Mein Taig Ka Johar Dikhai Deta Hai.
Hasan Ka Dekh Ke Ikhlaq Kehti Thi Dunya.
Hasan Mein Husan-e-Payamber Dikhai Deta Hai.
Bhala Ghataye Ga Kya Koi Martaba Oss Ka.
Jo Dosh-e-Mursal-e-Dein Par Dikhai Deta Hai.
Hai Sulha Namay Mein Jitnay Haroof Likhe Hasan.
Ameer-e-Sham Ko Lashkar Dikhai Deta Hai.
Kaha Hasan Ne Utar Yeh Jagah Hai Nana Ki.
Tu Sheikh Kyun Sar-e-Mimber Dikhai Deta Hai.
Ameer-e-Sham Hai Be Fateha Tu Dastar Khuwaan.
Mere Imam Ka Ghar Ghar Dikhai Deta Hai.
Ali Ke Lal Ka Fazal-o-Karam Hai Talib Par.
Jo Mehfilon Mein Barabar Dikhai Deta Hai.
