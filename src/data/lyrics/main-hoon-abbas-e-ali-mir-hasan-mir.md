1
Main Hoon Abbas-e-Ali Mir Hasan Mir
Main Hoon Abbas-e-Ali Main Hoon Abbas-e-Jarri
Abu Talib Ki Wafaon Ka Asasa Main Hoon
Shair-e-Yazdaan Ki Shujat Ka Khulasa Main Hoon

2
Main Akela Hoon Jahan Mein Koi Sani Na Nazeer
Meri Thokar Mein Zamana Main Ameeron Ka Ameer
Yeh Taruf Bhi Ayyaan Karta Hai Meri Tauqeer
Main Hoon Attaye Jahan Our Mera Aaqa Shabeer

3
Himat-e-Tashna Labi Jurat-e-Ahsas Hoon Main
Sari Dunya Mujhe Pehchan Le Abbas Hoon Main
Mehram-e-Raz-e-Rasool-e-Do Sara Se Pocho
Gar Yeh Uqdah Hai Kissi Uqdah Kusha Se Pocho

4
Khas Nuqta Hai Issay Nuqta-e-Ba Se Pocho
Meri Khilqat Ka Sabab Shair-e-Khuda Se Pocho
Qirat-e-Ain-e-Rasool-e-Arbi Ki Khatir
Main Tu Zindah Hoon Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Ki Khatir

5
Pyas Honton Pe Jo Rakhon Tu Tajub Kya Hai
Mur Ke Darya Ko Na Dekhon Tu Tajub Kya Hai
Aag Pani Mein Laga Doon Tu Tajub Kya Hai
Nehar Chulo Mein Sameto Tu Tajub Kya Hai

6
Guftgu Jis Pe Sada Hogi Woh Unwan Hoon Main
Kahin Nuqta Kahin Aayat Kahin Quran Hoon Main
Jane Kya Zoam Hai Inn Maut Ke Beemaron Ko
Tashna Lab Rakha Hai Kausar Ke Shehanshahoon Ko

7
Khush Hain Hathoon Mein Sanbhalay Hoye Talwaron Ko
Abhi Maloom Nahi Sham Ke Robahon Ko
Main Paharon Ki Tanabon Ko Hila Sakta Hoon
Nehar Totay Hoye Naizay Se Utha Sakta Hoon

8
Koi Samjhay Tu Zarra Misra-e-La Saif Ki Baat
Kuch Na Kuch Mujh Mein Bhi Mojood Hain Haider Ki Sifaat
Jantay Hain Woh Jinhein Yaad Hai Siffeen Ki Raat
Aik Hamla Hi Bohat Hoga Paye Qehat-e-Hayat

9
Sheh Ragein Hoon Gi Juda Sar Ko Badan Tarsay Ga
Aaj Pani Nahi Barsay Ga Lahu Barsay Ga
Hai Mere Waar Ki Raftar Howa Ko Maloom
Khadma Taig-e-Ali Hai Mere Hamlay Makhdoom

10
Sabhi Jurat Ki Safon Mein Hai Azal Se Meri Dhoom
Jism Jab Hone Lagin Gay Apni Jaan Se Mehroom
Har Shujat Ke Dhani Ibn-e-Wali Aaye Hain
Tum Yeh Kehte Hoye Bhago Gay Ali Aaye Hain

11
Mere Sarkar Agar Hukam Mujhe Farmaein
Dosri Sans Bhi Ashrar Na Lainay Paein
Aik Hamlay Mein Nishanat-e-Jahan Mit Jaein
Zalzalay Youn Mere Qadmo Ki Dhamak Se Aaein

12
Kanp Uthay Sari Zameen Takht-e-Umayyah Totay
Main Rakhon Ran Mein Qadam Our Dar-e-Kofa Totay
Apne Dil Mein Hai Jawaan Fatah-e-Khaybar Ki Umang
Sang Ko Moom Karein Hum Tu Kabhi Moom Ko Sang

13
Zindagi Lamhon Mein Be Deno Pe Ho Jaye Gi Tang
Bhool Jao Gaye Abhi Rustam-o-Sohrab Ki Jang
Hum Jo Talwar Ke Qabzey Mein Kalai Dein Gay
Inn Ke Ajdad Bhi Qabron Se Duhai Dein Gay
