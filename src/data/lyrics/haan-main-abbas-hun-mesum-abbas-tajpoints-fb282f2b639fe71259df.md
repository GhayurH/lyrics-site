1
Hasab Nasab Mein Hoon Lasani.
Mashk-o-Alam Hain Meri Nishani.
Hai Mere Shano Mein Bhi Qowat-e-Yazdani.
Agar Karon Darya Ko Ishara.

2
Khaimon Mein Aa Jaye Woh Sara.
Mere Isharon Par Chalta Hai Darya Ka Pani.
Lo Jan Lo Mujh Ko, Pehchan Lo Mujh Ko.
Haan Main Abbas Hoon, Abbas.

3
Ayyo Hannas, Ayyo Hannas.
Sun Lo Yeh Taruf Mera.
Main Beta Hoon Haider Ka.
Saqa-e-Sakina Mujh Ko.

4
Kehti Hai Yeh Sari Dunya.
Main Bazu-e-Sarwar Hoon.
Ghairat Ka Samandar Hoon.
Khud Zaat Mein Lashkar Hoon.

5
Jab Ghaiz Mein Waar Karon Ga.
Aisi Yalgar Karon Ga.
Ik Lash Ke Do Do Tukray.
Phir Do Ke Chaar Karon Ga.

6
Mohlat Hai Sanbhal Jao.
Kasrat Pe Na Itrao.
Mat Shair Se Takrao.
Salar Hoon, Jarrar Hoon, Jeedar Hoon Main.

7
Haan Main Abbas Hoon, Abbas.
Main Ne Jo Khat Khainch Diya Hai Paar Karo Tu Jano.
Mujh Se Aa Kar Aankh Milao Phir Main Tum Ko Mano.
Sabaq Wohi Siffeen Ka Tum Ko Phir Se Yaad Dilaon.

8
Palat Ke Dharti, Ulat Ke Darya Tum Ko Ghaiz Dikhaon.
Yeh Taig Yeh Khanjar Kya Hai.
Bas Dekh Lo Tewar Kya Hai.
Pairon Se Masal Doon Dharti.

9
Nu Lakh Ka Lashkar Kya Hai.
Sar Tan Se Ura Doon Ga.
Mati Mein Mila Doon Ga.
Ik Hashar Macha Doon Ga.

10
Main Cheen Ke Tum Se Darya.
Bhar Loon Ga Abhi Mashkeza.
Tum Dekhtey Reh Jao Gay.
Na Rook Sako Gay Rasta.

11
Jo Keh Doon Woh Karta Hoon.
Kab Maut Se Main Darta Hoon.
Har Bar Yehi Kehta Hoon.
Jab Mashk Chidi Pyason Ki.

12
Our Beh Gaya Sara Pani.
Ghazi Ne Kata Ke Bazu.
Tareekh Wafa Ki Likh Di.
Shabbir Jo Lashay Par.

13
Pohanchay Tu Suna Aa Kar.
Jumley They Yehi Lab Par.
Ik Aankh Mein Teer Laga Tha.
Shabbir Ne Jab Woh Nikala.

14
Zanu Pe Rakha Jab Sar Ko.
Ghazi Ne Kiya Yeh Noha.
Aankhon Mein Sakina Ka.
Chehra Hai Mere Aaqa.

15
Ab Kaisay Yeh Bolon Ga.
Woh Shair Wafa Ka Paikar.
Jab Qatal Howa Darya Par.
Karti Thin Tarap Kar Zainab.

16
Yeh Bain Hi Meesum-o- Mazhar.
Ho Jaon Gi Jab Tanha.
Tab Khon Rulaye Ga.
Aye Bhai Tera Kehna.
