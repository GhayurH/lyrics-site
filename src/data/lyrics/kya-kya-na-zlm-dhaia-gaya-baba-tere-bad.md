Sayyada taahera raaziya marziya
Aey ummey abeeha,yaa fatema zehra

1
Kya kya na zulm dhaaya gaya baba terey baad,
Darbaar me bulaaya gaya baba terey baad.

2
1. Baba sitam ki intehaa mujhpar hi hogayi,
Baba teri judaayi me jee bhar na ro sakii,
Mujhko bohat sataaya gaya, baba terey baad...
Kya kya na zulm...

3
2. Ummat ney sab bhulaadiye ehsaan rasool key,
Taazeem kar sakey na ye dar ki batool key,
Is ghar ka dar jalaaya.gaya, baba terey baad...
Kya kya na zulm...

4
3. Cheena hai gaasibo ney fidak mujhsey bey sabab,
Haq maangney gayi thi adaalat me baba jab,
Mujhse gawah mangaaya gaya, baba terey baad...
Kya kya na zulm...

5
4. Wo zulm dhaaye mujhpe karoon ab bayaan kya,
Raushan dino pe padhtey agar hotey wo siyaah,
Mujhko bohat rulaaya gaya, baba terey baad...
Kya kya na zulm...

6
5. Mohsin shaheed hogaya zaalim ney kee jafaa,
Pehlu pe merey ghar ka jo darwaaza hai giraa,
Ye zulm mujhpe dhaaya gaya, baba terey baad...
Kya kya na zulm...

7
6. Sehmey huwey hai ghar me hussain aur mera hasan,
Mushkil kushaa ke dekhiye gardan me hai rasan,
Mujhko ye din dikhaaya gaya, baba terey baad...
Kya kya na zulm...

8
7. Bey deen ko dikhaayi sanad jab usool ki,
Haatho me lekey phaad dii aur na qubool kii,
Hukm e nabi bhulaaya gaya, baba terey baad...
Kya kya na zulm...

9
8. Ummat ney hum ko ajr e risaalat hai ye diyaa,
Ghar cheen hai ali se sitam mujhpe hai kiyaa,
Dil mera hai dukhaaya gaya, baba terey baad...
Kya kya na zulm...

10
9. Ye daastaa sitam ki ali deep ney likhii,
Dunya ke saamney ye haqeeqat bayaan kii,
Ye bayn jo sunaaya gaya, baba terey baad...
Kya kya zulm...
