Mashhad Mein Ye Chokhat Chom Kar.

1
Yeh Chokhat Chom Kar Ki Hai Dua Har Bar Mashhad Mein.
Elahi Thehar Jaye Waqt Ki Raftar Mashhad Mein.
Janazay Tu Bohat Aate Hoye Dekhey Magar Main Ne.
Nahi Dekhey Kahin Bhi Maut Ke Aasaar Mashhad Mein.
Suna Hai Khud Shifa Deney Ko Aate Hain Wahan Maula.
So Ab Khud Ja Ke Main Ho Jaon Ga Beemar Mashhad Mein.

2
Raza Ke Husan Par Yousuf Ko Biktey Dekhti Dunya.
Agar Hota Kahin Jo Misar Ka Bazar Mashhad Mein.
Suna Kar Manqabat Maula Ki Main Unn Se Dua Loon Ga.
Milein Gay Jab Kabhi Bhi Meesum-e-Tammar Mashhad Mein.
Woh Chahe Ghar Ki Sorat Ho Ya Chahe Qabar Ki Sorat.
Khudawanda Bana De Mir Ka Ghar Bar Mashhad Mein.
