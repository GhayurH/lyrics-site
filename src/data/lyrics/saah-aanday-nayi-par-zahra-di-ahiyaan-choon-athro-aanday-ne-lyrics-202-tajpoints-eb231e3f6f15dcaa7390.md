1
Saah Aanday Nayi Par Zahra Di Ahiyaan Choon Athro Aanday Ne.
Jadon Haider Zakhmi Pehlu Choon Haye Pehlu Choon Hath Zahra Da Haye Chanday Ne.
Haye Baad Qaza Zahra Aakhay Mainu Moaf Karein Sartaj Mera.
Main Mardi Na Te Ki Kar Di Haye Ki Kardi Aye Jhoot Di Tohmat Landay Ne.

2
Haye Khak Nasheen Hoye Haider Ujari Nu Wekh Ke Dardaan Wich.
Haye Maran Taun Baad V Zahra De Haye Zahra De Rowan De Hokay Aanday Ne.
Pehlu Nu Wekh Jay Lainday Te Mar Jana Mumkin Hona Si.
Haye Khon Bhari Tasbeeh Maa Di Tasbeeh Maa Di Gash Wekh Ke Baal Paye Khanday Ne.

3
Amari De Phar Ke Pairaan Nu Shabbir Kehway Aye Ro Ro Ke.
Aj Samajh Gaya Waan Main Amari Haye Main Amari Kiwein Jondiyaan Jee Mar Janday Ne.
Haye Joun Jehray V Zahra De Dushman Nu Rehbar Manday Ne.
Oh Loag Aye Maula Mehdi Nu Haye Mehdi Nu Wich Ghaibat Khon Rawanday Ne.
