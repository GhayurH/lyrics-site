Sakina Haye Qaid Muk Gayi Tauq Leh Gaye Bemar De Asghar Khan.
Teri Turbat Te Sakina Aye Sab Qaidi Baad Rehaiyaan Ronday Te Naal Pukarday.

1
Main Banaya Aye Bara Okha Ro Aakhay Zainab Mar Na Jaway Kidhray.
Teri Turbat Te Lahu Ronda Aye Kar Athro Saaf Sakina Uth Phophiyaan De Ghamkhuwar De.
Tainu Zindaan De Haneray Wich Sakina Kaliyaan Veer Chaliya Chad Ke.
Aina Sochaan Wich Chup Chaap Khara Aynj Roya Pak Muhari Wal Khul Gaye Ne Dastar De.

2
Aakhay Ghazi Nu Tere Baad Shimar Ne Mainu Jo Tamachay Marey.
Rooz-e-Mehshar Jadon Aawaan Gi Main Fair Wekhin Chacha Ghazi Aye Neel Mere Rukhsar De.
Tainu Ghaibat Che Sakina Di Aseeri Di Qasam Pak Maula Mehdi.
Ro Ro Nadir Aye Duawaan Mangda Dunya Te Khuliyan Akhiyaan Nal Mainu V Dedar De.
