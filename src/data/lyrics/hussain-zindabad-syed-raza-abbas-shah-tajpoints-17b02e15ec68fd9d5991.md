1
Hai Karbala Ko Basaya Hussain Zindah Aabad
Mualla Iss Ko Banaya Hussain Zindah Aabad
Na Kar Sakay Koi Mursal Woh Kam Tu Ne Kiya
Sar Anbiya Ne Jhukaya Hussain Zindah Aabad
Na Apne Sar Ko Jhukaya Kissi Ki Baiyat Par
Khuda Ki Rah Mein Kataya Hussain Zindah Aabad

2
Na Bujh Saka Kissi Zulmat Ki Taiz Aandhi Se
Charag Aisa Jalaya Hussain Zindah Aabad
Hain Kehte Sare Mazahib Hussain Sab Ka Hai
Hai Har Zaban Pe Aaya Hussain Zindah Aabad
Main Teri Zaat Se Razi Hoon Laut Ke Aa Ja
Tujhey Khuda Ne Bulaya Hussain Zindah Aabad

3
Zawwar Karne Zawwari Hain Karbala Jate
Unhein Hai Rab Se Milaya Hussain Zindah Aabad
Usool e Deen Ki Khatir Lutaya Ghar Apna
Hai Haq Ka Rasta Dikhaya Hussain Zindah Aabad
Quran Hum Se Our Hum Quran Ke Mazhar
Sina Pe Parh Ke Sunaya Hussain Zindah Aabad
