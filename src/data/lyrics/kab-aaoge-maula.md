1
Kab Aaoge Maula.
Jagay Howay Fitno Ko Solane Chale Aao.
Ab Beet Gaye Kitne Zamane Chale Aao.
Giyarah So Baras Kaise Guzaray Mere Aaqa.
Ghibat Mein Jo Guzri Hai Sunane Chale Aao.
Jab Aana Ho Aa Jana Magar Khuwab Mein Mere.
Kab Aao Gaye Mula Yeh Batane Chale Aao.
Yeh Matami Hai Yeh Hai Namazi Yeh Koi Our.
In Sari Faseelon Ko Girane Chale Aao.
Ho Qadir-e-Mutliq Ki Tumhi Aakhri Hujjat.
Majboron Ko Mukhtar Banane Chale Aao.
Har Samt Rawaan Hai Abu Sufyan Ka Sikka.
Sikka Abu Talib Ka Chalane Chale Aao.
Ghazi Ka Alam Aapke Lashkar Ka Nishan Hai.
Kabay Pe Issay Mula Sajane Chale Aao.
Arman Hai Ghibat Se Sadda Aaye Keh.
:SIBT-E-JAFAR: Zara Ashar Sunane Chale Aao.
