1
Jab Aaya-e-Baligh Tere Tewar Nazar Aaye.
Dunya Mein Ali Sab Se Qad Aawar Nazar Aaye.
Soraj Ki Kahin Dhop Kahin Ishq Ka Saya.
Ik Waqt Mein Do Tarha Ke Manzar Nazar Aaye.

2
Ik Noor Ke Do Tukray They Aaina-e-Khum Mein.
Jis Rukh Se Bhi Dekha Woh Barabar Nazar Aaye.
Ayelan Wilayat Ka Hazaron Ne Suna Tha.
Mangi Jo Shahadat Tu Bahatar Nazar Aaye.

3
Hathoon Se Mere Choot Gaya Saghar-e-Kausar.
Kausar Pe Jo Khud Saqi-e-Kausar Nazar Aaye.
ALLAH Re Yeh Mojza-e-Noor-e-Wilayat.
Andhon Ko Bhi Maidan Mein Haider Nazar Aaye.

4
Nabiyon Ko Jahan Dhond Raha Tha Main Wahan Par.
Fizza Nazar Aayi Kabhi Qanbar Nazar Aaye.
Keh Dena Keh Behlool Hai Yeh:MIR HASAN: Bhi.
Dunya Jo Uthaye Hoye Pathar Nazar Aaye.
