MANTA MAN MAN HAARI AKBAR TU NA AAYO

Main sadqay main vaari, Akbar tu na aayo.

Kee jeena behna da baaj bhiraavan day

Teri rul gayee behan piyaari, Akbar tu na aayo.

Nanay dai rozai tai jaa kai raat dinnay

May Ro ro araz guzaari, Akbar tu na aayo.

Khabaan dai vich vaikhaan dohri jhoolay di

Tera jhoola reh gaiya khaali, Asghar tu na aayo.
