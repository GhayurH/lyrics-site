Hussain Tha Hussain Hai Shahzad Abbas.

1
Haan Hussain Tha Hussain Hai.
Jab Se Hai Khuda Hussain Hai.
Raz-e-Kainat Likhna Tha.
Main Ne Likh Diya Hussain Hai.
Na Koi Hai Dosra Khuda.
Na Hi Dosra Hussain Hai.

2
Keh Rahe Hain Mosa Toor Se.
Noor-e-Fatima Hussain Hai.
Kyun Garor Na Karey Khuda.
Oss Ko Pojta Hussain Hai.
Arsh Kehte Hain Jisay Yeh Loag.
Ghar Woh Aap Ka Hussain Hai.

3
Woh Hi Tu Hai Lehja-e-Khuda.
Jis Mein Bolta Hussain Hai.
Cho Ke Hur Ko Pak Kar Diya.
Kyunke Innama Hussain Hai.
Hai Asad Ko Jis Ka Intezar.
Bas Woh Waqt Ka Hussain Hai.
