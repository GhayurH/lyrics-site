1
Hakim e Waqt Agar Sahib e Emaan Hota Hasan Sadiq.
Ghar Nabi Zadi Ka Aisay Tu Na Veraan Hota.
De Ke Sar Sajday Mein Di Shah Ne Sajday Ko Baqa.
Warna Masjood Zamane Mein Na Yazdaan Hota.

2
Bazu-e-Hazrat-e-Abbas Na Kattay Ban Mein.
Chup Hi Rehne Ka Na Gar Bhai Ka Farmaan Hota.
Sar Ko Mazloom Katatay Na Agar Sajday Mein.
Na Namazein Na Yeh Rozay Na Yeh Quraan Hota.

3
Ghar Ki Dehleez Bhi Apni Na Mukamal Dekhi.
Kash Zainab Ke Naseebon Mein Na Zindaan Hota.
Younhi Darbar Mein Rehti Na Khari Bint-e-Ali.
Gar Ameer Inn Mein Sahi Koi Musalmaan Hota.
