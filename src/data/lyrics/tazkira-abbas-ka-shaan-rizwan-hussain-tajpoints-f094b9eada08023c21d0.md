Jashan Mein Karna Tha Mujh Ko Tazkira Abbas Ka.
De Gaye Jibreel Quran-e-Sana Abbas Ka.

1
Iss Ko Kehte Hain Wafa Keh Aaj Bhi Shabbir Ke.
Dekh Lein Rozay Se Hai Roza Jura Abbas Ka.
Turbat-e-Abbas Se Lapeti Hoi Hai Iss Tarha.
Jaisay Chullo Dhondti Ho Alqama Abbas Ka.

2
Kyun Na Ho Har Ik Nabi Ke Lab Pe Phir Sallay Ala.
Martaba Batla Rahi Hain Fatima Abbas Ka.
Taziya Shabbir Ka Umm Ul-Baneen Le Aaein Sath.
Jab Alam Mehshar Mein Laein Fatima Abbas Ka.

3
Abarha Koi Gira Sakta Nahi Kabay Yeh Do.
Hai Ghadeer Our Karbala Ko Aasra Abbas Ka.
Dekh Kar Apni Jabeen Romal-e-Zahra Mein Bandhi.
Karta Tu Ho Ga Ada Hur Shukriya Abbas Ka.

4
Mas Paron Ko Karne Aaye Aasmano Se Malak.
Majlis-e-Sheh Mein Jonhi Parcham Khula Abbas Ka.
Koi Tu Hai Raaz Keh Janat Se Mangwaya Nahi.
Hathoon Se Zahra Ne Khud Kurta Siya Abbas Ka.

5
Aik Din Tu Dekhein Gay Kabay Ki Chat Par Hum Alam.
Our Haram Mein Ik Taraf Sehan-e-Wafa Abbas Ka.
Mere Ghar Aa Hi Nahi Sakti Balaein Ab Asad.
Naam Darwazay Pe Main Ne Likh Diya Abbas Ka.
