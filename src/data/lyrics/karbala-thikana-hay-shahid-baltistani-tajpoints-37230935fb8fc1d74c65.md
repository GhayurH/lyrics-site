Karbala Thikana Hay Shahid Baltistani.

1
Hamara Khuld Nahi Karbala Thikana Hai.
Jahan Se Aaye Hain Wapis Wahin Pe Jana Hai.
Yeh Faisla Abhi Kar Le Kahan Pe Jana Hai.
Idhar Imam-e-Zamana Udhar Zamana Hai.
Jab Aayi Teesri Shaban Bool Uthay Aanso.
Aye Roney Walo Tumhein Aaj Muskurana Hai.

2
Bas Itna Farq Hai Kabay Mein Our Mere Dil Mein.
Hussain Ka Yeh Niya Ghar Hai Woh Porana Hai.
Nabi Bhi Betay Dila Saktay Hain Khuda Se Magar.
Hussain Kya Hai Zamane Ko Yeh Batana Hai.
Zamana Samjha Nahi Maslak-e-Wafadari.
Alam Uthane Ka Matlab Half Uthana Hai.

3
Yeh Loag Hum Pe Na Hujjat They Our Na Hoon Gay Kabhi.
Muqassaron Munafiqon Se Aqeeday Ko Kya Chupana Hai.
Aye Tang Dast Azadar Dil Na Chota Kar.
Teri Tu Aankh Mein Shabeer Ka Khazana Hai.
Main Apna Haq Bhi Takallum Ossi Se Mangoon Ga.
Woh Jis Imam Ko Zahra Ka Haq Dilana Hai.
