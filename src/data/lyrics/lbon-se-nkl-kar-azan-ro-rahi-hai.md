Labon say nikal kar azaan ro rahi hay
Musallay pay Akbar ki maa ro rahi hay

1
1) Abhi to gaiya hi nahi ran main Akbar
Abhi dil hai Akbar ka seenay kay andar
Magaar phir bhi nok-e-sina ro rahi hay

2
2) Hai nazaon kay paalay zara sa tehar ja
Kay Zainab sajalay teray saar pah sehra
Kai Awn-o-Mohammad ki maa ro rahi hay

3
3) Wahan paar Medinay main roti hai Sughra
Yahan paar Sakina kay laab paar hay noha
Behan ik wahan ik yahan ro rahi hay
