Mujh ko maloom hai Abid peh qayamat guzri

1
Bharay bazaar say,  jab Malika-e-Ghairat guzri

2
1) Mola Shabbir say Sajjad talak atay howay
Khoon may doobha kay,  maqtal seh Imamat guzri

3
2) Qari Shabbir zamanay may baray dekhay hain
Nok-e-naiza peh,  bus teri talawat guzri

4
3) Zarf-e-qatil ko parakhnay kay liye Asghar tha
Kisi Masoom ki,  aisi na shahadat guzri

5
4) Har bashar roya hay ik jaisa bharay aalam may
Is kay ashko say,  duniya may nah rangat guzri

6
5) Gaye bazaar main Zainab kuch istara jaisa
Haya kay kafan main,  lipati howi mayaat guzri

7
6) Jiskay parday ka nigehban tha Abbas-e-jaari
Kaisay darbaaro seh,  buniyad sharafat guzri

8
7) Dil phata khoon jigaar cheer peh aansu hai bana
Haey Sajaad peh,  kya kya na museebat guzri

9
8) Jab giray ghaash seh toh beemar giray sajday may
Bhari sajdou seh,  Sajaad ki fidaraat guzri

10
9) Choomta rehta hay Adil to unhi raahon ko
Mola Abid ki,  jis raah seh ziarat guzri
