Lo Musalmano khamosh ho gayeen Zehra
Zakhm pehlo main liye,  aaj soo gayi Zehra

1
1) Jiskay ronay ki sadaoon say tarap jatay thay
Sun kay faryad Ali Pak chalay aatay thay
Moat kay gehray andharon may,  kho gayi Zehra

2
2) Log Yasrab kay baray chain say abh souein gay
Meri makhdoma kay masoom sabhi rooein gay
Is qadar baba kay sadmon may,  roo gayi Zehra

3
3) Ab to aahoon ki na awaaz-e-bukan aaye gi
Zakhm pehlo ka Payambar ko woh dikhlaye gi
Ashk aankhon may liye,  lo gayi Zehra

4
4) Sar-e-darbar gawahon ko bhi jhutlaya hay
Badh baba kay musalmano nay tarpaya hay
Ghum zada bar sar-e-darbar,  ho gayi Zehra

5
5) Chup na paey ga zamanay say kabhi ta mehshaar
Bharay darbar-e-sitamgar may khutba day kaar
Dagh hizyan ki tuhmat ka,  dho gayi Zehra
