Yeh Manqabat Ke Rang Mein Naat-e-Hazoor Hai
Kaisay Na Ho Gi Naat Ke Lehjay Mein Manqabat
Humshakal-e-Mustafa Ka Jahan Mein Zahoor Hai
Aamad Hai Ibn-e-Fatah-e-Badar-o-Hunain Ki
Shohrat Issi Khabar Ki Bohat Door Door Hai
Unwan Sulha Ka Hai Pa Anjam-e-Meer-e-Sham
Tehreer Sulha Namay Mein Bain Al-Stoor Hai
Kafi Hai Bas Kitab-e-Khuda Panjtan Hain Kya
Azhan e Kam Nasab Mein Yehi Tu Fatoor Hai
Jis Dil Mein Hub-e-Aal-e-Muhammad Na Mil Sakay
Pehchan Lo Nasab Mein Kharabi Zaror Hai
Jo Kuch Mila Hai Woh Dar-e-Aal-e-Rasool Se
Yeh Shayari Diya Howa Kis Ka Shaoor Hai
REHAN Madah Khuwan Ali-o-Batool Hoon
Qalb-o-Nazar Ko Iss Liye Hasil Saror Hai
