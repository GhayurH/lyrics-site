1
Wahin Pe Arsh-e-Mualla Dikhai Deta Hai.
Hai Jis Qadar Bhi Aqeedey Ka Rizq Jis Ke Pass.
Osse Hussain Bhi Utna Dikhai Deta Hai.
Khilaf-e-Ghaibat-e-Mehdi Ke Bolne Wale.
Mujhe Bata Tujhe Allah Dikhai Deta Hai.
Rasool Sajday Mein Hain Our Pusht Par Shabir.
Sakhi Ka Sajdon Pe Qabza Dikhai Deta Hai.
Bataon Kis Tarha Alfaz Mere Pass Nahi.
Dar-e-Hussain Pe Kya Kya Dikhai Deta Hai.
Ali Ka Naam Jo Leta Hoon Barsar-e-Mimber.
Tu Khul Ke Chehron Pe Shajra Dikhai Deta Hai.
Mere Hussain Se Jo Shaks Ho Giya Mansoob.
Woh Har Zamane Mein Alla Dikhai Deta Hai.
Qasam Khuda Ki Wafaon Ka Hai Khuda Abbas.
Utar Ke Pani Mein Piyasa Dikhai Deta Hai.
Dar-e-Hussain Pe Jhukne Ka Hai Sabab Raqib.
Jo Tere Naam Ka Charcha Dikhai Deta Hai.
