1
Phir Baqaiya Ko Hum Banaeingay Mir Hasan Mir.
Jab Imam-e-Zamana Aaein Gaye.
Qabar Zahra Ki Ho Gi Jab Tameer.
Ambiya Sab Bulaye Jaein Gaye.

2
Sehan-e-Ummul Baneen Ke Andar.
Pani Pyason Ko Hum Pilaein Gaye.
Qabar-e-Beemar-e-Karbala Ke Qareeb.
Aik Sehan-e-Shifa Banaein Gaye.

3
Ishq-e-Baqir Mein Our Sadiq Mein.
Aik Dar-al-Qalam Banaein Gaye.
Yaad-e-Qasim Mein Qabar-e-Shabar Par.
Aansoon Ke Diye Jalaein Gaye.

4
Hum Baqaiya Mein Misal-e-Bab-e-Jawad.
Barah Baab-e-Karam Banaein Gaye.
Apne Mula Hassan Ka Dastar Khuwan.
Zairon Ke Liye Bichaein Gaye.

5
Qabar-e-Zahra Pe Parcham-e-Abbas.
Khud Imam-e-Zamaan Lagaein Gaye.
Haan Baqaiya Mein Phir Takallum-o- Shamas.
Kafsh Bardari Karne Jaein Gaye.
