Aye Karbala Aye Karbala Mir Hasan Mir.
Aye Karbala Aye Karbala Aye Karbala Aye Karbala.

1
Qatal Hoi Aal-e-Nabi Ghar Ujar Gaya.
Haye Tere Samne Darya Ke Kinaray.
Pyasay Rahe Saqi-e-Kausar Ke Dularay.
Khak Mein Poshida Hoye Arsh Ke Taray.

2
Roth Gaye Zainab-e-Muztar Ke Saharey.
:SIBT-E-: Rasool-e-Dosara Qatal Ho Gaya.
Maa Ko Tha Arman Bohat Shadi Ka Jiski.
Raat Ko Jis Ki Sar-e-Maqtal Hoi Shadi.

3
Subha Ko Mara Gaya Woh Shair-e-Jarri Bhi.
Shab Ko Bani Thi Jo Dulhan Bewah Ho Gayi.
Dasht Mein Pamal Howa Dulha Ka Lasha.
Parcham-e-Abbas-e-Jarri Khon Mein Doba.

4
Kat Gaye Oss Shair Ke Bazu Lab-e-Darya.
Pyasa Hi Mara Gaya Saqa-e-Sakina.
Toot Gayi Sheh Ki Kamar Wa Musibata.
Sheh Ne Kaha Haye Mera Shair Mar Gaya.

5
Zakhmi Howa Akbar-e-Zee Jah Ka Seena.
Barchiyaan Paiwast Hoin Garh Gaya Naiza.
Koi Bhi Oss Waqt Madadgar Nahi Tha.
Baap Ne Khud Betay Ke Lashay Ko Uthaya.

6
Haye Kissi Ne Na Diya Sheh Ko Sahara.
Loriyaan Raton Ko Sunati Thi Jisay Maa.
Gaud Ke Jholay Mein Jhulati Thi Jisay Maa.
Dulha Tasawar Mein Banati Thi Jisay Maa.

7
Shair-e-Jarri Keh Ke Bulati Thi Jisay Maa.
Maa Se Juda Haye Woh Shehzada Ho Gaya.
Hashmi Sheron Ka Lahu Beh Gaya Ran Mein.
Lut Gayin Konain Ki Shehzadiyaan Bn Mein.

8
Aag Lagai Gayi Zahra Ke Chaman Mein.
Jakra Gaya Haye Yateemo Ko Rasan Mein.
Kitne Hoye Zulm-o-Sitam Iss Qadar Jafa.
Aaj Bhi Hai Waqt Ke Gohar Yehi Nalay.

9
Raakh Hoye Jal Gaye Sadaat Ke Khaimay.
Marey Gaye Bali Sakina Ke Tamachay.
Qaid Hoye Syed-e-Konain Ke Pyaray.
Ho Gayi Maqtal Mein Nabi Zadi Be Rida.
