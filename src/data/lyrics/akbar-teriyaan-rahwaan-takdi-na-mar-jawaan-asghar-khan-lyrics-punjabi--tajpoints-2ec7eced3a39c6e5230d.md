Akbar Teriyaan Rahwaan Takdi Na Mar Jawaan Asghar Khan.
Teri Aas De Deway Balay Ne Nanay De Rozay Ja Ke Main Mangiyaan Veer Duawaan.

1
Chali Gharaan Diyaan Main Kunjiyaan Kiwein Sanbhalan.
Teri Pak Haweli Rondi Aye Akbar Tera Naa Le Le Ke Jadon Kholni Aan Main Banwaan.
Qasad Choon Bhaij Ya Akbar Tu Aap Aa Ja.
Teri Shadi De Layi Shagna De Saman Sajay Ne Main Tainu Kiwein Veer Pohanchawan.

2
Takni Aan Khuwab Jad V Ro Paindiyan Ne Akhiyaan.
Mainu Baba Ayho Kainda Aye Hath Dil Te Rakh Le Sughra Barchi Di Gal Aye Sunawaan.
Tur Gayiyaan Naal Tere Khushiyaan Beemar Diyaan.
Tere Bajoon Aina Hasiyan Ne Kadi Shakal Wakhai Nayi Main Raat Diney Kurlawan.

3
Main Jan Gayi Aan Veera Kehri Gal Taun Almaan Wala.
Mere Naa Nu Lekan La Ke Karbala Taun Jandi Wari Bharda Si Ro Ke Hawaan.
Mangda Samar Duawaan Bibi Qabool Kar Le.
Akbar Tere Tutiyan Sehriyaan Da Sadqa Tere Nohay Parhda Kadi Shehar Tere V Aawaan.
