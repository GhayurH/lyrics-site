1
Khadim E Hussain Wajhi Hasan Zaidi.
Ashiqon Ke Dil Ki Hai Dua.
Be Haq e Fatima Ya Shah-e-Karbala.
Mere Ishq Ka Yehi Sila Do Ya Hussain.
Khadam-e-Haram Mujhe Bana Do Ya Hussain.
Lagta Hai Keh Mit Gaye Jahan Ke Sare Gham.
Karbala Mein Aa Ke Jab Bhi Rakhta Hoon Qadam.
Gham Zamane Bhar Ke Sab Bhula Do Ya Hussain.
Mere Qalb-o-Rooh Ko Bhi Chain Chahiye.
Ya Hussain Ka Sila Hussain Chahiye.
Karbala Ke Badlay Karbala Do Ya Hussain.
Jo Bhi Dekhey Woh Kahey Moazin e Haram.
Main Azaan Haram Mein Doon Karein Na Yeh Karam.
Soz Se Bhira Howa Gala Do Ya Hussain.
Jagon Main Haram Mein Our Haram Mein Soyon Main.
Misal-e-Jabir Aap Ki Lehad Ko Dhoyon Main.
Apne Dar Ki Nokari Dila Do Ya Hussain.
Unn Ki Khak-e-Pa Ko Apne Sar Pe Mein Rakhon.
Zaireen-e-Arbaeen Ki Khidmatein Karon.
Ab Ke Saal Yeh Sharf Dila Do Ya Hussain.
Khoon Ka Pursa Paish Main Kuch Iss Tarha Karon.
Na Giron Main Na Thakon Main Our Na Rukon.
Khon Mere Jism Ka Barha Do Ya Hussain.
Khuwab Roz Dekhta Hoon Syed-e-Jinna.
Saaf Kar Raha Hoon Main Haram Ki Jaliyan.
Mohar Mere Khuwab Pe Laga Do Ya Hussain.
Keh Raha Hai Yeh Raza Bhi Our Wajih Bhi.
Raas Hum Ko Hai Tumhare Dar Ki Nokari.
Bheek Hum Ko Behar-e-Fatima Do Ya Hussain.
