1
Itni Ghareeb Ho Gayi Nadeem Sarwar.
Itni Ghareeb Ho Gayi Gham Youn Lipat Gaye.
Zainab Ke Ik Libas Mein Do Saal Kat Gaye.
Kya Kya Guzar Gayi Hai Muharram Ke Chand Mein.
Syedaniyon Ke Bakht Achanak Ulat Gaye.
Zainab Ka Dil Bhi Kya Tha Ghamo Ki Kitab Thi.
Ik Dil Mein Oss Ghareeb Ke So Gham Simat Gaye.
Hathoon Se Maa Ke Khaimay Ka Pardah Ulat Gaya.
Hathoon Pe Sheh Ke Jis Ghari Asghar Ulat Gaye.
Bolay Hussain Beti Se Pani Na Mangna.
Jin Bazuon Pe Mashk Thi Woh Bazu Kat Gaye.
Marne Chaley Hussain Tu Rahwar Ruk Gaya.
Do Nanhay Nanhay Hath Summo Se Lipat Gaye.
Kaisay Utha Sakein Gay Bhala Lash Ko Hussain.
Aaza Hasan Ke Lal Ke Tukron Mein Bat Gaye.
