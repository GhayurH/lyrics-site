Sadaat hai pardes hai aur sham-e-ghariban

kya waqt hai ghazi nahi zainab hai nigehban

ay raat andheron ko zara aur badha de

maqtal ki hawa jalte charaghon ko bujha de

dekhe na alamdar behan ko sare uryan

sadaat hai…

bibi koi jhoole ki jali raakh utha kar

kehti hai agar tu nahi aaya ali asghar

khud ran may chali aayegi baysheer teri maa

sadaat hai…

tu hi na raha jee ke karoo kya mere bache

khud apne hi haathon se meri qabr banade

laila pe ye hoga ali akbar tera ehsaan

sadaat hai…

kehte hai ye jalte huwe qaimo may musallay

sajjad tahajjud to zara chain se padlay

afsos ye mohlat bhi nahi dete musalman

sadaat hai…

darya pe tadapta hai alamdar ka laasha

maqtal may ajab haal se aati hai sakina

qaimo ka dhuan aankhon may jalta hua daaman

sadaat hai…

wo waqt takallum tha ajab binte ali par

kehne lagay maula jo naqaab apni utha kar

mai baap hoo zainab mujhe pehchan meri jaan

sadaat hai…
