Ya Rab Koyi Masooma, Zindan Main Na Tanha Ho.

Paband Naho Aaain  Ronay Pai Na Pehra Ho.

Thi Jis Ko Neend Aati, Shabbir Kai Seenai Paar

Zinda Dar-e-Zinda  Uskai Liyai Kazaa Ho.

Zindanon Sai Aati Thi, Awaaz Sisaknai Ki

Jaisai Kai Sakina Ko  Haar Zindaan Ro Raha Ho.

Takrai Na Woh Kyun Kaar, Zindan Ki Deewaron Sai

Bhayi Sai Aur Phuphi Sai  Kisko Juda Kiya Ho.

Zindan Main Ai Najafi, Yad Aayi Sakina Ki

Dil Ro Ro Keh Raha Hai  Abh Na Ujala Ho.
