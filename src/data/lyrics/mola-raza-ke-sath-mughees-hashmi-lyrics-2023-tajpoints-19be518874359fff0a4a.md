Mola Raza Ke Sath Mughees Hashmi

1
Muddat Se Urta Phir Raha Tha Main Hawa Ke Sath.
Taqdeer Ne Mila Diya Maula Raza Ke Sath.
Rukhsar Rakh Ke Rozay Ki Jali Pe Ro Para.
Jaisay Keh Hum Kalam Howa Hoon Khuda Ke Sath.
Main Aap Tere Noor Ke Rakhon Ga Chodah Naam.
Khaliq Ne Yehi Tey Kiya Tha Mustafa Ke Sath.

2
Mashhad Ko Aik Our Najaf Kar Gaye Raza.
Our Qum Madina Ho Gaya Hai Fatima Ke Sath.
Arsh-e-Olla Se Khana e Kaba Ka Hai Jo Rabt.
Mashhad Ka Hai Woh Rabta Karb-o-Bala Ke Sath.
Aye Kash Main Bhi Ja Sakoon Mashhad Issi Tarha.
Jaisay Safar Mein Rehtey Hain Patay Hawa Ke Sath.

3
Maroof Hat Rahi Hai Samandar Ki Mauj Mauj.
Nalain Kis Ki Laya Hai Sar Par Utha Ke Sath.
Zawwar Hain Hamara Tu Milna Milana Hai.
Achi Chubhay Gi Khuld Mein Bhi Anbiya Ke Sath.
Mashhad, Najaf Ya Qum Mein Ho Do Gaz Zameen Naseeb.
 Akbar Kalam Pora Kiya Iss Dua Ke Sath.
