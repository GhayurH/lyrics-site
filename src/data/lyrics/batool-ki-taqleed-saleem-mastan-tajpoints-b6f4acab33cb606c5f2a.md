Batool Ki Taqleed Saleem Mastan Lyrics
Na Pocho Hum Se Keh Hai Kya Batool Ki Taqleed.
Ki Ismaton Ne Hamesha Batool Ki Taqleed.
Azal Se Karti Hain Dono Hi Rah-e-Ismat Par.
Janab-e-Mariyam-o-Hawwa Batool Ki Taqleed.
Janab-e-Aliya Zainab Gawahi Deti Hain.
Hai Sham-o-Kofay Ka Khutba Batool Ki Taqleed.
Jo Pocha Hazrat-e-Salman Se Keh Deen Hai Kya.
Tu Sar Jhuka Ke Pukara Batool Ki Taqleed.
Ghadeer Ka Hai Khulasa Wilayat-e-Haider.
Mubahlay Ka Khulasa Batool Ki Taqleed.
Khuda Gawah Keh Kar Saktay Hain Faqat Masoom.
Har Aik Kar Nahi Sakta Batool Ki Taqleed.
Imam-e-Asar Ka Jab Bhi Zahoor Hoga Aimaad.
Karey Ga Sara Zamana Batool Ki Taqleed.
