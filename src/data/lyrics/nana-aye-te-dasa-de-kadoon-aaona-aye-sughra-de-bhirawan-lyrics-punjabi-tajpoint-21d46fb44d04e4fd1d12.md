1
Nana Aye Te Dasa De Kadoon Aaona Aye Sughra De Bhirawan.
Main Chotiyaan Walay Nu Haye Din Raat Udeekaan Odha Jhola Roz Jholawan.
Toon Aap Janda Ayein Asghar Te Nahin Si Janda.
Na Odhay Kan Che Baba Teraan Di Gal Sunanda.
Oh Meriyaan Banwaan Choon Haye Ik Waar Aisa Lathiya Hun Beh Ke Wekhaan Main Rahwaan.
Chali Gharaan Nu Jandray Jis Waqt Babay Laye.
Aynj Lagda Si Ke Meri Qismat Nu Talay Laye.
Koi Veer Nahin Murya Haye Kis Aas Te Nana Ais Ghar Nu Fair Wasawaan.
Qasad V Bhaijiya Aye Kayi Waar Chithiyaan Paiyaan.
Mere Veer Aynj De Wichray Khabran Na Koi Aayiyaan.
De Aap Salah Nana Haye Main Maut Taun Pehlaan Veraan Nu Kiwein Bulawaan.
Akbar Rehya Na Asghar Hun Jag Te Mera Ki Aye.
Puchda Nahin Koi Mera Jiyondi Ke Mar Gayi Aye.
Aina Band Darwaziyan Te Haye Meri Sunda Nahin Koi Kino Dil Da Haal Sunawaan.
 Saqlain, Laila Da Chan Babay Di Kinj Na Manda.
Mehmal Taun Leh Ja Sughra Kinj Bhain Nu Aye Kainda.
Akbar E Janda Aye Haye Sughra Nu Kain Welay Kayi Wari Rukiyaan Ne Sahwaan.
