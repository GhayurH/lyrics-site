Aik Baar Aur Mujhe Godh Mein Le Lo Baba

1
Wa Hussaina Ka Howa Shoor Haram Mein Barpa
Ho Ke Rukhsat Jo Chaley Ghar Se Sheh-e-Karb-o-Bala
Keh Ke Yeh Dori Sakina Keh Thehar Jao Zara

2
Sheh Ne Bachi Ka Jo Yeh Haal Pareshaan Dekha
Ro Ke Farmaya Keh Kya Haal Meri Mah-e-Laqa
Joor Kar Nanhay Se Hathoon Ko Sakina Ne Kaha

3
Sochti Hoon Keh Tamachay Koi Marey Na Mere
Goshwaray Koi Kano Se Utaray Na Mere
Waham Aate Hain Mere Dil Mein Na Jane Kya Kya

4
Main Samajhti Hoon Keh Youn Hi Mujhe Behlatay Ho
Tha Na Maloom Keh Marne Ke Liye Jate Ho
Tum Hamesha Ke Liye Hotey Ho Ab Mujh Se Juda

5
Baba Seene Se Sakina Ko Lagatay Jao
Ab Kahan Hogi Mulaqaat Batatay Jao
Tum Ko Akbar Ki Qasam Guncha Dahan Ka Sadqa

6
Ab Na Pyaasi Houn Na Paani Ki Zaroorat Hai Mujhai
Koi Hajath Nahien Bus Itni Si Hasrath Hai Mujhai
Daikh Loon Aaj Mei Jee Bhar Kay Tumhara Chehra

7
Kya Khabar Tum Se Koi Baat Bhi Ab Ho Keh Na Ho
Kaun Jane Keh Mulaqaat Bhi Ab Ho Keh Na Ho
Dil Dharkta Hai Mera Behar-e-Nabi Behar-e-Khuda

8
Apni Aagosh Mein Aye Baba Chupa Lo Mujh Ko
Aakhri Bar Kalejay Se Laga Lo Mujh Ko
Jeetay Jee Aah Kahan Ab Tumhein Paon Gi Bhala

9
Tum Na Aao Gay Tu Aye Baba Bohat Royon Gi
Kis Ke Seene Pe Bata Dijiye Phir Soyon Gi
Na Tu Bhaiyya Ali Akbar Hain Na Abbas Chacha

10
Zabt Ki Taab Na Maula Ko Rahi Aye Anwar
Hath Phaila Ke Barhey Beti Ki Janib Sarwar
Jis Ghari Bekas-o-Masoom Ne Ro Ro Ke Kaha
