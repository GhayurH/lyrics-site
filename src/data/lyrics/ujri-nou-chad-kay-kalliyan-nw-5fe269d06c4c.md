Ujri nou chad kay kalliyan, Karbal wasaan walay, Aa saanja muka gaya aye

Dasween wi lung gaye aye, kyun wada bhul gaya aye,

satwen nou aan walay, Aa saanja muka gaya hai

Jadon shadi teri howay, aawa main veer karbal

Teri yaad aakhay veeran, mehno ro avay pal pal

Ayeho meray dil da cha ay, tenu sehray lag day waykhan

Na sehray laarn walay, Aa saanja muka gaya aye

Teray aun di A veeran, baythi yaan Aas lakay

Ujray gharanchay Akbar, tak haal mera aakay

Raavan meh mal kay baythi, kara intezar tera,

Karbal wasarn walay, aa saanja muka gaya aye

Teray pairan dae nishan main, kaj kaj kay rowan Akbar

Aaja way chain menu, howay jay coal Asghar

Kadi mour lay mohara, ujri da veer Akbar

Vichoray wadhan walay, aa saanja muka gaya aye

Behna day honday jag tay, bus marn veer Akbar

Veeran day naal behna da, wasda jahan Akbar

Ik wari aaja veeran, ro ro kay akhay Sughra

Du khaan chay parn walay, aa saanja muka gaya aye
