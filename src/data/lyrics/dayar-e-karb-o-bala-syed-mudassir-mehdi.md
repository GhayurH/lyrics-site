Dayar e Karb o Bala Syed Mudassir Mehdi.

1
Diyar-e-Karb-o-Bala Mein Maken Ho Jaon.
Giron Zameen Pe Aisay Zameen Ho Jaon.
Kissi Bhi Shair Mein Aaon Bara-e-Madh-e-Hussain.
Hai Lafz-e-Husan Ki Khuwaish, Hasen Ho Jaon.
Zameen Pe Dekh Ke Jah-o-Jalal-e-Karb-o-Bala.
Hai Aasmaan Ki Tamana Zameen Ho Jaon.

2
Karon Main Sajdah Tu Ho Zehan Mein Khiyal-e-Hussain.
Main Kund Zehan Bhi Itna Zaheen Ho Jaon.
Dar-e-Hussain Pe Islam Ne Dua Mangi.
Karam Ho Aap Ka Mujh Par Tu Deen Ho Jaon.
Dar-e-Ali Pe Ho Poray Wajood Se Sajdah.
Dua Karo Main Mukamal Jaben Ho Jaon.

3
Ho Karbala Mein Bapa Mehfil-e-Sana-e-Hussain.
Main Anbiya Ka Wahan Humnasheen Ho Jaon.
Sharf Miley Main Parhon Manqabat Bahatar Ki.
Sana Guzari Mein Main Behtareen Ho Jaon.
Farishtay Chomney Aa Jaein Mujh Ko:NOOR ALI:.
Agar Main Khak-e-Rah-e-Zaireen Ho Jaon.
