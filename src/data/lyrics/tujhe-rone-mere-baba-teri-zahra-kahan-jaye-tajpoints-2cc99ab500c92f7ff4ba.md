Tujhe Rone Mere Baba Teri Zahra Kahan Jaye.
Mere Roney Pe Hai Pehra Teri Zahra Kahan Jaye.

1
Mera Haq Cheen Kar Baba Mere Dar Ko Jalaya Hai.
Mujhe Darbar Mein Haye Sitam Karne Bulaya Hai.
Meri Dushman Hoi Dunya Teri Zahra Kahan Jaye.
Sitam Kya Kya Hoye Mujh Par Sar-e-Mehshar Bataon Gi.

2
Muqadma Apne Bachay Ka Qayamat Mein Uthaon Gi.
Mera Mohsin Gaya Mara Teri Zahra Kahan Jaye.
Bataya Hi Nahi Apne Kissi Humdard Ko Baba.
Chupa Rakha Hai Seene Mein Khud Apne Dard Ko Baba.

3
Dikhaney Zakhm Pehlu Ka Teri Zahra Kahan Jaye.
Qayamat Aap Ki Zahra Pe Woh Aayi Madine Mein.
Yeh Kaisi Be Wafai Ki Fiza Chayi Madine Mein.
Yahan Koi Nahi Apna Teri Zahra Kahan Jaye.

4
Main Apne Aansoon Se Apne Daman Ko Bhigoti Hoon.
Musalmano Ki Aabadi Se Ja Ke Door Roti Hoon.
Faqat Gham Khuwar Hai Sehra Teri Zahra Kahan Jaye.
Bula Lo Pas Hi Apne Jahan Tum Ho Mere Baba.

5
Bohat Mushkil Mein Hai Beti Kahan Tum Ho Mere Baba.
Mera Dushwar Hai Jeena Teri Zahra Kahan Jaye.
Yeh Jaan Apni Tere Gham Mein Mujhe Khoney Nahi Detey.
Tumhare Kalma Go Baba Mujhe Roney Nahi Detey.

6
Inhein Giriye Se Hai Shikwa Teri Zahra Kahan Jaye.
 Raza Zahra Ke Nohay Se Nabi Ki Qabar Hilti Thi.
Lehad Pe Iss Tarha Se Baap Ki Beti Tarapti Thi.
Labhoon Pe Tha Yehi Noha Teri Zahra Kahan Jaye.
