Qaid V Muk Gayi Kazim Nu Koi Rowan Te Aaye
Baghdad De Lokaan Ne Naa Tauq Lahaye.

1
Musa Kazim Da Koi Aisa Dardi Howay.
Sain Masooma Nu Ja Khabar Aye Sunaway.
Bhaina Nu Madine Toon Tu Lay Rowan Layi Aaye.
Pori Dunya Andar Iko Kofa Hoya.

2
Jithe Wasday Lokaan Nayi Ik Janaza Chaya.
Iss Shehar De Wich Lashay Mazdoraan Ne Chaye.
Sham Toon Bad Nayi Qatal Gah Ban Gayi Aye.
Pul Te Rahi Maiyyat Eho Payi Aakhdi Aye.

3
Koi Aal-e-Muhammad Choo Baghdad Na Jaye.
Eho Hasrat Mula Tere Akbar Di Aye.
Sain Zahra Nu Enj Tera Ohh Pursa Deway.
Baghdad Walay Pul Te Taboot Uthaye.
