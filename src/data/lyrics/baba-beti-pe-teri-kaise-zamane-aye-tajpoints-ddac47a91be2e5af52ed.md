Baba Beti Pe Teri Kaise Zamane Aye.
Tere Darbari Mere Ghar Ko Jalanay Aaye.

1
Jald Hi Aa Ke Milon Gi Main Haye Tujh Se Baba.
Zakhm-e-Jaan Lewa Mujhe Zalim Lagane Aaye.
Baba Meri Tu Udasi Pe Tarap Jata Tha.
Teri Ummat Ke Fard Mujh Ko Rulanay Aaye.

2
Teri Tehreer Ko Jhutla Ke Sand Phari Gayi.
Ab Gawahon Ko Hai Rastay Se Hatanay Aaye.
Kalma Parh Ke Tere Hathoon Jo Musalman Hoye.
Baba Woh Loag Mere Ghar Ko Giranay Aaye.

3
Baad Tere Kya Bataon Main Haye Baba Saein.
Waqt Kya Kya Teri Beti Pe Najane Aaye.
Hai Dua Maula Fida Ki Yeh Tu Ab Bhaij Ossay.
Turbat-e-Syeda Zahra Jo Bananey Aaye.
