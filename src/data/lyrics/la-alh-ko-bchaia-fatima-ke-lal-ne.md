La illah ko bachaya Fatima kay laal nay

1
Deen zinda kar dekhaya,  Fatima kay laal nay

2
1) Khainch li Akbar ki barchi jurrat-o-himaat kay saath
Apni ankhain bhi na bandhi aur na baitay kay haath
Aik aansu na bahaya Fatima kay laal nay

3
2) Dekh kaar sabar-e-Hussaini ambiya ronay lagay
Rotay rotay yon khalil ullah say kehnay lagay
Aap ka waada nibhaya Fatima kay laal nay

4
3) Ladala hoon Murtaza ka aur hoon ibnay Rasool
May jhukoon batil kay agay yeh nahin mujh ko qabool
Faisala apna sunaya Fatima kay laal nay

5
4) Deen ka daman baqa ki naimaton say bhar diya
Aakhiri sajda khudayey lam-yazal ko kar diya
Sar na sajday say uthaya Fatima kay laal nay

6
5) Sabar say jabar-o-sitam ki toar daali har umeed
Zulm saray say liya lakin na ki bait-e-Yazid
Sar usoolon par kataya Fatima kay laal nay

7
6) Chay mahinay kay pisar ko kar kay hathon say dafan
Aey khuda teri raza yak eh raha ta bay watan
Hosala apna bataya Fatima kay laal nay

8
7) Zakham seh kar shukar karta tha Sakina ka pidar
Ro rahay thay arsh walay yeh nazara dekh kar
Arsh walon ko rulaya Fatima kay laal nay

9
8) Matami Touqeer tumko day rahain hay yeh dua
Apni ankhon say tu dekhay woh deyaar-e-karbala
Jis pay apna ghar lutaya Fatima kay laal nay
