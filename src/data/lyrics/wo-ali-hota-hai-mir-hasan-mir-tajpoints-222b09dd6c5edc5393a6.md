Jo Khudai Ko Chalaye Woh Ali Hota Hai.
Kam Nabiyon Ke Jo Aaye Woh Ali Hota Hai.

1
Aik Shab Soney Ki Qeemat Mein Khuda Ki Marzi.
Shab-e-Hijrat Jo Kamaye Woh Ali Hota Hai.
Mil Ke Kaba Jo Banaein Woh Nabi Hotey Hain.
Our Kabay Mein Jo Aaye Woh Ali Hota Hai.

2
Rooz-e-Mehshar Jissay ALLAH Adalat Ke Liye.
Apni Kursi Pe Bithaye Woh Ali Hota Hai.
Ban Ke Mehman Jo Mairaj Pe Jaye Woh Nabi.
Hath Jo Aa Ke Milaye Woh Ali Hota Hai.

3
Aik ALLAH Ke Darbar Mein Sajday Ke Siwa.
Jo Kahin Sar Na Jhukaye Woh Ali Hota Hai.
Aasmano Se Utari Hoi Talwar Ko Bhi.
Waar Karna Jo Sikhaye Woh Ali Hota Hai.

4
Apne Dushman Ko Bhi Hijrat Ke Haseen Bistar Par.
Jo Muhammad Nazar Aaye Woh Ali Hota Hai.
Hum Ko Bas Itna Pata Hai Keh Jo Hubdaron Ka.
Qabar Tak Sath Nibhaye Woh Ali Hota Hai.

5
Khol Kar Khutboon Se Takhleeq Ke Parday Khud Jo.
Kun Ke Asbab Bataye Woh Ali Hota Hai.
Looh-e-Mehfooz Se Jibreel e Ameen Ko Akbar.
Wahi Parhna Jo Sikhaye Woh Ali Hota Hai.
