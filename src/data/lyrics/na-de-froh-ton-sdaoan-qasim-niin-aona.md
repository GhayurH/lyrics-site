Na de Farwa tu sadawan Qasim nai auna
Na vekheya kar hun rahwan Qasim nai auna

1
Kadi valaan nu main kholan kadi putar di yaad ich rolan
Mere laal de hath nai labde kiday layi main mehndiyan gholan
Ohde kiwen shagan manawan, Qasim nai aouna nae aouna

2
Farmaya Paak Hussain eh, karo bibiyo raj ke vainay
Sarmaya veer Hassan da Farwa de dil da chain ae
Gaya mareya vich sehravan, Qasim nai aouna nae aouna

3
Farwa diyan rondiyan akhiyan haye chalan hawawan tatiyaan
Qasim de hoye tukre jeevain vikhriyan phul diyan patiyan
Enj puttar na vekhen maawan, Qasim nai aouna nae aouna

4
Sehre de phul murjhaye, ganay ne rang badlaye
Qasim di laash de tukre, jadon khaime de vich aye
Keeta matam phupiyan maavan, Qasim nai aouna nae aouna

5
Sajjad Hassan da laal ae kita zaliman ne pamaal ae
Ohdi laash utay maa Farwa, hondi rosi mominaa naal ae
Kehndi hosi rooz bulawan, Qasim nai aouna nae aouna
