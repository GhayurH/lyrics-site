Qayamat ban ke din Ashoor ka Zainab pe aaya hai
(Hazaro qatilo kai darmiyaan)Zahra ka jaayaa hai

1
(Sada Zahra ki aati thi utho ghazi sahara do)
(Mere bete ne tanha laasha-e Akbar uthaaya hai)

2
(Sakina royi hai shaayad tamaache Shimr ne maare)
(Badal ke karwatein Ghazi ka lasha thartharaya hai)

3
(Kiya veeraan ek jhoola ujaada ek madar ko)
(Bata aye qatile Asgher tere kya haath aaya hai)

4
(Jigar par haath rak ker Shah dorai hai suwe maidan)
(Seena khaa ker Ali Akber nai Baba ko bulaya hai)

5
(Suna ker khat sir-e-lashai pisar Shabbir kehte the)
(Utho Akber Madine jaao Sughra ne bulaaya hai)

6
(Asar khoon-e-Abu Talib rajas purdae yeh sehra par)
(Rasoolallaha ke deen o shariat ko bachaya hai)
