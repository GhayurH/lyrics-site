Oh my brother, your sister is poor
Oh my brother, there seems no cure

1
Remember the times you played with Sakinah?
Gave her those earrings back in Madinah?
They ripped them off her ears, her ears are sore

2
Remember the times she slept on your chest
Bonded together with such high zest
Crying to sleep she just lies on the floor

3
Remember the times you talked about pains
Look at your Abid all dressed in chains
Those heavy shackles just tear him even more

4
Remember the times we spent in Madinah
Happily we spent them with Sakinah
What should I do, God, she is no more

5
Remember the times I used to see your face
At morning, evening and at night's phase
I want to see you just like I did before
