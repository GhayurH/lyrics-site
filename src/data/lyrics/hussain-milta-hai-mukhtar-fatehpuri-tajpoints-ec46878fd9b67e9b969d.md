Hussain Milta Hai Mukhtar Fatehpuri.
Khalos Se Jo Ho Sajdah Hussain Milta Hai.

1
Agar Khuda Nahi Milta Hussain Milta Hai.
Janab-e-Hawa Ne Manga Hussain Sa Beta.
Tu Ik Garor Ke Aalam Mein Bool Utha Khuda.
Jo Maa Ho Fatima Zahra, Hussain Milta Hai.

2
Jo Zaireen Hain Sab Hain Hussain Se Miltay.
Magar Jo Qurbat e Abbas Rakhtay Hain Unn Se.
Bohat Qareeb Se Maula Hussain Milta Hai.
Rasool Bolay Yeh Mairaj Mein Likha Dekha.

3
Hussain Rah-e-Nijat Our Hussain Shamay Huda.
Wahan Bhi Mera Nawasa Hussain Milta Hai.
Jo Karbala Ko Hain Jate Hussain Se Milney.
Karam Yeh Dekhiye Jab Maut Unn Ko Aati Hai.

4
Khud Unn Se Qabar Mein Maula Hussain Milta Hai.
Sawal Pocha Kissi Matami Se Jab Hum Ne.
Keh Milta Kya Hai Tumhein Khon Ke Bahane Se.
Jawab Oss Ka Yeh Aaya Hussain Milta Hai.

5
Dar-e-Hussain Pe Zair Jo Sajdah Karta Hai.
Oss Aik Sajday Ke Haq Mein Khuda Yeh Kehta Hai.
Milon Ga Main Jisay Mera Hussain Milta Hai.
Kabhi Badosh e Payamber Kabhi Ba Pusht Nabi.

6
Kabhi Ba Zeen e Fars Our Ba Nook-e-Neza Kabhi.
Har Aik Haal Mein Oncha Hussain Milta Hai.
Kahan Woh Ghair Ke Betay Kahan Woh Dosh-e-Nabi.
Bata De Unn Ko Koi Rab Ka Faisla Hai Yehi.

7
Yahan Tu Sirf Hasan Ya Hussain Milta Hai.
Aye Majid Ab Hai Shab-e-Qadar Ka Hussain Waqar.
Hussain Chahe Tu Majboor Ko Karey Mukhtar.
Muqadron Se Bhi Aala Hussain Milta Hai.
