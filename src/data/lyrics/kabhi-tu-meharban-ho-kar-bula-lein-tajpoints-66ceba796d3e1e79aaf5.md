1
Kabhi Tu Meharban Ho Kar Bula Lein.
Yeh Mehwash Hum Faqeeron Ki Dua Lein.
Na Jane Phir Yeh Rut Aye Na Aye.
Jawan Pholon Ki Kuch Khushbo Chura Lein.

2
Bohat Roye Hum Zamane Ke Liye Hum.
Zara Apne Liye Aanso Baha Lein.
Hum un Ko Bholne Wale Nahi.
Samajte Hain Gham-e-Doraan Ki Chalein.

3
Humari Bhi Sanbhal Jaye Gi Halat.
Wo Pehle Apni Zulfein Tu Sanbhalein.
Nikalne Ko Hai Wo Mehtab Ghar Se.
Sitaron se Kaho Nazrein Jhuka Lekin.

4
Hum Apne Raste Par Chal Rahe Hain.
Janab Sheikh Apna Rasta Lein.
Zamana TU Younhi Rotha Rahe Ga.
Chalo Jalib Unhein Chal Kar Mana Lein.
