1
Sana E Shabbir Syed Zakariya Rizvi
Youn Bhi Hum Ahmad-e-Mursal Se Wafa Rakhtay Hain.
Lab Pe Shabbir Ki Har Waqt Sana Rakhtay Hain.
Hum Sukhanwar Hain Zameenein Bhi Juda Rakhtay Hain.
Madh-e-Shabbir Mein Mazmoon Bhi Niya Rakhtay Hain.
Dil Mein Jo Qabar-e-Shah e Karb-o-Bala Rakhtay Hain.
Qabar Ki Karb-o-Bala Mein Wohi Ja Rakhtay Hain.
Apne Kashkool Mein Zahra Ki Atta Rakhtay Hain.
Hum Azadar Hain Bibi Ki Dua Rakhtay Hain.
Larkharati Hoi Dunya Mein Girein Hum Kyunkar.
Hath Mein Shah Ki Ulfat Ka Assa Rakhtay Hain.
Hai Hussain Aks-e-Nabi, Aks-e-Ali Hai Abbas.
Aik Tasveer Ke Rukh Dono Siwa Rakhtay Hain.
Yeh Nishani Hai Ibadat Mein Azadari Ki.
Hum Musallay Pe Jo Yeh Khak-e-Shifa Rakhtay Hain.
Rashk Se Dekhtey Hain Shams-o-Qamar Woh Seene.
Sheh Ke Matam Se Jo Zakhmo Mein Zia Rakhtay Hain.
Martaba Unn Ka Barhatay Hain Hussain Ibn-e-Ali.
Dar Pe Abbas Ke Jo Khud Ghata Rakhtay Hain.
Mangney Ke Liye Shabbir Se Aaye Tu Koi.
Deney Walay Hain So Darwaza Khula Rakhtay Hain.
Dil Se Jo Baithta Hai Farsh-e-Azza Par Aa Kar.
Aisay Har Shaks Ko Hum Sar Pe Bitha Rakhtay Hain.
Aana Jana Hai Farishton Ka Unhi Ke Ghar Mein.
Majlis-e-Shah Ka Jo Farsh Bicha Rakhtay Hain.
Hum Gadagar Hain Dar-e-Aal-e-Muhammad Ke Asad.
Iss Liye Dunya Se Hum Hath Utha Rakhtay Hain.
