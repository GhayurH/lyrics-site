Ismat e kul darbar may aayain aur qayamt kia ho gi
Binte Nabi gayi jhutlae aur qayamat kia ho gi

1
Jis dar pe tazeem ki khaatir pak Muhammad jhuktay thay
Us dar ko hi aag lagyain aur qayamat kia ho gi

2
Deta hay Salman zamanat khaak pe baith rahi Zahra
Phir bhi Zehra baith na payain aur qayamat kia ho gi

3
Ho nahi sakta woh razi allah dil jo dukhaye Zahra ka
Pak Nabi ki hadees bhulayain aur qayamat kia ho gi

4
Ik darbar ne loota hay Zehra tat-heer ki waris ko
Phir darbar may Zehra jayain aur qayamat ki ho ga
