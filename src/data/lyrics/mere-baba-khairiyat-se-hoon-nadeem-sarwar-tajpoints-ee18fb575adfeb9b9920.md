1
Mere Baba Khairiyat Se Hoon Nadeem Sarwar.
Sughra Gash Se Chonk Kar Uthi Boli Sar Ko Peetti Hoi.
Haye Meri Neend Urr Gayi Mere Baba Khairiyat Se Hoon.
Dekha Aaj Main Ne Kaisa Khuwab Rait Par Paray Hain Kuch Ghulab.
Chun Rahe Hain Jin Ko Buturab Khon Ro Raha Hai Aaftab.
Ik Taraf Alam Hai Khon Mein Tar Jhuk Gayi Hai Shah Ki Kamar.
Ja Rahi Hai Jis Taraf Nazar Bibiyaan Khari Hain Nangay Sar.
Haye Haye Qasim-e-Jawaan Bain Kar Rahi Hai Jis Ki Maa.
Hill Gaye Zameen-o-Aasmaan Tori Jab Dulhan Ne Choriyaan.
Aik Bachi Seena-e-Padar Dhondti Rahi Idhar Udhar.
Baap So Raha Tha Khak Par Jis Ke Tan Se Kat Chuka Tha Sar.
Qaid Hain Rasool Zadiyaan Hath Mein Bandhi Hain Rasiyaan.
Be Kajawa Hain Amariyaan Khoon Ro Raha Hai Sarbaan.
Khuwab Yeh Bara Ajeeb Tha Baad Shah-e-Dein Ghareeb Tha.
Kya Yehi Mera Naseeb Tha Be Kafan Mera Tabeeb Tha.
:SARWAR-o-REHAN: Ki Dua Ho Qabool Shah-e-Karbala.
Koi Beti Our Iss Tarha Phir Tarap Ke Youn Na De Sada.
