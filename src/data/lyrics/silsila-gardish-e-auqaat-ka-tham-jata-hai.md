1
Silsila Gardish-e-Auqaat Ka Tham Jata Hai
Qaidi Zanjeer Hilata Hai Tu Dam Jata Hai
Pusht-e-Beemar Hai Youn Zehmat-e-Zanjeer Se Kham
Halqa-e-Tauq Giraan Ta Ba-Qadam Jata Hai

2
Zoaf Se Khoon Ko Tapakne Ka Bhi Yarana Raha
Zakhm Se Rista Nahi Aankh Mein Jam Jata Hai
Aasmaa Sharm Se Hai Daman-e-Khursheed Mein Gum
Sar Khule Qafila Ahl-e-Harm Jata Hai

3
Ab Tilawat Ho Sar-e-Naiza Bhi Hufaaz Ke Beech
Warna Quraan Ki Tilawat Ka Bharam Jata Hai
Yeh Zamana Hai Hadood-e-Gham-e-Shabir Se Ka
Main Jahan Jaon Mere Sath Yeh Gham Jata Hai

4
Aish-e-Dunya Yeh Tere Daam Mein Kaise Aa Jaye
Dil Azza Khane Se Bahir Bhi Tu Kam Jata Hai
Main Chala Roza-e-Shabir Ki Janib Tu Laga
Mujh Se Aagay Meri Qismat Ka Qadam Jata Hai
