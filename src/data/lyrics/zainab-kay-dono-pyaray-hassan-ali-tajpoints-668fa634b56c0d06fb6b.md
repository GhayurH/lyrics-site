Zainab Kay Dono Pyaray Hassan Ali.
Pohanchay Nehar Kinaray Zainab Ke Dono Pyaray.

1
Ik Dosrey Ko Dono Kartay They Yeh Isharey.
Main Dekhon Maisara Ko Tum Maimana Sanbhalo.
Main Inn Ko Cheer Dalon Tum Unn Ko Cheer Walo.
Dikhlaein Din Mein Taray Zainab Ke Dono Pyaray.

2
Mah-o-Hasham Ali Ka Dono Mein Dekh Raha Tha.
Yeh Shair Hain Ali Ke Chehron Pe Hi Likha Tha.
Maqtal Jinhein Pukarey Zainab Ke Dono Pyaray.
Qehar-e-Khuda Woh Ban Kar Totay Sitamgaron Par.

3
Yaad Aayi Shamiyon Ko Siffeen Our Khaybar.
They Maut Ke Isharey Zainab Ke Dono Pyaray.
Urtay They Sar Howa Mein Ik Shoor Tha Fiza Mein.
Kuch Farq Hi Nahi Tha Talwar Our Fiza Mein.

4
Dikhla Gaye Nazarey Zainab Ke Dono Pyaray.
Dekha Sheh-e-Huda Ne Abbas e Bawafa Ne.
Janat Se Phir Sada Di Khud Shah-e-La Fatah Ne.
Yeh Fakhar Hain Hamarey Zainab Ke Dono Pyaray.

5
Qabzey Mein Alqama Hai Sokha Howa Gala Hai.
Pyasay Hi Laut Aaye Maa Ne Yehi Kaha Hai.
Tashna Labi Ke Marey Zainab Ke Dono Pyaray.
Charon Taraf Se Hamla Aada Ne Jab Kiya Tha.

6
Teeron Se Unn Ko Chalni Maqtal Mein Kar Diya Tha.
Be Sar Hoye Becharay Zainab Ke Dono Pyaray.
Maula Ko Di Sadaein Lashay Uthane Aaein.
Shah-e-Zaman Jo Pohanchay Lainay Lagay Balaein.

7
Sadqay Hoye Hamarey Zainab Ke Dono Pyaray.
Noha Hasan Parho Tu Oss Waqt Yeh Bhi Socho.
Bakhshish Ki Fikar Kya Ho Ab:MAZHAR ABIDI: Ko.
Bakhshish Ke Hain Saharey Zainab Ke Dono Pyaray.
