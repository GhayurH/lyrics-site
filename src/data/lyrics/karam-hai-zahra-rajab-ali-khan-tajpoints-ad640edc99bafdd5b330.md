1
Karam Hai Zahra Rajab Ali Khan Lyrics
Yeh Sab Tumhara Karam Hai Zahra Keh Baat Ab Tak Bani Hoi Hai.
Hai Dil Ki Nazron Se Dekha Main Ne Yeh Baat Kabay Pe Bhi Likhi Hai.
Khuda Ki Qudrat Bhi Keh Rahi Hai Tumhari Jaisi Na Phir Bani Hai.
Ali Dobarah Bane Hain Lekin Tu Fatima Hai Tu Aik Hi Hai.
Nahi Thi Mariyam Tumharey Jaisi Woh Esa Lainay Kahin Gayi Thi.
Hasan Miley Hain Tujhey Wahin Par Musalay Se Bhi Tu Kab Uthi Hai.
Tu Bibi Jo Faqay Rakh Rahi Hai Yeh Dil Gareebon Ke Rakh Rahi Hai.
Wagarna Raziq Hai Tera Shohar Tujhey Kahan Rizq Ki Kami Hai.
Tawaf Kabay Ka Karne Walo Jabeen-e-Hur Pe Nazar Tu Dalo.
Ghilaf-e-Kaba Hai Iss Se Sasta Yeh Hur Ka Romal Qeemati Hai.
Nabi Ne Mehshar Mein Sab Se Bola Utho Utho Aa Rahi Hai Zahra.
Koi Wali Tab Talak Na Baithay Keh Fatima Jab Talak Khari Hai.
Taqaiyya Oss Par Nahi Karon Ga Woh Jis Ne Bibi Tujhey Sataya.
Woh Koi Bhi Woh Jahanumi Hai Woh Dozakhi Hai Woh Lanati Hai.
Rajab Hai Sajjad Aane Wala Duaein Dein Gi Ossi Ko Zahra.
Woh Jis Zabaan Par Ali Ali Hai Woh Jis Ke Dil Mein Ali Ali Hai.
