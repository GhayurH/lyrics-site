Haay Hassan - Haay Hussain

1
AJEEB HAL MAIN HAIN fATIMA kay douno pisar ----
Hasan kay teer badan main - Hussain teroun per

2
AJEEB HAL MAIN HAIN fATIMA kay douno pisar ----
Hasan ko daikha hay Qasim nay khun ugaltay howay ----
Sakina dekhtee hay bap ko tahay khanjar

3
AJEEB HAL MAIN HAIN fATIMA kay douno pisar
Najany daikahin hain Zainab nay tasht main kaysay ----
Jigaer kay tukray Hasan kay kabhe Hussain ka Sir

4
AJEEB HAL MAIN HAIN fATIMA kay douno pisar ----
Hasan nay jatay haoway choomay bazoo Zainab kay ----
HUSSAIN JATY HOWAY CHOOMTAY RAHAY cHADER

5
AJEEB HAL MAIN HAIN fATIMA kay douno pisar ----
Hasan kay baitay kay ummat nay kerdeya tukray ----
Hussain latya hain tukray wo saray chun chun ker

6
AJEEB HAL MAIN HAIN fATIMA kay douno pisar ----
Udher Hasan ka janazay per Phool terroun kay ----
Idher Hussain kay lashay kee nazer hain paather

7
AJEEB HAL MAIN HAIN fATIMA kay douno pisar ----
Udher Hasan ka janaza phiraya galiyoin main ----
Saray Hussain phiraya gaya idher Dar dar

8
AJEEB HAL MAIN HAIN fATIMA kay douno pisar
Tarap rahay hain janazay kuch or youn Akber ----
Kay teer khainch rahee hay Badan say khud Mader

9
AJEEB HAL MAIN HAIN fATIMA kay douno pisar ----
