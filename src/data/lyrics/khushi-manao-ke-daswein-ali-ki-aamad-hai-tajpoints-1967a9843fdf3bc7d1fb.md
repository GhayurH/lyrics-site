Khushi Manao Ke Daswein Ali Ki Aamad Hai.
Taqi Ke Ghar Mein Imam-e-Naqi Ki Aamad Hai.

1
Zamana Poch Raha Tha Yeh Kis Ki Aamad Hai.
Tu Main Yeh Bola Dobara Ali Ki Aamad Hai.
Ali Hai Naam Tu Seerat Mein Bhi Ali Hain Naqi.
Jahan Mein Aaj Shabih-e-Nabi Ki Aamad Hai.

2
Khuda Ne Khool Diye Hain Sakha Ke Darwaze.
Na Koi Jaye Ga Khali Sakhi Ki Aamad Hai.
Irade Mati Mein Batil Ke Milne Wale Hain.
Hai Jis Ka Dada Ali Oss Jari Ki Aamad Hai.

3
Farishte Dekhne Utre Naqi Ke Aane Se.
Deyar-e-Noor Mein Phir Se Roshni Ki Aamad Hai.
Kaha Farishton Ne Afraz Se Na Ghabrao.
Tumhari Qabar Mein Mula Naqi Ki Aamad Hai.
