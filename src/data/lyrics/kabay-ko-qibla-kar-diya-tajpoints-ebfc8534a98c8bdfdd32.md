1
Khaybar Ko Uthane Wala Aadam Ko Bananey Wala.
Marhab Ko Marne Wala Har Mushkil Talney Wala.
Toor Ke Jab Dewar Ko Aaya Dunya Ko Hairan.
Kar Diya Kar Diya Kar Diya Kabay Ko Qibla Kar Diya.

2
Shukriya Maula Kaba Yeh Bola Itna Bara Ahsan.
Kar Diya Kar Diya Kar Diya Kabay Ko Qibla Kar Diya.
Ab Mere Dar Par Sar Ko Jhuka Kar Dunya Parhey Gi Namazein.
Jin-o-Malak Bhi Sajdah Karein Gaye Nabiyon Ki Hoon Gi Qatarein.

3
Ghar Pe Khuda Ke Kaun Karey Ga Itna Bara Ahsan.
Kaun-o-Makaan Mein Na Aasmaan Mein Koi Nahi Hai Aisa Ghar.
Tujh Ko Samjhna Aye Mere Maula Hai Marifat Ka Yehi Safar.
Hukam-e-Khuda Tha Ya Tera Mansha Itna Bara Ahsan.

4
Salman-o-Qambar Miqdad-o-Buzar Ab Yahan Aate Rahein Gaye.
Shah-o-Gadagar Sab Tere Nokar Madah Sunatay Rahein Gaye.
Terah Rajab Ko Hai Tera Aana Itna Bara Ahsan.
Meri Zameen Ki Sab Ki Jabeen Ki Tauqeer Tu Ne Barha Di.

5
Jhotay Khudaon Ko Tu Ne Tora Dunya Yahin Par Jhuka Di.
Par Gaya Saya Tere Karam Ka Itna Bara Ahsan.
Tera Waseela Mil Gaya Maula Ab Yahan Aaein Gi Zahra.
Tu Ne Aa Kar Toor Diya Hai Zulm-o-Joor Ka Pehra.

6
Bint-e-Nabi Ka Aana Jana Itna Bara Ahsan.
Madah Ali Ki Lehja Hasan Ka Our Qalam Hai Mazhar Ka.
Sare Jahan Mein Izzat-o-Shohrat Sadqa Hai Tere Hi Ghar Ka.
Mujh Ko Banaya Zakir Apna Itna Bara Ahsan.
