Bheni da maan Ghazi tak leh gaiyan ridawan
Maqtal de pasay sehni,  paiyan daindiyan sadawan.

1
1) Ghazi de khaiman de wal kayi waar gayi Sakina
Mera veer dada pyasa hai,  unno pani kinjh pilawan.

2
2) Ghazi nun jad Sakina haye mashq cha pawaeyi
Shala khairi murr kay aaway,  Zainab karay duawan.

3
3) Khali alaam nun tak kay kurlan ich pay gaye ne
Syeda de baal waikhan,  Abbas diyan rawan.

4
4) Bazoo kalam kara kay raitan ich dum gaya hai
Teray baad Zahra jaiyan,  rahiyan bhardiyan ne hawan.

5
5)????? jaira momin Alay Nabhi nun ronda ai
Onday dar te rehwan shala,  Abbas diyan nighawan.
