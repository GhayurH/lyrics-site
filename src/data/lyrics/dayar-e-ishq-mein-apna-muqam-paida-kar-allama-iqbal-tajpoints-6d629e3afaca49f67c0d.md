Dayar-e-Ishq Mein Apna Muqam Paida Kar Allama Iqbal.
Niya Zamana Naye Subh-o-Sham Paida Kar.

1
Khuda Agar Dil-e-Fitrat Shanaas De Tujh Ko.
Sakoot-e-Lalah-o-Gul Se Kalam Paida Kar.
Utha Na Sheesha Giran-e-Faring Ke Ahsaan.
Sifal-e-Hind Se Mina-o-Jaam Paida Kar.

2
Main Shakh-e-Taak Hoon Meri Ghazal Hai Mera Samar.
Mere Samar Se May Lalah Faam Paida Kar.
Mera Tareeq Ameeri Nahi Faqeri Hai.
Khudi Na Baich Gharebi Mein Naam Paida Kar.
