1
Ma Dua Karti Thi Akbar Tera Sehra Dekhon Hasan Sadiq.
Chand Chup Jaye Tera Chand Sa Chehra Dekhon.
Boli Ro Kar Ali Akbar Ki Phophi Aye Malik.
Kis Tarha Barchi Mein Akbar Ka Kaleja Dekhon.

2
Lash-e-Akbar Pe Khat-e-Sughra Sunatay Hain Hussain.
Bhai Ab Our Kahan Tak Tera Rasta Dekhon.
Aao Zainab Yeh Jawaan Lash Uthaein Mil Kar.
Jab Nahi Ghazi Tu Kyun Janib-e-Darya Dekhon.

3
Maa Pukari Keh Howa Waqt-e-Azaan Lal Mere.
Tu Azaan De Tu Muhammad Ka Main Lehja Dekhon.
Raat Din Karta Hoon Rehan Yehi Aik Dua.
Karbala Dekhon Najaf Our Madina Dekhon.
