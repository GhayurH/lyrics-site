Jukta huwa alam jo nazar aya shah ko. Wapas jo dekha nehr pa ate sipah ko.
Bole hussain kaise ke aik lambhi aah ko. Benur kardiya mere bhayi nigaah ko.

1
Haey Abbas meray, haey alamdar meray
Chal gaye baad teray,  qalb pe talwar meray
Haey Abbas meray, haey alamdar meray

2
1) Bund pani ke liye haat katane wale
Pyaase bacho ke liye khun bahane wale
Kisliye sogaya tu,  Nehr pe gamkhwar mere
Haey Abbas meray, haey alamdar meray

3
2) Tere dam pe mere lashkar ka bharam baki tha
Ay alamdar tere sath alam baki tha
Kardiya tanha mujay,  Tu nay madadgar meray
Haey Abbas meray, haey alamdar meray

4
3) Juk gayi meri qamar phat gaya sina mera
Jis ghari kat ke gira khak pe bazu tera
Sainkron teer chale,  Dil se huwe paar meray
Haey Abbas meray, haey alamdar meray

5
4) Pani jis dam se bhara khun bahayaa us utna
Mashke ke sath jigar karliya zakhmi apna
Tere qurban gayaa,  May to wafadaar meray
Haey Abbas meray, haey alamdar meray

6
5) Alatash ki ye sadaa tum bhi to sunte ho gay
Khaymo me aaho buqaa tum bhi to sunte ho gay
Zoo fisha gham me tere,  hain daro diwaar meray
Haey Abbas meray, haey alamdar meray

7
6) Khali kuzoon me bhare bacho ne ansu apnay
Kehti hay bali Sakina koyi faryad sunay
Legaya cheen ke dur,  Shimr sitamgaar meray
Haey Abbas meray, haey alamdar meray

8
DORAY
-
Inse sikhe koi Shabbir ki nusrat karna
Mayi ni hajre risaalat ki hifazaat karna
Aaj bhi parchame abbas hai aage aage
Inki miras me ayaa hai kayadat karnaa
-
Haey Abbas meray, haey alamdar meray

9
7) Bole sheh hashr talak rasm rahay gi jaari
Sari duniya me jahaa ho gi yeh matam dari
Tum jalooso ke ho gay,  qafela salaar meray
Haey Abbas meray, haey alamdar meray

10
Chalgayi baad teray,  qalb pe talwar mere
Haey Abbas meray, haey alamdar meray
