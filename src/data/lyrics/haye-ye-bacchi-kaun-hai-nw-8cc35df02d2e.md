Haye Ye Bacchi Kaun Hai

ye to sakina lagti hai

aati hai maqtal ki taraf

dekha hai kyun suyt najaf

kya is ka dada hai Ali

ye to sakina lagti hai

ati hai jo roti hui

sadmo say jaan khoti hui

phayrti hui naad e ali

ye to sakina lagti hai

kyun ro ke jaan deti hai ye

kyun siskiyan leti hai ye

kya margaya is ka koi

ye to sakina lagti hai

kano say kyun khon’ hai ravan

uff ye tamachon ke nishan’

ye zulm aur ye kamsini

ye to sakina lagti hai

kausar ye maqtal ki faza

tarpa rahi hai dil mera

taari hai gham ki teeragi

ye to sakina lagti hai
