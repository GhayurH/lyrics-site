1
Teri Lash Salamat Aayi Nayi Kiwein Qasim Porey Shagan Karan.
Nayi Tukriyaan Wichon Hath Milday Tainu Kiwein Mehndi Laway Maa.
Tere Baap Wasiyat Keeti Si Toon Chachey Taun Qurban Howein.
Toon Apna Farz Nibhaya Aye Main Wadiyon Surkhuru Ho Gayi Aan.

2
Farwa Diyaan Sadraan Zeen Utton Jadon Khilriyaan Garam Zameen Uttay.
Tak Haal Hasan De Jaye Da Munh Phair Liya Aye Sab Nabiyaan.
Khaimay Na Bola Ke Kubra Nu Ro Aakhiya Hasan Di Bewah Ne.
Tera Banra Ummat Mar Dita Hun Pa Le Sir Wich Toon Mitiyaan.

3
Ban Gathri Lash-e-Qasim Di Kehya Ro Shabeer Ne Veer Hasan.
Tan Tukray Wekh Ke Qasim Da Tere Jigar De Tukray Bhul Gaya Waan.
 Ijaz Ghareebi Syedaan Di Main Karbal De Wich Wekhi Aye.
Tabeer Khalil De Khuwab Di Si Das Kiwein Chad Ke Main Aawaan.
