1
Kazim Kay Pak Janazay Ko Sonu Monu.
Kazim Ke Pak Janazay Ko Zanjeer-e-Sitam Pehnai Hai.
Yeh Kaisa Sitam Hai Zalim Ne Maiyyat Pul Pe Rakhwai Hai.
Yeh Kaisa Bekas Qaidi Hai Marne Pe Bhi Jo Aazad Nahi.
Lashay Pe Baqiya Se Haye Ik Bibi Roney Aayi Hai.
Yeh Chodah Saal Se Qaidi Tha Our Aaj Bhi Haye Qaidi Hai.
Mazloom Ke Lashay Pe Ab Tak Faryad Kuna Tanhai Hai.
Taboot Ko Mere Maula Ke Mazdoor Utha Kar Latay Hain.
Mazloom Ki Lash Uthane Ko Na Beta Hai Na Bhai Hai.
Tan Ki Zanjeerein Maula Ki Ghurbat Pe Noha Karti Hain.
Our Khak Ne Urr Kar Kazim Ke Lashay Ko Rida Pehnai Hai.
Woh Tauq-e-Gala Ka Aik Nishaan Gardan Pe Abhi Tak Baqi Hai.
Maula Ne Mere Iss Tarha Se Rasam-e-Sajjad Nibhai Hai.
Haq Baat Raza Tum Likh Dena Keh Dena Amanat Our Abbas.
Zahra Ke Bachoon Se Ab Tak Ummat Na Wafa Kar Payi Hai.
