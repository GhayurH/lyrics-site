Hasil hai sharaf Panjtan ke ghulamon ko anokha
Kartay hain ghulaam in kay faqeeri mein bhi shahi

1
Shehan shahi se bhi keemti hai
Habsh ko chor kar us se moo mor kar Sayeda ki
Ye jo Fizza ne ki nokri hai

2
Dehar mein dekh lo har jagah par
Bohat mashoor inki wafa hai
Ek Hain Abbas aur Paak Fizza
Farq in mein bhala kaun sa hai
Mujhse pucho to falq-e-wafa ka
Ek hai chand aur ek chandni hai

3
Ashq beh beh ke ye hain batatay
Ye maqam-e-aqeedat hai kaisa
Koi duniya mein mujhko bataye
Martaba hai mila kisko aisaa
Usko kis naam se main pukaron
Jiskay daman mein Zainab pali hai

4
Seh ke koi dikhaye zara ab
Is ne mushkil jo sar pe sahi hai
Karbala ho ya shaam-e-ghareeban
Har jaga sath hi ye rahi hai
Jis say bandhi thi zalim ne Zainab
Us rasan se Fizza bandhi hai

5
Ja rahay ho magar yaad rakhna
Be ijazat lehad mein na jana
Roub un pe na hargiz jamana
Baat kartay huay sar jhukana
Dar haqeeqat wo meray Nabi ko Fatima ki tarah ladli hai

6
Humne dekha wahan humse pehlay
Ambiya sar jhukaye huay hain
Sakht parda hai lautay hain foran
Unke mehmaan aye huay hain
Le kay beton ko Fizza se milnay
Lehad mein Fatima agayi hai
