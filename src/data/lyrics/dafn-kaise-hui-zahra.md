Dafn Kaise Hui Zahra
Dafan Kaisay Hoin Zahra Yeh Ali Jantay Hain
Kaisay Turbat Mein Utara Yeh Ali Jantay Hain
Dar Ke Girney Se Howa Jis Ka Shikasta Pehlu
Pasliyaan Zakhmi Thin Our Toot Chuka Tha Bazu
Kaisay Utha Woh Janaza Yeh Ali Jantay Hain
Samne Oss Ke Tha Amma Ne Tamacha Khaya
Kitne Din Jane Hasan Kuch Na Kissi Se Bola
Woh Na Bholay Ga Tamacha Yeh Ali Jantay Hain
Ghusal Detey Hoye Zahra Ko Ali Rotay Rahe
Ab Talak Hath Pe Zakhmo Ke Nishaan Baqi They
Kis Ne Mara Tha Woh Durrah Yeh Ali Jantay Hain
Pasliyaan Toot Chukin Jab Yeh Ali Ne Dekha
Karb Se Cheekh Ke Maula Ne Kaha Ya Zahra
Ghusal Kis Tarha Diya Tha Yeh Ali Jantay Hain
Sans Letey Hoye Takleef Se Karahti Thi
Dard Itna Tha Ke Karwat Bhi Na Le Pati Thi
Kab Se Soyi Nahi Zahra Yeh Ali Jantay Hain
Khud Tu Sab Seh Gayi Seh Na Saki Betay Pe Sitam
Maut Se Kam Na Tha Mohsin Ki Judai Ka Gham
Kaisay Ik Maa Ko Sanbhala Yeh Ali Jantay Hain
Baap Ka Pursa Jissay Deney Na Aaya Koi
Roti Rehti Thin Jo Turbat Se Lipat Kar Bibi
Kitna Yaad Aate They Baba Yeh Ali Jantay Hain
Zakhm Kha Kar Bhi Wilayat Ki Hifazat Ki Hai
Ja Ke Dar Dar Pe Youn Zahra Ne Gawahi Di Hai
Kitna Ahsan Hai Oss Ka Yeh Ali Jantay Hain
Kitni Mazloom Thi Zakhmi Thin Nabi Ki Beti
Qabar Bhi Maula Ne ZEESHAN Chupa Di Oss Ki
Hai Kahan Marqad-e-Zahra Yeh Ali Jantay Hain
