1
Zainab Yeh Keh Na Paaein Ke Shabir Mar Gaye
Jab Bhi Kisi Ne Pocha Ke Bhai Kidhar Gaye.
Sheh Ne Sina Ki Nook Se Wadah Nibha Diya.
Barat Le Ke Bete Ki Sherin Ke Ghar Gaye.

2
Jholay Ko Khali Dekh Ke Roti Rahin Rubab.
Mula Se Kaise Pochtin Asghar Kidhar Gaye.
Mula Yeh Ro Ke Kehte The Qasim Ki Lash Par.
Tukre Samaittay Hoye Hum Khud Bikhar Gaye.

3
Barson Ke Bad Utha Hai Pehlu Se Maa Ka Hath.
Jis Ke Sahare Zeen Se Ghazi Utar Gaye.
Bazo Bandhe The Maon Ke Kaise Sanbhaltin.
Kitne Hi Bache Naqoon Se Gir Gir Ke Mar Gaye.

4
Maein Hi Janti Hain Kahan Kon Dafan Hai.
Lashon Se Jin Ke Samne Naqay Guzar Gaye.
Abid Se Koi Poche Na Akbar Dua Karo.
Tum Par Kaise Sham Ka Bazar Kar Gaye.
