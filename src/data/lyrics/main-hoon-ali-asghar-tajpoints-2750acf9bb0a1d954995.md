Tum Samajhtay Ho Keh Mein Chota Sa Ik Bacha Hoon.
Umar Mein Kam Hoon Main Khaybar Ke Ali Jaisa Hoon.

1
Main Bhi La Sakta Hoon Khaimon Mein Utha Kar Darya.
Baat Yeh Kam Hai Keh Abbas Ka Bhatija Hoon.
Yeh Tabasum Mera Talwar Se Kam Thori Hai.
Gardanein Iss Se Mein Nu Lakh Ura Sakta Hoon.

2
Jane Kyun Sham Se Takrati Hain Fojein Ja Kar.
Main Tu Bas Baba Ke Hathoon Mein Abhi Khaila Hoon.
Meri Rag Rag Mein Rawaan Khon-e-Abu Talib Hai.
Karbala Main Tere Khaybar Ko Ura Sakta Hoon.

3
Iss Liye Chomtay Hain Mujh Ko Sabhi Ahl-e-Haram.
Misal-e- Quraan Dil-e- Shabeer Pe Mein Utra Hoon.
Mere Jholay Se Na Khali Gaya Afraz Koi.
Le Ke Mein Baab-e- Hawaij Ki Sand Aaya Hoon.
