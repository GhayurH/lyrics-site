Namaz-e-Fatima 5-8 Lyrics

1
Beemar Rooh-o-Jism Hoon Kar Dein Koi Dawa.
Bibi Har Ik Mareez Ko Mil Jaye Ab Shifa.
Uthay Hain Hath Samne Tere Hai Sar Jhuka.
Umeed Ka Bujhay Na Hamara Kabhi Diya.
Beemar-e-Karbala Ka Jo Sadqa Atta Karein.
Bibi Har Ik Mareez Ke Haq Mein Dua Karein.

2
Mazloom Dono Lal Hain Dono Ka Wasta.
Dono Hi Ba-Kamal Hain Dono Ka Wasta.
Dono Hi Dein Ki Dhal Hain Dono Ka Wasta.
Hasnain La-Zawal Hain Dono Ka Wasta.
Pori Hamari Aas Ho Bibi Jo Laye Hain.
Fitrus Ko Par Hussain Ne Jaise Dilaye Hain.

3
Be-Parda Ahl-e-Bait Safar Karbala Ta Sham.
Syedaniyaan Thin Our Tha Logon Ka Azdham.
Darbar Mein Yazeed(L) Ka Majma Woh Khas-o-Aam.
Abid Ko Khoon Rolata Hai Ab Bhi Woh Haye Sham.
Oss Be-Ridai Our Aseeri Ka Wasta.
Chotay Na Mominaat Se Fizza Ka Rasta.

4
Ghurbat Pe Tere Lal Ki Rote Rahein Gaye Hum.
Matam Sada Hussain Ka Karte Rahein Gaye Hum.
Ashkon Se Iss Ki Qabar Ko Dhotay Rahein Gaye Hum.
Qayam Ka Intezar Bhi Karte Rahein Gaye Hum.
Izn-e-Khuda Se Rizvi Zahoor-e-Imam Ho.
Hum Sab Ka Joan In Ke Ghulamo Mein Naam Ho.
