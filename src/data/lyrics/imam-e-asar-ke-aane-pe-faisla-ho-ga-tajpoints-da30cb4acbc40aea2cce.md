1
Imam-e-Asar Ke Aane Pe Faisla Ho Ga.
Hisab-e-Khon-e-Shaheedan-e-Karbala Ho Ga.
Khuda Ki Tarha Se Rehtey Ho Tum Bhi Parday Mein.
Tumhare Jaisa Bhala Kaun Dosra Ho Ga.

2
Kisay Banana Hai Our Kis Ka Sar Urana Hai.
Yeh Zulfiqar Ko Pehle Se Hi Pata Ho Ga.
Imam Aaein Gaye Tabdeeli Sath Laein Gaye.
Alam Ke Saye Mein Momin Ka Hajj Ada Ho Ga.

3
Jab Hi Tu Milne Lagay Khud Bakhud Jawab Mujhe.
Mere Arezay Ko Maula Ne Parh Liya Ho Ga.
Naqab Aap Ke Chehray Se Hat Bhi Jaye Agar.
Mere Yaqeen Mein Izafa Kahan Bhala Ho Ga.

4
Nazar Ka Dhoka Hai Hum Ko Nazar Nahi Aata.
Woh Aane Wala Nahi Hai Woh Aa Gaya Ho Ga.
Hum Intezar Takallum Na Unn Ka Chorein Gaye.
Hamari Jan Hi Jaye Gi Our Kiya Ho Ga.
