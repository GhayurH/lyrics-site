Muntazir Kab Talak Main Rahon Ae Imam
Meri Sansein Kahin Ho Na Jaein Tamam
Deedar Ki Aakhri Aas Mein
Khat Likh Raha Hai Yeh Khadam Tumhein

1
Kijiye Qabool Aaqa Mera Salam
Bas Ik Azadar Hai Mera Naam
Qabil Nahi Janta Hoon Magar
Kehta Hoon Khud Ko Tumhara Ghulam
Kya Hoon Mujhe Khud Yeh Batlaiye

2
Faryadein Aur Istegase Hazoor
Sarkar Sunte Tu Hoon Gaye Zaror
Be-Chain Khud Bhi Hain Ghaibat Mein Aap
Kab Ho Ga Mula Bata Dein Zahoor
Hum Ko Na Ab Aur Tarpaiye

3
Be-Noor Kab Se Mere Nain Hain
Tanha Azadar-e-Behrain Hain
Kaba Ho Ya Ke Ho Karb-o-Bala
Sab Aap Ke Gham Mein Be-Chain Hain
Sarkar Tashreef Le Aaiye

4
Lafzon Se Pardah Hatati Nahi
Apna Taruf Karati Nahi
Jab Se Gaye Aap Khamosh Hai
Aayat Maani Batati Nahi
Raz-e-Alif Lam Samjahiye

5
Tarteeb Marboot Ho Baat Se
Zahir Jalalat Ho Aayat Se
Kehta Hai Zooq-e-Tilawat Mera
Likha Jo Mula Ne Khud Hath Se
Mujh Ko Woh Quran Parhwaiye

6
Karni Hai Taskheer Sham Aap Ko
Lana Hai Haq Ka Nizam Aap Ko
Ae Waris-e-Zulfiqar-e-Ali
Lena Hai Jab Inteqam Aap Ko
Ghazi Ko Bhi Sath Le Aaiye

7
Hum Jamkaran Aur Sehla Gaye
Ashkon Se Apne Aareeze Likhe
Giriya Kiya Aur Nimazein Parhein
Jo The Jatan Bas Mein Sab Kar Liye
Har Ja Guzarish Thi Aa Jaiye

8
Khat Apna Karta Hoon Iss Par Tamam
Hai Mir Bhi Aap Ka Ik Ghulam
Hai Sibt-e-Jafar Ki Bhi Iltija
Kijiye Talab Ab Humein Ya Imam
Ya Aap Tashreef Le Aaiye
