Shabbir teri azmat ki kasam main shaan-e-Ali dikhla deti

1
Hoti na wasiyat gar teri,  Kufay ki zameen ulta deti

2
1) Abbas ki khuwahar hu bhaiya mil jaati ijazat agar teri
Darbar ullati main asay,  khaibar ki yaad dila deti

3
2) Sun sun kay sadain bachi ki dil chaak howa jata tah magaar
Bay taab thi woh baba kay liye,  main kaisi tumhe bulwa deti

4
3) Ay bhayi Sakina yun tarpi aiwani Yazeedi kamp utha
Tay meray haath bhi jikray howay,  main kaisay usay bhula deti

5
4) Aik haath utha darbar chonka bachi cheeki dum toor diya
Sab qaidi tarap kaar rotay tay,  main kis kis ko pursa deti

6
5) Abbas tah mera na Akbar maa gaash main paari ti dukhiyari
Maiyyat tay zameen par haath bandhay,  main kaisay rassan khulwa deti

7
6) Ghurbat main Sakina rooth gayi zindaan main lahed banwayi hai
Milti jo ijazat Zainab ko,  pehlu main teray dafna deti

8
7) Abbas teri shahzadi kay bay jurm tamachai maar gaye
Agar laash nikalti zindaan say,  main lahkay tujhe dikhla deti

9
8) Ay madar-e-Gohar yeh keh kar marqad peh ghiree mazloom behan
Majboor ti warna main bhaiya,  bachi ko wantan pohncha deti
