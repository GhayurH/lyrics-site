Gar Fazeelat Rasool-Zaadi Ki Zehen E Insaan Mein Sama Jaati

1
Na Hi Bibi Ka Haq Ghasab Hota Aur Na Darbar Fatima Jaati

2
Jis Ne Sab Betiyon Ki Izzat Ki Uski Beti Rulayi Hai Tum Ne
Jis Ki Tazeem Ambiya Kartay Wohi Zahra Satayi Hai Tum Ne
Khaali Darbar Se To Jaati Hoon Par Nahi Kar Ke Badua Jaati

3
Haal Kesay Bayan Karoon Logo Jis Ghari Ghar Batool Ka Hai Jala
Jalta Darwaza Paak Bibi Ke Haye Pehlu Pea An Kar Hai Laga
Boli Fizza Ke Ya Ali Aao Warna Dunia Se Syeda Jaati

4
Meri Tazeem Ko Meray Baba Yaad Hay Sar Jhukaye Uth-Te The
Jab Kabhi Bhi Jahan Mein Roti Thi Mujh Se Pehlay Hi Wo Ro Partay The
Aj Roti Hoon Saray Haste Ho Ho Ke Naraaz Fatima Jaati

5
Pucha Zainab Ne Amma Batlao Kya Musalmaano Ne Salook Kiya
Boli Beti Se Ro Ke Mehrooma Meri Tehreer Ko Hay Chaak Kiya
Meray Jhutlaye Dono Betay Bhi Ghash Ye Keh Kar Batool Kha Jaati

6
Aal E Ahmed Khuda Ki Hujjat Hain Fatima Aal Par Bi Hain Hujjat
Jisko Kethay Ho Masla Bhuli Dar Asal Wo Hijab E Qudrat Hain
Zakhmi Pehloon Ko Lekay Duniya Se Aj Tafseer E Ina Ma Jaati

7
Haan Hasan Gar Saqifa Na Hota Qatl Zahra Ka Na Kabhi Hota
Zarb Khatay Ali Na Kufay Mein Ghar Hasan Ka Janaza Na Aata
Vakeya Karbala Ka N Hota Shaam Zainab Na Be-Rida Jaati
