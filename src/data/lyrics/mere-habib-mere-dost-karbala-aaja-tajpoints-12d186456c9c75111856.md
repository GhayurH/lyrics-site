Mere Habib Mere Dost Karbala Aaja.
Tumhein Bulata Hai Zahra Ka Ladla Aaja.

1
Tumhare Aane Se Sehra Mein Mujh Ko Chain Mile.
Mile Hussain Se Tu Our Tujhe Hussain Mile.
Na Dair Ibn-e-Mazahir Tu Ab Laga Aaja.
Nabi Ke Lal Se Ummat Ne Munh Hi Phir Liya.

2
Sulagte Dasht Mein Ibn-e-Batool Ghair Liya.
Kathan Yeh Waqt Hai Aye Mere Humnawa Aaja.
Ali Ki Betiyaan Humrah Mere Aayi Hain.
Bachane Deen Woh Bache Bhi Sath Layi Hain.

3
Agar Tu Aaye Barhe Unn Ka Hosla Aaja.
Mera Irada Hai Qismat Sanwar Doon Teri.
Wahin Ho Teri Lahad Qabar Ho Jahan Meri.
Habib Jald Mere Pass Nainwa Aaja.

4
Madina Choot Gaya Karbala Mein Aaya Hoon.
Main Ik Azeem Sa Maqsad Bhi Dil Mein Laya Hoon.
Mujhe Moalla Banani Hai Yeh Jagah Aaja.
Umeed Aane Ki Tere Lagaye Betha Hoon.

5
Main Teri Rahoon Mein Aankhein Bichaye Betha Hoon.
Sahra Dene Mujhe Mere Bawafa Aaja.
Itaton Mein Wafaon Mein La-Jawab Hai Tu.
Ali Ke Bete Ka Be-Misal Intehab Hai Tu.

6
Na Intezar Zyda Mujhe Kara Aaja.
 Tauqeer Apne Watan Ko Habib Ne Chora.
Zamane Sare Ko Oss Khush Naseeb Ne Chora.
Jo Khat Mein Mula Ka Lika Howa Parha Aaja.
