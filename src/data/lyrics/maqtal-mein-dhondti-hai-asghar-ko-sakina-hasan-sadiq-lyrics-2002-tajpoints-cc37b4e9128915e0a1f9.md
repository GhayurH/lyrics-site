1
Maqtal Mein Dhondti Hai Asghar Ko Sakina Hasan Sadiq.
Jhola Bhi Mila Khali Asghar Ka Kahin Na.
Kaisay Piyon Main Pani Keh Pani Mein Bhi Tum Ho.
Tumhein Dhondon Kaisay Asghar Kahan Rait Mein Gum Ho.
Tum Se Bichar Ke Bhaiyya Nahi Behan Ko Jeena.
Choti Si Qabar Dekhi Masoma Ne Jis Dam.
Banhoon Mein Ossay Le Kar Karne Lagi Matam.
Khali Qabar Pari Thi Our Gum Tha Nagina.
Khon-e-Jigar Se Likhon Woh Zoor-e-Qalam Do.
Juz Gham Ali Asghar Ke Koi Our Na Gham Ho.
Barhta Hi Rahe Har Dam Iss Gham Ka Khazeena.
Palkon Pe Durray Gham Ko Tum Logo Sajao.
Matam Ko Tum Waseela Bakhshish Ka Banao.
Hai Misal-e-Kashti-e-Nooh Panjtan Ka Safena.
Jis Tarha Zalimo Ne Naizay Mein Proya.
Yeh Dekh Roi Dharti Anbar Bhi Tha Roya.
 Khursheed Ko Phir Bhola Manzar Woh Kabhi Na.
