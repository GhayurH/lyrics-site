Aa raha hai karbala se

qafla sadaat ka

kal talak salaar iska tha alamdare jari

aaj hai salaar iska bediyo wala ali

haye sajjad haye sajjad

qafla saadaat ka hai qafla sadaat ka

jab madine se chala tha qafla sadaat ka

misle hyder paasban abbas tha sadaat ka

kaisi ghurbat ek din may bibiyo par chagayi

ek abid natawan hai aasra sadaat ka

haye sajjad…

qafla saadaat ka…

kal talak abbas ki thi har qadam par ye sada

ay parindo moo chupalo hai safar may sayyeda

aaj ye salaar hai aur shamiyon ki bheed hai

maangta hai har qadam par haye zainab ki rida

haye sajjad…

qafla saadaat ka…

qafla ek wo tha mehmil may thi sab saydaniya

qafla ek ye hai paidal chal rahi hai bibiya

qafla salaar ki ghurbat ka aalam dekh kar

bain karti jaa rahi hai har qadam par bediya

haye sajjad…

qafla saadaat ka…

har ghadi saabir rahe hai dono mere kaarwan

karbala tha ek ka maidan shaam ek ka imtehan

aaj hai rehwar ki gardan may sar abbas ka

abid-e-bemaar ki gardan may hai tauqe giran

haye sajjad…

qafla saadaat ka…

shor karte hai sitamgar taaziyane maarke

muntazir kursi nashin hai itrate athaar ke

qatl ghairat ho rahi hai har gali har mod par

innalillah hai labon par abide bemaar ke

haye sajjad…

qafla saadaat ka…

hai kamar kham aur haatho may asa thaame huay

kis tarha pehchane aakhir fatema sughra isay

aagaya yawar madine may mera maula magar

laut kar aayi na abid ki jawani shaam se

haye sajjad…

qafla saadaat ka…
