1
Teroon Ki Barishion Mein Janaza Hasan Ka Hai.
Tar Khoon Mein Kafan Logo Shah-e-Aman Ka Hai.
Roza-e-Mustafa Pe Kyun Pehre Hain Lagaye.
Shabir Ro Rahe Hain Roze Pe Sir Jhukaye.
Jageer Ka Nahi Yeh Masla Kafan Ka Hai.
Qasim Yateem Kar Ke Tujh Ko Mila Kya Jodha(L).
Bhai Hamara Zalim Tu Ne Kya Maar Dala.
Ye Bain Tu Hassan Ki Har Ik Behan Ka Hai.
Gash Kar Rahi Hai Farwa Zainab Ka Munh Pe Matam.
Kalsoom Ro Rahi Hai Seene Lagaye Qasim.
Manzar Ye Fatima ke Ujray Sehan Ka Hai.
Ghar Loat Ke Janaza Sabaz-e-Qaba Ka Aya.
Arsh-e-Barin Bhi Roya Our Farsh Thar Tharaya.
Roya Qabar Mein Logo Nana Hassan Ka Hai.
Hota Hai Jab Baraamad Taboot Mujtaba Ka.
Aati Hain Karti Matam Wahan Meri Dadi Zahra.
Farman Tu Ye “Johar” Mula Zaman Ka Hai.
