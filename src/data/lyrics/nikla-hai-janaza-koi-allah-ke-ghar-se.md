1
Nikla Hai Janaza Koi Allah Ke Ghar Se Mir Hasan Mir.
Aflak Mein ik Shoor Hai Matam Ke Asar Se.
Afoos Keh Masjid Mein Namazi Giya Mara.
Kuch Kam Nahi Yeh Mehshar Qayamat Ke Asar Se.

2
Zainab Se Yeh Keh Do Keh Woh Dil Khol Ke Roye.
Shabir Ki Hamsheer Hai Rone Ko Na Tarse.
Abbas Zara Zainab-e-Muztar Se Khabardar.
Faryad Mein Hat Jaye Na Chadar Kahin Sar Se.

3
Han Bhol Na Jana Inhein Kofe Ke Makeeno.
Guzrein Gein Yeh Shehzadiyaan Iss Rah Guzar Se.
Ashab Bhi Sun Paye Na Jis Bibi Ki Ahat.
Sar Nangay Wohi Guzre Gi Iss Rah Guzar Se.

4
Shabir Ko Yaad Aa Gayi Khud Apni Sakina.
Zainab Ko Hataya Giya Jab Lash-e-Padar Se.
Yaad Aa Giya Zainab Ko Woh Pehlu-e-Shakista.
Roomal Hataya Giya Baba Ke Jo Sar Se.
