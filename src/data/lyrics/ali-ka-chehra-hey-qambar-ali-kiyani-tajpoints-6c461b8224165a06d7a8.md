Ali Ka Chehra Hey Qambar Ali Kiyani.

1
Jabhi Tu Laiq e Sajdah Ali Ka Chehra Hai.
Khuda Ka Chehra Hi Maula Ali Ka Chehra Hai.
Nabowaton Pe Bhi Wajib Hai Dekhna Iss Ka.
Har Ik Rasool Ka Qibla Ali Ka Chehra Hai.
Ibadaton Ki Ibadat Tu Zikar-e-Zahra Hai.
Magar Ibadat e Zahra Ali Ka Chehra Hai.

2
Jo Wajho Rabeyka Baqi Alaiha Fanin Se Hai.
Woh Zuljalal Ka Chehra Ali Ka Chehra Hai.
Tu Aik Waqt Mein Chalees Ghar Mein Hai Maula.
Kaha Sabhi Ne Keh Har Ja Ali Ka Chehra Hai.
Ali Ke Haq Mein Hadesein Gawahi Deti Hain.
Rasool-e-Pak Ki Dunya Ali Ka Chehra Hai.

3
Nikal Do Meri Aankhein Kaha Yeh Meesum Ne.
Hai Inn Mein Ab Bhi Samaya Ali Ka Chehra Hai.
Khuda Samajh Kar Tu Jis Se Kalam Karta Tha.
Woh Koh-e-Toor Pe Mosa, Ali Ka Chehra Hai.
Khuda Ki Deed Ke Khawahan Ko Bata Qambar.
Jo Rooz-e-Hashar Dikhey Ga, Ali Ka Chehra Hai.
