1
Mehndiyaan Wale Nu Wekhin Aan Sawerey Write Up
Main Toor Charawaan Ga Amari Sab Hukam Aye Tere.
Main Ghoriyaan De Summa Uttay Pehle Te Rang Charhawaan Ga.
Fair Safraan De Shagna Kitay Wich Aap E Was Jawaan Ga.

2
Meri Mehndi De Wich Sham De V Honrey Rang Batheray.
Phulaan Di Kehri Gal Aye Maa Jadon Piyo Kubra Da Aaway Ga.
Jis Pasay Howaan Ga Mainu Apne Qadma Wich Paway Ga.
Main Istaqbal Layi Howaan Ga Odhay Chaar Choferay.

3
Maqtal Wich Veer Hussain Tera Mainu Doja Ghazi Jaye Na Kahey.
Haye Shabar Lazmi Aakhay Ga Beh Beh Ke Bahatar Thawaan Te.
Tasveer Howay Gi Babay Di Har Tukray Te Mere.
Akbar Asghar Diyaan Mawaan San Meri Phophiyaan Dowein Aan Gayiyaan.

4
Tainu Ro Ro Ke Parchaan Gayiyaan Nalay Fakhar V Kardi Jan Gayiyaan.
Tainu Kehn Gayiyaan Tere Qasim Ne Rakhay Maan Batheray.
Mera Wadah Aye Hun Shagna Di Tarteeb Badal De Ga Qasim.
Mehndi De Naa Te Galiyaan Wich Tere Putar Da Howay Ga Matam.

5
Taboot Mera Jadon Nikley Ga Lagay Hoan Gaye Sehrey.
Aye Aakh Ke Gathri Band Keeti Akbar Har Wain Muka Ke Maa.
Quran Samajh Ke Manya Aye Har Hukam Mera Main Razi Aan.
Pawein Cha Gaye Ne Main Ujari Di Dunya Te Haneray.
