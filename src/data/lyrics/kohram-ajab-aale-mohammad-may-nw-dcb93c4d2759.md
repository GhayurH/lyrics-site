kohram ajab aale mohamed may bapa hai

ab sibte nabi behre bagha ran ko chala ha

lipti hui daman se ye kehti thi sakina

mai jaane nahi doongi ye ran dashte bala hai

kohram ajab….

jo bhi gaya wo laut ke ghar may nahi aaya

qasim ka na akbar ka na ammu ka pata hai

kohram ajab….

asghar hai kahan aap gaye thay unhe lekar

kya wo bhi giraftaar-e-bala ran may hua hai

kohram ajab….

aap aaye hai ruksat ke liye faisla karke

kyun zuljana-e-shah bhi dweodi pe khada hai

kohram ajab….

mai jaake lipat jaaungi paon se jo uske

jaayega na rehbaar agar kuch bhi haya hai

kohram ajab….

goya hue lipta ke sakina ko ye shabbir

jo kuch bhi kaho tum ye meri laal baja hai

kohram ajab….

ham jaate hai lene ke liye pani jo mil jaaye

beti ko meri tashnagi had se siva hai

kohram ajab….

farmaya tadap kar ye sakina ne ke baba

pani ka na lo naam mujhe marna rawa hai

kohram ajab….

baba na kabhi maangungi mai ab pani kisi se

is pani ki khatir hi chacha mujhse chuta hai

kohram ajab….

ruqsat ka anees haal raqam kis tarha hoga

ab gham se khalam bhi to lahoo dena laga hai

kohram ajab….
