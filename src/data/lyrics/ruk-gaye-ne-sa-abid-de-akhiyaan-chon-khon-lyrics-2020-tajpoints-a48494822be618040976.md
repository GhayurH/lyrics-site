Ruk Gaye Ne Sa Abid De Akhiyaan Chon Khon Aye Jari.
Aidi Akhiyaan Wich Teraan Wangoon Chub Gayi Aye Bheer Bazari.

1
Wekh Ke Zainab Nu Be Rida Qaid Hoiyaan Wain Kita Abid.
Aj Wekhi Bin Chadar Zainab Soraj Ne Pehli Wari.
Qabar Wich Masooma Bhain De Lashay Nu Rakh Ke Abid Aakhay.
Karbal Di Bahatar Lashaan Taun Teri Lash Si Wadh Ke Bhari.

2
Sham Wal Munh Kar Ke Wain Baqir Kitay Dadi Zainab Wekhay.
Mainu Chad Ke Aj Aa Miliya Aye Tere Qafilay Naal Muhari.
Sangh Te San Takiyaan Jadon Ainay Chadraan Fair Na Akhiyaan Uthiyaan.
Phophiyaan De Sir Wal Nayi Takiya Sajjad Ne Zindagi Sari.

3
Sham Bazaraan Da Aisa Dil Te Si Asar Kadi Yasrab De Agar.
Bazar Choon Abid Langda Si Hondi Maut Di Halat Tari.
Khon Rowan Walay Sabar Saqlain Kita Pani Ohno V Dita.
Aiday Veer Akbar De Seene Wich Jinay Maut Di Barchi Mari.
