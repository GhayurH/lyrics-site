1
Younhi Dar e Zahra Pe Malaik Nahi Jhuktey Hasan Sadiq.
Mehboob-e-Khuda Khud Bhi They Tazeem Ko Uthtey.
Islam Ki Wahdat Ka Taqaza Tu Nahi Tha.
Aye Kash Kabhi Musalmaan Firqoon Mein Na Battay.

2
Salman Zaeefi Mein Khuda Jane Ke Kaisay.
Chuntay Rahe Sarwar Ki Tehreer Ke Purzay.
Afsoos Musalmaan Ne Ossi Ghar Ko Jalaya.
Jis Ghar Pe Tu ALLAH Ke Payamber Bhi They Ruktay.

3
Islam Ka Mafhoom Kabhi Pora Na Hota.
Tatheer Ke Gulshan Mein Agar Phool Na Khiltay.
Makhdoma Ko Ameer Woh Haq Detey Na Detey.
Musalmano Pe Wajib Tha Tazeem Tu Kartay.
