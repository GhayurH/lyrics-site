Akbar Meray Sehriyan Wala Akbar

Zakham Dil Kay Dikhaoon Main Kaisay  Tera Laasha Utaoon Main Kaisay.

Laashay Akbar Pe Shah Jab Ponchay Dil Ko Haato Seh Thaam Kar Bolay

Teri Furqat May Ay Ali Akbar Aag Dil Main Meray Lagadi Hai

Aag Dil Ki Bujaoon Main Kaisay  Tera Laasha Utaoon Main Kaisay.

Tu Hai Kariyal Jawaan Ay Beta Baap Tera Zaeef Hai Kitna

Noor Ankhon Ka Hogaya Rukhsat Mujh Ko Ata Nahin Nazar Khaima

Laikay Khaimon Mai Jaoon Mai Kaisay  Tera Laasha Utaoon Main Kaisay.

Jis Neh Athara Saal Pala Tha Kaisay Dekhegi Woh Tera Laasha

Teri Shaadi Ka Tha Jisay Armaan Kaisay Jhelaygi Vo Tera Sadma

Teri Maa Ko Bataoon Main Kaisay  Tera Laasha Utaoon Main Kaisay.

Dekh Aya Hai Qasid-e-sughra Raah Tak Ti Hai Woh Dukhi Behna

Tujh Ko Bemaar Ney Bulaya Hai Wada Sughra Ka Kyon Bhuladala

Ghum Ye Dil Seh Lagaoon Mai Kaisay  Tera Laasha Utaoon Main Kaisay.

Bhayi Bheno Ka Hai Ajab Rishta Jiska Saani Kahin Nahin Milta

Rokay Sughra Ney Ye Likha Akbar Aik Dukhiya Ki Laaj Rakh Layna

Mar Gaya Tu Bataoon Main Kaisay  Tera Laasha Utaoon Main Kaisay.

Lutaf Jeene Mai Ab Kahan Akbar Dil Seh Uthti Hai Yeh Fughan Akbar

Sub-e-ashur Di Thi Jo Tunay Khoon Rulaigi Wo Azaan Akbar

Teri Suraat Bhulaoon Mai Kaisay  Tera Laasha Utawo Main Kaisay.

Zabt Ghum Kah Woh Haal Hai Beta Dekhu Kaisay Yeh Zakaam Seenay Ka

May Toh Pardes Mai Akela Hun Dekh Kar Zakham Kya Karo Batala

Is Peh Marhum Lagaoon Mai Kaisay  Tera Laasha Utawo Main Kaisay.

Aysi Gurbaat Peh Moou Nahin Morro Mai Hun Tanha Mujhe Na Youn Choro

Kya Guzarti Hai Baap Kay Dil Par Ghum Zada Dil Ko Youn Nahi Toro

Apni Gurbaat Bataoon Mai Kaisay  Tera Laasha Utaoon Main Kaisay.
