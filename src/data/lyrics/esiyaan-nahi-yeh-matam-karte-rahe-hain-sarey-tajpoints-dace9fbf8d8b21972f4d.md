Esiyaan Nahi Yeh Matam Karte Rahe Hain Sarey.
Iss Farz Mein Shamil Hain ALLAH Ke Nabi Pyaray.

1
Rota Furat Bhi Hai Bachoon Ki Tashnagi Par.
Pani Se Peettay Hain Abbas Ko Kinaray.
Main Dekhon Kis Tarha Se Kartay Nahi Ho Matam.
Kariyal Jawaan Ka Lasha Ho Samne Tumharey.

2
Be Goor-o-Be Kafan Hoon Kunbay Tere Ki Lashein.
Khaimay Jala Ke Tere Koi Chadrein Utaray.
Paband Rasiyon Mein Zahra Ki Betiyaan Thin.
Hum Kyun Na Sar Ko Peetein Abid Ko Koray Marey.

3
Ahsaan Hussain Ka Hai Zibh-e-Azeem Ban Kar.
Qurban Kar Ke Apne Hain Bana Liye Tumharey.
 Najfi Hussaini Matam Momin Ki Ibadat Hai.
Karti Hain Hoor-o-Gilma Kartay Hain Chand Taray.
