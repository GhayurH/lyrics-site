Aankhon Mein Liye Hasrat-e-Deedar Areza
Khizr Raah Mein Pahunchne Ko Hai Tayyar Ariza
Ye Shauq-e-Ziyarat Mein Bahot Aage Hai Mujh Se
Is Paar Agar Main Hun To Us Paar Ariza

1
Barhta Hi Chala Jata Hai Jazbaat Ki Rau Mein
Maujon Se Siwa Rakhta Hai Raftar Ariza
Har Haal Mein Manzil Pe Pahunch Kar Hi Rukega
Hai Moataqid-e-Abid-e-Bimar Ariza

2
Ik Bund Bhi Peeta Nahi Daryaon Ka Paani
Pyason Ke Qabile Ka Wafadar Ariza
Ye Soch Ke Duba Hun Arize Ke Bajaaye
Seene Se Laga Lete Hain Sarkar Ariza
