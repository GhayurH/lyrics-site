1
Haye Zalim Chala Dita Khanjar Syed Mudassir Mehdi
Haye Zalim Chala Dita Khanjar.
Ik Galay Te Wala Wala Khanjar.
Karbala Wich Hi Aayi Sham Nazar.
Jadon Zainab Ne Wekhiya Khanjar.
Terah Zarbaan Chala Ke Gardan Te.
Hasiya Qatil Te Ro Piya Khanjar.
Jainday Bosay Rasool Lainda Si.
Oss Galay Te Hai Chal Rehya Khanjar.
Jis Nu Maran Layi Kujh Na Miliya.
Rait Nu E Bana Liya Khanjar.
Ro Ro Zainab Aye Aakhay Ghazi Nu.
Shimar De Hath Wichon Chura Khanjar.
Aliya Sham Jana Kis Welay?.
Kar Deway Ga Aye Faisla Khanjar.
Zakhmi Hath Cha Ke Zakhmi Pehlu Taun.
Bibi Zahra Ne Rokiya Khanjar.
Baad Shabeer De Lahaway Ga.
Ghazi Di Bhain Di Rida Khanjar.
Pak Bibi Di Pak Chadar Nal.
Shimar Ne Aa Ke Ponjhiya Khanjar.
Shimar Zalim Tamachay De Roop Inn.
Hai Sakina Nu Mariya Khanjar.
Mainu Beshak Tu Qatal Kar Zalim.
Meriyaan Bhaina Nu Na Wakha Khanjar.
Bhain Nu Sham Kha Gayi Nasir.
Zakhmi Veran Nu Kha Gaya Khanjar.
