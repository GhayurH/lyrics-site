1
Laraib Ibadat Bhi Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.
La Raib Ibadat Bhi Shahadat Ka Biyaan Hai.
Sajday Ki Jabeen Par Bhi Tu Matam Ka Nishaan Hai.
Hai Kaun Jamat Mein Jo Fuztu Ki Sada De.
Ab Masjid-e-Kofa Mein Namazi Woh Kahan Hai.
Roti Hai Lahu Chashm-e-Ibadat Tujhe Maula.
Sajdah Hai Keh Giriya Hai Azaan Hai Keh Fogaan Hai.
Maula Ke Mawali Hi Nahi Soag Mein Dobay.
Maula Ka Azadar Tu Sab Kaun-o-Makaan Hai.
Haider Ke Yateemo Ka Bura Haal Hai Gham Se.
Gash Khatay Hain Ghamkhuwar Qayamat Ka Samaa Hai.
Tafseer-e-Shahadat Hai Teri Karb-o-Bala Mein.
Gham Seena-e-Aflak Mein Tera Hi Nihaan Hai.
 Sajjad-o- Kaleem Aaye Hain Sar Peettay Rotay.
Har Qatra-e-Khoon Aaj Ajab Noha Kuna Hai.
