zehra ke dularon pa ajab zulmo jafa hai

toofane ghamo dard hai aur aale abaa hai

ay qaayam o qayoom tu sab dekh raha hai

pyase hai gulistane payambar ke gulay tar

lab khushk hai bachon ke har ek fard hai muztar

garmi se ghareebo se bura haal hua hai, tu dekh raha hai

zehra ke….

kehte hai jise log mohamed ka nawasa

afsos hai is baat ka wo shaks hai pyasa

band uske dularon ke liye aab-o-ghiza hai, tu dekh raha hai

zehra ke….

maare gaye jab nehr par abbase dilawar

zainab ko yakheen hogaya ab jayegi chadar

jo aasra mazloomo ka tha tooth chuka hai, tu dekh raha hai

zehra ke….

ek baap pa kaisi ye qayamat ki ghadi hai

qadmo may ghamo dard ki zanjeer padi hai

farzand jawan saamne dam todh raha hai, tu dekh raha hai

zehra ke….

de kisko sada koi nahi sab gaye maare

abbas ke shane kate darya ke kanare

ek din may chaman fatema zehra ka luta hai, tu dekh raha hai

zehra ke….

aaghosh se madar ki jise laaye thay shabbir

pani ke ewaz aada ne ki unki ye tauqeer

baysheer ki gardan may laga teere khaza hai, tu dekh raha hai

zehra ke….

mehman tere bhayd tu behtar hai samajhta

mamoor ka dil isliye khoon rota hai maula

shabbir ka sar shimre layeen kaat raha hai, tu dekh raha hai

zehra ke….
