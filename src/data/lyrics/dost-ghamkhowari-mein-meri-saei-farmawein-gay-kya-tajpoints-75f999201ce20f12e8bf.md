Dost Ghamkhowari Mein Meri Saei Farmawein Gay Kya.
Zakhm ke Bharte Talak Nakhun Nah Barh Jawein Gay Kya?

1
Be Niyazi Had Se Guzri Bandah Parwar Kab Talak.
Hum Kahein Gay Haal-e-Dil Our Aap Farmawein Gay Kya?
Hazrat-e-Naseh Gar Aawein Deedah-o-Dil Farsh-e-Raah.
Koi Mujh Ko Ye Tu Samjah Do Keh Samjahwein Gay Kya?

2
Aaj Waan Taig-o-Kafan Bandhe Howay Jata Hoon Main.
Uzar Mere Qatal Karne Mein Woh Aab Lawein Gay Kya?
Gar Kiya Naseh Ne Hum Ko Qaid Acha Youn Sahi.
Yeh Jonoon-e-Ishq Ke Andaaz Chut Jawein Gay Kya?

3
Khana Zaad-e-Zulf Hain Zanjeer Se Bhaag Gay Kyun?
Hain Girftar-e-Wafa Zindaan Se Ghabrawein Gay Kya?
Hai Aab Iss Mamorah Mein Qeht-e-Gham-e-Ulfat Asad.
Hum Ne Ye Mana Keh Dilhi Mein Rahein Khawein Gay Kya?
