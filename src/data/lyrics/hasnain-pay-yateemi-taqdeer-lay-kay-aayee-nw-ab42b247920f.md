Hasnain pay yateemi, taqdeer lay kay aayee

Mola Ali kay ghum main, mugmoom hai khudaaee

Talwaar o zehar dono, baba kay sir pay baahum

bayto nay yeh virasat, aik aik kar kay paaee

Hi kaanp utha tha Kufa, Zainab ki ah buka say

Jin o Bashar malayak, daytay hainn sub duhaaee

Lo chal basa yateemo, baywaaoo ka sahara

karta tha jo hamesha, dushman say bhi bhalaeo

Aghaaz hogaya hai, karbal ka ya khudaaya

sajday main jub shaki nay, talwaar hai chalaee
