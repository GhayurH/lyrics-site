1
Kaise Bayan Hon Tere Kamalaat Fatima.
Kab Hain Shumar Mein Teri Naimaat Fatima.
Oss Noor Ko Zameen Pe Layi Teri Dua.
Abbas Hum Pe Hai Teri Sogat Fatima.

2
Fitrus Ko Par Farishton Ko Roti Humein Nijat.
Yeh Sab Tere Hi Dar Ki Hai Khairat Fatima.
Langar Tumhare Ghar Ka Farishton Mein Bant Kar.
Fizza Khareed Leti Hai Aayat Fatima.

3
Yeh Tu Hai Aik Bag-e-Fidak Ka Muawza.
Jannat Mein Hain Sabhi Tere Bagaat Fatima.
Dulhan Nabi Ki Ladli Dulha Khuda Ka Lal.
Kya Arsh Pe Charhi Teri Baraat Fatima.

4
Jin Mein Woh Kibriya Bhi Hai Kab Se Chupa Howa.
Qabal Uz Adam Hain Tere Hijabaat Fatima.
Yeh Sab Tere Hi Zikar Ki Nisbat Ka Faiz Hai.
 Shaukat Bhi Ab Hai Sahib-e-Oqaat Fatima.
