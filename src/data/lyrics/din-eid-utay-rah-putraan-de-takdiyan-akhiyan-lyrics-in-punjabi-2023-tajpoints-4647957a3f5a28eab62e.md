Din Eid Utay Rah Putraan De Takdiyan Akhiyan.
Eid Guzar Gayi Sari Rah Tak Tak Maa Hari Thak Gayiyaan Akhiyaan.
Maa Lal Kaindi Rayi Mainu Mila Ghazi.
Aye Roog Maa Jayi Da Tu Muka Ghazi.
De Koi Veer Jawab Aye Tere Naal Sawal Aye Kardiyaan Akhiyaan.
Na Chain Aaway Koi Maa Rondi Reh Gayi Aye.
Maa Apne Lalaan Nu Bolandi Reh Gayi Aye.
Samajh Na Aaway Maa Nu Ro Ro Ke Putraan Nu Labdiyaan Akhiyaan.
Horay Oh Aa Jawan Aye Waham Nayi Janda.
Jaye Samne Marday Sabar Te Aa Janda.
Rokdi Aye Balaan Nu Maa Labdi Lalaan Nu Wasdiyan Akhiyaan.
Main Khab Wekhiya Aye Galay Lagay Dowein.
Na Piyo De Naal Rahe Wichar Gaye Dowein.
Haris Zulm Kamaye Sir Putraan De Laye Dar Gayiyaan Akhiyaan.
Maa:AAL-E-ABDULLAH: Jadon Bazar Aayi.
Latakdi Kofay Andar Wali Di Lash Aayi.
Kehri Jah Te Chori Maa Ujari Di Jori Puchdiyaan Akhiyaan.
