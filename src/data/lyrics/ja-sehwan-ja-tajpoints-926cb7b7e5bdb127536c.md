1
Mushkil Se Ghabrana Kya Jaa Sehwan Ja.
Kar De Ga Shahbaz Bhala Jaa Sehwan Jaa.
Main Ne Apni Mushkil Mein Jab Shair-e-Khuda Ko Pukara.
Aayi Saday-e-Mula Har Ik Mushkil Hal Kar De Ga.
Kafi Hai Shahbaz Mera Jaa Sehwan Ja.
Aal-e-Nabi Ki Aankh Ka Tara Hai Shahbaz Qalandar.
Mula Ali Ka Raaj Dulara Hai Shahbaz Qalandar.
Rad Nahi Kart Koi Dua Ja Sehwan Jaa.
Nam-e-Ali Har Mushkil De Ga Tal Qalandar Saein.
Dil Se Lagao Narah Jholay Lal Qalandar Saein.
Kar Ho Gar Rad-e-Bala Jaa Sehwan Ja.
Lal De Roze De Utay Ghazi De Alam Da Saya.
Ik Wari Jaihra Pohanchiya Utay Khali Hath Na Aaya.
Lenray Sadqa Ghazi Da Ja Sehwan Jaa.
Matamdar Hussain Sakhi Da Lal Qalandar Saein.
Matamdar Nu Pyar Kare Lajpal Qalandar Saein.
Matam kar Ke Mang Lay Dua Jaa Sehwan Ja.
Door Agar Aye Karb-o-Bala Te Door Najaf Di Zameen Aye.
Mashhad Janray Da V Kharcha Tere Kool Nayi Aye.
Aina Te Tu Kar Lay Bhira Ja Sehwan Jaa.
Yeh Hai Wajhi Our:ZILL-E-RAZA: Ke Dil Ki Sadaein Paiham.
Lal Sakhi Aane Nahi Dein Gaye Tujh Tak Koi Bhi Gham.
Matamdar-e-Shah-e-Khuda Jaa Sehwan Ja.
