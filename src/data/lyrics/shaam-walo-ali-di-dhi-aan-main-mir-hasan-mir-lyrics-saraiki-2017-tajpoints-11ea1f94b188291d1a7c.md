1
Shaam Walo Ali Di Dhi Aan Main Mir Hasan Mir.
Shaam Walo Ali Di Dhi Aan Main Maidi Rasiyaan Ta Hun Laha Dewo.
Ayhe Bazar Main V Zainab Haan Maikoon Chadar Maidi Wala Dewo.
Jainda Kalma Tusaan Hiway Parhday Owon Muhammad Di Main Nawasi Haan.
Main Haan Ghairat Nabi De Wehray Di Hun Ta Bazaar Band Kara Dewo.
Main Haan Tauqeer Apren Babe Di Aprin Amari Da Dukhiya Pasa Haan.
Main Haan Mafhoom-e-Innama Logo Maidi Tauqeer Kar Adda Dewo.
Maidi Masooma Dhi Sakina De Pair Zakhmi Nayi Khak Te Aanday.
Aindi Gardan Choon Khool Ke Rasiyaan Aykoon Kuj Dair Taan Bilha Dewo.
Main Bhirawaan Di Maut Wekhi Aye Wekhe Putraan De Be-Kafan Lashay.
Maikoon Rowan Da Waqt Nayi Miliya Maikoon Matam Di Saf Wicha Dewo.
Oss Zahra Di Dhi Main Zainab Haan Jais Nasraniyaan Adab Kita Aye.
Main Fidaq Nayi Ridawaan Mangdi Aan Maikoon Tableeg Da Sila Dewo.
Mera Wadah Aye Main Chukaindi Aan Apne Babe Ameer De Badle.
Maidi Bharjaiyaan Te Yateema Koon Maiday Hise Di Na Saza Dewo.
Main Haan Azmat Janab-e-Mariyam Di Maikoon Zahra Di Aabro Aahdahin.
Day Ke Burqay Main Ujari Dukhiya Koon Bheer Lokaan Di Aye Hata Dewo.
