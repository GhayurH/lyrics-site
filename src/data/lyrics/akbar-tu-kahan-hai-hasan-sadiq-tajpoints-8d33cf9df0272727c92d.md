Akbar Tu Kahan Hai Hasan Sadiq Lyrics
Kehti Hai Sahar Gham Ki Akbar Tu Kahan Hai.
Sajday Bhi Hain Afsurda Betab Azaan Hai.
Shadi Ka Teri Waqt Hai Ya Waqt-e-Shahadat.
Sehra Hai Duaon Ka Our Maa Ki Zabaan Hai.
Lariyaan Tere Sehrey Ki Chomein Tere Seene Ko.
Afsoos Magar Tere Seene Mein Sina Hai.
Islam Ke Daman Mein Our Iss Ke Siwa Kya Hai.
Ik Sajdah Hai Ik Zarbat Our Aik Azaan Hai.
Hai Pyas Ke Aalam Mein Akbar Ko Ajal Aayi.
Our Samne Aankhon Ke Darya Bhi Rawaan Hai.
Jate Hain Ali Akbar Marne Ke Liye Ran Mein.
Zainab Ke Liye Kitna Pur Dard Samaa Hai.
 Rehan Yeh Kehti Thi Madar Ali Akbar Ki.
Kya Dar Hai Laeeno Ka Mera Lal Jawaan Hai.
