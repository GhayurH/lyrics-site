1
Kis Ki Hai Yaad Aati Kyun Aisay Satata Hai.
Iss Chand Ke Charhne Se Lut Jaye Ga Yeh Gulshan.
Yeh Chand Bhi Ro Ro Ke Sughra Ko Batata Hai.
Hoti Hai Koi Aahat Darwazay Pe Jati Hai.
Aata Nahi Jab Koi Dil Dobta Jata Hai.
Kehti Hai Ro Ke Sughra Bhaiyya Ali Akbar Ko.
Behno Ko Koi Bhai Youn Chor Ke Jata Hai.
Kya Bhai Ke Seene Mein Dekhi Hai Lagi Barchi.
Shabbir Ki Beti Ko Kyun Khuwab Darata Hai.
Kyun Aate Nahi Wapis Baba-o-Chacha Ghazi.
Na Hi Koi Sughra Ko Haye Pas Bulata Hai.
Koi Tu Khabar Deday Kis Haal Mein Hai Asghar.
Woh Khon Mein Doba Kyun Mujh Ko Nazar Aata Hai.
Dekha Tha Khuwab Shab Mein Zeeshan Ro Ke Uthi.
Ik Baap Jawaan Lasha Maqtal Mein Uthata Hai.
