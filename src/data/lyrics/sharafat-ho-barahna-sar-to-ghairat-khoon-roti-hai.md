Sharafat ho barahna sar to ghairat khoon roti hay
Jo balon ki banay chadar to ghairat khoon roti hay

1
Kisi ghayyur ki beti Muslamano ki basti may
Barahna sar phiray dar dar to ghairat khoon roti hay

2
Muarrikh yeh hi likhti hain watan se door jangal may
Sharifoon ka lutay jo ghar to ghairat khoon roti hay

3
Jisay Zehra ne pala ho haya ki goad may logo
Wohi mangay agar chadar to ghairat khoon roti hay

4
Kisi ke khanwaday par ho balwa ghair logon ka
Bicharr jaye agar dukhtar to ghairat khoon roti hay

5
Kisi Toqeer wali se kaha jaye ke bhai ke
Tu lashay par na matam kar to ghairat khoon roti hay
