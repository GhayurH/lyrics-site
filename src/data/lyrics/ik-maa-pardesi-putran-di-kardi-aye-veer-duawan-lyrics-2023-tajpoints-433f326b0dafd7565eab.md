1
Ik Maa Pardesi Putran Di Kardi Aye Veer Duawan
Na Mere Wangoon Maa Koi Putraan Diyaan Wekhay Rahwaan.
Din Kal Da Kyun Mainu Veera Wich Khon De Dobiya Lagda Aye.
Chup Kar Gayiyaan Veer Hawawaan Ne Aj Waqt V Rukya Lagda Aye.
Main Maa Haan Ghazi Das Mainu Kiwein Veran Eid Manawaan.
Mainu Haal Dasa Mere Putraan Da Mere Sah Kyun Ruk Ruk Janday Ne.
Aynj Lagda Aye Jiwein Lal Mere Mainu Apne Kool Bulanday Ne.
Dukh Hoan Jinna Nu Putraan De Kiwein Jiondiyaan Ne Oh Mawaan.
Zahra De Lal Da Ik Athro Meri Sari Zindagi Thori Aye.
Akbar Te Asghar Da Sadqa Main Pali Veran Jori Aye.
Na Roway Veer Shabeer Mera Main Bachray Waar Wakhawaan.
Ik Pasay Lal Way Muslim Da Zindan Che Wain Aye Paway.
Mere Veer Di Ban Ke Maa Koi Ainu Gash Wichon Hosh Karaway.
Aj Raat Main Kiwein Maut Kolon Maa Apna Veer Bachawaan.
Aynj Wekhiya Ro Ke Aj Ghazi Jiwein Ujari Bhain Nu Takiya Aye.
Ghazi Toon Rowan Wala Nayi Kis Dard Kaleja Katiya Aye.
Rayi Kaindi Veer Nu Oh Najfi Kujh Das Main Mar Na Jawaan.
