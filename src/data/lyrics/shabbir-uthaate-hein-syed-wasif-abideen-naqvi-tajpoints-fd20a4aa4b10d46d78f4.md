1
Shabbir Uthaate Hein Syed Wasif Abideen Naqvi.
Che Mah Ke Asghar Ko Shabeer Uthatay Hain.
Quraan Ki Tarha Apne Seene Se Lagatay Hain.
Shabeer Ki Aankhon Mein Aanso Hain Jo Yeh Asghar.
Lagta Hai Na Aao Gay Maa Kehti Thi Yeh Ro Kar.
Maqtal Ki Taraf Jaisay Sheh Tujh Ko Le Jate Hain.
Hal Min Kaha Maqtal Mein Shabeer Ne Jis Dam Tu.
Hal Min Ki Sada Sun Kar Be Sheer Wahin Khud Ko.
Nusrat Mein Shah e Dein Ki Jholay Se Giratay Hain.
Be Haal Hoye Asghar Jab Pyas Ki Shiddat Se.
Asghar Ko Sakina Yeh Kehti Rahi Riqqat Se.
Abbas Abhi La Kar Tumhein Pani Pilatay Hain.
Aye Zalimo Pani Do Be Shak Na Mujhe Lekin.
Ik Ghont Issay De Do Pyasa Hai Mera Kam Sin.
Yeh Keh Ke Sheh Asghar Ko Reti Pe Litatay Hain.
Asghar Ke Qatilon Se Sheh Ro Ke They Farmatay.
Aye Zalimo Pyason Par Kya Zulm Hain Youn Dhatay.
Kya Chotay Se Bachay Par Youn Teer Chalatay Hain.
Asghar Bhi Surkhuru They Gardan Pe Teer Kha Ke.
Dikhlaon Ga Main Ja Kar Nana Ko Yehi Keh Ke.
Sheh Khon-e-Ali Asghar Chehray Pe Lagatay Hain.
Maa Dekh Na Paye Gi Lasha Ali Asghar Ka.
Khaimon Ki Taraf Chalte Kayi Bar Sheh Ne Socha.
Kabhi Aagay Qadam Rakhtay Kabhi Pichey Hatatay Hain.
Tan Se Ali Asghar Ka Sar Kaat Na Lein Azlam.
Pamal Na Ho Jaye Lasha Tha Asad Yeh Gham.
Yeh Soch Ke Asghar Ki Sheh Qabar Banatay Hain.
