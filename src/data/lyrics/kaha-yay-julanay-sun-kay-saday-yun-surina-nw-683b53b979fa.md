Kaha yay julanay sun kay saday yun surina

-Mein aa raha hoon madad ko, tehr na baba zara

-Ajibay rung hay kaliq key baynyazi kay

Labay furat tay katay haath abbas’ ghazi kay

Alawd’ saqiyay kasar, aur alathash key sada

-Agar chay pyasa hoon, laykin thumharay khatil bhi

Laraz utangay jahan kay ay wanay batil bhi

Mein ehsay jeeton ga bazi kay roo paray gi kaza

-Zehr may duba howa teer’ kushk halk tayra

Bhula sakon gi main kehsay, bhala yay dard’ tayra

Yay teer’ wazni hay tujsay samal na beta zara

-Janabay Adam o Eesa, O Nooh o saray nabi

Shukar ghuzar’ hay tayray nabi nanay wasi

Tayray lahu nay bachi hay ambiya key ana

-Uto nasib mayray ma say kohi baat’ karo

Mayrey udasey o mungo may kohi rung bharo

Jo kurtya behjay hain Sughra nay, pehkay ma ko dika

-Pyas’ tayray shehzaday key rakam kar ta rahon

Tumhari behti kay saffron ka haal liktha maron

Ali key pyari baho say, Raza key hay yay dua
