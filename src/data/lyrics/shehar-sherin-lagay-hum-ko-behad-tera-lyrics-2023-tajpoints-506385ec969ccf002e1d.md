1
Shehar Sherin Lagay Hum Ko Behad Tera.
Kiya Shehad Se Hai Makhuz Mashhad Tera.
Hai Tabiyat Ko Tera Tabaruk Pasand.
Aankh Roza Hi Mangay Hai Sarmad Tera.

2
Alam-e-Aal-e-Ahmad Kahey Tujh Ko Aal.
Imtiyazi Hai Rutba-e-Amjad Tera.
Mujh Ko Tauheed Aayi Nazar Ko Ba Ko.
Main Ne Hal Kar Ke Dekha Hai Abjad Tera.

3
Kul Nizam-e-Khudai Ka Malik Khuda.
Woh Muhammad Ka Our Hai Muhammad Tera.
Kya Shifat Ki Naimat Hai Mumkin Raza?.
Jantay Hain Keh Yasin Hai Jad Tera.

4
Hum Ne Dekha Hai Gham Se Woh Aazad Hai.
Jo Dil-o-Jan Se Hai Moqaiyyad Tera.
Main Ne Dekha Nahi Itni Taseer Hai.
Shair Kya Hoon Gay Dekhon Jo Marqad Tera
