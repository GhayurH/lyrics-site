Sub^ Hay Jihad^ Shaam^ Ghariban may Dhal Gayi

Kaymay jalay tho chadar^, Zahra bhi jal gayi

-Akbar kay gharmay koon^ key dahray na ruk sa key

Barchi ka kyat ha jehsay, nikala nikal gayi

-Asghar ko daikh kar shahay bay khas key gowd may

Laytey huwi balayn, layd^ tak ajalgayi

-Asghar nay teer^ kha kay shikayat^ na key koyi

Chup^ hogaay kay jehsay, tabiyat behl gayi

-Ruksat huway imam^ tho yad^ ga yay chcha

Daman pakar kay bali, Sakina machal gayi

-Towq o rasan tho hain jo shadat^ na mil saki

Bemaray Karbala key, bhi hasrat nikal gayi
