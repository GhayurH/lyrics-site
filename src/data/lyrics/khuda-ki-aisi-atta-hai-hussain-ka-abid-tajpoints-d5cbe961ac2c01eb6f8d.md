1
Khuda Ki Aisi Atta Hai Hussain Ka Abid.
Hussainiyat Ki Sada Hai Hussain Ka Abid.
Ho Sath Madah Sara Jibreel Mere Jab.
Sunaon Aapko Kiya Hai Hussain Ka Abid.

2
Ali Hai Khud Bhi Ali Bhai Our Ali Dada.
Shujaton Ki Baqa Hai Hussain Ka Abid.
Sajood Karne Lagay Hain Tawaf Kagaz Par.
Qalam Ne Jab Se Likha Hai Hussain Ka Abid.

3
Zamane Bhar Ke Marezo Utho Baqiya Chalo.
Shifaein Bant Raha Hai Hussain Ka Abid.
Yazid-e-Waqt Ka Naam-o-Nishaan Mitanay Ko.
Woh Dekho Sham Chala Hai Hussain Ka Abid.

4
Yeh Eid-e-Zahra Hai Khushiyaan Manana Wajib Hain.
Keh Aaj Hi Tu Hansa Hai Hussain Ka Abid.
Kissi Bhi Ghair Ko Afraz Doon Sada Kyun Kar.
Mujhe Nawaz Raha Hai Hussain Ka Abid.
