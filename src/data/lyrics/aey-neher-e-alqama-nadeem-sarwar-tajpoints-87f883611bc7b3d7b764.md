Aey Neher E Alqama Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics
Aaj Bhi Iss Bekasi Par Ro Rahi Hai Karbala.

1
Alqama Behti Rahi Our Qafila Pyasa Raha.
Pyasa Tha Tere Samne Kunba Rasool Ka.
Aye Nehar-e-Alqama Tera Mehman Tha Hussain.
Kyun Tere Pas Reh Ke Bhi Oss Ko Mila Na Chain.

2
Tujh Se Hai Yeh Gila Aye Nehar-e-Alqama.
Khaimay They Ahl-e-Bait Ke Kitne Tere Qareeb.
Pyasay Hi Tere Samne Woh Mar Gaye Ghareeb.
Roti Thi Karbala Aye Nehar-e-Alqama.

3
Mashkeza Jis Ke Hath Mein Tha Doosh Par Alam.
Sahil Pe Tere Aaye They Abbas Ke Qadam.
Rutba Tujhe Mila Aye Nehar-e-Alqama.
Sokhi Pari Thi Mashk-e-Sakina Tarai Mein.

4
Do Bond Pani Kya Na Tha Sari Khudai Mein.
Haye Tu Ne Yeh Kya Kiya Aye Nehar-e-Alqama.
Ik Bekasi Si Ahl-e-Haram Par Tamam Thi.
Kitni Udas Sham-e-Ghareebaan Ki Sham Thi.

5
Aye Wa Musibata Aye Nehar-e-Alqama.
Nohon Mein Hain Jo Tazkira-e-Alqama Hilal.
Charon Taraf Hai Aaj Bhi Ik Matam-e-Malal.
Jari Hai Yeh Azza Aye Nehar-e-Alqama.
