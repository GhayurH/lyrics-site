1
Baba Mera Veer Mariya Zalimaa Be Khata
Ik Din Che Baba Kayi Wari Main Veer Nu Marda Wekhiya.
Baba Shabir Utey Maut Ban Ke Aya Sab Tu Okha Wela.
Jadon Barchi Khichdiyaan Akbar Da Jigar V Bahir Aa Giya.

2
Jandiyaan Khaimiyaan Choon Mar Giya Veer Mera Jadoon Takiyaan Dhiyaan.
Ayho Wain Aakhri Kita Kubra Te Sakina Alvida.
Haye Bara Roya si Jadoon Pochiya Farwa NE Mere Veer Kolon.
Das Veer Mere Qasim Ka Kya Har Ik Tukra Lab Giya.

3
Nanay Asghar Nu Jadon Teer Wajiya Baba Maut Da Manzar Si.
Mera Veer Othe Mar Janda Jaye Na Asghar Hans Ke Wekhda.
Kithe Abbas Giya Aye Na Dasiya Bibi Na Hi Haider Pochiya.
 Saqlain Jaan Gaye Maula Jadoon Wekhi Zainab Be-Rida.
