1
Jab Raat Ko Ghar Mein Na Tumhein Paye Gi Sughra Mar Jaye Gi Sughra Hasan Sadiq.
Dewaron Se Sar Dekhna Takraye Gi Sughra Mar Jaye Gi Sughra.
Baba Mujhe Ab Tum Se Shikayat Nahi Koi.
Ro Ro Ke Tumhein Jaan Se Guzar Jaye Gi Sughra Mar Jaye Gi Sughra.

2
Aawaz-e-Azaan Kan Mein Jab Aaye Gi Baba.
Tanhai Mein Bhai Ko Kahan Paye Gi Sughra Mar Jaye Gi Sughra.
Tum Ghar Se Gaye Jab Se Mera Haal Na Pocha.
Tum Khud Hi Kaho Kis Tarha Jee Paye Gi Sughra Mar Jaye Gi Sughra.

3
Veeran Nazar Aaye Ga Jab Aap Ka Hujrah.
Aankhon Se Lahu Qalb Ka Barsaye Gi Sughra Mar Jaye Gi Sughra.
 Rehan Magar Kaun Yeh Sughra Ko Bataye.
Jab Aisi Madine Mein Khabar Paye Gi Sughra Mar Jaye Gi Sughra.
