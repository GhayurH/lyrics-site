Tashnagi Hoton Pe Le Kar Chal Diye Asghar Maidaan

Maa Ko Ab Tak Yaad Hai, Khushk Hai Uski Zubaan

Godh e Madar Se Liya, Haathon Pe Jab Shabbir Ne

Saamne Hurmal Ke Jaa, Ke Yeh Kaha Beysheer Ne

Torh Doon Ga Has Ke Baba, Aise Zaalim Ki Kamaan

Hurmala Ke Teer Ne, Sookhi Zubaan Ko Tarr Kiya

Haq Risalat Ka Adaa, Yun Kalmago Ne Kar Diya

Baba Ke Haathon Pe Lee Hain, Pyaase Ne Chund Hichkiyaan

Khaymey Mai Baba Mera, Lasha Na Le Kar Jaaiye

Maa Pareshaan Hogi Mer,i Unko Na Kuch Batlaaiye

Aapke Chehre Se Khoon Ke, Dekhlayngi Khud Nishaan
