1
Baba Mai Rul Gye Aan Azhar Ali Khan – Ahsan Ali Khan.
Baba Main Rul Gayi Aan Baba Main Rul Gayi Aan.
Kita Wain Sakina Ro Ro Ke Jad Babay Da Sir Paya.
Daswein Di Asar Welay Pochiya Jo Tera Baba.
Tere Baaj Tere Qatil Ne Main Ujari Te Hath Chaya.
Ag Layi Baba Zalim Ne Saday Khaimiyaan Nu Jis Dam.
Mera Kurta Jal Gaya Baba Zalim Nu Taras Na Aaya.
Haye Baba Wekh Mere Kana Choon Khon Jari.
Zalim Ne Mera Dur Baba Be Dardi Naal Lahaya.
Main Dadi Zahra Wangoon Ho Gayi Zaeef Baba.
Dukh Dard De Paharaan Ne Rang Walaan Da Badlaya.
Unwan Ranj-o-Gham Da Ban Gayi Masoom Bachi.
Parwaz Rooh Ne Keeti Haye Sir Seene Naal Jo Laya.
 Osaaf Sari Zindagi Abid Aye Keh Ke Roya.
Jalay Kurtay Naal Zanjeeraan Masoma Nu Dafnaya.
