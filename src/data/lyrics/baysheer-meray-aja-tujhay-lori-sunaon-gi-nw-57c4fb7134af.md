Be Sheer mere aa ja tujhe lori sunaungi
Apne Ali Asghar ko godi meiṅ sulaungi

۱
Ae laal tasawwur meiṅ de de ke tujhe lori
Maqtal meiṅ tere khaali jhule ko jhulaungi

۲
Milne ke liye maaṅ se kuchh der ko aa jaao
Mujh dukhiya pe kya guzri sab haal sunaungi

۳
Jaate hue khaime se jo tu ne kiye beta
Un tere ishaaroṅ ko maiṅ bhul na paaungi

۴
Gar fauj-e-sitam aayi aur chhini meri chaadar
Is dasht-e-biyaabaaṅ meiṅ maiṅ kis ko bulaungi

۵
Aana ho agar mushkil aawaaz do maadar ko
Maqtal se tumheṅ lene maiṅ khud chali aaungi

۶
Godi meiṅ tujhe le kar maiṅ socha ye karti thi
Is sher ko maiṅ apne Abbas banaungi

۷
Ye kehte hue Gohar ghash kar gayi wo maadar
Maiṅ tere bina Asghar waapas nahiṅ jaaungi
