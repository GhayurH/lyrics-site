Baysheer meray aja tujhay lori sunaon gi

Apnay Ali Asghar ko, godi main sulaon gi

aye lal tasuwur main day day kay tujhay lori

maqtal main teray khali jholay ko jhulaon gi

milnay kay liye maa say kuch dair ko ajao

mujh dukhiya pay kiya guzri sab haal sunaon gi

Jatay howe khemay say jo tu nay kiye beta

Un teray isharon ko main bhool na paon gi

Gar foojay sitam ayi aur chini meri chadar

Is dashtay bayaban mai, main kiss ko bulaon gi

Ana ho agar mushkil awaz do madar ko

Maqtal say tumhain lainay main khud chali aon gi

Gaudi main tujhay lay ker main soocha yeh kerti thi

Is shair ko main apnay Abbas banon gi

Yeh kehtay howe Gauhar gash kar gayi woh madar

Main teray bina Asghar wapas nahi jaon gi
