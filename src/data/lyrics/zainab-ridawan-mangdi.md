Zainab ridawan mangdi agaiye vich bazaaran.

1
Bay aasaran tay qaidi,khari rovay majmeya vich
Ehday naal parday daraan.

2
1) Saatar kadam da pand si pandara azaana suniya, haye
Ronday karay si qaidi, lag lag kay sang deewara.

3
2) Saab turr gaye watan nu kaliyan Sakina rehgaye,haye
Ehnu maar kay vi na bhuliya, haye Shimaar diyan maara.

4
3) Roomi tay Shaami saaray baitay si kursiya tay, haye
Zainab khari si rondi, jiday veer saan ataara.

5
4) Ujri Rubab sochay Asgharday khol hovay, haye
Tenu gaud vich kadava, teray vaal vi savara.

6
5) Kisay puchiya Abidi tu kyu khoon roh raye ho, haye
Farmaya Shaam aakay, mai hogaya beemara.

7
6) Dhosak abaad karna mataam day munkara nay, haye
Janaat wa soni Akhtar Zahra day pursay dara
