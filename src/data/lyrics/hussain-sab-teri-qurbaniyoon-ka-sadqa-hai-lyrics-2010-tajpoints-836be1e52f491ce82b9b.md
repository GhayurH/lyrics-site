1
Hussain Sab Teri Qurbaniyoon Ka Sadqa Hai.
Jo La Ilaha Ka Baqi Jahan Mein Kalma Hai.
Tabah Deen Ka Maiyar Kar Diya Hota.
Yazeediyat Ne Tu Mismar Kar Diya Hota.
Jo Apni Asal Pe Qayam Wojod-e-Kaba Hai.
Jo Hur Ki Tarha Se Chahein Naseeb Chamkane.
Qadam Qadam Pe Hain Mojood Yeh Azza Khane.
Khula Howa Hai Jo Zamane Pe Bab-e-Tauba Hai.
Lagaye Rakhi Hai Har Dam Hussainiyat Dill Se.
Jahan Ko Itni Hai Nafrat Tumhare Qatil Se.
Yazeed Naam Bhi Rakhna Gunah Samjha Hai.
Woh Marifat Ho Itat Ho Ya Shifat Ho.
Shifa Ho Rizq Ho Aulad Ho Ya Janat Ho.
Tere Sagheer Ke Jholay Se Jo Bhi Milta Hai.
Qasam Khuda Ki Yehi Keh Rahe Hain Sare Nabi.
Yeh Kainat Humein Yaad Bhi Nahi Rakhti.
Hamara Naam Jo Dunya Mein Koi Leta Hai.
Kahan Yeh Khak Ke Paikar Kahan Khuda-e-Jali.
Yeh Tere Zikar Se Mairaj Aadmi Ko Mili.
Jo La-Shareek Hai Woh Bhi Shareek Hota Hai.
 Wajhi Our Takallum Ki Ban Gayi Qismat.
Hai Tere Zikar Se Mula Mili Hoi Izat.
Jo Matami Hai Tea Sar Utha Ke Chalta Hai
