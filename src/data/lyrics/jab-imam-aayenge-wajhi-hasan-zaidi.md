Jab Imam Aayenge Wajhi Hasan Zaidi

1
Hal-e-Gham Sunaein Gaye Jab Imam Aayenge
Zakhm-e-Dil Dikhaein Gaye Jab Imam Aayenge
Mehfilein Jamaein Gaye Jab Imam Aayenge
Bam-o-Dar Sajaein Gaye Jab Imam Aayenge
Jashan Hum Manaein Gaye Jab Imam Aayenge
Zakhm Hain Abhi Taza Maka Aour Madina Ke

2
Sham-o-Kofa Karb-o-Bala Hum Bhula Nahi Sakte
Az Saqeefa Ta Ayeen Dam Ghair Se Nahi Pohanche
Jitne Dukh Uthaye Hain Hum Ne Kalma Goyon Se
Aik Ik Chukaein Gaye Jab Imam Aayenge
Ye Hamari Makar-o-Riya Shatrana Ayyari
Kya Hamare Nohon Salam Our Yeh Azadari

3
Kitna Hai Khaloos Unn Mein Kis Qadar Riya Kari
Kya Imam Ki Khatir Hum Ne Ki Hai Teyari
Kaise Munh Dikhaein Gaye Jab Imam Aayenge
Al-Ajal Jo Kehte Hain Aa Gaye Tu Kya Ho Ga
Kya Hai Apni Teyari Paish Hum Karein Gaye Kya
:SIBT-E-JAFAR: Apna Tu Kul Yehi Hai Sarmaya

4
Hamd-o-Naat-o-Soz-o-Salam Our Manqabat Noha
Hum Yehi Sunaein Gaye Jab Imam Aayenge
Maut Un Ke Mohibon Ko Aati Nahi
Aa Bhi Jaye Tu Phir Bach Ke Jati Nahi
Maut Se Kya Khatar Maut Se Kya Hazar
Iss Se Bachna Bhi Kya Aa Gayi Aa Gayi

5
Jab Khuda Ko Pukara Tu Ali Aa Gaye
Jab Ali Aa Gaye Tu Zindagi Aa Gayi
Ghusal-e-Maiyyat Na Kehna Mere Ghusal Ko
Ujlay Malbos Ko Mat Kafan Naam Do
Main Chala Hoon Ali Se Mulaqaat Ko
Jis Ki Thi Aarzu Woh Ghari Aa Gayi

6
Mujh Koi Gham Nahi Hai Magar Aye Hussain Tera
Meri Fikar Ki Hai Manzil Safar Aye Hussain Tera
Aye Jina Ke Shehzaday Tu Zameen Pe Aa Gaya Kyun
Keh Musalsal Ibtila Hai Safar Aye Hussain Tera
Na Dobara Hazari Gar Ho Naseeb-e-Sibt-e-Jafar
Keh Phir Oss Ki Samt Se Ho Guzar Aye Hussain Tera
