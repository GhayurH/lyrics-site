Bhai Ka Lasha Mir Hasan Mir Lyrics
Bhai Ko Khuda Bhai Ka Lasha Na Dikhaye.
Lashay Pe Alamdar Ke Sheh Kehte They Haye.
Kya Sani-e-Zahra Ko Main Batlaon Ga Bhaiyya.
Main Lash Se Uth Kar Tu Nahi Jaon Ga Bhaiyya.
Ab Shimar Yahin Aa Ke Churi Mujh Pe Chalaye.
Phat Jaye Ga Gham Se Mera Dil Tham Lo Abbas.
Ab Dair Bohat Ho Gayi Ghar Ko Chalo Abbas.
Aisa Na Ho Maqtal Mein Sakina Chali Aaye.
Ab Hathoon Mein Larzah Hai Kahan Jan Rahi Hai.
Mazloom Ki Ghurbat Mein Kamar Toot Gayi Hai.
Ya Rab Kissi Dushman Pe Bhi Yeh Waqt Na Aaye.
Ho Gaya Marna Sitam Abbas Ka.
Abbas Pani Lao, Abbas Pani Lao.
Hathoon Mein Khali Kozay Bachay Liye Hoye Hain.
Girday Sakina Dekho Halqa Kiye Hoye Hain.
Bachay Na Jane Kab Ka Pani Piye Hoye Hain.
Bin Aab Na Mar Jaein Pani Inhein Pilao.
Pehlu Mein Sada Main Ne Bithaya Tumhein Bhai.
Jis Bhai Ne Godi Mein Uthaya Tumhein Bhai.
Woh Bhai Bhala Kaisay Teri Lash Uthaye.
Maa Behnein Hain Humshakal-e-Payamber Ke Hawalay.
Main Mashk-o-Alam Karta Hoon Akbar Ke Hawalay.
Himmat Nahi Shabeer Mein Jo Khaimon Mein Jaye.
Sadaat Hain Yeh Sham Ke Lashkar Ko Batao.
Jate Hoye Yeh Shimar Se Kehte Hoye Jao.
Woh Meri Sakina Ko Tamachay Na Lagaye.
Toot Gayi Aas Meri Toot Gayi Aas.
Dair Hoi Mere Chacha Jan Ko Sidharay.
Akbar-o-Baba Hain Gaye Nehar Kinaray.
Aray Amma Mera Qalb Jala Pyas Ke Marey.
Toottay Jate Hain Mere Dil Ke Saharey.
Akbar Ko Abhi Jane Do Hum Ne Tu Kaha Tha.
Aoulad Tu Mil Jati Hai Bhai Nahi Milta.
Shabeer Kahan Se Tumhein Ab Dhond Ke Laye.
Sadaat Yeh Kehte Hain Keh Hum Pyasay Rahein Gay.
Tum Ne Na Piya Pani Tu Hum Bhi Na Peein Gay.
ALLAH Humein Pyasa Hi Dunya Se Uthaye.
Mur Mur Ke Takay Jate They Abbas Ka Lasha.
Girtay Kabhi Uthtey They Takallum Sheh-e-Wala.
Akbar Bari Mushkil Se Sanbhalay Hoye Aaye.
