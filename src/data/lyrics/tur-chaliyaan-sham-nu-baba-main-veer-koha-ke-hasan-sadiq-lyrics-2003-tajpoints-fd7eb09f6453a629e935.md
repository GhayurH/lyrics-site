1
Tur Chaliyaan Sham Nu Baba Main Veer Koha Ke Hasan Sadiq.
Main Mar Ke V Nayi Bhulna Gayi Veer Koon Na Dafna Ke.
Tati Rait Te Sum Gaya Akbar Nalay Tur Gaya Ghazi.
Piya Pathraan Heth Hai Baba Mainda Veer Namazi.

2
Aa Zainab Koon Aswar Karan Wich Karbal Baba Aa Ke Haye.
Shala Na Koi Mere Wangoon Aynj Lash Te Aaway.
Na Bhain Kisay Veeranr Di Aynj Uth Te Jaway.
Uthi Veer Di Lash Taun Rondi Main Jiwein Wal Wal Jhirkaan Kha Ke Haye.

3
Nayi Lash Te Rowan Denday Paye Zalim Mainu.
Ayho Jee Karda Aye Mera Dhi Aakhay Tainu.
Raj Rowaan Jandi Wari Main Taiday Gal Wich Bawaan Pa Ke Haye.
Aa Wekh Zara Farwa Da Jo Baba Haal Aye.

4
Nayi Ambari Koon Paye Labhday Hath Mehndiyaan Walay.
Aa Hath Labh Bachray Qasim De Maiday Naal Tu Maqtal Ja Ke Haye.
Reyon Aap Bananda Parday Taun Babal Mere.
Sang Poor De Wanjraan Aye Zainab Wich Shehar De Tere.

5
Teri Rah Wekhaan Sang Bharjaiyaan Hathkariyaan Rasiyaan Pa Ke Haye.
Odha Matam Jag Te Reh Si Ta Hashar De Jari.
Jidi Lash Te Na Raj Roi Ik Dardaan Mari.
 Khursheed Bacha Gaya Deen Jehra Wich Sajday Sees Kata Ke Haye.
