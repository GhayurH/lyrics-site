Mazloom Ke Safeer Ko Mara Hai Be Khata.
Muslim Ko Zalimo Ne Chat Se Diya Gira.

1
Le Ke Paigham-e-Aman Gaye Qatal Kar Diya.
Di Zalimo Ne Unn Ko Kis Jurm Ki Saza.
Ho So Lagay They Pehrey Kofay Mein Zalimo Ke.
Mazloom Be Watan Pe Roti Rahi Qaza.

2
Betiyon Ko Sath Le Kar Muslim Chaley They Ghar Se.
Afsoos Kalma Go Ko Aayi Na Kuch Haya.
Thi Raat Zulm Ki Woh Tanha Janab-e-Muslim.
Pursan e Haal Koi Kofay Mein Na Raha.

3
Ya Rab Na Lal Bichrein Pardais Mein Kissi Ke.
Ro Ro Ke Kar Rahe They Muslim Yehi Dua.
Abbas Ki Behan Ko Paigham Koi Deday.
Hai Maut Har Qadam Pe Betay Hoye Juda.

4
Rahoon Mein Keh Rahi Thi Abbas Se Ruqaiya.
Mere Lal Mujh Ko La Do Karti Hoon Iltija.
Sadaat Ke Lahu Se Dein Ke Charag Roshan.
Shabeer Ne Kiye Hain Sardar Kis Tarha.
