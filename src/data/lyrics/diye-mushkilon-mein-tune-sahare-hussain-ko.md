Diye mushkilon main tu nay saharay Hussain ko

1
Zainab laga lo aaj,  kinaaray Hussain ko

2
1) Inkaar na karo tum bazaar-e-Shaam say
Baaghi bana rahay hain,  tumharay Hussain ko

3
2) Zainab ki bay ridaye nay ansoo rula diye
Nok-e-sina sai koye,  utaaray Hussain ko

4
3) Zainab???? diya??? bazaaron main safar
Tarpa rahay hain laug,  hamaray Hussain ko

5
4) Yeh chehra-e-Hussain-e-Ali-o-Batool ka
Kaisay Shimar tamachay,  maaray Hussain ko
