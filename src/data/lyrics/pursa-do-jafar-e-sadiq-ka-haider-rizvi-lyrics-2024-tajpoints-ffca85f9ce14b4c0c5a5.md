1
Pursa Do Jafar e Sadiq Ka Haider Rizvi
Aye Ahl-e-Azza Do Kazim Ko Sab Pursa Jafar-e-Sadiq Ka.
Rota Hai Jigar Ko Thamay Hoye Shehzada Jafar-e-Sadiq Ka.
Mansor Ne Aisa Zehar Diya Zahra Ka Chand Tarapta Tha.
Kabhi Zard Howa Kabhi Sabz Howa Hai Chehra Jafar-e-Sadiq Ka.
Yeh Soch Ke Maula Sadiq Ki Aankhon Se Barastay They Aanso.
Zindan Mein Barson Qaid Rahe Ga Beta Jafar-e-Sadiq Ka.
Jab Mah-e-Muharram Aata Tha Dil Gham Mein Doob Sa Jata Tha.
Sab Ahl-e-Madina Suntay They Bas Giriya Jafar-e-Sadiq Tha.
Tarveej e Ilm Mein Din Guzrey Shabein Guzrein Shah Ke Matam Mein.
Ban Jata Tha Misal-e-Farsh-e-Azza Ghar Sara Jafar-e-Sadiq Ka.
Zahra Ke Shikasta Pehlu Par Din Raat Woh Noha Parhtay They.
Ab Fatima Zahra Parhti Hain Khud Noha Jafar-e-Sadiq Ka.
Kabhi Akbar Ke Gham Mein Roye Kabhi Sarwar Ki Tanhai Par.
Taboot Uthaya Kazim Ne Jab Maula Jafar-e-Sadiq Ka.
Darwaza e Bint-e-Paygambar Jis Tarha Jalaya Bad-e-Nabi.
Aisay Hi Jalaya Ummat Ne Darwaza Jafar-e-Sadiq Ka.
Kuch Pathar Noha Parhtay Hain Maula Ki Lehad Par Aaj Talak.
Zahra Ki Tarha Hi Ban Na Saka Ik Roza Jafar-e-Sadiq Ka.
Aankhon Se Lahu Barsao Hasan, Jafar Ke Dard Sunao Hasan.
 Haider Yeh Baqi Mein Ja Ke Parhey Ga Noha Jafar-e-Sadiq Ka.
