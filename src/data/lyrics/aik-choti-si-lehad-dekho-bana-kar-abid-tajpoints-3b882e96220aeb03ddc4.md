1
Aik Choti Si Lehad Dekho Bana Kar Abid.
Khon Roya Hai Sakina Ko Sula Kar Abid.
Kaise Baba Ne Uthaya Tha Badan Asghar Ka.
Uth Na Paya Hai Sakina Ko Utha Kar Abid.

2
Phol Se Galon Pe Kehte Hain Tamachon Ke Nishaan.
Kash Baba Ko Dikhati Main Bitha Kar Abid.
Aye Musalmano Tumhein Ab Na Sataye Gi Sada.
Bole Turbat Mein Sakina Ko Sula Kar Abid.

3
Dekh Kar Khoon Bhare Kurtay Ko Badan Se Chipka.
Gir Gaye Khak Pe Yeh Noha Suna Kar Abid.
Aik Kuhram Utha Roye Tarap Kar Mula.
Amma Zainab Ko Galay Apne Laga Kar Abid.

4
Jis Tarha Sunti Thi Baba Se Yeh Bibi Lori.
Aise Talqeen Parhi Shane Hila Kar Abid.
Bhol Na Paye Ga Momin Yeh Judai Yawar.
Gham Sakina Ka Chale Dil Mein Basa Kar Abid.
