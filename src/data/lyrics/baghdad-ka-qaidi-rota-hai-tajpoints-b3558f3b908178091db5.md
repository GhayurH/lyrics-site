1
Kar Kar Ke Yaad Sakina Ko Baghdad Ka Qaidi Rota Hai.
Bazar-e-Sham Hai Aankhon Mein Sajjad Ke Lab Par Noha Hai.
Khud Qaid Jahan Par Kazim Hai Har Din Hai Raat Ke Jaisa.
Zindan Hai Yeh Bhi Tang Magar Har Raat Raza Ka Baba.
Zindan-e-Sham Jo Yaad Aaye Dewar Se Sar Takrata Hai.
Hai Door Nigahon Se Lekin Iss Ka Bhi Jawan Hai Beta.
Jab Yaad Raza Ki Aati Hai Rukh Kar ke Karb-o-Bala Ka.
Zakhmi Akbar Ke Seene Ko Nazroon Se Bose Leta Hai.
Jab Sham Ke Saye Barhte Hain Barhti Hai Saday-e-Giriya.
Ik Dard Sa Dill Mein Uthta Hai Kehte Hain Tarap Kar Mola.
Hai Sath Raza Ke Masooma Zindaan Mein Sakina Tanha Hai.
Mazloom Paraye Dais Mein Hai Dharas Hai Dil Ko Phir Bhi.
Mill Jaye Ga Mujh Ko Ghusal-o-Kafan Basti Hai Musalmano Ki.
Mazool Hussain Ki Ghurbat Par Dil Khoon Ke Aanso Rota Hai.
Hai Chodah Bars Se Qaid Magar Phir Bhi Wo Yehi Hai Kehta.
Abid Ka Raku Our Mera Raku Ik Jaisa Nahi Ho Sakta.
Main Tanha Hoon Wo Maa Behnein Bazaar Se Le Kar Guzra Hai.
Mazlomi Matam Karti Hai Parhti Hai Ghurbat Noha.
Wo Jis Ke Dada Ko Aa Kar Jibreel Jholaein Jhola.
Lasha Oss Ka Mazdoron Ne Baghdad Ke Pul Par Rakha Hai.
Baghdad Ke Qaidi Ne Har Dam Abid Pe Kiya Hai Giriya.
Lekin Akbar Iss Ghurbad Par Sajjad Bhi Rota Ho Ga.
Zanjeeron Mein Tha Jakra Howa Jab Kazim Ne Dam Tora Hai.
