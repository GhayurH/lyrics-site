1
Shabir Ka Sar Naizay Par Hai Zainab Ki Chadar Naizay Par.
Dono Ki Sorat Mein Aaya Haye Deen-e-Payamber Naizay Par.
Tasbeeh Kari Tafseeron Ne Aayat Ne Paihum Sajday Kiye.
Jis Waqt Nazar Aaye Ran Mein Quran Ke Paikar Naizay Par.

2
Yeh Woh Hain Keh Jin Ke Chehron Mein Khaliq Ki Ziyarat Hoti Hai.
Kya Shan Hai Dekho Aate Hain ALLAH Ke Mazhar Naizay Par.
Choti Si Sakina Kanp Uthi Bano Ne Kaleja Tham Liya.
Jis Waqt Uthai Zalim Ne Lash-e-Ali Asghar Naizay Par.

3
Pathra Gayin Zainab Ki Aankhein Jab Dekha Do Shehzadoon Ko.
Abbas Ka Sar Rahwaar Pe Tha Farq-e-Ali Akbar Naizay Par.
Awaz-e-Tilawat Sunte Hi Zainab Ne Kaha Hum Jeet Gaye.
Goya Hoye Dunya Walon Se Jab Sibt-e-Payamber Naizay Par.

4
Ik Shoor Utha Kohram Howa Sajjad Ne Aankhein Band Kar Lein.
Jis Waqt Sar-e-Darbar-e-Sitam Sar Aaye Bahatar Naizay Par.
Yeh Mujh Se Hai Main Iss Se Hoon Iss Qaul Ki Ro Se Aye Yasir.
Shabir Ka Sar Kab Hai Yeh Hai Sar Saqi-e-Kausar Naizay Par.
