1
Woh Ali The Woh Ali Hain Ali Rahein Gaye Sada
Na Koi Tha Na Koi Hai Na Hoga Shair-e-Khuda
Imam Aisay Howay Woh Namaz Khud Hai Fida
Na Howa Hai Na Kare Ga Koi Youn Sajda Adda

2
Do Jahano Ke Sabhi Ilm Ke Malik Ne Kaha
Isse Har Haal Mein Guzro Gaye Hai Ali Darwaza
Ali Ko Sochna Our Dekhna Ibadat Hai
Keh Rahe Hain Yeh Muhammad Keh Keh Raha Hai Khuda
