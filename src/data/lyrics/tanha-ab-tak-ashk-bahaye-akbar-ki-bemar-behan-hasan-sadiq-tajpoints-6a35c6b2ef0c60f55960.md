1
Tanha Ab Tak Ashk Bahaye Akbar Ki Bemar Behan Hasan Sadiq.
Dewaron Se Sar Takraye Akbar Ki Beemar Behan.
Khon Rawaan Hai Aankhon Se Our Lab Pe Noha Bhai Ka.
Kab Se Gham Ke Deep Jalaye Akbar Ki Beemar Behan.

2
Darwazay Par Aas Lagaye Rasta Takti Rehti Hai.
Ashkon Se Khat Likhti Jaye Akbar Ki Beemar Behan.
Aa Ja Akbar, Aa Ja Akbar Toot Rahi Hai Sans Meri.
Zakhm-e-Jigar Kis Ko Dikhlaye Akbar Ki Beemar Behan.

3
Gaye Shabaan Ramzan Bhi Guzrey Eid Ka Din Bhi Guzar Gaya.
Kis Ko Apna Haal Bataye Akbar Ki Beemar Behan.
Seena Chid Gaya So Gaye Akbar Kaun Rehan Yeh Khabar Sunaye.
Bhai Se Na Aas Lagaye Akbar Ki Beemar Behan.
