1
Ghadeer E Khum Main Mir Sajjad Mir
Hub-e-Ali Rehmat Ullah
Bugz-e-Ali Lanat Ullah
Ban Gaya Mimber-e-Palaan Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein
Dab Gaye Sheikh Ke Arman Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein
Ab Koi Hukam Na Aaye Ga Zamane Ke Liye
Ho Gaya Aakhri Ayelan Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein
Garam Jhonkon Pe Hawaon Ke Abuzar Hans Kar
Parhtay Hain Sorah-e-Rehman Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein
Parda e Chashm Se Aanso Nikal Aaye Bahir
Iss Qadar Khush Hoye Salman Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein
Unn Ko Palaan Milein Gay Tu Kahin Jaein Gay
Sheikh Hain Be Sar-o-Saman Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein
Hain Sar-e-Mimber-e-Palaan Ali Our Nabi
Do Nazar Aate Hain Quran Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein
Unn Ko Dar Lagta Hai Pathar Na Baras Jaein Kahin
Youn Nahi Jate Musalman Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein
Kuch Tu Kam Ho Gaya Ho Ga Yeh Sada Di Rab Ne
Abu Talib Tera Ahsan Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein
Ya Nabi Mar Doon Pathar Unhein Qanbar Bolay
Phir Nazar Aa Gaye Shetan Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein
:MIR SAJJAD: Urein Sheikh Ki Neendein Sari
Ho Gaye Khuwab Pareshan Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein
