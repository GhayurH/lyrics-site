1
Hussain Ban Ke Zamane Pe Cha Gayi Zainab Mir Hasan Mir.
Zaban-e-Taig Se Kofa Hila Gayi Zainab.
Ali Ki Yaad Ko Zindah Kiya Zamane Mein.
Jagah Jagah Pe Jo Khutbay Suna Gayi Zainab.

2
Qadam Qadam Pe Jalaye Hussainiyaat Ke Charag.
Diye Yazid Ke Sare Bhuja Gayi Zainab.
Aseer Ho Gayi Dar Dar Phiri Zamane Mein.
Hussainiyaat Ko Magar Jagmaga Gayi Zainab.

3
Qasam Khuda Ki Yeh Ahsan Bint-e-Zahra Hai.
Humein Hussain Ka Matam Sikha Gayi Zainab.
Zaeef Bhai Ka Gham Bantne Ko Maqtal Mein.
Jawaan Bhatijay Ke Lashay Pe Aa Gayi Zainab.
