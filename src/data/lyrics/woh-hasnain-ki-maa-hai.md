Woh Hasnain Ki Maa Hai
Badla Kuch Aisay Bad-e-Nabi Shehar-e-Mustafa
Ummat Yeh Bhool Bethi Hai Kaun Fatima
Zakhmi Chehra Khon Rawaan Hai Woh Hasnain Ki Maa Hai
Jis Ke Rukh Par Surkh Nishaan Hai Woh Hasnain Ki Maa Hai
Shahid Hain Ghar Ki Dewarein Rukhney Par Hain Sansein
Jo Bas Kuch Din Ki Mehmaan Hai Woh Hasnain Ki Maa Hai
Jo Darbar Se Aa Kar Ghar Mein Poch Rahi Hai Sab Se
Meri Zainab Beti Kahan Hai Woh Hasnain Ki Maa Hai
Aik Ki Gardan Rasi Mein Hai Aik Ka Pehlu Zakhmi
Woh Hasnain Ka Baba Jaan Hai Woh Hasnain Ki Maa Hai
Mohsin Mohsin Bool Rahi Hai Jaltay Dar Ke Neechay
Jis Ke Charon Our Dhowaan Hai Woh Hasnain Ki Maa
Fizza Deti Hai Aawazein Band Hain Sab Darwazay
Jis Ke Liye Yasrab Veraan Hai Woh Hasnain Ki Maa
Jis Ki Maiyyat Shab Ko Uthi Thi Our Chalees Thin Qabrein
Jis Ki Qabar Na Jane Kahan Hai Woh Hasnain Ki Maa Hai
AKBAR Pasliyaan Tooti Hain Jis Ki Qabar Mein Bhi Hai Zakhmi
Ab Tak Jis Ka Khon Rawaan Hai Woh Hasnain Ki Maa Hai
