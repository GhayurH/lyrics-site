1
Bhula Sake Gi Na Dunya Teri Wafa Ghazi Hasan Sadiq.
Hain Dono Bazu Tere Deen Ki Baqa Ghazi.
Chahar Simt Thi Yalgar Ahl-e-Batil Ki.
Alam Na Jhukney Diya Our Na Khud Jhuka Ghazi.

2
Nibhai Iss Tarha Shabeer Se Wafa Tu Ne.
Wafa Ko Kehna Para Tu Hai Bawafa Ghazi.
Tareekh Aaj Bhi Jhutla Issay Nahi Sakti.
Tere Hi Khon Ka Sadqa Hai Lailaha Ghazi.

3
Kaha Sakina Ne Baba Na Pani Mangoon Gi.
Bola Lo Khaimon Mein Jaldi Mera Chacha Ghazi.
Pukarey Jaein Gay Mushtaq Hashar Mein Paihum.
Dar-e-Batool Ke Sag Hain Tere Gada Ghazi.
