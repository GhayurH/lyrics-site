1
Allah Teri Dunya Mein Pareshan Hai Zahra.
Ummat Ne Nazar Phair Li Hairan Hai Zahra.
Be Misal Gawahon Ki Gawahi Ko Na Mana.
Bibi Ki Fazeelat Ko Musalmaan Ne Na Jana.
Samjha Hi Nahi Bolta Quran Hai Zahra.
Tehreer Nabi Pak Hawaon Mein Ura Kar.
Darbar Mein Fasiq Ne Kaha Taish Mein Aa Kar.
Iss Masla-e-Islam Se Anjan Hai Zahra.
Bolay Yeh Ali Maula Zara Sharam Tu Khao.
Makhdoma Konain Ko Mat Aisay Rulao.
Farman-e-Muhammad Hai Meri Jan Hai Zahra.
Pehlu Ke Waram Bibi Ko Soney Nahi Detey.
Ik Taraf Musalman Bhi Roney Nahi Detey.
Ab Dunya Mein Kuch Roz Ki Mehman Hai Zahra.
Khaliq Ne Tujhe Dehar Mein Sadiqa Banaya.
Gasib Ne Tere Daway Ko Hai Jhota Bataya.
Arman Hai Arman Hai Arman Hai Zahra.
Zainab Ne Kaha Amma Hoin Kaisay Zaeefa.
Sar Peet Ke Boli Woh Zamane Ki Shareefa.
Dekh Aayi Teri Qaid Ka Zindan Hai Zahra.
Ik Roz Yeh Fizza Ne Kaha Peet Ke Sar Ko.
Hasnain Ke Baba Ko Koi Ja Ke Khabar Do.
Hujray Mein Musalay Pe Hi Be Jan Hai Zahra.
Jo Loag Bhi Zahra Ka Kiya Kartay Hain Matam.
Jo Aankhon Ko Kartay Hain Gham-e-Zahra Mein Purnam.
Ghabraein Woh Kyun Unn Ki Nigehban Hai Zahra.
 Tauqeer Tere Gham Ko Bhala Kaisay Bhulaye.
Nazranay Mein Ashkon Ke Diye Kyun Na Jalaye.
Haye Teri Lehad Aaj Bhi Veeran Hai Zahra.
