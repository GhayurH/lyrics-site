Jaag Uthein Gaye Dard Purane Zakhmo Ki Angnai Mein.
Dil Ki Chot Ubhar Aye Gi Mat Niklo Purwai Mein.

1
Koyal Koki Moaj-e-Saba Ne Paon Mein Ghungro Band Liye.
Pyar Ka Nagma Chair Raha Hai Aaj Koi Shehnai Mein.
Jo Pehle Badnam Howay The Unn Ko Dunya Bhol Gayi.
Hum Ne Kaise Rang Bhare Hain Ishq Teri Ruswai Mein.

2
Kaun Tumhare Dukh Bante Ga Kaun Yeh Naaz Uthaye Ga.
Hum Jis Waqt Na Hoon Gaye Jana Tarpo Gaye Tanhai Mein.
Aaj Fanna Ke Piche Piche Khak Urate Phirte Hain.
Logo Ne Kya Dekh Liya Hai Aaj Tere Sodai Mein.
