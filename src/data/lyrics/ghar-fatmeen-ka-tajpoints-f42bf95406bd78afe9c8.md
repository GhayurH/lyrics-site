1
Roshan Hai Jis Ke Noor Se Ghar Fatmeen Ka.
Abbas Woh Charag Hai Umm Ul-Banin Ka.
Zohrah Jahan Pe Utra Tha Umm Ul-Banin Ne.
Aa Kar Wahin Utara Sitara Jaben Ka.

2
Ghazi Se Maa Yeh Kehti Thi Ghar Syeda Ka Hai.
Bas Naqsh-e-Pa Nazar Mein Rahe Har Maken Ka.
Aanso Azza Ke Paunch Liye Aasteen Se.
Ik Sanp Marna Tha Mujhe Aasteen Ka.

3
Yeh Karbala Hai Teen So Terah Ki Saltanat.
Terah Ka Koi Kam Yahan Hai Na Teen Ka.
Abbas Ka Yeh Kabay Ki Chat Par Biyan Tha.
Abad-e-Mobeen Hoon Main Imam-e-Mobeen Ka.

4
ALLAH Our Ali Ke Addo Jab Ginay Gaye.
Dono Taraf Hisab Raha Teen Teen Ka.
 Akbar Wila Se Hota Hai Naslon Ka Faisla.
Hai Khon Mushrikeen Ka Ya Momineen Ka.
