1
Fizza Tur Payi Mazlooma Nal Apna Farz Nibhawan Layi.
Aal-e-Nabi Di Izzat Azmat Lokaan Nu Samjhawan Layi.
Kon Hussain Aye Putar Kida Aye Kinu Kinu Sadaan Main.
Jannat De Sardar De Wairi Wekhay Karbal Lakhaan Main.
Razi Ho Gaye Apne Hathi Jannat Aap Ganwawan Layi.
Naiziyaan De Nal Chadraan Layiyaan Siraan Taun Zahra Jaiyaan De.
Ki Eman Ne Socho Logo Arab Diyaan Sehraiyaan De.
Wekh Ke Aal Ali Di Aa Gaye Badar De Badlay Lawan Layi.
Dukh Takleefaan Syedaan De Nal Tu V Jardi Sehdi Rayi.
Bibiyaan Nu Na Pathar Maro Ik Ik Nu Tu Kehndi Rayi.
Bhul Gaye Nabi Nal Muslim Rishta Maal Yazeedi Khawaan Layi.
Kujh Te San Quran De Hafiz Kujh Mufti Kujh Alam Log.
Gharaan Choon Aa Gaye Aal-e-Nabi Te Zulm Karan Layi Zalim Log.
Hathaan De Wich Pathar Phar Ke Qaraz-e-Risalat Lawan Layi.
Har Ik Di Qismat Che Azai Aal-e-Abba Da Gham Nayi Honda.
Aye Oh Gham Aye Wadhda Janda Kisay V Haal Che Kam Nayi Honda.
Seene Khas Khalejay Honday Dard Hussaini Chawan Layi.
