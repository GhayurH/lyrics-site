Tu Ali Ki Karta Hai Humsari Yeh Tere Zameer Ki Bhool Hai.
Tujh Mein Ali Ki Wila Nahi Teri Bandagi Bhi Fazool Hai.

1
Yeh Jo Kar Raha Hai Tu Sakhtiyaan Mere Pyar Ka Hai Yeh Imtehaan.
Tujhe Takht Par Bhi Sakoon Nahi Mujhe Daar Par Bhi Mili Amaan.
Main Addo Ko Kyun Na Addo Kahon Meri Zindagi Ka Usool Hai.
Main Maron Tu Lab Pe Ho Ya Ali Mujhe Aisa Marna Qabool Hai.

2
Woh Ali Jo Hawala-e-Innama Woh Ali Jo Hasil-e-Kul Kaha.
Ossay Kiya Kahon Tu Khud Bata Chalo Tujh Pe Chora Yeh Faisla.
Jisay Haq Ne Bakhsha Hai Martaba Jo Azal Se Shohar-e-Batool Hai.
Raha Jagta Tu Imam Tha Kabhi So Gaya Tu Rasool Hai.
