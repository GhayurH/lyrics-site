Maa Dukhiyari Kissai Lori Dai Gi

1
Jungle Sai Maqtal Sai Hai Shaam Ki Ab Tayari
1) Tanhai ka Aalam hai Shabbir Nahi
Goad hai khali ab mera baisheer nahi
Ro ro ker yeh madar kerti hai Aaho Zaari

2
2) Qaidi ban ker dukhiya maa Hai shaam chali
Kiya Safar main Asgher ki jab Aayai Gi
Rastai main Ujri maa Marjayai na Gham ki Maari

3
3) Phool sa nazuk chera aksar Yaad aaya
Khak pai bethi maa ko Asgher yaad aya
Khaimain main Us Maa kai ansoo hain aag pai jari

4
4) Karbobala main ya Rab yeh kiya zulm huwa
Us galay pai Asghar kai jab teer laga
Phir kahan maan Zainab nai hai maa per sakta taari

5
5) Jaisai ujri main hun koi na ujray
Ya rab aisa waqat koi maa Na dekhai
Asgher kai Lashai per Thi Maa ki Aaho zari
