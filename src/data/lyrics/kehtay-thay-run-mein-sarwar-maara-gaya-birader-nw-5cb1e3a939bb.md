Kehtay Thay Run Mein Sarwar Maara Gaya Birader

Tooti Qamar Hai Meri Laasheyn Utha Utha Ker

Amma Mei Thak Gaya Hoon Amma Mei Thak Gaya Hoon

Seh Roz Ka Hoon Pyaasa Mei Hogaya Akaila
Asghar Ko Dafn Ker Kay Khud Ban Gaya Hoon Laasha
Daikho Mera Muqadar Amma Mei Thak Gaya Hoon

Ye Fikr Kha Rahi Hai Ub Shaam Aarahi Hai
Mansoob-e-Fouj-e-Ada Ub Ye Bana Rahi Hai
Cheenay Haram Ki Chader Amma Mei Thak Gaya Hoon

Aey Amma Ya Na Poocho Kya Hogaya Hai Mujhko
Tum Khud Zakhm-e-Akbar Maidaan Mei Aakay Daikho
Kaboo Nahien Hai Dil Per Amma Mei Thak Gaya Hoon

Maqtal Say Chun Kay Laaya Pamaal Aik Laasha
Hathon Pay Jiski Mehndi Daithee Thi Mujhko Pursa
Aansoo Baha Baha Ker Amma Mei Thak Gaya Hoon
