Makan-e-Hasat Koi La-Makaan Sanwarta Hai.
Yeh Kahk Woh Hai Jise Aasmaan Sanwarta Hai.

1
Hamare Sath Ajab Haim Moamle Oss Ke.
Yahan Bigar Ke Rakhta Wahan Sanwarta Hai.
Koi Bhi Kaam Saleeqe Se Kab Kiya Hum Ne.
Hamare Kaam Koi Na-Gihaan Sanwarta Hai.

2
Nishate-e-Shola Hai Ik Aani Jani Kaifiyat.
Cherag-e-Ishq Ko Oss Ka Dhuwaan Sanwarta Hai.
Yeh Husan-e-Reekhta Hargiz Nahi Hamara Kamal.
Yeh Rang Silsila-e-Raftgaan Sanwarta Hai.

3
Jiger Ke Khoon Se Hai Mashatagi-e-Harf-o-Biaan.
Keh Zulf Shair Lahu Ka Ziyaan Sanwarta Hai.
Hamare Seene Ko Tamga Hai Dag-e-Wehshat-e-Ishq.
Yeh Daag Woh Hai Jo Naam-o-Nishaan Sanwarta Hai.

4
Bohat Ujaar Thi Maqtal Ki Sar Zameen Magar.
Isse Dobara Koi Nojawaan Sanwarta Hai.
Osse Khabar Hai Keh Mushkil Pasand Hai Yeh Faqeer.
WOh Janta Hai Isse Imtehaan Sanwarta Hai.
