1
Shabbir Ka Charcha Baqi Hai Mir Hasan Mir
Chodah Sadiyaan Beet Gayin Shabeer Ka Charcha Baqi Hai
Quraan Ab Tak Bool Raha Hai Khana-e-Kaba Baqi Hai
Karb-o-Bala Ka Pa Ke Sahara Gunbad-e-Khizra Baqi Hai

2
Sare Jhanday Toot Chukay Hain Sabz Pharera Baqi Hai
Gaunj Raha Hai Nam-e-Muhammad Zikar Khuda Ka Baqi Hai
Inn Chodah Sadiyon Ke Andar Chodah So Tofan Uthay
Umvi Harkaray Doray Hain Abbasi Sultan Uthay

3
Laat-o-Hobul Seeno Mein Chupaye Hathoon Par Quran Uthay
Jin Ki Bunyadon Mein Lahu Tha Qasar Woh Alishan Uthay
Sab Dewarein Baith Gayin Mazloom Ka Roza Baqi Hai
Taig-o-Tabar Ne Rasta Roka Fojon Ki Yalgar Hoi

4
Lal-o-Gohar Ne Daam Lagaye Sikkon Ki Jhankar Hoi
Lambi Qabaon Walay Uthay Fatwon Ki Bharmar Hoi
Deeda-e-Ibrat Tu Hi Bata Yeh Jeet Hoi Ya Haar Hoi
Sajday Mein Sar Kaat Liya Tha Lekin Sajdah Baqi Hai

5
Kya Jane Kin Kin Qaumon Ne Deray Dalay Darya Par
Aaye Aa Kar Laut Gaye Sab Goray Kalay Darya Par
Ab Bhi Milein Gay Totay Hoye Kuch Khanjar Bhalay Darya Par
Qabza Karne Aaye They Sab Taqat Walay Darya Par

6
Sab Ka Qabza Khatam Howa Abbas Ka Qabza Baqi Hai
Pyason Ke Inkar Ka Khanjar Gardan Par Kuch Aisa Chala
Lakhon Fitney Uthay Lekin Phir Sar-e-Baiyat Uth Na Saka
Jab Bhi Kissi Ke Dil Mein Tamana Baiyat Ki Uthti Hai Zarra

7
Kehta Hai Abbas Ka Parcham Fikar-e-Yazeedi Hosh Mein Aa
Jis Ne Tujhey Mara Tha Tamacha Ab Bhi Woh Panja Baqi Hai
Deen-e-Nabi Ke Khoon Ki Hararat Seena-e-Aalam Ki Dharkan
Arzu-e-Zahra-o-Payamber Jan-e-Ali-o-Roh-e-Hasan

8
Betha Howa Hai Parday Mein Talwar Liye Ik Qala Shikan
Taqat Par Itrane Kaisa Deen-e-Elahi Ke Dushman
Badar-o-Ohad Se Bhagney Walo Aakhri Hamla Baqi Hai
Lakh Mitao Mit Na Sakay Ga Aal-e-Nabi Ka Naam-o-Nishaan

9
La Waris Samjho Na Issay Yeh Khaliq-e-Akbar Ka Hai Makaan
Bait Se Ahl-e-Bait Ka Rishta Kya Torein Gay Ahl-e-Jahan
Kya Jane Kab Baam-e-Falak Se Gaunj Uthay Aawaz-e-Azaan
Aye Kabay Ke Thekedaro Kabay Wala Baqi Hai
