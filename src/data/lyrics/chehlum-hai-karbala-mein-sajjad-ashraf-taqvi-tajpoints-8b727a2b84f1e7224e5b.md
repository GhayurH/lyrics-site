Chehlum Hai Karbala Mein Haram Laut Aaye Hain.
Aankhon Se Khoon Rawaan Hai Saron Ko Jhukaye Hain.

1
Kuhram Aik Ganjay Shaheedan Mein Hai Bapa.
Abid Ne Bibiyon Ko Maqatil Sunaye Hain.
Chota Sa Kyun Hai Marqad-e-Abbas-e-Bawafa.
Maula Pe Iss Sawal Ne Kya Hashar Dhaye Hain.

2
Baba Ki Ladli Ka Bhi Noha Labon Pe Hai.
Kaisay Kahin Behan Ko Kahan Chor Aaye Hain.
Bhai Se Kar Rahi Hai Biyaan Ghamzada Behan.
Jo Qaid-o-Be Ridai Ke Sadmay Uthaye Hain.

3
Sab Apne Warison Ke Saron Par Hain Noha Gar.
Shuhda Ke Sar Jo Sath Mein Sajjad Laye Hain.
Noha Atta Howa Hai Jo Chehlum Ke Roz Ka.
 Sajjad Our Kaleem Ne Aanso Bahaye Hain.
