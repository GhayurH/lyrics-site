Tu Nad e Ali Parh Yaar Mola Teri Khair Kare
Phir Narey Laga Do Chaar Maula Teri Khair Karey
Bhalay Se Anwar-e-Ali Se Khali Tera Seena Hai
Hub-e-Ali Ke Bin Yeh Tera Jeena Bhi Kya Jeena Hai
Kar Nam-e-Ali Se Pyar Maula Teri Khair Karey
Aankhon Ko Teri Din Raat Agar Rehmat Ke Nazarey Karne Hain
Dunya Mein Agar Reh Kar Tujh Ko Janat Ke Nazarey Karne Hain
Kar Ja Ke Najaf Dedar Maula Teri Khair Karey
Jo Hai Aaqa Tera Wohi Iss Ka Bhi Tu Maula Hai
Tu Hai Agar Molai Tu Phir Molai Se Kya Jhagra Hai
Nafrat Ki Gira Dewar Maula Teri Khair Karey
Shabeer Pe Marne Walon Se Jeene Ki Adaein Leta Ja
Aanso Ko Bana Ke Apne Gohar Zahra Se Duaein Leta Ja
Ban Sheh Ka Matamdar Maula Teri Khair Karey
Bhool Ke Bhi Na Aaein Gay Phir Ghar Pe Tere Mushkil Ke Qadam
Apne Ghar Pe Tu Bhi Saja Kar Mere Ghazi Ka Parcham
Qadmo Pe Jhuka Sansar Maula Teri Khair Karey
Hone De Mukhalif Dunya Yeh Amanat Zahra Ki
Meesum Ne TAKALLUM Mujh Se Kaha Kyun Shan Mein Mere Maula Ki
Tu Likhta Ja Ashar Maula Teri Khair Karey
