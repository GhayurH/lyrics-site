Qadi Gihn Aawo, “Qadi Gihn Aawo”

-Tusa evayain istaqbal karo, rung posha kanday lal karo

wad khub bazaar sajakay

Devo tofay shehar de was deyain koon

milo gal nal la kay lookan koon

har ghar te mashal jalao, din eid de wang manao

bhar key lay kaparay pakay “ ”

Jadun dakhil the warn shaam de vich

pehlay bazaar awam de vich

ja ri mai noshi kar devo, har shakhs de hat pat har devo

har ghar toon pat har wasa kay “ ”

Bul wow najis sharabiyain koon

na chadaran devo bagyain koon

na koyi taras wikhao, ja ja kay ut rukwow

bazaar da rush wadwa kay “ ”

Bazaar jadun kar par awarn

Kuj der tayin darbar awarn

Kuj shama evayain ruk ruk kay, rasiyan di thar ich luk luk kay

Bazaar azan suna kay “ ”

Kuj bazi khaard toon shat ranj di

wal pay shi ho si anj anj di

har ik da na cha cha ghirn soon, wat marn walay da raj puch soon

jal di darbar saja kay “ ”

Tahir aylanay surn surn kay

rak kamar te hat shah chuk chuk kay

sir sut kay rat ro pan da ay, vat najaf de pasay wenda ay

jadun wal wal zor da akhay “ ”
