Zahra Tayray Ghulshan May, Ab Kehsay Baharaay
Ujray Na Koyi Ehsay, Zainab Key Pukaraay

۱
Kub Tak Mujay Jeena Hay, Waday Pay Tayray Akbar
In Ahkon May Hasrat Hay, Dayko Tho Zara Aakar
Bhaya Main Tujay Deykhoo, Is Dil Ko Karar Aay

۲
Ma Tho Nahi Bulay Ghi, Yay Kushk Zaban Tayri
Kya Teer Key Kabil Thi, Yay Nani Say Jan Tayri
Ab Kis Ko Dilasay Doon, Ab Kis Pay Pyar Aay

۳
Matham Tho Karan Kyun Kar, Roay Tho Kisay Ahkir
Kohi Ehlay Saqifa Ki, Behti Bhi Ho Bin Chadar
Zainab Key Tharay Kariyan Pehnay Wo Bazaar Aahay

۴
Akbar Hain Na Qasim Hain, Ab Kis Ka Sahara Hay
Ghazi Bhi Nahi Baqi, Ab Kaun Humara Hay
Nannay Ali Asghar Ka, Marqad May Utara Hay
