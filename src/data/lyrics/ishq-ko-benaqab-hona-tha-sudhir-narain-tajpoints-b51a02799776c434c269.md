Ishq Ko Benaqab Hona Tha Sudhir Narain.
Aap Apna Jawab Hona Tha.

1
Mast-e-Jaam-e-Sharab Hona Tha.
Be Khud Iztarab Hona Tha.
Teri Aankhon Ka Kuch Qasoor Nahi.
Haan Mujh Hi Ko Kharab Hona Tha.

2
Aao Mill Jao Muskura Ke Galay.
Ho Chuka Jo Aytab Hona Tha.
Kocha-e-Ishq Mein Nikal Aya.
Jiss Ko Khana Kharab Hona Tha.

3
Mast-e-Jaam-e-Sharab Khaak Howay.
Garq Jaam-e-Sharab Hona Tha.
Dill Keh Jiss Par Hain Naqsh-e-Ranga Rang.
Oss Ko Sadah Kitab Hona Tha.

4
Hum Ne NaKamiyon Ko Dhond Liya.
Aakhrish Kamyab Hona Tha.
Haye Woh Lamha-e-Sakoon Keh Jissey.
Mehshar-e-Iztarab Hona Tha.

5
Nigh-e-Yaar Khud Tarap Uthti.
Shart-e-Awal Kharab Hona Tha.
Kyun Na Hota Sitam Bhi Be Payaan.
Karm Be Hisaab Hona Tha.

6
Kyun Nazar Hairaton Mein Doob Gayi.
Moaj-e-Sad Iztarab Hona Tha.
Ho Chuka Roaz-e-Awaaleen Hi Jigar.
Jiss Ko Jitna Kharab Hona Tha.
