‎Zainab Say Pehlay Zehra Darbar Mei Gaee

Umath Nay Kahan Laa Kay Khara Ker Diya Mujhai Beti Rasool Ki, Yeh Sochthi Rahi

Hasnain Shareefain Bhi Rothay Rahay Kharay

Darbar Mei Maa Chunthi Rahi, Sanad Kay Tukray Sub Log Has Deeyai

Zahra Ki Ghasibon Nay Thazeem Khoob Kee

Haey Baad-e-Nabi Zahra Ko Ronay Nahien Deeya

Jaltay Houay Darwazay Say Pehlu Kiya Zakhmi Umath Nay Kya Diya

Kaisee Batool Pay Haey Aaee Hai Bay Basee

Yaaraanay Mohammad Nay Hay Zahra Ko Dukh Diya

Luta Hai Musalmanon Nay Haq Aal-e-Abaa Ka Yaad Aaee Kerbala

Apnay Pidar Ko Logo Baitee Na Ro Sakee

Jibreel Bhi Jis Bibi Kay Dar Ka Khayath Hay

Mazlooma-o-Mehrooma Kay Shohar Kay Haath Mein Sub Qaeynaath Hai

Usnay Sitham Bhi Jhaylay Per Bud Dua Na Kee

Taraaj Kitnay Rozay Keeyai Zaalimeen Nay

Bint-e-Ali Kay Rozay Ko Dhaanay Kay Waastay Zaalim Jama Huay

Aal-e-Nabi Pay Hota Hai Zulm Aaj Bhi

Aati Hain Sadaaeyn Ye Baqeeya Ki Simt Say

Bas Ye Sawal Mera Musalmaan Tujh Say Hai

Batla Day Tu Mujhai

Zahra Ki Qabr Pur Kyun Saaya Nahien Koi

Haey Maiyat-e-Zahra Ko Tha Sab Nay Vida Kiya

Maa Aaounga Mei Milnay Faqat Daygi Gar Sada Shabeer Ro Diya

Band-e-Kafan Jo Toota Qainaat Ro Pari
