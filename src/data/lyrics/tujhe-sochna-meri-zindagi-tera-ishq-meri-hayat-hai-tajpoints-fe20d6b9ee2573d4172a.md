Tujhe Sochna Meri Zindagi Tera Ishq Meri Hayat Hai.
Mere Dil Mein Tera Qiyam Hai Yeh Mere Naseeb Ki Baat Hai.

1
Jise Tum Ne Mara Tha Be-Khata Woh Hussain Ab Bhi Safar Mein Hai.
Jise Tum Ne Samjha Tha Ashk-e-Gham Woh Tu Aansoaon Ki Farat Hai.
Mujhe Mushkilon Se Nikal Le Aye Hussain Mujh Ko Sanbhal Le.
Main Rasool Hoon Tu Hussain Hai Meri Zindagi Teri Zaat Hai.

2
Tere Dar Se Mujh Ko Baqa Mili Meri Fikar-o-Fan Ko Jila Mili.
Teri Aal Ka Main Hoon Madah Khuwan Yeh Teri Nazar Ki Zakaat Hai.
Yeh Pukarta Hai Khuda Ka Deen Mera Karbala Mein Koi Nahi.
Aye Nabi Ke Lal Bacha Mujhe Meri Aabro Tere Hath Hai.

3
Yeh JO Nohay Giriye Ka Shor Hai Ba-Khuda Yehi Hai Azan-e-Gham.
Yeh Jo Farsh-e-Gham Pe Ada Hoi Yehi Matami Ki Salaat Hai.
Aye Muhib Hussain Ke Matami Jo Rawaan Dawaan Hain Jaloos Mein.
Unhein Ba-Khuda Yeh Shahoor Hai Yeh Jaloos Rah-e-Nijaat Hai.
