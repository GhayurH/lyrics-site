1
Aik Ja Harf-e-Wafa Likha Tha So Bhi Mit Giya.
Zahran Kagaz Tere Khat Ka Galat Bardaar Hai.
Jee Jalay Zooq-e-Fana Ki Na Tamami Par Na Kyun.
Hum Nahi Jalte Nafas Har Chand Aatashbar Hai.

2
Aag Se Pani Mein Bhujte Waqt Uthti Hai Sada.
Har Koi Darmandgi Mein Nalay Se Na Chaar Hai.
Hai Wohi Bad Mastiye Har Zarah Ka Khud Uzar Khowah.
Jiss Ke Jalway Say Zameen Ta Aasmaan Sar Shaar Hai.

3
Mujh Se Mat Keh Tu Humein Kehta Tha Apni Zindagi.
Zindagi Se Bhi Mera Jee Inn Dino Be-Zaar Hai.
Aankh Ki Tasveer Sar Namey pe Kheinchi Hai Keh Ta.
Tujh Pe Khul Jaway Keh Iss Ko Hasrat-e-Deedar Hai.
