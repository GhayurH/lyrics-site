Waqt Sajjad Ne Kaisay Voh Guzara Hoga
Naam Zainab Ka Jo Ghairon Ne Pukara Hoga

Lo Hussain Ibne Ali Pyasay Zibha Honay Lagay
Kitna Sharminda Voh Dariya Ka Kinara Hoga

Voh Gira Zeen Se Zainab Ki Rida Ka Zamin
Koan Ab Fatima Jaee Ka Sahara Hoga

Sar-e-Maidan Tu Janay To Laga Hai Asghar
Kis Tarha Teyrai Bina Maan Ka Guzara Hoga

Nok-e-Neza Pe Bahanay Lagay Aansoon Maula
Phir Sakina Ko Kisi Shakhs Ne Maara Hoga

Roz-e-Mehshar Ka Hamein Dar Nahi Koi Shaukat
Haath Shabbir Ke Qadmon Pe Hamara Hoga
