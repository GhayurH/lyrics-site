Kull o Yaumin Ashura Nadeem Sarwar.
Kullo Youmin Ashora Kullo Arzin Karbla.

1
Ya Abalahrar Ya Abalahrar Ya Hussain.
Teri Qurbani Ka Sadqa Aye Shaheed-e-Karbala.
Hai Zameen-o-Aasmaan Ko Yeh Sharf Tab Se Mila.
Bar Sar-e-Nook-e-Sina Dekha Tu Qudrat Ne Kaha.

2
Misal-e-Ashora Hai Har Din Har Zameen Hai Karbala.
Jo Din Hai Rooz-e-Ashora Our Jo Zameen Hai Karbala.
Dekhiye Tafseer Iss Din Ki Zara Parh Ke Kisa.
Hazrat-e-Jibreel Se Jis Dam Muhammad Ne Kaha.
Yolemoni Ma Yolemohum Wa Yahzononi Ma Yahzonohum.
Jis Ne Inn Ko Dukh Diya Hai Mujh Ko Ranjeda Kiya.
Sabhi Nabi Ke Kalma Go They Sabhi Namazein Parhtay They.
Karb-o-Bala Mein Sibt-e-Nabi Ke Khon Ke Lekin Pyasay They.
Hifz Kiya Quran Magar Yeh Qaul-e-Nabi Na Yaad Raha.
Harbun Leman Harbahum Wasilmun Leman Salamahum.
Inn Se Rafaqat Mujh Se Rafaqat Inn Ka Addo Dushman Hai Mera.
Sab Ko Khabar Hai Sab Ko Pata Hai Khuld-e-Barin Ke Malik Hain.
Saqi-e-Kausar Qasim-e-Janat Arsh-o-Zameen Ke Malik Hain.
Shimar Sitamgar Soch Zara Tu Kaat Raha Hai Kis Ka Gala.
Adoun Leman Aadahum Wa Muhibun Leman Aa Habahum.
Inn Ka Addo Hai Mera Dushman Dost Hai Inn Ka Dost Mera.
Lahmohum Lahmi Wadamohum Dami Nafsoka Nafsi Jin Ko Kaha.
Chaar Nafas Hasnain-o-Zahra Maula Ali They Zair-e-Kisa.
Sad Afsoos Bhulaya Tum Ne Qaul Rasool-e-Akram Ka.
Roh-e-Payamber Roh-e-Zahra Kyun Na Karein Phir Wawela.
Jis Ke Liye Tatheer Ki Chadar Aayat Ban Kar Aayi Thi.
Dasht-e-Bala Mein Nangay Sar Woh Zainab Zahra Jayi Thi.
Baloon Se Pardah Karti Thi Karti Thi Yeh Aah-o-Boka.
Qatal Kiya Mere Bhai Ko Cheen Li Mere Sar Se Rida.
Bad-e-Nabi Ke Zulm Woh Dhaye Ahl-e-Sitam Ne Zahra Par.
Qatal Kiya Jalti Reti Par Bint-e-Nabi Ka Lakht-e-Jigar.
Aik Taraf Tha Pyas Ka Sehra Aik Taraf Behta Darya.
Lasha-e-Sarwar Ahl-e-Jafa Ne Ghoron Se Pamal Kiya.
Subha Daham Shabeer Ne Jab Akbar Se Kaha Azan Kaho.
Waqt Shahadat Aa Pohancha Hai Shukar-e-Khuda Har Aan Karo.
Hum Natiq-e-Quraan Hain Beta Quran Suno Quran Parho.
Teri Azaan Aagaz Hai Beta Our Anjam Mera Sajdah.
Sham-e-Ghareebaan Jaltay Khaimay Door Talak Ho Ka Aalam.
Aag Ke Sholon Ke Andar Barpa Tha Bharay Ghar Ka Matam.
 Rehan Labon Pe Zainab Ke Bas Aik Sada Thi Aik Dua.
Kuch Bhi Nahi Darkar Humein Hai Hum Ko Miley Bas Teri Raza.
