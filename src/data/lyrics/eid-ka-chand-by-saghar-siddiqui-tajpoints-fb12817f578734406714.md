1
Eid Ka Chand Hai Khushiyon Ka Sawali Aye Dost.
Khushiyaan Bheek Mein Mangay Se Kahan Milti Hain.
Dast-e-Sail Mein Agar Kasa-e-Gham Cheekh Uthay.
Tab Kahin Ja Ke Sitaron Se Garaan Milti Hain.

2
Eid Ke Chand Mujhe Mehram-e-Ishrat Na Bana.
Meri Sorat Ko Tamasha-e-Alam Rehne De.
Mujh Pe Hairaan Hain Ahl-e-Karam Hone Do.
Dehar Mein Mujh Ko Shanasa-e-Alam Rehne Do.

3
Yeh Musarrat Ki Fizaein Tu Chali Jati Hain.
Kal Wohi Ranj Ke Aalaam Ke Dharay Hoon Gay.
Chand Lamhon Ke Liye Aaj Galay Se Lag Ja.
Itnay Din Tu Ne Bhi Zulmat Mein Guzarey Hoon Gay.
