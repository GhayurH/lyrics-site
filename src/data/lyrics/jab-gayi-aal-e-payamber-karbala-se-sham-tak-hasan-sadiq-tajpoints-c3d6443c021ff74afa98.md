1
Jab Gayi Aal-e-Payamber Karbala Se Sham Tak Hasan Sadiq.
Sath Tha Zalim Ka Lashkar Karbala Se Sham Tak.
Ik Rasan Mein Bazu-e-Zainab Sakina Ka Gala.
Larkharaye Har Qadam Par Karbala Se Sham Tak.

2
Ummat-e-Aasi Se Ro Kar Shah Ke Ahl-e-Haram.
Mangtay Jate They Chadar Karbala Se Sham Tak.
Be Rida Ahl-e-Haram Ko Har Qadam Aati Rahi.
Yaad-e-Abbas-e-Dilawar Karbala Se Sham Tak.

3
Chehra Baloon Se Chupaye Ashk Aankhon Se Rawaan.
Ja Rahi Hai Bint-e-Haider Karbala Se Sham Tak.
ALLAH ALLAH Yeh Wafa Yeh Jaan Nisari Dekhiye.
Sath They Sheh Ke Bahatar Karbala Se Sham Tak.

4
Ja Rahi Thi Umm-e-Laila Zair-e-Lab Kehti Hoi.
Haye Akbar Haye Akbar Karbala Se Sham Tak.
Chomta Palkon Se Naqsh-e-Paye Bint-e-Murtaza.
Khuwab Mein Jata Jo Mehshar Karbala Se Sham Tak.
