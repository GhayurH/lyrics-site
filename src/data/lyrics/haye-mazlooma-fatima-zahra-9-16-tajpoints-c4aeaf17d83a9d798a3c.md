Haye Mazlooma Fatima Zahra 9-16

1
Zakhmi Pehlu Ko Sanbhale Zabt-e-Gham Kis Tarha Ho.
Jane Kyun Hasrat Se Dekha Karti Hain Shabir Ko.
Sochti Hain Kya Fatima Zahra.
Seena-e-Shabar Ko Choma Our Gala Shabir Ka.
Choom Ke Zainab Ke Bazu Ro Parein Be-Sakhta.
Kya Khiyal Aaya Fatima Zahra.

2
Kehti Hain Fizza Se Bibi Aye Kaneez-e-Ba-Wafa.
Tere Zime Zainab-o-Kulsoom Ko Main Ne Kiya.
Roti Hain Fizza Fatima Zahra.
Khana-e-Haider Mein Barpa Ik Qayamat Ho Gayi.
Waqt Woh Aaya Keh Bibi Ki Shahdat Ho Gayi.
Arsh Tharaya Fatima Zahra.

3
Zainab-o-Kulsoom-o-Fizza Shabar-o-Haider Yahan.
Door Kone Mein Khare Shabir Rote Hain Wahan.
Majrah Hai Kya Fatima Zahra.
Bole Haider Waqt-e-Rukhsat Maan Ko Kya Rulwaein Gaye.
Bole Sheh Amma Bulaein Gi Humein Tu Aaein Gaye.
Ro Parein Jo Suna Fatima Zahra.

4
Sun Ke Aawaz-e-Sheh-e-Dein Khul Gaye Band-e-Kafan.
Bolein Bibi Hath Utha Ke Aa Mere Gull Pairehan.
De Rahi Hai Sada Fatima Zahra.
Aik Na Ik Din Bane Ga Marqad-e-Weeran Bhi.
Dekhne Aaein Gaye Bibi Mazhar-o- Farhan Bhi.
Aap Ke Roza Fatima Zahra.
