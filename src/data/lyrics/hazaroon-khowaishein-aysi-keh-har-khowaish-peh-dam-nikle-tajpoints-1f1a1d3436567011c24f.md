1
Hazaroon Khowaishein Aysi Keh Har Khowaish peh Dam Nikle.
Bohat Nikle Mere Arman Lekin Phir Bhi Kam Nikle.
Dare Kyun Mera Qatil Kiya Rahe Ga Oss Ki Gardan Par.
Wo Khoon Jo Chashm Tar Se Umar Bhar Youn Dam Beh Dam Nikle.

2
Nikalna Khuld Se Adam Ka Sunte Aye Hain Lekin.
Bohat Be Abro Ho Kar Tere Koche Se Hum Nikle.
Bharm Khul Jaye Zalim Tere Qamat Ki Darazi Ka.
Agar Iss Turah Pur Paich-o-Kham Ka Paich-o-Kham Nikle.

3
Magar Likhwaye Koi Oss Ko Khat Tu Hum Se Likhwaye.
Hoi Subha Our Ghar Se Kaan Par Rakh Kar Qalam Nikle.
Hoi Iss Door Mein Mansoob Mujh se Badah Aashami.
Phir Aya Woh Zamana Jo Jahan Mein Jaam-e-Jam Nikle.

4
Hoi Jinn Se Towaqa Khustagi Ki Daad Pane Ki.
Woh Hum Se Bhi Zyda Khast-e-Taig-e-Sitam Nikle.
Mohabbat Mein Nahi Hai Farq Jeene Our Marne Ka.
Ossi Ko Dekh Kar Jeete Hain Jiss Qafir Peh Dam Nikle.

5
Khuda Ke Waste Pardah Na Kabay Se Utha Zalim.
Kahin Aysa Na Ho Yaan Bhi Wohi Kafir Sanam Nikle.
Kahan Maykhane Ka Darwaza Ghalib Our Kahan Waiz.
Par Itna Jante Hain Kal Wo Jata Tha Keh Hum Nikle.
