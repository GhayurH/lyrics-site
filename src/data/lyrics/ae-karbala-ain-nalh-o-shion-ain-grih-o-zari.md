1. Jar janib haey sitamgar (be khata aal-e-PAeyambar)
Koi be maqna o chadar (koi be hosh zamee par)

Aey Karbala Karbala Aey Karbala

3
2. Jal uthao aag se sehra (kahi khAeyma kahi jhoola)
Har taraf Aeyk hAey manzar (kahi matam kahi noha)
As aatishe shoza aznaar-e-Farawa paamaal-e-sitam aale aba shud
Aey Karbala Karbala Aey Karbala

4
3. Aeya yeh kAeysa Muharram (bibiya roti hAey pAeygham)
Khoon barsati hAey aankhe (khoon me duba hAey parcham)

Aey Karbala Karbala Aey Karbala

5
4. Haey kahi laasha-e-Akbar (to kahi turbat-e-Asghar)
Zakhm kis derja lage hAey (hAeye Bano ke jigar par)

Aey Karbala Karbala Aey Karbala

6
5. Binte Zahra ki dukhaee (saat roti hAey khudaee)

7
6. Aeyse bikra koi lasha (jAeyse bikra hua sehra)

Aey Karbala Karbala Aey Karbala

8
7. Ik taraf pyaaso ka girya (ik taraf rota hAey darya)
