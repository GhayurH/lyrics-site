Kesay Pardees main Kazim, pay zamanay aye

Haye mazdoor janazay ko, uthanay aye

Choor aye hain Najaf, aur Madina saray

Tou lawaris nahe ZAhra kay, hain betay pyaray

Han mera lal tujhay saray, laijanay aye

Ho gaye Qaid khatam, Mosa Kazim meray

Qaid khanay say nikal aao, main sadqay teray

Bakiyya walay hain tera soug, mananay aye

Baap zinda’n main hai, ghar mein hai soug bapa

betiyan Mosa e Kazim ki, yeh deti hain sada

Kash kay baba humain seenay, laganay ayeen

Qaid main kehtay rahay, sathway meray Imam

Haye Kazim ya Sakina ho, ya baghdad ya sham

Hum Tu nana hai tera deen, bachanay aye
