Veerna Main Teri Qabar Banawan Ya Shaam Nu Jawan

Kavey Zainab Ujri, Jung Karbala Di Muk Gayi

Lutiyan Gaiyan Ridawan

Keevain Shaam Le Jawan, Sajjad Beemaray

Hosh Naye Aundi Es Nu, Ay Sakhat Lacharay

Menu Samajh Nahi Aundi, Veeran Dasa Main Keevain

Enu Maut Ton Bachawan

Uth To Mein Lath Payian, Par Ro Nayi Sakdi

Laash Uthay Pathraan Da, Main Dair Han Takdi

Mere Hath Paband Hin, Keewain Laash Ton Main Teri

Pathran Nu Ah Hataavan

Baba Khaak Siraan Vich, Es Waal Lukaaye

Ummat Ne Rasiyan De, Sanu Zaivar Paaye

Main Suniyaye Veeran, Es Shaam De Shahar Diyan

Baun Okhiyan Ne Rawan

Shaam Shaher Vich Kisnay, Sadey Pardey Banaune

Mal Oona Dey Majmey, Sadey Raah Chon Hatonay

Mera Ghazi Veeran, Ja Naher De Kinarey

Sameya Kata Kay Baawan

Akhtar Duniya Faani, Ik Din Mukjani

Rehni Ey Do Jag Te, Syeda Di Kahani

Lakh Roke Duniya, Pursey Nu Qaym Rakhna

Zehra Diyan Duawan
