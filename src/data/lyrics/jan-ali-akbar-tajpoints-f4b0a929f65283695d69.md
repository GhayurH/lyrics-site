Aye Jan Ali Akbar.
Wawela Wawela Shud.

1
Shabeeh-e- Payamber Haye Haye Ali Akbar.
Kaisay Jiye Gi Tere Bin Haye Yeh Madar.
Beta Ali Akbar Madar Ko Pukaro Wawela Wawela Shud.
Ghamzada Madar Ko Chehra Tu Dikha Do Wawela Wawela Shud.

2
Khak Se Utho Mujh Ko Bola Lo Aray Maa Hai Pareshan Kahan Ho Mere Dilbar.
Kehti Hoi Beta Zindan Chali Maa Wawela Wawela Shud.
Aray Sare Shaheedon Ka ALLAH Nigehbaan Wawela Wawela Shud.
Ghar Howa Veraan Aao Meri Jaan Aray Ho Gaya Sajjad Akela Mera Dilbar.

3
Maa Ka Yeh Armaan Tha Tujhe Dulha Banati Wawela Wawela Shud.
Aray Maa Teri Dulhan Ko Nazoon Se Sajati Wawela Wawela Shud.
Ho Nahi Payi Aas Yeh Pori Aray Aisay Gaye Aaye Na Maidaan Se Palat Kar.
Maa Hai Rasan Basta Sar Tera Sina Par Wawela Wawela Shud.

4
Aray Balwa-e- Aada Mein Laya Hai Muqadar Wawela Wawela Shud.
Aye Mere Pyaray Nook-e- Sina Se Aray Dekh Le Maa Teri Hai Balway Mein Khulay Sar.
Beta Ali Akbar Madar Ka Hai Wadah Wawela Wawela Shud.
Aray Soag Manaon Gi Ta Umar Tumhara Wawela Wawela Shud.

5
Ab Na Piyon Gi Thanda Mein Pani Aray Ab Na Mein Baithon Gi Kabhi Saye Mein Ja Kar.
Poch Liya Mujh Se Sughra Ne Jo Tera Wawela Wawela Shud.
Aray Kaisay Bataon Gi Akbar Nahi Aaya Wawela Wawela Shud.
Roye Gi Sughra Tarpay Gi Sughra Aray Tere Bana Kaisay Jiye Gi Teri Khwahar.
