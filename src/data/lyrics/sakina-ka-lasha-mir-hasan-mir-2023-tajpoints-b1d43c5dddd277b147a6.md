Sakina Ka Lasha Mir Hasan Mir 2023

1
Chaley Aao Abbas Deney Ko Kandah Uthatay Hain Abid, Sakina Ka Lasha.
Qayamat Hai Zindan Mein Aaj Barpa Uthatay Hain Abid, Sakina Ka Lasha.
Hai Dastoor Yeh Parday Daroon Ke Ghar Ka Yeh Beti Ka Lasha Hai Shab Mein Uthay Ga.
Sitaron Se Keh Do Keh Kar Dein Andhera Uthatay Hain Abid, Sakina Ka Lasha.
Falak Hil Raha Hai Zameen Kanpti Hai Labon Par Musafir Ke Nad-e-Ali Hai.
Imamat Ke Hathoon Mein Hota Hai Larza Uthatay Hain Abid, Sakina Ka Lasha.

2
Galay Se Laga Lein Behan Ko Utha Lein Janazay Ko Sajjad Kaisay Sanbhalein.
Hai Dehyan Iss Ki Tooti Hoi Pasliyon Ka Uthatay Hain Abid, Sakina Ka Lasha.
Dua Kar Rahi Thi Musalsal Qaza Ki Bari Aarzu Thi Issay Karbala Ki.
Chali Apne Baba Se Milney Ko Dukhiya Uthatay Hain Abid, Sakina Ka Lasha.
Woh Kehti Thi Kab Ghar Ko Jaein Gay Bhai Yeh Hasrat Qaza Bhi Mitanay Na Payi.
Madine Ki Janib Hai Dukhiya Ka Chehra Uthatay Hain Abid, Sakina Ka Lasha.

3
Ossay Kya Khabar Thi Keh Aisay Lutay Gi Na Beta Rahe Ga Na Beti Rahe Gi.
Nikal Jaye Dam Hi Na Asghar Ki Maa Ka Uthatay Hain Abid, Sakina Ka Lasha.
Watan Mein Ghareeb Al-Watan Woh Ali Tha Hai Pardais Mein Yeh Ali Be Sahara.
Uthaya Tha Haider Ne Zahra Ka Lasha Uthatay Hain Abid, Sakina Ka Lasha.
 Takallum Yeh Maiyyat Hai Bekas Behan Ki Kamar Jhuk Rahi Hai Ghareeb Al-Watan Ki.
Bahatar Janazon Se Bhari Janaza Uthatay Hain Abid, Sakina Ka Lasha.
