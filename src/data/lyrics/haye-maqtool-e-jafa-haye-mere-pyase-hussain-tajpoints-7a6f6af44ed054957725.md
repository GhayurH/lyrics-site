Haye Maqtool-e-Jafa Haye Mere Pyase Hussain.
Be-Watan Be Aasra Haye Mere Pyase Hussain.

1
Woh Tere Bhai Bhatijay Woh Tere Dono Pisar.
Woh Tere Aoun-o-Muhammad Woh Tere Noor-e-Nazar.
Sara Kunba Lut Gaya Haye Mere Pyase Hussain.
Woh Shabih-e-Mustafa Woh Chand Woh Kariyal Jawaan.

2
Jis Ke Marne Par Larz Uthay Zameen-o-Aasmaan.
Haye Woh Akbar Tera Haye Mere Pyase Hussain.
Haye Woh Abbas Jis Ke Nehar Par Bazu Katay.
Mar Gaya Woh Bawafa Ik Mashk Pani Ke Liye.

3
Phir Bhi Ghar Pyasa Raha Haye Mere Pyase Hussain.
Pyase Asghar Ke Liye Woh Tere Pani Ka Sawal.
Goad Mein Kaise Tarapta Tha Woh Tera Nonehal.
Mar Gaya Bacha Tera Haye Mere Pyase Hussain.

4
Saqi-e-Kausar Ka Dilbar Our Pyasa Reh Gaya.
Tuff Ho Oss Darya Pe Jo Chup Chap Behta Reh Gaya.
Khushk Tha Seena Tera Haye Mere Pyase Hussain.
Bar Zameen-e-Karbala Woh Aakhri Sajda Tera.

5
Haye Jab Gardan Pe Teri Shimar Ka Khanjar Chala.
Kat Gaya Sokha Gala Haye Mere Pyase Hussain.
Aik Bibi Ghamzada Noha Kuna Maqtal Mein Hai.
Ho No Ho Shabir Ki Dukhyari Maa Maqtal Mein Hai.

6
Ro Rahi Hai Fatima Haye Mere Pyase Hussain.
Dekh Kar Pani Mussadiq Ka Larz Jata Hai Dil.
Pyas Bujh Jaye Tu Oss Par Our Tarpata Hai Dil.
Kehta Jata Hai Sada Haye Mere Pyase Hussain.
