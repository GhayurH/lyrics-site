Zainab Lipat Kay Royeen, Abbas Kay Alam Say

Sayedanian Thin Muztar, Chehray Thay Zard Gham Say

Liptee Hoee Alam Say, Kehthee Thi Bint-e-Zehra

Is Waqt-e-Bekasi Mein, Abbas Bichray Hum Say

Bachi Nay Maa Say Poocha, Kyun Itna Ro Rahee Ho

Amoo Nahi Milain Gai, Kya Phir Na Aakay Humsay

Baba Chacha Kahan Hain, Laey Ho Kyun Alam Ko

Yeh Poochthi Thi Bachi, Liptee Shah-e-Umam Say

Abbas Mar Gaey Hain, Sun Kar Khabar Yeh Sheh Ki

Tasweer-e-Yas-o-Hasrath, Har Bibi Ki Alam Say

Ulfath Ki Aur Wafa Ki, Zinda Misaal Ban Ker

Mashk-e-Sakina Ab Bhi, Hay Munsalik Alam Say

Bhai Say Bhai Bichray, Jis Ka Tha Us Ki Halath

Dard Aashna Say Poocho, Ya Phir Anis-e-Gham Say
