1
Tere Ishq Ki Inteha Chahta Hoon.
Meri Sadgi Dekh Kya Chahta HOon.
Sitam Ho Keh Ho Wadah Be Hijabi.
Koi Baat Sabar Aazma Chahta Hoon.

2
Yeh Janat Mubarak Rahe Zahdon Ko.
Keh Main Aap Ka Samna Chahta Hoon.
Zara Sa Tu Dil Hoon, Magar Shookh Itna.
Wohi Lan Tarani Suna Chahta Hoon.

3
Koi Dam Ka Mehmaan Hoon Aye Ahl-e-Mehfil.
Cherag-e-Sahar Hoon, Bujah Chahta Hoon.
Bhari Bazm Mein Raaz Ki Baat Keh Di.
Bara Be Adab Hoon, Saza Chahta Hoon.
