1
1) Sare jahan ko waafke,  karbobala karo
Sibtey nabi ke gham mein matam bapa karo

2
2) Sheh ki majlis mei jo aayenga bakshisho ki sanad payenga

Sibtey nabi ke gham mein matam bapa karo

3

Sibtey nabi ke gham mein matam bapa karo

4
