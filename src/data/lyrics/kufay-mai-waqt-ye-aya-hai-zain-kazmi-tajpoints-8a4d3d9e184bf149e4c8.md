1
Kufay Mai Waqt Ye Aya Hai Zain Kazmi
Haye Kaisa Zahra Jaiyon Par Kofay Mein Waqt Yeh Aaya Hai.
Zahra Ke Baad Ali Ka Bhi Ab Sar Se Utha Saya Hai.
Zainab Kulsoom Ko Ro Ro Kar Hasrat Se Dekhtey Jate Hain.
Shabar Shabeer Ko Reh Reh Kar Seene Ke Sath Lagatay Hain.
Ummat Ki Be Parwai Ne Haye Kitna Ali Ko Sataya Hai.
Sajday Mein Kha Kar Taig-e-Jafa Kabay Ka Waris Rota Hai.
Jo Karb-o-Bala Mein Hona Hai Kofay Mein Raaz Woh Khulta Hai.
Haider Pe Yeh Talwar Nahi Shabeer Pe Khanjar Chalaya Hai.
Zanjeerein Abid Ke Tan Par Zainab Ko Rasan Pehnaein Gay.
Abbas Akbar Qasim Asghar Sab Par Taig Chalaein Gay.
Ik Zarb Ne Haye Haider Ko Kya Kya Manzar Dikhlaya Hai.
Nana Ka Janaza Bholay Nahi Zahra Ke Dard Rolatay Hain.
Roti Hain Behnein Our Bhai Taboot Ali Ka Uthatay Hain.
Ummat Ne Deen Ke Warison Ko Har Qadam Pe Youn Tarpaya Hai.
Zainab Kulsoom Yeh Kehti Hain Ro Ro Kar Baba Bolo Zara.
Ik Bar Laga Lo Seene Se Ab Kafan Se Banhein Kholo Zara.
Sun Sun Kar Bain Yeh Behno Ke Abbas Ka Dil Bhar Aaya Hai.
Kya Kya Na Ali Par Zulm Kiye Har Door Mein Hi Logon Ne Asad.
Har Haq Jhutlaya Maula Ka Itna Tha Ali Se Bugz-o-Hasad.
Jis Mimber Ke Waris They Ali Oss Par Gasib Ko Bithaya Hai.
