Wo Teri Yaad Thi Ab Yaad Aaya.

1
Aaj Mushkil Tha Sanbhalna Aye Dost.
Tu Mosibat Mein Ajab Yaad Aaya.
Din Guzara Tha Bari Mushkil Se.
Phir Tera Wada-e-Shab Yaad Aaya.
Tera Bhoola Howa Paiman-e-Wafa.
Mar Rahein Gay Agar Ab Yaad Aaya.

2
Phir Kayi Loag Nazar Se Guzre.
Phir Koi Shehar-e-Tarb Yaad Aaya.
Haal-e-Dil Hum Bhi Sunate Lekin.
Jab Wo Rukhsat Howa Tab Yaad Aya.
Baith Kar Saya-e-Gul Mein Nasir.
Hum Bohat Roye Woh Jab Yaad Aaya.
