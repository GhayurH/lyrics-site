Laashay pe Mujtaba kay, teeron ka saibaan hay

1
Zehra ke dil dukhanay,  masroof haar kamaah hay

2
1) Barhti hi ja rahi hay, Zehra ki bayqarari
Baychain Mustafa hain, sooni hay kuld sari
Seenay pe Mujtaba ke,  haey teer ka nishan hay

3
2) Shabbir ki sada hay, logo nah teer maaro
Taboote Mujtaba ko, aisa toh nah savaro
Yeh woh Hasan hain jo ke,  Khaliq ka razdaan hay

4
3) Jo teer bachgaye hain, Karbal may woh chalana
May jaanta hu logo, nahin door woh zamana
Bhai kay bad mera,  Karbal main imtahaan hay

5
4) Lashay Hassan pay chup kay, jis jis nay teer maara
Mehshar may nah milagah, unko koyi sahara
Loh aaj tum ko deta,  Shabbire yeh zubaan hay

6
5) Chaho to may bhi kar doon, barpa abhi qayamat
Baazo abhi hain meray, Abbas kay salamat
Akbar hain saath meray,  woh nau abhi jawaan hay

7
6) Tauqeer zehar de kay, unko sabar nah aya
Pehlo bhi Mustafa ka, haq daar seh churaya
Ummat ki is wafa pay,  Aalay Nabi hairan hay
