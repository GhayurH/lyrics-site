Darwaza Giraya Baba.....2
Ummat Ne Teri Zehra Par.....2

1
Hasnain Ko Jhula Jhulane Sukkane Falaq Aate The
Jis Dar Pe Salami Dene Har Wakt Malak Aate The
Haai Kalma Goyo Ne.....
Haai Yeh Kalma Goyo Ne Us Dar Ko Jalaya Baba

2
Tuti Hai Meri Pasli Aur Mohsin Ki Hui Hai Shahadat
Hai Aap Ke Baad Aai Baba Har Lamha Mujh pe Qayamat
Kafir Ne Sitam Jo Na Dhaya.....
Kafir Ne Sitam Jo Na Dhaya Ummat Ne Woh Dhaya Baba

3
Ashab Me Kutch Aaise Hai Jo Dar Paiye Ruswaie Hai
Dhate Hai Sitam Woh Mujh par Kehne Ko To Shehdai Hai
Aaise Ashab Ne Mujhko.....
Aaise Ashab Ne Mujhko Har Lamha Sataya Baba

4
Gaddar Hui Hai Ummat Kismat Me Ranjo Mahan Hai
Zalim Ki Jasarat Dekhe Haider Ke Gale Me Rasan Hai
Hai Takdeer Ne Kaisa.....
Hai Takdeer Ne Kaisa Din Mujh ko Dikhaya Baba

5
Pehro Me Khadi Thi Lekin Darbar Me Koi Na Uttha
Tazeem Me Aap Ka Uthna Ummat Ko Yaad Na Aaya
Furkat Ne Aap Ki Har Dam.....
Furkat Ne Aap Ki Har Dam Mujh ko Tadpaya Baba

6
Baad Aap Ke Hai Yasrab Me Qayam Darbar E Shahi
Siddika Hun Mai Lekin Maangi Hai Mujh se Gawahi
Rad Kar Ke Meri Gawahi.....
Rad Kar Ke Meri Gawahi Haq Mera Chupaya Baba

7
Baitul Ahzan Hai Kismat Tanhaie Meri Aadat
Baba Main Na Seh Paungi Ab Aur Yeh Gam Ki Shiddat
Zinda Hi Turbat E Gam Me......
Zinda Hi Turbat E Gam Me Mujhko Dafnaya Baba

8
Main Hi Fakhre Hawwa Hun Main Hi Fakhre Maryam Hun
Attharah Baras Ke Sin Me Manind Zaeefa Kham Hun
Baad Aap Ke Misle Yatima.....
Baad Aap Ke Misle Yatima Main Hun Be Saya Baba

9
Hamid Phir Raat Hi Hoti Din Par Jo Musibat Padhti
Ro Ro Kar Boli Bibi Kab Tak Yeh Qayamat Sehti
Accha Hua Khawb Me Mujhko.....
Accha Hua Khawb Me Mujhko Pas Apne Bulaya Baba
