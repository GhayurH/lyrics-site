1
Ghazi Main Tere Baaj Roni Aan Veerna Be-Khata.
Saday Saar Ke Khaimay Lokaan Ne Haye Lut Layi Pak Rida.
Mein Te Baqiye V Kadi Na Tere Bajoon Jandi.
Kiwein Mein Sham Choon Jawaan Aye Dasa Ja Ghazi.
Mere Chaar Choferay Shami Ne Koi Veer Na Naal Riya.
Shamiyaan Naizay Te Chayi Aye Jo Chadar Meri.
Naal Le Chaliyaan Alam Mein Aye Amanat Teri.
Tere Kool Amanat Chad Chaliyaan Mein Kafno Baaj Bhira.
Waal Kinj Chehray Te Pandi Kiwein Mitiyaan Pandi.
Samne Tere Mein Aaondi Be Rida Mar Jandi.
Likh Shukar Keh Veran Ghazi Taun Nayi Takiya Haal Mera.
Dekh Halat Kiwein Badli Aye Teri Zainab Di.
Meri Tazeem Hi Ummat Choon Aye Veran Bhul Gayi.
Tu Sain Bulanda Sain Jis Choon Ohno Qaidi Deen Sada.
Sain Zainab Jadon Kita Wain Ban Wich Akbar.
Dowein Bazu Pas-e- Gardan Si Rasan De Andar.
Mehmal Tu Ohwein Dig Payi Si Jiwein Zeen Taun Veer Latha.
