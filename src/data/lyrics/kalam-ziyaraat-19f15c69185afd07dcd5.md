1
Aa Ali Asghar
Tujhko jo pawoon aey mere Asghar
Jhoola jhoolaono aa mere Asghar
Jhoola jhoolaoon lori sunaoon doodh pilaoon aa mere Asghar

2
1) Mere mujahid mere sipahi
Khatme huwi mein dame laraye
Ghar ko sidharo maa ko pukaro
Mein chain pawoon aa mere Asghar
Jhoola jhoolaoon lori sunaoon doodh pilaoon aa mere Asghar

3
2) Yaad arahi hein masum bete
Din mein sukun hai bechein raate
Ji na sakungi roti rahungi
Ansu bahao aa mere Asghar
Jhoola jhoolaoon lori sunaoon doodh pilaoon aa mere Asghar

4
3) Puch rahi hai bali Sakina
Kyu nahi aya lot ke bhayya
To hi batade maidan se aake
Mein kya batawu aa mere Asghar
Jhoola jhoolaoon lori sunaoon doodh pilaoon aa mere Asghar

5
4) Pehre huwe hai Shaam ke saye
Ab to parinde bhi lot aaye
Mein rorahi hu jaan ko rahi ho
Tujko sulao aa mere Asghar
Jhoola jhoolaoon lori sunaoon doodh pilaoon aa mere Asghar

6
5) Mehsus mujko ye ho raha hai
Jese ke Jhoola bhi roraha hay
Aey mere beta, ye rota Jhoola
Kaise Jhoolawu aa mere asghar
Jhoola jhoolaoon lori sunaoon doodh pilaoon aa mere Asghar

7
6) Maqtal se aaye aise khabar
Khurta lahu sab tera tar hay
Teer laga hai zakhmi gala hai
Jhoola jhoolaoon lori sunaoon doodh pilaoon aa mere Asghar
