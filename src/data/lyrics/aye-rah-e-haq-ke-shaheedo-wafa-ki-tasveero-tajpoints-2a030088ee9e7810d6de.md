1
Aye Rah-e-Haq Ke Shaheedo Wafa Ki Tasveero.
Tumhein Watan Ki Hawaein Salam Kehti Hain.
Lagane Aag Jo Aye The Aashyane Ko.
Wo Sholay Apne Laho Se Bhuja Liye Tum Ne.
Baca Liya Hai Yateemi Se Kitne Pholon Ko.
Sohag Kitni Baharon Ke Rakh Liye Tum Ne.
Tumhein Chaman Ki Fizaein Salam Kehti Hain.
Chale Jo Ho Gay Shahadat Ka Jaam Pee Kar Tum.
Rasool-e-Pak Ne Banhoon Mein Le Liya Ho Ga.
Ali Tumhari Shujat Pe Jhoomte Hoon Gay.
Hussain Pak Ne Irshad Yeh Kiya Ho Ga.
Tumhein Khuda Ki Rizaein Salam Kehti Hain.
Janab-e-Fatima Jigar-e-Rasool Ke Agay.
Shaheed Ho Ke Kiya Maa Ko Surkhuro Tum Ne.
Janab-e-Zaianb Gawahi Deti Hain.
Shaheedo Rakhi Hai Behno Ki Aabro Tum Ne.
Watan Ki Betiyaan Maein Salam Kehti Hain.
