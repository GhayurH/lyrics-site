1
Ghar Rab Da Aye Wich Mera Maula Aaya Aye
Mere Peer Ali Ne Aa Ke Kaba Qibla Banaya Aye.
Tere Putraan De Aj Matamiyan Tere Aan Di Khushi Manani.
Tere Munkiran Apne Wadiyan Di Aj Phori Pani.
Tainu Aisay Kam Layi Mustafa Ne Bulaya Aye.
Jiday Putar Sakhawat Sabar Wafa De Aap Khuda Ne Sare.
Jaye Nuseri Rab Na Aakhan Karan Ki Fair Wacharay.
Jinno Mar Ke Mere Maula Fair Uthaya Aye.
Jidi Khilqat Khak De Naal Howay Nabi Madad Layi Nayi Mangda.
Mera Peer Way Asadullah Tera Bakriyan Wangar Nasda.
Kachay Deen Da Rah Tainu Chakran De Wich Paya Aye.
Mojood Si Oh Jiwein Azlaan Taun Ainj Kabay Oon Bar Oh Aaya.
Jehray Nayi Manday San Najfi Rab Ne Ainj Samjhaya.
Main Apne Ghar Da Qabza Aap Choraya Aye.
Qasida Khuwan Party Ch Faiyaz Party Sialkot.
Matami Sangat
Anjuman-e-Sipah-e-Ahl-e-Bait Sialkot
