Karbal ki kahani jab koi bhi sunayega
Dil walon ki aankhon se ek darya baheyega

1
Wo qafela Sarwar ka jo shaan se nikla tha
Sughra ko khabr kya thi na laut ke aayega
Zehra Zehra pursa lo bahattar ka

2
Tooti hai qamar sheh ki ghabra na magar Akbar
Farzande nabi teri mayyat ko uthayega
Zehra Zehra akbar ka bhi pursa lo

3
Pamaal hua qasim jab ghodon ki tapon se
Kis tarha chacha teri mayyat ko uthayega
Zehra Zehra qasim ka bhi pursa lo

4
Tha hathon ke katne ka kuch gham na dilawar ko
Saydaniyo ka parda ab kaun bachayega
Zehra Zehra abbas ka pursa lo

5
Rotay thay Ali Asghar jhoolay may na sotay thay
Madar ko khabar kia thi ik teer sulaye ga

6
Mehman bulaye hay aey Koofiyo Sarwar ko
Kia kunba Sheh e deen ka pani bhi na paye ga

7
Sarwar ne uthaye thay sab saathiyon ke laashe
Shabbir tera laasha ab kaun utheyga
Zehra Zehra shabbir ka pursa lo
