Qaidi bani khari hay Karbal may Zehra sani
Bhoolai Gi Kaisai Zainab Adaz nabi jo ki hai ummat nai merhbani

1
1) Paband-e-Rasan Zainab chadar bhi nahi ser pai
Tuti hai qiyamat yeh Zehra kai bahrai ghar main
Bazu bandhai huwai hain haider ki baitiyon kai, hai Shaam ko Ravani

2
2) Kerbal na gayi Bibi, bethi hun Medinai main
Hai barchi lagi kaisai, terai bahi kai seenai main
Mer jayai gi Sughra to haii Asgher nai jab Sunayi, Kerbal ki yeh Kahani

3
3) Zehra bhi dua deti, Moula sai ajar milta
Is kaam kai sadqai main Firdos main ghar milta
Kerbal Main Musalmano haii Asgher ko pila dai tai, do boond agar pani

4
4) Qaidi beemar syed, ankhon sai lahu jari
Zainab ki aseeri pai, roya hai raat sari
Bhoola na mart-e-dam tak haii ker ker kai yah roya, Akber ki nojawani
