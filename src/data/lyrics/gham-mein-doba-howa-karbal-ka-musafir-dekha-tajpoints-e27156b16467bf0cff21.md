Gham Mein Doba Howa Karbal Ka Musafir Dekha.
Rote Shaibr Ko Zahra Ki Lahad Par Dekha.

1
Apna Sar Mar Ke Dewar Se Roya Ghazi.
Jab Nabi Zadi Ko Aate Howay Bahir Dekha.
Door Tak Phaili Hai Qasim Ki Jawani Ran Mein.
Dair Tak Dhondta Phir Sibt-e-Payamber Dekha.

2
Noor Aankhon Ka Gaya Qatilon Se Poche Hussain.
Koi Batlaye Kissi Ne Mera Akbar Dekha.
Ravi Likhte Hain Sajjad Ko Jab Hosh Aaya.
Jalte Khiyam Mein Zainab Ko Khule Sar Dekha.

3
Mujh Ko Barbad Khuda Kar De Yeh Bola Pani.
Sheh Ke Hathon Mein Jo Zakhmi Ali Asghar Dekha.
