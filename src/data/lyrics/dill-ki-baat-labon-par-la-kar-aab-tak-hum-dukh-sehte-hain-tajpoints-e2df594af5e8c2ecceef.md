Dill Ki Baat Labon Par La Kar Aab Tak Hum Dukh Sehte Hain.
Hum Ne Suna Tha Iss Basti Mein Dil Wale Bhi Rehte Hain.

1
Beet Giya Sawan Ka Mahina, Mosam Ne Nazrein Badlein.
Lekin Inn Piyasi Aankhon Se Aab Tak Aanso Behte Hain.
Aik Humein Awarah Kehna Koi Bara Ilzam Nahi.
Dunya Wale Dill Walon Ko Our Bohat Kuch Kehte Hain.

2
Jinn Ki Khatir Shehar Bhi Chora, Jinn Ke Liye Badnaam Howay.
Aaj Wohi Hum Se Begane Se Rehte Hain.
Woh Jo Abhi Iss Rah Guzar Se Chaak Girebaan Guzra Tha.
Oss Awaraah Dewane Ko:JALIB JALIB: Kehte Hain.
