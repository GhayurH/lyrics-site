1
Allaho Akbar Fatima Karbalai Brothers.
Kibriya Ke Baad Jaisay Kibriya Mumkin Nahi.
Fatima Ke Baad Koi Fatima Mumkin Nahi.
Kibriya, Ya Mustafa, Ya Murtaza Likha Faqat.
Fatima Ka Iss Se Kam Tu Qafia Mumkin Nahi.
Keh Rahe Hain Anbiya ALLAH Ho Akbar Fatima.
Khud Muhammad Bhi Kharey Hain Tere Dar Par Fatima.
Hath Ki Adna Lakeeron Mein Na Chup Jaye Kahin.
Tum Ne Likha Hur Ke Mathay Par Muqadar Fatima.
Keh Diya Umm-e-Abeeha Jab Muhammad Ne Tumhein.
Anbiya Sare Tumhein Kehte Hain Madar Fatima.
Sheh Ke Gham Mein Behney Walay Ashk Zaya Na Hoye.
Aap Ke Romal Mein Hain Woh Samandar Fatima.
Jo Khuda Ki Baat Kehti Thin Ali Ke Kan Mein.
Thin Shab-e-Mairaj Ke Parday Ke Andar Fatima.
Dar Nahi Kuch Matami Ko Bas Aqeeda Hai Yehi.
Bakhshwane Aaein Gi Khud Rooz-e-Mehshar Fatima.
Ishq Mein Shabbir Ke Mairaj Paye Kaun Kaun.
Naam Tum Ne Arsh Par Likhe Bahatar Fatima.
Aye Musalmano Bukhari Mein Bhi Hai Likha Howa.
Dunya Se Kin Se Gayin Naraz Ho Kar Fatima.
 Naqsh Kaisay Na Jamaein Apne Har Ik Jashan Mein.
Yeh Naqi, Asghar Tumharey Hain Sodagar Fatima.
