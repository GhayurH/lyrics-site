1
Sar Kata Kar Shah Ne Oncha Kiya Sar Deen Ka.
Khud Khuda Karta Hai Iss Qurbani Par Inn Ko Salam.
Keh Raha Hai Har Azadar-e-Hussain Ibn-e-Ali.
Meri Sari Zindagani Karbala Walon Ke Naam.
Hai Yeh Sansoon Ki Rawani Karbala Walon Ke Naam.
Ghoor Se Koi Sunay Gar Dil Ki Dhak Dhak Ki Sada.
Aisa Lagta Hai Keh Jaisay Dil Mein Hai Matam Bapa.
Zindagi Ki Yeh Nishani Karbala Walon Ke Naam.
Kis Mein Himmat Hai Keh Dein Se Hazaf Karday Karbala.
Karbala Ke Hi Tu Sadqay Aaj Tak Hai LaIlaha.
Deen Ki Sari Kahani Karbala Walon Ke Naam.
Sans Dharkan Zindagi Karbala Walon Ke Naam.
Yeh Khirad Our Aa Gahi Karbala Walon Ke Naam.
Khon Ki Har Bond Bhi Karbala Walon Ke Naam.
Ho Gaya Deen-e-Nabi Karbala Walon Ke Naam.
Alqama Ki Tashnagi Karbala Walon Ke Naam.
Yeh Sabeel-e-Ashk Bhi Karbala Walon Ke Naam.
Karbala Se Ishq Bhi Karbala Walon Ke Naam.
Kin Ke Ghoron Ki Yeh Qasmein Kha Raha Hai Kibriya.
Kaun Woh Sabir Hai Jin Ke Sath Hai Mera Khuda.
Sare Yeh Qissay Qurani Karbala Walon Ke Naam.
Hashar Tak Jari Rahe Ga Arbaeen-e-Be Nawa.
Yeh Azadaron Ke Lashkar Yeh Man-o-Salwa Gawah.
Munfarid Yeh Mezbani Karbala Walon Ke Naam.
Roza-e-Shabeer Mein Shab Bhar Woh Sajday Our Qiyam.
Roza-e-Abbas Par Soraj Ka Woh Pehla Salam.
Meri Woh Subhein Sohani Karbala Walon Ke Naam.
Gar Lahu Asghar Ka Maula Chehray Pe Miltay Nahi.
Na Zameen Sona Ugti Na Barasta Meen Kahin.
Rizq Ki Sari Rawani Karbala Walon Ke Naam.
Teen Din Ki Tashnagi Ko Kaisay Bholein Hum Bhala.
Haye Woh Masoom Asghar Our Teer-e-Hurmala.
Yeh Sabeelein Our Yeh Pani Karbala Walon Ke Naam.
Hai Diya Khon-e-Jigar Akbar Ne Kaisay Al-Amaan.
Dein Hoga Ab Kabhi Bhi Na Zaeef-o-Na Tawaan.
Deen Ki Sari Jawani Karbala Walon Ke Naam.
Hai Yehi Qurban Mere Dil Ki Har Lehza Dua.
Mar Ke Bhi Zindah Rahon Gar Chahein Wali-e-Karbala.
Ho Hayat Javedani Karbala Walon Ke Naam.
