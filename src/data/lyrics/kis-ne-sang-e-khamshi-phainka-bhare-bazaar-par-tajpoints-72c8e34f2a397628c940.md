1
Kis Ne Sang-e-Khamshi Phainka Bhare Bazaar Par.
Ik Sakoot-e-Marg Jari Hai Dar-o-Dewar Par.
Tu Ne Apni Zulf Ke Saye Mein Afsane Kahe.
Mujh Ko Zanjerein Mili Hain Jurat-e-Izhar Par.

2
Shakh-e-Uryaan Par Khile Ik Phool Iss Andaz Se.
Jiss Tarha Taza Laho Chamke Nayi Talwar Par.
Sang Dill Ahbab Ke Daman Mein Ruswai Ke Phool.
Main Ne Dekha Hai Niya Manzar Faraz-e-Daar Par.

3
Ab Koi Tohmat Bhi Wajh-e-Karb-e-Ruswai Nahi.
Zindagi Ik Umar Se Chup Hai Re Israr Par.
Main Sar-e-Maqtal Hadees-e-Zindagi Kehta Raha.
Ungliyaan Uthti Rahin Mohsin Mere Kirdar Par.
