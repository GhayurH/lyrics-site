Haye Zindan Tera

Meri mazloom behna sakina

meri masoom behna sakina

bole sajjad laasha uthakar

sakina sakina

Haye zindan tera watan bangaya

khoon bhara tera kurta kafan bangaya

kya karoo qaid khana hai ye ghar nahi

ghusl dene ko panni mayassar nahi

aur kafnaoo kaise ke chadar nahi

bekasi aur ghurbat may ay ghamzada

khoon bhara tera…

haye zindan tera…

jab

suroyi tamachon ki paayi saza

baliyan chin gayi aur daaman jala

aur kaano ke khoon se kurta bhara

haye tukhjo kafan bhi nahi mil saka

khoon bhara tera…

haye zindan tera…

tu andhero se darti rahi meri jaan

baap ke gham may roti rahi meri jaan

ghar chalo maa se kehti rahi meri jaan

tera armaan poora nahi ho saka

khoon bhara tera…

haye zindan tera…

aisi bichdi ke phir baap se na mili

chut ke baba se zindan ki qaidi bani

haye ye baat sajjad ko kha gayi

haye teeron se unka kafan ban gaya

khoon bhara tera…

haye zindan tera…

tujhko zindan may baba ka sar jo mila

tune baba ke moo par jo moo ko rakha

itna royi ke zindan larazne laga

rote rote hi aayi hai tujhko khaza

khoon bhara tera…

haye zindan tera…

kamsini may sitam tune kitne sahe

chaar saala yateema mai sadqe tere

baade ashoor soyi nahi chain se

chain marke bhi tujhko nahi mil saka

khoon bhara tera…

haye zindan tera…

haye farhan o mazhar ajab waqt tha

us yateema ka jis dam janaza utha

bibiyon may bapa ek kohraam tha

sabne qabre sakina pe noha kiya

khoon bhara tera…

haye zindan tera…
