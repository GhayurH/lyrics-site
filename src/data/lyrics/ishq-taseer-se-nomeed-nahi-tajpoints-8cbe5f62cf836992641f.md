1
Ishq Taseer Se Nomeed Nahi.
Jaan Sipari Shajar-e-Baid Nahi.
Saltanat Dast Badast Ayi Hai.
Jaam-e-May Khatam-e-Jamshaed Nahi.

2
Hai Tajali Teri Saman-e-Wojood.
Zarah Be Par Tu-e-Khursheed Nahi.
Raaz-e-Mashooq Na Ruswa Ho Jaye.
Warna Mar Jane Mein Kuch Bhaid Nahi.

3
Gardish-e-Rang-e-Tarb Se Dar Hai.
Gham-e-Mehromi-e-Javeed Nahi.
Kehte Hain Jeete Hain Umeed Pe Loag.
Hum Ko Jeene Ki Bhi umeed Nahi.
