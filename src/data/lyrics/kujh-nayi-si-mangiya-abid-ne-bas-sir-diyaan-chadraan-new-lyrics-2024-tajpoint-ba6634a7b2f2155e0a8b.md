1
Kujh Nayi Si Mangiya Abid Ne Bas Sir Diyaan Chadraan Mangiyaan San.
Bazar Si Sham De Lokaan Da Sar Nangay Odhiyaan Phophiyaan San.
Wasday Hoye Pathraan Choon Ewein Langiya Sardar Hijabaan Da.
Sanghaan Te Jehriyaan Chadraan San Oh V Sab Khon Ich Dubiyan San.

2
Koi Pathar Maran Wala Si Koi Kholda Pani Sutda Si.
Aithay Kaun Bachanda Bibiyaan Nu Banwaan Wich Rasiyaan Bajiyan San.
Tasheer De Naal Aziyat De Sab Rastay Rakhay Shamiyaan Ne.
Othon Qafila Le Ke Guzrey San Jehri Sham Diyaan Tang Galiyaan San.

3
Ik Chaar Baras Di Masooma Kana Te Khon Si Waliyaan Da.
Neelay Rukhsar Tamachiyaan Dey, Kharaan Nal Zakhmi Taliyaan San.
Tarteeb De Naal Shaheedan De Sir Rakhay Gaye Darbar De Wich.
Apne Apne Maqtool De Nal Akbar Sab Manwaan Khariyaan San.
