Pocho Tu Asghar Se Asad Awan Lyrics
Mawaddat Ke Ilaqay Ka Safar Pocho Tu Asghar Se.
Kahan Khuld-e-Barin Hai Rehguzar Pocho Tu Asghar Se.
Jo Hasti Hur Ki Qismat Ko Badal Dalay Sar-e-Sehra.
Ossi Hasti Ke Barey Mein Agar Pocho Tu Asghar Se.
Jahan Taqseem Hoti Hain Zamane Bhar Ki Taqderein.
Jahan Se Khair Milti Hai Woh Ghar Pocho Tu Asghar Se.
Jo Apni Kamsini Mein Haider-e-Karrar Jaisa Hai.
Fasana e Shujat Mukhtasir Pocho Tu Asghar Se.
Jo Abbas-e-Jari Ko Dad Deta Hai Wafaon Ki.
Zamane Mein Mata e Deedah War Pocho Tu Asghar Se.
Jo Hurmal Ki Taraf Dekhey Nigah-e-Ghaiz-o-Nakhoot Se.
Wigaye Haq-o-Batil Mein Jigar Pocho Tu Asghar Se.
 Asad Jis Ki Nigahon Mein Koi Pinhan Nahi Rehta.
Khudai Par Jo Paihum Hai Nazar Pocho Tu Asghar Se.
