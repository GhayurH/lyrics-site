1
Karna Hai Ya Hussain Kazmi Brothers
Woh Naz-e-Kibriya Hai Woh Shah-e-Karbala Hai.
Zahra Ke Dil Ka Chain Hai Woh Hi Mera Hussain Hai.
Dil Mein Ishq-e-Haider Ka Ik Diya Jalana Hai Karna Hai Ya Hussain.
Syeda Ke Bachoon Ka Farsh-e-Gham Bichana Hai Karna Hai Ya Hussain.
Lakh Mushkilein Aaein Gardanein Bhi Kat Jaein.
Fatwa Gar Daleelein Gar Aasmaan Se Bhi Laein.
Hum Ne Apni Naslon Ko Matami Banana Hai.
Yeh Jo Pursa Dari Hai Munkiron Pe Bhari Hai.
Aye Yazidiyo Sun Lo Karbala Hamari Hai.
Rab Ke Sab Khazano Mein Yeh Alag Khazana Hai.
Ya Hussain Ya Hussain.
Tera Lahu Rayegaan Na Jaye Ga.
Behan Ka Wadah Hai Yeh Rang Zaror Laye Ga.
Hai Dein Ki Baqa Binaye LaIlaha Hussain Badshah.
Aansoon Ke Badil Se Kya Larein Gay Yeh Jalsay.
Hai Nigah-e-Khaliq Mein Tey Yeh Rooz-e-Awwal Se.
Ik Taraf Hussaini Hain Ik Taraf Zamana Hai.
Mujh Gunahgar Pe Har Dam Hai Yeh Inam e Hussain.
Meri Har Gaam Madad Karta Hai Zargam e Hussain.
Chom Leta Ho Jahan Dekhta Ho Nam-e-Hussain.
Karbala Ishq-e-Manast-o-Manam Ghulam-e-Hussain.
Jan Se Pyari May Ishq-e-Wila Hai Mujh Ko.
Yeh Sila Maa Ki Taharat Se Mila Hai Mujh Ko.
Sangton Ke Lashkar Hain Kya Ghazab Ke Tewar Hain.
Yeh Nishan-e-Matam Hi Matami Ka Zewar Hain.
Matami Jaloson Ki Shan Fatehana Hai.
Gham Hai Yeh 72 Bahatar Ka Syeda Ki Chadar Ka.
Karbala Mein Nezay Ki Nook Par Kissi Sar Ka.
Syedon Ko Iss Gham Ne Hashar Tak Rolana Hai.
Hashar Ki Fiza Hogi Masnad-e-Qaza Ho Gi.
Kaun Janati Hoga Kis Ko Kiya Saza Ho Gi.
Syeda Ne Akhtar Yeh Faisla Sunana Hai.
