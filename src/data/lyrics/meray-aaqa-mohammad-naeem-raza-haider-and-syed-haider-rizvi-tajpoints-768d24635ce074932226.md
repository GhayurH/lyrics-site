Meray Aaqa Mohammad Naeem Raza Haider and Syed Haider Rizvi.

1
Mere Aaqa Mere Maula Mere Rehbar Muhammad Hain.
Utaray Jis Pe Quraan Rab Woh Paygambar Muhammad Hain.
Nahi Koi Nabi Baad Az Janab-e-Mursal-e-Azam.
Keh Masnad Pe Risalat Ki Nabi Aakhir Muhammad Hain.
Gul-e-Gulzar-e-Hasti Mein Yeh Ronaq Unn Ke Dam Se Hai.
Hayat-e-Bazm-e-Aalam Ka Haseen Mehwar Muhammad Hain.

2
Inhi Ki Zaat-e-Aqdas Hi Wajood-e-Ishq Wahdat Ka.
Azal Se Qalb-e-Momin Mein Haan Jalwa Gar Muhammad Hain.
Yeh Aadam Se Talak Esa Sabhi Nabiyon Pe Hujjat Hain.
Keh Baad Az Kibriya Afzal Our Bartar Muhammad Hain.
Inhi Ke Hi Taruf Mein Kalam-e-Haq Howa Nazil.
Muzamil Taha-o-Yaseen Kahin Kausar Muhammad Hain.

3
Kaleem-e-Toor Se Pocho Tu Woh Bhi Yeh Hi Keh De Ga.
Jo Chamka Tha Sar-e-Koh Woh Noor-e-Khawar Muhammad Hain.
Raqam Hai Noor Se Jibreel Ke Par Pe Abarat Yeh.
Keh Har Wasf-e-Elahi Ka Woh Ik Mazhar Muhammad Hain.
Ho Kaisay Khof Mehshar Ka Bhala Unn Ke Ghulamo Ko.
Yaqeen Jab Unn Ko Hai Azhar Shafee Mehshar Muhammad Hain.
