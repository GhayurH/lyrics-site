1
Zainab Teri Chadar Taun Sadqay Jawan Dastar Ali Di Rondi Aye Lyrics New Noha 21 Ramzan
Darya Te Katiyaan Wekhiyaan Main Tere Pehrey Daar Diyaan Bahwaan Dastar Ali Di Rondi Aye.
Zahra Di Shafqat Ban Ke Veraan Te Saya Karna Aye.
Toon Aaj Taun Ali De Wangoon Har Hukam Sunaya Karna Aye.

2
Dunya Taun Chaliya Waan Kar Ke Main Tere Hawalay Aye Chawaan Dastar Ali Di Rondi Aye.
Har Baap Da Dil Aye Ronda Takleef Jay Howay Dhiyaan Nu.
Dushman V Na Koi Shala Zindagi Wich Roway Dhiyaan Nu.
Nanay Di Ummat Chahndi Aye Toon Wekhin Sham Diyaan Rahwaan Dastar Ali Di Rondi Aye.

3
Masjid V Rondi Rayi Aye Te Pak Musalla Roya Aye.
Aynj Zehar Aalodah Khanjar Da Waar Mere Te Hoya Aye.
Khoiyan Ne Ibn-e-Muljim Ne Tere Babay Shair Diyaan Sahwaan Dastar Ali Di Rondi Aye.
Ramzan De Aman Maheeney Di Har Raat Fazeelat Wali Aye.

4
Jis Raat Quran Si Aaya Oh Raat Shahadat Wali Aye.
Nabiyaan Ne Ro Ro Ke Suniyaan Natiq Quran Diyaan Hawaan Dastar Ali Di Rondi Aye.
Aye Peer Ali Di Hasrat Ban Gayi Aye Noha Mere Layi.
Kiwein Samar Qaza Da Wela Aaya Si Maula Tere Layi.

5
Putraan De Chehray Wekhi Jaan Dhiyaan Nu Main Seene Lawaan Dastar Ali Di Rondi Aye.
Matami Sangat
Anjuman Ghulaman-e-Aoun-o-Mohammad
Bangali Bagh Lahore
