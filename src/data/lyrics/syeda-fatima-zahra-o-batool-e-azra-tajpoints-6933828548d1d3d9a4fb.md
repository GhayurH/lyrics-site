Syeda Fatima Zahra O Batool e Azra
Rab Ke Hone Ki Zamanat Hai Musalla Tera.

1
Koi Tafseel Se Likhe Jo Teri Azmat Ko Kam Se Kam Quraan Ho.
Mukhtasir Likhe Tu Kausar Hai Qaseeda Tera.
Nisf Eman Ki Hoti Hai Mohafiz Zojah Iss Se Sabit Yeh Howa.
Kul-e-Eman Ka Eman Hai Aadha Tera.

2
Ab Samajh Aaya Keh Jibreel Kyun Aagay Na Barhey Ta Keh Dunya Samjhay.
Sarhad-e-Sidrah Se Aagay Hai Ilaqa Tera.
Tu Ne Dozakh Se Banana Hai Gunahgaron Ko Hum Azadaron Ko.
Naam ALLAH Ne Youn Fatima Rakha Tera.

3
Noor Hai Aaya-e-Tatheer Ka Mafhoom Hai Woh Sach Hai Masoom Hai Woh.
Tu Ne Jab Keh Diya Abbas Hai Beta Tera.
Teri Fizza Ko Abhi Tak Nahi Samjha Koi Aye Meri Shehzadi.
Kaisay Dunya Ko Samajh Aaye Ga Rutba Tera.

4
Tab Kahin Ja Ke Andheron Mein Ujalay Utray Chand Soraj Chamkay.
Jab Utara Gaya Aflak Pe Sadqa Tera.
Kya Hai ALLAH Ka Kya Hai Tera Maloom Nahi Hai Takallum Ko Yaqeen.
Maslak-e-Ishq Mein Hota Nahi Mera Tera.
