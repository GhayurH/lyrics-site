Zindaan Ich Ro Ro Ke, Puchdi Sakina Veer Nu

Kadoon Muk Niyaan Ay Kehdaan Umat Ne Kadoon Laona Teray Pairaan De Zanjeer Nu

Kayi Din Ton Mien Tarsi Haan Haiy Babay De Senay Nu
Phir Bhavain Mien Marjavaan Ek Vaar Milajakay Ronday Hoyay Baysheer Nu

Mazloom Di Mayiyat Te Ro Own Di Ma Akhay
Tera Kafan Banadaindi Kaday Shimar Jay Na Khaonda Haiy Chadaray Tatheer Nu

Sajjad Kavay Ro Ro Ay Shaam Deyo Loko
Mien Hashar Da Zamin Haan Jay Qabar Di Thaan Miljayay Dafnaona Ay Hamsheer Nu

Mar Jaona Ay Zainab Ne Jay Rukaiya Na Tu Shimara
Ghazi Di Bhatijee Nu Na Maar Tamachay Tu Fizza Fizza Kaya Bay Peer Nu

Saan Kholay Sakina De Zindaan Diyaan Seekhaan
Tenu Sekhdi Ay Mar Gayi Ay Aj Kon Devay Ja Ke Paighaam Ay Shabbir Nu
