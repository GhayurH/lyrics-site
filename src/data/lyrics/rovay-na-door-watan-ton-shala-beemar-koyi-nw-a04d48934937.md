Waang Sajjad Na Koi Beemar Wekhay Phophiyaan Wich Bazar
Rovay Na Door Watan Ton Shala Beemar Koyi.
Nangai Saar Vaikhai Na Puphiyan Bharai Bazaar Koyi.

1
Des Apnai’ch Na Perdesi Koi Vi Hovai
Dekh Berjayaina Kun Sir Nangai Koi Na Rovai
Javai Her Mor Tai Rukdi Na Perdai Daar Koyi.

2
Dekhai Na Putar Tai Gal Tauq Zulam Dai Paindai
Naizi Ton Waikhai Na Bachri Dai Koi Dur Rendai
Shala Ghash Khawai Sina Tai Na Baar Baar Koyi.

3
Shaam Wich Pur Jinai Lai Kai Hai Beemar Gaiya
Dekh Kai Veer Bazarin Kun Jivain Ronda Raiya
Rovai Na Khoon Dai Athru Enj Salaar Koyi.

4
Koi Mastoor Kadi Shala Na Pavai Keriyan
Saikh Darbar Dai Wich Behnra Tai Puphiyan Kheriyan
Hovai Sajaad Dai Vanjoun Na Bai Karara Koyi.

5
Tera Khursheed Kerai Mola Ai Ro Ro Kai Dua
Gham Kissai Nu Na Milai Koi Terai Gham Dai Siva
Rovai Gham Terai Dai Bajoun Na Azadaar Koyi.
