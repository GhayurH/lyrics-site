Jab mah-e-Muharram ka namoodar hua chand
Aur Karb o bala pohoncha to ghamkhaar hua chand

1
Rotay rahay ban may bani hashim ke sipahain
Pamal huay dasht may Zehra ke dularay
Mazloom Sheh e deen ka azadar hua chand

2
Tukrray huay zehra ke kaye chand zameen par
Ankhon may samaya hay usi dasht ka manzar
Ashoor se is gham may giraftar hua chand

3
Karta hay tawaf aaj bhi woh Kaaba-e-deen ka
Kia us se taqabul karain Qasim se haseen ka
Yun roza e Shabbir ka zawwar hua chand

4
Yun hota hay zahir ye ghariboon ke alam may
Kehlata hay mazloom sheh e wala ke gham may
Yun matmi kehlanay ka haqdar hua Chand

5
Kehlate pehan leta hay malboos e gham o par
Mathay pe saja leta hay maqtal ki wohi gard
Aisa shab e Ashoor dil-afgar hua chand

6
Jab qatl huay shah to wo dekh raha tha
Zainab ne bhari aah to woh dekh raha tha
Yun bhi to gawah e shah e abrar hua chand

7
Jab subhe dahum goonji Azan e Ali Akbar
Sehray pe saja kar woh nishan e Ali Akbar
Akbar ki jawani ka parastar hua chand

8
Matam ka nishan ban ke azadaron ki soorat
Kab zair o zabar aine razakaron ki soorat
Har halqa e matam may namoodar hua chand
