Veerna Main Teri Qabar Banawaan Ya Sham Nu Jawaan.
Kahway Zainab Ujari Jang Karbala Di Muk Gayi Lutiyaan Gayiyaan Ridawaan.
Kiwein Sham Lay Jawaan Sajjad Bemar Aye.
Hosh Nahi Aoundi Ays Nu Aye Sakht Lachar Aye.
Menu Samaj Nahi Aoundi Weran Disa Main Kiwein Aynu Maut Tu Bachawaan.
Pa Pa Khak Siraan Wich Ays Waal Lokaye.
Umat Ne Rasiyaan De Sanu Zewar Paye.
Main Sunya Aye Veran Haye Sham De Shehar Diyaan Boo Aoukhiyaan Ne Rahwaan.
Aounth Tu Main Lath Payi Aan Par Ro Nahi Sakdi.
Lash Utaye Pathraan Da Hai Dhair Haan Takdi.
Mere Hath Paband Hin Kiwein Lash Tu Main Teri Pathraan Nu Aa Hatawaan.
Sham Shehar Wich Kis Ne Saday Parday Banaone.
Maloona De Majmay Saday Raah Choon Hataone.
Mera Ghazi Veran Ja Nehar De Kinare Samiyaa Kata Ke Bahnwaan.
Akhtar Dunya Fani Ik Din Muk Jani.
Reni Aye Do Jag Te Syedaan Di Kahani.
Lakh Roke Dunya Pursay Nu Qayam Rakhna Zahra Diyaan Duawaan.
