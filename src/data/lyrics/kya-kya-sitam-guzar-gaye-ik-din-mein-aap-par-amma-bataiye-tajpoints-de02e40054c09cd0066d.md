Kya Kya Sitam Guzar Gaye Ik Din Mein Aap Par Amma Bataiye.

1
Masjid Se Ghar Mein Laut Ke Jab Aain Syeda.
Zainab Ne Maa Ka Haal Jo Dekha Tu Yeh Kaha.

2
Balon Ka Rang Badla Hai Kyun Jhuk Gayi Kamar Amma Bataiye.
Kyun Kar Chupaye Rakha Hai Kya Dil Mein Dard Hai Kyun Chehra Zard Hai.
Halat Tumhari Dekh Ke Lagta Hai Mujh Ko Dar Amma Bataiye.
Bas Muskurate Dekha Tha Hum Ne Tumhein Sada Batlao Kya Howa.
Aankhein Hoin Hain Aap Ki Kyun Aanson Se Tar Amma Bataiye.
Main Ne Suna Hai Masjid Mein Rulwaya Aap Ko Jhutlaya Aap Ko.
Kya Baat Dil Pe Lag Gayi Uthta Nahi Jo Sar Amma Bataiye.
Dar Pe Hamare Pehle Tu Aate The Subh-o-Sham Karne Ko Sab Salam.
Nana Ke Bad Phera Hai Ummat Ne Rukh Kidhar Amma Bataiye.
Baba Ka Raaj Hota Tu Yeh Gham Na Jhailtin Yeh Din Na Dekhtin.
Haq Cheena Mere Baba Ka Ummat Ne Kyun Magar Amma Bataiye.
Kya Dard Hai Jo Aap Ko Ha Dam Rulata Hai Dil Doba Jata Hai.
Kyun Roz Ja Ke Roti Hain Nana Ki Qabar Par Amma Bataiye.
Ashkoon Ki Iss Rawani Ne Zainab Ko Aye Raza Sab Kuch Bata Diya.
Zahra Khamosh Ho Gayin Jab Iss Sawal Par Amma Bataiye.
