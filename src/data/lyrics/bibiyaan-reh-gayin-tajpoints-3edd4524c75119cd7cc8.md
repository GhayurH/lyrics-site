1
So Gaya Lashkar Aye Sham-e-Ghareebaan.
Jaltay Hoye Khaimon Ki Bhujti Raakh Par Bibiyaan Reh Gayin.
Dil Se Lagaye Dagh Bahatar Noha Gar Bibiyaan Reh Gayin.
Sham-e-Ghareebaan Se Kuch Pehle Jhola Chadar Bistar Tha.
Pehlu Mein Humshakal-e-Payamber Gaud Mein Maa Ki Asghar Tha.
Godiyaan Khali Mangein Ujrein Be Pisar Bibiyaan Reh Gayin.
Haan Yeh Abu Talib Ka Kunba Betiyaan Sari Haider Ki.
Azmat-e-Zahra Azmat-e-Mariyam Izzat Thin Paygambar Ki.
Chadrein Mangtin Nam-e-Payamber Dar Badar Bibiyaan Reh Gayin.
Dhalta Soraj Soch Raha Hai Waqt-e-Sahar Kya Manzar Tha.
Mehway Ibadat Zair-e-Alam Shabeer Ka Sara Lashkar Tha.
So Gaya Lashkar Jagney Wali Raat Bhar Bibiyaan Reh Gayin.
Darya Mojein Mar Raha Hai Pyas Abhi Tak Baqi Hai.
Shayad Ghazi Laut Ke Aaye Aas Abhi Tak Baqi Hai.
Dekh Zara Zainab Ke Baradar Jaag Kar Bibiyaan Reh Gayin.
Koi Nahi Jo Poch Le Aa Kar Kya Hoi Zainab Teri Chadar.
Tere Tu They Atharah Baradar Kyun Hai Akeli Pehrey Par.
Humsafar Maqtool Hoye Sab Humsafar Bibiyaan Reh Gayin.
Sochon Zara:REHAN-o-SARWAR: Sham-e-Ghareebaan Ka Woh Manzar.
Chak Girebaan Aaye Jo Haider Dekh Ke Zainab Ko Pehrey Par.
Ro Diye Haider Khaimay Maqtal Dekh Kar Bibiyaan Reh Gayin
