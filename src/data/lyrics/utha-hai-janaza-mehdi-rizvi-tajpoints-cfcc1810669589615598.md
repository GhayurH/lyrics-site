1
Shab Ki Tareeki Mein Utha Hai Janaza Haye Haye.
Fatima Zahra Pe Kaisa Waqt Aaya Haye Haye.
Thin Janab-e-Syeda Ki Pasliyaan Tooti Hoi.
Dard Iss Shiddat Ka Rehta Tha Keh Dukhiya Ne Kabhi.

2
Hath Pehlu Se Na Hargiz Bhi Hataya Haye Haye.
Jab Gayin Darbar Mein Bint-e-Nabi Haq Mangney.
Gham Ka Tha Ahsas Zahra Se Qadam Uthtey Na They.
Kha Gaya Darbar Ka Bibi Ko Rasta Haye Haye.

3
Phir Tu Ghar Mein Our Ik Barpa Qayamat Ho Gayi.
Dar Gira Unn Par Tu Mohsin Ki Shahadat Ho Gayi.
Yeh Bhi Zahra Ke Muqadar Mein Tha Sadma Haye Haye.
Ranj Tha Iss Ka Tu Rehti Thi Hamesha Aankh Nam.

4
Fatima Zahra Ko Reh Reh Kar Rulata Tha Yeh Gham.
Hain Madine Mein Akelay Kul Ke Maula Haye Haye.
Ho Raha Hai Nalah-o-Shewan Se Ik Mehshar Bapa.
Khuld Ki Janib Rawana Ho Rahi Hain Syeda.

5
Ja Rahi Hain Chor Kar Bachoon Ko Tanha Haye Haye.
Keh Raha Ab Bhi Koi Ro Ke Yeh Izhar Se.
Karbala Ke Dasht Mein Pyasa Woh Maa Ke Samne.
Ho Raha Hai Qatal Zahra Ka Dulara Haye Haye.
