Shabbar Ka Woh Maqam Hai Qaiser Abbas.

1
Jo Kissi Ko Bhi Na Mil Saka Shabbar Ka Woh Maqam Hai.
Teer-o-Tabar Na Kar Sakay Woh Kiya Qalam Se Kam Hai.
Kitni Azeem Maa Hai Woh Jis Ka Hasan Hai Ladla.
Oss Maa Ki Pak Gaud Mein Jalwa Numa Imam Hai.
Dosh-e-Nabi Pe Hain Hasan Zulf-e-Nabi Hai Hath Mein.
Goya Hasan Ne Dehar Ka Thama Howa Nizam Hai.

2
Jaisay Ke Zulfiqar Ne Ulta Tha Baab-e-Khaibari.
Ulta Qalam Ki Nook Se Shabbar Ne Takht-e-Sham Hai.
Jis Pe Khuda Ko Naaz Hai Jo Hai Binaye LaIlaha.
Aisay Haseen Hussain Ka Maula Hasan Imam Hai.
Kyun Kar Na Khush Hoon Bazm Mein Rizwan-o-:QAISER-E-: Jawaan.
Inn Ashiqon Ke Hath Mein Ishq-e-Hasan Ka Jaam Hai.
