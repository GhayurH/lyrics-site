Zehra tujhay darbar may haq kiyun na mila hay

1
Shayed ke yehi ajr-e-risalat ka sila hay

2
1) Mehroom rahi haq se to ummat ki satai
Jhutlayi shareefon ne teri pak gawahi
Is ghum ne teray dil ka sakoon cheen liya hay

3
2) Ehsan faramoshon ne hay tujh ko rulaya
Aur baad nabi kyun saqeefay may bulaya
Choda so baras se humain yeh hi gilla hay

4
3) Darwaza teray pehlu pe zalim ne giraya
Kyun bint mohammad pay ussay reham na aaya
Kis jurm may zalim ne yehi zakhm dia hay

5
4) Matam teri aulaad ka din raat karain gay
Parhtay hain ager kalma to haq baat karain gay
Jannat teri aulaad ko ronay ka sila hay

6
5) Farman e risalat ko jo bhoolein hain woh zalim
Beshak woh namazi hain ya deen kay hain aalim
Un kay liye Sardar jahanum ki saza hay
