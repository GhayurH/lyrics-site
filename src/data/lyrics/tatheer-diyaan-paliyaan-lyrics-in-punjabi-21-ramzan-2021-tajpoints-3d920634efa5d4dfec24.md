Tatheer Diyaan Paliyaan Babay De Janazay Te Payiyaan Wain Pandiyaan Ne.
Jadon Maa Roway Na Babal Sanu Das Ja Baba Dhiyaan Kino Dukh Sunandiyaan Ne.

1
Zahra De Ghar Nu Lutiya Aa Wekh Musalmana.
Sajday Na Mariya Aye Kul-e-Eman Nana.
Assi Ujar Gaye Aan Nana Aj Bolda Nayi Baba Dhiyaan Bulandiyaan Ne.
Kita Aye Waar Qatil Jadon Natiq-e-Quraan Te.

2
Tareef Si Khuda Di Mere Babay Di Zabaan Te.
Aye Wain Pa Ke Bhaina Veraan Nu Chup Kara Ke Gal Naal Landiyaan Ne.
Zainab Nu Jad Sunaya Piyo Sham Wala Haal Aye.
Pochiya Si Ro Ke Zainab Babay Nu Aye Sawal Aye.

3
Jay Maa Batool Howay Kiwein Be Rida Oh Dhiyaan Bazar Jandiyaan Ne.
Qatil Diyaan Rasiyaan Nu Tu Kholna Aye Baba.
Aina Pa Ke Sanu Rasiyaan Haye Rolna Aye Baba.
Nazaan Diyaan Ne Paliyaan Babay De Sir Nu Chum Ke Ro Ro Ke Kehndiyaan Ne.

4
Haider De Baad Najfi Panjtan Da Ghar Veeran Aye.
Laya Taj Ohday Sir Taun Jehri Mustafa Di Jan Aye.
Athro Qabool Howan Syeda De Gham Che Jehray Akhiyaan Wasandiyaan Ne.
Noha Khuwan Party Ch Faiyaz Party Sialkot.
