Maqtal-e-Maula Imam Hussain 7 11 Mir Hasan Mir

1
Yeh Keh Ke Aagay Barga Hath Mein Liye Khanjar.
Udhar Tarapne Lagi Aik Lash Darya Par.
Idhar Khaiyam Se Nikali Batool Ki Dukhtar.
Ajeeb Be-Adabi Shimar Ne Ye Ki Aa Kar.
Aray Zara Si Bhi Na Marawat Dikhai Zalim Ne.
Kalam-e-Pak Ko Thokar Lagai Zalim Ne.

2
Ajab Tarha Se Sitamgar Yeh Kar Raha Tha Sitam.
Kabhi Hussain Ki Zulfon Mein Hath They Bahum.
Pakar Ke Reesh-e-Mubarak Kabhi Hansa Azlam.
Hataya Balon Ko Khanjar Se Shimar Ne Ik Dam.
Kaha Hussain Ne Dekho Zara Ali Akbar.
Laeen Ne Rakh Diya Zanu Hamare Seene Par.

3
Chali Jo Zarb Farishton Ne Munh Ko Phair Liya.
Farishtay Kya They Nabiyon Ne Munh Ko Phair Liya.
Aray Hatay Nabi Tu Rasoolon Ne Munh Ko Phair Liya.
Chalay Rasool Tu Waliyon Ne Munh Ko Phair Liye.
Hasan Rasool Ali Bolay Ab Khuda Jane.
Hussain Jane Gala Jane Fatima Jane.

4
Ab Iss Ke Aagay Takallum Biyan Kya Ho Ga.
Lagin Jo Zarbein Na Nikla Lahu Ka Ik Qatra.
Laeen Ghaiz Mein Aa Kar Sada Yeh Deney Laga.
Hussain Jaldi Bata Maujza Yeh Hai Kaisa.
Kaha Ghareeb Ne Tu Pyar Maa Ka Kya Jane.
Galay Pe Mere Gala Rakh Diya Hai Zahra Ne.

5
Kaha Hussain Ne Amma Hata Lein Apna Gala.
Mazeed Aap Hoon Zakhmi Yeh Ho Nahi Sakta.
Galay Pe Shah Ke Khanjar Laeen Ka Jo Chala.
Sadaein Aati Thin Subhana Rabbi Al Aala.
Pisar Ka Khon Baha Syeda Ki Gaudi Mein.
Sar-e-Hussain Kata Fatima Ki Gaudi Mein.
