Itni ghareeb ho gaye gham youn lipat gaye
Zainab ko ek libas mein do saal kat gaaye

1
1. Zainab ka dil bhi kya hai ghamoon ki kitab hai
Ek dil mein us ghareeb kay sou gham simmat gaaye

2
2. Kaisay utha Sakein gay bhala laash ko Hussain
Aazaa Hassan kay Lal kay tukdroon mein bat gaaye

3
3. Hathon say Maa kay khaimay ka perda ullat gaya
Hathon pay Sheh kay jis gharri Asghar pallat gaaye

4
4. Kya kya guzar chuke hai Muharram kay chand mein
Saydaniyoon kay bakht achanak ullat gaaye

5
5. Boolay Hussain Bayti say pani na mangna
Jin bazooyoon pay mashq thi woh bazoo kat gaaye

6
6. Marnay chalay Hussain to rahwaar ruk gaya
Do nanhay nanhay hath samou say lipat gaaye
