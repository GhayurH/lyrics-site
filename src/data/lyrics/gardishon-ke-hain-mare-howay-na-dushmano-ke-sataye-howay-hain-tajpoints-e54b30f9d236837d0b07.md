1
Jitne Bhi Zakhm Hain Mere Dill Par Doston Ke Lagaye Howay Hain.
Jab Se Dekha Tera Qad-o-Qamat Dill Pe Toti Hai Wo Qayamat.
Har Bala Se Rahe Tu Salamat Din Jawani Ke Aye Howay Hain.
Our De Mujh Ko De Our Saqi Hosh Rehta Hai Thora Sa Baqi.
Aaj Talkhi Bhi Hai Inteha Ki Aaj Wo Bhi Paraye Howay Hain.
Kal The Aabad Pehlu Mein Mere Aab Hain Ghairon Ki Mehfil Mein Dehray.
Meri Mehfil Mein Kar Ke Andehray Apni Mehfil Sajaye Howay Hain.
Apne Hathon Se Khanjar Chala Kar Kitna Masoom Chehrah Bana Kar.
Apne Kandhon Pe Aab Mere Qatil Meri Maiyat Uthaye Howay Hain.
Mehwashon Ko Wafa Se Kya Matlab Inn Butoon Ko Khuda Se Kya Matlab.
Inn Ki Masoom Nazroon Ne Nasir Loag Pagal Banaye Howay Hain.
