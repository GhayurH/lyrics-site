Mola raza pay hai kyon zulm o sitam howay hain

Ghurbat ki zindagi main aaqa sada jiyay hain

Mamoon nay diya hai zehar-e-daga raza ko

Qatal-e-imam kay sab saaman kar diyay hain

Aangoor-e-zahar kha kar haalat main tagyi yur hai

Lagta hai jaisay inn kay qalb o jigar katay hain

Uthi hai siyah aandhi mashad main madinay main

Gham kay pahar hain jo saadaat par giray hain

Larzaaan’ hai aasmaan’ bhi hai zalzala zameen’ ko

Jin o malak main matam kay shoor woo uthay hain

Bhai say milnay pohanchin masooma-e-qum jis dam

Thama jigar yeh sun kay bhayya nahi rahay hain

Jin ki zamanatoon main hain moomeneen-e-aalam

Hain yeh wahi raza jo dunya say ja rahay hain

Mashhad bula layn mola ashhar kay gharanay ko

Arman-e-ziarat yeh dil main liyay howay hain
