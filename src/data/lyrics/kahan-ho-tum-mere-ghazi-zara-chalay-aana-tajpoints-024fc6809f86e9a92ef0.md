1
Main Qaid Ho Ke Chali Our Watan Hai Begana.
Banaye Parda Mera Kon Ab Siwa Tere.
Na Sar Pe saya Tera Na Rahi Rida Mere.
Rida Tu Ban Ke Mere Sar Pe Ghazi Cha Jana.

2
Jo Ghar Se Nikli Na Waqif Na Thi Bazaron Ki.
Hain Har So Bikhari Hoi Lashein Thin Pyaron Ki.
Isse Pehan Ke Hain Zanjeer Sham Ko Jana.
Tamache Mar Ke Zalim Humein Rulate Hain.

3
Meri sakina Ke Kano Se Khoon Bahate Hain.
Lagi Hai Aag Jo Daman Mein Woh Bhuja Jana.
Aa Dekh Hath Bandhe Hain Mere Pas-e-Gardan.
Pehan Ke Baqir-o-Sajjad Dekho Tauq-o-Rasan.

4
Chali Hoon Aoun-o-Muhammad Ka De Ke Nazrana.
Misal Ban Gayi Dunya Mein Aaj Teri Wafa.
Zamana Rota Hai Kar Kar Ke Yaad Teri Wafa.
Har Aik Lab Pe Alamdar Tera Afsana.
