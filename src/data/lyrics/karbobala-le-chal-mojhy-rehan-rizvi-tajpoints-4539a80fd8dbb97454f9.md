1
Karbobala Le Chal Mojhy Rehan Rizvi.
Doosh Par Bad-e-Saba Karb-o-Bala Le Chal Mujhe.
Jane Kab Yeh Sans De Jaye Daga Le Chal Mujhe.
Jeetay Jee Main Dekh Loon Roza-e-Shabir Ko.
Our Badaltay Dekh Loon Aankhon Se Main Taqdeer Ko.
Chom Kar Chokhat Karon Hadiya Dil-e-Dilgeer Ko.
Dar Se Mil Jaye Shifat Ki Jaza Le Chal Mujhe.
Maula Ghazi Se Wahan Dil Ki Muradein Paon Ga.
Our Jahan Mein Zair-e-:Sibt-e-Nabi Kehlaon Ga.
Apni Turbat Ke Liye Khak-e-Shifa Le Aaon Ga.
Karbala Jane Mein Hai Meri Baqa Le Chal Mujhe.
Khoob Matam Ho Wahan, Khoob Giriya Ho Wahan.
Sab Biyaan Kartay Chalein Baino Al Haram Yeh Dastaan.
Matami Sar Peettay Hoon Ashk Aankhon Se Rawaan.
Ho Aza-e-Sheh Ka Haq Kuch Tu Ada Le Chal Mujhe.
Akbar-e-Kariyal Jawaan Jab Gira Kha Kar Sina.
Khon Mein Galtaan Pisar Ko Khaimay Se Takti Thi Maa.
Sun Ke Betay Ki Sada Dasht Mein Giriya Kuna.
Ghutniyon Chal Kar Mera Maula Gaya Le Chal Mujhe.
Qasim-o-Aoun-o-Muhammad Jis Jagah Marey Gaye.
Our Qalam Bazu Hoye Jis Ja Mere Abbas Ke.
Zibha Hathoon Par Howa Tha Sheh Ke Asghar Teer Se.
Khak Ura Kar Main Karon Matam Bapa Le Chal Mujhe.
Maqtal-e-Khonnab Mein Haye Tanha Reh Gaya.
Har Taraf Se Teer-o-Khanjar Our Lakht-e-Syeda.
Khon Se Jis Ke Hoi Karb-o-Bala Khak-e-Shifa.
Pusht-e-Gardan Se Kata Jis Ja Gala Le Chal Mujhe.
Haye Woh Sham-e-Ghareebaan Haye Woh Be Warsi.
Khak Par Giriya Kuna Thi Dasht Mein Aal-e-Nabi.
Dur Sakina Ke Chiney Aag Daman Mein Lagi.
Gonjti Thi Wa Hussaina Ki Sada Le Chal Mujhe.
Kis Tarha Likhon Najam Be Basi Sajjad Ki.
Narga-e-Agyar Mein Thi Sar Khulay Bint-e-Ali.
Mehway Gham Ahl-e-Azza They Ro Diya Rehan Bhi.
Lab Pe Oss Ke Thi Sada Behar-e-Khuda Le Chal Mujhe.
