1
Allah Allah Min Rasil Hussain Nadeem Sarwar.
Rasil Hussain Dil-e-Jahan.
Salam Bar Muharram.
Salam Bar Karbala.

2
Salam Bar Ashora.
Salam Bar Hussain.
Aye Naiza Sawaron Ki Qayadat Ke Shehanshah.
Zulfein Teri Wal Lail Hain Washams Hai Chehra.

3
Aankhon Se Ayyaan Noor-e-Muhammad Ka Ujala.
Lab Tere Hain Masroof-e-Tilawat Bin-e-Zahra.
Sajday Mein Jhuka Sar Tu Ibadat Ka Sharf Tha.
Naizay Pe Utha Sar Tu Yeh Sar Dur-e-Najaf Tha.

4
Thi Pusht-e-Muhammad Tu Issi Sar Ka Musalla.
Our Chadar-e-Tatheer Hai Iss Sar Ka Amama.
Man Kuntu Ka Khutba Hai Issi Sar Ka Khulasa.
Iss Sar Ke Sabab Karb-o-Bala Ab Hai Mualla.

5
Yeh Sar Na Jhuka Khanjar-o-Talwar Ke Aagay.
Sar Zulm Ka Khum Hai Tere Inkar Ke Aagay.
Roh-e-Hussain, Jabeen-e-Hussain, Abroye Hussain.
Chashm-e-Hussain, Rukhsar-e-Hussain, Dahan-e-Hussain.

6
Lab-e-Hussain, Dandan-e-Hussain, Rukhsar-e-Hussain.
Reesh-e-Hussain, Gul-e-Hussain, Zulf-e-Hussain.
Khushbo-e-Hussain, Zehan-e-Hussain.
Fikar-e-Hussain, Guftar-e-Hussain.

7
Inn Aankhon Ko Hai Ahmad Mukhtar Ne Choma.
Zahra-o-Ali Ne Lab-o-Rukhsar Ko Choma.
Zulfoon Ko Hasan Ne Hai Barey Pyar Se Choma.
Behno Ne Galla Iss Ka Kayi Bar Hai Choma.

8
Sardari-e-Abbas Issi Sar Se Juri Hai.
Our Zainab-o-Kulsoom Ko Yeh Misal-e-Ali Hai.
Mojood Tha Iss Sar Mein Muhammad Ka Khazana.
Maqsad Tha Issi Sar Ka Tu Islam Bachana.

9
Zalim Ne Bohat Chaha Tha Iss Sar Ko Jhokana.
Yeh Sar Na Jhuka Kat Gaya Zahra Ka Gharana.
Iss Sar Ka Safar Sun Ke Jahan Rota Hai Ab Tak.
Ghaibat Mein Jo Hai Marsiya Khuwano Ka Hai Ab Tak.

10
Ashor Ko Yeh Sar Pas-e-Gardan Se Kata Tha.
Zarbain Thin Lagin Terah Bohat Khushk Galla Tha.
Zainab Ke Liye Waqt Qayamat Se Kara Tha.
Zulfoon Ko Pakar Kar Jo Yeh Sar Shimar Chala Tha.

11
Afsoos Sar-e-Nook-e-Sina Jab Yeh Sar Aaya.
Jalta Howa Ghar Sham-e-Ghareebaan Ka Tha Saya.
Iss Sar Ke Musayab Ka Yeh Pur Dard Hai Qissa.
Kholi Ne Issay Ghar Mein Jo Tandoor Mein Rakha.

12
Raat Aayi Tu Phir Zoja-e-Kholi Ne Yeh Dekha.
Tandoor Se Ik Noor Ka Darya Hai Ubalta.
Ik Bibi Siyah Posh Ne Iss Sar Ko Uthaya.
Ro Ro Ke Kaha Haye Ghareeb-e-Bin-e-Zahra.

13
Rahib Ne Yeh Sar Dekha Tha Jab Nook-e-Sina Par.
Kholi Se Kaha Kaun Hai Yeh Kis Ka Hai Yeh Sar.
Bola Woh Bad Atwar Ke Yeh Hai Bin-e-Haider.
Rahib Ne Woh Sar Le Liya Kholi Ko Diya Zar.

14
Sar Gaud Mein Rakhay Hoye Rota Raha Rahib.
Aawaz-e-Fogaan Zahra Ki Sunta Raha Rahib.
Yeh Sar Kabhi Sandooq Mein Tandoor Mein Rakha.
Iss Sar Ko Darakhton Pe Laeeno Ne Sajaya.

15
Naizay Pe Issay Shehar Ki Galiyon Mein Phiraya.
Humrah Aseeron Ke Yeh Sar Sham Jab Aaya.
Sajjad Ko Zainab Ko Rolatay They Sitamgar.
Sar Bali Sakina Ko Dikhatay They Sitamgar.

16
Darbar-e-Yazeedi Mein Bapa Jashan-e-Karb Tha.
They Aik Taraf Ahl-e-Haram Muztar-o-Giriya.
Our Samne Tha Takht Pe Marjana Ka Beta.
Oss Takht Talay Sar Tha Hussain-e-Be Khata Ka.

17
Badmast Laeen Nashay Mein Lalkar Raha Tha.
Dandan-e-Sheh-e-Dein Pe Chari Mar Raha Tha.
Jis Waqt Yeh Sar Sham Ke Zindan Mein Aaya.
Sar Bhai Ka Zainab Ne Kalejay Se Lagaya.

18
Sajjad Ne Be Hosh Sakina Ko Jagaya.
Sar Baba Ka Pehlu Mein Sakina Ne Jo Paya.
Aye:SARWAR-o-REHAN: Qayamat Ka Samaa Tha.
Shabeer Ka Sar Rota Tha Our Shoor-o-Fogaan Tha.
