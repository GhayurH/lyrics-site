1
Mehndiyaan Wale Nu Dardaan Di Mari Maa Wich Khaimiyan De Samjhaway Asghar Khan
Meri Marzi Aye Qasim Bachra Aj Piyo De Farz Nibhaway.
Ban Wich Hussain Da Aj Ainj Maan Taun Wadhawin.
Rahwaan Na Tukray Ho Ke Mera Lal Wichda Jawein.
Phul Sehrey De Khilray Howan Jadon Piyo Kubra Da Aaway.
Zainab De Dovein Bachray Laila Da Sohnra Akbar.
Ainj Jang Karein Tu Qasim Jiwein Jang Karey Ga Asghar.
Aye Yaad Rakhein Teri Amari Nu Kisay Maa Taun Sharam Na Aaway.
Raitaan Te Pathraan Te Teraan Te Neziyaan Te.
Summa Che Ghoriyaan De Sehra De Zarriyan Te.
Mera Jee Karda Aye Teri Mehndi Da Rang Karbal Te Charh Jaway.
Jadon Tere Samne Aj Foaj-e-Laeen Howay.
Ainj Da Jehad Karna E Tainu Wekh Maut Roway.
Tainu Aj Doja Ghazi Keh Ke Mera Veer Hussain Bulaway.
 Akbar Jadon Vein Gathri Farwa Ne Kholi Hosi.
Aawaz Tukriyaan Choon Qasim Di Aayi Hosi.
Maa Razi Ayein Ya Har Tukra Fair Laran Nu Maqtal Jaway.
