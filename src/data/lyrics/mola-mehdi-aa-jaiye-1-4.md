Mola Mehdi Aa Jaiye 1-4

1
Muntazir Kab Talak Main Rahon Aye Imam
Meri Sansein Kahin Ho Na Jaein Tamam

2
Deedar Ki Aakhri Aas Mein
Khat Likh Raha Hai Yeh Khadam Tumhein

3
Kijiye Qabool Aaqa Mera Salam
Bas Ik Azadar Hai Mera Naam
Qabil Nahi Janta Hoon Magar
Kehta Hoon Khud Ko Tumhara Ghulam
Kya Hoon Mujhe Khud Yeh Batlaiye
Faryadein Our Istegase Hazoor
Sarkar Sunte Tu Hoon Gaye Zaror
Be-Chain Khud Bhi Hain Ghaibat Mein Aap
Kab Ho Ga Mula Bata Dein Zahoor
Hum Ko Na Ab Our Tarpaiye
Be-Noor Kab Se Mere Nain Hain
Tanha Azadar-e-Behrain Hain
Kaba Ho Ya Ke Ho Karb-o-Bala
Sab Aap Ke Gham Mein Be-Chain Hain
Sarkar Tashreef Le Aaiye
Lafzon Se Pardah Hatati Nahi
Apna Taruf Karati Nahi
Jab Se Gaye Aap Khamosh Hai
Aayat Maani Batati Nahi
Raz-e-Alif Lam Samjahiye
