1
Aye Mere Ghareeb Hussain.
Banday Kafan Ko Toor Ke Aayi Hai Phir Se Maa.
Mathay Pe Teer Pehlu Mein Paiwast Barchiyaan Our Qalab Mein Sina.
Marham Lagaye Gi Teri Madar Kahan Kahan.

2
Bahein Galay Mein Daal Lo Himat Zara Karo Aye Lal Ghar Chalo.
Inn Pathron Ke Dhair Mein Tum Chup Gaye Kahan.
Rakh Doon Gi Hath Gardan-o-Khanjar Ke Darmiyaan Wadah Hai Meri Jaan.
Ghazi Ki Tarha Dasht Mein Qurbaan Ho Gi Maa.

3
Kariyal Jawaan Ki Lash Pe Tera Tha Imtehaan Tiyar Ab Hai Maa.
Beta Main Tere Seene Se Khainchoo Gi Khud Sina.
Mohsin Ke Bad Se Mere Jaise Jhuki Kamar Tujh Ko Tu Hai Khabar.
Aise Hai Bad Ghazi Ke Teri Kamar Kamaan.

4
Betay Ke Sath Dasht Mein Madar Ka Khoon Bahe Keh Do Yeh Shimar Se.
Gardan Pe Pehle Woh Kare Khanjar Mere Rawaan.
Amma Hata Lo Hath Takallum Thi Yeh Sada Katne Do Ab Gala.
Har Zarb Par Yeh Kehti Thi Pehlu Shikasta Maa.
