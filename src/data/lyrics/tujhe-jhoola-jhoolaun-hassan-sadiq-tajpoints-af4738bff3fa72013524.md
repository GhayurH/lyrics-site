1
Tujhe Jhoola Jhoolaun Hassan Sadiq.
Main Jhola Jholaon Main Tujhey Jhola Jholaon.
Main Khuwab Mein Asghar Tujhey Lori Bhi Sunaon.
Laut Aao Safar Se Keh Bari Dair Hoi Hai.
Betab Hoon Kab Se Tujhey Seene Se Lagaon.
Koi Mujhe Batlata Nahi Teri Nishani.
Barsat Mein Teeron Ki Kahan Dhondne Jaon.
Socha Tha Teri Salgirah Ghar Mein Karon Gi.
Taqdeer Yeh Kehti Hai Tera Soag Manaon.
Baba Se Tere Tera Janaza Na Uthay Ga.
Ruk Ja Zara Abbas Ko Darya Se Bulaon.
Yeh Khon Bhara Kurta Yeh Mati Mein Bharay Baal.
Seene Mein Chupaon Keh Main Aankhon Se Lagaon.
Iss Tarha Koi Roth Ke Maa Se Nahi Jata.
Tu Khud Hi Bata De Main Tujhey Kaisay Manaon.
 Rehan Ke Nohay Pe Sada Bano Ki Aayi.
Main Aankhon Se Ashkon Ke Samandar Ko Bahaon.
