Nok e Sina Pe Kehta Tha Ye Sar Hussain Ka Hasan Sadiq.

1
Ghar Ghar Mein Hoga Dekhna Matam Hussain Ka.
Kya Kar Saka Laeen Tera Khanjar Hussain Ka.
Aankhon Ka Noor Qalb Ki Taqat Chali Gayi.
Ghorey Se Jab Gira Ali Akbar Hussain Ka.
Zahra Ne Apne Hath Rakhay Taig Ke Talay.
Sar Katne Laga Jo Sitamgar Hussain Ka.

2
Yeh Imtehan-e-Sabar Tha Warna Aye Shamiyo.
Bhari Tha Pori Foaj Pe Asghar Hussain Ka.
Kuch Borhay Kuch Jawan Our Aik Sheer Khawar.
Lakhon Ke Samne Tha Yeh Lashkar Hussain Ka.
 Rehan Mujh Ko Dekh Kar Janat Pukar Uthi.
Aane Do Aa Raha Hai Yeh Nokar Hussain Ka.
