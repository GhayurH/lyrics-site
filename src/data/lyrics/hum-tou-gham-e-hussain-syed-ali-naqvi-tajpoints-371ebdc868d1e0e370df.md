1
Hum Tou Gham e Hussain Syed Ali Naqvi.
Hum Tu Gham-e-Hussain Manaein Gay Raat Din.
Our Zikar Karbala Ka Sunaein Gay Raat Din.
Tum Jis Tarha Bhi Chaho Humein Roktay Raho.
Hum Tu Azza Ka Farsh Bichaein Gay Raat Din.
Qatal-e-Hussain Asal Mein Marg e Yazid Hai.
Narah Yeh Haq Ka Hum Tu Lagaein Gay Raat Din.
Abbas Ne Diya Hai Jo Dars-e-Wafa Humein.
Hum Woh Wafa Ki Rasam Nibhaein Gay Raat Din.
Kitni Thi Dard Naak Sakina Ki Be Basi.
Iss Gham Mein Aansoon Ko Bahaein Gay Raat Din.
Sughra Ki Thi Fogaan Mere Baba Ab Aap Ki.
Furqat Mein Hum Bhi Chain Na Paein Gay Raat Din.
Beemar e Karbala Teri Har Be Kasi Ka Hum.
Kar Kar Ke Zikar Sab Ko Rulaein Gay Raat Din.
Akbar Ke Our Aoun-o-Muhammad Ke Bhi Sadmay.
Zainab Ki Na Tawani Barhaein Gay Raat Din.
Shashmaha Pyasa Teer-e-Sitam Se Howa Shaheed.
Yeh Dastan-e-Zulm Bataein Gay Raat Din.
Cheeni Hai Jis Ne Zainab-e-Dilgeer Ki Chadar.
Putla Bana Ke Oss Ka Jalein Gay Raat Din.
Lanat Yazid Our Ho Shimar e Laeen Par.
Hum Apna Wird Iss Ko Banaein Gay Raat Din.
Ahmad Ki Nawasi Hi Tu Hain Umm Ul-Mosayab.
Yeh Taziyana Ummati Khaein Gay Raat Din.
 Zahid Hussainiyaat Ke Jo Dushman Jahan Mein Hain.
Inn Sab Yazidiyon Ko Mitaein Gay Raat Din.
