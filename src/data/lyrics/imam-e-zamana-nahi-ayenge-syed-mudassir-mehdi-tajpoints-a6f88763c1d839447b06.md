1
Imam e Zamana Nahi Ayenge Syed Mudassir Mehdi.
Rab Ka Munkir Hai Jo Koi Bhi Keh Raha Hai Imam-e-Zamana Nahi Aaein Gay.
Reh Raha Hai Woh Dhokay Mein Jis Ko Laga Hai Imam-e-Zamana Nahi Aaein Gay.
Mimber-e-Mustafa Par Jo Qabiz Howa Our Deen-o-Shariyat Badalney Laga.

2
Fatway Sadar Kiye Zikar-e-Shabeer Par Our Wilayat Ko Hai Mustahib Keh Raha.
Molvi Ko Yeh Shayad Yaqeen Ho Chala Hai Imam-e-Zamana Nahi Aaein Gay.
Hum Pe Wajib Itaat Hai Mehdi Ki Par Iss Qadar Unn Se Hum Loag Hain Be Khabar.
Dil Mein Khuwaish Bhi Hai Unn Ki Nusrat Karein Daway Ulfat Ke Bhi Lab Pe Sham-o-Sahar.

3
Kyun Amal Se Hamarey Magar Lag Raha Hai Imam-e-Zamana Nahi Aaein Gay.
Yeh Nuseri Muqassar Ke Jhagrey Sabhi Khums Khori Ho Ya Shoq-e-Fitna Giri.
Hum Wilayat Wilayat Tu Kartay Hain Par Sochiye Hum Se Khush Hain Kya Haq Ke Wali.
Jab Talak Apna Mahool Kofa Numa Hai Imam-e-Zamana Nahi Aaein Gay.

4
Zulm Jitna Bhi Karlo Tum Aye Zalimo Aane Wala Hai Woh Waris-e-Karbala.
Jis Ke Bazu Mein Qowat Hai Karrar Ki Jis Ke Chehray Pe Hai Noor-e-Saley Ala.
Sochna Yeh Tumhara Tu Ain-e-Khata Hai Imam-e-Zamana Nahi Aaein Gay.
Ahl-e-Khaybar They Jis Tarha Se Mutmain Foaj-e-Islam Se Jang Hogi Nahi.

5
Jab Talak Na Ali Aaye Maidan Mein Woh Yeh Samjhay They Mard Unn Mein Koi Nahi.
Marhab-e-Waqt Ko Bhi Yeh Dhoka Howa Hai Imam-e-Zamana Nahi Aaein Gay.
Ohad Mein Jaisay Iblees Ne Di Sada Bhago Bhago Keh Ab Na Rahe Mustafa.
Teen Din Baad Sheikh Apne Ghar Aaye They Aisay Maidan Se They Hoye La Pata.

6
Aaj Bhi Shar Yeh Iblees Phaila Raha Hai Imam-e-Zamana Nahi Aaein Gay.
Aye Khuda Sun Le Mukhtar Ki Iltija Karday Qayam Ko Izn-e-Zahoor Ab Atta.
Door Hai Pur Fotan, Zulmatein Hain Bohat Our Aqeeda Banana Hai Mushkil Bara.
Ghair Tu Ghair Apno Ko Lagne Laga Hai Imam-e-Zamana Nahi Aaein Gay.
