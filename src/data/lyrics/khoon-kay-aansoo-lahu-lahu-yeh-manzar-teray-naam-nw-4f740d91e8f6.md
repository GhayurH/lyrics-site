Khoon Kay Aansoo, Lahu Lahu Yeh Manzar Teray Naam.

Tapti Raiti, Bay Saar Lashay

Sarwar Teray Naam.

Chala Abu Talib Ka Beta

Aa`in Dar-e-Rab

Arsh Nabi Per Sonay Walay

Khaybar Teray Naam.

Hass Kar Boli Binte Asad Sai

Kaabay Ki Diwaar

Sher Ki Dukhtar, Shear Ki Maadar

Yeh Ghaar Teray Naam.

Teri Ghulami Rashke Ameeri

Waah Teri Taqdeer

Aal Ki Khidmat Deen Ki Nusrat

Qambar Teray Naam.

Ghurbat Main Jaan Day Di Laikin

Daman Aal Na Choro

Ap Ki Rifaaqat Fakr Ki Daulat

Abu Zar Teray Naam.

Kaysee Chain Ki Neend Ayi Hain

Talwaro’ Kay Neechay

Sonay Walay Aaj Nabhi Ka

Bistar Teray Naam.

Sehray Ka Arman Hain Dil Mein

Maa Ko Kya Maloom

Maut Javani Me Likhdi Hain

Akbar Teray Naam.

Sar Nangay Ay Janay Wali

Bazaar-e-Kufa Mein

Qudrat Ne Tatheer Ki Likhdi

Chadar Teray Naam.

Chand Sa Chehra Khoon Mai Malta

Baap Ke Bazoon Zakhmi

Pyaas Ki Shiddat, Maut Ki Lazzat

Asghar Teray Naam.
