Rakhe Mabood Ne Bakhshish Ke Bahane Kitne Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.
Naat Le Jaye Gi Janat Mein Na Jane Kitne.

1
Jhar Dein Syed-e-Lolaak Tu Pa Jaein Wajood.
Gard-e-Nalain-e-Payamber Se Zamane Kitne.
La Na Paya Koi Bhi Sorah-e-Kausar Ka Jawab.
Ahl-e-Maka Ne Gharay Jhotay Fasaney Kitne.

2
Aap Jholay Mein Hain Jibraeel Zarra Gin Ke Bata.
Painti Kitne Payamber Hain Sirhaney Kitne.
Murtaza Hain Shab-e-Hijrat Ka Haseen Bistar Hai.
Khuwab Dekhein Gay Ali Aaj Sohane Kitne.

3
Paal De Mera Nabi, Naat Sikha De Sab Ko.
Kam Sonpne Abu Talib Ko Khuda Ne Kitne.
Yeh Tu Bas Gunbad-e-Khizra Ke Satoon Jantay Hain.
Deep Aa Aa Ke Jalaye Hain Hawa Ne Kitne.

4
Ya Elahi Yeh Bata Loag Nabi Ke Ashiq.
Kitne Kabay Ne Kiye Karb-o-Bala Ne Kitne.
Zikar-e-Sarkar-e-Do Aalam Ke Karam Se Akbar.
Moatbar Ho Gaye Na Cheez Gharanay Kitne.
