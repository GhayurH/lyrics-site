Qaidi bani khari hai Karbal mein Zahra sani
Bhulegi kaise Zainab, baad az Nabi jo ki hai ummat ne meharbani

1
Zahra bhi dua detin, Maula se ajr milta
Is kaam ke sadqe mein Firdaus mein ghar milta
Karbal mein Musalmano, do ghunt pila dete Asghar ko agar paani

2
Paaband-e-rasan Zainab, chaadar bhi nahi sar pe
Tuti hai qayaamat wo Zahra ke bhare ghar pe
Baazu bandhe hue hain Haider ki betiyon ke, hai Shaam ko rawaani

3
Karbal na gayi Bibi, baithi hai Madine mein
Haaye barchhi lagi kaise tere bhaai ke sine mein
Mar jaayegi Sughra tu, Zainab ne jab sunaayi Akbar ki ye kahaani

4
Qaidi bimaar Sayyid, aankhoṅ se lahu jaari
Zainab ki asiri pe roya hai raat saari
Bhula na marte dam tak, kar kar ke yaad roya Akbar ki naujawaani

5
Maatam namaaz teri, Qaaim namaaz kar lo
Zahra ki duaaon se daaman ko apne bhar lo
Maula ka zikr karna, Zainab ko rona Akhtar momin ki hai nishaani
