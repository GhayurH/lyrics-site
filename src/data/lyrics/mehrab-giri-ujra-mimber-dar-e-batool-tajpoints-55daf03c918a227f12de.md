Mehrab Giri Ujra Mimber Dar-e-Batool.

1
Mehrab Giri Ujra Mimber Jibreel Sadaein Dete Hain.
Sajday Mein Howay Zakhmi Haider Jibreel Sadaein Dete Hain.
Wo Taig Shaqi Ne Mari Hai JO Zehar Mein DObi Sari Hai.
Jaye Ga Zehar Ye Jaan Le Kar Jibreel Sadaein Dete Hain.
Shabar Ne Ammama Phaink Diya Sarar Ne Abba Ko Chaak Kiya.
Zainab Ki Giri Sar Se Chadar Jibreel Sadaein Dete Hain.

2
Logo Na Yahan Se Agay Barho Dill Tham Lo Dar Se Door Ruko.
Ye Girya-e-Zainab Ko Sun Kar Jibreel Sadaein Dete Hain.
Zainab Tere Sar Se Saya Utha Aab Thaam Rida Bazo Ko Chupa.
Haye Dar-e-Zainab Par Aa Kar Jibreel Sadaein Dete Hain.
Ikees Ka Din Nikla Jo Naveed Haider Se Yeh Dunya Khali Thi.
Rukhsat Howa Zahra Ka Shohar Jibreel Sadaein Dete Hain.
