jab maqtal ko mazloom chala
zainab ne lagaam ko thaam liya
tujhe jaane doon dil nahi karta
bin tere jiyun dil nahi karta
maula ne kaha mujhe jaana hai
mujhe waada behan nibhana hai
na lashkar na ghar baar raha
tu ujad gayi main ujad gaya
haan nai lagda haan dil mera
mera koi raha na ghamkhwar
zainab rok na tu rahwar

zainab rok na tu rahwar
haan mera lashkar raha na salaar
zainab rok na tu rahwar
aao behna zainab aao
mere seene se lag jao
beti ali ki na ghabrao
aao meri aas bandhao
na hoga haan ab milna
haan mujhe dekhle aakhri baar
zainab rok na..
haan mera lashkar raha na salaar
zainab rok na tu rahwar

mai hoon aur meri tanhaai

mai tanha haan mai pyasa

zainab rok na..

baad mere masoom sakina
yaad karegi mera seena
mujhse milegi ab wo kabhi na
mushkil hoga us ka jeena
meri pyari haan meri beti
haan mere seene pe soyi har baar
zainab rok na..
