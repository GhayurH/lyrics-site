Na Ho Kyun Kar Afzal Hamara Muhammad.
Keh Hai Apne Pyare Ka Pyara Muhammad.

1
Elahi Yeh Mehshar Mein Hum Kehte Jaein.
Kahan Hai Kahan Hai Hamara Muhammad.
Wahin Kashti-e-Nooh Bhi Doob Jati.
Na Dete Jo Oss Ko Sahara Muhammad.

2
Abhi Farsh Se Arsh Mil Jaye Jhuk Kar.
Karein Gar Talab Ka Ishara Muhammad.
Yehi Baat Aashiq Ne Mashooq Se Ki.
Nahi Teri Grift Gawara Muhammad.

3
Kahein Gay Bhi Iss Sheh-e-Anbiya Se.
Wahan Hoon Ge Jab Aashkara Muhammad.
Shafiye Umam Roz-e-Mehshar Tum Hi Hoo.
Humein Hai Tumhara Sahra Muhammad.

4
Sada Khair Muqadam Ki Kabay Se Ayi.
Haram Se Jab Aye Dobara Muhammad.
Bula Lo Madine Mein Phir Daag Ko Tum.
Nahi Hind Mein Aab Guzara Muhammad.
