Sajjad Kare Wain Sir Wekh Ke Mawaan De.
Bazar Ainu Langney Paye Gaye Baaj Ridawaan De.

1
Ghazi De Sir Nu Tak Ke Syeda Kaindi Rayi.
Muk Maan Gaye Ghazi Veerna Teriyaan Banwaan De.
Zainab Te Wasday Pathar Shamiyaan Rokay Na.
Oh Shamiyaan Nu Dasdi Reh Gayi Naam Bhirawaan De.

2
Deena Aye Pehla Khutba Veer Main Sham Da.
Shabeer Mainu Babay Di Jagah Veer Salawaan De.
Kuj Neziyaan Te Chadraan Ho Gayiyaan Humsafar.
Kujh Neziyaan Te Chalday Sir Ray Naal Bhirawaan De.

3
Puchdi Rayi Sakina Rondiyaan Veer Nu.
Bhul Veer Gaye Kalma Go Kiwein Rutbay Shahwaan De.
Khutbay Sunaye Zainab Te Bhira Aayataan.
 Saqlain Ewein Jari Wain Ne Bhain Bhirawaan De.
