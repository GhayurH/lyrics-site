Mustafa Jane Rehmat Pe Lakhon Salam Shahid Baltistani.

1
Shama-e-Bazm-e-Hidayat Pe Lakhon Salam.
Woh Madina Jahan Adal Qayam Howa.
Mustafa Ki Riyasat Pe Lakhon Salam.
Zulashera Mein Jhuk Kar Yeh Bolay Nabi.
Aye Chacha Teri Haibat Pe Lakhon Salam.
Jis Ke Tukron Pe Islam Palta Raha.

2
Oss Khadija Ki Dolat Pe Lakhon Salam.
Jis Ke Had Se Talu e Imamat Hoi.
Ikhtitam e Nabowat Pe Lakhon Salam.
Kar Ke Faqay Farishton Mein Bantti Giza.
Panjtan Ki Sakhawat Pe Lakhon Salam.
Baad Mere Yateemo Ka Rakhna Khiyal.

3
Murtaza Ki Wasiyat Pe Lakhon Salam.
Tujh Ko Rab Ke Muhammad Ne Madar Kaha.
Fatima Teri Ismat Pe Lakhon Salam.
Aik Pyasay Ne Nook-e-Sina Par Jo Ki.
Aisi Zindah Tilawat Pe Lakhon Salam.
Le Ke Qabzey Mein Darya Tu Pyasa Raha.

4
Aye Sakhi Teri Ghairat Pe Lakhon Salam.
Jo Daleel-e-Wajood-e-Khuda Ban Gayi.
Maula Mehdi Ki Ghaibat Pe Lakhon Salam.
Jaisi Salman-o-Fizza Ne Darwaish Ki.
Ishq Ki Oss Riyazat Pe Lakhon Salam.
