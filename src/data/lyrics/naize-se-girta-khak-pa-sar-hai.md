NAIZE SE GIRTA KHAK PA SAR HAI
ABBAS KA YE KAISA SAFAR HAI

1
AGE HAIN SAJJAD BEDI PAHAN KAR
PICHE BEPARDA AALE PAYAMBAR
JABSE YE MANZAR HADDE NAZAR HAI

2
SATH SAFAR ME LASHKARE QATIL
AHLE HARAM QAID DOOR HAI MANZIL
SHAM KI KAISI YE REHGUZAR HAI

3
ARSHE WAFA PAR JISKI KHUDAI
ZERE QADAM HAI JISKE TARAI
SHERE KHUDA KA LAKHTE JIGAR HAI

4
ISHQO WAFA KE MADRASE JISKE
ILMO HUNAR KE MAIQADE JISKE
HAAN WO HI SAQQA KHOON ME TAR

5
KAATE GAYE HAIN HATH JO ISKE
NAHAR KINARE LAGTA HAI AISE
JAISE FARISHTA BE BALO PAR HAI

6
NOKE SINA PAR JO NA RUKA SAR
GHODE KI GARDEN ME WO BANDHA SAR
KAISI QAYAMAT KI YE KHABAR HAI

7
NANHE SE BACHCHE PUCHTE SARE
KAH DO KAHA HAIN AMMU HAMARE
BACHCHON KE DIL ME SHIMR KA DAR HAI

8
DHOOP ME SAYA DHOONDH RAHE HAIN
TOOTI SAANSE JOD RAHE HAIN
KYA KAHE ZAINAB DEEDAE TAR HAI

9
KARBOBALA SE SHAHRE SITAM TAK
JANA HAI YU HI DARDO ALAM TAK
KAISA NA JANE AZME SAFAR HAI

10
AHLE HARAM PAR BARSE JO PATHTHAR
KAISA WO GUZRA WAQT JARI PAR
ANKHO SE TAPKA KHOONE JIGAR HAI

11
KAHTI HAI ZAINAB DEKH LO BHAYYA
FAZLE KHUDA SE HO GAYA PARDA
MAATHE PE MERE GARDE SAFAR HAI

12
KAISE RAQAM HO DARD WAFA SE
HARF BAYAA’N HO KAISE SHUJA SE
KHOON ME HASHIM KA JO QAMAR HAI
