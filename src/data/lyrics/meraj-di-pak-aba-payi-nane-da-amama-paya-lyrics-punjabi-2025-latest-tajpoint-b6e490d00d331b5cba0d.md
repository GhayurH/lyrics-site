Meraj Di Pak Aba Payi Nane Da Amama Paya.
Shabbir Ne Maqtal Bhaijan Layi Akbar Nu Rasool Banaya.

1
Phophiyaan Ne Aap Buraq Uttay Chan Putar Sawar Si Kita.
Kujh Door Hussain, Muhammad De Ghorey Nal Bhajda Aaya.
Jehray Zakhm Rasool De Baqi San Ummat Ne Raj Ke Laye.
Taif De Pathraan Da Zakhmi Jadon Barchiyaan Samne Aaya.

2
Shabbir Putar Layi Ronda Si Zahra Babay Layi Roi.
Neza Akbar De Jigar Wichon Dowaan Mil Ke Naal Choraya.
Ainj Putar Jawan Di Mondiyan Te Maiyyat Shabbir Ne Chayi.
Shabbir Nu Jiwein Mondiyan Te Bachpan Wich Nanay Chaya.

3
Akbar De Zakhmi Seene Te Ainj Chadar Laila Payi.
Jiwein Ohad De Zakhmi Babay Nu Zahra Ne Marham Laya.
Ummat Tasveer Te Sorat Nu Saqlain Satandi Aayi.
Aagaz Muhammad Taun Kita Akbar Te Zulm Mukaya.
