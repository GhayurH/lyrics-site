1
Khat Sughra Da Aaya Aye Akbar Toor Na Sawaan.
Ik Waar Khol Akhiyaan Odha Haal Sunawaan.
Khat Da Aye Sarnawan Karbal Naa Way Likhiya Mera.
Tere Naa Taun Pehlaan Likhiya Jiway Akbar Tera.
Mere Wal Toon Wekh Akbar Tainu Bhain Milawan.
Likhiya Aye Sughra Ne Akbar Lagda Main Mar Jana.
Reh Gayi Hasrat Dil Wich Mere Tainu Sehra Lana.
Puchna Main Tainu Akbar Sughra Nu Bulawaan.
Turya Nahin Hun Jana Akbar Doon Lashaan Da Bhar Aye.
Tere Seene Wich Way Barchi Sughra V Beemar Aye.
Khat Naal Lash Teri Kiwein Akbar Chawaan.
Ibrahim Ne Khuwab Ik Takiya Aye Odi Tabeer Aye.
Na Main Akhiyaan Baniyaan Sughra Na Hi Tere Veer Aye.
Dewin Hosla Toon Sughra Main Sabar Wakhawaan.
Barchi Ik Kayi Jana Le Gayi Najfi, Akbar Pehla.
Piyo Akbar Da Zainab, Sughra Mar Gayi Aye Laila.
Khat V Nahin Tera Bachiya Hun Wekh Toon Rahwaan.
