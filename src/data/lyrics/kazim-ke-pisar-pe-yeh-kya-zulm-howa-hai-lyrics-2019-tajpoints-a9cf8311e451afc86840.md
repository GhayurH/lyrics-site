Kazim Ke Pisar Pe Yeh Kya Zulm Howa Hai.
Sar Peet Ke Roti Jo Khud Fatima Zahra Hai.

1
Aayi Hai Baqaiya Se Rone Ke Liye Aaj.
Maiyyat Pe Raza Ki Haye Fatima Zahra Hai.
Haider Bhi Ro Rahe Hain Ghurbat Yeh Dekh Kar.
Baba Ki Tarha Iss Ko Bhi Zehar Mila Hai.

2
Mehsoos Yeh Hota Hai Majlis Mein Raza Ki.
Rone Ke Liye Aayi Ik Behan Masooma Hai.
Sughra Ko Kha Gayi Thi Akbar Ki Judai.
Ab Hijar Masooma Ko Baradar Ka Kha Gaya Hai.

3
Aflak Pe Matam Hai Naam Raza Ka Logo.
Magmoom Iss Ke Gham Mein Khud Sare Anbiya Hai.
Qum Mein Sunaon Ga Yeh Ja Ke Dilawar Main.
Likha Jo Khon-e-Dil Se Maula Ka Marsiya Hai.
