Sar pe ab Jafar-e-Sadiq ka jo saya na raha,
Hum ghareebo ke liye koi sahara na raha

1
1. Khaak par laut tey hay alame tanhaai me,
Aysi ghurbat hai koi poochney wala na raha

2
2. Zehr ka aysa asar hay ke taraptey hai imam,
Is musibat ko koi dekhney wala na raha

3
3. Haey haye rokey yehi kehtey hay Kazim maula,
Sar pe afsos ke ab baap ka saya na raha

4
4. Ro rahe hain jo haram maula teri furqat par,
Gham se dil phat-ta hay ab zabt ka yara na raha

5
5. Dhaa diya rauza baqee ka bhi musalmano ne,
Koi shamma nahi jalti koi saya na raha
