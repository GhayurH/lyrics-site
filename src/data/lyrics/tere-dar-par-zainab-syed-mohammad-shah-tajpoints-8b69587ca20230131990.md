Tere Dar Par Zainab Syed Mohammad Shah.

1
Jab Se Peshani Rakhi Hai Tere Dar Par Zainab.
Jaag Utha Hai Marey Sajdoon Ka Muqadar Zainab.
Aik Do Mojzay Nabiyon Ke Howa Kartay Hain.
Kam Se Kam Mojzay Tere Hain Bahatar Zainab.
Shukriya Kehne Ko Aaye Hain Tere Dar Pe Hussain.
Le Ke Humrah Sawa Lakh Payamber Zainab.

2
Youn Tu Pali Hain Kayi Hujatein Bibi Tu Ne.
Mojza Sab Se Bara Hai Tera Akbar Zainab.
Taig-e-Haider Ke Bohat Waar Hain Mashoor Magar.
Kab Koi Hai Tere Khutbay Ke Barabar Zainab.
Gar Zamane Ko Tera Naqsh-e-Qadam Mil Jaye.
Phir Koi Our Na Kaba Ho Zameen Par Zainab.

3
Rondh Ke Guzra Tha Nu Lakh Ka Lashkar Jis Ko.
Aayi Hai Phir Ossay Shabeer Bana Kar Zainab.
Hai Rida Zahra Ki Gar Zoaf-e-Payamber Ka Elaj.
Zoaf-e-Tauheed Ka Hal Hai Teri Chadar Zainab.
Dosh-e-Ahmad Ka Chuka Aayi Hai Ahsaan Muhsin.
Doosh Par Dein Ki Zarorat Ko Utha Kar Zainab.
