Dost Kya Khoob Wafaon Ka Sillah Detey Hain.
Har Naye Mor Pe Ik Zakhm Naya Detey Hain.

1
Tum Se To Khair Ghari Bhar Ki Mulakat Rahi.
Log Sadiyon Ki Rafaqat Ko Bhula Detey Hain.
Kaise Mumkin Hai Dhuwan Bhi Na Ho Aur Dill Bhi Jaley.
Choot Parti Hai To Patthar Bhi Sada Detey Hain.

2
Kaun Hota Hai Mosibat Mein Kisi Ka Aye Dost.
Aag Lagti Hai To Patte Bhi Hawa Detey Hain.
Jinn Pe Hota Hai Bohat Dill Ko Bharosa Tabish.
Waqt Parne Pe Wohi Log Daga Detey Hain.
