Tab Imam Ayenge Syed Mudassir Mehdi.

1
Nafratein Mitaein Gay Tab Imam Aaein Gay.
Ishq Ko Barhaein Gay Tab Imam Aaein Gay.
Apne Gham Bholaein Gay Tab Imam Aaein Gay.
Sabar Aazmaein Gay Tab Imam Aaein Gay.
Dil Se Jab Bulaein Gay Tab Imam Aaein Gay.
Har Koi Jahan Wala Baat Ghoor Se Sun Le.

2
Dehar Se Na Kam Hoon Gay Ashiqain Hujjat Ke.
Yeh Yaqeen e Kamil Hai Jald Hum Bhi Dekhein Gay.
Gaib Mein Imam-e-Zamaan, Mustafa Ki Beti Se.
Izn Joon Hi Paein Gay Tab Imam Aaein Gay.
Dil Yeh Mera Kehta Hai Mujh Se Dosto Aksar.
Khud Jala Ke Hathoon Se Itehad Ka Banner.

3
Yeh Khateeb-o-Zakir Sab Sach Zaban Par La Kar.
Qatilan-e-Zahra Ke Naam Bar Sar-e-Mimber.
Khul Ke Jab Sunaein Gay Tab Imam Aaein Gay.
Syeda Ki Hurmat Ka Dehyan Iss Tarha Rakha.
Sheh Ke Samne Ki Hai, Hur Ne Jis Tarha Toba.
Aye Jahan Walo Suno, Yeh Pyam Hai Mera.

4
Hum Bhi Hur Ki Tarha Se, Apne Dil Ka Darwaza.
Jab Bhi Khatkhataein Gaye, Tab Imam Aaein Gay.
Yeh Nuseri Woh Gali Khatam Kijiye Jhagra.
Aap Bhi Aqeeday Par Daal Kar Rakhein Pardah.
Sab Hi Apni Nazron Ko Rakhein Janib-e-Kaba.
Jaisa Rab Ka Wadah Hai, Waisay Teen So Terah.

5
Khud Ko Jab Banaein Gaye, Tab Imam Aaein Gay.
Aaj Bhi Jary Yeh Dua Lab Pe Hai Mudassir Ke.
Apni Zeest Mein Jaldi Woh Bhi Waqt Aa Jaye.
Maula Pardah Sarka Ke Jald Aaein Ghaibat Se.
Our Hum Malangon Ko Phir Kabhi Na Parhna Paray.
Jab Imam Aaein Gaye, Tab Imam Aaein Gay.
