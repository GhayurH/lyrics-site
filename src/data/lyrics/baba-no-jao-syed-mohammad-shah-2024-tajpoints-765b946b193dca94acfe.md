1
Baba No Jao Syed Mohammad Shah Write Up
Ahsan Hoga Mujh Par Khudara Baba Na Jao.
Dastar Zakhmi Main Orh Loon Gi.
Jo Khon Hai Woh Sar Pe Maloon Gi.

2
Meri Rida Ka Bandho Amama.
Kaisay Meri Fajar Hogi Mukamal.
Gar Ho Gaye Aap Aankhon Se Ojhal.
Dekhon Gi Uth Kar Main Kis Ka Chehra.

3
Yaad Aa Raha Hai Mujh Ko Madina.
Wapis Watan Ko Chalte Hain Baba.
Mujh Ko Nahi Ab Kofay Mein Rehna.
Eid Aa Rahi Hai Rizwaan Bulao.

4
Janat Se Mere Kurtay Mangao.
Pehno Gi Kya Main Malboos Kala.
Main Sun Rahi Hoon Kuch Mujh Se Bolo.
Ik Bar Baba Aankhein Tu Kholo.

5
Dekho Keh Ab Dil Phatta Hai Mera.
Tum Ko Sadaein Mehrab De Ga.
Mimber Tumhara Noha Parhey Ga.
Veraan Rahe Ga Ab Yeh Musalla.

6
Main Dekhney Ko Aayi Thi Shahi.
Mujh Par Yateemi Kofay Mein Aayi.
Aaya Na Zainab Ko Raas Kofa.
Ro Ro Ke Talwar Ko Dekhta Hai.

7
Ghazi Ki Yeh Pehli Karbala Hai.
Chup Chup Ke Rota Hai Abbas Mera.
Maiyyat Uthi Tu Zainab Pukari.
Tarpaye Gi Yaad Har Pal Tumhari.

8
Roti Hai Dekho Kamsin Khadija.
Yeh Chup Tumhari Batla Rahi Hai.
Mohsin Ki Tum Ko Yaad Aa Rahi Hai.
Le Jao Mujh Ko Bhi Sath Warna.
