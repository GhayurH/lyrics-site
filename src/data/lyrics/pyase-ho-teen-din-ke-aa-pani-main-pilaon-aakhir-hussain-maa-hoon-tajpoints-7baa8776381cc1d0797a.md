Pyase Ho Teen Din Ke Aa Pani Main Pilaon Aakhir Hussain Maa Hoon.
Majboor Maa Hai Beta Kaise Tujhe Bachao Aakhir Hussain Maa Hoon.

1
Hal Min Ki Sada Sun Kar Baqaiya Main Choor Aayi.
Dukh Hai Keh Bach Na Paya Abbas Tera Bhai.
Teeron Pe Tera Lasha Shaayd Na Dekh Paon.
Nazoon Se Tujh Ko Pala Tha Nana Mustafa Ne.

2
Khana Tujhe Khilaya Tha Baba Murtaza Ne.
Bachpan Ki Teri Yadein Kaise Bhala Bhulaon.
Maqtal Se Saray Lashe Tu Ne Uthaye Beta.
Ahsas Hai Yeh Mujh Ko Thak Tu Gaya Hai Beta.

3
Soya Nahi Hai Kab Se Aajao Main Sulaon.
Zalim Thehar Chala Na Mere Samne Churi Tu.
Shabir Ko Dikha Na Mere Samne Churi Tu.
Manzar Yeh Dekh Kar Keh Shayad Main Mar Na Jaon.

4
Qasim Ke Tukray Tu Ne Kis Tarha Se Uthaye.
Iss Haal-e-Be-Kasi Par Khud Arsh Roya Haye.
Aankhon Se Apni Beta Kyun Khoon Na Main Bahaon.
Lori Se Meri Bachpan Mein SotaTha Tu Beta.

5
Hai Arzo Yeh Meri Aagosh Mein Chala Aa.
Bas Aakhri Yeh Beta Lori Tujhe Sunaon.
Darbar-e-Sahaba Mein Tum Gawah Mere.
Ab Yaan Hain Ali Zahra Nana Gawah Tere.

6
Jangal Mein Yahan Tujh Ko Kaise Main Choor Jaon.
 Afraz Gaud Lene Se Sar Shah-e-Shaeedaan Ka.
Zahra Yeh Kehti Aayi Aa Paas Mere Aa Ja.
Chehray Se Dhool Tere Chadar Se Main Hataon.
