Yeh Matam ki sada zinda rakay gi

Humay kerbobala zinda rakay gi

Alam Kay saye main Aja Kay tuj ko

Pharay ray ki hawa zinda rakhay gi

Main dunya say chala jaoonga laykin

Mujay mayri kaza zinda rakhay gi

Kafan Kay sath rakh deyna lahad main

Mujhay khak e shifa zinda rakhay gi

Aza shabeer ki mar nay na day gi

Mujay shay ki aza zinda rakhay gi

Yeh azadari kabi ruk nahi sakti

Zahara ki dua zinda rakhay gi
