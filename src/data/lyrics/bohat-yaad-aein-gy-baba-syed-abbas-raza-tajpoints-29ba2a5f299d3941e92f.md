1
Kaha Zainab Ne Dekhon Gi Main Jab Abbas Ka Chehra Bohat Yaad Aaein Gay Baba.
Abba Pehne Ga Woh Jab Bhi Amama Sar Pe Bandhay Ga Bohat Yaad Aaein Gay Baba.
Woh Baba Ka Shikasta Sar Na Zainab Bhool Paye Gi.
Musalay Se Mujhe Baba Ke Khoon Ki Khushbo Aaye Gi.
Namaz-e-Fajar Mein Jab Bhi Karon Gi Main Ada Sajdah Bohat Yaad Aaein Gay Baba.
Woh Galiyon Mein Nikal Kar Unn Ko Seene Se Lagatay They.
Unhein Khana Khilatay They Unhein Pani Pilatay They.
Khiyal Aaye Ga Jab Zainab Ko Kofay Ke Yateemo Ka Bohat Yaad Aaein Gay Baba.
Meri Maiyyat Pe Ronaq Ho Meri Beti Ke Hone Se.
Ali Ne Iss Liye Zainab Ko Bulwaya Madine Se.
Yeh Maqsad Baap Ka Beti Ko Jab Koi Bataye Ga Bohat Yaad Aaein Gay Baba.
Tamacha Jab Se Zahra Ko Laga Ik Pal Na Sotay They.
Woh Amma Ki Musibat Yaad Kar Ke Chup Ke Rotay They.
Kahey Ga Samne Zainab Ke Jab Bhi Koi Hay Zahra Bohat Yaad Aaein Gay Baba.
Kahan Se Dhond Kar Laye Gi Zainab Baap Ka Saya.
Padar Ke Baad Hota Hai Bara Bhai Padar Jaisa.
Hasan Jab Eid Par Zainab Ke Sar Par Hath Rakhay Ga Bohat Yaad Aaein Gay Baba.
Ali Keh Kar Gaye Hain Jab Bulao Gi Main Aaon Ga.
Tumhein Khud Hosla Doon Ga Galay Se Bhi Lagaon Ga.
Tu Phir Jab Sham Ke Darbar Ka Aaye Ga Darwaza Bohat Yaad Aaein Gay Baba.
Koi Zeeshan Our Abbas Jab Bhi Baap Marta Hai.
Yeh Gham Beti Ke Dil Ko Jane Kitna Zakhmi Karta Hai.
Yateemo Ke Liye Hai Hosla Zainab Ka Yeh Noha Bohat Yaad Aaein Gay Baba.
