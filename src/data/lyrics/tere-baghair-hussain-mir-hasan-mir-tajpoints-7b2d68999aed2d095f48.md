Tere Baghair Hussain Mir Hasan Mir
Fakhar-e-Kibriya Hussain.

1
Shah-e-Karbala Hussain.
Deen Ki Baqa Hussain.
Jan-e-LaIlaha Hussain.
Na Deen Kuch Hai Na Dunya Tere Beghair Hussain.

2
Har Aik Shay Hai Khasara Tere Beghair Hussain.
Kaha Batool Ne Aye Jan-e-Fatima Zahra.
Hai Aaj Teesri Shabaan Zameen Par Aa Ja.
Na Hoga Fajar Ka Sajdah Tere Beghair Hussain.

3
Muhammad e Arbi Jis Tarha Ali Ke Beghair.
Ossi Tarha Se Woh Be Chain Hai Akhi Ke Beghair.
Hasan Ka Dil Nahi Lagta Tere Beghair Hussain.
Tere Hi Faqoon Se Rozay Ki Shan Barhti Hai.

4
Namaz Khud Tere Pichey Namaz Parhti Hai.
Kahin Mila Nahi Sajdah Tere Beghair Hussain.
Meri Nazar Mein Barey Bad Naseeb Haji They.
Jo Tujh Ko Chor Ke Kabay Ke Gird Phirtay Rahe.

5
Tawaf-e-Kaba Bhala Kya Tere Beghair Hussain.
Zameen Haram Ki Tujhey Naaz Se Bulati Hai.
Sada Yeh Khana-e-Kaba Se Ab Bhi Aati Hai.
Kissi Ka Hajj Nahi Hoga Tere Beghair Hussain.

6
Sahabiyon Ne Kaha Aakhri Karam Kar De.
Tu Apne Hath Se Hum Sab Ke Sar Qalam Kar De.
Teri Qasam Nahi Jeena Tere Beghair Hussain.
Bata Raha Musalsal Sakoot Ummat Ka.

7
Kissi Ke Pas Nahi Tha Jawab Baiyat Ka.
Teri Behan Ke Elawah Tere Beghair Hussain.
Khateeb-e-Nook-e-Sina Ko Nazar Mein Rakha Hai.
Yeh Al-Kitab Mein Aaya Shaheed Zindah Hai.

8
Hai Be Daleel Yeh Dawa Tere Beghair Hussain.
Gawah Rahib-o-Fitrus Ko Pehle Banwaya.
Nabi Ne Phir Yeh Takallum Hai Sab Ko Samjhaya.
Khuda Bhi Kuch Nahi Deta Tere Beghair Hussain.
