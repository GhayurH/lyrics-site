1
Thi Sham o Sahar Yehi Dua Ahl e Haram Write Up
Thi Sham-o-Sahar Yehi Dua Ahl-e-Haram Mein.
Mar Jaye Na Sajjad Kahin Baap Ke Gham Mein.
Mashkeeze Mein Sajjad Ko Dikhti Thi Sakina.
Abbas Nazar Aata Tha Behno Ko Alam Mein.
Haj Karte Howay Sham Ki Ghaliyaan Nahi Bhola.
Aankhon Se Laho Behta Raha Sehan-e-Haram Mein.
Durah Koi Likhta Tah Jabhi Lafz Laho Ke.
Ik Noha Raqam Hota Tha Tab Loh-o-Qalam Mein.
Masroof-e-Azza Sirf Musallay Hi Nahi Hain.
Sajday Bhi Laho Rote Hain Sajjad Ke Gham Mein.
Chalees Bars Hota Tha Ahsas Yeh Har Aan.
Abid Ka Hai Dam Tootne Wala Kisi Dam Mein.
Ho Qatil-e-Akbar Bhi Sajjad Ka Mehmaan.
 Akbar Kami Aati Hi Nahi Bab-e-Karam Mein.
