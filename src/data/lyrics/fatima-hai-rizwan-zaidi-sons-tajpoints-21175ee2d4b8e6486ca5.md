Fatima Hai Rizwan Zaidi Sons Lyrics

1
Fatima Hai, Fatima Hai, Fatima Hai, Fatima Hai.
Yeh Nabi Ko Khuda Ki Atta Hai.
Iss Ke Baba Shariyat Ke Bani.
Har Sharf Iss Ka Hai Khandani.
Iss Ke Betay Hain Dein Ki Jawani.
Betiyaan Iss Ki Ismat Ki Rani.

2
Iss Ka Sartaj Shair-e-Khuda Hai.
Dono Aalam Pe Hai Iss Ki Shahi.
Iss Ke Mansab Mein Hai Dein Panahi.
Iss Ne Batil Ki, Ki Hai Tabahi.
Khud Yeh Darbar Ne Di Gawahi.
Iss Ke Lehjay Mein Haq Bolta Hai.

3
Hai Khuda Ki Raza Iss Ki Marzi.
Anbiya Bhi Issay Bhejein Arzi.
Hai Issi Ka Makaan Khuld-e-Arzi.
Hain Malak Iss Ke Bachoon Ke Darzi.
Khul Ke Quran Bhi Keh Raha Hai.
Aisi Hai Shahzadi Hamari.

4
Iss Ki Mamnon Toheed-e-Bari.
Dono Aalam Hain Qurban Wari.
Iss Se Jo Bhi Jalay Woh Hai Nari.
Yeh Hi ALLAH Ka Faisla Hai.
ALLAH ALLAH Yeh Shan-o-Azmat.
Iss Ki Sorat Hai Quraan Ki Sorat.

5
Sab Pe Wajib Hai Iss Ki Itaat.
Fatima Hai Wakeel e Wilayat.
Iss Ke Hathoon Mein Dast-e-Khuda Hai.
Youn Zamane Mein Yeh Jalwa Gar Hai.
Yeh Jigar Band-e-Khair-Al-Bashar Hai.
Iss Ka Jo Faisla Hai Amar Hai.

6
Iss Ka Darwaza Jannat Ka Dar Hai.
Iss Ka Ghar Hi Makan e Khuda Hai.
Iss Ke Jalwon Ne Bakhsha Ujala.
Chand Naqsh-e-Qadam Ka Hai Hala.
Iss Ne Deen-e-Elahi Ko Pala.
Iss Ne Batil Ka Dam Ghont Dala.

7
Iss Ki Godi Mein Quraan Pala Hai.
Shan Yeh Bhi Hai Rizwan-o- Gohar.
Panjtan Pak Ka Hai Yeh Mehwar.
Innama Iss Ki Ismat Ka Zewar.
Kaba Sajday Karey Iss Ke Dar Par.
Arsh-e-Azam Issi Ka Wila Hai.
