Shadi Hai Syeda Se Janab E Ameer Ki Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi
Baraat Aa Rahi Hai Nabi Ke Wazeer Ki

1
Masroof-e-Intezar Khuda Ka Rasool Hai
Farsh-e-Zameen Pe Hoor-o-Malak Ka Nazool Hai
Yeh Jashan-e-Ishtarak-e-Ali-o-Batool Hai
Rehmat Baras Rahi Hai Khuda-e-Qadeer Ki

2
Hain Be Misal Sare Barati Baraat Mein
Dulha Ali Sa Kaun Hai Iss Kainat Mein
Beti Ka Hath Dein Gay Nabi Oss Ke Hath Mein
Aadam Ki Jis Ke Hathoon Ne Matti Khameer Ki

3
Noshah Par Nisar Hai Yousuf Sa Khobro
Sehra Ali Ka Parhtay Hain Dawood-e-Khush Galu
Sadqa Ali Ke Naam Ka Batta Hai Chaar So
Jholi Khuda Ne Bhar Di Hai Ik Ik Faqeer Ki

4
Saj Dhaj Ali Ki Chashm-e-Khuda Ka Garor Hai
Chehray Se Aashkar Qayamat Ka Noor Hai
Jamay Ki Har Kiran Pe Fida Barq-e-Toor Hai
Sharminda Aab-o-Taab Hai Mah-e-Muneer Ki

5
Janat Nasheen Shana Ba Shana Ali Ke Hain
Paikar Hasen Shana Ba Shana Ali Ke Hain
Rooh Al-Amin Shana Ba Shana Ali Ke Hain
Hairat Mein Hai Nigah Har Ik Rahgeer Ki

6
Sehrey Mein Aayaton Ki Hai Khushbo Khuli Hoi
Sorat Hai Aab-e-Noor-e-Khuda Se Dhuli Hoi
Chehra Hai Ya Kitab-e-Khuda Hai Khuli Hoi
Baraat Kiya Hasen Hai Roshan Zameer Ki

7
Aayi Hai Fikar Noor Ki Wadi Mein Ghoom Ke
Maula-e-Kainat Ke Sehrey Ko Chom Ke
Kehta Hai Dil Yeh:AKHTAR-o-SAJJAD: Jhoom Ke
Dunya Nazeer Laye Marey Dast Geer Ki
