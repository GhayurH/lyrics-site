1
Hussain Ka Baba Zakir Bilawal Ali Hussaini.
Fazeelaton Ka Samandar Hussain Ka Baba.
Hai Deen-e-Haq Ka Muqadar Hussain Ka Baba.
Shajar Hajar Hai Kya Murdah Bhi Uth Ke Chalne Lagay.
Jo Aik Mar De Thokar Hussain Ka Baba.
Parhi Hai Nad-e-Ali Iss Liye Payamber Ne.
Keh Aa Ke Jeet Le Khaybar Hussain Ka Baba.
Woh Zindagi Mein Sada Thokarein Hi Khaye Ga.
Jisay Bhi Yaad Na Ho Gar Hussain Ka Baba.
Jo Pak Nasal Hai Oss Ke Liye Hai Ishq-e-Ali.
Nahi Hai Sab Ko Muyassar Hussain Ka Baba.
Yeh Kainat Hai Kya Sheh Keh Khud Payamber Ka.
Bana Hai Monis-o-Yawar Hussain Ka Baba.
Ali Ka Naam Hi Leta Hoon Ghar Lagay Thokar.
Sanbhal Leta Hai Aa Kar Hussain Ka Baba.
Nabi Yeh Bolay Keh Mairaj Hai Yeh Kabay Ki.
Hai Mere Doosh Ke Opar Hussain Ka Baba.
Ali Beghair Koi Ilm Tak Nahi Pohancha.
Hai Shehar-e-Ilm Nabi Dar Hussain Ka Baba.
Yeh Bolay Mar Ke Marhab Ke Sar Pe Taig Ali.
Haan Mera Naam Hai Haider Hussain Ka Baba.
Na Hoti Khilqat-e-Haider Na Hoti Gar Zahra.
Hai Kofway Bint-e-Payamber Hussain Ka Baba.
Bas Aik Raat Hi So Kar Nabi Ke Bistar Par.
Khareday Marzi-e-Dawar Hussain Ka Baba.
Karam Hai Hum Pe Yeh Parwardigar Ka Abbas.
Keh Hai Hamara Muqadar Hussain Ka Baba.
