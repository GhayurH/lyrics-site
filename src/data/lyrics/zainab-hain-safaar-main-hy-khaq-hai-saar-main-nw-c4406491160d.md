Zainab Hain Safaar Main, Hy Khaq Hai Saar Main.

Kaab Hogi Reha Qaid Sai  Kaab Jayaigi Ghaar Main.

Bikhrai Howai Balon Sai Mun Dhampalo Bibi
Yeh Log Sharabi Hain  Teri Rah Guzar Main.

Thai Tang Phatai Kaprai Aur Kaan Bhi Zakhmi
Abid Nai Utara Jo  Sakina Ko Qabar Main.

Jis Pardai Ka Zamin Tha Abbas Dilawar
Hy Zakham Ussi Pardai Ka  Abid Kai Jigar Main.

Aik Waqat Tha Shehzadi Thi Hy Kufay Ki Zainab
Kis Haal Main Aaayi Hai  Yeh Baba Keh Shehar Main.

Tareekh Teri Jang Ko Dohrati Rahai Gi
Asghar Jo Laari Tu Nai  Naani Si Umar Main.
