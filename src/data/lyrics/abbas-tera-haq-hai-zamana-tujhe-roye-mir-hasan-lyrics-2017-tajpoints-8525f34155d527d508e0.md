1
Abbas Tera Haq Hai Zamana Tujhe Roye Mir Hasan Mir.
Jab Sham-o-Sahar Fatima Zahra Tujhe Roye.
Dura Liye Pehre Pe Sitmagar Khara Hai.
Kaise Tere Lashay Pe Sakina Tujhe Roye.

2
Jis Waqt Uthaya Na Giya Lasha Jawan Ka.
Sheh Oss Ghari Akbar se Zyda Tujhe Roye.
Zainab Ne Kaha Sham-e-Ghareeban Mein Hai Ghazi.
Be-Parda Mujhe Dekh Ke Baba Tujhe Roye.

3
Sajday Ke Liye Jab Liya Hathon Ka Sahara.
Tu Sajjad Musalay Pe Hamesha Tujhe Roye.
Bin Hathon Ke Kis Tarha Tu Ghoray Se Gira Tha.
Yeh Sun Ke Bohat Ahl-e-Madina Tujhe Roye.

4
Mashkeeza Kahin Jism Kahin Hath Kahin Hai.
Ja Ja Ke Kahan Fatima Zahra Tujhe Roye.
Arman Sakina Ka Tha Jitna Tujhe Roti.
Sajjad Teri Qabar Pe Utna Tujhe Roye.

5
Hashim Ke Qamar Kyun Na Tera Chahne Wala.
Washams Ka Parhte Howay Sorah Tujhe Roye.
Hasrat Hai Keh Ghazi Ho Laho Aankh Se Jari.
Yeh:MIR TAKALLUM: Kabhi Aisa Tujhe Roye.
