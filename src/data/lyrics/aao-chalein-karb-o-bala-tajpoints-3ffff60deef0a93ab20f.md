Hai Hamari Darsgah Karbala Karbala.
Haq Ka Seedha Rasta Karbala Karbala.

1
Chalo Chalo Humein Tumhein Pukarti Hai Karbala.
Chalte Raho Sath Liye Sara Zamana.
Aao Chalein Karb-o-Bala Ban Ke Safeeran-e-Azza.
Tum Bhi Chalo Hum Bhi Chalein Sath Ho Zahra Ki Dua.

2
Karb-o-Bala Mein Tumhein Quran Miley Ga.
Fatima Tul Zahra Ka Faizan Miley Ga.
Tum Ko Har Ik Mulk Ka Insan Miley Ga.
Parcham-e-Abbas Nigehban Miley Ga.

3
Aao Chalein Parcham-e- Abbas Utha Kar.
Shehar-e-Najaf Se Chalein Karb-o-Bala Mil Kar.
Sheh Ke Azadaron Ka Ho Sath Samandar.
Pal Mein Badal Jaye Ga Hum Sab Ka Muqadar.

4
Deney Chalein Pursa Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Ka.
Noha Parhein Chal Ke Alamdar-e-Jarri Ka.
Momino Matam Karein Humshakal-e- Nabi Ka.
Qasim-o-Be Shair Ka Pursa Dein Sabhi Ka.

5
Shukar-e-Khuda Maula Ke Zawwar Bachay Hum.
Aal-e-Muhammad Ke Tarafdar Bachay Hum.
Haider-e-Karrar Ke Hubdar Bachay Hum.
Zahra Ke Bachoon Ke Azadar Bachay Hum.

6
Mango Dua Unn Ki Bhi Pori Ho Duaein.
Detey Hain Maula Ko Jo Ro Ro Ke Sadaein.
Maula Mere Jald Unhein Dar Pe Bulaein.
Kartay Hoye Matam-e-Shabeer Woh Aaein.
