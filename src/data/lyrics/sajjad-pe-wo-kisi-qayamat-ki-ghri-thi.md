Naatawan aur qayd mei, haay beemar haay haay Sajjad
Sajjad pe wo kaisi qayamat ki ghari thi

1
Jab sani-e-zehra sare,  darbar khari thi

2
1) Haathon pe tha masoom Sakina ka janaza
Aankhon mei lagi khun ke,  Ashkon ki jali thi

3
Jab sani-e-zehra sare,  darbar khari thi

4
2) Sar ko wo jhukaaye huwe, har mor se guzre
Tapta huwa sehra tha,  Aur dhoop kari thi

5
Jab sani-e-zehra sare,  darbar khari thi

6
3) Kaanton ko rulaati thi, teri aabla paayi
Zanjeer bhi roti thi,  jo paawon mei pari thi

7
Jab sani-e-zehra sare,  darbar khari thi

8
4) Zindan ke andhere mei, tanha thi Sakina
Aankhon mei uske kaisi,  ashkon ki jali thi

9
Jab sani-e-zehra sare,  darbar khari thi

10
5) Shah "Najm" ne band kardiye, is pe sukhan tamam
Izzat bhi bari thi to,  Musibat bhi bari thi
