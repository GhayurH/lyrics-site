1
Teri Aankh Ko Aazmana Para.
Mujhe Qisa-e-Gham SUnana Para.
Gham-e-Zindagi Teri Khatir Humein.
Sar-e-Waar Bhi Muskurana Para.

2
Hawadas Ki Shab Itni Tareek Thi.
Jawani Ko Sagar Uthana Para.
Mere Dushman-e-Jaan Tere Waste.
Kayi Doston Ko Bholana Para.

3
Zamane Ki Raftaar Ko Dekh Kar.
Qiyamat Pe Emaan Lana Para.
Jinhein Dekhna Bhi Na Chahe Nazar.
Unhin Se Taluk Barhana Para.

4
Kayi Saanp The Qimti Iss Qadar.
Unhein Aasteen Mein Chupana Para.
Hawaon Ke Tewar Jo Buham Howay.
Cheragon Ko Khud Jhilmilana Para.
