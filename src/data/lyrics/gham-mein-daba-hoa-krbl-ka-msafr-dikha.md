Gham mein dooba hua Karbal ka musafir dekha
Rotay Shabbir ko Zahra ki lehad par dekha

1
Apna sar maar kay deewar say roya Ghazi
Jab Nabizaadi ko aatay huye bahar dekha

2
Noor aankhon ka gaya katilon say puchay Hussain
Koi batlaaye kisi nay mera Akbar dekha

3
Door tak phaili hai Qasim ki jawani rann mein
Dair tak dhoondta phir Sibt e Payambar dekha

4
Ravi likhtay hain kay Sajjad ko jab hosh aaya
Jaltay khayam mein Zainab ko khulay sar dekha

5
Mujh Ko Barbad Khuda Kar De Yeh Bola Pani
Sheh Ke Hathon Mein Jo Zakhmi Ali Asghar Dekha
