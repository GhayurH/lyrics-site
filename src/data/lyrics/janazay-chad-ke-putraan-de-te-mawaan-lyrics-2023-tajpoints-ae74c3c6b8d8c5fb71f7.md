1
Janazay Chad Ke Putraan De Te Mawaan Sham Aa Gayiyaan.
Amamay Khak Te Rul Gaye Ridawaan Sham Aa Gayiyaan.
Ewein Lagda Aye Basti Wich Ali Akbar Di Janj Aayi.
Siraan Te Mehndiyaan La Ke Sinawaan Sham Aa Gayiyaan.

2
Rozana Khak Di Chadar Siraan Te Sanbhdi Hoiyaan.
Safar Wich Karbala Taun Kujh Hawawaan Sham Aa Gayiyaan.
Sakina Piyo De Sir Wali Sina Nu Tak Ke Kaindi Si.
Main Phophiyaan Waang Hi Seh Ke Sazawaan Sham Aa Gayiyaan.

3
Sar-e-Abbas Nu Tak Ke Ruqaiya Wain Kardi Si.
Nibhawan Veer Main Teri Wafawaan Sham Aa Gayiyaan.
Kinu Kenday Ne Zainab Shamiyaan Nu Lag Pata Jaway.
Jay Katiyaan Hoiyaan V Ghazi Di Banwaan Sham Aa Gayiyaan.

4
Aydhar Bazar Da Majma Odhar Abid De Rat Athro.
Khabar Phophiyaan Nu Ho Gayi Si Keh Rahwaan Sham Aa Gayiyaan.
Na Aynj Durray Na Aynj Pathar Sahey Saqlain Nabiyaan V.
Jiwein Syedaniyaan Seh Ke Sazawaan Sham Aa Gayiyaan.
