Aaye Zilqad Mein Mosa Kazim Ke Ghar.
Bibi Masooma-e-Qum Imam-e-Raza.

1
Ik Sadaf Mein Khuda Se Miley Do Gohar.

2
Naam Dukhtar Ka Apni Rakha Fatima.
Our Betay Pe Dada Ali Ka Rakha.
Najma Khaton Ke Dono Noor-e-Nazar.

3
Pehli Zilqada Ko Fatima Masoma.
Gayarah Zilqada Ko Maula Imam-e-Raza.
Farsh Par Aaye Nori Jahan Chor Kar.

4
Pehli Zilqada Se Gayarah Zilqada Talak.
Keh Rahe They Musalsal Yeh Aa Kar Malak.
Maula Dono Mubarak Ho Lakht-e-Jigar.

5
Ik Ali Ke Amamay Ka Waris Howa.
Ik Ke Hissay Mein Hai Fatima Ki Rida.
Shajra e Tayyaba Ke Hain Dono Samar.

6
Ik Ne Aabad Mashhad, Khurasaan Kiya.
Ik Ne Aabad Qum Ka Shehar Kar Diya.
Mulk-e-Iran Mein Ilm Ke Do Hain Dar.

7
Mosa Kazim Ke Ghar Utray Quran Do.
Aaye Deen-e-Khuda Ke Nigehban Do.
Ban Ke Deen-e-Elahi Ke Khatir Sapar.

8
Ho Agar Aap Ki Mujh Pe Nazar-e-Karam.
Dono Rozon Ka Dekhon Main Jah-o-Hasham.
Hai Yehi Iltija Lab Pe Sham-o-Sahar.

9
Iss Qasiday Ka Mujh Ko Sila Chahiye.
Malkoon! Aap Ki Bas Raza Chahiye.
 Mohtasham Ki Dua Hai Yehi Mukhtasir.
