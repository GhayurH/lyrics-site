Kis shan say utha hai haye ghazi alam tera

Har ankh roo rahi hai, seenay main hai gham tera

Qayam hai teray dam say rasmay wafa jahan main

Ho ga na zamanay main, parcham kabhi kham tera

Hai toor diye koozay masoom sakina nay

Jab aatay howay dekha hai khali alam tera

Zainub ko nazar aaye nayzay pay rida apni

Jab khon main tar deekha abbas alam tera

Thi sham-e-ghareban main hai maut ki khamooshi

Zainub nay diye pehray hai lay kay alam tera

Mola nay kaha ro kar hai tooti kamar meri

Girtay howay jab dekha mola nay alam tera

Zainub nay yeh maqtal main tujh say tha kiya wada

Har ghar main lagawoon gi abbas alam tera

Hai sham ki rahoon main rotay howay bachoon ko

Zainub nay sulaya hai dikhla kay alam tera

Sardar jo hota hai aaghaz muharram ka

Hum roo kay sajatay hain abbas alam tera
