Menu Shaam Nahi Bhuldi Maa, Mere Athru Saaf Na Kar
Menu Shaam Diyan, Galliyan Yaad Aawan, Mai Kairay Pasay Jaa

1
Bhulnaa, Bhulna Nai Menu Zainab Da, Bazar’chon Lungna
Kevain Turdi Rahi, Parhdi Naad e Ali, Wich Wasde Hue Pathraan…Mere Athru

2
Honaa, Honaa Nai Mazloom Bhira, Mere Vangun Dooja
Kissay Veer Ne Nai, Khudeya Bhaina Layi, Zindaan De Wich Kabraan…

3
Takkeya, Takkeya Ve Mai Sakina Nu, Khaande Hue Patthar
Har Patthar Te, Ro Ro Lendi Si, Ghazi Chachay Da Naaa….

4
Vekhay, Vekhay Saare Manzar Ne, Par Main Nahi Bhulda
Mai Wich Balway, Kubra Nu Takkeya, Mai Kyun Na Khoon Rowaan
