1
Nazil Dil-e-Rasool Pe Jo Mojza Howa.
Charkha Jo Katti Hai Bunti Hai Aasmaan.
Chaki Jo Peesti Hai Tu Chalta Hai Ye Jahaan.
Youn Haath Kibriya Ka Batati Hai Jo Sada.

2
Zahra Ki Azmatoon Ko Kya Samjhe Ga Ye Bashar.
Hasnain Baag-e-Khuld Ke Sardar Hain Magar.
Janat Hassan Hussain Ki Hai Jiss Ke Zair-e-Paa.
Qanoon-e-Kibriya Hai Yeh Iss Kainaat Mein.

3
Shohar Ka Rizq Likha Hai Zoja Ke Hath Mein.
Tehreer Jiss Ke Hath Mein Hai Rizq-e-Murtaza.
Tanzeem-e-Kainaat Mein Haider Ke Saath Thi.
Matti Khameer Hazrat-e-Adam Se Jo Bachi.

4
Jiss Ne Ossi Khameer Se Hawa Ko Di Jila.
Masoom Ki Hadees Hai Sab Ko Hai Yeh Khabar.
Chodah Naqab-e-Noor Hain Khaliq Ke Noor Par.
Pehla Naqaab Jo Hai Rokh-e-Kirdgar Ka.

5
Haq To Yehi Tha Hota Taruf Rasool Se.
Rishte Magar Khuda Ne Ginaye Batool Se.
Jiss Ke Liye Nizaam-e-Masheeat Badal Giya.
Ismat Ka Rab Ne Paas Youn Rakha Kitab Mein.

6
Aayat Mein Bhi Zikar Kiya Tu Hijab Mein.
Quraan Mein Jiss Ka Naam bhi Khul Kar Nahi Likha.
Mangwa Ke Do Libase Falak Ikhteyar Se.
Jiss Ne Hassan Hussain Ki Khowaish Ke Waste.

7
Aayat Lane Wale Ko Darzi Bana Diya.
 Akbar Ko Noor-e-Aal-e-Muhammad Ki Hai Qasam.
Dunya Mein Mir Koi Nahi Oss Se Muhtaram.
Wo Jiss Ke Ayehteram Mein Uthte Hoon Mustafa.
