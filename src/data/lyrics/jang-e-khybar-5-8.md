1
Jang-e-Khybar 5-8
Har Ik Ko Shak Tha Mera Hi Lein Gaye Rasool Naam
Jagay Howay The Raat Ke Aankhein Thi Surkh Faam
Jo Chote Qad The Panjon Ke Bal The Khare Tamam

2
Itne Mein Laye Arsh Se Jibreel Yeh Pyam
Lashkar Na Kar Sake Ga Tumhari Koi Madad
Allah Keh Raha Hai Kaho Ya Ali Madad
Nad-e-Ali Zuban-e-Muhammad Pa Aa Gayi

3
Our Oss Taraf Madeene Mein Kehne Lagay Ali
Qanbar Chalo Pukar Rahe Hain Humein Nabi
Duldul KO Pyar Kar Ke Yeh Farmaya Oss Ghari
Aaj Iss Tarha Se Chal Ke Falak Kanpne Lagay

4
Dorey Jo Tere Sath Hawa Hanpne Lagay
Jaisa Kaha Tha Badshah-e-Khas-o-Aam Ne
Waisa Kiya Hai Duldul-e-Mehshar Kharam Ne
Hairat Se Dekha Lashkar-e-Khairul Inam Ne

5
Shair-e-Khuda Khare The Muhammad Ke Samne
Har Chehre Par Sawal Tha Hazrat Kahan Se Aa
Salman Bole Aaj Ali Aasmaan Se Aa
Haq Se Alam Mila Soye Khybar Ali Chale

6
Chomi Rakab Fateh Ne Aa Kar Ali Chale
Deen-e-Khuda Ka Ban Ke Muqadar Ali Chale
Aaya Hai Phir Jala Imam-e-Mubeen Ko
Jibreel Jaldi Tham Ko Aa Kar Zameen Ko
