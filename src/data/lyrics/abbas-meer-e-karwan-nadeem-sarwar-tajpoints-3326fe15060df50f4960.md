1
Abbas Meer e Karwan Nadeem Sarwar.
Karwaan, Karwaan Abbas Meer-e-Karwaan.
Yasrab Se Soye Karbala.
Ik Karwaan Rukhsat Howa.

2
Sar Pe Alam Saya Figan.
Ghazi Ki Aati Hai Sada.
Pehla Safar Zainab Ka Hai.
Sunsaan Kardo Rasta.

3
Kya Shan Hai Kya Dabdaba.
Kya Loag Hain Quraan Numa.
Sallay Alla, Sallay Ala.
Aayat Ka Hai Qafila.

4
Mashk-o-Alam Inn Ka Nishaan.
ALLAH Ke Pyaray Hain Yeh.
Aal-e-Nabi Sare Hain Yeh.
Quran Ke Parey Hain Yeh.

5
Konain Ke Taray Hain Yeh.
Zair-e-Qadam Hai Aasmaan.
Koi Shabeeh-e-Mustafa.
Koi Hasan Ka Dilruba.

6
Hai Koi Aks-e-Syeda.
Shabeer Ka Yeh Qafila.
Thamay Alam Jo Hai Jawaan.
Yasrab Se Taba Karbala.

7
Har Dam Tawaf-e-Qafila.
Abbas Karta Hi Raha.
Parday Ka Zimmay Daar Tha.
Youn Mutmain Thin Bibiyaan.

8
Abbas Woh Kirdar Hai.
Bad-e-Ali Salar Hai.
Aisa Alam Bardar Hai.
Maidaan Mein Be Talwar Hai.

9
Phir Bhi Alam Ka Pasbaan.
Lo Aa Gaya Oss Ka Alam.
Ghazi Jisay Kehte Hain Hum.
Kehte Chalo Yeh Dam Ba Dam.

10
Abbas Meer-e-Lashkaram.
Baab Ul Hawaij Meharbaan.
Jab Sham Pohancha Qafila.
Khutba Yeh Zainab Ne Diya.

11
Sun Le Yeh Qaum-e-Ashqiya.
Hum Hain Binaye LaIlaha.
Kashti Hai Dein Hum Badbaan.
Tha Hashar Mein Maula Ali.

12
Yeh:SARWAR-o-REHAN: Bhi.
Nazar-e-Karam Se Aap Ki.
Rakhtay Hain Umeed-e-Qavi.
Nohon Se Dein Gay Yeh Biyaan.
