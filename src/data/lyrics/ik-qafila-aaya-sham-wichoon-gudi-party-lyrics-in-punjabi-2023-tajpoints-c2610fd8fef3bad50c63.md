Ik Qafila Aaya Sham Wichoon Gudi Party Lyrics In Punjabi
Ik Qafila Aaya Sham Wichon Jidda Wakhra Lutiya Haal Aye Salar Khara Rat Roway.
Har Bibi De Hath Zakhmi Ne Zakhmi Masoom Ik Baal Aye.
Salar Aakhay Mere Samne Aye Haye Zeen Utton Baba Lathiya.
Haye Leh Gaye Bazu Ghazi De Haye Maa Da Burqa Lutiya Gaya.
Haye Ghoriyaan Ne Kar Chadiya Aye Qasim Da Badan Pamal Aye.
Zalim Koray Barsanday San Tati Rait Te Aan Turanday San.
Gal Tauq Hathaan Wich Kariyaan San Sir Nangay Mawaan Phiranday San.
Pakaan De Pak Lahu Naal Aye Hoye Sham De Rastay Lal Aye.
Ik Bibi Naam Ruqaiya Aye Kaindi Abid Das Ghazi Nu.
Sardar Ne Wal Wapis Aaye Ja De Paigham Namazi Nu.
Aa Gayi Aye Sain Karan Chehlum Lutay Hoye Poor De Naal Aye.
Karbal De Sare Shaheedan Di Aawaz Aayi Kuhram De Wich.
Asaan Deen Banaya Wich Karbal Taun Sanu Bachaya Sham De Wich.
Toon Khutbay Parh Darbaraan Wich Ditti Deen Di Mushkil Taal Aye.
Jis Welay Da Aye Poor Aaya Karbal Di Tarti Kaindi Aye.
Ik Bibi Umm Ul-Mosayab Aye Jehri Khak Te Uthdi Baindi Aye.
Oss Maqtal De Wich Muk Gayi Aye Do Jag De Shah Di Aal Aye.
Phatda Aye Seena Matami Da Oh Manzar Jad V Yaad Aaye.
 Hadi Jiwein Ujray Syed Aye Sochda Sochda Mar Jaway.
Aye Wakhra Gham Aye Pakaan Da Jis Di Na Koi Misal Aye.
