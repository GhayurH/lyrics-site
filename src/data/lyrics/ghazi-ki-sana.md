Suno Shabbir Se Ghazi Ki Sana
Sarwar Ke Alamdar
Zainab Ke Wafadar
Ya Sani-e-Karrar
Sardaron Ke Sardar
Woh Bataein Gaye Humein Kiya Hai Wilaye Abbas
Aao Shabeer Se Suntay Hain Sana-e-Abbas

1
Sorah-e-Qadar Ke Parday Mein Pala Hai Abbas
Sorah-e-Hamd Ki Godi Mein Raha Hai Abbas
Aal-e-Imran Ke Aangan Mein Chala Hai Abbas
Dil-e-Yaseen Dharakne Ki Sada Hai Abbas
Be Ke Nuktay Se Jo Wal Naas Talak Jaye Ga
Sare Quran Mein Abbas Nazar Aaye Ga

2
Jis Ko Aandhi Kahey Tofaan Woh Mera Ghazi Hai
Jis Ke Khadam Hain Sulemaan Woh Mera Ghazi Hai
Maut Hai Jis Se Pareshaan Woh Mera Ghazi Hai
Jis Mein Shabeer Ki Hai Jaan Woh Mera Ghazi Hai
Iss Ki Azmat Ki Tu Zainab Ne Qasam Khayi Hai
Fatima Zahra Ka Beta Hai Mera Bhai Hai

3
Apne Pehlu Mein Hamesha Se Bithaya Main Ne
Mujh Ko Zahra Ne Issay Jhola Jholaya Main Ne
Farz Nana Ki Tarha Apna Nibhaya Main Ne
Iss Ko Kandhoon Pe Kayi Bar Bithaya Main Ne
Sab Yeh Kehte Hain Samaa Khoob Bana Rakha Hai
Dekh Lo Chand Ne Soraj Ko Utha Rakha Hai

4
Apne Mabood Ki Masoom Gawahi Abbas
Chalti Phirti Hoi Shamsheer-e-Elahi Abbas
Dushman-e-Aal-e-Muhammad Ki Tabahi Abbas
Lashkar-e-Fatima Zahra Ka Sipahi Abbas
Kufar Ko Ishq Ka Kaba Nahi Dhaney De Ga
Yeh Wilayat Pe Koi Aanch Na Aane De Ga

5
Iss Ki Har Aik Sift Hai Mere Baba Jaisi
Woh Sakhi Yeh Bhi Sakhi Woh Bhi Wali Yeh Bhi Wali
Farq ALLAH Ne Hum Dono Mein Rakha Hai Yehi
Main Muhammad Ka Muhammad Yeh Ali Ka Hai Ali
Karbala Mein Shab-e-Hijrat Ka Jo Manzar Hota
Mere Bistar Pe Bhi Abbas-e-Dilawar Sota

6
Masla Iss Ke Liye Sirf Meri Chah Ka Hai
Foaj Ki Fikar Na Khatrah Issay Jangah Ka Hai
Ghaat Par Pehra Agar Lashkar-e-Robah Ka Hai
Zoor Ghazi Ki Kalai Mein Yadullah Ka Hai
Sirf Naizay Se Agar Hath Mila Le Abbas
Nehar Kiya Cheez Hai Soraj Ko Utha Le Abbas

7
Kitne Lakhon Ka Hai Yeh Lashkar-e-Shar Kya Matlab
Samne Shair Ke Yeh Teer-o-Tabar Kya Matlab
Ain ALLAH Ki Aankhon Mein Ho Dar Kya Matlab
Iss Ke Hotey Hoye Khaimon Pe Nazar Kya Matlab
Khailta Aaya Hai Gehwarey Mein Yeh Teer Ke Sath
Bachpana Kata Hai Abbas Ne Shamsheer Ke Sath

8
Dast-e-Zahra Ka Tarasha Howa Almas Hai Yeh
Khandan-e-Bani Hashim Ke Liye Aas Hai Yeh
Karbala Jis Pe Hai Tehreer Woh Qirtas Hai Yeh
Shair Jis Shair Se Dartay Hain Woh Abbas Hai Yeh
Jab Mera Shair-e-Jawaan Ran Mein Biphar Jata Hai
Zindagi Kya Malak Al-Maut Bhi Dar Jata Hai

9
Milti Julti Mere Baba Se Hai Sorat Iss Ki
Hai Dil-e-Sani-e-Zahra Pe Hakomat Iss Ki
Poch Lo Malik-e-Ashtar Se Shujat Iss Ki
Aik Jumlay Mein Sunaon Main Fazeelat Iss Ki
Mawraye Samajh-o-Soch-o-Dil-o-Aqal Hai Yeh
Kya Fazail Hoon Biyaan Khud Hi Abalfazl Hai Yeh

10
Kar De Khamosh Sitamgaron Ki Sari Bar Bar
Taig Bijli Ki Tarha Iss Ki Jo Chamkay Kar Kar
Sar Girein Khak Pe Olon Ki Tarha Se Tar Tar
Maut Dozakh Ki Taraf Hank De Sara Rewar
Nehar Tak Sham Ke Lashkar Ke Gira De Lashay
Na Ho Chalne Ki Jagah Itnay Bicha De Lashay

11
Koi Ghazi Sa Wafadar Nahi Ho Sakta
Iss Se Acha Koi Maiyar Nahi Ho Sakta
Iss Se Barh Kar Koi Haqdar Nahi Ho Sakta
Ab Koi Our Alamdar Nahi Ho Sakta
Jab Sar-e-Hashar Alam Le Ke Nikalna Ho Ga
Iss Ke Pichey Tu Rasolon Ko Bhi Chalna Ho Ga

12
Chat Pe Kabay Ki Woh Khutba Diya Hairaan They Malak
Waqt Thehra Howa Khamosh They Sab Arsh Talak
Roab Aisa Tha Jhapakti Na Thi Logon Ki Palak
Sahib-e-Minber-e-Kofa Ki Nazar Aayi Jhalak
Sab Pe Israr-e-Jahan Khol Rahe Hoon Jaisay
Aisa Lagta Tha Ali Bool Rahe Hoon Jaisay

13
ALLAH ALLAH Ajab Tarha Se Yeh Biphra Tha
Mein Ne Zainab Ki Qasam De Ke Issay Roka Tha
Warna Lashkar Tu Ossi Roz Tah-o-Bala Tha
Iss Ne Jab Ghaiz Mein Shamsheer Se Khat Khaincha Tha
Oss Ki Haibat Ka Yeh Aalam Hai Keh Darti Hai Howa
Ab Bhi Do Hison Mein Oss Khat Se Guzarti Hai Howa

14
Yaad Hai Mujh Ko Woh Siffeen Ka Sara Manzar
Fakhar Se Sar They Uthaye Hoye Baba Haider
De Ke Ghazi Ko Dua Kehte They Bhai Shabar
Tum Ne Dushman Se Bhi Karwa Diya Haider Haider
Fatha Aisi Thi Keh Har Fatima Zadah Khush Tha
Khud Main Siffeen Mein Ghazi Se Zyada Khush Tha

15
Khul Ke Yeh Bazu-e-Shabeer Laray Ga Ik Din
Yani Mehzar Mein Hai Tehreer Laray Ga Ik Din
Ban Ke Wal Fatah Ki Tafseer Laray Ga Ik Din
Aye Takallum Yeh Mahaveer Laray Ga Ik Din
Jab Issay Taig Chalaney Ki Ijazat Ho Gi
Our Phir Kuch Bhi Nahi Ho Ga Qayamat Ho Gi
