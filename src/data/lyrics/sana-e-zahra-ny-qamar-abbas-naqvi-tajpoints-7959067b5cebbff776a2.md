1
Sana e Zahra Ny Qamar Abbas Naqvi.
Karam Kiya Hai Youn Mujh Par Sana-e-Zahra Ne.
Nazar Mein Rakha Hai Mujh Ko Khuda-e-Zahra Ne.
Nabi Pe Aya-e-Tatheer Ho Gayi Nazil.
Nabi Pe Saya Kiya Jab Kisaye Zahra Ne.
Yeh Arsh Walon Se Pocho Keh Kaisa Zaiqa Hai.
Safar Kiya Hai Wahan Tak Ghizaye Zahra Ne.
Ali Lagatay Hain Seene Se Roz Chom Ke Woh.
Jo Kurtay Ghazi Ki Khatir Banaye Zahra Ne.
Hamari Khak Mein Shamil Hai Khak-e-Karb-o-Bala.
Banaya Matami Hum Ko Dua-e-Zahra Ne.
Karey Na Kaisay Woh Quran Ki Zabaan Mein Kalam.
Tamam Soray Hain Jis Ko Parhaye Zahra Ne.
Bana Le Sona Ya Chandi Yeh Fizza Ki Marzi.
Ossay Hain Mojzay Karne Sikhaye Zahra Ne.
Unhi Ke Sadqay Mujhe Rizq Mil Raha Hai Asad.
Niwalay Hathoon Se Jin Ko Khilaye Zahra Ne.
