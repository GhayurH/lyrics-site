Ab Aaye Ho Baba

Baba agar aana hi tha qaliq ki reza say us waqt na aaye
Jab khaak pe dam torh raha tha mera Akbar
Ab aaye ho baba.

Jab farsh-e-zameen baam-e-falak larza fishaan thay
Us waqt kaha thay
Jab baap ke chullu mein tha khoon-e-Ali Asghar
Ab aaye ho baba.

Jab bhai ka saar kaat ta tha main dekh rahi thi
Hazrath ko sada di
Sar kholay hue roti thi mai khaimein ke dar par
Ab aaye ho baba.

Jab log bacha le gaye lashay shohda kay
Haq apna jata kay
Bas ek tan-e-Shabbir tha pamal ki zad par
Ab aaye ho baba.

Jab Shaam ke qazaaq hamein loot rahay thay
Khaimon ko jala kay
Ab aagaye hotein to na chin ti meri chadar
Ab aaye ho baba.

Kya aapnay firdous say ye dekha na hoga
Kya hashr bapa tha
Jab pusht say beemar ki khaincha gaya bistar
Ab aaye ho baba.

Shahid ruq-e-Haider pe bhi bikhar jaatay thay aason
Jab khol ke gesun
Chillati thi Zainab mere baba meri chadar
Ab aaye ho baba.
