Nana Teri Zainab Ne Woh Dard Uthaye Hain Hasan Sadiq.
Zalim Ka Zulm Dekha Aanso Nikal Aaye Hain.

1
Main Sham Ki Galiyon Se Sar Nangay Guzar Aayi.
Nana Teri Ummat Ne Din Yeh Bhi Dikhaye Hain.
Pathar Jo Barastay They Zahra Ke Gharanay Par.
Iss Haal Pe Soraj Ne Khud Ashk Bahaye Hain.

2
Yeh Khon Bhara Kurta Hai Tere Nawasay Ka.
Bhai Ki Nishani Hum Tere Liye Laye Hain.
Gham Kha Gaya Zainab Ko Nana Ali Akbar Ka.
Mat Pocho Watan Mein Hum Kis Tarha Se Aaye Hain.

3
Zindaan Ke Andherey Mein Neend Aayi Sakina Ko.
Hum Qabar Sakina Ki Waan Chor Ke Aaye Hain.
Sogat-e-Safar Nana Pardais Se Hum Qaidi.
Yeh Neel Rasan Ke Hain Jo Shano Pe Laye Hain.
