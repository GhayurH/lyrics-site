Haey ye bachchi kon hay
Yeh to Sakina lagti hay

1
Chayi hay jis par be basi

2
Jati hay maqtal ki taraf
Dekha hay kyun soey Najaf
Kia is ka baba hay Ali

3
Aati hay jo roti hui
Sadmo se jan khoti hui
Parhti hui nad-e-Ali
Ye to Sakina lagti hay
Ro ro ke jaan deti hay ye
Yun siskian leti hay ye
Kia mar gaya is ka koi
Ye to Sakina lagti hay
Shano se kyun khoon hay rawan
Uf yeh tamachon ke nishan
Ye zulm aur ye kamsini
Ye to Sakina lagti hay
Kausar ye maqtal ki fiza
Tarrpa rahi hay dil mera
Tari hay gham ki teeragi
Ye to Sakina lagti hay
