1
Zakhmo Se Choor Choor Tha Jab Fatima Ka Chain.
Madar Ko Dekha Our Yeh Karne Laga Yeh Bain.
Amma Meri Amma Main Bohat Pyasa Hoon.
Main Bohat Pyasa Hoon.
Amma Main Bohat Pyasa Hoon.
Maqtal Mein Bikhrey Hain Lashay.
Sab Teen Din Ke Hain Pyasay.
Pardais Mein Ujra Ghar Mera.
Iss Dam Labon Pe Hain Nalay.
Amma Idhar Tu Dekho Zara.
Main Ne Basai Karb-o-Bala.
Waday Nibhaye Bachpan Ke.
Yeh Marsiya Hai Pyasay Ka.
Bali Sakina Roti Hai.
Munh Aansoon Se Dhoti Hai.
Bethi Hoi Hai Zair-e-Alam.
Baba Ka Noha Sunti Hai.
Abbas Baqi Na Akbar.
Maqtal Mein Sotay Hain Asghar.
Aoun-o-Muhammad Our Qasim.
Bichrey Hain Mere Sab Dilbar.
Zainab Hai Khaimay Ke Dar Par.
Woh Dekh Na Le Gham Ka Manzar.
Jao Sanbhalo Zainab Ko.
Roti Hai Noha Yeh Sun Kar.
Tanha Lara Hoon Lakhon Se.
Guzra Hoon Sab Ke Lashon Se.
Bas Reh Gaya Hai Ik Sajdah.
Zakhmi Howa Hoon Teeron Se.
Aye Laeeq Maula Ka Noha.
 Mazhar Ke Lafzoon Ka Pursa.
Ahl-e-Azza Hain Matam Mein.
Iss Baat Par Hai Yeh Giriya.
