1
Jo Dhondne Tumhein Maqtal Mein Aaon Gi Baba.
Howa Jo Sham-e-Ghareebaan Bataon Gi Baba.
Abhi Main Choti Hoon Nanhay Se Hath Hain Mere.
Main Ghoop Andherey Mein Nad-e-Ali Ko Parh Parh Ke.

2
Tumhari Lash Se Pathar Hataon Gi Baba.
Yeh Dur Jo Aap Ne Bakhshey Utaray Jaein Gay.
Tamachay Aap Ki Beti Ko Marey Jaein Gay.
Main Neelay Gaal Chacha Ko Dikhaon Gi Baba.

3
Safar Ki Terah Ko Milney Jo Mujh Se Aao Gay.
Karo Yeh Wadah Mujhe Sath Le Ke Jao Gay.
Hai Ab Yaqeen Madine Na Jaon Gi Baba.
Jo Aag Khaimon Mein Le Ke Laeen Aaein Gay.

4
Tu Apni Qeemati Chezein Haram Bachaein Gay.
Main Jaltay Khaimon Se Jhola Uthaon Gi Baba.
Na Sar Pe Hath Youn Rakhiye Jo Dekh Le Ga Shimar.
Pakar Ke Baloon Se Mujh Ko Uthaye Ga Aa Kar.

5
Laeen Se Baal Main Kaisay Choraon Gi Baba.
 Khizar Ka Maan Ho Baba Karam Yeh Farma Do.
Najaf Hai Pas Tu Dada Ke Pas Pohancha Do.
Wafa Main Kofay Ki Unn Ko Sunaon Gi Baba.
