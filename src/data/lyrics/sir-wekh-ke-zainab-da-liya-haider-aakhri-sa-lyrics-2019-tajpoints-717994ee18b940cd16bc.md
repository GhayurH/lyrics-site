1
Sir Wekh Ke Zainab Da Liya Haider Aakhri Sa.
Sanwaan Muk Gayiyaan Nazraan Ruk Gayiyaan Rihya Takda Pak Rida.
Rondiyaan Aakhiya Babay Ne Ruqaiya, Ghazi.
Yaad Aye Rakhiyo Keh Babay Di Nayi Shahi Baqi.
Dowaan Hun Tusaan Zahra Di Dhiyaan Da Dena Sath Sada.
Kadi Zainab Diyaan Bawaan Te Si Bosay Dainda.
Kadi Shabir Di Gardan Si Oh Chum Ke Ronda.
Piyo Di Jandiyaan Halat Wekh Ke Ray Ronday Bhain Bhira.
Hun Janazay Layi Lagiyaan Ne Qataraan Jiwein.
Fair Pathraan Layi Lawan Gay Qataraan Ewein.
Ronday Ne Jithay Hasan Gaye Othay Zainab De Rook Ke Rah.
Mojza Koi Hasan Kar Ke Hawawaan Rokay.
Char Dewari De Andar Hi Sadawaan Rokay.
Zainab Roway Te Kofay Choon Koi Na Sun Le Wain Aydha.
Baal Saqlain Jeray Ronday Howay Aaye Si.
Kash Kofay De Yateema Che Aye Kehda Main V.
Palan Waliya Bohe Te Tere Aaya Pursa Dain Tera.
