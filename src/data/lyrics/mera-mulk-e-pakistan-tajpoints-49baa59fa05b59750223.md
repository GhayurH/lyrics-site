Meri Jan-o-Emaan Hai Mera Mulk-e-Pakistan.
La-Ilaha Ka Unwaan Hai Mera Mulk-e-Pakistan.

1
Muhammad Ali Fatima Ne Jab Chaha Yeh Kam Kiya.
Inhi Ka Tu Ahsaan Hai Mera Mulk-e-Pakistan.
Hai Yeh Pak Nisbat Jo Iss Ki Dekho Chodah 14 Se.
Tab Hi Pak Sataan Hai Mera Mulk-e-Pakistan.

2
Sunni Ho Keh Shia Ho, Ho Maseeh Ya Hindu.
Aik Sab Ka Elaan Hai Mera Mulk-e-Pakistan.
Sindhi Ho Ke Balti Ho, Ghilgati Ya Punjabi.
Aik Sab Ki Pehchaan Hai Mera Mulk-e-Pakistan.

3
Wadiyaan Shamali Woh Our Nizare Kashmiri.
Ik Saja Gulsitaan Hai Mera Mulk-e-Pakistan.
 Yousufi Ki Hai Yeh Dua Ta-Abad Rahe Aabad.
Kyunke Sab Ka Armaan Hai Mera Mulk-e-Pakistan.
