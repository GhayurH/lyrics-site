Shabeer Zamaanay Ko Ata Kon Karega

Zainab Ke Siwa Kaar e Khuda, Kon Karega

Darkaar Hai Mazloom Aseeron Ko Rihaai

Ye Kaam Sakina Ke, Siwa Kon Karega

Sab Jaatey Hain Zindaan Se Ay Baali Sakina

Roshan Teri Turbat Pe, Diya Kon Karega

Bhaiyon Ke Mazaarat Pe Bibi Ne Kaha Tha

Ab Tum Se Ulajhne Ki, Khata Kon Karega

Sar Tunn Se Juda Hotay Hi Zainab Ye Pukaari

Quraan Bhala Humse, Juda Kon Karega

Ye Khutba e Zainab Ki Sada Goonjh Rahi Hai

Hai Kon Sitamgaar, Jafa Kon Karega

Sadiyon Se Isi Soch Mai Gum Karbobala Thi

Hoon Khaak Mujhe Khaak e, Shifa Kon Karega
