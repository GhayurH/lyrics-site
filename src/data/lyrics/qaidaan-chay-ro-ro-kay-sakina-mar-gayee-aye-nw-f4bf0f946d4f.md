Qaidaan Chay Ro Ro Kay, Sakina Mar Gayee Aye

Sir Lay jaooo, ayday babay da, Chup kar gayee Aye

Aylaan hoya aye, Shaam walay zindaana wich

Bara rondi see, bachee jayree, O mar gayee aye

Saawaa noo rehaee, mil gayee aye per ainoo naee

Kado laash ajee, zindaan wichoo, bahar gaee aye

Poochda aye reya O, Shaamiyaa noo Shabbir da Sir

Kyu rondi naee, kithay aj meri, dukhtar gayee aye

Meri Saanj muka gayee, aye Baba Baqir aakhay

meray hissay dee, Zainab safraa, wich mar gayee aye

Baykafan behen dee, qabr uttay, Abid aakhay

mil shaamiyaa tau, wapas teri, chadar gayee aye

Saqlain tamachay, khaandi raee, per haari naee

aye zulm tau naee, babay day pyaar, tau har gayee aye
