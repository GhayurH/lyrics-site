1
Haider e Karrar Kufay Main Haider Rizvi.
Hoye Hain Aaj Zakhmi Haider-e-Karrar Kofay Mein.
Saron Ko Peettay Hain Sare Roza Daar Kofay Mein.
Hasan Rotay Hain Our Shabbir Gash Kha Kha Ke Girtay Hain.
Boka Kartay Hain Abbas-e-Alambardar Kofay Mein.
Rasool ALLAH Ke Dil Ke Bhi Tukray Kar Diye Oss Ne.
Chali Hai Zehar Mein Dobi Jo Aik Talwar Kofay Mein.
Fogaan Karta Hai Mimber Khon Mein Mehrab Dobi Hai.
Khuda Ke Ghar Ayyaan Hain Hashar Ke Aasaar Kofay Mein.
Baqi Se Sada Deti Hoi Haye Mere Wali.
Chali Aayi Hain Bint-e-Ahmad-e-Mukhtar Kofay Mein.
Ali Marey Gaye Har Ghar Mein Hota Hai Yehi Noha.
Bicha Hai Farsh-e-Gham Har Dil Hai Matamdar Kofay Mein.
Yeh Bachay Doodh Ke Pyalay Uthaye Kehte They Ro Kar.
Yateemo Ko Na Mil Paye Ga Aisa Pyar Kofay Mein.
Ali, Zainab Ki Chadar Chom Kar Ro Ro Ke Kehte They.
Phir Aaein Gay Tumhein Aada Sar-e-Bazar Kofay Mein.
Saron Par Khak Hai Aankhon Mein Aanso Hath Seeno Par.
Uthaye Hain Janaza Khuld Ke Sardar Kofay Mein.
 Hasan Aagaz Hai Ikeeswin Ramzan Ki Shab Tu.
Ali Ki Beti Dekhey Gi Bohat Aazar Kofay Mein.
