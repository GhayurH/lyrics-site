1
Hussain Nu Gham Shehar Shehar Chey Nadeem Sarwar.
Hussain Nu Gham Shehar Shehar Chey Hussaini Matam Ghali Ghali Chey.
Aa Gham Ne Dil Ne Basaway Ainay Dua-e-Zahra Mari Gayi Chey.
Niharo Tareekh Na Warq Ne Keh Karbala Ke Woh Mojzoon Chey.
Hati Faqat Be Peharni Wato Keh Jeene Sadiyon Thayi Gayi Chey.
Howay Tu Dunya Amo Ne Samjhay Howay Tu Dunya Amo Ne Janrey.
Amara Aaqa Hussaini Maula Amara Maula Ali Ali Chey.
Hunain-o-Badar-o-Ohad Ne Khandaq Larai Khaybar Ni Keh Rahi Chey.
Bohadari Maa Ali Na Samay Batao Koni Barabari Chey.
Furat Opar Kari Ne Qabzo Na Bond Panrin Jerien Paidho.
Wafa-e-Abbas So Wafa Chey Furat Sajdah Kari Rahi Chey.
Jawan Akbar Na Dil Thi Barchi Ne Kadhi Maula Radi Rehya Chey.
Salam Sabar-e-Hussain Tum Ne Jigar Maa Barchi Rahi Gayi Chey.
Nihari Asghar Ne Rooz-e-Mehshar Ajab Na Thi Khud Khuda Kahey Panr.
Rubab Tari Mata Ki Qeemat Bahisht Pori Adhori Thayi Chey.
Jarik Dhemay Thi Bolo Zainab Jarik Dhemay Jagaro Zainab.
Keh Lash Maidaan Maa Nihari Sakina Bibi Dari Gayi Chey.
Meri Jo Zindan Thi Rehai Ali Ne Potari Rari Ne Boli.
Keh Mara Bhai Ni Ik Nishani Aa Qaid Khana Rahi Gayi Chey.
Qabar Na Andhkar Thi Munawar Na Khof Kar Jay Rehan-o- Sarwar.
Keh Zikar-e-Sarwar Na Sadqay Tujh Par Ahinjay Janat Mari Gayi Chey.
