Taboot Hai Yeh Logo Damad e Mustafa Ka.
Masjid Mein Khoon Baha Hai Haye Dein Ke Rehnuma Ka.

1
Maiyyat Se Lipat Kar Youn Zainab Ne Kaha Ro Kar.
Dekho Hai Yehi Manzar Aagaz-e-Karbala Ka.
Zahra Ke Lal Zakhmi Baba Ko Utha Laye.
Rang Surkh Ho Gaya Hai Hasnain Ki Abba Ka.

2
Kulsoom, Ruqaiya Our Zainab Hai Mehway Matam.
Ashkon Se Tar Hai Chehra Abbas Bawafa Ka.
Hain Shams-o-Qamar Roye Kuhram Farishton Mein.
Horoon Ne Kiya Matam Misdaq-e-Hal Atta Ka.

3
Quraan Ke Aansoon Ki Barsat Keh Rahi Hai.
Dekho Hai Khoon Mein Doba Chehra Kul Aasifa Ka.
Aye Lal Kahon Ga Yeh Mehshar Ko Khuda Se Main.
Loota Hai Kalma Go Ne Ghar Fatima Zahra Ka.
