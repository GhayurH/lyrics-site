Shabir Tere Gham Ko Manaein Gaye Azadar.
Zahra Se Kiya Wadah Nibhaein Gaye Azadar.

1
Bomb Walon Se Darte Nahi Mula Tere Ashiq.
Har Haal Mein Karb-o-Bala Jaein Gaye Azadar.
Aye Ibn-e-Ali Aap Ke Darbar Mein Aa Kar.
Pholon Ki Tarha Dil Bhi Charhaein Gaye Azadar.

2
Nasloon Mein Rahe Gi Yeh Teri Taziyadari.
Bachoon Ko Azadar Banaein Gaye Azadar.
Kar Kar Ke Bapa Majlis-e-Gham Aap Ki Maula.
Maqsad Tera Dunya Ko Batein Gaye Azadar.

3
Mehdi Jo Banaein Gaye Baqaiya Ko Dobara.
Chadar Wahan Ashkon Ki Charhaein Gaye Azadar.
Bidat Jo Batatay Hain Gham-e-Shah-e-Huda Ko.
Lanat Ka Unhein Tauq Pinhaein Gaye Azadar.

4
Haan Yaad Mein Karbal Ke Pyason Ki Hamesha.
Pani Ki Sabeelon Ko Lagaein Gaye Azadar.
Jannat Nahi Alam Gham-e-Shabir Ki Qeemat.
Mehshar Mein Silla Our Bhi Paein Gaye Azadar.
