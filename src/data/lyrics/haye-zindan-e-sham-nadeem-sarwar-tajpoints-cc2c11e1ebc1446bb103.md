Haye Zindan-e-Sham Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics
Meri Tu Aas Mere Seene Mein Mar Jati Hai.

1
Dekh Iss Qaid Se Kab Milti Hai Rukhsat Amma.
Be Basi Kaisi Hai Yeh Kaisi Hai Ghurbat Amma.
Kya Banay Gi Issi Zindan Mein Turbat Amma.
Kehna Baba Se Khuda Hafiz-o-Nasir Baba.
Lo Keh Ab Hoti Hoon Zindan Se Rukhsat Amma.
Ab Tu Din Mein Bhi Ujalon Se Mulaqaat Nahi.
Jo Tarap Kar Na Guzari Ho Koi Raat Nahi.
Kya Karon Jee Ke Chacha Meray, Mere Pas Nahi.
Do Dua Khatam Ho Ab Jald Musibat Amma.
Ab Sar-e-Sham Parindon Pe Nazar Jati Hai.
Aisi Makhlooq Hai Urtay Hoye Ghar Jati Hai.

2
Dekh Iss Qaid Se Kab Milti Hai Rukhsat Amma.
Tum Inhi Kapron Ko Aye Amma Bana Dena Kafan.
Ho Nahi Saktay Yeh Tabdeel Keh Zakhmi Hai Badan.
Baba Aa Jaein Tu Keh Dena Unhein Mera Sukhan.
Apne Baba Ki Na Kar Payi Main Khidmat Amma.
Mere Baba Ka Woh Seena Meri Amma La Do.
Jo Tha Jeene Ka Qareena Meri Amma La Do.
Jo Tha Aaram-e-Sakina Meri Amma La Do.
Iss Se Pehle Keh Ho Ab Maut Se Qurbat Amma.
Ho Sakay Tu Ali Asghar Ka Woh Jhola La Do.
Amma Bhaiyya Ka Mere Mujh Ko Shaloka La Do.
Woh Jo Taweez Tha Mannat Ka Khudara La Do.
Mujh Ko Phir Marne Mein Howay Gi Na Diqqat Amma.
Thehar Jao Keh Khuda Hafiz-o-Nasir Amma.
Sath Deti Nahi Ab Bali Sakina Ki Zabaan.
Keh Do Zalim Se Keh Ab Khatam Hoi Aah-o-Fogaan.
Aah Karne Ki Bhi Baqi Nahi Taqat Amma.
Lab-e- Sarwar Se Jo Rehan Ka Noha Aaya.
Sham Ke Zindaan Se Aane Lagi Aawaz-e-Boka.
Jaisay Bachi Ka Koi Zakhm Howa Phir Taza.
Boli Kyun Ahl-e-Azza Kartay Hain Riqqat Amma.
