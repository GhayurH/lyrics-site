Zahe Muqaddar Syed Mudassir Mehdi

1
Zahe Muqadar Hazoor-e-Haq Se Salam Aaya Pyam Aaya.
Jhukao Nazrein Bichao Palkein Adab Ka Aala Maqam Aaya.
Hai Dein Nabi Ka Musheer Haider Chala Rahi Hain Nizam Zahra.
Hussain Pani Pila Rahe Hain Hasan Ke Sadqay Taam Aaya.
Utha Ke Godi Mein Mustafa Ne Ali Ko Bint-e-Asad Se Bola.
Bohat Mubarak Bohat Mubarak Zameen Pe Pehla Imam Aaya.

2
Kahan Main Khaki Kahan Woh Nori Kahan Woh Zahra Ka Ism-e-Azam.
Qaseeda Zahra Ka Parh Raha Tha Farishta Behar-e-Salam Aaya.
Pukara Deen-e-Nabi Ne Jis Dam Madad Ko Aaye Khudara Koi.
Yahan Se Sarwar Ne Bola Kunba Nisar Karne Tamam Aaya.
Yeh Kehna Aaqa Bohat Se Ashiq Taraptay Se Chor Aaya Hoon Main.
Bulaway Ke Muntazir Hain Lekin Na Subh-o-Aaya Na Sham Aaya.
