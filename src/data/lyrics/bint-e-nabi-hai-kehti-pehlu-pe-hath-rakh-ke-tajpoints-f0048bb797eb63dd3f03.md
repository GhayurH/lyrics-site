1
Bint-e-Nabi Hai Kehti Pehlu Pe Hath Rakh Ke.
Muslim Ko Koi Aa Ke Bacha Le Kofiyon Se.
Haye Reh Gaya Hai Tanha Kofay Mein Mera Muslim.
Begana Bewatan Hai Koi Panah Tu De Dey.

2
Hai Para Kiya Tum Ne Ab Yeh Jigar Nabi Ka.
Muslim Pe War Kab Hai War Yeh Nabi Pe.
Kya Haal Hai Ghurbat Ka Muslim Pe Aaya Logo.
Charon Taraf Hain Zalim Ke Waar Yeh Jafa Ke.

3
Ik Eid Pe Haider Tu Ik Par Liya Muslim.
Do Baar Rulaya Hai Zahra Ko Kofiyon Ne.
Muslim Ka Badan Farman Zakhmo Se Choor Hai.
Kaisa Mila Ummat Se Yeh Ajar Nabi Ko Hai.
