Raitaanch dul gaye, mityaanch rul gaye, maa nu rawavay, Qasim di mehndi.

1
Kubra da baba chadar vicha kay, chun chun kay pavay, Qasim di mehndi.
1) Khaiyyam andar jadoon mehndi aaye banray di maa nay sir khaak paaye
Banree da baba behni bulaakay, ro ro vakhaa vay

2
2) Maqtal cho Syed ey vain paaya kallah hay nana Zahra da jayaa
Kausar kinaaray mairay turgay saray, kayra uthavay

3
3) Behni di ghatree Zainab utha kay Farwah nu aakhay ohday kowl jaakay
Putaraan da sadma Akbar da sadma, mainu bhula vay

4
4) Bachra Hassan da pamaal hoya Karbobala da rang laal hoya
Shabbir kewain maqtal cho puri, khaimay pocha vay

5
5) Kuj mehndi gohvay summach lay gay kuj eiday tukray maqtalch ray gay
Syed musafir Qasim di maa ton, kewain lukaa vay

6
6) Putraan nu lokhoon sehray day laa O Qasim di pehlaa majalis kara o
Banray nu aakho shagnaa day vailay, Oh din nah aavay

7
7) Tauqeer oh day tu sain raazi Mazloom-e-Karbal di bhain raazi
Qasim di turbat dil vich waasa kay, jeyra sajaa vay
