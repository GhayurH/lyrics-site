Safar Phophiyaan Di Peshi De Sonanre Write Up
Safar Phophiyaan Di Peshi De Sonanre Pay Gaye Menu Mir Hasan Mir.
Chupanre San Zakhm Jairey Wakhanre Pay Gaye Menu.
Kadon Koi Veer Zinda Si Mera Lasha Uthawan Layi.
Meri Ghurbat Ke Janglaan Wich Jadoon Koi Baal Mariya Si.
Janaze Apne Phophiyaan Taun Chukanre Pay Gaye Menu.
Bazar Ech Apni Phophiyaan Layi Kithon Chacha Main Lay Aanda.
Odhi Banwaan Taun Sadqe Kis Tarha Abbas Banr Janda.
Bara Roya Jadoon Majme Hatanre Pay Gaye Menu.
Jigar Akbar Da Naize Taun Choraya Mere Babe Ne.
Mosibat Hoor Ki Honri Aye Safraan Wich Sakina De.
Shimar Tu Waal Khud Aapi Choranre Pay Gaye Menu.
Hoi Jad Sang Bari Te Meri Bhina Wichar Gayiyaan.
Bara Ghayoor Haan Lekin Sadawaan Derniyaan Paiyaan.
Bazar Ech Naam Bhaina De Bulanre Pay Gaye Menu.
Kithe Pehchan Na Lay Hind Aye Zainab Aye Zahra Di.
Hanera Sham Wale Qaid Khane Da Wadhawan Layi.
Jairay Door Chaar Deway San Bhujanre Pay Gaye Menu.
Aye Wain Abid De San Akbar Bhira Jay Koi Na Bachiya.
Kithe Jhola Kithe Sehra Musalla Kidre Main Rakhiya.
Ewein Dadi De Chali Ghar Wasanre Pay Gaye Menu.
