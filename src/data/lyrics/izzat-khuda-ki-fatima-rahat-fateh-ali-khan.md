Izzat Khuda Ki Fatima Bint-e-Nabi Batool Hain
Woh Jis Ke Ayhteram Mein Dar Pe Kharey Rasool Hain

1
Sajday Karey Hazar Tu Dil Mein Ho Bughz-e-Fatima
Janat Mein Tu Bhi Jaye Ga Yeh Sochna Fazool Hai
Hai Jo Binaye LaIlaha Khair-Al-Nisa Ka Lal Hai
Jis Ke Liye Nabi Diya Sajday Ko Rehlay Tool Hai

2
Har Shey Do Jahan Ki Unn Ko Jaheiz Mein Mili
Mujh Ko Qasam Khuda Ki Fidak Unn Ke Qadam Ki Dhool Hai
