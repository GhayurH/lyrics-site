1
Chor Ke Zainab Ko Na Jana Baba Ali Abbas Khaku.
Chor Ke Rota Youn Zainab Ko Na Jana Baba.
Gham Se Phat Jaye Ga Zainab Ka Kaleja Baba.
Aap Ki Jan Mein Hi Jan Thi Iss Beti Ki.
Aap Ke Baad Bhala Chain Se Kya Jee Loon Gi.
Ab Musayab Mein Mera Guzrey Ga Jeena Baba.
Uth Ke Ik Bar Lo Kofay Ke Yateemo Ki Khabar.
Kozay Hathoon Mein Liye Kehte Hain Yeh Ro Ro Kar.
Kaun Kofay Mein Hai Ab Unn Ka Sahara Baba.
Roti Behno Ko Janazay Se Hatatay Hain Hasan.
Our Jab Daltay Hain Baba Ke Chehray Pe Kafan.
Behnein Gash Kha Ke Yeh Kehti Hain Na Jana Baba.
Dekh Kar Dil Unhein Behno Ka Tarap Jata Hai.
Rona Youn Bhaiyon Ka Behno Ko Batlata Hai.
Ab Rulaye Ga Humein Sara Zamana Baba.
Aap Ka Qatal Tu Oss Din Wahin Par Ho Gaya Tha.
Meri Maa Zahra Ka Jis Din Howa Zakhmi Chehra.
Kha Gaya Aap Ko Haye Woh Tamacha Baba.
Aap Ke Sar Ki Tarha Zakhmi Mera Sar Ho Ga.
Mera Chehra Bhi Issi Kofay Mein Youn Tar Ho Ga.
Khon Se Jaisay Hai Tar Aap Ka Chehra Baba.
Ab Ali Un Wali Ullah Ki Khatir Main Bhi.
Har Sitam Fatima Zahra Ki Tarha Seh Loon Gi.
Chahe Darbar Paray Mujh Ko Bhi Jana Baba.
Ik Qayamat Woh Asad Ujray Hoye Ghar Mein Thi.
Khak Oss Ghar Ki Thi Jo Betiyon Ke Sar Mein Thi.
Noha Tha Zainab-o-Kulsoom Ka Baba Baba.
