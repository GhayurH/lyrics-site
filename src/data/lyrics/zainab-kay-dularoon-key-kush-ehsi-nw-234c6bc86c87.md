Zainab Kay Dularoon Key Kush Ehsi Shahadat Hay

-Kurbani-e-Humsheer’ kay jazboon say ibarat hay

-Kartay hain jo yay matham Hum Aun-o-Mohammad ka

Yay sunnat-e-Zahra hay mehraj-e-shahadat hay

-Haidar kay nawasay hain, Jafar kay hain yay pothay

Dono say mili in ko, virsay may shu ja aat hay

-Mamoon jo ijazat dayn ada ko bata dayna

In nanay say hathoon may Haidar key si thaqat hay

-Rukh karna na thum dono pani key tharaf deakho

Yay Aun-o-Muhammad ko, Madar key nasihat hay

-Kartay hain bapa hum jo zanjeer’ ka yay matam

Is ka hay yaqin hum ko yay ayn-e-ibadat hay

-Ay arzo sama tu nay wo wakt’ bhi deaka hay

Jab kehtay tay sheh roh kar, Yay kehsay qayamat hay

-Tha umr karain girya hum aun-o-muhammad par

Zainab’ ko hay yay pursa, Shabbir’ key nusrat hay

-Measum ka hay yay pehro, iss was’tay Ashhar’ ko

Na khauf’ hay dozakh ka, na kahish-e-jannat’ hay
