Meri Shohrat Zamane Mein Hai Ko Ba Ko.
Mere Bare Mein Karta Hai Rab Guftugu.

1
Qalab-e-Bint-e-Payamber Ki Hoon Aarzu.
Munkir-e-Shah-e-Deen Ab Bhi Samjha Na Tu.
Bugz Ka Apni Se Parda Hata.
Phir Chale Ga Meri Manzilat Ka Pata.

2
Mere Sar Pe Hai Taj-e-Nishan-e-Qama.
Dag-e-Matam Hain Seene Pe Jalwa Numa.
Hai Baray-e-Azza Yeh Mere Dast-o-Pa.
Mustanad Hoon Meri Peet Dekho Zara.

3
Kyun Na Hasrat se Dekhein Mujhe Anbiya.
Hai Misal-e-Tabaruk Sarapa Mera.
Khuwakhir-e-Shah-e-Deen Zainab-e-Ghumzada.
Syed-us-Sajedeen Abid-e-Be-Khata.

4
Our Jumla Aseeran-e-Karb-o-Bala.
Hum Ne Seekha Hai In Sab Se Matam Hai Kya.
Kaise Karte Hain Matam Bataya Humein.
Har Saleeqa Azza Ka Sikhaya Humein.

5
Nazriyati Safoon Mein Jo Paykar Hai.
Dushman-e-Deen Ki Sazish Ka Izhar Hai.
Iss Ke Chakar Mein Aana Hi Be-Kar Hai.
Baat Samjhe Ga Woh Jo Samajhdar Hai.

6
Main Zamane Ki Baton Se Razi Nahi.
Kaun Kehta Hai Keh Main Namazi Nahi.
Kar Raha Hoon Main Yasir Riwayat Raqam.
Umm-e-Salma Se Bole Rasool-e-Ummam.

7
Rab Yeh Kehta Hai Qaum Ik Utarein Gaye Hum.
Jo Manay Gi Shabir-o-Shabar Ka Gham.
Haisiyat Un Ki Ho Gi Azadar Ki.
Woh Shifat Karein Gaye Gunahegar Ki.
