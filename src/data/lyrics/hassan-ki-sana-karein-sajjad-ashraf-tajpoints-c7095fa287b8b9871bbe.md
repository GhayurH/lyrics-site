Hassan Ki Sana Karein Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi Write Up
Jo Chahtey Hain Khair Se Har Shar Juda Karein.
Le Kar Khuda Ka Naam Hasan Ki Sana Karein.
Wajib Hai Ahl-e-Sham Pe Sulh-e-Hasan Ke Baad.
Taj-e-Shahi Ko Bheek Ka Kasa Kaha Karein.
Tehreer Sulha Nama Hai Ya Fard-e-Jurm Hai.
Shartein Hasan Ki Parh Ke Kabhi Tabsara Karein.
Taqdeer Oss Ko Kehti Hai Tehreer-e-Mujtaba.
Jis Ke Qalam Se Loh-o-Qalam Mashwara Karein.
Shabar Duaein Kartay They Qasim Ka Ho Zahoor.
Zainab Yeh Chahti Hain Hasan Karbala Karein.
Samjho Hasan Ko Mannay Walay Ghulam Hain.
Bag-e-Fidak Se Baat Ki Jo Ibtida Karein.
Hasrat Hai Ho Imam-e-Zamaan Aap Ka Zahoor.
Hum Janat Al-Baqee Mein Pursa Ada Karein.
Madh-e-Hasan Ko Aya-e-Tatheer Jan Kar.
 Akbar Darood Awwal-o-Aakhir Parha Karein.
