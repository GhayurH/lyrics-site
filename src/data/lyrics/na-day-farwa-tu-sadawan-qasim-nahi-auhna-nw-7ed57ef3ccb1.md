Na Day Farwa Tu Sadawan Qasim Nahi Auhna

Na vaikhai ker hurn ravan Qasim Nahi Auhna Nahi Auhna

Fermaya paak Hussain Nai Kero Bibiyo Raj Kai Vairn ai

Sermaya veer Hassan da Zahra dai dil da chain ai

Gaiya maraiya wich sehra waan Qasim Nahi Auhna Nahi Auhna

Sehrai dai phul mur jayain da nai dai rang badlain

Qasim di laash dai tukrai jadon khaiamin tai wich aayai

Kita Matam puphiyan mavaan Qasim Nahi Auhna Nahi Auhna

Fermandian Rondian Akhiyan Hai Chalar’n havavan tatiyan

Qasim dai hoyai tukrai keevai vikhriyan puhl di an’ patiyan

Enj puttar na vaikh’rn mavaan Qasim Nahi Auhna Nahi Auhna

Kadi vaalan nu main khowan kadi puttar di aas’ich rovan

Merai laal dai hath nahi labdai kithai layi main mehndi gholan

Ohday keerai shagan manavan Qasim Nahi Auhna Nahi Auhna
