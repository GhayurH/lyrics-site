Itna muqa ali akber ko qaza ne na diya
Maa’n tadpti rhi dulha bhi bnane na diya

Jane kiya soch ke seene se ali akber ne
Hath baba ko bohot der htane na diya

Lashe akber pa ye kehti thi tadap kr laila
Fayeda koi tujhe maa’n ki Dua ne na diya

Le gyi cheen ke baba se ajal akber ko
Khat bhi humsheer ka bhai ko sunane na diya

Baap sharmindah hai is aalam e ghurbat ne mujhe
Chand qatre tujhe paani bhi pilane na diya

Lag gayi mout ki bhar por jawani me nazar
Haye taqdeer ne sehra bhi sjaane na diya

Mout se sirf rahega ye gila akber ko
Jo tha sughra se wo wada bhi nibhane na diya

Kese utthega zaifee me janaza iska
Baap ko jisne kbhi ranj uthane na diya

Lag gaya chand ko laila ke gehn maqtal me
Maa’n ke armano ko prwaan chdhane na diya

Keh ke roti thi yehi Aamir o Akhter ik maa’n
Kon sa zakhm mujhe karb o bala ne na diya
