1
Kaisay Milta Hai Khuda Dekh Liya Hai Main Ne.
Ja Ke Khud Karb-o-Bala Dekh Liya Hai Main Ne.
Tere Khaimay Ka Diya Dekh Liya Hai Main Ne.
Kitni Be Bas Hai Howa Dekh Liya Hai Main Ne.

2
Roz Kashkool Uthaye Chala Aata Hoon Hussain.
Tera Darwaza Khula Dekh Liya Hai Main Ne.
Haza Min Fazl-e-Hussain Ibn-e-Ali-o-Zahra.
Hur Ke Mathay Pe Likha Dekh Liya Hai Main Ne.

3
Tere Sadqay Mein Aye Abbas-e-Dilawar Ke Alam.
Kaisay Chalti Hai Howa Dekh Liya Hai Main Ne.
Samne Karta Hai Beta Mera Sheh Ka Matam.
Kya Hai Anjam Mera Dekh Liya Hai Main Ne.

4
Mushkilein Janein Our Ab Jane Alamdar-e-Hussain.
Janib-e-Karb-o-Bala Dekh Liya Hai Main Ne.
Wahab Kehta Tha Main Kalma Na Parhon Kyun Maula.
Teri Aankhon Mein Khuda Dekh Liya Hai Main Ne.

5
Turbat-e-Fatima Zahra Pe Kiya Hai Matam.
Khuwab Tu Qad Se Bara Dekh Liya Hai Main Ne.
Ali Akbar Meri Imdad Ko Aaye Akbar.
Kis Ko Kehte Hain Atta Dekh Liya Hai Main Ne.
