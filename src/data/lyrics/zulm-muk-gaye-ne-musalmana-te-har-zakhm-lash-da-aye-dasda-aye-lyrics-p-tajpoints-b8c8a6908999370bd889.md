Zulm Muk Gaye Ne Musalmana Te Har Zakhm Lash Da Aye Dasda Aye.
Jurm Kehra Aye Mosa Kazim Da Lash Zanjeeraan Wich Koi Rakhda Aye.

1
Sir Te Rakh Ke Quran Das Mainu Ais Taun Wadh Kehra Musayab Aye.
Lash Dafnan Taun Kadi Pehlaan Koi Waris Lohar Labda Aye.
Jehray Zanjeer Paye Qaidi Ne Oh V Mazloom Naal Mar Gaye Ne.
Kafan De Ke Haye Kariyaan Beriyaan Nu Kaun Maiyyat De Naal Dabda Aye.

2
Zehar Miliya Te Aakhri Wari Millan Dhiyaan Nu Maula Aaya Aye.
Aye Ziyarat Way Aakhri Baba Sariyaan Dhiyaan Nu Aye Lagda Aye.
Sut Ke Aaye Ne Lash Maula Di Jhuk Ke Rakhna Qabool Nayi Kita.
Le Ke Yasrab Taun Kazmain Taein Kaun Lashaan Te Zulm Karda Aye.
