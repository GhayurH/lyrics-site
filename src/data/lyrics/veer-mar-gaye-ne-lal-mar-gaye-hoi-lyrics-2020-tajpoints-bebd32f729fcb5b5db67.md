1
Veer Mar Gaye Ne Lal Mar Gaye Hoi Qaidan Zahra Jayi.
Bhain Ghazi Di Sir Nangay Haye Sham Bazar Che Aayi.
Har Welay Ayho Zainab Nu Safraan Wich Dard Sataway.
Zindan Di Aseeri Wich Yaad Ayho Aaway.
Aawan Welay Karbal Taun Naa Veer Di Qabar Banai.
Abbas Meri Chadar Nu Naizay Nal Laya Aye.
Tak Bhain Teri Te Ki Aj Wela Aaya Aye.
Main Parday Layi Sir Apne Karbal Di Rait Way Payi.
Shabeer Diyaan Akhiyaan V Band Hoiyaan Naizay Te.
Bhaina De Jadon Wekhay Pathraan De Waar Honday.
Wekh Ke Ghazi Aye Manzar Kayi Waar Shahadat Payi.
Haye Sham Diyaan Tang Galiyaan Wich Roway Te Kurlaway.
Namoos-e-Nabi Haan Main Ro Ro Ke Dasdi Jaway.
Aye Sab Qari Neziyaan Te Main Ujari De Ne Bhai.
 Saqlain Jiwein Chehray Nu Walaan Naal Chupaya Aye.
Tatheer Diyaan Paliyaan Ne Pardah Way Banaya.
Syeda Zahra Dhiyaan Koon Tehzeeb Way Khoob Sikhayi.
