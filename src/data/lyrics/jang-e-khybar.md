Suno Suno Suno Suno Khybar Ka Majrah Suno
Khybar Ki Karzaar Ka Manzar Sunata Hoon
Jibreel Sath Do Mera Khybar Sunata Hoon

1
Khyber Ki Jang Ab Bhi Hai Dunya Mein Yadgar
Chalees Din Taveel Hoi Hai Yeh Karzaar
Khudsakhta Rasool Ke The Jitne Janisar
Maidan Choor Choor Ke Hote Rahe Farar
Lashkar Mein Muslimo Ke Ajab Inteshar Tha
Har Aik Ke Dimag Pe Marhab Sawar Tha

2
Hansti Thi Maut Dekh Ke Har Rooz Marika
Masroof Bhag Door Mein The Sare Soorma
Woh Jang Thi Keh Jang Bhi Sharmaye Barha
Chalees Din Mein Aik Bhi Zakhmi Nahi Howa
Marhab Jo Pochta Tha Laro Gaye Abhi Yahin
Taigein Chupa Ke Kehte The Sare Nahin Nahin

3
Lashkar Ki Abtari Pe Yeh Kehne Lage Nabi
Ghabrao Mat Larai Ki Yeh Shab Hai Aakhri
Ab Kal Jo Mard Ho Ga Alam Paye Ga Woh Hi
Dar Pardah Tha Nabi Ke Labon Par Ali Ali
Yani Alam Alif Se Yeh Lashkar Uthaye Ga
Jo Ain Se Alam Hai WOh Haider Uthaye Ga

4
Mazi Se Apna Peecha Churate Hain Kis Liye
Apna Hadaf Alam Ko Banate Hain Kis Liye
Panjay Ki Samt Ungli Uthate Hain Kis Liye
Ab Samjhe Hum Alam Woh Jalate Hain Kis Liye
Unn Ke Baron Ko Haq Se Yeh Mansab Mila Nahin
Khybar Ka Zakhm Hai Jo Abhi Tak Bhara Nahin

5
Har Ik Ko Shak Tha Mera Hi Lein Gaye Rasool Naam
Jagay Howay The Raat Ke Aankhein Thi Surkh Faam
Jo Chote Qad The Panjon Ke Bal The Khare Tamam
Itne Mein Laye Arsh Se Jibreel Yeh Pyam
Lashkar Na Kar Sake Ga Tumhari Koi Madad
Allah Keh Raha Hai Kaho Ya Ali Madad

6
Nad-e-Ali Zuban-e-Muhammad Pa Aa Gayi
Our Oss Taraf Madeene Mein Kehne Lagay Ali
Qanbar Chalo Pukar Rahe Hain Humein Nabi
Duldul KO Pyar Kar Ke Yeh Farmaya Oss Ghari
Aaj Iss Tarha Se Chal Ke Falak Kanpne Lagay
Dorey Jo Tere Sath Hawa Hanpne Lagay

7
Jaisa Kaha Tha Badshah-e-Khas-o-Aam Ne
Waisa Kiya Hai Duldul-e-Mehshar Kharam Ne
Hairat Se Dekha Lashkar-e-Khairul Inam Ne
Shair-e-Khuda Khare The Muhammad Ke Samne
Har Chehre Par Sawal Tha Hazrat Kahan Se Aa
Salman Bole Aaj Ali Aasmaan Se Aa

8
Haq Se Alam Mila Soye Khybar Ali Chale
Chomi Rakab Fateh Ne Aa Kar Ali Chale
Deen-e-Khuda Ka Ban Ke Muqadar Ali Chale
Aaya Hai Phir Jala Imam-e-Mubeen Ko
Jibreel Jaldi Tham Ko Aa Kar Zameen Ko

9
Hujjat Mareez-e-Maut Pe Hujjat Ne Ki Tamam
Ik Fikre Ne Samait Liye Maqsad-e-Kalam
Haider Hamari Maa Ne Rakha Hai Hamara Naam
Hoshiyar Zulfiqar Hoi Meri Be-Niyam
Shamsheer Buturab Ki San Se Pohanch Gayi
Marhab Ke Sar Pe Maut Se Pehle Pohanch Gayi

10
Sar Se Chali Tu Seene Pe Aa Kar Thehar Gayi
Seene Pe Sans Le Ke Jigar Mein Utar Gayi
Nikali Jigar Ko Kaat Ke Taba Kamar Gayi
Thi Maut Peeche Peeche Yeh Bijli Jidhar Gayi
Kat Kar Adoy-e-Deen-e-Khuda Khak Par Gira
Aadah Idhar Zameen Pe Aadah Udhar Gira

11
Jata Hai Kis Shekoh Se Allah Ka Asad
Khybar Ka Dar Ukharne Tanha Basad-o-Mad
Pohancha Qareeb-e-Dar Jonhi Woh Bandah-e-Ahad
Be-Sakhta Ali Ne Kaha Ya Ali Madad
Aise Ukhara Lamhe Mein Khybar Ke Baab Ko
Jis Tarha Shakh Se Koi Tore Ghulab Ko

12
Taqseem Sab Mein Maal-e-Ghanimat Kiya Giya
Haider Ko Hissa Sab Se Zyda Diya Giya
Iss Amar Par Sawal Nabi Se Kiya Giya
Kyun Adal Se Na Kaam Yahan Par Liya Giya
Bole Rasool Dakhal Kya Tujh Jaise Fard Ko
Dete Hain Hissa Dognah Shariyat Mein Mard Ko

13
Allah Re Sakhawat-e-Mushkil Khush Ali
Aisa Kahan Hai:MIR TAKALLUM: Koi Sakhi
Dolat Banam-e-Maal-e-Ghanimat Jo Thi Mili
Khairat Kar Di Rah-e-Khuda Mein Hansi Khushi
Dehleez-e-Dar Pe Pohanche Tu Salman Sath The
Lab Par Khuda Ka Shukar Tha Our Khali Hath The
