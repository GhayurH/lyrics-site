Iss Baras Chehlum Mananay Aao Chalein Karbala.
Wadah Zahra Se Nibhanay Aao Chalein Karbala.

1
Karbala Janat Hamari Karbala Hai Darsgah.
Sar Ka Kaba Hai Makkay Mein Dil Ka Kaba Karbala.
Momino Dil Ko Jhukanay Aao Chalein Karbala.

2
Karbala Ki Sar Zameen Pe Milti Hai Rab Ki Raza.
Sab Ko Milta Karbala Se Rasta Hai Khuld Ka.
Yeh Zamane Ko Bataney Aao Chalein Karbala.

3
Ishq-e-Maula Ka Safar Dil Se Karein Gay Momino.
Hum Najaf Ta Karbala Paidal Chalein Gay Momino.
Gham Jahan Bhar Ke Bholaney Aao Chalein Karbala.

4
Apne Hathoon Mein Alam Abbas Ka Le Kar Chalo.
Ro Ke Pursa Tum Sakina Ka Unhein De Kar Chalo.
Ashk Aankhon Se Bahane Aao Chalein Karbala.

5
Haq Wahan Ajar-e-Risalat Ka Karein Gay Hum Ada.
Matam-e-Shabeer Kar Lein Karbala Mein Hum Bapa.
Farsh Matam Ka Bichanay Aao Chalein Karbala.

6
Sajday Mein:Sibt-e-Nabi Ka Sar Wahan Pe Kat Gaya.
Aik Din Mein Fatima Ka Ghar Wahan Pe Lut Gaya.
Sari Dunya Ko Bataney Aao Chalein Karbala.

7
Gham Hai Maula Na Mila Pani Tere Be Sheer Ko.
Aye Waheed Hum Iss Baras Chehlum Pe Yeh Shabeer Ko.
Noha Ro Ro Kar Sunanay Aao Chalein Karbala.
