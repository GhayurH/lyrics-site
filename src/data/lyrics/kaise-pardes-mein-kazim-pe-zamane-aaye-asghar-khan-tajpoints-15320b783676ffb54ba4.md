1
Kaise Pardes Mein Kazim Pe Zamane Aaye Asghar Khan.
Haye Mazdoor Janazay Ko Uthane Aaye.
Chor Aaye Hain Najaf Our Madina Sare.
Tu La Waris Nahi Zahra Ke Aye Betay Pyaray.
Aa Mera Lal Tujhey Sare Le Jane Aaye.
Ho Gayi Qaid Khatam Mosa Kazim Mere.
Qaid Khane Se Nikal Jao Main Sadqay Tere.
Baqiya Walay Tera Soag Mananay Aaye.
Aap Zindan Mein Hain Ghar Mein Hai Soag Bapa.
Betiyaan Mosa-e-Kazim Ki Yeh Deti Hain Sada.
Kash Keh Baba Humein Seene Lagane Aaye.
Keh Keh Haye Rota Raha Satwaan Mera Imam.
Haye Kazim Ya Sakina Ho Keh Baghdad Ya Sham.
Hum Tu Hain Nana Tera Deen Bachaney Aaye.
Kaisi Ghurbat Hai Tere Kafan Mein Zanjeerein Hain.
