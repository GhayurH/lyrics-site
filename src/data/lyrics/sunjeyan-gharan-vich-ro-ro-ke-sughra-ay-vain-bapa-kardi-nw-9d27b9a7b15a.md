Sunjeyan gharaaṅ vich ro ro ke Sughra ae vain bapa kardi
Menu bhul gayo Akbar veeran, par maiṅ ujri
Tenu chete veer bara kardi

1
Rab khair kare kujh hoya ae, dil dhalda shaam da
Main baal diyaaṅ bujhda diva, Akbar de naam da
Mere saah lag jaawan veeran, Ali Akbar nu
Dukhiyari behn dua kardi

2
Hun teek sunere na aaye, jis din da tu gaya
Kiuṅ deraaṅ laayaaṅ aawan nu, pardesi veerna
Teri taaṅg ne maar mukauna, haaye ujri nu
Dukh har waile tera kardi

3
Tenu tor dita wal Karbal de, bara kar ke hosla
Tere aan de waade lang gaye ne, main wekhaaṅ tere raah
Je jaandi Akbar mur ke tu nahiṅ auna
Tenu veeran naa main vida kardi

4
Tenu sehre laa ke shagna de, main laandi mehndiyaṅ
Tere waag pharan de laag bhira, assi bhainaaṅ kehndiyaṅ
Ae hasrat reh gayi Akbar, teri shaadi te
Sang bhainaaṅ saare chaa kardi

5
Veeraaṅ de baajoṅ Sughra da, jina muhaal si
Aqdas ik pal es ujri layi, sadiyaaṅ te saal si
Manzur vichhore jhal di, jag toṅ tur gayi
Ki dukhiyari hor bhala kardi
