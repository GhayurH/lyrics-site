Sunjeyan Gharan Vich Ro Ro Ke Sughra, Ay Vain Bapa Kardi

Menu Bhul Gayo Akbar Veeran, Par Mai Ujri

Tenu Chaite Veer Bara Kardi

Rab Khair Karay Kujh Hoya Ay, Dil Dhalda Shaam Da

Main Baal Diyan Bujhda Diwa, Akbar De Naam Da

Mere Saa Lagjaawan Veeran, Ali Akbar Nu

Dukhiyari Bhen Dua Kardi

Hun Teek Sunehre Naa Aaey, Jis Din Da Tu Gaya

Kyun Dairaan Laayan Aavan Nu, Pardesi Veerna

Teri Taang Ne Maar Mukauna, Haye Ujri Nu

Dukh Har Vaile Tera Kardi

Tenu Tor Dita Wal Kerbal De, Bara Kar Ke Hosla

Tere Aan De Waade Lang Gaye Ne, Main Vaikhaan Tere Raah

Jai Jaan Di Akbar Mur Ke, Tu Nayi Auna

Tenu Veeran Naa Mei Vidaa Kardi

Tenu Sehre Laa Ke Shagna De, Main Laandi Mehndiyan

Tere Waag Pharan De Laag Bhira, Assi Bhainaan Kehndiyan

Ai Hasrat Reh Gayi Akbar, Teri Shaadi Te

Sang Bhainaan Saare Chaa Kardi

Veeraan De Baajo Sughra Da, Jeena Muhaal Si

Aqdas Ik Pal Es Ujri Layi, Sadiyan Te Saal Si

Manzoor Vichore Jhal Di, Jag Ton Tur Gayi

Ki Dukhiya Hor Bhala Kardi
