1
Chand Muharram Ka Syed Wajhi Hasan.
Sughra Pe Qayamat Thi Barpa Dekha Jab Chand Muharram Ka.
Yaad Aa Gaya Akbar Ka Chehra Dekha Jab Chand Muharram Ka.
Kulsoom, Rubab Our Zainab Ne Ghabra Kar Apne Hathoon Se.
Baloon Ko Apne Dhanp Liya Dekha Jab Chand Muharram Ka.
Akbar Ka Jigar Asghar Ka Gala Baba Ka Badan Zainab Ki Rida.
Sajjad Ko Kya Kya Yaad Aaya Dekha Jab Chand Muharram Ka.
Roney Lagay Sare Ahl-e-Haram Sajjad Ki Aankhon Mein Oss Dam.
Zindaan Ka Andhera Phail Gaya Dekha Jab Chand Muharram Ka.
Kurtay Ko Laga Kar Seene Se Ik Aah Bhari Our Bano Ne.
Ik Khali Jholay Ko Dekha, Dekha Jab Chand Muharram Ka.
Shabeer Ka Lasha Yaad Aaya Akbar Ka Kaleja Yaad Aaya.
Gash Kha Ke Girein Umm-e-Laila Dekha Jab Chand Muharram Ka.
Yaad Aa Gaye Bachoon Ke Lashay Our Sani-e-Zahra Ke Aagay.
Bazar Ka Manzar Ghom Gaya Dekha Jab Chand Muharram Ka.
Aye Rab-e-Jahan Kar Sab Pe Karam De Hum Ko Faqat Shabeer Ka Gham.
Ki:NOOR ALI: Ne Bas Yeh Dua Dekha Jab Chand Muharram Ka.
