1
Main Khak-e-Karbala Hoon Shifa Bantti Hoon Main.
Har La-Elaj Gham Ki Dawa Bantti Hoon Main.
Kya Mujh Se Pochte Ho Keh Kya Bantti Hoon Main.
Shams-o-Qamar Ko Rizq-e-Zia Bantti Hoon Main.
Socho Zara Si Diar Kidhar Jaye Kainaat.
Main Bhi Shifa Na Bantoon Tu Mar Jaye Kainaat.

2
Jo Dushman-e-Hussain Hai Mera Hai Woh Raqeeb.
Mera Matab Hai Karb-o-Bala Our Main Tabeeb.
Aatay Hain Sab Yahan Pe Tawangar Ho Ya Ghareeb.
Dawa Hai Mera Jaisa Bhi Jis Ka Bhi Ho Naseeb.
Le Aao Oss Ko Jis Ki Jahan Mein Dawa Nahi.
Oss Ko Shifa Na Doon Tu Main Khak-e-Shifa Nahi.

3
Aankhoon Se Mas Karo Mujhe Binai Pao Gaye.
Izat Meri Karo Gaye Pazeerai Pao Gaye.
Aagay Mere Jhuko Gaye Tu Onchai Pao Gaye.
Mujh Se Jo Door Ho Gaye Tu Ruswai Pao Gaye.
Bahu Bhittai Ka Yehi Irshad-e-Pak Hai.
Yeh Khak Gar Na Ho Tu Zamane Pe Khak Hai.

4
Mera Muqam Deen-e-Payamber Se Poch Lo.
Hoon Kitni Khush Naseeb Muqaddar Se Poch Lo.
Johar Hain Mujh Mein Kitne Yeh Gohar Se Poch Lo.
Hain Kitne Lal Mujh Mein Qalandar Se Poch Lo.
Pocho Aqeedatoon Se Mera Kya Muqaam Hai.
Mati Hoon Phir Bhi Paon Lagana Haram Hai.

5
Hai Mere Jaisi Izzat-o-Tauqeer Kis Ke Pas.
Janat Se Bhi Haseen Hai Jageer Kis Ke Pas.
Dunya Mein Mere Jaisi Hai Taseer Kis Ke Pas.
Al-Mukhtasir Hai Roza-e-Shabir Kis Ke Pas.
Rehta Hai Aana Jana Kahan Par Rasool Ka.
Seene Pe Mere Sota Hai Beta Batool Ka.

6
Kuch Aise Loag Hain Jinhein Mujh Par Nahi Yaeen.
Batoon Ka Unki Mujh Pe Zara Sa Asar Nahi.
Marne Ke Bad Unse Nimat Le Gi Khud Zameen.
Jab Sath Unke Koi Nahi Ho Ga Humnasheen.
Jis Dam Lahad Mein Teergi Unko Sataye Gi.
Reh Reh Ke Sab Ko Khak-e-Shifa Yaad Aaye Gi.

7
Mera Muqam Kya Hai Khadija Bataein Gi.
Hawa Se Pocho Mariyyam-o-Sara Bataein Gi.
Main Chand Se Haseen Hoon Fizza Bataein Gi.
Tahir Hoon Kitni Fatima Zahra Bataein Gi.
Pocho Faroh-e-Deein Se Yeh Kaisa Usool Hai.
Jo Sajda Mujh Pe Ho Ga Woh Sajda Qabool Hai.

8
Sajjad Kibriya Ki Qasam Main Kareem Hoon.
Esa Bhi Jis Se Mangay Dawa Woh Hakeem Hoon.
Goya Zabaan Nahi Hoon Main Phir Bhi Kaleem Hoon.
Ghalti Se Bhi Yeh Kehna Nahi Main Yateem Hoon.
Nabiyoon Se Sar Ko Youn Mere Aagay Jhukaya Hai.
Mujh Ko Abu Turab Ne Beti Banaya Hai.
