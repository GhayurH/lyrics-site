Piyo Di Maiyat Te Ghazi Diyaan Behna Rab Jane Kinj Jeoindiyaan Raiyaan.
Jinna Sir Diyan Chadraan Khoniya Ne onna Kofiyaan Ne Babe Diyan Sanhwaan Kho Layiyan.

1
Dhiyaan Di Kul Khudai Peo De Chehre Wich Samayi.
Ik Te Pardais Di Gurbat utton Dhiyaan Di Aye Fitrat.
Babe Nu Jandaan Wekh Ke Sanhwaan Ruk Gayiyaan Uthi Maiyat Khaak Te Dig Payiyaan.
Loki Sun Lain Na Kitey Koi Vain V Baar Na Nikle.

2
Iss Dar Toon zahra Jaiyaan Jee Khool Ke Ro Na Sakiyaan.
Iztaan Wale Sharma Wale Ghar Diyaan Dhiyaan Awaazaan Dak Diyaan Reh Gayiyaan.
Koi Aakhe Main Aan Rukaiyaa Mere Sab Toon Farz Te Dasja.
Koi Aakhe Main Kulsoom Aan Main Shaam Da Rah Ki Janaa.

3
Koi Aakhe Baba Zainab Aan Toon Janda Ayein Main Aj Tak Jirkaan Nayi Suniyaan.
Kalma Gowaan Di Jafa Ne Toor Dite Tasbi De Dane.
Quran Te Zarbat Chal Gayi Te Har Ik Ayat Rul Gayi.
Zahra Jaiyaan Da Kaba Tur Giya Jidday Wich Akbar Aye Maseetaan Hun Kayiyaan.
