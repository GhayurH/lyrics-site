1
Yani Beghair-e-Yak Dil Be Mudua Nah Maang.
Aata Hai Daag-e-Hasrat-e-Dil Ka Shumaar Yaad.
Mujh Se Mere Gunha Ka Hisab Aye Khuda Nah Maang.
