1
Karbala Na Bhuleingey Nadeem Sarwar.
Bhool Jaein Gay Har Gham, Gham Tera Na Bholein Gay.
Karbala Na Bholein Gaye, Karbala Na Bholein Gay.
Kaisay Bhool Jaein Maqtal-e-Hussain Ko.

2
Kaisay Bhool Jaein Fatima Ke Bain Ko.
Ba Khuda Na Bholein Gay Gham Tera Na Bholein Gay.
Woh Sitam Woh Zulm Woh Jafa Ke Silsilay.
Alqama Ke Samne Khiyam Jal Gaye.

3
Nainawa Na Bholein Gay Gham Tera Na Bholein Gay.
Jis Jagah Hussain Ka Jawaan Howa Shaheed.
Jis Jagah Howa Tha Aik Be Zabaan Shaheed.
Woh Jagah Na Bholein Gay Gham Tera Na Bholein Gay.

4
Woh Lahu Bhara Alam Woh Mashk Khoon Bhari.
Intezar-e-Aab Mein Sakina Mar Gayi.
Saneha Na Bholein Gay Gham Tera Na Bholein Gay.
Khon Roye Jis Ke Gham Mein Umar Bhar Imam.

5
Le Gayi Thi Loot Kar Rida Jo Foaj-e-Sham.
Woh Rida Na Bholein Gay Gham Tera Na Bholein Gay.
Aslam Aye Karbala Ki Khak Aslam.
Aslam Aye Dafan Jism-e-Pak Aslam.

6
Yeh Sada Na Bholein Gay Gham Tera Na Bholein Gay.
:SARWAR-o-REHAN: Aarzu Hai Yehi.
Roza-e-Hussain Pe Ho Sab Ki Hazari.
Yeh Dua Na Bholein Gham Tera Na Bholein Gay.
