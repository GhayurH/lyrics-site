1
Utho Sakina Chalein Karbala Mir Hasan Mir.
Zindaan Se Jab Reha Howay Sadaat-e-Karbala.
Maa Ne Lipat Ke Qabar-e-Sakina Pe Di Sada.
Choti Si Turbat Pe Maa Ne Kaha.
Zindaan Se Jata Hai Ab Qafila.
Mujhe Shehzadi Tumhari Qasam.
Na Rakhon Gi Zindaan Se Bahir Qadam.
Safar Ki Ab Hoti Hain Teyariyaan.
Amari Mein Bethey Hain Ahl-e-Haram.
Ghurbat Ka Meri Tumhein Wasta.
Woh Pehle Ke Jaisi Tu Ghurbat Nahi.
Aye Bibi Hamari Woh Halat Nahi.
Tumhein Shamiyon Se Ab Aye Meri Jaan.
Kafan Mangne Ki Zarorat Nahi.
Lo Mil Gayi Hai Tumhari Rida.
Mujhe Yaad Asghar Ki Hai Bekasi.
Lahad Se Woh Maiyyat Nikali Gayi.
Akele Main Rehne Na Doon Gi Tumhein.
Bharosa Nahi Shamiyon Ka Koi.
Rehte Hain Charon Taraf Ashqiya.
Yeh Shami Kahan Tum Ko Behlaein Gaye.
Tumhari Lahad Par Nahi Aaein Gaye.
Rahe Ga Andhera Hi Zindan Mein.
Haram Jab Yahan Se Chale Jaein Gaye.
Kon Aaye Ga Phir Jalane Diya.
Kahani Padar Ki Sunaon Gi Main.
Agar Neend Aayi Sulaon Gi Main.
Akele Na Naqay Pe Betho Gi Tum.
Tumhein Sath Apne Bithaon Gi Main.
Ab Tu Rasan Mein Na Ho Ga Gala.
MUjhe Maar Dalay Ga Basi Yehi Gham.
Kamar Mein Hai Beti Tumhari Jo Kham.
Warm Bazuon Ka Mujhe Yaad Hai.
Tumhein Tu Dikhai Bhi Deta Hai Kam.
Akbar Se Bibi Mile Gi Shifa.
Bari Dair Thehari Hain Darbar Mein.
Yahan Par Na Roko Zyda Unhein.
Keh Pehlu Pe Ik Hath Rakhe Howay.
Sada De Rahi Hain Sakina Tumhein.
Lene Ko Aaein Hain Khud Syeda.
Thi Zindaan Mein Akbar Ajab Woh Ghari.
Rehai Pe Roti Thi Har Ik Phophi.
Idhar Khon Rota Tha Abid Udhar.
Sakina Lahad Mein Tarapti Rahi.
Jab Bhi Yeh Madar Ne Ro Kar Kaha.
