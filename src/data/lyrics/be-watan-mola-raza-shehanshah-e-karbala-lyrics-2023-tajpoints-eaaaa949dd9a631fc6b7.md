1
Be Watan Mola Raza Shehanshah e Karbala.
Be Watan Maula Raza, Haye Ghareeb Ul Ghuraba.
Aaj Hain Gham Mein Tere, Ghamzada Arz-o-Sama.
Ho Gaya Tukray Jigar, Zehar-e-Halahil Se.
Shah Ko Angoron Mein Jo, Bhar Ke Zalim Ne Diya.
Ranj Be Watani Sahey, Safar Dushwar Kiye.
Tujh Se Milney Ke Liye, Teri Humsheer Raza.
Hijar-e-Akbar Mein Rahi, Jaisay Tarpi Sughra.
Ho Baho Malka-e-Qum, Ne Tera Sadma Saha.
Jane Kaisi Thi Zehar, Jiski Shiddat Ke Asar.
Ho Gaya Tukray Jigar, Janta Hai Bas Khuda.
Khak Par Luttay Rahe, Qaid e Tanhai Mein.
Misal-e-Kazim Hai Jahad, Aap Ne Jo Hai Kiya.
Koi Apna Bhi Nahi, Hai Musibat Ki Ghari.
Kamsini Mein Hain Taqi, Our Janaza Hai Para.
Kya Khata Thi Haider, Kyun Musalmaan Tu Ne.
Qatal Har Door-e-Khilafat, Mein Imamat Ka Kiya.
