Alamdar Na Aaya Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics
Aye Ahl-e-Haram Mir-o-Alamdar Na Aaya.
Saqa-e-Hussain Syed-o-Salar Na Aaya.
Maula Ki Kamar Tooti Jis Dam Yeh Khabar Aayi.
Mara Gaya Darya Par Shabeer Ka Shedayi.
Khoon Bhar Gaya Aankhon Mein Jati Rahi Binayi.
Maqtal Se Sakina Ka Woh Ghamkhuwar Na Aaya.
Jab Khon Mein Tar Aaya Abbas Ka Woh Parcham.
Tapti Hoi Reti Par Atfal They Sab Be Dam.
Sarwar Ne Kiya Noha Zainab Ne Kiya Matam.
Chilaye Haram Ghazi-o-Jarrar Na Aaya.
Hukam-e-Sheh-e-Wala Par Darya Ko Utha Lata.
Kya Karta Bhala Ghazi Mehroom-e-Ijazat Tha.
Koshish Tu Bohat Ki Par Lakhon Mein Ghira Tanha.
Jab Mashk Chidi Phir Woh Wafadar Na Aaya.
Zahra Ke Gharanay Ka Woh Zamin-e-Chadar Tha.
Maslak Mein Thi Saqayi Kirdar Mein Haider Tha.
Jis Waqt Katay Bazu Ghazi Ke Bahey Aanso.
Lainay Gaye Maula Woh Dil Afgar Na Aaya.
Bachoon Ki Sadaein Thin Abbas Chacha Pani.
Ik Mashk Lahu De Kar Ik Mashk Bhara Pani.
Oss Mashk-e-Sakina Se Jis Waqt Baha Pani.
Woh Mar Gaya Darya Pe Haya Daar Na Aaya.
Woh Shair-e-Dilawar Tha Tanha Bhi Jo Lashkar Tha.
Afsoos Lab-e-Darya Ik Lasha-e-Be Sar Tha.
Hal Min Ki Sada Sunta Rota Tha Tarapta Tha.
Shabeer Ka Sheda Woh Madadgar Na Aaya.
Jaltay Hoye Khaimon Se Zainab Ki Sada Aayi.
Aye Baba Ali Dekho Beti Ki Yeh Tanhai.
Yeh Mashk-o-Alam Aaya, Aaya Na Mera Bhai.
Baba Mere Kunbay Ka Tarafdar Na Aaya.
 Rehan Se Sarwar Se Ho Dard Biyaan Kaisay.
Jis Shair Ki Dharas Par Aabad Rahe Khaimay.
Jaltay Hoye Khaimon Se Ghabra Ke Jo Zainab Ne.
Kitni Bhi Sadaein Dein Woh Ik Bar Na Aaya.
