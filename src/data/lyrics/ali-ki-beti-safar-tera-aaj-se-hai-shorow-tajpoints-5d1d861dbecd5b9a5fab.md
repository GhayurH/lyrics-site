Ali Ki Beti Safar Tera Aaj Se Hai Shorow.
Sada Janazay Se Aati Hai Sham Jaye Gi Tu.

1
Baad Mere Yeh Musalman Sataein Gay Tumhein.
Sar Barehna Sar-e-Bazar Phiraein Gay Tumhein.
Koi Na Aaye Ga Dardi Bara Pukarey Gi Tu.
Kaisa Aoulad-e-Muhammad Pe Yeh Ramzan Aaya.

2
Masjid-e-Kofa Se Ho Zakhmi Hai Quran Aaya.
Kha Gayin Betiyaan Gash Dekha Jo Baba Ka Lahu.
Miley Ga Zehar Hasan Ko Lutay Gi Teri Rida.
Meri Zainab Mera Hussain Karey Ga Jaisay Ada.

3
Namaz Aakhri Hogi Woh Khoon Se Kar Ke Wozu.
Teri Zindagi Mein Tu Dukh Koi Na Paye Zainab.
Teri Zindagi Ka Jo Maqsad Hai Rida e Zainab.
Yeh Yaad Rakhna Baad Mere Mere Ghazi Tu.

4
Meri Aoulad Se Youn Badla Badar Ka Lein Gay.
Chadrein Lotein Gay Pani Ka Na Qatra Dein Gay.
Tujhe Le Jaein Gay Sar Nangay Sham Mere Addo.
Dekho Faryad Yateemo Ki Youn Sunta Hai Khuda.

5
Ibn-e-Muljam Ta Hashar Gonjay Gi Lanat Ki Sada.
Ban Gaya Kaisay Hai Ibrat Dekho Haider Ka Addo.
Bahaein Aanso Falak Our Ro Rahi Hai Zameen.
Na Jao Baba Hamara Yahan Pe Koi Nahi.

6
Be Wafaon Ka Shehar Hai Yahan Dushman Har So.
Ho Madina Ya Woh Kofa Ho Ya Karbal Ki Zameen.
Hogi Sajjad, Abu Talib Ke Khoon Se Rangeen.
Bacha Hai Deen-e-Khuda Inn Ke Kar Ke Sajdah Raku.
