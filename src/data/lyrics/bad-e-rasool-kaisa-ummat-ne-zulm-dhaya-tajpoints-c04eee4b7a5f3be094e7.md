Bad e Rasool Kaisa Ummat Ne Zulm Dhaya.
Miras e Mustafa Se, Haqdar Ko Hataya.

1
Amar-e-Elahi Jis Ki, Tazeem Ki Nabi Ne.
Jhutla Diya Hai Oss Ko, Darbar Mein Shaqi Ne.
Bughz-e-Ali Musalmaan, Kya Khoob Hai Nibhaya.
Pehchan Mujh Ko Hakim, Main Bint-e-Mustafa Hoon.

2
Unwan e Sorah Kausar, Our Chadar-e-Kisa Hoon.
Mimber Pe Baithay Ghasib, Ko Barha Bataya.
Qaul-e-Rasool Jhotay, Parhtay Rahe Musalmaan.
Umm-e-Abeeha Rad Mein, Parhti Rahi Thi Quraan.

3
Sun Kar Daleelein Ghasib, Ko Par Haya Na Aaya.
Kagaz Qalam Diya No, Waqt-e-Qaza Nabi Ko.
Keh Ke Quraan Hai Kafi, Jhutla Diya Nabi Ko.
Bohtan Mustafa Par, Hizyan Ka Hai Lagaya.

4
Hukam-e-Laeen Se Dar Ko, Kunfuz Ne Youn Jala Kar.
Tora Hai Pasliyon Ko, Zahra Pe Dar Gira Kar.
Pehlu Ko Qabar-e-Mohsin, Zalim Ne Hai Banaya.
Jageer Ka Nahi Gham, Yeh Baat Dil Ko Kha Gayi.

5
Lafz-e-Laeen Se Yaqdam, Sar Mein Sofaidi Aa Gayi.
Gasib Ne Keh Ke Jhoti, Zahra Ko Jab Bulaya.
Bag-e-Fidak Tha Farzi, Baat Iss Se Bhi Bari Thi.
Haq-e-Ali Ki Khatir, Gasib Se Lar Rahi Thi.

6
Darasal Roap Unka, Dunya Ko Hai Dikhaya.
Naraz Waqt-e-Rukhsat, Jin Se Rahi Hain Zahra.
Mehshar Mein Na Miley Ga, Haider Unhein Sahara.
Jis Jis Ne Bhi Nabi Ki, Beti Ko Hai Sataya.
