Ghadeer e Khum Mein Iftekhar Haider.

1
Bar Sar-e-Mimber-e-Palaan Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein.
Kis Bulandi Pe Hai Insan Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein.
Loag Chehron Pe Naye Chehray Saja Letey Hain.
Our Phir Hoti Hai Pehchan Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein.
Iss Mein Bas Aik Hi Aayat Ki Jagah Khali Thi.
Lo Mukamal Howa Quran Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein.

2
Haan Tujhe Our Bhi Tehwar Nazar Aate Hain.
Our Rehta Hai Mera Dehyan Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein.
Ik Ajab Roop Ajab Rang Mein Dekhey Gaye They.
Qanbar-o-Buzar-o-Salman Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein.
Mannay Wala Ali Ka Hoon Yeh Dawa Hai Tera.
Mannay Wala Hai Tu Maan Ghadeer-e-Khum Mein.
