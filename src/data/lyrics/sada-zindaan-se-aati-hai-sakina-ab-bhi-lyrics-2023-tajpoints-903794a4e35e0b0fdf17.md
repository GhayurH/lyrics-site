Sada Zindaan Se Aati Hai Sakina Ab Bhi Qaidi Hai Sheraz Khan.
Qaza Ke Baad Bhi Iss Ke Galay Mein Aik Rasi Hai.

1
Chupati Ho Gi Woh Turbat Mein Bhi Rukhsar Hathoon Se.
Bhateeji Hai Chacha Abbas Ki Ghairat Samajhti Hai.
Kaneezon Ko Kiya Aazad Jis Ke Aane Par Sheh Ne.
Kayi Sadiyon Se Woh Beti Kissi Zindaan Mein Rehti Hai.

2
Tamachay Kis Ne Kitne Marey Hain Tadad Yeh Sari.
Dil-e-Sajjad Par Ya Neelay Rukhsaron Pe Likhi Hai.
Sakina Ki Sadaein Gonjti Hain Oss Ke Kano Mein.
Madina Wapisi Ke Rastay Se Maa Guzarti Hai.

3
Kuch Aisa Sabar Dikhlaya Hai Iss Ne Chaar Barson Mein.
Na Ahl-e-Sham Bholein Gay Sakina Kis Ki Beti Hai.
Kabhi Mitna Nahi Saqlain Yeh Manzar Yateemi Ka.
Sitamgar Marta Hai Our Woh Maiyyat Se Lipatti Hai.
