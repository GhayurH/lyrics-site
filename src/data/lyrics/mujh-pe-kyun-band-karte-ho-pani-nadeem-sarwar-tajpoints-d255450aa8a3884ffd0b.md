Mujh Pe Kyun Band Karte Ho Pani Nadeem Sarwar.

1
Mujh Pe Kyun Band Kartay Ho Pani Kya Muhammad Ka Pyara Nahi Hoon.
Mujh Pe Kyun Band Kartay Ho Pani Kalma Goyo Muhammad Ke Shami.
Kya Main Mehman Aaya Nahi Hoon Kya Main Zahra Ka Jaya Nahi Hoon.
Mera Baba Hai Mushkil Kusha Meri Madar Bhi Hai Syeda.
Mera Beta Hai Zain-Ul-Abba Mere Nana Ka Kalma Parha.
Jis Ke Saye Ko Tum Dhondtay Ho Kya Main Oss Ka Hi Saya Nahi Hoon.

2
Tum Ne Maktoob Likhe Kayi Aaiye Jald Ibn-e-Ali.
Deen Ki Roshni Kam Hoi Shama-e-Islam Bhujney Lagi.
Tum Ne Chaha Tha Main Aa Gaya Hoon Khud Madine Se Aaya Nahi Hoon.
Dastaras Mein Meri Kya Nahi Ilm Taqwa Shujat Yaqeen.
Mere Pairon Ke Neechay Zameen Jis Pe Rakhein Farishtay Jabeen.
Main Hi Sardar-e-Ahl-e-Jinna Hoon Koi Toota Sitara Nahi Hoon.

3
Meri Gardan Pe Khanjar Chaley Aag Lagti Hai Ghar Mein Lagay.
Bint-e-Zahra Ki Chadar Chiney Hath Doon Ga Na Apna Tujhe.
Hath Mera Yeh Dast-e-Khuda Hai Main Tu Munkir Khuda Ka Nahi Hoon.
Sath Zahra Ki Hain Betiyaan Mohtaram Inn Sa Koi Kahan.
Pyas Ki Dhop Ki Sakhtiyaan Sab Ke Chehray Hoye Hain Dhowaan.
Betiyaan Fatima Ki Hain Pyasi Sirf Ik Main Hi Pyasa Nahi Hoon.

4
Hukam Abbas Ko Doon Agar Nehar Le Aaye Woh Cheen Kar.
Karbala Hogi Zair-o-Zabar Lashein Terein Gi Har Mauj Par.
Main Nahi Chahta Jang Karna Foaj Larney Ko Laya Nahi Hoon.
Gonji Hal Min Ki Aisi Sada Sheh Ko Rehan Kehte Suna.
Ya Ali Ya Ali Elia Waqt Imdad Ka Aa Gaya.
Mere Bachay Bilaktay Hain Pyasay Phir Bhi Kya Muskuraya Nahi Hoon.
