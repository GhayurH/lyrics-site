Yeh Meri Dua Hai Ali Jee Lyrics

1
Zahra Ne Kaha Matam-e-Sarwar Na Rukay Ga Yeh Meri Dua Hai.
Islam Sada Zindah-o-Painda Rahe Ga Yeh Meri Dua Hai.
Ik Pyasay Ne Sajdah Jo Tah-e-Taig Kiya Hai.
Tab Ja Ke Kahin Deen Payamber Ka Bacha Hai.
Yeh Our Kissi Ne Nahi Zahra Ne Kaha Hai.
Paigham-e-Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Zindah Rahe Ga.

2
Hum Matami Shabeer Ke Hum Ahl-e-Azza Hain.
Hum Qalb-e-Payamber Ke Dharakne Ki Sada Hain.
Hum Madar-e-Shabeer Ki Makhsoos Dua Hain.
Hathoon Mein Sada Parcham-e-Abbas Rahe Ga.
Hai Ulfat-e-Shabeer Meri Maa Ki Amanat.
Ghuti Mein Pilayi Hai Sheh-e-Dein Ki Muhabbat.

3
Hai Taziya Dari Meri Makhsoos Ibadat.
Har Door Mein Sikka Gham-e-Sarwar Ka Chaley Ga.
Khush Hoon Gay Nabi Majlis-e-Shabeer Mein Aao.
Hur Ban Ke Chalo Bigray Muqadar Ko Banao.
Mazloom Ke Matam Mein Zara Ashk Bahao.
Ghazi Ka Alam Dhop Mein Saya Tumhein De Ga.

4
Hum Aman Ka Paigham Sunatay Hain Jahan Ko.
Ashor Ko Kya Guzri Batatay Hain Jahan Ko.
Farsh-e-Gham-e-Sarwar Pe Bulatay Hain Jahan Ko.
Iss Dar Pe Jo Aaya Hai Woh Izzat Se Jiye Ga.
Baqi Hai Azza Akbar-e-Dilgeer Ke Dam Se.
Sajdah Hai Bacha Hazrat-e-Shabeer Ke Dam Se.

5
Baqi Hai Haya Zainab-e-Dilgeer Ke Dam Se.
Aandhi Mein Charag-e-Gham-e-Shabeer Jalay Ga.
Mehsos Yeh Hota Hai Raza Kehti Hai Zahra.
Parhtay Raho Youn Hi Mere Shabeer Ka Noha.
Khud Aaon Gi Main Sunney:ALI JEE: Jo Parhey Ga.
Jo Roye Ga Janat Mein Mere Sath Rahe Ga.
