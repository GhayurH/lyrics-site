1
Sajjad Pe Woh Kaisi Qayamat Ki Ghari Thi Hasan Sadiq.
Jab Sani-e-Zahra Sar-e-Darbar Khari Thi.
Hathoon Pe Tha Masoom Sakina Ka Janaza.
Aankhon Mein Lagi Khon Ke Ashkon Ki Jhari Thi.

2
Sar Ko Woh Jhukaye Hoye Har Moor Se Guzrey.
Tapta Howa Sehra Tha Our Dhop Kari Thi.
Kanton Ko Rulati Thi Teri Aabla Payi.
Zanjeer Bhi Roti Thi Jo Paon Mein Pari Thi.

3
Zindaan Ke Andherey Mein Tanha Thi Sakina.
Aankhon Mein Oss Ke Kaisi Ashkon Ki Jhari Thi.
Shah Najam Ne Band Kar Diye Iss Pe Sukhan Tamam.
Izzat Bhi Bari Thi Tu Musibat Bhi Bari Thi.
