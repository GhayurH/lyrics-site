1
Bharay Darbar Mein Bulaya Gaya.
Haye Zahra Ko Youn Sataya Gaya.
Baap Ke Naam Par Hakomat Thi.
Our Beti Ka Ghar Jalaya Gaya.

2
Kya Madine Ke Loag Andhay Thay?.
Hath Zahra Pe Jab Uthaya Gaya.
Murtaza Cheekh Cheekh Kar Roye.
Hath Pehlu Se Jab Hataya Gaya.

3
Loag Chalte Rahe Jalay Dar Par.
Dar Ko Zahra Se Na Uthaya Gaya.
Raat Ki Jal Chuki Thi Woh Chokhat.
Koi Roney Magar Na Aaya Gaya.

4
Parha Jis Dar Pe Mustafa Ne Darood.
Thokaron Se Ossay Giraya Gaya.
Kaisay Saqlain Pasliyaan Totein.
Sirf Zainab Ko Yeh Bataya Gaya.
