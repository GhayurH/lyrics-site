Apni Qismat Azma Kar Dekhna Mir Hasan Mir.
Tum Azza Khana Saja Kar Dekhna.

1
Khud Munafiq Ka Pata Chal Jaye Ga.
Narah-e-Haider Laga Kar Dekhna.
Ho Agar Janat Zameen Par Dekhni.
Karbala Ik Bar Ja Kar Dekhna.

2
Anbiya Ko Dekhna Chaho Agar.
Halqa-e-Matam Mein Aa Kar Dekhna.
Tum Ko Zahra Ki Dua Mil Jaye Gi.
Parcham-e-Ghazi Utha Kar Dekhna.

3
Woh Tu Phir Maula Ali Hain Hashar Mein.
Martaba Qanbar Ka Aa Kar Dekhna.
Chand Taray Dekhna Chaho Agar.
Dagh Matam Ke Saja Kar Dekhna.

4
Khushbo-e-Romal-e-Zahra Chahiye.
Ashk Majlis Mein Baha Kar Dekhna.
Panjtan Mehman Khud Hoon Gay Zaki.
Farsh Majlis Ka Bicha Kar Dekhna.
