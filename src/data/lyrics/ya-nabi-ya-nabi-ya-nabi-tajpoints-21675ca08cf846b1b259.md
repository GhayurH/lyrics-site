Ya Nabi Ya Nabi Ya Nabi
Aap Khair-ul-Wara Syed-ul-Anbiya.
Aap Shah-e-Ummam Mehboob-e-Khuda.
Husan Aisa Khuda Aap Par Hai Fida.
Ho Karam Ki Nigah Sadqa Mula Ali.
Aap Ke Sadqay Mein Hain Yeh Dono Jahan.
Aagay Sidrah Se Manzil Thi Jo La-Makaan.
Jaye Jibreel Na Aap Pohanche Wahan.
Mezbaan Aap Ka Arsh Par Tha Jali.
Khila Tauheed Ka Aap Se Gulstaan.
Aap Ki Khushbo Se Mehka Sara Jahan.
Aap Ka Sani Dunya Mein Koi Kahan.
Pholon Ki Hai Sada Kahe Har Ik Kali.
Beti Hai Aap Ki Syeda-Tun-Nisa.
Shan Hai Dahar Mein Jis Ki Sab Se Juda.
Betay Do Aap Ke Hain Hussain-o-Hasan.
Bhai Hai Aap Ka Waliyon Ka Wali.
Tujh Ko Mula Ali Ne Hai Mula Kaha.
Laqab Jin Ka Hai Haider Sheh-e-La-Fata.
Oss Ke Sadqay Mein Khursheed Ko De Shifa.
Ho:RAZA HASAN: Ki Mere Mula Bhali.
