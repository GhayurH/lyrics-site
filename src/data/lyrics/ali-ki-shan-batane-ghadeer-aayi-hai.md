1
Ali Ki Shan Batane Ghadeer Aayi Hai.
Munafiqon Ko Jalane Ghadeer Aayi Hai.
Rasool Kar Do Woh Aylaan Jo Farz Hai Tujh Par.
Diya Yeh Hukam Khuda Ne Ghadeer Aayi Hai.

2
Hai Aaj Khatray Mein Kar-e-Risalat-e-Mursal.
Nabowaton Ko Bachane Ghadeer Aayi Hai.
Ali Wali Ki Wilayat Ka Ho Gaya Ayelaan.
Wila Ki Khushiyaan Manane Ghadeer Aayi Hai.

3
Rasool Maula Hain Jiske Hain Osske Maula Ali.
Samaa Yeh Aaj Dikhane Ghadeer Aayi Hai.
Ali Se Ishq Hai Kis Ko Hai Kis Ko Bugz-e-Ali.
Naqaab Sab Ke Hatane Ghadeer Aayi Hai.

4
Yeh Bole Qanbar-o-Miqdad, Buzar-o-Salman.
Khushi Ke Aaye Zamane Ghadeer Aayi Hai.
Kahin Pe Aag Giri Tu Siyah Chehrey Howay.
Lo Aaj Unn Ko Jalne Ghadeer Aayi Hai.

5
Bakhin Bakhin Tu Kaha Par Dilon Pe Teer Lagay.
Qoloob Unke Dukhane Ghadeer Aayi Hai.
Marey Be-Maut Kayi Loag Sun Ke Hukam-e-Rasool.
Lapaita Unko Qaza Ne Ghadeer Aayi Hai.

6
Ghadeer Waloo Piyon Ishq-e-Murtaza Ki Sharab.
Sharab-e-Ishq Pilane Ghadeer Aayi Hai.
Mujhe Hai Bazm-e-Wila Mein Aye:DANISH-E-ABBAS:.
Ali Ke Geet Sunane Ghadeer Aayi Hai.
