Yeh Sheh Ne Khat Mein Hai Likha Hussain Tanha Hai.

1
Youn Deney Sath Hamara Aye Bhai Aa Jao.
Wafa Nabi Se Nibhanay Aye Bhai Jao.
Tumhari Rahein Hain Takta Hussain Tanha Hai.
Lipat Ke Fizza Se Boli Yeh Beti Zahra Ki.
Nabi Ki Aal Ki Nusrat Ko Aaye Ga Koi.
Ghira Laeeno Mein Mera Hussain Tanha Hai.

2
Main Nana Jan Ke Waday Ko Ab Nibhaon Ga.
Main De Ke Akbar-o-Asghar Yeh Dein Bachaon Ga.
Do Mujh Ko Aa Ke Sahara Hussain Tanha Hai.
Tumhari Deed Se Hoga Sakon Iss Dil Ko.
Miley Gi Our Bhi Himmat Youn Meri Zainab Ko.
Woh Tak Ke Roti Hai Kitna Hussain Tanha Hai.

3
Laga Ke Aankhon Se Khat Ko Habib Rotay They.
Ghareeb-e-Zahra Ki Ghurbat Par Bain Kartay They.
Likha Jo Khat Mein Hai Dekha Hussain Tanha Hai.
Khuda Hi Jane Woh Zeeshan Kaisa Manzar Tha.
Jo Dekha Ibn-e-Mazahir Ne Khat Mein Hai Likha.
Janab-e-Zahra Ka Beta Hussain Tanha Hai.
