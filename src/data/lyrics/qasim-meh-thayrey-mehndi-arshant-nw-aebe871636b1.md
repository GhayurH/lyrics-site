Qasim meh thayrey mehndi Arshant^ banayii hay

Iss mehndi nu bana kay, ma Zahra lay ahyi hay

-Meh pura key tha wada, aj^ veer^ Hasan tayra

Tasveer^ Hasan thayrey, Zainab nay sajayi hay

-Sab ayatan qurani nay, sehray diya lariyan vich

Qasim dey lash^ sha nay, kayi ja toun utayi hay

-Qasim^ nu shagnay waylay, pyin ja kay qaza thak

dey

Qasim day khoon dey mehndi, Kubra nay lagayi hay

-Fizza nay dua key they, Qasim nu ban kay sahra

Shala jawani manay, bay wa dey kamayi hay

-Farva day dil day tuk^ ray, umat^ nay key tay logo

Tokeer^ nabi zadi, kewan lashay tay ayi hay
