Dilon Pe Nash-e-Mowadat Hain Jo Ubhare Howay.
Wo Qarz Ajr-e-Risalat Ka Hai Utare Howay.

1
Nahum Imam Muhammad Taqi Hain Itrat Mein.
Ali Raza Ki Imamat Ka Aks Dhare Howay.
Wohi Hai Shan-e-Sakha Jo Keh Thi Ab Wajd Mein.
Unhi Ki Misal Madadar Yeh Hamare Howay.

2
Wohi Hamesha Rahe Hain Aman Mein Momin.
Woh Jinn Ke Amr-e-Imamat Se Istekhare Howay.
Dar-e-Imam Taqi Par Gay Hain Jo Zair.
Wila Ke Naqsh Dilon Par Hain Sab Nikhare Howay.

3
Woh Chand Utra Imam-e-Raza Ke Gulshan Mein.
Iss Se Chehre Mohibon Ke Sab Sitare Howay.
Dar-e-Taqi Se Mila Jinn Ko Dars-e-Haq Mumtaz.
Rah-e-Talb Mein Wohi Kamyab Sare Howay.
