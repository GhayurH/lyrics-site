Nana Main Veer Di Maiyyat Nu Kiwein Kol Tere Dafnawaan.
Paye Chup Ke Teer Chalanday Ne Haye Zalimaan Maliyaan Rahwaan.

1
Haye Zehar Kaleja Cheer Gaya Utton Lash Te Teer Chalanday Ne.
Kadi Ja Ke Janazay Dunya Te Nayi Mur Ke Ghar Nu Aanday Ne.
Main Veer De Pak Janazay Nu Kiwein Mur Ke Ghar Le Jawaan.
Jaye Ummat Tere Rozay Te Mere Lal Nu Dafan Di Ja Dendi.

2
Tareekh Islam Di Aynj Baba Haye Khon De Athro Na Rondi.
Amari Payi Lal Udeekdi Aye Baqiya Wich Khol Ke Banwaan.
Zahra Diyaan Jaiyaan Ro Ro Ke Paiyaan Wain Hasan De Pandiyaan Ne.
Jadon Murran Janazay Veraan De Nana Bhaina Mar Jandiyaan Ne.

3
Main Teraan Naal Pamal Hoya Kiwein Bhainr Nu Kafan Wakhawaan.
Taboot Hasan Da Main Nana Wasday Teraan Choon Le Aaya.
Tatheer Di Waris Zainab Ne Haye Wain Hasan Da Aye Paya.
Mera Veer Ameer Zamane Da Kiwein Lash Choon Teer Hatawaan.
