Hai Wali-e-Mashhad Ye Barayi Tere Dar Ki
Taqdeer Banati Hai Gadayi Tere Dar Ki

1
Hai Rashk-e-Jinna Aaj Bhi Mashhad Ka Ilaqa
Khaliq Ne Ajab Shan Banai Tere Dar Ki
Be Noor Nigahon Ko Mila Noor-e-Mujasam
Inn Aankhon Mein Jab Khak Lagai Tere Dar Ki

2
De Phir Se Sada Behar-e-Karam Shah-e-Khurasaan
Bardasht Nahi Hoti Judai Tere Dar Ki
Lab Chom Ke Jhoom Uthay Malak Qabar Mein Aa Kar
Khushbo Meri Sansoon Se Jo Aayi Tere Dar Ki

3
Turbat Mein Farishton Ke Sawalaat Ke Badlay
Main Karta Raha Madah Sarai Tere Dar Ki
Jaroob Kashi Ka Jo Sharf Mujh Ko Miley Ga
Palkon Se Karon Ga Main Safai Tere Dar Ki

4
Wabasta Hai Chokhat Se Teri Marzi-e-Dawar
Phir Kis Ko Gawarah Ho Judai Tere Dar Ki
Rutba Mera Har Door Ke Shahon Se Sawa Hai
Hasil Hai Hasan Ko Jo Gadayi Tere Dar Ki
