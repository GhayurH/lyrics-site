1
Main Giya Waqt Nahi Hoon Keh Phir Aa Bhi Na Sakoon.
Zoaf Mein Tana-e-Agyar Ka Shikwa Kiya Hai?
Baat Kuch Sar Tu Nahi Hai Keh Utha Bhi Na Sakoon.
Zehar Milta Hi Nahi Mujh Ko Sitam Gar Warna.
Kya Qasam Hai Tere Milne Ki Keh Kha Bhi Na Sakoon?
