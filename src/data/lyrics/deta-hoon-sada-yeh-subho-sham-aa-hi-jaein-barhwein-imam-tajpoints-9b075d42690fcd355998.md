1
Deta Hoon Sada Yeh Subho Sham Aa Hi Jaein Barhwein Imam.
Rab Ke Ghar Mein Karne Ko Qiyam Aa Hi Jaein Barhwein Imam.
Gham Se Hum Nidhal Ho Gaye Aaein Kitne Saal Ho Gaye.
Sans Ho Na Jaye Yeh Tamam Aa Hi Jaein Barhwein Imam.

2
Bomb Dhamakay Karte Hain Shaqi Phir Bhi Dar Humein Nahi Koi.
Khatam Karne Zulm Ki Yeh Sham Aa Hi Jaein Barhwein Imam.
Hai Sada-e-Janat-ul-Baqi Aapko Qasam Hussain Ki.
Lene Fatima Ka Inteqam Aa Hi Jaein Barhwein Imam.

3
Haan Ghulami-e-Yazeed Mein Jo Hayat Kaat Rahe Hain.
Zair-e-Zameen Karne Woh Ghulam Aa Hi Jaein Barhwein Imam.
Iss Yazeed-e-Waqt Ke Liye Iltija Hai Yeh Hi Aapse.
Zulfiqar Kar Ke Be-Niyam Aa Hi Jaein Barhwein Imam.

4
Jis Ne Sani-e-Batool Ki Sham Mein Rida Thi Cheen Li.
Maut Ka Pilane Ossko Jaam Aa Hi Jaein Barhwein Imam.
Jab Talak Afraz Mein Hai Dam Bhejay Ga Aye Mola-e-Ummam.
Yeh Areezay Aapke Hi Naam Aa Hi Jaein Barhwein Imam.
