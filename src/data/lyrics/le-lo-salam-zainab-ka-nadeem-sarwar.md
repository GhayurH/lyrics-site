1
Le lo Salam Zainab Ka Nadeem Sarwar.
Shah-e-Shaheed-e-Karbala Le Lo Salam Zainab Ka.
Aayi Hoon Phir Se Nainwa Le Lo Salam Zainab Ka.
Haye Bhaiyya Bahatar Ka Chehlum Karon Gi.

2
Dill Khol Kar Aaj Matam Karon Gi.
Farsh-e-Azza Lo Bich Gaya.
Haye Bhaiyya Laho Mera Sajjad Roya.
Main So Saki Our Na Sajjad Soya.

3
Har Har Qadam Thi Karbala.
Haye Bhaiyya Watan Ja Ke Main Karon Gi.
Haye Ujray Madine Mein Kaise Rahoon Gi.
Mar Jaye Gi Yeh Ghamzada.

4
Haye Main Bhi Yahin Zairon Mein Rahon Gi.
Our Zairon Ki Main Khidmat Karon Gi.
Yeh Waqt Phir Kab Aaye Ga.
Haye Khoon Mein Bhara Kurta Le Ja Rahi Hoon.

5
Lekin Tumhari Qasam Kha Rahi Hoon.
Haan Haron Gi Na Main Honsla.
Saray Shaheed Ab Tumhare Hawalay.
Main Apne In Ghamzadoon Ko Sanbhale.

6
Haye Le Ja Rahi Hoon Qafila.
Haye Bhaiyya Sakina Ko Na Layi Zainab.
Haye Zindan Mein Oss Ko Choor Aayi Zainab.
Haye Roti Rahi Woh Meh Laqa.
