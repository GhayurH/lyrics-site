Wich Pardesaan Kyun Tur Pay Ne Veer Pyare.
Na Takiya Jandi Wari Kand Kar Ke Tur Gaye Ne Main Sab De Naam Pukaray.

1
Kiwein Be-Chain Ne Guzray Din Kiwein Rataan Ohh Main Kadiyaan.
Aa Soraj Taun Aapein Puch Charh De Chan Nu Puch Veera Main Kinj Din Raat Guzaray.
Jina Sab Maan Rakhe Mere Kadi V Roon Nahi Ditta.
Main Manzar Nayi Pul Sakdi Meriyaan Hawaan Sun Sun Ray Ronday Veran Saray.

2
Teri Jageer Walay Tak Kay Tainu Din Raat Udeekan Gi.
Chithiyaan Likh Likh Pawaan Gi Tainu Pohanchan Gaye Veera Kha Mere Hanjwaan Maray.
Bhira Dunya Tay Koi Akbar Kadi Jaway Na Ewein Chad Kay.
Rondi Dhi Da Baba Aye Ronday Chan Nu Aakh Daway Mehmal Toon Bhain Utaray.
