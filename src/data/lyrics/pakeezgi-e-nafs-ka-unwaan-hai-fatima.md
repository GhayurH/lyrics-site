1
Pakeezgi-e-Nafs Ka Unwaan Hai Fatima
Hasnain Ayatein Hain Tu Quran Hai Fatima
Mushkil Kusha Ki Zeest Ka Samaan Hai Fatima
Misal-e-Ali Mohafiz-e-Emaan Hai Fatima
Chokhat Ka Jis Ki Sara Zamana Ghulam Hai
Beta Ossi Ka Ab Bhi Jahan Ka Imam Hai

2
Qalab-e-Nabi Ki Khas Musarat Hai Fatima
Har Door Ke Rasool Ki Qismat Hai Fatima
Bas Kul Ki Kul Khadija Ki Dolat Hai Fatima
Ya Yeh Kaho Ke Juzw-e-Risalat Hai Fatima
Pore Kiye Hain Sare Taqaze Rasool Ke
Ahsan Hain Har Aik Nabi Par Batool Ke

3
Dein-e-Muhammadi Ka Sahara Hai Fatima
Tofan-e-Aakhrat Ka Kinara Hai Fatima
Rab Ne Mubahle Mein Pukara Hai Fatima
Likha Hai Jo Nabi Ne Woh Parah Hai Fatima
Jiss Ne Parha Woh Sahib-e-Eman Ho Giya
Jiss Ne Bhula Diya Wohi Shaitan Ho Giya

4
Hai Fatima Ali Ke Iradoon Ki Pukhtagi
Imran Ke Chaman Ko Bhi Bakhshi Hai Tazgi
Karti Rahi Ali ki Itaat Mein Bandgi
Faqoon Mein Jis Ne Kat Di Khush Ho Ke Zindagi
Dunya Ko Sabar-o-Zabt Ki Tasveer De Gayi
Jo Karbala Ke Waste Shabir De Gayei

5
Kafir Bata Raha Hai Musalmaan Ossi Ko Tu
Dolat Kade Ki Jiss Ke Bani Fatima Baho
Aangan Mein Jiss Ke Rooz Payamber Karein Wozu
Shabir Ki Ragoon Mein Hai Jiss Noor Ka Laho
Shabir Haq Paraston Ke Rehbar Ka Naam Hai
Shabir Anbiya Ke Muqadar Ka Naam Hai

6
Tareekh-e-Karbala Ka Muqadar Hai Fatima
Dunya-e-Aabro Ki Payamber Hai Fatima
Taqdees Jiss Ka Ghar Hai Woh Paikar Hai Fatima
Saya Hai Jo Nabi Ka Woh Chadar Hai Fatima
Chadar Jo Pardah Daroon Ki Taqdeer Ban Gayi
Paiwand Lag Ke Sorat-e-Tatheer Ban Gayi

7
Allah Ke Rasool Ki Tasveer Fatima
Ahmad Hai Noor, Noor Ki Tanveer Fatima
Quraan Ki Bolti Hoi Tafseer Fatima
Jab Likh Raha Tha Katib-e-Taqdeer Fatima
Sal-e-Alla Tha Hoor-o-Malak Ki Zuban Par
Goya Yeh Pehli Eid Hoi Aasman Par

8
Har Aik Nabi Ke Waste Qibla Hai Fatima
Qool-e-Nabi Hai Umm-e-Abeeha Hai Fatima
Parhte The Jo Ali Woh Wazeefa Hai Fatima
Aagay Khuda HI Jane Raza Kya Hai Fatima
Dar Hai Keh Koi Kufar Ka Fatwa Laga Na De
Mufti Kahin Mujhe Bhi Nuseri Bata Na De
