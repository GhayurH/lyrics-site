Haey sham e ghariban ko Zainab ne kaha ro ke – may ujarr gayi Ghazi
Mera bhai bhi mara gaya aur sar pe meray maa ki – chadar na rahi Ghazi

1
Maqtal may parri lashain hay khof zada manzar
Darya se chalay aao masoom yateemo ko - hay pyas lagi Ghazi

2
Lashon may kharri ho ke baba ko bulati hay
Kano se lahoo jari aur dar ki shiddat se - ye so na saki Ghazi

3
Akhtar kaha Zainab ne ro ro ke Khuda hafiz
Tu jis ka nigehban tha chadar na rahi sar pe – may Shaam chali Ghazi
