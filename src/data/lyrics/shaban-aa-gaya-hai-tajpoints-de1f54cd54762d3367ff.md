Khushiyon Ka Sath Le Ke Saman Aa Gaya Hai.

1
Dunya Mein Aa Rahi Hai Tasveer Fatima Ki.
Aamad Hai Aaj Logo Tafseer-e-Innama Ki.
Pehli Ka Chand Karne Ayelan Aa Gaya Hai.
Ho Momino Mubarik Takmeel-e-Panjtan Ki.
Pehne Ka Apne Sar Pe Dastar Jo Hasan Ki.
Dunya Mein Sabar Ka Woh Sultan Aa Gaya Hai.

2
Karti Thi Jis Ki Khatir Khud Syeda Duaein.
Jiss Ghazi Ki Jahan Mein Mashoor Hain Wafaein.
ALLAH Ka Woh Ban Ke Ahsan Aa Gaya Hai.
Bag-e-Hasan Mein Koi Ban Ke Bahar Aaya.
Tha Jis Ka Umm-e-Farwa Ko Intezar Aaya.
Khushiyaan Manao Qasim Zeeshan Aa Gaya Hai.

3
Iss Maah Mein Aya Humshakal Mustafa Ka.
Ghar Hai Laga Mehakne Dil Band-e-Murtaza Ka.
Akbar Haseen Ban Ke Quran Aa Gaya Hai.
Jo Hujjat-e-Khuda Hai Jo Hai Imam-e-Aakhir.
 Jawad Ghaib Ke Jo Parde Se Ho Ga Zahir.
Deen Ke Ghamo Ko Karne Darman Aa Gaya Hai.
