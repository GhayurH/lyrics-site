1
Kuch Tu Hawa Bhi Sard Thi Kuch Tha Tera Khiyal Bhi.
Dil Ko Khushi Ke Sath Sath Hota Raha Malal Bhi.
Baat Wo Aadhi Raat Ki, Raat Wo Pore Chand Ki.
Chand BHi Ain Cheit Ka Oss peh Tera Jamal Bhi.

2
Sab Se Nazar Bacha Ke Wo Mujh Ko Kuch Aise Dekhta.
Aik Dafa Tu Ruk Gayi Gardish-e-Maah-o-Saal Bhi.
Dil Tu Chamak Sake Ga Kiya, Phir Bhi Tarsh Ke Dekh Lein.
Sheesha Giran-e-Shehar Ke Hath Ka Yeh Kamal Bhi.

3
Oss Ko Na Pa Sake The Jab Dil Ka Ajeeb Haal Tha.
Aab Jo Palat Ke Dekhte, Baat Thi Kuch Muhaal Bhi.
Meri Talab Tha Aik Shaks Woh Jo Nahi Mila Tu Phir.
Haath Dua Se Youn Gira, Bhool Giya Sawal Bhi.

4
Oss Ki Sukhan Taraziyaan Mere Liye Dhaal Thin.
Oss Ki Hansi Mein chup Giya Apne Ghamon Ka Haal Bhi.
Gaah Qareeb Shah Rag, Gaah Baeid-e-Weham-o-Khowab.
Oss Ki Rafaqton Mein Raat, Hijar Bhi Tha Wisaal Bhi.

5
Oss Ke Hi Bazoon Mein Our Oss Ko Hi Sochte Rahe.
Jism Ki Khowaishon Pe The Rooh Ke Our Jaal Bhi.
Shaam Ki Na Samaj Hawa Poch Rahi Ik Pata.
Moaj-e-Howaye Koway Yaar, Kuch Tu Mera Khiyal Bhi.
