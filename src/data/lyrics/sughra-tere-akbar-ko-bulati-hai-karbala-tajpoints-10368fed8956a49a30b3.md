Sughra Tere Akbar Ko Bulati Hai Karbala.
Aangan Mein Tere Gham Ko Youn Lati Hai Karbala.

1
Sar Se Tumhare Bhai Ka Saya Bhi Chiney Ga.
Rab Se Tere Akbar Ko Milati Hai Karbala.
Tha Lash-e-Abbas Bhi Tarpa Yeh Dekh Kar.
Shabir Ko Akbar Pe Rulati Hai Karbala.

2
Hai Roza-e-Rasool Pe Sughra Ki Yeh Faryad.
Daman Judai Se Youn Jalati Hai Karbala.
Aye Fatima Ki Seerat-o-Kirdar Si Behan.
Ranj-o-Alam Mein Tujh Ko Rulati Hai Karbala.

3
Sughra Tere Bhai Humshakal-e-Payambar Ko.
Jaam Shahadat Ka Pilati Hai Karbala.
Kyun Kar Na Ho Farman Afzal Yeh Sar Zameen.
Gaudi Mein Bahatar Ko Sulati Hai Karbala.
