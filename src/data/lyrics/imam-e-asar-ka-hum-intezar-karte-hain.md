Imam e Asar Ka Hum Intezar Karte Hain.
Yeh Intezar Ibadat Guzar Kartay Hain.

1
Woh Aaein Gay Unhein Aana Hai Aane Walay Hain.
Nazar Jamaye Hoye Sab Zamane Walay Hain.
Unhi Ke Waday Pe Hum Aytbar Kartay Hain.
Abhi Bhi Waqt Hai Aye Munkir-e-Hussain Sanbhal.

2
Yeh Waqt Hath Se Nikla Tu Roye Ga Tu Kal.
Hamara Farz Hai Hum Hoshiyar Kartay Hain.
Arezay Nazar Kiye Ja Rahe Hain Saal Ba Saal.
Unhi Ko Bhaijtay Hain Likh Ke Apne Qalb Ka Haal.

3
Yeh Al-Ajal Ki Jo Hum Sab Pukar Kartay Hain.
Yeh Aakhri Hai Muhammad Yeh Barhwan Hai Ali.
Inhi Ke Qabza Qudrat Mein Hai Qayamat Bhi.
Yeh Khushk Pair Ko Bhi Saya Daar Kartay Hain.

4
Hussaini Khon Ke Tajar Khateeb Our Mulla.
Imam-e-Asar Ke Hathoon Hai Tera Qatal Likha.
Manadi Hum Tu Yehi Bar Bar Kartay Hain.
Jo Inn Ke Jad Ki Musibat Pe Sogowar Howa.

5
Gham-e-Hussain Mein Jo Shaks Ashkbar Howa.
Imam-e-Waqt Ossi Se Tu Pyar Kartay Hain.
Sadaein Deta Hai Kaba Bhi Karbala Bhi Inhein.
Imam-e-Asar Alh Aslam Aa Jaein.

6
Yazid Waqt Kaleja Figar Kartay Hain.
:REHAN AZMI: Yeh Zikar Hi Ibadat Hai.
Khudaya Bhaij De Oss Ko Jo Teri Hujjat Hai.
Bas Ik Dua Yehi Parwardigar Kartay Hain.
