Main Shaam Di Qaidan Nadeem Sarwar.
Ho Ke Zindaan Se Aazad Bint-e-Ali.

1
Soye Karb-o-Bala Kar Ke Matam Chali.
Pohanchi Jab Karbala Fatima Ki Pali.
Qabar-e-Shah-e-Shaheedan Pe Aa Ke Ruki.
Ghorey Ki Zeen Se Jaisay Ghazi Girey.

2
Aisay Bhai Ki Turbat Pe Zainab Giri.
Our Kehne Lagi Mere Mazloom Bekas Bhira.
Main Sham Di Qaidan Aayi Way Pardesiya Veera.
Haye Bhainr Di Thayi Aye Rehai Way Pardesiya Veera.

3
Safraan Da Kya Haal Sunawaan.
Wich Pardesan Baaj Bhirawaan.
Haye Rul Gayi Zahra Jayi Way Pardesiya Veera.
Baad Tere Oh Wela Aaya.

4
Naizay De Nal Ambari Jaya.
Maidi Chadar Shimar Lahayi Way Pardesiya Veera.
Rasiyaan De Wich Bandhiyan Banwaan.
Sang Bharjaiyaan Baaj Ridawaan.

5
Main Shehar Ba Shehar Phirayi Way Pardesiya Veera.
Haye Pohanchi Jad Main Sham Wichalay.
Haye Ghar Ghar De Wich Kar Ke Ujalay.
Aina Shamiyaan Eid Manai Way Pardesiya Veera.

6
Har So Tha Mahool Sharabi.
Sar Nangay Thi Behan Tumhari.
Main Gayi Darbar Bulayi Way Pardesiya Veera.
Ghairatan Wala Putar Muhari.

7
Khon-e-Jigar Akhiyaan Choon Jari.
Sajjad Ne Sham Bhulai Way Pardesiya Veera.
Haye Shimar Diyaan Jhirkaan Tu Dar Gayi.
Wich Zindan Sakina Mar Gayi.

8
Ik Cholay Wich Dafnai Way Pardesiya Veera.
Haye Qabar Hussain Koon Gal Nal La Ke.
Sainr:JAWAD-o-SARWAR: Aakhay.
Tera Chehlum Karan Aayi Way Pardesiya Veera.
