Paani Nahi Chahiye Mesum Abbas Urdu Lyrics
Dhal Gaya Din Woh Takti Rahi Rasta.
Hath Se Daman-e-Sabar Jata Raha.
Rukh Kiya Soye Darya Yeh Ro Kar Kaha.
Haye Ro Kar Kaha.
Chacha Jan Pani Nahi Chahiye.
Bas Aap Darya Se Aa Jaiye.
Meri Pyas Tu Tum Se Barh Kar Nahi.
Main Mar Jaon Gi Mera Kar Lo Yaqeen.
Mujhe Raas Aayi Na Yeh Sar Zameen.
Khudara Na Ab Our Tarpaiye.
Chacha Mujh Ko Maloom Hota Agar.
Nahi Aaein Gay Aap Bhi Laut Kar.
Tu Phir Mashk Deti Nahi Noha Gar.
Meri Mashk Khali Hi Le Aaiye.
Na Jane Woh Kaisi Ghari Aayi Thi.
Padar Se Ijazat Jo Dilwayi Thi.
Main Khud Apni Ghurbat Se Ghabrai Thi.
Khata Dar Guzar Meri Farmaiye.
Chacha Yeh Sakina Ka Wadah Raha.
Ke Ab Al-Atash Ki Na Doon Gi Sada.
Bina Aap Ke Hai Yeh Jeena Saza.
Jiyon Kaisay Itna Tu Batlaiye.
Sakina Pe Jaan Deney Walay Chacha.
Kiya Mujh Ko Kis Ke Hawalay Chacha.
Bhula Doon Gi Honton Ke Chalay Chacha.
Mujhe Aa Ke Seene Se Liptaiye.
Khabar Aap Ke Qatal Ki Youn Mili.
Mere Baba Jaan Ki Kamar Jhuk Gayi.
Rida Phaink Ke Ro Rahi Hain Phophi.
Yeh Manzar Zara Dekhney Aaiye.
Chidi Mashk Meri Koi Gham Nahi.
Meri Aas Tooti Koi Gham Nahi.
Main Pyasi Rahon Gi Koi Gham Nahi.
Dawa Mere Zakhmo Ki Ban Jaiye.
Yeh Mazhar Yeh Meesum Yeh Ahl-e-Azza.
Yehi Keh Ke Kartay Hain Matam Bapa.
Yeh Jumla Sakina Ka Hai Marsiya.
Bas Ab Aap Darya Se Aa Jaiye.
