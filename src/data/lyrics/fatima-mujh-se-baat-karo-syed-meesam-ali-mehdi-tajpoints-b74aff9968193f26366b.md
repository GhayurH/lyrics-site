1
Fatima Mujh Se Baat Karo Syed Meesam Ali Mehdi.
Qabar-e-Zahra Pe Ro Ke Bolay Ali, Fatima Mujh Se Koi Baat Karo.
Yeh Khamoshi Nahi Sahi Jati Fatima Mujh Se Koi Baat Karo.
Teri Furqat Ka Hai Asar Zahra Taab Mujh Mein Nahi Hai Jeene Ki.

2
Aik Lamhey Ko Hi Sahi Dil Ki Khatam Ho Jaye Gi Yeh Betabi.
Sun Le Aawaz Jo Ali Teri Fatima Mujh Se Koi Baat Karo.
Kalma Goyon Ki Ho Na Fikar Tumhein Pas Yeh Gham Gusaar Betha Hai.
Aaj Jee Bhar Ke Hal-e-Dil Keh Do Tum Se Zahra, Ali Ka Wadah Hai.

3
Ab Shikayat Nahi Karey Ga Koi Fatima Mujh Se Koi Baat Karo.
Janta Hoon Keh Dekhey Jate Nahi Tum Se Aanso Mere Yeh Shehzadi.
Kya Karon Gham Tumhare Jane Ka Mar Dalay Ga Mujh Ko Jeetay Jee.
Rook Loon Ga Main Aansoon Ko Abhi Fatima Mujh Se Koi Baat Karo.

4
Zindagi Meri Ho Gayi Soni Mujh Se Ab Kis Tarha Jiya Jaye.
Aaj Se Ho Gaya Hoon Main Tanha Aakhri Bar Youn Kiya Jaye.
Sirf Itni Si Iltija Hai Meri Fatima Mujh Se Koi Baat Karo.
Kho Raha Hoon Main Apni Binayi Our Jhuki Ja Rahi Hai Meri Kamar.

5
Tere Sadmay Mein Iss Zaeefi Ne Cheer Kar Rakh Diya Hai Mera Jigar.
Youn Dawa Ho Dil-e-Shikasta Ki Fatima Mujh Se Koi Baat Karo.
Teri Nazuk Mizagi Ke Aasaar Maut Ke Baad Bhi Hain Jalwa Figan.
Jaisay Shabbir Ke Liye Haye Tum Ne Kholay They Apne Band-e-Kafan.

6
Junbish-e-Lab Ko De Ke Aazadi Fatima Mujh Se Koi Baat Karo.
Keh Ke Meesam Yeh Uth Gaye Maula Tum Se Milney Ko Roz Aaon Ga.
Kar Ke Shab Bhar Tilawat-e-Quraan Ashk Turbat Pe Main Bahaon Ga.
Dil Mein Mere Yehi Tarap Hogi Fatima Mujh Se Koi Baat Karo.
