1
Waar Zalim Ne Chalaya Murtaza Ameer Te.
Sajday Wich Maula Ali Khanjar Si Hath Be Peer De.
Mushkilaan De Badshah Te Waar Ainj Chal Gaya.
Kho Gaye Hawas Oss Dam Maula Shabbar, Shabbir De.

2
Jis Nu Apni Zindagi Taun Haq Sada Hoya Azeez.
Oss De Bachray Ba Khuda Waris Aidi Jageer De.
Roko Aj Abbas Nu Farmaya Aye Hasnain Ne.
Zindah Rehna Koi Nayi Zalim Nanay Di Jageer Te.

3
Ainj Khuda Di Bargah Wich Sajdah Kita Murtaza.
Zulm Da Khanjar Lehraya Oss Waqt Bad Zameer Ne.
Jehra Zulm Kofay Hoya Bhul Nayi Sakda Oh Akhtar.
Ibn-e-Muljim Zarb Chalai Mustafa De Veer Te.
