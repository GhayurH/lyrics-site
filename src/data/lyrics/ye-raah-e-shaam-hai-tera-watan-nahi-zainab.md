Yeh Raah-e-Shaam Hai Tera Watan Nahi ZAINAB
Yahan Hussain Nahi Hai Hassan Nahi ZAINAB

1
Yahan Rida Ka Tasawur Muhaal Hai Bibi
Yahan Janazoun Ko Milta Nahi Kafan ZAINAB

2
Lahu Lahu Jou Qadam Hoarahay Hain ABiD K
Yeh Shaam Hai Yeh Ali Ka Chamman Nahi ZAINAB

3
Tu Chour Aai Thi Maqtal Main Bay Kafan Lashay
Sina Ki Nauk Pay Sar Hain Badan Nahi ZAINAB

4
Safar Main Saath Hai Qatil Bhi Tairay Ghazi Ka
Teray lye Bara is say Muhhan Nahi ZAINAB

5
Yeh Loug Bandhtay Hain Jou Teray Baazu main Rassan
Inhay Khayaal-e Rasool-e-Zamman Nahi ZAINAB

6
Hazaar Zulm-o-sitam Karkay Thak Gaya Zailm
Tairay azzam Main Zara Bhi Thakkan Nahi ZAINAB
