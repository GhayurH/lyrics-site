1
Barsein Gay Teer Yasrab Mein Lasha-e-Hasan Par.
Likha Tha Behtay Khoon Ne Zahra Ke Yeh Kafan Par.
Ugla Tha Khoon Hasan Ne Youn Zehar Ke Asar Se.
Yaad Aata Kha Ke Pathar Jis Waqt Zakhmi Sar Se.
Zainab Ka Khon Beh Kar Jam Jata Tha Rasan Par.
Tukray Jigar Ke Ugalay Shabar Ne Tu Pukarey.
Qasim Tumhare Tukray Youn Karbala Mein Hoon Gay.
Totay Gi Ik Qayamat Qasim Teri Dulhan Par.
Teeron Se Khoon Hasan Ka Chadar Se Saaf Kar Ke.
Kehti Thi Ro Ke Zainab Dekho Hussain Haye.
Likh Di Hai Sari Teeron Ne Karbala Badan Par.
Bolay Hussain Ro Kar Sath Ab Hasan Se Choota.
Suntay Ho Nana Dekho Yeh Kis Ne Hum Ko Loota.
Yeh Kaun Teer Zan Hai Nana Tere Hasan Par.
Wapis Hasan Ka Ghar Mein Jaisay Janaza Aaya.
Qasim Ka Khaimay Wapis Waisay Na Lasha Aaya.
Doraye Zalimo Ne Ghorey Youn Gulbadan Par.
Teeron Se Khoon Bhara Tha Jaisay Kafan Hasan Ka.
Durron Se Pathron Se Waisay Sakina Tera.
Abid Ne Khon Dekha Tha Tere Pairhan Par.
Taweez Ik Hasan Ne Qasim Ko Haye Bandha.
Bolay Asad Hai Parhna Tab Khol Kar Yeh Beta.
Jab Karbala Mein Mushkil Ho Shah-e-Be Watan Par.
