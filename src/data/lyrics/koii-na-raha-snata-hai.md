Maqtal se yehi aati thi sada
Koyi na raha sannata hay

1
Pardes may aa kar kaun luta

2
Allah ye kaisa manzar hay
Jo lash bhi hay wo be sar hay
Behta hay yahan khoon ka darya

3
Hay aaj fizain bhi ghamgeen
Lashon ke sarhanay koi nahi
Chaayi hay falak par gham ki ghata

4
Kuch khaimay nazar aatay hain jalay
Aur sookhay huay tootay koozay
Kia qafila tha ye pyason ka

5
Ye lash hay kis shehzaday ki
Kis tarha hua seena zakhmi
Qismat ne sitam kis maa pe kiya

6
Hain qad may barabar do lashay
Doobay hain lahoo ke darya may
Dono ko ajal ne loot liya

7
Ye maara gaya hay kaun haseen
Sehra hay kaheen kangna hay kaheen
Kaise ye sitam dunya ne kiya

8
Ik shair parra hay saahil par
Haibat ka abhi tak ye hay asar
Sehmi sehmi chalti hay hawa

9
Har simt udasi chaayi hay
Teeron pay kisi neend aayi hay
Be goar o kafan lasha hay parra
