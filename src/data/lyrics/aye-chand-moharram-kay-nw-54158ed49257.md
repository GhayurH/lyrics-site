Aye chand moharram kay tuu badli mein chala jaa

tujhay daikh kay maar jaye na beemar hai Sughra

Ghar Zahra ka lutnay ki khabar tuu nay sunai

tujhay daikh kay rooti hai moharram mein khudai

chodah(14) soo baras beetay sab kartay hein shikwa

Milnay kay liye bhai, ko baychain bari hai

kab say Ali Akbar ki yeh rahoon mein khari hai

bichri hai yeh muddat say, isay tuu na nazar aa

Veeran gharon mein na, usay neand hai aati

Akbar, ki judai hai, usay khoon rulati

qadmoon, kay nishan dhanp kay, baithi hai sar e rah

Gin gin kay jo sughra nay, yeh din hein guzaray

zinda, hai to Akbar kay, wadon kay saharay

din raa,t tarapti hai, isay or na tarpa

Bhaiya ki judai mein, pareshan hai rehti

har roo,z yeh nana say, roo roo kay hai kehti

Akbar, na milla nana, mein maar jaoongi tanha

Roonay nahee daitay mujhay ratoon, ko musalman

beema,ri say bayhaal hoon kuch roo,z ki mehman

haar sim,t say hai mujhko, ab moat nay gheera

Wada jo kiya behen ko seenay, say laga kay

Main shaa,di karon ga toh, teray paas hi aakay

Main saa,th(7) muharram ko, lauton ko na ghabra
