Nazm-e-Hayat o Rizq Ka Nazim Mera Taqi.
Shagird Inn Ka Ilm Hai Aalam Mera Taqi.

1
Sorat Nabi-e-Pak Ki Seerat Hussain Ki.
Haibat Mein Ali, Sabar Mein Kazim Mera Taqi.
Sari Jo Saltanat Hai Jahan Motaqeen Ki.
Oss Sari Saltanat Ka Hai Hakim Mera Taqi.

2
Janat Hai Inn Ka Ishq Tu Dozakh Hain Inn Ka Bair.
Yani Hai Khuld-o-Naar Ka Qasim Mera Taqi.
 Taseer Howa Jaisay Hai Lazim Hayat Ko.
Hai Youn Quran-e-Pak Ko Lazim Mera Taqi.
