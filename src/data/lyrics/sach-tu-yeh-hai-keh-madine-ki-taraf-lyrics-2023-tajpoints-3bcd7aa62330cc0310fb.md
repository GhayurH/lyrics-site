Sach Tu Yeh Hai Keh Madine Ki Taraf Jana Hai Naat.
Our Ijazat Ke Liye Pehle Najaf Jana Hai.

1
Jane Kis Aouj Pe Hai Abad-e-Muhammad Hona.
Iss Bulandi Ko Ali Ne Bhi Sharf Jana Hai.
Sarkashi Jis Ne Bhi Ki Syed-e-Do Alam Se.
Taig-e-Haider Ne Ossi Sar Ko Hadaf Jana Hai.

2
Aap Ummat Ke Padar Hain So Hamesha Hum Ne.
Ma Sabaq Nabiyon Ko Aaqa Ka Khalf Jana Hai.
Tarbiyaat Ga Hai Akbar, Abu Talib Ka Makaan.
Jis Ko Har Noor Ne Aagosh-e-Sadaf Jana Hai.
