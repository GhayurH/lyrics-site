Mera ghar jalaya lutiyan ridawan ro akhay Zainab kis pasay jawan

Abid bachawan yaan agh bujawaan, Ro akhay Zainab kis pasay jawan

Yusuf ton sohnay meray veer maaray, may shaam chaliyan be kafan saray

Qabaran banawaan yaan rasiyan pawaan, ro akhay Zainab kis pasay jawan

Mein deen bachawarn vich shaam aayi, sab logh vekharn meri be ridayi

Khutbay sunawaan yaan mu lukawaan, ro akhay Zainab kis pasay jawan

Khaimaych Qasim di laash aayi, choom choom ke tukray kavay Zahra jaayi

Hurn sehray lawaan yaan saf vichawaan, ro akhay Zainab kis pasay jawan

Jis veer de lay chadya Medina, jiwain qatil hoya bulna kadi na

Uno kafan pawan ya shaam jawaan, ro akhay Zainab kis pasay jawan

Veeran de qatil khaimaych aagay, ay Hassan Zainab Abid nu akhay

Hurn jal may jawaan ya bar awaan, ro akhay Zainab kis pasay jawan
