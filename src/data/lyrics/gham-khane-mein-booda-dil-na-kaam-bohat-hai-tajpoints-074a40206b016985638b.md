Gham Khane Mein Booda Dil Na Kaam Bohat Hai.
Yeh Rang Keh Kam Hai May Gulfam Bohat Hai.

1
Kehte Howay Saqi Sey Haya Aati Hai Warna.
Hai Youn Keh Mujhe Durd-e-Teh-e-Jaam Bohat Hai.
Ne Teer Kamaan Hai Nah Siyaad KeyeeN Mein.
Goshe Mein Qafs Keh Mujhe Aaram Bohat Hai.

2
Kiya Zohd Ko Manoo Keh Nah Ho Garchay Reyai.
Padaash Amal Ki Tamaye Khaam Bohat Hai.
Hain Ahl-e-Khird Kiss Rawish-e-Khaas Peh Nazaan?
Pabastagi Rasm-o-Rah-e-Aam Bohat Hai.

3
Zamzam Hi Peh Choro Mujhe Kiya Toof-e-Haram Say?
Aalodah Beh May Jaamay Ehraam Bohat Hai.
Hai Qahar Gar Aab Bhi Nah Banay Baat Keh Unn Ko.
Inkaar Nahi Our Mujhe Ibraam Bohat Hai.

4
Khoon Ho Ke Jigar Aankh Se Tapka Nahi Aye Marg.
Rehne De Mujhe Yaan Keh Abhi Kaam Bohat Hai.
Ho Ga Koi Aysa Bhi Keh Ghalib Ko Na Jane?
Shayar Tu Wo Acha Hai Peh Badnaam Bohat Hai.
