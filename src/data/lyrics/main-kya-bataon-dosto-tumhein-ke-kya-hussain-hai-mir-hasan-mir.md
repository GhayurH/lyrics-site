1
Main Kya Bataon Dosto Tumhein Ke Kya Hussain Hai Mir Hasan Mir.
Bashar Tu Kya Hai Anbiya Ka Aasra Hussain Hai.
Hussain Fakhar-e-Anbiya Hussain Dein Ki Laaj Hai.
Har Aik Dil Pe Aaj Bhi Hussain Hi Ka Raaj Hai.

2
Yazid Kab Ka Mit Chuka Hussain Tha Hussain Hai.
Hai Dil Mein Tere Bugz Our Zabaan Pe Hai Khuda Khuda.
Dar-e-Hussain Chor Ke Bhatakne Walay Sun Zarra.
Khuda Bhi Oss Taraf Hai Jis Taraf Mera Hussain Hai.

3
Sina Ki Nook Se Khuda Ne Apna Zikar Jab Suna.
Tu Oss Ne Tere Zikar Ko Yeh Martaba Atta Kiya.
Jahan Azan Bhi Nahi Wahan Bhi Ya Hussain Hai.
Idhar Rasool-e-Pak Hain Udhar Janab-e-Syeda.

4
Hain Pusht Par Hasan Tu Samne Ali-e-Murtaza.
Bas Inn Ke Darmiyan Hi Pala Barha Hussain Hai.
Tha Ik Nuseri Shoq Mein Najaf Se Karbala Gaya.
Jo Dekha Jalwa Noor Ka Wahan Tu Oss Ne Yeh Kaha.

5
Qasam Ali Ki Aaj Se Mera Khuda Hussain Hai.
Miley Jo Par Tu Ro Diya Khushi Se Fitrus-e-Hazeen.
Kaha Paron Ko Dekh Kar Woh Arsh Ho Ya Ho Zameen.
Wohi Hai Marzi-e-Khuda Jo Chahta Hussain Hai.
