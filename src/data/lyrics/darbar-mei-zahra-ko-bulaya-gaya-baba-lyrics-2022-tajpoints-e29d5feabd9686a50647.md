1
Darbar Mei Zahra Ko Bulaya Gaya Baba Mujtaba Hassan.
Hans Hans Ke Wahan Mujh Ko Rulaya Gaya Baba.
Maloom Tha Darwazay Ke Main Pichey Khari Hoon.
Phir Bhi Marey Pehlu Pe Giraya Gaya Baba.

2
Zahra Ko Yehi Baat Rulaye Gi Hamesha.
Beti Pe Teri Hath Uthaya Gaya Baba.
Ghar Jal Gaya Talwar Ali Ne Na Uthai.
Jo Wadah Kiya Tha Woh Nibhaya Gaya Baba.

3
Jis Dar Pe Parha Kartay They Tum Aayat-e-Tatheer.
Hanstay Hoye Woh Dar Bhi Jalaya Gaya Baba.
Roney Kahan Detey They Musalmaan Mujhe Ghar Mein.
Ghar Door Madine Se Banaya Gaya Baba.

4
Jis Roz Se Zalim Ne Mera Bazu Hai Tora.
Zainab Ko Na Godi Mein Uthaya Gaya Baba.
 Sajjad Woh Kehti Thi Kamar Jhuk Gayi Meri.
Mushkil Se Teri Qabar Pe Aaya Gaya Baba.
