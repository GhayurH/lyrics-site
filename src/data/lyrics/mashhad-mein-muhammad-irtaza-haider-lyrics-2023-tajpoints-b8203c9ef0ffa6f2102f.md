Mashhad Mein Muhammad Irtaza Haider

1
Uthaya Main Ne Dua Ko Jo Hath Mashhad Mein.
Tu Mere Hath Mein Thi Kainat Mashhad Mein.
Khuda Ka Qarb Mila, Our Kabhi Khuda Hi Mila.
Hoye Hain Aisay Kayi Tajurbat Mashhad Mein.
Hijabiyat Keh Tadrees Qum Mein Hoti Hai.
Parha Raha Hai Koi Deniyaat Mashhad Mein.

2
Rehn Rakhi Hai Sar-e-Karbala Yeh Dil Ki Zameen.
Rakhay Zameen Ke Hain Kagzat Mashhad Mein.
Suna Wafaon Ka Qissa Janab-e-Meesum Se.
Hoi Khuda Se Khudai Pe Baat Mashhad Mein.
Mujhe Raza Ki Lehad Ka Tawaf Karne Do.
Dikhaon Ga Main Tumhein Mojzaat Mashhad Mein.

3
Hamari Rohein Milein Gi Najaf Ki Rahoon Mein.
Milein Gi Jism Ki Kuch Baqiyat Mashhad Mein.
Uthaye Phirta Hai Kashkool Izrail Yahan.
Mili Hai Maut Ko Aksar Hayat Mashhad Mein.
Dikhaein Chehra e Aqdas Janab-e-Qayam Tu.
Dhalay Firaq Ki Haider Yeh Raat Mashhad Mein.
