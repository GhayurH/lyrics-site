1
Apna Na Wekhan Layi Loko Sughra Bemar Diya Akhiyaan.
Nawaan Diyaan Fehristaan Ro Ro Te Mur Mur Ke Par Di Raiyaan.
Na Likhiya Howay Ga Baba Akbar De Naa De Naal Mera.
Aye Soch Ke Veer De Sehrey Di Sughra Ne Rakh Layiyaan Lariyaan.

2
Ik Wangoon Sughra Asghar Nu Haye Mare Sadma Judai Da.
Sughra Te Asghar Dowaan Di Hanjwaan Nal Akhiyaan Ne Bhariyaan.
Aynj Ja Ke Kool Aye Chumdi Aye Chache Diyaan Banwaan Nu Sughra.
Jiwein Chache Ghazi Di Banwaan Sughra Ne Wekh Laiyaan Katiyaan.

3
Main Takiya Baba Be-Parda Phophiyaan Nu Galiyaan Bazaraan Wich.
Sang Veer Mera Nazaan Paliya Rat Ronda Aye Pa Kariyaan.
Sughra De Hath Na Chutday Ne Zainab Kulsoom De Hathaan Choon.
Khamosh Aye Sughra Nu Gal La Zainab Kulsoom Raiyaan Khariyaan.

4
Rawaan Te Beh Ke Akbar Nu Aye Jandiyaan Takdi Rayi Ujari.
Akbar Haye Akbar Keh Keh Ke Sughra Diyaan Sawaan Ne Rokiyaan.
 Zeeshan Biyaan Kar Sakda Nayi Lamhaat Ohh Sare Dardaan De.
Ik Pasey Sughra Ik Pasay Band Bohe De Rowan Phophiyaan.
