1
Giraftar K Bazo Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.
Bandhay Jo Sitamgar Ne Giraftar Ke Bazu.
Yaad Aaye Sakina Ko Alamdar Ke Bazu.
Bhaiyya Tere Jholay Ko Jhulaein Gay Farishtay.
Jholay Se Hamail Hain Marey Pyar Ke Bazu.
Baba Ki Kamar They Woh Phophi Jaan Ki Rida They.
Bazu Na They Logo Marey Ghamkhuwar Ke Bazu.
Sar Tasht Se Baba Ka Chala Aaye Ga Khud Hi.
Phailay Nazar Aaein Gay Jo Haq Daar Ke Bazu.
Seene Se Lipat Jaye Sakina Tere Baba.
Sapne Mein Bhi Khul Jaein Jo Dedar Ke Bazu.
Sajjad Ke Seene Hi Pe So Jaye Sakina.
Khulwa Dein Chacha Jan Jo Beemar Ke Bazu.
Tofan-e-Bala Khaiz Mein Thamay Hoye Mujh Ko.
Hain Karb-o-Bala Bas Tere Kirdar Ke Bazu.
Sajjad Ko Zindaan Se Koi Kam Hai Warna.
Dar Khud Mein Bana Lein Abhi Dewar Ke Bazu.
Mumkin Nahi Mehshar Mein Keh Bibi Tere Hotey.
Bandhwaye Koi Sheh Ke Azadar Ke Bazu.
Gar Matam-e-Sarwar Mein Na Kam Aaein Tu Wallah.
Kat Jaein Kaleem Aaj Yeh Bekar Ke Bazu.
