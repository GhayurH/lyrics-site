1
Kal Jal Rahe The Khaimay Ab Roza Jal Raha Hai.
Aye Mula Tum Bata Do Ab Hum Pe Farz Kya Hai.
Momin Ka Iss Jahan Mein Hathyar Bas Dua Hai.
Bint-e-Ali Ka Roza Woh Sham Ka Sawera.
Yani Damishq Mein Jo Saya Hai Karbala Ka.
Hai Aas Be-Kason Ki Mazloomo Ka Sahara.
Sad Haif Woh Sitam Ke Nargay Mein Aa Gaya Hai.
Bint-e-Ali Ne Rakhi Bunyad Majlison Ki.
Jo Karbala Mein Guzri Woh Sham Mein Sunai.
Pehli Saf-e-Azza Bhi Jis Shehar Mein Bichai.
Ab Oss Nagar Mein Haye Noha Na Marsiya Hai.
Ahl-e-Haram Pe Aakhir Kab Zulm Khatam Ho Ga.
Kab Zalimon Ka Ho Ga Youm-e-Hisab Mula.
Afsos Shamiyo Ka Ab Tak Chalan Na Badla.
Batla Do Unn Ko Aa Kar Ab Zulfiqar Kya Hai.
Mula Mein Janta Hoon Jo Zulm Utha Rahe Hain.
Har Zulm-e-Karbala Par Aanso Baha Rahe Hain.
Aap Apni Nahiya Mein Noha Suna Rahe Hain.
Woh Aap Dekhte Hain Sab Hum Ne Jo Suna Hai.
Hai Sheh Ke Matami Ka Matam Hi Kam Mula.
Har Saal Matami Jo Jate The Sham Mula.
Inn Sab Ko Ab Suna Dein Jaldi Payam Mula.
Jo Band Kal Tha Rasta Woh Aaj Khul Gaya Hai.
Phir Jald Din Woh Aaye Majid Bhi Sham Jaye.
Noha Wajhi Ja Kar Bibi Ko Phir Sunaye.
Phir Se Sada Yeh Gonji Haye Hussain Haye.
Jo Tum Ko Manta Hai Tum Ko Pukarta Hai.
