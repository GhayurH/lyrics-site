1
Mola Raza Ke Baba Mohammed Abbas Karim.
Maula Raza Ke Baba Dunya Se Ja Rahe Hain.
Mazloom Ka Janaza Mazdoor La Rahe Hain.
Baghdad Ka Jo Pul Hai Oss Par Rakhi Hai Gathri.

2
Maiyyat Pe Roney Wala Waris Nahi Hai Koi.
Iss Haal Par Paraye Aanso Baha Rahe Hain.
Pandrah Baras Rahe Hain Zindaan Mein Mere Maula.
Sarwar Ki Ladli Ka Parhtay Rahe Hain Noha.

3
Martay Hoye Bhi Unn Ka Noha Suna Rahe Hain.
Aisa Asar Dikhaya Haroon Ke Zehar Ne.
Zanjeer Mein Ulajh Kar Utray Badan Ke Tukray.
Maiyyat Nahi Kafan Mein Tukray Utha Rahe Hain.

4
Pacheeswin Rajab Ko Tooti Ajab Qayamat.
Hai Satwein Ali Ki Dunya Se Aaj Rukhsat.
Janat Mein Farsh-e-Matam Haider Bicha Rahe Hain.
Do Betiyon Ki Haye Qismat Hai Aik Jaisi.

5
Ro Bhi Nahi Saki Hain Maiyyat Pe Woh Padar Ki.
Syedaniyon Ko Zalim Kitna Sata Rahe Hain.
Kazim Ki Lash Haye Jis Waqt Pul Pe Pohanchi.
Roney Ko Aa Gayi Thi Pehlu Shikasta Bibi.

6
Le Kar Behan Ko Apni Abid Bhi Aa Rahe Hain.
:AZLAN AZMI: Jab Main Kazmain Pohancha.
Dekha Karim Main Ne Maula Ka Qaid Khana.
Ab Tak Wohi Manazir Mujh Ko Rula Rahe Hain.
