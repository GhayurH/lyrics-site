Hasan Raza di mayyat tay teeran diyan hoiyan chawan

1
Jadon pak janaza veeran da haey chaya aan bhirawan

2
1) Kadi maran vajjan haey nanay nun kadi dain sadawan babay nu
Kalsoum Zainab ne jadh dithiyan veeran diyan tutdiyan sawan

3
2) Qasim diyan phupyan mar jan gaiyan eh soch ke Farwa ne puchiya
May Hasan de dil diyan tukrayian nun bhena tun kinjh lukawan

4
3) Zahra diya jaiyan paray nad-e-Ali chikhay teer veeran diyan maiyat tun
Karbal de aan tun pehlan hi kaiyan rondiyaan baaj ridawan

5
4) Zainab Zahra nun eh kehndi si aaj baqiya chor ke aa mayin
Haye khoon aloodha kafan de vich tere putar di maiyat vakhawan

6
5) Haey Najaf day passay Zainab ujhri ro ro ke piyo tun puchdi si
Dus veer Hasan di pag baba Qasim nun kinjh pehnawan

7
6) Akhtar piya momin taboot uthay mola ton ro ro mangday
Mazloom Hasan de sadqay sub pooriya hon duawan
