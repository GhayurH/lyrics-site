YA BAABE HAWAIJ YA MOLA

SAQQA E HARAM RAKHLE NA BHARAM HUM DAR PE TUMHARE AAYE HAY

TUM BHI NAHI HUM KO TUKHRANA  HUM DUNIYA KE TUKHRAYE HAY

AB SAARE SAHARE TOOT CHUKE HUME APNE PARAYE LOOT CHUKE

TUM APNI PANAHO MEI LELO HUM RAN SE BHOT GHABRAYE HAY

MOLA TUM BAABE HAWAIJ HO ROZE PE UNHE BHI BULWALO

GHURBAT KE SABAB JO AA NA SAKE HAM UNKE AREEZE LAAYE HAY

AB QABR SE PAANI LIPTA HAY SAQQA E SAKINA PYASA HAY

AB HASHR TALAK DARIYA SAARE DENE KO SALAAMI AAYE HAY

IK JAANIB ROZA TUMHARA HAY SHABIR KA ROZA EK TARAF

BAINUL HARAMAN AAKAR HUMNE DAMAN APNE PHELAAYE HAY

KYA AP PE AAKA GUZRI THI KUCH KAHIYE MUSIBAT APNI BHI

BIN HAATHO KE KAISE LEHRA KAR GHORE SE ZAMEEN PAR AAYE HAY

ZAHRA KE LAAL KA PYASA GALLA GARDAN KI PUSHT SE KATTA GAYA

KHANJAR KI HAR IK ZARBAT PAR YEH ARZ O SAMAA THARRAYE HAY

EK QABR HAY SHAAM KE ZINDAAN MEI EK MASHQ ABHI TAK KHAALI HAY

HUM PYASI SAKINA KI KHATIR AANKHO MEI AANSU LAAYE HAY

DARBAAR SAKHI ABBAS KA THA KUCH MAANGNA THA LAAZIM MAANGA

LEKIN YEH HAQIQAT BHI HAI QAMAR HAM PURSA DENE AAYE HAY

SAQQAE HARAM…
