Nana Tere Hasnain Hain Hum Gham e Madar Sunane Aaye Hain
Teri Turbat Pe Ladle Tere Chand Tukre Sanad Ke Laye Hain.

1
Innama Rooz Jiss Pe Parhte They Aab Wo Darwaza Jal Gaya Nana.
Tere Dar Pe Diya Jalane Hum Ossi Dar Ko Bhuja Ke Aaye Hain.
Daag Duroon Ke Kaise Dikhlati Baap Ho Tum Se Beti Kya Kehti.
Jiss Kissa Ke Taley They Hum Sare Neil Oss Key Talay Chupaye Hain.

2
Dil Pe Ayi Thi Moat Ki zarbat Sar Ke Baloon Tak Aa Gayi Gurbat.
Jab Yeh Kehte They Loag Zahra Ne Dein Ke Maslay Bhulaye Hain.
Mushkilon Se Qadam Uthati Hai Do Qadam Chal Ke Baith Jati Hai.
Bin Sahare Wo Chal Nahi Sakti Zakhm Ayse Jigar Peh Khaye Hain.

3
Hath Uthta Nahi Hai Pehlu Se Haye Mohsin Ke Bain Uthtay Hain.
Maa Ka Bachna Mohaal Hai Nana Aab Yateemi Ke Sar Peh Saye Hain.
Bolay Akbar Yeh Ro Ke Shehzaday Sirf Tehreer Hi Nahi Thi Wo.
Bizat-o-Mini Ke Wo Tukrey The Jo Hawa Mein Gaye Uraye Hain.
