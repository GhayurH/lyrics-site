1
Hijab e Ghaib Sy Mehdi Zaror Aein Gaey Qamar Abbas Naqvi.
Hijab-e-Gaib Se Mehdi Zaror Aaein Gay.
Khuda Ke Noor Ka Jalwa Humein Dikhaein Gay.
Khuda Ki Hujat-e-Aakhir Ke Jitnay Munkir Hain.
Zahoor Hone Do Bach Kar Kahan Woh Jaein Gay.
Milein Gay Shan Se Hum Ya Ali Madad Keh Kar.
Imam-e-Asar Se Jab Hath Hum Milaein Gay.
Suna Sakay Na Fazail Jo Daar Par Meesum.
Zaban Mehdi Se Le Kar Woh Sab Sunaein Gay.
Phir Uth Ke Narah-e-Haider Lagaein Gay Salman.
Imam Mohar Aqeedon Pe Jab Lagaein Gay.
Agar Zahoor Se Pehle Hi Mar Gaye Hum Loag.
Tu Aa Ke Qabar Se Mehdi Humein Jagaein Gay.
Tu Chahta Hai Tere Ghar Abhi Imam Aaein.
Shorow Kar De Hadees-e-Kisa Woh Aaein Gay.
Nabi Ke Barhwein Betay Ki Main Raiyyat Hoon.
Yeh Essa Khud Tumhein Esaiyo Bataein Gay.
Tum Unn Ko Dekh Ke Khud Khandani Bolo Gay.
Jo Zikar-e-Mehdi Pe Uth Uth Ke Sar Jhokaein Gay.
Imam-e-Waqt Pe Jo Ungliyaan Uthaein Asad.
Woh Apna Shajrah Hai Kya Yeh Bata Na Paein Gay.
