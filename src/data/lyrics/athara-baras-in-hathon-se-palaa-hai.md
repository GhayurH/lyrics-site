Athara Baras In Hathon Se Palaa Hai
Marne Ki Ijazat Jab Mangi Akbar Ne Tu Yeh Sarwar Ne Kaha
Zainab Se Ijazat Lo Beta Tum Par Hai Zyada Haq Oss Ka
Khaimay Mein Phophi Amma Ke Jab Atharah Baras Wala Pohancha
Mangi Jo Ijazat Akbar Ne Zainab Ne Pukara Aye Beta
Atharah Baras Tum Ko Inn Hathoon Se Pala Hai
Main Kaisay Kahon Akbar Marne Ke Liye Jao
Akbar Tujhey Dekhon Tu Chalti Hain Meri Sansein
Kis Tarha Tujhey Zakhmi Dekhein Gi Meri Aankhein
Mere Gulbadan Mere Nojawaan
Kalejay Mein Jab Lagay Gi Sina
Meri Jan Tum Na Seh Pao Gay
Tumhein Kis Tarha Sanbhalay Gi Maa
Maloom Hai Zainab Ko Nazuk Tera Seena Hai
Ik Bar Zara Apne Baba Ki Taraf Dekho
Kis Tarha Uthaein Gay Woh Tere Janazay Ko
Ajab Haal Hai Kamar Mein Hai Khum
Hai Abbas Ki Judai Ka Gham
Ghareeb Al-Watan Bohat Thak Chuka
Zaeefi Bhi Hai Nazar Bhi Hai Kam
Aye Lal Mera Bhai Seh Roz Ka Pyasa Hai
Main Palney Wali Hoon Itna Tu Mera Haq Hai
Socha Tha Ke Ik Din Main Jab Jaon Gi Dunya Se
Lehad Mein Mujhe Utaray Ga Tu
Yeh Arman Hai Yehi Aarzu
Rahe Tu Sada Mere Samne
Rukay Sans Bhi Tere Robaro
Aye Lal Abhi Mera Arman Adhora Hai
Jab Pehle Pehal Tujh Ko Hathoon Pe Uthaya Tha
Yeh Kehte Hoye Main Ne Seene Se Lagaya Tha
Meri Zindagi Mere Ladlay
Kissi Ki Tujhey Nazar Na Lagay
Tujhey Dekhney Ko Be Chain Thi
Sakoon Mil Gaya Tujhey Dekh Ke
Tu Aankhon Ka Tara Hai Tu Raaj Dulara Hai
Sughra Se Kiya Wadah Kya Yaad Nahi Akbar
Beemar Madine Mein Mar Jaye Na Ro Ro Kar
Ossay Aas Hai Ke Aao Gay Tum
Jo Wadah Kiya Nibhao Gay Tum
Sakina Se Woh Bohat Door Hai
Behan Se Ossay Milao Gay Tum
Beta Tumhein Sughra Se Woh Wadah Nibhana Hai
Akbar Teri Shadi Ka Armaan Hai Bohat Hum Ko
Hum Seh Nahi Paein Gay Aye Lal Tere Gham Ko
Jabeen Par Teri Jo Sehra Bandhay
Tamana Hai Woh Phophi Dekh Le
Dulhan Ko Teri Sajaon Gi Main
Aye Mere Haseen Jawaan Ladlay
Zainab Ne Abhi Tujh Ko Dulha Bhi Banana Hai
Yeh Kehne Lagay Akbar Zainab Ki Sada Sun Ke
Afzal Tu Nahi Amma Main Dilbar-e-Zahra Se
Akelay Hain Woh Nahi Hai Koi
Musibat Mein Hai Zarorat Meri
Nazar Kis Tarha Milaon Ga Main
Agar Aap Ne Ijazat Na Di
Kyun Aap Ke Honton Pe Iss Waqt Yeh Noha Hai
Akbar Ki Sada Sun Kar Zainab Ne Ijazat Di
Rukhsat Jo Hoye Akbar Kaisi Woh Qayamat Thi
Duaein Khuda Se Karti Thi Woh
Mere Lal Ki Khuda Khair Ho
Kaha Ya Ali Madad Kijiye
Main Rukhsat Karon Jawaan Lal Ko
ZEESHAN-o-RAZA Phir Na Zainab Ne Pukara Hai
