Chaya holay holay pak Ali dheeyaan da naam musallay tay

1
Koy aaiy jay rang wich zarab lagee,  yaad aa gayi Shaam musallay tay

2
1) Baabay ku aain namaaz samajh dheeyaan masroof hun tashhud wich
Kaad qutila da aaiylaan theeya
Much gaaiy kuhraam musallay tay

3
2) Chehray tay phool jo chunan laga Sajaad jay safaar ju deed gaee
Lagay aawan shoar zanjeeraa day
Koy naeee araam musallay tay

4
3) Saday dhalay hoaiy bakht da wasta e zakhmee baba ghaar jaldi ah
Ghayoor Imam ku dheeyaan nay
Milay aaan salaam musallay tay

5
4) Chum kadam Ali jay baba day roo kay Abbas sawal kita
Kufay day sadar bazaar da kayoon
Payaa chainda aay naam musallay tay

6
5) Eek roaza qatal nahee hoya naee sirf namaaz shaheed thaee
Paya khoon day wich saar disdha ha
Sara Islam musallay tay

7
6) Shah wal wal aakhaya putraa ku ghaar wanjan da jaldi raha bolo
Maida dl gabranda ay logaa wich
Hay balwa aaam musallay tay
