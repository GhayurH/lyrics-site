Khaimazan Hain Yeh Jinna Do Haram Ke Darmiyaan
Aashiqon Ke Karwaan Hain Do Haram Ke Darmiyaan

1
Aasmaan Wale Kahan Hain Do Haram Ke Darmiyaan
Do Jahan Ke Do Jahan Hain Do Haram Ke Darmiyaan
Jis Muhammad Se Yahan Chahein Mulaqatein Karein
Aiye Iss Arsh Par Allah Se Batein Karein

2
Hai Aik Taraf Abbas Tere
Our Aik Taraf Hain Mula Hussain
Aye Arz-e-Yaqeen Kabe Ki Zameen
Kehti Hai Tujhe Kaba Qosain

3
Yeh Zameen Chand Sitaron Ki Jabeen Lagti Hai
Kehkishaan Iss Ke Tasaduq Mein Haseen Lagti Hai
La-Makani Ki Hadoon Se Yeh Qareen Lagti Hai
Yeh Tu Qosain Se Aagay Ki Zameen Lagti Hai

4
Jo Arsh Pe Tha Pehle Nalain
Oss Ne Bhi Utari Hai Nalain
Han Bolne Lagti Hai Khamoshi-e-Jazbaat Yahan
Ishq Ki Jari Hai Sadiyon Se Ibadaat yahan

5
Sare Aashiq Humein Mil Jate Hain Ik Sath Yahan
Our Nabiyon Se Bhi Hoti Hai Mulaqat Yahan
Adam Bhi Yahin Esa Bhi Yahin
Milte Hain Yahin Par Zulqarnain

6
Is Zameen Par Chale Aana Hi Hai Mairaj-e-Bashar
Sidra-e-Ishq Hai Kab Aam Hai Yeh Rahguzar
Tair-e-Aqal Ke Jalte Hai Yehin Par Sheh Par
Farsh Ban Jata Hai Khud Arsh Yahan Par Aakar

7
Qadmon Se Na Chal Ja Sar Ke Bal
Are Rehti Hai Yahan Umm-ul-Hasnain
Teri Qismat Se Woh Qismat Ne Sitara Dekha
Khalq Kar Ke Tujhe Khaliq Ne Dobara Dekha

8
Sab Zameeno Pe Faqat Tera Ijara Dekha
Our Wahin Par Yeh Zamane Ne Nazara Dekha
Haan Daman Mein Tere Ik Sath Bhi Gar
Aein Tu Sama Jaein Konain

9
Sar Zameen Par Teri Hoti Hai Bashar Ki Mairaj
Dekh Lena Tujhe Hoti Hai Nazar Ki Mairaj
Tujh Pe Sajday Se Howa Karti hai Sar Ki Mairaj
Khatam Hai Tujh Pe Bulandi Ke Safar Ki Mairaj

10
Kya Samjhein Gaye Woh Mairaj Teri
Jo Samjhe Nahi Mairaj Ki Ain
Kahin Jate Howay Dekha Hi Nahi Dono Ko
Ishq Ne Dekha Hai Barson Se Wahin Dono Ko

11
Soratan Jante Hain Ahl-e-Yaqeen Dono Ko
Dhondne Jao Na Dunya Mein Kahin Dono Ko
Aashoor Ke Din Matam Karte
Aein Gaye Nazar Tum Ko Mirain
