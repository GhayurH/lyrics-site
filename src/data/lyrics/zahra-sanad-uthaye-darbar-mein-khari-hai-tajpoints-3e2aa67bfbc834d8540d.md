1
Zahra Sanad Uthaye Darbar Mein Khari Hai.
Deen-e-Nabi Ki Waris Haq Mang Rahi Hai.
Tazeem Jis Ki Karne Khud Mustafa Kharey Hoon.
Baithay Hain Sab Sahabi Woh Aaj Khari Hai.

2
Aye Kalma Go Bata De Yeh Raaz Kya Hai Aakhir.
Zahra Pe Kyun Zaeefi Aa Pal Mein Gayi Hai.
Jis Ki Haya Ki Khatir Khud Shams Chup Gaya Tha.
Bazar Oss Ki Beti Be Pardah Gayi Hai.

3
Baqiya Mein Karbala Mein Baghdad Samarra Mein.
Aal-e-Abba Ko Har Ja Ro Zahra Rahi Hai.
Ugalay Ka Khon Seena Sar Bhi Kamar Bhi Sun Lo.
Qabar-e-Hussain Shahid Jis Dil Mein Basi Hai.
