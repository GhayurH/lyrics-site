Aye Jaun Bhai Ho Tum Meray

Abbas Ki Kasam Tumhain Lag Jao Na Galay

Baba Bhi Aye Hain Meray Nana Bhi Aye Hain

Chehray Ka Noor Dekhiye Musa Bhi Aye Hain

Yousuf Kharay Huye Hain Ziyarat Ky Waste

Tujh Ko Duain Ajj Sy Dy Gi Dua-e-Noor

Khushboo Teray Paseenay Ki Phelay Gi Dur Dur

Mil Jaye Ga Lahu Tera Khoon-e-Hussain Sy

Ahl-e-Haram Ko Tum Sy Dharas Meray Delar

Pehra Do Phir Khayam Ka Utho Aye Meray Sher

Kyun Ja Rahe Ho Tum Mujhay Ghurbat Main Chor Ky

Karna Dua Hussain (as) Py Asa Na Waqt Aye

Koi Sada Dy Hum Ko Aur Hum Sy Chala Na Jaye

Lasha Tumhara Dhasht Sy Hum Khud Uthainge

Ahista Kehna Saqqi-e-Kausar Sy Mera Haal

Rakhti Thi Meri Pyas Ka Amma Bohat Khayal

Ghurbat Na Ho Bayan Meri Amma Ky Samne

Aye Hain Zakhm Tum Ko Meri Jaan Kahan Kahan

In Zalimon Ny Mari Hain Seenay Py Barchiyan

Shabbir Kis Tarhan Sy Tumhare Galay Lagay

Tou Ny Gala Kataya Hai Pehle Hussain (as) Sy

Dilwainge Jaza Woh Shahe Mashriqain Sy

Chadar Utha Ky Deti Hai Amma Dua Tujhay

Seenay Laga Ky Kehne Laga Ibn-e-Fatima

Khushboo Sy Teri Mehkee Gi Ta Hashr Karbala

Bou Hai Tere Paseenay Main Kis Ny Kaha Tujhay

Jaldi Thi Dekho Jaun Ko Sajjad Kis Qadar

Dhal Aur Zera Ko Phenk Ky Seena Kiya Sepur

Jis Dum Suna Hussain (as) Sy
