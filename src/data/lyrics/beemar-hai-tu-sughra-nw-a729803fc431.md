yasrab se shahe deen jo suye karbala chale

ba hasrat o masaib o aah o buka chale

israar tha ye fatema sughra ka shah se

baba khudara saath mujhe le ke jaaiye

sunkar fughan ye fatema sughra se bole shah

bemaar hai tu sughra batlade mujhe itna le jaun tujhe kaise

aasan nahi beta raahon ki jafa sehna le jaun tujhe kaise

har gaam musibat hai raahon may aziyat hai

tu chal nahi payegi kanton pe musafat hai

tu ghar ka ujaala ay sughra tu soorate zahra ay sughra

kar sabr kar yaara ay sughra kar zapt khudara ay sughra

tu seh nahi payegi hai sakht safar beta le jaun tujhe kaise

bemaar hai tu sughra…

humko hai jahan rukna pur haal hai wo sehra

kehte hai jise karbal humko hai wahan lutna

har simt kaza hai ay sughra, na aab o ghiza hai ay sughra

karbal wo jagah hai ay sughra, bas zum o jafa hai ay sughra

is gham to behtar hai tu ghar may rahe tanha le jaun tujhe kaise

bemaar hai tu sughra…

le godh may asghar ko is nanhe musafir ko

jee bhar ke tu ay sughra ab dekh le akbar ko

ek mera guletar ay sughra, ek shakle payambar ay sughra

de ruqsat mil kar ay sughra, tayyar hai dilbar ay sughra

ye khaafila jaata hai ab jaane de karbobala le jaun tujhe kaise

bemaar hai tu sughra…

soyi ek din sughra aur khwaab may ye dekha

lut-ta hai bhara kunba shabbir huwe tanha

kehte thay tadap kar ay sughra, meri pyari dukhtar ay sughra

tu dekh ye manzar ay sughra, koi nahi yawar ay sughra

ab jaan liya tune mai isliye kehta tha le jaun tujhe kaise

bemaar hai tu sughra…

ghabrake uthi muztar nani se kaha ro kar

dekha abhi maine kehte thay yehi sarwar

ab mere galay par ay sughra, chalne ko hai khanjar ay sughra

koi nahi yawar ay sughra, maare gaye dilbar ay sughra

ye soch ke hi maine bola tha tujhe beta le jaun tujhe kaise

bemaar hai tu sughra…

shabbir ka jab kunba nana ke shehr pahuncha

farhan aur safdar ye zainab ne kaha sughra

barbaad hua ghar ay sughra, hua qatl baradar ay sughra

abbas aur akbar ay sughra, koi nahi sar par ay sughra

is waaste bhai ne bola tha tujhe beta le jaun tujhe kaise

bemaar hai tu sughra…
