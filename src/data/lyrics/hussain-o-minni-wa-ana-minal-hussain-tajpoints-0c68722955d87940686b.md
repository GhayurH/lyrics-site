Hussain-o-Minni Wa Ana Minal Hussain.

1
Utra Jo Aasman Se Mehzar Ba Shoor-o-Shain.
Bait-ul-Sharaf Mein Goanj Uthe Syeda Ke Bain.
Farmate The Habib-e-Khuda Khuda Se Shah-e-Masreqain.

2
Karb-o-Bala Mein Mera Pisar Mara Jaye Ga.
Pyasa Rahe Ga Bond Na Pani Ki Paye Ga.
Ansar-o-Aqarba Ki Yeh Lashein Uthaye Ga.
Islam Ko Bachaye Ga Mera Yeh Noor-e-Ain.
Hans Kar Sahe Ga Yeh Har Ik Ranj-o-Malal Ko.
Jhole Se Le Ke Jaye Ga Bano Ke Lal Ko.
Pani Magar Na De Ga Koi Nonehal Ko.
Pyasa Hi Mara Jaye Ga Asghar Sa Noor-e-Ain.
Sar Sab Se Aagay Naize Pe Ho Ga Hussain Ka.
Gar Bhai Be-Kafan Tu Bahain Ho Gi Be-Rida.
Darbar Mein Yazid Ke Jaye Ga Qafila.
Zainab Darbar-e-Sham Mein Ban Jaye Gi Hussain.
Yeh Sun Ke Bolein Fatima Zahra Dohai Hai.
Fart-e-Alam Se Aankhon Mein Tareeki Chai Hai.
Baba Khabar Yeh Aap Ne Kaisi Sunai Hai.
Pata Nahi Hai Mera Dil-e-Dard Mand Chain.
Phir Syeda Ne Pocha Aye Taj-e-Sar-e-Yaqeen.
Kahiye Abu-Al-Hasan Bhi Tu Hoon Gaye Wahin Qareen.
Main Our Aap Hoon Gaye Kaha Koi Bhi Nahi.
Na Main Na Tum Na Hoon Gaye Ali Fateh-e-Hunain.
Dekhe Ga Aik Din Mein Akehtar Ki Lash Ko.
Kariyal Jawaan Pisar Ali Akbar Ki Lash Ko.
Darya Pe Choor De Ga Baradar Ki Lash Ko.
Meri Tarha Yeh Rakhe Ga Emaan Ki Zaib-o-Zain.
Jal Jaein Gaye Khiyam Yeh Aafat Bhi Aaye Gi.
Zainab Ke Bazuon Mein Rasan Bandhi Jaye Gi.
Rukhsar Pe Tamache Sakina Bhi Khaye Gi.
Jalti Zameen Pe Ho Ga Tan-e-Shah-e-Mashreqain.
 Mohsin Yeh Sun Ke Fatima Zahra Hoin Malol.
Rooh-Al-Ameen Pukare Ke Keh Dijiye Rasool.
Ik Qaum Khalq Ho Gi Ba-Andaz-e-Boye-e-Phool.
Ta Ba-Abad Jo Roye Gi Keh Kar Hussain Hussain.
