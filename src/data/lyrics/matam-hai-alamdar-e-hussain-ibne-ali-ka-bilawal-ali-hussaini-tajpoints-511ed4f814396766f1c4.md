1
Matam Hai Alamdar e Hussain Ibne Ali Ka Bilawal Ali Hussaini.
Yeh Gham Hai Wali Ibn-e-Wali Shair-e-Jarri Ka.
Pani Na Piya Soch Ke Pyasi Hai Sakina.
Chacha Nahi Aisa Koi Howay Ga Kissi Ka.

2
Uthtey Rahe Girtay Rahe Parcham Zamane Ke.
Oncha Hai Alam Aaj Bhi Mere Ghazi Jarri Ka.
Mashk-e-Sakina Teeron Ke Nargay Mein Aa Gayi.
Ghazi Ke Sar Pe Gurz Laga Aa Ke Shaqi Ka.

3
Tooti Qamar Hussain Ki Bhai Guzar Gaya.
Ghazi Sa Bhai Hoga Na Dunya Mein Kissi Ka.
Jis Dam Sunaya Noha Bilawal Hussaini Ne.
Matam Kiya Hatif Ne Mere Ghazi Sakhi Ka.
