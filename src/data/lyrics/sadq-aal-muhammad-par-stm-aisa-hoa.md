Sadiq e Aale Muhammad par sitam aisa hua
Yeh Zameeñ Bhi Ro Padi, Yeh Aasmañ Bhi Ro Pada

1
SADIQ (ASWS) E AALE MOHAMMAD Par Sitam Kaisa Hua
1.Pee Ke Pyala Zehr Ka NOOR E KHUDA(SWT) Bechain Hai
FATEMA(SAWS) Bechain Roohe MURTUZA(ASWS) Bechain Hai
Kaiyenat E MUSTAFA Mein Ek Andhera Cha Gaya
SADIQ (ASWS) E AALE MOHAMMAD Par Sitam Kaisa Hua
2.Hogaye Haiñ Aaj Haye MUSA E KAZIM (ASWS) Yateem
Ambiya Haiñ Girya Kun Rote Haiñ Musaa e Kaleem
Tukde Tukde Ho Gaya, Dil BOLTE QURAN Ka
SADIQ(ASWS)-E-AALE MOHAMMAD Par Sitam Kaisa Hua
3.Zehr Mansoor e dawaniqi(l.a) ne woh qatil diya
Haaey Lamhoñ Meñ Hi MAULA(ASWS) Ka Kaleja Kat Gaya
Khana E Kaba Me Aaya Zalzaloñ Par Zalzala
SADIQ(ASWS)-E-AALE MOHAMMAD Par Sitam Kaisa Hua
4.Khushbu-oñ Par Yuñ Hawae Zulm Aafat Dhaaye Hai
FATEMA(SAWS) Ke Baagh Ka Har PHOOL Masla Jaaie Hai
Gulshan E Ismat Pe Hai Zulmo Sitam Ki Inteha
SADIQ(ASWS)-E-AALE MOHAMMAD Par Sitam Kaisa Hua
5.Madrasa AALE MOHAMMAD Ka Hua Veeran Aaj
BAAB-E-SHEHR-E-ILM Ka Khooñ Ho Gaya Armaan Aaj
Haye SHEHR-E-ILM Bhi Gham Se Lahu Rone Laga
SADIQ(ASWS)-E-AALE MOHAMMAD Par Sitam Kaisa Hua
6.Shamme Fiqhe JAFAR E SADIQ (ASWS) Pe Hamid Hai Sitam
Har Kiran Zakhmi Hai Rote Hai MOHAMMAD Dam Ba Dam
Kalma Goyoñ Ne Dukhaya Dil RASULULLAH Ka
SADIQ(ASWS)-E-AALE MOHAMMAD Par Sitam Kaisa Hua
