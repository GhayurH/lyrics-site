Ro Raha Hai Khud Andhera Sham Ke Zindan Mein.
Reh Gayi Tanha Sakina Sham Ke Zindan Mein.

1
Mar Gayi Yeh Kehte Kehte Be-Watan Pardais Mein.
Kya Nahi Aaein Gaye Baba Sham Ke Zindan Mein.
Lehja-e-Shabir Mein Lori Sonane Ke Liye.
Khuld Se Aati Hain Zahra Sham Ke Zindan Mein.

2
Beriyon Ke Shoor Mein Zanjeer Ki Jhankar Mein.
Uth Raha Hai Ik Janaza Sham Ke Zindan Mein.
Sahib-e-Aulad Ja Kar Khud Hi Andaza Karein.
Munh Ko Aata Hai Kaleja Sham Ke Zindan Mein.

3
Dekh Kar Masoom Ke Rukh Par Tamachon Ke Nishaan.
Maut Ko Aaya Paseena Sham Ke Zindan Mein.
Iss Tarha Taqseem Banu Ki Kamai Ho Gayi.
Dasht Mein Asghar Sakina Sham Ke Zindan Mein.
