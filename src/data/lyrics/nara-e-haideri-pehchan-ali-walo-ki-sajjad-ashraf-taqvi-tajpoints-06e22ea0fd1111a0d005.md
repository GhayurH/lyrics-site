1
Nara E Haideri Pehchan Ali Walo Ki Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi.
Haan Issi Narey Mein Hai Jan Ali Walon Ki.
Be Jhijhak Khuld Mein Ghar Bech Raha Hai Behlool.
Aao Dikhlaein Tumhein Shan Ali Walon Ki.

2
Aarzu Tujh Ko Musalman Hai Jis Janat Ki.
Woh Adaoon Pe Hai Qurban Ali Walon Ki.
Sar-e-Maqtal Ali Asghar Ne Kiya Hai Sabit.
Hashar Kar Deti Hai Muskaan Ali Walon Ki.

3
Yeh Barey Loag Hain Jageer Hai Janat Inki.
Humsari Chor De Nadan Ali Walon Ki.
Iss Qadar Rehta Hai Manoos Kabhi Koi Dua.
Rad Nahi Karta Hai Rehman Ali Walon Ki.

4
Saltanat Dekh Ke Kehte Hain Tumhari Jibreel.
Badshahat Hai Suleman Ali Walon Ki.
Maut Ke Aagay Taqaiyya Nahi Kartay Yeh Loag.
Juratein Dekh Musalman Ali Walon Ki.

5
Baaz Aa Deen Na Dunya Ka Rahe Ga Waiz.
Dushmani Deti Hai Nuqsan Ali Walon Ki.
Hoga Ahsan Bohat Akhtar-o- Sajjad Ka Naam.
Likh Lo Fehrist Mein Salman Ali Walon Ki.
