Ik Zainab Te Haye Dard Hazaraan
Ali Di Jayi Nu Mar Mukaya Aye Bazaraan Te Darbaraan.

1
Veer Izataan Walay Gaye Ban Wich Mare Maan Muk Gaye Sare.
Bhirawaan De Zainab Sangaan Te Sir Waikhe Te Labdhi Aye Dastaraan.
Jadoon Zainab Guzari Sham Bazar Wichon Kuj Shami Bole.
Aye Kiwein Ho Gayi Aye Qaidi Kithe Gaye Ne Iss Bhain De Veer Atharaan.

2
Shoor Galiyaan Che Hoya Zahra Jaiyaan Nu Ewein Aa Ke Pathar Maro.
Bhira De Lashe Te Zakhm Jiwein Laye Nu Lakh Teeraan Talwaraan.
Sham Zindan Andar Kiya Abid Nu Phophi Meri Qaidaan Te Na Ro.
Main Razi Haan Abid Pardah Te Keeta Aye Zindan Diyaan Dewaraan.

3
Ayho Fizza Soche Ayho Sheerein Aakhe Ayeho Hind Wain Kare.
Kiyon Wasaye Ne Sham Wale Zindaan Haye Janat De Sardaraan.
Ghairtaan Di Khaliq Bint-e-Zahra Da Sada Shukar Saqlain Karo.
Hai Sadqa Zainab Da Baqi Jahan Te Dhiyaan Aye PardeDaraan.
