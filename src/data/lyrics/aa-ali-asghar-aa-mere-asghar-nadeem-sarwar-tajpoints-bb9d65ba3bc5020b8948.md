1
Aa Ali Asghar Aa Mere Asghar Nadeem Sarwar.
Tujh Ko Jo Paon Aye Mere Asghar Jhola Jholaon Aa Mere Asghar.
Jhola Jholaon Lori Sunaon Doodh Pilaon Aa Mere Asghar.
Mere Mujahid Mere Sipahi Khatam Hoi Maidaan Mein Larai.
Ghar Ko Sidharo Maa Ko Pukaro Main Chain Paon Aa Mere Asghar.
Yaad Aa Rahi Hain Masoom Batein Din Mein Sakoon Hai Be Chain Ratein.
Jee Na Sakoon Gi Roti Rahon Gi Aanso Bahaon Aa Mere Asghar.
Poch Rahi Hai Bali Sakina Kyun Nahi Aaya Laut Ke Bhaiyya.
Tu Hi Bata De Maidaan Se Aa Ke Main Kya Bataon Aa Mere Asghar.
Gehray Hoye Hain Sham Ke Saye Ab Tu Parinday Bhi Laut Aaye.
Main Ro Rahi Hoon Jaan Kho Rahi Hoon Tujh Ko Solaon Aa Mere Asghar.
Zakhmi Gala Our Pyas Ki Shiddat Hoti Tu Hogi Maa Ki Zarorat.
Tu Jo Pukarey Dil Ke Saharey Main Dori Aaon Aa Mere Asghar.
Mehsos Mujh Ko Yeh Ho Raha Hai Jaisay Ke Jhola Bhi Ro Raha Hai.
Aye Mere Beta Yeh Rota Jhola Kaisay Jholaon Aa Mere Asghar.
Pyas Bhujaney Nehar Kinaray Jab Se Gaye Ho Aye Mere Pyaray.
Dharka Laga Hai Dil Dobta Hai Palkein Bichaon Aa Mere Asghar.
Maqtal Se Aayi Kaisi Khabar Hai Kurta Lahu Se Sab Tera Tar Hai.
Teer Laga Hai Zakhmi Gala Hai Main Mar Na Jaon Aa Mere Asghar.
:REHAN-o-SARWAR: Bano Ke Lab Par Aik Sada Thi Aye Mere Asghar.
Khoni Hai Jangal Hai Gham Ka Badil Tujh Ko Chupaon Aa Mere Asghar.
