1
Baar Baar Abbas Tasawar Abbas Tashna.
Pukara Dekh Ke Dil Tujh Ko Bar Bar Abbas.
Hai La Shareek Musavir Ka Shahkar Abbas.
Tumhara Zikar Kiya Jab Khizaan Ke Mosam Mein.
Tu Phool Khilney Lagay Aa Gayi Bahar Abbas.
Duaein Mangi Thin Zahra Ne Teri Khilqat Ki.
Ali Ko Tha Teri Aamad Ka Intezar Abbas.
Kaha Hussain Ne ”Uth Kar Jalao Ghazi Charag”
Keh Tum Ho Khaimay Mein Sheh Ko Tha Aytbar Abbas.
Saji Hai Mashk-e-Sakina Alam Ke Seene Par.
Tumhare Jeetne Ka Ban Ke Ishtehar Abbas.
Koi Jo Naam Tera Leta Bar Bar Tu Phir.
Adab Se Uthtey They Sajjad Bar Bar Abbas.
Jo Tera Rutba Na Maney, Khud Oss Pe Bhaijtay Hain.
Hussain Aaj Bhi Lanat Hazar Bar Abbas.
Dua-e-Fatima Abbas Ko Kahin Jo Asad.
Tu Khud Ko Fakhar Se Taktay Hain Bar Bar Abbas.
