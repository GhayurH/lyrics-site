1
Sadqy Hussain Ky Kazmi Brothers Lyrics
Nazara Dekh Ke Fizza Bhi Muskurati Hain.
Batool Betay Pe Youn Sadqay Wari Jati Hain.
Zahra Ne Khud Utaray Hain Sadqay Hussain Ke.

2
Bachpan Mein Mojzaat Jo Dekhey Hussain Ke.
Mati Farishto Karb-o-Bala Se Utha Ke Lao.
ALLAH Ko Bananey Hain Banday Hussain Ke.
Karey Jo Koi Humsari Kissi Ki Kya Majal Hai.

3
Jahan Mein Har Lehaz Se Hussain Be Misal Hai.
Yeh Ho Chuka Hai Faisla Na Koi Dosra Khuda Na Koi Dosra Hussain Hai.
ALLAH Aik Bar Hi Aaya Zameen Par.
Aaya Bhi Sirf Dekhney Sajday Hussain Ke.

4
Mera Jahan Mera Sakoon Meri Zindagi.
Yeh Naam Hain Sakina Ne Rakhay Hussain Ke.
Majlis Mein Hum Ko Bhaija Hai Pehna Ke Khud Kafan.
Maa Kar Chuki Hai Hum Ko Hawalay Hussain Ke.

5
Jitna Bhi Zakhm Gehra Ho Lagta Hai Inn Ko Kam.
Yeh Matami Hain Sare Dewanay Hussain Ke.
Jo Ho Gaye Hain Matam-e-Zanjeer Ke Khilaf.
Yeh Loag Tu Kabhi Bhi Nahi They Hussain Ke.

6
Hai Eid Intezar Mein Bethi Hain Fatima.
Jannat Se Aane Walay Hain Kurtay Hussain Ke.
Ghabra Rahe Hain Khof Se Nu Lakh Ahl-e-Sham.
Maidaan Mein Aane Walay Hain Betay Hussain Ke.

7
Hai Kaun Kainat Mein Abbas Ke Siwa.
Woh Shaks Jo Isharey Bhi Samjhay Hussain Ke.
Mehshar Mein Arsalan, Moazzim, Aman, Ali.
Hum Sath Hi Parhein Gay Qasiday Hussain Ke.
