Hogayi rihaayi, lo kafela chalaa,
Sar jab har ek shahid ka, sajaad ko mila

1
Tab marqade Sakina,  pe Sajaad ne kahaa
Jaag Sakina jaag baba ka sar aaya
Baba tumhara aya,  gaya waqt neend ka
Jaag Sakina jaag baba ka sar aaya

2
1) Seenay pe sone wali, matti pe so gaye
Baba ko rote rote, jo khamosh ho gaye
Roro ke maan kabre,  Sakina pe ye kaha
Jaag Sakina jaag baba ka sar aaya

3
2) Bibi karo salaam, adab ka makaam hay
Bibi tere Sarhanay, shahe tishna kaam hay
Ay jaan jaldi badlo,  Ye kurta phata huwa
Jaag Sakina jaag baba ka sar aaya

4
3) Baba ko dhundti thi, Sakina utho utho
Raatoon ko jaagti thi, Sakina utho utho
Lo aa gaye hain baba,  Aey jaan jag ja
Jaag Sakina jaag baba ka sar aaya

5
4) Baba ke paas sona, na sona zamin par
Rakho labo ko lab par, Jabeen ko jabeen par
Aada ki ghurkiyo ka,  Zamaana guzar gaya
Jaag Sakina jaag baba ka sar aaya

6
5) Payghaam roz deti, the amu ke naam tum
Roti thi yaad kar ke, unhe subh shaam tum
Jaago ke jaa rahay hay,  Jahaan sote hain chacha
Jaag Sakina jaag baba ka sar aaya

7
6) Kis tarha soyi baali, Sakina na puchiye
Ranjo alam se phat-ta, hay sina na puchiye
Jaagi na phir kabhi woh,  Bohot maa ne di sadaa
Jaag Sakina jaag baba ka sar aaya
