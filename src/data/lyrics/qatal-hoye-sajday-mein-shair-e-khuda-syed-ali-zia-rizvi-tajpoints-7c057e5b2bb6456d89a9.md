1
Qatal Hoye Sajday Mein Shair-e-Khuda Syed Ali Zia Rizvi
Aati Hai Gardoon Se Yeh Paihum Sada Qatal Hoye Sajday Mein Shair-e-Khuda.
Khana-e-Haq Mein Yeh Ghazab Ho Gaya Qatal Hoye Sajday Mein Shair-e-Khuda.
Gham Se Hai Zainab Nidhal Khol Deiye Sar Ke Baal.
Kehti Hai Yeh Pur Malal Dekho Tu Baba Ka Haal.
Detey Hain Jibreel Yeh Kaisi Nida.
Khak Pe Hai Jalwa Gar Kehti Hai Sar Peet Kar.
Phatta Hai Mera Jigar Koi Tu Laye Khabar.
Ghar Mein Khuda Ke Yeh Sitam Kya Howa.
Dekho Tu Hasnain Jao Baba Ko Ghar Mein Tu Lao.
Kaisa Laga Sar Pe Ghao Mujh Ko Khudara Batao.
Pocho Tu Jarah Se Ho Gi Shifa.
Nashtar-e-Gham Dil Pe Khaye Door Kar Hasnain Aaye.
Dekha Ajab Haal Haye Khoon Mein Hai Baba Nahaye.
Farsh Se Ta Arsh Hai Mehshar Bapa.
Haye Qayamat Hoi Taig Sitam Ki Chali.
Mara Ali-e-Wali Giriya Hai Roh-e-Nabi.
Kaisi Laeen Ne Yeh Ki Joor-o-Jafa.
Aye Mere Zakhmi Imam Tum Pe Darood-o-Salam.
Khoob Ki Tableeg-e-Aam De Diya Qatil Ko Jaam.
Ho Ga Anees Aisa Koi Paishwa.
