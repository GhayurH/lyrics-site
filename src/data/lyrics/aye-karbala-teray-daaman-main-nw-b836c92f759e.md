Aye karbala teray daaman main daastaan hain koi

Hussain bun na sakay ga, na Karbala hain koi

Ali ki beti hoon nana hai, Mustafa meray…. hai Mustafa meray

Main bayrida hoon meray sir pay na rida hai koi

Na laashay baba pay aaogee, chor day zaalim… hai chor day zaalim

yeh keh kay nunnay say haatho ko, jorta hai koi

Na pooch madar-e-Asghar kay, dil ki bay chainee… hai dil ki bay chainee

tamam raatei gayee jhoola, jhula raha hai koi

khayaal aaya tha baazaar-e-shaam ka shayad… hai shaam ka shayad

behen nay sir ko Jo chooma tau, ro diya hai koi

Sahaara meri zaeefi ka kho gaya yaarab…. hai kho gaya yaaraab

zameen …… jawaani ko dhoondta hai koi
