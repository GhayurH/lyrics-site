Mujhe Karbala Mein Bula Mere Maula.
Ho Izn-e-Zyarat Atta Mere Maula.

1
Siyahkar Hoon Main Gunahgar Hoon Main.
Zyarat Ka Teri Talabgar Hoon Main.
Mujhe Moaf Karna Tu Waisey Hi Maula.
Jaise Hur Ki Bakhshi Khata Mere Maula.

2
Hain Tarpe Kahan Par Mere Shah Ke Betay.
Bula Mere Maula Mere Aankh Dekhe.
Jahan Khoon Mein Tar Tujh Ko Beti Ne Dekha.
Mujhe Bhi Jagah Woh Dikha Mere Maula.

3
Yeh Hasnain Ro Ro Salami Kare Ga.
Tere Dar Pe Maula Ghulami Kare Ga.
Tere Dar Pe Aaye Mujhe Maut Maula.
Ho Pori Yeh Meri Dua Mere Maula.
