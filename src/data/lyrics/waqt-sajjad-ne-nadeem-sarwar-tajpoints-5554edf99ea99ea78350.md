Waqt Sajjad Ne Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics
Waqt Sajjad Ne Kaisay Woh Guzara Hoga.
Naam Zainab Ka Jo Ghairon Ne Pukara Hoga.
Ya Rab Koi Mil Jaye Na Pehchanane Wala.
Bazar Mein Sajjad Dua Mang Raha Hai.
Lo Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Pyasay Zibha Hone Lagay.
Kitna Sharminda Woh Darya Ka Kinara Hoga.
Woh Gira Zeen Se Zainab Ki Rida Ka Zamin.
Kaun Ab Fatima Jayi Ka Sahara Hoga.
Sar-e-Maidan Tu Jane Tu Laga Hai Asghar.
Kis Tarha Tere Bina Maa Ka Guzara Hoga.
Nook-e-Naiza Pe Bahane Lagay Aanso Maula.
Phir Sakina Ko Kissi Shaks Ne Mara Hoga.
Rooz-e-Mehshar Ka Humein Dar Nahi Koi Shaukat.
Hath Shabeer Ke Qadmo Pe Hamara Hoga.
