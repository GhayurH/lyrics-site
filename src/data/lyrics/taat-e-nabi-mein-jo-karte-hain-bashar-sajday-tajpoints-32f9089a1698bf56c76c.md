Taat-e-Nabi Mein Jo Karte Hain Bashar Sajday.
Haan Wohi Khuda Ke Haan Sirf Moatbar Sajday.

1
Bin Parhe Darood Un Par Raigaan Ibadat Hai.
Manta Khuda Kab Hai Kar Bhi Lein Agar Sajday.
Taat-e-Nabi Na Ho Tu Taat-e-Khuda Kaisi.
Kar Ke Dekh Le Koi Chahe Umhar Bhar Sajday.

2
Hai Hazoor Ki Hasti Aalmeen Par Rehmat.
Aap Hi Se Khaliq Ke Hain Nagar Nagar Sajday.
Uswa-e-Paiyambar Mein Reh Ke Bandagi Kar Lo.
Warna Yaad Rakh Lena Hoon Gay Be Samar Sajday.

3
Jo Tere Nawasay Ka Karbala Mein Hai Sajda.
Gir Pare Hain Sajday Mein Oss Ko Dekh Kar Sajday.
Ulfat-e-Paiyambar Jab Mehwar-e-Shifat Hai.
Kyun Na Hum Karein Mumtaz Oss Mein Doob Kar Sajday.
