1
Ali Banate Hain Syed Mohammad Shah.
Ali Banatay Hain Maula Ali Banatay Hain.
Khuda Ke Hathoon Se Dunya Ali Banatay Hain.
Faqat Woh Chodah Hain Jin Ko Khuda Banata Hai.
Hain Jo Bhi Inn Ke Elawah Ali Banatay Hain.
Yeh Baat Khilqat-e-Aadam Se Ho Gayi Sabit.
Koi Nabi Nahi Banta Ali Banatay Hain.
Munafiqeen Ki Mujh Ko Khabar Nahi Lekin.
Halal Zadoon Ka Chehra Ali Banatay Hain.
Hai Mere Gayarah Imamo Pe Bhi Ali Wajib.
Unhein Imam-e-Zamana Ali Banatay Hain.
Qaleel Ho Ke Ibadat Bhi Kam Nahi Hoti.
Hamarey Kam Ko Zyda Ali Banatay Hain.
Hai Aks Terah Rajab Ka Hi Chaar Shabaan Mein.
Ali Shareek Bhi Apna Ali Banatay Hain.
Ali Ka Naam Tashahud Mein Rooh Phonkta Hai.
Meri Namaz Ko Zindah Ali Banatay Hain.
Hussain Ke Liye Abbas Ki Zarorat Hai.
Ba Hukam-e-Fatima Zahra Ali Banatay Hain.
Haram Ko Qibla Banaya Tu Iss Mein Hairat Kya.
Hamari Qabar Ko Kaba Ali Banatay Hain.
Mubahlay Mein Yeh Kis Ka Hai Naqsh-e-Pa Muhsin.
Keh Jis Ka Khak Pe Pardah Ali Banatay Hain.
