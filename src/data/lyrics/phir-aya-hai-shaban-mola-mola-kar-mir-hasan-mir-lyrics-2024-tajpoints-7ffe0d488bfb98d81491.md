1
Phir Aya Hai Shaban Mola Mola Kar Mir Hasan Mir.
Khushiyaan Mananay Ke Yeh Din Hain.
Jashan Mein Jane Ke Yeh Din Hain.
Taza Hoga Eman Maula Maula Kar.
Phir Aaya Hai Shaban Maula Maula Kar.
Aal-e-Nabi Ki Khushiyon Mein Jo Khush Hota Hai Zindah Hai.
Unn Ki Khushi Se Jo Jalta Hai Murdah Hai Woh Murdah Hai.
Hai Zeest Ka Yeh Ayelan Maula Maula Kar.
Pehli Shabaan Behno Wala Din Hai Dil Yeh Kehta Hai.
Har Bhai Se Oss Ki Behan Ne Tohfay Mein Yeh Manga Hai.
Rakh Apni Behan Ka Maan Maula Maula Kar.
Eid Ki Khushiyon Mein Sab Ne Malboos Naye Silwaye Hain.
Phir Se Tawala Our Tabarah Karne Ke Din Aaye Hain.
Kar Dushman Ki Pehchan Maula Maula Kar.
Apni Fursat Apni Zarorat Apni Saholat Yaar Na Dekh.
Zikar-e-Ali-o-Aal-e-Ali Mein Hafta Our Itwar Na Dekh.
Har Pal Har Dam Har Aan Maula Maula Kar.
Gaud Mein Baba Ki Hai Ghazi Our Nuseri Takta Hai.
Shana Hila Kar Hanstay Hoye Salman Phir Oss Se Kehta Hai.
Kyun Shak Mein Hai Nadan Maula Maula Kar.
Moqa Hai Ramzan Se Pehle Tu Bhi Mana Le Khaliq Ko.
ALLAH ALLAH Karne Walay Razi Kar Le Raziq Ko.
Khush Hoga Khud Rehman Maula Maula Kar.
Samarra Se Dein Gi Duaein Khud Yaseen Ki Aal Tujhey.
Qadar Ki Raton Mein Hoon Gay Maqbool Sabhi Amal Tere.
Sar Par Rakh Kar Quran Maula Maula Kar.
Kehte Hain Yeh Sare Arif Aqal Se Uthein Gay Parday.
Rang-e-Ibadat Our Hi Hoga Dein Gay Dua ALLAH Walay.
Har Sajday Ke Doran Maula Maula Kar.
Tujh Se Kiya Tha Jo Wadah Woh Wadah Nibhanay Aate Hain.
Aa Gaye Maula Shehar-e-Madain Boli Qaza Hum Jate Hain.
Uth Kar Lash-e-Salman Maula Maula Kar.
Sadqa Hai Yeh Pak Nabi Ka Apni Dharti Apni Hai.
Roh-e- Quaid Ab Bhi Takallum Har Ik Se Yeh Kehti Hai.
Hai Apna PAKISTAN Maula Maula Kar.
