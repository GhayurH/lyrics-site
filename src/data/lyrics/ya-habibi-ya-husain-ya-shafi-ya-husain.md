Ya habibi ya Hussain ya shafi ya Hussain,  ya gharibi ya Hussain

1
He was Hussain the grandson of Prophet Muhammad
Salallahu alayhi wasallam
Hussain mercy upon mankind, saviour of all mankind

2
1) Rab ki raza say jisnay gaardan kataye
Deen-e-Nabi ki jisnay laaj bachayi
Haar ghaar mai jiska farshe matam bicha hay
Roti hai jiskay ghum mai sari khudayi

3
Ya Hussainan ya gharibun ya zabeehun bekhata

4
2) Mehman kar kay usko Kufa bulaaya
Bayyat-e-Yazid ka woh masla uthaaya
Bolay Hussain kabhi may naa karunga
Sajday may jisne apne saar ko kataya

5
Ya Hussainan ya gharibun ya zabeehun bekhata

6
3) Zehra aur Haider jiska jhula jhulaye
Khatm-e-mursal bhi jiske naaz uthaaye
Khaali sawaali jiske ghaar say naa jaaye
Hal min ki deta hay woh ban may sadaayain

7
Ya hussainan ya gharibun ya zabeehun bekhata

8
4) Akbar sa beta diya Ghazi sa bhaaye
Luti gaye hay ran may jiski kamaaye
Ab bhi gawah hay uski shaam-e-gharibaan
Apne lahu say shamma deen ki jalaaye

9
Ya Hussainan ya gharibun ya zabeehun bekhata

10
5) Koyi naa dekha humnay aisa namaazi
Deen-e-Nabi pe jisnay haar shay lutaye
Phool bahatar diye raahe khuda may
Sab kuch luta ke kiya Allah ko raazi

11
Ya Hussainan ya gharibun ya zabeehun bekhata

12
6) Jawaad-o-Meesam uska matam hay ghar ghar
Roye janaaze jisne pyaaro ke din bhar
Phera gaya hay jiski gardan peh khanjar
Chini gaye hay jiski behna ki chadar

13
Ya Hussainan ya gharibun ya zabeehun bekhata
