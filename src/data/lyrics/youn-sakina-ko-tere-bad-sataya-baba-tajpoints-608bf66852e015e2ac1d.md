1
Youn Sakina Ko Tere Bad Sataya Baba.
Tere Qatil Ne Tamachon Se Rulaya Baba.
Gir Pari Khak Pe Gash Kar Ke Sakina Oss Dam.
Barh Gaye Dard Mere Ro Ke Kiya Tha Matam.
Shimar Ne Tasht Mein Sar Tera Dikhaya Baba.
Jab Kissi Moor Pe Sar Tera Hai Manga Main Ne.
Apne Seene Se Laga Loon Tujhe Chaha Main Ne.
Jhirkiyaan De Ke Hai Zalim Ne Hataya Baba.
Mujh Ko Pehchano Muhammad Ki Nawasi Hoon Main.
Paon Mein Chalay Hain Kayi Roz Ki Pyasi Hoon Main.
Phir Bhi Bazar Rasan Basta Phiraya Baba.
Aisay Zalim They Keh Pani Ko Giratay They Woh.
Our Mere Samne Ghoron Ko Pilatay They Woh.
Ghont Ik Pani Ka Mujh Ko Na Pilaya Baba.
Kaisay Bholon Main Woh Manzar Mujhe Batla Baba.
Shimar Ne Tujh Ko Bhi Mara Tha Tamacha Baba.
Teri Gardan Pe Bhi Khanjar Tha Chalaya Baba.
Dhop Hai Sar Pe Mere Khak Bichona Mera.
Yaad Aata Hai Musalsal Mujhe Seena Tera.
Tujh Se Ik Bar Na Zalim Ne Milaya Baba.
Aaj Bhi Qabar e Sakina Ki Sada Hai Qambar.
Mujh Ko Rasiyon Se Chura Lo Mere Baba Aa Kar.
Hai Gala Aaj Bhi Rasi Ne Dukhaya Baba.
