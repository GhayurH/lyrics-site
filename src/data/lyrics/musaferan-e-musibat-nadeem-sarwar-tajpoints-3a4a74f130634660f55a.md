Musaferan e Musibat Nadeem Sarwar.

1
Na Mehmal Hai Na Hodaj Hai.
Na Lashkar Ki Woh Saj Dhaj Hai.
Na Sar Pe Alam Na Chadar Hai.
ALLAH Yeh Kaisa Manzar Hai.
Musafiran-e-Musibat Watan Mein Aate Hain.
Safar Se Aate Hain Sogat-e-Dard Latay Hain.

2
Kiya Salam Muhammad Ke Aastane Ko.
Kata Ke Aaye Hain Zahra Ke Sab Gharanay Ko.
Na Kar Qabool Tu Hum Be Kason Ke Aane Ko.
Yeh Noha Kartay Hain Our Ashk Khoon Bahatay Hain.
Madina Hum Tere Wali Ko Aaye Hain Kho Kar.
Madina Kofay Mein Sar Nangay Hum Phiray Dar Dar.

3
Madina Daag-e-Rasan Hai Hamarey Shano Par.
Utha Ke Hath Madine Ko Yeh Sunatay Hain.
Har Ik Qadam Hai Boka Kab Labon Pe Bain Nahi.
Hum Aaye Zindah Pa Zahra Ka Noor-e-Ain Nahi.
Madina Akbar-o-Qasim Nahi Hussain Nahi.
Yeh Kaisay Kaisay Jawaan They Jo Yaad Aate Hain.

4
Madine Walo Kaho Oss Behan Ki Kya Taqdeer.
Jo Dekhey Apne Baradar Ke Halq Par Shamsheer.
Lahu Mein Garq Jo Dekhey Hussain Ki Tasveer.
Lahu Mein Kya Youn Hi Bhai Ka Sar Dikhatay Hain.
Batao Kehte Hain Kya Sab Oss Amma Jayi Ko.
Jo Arbaeen Talak De Kafan Na Bhai Ko.

5
Behan Dikhaye Gi Munh Kis Tarha Khudai Ko.
Kafan Diya Hai Na Unn Ki Lehad Banatay Hain.
Chala Basheer Yeh Deta Howa Khabar Har Ja.
Magar Mahalla-e-Hashim Mein Dekhta Hai Kya.
Ke Ik Mareeza Sar-e-Rah Hai Khari Tanha.
Hai Muntazir Ke Woh Kunbay Ke Loag Aate Hain.

6
Khabar Yeh Phaili Tu Matam Har Aik Ghar Mein Howa.
Nikal Nikal Parin Sab Aurtein Barehna Pa.
Giri Zameen Pe Sughra Ke Oss Pe Charkh Gira.
Jo Sogowar Hain Sughra Ke Pas Aate Hain.
Fizaye Dard Mein Le Kar Udasiyaan Aaein.
Safar Se Lut Ke Idhar Bhoki Pyasiyaan Aaein.

7
Utho Rasool Tumhari Nawasiyaan Aaein.
Keh Khud Ko Ontoon Se Ahl-e-Haram Giratay Hain.
Jo Aayi Kan Mein Aawaz-e-Banu-e-Muztar.
Kahan Ho Aye Mere Wali Imam-e-Jin-o-Bashar.
Tarap Ke Reh Gayi Sughra Bapa Howa Mehshar.
 Dabeer Bas Ke Ab Aflak Thartharatay Hain.
