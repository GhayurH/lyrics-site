Ali Ke Zikar Ka Kya Hai Sawab Mat Pocho.
Hisab Kehta Hai Thak Kar Hisab Mat Pocho.

1
Khuda Ke Ghar Ka Main Karta Raha Tawaf Magar.
They Yaad Kitne Mujhe Buturab Mat Pocho.
Jo Loag Khak-e-Shifa Ke Khilaf They Unki.
Hai Kitni Qabar Mein Mati Kharab Mat Pocho.

2
Jo Pochna Hai Woh Pocho Nishan-e-Matam Se.
Farishto Mujh Se Sawal-o-Jawab Mat Pocho.
Jo Aazmana Ho Jao Najaf Chaley Jao.
Yeh Sirka Banti Hai Kaisay Sharab Mat Pocho.

3
Jahan Mein Khana-e-Zahra Se Bugz Rakh Rakh Kar.
Howa Hai Kitno Ka Khana Kharab Mat Pocho.
Gaye Hain Pani Ko Sheh Jab Se Munh Lagaye Bina.
Hai Kitna Sharam Se Yeh Aab Aab Mat Pocho.

4
Ajab Tareeqa Hai Aye Sheikh Jee Hakomat Ka.
Sawal Poch Lo Lekin Jawab Mat Pocho.
Jo Pochi Had-e-Takhayul Tumhari Aye Sajjad.
Tu Aasman Se Bolay Uqab Mat Pocho.
