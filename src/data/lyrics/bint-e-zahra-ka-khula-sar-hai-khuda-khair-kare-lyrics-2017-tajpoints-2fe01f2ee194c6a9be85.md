Bint e Zahra Ka Khula Sar Hai Khuda Khair Kare Mir Hasan Mir.
Our Har Hath Mein Pathar Hai Khuda Khair Kare.

1
Hur Ke Bete Pe Hi Shabir BOhat Roye The.
Haye Ab Tu Lashe Ali Akbar Hai Khuda Khair Kare.
Marte Hain Wahan Naizon Ko Zameen Par Zalim.
Dafan Jis Ja Ali Asghar Hai Khuda Khair Kare.

2
Kuch Qadam Door Khari Dekh Rahi Hai Zainab.
Shimar Ke Hath Mein Khanjar Hai Khuda Khair Kare.
Chonk Kar Khuwab Se Kehti Thi Watan Mein Sughra.
Kissi Mushkil Mein Mera Ghar Hai Khuda Khair Kare.

3
Peeche Dewar Hai Our Jalta Howa Dar Aagay.
Beech Mein Bint-e-Payamber Hai Khuda Khair Kare.
Be-Rida Jana Hai Zainab Ko Wahan Par Keh Jahan.
Har Koi Dushman-e-Haider Hai Khuda Khair Kare.

4
Hai Chari Hath Mein Zalim Ke Laboon Par Hai Hansi.
Tasht Mein Sheh Ka Rakha Sar Hai Khuda Khair Kare.
:MIR SAJJAD: Yeh Sherein Ne Kaha Rote Howay.
Kis Ki Naizay Pe Yeh Chadar Hai Khuda Khair Kare.
