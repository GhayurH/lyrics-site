1
Do Honsla Baba Zainab Ko Mir Hasan Mir.
Zainab Ne Kaha Yeh Ro Ro Kar Do Honsla Baba Zainab Ko.
Tanha Hai Laeino Mein Dukhtar Do Honsla Baba Zainab Ko.
Baba Main Bari Beti Hoon Teri Sab Badla Lein Gaye Mujh Se Tera.
Logon Ke Badalte Hain Taiwar Do Honsla Baba Zainab Ko.
Yeh Zainab. Hai Kulsoom. Hai Yeh Aapas Mein Sitmgar Kehte Hain.
Hanste Hain Tamashai Hum Par Do Honsla Baba Zainab Ko.
Himat Youn Barhao Beti Ki Abid. Ko Bacha Kar Le Jaye.
Har Ghar Se Baraste Hain Pathar Do Honsla Baba Zainab Ko.
Ahsas Rida Ka Ho Ga Mujhe Tum Hath Mere Sar Par Rakh Do.
Mushkil Hai Agar Dena Chadar Do Honsla Baba Zainab Ko.
Logon Ki Nigahein Uthti Hain Tasheer Ki Zarbein Chalti Hain.
Beti Hai Teri Zair-e-Khanjar Do Honsla Baba Zainab Ko.
Darbar Ke Oss Darwaze Par Amma Ko Sanbhala Tha Tum Ne.
Darbar Ke Iss Darwaze Par Do Honsla Baba Zainab Ko.
Bolein Woh Takallum Logon Mein Amma Ko Bulaon Tu Kaise.
Beti Ke Liye Ban Kar Chadar Do Honsla Baba Zainab Ko.
