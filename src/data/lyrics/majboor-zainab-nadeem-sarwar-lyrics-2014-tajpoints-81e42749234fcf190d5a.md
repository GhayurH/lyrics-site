Majboor Zainab Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics
Na Qasim Hai Na Akbar Hai Na Lashkar Na Abbas Bhira.
Majboor Zainab Lashay Bhira Te Haye Ja Na Sakdi Oh Dafna Na Sakdi.
Akbar Te Qasim Raitaan Che Rul Gaye Haye Ja Na Sakdi Oh Gal La Na Sakdi.

1
Akbar te Qasim reytan ch rul gae
Haye ja na sakdi o gal la na sakdi
Majboor Zainab lashey bhiraa tay
Haye ja na sakdi o dafna na sakdi

2
Pathraan De Wich Aye Veran Da Lasha.
Jehra Muhammad Da Hay Nawasa.
Rowan Na Denday Zalim Ne Okon.
Kurla Na Sakdi Oh Parha Na Sakdi.

3
Sar Gaye Ne Khaimay Abid Beemar Aye.
Lutiyaan Ne Veran Zalim Ridawaan.
Hath Joor Aakhay Karein Moaf Maikon.
Kafna Na Sakdi Oh Chad Ja Na Sakdi.

4
Puchdi Sakina Maikon Aye Ro Ro.
Teraan De Wich Kyun Aye Babay Da Seena.
Kyun Kapiya Zalim Sir Teda Veran.
Samjha Na Sakdi Oh Parcha Na Sakdi.

5
Lashay Paray Hain Khaimay Jalay Hain.
Zanjeer Ik Hai Barah Galay Hain.
Ghazi Raha Na Qasim Na Akbar.
Zainab Pe Hain Yeh Din Aaye Kaisay.

6
Chah Chah Ke Chaway Lasha Halaway.
Abbas Da Naa Wal Wal Ke Chaway.
Jehray Thi Gaye Zakhmi Kan Kha Tamachay.
Dikhla Na Sakdi Oh Kurla Na Sakdi.

7
Tak Tak Ke Tukray Ro Farwa Aakhay.
Jawaan Main Bachra Hunr Kehrey Pasay.
Taidi Shakal Qasim Main Banra Na Sakdi.
Hath Taiday Lab Ke Main Le Aa Na Sakdi.

8
Boli Sakina Amma Batao.
Dhondon Kahan Main Baba Ka Seena.
Seene Pe Har So Paiwast Paikaan.
Dhondon Gi Seena Ab Haye Kaisay.

9
Nook-e-Sina Te Shabeer Roway.
Bachri De Gal Wich Zanjeer Tak Ke.
Zanjeer Ik Te Barah Galay Ne.
Bhar Haa Na Sakdi Oh Le Sah Na Sakdi.

10
Khursheed Sarwar Hubdar Tere.
Parh Parh Ke Noha Ro Ro Aye Kehnday.
Wirsay Che Mili Aye Hub Teri Bibi.
Kadi Ja Na Sakdi Oh Kadi Ja Na Sakdi.
