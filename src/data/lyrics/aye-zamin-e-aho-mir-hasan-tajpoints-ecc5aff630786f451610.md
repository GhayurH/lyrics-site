Aye Zamin e Aho Mir Hasan Mir
Aaho Ki Tarha Har Ghari Zamin Hai Mera Tu.

1
Bas Iss Mein Ameeri-o-Ghareebi Ka Nahi Hai.
Toheed Ki Manind Mujhe Iss Ka Yaqeen Hai.
Mashad Wohi Jata Hai Bulata Hai Jise Tu.
Wallah Woh Ik Lamhe Mein Yousuf Ko Bhula De.

2
Angusht Ke Badle Woh Sar Apna Kata De.
Mil Jaye Zulekha Ko Agar Aap Ki Khusbu.
Na Sur Ki Samajh Hai Na Kissi Rag Ki Mula.
Sab Hai Teri Dehleez Ke Zarron Ka Yeh Sadqa.

3
Kehte Hain Jo Sab Hai Meri Aawaz Mein Jadu.
Main Qabar Mein Gar Apna Taruf Na Karata.
Madah Hoon Tera Main Agar Yeh Na Batata.
Hoti Na Kabhi Khatam Malaik Ki Man-o-Tu.

4
Woh Sehan-e-Maseeha Hai Keh Hai Sehan Shifa Ka.
Main Ne Wahan Khud Api Nigahon Se Hai Dekha.
Zair Tera Wahan Maut Ko Kar Leta Hai Qabo.
Haq Aap Ki Midhat Ka Ada Kaise Karon Main.

5
Kis Tarha Bhala Dosra Quran Likhon Main.
Aati Nahi Jab Mujh Ko Abhi Theek Se Urdu.
Mehsos Ye Har Bar Mere Dil Ne Kiya Hai.
Mula Tere Darbar Ki Taseer Juda Hai.

6
Hoti Hai Khushi Our Kabhi Aa Jate Hain Aansu.
Khul Jaye Ga Dunya Pe Tere Mir Ka Rutba.
Ho Jaye Ga Maloom Keh Banda Hoon Main Kis Ka.
Aaye Gi Meri Qabar Se Jis Dam Teri Khushbu.
