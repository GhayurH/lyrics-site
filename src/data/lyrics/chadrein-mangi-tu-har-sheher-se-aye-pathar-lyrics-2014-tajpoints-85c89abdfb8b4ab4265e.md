1
Chadrein Mangi Tu Har Sheher Se Aye Pathar Mir Hasan Mir.
Bint-e-Zahra Ne Kaha Par Nahi Khaye Pathar.
Sar Jhukaye Howay Roti Hai Ali Ki Beti.
Aur Laeen Hanste Hain Hathon Mein Uthaye Pathar.

2
Kya Kareemi Hai Keh Bazaar Mein Chalte Chalte.
Khoon-e-Sajjad Ne Yaqoot Banaye Pathar.
Hath Tu Tere Pas-e-Pusht Bandeh The Zainab.
Kaise Phir Bahi Ke Lashe Se Hataye Pathar.

3
Yeh Tu Insaf Nahi Jiss Ne Bachaya Islam.
Oss Ki Beti Bhare Bazaar Mein Khaye Pathar.
Tane De De Ke Kiya Zakhmi Dil-e-Zainab Ko.
Kuch Musalmano Ke Jab Hath Na Aye Pathar.

4
Tera Bahi Na Raha Bint-e-Ali Haye Ghazab.
Warna Mumkin Tha Koi Tujh Pe Uthaye Pathar.
Khoon Tapka Tha Sar-e-Shah Se Oss Par Sajjad.
Khoon Phir Kaise Halab Mein Na Bahaye Pathar.
