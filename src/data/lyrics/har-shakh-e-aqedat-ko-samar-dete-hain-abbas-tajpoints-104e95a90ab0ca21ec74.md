1
Har Shakh e Aqedat Ko Samar Dete Hain Abbas.
Nazara Ba Taufeeq-e-Nazar Detey Hain Abbas.
Mit Saktay Nahi Harf Woh Quran-e-Wafa Ke.
Tehreer Sar-e-Aab Jo Kar Detey Hain Abbas.

2
Aankhein Gham-e-Shabbir Mein Nam Hone Se Pehle.
Ashkon Ke Ewaz Lal-o-Gohar Detey Hain Abbas.
Shabbir Ke Qadmo Mein Badal Jati Hai Taqdeer.
Hur Ko Shab-e-Ashor Khabar Detey Hain Abbas.

3
Mujh Ko Kahin Jane Ki Zarorat Nahi Hoti.
Har Cheez Mohaiya Yahin Kar Detey Hain Abbas.
Ghazi Se Na Daryaft Karo Wosat-e-Darya.
Darya Ko Tu Mashkeza Mein Bhar Detey Hain Abbas.

4
Lau De Ke Kaha Dast-e-Buredah Ne Sar-e-Sham.
Tabandagi e Noor-e-Sahar Detey Hain Abbas.
Aa Jaye Abad Maut Dar-e-Aal-e-Nabi Par.
Kehte Hain Yahan Umar-e-Khizar Detey Hain Abbas.
