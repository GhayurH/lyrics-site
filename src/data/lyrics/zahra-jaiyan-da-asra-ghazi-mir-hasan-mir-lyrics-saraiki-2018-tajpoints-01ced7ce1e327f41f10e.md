1
Zahra Jaiyan Da Asra Ghazi Mir Hasan Mir.
Hoya Karbal De Wich Juda Ghazi.
Aj V Bint-e-Hussain Rondi Aye.
Hath Mere Qaid Taun Chura Ghazi.

2
Sir Taun Zainab De Leh Gayi Chadar.
Jadoon Maqtal Nu Tur Paya Ghazi.
Kiwein Jawaan Gi Sham Te Kofa.
Maikon Mahmal Te Aa Baha Ghazi.

3
Che Jagah Taun Hussain Chunriya Aye.
Fair V Pora Na Mil Saka Ghazi.
Meri Chadar Khuda Da Parda Aye.
Meri Chadar Di Tu Baqa Ghazi.

4
Aakhay Ummal Baneen Das Zainab.
Zeen Taun Haye Kiwein Latha Ghazi.
Shimar Raj Raj Hussain Koon Maray.
Janrda Aye Nayi Rehya Ghazi.

5
Aan Shabir Wainr Kita Aye.
Haye Zainab Nayi Riya Ghazi.
Kar Ke Matam Raza Baqiye Wich.
Aap Rondi Aye FatimaS Ghazi.
