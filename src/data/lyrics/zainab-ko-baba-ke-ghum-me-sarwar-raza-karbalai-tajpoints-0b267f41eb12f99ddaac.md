1
Zainab Ko Baba Ke Ghum Me Sarwar Raza Karbalai.
Zainab Ko Baba Ke Gham Mein.
Madar Bohat Yaad Aaein.
Uthnay Laga Jab Janaza Tabhi.

2
Zainab Pukari Aye Baba Ali.
Phota Hamara Muqadar.
Kehti Hai Zainab Yeh Hasnain Se.
Mushkil Hai Ab Hum Rahein Chain Se.

3
Sara Jahan Hai Sitamgar.
Baba Ke Zakhmo Ko Dekha Tabhi.
Kulsoom Boli Main Wari Gayi.
Kis Ne Chalaya Hai Khanjar.

4
Miqdad-o-Meesum Ya Jin-o-Bashar.
Jibreel Rotay Hain Sar Peet Kar.
Ghazi Sa Roye Dilawar.
Kofay Ke Bachoon Ne Di Yeh Sada.

5
Koi Yateemo Ka Ab Na Raha.
Apna Sahara Tha Haider.
Roney Ke Din Hain Aye Ahl-e-Azza.
Maula Pe Zalim Ka Khanjar Chala.

6
Barpa Hai Har So Hi Mehshar.
 Asif Ka Kehna Hai Sun Lo Zara.
Aati Hai Ahl-e-Azza Ki Sada.
Baba Ke Gham Mein Youn Sarwar.
