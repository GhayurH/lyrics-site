1
Ki Ziyarat Jo Ali Ki Tu Muhammad Ne Kaha Chashme Baddoor.
Ho Mubarak Ho Chachi Bint-e-Asad Shair-e-Khuda Chashme Baddoor.
Aaj Kabay Mein Muhammad Ka Wasi Aaya Hai.
Deen Ki Shan Hai Islam Ka Sarmaya Hai.
Nazar-e-Bad Se Issay Door Rakhe Mera Khuda Chashme Baddoor.
Bolin Yeh Bint-e-Asad Dekhein Rasool-e-Do Sara.
Jane Kyun Aankh Nahi Kholta Bhai Tera.
Umar Lag Jaye Meri Jeeta Rahe Lal Mera Chashme Baddoor.
Barh Ke Aagosh Mein Haider Ko Liya Aaqa Ne.
Khol Deen Aankhein Ossi Waqt Ali Mola Ne.
Keh Uthein Dekh Ke Yeh Bint-e-Asad Sal-e-Alla Chashme Baddoor.
Phir Nabowat Ne Imamat Ki Balaein Le Lein.
Yeh Kaho Bhai Ne Bhai Se Duaein Le Lein.
Farsh Se Arsh Talak Goonj Uthi Aik Sada Chashme Baddoor.
Asslam Aye Shah-e-Abrar Ali Ne Keh Kar.
Haisiyaat Apni Ayaan Kar Di Saheefe Parh Kar.
Chashm-e-Hairat Ne Kayi Dair Qaseeda Yeh Parha Chashme Baddoor.
Munh Mein Haider Ke Muhammad Ne Rakhi Apni Zabaan.
Shashma-e-Ilm Se Serab Hoye Shah-e-Zamaan.
Noor Se Noor Mila Phail Gayi Haq Ki Zia Chashme Baddoor.
Chothe Din Aaye Ali Khan-e-Rab Se Bahir.
Majma-e-Talib-e-Deedar Utara Yasir.
Ain-Ullah Asad-Ullah Ali Wajh-Ullah Chashme Baddoor.
