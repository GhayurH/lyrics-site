1
Tha Karbala Mein Andhera Hussain Se Pehle.
Bara Udas Tha Sajda Hussain Se Pehle.
Kabhi Batool Ki Sora Kabhi Nabi Ban Kar.
Hasan Ki Shakal Mein Dhal Kar Kabhi Ali Ban Kar.
Hussain Hi Th Hamesha Hussain Se Pehle.
Salam Bint-e-Ali Aap Ki Muhabbat Par.
Nabi Batool Ali Mujtaba Sabhi Thay Magar.
Kisi Ko Tum Ne Na Dekha Hussain Se Pehle.
Shaheed Zinda Hai Sabit Kahan Tha Dunya Par.
Nabi Rasool Wali Bhi Howay Shaheed Magar.
Koi Sina Pe Na Bola Hussain Se Pehle.
Bata Raha Hai Zamane Ko Yeh Mazar-e-Habib.
Barah-e-Rast Na Ho Ga Koi Khuda Se Qareeb.
Bashar Ko Milna Pare Ga Hussain Se Pehle.
Unhein Hussain Ki Majlis Se Pyar Hai Kaisa.
Abhi Zameen Pe Farsh-e-Azza Bichaya Tha.
Falak Se Aa Gayin Zahra Hussain Se Pehle.
Ossay Bhi Hur Ki Tarha Ka Atta Kiya Rutba.
Naseeb Ho Gaya Romal-e-Fatima Zahra.
Yeh Ashk Kuch Bhi Nahi Tha Hussain Se Pehle.
Kaha Rubab Se Bache Ne Hans Ke Jholay Mein.
Main Kainat Ulat Doon Ga Aik Lamhe Mein.
Dila Do Izn Jo Baba Hussain Se Pehle.
Adab Se Aa Ke Jabeen-e-Niyaz Jhuk Jaye.
Kisi Ke Khail Ki Khatir Namaz Ruk Jaye.
Kabhi Howa Nahi Aisa Hussain Se Pehle.
Khud Apni Marzi Takallum Chala Nahi Sakta.
Beghair Izn Tere Sath Ja Nahi Sakta.
Aye Maut Pochna Ho Ga Hussain Se Pehle.
