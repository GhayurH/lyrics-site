1
Sakina Zindaan Ky Andhery Mein Sibtain Haider 2023.
Sakina Zindaan Ke Andherey Mein Baba Ko Dhondti Hai.
Baba Ka Seena Nahi Milta Ab Khak Pe Woh Soti Hai.
Naqay Par Laya Sham Talak Seene Pe Soney Wali Ko.
Bethi Nahi Jati Thi Oss Se Rasiyon Se Tha Bandha Bachi Ko.
Kar Kar Ke Yaad Yehi Zainab Ro Ro Ke Gash Khati Hai.
Iss Chaar Baras Ki Bachi Ko Ummat Ne Itna Rulaya Hai.
Jari Hai Lahu Kano Se Our Chehray Pe Tamacha Mara Hai.
Yeh Kis Ko Pukarey Behar-e-Madad Baba Na Chacha Ghazi Hai.
Aksar Woh Phophi Se Kehti Hai Kya Hum Nahi Wapis Jaein Gay.
Kya Dekhney Haal Sakina Ka Ammo Our Baba Na Aaein Gay.
Kuch Keh Nahi Pati Hai Zainab Bas Sun Ke Yehi Roti Hai.
Dadi Ki Tarha Poti Ne Bhi Zalim Se Tamacha Khaya Hai.
Roney Na Diya Baba Par Bhi Yeh Karb-o-Bala Ki Zahra Hai.
Dono Ko Sitam Se Ummat Ke Ghurbat Mein Qaza Aayi Hai.
Kehti Thi Yeh Zainab Ro Ro Kar Aye Shah-e-Arab Ki Shehzadi.
Bas Iss Liye Teri Our Meri Dushman Yeh Dunya Sari Hoi.
Main Zahra Ki Beti Hoon Our Tu Zahra Ki Poti Hai.
 Alvi Tha Haram Mein Hashar Bapa Zindaan Mein Jab Oss Ko Aayi Qaza.
Zakhmo Se Bhari Oss Maiyyat Ko Haye Ghusal-o-Kafan Bhi Na Mil Paya.
Ik Our Qayamat Abid Par Bazar Ke Baad Yehi Hai.
