Sajjad Tere Gham Mein Zanjeer Ro Rahi Hai Hasan Sadiq.
Aankhon Mein Khuwab Chup Hain Tabeer Ro Rahi Hai.

1
Kantay Bichay Hoye Hain Jalti Hoi Zameen Par.
Chalay Tarap Rahe Hain Taqdeer Ro Rahi Hai.
Abbas Bawafa Ki Jageer Karbala Hai.
Abid Ke Gham Mein Sari Jageer Ro Rahi Hai.

2
Abid Na Kaisay Roein Aye Shamiyon Batao.
Zakhmi Hain Kan Jis Ke Humsheer Ro Rahi Hai.
Sajjad Ke Badan Ka Sab Khon Jal Chuka Hai.
Paon Mein Jo Pari Hai Zanjeer Ro Rahi Hai.

3
Aankhon Se Khon Abid Aisay Baha Raha Hai.
Ho Ke Har Aik Bibi Dilgeer Ro Rahi Hai.
Ro Ro Ke Parh Raha Hoon Sajjad Ka Yeh Noha.
:REHAN AZMI: Ki Tehreer Ro Rahi Hai.
