Midhat-e-Shabir Tera Shukriya
Katib-e-Taqdeer Tera Shukriya

1
Yaad Akbar Ki Dila Deta Hai Tu
Nara-e-Takbeer Tera Shukriya
Mere Ghar Aate Hain Masoomeen Bhi
Majlis-e-Shabir Tera Shukriya

2
Mushkilon Mein Muskurana Aa Giya
Asghar-e-Besheer Tera Shukriya
Tere Dam Se Hai Sadaye Ya Hussain
Zainab-e-Dilgeer Tera Shukriya

3
Aaj Bhi Kabay Se Aati Hai Sada
Sajdah-e-Shabir Tera Shukriya
Rizq Milta Hai Humein Tere Tufail
Dast-e-Khaibar Geer Tera Shukriya

4
Ban Giya Turbat Mein Mahool-e-Kisa
Madar-e-Shabir Tera Shukriya
Karbala Hai:SIBT-E-JAFAR: Ka Watan
Khuld Ki Jageer Tera Shukriya
