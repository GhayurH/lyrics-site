1
Azza Ke Deep Jalao, Imam Aate Hain.
Gham-e-Hussain Barhao, Imam Aate Hain.
Namaz Parhne Ko Sadiyon Se Woh Taraptay Hain.
Khuda Se Esa Dua-e-Zahoor Kartay Hain.
Unhein Yeh Ja Ke Batao, Imam Aate Hain.
Na Koi Khof Karo Ab, Kissi Bhi Dushman Se.
Karein Gay Hazrat-e-Abbas, Sar Alag Tan Se.
Mazar-e-Zahra Banao, Imam Aate Hain.
Nabi, Wasi-o-Wali Hoonge Sab Wahan Hazir.
Parhein Gay Saye Mein Oss Ke Hussain Ke Zakir.
Alam Haram Mein Lagao, Imam Aate Hain.
Jinhein Fidak Ke Loteron Ka Hai Charha Howa Roog.
Maza Tu Hoga Keh Jab Cheekh Kar Kahin Gay Woh Loag.
Bachao, Hum Ko Bachao, Imam Aate Hain.
Kahin Gi Fatima Zahra, Aye Jibraeel Utho.
Thakay Hoye Hain Azadar-e-Karbala, Jao.
Unhein Sabeel Pilao, Imam Aate Hain.
Kaha Rubab Ne Bachi Se Hosla Karna.
Ho Muntaqim Ka Zahoor Ab Yehi Dua Karna.
Sakina Khof Na Khao, Imam Aate Hain.
Unhein Talab Hai Faqat Maut Ke Muhibbon Ki.
Kahan Unhein Hai Zarorat Bas Aisay Waison Ki.
Habib Jaisay Bolao, Imam Aate Hain.
Suna Jo Karwaan Aaya Hai, Laut Kar Mera.
Tu Boli Sughra Keh Akbar Bhi Aa Gaya Hoga.
Kaneezon Ghar Ko Sajao, Imam Aate Hain.
Likho Woh Jis Se Ho Razi, Imam Aye Taseer.
Parho Zohaib Woh Jis Se, Hoon Shadmaan Shabeer.
Tum Apna Farz Nibhao, Imam Aate Hain.
