Sham Chaliyan Ne Bhaina Baj Rida De Ghazi Sham Chaliyan Ne.
Ghar Bar Sab Lutaya Rang Deen Nu V Laya Zahra Diyaan Paliyaan Ne.

1
Kita Haya Na Ummat Kitay Zulm Batheray Ahmad De Warsaan Te.
Jis Maa Nu Sare Arshi Farshi Karan Salama Oss Maa Diyaan Dhiyaan Ne.
Haider Da Lal Toon Ayein Zahra Di Tu Dua Ayein Tasiyan Da Hosla Ayein.
Zamin Toon Pardiyaan Da Tere Te Veer Ghazi Sanu Aasaan Bariyaan Ne.

2
Shabbir Mera Lasha Khiyam Na Le Jawein Darya Te Chor Jawein.
Pani Nahin Kool Mere Teri Dhi Ne Masooma Nal Meri Rahwaan Maliyaan Ne.
Manzoor Hondiyaan Ne Ais Bawafa De Dar Te Sajid Sabhay Duawaan.
Sadqa Nabi Di Dhi Da Denda Aye Sare Jag Nu Ajay Banwaan Katiyaan Ne.
