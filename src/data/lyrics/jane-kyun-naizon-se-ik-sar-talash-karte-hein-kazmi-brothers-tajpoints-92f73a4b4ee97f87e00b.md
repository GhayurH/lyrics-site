1
Jaltay Jholay Ki Raakh Pe Bethi.
Maa Ki Nazrein Hain Nezay Walon Par.
Jane Kyun Nezon Se Ik Sar Talash Kartay Hain.
Woh Maa Ke Samne Asghar Talash Kartay Hain.

2
Hai Sheer Khawar Ka Khaybar Shikan Ka Sar Tu Nahi.
Jisay Yazid Ke Lashkar Talash Kartay Hain.
Laeen Nezay Se Asghar Talash Kar Ke Bhi.
Zibha Ke Wastay Khanjar Talash Kartay Hain.

3
Woh Nezay Jin Pe Hai Asghar Tera Lahu Taza.
Woh Ab Rubab Ki Chadar Talash Kartay Hain.
Sakina Kaisay Jhulati Thi Jhola e Asghar.
Hussain Ro Ke Woh Manzar Talash Kartay Hain.

4
Woh Muskuraney Ka Letey Hain Iss Tarha Badlah.
Woh Iss Ki Lash Ko Hans Kar Talash Kartay Hain.
Woh Aisay Mar Rahe Hain Zameen Par Nezay.
Keh Jaisay Sar Nahi Pathar Talash Kartay Hain.

5
Chupa Liya Na Ho Teeron Ke Darmiyan Kahin.
Woh Sheh Ke Seene Ke Opar Talash Kartay Hain.
Batool Nohay Pe Mujh Ko Woh Ajar Dein Gi Raza.
Jisay Tamam Sukhanwar Talash Kartay Hain.
