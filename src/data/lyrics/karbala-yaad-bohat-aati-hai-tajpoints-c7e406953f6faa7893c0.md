Karbala Yaad Bohat Aati Hai Lyrics
Kareem-e-Karb-o-Bala Mojza Dikha Dijay.
Karbala Yaad Bohat Aati Hai Bula Lijay.
Hussain Aap Jo Chahein Tu Kuch Nahi Mushkil.
Tarap Raha Hai Bohat Karbala Ki Yaad Mein Dil.
Phir Aik Bar Mujhe Karbala Dikha Dijay.
Tumhare Rozay Pe Aane Mein Aaj Diqqat Hai.
Magar Khabar Hai Tumhein Mujh Ko Kitni Chahat Hai.
Main Sar Ke Bal Chala Aaon Ga Bas Sada Dijay.
Mere Khiyal Mein Jab Aaye Do Katay Bazu.
Alam Ko Dekh Ke Aankhon Mein Aa Gaye Aanso.
Tarap Raha Hoon Alamdar Se Mila Dijay.
Galuye Asghar-e-Be Sheer Chid Gaya Tha Jidhar.
Sabeel Doodh Ki Ja Kar Lagaon Main Bhi Udhar.
Main Zairon Se Kahon Ga Mujhe Dua Dijay.
Kissi Bhi Haal Mein Jana Hai Rooz-e-Ashora.
Lahu Badan Se Bahana Hai Rooz-e-Ashora.
Hai Matami Ki Sada Phir Humein Sada Dijay.
Bulaein Aap Jisay Chahein Apne Rozay Par.
Ghulam Aap Ki Taqseem Pe Hain Razi Magar.
Sakon Dil Ko Miley Kis Tarha Bata Dijay.
Hain Ashk Aankhon Mein Our Dil Udas Hai Mera.
Mujhe Bulaein Gaye Kab Aap Karbala Maula.
Kuch Aa Ke Khuwab Mein Hi Mujh Ko Aasra Dijay.
Sada:REHAN-E-Azza Ki Hai Bar Zaban-e- Raza.
Main Arbaeen Pe Mashi Karon Ga Aye Maula.
Phir Aik Bar Mujhe Paon Par Chala Dijay.
