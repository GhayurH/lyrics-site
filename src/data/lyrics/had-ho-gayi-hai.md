Had Ho Gayi Hai Lyrics
Inhi Se Chali Thi Khuda Ki Khudai Inhi Par Khudai Ki Had Ho Gayi Hai.

1
Do Aalam Mein Aal-e-Nabi Hain Keh Jin Par Khuda Aashnai Ki Had Ho Gayi Hai.
Kissi Dam Bhi Sona Nahi Jis Ko Bhata Ossay Iss Ka Sona Kuch Aisa Hai Bhaya.
Mili Iss Ki Marzi Tu Soney Ke Badlay Riza-e-Khuda Le Ke Jibreel Aaya.
Nabi Bhi Bachaya Rizaein Bhi Le Lein Ali Ki Kamai Ki Had Ho Gayi Hai.

2
Khuda-o-Ali Jantay Hain Woh Manzil Bulaya Tha Ahmad Ko Rab Ne Jahan Par.
Chala Farsh Se Soye Arsh-e-Barin Jab Ruka Qafila Teesrey Aasmaan Par.
Kaha Phir Nabi Ne Jibreel Jao Tumhari Rasai Ki Had Ho Gayi Hai.
Khuda Ne Banaya Hai Aal-e-Nabi Ko Inho Ne Khuda Ki Khudai Bachai.

3
Agar Tum Meri Baat Se Mutafiq Ho Tu Phir Mujh Ko Yeh Baat Kehne Do Bhai.
Udhar Kibriyai Ki Had Ho Gayi Idhar Na Khudai Ki Had Ho Gayi Hai.
Hum Aal-e-Muhammad Ke Shia Hain Logo Batana Hai Hum Ko Yeh Apne Amal Se.
Magar Hum Yeh Afsoos Se Dekhtey Hain Khushi Ki Mahafil Hoon Ya Gham Ke Moqay.

4
Kahin Be Ridai Ki Had Ho Gayi Hai Kahin Khud Numai Ki Had Ho Gayi Hai.
Ohad Ki Larai Ho Ya Jang-e-Khaybar Har Ik Ko Safai Se Kata Hai Tu Ne.
Teri Dastaras Mein Jo Aaya Hai Oss Ko Barabar Ke Hison Mein Banta Hai Tu Ne.
Aye Taig-e-Ali Tere Qurban Jaon Ke Tujh Par Safai Ki Had Ho Gayi Hai.

5
Woh Kagaz Ke Maidaan Mein Lafzoon Ka Lashkar Na Lashay Girey Our Na Katay Gaye Sar.
Faqat Chand Satron Ki Tehreer Likh Kar Qalam Se Larai Yeh Jeetay Hain Shabar.
Kaha Dekh Kar Zulfiqar-e-Ali Ne Hasan Par Larai Ki Had Ho Gayi Hai.
