Hussain Teri Qasam Joan Rizvi

1
Rooz-e-Azal Se Rooz-e-Abad Oss Ke Baad Bhi.
Zindah Rahe Gi Roshni Nam-e-Hussain Ki.
Chodah So Saal Baad Bhi Shiddat Na Kam Hoi.
Barhta Hi Ja Raha Hai Tera Gham Sadi Sadi.
Khayi Yeh Maa Ki Gaud Mein Pehli Qasam Hussain.
Mar Ke Bhi Dil Se Khatam Na Hoga Yeh Gham Hussain.

2
Hussain Teri Qasam, Teri Qasam Ya Hussain.
Wallah Wallah Ya Hussain Ya Abba Abdillah.
Wallah Wallah Ya Hussain Ya Hussain Saar ALLAH.
Wallah Wallah Ya Hussain Ya Ibn Rasool ALLAH.
Hum Ko Paband Karein Lakh Zamane Walay.
Kab Tere Ishq Se Hum Baaz Hain Aane Walay.
Sar Pe Bandhein Gay Kafan Ghar Se Nikal Aaein Gay.
Tere Abbas Ke Parcham Ko Uthane Walay.
Kam Na Hoga Gham Tera Hussain Teri Qasam.
Naam Lein Gay Hum Tera Hussain Teri Qasam.
Tham Kar Alam Tera Hussain Teri Qasam.
Teri Qasam Ya Hussain.
Jab Diya Tu Ne Bhojaya Tha Shab-e-Ashora.
Chor Ke Tanha Tujhe Koi Bhi Nasir Na Gaya.
Jang Se Pehle Hi Dil Jeet Chuka Tu Sab Ke.
Dekh Ke Tujh Ko Ali Our Muhammad Ne Kaha.
Jaisay Sahabi Tere Hussain Teri Qasam.
Aisay Kisay Kab Miley Hussain Teri Qasam.
Dartay Nahi Maut Se Hussain Teri Qasam.
Teri Qasam Ya Hussain.
Har Baras Tere Liye Chehray Pe Mati Mal Ke.
Karbala Jate Hain Zawwar Najaf Se Chal Ke.
Na Hi Chehray Pe Thakawat Na Talab Rahat Ki.
Muntazir Hotey Hain Woh Loag Bas Oss Hi Pal Ke.
Aaye Gi Jab Karbala Hussain Teri Qasam.
Dekhein Gay Roza Tera Hussain Teri Qasam.
Hoon Gay Na Tujh Se Juda Hussain Teri Qasam.
Teri Qasam Ya Hussain.
Hajj Ko Umray Mein Badal Kar Tu Chala Jab Maula.
De Ke Aawaz Tujhe Khana e Rab Kehne Laga.
Tu Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Hai Tu Mera Waris Hai.
Karbala Sath Meri Rooh Ko Bhi Leta Ja.
Chor Ke Mat Ja Yahan Hussain Teri Qasam.
Kaun Hai Mera Yahan Hussain Teri Qasam.
Ab Nahi Rehna Yahan Hussain Teri Qasam.
Teri Qasam Ya Hussain.
Hum Karein Gay Matam Hussain Teri Qasam.
Oncha Hoga Parcham Hussain Teri Qasam.
Yeh Namazon Ka Safar Hussain Teri Qasam.
Yeh Tilawat Ka Asar Hussain Teri Qasam.
Jari Rakhein Gay Hum Hussain Teri Qasam.
Bichay Ga Farsh-e-Azza Hussain Teri Qasam.
Hogi Majlis Barpa Hussain Teri Qasam.
Tere Baba Ki Wila Maqsad-e-Karb-o-Bala.
Tere Akbar Ka Jigar Tere Asghar Ka Gala.
Ba Wafa Ke Bazu Our Zainab Ki Rida.
Woh Tera Zakhmi Badan Our Teeron Ki Chubhan.
Bhool Na Paein Gay Hum Hussain Teri Qasam.
Yaad Rakhein Gay Yeh Gham Hussain Teri Qasam.
Jab Talak Dam Mein Hai Dam Hussain Teri Qasam.
Teri Qasam, Teri Qasam Ya Hussain.
Rooz-e-Ashor Sada Deney Lagi Karb-o-Bala.
Hosla Aisa Nahi Dekha Kissi Bhi Maa Ka.
Ran Se Jab Aoun-o-Muhammad Ke Janazay Aaye.
Apne Bhai Ki Taraf Dekh Ke Zainab Ne Kaha.
Hotey Agar Our Bhi Hussain Teri Qasam.
Sar Pe Tere Warti Hussain Teri Qasam.
Roti Na Zainab Kabhi Hussain Teri Qasam.
Teri Qasam Ya Hussain.
Haye Afsoos Behan Dekh Rahi Thi Manzar.
Jab Chala Ran Mein Teri Khushk Ragoon Par Khanjar.
Dasht Mein Ghoron Se Lasha Tera Pamal Howa.
Asar-e-Ashor Dhali Aa Gaya Sar Naizay Par.
Boli Behan Dekh Ke Hussain Teri Qasam.
Wadah Hai Bhai Merey Hussain Teri Qasam.
Marne Na Doon Gi Tujhe Hussain Teri Qasam.
Teri Qasam Ya Hussain.
 Joan-o- Zeeshan Ke Honton Pe Yehi Narah Hai.
Tu Mera Dil Tu Meri Jan Mera Maula Hai.
Mar Gaya Kab Ka Yazid Aaj Bhi Zindah Hai Hussain.
Sar Kata Kar Bhi Tera Naam Sada Oncha Hai.
Kehta Hai Khud Yeh Khuda Hussain Teri Qasam.
Ho Na Saka Jo Tera Hussain Teri Qasam.
Ho Nahi Sakta Mera Hussain Teri Qasam.
Teri Qasam Ya Hussain.
