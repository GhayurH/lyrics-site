1
Ay Mere Mola Hussain Wajhi Hasan Zaidi
Ab Iss Se Jan Lo Keh Hai Kya Kya Hussain Ka
Marzi Hai Kibriya Ki Iradah Hussain Ka
Jab Se Nabi Ke Sajday Mein Baithay Hain Pusht Par
Ahsan Mand Tab Se Hai Sajdah Hussain Ka
Masroof Hain Tawaf Mein Kabay Ke Kul Jahan
Lekin Tawaf Karta Hai Kaba Hussain Ka
Wajib Kiye Hain Rozay Khuda Ne Iss Liye
Ta Keh Na Bholay Koi Bhi Faqa Hussain Ka
Nezay Pe Haq Sunanay Ka Manzar Nazar Mein Hai
Chup Kaisay Hoga Chahney Wala Hussain Ka
Kam Aaein Gi Kissi Ki Na Jab Rishtay Dariyan
Mehshar Mein Kam Aaye Ga Rishta Hussain Ka
Khak-e-Shifa Ke Sadqay Maseeha Bana Diya
Ahsan Kaisay Bholein Gay Esa Hussain Ka
Fakhar-e-Hussain Kab Hai Yeh Hai Fakhar-e-Jibreel
Jibreel Ne Jholaya Hai Jhola Hussain Ka
Be Shak Zareeh Unn Ki Hai Be Had Nashaib Mein
Lekin Hai Roza Phir Bhi Musalla Hussain Ka
Hum Ko Khuda Ke Chehray Ka Dedar Ho Gaya
Karb-o-Bala Mein Dekha Jo Roza Hussain Ka
Fatwa Garoon Se Keh Do Manaein Ab Apni Khair
Parday Se Aane Wala Hai Beta Hussain Ka
Abbas Ki Nigahon Mein Qibla Wohi Tu Hai
Hai Jis Jagah Bhi Naqsh-e-Pa Hussain Ka
Dali Hai Maa Ne Chehra e Akbar Pe Youn Naqab
Yeh Nasal Qeemati Hai Khazana Hussain Ka
Nu Lakh Shamiyon Ka Nishaan Dhondta Jahan
Abbas Ko Jo Milta Ishara Hussain Ka
Sheh Karbala Chaley Gaye Kabay Ko Chor Kar
Kabay Ne Chora Phir Bhi Na Peecha Hussain Ka
Ojhal Nazar Se Ho Ke Woh Jata Hai Karbala
Koi Bhi Matami Nahi Marta Hussain Ka
