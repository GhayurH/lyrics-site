1
Safeer e Aal e Nabi Kalma Gowaan Marya Ravi Road
Haye Baiyat Hussaini Kar Ke Fair Namaziyan Ne Muslim Da Sir Utariya.
Ghar Rondiyaan Pitdiyan Dhiyaan Muslim Chor Ke Turya.
Ummat Mehman Nawazi Keeti Fair Na Murya.

2
Ghazi Di Bhain Ruqaiya Bachray Daan Kitay Nalay Sir Da Taj Wariya.
Kofay Wich Kaliyaan Reh Gaya Haider Da Damad Aye.
Ainu Aakhri Welay Aayi Veer Abbas Di Yaad Aye.
Lagay Zakhm Hazaran Badan Uttay Talwaraan De Par Hosla Na Harya.

3
Zahra Diyaan Jaiyaan Jad Babay De Shehar Choon Langiyaan.
Haye Pathraan Di Barsat Che Hoan Siraan Taun Nangiyan.
Jithay Sir Muslim Da Tangiya Shimar Kaminay Ne Othon Qafila Guzriya.
Hath Walaan De Wich Pa Ke Haris Wari Wari.

4
Sir Kitay Qalam Ruqaiya Di Ainay Jori Mari.
Pokhiyan Te Pyasiyaan Balaan Ne Haye Rab Jane Kiwein Zulm Aye Sahariya.
Haris Di Zojah Baal Sawaye Mar Ke Talay.
Aye Ronday Uth Paye Maa Nu Wekh Ke Khab Wachalay.

5
Zalim Ne Sun Liya Bachiyaan Ne Jadon Ro Ro Ke Baba, Baba Pokariya.
Ik Zakhmi Doja Pyas Hathoon Hoya Majboor Aye.
 Akhtar Muslim La Char Khara Watna Taun Door Aye.
Ik Ghut Pani Da Deway Jo Pardesi Nu Koi Sahara Na Riya.
