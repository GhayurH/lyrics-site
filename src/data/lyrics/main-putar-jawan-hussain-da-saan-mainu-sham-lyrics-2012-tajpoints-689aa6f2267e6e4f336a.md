1
Main Putar Jawan Hussain Da Saan Mainu Sham Zaeef Banaya.
Meri Moraan Kamar Jhuka Chori Te Paishiyaan Khon Rawaya.
Assi Loag Shareef Madine De Kadi Galiyaan Moor Na Wekhey.
Mastoraan Te Majboraan Nu Main Aap Bazar Lay Aaya.

2
Main Gairtaan Wala Abid Haan Tusi Sabar Mera Aazmao Na.
Main Bewah Bhain De Hathaan Wich Khud Sham Da Zewar Paya.
Sajjad Di Khasta Hali Da Ais Gal Toon Andaza Kar Lo.
Jinay Qaidan Bhain De Lashay Nu Ik Cholay Wich Dafnaya.

3
Tafseeraan Pak Quran Diyaan Aj Baj Ghilaf De Aayiyaan.
Tussan Qaidi Parday Daraan Nu Sir Nangay Sham Phiraya.
Taboot Te Pak Jageeraan Nu Akhiyaan Nal Bosay De Ke.
 Samar Ne Ujariyaan Dhiyaan Da Aj Sara Haal Sunaya.
