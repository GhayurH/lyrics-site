1
Masooma Aakhay Veran Nu Aye Pyas Di Had Ki Hondi Aye.
Rang Zard Piya Lab Zakhmi Ne Puchdi Be Hosh Payi Hondi Aye.
Pehli Aye Had Jiwein Aag Lag Gayi Seene Wich Nazar Nahin Aanda.
Jadoon Digiya Qasim Aakhda Si Har Shey Dhundli Payi Lagdi Aye.

2
Doji Aye Had Aye Talu De Nal Haye Jeeb Chamar Jandi Aye.
Main Aakhni Saan Kyun Bolda Nahin Akbar Na Waaz Nikaldi Aye.
Teeji Aye Had Aye Jiwein Khak Uttay Machli Tarfay Bin Pani.
Main Tarafdiyan Asghar Takiya Karbal Di Khak Rayi Uddi Aye.

3
Chothi Had Aye Jadon Hadiyaan Taun Haye Maas Juda Ho Jaway.
Banwaan Chuk Aakhay Wekh Ta Sayi Har Jah Hun Maas Payi Chaddi Aye.
Koi Pyasa Howay Dunya Te Pani Mangda Aye Loko.
:AAL E ABDULLAH: Aye Pyasi Pani De Naa Taun Dardi Aye.
