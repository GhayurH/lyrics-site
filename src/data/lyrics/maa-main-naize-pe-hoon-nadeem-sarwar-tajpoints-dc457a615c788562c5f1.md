1
Maa Main Naize Pe Hoon
Naize Se Asghar Ko Nazar Ayi Jo Madar.
Dekha Khula Sar Hai Pareshan Hai Muztar.
Rone Lage Kehne Lage Tab Ali Asghar.
Maa Mujhe Apne Seene Se Laga Le.
Maa Mujhe Apni Godi Mein Chupa Le.
Main Main Naize Pe Hoon.
Amma Bohat Din Howay Jhole Mein Na Soya.
Maa Teri Izzat Ki Qasam Phir Bhi Na Roya.
Ab Tujhe Dekha Tu Mere Lab Howay Goya.
Aaj Bandhe Hathon Ko Jhola Sa Bana Le.
Jism Kahan Sar Kahan Haye Muqadar.
Amma Badan Reh Giya Karbal Ki Sar Zameen Par.
Amma Tere Sath Chala Aaya Mera Sar.
Karta Tujhe Kaise Laeeno Ke Hawaly.
Garm Hawa Garam Zameen Jalta Howa Teer.
Amma Zara Socho Mere Qad Se Bara Teer.
Kaise Kahon Halq Pe Jis Waqt Laga Teer.
Ho Gaye Be-Hoosh Mujhe Dekhne Wale.
Kab Se Sakina Ne Nahi Mujh Ko Kiya Pyar.
Karb-o-Bala Kofa Kahan Sham Ka Bazar.
Darti Hai Kya Khoon Se Bhare Dekh Ke Rukhsar.
Naize Ke Nazdeek Zara Oss Ko Bula Le.
Dhoop Mein Tu Ne Na Kabhi Ghar Se Nikala.
Amma Bare Pyar Se Tu Ne Mujhe Pala.
Aaj Nahi Koi Mujhe Dekhne Wala.
Tu Hi Mujhe Dhoop Ki Shidat Se Bacha Le.
Dekh Tera Lakht-e-Jigar Youn Tu Hai Asghar.
Naiza Sawari Mein Hai Akbar Ke Barabar.
Nook-e-Sina Par Hai Sitara Sa Mera Ghar.
Meri Shujaat Pe Gale Mujh Ko Laga Le.
Baba Ka Sar Bahi Ka Sar Nook-e-Sina Par.
Aap Ke Sar Par Nahi Amma Koi Chadar.
Kaise Main Bardasht Karon Amma Yeh Manzar.
Urti Hoi Khak Se Munh Apna Chupa Le.
Tukre Kaleja Howa Bano Ka Har Ik Aan.
Naize Pe Goya Howa Jab Choota Sa Quran.
Maa Ki Yeh Aawaz Thi Bas:SARWAR-O-REHAN:.
Hath Hoon Aazad Tu Arman Nikale.
