1
Mazloom Mere Baba Sagar Ali Bhatti.
Mazloom Mere Baba Mazloom Mere Baba.
Naqay Se Gir Gayi Hoon Mujh Ko Sanbhalo Aa Kar.
Zakhmo Se Choor Dekho Pardesi Be Watan Hoon.

2
Umar-e-Raseda Lagti Main Tu Sheh-e-Zaman Hoon.
Aaya Hai Kamar Mein Khum Parti Hai Kam Nazar.
Logon Ke Bachay Mujh Ko Hans Kar Sata Rahe Hain.
Bagi Ki Beti Keh Kar Mujh Ko Rula Rahe Hain.

3
Ummat Ki Be Rukhi Ka Sadma Hai Mere Dil Par.
Kano Ki Baliyon Ke Zakhmo Se Khon Jari.
Takleef Ki Shiddat Yeh Mujh Par Hai Bohat Bhari.
Khon-e-Badan Se Sara Kurta Bhi Ho Gaya Tar.

4
Shimar e Laeen Ne Mujh Ko Marey Hain Youn Tamachay.
Rukhsar Mere Neelay Dono Hoye Hain Haye.
Be Jurm Saza Mujh Ko Deta Hai Yeh Sitamgar.
Gardan Ki Rasiyon Se Ghutta Yeh Mera Dam Hai.

5
Zindah Rahon Gi Baba Umeed Bohat Kam Hai.
Ruk Jaein Gi Yeh Sansein Mar Jaye Gi Yeh Muztar.
Amma Bichar Gayi Hai Bhai Bichar Gaya Hai.
ALLAH Hi Jane Kunba Mera Kidhar Gaya Hai.

6
Anjan Raston Se Baba Hai Teri Dukhtar.
 Fayaz Sheh Ke Sar Se Sagar Sada Yeh Aayi.
Naqay Se Gar Gayi Hai Sajjad Meri Jayi.
Oss Ko Talash Kar Ke Aagay Karo Safar.
