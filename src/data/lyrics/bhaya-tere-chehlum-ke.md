Ae hai zainab (2)
Bhaiya tere chalum k leye ae hain zainab (2)

1
Chadar ka kafan tere leye lae hain zainab

2
Us lash ko jis lash pe tha dhoop mai chora (2)
Us lash pe badal ki tarha chahee hai zainab

3
De mujko ijazat tere majlis karo barpa (2)
Tarsi hoi matab ki chali ae hain zainab

4
Mil jaye sukoon uth k kaleje se laga ley(2)
Maloom hai tujko bare dukh pae hai zainab

5
Ankhen tu zara khol tere pyas k sadqe (2)
Ye behan nahi hain tere maajae hai zainab

6
Jis bhai pe parne na deya ranj ka saya (2)
Be ghoro kafan dasht main wo bhai hain zainab

7
Chupne nahi dege tere bhaiya k lahu ko (2)
Wo bazmey aza tu ne jo mehkae hain zainab

8
Bhaiya tere masoom nishani wo sakina (2)
Chor ae hai zindan se nai lae hai zainab

9
Rehan kabhi nohey se ghafil na milega (2)
Abbas k parcham ki kasam khae hain zainab
