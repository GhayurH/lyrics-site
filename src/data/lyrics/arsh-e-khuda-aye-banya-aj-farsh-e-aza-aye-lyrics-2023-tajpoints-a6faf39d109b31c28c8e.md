1
Arsh e Khuda Aye Banya Aj Farsh e Aza Aye
Kyun Mariya Aye Ummat Haye Maula Raza Aye.
Aynj Miliya Maula Behna Nu Jehra Veer Kadi V Nayi Murda.
Toriya Si Jiyonda Veer Aouna Jiwein Ghar Choon Janaza Aye Turda.
Behna Taun Jiondiyaan Jee Mar Ke Hoya Juda Aye.
Na Behna Roiyaan Wich Karbal Na Aj Roi Aye Behan Koi.
Watna Taun Door Musafir Di Hondi Behan Te Kardi Wain Koi.
Zahra Diyaan Putraan Nu Kis Jurm Di Saza Aye.
Baad Tere Fair Yateem Hoiyaan Kazim Diyaan Dhiyaan Rul Jana.
Wichri Aye Sughra Waang Jehri Onay Qum Choon Mur Ke Nayi Aana.
Aj Kafan De Wich Loko Behna Da Aasra Aye.
Qatal Hoya Lal Aye Kazim Da Aye Qatal Rasool Da Aye Loko.
Mar Gaya Aye Haye Be Watan Jehra Aye Putar Batool Da Aye Loko.
Ik Zakhmi Qafila Aye Aaya Karan Layi Wida Aye.
Wasta E Oss Masooma Da Jino Veer Kadi Na Milna Aye.
Rawaan Te Wich Sehrawan De Jis Bhain Teri Ne Rulna Aye.
Na Wichar Ke Maut Aaway Najfi Di Iltija Aye.
