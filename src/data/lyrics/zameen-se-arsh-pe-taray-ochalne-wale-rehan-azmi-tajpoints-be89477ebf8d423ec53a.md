1
Zameen Se Arsh Pe Taray Ochalne Wale Rehan Azmi.
Bhanwar Se Nooh Ki Kashti Nikalne Walay.
Ahad Hai Wajid-o-Hayi-o-Wahab-o-Wajid Tu.
Tamam Bandishein Lamhon Mein Talney Walay.

2
Karam, Kareem Tera Har Taraf Karan Ta Karaan.
Aye Aab-e-Nisaan Ko Gohar Mein Dhalne Walay.
Majeed-o-Majid-o-Mabood Our Mueed Hai Tu.
Hamara Paikar-e-Khaki Sanwarne Walay.

3
Qadeer Qadir-o-Ghaffar-o-Awwal-o-Aakhir.
Nizam-e-Shams-o-Qamar Ko Sanbhalne Walay.
Mila Ke Aab Mein Khak-o-Hawa-o-Aatish Ko.
Phir Oss Khameer Ko Banda Pokarne Walay.

4
Zameen Pe Sabzay Ki Chadar Bichayi Kun Keh Kar.
Aye Farsh-e-Khak Ki Qismat Nikharne Walay.
Shikam Mein Sang Ke Deta Hai Rizq Keray Ko.
Hijar Shajar Ko Parindon Ko Palney Walay.

5
Banaya Ashraf e Makhlooq Tu Ne Aadam Ko.
Farishtay Ho Gaye Sajdah Guzarne Walay.
 Rehan Haar Gaye Oss Ke Samne Woh Bhi.
Woh Loag Jo They Kissi Se Na Harne Walay.
