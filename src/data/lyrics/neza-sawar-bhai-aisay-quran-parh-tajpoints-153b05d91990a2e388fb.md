Naiza Sawar Bhai Aisay Quran Parh.
Koi Na Dekh Paye Mujh Ko Barehna Sar.

1
Main Dekhti Rahi Hoon Teri Jang-e-Karbala.
Ab Mera Marhala Hai Aankhein Na Band Kar.
Barsat Pathron Ki Sar Nangay Bibiyaan.
Kitna Kathin Hai Bhai Sadaat Ka Safar.

2
Teri Yaad Mein Hai Roti Sakina Bazar Mein.
Behlaye Oss Ko Kaisay Jaye Behan Kidhar.
Chadar Ki Hain Sawali Zahra Ki Betiyaan.
Hota Nahi Kissi Par Faryad Ka Asar.

3
Baloon Se Munh Chupaon Ya Khak Uraon Main.
La De Kahin Se Chadar Ya Aik Kam Kar.
Sab Dekhtey Ossi Ko Hota Agar Yahan.
Deta Aazan Aisi Akbar Mera Pisar.

4
Zehmat Na Tujh Ko Deti Majboor Hai Behan.
Aye Bhai Har Shaqi Ki Na Paak Hai Nazar.
 Tauqeer Rooz-e-Mehshar Gar Chahiye Nijat.
Dil Mein Basa Le Apne Shabeer Ki Qabar.
