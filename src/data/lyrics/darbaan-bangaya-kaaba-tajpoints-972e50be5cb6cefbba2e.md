1
Yeh Ghar Ali Ka Hai Darban Ban Gaya Kaba.
Ali Ke Ishq Mein Meezan Ban Gaya Kaba.
Ali Ke Kabay Mein Aane Ko Hadsa Na Samajh.
Ali Ke Aane Se Sultan Ban Gaya Kaba.

2
Kaha Khuda Ne Mubarak Ho Tum Ko Bint-e-Asad.
Tumhare Aane Se Zeeshan Ban Gaya Kaba.
Ali Ke Aane Ka Bas Intezar Tha Iss Ko.
Ali Ke Aate Hi Quran Ban Gaya Kaba.

3
Ali Se Bhagney Walo Aye Hajiyo Socho.
Tumhare Hajj Ka Bhi Imkan Ban Gaya Kaba.
Buttoon Ko Toor Ke Kabay Ko Pak Saaf Kiya.
Qasam Ali Ki Musalman Ban Gaya Kaba.

4
Ali Ke Aane Se Iss Ghar Ko Chaar Chand Lagay.
Abu Turab Ka Ahsan Ban Gaya Kaba.
Tujhe Zamane Khabar Hi Nahi Ali Ke Sabab.
Ali Wali Ka Madah Khuwan Ban Gaya Kaba.

5
Yeh Zacha Khana Ali Ka Hai Sajdah Ga Hai Magar.
Youn Lag Raha Hai Keh Yazdan Ban Gaya Kaba.
Abu Turab Ka Aana Tu Ik Bahana Bana.
Yeh Kibriya Ki Bhi Pehchan Ban Gaya Kaba.

6
Ali Ke Aane Se Pehle Yeh Maizbaan Tha Magar.
Ali Ke Aate Hi Mehman Ban Gaya Kaba.
Ali Ali Ka Ossay Wird Bha Gaya Hai Batool.
Ali Ali Ki Tu Gardaan Ban Gaya Kaba.
