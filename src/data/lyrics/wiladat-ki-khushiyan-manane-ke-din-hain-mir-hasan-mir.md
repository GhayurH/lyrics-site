Wiladat Ki Khushiyan Manane Ke Din Hain Mir Hasan Mir
Yehi Tu Muqadar Bananey Ke Din Hain

1
Bajuz Mah-e-Shabaan Hai Har Mah Purnam
Yehi Apne Hansne Hansanay Ke Din Hain
Chalo Chal Ke Baithein Qalandar Ke Dar Par
Yeh Maston Ke Dhomi Dhamane Ke Din Hain

2
Rajab Ho Keh Shaban-o-Ramzan-o-ZillHajj
Malangon Ke Khaney Kamane Ke Din Hain
Bola Laye Koi Farazdaq Ko Ja Kar
Yehi Apne Sunney Sunanay Ke Din Hain

3
Zahoor Unn Ka Hoga Tu Khushiyaan Milein Gi
Abhi Apne Roney Rulanay Ke Din Hain
Bas Ab Katney Wali Hain Furqat Ki Ratein
Imam-e-Zamana Ke Aane Ke Din Hain
