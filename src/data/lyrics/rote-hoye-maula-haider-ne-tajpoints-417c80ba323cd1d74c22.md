1
Rotay Hoye Maula Haider Ne Zainab Ko Pas Bulaya Hai.
Sar Choma Hai Bazu Chomay Kya Yaad Ali Ko Aaya Hai.
Tum Apne Baap Ki Zeenat Ho Aye Beti Tum Ho Fakhar-e-Ali.
Iss Wastay Khaliq Ne Mujh Se Yeh Naam Tera Rakhwaya Hai.

2
Tujhe Kofa Raas Na Aaye Ga Yeh Shehar Tujhe Tarpaye Ga.
Rotay Hoye Fatah-e-Khaybar Ne Yeh Beti Se Farmaya Hai.
Iss Shehar Ki Galiyon Mein Tum Par Pathar Barsaye Jaein Gaye.
Pehchan Lo Iss Ke Rastoon Ko Bibi Yeh Dais Paraya Hai.

3
Shabeer Ko Aye Meri Zainab Main Tere Hawalay Karta Hoon.
Ab Waqt Juda Ho Jane Ka Aye Bibi Mera Aaya Hai.
Apne Bhai Ka Sath Tumhein Iss Tarha Nibhana Hai Beti.
Jis Tarha Tumhare Nana Ka Haider Ne Sath Nibhaya Hai.

4
Jab Waqt-e-Aakhir Aaya Tu Haider Ne Laga Kar Seene Se.
Aaon Ga Sham-e-Ghurbat Mein Yeh Beti Se Farmaya Hai.
Hum Mein Kuch Aisi Baat Nahi Hai Nazar-e-Karam Ka Faiz Raza.
Taeed Hai Mere Maula Ki Jis Ne Noha Likhwaya Hai.
