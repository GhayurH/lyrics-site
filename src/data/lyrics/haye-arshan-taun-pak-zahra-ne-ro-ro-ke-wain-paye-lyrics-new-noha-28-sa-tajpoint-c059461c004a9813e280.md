Haye Arshan Taun Pak Zahra Ne Ro Ro Ke Wain Paye.
Kisay Syed De Lashay Te Ummat Ne Pehli Wari Aj Teer Chalaye.

1
Hondi Jay Aj Main Jondi Zainab Nu Khud Sikhandi Har Teer Main Kadondi.
Koi Veran Das De Ohno Shabar Di Maiyyat Taun Kiwein Teer Churaye.
Ayelan Ho Riya Aye Le Jao Aye Janaza Nayi Dafan Hoan Dena.
Jithay Chup Ke Maa Dafnai Osay Ja Te Apna Bhai Shabbir Sulaye.

2
Ghazi Nu Rook Zainab Mere Babay Da Madina Veeran Ho Na Jaway.
Aj Meri Pak Dua Aye Babay Da Sabar Wakhaye Talwar Na Chaye.
Shabbir Sochda Aye Kiwein Janaza Wapis Ghar Le Ke Jaway Akbar.
Kiwein Teraan De Nal Zakhmi Maiyyat Oh Hasan Bhira Di Bhaina Nu Wakhaye.
