Fatima Da Ladla Maqtal De Pasay Tur Paya

Jism Tay Mazloom De Phateya Libaas Ay Tur Paya

Qafila Kar Ke Hawalay Abid E Beemar De

Rondiyaan Behnaan Nu Oh Denda Dilasay Tur Paya

Choor Ke Ghar Baar Mil Kay Naal Behna Betian

Ahmed E Mukhtar Nu Kar Ke Udaas Ay Tur Paya

Baad Ghazi De Kamar Kham Ho Gayee Shabbir Di

Fer Vi Maqtal Vichon Ay Chaan Lashay Tur Paya

Cha Ke Asghar Nu Hathan Te Ashkia Di Fouj Val

Zulm O Istabdad De Kholan Khulasay Tur Paya

Enj Di Aktar Ghareebi Cha Gayi Mazloom Tay

Choor Kay Wich Khamiyan Day Baal Pyasay Tur Paya
