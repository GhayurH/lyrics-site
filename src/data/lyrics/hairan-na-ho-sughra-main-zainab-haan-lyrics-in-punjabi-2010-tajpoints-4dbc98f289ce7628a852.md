Hairan Na Ho Sughra Main Zainab Haan.
Mainu Maar Dita Tere Piyo De Dardaan.

1
Hoye Wain Bhare Sughra Tera Naa Lay Ke.
Jadon Toriya Maqtal Nu Tera Veer Jawaan.
Gal Aoun-o-Muhammad Di Na Puch Maithon.
Shabir Da Ban Sadqa Ho Gaye Qurbaan.

2
Aina Ujray Gharan Andar Kadi Wasda Si.
Jaino Lutiya Wich Karbal Ral Musalmana.
Safraan Wich Rondi Rayi Raj Babal Nu.
Aayi Maut Sakina Nu Ja Wich Zindaan.

3
Be-Misal Shahadat Si Tere Asghar Di.
Jiday Qatal Utay Roya Khud Teer Kamaan.
Darbar Thayi Peshi Teri Phophiyaan Di.
Riya Sang Te Haye Parhda Shabir Quraan.

4
Kitay Safar Raku Andar Tere Abid Ne.
Beemar Di Akhiyaan Choon Riya Khon Rawaan.
Hun Hasrat Haye Bibi Ayho Adil Di.
Teri Turbat Wekhan Layi Kadi Sham Nu Jaan.
