1
Fatima Jaiyan Veraan Diyan Lashan Te Ro Wain Pandiyan.
Veerna Jay Rasiyaan Na Pandiyaan Teri Turbat Banandiyan.
Kuj Loag Sham Walay Mere Ghar Nu Jalanday Ne.
Nanay De Kalma Go Pe Sir Nangay Turanday Ne.

2
Pathraan Diyaan Barshaan Che Bhaina Tera Abid Bachandiyan.
Tere Aasrey Te Ghazi Pardais Main Wasaya.
Wajaan V Mariyaan Main Ro Ro Ke Main Bulaya.
So Gaya Darya De Kanday Ja Ke Tainu Kiwein Uthandiyan.

3
Jadon Pochiya Sitam Garaan Kehri Ali Di Dhi Aye.
Rat Pak Muhari Di Akhiyaan Choon Dul Payi Aye.
Maa Fizza Tu Dasa Fizza Di Kand De Ohlay Rayiyaan Athro Wagandiyan.
Tafseer Innama Di Waris Kisa Di Haan Main.

4
Bagi Na Aakho Loko Dhi Mustafa Di Haan Main.
Ujriyaan Har Moor Te Ro Ro Ke Rayiyaan Khutbay Sunandiyaan.
Darbar Wali Peshi Zainab Nu Mar Gayi Aye.
Nanay De Deen Taun Haye Jehri Bachray Waar Gayi Aye.

5
Be Khata Na Mehrama De Kolon Rayiyaan Jhirkaan Ne Khandiyaan.
Chan Peer De Malang Pe Akbar Da Noha Parhday.
Kul Anbiya V Matam Asghar De Naal Karday.
Aina Layi Bibi Diyaan Duawaan Arshaan Taun Aandiyaan.
