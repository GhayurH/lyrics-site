Sajjad Laee Ey Mushkil, Aey Shaam Da Safar

Akbar Bhira Da Qatil, Abid Da Hum Safar

Hoya Reha Jadon Da, Chup Chup Sajjad Rehnda

Puchay Haal Koi Ro Kay Naa Shaam Da Ey Laynda

Jere Zulm Vaikhay Abid, Duniya Nu Ki Khabar

Asghar Da Kurta Veeran, Meri Lehed Wich Rakha Day

Ehsaan Ker Kay Parda Abid Nishaan Mita Day

Tere Baad Aa Na Javay, Meri Thay Shimar

Akhay Rubab Abid, Mushkil Hay Mera Jeena

Asghar Hey Kerbala Wich Reh Shaam Gaee Sakina

Hoi Goud Meri Khali, Kis Di Lagi Nazar

Is Shaam Day Shehr Wich, Shabeer Rul Gaeeaan

Darbar Vaikh Veeran Teri Mout Bhul Gaeeaan

Beemar Mar Na Jaway, Zainab Nu Aey Fikar

Jinno Bashar Malaik, Kerday Baree Ibadat

Laiken Hai La Illa Di Duniya Di Hay Shahadat

Sajda Hussain Tera, Bayshak Azeem Thar

Chan Veer Aa Vay Mehdi, Karo Mil Kay Sub Dua Eh

Ruk Jaway Ruth Akhan Di, Abid Nu Chayn Aavay

Abaad Fir Howay, Aey Fatima Da Ghar
