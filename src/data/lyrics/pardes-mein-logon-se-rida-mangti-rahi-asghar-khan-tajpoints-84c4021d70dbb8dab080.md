1
Pardes Mein Logon Se Rida Mangti Rahi Asghar Khan.
Ujray Na Koi Aisay Dua Mangti Rahi.
Zainab Ne Kaha Logo Beemar Bara Hai.
Mujhe Maro Mera Abid Be Hosh Para Hai.
Sajjad Ke Hissay Ki Saza Mangti Rahi.
Nahi Loag Yateemo Par Haye Zulm Youn Dhatay.
Teri Ladli Ko Jaisay Marey Hain Tamachay.
Bas Jurm Tha Yeh Sar Woh Tera Mangti Rahi.
Nana Teri Ummat Ne Bara Zulm Kiya Hai.
Mazlomo Ki Lashon Par Roney Na Diya Hai.
Har Moor Pe Roney Ki Jagah Mangti Rahi.
Haye Aoun-o-Muhammad Our Akbar Ko Ganwa Kar.
Maa Zahra Ki Chadar Ko Karbal Mein Luta Kar.
Nana Main Tere Dein Ki Baqa Mangti Rahi.
 Sajjad Woh Kehti Thi Aye Kalma Go Bata.
Kya Ajar Risalat Ka Nana Ne Tha Manga.
Bazaron Mein Zainab Woh Sila Mangti Rahi.
