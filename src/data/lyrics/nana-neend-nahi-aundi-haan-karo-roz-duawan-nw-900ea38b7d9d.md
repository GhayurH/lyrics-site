Nana Neend Nahi Aundi Haan Karo Roz Duawan.

Viran Kyun Nahi Ayai Hai Ro Ro Tak Diyan Ravan.

Hain Muntazir Nighain Baithi Hun Raah Per
Deti Hun Main Sadain Baithi Hun Raah Per
Viran Kyun Nahi Ayai Hai Ro Ro Tak Diyan Ravan.

Nana Mairai Baradar Kyun Chor Ker Gayai
Beemar Ko Rula Kar Kyun Chor Ker Gayai
Viran Kyun Nahi Ayai Hai Ro Ro Tak Diyan Ravan.

Qasim Ko Yaad Kaar Kai Roti Hun Raat Din
Ankhon Main Ashk Bhar Kai Roti Hun Raat Din
Viran Kyun Nahi Ayai Hai Ro Ro Tak Diyan Ravan.

Tarsi Howi Hain Aankhai Asghar Terai Liyai
Jhula Bani Hain Baahain Asghar Terai Liyai
Viran Kyun Nahi Ayai Hai Ro Ro Tak Diyan Ravan.

Lainai Na Muj Ko Aayai Naraaz Kyun Howai
Baba Bhi Ghar Na Aayai Naraz Kyun Howai
Viran Kyun Nahi Ayai Hai Ro Ro Tak Diyan Ravan.

Lo Aun Aur Jaffar Kyun Lout-e-nahi
Nana Merai Baradar Kyun Lout-e-Nahi
Viran Kyun Nahi Ayai Hai Ro Ro Tak Diyan Ravan.

Hujron Main Sab Kai Jaaker Karti Hun Yaad Main
Akbar Ko Jaakai Dar Per Karti Hun Yaad Main
Viran Kyun Nahi Ayai Hai Ro Ro Tak Diyan Ravan.

Kisko Galain Lagaun Ab Kaun Paas Hai
Kyun Roz Daar Pai Jaun Kya Mujko Aas Hai
Viran Kyun Nahi Ayai Hai Ro Ro Tak Diyan Ravan.
