1
Kazim Pay Intahaye Aseri Guzar Gayi Syed Zill e Raza Zaidi.
Zindan Mein Jawani-o-Peri Guzar Gayi.
Kitna Ghareeb Ho Gaya Jafar Ka Ladla.
Zehar-e-Jafa Se Mara Gaya Wa Musibata.

2
Aa Kar Yeh Oss Ki Lash Pe Sajjad Ne Kaha.
Sadmay Se Hain Nidhal Ali Our Syeda.
Gham Se Hai Ghair Haal Hasan Our Hussain Ka.
Kehte Hain Ro Ke Sare Aseeran e Karbala.

3
Zahra Jinna Se Lash Pe Roney Ko Aa Gayin.
Lashay Ko Dekha Our Yeh Noha Suna Gayin.
Iss Ke Badan Ka Gausht Tu Zanjeerein Kha Gayin.
Yeh Keh Ke Iss Ki Lash Pe Rotay Hain Mustafa.

4
Hathkariyaan Tauq-o-Beriyaan Hotin Nahi Juda.
Mar Kar Bhi Iss Ka Sara Badan Qaid Mein Raha.
Kaisay Biyaan Karon Sukhan-e-Dilkharash Ko.
Pul Par Rakha Gaya Hai Tan-e-Pash Pash Ko.

5
Koi Nahi Uthata Hai Qaidi Ki Lash Ko.
 Saqlain Taziyat Hain Yeh Jumlay Imam Se.
Majlis Mein Hashar Barpa Hai Mere Kalam Se.
Aawaz Aik Bachi Ki Aati Hai Sham Se.
