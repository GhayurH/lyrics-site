Hai Shahadat Askari Ki Ro Raha Hai Yeh Jahan.
Soag Hai Ahl-e-Azza Mein Ro Raha Hai Aasmaan.

1
Chak Kar Ke Tum Giraybaan, Dam Ba Dam Matam Karo.
Khak Ura Kar Sar Ko Peeto, Apni Aankhein Nam Karo.
Zulm Unn Par Jo Hoye Hain Woh Karein Gay Sab Biyaan.

2
Jis Ne Tarveej e Sadaqat Mein Guzari Zindagi.
Jis Ki Seerat Se Ayyaan Thi, Seerat-e-Paygambari.
Jo Tha Quran-e-Muhammad Ka Haqeeqi Tarjumaan.

3
Qaid Khane Ki Woh Sakhti, Jis Mein Guzari Zindagi.
Phir Bhi Logo Kar Rahe They, Kaisay Sab Ki Rehbari.
Seh Rahe They Kis Ki Khatir Qaid Mein Bhi Sakhtiyaan.

4
Dast-e-Matam Se Zamane Ko Khabar Do Noha Gar.
Ho Gaya Rukhsat Jahan Se Aaj Mehdi Ka Padar.
Jo Tha Mohkim Wasta Banday Khuda Ke Darmiyaan.

5
Askari Zulm-o-Sitam Sehtay Rahe Kehte Rahe.
Askari Paigham-e-Haq Detey Rahe Kehte Rahe.
Rah-e-Haq Mehdi Qasam Lo Hum Karein Gay Sab Biyaan.

6
Jab Gaya Dunya Se Logo Gayarwaan Woh Be Niyaz.
Oss Ke Betay Ne Parhayi Thi Janazay Ki Namaz.
Noha Gar Thin Ghar Ke Andar Sab Ki Sab Woh Bibiyaan.

7
Tera Matam Tera Noha Zindagi Ke Sath Hai.
Anjuman e Sajjadia Our Zaidi Tere Sath Hai.
Matami Giriya Kuna Hain Soag Hai Soye Jinna.
