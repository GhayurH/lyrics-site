1
Shah-e-Deen Ka Hosla Abbas Hai Sarwar Raza Karbalai.
Bibiyon Ka Aasra Abbas Hai.
Zair-e-Parcham Aa Ke Mangi Jab Dua.
Sun Li Mere Ghazi Ne Meri Sada.
Yani Har Gham Ki Dawa Abbas Hai.
Gir Gaya Darya Pe Jab Shair-e-Jarri.
Chin Gayi Zainab Ke Sar Ki Orhni.
Yani Zainab Ki Rida Abbas Hai.
Mere Aaqa Aap Se Sharminda Hoon.
Pyasay Bachay Reh Gaye Main Kya Karon.
Itna Keh Kar Ro Diya Abbas Hai.
Toor Kar Koza Sakina Ne Kaha.
Pani Laney Wala Koi Na Raha.
Pyasi Hoon Main Mar Chuka Abbas Hai.
Thokarein Khatay They Maqtal Mein Hussain.
Aik Ik Lashay Pe Kartay They Bain.
Bool Kya Tu Hi Mera Abbas Hai.
Jis Ghari Mashk-e-Sakina Chid Gayi.
Gir Gaya Ghorey Se Sar Ke Bal Jarri.
Phir Zameen Se Na Utha Abbas Hai.
Chomtay Jate Hain Bazu Bhai Ke.
Shah-e-Dein Akbar Se Yeh Kehne Lagay.
Chor Kar Mujh Ko Chala Abbas Hai.
Bibiyon Ka Jis Ghari Tha Sar Khula.
Dekhta Kis Tarha Ghairat Ka Khuda.
Nook-e-Naiza Se Gira Abbas Hai.
Qabar Mein Bola Malak Asif Bata.
Mutmain Hai Tu Yahan Kitna Raza.
Main Yeh Bola Aasra Abbas Hai.
