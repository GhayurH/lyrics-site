PAR NAD-E-ALI ZAINAB AKHAY MERI KAR BABA IMDAAD AY

MAIN TALIYAN MAALDI REHGAIYAN HAYE BOLDA NAI SAJJAD AY

ESS WASTAY KAMAR JHUKA TURDA MERA ISTAQBAL NAI SEH SAKEYA,

JISS VAYLAY DI CHADAR UTRI AY KADAM KADAM TAY KATAL HOYA,

TERI NASAL DA WARIS GIRGAYA AY INNU AKHAY HOSH KARADAY

SUN HOKAY BABA UJRI DAY KHAIBAR NU CHANA SOKHA AY,

ZAINAB DAY NAL SHARABI DAY DARBAR CH JAANA OKHA AY,

HARR ZULAM NU JAYRA SEHGAYA AY BASS ‘O’ MAZLOOM SAJJAD AY

NAI LASH HUSSAIN TAY ROUWN DITTA,

MAIN FER VI BABA CHUP RAIYAN,

KAYI BAAR BACHAYA ABID NU, KHUD AAAP MAIN SAINDI DHUK RAIYAN,

BAZAR ABOOR MAIN KAR AIYAN DARBAR ABOOR KARADEY

KARBAL TOUN LEHKAY SHAAM TAYEIN, JAYRI ROUNDI RAYI DIN RAINDAY AYE,

KEETA HAMLA FAAYR YAZEEDIYAN NAY, NAI QABAR DAY WICH VI CHAIN AY,

TU JAG DA WARIS HAY MOLA ZAINAB DA HARAM BACHADAY

TERAY PUTAR ABBAS DI CHUP BABA, MENU SHAAM DAY WICH LEH AYI AY,

FARIYAD HASSAN AY KARDI SI, SIR NANGAY ZAHRA JAYI AY,

IK CHADAR NAJAF CHOU LEH-AA TU UJRI DAY SIRR TAY PADAY

METHO PUCH BABA SIRR GHAZI DA, KYON NAYZEY TAY NAI ANDA AY,

SIRR NANGA VEKH KAY ZAINAB DA ABBAS DA SIR GIR JANDA AY,

BARDASH NA GHAZI KARSAKEYA KHULEY VAALAY DHEE ZAHRA DAY
