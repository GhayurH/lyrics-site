1
Ashab Tere Baba Mere Wair Paye Gaye Ne
Teri Dhi Nu Mariya Aye Darbariyaan Bola Ke Haq Kho Ke Leh Gaye Ne.
Putraan De Samne Si Jhutlanday Ray Mainu.
Peshi Taan Muk Gayi Par Peshi De Fatima Te Aasaar Reh Gaye Ne.

2
Hasrat Si Mere Dil Di Mimber Tere Te Baba.
Tere Baad Wekhdi Main Ik Waar Ya Ali Nu Ayeh Aap Beh Gaye Ne.
Rona Jaye Nayi Tu Chadna Chad De Madina Bibi.
Ghar Nu Jalan Walay Bait-e-Huzan Jala Ke Mainu Ayeh Keh Gaye Ne.

3
Hondi Makkay Che Jaye Main Kabay Nu Ja Ke Kaindi.
Baitullah Tere Haji Apne Rasool Diyaan Nazraan Choon Leh Gaye Ne.
Maa Te Jiwein Si Barsay Wekhay Ne Taziyane.
ALLAH Jane Samar Kiwein Zahra De Lal Dowein Ais Gham Nu Seh Gaye Ne.
