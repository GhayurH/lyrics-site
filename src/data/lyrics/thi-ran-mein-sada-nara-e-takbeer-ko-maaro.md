Thi Ran Mein Sada Nara e Takbeer Ko Maaro
Sajdah Na Ada Karne Do Shabeer Ko Maro
Khaimon Mein Sada Deti Thi Yeh Zahra e Sani
Rehne Do Marey Pas Muhammad Ki Nishani
Barchi Na Marey Nana Ki Tasveer Ko Maro
Shabeer Yeh Kehte Rahe Aye Sham Ke Logo
Tum Marne Hi Aaye Ho Tu Mar Lo Mujh Ko
Par Teer Na Youn Asghar-e-Be Sheer Ko Maro
Sab Laut Lo Karti Thi Manadi Yehi Ummat
Khaimon Mein Yateemo Ke Bachay Kuch Na Salamat
Quran Karo Pamal Tu Tafseer Ko Maro
Kehta Tha Laeen Lashay Se Sar Kaat Ke Lao
Beti Koi Rokay Tu Tamachon Se Hatao
Humsheer Agar Rokay Tu Humsheer Ko Maro
Tha Lashkar-e-Ashrar Ka AKBAR Yeh Iradah
Baqi Na Rahe Koi Bhi Shabeer Ka Beta
Jageer Hi Mein Sahib-e-Jageer Ko Maro
