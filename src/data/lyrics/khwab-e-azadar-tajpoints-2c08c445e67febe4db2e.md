1
Khwab e Azadar.
Jee Raha Hai Aik Hi Hasrat Mein Sheda-e-Hussain.
Khwab Hai Mera Keh Mere Khwab Mein Aaye Hussain.
Mera Dill Bhi Karbala Wale Teri Jageer Ho.

2
Tu Jo Aa Jaye Tu Mujh Ko Khuwab Hi Tabeer Ho.
Zindagi Iss Khawab Ke Doraan Guzar Jaye Hussain.
Aamad-e-Maula Ki Khushiyaan Aisi Hoon Dam Toor Doon.
Main Habib Ibn-e- Mazahir Ki Tarha Chat Se Giron.

3
Qum Ba-Izni Lash Par Meri Bhi Duhraye Hussain.
Sabz Rangat Ka Ujala Khwab Mein Ho Chaar So.
Samne Ho Kibriyai Noor Sa Ik Khubaro.
Main Tera Mula Hoon Mujh Se Aa Ke Farmaye Hussain.

4
Main Yeh Pochon Man Tasha Kya Our Izzat Kon Hai.
Man Tasha Main Hoon Our Bolein Our Izzat Jaun Hai.
Na Samaj Ko Iss Tarha Quran Samjhaye Hussain.
Main Yeh Pochon Waqiya Meraj Ka Kaise Howa.

5
Muskura Dein Pehle Tu Woh Dekh Kar Chehra Mera.
Our Phir Abbas Ke Nazdeek Aa Jaye Hussain.
Khawab Se Jagon Tu Main Arsh-e-Alla Tak Dekh Loon.
Fakhar Se Fitrus Ki Sorat Mein Bhi Mam Misli Kahon.

6
Jate Jate Hath Mas Aankhon Pe Kar Jaye Hussain.
Dekh Kar Roye Munawar Zikar Youn Akbar Karon.
Ya Kareemo Ya Raheemo Wird Main Karta Rahon.
Yani Ye Asma-e-Hasna Bhi Hain Asma-e-Hussain.
