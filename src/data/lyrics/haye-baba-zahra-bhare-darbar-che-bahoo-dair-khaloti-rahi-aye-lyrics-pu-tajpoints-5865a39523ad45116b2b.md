1
Haye Baba Zahra Bhare Darbar Che Bahoo Dair Khaloti Rahi Aye.
Teri Tehreer De Lashe De Tukre Chunridi Rahi Aye.
Tere Bajon Baba Zulm Di Had Muk Gayi.
Teri Umat Mere Haider Tu Bait Mangdi Rahi Aye.

2
Jalda Darwaza Jadon Fatima Te Aa Giraya.
Pak Bibi Fair Haider Nu Sad Ke Rondi Rahi Aye.
Bhare Dabar De Wich Yaad Onno Sham Ayi.
Fair Shabir De Har Pase Qatal Girndi Rahi Aye.

3
Tera Farman V Main Ayna Nu Sunandi Rayi.
Rondi Rayi Aan Main Par Teri Ummat Hasdi Rahi Aye.
Tere Ashab Menu Jirhkiyaan V Denday Re.
Main Aan Dhi Pak Mumammad Di Ayho Kendi Rahi Aye.

4
Bhare Darbar Da Jad Wekh Ke Mahool Ayi.
Dhi Nu Apne Gal Nal La Ke Hoke Bhardi Rahi Aye.
Tenu Hazyaan Jaina Lokaan Ne Sunaya Si.
Fatima Onna Hi Lokaan Wich Khutbe Parhdi Rahi Aye.

5
Dein Islam Shariyat Hai Bachayi Iss ne.
Jeri Bibi Apne Ghar Nu Sarda Takdi Rahi Aye.
Howay Razi Bib Ayho Dua Qambar Di.
Jeri Har Matami Da Aa Ke Pursa Laindi Rahi Aye.
