Besheer ka lasha

1
(Lashe to shah-e-dee(n) ne uthaaye hai bahattar)
(Lekin kisi lashe pe nahi dekha yei manzar)
Kis ghurbat-o-mazloomi se maula nei uthayaa

2
(Wo laash liye sath dafa aagae baray hei)
(Phir soch kei kuch rotey huwe peechey hate hei)
Hasrat se bohot dekh rahey hei shah-e-walaa

3
(Ay Shah-e-Najaf aap se faryad hai maula)
(Aasan thaa wo haatho se khayber ka uthanna)
Mushkil hai magar baap kaa haatho se uthanaa

4
(Ley jaawu jigar thaam ke is laal ko aake)
(Awaz thi phir madare-e-asghar ko ye jake)
Ye kehtay huwe maula ne phir agay barayaa

5
(Ik jumla bari aas se farmaya hei maa ne)
(Kya zibha kiye jaate hei tujh jaise bhi bachey)
Jub madarey asghar nei nigaaho(n) se hei dekha

6
(Phir soch lehed khod ke dafanadu yei mayyat)
(Ik nanni si mayyat pe na ayye ye qayamat)
Pamaal na ho goro(n) se ye phool ke jaisa

7
(Phir maula nei talwaar se hei kabrei banayi)
(Aur nani si mayyat ussi turbat mei chuppayi)
Phir rote hai jab kabre mei dafana chukei maula

8
(Jub ho chuki hei sheh kei saro tan me judaayi)
(Maqtal mei phir ik baar qayamat hei ye aayi)
Hai dhund ne ko eik shaki dasht mei ayaa

9
(Wo dhundta phirta hai liye haath mei khanjar)
(Shayad mujhe miljaae kahi mayyat-e-asghar)
Bas eik dafa usne jo maqtal mei hei payaa

10
(Khanjar ko rakha nani se halkoom ke uppar)
(Bedardi se phir kaat liya nana sa wo sar)
Aur chordiya khaak pe wo phool ke jaisa

11
("Mesum-o-Zafar" chahha bohut shah-e-zaman ne)
(Bach jaye mera laal hi pamaali ki zad se)
Paamali se afsoos magar bach nahi paya
