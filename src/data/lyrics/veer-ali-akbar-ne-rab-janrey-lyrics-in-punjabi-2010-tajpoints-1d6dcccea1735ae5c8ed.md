Veer Ali Akbar Ne Rab Janrey Keray Dais Wasaye Ne.
Qasad V Ghaliya Nana Main Dardaan Mari Ne Kayi Khat V Paye Ne.

1
Keeti Bismillah Tu Aynj Khat Di Main Ibtida Haye Likh Chadiya Inna Lillah.
Andaz Ghareebi Wali Tehreeraan De Mainu Babay Ne Sikhaye Ne.
Bhawein Chan Veer De Naa Likhna Si Khat Mainu Main Salam Kita Pehle Piyo Nu.
Aye Soch Na Le Kitay Baba Russi Hoi Sughra Ne Aadab Bhulaye Ne.

2
Fair Shagna Diyaan Aynj Likhiyaan Ne Hasrataan Khali Chad Ke Mehndi Di Thaan.
Sehra Te Gana Likh Ke Main Aglay Lafzaan Te Athro Barsaye Ne.
Lori Asghar De Layi Likh Ke Main Bheji Aye Ayeh V Likhiya Aye Main Naal Odhay.
Bhaijan Taun Pehlaan Lori De Lafz Main Asghar De Jholay Che Jholaye Ne.

3
Aas Aawan Di Teri Tut Gayi Jaye Veerna Aye Sawaan V Tut Jan Gayiyaan.
Aye Likh Ke Main Aakhir Te Akbar Nu Dadi De Barey Wastay Paye Ne.
Ayho Saqlain Karey Bibi Guzarshaan Ik Waar Kadi Dedar Karan.
Wekhaan Main Akhiyaan De Chal Tere Veraan Ne Wakhrey Jehray Dais Wasaye Ne.
