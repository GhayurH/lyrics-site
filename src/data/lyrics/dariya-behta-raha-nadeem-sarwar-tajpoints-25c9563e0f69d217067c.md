Dariya Behta Raha Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics
Aye Waye Nehar-e-Alqama, Darya Behta Raha.

1
Samne Ossi Darya Ke Pyasay Taraptay Hain.
Bana Jin Ki Khatir Pani Unhi Ko Mila Na.
Unhi Se Tu Seekha Iss Ne Zameen Par Barasna.
Sitam Hai Dikha Kar Inn Ko Hai Pani Bahana Haye.

2
Saweray Issi Darya Pe Alam Wala Aaya.
Kissi Tashna Lab Bachi Ka Woh Mashkeza Laya.
Chidi Mashk Oss Pyasay Ki Woh Wapis Gaya Na Haye.
Woh Jis Ki Azaan Gonji Thi Saweray Saweray.

3
Charha Din Tu Baba Oss Ka Ossi Ko Sanbhalay.
Pukara Hai Khak Ab Dunya Tere Baad Beta Haye.
Udhar Be Kafan Tha Bhai Behan Be Rida Thi.
Jalati Hoi Khaimon Ko Ajab Sham Aayi.

4
Koi Maa Pukari Baba Luta Mera Kunba Haye.
Ameer-e-Arab Ka Beta Libas-e-Ghareebi.
 Rehan Our Sarwar Hum Na Yeh Bholay Kabhi Bhi.
Woh Darya Ka Behta Pani Woh Pyasay Ka Sajdah Haye.
