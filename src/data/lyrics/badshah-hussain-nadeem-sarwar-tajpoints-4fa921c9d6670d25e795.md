Badshah Hussain Nadeem Sarwar Lyrics

1
Badshah Hussain Hussain, Badshah Hussain.
Yeh Deen Panahi Ka Sharf Kis Ko Mila Hai.
Yeh Kis Ka Laqab Hai Jo Sar-e-Arsh Likha Hai.
Ajmer Ke Khuwaja Ne Yeh Sach Khoob Kaha Hai.
La Raib Keh Shabeer Hi Kalmay Ki Baqa Hai.
Shah Asat Hussain, Badshah Asat Hussain.

2
Deen Asat Hussain, Dein Panah Asat Hussain.
Sar Dad Na Daad, Dast Dar Dast-e-Yazid.
Haqqa Keh Bina LaIlaha Asat Hussain.
Karb-o-Bala Ishq-e-Khuda Ishq Ka Tu Sultan Hussain.
Maula Bara Kam Kiya Deen-e-Khuda Aam Kiya.
Kar Na Saka Koi Kabhi Tu Ne Wohi Kam Kiya.

3
Wadah Wafa Kar Ke Raha Sar Na Jhuka Haq Pe Jiya.
Sab Se Bara Mera Khuda Karta Raha Ayelan Hussain.
Ishq Tera Ishq-e-Khuda Nook-e-Sina Tak Jo Gaya.
Ishq Mein Jo Tu Ne Diya Koi Bhi Woh De Na Saka.
Noor-e-Khuda Dast-e-Khuda Tere Siwa Kaun Howa.
Kehna Para Likhna Para Hai Yeh Tera Ahsan Hussain.

4
Sarwar-e-Zeeshan Hussain Azmat-e-Insan Hussain.
Bolta Quran Hussain Main Tere Qurban Hussain.
Meer-e-Haram Rab Ki Qasam Tera Yeh Gham Hoga Na Kam.
Farsh-e-Azza Haq Ki Sada Hai Yeh Tera Faizan Hussain.
Azm-e-Jawaan Tashna Labaan Be Watna Be Kafna.
Tere Liye Hai Yeh Fogaan Arsh-o-Zameen Noha Kuna.

5
Dard-e-Dilaan Tashna Dahaan Khushk Zabaan Nook-e-Sina.
Tan Se Juda Sar Tha Magar Parhta Raha Quran Hussain.
Har Ja Hai Hakomat Teri Har Dil Mein Basa Hai.
Har Dil Mein Azza Khana Tera Aaj Saja Hai.
Quran-e-Musayab Mein Tera Naam Likha Hai.
Matam Ki Sada Hai Keh Yeh Tu Bool Raha Hai.

6
Ishq-e-Khuda Teri Atta Naam Tera Zindah Raha.
Karb-o-Bala Ki Hai Sada Tu Hai Bara Insan Hussain.
Tu Zindah Azadari Teri Zindah Rahe Gi.
Woh Qaum Jo Bedar Hai Gham Tera Karey Gi.
Aawaz-e-Azaan Jo Tere Rozay Se Uthay Gi.
Wallah Khudai Teri Aawaz Sunay Gi.

7
Shah-e-Zaman Hadi-e-Man Naz-e-Chaman Shereen Sukhan.
Shan-e-Nabi Shan-e-Ali Yani Meri Jan Hussain.
Janat-e-Man Karb-o-Bala Rahat-e-Man Karb-o-Bala.
:SARWAR-o-REHAN-E-: Azza Dolat-e-Man Karb-o-Bala.
Hum Hain Safeeran-e-Azza Shah-e-Huda Tum Pe Sada.
Jan Karein Tum Pe Fida Hai Yeh Mera Arman Hussain.
