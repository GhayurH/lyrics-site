Lajpal Hussain Shahid Baltistani Lyrics
Mera Maula Maula.

1
Mera Maula Hussain Aye.
Mera Murshid Hussain Aye.
Nabi Da Lal Hussain Aye.
Mera Lajpal Hussain Aye.
Zahra Di Jan Aye.
Ali Da Maan Aye.
Khaliq Da Ladla.
Mushkil De Welay Main Ghabrawan Kyun Kar Wawela Kyun Main Karan.
Be Fikar Mastaan Malangaan De Wangoon Aye Wird Karda Rawaan.
Mushkil Kushai Aye Kam Jis Sakhi Da Malik Aye Ayho Mera.
Taqdeer De Mojzay Sare Le Ja Ik Wari Hur Ban Ke Aa.
Sehra Di Miti Nu Jholi Che Pa Ke Janat De Wehray Bana.
Behlool Ban Ke Tu Karb-o-Bala Da Galiyaan Che Narah Laga.
Sajdah Dar-e-Syeda Par Kiya Tu Tabdeel Qismat Hoi.
Shabeer Bolay Keh Rahib Mubarak Ja Saat Beton Ki Di.
Tehreer Qismat Ki Tabdeel Hoti Dekhi Tu Kehta Gaya.
Har Waqt Qayam Rahe Tere Sar Pe Maula Ka Dast-e-Karam.
Apne Makaan Ko Tu Ghar Mein Badal Le Chat Pe Saja Ke Alam.
Darwazay Pe Likh De Keh Iss Makaan Ki Hai Malika Syeda.
Jibreel De Naal Rizwaan Hazaran Aaye Mubarak Layi.
Jholay Choon Kitay Inayat Naway Par Fitrus Nu Jis Dam Sakhi.
Zahra De Wehray Taun Arsh-e-Olla Taein Aandi Si Bas Ik Sada.
Charan Katabaan Nu Sah Sukh Da Aaya Zainab Di Taqreer Te.
 Akbar Bacha Li Jo Nabiyaan Di Mehnat Sajday Che Shabeer Ne.
Sar Rakh Ke Sajday Che Karbal De Pasay Kehnday Ray Anbiya.
Har Rang-e-Bandagi Ka Hawala Hussain Hai.
Kabay Ke Har Diye Ka Ujala Hussain Hai.
Sab Naimaton Ka Khaliq-o-Malik Tu Hai Khuda.
Lekin Jahan Mein Bantne Wala Hussain Hai.
