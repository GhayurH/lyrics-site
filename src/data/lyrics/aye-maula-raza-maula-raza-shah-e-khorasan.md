Aye Maula Raza Maula Raza Shah e Khorasan
Ya Imam-e-Raza
Aye Ghareeb Ul-Gurba Aye Moeen Uz Zoafa
Tujh Pe Konain Fida Aye Ali Mosa Raza
Aye Ali Ali Ali Mosa Raza Jan
Aye Maula Raza Maula Raza Shah-e-Khurasan
Yeh Jan Hai Kya Dono Jahan Aap Pe Qurban
Jab Koi Thikana Nahi Milta Hai Jahan Mein
Aa Jate Hain Woh Aap Ke Daman-e-Amaan Mein
Haiwan Ho Chahe Woh Parinday Ho Ya Insan
Nobat Yehi Kehti Hai Keh Aane Ko Hai Nobat
Hone Ko Hai Qayam Tere Qayam Ki Hakomat
Mashhad Mein Yehi Sham-o-Sahar Hota Hai Ayelan
Aye Mulk-e-Sakhawat Ke Shehanshah-e-Sakhawat
Main Ne Tere Darbar Se Mangi Hai Wazarat
Ho Mujh Ko Atta Midhat-e-Haider Ka Qalamdan
Kya Shan Hai Wallah Muhammad Ke Wali Ki
Masroof Mila Mujh Ko Woh Midhat Mein Ali Ki
Haan Main Ne Uthaya Tere Rozay Pe Jo Quran
Sab Ne Jo Khurasan Ke Rastay Mein Suna Hai
Tu Ne Tere Baba Ne Tere Jad Ne Kaha Hai
Hum Sab Ke Sabab Kalma-e-Tauheed Mein Hai Jan
Behlool Ki Maroof Ki Buzar Ki Qasam Hai
Malik Tujhey Bas Malik-e-Ashtar Ki Qasam Hai
Kun Keh Ke Bana De Mujhe Salman Ka Salman
De Midhat-e-Ajdad Ka Aaqa Youn Mujhe Phal
Jis Tarha Se Kehlata Hai Shayar Tera DEBAL
Kehlane Lagay MIR Bhi Youn Tera Sana Khuwan
