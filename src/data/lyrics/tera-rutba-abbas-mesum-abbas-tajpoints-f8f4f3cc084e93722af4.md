Iss Se Zahir Hai Jahan Par Tera Rutba Abbas.
Khud Ali Ne Bhi Kayi Bar Kaha Ya Abbas.

1
Jab Nabi Ne Kaha Hasnain Mere Betay Hain.
Fatima Zahra Pukarein Mera Beta Abbas.
Pocha Abbas Se Jab Unn Ka Pasandida Laqab.
Muskura Kar Kaha Saqa-e-Sakina, Abbas.

2
Aap Ki Qabar Ke Gird Ab Bhi Hai Masroof-e-Tawaf.
Aap Ke Qabzey Mein Hai Aaj Bhi Darya Abbas.
Lagta Hai Sath Nikal Aaye Hain Chand Our Soraj.
Dekh Kar Aap Ka Our Shah Ka Roza Abbas.

3
Hath Walon Ne Bhi Utna Nahi Taqseem Kiya.
Aap Ne Jitna Katay Hathoon Se Banta Abbas.
Tujh Ko Jurat Pe Mila Sab Se Bara Yeh Inam.
Ban Gayi Mashk-e-Sakina Tera Tamga Abbas.

4
Loag Toheed-e-Ali Mein Na Karein Shirk Kahin.
Dhanp Letey They Yehi Soch Ke Chehra Abbas.
Ban Gaye Hazrat-e-Abbas Ke Mesum, Saqlain.
Daar Par Jake Bhi Hum Bolein Gay Maula Abbas.
