1
Saja Howa Hai Tamam Aalam Hussain Tashreef La Rahe Hain.
Najaf Ke Wali Bhi Shadmaan Hain Rasool Khushiyaan Mana Rahe Hain.
Nabi Ke GHar MEin Woh Aa Raha Hai Hussain Jis Ka Hai Naam-e-Nami.
Malaika Ne Safein Bana Kar Rasool-e-Barhaq Ko Di Salami.
Falak Se Tare Utar Utar Kar Zameen Par Jagmaga Rahe Hain.
Chamak Utha Hai Rukh-e-Payamber Damak Raha Hai Ali Ka Chehra.
Zameen Pe Hai Aayton Ka Pehra Lo Do Jahan Mein Howa Sawera.
Ubhar Raha Hai Atta Ka Soraj Andherey Ko Chupa Rahe Hain.
Keh Ab Kissi Ko Pisar Milein Gaye Kissi Ko Lal-o-Gohar Milein Gaye.
Habib Ko Zindagi Mile Gi Keh Aaj Fitrus Ko Par Milein Gaye.
Dua Ka Waqt-e-Qaboliyaat Hai Khabar Yeh Jibreel La Rahe Hain.
Bawaqt-e-Mushkil Khuda Ke Deen Ko Hayat Sibt-e-Rasool Dein Gaye.
Hussain Woh Hai Keh Jiss Ki Khatir Rasool Sajday Ko Tool Dein Gaye.
Nabi Ki Zulfoon Se Khailne Ke Zamane Nazdeek Aa Rahe Hain.
Zahoor-e-Waqt-e-Imam Hai Yeh Jahan Ki Tanzeem Ho Rahi Hai.
Keh Dast-e-Qudrat Hai Har Bashar Ko Nijaat Taqseem Ho Rahi Hai.
Nuseriyon Ke Khuda Ke Dar Se Farishte Khairat Pa Rahe Hain.
Ossi Ka Sadqa Yeh Chand Tare Nisar Oss Par Yeh Maah-o-Anjum.
Hasan Ke Dill Ka Qarar Hai Woh Hai Panjtan Ka Woh Noor Panjum.
Batool Deti Hain Jiss Ko Lori Rasool Jhola Jhula Rahe Hain.
Rah-e-Wafa Par Mile Shahdat Tu Oss Ko Hans Kar Qabool Karna.
 Wajhi Ikhlas Our Mawadat Se Zikar-e-Aal-e-Rasool Karna.
 Muhib Wohi Bas Hussain Ke Hain Jo Loag Mehway-e-Sana Rahe Hain.
