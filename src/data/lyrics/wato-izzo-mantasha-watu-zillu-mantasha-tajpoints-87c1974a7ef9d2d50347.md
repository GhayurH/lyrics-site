1
Wato Izzo Mantasha Watu Zillu Mantasha.
Aye Khuda-e-Panjtan Aye Khuda-e-Do Jahan.
Yeh Zameen Ki Wosatein Yeh Wiqar-e-Aasmaan.
Tere Faiz Se Gayi Yeh Nazar Jahan Jahan.
Teri Kainat Mein Thi Buland Yeh Sada.
Woh Aisa Meharban Keh Jis Ki Nahi Misal.
Had Se Zyda Rakhta Hai Makhloq Ka Khiyal.
Chahe Jisay Aroj Day Chahe Jisay Zawal.
Oss Ki Issi Adda Pe Tu Kehte Hain Anbiya.
Izat Mili Yeh Khak Ko Adam Bana Diya.
Phir Ilm Our Kamal Se Oss Ko Saja Diya.
Sajday Ka Pehle Oss Ko Saleeqa Sikha Diya.
Our Phir Ossi Ko Laiq-e-Sajda Bana Diya.
Kashti Ko Jis Ne Nooh Ki Sahil Atta Kiya.
Mosa Ko Baam-e-Toor Pe Jalwa Dikha Diya.
Murday Jilaye Essa Ko Yeh Mojza Diya.
Dast-e-Khalil Se Bana Kaba Tu Yeh Khula.
Yeh Wasf Khas Sirf Muhammad Ko Hai Mila.
Mushtaq-e-Deed-e-Abad Ka Mabood Ho Gaya.
Hukam-e-Khuda Se Waqt Ko Tehra Diya Gaya.
Mairaj Par Sunni Gayi Manoos Ik Sada.
Tarekh Mein Ajeeb Hai Hijrat Ka Waqiya.
Taig-o-Sina Ki Zad Pe Tha Mehbob-e-Kibriya.
Woh Waqt Mustafa Ki Hifazat Ka Waqt Tha.
Yeh Keh Ke Kon Bistar-e-Ahmad Pe So Gaya.
Har Aik Ke Naseeb Mein Deen Ka Alam Kahan.
Pehle Ali Sa Shir Janam De Tu Koi Maa.
Abbas Jaisi Chahiein Iss Ko Kalaiyaan.
Aati Hai Iss Alam Ke Phareray Se Yeh Sada.
Kirdar Aik Hur Ka Bhi Hai Kainat Mein.
Jannat Khareedi Jis Ne Faqat Aik Raat Mein.
Dozakh Ki Aag Bethi Rahi Oss KI Ghaat Mein.
Bola Woh Sar Pe Bandh Ke Romal-e-Fatima.
Aatish Kaday Ko Chor Keh Salman Chal Diya.
Rehmat Khuda Ki Sath Thi Rasta Bhi Mill Gaya.
Ahmad Ne Our Ali Ne Issay Bhai Keh Diya.
Izat Mili Tu Fakhar-e-Suleman Ban Gaya.
Teri Naimaton Mein Aye Khuda Koi Kami Na Ho.
Muhtaj Momiineen Mein Ya Rab Koi Na Ho.
Fuqdan-e-Ilm Bachoon Mein Mere Kabhi Na Ho.
Kar Sarfaraz Mujh Ko Mere Bakht Ko Jaga.
Ya Rab Ba-Haq-e-Fatima Zahra Hai Yeh Dua.
Hai Sarwar-O- Rehan Ki Tujh Se Yeh Iltija.
Jab Tak Jura Howa Hai Yeh Sansoon Ka Silsila.
Zahra Ke Khandan Ki Karte Rahein Sana.
