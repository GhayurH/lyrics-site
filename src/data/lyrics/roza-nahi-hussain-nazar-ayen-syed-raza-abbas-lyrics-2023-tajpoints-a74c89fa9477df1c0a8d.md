1
Roza Nahi Hussain Nazar Ayen Syed Raza Abbas Zaidi
Aye Kash Ab Ki Bar Main Jab Jaon Karbala.
Roza Nahi Hussain Nazar Aaein Samne.
Aankhein Nahi Hoon Phir Bhi Nazar Aaye Rasta.
Main De Raha Hoon Aap Ko Jabir Ka Wasta.
Itna Boland Kijiye Maiyar Ishq Ka.
Jis Ko Hussain Mil Gaye Sab Mil Gaya Ossay.
Ab Our Kya Main Mangoon Ga Gunbad Ko Dekh Ke.
Pehli Nazar Mein Ho Gi Labon Par Yehi Dua.
Palkein Jhuka Ke Jab Rakhon Darbar Mein Qadam.
Ho Jaye Meri Aankhon Pe Abbas Ka Karam.
Nazrein Main Jab Uthaon Tu Dekhon Yeh Mojza.
Pori Ho Kaisay Maula Ziyarat Ki Aarzu.
Jab Tak Na Aap Se Ho Mulaqaat Robaro.
Tab Ja Ke Ho Ga Meri Ziyarat Ka Haq Ada.
Qabil Nahi Hoon Aap Ke Haan Manta Hoon Main.
Par Aap Tu Kareem Hain Yeh Janta Hoon Main.
Ho Jaye Iss Ghulam Ko Yeh Marifat Atta.
Pohancho Haram Mein Jab Kaf-e-Abbas Dekh Kar.
Chehray Ke Bal Gira Doon Main Khud Ko Zameen Par.
Ghazi Ka Pursa Lijiye Jab Doon Main Yeh Sada.
Gham Ka Zaeef Chehray Pe Ahsas Dekh Loon.
Akbar Ki Qabar Dekh Ke Maula Se Jab Kahon.
Kaisay Uthaya Aap Ne Lasha Jawan Ka.
Milna Hai Hum Ko Fatima Zahra Ke Chain Se.
Jo Dil Mein Aarzu Thi Woh Keh Di Hussain Se.
Iss Aas Par Hi Jaein Gay Zeeshan Our Raza.
