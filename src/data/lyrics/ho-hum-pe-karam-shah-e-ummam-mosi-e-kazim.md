1
Rakh Lijiye Hum Sab Ka Bharam Mosi-e-Kazim
Hai Tum Ko Muhammad Ki Qasam Mosi-e-Kazim
Khaliq Ne Banaya Hai Tumhein Qazi-e-Hajaat
ALLAH Ki Tarha Sunte Ho Tum Sab Ki Munajaat
Hazir Hain Liye Deeda-e-Nam Mosi-e-Kazim
Har Samt Dukanein Hai Saji Hirs-o-Hawas Ki
Bazar Mein Afsoos Tamashai Hain Hum Bhi
Kho Jaein Na Iss Bheer Mein Hum Mosi-e-Kazim
Masoma-e-Qum Ho Ke Ghareeb-ul-Ghuraba Ho
Kaisa Hi Salook Inn Se Zamane Ne Kiya Ho
Ab Dekhiye Dono Ka Hasham Mosi-e-Kazim
Jo Bab-e-Hawaij Ke Dar-e-Pak Pe Aa Jaye
Mumkin Hi Nahi Aa Ke Woh Mayoos Chala Jaye
Hoon Door Na Sab Ranj-o-Alam Mosi-e-Kazim
Dunya Ke Masaib Mein Girftar Na Hoon Hum
Kafi Hai Gham-e-Karb-o-Bala Shah-e-Do Aalam
Ho Hum Ko Na Darain Ka Gham Mosi-e-Kazim
Ho Hashar Ke Maidan Mein Bakhshish Ka Yeh Samaan
Ik Hath Mein Ho Daman-e-Aal Aik Mein Quraan
Abbas Ka Ho Sar Pe Alam Mosi-e-Kazim
Hum Kaise Bhi Hain Aap Ke Ghamkhuwar Hain Mula
Jaise Bhi Hain Sarwar Ke Azadar Hain Mula
Rakh Lena Sar-e-Hashar Bharam Mosi-e-Kazim
