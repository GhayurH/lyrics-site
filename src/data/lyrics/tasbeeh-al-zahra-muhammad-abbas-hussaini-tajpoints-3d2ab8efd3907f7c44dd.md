Tasbeeh Al Zahra Muhammad Abbas Hussaini.

1
Ya Fatima Jan-e-Rasool-e-Girami.
Tu Hai Wilayat Ki Hami, Be Abi Ant-o-Ummi.
Teri Dua Hai Yeh Farsh-e-Azza, Teri Tamana Hai Karb-o-Bala.
Wadah Hai Tum Se Aye Bint-e-Nabi, Maa Ninsa Maula Ba Abdillah.
Ya Syeda Chaha Tere Dushmano Ne.
Sadaat Ka Gham Na Howay Par Teri Chadar Talay.

2
Jeeti Rahi Har Sadi Mein Azza Martay Rahe Dushmanan-e-Wila.
Hogi Zahoor-e-Imam Talak Ashhado Unn Ali Ki Sada.
Uthtey Rahe Tofan Zulm-o-Sitam Ke.
Barhtey Rahe Koh Gham Ke Ohlay They Ranj-o-Alam Ke.
Tu Phir Bhi Thehri Wakeel e Wila Karti Rahi Tu Ali Se Wafa.
Turbat Talak Hum Se Kehti Rahi Jazba Ghadeeri Na Kam Ho Tera.

3
Tera Hi Tha Beta Khumaini Keh Jis Ne.
Tagoot Ke Sar Jhukaye Betay Bhi Apne Ganwaye.
Aaj Oss Ke Pairo Hain Nasarullah Larzaan Hain Jis Se Sabhi Dunya.
Hizbullah Hi Tu Haqeeqat Mein Mehdi Ka Lashkar Hai Insha ALLAH.
Roti Hai Woh Unn Zakiron Pe Hamesha.
Jinhoon Ne Gham Unn Ka Becha Mimber Ka Rutba Na Samjha.

4
Yeh Bhi Tu Hain Ahl-e-Kofa Se Kartay Hain Dhoka Yeh Maula Se.
Mehshar Mein Pocha Jo Bibi Ne Bolein Gay Kya Yeh Zahra Se.
Bibi Humein Kar Do Atta Yeh Hosla.
Sare Azadar-e-Karbala Aa Jaein Shehar-e-Madina.
Our Phir Yeh Hasrat Bhi Pori Karein Gunbad Baqiya Pe Banwaein.
 Abbas-o- Taseer Mango Dua Majlis Wahan Aik Karwaein.
