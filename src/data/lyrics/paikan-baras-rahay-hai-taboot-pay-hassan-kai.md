Paikan Baras Rahay Hai Taboot Pay Hassan Kai
Ummat Mita Rahi Hai Aasar Panjatan Kai

۱
Baba Ke Dil Ke Tukreh, Tunay Ginnay Bahatur
Tukray Hazaar Hongay, Qasim Tere Badaan Kai

۲
Taboot Say Lipat Kaar, Qasim Pukareh Baba
Ansoo Adi Kay Dekho, Nohay Suno Bahen Kai

۳
Zainab Pukari Bhai, Dil Doobta Hai Mera
Oghlo Na Dil Ke Tukreh, Sudkay Gaye Dahan Kai

۴
Aghji Ke Hish Ki On Meh, Shabbar Leylo Ke Chumreh
Shabbir Ke Ga Gaala Aur, Bazoo Bari Behan Kai

۵
Zainab Hai Baal Kholay, Ummat Hai Teer Tolay
Poocho Na Shaam Ke Jab, Yeh Haal Hai Watan Kai

۶
Tan Paar To Hai Hassan Ke, Go Chid Gaya Hai Sara
Mohtaj He Rahain Gay, Shabbir To Kafaan Ke
