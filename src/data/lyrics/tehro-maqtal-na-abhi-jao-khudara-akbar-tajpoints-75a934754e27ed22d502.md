1
Tehro Maqtal Na Abhi Jao Khudara Akbar Qamar Baltistani.
Baap Se Cheno Na Jeene Ka Sahara Akbar.
Mere Jeene Ki Koi Aas Nahi Hai Beta.
Tum Ko Maloom Hai Abbas Nahi Hai Beta.
Ab Tu Dunya Mein Nahi Koi Hamara Akbar.
Sath Hi Sath Yeh Binayi Bhi Rukhsat Hogi.
Apne Nana Ki Mujhe Kaisay Ziyarat Hogi.
Tum Ko Laon Ga Kahan Se Main Dobarah Akbar.
Sab Ke Lashon Ko Utha Laya Hussain Ibn-e-Ali.
Beta Iss Alam-e-Ghurbat Mein Hai Khuwaish Meri.
Ran Se Tum Lasha Utha Lana Hamara Akbar.
Do Qadam Bhi Yeh Zaeefi Nahi Chalne Deti.
Bad-e-Abbas Kamar Toot Gayi Hai Meri.
Mujh Se Lasha Nahi Uthay Ga Tumhara Akbar.
Aye Marey Lal Tumhein Dekh Ke Zindah Hai Phophi.
Tum Chaley Jao Gay Mar Jaye Gi Zainab Meri.
Ghar Ujar Jaye Ga Pardais Mein Sara Akbar.
Dasht Mein Noor-e-Nazar Jab Mera Chin Jaye Ga.
Ghutniyon Chalta Howa Lash Pe Baap Aaye Ga.
Hans Ke Dekhein Gay Sitamgar Yeh Nazara Akbar.
Jis Ghari:MIR TAKALLUM: Howa Rukhsat Beta.
Baap Yeh Kehte Hoye Khak Pe Bas Baith Gaya.
Jao ALLAH Nigehban Tumhara Akbar.
