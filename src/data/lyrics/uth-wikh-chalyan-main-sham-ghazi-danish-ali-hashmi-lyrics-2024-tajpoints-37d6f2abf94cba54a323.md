1
Uth Wikh Chalyan Main Sham Ghazi Danish Ali Hashmi.
Uth Wekh Chaliyaan Main Sham Ghazi.
Chadar Luta Ke Hath Rasiyaan Pa Ke Chaliyaan Bachawan Islam Ghazi.
Veran Te Bachray Mehman Sare Hoye Karbala Wich Qurban Sare.
Zainab Wachari Dardaan Di Mari Rondi Aye Le Ke Tera Naam Ghazi.
Jadon Veer Aa Ke Khabran Sunaiyaan Darya Te Ghazi Banwaan Kataiyan.
Zahra Di Jaiyaan Sir Mitiyaan Paiyaan Hoya Khaimiyaan Wich Kuhram Ghazi.
Ummat Dita Aye Ajar-e-Risalat Sajday Che Hoi Jeendi Shahadat.
Oss Veer De Sar Shabbir De Sar Bagi Da Laya Ilzam Ghazi.
Kofay De Pasay Ho Gayi Teyari Qaidi Banri Aye Sadaat Sari.
Rasiyaan De Zewar Kariyaan De Zewar Ummat Taun Maliya Inaam Ghazi.
Sang Veer De Wadah Nibhaiyan Bazar Waisaan Darbar Waisaan.
Rab Di Raza Layi Dein Di Baqa Layi Parh Saan Main Khutbay Sar-e-Aam Ghazi.
Bazaraan De Wich Paidal Turawan Shami Sir Te Pathar Wasawan.
Kar Ke Duawaan Sab Nu Sunawaan Nanay Nabi Da Paigham Ghazi.
Meri Kalma Gowaan Lut Layi Rida Aye Mere Sir Di Chadar Khak-e-Shifa Aye.
Sir Nangay Hosi Malka Haya Di Ja Ja Te Balwa Hosi Aam Ghazi.
Shabbir Ne Sir Qurban Kita Main Apna Pardah Hai Daan Kita.
Keeti Veer Mere Karbal Fatha Aye Karni Fatha Aye Main Sham Ghazi.
 Jawad Mangdi Zainab Duawaan Tera Veer Parcham Ghar Ghar Sajawaan.
Howay Jag Te Jari Matam Hussaini Lanat Yazeedi Anjam Ghazi.
