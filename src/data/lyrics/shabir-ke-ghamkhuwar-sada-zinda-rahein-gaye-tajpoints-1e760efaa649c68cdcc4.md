1
Shabir Ke Ghamkhuwar Sada Zinda Rahein Gaye.
Sheh Ke Yeh Azadar Sada Zinda Rahein Gaye.
Yeh Naam-e-Ali Le Ke Jo Rakhte Hain Qadam.
Jo Halqa-e-Matam Mein Uthate Hain Alam Ko.
Yeh Loag Hain Mukhtar Sada Zinda Rahein Gaye.
Farsh-e-Sheh-e-Mazloom Bichate Hain Jo Ghar Mein.
Har Aan Jo Rehte Hain Mawadat Ke Safar Mein.
Dunya Mein Woh Kirdar Sada Zinda Rahein Gaye.
Sadiyoon Se Shaheedon Ka Laho Bool Raha Hai.
Yeh Khon Kabhi Chup Na Saka Tha Na Chupa Hai.
Jurt Ke Yeh Maimar Sada Zinda Rahein Gaye.
Shabri Ka Dushman Tu Hamesha Se Hai Abtar.
Kehti Hai Zamane Se Yehi Sorah-e-Kausar.
Zahra Ke Tarafdar Sada Zinda Rahein Gaye.
Bakhsha Hai Issi Gham Ne Ibadat Ka Qareena.
Ashk-e-Gham-e-Shabir Hai Anmool Khazina.
Iss Gham Ke Talabgar Sada Zinda Rahein Gaye.
Deti Hai Salami Inhein Tareekh Azza Ki.
Jin Logon Ne Aulad-e-Muhammad Se Wafa Ki.
Hur Ke Woh Paristar Sada Zinda Rahein Gaye.
Milta Hai Muhib Ouj Unhein Rah-e-Wafa Mein.
Har Aan Jo Masroof Hain Tableeg-e-Azza Mein.
Woh Sare Qalamkar Sada Zinda Rahein Gaye.
