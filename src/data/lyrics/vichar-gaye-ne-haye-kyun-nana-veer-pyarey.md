Vichar Gaye Ne Haye Kyun Nana Veer Pyarey Veer Pyarey
Mantaan Maniyaan Deway Balay Rahwaan Takiyaan Waqt Guzarey
Main Kali Hi Qabraan Kitay Hoan Mojawar Reh Gayi Nana
Dhiyaan Le Ke Teriyaan Tur Gaye Tere Bachray Watan Taun Sare
Mere Athro Veer Akbar Layi Hawaan Mere Babay Kitay
Aye Koi Khat Nayi Main Kagaz Te Wichran De Dukh Dard Utaray
Sanghaan Te Juzdan Ne Nana Har Pasay Tofan Aye Nana
Khuwabaan Wich Main Rulday Wekhay Mitiyaan Wich Quran De Parey
Main Takiya Aye Babay De Hath Tuti Hoi Ik Barchi Aye
Maa Laila Payi Veer Akbar De Sir Taun Apni Chadar Warey
Aas De Deway Bujhday Howan Nalay Sah V Mukday Howan
AKBAR Bhaina Layi Ho Janday Oh Welay Sadiyaan Taun Bharey
