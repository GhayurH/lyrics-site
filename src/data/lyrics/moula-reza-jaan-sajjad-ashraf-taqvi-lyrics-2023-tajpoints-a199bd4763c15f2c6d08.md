1
Moula Reza Jaan Sajjad Ashraf Taqvi
Jan-o-Dil Tum Pe Fida Sallay Ala Maula Raza.
Aye Mere Maula Raza.
Ya Ali Mosa Raza Qibla Numa Maula Raza.

2
Pas Dunya Ki Balaein Bhi Nahi Aati Hain.
Mushkilein Bach Ke Zamane Ki Guzar Jati Hain.
Tera Taveez Hai Bazu Mein Bandha Maula Raza.
Shan-e-Aqdas Teri Waliyon Ke Wali Jaisi Hai.

3
Har Ada Teri Sakhawat Ki Ali Jaisi Hai.
Kaun Dar Se Tere Mayoos Gaya Maula Raza.
Jan-e-Mashhad Tere Sar Par Hai Masihayi Ka Taj.
La Ilajon Ka Bhi Hota Hai Tere Dar Pe Elaj.

4
Tere Darbar Mein Batti Hai Shifa Maula Raza.
Dil-e-Momin Ke Har Ik Gham Ko Bhula Deti Hai.
Sans Letey Hain Tu Janat Ka Maza Deti Hai.
Hai Tere Shehar Ki Woh Aab-o-Hawa Maula Raza.

5
Jo Munafiq Hai Wohi Tujh Se Jala Karta Hai.
Tera Sikka Tu Sar-e-Arsh Chala Karta Hai.
Tujh Pe Mabood Ki Hai Khas Atta Maula Raza.
Charchay Konain Mein Ab Charon Taraf Hain Tere.

6
Yeh Jahan Gin Nahi Sakta Jo Sharf Hain Tere.
Waris-e-Uqdah Kusha Shair-e-Khuda Maula Raza.
Aik Karam Kheiz Nazar Hum Pe Bhi Dalo Aaqa.
Zindagi Halqa-e-Mushkil Se Nikalo Aaqa.

7
Sun Lo Hum Jaisay Gareebon Ki Sada Maula Raza.
De De Darbar Mein Aane Ki Ijazat Maula.
Phir Kara De Humein Janat Ki Ziyarat Maula.
Yeh Hai Sajjad Ki Akhtar Ki Dua Maula Raza.
