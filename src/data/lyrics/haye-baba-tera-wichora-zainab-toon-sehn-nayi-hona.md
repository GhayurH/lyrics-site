Haye Baba Tera Wichora Zainab Toon Sehn Nayi Hona
Kiwein Aawan Gi Paikay Wehray Jadon Babal Nazar Nayi Aouna
Assa Wekhiya Nayi Raj Ke Babal Nu Zindagi Sari
Baba Toon Kar Layi Aye Keray Dais Di Teyari
Tera Aakhri Wari Chehra Band Kafan Da Khol Wakhana
Mawaan Na Hoan Jag Te Babal Ne Dukh Wandanday
Kiwein Jiyondiyaan Ne Dhiyaan Jadoon Piyo Wi Chad Ne Janday
Zahra Diyaan Dhiyaan Baba Kinu Apna Dard Sunana
Kofay Che Teri Shahi Zainab Ne Wain Paya
Mere Sir Te Aj Rida Aye Putraan Ne Tainu Chaya
Ais Kofay De Har Moor Uttay Main Bin Chadar De Rona
Mera Naam Le Ke Baba Zainab Mainu Bula Ja
Ik Waar Khol Akhiyaan Aawaz Oh Suna Ja
Tere Bad Musalmana Ne Mainu Zainab Aakh Bulana
Ummat Ne Mariya Aye Aj Veer Mustafa Da
Zainab Khuda Aye Hafiz Tatheer Di Rida Da
Hun Veraan De Sir De NAJFI Zainab Ne Deen Bachana
