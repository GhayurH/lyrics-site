Kainaat nai pura kar sakdi jo Maa da ho nuksaan gaya
Mera jhoolay wala chan Asghar kar Maa di god veeran gaya

1
Maa joondi margayi khaimay vich koi enjj da zulam kamanda ay
Haye nazuk pyaasi gardan te koi enjj da teer chalanda ay
Jiwen meray pyaase Asghar di hurmal(la) si pyaas bhujaan gaya

2
Tera naa le le ke rondi aan jadon teri yaad satandi ay
Teri behn Sakina chan Asghar tera jhooli seenay landi ay
Kadi chumdi jalday khaimeyan chon bach jo tera samaan gaya

3
Takk mar javay gi Maa teri kiwen khaimay de vich jaa Asghar
Pamal na ho javay roya Sajjad Hussain jeya saabir
Bohat okha tapdiyan raitan vich Syed Asghar dafnaan gaya

4
Asghar di gardan chum akhay Babay di laaj nibhawin tu
Tu khoon Abu Talib da hain enaa zaliman nu dikhlawin tu
Fer pyaasa tin din da hass ke ho Babay ton qurban gaya
