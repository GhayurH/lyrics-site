Sadiq e Aal e Muhammad Ki Jahan Par Baat Ho.
Dil Yeh Chahe Phir Wahin Din Ho Wahin Par Raat Ho.

1
Natiya Ashar Mein Madh e Imam Aaye Nazar.
Manqabat Unn Ki Kahon Tu Ho Baho Woh Naat Ho.
Haider-o-Zahra Ke Sadqay Izn Yeh Mujh Ko Miley.
Zikar-e-Sadiq Ho Marey Ghar Noor Ki Barsat Ho.

2
Kulona Ki Manzalat Par Mansab e Jafar Hai Youn.
Farq Kuch Parta Nahi Keh Chup Ho Ya Phir Saat Ho.
Mozu e Sidq-o-Safa Par Baat Karni Ho Agar.
Phir Mera Unwan Maula Aap Hi Ki Zaat Ho.

3
Oss Ko Phir Dunya Kabhi Muflis Bana Sakti Nahi.
Jis Ne Payi Maula Jafar Aap Se Khairat Ho.
Main Imam-e-Jafar-e-Sadiq Ke Dar Ka Hoon Gada.
Mujh Se Bas Hasnain Mere Badshah Ki Baat Ho.
