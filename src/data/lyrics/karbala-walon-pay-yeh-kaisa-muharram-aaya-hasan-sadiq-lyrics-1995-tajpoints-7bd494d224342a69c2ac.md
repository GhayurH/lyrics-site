1
Karbala Walon Pay Yeh Kaisa Muharram Aaya Hasan Sadiq.
Khoon Tapakta Howa Abbas Ka Parcham Aaya.
Jaisay Jaisay Mere Ghazi Ka Alam Jhukta Gaya.
Pusht Mein Hazrat-e-Shabeer Ki Bhi Khum Aaya.

2
Karbala Dekh Le Islam Bachaney Ke Liye.
Sath Kunbay Ko Liye Waris-e-Aadam Aaya.
Zalimo Iss Ko Tu Do Bond Pila Do Pani.
Ab Labon Par Mere Be Sheer Ka Hai Dam Aaya.

3
Sare Mosam Tu Guzar Jate Hain Aa Kar Lekin.
Bas Hamesha Ke Liye Mosam-e-Matam Aaya.
Chand Rehan Nazar Aaya Muharram Ka Idhar.
Qulzum-e-Ashk Meri Aankh Mein Paihum Aaya.
