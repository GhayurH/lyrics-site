Samnay Laila day kujh dair tan beh Akbar
Main taras gayian kadi maa akhay,  aik waar tay keh Akbar

1
1) Abbas da eh ehsan aaj Akbar mera mehman hai
Zainab ton Laila de tayin,  kitta faisla tay Akbar.

2
2) Abbas gawah eh mera nehi naam lawan gi tera
Mennu Akbar ne maa akhiya eh,  mera wadah eh Akbar.

3
3) Haar saal banandi rahiyan sochan ich sajandiyan rahiyan
Teray saal atharan de cholay,  oh waikh le paye Akbar.

4
4) Maar jawin maa na jawein na piyo nun maar mukawein
Mawan nun bachray nehi labday,  haye tere jaye Akbar.
