1
Piyo Hassan Da Zakhmi Ho Ke V Ro Putraan Nu Farmaway.
Meri Zainab Rondi Howay Gi Koi Ghair Na Dar Taein Aaway.
Jis Din Da Zahra Safar Kita Nahi Mere Samne Ayi.
Kandaan De Olay Rain Wali Aa Piyo Nu Seene Laway.

2
Main Taras Giyaan Nayi Aj Taein Kita Mere Naal Kalam Aye.
Meri Hasrat Aye Ik Wain Kare Zainab Aawaz Sonaway.
Mere Sir Shamsheer Di Zarb Lagi Aydha Sir Naize Zakhma Nal.
Mere Sir Te Bosay De Na De Menu Apna Sir Chamwaday.

3
Hun Miriyaan Sawaan Thoriyaan Ne Zainab De Dard Batheray.
Meri Maiyat Turn Tu Pailaan Bas Kinj Turna Aye Aan Wakhaway.
Main Aj Da Wichriya Zainab Nu Aa Sham-e-Ghareeban Milna Aye.
 Akbar Aye Aakh Ke Zainab Di Bas Chadar Chumda Jaway.
