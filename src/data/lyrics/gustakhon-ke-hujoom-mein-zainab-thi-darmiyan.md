1
Aale Nabi pe ye kaisa aaya hay imtehan
Al amaan al amaan
Gustakhon ke hajoom may Zainab thi darmiyan

2
Hairan hoon bazar se kaise guzar gaye
Tat-heer ki pali thi,  bazaar kar samaan
Al amman al amaan

3
Ye soch ke Sajjad ka dil khoon ro parra
Tafseer be rida thi,  naizay pe tha Quran

4
Kaise uthay lash ko mazloom chal diye
Shabbir ki zaeefi,  Akbar sa no jawan

5
Kyun bar bar letay ho tum naam balwa aam may
Mar jaye gi bazar may,  haey aaj meri maa

6
Fakr e Khaleel teri himmat ko hay salam
Phal kahinchna pisar ka,  hota nahi asaan

7
Yasrab se jo Ameer chala tha Ashoor ko
Karbal may woh luta hay,  Risalat ka karwan
