Khank Hawaon Ka Charcha Nahi Tha Khaimo Mein
Kissi Furat Ka Rasta Nahi Tha Khaimon Mein
Palat Palat Ke Hawa Karbala Ko Dekhti Thi
Kahin Bhi Habs Ka Shikwa Nahi Tha Khaimon Mein
Safaid Phool They Our Surkh Hone Aaye They
Kuch Our Rang Ka Khadsha Nahi Tha Khaimon Mein
Tamam Mannay Walay They Hukam-e-Maula Par
Koi Bhi Sochne Wala Nahi Tha Khaimon Mein
Sabhi Ko Fikar Thi Woh Zindah Reh Na Jaein Kahin
Kissi Ko Maut Ka Khatrah Nahi Tha Khaimon Mein
Woh Ya Hussain Kahey Our Roshni Dekhey
Jisay Yeh Shak Hai Ujala Nahi Tha Khaimon Mein
Ossi Ka Rasta Taktay They Rastay Sare
Woh Mehman Jo Pohancha Nahi Tha Khaimon Mein
Sadaein Sansoon Ki Jitni Thin Utni Baqi Rahein
Charag Jis Ghari Jalta Nahi Tha Khaimon Mein
Fasoon e Zindagi Maqtal Ke Naam Tha AKBAR
Magar Woh Lehja Keh Larzah Nahi Tha Khaimon Mein
