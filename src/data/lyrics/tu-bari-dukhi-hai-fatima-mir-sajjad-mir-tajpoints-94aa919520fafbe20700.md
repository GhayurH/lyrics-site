Tu Bari Dukhi Hai Fatima Mir Sajjad Mir Mir Hasan Mir
Chodah So Saal Baad Bhi Madine Mein Jab Soraj Ki Pehli Kiran Gunbad-e-Khizra Se Takrati Hai- Kuch Qadmo Ki Aahat Mehsos Hoti Hai- Aisa Lagta Hai Koi Borha Baap Apne Kandhoon Par Kali Kamli Dalay. Sar Khum Kiye Aik Aisay Na Humwar Rastay Par Chal Raha Hai- Jahan Kuch Tooti Hoi Qabrein Hain Jin Par Shabnam Aansoon Ki Tarha Girti Dikhai Deti Hai- Wahan Par Paray Pathron Se Khud Ko Bachata Howa Larkharata Howa Kissi Qabar Par Ruk Kar Apni Kali Kamli Daal Kar Giriya Karta Hai- Our Bar Bar, Bar Bar, Bas Yehi Kehta Hai.

1
Tu Bari Dukhi Hai Fatima.
Ab Bhi Ro Rahi Hai Fatima.
Jo Rida Teri Hai Fatima.
Khon Se Bhari Hai Fatima.

2
Pasliyon Se Ris Raha Hai Khon.
Sans Le Rahi Hai Fatima.
Chup Ke Dekhtey Hain Murtaza.
Chup Ke Ro Rahi Hai Fatima.

3
Fatima Ko Kha Gayi Yeh Baat.
Jhoot Keh Rahi Hai Fatima.
Thokaron Se Martay Ho Kyun?.
Dukhtar-e-Nabi Hai Fatima.

4
Gir Gaye Hain Munh Ke Bal Ali.
Aaj Chal Basi Hai Fatima.
Neend Se Hussain Uth Gaye.
Yaad Aa Rahi Hai Fatima.

5
Qatilon Ke Naam Bhi Na Loon.
Kaisi Be Basi Hai Fatima.
 Mir Bas Ghadeer Ke Liye.
Aag Par Chali Hai Fatima.
