1
Kya Waqt Para Hai Baba Rajab Ali Khan.
Aap Ki Zahra Pe Kya Waqt Para Hai Baba.
Haye Durrah Mere Bazu Pe Laga Hai Baba.
Samne Beton Ke Mara Hai Tamacha Mujh Ko.
Gham Issi Baat Ka Zahra Ko Bara Hai Baba.
Kam Nazar Aata Hai Aankhon Se Hoye Baal Safaid.
Nojawani Mein Hi Hathoon Mein Assa Hai Baba.
Dar Ke Girney Se Woh Pehlu Pe Qayamat Tooti.
Haye Mohsin Bhi Mera Qatal Howa Hai Baba.
Teri Ummat Teri Beti Ki Hoi Hai Dushman.
Kya Yehi Aap Ki Mehnat Ka Sila Hai Baba.
Jaan Bachaney Ke Liye Aik Faqat Zaat-e-Khuda.
Jan Lainay Ke Liye Shehar Khara Hai Baba.
Kar Diya Aap Ke Baad Itna Zamane Ne Ghareeb.
Mujh Se Roney Ka Bhi Haq Cheen Liya Hai Baba.
