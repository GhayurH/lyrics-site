May Bethi Mal Kai Ravan Mera Veer Nahi Aya, Nahi Aya
May rovan dadrdan maari Mera ma jaya kyun nahi aya

1
1) Raat Andheri mainu aan daravai chain na avai dil ghabrai
Kera jaldi veer marai nu ai paigham ponchavai
Tu ja kai vich perdaisan dai daira paya kyun nahi aya

2
2) Vasdai gharan nu jandrai nai lag gayai akhiyon derya rait dai vagdai
Mer Javan gi vichri veeran teri yaad satavai
Mainu teriyan socha paya gham da saya kyun nahi aya

3
3) Sehrai terai May vaikh kai rovan hanjuvan dai May haar parovan
Hondai maan baihraan da veeran shala kahairi aavai
Hai shagan manai terai sehra laya kyun nahi aya

4
4) Satvin da si vada ai tera gham di surkhi pai savaira
Her pasai nai kaaliyan ratan koi nazar na aavai
May raavan tak tak hari mera ma jaya kyun nahi aya

5
5) Des marai liayi hoya hai paraya veer Asgher na Qasim Aya
Chacha ghazi naal terai gayai koi mur na aavai
Terai vangun koi jagtai nazri aaya kyun nahi aya
