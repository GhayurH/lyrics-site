1
Ro Ke Shah Kehte They Bhai Mar Gaya Zainab.
Jhuk Gayi Kamar Meri Chin Gaya Assa Zainab.
Hum Ko Bhi Ijazat Do Hum Bhi Jaein Dunya Se.
Hum Ko Ab Nahi Jeena Kaun Hai Mera Zainab.

2
Chand Jaisay Chehray Par Zakhm Lag Gaya Oss Ke.
Jab Woh Zeen Se Ghorey Ki Munh Ke Bal Gira Zainab.
Lashay Ki Tarha Rakh Kar Khaimay Mein Alam Bolay.
Ab Yeh Hi Janaza Hai Mere Shair Ka Zainab.

3
Jis Ne Mere Hathoon Par Apni Aankhein Kholi Thin.
Aaj Ossi Ka Godi Mein Dam Nikal Gaya Zainab.
Main Ne Apne Hathoon Se Osske Hath Uthaye Hain.
Tukray Tukray Aada Ne Oss Ko Kar Diya Zainab.

4
Raat Bhar Jo Pehrey Par Mere Hota Tha BiBi.
Aaj Mujh Se Pehle Woh Ran Mein So Gaya Zainab.
Woh Jo Sare Lashon Ko Ran Se Le Ke Aaya Tha.
Oss Ka Lasha Darya Se Main Na La Saka Zainab.

5
Yaad Hai Tumhein BiBi Ik Sada Pe Aata Tha.
Aaj Hum Uthatay They Woh Nahi Utha Zainab.
Kaisay Ho Biyaan Sajjad Sheh Ne Jab Kaha Ro Kar.
Zakhm Ho Gaya Taza Aaj Baba Ka Zainab.
