Gham Shah-e-Karbala Ka Manana Na Chorna.
Hargiz Gham-e-Hussain Mein Aana Na Chorna.

1
Razi Rahe Gi Tum Se Sakina Hussain Ki.
Abbas Ke Alam Ko Uthana Na Chorna.
Aati Hain Sar-e-Farsh-e-Azza Maa Hussain Ki.
Yeh Farsh-e-Azza Sheh Ka Bichana Na Chorna.

2
Taboot Alam Taziye Jhula Sageer Ka.
Dekho Kabhi Bhi In Ko Sajana Na Chorna.
Yeh Ik Ghareeb Maa Ki Tamana Hai Momino.
Mehndi Bin-e-Hasan Ki Sajana Na Chorna.

3
Majlis Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Ki Sada Rahe.
Yeh Majlis-e-Hussain Karana Na Chorna.
Karb-o-Bala Ke Pyasay Musafir Ki Yaad Mein.
Pyason Ko Yeh Sabeel Pilana Na Chorna.

4
Fatway Lagein Gaye Tum Pe Zamane Ke Lakh Par.
Tum Matmi Ho Khon Bahana Na Chorna.
Matam Ke Sath Bachoon Ko Sikhao Namazein.
Bachoon Ko Marifat Yeh Dilana Na Chorna.

5
Hayya Alal Azza Ki Sada Gonjti Rahe.
Matam Ki Saf Pe Sab Ko Bulana Na Chorna.
 Danish Hussain Se Hai Wafa Majlis-o-Matam.
Shabir Se Wafa Yeh Nibhana Na Chorna.
