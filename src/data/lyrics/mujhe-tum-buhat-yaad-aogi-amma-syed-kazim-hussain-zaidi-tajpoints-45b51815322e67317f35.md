Mujhe Tum Buhat Yaad Aogi Amma Syed Kazim Hussain Zaidi.

1
Tha Teyar Jab Syeda Ka Janaza.
Tha Jab Ghar Mein Bibi Ke Ik Shor-e-Giriya.
Woh Zahra Ka Band-e-Kafan Jab Khula Tha.
Sada Aayi Thi Jab Keh Aa, Pas Beta.
Tarap Kar Jo Shabbir Liptay They Maa Se.
Zabaan Par They Mazloom Ke Bas Yeh Nalay.

2
Lipat Kar Yeh Maiyyat Se Kehte They Sarwar.
Mujhe Tum Bohat Yaad Aao Gi Amma.
Main Har Dam Hi Royon Ga Tum Se Bichar Kar.
Mujhe Tum Bohat Yaad Aao Gi Amma.
Tha Jab Dard Tooti Hoi Pasliyon Ka.
Tu Ik Hath Pehlu Pe Tum Ne Tha Rakha.

3
Mujhe Yaad Aaye Ga Jab Jab Woh Manzar.
Mujhe Tum Bohat Yaad Aao Gi Amma.
Woh Dar Tum Pe Girna Bhi Had Hai Sitam Ki.
Tu Amma Tumhari Qasam Hai Keh Jab Bhi.
Nigahon Mein Aaye Ga Jalta Howa Dar.
Mujhe Tum Bohat Yaad Aao Gi Amma.

4
Mujhe Pyas Mehsos Jab Bhi Hoi Hai.
Tumhein Hi Tu Bas Mein Ne Aawaz Di Hai.
Mujhe Tashnagi Jab Sataye Gi Aksar.
Mujhe Tum Bohat Yaad Aao Gi Amma.
Tumhare Hi Saye Mein Zainab Pali Hai.
Tumhare Hi Tu Sath Har Dam Rahi Hai.

5
Nazar Aaye Gi Mujh Ko Tanha Jo Khwahar.
Mujhe Tum Bohat Yaad Aao Gi Amma.
Woh Bhai Hasan Hoon Ya Kulsoom-o-Zainab.
Lipat Kar Jo Aapas Mein Roein Gay Hum Sab.
Bapa Hoga Jis Waqt Ghar Mein Hi Mehshar.
Mujhe Tum Bohat Yaad Aao Gi Amma.

6
Tumhein Hi Pukara Main Ghar Jab Bhi Aaya.
Tumhein Dekh Kar Hi Sakoon Main Ne Paya.
Main Bahir Se Aaon Ga Jis Waqt Bhi Ghar.
Mujhe Tum Bohat Yaad Aao Gi Amma.
Nahi Hoon Gi Amma Jo Tum Pas Mere.
Mujhe Meri Zainab Hi De Gi Dilasay.

7
Galay Se Lagaye Gi Jab Mujh Ko Muztar.
Mujhe Tum Bohat Yaad Aao Gi Amma.
Kaha Ro Ke Izhar Bas Sheh Ne Yeh Hi.
Abhi Tu Basani Hai Karb-o-Bala Bhi.
Wahan Hoga Mera Na Jab Koi Yawar.
Mujhe Tum Bohat Yaad Aao Gi Amma.
