Eyho Gham Muka Gaya Aye Sajjad Di Jawani Ansar Party.
Ruliyaan Ne Wich Bazaraan Kyun Aytaan Qurani.

1
Rasiyaan Che Hath Wekhay Kubra De Mehndi Walay.
Akhiyaan Cho Jari Hoi Taiyon Khoon De Rawani.
Maa Aoun Di Pukaray De De Jay Koi Chadar.
Mushkil Che Koi Kar De Syedaan Te Meharbani.

2
Dikhla Zameen Te Roran Aounthan Nu Ja Pilawan.
Syedaan De Baal Payase Reh Gaye Na Miliya Pani.
Gal Cho Na Laway Bawaan Ro Ro Sakina Aakhe.
Chan Veer Mar Desi Zindaan Di Kahani.

3
Asghar Nu Yaad Kar Di Har Ujri Gaud Maa Di.
Jiday Qatal Te Cha Gayi Aye Kainat Te Hairani.
Sajjad De Jigar Nu Gal Cheer Gayi Aye Adil.
Tareekh De Wich Aa Si Darbar Di Kahani.
