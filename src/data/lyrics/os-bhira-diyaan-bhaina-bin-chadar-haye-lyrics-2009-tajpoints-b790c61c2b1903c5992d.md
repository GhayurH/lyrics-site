Os Bhira Diyaan Bhaina Bin Chadar Haye Wich Sham Bazar Ne Aayiyaan.
Jis Veer Ne Bhaina De Pairaan Di Oddi Khak Te Parday Dari Layi Chadraan Paiyaan.

1
Jeewein Valan Nu Chahray Tay Paya Sirr Tay Laye Ay Rait Sajad
Jiwain Nikliyan Khaimiyan Chon Sariyan Zahra Nay Parday Daariyan Khoob Sikhayan

2
Moor Pehla Si Ajay Har Pasay Ho Gayi Pathraan Di Sham Wich Barish.
Rayiyaan Samne Abid De Aaondiyaan Wadh Wadh Ke Pathar Khandiyaan Zahra Jaiyaan.

3
Teraan Talwaraan Taun Jehray Baal Bach Gaye Dig Dig Ke Onthaan Taun Mar Gaye.
Lang Lang Ke Balaan Taun Onthaan Ne Qaidan Manwaan De Samne Qabraan Banaiyaan.

4
Veer Masooma Da Kandiyaan Te Pairaan Nu Rakh Rakh Turda Aaya Si.
Kera Kadda Kandiyaan Nu Veer De Kitay Hath Qaidi Humsheer De Aan Sipayiyaan.

5
Aa Ke Darbar Andar Takiya Si Bhain Ne Honda Zulm Veer De Sir Te.
Ohno Apni Musibataan Si Bhul Gayiyaan Jadon Veer De Lab Te Zalimaan Zarbaan Laiyaan.

6
Paiyaan Ik Dojay Nu Gal La Ke Rondiyaan Nalay Wain Ayho Kardiyaan Ne.
Be Goor-o-Kafan Chad Ke Aa Gayiyaan Lashaan Apne Veraan Diyaan Naa Dafnaiyaan.

7
Kiwein Saqlain Likhe Manzar Oh Sham Da Bazar Wich Sakina Jadon.
Wasday Hoye Pathraan Nu Wekh Ke Hasrat Nal Sir Te Ghazi De Nazraan Jamaiyaan.
