Haaye mara gaya kull-e-Imaan hay
Qatl-e-Haider nahi qatle Quraan hay

1
1) Kar rhi hai namaazein bhi aaho buqa
Khud tadap kar lahoo sajda rone laga
Aaj nauha kuna roohe aazaan hai

2
2) Yeh malaaek ki nauha bayaani suno
Farsh walon se gham ki kahani suno
Arsh se ho raha kaisa elaan hai

3
3) Beh raha hai jo mehraab-o-mimbar se khoon
Sar patak kar namaazi tadapte hai yun
Mar hi jaaenge kuch aisa imkaan hai

4
4) Roke Shabbir-o-Shabbar ne nauha kiya
Haaye Kulsum-o-Zainab ko ghash aagaya
Aaj ruksat hua Ibne Imran hai

5
5) Jo yateemo ke gham ka madaawa bana
Uske bachhon ko hi bepadar kar diya
Kya yeh ehsaan ka badla ehsaan hai

6
6) Aye shaki yeh sitam tune kaisa kiya
Ruswa kis darja islam ko kar diya
Ibne muljim(laeen) tu kaisa musalmaan hai

7
7) Uske purse ka haq hoga kaise adaa
Jisne tauheed se humko waakif kiya
Jiske ghr se mili haq ko pehchaan

8
8) Kaise SALMAN mera qalam likh sake
Zulm aisa hai patthar bhi rone lage
Roye na phir woh kyun jo ke insaan hai
