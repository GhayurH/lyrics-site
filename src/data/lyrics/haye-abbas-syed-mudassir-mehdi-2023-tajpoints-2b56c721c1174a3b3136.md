Haye Abbas Syed Mudassir Mehdi 2023 Lyrics
Phar Kamar Shabeer Aye Wainr Kita, Zahra Mitiyaan Te Beh Gayi Aye.

1
Abbas Nahin Jhanriya Ghorey Taun Chadar Zainab Di Leh Gayi Aye.
Jehri Piyo De Seene Sondi Rayi, Kinj Oh Mitiyaan Te Soway Gi.
Abbas Di Murshid Zadi Hunr Wal Wal Babay Koon Roway Gi.
Aye Mashk Choon Pani Nahin Duliya, Taqdeer e Sakina Beh Gayi Aye.

2
Jehri Ghairat Aye Kul Panjtan Di Uttay Dhi Islam De Rehbar Di.
Hunr Ban Ke Qaidanr Sainr Medhi Kinj Sham De Safraan Jaway Gi.
Ghazi De Bazu Qalam Hoye, Zainab Dukh Sehn Nu Reh Gayi Aye.
Hunr Sham Te Kofay Wich Ya Rab Oh Bheer Bazari Wekhay Ga.

3
Sir Nangay Mawaan Phophiyaan De Sajjad Muhari Wekhay Ga.
Haye Khak Te Ghazi Nahin Aaya, Gal Tauq Beemar De Pe Gayi Aye.
Taun Tarpay Ga Tu Jis Waqt Laeen Mere Gaysowan Wich Hath Paway Ga.
Mera Gal Hosi Khanjar Hosi Oss Welay Kaun Bachaway Ga.

4
Mere Chan Veran Tere Baaj Meri Zindagi Thori Jayi Reh Gayi Aye.
Na Dar Safak Zamane Taun Haider Be Khof Tu Matam Kar.
Nahin Rooz-e-Hashar Da Dar Sanu Hai Alam Da Saya Sir Opar.
Hubdar Te Janat Wajib Aye Shabeer Di Madar Keh Gayi Aye.
