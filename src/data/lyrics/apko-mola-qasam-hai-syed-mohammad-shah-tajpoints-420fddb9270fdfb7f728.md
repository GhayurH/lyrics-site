Apko Mola Qasam Hai Syed Mohammad Shah.

1
Muntazir Kab Se Hoon Par Khulti Nahi Qismat Meri.
Zindagi Mein Hai Yehi Khuwaish Meri Sab Se Bari.
Zairon Ke Sath Likh Dein Ab Ke Mera Naam Bhi.
Aap Ko Maula Qasam Hai Aap Ke Abbas Ki.
Maut Se Pehle Ho Meri Karbala Mein Hazari.
Aap Ko Maula Qasam Hai Aap Ke Abbas Ki.

2
Wasta Jabir Ka Maula Pehla Zair Jo Bana.
Pa Barehna Sar Barehna Qabar Sarwar Tak Gaya.
Main Bhi Iss Tarha Se Aaon Karbala Kartay Mashi.
Ranj-o-Gham Se Our Mushkil Se Nikalein Ya Imam.
Apne Dar Par Apne Nokar Ko Bulaein Ya Imam.
Karbala Se Door Reh Kar Bhi Koi Hai Zindagi.

3
Khak Orhay Jis Tarha Aate Hain Zair Sar Ba Sar.
Main Bhi Youn Mati Malay Aaon Wahan Rukhsar Par.
Khak Mein Mil Jaon Aisay Dhond Na Paye Koi.
Jis Jagah Akbar Ke Seene Par Laga Barchi Ka Phal.
Oss Jagah Matam Ho Youn Seene Se Khoon Aaye Nikal.
Sath Mein Matam Karey Seene Ke Andar Sans Bhi.

4
Jis Jagah Par Khon-e-Dil Ko Mashk Par Wara Gaya.
Jis Jagah Ghazi Ke Sar Par Gurz Tha Mara Gaya.
Oss Jagah Mujh Ko Ijazat Chahiye Tatbeer Ki.
Jis Jagah Khaimay Jalay Our Shoor Tha Faryad Ka.
Jis Jagah Bistar Jala Tha Syed-e-Sajjad Ka.
Aag Ka Matam Wahan Karne Ki Khuwaish Hai Meri.

5
Zairon Mein Shah Ke Main Naam Youn Likhwaon Ga.
Be Kafan Ke Dais Se Apna Kafan Main Laon Ga.
Hai Yaqeen Mujh Ko Barhey Gi Our Meri Zindagi.
Hai:MUHAMMAD SHAH: Our Saqlain Ki Yeh Iltija.
Bar Bar Aaqa Humein Bulwaiye Karb-o-Bala.
Yeh Ziyarat Ban Nahi Jaye Ziyarat Aakhri.
