Sada Aye Rehniyaan Jag Te Sadawaan Naal Write Up

1
Sada Aye Rehniyaan Jag Te Sadawaan Naal Abid De.
Bazar Ich Aa Gayiyaan Sir Nangay Manwaan Naal Abid De.
Nibhaya Sath Naize Te Ali Akbar Te Asghar Ne.
Safar Pathraan Che Kat De Re Bhirawaan Naal Abid De.
Jiwein Zindan Tak Rondi Sakina Naal Ayi Si.
Qabar Taein Turdi Ayiyaan Ne O Hawaan Naal Abid De.

2
Rabab Aye Wain Kardi Ijazat Sain Jay Deway.
Qabar Ja Ke Sakin Di Banawaan Naal Abid De.
Watan Taun Toriyaan Sughra Duawaan De Ke Veran Nu.
Magar Na Sham Tak Ayiyaan Duawaan Naal Abid De.
Siraan Nal Wain Hoye Kuj Te Kuj Phophiyaan De Nal Hoye.
Te Kuj Nohay Hoye Akbar Ridawaan Naal Abid De.
