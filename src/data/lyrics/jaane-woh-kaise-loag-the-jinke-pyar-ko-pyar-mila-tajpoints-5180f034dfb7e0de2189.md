Jaane Woh Kaise Loag The Jinke Pyar Ko Pyar Mila.
Hum Ne Tu Jab Kaliyaan Mangein Kanton Ka Haar Mila.

1
Khushiyon Ki Manzil Dhondi Tu Gham Ki Gard Mili.
Chahat Ke Nagme Chahe Tu Aahein Sard Mili.
Dil Ke Boaj Ko Dhundla Kar Giya Jo Ghumkhowar Mila.
Bichar Giya Har Sathi De Kar Pal Do Pal Ka Sath.

2
Kiss Ko Fursat Hai Jo Thame Dewano Ka Hath.
Hum Ko Tu Apna Saya Aksar Be Zaar Mila.
Oss Ko Hi Jeena Kehta Hain Tu Youn Hi Jee Lein Gay.
Uff Na Karein Gay Lab Si Lein Gay Aanso Pee Lein Gay.

3
Ghum Se Aab Ghabrana Kaisa Gham Soo Baar Mila.
