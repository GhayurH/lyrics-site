Mojza Aisa Howa Matam e Zanjeer Ke Bad Master Syed Muhammad Shah
Ho Gaya Dard Dawa Matam-e-Zanjeer Ke Bad.

1
Kab Mere Lab Pe Faqat Dawa-e-Ya Laytani Hai.
Ishq Sabit Bhi Kiya Matam-e-Zanjeer Ke Bad.
Samne Aankhon Ke Thi Karb-o-Bala Our Hussain.
Koi Parda Na Raha Matam-e-Zanjeer Ke Bad.

2
Mera Dil Mera Laho Meri Ragein Mera Badan.
Shukriya Kehne Laga Matam-e-Zanjeer Ke Bad.
Shukar Iss Naimat-e-Uzma Ka Tha Wajib Mujh Par.
Bas Main Sajday Mein Jhuka Matam-e-Zanjeer Ke Bad.

3
Sirf Khushnodi-e-Zahra Thi Mere Paish-e-Nazar.
Kab Koi Mangi Dua Matam-e-Zanjeer Ke Bad.
Ungliyaan Kat Gayin Zakhmi Hoye Dandan-e-Awais.
Haan Magar Fatwa Utha Matam-e-Zanjeer Ke Bad.

4
Mere Betoon Ke Azadaro Sada Jeetay Raho.
Ayi Zahra Ki Sada Matam-e-Zanjeer Ke Bad.
Waar Jitne Bhi Aqeeday Pe Howay Thay Muhsin.
Be-Asar Sab Ko Kiya Matam-e-Zanjeer Ke Bad.
