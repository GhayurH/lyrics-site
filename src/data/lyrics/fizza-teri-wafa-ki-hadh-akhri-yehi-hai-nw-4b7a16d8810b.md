Fizza Teri Wafa Ki Hadh Akhri Yehi Hai

Zainab Kay Saat Tu Bhi Bazaar Mei Khari Hai

Tu Nay Tho Syedon Ka Abaad Ghar Bhi Daikha
Ore Kis Tarha Hua Wo Barbaad Ghar Bhi Daikha
Kis Nay Kissay Sataya Tu Khoob Jaanti Hai

Jub Hasan Ka Janaza Wapis Hay Ghar Mei Aaya
Ferva Kay Saath Ro Key Qasim Galay Lagaya
Lash-e-Hasan Say Tu Bhi Rahi Teer Khainchti Hai

Baandhi Hui Rasan Mei Zainab Jo Shaam Aaee
Teri Kamar Kay Peechai Chupti Thi Zehra Jaee
Her Mor Pay Dilasay Zainab Ko Day Rahi Hai

Yasrab Ki Taraf Daikhay Ro Ro Kay Zahra Jaee
Teri Betion Pay Nana Kaisee Qayamat Aaee
Fizza Kay Bhi Hain Waris Yahan Mera Na Koi Hai

Jub Rothi Thi Sakina Her Baar Chup Keraya
Aur Ghash Say Paak Abid Ko Tu Nay Bhi Jagaya
Zainab Kay Saath Jhirkain Fizza Bhi Kha Rahi Hai

Aal-e-Nabi Pay Gham Ki Bunyaad Hai Saqifa
Din Raat Ub Hasan Tho Apna Yehi Wazifa
Jisnay Inhay Rulaya Wo Shakhs Lanati Hai
