Ali Aa Rahe Hain Ali Aa Rahe Hain 1-4

1
Hai Shukar-e-Khuda Bar- Zaban-e-Payamber

2
Bana Hai Jadar-e-Haram Mein Niya Dar

3
Sadaein Hain Salle Alla Ki Falak Par
Tu Badla Howa Hai Zameen Ka Bhi Manzar
Hain Sajde Mein Esa Lab-e-Hoz-e-Kausar
Yahan Soye Kaba Chale Hain Payamber
Sada Hai Yeh Rooh-Al-Ameen Ki Makrrar

4
Munnawar Hai Noor-e-Khuda Se Zamana
Mili Momino Ko Khushi Jawedana
Fizaon Mein Hai La-Fatah Ka Tarana
Laga Pusht-e-Bo-Jahal Par Taziyana
Yeh Sun Kar Chala Kufar Ke Dil Pe Khanjar

5
Fazail Mein Yakta Hai Rutbe Mein Aali
Banein Gaye Malak Inn Ke Dar Ke Sawali
Aroj Iss Ke Hise Mein Hai La-Zawali
Shujat Ki Abjad Sakhawat Ka Wali
Jo Be-Zar Hai Oss Ko Banane Abuzar

6
Yeh Keh De Koi Qaum-e-Marhab Se Ja Kar
Wasee Our Kar Le Zara Apna Lashkar
Bana Le Jo Mazboot Tar Bab-e-Kahiber
Badal Na Sake Gi Woh Apna Muqaddar
Do Tukron Mein Marhab Batay Ga Baraber
