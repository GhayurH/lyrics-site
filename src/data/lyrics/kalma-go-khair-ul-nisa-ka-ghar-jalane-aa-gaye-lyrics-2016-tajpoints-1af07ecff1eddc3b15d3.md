1
Kalma Go Khair-ul-Nisa Ka Ghar Jalane Aa Gaye.
Kaba-e-Arsh-e-Bareen Ka Dar Girane Aa Gaye.
Pusht-e-Zainab Tak Pohanchne Mein Bhala Ab Dair Kya.
Bazo-e-Zahra Talak Tu Tazyane Aa Gaye.

2
Turbat-e-Asghar Ki Pamali Se Zahir Ho Gaya.
Turbat-e-Zahra Ko Mola Kyun Chupane Aa Gaye.
Jis Ghari Jalta Howa Dar Syeda Par Aa Gira.
Aag Mein Bache Sabhi Maa Ko Bachane Aa Gaye.

3
Koi Abbas-e-Jari Jaisa Tera Bhai Na Tha.
Iss Liye Bibi Musalmaan Ghar Jalane Aa Gaye.
Ibtada-e-Sham Yasrab Mein Hoi Jab Kalma Go.
Fatima Ke Dar Pe Le Kar Tazyane Aa Gaye.

4
Jalte Darwaze Ke Piche Dekh Kar Zahra Ka Haal.
Yaad Haider Ko Payamber Ke Zamane Aa Gaye.
Jane Kab Saqlain Aye Gi Yeh Mehdi Ki Sada.
Hum Mazar-e-Fatima Zahra Banane Aa Gaye.
