Shaam e gharibaan hoyi Zainab aey keh ko royi Hun Shaam tayyari aey
Meray naal rownda chaliya Nazan da jairra palya Sajjad muhari aey

1
Zainab Rukkaya aakhan Aadi nahi turan da Bazaran wich turn da
Kiwain tur si pak Imam aey Karbal to Kufa Shaam aey Jithay bherr bazari aey

2
Sajjad kar akheri Babay nu hun salam Manzal te sadi sham aey
Phar ounth di muhar aey Zainab nu kar sawar aey Tayyar amari aey

3
Abid ne toq paya Islam di baqi laye Toheed di fath laye
Wich halat e beemari Aenay karni aey Fizza aey pukari aey

4
Kha ke tamachay roway Shabbir nu bulawey Sajjad chup karaey
Lashan te digdi uth-di Babay nu apnay labdi Masoomi bechari aey

5
Ho jandi Karbala wich Abid di jay shahadat Kiwain bachdi fair imamat
Aenay roz jeena marna Haey Shaam shaam karna Zindagi aenay sari aey

6
Sajjad ne aey Johar Aisa jihad keeta Gham e shah abad keeta
Matam diyan pukaran Aj wi ne wich bazaran Aey jahad te jari aey
