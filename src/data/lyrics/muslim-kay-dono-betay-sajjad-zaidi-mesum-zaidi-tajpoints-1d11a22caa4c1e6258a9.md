1
Muslim Kay Dono Betay Sajjad Zaidi & Mesum Zaidi.
Muslim Ke Dono Betay Jahan Se Guzar Gaye.
Do Shah Zaday Alam-e-Ghurbat Mein Mar Gaye.
Banhein Galay Mein Bhai Ne Bhai Ke Daal Dein.
Youn Apni Janein Shah Pe Qurban Kar Gaye.
Pardais Mein Yateemo Ki Ghurbat Ka Tha Yeh Haal.
Dunya Se Dono Tashna Dahan Khoon Mein Tar Gaye.
Zalim Bhi Phoot Phoot Ke Rotay They Dekh Kar.
Darbar Mein Jo Chand Se Bachoon Ke Sar Gaye.
Sar Peet Ke Yeh Kehti Thi Ik Be Qarrar Maa.
Dhondon Kahan Unhein Mere Bachay Kidhar Gaye.
Jonhi Suna Keh Kofay Mein Bachay Hoye Shaheed.
Khanjar Se Dil Mein Ghamzada Maa Ke Utar Gaye.
Niklay They Dein Bachaney Bachaya Khuda Ka Dein.
Muslim Ke Lal Laut Ke Wapis Na Ghar Gaye.
 Gohar Woh Apne Noor Ki Khairat Bakhsh Kar.
Islam Ka Wajood Ujalon Se Bhar Gaye.
