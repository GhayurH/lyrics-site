1
Bhaiyya Sajjad Main Mar Jaon Gi.
Dam Rukda Aye Sah Tutda Aye Kyun Aa Ke Dard Wandanda Nahin.
Mere Kun Zakhmi Mera Gal Zakhmi Kyun Koi Marham Landa Nahin.
Taun Soch Te Sahi Zindan De Wich Mainu Kiwein Neendar Aa Jaway.
Kithay Gaud Naram Kithay Rait Garam Teri Bhainr Taun Samiya Janda Nahin.
Taun Janrda Ayein Sajjad Bhira Meri Taliyaan De Wich Chalay Nahin.
Hathkariyaan Walay Bazuwaan Te Kyun Main Dukhiya Nu Chanda Nahin.
Sajjad Mera Dil Chahnda Aye Asghar Nu Gaud Khadawaan Main.
Main Rondi Aan Kurlandi Aan Kyun Chota Veer Milanda Nahin.
Main Janrdi Aan Meri Maa Abid Hunr Tak Dhupaan Te Bethi Aye.
Oss Ujari Nu Meri Amari Nu Kyun Chaan Te Veer Baithanda Nahin.
Jadon Ummat Khaimay Sarey Sanr Main Ro Ro Wajaan Mardi Saan.
Mere Burqay Di Mere Kurtay Di Kyun Aag Chan Veer Bhujanda Nahin.
Mainu Khuwab Che Qatil Babay Da Har Roz Tamachay Marda Aye.
Oss Zalim Nu Oss Qatil Nu Kyun Maithon Door Hatanda Nahin.
Main Thaki Hoi Paidal Safraan Di Mainu Dard Ne Bohat Sataya Aye.
Na Uth Sakdi Na Tur Sakdi Kyun Phophiyaan Kool Bithanda Nahin.
Main Shimar Taun Luk Ke Rondi Aan Haye Mitiyaan Te Payi Sondi Aan.
Mere Ronr Nu Mere Soanr Nu Kyun Veer Aasan Baranda Nahin.
Main Teriyaan Sham De Safraan Wich Taliyaan Choon Kharaan Kadhdi Rayi.
Mere Wekh Zakhm Mere Wekh Waram Kyun Mainu Jholi Panda Nahin.
Cholay Da Kafan Pawa Dewin Zindan Che Qabar Banra Dewin.
Jiwein Taun Dafna Sain Bhainr Apnri Aynj Maiyyataan Koi Dafnanda Nahin.
Main Dhi Aan Pyasay Babay Di Meri Qabar Che Panri Aa Gaya Aye.
Meri Qabar Kolon Meri Nazar Kolon Kyun Panri Door Le Janda Nahin.
Zindan Di Ais Tanhai Wich Main Dar Dar Neer Wahandi Aan.
Ghayoor Bhira Majboor Bhira Kyun Bhainr Nu Aa Behlanda Nahin.
Ayeh Gal Tauqeer Sakina Di Beemar Nu Khon Rawandi Rayi.
Aye Zain Ul-Abba Karwa Ke Reha Kyun Mainu Watan Pochanda Nahin.
Woh Zalim Jis Ne Aye Bhaiyya Teri Kamar Pe Koray Marey Hain.
Woh Mere Samne Koray Ko Hansta Hai Our Lehrata Hai.
Har Moor Pe Teri Bhaina Ko Marey Hain Tamachay Zalim Ne.
Main Pyasi Hoon Our Pani Woh Mere Samne Aa Ke Girata Hai.
Burhoon Ke Jaisay Gaal Mere Ab Neelay Par Gaye Hain Bhaiyya.
Na Mar Tu Shimar Se Keh Bhaiyya Ab Kyun Maloon Satata Hai.
