1
Dard Ka Sultan Abid Aise Gham Mein Choor Hai.
Be Rida Hain Bibiyaan Our Sham Kitni Door Hai.
Waris-e-Tatheer Le Kar Chal Para Hai Sham Ko.
Mushkilein Hi Mushkilein Hain Safar Mein Imam Ko.
Beriyon Mein Qaid Logo Mustafa Ka Noor Hai.
Har Qadam Pe Ruk Raha Hai Tang Youn Zanjeer Hai.
Sar Utha Ke Jab Bhi Dekhey Saang Pe Be Sheer Hai.
Qatil-e-Akbar Safar Mein Kitna Woh Majboor Hai.
Dard-e-Abid Barh Gaya Tha Sham Ke Bazar Mein.
Naam Le Kar Jab Pukara Sad Ke Darbar Mein.
Kis Tarha Darbar Jaye Be Rida Mastoor Hai.
Beriyaan Zanjeer Abid Se Yeh Karti Hain Sawal.
Paon Mein Chalay Hain Zakhmi Kyun Tere Ahl-o-Ayal.
Kaun Sa Hai Dard Qaidi Jo Bana Na Soor Hai.
Dukh Zaheer Iss Baat Ka Hai Tauq Mein Beemar Ko.
Martay Pathar Rahe Hain Kyun Dukhi Salar Ko.
Qaid Mein Maqtool Logo Kaisa Yeh Dastoor Hai.
