1
Tum Rehnuma Ho Mola Payamber Ki Shan Ke.
Tum Ho Imam Maula Imam-e-Zaman Ke.
Tehrein Tumharey Samne Kya Lashkar-o-Sipah.
Tum Khud Hi Askari Ho Zaman-o-Makan Ke.

2
Beta Tumhara Aakhri Hujjat Khuda Ki Hai.
Sar Jis Ke Dar Pe Khum Hain Zameen Aasman Ke.
Quran Manzalat Hai Tumhari Har Aik Baat.
Tum Gyarween Ali Ho Zaban-o-Biyan Ke.

3
Woh Jurat Biyan Thi Hakim Ke Samne.
Hotey They Pash Pash Kalejay Chatan Ke.
Tum Waris-e-Ali-o-Nabi Ibn-e-Fatima.
Tum Tarjumaan Rahe Ho Khuda Ki Zaban Ke.

4
Husan-e-Hasan Ali Ki Zakawat Raza Mizaj.
Rab Ne Banaya Noor-e-Risalat Mein Chan Ke.
Ajdad Ki Tarha Pas-e-Zindaan Bohat Hai.
Dushman They Munkiran e Khuda Inn Ki Jan Ke.

5
Mashoor Hai Zamane Mein Tafseer-e-Askari.
Sotay They Aap Ilm Ki Chadar Ko Taan Ke.
 Rehan Jo Bhi Inn Ki Fazeelat Mein Shak Karein.
Pehchan Lijay Loag Hain Kis Khandan Ke.
