Aa Zainab Wain kraindi ri Sanu Chor K Anj Na Ja Baba
Outh Baba Khol K Kafan Wichon Dhiyan Nu Sina La Baba

1
(1)
Dastor Aa sari dunia da jadon eidan nere Aoundiyan na
Dhiyan Nu Pake Wereyan Chon Kai Kai sogatan Aandiyan na
Aa Eid Guzar Si Kinj Sadi Outh Ojriyan Nu Samjha Baba

2
(2)
Tera Pak janaza Putran Na Apne Hatan Na Chana Aa
Taboot Tere Nu Najaf de Wich Husnain Na Khud Dafnana Aa
Jadon Ma Marna Koi Ni Hona Mera Sajad Siwa Baba
