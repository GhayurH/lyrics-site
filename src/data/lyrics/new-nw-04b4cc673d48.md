Karbobala Mei Toota Sitam Ka Jo Aasman

Dil Raiza Raiza Hogaya  Ya Sahibaz ZamaaN (ajtfs)

Kis Tarha Dein Woh Zainab o, Laila Ko Hauslay Dil Thaamay Apna Khaak Pe, Shabbir Gir Paray

Lehjay Mei Mustafa Ke Dii  Akbar Ne Jab AzaaN

Roya Hoon Kis Qadar Ke, Bandhi Meri HichkiyaN

Ik Maa Pe Kaisa Waqt Tha, Mei Kya Karu BayaaN

Sunta Hoon Woh Sunaati Thi  Jhoolay Ko LoriyaN

Laasha Kya Dikh Gaya Unhain, Haidar Ke Sher Ka

Naulaakh Ghur Sawaaron Ko, Ik Khel Mil Gaya

Tori Hain Dor Dor Ke  Ghazi Ki PasliyaaN

Ik Din Mein Apnay PyaroN Ke, Laasho Ko Ro Liya

Ab Dekhta Bahan Ko Hain, Majmay Mei Be Ridaa

Naizay Pe Bhi Na Khatm, Huwa Sheh Ka ImtehaaN

Shaami Kharray Hain Naizo Pe, Chadar Liye Huway

Aur Kuch Kharray Hain Haathon Mei, Pathar Liye Huway

Kehtay Hain Aanay Do Zaraa  Haidar Ki BetiyaaN

Maula Gham e Hussain Mei, KhooN Rang Khaak Hai

Dil Gham Se Phat Raha Hain, Jigar Chaak Chaak Hai

Khaimon Se Uth Raha Hai, “Asad Azmi” DhuwaaN
