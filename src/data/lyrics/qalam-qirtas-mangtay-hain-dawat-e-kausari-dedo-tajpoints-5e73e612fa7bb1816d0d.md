Qalam Qirtas Mangtay Hain Dawat e Kausari Dedo.
Naqi Ki Shan Likhni Hai, Mujhe Kuch Shayari Dedo.

1
Ali Awwal Ne Bhi Murdoon Ko Di Thi Zindagi Maula.
Aye Chothay Murtaza Mere Qalam Ko Zindagi Dedo.
Sama Na Bibi Main Bhi Aaya Hoon Fitrus Sa Gham Le Kar.
Mujhe Bhi Lams Lainay Do, Mujhe Bhi Hazari Dedo.

2
Suna Hai Jo Ali Ka Hota Hai Salman Hota Hai.
Mujhe Bhi Baichni Hai Khuld, Mujhe Kuch Nokari Dedo.
Ali Walay Ali Ke Naam Par Jhagra Karein Gay Kya?
Khudara Buzar-o-Salman Jaisi Aa Gahi Dedo.

3
Teri Khatir Rahein Zindah, Marein Bhi Tu Teri Khatir.
Naqi, Hur, Jaun Ke Sadqay Humein Woh Aashiqui Dedo.
Abaidullah Junedi Bhi Yeh Kehne Par Howa Majboor.
Main Jahil Hoon, Mujhe Kuch Ilm Aye Maula Naqi Dedo.

4
(Abu Hashim).
Woh Pathar Jis Se Jahil Bhi Hoye Aalam Zabno Ke.
Woh Kankar Aye Naqi Maula, Ba Sadqay Askari Dedo.
(Abu Hashim).

5
Tere Hathoon Mein Mati Bhi Jo Aaye Tu Woh Sona Ho.
Mere Sar Par Bhi Rakh Lein Hath, Mujhe Bhi Nokari Dedo.
Dua Taseer Kartay Hain, Jo Pota Hai Tera Maula.
Jo Unn Ke Sath Mein Guzray, Humein Woh Zindagi Dedo.
