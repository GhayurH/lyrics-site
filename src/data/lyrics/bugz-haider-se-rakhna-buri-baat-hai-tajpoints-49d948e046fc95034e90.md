Bugz Haider Se Rakhna Buri Baat Hai.
Door Haider Se Rehna Buri Baat Hai.

1
Mard Woh Hai Jo Maidaan Mein Jam Ke Laray.
Dar Ke Youn Bhag Jana Buri Baat Hai.
Matam-e-Sheh Pe Fatwa Lagatay Ho Tum.
Gar Mein Bhi Tu Rona Buri Baat Hai.

2
Yeh Hain Sarkar-e-Konain Farma Gaye.
Khum Ka Minber Bholana Buri Baat Hai.
Sab Se Pehle Tha Al-Hamd Jis Ne Parha.
Unn Ko Momin Na Kehna Buri Baat Hai.

3
Jis Ne Dar Fatima Ka Jalaya Ossay.
Razi ALLAH Kehna Buri Baat Hai.
Jis Zabaan Se Na Zikar-e-Ali Ho Kabhi.
Woh Zabaan Munh Mein Rakhna Buri Baat Hai.

4
Jis Ke Ghar Par Alam Ho Na Abbas Ka.
Unn Ke Ghar Aana Jana Buri Baat Hai.
Jis Ko Farman Khaliq Ne Ghar De Diya.
Oss Ka Humsar Banana Buri Baat Hai.
