Ik Areza Jo Unhein Sorat-e-Midhat Likha.
Aik Ik Harf Ko Az Roye Muhabbat Likha.

1
Shart Yeh Thi Keh Shariyat Ki Zabaan Mein Likhon.
Bas Yehi Soch Ke ALLAH Ki Hujjat Likha.
Jab Kabhi Aap Ki Aamad Ki Khabar Dil Ko Mili.
Hum Ne Oss Lamhey Ko Lamhat e Qayamat Likha.

2
Aap Aa Jaein Tu Badlay Yeh Nizam-e-Hasti.
Hum Ne Iss Baat Ko Har Saal Ba Shiddat Likha.
Kabhi Qayam Kabhi Mehdi Bhi Maula Aaqa.
Wasf Ko Inn Ke Sada Hasb-e-Zarorat Likha.
