kehti thi ye zainab se mohamed ki dulaari, mai kya karoon bibi

qismat may judaai ki ghadi aagayi apni, mai kya karoon bibi

taqdeer pe bas nahi chalta hai kisi ka, ek roz hai marna

kya maa ki judaai alam jhel sakogi, mai kya karoon bibi

kehti thi ye….

jab jaayega shabbir mera karbobal ko, maidane wagha ko

jaayegi meri jaan sawaari bhi tumhari, mai kya karoon bibi

kehti thi ye….

qaime mere abbas ke saahil se hatenge, pyase hi rahenge

atfal ki durro se wahan pyas bujhegi, mai kya karoon bibi

kehti thi ye….

tum auno mohamed ko raza marne ki dogi, khamosh rahogi

jab saamne aayegi mohamed ki jawani, mai kya karoon bibi

kehti thi ye….

miljayegi matti may payambar ki nishani, par aye mere jaani

seene may hi ghut jaayega dum ro na sakogi, mai kya karoon bibi

kehti thi ye….

marjayega pyasa labe darya mera saqqa, qasim na rahega

tum bhai hassan ko bhi bahot yaad karogi, mai kya karoon bibi

kehti thi ye….

sar peet-ti rehjaogi tum qaime ke darr par, aur shimr ka khanjar

chal jaayega mit jaayegi hyder ki nishani, mai kya karoon bibi

kehti thi ye….
