Bhen Teri Ujar Gayi Ghazi Abbas Haider Lalji.

1
Aakhay Zainab Aye Sir Che Pa Mitiyaan Bhainr Teri Ujar Gayi Ghazi.
Dukh Te Bhul Jawaan Gi Main Qaidaan De Maut Bhul Sakdi Nayi Teri Ghazi.
Baad Tere Bara Rawaya Aye Qatilaan Ne Tere Sataya Aye.
Chain Di Neend Tere Baad Kadi Bhainr Teri Na So Saki Ghazi.
Wich Bazaraan Mainu Phiranday Rahey Mar Ke Nezay Haye Turanday Rahey.
Ghar Choon Jehri Kaday Na Nikli Si Solaan 16 So Meel Oh Turi Ghazi.

2
Lash Teri Te Jad Main Aayi Saan Qaidi Saan Aounth Taun Leh Na Payi Saan.
Jiwein Digiya Sain Haye Zeen Taun Tu Main V Naqay Taun Unj Digi Ghazi.
Veraan Bahjon Ne Bhainraan Kinj Jewan Muk Gaye Manr Bhainr De Veran.
Beh Ke Hunr Khali Wehriyaan De Wich Ronran Main Sari Zindagi Ghazi.
Wekh Uth Ke Qabar Choon Haal Mera Khon De Naal Sir Aye Lal Mera.
Tainu Pathraan Diyaan Main Barshaan Wich Yaad Ro Ro Ke Kardi Rahi Ghazi.

3
Tu Na Tak Sakiyaan Be Rida Mainu Gham Aye Nezay Te Si Bara Tainu.
Kash Zainab Aye Tainu Das Sakdi Sir Taun Chadar Kiwein Lathi Ghazi.
Shimar Tak Tak Ke Mainu Hasda Rehya Bhainr Teri Nu Ohnay Bagi Kehya.
Roya Sajjad Mere Wal Tak Ke Tere Sir Wal Main Takdi Rahi Ghazi.
Aye Asad Wainr Kardi Rahi Bibi Gal Sunrawan Main Ki Sakina Di.
Qaid Koray Tamachay Seh Seh Ke Mar Gayi Teri Ladli Ghazi.
