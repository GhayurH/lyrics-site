1
Tauheed Ki Baqa Hussain Badshah Zakir Bilawal Ali Hussaini.
Hai Fakhar-e-Anbiya Hussain Badshah.
Jin Ki Wajah Se Masjidon Mein Aati Hai Aazaan.
Islam Ki Ragoon Mein Inn Ka Lahu Rawaan.

2
Woh Hi Binaye Lailaha Hussain Badshah.
Har Ba Zameer Shaks Par Inn Ka Ahsan Hai.
Maa Baap Behan Bhai Ki Batai Shan Hai.
Aisa Kareem Aaqa Hussain Badshah.

3
Sarwar Ko Bulaya Tha Khaliq Ne Arsh Par.
Rooz-e-Ashor Jis Ne Bulaya Hai Farsh Par.
Woh Nafs-e-Mutmaina Hussain Badshah.
Janat Ki Aarzu Our Malik Se Dushmani.

4
Bughz-e-Ali Hai Dil Mein Zarra Socha Hai Kabhi.
Janat Ka Bhi Tu Shehanshah Hussain Badshah.
Rokay Ga Kaisay Matam-o-Majlis Ko Yeh Laeen.
Hai Jan Se Bhi Pyari Azadari Bilyaqen.

5
Sansoon Mein Jo Basa Hussain Badshah.
Zikar-e-Hussain Pak Hai Sajid Ki Bandagi.
Inn Ki Wila Hi Asal Mein Hai Meri Zindagi.
Zamin Hai Yeh Nijat Ka Hussain Badshah.
