Ya Rab Har Aik Ko Yeh Sadat Naseeb Ho.

1
Karb-o-Bala Ki Sab Ko Zyarat Naseeb Ho.
Aye Sheh Ke Azadar Chalo Karbala Chalein.
Yeh Dil Hai Be-Qarar Chalo Karbala Chalein.
Abbas-e-Namdar Ke Roze Pe Jaein Gaye.
Ja Kar Wahan Sakina Ko Noha Sunaein Gaye.
Dekhein Gaye Woh Deyar Chalo Karbala Chalein.

2
Ahl-e-Haram Ke Khaimay Jalaye Gaye Jahan.
Matam Karein Gaye Aag Ke Sholon Pe Hum Wahan.
Janein Karein Nisar Chalo Karbala Chalein.
Dasht-e-Bala Mein Bano Ke Dilgeer Ki Fogaan.
Ab Bhi Sunai Deti Hain Laila Ki Siskiyaan.
Aaho Ke Hain Hisar Chalo Karbala Chalein.

3
Ashk-e-Gham-e-Hussain Milaein Furat Mein.
Nohon Ke Sab Aareezay Bahaein Furat Mein.
Mojoon Ko Dein Qarar Chalo Karbala Chalein.
Phir Ba-Barehna Jaein Gaye Bazar-e-Sham Mein.
Zainab Gayin Thin Jis Tarha Darbar-e-Aam Mein.
Yeh Dil Ki Hai Pukar Chalo Karbala Chalein.

4
Chalees Saal Ghutti Rahin Jis Mein Siskiyaan.
Rote The Jis Ke Saye Mein Sajjad-e-Na-Tawaan.
Chomein Gaye Woh Jidar Chalo Karbala Chalein.
 Mehshar Bari Hai Baat Keh Tum Shayar-e-Hussain.
Phir Tu Kahein Gaye Loag Tumhein Zair-e-Hussain.
Barh Jaye Ga Waqar Chalo Karbala Chalein.
