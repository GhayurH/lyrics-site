1
Veer Akbar Kiwein Jewaan Gi Bhain Ujari Nu Dasda Ja Write Up
Mar Chadna Tere Ghama Ne Veera Haye Na Pardais Wasa.
Be Warsaan De Wangoon Chad Chaliya Ayein Sughra Aakhdi Reh Gayi.
Jiondiyaan Jee Mar Jawaan Gi Main Veera Na Rowan De Was Pa.

2
Khali Gharaan De Jandray Kyun Kholaan Kis Ne Rehnra Aithay.
Naal Apne Mere Ghama Diyaan Taun Kunjiyaan V Lainda Ja.
Aye Lazmi Nayi Akbar Chan Veran Meriyaan Sariyaan Mannay.
Mainu Tu Babay Di Man Ke Akbar Mehmal Taun Aap Laha.

3
Ujray Tere Tu Ghar De Bohey Tak Sarah Ban Ke Jawaan.
Mere Layi Ayho Safa Te Marwa Hajj Ayho Veer Mera.
 Akbar Samajh Nayi Aandi Marzi Si Ki Peer Hussain Sakhi Di.
Rab Janrey Dunya Kyun Baqi Reh Gayi Jadon Wichrey Bhain Bhira.
