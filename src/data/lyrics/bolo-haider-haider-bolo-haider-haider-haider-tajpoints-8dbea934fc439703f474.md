1
Bolo Haider Haider Bolo Haider Haider Haider.
Azmat-e-Kaba Noor Ka Paikar Fatah-e-Badar-o-Khaibar.
Yaad Zara Kar Tu Bhi Musalmaan Woh Kofay Ka Mimbar.
Janat In Ki Thokar Mein Hai Jo Hain Ali Ke Nokar.
Dozakh Inn Ki Qasmat Mein Hai Jo Hain Dushman-e-Haider.
Door Ali Se Rehne Walo Haq Ko Zara Pehchano.
Haq Tu Wahin Pe Ho Ga Jahan Pe Hoon Gaye Saqi-e-Kausar.
Aisa Hakim Jis Ke Nagar Mein Koi Na Bhoka Soye.
Raat Ko Rooz Andheray Mein Jo Bantay Rotiyaan Ghar Ghar.
Natiq-e-Quraan Nafas-e-Nabi Our Kul Ka Kul Hai Emaan.
Iss Se Bara Kya In Ka Sharaf Ho Hain Zahra Ke Shohar.
Jaldi Jao Wapis Mariyam Aayi Sada Yeh Ghaibi.
Bint-e-Asad Hi Dakhal Hoon Gi Iss Kabay Ke Andar.
Hai Yeh Wajhi-o- Hasan Ki Dua Pohanchein Jo Ghulam Najaf Mein.
Jonhi Salami Dein Mula Ko Dam Nikale Chokhat Par.
