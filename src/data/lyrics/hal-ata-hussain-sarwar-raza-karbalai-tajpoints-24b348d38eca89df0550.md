Ibtida Hussain, Inteha Hussain, Na Khuda Hussain, Hal Atta Hussain.

1
Shams-o-Qamar Taba Hain Tere Sang-e-Haram Tujh Pe Wari.
Koh-o-Safa Tere Sadqay Arsh Ki Zoo Tujh Pe Tari.
Rooh Ki Azaan, Haq Ka Aasmaan,
Hai Khuda Jahan, Yeh Bhi Hai Wahan.
Aala Mukaram Zahid Hai Jag Se Nirala Abid Hai.
Noor Hai Yeh Bhi Khaliq Ka Iss Ka Khuda Bhi Shahid Hai.

2
Tera Gham Sada, Hota Hi Raha,
Mit Na Paye Ga, Shoor Karbala.
Sare Malak Iss Ke Nokar Sare Shaheedon Se Behtar.
Rehtey Hain Qadmo Mein Paray Dur-e-Najaf Ho Lal-o-Gohar.
Badbaan Hussain, Sarbaan Hussain,
Saibaan Hussain, Javedaan Hussain.

3
Naz-e-Khuda Hai Naz-e-Nabi Qamar-e-Bani Hashim Ka Wasi.
Arshi Mangtay Rehtey Hain Ibn-e-Ali Hai Aisa Sakhi.
Badshah Hussain, Shehanshah Hussain,
Dein Panah Hussain, La Fatah Hussain.
Jalti Zameen Par Haye Para Shimar Ka Khanjar Oss Pe Chala.
Khushk Gala Hai Oss Ka Bara Waye Hussaina Wawela.

4
Shikwa Na Kiya, Sar Kata Diya,
Ghar Luta Diya, Dein Bacha Liya.
Ibn-e-Mazahir Marey Gaye Ghazi Ke Bazu Na Rahe.
Qasim-o-Akbar Qatal Hoye Kaun Bach Hai Jo Aaye.
Koi Na Raha Tera Aasra,
Tanha Reh Gaya Ibn-e-Syeda.

5
Dar Se Sadaein Deti Hai Zainab Meri Roti Hai.
Baba Ko Na Mar Mere Haye Sakina Kehti Hai.
Zulm Hai Bara, Shimar Ne Kiya,.
Dekho Be Khata, Kata Hai Gala.
Kehti Hai Zainab Ro Kar Meri Bandhi Hathoon Mein Rasan.
Chor Ke Jaon Kaisay Bhala Bhai Para Be Goor-o-Kafan.

6
Dasht Mein Para, Lasha Hai Tera,.
Na Kafan Mila, Ho Gayi Jafa.
 Asif-o- Sarwar Kartay Hain Tujh Se Duaein Aye Maula.
Hum Ko Bola Le Rozay Pe Sun Le Sadaein Aye Maula.
Sun Dua Hussain, Kar Atta Hussain,
Ab Bola Hussain, Karbala Hussain.
