Run ko jaatay howay sir jhukayey howay, Sheh nay rokar kaha

Alvida Alvida

Aaya waqt-e-safar aye behen aaj hum, horahay hain juda

Alvida Alvida

Shah barh kar behen kay galay lag gayey,

Aur dukhiya behen say yeh kehnay lagay, ro kekehnay lagay

Meri mazloom pyaasi musafir behen tera haafiz khuda

Jis say dartee thee tum woh ghari aagayee,

Dekho chaaro taraf teergee chah gayee, teergee chah gayee

Dasht-e-purhaul main shaam honay ko hai lo musafir chala

Lay kay baali Sakina ko aaghosh main,

Nunnay nunnay say haatho kay bosay liye, ro kay bosay liye

Sheh nay hasrat say beti ko tuktay howay aaha bhur kar kaha

Bola beemar baytay say aaqa mera,

Ab na ayeyga aye, beta baba tera, beta baba tera

Maut ki aahatain taiz honay lagee aarahee hai sada

Aya khaimay say bahar jo mola mera,

Bhai beta bhateeja koi bhi na tha, hi koi bhi na tha

Aik hasrat say chaaro taraf dekh kar sheh nay khud he kaha

Jaab tarap kar kaha meray shero uthoo,

Aao Zainab kay bhai koi rukhsat karo, aao rukhsat karo

Aik Aik laash-e-bay sir say aayee sada ayey Shah-e-Karbala

Aisa matam karo aisa matam karo,

Pak bibi kahay marhaba marhaba, marhaba marhaba

Khoon ugaltay howay aik aik zakham say aaj aayey sada
