Ko Ba Ko Phail Gayi Baat Shanasai Ki.
Oss Ne Khushbu Ki Tarha Meri Pazeerai Ki.

1
Kaise Keh Doon Keh Mujhe Choor Diya Oss Ne.
Baat Tu Sach Hai Magar Baat Hai Ruswai Ki.
Wo Kahin Bhi Giya Lota Tu Mere Pass Aya.
Bas Yehi Baat Hai Achi Mere Harjai Ki.

2
Tera Pehlu Tere Dil Ki Tarha Aabaad Hai.
Tujh Pe Guzre Na Qayamat Shab-e-Tanhai Ki.
Oss Ne Jalti Hoi Paishani Pe Jab Hath Rakha.
Rooh Tak Aa Gayi Taseer Maseehai Ki.

3
Ab Bhi Barsat Ki Raton Mein Badan Toota Hai.
Jaag Uthti Hain Ajab Khuwaishein Angrai Ki.
