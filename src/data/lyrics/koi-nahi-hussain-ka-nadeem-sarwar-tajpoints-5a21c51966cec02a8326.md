Koi Nahi Hussain Ka Nadeem Sarwar.
Hangam-e-Asar Ka Manzar Hai.

1
Na Ghazi Na Koi Lashkar Hai.
Wa Ghurbata, Aye Wa Gurbata, Aye Wa Gurbata.
Koi Nahi Hussain Ka Begane Dais Mein.
Hal Min Ki Hai Sada Koi Nahi Hussain Ka.

2
Haye Asar Ka Sama, Rota Hai Aasmaan.
Hai Aakhri Azaan, Gham Se Hai Neem Jaan.
Hussain Ya Hussain, Hussain Badshah.
Akela Reh Gaya, Woh Ghar Luta Chuka, Bacha Ke Dein Tera.

3
Khuda Aye Khuda, Aye Khuda Aye Khuda.
Kunba Ujar Gaya, Begane Dais Mein.
Lashay Utha Chuka, Ghar Bhi Luta Chuka.
Har Aik Lash Par Aanso Baha Chuka.

4
Hussain Ya Hussain, Hussain Badshah.
Akela Reh Gaya, Woh Ghar Luta Chuka, Bacha Ke Dein Tera.
Khuda Aye Khuda, Aye Khuda Aye Khuda.
Rota Hai Zuljana, Begane Dais Mein.

5
Lakhon Mein Ghir Gaya, Mazloom-e-Karbala.
De Kis Ko Ab Sada, Ghazi Tu So Gaya.
Hussain Ya Hussain, Hussain Badshah.
Akela Reh Gaya, Woh Ghar Luta Chuka, Bacha Ke Dein Tera.

6
Khuda Aye Khuda, Aye Khuda Aye Khuda.
Lashkar Bichar Gaya, Begane Dais Mein.
Zainab Ki Thi Sada, Aye Wa Musibata.
Nana Tera Hussain, Ghorey Se Gir Gaya.

7
Hussain Ya Hussain, Hussain Badshah.
Akela Reh Gaya, Woh Ghar Luta Chuka, Bacha Ke Dein Tera.
Khuda Aye Khuda, Aye Khuda Aye Khuda.
Zainab Ki Thi Sada, Begane Dais Mein.
