Sar E Hussain Laeeq Raza Write Up
Aap Ka Sar Youn Sakina Ko Mila Hai Baba.
Sansein Rukti Hain Meri Waqt-e-Qaza Hai Baba.
Sar Barehna Hoon Abhi Aankhein Na Apni Kholein.
Mere Baloon Pe Faqat Khak-e-Shifa Hai Baba.
Naam Suntay Hi Mujhe Oss Ne Tamachay Marey.
Wasta Dadi Ka Jis Ko Bhi Diya Hai Baba.
Mujh Ko Neelon Se Safar Yaad Hai Meelon Se Nahi.
Kitne Bazar They Chehray Pe Likha Hai Baba.
Aap Ke Baad Na Chaon Mein Kabhi Baithi Main.
Our Jee Bhar Ke Na Pani Hi Piya Hai Baba.
Jhuriyaan Chehray Pe Khum Aaya Kamar Mein Meri.
Sham Tak Aane Mein Yeh Haal Howa Hai Baba.
Sar-e-Shabeer Se Akbar Yeh Pukarein Bibi.
Maut Hi Ab Marey Dardoon Ki Dawa Hai Baba.
