Zamenain Nabi Ki Zamana Nabi Ka Lyrics
Yeh Sari Khudai Khazana Nabi Ka.

1
Na Bholay Ga Teri Sada Ko Zamana.
Utar Kar Falak Se Zameen Par Woh Aana.
Woh Makkay Ki Galiyon Mein Khoon Mein Nahana.
Woh Wazn-e-Risalat Uthana Nabi Ka.

2
Woh Bandoon Ko Apne Khuda Se Mila Kar.
Saleeqa Humein Bandagi Ka Sikha Kar.
Jo Thay Darmiyaan Unn Butoon KO Hata Kar.
Khuda Ka Hi Sajda Karana Nabi Ka.

3
Khuda Jis Se Dekhay Nabi Woh Basarat.
Sunay Jis Khaliq Nabi Woh Samaat.
Mashiyaat Khuda Ki Nabi Ki Mashiyat.
Iradah Khuda Ka Irada Nabi Ka.

4
Salam Un Pe Jo Hain Zamane Pe Saya.
Keh Unn Mein Khuda Ne Hai Khud Ko Basaya.
Hussain-o-Hasan Hoon Ali Hoon Ke Zahra.
Nabi Ki Tarha Hai Gharana Nabi Ka.

5
Yeh Muzda Sunein Hum Se Aqwam-e-Alam.
Keh Ab Waqt Shayad Bohat Reh Gaya Kam.
Keh Aaein Sawar Is Mein Ho Jaein Ab Hum.
Safina Howa Hai Rawana Nabi Ka.

6
Hain Nalain-e-Sarwar Navaid Our Sarwar.
Ghulam-e-Khadija Nabi Ke Hai Nokar.
Dua Hai Yeh In Ki Jo Ho Rooz-e-Mehshar.
Tu In Par Bhi Ho Shamiyana Nabi Ka.
