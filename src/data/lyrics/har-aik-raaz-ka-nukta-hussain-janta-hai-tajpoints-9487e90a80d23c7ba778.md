KHuda Ki Marzi-o-Mansha Hussain Janta Hai.

1
Zameen-e-Garam Pe Sajday Mein Rakh Ke Sar Apna.
Khulasa Kar Diya Fuzto Be Rab-e-Kaba Ka.
Ali Ke Sajday Mein Kya Tha Hussain Janta Hai.
Shahadton Ke Gulistaan Ki Rooh Ban Ke Utha.
Jo Sar Kata Tu Woh Quraan Ki Rooh Ban Ke Utha.
Sina Pe Shan Se Jana Hussain Janta Hai.

2
Habib Ibn-e-Mazahir Ko Mar Jo Chali.
Ajal Ko Naz Se Bole Hussain Ibn-e-Ali.
Marey Howon Ko Jagana Hussain Janta Hai.
Pohanch Na Paya Jahan Pehle Deen-e-Haq Ka Alam
Larz Ke Reh Gaye Jis Ja Pe Anbiya Ke Qadam.
Unhi Hadon Se Guzarna Hussain Janta Hai.

3
Ishara Kar De Tu Khanjar Ki Dhar Kat Jaye.
Iradah Kar Le Tu Foaj-e-Yazeed Ulat Jaye.
Magar Khuda Ka Iradah Hussain Janta Hai.
Azza Hussain Ki Hai Ahtamam Zainab Ka.
Khuda Ke Dein Mein Kya Hai Maqam Zainab Ka.
Khuda Hi Janta Hai Ya Hussain Janta Hai.

4
Kahan Talak Hai Khuda Ki Khudai Kya Jane.
Mere Hussain Ki Had-e-Rasai Kya Jane.
Akela SOch Ke Kya Kya Hussain Janta Hai.
Jo Aaftab Hai Oss Ko Diye Ki Kya Hajat.
Saja Raha Hai Andhere Mein Khaima-e-Nusrat.
Diya Bhuja Ke Parkhna Hussain Janta Hai.

5
Kahan Naseeb Zameeno Ko Arsh Ho Jana.
Mohal Hota Bohat Karbala Ka Ban Pana.
Magar Yeh Moajza Karna Hussain Janta Hai.
Dar-e-Hussain Pe Kuch Kehte Hain Na Mangte Hain.
 Turab Our Mohammad Bas Itna Jante Hain.
Keh In Ke Dil Ki Tamana Hussain Janta Hai.
