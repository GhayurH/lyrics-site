Uth Gaya Baap Ka Sir Se Saya Aaj Zainab Yateem Ho Gayi.

1
Ghar Mein Farsh-e-Azza Bich Gaya Hai.
Khana-e-Rab Matam Bapa Hai.
Soag Mein Doba Ghar Syeda Ka.
Utha Raha Hai Janaza Ali Ka.
Aik Mehshar Ka Alam Tha Barpa.
Sab Ke Honton Pe Tha Aik Noha.

2
Ro Rahe Hain Yeh Mehrab-o-Minbar.
Keh Rahe Hain Yeh Jibreel Ro Kar.
Ibn-e-Muljim Ne Yeh Zulm Dhaya.
Arsh Par Mustafa Ro Rahe Hain.
Sab Ke Sab Anbiya Ro Rahe Hain.
Hai Farishton Mein Kuhram Barpa.

3
Koi Kulsoom Ko De Dilasa.
Our Tarapti Hain Gham Se Khadija.
Mula Abbas Karte Hain Noha.
Gham Hai Matam Hai Shoor-e-Buka Hai.
Aasmaan Bhi Laho Ro Raha Hai.
Uth Gaya Sar Se Zainab Ke Saya.

4
Qabar Mein Fatima Bhi Hain Muztar.
Kar Rahi Hain Yeh Faryad Ro Kar.
Waqt Kaisa Yeh Zainab Pe Aaya.
Sare Alfaz Rote Hain Mazhar.
Farsh-e-Majlis Pe Yeh Baat Keh Kar.
Dijiye Apni Bibi Ko Pursa.
