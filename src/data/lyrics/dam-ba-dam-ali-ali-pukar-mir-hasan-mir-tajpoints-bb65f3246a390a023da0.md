Dam Ba Dam Ali Ali Pukar Mir Hasan Mir.
Dam Ba Dam Ali Ali Pukar Har Qadam Ali Ali Pukar.

1
Tere Sonay Aangan Mein Khailay Gi Bahar.
Dar Badar Bhatakne Ka Kyun Yeh Roog Pala Hai.
Sab Ke Sab Bhikari Hain Kaun Deney Wala Hai.
Oss Se Mang Ontoon Ki De De Jo Qatar.

2
Pak Hai Lahu Kis Ka Kis Mein Kya Kharabi Hai.
Kaun Hai Saqifayi Kaun Boturabi Hai.
Pal Mein Sab Ke Chehron Se Hoga Aashkar.
Aal Ki Taraf Gar Tu Ik Qadam Barhaye Ga.

3
Das Qadam Zamane Se Khud Ko Aagay Paye Ga.
Kyunke Yeh Nahi Rakhtay Kissi Ka Udhar.
Ranj-o-Gham Ki Rahoon Mein Bad Hawas Betha Hai.
Chal Ali Ki Chokhat Par Kyun Udas Betha Hai.

4
Bas Palak Jhapaktay Hi Hoga Bera Paar.
Nam-e-Fatah-e-Khaybar Gar Tera Wazeefa Hai.
Door Hogi Har Mushkil Yeh Hamara Dawa Hai.
Khali Ja Nahi Sakta Tera Koi Waar.

5
Phir Na Koi Gham Hoga Phir Na Koi Dar Hoga.
Mar Bhi Jaye Ga Lekin Phir Bhi Tu Amar Hoga.
Kar De Apni Jaan Mere Maula Par Nisar.
Chahta Hai Gar Tujh Se Syeda Bhi Razi Hoon.

6
Khush Ho Khaliq-e-Akbar Mustafa Bhi Razi Hoon.
Munkiran-e-Haider Pe Kar De Beshumar.
Tujh Ko Fatima Zahra Ki Duaein Mil Jaein.
Khud Teri Ziyarat Ko Anbiya Chaley Aaein.

7
Karbala Pohanch Jaye Tu Jo Aik Bar.
Adal Our Adalat Ka Intezam Lainay Ko.
Karbala Ka Aye Nayab Inteqam Lainay Ko.
Aane Wala Hai Koi Le Ke Zulfiqar.
