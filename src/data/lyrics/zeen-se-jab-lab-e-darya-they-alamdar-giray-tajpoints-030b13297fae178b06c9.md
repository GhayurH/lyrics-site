Zeen Se Jab Lab-e-Darya They Alamdar Giray.
Thamay Kausar Pe Jigar Haider-e-Karrar Girey.

1
Dasht Mein Bikhrey Jo Abbas Ke Dekhey Bazu.
Arsh Par Rotay Hoye Jafar-e-Tayyar Girey.
Do Katay Hathoon Mein Jab Sheh Ko Nazar Aaya Alam.
Khak Par Thamay Kamar Ko Sheh-e-Abrar Girey.

2
Shimar Kehta Tha Lo Ab Ho Gaye Tanha Shabeer.
Foaj-e-Shabeer Ke Hain Ghorey Se Salar Girey.
Khak Uratay They Ali Peetti Thin Sar Zahra.
Dasht Mein Dosh-e-Muhammad Ke Jab Aswar Girey.

3
Diya Abbas Ne Bin Bazu Sahara Hamza.
Shukar-e-Abbas Keh Jab Jab Bhi Azadar Girey.
Lash-e-Abbas Pe Our Lasha-e-Akbar Pe Asad.
Aate Aate Hoye Shabeer Kayi Bar Girey.
