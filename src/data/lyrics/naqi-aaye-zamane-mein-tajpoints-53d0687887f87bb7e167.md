Naqi Aaye Zamane Mein Bahar Be Khizaan Aayi.
Kaha Islam Ne Ik Bar Phir Se Jaan Mein Jaan Aayi.

1
Naqi Ki Gaud Mein Quran Hai Ya Aks-e-Peygamber.
Bara-e-Tehniyat Arsh-e-Barin Se Kehkashan Aayi.
Imamat Ke Falak Pe Aaj Khursheed-e-Daham Aaya.
Imam Aaya Imamat Ko Tu Aawaz-e-Azaan Aayi.

2
Naqi Ke Naak Ki Nikhat Se Ik Aalam Moatar Hai.
Yeh Khushbo Bag-e-Zahra Ki Karan Taba Karan Aayi.
Zameen Ne Paye Aqdas Chom Ke Madah Guzari Hai.
Parho Salay Ala Mil Kar Sada-e-Aasmaan Aayi.

3
Aloom-o-Faiz Ke Darya Atta-o-Khalq Ki Barish.
Zameen Walon Ke Haq Mein Aaj Hokay Meharbaan Aayi.
Dikhaye Mojzay Aisay Keh Munkir Bhi Hoye Qayal.
Kaha Quran Ne Barh Kar Mere Munh Mein Zabaan Aayi.

4
Yeh Seerat Mein Muhammad Hai Shujat Mein Hai Yeh Haider.
Imam-e-Waqt Hai Qabzey Mein Raftar-e-Zamaan Aayi.
Zamana Aaj Bhi Rehan Inn Se Faiz Pata Hai.
Zaban Quran Ki Yeh Raaz Bhi Karti Ayyaan Aayi.
