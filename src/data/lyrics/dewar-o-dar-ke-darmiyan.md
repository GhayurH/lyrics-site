Dewar o Dar Ke Darmiyan
Dewar-o-Dar Ke Darmiyaan Zakhmi Hai Fatima
Aao Ali Madad Ko Bulati Hai Fatima
Choma Jisay Rasool Ne Woh Dar Jala Diya
Kya Ummat-e-Rasool Ko Aayi Nahi Haya
Yeh Baat Soch Soch Ke Roti Hai Fatima
Haan Woh Keh Jis Ko Rehmat-e-Alim Kaha Gaya
Jis Ne Kabhi Kissi Pe Sitam Hi Nahi Kiya
Logo Ossi Rasool Ki Beti Hai Fatima
Bazu Mein Iss Ke Keel Lagi Sar Se Khoon Rawaan
Pehlu Shikasta Ho Gaya, Tooti Hain Pasliyaan
Sanbhal Na Sakay, Yehi Soch Ke, Ali Ro Deiye
Sansein Na Jane Kis Tarha Leti Hai Fatima

Ab Kaun Mere Baad Hai Bachoon Ki Mere Aas
Haye Mere Hussain Ko Lagti Bohat Hai Pyas

Roney Pe Shahzadi Ke Pehra Laga Diya
Apna Watan Hi Jis Ghari Pardais Ho Gaya
Matlab Yateem Hone Ka Samjhi Hai Fatima
Ab Tak Sitam Ruka Nahi AZLAN AZMI
Turbat Pe Haye Zahra Ki Saya Nahi Koi
Mehdi Tumhara Rasta Takti Hai Fatima
