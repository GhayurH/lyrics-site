Sajda Ik Aisa Karb o Bala Mein Ada Howa.
Jis Ke Sabab Hai Khana-e-Kaba Bacha Howa.

1
Iss Baat Ke Gawah Sheh-e-Mursalin Hain.
Jis Khak Ke Khameer Se Yousuf Hasen Hain.
Oss Mein Hussain Ka Hai Paseena Mila Howa.
Herey Paray They Kanch Ke Tukron Ke Sath Sath.

2
Zahra Ne Chun Liye Hain Yeh Keh Kar Jawahrat.
Inn Aansoon Pe Naam Hai Mera Likha Howa.
Jaisa Diya Hai Waisa Hi Fanoos Chahiye.
Janat Ke Shehzaday Ko Malboos Chahiye.

3
Rizwan Lao Arsh Se Kurta Sila Howa.
Darwaza-e-Ali Ke Namak Khawar Aa Gaye.
Janat Mein Shah-e-Dein Ke Azadar Aa Gaye.
Sach Ho Gaya Hussain Ki Maa Ka Kaha Howa.

4
Pusht-e-Nabi Pe Aaj Imamat Ka Naaz Hai.
Waiz Mujhe Bata Ke Yeh Kaisi Namaz Hai.
Kis Wastay Nabi Ka Hai Sajdah Ruka Howa.
Woh Bhi Hai Ik Sipahi Hussaini Sipah Ka.

5
Karta Hoon Ayhteram Main Youn Zuljinah Ka.
Khak-e-Shifa Mein Oss Ka Bhi Khoon Hai Mila Howa.
Chaya Howa Damag Pe Oss Ke Ghubar Hai.
Oss Ko Bhi Ab Hussain Ka Gham Na Gawar Hai.

6
Tukron Pe Jo Hussain Ke Pal Kar Bara Howa.
Iss:MIR-E-Khush Nawa Ke Taranum Pe Naaz Hai.
Sachaiyon Ko Iss Ke Takallum Pe Naaz Hai.
Kyun Ke Hai Yeh Hussain Ke Dar Ka Parha Howa.
