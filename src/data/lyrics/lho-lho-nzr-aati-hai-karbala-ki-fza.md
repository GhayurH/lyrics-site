1
Lahu lahu nazar aati hai Karbala ki fiza
Ragge Batool hui hay
Jaga jaga se zibhaaa

2
1) Watan se door basera hay dasht may logo
Gharib jaan ke looto
Na mere sar se rida
Lahu lahu nazar aati hay Karbala ki fiza

3
2) Giray Abbas ko Karbal ke shenshah ne kaha
Dilon me naqsh rahaygi
Tumhari Ghazi wafaa
Lahu lahu nazar aati hay Karbala ki fiza

4
3) Pehan ke arsh e Mualla jisay gaye Ahmed
Tan e Hussain pe tukrray
Hui hay woh hi qaba
Lahu lahu nazar aati hay Karbala ki fiza

5
4) Ujarr ke dard ke mari hain bibiyan baithi
Sakina pooch rahi hay
Kahan gaye baba
Lahu lahu nazar aati hay Karbala ki fiza

6
5) Masum beti ka sar dekh ke na mar jaye
Wo dekho madare Asghar
Jhula rahi jhula
Lahu lahu nazar aati hay Karbala ki fiza
