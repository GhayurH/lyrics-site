Aisay Aaye Ali Akbar Sar-e-Maidan-e-Wiga.

1
Jaisay Badli Se Niya Chand Namodar Howa.
Jaisay Tasveer-e-Muhammad Se Utha Ho Pardah.
Jaisay Haider Ki Jawani Ka Chalan Jalwa Numa.
Yak Bayak Sallay Ala Narah-e-Akbar Gonja.
Naam Akbar Ali Akbar Hai Mera.
Main Hussain Ibn-e-Ali Ka Beta.

2
Kha Raha Hoon Qasam-e-Bait ALLAH.
Hum Ko Hasil Sharf-e-Khon-e-Nabi.
Bad Nasab Hum Pe Hakomat Karta.
Na Howa Hai Na Kabhi Ho Sakta.
Wallah Wallah Abba Abdillah.
Main Fida Tum Pe Hussain Ibn-e-Ali.

3
Shamiyon Ab Mera Hamla Dekho.
Zarbat-e-Syed-e-Wala Dekho.
Meri Talwar Ka Chalna Dekho.
Zarbat-e-Hashmi Zarb-e-Alvi.
Keh Ke Yeh Foaj Mein Doba Woh Shujaye Azali.
Narah Zan Tha Ke Ana Ibn-e-Hussain Ibn-e-Ali.

4
Teer Chalne Lagay Naizay Chaley Talwar Chali.
Barq-e-Shamsheer-e-Do Dam Se Saf-e-Kuffar Jali.
Kis Tarha Larta Hai Hum Shakal-e-Payamber Dekho.
Aaj Shamsheer-e-Hussaini Ke Bhi Johar Dekho.
Paltay Maidaan Se Waga Kartay Hoye.
Aa Ke Baba Se Yeh Akbar Bolay.

5
Thora Pani Mere Baba Jo Miley.
Dekhna Phir Se Meri Taig Zani.
Bolay Sheh Sokhi Zabaan Dikhla Kar.
Munh Mein Rakh Lo Yeh Angothi Akbar.
Aisa Sharbat Tumhein Dein Gay Haider.
Jis Ko Pe Kar Tumhein Kehna Hai Yehi.

6
Ran Mein Ja Kar Woh Bohat Dair Laray.
Chaar Janib Se Addo Toot Paray.
Nobatein Banje Lagin Jab Woh Girey.
Ik Sada Gonji Abba Adrikni.
Sun Ke Aawaz-e-Pisar Sheh Doray.
Khayi Thokar Kabhi Gir Kar Sanbhlay.

7
Ghutniyon Chalte Hoye Jab Pohanchay.
Ali Akbar Ki Yeh Aawaz Suni.
Kya Karey Ab Tera Baba Jee Kar.
Khak Dunya Hai Tere Baad Akbar.
Noha Jari Tha:REHAN-o-SARWAR:.
Ran Mein Roti Thi Sada-e-Zakhmi.
