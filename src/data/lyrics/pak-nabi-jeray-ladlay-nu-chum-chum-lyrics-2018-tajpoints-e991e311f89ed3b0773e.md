1
Pak Nabi Jeray Ladlay Nu Chum Chum Seenay Landay Ray.
Haye Ummati Is Hasan De Pak Janaze Te Teraan Da Meenh Barsanday Ray.
Aaye Si Jiwein Joray Dowaan Veraan De Arsh Toon Aynj Hi Aye Kafan v Do E.
Haye Aakhir Be Kafan Hussain Jo Rehna Si Shabar Nu Dowein Paonday Ray.

2
Nanay Da Shehar Yasrab Kitay Veran No Ho Jaway Aye Ghazi Bhira Zameen Te.
Haye Shabar Da Khon Giray Na Qatra V Ro Ro Maula Farmanday Ray.
Zainab Aye Wain Paye Teri Maiyyat Te Teer Tak Ke Main Aye Jan Gayi Aan Veera.
Haye Baba Adh Raat Batool Di Maiyyat Nu Chup Chup Ke Kyun Dafnanday Ray.

3
Tareekh Dasdi Payi Aye, Aye Faqat Ghasib-e-Fidak E Nayi Ahmad De Bad Aina.
Haye Ghurbat Haqdar Nu Rok Ditta Lekin Aye Ghiaraan Nu Dafnanday Ray.
 Akbar Hussain Maula Kadi Teraan Di Chan Che Maiyyat Nu Tak Tak Ke Roi Jawan.
Haye Qasim V Sambhday Re Shabir Othay Te Bhaina V Parchanday Ray.
