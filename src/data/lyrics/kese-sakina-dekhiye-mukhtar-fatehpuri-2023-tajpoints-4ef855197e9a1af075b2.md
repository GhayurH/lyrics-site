1
Kese Sakina Dekhiye Mukhtar Fatehpuri 2023
Jee Rahi Hai Baba Jaan Kaisay Sakina Dekhiye.
Neel Chehray Par Hain Kitne Mera Chehra Dekhiye.
Jab Miley Pairon Ko Baba Khar Zakhmi Kar Chukay.

2
Kanch Ke Tukray Unhi Zakhmo Mein Aa Kar Bas Gaye.
Khon Se Mere Hai Tar Zindaan Ka Rasta Dekhiye.
Nanhay Nanhay Bachoon Ko Bazar Mein Le Aate They.
Sham Walay Mar Kar Mujh Ko Rolatay Jate They.

3
Our Bachoon Se Yeh Kehte They Tamasha Dekhiye.
Pathron Se Iss Tarha Zakhmi Yeh Peshani Hoi.
Aap Hi Samjhein Gay Mujh Ko Jo Pareshani Hoi.
Kaisay Main Karti Hoon Ab Mati Pe Sajdah Dekhiye.

4
Sabiron Ki Beti Ho Tu Sabar Kyun Khoti Ho Tum.
Dard Hota Hai Tamachon Se Tu Kyun Roti Ho Tum.
Aap Mujh Ko Marne Walon Ka Shikwa Dekhiye.
Woh Bani Hashim Ke Roshan Ghar Madina Aap Ka.

5
Yeh Andherey Qaid Khane Yeh Aseeri Ki Fiza.
Ik Nazar Aye Baba Jaan Mera Madina Dekhiye.
Habs Ke Aalam Mein Rakha Aap Ke Sar Par Jo Sar.
Jaisay Janat Ki Hawaon Ka Howa Jari Safar.

6
Hota Jata Hai Ragoon Mein Khon Thanda Dekhiye.
Jane Kis Andaz Se Bibi Ne Akbar Ki Boka.
Sheh Ne Aankhein Khol Dein Jis Dam Sakina Ne Kaha.
Ik Nazar Dam Torti Beti Ko Baba Dekhiye.
