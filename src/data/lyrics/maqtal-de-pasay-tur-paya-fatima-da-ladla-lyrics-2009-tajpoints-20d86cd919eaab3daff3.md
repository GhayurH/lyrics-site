1
Maqtal De Pasay Tur Paya Fatima Da Ladla Ravi Road
Jism Te Mazloom De Phatiya Libas Aye Tur Piya.
Qafila Kar Ke Hawalay Abid-e-Beemar De.
Rondiyaan Bhainraan Nu Oh Denda Dilasay Tur Piya.

2
Chor Ke Ghar Bar Ghin Ke Naal Bhainraan Betiyaan.
Ahmad-e-Mukhtar Nu Kar Ke Udas Aye Tur Piya.
Baad Ghazi De Kamar Khum Ho Gayi Mazloom Di.
Fair V Maqtal Wichon Aye Chanr Lashay Tur Piya.

3
Cha Ke Asghar Nu Hathaan Te Ashqiya Di Foaj Wal.
Zulm-o-Istabdad De Kholan Khulasay Tur Piya.
Aynj Di Akhtar Ghareebi Cha Gayi Shabeer Te.
Chor Ke Wich Khaimiyaan De Baal Pyasay Tur Piya.
