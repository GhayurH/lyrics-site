Darwaza Giraya Baba
Ummat Ne Teri Zahra Par Darwaza Giraya Baba

1
Hasnain Ko Jhula Jhulane Sultan-e-Falak Aate They
Jis Dar Pe Salami Dene Har Waqt Malak Aate They
Haye Kalma Goyon Ne Us Dar Ko Jalaya Baba

2
Kayi Pehar Khaloti Rayi Aan Main Haye Wich Darbar De Baba
Tere Ashab Hasday Ray Oh Koi Uthya Na Wekh Ke Baba
Main Dasdi Rayi Main Zahra Haan Main Dhi Aan Pak Nabi Di
Haye Sanad De Tukray Chun Di Rayi Attay Main Rondi Rayi Baba

3
Pehron Main Khari Thi Lekin Darbar Mein Koi Na Utha
Tazeem Mein Aap Ka Uthna Ummat Ko Yaad Na Aaya
Furqat Ne Aap Ki Har Dam Mujh Ko Tarpaya Baba

4
Zakhmi Hai Mera Pehlu Aur Mohsin Ki Hui Hai Shahadat
Haye Aap Ke Baad Aayi Hai Har Lamha Mujh Pe Qayamat
Kafir Ne Sitam Jo Na Dhaya Ummat Ne Woh Dhaya Baba

5
Ashab Mein Kuch Aise Hain Jo Dar Pe Ruswai Hain
Dhate Hain Sitam Woh Mujh Par Kehne Ko To Shaidai Hain
Aise Ashab Ne Mujhko Har Lamha Sataya Baba

6
Gaddar Hui Hai Ummat Kismat Mein Ranj-o-Mehan Hai
Zalim Ki Jasarat Dekho Haider Ke Gale Mein Rasan Hai
Hai Taqdeer Ne Kaisa Din Mujhko Dikhaya Baba

7
Baad Aap Ke Hai Yathrib Mein Qayam Darbar-e-Shahi
Siddiqa Hoon Main Lekin Mangi Hai Mujh Se Gawahi
Rad Kar Ke Meri Gawahi Haq Mera Chupaya Baba

8
Munkir Hain Imamat Ke Aur Karte Hain Nabi Par Shak Yeh
Dete Nahi Aap Ka Pursa Karte Hain Ghasb Fidak Yeh
Hum Aal-e-Muhammad Par Yeh Kya Waqt Hai Aaya Baba

9
Baitul Ahzan Hai Kismat Tanhai Meri Aadat
Baba Main Na Seh Paungi Ab Aur Yeh Gham Ki Shiddat
Zinda Hi Turbat-e-Gham Mein Mujhko Dafnaya Baba

10
Main Hi Fakhr-e-Hawwa Hoon Main Hi Fakhr-e-Maryam Hoon
Atharah Baras Ke Sin Mein Manind-e-Zaifa Kham Hoon
Baad Aap Ke Misle Yatima Main Hoon Be-Saya Baba

11
Hamid Phir Raat Hi Hoti Din Par Jo Musibat Parti
Ro Ro Kar Boli Bibi Kab Tak Yeh Qayamat Sehti
Achha Hua Khwab Mein Mujhko Paas Apne Bulaya Baba
