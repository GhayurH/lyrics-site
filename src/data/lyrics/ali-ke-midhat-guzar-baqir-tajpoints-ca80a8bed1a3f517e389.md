Ali Ke Midhat Guzar Baqir Lyrics
Ali Ke Betay Ali Ke Potay Ali Ke Midhat Guzar Baqir.
Dua-e-Zahra Se Khilney Walay Chaman Ki Shan-e-Bahar Baqir.
Aloom-e-Haider Aloom-e-Sarwar Aloom-e-Sajjad Ka Khazana.
Pyam-e-Shabeer Ke Mobalig Attaye Parwardigar Baqir.
Padar Ke Sajdoon Ka Tum Samar Ho Hara Bhara Deen Ka Shajar Ho.
Tumhi Ne Bakhsha Hai Be Kali Mein Sakoon-e-Sabar-o-Qarrar Baqir.
Woh Feham-o-Idrak Our Farasat Tamam Khalq Khuda Ki Khidmat.
Ali Ki Sorat Nabi Ki Sorat Raha Tumhara Shaar Baqir.
Aloom-e-Makhfi-o-Zahir Se Kamal Darjah Jo Aa Gahi Thi.
Bahaye Tashna Labon Ki Khatir Aloom Ke Aabshar Baqir.
Woh Kamsini Our Woh Aziyat Woh Pa Payada Safar Ke Lamhey.
Na Aati Larzish Qadam Mein Phir Bhi Sitam Sahey Sad Hazar Baqir.
Muhabbaton Ke Safeer Ban Kar Rahe Zameen-e-Khuda Par Aisay.
Keh Dushmano Ko Bhi Banttay They Zar-e-Khalos Our Pyar Baqir.
Wohi Tareeqa Tha Bandagi Ka Wohi Saleeqa Tha Zindagi Ka.
Khuda Ki Marzi Ho Jis Mein Shamil Ali Ja Jis Mein Waqar Baqir.
Padar Ki Sorat Kalam Mein Thi Wohi Fasahat Wohi Balagat.
Chalai Khutboon Se Aap Ne Bhi Jagah Jagah Zulfiqar Baqir.
Tumhari Midhat Sarai Karna Tumharey Dar Ki Gadayi Karna.
Issi Mein Rehan Ki Baqa Ka Likha Hai Daro Maddar Baqir.
