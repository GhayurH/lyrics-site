Kaliyaan Chaliyaan Kaliyaan Ne Chaliyaan.
Koi Aaway Ghazi Diyaan Bhaina Baaj Ridawaan Khaliyaan.

1
Toon Zeen Jaye Na Chadda Aye Te Na Loki Takde Burqay Siraan Te Honde.
Bazar De Wich Aoundiyaan Paiyaan Ne Ghar Zahra Diyaan Paliyaan.
Zainab Ne Wain Paye Ghaziz De Bad Loko Be-Waris Assi Hoye.
Sun Wain Aye Okhe Haye Sham Diyaan Dewaraan Ne Haliyaan.

2
Mahool Ki Aye Baniya Rawaan Che Ujriyaan De Ki Shamiyaan Ne Keeta.
Tak Ghazi Kyun Menu Wekhan Layi Haye Mashallaan Ne Jaliyaan.
Zainab Ne Waday Keete Te Jaun Pore Karne Sun Lay Taun Veer Ghazi.
Teriyaan Bhaina Tenu Zinda Karna Jay Watna Te Waliyaa.
