Sadi Zakhmi Maa Aey Syed Raza Abbas Shah.

1
Sardar Nana Beemar Koi Nahi ALLAH Gawah Hay Sadi Zakhmi Maa Aye.
ALLAH De Kolon Ghinwa Day Nana Ohnko Shifa Aye Sadi Zakhmi Maa Aye.
Taiday Baad Nana Darbar Gayi Aye Logaan De Kolon Haq Mangdi Rayi Aye.
Ainj Haq Hai Maliya Jo Dadha Okha Hunr Ghindi Sah Aye Sadi Zakhmi Maa Aye.
Taidi Sand V Dakhlendi Rayi Aye Bhawein Sadiyaan Qasmaan V Chendi Rayi Aye.
Wal V Aye Nana Ummat Ne Chori Khali Walaye Sadi Zakhmi Maa Aye.

2
Kyun Haq Manganr Te Taun Bibi Tul Gayin Hin Loag Aahday Maslay Taun Bhul Gayin.
Haan Dhi Nabi Di Kayi Waar Nana Bhawein Dasaye Sadi Zakhmi Maa Aye.
Hasda Rehya Aye Teda Shehar Sara Saday Dhaday Logaan Sadi Maa Koon Mara.
Jeendi Wadi Hay Saday Babay Kolon Chehra Lokaye Sadi Zakhmi Maa Aye.
Oh Aayataan Walay Dar Koon Jala Gayi Saday Wehray Nana Ha Foaj Aa Gayi.
Khud Apnray Ghar Wich Dhadi Wadi Hai Karb-o-Bala Aye Sadi Zakhmi Maa Aye.

3
Taun Sunr Naa Sagh Sain Sadiyaan Be Wasiyaan Logaan Pawaiyaan Saday Piyo Koon Rasiyaan.
Ummat De Kolon Churwendi Reh Gayi Baba Sada Aye Sadi Zakhmi Maa Aye.
Nana Musallay Te Khar Nahi Saghdi Taidi Bachri Tasbeeh V Parh Nahi Saghdi.
Bas Maut Apnrin Di Lahzay Lahzay Mangdi Dua Aye Sadi Zakhmi Maa Aye.
Thi Ke Aye Kazim Be Chain Ro Paye Aakh Ke Han Hasnain Ro Paye.
Rozay Taiday Wich Mangdi Hay Nana Turbat Di Ja Aye Sadi Zakhmi Maa Aye.
