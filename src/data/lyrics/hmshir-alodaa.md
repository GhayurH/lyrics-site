LAASHA-E-SHAH SE MAQTAL ME YE ATI THI SADA
HAMSHEER ALVIDA, HAMSHEER ALVIDA

1
SAR KHULE SHAM KE BAZAAR ME JANA HAI TUMHE
KHUTBA DENE KO BHI DARBAR ME JANA HAI TUMHE
KUFR KI ZAD SE BACHANA HAI TUMHE DEEN-E-KHUDA

2
HAR QADAM PAR NAHI SAH PAYEGI WO RANJ-O-MALAL
MERI MASOOM SAKINA KA BHI TUM RAKHNA KHAYAL
QAIDKHANE ME USE KHAAK PA SONA HOGA

3
BANIYE ZULM SE DARTI THI NA GHABRATI THI
JIS KE HOTE HUE TUM CHAIN SE SO JATI THI
WAQT-E-GHURBAT ME HUA TUM SE WO GHAZI BHI JUDA

4
QAIDKHANE SE YAHA’N LAUT KE ANA ZAINAB
MERI MAJLIS MERI TURBAT PA KARANA ZAINAB
KARNA IS DASHT ME DIL KHOL KE MATAM MERA

5
JIS GHADI SHAM KE BAZAR ME TUM JAOGI
TUM SARE NAIZA MUJHE ROTA HUA PAOGI
MUJH SE DEKHA NAHI JAYEGA WO MANZAR GHAM KA

6
DASHT-E-KARBAL SE KHUDA HAFIZ-O-NASIR ZAINAB
KHOO’N KE MAQTAL SE KHUDA HAFIZ-O-NASIR ZAINAB
DAFN KARNE KO MUJHE AYEGA MERA BETA

7
ZULM SAHTE HUE TUM KO YE SAFAR KARNA HAI
BERIDA REHNA HAI FAQO’N ME GUZAR KARNA HAI
BAAD MUDDAT KE TUMHE AHLE SITAM DENGE RIDA

8
KAISE YAQUB SE MANZAR YE SHUJA HO TEHREER
SISKIYA’N LENE LAGI SIBTE NABI KI HAMSHEER
APNE MAA’N JAYE KI MAQTAL ME SUNI JAB YE SADA
