1
Ran Se Wapis Mere Baba Nahi Aane Walay.
Mujh Pe Ab Saye Yateemi Ke Hain Chanay Walay.
Subha Se Dekh Rahi Hai Yeh Sakina Baba.
Ran Mein Hai Charon Taraf Phaila Qaza Ka Saya.
Laut Kar Zindah Nahi Aaye Hain Jane Walay.
Aap Jis Tarha Mujhe Seene Laga Kar Roye.
Khak Par Jaisay Sakina Ko Sula Kar Roye.
Hain Yeh Andaz Yateemo Ko Solaney Walay.
Aap Ne Jab Se Hai Rukhsat Ka Iradah Bandha.
Jane Kyun Dukhney Laga Hai Mera Chehra Baba.
Neel Jaisay Mere Chehray Pe Hoon Aane Walay.
Jaisay Khat Aik Chacha Ibn-e-Mazahir Ko Likha.
Bhaij Kar Khat Meri Khatir Hi Bola Lein Baba.
Reh Gaye Hoon Jo Koi Dost Bulanay Walay.
Aap Ke Seene Se Lagti Hoon Tu Youn Lagta Hai.
Zakhm Hi Zakhm Hain Zakhmo Se Lahu Behta Hai.
Jaisay Iss Seene Mein Hain Teer Samaney Walay.
Meri Chadar Mere Baloon Se Hatatay Kyun Hain.
Rait Ka Pardah Mere Sar Pe Banatay Kyun Hain.
Kab Rida Kartay Hain Youn Sheh Ke Gharanay Walay.
Sheh Se Lipati Hoi Kehti Thi Sakina Akbar.
Ho Na Ho Aap Pe Barsaein Gay Aada Mil Kar.
Jitnay Pathar Hain Yeh Maqtal Se Uthane Walay.
