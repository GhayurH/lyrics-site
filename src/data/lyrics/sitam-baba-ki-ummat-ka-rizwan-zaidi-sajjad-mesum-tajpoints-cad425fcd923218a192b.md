Sitam Baba Ki Ummat Ka Rizwan Zaidi Sajjad & Mesum.

1
Sitam Baba Ki Ummat Ka Kabhi Bholin Nahi Zahra.
Woh Jalta Ghar Ka Darwaza Kabhi Bholin Nahi Zahra.
Nabi Zadi Ke Baloon Ki Safaidi Yeh Batati Hai.
Bharay Darbar Mein Jana Kabhi Bholin Nahi Zahra.
Tamachon Ke Nishaan Shabar Na Dekhey Iss Liye Apna.
Chupana Lal Se Chehra Kabhi Bholin Nahi Zahra.

2
Rasan Dekhi Galay Mein Murtaza Ke Haye Bibi Ne.
Ali Par Zulm Aada Ka Kabhi Bholin Nahi Zahra.
Thin Inn Ki Pasliyaan Tooti Hoin Our Zakhm-e-Pehlu Tha.
Musalsal Dard Ka Uthna Kabhi Bholin Nahi Zahra.
Rahin Jab Tak Bhi Zindah Bas Ossi Ko Roti Rehti Thin.
Bicharna Apne Mohsin Ka Kabhi Bholin Nahi Zahra.

3
Khulay They Jis Ghari Band-e-Kafan Tu Maa Ki Maiyyat Se.
Lipat Kar Lal Ka Rona Kabhi Bholin Nahi Zahra.
Chaley They Turbat-e-Madar Se Sheh Karb-o-Bala Jis Dam.
Sheh-e-Mazloom Ka Jana Kabhi Bholin Nahi Zahra.
Jisay Izhar Ban Mein Kar Diya Pamal Aada Ne.
Woh Sheh Ka Be Kafan Lasha Kabhi Bholin Nahi Zahra.
