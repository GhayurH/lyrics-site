Haye Karbala ch loko kalla Husain reh geya
Sab tur gaye puttar bhira, aede mittiyaan te aa ke beh geya

1
Mera Abid bimaar we, Zainab tu hi waaris ae mere waaris di
Maqtal wal jaandiyaan ro ro ke Zainab nu veer keh geya

2
Kitthe Laila na tak ke mar jaawe, kiwein laashe nu khaime le jaawe
Haye Sayyid laash-e-Akbar le ke khaime de dar te beh geya

3
Arsh toṅ Fatima te Haider di aayi aawaaz marhaba Shabbir
Asghar nu qabr ch paawan da manzar tuṅ kiwein seh geya

4
Jiwein barchhi sahi si Akbar di
Maut bardaasht kiti Asghar di
Zakhm sab gin gin ke oh terah zarbaaṅ seh geya

5
Tir saare te kand de ole ne
Paar sine de sirf nokaaṅ ne
Ya Zahra jaandi ya Zainab tiraaṅ toṅ kiwein leh geya

6
Aenu sajde ch soch bhainraaṅ di
Aenu khanjar de waar nahiṅ dukhde
Khaimagaah wal nazraaṅ kar ke oh terah zarbaaṅ seh geya

7
Jism tiraaṅ de naal bharya si
Zin toṅ lath ke aaya nokaaṅ te
Tiraaṅ toṅ aakhri sajde lai Shabbir kiwein leh geya

8
Sheh di Saqlain ae wasiyyat si
Maatami thanda pine jad paani
Oh meri pyaas nu yaad rakhaṅ, Zainab nu jaande keh geya

9
Kai milaaṅ de tapde sehra wich aaya Saqlain pyaasa maula mera
Kadi Qasim te kadi Ghazi de tukre hi labhda reh geya
